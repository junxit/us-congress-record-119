# Text of Senate Amendment 2870

**Speaking:** Warnock, Raphael G. (W000790) [D-GA]

```
  SA 2870. Mr. WARNOCK submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 2853 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Schmitt) to the 
bill H.R. 4, to rescind certain budget authority proposed to be 
rescinded in special messages transmitted to the Congress by the 
President on June 3, 2025, in accordance with section 1012(a) of the 
Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control Act of 1974; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        In section 2(b)(12), strike ``$2,500,000,000 are 
     rescinded'' and insert ``$2,400,000,000 are rescinded. The 
     rescission under this paragraph shall not apply to amounts 
     designated for Power Africa''.
                                 ______
```
