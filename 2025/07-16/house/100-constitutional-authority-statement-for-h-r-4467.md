# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4467

**Measures:** H.R. 4467

```
           By Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi:
       H.R. 4467.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2 (Property Clause)
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2, provides Congress with the 
     power to ``dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory and other Property 
     belonging to the United States.''
```
