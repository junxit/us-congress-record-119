# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.J. Res. 103

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 103

```
            By Mr. BARR:
        H.J. Res. 103.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to propose amendments to the 
     Constitution of the United States pursuant to Article V of 
     the Constitution, which provides that Congress may, by a two-
     thirds vote of both the House of Representatives and the 
     Senate, propose amendments to the Constitution.
```
