# Text of Senate Amendment 2797

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

```
  SA 2797. Mr. PETERS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 90007, add at the end the following: ``Such 
     funds shall be expended in compliance with chapter 83 of 
     title 41, United States Code (commonly known as the `Buy 
     American Act').''.
                                 ______
```
