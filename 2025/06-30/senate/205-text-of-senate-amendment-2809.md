H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        In section 1181(3) of title 14, United States Code, as

     ``$242,000,000''.

     unobligated balance of amounts appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by such section 40004, which shall be used for a 
     Phased Array Radar Test Article'' after ``rescinded''.
