# Text of Senate Amendment 2822

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 2822. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title II, insert the following:

     SEC. __. LIMITATION ON USE OF FUNDS FOR DEPLOYMENTS FOR 
                   DOMESTIC LAW ENFORCEMENT PURPOSES WITHOUT STATE 
                   CONSENT.

       None of the funds appropriated by this title may be made 
     for the deployment of members of the Armed Forces for use in, 
     or as a support function to, domestic law enforcement 
     purposes without a written request of the Governor of a State 
     (or the Mayor, in the case of the District of Columbia) or 
     the invocation of authority provided under chapter 13 of 
     title 10, United States Code (commonly referred to as the 
     ``Insurrection Act'').
                                 ______
```
