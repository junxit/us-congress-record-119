# Text of Senate Amendment 2731

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 2731. Mr. HEINRICH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 50101(a)(1), strike ``Subsection (a) of'' and 
     insert ``Subject to paragraph (3), subsection (a) of''.
       In section 50101(a), add at the end the following:
       (3) Exception.--Notwithstanding the amendments made by 
     paragraph (1), during any month in which the average price of 
     Brent crude oil exceeds $67.80 per barrel--
       (A) the applicable royalty rate for leases issued pursuant 
     to section 17 of the Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 226) 
     shall be not less than 16\2/3\ percent; and
       (B) the applicable royalty rate for reinstated leases 
     issued pursuant to section 31 of the Mineral Leasing Act (30 
     U.S.C. 188) shall be not less than 20 percent.
                                 ______
```
