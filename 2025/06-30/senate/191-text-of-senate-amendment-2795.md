# Text of Senate Amendment 2795

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

```
  SA 2795. Mr. PETERS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 90003, add at the end the following new 
     subsection:
       (d) Buy American Compliance.--Funds appropriated by this 
     section for detention capacity shall be expended in 
     compliance with chapter 83 of title 41, United States Code 
     (commonly known as the ``Buy American Act'').
                                 ______
```
