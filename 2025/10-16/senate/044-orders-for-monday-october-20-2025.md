# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, 2025

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, 2025

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, October 20; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; finally, that the 
cloture motions filed during today's session of the Senate ripen at 
5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
