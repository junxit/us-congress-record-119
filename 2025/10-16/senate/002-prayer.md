# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, from whom all holy desires come, rise mercifully upon our 
hearts and sustain us in the trench warfare of this government 
shutdown. Wipe out all our selfish desires so that we may be true 
instruments for Your glory.
  Lord, remind our lawmakers that You are their strength and that those 
who abandon You cannot ultimately prevail.
  Be near to this Nation, O Lord, as we find protection in the shadow 
of Your grace. Surround us with the shield of Your glory and might.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
