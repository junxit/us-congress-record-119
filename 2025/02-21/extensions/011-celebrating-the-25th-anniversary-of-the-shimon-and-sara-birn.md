# CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SHIMON AND SARA BIRNBAUM JEWISH COMMUNITY CENTER

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SHIMON AND SARA BIRNBAUM JEWISH 
                            COMMUNITY CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 21, 2025

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 25th anniversary 
of the Shimon and Sara Birnbaum Jewish Community Center in Bridgewater, 
NJ. Through its wide range of services and diverse programming, the JCC 
works with the Jewish community and the Bridgewater community at large 
to preserve and strengthen Jewish identity. Over the past 25 years, 
members of all ages have had the opportunity to enjoy services such as 
fitness centers, educational classes, summer camps, and much more. I am 
honored to have such an incredible organization in the 7th 
Congressional District, and I look forward to witnessing the continued 
growth of the Shimon and Sara Birnbaum Jewish Community Center.
```
