                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 252

  At the request of Mr. Johnson, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 252, a bill

  At the request of Mr. Tillis, the name of the Senator from Alabama 
(Mrs. Britt) was added as a cosponsor of S. 336, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income amounts 
received from State-based catastrophe loss mitigation programs.

  At the request of Mr. Tuberville, the name of the Senator from North 
Dakota (Mr. Cramer) was added as a cosponsor of S. 405, a bill to 
modify eligibility requirements for amateur sports governing 
organizations.

Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 470, a bill 
to amend the CARES Act to remove a requirement on lessors to provide
