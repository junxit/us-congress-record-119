# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 2025

**Speaking:** Sullivan, Dan (S001198) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 130

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 2025

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 11 a.m. 
on Thursday, November 20; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of H.J. Res. 130, with all other provisions of the 
previous order remaining in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
