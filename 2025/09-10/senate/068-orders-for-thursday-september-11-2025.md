# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 2296

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Thursday, September 11; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 115, S. 2296, the National Defense 
Authorization Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
