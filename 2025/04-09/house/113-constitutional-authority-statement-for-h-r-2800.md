# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2800

**Measures:** H.R. 2800

```
           By Mr. VASQUEZ:
       H.R. 2800.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United State 
     Constitution, to provide for the general welfare and make all 
     laws necessary and proper to carry out the powers of the 
     Congress.
```
