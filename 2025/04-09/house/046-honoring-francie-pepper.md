# HONORING FRANCIE PEPPER

**Speaking:** Landsman, Greg (L000601) [D-OH]

```
                        HONORING FRANCIE PEPPER

  (Mr. LANDSMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a remarkable human 
being, a dear friend, Francie Pepper. Francie, at her core, was a mom 
and a grandmother but also an advocate, a philanthropist, and a 
champion for women and children.
  Francie led enormous change for survivors of domestic violence. It is 
just one of many examples of the impact she had, but a critical one. 
She never stopped fighting for what she knew was right.
  Her home in Cincinnati was a place for community and community 
change. She built coalitions and inspired generations of public 
servants, including me.
  Francie wasn't just a leader. She was a force. I am who I am in part 
because of her. That is true for so many of us. We are the 
beneficiaries of her grit and her toughness and her love.

                          ____________________
```
