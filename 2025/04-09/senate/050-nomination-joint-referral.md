# NOMINATION JOINT REFERRAL

**Speaking:** Cruz, Ted (C001098) [R-TX]

```
                       NOMINATION JOINT REFERRAL

  Mr. CRUZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that as if in 
executive session, the nomination of David Fogel, of Connecticut, to be 
Assistant Secretary of Commerce and Director General of the United 
States and Foreign Commercial Service received in the Senate on 
February 11, 2025, be jointly referred to the Committee on Banking, 
Housing, and Urban Affairs and the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
