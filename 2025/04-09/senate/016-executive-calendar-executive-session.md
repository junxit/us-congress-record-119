# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I move to proceed to executive session to 
consider Calendar No. 74.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of the 
following named officer for appointment as Chairman of the Joint Chiefs 
of Staff and appointment in the United States Air Force to the grade 
indicated while assigned to a position of importance and responsibility 
under title 10, U.S.C., sections 152 and 601: to be General, Lt. Gen. 
John D. Caine (Retired).
```
