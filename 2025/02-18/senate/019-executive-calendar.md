# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

of February 13 in relation to the Lutnick nomination.

  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Howard Lutnick, of New 
York, to be Secretary of Commerce.
