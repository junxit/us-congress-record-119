DO NO HARM IN MEDICAID ACT; PROTECT CHILDREN'S INNOCENCE ACT; LOWER 
HEALTH CARE PREMIUMS FOR ALL AMERICANS ACT
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 498, the ``Do 
No Harm in Medicaid Act''; H.R. 3492, the ``Protect Children's 
Innocence Act''; and H.R. 6703, the ``Lower Health Care Premiums for 
All Americans Act''. The Committee granted, by a record vote of 6-4, a 
rule providing for consideration of H.R. 6703, the ``Lower Health Care 
Premiums for All Americans Act'', H.R. 498, the ``Do No Harm in 
Medicaid Act'', and H.R. 3492, the ``Protect Children's Innocence 
Act''. The rule provides for consideration of H.R. 6703, the ``Lower 
Health Care Premiums for All Americans Act'', under a closed rule. The 
rule waives all points of order against consideration of the bill. The 
rule provides that the bill shall be considered as read. The rule 
waives all points of order against provisions in the bill. The rule 
provides one hour of general debate equally divided among and 
controlled by the respective chairs and ranking minority members of the 
Committees on Education and Workforce, Energy and Commerce, and Ways 
and Means, or their respective designees. The rule provides one motion 
to recommit. The rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 498, 
the ``Do No Harm in Medicaid Act'', under a closed rule. The rule 
waives all points of order against consideration of the bill. The rule 
provides that the bill shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the bill. The rule provides one 
hour of general debate equally divided and controlled by the chair and 
ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and Commerce or 
their respective designees. The rule provides one motion to recommit. 
The rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 3492, the ``Protect 
Children's Innocence Act'', under a structured rule. The rule waives 
all points of order against consideration of the bill. The rule 
provides that the amendment in the nature of a substitute recommended 
by the Committee on the Judiciary now printed in the bill shall be 
considered as adopted and the bill, as amended, shall be considered as 
read. The rule waives all points of order against provisions in the 
bill, as amended. The rule provides one hour of general debate equally 
divided and controlled by the chair and ranking minority member of the 
Committee on the Judiciary or their respective designees. The rule 
makes in order only the further amendment printed in the Rules 
Committee report. Each amendment may be offered only in the order 
printed in the report, may be offered only by a Member designated in 
the report, shall be considered as read, shall be debatable for the 
time specified in the report equally divided and controlled by the 
proponent and an opponent, shall not be subject to amendment, and shall 
not be subject to a demand for division of the question. The rule 
waives all points of order against the amendment printed in the Rules 
Committee report. The rule provides one motion to recommit. Finally, 
the rule provides that during consideration of H.R. 4776, pursuant to 
House Resolution 951, the further amendment specified in section 5 of 
this resolution shall be considered as adopted. Testimony was heard 
from Chairman Guthrie, Chairman Walberg, Chairman Smith of Missouri, 
and Representatives Pallone, Landsman, Scott of Virginia, DeSaulnier, 
Thompson of California, Moore of Alabama, Raskin, Fitzpatrick, Lawler, 
Spartz, and LaLota.
