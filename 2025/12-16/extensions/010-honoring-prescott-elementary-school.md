# HONORING PRESCOTT ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Quigley, Mike (Q000023) [D-IL]

```
                  HONORING PRESCOTT ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE QUIGLEY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Prescott 
Elementary School on their designation as a 2025 National Blue Ribbon 
School.
  Since 1982, the Blue-Ribbon School Award has recognized the most 
exemplary public and

[[Page E1205]]

private schools for outstanding academic performance and significant 
progress in closing achievement gaps among students.
  From its inception, Prescott Elementary has continued to pave a 
bright path of academic leadership and set an example for schools and 
communities in the Chicagoland area.
  Prescott Elementary has earned Illinois' top ``Exemplary'' rating for 
the fourth consecutive year. For academic performance, the school is 
once again in the top 10 percent of Illinois public schools. This K-8 
CPS school serving a majority student of color population and offering 
a rigorous academic program in reading, writing, math, science, and 
social studies maintains continued success. The school also prioritizes 
social-emotional learning (SEL), restorative practices, and a culture 
that helps students thrive both academically and personally.
  Although the Blue-Ribbon School Award has been canceled, it is 
imperative to recognize schools like Prescott Elementary, which deserve 
praise and congratulations for this outstanding achievement.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me and all our colleagues in the House of 
Representatives in recognizing Prescott Elementary for their 
exceptional and continuous success in educating and nurturing the 
brilliant future minds of this Nation.

                          ____________________
```
