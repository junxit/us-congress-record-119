# RECOGNIZING AVON HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE DIVISION II STATE GIRLS VOLLEYBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
  RECOGNIZING AVON HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE DIVISION II STATE GIRLS 
                        VOLLEYBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I rise 
to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school volleyball 
team in Ohio's 5th Congressional District. The young women of the Avon 
High School girls' volleyball team have represented their school ably 
on their way to achieving the Division II State Girls Volleyball Title. 
In their effort to surpass all other teams in the Division II State 
Volleyball Playoffs, the Avon Eagles overcame the challenges posed by 
Intense competition.
  In pursuing the State Championship, the Avon Eagles defeated the 
Anthony Wayne Generals to win the state volleyball championship. The 
members of this very special team have shown that their sport requires 
an individual effort for a team result. Their hard work and dedication, 
both on and off the court, in achieving this accomplishment, is truly 
outstanding.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special tribute 
to the Avon High School girls volleyball team. On behalf of the people 
of the 5th District of Ohio, I am proud to recognize this great 
achievement.

                          ____________________
```
