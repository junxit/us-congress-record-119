# CONGRATULATING MR. FLOYD AND MRS. CORA HENDERSON ON THE OCCASION OF THEIR 65TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
  CONGRATULATING MR. FLOYD AND MRS. CORA HENDERSON ON THE OCCASION OF 
                     THEIR 65TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANNY K. DAVIS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise to express congratulations 
to my dear cousin Cora and her wonderful husband Floyd, on the occasion 
of their 65th wedding anniversary. Sixty-five years is a long time, but 
when you can spend them with one person sharing every moment the time 
becomes more and more precious as the days go by.
  Cora and I grew up together in rural Arkansas, Parkdale to be exact, 
her father Mr. Willie

[[Page E1212]]

Vaughn (Uncle Dude) and my mother Mazzie Lee Davis were brother and 
sister. Our families worshipped together, and visited each other's 
homes. Cora and I were classmates in school and graduated from high 
school together. My wife of 56 years, Dr. Vera G. Davis and all of our 
family extend to her and Floyd our best wishes and pray they have, 
many, many more anniversaries.

                          ____________________
```
