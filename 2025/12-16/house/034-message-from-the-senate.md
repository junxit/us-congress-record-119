# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 550, S. 748, S. 1510

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 550. An act to provide for the equitable settlement of 
     certain Indian land disputes regarding land in Illinois, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. 748. An act to reaffirm the applicability of the Indian 
     reorganization Act to the Lytton Rancheria of California, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. 1510. An act to amend the Civil Rights Cold Case Records 
     Collection Act of 2018 to strengthen the powers of the Civil 
     Rights Cold Case Records Review Board, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
