# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the Apostle Paul admonished us in Romans 12:15 with 
these words:

       Rejoice with those who rejoice, and weep with those who 
     weep.

  Lord, we weep because of the Bondi Beach shooting during Hanukkah in 
Australia. We weep because of the murders of Rob and Michele Reiner. We 
weep because of the victims of the Brown University shooting. Because 
of events like these, we weep for our Nation and world. Wipe away our 
tears because we weep.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
