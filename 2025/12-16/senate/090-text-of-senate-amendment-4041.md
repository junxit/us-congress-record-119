# Text of Senate Amendment 4041

**Speaking:** Reed, Jack (R000122) [D-RI]

```
  SA 4041. Mr. REED submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  No funds appropriated under any division of this 
     Act may be expended for expenses related to procurement of 
     statues or other activities as described in Executive Order 
     13934 (85 Fed. Reg. 41165; relating to building and 
     rebuilding monuments to American heroes), Executive Order 
     13978 (86 Fed. Reg. 6809; relating to building the National 
     Garden of American Heroes), and Executive Order 14189 (90 
     Fed. Reg. 8849; relating to celebrating America's birthday).
                                 ______
```
