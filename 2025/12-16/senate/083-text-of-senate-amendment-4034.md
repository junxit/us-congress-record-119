# Text of Senate Amendment 4034

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 4034. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department 
of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. RESCISSION LIMITATION.

       No amounts may be rescinded from amounts provided under 
     this Act or any other appropriation Act for fiscal year 2026, 
     unless the rescission is made through an appropriation Act 
     (as defined in section 3 of the Congressional Budget and 
     Impoundment Control Act of 1974 (2 U.S.C. 622)).
                                 ______
```
