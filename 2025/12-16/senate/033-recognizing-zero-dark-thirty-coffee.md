# RECOGNIZING ZERO DARK THIRTY COFFEE

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                  RECOGNIZING ZERO DARK THIRTY COFFEE

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, as a member and former chairman of 
the Senate Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each month 
I recognize and celebrate the American entrepreneurial spirit by 
highlighting the success of a small business in my home State of Idaho. 
Today, I am proud to honor Zero Dark Thirty Coffee in Meridian, as the 
Idaho Small Business of the Month for December 2025.
  Zero Dark Thirty Coffee began following founder Jarad Webb's battle 
with PTSD after serving in the U.S. Marine Corps for 4 years and the 
Nampa Police Department for 16 years. Like many veterans, Jarad left 
military service with undiagnosed PTSD which, along with a series of 
surgeries, led to an opiate addiction that disconnected him from his 
family.
  Jarad decided to seek help and entered the Ada County Veterans 
Treatment Court to begin his healing process. There he met Brent Rowe, 
an Air Force and Army veteran with years of coffee-roasting experience. 
The pair bonded over their shared military experiences and a love for 
rich roasts.
  In 2020, Jarad and Brent launched Zero Dark Thirty Coffee, naming the 
company after the military term for ``way too early.'' They aspired to 
do more than just sell coffee; they wanted to create a business that 
would fund trauma recovery for veterans and first responders. To 
achieve this, Jarad ran their nonprofit, the ZDT Foundation, while 
Brent focused on roasting high-quality coffee beans to sell. Four years 
later, Brent remains a mentor and customer, and Jarad has immersed 
himself in the craft of coffee roasting.
  Zero Dark Thirty Coffee donates a large portion of its profit to 
support trauma recovery and ensure that no warrior suffers alone. The 
ZDT Foundation works closely with organizations such as Homeward for 
Heroes, the Taylor Foundation, Save A Warrior, and many others across 
the Treasure Valley to serve veterans, first responders, and their 
families. To date, they have helped over 50 local warriors reclaim 
their lives from PTSD and it all started with a single cup of coffee.
  Congratulations to Jarad Webb and all the employees at Zero Dark 
Thirty Coffee on being recognized as the Idaho Small Business of the 
Month for December 2025. Your dedication to excellence makes Idaho 
proud, and I look forward to your continued growth and success.●
```
