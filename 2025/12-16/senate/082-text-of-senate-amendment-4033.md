# Text of Senate Amendment 4033

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4033. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:


[[Page S8790]]


  

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  Notwithstanding any other provision of law, none 
     of the funds appropriated by this Act may be used to support 
     or engage in hostilities by the Armed Forces of the United 
     States against any organization designated on or after 
     February 20, 2025, as a foreign terrorist organization, or 
     any states in which those organizations operate, unless such 
     hostilities are expressly authorized by a declaration of war 
     or authorization for use of military force.
                                 ______
```
