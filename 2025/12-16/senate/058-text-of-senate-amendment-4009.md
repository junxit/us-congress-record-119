# Text of Senate Amendment 4009

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 4009. Mr. KIM submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. _____.  Not later than 30 days after the date on which 
     the Attorney General submits to Congress the report required 
     under section 3 of the Epstein Files Transparency Act (Public 
     Law 119-38; 139 Stat. 657), the Attorney General and the 
     Deputy Attorney General shall testify before the Committee on 
     the Judiciary of the Senate and the Committee on the 
     Judiciary of the House of Representatives about the substance 
     of the report.
                                 ______
```
