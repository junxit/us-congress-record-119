# TRIBUTE TO BILL CARBERRY

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BILL CARBERRY

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, I rise today, along with my 
colleagues Senator Mike Crapo and Representative  Mike Simpson, to 
recognize Bill Carberry for his 24 years of dedicated service as 
airport manager of the Magic Valley Regional Airport.
  A lifelong aviation enthusiast, Carberry earned his private pilot's 
license in 1985 and received a bachelor's degree from Embry-Riddle 
Aeronautical University. Prior to his tenure at the Magic Valley 
Regional Airport, Carberry worked at the Centennial Airport in Denver 
for 9 years, where he learned the skills that laid the foundation for 
his successful career.
  Since assuming the manager role in May 2001, Carberry guided the 
airport through many milestones and improvements that have benefited 
operations and the community. His leadership resulted in many important 
projects, including partnering with the Bureau of Land Management to 
develop a modern aviation wildland firefighting facility that supports 
local firefighting efforts. Carberry was also a key figure in 
transitioning the airport from servicing turboprop aircraft to regional 
jets, opening new travel destinations for Magic Valley residents, and 
introducing commercial air services.
  Carberry's efforts went beyond operational excellence. He has been a 
driving force in fostering stronger community connections, including 
through

[[Page S8774]]

the annual Magic Valley Airshow, which featured three shows and the 
U.S. Navy Blue Angels this year.
  In his well-deserved retirement, Carberry is looking forward to new 
travels, gardening, and spending time with his family.
  It is with great pride that we recognize the achievements of Bill 
Carberry as the manager of the Magic Valley Regional Airport. Thank 
you, Bill, for your many years of service. We wish you all the best in 
your well-earned retirement.●

                          ____________________
```
