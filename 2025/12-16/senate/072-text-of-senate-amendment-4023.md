# Text of Senate Amendment 4023

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
  SA 4023. Mrs. SHAHEEN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department 
of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. _____.  Section 586(b) of title 28, United States 
     Code, is amended by striking the first sentence and inserting 
     ``The United States trustee for such region shall appoint 1 
     or more individuals who are residents of a State to serve as 
     standing trustee for that State.''.
                                 ______
```
