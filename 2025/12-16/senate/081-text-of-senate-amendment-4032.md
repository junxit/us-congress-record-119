# Text of Senate Amendment 4032

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4032. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __. (a) Notwithstanding section 430 of the General 
     Education Provisions Act (20 U.S.C. 1231), section 415 or 
     section 419 of the Department of Education Organization Act 
     (20 U.S.C. 3475, 3479), section 1501 or 1535 of title 31, 
     United States Code, or any other provision of law, none of 
     the funds appropriated in this Act or any other 
     appropriations Act may be used to transfer or delegate 
     statutory responsibilities of the Department of Education to 
     another Federal agency or to consolidate offices of the 
     Department of Education that are established by statute.
       (b) The Department of Education shall support staffing 
     levels at the Department of Education, including staffing 
     levels at each office within the Department of Education, 
     that are necessary to fulfill its statutory responsibilities 
     including carrying out programs, projects, and activities 
     funded under this Act in a timely and effective manner.
                                 ______
```
