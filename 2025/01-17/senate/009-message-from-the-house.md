# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 30

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 9:33 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 30. An act to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to provide that aliens who have been convicted of or who 
     have committed sex offenses or domestic violence are 
     inadmissible and deportable.
```
