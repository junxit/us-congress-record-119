# Text of Senate Amendment 94

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
  SA 94. Mr. PAUL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 8 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Ms. Ernst (for herself and 
Mr. Grassley)) to the bill S. 5, to require the Secretary of Homeland 
Security to take into custody aliens who have been charged in the 
United States with theft, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

       In lieu of the matter proposed to be inserted, insert the 
     following:
       ``(ii) is charged with, is arrested for, is convicted of, 
     admits having committed, or admits committing acts which 
     constitute the essential elements of any burglary, theft, 
     larceny, shoplifting, or assault of a law enforcement officer 
     offense, or any crime that results in death or serious bodily 
     injury to another person; or
       ``(F) is unlawfully present in the United States and who 
     has voted in a Federal election in violation of section 611 
     of title 18, United States Code,'';
       (2) by redesignating paragraph (2) as paragraph (4); and
       (3) by inserting after paragraph (1) the following:
       ``(2) Definitions.--For purposes of paragraph (1)(E) the 
     terms `burglary', `theft', `larceny', `shoplifting', `assault 
     of a law enforcement officer', and `serious bodily injury' 
     have the meanings given such terms in the jurisdiction in 
     which the acts occurred.
```
