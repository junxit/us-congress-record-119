# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, our creator, the father who knows us better than we know 
ourselves, who understands what we need better than what we may want, 
and who shows us what we should do when our shortsightedness meets 
uncertainty, we approach Your generous and immutable presence with both 
fear and faith, with doubt and hope.
  From Your heavenly throne, reveal Your love to us in these days. Set 
the world right and our country back on course, that we, who were made 
in Your image, would reflect the goodness and grace You desire for all 
of us.
  Give each one this day their daily bread, that none would be 
prevented by human hindrance or withheld by human pride.
  Forgive us the broken confidences among ourselves and the wounded 
trust in our sacred contract with our country's Constitution.
  Then call us to forgive those whom we think have wronged us, 
trespassing against our shared sense of moral and communal 
accountability.
  And deliver us each from the evil festering in our own hearts and 
from the intended and unintended malice of others.
  For in the face of all this--hunger and want, self-interest and self-
assumption--You alone are God. Deliver us from all that dares to 
undermine Your righteous grace plan.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
