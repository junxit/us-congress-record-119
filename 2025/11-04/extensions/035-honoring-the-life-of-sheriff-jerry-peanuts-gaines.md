# HONORING THE LIFE OF SHERIFF JERRY "PEANUTS" GAINES

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
         HONORING THE LIFE OF SHERIFF JERRY ``PEANUTS'' GAINES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of my dear 
friend, Sheriff Jerry ``Peanuts'' Gaines. Sheriff Gaines dedicated his 
life to serving the Warren County community for nearly half a century. 
Prior to his service in law enforcement, Sheriff Gaines served our 
Nation in the United States Army and as a Warren County Magistrate. In 
1978, Sheriff ``Peanuts'' Gaines was first elected as Warren County 
Sheriff, serving until 1982, and then was re-elected, serving from 1987 
to 2018. His 41 years of service to our county made him the longest 
serving sheriff in the history of Kentucky. He was a five-time 
President of the Kentucky Sheriffs' Association, the 1995 Sheriff of 
the Year, President of the National Sheriffs' Association and the only 
Kentucky sheriff to ever serve as President of the Kentucky Association 
of Counties. Sheriff Gaines was also a founding member of the Kentucky 
Sheriffs' Association's Boys & Girls Ranch, which has served children 
in every part of the Commonwealth.
  I offer my deepest condolences to the Gaines family, the Warren 
County Sheriff's Office, and to all who had the pleasure of knowing 
Sheriff Jerry ``Peanuts'' Gaines. His legacy will endure for 
generations to come. He will be remembered as a stranger to none. He 
exemplified his kindness and love for people daily. I thank him for 
selflessly answering the call to serve and devoting his life to making 
Warren County a better place. His service should inspire us all.

                          ____________________
```
