# HONORING RENNAE ROSS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
     HONORING RENNAE ROSS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Rennae Ross, 
recognized as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of 
the Year. Ms. Ross is a Marine Corps veteran, skilled tradeswoman, and 
tireless advocate for equality and inclusion within the labor movement. 
A proud member of Boilermakers Local 549, she has dedicated her career 
to advancing opportunities for women and underrepresented workers in 
the skilled trades. Rennae's journey began after completing the 
W.I.N.T.E.R. Program, known as Women in Non-Traditional Employment 
Roles, in Los Angeles. She later worked as a Petroleum Tank Field 
Fabricator before being organized into the Boilermakers through 
California's SB-54 ``Skilled and Trained Workforce'' legislation for 
high-hazard facilities. A graduate apprentice of Local 549, she now 
serves as an elected Trustee on the Executive Board and as the 
Chairperson of the Boilermakers Women at Work Committee. In these 
roles, she has become a mentor and role model, guiding members across 
trades and championing her vision to ``Bridge the Gap'' for equality on 
every job site. Rennae's passion for advocacy extends beyond her local 
union--she participates in all-female cohorts of MC3 pre-apprenticeship 
programs and outreach initiatives in correctional facilities, inspiring 
women to pursue meaningful, well-paying careers in the trades. Her 
leadership embodies the true spirit of solidarity: empowering others 
and fostering solidarity. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, I am honored to recognize Rennae Ross for her 
service to country and community, her exceptional leadership in 
organized labor, and her unwavering commitment to advancing equality in 
the workforce.
```
