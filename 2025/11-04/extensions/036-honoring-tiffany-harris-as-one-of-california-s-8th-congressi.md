# HONORING TIFFANY HARRIS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING TIFFANY HARRIS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Tiffany Harris as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. A proud Richmond native, Tiffany Harris has 
dedicated her career to empowering young people and strengthening her 
community. A former participant of the Bay Area Girls Club (BAGC), she 
returned to the organization as a leader in 2011 and has served as 
Executive Director since 2015. Under her leadership, BAGC has reached 
more than 7,000 girls aged five to eighteen through afterschool 
programs, mentoring, and enrichment activities that promote confidence 
and leadership. She has expanded the organization's reach by securing 
and distributing more than $100,000 in scholarships for college-bound 
girls and launching programs in STEM, leadership, and college and 
career readiness. BAGC's signature event, Men Who Cook, has become a 
beloved community tradition and a vital source of funding to sustain 
youth programs. With an 89 percent participant success rate in higher 
education or career pathways, BAGC continues to serve as a trusted 
community anchor and was recently named the 2025 Best Non-Profit for 
Activities and Fun by the Best of El Cerrito. Beyond her role at BAGC, 
Tiffany is an active civic leader throughout West Contra Costa County. 
She has served as Chair of the Richmond Chamber of Commerce, Program 
Chair of the Richmond Rotary Club, President of the Richmond Kiwanis 
Club, and Co-Treasurer of the West County Forum. She also serves on the 
Task Force for the Black Women and Girls Initiative with RCF Connects, 
continuing to champion equity, leadership, and opportunity for young 
women of color. A graduate of The Branson School and Holy Names 
University, where she earned her bachelor's degree in communications 
and English, Tiffany combines strong academic training with deep 
community roots. She is also a devoted wife and mother of four whose 
compassion and vision continue to inspire the youth she serves and the 
community she represents. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, I am honored to recognize Tiffany Harris for 
her extraordinary leadership, her dedication to uplifting the next 
generation, and her unwavering commitment to building a stronger, more 
equitable community.
```
