# HONORING RITA XAVIER AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
     HONORING RITA XAVIER AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Councilmember Rita 
Xavier, a dedicated public servant, community leader, former mayor, and 
longtime resident of the City of San Pablo, whose commitment to civic 
engagement, policy planning, and regional collaboration has left a 
lasting impact on the City of San Pablo as well as throughout the Bay 
Area. A proud resident of San Pablo for 35 years, Councilmember Xavier 
has devoted much of her life to advancing public service and improving 
the quality of life for local families. She has served on the San Pablo 
City Council for the past seven years, where she has been a strong 
advocate for economic development, neighborhood safety, and community 
partnerships that reflect the city's diverse and dynamic character. 
Before joining the Council, she served as a Planning Commissioner for 
the City of San Pablo, where she helped shape responsible growth and 
long-term planning initiatives. Her leadership has also extended across 
the region; she has held an appointment with the League of California 
Cities East Bay Division, served as Past President of the League of 
Women Voters of Contra Costa County, and as Board Treasurer for Lao 
Family Community Development, Inc., among other roles. Throughout her 
decades of service, Councilmember Xavier has exemplified integrity, 
vision, and compassion. Her work reflects a deep dedication to 
inclusive governance and to building stronger, more resilient 
communities for all. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, I am honored to recognize Councilmember Rita 
Xavier for her exemplary leadership, her unwavering dedication to 
public service, and her lifelong commitment to

[[Page E1035]]

strengthening the communities of San Pablo and the greater East Bay.

                          ____________________
```
