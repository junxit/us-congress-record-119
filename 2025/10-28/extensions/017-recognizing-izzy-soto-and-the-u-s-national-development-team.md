# RECOGNIZING IZZY SOTO AND THE U.S. NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT TEAM

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
      RECOGNIZING IZZY SOTO AND THE U.S. NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT TEAM

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 28, 2025

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Izzy Soto and her 
teammates on the United States National Development Team for winning a 
gold medal at the 2025 Pan American Inline Hockey Championships in 
Oaxtepec, Morelos, Mexico.
  Representing our Nation with pride and determination, the team 
demonstrated remarkable resilience by winning four consecutive games to 
capture the championship title. I am especially proud to note that Izzy 
is from Myrtle Beach and was the only member of the Women's Junior Team 
from South Carolina, making her achievement even more meaningful for 
our state and the Grand Strand community.
  Their accomplishment reflects the best of American spirit, teamwork, 
and perseverance. Competing at this level not only showcased their 
skill but also provided invaluable experience playing alongside and 
against some of the best athletes in the world.
  I commend Izzy, her teammates, and their coaches for their dedication 
and sportsmanship, and I extend my best wishes to all those continuing 
to compete in future tournaments. Their success brings great pride to 
the United States and serves as an inspiration to young athletes 
everywhere.

                          ____________________
```
