# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our Lord and our God, as the waters fill the sea, fill our Nation 
with people who know and love You.
  Today, guide our lawmakers so that they will live for Your honor, 
ensuring that our citizens will have a government of, by, and for the 
people. Lord, use our legislators to help end this government shutdown. 
Increase their faith, hope, and love, enabling them to transform 
cacophony into concord, doubt into faith, and falsehood into truth. May 
our Senators seek to become a voice for those who are suffering during 
these challenging times, until justice rolls down like waters and 
righteousness like a mighty stream.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
