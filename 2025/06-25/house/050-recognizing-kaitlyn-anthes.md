# RECOGNIZING KAITLYN ANTHES

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING KAITLYN ANTHES

  (Mr. VINDMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize my district's 
2025 Congressional Art Competition winner, Kaitlyn Anthes as my 
Constituent of the Week.
  Kaitlyn's charcoal drawing, titled To Light and Guard, will hang in 
the U.S. Capitol representing Virginia's Seventh District for the next 
year.
  Her artwork was so impressive that when we received her piece, my 
district director initially thought we had purchased professional 
artwork to hang in the office.
  In addition to being a talented artist, Kaitlyn recently graduated 
from Courtland High School in Spotsylvania.
  In Congress, I am working to strengthen public education so students 
like Kaitlyn have access to programs that allow them to pursue their 
passions.
  I congratulate Kaitlyn on this well-earned accomplishment and wish 
her luck as she heads off to college in the fall.

                          ____________________
```
