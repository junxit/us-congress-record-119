# HONORING BARBARA KULIG'S RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Pappas, Chris (P000614) [D-NH]

```
                  HONORING BARBARA KULIG'S RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHRIS PAPPAS

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 25, 2025

  Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the career of Barbara 
Kulig as she retires after twenty-six years of service with the 
Manchester School District. Barbara has dedicated her time as a 
Paraprofessional to making a difference in students' education and 
being involved within the community.
  As a lifelong Manchester resident, Barbara dedicated her career to 
public service and giving back to her community. She has spent twenty-
five years teaching at Hillside Middle School, the same institution she 
attended for middle school, and she spent her final year teaching at 
Weston Elementary. She also previously served as a school bus driver in 
Bedford. She has left a lasting impact on the students she has worked 
with over the years and will be greatly missed by students and staff 
alike.
  Barbara is not only a diligent public servant but also an individual 
of outstanding character and passion. She committed tireless days to 
ensuring that the students of the Manchester School District were given 
the care and resources they needed to thrive and grow into successful 
young adults.
  In all, Barbara Kulig represents the very best of America's public 
servants--dedicated, thoughtful, and unwavering in her commitment to 
service to others. On behalf of a grateful district, I want to 
congratulate Barbara on a well-deserved and restful retirement.

                          ____________________
```
