# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, use the hearts and hands of our lawmakers to lift 
burdens and liberate lives. Give our Senators the wisdom that will lead 
them to know what must be done to make a better nation and world. Open 
their ears to hear the cries of those on life's margins. Infuse them 
with courage to act by bringing relief and release to those who are 
bruised by life's storms.
  Lord, whisper words of counsel to our leaders, particularly during 
their moments of important decision making. And, Lord, help them to do 
the very best they can each day, leaving the results to You.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
