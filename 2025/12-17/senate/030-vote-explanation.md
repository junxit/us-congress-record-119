# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S. 1071

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, had I been present for the rollcall vote 
No. 648, on the motion to concur in the House amendment to S. 1071, the 
National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026, my vote would 
have been in the affirmative.
  My absence is due to my attendance at the dignified transfer ceremony 
at Dover Air Force Base to receive the remains of the two Iowa National 
Guard servicemembers who were killed in Syria this past week. I 
appreciate the opportunity to record my position.
  Had I been present for the rollcall vote No. 651, on the motion to 
invoke Cloture on the Nomination of Douglas Weaver, of Maryland, to be 
a member of the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, my vote would have been 
in the affirmative.
  My absence is due to my attendance at the dignified transfer ceremony 
at Dover Air Force Base to receive the remains of the two Iowa National 
Guard servicemembers who were killed in Syria this past week. I 
appreciate the opportunity to record my position.
```
