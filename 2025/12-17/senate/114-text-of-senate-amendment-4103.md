# Text of Senate Amendment 4103

**Speaking:** Lujan, Ben Ray (L000570) [D-NM]

```
  SA 4103. Mr. LUJAN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. AAMODT LITIGATION SETTLEMENT PROTECTION.

       Section 623(g) of the Aamodt Litigation Settlement Act 
     (Public Law 111-291; 124 Stat. 3153) is amended--
       (1) by striking ``The dates'' and inserting the following:
       ``(1) Conditions precedent; expiration date.--The dates''; 
     and
       (2) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(2) Determination of substantial completion.--The dates 
     in subsection (e) may be extended if the Pueblos, the United 
     States (acting through the Secretary), the State, the City, 
     and the County agree that an extension is reasonably 
     necessary.''.
                                 ______
```
