# Text of Senate Amendment 4082

**Speaking:** King, Angus S., Jr. (K000383) [I-ME]

```
  SA 4082. Mr. KING submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3951 submitted by Ms. Collins and intended to be proposed 
to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        On page 241, line 18, insert before the period the 
     following: ``: Provided, That not less than $1,000,000 shall 
     be for the collection, compilation, analysis, and publication 
     of data and statistics on the costs to importers of a change 
     in the duty owed for an item that occurs while such item is 
     in transit to the United States''.
                                 ______
```
