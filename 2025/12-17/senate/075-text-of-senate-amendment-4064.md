# Text of Senate Amendment 4064

**Speaking:** Warnock, Raphael G. (W000790) [D-GA]

```
  SA 4064. Mr. WARNOCK submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 3951 submitted by Ms. Collins and intended to be 
proposed to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, 
and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place in division D, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. __.  There is appropriated an additional $100,000,000 
     under the heading ``Department of Health and Human Services--
     Centers for Disease Control and Prevention--Buildings and 
     Facilities'', for purposes of maintaining and improving 
     security on the Roybal campus of the Centers for Disease 
     Control and Prevention in Atlanta, Georgia, and making 
     repairs to buildings on such campus.
                                 ______
```
