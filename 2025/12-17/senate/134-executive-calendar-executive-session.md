                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Ms. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations en 
bloc: Calendar No. 581, Calendar Nos. 602 through 607, and all 
nominations on the Secretary's desk, with the exception of PN89; that 
the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate; that no further motions be in order to any of the nominations; 
that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action; and 
that the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
