# Text of Senate Amendment 4098

**Speaking:** Hickenlooper, John W. (H000273) [D-CO]

```
  SA 4098. Mr. HICKENLOOPER submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, 
and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. PROHIBITION OF IMPLEMENTATION OF RULE.

       None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made available 
     by this Act may be used to finalize, issue, or implement the 
     proposed rule of the Environmental Protection Agency entitled 
     ``Reconsideration of 2009 Endangerment Finding and Greenhouse 
     Gas Vehicle Standards'' (90 Fed. Reg. 36288 (August 1, 
     2025)).
                                 ______
```
