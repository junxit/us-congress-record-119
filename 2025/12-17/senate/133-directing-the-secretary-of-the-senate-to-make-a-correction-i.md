# DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE TO MAKE A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF THE BILL S. 1071

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 66, S. 1071

```
   DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE TO MAKE A CORRECTION IN THE 
                     ENROLLMENT OF THE BILL S. 1071

  Ms. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 66, which was 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 66) directing the 
     Secretary of the Senate to make a correction in the 
     enrollment of the bill S. 1071.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Ms. MURKOWSKI. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution 
be agreed to and that the motion to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 66) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
