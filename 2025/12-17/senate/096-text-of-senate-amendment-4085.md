# Text of Senate Amendment 4085

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
  SA 4085. Mrs. SHAHEEN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 3951 submitted by Ms. Collins and intended to be 
proposed to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, 
and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 178, line 25, strike ``Labor.'' and insert the 
     following: ``Labor: Provided further, That the Bureau of 
     Prisons shall use amounts made available under this heading 
     for the deployment in prison mail-receiving facilities of 
     advanced, portable screening

[[Page S8854]]

     technology, equipped with terahertz scanning capabilities to 
     detect dangerous powders, liquids, drug-laced papers, and 
     other contraband, including fentanyl, that pose a threat to 
     the safety of facility personnel and inmates and shall ensure 
     that such equipment is used in a manner that preserves the 
     confidentiality of legal and attorney-client privileged 
     mail.''
                                 ______
```
