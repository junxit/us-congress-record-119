# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3481, H.R. 3633

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3481. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to provide for 
     electronic communication relating to educational assistance 
     benefits under the laws administered by the Secretary, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       H.R. 3633. An act to provide for a system of regulation of 
     the offer and sale of digital commodities by the Securities 
     and Exchange Commission, and the Commodity Futures Trading 
     Commission, to amend the Federal Reserve Act to prohibit the 
     Federal reserve banks from offering certain products or 
     services directly to an individual, to prohibit the use of 
     central bank digital currency for monetary policy, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
     Urban Affairs.
```
