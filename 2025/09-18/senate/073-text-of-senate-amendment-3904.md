# Text of Senate Amendment 3904

**Speaking:** Lujan, Ben Ray (L000570) [D-NM]

```
  SA 3904. Mr. LUJAN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3748 proposed by Mr. Wicker (for himself and Mr. Reed) to 
the bill S. 2296, to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2026 for 
military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, 
to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in subtitle F of title X, insert 
     the following:

     SEC. 10__. NORTHWESTERN NEW MEXICO RURAL WATER PROJECTS ACT.

       (a) Project Contracts.--Section 10604(b)(3)(B) of the 
     Northwestern New Mexico Rural Water Projects Act (Public Law 
     111-11; 123 Stat. 1389) is amended by striking ``shall be at 
     least'' and all that follows through the period at the end 
     and inserting ``shall not exceed $76,000,000.''.
       (b) Authorization of Appropriations.--Section 10609(a)(1) 
     of the Northwestern New Mexico Rural Water Projects Act 
     (Public Law 111-11; 123 Stat. 1395) is amended--
       (1) by striking ``$870,000,000'' and inserting 
     ``$1,815,000,000''; and
       (2) by striking ``2024'' and inserting ``2026''.
                                 ______
```
