# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 164

**Speaking:** Luna, Anna Paulina (L000596) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 164

```
  NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES 
           FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 164

  Mrs. LUNA. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, I rise to 
provide notice of my intent to offer a motion to discharge the 
Committee on Rules from further consideration of House Resolution 164.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Obernolte). Did the gentlewoman sign the 
petition?
  Mrs. LUNA. I did, Mr. Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, a motion 
to discharge is privileged only at a time designated by the Speaker 
within 2 legislative days after the intent to offer such motion is 
offered.
  The Chair will entertain the gentlewoman's motion at that time.

                          ____________________
```
