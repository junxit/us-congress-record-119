# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, on this day of pranks and lighthearted fun, April fools 
that we are, may we not be so foolish that we overlook Your instruction 
and reject Your wisdom.
  As we pull pranks and tease one another, may we not be so laughable 
that our silliness drowns out the voice of Your wise counsel.
  Quick to wield our cleverness, may we not be so convinced of our own 
rectitude that we fail to see that what appears to be Your foolish plan 
is wiser than our own perceived insight.
  Grant us the joy of laughter, as well as the contentedness of hearts 
grounded in You. Let us delight in a day of slaphappiness, as well as 
the joy to be found in the wisdom You reveal to us every day.
  For Your love for us is far more than a whim, but Your faithfulness 
endures in the craziest and best of times. We are all the happier for 
it.
  In Your wise name, we pray.
  Amen.
```
