# CONGRATULATING RALPH HUTCHINSON ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
           CONGRATULATING RALPH HUTCHINSON ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 28, 2025

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate East Lampeter 
Township Manager Ralph Hutchinson on his retirement.
  Mr. Hutchinson's career in public service spans four decades. After 
graduating from Lafayette College and Penn State's Capitol Campus, 
known today as Penn State Harrisburg, Mr. Hutchinson worked as 
Executive Secretary for the Borough of Fountain Hill in Lehigh County, 
as well as Borough Manager for Millersville Borough in Lancaster 
County. He spent the last 34 years as Township Manager for East 
Lampeter Township in Lancaster County.
  Mr. Hutchinson has witnessed the township grow significantly over the 
past three decades, and has worked to balance the needs of commercial 
enterprises and farming communities along the bustling Route 30 
Corridor. Mr. Hutchinson has lent his experience and expertise in 
municipal management to numerous boards and committees.
  As he transitions into a well-deserved retirement, the community both 
thanks and honors Ralph Hutchinson for his years in public service.
```
