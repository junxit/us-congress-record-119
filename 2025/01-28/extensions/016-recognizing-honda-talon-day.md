# RECOGNIZING HONDA TALON DAY

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
                      RECOGNIZING HONDA TALON DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 28, 2025

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, Wednesday, January 29, has been recognized as 
Honda Talon Day in South Carolina.
  Governor McMaster declared this day of official celebration in 
response to the Honda Talon being named ``Coolest Thing Made in South 
Carolina'' by the South Carolina Manufacturers Alliance.
  The Honda product stood out against 166 other nominated products with 
class-leading technology and numerous victories in racing competitions.
  Since 1998, Timmonsville has been the exclusive home of Honda side-
by-side vehicle manufacturing, where it has produced nearly 4 million 
units and currently employs over 1,000 South Carolinians--I'm looking 
forward to the future of Honda manufacturing here in South Carolina's 
Seventh District.

                          ____________________
```
