# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                 January 24, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Pursuant to Section 4(c) of House of 
     Resolution 5, 119th Congress, I am pleased to appoint the 
     following Member to the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission to 
     serve as the Democratic Co-chairman:
       The Honorable James P. McGovern of Massachusetts.
       Thank you for your attention to this matter.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
