# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

**Measures:** H.Res. 5

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                 January 24, 2024.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Pursuant to Section 4(b) of House 
     Resolution 5, 119th Congress, I am writing to appoint the 
     following members to the House Democracy Partnership:
       The Honorable Dina Titus of Nevada, Ranking Member.
       The Honorable Gwen Moore of Wisconsin.
       The Honorable Ted Lieu of California.
       The Honorable Norma Torres of California.
       The Honorable Robin Kelly of Illinois.
       The Honorable Terri Sewell of Alabama.
       The Honorable Diana DeGette of Colorado.
       The Honorable Ilhan Omar of Minnesota.
       The Honorable Delia Ramirez of Illinois.
       Thank you for your attention to these appointments.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
