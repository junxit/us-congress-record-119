# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Where, O Lord, do we find insight when all around us, the competing 
narratives muddle the path toward discernment? Voices cry out for 
understanding, searching as if for hidden treasure and longing to find 
the wisdom needed to meet today's challenges.
  You alone, O God, give wisdom; from Your mouth only come knowledge 
and understanding.
  Teach us to listen for the Word You speak, that we will learn what is 
right, just, and fair.
  Open our eyes to perceive the path of blamelessness. Remind us that 
You set before us guardrails to keep us safe if we are honest and to 
protect us when we are faithful.
  Incline our hearts toward discretion. Lead us on straight paths, and 
guard us against our own willfulness.
  In You alone may we seek wisdom. In You only will we find insight.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
