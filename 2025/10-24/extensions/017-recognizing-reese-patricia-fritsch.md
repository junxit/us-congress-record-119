# RECOGNIZING REESE PATRICIA FRITSCH

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                   RECOGNIZING REESE PATRICIA FRITSCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 2025

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the birth of 
Reese Patricia Fritsch.
  Reese was born on October 7, 2025 at 2:45 P.M. at Luminis Health Anne 
Arundel Medical Center weighing seven pounds, two ounces and measuring 
21 inches long to loving parents Taylor and Kristin Flukey Fritsch, who 
serves as a valuable member of my team on the Subcommittee on Health 
for the House Committee on Energy and Commerce.
  I'm grateful to have Kristin as a part of the Energy and Commerce 
family, and I'm excited for her and Taylor to start their family with 
their newest addition.

                          ____________________
```
