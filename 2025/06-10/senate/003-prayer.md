# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O mighty God, teach us the mystery of life. Help us not to be victims 
but victorious in the living of our days. Lord, lead us to a place of 
understanding in spite of sorrow, pain, and setbacks. Make us more than 
conquerors because You love us. Today, instruct our lawmakers as they 
seek to be guided by honesty. May they perform their daily tasks 
following Your priorities. Show them Your truth so that they will be 
instruments of Your purposes when their light of hope is threatened. 
Renew them with faith in Your providence and power. We pray in Your 
omniscient Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
