       S. Res. 269. A resolution recognizing the 250th birthday of 
     the United States Army; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. SCOTT of Florida (for himself, Mr. Markey, Mr. 
             Justice, Ms. Cantwell, Mrs. Moody, Mr. Whitehouse,
