# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 1136, S. 1809

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine the nominations of Brian Quintenz, of Ohio, to be 
a Commissioner and Chairman of the Commodity Futures Trading 
Commission, after the nominee testified and answered questions in his 
own behalf.
APPROPRIATIONS: NIH
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Departments of Labor, 
Health and Human Services, and Education, and Related Agencies 
concluded a hearing to examine proposed budget estimates for fiscal 
year 2026 for the National Institutes of Health, after receiving 
testimony from Jay Bhattacharya, Director, National Institutes of 
Health, Department of Health and Human Services.
DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION REQUEST AND FUTURE YEARS DEFENSE PROGRAM
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded open and closed 
hearings to examine the posture of the Department of the Navy in review 
of the Defense Authorization Request for Fiscal Year 2026 and the 
Future Years Defense Program, after receiving testimony from John C. 
Phelan, Secretary of the Navy, Admiral James W. Kilby, USN, Vice Chief 
of Naval Operations, and General Eric M. Smith, USMC, Commandant of the 
Marine Corps, all of the Department of Defense.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of Jacob Helberg, of Florida, to be an Under 
Secretary for Economic Growth, Energy, and the Environment, who was 
introduced by Senator Hagerty and Senator Scott (FL), Andrew Puzder, of 
Tennessee, to be Representative of the United States of America to the 
European Union, with the rank of Ambassador, who was introduced by 
Senator Hagerty, Paul Kapur, of California, to be Assistant Secretary 
for South Asian Affairs, and Howard Brodie, of Florida, to be 
Ambassador to the Republic of Finland, who was introduced by Senator 
Cruz, all of the Department of State, and Benjamin Black, of New York, 
to be Chief Executive Officer of the United States International 
Development Finance Corporation, who was introduced by Senator Cotton, 
after the nominees testified and answered questions in their own 
behalf.
BORDER MANAGEMENT AND PERSONNEL READINESS FOR THE DECADE OF SPORTS
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Subcommittee 
on Border Management, Federal Workforce, and Regulatory Affairs 
concluded an oversight hearing to examine the Federal Government's 
border management and personnel readiness efforts for the decade of 
sports, including S. 1809, to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
prohibit taking or transmitting video of defense information, and 
S.1136, to authorize sentencing enhancements for certain criminal 
offenses directed by or coordinated with foreign governments, after 
receiving testimony from Reynold Hoover, LA28 Olympics and Paralympic 
Games, Los Angeles, California; Shawn Kinder, Ferrovial Airports 
International, Austin, Texas; and Gina Ligon, University of Nebraska 
National Counterterrorism Innovation, Technology, and Education Center, 
Omaha.

INTELLIGENCE

intelligence community.
```
