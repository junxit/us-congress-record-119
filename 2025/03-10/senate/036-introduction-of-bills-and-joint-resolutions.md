              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the

           By Mr. GALLEGO (for himself and Mr. Kelly):

     Resources.

     establish a pilot program to implement a pre-qualification or

     Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.

     benefits program; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

     dependency on critical agricultural products or inputs from
