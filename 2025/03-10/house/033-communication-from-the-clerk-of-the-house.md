               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, March 6, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.

     Honorable David Lyle Boren, former United States Senator from 
     the State of Oklahoma S. Res. 115.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                Kevin F. McCumber,
