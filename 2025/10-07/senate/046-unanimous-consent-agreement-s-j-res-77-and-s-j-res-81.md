# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S.J. Res. 77 and S.J. Res. 81

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 77, S.J.Res. 81

```
       UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S.J. Res. 77 and S.J. Res. 81

  Ms. LUMMIS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, at a time to be determined by the majority 
leader, following consultation with the Democratic leader, no later 
than Friday, October 31, it be in order to discharge the Committee on 
Finance of S.J. Res. 77 and S.J. Res. 81, individually, and when 
discharged, the Senate proceed to its consideration; further, that 
there be up to 6 hours for debate only on each joint resolution, with 
the time equally divided between the leaders or their designees; and 
that following the use or yielding back of that time, each joint 
resolution be considered read a third time and the Senate vote on the 
resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
