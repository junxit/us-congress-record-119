  The following joint resolutions were discharged from the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources, by petition, pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 802(c), 
and placed on the calendar:

       S.J. Res. 61. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management 
     relating to ``Miles City Field Office Record of Decision and 
     Approved Resource Management Plan Amendment''.
       S.J. Res. 62. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management 
     relating to ``North Dakota Field Office Record of Decision 
     and Approved Resource Management Plan''.
       S.J. Res. 63. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management 
     relating to ``Central Yukon Record of Decision and Approved 
     Resource Management Plan''.
