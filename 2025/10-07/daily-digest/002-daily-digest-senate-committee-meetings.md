# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the nominations of John Noh, of Texas, to be an Assistant Secretary, 
Charles Young III, of West Virginia, to be General Counsel of the 
Department of the Army, and William Lane III, of Virginia, to be 
General Counsel of the Department of the Air Force, who was introduced 
by Senator Blackburn, all of the Department of Defense, and David Beck, 
of Tennessee, to be Deputy Administrator for Defense Programs, National 
Nuclear Security Administration, Department of Energy, after the 
nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
DOD
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and 
Capabilities concluded a hearing to examine on threats and challenges 
posed to Department of Defense personnel and operations from 
adversarial access to publicly available data coupled

[[Page D1014]]

with advanced data analysis tools now widely available on the 
commercial market, after receiving testimony from Joseph W. Kirschbaum, 
Director, Defense Capabilities and Management, Government 
Accountability Office; Justin Sherman, Global Cyber Strategies; John 
Doyle, Cape; and Michael Stokes, Ridgeline International.
INDO-PACIFIC
Committee on Foreign Relations: Subcommittee on East Asia, the Pacific, 
and International Cybersecurity Policy concluded a hearing to examine 
combatting the People's Republic of China's behavior in the Indo-
Pacific, after receiving testimony from Craig Singleton, Foundation for 
Defense of Democracies, and Ely Ratner, The Marathon Initiative, both 
of Washington, D.C.; and Raymond M. Powell, SeaLight Foundation, 
Stanford, California.
DOJ OVERSIGHT
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded an oversight hearing to 
examine the Department of Justice, after receiving testimony from 
Pamela Bondi, Attorney General, Department of Justice.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
