# CELEBRATING EDUCATION AND SHARING DAY IN HONOR OF THE REBBE, RABBI MENACHEM MENDEL SCHNEERSON

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
  CELEBRATING EDUCATION AND SHARING DAY IN HONOR OF THE REBBE, RABBI 
                       MENACHEM MENDEL SCHNEERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 14, 2025

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, known as the 
Rebbe, a spiritual leader whose impact on faith, education, and 
morality continues to inspire millions.
  Born in 1902, the Rebbe endured the horrors of Russian communism and 
evils of Nazi Germany before arriving in the United States

[[Page E318]]

in 1941. From here, he led efforts to rebuild Jewish life, promoting 
values of kindness, education, and service to others.
  He sent rabbinical students across the country, including to South 
Dakota, to reach Jews in isolated communities. His vision led to the 
Chabad-Lubavitch movement becoming the world's largest Jewish 
educational organization, now active in all 50 states and 109 
countries.
  The Rebbe believed education must include ethics and character, not 
just academics. His message resonated far beyond his faith, earning him 
the Congressional Gold Medal and recognition through Education and 
Sharing Day, proclaimed annually by U.S. Presidents since 1978.
  Education and Sharing Day represents an excellent opportunity for us 
to reflect on the Rebbe's vision and leadership. May we carry forward 
the Rebbe's legacy by choosing kindness, living with purpose, and 
working toward a more compassionate world.
```
