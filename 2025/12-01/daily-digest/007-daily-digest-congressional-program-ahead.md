                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of December 2 through December 5, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, After the transaction of any morning business (not to 
extend beyond 11:30 a.m.), Senate will vote on confirmation of the 
nomination of David A. Bragdon, of North Carolina, to be United States 
District Judge for the Middle District of North Carolina.
  Following disposition of the nomination of David A. Bragdon, Senate 
will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Lindsey 
Ann Freeman, of North Carolina, to be United States District Judge for 
the Middle District of North Carolina. If cloture is invoked on the 
nomination, Senate will vote on confirmation thereon at 2:15 p.m.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
