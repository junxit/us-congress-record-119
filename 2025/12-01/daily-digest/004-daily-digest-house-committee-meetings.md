# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1005, H.R. 1049, H.R. 1069, H.R. 2965, H.R. 4305, H.R. 4312

```
Committee Meetings
SCORE ACT; COMBATING THE LIES OF AUTHORITARIANS IN SCHOOL SYSTEMS ACT; 
TRANSPARENCY IN REPORTING OF ADVERSARIAL CONTRIBUTIONS TO EDUCATION 
ACT; PROTECT OUR KIDS ACT; REGULATORY REDUCTION ACT OF 2025; DUMP RED 
TAPE ACT
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 4312, the 
``SCORE Act''; H.R. 1005, the ``Combating the Lies of Authoritarians in 
School Systems Act''; H.R. 1049, the ``Transparency in Reporting of 
Adversarial Contributions to Education Act''; H.R. 1069, the ``PROTECT 
Our Kids Act''; H.R. 2965, the ``Regulatory Reduction Act of 2025''; 
and H.R. 4305, the ``DUMP Red Tape Act''. The Committee granted, by a 
record vote of 7-4, a rule providing for consideration of H.R. 4312, 
the ``SCORE Act'', H.R. 1005, the ``Combating the Lies of 
Authoritarians in School Systems Act'', H.R. 1049, the ``Transparency 
in Reporting of Adversarial Contributions to Education Act'', H.R. 
1069, the ``PROTECT Our Kids Act'', H.R. 2965, the ``Small Business 
Regulatory Reduction Act of 2025'', and H.R. 4305, the ``DUMP Red Tape 
Act''. The rule provides for consideration of H.R. 4312, the ``SCORE 
Act'', under a closed rule. The rule waives all points of order against 
consideration of the bill. The rule provides that, in lieu of the 
amendments in the nature of a substitute recommended by the Committees 
on Education and Workforce and Energy and Commerce now printed in the 
bill, an amendment in the nature of a substitute consisting of the text 
of Rules Committee Print 119-14 shall be considered as adopted and the 
bill, as amended, shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the bill, as amended. The rule 
provides one hour of general debate equally divided among and 
controlled by the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on 
Education and Workforce or their respective designees and the chair and 
ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and Commerce or 
their respective designees. The rule provides one motion to recommit. 
The rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 1005, the 
``Combating the Lies of Authoritarians in School Systems Act'', H.R. 
1049, the ``Transparency in Reporting of Adversarial Contributions to 
Education Act'', and H.R. 1069, the ``PROTECT Our Kids Act'', under 
closed rules. The rule waives all points of order against consideration 
of each such bill. The rule provides that the respective amendments in 
the nature of a substitute recommended by the Committee on Education 
and Workforce now printed in each such bill shall be considered as 
adopted and each such bill, as amended, shall be considered as read. 
The rule waives all points of order against provisions in each such 
bill, as amended. The rule provides one hour of general debate on each 
such bill equally divided and controlled by the chair and ranking 
minority member of the Committee on Education and Workforce or their 
respective designees. The rule provides each such bill one motion to 
recommit. The rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 2965, the 
``Small Business Regulatory Reduction Act of 2025'', under a closed 
rule. The rule waives all points of order against consideration of the 
bill. The rule provides that the amendment in the nature of a 
substitute recommended by the Committee on Small Business now printed 
in the bill shall be considered as adopted and the bill, as amended, 
shall be considered as read. The rule waives all points of order 
against provisions in the bill, as amended. The rule provides one hour 
of general debate equally divided and controlled by the chair and 
ranking minority member of the Committee on Small Business or their 
respective designees. The rule provides one motion to recommit. The 
rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 4305, the ``DUMP Red 
Tape Act'', under a closed rule. The rule waives all points of order 
against consideration of the bill. The rule provides that the amendment 
in the nature of a substitute recommended by the Committee on Small 
Business now printed in the bill shall be considered as adopted and the 
bill, as amended, shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the bill, as amended. The rule 
provides one hour of general debate equally divided and controlled by 
the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on Small 
Business or their respective designees. Finally, the rule provides one 
motion to recommit. Testimony was heard from Chairman Van Duyne, 
Chairman Walberg, and Representatives Bonamici, Bilirakis, Pallone, 
Bonamici, Velazquez, Baumgartner, and Trahan.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
