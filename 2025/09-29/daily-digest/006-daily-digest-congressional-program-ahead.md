# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 2296, S. 2882, H.R. 5371

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of September 30 through October 3, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of S. 2296, National 
Defense Authorization Act. Senators should expect roll call votes on or 
in relation to S. 2882, Continuing Appropriations and Extensions and 
Other Matters Act, and H.R. 5371, Continuing Appropriations and 
Extensions Act.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
```
