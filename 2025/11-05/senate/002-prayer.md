# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, thank You for not leaving us alone in a challenging 
world. You remain our refuge in every storm. As our lawmakers commit 
this day to You, help them to navigate through the turbulence of this 
government shutdown.
  May Your spirit control their minds, guiding them by the light of 
Your truth. Lord, inspire them to live worthy of the price You paid for 
their redemption, making them humble, patient, and kind. Be the 
resource they need to be faithful stewards of Your unfolding 
providence.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
