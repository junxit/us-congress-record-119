# SCHOOL BUS SAFETY MONTH

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 484

```
                        SCHOOL BUS SAFETY MONTH

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 484, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 484) designating October 2025 as 
     ``School Bus Safety Month''.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. BARRASSO. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed 
to, the preamble be agreed to, and that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 484) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
