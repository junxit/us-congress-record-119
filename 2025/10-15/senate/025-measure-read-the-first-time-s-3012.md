# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3012

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 3012

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3012

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3012) to appropriate funds for pay and 
     allowances of excepted Federal employees for periods of work 
     performed during a lapse in appropriations, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule 
XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
