# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 16, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 4016, H.R. 5371

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 16, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Thursday, October 16; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motions, with respect to the 
motion to proceed to Calendar No. 168, H.R. 5371, and Executive 
Calendar No. 460, ripen at 11 a.m., and if cloture is invoked on the 
Mooty nomination, all postcloture time be considered expired and the 
Senate vote on confirmation at a time to be determined by the majority 
leader, in consultation with the Democratic leader, no earlier than 
Monday, October 20; and, finally, that the cloture motion, with respect 
to the motion to proceed to Calendar No. 136, H.R. 4016, ripen at 1:30 
p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. For the information of my colleagues, Senators should 
expect two rollcall votes at 11 a.m. and a further vote at 1:30 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
