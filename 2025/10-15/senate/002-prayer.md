# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Merciful God, enthroned far above all other earthly powers, help us 
to put our complete trust in You.
  Mighty God, You have the whole world in Your hands. As we wrestle 
with the gridlock of this government shutdown, inspire our lawmakers to 
do what is best for our Nation and world. Remind them that Your power 
is far above any conceivable command, authority, or control.
  Lord, help them to appreciate their accountability to You as You 
guide them to contribute to unity and finding common ground. Be near to 
those who are the collateral damage of this impasse, supporting them 
with Your wisdom, power, mercy, and grace.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
