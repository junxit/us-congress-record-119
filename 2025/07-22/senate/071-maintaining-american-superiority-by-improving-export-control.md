# MAINTAINING AMERICAN SUPERIORITY BY IMPROVING EXPORT CONTROL TRANSPARENCY ACT

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1316

```
     MAINTAINING AMERICAN SUPERIORITY BY IMPROVING EXPORT CONTROL 
                            TRANSPARENCY ACT

  Mr. HUSTED. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs be discharged from further 
consideration of H.R. 1316 and the Senate proceed to its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1316) to amend the Export Control Reform Act 
     of 2018 relating to licensing transparency.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. HUSTED. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read 
a third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1316) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
