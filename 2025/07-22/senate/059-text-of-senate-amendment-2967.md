# Text of Senate Amendment 2967

**Speaking:** Hawley, Josh (H001089) [R-MO]

```
  SA 2967. Mr. HAWLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3944, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 102, strike lines 21 and 22 and insert the 
     following ``$328,558,000, to remain available until September 
     30, 2030: Provided, That $100,000,000 is used for a Military 
     Housing Privatization Initiative equity investment to replace 
     aging family housing of the Army at Fort Leonard Wood, 
     Missouri.''.
                                 ______
```
