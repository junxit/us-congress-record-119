# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, JUNE 28, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                   ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, JUNE 28, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on 
Saturday, June 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
