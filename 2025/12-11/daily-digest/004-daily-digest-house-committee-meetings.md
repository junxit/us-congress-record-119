Committee Meetings
COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION REAUTHORIZATION: STAKEHOLDER

Committee on Agriculture: Full Committee held a hearing entitled ``CFTC 
Reauthorization: Stakeholder Perspectives''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.

in-state tuition rates for certain residents of Guam, the Commonwealth

RIGHT-SIZING THE U.S. BANK CAPITAL FRAMEWORK: A RETURN TO TAILORING, 
ECONOMIC GROWTH, AND COMPETITIVENESS
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Financial Institutions 
held a hearing entitled ``Right-Sizing the U.S. Bank Capital Framework: 
A Return to Tailoring, Economic Growth, and Competitiveness''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
MEMBER DAY
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Member Day''. Testimony was heard from Representatives LaLota, Smith 
of New Jersey, and Lois Frankel of Florida.
STOPPING THE BLOODSHED: U.S. RESPONSE TO CRIMES AGAINST HUMANITY IN 
SUDAN
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Africa Subcommittee held a hearing 
entitled ``Stopping the Bloodshed: U.S. Response to Crimes Against 
Humanity in Sudan''. Testimony was heard from Vincent D. Spera, Deputy 
Assistant Secretary, Bureau of African Affairs, Department of State; 
and public witnesses.
WORLDWIDE THREATS TO THE HOMELAND
Committee on Homeland Security: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Worldwide Threats to the Homeland''. Testimony was heard from Kristi 
Noem, Secretary, Department of Homeland Security; Michael Glasheen, 
Operations Director, National Security Branch, Federal Bureau of 
Investigation; and Joe Kent, Director, National Counterterrorism 
Center, Office of the Director of National Intelligence.
OVERSIGHT OF THE FOREIGN INTELLIGENCE SURVEILLANCE ACT
Committee on the Judiciary: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Oversight of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
MODERNIZING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF 638 CONTRACTING AT THE INDIAN HEALTH 
SERVICE
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Indian and Insular 
Affairs held a hearing entitled ``Modernizing the Implementation of 638 
Contracting at the Indian Health Service''. Testimony was heard from 
Benjamin Smith, Deputy Director, Department of Health and Human 
Services; and public witnesses.
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Federal Lands held a 
hearing on H.R. 3924, the ``Wildfire Risk Evaluation Act''; H.R. 5095, 
the ``HOMEFRONT Act of 2025''; H.R. 5419, the ``Enhancing 
Administrative Reviews for Broadband Deployment Act''; H.R. 5729, the 
``North Rim Restoration Act of 2025''; H.R. 6365, the ``Wintergreen 
Emergency Egress Act''; H.R. 6380, the ``Chiricahua National Park 
Act''; and legislation on the American Products in Parks Act''. 
Testimony was heard from Representatives Neguse, Ciscomani, Crane, 
Kean, McGuire and Patronis; Greg Hancock, Mayor, Willcox, Arizona; 
Curtis Sheets, Chief, Wintergreen Fire and Rescue, Nellysford, 
Virginia; Brad White, Chief, Grand Fire Protection District No. 1, 
Granby, Colorado; and public witnesses.

CUTTING COSTS, ADDING VALUE: THE FUTURE OF FEDERAL PROPERTY
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Subcommittee on 
Economic Development, Public Buildings, and Emergency Management held a

Heather Krause, Managing Director, Physical Infrastructure, Government 
Accountability Office; and Michael E. Capuano, Member, Public Buildings 
Reform Board.
