# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 593, Jared Isaacman, of 
     Pennsylvania, to be Administrator of the National Aeronautics 
     and Space Administration.
         John Thune, John R. Curtis, Tim Sheehy, Roger F. Wicker, 
           Joni Ernst, Markwayne Mullin, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Pete 
           Ricketts, John Boozman, Lindsey Graham, John Barrasso, 
           Dan Sullivan, Steve Daines, Tom Cotton, Ted Cruz, John 
           Kennedy, Deb Fischer.

           

                          ____________________
```
