# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 541, S.Res. 542, S.Res. 543, S.Res. 544, S.Res. 545

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. WELCH (for himself and Mr. Sanders):
       S. Res. 541. A resolution congratulating Vermont Green 
     Football Club on winning the United Soccer League Two 
     National Championship; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, 
     and Transportation.
           By Ms. HIRONO (for herself, Ms. Baldwin, Ms. Duckworth, 
             Mr. Kim, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Markey, Mr. Padilla, Ms. 
             Rosen, Mr. Schatz, Mr. Schiff, Mr. Van Hollen, and 
             Mr. Wyden):
       S. Res. 542. A resolution commemorating the 50th 
     anniversary of Southeast Asian refugee resettlement and the 
     many contributions and sacrifices of Southeast Asian 
     Americans to the United States; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Mr. KENNEDY (for himself and Mr. Cassidy):
       S. Res. 543. A resolution commending Centenary College of 
     Louisiana on the occasion of its bicentennial and its years 
     of service to the State of Louisiana and the United States; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Ms. COLLINS (for herself and Mr. King):
       S. Res. 544. A resolution designating December 13, 2025, as 
     ``National Wreaths Across America Day''; considered and 
     agreed to.
           By Mr. JUSTICE (for himself, Mr. Gallego, Mr. Daines, 
             Mr. Hoeven, Mrs. Capito, Mr. Kelly, Mr. King, Mr. 
             Lee, Mr. Kaine, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Risch, Ms. Cortez 
             Masto, Mr. Fetterman, Mr. Hickenlooper, Mr. Sheehy, 
             Ms. Lummis, Ms. Smith, Mr. McConnell, and Mr. 
             Barrasso):
       S. Res. 545. A resolution designating December 6, 2025, as 
     ``National Miners Day''; considered and agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
