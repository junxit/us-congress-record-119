# Text of Senate Amendment 3971

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
  SA 3971. Mr. THUNE (for Ms. Murkowski (for herself and Mr. Schatz)) 
proposed an amendment to the bill S. 640, to amend the Omnibus Public 
Land Management Act of 2009 to make a technical correction to the 
Navajo Nation Water Resources Development Trust Fund, to amend the 
Claims Resolution Act of 2010 to make technical corrections to the Taos 
Pueblo Water Development Fund and Aamodt Settlement Pueblos' Fund, and 
for other purposes; as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

     SEC. 6. INVESTMENT EARNINGS.

       In addition to the deposits authorized under this Act, any 
     investment earnings, including interest, credited to amounts 
     held in the trust funds as provided for in this Act are 
     authorized to be appropriated.
```
