# WOUNDED KNEE MASSACRE MEMORIAL AND SACRED SITE ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 165

```
           WOUNDED KNEE MASSACRE MEMORIAL AND SACRED SITE ACT

       A bill (H.R. 165) to direct the Secretary of the Interior 
     to complete all actions necessary for certain land to be held 
     in restricted fee status by the Oglala Sioux Tribe and 
     Cheyenne River Sioux Tribe, and for other purposes, was 
     ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and 
     passed.
```
