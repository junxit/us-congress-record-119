# MICCOSUKEE RESERVED AREA AMENDMENTS ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 504

```
                MICCOSUKEE RESERVED AREA AMENDMENTS ACT

       A bill (H.R. 504) to amend the Miccosukee Reserved Area Act 
     to authorize the expansion of the Miccosukee Reserved Area 
     and to carry out activities to protect structures within the 
     Osceola Camp from flooding, and for other purposes, was 
     ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and 
     passed.
```
