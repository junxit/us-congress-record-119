# RECOGNIZING THE TIFFIN CALVERT CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE DIVISION VII STATE GIRLS VOLLEYBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE TIFFIN CALVERT CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE 
            DIVISION VII STATE GIRLS VOLLEYBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I rise 
to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school volleyball 
team in Ohio's 5th Congressional District. The young women of the 
Tiffin Calvert Catholic High School girls volleyball team have 
represented their school ably on their way to achieving the Division 
VII State Girls Volleyball Title. In their effort to surpass all other 
teams in the Division VII State Volleyball Playoffs, the Calvert 
Senecas overcame the challenges posed by intense competition.
  In pursuing the State Championship, Tiffin Calvert defeated the 
Monroeville Eagles to win the state volleyball championship. The 
members of this very special team have shown that their sport requires 
an individual effort for a team result. Their hard work and dedication, 
both on and off the field, in achieving this accomplishment, is truly 
outstanding.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special tribute 
to the Tiffin Calvert Catholic High School girls volleyball team. On 
behalf of the people of the 5th District of Ohio, I am proud to 
recognize this great achievement.

                          ____________________
```
