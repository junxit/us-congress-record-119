# RECOGNIZING THE 2025-2026 SERVICE ACADEMY NOMINATION BOARD

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE 2025-2026 SERVICE ACADEMY NOMINATION BOARD

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with profound appreciation 
to recognize the exemplary work of the 2025-2026 Service Academy 
Nomination Board, whose service has been indispensable to the success 
of this year's nomination cycle.
  Each year, talented young men and women from Puerto Rico seek a 
congressional nomination to the United States Service Academies, driven 
by a commitment to serve our Nation. Evaluating these nomination 
applications is both an honor and a significant undertaking: one that 
demands integrity, discernment, and unwavering fairness. This year's 
Nomination Board carried out that responsibility with exceptional 
professionalism.
  The members of the Board devoted extensive hours to reviewing 
application packages, assessing academic records, weighing leadership 
experiences, and conducting thoughtful and rigorous interviews. Their 
work ensured that every candidate received a fair, comprehensive, and 
respectful evaluation. Their collective expertise and diverse 
professional backgrounds were instrumental in selecting nominees who 
embody the highest ideals of service, character, and leadership.
  Beyond the technical demands of their role, the Board demonstrated a 
profound dedication to Puerto Rico's youth and to strengthening 
pathways into military and public service. Their contributions have 
directly expanded opportunities for our Island's future officers, young 
leaders who may one day command units, pilot aircraft, safeguard 
national security, or serve our Nation in critical moments.
  This year's Board approached its work with seriousness of purpose and 
an unwavering commitment to excellence. Their service reflects the very 
values we ask of our applicants: integrity, judgment, discipline, and a 
commitment to something greater than oneself.
  On behalf of the people of Puerto Rico, I extend my deepest gratitude 
to the members of the 2025-2026 Service Academy Nomination Board: Brig. 
Gen. Carlos Gorbea; COL. (retired) Marta Carcana; COL. (retired) 
Michelle Fraley; CW4 (retired) Cristino Lozada; MAJ. Ricardo Herrero; 
Cpt. Joshua Eldelman; Mr. Hector Luis Acevedo; Mr. Jose Cabrera; Mrs. 
Tasha Endara; Mr. Luis Soto; and Mrs. Wanda Ayala de Torres for their 
extraordinary work, their professionalism, and their service. Their 
efforts have strengthened this program, uplifted deserving candidates, 
and helped shape the next generation of leaders who will proudly 
represent Puerto Rico and our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
