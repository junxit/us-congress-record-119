# RECOGNIZING AVON HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE DIVISION II STATE FOOTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
RECOGNIZING AVON HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE DIVISION II STATE FOOTBALL 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I rise 
to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school football 
team in Ohio's 5th Congressional District. The young men of the Avon 
High School football team have represented their school ably on their 
way to achieving the Division II State Football Title. In their effort 
to surpass all other teams in the Division II State Football Playoffs, 
the Avon Eagles overcame the challenges posed by intense competition.
  In pursuing the State Championship, the Avon Eagles defeated the 
Cincinnati Anderson Raptors to win their second straight state football 
championship. The members of this very special team have shown that 
their sport requires an individual effort for a team result. Their hard 
work and dedication, both on and off the field, in achieving this 
accomplishment, is truly outstanding.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special tribute 
to the Avon High School football team. On behalf of the people of the 
5th District of Ohio, I am proud to recognize this great achievement.
```
