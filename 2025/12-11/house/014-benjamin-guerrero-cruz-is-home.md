# BENJAMIN GUERRERO-CRUZ IS HOME

**Speaking:** Rivas, Luz M. (R000620) [D-CA]

```
                              {time}  1110
                     BENJAMIN GUERRERO-CRUZ IS HOME

  (Ms. RIVAS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. RIVAS. Mr. Speaker, for months I have been sharing the story of 
Benjamin Guerrero-Cruz, a constituent of mine who is a senior in high 
school. He was detained by ICE in August while walking his dog.
  In the span of 24 hours, ICE shuffled Benjamin between detention 
centers and across State lines without his family knowing. I visited 
Benjamin at the Adelanto Detention Center and observed a recent court 
hearing of his, and my team has been working with his family on this 
case throughout the ordeal.
  Today, I am excited and relieved to report that after months of 
living through a nightmare, Benjamin is home. My heart goes out to his 
family, especially his mother, who can hold her son again after months 
of fear and uncertainty at the hands of ICE. No family should ever have 
to live through this.
  I will continue showing up to detention facilities to hold ICE and 
this administration accountable for the terror that they are forcing 
families to live through each day.
  Mr. Speaker, I am glad that Benjamin is home, and I hope he and his 
family can begin the healing process.
  Welcome home, Benjamin.

                          ____________________
```
