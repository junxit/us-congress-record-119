# HONORING SHERIFF ROB SNAZA ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Perez, Marie Gluesenkamp (G000600) [D-WA]

```
              HONORING SHERIFF ROB SNAZA ON HIS RETIREMENT

  (Ms. PEREZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. PEREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a public servant 
who has given nearly four decades to the people of Lewis County, 
Sheriff Rob Snaza.
  He is a man with a gentle spirit and a decisive character. He is 
eager to help others, not to be helped. He is quick to apologize and 
forgive and find goodness in others, though he has spent his career 
seeing some of the ugliest things this world has to offer.
  He is persistent in his trust in the Lord and a diligent encourager 
of those around him. He has been a source of strength to all of us, and 
I am confident that the eternal story of his work will go on long after 
he is retired.
  We are profoundly grateful for the sheriff's service. I sincerely 
thank him for the example he has set for us all.
```
