# RECOGNIZING VANNAH HEIL

**Speaking:** Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

```
                        RECOGNIZING VANNAH HEIL

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. BRAD FINSTAD

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 21, 2025

  Mr. FINSTAD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
young leader from southern Minnesota, Vannah Heil of St. James. This 
November, Vannah will join more than 700 youth from across the Nation 
at the National 4-H Congress in Atlanta.
  Vannah is one of only nine students selected to represent Minnesota 
at this prestigious event, where she will take part in workshops, 
service projects, and opportunities to strengthen her leadership and 
communication skills. As a Minnesota 4-H State Ambassador, Vannah has 
already shown her commitment to building stronger communities and 
inspiring her peers.
  The 4-H program has a long tradition of equipping young people with 
the tools they need to succeed in school, careers, and civic life. 
Vannah embodies the very best of that mission. I am proud to represent 
her and look forward to seeing the great things she will accomplish in 
the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
