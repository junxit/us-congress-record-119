# CELEBRATING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF ST. JAMES CATHOLIC CHURCH

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
     CELEBRATING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF ST. JAMES CATHOLIC CHURCH

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 21, 2025

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 175th 
anniversary of St. James Catholic Church in Elizabethtown, Kentucky.

[[Page E1116]]

  Since 1851, the St. James Catholic Church community has served the 
residents of Elizabethtown and the surrounding area. In 1852, the first 
church was constructed, and twenty years later, St. Mary's Academy was 
established to educate the parish children by the Sisters of Loretto. 
Eventually being renamed after the church's patron saint, St. James 
School continues to serve parish families, providing a religious-based 
education. Seeing continued growth, the parish eventually constructed a 
high school, which still operates today as Elizabethtown Catholic High 
School.
  In 1969, the parish broke ground on a new church, which was completed 
in 1971. Since then, the parish has continued to expand its missionary 
work in the surrounding community. Saint James Catholic Church has 
proven to be a place where families can flourish in faith and 
fellowship. I am proud to recognize their incredible 175 years of 
commitment to faith, education, and service in their community.
```
