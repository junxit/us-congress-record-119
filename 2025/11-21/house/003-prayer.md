# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy Lord, we, too, want to be holy. As the Gospel song goes, we want 
to be set apart for You, a vessel tried and true.
  But as we come before Your throne, we soon realize that our efforts 
often fall short. Our lives, our attitudes, and our behaviors are far 
from what anyone would deem holy.
  For You have counseled us that holiness does not just mean 
perseverance and prayer or attempts to be tolerant with one another. 
Rather, You have commanded us to live in peace with one another without 
the continual strife or constant drama we perpetuate with those whom we 
perceive to be our enemies.
  Forgive our bitter spirits, which threaten to poison the very people 
You have called us to serve. Prevent us from selling our birthright to 
righteousness when our own hunger for immediate satisfaction gets in 
the way.
  You have said, ``Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute 
you,'' that we may be faithful to the loving relationship You desire 
with each of us and all of us with each other. You have shown that when 
we allow ourselves to be vessels of Your unconditional love, then we 
receive the strength we need. Then our lives become a living and holy 
sacrifice to You.
  Lord, we want to be holy. Set us apart for Your use on this day.
  In Your redeeming name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
