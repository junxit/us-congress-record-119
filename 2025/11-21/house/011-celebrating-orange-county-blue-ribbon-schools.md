# CELEBRATING ORANGE COUNTY BLUE RIBBON SCHOOLS

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
             CELEBRATING ORANGE COUNTY BLUE RIBBON SCHOOLS

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate four Orange County 
schools named 2025 California Blue Ribbon Schools. The honorees include 
Oxford Academy, Troy High School, Orange Lutheran High, and Tustin 
Memorial.
  These schools were selected for their exceptional academic 
performance or progress in closing the opportunity gap.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank all the students, teachers, administrators, and 
staff who have worked very hard to make Orange County schools great 
places and the best places to learn.
```
