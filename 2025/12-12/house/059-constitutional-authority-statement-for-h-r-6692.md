# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6692

**Measures:** H.R. 6692

```
            By Mr. MILLS:
        H.R. 6692.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 2 of Section 5 of Article 1 of the Constitution: 
     ``Each House may determine the Rules of its Proceedings''
       Clause 1 of Section 6 of Article 1 of the Constitution: 
     ``The Senators and Representatives shall receive a 
     Compensation for their Services, to be ascertained by Law, 
     and paid out of the Treasury of the United States.''
       Section 1 of Amendment 27 to the Constitution: ``No law, 
     varying the compensation for the services of the Senators and 
     Representatives, shall take effect, until an election of 
     Representatives shall have intervened.''
```
