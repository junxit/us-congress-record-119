# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, today, give Your guidance to our lawmakers. Help them to 
seek first Your Kingdom so that everything will fall into its proper 
place and their lives will honor You. As they seek greater intimacy 
with You, empower them to relate honestly with themselves and one 
another.
  Lord, lead them together to find solutions to the problems that beset 
this great land. Calm their fears and strengthen their faith. Use them 
to serve You and country with faithfulness. Let Your peace guard their 
hearts.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
