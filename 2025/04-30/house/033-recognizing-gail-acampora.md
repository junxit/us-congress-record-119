# RECOGNIZING GAIL ACAMPORA

**Speaking:** McGuire, John J. (M001239) [R-VA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING GAIL ACAMPORA

  (Mr. McGUIRE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGUIRE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Gail Acampora from 
Prospect, Virginia. I am lucky enough to call Gail a close friend of 
mine. She has always been interested in politics and is an avid 
supporter of President Trump.
  In 2021, Gail was diagnosed with lung cancer. She has had a lobe and 
lymph nodes removed and went through tough chemotherapy. Unfortunately, 
she is still fighting after another cancer diagnosis.
  Her strong faith in God and positive attitude will keep her on the 
road to recovery.
  Tracy and I are with Gail, and I know she is going to fight hard and 
beat this thing. I ask that everyone include her in your prayers.

                          ____________________
```
