# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God of light, illumine our way. O God of hope, strengthen our 
resolve. O God of truth, edify our souls so that we may live today for 
Your glory. May our lawmakers bring honor to You by being faithful 
stewards of love, grace, compassion, and patience.
  Lord, use them to meet the pressing needs of our Nation and world, 
providing our Senators with opportunities to be Your hands and heart in 
these challenging times. Let them never lack the courage or the will to 
do Your work. May their words, thoughts, and actions reflect the 
content of Your character.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
