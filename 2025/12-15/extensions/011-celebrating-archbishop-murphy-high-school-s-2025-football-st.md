# CELEBRATING ARCHBISHOP MURPHY HIGH SCHOOL'S 2025 FOOTBALL STATE TITLE

**Speaking:** Larsen, Rick (L000560) [D-WA]

```
 CELEBRATING ARCHBISHOP MURPHY HIGH SCHOOL'S 2025 FOOTBALL STATE TITLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RICK LARSEN

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 15, 2025

  Mr. LARSEN of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise today along with my 
colleague, Congresswoman Suzan DelBene, to congratulate the Archbishop 
Murphy High School football team on their first state title since 2016. 
The team earned the opportunity to play Tumwater in the 2A state 
championship game at Husky Stadium on December 6, 2025, led by their 
head coach Joe Cronin.
  We would also like to extend our congratulations to Archbishop Murphy 
senior receiver Henry Gabalis for signing to the University of Arizona, 
offensive lineman Hakiem Smalls for signing to Boise State University, 
and tight end Jack Sievers for signing to the University of Wisconsin--
Madison. These students, as well as their entire team, should be proud 
of their performance this season.
  Mr. Speaker, we ask our colleagues to join us in celebrating the 
achievements of the Archbishop Murphy High School football team for 
their hard work and championship win. Go Wildcats.

                          ____________________
```
