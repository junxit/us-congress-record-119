# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 16, 2025

**Speaking:** Hoeven, John (H001061) [R-ND]

**Measures:** S. 1071

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 16, 2025

  Mr. HOEVEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 11 a.m. 
on Tuesday, December 16, 2025; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
concur in the House amendment to S. 1071, postcloture, and that all 
time during recess, adjournment, morning business, and leader remarks 
count postcloture on the motion to concur; further, that the Senate 
recess from 1 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly conference 
meetings; and, finally, that if any nominations are confirmed during 
Tuesday's session of the Senate, the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table, and the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
