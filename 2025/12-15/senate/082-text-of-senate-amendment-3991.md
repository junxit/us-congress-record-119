# Text of Senate Amendment 3991

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4016

```
  SA 3991. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 3951 submitted by Ms.

[[Page S8742]]

Collins and intended to be proposed to the bill H.R. 4016, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. PROHIBITION ON DEPLOYMENT OF OTHER STATE NATIONAL 
                   GUARD UNITS FOR THE FEDERAL PROTECTIVE MISSION 
                   IN CALIFORNIA.

       The President may not use the authority under section 12406 
     of title 10, United States Code, or any other provision of 
     law to call into Federal service members of the National 
     Guard of a State other than California for the Federal 
     protective mission announced in the June 7, 2025, 
     Presidential Memorandum entitled ``Department of Defense 
     Security for the Protection of Department of Homeland 
     Security Functions''.
                                 ______
```
