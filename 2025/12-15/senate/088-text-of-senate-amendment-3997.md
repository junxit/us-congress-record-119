# Text of Senate Amendment 3997

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

```
  SA 3997. Mr. PETERS (for himself and Mr. Young) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 4016, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. GREAT LAKES RESTORATION INITIATIVE REAUTHORIZATION.

       Section 118(c)(7)(J)(i)(VI) of the Federal Water Pollution 
     Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1268(c)(7)(J)(i)(VI)) is amended by 
     striking ``fiscal year 2026'' and inserting ``each of fiscal 
     years 2026 and 2027''.
                                 ______
```
