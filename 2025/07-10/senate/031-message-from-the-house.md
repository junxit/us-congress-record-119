# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 4

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:57 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. McLaughlin, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
has passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of 
the Senate:

     H.R. 4. An act to rescind certain budget authority proposed 
     to be rescinded in special messages transmitted to the 
     Congress by the President on June 3, 2025, in accordance with 
     section 1012(a) of the Congressional Budget and Impoundment 
     Control Act of 1974.
```
