# Vote on Gould Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                        Vote on Gould Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Gould nomination?
  Mr. KENNEDY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from South Carolina (Mr. Graham), the Senator from Missouri 
(Mr. Hawley), and the Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Tillis).
  Further, if present and voting: the Senator from North Carolina (Mr. 
Tillis) would have voted ``yea.''
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Connecticut (Mr. 
Blumenthal) and the Senator from Connecticut (Mr. Murphy) are 
necessarily absent.
  The result was announced--yeas 50, nays 45, as follows:
```
