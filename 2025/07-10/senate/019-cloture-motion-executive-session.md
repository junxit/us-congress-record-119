# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair 
lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will 
state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 64, Jonathan Gould, of Virginia, to 
     be Comptroller of the Currency for a term of five years.
         John Thune, John Barrasso, Cindy Hyde-Smith, John R. 
           Curtis, Rick Scott of Florida, Bernie Moreno, Pete 
           Ricketts, Eric Schmitt, Jon A. Husted, Roger Marshall, 
           Jim Justice, Tommy Tuberville, Bill Hagerty, Joni 
           Ernst, James E. Risch, Marsha Blackburn, Tim Sheehy.

call has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of Jonathan Gould, of Virginia, to be Comptroller of the 
Currency for a term of five years, shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
```
