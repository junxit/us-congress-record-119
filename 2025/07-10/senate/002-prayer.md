# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, before You nations rise and fall. They grow strong or 
whither by Your design. Help our Nation and world to embrace 
righteousness and to strive for unity and renewal.
  Lord, hasten the coming of Your Kingdom, where pain, tears, and death 
will be no more. Teach all nations the way of peace so we may plow up 
battlefields and pound weapons into liberation tools. Teach us to talk 
across boundaries as brothers and sisters, united by Your love.
  Today, inspire our Senators and all who work with them to strive in 
their efforts to transform dark yesterdays into bright tomorrows.
  And, Lord, we continue to pray for those involved with the Texas 
flooding.
  We pray in Your magnificent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
