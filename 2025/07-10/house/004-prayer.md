# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God, our shepherd, we listen for Your voice. Though the cares and 
concerns of the world create a cacophony of unsound counsel, we wait 
upon a Word from You to break into the din of our confusion.
  You know us. You know our hearts, our desires, and our deepest fears. 
You know the promise of our potential and the discomfort of our 
inhibitions. You know both our quirks and our character.
  Because of Your gracious welcome into Your tender care, may we, in 
our work and play, our plans and purpose, follow where You lead us.
  Savior, like a shepherd, guide us. Be the guardian of our way, the 
protector of our lives, this day and always.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
