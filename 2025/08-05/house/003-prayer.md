# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Vincent DeRosa, Archdiocese of Washington, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  Lord God, from whom all authority flows, grant the Members of this 
House wisdom and prudence as they deliberate the service of the common 
good on behalf of our people.
  Give them a special care for the neediest among us.
  Give them an enduring respect for families at the foundation of our 
great society, and finally, Lord, give them holy reference for those 
who have gone before us in these Halls, that we may build on their good 
work for the sake of a peaceful, prosperous future.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
