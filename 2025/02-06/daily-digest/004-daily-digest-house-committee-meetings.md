# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
COMBATTING EXISTING AND EMERGING ILLICIT DRUG THREATS
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``Combatting Existing and Emerging Illicit Drug Threats''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
OPERATION CHOKE POINT 2.0: THE BIDEN ADMINISTRATION'S EFFORTS TO PUT 
CRYPTO INTO THE CROSSHAIRS
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a hearing entitled ``Operation Choke Point 2.0: The 
Biden Administration's Efforts to Put Crypto into the Crosshairs''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
CALIFORNIA FIRES AND THE CONSEQUENCES OF OVERREGULATION
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on the Administrative State, 
Regulatory Reform, and Antitrust held a hearing entitled ``California 
Fires and the Consequences of Overregulation''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
NOW ORE NEVER: THE IMPORTANCE OF DOMESTIC MINING FOR U.S. NATIONAL 
SECURITY
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Energy and Mineral 
Resources held a hearing entitled ``Now Ore Never: The Importance of 
Domestic Mining for U.S. National Security''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
TRANSGENDER LAB RATS AND POISONED PUPPIES: OVERSIGHT OF TAXPAYER FUNDED 
ANIMAL CRUELTY
Committee on Oversight and Accountability: Subcommittee on 
Cybersecurity, Information Technology, and Government Innovation held a 
hearing entitled ``Transgender Lab Rats and Poisoned Puppies: Oversight 
of Taxpayer Funded Animal Cruelty''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
COUNTER-UNMANNED AIRCRAFT SYSTEMS
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Subcommittee on 
Aviation held a hearing entitled ``Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
VETERANS ADMINISTRATION FIRST, VETERAN SECOND: THE BIDEN-HARRIS LEGACY
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a hearing entitled ``VA First, Veteran Second: The 
Biden-Harris Legacy''. Testimony was heard from Ted Radway, Acting 
Assistant Secretary, Office of Accountability and Whistleblower 
Protection, Department of Veterans Affairs; David Casei, Acting 
Inspector General/Deputy Inspector General, Office of the Inspector 
General, Department of Veterans Affairs; and a public witness.
TIME'S RUNNING OUT: PROSECUTING FRAUDSTERS FOR STEALING BILLIONS OF 
UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFITS FROM AMERICAN WORKERS
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Work and Welfare held a 
hearing entitled ``Time's Running out: Prosecuting Fraudsters for 
Stealing Billions of Unemployment Benefits from American Workers''. 
Testimony was heard from Anna S. Hui, Director, Department of Labor and 
Industrial Relations, Missouri; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
