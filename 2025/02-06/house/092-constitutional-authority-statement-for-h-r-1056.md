# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1056

**Measures:** H.R. 1056

```
            By Mr. COHEN:
        H.R. 1056.
       Article 1, Section 8
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       To include the Czech Republic in the list of foreign states 
     whose nationals are eligible for admission into the United 
     States as E-1 nonimmigrants if United States nationals are 
     treated similarly by the Government of the Czech Republic.
```
