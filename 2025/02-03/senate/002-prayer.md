# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Move deeply in our hearts today, O Lord, so that we will conform to 
Your ways. Help us to understand Your purposes and submit to Your 
providence. Empower our lawmakers to do Your will. Make them thirsty 
and hungry for Your Spirit and power. Show them Your plans. Teach them 
Your paths.
  Lord, instruct them on how to make our Nation and world better. Open 
doors of greater opportunity for service as our Senators seek to be 
instruments for Your glory. May pleasing You become the primary aim of 
their work.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
