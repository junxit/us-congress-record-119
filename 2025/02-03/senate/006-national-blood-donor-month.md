# NATIONAL BLOOD DONOR MONTH

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                       NATIONAL BLOOD DONOR MONTH

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, January is National Blood Donor Month. 
Every two seconds someone in the United States needs blood. Yet less 
than 10 percent of the eligible population donates blood annually.
  Blood donations are especially critical for patients receiving an 
organ transplant, having surgery, premature infants, and those involved 
in car accidents. Regular blood donations can help your neighbor or a 
stranger.
  Following a natural disaster, we often hear the call for blood 
donations. Yet we often forget about it when those events pass.
  Blood donations are needed all the time. I encourage Iowans--I 
suppose I should encourage all Americans--who can donate blood to find 
their local blood center and donate today.

                          ____________________
```
