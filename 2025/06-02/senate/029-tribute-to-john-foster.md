# TRIBUTE TO JOHN FOSTER

**Speaking:** Cassidy, Bill (C001075) [R-LA]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                         TRIBUTE TO JOHN FOSTER

● Mr. CASSIDY. Mr. President, I want to congratulate John 
Foster, a Louisiana native and fellow LSU Tiger, on an impressive run 
on American Idol.
  John was the runner-up on this season of American Idol. And while it 
was not the outcome everyone in Louisiana was hoping for, he 
represented himself and Louisiana well on a national stage.
  John's performances were excellent and inspiring. He was also 
practicing and performing as he is pursuing pre-med.
  John, well done on pursuing both your dream and your education. From 
all of us back home, keep singing. You are first place in 
Louisiana.●

                          ____________________
```
