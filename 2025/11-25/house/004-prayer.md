# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  With grateful hearts, we come before You, O Lord, to give thanks for 
all that Your gracious hand has provided: family and friends, life and 
love, all of which flow from the bounty of Your mercy on us.
  Should this season find our souls anxious, allow us to feel the 
surround of Your gentleness. Remain near to us in every situation and 
receive our prayers and petitions. Hear our thanksgiving even in our 
most dire circumstances, for we claim our faith in Your goodness in all 
things and our hope in Your plan for our future.
  Guard our hearts and minds as we dare to open them to receive Your 
compassion, even when we cannot understand the whys of our moments. May 
we learn to make ourselves vulnerable to Your abiding and abundant 
love, even while we wait for the fullness of Your grace to be revealed 
in our lives.
  Gather Your people into Your will, that we may share in the harvest 
of Your love and abide in Your presence.
  God, our maker, we offer thanksgiving to You on this day and in all 
things.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
