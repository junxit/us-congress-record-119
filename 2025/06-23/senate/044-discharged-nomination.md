# DISCHARGED NOMINATION

```
                         DISCHARGED NOMINATION

  The Senate Committee on Finance was discharged from further 
consideration of the following nomination pursuant to Sec. 411(c) of 
P.L. 109-280 and the nomination was placed on the Executive Calendar:

       * JANET DHILLON, OF VIRGINIA, TO BE DIRECTOR OF THE PENSION 
     BENEFIT GUARANTY CORPORATION FOR A TERM OF FIVE YEARS.

  * Nominee has committed to respond to requests to appear and testify 
before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.
```
