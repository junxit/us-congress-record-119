# EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

```
                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communication was laid before the Senate, together with 
accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and was referred as 
indicated:

        EC-1171. A communication from the President of the United 
     States to the President pro tempore of the United States 
     Senate, transmitting, consistent with the War Powers Act, a 
     report relative to United States forces conducting a 
     precision strike against three nuclear facilities in Iran 
     used by the Government of the Islamic Republic of Iran for 
     its nuclear weapons development program; to the Committee on 
     Foreign Relations.
```
