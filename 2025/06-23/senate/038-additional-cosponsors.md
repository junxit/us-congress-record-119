# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.Res. 271, S.Res. 281, S.Res. 292, S. 1064, S. 1318, S. 1538, S. 1547, S. 1742, S. 1745, S. 1748, S. 1900, S. 2067, S. 2070, S. 2073, S. 2103

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1064

  At the request of Mr. Young, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Mr. Scott) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1064, a bill to 
preserve open competition and Federal Government neutrality towards the 
labor relations of Federal Government contractors on Federal and 
federally funded construction projects, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1318

  At the request of Mr. Moran, the name of the Senator from Texas (Mr. 
Cruz) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1318, a bill to direct the 
American Battle Monuments Commission to establish a program to identify 
American-Jewish servicemembers buried in United States military 
cemeteries overseas under markers that incorrectly represent their 
religion and heritage, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1538

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the names of the Senator from New 
York (Mrs. Gillibrand) and the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Welch) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 1538, a bill to amend the Animal Welfare Act 
to expand and improve the enforcement capabilities of the Attorney 
General, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1547

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the names of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick), the Senator from Arizona (Mr. Gallego), 
the Senator from Maine (Ms. Collins) and the Senator from Delaware (Mr. 
Coons) were added as cosponsors of S. 1547, a bill to amend title 54, 
United States Code, to reauthorize the National Parks and Public Land 
Legacy Restoration Fund, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1742

  At the request of Mr. Durbin, the name of the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Sanders) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1742, a bill to amend the 
Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to prohibit employment of children in 
tobacco-related agriculture as oppressive child labor.


                                S. 1745

  At the request of Mr. Cotton, the name of the Senator from Texas (Mr. 
Cornyn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1745, a bill to repeal certain 
provisions of the CHIPS Act of 2022 and the Research and Development, 
Competition, and Innovation Act, to limit Federal mandates imposed on 
entities seeking Federal funds, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1748

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the names of the Senator from 
Alaska (Ms. Murkowski) and the Senator from Alaska (Mr. Sullivan) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 1748, a bill to protect the safety of 
children on the internet.


                                S. 1900

  At the request of Mr. McCormick, the names of the Senator from 
Nebraska (Mr. Ricketts), the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Merkley) and the 
Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Budd) were added as cosponsors of S. 
1900, a bill to require the Secretary of the Treasury to pursue more 
equitable treatment of Taiwan at the international financial 
institutions, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2067

  At the request of Mr. Schmitt, the name of the Senator from Alabama 
(Mr. Tuberville) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2067, a bill to rescind 
certain budget authority proposed to be rescinded in special messages 
transmitted to the Congress by the President on June 3, 2025, in 
accordance with section 1012(a) of the Congressional Budget and 
Impoundment Control Act of 1974.


                                S. 2070

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from 
Arizona (Mr. Gallego) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2070, a bill to 
provide limited authority to use the Armed Forces to suppress 
insurrection or rebellion and quell domestic violence.


                                S. 2073

  At the request of Mr. Schatz, the name of the Senator from Hawaii 
(Ms. Hirono) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2073, a bill to establish a 
State public option through Medicaid to provide Americans with the 
choice of a high-quality, low-cost health insurance plan.


                                S. 2103

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the name of the Senator from New

[[Page S3494]]

Mexico (Mr. Lujan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2103, a bill to amend 
the Department of Agriculture Reorganization Act of 1994 to authorize 
mandatory funding for the Healthy Food Financing Initiative.


                              S. RES. 271

  At the request of Mr. Mullin, the name of the Senator from Missouri 
(Mr. Hawley) was added as a cosponsor of S. Res. 271, a resolution 
commemorating June 11, 2025, as ``World Franchise Day''.


                              S. RES. 281

  At the request of Ms. Cantwell, the name of the Senator from 
Minnesota (Ms. Klobuchar) was added as a cosponsor of S. Res. 281, a 
resolution commemorating the 95th anniversary of the enactment of the 
Tariff Act of 1930.


                              S. RES. 292

  At the request of Mr. Booker, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. Res. 292, a resolution 
expressing support for the designation of June 19, 2025, as ``World 
Sickle Cell Awareness Day'' in order to increase public awareness 
across the United States and global community about sickle cell disease 
and the continued need for empirical research, early detection 
screenings, novel effective treatments leading to a cure, and 
preventative care programs with respect to complications from sickle 
cell anemia and conditions relating to sickle cell disease.
```
