# TRIBUTE TO TAYLOR QUINN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO TAYLOR QUINN

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Taylor Quinn, an intern 
in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.
  Taylor is a graduate of Lincoln High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, he is attending Augustana University in Sioux Falls, SD, 
where he is pursuing degrees in government and economics. He is a hard 
worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Taylor for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
