# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 295, S.Res. 296

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Ms. ERNST (for herself, Mr. Cramer, Mr. Lankford, 
             Mr. Tuberville, Mr. Risch, Mr. Crapo, Mrs. Britt, 
             Mrs. Hyde-Smith, and Mr. Hawley):
       S. Res. 295. A resolution supporting the designation of the 
     week of June 23 through June 29, 2025, as ``National Women's 
     Sports Week'' to celebrate the anniversary of the enactment 
     of title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 and the 
     growth of women's sports; to the Committee on Commerce, 
     Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. LANKFORD (for himself, Ms. Rosen, Mr. Bennet, 
             Mr. Hickenlooper, Mr. McCormick, Mr. Fetterman, Mr. 
             Moran, Mr. Thune, and Mr. Schumer):
       S. Res. 296. A resolution condemning antisemitism and 
     recent antisemitic attacks in the United States; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
```
