# TRIBUTE TO KADEN SCHMIDT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO KADEN SCHMIDT

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Kaden Schmidt, an intern 
in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.
  Kaden is a graduate of Washington High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, he is attending Iowa State University in Ames, IA, where he 
is pursuing a degree in aerospace engineering. He is a hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kaden for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
