# HONORING THE 2025 ALLEN COUNTY AGRICULTURE HALL OF FAME INDUCTEES

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

```
   HONORING THE 2025 ALLEN COUNTY AGRICULTURE HALL OF FAME INDUCTEES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 23, 2025

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, the Lima/Allen County Chamber of Commerce 
will hold an induction ceremony in Lima on July 10 to mark the 
induction of the 2025 Allen County Agriculture Hall of Fame class. I am 
honored to commend and include in the Record this year's inductees:

       John and Mary Rudolph of Rudolph Foods
       Walter Paxson
       Jim Hefner
       Allen County Agricultural Society

  Selection for the Hall of Fame is a high honor that has been accorded 
to fewer than 50 people and organizations since 2013. Consideration is 
based on each nominee's contributions to the agriculture industry in 
Allen County.
  On behalf of the people of Ohio's Fourth Congressional District, I am 
pleased to join in the accolades for these distinguished inductees as 
they are recognized for their commitment to agriculture and their 
service to others.

                          ____________________
```
