# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our Father, how excellent is Your Name in all the Earth. From 
dawn to sunset, Your mercies sustain us.
  Today, inspire our Senators to embrace Your promises. May they 
remember Your promises to supply their needs, to never forsake them, 
and to prevent anything from separating them from Your love.
  Lord, bestow Your blessings upon our lawmakers, making them wiser, 
stronger, and better, glorifying You in their work. Use them to advance 
Your Kingdom in our Nation and world as they attune their will to Your 
purposes. Create in them a life of purity, honesty, and altruism that 
contributes to solving the problems we face.
  We pray in Your blessed Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
