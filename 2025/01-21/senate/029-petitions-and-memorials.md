# PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

```
                        PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

  The following petition or memorial was laid before the Senate and was 
referred or ordered to lie on the table as indicated:

       POM-1. A petition from citizens of the United States 
     relative to votes on motions to exclude Members-elect and 
     Senators-elect who are disqualified under Section 3 of the 
     14th Amendment, to exclude electoral votes for candidates 
     disqualified thereby, and to invoke the 12th Amendment as 
     necessary; to the Committee on Rules and Administration.
```
