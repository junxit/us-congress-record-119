                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

             Week of February 11 through February 14, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of the nomination of 
Tulsi Gabbard, of Hawaii, to be Director of National Intelligence, 
post-cloture.
  Absent an agreement and if all time is used, at approximately 12:30 
a.m., Senate will vote on confirmation of the nomination of Tulsi 
Gabbard, and on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of 
Robert F. Kennedy, Jr., of California, to be Secretary of Health and 
Human Services.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
