# Tariffs (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                Tariffs

  Mr. President, on tariffs, today, Donald Trump takes a sledgehammer 
to the American economy and even to the American dream. With the flick 
of a pen, Donald Trump is set to unleash the most reckless tsunami of 
tariffs Americans have seen in over half a century. Prices will rise on 
virtually every kind of product--produce, medicines, cars, homes, 
appliances, metals, paper products, beer, lumber, fertilizer, and more.
  But is Donald Trump worried? Not even a little bit. In fact, he said 
the other day he couldn't care less if prices go up. You hear that, 
America? Life is about to become far more expensive, and Donald Trump, 
the billionaire who promised to bring down inflation on day one, says 
he could not care less if you have to pay more.
  Maybe tariffs aren't such a bad deal if you are rich like Donald 
Trump and the people running this MAGA government. They are in such a 
bubble. To have billionaires and wealthy, wealthy people running the 
show, which is what has happened in this Trump administration, they are 
in a bubble.
  They don't understand that when tariffs raise the average costs the 
American family pays by $5,000, the plans to buy a new car, maybe put a 
new room on your house, go take a vacation, or fly to see grandma are 
out the window, and life becomes much tougher.
  But these billionaires living in their bubble don't know, don't care, 
don't give a hoot. For average families, Donald Trump's trade war is a 
financial gut punch. The average household is going to pay, on average, 
$6,000 extra a year. And why is Donald Trump so dead set on starting 
this ill-conceived trade war? Simply to pay for tax breaks for their 
billionaires.
  They talk about all the money they are going to gain, and what are 
they using it for? Reducing taxes on the very wealthy. The money they 
gain out of the pockets of middle-class working families who are paying 
more for almost everything because of the tariffs goes to pay for tax 
breaks for billionaires. It is disgraceful.
  Fortunately, the Senate has a chance to slow this trade war down 
today if Republicans join us. Later this afternoon, the Senate will 
vote on a resolution from Senators Kaine, Klobuchar, and Warner to undo 
Donald Trump's emergency declaration with respect to Canada.
  I am proud to support this resolution because we should be lowering 
costs for American families, not sending them skyrocketing like Donald 
Trump is doing. I know Donald Trump is pushing my colleagues on the 
other side of the aisle to vote it down, but my Republican colleagues 
should tune Donald Trump out. They need to stand up to the President 
when he is hurting American families so badly. Our Republican 
colleagues know deep down in their hearts, these tariffs are 
wrongheaded, harmful, and chaotic. I hope they do the right thing.
```
