# Wisconsin Supreme Court (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                        Wisconsin Supreme Court

  Mr. President, last night, the people of Wisconsin sent a clear 
message to Elon Musk, Donald Trump, and DOGE: Democracy is not for 
sale. There is only one way to interpret what happened last night. It 
was a political warning shot from the American people. Just 70 days 
into Trump 2.0, Americans are tired of the chaos. They are tired of 
Elon Musk attacking Social Security, Medicaid, Medicare.
  And I want to be clear about something else. Yesterday was a sign 
Democrats' message is resonating. When Democrats shine a light on the 
fact that Republicans are taking vital programs away from the middle 
class simply to cut taxes for the ultrarich, the

[[Page S2106]]

public doesn't like it. When we shine a light on Republican attacks on 
Medicaid, on Social Security, on veterans' healthcare simply to cut 
taxes for the rich, Americans listen, and they are aghast at what they 
see. That is one of the main reasons that the results in Wisconsin came 
in as resoundingly as they did.
```
