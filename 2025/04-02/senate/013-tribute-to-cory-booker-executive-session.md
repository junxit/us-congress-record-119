# Tribute to Cory Booker (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                         Tribute to Cory Booker

  Mr. President, just a moment on Senator Booker. Yesterday, my good 
friend Senator Cory Booker delivered the longest address in the history 
of the Senate to lay the case of Republican cruelty for all the 
American people to see.
  And as important as the length of Senator Booker's speech was--and it 
was phenomenal, the stamina, the preparation, the focus he showed by 
speaking for 25 hours was amazing enough--but just as amazing was what 
he said. He shined a light on the terrible dangers the Trump 
administration poses to the country we love.
  I thank him for his brilliance, for his moral clarity, for his 
indistinguishable passion. It was a speech for the ages. It will be 
long-remembered, not only here in the Senate but throughout America. It 
was a tour de force like we rarely see here.
  Senator Booker, I thank you. Our Senate thanks you. But most of all, 
the American people thank you.
```
