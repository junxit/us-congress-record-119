# World Trade Center Health Program (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   World Trade Center Health Program

  Mr. President, another thing that is just infuriating is what they 
are doing to the 9/11 families.
  Donald Trump's assault on the American people takes another sinister 
turn

[[Page S2107]]

this week. It is revolting that Secretary Kennedy, Secretary of Health 
and Human Services, has now fired staffers at the World Trade Center 
Health Program, including its Administrator, Dr. John Howard, a Trump 
appointee who dedicated his life serving 9/11 families and who is 
respected by all sides of the aisle.
  As of right now, Secretary Kennedy has effectively shut the World 
Trade Center Health Program down. This is a betrayal--a betrayal--of 
the 9/11 families. I am sending Secretary Kennedy a letter to 
immediately undo this terrible, terrible decision. This is not a 
``mistake.'' Some of the Republicans who care about the 9/11 program 
are saying it is a mistake. It is on purpose.
  When I met Secretary Kennedy--then he was the nominee--he made a 
commitment to me, an unequivocal commitment that he would protect the 
World Trade Center Health Program. These firings break that promise, 
and I am calling on Secretary Kennedy to answer for this betrayal of 9/
11 families. I am sending him a letter about it, as I mentioned.
  None of these cuts have anything to do with efficiency. This is 
rightwing extremism, pure and simple. And this hurts our brave first 
responders and others who risked life and limb on 9/11, who rushed to 
the tower. They are suffering. They are getting cancers from the gunk 
they breathed in when they ran to the towers to help protect us and 
help us.
  You want to cut that off to these heroes? How un-American can that 
be? How vicious, how nasty, how callous. We are abandoning the people 
who ran to the towers.
  It is not efficiency. It is cruelty; it is meanness; and, 
fundamentally, it is anti-American. We always defend those who have 
risked their lives to defend and help us. All of a sudden, that seems 
to be out the window when it comes to the 9/11 responders.
  These are our heroes. Secretary Kennedy and Donald Trump should not 
betray them. Donald Trump and RFK should not pull the rug out from 
these families who so desperately need this healthcare.
```
