# RECOGNIZING OMAR LAUTENBACH

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING OMAR LAUTENBACH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 2, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Omar Lautenbach 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Omar has overcome many challenges along their journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Omar, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Omar's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Omar Lautenbach on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
