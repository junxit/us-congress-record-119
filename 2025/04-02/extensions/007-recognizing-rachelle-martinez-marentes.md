# RECOGNIZING RACHELLE MARTINEZ MARENTES

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                 RECOGNIZING RACHELLE MARTINEZ MARENTES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 2, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Rachelle 
Martinez Marentes for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service 
Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Rachelle has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Rachelle, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Rachelle's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of 
a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Rachelle Martinez Marentes on 
achieving the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
