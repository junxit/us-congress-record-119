# RECOGNIZING CHRISTELLE KUMESO

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING CHRISTELLE KUMESO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 2, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Christelle 
Kumeso for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Christelle has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Christelle, develop crucial skills 
and a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Christelle's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of 
a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Christelle Kumeso on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
