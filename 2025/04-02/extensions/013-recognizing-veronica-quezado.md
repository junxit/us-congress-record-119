# RECOGNIZING VERONICA QUEZADO

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING VERONICA QUEZADO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 2, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Veronica 
Quezada for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Veronica has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Veronica, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Veronica's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of 
a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Veronica Quezada on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
