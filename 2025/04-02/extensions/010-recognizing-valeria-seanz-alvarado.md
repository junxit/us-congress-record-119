# RECOGNIZING VALERIA SEANZ ALVARADO

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                   RECOGNIZING VALERIA SEANZ ALVARADO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 2, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Valeria Seanz 
Alvarado for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Valeria has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Valeria, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Valeria's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Valeria Seanz Alvarado on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
