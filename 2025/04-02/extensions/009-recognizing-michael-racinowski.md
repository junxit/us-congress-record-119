# RECOGNIZING MICHAEL RACINOWSKI

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING MICHAEL RACINOWSKI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 2, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Michael 
Racinowski for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Michael has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Michael, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Michael's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Michael Racinowski on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
