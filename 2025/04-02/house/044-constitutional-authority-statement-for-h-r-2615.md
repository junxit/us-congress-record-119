# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2615

**Measures:** H.R. 2615

```
           By Mr. WOMACK:
       H.R. 2615.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3:
       The Congress shall have the power to regulate Commerce with 
     foreign Nations, and among States, and with the Indian 
     Tribes.
```
