# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  To the only wise God, our Savior, we offer our prayers this day. It 
is for Your wisdom we appeal, for You alone know how we are to order 
the outcome of these challenging moments, and You alone determine the 
course our country should take.
  In so doing, we dare to submit our deeds to Your judgment, our will 
to Your correction. You, O Lord, will bring to light the things we have 
hidden, and You will disclose our ulterior purposes. May they be found 
worthy of You.
  Give then Your servants an understanding mind and a discerning heart 
to judge between right and wrong in the governance of Your people. For 
without Your guiding presence, Your abiding grace, and Your infinite 
wisdom, who is able to govern this Nation?
  We call on Your merciful name as we offer our prayers this day.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
