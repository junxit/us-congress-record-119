# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 647, H.R. 1336, H.R. 2159, H.R. 4323, H.R. 4371

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 647. A bill 
     to amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the 
     provision of certain additional burial benefits for 
     individuals for whom an urn or plaque is furnished, and for 
     other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-343). Referred 
     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 1336. A bill 
     to direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish a 
     pilot program to furnish hyperbaric oxygen therapy to a 
     veteran who has a traumatic brain injury or post-traumatic 
     stress disorder; with an amendment (Rept. 119-344). Referred 
     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. JORDAN: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 4371. A bill 
     to amend the William Wilberforce Trafficking Victims 
     Protection Reauthorization Act of 2008 to enhance efforts to 
     combat the trafficking of children; with amendments (Rept. 
     119-345). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. JORDAN: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 2159. A bill 
     to direct the Attorney General of the United States to submit 
     to the Congress a report on Federal criminal offenses, and 
     for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-346). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. JORDAN: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 4323. A bill 
     to provide for the vacating of certain convictions and 
     expungement of certain arrests of victims of human 
     trafficking; with an amendment (Rept. 119-347). Referred to 
     the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.

                          ____________________
```
