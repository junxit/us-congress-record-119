# RECOGNIZING TOM DOWNEY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING TOM DOWNEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 17, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tom Downey of 
Ireland Stapleton, PC for being named Lawyer of the Year. Additionally, 
Tom has been named one of The Best Lawyers in America for 
Administrative and Regulatory Law and Government Relations by The Best 
Lawyers in America publication.
  As a regulatory attorney, Tom has provided legal assistance to a wide 
range of clients, helping them navigate regulatory agencies. Tom has 
been particularly impactful within the City of Denver, having rewritten 
Denver's liquor licensing rules, helped create regulations for 
recreational marijuana, and designed the licensing structure for pedal 
cabs in the city. His work with the American Arbitration Association 
has helped further its mission for more equitable and accessible 
conflict resolution.
  Tom also gives back to the legal community by serving as a guest 
lecturer at law schools such as the University of Denver and helps 
clients in his private practice.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Tom Downey for his awards and thank him for 
his contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
