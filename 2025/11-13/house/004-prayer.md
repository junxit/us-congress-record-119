# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  We give thanks to You, O God, for You are good. Your steadfast love 
endures forever. For You have guided this body through the complex 
challenges of these times and have restored to us the assurance of Your 
steadfast love in all circumstances.
  Why, then, do we doubt Your provision?
  Why do we fail to trust in Your care and keeping?
  Before we even call, You are already listening, providing for our 
needs in ways we cannot comprehend. While we are still sharing with You 
our worries and concerns, You have already heard the yearnings of our 
hearts.
  Praise be to You, O Lord, who has not rejected our prayers or 
withheld Your love from us.
  So in confidence we set before You the work of our hands and the 
desires of our hearts, praying in the strength that is ours when we 
call on Your name.
  It is in Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
