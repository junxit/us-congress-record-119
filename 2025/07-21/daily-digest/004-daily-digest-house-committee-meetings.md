# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 104, H.J.Res. 105, H.J.Res. 106, H.R. 3486, H.R. 3898

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Financial Services and 
General Government held a markup on the Subcommittee on Financial 
Services and General Government FY 2026 Appropriations Bill. The 
Subcommittee on Financial Services and General Government FY 2026 
Appropriations Bill was forwarded to the full Committee, without 
amendment.
PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE 
BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT RELATING TO MILES CITY FIELD OFFICE RECORD OF 
DECISION AND APPROVED RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN AMENDMENT; PROVIDING FOR 
CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE BUREAU OF LAND 
MANAGEMENT RELATING TO NORTH DAKOTA FIELD OFFICE RECORD OF DECISION AND 
APPROVED RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN; PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL 
DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT 
RELATING TO CENTRAL YUKON RECORD OF DECISION AND APPROVED RESOURCE 
MANAGEMENT PLAN; STOP ILLEGAL ENTRY ACT; PERMIT ACT
Committee on Rules: Full Committee began a hearing on H.J. Res. 104, 
providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land 
Management relating to ``Miles City Field Office Record of Decision and 
Approved Resource Management Plan Amendment''; H.J. Res. 105, providing 
for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States 
Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating 
to ``North Dakota Field Office Record of Decision and Approved Resource 
Management Plan''; H.J. Res. 106, providing for congressional 
disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating to ``Central Yukon 
Record of Decision and Approved Resource Management Plan''; H.R. 3486, 
the ``Stop

[[Page D765]]

Illegal Entry Act''; and H.R. 3898, the ``PERMIT Act''. Testimony was 
heard from Chairman Westerman, and Representatives Elfreth, Knott, 
Raskin, Collins, and Scholten.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
