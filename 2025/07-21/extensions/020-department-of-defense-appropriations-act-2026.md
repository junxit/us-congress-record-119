# DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2026

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

```
             DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2026

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                            HON. BRYAN STEIL

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 16, 2025

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Chair, I rise today in support of my amendment to the 
Fiscal Year 2026 Defense Appropriations Act, which provides $1 million 
to complete the Navy Sea Systems Command's final review of centrifuge 
purifiers for surface combatant vessels.
  Centrifuge purifiers are machines that separate liquids into their 
component parts and are critical to the proper functioning of our naval 
ships.
  The Navy currently relies on a single, non-U.S.-based manufacturer 
for these centrifuge purifiers, which creates supply chain 
vulnerabilities and stifles domestic innovation.
  My amendment No. 273, included in Bipartisan En Bloc No. 2, gives the 
Navy the resources it needs to finish a review process it began several 
years ago. Completing its review will help future delays and ensure the 
Navy has access to the most advanced and reliable technology available.
  I urge my colleagues to support this amendment.
```
