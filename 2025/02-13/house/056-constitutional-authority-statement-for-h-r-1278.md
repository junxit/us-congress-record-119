# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1278

**Measures:** H.R. 1278

```
           By Mr. AMODEI of Nevada:
       H.R. 1278.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to coin money under Article 1, Section 
     8 of the U.S. Constitution.
```
