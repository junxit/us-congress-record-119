# Text of Senate Amendment 1811

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 1811. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the concurrent resolution H. Con. Res. 14, establishing the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2025 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal 
years 2026 through 2034; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. REQUIRING ESTIMATES OF THE IMPACT ON INFLATION FOR 
                   RECONCILIATION LEGISLATION.

       The Congressional Budget Office shall include in any 
     estimate for a bill or joint resolution reported pursuant to 
     section 2002, or an amendment to, conference report on, or 
     amendment between the Houses in relation to such a bill or 
     joint resolution, the effect such measure would have on 
     inflation.
                                 ______
```
