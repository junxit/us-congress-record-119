# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 7, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 7, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, April 7; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date; the morning hour be deemed expired; 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, at 5:30 p.m. the Senate vote on the motion 
to invoke cloture on Executive Calendar No. 56, Elbridge Colby.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
