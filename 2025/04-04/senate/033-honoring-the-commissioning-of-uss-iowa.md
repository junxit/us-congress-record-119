# HONORING THE COMMISSIONING OF USS "IOWA"

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
               HONORING THE COMMISSIONING OF USS ``IOWA''

  Ms. ERNST. Madam President, with great pride and admiration, I extend 
my heartfelt congratulations to all involved in the commissioning of 
the USS IOWA (SSN 797), including commanding officer Gregory Coy. While 
I am sorry I cannot be with you in person, I would like to express my 
best wishes on this special occasion for the U.S. Navy and the great 
State of Iowa.
  The USS IOWA will be a powerful symbol of our Nation's maritime 
strength and the unwavering spirit of those who serve in uniform. It 
honors the legacy of previous battleships named USS IOWA, which played 
pivotal roles during the Spanish-American War, World War II, and the 
Korean war. Today's commissioning reflects this rich history and 
underscores the U.S. Navy's 21st-century technological superiority and 
the essential role of nuclear fast-attack submarines in ensuring our 
Nation's undersea deterrence.
  I commend the officers and crew of the USS IOWA for their courage and 
professionalism, and I recognize the families who support them in their

[[Page S2363]]

vital mission. As you embark on your service, know that you carry with 
you the gratitude of the American people. Your work is critical to 
safeguarding our Nation's security and preserving peace and stability 
worldwide.
  As the USS IOWA joins the fleet, it will undoubtedly serve as a 
powerful reminder to both allies and adversaries of the strength and 
resolve of our Armed Forces. The State motto of Iowa, featured on the 
crest of the USS IOWA, is ``Our Liberties We Prize, Our Rights We Will 
Maintain.'' This message encapsulates your ship's vital role in 
securing the liberties and rights we hold dear as Americans.
  As a senator, a soldier, and an Iowan, it is truly an honor to 
congratulate you on this momentous occasion. May God Bless you all, may 
God Bless our men and women in uniform, and may God Bless America.

                          ____________________
```
