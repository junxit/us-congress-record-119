# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Lord most holy, we confess to You our unworthiness. Grant that we 
may every day crave those dispositions which shall make us worthy to be 
called Your children.
  Lord, bless our Senators. Guide them so that in all getting, they 
will receive understanding. Whatever they lose, may they retain Your 
powerful providence, growing in grace and in a deeper knowledge of You.
  Lord, give them a hunger to know Your sacred Word and a willingness 
to follow Your precepts. Consecrate with Your presence the road our 
lawmakers travel, and lead them to Your desired destination.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
