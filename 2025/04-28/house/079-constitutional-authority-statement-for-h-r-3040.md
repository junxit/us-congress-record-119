# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3040

**Measures:** H.R. 3040

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 3040.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is constitutionally authorized under Article I, 
     Section 4, Clause 1 of the United States Constitution, which 
     states: ``The Times, Places and Manner of holding Elections 
     for Senators and Representatives, shall be prescribed in each 
     State by the Legislature thereof; but the Congress may at any 
     time by Law make or alter such Regulations, except as to the 
     Places of chusing Senators.''
```
