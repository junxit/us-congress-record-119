# RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 464, S.Res. 465

```
                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
proceed to the en bloc consideration of the following resolutions, 
which are at the desk: S. Res. 464, S. Res. 465.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolutions en bloc.
  Ms. ERNST. I ask unanimous consent that the resolutions be agreed to, 
the preambles be agreed to, and that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions were agreed to.
  The preambles were agreed to.
  (The resolutions, with their preambles, are printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
