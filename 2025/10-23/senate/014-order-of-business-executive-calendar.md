# Order of Business (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                           Order of Business

  Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the postcloture time with 
respect to the Taibleson nomination be expired and the Senate vote on 
confirmation of the nomination at a time to be determined by the 
majority leader in consultation with the Democratic leader no earlier 
than Monday, October 27; further, that if confirmed, the motion to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table and the President 
be immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
