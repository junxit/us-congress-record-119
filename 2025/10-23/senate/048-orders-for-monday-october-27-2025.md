# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2025

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2025

  Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, October 27; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; finally, at 5:30 
p.m., the Senate execute the orders with respect to the Taibleson and 
Lewis nominations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
