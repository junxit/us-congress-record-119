# ADMINISTRATION OF THE OATH OF OFFICE

```
                  ADMINISTRATION OF THE OATH OF OFFICE

  The VICE PRESIDENT. If the Senator from Iowa will present himself at 
the desk, I will administer the oath of office.
  The Senator, escorted by Mr. THUNE, Mr. SCHUMER, and Mrs. MURRAY, 
respectively, advanced to the desk of the Vice President; the oath 
prescribed by law was administered to him by the Vice President; and he 
severally subscribed to the oath in the Official Oath Book.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. Congratulations.
  (Applause.)
  (The PRESIDENT pro tempore assumed the Chair.)

                          ____________________
```
