# SENATE RESOLUTION 3--TO ELECT CHARLES E. GRASSLEY, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF IOWA, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 3--TO ELECT CHARLES E. GRASSLEY, A SENATOR FROM THE 
STATE OF IOWA, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE UNITED

and agreed to:
```
