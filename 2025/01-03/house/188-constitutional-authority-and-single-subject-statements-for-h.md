# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 172

**Measures:** H.R. 172

```
           By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
       H.R. 172.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill prohibits federal funds from being made available 
     to a state, local, tribal, or private entity that operates or 
     controls an injection center (i.e., a medically supervised 
     injection site) in violation of the federal statute commonly 
     known as the Crack House Statute.
```
