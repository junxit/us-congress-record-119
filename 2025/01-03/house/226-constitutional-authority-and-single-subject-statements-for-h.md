# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 3

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 3

```

       H.J. Res. 3.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant

       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
```
