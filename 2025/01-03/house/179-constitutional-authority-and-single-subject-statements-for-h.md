# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 163

**Measures:** H.R. 163

```
           By Mr. HIGGINS of Louisiana:
       H.R. 163.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power ``to make all Laws which shall be necessary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing Powers, and 
     all other Powers vested by this Constitution in the 
     Government of the United States, or any Department or Officer 
     thereof''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To immediately resume construction of the border wall 
     system along the international border between the United 
     States and Mexico to secure the border, enforce the rule of 
     law, and expend appropriated funds as mandated by Congress.
```
