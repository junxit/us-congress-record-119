# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 25

**Measures:** H.R. 25

```
           By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
       H.R. 25.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To promote freedom, fairness, and economic opportunity by 
     repealing the income tax and other taxes, abolishing the 
     Internal Revenue Service, and enacting a national sales tax 
     to be administered primarily by the States.
```
