# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 100

**Measures:** H.R. 100

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 100.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend Rule 23 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure to 
     protect the ``gig economy'' and small businesses that operate 
     in large part through contractor services from the threat of 
     costly class action litigation.
```
