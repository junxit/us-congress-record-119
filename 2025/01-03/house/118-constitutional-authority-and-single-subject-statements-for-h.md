# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 102

**Measures:** H.R. 102

```

       H.R. 102.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to amend the Endangered
```
