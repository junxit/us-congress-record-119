# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 71

**Measures:** H.R. 71

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 71.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to direct the Secretary 
     of Veterans Affairs to carry out a pilot program to improve 
     the ability of veterans to access medical care in medical 
     facilities of the Department of Veterans Affairs and in the 
     community by providing the veterans the ability to choose 
     health care providers.
```
