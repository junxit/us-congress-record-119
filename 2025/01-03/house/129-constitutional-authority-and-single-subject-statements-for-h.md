# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 113

**Measures:** H.R. 113

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 113.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to remove the 
     discretionary inflater from the baseline and to provide that 
     the salaries of Members of a House of Congress will be held 
     in escrow if that House has not agreed to a concurrent 
     resolution on the budget.
```
