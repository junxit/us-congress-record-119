# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 171

**Measures:** H.R. 171

```
           By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
       H.R. 171.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the inspector general of the Department of 
     Transportation to conduct an audit on the use of Federal 
     funds by certain entities providing public transportation, 
     and for other purposes.
```
