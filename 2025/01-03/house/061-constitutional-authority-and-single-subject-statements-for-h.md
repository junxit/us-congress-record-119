# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 45

**Measures:** H.R. 45

```
           By Mr. BERGMAN:
       H.R. 45.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Federal Government from entering into 
     contracts with an entity that discriminates against the 
     firearm and ammunition industry.
```
