# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 194

**Measures:** H.R. 194

```
           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 194.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The VENUE Act would allow individuals charged with a crime 
     on federal property in the National Capital Region to 
     transfer their trial to a federal court in their home 
     jurisdiction.
```
