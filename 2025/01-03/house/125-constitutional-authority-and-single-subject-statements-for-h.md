# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 109

**Measures:** H.R. 109

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 109.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       The single subject of this bill is to require the Secretary 
     of Veterans Affairs to formally recognize caregivers of 
     veterans, notify veterans and caregivers of clinical 
     determinations relating to eligibility for caregiver 
     programs, and temporarily extend benefits for veterans who 
     are determined ineligible for the family caregiver program.
```
