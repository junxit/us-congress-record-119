# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 79

**Measures:** H.R. 79

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 79.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to nullify certain 
     Executive Orders regarding COVID-19 vaccine mandates and to 
     prohibit the Secretary of Labor from issuing a rule mandating 
     vaccination against COVID-19.
```
