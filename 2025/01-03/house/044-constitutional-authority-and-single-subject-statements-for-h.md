# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 28

**Measures:** H.R. 28

```
           By Mr. STEUBE:
       H.R. 28.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Education Amendments of 1972 to provide that 
     for purposes of determining compliance with title IX of such 
     Act in athletics, sex shall be recognized based solely on a 
     person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.
```
