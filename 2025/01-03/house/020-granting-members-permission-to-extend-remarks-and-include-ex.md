# GRANTING MEMBERS PERMISSION TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD DURING THE 119TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Fischbach, Michelle (F000470) [R-MN]

```
 GRANTING MEMBERS PERMISSION TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE EXTRANEOUS 
     MATERIAL IN THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD DURING THE 119TH CONGRESS

  Mrs. FISCHBACH. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
119th Congress, all Members be permitted to extend their remarks and to 
include extraneous material within the permitted limit in that section 
of the Record titled, ``Extensions of Remarks.''
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Minnesota?
  There was no objection.
```
