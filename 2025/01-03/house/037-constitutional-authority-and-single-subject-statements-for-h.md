# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 21

**Measures:** H.R. 21

```
           By Mrs. WAGNER:
       H.R. 21.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       (1) Section 5 of the 14th Amendment, including the power to 
     enforce the prohibition on government action denying equal 
     protection of the laws; and (2) Section 9 of Article I, to 
     make all laws necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution the powers vested by the Constitution of the United 
     States, including the power to regulate commerce under Clause 
     3 of such action.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to prohibit a health 
     care practitioner from failing to exercise the proper degree 
     of care in the case of a child who survives an abortion or 
     attempted abortion.
```
