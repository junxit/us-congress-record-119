# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 151

**Measures:** H.R. 151

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 151.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires a citizenship question on the decennial census, 
     reporting on certain census statistics, and modifies 
     apportionment of Representatives to be based on United States 
     citizens instead of all persons.
```
