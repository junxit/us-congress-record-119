# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 103

**Measures:** H.R. 103

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 103.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to provide Members of 
     Congress lawful access to certain Indian land to assess the 
     security of the international boundary between the United 
     States and Mexico located on that Indian land.
```
