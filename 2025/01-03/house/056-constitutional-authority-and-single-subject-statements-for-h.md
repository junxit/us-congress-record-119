# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 40

**Measures:** H.R. 40

```
           By Ms. PRESSLEY:
       H.R. 40.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Section 5 of the Fourteenth Amendment to the 
     United States Constitution, Congress shall have the power to 
     enact appropriate laws protecting the civil rights of all 
     Americans

[[Page H37]]

       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To address the fundamental injustice, cruelty, brutality, 
     and inhumanity of slavery in the United States
```
