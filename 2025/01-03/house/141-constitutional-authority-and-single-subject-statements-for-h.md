# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 125

**Measures:** H.R. 125

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 125.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to amend the National 
     Emergencies Act to provide that a national emergency declared 
     by the President terminates 30 days after the declaration 
     unless a joint resolution affirming such declaration is 
     enacted into law.
```
