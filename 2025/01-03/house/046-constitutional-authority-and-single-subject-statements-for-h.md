# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 30

**Measures:** H.R. 30

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 30.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide that aliens who have been convicted of or who 
     have committed sex offenses or domestic violence are 
     inadmissible and deportable.
```
