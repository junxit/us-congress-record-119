# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 23

**Measures:** H.R. 23

```
           By Mr. ROY:
       H.R. 23.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I. Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To sanction the ICC if they investigate, arrest, detain, or 
     prosecute a United States person, or ally of the United 
     States that are not part of the ICC or have not granted the 
     ICC jurisdiction.
```
