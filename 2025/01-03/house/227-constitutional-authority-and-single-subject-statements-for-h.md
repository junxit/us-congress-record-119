# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 4

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 4

```
           By Mr. FITZPATRICK:
       H.J. Res. 4.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 5.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the U.S. Constitution to provide that debate upon 
     legislation pending before the Senate may not be brought to a 
     close without the concurrence of a minimum of three-fifths of 
     the Senators.
```
