# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 137

**Measures:** H.R. 137

```
           By Mr. BUCHANAN:
       H.R. 137.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make 
     permanent certain provisions of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act
```
