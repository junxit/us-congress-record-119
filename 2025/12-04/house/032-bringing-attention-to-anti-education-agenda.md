# BRINGING ATTENTION TO ANTI-EDUCATION AGENDA

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
              BRINGING ATTENTION TO ANTI-EDUCATION AGENDA

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to bring attention to the 
anti-education agenda of the Trump administration.
  The Department of Education's RISE Committee is offering a proposal 
to downgrade the professional status of various careers that they 
think, after many years, don't deserve academic stature and access to 
vital funding. These include nursing, physical therapy, audiology, 
architecture, and social work.
  This assessment comes from the leader of the Department of Education, 
who has no advanced degree in education but does know a lot about pro 
wrestling.
  I was the chief executive officer of a government for over 1 million 
people, with a budget of $2.5 billion and 4,200 employees. These 
Secretaries and committees can theorize all they want. I needed 
professional, well-trained, and certified nurses, social workers, and 
the like to deliver real-world services to real people in the day-to-
day management of a large county.
  Let's stop the ideological foolishness. Place this proposal to 
downgrade careers where it belongs: in the department of garbage and 
sanitation. Let's get back to grownup, serious government that 
understands the problems in America and addresses those problems.

                          ____________________
```
