# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6475

**Measures:** H.R. 6475

```
           By Mr. OWENS:
       H.R. 6475.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII, Congress has the 
     authority to enact this legislation under Article I, Section 
     8 of the Constitution, including the powers to provide for 
     the general welfare, to regulate commerce among the several 
     States, and to make all laws necessary and proper for 
     carrying those powers into execution.
```
