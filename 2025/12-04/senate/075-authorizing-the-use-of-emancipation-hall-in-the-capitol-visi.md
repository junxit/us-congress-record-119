# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER FOR AN EVENT TO UNVEIL THE STATUE OF BARBARA ROSE JOHNS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 62

```
AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER 
        FOR AN EVENT TO UNVEIL THE STATUE OF BARBARA ROSE JOHNS

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to H. Con. Res. 62, which was received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 62) authorizing the 
     use of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for an 
     event to unveil the statue of Barbara Rose Johns.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 62) was agreed to.
```
