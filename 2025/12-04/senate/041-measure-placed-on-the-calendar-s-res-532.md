# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. RES. 532

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S.Res. 532

```
              MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. RES. 532

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send an executive resolution to the desk 
for consideration of certain nominations en bloc and ask for its 
immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the title of the 
resolution for the information of the Senate.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       An executive resolution (S. Res. 532) authorizing the en 
     bloc consideration in Executive Session of certain 
     nominations on the Executive Calendar.

  Mr. THUNE. In order to place the executive resolution on the 
calendar, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the executive 
resolution will lie over 1 day.

                          ____________________
```
