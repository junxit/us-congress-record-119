# TRIBUTE TO HAILEY STORY

**Speaking:** Sheehy, Tim (S001232) [R-MT]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO HAILEY STORY

● Mr. SHEEHY. Mr. President, I would like to take a moment to 
express my appreciation to Hailey Story for her hard work as an intern 
in my Washington, DC, office this fall.

for me and my staff. It has been a pleasure having her as part of our 
team this fall, and I wish her all the best as she continues to build a 
bright future for herself.●
```
