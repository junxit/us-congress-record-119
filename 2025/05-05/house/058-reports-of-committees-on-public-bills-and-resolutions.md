# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 276, H.Res. 377, H.R. 677, H.R. 881

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. GREEN of Tennessee: Committee on Homeland Security. 
     H.R. 881. A bill to establish Department of Homeland Security 
     funding restrictions on institutions of higher education that 
     have a relationship with Confucius Institutes, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-87). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 677. A 
     bill to establish a process to expedite the review of appeals 
     of certain decisions by the Department of the Interior, with 
     an amendment (Rept. 119-88). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. AUSTIN SCOTT of Georgia: Committee on Rules. House 
     Resolution 377. Resolution providing for consideration of the 
     bill (H.R. 276) to rename the Gulf of Mexico as the ``Gulf of 
     America'', and providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     881) to establish Department of Homeland Security funding 
     restrictions on institutions of higher education that have a 
     relationship with Confucius Institutes, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 119-89). Referred to the House Calendar.
```
