# KINSTON BBQ FEST ON THE NEUSE

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                     KINSTON BBQ FEST ON THE NEUSE

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, no one does barbecue like

[[Page H1843]]

eastern North Carolina, and I am so proud to share some mouthwatering 
news.
  The BBQ Fest on the Neuse, held in Kinston, North Carolina, has set a 
new world record. The Guinness World Record for the number of barbecue 
sandwiches sold in an 8-hour period is now 4,775.
  I was proud to attend this year's festival. The festival is more than 
just delicious food. It is a showcase of community, local businesses, 
music, and the proud spirit of eastern North Carolina. It highlights 
everything that makes Kinston and Lenoir County such a special place.
  We take great pride in being home of such rich tradition.
  Mr. Speaker, there is no better barbecue on Earth than eastern North 
Carolina.
```
