# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                      May 5, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to Section 214(a) of the Help 
     America Vote Act of 2002 (52 U.S.C. 20944), I hereby 
     reappoint Mr. Brian Michael Lemek of Potomac, Maryland to the 
     U.S. Election Assistance Commission Board of Advisors.
       Thank you for your attention to his appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     House Democratic Leader.
```
