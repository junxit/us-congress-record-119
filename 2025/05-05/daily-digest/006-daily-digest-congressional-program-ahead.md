# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 7, S.J.Res. 13, H.J.Res. 60, H.J.Res. 61, S. 1462

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                   Week of May 6 through May 9, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of H.J. Res. 61, 
National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: Rubber Tire 
Manufacturing, with a vote on passage thereon at approximately 11:30 
a.m. Following disposition of H.J. Res. 61, Senators should expect a 
roll call vote on the motion to proceed to consideration of S.J. Res. 
13, disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the 
rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency of the 
Department of the Treasury relating to the review of applications under 
the Bank Merger Act.
  At approximately 2:15 p.m., Senate will vote on confirmation of the 
nomination of Frank Bisignano, of New Jersey, to be Commissioner of 
Social Security Administration, to be followed by a vote on the motion 
to proceed to consideration of S.J. Res. 7, disapproval under chapter 8 
of title 5, United States

[[Page D430]]

Code, of the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission 
relating to ``Addressing the Homework Gap Through the E-Rate Program''.
  At 5:15 p.m., Senators should expect a roll call vote on the motion 
to proceed to consideration of H.J. Res. 60, disapproval under chapter 
8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Federal 
Communications Commission relating to ``Addressing the Homework Gap 
Through the E-Rate Program''.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
```
