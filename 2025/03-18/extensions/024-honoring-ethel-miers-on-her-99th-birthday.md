# HONORING ETHEL MIERS ON HER 99TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
               HONORING ETHEL MIERS ON HER 99TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 18, 2025

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 99th birthday of 
Ethel Miers. Ms. Miers is a dedicated resident from the town of Milford 
in New Jersey's 7th Congressional District who is committed to 
preserving American values and strengthening our Nation's future. As we 
celebrate her birthday, we recognize her contributions and take this 
opportunity to reflect on the many blessings we share as a community. 
It is my honor to represent her, and I extend my warmest wishes for a 
joyous 99th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
