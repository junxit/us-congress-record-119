# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of all life, we seek You in a world filled with challenges. 
Today, prepare the Members of this body for the rigors of solving 
life's riddles. Give them the wisdom to seek common opportunities to 
accomplish Your will in our Nation and world. Teach them to spend and 
be spent for the good of others.
  Lord, give them the spiritual tools for strength of thought, 
lightness of heart, sincerity of conviction, and clarity of purpose.
  Renew their commitment to You, for You are their inspiration, their 
strength, their courage, their guide, and their Lord.
  We pray in Your magnificent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
