# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Oh, merciful Lord, we thank You for Your light that directs our 
steps. May the Members of this body feel Your guidance, peace, and 
power today. Join our lawmakers to Yourself with an inseparable bond of 
love, for You alone truly satisfy. Lord, grant that their love may 
abound more and more in knowledge and depth of insight so that they may 
be able to deserve what is best and may be pure and blameless when they 
stand before You.
  Send out Your light and Your truth that they may illuminate our 
lawmakers, our Nation, and our world.
  We pray in Your sovereign name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
