# TRIBUTE TO ROGER A. NUTT

**Speaking:** Scott, Tim (S001184) [R-SC]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ROGER A. NUTT

● Mr. SCOTT of South Carolina. Mr. President, today I wish to 
recognize and honor the service of South Carolina State Senator Roger 
A. Nutt.
  A man of faith and a lifelong resident of Spartanburg County, Senator 
Nutt has devoted nearly 15 years of his life to public service, 
advocating for values and policies that build a better future for our 
families.
  During his service on the Spartanburg County Council, in the South 
Carolina House of Representatives, and now in the State senate, Senator 
Nutt has brought the practical, technical perspective of a private 
sector engineer to the work of governing. He has used his experience to 
make thoughtful, informed decisions and stood up for conservative 
principles that have improved the lives of South Carolinians.
  His tenure has been marked by steady leadership, genuine heart for 
service, and a deep dedication to the people of the Palmetto State; yet 
his impact extends well beyond the halls of government.
  Senator Nutt has faithfully served his home church, from leading 
worship to guiding others in the study of Scripture. His Christian 
faith shaped both his public and private life, and our State is better 
off because of it.
  As he prepares for a new chapter, I want to share a few of his own 
words: ``[ . . . ] we will face this trial with faith in the grace of 
God that has sustained our lives, our marriage, and my career in public 
service for nearly fifteen years.''
  Indeed, the God who created him and sustained him through every 
valley and every peak will continue to be near to him, and the prayers 
of countless South Carolinians are with him and his family.
  He has represented a high standard of what it means to lead and 
serve, and it is a privilege to honor Senator Nutt for his years of 
public service to the people of South Carolina.●

                          ____________________
```
