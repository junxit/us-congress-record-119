# TRIBUTE TO CARL "SPIKE" BENTON

**Speaking:** Scott, Tim (S001184) [R-SC]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO CARL ``SPIKE'' BENTON

● Mr. SCOTT of South Carolina. Mr. President, I rise today to 
honor the

[[Page S6318]]

long and exceptional career of Deputy Chief Carl ``Spike'' Benton, who 
has served the Charleston County EMS for over 42 years.
  Raised in North Charleston, SC, Benton began his career with 
Charleston County EMS in 1983 and has since become a steadfast leader 
and mentor. Twice named Paramedic of the Year, he has received numerous 
awards for clinical excellence and commendations from law enforcement 
agencies, the U.S. Secret Service, and the South Carolina College of 
Emergency Physicians.
  Beyond his clinical skill, Benton has shaped generations of EMS 
providers, serving as senior crew chief, shift supervisor, and deputy 
chief. He has played an instrumental role in shaping Charleston County 
EMS into one of the finest EMS agencies in the country. His leadership 
has inspired and guided countless providers, many of whom now serve as 
leaders in medicine, public safety, and government.
  In 1996, Benton's vision and initiative led to the creation of the 
TEMS special team, a tactical EMS team that built bridges between first 
responder agencies and ensured coordinated care during high-risk 
operations. Over the course of more than three decades as its 
commander, he has supported visits from four U.S. Presidents, multiple 
Vice Presidents, and numerous dignitaries, demonstrating not only his 
skill but also his courage and steady leadership.
  Deputy Chief Benton's career reflects the Charleston County EMS 
motto, ``We Care.'' His career is a testament to that simple but 
profound promise. His leadership, courage, and compassion have saved 
lives, strengthened his community, and set a standard of excellence 
that will endure long after his retirement.●
```
