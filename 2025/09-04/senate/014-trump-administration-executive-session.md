# Trump Administration (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                          Trump Administration

  Finally, Mr. President, on the good, old feeling of American 
optimism, unfortunately, in Donald Trump's America, American optimism 
is dying.
  This week, a Wall Street Journal/NORC poll found the share of 
Americans who are positive about their future has fallen to a record 
low. It is the lowest level in the poll's history--the lowest level in 
the poll's history. The sunny American optimism that if I work hard, I 
will be doing better 10 years from now than I am doing today, and my 
kids will be doing still better than me is fading rapidly. Only one in 
four Americans believes they can improve their standard of living, and 
70 percent of Americans believe the American dream no longer holds. 
That is a tragedy. The great, sunny American optimism, which has fueled 
us to greatness, which has pushed us to move forward despite crises and 
obstacles in the way, is dying.
  This is a tragedy, and it is a warning, and most of all, it reflects 
how Americans feel under Donald Trump's leadership, and it is hard to 
blame Americans who feel this way. When rents go up, when mortgages go 
up, when the electric bill goes up--we just talked about what they are 
doing with offshore wind--and when groceries get more expensive, people 
don't want a President obsessed with military parades and winning the 
Nobel Peace Prize. They want someone with real solutions.
  Donald Trump is stuck in his own little fantasy world. There is even 
another study that says Americans now think they are going to be 
spending less on Christmas goods this year, less on Christmas gifts. 
That is when you know something is deeply, deeply wrong, because 
Americans want to spend more for their children, for their

[[Page S6045]]

families, for their friends, and for their coworkers when it comes to 
Christmastime, to buy gifts.
  And it is no accident why we are here. A lot of Donald Trump's 
policies have pushed us here. His tariffs are sending prices up. They 
are not creating jobs. His ``Big Ugly Bill,'' which he is desperately 
trying to rebrand, made the biggest cuts to American healthcare in 
history, and it took away food from hungry kids to give tax breaks to 
billionaires. These are acts of betrayal. That is why this Big 
Beautiful Bill--they want to rename it, but the American people know 
what it is. It is a big ugly betrayal about everything Donald Trump 
promised.
  Donald Trump's actions steal people's hope. It is like the air being 
sucked out of your lungs. And while parents pay more for school 
supplies, Donald Trump's family is making billions off crypto schemes 
while small businesses are trying to make sense of his tariffs. He uses 
the White House lawn as a Tesla dealership. While honest Americans 
watch manufacturing jobs disappear in red States, Trump is handing 
government jobs to his cronies and his sycophants. While people see 
their healthcare premiums go up, he is enriching his billionaire 
friends with massive tax breaks.
  This is a not a golden age. This is not American greatness. This is 
not what the American people signed up for.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
