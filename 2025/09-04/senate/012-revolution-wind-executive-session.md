# Revolution Wind (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                            Revolution Wind

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, this morning, I spoke with Revolution 
Wind, informing me that they are suing the Trump administration over 
their attacks on offshore wind projects. The suit is a regrettable but 
necessary step, illustrating the deep damage Donald Trump's 
administration is causing to American energy and to U.S. jobs.
  Donald Trump has made no secret he detests offshore wind strategy--
and for no good reason. He doesn't like the way the windmills look. 
Someone built windmills off the coast of his golf course in Scotland, 
and he thought it was ugly. So what Donald Trump will do because of 
these whims is raise everybody's electricity prices dramatically. When 
your electric bill goes up, it is because of Donald Trump's whims. This 
one is ridiculous as 80 percent--or over 80 percent of it has been 
built. Billions of dollars have been sunk into the ground--and they 
call up, and they say stop, and they don't even give them a reason.
  How can so many people go along with his idiotic behavior? How can so 
many people go along when Donald Trump simply has a whim that is just 
dumb, stupid, and harmful to the American people and no one does 
anything about it? Then he has all his minions in the various Agencies 
shut it off. People's electric bills are spiking. Our energy grid is 
strained. Everyone admits we need more energy, particularly as AI sucks 
up so much of it, and Donald Trump is making things worse based on no 
logic--based on a ridiculous whim.
  So we are going to lose thousands of jobs; prices will go up; we 
won't expand American power; China will gain on us. So it is good that 
Revolution Wind is taking this step by going to court. But the idiocy 
of this policy has not been rebutted by anyone in the administration. 
They just go, ``Yes, sir,'' and go along. It is a disgrace.
```
