# Senate Accomplishments (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         Senate Accomplishments

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I spent the last 4 weeks traveling all 
around the great State of Wyoming--from Cheyenne to Cody--and visited 
with people and families from all across the State. There was the State 
fair in Douglas, the Dubois firefighters' picnic, the River Festival 
and Shrimp Boil in Green River, WY, and pancake festivals in New Castle 
and Meeteetse.
  One year ago, at events like this all around Wyoming, Wyoming 
families told me that they were very concerned because America, as they 
saw it, was heading in the wrong direction. They were really stressed 
about paying high prices a year ago. They were very angry about the 
wide open southern border. They were very anxious about what could have 
been a $4 trillion tax increase had the Democrats gotten their way. 
Well, Democrats have been the party of high prices and open borders, so 
it is no surprise that people in my home State and in the Presiding 
Officer's were so very concerned. They were concerned because they saw 
our country and their future. They were less safe and less prosperous. 
They were facing a grim reality--but, oh, what a difference a year 
makes.
  Today, America is heading in the right direction. America is back on 
track. It is what I heard in every corner of Wyoming and what Americans 
are seeing all across the Nation.
  On Labor Day, just this past weekend, gas prices were at a 5-year low 
for Labor Day gas prices. Small business optimism is at the highest in 
5 months. Half a million new jobs have been created. Real wages 
continue to go up. Inflation is staying low, and we are repeatedly 
beating the economists' expectations.
  Here in Washington, DC, a city known for its crime--now this city is 
safer and cleaner thanks to President Trump's commonsense policies. The 
American people were right to trust President Trump and Republicans in 
Congress to get America back on track. This is the beginning of the 
great American comeback.
  The Republicans' working families tax cuts law is going to continue 
to make historic progress. Wyoming families are going to be saving 
$2,400 this year because of the tax cuts. Now, that is according to a 
new analysis by the Tax Foundation. That is money back in people's 
pockets, paychecks, purses. That is where it belongs. This law cuts 
taxes on tips. No tax on tips puts an extra $1,300 back into the 
pockets of waiters, barbers, bartenders. The law also cuts taxes on 
overtime. No taxes on overtime puts an extra $1,400 back into the 
paychecks of nurses, firefighters, police officers. The child tax 
credit is higher. This saves working families $2,200 per child per 
year. Seniors are going to pay less in taxes this year because of a new 
$6,000 deduction on Social Security benefits.
  The bottom line is this: Americans are going to keep more of what 
they earn. This is going to send less of the hard-earned money to 
Washington, DC, and Wyoming people clearly understand the meaning of 
wasteful Washington spending.
  At the Wyoming State Fair in Douglas, I joined Wyoming families in 
celebrating our State's rich heritage and history. Republicans' new law 
supports our farmers and ranchers. The law's disaster assistance helps 
families whose grazing lands are destroyed by wildfires or by drought. 
Its full expensing of up to $2.5 million for new equipment is also 
going to help grow businesses. The change in our law to the death tax? 
Well, that is going to ease families' burdens and our worries, 
specifically providing certainty for families into the future. These 
policies make our farmers and our ranchers more prosperous and help 
feed the Nation.
  Not a single Democrat voted for these popular policies--policies that 
are making America safer and more prosperous--not a single one. Every 
single Democrat voted, instead, to raise taxes by $4 trillion. That 
would have been the largest tax increase in American history. So it is 
no surprise Democrats are putting the fear before the facts when it 
comes to this law.
  Just yesterday, Senator Schumer came to the floor and falsely claimed 
that the law doesn't help our rural hospitals. I need to set the record 
straight on that. Republicans created a $50 billion hospital--rural 
hospital--fund. It is specifically for rural hospitals. The Presiding 
Officer is from a rural hospital State, as am I. I am a doctor. I 
practiced medicine in Wyoming for two decades. I was the president of 
our Wyoming State Medical Society. I was the chief of staff of the 
hospital in which I practiced. I am in close contact with the hospitals 
and the doctors in my State.
  Since ObamaCare became law over a decade ago, more than 100 rural 
hospitals have closed across the country. Many more are now at risk 
today. This $50 billion rural hospital fund means real relief for our 
Nation's rural hospitals. Yet every Democrat in this body voted against 
helping rural hospitals--every one of them. Democrats in this very body 
even introduced legislation in July to eliminate this $50 billion rural 
hospital fund.
  Senator Schumer came to this very floor, and he bragged about the 
introduction of this bill that would hurt rural hospitals. At the same 
time, Democrats voted to continue to give free--free--taxpayer-funded 
healthcare to illegal immigrants. That compounded the disaster of 4 
years of Joe Biden's open border policies.
  Well, President Trump took immediate action--action to secure the 
border, action to end the chaos, action to protect American citizens. 
This message is now clear around the world: Don't come here and expect 
to get through our border if you are illegally trying to come here.
  Illegal crossings are now at record lows. For 3 months in a row, zero 
illegal immigrants were released into our country--zero. Republicans in 
Congress went even further with our recent bill. We are finishing the 
wall at the southern border, hiring more border agents, and investing 
in Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
  From cutting taxes to securing the border, America is safer, 
stronger, and more prosperous today than it was a year ago. This is all 
the result of the work of President Trump and Republicans in Congress.
  The people of Wyoming feel the difference, and they are not alone. 
People all across America see that our Nation is getting back on track.
```
