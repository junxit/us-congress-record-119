# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1038

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1038. An act to direct the Attorney General to include a 
     data field in the National Missing and Unidentified System to 
     indicate whether the last known location of a missing person 
     was confirmed or was suspected to have been on Federal land, 
     and for other purposes.
```
