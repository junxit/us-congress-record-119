           By Mr. CASSIDY (for himself, Mrs. Blackburn, Ms. Ernst,

     to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.

       S. 3115. A bill to amend the National Labor Relations Act

     Labor, and Pensions.

     Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
