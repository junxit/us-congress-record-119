# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, thank You for Your love, expressed in the beauty of the 
Earth and the glory of the skies.
  Lord, use the Members of this body as instruments of Your providence. 
Where there is loneliness, let them bring community. Where there is 
sadness, let them bring joy. Where there is sickness, let them bring 
health. Where there is despair, let them bring hope.
  Lord, as they seek to serve You, give them the peaceful satisfaction 
of knowing that they are pleasing You. Strengthen them to press on with 
the work of the day, alert to the reality that they are doing Your 
will.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
