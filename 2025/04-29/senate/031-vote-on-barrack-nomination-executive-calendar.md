**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                       Vote on Barrack Nomination

  Mr. RISCH. I ask unanimous consent that we move immediately to the 
vote on the two nominations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Barrack 
nomination?
  Mr. RISCH. I ask for the yeas and nays.

Hickenlooper), the Senator from Maine (Mr. King), the Senator from 
Vermont (Mr. Sanders), and the Senator from Rhode Island (Mr.
```
