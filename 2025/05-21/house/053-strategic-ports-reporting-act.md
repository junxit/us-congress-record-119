# STRATEGIC PORTS REPORTING ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 1701

```
                     STRATEGIC PORTS REPORTING ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Moran). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, 
the unfinished business is the question on suspending the rules and 
passing the bill (H.R. 1701) to require the Secretary of Defense and 
the Secretary of State to monitor efforts by the Peoples Republic of 
China to build or buy strategic foreign ports, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Michigan (Mr. Huizenga) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
