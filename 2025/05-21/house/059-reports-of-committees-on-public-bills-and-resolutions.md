# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     2987. A bill to amend the Small Business Act to require a 
     limit on the number of small business lending companies, and 
     for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-107). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     1163. A bill to amend title 5, United States Code, to require 
     greater transparency for Federal regulatory decisions that 
     impact small businesses, and for other purposes; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 119-108, Pt. 1). Ordered to be printed.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     2027. A bill to require the Administrator of the Small 
     Business Administration to relocate 30 percent of the 
     employees assigned to headquarters to duty stations outside 
     the Washington metropolitan area, and for other purposes; 
     with an amendment (Rept. 119-109). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     2931. A bill to direct the Administrator of the Small 
     Business Administration to relocate certain offices of the 
     Small Business Administration in sanctuary jurisdictions, and 
     for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-110). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     2965. A bill to require the Administrator of the Small 
     Business Administration to ensure that the small business 
     regulatory budget for a small business concern in a fiscal 
     year is not greater than zero, and for other purposes; with 
     an amendment (Rept. 119-111). Referred to the Committee of 
     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     2966. A bill to require the Administrator of the Small 
     Business Administration to require an applicant for certain 
     loans of the Administration to provide certain citizenship 
     status documentation, and for other purposes; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 119-112). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mrs. HOUCHIN: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 436. 
     Resolution providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1) 
     to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
     Res. 14 (Rept. 119-113). Referred to the House Calendar.
```
