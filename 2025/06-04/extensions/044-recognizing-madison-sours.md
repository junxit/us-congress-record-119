# RECOGNIZING MADISON SOURS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MADISON SOURS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 4, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Madison Sours 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Madison has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Madison, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Madison's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Three Creeks K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Madison Sours on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
