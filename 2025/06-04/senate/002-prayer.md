# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, how excellent is Your Name in all the Earth.
  As our Senators face today's difficulties, help them to trust in Your 
goodness. May their faith in You prompt them to wait for the triumph of 
Your prevailing providence.
  Lord, remind them that all things work together for good to those who 
love You and strive to do Your will. Continue to be their refuge and 
ever-present help for life's valleys. Renew their strength, enabling 
them to run and not be weary, to walk and not faint.
  Lord, infuse them with a reverential awe that will strengthen them to 
honor You with their thoughts, words, and deeds.
  We pray in Your magnificent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
