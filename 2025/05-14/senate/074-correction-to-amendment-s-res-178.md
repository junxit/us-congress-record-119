**Measures:** S.Res. 178

```
                  CORRECTION TO AMENDMENT--S. RES. 178

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that, 
notwithstanding the adoption of S. Res. 178,

the Cornyn amendment to the preamble which is at the desk be agreed to 
and the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table
```
