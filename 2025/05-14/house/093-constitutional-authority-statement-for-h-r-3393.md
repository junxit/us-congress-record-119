# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3393

**Measures:** H.R. 3393

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 3393.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United 
     States Constitution, which grants Congress the power ``To 
     establish Post Offices and post Roads.''
```
