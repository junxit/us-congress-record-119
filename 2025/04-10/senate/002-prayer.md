# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our shelter in the time of storm, use the Members of 
this body to accomplish Your will, as they permit honesty to guide 
them. Lord, strengthen them to never abandon the struggle, and inspire 
them to endure to the end. Help them to press forward to the goal of 
Your ideal for humanity. May they refuse to take the easy path as they 
strive to stay on the right road. Remind them that perspiration is 
usually the price for accomplishing laudable goals and that without the 
cross, there is rarely a crown. Lord, keep and sustain our lawmakers by 
Your grace.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
