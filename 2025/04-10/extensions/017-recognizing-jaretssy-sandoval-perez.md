# RECOGNIZING JARETSSY SANDOVAL-PEREZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                  RECOGNIZING JARETSSY SANDOVAL-PEREZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 10, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jaretssy 
Sandoval-Perez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors 
for Youth Award.
  Jaretssy has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Jaretssy, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Jaretssy's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning 
of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Jaretssy Sandoval-Perez on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
