# Moment of Silence in Remembrance of Charlie Kirk (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH], Warren, Elizabeth (W000817) [D-MA]

```
            Moment of Silence in Remembrance of Charlie Kirk

  Madam President, I ask unanimous consent for a moment of silence.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  (Moment of Silence.)
  Mr. MORENO. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Ms. WARREN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
