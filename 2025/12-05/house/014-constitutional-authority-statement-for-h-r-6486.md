# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6486

**Measures:** H.R. 6486

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 6486.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ``Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 2, Clause 3 of the Constitution, which 
     provides Congress authority to direct the manner of 
     conducting the decennial census.''
```
