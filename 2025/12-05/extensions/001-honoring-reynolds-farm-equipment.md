# HONORING REYNOLDS FARM EQUIPMENT

**Speaking:** Spartz, Victoria (S000929) [R-IN]

```
                    HONORING REYNOLDS FARM EQUIPMENT

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. VICTORIA SPARTZ

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, December 5, 2025

  Mrs. SPARTZ. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize Reynolds Farm 
Equipment of Indiana as it celebrates 70 years of service to Hoosier 
agriculture. For seven decades, the Reynolds family has supported 
Indiana farmers--advancing technology, strengthening rural communities, 
and helping sustain one of our state's most vital industries.
  Since 1955, Reynolds has provided dependable machinery, service, and 
innovation. By 1959, they had already grown into the largest John Deere 
dealer in Indiana, later expanding into lawn and garden equipment and 
earning Farm Equipment Magazine's 2018 North American Dealer of the 
Year award. Founded by A.W. ``Mac'' and Arline Reynolds in Fishers, the 
business has grown from a family dealership into a respected multi-
location operation.
  Gary Reynolds, who joined in 1969, helped guide that growth and was 
inducted last year into the Indiana Historical Society. The company has 
also supported Conner Prairie and other living history organizations, 
ensuring Indiana's agricultural heritage is preserved and shared.
  Today, Reynolds Farm Equipment remains a leading John Deere dealer 
while maintaining strong family values and a commitment to community 
philanthropy. Their work continues to strengthen Indiana agriculture 
now and for the future.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Reynolds Farm 
Equipment and the Reynolds family on 70 years of excellence.
```
