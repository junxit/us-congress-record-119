# CONGRATULATING THE GALENA PARK NORTH SHORE MUSTANGS

**Speaking:** Garcia, Sylvia R. (G000587) [D-TX]

```
          CONGRATULATING THE GALENA PARK NORTH SHORE MUSTANGS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SYLVIA R. GARCIA

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 23, 2025

  Ms. GARCIA of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the 
Galena Park North Shore Mustangs on winning the UIL Class 6A Division I 
State Championship on December 20, 2025, an extraordinary achievement 
for Texas's 29th Congressional District.
  On the biggest stage in Texas high school football, the Mustangs 
earned a state championship through discipline, preparation, and belief 
in one another. This victory marks the program's sixth state title and 
a return to the top, reaffirming North Shore's tradition of excellence.
  This championship reflects months of hard work, and a season defined 
by focus and resilience. It is a testament to the commitment of these 
student-athletes and the pride they bring to their school and 
community.
  The Galena Park North Shore Mustangs have once again given Texas 29 a 
moment to celebrate. Their achievement will be remembered not only for 
the title itself, but for what it represents about teamwork, 
perseverance, and community pride.

                          ____________________
```
