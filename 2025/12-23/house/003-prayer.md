# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of all time and of all people, we pause in this moment to offer 
You our prayers of Thanksgiving for this season and to lay before You 
our prayers for ourselves, for our neighbors, and for our Nation in the 
living of these days.
  Allow us to emerge from any darkness brought on by despair and walk 
instead with faith. In the depth of our night, shine Your light.
  Increase our joy as You increase a harvest of peace in our lives and 
in our world. Of the increase of Your divine governance and Your peace, 
may there be no end.
  For upon Your shoulder rests the government of truth and love, of 
justice and righteousness.
  May all who call You Lord heed Your commandments. May all who call 
upon Your name live according to Your Gospel of love.
  Wonderful counselor, mighty God, everlasting Father, Prince of Peace, 
rule in our hearts this day.
  In Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
