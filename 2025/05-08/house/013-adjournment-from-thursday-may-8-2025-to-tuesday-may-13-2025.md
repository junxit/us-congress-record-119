# ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, MAY 8, 2025 TO TUESDAY, MAY 13, 2025

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

```
    ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, MAY 8, 2025 TO TUESDAY, MAY 13, 2025

  Mr. STEIL. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 1 p.m. tomorrow, and further, 
when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at noon on 
Tuesday, May 13, 2025, for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for 
legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Biggs of South Carolina). Is there 
objection to the request of the gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
```
