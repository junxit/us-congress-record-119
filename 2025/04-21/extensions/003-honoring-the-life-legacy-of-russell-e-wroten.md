# HONORING THE LIFE & LEGACY OF RUSSELL E. WROTEN

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE & LEGACY OF RUSSELL E. WROTEN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                                maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 21, 2025

  Mr. HARRIS of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, it was with great sadness that I 
learned about the passing of Russell E. Wroten of East New Market, 
Maryland.
  Russell E. Wroten served as Chief of Police for the City of Cambridge 
from 1971 until he retired in 2001. His years of service were marked by 
bravery and dedication, and his accolades include the Law Enforcement 
Commendation Medal, Wounded Combat Medal, an Exceptional Service Award, 
and Governor's Citations. Russell was well respected for his unwavering 
commitment to his officers and community. In 2024, Mr. Wroten was 
honored with being the first recipient to be inducted into the 
Cambridge Police Department Hall of Fame.
  Chief Wroten passed away at the age of 84 years old on Monday, April 
14, 2025, in Easton, Maryland. He is survived by his wife of 62 years, 
Regina Wroten of East New Market; their daughter, Christina (Chrissy) 
and son-in-law, Tom Thompson of Cambridge; granddaughter, Savana (Jon) 
Linthicum of

[[Page E328]]

Rhodesdale; very special great-granddaughter, Arya; and several nephews 
and a niece. Besides his parents, Mr. Wroten is preceded in death by 
his brothers; Matthew and George Wroten.
  The Eastern Shore is forever grateful for Chief Wroten's commitment 
to service and his dedication to Maryland.
```
