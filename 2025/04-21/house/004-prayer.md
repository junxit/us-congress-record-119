# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Dr. Edrees Bridges, Chaplain, United States Capitol Police, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  O merciful benefactor, merciful redeemer, ground us in this solemn 
moment.
  Bestow clarity upon our congressional leaders. May their voices and 
votes strengthen the pillars of democracy.
  Remain present in their hearts and minds, even when the path 
diverges, and allow their presence to reflect the strength of 
principled conviction. Grant them ears for active listening to the 
calls for collaboration and the voices of the people whom they serve.
  Let them carry their responsibilities with humility, for within these 
walls resides the weight of a nation's will.
  May they be a symbol of hope through triumph and trial.
  Grant them strength amid uncertainty and creativity to shape a more 
perfect union.
  O merciful, You fashioned us differently to inspire a shared purpose, 
so let unity be our highest pursuit.
  Unite us as we humbly submit to Your eternal guidance for the good of 
our country and good for humanity.
  We ask this with Your name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
