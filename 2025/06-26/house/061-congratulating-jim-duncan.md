# CONGRATULATING JIM DUNCAN

**Speaking:** Downing, Troy (D000634) [R-MT]

```
                              {time}  1550
                       CONGRATULATING JIM DUNCAN

  (Mr. DOWNING asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DOWNING. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my dear 
friend, Jim Duncan, on his well-earned retirement as president of the 
Billings Clinic Foundation.
  For three decades, Jim served the great folks of the Treasure State, 
advocating tirelessly for rural healthcare access and the expansion of 
community wellness programs. Jim grew the foundation's endowments from 
$3 million to an astounding $150 million, funds that will benefit the 
community for generations to come.
  Jim also played a key role in introducing innovation and excellence 
in Montana health services, helping to bring new cancer, cardiac, 
pediatric, psychiatric, and trauma care to eastern Montana.
  Jim's advocacy will be missed, but I know the foundation will be left 
in

[[Page H2997]]

good hands with Nichole Mehling at the helm.
  Jim led with integrity, vision, and compassion. I wish him and his 
wife, Heidi, a well-deserved, adventure-filled retirement.

                          ____________________
```
