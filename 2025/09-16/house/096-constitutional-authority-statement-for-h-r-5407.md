# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5407

**Measures:** H.R. 5407

```
           By Mr. MORELLE:
       H.R. 5407.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has broad authority to regulate the time, place, 
     and manner of congressional elections under the Elections 
     Clause of the U.S. Constitution (Article I, Section 4, Clause 
     1).
```
