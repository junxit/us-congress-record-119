              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. CORNYN (for himself, Ms. Hirono, Ms. Murkowski, 
             Mr. Fetterman, Mr. Scott of Florida, and Mr. Schiff):
       S. 2807. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     modify the applicability of the authority to reconsider 
     decisions of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs or the 
     Secretary of the Army to inter remains or honor the memory of 
     a person in a national cemetery, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.

     purposes; to the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and 
     Forestry.
