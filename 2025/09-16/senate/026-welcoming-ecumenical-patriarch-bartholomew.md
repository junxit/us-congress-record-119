# WELCOMING ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

```
               WELCOMING ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW

  Mr. HUSTED. Mr. President, today I would like to recognize His All-
Holiness Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew's visit to Washington, DC. 
His All-Holiness is visiting our Nation's Capital in conjunction with 
his acceptance of the prestigious Templeton Prize, an award which has 
been received by faith leaders including Mother Teresa, the Dalai Lama, 
and Archbishop Desmond Tutu. The Greek Orthodox community in Ohio and 
across the country take great pride in this recognition of their faith 
leader's global influence.
```
