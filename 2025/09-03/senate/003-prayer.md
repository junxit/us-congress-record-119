# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Spirit, the fountain of all wisdom, we bring our fragmented 
lives into Your presence, seeking Your wholeness. We bring our restless 
spirits to You, seeking Your calm strength and everlasting purpose. 
Lord, we bring You our transient thoughts, seeking the permanence of 
Your gracious providence.
  Remind our lawmakers that only as we lose ourselves in something 
higher, can we truly find ourselves. To this end, give them great 
causes to embrace and a great faith to energize their work.
  Lord, lead them from doubt and disillusionment, from cynicism and 
frustration to a confidence that, in everything, You are working for 
the good of those who love You, who are called according to Your 
purposes.
  We pray in your Holy Name. Amen.
```
