# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3498

**Measures:** H.R. 3498

```
           By Mr. MFUME:
       H.R. 3498.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause, 6 empowers Congress to coin 
     money. The U.S. Treasury through the United States Mint has 
     historically exercised its power over coinage to strike 
     national medals.
```
