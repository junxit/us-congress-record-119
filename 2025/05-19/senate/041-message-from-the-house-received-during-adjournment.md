# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 146

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2025, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on May 16, 2025, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 146. An act to require covered platforms to remove 
     nonconsensual intimate visual depictions, and for other 
     purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2025, 
the enrolled bill was signed on May 19, 2025, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, by the President pro tempore (Mr. Grassley).

                          ____________________
```
