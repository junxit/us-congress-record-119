# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Zack Randles, Waterfront Church, Washington, D.C., offered the 
following prayer:
  Awesome and Almighty God, we come before You today remembering the 
powerful principle outlined in Mark 1:35-38.
  It says: ``Very early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus 
got up, left the house, and went off to a solitary place where he 
prayed. Simon and his companions went to look for him. When they found 
him, they exclaimed: Everyone is looking for You.''
  God, give us the courage and wisdom to follow the example of Jesus. 
In the midst of endless demands, may we find time early in the morning 
to be with You and to seek Your will for our days. Through that time, 
may You restore us and strengthen us for what lies ahead. Time with You 
reminds us that we are not abandoned in our journey. Although You have 
placed great responsibilities on our shoulders, we do not carry this 
burden alone.
  On a number of occasions, King David calls You his fortress, his 
hiding place, and his refuge. Thank You, God, for being a place we can 
run to. Truly, there is none like You and none beside You.
  May Your blessing and favor rest upon the Members of this body, their 
teams, their families, and our country. I pray this in the mighty name 
of Jesus.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
