# RULES AND REPORTS SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO THE CONGRESSIONAL REVIEW ACT

```
  RULES AND REPORTS SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO THE CONGRESSIONAL REVIEW ACT

       Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 801(d), executive communications 
     [final rules] submitted to the House pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 
     801(a)(1) during the period of August 16, 2024, through 
     January 3, 2025, shall be treated as though received on 
     January 31, 2025. Original dates of transmittal, numberings, 
     and referrals to committee of those executive communications 
     remain as indicated in the Executive Communication section of 
     the relevant Congressional Record.

                          ____________________
```
