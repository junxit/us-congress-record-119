# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2054

**Measures:** H.R. 2054

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 2054.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4 of the Constitution (the Elections 
     Clause), which gives Congress authority to make or alter 
     regulations for federal elections
       Article I, Section 8 (the Spending Clause), which gives 
     Congress authority to provide for the general welfare and 
     attach conditions to federal funding
```
