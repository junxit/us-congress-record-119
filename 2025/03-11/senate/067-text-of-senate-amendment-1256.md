# Text of Senate Amendment 1256

**Speaking:** Hawley, Josh (H001089) [R-MO]

```
  SA 1256. Mr. HAWLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 331, to amend the Controlled Substances Act with 
respect to the scheduling of fentanyl-related substances, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. REAUTHORIZATION.

       Section 1001(a)(21) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control 
     and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10261(a)(21)) is 
     amended by striking ``2020 through 2024'' and inserting 
     ``2025 through 2029''.
                                 ______
```
