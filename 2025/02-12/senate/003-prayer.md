# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of mystery and clarity, open our eyes to see the unexpected ways 
You come to us. Lord, reveal to us Your presence in the beauties of 
nature and the promises of Sacred Scriptures and in the challenges that 
deepen our dependence on You.
  Make clear Your plans to our lawmakers and infuse them with 
confidence in Your power. Inspire them to use their talents as 
instruments of liberation and healing. Lord, keep them purposeful and 
expectant so they will experience a deeper friendship with You in the 
living of their days.
  We pray in Your abiding Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
