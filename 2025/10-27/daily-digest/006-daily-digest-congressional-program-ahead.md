# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 287, S. 1884, S. 2082, S. 2110, S. 2235, S. 2319, S. 2741, S. 2878, S. 3022, H.R. 5371

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of October 28 through October 31, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the 
motion to proceed to consideration of H.R. 5371, Continuing 
Appropriations and Extensions Act, upon reconsideration, and on the 
motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Jordan Emery Pratt, of 
Florida, to be United States District Judge for the Middle District of 
Florida. If cloture is invoked on the nomination, Senate will vote on 
confirmation thereon at 2:20 p.m.
  Following disposition of the nomination of Jordan Emery Pratt, Senate 
will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Edmund 
G. LaCour, Jr., of Alabama, to be United States District Judge for the 
Northern District of Alabama. If cloture is invoked on the nomination, 
Senate will vote on confirmation thereon after a period of debate.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
```
