# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our King, when our children and grandchildren want to 
know what we were doing in the 119th Congress during the famous 
shutdown, may we not have to give these answers: I helped set a new 
record for keeping the government closed. I failed to appeal to the 
better angels of my nature. I forgot Matthew 7:12, which states:

       Do to others whatever you would like them to do to you.

  This is the essence of all that is taught in the law and the 
prophets.
  Lord, remind our lawmakers that no gold medals are given for breaking 
shutdown records, but a crown of righteousness is given to those who 
take care of the lost, last, and least.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
