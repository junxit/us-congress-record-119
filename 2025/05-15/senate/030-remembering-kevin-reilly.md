# REMEMBERING KEVIN REILLY

**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL]

```
                        REMEMBERING KEVIN REILLY

● Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Mr. President, I rise today to honor the 
life of Kevin Reilly who recently passed away. Kevin graduated from 
Palm Harbor University High School in Pinellas County and was a proud 
Florida Gator, where he served as student body president at the 
University of Florida. Kevin spent much of his professional life in 
service to the State of Florida where he had key roles in the Florida 
legislature, State agencies, Congress, and in my Gubernatorial 
administration. As my legislative affairs director when I was Governor, 
Kevin was a key part of our success and helped us pass critical 
legislation to make Florida the best State to live, work, and raise a 
family. He fought for our tourism industry, championed policies to grow 
Florida's economy and spearheaded efforts to ensure Florida remained a 
safe place for every family. Kevin was a caring son, brother, friend, 
and colleague. Today, we honor his life and the incredible impact he 
made on our State, Nation, and all those who knew him.●

                          ____________________
```
