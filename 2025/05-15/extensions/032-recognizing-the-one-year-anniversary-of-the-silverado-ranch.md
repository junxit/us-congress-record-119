# RECOGNIZING THE ONE-YEAR ANNIVERSARY OF THE SILVERADO RANCH COMMUNITY CENTER IN CLARK COUNTY, NEVADA

**Speaking:** Lee, Susie (L000590) [D-NV]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE ONE-YEAR ANNIVERSARY OF THE SILVERADO RANCH COMMUNITY 
                     CENTER IN CLARK COUNTY, NEVADA

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. SUSIE LEE

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 15, 2025

  Ms. LEE of Nevada. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and celebrate the 
one-year anniversary of the Silverado Ranch Community Center in Clark 
County, Nevada.
  Since opening its doors, the Silverado Ranch Community Center has 
become a cornerstone of engagement, learning, and well-being for 
families and residents of all ages. From youth programs and senior 
activities to public meetings and fitness classes, the Center has 
served as a vital hub for connection and opportunity in the growing 
southeast area of the Las Vegas Valley.
  This achievement would not have been possible without the foresight 
and commitment of the Clark County Commission, whose leadership 
continues to invest in high-quality community infrastructure that 
enhances the lives of local residents. Their support reflects the 
values of accessibility, inclusion, and forward-thinking civic 
development.

[[Page E439]]

  I would also like to extend heartfelt gratitude to the dedicated 
staff of the Silverado Ranch Community Center. Their tireless efforts, 
professionalism, and welcoming spirit have made this first year a 
resounding success.
  As we celebrate this anniversary, let it serve as a testament to what 
can be accomplished when local government, public servants, and 
community members come together with a shared vision. This is a great 
milestone in our community.
  I look forward to many more years of growth, service, and celebration 
at Silverado Ranch.

                          ____________________
```
