# STAND UP FOR THE RULE OF LAW

**Speaking:** Min, Dave (M001241) [D-CA]

```
                      STAND UP FOR THE RULE OF LAW

  (Mr. MIN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to remind my colleagues that 
Congress and only Congress has the power of the purse. Yet, Donald 
Trump and Elon Musk have unlawfully impounded billions of dollars of 
congressionally approved funds. They have illegally slashed funding for 
veterans, food for hungry babies, and purported to terminate entire 
agencies created by Congress. This is not consistent with the 
Constitution or with democracy.
  Trump and Musk are asserting the powers of dictatorship. 
Unfortunately, our Republican colleagues have refused to enforce the 
law or the Constitution when it comes to this administration.
  That is why I, along with Representatives Liccardo and Vindman, 
introduced the Protect Our Constitution and Communities Act, or POCCA.
  POCCA affirms the separation of budgetary powers and creates a 
private right action for individuals, States, and local jurisdictions 
that have been harmed by the administration's repeated violations of 
the ICA.
  POCCA would also hold accountable any public official with 
decisionmaking authority who knowingly and intentionally violates the 
ICA, such as Elon Musk.
  It is time to stand up for the rule of law and to put the power of 
the purse back where it belongs, in the hands of Congress.
  This National Police Week, I urge my colleagues to consider 
supporting this important law enforcement priority.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to refrain from 
engaging in personalities toward the President.

                          ____________________
```
