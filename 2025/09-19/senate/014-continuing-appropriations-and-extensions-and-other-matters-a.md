                           Order of Procedure

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, notwithstanding rule XXII, I ask unanimous 
consent that following the vote on passage of H.R. 5371, the cloture 
motion filed during yesterday's session of the Senate on Executive 
Calendar No. 410 ripen immediately; further, I ask that the mandatory 
quorum call with respect to the Waltz nomination be waived; and that if 
cloture is invoked, all debate time be expired and the Senate vote 
immediately on confirmation of the nomination; finally, that if 
confirmed, the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table and the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action.
  I ask unanimous consent that these votes be 10-minute votes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
