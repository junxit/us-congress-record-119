# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, thank You for Your sanctifying truth. Use our lawmakers 
to live Your truth for the glory of Your Name. May Your truth enable us 
to strive to have a strong faith, a pure love, and a focused obedience. 
Lord, empower us to submit to You with our bodies, minds, and spirits. 
Make us all vessels of honor; prepare for every good work. And, Lord, 
as the Jewish New Year, Rosh Hashana, approaches, may all people of 
faith chase the fruit of Your mercy and grace. Lead us to reflection, 
repentance, and renewal.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
