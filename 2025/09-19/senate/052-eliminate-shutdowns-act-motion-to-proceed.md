# ELIMINATE SHUTDOWNS ACT--Motion to Proceed

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 2806

```
               ELIMINATE SHUTDOWNS ACT--Motion to Proceed

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 161, S. 
2806.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2806) to provide for automatic continuing 
     appropriations.

                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 161, S. 2806, a bill to provide for 
     automatic continuing appropriations.
         John Thune, Bernie Moreno, Mike Crapo, Chuck Grassley, 
           Ashley B. Moody, Markwayne Mullin, John Barrasso, Tim 
           Sheehy, Pete Ricketts, Ted Budd, Bill Hagerty, John R. 
           Curtis, David McCormick, Tim Scott of South Carolina, 
           John Cornyn, Cynthia M. Lummis, Steve Daines.
```
