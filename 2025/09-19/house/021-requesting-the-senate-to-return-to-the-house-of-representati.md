# REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES H.R. 3426

**Speaking:** Cole, Tom (C001053) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.Res. 747, H.R. 3426

```
 REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES H.R. 
                                  3426

  Mr. COLE. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kennedy of Utah). The Clerk will report 
the resolution.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 747

       Resolved, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
     request the Senate to return to the House the bill (H.R. 
     3426) entitled ``To amend title 40, United States Code, to 
     limit the construction of new courthouses under certain 
     circumstances, and for other purposes.''

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the resolution was agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
