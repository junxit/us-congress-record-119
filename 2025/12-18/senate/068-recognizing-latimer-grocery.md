# RECOGNIZING LATIMER GROCERY

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING LATIMER GROCERY

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Latimer 
Grocery of Franklin County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Founded in 2008, Latimer Grocery began with a straightforward mission 
to ensure that communities have access to a dependable, local grocery 
store. At a time when rural areas increasingly faced the risk of 
becoming food deserts, founder Landon Plagge, along with a committed 
group of community members, stepped in to fill a critical need. Their 
efforts created more than a business; they created a place where 
neighbors could reliably find the essentials that supported their daily 
lives.
  In 2020, ownership transitioned to a group of eight families, which 
includes Karla and Doug Peter, Mark and LuAnn Johansen, Roy Plagge, 
Delray and Barb Farrer, Tyler and Sarah DeBour, Randy and Sue Debour, 
Kent and Chris Morton, and Scott and Jennifer Borcherding. The families 
embraced the store's community-centered foundation while guiding its 
continued growth. Under their leadership, Latimer Grocery has 
strengthened its role as a hometown hub, offering the trusted service 
customers have come to expect. They also expanded partnerships with 
local producers to provide groceries, baked goods, and a full deli. 
Today, customers can purchase locally raised meats, area-grown grains, 
and locally produced bakery goods from Sheffield, IA, or products from 
The Olde Creamery in Garner, IA. The deli remains at the heart of 
Latimer Grocery. Known for hot and cold sandwiches, it also serves 
seasonal dinner deliveries during harvest, ensuring farmers working 
long hours have access to fresh and warm meals. Weekend pizzas have 
become a favorite among families, and the store's delivery service, 
which is especially valuable for elderly and disabled residents, helps 
ensure that everyone in the area maintains access to quality food. 
These services highlight Latimer Grocery's dedication to meeting the 
evolving needs of the community.
  Latimer Grocery is equally invested in supporting local families and 
children. The store partners with local schools through a reading 
rewards program, giving students coupons for treats based on the number 
of books they read. Each fall, the annual Halloween Spooktacular draws 
families from across the region for a festive evening of costumes, 
games, and free food, showcasing the store's commitment to creating 
positive, memorable experiences for its customers. With a team of seven 
employees and nearly two decades of service, Latimer Grocery stands as 
a testament to resilience, local collaboration, and heartfelt service. 
Under its united leadership, Latimer Grocery remains a cherished 
gathering place and an essential resource, demonstrating how a 
smalltown business can strengthen an entire community through 
dedication and care.
  It is my honor to recognize the entire Latimer Grocery team for their 
outstanding work and dedication to their community. I look forward to 
their continued success and wish them the very best in the years 
ahead.●
```
