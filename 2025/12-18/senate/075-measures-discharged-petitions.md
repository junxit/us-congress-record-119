# MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITIONS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 95

```
                     MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITIONS

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with chapter 8 
     of title 5, United States Code, hereby direct that the Senate 
     Committee on Finance be discharged of further consideration 
     of S.J. Res. 95, a joint resolution providing for 
     congressional disapproval of the rule submitted by the 
     Internal Revenue Service relating to ``Interim Guidance 
     Simplifying Application of the Corporate Alternative Minimum 
     Tax to Partnerships,'' and, further, that the resolution be 
     immediately placed upon the Legislative Calendar under 
     General Orders.
         Ron Wyden, Tim Kaine, Peter Welch, Tina Smith, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, John W. Hickenlooper, Tammy Baldwin, Chris 
           Van Hollen, Andy Kim, Angus S. King, Martin Heinrich, 
           Patty Murray, Jack Reed, Edward J. Markey, Angela D. 
           Alsobrooks, Cory A. Booker, Ben Ray Lujan, Tammy 
           Duckworth, Bernard Sanders, Gary C. Peters, Mark R. 
           Warner, Catherine Cortez Masto, Richard Durbin, Jeanne 
           Shaheen, Elizabeth Warren, Richard Blumenthal, Mazie K. 
           Hirono, Jeff Merkley, Jacky Rosen, Lisa Blunt 
           Rochester, Christopher A. Coons, Alex Padilla.
```
