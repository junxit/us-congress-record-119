# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our help in ages past, keep our lawmakers under the canopy of 
Your care. We do not ask You to separate them from life's stresses, 
strains, and setbacks but to shelter them by Your grace in sunshine and 
shadows. Accompany them in their coming in, their going out, and their 
daily work. Make them instruments to advance Your Kingdom. May no 
weapon formed against them prosper. Lord, encompass them with the 
everlasting arms of Your love, grace, and mercy.
  And, Lord, we praise You for this season of peace and good will to 
humanity.
  We pray in the Name of the Prince of Peace. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
