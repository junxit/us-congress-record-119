# RECOGNIZING SISTER'S HOME STYLE ENTREES

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                RECOGNIZING SISTER'S HOME STYLE ENTREES

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Sister's 
Home Style Entrees of Humboldt County, IA, as the Senate Small Business 
of the Week.
  In 2011, Sister's Home Style Entrees began as a simple passion 
project in the kitchen of founder Deb Davis. What started as Deb 
preparing home-cooked meals for family and friends quickly evolved into 
a service rooted in compassion, quality, and community. Her dedication 
and the growing demand for dependable, nutritious, ready-made meals 
transformed a small hobby into a thriving commercial kitchen operation 
serving customers across Iowa and neighboring States.
  As the company grew, Deb was joined by co-owner Mary Catherine Ferry, 
whose leadership and partnership helped guide Sister's Home Style 
Entrees through major expansions and increased production. Together 
with a dedicated team of 48, they built an operation that now prepares 
over 75,000 meals every month. Their focus on supporting the elderly 
and homebound population has made the small business not just a meal 
provider, but a support system for individuals striving to maintain 
independence. Families, too, have embraced the convenience and 
flexibility of reliable, high-quality frozen meals when they need them.
  Under Deb's vision and Mary's continued leadership, Sister's Home 
Style Entrees has grown into an industrial-scale commercial kitchen 
without ever losing sight of its founding values: service, heart, and 
consistency. The company remains proudly women-owned in Humboldt, IA, 
and embodies the spirit of a close-knit, mission-driven enterprise. As 
Deb transitioned into retirement and ownership passed fully to Mary 
Catherine this past June, the business represents a model of 
thoughtful, well-planned succession, ensuring stability for employees, 
customers, and community partners for years to come.
  Sister's Home Style Entrees has been widely recognized for its 
impact, receiving honors such as the 2015 SBA Iowa Top Small Business 
Award and the 2021 Iowa Venture Award Honoree. The company is also 
known for its generosity, regularly donating meals to food pantries, 
nonprofit organizations, and surrounding communities. Their 
contributions have strengthened local support networks and provided 
vital resources to families facing food insecurity. Sister's Home Style 
Entrees' story reflects the power of community-minded entrepreneurship 
and the lasting difference a mission-driven small business can make in 
the lives of the people it serves.
  It is my honor to recognize Mary Catherine Ferry, Deb Davis, and the 
entire Sister's team for their outstanding work and dedication to their 
community. I look forward to their continued success and wish them the 
very best in the years ahead.●
```
