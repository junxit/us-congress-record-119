# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1000

**Speaking:** Biggs, Sheri (B001325) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 1000

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1000

  Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that I may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 
1000, a bill originally introduced by Representative Green of 
Tennessee, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
