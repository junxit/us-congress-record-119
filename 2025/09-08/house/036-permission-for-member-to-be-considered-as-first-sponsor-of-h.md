# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1510

**Speaking:** Dexter, Maxine (D000635) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 1510

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1510

  Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 1510, the Due Process 
Continuity of Care Act, a bill originally introduced by Representative 
Sylvester Turner of Texas, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and 
requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Oregon?
  There was no objection.
```
