# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5167

**Measures:** H.R. 5167

```
           By Mr. CRAWFORD:
       H.R. 5167.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which states, in part that Congress shall have the power to 
     ``provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States. . .'' and ``to make all laws which shall be 
     necessary and proper for carying into execution the foregoing 
     powers.''
```
