# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, teach us the mystery of life. Help us to not be victims 
but victorious in the living of our days. Lead us to a place of 
understanding in spite of pain and sorrow. Lord, make us more than 
conquerors because You love us.
  Today, instruct our lawmakers as they seek to do Your will. As they 
perform their daily tasks, guide their priorities. Show them Your truth 
so that they will be instruments of Your purposes. Lord, transform 
their lives from a hurried succession of days into a walk with You that 
brings enduring peace.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
