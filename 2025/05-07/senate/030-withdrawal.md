# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on May 
7, 2025 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       JANETTE NESHEIWAT, OF NEW YORK, TO BE MEDICAL DIRECTOR IN 
     THE REGULAR CORPS OF THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE, SUBJECT TO 
     QUALIFICATIONS THEREFOR AS PROVIDED BY LAW AND REGULATIONS, 
     AND TO BE SURGEON GENERAL OF THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE FOR A 
     TERM OF FOUR YEARS, VICE VIVEK HALLEGERE MURTHY, RESIGNED, 
     WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 20, 2025.
```
