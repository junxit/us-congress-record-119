# Text of Senate Amendment 3114

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 3114. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 3944, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in the matter preceding division 
     A, insert the following:

     SEC. __. RESCISSION LIMITATION.

       No amounts may be rescinded from amounts provided under any 
     division of this Act or any other appropriation Act for 
     fiscal year 2026, unless the rescission is made through an 
     appropriation Act (as defined in section 3 of the 
     Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control Act of 1974 (2 
     U.S.C. 622)).
                                 ______
```
