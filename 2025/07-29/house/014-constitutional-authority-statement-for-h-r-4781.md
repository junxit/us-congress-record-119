# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4781

**Measures:** H.R. 4781

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 4781.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the Constitution, which 
     grants Congress the authority to regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, and among the several states, and with the 
     Indian tribes. This bill facilitates infrastructure 
     development and the domestic supply chain for critical 
     materials essential to national commerce and energy security.
```
