# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of life, thank You for the opportunity to honor the life and 
legacy of the 39th President of the United States.
  Lord, Your mercies are new to us every day. Take our Senators by the 
hand and lead them on the road You desire them to travel. Lord, help 
our lawmakers to seek Your guidance as they establish their priorities, 
always remembering their accountability to You. May this accountability 
motivate them to never deviate from the path of integrity but to seek 
to ensure that Your will is done on Earth even as it is done in Heaven. 
May they never forget that You are with them and will guide them.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
