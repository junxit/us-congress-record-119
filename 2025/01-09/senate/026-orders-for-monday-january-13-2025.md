# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 13, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 5

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 13, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m., 
Monday, January 13; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed; further, upon conclusion of morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to proceed to 
Calendar No. 1, S. 5 postcloture; finally, that all postcloture time 
expire at 5:30 p.m. and the Senate vote on the motion to proceed to 
Calendar No. 1, S. 5.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
