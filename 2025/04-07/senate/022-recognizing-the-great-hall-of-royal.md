                  RECOGNIZING THE GREAT HALL OF ROYAL

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize The 
Great Hall of Royal of Royal, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.

[[Page S2455]]

  In 2018, Sara and Matt Ricke acquired the historic St. Louis Catholic 
Church in Royal, IA. The couple envisioned preserving the religious 
landmark while integrating modern touches to transform the former 
church into an event space. Originally from Sioux City, IA, Sara and 
Matt spent weekends traveling to Royal, dedicating countless hours to 
renovating the space. Within 1 year, their hard work paid off, and The 
Great Hall of Royal was ready for business. Today, the venue features 
sleek, elegant woodwork that enhances the historic charm for any event 
ranging from intimate private parties to weddings and fundraisers.
  Over the years, The Great Hall of Royal has become a beloved 
destination for families and organizations across northwest Iowa. The 
company has dedicated staff members that help with the diverse needs of 
event coordination. As the primary owner and event coordinator, Sara 
personally oversees each event, ensuring guests have a seamless and 
enjoyable experience. The Great Hall of Royal's catering services began 
unexpectedly when a client requested on-site food service for a one-
time event. The positive feedback led to frequent catering requests, 
and in 2021, the business officially launched its catering division. In 
2023, The Great Hall of Royal catered its first wedding, marking a 
milestone for the business. This March, Sara and Matt welcomed their 
daughters Jackie, Lexi, and Becky into the company as partners. Each 
daughter plays a vital role, from website design to event coordination 
and guest services.
  The Great Hall of Royal's commitment to serving the community shines 
beyond their investment in bringing joy to others through social 
gatherings. Sara and Matt remain deeply involved in the local 
community. Both are active members of the Spencer Chamber of Commerce, 
and Sara serves on the Tourism Committee while Matt contributes his 
expertise to the Agriculture Committee. Additionally, Sara serves on 
the board of the Clay County Long Term Recovery Group (CCLTRG), where 
she is helping to lead the effort to restore Spencer, IA, after the 
devastating floods the community endured in the summer of 2024. Sara 
and Matt also serve as volunteer firefighters, specializing in medical 
care. This October, The Great Hall of Royal will celebrate its fifth 
anniversary in Iowa.
  The entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to excellence demonstrated 
by The Great Hall of Royal are clear. I want to congratulate Sara and 
Matt Ricke, their family, and the entire team for their hard work and 
dedication to providing an exceptional venue for families across Iowa. 
I look forward to seeing their continued growth and success.●
