# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 8, 2025

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 8, 2025

  Mr. MORENO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Tuesday, April 8; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour

[[Page S2460]]

be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning 
business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each; further, that notwithstanding rule XXII, all postcloture time 
with respect to Executive Calendar No. 56, Elbridge Colby, be deemed 
expired and, at 11:30 a.m., the Senate proceed to executive session and 
resume consideration of the Colby nomination and the Senate vote on 
confirmation of the nomination; further, that the cloture motions filed 
on Saturday, April 5, ripen following confirmation of the Colby 
nomination; that following the confirmation vote on the Colby 
nomination, the Senate vote on the motion to invoke cloture on Calendar 
No. 54, George Glass; that if cloture is invoked on the Glass 
nomination, the Senate recess until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly 
conference meetings; further, that at 2:15 p.m., the Senate vote on 
confirmation of the Glass nomination; that following disposition of the 
Glass nomination, the Senate vote on the motion to invoke cloture on 
Calendar No. 67, Mike Huckabee; finally, that if any nominations are 
confirmed during Tuesday's session, the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table and the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
