# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S.Res. 159

```

  Mr. MORENO. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions
```
