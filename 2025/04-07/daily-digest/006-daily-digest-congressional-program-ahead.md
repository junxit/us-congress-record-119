                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of April 8 through April 11, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, After the transaction of any morning business (not to 
extend beyond 11:30 a.m.), Senate will continue consideration of the 
nomination of Elbridge Colby, of the District of Columbia, to be Under 
Secretary of Defense for Policy, post-cloture, and vote on confirmation 
thereon.

[[Page D348]]


  Following disposition of the nomination of Elbridge Colby, Senate 
will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of George 
Glass, of Oregon, to be Ambassador to Japan. If cloture is invoked on 
the nomination, Senate will vote on confirmation thereon at 2:15 p.m.
  Following disposition of the nomination of George Glass, Senate will 
vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Mike 
Huckabee, of Arkansas, to be Ambassador to the State of Israel. If 
cloture is invoked on the nomination, Senate will vote on confirmation 
of the nomination after a period of debate.
  Following disposition of the nomination of Mike Huckabee, Senate will 
vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Peter 
Hoekstra, of Michigan, to be Ambassador to Canada.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
