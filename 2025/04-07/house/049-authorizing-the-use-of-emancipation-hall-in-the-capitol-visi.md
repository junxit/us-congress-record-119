# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDALS AWARDED UNDER THE `SIX TRIPLE EIGHT' CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL ACT OF 2021

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 22

```
AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER 
 FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDALS AWARDED UNDER 
      THE `SIX TRIPLE EIGHT' CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL ACT OF 2021

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee on 
House Administration be discharged from further consideration of H. 
Con. Res. 22, and ask for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the concurrent resolution is as follows:

                            H. Con. Res. 22

       Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate 
     concurring),

     SECTION 1. USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT 
                   THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDALS AWARDED UNDER THE 
                   `SIX TRIPLE EIGHT' CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL ACT 
                   OF 2021.

       (a) Authorization.--Emancipation Hall in the Capitol 
     Visitor Center is authorized to be used on April 29, 2025, 
     for a ceremony to present the Congressional Gold Medals 
     awarded under the `Six Triple Eight' Congressional Gold Medal 
     Act of 2021 (Public Law 117-97).
       (b) Preparations.--Physical preparations for the ceremony 
     described in subsection (a) shall be carried out in 
     accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the 
     Capitol may prescribe.
  The concurrent resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
