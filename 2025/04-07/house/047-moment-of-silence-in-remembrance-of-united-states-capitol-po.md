# MOMENT OF SILENCE IN REMEMBRANCE OF UNITED STATES CAPITOL POLICE OFFICER WILLIAM F. EVANS

```
   MOMENT OF SILENCE IN REMEMBRANCE OF UNITED STATES CAPITOL POLICE 
                        OFFICER WILLIAM F. EVANS

  The SPEAKER. The Chair asks that all those present in the Chamber, as 
well as Members and staff throughout the Capitol, to please rise for a 
moment of silence in remembrance of Officer William F. Evans, United 
States Capitol Police Officer, who was killed in the line of duty 
defending our Capitol on April 2, 2021.

                          ____________________
```
