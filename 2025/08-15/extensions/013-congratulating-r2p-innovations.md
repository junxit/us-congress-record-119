# CONGRATULATING R2P INNOVATIONS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                     CONGRATULATING R2P INNOVATIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 15, 2025

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am grateful to recognize 
R2P Innovations for being ranked number five on the 2025 Inc. 5000 list 
of the fastest-growing private companies in America.
  Those on this annual list are ranked according to their percentage 
revenue growth over three years from 2021 to 2024, a further testament 
to R2P Innovations for their unique and much-desired product.
  The Ladson, South Carolina-based company is the `leading developer 
and manufacturer of the world's most advanced bulletproof doors, 
specifically designed to blend seamlessly into office and school 
settings.'
  Notably, R2P Innovations is currently manufacturing and installing 
secure door systems in the U.S. Capitol Building historic Rotunda. The 
beautifully crafted doors of simulated mahogany add to an already 
magnificent center of tribute to American democracy.
  Led by R2P Innovations Founder and CEO, Tony Deering, and joined by 
South Carolina Attorney General Alan Wilson, just last week I received 
an informative tour of the company where I was able to witness its 
state-of-the-art technology.
  I appreciate Tony Deering and the R2P Innovations team for their 
dedicated work in protecting lives and offer the company my best wishes 
for success in their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
