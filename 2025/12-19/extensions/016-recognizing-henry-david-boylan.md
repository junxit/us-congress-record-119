# RECOGNIZING HENRY DAVID BOYLAN

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                     RECOGNIZING HENRY DAVID BOYLAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 19, 2025

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the birth of 
Henry David Boylan.
  Hank was born on November 24, 2025 at Virginia Hospital Center 
weighing eight pounds, two ounces to loving parents Ansley and Matt 
Boylan. Ansley serves as a valuable member of my team on the House 
Committee on Energy and Commerce.
  I am grateful to have Ansley as a part of the Energy and Commerce 
family, and I am excited for her and Matt to start their family with 
their newest addition.
```
