# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 496

**Measures:** H.R. 496

```
            By Mr. CRANE:
        H.R. 496.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power to regulate commerce among the states, in 
     addition to commerce involving foreign nations and Native 
     American tribes.
```
