# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, unto whom all hearts are open, all desires known, and 
from whom no secrets are hidden, abide with our lawmakers. Teach them 
to speak the right words at the right time. Make their speech like 
precious gold set in silver. May they seek to persuade with patient and 
gentle words. Lord, give them the wisdom to be friends of that which is 
eternal and abiding. Teach them reverence for the transcendent as You 
induce them to ascribe all good things to You.
  We pray in Your precious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
