# Text of Senate Amendment 84

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 84. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 5, to require the Secretary of Homeland Security to 
take into custody aliens who have been charged in the United States 
with theft, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 3, between lines 8 and 9, insert the following:
       (b) Effective Date.--
       (1) In general.--Section 236(c)(1)(E) of the Immigration 
     and Nationality, as added by this section, shall take effect 
     upon the earlier of--
       (A) the date on which the Secretary of Homeland Security 
     certifies to the appropriate congressional committees that 
     sufficient Federal funds are available to carry out such 
     section 236(c)(1)(E); or
       (B) the first day of a fiscal year for which sufficient 
     Federal funds have been appropriated to carry out such 
     section 236(c)(1)(E).
       (2) Contents.--The certification described in paragraph 
     (1)(A) shall include a detailed list of the amounts and 
     programs, including any congressionally-directed spending, 
     that are necessary to carry out such section 236(c)(1)(E).
                                 ______
```
