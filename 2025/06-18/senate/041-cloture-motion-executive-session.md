# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 93, Kenneth Kies, of Virginia, to 
     be an Assistant Secretary of the Treasury.
         John Thune, Pete Ricketts, Bill Hagerty, Tim Scott of 
           South Carolina, Roger F. Wicker, John R. Curtis, Cindy 
           Hyde-Smith, Bernie Moreno, Katie Boyd Britt, Mike Lee, 
           Mike Rounds, Deb Fischer, Steve Daines, Rick Scott of 
           Florida, James Lankford, Jon A. Husted, Ted Cruz.

  Mr. THUNE. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
