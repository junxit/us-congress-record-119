# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 5

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 5

```
                  MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 5

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 5) to require the Secretary of Homeland Security 
     to take into custody aliens who have been charged in the 
     United States with theft, and for other purposes.

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, in order to place the bill on the 
calendar under provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further 
proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
