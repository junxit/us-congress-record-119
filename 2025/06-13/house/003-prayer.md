# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Steadfast and loving God, lead us to walk today in faith, a faith 
that allows us to have confidence in what we cannot see but which You 
provide for us without fail.
  May we trust in Your provision, finding the strength and courage to 
give ourselves and this day into Your care and keeping.
  Cause this trust to lead to faith in You, and in faith may we 
experience the fullness of what You offer us: to walk in Your presence 
no matter where we find ourselves; to enjoy our relationship with You, 
a relationship that transcends all the inadequacies of our lives; to 
discover Your purpose even in the most uncertain of times.
  Lord, we want to believe in You. Help our unbelief.
  In Your eternal name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
