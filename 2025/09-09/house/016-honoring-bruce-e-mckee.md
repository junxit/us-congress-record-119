# HONORING BRUCE E. McKEE

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING BRUCE E. McKEE

  (Mr. Baird of Indiana was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Bruce E. McKee, a 
constituent from Morgan County who served our country as a member of 
the United States Air Force from 1979 to 1986, including serving 
overseas in Korea.
  Bruce McKee has demonstrated outstanding service by supporting 
veterans in our Morgan County communities. Bruce founded the Indiana 
Spirit of '45, focused on remembering veterans of the Greatest 
Generation.
  In December of 2012, McKee and the Indiana Spirit of '45 started Taps 
on the Square to honor fallen veterans and law enforcement officers 
killed in the line of duty. Taps on the Square continues, rain or 
shine, in downtown Martinsville. McKee also works with the Battle 
Buddies group to support veterans struggling with reentering civilian 
life or PTSD.
  As a veteran myself, I am grateful for the work Bruce is doing to 
support fellow veterans and servicemembers in our district.
```
