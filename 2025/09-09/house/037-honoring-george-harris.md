# HONORING GEORGE HARRIS

**Speaking:** Johnson, Julie (J000310) [D-TX]

```
                              {time}  1210
                         HONORING GEORGE HARRIS

  (Ms. JOHNSON of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor George Harris, a 
giant in the Dallas community and someone who dedicated his entire life 
to fighting for LGBTQ equality.
  Last month, George passed away at the age of 92, leaving behind a 
legacy of courage, service, and sacrifice. After serving in the Army 
and working at the CIA, he was discharged simply because he was gay. He 
built a life of love alongside his partner of more than 50 years, Jack 
Evans.
  In 2015, George and Jack made history as the first same-sex couple 
legally married in Dallas County, a moment that inspired Americans from 
across the country.
  George helped create institutions that still serve our community, 
like the Resource Center; the LGBTQ Chamber of Commerce; and The Dallas 
Way, one of our Nation's largest LGBTQ archives.
  Preserving our history was one of George's last labors of love, 
ensuring future generations know the struggles and triumphs of those 
who came before him.
  Dallas and our Nation are better because of George Harris.

[[Page H3907]]

  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of this entire House and our Dallas community, 
I extend my deepest condolences to his family, friends, and neighbors. 
May we honor his legacy by continuing his fight for equality and 
dignity for all.
```
