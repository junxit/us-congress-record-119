# RECOGNIZING U.S. ARMY SERGEANT MAJOR KEVIN FACEMYER

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
          RECOGNIZING U.S. ARMY SERGEANT MAJOR KEVIN FACEMYER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2025

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to recognize Sergeant 
Major Kevin Facemyer, a remarkable leader from South Jersey, who is 
retiring after 27 years of dedicated military service to our great 
Nation.
  From enlisting in the U.S. Army with a Ranger contract in 1996, to 
serving with the 75th Ranger Regiment, to deployments in Iraq, 
Afghanistan, and Pakistan, Kevin exemplified courage and commitment in 
some of the most challenging missions of our time. He went on to lead 
with distinction in the Maryland Army National Guard, where he trained 
future leaders, prepared units for combat, and supported critical 
domestic operations. Over his career, he earned some of the Army's 
highest honors, including the Legion of Spirit Medal, Bronze Star, and 
Purple Heart, as well as recognition in the orders of Saint Maurice and 
Saint Michael. As Kevin retires with 27 years of combined service, we 
salute his extraordinary career, his devotion to his soldiers, and his 
steadfast defense of our country.
  God bless Kevin and God bless the United States of America. The 
greatest country on the face of the Earth.

                          ____________________
```
