# Text of Senate Amendment 3839

**Speaking:** Graham, Lindsey (G000359) [R-SC]

```
  SA 3839. Mr. GRAHAM submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 2296, to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
2026 for military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, 
to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in subtitle F of title X, insert 
     the following:

     SEC. 10___. UNITED STATES SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL COMMISSION.

       Section 4 of the United States Semiquincentennial 
     Commission Act of 2016 (Public Law 114-196; 130 Stat. 685; 
     134 Stat. 3386) is amended--
       (1) in subsection (b)--
       (A) by redesignating paragraphs (1) through (4) as 
     paragraphs (2) through (5), respectively; and
       (B) by inserting before paragraph (2) (as so redesignated) 
     the following:
       ``(1) 12 members who shall be appointed by the 
     President.''; and
       (2) in subsection (c)(3), by striking ``(b)(3)'' and 
     inserting ``(b)(4)''.
                                 ______
```
