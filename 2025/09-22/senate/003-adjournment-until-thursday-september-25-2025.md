# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2025

```
             ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2025

Senate stands adjourned until 12 noon on Thursday, September 25, 2025.

Thursday, September 25, 2025, at 12 noon.
```
