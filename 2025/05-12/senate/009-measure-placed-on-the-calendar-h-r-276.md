# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 276

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 276

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 276

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
due a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The assistant bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 276) to rename the Gulf of Mexico as the 
     ``Gulf of America''.

  Mr. THUNE. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
