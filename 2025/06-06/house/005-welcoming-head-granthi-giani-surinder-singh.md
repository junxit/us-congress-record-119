# WELCOMING HEAD GRANTHI GIANI SURINDER SINGH

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
              WELCOMING HEAD GRANTHI GIANI SURINDER SINGH

  The SPEAKER. Without objection, the gentleman from New Jersey (Mr. 
Van Drew) is recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor today to welcome Giani 
Singh as our guest chaplain.
  Giani Singh has served as Head Granthi at the Gurdwara in Vineland, 
New Jersey, since 2012.
  Day after day, year after year, he leads not just with words but with 
example, with peace, with humility, and with service toward all.
  These are not just Sikh values. They are American values. They truly 
are human values. We have seen that shared spirit in action.
  Today is the 81st anniversary of D-day. On the beaches of Normandy, 
Sikh soldiers fought shoulder to shoulder with Allied forces. That same 
spirit and that same bravery lives on today, as Sikh Americans continue 
to serve our great communities in the great Nation of the United States 
of America.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank all my colleagues for coming here today.

                          ____________________
```
