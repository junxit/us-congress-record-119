# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 107, S. 1884, H.R. 5371

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                       Week of November 4 through
                            November 7, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the 
motion to proceed to consideration of H.R. 5371, Continuing 
Appropriations and Extensions Act.
  At 2:15 p.m., Senate will vote on confirmation of the nomination of 
Joshua D. Dunlap, of Maine, to be United States Circuit Judge for the 
First Circuit.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: November 5, to 
hold hearings to examine the nomination of Glen Smith, of Iowa, to 
be Under Secretary of Agriculture for Rural Development, 3 p.m., SR-
328A.
  Committee on Armed Services: November 4, to hold hearings to 
examine the nominations of Austin Dahmer, of Arizona, and Robert 
Kadlec, of New York, both to be an Assistant Secretary, and Michael 
Borders, of Florida, to be an Assistant Secretary of the Air Force, 
all of the Department of Defense, 9:30 a.m., SD-G50.
  November 6, Full Committee, to hold hearings to examine the 
nominations of Michael Payne, of Virginia, to be Director of Cost 
Assessment and Program Evaluation, Alexander Velez-Green, of 
Virginia, to be a Deputy Under Secretary, and Timothy Dill, of Ohio, 
and Maurice Todd, of Florida, both to be an Assistant Secretary, all 
of the Department of Defense, 9:30 a.m., SD-G50.

  Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: November 5, to 
hold hearings to examine the nominations of Ryan McCormack, of 
Virginia, to be Under Secretary for Policy, Daniel Edwards, of North 
Carolina, to be an Assistant Secretary, both of the Department of 
Transportation, and Trent Morse, of Florida, to be a Member of the 
Board of Directors of the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority 
for a term expiring May 30, 2030, 2:15 p.m., SR-253.
  November 6, Full Committee, to hold hearings to examine the 
nominations of John DeLeeuw, of Texas, Richard Kloster, of West 
Virginia, and Michelle A. Schultz, of Pennsylvania, each to be a 
Member of the Surface Transportation Board, 10 a.m., SR-253.
  Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: November 5, 
to hold hearings to examine registered apprenticeship, focusing on 
scaling the workforce for the future, 10 a.m., SD-430.
  November 6, Full Committee, to hold hearings to examine reforming 
financial transparency in higher education, 10 a.m., SD-430.
  Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: November 
5, business meeting to consider the nominations of Edward Forst, of 
Florida, to be Administrator of General Services, Charles Arrington, 
of Illinois, to be a Member of the Federal Labor Relations 
Authority, and John Cuong Truong, Elana S. Suttenberg, and Stephen 
F. Rickard, each of the District of Columbia, all to be an Associate 
Judge of the Superior Court of the District of Columbia, 10 a.m., 
SD-342.
  November 6, Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations, to hold 
hearings to examine assessing Obamacare, 1 p.m., SD-342.
  Committee on Indian Affairs: November 5, to hold hearings to 
examine S. 107, to amend the Lumbee Act of 1956, 2:30 p.m., SD-106.
  Committee on the Judiciary: November 6, business meeting to 
consider the nominations of Robert P. Chamberlin, and James D. 
Maxwell II, both to be a United States District Judge for the 
Northern District of Mississippi, Thomas Ferguson III, to be United 
States Attorney for the Western District of North Carolina, James 
Kruger, to be United States Attorney for the Southern District of 
Mississippi, Scott Leary, of Mississippi, to be United States 
Attorney for the Northern District of Mississippi, and S. 1884, to 
clarify the Holocaust Expropriated Art Recovery Act of 2016, to 
appropriately limit the application of defenses based on the passage 
of time and other non-merits defenses to claims under that Act, 
10:15 a.m., SH-216.
  Committee on Veterans' Affairs: November 5, to hold hearings to 
examine a grateful nation, focusing on maximizing veteran's success 
after service, 4 p.m., SD-G50.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: November 4, closed business 
meeting to consider pending calendar business, 3 p.m., S-128, 
Capitol.
  November 5, Full Committee, to receive a closed briefing on 
certain intelligence matters, 3 p.m., SH-219.
  Special Committee on Aging: November 5, to hold hearings to 
examine how the Older Americans Act uplifts families living with 
aging-related diseases, 3:30 p.m., SH-216.


                            House Committees

  No hearings are scheduled.
```
