# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You look down from Heaven upon humanity's children, 
seeking for those to use for Your glory. May our lawmakers live with 
such faithfulness that they will be Your redemptive instruments at a 
time when truth seems a rare commodity.
  Lord, when a house is burning, the first step should be to unite in 
extinguishing the flames, not seeking to discover who started the fire. 
Today, inspire our lawmakers to unite in putting out the fire of this 
government shutdown that has already burned far more than anticipated.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
