# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 161, H.R. 227, H.R. 2716, H.R. 4214, H.R. 4218, H.R. 4242, H.R. 4930, H.R. 4993, H.R. 6373, H.R. 6387, H.R. 6398, H.R. 6409, H.R. 6495, H.R. 6500, H.R. 6504, H.R. 6506

```
Committee Meetings
MEMBER DAY
Committee on Agriculture: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Member Day''. Testimony was heard from Representatives Costa, 
Figures, Vicente Gonzalez of Texas, Hageman, Hayes, Hernandez, Moore of 
North Carolina, Scholten, Schrier, Takano, Tlaib, and Underwood.
BUILDING A TALENT MARKETPLACE: HOW LEARNING AND EMPLOYMENT RECORDS 
EMPOWER WORKERS AND EXPAND OPPORTUNITY
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Higher Education 
and Workforce Development held a hearing entitled ``Building A Talent 
Marketplace: How LERs Empower Workers and Expand Opportunity''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Environment held a 
markup on H.R. 6409, the ``Foreign Emissions and Nonattainment 
Clarification for Economic Stability''; H.R. 4218, the ``Clean Air and 
Economic Advancement Reform Act''; H.R. 6387, the ``Fire Improvement 
and Reforming Exceptional Events Act''; H.R. 4214, the ``Clean Air and 
Building Infrastructure Improvement Act''; H.R. 161, the ``New Source 
Review Permitting Improvement Act''; H.R. 6373, the ``Air Permitting 
Improvements to Protect National Security Act''; and H.R. 6398, the 
``Reducing and Eliminating Duplicative Environmental Regulations Act''. 
H.R. 6409, H.R. 4218, H.R. 6387, H.R. 4214, H.R. 161, and H.R. 6398 
were forwarded to the full Committee, without amendment.
FROM PRINCIPLES TO POLICY: ENABLING 21ST CENTURY ARTIFICIAL 
INTELLIGENCE INNOVATION IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``From Principles to Policy: Enabling 21st Century AI Innovation in 
Financial Services''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
UNDERSTANDING JUDEA AND SAMARIA: HISTORICAL, STRATEGIC, AND POLITICAL 
DYNAMICS IN U.S.-ISRAEL RELATIONS
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Middle East and North Africa Subcommittee 
held a hearing entitled ``Understanding Judea and Samaria: Historical, 
Strategic, and Political Dynamics in U.S.-Israel Relations''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
THE U.S. INDIA STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP: SECURING A FREE AND OPEN INDO-
PACIFIC
Committee on Foreign Affairs: South and Central Asia Subcommittee held 
a hearing entitled ``The U.S. India Strategic Partnership: Securing a 
Free and Open Indo-Pacific''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
EXAMINING POTENTIAL UPDATES TO THE NATIONAL VOTER REGISTRATION ACT
Committee on House Administration: Subcommittee on Elections held a 
hearing entitled ``Examining Potential Updates to the NVRA''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
FIGHTING OBAMACARE SUBSIDY FRAUD: IS THE ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT 
WORKING AS INTENDED?
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Oversight; and Subcommittee 
on the Administrative State, Regulatory Reform, and Antitrust held a 
joint hearing entitled ``Fighting Obamacare Subsidy Fraud: Is the 
Administrative Procedure Act Working as Intended?''. Testimony was 
heard from Seto Bagdoyan, Director of Audit Services, Forensic Audits 
and Investigative Service, U.S. Government Accountability Office; and 
public witnesses.
ABUSE OF THE EQUAL ACCESS TO JUSTICE ACT BY ENVIRONMENTAL NON-
GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a hearing entitled ``Abuse of the Equal Access to 
Justice Act by Environmental NGOs''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
LOWERING THE COST OF HEALTHCARE: TECHNOLOGY'S ROLE IN DRIVING 
AFFORDABILITY
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on Economic 
Growth, Energy Policy, and Regulatory Affairs; and Subcommittee on 
Health Care and Financial Services held a joint hearing entitled 
``Lowering the Cost of Healthcare: Technology's Role in Driving 
Affordability''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
USING MODERN TOOLS TO COUNTER HUMAN TRAFFICKING
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on 
Cybersecurity, Information Technology, and Government Innovation held a 
hearing entitled ``Using Modern Tools to Counter Human Trafficking''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.

[[Page D1246]]


THE GENESIS MISSION: PRIORITIZING AMERICAN SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY 
LEADERSHIP
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Full Committee held a 
hearing entitled ``The Genesis Mission: Prioritizing American Science 
and Technology Leadership''. Testimony was heard from Dario Gil, Under 
Secretary for Science, Department of Energy.
FROM SERVICE TO STARTUP: EMPOWERING VETERAN ENTREPRENEURS
Committee on Small Business: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``From Service to Startup: Empowering Veteran Entrepreneurs''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
PUTTING FAMILIES FIRST: STRENGTHENING CIVILIAN HEALTH AND MEDICAL 
PROGRAM OF THE DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS FOR SURVIVORS AND 
DEPENDENTS
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``Putting Families First: Strengthening CHAMPVA for Survivors 
and Dependents''. Testimony was heard from Maria Llorente, M.D., Acting 
Assistant Under Secretary for Health, Office of Integrated Veteran 
Care, Department of Veterans Affairs; and public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Ways and Means: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 6500, 
the ``AGOA Extension Act''; H.R. 6504, the ``Haiti Economic Lift 
Program Extension Act''; H.R. 4930, to expand the sharing of 
information with respect to suspected violations of intellectual 
property rights in trade; H.R. 227, the ``Clergy Act''; H.R. 2716, the 
``Ending Improper Payments to Deceased People Act''; H.R. 4993, the 
``Joe Fiandra Access to Home Infusion Act of 2025''; H.R. 6506, the 
``Taxpayer Due Process Enhancement Act''; H.R. 6495, the ``Taxpayer 
Notification and Privacy Act''; and H.R. 4242, the ``Innovate Less 
Lethal to De-Escalate Tax Modernization Act''. H.R. 6500, H.R. 6504, 
H.R. 4930, H.R. 227, H.R. 2716, H.R. 4993, H.R. 6506, H.R. 6495, and 
H.R. 4242 were ordered reported, as amended.

Joint Meetings
DEFENSE SUPPLY CHAINS
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe: Commission received a 
briefing on production to procurement, focusing on how Europe and 
Ukraine are transforming defense supply chains, from Major General 
Karsten F. Jensen, Defense Attache, Royal Danish Embassy in the United 
States; Kateryna Bondar, Center for Strategic and International Studies 
Wadhwani AI Center; and Sophia Besch, Carnegie Endowment for 
International Peace Europe Program.
```
