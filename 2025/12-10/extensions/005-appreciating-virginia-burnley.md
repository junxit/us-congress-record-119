# APPRECIATING VIRGINIA BURNLEY

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                     APPRECIATING VIRGINIA BURNLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 10, 2025

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am grateful to Virginia 
J. Burnley, Chief Government Relations Officer at the United Service 
Organizations (USO), for her service to America over the past eight 
years.
  Virginia joined the USO senior staff team in 2017 as Senior Vice 
President of Government Relations and External Affairs, earning herself

[[Page E1174]]

the title of Chief Government Relations Officer. She has excelled in 
this role and provided my staff and myself with outstanding service.
  With many accomplishments, Virginia notably has more than 30 years of 
experience in the nonprofit community, senior levels of federal 
service, state government, and the private sector.
  Since joining the USO, she has been a strong and steady champion for 
service members and their families, earning the deep respect of her 
colleagues and those she interacted with.
  As we wish the best to Virginia, I am grateful to continue working 
with Clint Cowan, who currently serves as Senior Director of Government 
Relations and External Affairs at the USO.
  America values Virginia's leadership, knowledge, and dedication. I 
wish for her continued success.

                          ____________________
```
