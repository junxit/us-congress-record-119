# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. J. Res. 82

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 82

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. J. Res. 82

  Mr. BARRASSO. I ask unanimous consent that all time on Calendar No. 
203, S.J. Res. 82, be expired, and at a time to be determined by the
```
