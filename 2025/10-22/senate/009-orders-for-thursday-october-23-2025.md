# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 23, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 3012

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 23, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Thursday, October 23, 2025; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the

[[Page S7704]]

time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motion with respect to the 
motion to proceed to Calendar No. 191, S. 3012, ripen at 12:15 p.m.; 
further, that following disposition of that vote, the Senate execute 
the order of October 22 in relation to the Meredith nomination; 
finally, that the cloture motion with respect to the Taibleson 
nomination ripen following disposition of the Meredith nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
