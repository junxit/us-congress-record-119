# HONORING MOUNT NITTANY HEALTH EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES TEAM

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
     HONORING MOUNT NITTANY HEALTH EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES TEAM

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the outstanding achievement of Mount Nittany Health Emergency Medical 
Services team.
  Earlier this month, Mount Nittany Health Emergency Medical Services 
team was nationally recognized by the American Heart Association's 
Mission: Lifeline program for excellence in caring for patients with 
heart attacks and strokes.
  This award recognizes exceptional performance and critical 
prehospital care, from the rapid identification of heart attacks and 
stroke symptoms to timely notifications to the hospital, precise 
assessment, and treatment protocols that save lives.
  The Mount Nittany Health EMS team exemplifies the dedication, skill, 
and compassion that our communities expect and deserve.
  Prior to my time serving in Congress, I spent decades as a volunteer 
firefighter and cross-trained as an emergency medical technician. I 
recognize the value and commitment these individuals have to their 
communities and understand firsthand the vital role they play in 
ensuring public health and safety.
  Their efforts translate into faster treatment, reduced complications, 
and renewed hope for countless patients and families.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend and thank Mount Nittany EMS for setting the 
standard in emergency medical care and leading the way in national 
excellence.

                          ____________________
```
