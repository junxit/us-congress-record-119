# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF SUPERINTENDENT, DR. CISSY REYNOLDS-PEREZ

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
 CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF SUPERINTENDENT, DR. CISSY REYNOLDS-PEREZ

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 14, 2025

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate 
Kingsville Independent School District (ISD) Superintendent, Dr. Cissy 
Reynolds-Perez on her retirement from 38 years of public service.
  Dr. Reynolds-Perez is a proud graduate of Texas A&M University-
Kingsville where she earned a bachelor's degree in education with a 
minor in English. She later went on to earn a Doctoral Degree in 
Educational Leadership from Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi.
  Dr. Reynolds-Perez has made incredible contributions to our state's 
education system, having served as principal for multiple schools and 
Assistant Superintendent of West Oso ISD. During her tenure as an 
administrator, Dr. Reynolds-Perez established herself as a leader in 
school safety. In 2018, Governor Greg Abbott appointed her to serve on 
the Texas School Safety Center Board of Directors for two terms. Later 
she was selected to sit on Texas House Chairman Todd Hunter's Suicide 
Prevention Task Force, where she helped spearhead the passage of HB 
3980 and HCR 137, monumental suicide prevention legislation
  In August of 2020, in the midst of the COVID-19 Pandemic, Dr. 
Reynolds-Perez was appointed as the Superintendent in her hometown of 
Kingsville, Texas. Her dedication to education and public service is 
unmatched, and I thank her for her tremendous work in educating the 
future generations of South Texans.
  Mr. Speaker. I wish to thank Dr. Reynolds-Perez for her 38 years of 
public service and congratulate her on her retirement.
```
