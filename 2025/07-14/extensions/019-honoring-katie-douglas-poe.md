# HONORING KATIE DOUGLAS-POE

**Speaking:** Carson, Andre (C001072) [D-IN]

```
                       HONORING KATIE DOUGLAS-POE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANDRE CARSON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 14, 2025

  Mr. CARSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Katie Douglas-Poe, an 
accomplished

[[Page E667]]

athlete, successful entrepreneur, and proud daughter of Indianapolis 
who continues to inspire Hoosiers across our state.
  Katie's journey began on the Southside of Indianapolis at Perry 
Meridian High School. Her standout high school career led her to Purdue 
University, where she helped lead the Boilermakers to the 1999 NCAA 
National Championship. A two-time Big Ten Player of the Year and 
Academic All-American, Katie is known for her leadership, talent, and 
relentless work ethic both on and off the court.
  In 2001, Katie was selected 10th overall in the WNBA Draft, launching 
a 14-year professional career that included multiple All-Star 
selections, All-WNBA honors, and most notably, a 2012 WNBA Championship 
with Indiana Fever. Her number 23 jersey was retired by the Connecticut 
Sun, a lasting tribute to her legacy in women's basketball.
  Katie's success did not end when she left the court. She brought her 
competitive spirit and passion for service into business and community 
leadership. As the owner of several Orangetheory Fitness studios across 
Indiana, she has promoted health and wellness while creating jobs and 
opportunities. Through mentorship, media, and civic engagement, Katie 
continues to uplift the next generation of athletes and leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Katie 
Douglas-Poe for her extraordinary accomplishments, her commitment to 
Indiana, and her lasting impact on both sport and community. She 
embodies the heart, hustle, and spirit that make our state proud.

                          ____________________
```
