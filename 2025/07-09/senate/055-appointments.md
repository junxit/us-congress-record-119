# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  THE PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President of the 
Senate, pursuant to Public Law 85-874, as amended, appoints the 
following individuals to the Board of Trustees of the John F. Kennedy 
Center for the Performing Arts: the Honorable Susan Collins of Maine 
(Reappointment); and the Honorable Deb Fischer of Nebraska.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President of the Senate, pursuant to 
Public Law 106-286, appoints the following Members to serve on the 
Congressional-Executive Commission on the People's Republic of China 
during the 119th Congress: the Honorable Dan Sullivan of Alaska 
(Chairman); and the Honorable Tom Cotton of Arkansas.
```
