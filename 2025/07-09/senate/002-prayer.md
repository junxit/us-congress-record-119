# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Today's opening prayer will be offered by 
Pastor Clint Pressley, lead pastor at Hickory Grove Baptist Church, 
Charlotte, NC.
  Welcome.
  The guest Chaplain offered the following prayer:
  Join me in prayer.
  Father in Heaven, we come to You in the name of Jesus, our crucified, 
resurrected Savior. We come to You by the power of the Holy Spirit to 
seek Your blessings on this deliberative body.
  We thank You, Lord, for Your constant grace, for Your providing hand, 
for Your guiding Word. We ask You this day to fill us with Your 
knowledge, the great knowledge of Your will. Give us wisdom and 
understanding that we might walk in a manner worthy of being called 
Your people.
  Father, we ask that You make us a righteous people, a joyful people--
joyful in adversity, hopeful in struggle, and just in all our dealings.
  We pray, Lord, that You would make us a prosperous people, a happy 
people.
  Lord, You have told us what is good and what You expect of us: to do 
justice and to love mercy and to walk humbly with You, our God. May we, 
this day, flourish in kindness and strive for goodness under Your 
gracious and sovereign hand.
  Father, we confess how good it is to live in this country. We pray 
that You would continually empower us to be the land of the free and 
the home of the brave.
  Help us, Lord. Help us. Bless us. Be honored through us.
  We ask this in the Name and for the sake of Jesus Christ, our Lord. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
