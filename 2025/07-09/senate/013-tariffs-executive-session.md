# Tariffs (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                Tariffs

  Mr. President, finally, on tariffs, 90 days ago, Donald Trump 
promised the world that his tariffs will lead to 90 deals in 90 days. 
Another Donald Trump promise. He makes these promises left and right. 
He doesn't care whether he executes them or not, but the American 
people do.
  So remember 90 deals in 90 days. Well, by my count, he is about 88 
trade deals short.
  So what has Donald Trump done? He kicks the can down the road again 
and again. He talks a big game but shows little followthrough or 
strategic understanding of how to secure America's interests. He seems 
to just talk. Whatever pops into his head, he says.
  Worse, Donald Trump's tariffs are yet another one of his broken 
promises to working Americans, just like his promise to bring costs 
down on day one, just like his promise to immediately end the war in 
Ukraine, just like his promise not to touch Medicaid.
  Donald Trump's tariffs are another example of why he cannot be 
trusted to fight for the interests of everyday Americans. Instead of 
making America great, Donald Trump's tariffs are making Americans pay. 
The Yale Budget Lab says his tariffs will cost American families an 
extra $2,300 a year. That is on top of all their other costs going up: 
their electricity costs going up because of the reconciliation bill, 
their healthcare costs going up because of the reconciliation bill, and 
the tariffs add an extra $2,300, according to the Yale Budget Lab.
  His tariffs are weakening America's standing on the world stage 
because he always makes big threats only to back down. As we all know, 
Donald Trump always chickens out when it comes to tariffs.
  So today I urge Donald Trump to stop governing in chaos and focus on 
a trade plan that actually will protect American workers while holding 
adversaries like China in check. He has failed to do that thus far.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The majority whip.
```
