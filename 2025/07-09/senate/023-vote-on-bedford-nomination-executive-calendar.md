# Vote on Bedford Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                       Vote on Bedford Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Bedford nomination?
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the 
Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Tillis).

Tillis) would have voted ``yea.''
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Maryland (Ms. 
Alsobrooks), the Senator from Arizona (Mr. Gallego), and the Senator 
from Connecticut (Mr. Murphy) are necessarily absent.
  The result was announced--yeas 53, nays 43, as follows:
```
