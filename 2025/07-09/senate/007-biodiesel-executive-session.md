# Biodiesel (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                               Biodiesel

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, this year, I have been working hard to 
help the renewable fuels industry, which provides about 40,000 to 
50,000 jobs in the State of Iowa and is a very big help for the family 
farmers of Iowa. These family farmers rely on the renewable fuels 
market as a source for the use of the bountiful crops that we raise 
each year.
  The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, passed last week, signed by the 
President on July 4, supports family farmers and advances energy 
production--renewable energy production and, also, fossil fuel 
production.
  During the consideration of this legislation, I pushed hard for a 
provision to provide transition relief to small biodiesel producers by 
temporarily extending the small agri-biodiesel tax credit. The previous 
administration, the last 4 years, failed to release functional guidance 
implementing the clean fuel production tax credit. Their refusal to do 
that contributed to many small producers shutting down their operations 
and leaving hundreds of people unemployed. The extension of this credit 
will provide relief to these plants while they await further 
implementation guidance from the Department of the Treasury under the 
One Big Beautiful Bill Act.
  I am proud to deliver this certainty to Iowa's small biodiesel 
producers, to the consumers, and to the benefit of the 86,000 family 
farmers of Iowa.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.

[[Page S4264]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
