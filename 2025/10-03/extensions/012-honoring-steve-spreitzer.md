# HONORING STEVE SPREITZER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                        HONORING STEVE SPREITZER

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 3, 2025

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today, the 12th Congressional District honors 
Steve Spreitzer for his decades of bringing our communities together. 
His love for interfaith work shined through with his work at the 
Michigan Roundtable for Just Communities. For over forty years, Steve 
has dedicated his life's work to advancing racial equity, building 
bridges across equity divides, and uplifting the voices of those too 
often left out of the decision-making that directly affects them. His 
steady and compassionate leadership guided the Roundtable through times 
of challenge and transformation, leaving a legacy that continues to 
inspire us all.
  Under his leadership, the Michigan Roundtable has become a hub for 
justice, dialogue, and action, forming coalitions, strengthening 
partnerships, and creating programs that brought communities together 
to confront systemic racism and discrimination. His vision has paved 
the way for a new generation of leaders who will continue this vital 
work.
  On behalf of Michigan's 12th District. I thank Steve for his 
extraordinary service. His impact will echo through our communities for 
years to come and inspire all who fight for justice, equity, and 
workers' rights. We wish him well in his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
