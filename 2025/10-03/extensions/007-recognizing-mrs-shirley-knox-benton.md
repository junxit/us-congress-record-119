# RECOGNIZING MRS. SHIRLEY KNOX BENTON

**Speaking:** Veasey, Marc A. (V000131) [D-TX]

```
                  RECOGNIZING MRS. SHIRLEY KNOX BENTON

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. MARC A. VEASEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 3, 2025

  Mr. VEASEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the remarkable legacy 
of Mrs. Shirley Knox Benton, a pioneering educator whose leadership and 
vision broke barriers as the first female principal in her community.
  Throughout her distinguished career, Mrs. Benton dedicated herself to 
creating opportunities for young people, ensuring that every student 
had access not only to quality education but also to the encouragement 
and support needed to succeed. Her historic appointment as the first 
woman to lead a school as principal paved the way for generations of 
women to follow in her footsteps, serving as a beacon of progress and 
possibility.
  Mrs. Benton's leadership extended beyond the classroom. She 
championed innovation in education, built meaningful partnerships with 
families and communities, and instilled in her students a lasting sense 
of discipline, perseverance, and hope. Her contributions have had a 
profound and enduring impact, shaping not only the schools she led but 
also the countless lives she touched.
  Today, we celebrate Mrs. Shirley Knox Benton not only for her 
historic achievement but also for her unwavering devotion to education 
and the advancement of future generations. Her example continues to 
inspire, reminding us of all of the power of determination, service, 
and trailblazing leadership.
  I congratulate Mrs. Benton on a legacy that she will endure for many 
more years through the lives of those she guided and inspired.
```
