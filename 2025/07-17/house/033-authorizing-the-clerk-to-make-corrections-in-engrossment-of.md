**Measures:** H.R. 4016

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 4016, 
             DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2026

  Mr. CALVERT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, in the 
engrossment of H.R. 4016, the Clerk will be authorized to correct
```
