# Waiving Quorum Call (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Hawley, Josh (H001089) [R-MO]

```
                          Waiving Quorum Call

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to waive the 
mandatory quorum call with respect to the Divine nomination.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. BARRASSO. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HAWLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
