# TRIBUTE TO EVAN FIRMAN

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO EVAN FIRMAN

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Evan Firman, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has done 
on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Mr. Firman is 
from Flandreau, SD. He is currently attending the University of South 
Dakota, where he studies economics and political science. Mr. Firman is 
a dedicated and diligent individual who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of his internship experience. He has been a true asset to my 
office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mr. Firman for 
all of the work he has done and wish him continued success in the years 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
