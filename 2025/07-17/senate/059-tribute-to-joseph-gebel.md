# TRIBUTE TO JOSEPH GEBEL

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JOSEPH GEBEL

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Joseph Gebel, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has done 
on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Mr. Gebel is a 
graduate of Mitchell High School in Mitchell, SD. He is currently 
attending Augustana University, where he studies government studies, 
religion, and philosophy. Mr. Gebel is a dedicated and diligent 
individual who has been devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience. He has been a true asset to my office. I extend 
my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mr. Gebel for all of the work he 
has done and wish him continued success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
