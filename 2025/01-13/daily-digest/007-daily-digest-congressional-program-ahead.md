# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 5

                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of January 14 through January 17, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will be in a period of morning business for debate 
only. At 2:45 p.m., Senate will continue consideration of S. 5, Laken 
Riley Act.

legislative and executive business.
