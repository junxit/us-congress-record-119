# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 14, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 5

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 14, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
on Tuesday, January 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business for 
debate only, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10

[[Page S126]]

minutes each; further, that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 
p.m. to allow for the weekly conference meetings; finally, that at 2:45 
p.m., morning business be closed and the Senate resume consideration of 
Calendar No. 1, S. 5.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
