# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty and eternal God, You shine in the darkness. The whole Earth 
is bathed in Your light, and for that, we are grateful.
  Today, be near to our lawmakers. Penetrate the springs of their 
being, bringing cleansing, healing, and unity. Drive them away from the 
shadows of a stalemate with our government shutdown, enabling them to 
find common ground. In times of challenges and trials, may they 
remember they are serving You.
  Lord, as we all trust in Your mercies, surround our Nation and world 
with the shield of Your favor and protection.
  And Lord, we thank You for the progress we are seeing in the Middle 
East.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
