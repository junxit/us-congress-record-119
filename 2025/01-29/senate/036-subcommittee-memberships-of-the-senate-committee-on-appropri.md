# SUBCOMMITTEE MEMBERSHIPS OF THE SENATE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE 119TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
SUBCOMMITTEE MEMBERSHIPS OF THE SENATE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS FOR 
                           THE 119TH CONGRESS

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
memberships on the twelve subcommittees of the Committee on 
Appropriations for the 119th Congress be printed in the Record. I thank 
Senator Murray, the vice chair of the committee, for her leadership and 
cooperation with me in making these important assignments.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:

                        Subcommittee Assignments

       Senator Collins, as Chair of the Committee, and Senator 
     Murray, as Vice Chair of the Committee, are ex officio 
     members of all subcommittees of which they are not regular 
     members.

     Smith, Fischer, Mullin, Rounds, Shaheen (ranking member),
```
