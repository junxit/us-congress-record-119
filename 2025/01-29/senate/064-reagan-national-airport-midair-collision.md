# REAGAN NATIONAL AIRPORT MIDAIR COLLISION

**Speaking:** Hassan, Margaret Wood (H001076) [D-NH]

```
                REAGAN NATIONAL AIRPORT MIDAIR COLLISION

  Ms. HASSAN. Mr. President, before I start my remarks, I do want to 
note that we have had news just now of a terrible tragedy at DCA. There 
has been a plane crash. We do not know all the details yet, but it is 
fair to say that, here in DC and all around the country, we should all 
be praying for the loved ones of those involved in this crash, hoping 
for some positive news, but knowing that this was a very, very serious 
crash, and the news is not likely to be good as we learn more. Our 
hearts and our prayers go out to all of those affected.

                          ____________________
```
