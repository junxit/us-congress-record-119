# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Immortal God, Your Name is great throughout the Earth. We thank You 
for the undeserved blessings You give us each day. Lord, You bless us 
with life, health, faith, hope, and love. You give us Your peace. Great 
and marvelous are Your works.
  Today, guide the Members of this body with Your wisdom. Help them to 
resolve to become part of the solutions to our Nation's problems. Make 
clear to them the path of duty, and lead them in the doing of Your 
will. Lord, provide them with counsel to deal with complex challenges 
and infuse them with divine discernment to accomplish Your purposes.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
