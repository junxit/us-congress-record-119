# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3767

**Measures:** H.R. 3767

```
            By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
        H.R. 3767.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has authority under Article I, Section 8 of the 
     Constitution, which empowers Congress to provide for the 
     general welfare of the United States and make rules for the 
     government and regulation of the armed forces by supporting 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs
```
