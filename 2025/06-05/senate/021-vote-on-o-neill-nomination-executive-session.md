# Vote on O'Neill Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schatz, Brian (S001194) [D-HI], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                       Vote on O'Neill Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the O'Neill nomination?
  Mr. SCHATZ. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
```
