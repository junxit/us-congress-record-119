# Tribute to the Senate Pages (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Tribute to the Senate Pages

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today is the last session day for our 
spring pages, and I want to thank them for their service to the Senate 
and to our country over the last few months. The Senate could not do 
all that we do without their hard work--work that they do at all hours 
of the day, late at night, and while keeping up with schoolwork.
  So to this year's pages, thank you for all that you have contributed 
to the Senate this session, and best of luck in all your future 
endeavors.
  Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to print the names of the 
spring 2025 page class into the Congressional Record.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:
```
