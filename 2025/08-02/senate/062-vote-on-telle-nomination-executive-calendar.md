# Vote on Telle Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                        Vote on Telle Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Telle nomination?
  Mr. MURPHY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Kentucky (Mr. McConnell), the Senator from South Carolina 
(Mr. Scott), and the Senator from Mississippi (Mr. Wicker).
  Further, if present and voting: the Senator from Mississippi (Mr. 
Wicker) would have voted ``yea.''
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Colorado (Mr. Bennet), 
the Senator from Arizona (Mr. Gallego), and the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Welch) are necessarily absent.
  The result was announced--yeas 72, nays 22, as follows:
```
