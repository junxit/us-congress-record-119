# LEONARD G. `BUD' LOMELL, VA CLINIC

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 2170

```
                   LEONARD G. `BUD' LOMELL, VA CLINIC

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Committee on 
Veterans' Affairs be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 
2170, and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2170) to name the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in Toms River, New 
     Jersey, the Leonard G. `Bud' Lomell, VA Clinic, and for other 
     purposes.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 2170) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
