# Change of Vote (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Schatz, Brian (S001194) [D-HI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             Change of Vote

  Mr. SCHATZ. Mr. President, on rollcall vote No. 494, I voted no. It 
was my intention to vote yea; therefore, I ask unanimous consent that I 
be permitted to change my vote since it will not change the outcome.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Under the previous order, the motion to reconsider is considered made 
and laid upon the table, and the President will be immediately notified 
of the Senate's action.
  The majority leader.
```
