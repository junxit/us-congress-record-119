# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

Committee Meetings
FISCAL YEAR 2026 REVIEW OF THE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE'S CYBER POSTURE
Committee on Armed Services: On Friday, May 16, 2025, Subcommittee on 
Cyber, Information Technologies, and Innovation held a hearing entitled 
``Fiscal Year 2026 Review of the Department of Defense's Cyber 
Posture''. Testimony was heard from Laurie Buckhout, Performing the 
Duties of Assistant Secretary of Defense for Cyber Policy, Office of 
the Secretary of Defense, Department of Defense; and Lieutenant General 
William J. Hartman, U.S. Army, Acting Commander, U.S. Cyber Command, 
and Performing the Duties of Director, National Security Agency, and 
Chief, Central Security Service, Department of Defense.
