# RECOGNIZING NATHAN MORRIS, 2026 ARKANSAS SUPERINTENDENT OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Crawford, Eric A. "Rick" (C001087) [R-AR]

```
  RECOGNIZING NATHAN MORRIS, 2026 ARKANSAS SUPERINTENDENT OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ERIC A. ``RICK'' CRAWFORD

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. CRAWFORD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Nathan 
Morris, current Superintendent of the Cross County School District. He 
is a dedicated Arkansan, a passionate educator, and a strong leader.
  On September 15, 2025, Dr. Morris received the prestigious honor of 
2026 Arkansas Superintendent of the Year. This award is presented to 
those who successfully lead learning in their districts, demonstrate 
strength in both personal and organizational communication, show 
continuous improvement in administrative knowledge and skills, and 
actively participate in local community activities while maintaining a 
deep understanding of regional, national, and international issues.
  Dr. Morris' efforts have ensured a graduation rate of 98 percent with 
a growing academic proficiency rating. Cross County High School has 
been included in the lists by U.S. News and World Report for both Best 
High Schools and Best Charter High Schools. At the elementary school 
level, Cross County Elementary Technology Academy has been recognized 
as Top 10 World's Best Schools for Overcoming Adversity while under Dr. 
Morris' leadership.
  Dr. Morris began his tenure as superintendent in 2018 and has since 
guided Cross County through a significant transformation. He has 
implemented programs that foster teacher leadership, professional 
collaboration, and growth which have led to lowered office referrals, 
boosted teacher effectiveness, and 20 percent growth in enrollment.
  Dr. Morris exemplifies commitment, hard work, and an unwavering 
passion for academic success. His recognition as Superintendent of the 
Year is not only an incredible personal milestone, but a reflection of 
the great community we have throughout the First District of Arkansas.
  I want to offer my sincere congratulations to Dr. Morris, and I look 
forward to his continued work in education.

                          ____________________
```
