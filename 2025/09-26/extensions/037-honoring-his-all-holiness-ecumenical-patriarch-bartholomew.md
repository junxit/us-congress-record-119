# HONORING HIS ALL-HOLINESS ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
       HONORING HIS ALL-HOLINESS ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor His All-Holiness 
Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew of Constantinople, a spiritual leader 
recognized around the world as both a bridge builder and a peacemaker.
  For decades, Patriarch Bartholomew has endured harassment and 
persecution, yet he has remained steadfast in his mission to foster 
reconciliation between East and West, to promote interfaith dialogue, 
and to speak out forcefully against fanaticism and violence. His 
efforts to defend human rights and religious freedom have earned him 
deep respect among people of many faiths.
  Patriarch Bartholomew's unwavering commitment to peace, compassion, 
and justice has left a profound impact on the world, and his example 
continues to inspire people across the globe.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing his extraordinary 
service and lasting contributions to humanity.

                          ____________________
```
