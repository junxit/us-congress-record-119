# HONORING TEMPLE EMANU-EL'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
              HONORING TEMPLE EMANU-EL'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 75th Anniversary 
of Temple Emanu-El in Westfield, New Jersey.
  Since its founding in 1950, Temple Emanu-El has been committed to 
embracing innovation and creativity in the practice of Jewish life and 
support of the Jewish community. The congregation works to create a 
space for each member to find the expression of Judaism that makes them 
feel connected, included, and seen within their community. Temple 
Emanu-El was founded by 43 Westfield Jewish families of varying Jewish 
backgrounds who came together to create their own religious home. At 
this time, Jewish individuals could not purchase land in much of 
Westfield, and, despite local residents circulating petitions against 
the founding of a synagogue, the founders of Temple Emanu-El remained 
resilient and persevered.
  Through traditional Jewish liturgy with Reform Jewish prayer and 
practice, Temple Emanu-El established a one-of-a-kind expression of 
Reform Judaism. Now comprised of 950 families, Temple Emanu-El creates 
a space where members participate in Jewish ritual life, educational 
programming, and lifelong learning. The values that members learn 
within the walls translate into the greater community of Westfield and 
Union County, where congregants engage in local, state, and national 
advocacy, philanthropy, and justice. The temple also encourages members 
to form a meaningful connection to the Land and People of Israel. 
Temple Emanu-El stands as the model of an engaged, creative, and 
vibrant Reform synagogue.
  Mr. Speaker, as Temple Emanu-El celebrates 75 years of Jewish 
spiritual, cultural, and communal excellence, I am pleased to commend 
Senior Rabbi Ethan Prosnit and the congregation for being an integral 
part of the Westfield area. I invite my colleagues to join me in 
honoring this community truly dedicated to faith and education.
```
