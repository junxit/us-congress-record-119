# CELEBRATING NATIVITY MIGUEL SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
                   CELEBRATING NATIVITY MIGUEL SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
celebrate Nativity Miguel School, a school dedicated to providing 
quality education to students throughout Western New York. The school 
fosters academic and personal growth within a supportive community 
where students are empowered to reach their full potential.
  Established more than two decades ago, Nativity Miguel School was 
grounded in the values and educational principles of Father James F. 
Joyce. Father Joyce was sent to Buffalo, New York, to conduct a study 
of the educational needs and challenges of students in underserved 
communities. To address the problems, Father Joyce proposed the 
``Nativity Model,'' a proven method that has been successfully 
implemented in other Catholic schools throughout the state.
  Drawing from its four pillars of courage, compassion, community, and 
contemplation, Nativity Miguel School walks arm-in-arm with its 
students and families throughout their educational journey, long after 
they have graduated. The school's delivery of unique and effective 
education, including dedicated teachers, personal and peer mentoring, 
and a strong emphasis on learning, creates an environment where every 
student can thrive.

[[Page E904]]

  Today, as we commemorate the ribbon-cutting ceremony of Nativity 
Miguel School's newest location, let us celebrate the growth of a 
mission rooted in hope, opportunity, and transformative education for 
every student who walks through these doors. Each graduating class 
builds on this legacy, carrying forward the values, education, and 
sense of purpose that Nativity Miguel School instills, and Western New 
York is all the better for it.

                          ____________________
```
