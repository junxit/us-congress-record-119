# Waiving Quorum Call (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Marshall, Roger (M001198) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S.Res. 377

```
                          Waiving Quorum Call

  Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to waive the mandatory quorum 
call with respect to Executive Calendar No. 1, S. Res. 377.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. BARRASSO. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. MARSHALL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
