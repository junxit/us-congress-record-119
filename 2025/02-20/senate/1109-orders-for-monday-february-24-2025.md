# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, February 24; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to executive 
session and resume Executive Calendar No. 21, Daniel Driscoll to be 
Secretary of the Army; further, notwithstanding rule XXII, that the 
cloture motions with respect to the Driscoll and Greer nominations 
ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. For the information of all Senators, Senators should 
expect multiple votes on Monday evening.

                          ____________________
```
