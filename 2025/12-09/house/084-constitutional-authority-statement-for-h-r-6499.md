# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6499

**Measures:** H.R. 6499

```
           By Mr. FULCHER:
       H.R. 6499.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 allows Congress to make all 
     laws ``which shall be necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution'' any of Congress' enumerated powers, including 
     Congress's powers over appropriations.
```
