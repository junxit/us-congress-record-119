# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE AT-LARGE CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF ALASKA

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 310

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
   REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE AT-LARGE CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF ALASKA

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I call up H. Res. 310 and ask unanimous 
consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 310

       Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office 
     of Representative from the at-large Congressional District of 
     Alaska is dismissed on the grounds that, under section 2(1) 
     of the Federal Contested Election Act (2 U.S.C. 381(1)), the 
     House of Representatives has jurisdiction over official 
     general and special elections to choose a Representative in, 
     or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress, but 
     does not have jurisdiction over a primary election, or a 
     caucus or convention of a political party.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
