# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTEENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 312

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
  REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTEENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I call up H. Res. 312 and ask unanimous 
consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 312

       Resolved, That the election contest filed on January 9, 
     2025, relating to the office of Representative from the 
     Fourteenth Congressional District of Florida is dismissed on 
     the grounds of untimely filing with the House of 
     Representatives.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
