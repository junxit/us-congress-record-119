# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTEENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 308

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
  REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTEENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I call up H. Res. 308 and ask unanimous 
consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Taylor). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
  The title of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 308

       Resolved, That the election contest filed on November 17, 
     2024, relating to the office of Representative from the 
     Fourteenth Congressional District of Florida is dismissed on 
     the grounds that, under section 2(1) of the Federal Contested 
     Election Act (2 U.S.C. 381(1)), the House of Representatives 
     has jurisdiction over official general and special elections 
     to choose a Representative in, or Delegate or Resident 
     Commissioner to, the Congress, but does not have jurisdiction 
     over a primary election, or a caucus or convention of a 
     political party.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
