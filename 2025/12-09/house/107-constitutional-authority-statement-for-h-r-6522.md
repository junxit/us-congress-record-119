# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6522

**Measures:** H.R. 6522

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 6522.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 3 of section 8 of article I of the 
     United States Constitution, Congress has the power to 
     ``provide for the common Defence and general Welfare of the 
     United States.'' Additional authority derives from article I, 
     section 8, clauses 12 through 14, which empower Congress to 
     raise and support the Armed Forces and to make rules 
     governing such forces. These provisions collectively 
     authorize legislation relating to the health care, benefits, 
     and support of veterans of the Armed Forces.
```
