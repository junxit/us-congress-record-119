# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 3, H.Con.Res. 35

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-31. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the General 
     Assembly of the State of Ohio, relative to Senate Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 3, to urge Congress to make changes to the 
     Fair Labor Standards Act to allow a person under 16 years of 
     age to be employed between 7 p.m. and 9 p.m. during the 
     school year; to the Committee on Education and Workforce.
       ML-32. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Texas, relative to House Concurrent Resolution 35, urging the 
     United States Congress to amend the Internal Revenue Code to 
     make spaceports eligible for tax-exempt private activity 
     bonds; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
```
