# Secretary Hegseth (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           Secretary Hegseth

  Mr. President, on the NDAA and Hegseth, well, today, as has already 
been widely reported, Secretary Hegseth and top administration 
officials will hold a classified briefing for the Gang of 8.
  At the Gang of 8 hearing, I will ask Secretary Hegseth directly: Will 
he commit to providing all Senators access to unedited tapes of the 
September 2 boat strike?
  Again, Hegseth, will you commit, with no BS, to providing all 
Senators with access to the unedited tapes of the September 2 boat 
strikes?
  Because the longer the White House and the Pentagon hold on to the 
tapes, the more the American people are wondering: What are Donald 
Trump and Pete Hegseth trying to cover up? Are they lying to the 
American people or are they just trying to hide their incompetence?
  No one in the administration can seem to get their story straight. 
Last week, Donald Trump said he would have ``no problem releasing the 
full video of the strike.'' Then, yesterday, he walked back his 
statement, claiming he never said it and it was fake ABC news.
  You know, Donald Trump says something, doesn't like what he said and 
made a mistake, and then he says it is fake news. But he said it, and 
the American people heard it, and it is on tape, and then he blames one 
of the networks for simply broadcasting what he said. What is going on 
in this country and how do we let this man do what he does--destroying 
our values, our morals, the fact that America has always been an honest 
country?
  So let me be clear. The only thing that can bring more clarity to the 
American people is the full release of the unedited tapes, plain and 
simple. Republicans and Democrats in Congress agree. That is why there 
is a provision in the most recent version of the NDAA that demands a 
release of the unedited tapes to Congress. If the Pentagon fails to 
hand over those tapes, Pete Hegseth's travel budget will be slashed by 
25 percent until he complies. That was a bipartisan shot across the bow 
to Donald Trump--hand over the tapes--done by Republicans. I salute 
them for their courage in bucking him and bucking Hegseth.
  The inclusion of this NDAA provision restricting travel shows 
Congress is united in wanting to see these tapes as soon as possible, 
and both Donald Trump and Pete Hegseth must comply, say the Republican 
Senators, or the DOD is penalized.
  I strongly support this provision and worked with Senator Reed, 
Senator Wicker, and others to keep it in. I hope it will compel Donald 
Trump and Pete Hegseth to do the right thing: release the tapes and 
give the American people the transparency we all deserve.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority whip.
```
