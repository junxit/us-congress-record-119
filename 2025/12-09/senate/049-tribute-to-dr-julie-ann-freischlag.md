# TRIBUTE TO DR. JULIE ANN FREISCHLAG

**Speaking:** Tillis, Thomas (T000476) [R-NC]

```
                  TRIBUTE TO DR. JULIE ANN FREISCHLAG

● Mr. TILLIS. Mr. President, today I rise to recognize the 
exceptional career of Dr. Julie Ann Freischlag, a nationally respected 
surgeon, educator, and healthcare leader whose service has transformed 
Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist and strengthened medical care across 
North Carolina.
  Dr. Freischlag arrived at Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist in 2017 
after a distinguished career as a vascular surgeon, professor, section 
chief, and academic leader, including service as dean of the school of 
medicine at the University of California, Davis. Since coming to North 
Carolina, she has guided a period of significant growth, including the 
addition of High Point and Wilkes medical centers, the creation of the 
Birth Center, and the expansion of the Wake Forest University School of 
Medicine to Charlotte.
  She also played an important role in the 2020 integration of Wake 
Forest Baptist and Atrium Health and later in the formation of Advocate 
Health, where she became the system's first chief academic officer. 
Under her leadership, research funding at the Wake Forest University 
School of Medicine has nearly doubled, reflecting her deep commitment 
to scientific discovery and innovation.
  Throughout her career, Dr. Freischlag has been a national leader in 
academic medicine, including service as president of the Society of 
Vascular Surgery, president of the American College of Surgeons, and 
chair of the Association of American Medical Colleges Board of 
Directors. She has published extensively, delivered hundreds of 
presentations and lectures, and mentored generations of students, 
residents, and faculty.
  While her accomplishments are extraordinary, she is equally admired 
for her warmth, optimism, and the support she provides to colleagues 
and students. In recognition of her profound impact, the medical center 
board named its newest care tower the Julie Ann Freischlag Tower as a 
lasting tribute to her leadership and legacy.
  Dr. Freischlag has dedicated her career to improving health and 
helping others, and North Carolina is grateful for her remarkable 
service. I extend my sincere appreciation to her for all she has done 
for our State, our medical community, and the many patients whose lives 
have been touched by her work.●
```
