# Border Security (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                            Border Security

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, it wasn't long ago that the southern border 
was in a state of chaos. During the Biden administration, we routinely 
saw hundreds of thousands of illegal immigrants arriving at the border 
in a single month. It was a situation that the Democratic Senator from 
Pennsylvania likened to the population of the city of Pittsburgh 
showing up at the border.
  And that is to say nothing of the 1.9 million known ``got-aways'' who 
slipped into the country during the Biden years or the suspected 
terrorists, gang members, and other criminals who were among those 
coming across our open border.
  And then there was the Biden administration's catch-and-release 
approach to illegal immigration and the way the Biden administration 
abused asylum and parole to allow individuals to take up residence in 
our country.
  It is hard to believe that the Biden administration and Democrats 
allowed their border crisis to rage quite literally for years, but they 
did.
  Even the New York Times now admits that President Biden chose not to 
secure the border when given the

[[Page S8550]]

chance. The Times reported this weekend:

       [An] examination of Mr. Biden's record found that he and 
     his closest advisers repeatedly rebuffed recommendations that 
     could have addressed the border crisis faster.

  Let me repeat that: They ``repeatedly rebuffed recommendations'' to 
ease the border crisis.
  Well, I am glad that the New York Times is finally reporting what we 
have known for a long time, and that is that the Biden administration 
was completely uninterested in solving the border crisis.
  Fortunately, those days are over. When President Trump and 
Republicans took control this year, we moved to quickly restore order 
at the southern border, and the numbers speak for themselves.
  Customs and Border Protection reported a record low number of 
encounters in November. Fewer than 250 people per day are being 
apprehended. Compare that to the Biden administration, where law 
enforcement was catching more than 5,000 per day on average.
  And, in November, for the seventh month in a row, zero illegal 
immigrants have been released into the United States.
  So under the Trump administration, we have a 95-percent drop in 
apprehensions at the southern border, and not a single one of the 
individuals apprehended is being released into the country. This is 
remarkable, remarkable progress. This is what the American people 
expect when it comes to securing our border.
  Republicans are doing everything we can to ensure that this will be 
the new standard for border security for a long time to come.
  Our One Big Beautiful Bill, which we passed in July, makes a 
generational investment in the resources that law enforcement needs to 
secure the border and deter illegal immigration. We funded completion 
of the border wall, new technology and equipment for law enforcement, 
additional Border Patrol agents, expanded detention capacity, and 
expanded partnerships with local law enforcement to enforce immigration 
law.
  These investments are beginning to be implemented across the 
immigration system. Hiring efforts are underway to bring on additional 
agents. More than 1,000 State and local law enforcement agencies have 
partnered with the Federal Government to enforce immigration laws 
through the 287(g) Program. And the Department of Homeland Security is 
awarding contracts for new technologies and wall construction at the 
southern border--investments that will improve law enforcement's 
ability to apprehend the illegal immigrants and smugglers who attempt 
to cross our southern border.
  Republicans promised to secure the border. We wasted no time in 
delivering on that promise. And thanks to the One Big Beautiful Bill, 
the American people can be certain that border security is here to 
stay.
  I am grateful to the men and women on the frontlines who come to work 
every day committed to a safer and a more secure America, and 
Republicans will continue to work to ensure that they have everything 
they need to get the job done.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
