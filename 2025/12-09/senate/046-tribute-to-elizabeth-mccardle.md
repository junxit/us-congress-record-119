# TRIBUTE TO ELIZABETH McCARDLE

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO ELIZABETH McCARDLE

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Elizabeth 
McCardle, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work 
she has done on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. 
McCardle is a graduate of Mitchell High School in Mitchell, SD. She is 
currently attending the University of South Dakota, where she studies 
criminal justice. Ms. McCardle is a dedicated and diligent individual 
who has been devoted to getting the most out of her internship 
experience. She has been a true asset to my office. I extend my sincere 
thanks and appreciation to Ms. McCardle for all of the work she has 
done and wish her continued success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
