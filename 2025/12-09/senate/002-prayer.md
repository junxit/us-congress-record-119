# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Ever-present and ever-gracious God, touch the hearts of our lawmakers 
today with the warmth and wonder of Your wisdom and grace. Infuse their 
lives with an exemplary integrity that illuminates the darkness of 
doubt, division, and despair.
  May our Senators see beyond baffling events to the power of Your 
prevailing providence, providing them with a vision of a better nation 
and world.
  Lord, use our legislators for such power that they may honor their 
calling by faithfully serving You and country.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
