              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. MARSHALL:
       S. 3389. A bill to lower health care costs for Americans; 
     to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. BLUMENTHAL (for himself and Mr. Graham):
       S. 3390. A bill to require a report on the purchases of 
     Iranian oil by the People's Republic of China, support from 
     entities and individuals in the People's Republic of China 
     for Iran's ballistic missile program, and for other purposes; 
     to the Select Committee on Intelligence.
           By Mr. HUSTED:
       S. 3391. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to extend and modify the enhanced premium tax credits, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. WYDEN (for himself, Mr. Budd, Mr. Merkley, Mr. 
             Tillis, Mr. Welch. Mr. Bennet. Ms. Baldwin, Mrs.

       S. 3392. A bill to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to 
     designate an Agritourism Advisor, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.

             Hollen, and Mr. Welch):
       S. 3393. A bill to authorize contributions to the United 
     Nations Population Fund, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.

             Blackburn, and Mr. Graham):
       S. 3394. A bill to direct the United States Sentencing 
     Commission to amend the sentencing guideline relating to 
     child sexual abuse material; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.

       S. 3395. A bill to expand the telescreening mammography

     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. GRASSLEY (for himself, Mr. Durbin, Ms. 
             Klobuchar, Mr. Cornyn, and Mr. Graham):
       S. 3397. A bill to make coercion of children to commit harm

     on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. GRASSLEY (for himself, Mr. Durbin, Ms.

             Graham):

     criminalize intentional threats to distribute child sexual 
     abuse material, and to provide appropriate penalties for the 
     use of child sexual abuse material to extort or coerce 
     victims; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. YOUNG (for himself, Mr. Coons, Mr. Moran, and

       S. 3399. A bill to authorize the President to enter into

     Committee on Finance.

       S. 3401. A bill to establish, improve, or expand high-

       S. 3406. A bill to establish a grant program for innovative

     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       S. 3407. A bill to direct the Secretary of Energy to 
     establish a Western Refined Fuel Storage Reserve as part of 
     the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. MERKLEY (for himself, Ms. Cortez Masto, Mr. 
             Wyden, Mr. Blumenthal, and Mr. Kim):
       S. 3408. A bill to prohibit a United States currency 
     featuring the likeness of a living or sitting President; to 
     the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
           By Mr. DAINES:
       S. 3409. A bill to reaffirm that the Bureau of Reclamation 
     retains exclusive ownership, operational control, and 
     financial responsibility for the Lower Yellowstone Fish 
     Bypass Channel, ensuring long-term conservation of the 
     endangered pallid sturgeon and other native aquatic species 
     in the Yellowstone River while protecting the Lower 
     Yellowstone Irrigation Project and District from undue 
     financial and operational burdens, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
