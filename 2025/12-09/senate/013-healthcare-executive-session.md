# Healthcare (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                               Healthcare

  Mr. President, but I have good news for the President. He and the 
Republicans do have one chance left to do something meaningful about 
lowering costs, something real--not a speech, something real--and that 
is to work with Democrats to extend the enhanced ACA premium tax 
credits before they expire at the end of the year.
  Democrats have put forth a simple proposal for a clean, 3-year 
extension of the ACA credits. We will move forward on this bill on 
Thursday. Senate Republicans have 2 days--2 days--to choose to vote to 
lower the American people's healthcare costs or to vote to continue 
sending those costs through the roof. That is what is at stake on this 
vote.
  If our bill becomes law, we will accomplish the single most important 
thing we can do right now to lower prices, prevent premiums for tens of 
millions of Americans from shooting through the roof. We have an 
obligation to act now.
  Now, if Republicans are left to their own devices, there is not a 
snowball's chance in hell that they fix this healthcare crisis on their 
own. Republicans are nowhere, by their own admission.
  Leader Thune has come down to the floor day after day excoriating the 
ACA, brings a lot of charts, has quotes at the ready, and on and on and 
on, but he doesn't have a plan. He has no proposal.
  Charts are not a plan, Leader Thune.
  It is not enough for Republicans to say that they don't like the ACA. 
That is not a secret. We know that. They have been trying to get rid of 
it since 2008. They nearly succeeded until the courage of John McCain 
stopped them in 2019.
  The real question is, What will Republicans do to prevent premiums 
from skyrocketing in 3 weeks? So far, we have heard no real answer. The 
only ideas we have heard from Republicans are loaded with poison pills, 
unworkable restrictions, and don't do anything to bring premiums down, 
which is most important. None of their proposals address extending the 
ACA credits--the most important thing we can do now. So those kinds of 
proposals don't get the American people's support or Democrats' 
support.
  The party is adrift at sea. The Republican Party is adrift at sea, 
trying to find a plan. So far, we have heard no serious plan at all. 
And that is not just me saying it. Here is what a Republican Senator 
said yesterday:


[[Page S8551]]


  

       I mean, what signal will it send if Republicans say, 
     ``we're going to say no to the Democrats' plan. But we're not 
     gonna offer anything?'' The message that would send is: 
     ``Good luck to the American people, we don't really care.''

  That is from a Republican Senator. I couldn't have said it better 
myself.
  So Republicans should join us on Thursday, vote yes on our proposal, 
extend the ACA tax credits by 3 years, and greatly reduce the specter 
of premiums going up and up and up.
```
