# TRIBUTE TO STEPHEN A. EDWARDS

**Speaking:** Kaine, Tim (K000384) [D-VA]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO STEPHEN A. EDWARDS

● Mr. KAINE. Mr. President, I rise to recognize and honor 
Stephen A. Edwards as he concludes his tenure as CEO and executive 
director of the Virginia Port Authority. Mr. Edwards leadership from 
January 2021 to December 2025 has steered the Port of Virginia to new 
heights of success, positioning it as one of the most modern, 
efficient, and future-ready maritime gateways in the United States.
  Over the past 5 years, the Port of Virginia has emerged as one of the 
top container gateways in the United States, ranking as the sixth-
busiest container port in the Nation and handling more than 3.28 
million TEUs in FY 2023, following a record 3.7 million TEUs in FY 2022 
and 3.5 million TEUs in FY 2024, the second-best performance in its 
history. During this same

[[Page S8573]]

period, the Port of Virginia has been consistently cited by customers 
and industry observers as one of America's best ports. Recent economic 
impact studies from the College of William & Mary have documented that, 
in FY 2022, the Port of Virginia and Virginia's maritime industry 
helped support approximately 565,000 jobs, more than $124.1 billion in 
spending, and $63 billion in gross State product, and those numbers 
have grown since then.
  Stephen Edwards brought more than two decades of international 
experience leading major port and terminal operations to the 
Commonwealth and provided the Port of Virginia with a global 
perspective on port competitiveness, maritime logistics, and 
infrastructure delivery. Mr. Edwards advanced a $450 million dredging 
and widening initiative that upon completion will give the Commonwealth 
the deepest and widest commercial shipping channels on the East Coast. 
During his tenure, the Port of Virginia executed the $1.4 billion 
Gateway Investment Program, and delivered it on time and on budget, to 
expand capacity, modernize operations, and position Virginia to handle 
the next generation of ultra-large container vessels. The Port also 
completed major modernization efforts at Norfolk International 
Terminals, the Virginia Inland Port, and Richmond Marine Terminal. It 
doubled its port-wide rail lift capacity to 2 million rail lifts per 
year, significantly strengthening Virginia's intermodal reach into the 
Midwest. Finally, it concluded the $220 million transformation of 
Portsmouth Marine Terminals into a major staging and logistics platform 
for offshore wind development, supporting Virginia's position as a 
national leader in clean energy infrastructure.
  Throughout his tenure, Mr. Edwards has been widely recognized for his 
global maritime expertise, his commitment to operational excellence, 
his stewardship of public resources, and his dedication to 
strengthening Virginia's position as a premier gateway for 
international commerce. His staff was also a reliable source of helpful 
information on the impact of Federal policy changes on the port and 
quick-turnaround information that helped me and my team advocate 
effectively for Federal infrastructure resources to come to Virginia.
  On behalf of myself and Senator Warner and our colleagues in the 
Virginia congressional delegation, we wish Stephen A. Edwards well in 
his future endeavors and thank him for his contributions to Virginia's 
economic success.●
```
