# HONORING THE LIFE OF ERNEST L. "ERNIE" STEVENS, JR.

**Speaking:** Davids, Sharice (D000629) [D-KS]

```
         HONORING THE LIFE OF ERNEST L. ``ERNIE'' STEVENS, JR.

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Ernest L. ``Ernie'' Stevens, Jr. Ernie was a fierce and loyal 
advocate for Indian country who dedicated his life to advancing tribal 
sovereignty and protecting tribal rights through his advocacy.
  Ernie was an active leader among his tribe and community, the Oneida 
Nation of Wisconsin. Ernie most notably served as the Chairman for the 
Indian Gaming Association for more than two decades and demonstrated 
his unwavering commitment to tribal communities across the country 
through his policy contributions and his advancements to Indian gaming.
  Ernie was a mentor, a passionate community leader, and a friend to 
all. He will be remembered for his countless contributions and 
commitments kept to Indian country. He will be deeply missed.

                          ____________________
```
