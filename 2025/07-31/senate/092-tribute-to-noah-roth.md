# TRIBUTE TO NOAH ROTH

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO NOAH ROTH

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Noah Roth, an intern in 
my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has done for me, 
my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several weeks.
  Noah is a recent graduate of South Dakota State University, SDSU, in 
Brookings, SD, having earned a degree in sports and recreation. This 
fall, he will return to SDSU to pursue a master's degree in sports and 
recreation administration. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated 
to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Noah for all of the 
fine

[[Page S4989]]

work he has done and wish his continued success in the years to come.
```
