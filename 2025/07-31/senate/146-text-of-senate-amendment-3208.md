# Text of Senate Amendment 3208

**Speaking:** Rosen, Jacky (R000608) [D-NV]

```
  SA 3208. Ms. ROSEN (for herself and Ms. Cortez Masto) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by her to the bill H.R. 3944, making 
appropriations for military construction, the Department of Veterans 
Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2026, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place in title II of division A, insert 
     the following:
       Sec. ___.  Not later than 90 days after the date of the 
     enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs 
     shall provide to the Committee on Appropriations of the 
     Senate and the Committee on Appropriations of the House of 
     Representatives a briefing on the current status, path 
     forward, and timeline to construct a new medical center of 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs in Reno, Nevada, utilizing 
     in full the funds that have been previously appropriated for 
     such purpose prior to their expiration.
                                 ______
```
