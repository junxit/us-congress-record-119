# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 186, Andrew Puzder, of Tennessee, 
     to be Representative of the United States of America to the 
     European Union, with the rank of Ambassador Extraordinary and 
     Plenipotentiary.
         John Thune, John R. Curtis, Bernie Moreno, Tommy 
           Tuberville, Kevin Cramer, Ron Johnson, Shelley Moore 
           Capito, John Boozman, John Barrasso, Marsha Blackburn, 
           Roger Marshall, Jon A. Husted, Roger F. Wicker, Jim 
           Banks, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Ted Budd, David McCormick.

           

                          ____________________
```
