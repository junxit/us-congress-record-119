# TRIBUTE TO JORJA WHEMPNER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JORJA WHEMPNER

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jorja Whempner, an intern 
in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.
  Jorja is a graduate of Summit High School in Wilmot, SD. Currently, 
she is attending Northern State University in Aberdeen, SD, where she 
is pursuing degrees in history and secondary education. She is a hard 
worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jorja for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
