# Text of Senate Amendment 3328

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  SA 3328. Mr. SCHIFF submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 2296, to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
2026 for military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, 
to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of subtitle F of title X, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. SNAP WORK REQUIREMENT EXEMPTION FOR VETERANS.

       Section 6(o)(3) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 
     U.S.C. 2015(o)(3)) is amended--
       (1) by redesignating subparagraphs (F) and (G) as 
     subparagraphs (G) and (H), respectively; and
       (2) by inserting after subparagraph (E) the following:
       ``(F) a veteran;''.
                                 ______
```
