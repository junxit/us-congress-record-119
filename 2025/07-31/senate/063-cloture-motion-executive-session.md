# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 151, Jason Reding Quinones, of 
     Florida, to be United States Attorney for the Southern 
     District of Florida for the term of four years.
         John Thune, Tim Sheehy, Markwayne Mullin, Mike Crapo, 
           John Barrasso, John Boozman, Bill Cassidy, Kevin 
           Cramer, Todd Young, David McCormick, Rick Scott of 
           Florida, Mike Rounds, Marsha Blackburn, Shelley Moore 
           Capito, Pete Ricketts, Ashley B. Moody, Roger Marshall.

           

                          ____________________
```
