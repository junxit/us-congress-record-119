# Text of Senate Amendment 3274

**Speaking:** Cruz, Ted (C001098) [R-TX]

```
  SA 3274. Mr. CRUZ submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 2296, to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
2026 for military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, 
to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in subtitle D of title I, insert 
     the following:

     SEC. __. EXTENSION OF PROHIBITION ON CERTAIN REDUCTIONS TO B-
                   1 BOMBER AIRCRAFT SQUADRONS.

       Subsection (d)(1) of section 133 of the National Defense 
     Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2022 (Public Law 117-81; 
     135 Stat. 1574), as most recently amended by section 146 of 
     the Servicemember Quality of Life Improvement and National 
     Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2025 (Public Law 
     118-159; 138 Stat. 1810), is further amended by striking 
     ``September 30, 2026'' and inserting ``September 30, 2030''.
                                 ______
```
