proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations en 
bloc: Calendar Nos. 325 through 340 and all nominations on the 
Secretary's desk, with the exception of PN 89; that the nominations be 
confirmed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order to any of the nominations; that the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the 
Senate then resume legislation session.
