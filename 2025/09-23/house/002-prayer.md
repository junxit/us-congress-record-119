# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, bless the leaders of our land. Watch over our country 
now and in the days to come. Grant our Representatives wisdom, 
integrity, and courage in the exercise of their duties. Give them quiet 
spirits and steadfast hearts as they seek to provide for the needs of 
our people.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
