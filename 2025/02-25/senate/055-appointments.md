# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, and upon the recommendation of the Majority Leader, pursuant 
to 22 U.S.C. 2761, as amended, appoints the following Senator as 
Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the British-American 
Interparliamentary Group Conference during the 119th Congress: the 
Honorable John Boozman of Arkansas.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President pro tempore, and upon the 
recommedation of the Majority Leader, pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 2761, as 
amended, appoints the following Senator as Vice Chairman of the Senate 
Delegation to the British-American Interparliamentary Group Conference 
during the 119th Congress: the Honorable Sheldon Whitehouse of Rhode 
Island.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 
2761, 276d-276g, as amended, appoints the following Senator as Vice 
Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the Canada-U.S. Interparliamentary 
Group Conference during the 119th Congress: the Honorable Amy Klobuchar 
of Minnesota.
```
