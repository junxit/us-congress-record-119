# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  The Word of the Lord is right and true. O Lord, bind Your Word this 
day to our hearts that it would guide our steps and direct our lives 
with Your unwavering righteousness.
  By Your Word the heavens were made and the starry host by the breath 
of Your mouth. Speak Your Word into our lives that what we do would be 
as You command, and what we profess would revere You.
  Lord, You foil the plans of the nations, You thwart the purposes of 
the peoples. Then show forth Your plans which are sure and stand firm 
forever. Reveal the purposes of Your grace plan through all 
generations.
  For You love righteousness and justice. Fill the Earth and all who 
labor in this place with Your love.
  We wait in hope for You, Lord, for You alone are our help and shield. 
May Your unfailing love be with us, Lord, even as we put our hope in 
You.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
