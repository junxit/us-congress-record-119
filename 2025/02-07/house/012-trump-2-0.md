  (Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to address

on government reform, our hope is for more efficiency, lower costs, and 
stronger opportunities. It is clear that these policies are hurting 
everyday Americans. Mr. Speaker, this is not normal. It is immoral, and 
it is unlawful.
