                    EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

       * Andrew Hughes, of Texas, to be Deputy Secretary of 
     Housing and Urban Development.

     Department of Housing and Urban Development.

     Supervision of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve 
     System for a term of four years.
       By Mr. COTTON for the Select Committee on Intelligence.
       * John Andrew Eisenberg, of Virginia, to be an Assistant 
     Attorney General.

subject to the nominee's commitment to respond to requests to appear
