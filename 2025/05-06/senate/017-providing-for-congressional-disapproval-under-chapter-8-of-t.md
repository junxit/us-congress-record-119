 RELATING TO ``GLEN CANYON NATIONAL RECREATION AREA: MOTOR VEHICLES''--
                           Motion to Proceed

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 65, H.J. 
Res. 60.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 65, H.J. Res. 60, a joint 
     resolution providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the National Park Service relating to ``Glen 
     Canyon National Recreation Area: Motor Vehicles''.

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.

immediately.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The clerk will call the roll.
