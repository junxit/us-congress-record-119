# PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL UNDER CHAPTER 8 OF TITLE 5, UNITED STATES CODE, OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE OFFICE OF THE COMPTROLLER OF THE CURRENCY OF THE DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY...

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 13

                                Proceed

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 69, S.J. Res. 13, a joint 
     resolution providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency of 
     the Department of the Treasury relating to the review of 
     applications under the Bank Merger Act.
