# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2995

**Measures:** H.R. 2995

```
           By Ms. DeGETTE:
       H.R. 2995.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3, provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.'' The single 
     subject of this legislation is: Remediation of harm from 
     pollution
```
