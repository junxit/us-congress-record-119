# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. Vincent DeRosa, Archdiocese of Washington, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  O God who creates and sustains all, living in times of great change 
and discernment, we ask that You bless this Chamber. Grant to its 
Members deliberative spirits, humble hearts, and faculties completely 
dedicated to the service of our people that the United States of 
America may ever abide in peace, prosperity, and fraternal charity 
among the nations.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
