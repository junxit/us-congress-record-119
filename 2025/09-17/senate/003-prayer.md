# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You are holy. We rejoice because we are sustained by 
Your strength. We receive honor because of the victories You bring to 
our lives.
  Bless our lawmakers. Increase their faith in You. May they receive 
joy as they strive to follow Your guidance. Renew their lives with Your 
goodness. Empower them to make our Nation and world better as they 
receive inspiration by practicing Your precepts. Continue to be their 
mighty fortress in times of trouble.
  Lord, we praise You for Your glorious power. We celebrate Your mighty 
acts.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
