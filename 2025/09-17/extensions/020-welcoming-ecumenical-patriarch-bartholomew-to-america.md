# WELCOMING ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW TO AMERICA

**Speaking:** Crank, Jeff (C001137) [R-CO]

```
         WELCOMING ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW TO AMERICA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JEFF CRANK

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 17, 2025

  Mr. CRANK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to welcome Ecumenical Patriarch 
Bartholomew, the Spiritual Head of the second largest Christian church 
in the world who is visiting our Nation's Capital this week. Back home 
in Colorado's 5th Congressional District, Archangel Michael Greek 
Orthodox Church is the home for many of my constituents of the Greek 
Orthodox faith. I welcome the Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew, wish 
him well during his time in America, and thank him for his leadership 
for the Orthodox Christian faith around the world, numbering 300 
million.

                          ____________________
```
