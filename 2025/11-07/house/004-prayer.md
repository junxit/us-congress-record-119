# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Most gracious God, we appeal to You, laying before You our best-laid 
plans and our last resorts in our effort to set this time of upheaval 
to rights.
  If these last weeks have taught us anything, it is that, though we 
have worked, even pleaded, for resolution of the discord and 
disagreement, for relief from this political impasse and any 
intransigence, and for the provision of resources to those who bear the 
relentless burdens of these days, we have learned that, without Your 
intercession, even our all falls short.
  Only Your grace is sufficient for us, for Your strength is made 
perfect in our weakness and made available to us when we acknowledge 
our want.
  For Your sake, may we quiet our heated provocations, confront our 
hardheaded positions, and confess our harsh attitudes toward colleagues 
with whom we must work to move forward.
  Then, may Your sovereign power rest on us, and the work You have 
called us to do be blessed.
  In the strength found only in Your name do we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
