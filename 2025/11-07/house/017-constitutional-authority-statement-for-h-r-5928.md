# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5928

**Measures:** H.R. 5928

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 5928.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation under 
     Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, including its 
     authority to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying 
     into execution the powers vested by the Constitution in the 
     Government of the United States (Art. I, Sec. 8, Cl. 18).
```
