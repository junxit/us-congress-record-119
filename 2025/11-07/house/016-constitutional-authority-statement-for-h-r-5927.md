# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5927

**Measures:** H.R. 5927

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 5927.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation under 
     Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, including its 
     authority to provide for the common defense, to regulate 
     commerce with foreign nations, and to make all laws necessary 
     and proper for carrying these powers into execution (Art. I, 
     Sec. 8, Clauses 1, 3, and 18).
```
