# HONORING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF SUNSHINE HAVEN, INC. FOR PROVIDING HOSPICE CARE

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
  HONORING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF SUNSHINE HAVEN, INC. FOR PROVIDING 
                              HOSPICE CARE

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 7, 2025

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
25th anniversary of Sunshine Haven, a local hospice care center in 
Olmito, Texas.
  Throughout their 25 years, Sunshine Haven has shown true care and 
empathy for their patients. Their work highlights the best of South 
Texas--caring for the most vulnerable of our neighbors and community 
members. I am honored to represent such dedicated individuals who 
devote their time and compassion to caring for our senior citizens.
  The end-of-life care Sunshine Haven provides brings peace of mind to 
patients and their families, knowing their loved ones are cared for 
with dignity, comfort, and respect.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of a grateful South Texas, I congratulate 
Sunshine Haven and all those who have contributed to its mission over 
the past two and a half decades. May they continue to serve and care 
for our communities for many years to come.

                          ____________________
```
