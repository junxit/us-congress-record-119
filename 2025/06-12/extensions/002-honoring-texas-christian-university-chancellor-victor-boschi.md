# HONORING TEXAS CHRISTIAN UNIVERSITY CHANCELLOR VICTOR BOSCHINI

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
     HONORING TEXAS CHRISTIAN UNIVERSITY CHANCELLOR VICTOR BOSCHINI

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 12, 2025

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Texas 
Christian University's Chancellor Victor Boschini on his exceptional 
tenure as TCU'S Chancellor and his transition to Chancellor Emeritus. 
In his 22 years of service to the university, Chancellor Boschini has 
been renowned for his dedication to executing TCU's mission Under his 
guidance, TCU's reputation as an institution for academic excellence, 
student achievement, and community has grown considerably. In his new 
role, he will continue his remarkable career as an educator by 
remaining involved in teaching, fundraising, and alumni engagement.
  Chancellor Boschini helped facilitate several milestones at TCU. In 
the last few years, he has overseen the completion of 35 new buildings 
and renovation projects, including the Van Cliburn Concert Hall and the 
Burnett School of Medicine. He also helped spearhead the $1 billion 
Lead On, a fundraising initiative that will impact students at TCU for 
generations to come. His dedication to excellence has made TCU a hub 
for academics, athletic achievement, and a significant benefit to the 
state and country.
  In addition to academics, Chancellor Boschini also oversaw incredible 
athletic success at the university. Since joining the Big 12 in 2012, 
TCU has won 20 conference championships. In 2022, the TCU Football Team 
concluded its historic season in the College Football Playoff National 
Championship. The continuous athletic success has helped bolster TCU to 
the national stage and has created opportunities for athletes from 
around the world to play in Fort Worth.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize Chancellor Victor Boschini on his exceptional career and 
leadership at TCU. His work ethic, passion for education, and 
dedication towards the university will have a lasting impact on the 
City of Fort Worth. I congratulate him on his spectacular career and 
wish the best to him and his family.

                          ____________________
```
