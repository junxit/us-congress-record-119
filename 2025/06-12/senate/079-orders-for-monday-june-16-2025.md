# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 16, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 16, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 4 p.m. on 
Monday, June 16; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motions filed on June 12, ripen 
at 5:30 p.m. and the Senate vote on the motion to invoke cloture on 
Executive Calendar No. 145, Gary Andres.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
