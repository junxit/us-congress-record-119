# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 28, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 28, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m., 
Monday, July 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, morning hour be deemed expired, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed, and the Senate resume consideration of 
Executive Calendar No. 85, Sam Brown; that notwithstanding rule XXII, 
the cloture motions filed on July 24 ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
Senators should expect one vote at 5:30 p.m. on Monday and two votes at 
approximately 8 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
