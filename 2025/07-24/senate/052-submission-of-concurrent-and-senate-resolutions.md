  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

     on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Ms. DUCKWORTH (for herself, Mr. Reed, Mr. Van 
             Hollen, Ms. Warren, Ms. Baldwin, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. 
             Coons, Mr. Hickenlooper, Mr. Padilla, Mrs. 
             Gillibrand, Mr. Whitehouse, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. 
             Blumenthal, Ms. Hirono, Mr. Kaine, Mr. Kelly, Mrs. 
             Murray, Mr. Sanders, Mr. Warnock, Mr. King, Mr. 
             Welch, Mr. Booker, Ms. Blunt Rochester, Mr. Durbin,

     with Disabilities Act of 1990 and calling for further action

     disabilities to work and live in the community; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
