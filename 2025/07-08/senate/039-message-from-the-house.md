# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.Con.Res. 14, S. 331

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agreed to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 1) to provide 
for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. Res. 14.


                          enrolled bill signed

  The President pro tempore (Mr. GRASSLEY) announced that on today, 
July 8, 2025, he had signed the following enrolled bill which was 
previously signed by the Speaker of the House:

       S. 331. An act to amend the Controlled Substances Act with 
     respect to the scheduling of fentanyl-related substances, and 
     for other purposes.
```
