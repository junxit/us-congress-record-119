# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4107

**Measures:** H.R. 4107

```
           By Mr. MESSMER:
       H.R. 4107.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Constitution--Art. 1--SEC.8
       The Congress shall have power to raise and support armies
       Provide and maintain a Navy
       Make Rules for the Government and regulation of the land 
     and Naval Forces.
```
