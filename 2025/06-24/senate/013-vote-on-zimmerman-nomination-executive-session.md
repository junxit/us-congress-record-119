# Vote on Zimmerman Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Marshall, Roger (M001198) [R-KS], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                      Vote on Zimmerman Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Zimmerman nomination?
  Mr. MARSHALL. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.

Senator from Indiana (Mr. Banks).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Delaware (Mr. Coons), 
the Senator from Arizona (Mr. Gallego), and the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) are necessarily absent.
```
