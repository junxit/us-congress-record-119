# Text of Senate Amendment 3045

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3944

```
  SA 3045. Mr. KENNEDY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 3038 submitted by Ms. Collins and intended to be 
proposed to

[[Page S4779]]

the bill H.R. 3944, making appropriations for military construction, 
the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 2, after line 19, add the following:

     SEC. 4. ACROSS THE BOARD 2 PERCENT REDUCTION.

       For each division of this Act, each amount made available 
     under such division is reduced, on a pro rata basis, by the 
     amount necessary to reduce the amount made available under 
     such division, but for this section, by 2 percent.
                                 ______
```
