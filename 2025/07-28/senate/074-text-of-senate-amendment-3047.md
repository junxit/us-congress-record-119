# Text of Senate Amendment 3047

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

```
  SA 3047. Ms. HIRONO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
her to the bill H.R. 3944, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title II, add the following:

     SEC. 2__. REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS 
                   REPROGRAMMING OR REALLOCATION OF FUNDS.

       Not later than 60 days after the date of the enactment of 
     this Act, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall submit to 
     Congress a report that details all reprogramming or 
     reallocations of funds within the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs since January 20, 2025, including--
       (1) the amount and account from which funds were 
     reprogrammed or reallocated;
       (2) the amount and account to which funds were reprogrammed 
     or reallocated; and
       (3) the office within the Department that approved the 
     reprogramming or reallocation.
                                 ______
```
