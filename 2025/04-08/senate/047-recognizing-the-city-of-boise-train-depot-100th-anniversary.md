      RECOGNIZING: THE CITY OF BOISE TRAIN DEPOT 100TH ANNIVERSARY

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, today, I rise, with my colleagues 
Senator Mike Crapo and Representatives Mike Simpson and Russ Fulcher, 
to celebrate a significant milestone in the history of Idaho's capital 
city: the 100th anniversary of the Boise Depot. For a century, this 
iconic structure, which faces our State capitol building, has stood as 
a prominent landmark of the Boise skyline and become a cherished symbol 
of our community's past and present.
  Built in 1925 by the Union Pacific Railroad, the Boise Depot brought 
people from all over the nation to witness the wonders of Idaho. Once 
hailed as ``the most beautiful structure of its kind in the west,'' the 
depot overlooks a panoramic view of our great city and surrounding 
foothills. Its iconic 96-foot bell tower can be seen throughout Boise 
and remains as a beacon of our community. It is a favorite site enjoyed 
by many, and it continues to be a place where memories are made.
  As we celebrate its centennial anniversary, we join many Idahoans in 
reflecting on the depot's rich history. Since its opening day, when 
thousands gathered to witness the arrival of the first transcontinental 
steam engine, the depot has served as a testament to the Treasure 
Valley's growth and resilience. The depot, now operated by the city of 
Boise, provides a unique and historic setting for community 
celebrations. Over the decades, it has served as a hub for 
transportation and community gatherings, welcoming countless Idahoans 
and Americans.
  In addition to the depot's beautiful gardens and koi pond, the site 
is home to the steam locomotive Big Mike, donated by the Union Pacific 
Railroad in 1956. Big Mike represents the spirit of innovation and 
progress that has defined our Nation's economy, infrastructure, and 
heritage for generations.
  It is an honor to recognize the 100th anniversary of the Boise Depot 
and the people who have contributed to its legacy--from the passengers 
who rode its rails, to the Idahoans who celebrated their milestones, 
and the families who made memories that will last a lifetime.
  I invite my colleagues and all Idaho residents to join us in 
celebrating the Boise Depot, a historic treasure that has enriched our 
lives for 100 years and hopefully many more.●
