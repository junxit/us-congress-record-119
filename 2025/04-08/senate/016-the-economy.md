# THE ECONOMY

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                              THE ECONOMY

to my colleague, the senior Senator from New York, the minority leader,

down to the purpose of the bill, and it is to prevent a $4 trillion tax 
increase that the Democrats support.
```
