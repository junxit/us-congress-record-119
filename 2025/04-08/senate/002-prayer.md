# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord, thank You for this day and for the countless gifts You have 
showered upon us. You give us love and laughter, faith and fulfillment, 
hope and happiness. Lord, even in turbulent times, You give us Your 
peace. May we use these blessings to serve You and to bring glory to 
Your Name.
  Bless our Senators as they strive to do Your will. Give them the 
wisdom to hear Your voice and the courage to carry out Your commands. 
Keep them from weariness, doubts, and despair, and give them an 
abundant harvest in due season.
  Finally, Lord, bless us all. Teach us to love the goodness and the 
justice of Your law. Remind us to do justly, love mercy, and walk 
humbly with You.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
