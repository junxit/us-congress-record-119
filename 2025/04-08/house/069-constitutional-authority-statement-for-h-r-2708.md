**Measures:** H.R. 2708

```
           By Mr. DOWNING:
       H.R. 2708.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution authorizes 
     Congress to enact laws relating to the removal of aliens from 
     the United States.
```
