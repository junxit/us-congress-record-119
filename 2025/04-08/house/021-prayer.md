# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God, You are the giver of all good things. Thank You for having 
granted to us all that brings life and directs us toward godliness. We 
pause to reflect in this moment how countless and comprehensive are 
these gifts of grace that You have laid before us to enjoy and to 
steward.
  Cause us to understand that we can only appreciate the responsibility 
You have placed in our hands if we first acknowledge that all this is 
ours that we would know both Your power and appreciate Your grace. All 
that we have is ours to glorify You and to reveal Your generosity to 
each and everyone we encounter.
  On this day, in all that we do, in all that we say, in the use of our 
time and the stands that we take, may Your good gifts, especially the 
immeasurable gift of Your steadfast love, flow through us and reveal 
Your glory and excellence.
  In the name of Him who is able to do far more than we could ask or 
imagine, we offer our prayers.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
