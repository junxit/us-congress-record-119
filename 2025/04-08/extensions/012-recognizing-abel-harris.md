# RECOGNIZING ABEL HARRIS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING ABEL HARRIS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 8, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Abel Harris for 
earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Abel has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Abel, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Abel's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Abel Harris on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
