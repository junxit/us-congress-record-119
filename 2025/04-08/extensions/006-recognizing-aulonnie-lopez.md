# RECOGNIZING AULONNIE LOPEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING AULONNIE LOPEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 8, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Aulonnie Lopez 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Aulonnie has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Aulonnie, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Aulonnie's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning 
of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Aulonnie Lopez on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
