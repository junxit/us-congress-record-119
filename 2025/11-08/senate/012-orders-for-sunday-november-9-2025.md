# ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2025

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2025

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 1:30 p.m. 
on Sunday, November 9, 2025; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
