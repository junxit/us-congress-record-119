# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our Father, we belong to You. Thank You that our government 
has reopened.
  Give our lawmakers the wisdom to discontinue repeating the same 
actions while hoping for different results. Lord, empower them to find 
ways where no one will go hungry, thirsty, sick, or shackled because of 
the legislative process.
  Use our Senators to set the captives free. Reveal to our legislators 
resources of power adequate to make them more than conquerors.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
