              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the

           By Mr. LEE:
       S. 1721. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to repeal green energy tax subsidies; to the Committee on

     Transportation.

       S. 1723. A bill to support the creation and implementation

     technical assistance that will support the growth and 
     expansion of high-quality charter schools; to the Committee

 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S2895, May 13, 2025, in the second column, the following 
appears: S. 1731. A bill to require the Secretary of State and the 
Secretary of Defense to develop a strategy in response to the 
global basing intentions of the People's Republic of China; to the 
Select Committee on Intelligence.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: S. 1731. A bill to 
require the Secretary of State and the Secretary of Defense to 
develop a strategy in response to the global basing intentions of 
the People's Republic of China; to the Committee on Foreign 
Relations.
