# Vote on Rubinstein Nomination (Executive Session)

nomination?
  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.

  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the 
Senator from South Carolina (Mr. Graham).
