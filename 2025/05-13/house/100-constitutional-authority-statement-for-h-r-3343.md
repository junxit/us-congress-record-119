**Measures:** H.R. 3343

```

       H.R. 3343.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3: To regulate commerce with

     Section 8, Clause 18: Authority to create laws that are
```
