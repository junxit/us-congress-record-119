# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS TO GOVERNING BOARD OF THE OFFICE OF CONGRESSIONAL CONDUCT

**Measures:** H.Res. 5

```
    APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS TO GOVERNING BOARD OF THE OFFICE OF 
                         CONGRESSIONAL CONDUCT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Cline). The Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment, pursuant to section 4(d) of House Resolution 5, 
119th Congress, and the order of the House of January 3, 2025, of the

Congressional Conduct:

  Mr. Leon A. Westmoreland, Georgia

  Mr. William P. Luther, Minnesota, Co-chair
  Ms. Lorraine C. Miller, Texas
```
