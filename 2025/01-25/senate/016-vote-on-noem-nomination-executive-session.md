# Vote on Noem Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Noem Nomination

  All postcloture time has expired.
  Under the previous order, the question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Noem nomination?
  Mr. BARRASSO. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Kansas (Mr. Moran).

Schatz), the Senator from Minnesota (Ms. Smith), the Senator from 
Georgia (Mr. Warnock), and the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Wyden) are 
necessarily absent.
  The result was announced--yeas 59, nays 34, as follows:
```
