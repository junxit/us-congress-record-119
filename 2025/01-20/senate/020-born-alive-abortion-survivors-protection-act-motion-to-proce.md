# BORN-ALIVE ABORTION SURVIVORS PROTECTION ACT--Motion to Proceed

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 6

```
    BORN-ALIVE ABORTION SURVIVORS PROTECTION ACT--Motion to Proceed

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 4, S. 6.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 4, S. 6, a bill to amend 
     title 18, United States Code, to prohibit a health care 
     practitioner from failing to exercise the proper degree of 
     care in the case of a child who survives an abortion or 
     attempted abortion.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S258, January 20, 2025, second column, the following 
appears: Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 4, S.6, a bill to amend 
title XVIII, United States Code, to prohibit a health care 
practitioner from failing to exercise the proper degree of care in 
the case of a child who survives an abortion or attempted 
abortion.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: Motion to proceed 
to Calendar No. 4, S.6, a bill to amend title 18, United States 
Code, to prohibit a health care practitioner from failing to 
exercise the proper degree of care in the case of a child who 
survives an abortion or attempted abortion.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk for the 
motion to proceed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 4, S. 6, a bill to amend title 18, 
     United States Code, to prohibit a health care practitioner 
     from failing to exercise the proper degree of care in the 
     case of a child who survives an abortion or attempted 
     abortion.
         John Thune, Steve Daines, John Kennedy, Jim Justice, 
           James E. Risch, Tim Sheehy, Mike Crapo, Deb Fischer, 
           Tommy Tuberville, Rick Scott of Florida, Pete Ricketts, 
           Katie Britt, Ted Budd, Roger F. Wicker, Mike Rounds, 
           Roger Marshall, Eric Schmitt.

  Mr. THUNE. And I ask consent that the mandatory quorum call be 
waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
