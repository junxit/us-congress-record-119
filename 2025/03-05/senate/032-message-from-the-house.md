# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 579, H.R. 919

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:03 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 579. An act to amend parts B and E of title IV of the 
     Social Security Act to improve foster and adoptive parent 
     recruitment and retention, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 919. An act to codify Internal Revenue Service 
     guidance relating to treatment of certain services and items 
     for chronic conditions as meeting the preventive care 
     deductible safe harbor for purposes of high deductible health 
     plans in connection with health savings accounts.
```
