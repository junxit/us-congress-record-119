# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy One, who expresses Your love to us each day, shower us with Your 
mercy so that we may rejoice and be glad. May the gift of Your presence 
be more than sufficient to meet the needs of our Nation and world. 
Bring peace to those who are devastated by war.
  Lord, empower the Members of this body to depend on Your might and to 
stand united as they meet the challenges of our time. As they strive to 
do Your will, teach them to say the right thing at the right time and 
to serve You with faithfulness. Keep them humble and fill them with 
Your peace.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
