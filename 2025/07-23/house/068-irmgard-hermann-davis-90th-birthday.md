# IRMGARD HERMANN DAVIS' 90TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                  IRMGARD HERMANN DAVIS' 90TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to celebrate the 90th birthday 
of Irmgard Hermann Davis.
  Irmgard grew up in Germany during World War II. After the war, she 
came to visit her sister in Garden Grove, California, my district, 
where her life changed forever.
  She met Wally Davis. They got married and had seven beautiful 
children. She never returned to Germany. Wally Davis was one of Orange 
County's first Hispanic attorneys and a civil rights icon. Irmgard was 
always there for him, and she was the story behind Wally's success.
  I wish Irmgard a happy birthday. We love her and look forward to her 
next few birthdays.

                          ____________________
```
