# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Richard Kunst, St. James Catholic Church, Duluth, Minnesota, 
offered the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, we offer You thanks in giving our Nation the 
opportunity to self-govern and self-legislate, knowing that all power 
is a sharing in Your power and also recognizing that You have blessed 
the United States of America in a way that other peoples can only long 
for.
  May our philosophical and political differences serve only to 
strengthen who we are as a people rather than cause division and 
hatred.
  We pray for protection of all Members of this body and their staffs 
that they may continue to carry out the will of the people who elected 
them.
  Finally, for allowing us to enter into our 250th year as a nation, we 
give thanks to You, Heavenly Father, for all that You have blessed us 
with. May those blessings continue for many years, and may we, Your 
people, always be fully aware that You and no other entity are the 
source of these gifts.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
