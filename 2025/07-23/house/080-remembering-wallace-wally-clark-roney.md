# REMEMBERING WALLACE "WALLY" CLARK RONEY

**Speaking:** LaMalfa, Doug (L000578) [R-CA]

```
               REMEMBERING WALLACE ``WALLY'' CLARK RONEY

  (Mr. LaMALFA asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LaMALFA. Mr. Speaker, I rise in sadness to remember a personal 
friend from home in the district. Wallace Clark Roney, who everyone 
knows as Wally Roney, passed recently due to health issues.
  Wally was a fifth-generation cattle rancher, lifelong steward of the 
land, and, indeed, a towering, imposing figure--a big guy in California 
agriculture.
  Yet, that big guy, as imposing as he was, was also like a big bear 
cub. He loved what he did. He loved helping his neighbors. He loved 
helping the cattle industry and sticking up for agriculture. You won't 
find a better friend.
  Wally and his wife, Billie Jean, are good friends of mine. As we 
remember him today and feel that sadness, know that his spirit of 
fighting for agriculture, as a fellow Cal Poly Mustang graduate, will 
also be remembered and appreciated.
  Wally was a respected leader, of course, in his field in all those 
areas, blending old-school ranching with a sharp business sense and a 
deep understanding of land management.
  Wally grew up alongside seasoned hands, learning the value of grit, 
responsibility, and persistence. He trained horses, rodeoed, raised 
cattle, and never hesitated to speak up when it was important to help 
preserve his industry and preserve a lifestyle not just for him but for 
his neighbors and everybody who depends on us as a food supply, making 
America stronger.
  God bless Wally and his wife, Billie Jean. We will miss him. He was a 
good friend, and he will never be replaced but will always be 
remembered.

                          ____________________
```
