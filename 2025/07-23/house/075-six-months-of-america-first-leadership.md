# SIX MONTHS OF AMERICA FIRST LEADERSHIP

**Speaking:** Stutzman, Marlin A. (S001188) [R-IN]

```
                 SIX MONTHS OF AMERICA FIRST LEADERSHIP

  (Mr. STUTZMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. STUTZMAN. Mr. Speaker, this week marks 6 months of the America 
First leadership under President Trump Donald Trump.
  He has had innumerable accomplishments over the first half of this 
year and has kicked off a new golden age for our country. His strong 
leadership has single-handedly added over half a million jobs to the 
U.S. economy. Many of these jobs came back to my home State of Indiana, 
like the announcement from General Motors about increasing its 
production in the Fort Wayne truck plant.
  He has brought gas prices to their lowest point since 2021 by 
unleashing American energy independence.
  Outside of the economic impacts that have saved money for everyday 
Americans, President Trump has kept his commitment to protect American 
children and families by halting so-called gender-affirming care for 
children in this country.
  As another testament to his impressive pace, President Trump has also 
signed 27 bills into law, the greatest of which, the One Big Beautiful 
Bill Act, delivers tax cuts to American families, solidifies funding 
for America's Golden Dome, and defunded Planned Parenthood.
  Over the past 6 months, President Trump has delivered on his promise 
to restore America's strength and dominance, and I look forward to 
continuing the fight with President Trump leading the way to ensure 
America's golden age lasts for decades to come.

                          ____________________
```
