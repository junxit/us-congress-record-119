# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4728

**Measures:** H.R. 4728

```
           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 4728.
       Congress has the power to regulate activities affecting the 
     receipt and use of federal funds pursuant to Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution of the United States, 
     which grants authority to provide for the general welfare of 
     the United States.
```
