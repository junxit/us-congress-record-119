# Text of Senate Amendment 2998

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

```
  SA 2998. Mr. BLUMENTHAL submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3944, making appropriations for 
military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title II, add the following:

     SEC. 2__. EXPANSION OF VETSUCCESS ON CAMPUS PROGRAM.

       The amount appropriated by this title under the heading 
     ``readjustment benefits'' is increased by $15,000,000, with 
     the amount of the increase to be available to expand the 
     VetSuccess On Campus program by hiring VetSuccessOn Campus 
     program counselors, with priority provided to schools in 
     States in which such program has no counselors.
                                 ______
```
