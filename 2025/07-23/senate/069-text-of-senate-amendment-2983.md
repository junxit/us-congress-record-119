# Text of Senate Amendment 2983

**Speaking:** Cortez Masto, Catherine (C001113) [D-NV]

```
  SA 2983. Ms. CORTEZ MASTO submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the bill H.R. 3944, making appropriations for 
military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. APPROPRIATION OF ADDITIONAL AMOUNTS FOR MEDICAL 
                   SERVICES FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS 
                   AFFAIRS.

       (a) In General.--The amount appropriated under title II 
     under the heading ``medical services'' under the heading 
     ``Veterans Health Administration'' under the heading 
     ``DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS'' for fiscal year 2027 is 
     hereby increased by $100,000,000.
       (b) Offset.--The amount appropriated under section 90103 of 
     Public Law 119-21 (commonly known as the ``One Big Beautiful 
     Bill Act'') is hereby rescinded.
                                 ______
```
