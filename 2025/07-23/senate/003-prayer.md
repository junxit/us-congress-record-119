# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the fountain of all wisdom, we bring our fragmented 
lives into Your presence, seeking Your wholeness and seeking Your 
peace. We bring our restless spirits to You, seeking Your calm, 
strength, and everlasting purpose. We bring You our transient thoughts, 
seeking the permanence of Your gracious providence.
  Lord, remind our lawmakers that only as we lose ourselves in 
something higher can we truly find ourselves. To this end, give them 
great causes to embrace and a great faith to energize their work.
  Lead them to a confidence that in everything, You are working for the 
good of those who love You who are called according to Your purposes.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
