**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

                        Vote on Szabo Nomination

  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Szabo 
nomination?
  Mr. SCOTT of Florida. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?

Tillis) would have voted ``yea.''
