# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDALS AWARDED UNDER THE HARLEM HELLFIGHTERS CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL ACT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 39

```
AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER 
 FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDALS AWARDED UNDER 
          THE HARLEM HELLFIGHTERS CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL ACT

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 39, which was 
receive from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 39) authorizing the 
     use of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for a 
     ceremony to present the Congressional Gold Medals awarded 
     under the Harlem Hellfighters Congressional Gold Medal Act.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to and the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res 39) was agreed to.
```
