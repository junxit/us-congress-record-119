# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 330, S. 853, S. 1167, S. 1733, S. 1828

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATION
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine the nomination of Richard Fordyce, of Missouri, to 
be Under Secretary of Agriculture for Farm Production and Conservation, 
after the nominee, who was introduced by Representative Sam Graves, 
testified and answered questions in his own behalf.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Committee ordered 
favorably reported the nominations of Benjamin DeMarzo, of Virginia, to 
be an Assistant Secretary, and Craig Trainor, of Virginia, to be an 
Assistant Secretary, both of the Department of Housing and Urban 
Development, Jovan Jovanovic, of Pennsylvania, to be President of the 
Export-Import Bank of the United States for a term expiring January 20, 
2029, Francis Brooke, of Virginia, to be an Assistant Secretary of the 
Treasury, and David Peters, of Virginia, to be an Assistant Secretary 
of Commerce.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine the nominations of Seval Oz, of California, and 
Michael Rutherford, of Florida, both to be an Assistant Secretary, and 
Gregory Zerzan, of Texas, to be General Counsel, all of the Department 
of Transportation, after the nominees testified and answered questions 
in their own behalf.
INCREASED ELECTRICITY DEMAND
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine challenges to meeting increased electricity demand, 
after receiving testimony from Peter R. Hunstman, Huntsman Corporation, 
The Woodlands, Texas; Jeff Tench, Vantage Data Centers, Denver, 
Colorado; and Rob Gramlich, Grid Strategies LLC, Bethesda, Maryland.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine the nominations of Katherine Scarlett, of Ohio, to 
be a Member of the Council on Environmental Quality, and Jeffrey Hall, 
of Virginia, to be an Assistant Administrator, both of the 
Environmental Protection Agency, after the nominees testified and 
answered questions in their own behalf.
AMERICA'S TRANSPORTATION INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Subcommittee on 
Transportation and Infrastructure concluded a hearing to examine the 
road ahead, focusing on proposals to improve America's transportation 
infrastructure, including S. 1167, to amend title 23, United States 
Code, to improve transportation asset management plans, S. 1828, to 
amend title 23, United States Code, to require States to designate a 
coordinator of the safe routes to school program in the State, and S. 
1733, to amend title 23, United States Code, to provide for increased 
transferability of Federal-aid highway funds, after receiving testimony 
from Chad M. Orn, North Dakota Department of Transportation Deputy 
Director of Planning, Bismarck; Samantha Biddle, Maryland Department of 
Transportation Deputy Secretary, Hanover; and Marisa Jones, Safe Routes 
Partnership, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee ordered favorably reported 
the nominations of John Arrigo, of

[[Page D781]]

Florida, to be Ambassador to the Portuguese Republic, and Christine 
Toretti, of Pennsylvania, to be Ambassador to the Kingdom of Sweden, 
both of the Department of State.
TRANSNATIONAL CRIMINAL ORGANIZATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine dismantling transnational criminal organizations in the 
Americas, after receiving testimony from Christopher Urben, Nardello 
and Co, and Vanda Felbab-Brown, The Brookings Institution, both of 
Washington, D.C.
MIDDLE EAST
Committee on Foreign Relations: Subcommittee on Near East, South Asia, 
Central Asia, and Counterterrorism concluded a hearing to examine 
United States diplomatic strategies for a dynamic Middle East, after 
receiving testimony from Brian Hook, former Special Representative for 
Iran, and Daniel B. Shapiro, former Ambassador to Israel, both of the 
Department of State, Washington, D.C.; and Shelly Culbertson, RAND, 
Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.
CHOICE AND LITERACY
Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: Subcommittee on 
Education and the American Family concluded a hearing to examine choice 
and literacy, focusing on empowering families for better educational 
results, after receiving testimony from Tyler Barnett, New Schools for 
Alabama, Birmingham; Anne Wicks, George W. Bush Institute, Dallas, 
Texas; Virginia Gentles, Defense of Freedom Institute, Arlington, 
Virginia; and Richard Barrera, San Diego Unified School Board, San 
Diego, California.
CIVIL RIGHTS LAWS
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on the Constitution concluded 
a hearing to examine ending illegal discrimination and preferences, 
focusing on enforcing our civil rights laws, after receiving testimony 
from Harmeet K. Dhillon, Assistant Attorney General, Civil Rights 
Division, Department of Justice; Alabama State Senator Robert Stewart, 
Jr., District 23, Selma; and Gene Hamilton, America First Legal 
Foundation, Washington, D.C.
INNOVATION IN THE CROSSHAIRS
Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine innovation in the crosshairs, focusing on countering 
China's industrial espionage, including S.853, to improve the SBIR and 
STTR programs under the Small Business Act, and S.330, to impose 
sanctions with respect to persons that operate in a sector of the 
economy of the People's Republic of China in which the person has 
engaged in a pattern of significant theft of the intellectual property 
of a United States person, after receiving testimony from Emily De La 
Bruyere, Foundation for Defense of Democracies, Sewickley, 
Pennsylvania; Sujai Shivakumar, Center for Strategic and International 
Studies, Washington, D.C.; and William C. Hannas, Georgetown University 
Center for Security and Emerging Technology, Reston, Virginia.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of John Bartrum, of Indiana, to be Under 
Secretary for Health of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and 
Jeremiah Workman, of Ohio, to be Assistant Secretary of Labor for 
Veterans' Employment and Training, who was introduced by Senator 
Sheehy, after the nominees testified and answered questions in their 
own behalf.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
