              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. McCORMICK (for himself and Ms. Alsobrooks):
       S. 1808. A bill to permit a registered investment company 
     to omit certain fees from the calculation of acquired fund 
     fees and expenses, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.

     and for other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

     to allow a credit against tax for charitable donations to 
     nonprofit organizations providing education scholarships to 
     qualified elementary and secondary students; to the Committee 
     on Finance.

       S. 1811. A bill to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965

     financial assistance if such schools adopt certain policies

     inclusion; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
