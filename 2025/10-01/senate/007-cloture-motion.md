# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 412, S. 2882, H.R. 5371

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair 
lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will 
state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 167, S. 2882, a bill making 
     continuing appropriations for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2026, and for other purposes.
         Charles E. Schumer, Patty Murray, Tim Kaine, Richard J. 
           Durbin, Tina Smith, Jack Reed, Alex Padilla, Mazie K. 
           Hirono, Jeanne Shaheen, Tammy Baldwin, Richard 
           Blumenthal, Kirsten E. Gillibrand, Ben Ray Lujan, Brian 
           Schatz, Sheldon Whitehouse, Michael F. Bennet, 
           Christopher Murphy.

call has been waived.

motion to proceed to S. 2882, a bill making continuing appropriations 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes, 
shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.

                           Order of Procedure

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that it be in order 
for the two leaders to enter motions to reconsider without being on the 
prevailing side with respect to the cloture votes on the motions to 
proceed to S. 2882 and H.R. 5371; further, that the mandatory quorum 
call with respect to the Cao nomination in S. Res. 412 be waived.
```
