     SEC. __. TREATMENT UNDER THE BANK SECRECY ACT.

       (a) Sense of Congress.--Any entity or service that 
     functions as a financial institution, as defined in the Bank 
     Secrecy Act, regardless of whether the entity or service is 
     centralized or decentralized, is required to comply with Bank 
     Secrecy Act obligations, including anti-money laundering and 
     countering the financing of terrorism obligations, to prevent 
     abuse by criminals, terrorists, and adversaries who seek to 
     exploit perceived gaps in required safeguards across 
     different types of entities and services.

     Digital asset service providers shall be treated as financial 
     institutions for the purposes of the Bank Secrecy Act.
