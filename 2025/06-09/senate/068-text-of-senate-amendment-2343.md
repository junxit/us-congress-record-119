# Text of Senate Amendment 2343

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
  SA 2343. Mr. TUBERVILLE submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 2307 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Hagerty 
(for himself and Mrs. Gillibrand)) to the bill S. 1582, to provide for 
the regulation of payment stablecoins, and for other purposes; which 
was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:
        At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       (5) Any of the following countries does not have a 
     controlling interest of 20 percent or more in the foreign 
     payment stablecoin issuer:
       (A) The People's Republic of China, including the Hong Kong 
     Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special 
     Administrative Region.
       (B) The Republic of Cuba.
       (C) The Islamic Republic of Iran.
       (D) The Democratic People's Republic of Korea.
       (E) The Russian Federation.
       (F) The Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela under the regime 
     of Nicolas Maduro Moros.
                                 ______
```
