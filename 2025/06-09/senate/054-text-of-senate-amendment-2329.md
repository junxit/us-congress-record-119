# Text of Senate Amendment 2329

**Speaking:** Warren, Elizabeth (W000817) [D-MA]

```
  SA 2329. Ms. WARREN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 2307 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Hagerty (for himself 
and Mrs. Gillibrand)) to the bill S. 1582, to provide for the 
regulation of payment stablecoins, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        Strike section 4(a)(13) and insert the following:

[[Page S3289]]

       (13) Eligibility.--No permitted payment stablecoin issuer, 
     other than an insured depository institution, shall have 
     access to services made available from a Federal Reserve 
     bank, including an account with a Federal Reserve bank.
                                 ______
```
