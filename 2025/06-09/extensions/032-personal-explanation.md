# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2035

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 9, 2025

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, on Roll Call No. 157 of the 
119th Congress, which was H.R. 2035, the American Cargo for American 
Ships Act, I voted in error YEA, when I fully intended to vote NAY. I 
am here to set the record straight: the Jones Act and any legislation 
furthering the antiquated policy positions of the Jones Act, like H.R. 
2035 are harmful and must be rectified.
```
