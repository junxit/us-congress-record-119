# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MAY 23, 2025, AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 255

```
               ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MAY 23, 2025, AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come
```
