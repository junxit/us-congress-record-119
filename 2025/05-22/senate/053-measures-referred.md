# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1701, H.R. 1969

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1701. An act to require the Secretary of Defense and 
     the Secretary of State to monitor efforts by the People's 
     Republic of China to build or buy strategic foreign ports, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.

     Sergeant Parker Gordon Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program 
     of the Department of Veterans Affairs; to the Committee on 
     Veterans' Affairs.
```
