# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations en 
bloc: all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, 
Coast Guard, Marine Corps, Navy, and Space Force, with the exception of 
PN89, Matthew Ryan, of the Army; that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc; that the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions 
be in order to any of the nominations; that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action; and that the Senate then 
resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
