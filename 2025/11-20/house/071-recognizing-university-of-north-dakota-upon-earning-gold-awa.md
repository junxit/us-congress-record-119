# RECOGNIZING UNIVERSITY OF NORTH DAKOTA UPON EARNING GOLD AWARD

**Speaking:** Fedorchak, Julie (F000482) [R-ND]

```
     RECOGNIZING UNIVERSITY OF NORTH DAKOTA UPON EARNING GOLD AWARD

  (Mrs. FEDORCHAK asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. FEDORCHAK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the University 
of North Dakota for earning the Gold Award as a 2025-2026 Military 
Friendly School.
  UND was named among the nation's top 10 military-spouse friendly 
schools nationwide. This honor places UND in the top 10 percent of 
universities across the U.S. for its commitment to serving our 
servicemembers, veterans, and their families.
  UND's leadership reflects a long tradition of service that dates back 
to its founding. This gold recognition marks a proud milestone, 
especially with more than 2,000 military-affiliated students enrolled 
this year.
  From peer-to-peer mentoring to faculty training and veteran advocacy,

[[Page H4853]]

UND continues to raise the standard for how universities can support 
members of our military.
  I congratulate President Armacost, Director Angie Carpenter, and the 
entire UND community for their dedication to those who defend our 
freedoms.

                          ____________________
```
