# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The assistant bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 263, Cristian M. Stevens, of 
     Missouri, to be United States District Judge for the Eastern 
     District of Missouri.
         John Thune, Todd Young, Markwayne Mullin, John R. Curtis, 
           Shelley Moore Capito, Ted Budd, Ashley B. Moody, Tommy 
           Tuberville, Joni Ernst, John Barrasso, Cindy Hyde-
           Smith, Mike Rounds, Lindsey Graham, Pete Ricketts, Tim 
           Sheehy, Roger F. Wicker, Ted Cruz.
```
