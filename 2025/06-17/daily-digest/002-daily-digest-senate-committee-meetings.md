# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
APPROPRIATIONS: INTELLIGENCE COMMUNITY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Department of Defense 
concluded a closed hearing to examine proposed budget estimates for 
fiscal year 2026 for the intelligence community, after receiving 
testimony from Tulsi Gabbard, Director of National Intelligence; and 
John Ratcliffe, Director, Central Intelligence Agency.
APPROPRIATIONS: MILITARY CONSTRUCTION AND FAMILY HOUSING
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Military Construction, 
Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies concluded hearings to examine 
proposed budget estimates for fiscal year 2026 for military 
construction and family housing, after receiving testimony from Dale R. 
Marks, Assistant Secretary for Energy, Installations, and Environment, 
Lieutenant General David Wilson, Deputy Chief of Staff, G-9 
(Installations), USA, Vice Admiral Jeffrey T. Jablon, Deputy Chief of 
Naval Operations for Installations and Logistics, USN, Lieutenant 
General Stephen D. Sklenka, Deputy Commandant, Installations and 
Logistics, USMC, Lieutenant General Tom D. Miller, Deputy Chief of 
Staff for Logistics, Engineering, and Force Protection, USAF, and 
Brigadier General Zachary S. Warakomski, Assistant Deputy Chief of 
Space Operations for Operations, Cyber, and Nuclear, USSF, all of the 
Department of Defense.
WORLD ANTI-DOPING AGENCY
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Subcommittee on 
Consumer Protection, Technology, and Data Privacy concluded a hearing 
to examine the World Anti-Doping Agency, focusing on swimming in denial 
over Chinese doping, after receiving testimony from Travis T. Tygart, 
United States Anti-Doping Agency, Colorado Springs, Colorado; Rahul 
Gupta, GATC Health Corporation, Irvine, California; Dionne Koller, 
University of Baltimore School of Law Center for Sport and the Law, 
Potomac, Maryland; and Kathryn McLaughlin, Dana Point, California.
PROTECTING OLDER AMERICANS FROM SCAMMERS
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
scammers exposed, focusing on protecting older Americans from 
transnational crime networks, after receiving testimony from April 
Helm, Advocating Against Romance Scammers, Tulsa, Oklahoma; Jilenne 
Gunther, AARP BankSafe Initiative, New York, New York; Joshua M. Bercu, 
USTelecom-The Broadband Association, Washington, D.C.; and Brady Finta, 
National Elder Fraud Coordination Center, Oceanside, California.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee held closed hearings on 
intelligence matters, receiving testimony from officials of the 
intelligence community.
  Committee recessed subject to the call.
```
