# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Today's opening prayer will be offered by 
Pastor Randy Phillips, lead pastor of Life Family Austin, from Austin, 
TX.
  The guest Chaplain offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  All mighty God, we humbly come before You, recognizing that all 
authority comes from Your hand. We thank You for this Chamber and for 
the leaders You have raised up to serve this Nation.
  Grant to the Members of this Senate wisdom beyond their own. Let 
truth guide their decisions and justice be their aim. May their debates 
be marked with dignity and their hearts governed by compassion, and 
their service driven by the desire for the common good. Help them to 
seek unity where there is division, courage where there is fear, and 
clarity where there is confusion. Let integrity be their compass and 
humility their constant companion.
  Bless their families and keep them safe. Give their staff endurance, 
and let their conscience be a sign with assurance that their labor is 
not in vain.
  Let a spiritual awakening happen in this country. Let this government 
be a reflection of Your mercy, justice, and care for all people, 
especially the weak, the voiceless, and the forgotten.
  We pray this not only for our glory, but that Your will would be done 
on Earth as it is in Heaven.
  In the mighty Name of Jesus, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
