# RECOGNIZING THE BRAVERY AND LIFE-SAVING RESPONSE OF LIEUTENANT BRANDON HALL OF BUCHANAN COUNTY, VIRGINIA

**Speaking:** Griffith, H. Morgan (G000568) [R-VA]

```
RECOGNIZING THE BRAVERY AND LIFE-SAVING RESPONSE OF LIEUTENANT BRANDON 
                   HALL OF BUCHANAN COUNTY, VIRGINIA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. H. MORGAN GRIFFITH

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 17, 2025

  Mr. GRIFFITH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the exceptional 
courage and service of Lieutenant Brandon Hall of Buchanan County, 
Virginia.
  On May 1st, Lieutenant Hall responded to an emergency in which a 
camper had filled with dangerous levels of carbon monoxide. Inside the 
camper were two adults and a child, all unconscious. When faced with an 
invisible but deadly threat, Lieutenant Hall entered the camper, 
removed the victims, and quickly administered critical first aid.
  In a moment of crisis, Lieutenant Hall was among the first to arrive, 
and when faced with danger, he did not pause. Lieutenant Hall's bravery 
and quick thinking saved these lives and stand as a testament to the 
selfless courage of the public servants in Virginia's Ninth 
Congressional District.
  Today, we recognize Lieutenant Hall for his swift and courageous 
response. The strength and skill he demonstrated in a time of 
emergency, reflect the very best of Southwest Virginia. We thank him 
for his service and are deeply grateful for his dedication.
```
