# HONORING ANGELICA MICHELLE JONES AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA DURING LGBTQ+ PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING ANGELICA MICHELLE JONES AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                   FLORIDA DURING LGBTQ+ PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 17, 2025

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Angelica Michelle Jones, aka Angelica Sanchez, 
was born in Jacksonville, Florida, on November 8, 1977.
  She moved to Orlando in 1996 to live in her truth and become her 
authentic self.
  A Black trans woman and drag performer, she has won numerous national 
pageants, including Miss. Continental Plus, Miss. Gay U.S. of A at 
Large, Miss. Duval, and Miss. Florida FFI at Large.
  She's an LGBTQIA advocate as well as a Pulse survivor.
```
