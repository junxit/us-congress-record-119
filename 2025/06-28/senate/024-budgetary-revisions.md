                          BUDGETARY REVISIONS

  Mr. GRAHAM. Mr. President, section 3001 of H.Con. Res. 14, the fiscal 
year 2025 congressional budget resolution, allows the chairman of the 
Senate Budget Committee to revise committee allocations, budgetary 
aggregates, and the pay-as-you-go ledger for legislation considered 
under the resolution's reconciliation instructions.
  I find that amendment No. 2360 fulfils the conditions found in 
section 3001 of H.Con. Res. 14. Accordingly, I am revising the 
allocations for the reconciled committees and other enforceable 
budgetary levels to account for the updated budgetary effects of the 
amendment.
  I ask unanimous consent that the accompanying tables providing 
details about the adjustments be printed in the Record.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered tobe printed in 
the Record, as follows:
