# Text of Senate Amendment 2494

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 2494. Mr. KIM submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title 
II of H. Con. Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. _____. CFPB CERTIFICATION.

       If any provision of this Act results in a change in outlays 
     or revenues of the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection, 
     as in effect on of the day before the date of enactment of 
     this Act, the Director of the Bureau of Consumer Financial 
     Protection, the Chairman of the Board of Governors of the 
     Federal Reserve System and the Secretary of the Treasury 
     shall submit to Congress a certification on the number of 
     consumer scams or complaints or other abuses committed 
     against consumers that were reported to Bureau but went 
     unanswered or were not investigated in a reasonable manner 
     during the 1-year period beginning on January 20, 2025, and 
     for each 1-year period thereafter.
                                 ______
```
