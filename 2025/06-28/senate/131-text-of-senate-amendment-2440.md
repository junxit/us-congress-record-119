# Text of Senate Amendment 2440

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI]

```
  SA 2440. Ms. BALDWIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed

H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        In section 1181(8) of title 14, United States Code (as 
     added by section 40001), strike ``and medium'' and insert ``,
```
