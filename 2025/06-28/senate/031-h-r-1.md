# H.R. 1

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                                 H.R. 1

  Mr. BLUMENTHAL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
following motion to commit for the bill H.R. 1 be placed in the 
Congressional Record:

       A motion to commit the bill to the committee on Armed 
     Services with instructions to prohibit the Department of 
     Defense from using any funds appropriated by Congress for 
     national security purposes and to support the troops of the 
     United States to accept, retrofit, or transfer a plane given 
     by a foreign government for use as Air Force One during the 
     Trump Administration.

                          ____________________
```
