# Text of Senate Amendment 2387

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.Con.Res. 14

```
  SA 2387. Ms. HIRONO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to

[[Page S3972]]

amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 90001, strike ``$46,550,000,000'' and insert 
     ``16,550,000,000''.
       At the end of subtitle B of title IX, insert the following:

     SEC. 901__. RURAL MAIL SERVICE.

       In addition to amounts otherwise available, there is 
     appropriated to the United States Postal Service 
     $30,000,000,000 for expenses to maintain rural mail service, 
     to remain available for 10 years.
```
