# Text of Senate Amendment 2471

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  SA 2471. Mr. SCHIFF submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        In section 50301(f), add at the end the following:
       (5) Reversionary interest.--If the Secretary determines 
     that housing has not been constructed on a tract of covered 
     Federal land conveyed under this section by the date that is 
     5 years after the date of the conveyance of the tract of 
     covered Federal land, the tract of covered Federal land shall 
     revert to the United States.
```
