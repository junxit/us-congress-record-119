# Text of Senate Amendment 2403

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
  SA 2403. Mr. RISCH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 2360 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
H.R. 1, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. 
Res. 14; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of section 50302(a)(3), add the following:
       (D) Use of proceeds.--Of the monies derived from a timber 
     sale contract entered into to meet the requirements under 
     this paragraph and deposited in the general fund of the 
     Treasury, 25 percent shall be distributed in accordance with 
     the Act of May 23, 1908 (commonly known as the ``Forest 
     Reserve Revenue Act of 1908'') (35 Stat. 260, chapter 192; 16 
     U.S.C. 500).
                                 ______
```
