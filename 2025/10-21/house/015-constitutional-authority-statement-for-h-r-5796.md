# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5796

**Measures:** H.R. 5796

```
            By Mr. COSTA:
        H.R. 5796.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 3 of rule XII of the Rules of the House 
     of Representatives, the Congress finds the authority for this 
     legislation in the following provisions of the Constitution:
       Clause 3 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution, 
     which grants Congress the power to regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, and among the several states, and with the 
     Indian tribes.
       Clause 1 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution, 
     which provides Congress the power to lay and collect taxes, 
     duties, imposts, and excises, and to pay the debts and 
     provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States.
```
