```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 3483. A bill

     fraud, waste, and abuse regarding claims for payment

     Program; with an amendment (Rept. 119-348). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 3834. A bill 
     to amend title 38, United States Code, to clarify the 
     jurisdiction and certain rules of evidence of the Board of 
     Veterans' Appeals; with an amendment (Rept. 119-349). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 1663. A bill 
     to amend title 38, United States Code, to establish in the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs a Veterans Scam and Fraud 
     Evasion Officer, and for other purposes; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 119-350). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.
```
