# SENATE RESOLUTION 458--EXTENDING THE ENFORCEMENT OF CERTAIN BUDGETARY POINTS OF ORDER IN THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 458

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

 SENATE RESOLUTION 458--EXTENDING THE ENFORCEMENT OF CERTAIN BUDGETARY 
                     POINTS OF ORDER IN THE SENATE

  Mr. GRAHAM (for himself and Mr. Merkley) submitted the following 
resolution; which was referred to the Committee on the Budget:

                              S. Res. 458

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. EXTENSION OF ENFORCEMENT OF BUDGETARY POINTS OF 
                   ORDER IN THE SENATE.

       Notwithstanding any provision of the Congressional Budget 
     Act of 1974 (2 U.S.C. 621 et seq.), subsections (c)(2) and 
     (d)(3) of section 904 of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 
     (2 U.S.C. 621 note) shall remain in effect for purposes of 
     Senate enforcement through September 30, 2027.

                          ____________________
```
