                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 49

  At the request of Mr. Cotton, the name of the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Scott) was added as a cosponsor of S. 49, a bill to impose 
sanctions with respect to police departments in the People's Republic 
of China and other persons seeking to establish a Chinese police 
presence in the United States.

  At the request of Ms. Murkowski, the name of the Senator from Georgia 
(Mr. Ossoff) was added as a cosponsor of S. 139, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to reauthorize and extend the Fetal Alcohol 
Spectrum Disorders Prevention and Services program, and for other 
purposes.

Senator from Oklahoma (Mr. Mullin) were added as cosponsors of S. 167, 
a bill to amend title 18, United States Code, to punish criminal
