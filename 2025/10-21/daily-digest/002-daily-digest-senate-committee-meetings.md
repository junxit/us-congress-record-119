                                  JOINT INTERAGENCY TASK FORCE 401
 Committee on Armed Services: Committee received a closed briefing on 
the Joint Interagency Task Force 401 from Brigadier General Matthew S. 
 Ross, USA, Director, Joint Interagency Task Force 401, and Alexander 
 Miller, Chief Technology Officer to the Chief of Staff of the United

                                                      U.S. HOUSING
   Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Subcommittee on 
Housing, Transportation, and Community Development concluded a hearing 
 to examine innovation in United States housing, focusing on solutions 
   and policies for America's future, including S.1511, to amend the 
    Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to expand housing investment with 
  mortgage revenue bonds, after receiving testimony from Lars Powell, 
 University of Alabama Culverhouse College of Business Center for Risk 
   and Insurance Research, Tuscaloosa; Mary Tingerthal, Construction 
  Revolution, Minneapolis, Minnesota; and Dennis C. Shea, Bipartisan 
    Policy Center J. Ronald Terwilliger Center for Housing Policy, 
                            Washington, D.C.
                                                  BUSINESS MEETING
 Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee ordered 
            favorably reported the following business items:
  S. 1070, to establish a National STEM Week to promote American 
innovation and enhance STEM education pathways for all students, 
including those in rural, urban, and underserved communities, with an 
amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 2126, to reauthorize the Integrated Coastal and Ocean Observation 
System Act of 2009, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 2245, to amend the Digital Coast Act to improve the acquisition, 
integration, and accessibility of data of the Digital Coast program and 
to extend the program, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 2357, to reauthorize the Young Fishermen's Development Act, with 
an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 2563, to direct the Secretary of Commerce, in coordination with 
the heads of other relevant Federal departments and agencies, to 
conduct an interagency review of and report to Congress on ways to 
increase the global competitiveness of the United States in attracting 
foreign direct investment, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  S. 2666, to direct the Federal Communications Commission to establish 
a taskforce on unlawful robocalls, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  S. 2975, to amend title 49, United States Code, to enhance the safety 
of pipeline transportation, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  S. 2503, to require all aircraft to be equipped with Automatic 
Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast In, to improve aviation safety, with 
an amendment in the nature of a substitute; and
  The nominations of Joyce Meyer, of Virginia, to be Under Secretary 
for Economic Affairs, and Harry Kumar, of New York, to be an Assistant 
Secretary, both of the Department of Commerce, and Seval Oz, of 
California, to be an Assistant Secretary of Transportation.
