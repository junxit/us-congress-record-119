# APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR ROGER WICKER TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

```
   APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR ROGER WICKER TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL 
                                ADDRESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, as modified by 
the order of February 11, 2025, appoints the Senator from Mississippi, 
Mr. Wicker, to read Washington's Farewell Address on Tuesday, February 
18, 2025.

                          ____________________
```
