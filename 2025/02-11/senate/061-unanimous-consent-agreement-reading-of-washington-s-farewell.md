# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

```
 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Ms. LUMMIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the order of January 24, 1901, the traditional reading 
of Washington's Farewell Address take place on Tuesday, February 18, 
2025, following the prayer and pledge of the flag.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
