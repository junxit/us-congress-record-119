# HONORING SUZANNE EVANS SHEPPARD

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                    HONORING SUZANNE EVANS SHEPPARD

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and 
celebrate the remarkable life of Dr. Suzanne Evans Sheppard, an 
exceptional member of our community who touched many lives.
  She was a devoted mother, educator, and wife, an inspiring figure who 
cherished her church, Victory & Dominion World Outreach Center.
  To serve as a reminder of her tremendous contributions, her church 
has renamed its family life center in her honor. On her birthday, 
family and community members gathered to celebrate. We released 
balloons into the sky, and Pastor Dr. Zebedee Sheppard, her beloved 
husband, made the heartfelt announcement. The new name is fitting, 
reflecting the center's role in serving the community to which she 
dedicated so much of her life.
  I am truly grateful for Dr. Suzanne Sheppard, and the new name of 
this incredible facility in our community now makes it complete.

                          ____________________
```
