# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 16, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 5

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 16, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
on Thursday, January 16; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 1, S. 
5.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
