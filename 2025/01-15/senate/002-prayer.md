# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You are our rock and fortress. Keep us from 
disappointing You.
  Help our lawmakers to walk in Your precepts so that they can remain 
within the circle of Your protection and blessings. Lord, turn their 
ears to listen to Your admonition as You infuse them with the courage 
to obey Your commands. We have trusted You since the birth of this land 
we love. That is why we will declare Your glory all day long. Lord, as 
our Senators strive to please You today, we declare that You alone are 
our hope.
  And, Lord, continue to be with those who battle the catastrophic 
wildfires in Southern California.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
