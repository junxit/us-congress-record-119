**Speaking:** Thanedar, Shri (T000488) [D-MI]

```
    U.S. GOVERNMENT SERVES AMERICAN PEOPLE, NOT WEALTHY SHAREHOLDERS

  (Mr. THANEDAR asked and was given permission to address the House for

contribution to Trump is the same as about $130 for an average 
American.

American people, not the wealthy shareholders.
  Thousands will die around the world because USAID won't deliver 
lifesaving

information, such as Social Security numbers, banking information,

  Elon Musk does not care about us because he does not have to and does 
not want to. Rest assured, Democrats care, I care, and we will use 
every tool at our disposal to get rid of this unelected, power-hungry, 
egocentric billionaire.
```
