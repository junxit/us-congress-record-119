# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, preserver, redeemer, and judge, strengthen our Senators 
for their work today. Lord, provide them with the wisdom needed to 
handle challenges and pressures. As You illuminate their path with the 
light of Your wisdom, infuse them with patience to persevere in their 
efforts to do Your will.
  In the storms and strains of leadership, may they not deplete their 
faith by majoring in minors and minoring in majors. Instead, may they 
trust You in the face of perplexities.
  Empower them to practice the Golden Rule of treating others the way 
they themselves desire to be treated.
  And, Lord, bless the National Prayer Breakfast program tomorrow.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
