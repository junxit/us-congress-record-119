# RECOGNIZING ORANGE CITY FAMILY ANIMAL CARE

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
               RECOGNIZING ORANGE CITY FAMILY ANIMAL CARE

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize 
Orange City Family Animal Care of Orange City, IA, as the Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  In 1984, Dr. Dave Bomgaars established the Orange City Veterinary 
Clinic with the goal of providing exemplary veterinary services for the 
animals of northwest Iowa. Over the years, Dr. Bomgaars expanded the 
practice by adding more veterinarians and partners. In 2014, Dr. Laura 
Vander Stelt joined the practice as a companion animal veterinarian. 
Companion animal veterinarians primarily care for cats, dogs, and small 
animals typically owned by families and individuals. The team continued 
to grow with the addition of a technician and receptionist to care for 
and greet the animals and their owners. In 2018, Pipestone Veterinary 
Services bought the practice and renamed it the Orange City Family 
Animal Care. In 2023, Dr. Vander Stelt purchased Orange City Family 
Animal Care to broaden the scope and services available. Under her 
leadership, the business has thrived, growing to 10 employees and 
prompting the construction of a larger facility in downtown Orange 
City.
  In addition to providing exceptional veterinary care, Orange City 
Family Animal Care is deeply engaged in community service. The clinic 
contributes to local schools and sports booster organizations and 
educates children on responsible pet care. When a puppy tragically 
perished in a fire in nearby Alton, the clinic donated pet oxygen masks 
to two local fire departments, so they are prepared for future pet 
emergencies.
  Orange City Family Animal Care's commitment to excellence has earned 
well-deserved recognition. In 2023, the Northwest Iowa Review named Dr. 
Vander Stelt the runner-up for both People's Choice Awards Best of 
Northwest Veterinarian and People's Choice Awards Best of Northwest 
Veterinarian Clinic. This year, the clinic's newest veterinarian Dr. 
Hoogland was recognized by the Northwest Iowa Review on its 20 under 40 
list and has also been nominated for the People's Choice Awards Best of 
Northwest Veterinarian.
  I commend Dr. Vander Stelt and her entire team for their 
entrepreneurial drive, remarkable growth, and generosity to the 
community. The care they provide for companion animals in northwest 
Iowa leaves a lasting impact on the Orange City community. I look 
forward to following their continued growth and success in the years to 
come.●
```
