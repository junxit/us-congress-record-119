# RECOGNIZING ARTISAN LABS

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                        RECOGNIZING ARTISAN LABS

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, as a member and former chairman of 
the Senate Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each month 
I recognize and celebrate the American entrepreneurial spirit by 
highlighting the success of a small business in my home State of Idaho. 
Today, I am proud to honor Artisan Labs of Hansen, ID, as the Idaho 
Small Business of the Month for August 2025.
  Founded in 2020 by Matt Bryant, Artisan Labs has quickly grown into a 
world-class cosmetic and personal care contract manufacturer. 
Specializing in innovative skin and hair care products, Artisan Labs is 
known for its ``hot pour'' items such as deodorants, balms, beauty 
sticks, candles, and ski wax. Today, the business serves nearly 100 
brand partners, with products retailing in stores like Ulta and 
Sephora, as well as through direct-to-consumer markets.
  Artisan Labs' state-of-the-art research, development, and production 
facility became fully operational in 2021. Now in their 5th year, 
Artisan Labs has plans for a 40,000-square-foot facility expansion that 
will support their continued growth and industry-leading formulation 
services.
  Artisan Labs' story is rooted in innovation, community, and 
empowerment. The company is committed to supporting STEM within the 
Magic Valley community and throughout Idaho. They host student groups, 
participate in career fairs, and partner with local youth development 
programs. A core aspect of Artisan Labs' mission is giving back to the 
Hansen community, which has welcomed them with open arms. By fostering 
educational opportunities in rural areas, Artisan Labs aims to inspire 
the next generation of entrepreneurs and support the southern Idaho 
economy.
  Congratulations to Artisan Labs and its team on being recognized as 
the Idaho Small Business of the Month for August 2025. Your dedication 
to excellence make Idaho proud, and I look forward to your continued 
growth and success.●
```
