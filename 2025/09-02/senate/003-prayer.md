# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of new beginnings, the author and sustainer of our faith, thank 
You for this fresh start as we strive again to do the work of freedom.
  Bless our lawmakers to seek to do Your will, empowering them with 
greater knowledge and discernment so that they may approve the things 
that are excellent. Lord, give them a productivity that comes from the 
power of Your Spirit, using them to do Your work on Earth. Show them 
Your greatness and Your mighty hand during this season of wars, 
violence, and polarization.
  You are our rock, our fortress, and our deliverer. We will trust in 
Your strength and preserve this land we live in so that we will be 
instruments of Your peace. We pray in Your invincible name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
