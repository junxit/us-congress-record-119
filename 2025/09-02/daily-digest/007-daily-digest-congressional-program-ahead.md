# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 2296

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

             Week of September 3 through September 5, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Wednesday, Senate will continue consideration of the motion to 
proceed to consideration of S. 2296, National Defense Authorization 
Act, post-cloture.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: September 4, to 
hold hearings to examine the nominations of Benjamin Hobbs, of Ohio, 
and Ronald Kurtz, of Georgia, both to be an Assistant Secretary of 
Housing and Urban Development, Stephen Miran, to be a Member, Board 
of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, and Chris Pilkerton, of 
Maryland, to be Assistant Secretary for Investment Security, and 
Jonathan Burke, of Georgia, to be Assistant Secretary for Terrorist
```
