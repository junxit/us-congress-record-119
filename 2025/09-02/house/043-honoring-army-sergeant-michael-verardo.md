# HONORING ARMY SERGEANT MICHAEL VERARDO

**Speaking:** Harris, Mark (H001102) [R-NC]

```
                 HONORING ARMY SERGEANT MICHAEL VERARDO

  (Mr. HARRIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HARRIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life 
of Army Sergeant Michael Verardo, a true American hero who passed away 
last week following a 15-year battle with injuries from his deployment 
in Afghanistan.
  Michael served his country with distinction as a proud infantryman in 
the 82nd Airborne Division. He survived devastating injuries from IED 
explosions that claimed one of his legs and left him with countless 
other seen and unseen injuries, resulting in over 120 surgeries and 
hours and hours of therapy.
  Michael's life was not simply defined by his courage but also by the 
love that he had for his dear wife, Sarah, and their three precious 
daughters.
  Mr. Speaker, his legacy will continue to empower thousands upon 
thousands of wounded veterans with hope as they live full lives back 
here at home. Michael's life is a powerful reminder that freedom is not 
free, and it comes at a cost.
```
