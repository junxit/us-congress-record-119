# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4851

**Measures:** H.R. 4851

```
            By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
        H.R. 4851.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Congress's enumerated 
     authority under Article I, Section 8, Clause 17 (Enclave 
     Clause), and Article I, Section 4, Clause 1 (Elections 
     Clause) of the U.S. Constitution, granting Congress exclusive 
     legislative control over the District of Columbia and broad 
     powers to regulate the time, place, and manner of federal 
     elections.
```
