# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4835

**Measures:** H.R. 4835

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 4835.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution, which 
     provides Congress the power to provide for the common defense 
     and general welfare of the United States, and Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 3, which grants Congress the authority to 
     regulate commerce with foreign nations and among the several 
     states.
```
