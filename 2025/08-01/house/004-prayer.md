# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, we entrust the work of this Nation into Your care. We 
give thanks for the Members of the House of Representatives and for 
their commitment to serve the people of this land. Bless them, along 
with their staffs and families with strength, wisdom, and discernment. 
Grant them patience, clarity, and unity in purpose as they seek the 
common good.
  May their service be marked by integrity and guided by compassion. It 
is with grateful and trusting hearts that we lift this prayer to You.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
