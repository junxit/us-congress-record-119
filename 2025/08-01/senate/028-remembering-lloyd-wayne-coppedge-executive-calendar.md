# Remembering Lloyd Wayne Coppedge (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Scott, Tim (S001184) [R-SC], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    Remembering Lloyd Wayne Coppedge

  Mr. SCOTT of South Carolina. Mr. President, I rise today to talk 
about two important topics. One is the loss of a remarkable South 
Carolinian. Mr. Lloyd Wayne Coppedge of Myrtle Beach went to be with 
the Lord on July 19 at the young, spry age of 80. Lloyd Coppedge was a 
great American, a great friend of mine. He was a devoted husband, 
father, grandfather, great-grandfather, and a dedicated friend.
  He had a business career marked by both heart and integrity. Lloyd 
purchased Wolverine Brass in Myrtle Beach, where he not only led the 
company to success but also created a work culture built on compassion 
and respect.
  His legacy lies in the lives he has touched, whose lives are better 
because of him. Lloyd was a giant of a man, and he will be missed by me 
and thousands and thousands of South Carolinians.
```
