# Text of Senate Amendment 3456

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

```
  SA 3456. Mr. HUSTED (for himself and Mr. Moreno) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill S. 2296, to 
authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2026 for military activities 
of the Department of Defense, for military construction, and for 
defense activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military 
personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes; which 
was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title II, insert the following:

     SEC. 2__. SILICON HEARTLAND NATIONAL CAMPUS.

       The amount authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 
     2026 by section 201 for research, development, test, and 
     evaluation is hereby increased by $30,000,000, with the 
     amount of the increase to be available for Aerospace Sensors 
     (PE 0602204F) for Silicon Heartland National Campus.
                                 ______
```
