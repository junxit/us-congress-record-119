# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 20, S.Res. 363, S.Res. 364, S.Res. 365, S.Res. 366, S.Res. 367

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. SCHMITT (for himself, Mr. Fetterman, Mr. 
             McCormick, Mr. Cassidy, Mr. Justice, Mr. Lee, Mrs. 
             Blackburn, Mr. Ricketts, Mrs. Britt, Ms. Ernst, Mr. 
             Tillis, Mr. Hagerty, Mr. Cornyn, Mr. Hoeven, Mr. 
             Barrasso, Mr. Banks, Mr. Moreno, Mr. Hawley, Mr. 
             Budd, Mr. Mullin, Mr. Cruz, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. 
             Sullivan, Ms. Lummis, Mr. Risch, Mr. Tuberville, Mrs. 
             Moody, and Mr. Marshall):
       S. Res. 363. A resolution solemnly marking the one-year 
     anniversary of the attempted assassination of President 
     Donald J. Trump, condemning the multiple attempts against the 
     President's life, condemning those who incite violence 
     against political officials, and honoring the victims of the 
     shooting; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. RICKETTS (for himself, Ms. Klobuchar, Mrs. 
             Fischer, Mr. Grassley, Ms. Smith, and Ms. Ernst):
       S. Res. 364. A resolution recognizing the 20th anniversary 
     of the Renewable Fuel

[[Page S5209]]

     Standard and its foundational role in strengthening American 
     energy security, supporting rural communities, growing the 
     United States economy, and improving environmental 
     performance; to the Committee on Environment and Public 
     Works.
           By Mr. WICKER (for himself and Mr. Kelly):
       S. Res. 365. A resolution designating August 1, 2025, as 
     ``Gold Star Children's Day''; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Ms. HIRONO:
       S. Res. 366. A resolution commemorating the 69th 
     anniversary of the continuous operations of the Mauna Loa 
     Observatory; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Mr. PADILLA (for himself, Mr. Sullivan, and Mr. 
             Schiff):
       S. Res. 367. A resolution designating July 2025 as 
     ``American Grown Flower and Foliage Month''; considered and 
     agreed to.
           By Mr. WICKER (for himself, Mr. Whitehouse, Mr. Tillis, 
             Ms. Smith, Mr. Boozman, Mr. Fetterman, Mrs. Britt, 
             and Mrs. Shaheen):
       S. Con. Res. 20. A concurrent resolution recognizing the 
     50th anniversary of the Helsinki Final Act and reaffirming 
     the United States' commitment to its principles and values; 
     to the Committee on Foreign Relations.

                          ____________________
```
