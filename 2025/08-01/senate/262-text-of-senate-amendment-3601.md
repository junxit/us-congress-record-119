# Text of Senate Amendment 3601

**Speaking:** Hickenlooper, John W. (H000273) [D-CO]

```
  SA 3601. Mr. HICKENLOOPER (for himself and Mr. Crapo) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill S. 2296, to 
authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2026 for military activities 
of the Department of Defense, for military construction, and for 
defense activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military 
personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes; which 
was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of subtitle B of title V, add the following:

     SEC. 515. LIMITATIONS APPLICABLE TO THE AUTHORITY TO TRANSFER 
                   SPACE FUNCTIONS OF THE AIR NATIONAL GUARD TO 
                   THE SPACE FORCE.

       Section 514 of the National Defense Authorization Act for 
     Fiscal Year 2025 (Public Law 118-159; 10 U.S.C. 20001 note) 
     is amended--
       (1) by redesignating subsection (k) as subsection (l); and
       (2) by inserting after subsection (j) the following new 
     subsection:
       ``(k) Rule of Construction.--Nothing in this section shall 
     be construed as--
       ``(1) authorizing the transfer of a member of the Air 
     National Guard of the United States other than on a one-time 
     basis as specified in subsection (c); or
       ``(2) setting future precedent with respect to waiving the 
     applicability of any provision of title 32.''.
                                 ______
```
