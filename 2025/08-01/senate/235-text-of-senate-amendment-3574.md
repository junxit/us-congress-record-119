# Text of Senate Amendment 3574

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
  SA 3574. Mr. CORNYN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 2296, to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
2026 for military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, 
to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title II, insert the following:

     SEC. 2__. MANUFACTURING DOMESTICALLY PRODUCED COMPONENTS FOR 
                   5G OPEN RADIO ACCESS NETWORK SYSTEMS.

       The amount authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 
     2026 by section 201 for research, development, test, and 
     evaluation and available for Next Generation Information 
     Communications Technology (5G) (PE 0604011D8Z) as specified 
     in the funding table in section 4201 is increased by 
     $75,000,000, with the amount of the increase to be available 
     for manufacture of domestically produced components for fifth 
     generation Open Radio Access Network (ORAN) systems.
                                 ______
```
