# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, we pray many prayers for many reasons, and we thank You for 
hearing us. Today we ask You to give our Senators a spirit of wisdom 
that will save them from despair and provide them with a straight path 
on which to walk without stumbling. Set a seal upon their lips so that 
no thoughtless words shall stain or harm others. May they meet today's 
tasks with courage and kindness, showing that they are truly Your 
children.
  Lord, empower them to see clearly solutions they couldn't discover 
without Your help, as You remind them that all things are possible to 
those who believe in You. Inspire them to commit to You the challenges 
and decisions they will face, believing that You will enable them to 
serve with excellence.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
