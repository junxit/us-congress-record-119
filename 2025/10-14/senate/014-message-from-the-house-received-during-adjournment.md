# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 104

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                    Enrolled Joint Resolution Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2025, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on October 10, 2025, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives announcing that the Speaker has signed the following 
enrolled joint resolution:

       H.J. Res. 104. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management 
     relating to ``Miles City Field Office Record of Decision and 
     Approved Resource Management Plan Amendment''.

                          ____________________
```
