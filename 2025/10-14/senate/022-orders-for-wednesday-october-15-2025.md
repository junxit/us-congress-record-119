# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 5371

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, October 15; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; finally, 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. to allow for 
weekly conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. For the information of my colleagues, Senators should 
expect a vote at approximately 2:15 p.m. on the motion to reconsider 
Calendar No. 168, H.R. 5371.

                          ____________________
```
