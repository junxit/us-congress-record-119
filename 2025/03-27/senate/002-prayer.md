# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our rock, fortress, and deliverer, we trust You to strengthen 
us today. Empower our Senators with humility to listen, wisdom to 
understand, courage to attempt, and power to obey. May they devote 
themselves to the honorable, the noble, and the good. Keep them from 
deviating from the path of strict integrity as You guide their hearts 
and minds in the knowledge of Your love.
  Lord, purify their ambitions so that they may set their hearts only 
on the things which please You. May they find, even in problems, 
opportunities to discover Your mighty power.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
