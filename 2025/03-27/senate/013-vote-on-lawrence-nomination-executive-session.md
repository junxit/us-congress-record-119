# Vote on Lawrence Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Cramer, Kevin (C001096) [R-ND], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                      Vote on Lawrence Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Lawrence nomination?
  Mr. CRAMER. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Louisiana (Mr. Kennedy) and the Senator from South Dakota 
(Mr. Rounds).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) 
and the Senator from New York (Mr. Schumer) are necessarily absent.
  The result was announced--yeas 51, nays 45, as follows:
```
