# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, MARCH 31, 2025, AT 3 P.M.

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

**Measures:** S.Res. 148

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, MARCH 31, 2025, AT 3 P.M.

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions 
of S. Res. 148.
  There being no objection, under the previous order and pursuant to 
the provisions of S. Res. 148, as a further mark of respect for the 
late Alan K. Simpson, former Senator from Wyoming, the Senate, at 4:39 
p.m., adjourned until Monday, March 31, 2025, at 3 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
