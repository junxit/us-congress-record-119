# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 25

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, announced

House of the following title:

       H.J. Res. 25. Joint Resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code,

     relating to ``Gross Proceeds Reporting by Brokers That
```
