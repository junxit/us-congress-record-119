# RECOGNIZING THE KOREAN AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF THE WASHINGTON METROPOLITAN AREA AND THE 2025 KORUS FESTIVAL VOLUNTEERS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE KOREAN AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF THE WASHINGTON 
        METROPOLITAN AREA AND THE 2025 KORUS FESTIVAL VOLUNTEERS

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 6, 2025

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Korean 
American Association of the Washington Metropolitan Area (KAAW) and its 
President, Steve Lee, on the occasion of the 22nd KORUS Festival.
  As one of the largest festivals of its kind, the KORUS festival 
brings together Koreans, Korean Americans, and others from our rich 
multicultural community to celebrate our dynamic and harmonious 
relationship. The festival provides not only the opportunity for us to 
experience the customs of the Korean culture, but also to recognize the 
shared history and partnership between our two nations. As a member of 
Congress, I look forward to continuing my friendship with KAAW and the 
Korean American community as we continue to build on the success of our 
alliance and work together toward the achievement of our common goals. 
I will continue to do all that I can to ensure that the U.S.-Korea 
alliance remains strong and enduring.
  It would not be possible to hold an event of this magnitude without 
the support and commitment of volunteers from the community. It is my 
great honor to recognize the efforts and include in the Record the name 
of the following individuals:

       Steve S. Lee, Victoria Lee, Taesung Lee, Brian Kim, Mi 
     Kyung An, Jae Byun, Jae Lee, Kwang Hyung Park, Wan J. Chae, 
     Chun J. Park, Elwin Maldonado, Mi Ja Kim, Jenny Hwang, Eric 
     Shin, William Hwang, Bethel Lee, Ella Jung, Naomi Mercy Han, 
     Zephaniah Kim, Sena Kajiyama, Sophia Moon, Jeffrey Kim, Roi 
     Lee, Sophia Patton, Enoch Hayul Lee, Ellie Harin Lee, Iriel 
     Kang, Kaylee Kim, Luke Lee, Leonard Kim, Aria Kwon, Kevin 
     Kim, Irene Kang, Han June Lee, Veronica Patton, Luke An, and 
     Genevieve An.

  I would also like to give special recognition to Ms. Anna Ko, whose 
tireless work as KORUS coordinator and Master of Ceremonies helped make 
this amazing event possible.
  Mr. Speaker, festivals like KORUS are a wonderful example of the 
tremendous diversity we enjoy in Northern Virginia. Such a variety of 
cultures enriches the lives of our citizens and fosters greater 
tolerance and understanding. I commend KAAW and the volunteers listed 
above for helping to bring such wonderful events to fruition and ask my 
colleagues to join me in wishing them great success in their future 
endeavors.
```
