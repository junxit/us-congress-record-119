# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S.Res. 412, S. 1070, S. 2126, S. 2245, S. 2357, S. 2563, S. 2666

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

               Week of October 7 through October 10, 2025

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of the en bloc 
nominations provided for under S. Res. 412, En Bloc Consideration of 
Certain Nominations, post-cloture.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
```
