# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
                    ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. If the Senator-elect will now present 
himself at the desk, the Chair will administer the oath of office.
  The Senator, escorted by Mrs. CAPITO, advanced to the desk of the 
President pro tempore; the oath prescribed by law was administered by 
the President pro tempore; and he subscribed to the oath in the 
Official Oath Book.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Congratulations, Senator.
  (Applause, Senators rising.)
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Hyde-Smith). The Senator from Texas.
  Mr. CORNYN. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CORNYN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
