# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The Presiding Officer. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to Public Law 94-304, as amended by Public Law 99-7, appoints 
the following Senators as members of the Commission on Security and 
Cooperation in Europe (Helsinki Commission) during the 119th Congress: 
the Honorable Sheldon Whitehouse of Rhode Island; the Honorable Jeanne 
Shaheen of New Hampshire; the Honorable Tina Smith of Minnesota; and 
the Honorable John Fetterman of Pennsylvania.
```
