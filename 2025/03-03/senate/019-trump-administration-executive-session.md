# Trump Administration (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schatz, Brian (S001194) [D-HI]

```
                          Trump Administration

  Madam President, we are witnessing one of the worst starts to any 
Presidency in generations, and it is not even close. There is a 
possibility of negative growth in the first quarter of this year--which 
would have been unheard of--so bad economically that the Secretary of 
Commerce is trying to concoct another way to calculate GDP. If you 
wonder how bad the data is, you have the Secretary of Commerce going, 
like, ``Well, this whole GDP thing--we have been measuring it wrong for 
all of these decades.''
  There is no comparison for the staggering--staggering--levels of 
incompetence and lawlessness coursing through this administration, and 
regular people are paying a steep price.
  Tomorrow night, the President will try to rewrite his record and 
recast blame when he addresses Congress. I want to go over some of the 
worst things that he has done that actually hurt people, because unless 
you are a billionaire, life has gotten harder, not easier, over the 
last couple of months.
  No. 1. He is actively raising the price of everything. He is actively 
raising the price of everything. After specifically promising that he 
would bring down prices on day one, he has gone out of his way to raise 
them. The price of eggs is up 15 percent. Coffee prices are at record 
highs. The price of everything from tomatoes, to strawberries, to 
clothes, to toys, to cars, to homes and home building is about to go up 
starting tomorrow because Trump is picking a needless fight with our 
largest trading partner with tariffs. Prices will skyrocket, businesses 
will be hurt, and people will lose their jobs.
  No. 2. Not even a week into his second term, Trump illegally froze 
Federal funding for all 50 States--all 50 States. With one Monday night 
memorandum, the White House locked States out of portals for everything 
from Medicaid payments to housing assistance. Schools, childcare 
centers, rural hospitals, and VA clinics were suddenly left wondering 
if they would have to close their doors or lay off staff. Almost a 
month later, the taps are not fully turned back on.
  As a result of the freeze and Trump's other Executive orders, 
hundreds of billions of dollars continue to be withheld from States and 
communities where they belong. These are taxpayer dollars. These are 
dollars that are appropriated by this branch of government, the article 
I branch of government.
  If you don't like how appropriated funds are going to be spent, there 
is a mechanism for an executive branch to do something about it. They 
can propose a new budget, and they can either sign or veto a new 
appropriations bill. But that is not what they did. They just acted as 
though they could cross out things that they found objectionable. And 
it is not even as though they did that in some sort of methodical way. 
They had a bunch of kids going in there--literally kids--going 
``Control+F.'' Oh, gender? Kill that. Oh, Native? Kill that. Oh, 
climate? Kill that. That is how they figured out what they were going 
to cut.
  No. 3. Trump is illegally firing tens of thousands of government 
workers, many of whom are veterans, and he is not done yet. These are 
people who have dedicated their careers to public service, and in the 
case of veterans, risked their lives to protect our country. They are 
just like the rest of us--bills to pay, kids to feed, parents to care 
for. And they are being discarded. This is the biggest mass firing of 
veterans in U.S. history.
  No. 4. No one stands to benefit more from this ransacking of the 
government than billionaires, which is no surprise considering it is 
being led by the richest people on the planet.
  Eliminating the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau that was created 
in the aftermath of the 2008 financial crisis specifically to protect 
people from fraud and deceptive lending practices or dramatically 
shrinking the IRS, which means refunds will be delayed during tax 
season, and tax fraud will skyrocket without proper scrutiny--none of 
that benefits a regular person. None of that benefits a regular person, 
but it is great news if you are a billionaire trying to wiggle out of 
paying taxes or a shady lender who is looking to defraud people.

  No. 5. If all of this wasn't enough, Trump and the Republicans are 
working to cut taxes for billionaires and make regular people foot the 
bill. Now, that is not unusual for them. Cutting taxes for billionaires 
is kind of their thing. They like it. It is their reason for being. But 
it is alarming nonetheless in the brazenness of how they are going 
about doing this.
  They basically want to cut money that goes to regular people and book 
that as savings, right? So whenever you do a tax bill, if you are doing 
it right, it pays for itself, which means you have to have something on 
the side of the ledger where you are bringing in more money and then 
another side of the ledger where you are cutting revenue, and then it 
adds up to zero, nets out, deficit neutral.
  The problem is, they can't really figure out how to deliver this $4 
trillion-or-so tax cut to the wealthiest corporations that have ever 
existed in human history and do it in a deficit-neutral way, and so 
they are just, like, committing arson within Departments and then 
trying to book that as savings to pay for the tax cut.
  Now, it is true that--well, all political parties have a tendency 
towards exaggeration, and so you would be forgiven if you hear this and 
go ``Well, the Democrats are saying this is what the tax policy is, and 
maybe it is not exactly like that.'' But I want you to understand that 
what is happening in the U.S. House as they negotiate over this big tax 
bill is exactly that.
  They are demanding more and more cuts from the Affordable Care Act; 
from the Social Security Administration in terms of the staffing of the 
place; from Medicaid, where they want to turn it into block grants, 
which is just a euphemism for ``Medicaid cuts.'' Then they want to take 
all of those savings and not, like, reinvest it in some other part of 
the government, not put it in the Department of Education, not give it 
to low-income individuals so that they can climb out of poverty; they 
actually just want to take trillions--trillions--of dollars from you 
and give it to them.
```
