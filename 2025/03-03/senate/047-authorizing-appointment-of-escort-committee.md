# AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
              AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Presiding 
Officer of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part 
of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort the President of the United States into the 
House Chamber for the joint session to be held at 9 p.m. on Tuesday, 
March 4, 2025.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
