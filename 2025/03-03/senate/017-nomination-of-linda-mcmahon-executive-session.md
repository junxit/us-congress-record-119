# Nomination of Linda McMahon (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schatz, Brian (S001194) [D-HI], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                      Nomination of Linda McMahon

  Mr. President, and finally, on Linda McMahon, the nominee for 
Secretary of Education. I am very proud that every single Democrat will 
vote against Mrs. McMahon. Mrs. McMahon's confirmation will be a slap 
in the face to students, parents, teachers who care about our public 
schools. Americans believe in public education. They don't want to see 
these cuts. They don't want to see the Department of Education 
abolished.
  If the Trump administration follows through on cuts to education, 
schools will lose billions in funding, and what is going to happen, Mr. 
and Mrs. Average American Taxpayer? Your property taxes will go up. The 
least popular tax probably that there is.
  The American people do not want to see cuts to education and the 
consequent rising property taxes, but that is the danger of confirming 
Mrs. McMahon. Before Republicans vote for Mrs. McMahon, you should 
remember, slashing funds for schools, students, parents, and teachers, 
all for the sake of billionaire tax breaks, is a very bad idea. Senate 
Democrats know it is a horrible idea. The American people know it is a 
horrible idea.
  Today, we will see where Senate Republicans stand.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHATZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
