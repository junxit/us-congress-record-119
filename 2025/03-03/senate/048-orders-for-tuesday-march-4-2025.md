# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 4, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 3

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 4, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Tuesday, March 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business for debate only 
until 11 a.m.; finally, that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 
2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly conference meetings, and that if the 
motion is agreed to on the motion to proceed to Calendar No. 11, S.J. 
Res. 3, all time during recess count equally between the proponents and 
opponents.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
Senators should expect a vote to proceed to Senator Cruz's CRA at 11 
a.m. tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
