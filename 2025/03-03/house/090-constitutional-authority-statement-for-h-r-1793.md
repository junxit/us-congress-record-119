# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1793

**Measures:** H.R. 1793

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 1793.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 14, which empowers Congress 
     ``To make Rules for the Government and Regulation of the land 
     and naval Forces.''; and
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 12 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which grants Congress the power ``To raise and support 
     Armies,'' and Clause 13, which provides the authority ``To 
     provide and maintain a Navy.''
```
