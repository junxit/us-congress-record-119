# AMERICA FIRST

**Speaking:** LaMalfa, Doug (L000578) [R-CA]

```
                              {time}  1915
                             AMERICA FIRST

  (Mr. LaMALFA asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LaMALFA. Mr. Speaker, we welcome President Trump tomorrow to the 
Capitol for his joint address here in Congress.
  It is more than just a speech. It is an opportunity to lay out the 
successes so far of this administration and the important work ahead to 
restore American strength, the economy, as well as world affairs.
  In just a short time, President Trump has delivered real results, 
ending the taxpayer-funded DEI programs and stopping the government 
from wasting money on divisive policies.
  He took decisive action to secure our border. The numbers have been 
dropping dramatically from those who were illegally crossing just a 
couple of months ago to just a couple hundred a day. This puts the 
safety of American citizens first.
  The President is cutting through bloated bureaucracy and the alarming 
waste and missed priorities of programs that the American taxpayers 
don't even want any part of. This makes government work for the people, 
not against them, which respects taxpayers.
  On the world stage, he has proven that peace comes through strength, 
rejecting the weak leadership that left America vulnerable and our 
adversaries emboldened, which helps to prevent wars that would happen 
and end current wars.

                          ____________________
```
