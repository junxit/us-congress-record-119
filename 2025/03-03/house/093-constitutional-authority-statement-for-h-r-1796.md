# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1796

**Measures:** H.R. 1796

```
           By Mr. JAMES:
       H.R. 1796.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Clause 7(c)(1) of Rule XII of the Rules of the 
     House of Representatives, the constitutional authority for 
     enacting this legislation is found in Article I, Section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution, specifically:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1: The General Welfare Clause, 
     which grants Congress the power to provide for the general 
     welfare of the United States, including promoting public 
     health initiatives.
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Commerce Clause, which 
     grants Congress the authority to regulate commerce among the 
     several states, including the regulation of healthcare 
     services and programs that impact interstate commerce.
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18: The Necessary and Proper 
     Clause, which grants Congress the authority to enact laws 
     necessary and proper for carrying out its enumerated powers, 
     including the implementation of public health programs to 
     address diseases such as sickle cell disease.
```
