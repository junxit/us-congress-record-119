# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1774

**Measures:** H.R. 1774

```
           By Mr. CLYDE:
       H.R. 1774.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8, Clause 17 of the U.S. 
     Constitution Congress has the exclusive power to address 
     legislation governing the affairs of the District of 
     Columbia, to which Congress has the exclsuive power ``to 
     excercise exclusive Legislation in all Cases whatsover, over 
     such District (not exceeding ten Miles square) as may . . .''
```
