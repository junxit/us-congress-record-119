# HONORING CAROL ANN CLAY

**Speaking:** Bell, Wesley (B001324) [D-MO]

```
                        HONORING CAROL ANN CLAY

  (Mr. BELL asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to honor the 
life and legacy of Carol Clay, a remarkable woman whose impact on St. 
Louis and our Nation will never be forgotten.
  Mr. Speaker, it saddens me that my first opportunity to honor the 
Clay family comes at such a difficult time as we mourn an extraordinary 
woman.
  Carol Clay was a pillar of strength, grace, and conviction. She was a 
devoted wife, mother, and grandmother. She stood shoulder to shoulder 
with her husband, former Congressman Bill Clay, in the fight for 
justice.
  Her legacy lives on in the young people who were uplifted through the 
CBC Spouses Scholarship Fund, the community she strengthened, and the 
family she nurtured.
  My deepest condolences to Congressman Clay, Sr.; to Lacy; and the 
entire Clay family. May Carol Clay rest in peace, and may her memory 
continue to guide us toward a more just and compassionate world.

                          ____________________
```
