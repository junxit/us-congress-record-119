# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 31, 2025

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 31, 2025

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Thursday, July 31; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and that the Senate proceed to 
executive session and resume consideration of the Executive Calendar 
No. 271, Cheryl Mason; further, notwithstanding rule XXII, at 11 a.m., 
the cloture motions filed on Tuesday ripen and the Senate vote on the 
motion to invoke cloture on the Mason nomination; if cloture is invoked 
on the Mason nomination, the Senate then execute the order of July 30 
in relation to the Clarkson nomination; further, at 2:15 p.m., the 
Senate execute the order in relation to the Serralta nomination, and 
following the disposition of the Serralta nomination, the Senate vote 
on confirmation of the Mason nomination, and if confirmed, the Senate 
vote on the motion to invoke cloture on Executive Calendar No. 254, 
Andrea Lucas, and if cloture is invoked, the Senate then execute the 
order in relation to the Kozma nomination; finally, if any nominations 
are confirmed during Thursday's session of the Senate, the motions to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table and the President 
be immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. For the information of all Senators, Senators should 
expect two votes at 11 a.m., four votes at 2:15 p.m., and further votes 
throughout the day.

                          ____________________
```
