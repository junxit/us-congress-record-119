# Cloture Motion (Executive Calendar)

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 182, Thomas Gaiser, of Ohio, to be 
     an Assistant Attorney General.
         John Thune, Eric Schmitt, John R. Curtis, Tim Scott of 
           South Carolina, Bill Cassidy, Jon Husted, Steve Daines, 
           Marsha Blackburn, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Ron Johnson, John 
           Barrasso, Tim Sheehy, Roger F. Wicker, Mike Rounds, 
           Bernie Moreno, Pete Ricketts, Jim Justice.
