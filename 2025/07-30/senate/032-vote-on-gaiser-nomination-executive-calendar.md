# Vote on Gaiser Nomination (Executive Calendar)

                       Vote on Gaiser Nomination

  Mrs. HYDE-SMITH. Mr. President, I know of no further debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there further debate?
  If not, the question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the 
Gaiser nomination?
