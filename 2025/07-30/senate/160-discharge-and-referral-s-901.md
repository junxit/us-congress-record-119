# DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--S. 901

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 901

```
                     DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--S. 901

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs be discharged from further 
consideration of S. 901, a bill to amend the Small Business Act and the 
Small Business Investment Act of 1958 to increase the maximum loan 
amount for certain loans, and for other purposes, and be referred to 
the Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
