                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and were referred as 
indicated:

        EC-1895. A communication from the Senior Bureau Official, 
     Legislative Affairs, Department of State, transmitting, 
     pursuant to section 36(c) of the Arms Export Control Act, the 
     certification of a proposed license for the export of 
     firearms, parts, and components controlled under Category I 
     of the U.S. Munitions List to Denmark in the amount of 
     $1,000,000 or more (Transmittal No. DDTC 25-083) received in 
     the Office of the President pro tempore; to the Committee on 
     Foreign Relations.

     Legislative Affairs, Department of State, transmitting, 
     pursuant to sections 36(c) and 36(d) of the Arms Export

     defense services to the United Kingdom in the amount of 
     $50,000,000 or more (Transmittal No. DDTC 25-038) received in 
     the Office of the President pro tempore; to the Committee on 
     Foreign Relations.
