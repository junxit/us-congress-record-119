# TRIBUTE TO KELLEY ERSTINE

**Speaking:** Cotton, Tom (C001095) [R-AR]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       TRIBUTE TO KELLEY ERSTINE

● Mr. COTTON. Mr. President, today I would like to offer my 
congratulations to Mr. Kelley Erstine for his induction into the 
Arkansas Insurance Hall of Fame. A native of Sheridan, AR, Mr. Erstine 
is the CEO of Independent Insurance Agents of Arkansas. As CEO,

[[Page S6872]]

he has dedicated his career to advancing the insurance industry in the 
Natural State with excellence, professionalism, and a commitment to 
serving his fellow Arkansans. I would like to recognize Mr. Erstine for 
this professional achievement, for his more than 20 years of work in 
the insurance industry, and for his numerous contributions to the state 
of Arkansas.●
```
