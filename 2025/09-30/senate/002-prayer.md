# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our God, we are thankful that You have not only known us, but You 
have made Yourself known to us. You told us in Genesis 8:22 that as 
long as time shall last, there will always be seedtime and harvest.
  May our lawmakers plant seeds that will bring a harvest for Your 
glory. Inspire them to seek Your wisdom and guidance as they 
acknowledge that without You, they can accomplish nothing that will 
endure. As they seek to be Your ambassadors to our Nation and world, 
may they remember to use our liberties and privileges bought with so 
crimson a cost to promote the common good of humanity.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
