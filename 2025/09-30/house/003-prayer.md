# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Charles Trullols, Catholic Information Center, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty and eternal Father, creator of Heaven and Earth, we come 
before You with praise and trust as Your children. You have given us 
laws to guide our lives so that we may flourish as individuals and as a 
society.
  Bless and guide the Members of this Chamber, that they may enact laws 
which are just and righteous for the good of our Nation. If we have 
failed, grant us the wisdom and humility to pursue solutions for the 
betterment of our people.
  As we prepare to celebrate the 250th anniversary of our Nation, we 
thank You for being its foundation, knowing that without You, we are 
powerless.
  Holy Spirit, enkindle in our lawmakers the fire of Your love. May 
their work be devoted to truth, life, and peace, pursued with unity and 
selflessness. Make this Capitol a beacon of light for our country today 
and always.
  Through Christ our Lord.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
