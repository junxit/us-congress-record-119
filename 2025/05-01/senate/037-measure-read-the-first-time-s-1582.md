# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1582

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

**Measures:** S. 1582

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1582

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.

       A bill (S. 1582) to provide for the regulation of payment
```
