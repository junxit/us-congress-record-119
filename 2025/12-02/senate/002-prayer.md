# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, strong to save, empower our lawmakers to serve You 
today, to solve problems, and to remove impediments. Give them Your 
higher wisdom as they seek You, the source of their strength. Lord, 
surround them with the shield of Your divine approbation so that no 
weapon formed against them will prosper.
  Almighty God, provide our Senators strength for the adventures and 
challenges of these hours, inspiring them with the courage to oppose 
falsehood in all of its forms. May Your truth, love, and power fill 
their hearts.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
