Committee Meetings
PENSION PREDATORS: STOPPING CLASS ACTION ABUSE AGAINST WORKERS' 
RETIREMENT
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Health, 
Employment, Labor, and Pensions held a hearing entitled ``Pension 
Predators: Stopping Class Action Abuse Against Workers' Retirement''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LEGISLATIVE SOLUTIONS TO PROTECT CHILDREN AND TEENS ONLINE
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Commerce, 
Manufacturing, and Trade held a hearing entitled ``Legislative 
Solutions to Protect Children and Teens Online''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
SECURING AMERICA'S ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE: ADDRESSING CYBER AND PHYSICAL 
THREATS TO THE GRID
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Energy held a hearing 
entitled ``Securing America's

[[Page D1194]]

Energy Infrastructure: Addressing Cyber and Physical Threats to the 
Grid''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
OVERSIGHT OF PRUDENTIAL REGULATORS
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Oversight of Prudential Regulators''. Testimony was heard from 
Michelle Bowman, Vice Chair for Supervision, Board of Governors of the 
Federal Reserve System; Jonathan Gould, Comptroller, Office of the 
Comptroller of the Currency, Department of the Treasury; Travis Hill, 
Acting Chairman, Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation; and a public 
witness.
EXAMINING PRIMARY DEALERS AND THEIR BALANCE SHEET CONSTRAINTS
Committee on Financial Services: Task Force on Monetary Policy, 
Treasury Market Resilience, and Economic Prosperity held a hearing 
entitled ``Examining Primary Dealers and Their Balance Sheet 
Constraints''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.

Committee on Foreign Affairs: Europe Subcommittee held a hearing 
entitled ``Flashpoint: A Path Toward Stability in the Western

Enforcement and Security, Department of the Interior; Denise Bowden, 
Mayor, Chincoteague, Virginia; and public witnesses.
