# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Redeemer God, having enjoyed weeks of anticipation, when we held onto 
the hope that somehow this almost magical season would bring peace to 
the Earth and inspire the goodwill of all people, now that the 
celebration and merriment are ending, let us not discard our deepest 
desires and pack them away like old wrapping paper.
  Instead, let us see that we can and should linger in our hope for 
peace and still anticipate the miracles You will reveal to us this day.
  For each day is a sacred time to cherish, an opportunity to witness 
Your work in our world.
  Lord, let the eyes of the blind be opened, the ears of the deaf 
unstopped. Let the lame leap like a deer and the mute tongue sing for 
joy.
  For You are faithful to us at all times, in days of celebration and 
in times of solemnity. You have done, are doing, and will do marvelous 
things--things You planned long ago, that we would know and rejoice in 
Your steadfast love for us.
  In the power that is ours when we call on Your name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
