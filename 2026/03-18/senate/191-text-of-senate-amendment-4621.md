# Text of Senate Amendment 4621

**Speaking:** Hickenlooper, John W. (H000273) [D-CO]

```
  SA 4621. Mr. HICKENLOOPER submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory 
Committee on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. REQUIREMENT FOR STRATEGIC BASING PROCESS BEFORE 
                   RELOCATING A COMBATANT COMMAND.

       The Secretary of Defense may not relocate a combatant 
     command that has reached full operational capacity until the 
     Secretary conducts a new strategic basing process with 
     respect to the location of that combatant command.
                                 ______
```
