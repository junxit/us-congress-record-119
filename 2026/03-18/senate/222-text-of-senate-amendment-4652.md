# Text of Senate Amendment 4652

**Speaking:** Coons, Christopher A. (C001088) [D-DE]

S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee on Equal Access,

     individual who resides 50 miles or more away from the office 
     of such official, provides a copy of such documentary proof 
     to such office via mail or online)'' after ``official''.
