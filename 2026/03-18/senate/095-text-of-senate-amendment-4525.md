# Text of Senate Amendment 4525

**Speaking:** Lujan, Ben Ray (L000570) [D-NM]

```
  SA 4525. Mr. LUJAN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee 
on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. _____. PROVISION OF EPSTEIN FILES.

       The Department of Justice shall provide the Epstein files 
     to the New Mexico Department of Justice.
                                 ______
```
