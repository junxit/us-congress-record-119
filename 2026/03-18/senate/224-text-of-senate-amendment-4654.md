# Text of Senate Amendment 4654

**Speaking:** Coons, Christopher A. (C001088) [D-DE]

```
  SA 4654. Mr. COONS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee 
on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the end of the bill, add the following:

     SEC. 4. PROHIBITION AGAINST POSTING ICE OFFICERS AND AGENTS 
                   AT POLLING PLACES.

       Consistent with the prohibition described in section 592 of 
     title 18, United States Code, no Federal funds may be used to 
     post any U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer or 
     agent at or near any polling place in any State while a 
     general or special election is being held.
```
