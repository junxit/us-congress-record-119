# Text of Senate Amendment 4509

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  SA 4509. Mr. SCHIFF submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee 
on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION ON USE OF NEWS DISTORTION AUTHORITY FOR 
                   VIEWPOINT-BASED INVESTIGATIONS.

       The Federal Communications Commission may not use the news 
     distortion authority of the Commission to conduct any 
     viewpoint-based investigation.
                                 ______
```
