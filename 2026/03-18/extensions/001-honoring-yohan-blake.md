# HONORING YOHAN BLAKE

**Speaking:** Cherfilus-McCormick, Sheila (C001127) [D-FL]

```
                          HONORING YOHAN BLAKE

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 18, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Yohan Blake and include in the Record the following proclamation in his 
honor:

       Whereas, Yohan Blake is a world-renowned Jamaican sprinter 
     who has established himself as one of the fastest athletes in 
     the history of track and field, achieving extraordinary 
     success in the 100-meter and 200-meter sprint events; and
       Whereas, Blake rose to international prominence when he 
     became the youngest 100-meter World Champion in history after 
     winning gold at the 2011 World Athletics Championships, 
     demonstrating exceptional talent, discipline, and 
     determination on the global stage; and
       Whereas, at the 2012 Summer Olympics, Blake earned two 
     silver medals in the 100-meter and 200-meter events while 
     representing Jamaica, competing among the world's greatest 
     athletes and further cementing his legacy in international 
     athletics; and
       Whereas, known by fans worldwide as ``The Beast,'' Blake 
     has achieved some of the fastest sprint times ever recorded, 
     including a personal best of 9.69 seconds in the 100-meter 
     and 19.26 seconds in the 200-meter, making him one of the 
     fastest men in history; and
       Whereas, throughout his career, Blake has inspired 
     countless young athletes across the Caribbean and around the 
     world, serving as a powerful example of perseverance, 
     excellence, and dedication to sport; and
       Therefore, be it resolved that we, Florida's 20th 
     Congressional District, proudly recognize and honor Yohan 
     Blake for his remarkable achievements in athletics and for 
     the inspiration he continues to provide to aspiring athletes 
     and communities worldwide.

                          ____________________
```
