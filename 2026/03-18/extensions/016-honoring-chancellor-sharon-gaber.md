# HONORING CHANCELLOR SHARON GABER

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
                    HONORING CHANCELLOR SHARON GABER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 18, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Chancellor Sharon 
Gaber for her selection as the Charlotte Business Journal's 2025 
Business Person of the Year. Chancellor Gaber is an extraordinary 
leader representing an equally extraordinary institution in the great 
state of North Carolina--one that also happens to be my alma mater.
  I have personally known every one of the chancellors who have led UNC 
Charlotte. Each was an exceptional leader, with particular gifts who 
served our university at the perfect time. Chancellor Gaber continues 
this legacy. She is a driven visionary who excels at building teams and 
inspiring excellence in others.
  Under the leadership of Chancellor Gaber, UNC Charlotte has 
experienced record enrollment, achieved R1 research status, and made 
major strategic investments to transform the university and the region 
it serves.
  This progress is not happening in isolation. UNC Charlotte has become 
a powerful engine for economic growth--driving innovation, workforce 
development, and research partnerships that benefit North Carolina and 
the country. From expanding access to higher education, to supporting 
cutting-edge research, Chancellor Gaber has positioned UNC Charlotte as 
a model for how public universities can thrive.
  I congratulate Chancellor Gaber and the entire UNC Charlotte 
community on this well-deserved recognition and their continued impact. 
It is fitting that her leadership has been recognized with the 
Charlotte Business Journal's 2025 Business Person of the Year award. 
This is a proud moment for the 49ers community.
  I would like to once again congratulate Chancellor Gaber and 
recognize the tremendous contributions she has made to UNC Charlotte 
and the state of North Carolina.

                          ____________________
```
