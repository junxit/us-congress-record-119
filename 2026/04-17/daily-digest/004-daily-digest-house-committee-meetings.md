# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--NATIONAL GUARD AND RESERVES FORCES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Defense held a budget 
hearing on the National Guard and Reserves Forces. Testimony was heard 
from Lieutenant General Leonard F. Anderson IV, Commander, U.S. Marine 
Corps Reserve, Department of the Navy; Lieutenant General Robert D. 
Harter, Chief of Army Reserve and Commanding General, U.S. Army Reserve 
Command; Lieutenant General John P. Healy, Chief of Air Force Reserve; 
Rear Admiral Lower Half Richard S. Lofgren, Acting Chief of Navy 
Reserve, Department of the Navy; and General Steven S. Nordhaus, Chief, 
National Guard Bureau.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Military Construction, 
Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies held a markup on the 
Subcommittee on Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related 
Agencies Appropriations Bill, FY 2027. The Subcommittee on Military 
Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies Appropriations 
Bill, FY 2027, was forwarded to the full Committee, without amendment.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Financial Services and 
General Government held a markup on the Subcommittee on Financial 
Services and General Government Appropriations Bill, FY 2027. The

[[Page D407]]

Subcommittee on Financial Services and General Government 
Appropriations Bill, FY 2027, was forwarded to the full Committee, 
without amendment.
EXAMINING THE POLICIES AND PRIORITIES OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND 
HUMAN SERVICES
Committee on Education and Workforce: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Examining the Policies and Priorities of the Department of 
Health and Human Services''. Testimony was heard from Robert F. 
Kennedy, Jr., Secretary, Department of Health and Human Services.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
