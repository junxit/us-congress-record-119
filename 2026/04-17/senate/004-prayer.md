# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, today, give us zeal with knowledge that will order 
our priorities for Your glory. Remind us to strive to bring deliverance 
to captives, sight to the morally blind, and uplift to the downtrodden.
  As our lawmakers make plans, give them faith to know that Your 
principles, priorities, and purposes will prevail. As our Senators 
cultivate reverence for You, bless them with safety, security, and 
peace.
  Lord, let there be peace on Earth, and let it begin with us.
  We pray in Your transcendent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
