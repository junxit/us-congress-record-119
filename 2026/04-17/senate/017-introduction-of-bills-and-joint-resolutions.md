# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 4343, S. 4344

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. HAWLEY:
       S. 4343. A bill to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     provide that a Member of Congress convicted of certain felony 
     offenses relating to sexual abuse shall not be eligible for 
     retirement benefits based on that individual's Member 
     service, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Homeland 
     Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Mr. COTTON (for himself and Mr. Grassley):
       S. 4344. A bill to extend section 702 of the Foreign 
     Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 for 3 years; read the 
     first time.
```
