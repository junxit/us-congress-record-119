# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 4288, H.R. 6007, H.R. 6963

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 4288. An act to name the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in San Jose, 
     California, as the ``Corporal Patrick D. Tillman VA Clinic''; 
     to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       H.R. 6007. An act to name the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in Newton, New 
     Jersey, as the ``Anthony `Tony' J. Gallopo VA Clinic''; to 
     the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       H.R. 6963. An act to designate the community-based 
     outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in 
     Lafayette, Louisiana, as the ``Rodney C. Hamilton Sr. VA 
     Clinic''; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
