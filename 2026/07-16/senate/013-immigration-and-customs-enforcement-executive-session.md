# Immigration and Customs Enforcement (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                  Immigration and Customs Enforcement

  Now on Republican inaction on ICE, two more people have been gunned 
down in the street by Trump's ICE agents, and all Republicans have done 
is shrug their shoulders and point fingers. Senate Republicans know 
full well they were the ones who abandoned commonsense reforms to rein 
in ICE on multiple occasions.
  Yesterday, Senator Collins of Maine tried pinning the blame on 
Democrats even though she voted against an amendment requiring ICE to 
wear body cameras just last month. Absolutely shameless. Absolutely 
shameless.
  If Republicans had accepted our proposal for body cameras, warrant 
requirements, use-of-force standards, training, and other basic reforms 
used by every law enforcement agency across the country, these killings 
could have been prevented. Instead, Republicans jammed through a 
partisan bill

[[Page S4121]]

that pumped tens of billions of dollars into Trump's rogue Agencies 
without any restraints whatsoever.
  So it was Senate Republicans who chose not to make reforms to ICE, as 
Democrats tried to push them. Americans know exactly who to blame for 
unleashing the chaos on the streets.
  Where were Senate Republicans when Trump immediately reinstated the 
vehicle stops policy that led to these killings? Where were Senate 
Republicans when the administration said ICE agents had ``absolute 
immunity'' after the Minneapolis murders? Where were Senate Republicans 
when we gave them opportunity after opportunity after opportunity to 
reform ICE?
  There is blood on the streets, and the blame is on the Senate 
Republicans.
  I yield the floor.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
