# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, as we begin this new day, filled with great 
responsibilities and complex challenges, we are drawn into Your 
presence by the magnitude of Your love. We come at Your invitation, 
longing to know Your will, encouraged by Your even greater desire to 
lead, direct, and inspire those who seek You.
  Lord, in the quietness of communion with You, may the tightly wound 
springs of pressure and stress within every lawmaker be gently 
released, and may a deep and abiding peace settle upon their hearts and 
minds. Give them clarity of thought, steadiness of purpose, and 
humility of spirit as they carry out the trust placed in their hands.
  We pray in Your magnificent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
