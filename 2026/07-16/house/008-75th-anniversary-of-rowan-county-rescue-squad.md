# 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF ROWAN COUNTY RESCUE SQUAD

**Speaking:** McDowell, Addison P. (M001240) [R-NC]

```
             75TH ANNIVERSARY OF ROWAN COUNTY RESCUE SQUAD

  (Mr. McDOWELL asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McDOWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Rowan County 
Rescue Squad as we celebrate its 75th anniversary of protecting and 
serving the community.
  Since 1951, the rescue squad has answered the call for more than 
140,000 residents who call Rowan County their home. While at its core 
the rescue squad is made up of brave volunteers, they also are joined 
by staff to help provide enhanced services of the highest quality to 
everyone in Rowan County.
  Over its first 75 years, they have always been there in times of 
emergency ready to protect and respond. No matter where, whether on our 
waterways or in our neighborhoods, they stand ready 24 hours a day.
  Their dedication reflects the very best of North Carolina and the 
spirit of service that strengthens our communities. North Carolina's 
Sixth District is better because of their dedication.
  We thank the Rowan County Rescue Squad, and congratulate them on 75 
years.

                          ____________________
```
