# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9720

**Measures:** H.R. 9720

```
           By Mr. COMER:
       H.R. 9720.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 17, provides Congress with the 
     exclusive jurisdiction over the District of Columbia and 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, provides Congress with 
     jurisdiction over taxation.
```
