# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9731

**Measures:** H.R. 9731

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 9731.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the United States 
     Constitution, which grants Congress the power to lay and 
     collect taxes to pay the debts and provide for the common 
     defense and general welfare of the United States, including 
     the authority to attach conditions to the receipt of federal 
     funds. This authority is further supported by Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 18, which grants Congress the power to make 
     all laws necessary and proper for carrying into execution its 
     enumerated powers, including its authority over federal 
     spending and grant programs administered by the Department of 
     Justice.
```
