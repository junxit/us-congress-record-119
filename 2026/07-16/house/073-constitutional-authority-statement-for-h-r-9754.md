# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9754

**Measures:** H.R. 9754

```
            By Mrs. McBATH:
        H.R. 9754.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18, provides Congress with the 
     power to ``make all Laws which shall be necessary and proper 
     for carrying into Execution'' the powers enumerated in 
     Article I and ``all other Powers vested by [the] Conititution 
     in the Government of the United States, or in any Department 
     or Officer thereof.''
```
