# WELCOMING REVEREND JON THOMAS

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
                     WELCOMING REVEREND JON THOMAS

  The SPEAKER. Without objection, Mr. Norcross of New Jersey was 
recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to welcome guest chaplain, 
Father Jon Thomas, priest and vicar general of the Roman Catholic 
Diocese of Camden.
  He was born and raised in south Jersey and educated at Catholic 
University right here in D.C. Father Jon was ordained in 2010 and has 
since served the parishes across south Jersey.
  As pastor in Atlantic City, he provided assistance and spiritual 
guidance to the workers in the hospitality industry in Atlantic City.
  Since 2020, he has served as pastor of Christ the King in 
Haddonfield. The year I met him, he was hosting a virtual mass for 
frontline workers who were, during the pandemic, being challenged.
  Since 2022, he has served as chaplain to the New Jersey AFL-CIO.
  In 2025, he was appointed vicar general of the Diocese of Camden, 
serving roughly 475,000 Catholics across south Jersey.
  I thank Father Thomas for the prayer, the call for unity, and to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
