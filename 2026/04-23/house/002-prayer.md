# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Aaron Derstine, Hopewell Christian Fellowship, Telford, 
Pennsylvania, offered the following prayer:
  God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, as we gather in this Chamber today, 
we recognize Your sovereign presence over all time and in the 
foundations and calling of this great Nation, the United States of 
America.
  We acknowledge our dependence upon You for salvation, for life, for 
breath, and for guidance. Remembering the words of Proverbs that the 
fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, we pray for the fear of 
the Lord and Your divine wisdom for our legislators, that You would 
grant them the strength and the understanding to walk continually in 
Your righteousness, justice, and truth.
  We remember Your exhortation to us all ``to act justly, to love 
mercy, and to walk humbly before our God.'' May this ever ring true of 
our Nation, that we all as Your servants may further Your peace and 
bring Godly light and freedom throughout this Nation and to the world.
  Lord, let Your kingdom come, let Your will be done on Earth as it is 
in Heaven for the United States of America.
  In the name of Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
