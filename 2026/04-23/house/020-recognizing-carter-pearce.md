# RECOGNIZING CARTER PEARCE

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING CARTER PEARCE

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Carter Pearce, a senior at DuBois Area High School, for winning an 
international vocabulary competition.
  Vocabulary Bowl is sponsored by Vocabulary.com and invites more than 
900,000 high school students to compete in a virtual competition, which 
lasts over a month.
  Carter, who placed first, mastered over 8,000 words. After winning in 
the fall, he competed in the spring competition and mastered all the 
featured words.
  Carter is also a member of the DuBois Area High School Vocabulary 
Quiz Bowl team and has helped the team win multiple regional 
competitions. Since joining 2 years ago, Carter has helped the team win 
four State titles.
  Carter graduates later this spring and plans to major in history. His 
goal is to become a history teacher.
  Mr. Speaker, this is a fantastic achievement, and I thank Carter for 
representing Pennsylvania so well. I wish Carter all the best in his 
future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
