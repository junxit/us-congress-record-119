# HONORING BOB FUCHS FOR HIS INDUCTION INTO THE DADE COUNTY FARM BUREAU HALL OF HONOR

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
 HONORING BOB FUCHS FOR HIS INDUCTION INTO THE DADE COUNTY FARM BUREAU 
                             HALL OF HONOR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 23, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Bob Fuchs, a 
leader in South Florida's agricultural community and a third-generation 
orchid grower from Homestead, Florida, on his induction into the Dade 
County Farm Bureau Hall of Honor.
  For more than six decades, Mr. Fuchs has dedicated his life to the 
cultivation, preservation, and advancement of orchids, helping to 
elevate Miami-Dade County as a global hub for horticultural excellence. 
From working alongside his grandfather, Mr. Fuchs developed a deep 
respect for the land and a passion that would shape his life's work.
  Mr. Fuchs went on to build that passion into a world-renowned 
enterprise, R.F. Orchids, while also serving his community as an 
educator. His contributions to the field extend far beyond our 
district. Mr. Fuchs has been credited with the discovery of new orchid 
species in Central and South America and led the American Orchid 
Society as President.
  Mr. Fuchs' achievements have earned him widespread recognition, 
including induction into the Florida Agriculture Hall of Fame and 
international honors from leading horticultural institutions.
  Mr. Fuchs' story reflects the very best of South Florida, hard work, 
resilience, and a deep-rooted connection to the land. His lifelong 
contributions have strengthened our local economy, advanced 
agricultural science, and inspired generations of growers and 
conservationists.
```
