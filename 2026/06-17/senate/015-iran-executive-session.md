# Iran (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  Iran

  Mr. President, there is a lot going on this morning, and I want to 
speak about Iran. We have been told dozens of times that this war is 
over and dozens of times we have been disappointed. Three days after 
Trump announced his so-called understanding with Iran, the American 
people still have no understanding of what it actually is because Trump 
refuses to tell us.
  Now, more than ever, the administration must come clean and let 
people actually know what is in the so-called understanding because 
when details start to leak in the press, it only adds to the confusion. 
The administration still hasn't released the text officially and 
continues to send mixed messages about what the so-called understanding 
in future negotiations entail.
  One administration official told CNN:

       People shouldn't read too much into the language of the 
     [memorandum of understanding].

  Can you believe it? There is something out there, and they say don't 
believe it, but they won't tell us what it is. The American people have 
a right to know what it is. The Congress has a right to know what it 
is.
  And they said--another administration official: ``What's more 
important than the actual document,'' he said, ``is the understandings 
we have with each other.''
  What are they? We need to know. It is just outrageous--outrageous--
that the administration is saying that whatever is in the MOU is not 
the real agreement. This is foreign policy by clown car, except it is 
dangerous.
  The Trump administration insists that this understanding will bring 
peace, but they refuse to release the text officially and then say we 
shouldn't give the memo too much weight because there are secret side 
negotiations that are more important.
  How deceptive. How nasty. How dangerous for the American people.
  I call on Trump to brief Congress, release the official text of the 
so-called understanding to the public, and disclose any secret deals to 
the American people immediately.
  If there are secret side deals, we ought to know about them. The 
American people have a right to know about them in this dangerous 
world.
  So why is the administration deliberately deceiving the American 
people by saying: Well, what is in this agreement or what at least is 
published in this agreement is not true.
  The administration is deliberately deceiving the American people 
because they are afraid it will show that Trump has capitulated and 
been taken advantage of, that Iran has gotten the better of this deal 
because Trump didn't know what his goals were when he began it and kept 
going back and forth, back and forth, back and forth about what it 
should be, what it is, what is happening.
  So I say to Donald Trump: Stop keeping the American people in the 
dark. Your actions, President Trump, leave Americans wondering, Are our 
troops safe? What are you promising to Iran? How much longer will gas 
prices continue to skyrocket?

[[Page S2870]]

  Americans want to know, What has Trump's war gotten them? What was 
this whole blunder for?
  By every measure--every measure--Trump's disastrous war has left 
America worse off. No wonder they don't want to release the real 
details. They are probably--Trump is probably embarrassed.
  Iran has more control over the Strait of Hormuz than before Trump's 
war. The Iranian regime is more radical, more extreme, more hard-line 
than before the war. Gas prices are dramatically higher than they were 
before the war.
  Americans need to know when this war will end, not in Trump's 
imagination, not in some document that they discredit but in real life.
  Donald Trump, reveal the deal, stop hiding any secret negotiations, 
and end this war now.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
