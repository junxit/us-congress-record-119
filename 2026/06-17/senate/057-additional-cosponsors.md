# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 6, S.J.Res. 56, S. 478, S. 494, S. 574, S.Res. 759, S. 877, S. 879, S. 1294, S. 1370, S. 1380, S. 1552, S. 1630, S. 1705, S. 1829, S. 1906, S. 2859, S. 3267, S. 3332, S. 3383, S. 3524, S. 3940, S. 4167, S. 4411, S. 4421, S. 4427, S. 4429, S. 4446, S. 4610, S. 4744

                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 478

  At the request of Mr. Kennedy, the name of the Senator from Wyoming 
(Ms. Lummis) was added as a cosponsor of S. 478, a bill to amend title 
38, United States Code, to prohibit the Secretary of Veterans Affairs 
from transmitting certain information to the Department of Justice for 
use by the national instant criminal background check system.


                                 S. 494

  At the request of Mr. Schmitt, the name of the Senator from Louisiana 
(Mr. Kennedy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 494, a bill to establish a 
national plan to coordinate research on epilepsy, and for other 
purposes.


                                 S. 574

  At the request of Mr. Wicker, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 574, a bill 
to amend the Animal Health Protection Act to provide compensation for 
poultry growers and layers in control areas, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 877

  At the request of Mr. Schumer, the names of the Senator from Illinois 
(Mr. Durbin) and the Senator from Florida (Mrs. Moody) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 877, a bill to require the Secretary of the Treasury 
to mint commemorative coins in recognition of the life and legacy of 
Roberto Clemente.


                                 S. 879

  At the request of Mr. Moran, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 879, a bill to expand 
medical, employment, and other benefits for individuals serving as 
family caregivers for certain veterans, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1294

  At the request of Mr. Durbin, the names of the Senator from Delaware 
(Mr. Coons) and the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Merkley) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 1294, a bill to modernize the business of selling 
firearms.


                                S. 1370

  At the request of Mr. Heinrich, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Mr. Coons) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1370, a bill to amend title 
18, United States Code, to restrict the possession of certain firearms, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 1380

  At the request of Ms. Rosen, the name of the Senator from New York

the Public Health Service Act to authorize a loan repayment program to 
encourage specialty medicine physicians to serve in rural communities

purposes.


                                S. 1552

Mexico (Mr. Heinrich) and the Senator from Mississippi (Mr. Wicker) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 1552, a bill to promote and protect from

(Mr. Kennedy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1630, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to provide more opportunities for mothers to 
succeed, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1705

Secretary of Commerce to issue standards with respect to chip security 
mechanisms for integrated circuit products, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1829

  At the request of Mr. Durbin, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Mr. Coons) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1829, a bill to combat the 
sexual exploitation of children by supporting victims and promoting

                                S. 1906

  At the request of Mr. Marshall, the name of the Senator from North 
Carolina (Mr. Budd) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1906, a bill to 
amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act with respect to the 
regulation of zootechnical animal food substances.


                                S. 2859

  At the request of Mr. Lankford, the name of the Senator from Utah 
(Mr. Lee) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2859, a bill to amend the 
Higher Education Act of 1965 to ensure campus access at public

                                S. 3267

  At the request of Ms. Collins, the name of the Senator from New

amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide for Medicare

                                S. 3332
