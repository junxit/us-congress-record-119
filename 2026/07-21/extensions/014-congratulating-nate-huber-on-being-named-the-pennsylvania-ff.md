# CONGRATULATING NATE HUBER ON BEING NAMED THE PENNSYLVANIA FFA VICE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
  CONGRATULATING NATE HUBER ON BEING NAMED THE PENNSYLVANIA FFA VICE 
                               PRESIDENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I am pleased to congratulate and recognize 
Nate Huber, who was recently installed as the 2026 to 2027 Vice 
President for the Pennsylvania FFA.
  Having emerged from a rigorous nomination and election process, Nate 
Huber was one of seven officers chosen to lead the Pennsylvania FFA in 
2026 to 2027. Nate is a recent graduate of Manheim Central High School 
and held leadership positions in the Manheim FFA.
  Nate Huber's commitment to agriculture is evident from his experience 
at a local poultry company to his introduction of agricultural 
education programs at his school. Beyond his leadership roles with the 
FFA, he also holds positions with the Pennsylvania Farm Show and 
Manheim Community Farm Show.
  Agriculture is a cornerstone of Lancaster County's economy, and Nate 
Huber has proven to be an ardent support of this legacy. I congratulate 
Nate Huber on his well-earned title as Vice President of the 
Pennsylvania FFA. As he settles into his new role and prepares for a 
bright future ahead, I wish him continued success in all his endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
