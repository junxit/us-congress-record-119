# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF THE LATE PASTOR JAMES FREDERICK "RICK" HINES, JR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
    HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF THE LATE PASTOR JAMES FREDERICK 
                          ``RICK'' HINES, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, to pay tribute to 
and honor the life and legacy of the late Pastor James Frederick 
``Rick'' Hines, Jr., a well-respected man of God, spiritual leader and 
mentor, loving son, husband and father, and I include in the Record the 
following proclamation in his honor:

       Whereas, Pastor Hines was a passionate and beloved youth 
     pastor, at Koinonia Worship Center and Village, who was 
     dedicated to spreading the Word of God;
       Whereas, Pastor Hines devoted countless hours to mentoring 
     young people and often credited the church's success with its 
     youth, practicing the teachings of one of his favorite Bible 
     verses Proverbs 22:6: ``Train up a child in the way he should 
     go and when he is old, he will not depart from it;''
       Whereas, Pastor Hines served his country dutifully as a 
     soldier in the United States Army from 1977 to 1979;
       Whereas, Pastor Hines was a lifelong resident of South 
     Florida who loved his community and worked to make it a 
     better place to live; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida honor 
     the life and legacy of the late Pastor James Frederick 
     ``Rick'' Hines, Jr. and commend him for his commitment and 
     dedications to his family, his church and our community.

                          ____________________
```
