# RECOGNIZING VALLEY GROVE MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
           RECOGNIZING VALLEY GROVE MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Valley 
Grove Missionary Baptist Church for 50 years of service to the City of 
Miami, and I include in the Record the following proclamation in its 
honor:

       Whereas, Valley Grove Missionary Baptist Church was founded 
     in 1963 and has been a leader in our community spreading the 
     Word of God;
       Whereas, Valley Grove Missionary Baptist Church's outreach 
     ministries have for decades extended a helping hand to those 
     in need, restoring hope and dignity in their darkest hour;
       Whereas, Valley Grove Missionary Baptist Church's 
     commitment to empowering people has provided the community 
     with an enriching place of worship for anyone seeking 
     spiritual edification;
       Whereas, Valley Grove Missionary Baptist Church is rich in 
     tradition and history, and has played a major role in shaping 
     and fostering relationships between the church, the community 
     and local government;
       Whereas, Valley Grove Missionary Baptist Church continues 
     to be a beacon of light, hope and faith in South Florida; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend Valley Grove Missionary Baptist Church, under the 
     leadership of Elder Johnnie L. Robinson, for 50 years of 
     service, dedication and commitment, to our community.

                          ____________________
```
