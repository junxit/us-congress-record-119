# RECOGNIZING HARRY MULLINS AS COMMUNITY SERVICE AWARD RECIPIENT

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
     RECOGNIZING HARRY MULLINS AS COMMUNITY SERVICE AWARD RECIPIENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Harry 
Mullins of Williamsburg, Pennsylvania, for his selection as the 
recipient of the Norma Jean Shanholtz Community Service Award presented 
by the Williamsburg Civic Club.
  The award honors individuals who demonstrate an exceptional 
commitment to Williamsburg Borough through dedicated community service.
  Harry has held leadership positions in the Lions Club, Williamsburg 
Municipal Authority, Williamsburg Fire Commission, and Williamsburg 
Fire Police. His service has also benefited the Williamsburg Community 
Farm Show and Williamsburg Food Bank.
  As noted by another community leader, Harry is always willing to lend 
a hand to anyone in need. His servant's heart, generosity, and 
unwavering commitment have made him a true pillar of the Williamsburg 
community.
  On behalf of constituents throughout Williamsburg Borough and the 
entire 13th Congressional District, I congratulate Harry Mullins on 
this well-deserved award and thank him for striving each day to make 
the world around him a better place.

                          ____________________
```
