# HONORING THE N'DE APACHE NATION

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
                    HONORING THE N'DE APACHE NATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor 
the remarkable leaders of the N'de Apache Nation for their unwavering 
commitment to public service and preserving a rich cultural heritage 
that continues to inspire people across our Nation.
  I am proud to recognize Chief Angel Geronimo Sanchez RedEagle, Vice 
Chief Andrew Aranda, War Chief Armando Garcia, Jr., Chief Ricardo 
Gonzalez, Vice Chief Rudy Perez, War Chief Chuck Boers, Chief Darrell 
Chavez, Chief Gabriel Carrasco, Chief Carlos Abrego, Chief Dan Lara, 
Vice Chief Salvador Aranda, Chief Moises Hernandez, and Jerry Geronimo 
Martin, Medicine Man and Shaman of the N'de Apache Nation, for their 
dedicated leadership and service.
  Through their dedication to expanding educational opportunities, 
preserving history and traditions, supporting humanitarian efforts, and 
serving their communities, they have made a lasting difference in the 
lives of countless people. Their work to educate future generations, 
support children and families, and preserve Apache history and 
traditions reflects a steadfast commitment to uplifting others and 
strengthening the communities they serve.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas's 34th Congressional 
District, I am honored to recognize the N'de Apache Nation for their 
impact of their public service on South Texas and communities across 
the Nation. Their leadership, generosity, and commitment to preserving 
their heritage deserve our deepest gratitude and recognition.

                          ____________________
```
