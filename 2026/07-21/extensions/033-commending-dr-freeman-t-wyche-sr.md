# COMMENDING DR. FREEMAN T. WYCHE, SR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                  COMMENDING DR. FREEMAN T. WYCHE, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
commend Dr. Freeman T. Wyche, Sr., an inspirational man of God and a 
revered spiritual leader in the Miami's Liberty City community, who has 
served as Senior Minister of the Liberty City Church of Christ for 
thirty-five years, and I include in the Record the following 
proclamation in his honor:

       Whereas, Dr. Wyche is dedicated to his church and to his 
     community, having chaired and served on the boards of many 
     civic and educational organizations in Miami-Dade County and 
     the State of Florida;
       Whereas, Dr. Wyche was mentored by the late renowned Dr. 
     Marshall Keeble, and has earned several degrees from the 
     University of Houston, New York Theological Seminary, and 
     Faith College;
       Whereas, Dr. Wyche provided an invaluable service to his 
     country having served as an air traffic controller and a 
     hospital administrator in the United States Air Force, and 
     achieving the rank of Master Sergeant before retiring at 
     Homestead Air Force Base in 1990;
       Whereas, Dr. Wyche's unwavering devotion to God has been 
     evidenced by his decades of ministration, teaching, 
     evangelizing, and counseling across the Nation and around the 
     world; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend Dr. Freeman T. Wyche, Sr. for his years of faithful 
     service to his family, his church, and our community.

                          ____________________
```
