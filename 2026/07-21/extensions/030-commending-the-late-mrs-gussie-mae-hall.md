# COMMENDING THE LATE MRS. GUSSIE MAE HALL

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                COMMENDING THE LATE MRS. GUSSIE MAE HALL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
commend the late Mrs. Gussie Mae Hall of Miami, Florida, an exceptional 
woman who has left an enduring legacy of humbleness, kindness and 
strength, and I include in the Record the following proclamation in her 
honor:

       Whereas, Mrs. Hall was a tireless supporter of local 
     African-American elected officials, and actively engaged in 
     the democratic process by educating members of the community 
     on the importance of the right to vote and encouraging their 
     registration and participation;
       Whereas, Mrs. Hall was a lifelong resident of South Florida 
     who was born in Homestead, Florida, formally educated in the 
     Miami-Dade County Public School system, and worked through 
     her church, He Reigns Interdenominational Ministries, 
     Incorporated, to improve our community;
       Whereas, Mrs. Hall was an outstanding citizen, wife, mother 
     and friend; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend the late Mrs. Gussie Mae Hall for her years of loving 
     and faithful service to her family, her church, and our 
     community.

                          ____________________
```
