# RECOGNIZING TOMMY NAGLE AS THE NEW PRESIDENT OF THE PENNSYLVANIA FARM BUREAU

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING TOMMY NAGLE AS THE NEW PRESIDENT OF THE PENNSYLVANIA FARM 
                                 BUREAU

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Tommy 
Nagle of Patton, Cambria County, as the new President of the 
Pennsylvania Farm Bureau.
  The 10th president in the history of the Pennsylvania Farm Bureau, 
Tommy owns and operates an incredible farm that includes Angus beef 
cattle and acres dedicated to corn and soybeans.
  Tommy has held several leadership positions within the Farm Bureau 
over the past decade. He served on the Cambria County Farm Bureau Board 
of Directors, including a stint as county president, and later spent 
three years on the Pennsylvania Farm Bureau State Board before being 
elected vice president.
  Tommy possesses a wealth of knowledge, professional experience, and 
public service leadership that will enhance his impact as a passionate 
advocate and vital resource for agricultural producers throughout 
Pennsylvania.
  On behalf of constituents throughout Cambria County and the entire 
13th Congressional District, I express best wishes to Tommy for success 
in fulfilling his strategic vision for the Pennsylvania Farm Bureau and 
thank him for his leadership and service.

                          ____________________
```
