# HONORING MISS AJA WATKINS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                       HONORING MISS AJA WATKINS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor Miss Aja Watkins, Miss Miami Gardens USA 2012, a tireless and 
enthusiastic advocate for non-violence and goodwill for all people, but 
particularly for the youth of our country.
  Miss Watkins' mission began in response to the tragic death of her 
cousin, Trayvon Martin, on February 26, 2012. She lived in the same 
home as Trayvon and was deeply affected by the tragic event, not only 
because of the bond they shared, but also because of the loss of 
someone so young and with so much undeveloped promise.
  Compelled to take action, Miss Watkins used her grief and personal 
family tragedy as inspiration to try to make a difference. As she works 
to bring urgent awareness to the crucial, critical importance of non-
violence, self-protection and preservation. Miss Watkins also hopes to 
become a voice of hope and inspiration for adolescents and young adults 
who face the reality of violence on a daily basis.
  As she completes her education, Miss Watkins actively serves as a 
role model to many young people. In hopes of capturing the crown, 
gaining greater visibility for her life saving initiative, and to 
become a mentor and role model for many young people, she works with 
other delegates and people.
  On Friday, July 13, 2013, Miss Watkins competed for the state title 
at the Miss Florida USA Pageant.
  Please join me in honoring Miss Aja Watkins for her commitment, 
dedication and service to our community.

                          ____________________
```
