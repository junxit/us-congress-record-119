# REPORT (H. REPT. 119-755 TO ACCOMPANY HOUSE RESOLUTION 1438

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1438, H.R. 6955

```
      REPORT (H. REPT. 119-755 TO ACCOMPANY HOUSE RESOLUTION 1438

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. VIRGINIA FOXX

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Ms. FOXX. Mr. Speaker, the Rules Committee report (H. Rept. 119-755) 
to accompany House Resolution 1438 should have included in its waivers 
of all points of order against consideration of H.R. 6955 a disclosure 
of the following violations:
  Clause 10 of rule XXI, which prohibits consideration of a measure if 
it has the net effect of increasing mandatory spending over the 
current, five-year, or ten-year period.
  Section 303 of the Congressional Budget Act, which prohibits 
consideration of legislation providing new budget authority, a change 
in revenues, or a change in the public debt limit, for a fiscal year 
until the budget resolution for that year has been agreed to.
```
