# RECOGNIZING SHERIFF RICKY T. BUCHANAN

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
                 RECOGNIZING SHERIFF RICKY T. BUCHANAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sheriff Ricky T. 
Buchanan on his retirement after over twenty years of exceptional and 
steadfast service to the people of McDowell County.
  Sheriff Buchanan has devoted his life to public service, 
demonstrating integrity, courage, and an unwavering commitment to 
protecting his community. He began his public service as a volunteer 
firefighter with the Pleasant Gardens Volunteer Fire Department and as 
an EMT-Intermediate with McDowell County Medical Services. He went on 
to serve in numerous roles, including Patrol Deputy, K-9 Deputy, SWAT 
Operator, and Detective with McDowell County Drug Enforcement. He 
became the first person from McDowell County to be selected as a full-
time Task Force Officer with the Drug Enforcement Administration before 
moving onto the North Carolina DMV License and Theft Bureau, where he 
advanced to Assistant District Supervisor in both the Charlotte and 
Asheville Districts.
  Over the course of his career, Sheriff Buchanan distinguished himself 
through his professionalism, dedication to justice, and ability to lead 
with strength and compassion. His leadership as an instructor preparing 
the next generation of law enforcement officers has earned the respect 
of colleagues and ensured the future of justice across North Carolina.
  Sheriff Buchanan's impact extends far beyond his official duties. He 
is a committed partner in community health, substance abuse prevention, 
and local philanthropy, offering steady leadership on numerous boards 
and initiatives that strengthen McDowell County and improve lives. 
Today, he continues to serve as president of the North Carolina 
Sheriffs' Association, a testament to the trust and admiration he has 
earned statewide.
  Mr. Speaker, Sheriff Buchanan's career reflects a rare combination of 
service, sacrifice, and heart. His legacy is one of honor, devotion, 
and deep love for the community he has protected for decades. On behalf 
of the United States House of Representatives, I extend my deepest 
gratitude to Sheriff Buchanan and offer my best wishes as he enters a 
well-earned and fulfilling retirement.

                          ____________________
```
