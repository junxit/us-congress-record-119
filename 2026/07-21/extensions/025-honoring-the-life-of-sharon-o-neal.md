# HONORING THE LIFE OF SHARON O'NEAL

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                   HONORING THE LIFE OF SHARON O'NEAL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 21, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
life of Sharon O'Neal, a cherished member of our community. Sharon was 
an extraordinary individual who lived a full and truly beautiful life.
  Sharon was a passionate and active member of the Williamsburg 
community. She was a member of both the Junior Woman's Club and the 
Woman's Club for over ten years, while serving as club president for 
two years. Sharon was also a part of the Jamestown High School Parent 
Teacher Association (PTA) where she served as president for two years. 
When she was not leading the PTA or Woman's Club, Sharon loved to watch 
NASCAR, go camping, spend time at the beach, and above all else, be 
around her loving family.
  Sharon's smile coupled with her warm personality made everyone she 
met feel as if they had known her for years. Her kindness touched 
everyone, and she leaves behind a legacy full of compassion and love. 
Sharon is survived by her devoted husband, Michael O'Neal; her mother, 
Julie Northway; her children, Jeffrey O'Neal (Abby), Stacey Hughes 
(John), and Sean O'Neal (Kimberlee); her cherished grandchildren, 
Kendal, William, Corbin, Sadie, Hazel, Bodhi, Koa, and Lucy; and her 
brother, Michael Northway (Michelle).
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in celebrating Sharon's incredible life. Our community is 
eternally grateful for everything she has done.

                          ____________________
```
