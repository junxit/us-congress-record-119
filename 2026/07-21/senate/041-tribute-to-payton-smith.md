# TRIBUTE TO PAYTON SMITH

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO PAYTON SMITH

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Payton Smith for his hard work as an intern 
in my office in Cheyenne, WY. I recognize his efforts and contributions 
to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Payton is a native of Cheyenne. He graduated in 2024 from Central 
High School. He is a senior at Rutgers University, where he is a member 
of the Rutgers Pre-Law Society and the Conservation Club. After he 
graduates with a major in political science, along with minors in 
environmental science and creative writing, he hopes to attend law 
school and eventually practice environmental law. He has demonstrated a 
strong work ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our 
office. The quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over 
the last several months.
  I want to thank Payton for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
