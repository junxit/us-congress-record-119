# TRIBUTE TO ZACH THUROW

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ZACH THUROW

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my deep appreciation to Zach for his hard work and dedication 
as an intern in my Sheridan, WY, office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Zach is from Sheridan, WY. He recently completed his freshman year at 
George Mason University where he studied political science. He is 
currently working with a Navy recruiter for enlistment in the reserves. 
After completing training, he will serve as a reservist while enrolled 
at Purdue, studying international affairs and participating in ROTC. 
His end goal is serving as an officer while on active duty with the 
U.S. Navy. He has demonstrated an exceptionally strong work ethic, 
which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of 
his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last several 
months.
  I want to thank Zach for the resolute dedication he has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of 
our team. I know he will have continued success with all of his future 
endeavors. I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
