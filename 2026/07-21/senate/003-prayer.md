# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, all power and authority belong to You. You even rule 
the raging of the sea. Guide our lawmakers as they strive to serve Your 
purposes for their lives in this generation.
  May they acknowledge You as the source of their strength, finding 
their security and the wisdom and love of Your unfolding providence.
  Make our Senators shining examples of trustworthy and responsible 
stewardship. Lord, use them to strengthen the moral fiber of our Nation 
and world.
  And, Lord, during this season of wars and rumors of wars, use us all 
as instruments of Your peace.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
