# TRIBUTE TO SAM HUFFARD

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO SAM HUFFARD

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Sam for his hard work as an intern in my 
Washington, DC, office. I recognize his efforts and contributions to my 
office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Sam was born in Washington, DC, and now lives in McLean, VA. Sam is 
currently enrolled at Washington and Lee University, where he is 
pursuing a bachelor's degree in European and American history, global 
politics, and business administration. He has demonstrated a strong 
work ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Sam for the dedication he has shown while working for 
me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I 
know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I 
wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
