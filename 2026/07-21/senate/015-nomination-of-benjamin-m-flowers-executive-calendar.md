# Nomination of Benjamin M. Flowers (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   Nomination of Benjamin M. Flowers

  Now, Mr. President, on the Benjamin Flowers nomination, the 
nomination of Benjamin Flowers ought to concern every Member of this 
body. Flowers checks virtually every box the MAGA right expects from 
its judges.
  America, this is who Trump is putting on the bench--people far away 
from what you believe.
  Flowers has fought against birthright citizenship, against abortion 
rights, and against the result of the 2020 election.
  What Flowers has fought for is also disturbing. In 2021, in Ohio, 
Flowers tried to defend a $250,000 cap on damages for a child sex abuse 
survivor--a cap the Ohio Supreme Court deemed ``arbitrary and 
unreasonable.'' Flowers is also on record as saying that a President 
can simply ignore a court's reasoning when he disagrees with it.
  Election denial, attacks on reproductive freedom, siding with abusers 
over survivors and the President over the Constitution--is this the 
kind of person Republicans want on one of the highest courts in the 
land?
  I urge my colleagues to oppose this nomination.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.

[[Page S4168]]

  

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
