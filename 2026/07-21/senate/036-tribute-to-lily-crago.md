# TRIBUTE TO LILY CRAGO

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO LILY CRAGO

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Lily for her hard work as an intern in my 
Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and contributions to my 
office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Lily is a native of Kaycee, WY. Lily is currently enrolled at the 
University of Wyoming, where she is pursuing a bachelor's degree in 
political science and agricultural communications. She has demonstrated 
a strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our 
office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over 
the last several months.
  I want to thank Lily for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
