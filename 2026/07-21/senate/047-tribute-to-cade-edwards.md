# TRIBUTE TO CADE EDWARDS

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO CADE EDWARDS

  Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Cade Edwards, an intern 
in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has done on 
behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Mr. Edwards is a 
graduate of O'Gorman High School in Sioux Falls, SD. He is currently 
attending the University of Iowa, where he studies political science 
and economics. Mr. Edwards is a dedicated and diligent individual who 
has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship experience. 
He has been a true asset to my office. I extend my sincere thanks and 
appreciation to Mr. Edwards for all of the work he has done and wish 
him continued success in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
