# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 22, 2026

**Speaking:** Cassidy, Bill (C001075) [R-LA]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 22, 2026

  Mr. CASSIDY. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate complete 
its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. on Wednesday, July 
22; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
be in a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
