# Iran (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  Iran

  Mr. SCHUMER. It is an absolute disgrace that Pete Hegseth has 
withheld casualty numbers in the Iran war from the American public. One 
of the biggest questions Pete Hegseth has to answer today is why he was 
out marketing testosterone supplements for our troops last week while 
withholding casualty numbers from the Iran war to the American people.
  We learned yesterday that the Pentagon failed to disclose that our 
troops have sustained nearly 100 injuries in the past few weeks of the 
Iran war.
  Sending our troops in harm's way is the most serious thing we can do 
as a nation, but Hegseth, Trump, and Rubio certainly don't treat it 
that way.
  Hegseth is hiding more than he is sharing these days--whether it is 
the number of wounded, the damage to our bases overseas, or the immense 
ballooning cost of this Iran war blunder.
  We need answers, but Hegseth wants to give some to the GOP alone. 
Hegseth is reportedly attending a House Republican meeting today to 
brief their Members on the war without providing Democrats the same 
access.
  There is no room for this administration's partisan pettiness when 
our servicemembers' lives are at stake. But that seems to be a 
trademark for both Hegseth and Trump.
  I am calling on Pete Hegseth, Marco Rubio, and Donald Trump to tell 
the entire Congress everything they are telling the Republicans about 
the Iran war--both Chambers, both parties.
  Everything they tell Republicans behind closed doors needs to be 
shared in public during today's testimony. But I don't need to hear 
from Hegseth to know just how poorly this war is going.
  If we truly care about our servicemembers' well-being, we ought to be 
focused on getting them out of harm's way, not on their testosterone 
levels. We will be forcing another vote this week on our War Powers 
Resolution to bring our troops home and end this war.

  Senate Republicans, don't let Trump off the hook. The Iran war isn't 
a game. It is not some trifle Trump, Hegseth, and Rubio can brush under 
the rug. Trump's reckless war is an absolute catastrophe. It must end, 
and Republicans should join us in making sure that happens. So far, 
crickets from them.
```
