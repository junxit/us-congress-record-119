# Corruption (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                               Corruption

  Mr. President, every single day, we see a new example of Trump's 
rampant corruption. Senate Democrats are putting all of Trump's scams 
under the magnifying glass in the coming weeks, and Trump's new Truth 
API stands out as one of his most corrupt grifts yet.
  Every day, there is a new example of these kinds of grifts that Trump 
gets involved in. Instead of draining the swamp like he promised, Trump 
has filled it to the brim.
  Now look at this: Trump is charging admission. For the low price of 
$100,000 a month, an investor can turn a profit off early access to 
Trump's market-moving posts.
  Trump imposes new tariffs? Subscribers can short their stocks before 
anyone else. Trump announces a new contract? Subscribers know where to 
invest. Trump shakes up the Fed? Subscribers get to reposition before 
the rest of the market blinks. Meanwhile, Trump rakes in hundreds of 
millions of dollars in subscription fees.
  Truth API is the exact definition of ``insider trading.'' It is 
textbook pay-to-play. And this is what Donald Trump spends his time 
doing. And remember, traders need only a millisecond headstart to make 
a fortune. Just 1 millisecond and they can make a lot of money, the way 
trading works these days.
  As always, Trump's corruption benefits the rich at the expense of 
working families. How many American families do you think can afford 
$100,000 a month to get a leg up in the market?
  Trump is doing what he always does: selling out America, American 
interests, and the American people to the highest bidder.
  Republicans ought to be up in arms about Truth API and the latest 
news about Trump's foreign jet. Trump's $400 million Qatari jet is the 
biggest single bribe an American President has ever accepted in our 
history. Let me repeat that. Trump's $400 million Qatari jet is the 
single biggest bribe an American President has ever accepted in 
history. I have been warning about this for months.
  And somehow, the American people still get an invoice. It will 
reportedly cost the taxpayer as much as $1 billion to retrofit Trump's 
plane for Presidential use. We just learned that his luxury jet is 
grounded until it can be ``maxed out'' to meet the same standards as 
the old Air Force One. Give me a break. Commercial airline ticket costs 
are up 27 percent over last year, and Trump wants the American people 
to pay to get his airborne palace off the ground?
  Trump ought to rename the plane ``Air Farce One.'' It is not Air 
Force One; it is ``Air Farce One.''
```
