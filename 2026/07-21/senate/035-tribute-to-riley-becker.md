# TRIBUTE TO RILEY BECKER

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO RILEY BECKER

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Riley for her hard work as an intern in my 
Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and contributions to my 
office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Riley is a native of Big Sky, MT. She is attending law school at the 
Charleston School of Law in South Carolina, where she is entering her 
final year. She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made 
her an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of her work is 
reflected in her great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Riley for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
