# TRIBUTE TO CHARLEIGH MELLISH

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO CHARLEIGH MELLISH

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Charleigh Mellish for her hard work as an 
intern in my office in Cheyenne, WY. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Charleigh is a native of Cheyenne. She graduated in 2025 from South 
High School. She is a sophomore at Hastings College, where she plays on 
the collegiate softball team. After she graduates with majors in 
philosophy and religion, along with minors in sociology and writing, 
she hopes to attend law school at the University of Wyoming. She has 
demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Charleigh for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of 
our team. I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.
```
