# TRIBUTE TO OLIVIA WENDLAND

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO OLIVIA WENDLAND

  Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Olivia Wendland, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has done 
on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. Wendland is a 
graduate of Milbank High School in Milbank, SD. She is currently 
attending Clemson University, where she studies economics. Ms. Wendland 
is a dedicated and diligent individual who has been devoted to getting 
the most out of her internship experience. She has been a true asset to 
my office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ms. Wendland 
for all of the work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.

                          ____________________
```
