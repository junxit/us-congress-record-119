# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF ARMY PRIVATE ISABELLA GONZALES

**Speaking:** Gill, Brandon (G000603) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF ARMY PRIVATE ISABELLA GONZALES

  (Mr. GILL of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GILL of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
service of Army Private Isabella Gonzalez, a 19-year-old soldier from 
Carrollton, Texas, in the district I am proud to represent.
  Private Gonzalez was killed in action last Friday during an Iranian 
strike on American forces in Jordan where she stood in harm's way to 
defend this Nation and the troops beside her. She had barely begun her 
adult life, yet she had already given everything to protect ours.
  Carrollton and all of Texas grieves tonight for one of its own. We 
also give thanks for a young woman who chose service over safety and 
duty over comfort.
  To her family, her fellow soldiers, and all who mourn her, this 
Congress stands with you, and this Nation will not forget her 
sacrifice.

                          ____________________
```
