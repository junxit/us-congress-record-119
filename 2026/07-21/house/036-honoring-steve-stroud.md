# HONORING STEVE STROUD

**Speaking:** Moore, Tim (M001236) [R-NC]

```
                         HONORING STEVE STROUD

  (Mr. MOORE of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MOORE of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of Steve Stroud, a friend and a true leader who 
transformed North Carolina.
  After attending North Carolina State University, he built one of 
North Carolina's most respected commercial real estate firms, shaping 
the growth of the Triangle region. His impact extends far beyond 
commercial development.
  As the first chairman of the Centennial Authority, he played a 
pivotal role in the building of what is now known as the Lenovo Center 
and bringing the Carolina Hurricanes to North Carolina. His 
determination helped secure our State's first major league professional 
sports franchise, boosting economic growth, tourism, and pride 
throughout all of our communities.
  He was a leader who saw possibilities where others saw obstacles. He 
believed in North Carolina, invested in our people, and dedicated his 
life to creating opportunities for others.
  From humble roots in Rutherford County to the Research Triangle, he 
left a positive, lasting impact on communities all across North 
Carolina.
  Mr. Speaker, as we mourn his passing, may we remember Steve Stroud 
for his enduring legacy of leadership, service, and love of our great 
State. Our prayers are with his family and friends.

                          ____________________
```
