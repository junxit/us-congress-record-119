# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O God, our teacher, show us the way that we may walk in it. Encourage 
us to set aside the things that vie for our allegiance that are not of 
You and let go of the issues so close to our hearts that we cannot 
imagine You would offer us any other option.
  In these days, when it seems that we receive only the bread of 
adversity and the water of affliction, we wait for You to reveal 
yourself. Do not hide yourself but allow us to see with our own eyes 
Your abiding presence, to hear with our own ears whether we turn to the 
right or to the left, Your voice speaking Your clear guidance: This is 
the way. Walk in it.
  Wait no longer to be gracious to us. Rise up and show us mercy. O 
Lord, God of justice, we wait for Your blessing on this day.
  In the strength that is only found in Your name, do we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
