# RECOGNIZING PAMELA IRVINE

**Speaking:** Cline, Ben (C001118) [R-VA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING PAMELA IRVINE

  (Mr. Cline of Virginia was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CLINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
Virginian whose life has been defined by service to others. After 45 
years of leadership, Pam Irvine is retiring as president and CEO of 
Feeding Southwest Virginia.
  When Ms. Irvine founded the organization in 1981, it began with 
little more than a vision--and even the food bank's first truck caught 
fire on the road. Undeterred, she persevered, building

[[Page H4689]]

one of the Commonwealth's leading hunger relief organizations.
  Under her leadership, Feeding Southwest Virginia has grown from 
distributing 1 million pounds of food annually to more than 20 million 
pounds, serving 26 counties and 9 cities, and helping more than 127,000 
neighbors each month.
  Guided by compassion, faith, and an unwavering belief that no one 
should go hungry, Pam Irvine has transformed the fight against hunger 
throughout southwest Virginia.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Virginia's Sixth Congressional District, I 
thank Pamela Irvine for her extraordinary service, congratulate her on 
an exceptional career, and wish her every happiness in her well-
deserved retirement.


                   Recognizing Luray Fire Department

  Mr. CLINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Luray Fire 
Department of Luray, Virginia, as it marks an extraordinary milestone: 
125 years of dedicated service to the people of Luray and Page County.
  Founded in 1901 and chartered in 1933, the Luray Fire Department has 
stood as a proud, all-volunteer, and nonprofit organization with the 
goal of protecting the lives and property of so many for over a 
century. From its early days when the department attended its first 
State Firefighters Convention in Staunton and a year later sent its 
first reel team to a State contest in Portsmouth, the department has 
remained the cornerstone of community safety and pride.
  With a membership of nearly 100 volunteers, the LFD has celebrated 
many milestones and accomplishments since then, and it continues to 
strengthen community ties through annual traditions such as the concert 
and ceremony held on September 11 as well as its raffle and fundraiser 
held on September 12.
  Today, we honor the men and women, past and present, whose selfless 
service reflects the very best of our Commonwealth. Join me in 
celebrating the strong and brave Luray Fire Department on 125 years of 
steadfast commitment to not only their community but to the Nation.


                    providing certainty for america

  Mr. CLINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the passage of the 
concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 2027 out of the 
Budget Committee last week.
  Congress' primary responsibility is to defend the American people and 
preserve the strength of our Nation, and this resolution reflects that 
duty. It invests in our warfighters; supports our ranchers, farmers, 
and producers; and secures the integrity of our voting systems with the 
SAVE America Act.
  For rural districts like Virginia's Sixth, this resolution delivers 
real support and stability for our farmers. It empowers our national 
defense, stands behind our warfighters, secures our food supply, and 
ensures American elections are decided only by American citizens.
  This budget reflects a commitment to responsible governance while 
restoring the faith and confidence of voters in our election system.
  Mr. Speaker, this resolution reflects a vision of a stronger, safer, 
and more prosperous America. It demonstrates our commitment to 
defending our Nation, supporting the American people, and governing 
with responsibility. I urge my colleagues to support this resolution 
this week and join us in advancing policies that strengthen our country 
for generations to come.
```
