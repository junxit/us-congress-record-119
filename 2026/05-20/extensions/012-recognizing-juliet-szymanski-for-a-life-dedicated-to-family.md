# RECOGNIZING JULIET SZYMANSKI FOR A LIFE DEDICATED TO FAMILY AND COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
    RECOGNIZING JULIET SZYMANSKI FOR A LIFE DEDICATED TO FAMILY AND 
                               COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 20, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mrs. Juliet 
Szymanski for a lifetime of service, devotion, and civic engagement. 
She is an example to all of what it means to live the American Dream 
and to dedicate one's life to building up those around you.
  When Juliet's daughter, Stephanie, was diagnosed with Lyme disease at 
a young age, Juliet ensured that her family met that challenge head-
on--not only through treatment and support for her daughter, but 
through a commitment to helping countless others facing the same 
struggle.
  Juliet has been a steadfast advocate in the fight against Lyme 
disease, working tirelessly to increase public awareness and leading 
efforts in prevention, treatment, and research advocacy. Her compassion 
and determination have helped shine a light on an illness that impacts 
families across Western New York and our Nation.
  In addition to her advocacy work, Juliet has been a dedicated member 
of the Erie County Republican Committee, serving for nearly 20 years as 
a committeewoman and supporting countless candidates, campaigns, and 
elected officials. Her commitment to public service and civic 
participation has strengthened her community and inspired others to get 
involved. Since 2008, she has served as Secretary to Erie County Board 
of Elections Commissioner Ralph Mohr and has been a tireless public 
servant administering elections across Erie County.
  For 250 years, the United States of America has thrived because of 
the freedom, self-determination, and civic participation enshrined in 
our democratic values. Our representative democracy depends upon 
citizens willing to serve their neighbors and invest in their 
communities. Juliet Szymanski's life--devoted to her family, her work, 
and her community--embodies those values in action.
  We all have a responsibility to leave our communities better than we 
found them, and Juliet has done exactly that. I know that her

[[Page E472]]

husband, Ken, and daughters, Natalie and Stephanie, along with her son-
in-law, Dominic, are very proud of her many accomplishments.
  I am proud to join the Erie County Federation of Republican Women in 
recognizing Juliet Szymanski for her significant contributions to 
Western New York. I ask that this body join me in honoring an all-
American mother, advocate, and community leader--Juliet Szymanski.

                          ____________________
```
