# RECOGNIZING 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BOROUGH OF WATCHUNG IN SOMERSET COUNTY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
 RECOGNIZING 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BOROUGH OF WATCHUNG IN SOMERSET 
                                 COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 20, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Borough of 
Watchung in Somerset County as it celebrates its 100th anniversary, an 
extraordinary milestone marking a century of community, history, and 
civic pride.
  Established in 1926 and formally incorporated by an act of the New 
Jersey Legislature, Watchung began its journey in self-governance when 
its first Borough Council convened on May 24, 1926. From that moment, 
the Borough has fostered a proud tradition of civic leadership rooted 
in dedication to public service and community betterment.
  Nestled within the scenic Watchung Mountains, the Borough's natural 
landscape has long defined its character. Watchung Lake and Best Lake, 
beloved treasures of the community, serve not only as serene landmarks 
but also as environmental, recreational, and historical assets 
cherished by residents and visitors alike.
  The very name Watchung, derived from the Lenni Lenape language 
meaning ``high hills,'' reflects the Borough's roots and the geography 
that has shaped its identity for centuries. Settled in the early 
eighteenth century, the area played a notable role in the American

[[Page E470]]

Revolution. In 1777, General George Washington used the ridge as a 
vantage point from which to monitor British troop movements, an 
enduring reminder of Watchung's place in our Nation's early history.
  Throughout the past century, Watchung has been distinguished by a 
remarkable tradition of volunteerism. Its residents have consistently 
embodied the values of civic engagement, service, and community spirit. 
The Borough's commitment to preserving its heritage through 
institutions such as the Texier House Museum and the dedicated efforts 
of the Watchung Historical Committee have ensured that its rich past 
continues to inform its vibrant present.
  As Watchung reaches its Centennial Jubilee in 2026, we honor a 
community that has grown with resilience, preserved its natural beauty, 
and upheld a strong civic identity. This milestone stands as a 
testament to generations of residents whose contributions have shaped 
the Borough's legacy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commemorating the 
Watchung Centennial Jubilee, recognizing 100 years of history, 
community, and shared purpose. We extend our warmest congratulations to 
the Borough of Watchung and its residents on this momentous occasion 
and celebrate the strength and spirit that define this exceptional 
community.
```
