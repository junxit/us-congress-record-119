# RECOGNIZING THE EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICE PROFESSIONALS OF MEDFLEET IN HONOR OF NATIONAL EMS WEEK

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
RECOGNIZING THE EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICE PROFESSIONALS OF MEDFLEET IN 
                       HONOR OF NATIONAL EMS WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 20, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of National 
EMS Week and to honor the extraordinary emergency medical professionals 
of MedFleet for their exceptional service to the residents of Pasco, 
Hernando, and Citrus Counties and communities throughout the Tampa Bay 
region. Every day, the dedicated paramedics, EMTs, dispatchers, nurses, 
and support personnel of MedFleet answer the call when lives are on the 
line. Whether responding to medical emergencies, transporting 
critically ill patients, supporting hospitals and healthcare 
facilities, or assisting during times of crisis and natural disaster, 
these professionals deliver compassionate, high-quality patient care 
with remarkable skill and professionalism.
  Across Florida's Nature Coast and the broader Tampa Bay area, 
MedFleet has earned a reputation for excellence through its unwavering 
commitment to patient-centered care, rapid emergency response, and 
clinical professionalism, Their teams routinely operate under intense 
pressure and in unpredictable circumstances, yet they continue to 
demonstrate extraordinary composure, expertise, and compassion for 
those they serve. The work performed by MedFleet personnel often 
represents the critical link between emergency scenes and lifesaving 
medical treatment. Their efforts save lives, comfort families during 
moments of fear and uncertainty, and strengthen the overall health and 
safety of our communities. From emergency response and interfacility 
transport to disaster preparedness and coordinated healthcare support, 
MedFleet personnel play an indispensable role in our regional 
healthcare system. During National EMS Week, we are reminded that 
behind every ambulance response is a team of highly trained 
professionals willing to place the wellbeing of others ahead of their 
own comfort and safety. Their dedication reflects the very best of 
public service and embodies the values of compassion, courage, and 
selflessness.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in expressing our sincere gratitude to 
the men and women of MedFleet and all emergency medical service 
professionals serving Florida's Tampa Bay region. Their tireless 
efforts, clinical excellence, and steadfast commitment to caring for 
others deserve our deepest appreciation and recognition. May God bless 
the dedicated professionals of MedFleet and all EMS personnel who 
faithfully serve our communities each and every day.

                          ____________________
```
