# RECOGNIZING BRIS AVROHOM DAY OF UNITY AND TRUST

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
            RECOGNIZING BRIS AVROHOM DAY OF UNITY AND TRUST

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 20, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Bris Avrohom Day of 
Unity and Trust, which celebrates the organization's enduring mission 
of religious freedom, remembrance, and unity. Bris Avrohom, inspired by 
the Lubavitcher Rebbe, has spent more than forty-seven years helping 
immigrants integrate into American society while strengthening Jewish 
religious, humanitarian, and educational life.
  A Sefer Torah was written in Jerusalem, Israel, to unite everyone 
touched by Bris Avrohom with the partners whose support sustains acts 
of kindness, faith, and perseverance. In solidarity with Bris Avrohom, 
we remember the Holocaust, when the Nazi regime murdered six million 
Jews and countless other innocent victims, and we stand together 
against antisemitism and hatred in all forms.

[[Page E475]]

  We also recall the tragic events of October 7, 2023, when Hamas 
committed a horrific terrorist attack against the Jewish people of 
Israel, marking the deadliest day for the Jewish community since the 
Holocaust.
  Today, we honor the mission of Bris Avrohom, reaffirm our commitment 
to religious freedom, remembrance, and unity, and dedicate the Sefer 
Torah as a living symbol of faith and resilience.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commemorating Bris 
Avrohom Day of Unity and Trust and recognizing our Nation's role as a 
beacon of religious freedom and tolerance.
```
