# COMMEMORATING 80TH ANNUAL MEMORIAL DAY CEREMONY IN BOYLE HEIGHTS

**Speaking:** Gomez, Jimmy (G000585) [D-CA]

```
    COMMEMORATING 80TH ANNUAL MEMORIAL DAY CEREMONY IN BOYLE HEIGHTS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIMMY GOMEZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 20, 2026

  Mr. GOMEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 80th 
Anniversary of the Memorial Day Ceremony at the Mexican-American All 
Wars Memorial located in Boyle Heights.
  For the past 80 years, this Ceremony has stood as a sacred tradition 
of remembrance, unity, and gratitude. What began in the aftermath of 
World War II, has become a living testament to the enduring spirit of a 
community that honors the sacrifice of its servicemen and women with 
dignity and pride. Generation after generation, families, veterans, 
neighbors, and civic leaders have gathered here to reflect on the cost 
of freedom and to ensure that those who gave their lives in service to 
our Nation are never forgotten. This milestone anniversary is not only 
a celebration of history, but a reaffirmation of the values of courage, 
service, and community that define Boyle Heights.
  As we commemorate the 80th anniversary of this cherished ceremony, we 
pay tribute to the veterans whose bravery shaped our Nation and to the 
organizers and community members who have preserved this meaningful 
tradition for decades. Memorial Day reminds us that remembrance is a 
collective responsibility; by honoring the past, we must also remind 
future generations to uphold the ideals of sacrifice, compassion, and 
unity.
  May this 80th anniversary serve as both a solemn remembrance and a 
proud celebration of a community whose patriotism and resilience 
continue to shine through the decades.

                          ____________________
```
