# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy God, Creator and Sustainer of life, thank You for the inward 
prompting that draws us closer to You.
  Bless our Senators today. Make them leaders who are committed, 
competent, and courageous. Lord, empower them to live lives that are 
above reproach. Give them self-control, gentleness, and humility. 
Remind them that You do not ask for success but faithfulness. Help them 
to set exemplary standards in their speech, conduct, and purity. Give 
them a productive persistence that will keep them from weariness as 
they fulfill Your purposes. Bless their families, and surround their 
loved ones with Your favor.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
