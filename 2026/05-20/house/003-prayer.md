# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Take up residence in our hearts, O God. Move out of Your way the 
things that are not of You: anger, anxiety, fear, and frustration. By 
the power of Your holy spirit and out of Your glorious riches, inspire 
us to desire only You and strengthen us to remain faithful to You 
alone.
  May we establish our decisions and demeanor in Your truth, rooting 
ourselves in Your abiding love, that we would grow in faith in Your 
sovereign will for these days. May we recognize among us those in whom 
You dwell and work together with them for the common good through our 
common faith in You.
  Be present in and among us this day, and grant us the wisdom to grasp 
how wide and long and high and deep is Your love, surpassing all our 
knowledge and filling us with Your fullness.
  In You we lack nothing. With You we are empowered to do all things. 
Abiding in You and You in us, You are the only thing to which our 
hearts should cling.
  In Your strong and sacred name, we offer our prayers and ourselves.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
