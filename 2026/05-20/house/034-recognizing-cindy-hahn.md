# RECOGNIZING CINDY HAHN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                              {time}  1740
                         RECOGNIZING CINDY HAHN

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Cindy 
Hahn, executive director of C-NET Centre County.
  C-NET, a local not-for-profit television and media organization, 
broadcasts more than 500 government and educational programs annually.
  After 20 years of service, Cindy will retire on May 31. She has been 
fundamental in connecting community members with public information.
  Cindy joined C-NET in 2006 and quickly expanded their media 
offerings, increasing broadcasting capabilities to Bellefonte, State 
College, and the surrounding townships.
  Over the years, she has seen C-NET's internal infrastructure develop. 
From VHS to live streaming, the group continues to be a resource for 
thousands of residents.
  As C-NET's longest standing employee, Cindy has kept residents 
informed and provided transparency to the region's current affairs.
  Mr. Speaker, I applaud Cindy's commitment to connecting the public 
with local officials.
  She is a mentor to so many, including various Penn State 
communications students who have interned at C-NET.
  I congratulate Cindy on this milestone and thank her for her years of 
service.

                          ____________________
```
