# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SARAH GHIORSE

**Speaking:** Leger Fernandez, Teresa (L000273) [D-NM]

```
             HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SARAH GHIORSE

  (Ms. LEGER FERNANDEZ asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. LEGER FERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of an 
extraordinary woman from my beautiful New Mexico, Sarah Ghiorse.
  Her family and all who knew her were touched by her life and grieve 
her passing from cancer at the untimely age of 52.
  Sarah was rooted in love, love of her family, her colleagues, her 
communities, and our beautiful State.
  She led the amazing organization NewMexicoWomen.org with her fierce 
advocacy for justice. Sarah believed that the work for women and girls 
in these difficult political times must be rooted in and convey 
connection to love, that in building together in community, we all have 
a role and belong to this ecosystem.
  Sarah was the heart of her organization, moving with ease, grace, and 
integrity between multiple worlds, all connected by her commitment.
  She was as comfortable in staff and donor meetings as she was at 
ceremonies or in the streets for marches, parades, or protests.
  At the same time, she was a dedicated mother, a daughter, a sister, a 
partner, and a friend. Her light, love, and powerful legacy will 
continue and live on in those who loved and cared for her.
  Rest in power, Sarah.

                          ____________________
```
