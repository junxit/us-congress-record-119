# HONORING MAJOR JAMES MALLO

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                       HONORING MAJOR JAMES MALLO

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 30, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Major James Mallo, 
an exemplary community member whose leadership and service have made a 
lasting impact on children and families in our community. Through his 
dedicated service on the Board of Directors at Youth and Family 
Alternatives (YFA) for 10 years, James consistently demonstrates a deep 
commitment to strengthening families and improving outcomes for 
children. James brings thoughtful leadership, integrity, and compassion 
to his role on the Board. He is not only strategic in his guidance, but 
also genuinely invested in YFA's mission, ensuring the organization 
remains responsive, effective, and centered on the needs of those it 
serves. His insight and steady leadership help drive meaningful 
decisions that directly improve the lives of children and families. His 
role on the RAP River Run Event Committee has strengthened community 
support and advocacy, while his involvement with the Rotary Club of 
Trinity brings him and fellow Rotarians to the RAP House Youth Shelter 
each month, where they cook for children and teens in our care. The 
time spent with these youth is impactful for both the kids and the 
volunteers, who generously donate both their time and resources.
  Beyond his formal role on YFA's Board, James is known for 
consistently lending a helping hand across our entire community. He 
steps forward whenever there is a need--offering his time, expertise, 
or support--embodying the true spirit of community service. His 
willingness to serve others without hesitation inspires those around 
him and strengthens the organizations and people he supports.
  James's leadership, generosity, and unwavering dedication make him a 
truly deserving nominee. His contributions reflect the very best of 
community involvement and have created a lasting, positive ripple 
effect throughout our community. Always ready for a challenge, James is 
an invaluable member of the YFA village, and I am profoundly grateful 
for his service. For his unwavering commitment, inspiring leadership, 
and extraordinary service to our community. I am honored to recognize 
him as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District.

                          ____________________
```
