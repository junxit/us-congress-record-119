# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Shepherd of our souls, be enthroned above the angels, and let Your 
loving providence give wisdom to our lawmakers. Empower them to turn 
again to You for guidance as You lead them on the path of Your 
choosing. Lord, let Your smile of approval for their refusal to leave 
the path of integrity fill their hearts with joy. Listen to their 
prayers, and protect them with Your powerful salvation. May the words 
of their mouths and the meditations of their hearts receive Your divine 
approbation.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
