# Appropriations (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                             Appropriations

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, yesterday I was in the Oval Office with 
the President of the United States, and President Trump signed into law 
five appropriations bills. And as a result, 96 percent of the Federal 
Government is funded through the end of the fiscal year. That is good 
news for the American people.
  We accomplished all of this through the normal legislative process--a 
bill going through the House, through the Senate, and then to the White 
House. This is how Congress is supposed to work. The Appropriations 
Committee, led by Senator Susan Collins of Maine, made it happen here 
in the Senate. This is a welcome change from how things were done in 
the past during the Democrat administration.
  Today, Republicans work to continue to fully fund the last 4 percent 
of government, and that is the Department of Homeland Security. The 
Department of Homeland Security is charged with one of the Federal 
Government's most basic duties, and that is to keep Americans safe. The 
Department of Homeland Security enforces our Nation's immigration laws; 
it oversees TSA agents; it directs the Federal Emergency Management 
Agency; it commands the U.S. Coast Guard; and it protects our Nation 
from cyber attacks.
  Republicans are ready to fully fund the Department of Homeland 
Security. We are ready to do it through the end of the fiscal year. And 
we have been ready to do it for weeks. Democrats are holding the 
funding hostage. Democrats have an unreasonable and growing list of 
demands. Democrats' demands would undermine law enforcement, and 
Democrats' demands would make our Nation less safe.
  One of the Democrats' most dangerous demands is for local police to 
ignore Federal immigration law. Democrats in Congress and Democrats who 
run big cities do not want the law enforced. They have proven it by 
their words and by their actions.
  For years, the Democrats have been the party of ``defund the 
police.'' Democrats have now taken it a step further. Now, they are the 
party of defund American security. The debate is about much more than 
Federal funding. It is about whether Federal law is enforced or 
ignored. It is about whether our communities are safe or not. Seventy-
eight percent of Americans want to deport illegal immigrant criminals. 
Americans support more cooperation between local and Federal officials. 
This applies specifically to deporting illegal immigrant criminals.
  Americans also say that local jails should transfer criminal illegal 
immigrants to Federal officials. Let me point out that in today's 
Washington Post--and I have a copy of it right here, of their editorial 
page--the Washington Post this morning, February 4, 2026:

       Congress has its work cut out on DHS funding.

  They start a paragraph that says:

       Something else Schumer says he wants is an end to roving 
     patrols.

  Senator Schumer just spoke here on the floor of the U.S. Senate. The 
editorial said Democrat-run cities should ``hand over dangerous illegal 
immigrants in their jails'' instead of ``hiding behind sanctuary 
policies.''
  The Washington Post this morning is advising the Democrats and, 
specifically, Senator Schumer by name that we need more cooperation, 
not less, which is what we are getting from Democrat-run cities in big 
Democrat bastions and promoted by each and every one of the Democrats 
on the floor of the U.S. Senate. We need more cooperation.
  We have gotten to this place because of the law, specifically the 
Laken Riley law, which, Mr. President, you were one of the cosponsors 
and advocates and worked so hard on. Because of that law--the law that 
Senator Katie Britt wrote, you worked with, we got passed, signed into 
law--DHS has detained an additional 20,000 illegal immigrants who 
otherwise would have been released back onto the streets of our 
communities.
  Clearly, when local officials cooperate with Federal officers, the 
American people are safer. By contrast, Senate Democrats--and we are 
going to hear it again today--want to enact sanctuary city policies on 
a national level. People in my home State and yours hate the idea of 
sanctuary cities. American cities--already, over a dozen Democrat-

[[Page S478]]

run States embrace these sanctuary city policies.
  Let me tell you what the liberal Governor of New York now wants to 
do. She wants to make cooperating and coordinating with ICE illegal for 
the State of New York. We have seen who got elected mayor of New York 
City. Now, the Governor is following right behind him.

  And in Minnesota, the liberal Governor, the liberal mayor of 
Minneapolis, and the liberal attorney general openly encourage citizens 
to obstruct Federal law. That is what they are doing in liberal 
bastions around the country. If Democrats have their way, every city in 
America would be a sanctuary city and every city in America would be 
less safe.
  Here is why sanctuary cities are so dangerous: They are dangerous for 
the public, and they are dangerous for law enforcement. In sanctuary 
cities, local officials refuse to transfer illegal immigrants, even 
those convicted of crimes, to Federal law enforcement. These convicted 
criminals are often released from custody back onto the streets in our 
communities. That means Federal agents are forced to try to find these 
convicted criminals in the community, on the streets, instead of being 
able to secure and pick them up in the jails. It seems a lot safer way 
to do it for me.
  Look at what is happening in New York City. Since last year, New York 
has released 7,000 illegal immigrant criminals instead of cooperating 
with Federal officials. Last month, New York knowingly released a 
convicted sex offender. They did this instead of transferring him into 
Federal custody, which is where he belongs. That is a criminal who has 
already been deported before and got back into the country.
  Remember Joe Biden, 10,000 a day coming into the country--criminals, 
drug dealers, illegal immigrants who were part of criminal cartels--we 
had it all. Well, Federal officials were prepared to arrest him in New 
York. No, the local Democrats released him, let him blend in. Go away.
  That is what sanctuary city policies do. They put criminals back on 
the streets in our cities and make our cities less safe. Not a single 
American is safer because of the sanctuary city policies that the 
Democrats are promoting. When liberal cities provide sanctuary for 
criminals, they deny the safety of law-abiding citizens.
  If immigration laws are ignored nationwide, almost everyone who 
enters the United States illegally would remain here for the rest of 
their lives, free to do whatever they wanted, commit crimes, and not 
have to worry about ever being deported. Preventing cooperation between 
State and Federal officials would make the chaos of Joe Biden's wide-
open borders permanent. The American people don't want that.
  Let me tell you how this applies to the debate over the funding bill 
today for the Department of Homeland Security. The Democrats' list of 
demands is a liberal wish list. Republicans are not going to accept 
changes that make our communities less safe. Personally, I want to end 
sanctuary cities and sanctuary city policies completely and so do many 
of my Republican colleagues.
  Senator Lindsey Graham of South Carolina has introduced legislation 
to stop State and local officials from interfering with Federal 
immigration enforcement. It is a simple, commonsense concept: law 
enforcement officers should help law enforcement officers keep our 
communities safe.
  The safest cities in America are those that cooperate with 
Immigration and Customs Enforcement. There is a clear cause-and-effect 
relationship. Congress should encourage more cooperation, do it 
nationwide, not less, which is what the Democrats want.
  Republicans welcome this debate about immigration enforcement. 
Republicans are for cooperation. Democrats--they are the party of 
chaos. Republicans are for enforcing the law. Democrats are for 
sanctuary cities and once again side with illegal immigrants while 
Republicans stand on the side of making America more safe.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Connecticut.
```
