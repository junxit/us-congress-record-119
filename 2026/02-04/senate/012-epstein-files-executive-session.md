# Epstein Files (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Epstein Files

  Mr. President, now on Epstein, yesterday, Donald Trump said that the 
Nation should finally just move on with the Epstein files.
  Here is what he said: ``I think it's really time,'' Trump said, ``for 
the country to get on to something else, now that nothing came out 
about me.''
  Of course, it is only about Donald Trump, as usual.
  But let me just say this to Donald Trump. You are wrong again, Mr. 
President. America will not move on from the Epstein files until 
America gets the whole truth--the full truth--about the Epstein files. 
America will not move on until Donald Trump does what he promised--full 
transparency--and we haven't come close to that yet.
  Millions upon millions of documents remain shrouded in darkness. We 
have no idea what is left in them, what is in there about Donald Trump. 
Did the Justice Department particularly hide and not reveal documents 
that might be damaging to Trump?
  Instead of prioritizing transparency and complying with the law, the 
Department of Justice, to this day, is still obsessed with prioritizing 
protecting Donald Trump. It is pretty obvious.
  Trump chose people in the Justice Department to be his own attack 
dogs, his own puppets. They don't have any independence, and so we 
don't trust them. The American people don't trust them.
  So, President Trump, no, the Nation will not ``get on to something 
else'' until you do what you promised and release all the Epstein 
files, so the whole truth--the whole truth--can come out.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
