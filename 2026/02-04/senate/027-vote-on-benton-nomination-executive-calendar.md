# Vote on Benton Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                       Vote on Benton Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Benton nomination?
  Mrs. BLACKBURN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.

Senator from Idaho (Mr. Crapo) and the Senator from Kentucky (Mr. 
McConnell).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from New York (Mrs. 
Gillibrand) is necessarily absent.
```
