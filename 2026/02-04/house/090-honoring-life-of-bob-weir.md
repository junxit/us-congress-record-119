# HONORING LIFE OF BOB WEIR

**Speaking:** Huffman, Jared (H001068) [D-CA]

```
                       HONORING LIFE OF BOB WEIR

  (Mr. HUFFMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HUFFMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the extraordinary life and 
legacy of my constituent and the Rock and Roll Hall of Famer, Bob Weir. 
Bob was more than a musical icon. He was a neighbor, a friend, and a 
beloved part of our North Bay community.

[[Page H2016]]

  For decades, Bob made his home in Marin. He raised his family there, 
opened his doors to other artists, and helped build the vibrant local 
music vibe that so many of us cherish. He played intimate shows at Mill 
Valley's Sweetwater and took the stage at the Mountain Theater atop 
Mount Tamalpais.
  Bob's music, his generosity, and unmistakable creative spirit helped 
define the identity of the North Coast. His legacy will endure every 
time a kid picks up a guitar, every time the lights come up at the 
Sweetwater, and every time music echoes across Mount Tam.
  We are grateful that he made our community part of his life. We send 
our love to his family, friends, and all my fellow Dead Heads across 
the world.
  Mr. Speaker, let the record reflect, I delivered these remarks 
barefoot in honor of Bobby Weir. I think he would appreciate that.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kennedy of Utah). Members are reminded 
to observe proper decorum on the floor.

                          ____________________
```
