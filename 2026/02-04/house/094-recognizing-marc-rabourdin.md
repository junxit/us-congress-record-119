# RECOGNIZING MARC RABOURDIN

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MARC RABOURDIN

  (Mr. VINDMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor an exceptional 
member of my staff who is moving on to his next chapter.
  Marc Rabourdin, my deputy district director, has served the people of 
Virginia's Seventh Congressional District with distinction for more 
than 4 years.
  Marc began his service to the Seventh Congressional District working 
for my dear friend and predecessor, former Congresswoman and now 
Governor Abigail Spanberger.
  He has continued to deliver results for central and Northern Virginia 
on my team with the same professionalism and care. From day one, his 
dedication has made a real difference in the lives of the people we 
serve. Always calm, steady, and deeply knowledgeable, Marc has been a 
trusted presence and an invaluable resource to our constituents and our 
team.
  A true public servant, Marc earned his nickname, the mayor, in our 
office because he knows the communities, local leaders, and needs of 
the Seventh District better than just about anyone.
  Mr. Speaker, while we will miss him, we are grateful for his service 
to Virginia's Seventh Congressional District and wish him every success 
in his next endeavor serving Virginians.

                          ____________________
```
