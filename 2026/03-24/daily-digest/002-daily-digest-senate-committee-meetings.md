# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
LOW-COST MUNITIONS
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded open and closed 
hearings to examine low-cost munitions, after receiving testimony from 
Lieutenant General Steven P. Whitney, USSF, Director of Force 
Structure, Resources and Assessment, and Colonel Adam R. Boyd, USAF, 
Senior Strategist for the Warfighting Analysis Division, both of J8, 
The Joint Staff, Vice Admiral Elizabeth Okano, USN, Principal Military 
Deputy Assistant Secretary of the Navy for Research, Development and 
Acquisition, United States Navy, Lieutenant General Frank J. Lozano, 
USA, Portfolio Acquisition Executive Fires, United States Army, 
Lieutenant General Steven M. Marks, USA, Director, Defense Autonomous 
Warfare Group, and Brigadier General Robert P. Lyons III, USAF, 
Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Weapons and Director of the 
Armament Directorate, Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, United 
States Air Force Materiel Command, all of the Department of Defense.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Armed Services: Committee ordered favorably reported 1,983 
military nominations in the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps.
SUBCOMMITTEE ON CYBERSECURITY
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Cybersecurity concluded 
open and closed hearings to examine enterprise security and information 
technology operations of Department of Defense networks and systems, 
after receiving testimony from Kirsten A. Davies, Chief Information 
Officer, and Lieutenant General Paul T. Stanton, USA, Director, Defense 
Information Systems Agency/Commander, Cyber Defense Command, both of 
the Department of Defense.
ARMS CONTROL
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine arms control and transforming international security functions 
at the State Department, after receiving testimony from Thomas G.

[[Page D325]]

DiNanno, Under Secretary of State for Arms Control and International 
Security.
ARCTIC FROST
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Federal Courts, Oversight, 
Agency Action, and Federal Rights concluded a hearing to examine Arctic 
Frost, after receiving testimony from Christopher J. O'Leary, former 
Special Agent, Federal Bureau of Investigation, Department of Justice, 
New York, New York; Will Chamberlain, The Article III Project, Raleigh, 
North Carolina; and Margot Juliette Cleveland, The Federalist, 
Dearborn, Michigan.
```
