# DIRECTING THE REMOVAL OF UNITED STATES ARMED FORCES FROM HOSTILITIES WITHIN OR AGAINST THE ISLAMIC REPUBLIC OF IRAN THAT HAVE NOT BEEN AUTHORIZED BY CONGRESS--Motion to Discharge

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 116, H.R. 7147

```
 DIRECTING THE REMOVAL OF UNITED STATES ARMED FORCES FROM HOSTILITIES 
   WITHIN OR AGAINST THE ISLAMIC REPUBLIC OF IRAN THAT HAVE NOT BEEN 
              AUTHORIZED BY CONGRESS--Motion to Discharge

  Mr. MURPHY. Mr. President, pursuant to section 601(b) of the 
International Security Assistance and Arms Export Control Act, I move 
to discharge the Committee on Foreign Relations from further 
consideration of S.J. Res. 116, to direct the removal of the United 
States Armed Forces from hostilities within or against the Islamic 
Republic of Iran that have not been authorized by Congress.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to discharge S.J. Res. 116, a joint resolution to 
     direct the removal of United States Armed Forces from 
     hostilities within or against the Islamic Republic of Iran 
     that have not been authorized by Congress.


                             Vote on Motion

  Mr. MURPHY. Mr. President, I know of no further debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there further debate?
  Hearing none, the question is on agreeing to the motion to discharge.
  Mr. MURPHY. I would ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  The result was announced--yeas 47, nays 53, as follows:
```
