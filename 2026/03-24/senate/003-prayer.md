# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Oh, God, our refuge and strength, You have called our Senators to 
this place and time. May they be mindful of the responsibility to be 
faithful stewards of their calling. Protect them in the hour of 
temptation so that they will exercise self-control and glorify You. 
Lord, use their talents, skills, and perseverance to strengthen our 
Nation and bless the people of our world.
  Infuse them with such a spirit of gratitude that they will offer 
thanks to You by living according to Your will. Lord, remind them that 
You are with them and will guide them. And, Lord, let peace on Earth 
begin with us.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
