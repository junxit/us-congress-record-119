# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 25, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 1383

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 25, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
on Wednesday, March 25; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume the House 
message with respect to S. 1383.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
