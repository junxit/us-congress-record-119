# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

```
                    ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. If the Senator-designate will now present 
himself at the desk, the Chair will administer the oath of office.
  The Senator-designate, Alan Armstrong, escorted by Mr. Lankford, 
advanced to the desk of the President pro tempore; and he subscribed to 
the oath in the Official Oath Book.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Congratulations.
  (Applause, Senators rising.)
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Banks). The majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
