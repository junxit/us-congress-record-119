# CERTIFICATE OF APPOINTMENT

```
                       CERTIFICATE OF APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair lays before the Senate a 
certificate of appointment to fill the vacancy created by the 
resignation of former Senator Markwayne Mullin of Oklahoma. The 
certificate, the Chair is advised, is in the form suggested by the 
Senate.

[[Page S1568]]

  If there be no objection, the reading of the certificate will be 
waived and it will be printed in full in the Record.
  There being no objection, the reading of the certificate will be 
waived and it will be printed in full in the Record, as follows:

                           State of Oklahoma


                       certificate of appointment

     To the President of the Senate of the United States:
       This is to certify that, pursuant to the power vested in me 
     by the Constitution of the United States and the laws of the 
     State of Oklahoma, I, J. Kevin Stitt, the governor of said 
     State, do hereby appoint Alan Armstrong, a Senator from said 
     State to represent said State in the Senate of the United 
     States until the vacancy therein caused by the resignation of 
     Markwayne Mullin, is filled by election as provided by law.
       Witness: His excellency our governor J. Kevin Stitt, and 
     our seal hereto affixed at Oklahoma City this 24th day of 
     March, in the year of our Lord 2026.
           By the Governor:
                                                   J. Kevin Stitt,
                                                         Governor.
       Attest,
                                                   Benjamin Lepak,
                                                SECRETARY OF STATE
     [State Seal Affixed]

                          ____________________
```
