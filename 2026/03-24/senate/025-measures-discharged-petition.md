# MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITION

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 99

```
                      MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITION

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with chapter 8 
     of title 5, United States Code, hereby direct that the Senate 
     Committee on the Judiciary be discharged of further 
     consideration of S.J. Res. 99, a joint resolution on 
     providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by U.S. 
     Citizenship and Immigration Services relating to ``Removal of 
     the Automatic Extension of Employment Authorization 
     Documents'', and, further, that the resolution be immediately 
     placed upon the Legislative Calendar under General Orders.
         Jacky Rosen, Gary C. Peters, Edward J. Markey, Tammy 
           Baldwin, Chris Van Hollen, Mark Kelly, Raphael G. 
           Warnock, Andy Kim, Christopher A. Coons, Michael F. 
           Bennet, Catherine Cortez Masto, Margaret Wood Hassan, 
           Lisa Blunt Rochester, Alex Padilla, Angus S. King, Jr., 
           Mark R. Warner, Peter Welch, Jack Reed, John W. 
           Hickenlooper, Mazie K. Hirono, Maria Cantwell, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Ron Wyden, Elizabeth Warren, Tammy 
           Duckworth, Elissa Slotkin, Angela D. Alsobrooks, Amy 
           Klobuchar, Patty Murray, Tina Smith, Ruben Gallego, 
           Kirsten E. Gillibrand, Cory A. Booker, Ben Ray Lujan, 
           Jeanne Shaheen, Jeff Merkley, Martin Heinrich, Tim
```
