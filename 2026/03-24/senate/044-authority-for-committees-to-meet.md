# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I have four requests for committees to 
meet during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of 
the Majority and Minority Leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


                      COMMITTEE ON ARMED SERVICES

  The Committee on Armed Services is authorized to meet in closed and 
open session during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, March 24, 
2026, at 9:30 a.m.


                      COMMITTEE ON ARMED SERVICES

  The Committee on Armed Services is authorized to meet in open and 
closed session during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, March 24, 
2026, at 2:30 p.m.


                     COMMITTEE ON FOREIGN RELATIONS

  The Committee on Foreign Relations is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 10:30 a.m., to 
conduct a hearing.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 10:15 a.m., to 
conduct a hearing.

                          ____________________
```
