# CONGRATULATING SOPHIA ANGELICA

**Speaking:** McIver, LaMonica (M001229) [D-NJ]

```
                     CONGRATULATING SOPHIA ANGELICA

  (Mrs. McIVER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Sophia Angelica for 
her work in empowering children through music, acting, and writing.
  Sophia, a singer and songwriter from Argentina, uses her talents to 
advocate for human rights across the globe. She works closely with the 
United Nations and has traveled internationally to help combat bullying 
and discrimination.
  Not only does she travel across the United States for her 
performances, but she also performed at the sixth Asia-Pacific Human 
Rights Summit in Taiwan.
  By combining her vocal talents with her passion for activism, Sophia 
is making a remarkable impact on children across the world.
  On behalf of New Jersey's 10th Congressional District, I congratulate 
Sophia for her impactful global advocacy. I hope she keeps doing what 
she is doing.

                          ____________________
```
