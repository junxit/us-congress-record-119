  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to share with my colleagues 
information regarding a recent event held in my home district. On 
Saturday, October 17, 2026, I hosted a roundtable in Toledo, Ohio 
alongside the Democratic Women's Caucus and Chair Teresa Leger 
Fernandez to shed light on the corruption and abuse perpetrated by 
Jeffrey Epstein and Leslie Wexner. We must hold these crooks 
accountable and bring justice to the dozens of victims who suffered at 
their hands. Lex Wexner is the richest man in Ohio with a net worth of 
$11.5 Billion. He has been influential across the state, namely with 
his development of the New Albany community and his large donations to 
The Ohio State University. Wexner employed Epstein as his financial 
advisor, thus entrusting him with his entire estate and granting him 
permission to buy and sell properties and other holdings on his behalf. 
This status propelled Epstein into wealth and bankrolled his 
trafficking ring. Without Wexner, there is no Epstein. In bringing 
together survivors, advocates, and researchers, we amplify the 
consequences of Wexner's influence in Ohio and elevate the voices of 
survivors.
  Epstein survivors Wendy Pesante, Marina Lacerda, and Jena-Lisa Jones 
participated in the roundtable to share their unique perspectives, as 
did local Toledo sex trafficking survivors Angela Navarro and Marie 
Fitzpatrick. Dr. Celia Williamson of the University of Toledo's Human 
Trafficking and Social Justice Institute and former Ohio State Senator 
Teresa Fedor also participated.
