# HONORING ED BRAYNON, JR.

```
                        HONORING ED BRAYNON, JR.

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to to include in the 
Record a proclamation recognizing and honoring Dr. Ed Braynon, Jr., a 
life-long community servant, role model and mentor of West Park:
  Whereas, During his sixty eight years of membership in the Omega Psi 
Phi Fraternity, Inc. Dr. Braynon has served as basileus (president) of 
Sigma Alpha Chapter (five years); Florida State Representative (four 
years); First Vice Seventh District Representative (Alabama, Florida, 
Georgia and Mississippi) four years; Seventh District Representative 
(three years); First Vice Grand Basileus (three years); Grand Basileus 
(three years);
  Whereas, Gethsemane Missionary Baptist Church's outreach ministries 
have for decades extended a helping hand to those in need and restored 
hope and dignity in their darkest hour;
  Whereas, Gethsemane Missionary Baptist Church's open door policy, 
since its founding, has provided a place of worship for anyone seeking 
the Word of God;
  Whereas, Gethsemane Missionary Baptist Church is rich in tradition 
and history, and has played a major role in shaping the City of West 
Park and fostering relationships between the church, the community and 
local government;
  Whereas, Gethsemane Missionary Baptist Church continues to be a 
beacon of light, hope and faith in South Florida; and
  Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, a member 
of the United States House of Representatives, representing the 24th 
Congressional District of Florida, commend Gethsemane Missionary 
Baptist Church, under the leadership of Pastor Newell D. Tucker and for 
its commitment, dedication and service to God, the City of West Park 
and South Florida.

                          ____________________
```
