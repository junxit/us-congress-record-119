# HONORING CLARENCE FRANK BLANKENSHIP

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
                  HONORING CLARENCE FRANK BLANKENSHIP

                                  _____
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. BARR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor a true patriot, Mr. Frank 
Blankenship of Waco, Kentucky. Mr. Blankenship, a veteran of the United 
States Army and the United States Air Force, celebrated his 101st 
birthday on October 16th.
  Mr. Blankenship was born in Burdine, Kentucky on October 16, 1925. 
Burdine is located in Letcher County, in the heart of Kentucky's coal 
country. To avoid working in the coal mines and looking for a better 
life, Mr. Blankenship enlisted in the U.S. Army at the age of 15, 
fudging about his age as other young patriots did at the time. He 
enlisted on April 9, 1941. He was stationed at Schofield Barracks, 
Hawaii when Pearl Harbor was attacked on December 7, 1941. He remained 
with the Army throughout World War II in the Pacific Theatre, being 
honorably discharged on August 17, 1945.
  Mr. Blankenship re-enlisted on February 5, 1948, this time joining 
the newly formed United States Air Force. He served with the 2750th Air 
Base Wing stationed at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in Ohio. He was 
an Aircraft Mechanic Foreman and an Airplane Inspector II. Mr. 
Blankenship retired as Chief Master Sergeant on November 30, 1963, and 
was honorably discharged.
  Mr. Blankenship went on to work for the Bluegrass Army Depot (MRSA) 
as a civilian. He retired again in 1987. He married the late Judy 
Blankenship in 1969 and they have three children; Randall (deceased), 
Pamela, and Mark.
  Frank Blankenship is a true patriot, a great American, and an 
inspiration to us all. I am forever grateful to him and his fellow 
soldiers who sacrificed so much in service to our nation. They are 
indeed the Greatest Generation. It is my honor to recognize his service 
and his 101st birthday before the United States Congress.
```
