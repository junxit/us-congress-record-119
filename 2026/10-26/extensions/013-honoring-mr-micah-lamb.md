# HONORING MR. MICAH LAMB

```
                        HONORING MR. MICAH LAMB

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Michael (Micah) Lamb of 
Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, and his selfless service to our community 
in the fight to help the marginalized and less fortunate of our 
communities. Micah is a high school senior in the Mechanicsburg Area 
School District, with a passion for service to those in need. On any 
given day, 12,000 individuals find themselves homeless in Pennsylvania. 
Rather than simply talking about the challenge, Micah acted. During the 
summer of 2026, he created the Pennsylvania Provisions Project.
  Micah's goal was to collect 500 essential items to support 
individuals experiencing homelessness. He needed the support of locals 
and reached out to ask. Among those that answered his call were 
Merakey, the Mechanicsburg Marching Band, Delta Dental, Penn Credit, 
and the firms of Rolka Loube, along with many other generous 
contributors.
  Through his tireless diligence, Micah far exceeded his 500-item goal; 
the Pennsylvania Provisions Project collected more than 2,000 items for 
the most vulnerable in our communities. Micah donated these essential 
provisions to A Miracle 4 Sure, a Harrisburg service organization whose 
mission is to improve the quality of life for individuals and their 
families whose lives have been impacted by negative life experiences, 
including homelessness. For Micah, seeing the generosity of his 
community come together to make a difference in the lives of people 
they may never know, was the most impactful part of his service 
project. As the colder months approach, Micah will continue his 
selfless service to our community by collecting blankets for Community 
Cares, a homeless support group in Carlisle that recently opened a new 
facility to address the increasing need for housing.
  Micah has benefitted as much or more from this project as have 
individuals in need. He stepped outside of his comfort zone, learned 
how to advocate for a cause, sought and developed contacts, requested 
direct assistance, organized donations, and shepherded the project to 
his overachieving end goal. He learned that leadership isn't about 
having a title; it's having the passion, drive, and courage to take 
action, make a difference, and putting service before self.
  Mr. Speaker, I'm honored and privileged to commend and recognize 
Micah Lamb on his tireless service, generosity, and calling to our most 
vulnerable. His actions and character set the standard by which all 
should be judged.
```
