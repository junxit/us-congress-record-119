# TRIBUTE TO CHERYL MEADOWS

```
                       TRIBUTE TO CHERYL MEADOWS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. HAROLD ROGERS

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Kentucky. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to a 
dedicated public servant for Southern and Eastern Kentucky, Cheryl 
Meadows, as she retires from the Southeast Kentucky Economic 
Development Corporation (SKED) after 27 years of exceptional service.
  Since joining SKED in 1999, Cheryl has been rewriting the story of 
Kentucky's Appalachian region, by sharing successful examples of 
economic revitalization in our counties that were rarely ever 
independently covered by journalists across the Commonwealth. As the 
Communications Director, she has spent nearly three decades 
highlighting the resilience, ingenuity, and success of local small 
businesses and entrepreneurs who form the backbone of our Appalachian 
communities. Her stories have been shared on social media and various 
news outlets, reviving hope and inspiration for a brighter future in 
our region.
  A Knox County native and resident of McCreary County, Cheryl's deep 
connections in the region fueled her passion for her work, building 
meaningful relationships with clients, training graduates, funding 
partners, and community leaders. She has skillfully documented SKED's 
history, economic milestones and vision for job creation and industry 
growth in a part of the country that thrives on small business success.
  Throughout her career, Cheryl played an instrumental role in 
promoting critical regional initiatives, including the Supplier 
Education & Economic Development (SEED) Symposium. SKED's annual SEED 
event connects our rural region's advanced manufacturing companies with 
prime federal contractors, training them how to become certified to 
compete for federal contract opportunities and expand their operations. 
Her tireless efforts have boosted our workforce and inspired more 
entrepreneurs to reach out to SKED for support and guidance.
  Mr. Speaker, Cheryl Meadows established a legacy of professionalism, 
creativity and unwavering commitment to support small businesses across 
Kentucky's Appalachian region. Few people knew that Cheryl courageously 
battled cancer during her time with SKED. It was clear that not even 
cancer could diminish her friendly smile and determination to help 
others. Her fierce resolve is a testament to the heart of our workforce 
across the region. I am personally grateful that Cheryl chose to use 
her wisdom and talents at SKED for the last 27 years. As she begins her 
well-deserved retirement, I pray that it will be filled with great joy 
and peace.

                          ____________________
```
