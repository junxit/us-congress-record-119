# CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ARCADA THEATRE

```
          CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ARCADA THEATRE

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Arcada Theatre 
of St. Charles, Illinois, as it celebrates its 100th anniversary. Since 
opening its doors on September 6, 1926, the Arcada has been a landmark 
in downtown St. Charles and an enduring part of the Fox River Valley's 
cultural and entertainment history.
  Built by St. Charles philanthropists Lester and Dellora Norris, the 
Arcada was designed by architect Elmer F. Behrns in the Spanish 
Colonial Revival style. At a cost of approximately $500,000, the 
theater was an ambitious undertaking for a community of about 5,000 
residents. Its ornate architecture and 1,009-seat auditorium were 
designed to provide audiences with an experience comparable to the 
grand theaters of Chicago.
  The Arcada's opening on Labor Day 1926 drew visitors from throughout 
Northern Illinois, including Chicagoans who traveled to St. Charles by 
special train. The theater quickly became known for silent films and 
Vaudeville performances. Its stage welcomed nationally recognized 
entertainers, including George Burns and Gracie Allen, Edgar Bergen and 
Charlie McCarthy, and the John Philip Sousa Band. As entertainment 
evolved, the Arcada adapted as well, becoming a venue for stage 
productions, concerts, comedy, and other live performances.
  Beyond the performances held within its walls, the Arcada has played 
an important role in the life of St. Charles. For generations, 
residents and visitors from throughout the Fox Valley have gathered 
there to celebrate, enjoy entertainment, and create lasting memories. 
Its designation on the National Register of Historic Places in 1994 
further recognizes its architectural and historical significance.
  In 2005, Ron Onesti and Onesti Entertainment took over operation of 
the Arcada, helping usher the historic theater into a new chapter. 
Through renovations and the addition of Club Arcada, the theater has 
continued to evolve while preserving the character and history that 
have made it a St. Charles landmark.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize this remarkable milestone and 
ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the Arcada Theatre on 
its 100th Anniversary. For a century, the Arcada has enriched the 
cultural life of St. Charles and the greater Fox River Valley, bringing 
generations together through entertainment, history, and shared 
experiences.
```
