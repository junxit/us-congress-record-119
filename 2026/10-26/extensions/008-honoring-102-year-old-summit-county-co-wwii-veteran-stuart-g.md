# HONORING 102-YEAR-OLD SUMMIT COUNTY, CO, WWII VETERAN STUART GORDON

```
  HONORING 102-YEAR-OLD SUMMIT COUNTY, CO, WWII VETERAN STUART GORDON

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. JOE NEGUSE

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. NEGUSE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a remarkable 
member of the Summit County, Colorado, community and World War II 
veteran, Mr. Stuart Gordon.
  Affectionately known as ``Boot,'' Mr. Gordon celebrated his 102nd 
birthday earlier this year. He has led a remarkable life as a veteran, 
educator, author, artist, and mentor to generations of Coloradans, and 
he has enriched the Summit County community by living life to the 
fullest and bringing others along for the ride.
  Mr. Gordon's story of service to our country began long before he 
made Colorado his home. He served as an Air Force fighter pilot during 
both World War II and the Korean War, flying with three different 
fighter squadrons. He earned his nickname ``Boot'' after jumping out of 
an Air Force plane during training; his parachute malfunctioned and 
opened seconds before he would have hit the ground at 130 miles per 
hour, causing his boots to fly off his feet. Mr. Gordon's military 
service was a formative experience for him, and he continues to attend 
the annual Memorial Day ceremony in Dillon, Colorado. We are honored 
that Mr. Gordon shares lessons from his military service with younger 
generations of Coloradans.
  In the 1970s, Mr. Gordon settled in Silverthorne and hand-built his 
legendary home, which is nicknamed the ``Foam Dome'' for its 15 
interconnected igloo-like domes. He quickly became part of the fabric 
of the local community. He taught English, history, and the arts, and 
he has spent much of his life on the slopes as a ski instructor. He 
taught himself to ski at five years old, and he found himself at home 
in the beautiful mountains of Summit County.
  Even in his retirement, Mr. Gordon has continued to prioritize 
connection with his community. He has accomplished everything on his 
personal ``adventure'' bucket list (such as traveling in a hot air 
balloon, swimming in both the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans, and visiting 
the Great Wall of China), so now he's embarking on a second bucket list 
focused on helping others. He has shared his unique home with more than 
100 different tenants.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Gordon 
and thanking him for his service to our country and to the Summit 
County community. At 102 years old, Mr. Gordon has witnessed an 
extraordinary amount of history, but he has also helped make a little 
bit of history himself. I am grateful that he has chosen to make Summit 
County his home, and I am proud to represent him in Congress. Boot, 
thank you for your service, your contributions to our community, and 
the example you continue to set for all of us.
```
