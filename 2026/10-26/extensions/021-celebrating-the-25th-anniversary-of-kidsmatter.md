# CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF KIDSMATTER

```
             CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF KIDSMATTER

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize KidsMatter as they 
celebrate their 25th anniversary. For over two decades as a nonprofit 
organization, KidsMatter has worked to help young people build 
resilience, develop healthy relationships, and realize their full 
potential. Through its commitment to youth mental health, leadership 
development, and career exploration, KidsMatter has strengthened 
families and communities throughout DuPage and Will Counties.
  In the mid-1990's, Edward Hospital sponsored a community-wide health 
assessment that highlighted the challenges facing local youth. In 
response, hospital leadership, Naperville community leaders, and 
volunteers came together to develop a collaborative approach to youth 
development and prevention. This effort became the Naperville Youth 
Development Coalition, which received 501(c)(3) nonprofit status in 
2001 and later became known as KidsMatter.
  Guided by its mission to build resilient kids who say ``no'' to 
destructive choices and ``yes'' to endless possibilities, KidsMatter 
connects young people and families with resources, supportive 
relationships, and opportunities to thrive. Its programs address 
challenges including anxiety, stress, substance use, and digital well-
being, while encouraging leadership, community service, and career 
readiness. Through initiatives such as its Collaborative Youth Team and 
career exploration programs, KidsMatter empowers young people to 
discover their strengths and prepare for the future.
  KidsMatter's success reflects the power of community partnerships. By 
bringing together schools, families, health professionals, public 
safety agencies, local businesses, and community organizations, 
KidsMatter helps ensure that young people have the support and 
opportunities they need to succeed. Its work has received regional 
recognition, including NBC 5 Chicago's Making a Difference feature in 
2024 for its Youth Mental Health First Aid courses and CBS News 
Chicago's coverage of its Kindness Counts Campaign in 2023.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize KidsMatter on this milestone and 
ask my colleagues to join me in thanking its founders, board members, 
staff, and volunteers for their dedication to our young people. Their 
work demonstrates that when a community invests in its children, it 
invests in a stronger future for everyone.
```
