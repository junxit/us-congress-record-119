# RECOGNIZING ANDREW RASMUSSEN

```
                      RECOGNIZING ANDREW RASMUSSEN

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize 
and thank Andrew Rasmussen of Brookings, South Dakota, for his service 
to the people of South Dakota. Andrew began as an intern in my office 
and went on to serve as Legislative Correspondent and Press Assistant. 
He now serves as my Press Secretary.
  During his time, Andrew spearheaded projects with great impact on our 
state. Andrew led the office's high school graduation mailer. He worked 
directly with school administrators to collect addresses, and as a 
result, more than half of South Dakota's graduating seniors received a 
congratulatory letter directly from my office.
  Andrew built an efficient process for drafting and approving 
constituent correspondence and produced more than 350 letters and 
updates. He also wrote more than 30 outreach emails, reaching audiences 
from 200 to more than 60,000 South Dakotans, along with my weekly 
column.
  He was competent under pressure, and vital to ensuring I was prepared 
to communicate my policy goals efficiently and effectively. During the 
rollout and passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, he staffed 
eleven press interviews in a single day.
  Andrew is a valuable member of my team and I'm grateful for his 
service to the people of South Dakota.

                          ____________________
```
