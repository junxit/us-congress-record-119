# HONORING TRICIA BRUNSON ON HER RETIREMENT

```
               HONORING TRICIA BRUNSON ON HER RETIREMENT

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tricia Brunson 
on her retirement after nearly 30 years with the Niceville-Valparaiso 
Chamber of Commerce.

[[Page E1126]]

  Since 1996, Tricia has been a driving force behind the growth of our 
local business community. From helping build the Chamber's headquarters 
and visitors center to supporting local businesses through the COVID-19 
pandemic, she has spent her career bringing people together and making 
Northwest Florida stronger.
  Tricia's commitment to this community extends far beyond her work at 
the Chamber. Through her service to our schools, military 
installations, public safety organizations, and disaster relief 
efforts, she has made a lasting difference in the lives of the people 
she serves.
  Tricia believes there is room for every business to succeed, and she 
has spent nearly three decades turning that belief into action. 
Northwest Florida is better because of her leadership, dedication, and 
commitment to helping others.
  I thank Tricia for all she has done for our community and 
congratulate her on a well-deserved retirement. I wish her all the best 
in this next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
