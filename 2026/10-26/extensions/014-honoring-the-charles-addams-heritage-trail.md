# HONORING THE CHARLES ADDAMS HERITAGE TRAIL

```
               HONORING THE CHARLES ADDAMS HERITAGE TRAIL

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the success of the 
Charles Addams Heritage Trail and its important contribution to the 
cultural heritage of Westfield, New Jersey, during this historic 250th 
anniversary year.
  Charles Samuel Addams, the world-renowned cartoonist, was born in 
Westfield on January 2, 1912, and lived in the community for much of 
his early life. The streets, historic homes, civic buildings, and local 
landmarks of Westfield helped shape the creative imagination that would 
later captivate audiences around the world.
  The Charles Addams Heritage Trail preserves and celebrates that 
remarkable connection. The 1.5-mile recreational route invites 
residents and visitors to walk, bike, or ride through the Westfield 
neighborhoods that influenced Addams during his formative years. Along 
the way, participants encounter historic architecture, the 
Revolutionary War-era Presbyterian Church burial grounds, the Reeve 
House Museum, the Kimball Avenue Historic District, Addams's longtime 
Elm Street home, and the former Westfield High School that he attended 
from 1926 through 1929.
  The Trail offers more than a journey through the life of a celebrated 
artist. It provides a year-round, self-guided experience that connects 
art, architecture, local history, and community identity. It allows 
each new generation to better understand how the places that surround 
us can inspire creativity and leave an enduring mark on American 
culture.
  This initiative also reflects the power of partnership. The Trail was 
supported by the New Jersey Historical Commission, the Union County 
Office of Cultural and Heritage Affairs, the Town of Westfield, 
Downtown Westfield

[[Page E1129]]

Corporation, the Tee & Charles Addams Foundation, and AddamsFest, 
together with dedicated local officials, historians, organizers, and 
community members.
  As our Nation commemorates its 250th anniversary, the Charles Addams 
Heritage Trail reminds us that America's story is preserved not only in 
great national monuments, but also in local streets, schools, homes, 
burial grounds, and communities. Westfield's thoughtful preservation of 
the places that influenced Charles Addams ensures that this distinctive 
chapter of our cultural history will continue to educate, inspire, and 
delight.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
success of the Charles Addams Heritage Trail and commending all those 
whose vision and dedication have strengthened Westfield's cultural 
heritage and preserved the legacy of one of America's most original 
artistic voices.
```
