# HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF PLUCKEMIN PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH

```
    HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF PLUCKEMIN PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 175th anniversary of 
Pluckemin Presbyterian Church in Bedminster Township, New Jersey. For 
one hundred and seventy-five years, the church has supported Christian 
families from around New Jersey. Since its establishment in 1851, 
Pluckemin Presbyterian Church has served as a cornerstone of spiritual 
fulfillment through Sunday Worship, and a variety of community annual 
events that bring families together. Under the leadership of Rev. Ian 
Rankine and Rev. Dr. Maureen Paterson, the church continues to embrace 
traditional Presbyterian values while creating space for new ideas.
  This year's milestone celebrates Pluckemin Presbyterian Church's 
enduring dedication to service. Their commitment to a wide range of 
spiritual, social and charitable programs enables new and old members 
alike to thrive within a supportive environment. Their themed services 
and holiday concerts create and celebrate genuine connections with 
church members and the community.
  Mr. Speaker, I offer my sincere gratitude to Pluckemin Presbyterian 
Church for their unwavering dedication to enriching and uplifting New 
Jersey's Christian community. Their 175th anniversary stands as a 
significant milestone in our state's faith and cultural history. We 
celebrate their rich legacy knowing that the Pluckemin Presbyterian 
Church will continue to bless and uplift the community for years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
