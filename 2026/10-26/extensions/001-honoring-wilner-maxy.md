# HONORING WILNER MAXY

```
                          HONORING WILNER MAXY

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor Reverend Wilner Maxy, a well respected religious leader, and 
dedicated community and international activist, who has served as an 
inspiration and beacon of hope for Haitian-Americans and Haitian 
Immigrants in South Florida.
  Reverend Maxy is one of the founding members of Emmanuel Haitian 
Baptist Church, which was established in Miami, in 1973. He was 
appointed pastor of the church in 1983. Under his leadership, the 
congregation has grown from 200 members to over 1,300, and the church 
has become an integral part of the surrounding community.
  During the 1980's, Reverend Maxy assisted thousands of Haitian 
refugees, arriving in South Florida, with housing and job assistance. 
He created a scholarship program that has helped dozens of young men 
and women attend Seminary College. Reverend Maxy has expanded the 
church's outreach programs to include the support of eight churches and 
three schools in Haiti.
  Reverend Maxy has been a driving force in the community surrounding 
Emmanuel Haitian Baptist Church and because of his efforts to fight 
crime and drugs, he has been awarded the key to the City of Miami. 
Reverend Maxy has also has been recognized for his achievement and 
contributions with the designation of April 1, 2011, as Reverend Wilner 
Maxy Day, by the City of North Miami.
  In addition to his participation in numerous community service 
organizations, Reverend Maxy has served as a dedicated liaison between 
Miami-Dade County's religious communities, and local and statewide 
governments.
  Please join me in honoring Reverend Wilner Maxy for his commitment, 
dedication and service to our community.

                          ____________________
```
