# RECOGNIZING SYDNEY POWERS

```
                       RECOGNIZING SYDNEY POWERS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize 
and thank Sydney Powers of Brookings, South Dakota, for more than four 
years of dedication and service to the people of South Dakota. Sydney 
was a vital member of my policy team. Due to Sydney's hard work, 
persistence, and skill in navigating complex legislative challenges, 
the Center for Effective Lawmaking ranked me the most effective 
lawmaker on tribal issues and third most effective on transportation 
issues in the 118th Congress.
  While I have never served on the House Committee on Natural 
Resources, my office became the go-to office for tribal legislation 
thanks to Sydney's strong relationships with the committee and her 
dedication to advocating for South Dakota's nine tribal nations. During 
the Fiscal Year 2025 appropriations process, she worked closely with 
committee staff and tribal leaders. Her work helped secure a 
significant 34 percent overall increase in tribal law enforcement 
funding in the House Interior and Environment appropriations bill.
  I am most proud of Sydney's work on the Wounded Knee Massacre 
Memorial and Sacred Site Act. After much collaboration between two 
tribal councils, the bill was first introduced in 2023. The House 
passed it unanimously, and it was signed into law on December 19, 2025, 
just days before the 135th anniversary of the massacre.
  Sydney's success extended to the Transportation and Infrastructure 
and Judiciary Committees, where several bills she drafted advanced 
through the legislative process. She also helped organize a behavioral 
health roundtable at a critical time for our state, and stakeholders 
continue to praise it.
  I'm grateful for Sydney's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
