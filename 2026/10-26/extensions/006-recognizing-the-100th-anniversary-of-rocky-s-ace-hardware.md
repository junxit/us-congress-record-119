# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ROCKY'S ACE HARDWARE

**Speaking:** Neal, Richard E. (N000015) [D-MA]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ROCKY'S ACE HARDWARE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD E. NEAL

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. NEAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
milestone for a cherished New England institution: the 100th 
anniversary of Rocky's Ace Hardware, a family-owned business that has 
served generations of customers with integrity, expertise, and an 
unwavering commitment to our community.
  Rocky's Ace Hardware began on March 6, 1926, when founder Rocco 
``Rocky'' Falcone opened a hardware store in Springfield, 
Massachusetts, on the corner of Main and Union streets. From its 
inception, the store has reflected Rocky's belief that a hardware 
business should do more than sell tools; it should help people solve 
problems. One century and four generations later, it is that philosophy 
that has continued to guide the company.
  When the Great Depression struck, Rocky adapted by transforming the 
store into ``Rocky's Rental,'' ensuring that local families who could 
not afford to purchase equipment could still access the tools they so 
desperately needed. This spirit of innovation and sense of community is 
what enabled Rocky's to earn the trust and loyalty of generations of 
customers.
  Throughout the 1950s and 1960s, Rocky's evolved into a trusted 
neighborhood hardware store, known not only for its products, but for 
the knowledgeable employees who offered advice, guidance, and a 
friendly face. It wasn't long until Rocky's son Jim and his daughter 
Claire took over the business, helping to further grow the company 
while remaining true to the values instilled by their father.
  Today, under the leadership of Rocco's grandson, Rocco Falcone II, 
and with the dedication of the fourth generation of the Falcone family, 
including Johnny Falcone, Rocky's has grown to 50 stores across 
America. The company now employs more than 850 team members, each 
committed to the same values that Rocco established a century ago. 
Despite its growth, Rocky's has never lost sight of its roots. It 
remains proudly family-owned, community-oriented, and steadfast in its 
mission of helping people solve problems.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Rocky's 
Ace Hardware on this milestone anniversary. For 100 years, this 
establishment has exemplified the best of American small business--
resilience, innovation, and a commitment to service--and the Falcone 
family stands as a testament to what can be achieved when a business 
remains true to its values. We wish them and their dedicated employees 
continued success for the next hundred years.
```
