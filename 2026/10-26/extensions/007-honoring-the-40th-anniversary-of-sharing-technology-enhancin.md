# HONORING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF SHARING TECHNOLOGY ENHANCING LOCAL LIBRARY ACCESS (STELLA)

```
  HONORING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF SHARING TECHNOLOGY ENHANCING LOCAL 
                        LIBRARY ACCESS (STELLA)

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 26, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 40th 
anniversary of Sharing & Technology Enhancing Local Library Access 
(STELLA). Since its founding in October 1986, STELLA has fostered 
increased cooperation and resource sharing among independent public 
libraries in New Jersey. Over the last four decades, STELLA has grown 
into a consortium of 44 member libraries serving communities across 
Union, Middlesex, and Monmouth Counties.
  STELLA has enabled its member libraries to provide New Jersey 
residents with broader access to library resources, services, 
technology, expertise, and educational opportunities, all while 
maintaining their individual identities and connections to their local 
communities. Representing 1.4 million residents across six 
Congressional Districts, STELLA plays a vital role in providing 
equitable access to information, educational resources, cultural 
opportunities, and lifelong learning.
  I wish to express my gratitude for the library professionals, 
trustees, volunteers, and communities whose commitment to public 
libraries has created new opportunities for learning and innovation. I 
commend STELLA for its four decades of collaboration and service to New 
Jersey communities, and I am confident it will continue its vital work 
for the next 40 years and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
