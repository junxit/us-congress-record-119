# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O Lord, our God, You are our help and our deliverer. In these days of 
decision, grant us Your discernment, that we may hear beyond the words 
of divisiveness and derision, that we may see past the contest and the 
discontent, but that we would bear witness to the peace that is ours 
when we rest in You.
  Do not delay. Do not hold back Your guidance. We are weak in body, 
mind, and spirit. But You are mightier than anything that stands before 
us. Hold us with Your powerful hand and lead us on this journey.
  Open to us the fountain of faith, the healing waters of hope which 
flow from Your grace. Reveal the pillar of cloud and fire, reassuring 
us of Your abiding presence, and shepherd us in these days of unrest 
and uncertainty.
  Ever our strength and shield, to You, O God, we lift up our prayers 
in the power of Your name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
