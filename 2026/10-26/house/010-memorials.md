# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 20, H.Res. 324

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-137. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 
     324, to urge and request certain entities and medical, public 
     health, coding, and classification authorities to review and 
     revise medical terminology relating to miscarriage and 
     pregnancy loss to ensure that such terminology is medically 
     accurate, patient-centered, compassionate and sensitive to 
     women and families experiencing the loss of an unborn child; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       ML-138. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of New Hampshire, relative to House Resolution 20, 
     reaffirming the right for persons of all persuasions to keep 
     and bear arms, and urging Congress and the President to cease 
     infringement upon Second Amendment rights and to restore them 
     where infringed; which was referred to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
