# CELEBRATING THE JOHNSON COUNTY COMMUNITY COLLEGE CAVALIERS BASEBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Davids, Sharice (D000629) [D-KS]

```
  CELEBRATING THE JOHNSON COUNTY COMMUNITY COLLEGE CAVALIERS BASEBALL 
                                  TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 9, 2026

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
Johnson County Community College Cavaliers baseball team, which just 
won the Division 1 Junior College World Series national championship 
game for the first time in program history. As a JCCC alum, it is my 
pleasure to congratulate the team on this incredible accomplishment.
  This well-deserved victory marked the finale of an already-historic 
season. The Cavaliers ended with a 67-3 record, which included a 41-
game winning streak. As if that is not impressive enough, the team hit 
219 home runs--a college baseball record across every level.
  This past JCCC Cavaliers baseball season represents all the great 
ways we benefit from sports--instilling pride in our community, 
inspiring young people to set high goals, and offering new 
opportunities. In fact, the team expects more than 20 players to sign 
with Division I programs for next season.
  Once again, I congratulate the JCCC Cavaliers baseball team and, on 
behalf of the Johnson County community and beyond, I thank them for 
such an exciting season. Go Cavaliers.

                          ____________________
```
