# HONORING THE NATIONAL SPEECH & DEBATE ASSOCIATION'S 2026 NATIONAL TOURNAMENT

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
   HONORING THE NATIONAL SPEECH & DEBATE ASSOCIATION'S 2026 NATIONAL 
                               TOURNAMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 9, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
National Speech & Debate Association's 2026 National Tournament, which 
will be hosted in Richmond, Virginia, this June.
  This prestigious national tournament will welcome more than 10,000 
students, educators, and families from across the United States to the 
Commonwealth's capital. It is an extraordinary opportunity not only to 
showcase Richmond's abundant history and hospitality, but also to 
highlight the Commonwealth's commitment to educational excellence and 
civic engagement.
  The Richmond Forum Speech & Debate Initiative has showcased a deep 
understanding of a simple yet profound truth: the strength of our 
democracy depends on the ability of its citizens to communicate 
clearly, think critically, and engage one another with respect and 
kindness. I am proud to welcome these young leaders into our community. 
This tournament provides an opportunity for students across the country 
to develop the analytical and persuasive skills that empower the next 
generation to engage in the civil exchange of ideas and tackle the 
problems of tomorrow.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in welcoming these 
talented students to Virginia and celebrating the enduring value of 
speech and debate. I pray that they continue to find success in all 
their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
