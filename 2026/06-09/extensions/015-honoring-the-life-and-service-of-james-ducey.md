# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF JAMES DUCEY

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF JAMES DUCEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM WALBERG

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 9, 2026

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize James Ducey of Grass 
Lake as the Veteran of the Month for June and honor him for his 
sacrifice and service on behalf of our great Nation.
  After graduating from high school in 1966, James enlisted in the 
United States Air Force, serving proudly in the 341st Civil Engineering 
Squadron at the Malmstrom Air Force Base in Great Falls, Montana. James 
served in the Air Force until April of 1970, achieving the rank of 
Sergeant E-4, and was awarded the Small Arms Expert Marksmanship Ribbon 
and the Good Conduct Medal.
  Even after leaving active duty, James never stopped serving, shifting 
his efforts to his local community. James joined the Grass Lake 
American Legion Post 252, where he was appointed Adjutant in 2018, 
leading the annual Memorial Day celebration for several years and post 
activities through 2025. His leadership also extended to chairing the 
American Legion Fireworks Committee and the July 4th Parade in 
partnership with the Grass Lake Sportsman Club.
  While serving his local post, James also became Adjutant for the 
Jackson Veterans Council for four years. He served as backup Service 
Officer for Post 252 and has represented Post 252 as District 2 
Representative since 2021, continuing in that role today. In 2023, he 
was elected Jackson County Commander, serving until July 2025. He also 
served as Finance Person for District 2 for two years under Commander 
Greene.
  In 2024, James was appointed by Department Commander Mohlman to the 
Department Finance Committee, representing District 2 for a one-year 
term. He was subsequently appointed by Department Commander Cooper as 
Secretary of the Department's Internal Affairs Committee, a two-year 
appointment he currently holds.
  James Ducey's remarkable life of service and leadership on behalf of 
America's veterans and his community inspires us all. May others know 
of my high regard for James, as well as my deep gratitude for his 
lasting contributions to the country we love.
```
