# HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER 5 JUSTIN A. KARLS

```
   HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER 5 JUSTIN A. KARLS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 9, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Chief Warrant Officer 5 Justin A. Karls of the United States

[[Page E549]]

Army on the occasion of his retirement after 30 years of distinguished 
and selfless service to our Nation.
  Justin's story is one of extraordinary dedication. Enlisting in 1995 
as a native of Milwaukee, Wisconsin, he rose through the ranks to 
become one of the Army's most decorated and experienced aviators. After 
earning his wings at Fort Rucker in 1998, Justin went on to do what few 
ever accomplish. He was selected into the 160th Special Operations 
Aviation Regiment, the Night Stalkers, where he would spend more than 
two decades of his career. I am proud to say that I had the privilege 
of serving alongside Justin in the 160th, and I can speak firsthand to 
the caliber of soldier, aviator, and leader he is.
  Over the span of more than 20 years with the 160th, Justin served in 
virtually every leadership capacity imaginable, including Pilot in 
Command, Instructor Pilot, Standardization Instructor Pilot, Operations 
Officer, Senior Warrant Officer, and Regimental Flight Lead, deploying 
an astounding 30 times in support of the Global War on Terror and other 
contingency operations around the world. His decorations tell that 
story plainly: the Legion of Merit, the Distinguished Flying Cross, the 
Bronze Star Medal, and campaign medals bearing the marks of Kosovo, 
Afghanistan, and Iraq, among many others.
  But Justin is more than his record. He is a devoted husband to Dr. 
Leilani Karls and a proud father to Isabelle, Dr. Sydney, and Emma. The 
sacrifices made by his family in support of his service deserve equal 
recognition today.
  On behalf of a grateful Nation and a proud Congress, I thank Justin. 
The Night Stalkers never quit, and neither did he. I congratulate him 
on a magnificent career, and Godspeed. in the next chapter.
```
