# HONORING DR. KIA MONIQUE JONES

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                     HONORING DR. KIA MONIQUE JONES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 9, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Dr. Kia Monique Jones, an outstanding scholar, advocate, 
entrepreneur, and community leader whose work has positively impacted 
communities throughout the State of Mississippi.
  Dr. Jones is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of For Brown 
Girls, Inc., a nonprofit organization dedicated to addressing issues 
related to poverty, health equity, education, women empowerment, and 
community development. Through her leadership, the organization has 
created opportunities for professional networking, financial literacy, 
leadership development, and educational advancement for underserved 
communities throughout Mississippi.
  In addition to her nonprofit leadership, Dr. Jones has remained 
deeply engaged in civic and public health initiatives through her 
service with numerous organizations, including Leadership Greater 
Jackson, the Mississippi Black Leadership Institute, Planned Parenthood 
Southeast Advisory Council, the Mississippi Ending the HIV Epidemic 
Black Leadership Collaborative Task Force, and the American Red Cross 
Disaster Relief Program. Her commitment to social justice, equity, and 
public service continues to uplift communities across our state.
  Dr. Jones' dedication and leadership have earned her numerous honors, 
including the Mississippi Business Journal's Top 50 Under 40 Award, Top 
CEOs in Mississippi recognition, the Women Economic Forum Exceptional 
Women of Excellence International Award, and several awards recognizing 
her humanitarianism and entrepreneurial success.
  A proud three-time graduate of Jackson State University, Dr. Jones 
earned a Doctor of Public Health degree with a concentration in 
Epidemiology and Biostatistics, in addition to graduate and 
undergraduate degrees in Biology. She also earned a Master of Public 
Administration degree from Belhaven University.
  Mr. Speaker, Dr. Kia Monique Jones has demonstrated an unwavering 
commitment to improving the lives of others through leadership, 
advocacy, and service. I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing 
her outstanding contributions to the State of Mississippi and 
congratulating her on her continued success.

                          ____________________
```
