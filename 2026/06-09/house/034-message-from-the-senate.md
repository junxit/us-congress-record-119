# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 645, S. 1157

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Warner, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 645. An act to award a Congressional Gold Metal, 
     collectively, to the individuals and communities who 
     volunteered or donated items to the North Platte Canteen in 
     North Platte, Nebraska, during World War II from December 25, 
     1941, to April 1, 1946.
       S. 1157. An act to direct the Secretary of Health and Human 
     Services to conduct a review to evaluate the status of 
     research on lung cancer in women and underserved population, 
     and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
