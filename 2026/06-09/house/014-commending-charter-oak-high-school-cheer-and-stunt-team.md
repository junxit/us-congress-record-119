# COMMENDING CHARTER OAK HIGH SCHOOL CHEER AND STUNT TEAM

**Speaking:** Cisneros, Gilbert Ray (C001123) [D-CA]

```
        COMMENDING CHARTER OAK HIGH SCHOOL CHEER AND STUNT TEAM

  (Mr. Cisneros of California was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I would like to commend the Charter Oak 
High School Cheer and STUNT Team for making history by becoming the 
first San Gabriel Valley school to win a CIF Southern Section STUNT 
cheer championship.
  Their hard work and dedication paid off. They should all be very 
proud of themselves for capturing the Division 2 title.
  Congratulations to Coach Ashley Garcia for all the time and energy 
she spent training these talented athletes.
  An additional shout-out to sophomore Juliette Ramirez for being the 
top power player and point earner and to senior Kaelynn Quezada who 
made a triumphant return from injury this season. She came back 
stronger than ever and scored 249 points in 265 rounds. I'm not really 
sure what that means, but it sounds incredible.
  Kaelynn was also named the Valle Vista League MVP, and that I do know 
is incredible.
  I congratulate her on that achievement.
  This was a remarkable season capped by historic victory. I am 
incredibly proud of the Charter Oak team, and I hope they enjoy their 
major win.


                 Congratulating Edgewood Softball Team

  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations 
to the Edgewood softball team for their impressive victory in the CIF 
Southern Section championship.
  Their teamwork, hard work, and devotion to the game paid off with 
this success, and they should be so proud of their achievements.
  A special shout-out to junior Alexis Hurtado for her amazing 
performance. She pitched a complete game, played great defense, and 
helped guide her team to victory.
  The entire team demonstrated dedication to athletic excellence and 
made our district proud with this impressive game.
  I congratulate them on their accomplishment. They earned it through 
their hard work and dedication.
  I wish them good luck in the CIF Southern Section playoffs. I will be 
rooting for them.


                        Recognizing Pride Month

  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pride Month and 
celebrate the strength, resilience, and diversity of the LGBTQI+ 
community in California's 31st District and across the country.
  It was just over a decade ago that the Supreme Court announced their 
decision to affirm marriage equality as the law of the land. This 
historic step forward remains a landmark legal victory for LGBTQI+ 
Americans in the fight for equality, but today that fight continues. 
There is always more work to be done to make this country a more 
inclusive and welcoming place for all.
  This Pride Month is about coming together as a community to celebrate 
the freedom we all deserve because everyone deserves to love who they 
love and to live openly and authentically, without fear of 
discrimination.
  I also want to take a moment to reaffirm my commitment to fighting 
for trans equality. I will always defend their right to live as who 
they are. As a member of the Congressional Equality Caucus and an 
original cosponsor of the Equality Act, I am proud to champion their 
fight for equal justice, equal dignity, and equal rights.
  Happy Pride Month.

                      Gun Violence Awareness Month

  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Gun Violence 
Awareness Month and to honor the thousands of Americans who lost their 
lives to gun violence.
  In 2025, gun homicides killed an estimated 15,000 people. This number 
represents children taken from their parents, young people with bright 
futures stolen from them, beloved community members and so many 
families torn apart by the cruel injustice of an act of violence 
permitted by our broken policies.
  I know family and friends who have lost loved ones due to an act of 
gun violence. For them, it is pain that will live on forever.
  Thoughts and prayers are not enough. It is time for us all to 
confront this epidemic head on with long-overdue gun reform. 
Commonsense gun safety laws are not controversial. In fact, a majority 
of Americans say it is far too easy to legally obtain a gun in this 
country.
  No one should have to bear the trauma of losing a loved one to a 
preventable incident of gun violence. I promise you that I will fight 
every day to forge a better tomorrow where everyone is safe from this 
unconscionable harm of gun violence.
```
