# PUBLICATION OF BUDGETARY MATERIAL

**Measures:** S. 2, S.Con.Res. 33

```
                   PUBLICATION OF BUDGETARY MATERIAL


     REVISION TO THE AGGREGATES OF THE FISCAL YEAR 2026 CONCURRENT 
                        RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET

                                         House of Representatives,


                                      Committee on the Budget,

                                     Washington, DC, June 9, 2026.
       Mr. Speaker, I hereby submit for printing in the 
     Congressional Record a revision to the aggregates established 
     by S. Con. Res. 33, the Fiscal Year 2026 Concurrent 
     Resolution on the Budget. Section 3001 of S. Con. Res. 33 
     permits the Chairman of the Committee on the Budget to adjust 
     the aggregates and other appropriate levels established by 
     that resolution for legislation considered under the 
     resolution's reconciliation instructions.
       This adjustment is designated for S. 2, the Secure America 
     Act, as passed the Senate on June 5, 2026. The Congressional 
     Budget Office's estimate for S. 2 does not account for 
     commensurate discretionary savings that will occur in future 
     appropriations bills. The intent of S. 2 is to fully fund 
     Immigration and Customs Enforcement and Customs and Border 
     Protection though September 30, 2029. The bill complies with 
     section 2001 of S. Con. Res. 33. The adjustment is reflected 
     in the attached table.
       This revision represents an adjustment for purposes of 
     budgetary enforcement. These revised aggregates are to be 
     considered as the aggregates included in the concurrent 
     resolution on the budget, pursuant to S. Con. Res. 33. 
     Pursuant to section 4103 of S. Con. Res. 33, these 
     adjustments shall apply only while S. 2 is under 
     consideration or upon its enactment.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Jodey Arrington,
                                Chairman, Committee on the Budget.
```
