# RECOGNIZING RAM'S HEAD CAFE AND LODGE

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                 RECOGNIZING RAM'S HEAD CAFE AND LODGE

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, Idaho's small businesses are the 
backbone of our economy and our communities. These small businesses not 
only employ our friends and neighbors but also provide invaluable goods 
and services that showcase Idaho's ingenuity and shared values. They 
are an intrinsic element of the Gem State and deserve to be celebrated.
  I am proud to launch my seventh annual Support Local Gems, a 
successful statewide initiative, on June 12, to encourage Idahoans to 
support the small businesses that make our State thrive. As a member 
and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small Business and 
Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor Ram's Head Cafe and Lodge in 
Salmon during Support Local Gems as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of 
the Month for June 2026.
  Established in 1965 by Ozzie and Lois Schmidt, Ram's Head began as a 
small cafe and gas station serving recreationalists in the Salmon-
Challis National Forest. Positioned along the banks of the Salmon 
River, it was the perfect spot for Idahoans enjoying our public lands. 
Over time, the company changed ownership, expanded the cafe, and added 
hotel units, but eventually fell vacant for 15 years.
  In 2017, Chris and Brianna Reece took on the task of getting Ram's 
Head up and running again. By the fall of 2018, the pair had 
refurbished the building and hosted the grand opening of the new Ram's 
Head Cafe and Lodge.
  Now operating primarily as a hunting and fishing lodge, Chris and 
Brianna provide quality service and food to the surrounding downriver 
community, Lemhi County residents, and summer recreationalists 
traveling through Idaho. Through partnerships with local hunting and 
fishing outfitters, Ram's Head has grown to offer the best experience 
for locals, travelers, and adventure seekers of any kind in the Salmon-
Challis National Forest.
  Chris and Brianna are also active members of the Salmon Chamber of 
Commerce and support efforts like the Idaho Conservation Officers 
Association and Salmon River Days. The pair attributes their ability to 
renovate and reopen Ram's Head in under 2 years to strong community 
support and plan to continue giving back to their community as 
businessowners and through personal endeavors.
  Congratulations to Chris and Brianna Reece and the entire Ram's Head 
team for being selected as an Idaho Small Business of the Month for 
June 2026. You are an outstanding example of what it means to be one of 
Idaho's Local Gems. You make our great State proud, and I look forward 
to your continued growth and success.●
```
