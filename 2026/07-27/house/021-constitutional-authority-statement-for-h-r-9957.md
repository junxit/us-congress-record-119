# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9957

**Measures:** H.R. 9957

```
           By Mrs. HAYES:
       H.R. 9957.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United 
     States Constitution, Congress may regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, among states, and with the Indian tribes.
```
