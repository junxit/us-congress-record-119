# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God, our creator, we are humbled by Your power. You need only a 
mustard seed to grow a tree large enough to attract and feed the many 
birds of the air. From such small beginnings, You reveal the potential 
of even the most unlikely things.
  Lord, we call on You to use us in that same way. Help us to see how, 
when we allow ourselves to be used by You, You can use even our most 
minute efforts to effect something great for Your kingdom's purpose. 
Show us how, like leaven, when we yield ourselves to Your purpose, we 
can be used as agents of transformation in our communities.
  Plant Your word in our hearts. Touch our lives with Your love, that 
they would take root and change our lives. May we grow in wisdom and 
faith, revealing the grace plan You have always intended for us and for 
Your creation.
  In Your strong name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
