# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY PATRICIA ELLEN MALLEY

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY PATRICIA ELLEN MALLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 27, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Patricia Ellen Malley, a devoted wife, mother, grandmother, great-
grandmother, and woman of unwavering faith whose life was marked by 
kindness, compassion, and selfless devotion to her family.
  Born in Crane, Texas, Patricia was raised with the values of faith, 
hard work, and love for others. She carried those principles throughout 
her life, touching the lives of all who knew her with her gentle spirit 
and quiet strength.
  Patricia dedicated many years of faithful service to American 
Greetings, where she was admired for her dedication and warm 
personality. After retiring, she devoted herself to her greatest 
calling, which was her family. Whether preparing meals, traveling with 
loved ones, or simply opening her home to others, Patricia found joy in 
caring for those around her.
  She is survived by her beloved husband, Stephen H. Malley; her 
brother, Tom Hightower; her children, Billy Don Bratton, Kerry Aalbers, 
and Stephen S. Malley; six grandchildren; one great-granddaughter; and 
many extended family members and friends. She was preceded in death by 
her brothers, Mike, Richard, and Jack Hightower; her sister, Linda 
Pearce; and her granddaughter, Bailey Bratton.
  Mr. Speaker, Patricia Malley lived a life centered on faith, family, 
and service. Her love, grace, and steadfast example will continue to 
inspire those who knew her. It is my honor to recognize her life and 
extend my deepest condolences to her family and loved ones as they 
celebrate the legacy she leaves behind.

                          ____________________
```
