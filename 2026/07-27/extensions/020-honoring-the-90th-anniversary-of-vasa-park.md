# HONORING THE 90TH ANNIVERSARY OF VASA PARK

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
               HONORING THE 90TH ANNIVERSARY OF VASA PARK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 27, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 90th anniversary of 
Vasa Park in Mount Olive, New Jersey. In 1936 the Vasa Order of America 
established Vasa Park to serve as a retirement community for 
Scandinavian immigrants across the Morris County area. Today, the park 
has grown as a meeting place for descendants of the original founders 
to come with their families and vacation, experiencing the nature of 
Mount Olive.
  The park includes 31 cabins for families to rent and stay, and a 
Cultural Center for social events and commemoration of Scandinavian 
ancestry. Families descending from Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, 
and Iceland make up the over 1,000 members who return to the park year 
after year.
  For 90 years, Vasa Park has fostered the Scandinavian presence in 
Morris County, serving as a lasting symbol of culture and tradition. 
Throughout its history, the park has upheld the sense of community that 
is so important to the citizens of Mount Olive and Morris County as a 
whole.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask you and my colleagues to join me in commemorating 
Vasa Park's 90th Anniversary, as they continue to uphold Scandinavian 
tradition in Mount Olive, New Jersey.
```
