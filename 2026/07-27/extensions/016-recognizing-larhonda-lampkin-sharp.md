# RECOGNIZING LaRHONDA LAMPKIN SHARP

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                   RECOGNIZING LaRHONDA LAMPKIN SHARP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 27, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and pay 
tribute to LaRhonda Lampkin Sharp, a breast cancer survivor, and co-
founder and treasurer of the Miami, Florida, Chapter of Sisters Network 
Incorporated.
  Mrs. Sharp was diagnosed with breast cancer in 2007. In 2011, she and 
four other breast cancer survivors founded the Miami Chapter of Sisters 
Network, Inc., a national African-American breast cancer survivorship 
organization. Its mission is to address the needs particular to 
African-American women, the underinsured and the underserved around the 
country by getting the message out that ``we must stop the silence 
about breast cancer so women can start beating the odds.'' The 
organization has since provided a strong support group for hundreds of 
women with breast cancer, as well as survivors in South Florida.
  Experts estimate that about one out of every eight women born today 
will be diagnosed with breast cancer at some time during her life. The 
strongest risk factor for breast cancer is age. A woman's risk of 
developing this disease increases as she gets older. However, thanks to 
the work Mrs. Sharp is doing and improvements in cancer treatment and 
early detection, millions of women and thousands of men are surviving 
breast cancer today.
  Breast cancer is the most common cancer among women in the United 
States, other than skin cancer. It is the second leading cause of 
cancer death in women, after lung cancer. The disease occurs almost 
entirely in women, but men can get it, too. Today, there are more than 
2.9 million breast cancer survivors in the United States.
  I am honored to recognize LaRhonda Lampkin Sharp, a breast cancer 
survivor and co-founder and treasure of the Miami Chapter of Sisters 
Network, for her dedication and commitment to our community and the 
fight against breast cancer.
```
