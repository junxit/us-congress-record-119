# HONORING THE PINECREST HIGH SCHOOL WOMEN'S LACROSSE TEAM

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
        HONORING THE PINECREST HIGH SCHOOL WOMEN'S LACROSSE TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 27, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Pinecrest High 
School women's lacrosse team on an undefeated state championship 
season.
  On Friday, May 29, 2026, Head Coach Jacqueline Page and the Pinecrest 
High School women's lacrosse team capped off a perfect 22-0 season by 
winning the North Carolina High School Athletic Association Girls 
Lacrosse 8A State Championship.
  This tremendous achievement is a testament to the team's unity, 
discipline, and outstanding work ethic. Winning a state championship is 
a remarkable accomplishment and doing so with an undefeated record 
makes this achievement even more extraordinary.
  I know I speak for our entire community in offering my most heartfelt 
congratulations to the exceptional young women of Pinecrest High 
School's women's lacrosse team. These young leaders have made our 
community proud.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today celebrating the Pinecrest High 
School women's lacrosse team's remarkable undefeated state championship 
season.

                          ____________________
```
