# HONORING THE LIFE OF JULIA McCAA

**Speaking:** Ciscomani, Juan (C001133) [R-AZ]

```
                    HONORING THE LIFE OF JULIA McCAA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JUAN CISCOMANI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 27, 2026

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the incredible life 
of Julia McCaa, who sadly passed away on July 4, 2026, reuniting with 
her beloved husband, Dr. Clea McCaa I.
  Julia McCaa spent her life serving others--through ministry, 
counseling, prayer, support, and unwavering love. She devoted her life 
to advancing the Kingdom of God by doing everything for His glory.
  After graduating from high school, Julia continued her love of 
learning through her time at Wenonah State Technical College, MTI 
Business School in Miami, Florida, and Cochise College.
  Along with her gift of learning, Julia had the gift of music. As a 
teenager, she joined her sisters in a gospel singing group named The 
Three Sisters. Her love of singing continued throughout her life, 
allowing her to share the Gospel through song in countless settings.
  On May 14, 1961, Julia married Dr. Clea McCaa I at Trinity Church of 
God in Christ in Ensley, Alabama. Their marriage was wholly built on 
their love of and faith in Christ. Julia faithfully supported her 
husband throughout his time in the military and ministry as a prayer 
warrior, unwavering partner, and trusted counselor.
  Julia found great joy and fulfillment in her family, as she and her 
husband were blessed with two wonderful children, Michelle Denise McCaa 
and Mayor Clea Otis McCaa II. Together, they also shared three 
beautiful grandchildren, Diondra Julia Denise (``Jul''), Clea Otis III 
(``Trae''), and Marie Cene' (``Reecee'').
  Throughout her life, Julia demonstrated a servant's heart. She 
committed her time to helping others, serving in the Church of God in 
Christ, being a Court Appointed Special Advocate (CASA), Sierra Vista 
West End Commissioner, and receptionist at Canyon Vista Medical Center, 
as well as volunteering with several local and national organizations.
  Julia will be greatly missed. She was a light to those around her and 
all who knew her experienced genuine love, care, and counsel.
  May Julia rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
