# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JOANN SMITH

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JOANN SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 27, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of a remarkable Bronxite, educator, writer, wife, mother, 
sister, aunt, and friend, Joann Smith.
  Joann passed away on July 17, 2026, at Calvary Hospital in the Bronx, 
following a courageous battle with cancer. She leaves behind a legacy 
defined not by her illness, but by her bravery, her kindness, her 
creativity, and her love for others.
  Born in the Bronx in 1956, Joann grew up on the Grand Concourse and 
was educated in the Bronx Catholic school system. She went on to earn 
degrees from Iona College, the College of New Rochelle, and Lehman 
College before pursuing her Master of Fine Arts at Sarah Lawrence 
College.
  Joann spent fifteen years as an educator, teaching in both Catholic 
and New York City public schools. She later devoted her life to 
writing, teaching aspiring writers, and expanding access to education. 
She served as a professor, program director, creative director, editor, 
and mentor.
  She fulfilled her lifelong dream of becoming a published author, 
writing the historical novel When I Was Boudicca and, and in 2021, she 
published her acclaimed short story collection, A Heaven of the 
Choosing.
  But Joann's greatest joy was her family. She and her husband, Robert 
Fanuzzi, celebrated thirty years of marriage this year. And in 2001, 
Joann and Robert traveled to China to adopt their daughter, Mia--a 
journey Joann considered the highlight of her life.
  Joann also gave generously to others, volunteering with Girls Write 
Now to help young women prepare for college and find their voices.
  The Bronx mourns with Robert, Mia, her siblings, nieces and nephews, 
and her many friends.
  Joann's life was a testament to the power of courage, education, art, 
and love. Her stories will endure. Her students will carry her lessons 
forward. And all who knew her will carry her memory in their hearts.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to honor the life of Joann Smith.
  May she rest in peace.
```
