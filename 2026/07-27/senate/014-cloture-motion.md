# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 833, Walter Clayton, of New York, 
     to be Director of National Intelligence.
         John Thune, Tom Cotton, Jon Husted, Alan Armstrong, Jim 
           Banks, Bernie Moreno, Chuck Grassley, John Barrasso, 
           Marsha Blackburn, Joni Ernst, John R. Curtis, Roger F. 
           Wicker, Pete Ricketts, Katie Boyd Britt, Bill Hagerty, 
           Tim Scott of South Carolina, Mike Crapo.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the mandatory quorum 
call under rule XXII has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of Walter Clayton, of New York, to be Director of National 
Intelligence, shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Kentucky (Mr. McConnell), and the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mr. Ricketts).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from New York (Mrs. 
Gillibrand), the Senator from Arizona (Mr. Kelly), the Senator from 
Michigan (Mr. Peters), and the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Welch) are 
necessarily absent.
  The yeas and nays resulted--yeas 51, nays 43, as follows:
```
