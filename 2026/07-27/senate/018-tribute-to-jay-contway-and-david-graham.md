# TRIBUTE TO JAY CONTWAY AND DAVID GRAHAM

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                TRIBUTE TO JAY CONTWAY AND DAVID GRAHAM

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, today I have the honor to recognize 
Jay Contway and David Graham.
  Jay is a native Montanan, born and raised in Malta, MT.
  After settling near Great Falls in 1968, Jay built his foundry and 
became a renowned Western sculptor. Jay's work captured international 
recognition and has been displayed for audiences throughout North 
America for years, capturing the people, history, and traditions of the 
American West. Through Jay's work, he has preserved and illustrated the 
essence of the cowboy way of life, earning the title ``Montana's Own 
Story Teller in Bronze.''
  David Graham also has contributed to the rich artistry of Montana. 
Born in Miles City, David was raised on a ranch in the Powder River 
area and his family's Western way of life deeply influenced his 
artistic career.
  David gathers his inspiration by experiencing the Western way of 
life. whether he is riding horseback in the Badlands or hiking on a 
mountain trail. He is deeply inspired by the natural beauty he sees 
around him. David's paintings are shown throughout the country, 
including in many galleries and shows throughout the American West.
  It is my distinct honor to recognize Jay Contway and David Graham for 
their tremendous contributions to the arts community and their 
dedication to celebrating the people, art, and history of our great 
State.●
```
