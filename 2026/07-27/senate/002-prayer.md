# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, who has ordained the seasons of our lives, thank You for the 
steadfastness of Your mercy, grace, and love.
  Inspire our lawmakers to open themselves to the gifts of Your 
presence, power, and peace, remembering that You will never leave or 
forsake them.
  Where there is fear, give courage. Where there is anxiety, give 
peace. Where there is despair, give hope. Where there is sadness, give 
joy.
  May our Senators joyfully encounter You on a daily basis. Inspire 
them to hear Your words and obey Your commands.
  We pray in Your majestic Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
