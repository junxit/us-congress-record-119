# REMEMBERING BARBARA JOAN SALKIN

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
                    REMEMBERING BARBARA JOAN SALKIN

● Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, I rise today to pay tribute to 
the amazing life and legacy of Ms. Barbara Salkin, who passed recently 
at age 94. Ms. Salkin was a leader, organizer, and friend to all, and 
she leaves behind a resounding tradition of unity, mentorship, and 
civic engagement.

[[Page S4282]]

  Ms. Salkin was a true believer in the power of community and 
graciously dedicated her time, her knowledge, and her boundless energy 
into supporting and developing her own. She was an inextricable part of 
Montgomery County, MD, and her impact will remain woven into the fabric 
of its history. She used her unmatched understanding of the county to 
mentor generations of local and State leaders, investing in a brighter 
future for us all.
  Above all, Ms. Salkin was a beloved mother, grandmother, great-
grandmother, and friend. She blessed every person she encountered with 
the gift of her kindness, and I am grateful to have been one of those 
people. The outpouring of love since her passing is a testament to the 
impact she has had on so many.
  I invite my colleagues to join me in offering appreciation and 
condolences to Ms. Salkin's children, grandchildren, and great-
grandchildren, and in honoring her tremendous legacy.●

                          ____________________
```
