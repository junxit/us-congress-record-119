# ALLOWING EMANCIPATION HALL TO BE USED FOR A CEREMONY TO DEDICATE THE SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL CONGRESSIONAL TIME CAPSULE ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 24, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 109

```
 ALLOWING EMANCIPATION HALL TO BE USED FOR A CEREMONY TO DEDICATE THE 
 SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL CONGRESSIONAL TIME CAPSULE ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 24, 
                                  2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 109, which was 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 109) allowing 
     Emancipation Hall to be used for a ceremony to dedicate the 
     Semiquincentennial Congressional Time Capsule on Wednesday, 
     June 24, 2026.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the resolution 
be agreed to and that the motion to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 109) was agreed to.
```
