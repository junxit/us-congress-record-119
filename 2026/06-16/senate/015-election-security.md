# ELECTION SECURITY

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                           ELECTION SECURITY

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, on our Spotlight Forum, later today, 
Senator Padilla and I will hold a Spotlight Forum on State and local 
efforts to protect free and fair elections from the chaos coming out of 
the Trump administration.
  This Spotlight Forum is because the Republicans will not hold 
hearings on this important issue: protecting our elections. So we have 
our own Spotlight Forum, where people can testify, and the American 
people can learn the truth.
  As Trump gears up to interfere in our elections and discount the 
votes of the American people, Senate Democrats' Election Protection 
Task Force is preparing to protect the independence of American 
elections.
  Trump's anti-democracy agenda has left local and State election 
officials vulnerable to cyber attacks, intimidation from Trump 
loyalists, and Federal seizure of election equipment and ballots.
  Today, we are going to discuss what we can do to counter Trump's 
interference efforts and uphold the sanctity of our elections.
  Donald Trump, listen to this: You may not know it because you sure 
don't act like you know it, in America, voters pick their leaders; 
leaders don't pick their voters. And Democrats will ensure that remains 
the case in 2026.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The majority whip.

                          ____________________
```
