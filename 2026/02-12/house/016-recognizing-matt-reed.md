# RECOGNIZING MATT REED

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
                         RECOGNIZING MATT REED

  (Mr. NEWHOUSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my outgoing 
communications director, Mr. Matt Reed.
  Matt has been on my staff for 2\1/2\ years. During that time, he has 
been a crucial member of my team.
  Matt has tremendous work ethic, an infectious sense of humor, and is 
a friend to everyone in the office. From late hours in the D.C. office 
to road trips throughout central Washington, Matt has been my megaphone 
across three Congresses, and we have enjoyed every minute of it.
  As Matt departs from the House for, I am sorry to say, the upper 
Chamber, I am confident that he will succeed in his new role and that 
he will have a bright future ahead of him.

                          ____________________
```
