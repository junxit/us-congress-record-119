  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to

Communist Party missiles, and North Korean troops.
  The war continues as dictators with rule of gun invade democracies 
with rule of law: February 24, 2022, into Ukraine and October 7, 2023, 
into Israel.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies and insulting Trump and mocking Trump with 
murderous attacks this week in Ukraine, freezing civilians with no 
electricity, no heat, and no water.
