```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. SCOTT of Florida (for himself and Mrs. Moody):
       S. Res. 607. A resolution honoring the memories of the 
     victims of the senseless attack at Marjory Stoneman Douglas 
     High School on February 14, 2018; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Ms. ROSEN:
       S. Res. 608. A resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Senate that Ghislaine Maxwell should not be granted a 
     Presidential pardon or any form of clemency for her crimes 
     with Jeffrey Epstein relating to the sexual exploitation and 
     abuse of minors; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. THUNE (for himself and Mr. Schumer):
       S. Res. 609. A resolution to authorize testimony and 
     representation in United States v. Crouse; considered and 
     agreed to.
           By Mrs. MURRAY (for herself and Ms. Cantwell):
       S. Res. 610. A resolution congratulating the Seattle 
     Seahawks for winning Super Bowl LX; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. TILLIS:
       S. Res. 611. A resolution authorizing the use of the atrium 
     in the Philip A. Hart Senate Office Building for a Bipawtisan 
     Doggi Gras Pawrade on February 25, 2026, from 4:00pm to 
     6:00pm; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. WICKER (for himself and Mr. Merkley):
       S. Con. Res. 27. A concurrent resolution celebrating the 
     125th Anniversary of the Army Nurse Corps; to the Committee 
     on Armed Services.
```
