# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Lord our God, You are great, eternal, and wonderful in glory. You 
keep Your promises to those who serve You. Consecrate with Your 
presence the path that we should follow. Lord, kindle in the hearts of 
our Senators the true love of peace, the passion for Your unfolding 
providence, and the courage to trust You come what may.
  May the faith they confess with their lips put such hope in their 
hearts that they may live each day with integrity. Cleanse them from 
every thought displeasing to Your goodness, that with pure hearts, 
clear minds, and calm hope they may honor You.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
