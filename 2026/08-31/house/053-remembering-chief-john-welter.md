# REMEMBERING CHIEF JOHN WELTER

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                     REMEMBERING CHIEF JOHN WELTER

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
my very good friend, Chief John Welter, the 32nd police chief of my 
hometown, the city of Anaheim, California.
  Chief Welter made many contributions to the good people of Anaheim, 
including the creation of the department's first-ever chief advisory 
council. He also established the John Welter Family Justice Center to 
help those in need in the city of Anaheim.
  I also extend my deepest condolences to his spouse, Terri, and the 
whole Welter family in this very difficult time.
  I ask my colleagues today to join me in honoring John Welter's legacy 
of leadership, service, and his commitment to protecting the city of 
Anaheim that we call home.

                          ____________________
```
