# HONORING LIFE AND LEGACY OF KEITH ALLEN

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
                HONORING LIFE AND LEGACY OF KEITH ALLEN

  (Mr. FRY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Keith Allen of Latta, South Carolina. Keith was a treasure in his 
community and in our State, and I join those who knew him and loved him 
in mourning his passing.
  Keith was proud to be a fifth-generation farmer and considered 
himself blessed to manage Betty Allen Farms. That farm has been in the 
Allen family since 1837, and Keith was incredibly grateful for his 
roots to the land. For him, farming was more than just work. It was a 
calling and one that he was honored to step into.
  In 2022, Keith was awarded South Carolina Farm Bureau's Distinguished 
Service Award. That same year he was named South Carolina Farmer of the 
Year by Sunbelt Ag Expo. Over the years, Keith was a partner in helping 
me to understand how I could best serve the farming and agricultural 
community in our State, and for that I am incredibly grateful and 
blessed.
  I join the State in praying for Keith; his family and friends; his 
daughters, Kate and Karen; and his beloved wife, Libby. His life was 
one well-lived, and he will be remembered as a man of duty and honor.
  Rest in peace, my friend.

                          ____________________
```
