# MAIL-IN VOTING UNDER ATTACK

**Speaking:** Bynum, Janelle S. (B001326) [D-OR]

```
                              {time}  1920
                      MAIL-IN VOTING UNDER ATTACK

  (Ms. BYNUM asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. BYNUM. Mr. Speaker, mail-in voting, a system that has allowed 
millions of Americans to make their voices heard, is under attack.
  Over 99 percent of voting Oregonians cast their ballots by mail. 
These are deployed servicemembers, parents working to feed their 
families, and young Americans who are just trying to get by.
  Our own President voted by mail just this month, yet he wants to take 
away that same right from millions of Americans.
  President Trump isn't concerned about ensuring a fair and free 
election, in my opinion. He is scared that the American people will see 
right through him, scared that families and seniors and students are 
going to reject his foolishness at the ballot box.
  Mr. Speaker, grocery prices are skyrocketing, and gas prices are out 
of control. Americans cannot afford to live under Trump's economy, and 
he knows it.
  The simple truth is that Americans have a voice, and the President 
doesn't want them to use it. Mr. Speaker, we must wake up.

                          ____________________
```
