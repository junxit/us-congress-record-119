# SUPPORTING PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN PACIFIC ISLANDS FORUM AND UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Case, Ed (C001055) [D-HI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1492

```
 SUPPORTING PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN PACIFIC ISLANDS FORUM AND UNITED STATES

  (Mr. CASE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CASE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in support of H. Res. 1492, our 
bipartisan resolution reaffirming the enduring partnership between the 
Pacific Islands Forum and the United States.
  The United States is a Pacific nation. We share deep and longstanding 
ties and values with the peoples of the Pacific.
  The Pacific Islands Forum, currently 18 members strong, has served 
since 1971 as the Pacific's premier multilateral political and economic 
organization, advancing shared priorities, including regional security, 
economic development, and disaster resilience.
  The United States has partnered with the PIF for decades to pursue 
our shared goals of a peaceful, secure, resilient, and prosperous 
Pacific.
  As Pacific leaders gather in Palau for the 55th PIF leaders meeting, 
we wish the PIF continued success and express Congress' firm resolve 
that the United States will remain a strong, reliable, and active 
partner in the Blue Pacific.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to support and cosponsor our 
resolution.

                          ____________________
```
