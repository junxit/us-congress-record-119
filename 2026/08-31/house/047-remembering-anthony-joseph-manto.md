# REMEMBERING ANTHONY JOSEPH MANTO

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                    REMEMBERING ANTHONY JOSEPH MANTO

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on the floor of the U.S. House 
of Representatives to pay tribute to Anthony Joseph Manto, a Rye 
resident and neighbor whom we lost a few days ago at the age of 83.
  Tony was the consummate good guy, and for many years a dedicated 
family man, married to his wife, Claire, for over 50 years; father to 
Gina, Anthony, and Chrissy; and grandfather to five grandchildren.
  He served at Con Edison for decades, starting from the manhole up to 
the corporate offices. He coupled family and career with extensive 
community service, with POTS, Meals on Wheels, Helping Hands, and other 
groups.
  He served his country in the U.S. Army from 1965 to 1967, serving a 
stint in Vietnam. A Queens native, he graduated from Queens College and 
made his life in Westchester.
  Tony was the life of the party at every party on Adelaide Street, at 
Playland, and elsewhere. He was a warm, friendly, well-traveled man, 
and he was loved by all.
  We will miss Tony, and we remember him on this day.

                          ____________________
```
