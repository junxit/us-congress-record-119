# HATE HAS NO PLACE HERE

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Adelita S. (G000606) [D-AZ]

```
                         HATE HAS NO PLACE HERE

  (Mrs. GRIJALVA asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to remember the victims of a 
shooting that occurred outside of the Venture-N bar in Tucson, Arizona, 
last night--Vincent Siqueiros and Cameron Capara--and to demand that 
this Chamber take immediate action to ensure that this can never happen 
again.
  I am disgusted by the targeted act of hate and the cold-blooded 
murder of two members of our LGBTQIA+ community. This horrific attack 
is a painful reminder that even in welcoming communities like Tucson, 
hate and violence against our LGBTQIA+ community still exists.
  When the hate is combined with easy access to firearms, the 
consequences can be deadly. I am sick and tired of Congress refusing to 
lift a finger to protect our communities while every day dozens of 
people are shot and killed across this country. It does not have to be 
this way.
  The House of Representatives is returning to session this week, and 
our first action should be passing commonsense gun safety reforms that 
we know can save lives.

[[Page H5350]]

  In Tucson, we must come together as one city, mourn alongside victims 
and families and friends, and stand with our LGBTQIA+ community, saying 
in the most unconditional terms: Everyone is welcome in Tucson, and 
hate has no place here.

                          ____________________
```
