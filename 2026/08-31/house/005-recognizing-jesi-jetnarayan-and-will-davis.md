# RECOGNIZING JESI JETNARAYAN AND WILL DAVIS

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
               RECOGNIZING JESI JETNARAYAN AND WILL DAVIS

  (Mr. Joyce of Pennsylvania was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize 
high school students Jesi Jetnarayan and Will Davis of Franklin County 
for achieving a special distinction during their successful trek to the 
summit of Mount Kilimanjaro.
  By reaching the over 19,000-foot summit of Mount Kilimanjaro on 
August 1, Jesi and Will are believed to be the youngest high school 
students in the history of Franklin County to achieve this remarkable 
feat.
  Both seniors this year at Shalom Christian Academy, this achievement 
is a testament to Jesi and Will's spirit of adventure, personal 
endurance, and will to succeed.
  The team of hikers that accompanied Jesi and Will on their climb were 
their fathers, Vishal Jetnarayan and Ben Davis.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the constituents throughout Franklin County 
and the entire 13th Congressional District, I congratulate Jesi and 
Will on this outstanding achievement and express best wishes as they 
climb future mountains.


                 Recognizing Morrisons Cove Rotary Club

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, it is with respect and 
admiration I rise today to recognize the Morrisons Cove Rotary Club for 
100 years of service to the community.
  Founded in 1926, the organization is part of an international network 
of clubs that is dedicated to humanitarian service and community 
support.
  Over the years, support from the Morrisons Cove Rotary Club has 
benefited numerous community organizations as well as local school 
districts.
  It has been estimated that the club has contributed more than $1.5 
million to community projects.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of everyone in Pennsylvania's 13th 
Congressional District, I express gratitude to all former and current 
members of the Morrisons Cove Rotary Club.


                       Recognizing Lisa Kozorosky

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, it is with gratitude that I 
rise to recognize the public service of Lisa Kozorosky, who recently 
retired as Cambria County treasurer.
  Lisa was employed by the county for 26 years, including the past 14 
in the elected position of treasurer. She also served as a member of 
the Cambria County Retirement Board.
  While acknowledging her vital role in county government, colleagues 
have praised Lisa for her outstanding work ethic and her dedication.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of constituents throughout the 13th 
Congressional District, I thank Lisa for her faithful public service 
for the residents of Cambria County and express best wishes for a 
wonderful retirement.


                    Recognizing William Swartzwelder

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, it is with deep appreciation 
that I rise to recognize William Swartzwelder of Breezewood for an 
extraordinary record of public service that spans over seven decades.
  As a volunteer firefighter, Bill followed the example of his parents 
and joined the Breezewood Volunteer Fire Company in 1956. He has filled 
a variety of leadership roles over the years.
  A regular participant in Monday night work sessions, fundraising 
activities, and other fire company-related functions, Bill, actually to 
this date, continues to attend monthly meetings, where he serves on 
various committees.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the constituents throughout Bedford County 
and the entire 13th Congressional District, I personally thank Bill for 
his outstanding commitment to the Breezewood Volunteer Fire Company and 
congratulate him on this remarkable milestone of public service.
```
