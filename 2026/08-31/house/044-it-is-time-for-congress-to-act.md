# IT IS TIME FOR CONGRESS TO ACT

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Mike (K000403) [R-UT]

```
                     IT IS TIME FOR CONGRESS TO ACT

  (Mr. KENNEDY of Utah asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. KENNEDY of Utah. Mr. Speaker, last week Meta reached a $12.2 
billion settlement and agreed to implement new safety guardrails in 
youth protections.
  We have all witnessed the harms caused by an unchecked social media 
algorithm: anxiety and depression, lost sleep, and even a lost 
childhood. An entire generation of young people are struggling under 
the weight of a technology engineered to capture their attention and 
hold it for as long as possible.
  I thank Utah Attorney General Derek Brown for helping to lead us in 
this fight. This is a landmark win, but its reach is limited. It is one 
company. It does not cover the next platform. It does not cover the 
next algorithm. Most importantly, it does not guarantee protection for 
the next child.
  Congress needs to act to implement lasting changes. Congress has a 
responsibility to set one clear standard that applies to every platform 
before the harm happens.
  That is what my Algorithm Accountability Act does. It strips section 
230 immunity from platforms that violate a clear duty of care to their 
users, and it gives families, not just State attorneys general, the 
ability to hold these companies accountable in Federal court.
  It is time for Congress to act.

                          ____________________
```
