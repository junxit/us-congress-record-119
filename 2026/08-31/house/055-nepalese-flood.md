# NEPALESE FLOOD

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

```
                             NEPALESE FLOOD

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, still I rise, and I rise tonight on 
behalf of the community in Houston, Texas, where the Nepalese community 
resides. They have suffered a great disaster in their country, and we 
have been helping. We provided some millions of dollars. However, the 
amount of damage, the number of lives lost, and the help that will be 
needed will far exceed what they have received.
  I am just hoping that our country will be able to do more. This is a 
time when what we stand for globally can mean something in the lives of 
the people of Nepal.
  I pray that they will receive the help they need.

                          ____________________
```
