# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10189

**Measures:** H.R. 10189

```
           By Ms. JACOBS:
       H.R. 10189.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which grants Congress the power to provide for the common 
     defense and to make rules for the government and regulation 
     of the armed forces.
```
