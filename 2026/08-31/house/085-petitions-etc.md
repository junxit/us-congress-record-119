# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-19. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Miami-Dade Board 
     of County Commissioners, relative to Resolution No. R-439-26, 
     expressing the Board's intent to acknowledge May 20th as 
     Cuban Independence Day and to condemn the Cuban government's 
     many human rights abuses; and urging and calling on President 
     Donald J. Trump and the federal government to continue to 
     take all necessary steps to stand against these abuses; which 
     was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
