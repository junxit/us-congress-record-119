# RECOGNIZING INTERNATIONAL OVERDOSE AWARENESS DAY

**Speaking:** Bresnahan, Robert P. (B001327) [R-PA]

```
            RECOGNIZING INTERNATIONAL OVERDOSE AWARENESS DAY

  (Mr. BRESNAHAN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BRESNAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize International 
Overdose Awareness Day.
  Today, we remember the hundreds of thousands of Americans who have 
lost their lives to overdose, and we remember the families, friends, 
and the communities left behind.
  However, these aren't statistics. They are our neighbors, our family 
members, and our friends. No race, gender, ZIP Code, or family is 
immune from this epidemic.
  International Overdose Awareness Day is a chance to remember those we 
have lost, stand with those in recovery, and recognize the people on 
the front lines who are working to save lives.
  We have a responsibility to do everything we can to stop these deadly 
drugs from reaching our communities, hold those who traffic them 
accountable, and make sure people struggling with addiction can get the 
help that they need.
  Today, we remember those who were taken from this world far too soon, 
and we recommit ourselves to making sure fewer families experience this 
kind of pain.

                          ____________________
```
