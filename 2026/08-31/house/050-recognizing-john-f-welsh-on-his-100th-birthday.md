# RECOGNIZING JOHN F. WELSH ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
            RECOGNIZING JOHN F. WELSH ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. ALLEN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize John F. Welsh, 
better known as Jack, an American patriot and World War II combat 
veteran who will celebrate his 100th birthday this Friday, September 4, 
2026.
  Born to a second-generation Georgia timberman, Jack is no stranger to 
hard work and sacrifice. Upon graduating from high school on June 6, 
1944, D-day, of all days, Jack asked his parents to enlist in the Army, 
entering basic training later that fall.
  Through his service, Jack would go on to become a decorated hero. He 
was awarded the Asiatic-Pacific Theater Campaign Ribbon with two Bronze 
Stars, Good Conduct Medal, Philippine Liberation Ribbon with one Bronze 
Star, Army of Occupation Ribbon, Victory Ribbon, and three Overseas 
Service Bars.
  After discharge, this battle-tested veteran earned a degree in 
forestry, met the love of his life, and joined the U.S. Forest Service 
where he worked for 35 memorable years.
  When we speak of the Greatest Generation, we are speaking of American 
heroes like Jack Welsh.
  My wife, Robin, and I wish him the happiest of birthdays.

                          ____________________
```
