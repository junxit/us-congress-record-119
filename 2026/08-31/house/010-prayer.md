# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Rabbi Shlomo Litvin, Chabad of the Bluegrass, Lexington, 
Kentucky, offered the following prayer:
  O God and God of our fathers, great, mighty, and revered.
  God, we come before You today as this honorable body reconvenes in 
the 250th year of our exceptional Nation, which was conceived in 
liberty and founded on the belief in a moral creator.
  On the Jewish calendar, today is Chai, or the 18th of Elul, the 
birthday of two foundational Jewish leaders, the Baal Shem Tov and the 
Baal HaTanya Zalman of Liadi, who spread wellsprings of Godly light 
across the world, carried on here in America by Lubavitcher Rebbe, 
Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson.
  God, I invoke Your blessing today upon this body, the House of 
Representatives of the United States of America, who gather today in 
observance of one of Your seven Noahide universal laws for all mankind 
to establish just statutes.
  Strengthen them to uphold America's values, to stand against 
terrorism and evil, to proclaim liberty throughout the land, to spread 
wellsprings of light as the Rebbe taught, immediately to repentance, 
immediately to redemption, from sea to shining sea and beyond until the 
promised era of true liberty.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
