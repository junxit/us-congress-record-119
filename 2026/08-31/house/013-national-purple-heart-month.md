# NATIONAL PURPLE HEART MONTH

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                      NATIONAL PURPLE HEART MONTH

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
National Purple Heart Month, a dedicated time to express our gratitude 
to the brave men and women who have sacrificed blood, body, and mind.
  This month serves as a reminder that our freedom is thanks to those 
who served and those who died in uniform.
  Earlier this month, on August 7, which is Purple Heart Day, I 
attended a memorial in Union County to recognize our wounded warriors 
who have received the Purple Heart.
  The Purple Heart, inscribed with the phrase ``For Military Merit'' 
represents the courage of these servicemembers and is a direct 
reflection of the bravery of an estimated 1.8 million men and women who 
have received this distinction.
  As a proud Army dad of a former soldier who has a Purple Heart, I 
know the sacrifices our military members and their families make to 
protect our flag. This is why I am proud to support the Purple Heart 
Foundation, an incredible organization that puts our veterans first.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank our Nation's veterans for the commitment to 
country. God bless our veterans, and God bless the United States.

                          ____________________
```
