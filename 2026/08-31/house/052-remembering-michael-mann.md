# REMEMBERING MICHAEL MANN

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                              {time}  1930
                        REMEMBERING MICHAEL MANN

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of 
Michael Mann, a devoted husband, father, and servant leader who passed 
away on July 28 after a courageous 2-year battle with cancer.
  Michael dedicated more than 16 years to Chick-fil-A of Athens. He 
began as a team member in 2010 and most recently led as the director of 
development.
  He led with humility. He served without hesitation, and he believed 
leadership meant putting others first. He challenged people to grow. He 
celebrated their successes, and never stopped encouraging those around 
him.
  Michael served faithfully as an elder at Cleveland Road Baptist 
Church, as his greatest desire was to be with Christ, sharing his love 
for Him with his church family and community.
  He is survived by his wife of nearly 10 years, Stephanie, and their 
two sons, Estiven and Thomas.
  Michael's legacy lives on through his family, his church, his Chick-
fil-A family, and his vast community of friends.
```
