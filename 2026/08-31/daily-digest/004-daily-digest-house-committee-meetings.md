# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Res. 1490, H.R. 1501, H.R. 4795, H.R. 9436

```
Committee Meetings
PROTECTING DOMESTIC MINING ACT OF 2025; TO AMEND THE CONSOLIDATED 
APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2023 TO EXTEND THE TIME PERIOD FOR WHICH CERTAIN 
REGULATIONS CONCERNING THE NORTH ATLANTIC RIGHT WHALE ARE EFFECTIVE; 
PROTECT ECONOMIC AND ACADEMIC FREEDOM ACT OF 2026; PROVIDING FOR THE 
CONDEMNATION AND DENOUNCEMENT OF SOCIALISM IN ALL ITS FORMS
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 1501, the 
``Protecting Domestic Mining Act of 2025''; H.R. 9436, to amend the 
Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 to extend the time period for 
which certain regulations concerning the North Atlantic right whale are 
effective [Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act of 2026]; H.R. 4795, the 
``Protect Economic and Academic Freedom Act of

[[Page D849]]

2026''; and H. Res. 1490, providing for the condemnation and 
denouncement of socialism in all its forms, and for other purposes. The 
Committee granted, by a record vote of 7-4, a rule providing for 
consideration of H.R. 1501, the ``Protecting Domestic Mining Act of 
2025'', H.R. 9436, the ``Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act of 2026'', 
H. Res. 1490, Providing for the condemnation and denouncement of 
socialism in all its forms, and for other purposes, and H.R. 4795, the 
``Protect Economic and Academic Freedom Act of 2026''. The rule 
provides for consideration of H.R. 1501, the ``Protecting Domestic 
Mining Act of 2025'', under a closed rule. The rule waives all points 
of order against consideration of the bill. The rule provides that the 
amendment in the nature of a substitute recommended by the Committee on 
Natural Resources now printed in the bill shall be considered as 
adopted and the bill, as amended, shall be considered as read. The rule 
waives all points of order against provisions in the bill, as amended. 
The rule provides one hour of general debate equally divided and 
controlled by the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on 
Natural Resources or their respective designees. The rule provides one 
motion to recommit. The rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 
9436, the ``Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act of 2026'', under a 
closed rule. The rule waives all points of order against consideration 
of the bill. The rule provides that, in lieu of the amendment in the 
nature of a substitute recommended by the Committee on Natural 
Resources now printed in the bill, an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute consisting of the text of Rules Committee Print 119-40 shall 
be considered as adopted and the bill, as amended, shall be considered 
as read. The rule waives all points of order against provisions in the 
bill, as amended. The rule provides one hour of general debate equally 
divided and controlled by the chair and ranking minority member of the 
Committee on Natural Resources or their respective designees. The rule 
provides one motion to recommit. The rule further provides for 
consideration of H. Res. 1490, Providing for the condemnation and 
denouncement of socialism in all its forms, and for other purposes, 
under a closed rule. The rule provides that upon adoption of the 
resolution it shall be in order without intervention of any point of 
order to consider H. Res. 1490. The rule provides that H. Res. 1490 
shall be considered as read. The rule provides one hour of general 
debate equally divided and controlled by the chair and ranking minority 
member of the Committee on House Administration or their respective 
designees. The rule further provides for consideration of H.R. 4795, 
the ``Protect Economic and Academic Freedom Act of 2026'', under a 
closed rule. The rule waives all points of order against consideration 
of the bill. The rule provides that, in lieu of the amendment in the 
nature of a substitute recommended by the Committee on Education and 
Workforce now printed in the bill, an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute consisting of the text of Rules Committee Print 119-39 shall 
be considered as adopted and the bill, as amended, shall be considered 
as read. The rule waives all points of order against provisions in the 
bill, as amended. The rule provides one hour of general debate equally 
divided and controlled by the chair and ranking minority member of the 
Committee on Education and Workforce or their respective designees. The 
rule provides one motion to recommit. Finally, the rule provides that 
debate on motions that the House suspend the rules, under clause 1 of 
rule 15, relating to House Joint Resolution, shall be extended to one 
hour. Testimony was heard from Chairman Westerman, Chairman Steil, and 
Representatives Huffman, Wilson of South Carolina, Scott of Virginia, 
Morelle, and Moylan.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
