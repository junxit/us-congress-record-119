# HONORING BAILA VENEZUELA'S COMMUNITY SUPPORT FOR EARTHQUAKE VICTIMS IN CARACAS

**Speaking:** Omar, Ilhan (O000173) [D-MN]

```
HONORING BAILA VENEZUELA'S COMMUNITY SUPPORT FOR EARTHQUAKE VICTIMS IN 
                                CARACAS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ILHAN OMAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the countless Minnesotans 
who have dedicated their time and resources to helping those impacted 
by severe earthquakes in Venezuela, which took place in June 2026, and 
Baila Venezuela, a dance organization based in Richfield, Minnesota, 
which quickly acted and made a call for donations to support 
Venezuelans. According to initial estimates by the International 
Organization for Migration, up to 6.76 million Venezuelans may be 
affected by this natural disaster and the need for humanitarian aid is 
immediate.
  As many Minnesotans have family and friends in Venezuela who have 
been out of reach, organizations like Baila Venezuela are working 
tirelessly to collect and transport humanitarian aid supplies. While 
typically a dance group, Baila Venezuela shifted gears temporarily to 
help those in need, even from more than 3,000 miles away. What was 
initially intended to be an organizational initiative became a larger 
community response. Baila Venezuela collected over 220 boxes of non-
perishable supplies such as medicine, diapers, baby supplements, and 
much more, to be delivered to Miami and then Caracas, Venezuela. In the 
words of Baila Venezuela's leader, Roiny Rojas, ``We don't just show up 
to celebrate Venezuela's wins, we also show solidarity during difficult 
times.''
  As the death toll continued to rise and the dancers wait to hear from 
their loved ones, their commitment to aiding extended beyond their 
initial estimates and the donations received exceeded their space, 
leading them to sleep outside in the parking lot to ensure that these 
resources remained secure as they awaited shipment to Miami.
  This demonstrates to the world who we are and what we value as 
Minnesotans: generosity, community, and unwavering commitment to help 
those in need. We realize that no matter if you help your neighbor 
across the street or someone several thousand miles away, we share a 
common humanity that calls us to action. It is my great honor to pay 
tribute to Baila Venezuela and the many individuals dedicating their 
time and resources to support Venezuelans facing the brunt of climate 
change abroad. Minnesotans have shown us that there is no limit to 
compassion and willingness to help those who are in need. Our community 
responds to these issues, not because they are easy, but because it is 
the right thing to do. I am deeply proud to represent a community that 
values supporting those in need.

                          ____________________
```
