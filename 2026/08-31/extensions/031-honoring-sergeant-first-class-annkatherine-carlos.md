# HONORING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS ANNKATHERINE CARLOS

**Speaking:** Moylan, James C. (M001219) [R-GU]

```
           HONORING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS ANNKATHERINE CARLOS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES C. MOYLAN

                                of guam

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. MOYLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a woman whose life 
embodies the values of duty, perseverance, and selfless service--
Sergeant First Class Annkatherine Carlos of the United States Army.
  Raised on the island of Guam, Sergeant First Class Carlos has carried 
the strength and spirit of our island with her throughout a 
distinguished military career spanning nearly two decades.
  In 2006, shortly after graduating from high school, she made the 
decision to answer our Nation's call by enlisting in the United States 
Army.
  Throughout her military service, Sergeant First Class Carlos has 
taken on increasingly demanding assignments across the globe. From 
South Korea to Iraq, from Qatar to Afghanistan, and today serving on 
the Joint Staff at the Pentagon, she has consistently demonstrated 
exceptional leadership and professionalism in some of our Nation's most 
critical missions.
  With every new assignment came greater responsibility. Whether 
serving overseas, supporting combat operations, or advising senior 
military leaders, she has consistently earned the trust of those around 
her.
  She has supported Operations Iraqi Freedom, Operation Inherent 
Resolve, Operation

[[Page E823]]

Allies Refuge, and Operation Allies Welcome--missions that required 
courage during some of the most challenging moments facing our Armed 
Forces. In every role, she has demonstrated the professionalism and 
resolve that our servicemembers exemplify every day in defense of our 
Nation.
  As a Human Resources professional, her work may often take place 
behind the scenes, but anyone who has served in uniform understands 
that mission success depends on the Soldiers who ensure our forces are 
supported and cared for. Sergeant First Class Carlos has dedicated her 
career to taking care of America's warfighters, allowing them to focus 
on accomplishing the mission. It is often said that leaders take care 
of people first, because people accomplish the mission. Sergeant First 
Class Carlos has lived that principle throughout her career.
  Her remarkable record of achievement reflects that commitment. She 
has earned two Defense Meritorious Service Medals, five Army 
Commendation Medals, multiple Joint Service and Army Achievement 
Medals, a Combat Action Badge, and numerous other decorations 
recognizing both her leadership and her service in combat and joint 
operations.
  Yet what makes Sergeant First Class Carlos truly exceptional extends 
beyond the medals she wears.
  She has continued to invest in herself through higher education, 
earning a Bachelor of Science in Business Management from Post 
University and completing Yale University's Connected Leadership 
certification. She has also completed extensive military professional 
education throughout her career, demonstrating that true leaders never 
stop learning and never stop striving to improve.
  Leadership is not measured simply by rank or responsibility, but by 
the willingness to invest in others. Sergeant First Class Carlos has 
embraced that responsibility throughout her career, mentoring fellow 
Soldiers and helping pave the way for future generations of women in 
uniform. Through organizations such as the Women's Veterans Memorial, 
Sisters-in-Arms, and the Special Operations Spartan Women's Leadership 
Initiative, she continues to inspire others to lead with purpose. Her 
influence extends well beyond her own assignments and will continue to 
be felt through the many servicemembers she has encouraged and 
mentored.
  Behind every Soldier is a family that shares in the sacrifices of 
military service. Sergeant First Class Carlos is also a devoted mother 
to her two children, Aliyanah and Tyler. Like so many military 
families, they have experienced frequent moves, long separations, and 
the unique challenges that accompany a life of service. Their support 
has been an essential part of her success, and they deserve our 
gratitude as well. And of course, no family would be complete without 
mentioning their beloved dog, Tofu.
  For the people of Guam, Sergeant First Class Carlos represents 
something deeply meaningful. Our island has long answered our Nation's 
call to serve. Despite our small size, Guam has consistently produced 
men and women who have answered the call to defend our Nation with 
unwavering dedication. Across every generation and every branch of 
service, the people of Guam have proudly worn the uniform, making 
sacrifices far beyond what many realize. Sergeant First Class Carlos 
proudly carries on that proud tradition. From the villages of Guam to 
the Pentagon, she has represented our island with distinction and 
serves as an inspiration to young people who aspire to wear the uniform 
and serve something greater than themselves.
  On behalf of the people of Guam, I offer our heartfelt gratitude for 
her faithful service to our Nation. We are immensely proud to call her 
one of Guam's own, and we thank her for representing our island with 
honor, integrity, and distinction.
  May God continue to bless Sergeant First Class Annkatherine Carlos, 
her family, the men and women of our Armed Forces, Guam, and the United 
States of America.
```
