# HONORING COMMANDER JOHN JAMES BATTISTI

**Speaking:** Beyer, Donald S. (B001292) [D-VA]

```
                 HONORING COMMANDER JOHN JAMES BATTISTI

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. DONALD S. BEYER, JR.

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Commander John 
James Battisti for his 20 years of outstanding Naval service as an 
active-duty judge advocate. During the past three years, Commander 
Battisti has served as the Deputy Director of Legislation and Policy 
for the Department of the Navy's Office of Legislative Affairs, where 
he was responsible for managing all aspects of the Navy's participation 
in the National Defense Authorization Act legislative process. Prior to 
this assignment, he served as the principal Legal Advisor to the 
Director of the Navy Staff, where he was responsible for the legal 
oversight of all initiatives generated by Navy commands throughout the 
world for decision by either the Secretary of the Navy or Chief of 
Naval Operations.
  Commander Battisti began his distinguished career with the Department 
of the Navy as a defense counsel at Naval Legal Service Office, Mid 
Atlantic. His assignments included serving as Assistant Fleet Judge 
Advocate to Commander, U.S. Seventh Fleet aboard USS Blue Ridge (LCC-
19); Assistant Officer-in-Charge of Naval Legal Service Office Pearl 
Harbor; Assistant Fleet Judge Advocate to Commander, U.S. Pacific 
Fleet; Branch Head within the Office of the Judge Advocate General's 
Administrative Law Division; Master of Laws student at Georgetown 
University Law Center; and Region Environmental Counsel to Commander, 
Navy Region Southwest.
  Mr. Speaker, for these reasons, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
expressing our deepest gratitude to Commander John James Battisti for 
his exemplary dedication to our Nation as he retires from Naval 
service. Throughout his 20 years of public service, Commander Battisti 
has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to the defense of our Nation 
and the safety of our service members. His profound patriotism, stellar 
leadership, and tireless work ethic leave an enduring legacy that has 
strengthened our National Defense.
```
