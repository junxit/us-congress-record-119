# CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF TRI CITY HEALTH PARTNERSHIP

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
    CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF TRI CITY HEALTH PARTNERSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tri City Health 
Partnership as it celebrates its 25th anniversary of providing 
compassionate, high-quality health care to residents of Central Kane 
County.
  Since opening its doors in 2001, Tri City Health Partnership has 
played an indispensable role in our community by ensuring that 
uninsured individuals and families have access to the medical and 
dental services they need.
  Tri City was founded on a simple but powerful principle that no one 
should have to go without basic health care simply because they cannot 
afford it. Its mission is to provide quality health care in an 
environment of mutual respect while promoting better health choices 
through education and preventive care.
  That mission is reflected in the breadth of services Tri City 
provides. Its volunteer physicians, dentists, health care 
professionals, and dedicated staff help patients receive preventive and 
ongoing care, including medical examinations, dental services, chronic 
disease management, screenings, medications, and other essential 
services. By helping patients address health concerns early, Tri City 
works to prevent manageable conditions from becoming serious or life-
threatening illnesses.
  Tri City Health Partnership provides more than health care. It 
provides dignity, compassion, and peace of mind. Patients who walk 
through its doors can receive high-quality care in an environment where 
they are treated with respect and without the fear of overwhelming 
medical bills. For people who have struggled to navigate our health 
care system, that welcoming environment can be life-changing.
  Over its 25 years of service, Tri City Health Partnership has become 
a trusted institution in Central Kane County. Its impact can be 
measured not only in the thousands of medical and dental visits it has 
provided, but also in the healthier families, prevented illnesses, and 
improved quality of life made possible through its work.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to have Tri City Health Partnership in my 
district and grateful for its 25 years of service to the people of Kane 
County. I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
organization's staff, volunteers, supporters, and everyone who has 
contributed to its remarkable legacy.
```
