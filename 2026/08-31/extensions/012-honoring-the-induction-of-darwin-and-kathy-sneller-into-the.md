# HONORING THE INDUCTION OF DARWIN AND KATHY SNELLER INTO THE MICHIGAN FARMERS HALL OF FAME

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 HONORING THE INDUCTION OF DARWIN AND KATHY SNELLER INTO THE MICHIGAN 
                          FARMERS HALL OF FAME

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Darwin 
and Kathy Sneller of Sebewaing, Michigan, on their induction into the 
2026 Michigan Farmers Hall of Fame. For more than 50 years, the 
Snellers have dedicated their lives to farming, family, and investment 
in Michigan's agricultural community.
  At Starward Farms, Darwin and Kathy built a thriving operation while 
carrying forward the family traditions that have guided them throughout 
their careers. Their stewardship of their dairy herd and the land they 
farm has earned them recognition, including 34 consecutive Holstein USA 
Progressive Breeder Awards.
  The Snellers also place family and community at the heart of their 
work. They farm alongside their son and have opened their doors to 
dairy interns and students, providing valuable hands-on experience and 
a deeper appreciation for the hard work that sustains Michigan 
agriculture.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Darwin 
and Kathy Sneller on their induction into the 2026 Michigan Farmers 
Hall of Fame. They exemplify the very best of Michigan agriculture 
through their deep commitment to family, hard work, and stewardship of 
the land. I am proud to honor their remarkable contributions to our 
state and the agricultural community.
```
