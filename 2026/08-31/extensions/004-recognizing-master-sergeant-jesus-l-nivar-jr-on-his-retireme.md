# RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT JESUS L. NIVAR, JR. ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT JESUS L. NIVAR, JR. ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM 
                     THE UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize United States Marine 
Corps Master Sergeant (MSgt) Jesus L. Nivar, Jr. on his retirement 
after 22 years of honorable and dedicated service to our Nation. This 
well-deserved honor reflects the professionalism, discipline, and 
leadership expected of every Marine.
  A native of Santa Domingo, Dominican Republic, MSgt Nivar, Jr. began 
his career with the Marine Corps in August of 2004 when he attended 
recruit training in Parris Island, South Carolina. Upon completion of 
his training, he quickly rose the ranks from Private First Class to 
Sergeant before being deployed to Afghanistan in support of Operation 
Enduring Freedom in April of 2011.
  Following his deployment, MSgt Nivar, Jr. reported to Camp Lejeune, 
North Carolina, and served with the 2nd Law Enforcement Battalion and 
2nd Marine Logistics group.

[[Page E816]]

There he was trusted to manage personnel and support deployed forces, 
leading to a well-deserved promotion to Staff Sergeant. As a result, he 
was subsequently deployed to serve as the Administrative Chief for the 
Command Element of the Combined Join Task force in the Horn of Africa 
until November 2013.
  In the years following his deployment to the Horn of Africa, MSgt 
Nivar, Jr. continued to earn positions with increasing responsibility. 
In November of 2014, he received orders to serve as the Administrative 
Chief and Funeral Honors Staff Non-Commissioned Officer in Charge at 
Quantico, Virgina and was later transferred to the Marine and Family 
Programs to serve as the Administrative Chief. He later provided 
administrative leadership and inspection oversight at Headquarters, 1st 
Marine Corps District, and was promoted to Master Sergeant in 2021 
while serving with Marine Aviation Logistics Squadron 24. In his most 
recent position, he served as Adjutant Chief with the Marine Corps 
Embassy Security Group.
  Throughout his distinguished military career, MSgt Nivar, Jr. has 
earned numerous personal decorations, including the Joint Service 
Commendation Medal, four awards of the Navy and Marine Corps 
Commendation Medal, four awards of the Navy and Marine Corps 
Achievement Medal, and numerous additional service and campaign medals.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Master Sergeant Jesus L. 
Nivar, Jr. on his retirement from the United States Marine Corps. His 
humility, work ethic, and unwavering dedication to his fellow Marines 
embody the very best of what it means to serve this great Nation.
```
