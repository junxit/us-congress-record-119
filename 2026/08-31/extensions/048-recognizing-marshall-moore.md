# RECOGNIZING MARSHALL MOORE

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MARSHALL MOORE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Marshall Moore 
for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Young Professional 
Leadership Award.
  The Young Professional Leadership Award recognizes a young 
professional under the age of 40 whose leadership and dedication have 
impacted the world around them. Marshall is enthusiastic about creating 
lasting financial confidence and community momentum. As the Director of 
Community Impact and Financial Well-being at On Tap Credit Union, 
Marshall has forged 12 partnerships, delivered 100+ speaking 
engagements, and launched the award-winning ``Making Money Joyful'' 
program for 150 plus participants, for which he received two Credit 
Union Diamond Awards.
  He is also an active community member, having presented on the 
importance of financial wellness to the Arvada Chamber of Commerce, 
local high schools, Red Rocks Community College, and Jeffco teachers.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Marshall Moore for this award and thank him 
for his commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
