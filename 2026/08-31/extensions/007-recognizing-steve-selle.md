# RECOGNIZING STEVE SELLE

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING STEVE SELLE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Steve Selle for 
receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Image Award.
  Image Award winners are selected for their commitment to Arvada and 
the overall positive image they portray within the community. As a 
longtime member of the Lake Arbor Optimists Club, Steve has changed 
countless lives and brought a sorely-needed positive outlook to

[[Page E817]]

our community. He also founded the Optimists Bicycle Program, donating 
over 1,200 bicycles to schools, churches, nonprofits, and community 
organizations throughout Jefferson and Adams Counties. He also helped 
launch the annual Arvada Bicycle Recycling Day, which has given more 
than 800 bicycles a second life.
  For over 37 years, Steve has made education possible for young 
Coloradans, serving as a principal and teacher in multiple school 
districts before retiring in 2008. Through service as an educator, 
advocate, and a volunteer with multiple groups, Steve embodies the best 
of Arvada--a place where community, service, and humanity thrive.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Steve Selle for this award and thank him 
for his commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
