# RECOGNIZING LESLIE FEUERBORN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING LESLIE FEUERBORN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Leslie 
Feuerborn for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Image 
Award.
  Image Award winners are selected for their commitment to Arvada and 
the overall positive image they portray within the community. Through 
40 years as a program manager in health promotion, 500 volunteer hours 
each year dedicated to nonprofit service, and weekly readings with 
elementary students, Leslie tirelessly uplifts her community through 
action. As the treasurer for The Arvada Jefferson Kiwanis Club and 
Foundation, she supports initiatives such as Sweet Dreams in a Bag, 
which provides bedding and comfort items to children in need.
  Leslie is a behind-the-scenes force for good, quietly coordinating, 
tracking details, and following through so that every project--from 
fundraising to service days--has the structure it needs to succeed. For 
her unwavering service, conscious compassion, and profound impact on 
her environment, Leslie truly embodies the spirit of Arvada and the 
Image Award.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Leslie Feuerborn for this award and thank 
her for her commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
