# HONORING AUGUST CACCAVONE ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Gottheimer, Josh (G000583) [D-NJ]

```
            HONORING AUGUST CACCAVONE ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOSH GOTTHEIMER

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. GOTTHEIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor August Caccavone, 
who celebrated his 100th birthday on August 12, 2026.
  August Caccavone is a retired U.S. Army Sergeant, a lifelong resident 
of Fort Lee and Hackensack, and a proud World War II veteran who has 
dedicated his life to serving his country and community.
  August is a survivor of the Battle of the Bulge who served as a 
Sergeant in the 7th Army, was awarded the Bronze Star for destroying 
two German machine guns, and later served in the Philippines, and with 
the 8th Army in Japan, exemplifying extraordinary courage and 
dedication in defense of our Nation.
  August remains an active member of VFW Post 2342, where he continues 
to support and uplift fellow veterans with kindness and good humor; and 
where he greets neighbors, shares his enduring love of life, and serves 
as a living symbol of perseverance, strength, and selfless service.
```
