# RECOGNIZING ANDREW HEESACKER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING ANDREW HEESACKER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Andrew 
Heesacker for receiving

[[Page E827]]

the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Man of the Year Award.
  The title of Man of the Year is awarded to an exceptional individual 
who demonstrates extraordinary community involvement, generosity, and 
overall impact on Arvada. Andrew's passion for serving others through 
leadership and integrity has made him a standout citizen in Arvada's 
business and civic life.
  As the owner of Arvada Rent-Alls, he uplifts his neighbors and 
supports important causes through donating volunteers and equipment for 
events and fundraisers throughout the city. He also serves our 
community through his work on the Greater Arvada Chamber Board of 
Directors and his volunteer work with Team Rubicon, the National 
Western Stock Show, and Hope, Connection & Community.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Andrew Heesacker on receiving the 2025 Man 
of the Year Award and thank him for his contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
