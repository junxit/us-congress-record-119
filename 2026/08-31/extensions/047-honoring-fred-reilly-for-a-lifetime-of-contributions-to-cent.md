# HONORING FRED REILLY FOR A LIFETIME OF CONTRIBUTIONS TO CENTRAL FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING FRED REILLY FOR A LIFETIME OF CONTRIBUTIONS TO CENTRAL FLORIDA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Fred Reilly for a Lifetime of 
Contributions to Central Florida. Fred Reilly was a distinguished 
attorney, international legal professional, dedicated public servant, 
and longtime member of the Haines City community. A graduate of Haines 
City High School, Fred built an accomplished legal career while 
remaining deeply connected to the community he called home.
  Fred earned a Bachelor of Science in Management from Purdue 
University, a Juris Doctor from Cumberland School of Law at Samford 
University, and a Master of Laws (LL.M.) in International Business Law 
from the London School of Economics and Political Science. He also 
served as an Associate Editor of The Cumberland Law Review.
  His legal career extended nationally and internationally. Fred was 
admitted to practice law in Florida, California, and the District of 
Columbia and as a Solicitor of the United Kingdom. He was also admitted 
to practice before the United States Supreme Court and the United 
States Court of International Trade. He was the owner of Reilly 
International Law Firm, P.A., in Haines City.
  Public service was an important part of Fred's legacy. He served as 
City Attorney for the City of Haines City beginning in 2012 and for the 
City of Belleair Beach beginning in 2019. During his years with Haines 
City, Fred provided trusted guidance and counsel to the City 
Commission, administration, and staff. His father, Andrew ``Andy'' 
Reilly, also served as Haines City Attorney from 2000 to 2004.
  Fred's commitment to his community included supporting Relay for Life 
and Habitat for Humanity. His many honors included the International 
Individual of the Year Award in 1995, the American Cancer Society 
Courage Award in 2013, and the Haines City Chamber of Commerce Citizen 
of the Year Award in 2017.
  Throughout his battle with cancer, Fred demonstrated remarkable 
courage and determination.
  Fred's legacy is one of service, professionalism, courage, and 
commitment to community. His counsel, friendship, and dedication left a 
lasting impact on Haines City and all who had the privilege of knowing 
and working with him.
```
