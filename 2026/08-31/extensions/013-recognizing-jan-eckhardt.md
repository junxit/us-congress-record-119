# RECOGNIZING JAN ECKHARDT

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JAN ECKHARDT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jan Eckhardt 
for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Woman of the Year 
Award.
  The title of Woman of the Year is awarded to an exceptional 
individual who demonstrates extraordinary community involvement, 
generosity, and overall impact on Arvada. As a lifelong advocate for 
people, parks, and animals in Arvada, Jan Eckhardt is a deserving 
recipient of this honor.
  During her more than 32 years serving on the North Jeffco (now Apex) 
Park and Recreation District Board of Directors, Eckhardt has been a 
champion for our local open spaces--even securing a park near her home 
and preserving what is now Jack B. Tomlinson Park. She has also brought 
that sense of land stewardship to her work on the Board of Two Ponds 
Wildlife Refuge, the MaxFund Board of Directors, and the West Side 
Denver Symphony Guild, which integrates cultural prosperity with 
humanitarian advocacy.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Jan Eckhardt on receiving the 2025 Woman of 
the Year Award and thank her for her contributions to our community.
```
