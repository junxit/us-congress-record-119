# HONORING ALE-8-ONE BOTTLING COMPANY

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
                  HONORING ALE-8-ONE BOTTLING COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BARR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an important 
Kentucky business, Ale-8-One Bottling Company, which celebrated its

[[Page E828]]

100th year in business on August 1, 2026. It is the oldest privately 
held bottler in the United States still owned by the founding family.
  Located in Winchester, Kentucky, the business was founded in 1926 by 
George Lee Wainscott. His first creation was a soda called Roxa-Kola, 
named after Wainscott's wife Roxanne. The drink was quite successful 
but spurred a lawsuit from the Nation's largest cola company. Wainscott 
ultimately won the lawsuit but decided to develop a new, unique drink. 
He combined ginger with citrus to create a new flavor. He introduced it 
at the Clark County Fair in 1926 and held a contest to name his new 
recipe. A young fairgoer said the drink was the latest thing, or ``A 
Late One''. Thus Ale-8-One was used as the drink's name.
  The company and the family secret recipe stayed protected through 4 
generations. In the fall of 2009, Wainscott's great-great-nephew 
Fielding Rogers became the youngest family member to assume the 
leadership role. Rogers serves as owner, Chairman of the Board, and 
President. Ale-8-One is a very popular drink in Kentucky and other 
locations. Its signature green glass bottle is instantly recognized.
  I extend my sincere congratulations to this family business on their 
100th anniversary. Their success and longevity are inspiring, and I am 
honored to recognize Ale-8-One Bottling Company on their 100th 
anniversary before the U.S. House of Representatives.
```
