# RECOGNIZING THE DIXON FAMILY FOR BEING AWARDED ALABAMA'S OUTSTANDING YOUNG FARM FAMILY

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE DIXON FAMILY FOR BEING AWARDED ALABAMA'S OUTSTANDING 
                           YOUNG FARM FAMILY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Robin 
and Garrett Dixon and their children, Chandler Jane and Cash, for being 
named Alabama's Outstanding Young Farm family.
  The Lee County farmers received the honor during the Alabama Farmers 
Federation Farm and Land Conference on August 8 in Mobile, Alabama.
  The Dixon family primarily grows cotton and peanuts on their farm in 
Salem. The crops they tend clothe and feed America and the world. 
Garrett began growing the farm from near scratch in college and has 
invested countless hours into improving every acre of land his family 
tends. He has installed irrigation, improved soil fertility and has now 
accepted a new challenge of growing their cattle herd. The livestock 
graze cover crops planted in row crop fields each winter, adding value 
to the land.
  Their sphere of influence extends beyond the farm. Robin is an 
agriscience teacher at Smiths Station High School and shares on-farm 
experience with her students.
  The Dixons understand grassroots involvement and have deep ties to 
groups such as the Alabama Farmers Federation. Garrett has chaired the 
State Young Farmers Committee and serves on multiple boards.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing this young family on this 
great honor. I congratulate the Dixons.
```
