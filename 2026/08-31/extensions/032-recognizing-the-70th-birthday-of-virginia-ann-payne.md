# RECOGNIZING THE 70TH BIRTHDAY OF VIRGINIA ANN PAYNE

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
          RECOGNIZING THE 70TH BIRTHDAY OF VIRGINIA ANN PAYNE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
70th birthday of Virginia Payne.
  Virginia was born in Lee County, Alabama, on August 24, 1956, to Mr. 
Arthur Lee Lockhart and Mrs. Lucille Lockhart and raised with strong 
faith and family values. Virginia is married to Wilbert Payne and was 
blessed with four children: Audrey, David, Jacques and Doricho. The 
Paynes have 10 grandchildren.
  Virginia retired from Walmart in 2021 after 30 years of serving 
others during her time there. She was affectionately known as ``Mrs. 
V'' to her colleagues and customers--always lending a hand or a smile. 
Virginia was a member of the Zenith Horizons Social Club since the late 
1970s, demonstrating a longstanding commitment to fellowship, 
friendship and community. She has served as a table host for the Cheryl 
Comer Ministries Brunch, raising more than $2,100 to support local 
youth scholarships and ministry efforts.
  The Payne family are members of Truth and Love Ministries Church and 
faithfully attend Sunday worship and fellowship at Green Chapel 
Missionary Church.
  In her retirement, she enjoys spending her time with her family. She 
also loves to dance and is a member of a dancing group called the 
Opelika Dancercize Club. When not dancing or with family, The Paynes 
enjoy traveling. Virginia's family lovingly describes her as ``the 
rock'' and the reason for many of their individual successes. She has 
supported her family to help them accomplish their goals, assisting 
with celebrations and helping in any way she is needed. She has also 
provided unwavering and unconditional support to her husband throughout 
his civic involvement.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Virginia Payne and wishing 
her a Happy 70th Birthday.

                          ____________________
```
