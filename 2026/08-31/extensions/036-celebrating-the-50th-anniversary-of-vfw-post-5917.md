# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF VFW POST 5917

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
           CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF VFW POST 5917

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Bolingbrook 
Veterans of Foreign Wars Post 5917 as they celebrate their 50th 
anniversary, marking five decades of service, patriotism, and steadfast 
commitment to our Nation's veterans and the public.
  Under the leadership of Post Commander Greg Palmer and through the 
dedication of its officers and members, VFW Post 5917 has served as a 
vital pillar of the community. The Post upholds the mission of the 
Veterans of Foreign Wars by fostering camaraderie among veterans, 
honoring the fallen through funeral honors and memorial services, and 
supporting veterans and their families through casework and social 
services. In addition to supporting veterans, the Post makes a 
meaningful impact through its involvement in local blood drives and 
participation in the VFW Voice of Democracy student scholarship 
program.
  Mr. Speaker, VFW Post 5917 stands as a testament to the enduring 
strength, sacrifice, and unity of America's veterans. I am proud to 
recognize its 50 years of service to our veterans, their families, and 
the Bolingbrook community. I ask my colleagues to join me in 
celebrating this milestone and honoring VFW Post 5917's continued 
commitment to supporting the individuals who fought to defend and 
preserve the freedoms we hold dear.
```
