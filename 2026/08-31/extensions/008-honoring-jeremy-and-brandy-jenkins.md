# HONORING JEREMY AND BRANDY JENKINS

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
                   HONORING JEREMY AND BRANDY JENKINS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Jeremy and Brandy 
Jenkins, who tragically lost their lives on August 15, 2026. The 
Jenkins leave behind three sons and countless family and friends 
devastated by their loss, but grateful for the lives they dedicated to 
teaching, coaching, and loving so many around them.
  Jeremy served as head football coach and athletic director at East 
Bernard ISD, with previous coaching stops at Harmony, Tenaha, and Mount 
Enterprise ISDs. Brandy had just begun her career at East Bernard ISD 
as a junior high teacher. They were extraordinary parents to three 
sons, and were examples of service and commitment to their community 
and the schools they served.
  Through Jeremy's football coaching career and their work in 
education, they influenced countless students, athletes, coaches, 
teachers, families, and communities. They weren't simply teaching 
lessons in a classroom or coaching a game on Friday nights--they were 
pouring into people, building relationships, shaping young men and 
women, and leaving an impact that will continue long after their time 
here.
  On behalf of a grateful, but heartbroken community, we recognize with 
deep appreciation the lives of Jeremy and Brandy Jenkins, their years 
of service and devotion to East Texas, and the legacies they leave 
behind. And, we pray that our Lord above would watch over their sons, 
comfort them, and be with their family and friends as they mourn this 
tragedy.

                          ____________________
```
