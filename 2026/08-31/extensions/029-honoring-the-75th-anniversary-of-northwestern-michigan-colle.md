# HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF NORTHWESTERN MICHIGAN COLLEGE

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
     HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF NORTHWESTERN MICHIGAN COLLEGE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Northwestern Michigan 
College (NMC) as they celebrate 75 years of serving students and 
communities across Northern Michigan.
  Since opening its doors in 1951 as Michigan's first community 
college, NMC has provided generations of students with affordable, 
high-quality educational opportunities while helping meet the workforce 
needs of our region. For 75 years, NMC has strengthened Northern 
Michigan by preparing students for meaningful careers, supporting local 
employers, and creating opportunities for lifelong learning.
  NMC has long demonstrated a commitment to innovation. As Michigan's 
first community college, NMC has continued to find new ways to serve 
students and employers alike. Today, the college offers a variety of 
employer-driven degrees, certificates, and workforce training programs. 
They lead important research through its Freshwater Research and 
Innovation Center and provide students with valuable hands-on learning 
opportunities.
  NMC is also home to one of the Nation's premier aviation programs, 
where students can earn their private pilot licenses and continue on 
pathways toward commercial aviation careers. Its nationally recognized 
drone program is preparing the next generation of professionals to use 
unmanned aircraft systems in industries ranging from agriculture and 
infrastructure to public safety and environmental stewardship.
  The College has also built strong partnerships with universities 
across Michigan, giving students the opportunity to earn bachelor's and 
select master's degrees while remaining in Traverse City. These 
partnerships expand access to higher education, keep talented students 
in Northern Michigan, and help meet the workforce needs of our 
communities.
  For 75 years, NMC has remained committed to serving the people of 
Northern Michigan by expanding educational opportunities, strengthening 
our workforce, and investing in the future of our communities.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Michigan's 1st Congressional District, I 
congratulate Northwestern Michigan College on this significant 
milestone and thank its students, faculty, staff, alumni, and 
supporters for 75 years of service to Northern Michigan. I wish them 
continued success in the years ahead.
```
