# CELEBRATING THE 5TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NORTHEAST BRONX YMCA

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
      CELEBRATING THE 5TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NORTHEAST BRONX YMCA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
fifth anniversary of the Northeast Bronx YMCA, an institution that has 
become an important part of the fabric of the Northeast Bronx and a 
place where residents of all ages can come together, stay active, 
learn, and build community.
  For decades, residents of the Northeast Bronx advocated for a 
dedicated community facility that would provide children, families, 
seniors, and young people with access to recreation, education, health, 
wellness, and enrichment opportunities. The opening of the Northeast 
Bronx YMCA in September 2021 represented the culmination of years of 
community advocacy and a shared commitment to investing in the future 
of the neighborhood.
  Community leaders Alonzo de Castro and Shirley Fearon, along with 
many other residents and advocates, played an important role in keeping 
the vision for a Northeast Bronx YMCA alive. Through their work with 
the Northeast Bronx Community Coalition and their engagement with 
residents and public officials, they helped ensure that the community's 
desire for a high-quality recreational and community facility remained 
a priority.
  The result is a remarkable resource at the heart of the Northeast 
Bronx. Located on East 229th Street, the Northeast Bronx YMCA provides 
residents with access to swimming pools, a full gymnasium, fitness and 
wellness facilities, childcare, youth programming, community spaces, 
and educational and recreational activities. These resources provide 
opportunities for children to learn and grow, families to spend time 
together, seniors to remain active and connected, and residents of all 
ages to improve their physical and mental well-being.
  The importance of the YMCA became particularly clear during the 
COVID-19 pandemic, when its gymnasium served as a vaccination site and 
helped connect Bronx residents with critical public health resources. 
This demonstrated that the YMCA is more than a place for exercise--it 
is an institution capable of responding to the needs of the community 
in times of both celebration and crisis.
  The Northeast Bronx YMCA also represents what can be accomplished 
when residents, community organizations, nonprofit institutions, and 
government work together toward a common goal. The approximately $60 
million investment that helped make the facility possible reflects a 
broader commitment to ensuring that residents of the Northeast Bronx 
have access to the resources and opportunities they deserve.
  Five years after opening its doors, the Northeast Bronx YMCA has 
become a multigenerational gathering place and community hub. It 
provides a safe space for young people, opportunities for families, 
programs for seniors, and resources that promote health, education, and 
connection. Most importantly, it gives residents a place they can call 
their own.
  As we celebrate this fifth anniversary, we recognize the many 
community members and leaders who helped make this vision possible, 
including Alonzo de Castro and Shirley Fearon, while celebrating the 
broader community that has embraced the YMCA and made it an integral 
part of life in the Northeast Bronx.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate the Northeast Bronx YMCA on five years of 
service to our community. May it continue to be a place of opportunity, 
wellness, fellowship, and hope for generations of Bronx residents to 
come.
```
