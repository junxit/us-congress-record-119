# RECOGNIZING NATIONAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AWARENESS MONTH

**Speaking:** Mackenzie, Ryan (M001230) [R-PA]

```
       RECOGNIZING NATIONAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AWARENESS MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RYAN MACKENZIE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. MACKENZIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize National 
Emergency Management Awareness Month and to honor the dedicated 
emergency management professionals who work tirelessly to protect our 
families.
  Emergency managers play a critical role in preparing communities for 
natural disasters, severe weather, public health emergencies, and other 
threats. Long before a crisis occurs, they work with first responders, 
government agencies, businesses, nonprofit organizations, and community 
partners to develop preparedness plans, coordinate resources, and 
ensure that our communities are ready to respond when disaster strikes.
  When emergencies occur, emergency management professionals are often 
among the first to act and among the last to leave. Their leadership 
and coordination help protect lives, maintain essential services, 
support first responders, and guide communities through the difficult 
process of recovery and rebuilding. This work is especially important 
in communities across the Lehigh Valley and the Poconos, where first 
responders--including police, EMS, and professional and volunteer 
firefighters--play an essential role in keeping our families and 
neighborhoods safe.
  Across the Lehigh Valley and the Poconos, we are fortunate to have 
emergency management professionals who are committed to serving their 
neighbors and strengthening the resilience of their communities. Their 
work may often take place behind the scenes, but its impact is felt 
whenever a community is prepared to endure a crisis and recover from 
its effects.
  National Emergency Management Awareness Month is an opportunity to 
recognize these dedicated public servants and to remind every American 
of the importance of being prepared. I encourage families, businesses, 
and communities to take steps to understand their unique risks, develop 
emergency plans, and know where to turn for information and assistance 
during a crisis.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize the emergency management 
professionals and first responders who dedicate themselves to preparing 
for the unexpected and protecting their communities. Their commitment, 
expertise, and service are essential to the safety and resilience of 
our Nation, and they deserve our sincere gratitude.
```
