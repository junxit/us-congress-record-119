# HONORING PRIVATE FIRST CLASS PEDRO RAMOS RIVERA FOR A LIFETIME OF SERVICE TO CENTRAL FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING PRIVATE FIRST CLASS PEDRO RAMOS RIVERA FOR A LIFETIME OF 
                       SERVICE TO CENTRAL FLORIDA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Private First Class Pedro 
Ramos Rivera, who honorably served in the United States Army from 
February 5, 1951, to January 31, 1953, as a member of the 65th Infantry 
Regiment, Borinqueneers, assigned to the Medical Company, APO 468. He 
served during the Korean War and was recognized with the Korean Service 
Medal, Bronze Star Medal, National Defense Service Medal, United 
Nations Service Medal, and Combat Infantry Badge.
  After his military service, he continued serving his community with 
dedication and integrity. He worked as a maintenance supervisor for a 
United States company established in Puerto Rico, where he contributed 
through hard work and strong leadership. He also gave his time to the 
Little League Baseball program in Country Club, the Angel Ramos League, 
volunteering his efforts to help children play the game and grow in a 
positive environment. Through both his work and volunteer service, he 
was a productive and respected example of citizenship in his community.
  His service also reflects the proud legacy of the Borinqueneers, one 
of the most distinguished units in United States military history. He 
received the Borinqueneers 65th Infantry Regiment Congressional Gold 
Medal, a thank-you letter from the office of Senator Rick Scott, a 
Certificate of Recognition from CSM Anibal Arbertorio, an Ambassador 
for Peace Medal, a Return from Hell Patch, 3rd Division, a Letter of 
Bravery from Paula Stark, Florida House District 47, and recognition 
from me for his service.
  Private First Class Ramos Rivera's military and community service 
stand as a lasting example of courage, sacrifice, leadership, and 
devotion to family, community, and country.

                          ____________________
```
