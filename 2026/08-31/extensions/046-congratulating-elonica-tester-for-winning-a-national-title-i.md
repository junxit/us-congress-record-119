# CONGRATULATING ELONICA TESTER FOR WINNING A NATIONAL TITLE IN THE 100- METER HURDLES AND HIGH JUMP

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
 CONGRATULATING ELONICA TESTER FOR WINNING A NATIONAL TITLE IN THE 100-
                      METER HURDLES AND HIGH JUMP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Ms. Elonica Tester on 
her extraordinary achievement of capturing two national championships 
in track and field at the Under Armour Nationals, held May 29 through 
May 31 in Bradenton, Florida. Her remarkable accomplishments are a 
testament to the discipline, determination, and perseverance that 
define outstanding athletes of Northern Michigan.
  At just 15 years old, Elonica recently completed her third year at 
Traverse City East Middle School, where she has already established 
herself as one of the school's most accomplished student-athletes. She 
currently holds 13 school records and recently added two national 
titles to an already impressive list of achievements. This past season, 
she also helped lead the Traverse City East Trojans girls track and 
field team to its first Mid-Michigan MegaStar team championship, a 
competition widely regarded as the middle school equivalent of a state 
championship.
  Despite her success at the local and state levels, Elonica had never 
envisioned herself competing on the national stage. That changed when 
she decided to pursue national competition. This was no easy feat, 
unlike Michigan middle school track, where hurdle races are either 55 
or 200 meters long, Under Armour Nationals only provided 100 and 200-
meter competitions, requiring her to compete in an event where she had 
little experience.
  After a slow start on her first day of the competition, Elonica 
rallied to post a title winning 14.92 second run in the 100-meter 
hurdles the following day. She followed up that performance by adding 
another championship in the high jump with a leap of 5 feet 5 inches.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Ms. Elonica Tester on her 
extraordinary achievement of capturing two national championships at 
the Under Armour Nationals. Her willingness to embrace new challenges 
serves as an inspiration to young athletes across Michigan's 1st 
Congressional District.
```
