# CELEBRATING CORPORAL JAMES RUSSELL EVANS 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
        CELEBRATING CORPORAL JAMES RUSSELL EVANS 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate Corporal James 
Russell Evans, one of our last surviving World War II veterans, who 
turned 100 years old on August 9, 2026.
  Corporal Evans was born on August 9, 1926, in Plummers Landing, 
Kentucky, to James Hurst and Lena Maude Evans. At just four years old, 
Corporal Evans began attending a one-room schoolhouse because he wanted 
to be with his older sister, Sarah, while helping make it easier for 
his mother to care for his baby sister, Lena May.
  After graduating from high school at the age of sixteen, Corporal 
Evans hoped to enlist in the military and serve his country during 
World War II, but his parents would not yet consent. When he reached 
the age of eligibility, he enlisted in the United States Army and 
became part of the first Allied occupation forces in Japan, ultimately 
rising to the rank of Corporal. During his service, he traveled 
throughout Japan and witnessed firsthand the devastation left in the 
wake of the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, experiences he 
would remember throughout his life. After completing his Army service, 
Corporal Evans continued his commitment to our Nation by joining the 
Air Force Reserve.
  Corporal Evans was the first member of his family to attend college, 
enrolling at the University of Kentucky on the GI Bill. He earned a 
bachelor's degree in electrical engineering and went on to build a 
successful career with Westinghouse Electric Corporation.
  On June 28, 1958, Corporal Evans married the love of his life, Irene 
Evans. This year, they celebrated their 68th wedding anniversary, 
marking nearly seven decades of marriage and devotion. Together, they 
raised three children and have been blessed with ten grandchildren and 
two great-grandchildren.
  A longtime member of the Freemasons and a devoted Christian, Corporal 
Evans has remained active in his church and community throughout his 
life. Together, Jim and Irene have built a lasting legacy rooted in 
love, faith, family, and service to God and country. At 100 years old, 
Corporal Evans's life is a testament to the dignity of a life well 
lived and the enduring impact one person can have through a lifetime of 
service, devotion, and faith.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Pennsylvania's 14th Congressional District, 
I am pleased to wish Corporal James Russell Evans a very happy 100th 
birthday.
```
