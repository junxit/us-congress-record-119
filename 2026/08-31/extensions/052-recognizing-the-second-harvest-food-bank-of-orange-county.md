# RECOGNIZING THE SECOND HARVEST FOOD BANK OF ORANGE COUNTY

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE SECOND HARVEST FOOD BANK OF ORANGE COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Second Harvest 
Food Bank of Orange County in celebration of being named the Delhi 
Center's 2026 Heroe de la Comunidad.
  For decades, Second Harvest has stood as a beacon of compassion, 
ensuring that nutritious food reaches children, families, seniors, and 
individuals facing food insecurity throughout Orange County.
  Their work reflects a profound belief that a strong community is one 
in which neighbors care for one another and no family is left behind.
  Every meal provided, every family supported, and every partnership 
strengthened represents an investment in the health, dignity, and 
future of our community.
  Second Harvest embodies the heart and spirit of the Delhi Center's 
Zocalo dinner--a celebration of culture, connection, community, and 
service.
  With deepest gratitude, we honor Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange 
County for its extraordinary service and enduring commitment to the 
well-being of our community.

                          ____________________
```
