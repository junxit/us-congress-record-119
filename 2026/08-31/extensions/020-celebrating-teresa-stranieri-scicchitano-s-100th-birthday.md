# CELEBRATING TERESA STRANIERI SCICCHITANO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

```
       CELEBRATING TERESA STRANIERI SCICCHITANO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FRANK PALLONE, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mrs. Teresa 
Stranieri Scicchitano as she celebrates her 100th birthday this year. 
It is my pleasure to join her friends and family in marking this 
incredible milestone and honoring the wonderful life she has led.
  On August 29, 1926, Teresa Stranieri was born to Elizabeth and Rocco 
Stranieri. Teresa was one of three children, growing up alongside her 
sister, Catherine and brother, Thomas.
  On November 10, 1946, Teresa married her beloved husband, Salvatore 
Scicchitano at St. Rocco Church in Girifalco, Italy. The pair had their 
first son, Frank Scicchitano, in 1947. The couple then had their second 
child, Vincent Scicchitano, in 1956. Since 1949, Teresa has lived in 
Long Branch, New Jersey. For over seventy years, the Scicchitano family 
has been a beloved part of the Long Branch community.
  In addition to raising a beautiful family, Teresa worked as a 
seamstress for decades. Following her retirement in the 1980s, Teresa 
continues this practice as a passion. Today, Teresa is highly regarded 
for making her own clothing and being a prolific cook.
  Teresa is the grandmother of two beloved grandchildren, Stephanie 
Scicchitano Pizzo and Lauren Scicchitano Mounayer. She's also 
celebrating this occasion with her two great-grandchildren, Christian 
James Pizzo, II and Vincenzo Salvatore Pizzo.
  Mr. Speaker, once again, please join me in wishing Teresa Stranieri 
Scicchitano another year of good health and happiness as she celebrates 
the milestone of her 100th birthday.
```
