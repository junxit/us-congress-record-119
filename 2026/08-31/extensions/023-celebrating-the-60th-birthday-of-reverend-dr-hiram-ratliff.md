# CELEBRATING THE 60TH BIRTHDAY OF REVEREND DR. HIRAM RATLIFF

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
      CELEBRATING THE 60TH BIRTHDAY OF REVEREND DR. HIRAM RATLIFF

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
60th birthday of Reverend Dr. Hiram Ratliff, an extraordinary pastor, 
leader, author, servant, and proud son of the Bronx.
  For 30 years, Reverend Dr. Ratliff has answered the call to ministry, 
dedicating his life to serving God and uplifting others. For the past 
15 years, he has served as the Senior Pastor of New Tabernacle Baptist 
Church in the Bronx, where his leadership has extended far beyond the 
walls of the church.
  Under his ministry, New Tabernacle has provided health screenings, 
food and clothing giveaways, and programs for young people. Reverend 
Ratliff has also been a powerful advocate for social justice, 
partnering with community organizations to support working people, 
immigrants, children, and families in need.
  His service extends throughout our community--from his work with 
young people and children with special needs to his leadership on 
educational and religious institutions. He is also an accomplished 
author whose writing reflects the struggles, hopes, and triumphs of his 
life and community.
  Today, we celebrate not only Reverend Dr. Ratliff's 60th birthday, 
but also a life defined by faith, service, and purpose.
  I wish him a happy 60th birthday, and I congratulate him on 30 years 
of ministry and 15 years of faithful leadership at New Tabernacle 
Baptist Church.

                          ____________________
```
