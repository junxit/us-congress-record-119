# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF COMMUNITY LEADER GEORGE ROMAGNOLI

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
   HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF COMMUNITY LEADER GEORGE ROMAGNOLI

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of an extraordinary public servant, community leader, affordable 
housing advocate, and Scouter, George Romagnoli.
  George devoted his life and career to serving others and 
strengthening the communities he called home. For decades, his 
leadership made a meaningful difference throughout Pasco County and 
across the State of Florida, particularly for families seeking the 
stability and opportunity that come with having a safe, affordable 
place to call home. George spent many years serving Pasco County, 
including 16 years as Director of the Pasco County Community 
Development Department. In that role, he helped shape the County's 
approach to affordable housing, community development, homelessness, 
and neighborhood revitalization. His work was not simply about programs 
and policies; George understood that behind every housing initiative 
was a family, a senior, a Veteran, or an individual who deserved the 
opportunity to live with dignity and security. His expertise and 
commitment earned him recognition well beyond Pasco County. George 
served six years as Chairman of the Florida Housing Coalition, helping 
provide statewide leadership on affordable and workforce housing 
issues. He was also appointed by both Governor Jeb Bush and Governor 
Charlie Crist to Florida's Affordable Housing Study Commission. George 
carried that same commitment into the private and nonprofit sectors. 
Most recently, he served as Senior Vice President and Senior Credit 
Underwriter for Neighborhood Lending Partners, where he continued 
working to advance affordable housing and equitable community 
development. Even after leaving county government, George never stopped 
looking for ways to strengthen communities and expand opportunity for 
others. That spirit of service extended far beyond his professional 
career. George was deeply committed to the Boy Scouts of America, 
serving as a Council Vice President with the Greater Tampa Bay Area 
Council. He believed deeply in Scouting's mission of building 
character, responsibility, leadership, and service in young people. He 
gave generously of his time and talents because he understood that 
investing in the next generation is one of the most meaningful ways we 
can strengthen our communities.
  Perhaps the greatest measure of George's life, however, is not found 
in the positions he held or the projects he helped complete. It is 
found in the people and communities he impacted. Whether he was helping 
a family find an affordable home, advocating for stronger housing 
policies, mentoring a young Scout, or volunteering his leadership to a 
community organization, George lived by the principle that our greatest 
responsibility is to use our talents in service to others. That is a 
legacy that does not end with his passing. It lives on in the families 
and neighborhoods strengthened by his work, in the housing 
professionals and public servants who benefited from his leadership, 
and in the Scouts and community leaders he inspired to serve others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring George 
Romagnoli for his decades of distinguished service to Pasco County, the 
State of Florida, and the countless people whose lives were made better 
because of his work. I also extend my deepest condolences to his 
family, friends, colleagues, and fellow Scouts. May his memory be a 
blessing, and may his example of service continue to inspire us all.
```
