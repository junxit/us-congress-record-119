# RECOGNIZING TIM HYGH'S DEDICATED SERVICE TO MACKINAC ISLAND

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
      RECOGNIZING TIM HYGH'S DEDICATED SERVICE TO MACKINAC ISLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Mr. Tim Hygh for his 
retirement from the Mackinac Island Convention & Visitors Bureau 
(MICVB). His leadership has strengthened one of Michigan's most iconic 
tourist destinations while supporting local businesses and preserving 
the Island's unique character.
  For fifteen years, Tim has served as the Executive Director for the 
MICVB, after helping to found the organization in 2010. He has been 
instrumental in turning the MICVB into a premier destination marketing 
and advocacy organization. Under his leadership, the Bureau has 
expanded to encompass the Mackinac Island Convention & Visitors Bureau, 
the Mackinac Island Tourism Bureau, and the Mackinac Island Tourism 
Bureau Political Action Committee.
  Tim's leadership was evident during the COVID-19 pandemic when he led 
the island's Public Health Task Force. By fostering strong 
communication among local leaders, businesses, and public officials, he 
helped guide the community through uncertainty while establishing the 
Bureau as a trusted resource and effective partner. The relationships 
formed during that time helped the MICVB expand its advocacy efforts to 
advance the island's interests within federal agencies and the Michigan 
State Legislature.

[[Page E825]]

  Throughout his tenure, Tim has worked tirelessly to increase Mackinac 
Island's national profile. He established a lasting relationship with 
Advance Travel and Tourism to market the island on a broader scale and 
gained national recognition from media outlets such as Conde Nast 
Traveler, Travel + Leisure, USA Today's 10Best, among other accolades. 
These efforts have helped to keep the Island a world class destination, 
increasing tourism and strengthening the local economy.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Tim Hygh for his 
remarkable career and steadfast commitment to Mackinac Island. Tim's 
vision, integrity, and unwavering commitment to serving others have 
left an enduring legacy on Mackinac Island that will continue to 
benefit the community for years to come.
```
