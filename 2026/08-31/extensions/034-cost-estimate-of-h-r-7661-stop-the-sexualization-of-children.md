       H.R. 7661 would prohibit the use of federal funds awarded 
     under the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to 
     provide or promote programs, literature, or other materials 
     to children under the age of 18 that contain sexually 
     oriented content, including gender dysphoria or 
     transgenderism. The bill would exempt certain content that 
     includes science coursework, religious texts, and classic 
     works of art and literature, and instruction intended to help 
     students recognize sexual trafficking.
       CBO expects schools and other entities would comply with 
     the requirements in H.R. 7661 and that any federal funds that 
     would have been used for those purposes under current law 
     would be used for other purposes.
       Based on the cost of implementing similar prohibitions, CBO 
     estimates that the costs to the Department of Education to 
     implement H.R. 7661 would be insignificant; any related 
     spending would be subject to the availability of appropriated 
     funds.
