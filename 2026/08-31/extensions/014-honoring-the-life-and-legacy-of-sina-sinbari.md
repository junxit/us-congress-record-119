# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SINA SINBARI

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SINA SINBARI

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Sina Sinbari, of Laguna Niguel, California, who passed away on April 
20, 2026, at the age of twenty-four. On that day, Sina lost his 
courageous four-year battle with desmoplastic small round cell tumors, 
a rare and aggressive cancer. In honor of his passing, I reflect on the 
life he led and the impact he had on our community in the 49th 
District.
  Sina was born and raised in Orange County, where he established 
lifelong friendships. Sina attended elementary, middle, and high school 
in Mission Viejo, and was known for his big heart, his loyalty, and his 
infectious smile.
  With a lifelong passion for volleyball, he was preparing to play 
Division 1 volleyball at Fairleigh Dickinson University in the summer 
of 2022 when he was diagnosed with cancer. Over the next four years, 
Sina endured more than thirty rounds of chemotherapy, surgeries, and 
hospital stays. But Sina refused to let his diagnosis steal his dreams 
and break his spirit. While undergoing intense treatments, he would fly 
crosscountry on red-eye flights--straight from chemotherapy at UCLA to 
morning volleyball practices in New Jersey--just to be with his team.
  Even when his body was broken, his voice was the loudest in the gym. 
Sina's journey earned him national recognition, including the Wilma 
Rudolph Student-Athlete Achievement Award and FDU's Comeback Player of 
the Year. In May 2025, he proudly graduated with a bachelor's degree in 
business and promptly enrolled at UCLA to pursue a master's degree in 
business law. He was scheduled to receive his degree one month after 
his passing.
  Sina's true legacy lies in his profound empathy and willingness to 
uplift others. Even during long hospital stays, he found the strength 
to motivate other young cancer patients and

[[Page E819]]

dedicated himself to an internship with the Make-A-Wish Foundation. 
From his love for his family to his charismatic personality that turned 
strangers into friends, he embodied the best traits of the human 
spirit.
  Sina was a beloved son, brother, friend, and athlete who taught those 
around him what it means to be unbreakable. He was a light to all who 
knew him, and I share in the celebration of his life.
```
