# RECOGNIZING JOE HENGSTLER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING JOE HENGSTLER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the late Joe 
Hengstler for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Image 
Award posthumously.
  Image Award winners are selected for their commitment to Arvada and 
the overall positive image they portray within the community. Joe, who 
was a tremendous advocate for Arvada, was lovingly called the ``Mayor 
of Olde Town.'' Joe was instrumental in developing signature events 
like Second Saturdays, and he was a champion of initiatives that 
enhanced Olde Town's welcoming, walkable character, including with 
pedestrian-friendly design and public art. His commitment to our 
community helped make it a vibrant regional destination and one of the 
area's most cherished landmarks.
  Joe is described by those closest to him as ``hard on the issues, but 
easy on the people.'' In times of crisis, especially during the COVID-
19 pandemic, his bold and altruistic leadership was invaluable in 
helping local companies survive. Even after his diagnosis with Stage 4 
cancer in February 2024, Joe remained steadfast in his mission and 
remained committed to our community--planning events, collaborating 
with regional stakeholders, and tirelessly advocating for small 
businesses.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to help dedicate this award in memory of Joe Hengstler and 
thank him for his contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
