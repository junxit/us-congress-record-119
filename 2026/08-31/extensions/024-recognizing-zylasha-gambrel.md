# RECOGNIZING ZYLASHA GAMBREL

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING ZYLASHA GAMBREL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Zylasha Gambrel 
for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Rising Star Award.
  The Rising Star Award recognizes High School students who live or 
attend school in Arvada who have exemplified extraordinary skills in 
entrepreneurship, innovation, community impact, and leadership. As 
Student Body President at Arvada High School, Zylasha exemplifies 
innovative problem-solving by turning ideas into action--identifying 
school culture gaps amid rigorous academics and leadership demands.
  Her community involvement encompasses the Jeffco Student Leadership 
Board, National Honor Society membership, mentoring as an Outdoor Lab 
Leader, and participation in wrestling, softball, and tennis. She also 
orchestrates successful events, including pep rallies, school dances, 
Trick or Treat Street, and Empty Bowls Night to benefit The Community 
Table. Zylasha takes initiative in everything she does, from shadowing 
the mayor at work to driving creative solutions at school.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Zylasha Gambrel for this award and thank 
her for her commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
