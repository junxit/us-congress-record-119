# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF CAPTAIN JOSHUA PAUL NAUMAN

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
        CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF CAPTAIN JOSHUA PAUL NAUMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognizing Captain 
Joshua Paul Nauman for his 27 years of outstanding active-duty Navy 
service, including this past year serving as the Director for Policy 
and Legislation in the Navy's Office of Legislative Affairs. Prior to 
this assignment, he served as the Executive Assistant for the Judge 
Advocate General of the Navy, the Commanding Officer of Region Legal 
Service Office Southwest, and Special Counsel to the Chief of Naval 
Operations.
  A native of Nebraska who was commissioned through the Navy Judge 
Advocate General's Corps (JAGC) Student Program, Captain Nauman began 
his active service in 2000 at Naval Legal Service Office Southwest in 
San Diego--a unit he would later command. His distinguished career 
includes assignments as a Trial Counsel in San Diego and as the Staff 
Judge Advocate in Keflavik, Iceland, before serving as the Deputy Fleet 
Judge Advocate for the U.S. Second Fleet. In 2008, he was assigned to 
the Pentagon, from where he deployed to Iraq with the II Marine 
Expeditionary Force in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom. His 
subsequent assignments include a deployment with Commander, Carrier 
Strike Group ELEVEN to the 5th, 6th, and 7th Fleet areas of 
responsibility; service as Executive Officer of Region Legal Service 
Office Japan; Special Assistant for Legal and Legislative Matters for 
the Secretary of the Navy; and Senior Legal Counsel for the Chief of 
Naval Personnel.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask the House of Representatives to join me in 
expressing our deepest gratitude to Captain Joshua Paul Nauman for his 
exemplary dedication to our Nation as he retires from the United States 
Navy. Throughout his 27 years of military service, Captain Nauman has 
demonstrated an unwavering commitment to the defense of our Nation. His 
profound patriotism, stellar leadership, and tireless work ethic leave 
an enduring legacy that has strengthened our National Defense.
```
