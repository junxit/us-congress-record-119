# HONORING IRMA SARMIENTO

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                        HONORING IRMA SARMIENTO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a truly remarkable 
woman on her 90th birthday--Irma Sarmiento.
  Irma Sarmiento was born on August 29, 1935, in La Paz, Bolivia. She 
has devoted nine decades to family, entrepreneurship, philanthropy, and 
service.
  As a successful business owner, she became a source of inspiration 
and support for many single mothers striving for financial independence 
and homeownership.
  Her commitment to her heritage and community is equally inspiring. 
She founded a Bolivian folkloric dance troupe and supported 
improvements to schools, a hospital, soccer fields, and the town square 
in Quime, Bolivia. Since 1986, she has also sponsored the Downtown 
Santa Ana Christmas Food and Toy Giveaway, touching the lives of 
thousands of local children and families.
  Together with her husband, Vicente Sarmiento, she raised three 
children--Daisy, Vicki, and Vicente--with a strong emphasis on 
education and opportunity. Today, she is blessed with six grandchildren 
and seven great-grandchildren.
  At 90, her greatest legacy is the countless lives she has touched 
through love, hard work, generosity, and service. Today, we celebrate 
her extraordinary life and wish her a very happy 90th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
