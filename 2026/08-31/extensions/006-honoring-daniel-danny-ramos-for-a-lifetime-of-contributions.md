# HONORING DANIEL "DANNY" RAMOS FOR A LIFETIME OF CONTRIBUTIONS TO CENTRAL FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
  HONORING DANIEL ``DANNY'' RAMOS FOR A LIFETIME OF CONTRIBUTIONS TO 
                            CENTRAL FLORIDA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Daniel ``Danny'' Ramos, who 
was a distinguished Hispanic community leader, entrepreneur, media 
producer, and nonprofit founder based in Orlando, Florida. For more 
than four decades, he dedicated his life's work to promoting Hispanic 
culture, advancing educational and economic opportunity, assisting 
families with immigration-related needs, and recognizing the 
achievements of Hispanic professionals and artists throughout the 
United States. His legacy reflected a lifelong commitment to service, 
cultural preservation, and community empowerment.
  Ramos was the founder of the National Hispanic Corporate Achievers, a 
nonprofit organization established to recognize outstanding Hispanic 
professionals in corporate America while encouraging greater diversity, 
leadership, and advancement within the business community. Through his 
vision and leadership, the organization honored hundreds of executives 
and professionals from major corporations nationwide, becoming one of 
the longest-running recognition programs dedicated to Hispanic 
excellence in corporate leadership. In addition to its annual awards 
program, the organization expanded its mission to include educational 
seminars, leadership development initiatives, and partnerships with 
colleges and universities designed to strengthen the pipeline of future 
Hispanic leaders.
  A passionate advocate for Hispanic arts and cultural preservation, 
Ramos also founded the Arte Mundial Museum Gallery in Orlando. Opened 
in 2021, the museum served as a permanent exhibition space dedicated 
exclusively to Hispanic artists, featuring paintings, sculptures, and 
cultural artifacts representing dozens of countries across the Americas 
and beyond. The institution was created to preserve, elevate, and 
celebrate Hispanic cultural heritage while providing artists with a 
permanent venue to showcase their work.
  Under his leadership, the museum became a unique cultural landmark in 
Central Florida and was recognized in the Congressional Record for its 
contributions to the preservation of Hispanic art and history
  Among Ramos' most widely recognized accomplishments was the creation 
of the Raices Festival, one of Central Florida's largest Hispanic 
cultural celebrations. The festival was established to highlight the 
traditions, music, dance, cuisine, and heritage of the diverse Hispanic 
community while bringing together families, businesses, civic 
organizations, and cultural institutions. Over the years, Raices grew 
into a signature annual event that strengthened community pride and 
celebrated Hispanic identity throughout the region. According to 
organizational history, Ramos also played a key role in organizing one 
of the largest Hispanic festivals ever held in New York, further 
demonstrating his long-standing commitment to cultural celebration and 
community engagement.
  Throughout his career, Ramos founded, supported, and collaborated 
with numerous organizations and initiatives serving the Hispanic 
community. These included the Puerto Rican Chamber of Commerce, 
Hispanics SpeakOut TV, Latina Role Model TV, Christian Stars 
Chaplaincy, and a range of educational and business conferences focused 
on economic development, leadership training, and minority 
entrepreneurship. His media and public engagement efforts also 
addressed critical community issues, including human trafficking 
prevention, domestic violence awareness, homelessness outreach, 
consumer education, small business development, and assistance for 
Hispanic families navigating immigration status, documentation, and 
related paperwork. Through these efforts, he provided trusted guidance, 
resources, and advocacy to individuals and families working toward 
stability and legal security in the United States, while also creating 
platforms for education, awareness, and public dialogue.
  Ramos remained steadfast in his commitment to preserving Hispanic 
history and expanding opportunities for future generations. Through his 
work in leadership, media, education, the arts, cultural advocacy, and 
immigration support services, he combined entrepreneurship with public 
service in a way that left a lasting imprint on Central Florida's 
Hispanic community. His organizations, festivals, museums, recognition 
programs, and educational initiatives continue to reflect his vision of 
celebrating achievement, strengthening identity, and empowering future 
leaders.
  Daniel ``Danny'' Ramos' legacy endures through the institutions he 
built, the communities he strengthened, and the countless individuals 
he inspired through his lifelong dedication to service, cultural pride, 
and support for immigrant families seeking opportunity and belonging.
```
