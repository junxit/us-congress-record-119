# HONORING HEART GALLERY EAST TEXAS

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
                   HONORING HEART GALLERY EAST TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Heart Gallery East 
Texas on being named a 2026 Angels in Adoption Honoree by the 
Congressional Coalition on Adoption Institute, an award accepted on the 
organization's behalf by its Director, Gillian Sheridan.
  Heart Gallery East Texas serves children in foster care across 23 
counties in Region 4 of East Texas using portrait exhibits and 
storytelling to connect the roughly 100 children in our region awaiting 
adoption with families willing to welcome them home. Gillian has led 
that mission with a personal conviction for it--she has been a voice 
for children in foster care since her days as a television news anchor, 
she helped build local ministries to raise awareness of their needs, 
and she is an adoptive mother herself. Her prayer, in her words, is 
that every child in foster care finds a forever family. That conviction 
runs through the organization she leads.
  Each year, the Congressional Coalition on Adoption Institute selects 
individuals, families, and organizations across the Nation who have 
shown an extraordinary commitment to helping children and permanent, 
safe, and loving homes. The work of Heart Gallery East Texas, on behalf 
of those children has set it apart in our community and earned it this 
national recognition.
  I am proud to welcome Heart Gallery East Texas to the distinguished 
group of organizations that have carried the title Angels in Adoption 
Honoree. The people of East Texas are grateful for its tireless work on 
behalf of children who are waiting for a family to call their own, and 
I join the Institute in thanking Heart Gallery East Texas, and Gillian 
Sheridan, for helping make that dream a reality.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Heart 
Gallery East Texas on this well-deserved honor.
```
