# RECOGNIZING LUJANE HANNA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING LUJANE HANNA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lujane Hanna 
for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Young Professional 
Leadership Award.
  The Young Professional Leadership Award recognizes a young 
professional under the age of 40 whose leadership and dedication has 
impacted the world around them. As a financial professional at Valorem 
Financial, Lujane has already achieved the CFP designation--the 
industry gold standard--delivering financial clarity, stability, and 
long-term success for individuals, families, and business owners. She 
also serves as a mentor at her company, helping other employees with 
skill-building.
  Outside of work, she hosts free educational workshops around 
empowering women professionals through financial literacy, and 
participates in Old Town Arvada community events

[[Page E822]]

like Trick or Treat Street and Second Saturday during the summer with 
her ``Wheel of Wealth'' booth.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Lujane Hanna for this award and thank her 
for her commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
