# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF MR. DONALD P. "PETE" GERMAN

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
         CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF MR. DONALD P. ``PETE'' GERMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the life of 
Donald P. ``Pete'' German, who passed away on August 12, 2026, at the 
age of 87. Donald was a true public servant, dedicating over 30 years 
of his career to teaching woodworking across Pennsylvania public high 
schools. Outside of work, Donald was an avid outdoorsman and football 
fan. Donald's service to future generations is sure to leave a lasting 
impact across our commonwealth.
  Donald was born September 14, 1938, to Frank and Margret German in 
Wyano, Pennsylvania. He graduated from South Huntington High School in 
1957 and continued his education at California University, where he 
earned his bachelor's degree in education. For the next 30 years, 
Donald worked as the industrial art teacher at West Newton and Yough 
High Schools, teaching generations of Pennsylvanians woodworking 
skills. Donald also made it a priority to teach the importance of hard 
work and responsibility. Former students continue to share stories of 
the influence Donald had on their lives long after their time in his 
classroom had ended.
  Above all else, Donald will be remembered as a man who loved his 
family. Donald married his high school sweetheart, Gwendolyn Odelli 
German, and is survived by their two children, Lisa D. Nickles and 
Donald Hutch German. Additionally, Donald was a grandfather to four 
grandchildren, great-grandfather to five great-grandchildren, and loved 
by several extended family members who are sure to carry on his legacy.
  Mr. Speaker, Donald P. ``Pete'' German was a man loved by many. From 
teaching important life skills to his students to his love and support 
for his family, Donald's impact will be felt for years to come. I 
extend my thoughts and prayers to his wife, Gwendolyn, his two 
children, Lisa and Donald; his grandchildren; and all his family, 
friends, and loved ones during this difficult time.
```
