# HONORING THE MEDICAL CENTER OF THE AMERICAS FOUNDATION ON ITS 20TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Escobar, Veronica (E000299) [D-TX]

```
  HONORING THE MEDICAL CENTER OF THE AMERICAS FOUNDATION ON ITS 20TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VERONICA ESCOBAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. ESCOBAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Medical 
Center of the Americas Foundation as it celebrates 20 years of 
advancing health care, innovation, and economic opportunity across the 
Paso de Norte region.
  In El Paso, we know that progress is only built by people willing to 
see possibilities where others have seen limitations. Twenty years ago, 
the MCA was founded on the belief that our border community could 
become a center for exceptional health care, scientific discovery, and 
economic opportunity. What began as an ambitious idea has become a 
defining, indispensable part of our community.
  Because of the MCA, young El Pasoans can imagine a future in medicine 
and science, local innovators have turned their ideas into solutions, 
and families can receive critical, life-saving care closer to home. The 
MCA's success belongs to the many visionary leaders, health care 
professionals, researchers, educators, and community partners who 
believed deeply in El Paso's potential and worked together to realize 
it.
  I congratulate the entire MCA family on 20 remarkable years and thank 
them for helping build a healthier, more innovative, more prosperous 
future for our community.

                          ____________________
```
