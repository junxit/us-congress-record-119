# HONORING STERLING HEALTH CARE

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
                     HONORING STERLING HEALTH CARE

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BARR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the vital 
contributions of Sterling Health Care during Health Center Week. 
Sterling Health, founded in 2012, serves the people of Nicholas, 
Bourbon, Bath, Montgomery, Clark, and Powell counties in Kentucky.
  Sterling Health provides a robust list of vital medical services, 
including primary care, behavioral health, dental, vision, pharmacy, 
school-based health, a mobile unit, women's health, children's health, 
and substance abuse treatment. When they started in 2012, Sterling 
Health was focused on providing quality care to all, regardless of 
their ability to pay. That focus continues today.
  In 2025, Sterling Health had 31,260 patients with 134,053 visits, and 
255 full-time employees. Sterling Health is recognized by HRSA as a 
Quality Leader in several categories. They recently received 2 
Communicator Awards of Excellence in Commercial Campaign (radio) and 
Website. They are planning to break ground soon on a new clinic in 
Owingsville.
  Sterling Health is very community oriented. They sponsor mission-
related causes and donate to area communities to provide resources

[[Page E829]]

for many causes, including Special Needs Camp of Kentucky, First 
Responders Academy for youth, Camp Bravehearts for emotional regulation 
for children, the Bath County JAG team, and blood drives. In 
conjunction with their school-based health clinics, they hosted Teddy 
Bear Day, where young students used medical tools, examined their teddy 
bears, and learned about health care. Sterling Health is living their 
mission: ``To improve the lives of the people in the communities we 
serve.''
  I extend my sincere appreciation to Sterling Health Care CEO Tina 
Bryant and all their staff. Their dedication and service to the health 
and well-being of our communities is inspiring, and I am honored to 
recognize them during Health Center Week before the U.S. House of 
Representatives.
```
