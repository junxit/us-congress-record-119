# RECOGNIZING JEFF STREETER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING JEFF STREETER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank Executive Director 
Jeff Streeter of the Jefferson County Communications Center Authority 
(Jeffcom 911) for his service to Jefferson County, and to congratulate 
him on his retirement.
  Jeff has honorably served the Jefferson County community for 9 years, 
culminating in an exemplary law enforcement career of over 31 years. 
Jeff began his dedicated career in public service with the Broomfield 
Police Department, then continued on to serve as Lakewood Police 
Department Commander, and as Chief of Police for Lone Tree, before 
joining Jeffcom 911. Under Jeff's leadership, Jeffcom 911 grew from its 
early stages of development into a dispatch communications center 
recognized nationally for its stability, innovation, and success. As 
Executive Director, Jeff oversaw the operations and strategic direction 
of the center, establishing key initiatives including Advisory Planning 
Teams, a Peer Assistance Team, Training and Quality Assurance Teams, an 
Information Technology Division, and their Support Services Division.
  I join the communities Jeff has served throughout his career in 
gratitude for his leadership and commitment to keeping our 
neighborhoods safe with efficient emergency response efforts. Although 
Jeff's outstanding leadership will be greatly missed, his efforts to 
build Jeffcom 911 have created a lasting service for all future 
generations who depend on the critical services they provide.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Jeff Streeter on his well-earned retirement 
from a distinguished career of service to Jefferson County.

                          ____________________
```
