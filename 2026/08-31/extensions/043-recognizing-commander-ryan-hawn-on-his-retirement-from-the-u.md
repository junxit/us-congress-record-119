# RECOGNIZING COMMANDER RYAN HAWN ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE UNITED STATES COAST GUARD

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
   RECOGNIZING COMMANDER RYAN HAWN ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE UNITED 
                           STATES COAST GUARD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 31, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Commander Ryan Hawn on 
his retirement from the United States Coast Guard after 20 years of 
dedicated service. Throughout his career, Commander Hawn has 
exemplified the Coast Guard's core values of Honor, Respect, and 
Devotion to Duty and has demonstrated exceptional leadership in service 
to the American people.
  Prior to assuming Command in Traverse City, Commander Hawn served as 
Chief Pilot at Kodiak Air Station in Alaska where he oversaw aviation 
operations in challenging environments. At every stage of his career, 
he has demonstrated exceptional expertise in operational planning, 
interagency coordination, and military aviation. He has helped 
strengthen the Coast Guard's lifesaving capabilities while advancing 
innovative technologies that will support future missions.
  For the last two years, Commander Hawn has served as the Commanding 
Officer of Coast Guard Air Station Traverse City where he oversaw 
active-duty Coast Guardsmen and civilian personnel responsible for 
safeguarding communities across the Great Lakes. In addition to 
commanding one of the busiest air stations, he also served as an 
operational pilot, remaining actively engaged in search and rescue and 
maritime law enforcement missions. Whether responding to mariners in 
distress or preparing for emergencies, Commander Hawn ensured the 
station remained prepared to answer every call, living out the Coast 
Guard's motto of ``Always Ready.''
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Commander Ryan Hawn on 
his retirement from the United States Coast Guard. His dedicated 
leadership, professionalism, and commitment to protecting the 
communities of Northern Michigan and the Upper Peninsula exemplify the 
very best of the United States Coast Guard.
```
