# HONORING WORK OF WARRIOR HEALING CENTER

**Speaking:** Ciscomani, Juan (C001133) [R-AZ]

```
                HONORING WORK OF WARRIOR HEALING CENTER

  (Mr. Ciscomani of Arizona was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the work of the 
Warrior Healing Center in Sierra Vista, Arizona.
  The Warrior Healing Center, which I was honored to visit earlier this 
year, is a pillar of the local veteran community. The center provides 
community and peer support to veterans throughout Sierra Vista with the 
goal of eliminating veteran suicide throughout Cochise County.
  The Warrior Healing Center cares for those who have served through 
crisis intervention, command and control, and a one-stop shop with 
several veteran organizations under the same roof.
  The center's dedicated work ensures that local veterans have access 
to the resources and the tools that they need to thrive.
  Veterans who come to the Warrior Healing Center are met with a caring 
staff and volunteers, which I have met, who are committed to 
understanding their individual needs. Through their efforts, these 
veterans are able to receive amazing short-term and also long-term 
support.
  I thank Director Cathie Goodman, Chief Clerk Dr. Tim Kirk, and 
everyone at the Warrior Healing Center for all the work that they do 
for our veterans. Their efforts have made an incredible impact in 
Sierra Vista, in Cochise County, and beyond as well. I wish them all 
the best as they continue to provide veterans with the support they 
need after sacrificing so much for our country.

  recognizing tanque verde unified school district's 140th anniversary

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 140th 
anniversary of Tanque Verde Unified School District.

[[Page H5436]]

  Since 1886, the district has been providing public education in the 
Tanque Verde Valley. The first Tanque Verde school opening its doors 
all those years ago began a legacy of service and education that has 
impacted generations of valley residents.
  Through the work of dedicated staff and the commitment of students 
and their families, Tanque Verde schools have served as an example of 
excellent public education.
  The district will celebrate this historic milestone on September 23, 
bringing together members of the Tanque Verde community, both past and 
present, as a picture of the community's rich and unique history and 
honor the people who have made the district's success possible.
  To superintendent Dr. Scott Hagerman, teachers and staff, and 
students and families, congratulations on an incredible 140 years. 
Through their work and dedication, Tanque Verde Valley is home to 
outstanding public education. Congratulations again, and here is to 
many, many more wonderful academic years ahead and lives impacted of 
our future generations through the power of public education.


    Honoring Lisa Scott Being Named 2027 Arizona Teacher of the Year

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate Lisa 
Scott from the Vail School District earning the title of 2027 Arizona 
Teacher of the Year.
  Mrs. Scott, who is an eighth grade science teacher at Esmond Station 
K-8, has earned the highest honor among educators in being named the 
2027 Arizona Teacher of the Year by the Arizona Educational Foundation.
  This esteemed award celebrates and honors educators who represent the 
very best of their profession by both inspiring their students and also 
strengthening the local community.
  Not only has Mrs. Scott been an amazing educator, she has done so 
while also navigating significant life changes that resulted from a 
near-death experience in December of 2022.
  The discovery of a kidney stone led to Mrs. Scott experiencing a loss 
of consciousness, septic shock, and treatment with a medication that 
restricted her limbs' blood vessels to the point of needing to amputate 
both her hands and feet.
  Committed to continuing to do what she loves, even after a life-
altering event, Mrs. Scott returned to the classroom in July of 2024, 
where she has proven that anything is possible when you refuse to be 
defined by circumstances.
  Being a great example to all the students that she serves and 
teaches, her colleagues, her administrators, but also our entire 
community of what grit and dedication and passion for a career like 
education can do for someone.
  Congratulations to Mrs. Scott, Lisa, on this incredible achievement. 
I thank her for the impact she has made on her students and our entire 
community.
```
