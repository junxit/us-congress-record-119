# RECOGNIZING RECREATIONAL BOATBUILDERS

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                 RECOGNIZING RECREATIONAL BOATBUILDERS

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, as co-chair of the 
Congressional Boating Caucus, I am so proud to recognize Spencer 
Yachts, Bayliss Boatworks, and the skilled men and women who keep 
eastern North Carolina's boatbuilding tradition alive and strong.
  Boatbuilding is more than an industry. It is part of our coastal 
heritage, our economy, and our way of life. Visiting these businesses, 
I saw firsthand the craftsmanship, innovation, and pride that goes into 
building world-class boats.
  These companies support good-paying jobs, train skilled workers, and 
strengthen communities across eastern North Carolina. They also 
showcase what American manufacturing can accomplish when we protect our 
coastal economy and preserve our rich maritime heritage.

                          ____________________
```
