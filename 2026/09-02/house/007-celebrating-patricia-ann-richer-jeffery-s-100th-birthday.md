# CELEBRATING PATRICIA ANN RICHER JEFFERY'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Rutherford, John H. (R000609) [R-FL]

```
        CELEBRATING PATRICIA ANN RICHER JEFFERY'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. Rutherford of Florida was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. RUTHERFORD. Mr. Speaker, I stand today in celebration of the 
100th birthday of Ponte Vedra, Florida's own Pat Jeffery.
  Pat was born in Miles City, Montana, on August 30, 1926, but spent 
much of her childhood in Indiana. She attended Indiana University, and 
after graduating, she moved to New York City to attend the Katharine 
Gibbs School.
  Pat soon began working as a publicity writer at NBC Studios. It was 
in New York City that Pat met her husband, Scott Jeffery. Soon, they 
were engaged. Scott was promoted and transferred to Colombia by the 
Colgate-Palmolive Company. Together, they moved to Colombia and married 
in the Panama Canal Zone on January 25, 1956.
  Over the next 30 years, they raised four children and built a 
wonderful life together in Colombia.
  Pat immersed herself in community service through their Episcopal 
church, helping to establish the Trinity school, which provided 
educational opportunities for children without access to schooling.
  Pat also helped other mothers in the area earn incomes to help 
support their families by teaching them how to make handbags and 
macrame. Some of those bags actually were eventually sold at 
Bloomingdale's and other boutiques throughout the United States.
  Pat even started her own tennis apparel company called Aire, which 
her daughter, Barbara, and the family helped grow.
  However, living in Colombia was becoming increasingly more dangerous 
for the family. After three decades in Colombia, Pat and Scott made the 
difficult decision to leave and return home to the United States.
  In 1986, they retired and moved to Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida. There, 
they embraced their new chapter in life and found a community filled 
with wonderful friends, endless activities, and a true sense of 
belonging.
  From Montana to New York City and Colombia to Ponte Vedra Beach, Pat 
has spent her life inspiring those around her with selflessness, 
curiosity, appreciation, and always a zest for life.
  Mr. Speaker, today, I recognize and honor Pat for living an 
extraordinary century filled with adventure, courage, friendship, and 
community service. I wish Pat a happy 100th birthday from all the 
constituents of Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida, and Florida's Fifth 
Congressional District. God bless her, and we pray for many more.

                          ____________________
```
