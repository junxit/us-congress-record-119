# CONDEMNING TRUMP'S TRADE WAR WITH CANADA

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
                CONDEMNING TRUMP'S TRADE WAR WITH CANADA

  (Mr. Kennedy of New York was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to condemn 
President Trump's illogical trade war with Canada and juvenile 
executive order to rename Lake Ontario.
  Since the White House can't figure out how to lower costs for 
American families or reach a trade deal with our closest ally, it is 
resorting to meaningless distractions, like renaming a Great Lake.
  While my Democratic colleagues and I have introduced legislation to 
stop this nonsense, we are focused on the issues that actually matter: 
ending the affordability crisis fueled by Trump's erratic tariffs and 
reckless policies and stopping the most corrupt administration in 
American history.
  Whether it is his endless war in Iran, gutting healthcare and 
nutritional assistance for millions of Americans, or a trade war with 
Canada, each manufactured crisis created by Trump shows just how self-
interested, incompetent, and careless he truly is.
  From communities like mine across the northern border, where ties to 
our Canadian neighbors run deep, through the interior and every other 
part of our great country, people simply cannot afford the increased 
costs of goods because of these tariffs.
  In western New York, family members cross the border to visit one 
another, and businesses rely on efficient cross-border commerce. Our 
restaurants, hotels, shops, and attractions depend on Canadian 
visitors. This relationship grows jobs and strengthens our economy.
  Right now, Canadians are making a decision not to visit or do 
business with our country, creating economic instability at a time when 
Americans are already stretched too thin.
  People can't afford to put food on the table because grocery costs 
are too high. They are having trouble paying their utility bills to 
keep the lights on. The cost of gas is skyrocketing, and healthcare 
costs are through the roof.
  At the same time, people are worried whether or not they are going to 
have a job in the morning.
  Trump's continued chaos is making it harder for families to plan, 
businesses to invest, workers to save, and communities to grow. 
Americans are bearing the consequences of this economic ambush as 
Trump's failed policies make life more expensive and less secure 
for working families across our Nation.

  These tariffs are nothing more than a Trump tax, and American 
families are paying the price everywhere. Instead of creating jobs, 
growing wages, and giving working people a fair shot, this 
administration has pursued policies that make it harder for our 
families to get ahead. Americans deserve better than a President who 
embarrasses us on the world stage and the Republicans in Congress who 
rubberstamp his inflationary policies.
  When voters put Democrats back into the majority this November, we 
will overturn these failed GOP policies, lower costs, end corruption, 
and restore the promise of the American Dream.
  Mr. Speaker, in the meantime, let's stop Trump's tax, end these 
tariffs, and make life more affordable for the American people.

                          ____________________
```
