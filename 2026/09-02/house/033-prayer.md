# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Gather us together, O God, in the company of those whom You have 
called to serve in this time and place. Then, may we, together, draw 
near to You with sincere hearts to receive the law You would place 
within them and to respond to the truth that You would write on our 
minds.
  Cleanse us from our own guilty consciences and free us from our 
tendencies to seek our own well-being, to make our own rules, and to 
listen to our own voices.
  Release us from all that keeps us from seeing You more clearly, 
loving You more dearly, and following You more nearly, this day and 
every day.
  Then, having placed our faith in You, may we hold on unswervingly to 
the hope we profess. Allow the fruit of our faith to be borne in our 
efforts, and may we be more inclined to support one another with love 
and mutual encouragement and to live lives reflective of Your grace and 
mercy.
  These things we pray in the strength of Your eternal love and in the 
power of Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
