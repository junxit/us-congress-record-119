# PROTECTING ECONOMIC AND ACADEMIC FREEDOM

**Speaking:** Gottheimer, Josh (G000583) [D-NJ]

```
                PROTECTING ECONOMIC AND ACADEMIC FREEDOM

  (Mr. GOTTHEIMER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GOTTHEIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise in support of my bill with Ms. 
Virginia Foxx of North Carolina, the Protect Economic and Academic 
Freedom Act.
  No student or professor should be shut out of research, study abroad 
opportunities, academic partnerships, or feel unwelcome in the 
classroom because of where they are from or what religion they 
practice.
  Yet, that is what boycott, divest, and sanction seeks to do, which 
targets one country and one religion: Israel and Judaism.
  My bill prevents discriminatory BDS practices and policies from being 
enacted at universities that undermine academic freedom by cutting off 
study abroad, joint research and exchange, restricting students' and 
faculty's ability to study what and where they choose.
  It is narrow, targeted, and doesn't touch anyone's speech. It doesn't 
touch protest or academic debate. It simply says: If your university 
that takes federal taxpayer dollars, it can't discriminate against 
academic partnerships or investments with one country. That is it.
  This has been our Federal funding policy for decades. This is why 
this bill passed out of committee with overwhelming bipartisanship 
support, including the ranking member and five other Democrats.
  I urge my colleagues on both sides of the aisle to support this 
bipartisan bill.

                          ____________________
```
