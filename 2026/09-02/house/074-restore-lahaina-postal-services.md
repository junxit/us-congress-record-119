# RESTORE LAHAINA POSTAL SERVICES

**Speaking:** Tokuda, Jill N. (T000487) [D-HI]

```
                    RESTORE LAHAINA POSTAL SERVICES

  (Ms. TOKUDA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. TOKUDA. Mr. Speaker, 3 years after the Lahaina wildfires, 
survivors are rebuilding and finally returning home. They should be 
welcomed back and not told that the basic services they had before the 
fire are now gone.

[[Page H5463]]

  Yet, the United States Postal Service is denying street delivery to 
rebuilt homes and forcing residents to use cluster mailboxes instead.
  That is a slap in the face. A cluster mailbox is not a welcome mat 
for families who fought to come home. It is an insult. Now, adding 
insult to injury, the U.S. Postal Service is considering permanently 
closing the downtown Lahaina post office damaged in the fires, with 
little notice and virtually no meaningful community input.
  The United States Postal Service needs to restore home delivery, stop 
the closure, and listen to Lahaina. Our community has already lost 
enough. Recovery means restoring what was lost, not taking more away.

                          ____________________
```
