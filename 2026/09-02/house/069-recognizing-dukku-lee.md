# RECOGNIZING DUKKU LEE

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                              {time}  1700
                         RECOGNIZING DUKKU LEE

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight Dukku Lee.
  Mr. Lee is retiring from his position as general manager of Anaheim 
Public Utilities, where he oversaw the electric and water utilities for 
over 350,000 residents, 15,000 businesses, and 25 million visitors 
annually.
  In his 33 years in the industry, Mr. Lee has guided major investments 
in Anaheim in groundwater treatment, underground power lines, new high-
voltage substations, renewable resources, and the new Sustainability 
Education Center.
  Mr. Lee has made an immense improvement to the city of Anaheim, and I 
am proud to work with him and to see all of his accomplishments.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Dukku Lee 
and his 33 years of work for the good city of Anaheim.
  I congratulate Mr. Lee, and I look forward to continuing to work with 
him in his next career.

                          ____________________
```
