# HONORING DOLLY PARTON

**Speaking:** Harshbarger, Diana (H001086) [R-TN], Cohen, Steve (C001068) [D-TN]

```
                         HONORING DOLLY PARTON

  (Mrs. HARSHBARGER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mrs. HARSHBARGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a true daughter 
of Tennessee, Dolly Parton--a woman who needs no introduction.
  Dolly was a native of my district in East Tennessee, and she was from 
Locust Ridge in Sevier County. She never forgot where she came from. 
Her music told the truth about hard work, faith, and family, and that 
truth is why the whole world fell in love with Dolly.
  But it was her heart that made her a legend back home. She rebuilt 
the community after the wildfires in 2016 that tore through Sevier 
County. She opened doors for children to learn to read through her 
Imagination Library. She supported healthcare and research for 
Tennessee families. She protected the mountains that she loved. Most 
people will know her theme park that is in the district called 
Dollywood.
  She was guided by her faith and her East Tennessee upbringing, and 
Dolly used her success to lift people up around her at every turn.
  My prayers are with her family and everyone who loved her. The 
Tennessee delegation is committed to ensuring that her legacy is 
remembered for generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield to the gentleman from Tennessee (Mr. Cohen), my 
colleague.
  Mr. COHEN. Mr. Speaker, this is my first opportunity to serve as the 
dean of the Tennessee delegation, and it didn't come too soon. Time is 
running out.
  Dolly Parton was a phenomenal Tennessean, probably the best 
representative we ever had. She never looked down on anybody. She had a 
big heart. Whenever there was a tragedy or somebody in need, she came 
forward and wanted it to be anonymous. She was a good human being, and 
she had a beautiful voice.
  I had a friend who was involved in the music business in the early 
seventies in Laurel Canyon when the Eagles and others were getting 
together. He told me about Dolly being out there, and that is where she 
got to know Linda Ronstadt and others. He said she had such a beautiful 
voice, and she did.
  She will be remembered forever for all she did for humanity. 
Tennessee is proud to have her and will always love her.
  Mrs. HARSHBARGER. Mr. Speaker, she was a funny lady. She had a lot of 
quick sayings--and I won't go into that--but we lost an icon, not just 
to us in Tennessee but to the world.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the House now observe a moment of silence in 
her memory.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will all Members and those in the gallery 
please rise for a moment of silence.

                          ____________________
```
