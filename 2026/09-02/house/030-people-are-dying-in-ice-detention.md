# PEOPLE ARE DYING IN ICE DETENTION

**Speaking:** Barragan, Nanette Diaz (B001300) [D-CA]

```
                   PEOPLE ARE DYING IN ICE DETENTION

  (Ms. Barragan of California was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Ms. BARRAGAN. Mr. Speaker, Edwin Lopez-Cornejo told his mother that 
he could no longer feel his face. He was losing feeling in his arm, and 
he was scared. He was inside an ICE detention facility despite having a 
valid work permit.
  Edwin suffered from serious medical conditions, but ICE denied him 
care and the medications he needed. Edwin

[[Page H5439]]

died on August 1 after just 6 weeks in ICE custody. He was only 41 
years old. He was a father.
  Edwin's story should stop us in our tracks because he is not alone. 
People are dying in ICE detention. People have been killed during 
immigration enforcement operations. The people the Trump administration 
is going after are not serious criminals or the worst of the worst.
  In July, more than one-half of the people arrested by ICE had no 
criminal convictions and no pending criminal charges. These are 
workers, parents, and caregivers. They are people who have lived in our 
communities for years, yet the administration is stripping away legal 
protections to detain and deport them.
  Look at Haitian families with temporary protected status. For years 
our government told them: You can come live here, and you can come and 
work here.
  They built their lives here. They pay taxes. They cared for our 
parents and our grandparents. In Florida alone, an estimated 93,000 
Haitian workers with TPS spent years caring for Holocaust survivors. 
Some had worked there for over two decades, and then this 
administration got rid of their protections. Scores of people lost 
their jobs.
  Those caregivers were not here illegally. The government changed 
their status so they could send them back to a country where their 
lives are in danger.
  Then, let's look at what happened to Lorenzo Salgado Araujo. Lorenzo 
was a 52-year-old father of three with no criminal record, and he had 
applied for a legal work permit. Lorenzo had lived in this country for 
nearly 35 years and built his own construction business. Yet, in July, 
ICE agents killed him while looking for another person.
  One of Lorenzo's sons, Ronaldo, watched the video of his father and 
said: ``My father was always a strong man and never wanted us to know 
if he was in pain. He never complained . . . but it hurt my heart to 
hear his scream.''
  Two fathers: One killed by ICE in the street; one dead after 6 weeks 
in ICE custody.

                              {time}  1145

  This abuse does not stop with immigrants. U.S. citizens are being 
rounded up and deported.
  When the Supreme Court allowed ICE to profile people racially during 
enforcement stops based on their race and their language, Supreme Court 
Justice Kavanaugh wrote that if you are a U.S. citizen, you are going 
to be free to go after just a brief encounter.
  That is not what happened to Brian Jose Morales Garcia. He is a U.S. 
citizen who was deported to Mexico after a traffic stop in Texas, even 
though he repeatedly told the ICE officer he was a U.S. citizen and 
asked to get the proof of citizenship at his home. They ignored him. 
They deported him.
  For 4 months, Brian was stuck in Mexico. Imagine this: an American 
citizen stuck in Mexico for 4 months, separated from your work and 
family before you are finally allowed to come back to this country and 
having to hire a lawyer.
  Since Trump returned to office, more than 50 people have died in ICE 
custody. Others, including U.S. citizens, have died during immigration 
enforcement operations.
  Congress must stop this out-of-control agency and hold them 
accountable for every death at the hands of ICE. People are dying. 
Americans are less safe because of this rogue agency that is out in the 
streets with masks and no identification.
  Mr. Speaker, this administration and Republicans in Congress continue 
to kill innocent people and make us less safe. This must stop.
```
