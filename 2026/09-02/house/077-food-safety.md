# FOOD SAFETY

```
                              {time}  1710
                              FOOD SAFETY

  (Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, I sit on the House Agriculture 
Committee and grew up a potato farmer's daughter in Buhl, Idaho. I now 
represent Maryland's most agricultural district, Maryland's Sixth 
District. It encompasses 1,200 farms in Frederick County alone, the 
Agricultural Reserve in Montgomery, and specialty crops and livestock 
farms in Garrett, Allegany, and Washington Counties.
  I represent all working families in the Sixth that eat food and trust 
that our government has the necessary resources and mechanisms in place 
to ensure that it is safe.
  Unfortunately, that trust has been broken. That probably is what 
Project 2025 and massive cuts to our agencies has been all about.
  This summer, more than 17,000 Americans were confirmed sick with 
cyclospora, the largest outbreak of its kind in our history. Nearly 500 
were hospitalized, and two died. The lettuce came from a facility that 
the FDA had not inspected in 7 years. That is not bad luck. It is a 
choice.
  The administration has cut the FDA by 23 percent. It is closing the 
USDA Beltsville Agricultural Research Center facility. It has cut USDA 
by 25 percent and the CDC by 28 percent. The administration is 
requiring States to not track cyclospora at all.
  We need to reinstate the resources necessary to make sure that our 
food system is safe. National security is food security.
```
