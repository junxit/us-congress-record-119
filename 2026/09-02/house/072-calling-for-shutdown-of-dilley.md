# CALLING FOR SHUTDOWN OF DILLEY

**Speaking:** Rivas, Luz M. (R000620) [D-CA]

```
                     CALLING FOR SHUTDOWN OF DILLEY

  (Ms. RIVAS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. RIVAS. Mr. Speaker, during the August work period, I visited the 
Dilley family immigration detention center with my colleagues to see 
conditions firsthand.
  Over 500 people are being detained in inhumane conditions, including 
pregnant women, children, infants, and babies who are just 2 months 
old. These individuals should be living their lives, getting ready for 
school, and raising their families. Children should not be detained.
  Dilley must be shut down, and ICE must be abolished.
  While Republicans continue to let ICE run out of control, working 
families are struggling to afford basic essentials like groceries.
  I visited the North Valley Caring Services in North Hills to see the 
work that the L.A. Regional Food Bank does to help families who are 
struggling with food insecurity. I spoke with healthcare workers about 
the importance of protecting Medicare for our constituents. I visited a 
Head Start program to learn about how Republicans' cuts make it harder 
for working families to thrive.
  What I heard from my constituents at all of these visits is that they 
need relief from Republicans' affordability crisis.

                          ____________________
```
