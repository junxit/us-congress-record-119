# BAN DATA CENTERS IN RESIDENTIAL COMMUNITIES

**Speaking:** Khanna, Ro (K000389) [D-CA]

```
              BAN DATA CENTERS IN RESIDENTIAL COMMUNITIES

  (Mr. KHANNA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KHANNA. Mr. Speaker, today, I am calling to ban data centers in 
residential communities and to ban Federal funding for Flock cameras.
  We can't have a surveillance state in America. We didn't fight a 
revolution to be ruled by tech billionaires.
  Americans are for innovation but not for the extraction of natural 
resources. Americans are for safety but not for the abuse of their 
privacy. We need guardrails on this technology.
  Communities should have the right to say ``no'' to a data center 
being shoved down their throats, and we should have the right for women 
not to be surveilled by Flock cameras by rogue police officers.
  As a Representative of Silicon Valley, I am calling for democratic 
accountability of this technology revolution. We the people should call 
the shots.

                          ____________________
```
