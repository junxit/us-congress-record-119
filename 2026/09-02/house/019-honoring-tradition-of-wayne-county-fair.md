# HONORING TRADITION OF WAYNE COUNTY FAIR

**Speaking:** Miller, Max L. (M001222) [R-OH]

```
                HONORING TRADITION OF WAYNE COUNTY FAIR

  (Mr. Miller of Ohio was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. MILLER of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the treasured 
northeast Ohio tradition in Ohio's Seventh Congressional District, the 
Wayne County Fair.
  It is the largest junior fair in the State, with more than 7,800 
junior fair

[[Page H5434]]

entries. In a State with 88 counties, that ranking speaks for itself. 
In a county of just 116,000 residents, the fair welcomes over 150,000 
visitors each year. This displays just how central agriculture truly is 
to our community in Ohio.
  The first two lines of motto of the Future Farmers of America, also 
known as the FFA, are: Learning to Do, Doing to Learn.
  I believe this perfectly captures the spirit of our 4-H and FFA youth 
here in Ohio. These students are up before sunrise, feeding their 
livestock before school. They communicate the importance of agriculture 
with pride. They act with integrity even when no one is watching. They 
consistently exemplify the grit, determination, and character that will 
make them the leaders of tomorrow.
  Behind them stands a remarkable community. This year alone, the 
junior fair auction exceeded $2 million, a testament to the families, 
businesses, and neighbors who invest in these students and believe in 
their future.
  The Wayne County Fair is more than an event. It is a tradition. It is 
a classroom, and it is a way of life for us in the Seventh District of 
Ohio. I couldn't be more honored to represent the students, families, 
volunteers, and community who make this fair what it is.
  The Wayne County Fair reflects the very best of the American spirit: 
hard work, responsibility, community, and a commitment to the next 
generation.
  When I see the young people of Wayne County carrying those values 
forward, I have great confidence in the future of American agriculture 
and the next generation of leaders who will carry it forward.

                          ____________________
```
