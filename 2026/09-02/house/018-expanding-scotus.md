# EXPANDING SCOTUS

**Speaking:** Mejia, Analilia (M001246) [D-NJ]

```
                            EXPANDING SCOTUS

  (Ms. Mejia of New Jersey was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, our forefathers showed tremendous wisdom at 
the founding of our Nation. They created a republic based on democratic 
values but remained keenly aware of the danger power, ambition, and 
corruption could present to future generations.
  Today, House Republicans are asking Congress to surrender its power, 
a power that rightfully belongs to the American people.
  Our Founding Fathers gave Congress countervailing power to the 
executive, which this ideologically captured Court is actively 
attempting to upend. That is disgraceful, and if we do not forcefully 
push back, it will not end.
  Expanding the Court is one of the clearest tools we have to restore 
its integrity. Ethics reforms and staggered term limits would prevent 
any single President from stripping the American people of our 
democracy.
  Every day we let this administration tighten its grip on our highest 
court of the land puts every single constitutional protection we enjoy 
at risk.
  I did not come to Congress to watch our democracy get dismantled in 
silence. I will not watch it happen now.

  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to gather the intestinal fortitude 
to do what is right in this moment because history and the American 
people are watching.


                       recognizing niheema malloy

  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the leadership of 
Niheema Malloy, the first woman and the first Black woman to serve as 
chief of police in Maplewood, New Jersey.
  Chief Malloy was appointed to this position with over 25 years of law 
enforcement experience. Under her leadership, Maplewood would have a 
department built on community partnership, officers trained in de-
escalation, in crisis response, and in listening before a crisis ever 
happens.
  Chief Malloy is committed to putting Maplewood first by ensuring that 
community safety and protection go hand in hand with the well-being of 
our residents.
  New Jersey is proud of Chief Niheema Malloy. Her leadership reflects 
the very best of our communities, and I thank her for her remarkable 
service.


         congratulating major general christopher d. schneider

  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Major General Christopher D. Schneider for his new role as the 
commanding general of Picatinny Arsenal in addition to serving as the 
portfolio acquisition executive for agile sustainment and ammunition.
  General Schneider brings to this role more than three decades of 
distinguished service to our Nation. A graduate of the United States 
Military Academy, he began his career as an infantry officer, serving 
with the 1st Infantry Division, the 18th Airborne Corps, and the 82nd 
Airborne Division. Also, he served with the 75th Ranger Regiment, with 
deployments to Bosnia-Herzegovina, Iraq, and Afghanistan. He later 
transitioned to the Army Acquisition Corps, where he held senior 
leadership positions focused on strengthening our Nation's military 
capabilities and advancing critical technologies.
  As commanding general of Picatinny Arsenal, General Schneider takes 
on an important responsibility for the U.S. Army and will be supported 
by one of the most talented and hardworking workforces in the Army 
right here in New Jersey's 11th Congressional District.
  I congratulate General Schneider on his new role and look forward to 
working with him to support Picatinny Arsenal, its workforce, and our 
national security.
  I welcome General Schneider to Picatinny and thank him for his 
service to our country.
```
