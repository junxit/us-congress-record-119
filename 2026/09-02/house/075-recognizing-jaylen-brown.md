# RECOGNIZING JAYLEN BROWN

**Speaking:** Pressley, Ayanna (P000617) [D-MA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JAYLEN BROWN

  (Ms. PRESSLEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. PRESSLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the contributions of 
NBA Finals MVP Jaylen Brown to the city of Boston on and off the court.
  Born in Marietta, Georgia, and drafted number 3 overall by the 
Celtics from the University of California, Jaylen embraced our 
community and the city of Boston from the day he arrived in 2016.
  Fiercely competitive on the basketball court, never missing the 
playoffs in his 10 seasons with the Celtics, he has been equally driven 
to uplift those around him through mentorship in his nonprofit, Boston 
XChange, which seeks to build wealth in communities of color, as well 
as his 7uice Foundation, connecting young people in and around Boston 
with opportunities.
  In 2022, just before the Celtics turned their season around and made 
it all the way to the NBA Finals, Jaylen stated simply: ``The energy is 
about to shift.''
  Mr. Speaker, thanks to the example, impact, and influence of this 
brilliant and proud Black man, the energy has shifted in Boston. Jaylen 
delivered banner number 18, the most in NBA history. His legacy as an 
advocate, truth teller, community builder, justice seeker, and champion 
will endure.
  Job well done. We thank him. His faith, consistency, and hard work 
have certainly paid off.

                          ____________________
```
