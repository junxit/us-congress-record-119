# RECOGNIZING WILL PLUNKETT

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                       RECOGNIZING WILL PLUNKETT

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my 
constituents, Will Plunkett, who was recently selected by the Baltimore 
Orioles in the 2026 Major League Baseball draft.
  Will graduated from Mamaroneck High School this spring, where he led 
the baseball team to the Section 1 championship as the starting 
shortstop.
  During his senior season, Will had nearly a .500 batting average, 
hitting 6 home runs, knocking in 28 RBIs, and stealing 23 bases.
  Mamaroneck High School baseball coach Mike Chiapparelli describes 
Plunkett as a heck of a baseball player, being able to stay calm in a 
high-pressure situation, kind of like politics.
  Mr. Speaker, from the floor of the House of Representatives, I 
congratulate the Plunkett family and Will personally on this momentous 
accomplishment. I look forward to seeing Will on a professional 
baseball field very soon and on my television set thereafter.

                          ____________________
```
