# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF BARBARA MATTESON

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Adelita S. (G000606) [D-AZ]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF BARBARA MATTESON

  (Mrs. GRIJALVA asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Barbara Matteson of Tucson, Arizona.
  I can't think of a major education event, rally, or protest in Tucson 
where I didn't see Barbara front and center.
  A lifelong educator, the first in her family to go to college, 
Barbara spent over 38 years as a classroom teacher in the Tucson 
Unified School Direct. As mentioned in her obituary, her ability to 
make learning fun and meaningful left a lasting impact. Being out and 
about in Tucson with Barbara inevitably meant being stopped by a former 
student who would share their stories of how she influenced their 
lives.
  As a union organizer and fierce advocate for public education, 
Barbara dedicated herself to fighting for our students, our teachers, 
and our schools. She showed up, She spoke up, and she never stopped 
standing up for what she believed was right.
  Barbara was not only a champion for education, she was a friend and 
someone who made our community stronger through her service and her 
commitment.
  To Barbara, I thank her for always fighting for our students, 
teachers, and schools. To Barbara's family, I thank them for sharing 
her with us.
  Mr. Speaker, may my friend rest in peace. I will always remember her.

                          ____________________
```
