# HONORING BILL DOBA

**Speaking:** Baumgartner, Michael (B001322) [R-WA]

```
                           HONORING BILL DOBA

  (Mr. Baumgartner of Washington was recognized to address the House 
for 5 minutes.)
  Mr. BAUMGARTNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a legend in 
Washington State University Cougars' athletics, former head football 
coach, Bill Doba, who passed away last week at the age of 85.
  Bill was a great man and a great football coach, a longtime defensive 
coordinator, and 5-year head coach for the Cougars from 2003 to 2007. 
In all, he worked 19 seasons at WSU and was the lead architect of the 
renowned Palouse Posse defense, which led the country in 1994.
  Bill was also a very devoted husband. Many knew of his lifetime 
romance with his wonderful wife, Judy. Judy herself was a great woman. 
I knew her personally when she volunteered frequently in my mother's 
kindergarten classroom at Franklin Elementary.
  Bill Doba left a great impact on the entire State of Washington and 
the Pullman community, and he is remembered fondly.
  Given that this is Apple Cup week, Mr. Speaker, I think it is also 
worth noting that Coach Bill Doba had a winning record against the 
Huskies and gave them a spanking in his last Apple Cup.
  Rest in peace, Bill Doba, and thank you for all you have done for 
Cougar nation.


                          Honoring Walt Worthy

  Mr. BAUMGARTNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a great member of 
the Spokane community, Mr. Walt Worthy. Many people know Walt and his 
wonderful wife, Karen, for their tremendous work to revitalize the 
Davenport Hotel. Today, I honor Walt for his extremely generous 
establishment of the Karen Worthy Memorial Wildfire Grant that will 
fund personal $10,000 donations to victims of the Spokane fires.
  In total, his generous donation could approach beyond $5 million, 
perhaps even $10 million, depending on how many fire victims are deemed 
eligible. It was only fitting that this announcement was made at Balboa 
Elementary School in the neighborhood of the fires and named in honor 
of his beloved wife, Karen. Karen taught first grade at Balboa 
Elementary School for nearly 20 years as Walt transitioned from his 
career as a survival specialist with the United States Air Force into a 
career as a builder and a developer.

[[Page H5435]]

  I thank Walt Worthy for his generosity to the victims of these 
terrible fires. I thank him for all he has done for Washington State, 
our Spokane community, and indeed these United States of America. I am 
pleased to share his lifetime romance in memory of Karen here on the 
floor of Congress.


                   Honoring Ambassador Timothy Carney

  Mr. BAUMGARTNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a great patriot, 
a friend, and a giant of American diplomacy, Ambassador Tim Carney, who 
passed away earlier this month after a long fight with cancer.
  Ambassador Carney was the 14th United States Ambassador to Sudan and 
the 45th United States Ambassador to Haiti.
  I got to know Ambassador Carney during my year at the U.S. Embassy in 
Baghdad during the Iraq surge where we worked closely together on 
issues related to Baghdad.
  Ambassador Carney is one of those Americans that you read about in 
storybooks: an adventurer, a scholar, and indeed, truly a diplomat.
  His wife, Vicki Butler, is equally amazing. Vicki came from a 
prominent Spokane family. They met in Southeast Asia and had a 
patriotic romance that spanned the globe and made a lasting impact on 
Washington State and the United States of America.
  When Tim finished his diplomatic career, he came back to Spokane with 
Vicki and served as a very important director of our museum of arts and 
culture, helping bridge a challenging period in that institution's 
time.
  I remember Tim fondly. Here in this photo you can see Tim and Vicki 
with me and my wife, Eleanor, and I look to them as an inspiration in 
my own romance with my wonderful wife.
  Tim, you will not be forgotten. Vicki, you are loved, and we thank 
both of you for your tremendous impact on these United States of 
America.
```
