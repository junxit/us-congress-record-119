# HONORING THE LIFE OF JOHN P. O'MALLEY

**Speaking:** Lawler, Michael (L000599) [R-NY]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF JOHN P. O'MALLEY

  (Mr. LAWLER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1

[[Page H5462]]

minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LAWLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of John P. 
O'Malley, a devoted husband, father, grandfather, and respected law 
enforcement investigator, who passed away peacefully on July 16 at age 
73.
  Born and raised in the Bronx, John spent more than 20 years with the 
New York City Police Department, becoming one of the Bronx's most 
respected homicide detectives. Known for his integrity, 
professionalism, and investigative skill, he was widely regarded as a 
detective's detective.
  After retiring from the NYPD, John continued his commitment to 
justice as an investigator with the United States Attorney's Office for 
the Southern District of New York, earning the respect of prosecutors, 
agents, and investigators alike.
  But above all, John was a devoted family man. He and his wife, Annie, 
built their life in Pearl River, where they raised their children.
  My thoughts and prayers are with Annie; his sons; and especially his 
beloved grandchildren, who were his pride and joy.
  John will be remembered for his honor, humility, humor, and 
unwavering dedication to his country, community, friends, and family. 
May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
