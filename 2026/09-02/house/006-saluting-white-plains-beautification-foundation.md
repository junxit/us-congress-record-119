# SALUTING WHITE PLAINS BEAUTIFICATION FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
            SALUTING WHITE PLAINS BEAUTIFICATION FOUNDATION

  (Mr. Latimer of New York was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I would like to salute the work of the men 
and women volunteers and leaders of the White Plains Beautification 
Foundation. They have amassed an army of committed citizens under 
President Terry Hanson to get their hands dirty, and sometimes a little 
back and leg cramps, with sweat equity that enhances the visual 
improvements to the many city parks and open spaces across White 
Plains. This adds to the city's appeal to new residents and longtime 
neighbors alike.
  The Adopt-a-Park program, the extensive tree-planting efforts, and 
the improvements on Mamaroneck Avenue and North Street as travelers 
enter White Plains are very real achievements by this group and their 
generous base of donors.
  In addition to their president, let me recognize former presidents 
who serve as current directors: Mary Merenda, Deborah Donahoe, Beth 
Wallach, Marie Silverman Marich, Jean Bello, and Barbara Ramsdell; and 
former Westchester County Commissioner P. Lynn Oliva,

[[Page H5428]]

who heads up the Adopt-a-Park program.
  Mr. Speaker, from the floor of the U.S. House of Representatives, we 
praise the work of the White Plains Beautification Foundation for all 
they have accomplished.


                     recognizing co-op city leaders

  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I would like to continue to recognize the 
men and women who are leaders in their respective buildings in Co-op 
City in the Bronx, the largest housing cooperative in the world.
  These leaders organize community events, holiday celebrations, and 
fundraisers and help advocate for building improvements and upgrades.
  We salute the following individuals for their tireless commitment to 
their community and their neighbors: Douglas Handler, Building 3; Dawn 
Mitchell, Building 4; Debbie Lowden, Building 5; KaTrina Dinham, 
Building 12; Josie Ferguson, Building 14; Dorothea Castle, Building 
15A; Sandy Krasnov, Building 15B; Jacqueline Salmon, Building 16; 
Francine Jones, Building 20; Toran Smith, Sr., Building 21; Joan June, 
Building 22; Denise Gerrald, Building 25; Michael Adolphus, Building 
29; Susan Lecoq, Building 30; Kim Toulson, Building 31; and Melanie 
Clunie, Building 34. There are clearly a lot of these folks.
  There are also townhouse associations: Kenneth Graham, Adler Place; 
Eva Lazaar, Broun Place; Anika Green-Watson, Cooper Place; Jacqueline 
Mason, Debs Place; and Ida Clark, Earhart Lane.
  Mr. Speaker, from the floor of the House of Representatives, we 
commend all of these Co-op building association presidents for their 
dedication in improving the community around them.
```
