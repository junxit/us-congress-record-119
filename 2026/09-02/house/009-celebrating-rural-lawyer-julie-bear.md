# CELEBRATING RURAL LAWYER JULIE BEAR

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                  CELEBRATING RURAL LAWYER JULIE BEAR

  (Mr. Flood of Nebraska was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize an often 
overlooked segment of our Nation's legal profession: rural lawyers.
  Across our First District and in many of our communities, rural 
lawyers represent the very best of the profession, selfless service, 
and meaningful local impact.
  Today, I want to highlight the remarkable work of Julie Bear of 
Plattsmouth, specializing in criminal defense, family law, estate 
planning, personal injury, and more. Julie has built a reputation that 
is nothing short of exceptional.

[[Page H5429]]

  Before joining her firm, she served as a public defender for Cass 
County. In that role, she achieved the dismissal of a double-murder 
charge against a court-appointed client who had been wrongly accused.
  She is a graduate of Creighton University School of Law. She 
consistently rises to the occasion and goes above and beyond for her 
clients.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize her accomplishments and 
commend her as one of Nebraska's most skilled rural criminal defense 
attorneys.

  Today, I highlight and detail the larger-than-life career of Doug 
Stratton of Norfolk. Doug's focus areas include criminal defense, civil 
litigation, as well as domestic work.
  Many of you may have already heard of Doug through his breakthrough 
relitigation of the Beatrice Six. In sum, thanks to his belief in his 
clients' wrongful conviction and new evidence, Doug was able to award 
the defendants nearly $30 million from the United States Supreme Court.
  A native of northeast Nebraska, Doug worked as a small-town lawyer 
after he earned his J.D. from Yale Law School. Even when big-town law 
firms came to him to take up cases, Doug continuously worked on behalf 
of rural Nebraskans.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to highlight Doug's remarkable career and 
reputation.


                 Recognizing Rural Lawyer Bre Flaherty

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an often overlooked 
segment of our Nation's legal profession: rural lawyers.
  Across our First District and in many of our communities, rural 
lawyers represent the very best of the profession: selfless service and 
meaningful local impact.
  Today, I highlight the work of Norfolk's own Bre Flaherty. Through 
her career at the Platte County Attorney's Office, Bre built a strong 
reputation for excellence and community service, earning her 
appointment earlier this year as a county court judge in Nebraska's 
Fifth Judicial District.
  Her professionalism, grit, and passion for juvenile law have all 
paved the way for success in serving fellow Nebraskans. Earning her 
J.D. from the University of Nebraska College of Law, she has remained 
engaged in giving back to the communities and the experiences that 
shaped her.
  Mr. Speaker, I am truly honored to highlight Bre's work. I remember 
her as a mock trial student when I was the team coach. She should be 
celebrated for her community service and her integrity, which continue 
to guide her career.


                  Recognizing Rural Lawyer Tim Wollmer

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an often overlooked 
segment of our Nation's legal profession: rural lawyers. Across our 
First District and in many of our communities, rural lawyers represent 
the very best of the profession: selfless service and meaningful local 
impact.
  Today, I highlight the distinguished career of Tim Wollmer of David 
City with a practice spanning family law, business matters, civil 
litigation, estate planning, criminal defense, and more.
  Tim's integrity and steadfast commitment to the truth exemplify the 
vital role lawyers play in their communities. As an attorney, Tim has 
provided essential legal services to central and eastern Nebraska for 
more than 17 years, helping individuals and families navigate complex 
legal and financial matters.
  A graduate of Creighton University School of Law, Tim has earned the 
trust and the respect of the David City community and the surrounding 
areas.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Tim's accomplishments and his 
service today on the House floor.
```
