# PRESENTING CONGRESSIONAL BASEBALL GAME TROPHY

**Speaking:** Williams, Roger (W000816) [R-TX], Sanchez, Linda T. (S001156) [D-CA]

```
             PRESENTING CONGRESSIONAL BASEBALL GAME TROPHY

  (Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I yield to the gentlewoman from 
California (Ms. Sanchez), my friend and manager of the Democratic 
baseball team.
  Ms. SANCHEZ. Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Coach Roger Williams and the 
Republican team on their victory in the Congressional Baseball Game. It 
is well-deserved.
  I have to say I was very proud of how hard the Democrats worked this 
year. They spent the entire spring improving their batting, and it 
showed from the plate, even if it didn't show in the box score. Morgan 
McGarvey led and went 4 for 4, including a ground rule double that 
nearly cleared the fence. Dave Min added two RBI singles up the middle. 
Johnny O was hitting the ball well all night but got robbed by an 
absolutely outstanding catch from Senator Schmitt. Please don't let the 
other Chamber know I just congratulated a Senator.
  As proud as I am of this team, I am even more proud of what we all 
accomplished together, Democrats and Republicans alike. I have always 
said that two things that bring people together are sports and music. 
After playing in this game for nearly 20 years and coaching the 
Democratic team for the last 3, I believe that now more than ever.
  Despite our political differences, Republicans and Democrats came 
together to enjoy America's pastime, celebrate our Nation's 250th 
anniversary, and raise money for a good cause.
  Together, we raised more than $3 million for D.C.-based charities and 
scholarships for area students, and that is something we all can be 
proud of.
  Again, I congratulate Coach Williams and the entire Republican team. 
I am already looking forward to next year when Democrats have a chance 
to break your winning streak and reclaim that trophy.

                              {time}  1640

  Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I thank the manager very much for 
those kind words.
  Mr. Speaker, on June 10, 2026, we had a game at Nationals Park. 
Nearly 40,000 people attended, breaking a record. As we have already 
heard, $3.2 million was raised for charity. We had co-MVPs for the 
Republican team. We didn't play that good, but we played good enough to 
win.
  I thank Greg Steube who pitched a full seven innings and in seven 
innings threw only 97 pitches. I also thank Senator Schmitt for making 
a catch that made ESPN's top 10 catches for that week. We are proud of 
that.
  We won seven in a row, and we plan on doing better next year. We have 
lost some players, and I thank Morgan Luttrell and Randy Feenstra for 
all the time that they have committed to the game. To recover from the 
loss of players, you have to go recruit players. We are trying to do 
that now so we can be competitive, Linda, when we play next year. That 
is what we want to do is be competitive.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank all of my players for their hard work. We had a 
good first workout today. It was not too cold. It was good. We played 
pretty good this morning. The game is great. Baseball is the greatest 
game there is. It is always there. We plan on being as good as we can 
play next year if we can get the right people, but it is an honor to 
represent the team that we have. It is an honor to take this trophy. 
For the seventh year in a row, Republicans have received this, so let's 
get the trophy.
  The Democrats have a good team. They are very competitive.
  May God bless everybody. See you next year.

                          ____________________
```
