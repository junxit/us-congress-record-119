# HONORING THE LIFE OF RYLEE ROSE BELZA

**Speaking:** Gallagher, James (G000607) [R-CA]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF RYLEE ROSE BELZA

  (Mr. Gallagher of California was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Mr. GALLAGHER. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor the life of Rylee 
Rose Belza, a beloved 11-year-old from Marysville who entered into 
eternity with her Savior on July 8.
  Rylee was the cherished daughter of Dom and Julia Belza and a loving 
sister whose joy filled her family's life.
  Our hearts are with Dom and Julia. I have seen the deep faith, 
amazing grace, and love that has been guiding the Belza family during 
this painful time.
  Rylee was a bright light. She loved her family, her friends, 
softball, and fishing. Her church and her faith in the Lord were 
central to her life.
  The picture that really stuck with me at her memorial was Rylee 
worshiping with arms stretched wide even as she was battling cancer. 
She carried joy into every room and made people laugh. She consistently 
placed herself behind others.
  She had a beautiful voice, and she loved to sing ``The Star-Spangled 
Banner.'' She wanted to be the President. She told her classmates as 
much, and, Mr. Speaker, I bet you she would have been.
  The Yuba-Sutter community has responded with compassion, love, and 
prayer for the Belza family.
  Today, I ask the House to join me in praying for Rylee's family and 
every person her life touched. Rylee's life was far too short, but her 
outsized legacy will remain with her family and her community for many 
years to come.

                          ____________________
```
