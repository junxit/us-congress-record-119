# CATHERINE WATTS CELEBRATES 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Cline, Ben (C001118) [R-VA]

```
                              {time}  1030
               CATHERINE WATTS CELEBRATES 100TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. Cline of Virginia was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CLINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Catherine Watts of 
Buchanan, Virginia, who celebrated her 100th birthday on August 17, 
2026.
  For 100 years, Catherine's life has been rooted in Buchanan. She has 
built a life defined by faith, family, hard work, and service to her 
community.
  For more than 75 years, she has been a devoted member of Trinity 
United Methodist Church, serving on committees, singing in the choir, 
and continuing to give back to her church and her community.
  Catherine spent 45 years with Lee's Carpets and Burlington 
Industries, raised a family, and served organizations throughout the 
region.
  She and her late husband, Joseph, shared 53 years of marriage and 
raised a family grounded in faith and strong values. Catherine says: 
``God has been good to me.'' Her life is a testament to those 
blessings.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Catherine Watts on 100 
remarkable years and wishing her continued health and happiness.


          Recognizing Phoenix Wood 16U Fastpitch Softball Team

  Mr. CLINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Virginia 
Phoenix Wood 16U Fastpitch softball team of Vinton, Virginia, for 
capturing the 2026 USA Softball One True National Championship. These 
talented young women completed an undefeated tournament run, averaging 
10 runs per game, while outscoring their opponents 67-9, and finishing 
with an outstanding .490 team batting average.
  This outstanding team includes Alexis McFarland, Lilly Gillispie, 
Addie Lowry, Tinley Humphries, Averi Fuller, Heidi Dixon, Jocelyn 
Weddle, Morgan Smith, Elissa Caricofe, Madelynn Ray, Katelyn Mills, 
Olivia Pierce, Kenley Cundiff, Gianna Pitzer, Zoe Sartin, Emma Sartin, 
Madison McDaniel, Ava DeJesus, Matilyn Gouldthorpe, and Ashleigh 
Johnson.
  I also commend Head Coach Allen Wood and Assistant Coaches Kassie 
Brammer and Ashley McCallum for their exceptional leadership and 
dedication to these young women.
  I congratulate the Virginia Phoenix on this remarkable achievement. 
Their hard work, sportsmanship, and perseverance have brought great 
pride to Vinton, to the Roanoke Valley, and the Commonwealth of 
Virginia. I wish them continued success.


              Recognizing the Strasburg High Football Team

  Mr. CLINE. I rise today, Mr. Speaker, to recognize an extraordinary 
achievement by a high school football program from the Shenandoah 
Valley of Virginia. The Strasburg High School Rams captured the first 
State football championship in their school history, defeating Glenvar 
High School 49-27 in the

[[Page H5431]]

Class 2 State final on December 13, 2025.
  Strasburg's come-from-behind victory under the leadership of second 
year Head Coach Tripp Lamb showed resilience and composure. Senior 
Brady Knight, playing both running back and linebacker, helped lead the 
comeback and finished as one of three Rams to rush for over 100 yards. 
Juniors Quincy Williams and Linkyn Bosworth also surpassed the century 
mark on the ground, while Williams delivered game-changing defensive 
play. Quarterback Brayden Hough added a late rushing score to seal the 
victory.
  Mr. Speaker, this win belongs to not only this year's team, but to 
decades of Strasburg athletes and coaches, including Jeff Smoot, Glenn 
Proctor, and Mark Roller, who laid the foundation for success.
  This championship is a testament to perseverance, teamwork, and a 
community that never stopped believing. I proudly commend the Strasburg 
High School Rams on making history.


                      National News on Immigration

  Mr. CLINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to speak about the recent 
arrests of 1,328 illegal immigrants across Virginia. Among those 
arrested were individuals wanted for serious crimes, including murder, 
rape, sexual assault, kidnapping, DUI, and attempted murder.
  During the Biden administration, our immigration laws went 
unenforced. When individuals who entered our country illegally are 
allowed to remain in our communities, the rule of law is undermined and 
public safety is put at risk. Govern Spanberger and prosecutors like 
Fairfax Commonwealth Attorney Steve Descano are making it harder to 
enforce our immigration laws, weaken cooperation with ICE, and remove 
dangerous criminals from our communities.
  The Trump administration and House Republicans are taking action to 
restore accountability and protect our communities. These efforts not 
only make Virginia safer but also strengthen public safety across our 
entire Nation.
```
