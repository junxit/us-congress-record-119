# CONCERNS ON PROLIFERATION OF DATA CENTERS

**Speaking:** Ansari, Yassamin (A000381) [D-AZ]

```
               CONCERNS ON PROLIFERATION OF DATA CENTERS

  (Ms. Ansari of Arizona was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Ms. ANSARI. Mr. Speaker, this week, Donald Trump attacked communities 
across the country that are raising concerns about the proliferation of 
gigantic, power-hungry data centers. He called them backwards and poor.
  According to the President, Americans should welcome data centers 
without question if they want to become ``successful and rich.'' His 
exact declaration was: ``Let data reign.''
  That is a wildly out-of-touch response to Americans who are worried 
about their electricity bills, their water supply, and the future of 
their communities, all of which are being made dramatically worse by 
this administration.
  I represent Phoenix, Arizona, the Nation's fifth largest city, and we 
don't need any more data centers in our communities right now. We are 
already on the front lines of a severe and historic water crisis. The 
Trump administration just slashed our Colorado River water allocation 
by nearly 30 percent.
  Meanwhile, a single data center can consume tens if not hundreds of 
millions of gallons of water every year. If every currently planned 
project in the Phoenix area is built, data center water demand could 
increase by more than 450 percent.
  It is extremely alarming, and my constituents are right to express 
their concerns on how our government allocates its resources, including 
by providing immense tax breaks to ultra-rich AI and data center 
companies.
  Our electricity grid is also under enormous strain. Arizona families 
are facing higher utility bills, most especially as a result of Trump's 
illegal war in Iran, all while data centers demand staggering amounts 
of energy. Research has found that these facilities can make 
surrounding neighborhoods as much as four degrees hotter, in a city 
like Phoenix where extreme heat already kills people.
  These are not irrational concerns. Even Vice President JD Vance 
admitted that 99 percent--his words--of the backlash against data 
centers is happening in places where they are driving up utility bills.
  Protecting Arizona's water is not backwards. Keeping electricity 
affordable enough for seniors to turn their air conditioners on during 
the summer is not poor, and allowing the wealthiest companies on the 
planet to consume our water and power while working families absorb the 
cost is not progress.
  But here is the real reason that Donald Trump is so eager to tell 
communities to stop pushing back against data centers: Donald Trump is 
the sole beneficiary of a trust holding nearly 115 million shares of 
Trump Media. Trump Media is pursuing a pending $6 billion merger with 
TAE Technologies, an energy company that explicitly markets its 
technology for AI data center expansion.
  In other words, the President himself has an enormous financial 
interest in a company positioning itself to benefit from the growth of 
the data center energy market. He is once again using his Presidency to 
enrich himself and his family.
  That creates a glaring conflict of interest when it comes to Trump 
using the power and platform of the Presidency to attack communities 
resisting the construction of new data centers.
  Americans deserve to know whether he is speaking for the public 
interest or promoting his own investment portfolio.

                              {time}  1130

  I support Arizona Attorney General Kris Mayes' call for a statewide 
pause. Arizona should not approve another massive data center until we 
have a comprehensive plan to protect our water, our electrical grid, 
and Arizona ratepayers.
  Congress also needs to investigate the President's conflict of 
interest and demand full transparency about how financial holdings 
intersect with his administration's data center and energy policies.
  We can believe in American innovation without handing Big Tech a 
blank check on our water supply and electricity bills. Arizonans are 
not backward and poor. We are tired of being told to sacrifice more so 
that billionaires and the President himself can become richer.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to refrain from 
engaging in personalities toward the President.

                          ____________________
```
