# HONORING EARL "SKIP" GINGERICH, JR.

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
                 HONORING EARL ``SKIP'' GINGERICH, JR.

  (Mr. LANGWORTHY asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and the 
service of Marilla town supervisor Earl ``Skip'' Gingerich, Jr.
  Skip was a farmer, a patriot, and a man who deeply loved his 
community. His untimely passing is a profound loss for the people of 
Marilla.
  As a lifelong resident, Skip understood what made Marilla special, 
and he understood that preserving its traditions and rural way of life 
required work and dedication.
  I had the honor of joining him at Marilla's America 250 celebration, 
where he inaugurated a liberty bell as a reminder that freedom is never 
free.
  He tended to the town with the same care that he devoted to his 
third-generation family farm. He was a proud advocate for agriculture 
and a true steward of the land. Whether at the barn or the town hall, 
Skip embodied integrity, hard work, faith, and service.
  My prayers are with his family, his friends, and the entire Marilla 
community at this difficult time.
  May God bless Skip Gingerich, and may he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
