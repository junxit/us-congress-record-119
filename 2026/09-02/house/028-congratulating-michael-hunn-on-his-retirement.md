# CONGRATULATING MICHAEL HUNN ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
             CONGRATULATING MICHAEL HUNN ON HIS RETIREMENT

  (Mr. Correa of California was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to congratulate my good friend 
Michael Hunn on his retirement from CalOptima Health as its CEO. 
Throughout his time at CalOptima, Michael expanded Medi-Cal services 
and increased community investment initiatives for our working families 
throughout Orange County.
  As CEO, Michael followed through with his commitment to supporting 
our most vulnerable populations by creating a safety net so they could 
receive the healthcare services that they deserve.
  Because of Michael's efforts, Orange County today is healthier, 
stronger, and more resilient than ever before.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask all of you to join me in congratulating Michael 
for his accomplishments and innovative leadership.


               Recognizing Bill Thompson of Orange County

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize Orange County 
resident Bill Thompson for his generous contributions and support of 
Lestonnac Free Clinic.
  For years, Bill has worked with Lestonnac to provide critical 
healthcare for uninsured communities throughout southern California. 
With Bill's help and the help of other generous volunteers, Lestonnac 
has executed on its clear vision, expanding healthcare so that nobody 
goes without.
  Just last year, Bill worked with Lestonnac to open the Nation's first 
dedicated free surgery clinic, a groundbreaking initiative to provide 
life-changing procedures to veterans and others, no questions asked.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Bill for his 
commitment to helping our neighbors in need.


              Celebrating Assemblywoman Sharon Quirk-Silva

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to celebrate Assemblymember 
Sharon Quirk-Silva's 12-year career in the California State Assembly.
  Throughout her time representing north Orange County, she has led 
with vigor and determination and has set the standard for what it means 
to truly be a public servant. She supported our veterans, expanded 
affordable housing options, and secured millions of dollars for our 
working families here in Orange County.
  As she marks the close of a 12-year career in the California 
Legislature, Sharon will be leaving a legacy of hard work, partnership, 
and accomplishment that have strengthened our communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues today to join me in honoring Sharon 
Quirk-Silva's lasting impact on our communities. I thank Madam Quirk-
Lady.


                      Recognizing Dr. Ana Nogales

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Ana Nogales 
and her 30 years of leadership and service with Casa de la Familia.
  In 1996, Dr. Nogales founded Casa de la Familia with a vision of 
helping individuals heal their wounds of psychological trauma. Since 
then, Dr. Nogales and Casa de la Familia have followed through on that 
vision.
  From mental health services to stigma reduction, their work has 
impacted the lives of many here in California and Orange County.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Dr. Nogales 
and Casa de la Familia for 30 years of very important and critical 
work. We wish them another 30 years of success in Orange County.
  I congratulate and thank Dr. Ana Nogales and wish her a happy 30.
```
