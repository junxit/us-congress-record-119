# HONORING GRAND MASTER NAVIGATOR SESARIO SEWRALUR

**Speaking:** King-Hinds, Kimberlyn (K000404) [R-MP]

```
            HONORING GRAND MASTER NAVIGATOR SESARIO SEWRALUR

  (Ms. King-Hinds of the Northern Mariana Islands was recognized to 
address the House for 5 minutes.)
  Ms. KING-HINDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Grand Master Navigator 
Sesario Sewralur, captain of the Alingano Maisu, a cultural teacher, 
steward, and guardian of ancestral wayfinding knowledge.
  A son of Satawal in the Federated States of Micronesia and the son of 
legendary master navigator Mau Piailug, Sesario began sailing with his 
father at just 5 years old. He went on to lead 20 voyages across the 
Marianas, Palau, Yap, Taiwan, and Japan, helping preserve the ancient 
art of navigating the Pacific without modern instruments.
  Sesario carried forward his father's legacy and helped ensure 
traditional navigation remained a living heritage, one that connects 
the peoples and islands of Oceana across our vast Pacific.
  On behalf of the people of the Northern Mariana Islands, I extend my 
deepest gratitude to Captain Sesario; his wife, Florentina; his 
children; grandchildren; family; and friends.
  His teachings will endure for generations, and his spirit will live 
on in the stars, skies, and seas.
  ``A very big thank you''; ``Un dangkulu na Si Yu'us ma'ase yan, 
kinisou.''

                        Honoring Guam-CNMI ESGR

  Ms. KING-HINDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Guam-CNMI 
Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve State Committee for its 
steadfast support for our servicemembers, their families, and the 
employers who support them.
  ESGR helps our citizen-warriors balance military and civilian 
responsibilities through outreach, education, recognition, and 
assistance with employment protections under USERRA. This mission is 
especially meaningful in the Northern Mariana Islands, where military 
service is deeply rooted in our community.
  The Guam-CNMI ESGR has earned national recognition for three 
consecutive years. In 2024, Major General Benny Paulino received the 
James M. Roche Spirit of Volunteerism National Award. In 2025, Dave 
Sablan received national ESGR recognition, and in 2026 Don Clark was 
named National Employer Outreach Volunteer of the Year. These honors 
reflect the dedication of the entire committee and its volunteers.
  On behalf of the people of the Northern Mariana Islands, I thank the 
Guam-CNMI ESGR for supporting those who serve our nation and 
strengthening the communities they call home.
  ``Thank you''; ``Si Yu'us ma'ase.'' ``Thank you''; ``ghilisow.''
```
