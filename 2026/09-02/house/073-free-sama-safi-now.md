# FREE SAMA SAFI NOW

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                           FREE SAMA SAFI NOW

  (Ms. TLAIB asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, free Sama Safi now.
  Sama Safi is a 20-year-old U.S. citizen and honors student of 
psychology who was kidnapped from her home in the middle of the night 
by the Israeli forces. For exactly 3 months today, Sama has been 
suffering in the Israeli military prisons.
  Sama has a severe chronic medical condition that requires daily 
medication. Her doctors say that she could have irreversible organ 
damage.
  Mr. Speaker, 40 of my colleagues and I, along with 6 Senators, sent a 
letter to Secretary Rubio demanding him to work on her release 
immediately.
  I have actually spoken directly to Sama's parents, who are terrified 
for their daughter. They tell me that they wake up every morning hoping 
to hear Sama playing her music. They told me that Ben-Gvir, Israel's 
Minister of National Security, went to the women's prison where Sama is 
being held, and he taunted the prisoners and assaulted detainees. He 
mocked the inhumane conditions, saying that it is what they deserve.
  Sama doesn't deserve this. No one does. She deserves to be home with 
her family, studying for her classes.
  Free Sama now.

                          ____________________
```
