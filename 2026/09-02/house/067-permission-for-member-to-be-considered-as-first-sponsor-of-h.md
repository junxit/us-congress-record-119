# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4586, H.R. 1114, and H. RES. 179

**Speaking:** Jackson, Jonathan L. (J000309) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 179, H.R. 1114, H.R. 4586

```
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4586, 
                       H.R. 1114, and H. RES. 179

  Mr. JACKSON of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I 
may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 4586, H.R. 
1114, and H. Res. 179, all originally introduced by Representative 
Cherfius-McCormick of Florida, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and 
requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Illinois?
  There was no objection.
```
