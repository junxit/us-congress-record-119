# CHOICE BEFORE OUR COUNTRY

**Speaking:** Mann, Tracey (M000871) [R-KS]

```
                       CHOICE BEFORE OUR COUNTRY

  (Mr. Mann of Kansas was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. MANN. Mr. Speaker, there is a fundamental choice before our 
country today: Do we trust the American people, or do we trust Big 
Government?
  Conservatives believe that freedom grows when the American Government 
gets out of the way. We believe in rewarding hard work, protecting free 
speech, securing our borders, lowering taxes, giving parents autonomy, 
and creating an economy where Americans can build, grow, and prosper.

[[Page H5433]]

  The Democratic Party is moving in the opposite direction. Socialism 
was once dismissed as a fringe movement in American politics. Today, 
its ideas are increasingly becoming the agenda of the Democratic Party: 
bigger government, higher taxes, more spending, and more control over 
the daily lives of Americans.
  Their own DSA movement openly calls for extending voting rights to 
noncitizens. Meanwhile, Democrats in Congress continue to oppose 
commonsense election safeguards, like the SAVE America Act, even though 
more than 80 percent of Americans support requiring identification to 
vote. That tells you everything you need to know about the competing 
visions before us.
  Republicans believe that American elections should be decided by 
American citizens. We believe taxpayers' dollars should serve 
hardworking American families and not pay for gender transition 
surgeries for people who entered our country illegally. We believe that 
Americans should keep more of what they earn. We believe a sovereign 
country must have a secure border.
  President Biden and congressional Democrats gave us 4 years of chaos 
at our southern border. President Trump and House Republicans proved 
that chaos was a policy choice. We enforced the law, secured the 
border, and restored common sense.
  There is an inverse relationship between the size of government and 
the freedom of its people. Democrats want to grow government and shrink 
freedom. Congressional Republicans want to shrink the reach of 
government and grow individual freedom. That is the choice before this 
country: government control or individual liberty, dependence or 
opportunity, socialism or free markets, crazy or common sense.
  I know which side I am on.


                              farewell tel

  Mr. MANN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a dedicated public 
servant, a trusted member of my team, and a good friend, Tel Wittmer, 
as he concludes 3\1/2\ years of service to the people of the Big First 
District.
  Tel is a Kansan through and through. He grew up in Holton, graduated 
from K-State, and, like me, had the privilege of serving as student 
body president at K-State.
  From the beginning, I knew that Tel understood that leadership is 
ultimately about service. Tel first joined my office in Washington, 
where he quickly became someone who I trusted with some of the most 
difficult issues before the Congress. He brought tremendous 
thoughtfulness to his work as a legislative assistant, but he also 
brought personality and joy to the office.
  My Washington, D.C., staff was incredibly sad to see him leave the 
Capitol when he and his wife, Madelyn, moved home to Kansas last 
February.
  Fortunately for us, Tel was not leaving the team. He moved to Wamego 
and took on the responsibility of serving as my district director. Over 
the last 1\1/2\ years, Kansans across the Big First have gotten to know 
the same Tel who we knew in Washington.
  His thoughtfulness is apparent to everyone he meets. He listens 
carefully, treats people with respect, and approaches every 
conversation with a sincere desire to help. He has represented me and 
his office extremely well, and now my district staff feels the same way 
that my D.C. staff did when he left Washington. They are going to miss 
him tremendously, just as I will.
  However, the things I admire most about Tel go way beyond his work. 
He is a man of strong faith, a leader in his community, a devoted 
husband to Madelyn, and now a great father to their young daughter, 
Eve.
  I thank Tel for the 3\1/2\ years that he has given the people of 
Kansas. I thank him for his friendship, his counsel, his leadership, 
and the care he has shown to every Kansan who walked through the door.
  While we will miss having Tel on our team, I am grateful that he will 
continue serving our great State in a new capacity. I know that God has 
great things ahead for Tel, Madelyn, and Eve, and I look forward to 
watching the next chapter unfold.
  May God bless Tel, and I thank him for his service to the Big First.
```
