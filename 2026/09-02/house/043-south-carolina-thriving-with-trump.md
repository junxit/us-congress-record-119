# SOUTH CAROLINA THRIVING WITH TRUMP

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                   SOUTH CAROLINA THRIVING WITH TRUMP

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, under the progrowth agenda 
of President Donald Trump--and backed by Republicans, Governor Henry 
McMaster, Speaker Murrell Smith, and Senate President Thomas 
Alexander--South Carolina is one of the fastest growing States in 
America, driven by the creation of jobs and affordability.
  South Carolinians are achieving bigger paychecks, opportunity, 
business growth, and creating jobs.
  The Working Families Tax Cuts Act keeps money in the pockets of 
families and seniors. Workers no longer pay taxes on overtime. Seniors 
benefit from the deduction on Social Security. Taxpayers saw average 
tax refunds reach over $3,000.
  South Carolina's gross domestic product is $393 billion, increasing 
by $26 billion since the current Trump administration. Promises made. 
Promises kept.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops. Trump is reinstituting peace 
through strength, revealing that war criminal Putin lies, insulting 
Trump and mocking Trump. Yesterday, Putin announced: ``Necessary 
assistance'' to Iran as Putin provided Iran targeting assistance to 
kill Americans and our Gulf allies with new missile strikes today.
```
