# REMEMBERING FRANK BEARD OF ZZ TOP

**Speaking:** Nehls, Troy E. (N000026) [R-TX]

```
                   REMEMBERING FRANK BEARD OF ZZ TOP

  (Mr. Nehls of Texas was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. NEHLS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to my friend, a 
true Texas legend, Rock & Roll Hall of Famer and the heartbeat of ZZ 
Top for more than 50 years, Frank Beard.
  Frank passed away on August 17, 2026, in Richmond, Texas, at his 
ranch,

[[Page H5430]]

surrounded by his family. He is now reunited with ZZ Top's base player, 
Dusty Hill.
  As a teenager, Frank picked up the drums, and he never looked back. 
From 1966 to 1968, Frank played alongside Dusty Hill in the band 
American Blues before the two of them joined up with Billy Gibbons and 
formed the iconic band ZZ Top and made history.
  In 1970, that classic trio, Billy, Dusty, and Frank, played their 
first show at the Knights of Columbus Hall in Beaumont, Texas. From 
that night on, the trio stayed together for over half a century, 
putting out hit after hit and entertaining millions of fans all over 
the world.
  Frank's steady Texas backbeat was the foundation of that sound. He 
kept ZZ Top's rhythm locked in for more than 50 years.
  Billy Gibbons said it best: ``The world lost one of the most 
naturally innovative drummers and a great and true son of Texas.''
  I know Frank is deeply missed by his wife, Debbie, his three 
children, the whole ZZ Top family, and fans all over the world.
  Frank Beard will always be a staple of Texas and rock and roll 
history.
  Mr. Speaker, in closing, I will miss seeing my friend, Frank, on the 
golf course and riding around in his sports car in Pecan Grove. Rest in 
peace. May God bless him.

                          ____________________
```
