# RECOGNIZING JEFFREY A. KATZ

**Speaking:** McGuire, John J. (M001239) [R-VA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING JEFFREY A. KATZ

  (Mr. McGUIRE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGUIRE. Mr. Speaker, we wouldn't have a country without our 
brave men and women in law enforcement who risk their lives to keep us 
safe.
  Today, I have the privilege of recognizing a friend, a constituent, 
and a former law enforcement officer, Jeffrey A. Katz, from Virginia's 
Fifth Congressional District, whose dedication to honoring law 
enforcement officers has made a profound impact across central 
Virginia.
  Jeffrey A. Katz established the Lieutenant Jan McTernan Blue Friday 
Honor, a recognition that has been bestowed to many Federal, State, 
county, and city officers for their service and sacrifice.
  Mr. Katz' unwavering support to the law enforcement community extends 
far beyond the establishment of this honor. He is also a board 
certified mental health coach specializing in services for first 
responders and holds the certified advanced peer support provider 
designation from the Virginia Peer Support Association.
  Mr. Katz is an exemplary member of the Commonwealth, and I thank him 
for his commitment to the law enforcement community.

                          ____________________
```
