# RECOGNIZING PENN STATE'S NATIONAL SECURITY INSTITUTE

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
          RECOGNIZING PENN STATE'S NATIONAL SECURITY INSTITUTE

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Penn State's National Security Institute, which advances national 
security solutions through research and partnerships with Federal 
agencies and industry.
  For decades, Penn State has remained focused on addressing the 
challenges facing our Nation's security.
  The launch of the National Security Institute is proof that the 
university is a trusted adviser for keeping the United States safe and 
secure.
  Penn State's commitment to innovation allows us to increase the 
capabilities of our military and improve critical technologies.
  The research conducted at the institute provides educational 
opportunities

[[Page H5441]]

for younger generations and expands workforce development for 
businesses across our Commonwealth.
  This institute doesn't just impact Penn State. It reaches people 
across the State, the country, and the military personnel who rely on 
these technologies.
  The university's continued leadership serves our Commonwealth now and 
for generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank the leading engineers, scientists, Federal 
officials, and businessowners who are dedicated to improving our 
country's national defense.
  I congratulate Penn State on this historic achievement.

                          ____________________
```
