# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ASHLEA QUINONEZ

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ASHLEA QUINONEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Ashlea Quinonez. Ashlea is a North Texas native and accomplished 
leader with an impressive career in politics, government, and 
healthcare. She was recently appointed as Regional Administrator for 
Region VI of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. In 
this role, she will be based out of Fort Worth, and represent Texas, 
Oklahoma, Louisiana, New Mexico, and Arkansas.
  Ashlea's interest in politics began when she was just seven years old 
when she helped stuff envelopes with campaign letters for a family 
friend that was seeking county office. A graduate of Texas A&M with a 
B.S. in Political Science, she went to work for former Senator Phil 
Gramm. Her career has led her to working for a North Texas political 
party, running a district office for a member of Congress, and working 
in government relations for the Memorial Hermann Health System. Through 
her service on numerous boards and task forces across Texas, Ashlea has 
left a lasting impact on our communities across the state.
  I congratulate Ashlea on her new role and thank her for her continued 
commitment to serving others in North Texas and across the country.

                          ____________________
```
