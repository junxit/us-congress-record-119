# RECOGNIZING TIM MILLER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING TIM MILLER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tim Miller, who 
is retiring from his service to our community as a Drawing, Painting, 
and Graphic Arts Instructor at Golden High School.
  For more than 20 years, Tim has inspired generations of students 
through his creativity, dedication, and leadership. His positive 
outlook and ability to encourage and challenge students have left a 
lasting impact on both the students of Golden High School and the art 
community in Colorado.
  During his time teaching, Tim has consistently gone above and beyond 
for his students. He has regularly taken them on field trips to 
assisted living homes, the Children's Hospital, the Denver Indian 
Center, and the Denver Rescue Mission to inspire them through art in 
the Metro Denver community. Tim's commitment to his students has been 
evident in everything he has done--including obtaining a bus driver's 
license to ensure his students would continue to get these meaningful 
experiences while cutting down on costs and logistical hurdles for the 
school. These opportunities have challenged students artistically and 
taught them compassion, service, and connection.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Tim Miller on his retirement from Golden 
High School and sincerely thank him for his contributions to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
