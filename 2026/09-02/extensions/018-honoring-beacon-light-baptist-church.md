# HONORING BEACON LIGHT BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING BEACON LIGHT BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to salute a vibrant 
congregation in my district, Beacon Light Baptist Church of Baton 
Rouge, Louisiana, and to recognize the ministry of its pastor, Eric A. 
Williams, on behalf of the people of East Baton Rouge Parish.
  Pastor Williams attended Delgado Community College and Southern 
University at New Orleans, where he majored in Business Management. God 
allowed him to be one of the first of many instructors at the Darryl S. 
Brister College and Theological Seminary in New Orleans, Louisiana.
  In 1995, Pastor Williams humbly submitted to the call of God for his 
life, under the leadership of his pastor, Bishop Darryl S. Brister, 
Apostle and Overseer of Beacon Light International Ministries. He was 
licensed to preach the Gospel in 1996, and on January 31, 1997, he was 
ordained as Elder.
  On April 29, 2007, a historical transition occurred for the Family of 
Light, when Elder Eric A. Williams and Lady Iris J. Williams were 
installed as Pastor and First Lady of Beacon Light Baptist Church of 
Baton Rouge. Under Pastor Williams' leadership, Beacon Light has 
undoubtedly become a true beacon of light in the Baton Rouge community.
  Intercessory Prayer, Light of Praise Dance Ministry, Greeters and 
Ushers Ministry, Media Ministry, Homework Assistance Program, Frontline 
Warriors Men's Ministry, Jewels of Light Pre-teen and Teen Female 
Ministry, School Mentoring Program, Evangelism, Hospitality, Healing 
and Recovery, Parking Lot, Marriage Ministry, and New Members Ministry 
are just a few of the ministries and programs initiated since Pastor 
Williams' assignment began at Beacon Light of Baton Rouge.
  As a result of his being instrumental in the works of God Almighty, 
the membership of Beacon Light of Baton Rouge has flourished from 
several hundreds to several thousands within a short number of years. 
His congregation describes his life as a living testimony, and calls 
him a living miracle.
  Pastor Williams is an exceptional leader in ministry, as well as in 
the Baton Rouge community. He gives in many capacities as it relates to 
people and the community. He is truly a spiritual father and guide to 
many members and non-members alike, and his members describe him as the 
most down-to-earth person and the best pastor on this side of Heaven.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in saluting Beacon Light 
Baptist Church of Baton Rouge for its years of faithful service, and in 
wishing its pastor and congregation God's continued favor in the years 
ahead.
```
