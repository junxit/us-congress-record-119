# HONOR TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DR. BRAD HUNT

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
               HONOR TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DR. BRAD HUNT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Dr. Brad Hunt. After 35 years in public education, including 
eight years as Superintendent of Coppell ISD, Dr. Brad Hunt is set to 
retire at the end of the school year. Starting as a teacher at Coppell 
High School in 1990, he went on to serve as principal and then 
superintendent. His leadership has helped shape Coppell ISD into one of 
the most respected school districts in Texas.
  Dr. Hunt has inspired generations of students, supported countless 
educators, and left a lasting mark on every campus in the district. His 
dedication to student success and his vision for excellence have 
touched thousands of lives, and his impact will be felt for years to 
come.
  I thank Dr. Hunt, for his incredible service, his belief in our North 
Texas students, and his commitment to Coppell. We wish him the very 
best in his next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
