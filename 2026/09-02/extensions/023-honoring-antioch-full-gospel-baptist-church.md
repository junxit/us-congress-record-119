# HONORING ANTIOCH FULL GOSPEL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING ANTIOCH FULL GOSPEL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate a resilient 
congregation in my district, Antioch Full Gospel Baptist Church in 
Baton Rouge, Louisiana, as it commemorates eighty-five years of faith, 
perseverance, and service to our community.
  Antioch Baptist Church was established in the year of 1941. The 
church started with four members in a society hall under the leadership 
of Reverend Ed Smith. Its first church was built in 1942. The church 
purchased property for sixteen hundred dollars in 1961 and built its 
second church in 1962. The founding pastors that paved the way for 
Antioch to be such a strong church, and for it to still be standing 
today, are Reverend Ed Smith, Reverend Willie Smith, Reverend Leonard 
Smith, Reverend E. B. Robertson, Bishop Eugene Harris, Reverend Robert 
Gross, and Pastor George Spears.

[[Page E847]]

  Through God's divine plan and purpose, Pastor Gregory Cooper, Sr., 
was sent to Antioch as pastor in April 1991. In September of that same 
year he was officially installed as Pastor of Antioch Baptist Church. 
Under his firm yet gentle leadership the congregation completed and was 
able to march into its new sanctuary that year. By the power of God and 
faithful and committed members, the third church was paid off in 1997. 
Antioch Baptist Church became Antioch Full Gospel Baptist Church in 
April of 2005.
  Today the congregation stands strong, a thriving body of men, women, 
and children that have come this far by faith, leaning on the Lord. To 
God be the glory for the things He has done, and for using Bishop 
Gregory Cooper, Sr., to lead them into the promises, as God shows 
Himself strong and mighty in the lives of the believers.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Antioch 
Full Gospel Baptist Church for eighty-five years of faithful service, 
and in wishing its pastor and congregation every blessing in the years 
to come.
```
