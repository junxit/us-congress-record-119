# RECOGNIZING RECOVERYWORKS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING RECOVERYWORKS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
RecoveryWorks on its 5th anniversary.
  Since opening in 2021 at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, 
RecoveryWorks has served as a reliable safe place for the unhoused 
community in Colorado. RecoveryWorks offers comprehensive services for 
those in need, including mental health support, addiction recovery 
programs, and job placement assistance, as well as respite care and 
hygiene facilities. Originally founded as a low-barrier emergency day 
shelter, RecoveryWorks has since evolved. They have relocated to the 
Mountain View Flats, which now serves as the site for their Bridge 
Housing program and through this new facility, they offer 34 units of 
immediate, short-term housing.
  Having grown up as an at-risk youth, I understand firsthand that 
access to resources and a strong support system are foundational to 
achieving success. This is precisely what RecoveryWorks offers to so 
many individuals. RecoveryWorks brings dignity, hope, and stability to 
people during some of the most difficult moments in their lives. They 
have had and will continue to have a lasting impact on the entire 
community.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate RecoveryWorks on celebrating their 5th 
anniversary and thank them for their service to our community.
```
