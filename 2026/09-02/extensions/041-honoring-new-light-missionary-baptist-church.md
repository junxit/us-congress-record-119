# HONORING NEW LIGHT MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING NEW LIGHT MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor New Light Missionary 
Baptist Church of Baton Rouge, Louisiana, founded and organized in 1895 
by the late Reverend Tony Scott--a sugar cane farmer, devoted husband 
to Cecily Scott, and father of nine children. Reverend Scott's vision 
for an institution of worship was realized on a tract of land in East 
Baton Rouge Parish, where, with the help of P.H. Handy, S.T. Davis, and 
H. Warner, a church building was erected. From this humble beginning, 
Reverend Scott carried out his calling to preach, teach, and lead God's 
people. A charter for New Light Missionary Baptist Church was 
established in 1935, reflecting the congregation's growth from a one-
room wooden structure to a strong and faithful community.
  A devastating storm in 1910 destroyed the original church, forcing 
its reconstruction on the east side of the railroad tracks on Crane 
Street in Scotlandville. Years later, the church moved to its present 
location on Blount Road. After Reverend Scott's passing in 1921, 
pastoral leadership continued under the late Rev. R.W. Ball and, 
beginning in 1923, Rev. J.P. Handy. In 1927, after fervent prayer and 
consultation led by Sister Cicely Scott, Reverend Noah C. Chinn was 
elected pastor. Under his divinely inspired leadership, the church was 
rebuilt in 1955, and several associate ministers served faithfully 
alongside him. Despite limited financial resources, the pioneers of New 
Light Missionary Baptist Church remained steadfast, and the church 
prospered by keeping the Lord first in all things.
  In August 1965, Reverend Dr. H.B. Williams became Acting Pastor and 
was officially elected Pastor that December following Reverend Chinn's 
passing. Through his God-given vision, the church completed its 
Community Outreach Center in 2002, expanding efforts to reach, teach, 
and serve the North Baton Rouge community. Additional ministries were 
established, including the 4-K Karing Kitchen & Kloth Kloset, a 
computer lab dedicated in loving memory of Cherlyn Ann Fugler-Young, 
and the 4CLTP technology program. The center also hosted medical 
research studies that benefited the community. Mrs. Wilma Williams, Dr. 
Williams' wife, played an essential role in organizing ministries such 
as the Nursing Guild, Sisterhood, and recognition days for students. 
Together, their extraordinary partnership strengthened New Light's 
mission for decades.
  Mr. Speaker, on December 31, 2005, Dr. H.B. Williams installed Dr. 
Gil H. Wright as Pastor, marking a historic moment as the pastoral 
mantle was passed with grace and unity. Under Dr. Wright's leadership, 
New Light Missionary Baptist Church continues to thrive--purchasing a 
15-passenger van, remodeling facilities, retiring the loan on the 
community center, and strengthening marriage ministry, new member 
engagement, and communication through announcement monitors. As New 
Light marches forward, pointing souls to Christ, Dr. Wright's 
dedication, prayerful leadership, and decades of faithful service 
remain evident in every facet of ministry. I ask my colleagues to join 
me in honoring New Light Missionary Baptist Church for its rich legacy, 
enduring faith, and unwavering commitment to God's work in the Baton 
Rouge community.
```
