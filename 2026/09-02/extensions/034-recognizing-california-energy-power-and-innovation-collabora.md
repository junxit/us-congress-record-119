# RECOGNIZING CALIFORNIA ENERGY, POWER, AND INNOVATION COLLABORATIVE (CAL EPIC)

**Speaking:** Matsui, Doris O. (M001163) [D-CA]

```
RECOGNIZING CALIFORNIA ENERGY, POWER, AND INNOVATION COLLABORATIVE (CAL 
                                 EPIC)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DORIS O. MATSUI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. MATSUI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the California 
Energy, Power, and Innovation Collaborative, or Cal EPIC, for its 
significant contributions to advance the region's zero-carbon goals, 
strengthen the local workforce, and establish the Greater Sacramento 
area as a national hub for clean mobility innovation.
  In 2019, four distinguished regional institutions: the Sacramento 
Municipal Utility District, the Greater Sacramento Economic Council, 
Sacramento State University, and the University of California, Davis, 
came together to establish the California Mobility Center, now known as 
Cal EPIC, with a bold and forward looking vision to strengthen the 
clean energy workforce, foster innovation, and position the Sacramento 
region as a leader in the clean energy economy.
  Cal EPIC has reached another significant milestone with the opening 
of its Innovation Hub. This 33,000-square-foot research, development, 
manufacturing, and workforce accelerator brings together cutting-edge 
technology, hands-on training, and industry collaboration under one 
roof. The Hub includes dedicated office, classroom, and meeting space; 
5,000 square feet for EV technician training in partnership with Rivian 
Automotive; a 3,500-square-foot manufacturing and prototyping lab; and 
the 10,000-square-foot Capital Charge Yard, a $4 million EV charger 
testing center funded by the California Energy Commission. Through 
interoperability testing, vehicle-to-grid experimentation, and 
workforce development, the Innovation Hub is creating new opportunities 
for innovation, preparing workers for the clean-energy economy, and 
helping position Greater Sacramento at the forefront of the Nation's 
transition to clean transportation.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
California Energy, Power,

[[Page E850]]

and Innovation Collaborative for its leadership and commitment to build 
a stronger, more innovative, and prosperous clean energy economy in the 
Sacramento region.
```
