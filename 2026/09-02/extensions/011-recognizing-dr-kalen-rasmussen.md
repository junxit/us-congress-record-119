# RECOGNIZING DR. KALEN RASMUSSEN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                    RECOGNIZING DR. KALEN RASMUSSEN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Kalen 
Rasmussen, from Lakewood, Colorado, for his participation in the 
Department of Energy's National Laboratory Research SLAM in D.C. as the 
representative for the National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR). Dr. 
Rasmussen received the Energy Security award for his presentation, 
``From Microbes to Metals: Powering Energy Security.''
  Every year, Americans discard hundreds of thousands of tons of 
valuable metal-containing batteries. Dr. Rasmussen's work at NLR 
advances biologically enabled methods for recovering critical metals 
from battery waste, using microorganisms and bio-based systems to help 
break down and process complex materials. Through his research, he has 
advanced the United States' capability to utilize its domestic waste, 
recover critical metals, and strengthen energy security and 
independence.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Dr. Kalen Rasmussen on winning the Energy 
Security award and representing Colorado in the 2026 DOE National 
Laboratory Research SLAM.
```
