# HONORING JUDGE STEPHEN KOURI ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
             HONORING JUDGE STEPHEN KOURI ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Honorable 
Stephen Kouri on his retirement after nearly 24 years of distinguished 
service as a judge on Illinois' Tenth Judicial Circuit.
  Judge Kouri has dedicated more than three decades to serving Peoria 
and Central Illinois. A graduate of Spalding Institute and the 
University of Illinois College of Law, he practiced law for 20 years 
before being elected to the Tenth Judicial Circuit in 2002.
  During his time on the bench, Judge Kouri served communities 
throughout Peoria, Marshall, Putnam, Stark, and Tazewell Counties and 
was the Circuit's longest-serving judge at the time of his retirement. 
Throughout his career, he earned a reputation for his fairness, sound 
judgment, professionalism, and strong work ethic.
  Judge Kouri's commitment to public service extends well beyond the 
courtroom. He served on the Peoria City Council from 1993 to 1997 and 
has held numerous leadership roles with civic, charitable, and Catholic 
organizations throughout the Peoria area. He also played an 
instrumental role in bringing the IHSA boys basketball state finals to 
Peoria and helped establish ``Access the Experience,'' which provided 
young people with disabilities the opportunity to participate in state 
finals activities.
  I congratulate Judge Kouri on his retirement, thank him for his many 
years of service to Central Illinois, and wish him and his family all 
the best in this next chapter.
```
