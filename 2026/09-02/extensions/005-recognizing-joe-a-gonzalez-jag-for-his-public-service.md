# RECOGNIZING JOE A. GONZALEZ (JAG) FOR HIS PUBLIC SERVICE

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
        RECOGNIZING JOE A. GONZALEZ (JAG) FOR HIS PUBLIC SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Nueces County Commissioner Joe A. Gonzalez (JAG) for his decades of 
public service and contributions to the people of Nueces County.
  Commissioner Gonzalez has devoted more than four decades to serving 
his community and country. His career began in law enforcement and 
criminal justice, where he served as a Corpus Christi Police Officer, 
Texas State Parole Officer, and the United States Department of 
Justice. He later served as Nueces County Justice of the Peace, 
Precinct 1, Place 1, before becoming Nueces County Commissioner for 
Precinct No. 2 in 2011.
  Throughout his tenure at the county, Gonzalez focused on expanding 
opportunity and improving quality of life. As Justice of the Peace, he 
helped establish the Truancy Reduction Alternative Program Solutions, 
an initiative focused on early intervention, education, and helping 
young people succeed. He later established the Joe A. Gonzalez (JAG) 
Education Is Our Freedom GED College Scholarship Program, which has 
helped thousands of local students pursue higher education.
  As a Nueces County Commissioner, Gonzalez worked to improve 
healthcare access, veterans' services, rural infrastructure, public 
transportation, education, and community development. He helped 
establish Veterans Memorial Courtyards in the Agua Dulce, Banquete, and 
Bishop communities creating permanent places to honor those who served 
our Nation. He also worked to improve drainage, wastewater systems, and 
colonia infrastructure, while expanding access to healthcare services 
in underserved rural communities.
  Additionally, Gonzalez served six years in the United States Marine 
Corps Reserve and twelve years in the United States Air Force Reserve. 
His dedication to both his country and community reflects a lifetime of 
commitment to others.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the 34th Congressional District of Texas, I 
congratulate Joe A. Gonzalez (JAG), Nueces County Commissioner, on his 
remarkable career and thank him for his commitment to the people of 
Nueces County and South Texas.
```
