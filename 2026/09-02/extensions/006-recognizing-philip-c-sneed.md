# RECOGNIZING PHILIP C. SNEED

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING PHILIP C. SNEED

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Philip C. Sneed 
for his extraordinary leadership and lasting contributions to the 
Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities as he retires after nearly 14 
years as President and CEO.
  Philip Sneed's connection to the Arvada Center spans nearly its 
entire 50-year history. At 17, he received his first paycheck from the 
Arvada Center for a role in its inaugural theatre season. After a 
distinguished career in the arts in both Colorado and California, 
Philip returned to Arvada to lead the very institution where his 
artistic journey began--bringing his career full circle.
  During his tenure, Philip became the longest-serving leader in the 
Arvada Center's history. His leadership helped guide the organization 
through some of its most significant milestones and challenges, 
including its transition from a City of Arvada entity to an independent 
nonprofit organization. He also helped the Center endure the 
unprecedented challenges of the pandemic, ensuring that this treasured 
cultural institution remained strong during a time when communities 
could not gather in person. Philip has worked to make the Arvada Center 
a more welcoming and inclusive place, one where all people feel they 
belong and where the arts can be experienced by everyone in our 
community.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to recognize Philip C. Sneed and thank him for his 
contribution to our community.
```
