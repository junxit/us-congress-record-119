# HONORING MOUNT ZION FIRST BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                HONORING MOUNT ZION FIRST BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to a venerable 
congregation in my district, Mount Zion First Baptist Church in Baton 
Rouge, Louisiana, as it marks more than 165 years of faith, fellowship, 
and service to our community.
  Mount Zion First African Baptist Church was founded in 1858 by Pastor 
Isaac Palmer, a Black minister, and Pastor John Brady, a White 
minister. The organization included eight others, of whom only three 
names were recorded: Henry Strong, Silas Green, and Martha Hinton. 
Throughout the following years, Mount Zion extended its hand to many 
pastors who all contributed to bringing non-believers to Christ, 
increasing the membership, and obtaining great church recognition both 
locally and nationally, allowing the congregation to grow by leaps and 
bounds.
  However, there was still work to do. Dr. T.J. Jemison became pastor 
in June 1949. During the early years of Dr. Jemison's pastorate, he led 
many movements in the Baton Rouge community for the betterment of all 
citizens, especially the Black community. Because of his work in the 
community, the church began to grow larger in membership, and many 
young people were found in church each Sunday. While participating in 
many community activities, Pastor Jemison began the building program.
  On the first Sunday in June 1954, the Mount Zion First Baptist 
congregation moved from the old church facility and marched into the 
new one. The new church building was one of the first of its kind in 
the cjty of Baton Rouge. Dr. Jemison was voted Pastor Emeritus on 
Sunday, September 21, 2003, and subsequently retired as pastor of Mount 
Zion on October 5, 2003. Pastor Jemison served fifty-four years of 
dedicated leadership.
  In February 2007, Mount Zion issued the call of the pastorate to Dr. 
Rene F. Brown, former pastor of Ninth Street Missionary Baptist Church 
in Lawrence, Kansas. Pastor Brown now serves as the eighth pastor of 
Mount Zion First Baptist Church. Pastor Brown came in with a clarion 
call, preaching the Word of forgiveness and reconciliation. Led by the 
Holy Spirit, Pastor Brown began to redirect the church's focus toward 
family, introducing Men's, Women's, Seniors', Singles', Couples', and 
College Ministries. He also brought with him the church's current 
theme, ``Opening their Understanding . . . Boasting in the Cross.''
  As the congregation works toward magnifying God's presence through 
prayer and worship, communicating God's Word through evangelism and 
discipleship, and spiritually educating the membership through ministry 
and stewardship of time and talent, it aims to empower God's servants 
to grow toward spiritual maturity, and to engage in fellowship and 
understanding of the church, home, community, and the world.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying tribute to 
Mount Zion First Baptist Church for more than a century and a half of 
faithful service, and in wishing its pastor and congregation continued 
blessings in the years to come.
```
