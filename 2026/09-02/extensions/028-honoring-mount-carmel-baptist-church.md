# HONORING MOUNT CARMEL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING MOUNT CARMEL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a faithful 
congregation in my District, Mount Carmel Baptist Church of Baton 
Rouge, Louisiana, as it marks more than seventy-five years of worship, 
education, and service to the people of East Baton Rouge Parish.
  Mount Carmel Baptist Church of Baton Rouge was founded in 1949, when 
a group of Christian men and women met on North 32nd Street to 
establish a new congregation. On September 21, 1949, the members 
adopted the name Mount Carmel Baptist Church No. 2, and Reverend 
Herbert Burden was elected as the church's first pastor. The 
congregation worked together to construct its first church building, 
where services began in December 1949 while construction continued. The 
church was formally incorporated in 1972 as Mount Carmel Baptist Church 
of Baton Rouge.
  Throughout its history, Mount Carmel has emphasized spiritual growth, 
youth development, education, and service to the community. In 2001, 
the congregation relocated from North 37th Street to its current 
location on Prescott Road, bringing its ministries and commitment to 
community service to a new neighborhood. The church continues to build 
upon the legacy of its founders through worship,

[[Page E848]]

Christian education, and service to the Baton Rouge community.
  Mount Carmel Baptist Church is led today by Pastor Andra Johnson.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mount 
Carmel Baptist Church for more than seventy-five years of faithful 
service, and in wishing its pastor and congregation continued blessings 
in the years to come.
```
