# HONORING MINNESOTA STATE SENATOR SANDY PAPPAS

**Speaking:** McCollum, Betty (M001143) [D-MN]

```
             HONORING MINNESOTA STATE SENATOR SANDY PAPPAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETTY McCOLLUM

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. McCOLLUM. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the distinguished career 
of Minnesota State Senator Sandy Pappas on the occasion of her 
retirement after 42 years of public service in the Minnesota 
Legislature. First elected to the Minnesota House of Representatives in 
1984 and to the Minnesota Senate in 1990, Senator Pappas has dedicated 
more than four decades to serving the people of Minnesota, especially 
the residents of her district in Saint Paul.
  During her 42 years of legislative service, Senator Pappas has made 
lasting contributions on issues including education, health care, 
workers' rights, women's economic security, public safety, 
transportation, pensions, and economic and social equity. She has 
chaired the Senate Capital Investment Committee, the Legislative 
Commission on Pensions and Retirement, the Senate State and Local 
Government Committee, the Senate Higher Education Committee, and the 
Senate Education Committee. From 2013 to 2016, she served as President 
of the Minnesota Senate, a testament to the trust placed in her by her 
colleagues.
  The Senator's legislative accomplishments have touched the lives of 
so many Minnesotans. She was instrumental in advancing the Minnesota 
DREAM Act, the Women's Economic Security Act, statewide Earned Sick and 
Safe Time, and the creation of the Nursing Home Workforce Standards 
Board. She championed legislation to eliminate the statute of 
limitations for sexual assault and helped secure Minnesota's ban on 
child marriage. As a longtime leader on pensions, she helped strengthen 
retirement security for public employees.
  Senator Pappas has been a tireless champion for the city of Saint 
Paul and even ran for mayor in 1997. She helped secure state funding 
for projects throughout the city, including the Union Depot, the 
Minnesota Children's Museum, the Ordway, the Victoria Theater, Pedro 
Park, the North End Community Center, Reconnect Rondo, the 
reconstruction of the Third Street/Kellogg Bridge, and Shoreline 
reinforcements on the Mississippi River, Raspberry Island, Upper 
Landing and Harriet Island. In 2023, as chair of the Senate Capital

[[Page E842]]

Investment Committee and chief author of the legislation, she helped 
deliver a historic $2.6 billion capital investment package supporting 
critical infrastructure for communities across Minnesota.
  Beyond her legislative accomplishments, Senator Pappas has devoted 
considerable time and energy to public policy and community service. In 
2011, she co-founded Forward Global Women, a nonprofit organization 
that convenes and trains women leaders from Middle Eastern and North 
African countries and the United States to step into public leadership 
positions and build the next generation of women peacemakers. She also 
taught public policy at Metropolitan State University for 20 years. She 
earned her bachelor's degree in public policy from Metropolitan State 
University and a Master of Public Administration from Harvard 
University's John F. Kennedy School of Government as a Bush Fellow. 
Throughout her career, Senator Pappas has exemplified a steadfast 
commitment to public service, human rights, equality, and expanding 
opportunities for women to lead.
  It has been an honor to know and work with Senator Pappas. When I was 
first elected to the Minnesota House in 1992, Sandy was already a force 
in the state legislature. Over the years, our paths took us in 
different directions. I eventually went to Congress, while Sandy 
continued her work in the Minnesota Senate. But we never stopped 
working together because we both care deeply about the communities we 
have the privilege to represent. I had the pleasure of working with her 
on many projects for Saint Paul that brought together local, state, and 
federal government, including the historic Union Depot. Her legislative 
expertise, dedication to Saint Paul, and commitment to public service 
have made her a trusted partner and friend. She leaves public service 
having made Saint Paul and Minnesota stronger.
  I am grateful for Sandy's friendship and for all she has done for our 
communities. I wish her, her husband Neal, their three children, and 
many grandchildren and great grandchildren all the best in this next 
chapter.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in this well-deserved tribute to Senator 
Sandy Pappas on the occasion of her retirement after 42 years of 
distinguished public service in the Minnesota Legislature.
```
