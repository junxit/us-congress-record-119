# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF JUSTIN BALL

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF JUSTIN BALL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Chief Justin Ball. Dallas Fire-Rescue has named a new fire chief, 
Justin Ball, a veteran of the department with nearly three decades of 
service to North Texas.
  Chief Ball began his journey with Dallas Fire-Rescue in 1997, and has 
worn many hats throughout his time with the department, starting as 
Firefighter Paramedic, to Executive Assistant Chief of Operations, and 
most recently serving as the Interim Chief, before being named as Chief 
permanently. Through dedication, leadership, and commitment to public

[[Page E846]]

service, he earned the trust of the department, city leadership, and 
most importantly, the people of North Texas.
  I congratulate Chief Justin Ball on his well-deserved new role. His 
leadership and commitment to keeping our communities safe is 
appreciated by Texas-24 and all of North Texas.

                          ____________________
```
