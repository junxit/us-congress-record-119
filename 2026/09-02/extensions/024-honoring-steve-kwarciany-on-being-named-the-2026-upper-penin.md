# HONORING STEVE KWARCIANY ON BEING NAMED THE 2026 UPPER PENINSULA VETERAN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
   HONORING STEVE KWARCIANY ON BEING NAMED THE 2026 UPPER PENINSULA 
                          VETERAN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize Retired Air 
Force Master Sergeant Steve Kwarciany on being named the 2026 Upper 
Peninsula Veteran of the Year. For more than a decade, Steve has been a 
steadfast leader within numerous Veteran organizations across the Upper 
Peninsula, dedicating countless hours to improving the lives of those 
who have served our Nation.
  As a Life Member of VFW Hiawatha Post 2998, AMVETS Escanaba Post 123, 
DAV Delta County Chapter 24, and Bark River American Legion Post 438, 
Steve has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to serving his fellow 
Veterans. His leadership roles as Commander of the Delta County 
Veterans Council, Quartermaster of VFW Post 2998, Senior Vice Commander 
of DAV Chapter 24, and Chairman of the Delta County Department of 
Veterans Affairs Board reflect the confidence and respect he has earned 
from his fellow Veterans.
  Steve played a vital role in the ballot referendum that established 
the Delta County Department of Veterans Affairs, helping ensure that 
local Veterans have greater access to the resources and services they 
deserve. His service as Director of the Delta County Veterans Council's 
Memorial Day, POW/MIA Recognition Day, and Veterans Day ceremonies, 
along with his work as Funeral Honor Guard Commander and member of the 
Upper Peninsula Honor Flight Welcome Home Honor Guard, demonstrates his 
enduring commitment to honoring those who have worn the uniform.
  Steve's work has left an enduring mark on the Upper Peninsula, 
Whether coordinating the annual VFW Poppy Sale, serving as a mentor for 
young people while at the Bark River-Harris Leo Club, or volunteering 
with the U.P. Off Road Races, Steve has consistently placed service 
above self. By exemplifying the highest standards of leadership, he has 
inspired countless others to embrace the values of citizenship, 
volunteerism, and patriotism.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to ask my colleagues to join me in 
recognizing Retired Air Force Master Sergeant Steve Kwarciany on being 
named the 2026 Upper Peninsula Veteran of the Year. On behalf of 
Michigan's 1st Congressional District, I congratulate Steve on this 
well-deserved recognition and thank him for his extraordinary service 
to our Nation, our Veterans, and the people of the Upper Peninsula.

                          ____________________
```
