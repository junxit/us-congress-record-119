# RECOGNIZING THE BRIGHT START INITIATIVE

**Speaking:** Strickland, Marilyn (S001159) [D-WA]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE BRIGHT START INITIATIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARILYN STRICKLAND

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. STRICKLAND. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Bright 
Start Initiative and its founders, Michael and Marcus Kang. The Bright 
Start Initiative was founded by two brothers who saw firsthand that 
oral-health education and basic supplies were not reaching every family 
equally.
  The Bright Start Initiative is a student-founded 501(c)(3) nonprofit 
bringing free dental supplies and multilingual oral-health education to 
underserved students across the United States. Student-led teams 
partner with schools and dental professionals to deliver supplies and 
lessons families can actually use, in the languages they already speak 
at home.
  One in five children in the United States lives with untreated tooth 
decay, and it is the number one chronic illness among children. The 
Bright Start Initiative is prepared to support over 5,000 students, 
with lessons translated into over 18 languages, and have partnered with 
100 schools in six states.
  Michael and Marcus Kang are leaders in Washington State and in the 
country. I applaud Michael and Marcus's championship of their fellow 
students and look forward to all that they will accomplish in the 
future.
```
