# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO UNIVERSITY EMERGENCY MEDICAL RESPONSE AT UT DALLAS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO UNIVERSITY EMERGENCY MEDICAL RESPONSE 
                              AT UT DALLAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, University Emergency Medical Response at UT Dallas. The 
University Emergency Medical Response (UEMR) of the University of Texas 
at Dallas was recently recognized as the Collegiate EMS Organization of 
the Year for 2025 at the National Collegiate EMS Foundation Conference.
  Since being founded in 2017 by just two students, UEMR at UT Dallas 
has grown into an award winning team of over 90 volunteers who are 
certified as EMTs, Advanced EMTs, and paramedics. These volunteers 
answer over 500 emergency calls, support over 65 events each year.
  In addition to keeping the campus community safe, UEMR provides 
training to over 350 students annually through EMT and paramedic 
courses. With their seventh recognition, we are happy to celebrate 
their commitment to keeping the UT Dallas community safe.
```
