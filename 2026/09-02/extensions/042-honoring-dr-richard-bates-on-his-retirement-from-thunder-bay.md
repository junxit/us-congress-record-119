# HONORING DR. RICHARD BATES ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THUNDER BAY COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICE

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
HONORING DR. RICHARD BATES ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THUNDER BAY COMMUNITY 
                             HEALTH SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize Dr. Richard 
Bates on his retirement from Thunder Bay Community Health Service. 
Throughout his distinguished career, Dr. Bates has dedicated himself to 
improving access to quality healthcare for the people of Northern 
Michigan while exemplifying compassionate leadership and an unwavering 
commitment to rural communities.
  A graduate of Alma College, Michigan State University, and Wayne 
State University School of Medicine, Dr. Bates has served Northeast 
Michigan for more than twenty-five years as both a trusted physician 
and respected healthcare leader. During his tenure as Chief Executive 
Officer of Thunder Bay Community Health Service, he expanded access to 
care by growing the organization's services, including the launch of 
its Women's Health program, ensuring more families throughout the 
region received the care they deserve.

[[Page E852]]

  Beyond his leadership within the clinic, Dr. Bates has been a 
tireless advocate for Community Health Centers, regularly traveling to 
Washington to champion the needs of rural healthcare providers. He has 
led discussions with my office on behalf of health centers across our 
region and has been a valued partner in advancing policies that 
strengthen healthcare access for the people of Northern Michigan. His 
dedication to delivering quality care has also touched countless 
families, delivering thousands of babies throughout Northeast Michigan 
over the course of his medical career.
  Although retiring from his role as Chief Executive Officer, Dr. Bates 
will continue serving patients as a part-time clinician to assist with 
Thunder Bay's expanding women's health services, ensuring his 
experience and passion for medicine will continue to benefit our 
communities.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to recognize Dr. Richard Bates for 
his extraordinary service, leadership, and commitment to improving the 
health and well-being of Northern Michigan. On behalf of Michigan's 1st 
Congressional District, I congratulate him on his well-earned 
retirement and wish him, his wife Cheryl, and their family many years 
of health, happiness, and continued success.
```
