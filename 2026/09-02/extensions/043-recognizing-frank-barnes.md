# RECOGNIZING FRANK BARNES

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING FRANK BARNES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Frank Barnes, 
faculty member of Red Rocks Community College, for his service to our 
community.
  Frank has been an essential part of the Red Rocks Facilities 
department for nearly a decade, serving the campus with dedication and 
compassion.
  He started his career serving our country in the United States Air 
Force from 1981 to 1988, and continued working for the public as a 
civil engineer before joining Red Rocks Community College in 2016.
  Those who have worked with Frank at Red Rocks Community College know 
him for his hard work, reliability, and generosity. In addition, my 
staff has appreciated his support and reliability with our 
Congressional Art Competition over the last four years. Though Frank 
will be deeply missed within the Red Rocks Community, his retirement is 
well-deserved.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Frank Barnes on his time with Red Rock 
Community College and sincerely thank him for his service to our 
country and the Colorado community. I wish him the best of luck in all 
his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
