# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CASON WALLACE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CASON WALLACE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Cason Wallace. Cason, a Dallas native and Richardson High School 
alumni, recently helped lead the Oklahoma Thunder to a NBA 
Championship. In the Game 7 victory, he contributed 10 points and 3 
steals in 26 minutes played. His championship win is not just a 
personal victory, but a proud moment for the entire community that have 
watched him play in Richardson throughout the years.
  From the beginning of his athletic career at Richardson High School 
to his collegiate success at the University of Kentucky, and now a 
world champion on the NBA stage, his roots in North Texas remain 
strong, continuing to inspire young athletes across our community.
  I thank Cason for showing us what it means to lead with heart and 
hustle. Cason has given North Texas something unforgettable to 
celebrate, and we look forward to watching his NBA career continue.

                          ____________________
```
