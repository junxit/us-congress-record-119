# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO HERSTORY GALLERY

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO HERSTORY GALLERY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, HerStory Gallery. As wildfires continue to displace families and 
destroy homes in California, the need for aid is greater than ever. 
HerStory Gallery, a North Texas store dedicated to empowering and 
uplifting women, has stepped up in an incredible way. Their latest 
initiative embodies the spirit of compassion and service--organizing 
donation drops in HerStory locations across our community to support 
those affected by the devastating fires.
  Joining forces with Revell with a Cause, Giving Generously, and the 
L.A. Dream Center, HerStory cleared floor space at their galleries in 
Dallas and Fort Worth to make room for donations and rallied our 
community to provide essential supplies such as non-perishable food, 
clothing, hygiene products, and emergency relief items for those 
impacted. Their efforts remind us that, even from miles away, we can 
make a meaningful difference in the lives of those in crisis.
  I thank HerStory Gallery for leading with heart and showing what it 
truly means to serve others through their generosity and compassion.

                          ____________________
```
