# RECOGNIZING DONNA WALKER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING DONNA WALKER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Jefferson 
County Public Library Director Donna Walker on her retirement after 14 
years of service. Donna's tenure is best characterized by her 
commitment to making library services more accessible to all patrons.
  Under Donna's transformational leadership, underserved communities 
saw expanded library services, including greater access for Spanish-
speaking residents as she championed the hiring of bilingual employees. 
Donna strongly supported library services in rural communities by 
working on plans to develop three new campuses in our remote 
neighborhoods.
  Thanks to Donna's outstanding work, Jefferson County became home to 
our Nation's first staffless library, which provides residents with 
access to books, office supplies, and other library resources.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Donna Walker on her retirement and thank 
her for her commitment to serving our community.

                          ____________________
```
