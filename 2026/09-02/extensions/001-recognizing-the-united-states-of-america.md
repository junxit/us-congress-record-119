# RECOGNIZING THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the United 
States of America's 250th anniversary.
  Two hundred and fifty years ago, our founding fathers signed the 
Declaration of Independence, announcing our sovereignty as a Nation and 
proclaiming the unalienable rights of life, liberty, and the pursuit of 
happiness. While our country has often failed to live up to these 
ideals, through the work of many courageous Americans, we have made 
slow but steadfast progress toward our shared goal of a country that 
truly embodies liberty and justice for all.
  Across our fifty states and many diverse communities, our country has 
countless proud traditions and accomplishments. For myself, as a high-
risk youth who grew up in Jefferson County, Colorado. I know personally 
what is possible in this country when we invest in people and give them 
the resources they need to succeed. As a lifelong Coloradan, I was 
raised with a deep appreciation for our outdoor spaces and public 
lands. Our country's people and places are what make the United States 
so special. As we celebrate this momentous occasion, I am struck by the 
ingenuity, generosity, resilience, and bravery of my fellow citizens, 
both past and present, who have helped shape what it means to be an 
American and encourage our country to strive for the better angels of 
our nature.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, I 
celebrate our country's 250th anniversary. With an eye to how we can 
learn from our past, I am excited and hopeful as we move forward into a 
new period in our country's history.

                          ____________________
```
