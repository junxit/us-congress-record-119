# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ASSISTANT POLICE CHIEF KEVIN PALMER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
  HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ASSISTANT POLICE CHIEF KEVIN PALMER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Assistant Police Chief Kevin Palmer. Kevin lived a life of 
service, an Air Force veteran, dedicated public servant, and a 
respected leader in the North Richland Hills community.
  His journey of service began in the Air Force before he joined the 
North Richland Hills Police Department in 2004. Over the next two 
decades he rose through the ranks to become Assistant Police Chief in 
2023, overseeing patrol, traffic, SWAT, and criminal investigations. 
Throughout his career in public service, he received more than 40 
police service awards, graduated from the FBI National Academy's 283rd 
session, and graduate of the PERF--Senior Management Institute of 
Police.
  His absence is felt throughout the community today, Kevin's legacy 
lives on through the many men and women he mentored, and the community 
he served with dedication and heart.
  Assistant Chief Kevin Palmer served with honor and led with an 
unwavering commitment. We are thankful for his sacrifice and we will 
never forget the legacy he leaves behind. May his memory continue to 
inspire us all.

                          ____________________
```
