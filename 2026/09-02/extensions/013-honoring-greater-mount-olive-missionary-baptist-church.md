# HONORING GREATER MOUNT OLIVE MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
         HONORING GREATER MOUNT OLIVE MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Greater Mount Olive 
Missionary Baptist Church of Baton Rouge, Louisiana, a congregation 
whose beginning dates back to January 21, 1996, at the Baptist Student 
Center on the campus of Southern University. Under the leadership of 
Pastor S.C. Dixon, 134 members assembled with the adopted motto, `The 
Church Where Peace Abides.' On May 6, 1996, the church was incorporated 
with the State of Louisiana. Guided by the Holy Spirit, Pastor Dixon 
led the congregation only one year later, on May 1, 1997, to purchase 
its current church home on Victoria Drive. Through boundless faith and 
steadfast vision, Pastor Dixon moved forward with confidence, 
overseeing a facility consisting of three buildings--the sanctuary, 
office spaces, classrooms, a family center with gymnasium, kitchen, 
cafeteria, and an educational building.
  With Spirit-filled guidance, Greater Mount Olive Missionary Baptist 
Church has long been a trailblazer in carrying out the Great Commission 
of Jesus Christ. The church is not limited to Sunday morning worship; 
its ministries actively meet the needs of disciples, the community, and 
people abroad. In August 1997, Pastor Dixon further recognized a need 
for academic support within the community and opened Greater Mount 
Olive Day Care with just one student. Continued growth led to the 
renaming of the school as Greater Mount Olive Christian Academy in 
2001. By May 2005, the academy was approved as a Non-Public Private 
School in East Baton Rouge Parish, serving Pre-K through second grade. 
Greater Mount Olive remains affiliated with numerous Baptist 
organizations statewide and nationally, including the National Baptist 
Convention of America International, Inc.
  Over the years, Greater Mount Olive has continued to enhance its 
facilities, including a 2000 renovation of the sanctuary. During this 
period, New Hope Baptist Church under Reverend Dr. Leo D. Cyrus 
graciously allowed the congregation to worship in its sanctuary at no 
cost. Additional improvements included acquiring a church steeple, 
installing an electronic sign, upgrading the sound system, replacing 
the roof, remodeling the parking lot, installing electrical gates, and 
replacing air-conditioning units. On January 23, 2000, the church laid 
its cornerstone, placing behind it a time capsule to be opened in the 
year 2050. The church expanded further by acquiring nearly ten acres 
across the street and another four acres south of the main campus. 
Following Hurricane Katrina, the church allowed FEMA to house displaced 
families in 118 trailers on its property--Mount Olive Gardens.
  Mr. Speaker, Greater Mount Olive Missionary Baptist Church endured 
the 2016 flood that destroyed its campus but never missed a Sunday 
service, holding 21 months of worship at True Light Baptist Church. 
Supported by Baptist groups nationwide, the church demonstrated unity 
and resilience by providing FEMA housing units during recovery. After 
returning to its Victoria Drive home in May 2018, Pastor Dixon led a 
Strategic Planning Summit in January 2019 to guide future ministry. I 
urge my colleagues to honor Greater Mount Olive for its faith, 
perseverance, service, and dedication to rebuilding its mission.
```
