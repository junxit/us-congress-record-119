# RECOGNIZING PEACEWORKS, INC.

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING PEACEWORKS, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Peaceworks, 
Inc. on 40 years of important work serving domestic violence survivors 
in rural Colorado mountain communities. Since 1986, Peaceworks, Inc. 
has offered advocacy and support to survivors of intimate partner 
violence in Colorado. The non-profit has provided nearly 2,000 nights 
of shelter since its original establishment in the spare bedroom of a 
Park County Commissioner, who took on the need she saw in her town.
  Forty years later, Peaceworks, Inc. has become a critical resource, 
providing shelter, counseling, and educational services across 
Jefferson and Park counties as the only domestic violence shelter in a 
2,800-square-mile region within these mountain communities.
  Through its 24/7 SafeLine, Mountain Peace Shelter, mobile and non-
residential advocacy, Community Wellness Program, and prevention 
outreach, PeaceWorks, Inc. addresses both immediate safety and the 
long-term impact of trauma. With its SafePet program, Peaceworks, Inc. 
is one of the few domestic violence shelters in the state that allow 
survivors to bring their pets when seeking services. Under the 
leadership of Executive Director Sabrina Fritts, the organization has 
worked tirelessly to address trauma and promote healthy relationships 
through communal wellness sessions and educational outreach.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Peaceworks, Inc. on 40 years of serving and 
advocating for survivors and wish them continued success in the future.
```
