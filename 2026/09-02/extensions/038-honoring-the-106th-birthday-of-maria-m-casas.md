# HONORING THE 106TH BIRTHDAY OF MARIA M. CASAS

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
             HONORING THE 106TH BIRTHDAY OF MARIA M. CASAS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Mrs. Maria M. Casas, lovingly known by her family as ``Nana,'' and to 
commemorate her 106th birthday.
  Born on September 14, 1920, in Benavides, Texas, to parents Porfirio 
and Santos Mendez, Maria is the oldest of five children. From her early 
days in Los Horcones Ranch, she learned the values that would guide her 
throughout her life: faith, hard work, devotion to family, and 
generosity toward others.

[[Page E851]]

  Mrs. Casas met her husband, Victoriano Casas, at a church festival in 
Benavides. Together, they built a loving home and raised five children: 
Hilda Doris, Norma, Maria Isabel, Victoriano Jr., and Jose Daniel. Over 
the years, their family has grown to include 14 grandchildren, 32 
great-grandchildren, and 6 great-great-grandchildren, with another on 
the way. Mrs. Casas has the remarkable privilege of witnessing the 
strength of the family she helped build across many generations.
  Mrs. Casas has also spent a lifetime sharing her talents with her 
community. Whether sewing and quilting, tending her garden, baking and 
cooking for those she loves, or preparing Pan de Campo with her husband 
and family for local festivals, she finds joy in bringing people 
together. A lifetime member of the Catholic Daughters at Saint Francis 
de Paula Catholic Church, she faithfully supports her parish through 
quilt donations, fundraising, and service.
  For 106 years, Mrs. Casas has shown what it means to live a life 
grounded in faith, strengthened by family, and enriched by service to 
others. Her long life is not only an extraordinary milestone, but a 
tremendous blessing.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the 34th Congressional District of Texas, I 
congratulate Mrs. Maria M. Casas on the joyous occasion of her 106th 
birthday. I join her children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, 
great-great-grandchildren, extended family, and many friends in 
celebrating a truly remarkable life, and I wish ``Nana'' continued 
blessings, happiness, and the love of her family as they honor this 
very special day.
```
