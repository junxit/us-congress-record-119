# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF DANIEL COMEAUX

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
          HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF DANIEL COMEAUX

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Chief Daniel Comeaux. Daniel Comeaux is the new Chief of Police 
for the City of Dallas, bringing more than three decades of law 
enforcement experience to North Texas.
  Chief Comeaux began his career with the Houston Police Department in 
1991 before joining the DEA, where he eventually served as the Special 
Agent in Charge of the Houston Field Division. Over his 33 years of 
service, he has led major operations across the country, including 
along the Southern border, and achieved significant results, such as a 
40 percent reduction in overdose deaths in Laredo, Texas. He is an 
experienced leader known for his collaborative and community-focused 
approach.
  I congratulate Chief Comeaux, and welcome him to North Texas. I wish 
him the very best as he takes the helm of the Dallas Police Department 
and leads it into a new era of service and excellence.

                          ____________________
```
