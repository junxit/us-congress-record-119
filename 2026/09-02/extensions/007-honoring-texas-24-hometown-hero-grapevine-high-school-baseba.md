# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO GRAPEVINE HIGH SCHOOL BASEBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
  HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO GRAPEVINE HIGH SCHOOL BASEBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Grapevine High School Baseball Team. The Grapevine Mustangs 
Baseball Team clinched their second consecutive state title at the UIL 
5A Division II State Championship in Round Rock, Texas. With a 9-5 
victory over Humble Kingwood Park High School, the Mustangs became the 
first UIL 5A team to go back-to-back since 2003.
  Throughout the season, the team displayed incredible resilience, 
teamwork, and sportsmanship representing North Texas well. With the 
strong leadership of Head Coach Jimmy Webster, Grapevine finished with 
an impressive 36-3 record and ranked number 1 in the Texas High School 
Coaches Association Rankings.
  The Mustangs' hard-fought championship run is more than just a 
victory on a field, it is a testament to their dedication and 
commitment. I congratulate the players, coaches, and families on this 
unforgettable season. The Grapevine High School Mustangs Baseball Team 
has given us in Texas-24 something to celebrate.

                          ____________________
```
