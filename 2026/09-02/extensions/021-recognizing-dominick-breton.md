# RECOGNIZING DOMINICK BRETON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING DOMINICK BRETON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Dominick 
Breton of Wheat Ridge for his impactful contribution to small 
businesses and the community of Wheat Ridge. Leaving behind a legacy of 
kindness, Mr. Breton passed away on June 18, 2026.
  Fondly known as ``Mr. Wheat Ridge,'' Dominick was a beloved 
contributor to our community, participating in events, service 
projects, and business advocacy. Dominick served as president of the 
Wheat Ridge Chamber of Commerce for over seven years and was a member 
of Kiwanis for nine years. Dominick had an inspirational gift to 
mobilize his neighbors and community members to help with projects or 
initiatives to benefit the community. Through his dedication, he made a 
deeply positive impact on our community.
  During his final months, Dominick persevered through his work with 
the same strength and passion. Though he will be missed, his legacy of 
service lives on in the many lives he touched.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to recognize Dominick Breton for his impact and thank him 
for his contribution to the Wheat Ridge community and beyond. We will 
remember him fondly as Wheat Ridge carries on his mission.

                          ____________________
```
