# RECOGNIZING STEVE ART

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING STEVE ART

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Mr. Steve Art on his retirement from the City of Wheat 
Ridge and to thank him for 15 years of dedicated service to the 
community.

[[Page E849]]

  Since 2011, Steve has played a pivotal role in shaping the economic 
future of Wheat Ridge, serving as both the Economic Development Manager 
and the Executive Director of the Urban Renewal Authority. Since his 
first major project, which brought a Sprouts Farmers Market to the 
area, Steve has worked tirelessly to build strong partnerships with 
businesses, developers, and community leaders. His commitment to 
redevelopment and his talent for attracting new investment have helped 
guide Wheat Ridge into a new chapter of economic vitality.
  Thanks in large part to Steve's efforts, Wheat Ridge has grown into a 
thriving, sustainable suburban community with accessible public 
transportation, vibrant businesses, and improved amenities for 
residents. Steve's legacy will be represented in his final major 
project, the Clear Creek Crossing, which will bring new opportunities 
and continued growth to Wheat Ridge for years to come.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Steve Art on his outstanding service and 
wish him all the best in his well-earned retirement.

                          ____________________
```
