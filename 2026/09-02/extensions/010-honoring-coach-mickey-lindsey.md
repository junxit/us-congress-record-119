# HONORING COACH MICKEY LINDSEY

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
                     HONORING COACH MICKEY LINDSEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a true Northwest 
Florida legend, Coach Mickey Lindsey, as Pace High School will dedicate 
its football field in his honor on Friday, September 4, 2026.
  For generations of students and families in Pace, Coach Lindsey has 
represented far more than football and his impact reached far beyond 
the scoreboard.
  Coach Lindsey understood that the lessons learned through athletics, 
hard work, and commitment could shape young people for the rest

[[Page E844]]

of their lives. For the many young men who had the privilege of playing 
for him, he was not simply a coach. He was a mentor, a role model, and 
someone who believed in them.
  That is why it is so fitting that Pace High School will permanently 
attach Coach Lindsey's name to the field where so many of those lessons 
were taught. Every Friday night, when students take the field and 
families gather in the stands, his name will serve as a reminder of the 
standard he set and the legacy he leaves behind.
  Northwest Florida is blessed with communities that take pride in 
their schools, their traditions, and the people who invest in the next 
generation. Coach Mickey Lindsey is one of those people.
  I am proud to recognize Coach Lindsey for his years of service to 
Pace High School, his dedication to his players and students, and the 
lasting mark he has made on the Pace community.
  Coach Lindsey's name will now be part of Pace football history, but 
his greatest legacy will continue to live in the lives of the young 
people he helped shape.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Coach Mickey 
Lindsey and congratulating him on this well-deserved recognition.

                          ____________________
```
