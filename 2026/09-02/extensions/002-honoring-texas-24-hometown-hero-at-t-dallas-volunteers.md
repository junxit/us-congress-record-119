# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO AT&T DALLAS VOLUNTEERS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
         HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO AT&T DALLAS VOLUNTEERS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, AT&T Dallas Volunteers. In a remarkable display of teamwork and 
community spirit, 633 AT&T employees from across North Texas came 
together at the AT&T Discovery District in Dallas to break not one, but 
two Guinness World Records: the Most Backpacks Packed in an Hour and 
the Most People Simultaneously Stuffing Backpacks.
  Their collective efforts resulted in over 10,000 backpacks filled 
with essential school supplies, from pens and notebooks to headphones, 
and even laptops for qualifying families.
  The event showcased great volunteerism, as team members from across 
the region united to make a tangible impact. I thank everyone who 
dedicated their time to making a real difference in our community.

                          ____________________
```
