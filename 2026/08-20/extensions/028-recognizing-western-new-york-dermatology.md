# RECOGNIZING WESTERN NEW YORK DERMATOLOGY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
                RECOGNIZING WESTERN NEW YORK DERMATOLOGY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Western New York Dermatology as they celebrate the arrival of advanced 
patient friendly technology for the treatment of non-melanoma skin 
cancer.
  More than 9,500 Americans are diagnosed with skin cancer every day, 
and more than three million new cases are recorded annually across the 
globe. With skin cancer rising faster than any other cancer, skin 
protection from UV exposure is more important than ever. Under the 
leadership of Dr. Michael Nazareth, the practice treats its patients 
from chronic conditions including eczema and psoriasis to advanced skin 
cancer detection and cosmetic procedures.
  Today, we celebrate the continued success and technological 
advancements of Western New York Dermatology, as well as its steadfast 
commitment to empowering patients throughout the region. As the need 
for high-quality healthcare services continues to grow, Western New 
York Dermatology remains at the forefront of patient-centered care, 
continually evolving to meet the needs of all communities.
  I congratulate Western New York Dermatology on its ongoing dedication 
to providing exceptional care and enhancing the health and well-being 
of community members across Western New York.

                          ____________________
```
