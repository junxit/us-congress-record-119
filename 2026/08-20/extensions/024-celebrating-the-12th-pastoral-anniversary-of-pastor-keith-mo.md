# CELEBRATING THE 12TH PASTORAL ANNIVERSARY OF PASTOR KEITH MOBLEY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
    CELEBRATING THE 12TH PASTORAL ANNIVERSARY OF PASTOR KEITH MOBLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
remarkable leadership and service of Pastor Keith Mobley as we honor 
his 12th Pastoral Anniversary at Mt. Olive Missionary Baptist Church in 
Lackawanna, New York. Pastor Mobley's influence extends far beyond the 
walls of the church, making a lasting impact on the lives of those in 
need.
  More than two decades ago, Pastor Mobley earned his master's degree 
in Christian Studies from the Bible Centre College of Rochester, New 
York and received his license to preach. Soon after, he served under 
the spiritual leadership of Pastor Dion J. Watkins and became ordained 
at Mt. Olive Missionary Baptist Church. For nearly ten years, Pastor 
Mobley proudly served as the Sunday School Teacher and Assistant Pastor 
before he was spiritually guided to the role of Lead Pastor in 2014, 
serving as the eighth pastor in the 80-year history of Mt. Olive 
Missionary Baptist Church.
  Pastor Mobley is supported by his beloved wife, First Lady Elizabeth 
Mobley. Together, they have formed a family centered in faith as they 
raise their four blessed children: Tyson, Talia, Tevin, and Troy. Their 
marriage exemplifies the strength found in a family that prays and 
worships together. Pastor Mobley remains committed to serving both his 
family and his community with love, devotion, and unwavering trust in 
the Lord.
  Pastor Mobley has made a priority of reaching out to his community, 
serving as a mentor and advocate for youth, adults, and community 
leaders through his role as President of the Baptist Minister's 
Conference of Buffalo and Vicinity. Congregants and community members 
have gathered throughout the month of May to recognize Pastor Mobley's 
pastoral anniversary and his steadfast commitment to Christian 
leadership.
  Today, as we celebrate twelve years of Pastor Mobley's unwavering 
dedication, let us recognize the countless lives he has touched and the 
profound impact he has made at Mt. Olive Missionary Baptist Church and 
beyond. I congratulate Pastor Mobley on this significant milestone. His 
service is a blessing, and we look forward to many more years of his 
inspired leadership.

                          ____________________
```
