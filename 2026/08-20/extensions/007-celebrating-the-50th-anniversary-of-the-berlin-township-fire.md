# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BERLIN TOWNSHIP FIRE DEPARTMENT

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BERLIN TOWNSHIP FIRE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the Berlin 
Township Fire Department on its 50th anniversary. For five decades, the 
department has proudly answered the call whenever its neighbors need 
them most.
  This milestone is a well-deserved opportunity to recognize the 
excellence and dedication that have defined the department throughout 
its history. From responding to emergencies to supporting families in 
times of need, Berlin Township firefighters have worked tirelessly to 
keep their community safe. I thank the firefighters, officers, and 
staff for their bravery in the face of danger and their unwavering 
commitment to those they serve.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating the 50th 
anniversary of the Berlin Township Fire Department and recognizing its 
outstanding record of service. I congratulate them on this important 
milestone, and wish them the best in many more years of protecting and 
serving Berlin Township.

                          ____________________
```
