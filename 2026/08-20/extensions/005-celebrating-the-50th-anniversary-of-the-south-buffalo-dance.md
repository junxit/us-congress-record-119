# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SOUTH BUFFALO DANCE ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
  CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SOUTH BUFFALO DANCE ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the South 
Buffalo Dance Academy, which is proudly celebrating its golden 
anniversary in its fiftieth year. Founded in 1976 by Mrs. Eleanor 
Donovan Gilson with just one small dance studio and a handful of 
students, Mrs. Gilson offered a welcoming space for community members 
of all ages to discover a passion for movement, find their own beat, 
and express themselves.
  For half a century, South Buffalo Dance Academy has demonstrated 
unwavering dedication to its students, the arts, and the Western New 
York community, highlighting the talents of performers throughout the 
region by offering a mixture of modern and contemporary dance training. 
Thousands of students have come through its doors, each contributing to 
the vibrant legacy of the academy. Many alumni have gone on to 
successful careers as professional dancers and choreographers and often 
return to the studio to visit and teach. South Buffalo Dance Academy 
has also earned numerous awards at competitions locally and nationally, 
recognizing the talent, technical ability, flexibility, and creativity 
showcased in its routines.
  Through consistent instruction, performances, and mentorship, the 
academy has supported generations of young artists in developing 
discipline, confidence, and creative expression, while offering a 
welcoming space for community members of all ages to discover a passion 
for movement, find their own beat, and express themselves in a true 
``home away from home.''
  Today, we honor 50 years of commitment to the arts and look ahead to 
the many years of entertainment still to come. The South Buffalo Dance 
Academy's work has touched countless lives. Their commitment to 
excellence in dance has not only elevated the arts within our community 
but has created meaningful opportunities for students to grow both on 
and off the stage.
```
