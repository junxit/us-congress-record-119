# HONORING QUEEN MOTHER DR. KATHLEEN HARMON

**Speaking:** Vargas, Juan (V000130) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING QUEEN MOTHER DR. KATHLEEN HARMON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JUAN VARGAS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. VARGAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an extraordinary 
leader, humanitarian, and champion for justice, Queen Mother Dr. 
Kathleen Harmon, in celebration of her 95th birthday.
  A true San Diego icon, Queen Mother Dr. Harmon has devoted her life 
to service and uplifting our neighbors. Queen Mother Dr. Harmon has 
worn many hats--nurse, social worker, and advocate, but community is 
always at the heart of everything she does.
  Queen Mother Dr. Harmon is the founder and guiding force behind the 
San Diego Peace Coalition. Through this work, Queen Mother Dr. Harmon 
has brought together community leaders, faith organizations, educators, 
elected officials, young people, and advocates to collaborate on 
addressing our most pressing challenges. She has worked tirelessly to 
advance initiatives to promote peace, combat gun violence, increase 
educational opportunity, and foster dialogue across diverse 
communities.
  Queen Mother Dr. Harmon is also a respected spiritual leader who has 
touched many lives. Her guidance has helped shape community 
initiatives, inspired civic engagement, and encouraged others to 
dedicate themselves to lives of purpose and compassion.
  On this milestone occasion, we celebrate Queen Mother Dr. Kathleen 
Harmon and her unwavering commitment to bettering our communities. Her 
remarkable journey stands as a testament to the enduring power of 
faith, perseverance, and selfless service.
  I am honored to recognize her for her incredible work and the 
indelible mark she's left on San Diego, California, and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
