# RECOGNIZING THE LIBRE INITIATIVE

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING THE LIBRE INITIATIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize The LIBRE 
Initiative on the auspicious occasion of its 15th Anniversary. For a 
decade and a half, LIBRE has worked tirelessly to advance economic 
freedom and opportunity for Hispanic communities across Pennsylvania 
and the Nation.
  Founded in 2011, The LIBRE Initiative has grown into one of the most 
active grassroots organizations serving Hispanic families, small 
business owners, and entrepreneurs. Through community outreach, civic 
education, and direct engagement with elected officials, LIBRE has 
helped countless individuals navigate the path toward greater economic 
self-sufficiency.
  Over the years, LIBRE has expanded its work to include English-
language classes, financial literacy workshops, job fairs, and back-to-
school drives to support working individuals and families.
  This milestone anniversary reflects 15 years of selfless and tireless 
dedication to empowering individuals to pursue their own version of the 
American Dream--free from unnecessary government barriers.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored and privileged to recognize The LIBRE 
Initiative, commend its 15 years of service to Hispanic communities in 
Pennsylvania and across our Nation, and look forward to witnessing the 
positive and meaningful results of its tireless and dedicated work for 
decades to come.
```
