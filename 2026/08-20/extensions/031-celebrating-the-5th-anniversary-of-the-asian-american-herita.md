# CELEBRATING THE 5TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ASIAN AMERICAN HERITAGE FESTIVAL

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
CELEBRATING THE 5TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ASIAN AMERICAN HERITAGE FESTIVAL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
fifth anniversary of the Asian American Heritage Festival. This 
cultural event is dedicated and celebrated in honor of the cultural 
traditions and contributions of Asian Americans throughout the United 
States. For half a decade, the Asian American Heritage Festival has 
been committed to enriching the cultural fabric of the Western New 
York, fostering deeper appreciation for the traditions, struggles, and 
contributions that continue to shape Asian American communities today.
  The Asian American Heritage Festival is a dynamic hub for community 
engagement and cross-cultural connection. Through shared experiences, 
storytelling, and cultural expression, the festival strengthens 
understanding and unity among diverse communities. It stands as a vital 
bridge to connect culture, diversity, and unity through exciting 
performances, delicious food, and shared experiences among people of 
all backgrounds.
  Beyond the exciting attractions that the Asian American Heritage 
Festival offers, there is also a strong emphasis on community and 
education. Stories of resilience and cultural history, passed down 
through generations, are shared with all who attend.
  As we celebrate the fifth anniversary of the Asian American Heritage 
Festival, let us recognize the essential role that the festival plays--
not just as an event, but as a beacon of cultural exchange, 
generational growth, and community spirit. Please join me in 
celebrating and honoring the achievements of the Asian American 
Heritage Festival as it remains an enduring source of pride and 
connection for generations to come.
```
