  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tegain Boley

  Tegain has overcome many challenges along their journey to success,

at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Tegain Boley on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
