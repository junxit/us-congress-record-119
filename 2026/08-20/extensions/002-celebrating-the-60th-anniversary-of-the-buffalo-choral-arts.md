# CELEBRATING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BUFFALO CHORAL ARTS SOCIETY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
  CELEBRATING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BUFFALO CHORAL ARTS SOCIETY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 20, 2026

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the Buffalo 
Choral Arts Society, which is proudly celebrating its sixtieth 
anniversary. Founded in 1965 by Buffalo native, Mr. Robert F. Schulz, 
the society has spent six decades enriching the cultural life of the 
community through outstanding choral performances, artistic excellence, 
and a deep commitment to the power of music.
  For six decades, the Buffalo Choral Arts Society has demonstrated 
unwavering dedication to its students, the arts, and the Western New 
York community, highlighting the talents of one hundred and thirty 
vocal performers throughout the region by offering a diverse mixture of 
classical masterworks, contemporary compositions, and popular choral 
selections. Through its commitment to artistic excellence, music 
education, and community engagement, the society has inspired audiences 
and fostered a lasting appreciation for the choral arts across 
generations.
  Through consistent instruction, performances, and mentorship, the 
society has supported generations of artists in developing discipline, 
confidence, and creative expression. Over the course of its 
distinguished sixty-year history, the Buffalo Choral Arts Society has 
benefited from the leadership and vision of four dedicated conductors: 
Mr. Robert F. Schulz, Mr. John Ricca, Ms. Marcia A. Giambrone, and 
currently, Mr. David Curtis. Each conductor has played an important 
role in shaping the organization's growth, musical excellence, and 
enduring legacy within the Western New York community.
  Today, we honor 60 years of commitment to the arts and look ahead to 
the many years of entertainment still to come. The Buffalo ChoraI Arts 
Society's work has touched countless lives. The organization's 
commitment to excellence in vocal arts has not only elevated voices 
within our community but has created meaningful opportunities for 
students to grow both on and off the stage.
```
