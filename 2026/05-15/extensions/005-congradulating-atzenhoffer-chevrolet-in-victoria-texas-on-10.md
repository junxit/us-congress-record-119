# CONGRADULATING ATZENHOFFER CHEVROLET IN VICTORIA, TEXAS ON 100 YEARS OF OPERATIONS

**Speaking:** Cloud, Michael (C001115) [R-TX]

```
CONGRADULATING ATZENHOFFER CHEVROLET IN VICTORIA, TEXAS ON 100 YEARS OF 
                               OPERATIONS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL CLOUD

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 15, 2026

  Mr. CLOUD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Atzenhoffer 
Chevrolet in Victoria, Texas, for over 100 years of business. Founded 
on April 19, 1926, by Ed Atzenhoffer, this dealership began as a modest 
operation with just two employees in a small downtown building. Through 
determination, hard work, and a commitment to treating people right, Ed 
built a business and a lasting institution of the region. Known for his 
larger-than-life personality, he set a standard of service and 
integrity that continues to define the company today.
  Their success is deeply intertwined with the story of Victoria 
itself. Over the past century, Atzenhoffer Chevrolet has played a vital 
role in the economic growth and development of Victoria and the 
surrounding region. From the early days of South Texas expansion to the 
modern era, the dealership has provided reliable transportation, 
created jobs, and supported countless local families.
  This milestone is especially remarkable because of the continuity of 
leadership across four generations. Each generation of the Atzenhoffer 
and Greeson families has carried forward the founder's vision while 
adapting to changing times. From Ed Atzenhoffer, to Milton ``Bully'' 
Greeson, to Milton Greeson, Jr., and now to current dealer-principal 
Tommy Taylor, the business has remained rooted in the same values of 
hard work, community commitment, and long-term stewardship.
  Reaching 100 years in business is no small feat. It reflects 
resilience through economic downturns, industry shifts, and changing 
consumer needs. It is a testament not only to strong leadership, but 
also to the loyalty of employees and the trust of generations of 
customers throughout South Texas.
  Today, Atzenhoffer Chevrolet stands as a cornerstone of the Victoria 
community and a shining example of the American entrepreneurial spirit 
at work. Their century-long legacy demonstrates what is possible when 
dedication, family values, and community spirit animate the principles 
of an organization. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating Atzenhoffer Chevrolet on their 100th anniversary and for 
their continued work in representing the best of what Victoria has to 
offer.
```
