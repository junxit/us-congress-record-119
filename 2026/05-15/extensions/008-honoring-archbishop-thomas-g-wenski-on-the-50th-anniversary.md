# HONORING ARCHBISHOP THOMAS G. WENSKI ON THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF HIS PRIESTLY ORDINATION

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
  HONORING ARCHBISHOP THOMAS G. WENSKI ON THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF HIS 
                          PRIESTLY ORDINATION

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 15, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a remarkable servant 
of God and our South Florida community, Archbishop Thomas G. Wenski, on 
the 50th Anniversary of his Priestly Ordination.
  For five decades, Archbishop Wenski has dedicated his life to faith, 
service, and the defense of human dignity. Throughout his ministry, he 
has been a steadfast spiritual leader for the people of South Florida 
and a strong voice for the oppressed, especially those suffering under 
tyranny and persecution around the world.
  As Archbishop of the Archdiocese of Miami, he has guided countless 
families with compassion, wisdom, and unwavering faith. His commitment 
to serving the poor, welcoming the vulnerable, and defending freedom 
has left a lasting impact on our community.
  Archbishop Wenski's leadership has strengthened the moral fabric of 
South Florida and inspired generations through his humility, courage, 
and devotion to Christ and His Church.
  On behalf of Florida's 28th Congressional District, I congratulate 
Archbishop Thomas G. Wenski on this extraordinary milestone and thank 
him for fifty years of faithful service to God, the Church, and our 
community.
  May God continue to bless Archbishop Wenski and the Archdiocese of 
Miami.

                          ____________________
```
