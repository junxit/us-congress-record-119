# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty God, our protector against all evil, our defender in every 
danger, You and Your divine concern for Your people have set among us 
brave men and women to stand as a shield around us, and for this we are 
truly grateful. We pause today to thank You for their humble dedication 
to safeguarding our communities and our country against all those who 
would shatter the peace You offer us.
  Remind us, O Lord, that the security that our policemen and our 
police forces have committed themselves to provide comes at their own 
personal cost. Aside from long hours of training and watch-standing and 
the countless missions that place each officer at significant and 
inconceivable risk, these men and women in uniform are all too often 
called to place their service over self and the welfare of others above 
the preservation of their own life and limb.
  In honoring the line of duty deaths our communities have sustained 
over this past year, make it our duty never to take lightly the weight 
of their faithful service. Bless each ceremony across the Nation that 
it would honor appropriately all those who have made these selfless 
sacrifices on our country's behalf. Speak comfort to the families who 
have also paid the untold price for our peace and security.
  Even as we pay tribute to the bravery and courage of those who gave 
their all, may they and each who continue to serve be honored by Your 
blessing and held always in Your favor. Bestow on them, even through 
the inadequacy of our words, Your own ``well-done.''
  In Your powerful name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
