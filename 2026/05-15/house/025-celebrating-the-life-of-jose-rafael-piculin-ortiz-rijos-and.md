# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF JOSE RAFAEL "PICULIN" ORTIZ RIJOS AND HONORING JOSE LUIS AND IRAD ORTIZ

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
                              {time}  1040
    CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF JOSE RAFAEL ``PICULIN'' ORTIZ RIJOS AND 
                   HONORING JOSE LUIS AND IRAD ORTIZ

  (Mr. HERNANDEZ asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Piculin 
Ortiz.
  Born in Aibonito and raised in Cayey, Piculin became a symbol of 
Puerto Rican pride, perseverance, and excellence, our ``Atletico and 
Cangrejero and compatriota''; ``athletic crabber and compatriot,'' our 
guy in the NBA and in the Olympics where we beat the U.S. dream team in 
2004.
  Piculin Ortiz will live forever in the hearts of a grateful Puerto 
Rico.
  Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor two Puerto Rican brothers, Jose Luis and 
Irad Ortiz, for their triumphant performance at the Kentucky Derby. 
They reminded an entire generation of Puerto Ricans that where you come 
from does not determine how far you can go.
  This story is Puerto Rico's story: resilient, determined, and capable 
of competing with anyone in the world.
```
