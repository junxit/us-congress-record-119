# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JESUIT PRIEST PETER PABST

**Speaking:** Liccardo, Sam T. (L000607) [D-CA]

```
       HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JESUIT PRIEST PETER PABST

  (Mr. LICCARDO asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LICCARDO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of a 
Jesuit priest, Peter Pabst. I knew him as our community knew him as 
Father Peter.
  Beneath his Hawaiian shirts, easy demeanor, and good humor, his heart 
beat with a fierce commitment to the vision that every child, 
regardless of their family's poverty, have access to college and future 
success.
  That conviction spurred his founding of the Sacred Heart Nativity 
Middle Schools a quarter century ago to educate young students in the 
predominantly Latino neighborhood of Washington-Guadalupe in San Jose.
  A decade later, he launched Cristo Rey San Jose Jesuit High School to 
prepare students from high-poverty families for college, integrating 
job opportunities with Silicon Valley's top tech companies. For the 
last 3 years, every one of Cristo Rey's hundreds of graduating seniors 
was accepted by a 4-year university, nearly all the first in their 
families to attend college.
  Father Peter also connected deeply to families in our community, 
conducting hundreds of baptisms, weddings, and funerals.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in remembering Father 
Peter. May his memory inspire all who share his conviction in the 
boundless promise of every child.
```
