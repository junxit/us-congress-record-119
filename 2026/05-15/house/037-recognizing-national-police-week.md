# RECOGNIZING NATIONAL POLICE WEEK

**Speaking:** Pappas, Chris (P000614) [D-NH]

```
                    RECOGNIZING NATIONAL POLICE WEEK

  (Mr. PAPPAS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to mark National Police Week and 
express my thanks and support for the law enforcement officers in New 
Hampshire and across the country.
  Our police answer the call every day, regardless of the danger they 
might face. They do indispensable work to fight crime, keep the peace, 
and provide assistance to those in need.
  This week, we honor those tragically lost in the line of duty, and we 
can never forget their sacrifice. We must give support and respect to 
everyone who wears the uniform, as well as their families.
  I have had the privilege to work with the New Hampshire law 
enforcement community, and I am grateful for their professionalism and 
leadership. They deserve the resources to do their jobs.
  We should be passing legislation to increase funding for our local 
departments to modernize public safety infrastructure, provide new 
technology and equipment, and hire and train more officers.

[[Page H3545]]

  Mr. Speaker, I join my colleagues in thanking the men and women who 
swear an oath to protect and serve and keep us safe.

                          ____________________
```
