# HONORING CREW OF ARTEMIS II

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
                      HONORING CREW OF ARTEMIS II

  (Ms. KAPTUR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a full heart to honor the 
brave crew of Artemis II. They journeyed farther than any other human 
beings ever before them, returning home safely to Earth.
  Their mission, which was aided by training and research from northern 
Ohio's Glenn Research Facility and Armstrong Test Center, reminds us of 
the deep bonds that hold us together: faith, family, and country.
  These astronauts carried with them the hopes of a nation. In their 
words upon return, we heard humility, gratitude, and a renewed love for 
our fragile planet.
  Let me also pay tribute to the naval ship that welcomed them home, 
the USS John P. Murtha, named for a resolute Marine Corps veteran, 
former colleague here in the House, and son of Pennsylvania who devoted 
his life to serving America. That vessel stood as a symbol of steady 
resolve of duty carried out far from shore.
  From the heartland of northern Ohio and western Pennsylvania, and our 
shores from coast to coast to the heavens above, this mission belongs 
to every American who dares to dream.
  May we honor their courage not only with words but with continued 
commitment and funding to exploration, unity, and future generations 
who will follow in their footsteps.
  God bless them all.

                          ____________________
```
