# RECOGNIZING OFFICER TYLER CUNNINGHAM

**Speaking:** Hurd, Jeff (H001100) [R-CO]

```
                  RECOGNIZING OFFICER TYLER CUNNINGHAM

  (Mr. HURD of Colorado asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today during National 
Police Week to recognize the heroic actions of Officer Tyler Cunningham 
of the Montrose Police Department.
  While responding to a residential structure fire alongside fellow 
first responders, Officer Cunningham arrived to find the roof and back 
of the home fully engulfed in flames. After learning that a woman 
remained trapped inside, he quickly assessed the situation, forced 
entry through the front door, and entered the burning residence. 
Officer Cunningham was able to find the woman and carry her to safety, 
where EMS personnel were waiting on scene.
  In moments of crisis, first responders are called upon to make split-
second decisions under extraordinary pressure. Officer Cunningham's 
courage, quick judgment, and willingness to put himself in harm's way 
saved a life that day.
  His actions reflect the professionalism, selflessness, and dedication 
displayed every day by law enforcement officers and first responders 
across Colorado's Third District.
  I ask all of my colleagues to join me in thanking Officer Cunningham 
for his bravery and for his continued service to the people of 
Colorado.

                          ____________________
```
