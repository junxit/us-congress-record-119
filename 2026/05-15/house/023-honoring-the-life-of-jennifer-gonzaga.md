# HONORING THE LIFE OF JENNIFER GONZAGA

**Speaking:** Mejia, Analilia (M001246) [D-NJ]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF JENNIFER GONZAGA

  (Ms. MEJIA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the life of Mrs. Jennifer 
Gonzaga, who passed away on May 10, 2026, after dedicating her life to 
education and the joy of nurturing young minds.
  For 25 years, Jennifer served as a first and second grade teacher at 
Linden School in Glen Ridge, New Jersey. Her commitment to her students 
and her passion for teaching left an indelible mark on countless lives. 
She was known for her patience, creativity, and unwavering dedication 
to fostering a love of learning in her classroom.
  She is survived by her devoted husband, Rommel ``Mel'' Gonzaga. Their 
enduring 29-year partnership was a testament to love and support 
through all of life's seasons.
  Jennifer's memory will live on in the hearts of all who knew her, 
including myself.

                          ____________________
```
