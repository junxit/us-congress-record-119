# RECOGNIZING REVEREND O. ROBERT DeMARTINIS

**Speaking:** Tonko, Paul (T000469) [D-NY]

```
               RECOGNIZING REVEREND O. ROBERT DeMARTINIS

  (Mr. TONKO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TONKO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a man of unyielding 
faith, compassion, and love, Reverend O. Robert DeMartinis, known to 
New York's capital region as Father Bob. This year celebrates the 40th 
anniversary of his ordination as a priest.
  Born in Amsterdam, New York, on November 11, 1956, to Filomena Sasso 
and the late Tullio DeMartinis, Father Bob, guided by the deep faith of 
his parents and service as an altar boy for Fathers Robert Baltch and 
Joseph Beck, sensed his calling at an early age.
  He was ordained in Florida in 1986 and returned home to Amsterdam on 
Trinity Sunday to celebrate his first mass that year at St. Casimir's 
Church. He permanently returned to the Albany Catholic Diocese in 1990, 
serving at several parishes within the diocese.
  It was in 2010 that Father Bob was welcomed for service in his 
hometown of Amsterdam to shepherd the congregation of St. Stanislaus. 
Today, as pastor of both St. Stanislaus and Our Lady of Mount Carmel 
Churches, his devotion and spirit of service shine through every mass 
he offers and every religious assignment he assumes.
  As we honor his four decades of faithful service, a hearty ``for many 
more years''; ``ad multos annos,'' Father Bob, is rendered.
  I congratulate Father Bob on his many years of service.

                          ____________________
```
