# SENATE MUST ACT

**Speaking:** Downing, Troy (D000634) [R-MT]

```
                            SENATE MUST ACT

  (Mr. DOWNING asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DOWNING. Mr. Speaker, I will take this opportunity to recognize 
the importance of the passage of the 2026 farm bill.
  The House-passed 2026 farm bill delivers certainty for our producers 
through enhancing critical initiatives such as the USDA's farm credit 
and crop insurance programs.
  These measures will ensure that farmers and ranchers have access to 
the capital that they need in order to navigate ongoing challenges like 
volatile commodity prices and extreme weather events going forward.
  From wheat growers on the Hi-Line to cattle ranchers in eastern 
Montana, this bill will provide necessary stability for producers in my 
home district, allowing them to keep feeding our Nation.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Chairman GT Thompson for his hard work on this 
effort. The House has acted, and now the Senate must do the same. I 
urge swift passage of the farm bill in the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
