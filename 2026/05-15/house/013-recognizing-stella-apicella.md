# RECOGNIZING STELLA APICELLA

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING STELLA APICELLA

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Madam Speaker, let me celebrate young Stella Apicella, a 
sixth grader at Blind Brook Middle School in Rye Brook, as she prepares 
to become a bat mitzvah on May 30.
  I have seen Stella grow up over the years, the daughter of Joe 
Apicella and Tara Rosenblum, two prominent Westchester residents. 
Stella is growing as a confident, thoughtful, and compassionate young 
woman. She loves basketball--go Knicks--soccer, dancing, and fashion. 
She is a talented public speaker through debate club and by sharing the 
stage at times with her prominent mother.
  Most importantly, Stella gives back to her community. She stands up 
for others, and she always looks out for the underdog, qualities that 
will serve her well far beyond this special day.
  Stella, mazel tov on this great accomplishment.
  Madam Speaker, from the floor of the U.S. House of Representatives, I 
send my best to her.
```
