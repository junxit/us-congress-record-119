# HONORING AIRMEN JESSE ARGUELLO AND SKYLUV RIOS

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Mike (K000403) [R-UT]

```
             HONORING AIRMEN JESSE ARGUELLO AND SKYLUV RIOS

  (Mr. KENNEDY of Utah asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. KENNEDY of Utah. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend two of our 
Nation's finest who saved a life that otherwise would surely have been 
lost.
  On March 15, a man driving through Florida experienced a medical 
emergency that caused him to crash into a shallow body of water, fully 
submerging the vehicle.
  Airman Jesse Arguello and Airman Skyluv Rios, both of the Naval 
Aviation Schools Command leapt into action with military precision.
  While Airman Rios summoned emergency services, Airman Arguello jumped 
into the water in an attempt to rescue the now drowning driver.
  After kicking a hole in the sunroof and tearing out chunks of glass 
with his bear hands, Airman Arguello pulled the driver through the 
broken sunroof opening and dragged this individual to safety on shore.
  I couldn't be more proud of these two young airmen. They represent 
the finest tradition of our military warriors. I am especially proud to 
say that Airman Jesse Arguello hails from my own Third District in 
Utah.
  Mr. Speaker, may God bless these two great examples of what is making 
America great again.

                          ____________________
```
