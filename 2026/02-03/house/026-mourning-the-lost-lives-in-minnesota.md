# MOURNING THE LOST LIVES IN MINNESOTA

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

```
                  MOURNING THE LOST LIVES IN MINNESOTA

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Still I rise, Mr. Speaker, in mourning for the 
lives that have been lost at the hands and the color of law.
  Mr. Speaker, Mr. Pretti and Ms. Good should not have lost their lives 
as they did. Yes, I stand for impeachment of Ms. Noem, the Secretary, 
but that is not enough. I believe that under these circumstances more 
is required than a simple impeachment. I think it is a good thing to 
do, and I support it. However, there is some question of criminality 
involved with this. In such cases of criminality, we must do more than 
impeach. We must convict. She should do time.
  It is time for us to send a message to others that you cannot cover 
up and you cannot condone this kind of behavior when you hold public 
trust.
  Mr. Speaker, still I rise, and I stand where I stand.

                          ____________________
```
