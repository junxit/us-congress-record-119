# HONORING THE BOROUGH OF JESSUP'S 150TH ANNIVERSARY AND THE INAUGURAL JESSUP GROUNDHOG DAY

**Speaking:** Bresnahan, Robert P. (B001327) [R-PA]

```
 HONORING THE BOROUGH OF JESSUP'S 150TH ANNIVERSARY AND THE INAUGURAL 
                          JESSUP GROUNDHOG DAY

                                  _____
                                 

                     HON. ROBERT P. BRESNAHAN, JR.

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 3, 2026

  Mr. BRESNAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
Proclamation honoring the 150th Anniversary of the Borough of Jessup 
and the Inaugural Jessup Groundhog Day.

       Whereas, the Borough of Jessup proudly celebrates its 150th 
     anniversary, honoring generations of families, neighbors, and 
     volunteers who have built a strong and close-knit community; 
     and
       Whereas, community traditions bring people together, 
     strengthen civic pride, and reflect the shared history and 
     character that make Jessup a special place to call home; and
       Whereas, today the Borough of Jessup celebrates the 
     inaugural Jessup Groundhog Day at Station Park, marking the 
     beginning of a new hometown tradition inspired by 
     Pennsylvania folklore and shaped by local spirit; and
       Whereas, today's celebration includes music, traditional 
     attire, an official scroll reading, and the annual forecast 
     delivered by Jessup's own prognosticator, Jessup Giuseppe, 
     whose presence adds charm, creativity, and local character to 
     this community gathering; and
       Whereas, the event highlights the warmth, creativity, and 
     togetherness of the Jessup community, giving neighbors and 
     families of all ages the chance to connect, have fun, and 
     share in the joy of this special anniversary year; and
       Now therefore, I, Rob Bresnahan, Jr., Member of Congress, 
     do hereby recognize and commend the organizers, volunteers, 
     and community members who made the inaugural Jessup Groundhog 
     Day possible. I am proud to celebrate this new tradition with 
     the people of Jessup and to join in recognizing 150 years of 
     community, history, and shared pride.

                          ____________________
```
