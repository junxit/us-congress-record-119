# RECOGNIZING GARY BRAUNSCHEIDLE

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
                     RECOGNIZING GARY BRAUNSCHEIDLE

                                  _____
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 3, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
extraordinary dedication and service of Gary Braunscheidle, who has 
devoted 62 years to serving the Town of Pendleton and the Wendelville 
Volunteer Fire Company. Over the course of his long and distinguished 
career, Gary has responded to over 200 emergencies in the past year 
alone, demonstrating his unwavering commitment to public safety.
  Gary has held multiple roles within the company, including Assistant 
Chief and company mechanic, and has been a cornerstone of the social 
and fundraising efforts that support the Wendelville Volunteer Fire 
Company. He has played a vital role in events such as making chowder, 
managing the kitchen for the Gun Raffle, assisting with Fire 
Prevention, and supporting Company Field Days and the Harvest Moon 5K.
  Gary's guidance, leadership, and readiness to help in any capacity 
have made him an indispensable member of the Wendelville Volunteer Fire 
Company.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in honoring Gary Braunscheidle for his remarkable 62 years of 
service and in wishing him continued success and fulfillment in his 
ongoing commitment to the community.
```
