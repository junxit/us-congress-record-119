# RECOGNIZING RANDY MOJE

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
                         RECOGNIZING RANDY MOJE

                                  _____
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 3, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
extraordinary dedication and service of Randy Moje, who has devoted 50 
years to serving the Town of Pendleton and the Wendelville Volunteer 
Fire Company. A second-generation firefighter, Randy has held numerous 
leadership positions, including Chief, and has consistently 
demonstrated commitment to public safety and the growth of the 
Wendelville Volunteer Fire Company.
  Randy has been a leader in Fire Prevention, spending many hours with 
the Fire Prevention trailer educating the community, and has been an 
essential contributor to fundraising events such as chowder-making, 
lobster dinners, and the Farmers Breakfast. His dedication extends to 
supporting both the operational and social functions that sustain the 
Wendelville Volunteer Fire Company.
  Randy's experience, leadership, and willingness to serve in any 
capacity have inspired generations of firefighters and strengthened the 
company's presence in the community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in honoring Randy Moje for his remarkable 50 years of service 
and in wishing him continued success and fulfillment in his ongoing 
commitment to the community.
```
