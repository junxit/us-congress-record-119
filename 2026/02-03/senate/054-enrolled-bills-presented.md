# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 269, S. 3424

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, February 3, 2026, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 269. An act to improve coordination between Federal and 
     State agencies and the Do Not Pay working system.
       S. 3424. An act to amend titles 11 and 28, United States 
     Code, to modify the compensation payable to trustees serving 
     in cases under chapter 7 of title 11, United States Code, to 
     extend the term of certain temporary offices of bankruptcy 
     judges, and for other purposes.
```
