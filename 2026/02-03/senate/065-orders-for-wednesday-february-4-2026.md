# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2026

**Speaking:** Banks, Jim (B001299) [R-IN]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2026

  Mr. BANKS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, February 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to 
executive session and resume consideration of Executive Calendar No. 
614, Aaron Peterson, postcloture.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
