# Department of Homeland Security (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    Department of Homeland Security

  Mr. President, now on ICE, yesterday, Secretary of Homeland Security 
Kristi Noem, who for some reason still has a job--she should have been 
fired long ago--announced that all Federal immigration agents in 
Minneapolis are going to begin wearing body cameras.
  I want to be very clear about something. Kristi Noem does not get 
participation points by saying agents are going to start doing 
something they should have done all along.
  Why just Minneapolis? This policy, which is the right policy, should 
be nationwide. There is no reason to delay that.
  Most importantly, Executive actions alone will never be enough for 
the American people. We need to pass legislation. We know how whimsical 
Donald Trump is. He will say one thing one day and retract it the next.
  The same with Secretary Noem--we don't trust some Executive order, 
some pronouncement from some Cabinet Secretary. We need it enshrined 
into law.
  What Democrats want is exceedingly common sense. We need to end the 
roving patrols. This means we need arrest warrants and an end to racial 
profiling.
  Second, we need accountability. ICE needs to abide by the same rules 
as local police, including embedded investigations. And there has to be 
real accountability forcing them to do so when they don't.
  And, third, masks need to come off, and cameras need to stay on. 
Every ICE officer should do what every police officer, or just about 
every police officer, in America does--have an ID. Every citizen knows 
when they meet a police officer or sheriff, their ID is clear on them.
  There has always been accountability that we have had for decades and 
decades. And Noem thinks and some people think--like Speaker Johnson 
thinks--we shouldn't do it. Why is Johnson pro-secret police?
  It is absurd for the Speaker and others like him to argue that masked 
ICE agents should get special treatment and be held to a different 
standard from just about every law enforcement Agency in the country. 
Sheriffs, local police, traffic cops--you don't see them with masks, do 
you? No. Defending ICE agents wearing masks and giving them special 
treatment, especially when they are so brutal and thuggish, is totally 
against the views of most Americans who want basic accountability.
  Anyone defending these agents keeping their masks on is not seriously 
trying to solve this huge problem with the chaos we have seen in 
Minneapolis and in so many of our other cities when ICE is sent in 
there. It begs the question: Why are some Republicans speaking so 
loudly trying to defend masked secret police? Is it that these new ICE 
hires weren't qualified as police officers in other departments? Could 
it be that some of them were kicked out of police departments and then 
they signed up to snatch people off the streets and beat protesters up 
with impunity because that is what they wanted to do?
  The American people have a right to know, and the first step is 
identifying who they are.
  Finally, I want to make one other very important point. Since the 
deaths of Alex Pretti and Renee Good, nothing meaningful has changed 
about ICE's abuses. Nothing meaningful has changed about ICE's abuses 
even since the deaths of Pretti and Good.
  Last week, in Portland, OR, Federal agents fired tear gas at a crowd 
of demonstrators that included children.
  There is a video circulating online of an agent in Colorado stealing 
a woman's phone. When she tried to get it back, the agent shoved her to 
the ground, yanked her by the hair, and then battered her. If you saw 
that video, it would turn your stomach.
  Greg Bovino, the disgraced chief thug, former head of the Border 
Patrol, is seen in another video trying to motivate agents by saying--
this is what Bovino said: ``Arrest as many people who touch you as 
want. Those are the general orders way from the top.''
  This is absolutely not, not, not what the American people signed up 
for. Republicans in Congress need to see the chaos and realize it is 
finally time for ICE to be reined in.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Republican whip.
```
