# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our Father in Heaven, in a turbulent world, we turn our eyes to You. 
At a time when change, decay, and division seem to intimidate, You 
remain the changeless one.
  Mighty God, today, keep our lawmakers within the center of Your will, 
using them for Your glory. May their daily motive be to permit Your 
Kingdom to come and Your will to be done on Earth as it is in Heaven. 
Surround them with the shield of Your divine protection, as You do for 
them more than they can ask or imagine.
  Accept, O Lord, our thanks and praise for all You have done for us.
  We pray in Your glorious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
