                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 266

  At the request of Mr. Kaine, the names of the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Ms. Baldwin) and the Senator from Nevada (Ms. Cortez Masto) were added 
as cosponsors of S. 266, a bill to reauthorize the Dr. Lorna Breen 
Health Care Provider Protection Act, and for other purposes.

Connecticut (Mr. Blumenthal) and the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Husted) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 475, a bill to amend title XVIII of the 
Social Security Act to ensure appropriate access to non-opioid pain 
management drugs under part D of the Medicare program.
