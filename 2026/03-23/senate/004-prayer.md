# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, who does wondrous things, blessed be Your glorious name 
forever. Remake us in Your image, and bring our hearts under Your 
control.
  Lord, infuse our Senators with a love for You that will make their 
obedience willing, joyful, and consistent. Astound them with Your 
limitless resources and supply all their needs from Your bounty. Keep 
them humble with the conviction that they can't breathe a breath, think 
a thought, or speak a word without Your mercy, grace, and power.
  And, Lord, as the former chaplain of the U.S. Marine Corps, I thank 
You for the life and service of Robert S. Mueller III.
  We pray in Your great name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
