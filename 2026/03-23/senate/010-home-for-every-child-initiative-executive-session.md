# Home for Every Child Initiative (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                    Home for Every Child Initiative

  On another matter, Mr. President, in my years in the U.S. Senate, I 
have been a person very interested in the foster care system serving 
the young people. So I would like to recognize a meaningful initiative 
that the Administration for Children and Families, or ACF, launched 
called A Home for Every Child.
  ACF's A Home for Every Child initiative ``sets an ambitious goal of 
achieving a foster home-to-child ratio greater than one-to-one in every 
state.'' I applaud this very ambitious goal.
  I also have been working to beef up foster parent recruitment and 
retention efforts because kids in foster care deserve qualified, well-
trained, and loving parents.
  My bipartisan Recruiting Families Using Data Act complements ACF's A 
Home for Every Child initiative by enabling State child welfare 
agencies to

[[Page S1526]]

integrate best practices along with quality data. This will help ensure 
that foster youths and parents are best supported.
  When referring to raising a child, a common phrase is used: It takes 
a village. That phrase rings more true when a child experiences foster 
care.
  So I want to take this opportunity to thank First Lady Melania Trump 
for her work to modernize the child welfare system. It will strengthen 
partnerships for youth aging out of foster care through the program 
entitled Fostering the Future for American Children and Families 
initiative.
  I also want to thank Assistant Secretary Adams for following through 
on his commitment to tackle the foster recruitment and retention 
challenge that is tainting child welfare agencies nationwide.
  I look forward to continuing to work with the Senate Committee on 
Finance to advance the Recruiting Families Using Data Act.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
