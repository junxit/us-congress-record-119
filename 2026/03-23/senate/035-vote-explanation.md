# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, on Friday, March 20, 2026, I attended 
the funeral service of MSG Nicole Amor, of White Bear Lake, MN, who was 
killed in action while serving in Kuwait. I would have voted against 
the DHS appropriations bill that was before the Senate, (rollcall vote 
No. 59), as I had on rollcall vote Nos. 38, 39, 47, and 54.
  As I have stated in the past, I support proposals that will 
immediately fund the Department of Homeland Security's critical 
national security functions such as cyber defense, airport safety, and 
the Coast Guard. But, following DHS's actions in Minnesota which 
resulted in the deaths of two American citizens, as well as repeated 
violations of the Constitution and defiance of court orders, I will not 
vote to support continued funding for ICE, which already has $75 
billion in guaranteed funding from the bill that was passed last 
summer, until critical reforms to rein in unlawful behavior and restore 
accountability are secured.
```
