# Department of Homeland Security (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    Department of Homeland Security

  Mr. President, on Homeland Security talks. Over the weekend--and for 
weeks now--Democrats have engaged Republicans in good faith to pay our 
TSA workers, to fund DHS, while also enacting commonsense safeguards to 
rein in ICE.
  Both sides talked all weekend long. We had some constructive 
conversations, but there is considerably more work to be done, and 
Democrats are ready to keep negotiating. Democrats believe TSA staff 
should be paid now--now.
  We tried to move forward this weekend to pay these workers, but 
Republicans have blocked pay for TSA workers in a rollcall vote where 
they all voted no on Saturday; and additionally, they have blocked pay 
for TSA workers seven times when Democrats tried to bring it up.
  It is preposterous because the chaos at our airports is simply 
unacceptable. Still, Democrats are going to keep working in good faith.
  Donald Trump, meanwhile, is trying to sabotage negotiations, 
demanding that talks stop entirely until Congress passes the SAVE Act, 
a bill that has nothing--nothing--to do with paying TSA workers and 
nothing to do with ending the shutdown.
  That is right. Donald Trump is now saying: We will pay TSA only after 
Congress passes voter suppression.
  What a ridiculous thing to do. What a callous thing to do. He doesn't 
give a damn about the American people. He cares about his own election. 
He thinks this SAVE Act--which isn't going to pass--will change how the 
election comes out, and he uses millions and millions of Americans as 
hostages.
  How can our Republican friends on the other side of the aisle go 
along with this? It stinks on its face. You don't need any deeper 
explanation.
  This morning, unfortunately, Trump doubled down in Memphis, saying 
explicitly as possible he won't pay TSA workers and he won't end this 
shutdown until the SAVE Act is passed.
  Let's make it very clear: Donald Trump is the one standing in the way 
of paychecks for TSA workers with this ridiculous attempt to pass the 
SAVE Act; the SAVE Act, which would destroy a lot of what we know about 
democracy; the SAVE Act, which would disenfranchise 20 million American 
citizens in all likelihood from voting in the next election; the SAVE 
Act,

[[Page S1528]]

which is designed to do as Kristi Noem said, to allow people to vote 
who we like how they vote as opposed to others.
  Well, Donald Trump, your latest temper tantrum is not going to work. 
The SAVE Act does not have the votes to pass this Chamber, and 
Democrats will fight it every step of the way. The SAVE Act has nothing 
to do with DHS, Homeland Security; it has nothing to do with TSA 
workers or making sure ICE follows the same rules as every other 
Federal law enforcement Agency in the country.
  Every other law enforcement agency uses warrants when they bust--
needs a warrant when they bust into your home. Every other law 
enforcement agency doesn't wear masks.
  Trump and the Republicans are insisting that continue, and they are 
saying we are holding TSA workers and millions and millions of 
passengers hostage. And now all of the sudden, in addition to that, 
Trump says: I won't move forward unless we pass the SAVE Act, which 
isn't going happen, Mr. Trump. It is not going to happen.
  Democrats' position has not changed. We want to pay TSA workers 
today. We are ready to meet with the White House today to keep talking. 
In fact, we were going to meet this morning with Tom Homan, but 
apparently the White House pulled that meeting because of Donald 
Trump's temper tantrum.
  They are all scrambling around there in the White House. They don't 
know what to do. Their boss, the President, comes out with these 
idiotic statements every day, whether it is related to the war in Iran 
where he contradicts himself, whether it is related to the SAVE Act 
where he doesn't even know what it does, or related to what is 
happening in Homeland Security.
  We hope that Donald Trump will stop derailing these talks and let 
negotiations continue. Democrats want to be reasonable, and we are 
going to keep working in good faith to reach a solution. We need to pay 
TSA workers ASAP. The American people want ICE to change its violent 
ways. They do not want to repeat the kind of violence we saw earlier 
this year in Minnesota. They certainly do not want to see ICE detaining 
passengers at airports and making things worse.
  Donald Trump is tying himself in a triple knot on the Iran war, on 
the SAVE Act, and on TSA workers. And he makes--he tightened that knot 
around his waist last night by saying ICE should go to the airports, 
then by saying that he would not go forward unless the SAVE Act passed.
  He is making it harder and harder for himself. He is making himself 
less and less popular with the American people, and he is causing tens 
of millions to wonder: What is going on in that man's head?
  So that is the dilemma we all face, and it is the American people who 
suffer. They suffer at the airports. They suffer as gas prices go up 
because of the Iran war. They suffer because he is threatening their 
democracy.
  Now, let me talk about ICE at the airports. This morning, Tom Homan 
from the Department of Homeland Security said ICE agents have arrived 
at 14 airports--New York, Chicago, Atlanta, Pittsburgh, and elsewhere. 
Everywhere ICE goes, trouble follows. We have seen that, and it is 
highly likely the airports will be no exception.
  No one trusts that ICE is going to make things better. Patrolling 
airports is not in ICE's job description; it is certainly not what they 
are trained to do. Let's be clear: ICE agents performing the work of 
TSA officers are not going to make our airports any safer.
  It is going to make them less safe because ICE agents aren't trained 
in airport security. These agents aren't familiar with the layout of 
the airports they are walking into. They don't know the protocols and 
procedures in the same way TSA workers do.
  It is a simple solution, Donald Trump: Pay the TSA workers so you 
don't have to bring ICE agents in there.
  ICE agents are going to miss things, and that is assuming they focus 
on security. What if they do what they have done in other cities and 
start harassing travelers, asking to check papers, check their 
citizenship, detain them without due process even if they are American 
citizens, just as we have seen them do for months now?

  And I wouldn't be surprised if Donald Trump and Stephen Miller would 
encourage them to do just that at the airport.
  In any case, ICE's mere presence is going to inflame the situation at 
these airports where tensions are already high. Every time a traveler 
walks to their gate or stands in line at security and sees an ICE agent 
lurking about, they are going to be reminded of Donald Trump's chaos. 
They are going to be reminded of Minneapolis, and they are going to 
think: The chaos is now coming to my city too.
  So, Donald Trump, the more you keep ICE agents at our airports, the 
more you will be reminding people of how much chaos and fear ICE has 
already caused. It is a terrible idea that could backfire on the 
country and on Donald Trump.
  Instead of trying to replace TSA workers with ICE agents, Trump 
should pay TSA workers right away, get Republicans to come to a deal 
before the chaos gets worse.
  Let me repeat that because it is so simple. Instead of trying to 
replace TSA workers with ICE agents, Donald Trump should simply pay TSA 
workers and get Republicans to come to a deal before the chaos gets 
even worse.
```
