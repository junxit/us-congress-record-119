# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 28, S. 567, S. 1088, S. 1395, S. 1454, S. 1532, S. 1555, S. 1651, S. 3208, S. 3257, S. 3304, S. 3556, S. 3654, S. 3788, S. 3958, S. 4001, S. 4023, S. 4063, S. 4064, S. 4066, S. 4146

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 28

  At the request of Mr. Cruz, the name of the Senator from Georgia (Mr. 
Warnock) was added as a cosponsor of S. 28, a bill to require the 
disclosure of a camera or recording capability in certain internet-
connected devices.


                                 S. 567

  At the request of Mr. Whitehouse, the names of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick), the Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt) and 
the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch) were added as cosponsors of S. 567, 
a bill to award a Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to the First 
Rhode Island Regiment, in recognition of their dedicated service during 
the Revolutionary War.


                                S. 1088

  At the request of Mrs. Shaheen, the names of the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Scott) and the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 1088, a bill to provide that the memorial to 
commemorate the sacrifice and service of the women who worked on the 
home front to support the efforts of the United States military during 
World War II may be located on the National Mall, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 1395

  At the request of Mr. Coons, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1395, a bill to 
authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to carry out activities to 
reduce food loss and waste, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1454

  At the request of Mr. Kennedy, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1454, a 
bill to amend the Animal Welfare Act to provide for greater protection 
of roosters, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1532

  At the request of Mr. Crapo, the name of the Senator from Ohio (Mr. 
Moreno) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1532, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to modify the railroad track maintenance 
credit.


                                S. 1555

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the name of the Senator from Nevada (Ms. 
Cortez Masto) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1555, a bill to increase 
loan limits for loans made to small manufacturers, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 1651

  At the request of Mr. Mullin, the name of the Senator from Idaho (Mr. 
Risch) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1651, a bill to require the 
Federal Communications Commission to ensure equitable and 
nondiscriminatory contributions to the mechanisms that preserve and 
advance universal service, to reduce the financial burden on consumers, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 3208

  At the request of Mr. Curtis, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3208, a bill to 
codify the Six Assurances to Taiwan, provide congressional review of 
the Six Assurances, protect Taiwan from coercion, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 3257

  At the request of Mr. Hoeven, the names of the Senator from Idaho 
(Mr. Crapo) and the Senator from Delaware (Mr. Coons) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 3257, a bill to require the Administrator of the 
Federal Aviation Administration to revise regulations for certain 
individuals carrying out aviation activities who disclose a mental 
health diagnosis or condition, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3304

  At the request of Mr. Wicker, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Mr. Coons) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3304, a bill to provide for 
the coverage of medical food and vitamins and individual amino acids 
for digestive and inherited metabolic disorders under Federal health 
programs, to ensure State and Federal protection for existing coverage, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 3556

  At the request of Ms. Alsobrooks, the names of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) and the Senator from California (Mr. 
Schiff) were added as cosponsors of S. 3556, a bill to reauthorize and 
improve the wildlife crossings program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3654

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the names of the Senator from 
Georgia (Mr. Ossoff), the Senator from Nevada (Ms. Rosen) and the 
Senator from New York (Mrs. Gillibrand) were added as cosponsors of S. 
3654, a bill to improve housing and environmental health and safety 
protections for members of the Armed Forces and their families residing 
in military family housing, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3788

  At the request of Mr. Scott of Florida, the name of the Senator from 
Idaho (Mr. Risch) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3788, a bill to amend 
the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to require drug labeling to 
include original manufacturer and supply chain information.


                                S. 3958

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the name of the Senator from New 
Jersey (Mr. Kim) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3958, a bill to amend 
the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to transition Puerto Rico to the 
supplemental nutrition assistance program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4001

  At the request of Ms. Warren, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Lujan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4001, a bill to amend title 
XVI of the Social Security Act to update eligibility for the 
supplemental security income program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4023

  At the request of Mr. Young, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Lujan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4023, a bill to establish 
the America's Living Library Project, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4063

  At the request of Mr. Cruz, the name of the Senator from Pennsylvania 
(Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4063, a bill to impose 
sanctions on the Polisario Front if it cooperates with an Iranian-
affiliated terrorist organization.


                                S. 4064

  At the request of Mr. Boozman, the names of the Senator from Ohio 
(Mr. Husted) and the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Moreno) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 4064, a bill to provide for a system of regulation of 
the offer and sale of digital commodities by the Commodity Futures 
Trading Commission, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4066

  At the request of Mr. Hawley, the name of the Senator from North 
Carolina (Mr. Budd) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4066, a bill to 
provide that the approved application under the Federal Food, Drug, and 
Cosmetic Act for the drug mifepristone for the purpose of the 
termination of intrauterine pregnancy is deemed to have been withdrawn, 
to establish a Federal tort for harm to women caused by chemical 
abortion drugs, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4146

  At the request of Ms. Lummis, the names of the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Scott) and the Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 4146, a bill to amend the Endangered Species Act of 
1973 with respect to the listing of threatened and endangered species, 
and for other purposes.
```
