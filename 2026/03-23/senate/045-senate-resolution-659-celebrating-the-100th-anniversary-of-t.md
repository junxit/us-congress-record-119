# SENATE RESOLUTION 659--CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE AMERICAN SHORE AND BEACH PRESERVATION ASSOCIATION

**Measures:** S.Res. 659

```
    SENATE RESOLUTION 659--CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE 
           AMERICAN SHORE AND BEACH PRESERVATION ASSOCIATION

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE (for himself and Mr. Cassidy) submitted the following 
resolution; which was referred to the Committee on the Judiciary:

                              S. Res. 659

       Whereas the year 2026 marks the centennial of the American 
     Shore and Beach Preservation Association (in this preamble 
     referred to as the ``Association''), which has for a century 
     advanced a science-guided commitment to coastal restoration 
     and protection;
       Whereas, in 1926, the Association's visionary leaders 
     sought to establish a forum ``where the novice and he who has 
     long wrestled with these problems can seek more light; where 
     the man of science and the statesman can exchange ideas and 
     collaborate together; where idealist and lover of Nature and 
     the economist can arrive at a mutual understanding; where 
     landscape artist and philanthropist can find an outlet for 
     their activities,'' thereby fostering collaboration and 
     innovation for coastal stewardship;
       Whereas the critical issues of shore and beach 
     preservation, which promote community protection, a strong 
     economy, ecological health, and recreation, remain as vital 
     in 2026 as they were at the time of the Association's 
     founding;
       Whereas United States coastal communities and natural 
     resources have benefitted from the Association's work in 
     translating coastal science and engineering, guiding policy, 
     and advocating for resilient shores and beaches over the past 
     century; and
       Whereas, in 2026, the Association is celebrating its 100th 
     anniversary: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) recognizes and celebrates the 100th anniversary of the 
     American Shore and Beach Preservation Association (in this 
     resolution referred to as the ``Association'');
       (2) commemorates the centennial of the Association, 
     honoring a century of shore and beach preservation and 
     progress; and
       (3) congratulates the leaders and members of the 
     Association on a century of dedication to the shores and 
     beaches of the United States and recognizes the enduring 
     importance of its mission.
```
