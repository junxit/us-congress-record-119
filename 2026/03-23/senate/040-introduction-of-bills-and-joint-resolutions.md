# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 4160, S. 4161, S. 4162, S. 4163, S. 4164

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SCHIFF (for himself, Mr. Curtis, and Ms. Cortez 
             Masto):
       S. 4160. A bill to amend the Commodity Exchange Act to 
     prohibit certain event contracts involving sports and casino-
     style games; to the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and 
     Forestry.
           By Mr. SHEEHY (for himself and Mr. Kelly):
       S. 4161. A bill to authorize the transfer by the Secretary 
     of the Navy to the U.S. Space and Rocket Center Commission in 
     Huntsville, Alabama, of certain F-14 Tomcat aircraft; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. KING (for himself and Mr. Ricketts):
       S. 4162. A bill to improve the provision of services from 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs to incarcerated veterans, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Veterans' 
     Affairs.
           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND (for herself, Mrs. Blackburn, and 
             Mr. Schumer):
       S. 4163. A bill to amend the National Quantum Initiative 
     Act to require support for regional innovation initiatives in 
     quantum information science and technology, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Mr. WICKER (for himself and Mr. Reed):
       S. 4164. A bill to make technical corrections to the 
     National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026; 
     considered and passed.

                          ____________________
```
