# Department of Homeland Security (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ], Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI], Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
                    Department of Homeland Security

  Mr. President, we know that what the Democrats are doing in blocking 
the payment of appropriations for the Department of Homeland Security 
is endangering public safety in a number of ways--first, at the 
airports, where

[[Page S1531]]

our TSA agents are overwhelmed and are not being paid. They have now 
missed two paychecks. This was a basic security measure put in place 
after 9/11, after the terrorist attacks of 9/11.

  We know one component of ICE is the Homeland Security Investigations, 
which works hand in hand with the FBI to investigate domestic terrorism 
attacks. So, literally, by not funding Homeland Security 
Investigations, Democrats are making it more likely, not less likely, 
that we will have a successful terrorist attack on our home soil.
  We also know that the TSA agents had nothing to do with the 
differences that separate Democrats and Republicans here. In fact, 
their complaint is with ICE because, again, they are for the open 
borders policy, and they don't want immigration laws enforced. They are 
going to do everything they can to block funding for Immigration and 
Customs Enforcement, but that also includes Homeland Security 
Investigations.
  Then they come out here, and they offer unanimous consent requests, 
saying: Oh, we will pay for the salaries of the Transportation Security 
Administration, but we won't pay to support the rest of the functions 
of the Department of Homeland Security, including support for FEMA, the 
Federal Emergency Management Agency; for the Coast Guard; and for ICE.
  The irony is that we already passed funding for ICE in the One Big 
Beautiful Bill. So their blockade of the funding for the Department of 
Homeland Security is not going to have an immediate impact on ICE, but 
it does demonstrate how out of touch and how extreme the Democrats have 
become in their opposition to commonsense measures that protect public 
safety.
  It is inexcusable to have the Department of Homeland Security--the 
very Agency whose duty it is to protect and defend the homeland--shut 
down amidst all of this chaos. At a time when global tensions are 
rising and the threat landscape is even more perilous, we can't afford 
to play politics with the safety and security of the American people. 
It is time for this to end. It is past time for this to end. This is 
not how the Senate is supposed to work, and this certainly isn't what 
my constituents in Texas or any American deserves.
  My hope is that the negotiations that are ongoing right now will be 
fruitful immediately and that we will reopen the Department of Homeland 
Security not for the good of any political party but for the good of 
the American people.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from New Jersey.
  Mr. KIM. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. PETERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
