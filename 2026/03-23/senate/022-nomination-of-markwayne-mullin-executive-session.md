# Nomination of Markwayne Mullin (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
                     Nomination of Markwayne Mullin

  Mr. President, on another matter, later today, we will be voting on 
the confirmation of Senator Mullin as Secretary of the Department of 
Homeland Security.
  In the Senate, Markwayne has been a conservative warrior with a 
proven track record of supporting the ``America First'' agenda and 
President Trump. He has been dogged in advocating for the good of his 
home State of Oklahoma, and I have every confidence he will carry out 
the same spirit of zeal and unwavering determination in his new role in 
overseeing the Department of Homeland Security. As Democrats continue 
to hold funding for this Agency hostage, we need a strong hand at the 
helm more than ever.
```
