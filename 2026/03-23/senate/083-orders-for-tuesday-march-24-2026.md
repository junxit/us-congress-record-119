# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 24, 2026

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 1383

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 24, 2026

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
on Tuesday, March 24, that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume the House 
message with respect to S. 1383; further, that the Senate recess from 1 
to 2 p.m. to allow for the weekly conference meetings; finally, at 2:15 
p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session and execute the order in 
relation to the McDonald nomination.

                          ____________________
```
