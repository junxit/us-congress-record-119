              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. DUCKWORTH:
       S. 5771. A bill to require the Secretary of Defense to 
     carry out a pilot program for the development and use of an 
     online real estate inventory tool, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Ms. DUCKWORTH:
       S. 5772. A bill to require the Secretary of Defense to 
     establish a program to augment holistic rehabilitative care 
     for beneficiaries with limb loss or limb dysfunction through 
     peer mentoring, education, and community outreach, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Ms. DUCKWORTH (for herself and Mr. Rounds):
       S. 5773. A bill to amend title 10, United States Code, to 
     expedite specialty care referrals for members of the Armed 
     Forces and beneficiaries under the TRICARE program who are 
     undergoing a permanent change of station, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. WYDEN:
       S. 5774. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide a tax credit for investors in start-up businesses, 
     to provide a credit for wages paid by start-up businesses to 
     their first employees, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. BOOKER:
       S. 5775. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     expand eligibility for supportive services for very low-
     income veteran families to include former members of the 
     reserve components of the Armed Forces, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Ms. BALDWIN:
       S. 5776. A bill to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     permit a Federal employee to take parental bereavement leave 
     with respect to the death of a child who is younger than 26 
     years of age, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Mr. BOOKER:
       S. 5777. A bill to strengthen student achievement and 
     graduation rates and prepare children and youth for college, 
     careers, and citizenship through innovative partnerships that 
     meet the comprehensive needs of children and youth; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Ms. DUCKWORTH:
       S. 5778. A bill to direct the Secretary of Defense to 
     establish a pilot program for partnerships between covered 
     basic needs banks and military installations to provide 
     diapers and diapering supplies to military families; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
           By Ms. DUCKWORTH:
       S. 5779. A bill to require the Secretary of Defense to 
     establish a pilot program on blood pressure monitoring for 
     at-risk pregnant and postpartum beneficiaries under the 
     TRICARE program to increase the rate of early detection of 
     hypertensive disorder related to pregnancy and postpartum, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. YOUNG (for himself and Mr. Banks):
       S. 5780. A bill to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 501 West Memorial Drive in 
     Muncie, Indiana, as the ``Officer Blake A. Reynolds Post 
     Office Building''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       S. 5781. A bill to require the Director of the Office of 
     Personnel Management to carry out a research program and 
     demonstration project to study and test modernized methods of 
     classifying positions and compensating employees of the 
     Federal Government, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Ms. ERNST:
       S. 5782. A bill to provide for certain maps relating to the 
     availability of broadband service and funding for broadband 
     infrastructure deployment to be consolidated into a single 
     online mapping tool, to provide for certain improvements with 
     respect to broadband mapping data, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. HEINRICH:
       S. 5783. A bill to require the Administrator of the Drug 
     Enforcement Administration to temporarily exempt 
     buprenorphine from the Suspicious Orders Report System for 
     the remainder of the opioid public health emergency; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. KIM (for himself and Mr. Bennet):
       S. 5784. A bill to require the timely submission of a 
     detailed incident report with respect to every death of any 
     person while in the custody of the Department of Homeland 
     Security; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. WARNER (for himself, Mr. Kennedy, Ms. 
             Alsobrooks, and Mr. Rounds):
       S. 5785. A bill to require the Securities and Exchange 
     Commission to issue rules relating
