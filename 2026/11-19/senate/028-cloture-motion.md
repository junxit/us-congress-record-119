# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

  We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the provisions of 
rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, do hereby move to bring 
to a close debate on the nomination of Executive Calendar No. 968, 
Richard Wesley Bennett, of Texas, to be United States District Judge 
for the Southern District of Texas.
         John Thune, Jim Banks, Tom Cotton, John R. Curtis, James 
           Lankford, Tim Sheehy, John Barrasso, Mike Lee, Mike 
           Rounds, Rick Scott of Florida, Kevin Cramer, Mike 
           Crapo, Bernie Moreno, John Boozman, Cindy Hyde-Smith, 
           Darline Graham, Cynthia M. Lummis.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. By unanimous consent, the mandatory quorum 
call has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of Richard Wesley Bennett, of Texas, to be United States 
District Judge for the Southern District of Texas, shall be brought to 
a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Louisiana (Mr. Cassidy), the Senator from Texas, (Mr. 
Cornyn), the Senator from Utah (Mr. Curtis), the Senator from Kentucky

  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Connecticut (Mr. Murphy)
```
