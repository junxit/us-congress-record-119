# MUHAMMAD ALI AMERICAN BOXING REVIVAL ACT OF 2026

**Measures:** H.R. 4624

```
            MUHAMMAD ALI AMERICAN BOXING REVIVAL ACT OF 2026

  A bill (H.R. 4624) to amend the Professional Boxing Safety Act of 
1996 to establish requirements for unified boxing organizations, to 
further enhance the well-being of professional boxers, and for other 
purposes.
  The amendment (No. 7029) in the nature of a substitute, was agreed 
to.
  (The amendment is printed in today's Record under ``Text of 
Amendments.'')
  The amendment was ordered to be engrossed and the bill to be read a 
third time.
  The bill was read the third time.
  The bill (H.R. 4624), as amended, was passed.
```
