# TRIBUTE TO BRYAN CUTLER

**Speaking:** McCormick, David (M001243) [R-PA]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                        TRIBUTE TO BRYAN CUTLER

● Mr. McCORMICK. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize 
Pennsylvania State Representative Bryan Cutler as he retires from the 
Pennsylvania House of Representatives after nearly two decades of 
dedicated public service.
  First elected in 2006, Representative Cutler represented Lancaster 
County's 100th legislative district with integrity and a deep 
commitment to the communities he calls home. His colleagues entrusted 
him with some of the most demanding roles in the general assembly, 
including house majority leader, the 139th speaker of the Pennsylvania 
House of Representatives, and House Republican leader. In each role, he 
carried significant responsibility, often in difficult and 
consequential moments, and he led with a seriousness of purpose that 
earned respect on both sides of the aisle. Most recently, he served as 
Republican chairman of the house education committee.
  Representative Cutler's commitment to public service is rooted in his 
gratitude for the community that supported his family during difficult 
times. Drawing on his background in healthcare and law, he championed 
bipartisan legislation to expand access to rural trauma care, lower 
health insurance costs, and strengthen government accountability. His 
dedication to finding practical solutions has left a lasting impact on 
communities across Pennsylvania.
  Public service asks a great deal of those who answer the call and of 
the families who support them. I thank his wife Jennifer and their 
children for the sacrifices they have made alongside him.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Representative 
Cutler on his retirement and thanking him for his many years of service 
to Pennsylvania. Dina and I wish him and his family all the best as 
they begin this next chapter.●
```
