            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. KAINE:
       S. Res. 960. A resolution requesting information on the 
     Russian Federation's human rights practices pursuant to 
     section 502B(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Ms. ROSEN (for herself, Ms. Alsobrooks, Mr. 
             Blumenthal, Mr. Durbin, Mr. King, Mr. Lujan, Mr. 
             Padilla, Mr. Schiff, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Welch, Mr. 
             Bennet, and Ms. Duckworth):
       S. Res. 961. A resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Senate that the Federal Emergency Management Agency is an 
     essential Federal institution, that disaster response and 
     long-term recovery are critical statutory obligations of the 
     Federal Government, and that robust Federal coordination is 
     required to support States facing catastrophic natural 
     disasters, including devastating wildfires in the western 
     United States; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.

     27 through December 3, 2026, as ``National Electronic
