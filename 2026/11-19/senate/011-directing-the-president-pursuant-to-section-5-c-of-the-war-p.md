**Speaking:** Sheehy, Tim (S001232) [R-MT], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 93

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 93) directing the 
     President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers 
     Resolution, to remove United States Armed Forces from
```
