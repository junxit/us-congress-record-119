# Text of Senate Amendment 7031

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  SA 7031. Mr. SCHIFF (for himself and Mr. Curtis) proposed an 
amendment to the bill S. 4035, to amend the Commodity Exchange Act to 
prohibit the listing of contracts relating to war, death, and similar 
activities; as follows:

       Strike section 2 and insert the following:

     SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON LISTING OF CONTRACTS RELATING TO WAR, 
                   DEATH, AND OTHER SIMILAR CIRCUMSTANCES.

       Section 5c of the Commodity Exchange Act (7 U.S.C. 7a-2) is 
     amended by adding at the end the following:
       ``(d) Prohibition on Listing of Contracts Relating to War, 
     Death, and Other Similar Circumstances.--A registered entity 
     shall not list for trading or accept for clearing on or 
     through the registered entity any of the following:
       ``(1) An agreement, contract, transaction, or swap based on 
     an excluded commodity described in section 1a(19)(iv) that 
     expressly references--
       ``(A) terrorism;
       ``(B) assassination;
       ``(C) war;
       ``(D) United States Armed Forces military operations; or
       ``(E) any similar activity, as determined by the Commission 
     pursuant to rule or regulation to be necessary and 
     appropriate to achieve the purposes of this Act.
       ``(2) An agreement, contract, transaction, or swap based on 
     an excluded commodity described in section 1a(19)(iv) for 
     which the event for the contract, at the time of listing, is 
     reasonably expected to resolve principally based on a death 
     of an individual.''.

     SEC. 3. RULE OF CONSTRUCTION.

       Nothing in this Act or an amendment made by this Act shall 
     be construed to affect the permissibility of any agreements, 
     contracts, transactions, or swaps involving any activity 
     described in section 5c(c)(5)(C)(i) of the Commodity Exchange 
     Act (7 U.S.C. 7a-2(c)(5)(C)(i)) other than the activities 
     described in subsection (d) of section 5c of that Act (7 
     U.S.C. 7a-2) (as added by section 2).
```
