# Text of Senate Amendment 7034

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH]

```
  SA 7034. Mr. MORENO (for Mr. Peters) proposed an amendment to the 
bill S. 580, to require the Secretary of Commerce to provide training 
and guidance relating to violations of human rights, including such 
abuses perpetrated against Uyghur and other religious and ethnic 
minority groups by the Government of the People's Republic of China, 
and for other purposes; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``A bill to require the 
     Secretary of Commerce to provide training and guidance 
     relating to violations of human rights, including such abuses 
     perpetrated against Uyghur and other religious and ethnic 
     minority groups by the Government of the People's Republic of 
     China, and for other purposes.''.
```
