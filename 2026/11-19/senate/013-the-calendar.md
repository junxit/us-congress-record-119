# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Curtis, John R. (C001114) [R-UT]

**Measures:** S. 1135, S. 1288, S. 4238, S. 4538

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. CURTIS. Madam President, Utahns are blessed to have in our own 
backyard some of the world's best outdoor recreational opportunities. 
This is something we cherish. We love our trails, our mountains, our 
parks, and natural wonders. And with 80 percent of Utah's population 
living along the Wasatch Front, the Bonneville Shoreline Trail is one 
of the most accessible ways to get most Utahns outside enjoying 
activities like hiking and biking.
  The trail follows the ancient shoreline of Lake Bonneville, running 
through Cache, Box Elder, Weber, Davis, Salt Lake, and Utah Counties. 
Utahns love this trail. I love this trail.
  While several segments of the trail are completely well used, others 
remain under development. When fully connected, the route is expected 
to stretch approximately 280 miles from the Utah-Idaho border to Nephi, 
UT.
  In order to complete the trail, it needs to be first formally 
designated as part of the National Trail System. Federal law requires a 
feasibility study conducted by the Secretary of the Interior to do 
that. If the trail qualifies, it becomes eligible for Federal funding 
for construction, planning, and maintenance. It would also open up land 
agreements and priority status for environmental protections and trail 
development.
  I was proud to partner with my fellow Utah Senator Mike Lee on 
introducing legislation to conduct this very needed study. This bill 
takes proactive action to ensure we can preserve public access, protect 
the landscape, and finish what we started.
  Madam President, notwithstanding rule XXII, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate proceed to the immediate consideration of the following 
bills en bloc: Calendar No. 473, S. 1135, Bonneville Shoreline Trail; 
Calendar No. 658, S. 4538, Washington's Trail; Calendar No. 632, S. 
1288, Finger Lakes NHA; and Calendar No. 657, S. 4238, Endless 
Mountains NHA.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bills 
en bloc.
  Mr. CURTIS. I further ask that the committee-reported substitute to 
S. 1135 be agreed to; that the bills, as amended, where amended, be 
considered read a third time and passed; that the committee-reported 
title amendment to S. 1135 be agreed to; the title to S. 1135, as 
amended, be agreed to; and the motions to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate, all en 
bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
