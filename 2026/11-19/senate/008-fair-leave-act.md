# FAIR LEAVE ACT

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S. 3321

```
                             FAIR LEAVE ACT

  Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, my daughter Libby just welcomed her second 
beautiful, healthy baby, a baby girl, who joins her older brother in my 
family, and I am a very, very proud grandmother.
  I wish Libby and her husband Andrew success in raising my 
granddaughter and my grandson. My advice is to cherish and spend all 
the time you can with your children to help them grow into thriving, 
healthy, and successful adults. But parenting is hard--harder still 
when both parents are working parents.
  Under current law, the Family and Medical Leave Act, or FMLA, allows 
each parent to take up to 12 workweeks of unpaid leave a year to care 
for their newborn. These initial weeks are critical for arranging 
healthcare, childcare, reorienting life around a new child, and bonding 
with your new baby. Unfortunately, under current law, when two spouses 
work for the same employer, they are limited to a combined 12 weeks of 
FMLA to care for their child, meaning parents who work for the same 
employer only get half the time with their newborn or their newly 
adopted child.
  This problem impacts couples across the Nation in both the public and 
the private sector. Nearly half of the married women serving in the 
military, including my daughter Libby, are married to someone else in 
the military. So she and her husband and thousands of other new 
military families every year only get 12 weeks of combined FMLA leave 
when they have a child.
  Congress should be encouraging young parents to spend time with their 
newborn and newly adopted children, not penalizing them. This is why I 
introduced S. 3321, the FAIR Leave Act, to get rid of this discrepancy 
and ensure every new parent is entitled to their full FMLA leave 
whether they work for the same employer or not.
  Let's make this right. Let's prove to the American people that 
Congress is pro-family, and let's help parents across the Nation care 
for and love their new children.
  Mr. President, notwithstanding rule XXII, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions be 
discharged from further consideration of S. 3321 and the Senate proceed 
to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3321) to amend the Family and Medical Leave Act 
     of 1993, to repeal certain limits on leave for married 
     individuals employed by the same employer.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Ms. ERNST. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill was ordered to be engrossed for a third reading and was read 
the third time.
  Ms. ERNST. I know of no further debate on this bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. If there is no further debate on the bill, the 
bill having been read the third time, the question is, Shall the bill 
pass?
  The bill (S. 3321) was passed, as follows:

                                S. 3321

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

       This Act may be cited as the ``FAIR Leave Act'' or the 
     ``Fair Access for Individuals to Receive Leave Act''.

     SEC. 2. REPEAL OF CERTAIN TIME LIMITATIONS ON LEAVE FOR 
                   SPOUSES.

       Section 102(f) of the Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993 
     (29 U.S.C. 2612(f)) is repealed.
  Ms. ERNST. I ask unanimous consent that the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Iowa.
  Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, this is a huge step in getting this 
legislation to the President's desk, and I hope the House takes up this 
bill to support new parents across the country. I thank the Presiding 
Officer for the actions today and really look forward to the President 
considering it after passage in the House.
  With that, I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
