# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the source of our strength, we thank You for the 
opportunity You have given our Senators to protect and defend the 
Constitution of the United States. Empower them to be faithful stewards 
of this daunting responsibility. Let their words promote understanding, 
their actions advance justice, and their leadership inspire hope.
  Lord, bless them with discerning minds, steadfast hearts, and the 
courage to lead with integrity. May Your wisdom guide them, Your 
justice shape their decisions, and Your love govern their service.
  Grant that this Nation shall be strengthened by liberty, sustained by 
justice, and united by peace.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
