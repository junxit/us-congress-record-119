# TRIBUTE TO MARK FOX

**Speaking:** Cramer, Kevin (C001096) [R-ND]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO MARK FOX

  Mr. CRAMER. Mr. President, the November 3 election ended the tenure 
of Mark Fox as Chairman of the Mandan Hidatsa Arikara Nation as he 
stepped down after completing his third term in office.
  A Parshall, ND, native who was raised in Minot, Mark has 
distinguished himself as a Tribal, State, and national leader. He 
returned to the MHA Nation in 1994 following service in the U.S. Marine 
Corps and having earned a law degree at the University of North Dakota. 
Following various elected and administrative Tribal positions, he was 
elected Chairman in 2014.
  I knew Mark before I was elected to Congress in 2012 and he became 
MHA Chairman 2 years later. In fact, when I served as North Dakota's 
tourism director in the 1990s, the leaders of the Knife River Clan of 
Mandan Indians adopted me into their clan. This is an honor I will 
treasure the rest of my life.
  Being a Tribal leader today presents unique challenges that involve 
balancing the interests of each individual Tribal community, along with 
issues concerning the State in which the Nation is located. Add to the 
mix the myriad of Federal Agencies with programs impacting countless 
Tribal concerns. In these areas and more, I have worked with Chairman 
Fox as he has effectively navigated all of this with great skill and 
diplomacy.
  The total reservation land area of the MHA Nation is more than 1 
million acres, and it has nearly 18,000 enrolled members. With its rich 
history, beautiful scenery, and abundant natural resources, agriculture 
has been an economic mainstay. However, with its location in the center 
of the Bakken Oil Formation, nearly 3 percent of the crude oil in the 
United States is today produced on MHA land.
  In his years as chairman, Mark has championed Tribal sovereignty, 
Tribal economic development, education, health services, taxation 
energy development, and gaming. Under his leadership in these past 12 
years, the MHA Nation has launched many successful initiatives, 
financed in part by oil revenue. They have encompassed improved 
schools, expanded addiction services, and many infrastructure upgrades. 
One of the most innovative projects is the new Native Green Grow 
Greenhouse that is powered by captured and treated flared natural gas 
from nearby oil wells. It has more than 400,000 square feet of growing 
space and capacity to grow more than 2 million pounds of fresh produce 
every year.
  Another commendable emphasis has been on Tribal tourism with 
development of the new Three Affiliated Tribes National Park, a new 
fairgrounds, earth lodges, rodeos, casinos, and a world-class history 
and interpretive center.
  On the Federal level, Mark has lent his voice of reason and 
experience as a member of advisory boards for the Departments of 
Energy, Interior, and Environmental Protection Agency. He has also been 
an adviser to the Internal Revenue Service and the National Indian 
Gaming Commission, chaired the Intertribal Monitoring Association on 
Trust Funds and was treasurer for four terms for the National Gaming 
Association.
  I have had the honor of introducing him as a witness at several 
congressional hearings, and he has been a valued adviser to me on 
legislation encompassing many issues. I have always been able to count 
on him for wise counsel, a pragmatic perspective, and effective 
solutions.
  In his years of service to the MHA Nation, Mark has honored his 
ancestors, his family, his neighbors, and the citizens of North Dakota. 
He embodies all that is good about North Dakota and the rich traditions 
of the Tribal Nations in our State. His extraordinary work and 
influence will resonate for generations to come.
  On behalf of the people of North Dakota, I thank him for his years of 
leadership. And I wish him the very best in his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
