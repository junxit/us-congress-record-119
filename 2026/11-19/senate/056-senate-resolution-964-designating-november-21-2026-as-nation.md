# SENATE RESOLUTION 964--DESIGNATING NOVEMBER 21, 2026, AS NATIONAL ADOPTION DAY AND NOVEMBER 2026 AS NATIONAL ADOPTION MONTH TO PROMOTE NATIONAL AWARENESS OF ADOPTION AND THE CHILDREN AWAITING...

**Measures:** S.Res. 964

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 964--DESIGNATING NOVEMBER 21, 2026, AS NATIONAL 
 ADOPTION DAY AND NOVEMBER 2026 AS NATIONAL ADOPTION MONTH TO PROMOTE 
  NATIONAL AWARENESS OF ADOPTION AND THE CHILDREN AWAITING FAMILIES, 
CELEBRATING CHILDREN AND FAMILIES INVOLVED IN ADOPTION, AND ENCOURAGING 
THE PEOPLE OF THE UNITED STATES TO SECURE SAFETY, PERMANENCY, AND WELL-
                         BEING FOR ALL CHILDREN

  Mr. CRAMER (for himself, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Barrasso, Mrs. Blackburn, 
Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Boozman, Mrs. Britt, Mr. Budd, Mrs. Capito, Mr. 
Cassidy, Mr. Crapo, Ms. Ernst, Mr. Grassley, Mr. Hagerty, Ms. Hassan, 
Ms. Hirono, Mr. Hoeven, Mr. Husted, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. King, Mr. 
Lankford, Mr. Lujan, Mr. Risch, Ms. Rosen, Mr. Schiff, Mr. Whitehouse, 
Mr. Wicker, Mr. Young, Mr. Daines, Mr. Scott of Florida, and Mr. Van 
Hollen) submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 964

       Whereas there are millions of unparented children in the 
     world, including 331,747 children in the foster care system 
     in the United States, approximately 67,249 of whom have a 
     permanency plan of adoption as of September 30, 2025, 
     according to the Children's Bureau of the Administration for 
     Children and Families of the Department of Health and Human 
     Services;
       Whereas many waiting children wait nearly an average of 3 
     years in foster care before adoption;
       Whereas, for many unparented children, the wait for a 
     loving family, in which the children are nurtured, comforted, 
     and protected, seems endless;
       Whereas, in 2025, over 15,000 children were at risk of 
     aging out of foster care by reaching adulthood without being 
     placed in a permanent home;
       Whereas, every day, loving and nurturing families are 
     strengthened and expanded when committed and dedicated 
     individuals make an important difference in the life of a 
     child through adoption;
       Whereas, while 1 in 4 adults in the United States have 
     considered adoption, a majority of individuals in the United 
     States have misperceptions about the process of adopting 
     children from foster care and the children who are eligible 
     for adoption;
       Whereas family reunification, kinship care, and domestic 
     and intercountry adoption promote greater permanency and 
     stability for children;
       Whereas the Children's Bureau, an office of the 
     Administration for Children and Families within the 
     Department of Health and Human Services, supports programs, 
     research, and monitoring to help eliminate barriers to 
     adoption and find permanent families for children;
       Whereas National Adoption Day is a collective national 
     effort to find permanent, loving families for children in the 
     foster care system;
       Whereas, since the first National Adoption Day in 2000, 
     more than 95,000 children have joined permanent families on 
     National Adoption Day;
       Whereas the President traditionally issues an annual 
     proclamation to declare the month of November as National 
     Adoption Month, and the President has proclaimed November 
     2026 as National Adoption Month; and
       Whereas the Saturday before Thanksgiving has been 
     recognized as National Adoption Day since at least 2000, and 
     in 2026, the Saturday before Thanksgiving is November 21: 
     Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) designates November 21, 2026, as National Adoption Day 
     and November 2026 as National Adoption Month;
       (2) recognizes that every child should have a permanent and 
     loving family; and
       (3) encourages the people of the United States to consider 
     adoption during the month of November and throughout the 
     year.
```
