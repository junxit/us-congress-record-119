# BIPARTISAN AMERICAN AFFORDABILITY AND JOBS ACT OF 2026

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK], Lee, Mike (L000577) [R-UT]

```
         BIPARTISAN AMERICAN AFFORDABILITY AND JOBS ACT OF 2026

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President section 2105 of the permitting bill 
provides: ``Nothwithstanding any rule or order of the Federal Energy 
Regulation Commission in effect on the date of enactment of this 
section, a Federal right of first refusal is prohibited with respect to 
a transmission facility selected in a regional or interregional 
transmission plan for the purposes of cost allocation.''
  By its express terms, section 2105 applies to Federal rights of first 
refusal, particularly those created or implemented by any rule or order 
of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission. It is my understanding 
that section 2105 does not apply to rights of first refusal established 
under State law, and that section 2105 does not preempt, supersede, 
limit, or otherwise affects any law of a State or political subdivision 
of a State relating to the siting, permitting, ownership, or right to 
construct transmission facilities.
  I ask Senator Lee: Is that a correct understanding of the legislative 
intent underlying section 2105 of the bill?
  Mr. LEE. Yes, that is correct. Section 2105 applies only to Federal 
rights of first refusal. Section 2105 is not intended to apply to 
rights of first refusal established under State law or to preempt, 
supersede, limit, or otherwise affect any law of a State or political 
subdivision of a State relating to the siting, permitting, ownership, 
or right to construct transmission facilities.
```
