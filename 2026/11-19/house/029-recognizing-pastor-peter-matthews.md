# RECOGNIZING PASTOR PETER MATTHEWS

**Speaking:** Landsman, Greg (L000601) [D-OH]

```
                              {time}  1010
                   RECOGNIZING PASTOR PETER MATTHEWS

  (Mr. LANDSMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a good friend 
and a champion for southwest Ohio, Pastor Peter Matthews.
  Last month, Pastor Matthews walked 46 miles across our community to 
bring awareness to the needs of our children, particularly our homeless 
youth and children in foster care.
  I met with Pastor Matthews at one of the stops in Cincinnati, at 
Inner City Youth Opportunities, a nonprofit organization that helps 
children academically with their homework, tutoring, and mentorship. It 
was 1 of 25 organizations that Pastor Matthews visited along his walk.
  At each stop, he asked community leaders a very important question: 
How are our children, and what do you see in our children that the rest 
of the city does not?
  I thank Pastor Matthews for his lifetime of service. His work 
challenges all of us to do way more, particularly on behalf of our 
children.

                          ____________________
```
