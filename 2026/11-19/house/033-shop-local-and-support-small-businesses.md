# SHOP LOCAL AND SUPPORT SMALL BUSINESSES

**Speaking:** Amo, Gabe (A000380) [D-RI]

```
                SHOP LOCAL AND SUPPORT SMALL BUSINESSES

  (Mr. AMO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. AMO. Mr. Speaker, the people have spoken. They want an economy 
that works for all Americans, but Trump's tariffs, illegal war with 
Iran, ICE cruelty, and corruption are driving up costs and putting 
pressure on household budgets and small businesses as we head into the 
holiday season.
  The Saturday after Thanksgiving is Small Business Saturday, when 
shoppers head to Main Street to pick up gifts. Mom-and-pop shops count 
on this weekend to end the year strong.
  Rhode Islanders want to shop locally but are being forced to make 
hard choices between supporting small businesses and making ends meet.
  When families are paying more for gas and groceries because of Donald 
Trump, there is less left over for holiday gifts. Americans are sick of 
the high costs, chaos, and corruption making life more expensive.
  It is past time to end Trump's tariffs and illegal war with Iran. It 
is past time to bring down gas and grocery prices.
  If you can, shop local and support a small business, and I will keep 
fighting in Congress to make life more affordable.

                          ____________________
```
