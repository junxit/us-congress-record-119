# LEADERSHIP NEEDED TO FIGHT MEASLES

**Speaking:** Dean, Madeleine (D000631) [D-PA]

```
                   LEADERSHIP NEEDED TO FIGHT MEASLES

  (Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, for 26 years, measles was a 
vestige of the past. Measles is a highly contagious viral illness, and 
complications threaten the health of children, pregnant women, and 
those who are immunocompromised.
  Now, in a shameful historic first, the United States lost its measles 
elimination status. Decades of progress were unraveled by Secretary 
Robert Kennedy.
  Pennsylvania has the highest infection count, with more than 1,500 
cases, hundreds hospitalized, and 7 deaths. These were all avoidable.
  While our Governor and county health departments work to contain this 
outbreak, Secretary Kennedy dismisses reports as fabricated. I have 
spoken with the Secretary. He admitted that the MMR vaccine is safe and 
effective. Yet he sows doubt about vaccines, and our families and 
children suffer.
  Mr. Speaker, we know how to eliminate measles. We know the vaccines 
are safe and effective. What we need is leadership that follows science 
and does not undermine it. Let's eliminate ignorance. Let's eliminate 
measles once again in our country.

                          ____________________
```
