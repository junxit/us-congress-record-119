# RECOGNIZING KELLY STONE

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING KELLY STONE

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Kelly Stone 
from Baxley, Georgia, who has recently taken over as chairman of the 
Community Bankers Association of Georgia.
  Kelly currently serves as the president and CEO of Community Bank of 
Georgia in Baxley and previously served as the chairman-elect of the 
association. He has more than 25 years of experience in banking and has 
demonstrated a strong commitment to serving his community.
  Kelly's leadership extends well beyond the banking industry. He has 
served as president of the Appling County High School football 
Touchdown Club and serves on the finance committee at his church. 
Through these roles and many others, Kelly has made a lasting impact on 
his local community.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Kelly on this well-deserved leadership 
position and thank him for his continued service to the people of 
Baxley and communities across Georgia.

                          ____________________
```
