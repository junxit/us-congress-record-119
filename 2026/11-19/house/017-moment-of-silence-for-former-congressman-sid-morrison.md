# MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR FORMER CONGRESSMAN SID MORRISON

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
         MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR FORMER CONGRESSMAN SID MORRISON

  (Mr. NEWHOUSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, today, we honor the life and legacy of 
Congressman Sid Morrison, a true titan of central Washington politics.
  Raised on a fruit farm in Zillah, Sid brought the values of hard work 
and community to a public service career that spanned over six decades.
  After serving in the United States Army, he represented Washington in 
both the State legislature and here in the U.S. House of 
Representatives where he championed agriculture, energy, and the 
cleanup of the Hanford Nuclear Reservation.
  He later served as Washington's Secretary of Transportation, 
overseeing major infrastructure projects, including the completion of 
Interstate 90.
  Sid was universally remembered as a consensus builder who mastered 
the art of working across party lines to get things done.
  His dedication to civility and results-oriented government set a gold 
standard for public service, and his influence will be felt in our 
State for generations to come.
  I ask my colleagues to join me now in a moment of silence.

                          ____________________
```
