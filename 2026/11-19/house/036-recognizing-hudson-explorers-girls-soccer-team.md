# RECOGNIZING HUDSON EXPLORERS GIRLS' SOCCER TEAM

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
            RECOGNIZING HUDSON EXPLORERS GIRLS' SOCCER TEAM

  (Mrs. SYKES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Hudson 
Explorers girls' soccer team as the Champion of the Week for Ohio's 
13th Congressional District.
  After 26 years, the Hudson Explorers are State champions once again. 
On November 14, Hudson High School defeated Columbus Bishop Watterson 
with a score of 2-0 in the Division II State championship game, 
bringing home the program's first State title since the year 2000 and 
just the second in the program's history.
  The Explorers' championship was the culmination of an incredible 
season, finishing with an impressive 23-0 record. After a scoreless 
first half, freshman Elia Gresh broke through with the game's first 
goal, followed by Hannah Meese, who secured the victory with the second 
goal later in the game.
  This championship is about much more than what happened on the pitch. 
It reflects the dedication, discipline, teamwork, and perseverance of a 
group of athletes who continued to push themselves, learn from one 
another, and rise to the moment.
  After falling short in last year's State championship, these 
Explorers came back determined to finish what they started. Whether it 
is on the pitch or in the classroom, these student-athletes make us so 
very proud. Today's girl student-athletes are tomorrow's entrepreneurs, 
educators, scientists, and, yes, Congresswomen.
  I congratulate the Hudson Explorers girls' soccer team, and I thank 
the Hudson community, coaches, and families for supporting girls and 
women in sports.
```
