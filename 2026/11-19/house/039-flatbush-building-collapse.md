# FLATBUSH BUILDING COLLAPSE

**Speaking:** Clarke, Yvette D. (C001067) [D-NY]

```
                       FLATBUSH BUILDING COLLAPSE

  (Ms. CLARKE of New York asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. CLARKE of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart 
following the tragic building collapse last week in my Congressional 
District, the Ninth District in Brooklyn, New York.
  Eight cherished members of our community lost their lives, including 
two precious children. Today, I want to ensure their names are spoken 
and their lives are remembered: Nikol Daniela Salvada Rodriguez, age 9; 
Kevin Santiago Reyes Gonzalez, age 10; Ndiaye Mamadou; Barry Mamadou; 
Gabriel Siguencia; Edgar Sapon; Juan Daniel Salvada Guzman; and Jose 
Luis Caguana Cosquillo.
  They were our neighbors, our loved ones, and members of the Brooklyn 
family. Their lives mattered, and their absence leaves an immeasurable 
void.
  I commend the FDNY, the NYPD, EMS, all first responders, and 
volunteers who worked tirelessly in the rescue and recovery efforts.
  My prayers remain with their families, those injured, those still 
hospitalized, and everyone impacted. My office will continue working 
with local officials and agencies to ensure displaced residents receive 
the support they need.
  Brooklyn is a community built on strength, resilience, and unity. 
Though we grieve today and together, we stand together.
  May these eight souls rest in eternal peace and may their memories 
live forever in Brooklyn's heart.

                          ____________________
```
