# HONORING SONNY LEWIS

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
                          HONORING SONNY LEWIS

  (Ms. KAPTUR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and remember the life and 
legacy of a man who had a major impact

[[Page H6300]]

on the youth of Northwest Ohio through the sport of volleyball--Sonny 
Lewis.
  A Northwest Ohio volleyball legend, Sonny dedicated his life to 
giving back to the very community that raised him.
  He began his career at Springfield High School, where he served and 
developed students as players and community members for over 20 years.
  While coaching high school volleyball, he also cofounded the Toledo 
Volleyball Club.
  At Owens Community College, he led the women's volleyball team for 24 
seasons, bringing home 800 wins, 19 conference titles, and three 
national championships.
  Sonny Lewis was a winner, but more importantly, he taught his players 
how to win in life, both on and off the volleyball court.
  He was a man who poured his time and energy into making life better 
for those he led, giving them a safe place to land.
  Sonny did not just leave a mark on northwestern Ohio's volleyball 
community--he breathed life into it. His legacy will live on forever in 
every player and generation that steps onto the court in Northwest 
Ohio. Sonny Lewis gave players skills, joy, and lifelong memories of 
teamwork and friendship that binds our communities and Nation together.
  May God rest Sonny's great life and may some of his former students 
and players follow in his stead.

                          ____________________
```
