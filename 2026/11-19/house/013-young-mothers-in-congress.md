# YOUNG MOTHERS IN CONGRESS

**Speaking:** McIver, LaMonica (M001229) [D-NJ]

```
                       YOUNG MOTHERS IN CONGRESS

  (Mrs. McIVER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, ask yourself: Do I know three mothers?
  If the answer is yes, you know more mothers than there are Black 
Congresswomen who have ever given birth while serving in Congress.
  Mr. Speaker, 53 years ago, the first woman and first Black woman gave 
birth while serving in Congress. This place makes it clear that it is 
not made for people like us. It has been 53 years, and we still can't 
vote by proxy after giving birth.
  The Life Time gym in Arlington has more accessible childcare than 
Congress and a shorter wait list.
  It has been a joy of my life to be a mother and to give birth to two 
healthy baby girls and a joy of my life to serve New Jersey in 
Congress.
  We need to make it easier for more people, more young moms, to 
experience both those joys.
  We have important perspectives. Our voices belong here, and we are 
going to use them.

                          ____________________
```
