# HONORING THE LIFE OF MICHAEL ROBERT BAYHAM

**Speaking:** Scalise, Steve (S001176) [R-LA]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE OF MICHAEL ROBERT BAYHAM

  (Mr. SCALISE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. SCALISE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Michael 
Robert Bayham, a strong Roman Catholic, a proud resident of St. Bernard 
Parish, an effective State legislator, a loving son and friend.
  With many prayers from his family and friends, Michael Bayham entered 
the Heavenly kingdom on November 12 of this year.
  Born in New Orleans on July 30, 1974, he was the son of Kathleen 
Gomez and stepfather Daniel Boteler, and grandson of the late Mickey 
and Evette Bayham and the late Albert and Shirley Gomez.
  Among his many relatives, he was especially close to his sister 
Danielle Boteler and cousin Richie Baumy. He was also the older brother 
of Scotty Bayham, who predeceased him in 2002.
  Michael Bayham was a graduate of Holy Cross High School and Louisiana 
State University. A passionate Saints, LSU, and Astros fan, he had seen 
his professional football and baseball teams compete on the road 
against every opponent.
  His love for travel brought him to museums and battlefields around 
the world, visiting all 50 States and 63 countries across 6 continents.
  A devoted Catholic, Bayham rarely missed a Sunday mass. In fact, he 
attended mass in all 50 States and journeyed on religious pilgrimages 
to Mexico City, Canterbury, Fatima, Paris, Rome, Jerusalem, and 
Bethlehem.
  One of the greatest privileges of his life was meeting Pope Leo XIV 
and presenting him with gifts from St. Bernard Parish. Bayham was not 
just a lifelong Republican but a Reagan Republican. Meeting the Gipper 
in 1997 endured as the greatest political moment of his life.
  Bayham served as a volunteer campaign consultant and campaign manager 
for countless candidates, including my races. In 1996, Bayham won his 
first election at the ripe age of 22 to the Louisiana Republican State 
Central Committee. Five years later, he was elected to the St. Bernard 
Parish Council at the age of 26--an election I was proud to knock on 
doors with him for--and was elected to the Louisiana Legislature as a 
member of the house of representatives in 2023, earning 62 percent of 
the vote.
  Throughout his time as a public servant, Bayham remained a fierce 
advocate for the interests of St. Bernard Parish. Bayham was involved 
in every race possible. School board, council, PEC, animal control 
officer--you name it. I would often call him about races and candidates 
in Louisiana. It didn't matter where in the State; he always knew what 
was happening.
  Michael was a close friend to me, to many of my staff and to so many 
people involved in Louisiana politics. We both shared a love for public 
service and entered politics around the same time. He was always there 
for me and all of his friends through good times and in bad.
  As a final request, Michael asked that we perform random acts of 
kindness to remind people in society that there is still love in the 
world. Let's continue to remember those important words.
  Mr. Speaker, Louisiana was lucky to have known a true patriot and a 
great friend in Michael Bayham. He will be missed.
```
