# CELEBRATING NATIONAL BREAD MONTH

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                              {time}  0910
                    CELEBRATING NATIONAL BREAD MONTH

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, good bread, good meat, O 
Lord, let's eat.
  It is National Bread Month.
  A special thank you goes to the farmers of eastern North Carolina and 
across our Nation who grow the grain for our bread, the bakers who make 
it, and the truck drivers who deliver it to our local grocery stores.
  The baking industry employs nearly 800,000 workers nationwide and 
generates $186 billion in annual revenue.
  Our farm families, bakers, and truck drivers work hard to bring food 
to our tables, and we are extremely grateful.
  Baking feeds America.

                          ____________________
```
