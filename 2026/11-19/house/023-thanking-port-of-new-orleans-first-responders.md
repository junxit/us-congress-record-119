# THANKING PORT OF NEW ORLEANS FIRST RESPONDERS

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
                              {time}  1000
             THANKING PORT OF NEW ORLEANS FIRST RESPONDERS

  (Mr. CARTER of Louisiana asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, last Friday, we witnessed a 
terrifying scene on the Mississippi River.
  Mr. Speaker, 195 passengers and crew were on the American Heritage 
riverboat for an 8-day-long cruise, preparing to disembark and return 
to their friends and families.
  Moments later, an asphalt tanker veered off course and crashed into 
the docked cruise boat, splintering the ship, spinning it around, and 
causing nearly 100 passengers to fall into the water.
  That is when first responders, including Harbor Chief of Police 
Melanie Montroll, Sergeant Logan Tillery, and Officer Devon Allen, 
leapt into action. They didn't hesitate.
  They didn't wait until others went in first. They acted immediately, 
calmly, and efficiently. They carried out the search and rescue in the 
waters themselves, assessing the damage and ensuring all 195 people 
were rescued and accounted for without any serious injuries.
  Many people are calling it a Miracle on the Mississippi, but it 
wasn't luck or chance that saved those lives. It was the heroic actions 
of those first responders working to save lives under immense pressure.
  Mr. Speaker, I will take this moment to publicly thank Harbor Chief 
of Police Melanie Montroll and every single first responder for their 
heroism and quick thinking in the face of a disaster.
  First responders, Coast Guard, and Good Samaritans all stepped up to 
the plate to do what was instinctively right--not because they were 
seeking any joy, not because they recognized any valor, but because 
they knew it was the right thing to do. They stepped into action, and 
thank God they did.
  Mr. Speaker, because of these first responders, the Coast Guard, and 
the medical personnel at the University Medical Center, lives were 
saved. What could have been a tragedy was turned into a miracle. I know 
that I speak for many when I say that we are eternally grateful. God 
bless them.

                          ____________________
```
