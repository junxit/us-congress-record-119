# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SHARON ANN WEIDELMAN

**Speaking:** Burlison, Eric (B001316) [R-MO]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SHARON ANN WEIDELMAN

  (Mr. BURLISON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BURLISON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of my good 
friend, Sharon Weidelman of Springfield, Missouri.
  Sharon was one of the kindest people I have ever known. She was a 
stranger to no one, a pillar of strength to many, and someone whose 
word you could always rely on.
  As a devout Catholic, Sharon dedicated her life to serving others. At 
Immaculate Conception Catholic Church, she led family life ministries 
and religious education. She believed that every unborn child deserved 
a chance at life and every mother facing a difficult pregnancy deserved 
someone in their corner.
  A true servant of God and a light to the world, she spent years 
giving selflessly to others and supporting young women in need.
  Sharon was also a dedicated volunteer. She was involved in 
conservative movements across southwest Missouri, always willing to put 
in the work, even when no one was there to look.
  Above all, Sharon loved her family. She and her late husband, Gordon, 
shared 40 years of marriage and built a loving life together.

[[Page H6299]]

  She is survived by her brother, Robert; her two sons, Christian and 
Timothy; and her beloved grandchildren.
  Sharon lived a life defined by faith, generosity, and devotion to 
others. My prayers are with her family and all those who loved her.
  May she rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
