# RECOGNIZING RICK DUNN

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
                         RECOGNIZING RICK DUNN

  (Mr. NEWHOUSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Rick Dunn, the 
general manager of Benton Public Utility District, who is retiring in 
April after 40 years in the electric utilities industry.
  A native of central Washington and a fellow Washington State Cougar, 
Rick has been at Benton PUD since 2007. He previously held roles with 
the Umatilla Electric Cooperative and the Salt River Project in 
Arizona.
  Over his long career, Rick has been actively engaged in regional 
energy policy, working to balance the needs of adequate resources and 
environmental stewardship with affordable and reliable electricity for 
the communities that he has served.
  Rick has been a thought partner on the energy challenges facing our 
region and the solutions required to meet the moment of increased power 
demand.
  I thank Rick for his continuous advocacy and leadership over the 
years. He has been vital to not only central Washington but the 
Northwest Pacific as a whole and is leaving behind big shoes to fill.
  Mr. Speaker, I am sad to see Rick leave. His retirement is well 
deserved. I wish him the best in his next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
