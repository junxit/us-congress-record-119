# CELEBRATING BETTIE MAE BUIE'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Harris, Mark (H001102) [R-NC]

```
              CELEBRATING BETTIE MAE BUIE'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. HARRIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HARRIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, on November 28, my 
constituent, Bettie Mae Buie of Red Springs, North Carolina, will turn 
100 years old. Today, we celebrate not only this incredible milestone 
but her lifetime of service.
  For nearly a century, Mrs. Buie has dedicated herself to her 
community, from becoming the first Black female head cook at Womack 
Army Hospital to working at the Quartermaster Laundry at Fort Bragg.
  She has also faithfully shared the love of the Lord throughout North 
Carolina as a long-time member of the Willing Workers and as pastor of 
Garden of Yosemite Church in Laurinburg, North Carolina, where she 
became known as Evangelist Bettie Mae Buie.
  Her commitment to helping others continued all the way up to age 98 
as she provided transportation to those who could not drive themselves 
to church functions, ministry activities, and even doctors' 
appointments.
  Bettie Mae Buie's servant's heart reminds us all of what it means to 
be a good neighbor, and her legacy will be remembered for years to 
come.
  Mr. Speaker, we are proud to celebrate Mrs. Buie's 100th birthday and 
thank her for her incredible contributions to North Carolina's Eighth 
District. May God bless her and her family, and the United States of 
America.

                          ____________________
```
