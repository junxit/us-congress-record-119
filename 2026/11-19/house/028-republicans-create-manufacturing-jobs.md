# REPUBLICANS CREATE MANUFACTURING JOBS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                 REPUBLICANS CREATE MANUFACTURING JOBS

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, under the progrowth agenda 
of President Donald Trump and House Republicans, led by Speaker   Mike 
Johnson, American manufacturing is gaining momentum, with production 
growing at the fastest rate in 5 years.
  Factories are expanding. Jobs are being created, and paychecks are 
rising as companies and nations are making significant investments to 
build their futures in America.
  Achievements include: 165,000 jobs added in October, 19,000 being 
manufacturing jobs; manufacturing employment increased for the first 
time in 3 years; business investment increased nearly 13 percent in the 
third quarter, the fastest growth in 15 years; and weekly earnings for 
manufacturing workers increased 5 percent over the past year, with wage 
growth reaching its highest level of the year.
  In South Carolina, Westinghouse announced a $1 billion investment to 
build a next-generation nuclear fuel factory, securing American energy 
independence and creating jobs as Governor-elect Alan Wilson and 
Lieutenant Governor-elect Mike Reichenbach take office on January 13.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops. Trump is reinstituting peace 
through strength, revealing war criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump 
and mocking Trump, supporting the murderous regime in Tehran, which 
plots to kill Trump and annihilate Washington with a dirty bomb.

                          ____________________
```
