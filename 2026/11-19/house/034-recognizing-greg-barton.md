# RECOGNIZING GREG BARTON

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING GREG BARTON

  (Mr. SMUCKER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a familiar voice 
to generations of listeners across Lancaster County and south central 
Pennsylvania, Greg Barton of WDAC Radio.
  After 44 years with WDAC, including approximately 35 years as news 
director, Greg is preparing to retire. Since joining the station in 
December 1982, Greg has worn many hats, from reporting the news and 
sports to hosting programs like ``Spotlight,'' ``Legislative Report,'' 
and ``On Capitol Hill.'' According to his official biography, he has 
even been known to fix a technical problem or two along the way.
  Throughout his career, Greg has viewed his work as a calling from the 
Lord. His faith has guided his commitment to accurate, thoughtful 
reporting and to keeping his listeners informed.
  Mr. Speaker, on a personal note, I have had the pleasure of joining 
Greg nearly every Friday for recordings of his ``On Capitol Hill'' 
program. After what must have been close to 500 interviews over the 
years, I can tell you firsthand that Greg has always been thoughtful, 
prepared, and committed to keeping his listeners informed about what is 
happening here in Washington and how it impacts our community.

  I wish Greg and his wife, Louise, and their entire family many 
blessings in the years ahead.
  At the conclusion of each of our interviews, I normally sign off with 
the same three words: ``Thank you, Greg.'' While we may still have a 
few more interviews before his retirement, I wanted to take this 
opportunity to say those words from the floor of the U.S. House of 
Representatives.
  Thank you, Greg.
  Congratulations on an extraordinary career, and best wishes for a 
well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
