# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Mark Cowart, Church For All Nations, Colorado Springs, 
Colorado, offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, our Heavenly Father, we thank You for the blessings You 
have bestowed upon this Nation. As one privileged pastor in this great 
country, I am grateful for the freedoms we enjoy and for the Founders 
who sought Your guidance in establishing this Republic.
  Your Word tells us to pray for all who are in authority, so today we 
lift up this Congress. Grant these men and women wisdom to discern what 
is right, humility to seek Your counsel, and courage to act with 
integrity.
  Benjamin Franklin reminded our Founders that ``God governs in the 
affairs of men'' and asked whether a nation could ``rise without His 
aid.'' Lord, we acknowledge that truth today, and we appeal to Heaven 
for Your assistance and ask that Your providential hand guide these 
deliberations.
  Protect our Nation, preserve the liberties endowed by our creator, 
and help us fulfill the purpose for which You have allowed this 
Republic to stand.
  May all that is done here honor You and serve the good of this 
Nation.
  In the name of Jesus, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
