# HONORING CHARLIE HALL

**Speaking:** Magaziner, Seth (M001223) [D-RI]

```
                         HONORING CHARLIE HALL

  (Mr. MAGAZINER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MAGAZINER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Charlie Hall, a Rhode Island comedian, cartoonist, and songwriter 
who spent decades making the Ocean State laugh.
  Charlie got his start in stand-up, appearing on national television 
and opening for legends like Jerry Seinfeld and Jon Stewart, but his 
heart was always at home.
  He and his lifelong friend Frank O'Donnell opened Periwinkles, Rhode 
Island's first comedy club. With the ``Ocean State Follies,'' he poked 
fun at our quirks, our scandals, and yes, our politicians. Few of those 
in public life escaped his pen, and Rhode Island is better for it.
  He was the first inductee to Rhode Island's Comedy Hall of Fame, and 
he also gave us ``Rhode Island's It For Me,'' our official State song.
  Our hearts go out to his family, his friends, and his longtime 
collaborators.
  As his friends have said, the world is a little less funny without 
him, but much more funny because he was with us.
  May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
