# HONORING GLORIA STEINEM

**Speaking:** Pelosi, Nancy (P000197) [D-CA]

```
                        HONORING GLORIA STEINEM

  (Ms. PELOSI asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. PELOSI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Gloria Steinem, a 
champion for equality and a dear friend to so many of us here.
  She championed the equal rights amendment and helped found the 
National Women's Political Caucus to get more women elected. It is a 
joy to me to say that when I came to Congress, there were 12 Democratic 
women and now there are nearly 120, 10 times as many.
  So many people are responsible for that, and I take some credit 
myself, but Gloria Steinem gave women confidence to run, to lead, to do 
the job.
  Her memorial service was a celebration of life. Where else would you 
see a service where three First Ladies--Michelle Obama, Jill Biden, 
and, of course, Hillary Clinton, who was a candidate for President 
herself--all on the stage dancing to the music?
  When we would have our circle of women at our home--this was a 
tradition of Gloria Steinem--so many people would come. It would 
overflow into the streets not once, not twice, but many times. She was 
a star.
  Mr. Speaker, she championed the equal rights amendment. I hope we 
will make it the business of this Caucus in the new term to very early 
on pass the equal rights amendment, a tribute to all women, but led by 
Gloria Steinem.

                          ____________________
```
