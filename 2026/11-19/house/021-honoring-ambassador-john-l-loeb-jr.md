# HONORING AMBASSADOR JOHN L. LOEB, JR.

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                 HONORING AMBASSADOR JOHN L. LOEB, JR.

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ambassador John L.

[[Page H6297]]

Loeb, Jr., a long-time resident of Purchase, New York, who passed away 
on August 5 at the age of 96.
  An Air Force veteran, he served our country as Ambassador to Denmark 
and as a delegate to the United Nations. His enduring passion was 
religious freedom.
  Through the Loeb Visitors Center at Touro Synagogue and the Loeb 
Institute for Religious Freedom and Democracy at George Washington 
University, he championed religious liberty and fought anti-Semitism.
  A generous philanthropist and a patron of the arts, he encouraged 
people to move beyond tolerance toward warmth and understanding.
  Mr. Speaker, I extend my deepest condolences to his wife, Sharon; his 
family; and all who loved him. May his example of service, kindness, 
and conviction continue to inspire us.

                          ____________________
```
