# SALUTING RETIRED NAVY SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER KENTON STACY

**Speaking:** Davidson, Warren (D000626) [R-OH]

```
     SALUTING RETIRED NAVY SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER KENTON STACY

  (Mr. DAVIDSON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor retired Navy Senior Chief 
Petty Officer Kenton Stacy of Greenville, Ohio.
  Kenton served our Nation in the Navy as an explosive ordnance 
disposal technician, disarming explosives to protect the servicemembers 
around him. He loved that work and the sailors he served with, and he 
excelled at it. In 2010, he was named the USO Sailor of the Year.
  Nine years ago today, Kenton was severely wounded by an improvised 
explosive device in Syria. He has met every day since with the same 
courage he showed in uniform, through extensive rehabilitation.
  Recognizing his heroism, our country recognized his service with the 
Purple Heart, the Bronze Star, and three Navy Achievement Medals. 
Today, Greenville is working to dedicate a street in Kenton's name.

[[Page H6298]]

  Mr. Speaker, Senior Chief Stacy's hometown is proud of him. Ohio's 
Eighth District is grateful for his service. Charlie Mike.
```
