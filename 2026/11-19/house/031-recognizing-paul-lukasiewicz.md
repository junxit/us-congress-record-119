# RECOGNIZING PAUL LUKASIEWICZ

**Speaking:** Tonko, Paul (T000469) [D-NY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING PAUL LUKASIEWICZ

  (Mr. TONKO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TONKO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my 
constituents, Paul Lukasiewicz of Scotia, New York, for his years of 
dedicated advocacy on behalf of people with disabilities and those 
suffering from post-concussion syndrome.
  As a member of the spina bifida community, Paul has spent 13 years 
volunteering with the Spina Bifida Association of New York State. In 
that way, he has helped other members of the community to live full and 
meaningful lives.
  But Paul's work hasn't stopped there. After suffering a concussion 
and spending years dealing with the effects of post-concussion 
syndrome, Paul wanted to help raise awareness of this often overlooked 
health issue and to bolster concussion education in our schools.
  Mr. Speaker, Paul immediately took action alongside his State 
representatives. Earlier this year, thanks to his tireless efforts, the 
New York State Legislature designated September 18 as Concussion 
Awareness Day.
  I am so grateful to Paul for his passion and his commitment to 
helping others, and I commend him for all of his important work on 
behalf of our communities.
  Paul is an inspiration to anyone who knows him.

                          ____________________
```
