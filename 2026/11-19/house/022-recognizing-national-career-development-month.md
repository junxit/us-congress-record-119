# RECOGNIZING NATIONAL CAREER DEVELOPMENT MONTH

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
             RECOGNIZING NATIONAL CAREER DEVELOPMENT MONTH

  (Mr. THOMPSON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
November as National Career Development Month, an important time for 
students and workers of all ages to explore future career 
opportunities.
  Earlier this week, I introduced a resolution with my colleague, Ms. 
Bonamici of Oregon, to promote career development.
  Mr. Speaker, it is never too early for students to plan for their 
futures. As co-chair of the Career and Technical Education Caucus, it 
is important that students are aware of the career opportunities 
available to them. This knowledge helps students and young 
professionals compete in an ever-evolving job market. Prepared students 
strengthen our workforce, economy, and local communities.
  Mr. Speaker, this month, I urge Americans to recognize the 
professionals, teachers, and instructors who help learners become 
future leaders. I thank the instructors across Pennsylvania who ensure 
their students have the resources to succeed.
  Mr. Speaker, learning never stops, and we should help students 
understand the steps needed to achieve their future career goals.
```
