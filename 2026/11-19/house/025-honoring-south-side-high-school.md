# HONORING SOUTH SIDE HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Gillen, Laura (G000602) [D-NY]

```
                    HONORING SOUTH SIDE HIGH SCHOOL

  (Ms. GILLEN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. GILLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the students, 
teachers, and administrators at South Side High School for the honor of 
being named a Unified Champion School.
  Through the Special Olympics, the Unified Champion Schools program 
brings students together through sports, leadership, and schoolwide 
engagement. It promotes acceptance and respect for all. This program 
breaks down barriers and helps ensure every student feels welcomed and 
included.
  Mr. Speaker, I recognize Liz Marshall, Trish Takach, and Marisa 
Buckley for their outstanding dedication in bringing this program to 
the school. I also thank Principal Dr. Patrick Walsh, Assistant 
Superintendent for Special Education Marie Dalton, and Superintendent 
of Schools Matt Gaven for their support in making this possible.
  South Side High School is setting an outstanding example of what an 
inclusive school community should look like.
  Mr. Speaker, I am incredibly proud to recognize everyone who helped 
make this achievement a reality and create an inclusive community for 
every student.
```
