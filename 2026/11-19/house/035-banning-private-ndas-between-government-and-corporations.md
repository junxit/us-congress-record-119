# BANNING PRIVATE NDAs BETWEEN GOVERNMENT AND CORPORATIONS

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
        BANNING PRIVATE NDAs BETWEEN GOVERNMENT AND CORPORATIONS

  (Ms. TLAIB asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, I introduced the No Secret Deals 
Act to ban private nondisclosure agreements between government agencies 
and corporations.
  The fact that this even exists goes completely against what our 
residents expect of us. They expect transparency.
  Elected officials and corporate CEOs have been making agreements 
behind closed doors about AI data centers, water utility privatization, 
and these big tax break packages, all hidden behind these NDAs.
  In Michigan, these nondisclosure agreements have been abused by State 
and local governments to hide key details behind tax giveaways to 
corporations. Right now, in the city of Detroit, they are not debating 
$1 million or $100 million but a $548 million tax giveaway package for 
billionaire Dan Gilbert's Renaissance Center redevelopment, and we 
can't be informed. The public can't be informed of the details because, 
guess what, they signed an NDA.
  Mr. Speaker, the public deserves to know how their tax dollars are 
being spent, and the No Secret Deals Act stops this practice.
  We deserve to have a say in these projects that use our tax dollars 
and public land and that affect our health and our environment.

                          ____________________
```
