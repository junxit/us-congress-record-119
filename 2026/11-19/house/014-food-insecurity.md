# FOOD INSECURITY

**Speaking:** Jackson, Jonathan L. (J000309) [D-IL]

```
                            FOOD INSECURITY

  (Mr. JACKSON of Illinois asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. JACKSON of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, as families across Chicago 
prepare for Thanksgiving, I want to tell this body about a 9-year-old 
boy in my district named Alex Anning, who recently took great strides 
to help the families in the community of Englewood.
  This month, Alex passed out 500 bags of free groceries to dozens of 
residents and parents at his school alongside local volunteers.
  I thank Alex for continuing to selflessly invest in his community and 
for wanting to spread gratitude and happiness at such a young age.
  Now, I am calling on Congress to take a page out of Alex's book.
  This holiday season, one in three households in the city of Chicago 
cannot count on having enough food, and on the South Side, that number 
is more than half. Most of the adults in those homes are working and 
still are coming up short.
  With grocery prices continuing to rise and SNAP participation falling 
by more than 5.5 million people since Trump's big, beautiful bill 
became law, I am urging my colleagues to pass the Farm and Family 
Relief Act to give families and States the breathing room that they 
need.
  Alex did his part this Thanksgiving, and now it is time for this 
Congress to do ours.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend and thank Alex, at such a young and tender 
age, for having such a generous heart and great parents.

                          ____________________
```
