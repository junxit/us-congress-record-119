# HONORING PAT SEBOLD

**Speaking:** Mejia, Analilia (M001246) [D-NJ]

```
                          HONORING PAT SEBOLD

  (Ms. MEJIA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Patricia ``Pat'' Sebold of 
Livingston, New Jersey, who will retire in January after more than 33 
years in county government. She is one of the longest-serving county 
officials in Essex County history and one of the most dedicated.
  For 31 years, Pat taught English at West Orange High School. One of 
her students is now our colleague in the Senate, Mark Kelly.
  As a county commissioner, Pat helped keep Turtle Back Zoo open and 
sponsored Essex County's first Open Space Trust Fund ordinance. She 
understood what it meant for families to have parks and places they 
could enjoy together. When the county considered leasing jail space to 
ICE, she stood up and opposed it.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Pat for the years she gave her students and to 
the people of Essex County. I wish her a happy and well-earned 
retirement and our deepest Jersey gratitude.

                          ____________________
```
