# HONORING VICTIMS OF WAUKESHA CHRISTMAS PARADE ATTACK

**Speaking:** Fitzgerald, Scott (F000471) [R-WI]

```
          HONORING VICTIMS OF WAUKESHA CHRISTMAS PARADE ATTACK

  (Mr. FITZGERALD asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FITZGERALD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in honoring the victims of the devastating 2021 Waukesha 
Christmas Parade attack as we approach the tragedy's fifth anniversary.
  It is hard to believe that nearly 5 years have passed since a 
horrific act of violence shattered what should have been joyous 
celebrations and claimed the lives of six innocent people: Wilhelm 
Hospel, Virginia Sorenson, LeAnna Owen, Tamara Durand, Jane Kulich, and 
Jackson Sparks.
  For the families who lost loved ones and a community forever changed, 
the passage of time does not diminish the weight of the loss.
  In the years since, Waukesha has demonstrated extraordinary strength 
and compassion, coming together to support one another and honor those 
taken far too soon.
  As we approach this solemn fifth anniversary, I am proud to have 
introduced a resolution alongside my Wisconsin colleagues to ensure 
that the names and stories of the six lives lost are never forgotten.

[[Page H6294]]

  Mr. Speaker, may their memory endure for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
