# MOMENT OF SILENCE HONORING FORMER CONGRESSMAN TIM HOLDEN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA], Dean, Madeleine (D000631) [D-PA], Meuser, Daniel (M001204) [R-PA]

```
        MOMENT OF SILENCE HONORING FORMER CONGRESSMAN TIM HOLDEN

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, we rise today in memory of 
Congressman Tim Holden of Pennsylvania, who passed away on Sunday at 
his home.
  Tim was a proud son of Schuylkill County. During his 20 years in 
Congress, he was a tireless advocate for his district and never forgot 
where he came from.
  Tim understood that our responsibility as Members of Congress is not 
to serve a party but to serve the people who sent us here. He leaves 
behind a legacy of service, civility, and bipartisan friendship.
  I remember meeting Tim for the first time. It was actually at a Penn 
State home game between my election and the orientation to come here as 
a new Member of Congress. Tim came up, grabbed me by the hand, big 
smile on his face, and said: You need to be on the Agriculture 
Committee with me.
  I appreciate that. I credit Tim with actually putting me on the 
pathway to become chairman of the Agriculture Committee.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield to the gentlewoman from Pennsylvania (Ms. Dean) 
for some remarks. She had previous service with Tim.
  Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I express my deepest sympathy 
and prayers to the family and to the dearest friends of Tim Holden.

[[Page H6296]]

  I got to know Tim Holden when I was serving in the Pennsylvania State 
House. After his service here, he served on the Pennsylvania Liquor 
Control Board. He did everything with such decency and nonpartisan 
conversation.
  He lifted up others. He lifted me up in my work in the statehouse. I 
will remember him as a man of faith, decency, and good humor.
  God bless Tim Holden.
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, Tim Holden loved his 
congressional district, but he was a product of Schuylkill County.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield to the gentleman from Pennsylvania (Mr. Meuser) 
who today has the responsibility of serving Schuylkill County.

                              {time}  0950

  Mr. MEUSER. Mr. Speaker, I thank Chairman Thompson, Representative 
Dean, and all our colleagues in Pennsylvania and in the U.S. House of 
Representatives for joining me in this remembrance.
  Mr. Speaker, I rise today alongside my colleagues to honor the 
incredible life of a great man, Tim Holden. He was a constituent of 
mine. Like me, he had the honor of representing Schuylkill County in 
the U.S. House of Representatives.
  He was born in Pottsville in 1957. Congressman Holden's life was 
defined truly by service to his community and country. I knew Tim 
Holden for many, many years. He was always a good, fair, decent man, 
just a great guy, well-liked and widely respected by those on both 
sides of the political aisle.
  Before being elected to the House in 1992, Tim served our community 
as Schuylkill County Sheriff. He went on to become a highly respected 
Member and one of the original Blue Dog Democrats. He believed in 
working across the aisle, finding common ground, and putting the 
interests of his constituents and country first.
  After leaving Congress, as Secretary Dean mentioned, Tim continued 
his public service in State government with the liquor control board, 
not an easy job in Pennsylvania. He was always accessible and willing 
to pick up the phone.
  It was always a pleasure to see Tim out and about throughout 
Schuylkill County. You could always count on great conversation.
  Mr. Speaker, Congressman Tim Holden represented the other side of the 
aisle, but I know he shared the same commitment to public service 
everyone in this body cherishes and always held a genuine desire to 
make life better for the people that we represent. Congressman Holden 
served Schuylkill County and our entire region with distinction, and 
his legacy will live on in the communities he worked so hard to 
improve.
  Our entire community mourns our friend and the loss of this great 
American public servant. I extend my sincere condolences to the Holden 
family, to Tim's many, many friends and colleagues throughout 
Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania, and truly throughout our entire 
Nation.
  Mr. Speaker, I now ask our House to join us in a moment of silence 
and prayer for former Representative Tim Holden.
```
