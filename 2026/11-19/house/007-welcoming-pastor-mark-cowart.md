# WELCOMING PASTOR MARK COWART

**Speaking:** Crank, Jeff (C001137) [R-CO]

```
                      WELCOMING PASTOR MARK COWART

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the gentleman from 
Colorado (Mr. Crank) was recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. CRANK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pastor Mark Cowart 
of the Church For All Nations in Colorado Springs for his service to 
our God, our country, and our community.
  Pastor Cowart, who just prayed for the House of Representatives in 
prayer, has faithfully served the Church For All Nations since 1987. 
Under his leadership, the church has been a shining star in the Pikes 
Peak region, with an influence that reaches the edges of the globe 
through its faithful missions work.
  Since I was a teenager, I have known and appreciated Pastor Cowart as 
a family friend. Time with him and my dad on fishing trips has given me 
a close look at the kind of man he is and the faith he lives out every 
day.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Pastor Cowart for his faithful service and for 
the example he sets for all of us throughout the Pikes Peak region.

                          ____________________
```
