# RECOGNIZING BENTON CENTRAL WOMEN'S VOLLEYBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
           RECOGNIZING BENTON CENTRAL WOMEN'S VOLLEYBALL TEAM

  (Mr. BAIRD asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today recognizing the Benton Central 
women's volleyball team of Oxford, Indiana, for clinching the Indiana 
High School Athletic Association 2A State championship.
  Riding off of a 12-match win streak, the Lady Bison captured the 
title with a 3-1 win against the Heritage Christian Eagles. Their 
dominance on the court led them to secure the first State title for all 
of Benton Central athletics.
  I am so proud to congratulate the Benton County Lady Bison on their 
first State championship. These young Hoosiers did a superb job at 
showcasing their incredible talent. A job well done by the players, 
coaches, and parents.

                          ____________________
```
