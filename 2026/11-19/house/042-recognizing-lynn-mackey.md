# RECOGNIZING LYNN MACKEY

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING LYNN MACKEY

  (Mr. DeSAULNIER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Lynn Mackey on 
her retirement as Contra Costa County Superintendent of Schools in my 
district in the bay area.
  Lynn started her career volunteering at the county jail and later 
became principal at two bay area schools, where she focused on reading 
programs and strengthening relationships between teachers, students, 
and their families.
  Lynn later served as deputy superintendent in Contra Costa before her 
election as the county's superintendent of schools in 2018. Her 
passionate advocacy for local students earned her the ``Administrator 
of the Year'' award in 2012 and again in 2018.
  Lynn recently retired after a distinguished 30-year career with the 
Contra Costa County Office of Education. Please join me in honoring a 
distinguished American, Lynn Mackey, for her outstanding service and 
congratulating Lynn on her well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
