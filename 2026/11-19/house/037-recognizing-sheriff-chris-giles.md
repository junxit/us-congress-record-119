# RECOGNIZING SHERIFF CHRIS GILES

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
                              {time}  1020
                    RECOGNIZING SHERIFF CHRIS GILES

  (Mr. VINDMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate King George County 
Sheriff Chris Giles on his retirement after 36 years of service.
  A Marine Corps veteran, Sheriff Giles brought the same commitment to 
duty, honor, and service to his career in law enforcement.
  For 34 of his 36 years in law enforcement, he served the people of 
King George with distinction.
  His leadership, integrity, and commitment to public service have left 
a lasting impact on our community.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Sheriff Giles for his service to our country, 
for his service to the Commonwealth, and for his decades of dedication 
to the people of King George County.
  I wish Sheriff Giles a well-deserved retirement and congratulate him 
on an extraordinary career.

                          ____________________
```
