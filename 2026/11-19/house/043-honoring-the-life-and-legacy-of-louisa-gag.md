# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF LOUISA GAG

**Speaking:** Pressley, Ayanna (P000617) [D-MA]

```
                              {time}  1030
               HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF LOUISA GAG

  (Ms. PRESSLEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Ms. PRESSLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate World Day of 
Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims to honor the life and legacy of 
Louisa Gag. Born and raised in Boston, Louisa dedicated her life to the 
city she loved as a transportation planner, street safety advocate, and 
biking enthusiast.
  During my tenure on the Boston City Council, I worked with Louisa to 
pass the Nation's first truck side guard ordinance. Our city has been 
made better because of her.
  This July, we tragically lost Louisa to a truck collision while doing 
what she loved most, riding a bicycle.
  My husband, Conan, and I extend our deepest sympathies to Louisa's 
parents Laura and Steve, and her sister, Emily. While grieving this 
unimaginable loss, they have dedicated themselves to finishing the work 
Louisa started by raising awareness, fundraising, and working to pass 
critical legislation, Louisa's Law.
  Louisa's family is asking us to put our prayers into action and save 
lives. In her memory, and as she surely would have wanted, let us 
commit ourselves to safer streets and more livable communities.
```
