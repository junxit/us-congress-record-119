# HONORING COLONEL MYLES B. CAGGINS, JR., RETIRED, AND MRS. ANN CAGGINS ON THEIR 60TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Bishop, Sanford D. (B000490) [D-GA]

```
 HONORING COLONEL MYLES B. CAGGINS, JR., RETIRED, AND MRS. ANN CAGGINS 
                   ON THEIR 60TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. SANFORD D. BISHOP, JR.

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. BISHOP. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Colonel Myles B. 
Caggins, Jr., (Ret.), and Mrs. Ann Caggins as they celebrate 60 years 
of marriage. Family and friends will gather on Saturday, November 29, 
2026, at 4:00 p.m. at the 1918 Club in Fort Benning to give thanks for 
a love that has stood the test of time.
  Sixty years ago, two young people stood before God and made a 
promise. Ann was a student at Spelman College when she met a young man 
named Myles. A year after he asked for her hand, they said ``I do.'' 
With those two small words they began a journey that neither of them 
could fully see but both were willing to walk together.
  The road has not always been easy. The life of an Army family asks a 
great deal. It asks for new posts and new homes, for long separations, 
for nights of prayer and waiting, and for a quiet courage that is 
seldom praised. Through it all, Myles and Ann held fast to each other 
and to the faith that has anchored their home from the very beginning. 
Ann has often said that people, not places, create the memories that 
shape us. Wherever duty sent them, they made a home, because home was 
never an address. Home was each other.
  Their marriage has also been a partnership in service. Colonel 
Caggins served our Nation in uniform. Mrs. Caggins built a 
distinguished career in human resources, including service as National 
Staffing Officer for the American National Red Cross during Operation 
Desert Shield and Desert Storm. Together they served as Co-Chairs of 
the UNCF Columbus Campaign and raised record-breaking funds so that 
first-generation college students and high school seniors could pursue 
dreams that might otherwise have been out of reach.
  At the center of it all is their faith. As members of The Church of 
Christ, where Mrs. Caggins still teaches the ladies' Bible class, they 
have lived by simple commitments: to God, to family, to the task at 
hand, to service, and to growing every day. They have shown that a 
strong marriage is not built on grand gestures. It is built one 
ordinary day at a time, through patience, forgiveness, prayer, and 
showing up for one another.
  Scripture tells us in Ecclesiastes 4:12, ``And if one prevail against 
him, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly 
broken.'' For 60 years, Myles and Ann have been that threefold cord, a 
husband and a wife bound together with God at the center. Storms have 
come and seasons have changed, but that cord has never broken.
  Their greatest legacy sits in the front row of this celebration. 
Their daughter, Angela M. (Caggins) Trammel, and her husband, Ernest. 
Their son, Colonel Myles B. Caggins, III, retired, who watched his 
father wear the uniform, then wore it himself, and has now come home 
from a career of service of his own. Their grandchildren, Eric M. 
Trammel and Ariana M. Trammel. Every one of them carries the love that 
two young people promised each other six decades ago.
  Mr. Speaker, 60 years is more than 21,000 mornings of waking up and 
choosing each other again. The world has changed many times over since 
their wedding day. Their hair has turned silver and their children have 
grown, but the hand each one reaches for across the table is the same 
hand they first held as young sweethearts. That is a rare and beautiful 
thing, and it deserves to be honored.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me, my wife Vivian, and the 
more than 765,000 people of Georgia's 2nd Congressional District in 
congratulating Colonel Myles B. Caggins, Jr., (Ret.), and Mrs. Ann 
Caggins on 60 years of marriage. May God continue to bless them with 
good health, abiding joy, and many more years of growing old together, 
hand in hand.
```
