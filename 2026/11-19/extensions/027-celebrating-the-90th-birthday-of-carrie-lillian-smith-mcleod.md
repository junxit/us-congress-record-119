# CELEBRATING THE 90TH BIRTHDAY OF CARRIE "LILLIAN" SMITH McLEOD

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
    CELEBRATING THE 90TH BIRTHDAY OF CARRIE ``LILLIAN'' SMITH McLEOD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. STENY H. HOYER

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, Carrie ``Lillian'' Smith McLeod has led the 
kind of life to which so many aspire--one of faith, service, and love.
  Lillian's life began 90 years ago in Hendersonville, North Carolina. 
There, her parents taught her the importance of grace, generosity, and 
an enduring commitment to community. She strengthened her faith in God, 
cultivating a strong foundation rooted in prayer. This was just the 
beginning.
  Life took Lillian by the hand and brought her around the World, where 
she built lasting connections everywhere from North Carolina to 
Florida, from Wurzburg, Germany, to Washington, D.C. No matter where 
she resided, her kindheartedness made everyone around her feel 
welcomed, and her quick wit was always capable of putting anyone at 
ease.
  For decades, Lillian dedicated herself to civic engagement. She 
empowered communities through service and scholarship funding. As 
member of the Thirteenettes in Charlotte, Lillian supported the 
celebration and advancement of African-American women and worked to 
empower the next generation.
  Beyond her impactful career and her unmistakable sense of style, 
beyond her enduring love of jazz and her appreciation of the arts, 
above all Lillian's legacy is formed by her love of family and her love 
of God. For 49 years, Lillian shared a devoted marriage with Robert 
McLeod, Jr. Together, they built a family that includes three children, 
six grandchildren, and three great-grandchildren. In each of them, she 
instilled a strong communion with God, ensuring that faith remains an 
enduring part of her family for generations to come.
  I ask that my colleagues join me in offering well wishes and a happy 
90th birthday to Carrie ``Lillian'' Smith McLeod. Let us celebrate her 
remarkable life, both the moments that have passed and those yet to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
