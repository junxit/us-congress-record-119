# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MRS. NICOLE FOY

**Speaking:** Castro, Joaquin (C001091) [D-TX]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MRS. NICOLE FOY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOAQUIN CASTRO

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. CASTRO of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Mrs. Nicole Foy, who passed away on Friday, November 13, 
2026. A prominent presence within San Antonio for 30 years, Nicole 
dedicated herself to advocating for our city with a fervent and 
passionate voice and a deep understanding of the needs of the 
community. She is survived by her husband, Steve Spriester, and their 
three daughters, Peyton, Tatum, and Sloane.
  A native of Farmersville, Texas, Nicole received her bachelor's 
degree from Texas Christian University. Upon graduation, she began her 
career in journalism as a news reporter in 1993 at the Omaha World-
Herald in Omaha, Nebraska. Not only was this the beginning of her 
career, but also where she met her future husband.
  In 1995, Nicole moved to San Antonio to become a crime reporter at 
the San Antonio Express-News. Nicole would dedicate 12 years of service 
to the Express-News where she also covered City Hall, the Texas 
Legislature, and politics. While working for the newspaper, she won 
numerous awards and accolades for her work.
  In 2007, Nicole began a new chapter in her professional career as she 
transitioned into communications and non-profit work. From 2008 to 
2013, she was a communications strategist that worked with prominent 
institutions across San Antonio. She would go on to join Women's Global 
Connection, where she served as executive director. In 2020, she began 
working with the Texas Biomedical Research Institute as its Vice 
President of Communication.
  Nicole will be remembered as a kind, compassionate soul. For those 
who had the honor of knowing her, she made a positive impact on their 
lives. May her loved ones forever cherish their shared memories of 
laughter, care, and understanding. Even in the face of her cancer 
diagnosis, she approached the challenge of treatment with grace, 
humility, and immeasurable courage.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the incredible life and 
legacy of Mrs. Nicole Foy. Her kind, welcoming spirit will be 
remembered by all who had the pleasure of knowing her. May her voice 
continue to inspire future generations in their pursuit of truth and 
prosperity for the San Antonio community.
```
