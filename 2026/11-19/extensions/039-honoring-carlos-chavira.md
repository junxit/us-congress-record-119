# HONORING CARLOS CHAVIRA

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                        HONORING CARLOS CHAVIRA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Carlos Chavira, 
recipient of the Kern County Excellence in Beef Promotion Award. Carlos 
has made incredible strides in teaching the next generation about the 
importance of our agricultural heritage, nutrition, and cooking.
  Born in Santa Maria, California, in 1979, Carlos grew up in the 
Cuyama Valley and Taft--a place that would become his home. He was 
raised with three siblings by his parents, Rosie and Carlos, who 
instilled in him the values of hard work, dedication, and the 
importance of agriculture. Carlos is an active supporter of the Kern 
County Cattlewomen, helping with many of their events, and he is a 
tireless advocate for agricultural education.
  In 1998, after his graduation from Taft High School, Carlos joined 
the United States Army. He spent the next three years stationed in 
North Carolina and served as a U.S. Airborne Infantry Scout with the 
82nd Airborne Division. While serving in the Army, Carlos married his 
high school sweetheart, Maggie. After completing his active service, 
they moved back to the Central Valley, where they raised their two 
children Jazmyn and Chaz. Carlos continued his service to our Nation as 
a member of the California National Guard.
  A great believer in lifelong learning and the importance of 
education, Carlos attended Liberty University earning bachelor's 
degrees in criminal justice and history, and master's degrees in 
criminal justice and human services. His education reflects a desire to 
understand the people around him and strengthen our community, and he 
does so through one of his greatest passions, cooking. Today, Carlos 
works as a Culinary Arts Instructor at Taft High School, teaching 
students the art of cooking while nurturing a strong work ethic in them 
and teaching them about nutrition, agriculture, and farm-to-table 
practices. This program extends beyond the classroom and enriches their 
families and friends. Carlos says, ``it's not just about the food--it's 
about creating strong families and communities.''
  Carlos's commitment to Taft extends far beyond the classroom. Carlos 
has served as a Taft College Trustee, a board member of the West Side 
Recreation District, and a member of the Taft High School Hall of Fame 
Board. He remains a public servant today, serving on the Taft City 
Council where he continues to advocate for our community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Carlos Chavira as he 
receives the Kern County Excellence in Beef Promotion Award.
```
