# RECOGNIZING HALEY HARRIS FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
RECOGNIZING HALEY HARRIS FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Haley Harris for 
her outstanding contributions to my office and the people of New York's 
12th District.
  Haley's drive has been evident from the first steps of her career. 
Immediately after graduating from Wake Forest University in 2024, Haley 
returned to her home state of Florida to work as a Press Assistant on 
the Senate campaign for Debbie Mucarsel-Powell. Following its 
conclusion, she moved up to Washington, D.C., where she worked as a 
Press Assistant for Senate Democratic Leader Chuck Schumer. She joined 
my office as Press Secretary in April of 2026.
  In the time she has been on my team, Haley has proven to be a 
versatile communications workhorse, serving as my one-stop press, 
digital, and comms shop. She has written a litany of remarks, press 
releases, and statements to deliver my message to constituents in New 
York's 12th District and media markets across the country. She has 
staffed me for crucial moments ranging from brief calls with reporters 
in my D.C. office to long event days in the district.
  Across this last year, Haley has made the impacts of policy tangible 
to my constituents. For the 25th Anniversary of the September 11, 2001, 
attacks, Haley secured media coverage for my continued advocacy for 
those facing long-term health consequences from breathing in toxic air 
in the days, weeks, and months following the attacks.
  After Donald Trump escalated his attempted takeover of Penn Station, 
Haley brought national attention to my argument by highlighting the 
cost and corruption embedded in this plan by landing my op-eds in local 
and national publications and securing a wide range of news articles on 
the issue. She then made sure this issue stayed top-of-mind for my 
constituents, advocacy groups, and fellow lawmakers by leveraging 
social media to shine a light on the matter. She has thrown herself 
into learning the ins and outs of all my work, but I have been 
especially proud to see her transform her voice from one that naturally 
supported highways and trucking, due to her past job experiences with 
truckers and cross-country freight, to one that channeled my fight to 
prioritize mass transit and rail over highway infrastructure and heavy 
trucks.
  Crucially, Haley has managed the entirety of my communications shop 
as a single staffer--

[[Page E1255]]

ranging from drafting my remarks, to growing my social media platforms, 
to building relationships with reporters and pitching my message to 
cable and print publications.
  She has consistently analyzed the effectiveness of our communications 
strategy and distilled the audience and performance of our social media 
content into terms that are easily understandable summaries for staff.
  Haley has taken on these large responsibilities with a smile and 
positivity. She has consistently demonstrated her willingness to go the 
extra mile and to master new skills in a rapidly evolving media market.
  As much as she belongs to New York now, Haley will always cherish her 
Florida upbringing and Cuban heritage. Through conversations while 
walking the halls of Congress, sitting on the House floor, and 
navigating New York sidewalks with her, I have come to know her family, 
and especially her younger sister, Mercedes.
  Haley's next principal will be lucky to have such a hardworking and 
cheerful presence in the office. I look forward to watching her shape 
the future, and I thank her for her service.

                          ____________________
```
