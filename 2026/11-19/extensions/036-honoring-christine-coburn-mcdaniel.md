# HONORING CHRISTINE COBURN-MCDANIEL

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                   HONORING CHRISTINE COBURN-MCDANIEL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and congratulate Mrs. Christine Coburn McDaniel of Clarksdale, 
Mississippi, on being named the 2026 CSpire Citizen of the Year at the 
Chamber of Commerce's 80th Annual Banquet. This well-deserved honor 
recognizes her lifetime of service, leadership, and dedication to the 
Mississippi Delta.
  A graduate of W.A. Higgins High School, Mrs. McDaniel earned her 
nursing diploma in St. Louis and returned home in 1967. She spent 38 
years caring for patients at the local hospital, sewing in several 
leadership roles and earning the title of Nurse of the Year in 1985. 
After retiring, she continued serving seniors at the S.L.A. Jones 
Senior Activity Center.
  Her commitment to community service extends to health awareness, 
hunger relief, environmental stewardship, and youth development. From 
delivering meals and coordinating food distributions to supporting 
cancer awareness and community improvement initiatives, Mrs. McDaniel 
has consistently worked to improve the lives of others.
  Faith has also guided her remarkable journey. At Immaculate 
Conception Catholic Church, she serves in numerous leadership roles and 
dedicated 25 years to youth ministry. She received the Bishop Chanche 
Medal from the Catholic Diocese of Jackson in 2016. Additionally, she 
has been a proud member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated, 
for more than six decades, including serving eight years as president 
of the Iota Delta Omega Chapter.
  Beyond her professional accomplishments and community service, Mrs. 
McDaniel treasures her family. She has been a devoted wife, mother, 
bonus mother, grandmother, and great-grandmother. Her life reflects the 
importance of family, mentorship, and maintaining meaningful 
connections across generations. Whether caring for patients, serving 
her church, supporting local initiatives, or spending time with loved 
ones, she continues to demonstrate that a life of service is built 
through consistent acts of kindness and commitment.
  Mrs. McDaniel represents the very best of Clarksdale and the 
Mississippi Delta. Her decades of service as a nurse, faith leader, 
mentor, community organizer, and advocate have touched countless lives. 
The recognition of her as the 2026 CSpire Citizen of the Year is a 
fitting tribute to a woman whose contributions have strengthened her 
community and inspired others to serve.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mrs. 
Christine Coburn McDaniel for her outstanding achievements and this 
well-deserved honor and commend the Clarksdale Chamber of Commerce and 
CSpire for recognizing her remarkable legacy. May her example continue 
to inspire future generations to lead with compassion, serve with 
purpose, and remain committed to the well-being of their communities.
```
