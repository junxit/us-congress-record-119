# HONORING JAVADRIC "JAY" KELLY

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                    HONORING JAVADRIC ``JAY'' KELLY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Javadric ``Jay'' Kelly, an accomplished professional dancer, 
choreographer, and native of Bolton, Mississippi, whose remarkable 
talent and contributions to the performing arts have distinguished him 
as a representative of Mississippi's rich cultural heritage.
  Mississippi has long occupied a prominent place in American music and 
entertainment, producing generations of artists whose creativity has 
influenced the cultural landscape of this nation. Mr. Kelly continues 
this distinquished tradition, demonstrating that extraordinary talent 
can emerge from even the smallest communities.
  A proud product of the Jackson Public SchooI District, Mr. Kelly 
gaduated from Jim Hill High SchooI in Jackson, Mississippi, in 2013. 
His passion for dance began at the age of three, inspired by legendary 
entertainers Michael Jackson and James Brown. Largely self-taught he 
later attended Hinds Community College, where he refined his abilities 
in jazz, ballet, tap, and modern dance.
  Whlle attendlng Hinds Community College, Mr. Kelly served as a 
choreographer for the renowned Dancing Dolls of Jackson, featured on 
Lifetime's television series Bring It! Through this opportunity, he 
helped develop young performers while contributing to the national 
recognition of Mississippi's distinctive dance culture.
  Throughout his professional career, Mr. Kelly has performed alongside 
celebrated entertainers, including GloRilla, Akeem Ali, Omarion, and 
B2K. His artistry has taken him to numerous countries, performing 
before international audiences. Among his notable accomplishments is 
his participation in Kendrick Lamar's 2025 Super Bowl LIX halftime 
performance. More recently, Mr. Kelly was featured in Netflix's A 
Different World, further demonstrating his versatility and contribution 
to entertainment.
  Beyond his individual achievements, Mr. Kelly represents the 
dedication and artistic discipline of professional dancers and 
choreographers whose contributions are essential to the entertainment 
industry. His accomplishments reflect years of perseverance, 
creativity, and commitment to his profession.
  Mr. Kelly's journey from the classrooms of Jim Hill High School and 
the communities of Bolton and Jackson to nationally and internationally 
recognized stages serves as an inspiration to aspiring young artists. 
His success demonstrates the extraordinary potential within 
Mississippi's public schools and communities when talent is cultivated 
through education, opportunity, and determination.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Javadric 
``Jay'' Kelly for his outstanding contributions to professional dance, 
choreography, and American entertainment. His accomplishments bring 
distinction to his community and the State of Mississippi, while 
inspiring future generations to pursue their aspirations with 
determination and excellence.
```
