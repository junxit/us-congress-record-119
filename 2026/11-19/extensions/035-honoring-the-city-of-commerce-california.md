# HONORING THE CITY OF COMMERCE, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING THE CITY OF COMMERCE, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Commerce, California, a city I have represented in Congress for four 
years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  Since its incorporation in 1960, Commerce has followed a clear 
principle: a strong local economy should directly support the families 
who call the city home. Over the decades, Commerce has grown into a 
major center for manufacturing, logistics, and employment while 
reinvesting in parks, libraries, and public programs for residents.
  Working with the residents, civic leaders, and business operators who 
maintain this balance has been a rewarding experience. To support 
cleaner neighborhood transportation, I was proud to secure $500,000 in 
federal funding to help Commerce modernize its transit fleet, 
transition toward zero-emission buses, and redevelop former industrial 
land, improving local air quality while connecting residents to jobs, 
schools, healthcare, and destinations like the Citadel Outlets area.
  Commerce shows how economic activity and strong public services can 
work together. I thank the city staff, local leaders, and families for 
their welcome and partnership. I am honored to have represented this 
amazing city in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
