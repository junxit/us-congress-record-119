# HONORING THE PINK OUT BREAST CANCER AWARENESS COMMUNITY DAY IN GREENWOOD, MISSISSIPPI

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
    HONORING THE PINK OUT BREAST CANCER AWARENESS COMMUNITY DAY IN 
                         GREENWOOD, MISSISSIPPI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor the Pink Out Breast Cancer Awareness Community Day, an 
important community gathering held in Greenwood, Mississippi, on 
October 10, and to commend the continued efforts of those who have 
dedicated themselves to breast cancer awareness, education, and 
community support.
  Sponsored by Power of Pink in partnership with the Opportunity in 
Motion Foundation and Delta Girls, this annual gathering brings 
together residents, families, survivors, advocates, and community 
leaders in support of a shared mission: raising awareness about breast 
cancer, encouraging healthy living, and strengthening support for those 
affected by the disease.
  Although the threat of rain from the remnants of Hurricane Isaias 
required the event to be moved from its traditional location at East 
Park to the Senior Citizens Building, the change in venue did not 
diminish the importance of this occasion. The family-friendly 
celebration featured walking teams, informational booths, guest 
speakers, food, music, recognition, and activities designed to promote 
breast cancer awareness and community engagement.
  The Pink Out Breast Cancer Awareness Community Day represents more 
than an annual event. It reflects the power of community partnerships, 
the importance of early detection and regular screenings, and the need 
to ensure that individuals and families facing breast cancer diagnosis 
are surrounded by encouragement, information, and support. By bringing 
people together, the organizers help foster meaningful conversations, 
honor those whose lives have been touched by breast cancer, and remind 
residents that awareness and access to appropriate health care can make 
a difference.
  I commend Power of Pink, the Opportunity in Motion Foundation, Delta 
Girls, and all participating volunteers, sponsors, health advocates, 
and community members for their continued commitment to this worthy 
cause. Their efforts demonstrate the value of collaboration and the 
lasting impact of grassroots initiatives that promote health, hope, and 
unity throughout the Mississippi Delta.
  As we recognize the history and continued significance of the Pink 
Out Breast Cancer Awareness Community Day, let us renew our commitment 
to supporting breast cancer survivors, remembering those we have lost, 
encouraging preventative care, and working toward a future in which 
every individual has access to the information, resources, and 
compassionate care needed to face this disease.

[[Page E1260]]

  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the Pink 
Out Breast Cancer Awareness Community Day and honoring all those who 
continue to advance breast cancer awareness and support throughout 
Greenwood, the Mississippi Delta, and our Nation.
```
