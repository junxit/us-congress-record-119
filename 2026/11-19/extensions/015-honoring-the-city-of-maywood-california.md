# HONORING THE CITY OF MAYWOOD, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE CITY OF MAYWOOD, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Maywood, California, a city I have represented in Congress for four 
years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  Standing alongside the people of Maywood as the city marked its 100th 
anniversary and entered its second century is a memory I will always 
value. Incorporated in 1924, Maywood's first 100 years reflect working-
class perseverance, immigrant entrepreneurship, and neighbors looking 
out for one another. Celebrating a centennial here is about honoring 
the generations who built the foundation for everything Maywood is 
today.
  To support Maywood's next century, we focused on practical 
investments in daily infrastructure and youth services. Building on the 
prior work of Representative Lucille Roybal-Allard, we delivered $1.2 
million in federal funding to modernize aging sewer lines, protecting 
public health along heavily traveled corridors like Atlantic Boulevard 
and Slauson Avenue. We also secured $850,000 for Maywood's first 
dedicated Teen Center, an 8,188-square-foot facility near Maywood 
Riverfront Park designed to give young people and families in one of 
our region's densest cities a safe place to learn and spend time after 
school.
  I thank Maywood for welcoming me into their celebrations and 
community. I am grateful to have played a role in supporting their 
city's next chapter. I am honored to have represented this amazing city 
in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
