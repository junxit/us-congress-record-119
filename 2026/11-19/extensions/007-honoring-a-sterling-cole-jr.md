# HONORING A. STERLING COLE, JR.

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                     HONORING A. STERLING COLE, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor A. Sterling Cole, Jr. as 
he receives the Kern County Cattleman of the Year award.
  Born in Bakersfield and raised in the community of Delano with his 
brother, Ronald, and sister, Kathleen, Mr. Cole proudly advances his 
family's 141-year legacy in the cattle business as a third-generation 
cattleman. At a young age, he developed a lifelong love for cattle 
ranching under his father's mentorship and at his mother's insistence, 
working his way to a degree from Bakersfield College while pursuing his 
passion. While working through his education and his work on the ranch, 
Mr. Cole also became an active member of the Delano Future Farmers of 
America, demonstrating a lifelong commitment to strengthening the 
agricultural mentorship community.
  The Cole family has deep roots in the Central Valley, stretching back 
to Mr. Cole's grandfather Sterling Lincoln Cole, a cattleman, pioneer, 
and one of the first Delano city councilmen. His father, Amos S. 
``Bud'' Cole, Sr., served as the Mayor of Delano and served on the city 
council as well, devoting 13 years towards bettering his community. His 
mother, Helen Byrne Cole, served as Mayor Pro Tem of Delano and was a 
long-standing member of the California Women in Agriculture group. 
Today, Sterling continues to establish Central Valley roots with his 
wife Janie, and their five children.
  Mr. Cole also continues his family's legacy of civil service by 
supporting groups like the Delano Moose Club, local 4-H programs, and 
youth initiatives with the Sportsman's Club of Arroyo Grande. Most 
recently, Sterling and Janie were honored to be named the Delano 2026 
Harvest Holidays Agriculture Grand Marshals.
  Though he has enjoyed many remarkable milestones in his career, Mr. 
Cole's greatest pride remains the family ranch, which doubled in both 
size and number of cattle run under his management.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in congratulating Mr. 
Cole for the dedication, commitment, and years of hard work he invested 
into not only his family and their ranch, but to our community.
```
