# RECOGNIZING THOR REIMANN FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
RECOGNIZING THOR REIMANN FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Thor Reimann for 
his superb service to my office and the people of New York's 12th 
District.
  A proud Minnesotan, Thor graduated Phi Beta Kappa from Harvard 
College in 2025

[[Page E1261]]

where he studied environmental policy. After college, Thor moved to New 
York's 12th District to work as a Park Ranger at the Statue of Liberty 
National Monument. Inspired by the statue's representation of the 
steadfastness of the rule of law, he came to Washington, D.C. in early 
2026, where he started on the Hill as an intern at the Natural 
Resources Committee.
  Thor was my final hire in Congress when he joined my team as my 
Scheduler in May of this year. From his first days in the office, Thor 
quickly mastered the various administrative and operations tasks of his 
role, and became a tremendous asset to me in D.C. He managed my D.C. 
calendar expertly and helped make my office even more responsive to the 
numerous stakeholders seeking an audience with me and my staff. He 
keenly understands my priorities and how to best maximize my time to 
meet my goals. In time, Thor expanded his role to help coordinate and 
manage my district schedule, as well, ensuring communication and 
alignment between both of my offices.
  In addition to the day-to-day operations, Thor was a key player in 
smoothly closing my office. Liaising with House and other archiving 
officials, he created a system to process and archive over 200 boxes of 
files across both of my offices and ensured that we met transition 
deadlines ahead of schedule. And once our D.C. files were catalogued, 
he traveled up to New York to help my district staff get over the 
finish line, as well. Thor gets the job done.
  Always eager to learn, Thor went above and beyond his role as a 
Scheduler to manage the entirety of our office mail program. He 
processed over 13,000 pieces of constituent mail on numerous key 
legislative issues of concern to the people of New York.
  Finally, Thor's resolute belief in public service and our shared 
humanity guided his every interaction. Sitting in the front office, he 
always greeted visitors with a smile and heard what they had to share. 
He also took over 20 constituent meetings on a wide range of issues to 
ensure that my constituents could be heard and you could tell that Thor 
was taking a meeting by the shared laughter and camaraderie that echoed 
through the office, even on contentious issues.
  As an intern supervisor, this commitment to others translated into 
strong mentorship, and establishing touchpoints like entrance and exit 
interviews, constantly passing on events, and setting up special 
experiences to ensure that our interns had a meaningful time.
  Throughout it all, Thor took the work, but never himself, seriously. 
His quick and easy laughter always brightened the mood of our office 
and kept the team buoyant even in chaotic moments.
  I greatly appreciate having Thor in the office. He anticipates tasks 
and finishes them even before I assign them to him. He is incredibly 
reliable and I know I can count on him to perform his duties 
efficiently and effectively. Thor is a wonderful people person. He is 
smart and engaging. He puts people at ease and handles even the most 
awkward office visitor with professionalism and respect.
  Thor was always quick to share his love for Minnesota with the rest 
of our team. We cheered him on this year when he ran the Grandma's 
Marathon in Duluth, Minnesota and when he finished an entire jar of 
Sweet Martha's Cookies at the Minnesota State Fair.
  Once again, I thank Thor for his service to my office and the 
Congress and wish him all the best as he pursues what will certainly be 
a bright career.

                          ____________________
```
