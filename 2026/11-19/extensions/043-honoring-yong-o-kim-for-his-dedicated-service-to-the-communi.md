# HONORING YONG O. KIM FOR HIS DEDICATED SERVICE TO THE COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
    HONORING YONG O. KIM FOR HIS DEDICATED SERVICE TO THE COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Yong O. 
Kim for more than two decades of dedicated service to Good Spoon Urban 
Ministries and individuals and families in need throughout Northern 
Virginia and the Washington, D.C., metropolitan area.
  For over 20 years, Mr. Kim has devoted his time and energy to helping 
those facing poverty, homelessness, and addiction. Through his 
leadership of Good Spoon Urban Ministries' food bank program, he has 
worked tirelessly to combat hunger and ensure that individuals and 
families have access to food and other essential necessities.
  Founded in Northern Virginia in 2004, Good Spoon Urban Ministries 
began with a simple mission: to provide meals and compassionate 
assistance to people struggling to make ends meet. What started as an 
effort to feed Latino day laborers and underserved residents has grown 
into a community ministry that provides food, clothing, health 
services, employment assistance, and other vital resources to 
vulnerable populations throughout the region.
  Over the past two decades. Good Spoon has served more than 735,000 
meals and expanded its outreach to serve tens of thousands of 
individuals each year. Through its food distribution programs, health 
initiatives, and community partnerships, the organization has become an 
important source of support for families facing financial hardship and 
individuals experiencing homelessness.
  Mr. Kim's leadership has been an important pan of this mission. His 
dedication reflects a simple but powerful belief: that every person 
deserves dignity, compassion, and a helping hand during difficult 
times. By helping provide nutritious meals and essential supplies, he 
has made a meaningful difference in the lives of neighbors who might 
otherwise go without.
  Good Spoon's work also demonstrates the extraordinary contributions 
of our region's faith-based organizations and volunteers. By bringing 
together people from different backgrounds in service to others, the 
organization has helped strengthen the bonds that make Northern 
Virginia such a welcoming and compassionate community. Mr. Kim's years 
of service embody that same spirit of generosity and neighborIiness.
  Beyond the immediate assistance he provides, Mr. Kim has demonstrated 
the lasting impact of volunteerism and community service. His 
commitment reminds us that meaningful change often begins with 
individuals who recognize a need and dedicate themselves to meeting it. 
His example continues to inspire volunteers, community partners, and 
supporters to extend a helping hand to those facing hardship.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Yong 
O. Kim for his extraordinary commitment to serving others. I thank him 
for more than two decades of compassionate service and the meaningful 
difference he continues to make in the lives of individuals and 
families throughout our region.
```
