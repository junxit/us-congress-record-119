# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF STATE REPRESENTATIVE MIKE BAYHAM

```
    HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF STATE REPRESENTATIVE MIKE BAYHAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE JOHNSON

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. Johnson of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of my good friend; State Representative Mike Bayham. 
Although Mike left us all too soon, he certainly left his mark on his 
friends and the entire state of Louisiana. Mike is known for his love 
of his lifelong home, St. Bernard Parish, Louisiana, his faith in God, 
and his country.
  Mike always took time to share these loves with his friends. Whether 
it was traveling abroad to ensure our Louisiana values were shared 
internationally, or welcoming world leaders to our beloved state, 
everyone knew that Mike was the kind of person that had a deep passion 
for and devotion to the people over the political whims or wins. Mike's 
goal was not to be recognized or known for his talents and knowledge--
he simply devoted himself to being a public servant.
  Mike was a true patriot and fighter for conservative principles. In 
fact, this year he spearheaded the America 250 Louisiana commission, 
where he led the charge for the state in celebrating America's 250 
years, her founding documents, and our collective principles.
  Mike had been a trusted friend of mine for more than 35 years. He 
carried forward the same servant's heart and love of country that he 
did when we campaigned together as undergraduates at Louisiana State 
University, and when he was elected to the St. Bernard City Council at 
the young age of just 26.
  Like so many others, Kelly and I will miss Mike, and we continue to 
pray for all those across our state who are impacted by his passing.
  As scripture commends, ``well done, good and faithful servant.''

                          ____________________
```
