# HONORING THE COMMUNITY OF WALNUT PARK, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING THE COMMUNITY OF WALNUT PARK, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
community of Walnut Park, California, an unincorporated area I have 
represented in Congress for four years but will sadly no longer 
represent in the 120th Congress.
  Representing Walnut Park taught me an important lesson: a community 
does not need its own city hall to have a strong voice and a clear 
identity. Though geographically compact and unincorporated, Walnut Park 
is a vital part of Southeast Los Angeles.
  Corridors like Pacific Boulevard and Florence Avenue connect 
residents to neighborhood businesses, local schools like Walnut Park 
Elementary, and surrounding cities. Over generations, families have put 
down roots here, supporting mom-and-pop shops and organizing to secure 
the public resources their neighborhoods deserve. Every resident in an 
unincorporated community deserves to be heard and included in the 
decisions affecting their daily lives, and working alongside you to 
ensure Walnut Park was represented has been an honor.
  I thank the community leaders, families, and neighbors who welcomed 
me into their blocks and their lives. Their commitment to one another 
will keep Walnut Park strong for years to come. I am honored to have 
represented this amazing community in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
