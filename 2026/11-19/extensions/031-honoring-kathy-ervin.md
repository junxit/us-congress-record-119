# HONORING KATHY ERVIN

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                          HONORING KATHY ERVIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Kathy Ervin as she 
receives the Kern County Cattlewoman of the Year Award.
  Born in Bakersfield, Ms. Ervin's life reflects more than five decades 
of public service, community leadership, and dedication to the 
agricultural heritage of Kern County.
  She entered the cattle industry in 1993 after purchasing property in 
Glennville, continuing the property's existing grazing operation and 
becoming deeply engaged in ranch work. She remains active today, 
raising cattle on her property and supporting agricultural events 
throughout the region. She has served on the boards of the Kern County 
Cattlewomen, the Kern County Retired Employees Association, and the 
Backcountry Horsemen of California Kern Sierra Unit, contributing 
extensively to community programs and educational outreach.
  Ms. Ervin began her public service at the Kern County Welfare 
Department in 1971 and, in 1976, joined the District Attorney's Office, 
Child Support Division, where she spent 25 years ensuring fairness and 
accountability for families across the county. She retired as an 
Investigative Family Support Officer IV in 2001.
  Her dedication to Kern County did not end there. She served 13 years 
as a Reserve Deputy Sheriff and was an active member of the Sheriffs 
Mounted Unit. She was also appointed to the board of the Kern County 
Sheriffs Office Reserve Association, later serving as Vice President 
and Treasurer of the board.
  Over the past four decades, Ms. Ervin has tirelessly supported the 
Greenhorn Mountains Veterans Association as well, helping cook for 
Association events and catering their Small Miracles Dinners, 
Valentine's Dinners, and Junior Rodeo lunches and breakfasts. Her work 
has supported countless community fundraisers, rodeo events, and 
veterans' activities over the years, and she received the John Justin 
Standard of the West Award for her work with the Stampede Days Rodeo.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Ms. Kathy Ervin on this 
well-deserved honor and in recognizing her many contributions to the 
people of Kern County.
```
