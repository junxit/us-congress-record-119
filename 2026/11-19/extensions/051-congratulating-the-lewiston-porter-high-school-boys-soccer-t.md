# CONGRATULATING THE LEWISTON-PORTER HIGH SCHOOL BOYS SOCCER TEAM CHAMPIONS

**Speaking:** Tenney, Claudia (T000478) [R-NY]

```
    CONGRATULATING THE LEWISTON-PORTER HIGH SCHOOL BOYS SOCCER TEAM 
                               CHAMPIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. CLAUDIA TENNEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Ms. TENNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
the Lewiston-Porter High School boys soccer team of Youngstown, in 
Niagara County, for winning the 2026 New York State Public High School 
Athletic Association Class A State Championship, the first state title 
in the program's history.
  On Saturday, November 14, 2026, at Middletown High School, the 
Lancers defeated Jamesville-DeWitt 3-0, with second-half goals from 
Chace Burnham, John Wright, and Luke Leardini. The victory capped a 
remarkable 22-1-1 season in which Lew-Port allowed just five goals all 
year, and in four state tournament games the Lancers outscored their 
opponents 16 to 1. Senior Luke Leardini finished the season with a 
program-record 64 goals.
  For years, Lew-Port has been one of Section VI's premier programs, 
with seven consecutive sectional title game appearances, but a state 
championship had remained out of reach. Under head coach Marc Niccola, 
in his first season leading the team, these student-athletes finally 
brought it home. As Coach Niccola said of his team's defense, ``Just 
five goals all year is unbelievable.''
  This championship is a source of great inspiration and accomplishment 
for these student-athletes, their families, and the entire Lewiston-
Porter community.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Coach Niccola, his 
staff, and the Lewiston-Porter Lancers on an outstanding season and a 
historic championship.

                          ____________________
```
