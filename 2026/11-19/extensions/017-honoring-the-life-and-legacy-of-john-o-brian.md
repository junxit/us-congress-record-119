# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JOHN O'BRIAN

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JOHN O'BRIAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of John O'Brian, the beloved principal of Paterson Elementary 
School in Fleming Island, Florida.
  For 28 Years, Mr. O'Brian served Clay County students and families. 
From teaching mathematics at Ridgeview High School to leadership roles 
at Green Cove Junior High, Middleburg High School, and Paterson 
Elementary, he helped generations of young people learn and grow.
  Last year, Abby and I toured Paterson Elementary with Mr. O'Brian. At 
the time, his school ranked among the top ten public elementary schools 
in all of Florida. Yet what stood out most was how proud he was of his 
faculty and the good work they were doing for their students. He was 
quick to give others credit, a reflection of the kind of leader he was.
  His influence was evident in everyday moments: knowing students by 
name, encouraging his teachers, and making people feel they belonged. 
He showed how much a caring educator can mean to a child, a family, and 
an entire community.
  Abby and I extend our heartfelt condolences to his daughter, Bailey, 
and her husband, Dylan; his brother, Dink, and sister, Lynn; and the 
entire Paterson family.
  May God comfort all who mourn him. May the young people he guided 
carry forward his legacy through their own lives of purpose, kindness, 
and service.

                          ____________________
```
