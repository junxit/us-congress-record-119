# HONORING THE FIRST RESPONDERS OF THE "MIRACLE ON THE MISSISSIPPI"

**Speaking:** Scalise, Steve (S001176) [R-LA]

```
  HONORING THE FIRST RESPONDERS OF THE ``MIRACLE ON THE MISSISSIPPI''

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE SCALISE

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. SCALISE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
extraordinary first responders whose actions during the ``Miracle on 
the Mississippi'' saved lives and exemplified the very best of us in 
times of need. On Friday, November 13, a tanker traveling down the 
Mississippi River in New Orleans tragically collided with a passenger 
cruise ship, throwing passengers into the water in a dire situation.
  Within 22 minutes, the cruise ship had fully capsized, and more than 
100 passengers and crew faced the strong currents of the Mississippi 
River. Thanks to the incredible preparation and training of the men and 
women who serve the United States Coast Guard, the Port of New Orleans 
and their Harbor Police Department, and the New Orleans Police and Fire 
Departments, a swift rescue operation was conducted with all passengers 
and crew accounted for and no fatalities.
  This is a remarkable testament to the dedication of our first 
responders and their coordination with state and federal partners. God 
was looking after New Orleans that day, and He worked through those 
heroes who ran toward danger to help others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending these first 
responders for their incredible service and bravery and in praying for 
the health and recovery of all those affected. May God bless our first 
responders.

                          ____________________
```
