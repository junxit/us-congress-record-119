# RECOGNIZING AND HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE JOHN SMITHEE

**Speaking:** Jackson, Ronny (J000304) [R-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING AND HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE JOHN SMITHEE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RONNY JACKSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. JACKSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my 
friend and fellow Texan, Representative John Smithee, as he concludes 
41 years of distinguished service in the Texas House of 
Representatives.
  Born and raised in Amarillo, John has spent his entire life serving 
the people and communities of the Texas Panhandle. After graduating 
from Tascosa High School, he attended Amarillo College and West Texas 
State University, earning his Bachelor of Business Administration in 
1973. He went on to earn his law degree from Texas Tech University in 
1976 and has built a distinguished legal career in Amarillo as a 
partner at Smithee & Russell.
  Since first being elected in 1985, John has been a tireless advocate 
for public safety, economic opportunity, and responsible government 
while representing House District 86 in the Texas House of 
Representatives. Over more than four decades, John's colleagues have 
relied on his legal expertise, sound judgment and steady leadership on 
some of the most consequential issues facing the State of Texas. The 
respect of his colleagues ultimately allowed him to be selected to 
serve as the chairman of several important committees, including 
Criminal Jurisprudence, Judiciary and Civil Jurisprudence, and 
Insurance.
  That experience and commitment to crime prevention was especially 
evident during the 2025 passage of the strongest bail reform package in 
Texas history. Under Chairman Smithee's leadership, the sweeping 
package requires judges to deny bail for violent criminals, prohibits 
the use of public funds to be used to post bail for criminals, and 
mandates that magistrates must provide a written explanation within 24 
hours on why they determined an arrest was made without probable cause. 
John's persistence throughout the process led to a bipartisan package 
that will keep Texans safer today and for generations to come.
  Throughout his tenure, John has also made a significant impact on the 
Texas Panhandle. He played an important role in bringing 9-1-1 service 
to Amarillo and rural communities throughout the region. He helped 
bring West Texas State University into the Texas A&M University System, 
leading to the creation of West Texas A&M University, supported 
critical infrastructure for the growth of Texas' energy industry, and 
worked to secure funding for Amarillo's new mental health hospital. He 
also helped advance Texas Tech University's Schools of Pharmacy and 
Veterinary Medicine, strengthening educational, healthcare, and 
workforce opportunities across West Texas.
  These accomplishments have made impactful investments in the people, 
institutions, and communities of the Texas Panhandle, and reflect a 
public servant who understood the needs of his constituents.
  After 41 years, John Smithee leaves behind a remarkable legacy of 
service to the Texas Panhandle and the State of Texas. I know his 
constituents, his colleagues, and the many Texans whose lives have been 
impacted by his work are grateful for his dedication.
  On behalf of the people of Texas' 13th Congressional District, I want 
to thank John for his friendship and his many years of service to our 
great state. I wish him, his wife Becky, their three children, and 
eight grandchildren all the best as he begins this next chapter.
  I thank John for everything he has done for the Texas Panhandle and 
the great State of Texas.

                          ____________________
```
