# HONORING THE CITY OF HUNTINGTON PARK, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
            HONORING THE CITY OF HUNTINGTON PARK, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Huntington Park, California, a city I have represented in Congress 
for four years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th 
Congress.
  If you walk down Pacific Boulevard on any afternoon and you will see 
the daily life of Huntington Park. Since its incorporation in 1906, 
this city has served as a center of commerce, transportation, and 
culture in Los Angeles County, where generations of immigrant and 
Latino families have built thriving businesses and neighborhoods.
  Representing Huntington Park has shaped my approach to public 
service. Some of my most memorable days in office were spent walking 
past historic storefronts like the Warner Huntington Park Theatre, 
talking with local shop owners, and joining families at community 
gatherings to discuss local priorities. The educators, workers, 
students, and advocates here carry forward a strong tradition of civic 
involvement.
  As my time serving Huntington Park concludes, I want to thank the 
residents, city staff, and local leaders for their partnership and 
hospitality. Their community's work ethic and pride will continue to 
move the city forward. I am honored to have represented this amazing 
city in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
