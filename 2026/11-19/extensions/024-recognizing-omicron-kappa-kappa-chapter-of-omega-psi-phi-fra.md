# RECOGNIZING OMICRON KAPPA KAPPA CHAPTER OF OMEGA PSI PHI FRATERNITY INC. FOR ITS ACHIEVEMENT WEEK AND HONOREES

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
  RECOGNIZING OMICRON KAPPA KAPPA CHAPTER OF OMEGA PSI PHI FRATERNITY

                              of virginia

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Omicron 
Kappa Kappa Chapter of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc., for its 3rd 
Annual Coleman-Love Salute to Veterans and Achievement Week Awards 
Luncheon.
  Founded on November 17, 1911, at Howard University by Oscar J. 
Cooper, Edgar A. Love, Frank Coleman, and Dr. Ernest Everett Just, 
Omega Psi Phi has celebrated Achievement Week since 1925 to honor 
leadership, scholarship, and service.
  Since 1927, the fraternity has also recognized outstanding high 
school essay contest participants. This year's luncheon continues that 
tradition by honoring students, veterans, and community members for 
their achievements and contributions, and I include in the Record their 
names:

       High School Essay Contest First Place Winner: Chandler Ray.
       High School Essay Contest Second Place Winner: Natalia 
     Holbeckk.

  On April 11, 2026, the chapter received the inaugural Dr. Ernest 
Everett Just STEM Large Graduate Chapter of the Year Award at the 93rd 
Annual Third District Founder's Banquet in Virginia Beach, Virginia.
  The chapter is also presenting Outstanding Achievement Awards to 
OMEGA STEM<SUP>TM</SUP> students who participated in VEX V5 robotics 
and/or aerial drone competitions in 2024 and/or 2025 at Langston Hughes
```
