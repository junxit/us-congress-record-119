# RECOGNIZING TRENITY HUGHES FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
 RECOGNIZING TRENITY HUGHES FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE 
                           OF REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Trenity Hughes for 
her outstanding service to my office and the constituents of New York's 
12th Congressional District.
  Trenity graduated from Loyola University Chicago, where she studied 
Political Science and Global Studies, in 2022. She started her service 
in Washington D.C. as an intern for Representative Rick Larsen. Driven 
by her family's ties to Washington state, she was quickly brought back 
as a Staff Assistant upon graduating college, and a year later became a 
Legislative Correspondent. On top of her responsibilities there, when 
Representative Larsen became Ranking Member of the Transportation and 
Infrastructure Committee, Trenity helped ensure a seamless transition 
and efficient communication between his personal office and the 
Committee staff.
  Trenity started in my office in 2024, as a Legislative Correspondent 
and Legislative Aide. She brought her experience as a Legislative 
Correspondent to reorganize and revitalize my constituent mail program, 
as well as getting issue areas of her own. Over her time in my office, 
she has logged over 120,000 pieces of incoming mail from my 
constituents and sent countless letters in response. Trenity was vital 
in ensuring that I was kept updated on the opinions of my constituents, 
and that they were given a prompt and accurate response. As a 
Legislative Aide, she expanded this mail experience to advise me on 
financial services, postal, small business, and science issues.
  She then became a Legislative Assistant in 2025 and has excellently 
advised me on a wide range of topic areas: including many that she has 
long been passionate about: education, animal welfare, reproductive 
rights, nutrition, and LGBTQ+ issues. She has introduced many bills, 
like the FABRIC Act to improve the working conditions of garment 
workers, and the Refuge from Cruel Trappings Act, to ensure that our 
public lands are free from animal traps that pose needless risks to 
recreators.
  Trenity has shared that when she started as an intern, her goal for 
her Hill tenure was to become an education staffer, driven by a passion 
for helping underserved youth to achieve their full potential and 
receive the support they need to do so. I am proud that Trenity not 
only became an education staffer, but she became an excellent one.
  Trenity has also taken on tasks above and beyond her portfolio, 
having covered both scheduling and press responsibilities when staff 
changes had not yet been finalized. She helped me to build my 
Appropriations project submission form, to ensure that my constituents 
could request funding from the federal government. And she took up the 
unofficial mantle of the Social Secretary, to ensure that my staffs 
birthdays and other milestones were celebrated and embraced by all.
  Beyond these legislative accomplishments, however, and there are 
many, what Trenity will be remembered for most by our team is her 
incredible love for people. Myself, my team, and every constituent she 
met with over her tenure were immediately brought in by Trenity's quick 
laughter and embrace of everyone's unique abilities and 
characteristics. Additionally, she has prioritized mentoring every 
class of interns and helping college students adjust to the unique 
workflows of Congress, no matter their level of previous experience or 
education.
  Trenity loves travelling, and has lived in many places too, bringing 
her a worldliness and empathy that despite the stress inherent to our 
work, kept our office in good spirits and working efficiently.
  While I do not know where Trenity's next adventure will take her, I 
am sure that it will

[[Page E1259]]

be one filled with light, laughter, and a ceaseless spirit towards 
building and supporting community.
  I look forward to watching that next step and thank Trenity for her 
service.
```
