# COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE EAST PASADENA POST OF THE AMERICAN LEGION POST 280

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
 COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE EAST PASADENA POST OF THE 
                        AMERICAN LEGION POST 280

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and commend the 
distinguished East Pasadena Post of the American Legion Post 280 on 
their 100th Anniversary.

[[Page E1265]]

  Chartered in 1926, American Legion Post 280 has spent a century 
serving Veterans in East Pasadena and the surrounding communities. Over 
that time, Post 280 has upheld the values of service, patriotism, and 
commitment to those who have selflessly served to protect our Nation.
  Transitioning back to civilian life can bring profound challenges, 
and American Legion Post 280 ensures that no Veteran has to navigate 
these challenges alone. Whether guiding members through VA benefits or 
providing emotional support, Post 280 is there when Veterans need it 
most. Through the American Legion's nationwide services, the 
organization helps Veterans and their families access information about 
earned benefits, disability compensation, education and employment 
opportunities, and available health care resources. In the community 
and across the country, the American Legion remains committed to its 
mission to care for our Veterans.
  Beyond providing essential resources, the American Legion Post 280 
provides a welcoming environment to connect with fellow servicemembers 
and share their experiences. Through its monthly meetings and events, 
Post 280 brings people together across generations, ensuring that 
Veterans continue to find support and a sense of belonging with their 
fellow servicemembers after their military service. This type of 
commitment makes Post 280 an incredible community of Veterans who care 
deeply about one another and the people around them.
  The 100th anniversary of American Legion Post 280 a testament to the 
values of service, sacrifice, and solidarity that makes Post 280 vital 
to our Veterans. I am proud to honor the legacy of this organization 
and its impact in East Pasadena and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
