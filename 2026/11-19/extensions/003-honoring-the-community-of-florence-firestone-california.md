# HONORING THE COMMUNITY OF FLORENCE-FIRESTONE, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
        HONORING THE COMMUNITY OF FLORENCE-FIRESTONE, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
community of Florence-Firestone, California, an unincorporated area. I 
have represented in Congress for four years but will sadly no longer 
represent in the 120th Congress.
  In Florence-Firestone, community support is visible on every block. 
Serving this historic unincorporated neighborhood and working alongside 
the families, educators, small business owners, and advocates who 
anchor it has been a privilege of my public service.
  Over generations, residents here have invested their time and energy 
into creating opportunities for their neighbors. They see that 
commitment in neighborhood businesses along Compton Avenue, dedicated 
teachers at local schools, and grassroots organizers who work to make 
sure Florence-Firestone's voice is heard at the county and federal 
levels.
  Because a community's future starts with its youngest learners, I am 
proud of our ongoing work to secure $500,000 in federal funding for the 
Graham Early Education Center. This project will upgrade an existing 
playground into an outdoor learning space with natural play zones, 
raised garden beds, tricycle paths, and preschool-friendly equipment. 
Every child deserves a safe, well-equipped place to learn and grow.
  I thank the organizers, parents, and residents of Florence-Firestone 
for welcoming me into their neighborhood. Their dedication will 
continue to move this historic community forward. I am honored to have 
represented this amazing community in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
