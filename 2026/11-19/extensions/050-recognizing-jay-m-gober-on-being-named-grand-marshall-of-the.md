# RECOGNIZING JAY M. GOBER ON BEING NAMED GRAND MARSHALL OF THE 2026 THROCKMORTON HOMECOMING

**Speaking:** Arrington, Jodey C. (A000375) [R-TX]

```
  RECOGNIZING JAY M. GOBER ON BEING NAMED GRAND MARSHALL OF THE 2026 
                        THROCKMORTON HOMECOMING

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JODEY C. ARRINGTON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. ARRINGTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jay M. Gober, 
who has been selected by the Throckmorton Ex-Students Association to 
serve as Grand Marshal of the 2026 Throckmorton Homecoming.
  Jay's lifelong commitment to Throckmorton, his distinguished career 
in banking, and his decades of service to his community reflect the 
very best of West Texas. It is my privilege to join the people of 
Throckmorton in congratulating Jay on this well-deserved honor and 
include in the Record the following article recognizing his life, 
service, and selection as Grand Marshal:

       The Throckmorton Ex-Students Association homecoming 
     committee has selected Jay M. Gober as Grand Marshal for the 
     2026 Homecoming to be held October 16-17.
       From his early days in Throckmorton--driving a 1950 
     Chevrolet pickup delivering groceries for Henry Hext's store 
     (and he was so small he had to have something in the seat of 
     the truck to sit on to see over the steering wheel) to his 
     distinguished career in banking and community service, Jay 
     has remained deeply committed to Throckmorton and its people. 
     A 1965 graduate of Throckmorton High School, Gober earned his 
     bachelor's degree from West Texas State University in 1971. 
     He also proudly served six years in the Army National Guard.
       Throughout his career Gober has been a leader in the Texas 
     Banking Industry. He currently serves as Chairman of First 
     State Bank and is past Chairman of the Independent Bankers 
     Association of Texas. His service extends beyond banking. 
     Gober is a 32nd Degree Mason, a member of Maskat Shrine, and 
     was honored with the Throckmorton High School Distinguished 
     Alumni Award in 2014.
       Jay and his wife Becky have been married 58 years and have 
     raised three sons, two of whom are graduates of Throckmorton 
     High School.
       The Throckmorton Ex-Students Association is proud to 
     recognize Jay M. Gober's lifelong dedication, leadership, and 
     service by naming him Grand Marshal for the 2026 Throckmorton 
     Homecoming.
       When asked ``what does it mean to be chosen to be the Grand 
     Marshal of this year's homecoming parade?'' Jay replied 
     ``being selected as Grand Marshal for the 2026 Throckmorton 
     Homecoming is truly a great honor. I was fortunate to grow up 
     in Throckmorton and even more fortunate to have had the 
     opportunity to raise my own family here. As I look back over 
     the years, I realize how many people in this community helped 
     shape my life. I was blessed with parents, teachers, coaches, 
     neighbors and friends who set examples of hard work, 
     integrity, faith and service. They taught me values that have 
     guided me throughout my life and career. To be recognized by 
     the community that gave me so much is deeply humbling. It 
     causes me to reflect on those who invested in me, and it 
     makes me hope that, in some small way, I have been able to 
     give something back. If I have served as a positive role 
     model for even a few young people, encouraged someone to work 
     hard, to serve others, or to remain proud of their hometown, 
     then I feel I have honored the example that was set for me.
       Throckmorton has always been home. It is where I learned 
     life's most important lessons, where Becky and I raised our 
     family, and where friendships have lasted a lifetime. I am 
     sincerely grateful to the Throckmorton Ex-Students 
     Association for this recognition, and I accept it with 
     humility and appreciation.''
```
