# HONORING SOS MARYSVILLE FOOD PANTRY FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO THE COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING SOS MARYSVILLE FOOD PANTRY FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO THE COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Sharing Our 
Strength (SOS) Marysville Food Pantry for its continued dedication to 
serving individuals and families throughout the Marysville community.
  Through the generosity of local donors and the hard work of its 
volunteers, SOS Marysville helps provide food and essential support to 
neighbors in need. Its work brings the community together and ensures 
families have resources they can count on year-round.
  One example of this commitment is the organization's annual 
Thanksgiving Turkey Giveaway. Now in its 14th year, this tradition

[[Page E1262]]

brings together local donors, businesses, and volunteers to help 
provide families with everything they need to enjoy a Thanksgiving 
meal. Through the continued support of its community, SOS Marysville 
helps make the holiday a little brighter for families throughout the 
area.
  I also commend Carmela Burns and everyone at SOS Marysville who 
dedicate their time and energy to this important work. Their commitment 
to helping their neighbors reflects the compassion and strong community 
spirit that make Michigan's 9th Congressional District such a special 
place.
  Mr. Speaker, Sharing Our Strength is an important resource for the 
Blue Water community. I am proud to recognize this organization and the 
meaningful role it plays in supporting local families.

                          ____________________
```
