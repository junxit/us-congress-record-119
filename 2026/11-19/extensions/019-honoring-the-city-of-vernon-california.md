# HONORING THE CITY OF VERNON, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE CITY OF VERNON, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Vernon, California, a city I have represented in Congress for four 
years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  Vernon occupies a distinct role in California. Founded in 1905 just 
south of downtown Los Angeles, its significance is measured not by 
residential population numbers, but by the industrial activity that 
operates within its borders every day.
  For more than a century, Vernon's rail, freight, and manufacturing 
infrastructure has

[[Page E1258]]

supported Southern California's supply chain. From food production 
facilities to logistics and distribution hubs; the thousands of workers 
who commute into Vernon each morning produce and move goods across our 
region. Getting to know the business owners, city employees, and public 
servants who keep this industrial center operating has been an 
informative and rewarding part of my service.
  I want to thank Vernon's leadership, staff, and workforce for 
partnering with me to support our regional economy. I leave with 
respect for the essential role Vernon plays in Los Angeles County. I am 
honored to have represented this amazing city in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
