# HONORING LEE ANTHONY FORD

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                       HONORING LEE ANTHONY FORD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
remarkable servant, Lee Anthony Ford.
  Lee Anthony Ford is a United States Army veteran, retired law 
enforcement professional, and community leader who has dedicated much 
of his life to public service. His commitment to his community has been 
shaped by decades of experience in law enforcement, military service, 
and civic involvement, with a continued focus on public safety, 
equality, mentorship, and creating opportunities for future 
generations.
  Mr. Ford served in the United States Army from 1979 to 1982, 
attaining the rank of E-4 Acting Sergeant while serving as a Military 
Policeman and Multichannel Communications

[[Page E1257]]

Equipment Operator. Following his honorable discharge, he pursued a 
career in law enforcement that would span several decades.
  Mr. Ford's commitment is to help build the community in which young 
people have opportunities to succeed, citizens feel safe and heard, and 
individuals from all backgrounds are treated with dignity, fairness, 
and respect. His experiences as a veteran, law enforcement officer, and 
community leader have given him a unique perspective on both the 
challenges facing communities and the importance of collaboration in 
addressing them.
  After more than four decades of military, law enforcement, and civic 
service, Mr. Ford remains committed to the principle that has guided 
his professional life: service does not end with retirement, it 
continues wherever there is an opportunity to strengthen the community 
and improve the lives of others.
  He was married to the late Lucie Carolyn Ford and to this union three 
children were born, Lee Ford, Jr., Leandra Ford and LeZerrica Ford.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Lee 
Anthony Ford for his actions and achievements in the Natchez, 
Mississippi community.
```
