# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DAVID NIX

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DAVID NIX

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of David Nix, a Clay County educator whose service included 
leading Ridgeview Elementary School as principal.
  An educator's work reaches far beyond a classroom. The lessons 
children learn, the confidence they gain, and the opportunities 
education opens can influence the course of their lives. Today, we 
honor Mr. Nix's place in that important work and recognize the 
responsibility he accepted in serving our comminity's children.
  A school is also a community of teachers, staff, and families working 
toward a shared purpose. Leading that community is a meaningful 
calling, and Clay County is grateful for those who answer it.
  Abby and I extend our heartfelt condolences to the Nix family, his 
friends, and the students and colleagues who shared his years in 
education.
  May God comfort all who mourn David, and may his memory remind us of 
the lasting value of a life devoted to teaching and service.

                          ____________________
```
