# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MARIANNE MARSHALL

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MARIANNE MARSHALL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of my friend Marianne Marshall of Callahan, Florida, a devoted 
wife, mother, grandmother, and public servant.
  Marianne represented District Five on the Nassau County Commission 
from 1996 to 2008. For 12 years, she brought compassion to public 
service, helping neighbors find solutions and making sure their 
concerns were heard.
  Her advocacy extended to older residents, people with disabilities, 
and those facing challenges with housing and transportation. She 
understood that local government could make a meaningful difference in 
people's daily lives.
  Beyond her public service, Marianne loved preserving family memories 
through photography. She shared 57 years of marriage with her beloved 
husband, Bobby, and cherished their daughters, Tiffany and Lisa, and 
the grandchildren and great-grandchildren who knew her as ``Gaga.''
  Abby and I extend our heartfelt condolences to the entire Marshall 
family. May God comfort them, and may Marianne's example of kindness 
and service continue to inspire Nassau County.

                          ____________________
```
