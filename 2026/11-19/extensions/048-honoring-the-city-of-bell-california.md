# HONORING THE CITY OF BELL, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING THE CITY OF BELL, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Bell, California, a city I have represented in Congress for four 
years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  For a city spanning just under three square miles, Bell has a strong 
sense of pride and community. Evolving from early agricultural roots 
into a close-knit neighborhood built by generations of hardworking 
families, Bell has left a lasting mark on Southeast Los Angeles.
  Some of my favorite moments in public service have happened here, 
whether visiting local shops along Gage Avenue and Atlantic Avenue or 
joining families for community events. That warmth made working for 
Bell's future deeply rewarding. Together, we brought home more than 
$1.1 million in federal funding to renovate Pritchard Field, turning an 
underutilized lot into a green gathering place where kids can play and 
neighbors can connect. We are also working to secure $500,000 for Nueva 
Vista Park to upgrade recreational facilities and open spaces so 
families have safe places to gather and stay active.
  Working alongside Bell's residents, educators, city staff, and 
community leaders has been an honor. As my time representing them comes 
to a close, I carry a deep appreciation for this city and confidence 
that their determination will carry Bell forward for generations. I am 
honored to have represented this amazing city in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
