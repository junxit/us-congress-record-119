# RECOGNIZING ROBERT GOTTHEIM FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
RECOGNIZING ROBERT GOTTHEIM FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE 
                           OF REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Robert Gottheim 
for nearly three decades of outstanding service to me, to my 
constituents and the people of New York City, to the United States 
House of Representatives, and to our country.
  A born-and-bred New Yorker who grew up in Belle Harbor, Queens, 
Robert is a proud product of New York City public schools and a 
graduate of Beach Channel High School in Rockaway Park. He went on to 
attend Binghamton University, where he graduated cum laude with a 
degree in Law and Society. He spent his college summers as a toll 
collector for the Triborough Bridge and Tunnel Authority, a job that 
sparked his lifelong interest in transportation. He then earned his law 
degree from Brooklyn Law School, gaining experience at the Manhattan 
District Attorney's Office and the New York City Law Department before 
joining the firm of O'Dwyer & Bernstein as a litigation associate.
  In 1998, I was fortunate to have Robert join my office. Over the next 
quarter century, he served as my Policy Director, Senior Advisor, and 
District Director, directing my district operations in Manhattan and 
Brooklyn and serving as my principal liaison to elected officials, 
government agencies, community leaders, and constituents. There are few 
neighborhoods, institutions, or community leaders in our city that 
Robert does not know, and few who do not know him.
  Robert was with me in the days after September 11, 2001, when we 
visited Ground Zero together. In the months and years that followed, we 
worked side by side to help my constituents recover and get the 
services and health care they needed. He was part of our long fight to 
pass the James Zadroga 9/11 Health and Compensation Act, which created 
the World Trade Center Health Program for the responders and survivors 
who were sickened by the attacks.
  On issues facing New York's Jewish community, from the Hasidic 
communities of Brooklyn to the Upper East and West Sides of Manhattan, 
Robert's counsel has guided my efforts to combat antisemitism and to 
confront rhetoric that seeks to delegitimize Israel.
  On transit and freight, Robert has been my principal advisor. He has 
always been a champion of mass transit, fighting for federal funding 
for the Metropolitan Transportation Authority and for the New York City 
subway, the lifeblood of our city and a system millions of New Yorkers 
depend on every day. He has worked to ensure that the Port of New York 
and New Jersey, including its Brooklyn facilities in Red Hook and 
Sunset Park, is modern and up to date. He also advocated for the Cross 
Harbor Freight Tunnel and the broader effort to modernize freight rail 
in New York and New Jersey, including the float bridges that carry rail 
cars across New York Harbor.
  From 2019 to 2024, while continuing his work in my office, Robert 
served as Senior Advisor and Chief of Staff on the House Judiciary 
Committee during my tenure as Chairman and Ranking Member. He was at my 
side for the Committee's most consequential work, including the two 
impeachments of President Donald J. Trump.
  On January 6, 2021, Robert and I sheltered together in my office in 
the Rayburn Building as a violent mob, incited by President Trump, 
stormed the Capitol. It was one of the darkest days in our Nation's 
history. Through all the chaos and fear, Robert was a calm, reassuring, 
and level-headed voice. Living through it together is something that 
will forever bond us.
  In January 2024, Robert became my Chief of Staff, leading both my 
Washington and district offices. I have relied on his advice on every 
major decision, and he has run my office with a steady hand.
  Colleagues have long called Robert a consummate fixer, with an 
unmatched network and a talent for solving problems. His institutional 
knowledge is vast, deep, and extraordinary. If you need to get 
something done, need to know who to call, or want to understand how New 
York City and State government work, there is no one better than 
Robert.
  Robert has also been a fixture of New York Democratic politics. He 
has been a key advisor on my reelection campaigns and instrumental to 
their success, and he has served as a senior advisor and treasurer to 
numerous other campaigns, including Assemblywoman Linda Rosenthal's 
campaigns, Scott Stringer's mayoral campaign, and the campaign of my 
successor, Micah Lasher. He is the Democratic District Leader in the 
67th Assembly District, Part A, has long served as treasurer of my 
political club, the West Side Democrats (formerly the Community Free 
Democrats), and was a delegate to the 2024 Democratic National 
Convention.
  Outside of politics, Robert serves on the board of the Riverside Park 
Conservancy. He has been married for more than 30 years to his wife, 
Shari, whom he met at Binghamton University, and together they have a 
son, Dylan. Next to his family, Robert's great love is the New York 
Football Giants. On fall Sundays, the Gottheims can usually be found at 
a Giants home game, a family tradition going back generations.
  I thank Robert for his loyal service and his friendship. I look 
forward to seeing where his career takes him.
```
