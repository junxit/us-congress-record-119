# HONORING THE CITY OF CUDAHY, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE CITY OF CUDAHY, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Cudahy, California, a city I have represented in Congress for four 
years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  Cudahy's history is closely tied to the development of Southeast Los 
Angeles. From its early days as agricultural land in the 1900s to its 
incorporation in 1960, this city was shaped by the steady work ethic of 
the families who built their lives here. Serving this close-knit 
community has been a privilege.
  Because Cudahy's families work hard, I wanted to make sure federal 
resources reached the people who needed them most. I am proud that we 
secured $1.5 million to build affordable housing for low-income and 
unhoused seniors. This project supports more than 140 families with 
aging loved ones while contributing to the ongoing improvement of the 
Atlantic Avenue corridor. At the same time, we invested in local youth 
by backing $500,000 for the Lugo Park Teen Center Rehabilitation 
Project to upgrade lighting, safety, and programming spaces so Cudahy's 
young people have a reliable place to gather and learn.
  I thank every resident, teacher, business owner, and civic leader who 
welcomed me into Cudahy, including during visits near Clara Street Park 
and local schools. I leave with gratitude for their partnership and 
confidence in their future. I am honored to have represented this 
amazing city in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
