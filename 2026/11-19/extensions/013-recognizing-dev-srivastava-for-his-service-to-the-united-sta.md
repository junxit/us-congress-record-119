# RECOGNIZING DEV SRIVASTAVA FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
 RECOGNIZING DEV SRIVASTAVA FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE 
                           OF REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dev Srivastava for 
six years of exceptional service to my office and to the constituents 
of New York's 12th Congressional District.
  Born in Englewood and raised in Norwood, New Jersey, Dev graduated 
from Fordham University with degrees in Sociology and Architecture, 
where he studied how communities are built and how people live within 
them. That curiosity led him to New York University, where he earned a 
master's in social work in 2019. There he learned to listen closely and 
stay steady when someone else's situation felt overwhelming.
  In 2020, Dev joined my District Office as Congressional Caseworker, 
serving as a bridge between government and the people we represent. 
Whether the problem was a delayed benefit or a bureaucratic dead end, 
he took it head-on and saw it through. Every constituent who worked 
with Dev left knowing someone had their back.
  Over six years, Dev's casework reached nearly every corner of the 
federal government from FBI and SEC to the IRS and USCIS. There is 
hardly an agency he did not work with on behalf of a constituent, and 
he learned each one's rules, its people, and its pressure points. When 
a case stalled, Dev knew where to turn, and the answer was rarely no 
for long.
  He was especially effective on matters of finance, where constituents 
are often at their most vulnerable. When someone was defrauded or left 
stranded between banks and regulators who each pointed to the other, 
Dev found the step that moved everyone forward. In one such case, a 
constituent who had been turned away by multiple agencies saw every 
dollar returned within a week of Dev's involvement.
  Nowhere was his persistence more evident than in his work with the 
United States Postal Service. Dev helped countless individuals recover 
funds stolen through wire fraud, meeting each with patience and 
refusing to rest until they were made whole. His dedication earned him 
a nickname in our office, ``Mr. Mailbox.''
  Dev was indispensable, and his contributions reached well beyond his 
defined role. When a case went sideways, he stayed level-headed and 
kept things moving, never letting the pressure of the moment dull the 
care he brought to the person in front of him. My staff and the 
constituents we serve came to rely on the levity and warmth he brought 
to every interaction.
  Dev's time in my office was also marked by a happy milestone: he 
married his wife, Shannon. Through it all, he has remained steadfast in 
his devotion to his family and friends.
  As Dev moves on to the next professional chapter in his life, I wish 
him the best of luck and continued success. I thank him for his service 
and commend him for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
