# HONORING THE CITY OF BELL GARDENS, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
             HONORING THE CITY OF BELL GARDENS, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Bell Gardens, California, a city I have represented in Congress for 
four years but will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  When I think about serving Bell Gardens, what comes to mind is the 
energy of the people who call this city home. Partnering with local 
parents, educators, small business owners, community advocates, and 
elected officials has been a meaningful chapter of my public service.
  As one of California's most densely populated cities, Bell Gardens is 
built on family values and cultural traditions that strengthen our 
entire region. Knowing how important green space and reliable community 
facilities are here, I focused on delivering resources families can use 
every day. We directed $700,000 in federal funding to repair aging 
parks and modernize irrigation systems so public spaces stay green and 
usable. We also secured $250,000 to upgrade the Ford Park Gymnasium and 
Community Multi-Purpose Room, adding a much-needed HVAC system to a 
1950s facility so youth programs can run comfortably year-round while 
giving the city a dependable Resiliency Center during emergencies.
  From the schools and parks, to meeting amazing entrepreneurs at local 
businesses, I thank them for welcoming me into Bell Gardens. Though my 
official service is ending, my respect for this community remains 
strong. I am honored to have represented this amazing city in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
