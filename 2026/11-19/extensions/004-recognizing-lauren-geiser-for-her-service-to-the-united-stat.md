# RECOGNIZING LAUREN GEISER FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
RECOGNIZING LAUREN GEISER FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lauren Geiser for 
her eight years of outstanding service to the constituents of New 
York's 12th Congressional District.
  Lauren came to New York to receive her Master of Social Work from the 
Silver School of Social Work at New York University. She graduated in 
2014, eventually becoming a Licensed practitioner, and has since 
devoted her life to public service and advocating for the needs of 
others.
  She spent seven years at New York-based non-profits CASA and GEMS, 
becoming a trusted advocate for girls and women who had

[[Page E1253]]

experienced abuse, foster care placement, or sexual exploitation while 
providing them the tools of empowerment. She found herself drawn to the 
intersection of her clients lived experiences and the politics that 
shaped them and decided she wanted to pursue opportunities to 
effectuate change at the systemic level on a larger policy scale.
  Lauren joined my district office in 2018 first as a caseworker, 
getting promoted to Deputy District Director in 2020 where she oversaw 
our Constituent Services Department. Shortly after her promotion, 
Lauren adeptly navigated our casework and community relations teams 
throughout the COVID-19 pandemic. She not only kept track of the 
rapidly changing federal agency guidance and resources available to 
share with our constituents but also led our office's timely responses 
following a significant uptick in casework inquiries to our office. I 
know that whenever I refer a New York's 12 Congressional District's 
constituent to Lauren, be it to get Medicare coverage reinstated amid a 
dire situation or to expedite a visa so families can reunite after a 
years-long immigration process, she will do everything in her power to 
ensure a successful outcome.
  In addition to her invaluable contributions to our office as a 
caseworker, Lauren oversaw our District intern program for 
undergraduate and social work graduate students. Her calm, warm, and 
encouraging personality allowed our interns to seamlessly integrate 
themselves into the office. Her thoughtful assignments helped the 
dozens of interns that passed through my office during her tenure 
develop their skills in allowing constituents to feel heard and help 
them navigate the web of federal agencies.
  Lauren's love of service to others does not just extend to humans--
she is a true lover of animals and is a volunteer with equestrian 
therapy programs across the city. She also adores her two sons, Clank 
and LazIo, with whom she lives in Brooklyn along with her husband.
  As Lauren moves on to her next career step, I thank her for her 
dedicated service to the people of New York's 12th Congressional 
District as well as our entire staff.

                          ____________________
```
