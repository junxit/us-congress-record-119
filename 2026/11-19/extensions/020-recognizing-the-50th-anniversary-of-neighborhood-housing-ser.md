# RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING SERVICES OF GREATER BERKS, INC.

**Speaking:** Meuser, Daniel (M001204) [R-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING SERVICES OF 
                          GREATER BERKS, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DANIEL MEUSER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. MEUSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 50th 
anniversary of Neighborhood Housing Services of Greater Berks, Inc., 
which has served families and communities throughout Pennsylvania since 
its founding in Reading in 1977.
  Founded as a neighborhood improvement initiative, the organization 
has grown into a statewide resource dedicated to helping Pennsylvania 
families achieve and maintain homeownership. For five decades, the 
organization has provided education, financial guidance, affordable 
home loans, and assistance with down payments to help families overcome 
the challenges of purchasing a home. Its work extends beyond helping 
families become homeowners by providing continued support to help them 
remain in their homes and build long-term financial stability.
  Since 2017, the organization reports helping more than 2,100 families 
achieve homeownership, with approximately 97 percent of assisted 
families remaining in their homes. Its programs now reach all 67 
Pennsylvania counties, expanding the impact of an organization that 
began by serving neighborhoods in Reading. Through its partnerships and 
housing programs, the organization continues to help families access 
affordable homeownership opportunities while supporting the stability 
and growth of communities throughout the Commonwealth.
  On behalf of the United States House of Representatives and the 
citizens of Pennsylvania, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating Neighborhood Housing Services of Greater Berks, Inc. on 
this remarkable milestone and recognizing its continued contributions 
to Berks County and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
```
