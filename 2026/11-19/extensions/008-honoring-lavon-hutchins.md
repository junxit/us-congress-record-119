# HONORING LAVON HUTCHINS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING LAVON HUTCHINS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                              of missippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Ms. Lavon Hutchins of Indianola, Mississippi, an 
extraordinary woman whose unwavering faith, remarkable resilience, and 
lifelong commitment to serving others exemplify the very best of the 
Missippi Delta.
  A registered nurse for 39 years and director of M.K. Hutchins Academy 
of Healthcare, Ms. Hutchins has dedicated her professional life to 
caring for others and preparing future generations of healthcare 
professionals. Beyond her contributions to the healthcare field, she 
has demonstrated exceptional courage in overcoming breast cancer on 
three separate occasions.
  Ms. Hutchins first faced breast cancer in 1997 at the age of 30, 
undergoing surgery, chemotherapy, and radiation. When the disease 
returned in 2014, she confronted another difficult period of treatment 
with determination. In May 2025, she received a third diagnosis, this 
time of triple-negative breast cancer, requiring additional 
chemotherapy, surgery, and radiation treatments.
  Despite these tremendous challenges, Ms. Hutchins remained committed 
to her calling as an educator and healthcare professional. Throughout 
her treatment, she continued teaching nursing assistant classes in 
Greenville, Indianola, and Clarksdale, Mississippi, demonstrating her 
extraordinary dedication to her students and community.
  Supported by her husband, Antonio, her family, and organizations such 
as the Delta Cotton Belles and the Fannie Lou Hamer Foundation, Ms. 
Hutchins has drawn upon her faith and the compassion of others to 
sustain her through difficult times.
  Today, as a breast cancer survivor in remission, Ms. Hutchins uses 
her experiences to encourage and uplift others facing similar 
diagnosis. She is particularly passionate about reassuring younger 
women battling cancer, reminding them that their diagnosis does not 
define their future.
  Her story is not simply one of survival, but of service, purpose, and 
an unshakable commitment to making a difference in the lives of others. 
Through her work in healthcare education and her willingness to share 
her personal journey, Ms. Hutchins continues to be a source of hope and 
inspiration throughout the Mississippi Delta.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Ms. Lavon 
Hutchins for her courage in the face of adversity, her outstanding 
contributions to healthcare education, and her compassionate dedication 
to others. Her remarkable journey stands as a testament to the power of 
faith, preserverance, and a life devoted to service.
```
