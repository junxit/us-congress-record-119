# HONORING CHERYL FLEMING

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING CHERYL FLEMING

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Ms. Cheryl Fleming of Indianola, Mississippi, for her 
extraordinary courage, unwavering faith, and remarkable perseverance 
throughout her battle with breast cancer.
  Ms. Fleming, a dedicated supervisor at St. John's Care for 20 years, 
received her breast cancer diagnosis in January 2025 after noticing 
concerning changes in her health. What followed was an arduous journey 
that included 17 chemotherapy infusions, extensive treatment, and 
surgery. Despite the physical and emotional challenges she encountered, 
Ms. Fleming remained steadfast in her determination to overcome the 
disease.
  Throughout her journey, Ms. Fleming drew strength from her faith in 
God, the love of her family, and the encouragement of her community. 
Her children and brothers rallied around her, providing assistance and 
support during some of her most difficult moments. Remarkably, she 
continued working throughout her treatments, finding purpose and 
motivation in her dedication to others.
  Today, Ms. Fleming is in remission and has transformed her personal 
experience into a message of hope and awareness. Through her 
involvement with the Delta Cotton Belles breast cancer support 
organization, she has found encouragement among fellow survivors and 
countinues to advocate for early detection and greater attention to 
changes in one's health.
  Ms. Fleming's journey serves as a powerful reminder that faith, 
resilience, and the support of a caring community can provide strength 
during life's most difficult challenges. Her courage and determination 
are an inspiration to her family, her colleagues, and the entire 
Indianola community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. Cheryl 
Fleming for her remarkable strength, her enduring faith, and her 
commitment to encouraging others facing breast cancer. May her story 
continue to inspire hope, perseverance, and awareness throughout 
Mississippi and beyond.
```
