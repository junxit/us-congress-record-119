# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MARION SMITH

**Speaking:** Watson Coleman, Bonnie (W000822) [D-NJ]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MARION SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BONNIE WATSON COLEMAN

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mrs. WATSON COLEMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 100th 
birthday of my constituent, Marion Smith, on December 26, 2026.
  Ms. Smith is a lifelong New Jersey Resident, having served her 
community as a teacher in the Trenton Public School System until the 
age of 70. Her dedication to educating our youth continued for the next 
20 years in Trenton, New Jersey at Millhill Child Development Center. 
Beyond helping Trenton's youth, she is a beloved member of the NAACP 
Trenton Branch and the Shiloh Baptist Church.
  I thank her for her years of service in our community and 
congratulate her on this momentous milestone. Happy birthday.

                          ____________________
```
