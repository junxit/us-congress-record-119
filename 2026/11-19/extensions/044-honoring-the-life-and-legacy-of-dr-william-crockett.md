# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. WILLIAM CROCKETT

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. WILLIAM CROCKETT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 19, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of Dr. William Crockett of Mound Bayou, Mississippi, 
whose dedication to education, agriculture, leadership, and community 
service left a lasting impact on the Mississippi Delta.
  Dr. William Crockett's life was intertwined with the history and 
development of Mound Bayou, a community with a significant place in the 
story of African American independence, enterprise, and achievement. 
Through his work as an educator, superintendent, farmer, landowner, and 
fraternity member, he demonstrated the value of hard work, service, and 
a steadfast commitment to community advancement.
  Dr. Crockett served as superintendent of the Mound Bayou Public 
School District from 1992 until July 1998, approximately six years. In 
addition to his professional and agricultural pursuits, Dr. Crockett 
was a proud member of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc., an organization 
founded on the principles of manhood, scholarship, perseverance, and 
uplift. Through his fraternal affiliation and commitment to service, he 
carried forward a tradition of leadership, fellowship, and dedication 
to the betterment of others.
  His legacy serves as a reminder of the important role that educators, 
agricultural stewards, and community leaders play in preserving our 
history and preparing future generations for success. His contributions 
to Mound Bayou and the Mississippi Delta deserve recognition and 
respect. May his legacy continue to inspire future generations 
throughout Mississippi's 2nd Congressional District and beyond.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the memory of 
Dr. William Crockett for his outstanding service, dedication, and 
contributions to the State of Mississippi. We extend our deepest 
condolences to his family, friends, colleagues, and all who mourn his 
passing. May they find comfort in knowing that his life was one of 
purpose, service, and lasting impact.
```
