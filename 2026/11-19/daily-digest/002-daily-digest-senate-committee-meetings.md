# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the nominations of William Toti, of Florida, to be Under Secretary of 
the Navy, Daniel Zimmerman, of North Carolina, to be an Assistant 
Secretary, Richard Breckenridge, of New Hampshire, to be an Assistant 
Secretary of the Navy, and Arthur Miller, of the District of Columbia, 
to be an Assistant Secretary of the Navy, all of the Department of 
Defense, after the nominees testified and answered questions in their 
own behalf.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Committee ordered 
favorably reported the nominations of Jeremy Wiggins, of the District 
of Columbia, to be an Assistant Secretary of the Treasury, Matthew 
Jones, of Virginia, to be an Assistant Secretary of Housing and Urban 
Development, and David Slade, of Florida, to be First Vice President, 
and Michael Rigas, of Virginia, to be a Member of the Board of 
Directors, both of the Export-Import Bank of the United States.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine the nominations Mark Aesch, of Florida, to be 
Federal Transit Administrator, and Hugo Dante, Jr., of Alabama, to be 
Director, Office of Financial Research, and Rebekah Goshorn Jurata, of 
California, to be an Assistant Secretary, both of the Department of the 
Treasury, and Kristin N. Johnson, of Michigan, who was introduced by 
Senator Warnock, and Steven B. Levine, of Maryland, both to be a Member 
of the Securities and Exchange Commission, after the nominees testified 
and answered questions in their own behalf.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of Elliott Hulse, of California, to be Chief 
Executive Officer, Millennium Challenge Corporation, and Austin Holmes, 
of Florida, to be Ambassador to the Republic of Haiti, who was 
introduced by Senator Scott (FL), and Asa Pitt, of the District of 
Columbia, to be United States Representative to the Organization for 
the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons, both of the Department of State, 
after the nominees testified and answered questions in their own 
behalf.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine the nominations of Michael LoSapio, of 
New Jersey, to be Director of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure 
Security Agency, Department of Homeland Security, Eric Matthew Ueland, 
of Virginia, to be Comptroller General of the United States, Michael 
Chamberlain, of Virginia, to be Director of the Office of Government 
Ethics, Robert Steffens, of Texas, to be a Governor of the United 
States Postal Service, Jeffrey Zisk, of Texas, to be a Commissioner of 
the Postal Regulatory Commission, Mark Priebe, of Virginia, to be 
Inspector General, Department of Education, Dennis Kirk, of Virginia, 
to be Inspector General, Department of the Interior, Maria D' Avanzo, 
of New York, to be Inspector General, General Services Administration, 
Robert Steinau, of Maryland, to be Inspector General, National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration, and Eric Sebastian Glover, 
Fernando Campoamor-Sanchez, Roman Marian Chaban, and Bernard Michael 
Desrosiers, all of the District of Columbia, each to be an Associate 
Judge of the Superior Court of the District of Columbia, after the 
nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
```
