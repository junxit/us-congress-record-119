# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 10307, H.R. 10458, H.R. 10751, H.R. 10956

```
Committee Meetings
STRENGTHENING RURAL AMERICA THROUGH THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE 
RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS AND PARTNERSHIPS
Committee on Agriculture: Subcommittee on Commodity Markets, Digital 
Assets, and Rural Development held a hearing entitled ``Strengthening 
Rural America Through USDA Rural Development Programs and 
Partnerships''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Ethics: Full Committee held a business meeting to consider 
adopting changes to the Rules of the Committee pursuant to House Rule 
XI, clause 2(a)(1)(A). Changes to the Rules of the Committee pursuant 
to House Rule XI, clause 2(a)(1)(A) were adopted.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a business meeting on 
legislation on the Export Control Reform Act Disclosure.
BEYOND THE STORM: ASSESSING FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY MISSION 
READINESS
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Emergency Management 
and Technology held a hearing entitled ``Beyond the Storm: Assessing 
FEMA's Mission Readiness''. Testimony was heard from Cameron Hamilton, 
Administrator, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of 
Homeland Security.
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Indian and Insular 
Affairs held a hearing on H.R. 10307, the ``Anaktuvuk Pass Food 
Security Access Act''; H.R. 10458, the ``Grand Ronde Land Transfer Act 
of 2026''; H.R. 10751, the ``Tribal Oral Health Sovereignty Act''; and 
H.R. 10956, the ``IHS Workforce Parity Act of 2026''. Testimony was 
heard from Representatives Begich, Salinas, Bergman, and Kennedy of 
Utah; and public witnesses.
WEATHERING THE SOLAR STORM: ADVANCING AMERICA'S SPACE WEATHER 
CAPABILITIES
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Full Committee held a 
hearing entitled ``Weathering the Solar Storm: Advancing America's 
Space Weather Capabilities''. Testimony was heard from Joseph Westlake, 
Director, Heliophysics Division, Science Mission Directorate, National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration; and Ken Graham, Director, 
National Weather Service, National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
Administration, Department of Commerce.
BEYOND GO-LIVE: VETERANS AFFAIRS ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORD MODERNIZATION 
STATUS UPDATE
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Beyond Go-Live: VA Electronic Health Record Modernization Status 
Update''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
