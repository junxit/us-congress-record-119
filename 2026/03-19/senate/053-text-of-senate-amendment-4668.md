# Text of Senate Amendment 4668

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

```
  SA 4668. Ms. MURKOWSKI submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 4420 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Schmitt) to the 
bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee on Equal 
Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

                     TITLE IV--NO UNFUNDED MANDATES

     SEC. 401. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

       There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as are 
     necessary for the purpose of reimbursing States for carrying 
     out the provisions of and amendments made by this Act.
                                 ______
```
