# Text of Senate Amendment 4661

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

```
  SA 4661. Ms. MURKOWSKI submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 4420 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Schmitt) to the 
bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee on Equal 
Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

        On page 6, line 13, insert ``This subsection and any other 
     provision of this Act relating to the requirement to provide 
     documentary proof of United States citizenship shall not 
     apply with respect to an individual born before December 31, 
     1960.'' after ``application.''.
                                 ______
```
