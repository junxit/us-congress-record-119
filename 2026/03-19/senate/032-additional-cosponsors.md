# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 142, S. 177, S. 475, S. 545, S.Res. 645, S. 649, S. 844, S. 978, S. 1224, S. 1918, S. 2541, S. 2667, S. 2937, S. 2938, S. 3108, S. 3138, S. 3211, S. 3387, S. 3655, S. 3778, S. 3855, S. 3868, S. 4051, S. 4073, S. 4083, S. 4127, S. 4130

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 142

  At the request of Mr. Barrasso, the name of the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mr. Ricketts) was added as a cosponsor of S. 142, a bill to award a 
Congressional Gold Medal to wildland firefighters in recognition of 
their strength, resiliency, sacrifice, and service to protect the 
forests, grasslands, and communities of the United States, and for 
other purposes.


                                 S. 177

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the name of the Senator from Wyoming 
(Mr. Barrasso) was added as a cosponsor of S. 177, a bill to prohibit 
Federal funding of Planned Parenthood Federation of America.


                                 S. 475

  At the request of Mr. Tillis, the names of the Senator from Illinois 
(Ms. Duckworth) and the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Marshall) were added 
as cosponsors of S. 475, a bill to amend title XVIII of the Social 
Security Act to ensure appropriate access to non-opioid pain management 
drugs under part D of the Medicare program.


                                 S. 545

  At the request of Mr. Grassley, the name of the Senator from 
Tennessee (Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 545, a bill 
to prohibit certain uses of xylazine, and for other purposes.
  At the request of Ms. Cortez Masto, the name of the Senator from 
Texas (Mr. Cornyn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 545, supra.


                                 S. 649

  At the request of Mr. Moran, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 649, a bill to 
amend title 38, United States Code, to expand eligibility for Post-9/11 
Educational Assistance to members of the National Guard who perform 
certain full-time duty, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 844

  At the request of Mr. Booker, the names of the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Bennet) and the Senator from New York (Mrs. Gillibrand) were added 
as cosponsors of S. 844, a bill to accelerate workplace time-to-
contract under the National Labor Relations Act.


                                 S. 978

  At the request of Mrs. Moody, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 978, a bill to 
amend the National Housing Act to establish a mortgage insurance 
program for first responders, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1224

  At the request of Mr. Cotton, the name of the Senator from West 
Virginia (Mrs. Capito) was withdrawn as a cosponsor of S. 1224, a bill 
to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the firearm 
transfer tax, and for other purposes.
  At the request of Mr. Cotton, the name of the Senator from Wyoming 
(Mr. Barrasso) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1224, supra.


                                S. 1918

  At the request of Mr. Boozman, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1918, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow a refundable tax 
credit against income tax for the purchase of qualified access 
technology for the blind.


                                S. 2541

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from 
Maryland (Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2541, a bill 
to establish requirements for quality and discard date phrases that are 
voluntarily declared on the food label to display calendar dates.


                                S. 2667

  At the request of Mr. Booker, the name of the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Ms. Baldwin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2667, a bill to prevent 
violence in the West Bank and authorize the imposition of sanctions 
with respect to any foreign person endangering United States national 
security and undermining prospects for a two-state solution by 
committing illegal violent acts.


                                S. 2937

  At the request of Mr. Hawley, the name of the Senator from Tennessee 
(Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2937, a bill to 
establish legal standards for advanced artificial intelligence 
products.


                                S. 2938

  At the request of Mr. Hawley, the name of the Senator from Tennessee 
(Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2938, a bill to require 
the Secretary of Energy to establish the Advanced Artificial 
Intelligence Evaluation Program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3108

  At the request of Mr. Hawley, the name of the Senator from Tennessee 
(Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3108, a bill to require 
reports regarding artificial intelligence-related job impacts, and for 
other purposes.


                                S. 3138

  At the request of Mr. Banks, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3138, a bill to 
amend title 38, United States Code, to include adaptive prostheses and 
terminal devices for sports and other recreational activities in the 
medical services furnished to eligible veterans by the Secretary of 
Veterans Affairs.


                                S. 3211

  At the request of Ms. Hassan, the name of the Senator from New Jersey 
(Mr. Kim) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3211, a bill to require the 
Office of Management and Budget to consider revising the Standard 
Occupational Classification system to establish a separate code for 
direct support professionals, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3387

  At the request of Mr. Gallego, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3387, a bill to 
prohibit certain uses of automated decision systems to inform 
individualized prices, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3655

  At the request of Mr. Merkley, the name of the Senator from Indiana 
(Mr. Banks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3655, a bill to require the 
President to remove the extension of certain privileges, exemptions, 
and immunities to the Hong Kong Economic and Trade Offices if Hong Kong 
no longer enjoys a high degree of autonomy from the People's Republic 
of China, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3778

  At the request of Ms. Cantwell, the name of the Senator from Arizona 
(Mr. Kelly) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3778, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to expand the carbon oxide sequestration 
credit to include solid or liquid carbon capture facilities.


                                S. 3855

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the name of the Senator from 
Nevada (Ms. Rosen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3855, a bill to 
enhance bilateral defense cooperation between the United States and 
Israel, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3868

  At the request of Mr. Lee, the name of the Senator from California 
(Mr. Padilla) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3868, a bill to direct the 
Attorney General to submit to the Congress a report on Federal criminal 
offenses, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4051

  At the request of Ms. Collins, the names of the Senator from Arkansas 
(Mr. Boozman) and the Senator from Nevada (Ms. Rosen) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 4051, a bill to require the Secretary of the Treasury 
to mint and issue coins that are emblematic of the National Fallen 
Firefighters Memorial and the service and sacrifice of firefighters 
throughout the history of the United States.


                                S. 4073

                                S. 4127
```
