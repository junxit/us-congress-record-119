# Text of Senate Amendment 4675

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

```
  SA 4675. Ms. HIRONO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 4420 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Schmitt) to the bill 
S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee on Equal Access, 
and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       After section 1, insert the following:

     SEC. 2. FINDINGS.

       Congress finds that, according to the Cato Institute, a 
     libertarian policy research organization, investigations 
     conducted into the integrity of elections have found that 
     voting by noncitizens in the United States elections is 
     virtually nonexistent.
                                 ______
```
