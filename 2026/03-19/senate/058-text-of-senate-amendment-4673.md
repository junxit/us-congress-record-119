# Text of Senate Amendment 4673

**Speaking:** Gallego, Ruben (G000574) [D-AZ]

```
  SA 4673. Mr. GALLEGO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory 
Committee on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. TREATMENT OF LICENSES RELATING TO THE SALE OF 
                   IRANIAN OIL.

       On and after the date of the enactment of this Act, any 
     license issued by the Office of Foreign Assets Control of the 
     Department of the Treasury after the initiation of 
     hostilities with Iran on February 28, 2026, relating to the 
     sale of Iranian oil shall have no force or effect.
                                 ______
```
