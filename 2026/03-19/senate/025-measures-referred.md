# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 556, H.R. 1958, H.R. 4638

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 556. An act to prohibit the Secretary of the Interior 
     and the Secretary of Agriculture from prohibiting the use of 
     lead ammunition or tackle on certain Federal land or water 
     under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Interior and 
     the Secretary of Agriculture, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Environment and Public Works.
       H.R. 1958. An act to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to clarify that aliens who have been convicted of 
     defrauding the United States Government or the unlawful 
     receipt of public benefits are inadmissible and deportable; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       H.R. 4638. An act to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to provide that an alien who has been convicted of 
     harming animals used in law enforcement is inadmissible and 
     deportable, and for other purposes; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
