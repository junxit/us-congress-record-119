# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God of love, give our lawmakers wisdom to know what they ought to 
do. Create in them a passion to seek the truth, the humility to accept 
advice, and the courage to act with integrity. When they don't know 
which step to take, give them the wisdom to pray, ``Thy kingdom come. 
Thy will be done on earth, as it is in heaven.'' May Your wisdom 
motivate them to faithfully follow Your commands.
  Lord, empower each of them with the grace to seek and to find, to 
know and to love, to obey and to live the truth.
  And, Lord, comfort those who weep.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
