# HONORING THE LIFE AND MEMORY OF IAN FRANK TORTORICI

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND MEMORY OF IAN FRANK TORTORICI

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 24, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
memory of Ian Frank Tortorici. Ian lived a life defined by service, 
courage, and an enduring willingness to stand with others in their time 
of need.
  A decorated wrestler at Laguna Hills High School, Ian went on to 
serve his country as a United States Marine, attaining the rank of 
Corporal. He later continued his commitment to public service as a 
federal law enforcement officer.
  When Russia invaded Ukraine, Ian chose once again to answer the call 
to serve. He traveled to Ukraine in March 2022 and joined the 
International Legion, where he fought alongside Ukrainian forces for 
more than a year. Serving first as a machine gunner and ultimately as a 
team leader, Ian repeatedly placed himself in harm's way for the people 
beside him and for a cause he believed was worth defending.
  On June 27, 2023, Ian was killed in a ballistic missile attack in 
Kramatorsk, Ukraine. He was 32 years old.
  Ian's sacrifice was the culmination of a life spent serving others. 
He had already dedicated himself to protecting his fellow Americans, 
yet when faced with a distant war and people fighting for their homes 
and freedom, he was willing to go once more into danger.
  He should be remembered not simply for how he died, but for how he 
lived: with courage, conviction, and a profound sense of duty. His 
sacrifice is a lasting testament to the kind of person who chooses to 
stand beside others when standing with them comes at the greatest cost.
  His memory continues to be honored by those who knew him, served 
alongside him, and remember his dedication to his country, his fellow 
service members, and the people of Ukraine.
```
