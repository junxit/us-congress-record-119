# HONORING THE LIFE OF DAWN MARSONOSKI

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF DAWN MARSONOSKI

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 24, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
memory of Dawn Marsonoski, a longtime resident of Bristol Borough, 
Bucks County, Pennsylvania, who passed away on August 13, 2026, at the 
age of 54.
  Born at Lower Bucks Hospital, Dawn was a proud Bristol Borough 
resident and a graduate of Harry S. Truman High School, Class of 1990. 
She went on to earn her master's degree from Holy Family University and 
built a successful career.
  Dawn was a member of St. Mark Church and was well known throughout 
her community for her generous heart, kindness, and unwavering support 
for those around her. She found great happiness in caring for her 
beloved pets and took great pride in making her home warm, welcoming, 
and beautiful.
  Above all, Dawn was a devoted mother whose children and family were 
the center of her world. Her love for her family brought her tremendous 
joy, and her kindness and dedication will be remembered by all who had 
the privilege of knowing her. Her loved ones will continue to cherish 
her memory.
  I extend my deepest condolences to Dawn's family and am honored to 
recognize the lasting impact she had on her family, her friends, and 
the Bristol Borough community.

                          ____________________
```
