# HONORING FIRE CHIEF LATOSHA CLEMONS

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

```
                  HONORING FIRE CHIEF LATOSHA CLEMONS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 24, 2026

  Ms. LOIS FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Fire 
Chief Latosha Clemons and celebrate a remarkable career dedicated to 
public service.
  For 24 years, Chief Clemons served the Boynton Beach Fire Rescue 
Department, where she broke barriers as the first African American 
woman to join its ranks. Throughout her career, she has exemplified 
courage, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to protecting the 
health, safety, and well-being of her community. Her record of service 
reflects the very best of public leadership.
  Recruited by then-Fire Chief Floyd Jordan, Boynton Beach's first 
African American Fire Chief, Clemons began her career as a probationary 
firefighter and quickly established herself as a leader. Through hard 
work, determination, and exceptional performance, she rose through 
every level of the department--serving as Paramedic, Operator Engineer, 
Lieutenant, Captain, and Battalion Chief before taking on senior 
leadership roles as both Deputy Chief of Administration and Deputy 
Chief of Operations. At every stage, Chief Clemons earned recognition 
as one of the department's top candidates for advancement.
  In a profession where women make up only about 9 percent of 
firefighters nationwide--and Black women represent an even smaller 
share--Chief Clemons has opened doors for others while demonstrating 
what is possible through perseverance, skill, and leadership. She 
continues to serve as a powerful role model for women and girls 
considering careers in public service and emergency response. In 
addition to her extensive work in the field, Chief Clemons volunteers 
her time by hosting a free camp for girls to get them interested in the 
fire service.
  Her commitment to service reaches beyond the firehouse. As Interim 
City Manager for the City of Forest Park, Chief Clemens also oversees 
the daily operations of municipal government and brings the same steady 
leadership, sound judgment, and dedication to excellence that defines 
her firefighting career. The confidence placed in her reflects both the 
respect she commands and the breadth of experience she brings to every 
challenge.
  Today, I am proud to recognize Chief Clemons for her extraordinary 
contributions, her groundbreaking achievements, and her enduring 
commitment to serving others. Her leadership has strengthened every 
community she has touched, and her example will continue to inspire 
future generations of public servants.
```
