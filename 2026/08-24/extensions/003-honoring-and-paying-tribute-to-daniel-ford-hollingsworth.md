# HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO DANIEL FORD HOLLINGSWORTH

**Speaking:** Calvert, Ken (C000059) [R-CA]

```
        HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO DANIEL FORD HOLLINGSWORTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KEN CALVERT

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 24, 2026

  Mr. CALVERT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to my 
dear friend Dan Hollingsworth who passed away on Sunday, July 26, 2026. 
Dan was a leader in Riverside County's agriculture industry, a former 
staff member for Representative Victor Veysey, and a dedicated patriot 
who was involved in Republican Party politics for most of his life. He 
will be deeply missed.
  Dan was born in Grand Junction, Colorado. At a young age, his family 
headed west and moved to Pomona, California. Dan graduated from Pomona 
College in Pomona High School in 1953 and attended Cal Poly San Luis 
Obispo, where he earned a Bachelors Degree in Dairy Science. During 
college he also met Joan Clark, who he would marry on December 11, 
1955. Just last year, Dan and Joan celebrated their 70th anniversary. 
Dan started working on a dairy farm near San Luis Obispo while he was 
in college, then worked at various dairies in the Central Valley and 
Chino before buying a small dairy in San Jacinto in 1960. ``Dan's 
Dairy'' was a cash `n' carry dairy where Dan milked fifty cows and Joan 
helped bottle the milk.
  In 1969, Dan went to work for U.S. Representative Victor Veysey, the 
first of many roles he would fill in service to his conservative 
beliefs and the Republican Party. Dan served as Chairman of the 
Riverside County Central Committee twice and joined me as a founding 
director of the Lincoln Club of Riverside County. Dan volunteered in 
leadership roles in agriculture as well. He began serving on the 
Riverside County Farm Bureau Board of Directors in the mid-1960s, a 
position he filled until his passing. In 1978, Dan bought another dairy 
in San Jacinto, milking over 600 cows. In 1986, he left the dairy 
business again, and he and Joan bought land in Murrieta and started a 
wholesale tree nursery, which supplied large trees to the region for 39 
years.
  Dan is survived by his wife, Joan; his daughters, Tina Spurgin, Marcy 
Preston (Larry) and sons, Lennie (Heidi) and Dennis (Natalie) 
Hollingsworth. He is also survived by his brothers Bob and Charles 
Hollingsworth, seventeen grandchildren and step-grandchildren, and 
twenty-seven great-grandchildren and step-great grandchildren. Although 
he may be gone, the many contributions Dan made to our region and his 
family will have a lasting impact.
```
