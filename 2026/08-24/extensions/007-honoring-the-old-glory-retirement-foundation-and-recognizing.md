# HONORING THE OLD GLORY RETIREMENT FOUNDATION AND RECOGNIZING SEPTEMBER 12 AS OLD GLORY RETIREMENT FOUNDATION DAY

**Speaking:** Landsman, Greg (L000601) [D-OH]

```
HONORING THE OLD GLORY RETIREMENT FOUNDATION AND RECOGNIZING SEPTEMBER 
               12 AS OLD GLORY RETIREMENT FOUNDATION DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GREG LANDSMAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 24, 2026

  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Old Glory 
Retirement Foundation, a veteran-founded nonprofit in Franklin, Ohio 
dedicated to ensuring that our Nation's flag, ''Old Glory,'' is treated 
with dignity and respect throughout its entire life of service, while 
also honoring the Veterans, first responders, and families who have 
sacrificed in service to our Nation and communities.
  On July 4, 2026, as our Nation celebrated its 250th Anniversary, the 
Old Glory Retirement Foundation brought together Veterans, volunteers, 
civic leaders, and members of the community at Horsepower Farm in 
Middletown, Ohio, to conduct an extraordinary American flag retirement 
ceremony. During the ceremony, 17,169 unserviceable American flags were 
respectfully retired in a single event. The foundation believes this 
event to be the largest American flag retirement ceremony ever 
conducted. This remarkable community effort demonstrated the deep 
respect and patriotism that Americans continue to hold for our Nation's 
flag.
  The foundation's mission extends beyond flag retirement. Through its 
ceremonies and educational programs, the Old Glory Retirement 
Foundation works to teach proper flag etiquette, preserve the stories 
of Veterans, recognize first responders, and inspire younger 
generations to understand the meaning behind the flag they proudly see 
every day.
  It is also fitting to recognize September 12 as Old Glory Retirement 
Foundation Day. Following the tragedy of September 11, 2001, September 
12 became a powerful reminder of the unity, compassion, and patriotism 
demonstrated by Americans across the country. People from every 
background came together, setting aside their differences and standing 
united under one flag.
  The City of Middletown has officially proclaimed September 12 as Old 
Glory Retirement Foundation Day, recognizing both this spirit of unity 
and the foundation's commitment to preserving the dignity of the 
American flag and honoring those who have served our Nation and 
communities.
  I am proud to recognize Old Glory Retirement Foundation, its 
dedicated Veterans and volunteers, and the countless members of the 
Middletown community who came together to honor the American flag. 
Their commitment to service, patriotism, and community serves as an 
example of the enduring values that unite us as Americans.
```
