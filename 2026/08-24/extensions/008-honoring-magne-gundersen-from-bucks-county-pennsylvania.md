# HONORING MAGNE GUNDERSEN FROM BUCKS COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
        HONORING MAGNE GUNDERSEN FROM BUCKS COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 24, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Magne 
Gundersen for decades of dedicated service to Scouting America and The 
Washington Crossing Council, transforming lives of young people, 
families volunteers, and communities he has served throughout his 
distinguished career.
  For 40 years, Mr. Gundersen has devoted himself to the mission of 
Scouting and to helping young people develop the character, leadership, 
confidence, and sense of service that will guide them throughout their 
lives. As Scout Executive and CEO of the Washington Crossing Council, 
he has provided steady and principled leadership to a council serving 
communities across Pennsylvania and New Jersey.
  Those who have worked alongside Mr. Gundersen describe him as someone 
who leads by example. His career reflects not only professional 
excellence, but a genuine commitment to the people and communities 
entrusted to his care.
  Mr. Gundersen's impact will be measured not simply by the years he 
has served, but by the countless Scouts, families, volunteers, and 
fellow leaders whose lives have been strengthened by his dedication. 
His leadership has helped preserve and advance an institution 
responsible for supporting the next generation of leaders in their 
communities.
  As Magne Gundersen concludes this remarkable 40-year chapter of 
service, we extend our sincere gratitude for his dedication, 
leadership, and commitment to America's youth. His legacy will continue 
through the generations of Scouts and volunteers whose lives he has 
touched
  I congratulate Mr. Gundersen on his retirement and wish him every 
success and happiness in the years ahead. Our Nation is stronger 
because of citizens like Mr. Gundersen.
```
