# CONGRATULATING THE ELIZABETHTOWN AREA HIGH SCHOOL'S ESPORTS TEAM ON ITS SECOND STRAIGHT STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
CONGRATULATING THE ELIZABETHTOWN AREA HIGH SCHOOL'S ESPORTS TEAM ON ITS 
                   SECOND STRAIGHT STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 17, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and congratulate the 
Elizabethtown Area High School Esports team on securing its second 
consecutive Pennsylvania Interscholastic Esports Association state 
championship. The outstanding team of Peter Dunbar, Madalyn Guerrisi, 
Jackson Langlois, Anthony Lazzari Jaymian Ortiz, and Liam Shriner 
earned first place competing in Marvel Rivals, a superhero shooter game 
inspired by the Marvel Comics franchise. I also commend their coach, 
Mr. Austin Henderson, whose leadership and guidance were instrumental 
in achieving this remarkable victory.
  In addition to its impressive performance, the Elizabethtown Area 
High School Esports Team received an $80,000 state grant. This funding 
will allow the team to purchase gaming computers, consoles and 
furniture to establish a dedicated esports arena within the high 
school, providing a permanent space for practices and tournaments.
  I commend the Elizabethtown Area High School Esports Team for this 
exceptional achievement and the dedication its members have 
demonstrated. Programs like this foster teamwork, technical skills, and 
innovation among students, while strengthening educational 
opportunities across our District. I applaud the team's championship 
victory and wish them continued success.
```
