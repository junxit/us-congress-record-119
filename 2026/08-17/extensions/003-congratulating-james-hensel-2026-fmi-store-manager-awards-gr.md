# CONGRATULATING JAMES HENSEL, 2026 FMI STORE MANAGER AWARDS GRAND PRIZE WINNER

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
CONGRATULATING JAMES HENSEL, 2026 FMI STORE MANAGER AWARDS GRAND PRIZE 
                                 WINNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, August 17, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate and recognize James 
Hensel, store manager of Oregon Dairy in Lititz, Pennsylvania, as a 
2026 FMI Store Manager Awards Grand Prize Winner.
  The FMI Store Manager Awards, given by FMI, The Food Industry 
Association, recognize outstanding grocery store managers who generate 
sales growth, communicate effectively, lead strong teams, provide 
exceptional customer service, and contribute to their communities.
  James Hensel was one of three American small retail managers chosen 
for this prestigious award. James has worked for the family-owned 
Oregon Dairy store for 35 years, beginning as produce manager and 
perishable manager before being promoted to store manager three years 
ago.
  During his time with Oregon Dairy, James created a full-scale lawn 
and garden department, expanded Oregon Dairy's private brand program, 
helped launch their highly profitable sushi station, and implemented 
its loyalty program. With the knowledge and contributions that he 
provides, James is an integral member of the Oregon Dairy staff and is 
seen as a mentor by the rest of the team.
  Having dedicated 35 years to making Oregon Dairy one of the premier 
retailers in the region, James is a fitting recipient of the 2026 FMI 
Store Manager Award. I am pleased that both the store and James are 
receiving nationwide recognition, and I look forward to his continued 
impact on our community.
```
