# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Stephanie Vader, Capitol Hill United Methodist Church, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Holy One, here in the middle of August, we give thanks for the gift 
of tomatoes that have grown sweeter by gathering all the light they 
needed. Sweet tomatoes ask us: Will you choose this and every day to be 
a person who gathers light?
  Holy One, we know that choosing to be a gatherer of light does not 
mean everything will be resolved, cruelty will end, or that greed does 
not eat first at many tables. Help us choose to join those who daily 
make choices that set the oppressed free and proclaim release to the 
captives.
  Holy One, You remind us that it is easier to preach fear, vengeance, 
and despair than it is to preach resurrection. It is easier to destroy 
than to create. It is easier to curse than to honor and build up.
  Empower all of us, including the Members of this House, to choose the 
harder way, to choose the way of creation rather than destruction, and 
to choose the way of healing what is broken.
  Holy One, may we keep choosing to be gatherers of light as we 
practice resurrection.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
