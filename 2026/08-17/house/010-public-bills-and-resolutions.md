# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 1484, H.R. 10109, H.R. 10110, H.R. 10111, H.R. 10112, H.R. 10113, H.R. 10114, H.R. 10115, H.R. 10116, H.R. 10117

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BAUMGARTNER:
       H.R. 10109. A bill to amend the Commodity Exchange Act to 
     prohibit wildfire event contracts on prediction markets, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Agriculture, and in 
     addition to the Committee on the Judiciary, for a period to 
     be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Ms. CASTOR of Florida:
       H.R. 10110. A bill to require the disclosure of algorithmic 
     price fixing in the housing rental market, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. DELUZIO (for himself, Ms. Dean of Pennsylvania, 
             Ms. Lee of Pennsylvania, Mr. Evans of Pennsylvania, 
             and Ms. Scanlon):
       H.R. 10111. A bill to prohibit the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security from preventing Members of Congress, or employees 
     accompanying a Member, from entering, for the purpose of 
     conducting oversight, any facility operated by or for the 
     Department of Homeland Security used to detain or otherwise 
     house aliens, and for other purposes; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary, and in addition to the Committee on Homeland 
     Security, for a period to be subsequently determined by the 
     Speaker, in each case for consideration of such provisions as 
     fall within the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER (for himself and Mr. Nunn of Iowa):
       H.R. 10112. A bill to amend the Investor Protection and 
     Securities Reform Act of 2010 to provide grants to States for 
     enhanced protection of senior investors and senior 
     policyholders, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Financial Services.
           By Mrs. HOUCHIN:
       H.R. 10113. A bill to make certain revisions to the 
     Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Education and Workforce.
           By Mr. MIN (for himself, Mr. Thompson of California, 
             Mr. Issa, Ms. Tokuda, Ms. Maloy, Ms. Waters, Mr. 
             Gallagher, Mr. Sherman, Ms. King-Hinds, Mr. Takano, 
             Mrs. Radewagen, Ms. Chu, Mr. Finstad, Mr. Levin, Mr. 
             Kiley of California, Ms. Lofgren, and Mrs. Kim):
       H.R. 10114. A bill to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 15642 Sand Canyon Avenue in 
     Irvine, California, as the ``Mary Kageyama Nomura Post Office 
     Building''; to the Committee on Oversight and Government 
     Reform.
           By Mr. MOYLAN (for himself, Mr. Bell, Ms. Leger 
             Fernandez, Ms. Pettersen, Ms. King-Hinds, Ms. Titus, 
             Mr. Vasquez, Mr. Stanton, Ms. Budzinski, Mr. Neguse, 
             and Mr. Horsford):
       H.R. 10115. A bill to amend the Radiation Exposure 
     Compensation Act to provide for certain claims related to 
     atmospheric testing, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary, and in addition to the Committee on Energy 
     and Commerce, for a period to be subsequently determined by 
     the Speaker, in each case for consideration of such 
     provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the committee 
     concerned.
           By Ms. SALINAS (for herself, Mr. Evans of Colorado, Ms. 
             Davids of Kansas, and Mr. Kean):
       H.R. 10116. A bill to amend title 23, United States Code, 
     to establish an EMS and First Responder Wellness Grant 
     Program, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
           By Mr. STEUBE:
       H.R. 10117. A bill to require the Comptroller General of 
     the United States to carry out a study regarding certain 
     reptiles, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Natural 
     Resources.
           By Ms. McCOLLUM (for herself, Mr. Thompson of 
             Pennsylvania, Mr. Case, Ms. Norton, and Mr. 
             Thanedar):
       H. Res. 1484. A resolution recognizing the nonprofit sector 
     and the contributions of nonprofit employees, and expressing 
     support for August 17, 2026, to be designated as ``National 
     Nonprofit Day''; to the Committee on Oversight and Government 
     Reform.
```
