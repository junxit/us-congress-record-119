# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 5, H.Res. 15, H.Res. 37, H.Res. 38, S.Res. 133, H.Res. 170, H.Res. 237, H.Res. 343, H.Res. 369, H.Res. 401

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-125. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Louisiana, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 343, memorializing the United States Congress 
     to oppose any effort to transfer, privatize, divest, or 
     otherwise remove portions of the Kisatchie National Forest 
     located within Grant Parish from federal public ownership; to 
     the Committee on Agriculture.
       ML-126. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 133, urging the 
     President of the United States and the U.S. Department of 
     Agriculture to fully honor its commitments to Michigan Rural 
     Energy for America participating farmers and to reimburse 
     projects built in good faith under REAP guidance; to the 
     Committee on Agriculture.
       ML-127. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     California, relative to Senate Joint Resolution No. 5, urging 
     the United States Congress to explore advancing innovative 
     solutions in enteric methane emission reduction while 
     preserving the economic stability of the cattle industries; 
     to the Committee on Agriculture.
       ML-128. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 237, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to include in the 
     National Defense Authorization Act a provision directing the 
     Department of Defense to furnish to the United States Census 
     Bureau the exact number of military personnel, civilian 
     employees, and dependent family members residing on each 
     military installation in the contiguous United States during 
     each decennial census; to the Committee on Armed Services.
       ML-129. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 37, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to issue the Armed Forces Expeditionary 
     Medal to U.S. veterans who served Honduras from 1981 to 1992; 
     to the Committee on Armed Services.
       ML-130. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 401, 
     memorializing the Congress of the United States to prioritize 
     research, clinical trials, and treatment development related 
     to the cure of Sanfilippo syndrome; to the Committee on 
     Energy and Commerce.
       ML-131. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 15, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to pass the SAVE America Act requiring voter 
     identification for federal elections; to the Committee on 
     House Administration.
       ML-132. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 170, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to facilitate the future reconstruction of 
     the segment of United Sates Highway 90 in St. Tammany Parish 
     by taking such actions as are necessary to secure ten million 
     dollars in funding to be provided to the Louisiana Department 
     of Transportation and Rural Development and from the fiscal 
     year 2027 Transportation, Housing, and Urban Development 
     appropriations request for purposes of removing four closed 
     bridges on United States Highway 90 in St. Tammany Parish; to 
     the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
       ML-133. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 369, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     are necessary to pass legislation allowing taxpayers to 
     establish tax-advantaged catastrophe savings accounts for 
     disaster-related expenses; to the Committee on Ways and 
     Means.
       ML-134. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 38, 
     memorializing the Untied States Congress to fully

[[Page H5245]]

     repeal and rewrite every word of the USA Patriot Act to limit 
     the authority granted under the FISA and FISC with regard to 
     warrantless searches and investigations of United States 
     citizens and does hereby implore congress to turn its 
     attention to the rights of the free people of the United 
     States of America; jointly to the Committees on the Judiciary 
     and Intelligence (Permanent Select).

                          ____________________
```
