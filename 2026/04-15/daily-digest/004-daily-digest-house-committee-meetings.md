# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 6654, H.R. 7280, H.R. 7683, H.R. 7950, H.R. 8010, H.R. 8041, H.R. 8043, H.R. 8052

```
Committee Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Energy and Water 
Development and Related Agencies held a budget hearing on the 
Department of Energy. Testimony was heard from Chris Wright, Secretary, 
Department of Energy.
MEMBER DAY
Committee on Armed Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Member Day''. Testimony was heard from Representatives Amo, Baird, 
Barrett, Budzinski, Carter of Louisiana, Castor of Florida, Feenstra, 
Garcia of Texas, Vicente Gonzalez of Texas, Hernandez, Johnson of 
Texas, Kaptur, Kennedy of New York, Lee of Nevada, Leger Fernandez, 
Lucas, Pfluger, Randall, Scholten, Titus, Yakym, Biggs of South 
Carolina, Bilirakis, James, and Scanlon.
FY27 MISSILE DEFENSE AND MISSILE DEFEAT PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces held a 
hearing entitled ``FY27 Missile Defense and Missile Defeat Programs and 
Activities''. Testimony was heard from Marc Berkowitz, Assistant 
Secretary of Defense for Space Policy, Office of the Secretary of 
Defense, Department of Defense; General Gregory Guillot, Commander, 
U.S. Northern Command and North American Aerospace Defense Command; 
General Michael Guetlein, Direct Report Program Manager for Golden Dome 
for America, Office of the Secretary of Defense, Department of Defense; 
Lieutenant General Heath Collins, Director, Missile Defense Agency, 
Department of Defense; and Lieutenant General Sean Gainey, Commanding 
General, U.S. Army Space and Missile Defense Command.
MILITARY READINESS FOR FY27
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Readiness held a hearing 
entitled ``Military Readiness for FY27''. Testimony was heard from 
General Shawn N. Bratton, Vice Chief of Space Operations, U.S. Space 
Force; General Bradford J. Gering, Assistant Commandant, U.S. Marine 
Corps; Admiral James W. Kilby, Vice Chief of Naval Operations, U.S. 
Navy; General John Lamontagne, Vice Chief of Staff, U.S. Air Force; and 
General Christopher C. LaNeve, Vice Chief of Staff, U.S. Army.
THE PRESIDENT'S FISCAL YEAR 2027 BUDGET REQUEST
Committee on the Budget: Full Committee held a hearing entitled ``The 
President's Fiscal Year 2027 Budget Request''. Testimony was heard from 
Russell Vought, Director, Office of Management and Budget.
BUILDING AN AI-READY AMERICA: UNDERSTANDING AI'S ECONOMIC IMPACT ON 
WORKERS AND EMPLOYERS
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Workforce 
Protections held a hearing entitled ``Building an AI-Ready America: 
Understanding AI's Economic Impact on Workers and Employers''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.

[[Page D393]]


HEALTHIER AMERICA: LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS TO IMPROVE PUBLIC HEALTH
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``Healthier America: Legislative Proposals to Improve Public 
Health''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
COMPUTING POWER AND COMPETITION: EXAMINING THE SEMICONDUCTOR ECOSYSTEM
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Commerce, 
Manufacturing, and Trade held a hearing entitled ``Computing Power and 
Competition: Examining the Semiconductor Ecosystem''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
SAFEGUARDING MAIN STREET: COMBATTING FRAUD AND EXPLOITATION IN OUR 
CAPITAL MARKETS
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Capital Markets held a 
hearing entitled ``Safeguarding Main Street: Combatting Fraud and 
Exploitation in Our Capital Markets''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
HELPING AMERICAN BUSINESSES WIN ABROAD: STRENGTHENING U.S. COMMERCIAL 
DIPLOMACY
Committee on Foreign Affairs: East Asia and the Pacific Subcommittee; 
and Middle East and North Africa Subcommittee held a joint hearing 
entitled ``Helping American Businesses Win Abroad: Strengthening U.S. 
Commercial Diplomacy''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
FRAUD PREVENTION: UNDERSTANDING FRAUD IN FEDERALLY FUNDED PROGRAMS RUN 
BY THE STATES
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on 
Government Operations held a hearing entitled ``Fraud Prevention: 
Understanding Fraud in Federally Funded Programs Run by the States''. 
Testimony was heard from Seto Bagdoyan, Director, Forensic Audits and 
Investigative Service, Government Accountability Office; Allison Ball, 
Auditor of Public Accounts, Kentucky; and public witnesses.
THE STATE OF SCIENTIFIC PUBLISHING: ASSESSING TRENDS, EMERGING ISSUES, 
AND POLICY CONSIDERATIONS
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Subcommittee on 
Investigations and Oversight held a hearing entitled ``The State of 
Scientific Publishing: Assessing Trends, Emerging Issues, and Policy 
Considerations''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LOWER TAXES, STRONGER MAIN STREET: THE BENEFITS OF THE WORKING FAMILIES 
TAX CUTS
Committee on Small Business: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Lower Taxes, Stronger Main Street: The Benefits of the Working 
Families Tax Cuts''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
FASTER DECISIONS, STRONGER OUTCOMES: VETERANS ADMINISTRATION'S WORK TO 
STREAMLINE THE DISABILITY CLAIMS BACKLOG
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Faster Decisions, Stronger Outcomes: VA's Work to Streamline the 
Disability Claims Backlog''. Testimony was heard from Margarita Devlin, 
Principal Deputy Under Secretary for Benefits, Veterans Benefits 
Administration, Department of Veterans Affairs.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a markup on H.R. 7950, to establish the Office of 
Congressional and Legislative Affairs in the Department of Veterans 
Affairs, and for other purposes; H.R. 7683, the ``VA Fiscal Management 
and Modernization Act''; H.R. 8052, the ``Veterans Infection Prevention 
Act''; H.R. 6654, the ``VAMOSA Act of 2025''; H.R. 8043, the ``Vets 
CLEAR Act''; H.R. 7280, the ``Veteran DATA Act''; H.R. 8041, the 
``Supporting VA Families Act''; and H.R. 8010, the ``VA Police 
Recruitment and Retention Act of 2026''. H.R. 7950 and H.R. 7683 were 
forwarded to the full Committee, as amended. H.R. 8052, H.R. 6654, H.R. 
8043, H.R. 7280, H.R. 8041, and H.R. 8010 were ordered favorably 
forwarded to the Full Committee, without amendment.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
