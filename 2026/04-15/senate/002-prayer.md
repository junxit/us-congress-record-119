# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Loving and ever-present God, You have been our help in ages past; You 
are our hope for the years to come.
  Lord, forgive us when we fail to make an adequate response to the 
flagrant disrespect of Your sovereignty. Forgive us for our reluctance 
to speak in defense of Your majesty. Remind us that there are certain 
lines we should never cross.
  Lord, we trust You to direct our steps with Your providential power. 
Guide our lawmakers. Where there is perplexity, provide clarity. Where 
there is weakness, provide strength. Where there is fear, provide 
courage.
  We pray in the Name of the only One who can save us. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
