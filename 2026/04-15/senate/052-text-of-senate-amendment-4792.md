# Text of Senate Amendment 4792

**Speaking:** Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

```
  SA 4792. Mr. BENNET submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 4201, to require the Chief of Space Operations to 
submit a feasibility report on expanding the Multinational Force 
Operation Olympic Defender; which was referred to the Committee on 
Foreign Relations; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``To require the Commander 
     of the United States Space Command to submit a feasibility 
     report on expanding the Multinational Force Operation Olympic 
     Defender.''.
```
