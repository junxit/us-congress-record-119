                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1431. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2407 State Route 71, Suite 
     1, in Spring Lake, New Jersey, as the ``James J. Howard Post 
     Office''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 1706. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1200 William Street, Room 
     200, in Buffalo, New York, as the ``William J. Donovan Post 
     Office Building''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.

     States Postal Service located at 12208 North 19th Avenue in 
     Phoenix, Arizona, as the ``Officer Zane T. Coolidge Post 
     Office''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 4635. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 890 East 152nd Street in 
     Cleveland, Ohio, as the ``Technical Sergeant Alma Gladys 
     Minter Post Office Building''; to the Committee on Homeland 
     Security and Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 4707. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1019 Avenue H in Fort 
     Madison, Iowa, as the ``Martin L. Graber Post Office''; to 
     the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 5058. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 46164 Westlake Drive in 
     Sterling, Virginia, as the ``Firefighter Trevor Brown Post 
     Office Building''; to the Committee on Homeland Security and

       H.R. 5773. An act to designate the facility of the United

     States Postal Service located at 306 South Main Street in

     Security and Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 6020. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 14855 South Van Dyke Road in

     Post Office Building''; to the Committee on Homeland Security

       H.R. 6247. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 13355 North Lon Adams Road 
     in Marana, Arizona, as the ``Mayor Ed Honea Memorial Post

     States Postal Service located at 417 West 7th Street in

     States Postal Service located at 825 Highway 198 in Beaumont, 
     Mississippi, as the ``Jeremy Malone Post Office''; to the 
     Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.

     equipped with collision mitigation technology, to improve 
     helicopter route safety and separation around airports, to 
     update air traffic control processes and procedures, to

     Defense activities, and for other purposes; to the Committee
