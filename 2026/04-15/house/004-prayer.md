# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty God, unto whom all hearts are open, all desires known, and 
from You no secrets are hid, humbly we come before You, appealing to 
Your mercy and dependent on Your grace.
  For even in our noblest efforts to serve You, and in our honest 
desires to do what is best for this country, we discover how easy it is 
to mistake approbation for affirmation. All too often we find ourselves 
valuing veneration over virtue.
  Regardless of our attempts to disguise our misdeeds, what is hidden 
is known to You. Everything that is secret from everyone else is 
revealed to You.
  Silence then all voices but Your own, and speak to us Your words of 
forgiveness. Quell all desires save for the longing to receive Your 
love, and strengthen us to remain faithful to Your will.
  Purge us from all unrighteousness. Cleanse us from the thoughts of 
our minds and the willfulness of our hearts by the inspiration of Your 
holy spirit, that we may perfectly love You and worthily magnify Your 
holy name, in which we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
