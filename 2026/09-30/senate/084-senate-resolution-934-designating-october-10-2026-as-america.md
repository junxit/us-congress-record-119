# SENATE RESOLUTION 934--DESIGNATING OCTOBER 10, 2026, AS "AMERICAN GIRLS IN SPORTS DAY"

**Measures:** S.Res. 934

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 934--DESIGNATING OCTOBER 10, 2026, AS ``AMERICAN 
                         GIRLS IN SPORTS DAY''

  Mrs. BLACKBURN  (for herself, Mr. Cotton, Mr. Justice, Mr. Johnson, 
Mr. Tuberville, Mr. Lee, Mrs. Britt, Mr. Lankford, Mrs. Hyde-Smith, 
Mrs. Moody, Mr. Hoeven, Mr. Ricketts, Mr. Risch, Mr. Daines, Mr. 
Grassley, Mr. Hagerty, Mr. Crapo, Mr. Budd, Mr. Cramer, Mr. Rounds, Mr. 
Moreno, Mr. Cornyn, Ms. Graham, Mr. McCormick, Mr. Young, Mr. Sullivan, 
Mr. Scott of Florida, Mr. Barrasso, Mr. Scott of South Carolina, Mrs. 
Capito, and Mr. Schmitt) submitted the following resolution; which was 
referred to the Committee on the Judiciary:

                              S. Res. 934

       Whereas athletic participation has an important, positive 
     impact on young girls, improving their physical health, self-
     confidence, and discipline;
       Whereas women have been responsible for some of the 
     greatest athletic feats in American history, from the Olympic 
     games to professional competition;
       Whereas female athletes have served as inspirations for 
     generations of women and girls;
       Whereas the enactment of Title IX of the Education 
     Amendments of 1972 (20 U.S.C. 1681 et seq.) (referred to in 
     this preamble as ``Title IX'') marked a pivotal moment in the 
     Federal support of girls in sports;
       Whereas there are fundamental biological differences 
     between men and women that put women at a competitive 
     disadvantage in sports and jeopardize their safety during 
     competition;
       Whereas, in recent years, there has been an increase in the 
     number of biological men allowed to compete in women's 
     sports;
       Whereas, since the 1980s, biological men have displaced 
     women and girls from over 2,000 medals they should have 
     rightfully won;
       Whereas, on February 5, 2025, President Donald J. Trump 
     issued Executive Order 14201 (90 Fed. Reg. 9279), entitled 
     ``Keeping Men Out of Women's Sports,'' establishing it as the 
     policy of the United States to protect fair athletic 
     opportunities for women and girls and to oppose male 
     competitive participation in women's sports;
       Whereas, on June 30, 2026, in West Virginia v. B.P.J., 146 
     S. Ct. 2356 (2026), and in Little v. Hecox, 146 S. Ct. 2356 
     (2026), the Supreme Court of the United States affirmed that 
     Title IX permits schools to maintain separate athletic teams 
     for men and women based on biological sex, and upheld State 
     laws protecting female athletic competition for biological 
     women and girls;
       Whereas it is imperative that women's and girls' 
     opportunities to compete athletically are protected; and
       Whereas October 10th, as represented by the Roman numerals 
     ``XX'', signifies the female XX chromosomes: Now, therefore, 
     be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) recognizes October 10, 2026, as ``American Girls in 
     Sports Day'';
       (2) celebrates the impact that American women and girls 
     have had on sports culture throughout our history;
       (3) recognizes the importance of Title IX in protecting 
     biological women in sports; and
       (4) calls on sports-governing bodies in the United States 
     and abroad to protect biological women and girls in sports.
```
