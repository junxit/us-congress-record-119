# TRUMP ADMINISTRATION

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                          TRUMP ADMINISTRATION

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, on chaos, with a record so terrible, with 
an agenda so deeply unpopular, Trump has resorted to sowing chaos 
everywhere he can--chaos in our financial markets; chaos in our 
streets, where he unleashed his rogue ICE agents to go after Americans; 
chaos abroad, where his war has put our economy at risk--all because he 
didn't think to keep the Strait of Hormuz open when he began.
  Republicans have embraced Trump's chaos at every turn, but Senate 
Democrats have done everything we can to restore stability, especially 
in our elections.
  Trump has waged an all-out war on free and fair elections in the 
United States. He has tried to disenfranchise millions of Americans, 
kill vote-by-mail, and make it harder for people to make their voices 
heard in their democracy. Trump has given up on winning over the 
electorate, so he is trying to silence it.
  Republicans tried to jam through Trump's SAVE Act--the most devious 
anti-voting rights legislation Trump ever dreamed up--to purge millions 
of voters from the rolls.
  We told Republicans that the SAVE Act ain't going to happen. It 
didn't. We killed the SAVE Act in Congress, we took Trump to court over 
his attacks with his voter-suppression Executive orders, we protected 
mail-in voting, and we defeated Trump's attacks on our elections in 
case after case after case.
  Senate Democrats launched our Election Protection Task Force to plan 
how we will protect our elections on election day from Trump, we held 
tabletop exercises to prepare for Trump's efforts to interfere with our 
elections, and we launched a historic Election Observer Program to 
train and deploy Senate staff volunteers across the country to monitor 
for any meddling in our electoral system.
  In America, Donald Trump should know--he doesn't--that voters pick 
their leaders; leaders don't pick their voters.
  You hear that, Trump? Voters pick their leaders; leaders don't pick 
their voters.
  Senate Democrats have taken every precaution to make sure it stays 
that way.
  In conclusion, make no mistake, the past 2 years have been hard--
extremely hard--on Americans. Costs are through the roof. Millions are 
losing their healthcare. Trump's masked agents have run down Americans 
in the streets. At least 19 Americans have been killed, and hundreds 
more have been wounded. The global economy is in tatters, and Americans 
are watching costs climb even further because Trump launched a 
reckless, illegal war with absolutely no plan.
  We are fighting Trump tooth and nail on every front, but we are the 
first to recognize that stopping the worst of the worst isn't enough. 
We are ready to hit the ground running to lower costs, rein in chaos, 
and stop the corruption.
  Republicans broke faith with the American people. We know we need to 
build that trust with the Americans back up, and we will.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
