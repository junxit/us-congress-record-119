# RECOGNIZING THE 200TH ANNIVERSARY OF JEFFERSON BARRACKS

**Speaking:** Schmitt, Eric (S001227) [R-MO]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE 200TH ANNIVERSARY OF JEFFERSON BARRACKS

● Mr. SCHMITT. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize the 
200th anniversary of Jefferson Barracks in St. Louis County, MO, and to 
honor the generations of Americans whose service has shaped its 
remarkable history.
  For two centuries, Jefferson Barracks has stood as a testament to 
Missouri's enduring commitment to the defense of our Nation. 
Established in 1826, the installation has served the United States 
through periods of war, expansion, innovation, and change, becoming one 
of the most historically significant military sites in the country.
  From its earliest days as an Army training post, Jefferson Barracks 
grew into a critical center for military readiness. American soldiers 
trained there before heading into critical conflicts that shaped the 
Nation. It served as a recruiting and staging ground, a major hospital 
during the Civil War, and an enlistment and deployment center during 
World War I. Its history is intertwined with some of the most 
consequential figures in American history, including military heroes 
turned U.S. Presidents like Ulysses S. Grant and Dwight D. Eisenhower.
  The importance of Jefferson Barracks is not confined to the past. 
Today, it continues to serve the Missouri National Guard as the 
Jefferson Barracks Air National Guard Station and the surrounding 
veteran community as a prominent national cemetery. More than 200,000 
veterans have been laid to rest there, making the grounds not only a 
place of service but also one

[[Page S5248]]

of profound remembrance and gratitude.
  Few places can claim such a lasting connection to the men and women 
who have defended this country. For generations of Missourians, 
Jefferson Barracks has been a place where servicemembers trained, 
veterans were cared for, families honored loved ones, and our military 
history was preserved for those who came after them.
  As Jefferson Barracks marks its 200th anniversary, I am proud to 
honor its legacy and the countless Americans who have served there. Its 
history reflects the strength, patriotism, and commitment to service 
that define Missouri, and I look forward to seeing that legacy continue 
for generations to come.●
```
