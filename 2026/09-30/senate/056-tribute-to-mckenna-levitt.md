# TRIBUTE TO McKENNA LeVITT

**Speaking:** Rosen, Jacky (R000608) [D-NV]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO McKENNA LeVITT

  Ms. ROSEN. Mr. President, I rise today to honor and thank my 
constituent and longtime staffer McKenna LeVitt for her many years of 
service to the U.S. Senate and to the people of Nevada.

[[Page S5245]]

  McKenna has been a key member of my team since my first year in the 
Senate, joining my office as part of our first intern class and then 
returning to work for me in a full-time role after she graduated from 
the University of Nevada, Reno. Over time, she proved herself to be an 
incredibly dedicated and talented staffer, rising quickly from staff 
assistant to legislative correspondent to legislative correspondence 
manager to legislative aide to legislative assistant and finally 
becoming our lead policy adviser for all energy, environment, public 
lands, water, agriculture, and Tribal issues.
  In this role, McKenna has led my office's work on numerous public 
lands bills, multi-year legislative efforts that are critical to the 
landscape, economic development, safety, preservation, and 
accessibility of the land in our State. She has shepherded the Truckee 
Meadows Public Lands Management Act through Congress, one of the most 
significant pieces of legislation directly impacting northern Nevada in 
recent history. In introducing, negotiating, and working to amend this 
bill, McKenna has taken many hundreds of meetings and put in thousands 
of hours of work, collaborating with county and city officials, Tribes, 
the State of Nevada, conservationists, sportsmen, recreationalists, 
ranchers, landholders, housing developers, utilities, and others. She 
has taken public feedback, working parcel-by-parcel on changes and 
working across the aisle and with members of both the House and Senate 
and with committee staff to move this bill forward. Its future success 
in this Chamber will be due in very large part to her, and I am 
confident that we will get the Truckee Meadows Public Lands Management 
Act across the finish line thanks to her hard work on it these past 
many years.
  She has also played a leading role in working with my national 
security team to obtain fair compensation for ranchers impacted by the 
modernization of Naval Air Station Fallon, helped draft and introduce 
dozens of bills and amendments, secured millions of dollars in critical 
funding for Nevada through appropriations, and taken what can 
conservatively be estimated to be over a thousand meetings with 
Nevadans. Whenever McKenna wasn't at her desk, we knew it meant she was 
in a meeting talking with Nevadans about how we could help them. It is 
her personal touch in these interactions that sets her apart, not only 
coming prepared with policy knowledge and ready to answer any question 
that comes her way, but also listening with empathy and fully adopting 
the challenges of her fellow Nevadans as her own.
  As a colleague, McKenna is beloved, someone whom everyone on my team 
in both Nevada and DC considers a person they can trust. She has been 
there for people when they need her, just as she has been there for the 
State of Nevada. McKenna has a steady hand and an analytical mind, but 
also a heart of gold.
  McKenna, I thank you for your service to Nevada and the country, and 
I look forward to seeing you grow and excel as you move back home to 
Reno and begin a new life with Ken. We will miss you, but your impact 
will be felt forever.

                          ____________________
```
