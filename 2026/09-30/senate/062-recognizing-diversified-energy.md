# RECOGNIZING DIVERSIFIED ENERGY

**Speaking:** McCormick, David (M001243) [R-PA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING DIVERSIFIED ENERGY

● Mr. McCORMICK. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize 
Diversified Energy on the occasion of its 25th anniversary and to 
commend the company, its employees, and the communities it serves for a 
quarter century of responsible energy production.
  Founded in 2001 by its current chief executive officer Rusty Hutson, 
Diversified Energy has grown into a recognized leader in the 
responsible management of low-decline natural gas and oil assets. Over 
25 years, the company has shown that mature, long-life energy assets 
can be operated safely, efficiently, and sustainably while delivering 
reliable energy to families and businesses across the region.
  Diversified's model rests on long-term stewardship. By focusing on 
low-decline production, the company extends the productive life of 
existing wells, limits the need for new surface disturbance, and makes 
the most of resources that have already been developed. Their approach 
has also made measurable progress in reducing methane emissions. These 
efforts show that energy production and environmental responsibility 
can advance together.
  For 25 years, Diversified Energy has contributed to economic 
development, job creation, and energy reliability. Its success is also 
the success of the men and women who have built it and the communities 
that have supported it. I congratulate Diversified Energy on this 
milestone and thank its employees for their dedication. I wish the 
company continued success in the years ahead.●

                          ____________________
```
