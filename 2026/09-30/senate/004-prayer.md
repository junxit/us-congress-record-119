# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our sustainer, You know our thoughts from afar. Because 
of Your mercy, we are not consumed. Great is Your faithfulness. Lord, 
send Your spirit to empower us to live worthy of Your great Name.
  Guide our Senators as they confront the vicissitudes and challenges 
of our time. Give them the confidence and the assurance that Your 
purposes will prevail. In the hectic pace of their lives, help them to 
slow down long enough to hear Your still, small voice of wisdom. Ease 
the tensions that pull them apart, and keep them united as they strive 
to do Your will.
  Lord, You know us better than we know ourselves; therefore, have Your 
way in our Nation and throughout our world.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
