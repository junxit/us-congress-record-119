# STOP INSIDER TRADING ACT

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
                        STOP INSIDER TRADING ACT

  Mr. PADILLA. Mr. President, here we are, 34 days before election 
day--or as I like to suggest, 34 days before the final day to vote, 
because we are encouraging people to cast their ballot early, if they 
can, or vote by mail, if and where they can.
  But instead of talking about the issues that are most on the minds of 
American families, Republicans are here again trying to distract from 
those issues and to, frankly, create confusion amongst voters in order 
to protect the President and the party from their failed policies.
  Now, Senate Democrats agree that something real needs to happen to 
rein in the ability of elected officials, especially Members of 
Congress, from making decisions that impact the companies that are in 
their stock portfolios. That is true. The American people deserve to 
have confidence that their elected representatives are making decisions 
in the best interests of the American people and not in your bank 
account or your stock investments.
  But that is not what this bill will do. This bill does not ban 
Members of Congress from holding stocks or trading stocks, and it 
exempts the most powerful elected official of all--the President of the 
United States--from a stock trading ban.
  Now, let's be clear, colleagues, because we have seen this time and 
again. When the President makes announcements or posts on Truth Social, 
he has the potential to move markets, shift the economy. That is truly 
an awesome power, one that nobody should be able to exploit for their 
own profit. But that is exactly what Donald Trump has done and has done 
over and over again.
  That is why, since returning to office last year, his net worth has 
grown some $4 to $5 billion--billion with a ``b.''
  Now, ABC News service said, just last year, that he made over 21,000 
stock trades across 8 accounts in a matter of--the average was about 85 
trades per day--85 trades per day.
  Does that sound like something that is singularly focused on 
improving the quality of life of the American people? I don't think so.
  Bloomberg News found that President Trump has traded stocks more than 
every Member of Congress combined.
  Think about that for a minute. One person, Donald Trump, the 
President of the United States, the ``Trader in Chief,'' made more 
stock trades in 1 year than all 535 Members of Congress.
  Now, NBC News found that 327 of these trades were made exactly 1 day 
before he rolled back his ``Liberation Day'' tariffs. Was that a 
coincidence? I think not.
  How much grift from his own decision making? The American people 
deserve to know.
  But the President's corruption goes even further. His social media 
company is literally selling access to hedge funds and Wall Street 
brokers--early access--to his major market-shaping announcements.
  Let that sink in for a moment.
  Trump has been making more stock trades than all of Congress combined 
while backing a ban that excludes him. That is what the bill before us 
does. Is anyone surprised the President would support a bill that does 
nothing to rein him in? How can we possibly go home and tell the 
American people with a straight face that we are stopping the 
corruption in Washington when the biggest exploiter is the President 
and he is immune from this bill? The author of this bill refused to put 
any guardrails on the biggest, most corrupt stock trader of them all.
  Now, don't take that to mean that Democrats don't want to do anything 
in this space. We do. In fact, the Homeland Security and Government 
Affairs Committee, last year, put out a bipartisan bill that would 
actually address this issue.
  But folks watching at home should recognize that that bipartisan bill 
with teeth is not what Republicans have chosen to put on the floor 
today, 34 days before election. Instead, it is a sham bill that doesn't 
have the teeth and doesn't rein in the ``Trader in Chief.''
  I have tried to bring up that bill, by the way, repeatedly in the 
Senate, and Senate Republicans have blocked it on multiple occasions.
  So today is not a serious attempt to address the kind of corruption 
here in Washington that the American people may care about.
  And to add further proof to that point, this isn't just a sham 
attempt to ban Members of Congress who trade in stocks, because it 
includes a voter ID poison pill in it. This so-called voter ID bill 
would impose a far stricter and more burdensome Federal standard in all 
50 States.
  Now, some States have voter ID; some States don't. Those that do have 
laws, at the State level, that require an ID allow Tribal IDs or 
student IDs issued by the State government or State entity to count. 
But the Federal provisions in this bill do not.
  And it goes even worse for the one-third of American voters that 
prefer to vote by mail, if they have that option in their State--and 80 
percent of Californians, by the way. This requirement that you have to 
have a copy of your ID in the envelope with your ballot for it to count 
eviscerates the secret ballot that we value so much.
  Here is what the Republican Lieutenant Governor of Utah had to say--
and in Utah the Lieutenant Governor is also the State election 
official--when it was included in the SAVE Act. And now it is in the 
measure before us.

       The SAVE America Act requires a vote[r] to include a 
     photocopy of their ID . . . violating our Constitutional 
     right to a secret ballot.

  So don't just take my word for it. This is the Republican Lieutenant 
Governor of Utah. And she is not the only Republican opposed to this 
kind of legislation.
  The Republican Governor of Ohio, with a similar State bill, said:

       [The measure] would not discourage fraud, would not add any 
     real security, and would create an additional and significant 
     burden for Ohioans who vote by mail.

  All for what? In pursuit of the Big Lie that the President has been 
claiming for, what, a decade now about massive voter fraud? While every 
expert and every study have found the same thing. Despite the 
conspiracy theories, voter fraud is already illegal. Let's establish 
that. It is already on the books as illegal. But it is also extremely, 
extremely rare.
  So what is this bill truly about anyway? It is not about cracking 
down on corruption, as Republicans claim it is. It is not going to, you 
know, address the massive voter fraud that does not exist. Again, it is 
misdirection. It is a distraction, and it threatens to suppress the 
vote 34 days before the election.
  And all Senate Republicans realize that the only thing they have to 
show for their control of government for the last 2 years--right?--you 
are the majority in the Senate. You are the majority in the House. Your 
party occupies the White House. When families are struggling with 
rising inflation, the rising cost of healthcare and housing, groceries, 
mortgages, gas prices, an unauthorized war in Iran, instead of actually 
addressing those issues with real proposals, they are seeking to 
distract and confuse.
  The American people deserve better than this, and I urge all of my 
colleagues to vote no on this cynical measure.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). The Senator from Ohio.

                          ____________________
```
