# SENATE RESOLUTION 929--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE REGARDING EQUITY STAKES HELD BY THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT IN PRIVATE COMPANIES

**Measures:** S.Res. 929

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

  SENATE RESOLUTION 929--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE REGARDING 
   EQUITY STAKES HELD BY THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT IN PRIVATE COMPANIES

  Ms. ROSEN submitted the following resolution; which was referred to 
the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs:

                              S. Res. 929

       Whereas, since January 2025, Federal agencies have 
     announced the acquisition of equity or equity-like interests 
     in private companies through more than 35 transactions, 
     representing more than $27,000,000,000 in taxpayer funds;
       Whereas, in August 2025, 1789 Capital, a venture capital 
     firm in which the son of President Trump is a partner, 
     invested in Vulcan Elements, a rare earth magnet 
     manufacturer, and, months later, the Department of Defense 
     announced a conditional commitment to lend $620,000,000 to 
     the company and the Federal Government agreed to take a 
     $50,000,000 equity stake in the company;
       Whereas, in January 2026, the Department of Commerce 
     announced a tentative agreement to provide up to 
     $1,600,000,000 in funding and loans to USA Rare Earth and to 
     acquire approximately a 10 percent stake in the company, and, 
     on the same day, USA Rare Earth announced a $1,500,000,000 
     private offering of the stock of the company, for which the 
     lead placement agent was Cantor Fitzgerald, a firm formerly 
     led by the Secretary of Commerce and now led by the sons of 
     the Secretary of Commerce; and
       Whereas equity interests acquired with taxpayer funds 
     belong to the people of the United States and must never be 
     used to enrich Federal officials, Members of Congress, or the 
     families of Federal officials or Members of Congress: Now, 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That it is the sense of the Senate that the 
     President, the Vice President, members of the Cabinet, 
     Members of Congress, officials who participate in Federal 
     equity transactions, and the spouses, children, sons-in-law, 
     and daughters-in-law of any such individuals should be 
     prohibited from--
       (1) profiting from any Federal acquisition of an equity 
     interest in a private company, including through ownership of 
     the company or through any fee, commission, or other 
     compensation connected to any such transaction; and
       (2) purchasing or receiving any equity interest the Federal 
     Government sells or transfers, or receiving compensation 
     connected to any such sale or transfer.
```
