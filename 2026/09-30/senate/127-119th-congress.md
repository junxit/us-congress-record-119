# 119TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             119TH CONGRESS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, 20 months ago, Republicans won control of 
Washington on a promise to bring down costs, make government more 
efficient, and end senseless wars. But over the past 2 years, 
Republicans betrayed the trust of the American people. For the past 20 
months, Senate Republicans catered to Donald Trump's every wish, but 
Senate Democrats stood up for the American people.

[[Page S5300]]

  The Trump administration, empowered by his feckless Republican 
majorities in Congress, has been a catastrophe for working families 
everywhere. Rising costs, increasing chaos, and rampant corruption 
characterize the Trump administration and its Republican acolytes.
  Though we lack the majority in both the House and Senate, Democrats 
came together like never before to hold Trump and Republicans' feet to 
the fire and do everything we could to stop them. We fought 
Republicans' radical agenda on front after front and blunted some of 
their worst attacks on the American people.
  On costs, we worked tirelessly and fiercely to call attention to how 
Republicans' disastrous agenda is making costs for the American people 
skyrocket. Americans have watched the price of everything--everything--
explode these past 2 years--groceries, gasoline, healthcare, 
electricity, housing, childcare. Everything is more expensive.
  Americans are crying out for relief, and instead of listening to 
Americans, my Republican colleagues across the aisle back the very same 
administration policies that have cost families an average of $4,200 in 
higher prices since Trump took office--$4,200 in higher prices because 
of Republican and Trump policies.
  Senate Republicans back Trump's horrendous tariff tax to the hilt. 
Democrats fought Trump's tariff tax in court and won. Americans 
shouldn't have to shell out a fortune at the grocery store so Trump can 
pound his chest about his senseless trade wars.
  As Trump has pursued new tariffs, Republicans in Congress have 
abetted these tax increases on families and small businesses, while 
Democrats have pushed to end them.
  We fought back when Trump and congressional Republicans gleefully cut 
$1 trillion--$1 trillion--from our healthcare system so they could cut 
taxes for billionaires and their biggest corporation friends. The 
result: 5 million Americans have already lost their healthcare 
coverage. Can you imagine being a family with no healthcare when your 
kid gets sick? And another 10 million will lose it in the coming years. 
Meanwhile, tens of millions of Americans with ACA coverage have to 
shell out more than $1,000 extra because of soaring premiums and 
deductibles.
  Medicaid, food assistance--every program designed to help Americans 
keep their heads above water--all cut to the bones by Republicans in 
the Senate and in the country.
  Let's not forget how Americans' electricity bills are up 18 percent 
under Donald Trump because Republicans gutted the historic investments 
Democrats made in the cheapest, fastest forms to get energy on the 
grid. We fought hard to secure these investments, and you had better 
believe we are going to fight to bring them back.
  Republicans are also charging the American people for senseless 
foreign wars that they promised to end. The Republican majority 
continues to back Trump's disastrous war with Iran that has made gas 
prices skyrocket and mortgage rates soar.
  Democrats know just how badly Americans are hurting right now. 
Republicans can turn a blind eye to the affordability crisis all they 
want, but fixing it means offering a positive vision to lower costs. 
That is what Democrats will continue to do.

                          ____________________
```
