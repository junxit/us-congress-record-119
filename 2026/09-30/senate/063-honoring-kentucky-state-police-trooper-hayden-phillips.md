# HONORING KENTUCKY STATE POLICE TROOPER HAYDEN PHILLIPS

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
         HONORING KENTUCKY STATE POLICE TROOPER HAYDEN PHILLIPS

● Mr. PAUL. Mr. President, today I rise to recognize and honor 
Kentucky State Police Trooper Hayden Phillips. On Monday, September 28, 
2026, Trooper Phillips reported for duty just as he had for the past 3 
years, faithfully serving the Commonwealth of Kentucky. Tragically, he 
never returned home to his wife and their three young sons. In my 
hometown of Bowling Green, KY, Trooper Phillips paid the ultimate 
sacrifice, senselessly murdered while conducting a traffic stop on 
interstate 65 in Warren County.
  Trooper Phillips was a proud graduate of Kentucky State Police Cadet 
Class 103, completing his training in August 2023 before being assigned 
to KSP Post 3 Bowling Green. He voluntarily swore an oath to serve and 
protect the people of Kentucky, and he upheld that oath with unwavering 
honor. Choosing a career in law enforcement is a profound commitment, 
one made with full understanding of the risks and dangers it carries. 
Yet Trooper Phillips willingly accepted those risks to make a 
difference in the lives of countless people he would never meet.
  To his family, friends, coworkers, and community, Trooper Hayden 
Phillips was known as a true servant. Before joining the Kentucky State 
Police, he worked in other emergency service roles, demonstrating a 
lifelong dedication to helping those in need. Those who knew him 
described him as caring, hard-working, and genuinely pleasant--a man 
whose smile left a lasting impression on everyone he met.
  But Trooper Phillips was more than a dedicated officer. He was a 
loving husband to his wife Jordan and a devoted father to their three 
young sons. His legacy will endure through them, allowing his life and 
his example to continue touching others for generations.
  Attacks such as this on our law enforcement officers are not only 
tragedies; they are assaults on the freedoms we cherish as Americans. 
We must always stand firmly behind our first responders. I commend 
their dedication and express my deepest gratitude to all who carry the 
weight of the badge.
  Please join me in honoring the life and service of Kentucky State 
Police Trooper Hayden Phillips, Unit 369. May we never forget his 
sacrifice, and may we lift his family, friends, coworkers, and the 
entire community up during this difficult time.●

                          ____________________
```
