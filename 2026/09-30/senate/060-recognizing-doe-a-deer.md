# RECOGNIZING DOE A DEER

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                         RECOGNIZING DOE A DEER

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize Doe 
a Deer of Guthrie County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the Week.
  Doe a Deer initially started off as a side hustle in 2016, when the 
owner, Regan Doely, began creating designs while she worked a full-time 
job. Ever since she was young, Regan found a deep appreciation for 
patterns and designs on products such as linens, cookbooks, and 
packaging. As an adult, she carried that love into her full-time job as 
a graphic designer. As Regan continued to take on more freelance 
projects, she found herself wanting to expand her business offerings. 
Regan created a couple of greeting cards, notepads, and flour sack 
towels to showcase at a local Small Business Saturday craft show. Her 
products received numerous positive responses from shoppers, which 
motivated her to continue designing. Regan's designs even attracted 
local stores who were willing to sell her products. With enough support 
from her retailer partners, Regan quit her full-time job and began 
searching for a place to set up her own store. In 2018, Regan secured a 
storefront in downtown Stuart, IA, officially marking the start of Doe 
a Deer.
  Today, Doe a Deer has moved into a larger storefront, just a short 
distance from their previous space. In the store, Regan and her team of 
11 employees work diligently on creating unique designs for their 
products, including kitchenware, blankets, gifts, spices, books, and 
self-care products. The store's wide variety of offerings allows the 
business to reach a variety of audiences.
  Doe a Deer strives to serve its local community. Throughout the 
years, this woman-owned business has supported several local 
organizations including the Adair Boys & Girls Club, Stuart 
Revitalization Group, and Adair County Youth Action Committee. Regan 
and the team also help manage local events, such as high school proms 
and the Guthrie County Fair. The company continues to make impacts on a 
national scale, donating to organizations such as the Alzheimer's 
Association and Operation Christmas Child. In 2024, Regan was 
recognized for her service, earning the Ten Under Ten Award that is 
granted to Grand View University alumni who make a significant impact 
in their community.
  I want to thank Regan and the entire Doe a Deer team for their 
dedication to their customers and commitment to serving Iowa 
communities. I look forward to seeing their continued growth and 
success.●
```
