# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** H.R. 9340

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 684, H.R. 9340, a bill to amend the 
     Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 to establish a 
     Federal standard relating to the recovery of the full, 
     incremental costs of upgrades that serve large-load 
     customers, and for other purposes.
         John Thune, Jon A. Husted, Kevin Cramer, Bernie Moreno, 
           Tom Cotton, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Cynthia M. Lummis, Susan 
           M. Collins, Roger F. Wicker, John Cornyn, Pete 
           Ricketts, Katie Boyd Britt, Mike Rounds, Todd Young, 
           John Boozman, John Hoeven, Ron Johnson.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the mandatory quorum 
call under rule XXII has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
motion to proceed to Calendar No. 684, H.R. 9340, a bill to amend the 
Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 to establish a Federal 
standard relating to the recovery of the full, incremental costs of 
upgrades that serve large-load customers, and for other purposes, shall 
be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant bill clerk called the roll.
  The yeas and nays resulted--yeas 57, nays 43, as follows:
```
