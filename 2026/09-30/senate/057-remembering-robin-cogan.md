# REMEMBERING ROBIN COGAN

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                        REMEMBERING ROBIN COGAN

● Mr. BOOKER. Mr. President, today, we honor the selfless life 
of Robin Cogan, a New Jersey resident who passed away on June 11, 2026, 
at the age of 65. Robin was a beloved nurse, grandmother, friend, and 
outstanding member of her community in Cherry Hill, NJ. She will be 
remembered as a ``relentless'' advocate for nurses, our community's 
children, and for working tirelessly to prevent gun violence in our 
schools.
  Robin was the New Jersey director for the National Association of 
School Nurses and a teacher in the school nurse certification program 
at Rutgers University in Camden, NJ. As a voice of her profession, 
Robin authored the ``Relentless School Nurse'' blog, as well as a 
monthly column in ``My American Nurse,'' the official journal of the 
American Nurses Association. Her service to the profession was 
recognized through multiple major professional awards, including the 
2019 and 2020 National Association of School Nurses President's Award, 
the 2018 NCSN School Nurse of the Year, the 2017 Johnson & Johnson 
School Nurse of the Year, and the New Jersey Department of Health 2017 
Population Health Hero Award.
  One of Robin's guiding principles was that ``voice is our most 
renewable resource,'' a principle she not only espoused but which she 
lived out every day. Through programs like her ``No More Empty Desks'' 
art campaign at Charles Brimm Medical Arts High School in Camden, 
Robin's life is proof that ripples make waves if we are relentlessly 
brave in standing up for our communities. My staff and I had the great 
pleasure of meeting Robin during the ``Hill Days'' she coordinated 
while serving as the legislative cochair for the New Jersey State 
School Nurses Association. We were left humbled by her story, inspired 
by her message, and proud to honor all she has done for the great State 
of New Jersey.
  Amidst the pandemic of gun violence that has gripped our American 
communities, Robin's legacy is one of hope. I encourage members of this 
Chamber to reflect on Robin's life as testament to what we can 
accomplish if we are relentless in our compassion for others and for 
the well-being of our communities.●
```
