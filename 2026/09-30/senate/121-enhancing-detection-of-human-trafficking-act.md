# ENHANCING DETECTION OF HUMAN TRAFFICKING ACT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 4307

```
              ENHANCING DETECTION OF HUMAN TRAFFICKING ACT

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 4307, which was received 
from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4307) to direct the Secretary of Labor to 
     train certain employees of Department of Labor how to 
     effectively detect and assist law enforcement in preventing 
     human trafficking during the course of their official duties, 
     and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time and passed, and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 4307) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
