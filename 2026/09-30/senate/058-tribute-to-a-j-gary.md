# TRIBUTE TO A.J. GARY

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO A.J. GARY

● Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize A.J. 
Gary upon his retirement as director of the Arkansas Division of 
Emergency Management, ADEM, and State Homeland Security adviser.
  For more than four decades, Gary has dedicated his career to serving 
others and protecting the people of Arkansas. Since being appointed to 
manage ADEM in 2016, he has provided steady leadership through some of 
the most challenging situations our State has faced.
  His experience has been integral to ADEM's response and recovery 
efforts across 49 State and federally declared disasters. In every 
instance, he demonstrated an understanding that emergency management is 
ultimately about helping people and worked tirelessly to make sure 
Natural State communities had the resources and support they needed 
after disaster struck.
  He also sought to improve Arkansas' ability to prepare for and 
recover from future emergencies. From expanding ADEM's field operations 
to modernizing the State's emergency coordination system, Gary has 
helped ensure we are better equipped to protect Arkansans.
  But Gary's commitment to public service did not begin with ADEM. He 
spent more than 28 years with the Conway Police Department, where he 
retired as police chief, and later served as chief security 
administrator for Air Transport International. Throughout each chapter 
of his career, he answered the call to serve and took on the 
responsibility of protecting others.
  His impact has also extended beyond Arkansas, having earned the 
respect of emergency management professionals across the country. In 
2025, he was appointed president of the National Emergency Management 
Association, reflecting the expertise and dedication he has brought to 
the field.
  On behalf of the people of Arkansas, I congratulate A.J. Gary on his 
retirement and thank him for his more than 40 years of public service. 
I wish him and his family all the best in this well-deserved next 
chapter.●

                          ____________________
```
