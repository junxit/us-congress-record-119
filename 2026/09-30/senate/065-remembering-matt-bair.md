# REMEMBERING MATT BAIR

**Speaking:** Young, Todd (Y000064) [R-IN]

```
                         REMEMBERING MATT BAIR

● Mr. YOUNG. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize the life 
of one of Indiana's most iconic voices; Matt Bair.
  For years, residents of Indianapolis and central Indiana relied on 
Matt Bair to get them to work in the morning and safely back home in 
the afternoon. As the beloved traffic reporter for 93.1 FM WIBC in 
Indianapolis, Matt not only provided listeners with information on 
which roads to take or avoid during their commute but also brightened 
their days with his good cheer and quick wit.
  Matt's passing in August leaves a notable absence, not just for his 
family and colleagues at WIBC, but for his community as well. A 
graduate of Rochester High School and IUPUI, Matt started his career in 
broadcasting at his family's radio station WROI-FM in Fulton County. 
This was followed by 16 years at WIBC, a role he took seriously, 
particularly on the Sunday of Memorial Day weekend. Year after year, he 
was up at 2:30 a.m. to help race fans find their way to the 
Indianapolis Motor Speedway for the Indy 500.
  He loved his job, but maybe not as much as he was loved by his 
colleagues. They admired not only his humor but his honesty. Matt was 
candid about his own struggles with addiction in the hope that his 
experiences could help others battling the same illness. The Supporting 
Sobriety Podcast, which Matt cohosted and for which he received a 
Marconi Radio Award nomination, was one of the many ways he used his 
journey and platform to comfort and encourage others.
  Matt was also an advocate and supporter of the Salvation Army Harbor 
Light Center and of Horizon House, an Indianapolis homeless shelter 
where he worked to build a library.
  Matt Bair's voice will be deeply missed in central Indiana. He leaves 
behind so many friends and fans, as well as an enduring legacy of 
generosity and compassion.●
```
