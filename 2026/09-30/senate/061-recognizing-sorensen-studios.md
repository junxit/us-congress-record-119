# RECOGNIZING SORENSEN STUDIOS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING SORENSEN STUDIOS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the

[[Page S5247]]

American entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to 
recognize Sorensen Studios of Adair County, IA, as Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  The initial idea for Sorensen Studios was born when Maria Galanakis 
realized she needed a helping hand to complete her journalism projects 
at Iowa State. Luckily for Maria, her future husband Ray ``Bubba'' 
Sorenson was an artist himself, with a particular talent for painting 
murals. During college, Bubba was inspired by the movie ``Saving 
Private Ryan'' and came up with the idea to honor veterans by painting 
a mural on a 12-foot-tall boulder. This was dubbed ``The Freedom 
Rock,'' with the first rock mural completed in 1999. Every Memorial 
Day, Bubba repaints the rock to update the design and highlight stories 
of Iowa veterans.
  As the couple continued to work together, they soon realized that 
Bubba's talent for painting and Maria's knack for photography and 
logistics was the perfect combination. In 2008, Maria and Bubba opened 
their own small business. Through the U.S. Small Business 
Administration, the Sorensens were able to get the capital they needed 
from local banks and build their business. As the business gained 
traction, the market for murals grew, inspiring Maria and Bubba to 
reconfigure Sorensen Studios to focus more on painting projects.
  In 2013, Bubba and Maria started the Freedom Rock Tour, with the goal 
of painting one rock mural in each of Iowa's 99 counties. Over the next 
10 years, Bubba traveled across the State, creating a Freedom Rock 
tailored to the history and veterans of each county. Today, all 99 Iowa 
counties have a Freedom Rock, and the 100th Freedom Rock was completed 
in Altoona, IA.
  Nearly two decades later, Sorensen Studios is still solely owned and 
operated by Maria and Bubba. After years of success, the pair started 
the Freedom Rock Foundation, a nonprofit dedicated to maintaining 
Freedom Rocks and governed by a board of veterans.
  The Sorensens have taken their mission to honor veterans through art 
nationwide, starting the 50 State Freedom Rock Tour, with Bubba 
painting messages of gratitude for American veterans across the 
country. In addition to Sorensen Studios, Bubba serves his community as 
a State representative for the 23rd district in the Iowa House of 
Representatives. The couple has worked hard over the past 18 years and 
acknowledge they could not have done it without the support of their 
family.
  I am pleased to recognize Bubba and Maria of Sorensen Studios for 
their creative entrepreneurship and longstanding commitment to our 
Nation's veterans. I look forward to their continued success and wish 
them the very best in the years ahead.●

                          ____________________
```
