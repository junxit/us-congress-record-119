# Introductory Statement on S. 5634

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 5634

```
      By Mr. PADILLA (for himself and Mr. Kim):

[[Page S5265]]

  S. 5634. A bill to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to provide 
additional amounts of loan forgiveness to teachers of English learners 
and teachers of bilingual and dual language immersion students, and for 
other purposes; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
Pensions.
  Mr. PADILLA. Mr. President, I rise to speak in support of the 
Supporting Providers of English Language Learning, SPELL, Act, which I 
introduced today.
  English learners are one of the fastest growing student populations 
in the country. Across the United States, about 10 percent of all publc 
school students are English learners. In California alone, nearly 19 
percent of all public school students identify as English learners. Yet 
we are facing a devastating shortage of teachers with the skills needed 
to support these students. If we do not address this shortage, the 
achievement gap for this vulnerable group of students will continue to 
grow.
  Educating students learning English requires specialized instruction 
and unique teacher strategies. If we are going to meet the needs of 
these students, we need to facilitate the training and retention of 
high-quality teachers for our English learners.
  The SPELL Act, would amend the Higher Education Act to provide up to 
$17,500 in Federal student loan forgiveness for teachers of English 
learners, bilingual students, or dual immersion students in high-need 
schools. This would match the level of loan forgiveness offered to 
teachers of other in-demand subjects such as math, science, and special 
education.
  By lessening the financial burden on prospective English learner 
teachers, we can help incentivize a greater number of passionate and 
highly qualified teachers to enter and stay in the classroom.
  As a Senator representing one of the most diverse States in the 
country with the most English learners and as an English learner 
myself, I am proud to work with my colleagues to push forward 
legislation to support our teachers. I want to thank Representative 
Espaillat for introducing this bill in the House, and I hope our 
colleagues will join us in supporting our English learner teachers and 
students.
```
