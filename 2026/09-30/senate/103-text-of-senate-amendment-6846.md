# Text of Senate Amendment 6846

**Speaking:** Gallego, Ruben (G000574) [D-AZ]

```
  SA 6846. Mr. GALLEGO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 9340, to amend the Public Utility Regulatory 
Policies Act of 1978 to establish a Federal standard relating to the 
recovery of the full, incremental costs of upgrades that serve large-
load customers, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. IMPOSITION OF DATA CENTER FRESHWATER CONSUMPTION 
                   TAX.

       (a) In General.--Chapter 38 of subtitle D of the Internal 
     Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the 
     following new subchapter:

    ``Subchapter E--Tax on Consumption of Freshwater by Data Centers

``Sec. 4691. Data Center Freshwater Consumption Tax.

     ``SEC. 4691. DATA CENTER FRESHWATER CONSUMPTION TAX.

       ``(a) In General.--For each calendar year, there is hereby 
     imposed on any AI data center a tax equal to 1 percent of the 
     amount paid or incurred by the AI data center operator with 
     respect to direct freshwater consumption by such AI data 
     center during such calendar year.
       ``(b) Payment of Tax.--
       ``(1) In general.--The tax imposed by this section with 
     respect to any AI data center shall be paid by the AI data 
     center operator.
       ``(2) Remittance of tax.--The AI data center operator shall 
     remit such tax annually to the Secretary at such time and in 
     such manner as provided by the Secretary.
       ``(c) Definitions.--In this section--
       ``(1) AI data center.--The term `AI data center' means a 
     facility or group of facilities primarily used to host 
     computing infrastructure for the training or deployment of 
     artificial intelligence or machine learning models, including 
     associated storage, cooling, and networking equipment.
       ``(2) AI data center operator.--
       ``(A) In general.--The term `AI data center operator' means 
     person who owns, operates, or occupies an AI data center.
       ``(B) Multiple operators.--In the case where more than 1 
     person meets the description under subparagraph (A) with 
     respect to an AI data center, the person responsible for 
     payment of the public water utility account to the public 
     water system shall be deemed to be the AI data center 
     operator for purposes of this section.
       ``(3) Direct freshwater consumption.--The term `direct 
     freshwater consumption' means the volume of water an AI data 
     center withdraws from a public water system (such as a 
     municipal water provider).''.
       (b) Effective Date.--The amendment made by this section 
     shall apply to calendar years beginning after the date of 
     enactment of this Act.
```
