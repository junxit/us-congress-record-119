# STOP INSIDER TRADING ACT

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 7008

```
                        STOP INSIDER TRADING ACT

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, I want to be clear: We should ban 
congressional stock trading. But H.R. 7008, the so-called Stop Insider 
Trading Act, is a political scam. It is a disingenuous attempt to use 
figleaf new ethics rules to pass a sweeping, unrelated voter 
suppression law, while not even adequately addressing congressional 
stock trading. Americans are rightly concerned that lawmakers' stock 
portfolios can create conflicts of interest and opportunities to cash 
in on inside information. But instead of resolving those issues, this 
bill creates superficial changes to ethics standards while undermining 
the right to vote. Americans will not be fooled by this charade.
  It is long past time that we gave Americans confidence that lawmakers 
are not profiting from positions of public trust. That is why we should 
impose a real and effective ban on congressional stock trading. This 
bill, however, fails to clean up these ethics issues. For example, it 
would allow Members of Congress to sell stock during their term in 
office, transactions that could appear to be based on nonpublic 
information, like a heads up that a company would face regulatory 
action. It also would permit members to own stock in industries they 
oversee through their committee assignments. Loopholes like these 
create too many opportunities for corruption and self-dealing.
  And under the pretext of cleaning up Congress, this legislation would 
enact photo voter ID requirements like those in the so-called SAVE 
America Act, making it harder for Americans to exercise their right to 
vote. Specifically, the bill would require voters to present one form 
of ID from an overly restrictive list of identification documents in 
order to vote, excluding other reliable forms of ID such as a student 
ID, which would disproportionately harm eligible young voters and 
communities of color. It also imposes onerous new requirements on vote 
by mail. Congress should not be creating more unnecessary barriers for 
eligible voters to exercise their foundational right to have their 
voices heard and votes counted in our elections.
  The Senate should be passing a genuine ban on stock trading by 
lawmakers. But I cannot support this bill, which is clearly just 
another attempt by Republicans in Congress to pick their voters--which 
have repeatedly failed--under the guise of ethics reform. I remain 
committed to working with my colleagues on real legislation to clean up 
these ethics concerns.

                          ____________________
```
