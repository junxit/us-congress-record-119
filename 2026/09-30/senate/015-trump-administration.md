# TRUMP ADMINISTRATION

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                          TRUMP ADMINISTRATION

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, well, for 2 years, Democrats fought 
fiercely to protect the interests of the American people while Senate 
Republicans fought tooth and nail to shield Donald Trump. We all knew 
how disastrous this President would be. We set out to expose how Trump 
and his party were hurting the American people, and we would do 
everything we could to stop them.
  We fought to lower the skyrocketing costs of Trump's economy: the 
cost of food, the cost of housing, the cost of energy, the cost of 
groceries, the cost of gasoline. Republicans pushed policies to drive 
those costs up.
  We exposed Trump for his unprecedented corruption; Republicans aided 
and abetted his grifts every step of the way.
  We worked relentlessly to control Trump's chaos that we see every 
day; Republicans encouraged him as he spiraled out of control.
  Later today, I will talk more about how Senate Democrats have worked 
together to block the worst Trump's GOP had to throw at the American 
people.
  Now, of course, there is still a lot of work remaining to see America 
through the storm this administration has unleashed. But Senate 
Democrats are well positioned to stand up for the American people with 
the same devotion, cooperation, and discipline we showed these past 2 
years.

                          ____________________
```
