# RATEPAYER PROTECTION ACT

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

**Measures:** H.R. 9340

```
                        RATEPAYER PROTECTION ACT

  Mr. HEINRICH. Mr. President, I rise in opposition to the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 9340.
  Electricity demand, as we all know, is growing, driven in large part 
by hyperscalers and data centers. Tech companies have promised to pay 
their fair share and not make American households pay the extra costs, 
but it is not enough to take their word for it, and it is not enough to 
merely tell States to consider--consider--making utilities charge data 
centers for all of the costs associated with the generation, additional 
transmission, and distribution upgrades necessary to serve them.
  States are already taking action. According to the Smart Electric 
Power Alliance, more than 30 States have adopted specific retail rate 
structures for large loads like data centers.
  H.R. 9340 simply does not protect Americans from high utility bills. 
Let me be clear. This bill has no--zero--requirement that data centers 
actually pay for their own grid upgrades. H.R. 9340 might make some 
Members of the Senate feel good about voting on a bill that sounds like 
it does something about data centers. Let me be clear. This is nothing 
more than a messaging bill on the eve of an election.
  Rather than voluntary pledges or vague suggestions to States, 
Congress needs to pass a law with real teeth. If voluntary pledges and 
suggestions worked, families and small businesses wouldn't already be 
staring down rate hikes right now.
  So let's require data centers to pay for their incremental costs and 
prohibit by law passing those costs on to consumers.
  I would urge my colleagues to vote no.

                          ____________________
```
