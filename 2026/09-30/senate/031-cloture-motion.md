  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair 
lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will 
state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 912, Keith Sonderling, of Florida, 
     to be Secretary of Labor.
         John Thune, Roger Marshall, Jim Banks, John Boozman, Ted 
           Budd, Tom Cotton, Mike Crapo, Katie Boyd Britt, Tommy 
           Tuberville, Tim Sheehy, Mike Rounds, Kevin Cramer, 
           Shelley Moore Capito, John Barrasso, Roger F. Wicker, 
           Alan Armstrong, Bill Hagerty.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
mandatory quorum call under rule XXII has been waived.

  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of Keith Sonderling, of Florida, to be Secretary of Labor, 
shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
