# CHOICES FOR INCREASED MOBILITY ACT OF 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 1703

```
               CHOICES FOR INCREASED MOBILITY ACT OF 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Finance be discharged and the Senate proceed to the immediate 
consideration of H.R. 1703.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1703) to amend title XVIII of the Social 
     Security Act to clarify payment rules for manual wheelchairs 
     under part B of the Medicare program.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill was ordered to a third reading and was read the third time.
  Mr. THUNE. I know of no further debate on the bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. If there is no further debate on the bill, the 
bill having been read the third time, the question is, Shall the bill 
pass?
  The bill (H.R. 1703) was passed.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
