# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM], Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. HEINRICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that privileges 
of the floor be granted to Michael Kellermann, serving a detail from 
the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to the Committee on Natural 
Resources, and Francisco Alvarez, serving a fellowship from Sandia 
National Laboratories to the Committee on National Resources, for the 
duration of their assignments to the committee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the following 
fellows in my office be given floor privileges for the remainder of the 
119th Congress: Alandrea Harris, Stephanie Meredith, Jada Owens, Dana 
Moffat, Kathleen Hart, and Jessica Nielson.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
