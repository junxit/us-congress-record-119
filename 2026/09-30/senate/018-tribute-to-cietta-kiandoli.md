# TRIBUTE TO CIETTA KIANDOLI

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO CIETTA KIANDOLI

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, and finally, on a much happier note. 
Nearly a decade ago, I made one of the greatest decisions anyone in 
this line of work can make: I hired a top-notch, first-rate person who 
was so talented in every way. I hired Cietta Kiandoli.
  That was a time with a lot of change. Our party was still finding its 
footing after a stinging election loss. I had just become Democratic 
leader. My staff was expanding.
  We were determined to rally our coalition and make it bigger and 
stronger than ever before. Everyone my team and I talked to, we said we 
needed someone to create that coalition, help glue it together--
mentioned the same person. They said: Hire Cietta.
  Cietta joined my leadership staff as director of external affairs, 
but the title doesn't adequately capture all that Cietta has done and 
does for this country. I am not sure any title could.
  Cietta has always had the same star to connect people, policy, and 
politics, and to do it in such an exquisite and effective way. That is 
what she did before joining my team, that is what she did for the past 
decade on my team, and that is what she will continue to do as she 
starts her next chapter.
  Name any group in our big tent--any labor union, advocacy 
organization, caucus--the chances are they have not only met Cietta but 
have known her for years, and they most likely adore her.
  Cietta's job isn't easy. She gets a lot of telephone calls from 
various members of our coalition. Sometimes, they call her about a hard 
vote. Sometimes they call her about a fire that needs to be put out. 
Sometimes--actually, many times--they call her begging for her to get 
me to switch up the jokes that I repeat at the events, year after year 
after year.
  She has had moderate, marginal success in that regard. If they only 
knew how many times she has heard my jokes. And I see her eyes roll 
when I laugh at my own joke that I have told for the hundredth time.
  I am sure she will miss hearing me talk about the can of peas, the 
barber bookie, and all of my other greatest hits.
  On a more serious note, there are plenty of people who know how to 
command respect, but there are fewer who can match Cietta's ability to 
command the trust of any room she walks into.
  She is a truth-teller, and she tells the truth all the time. So they 
know, when she says something, it is the real deal. I have seen that 
hundreds--hundreds--of times to the benefit of all of us, myself, and 
the coalitions that we bring together.
  It doesn't matter who she is meeting. They can tell from the jump 
that her heart is in the right place. They can see how much she cares 
about serving New Yorkers and Americans from one end of the country to 
the other.
  So just as others have come to trust Cietta, so have I. She is among 
the people I trust most. She is a truth-teller.
  My staff knows that I move a mile a minute. When I get riled up, 
Cietta knows how to get me to slow down, to think about the bigger 
picture. Just having her in the room during a meeting or on the line 
during an important call helps put me at ease.
  I imagine Cietta won't miss the long days and late-night calls from 
yours truly, but I hope she knows how much we are going to miss her, 
her good humor, her calm, her willingness to share her wisdom with 
whoever happens to need it.
  Cietta, I look forward to seeing what you will do next, but I know 
what it will be. It will keep bringing people together for the good of 
the country. You will continue making everyone around you better--and 
she will always fight. You will always fight to connect people, policy, 
and politics.
  Cietta, thank you for your friendship. Thank you for your dedication. 
Thank you for your loyalty. Thank you for caring so much about this 
country and the people you have fought so hard for.
  You will always be a member of the Schumer family, and we wish you 
Godspeed.
  I yield the floor.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from California.
```
