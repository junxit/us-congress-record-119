# CLERGY ACT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 227

```
                               CLERGY ACT

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Finance be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 227 and the 
Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 227) to allow a period in which members of the 
     clergy may revoke their exemption from Social Security 
     coverage, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 227) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
