# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
GOLDEN DOME
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces concluded 
a hearing to examine an update on Golden Dome, after receiving 
testimony from David E. Mosher, Director, National Security Division, 
Congressional Budget Office; David J. Trachtenberg, National Institute 
for Public Policy, Oakton, Virginia; and Tom Karako, Center for 
Strategic and International Studies, Washington, D.C.
JACK SMITH'S AUTHORITY OVERSIGHT
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded an oversight hearing to 
examine Jack Smith's authority and Republicans and related matters, 
after receiving testimony from Jack Smith, Former Special Counsel, 
Department of Justice.
UNITED STATES PATENT AND TRADEMARK OFFICE OVERSIGHT
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Intellectual Property 
concluded an oversight hearing to examine the United States Patent and 
Trademark Office, after receiving testimony from John A. Squires, Under 
Secretary for Intellectual Property and Director of the United States 
Patent and Trademark Office, Department of Commerce.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
