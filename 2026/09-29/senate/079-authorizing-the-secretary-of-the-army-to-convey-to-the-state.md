# AUTHORIZING THE SECRETARY OF THE ARMY TO CONVEY TO THE STATE OF NORTH CAROLINA A CERTAIN PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY LOCATED AT FORT BRAGG, NORTH CAROLINA, ON WHICH THE STATE SHALL CONSTRUCT A VETERANS...

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 10159

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SECRETARY OF THE ARMY TO CONVEY TO THE STATE OF NORTH 
CAROLINA A CERTAIN PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY LOCATED AT FORT BRAGG, NORTH 
  CAROLINA, ON WHICH THE STATE SHALL CONSTRUCT A VETERANS' STATE HOME

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Armed Services be discharged from further consideration of 
H.R. 10159 and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 10159) to authorize the Secretary of the Army 
     to convey to the State of North Carolina a certain parcel of 
     real property located at Fort Bragg, North Carolina, on which 
     the State shall construct a veterans' State home, and for 
     other purposes.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 10159) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
