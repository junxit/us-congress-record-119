# RECOGNIZING LUDLOW'S STEAKHOUSE

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING LUDLOW'S STEAKHOUSE

● Ms. ERNST. Madam President, as chair of the Senate Committee 
on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize 
Ludlow's Steakhouse of Wayne County, IA, as the Senate Small Business 
of the Week.
  For more than two decades, Ludlow's Steakhouse has been a staple of 
the Wayne County community. Ron and Maureta Redmon first opened the 
restaurant in 2004 and in 2013, they sold the business to Craig and 
Brenda Onstot. In 2017, Truett and Sheena Pershy took ownership. Under 
their leadership, Ludlow's has expanded its menu and launched a 
catering business. From custom-cut aged steaks, seafood, pasta, 
sandwiches, and more, Ludlow's offers something for everyone to enjoy. 
In 2022, Ludlow's was named runner-up in the Iowa Pork Producer's 
Association's Best Breaded Pork Tenderloin Contest, earning statewide 
recognition for its food.
  In 2024, the Pershys relocated the restaurant to an 18,000-square-
foot former

[[Page S5160]]

American Legion building. Following major renovations, they transformed 
the building into a destination for dining, lodging, and community 
gatherings. Today, the property brings Ludlow's Steakhouse, the Brick 
Tavern, the Brick Event Center, and the Brick Suites together under one 
roof.
  Truett credits Sheena's continued support as instrumental to the 
growth of the business, and together, they are proud of the team they 
have built. Ludlow's currently employs 22 people, including Iowans who 
work at the restaurant while attending high school. Truett and Sheena 
take pride in giving their employees an opportunity to develop valuable 
skills and work experience before they graduate.
  That commitment to people extends throughout Wayne County. As members 
of the City of Corydon Chamber of Commerce, Ludlow's supports local 
schools and regularly donates to community fundraisers and benefits. 
Whether providing a young person with their first job, supporting a 
local cause, or creating a place where neighbors can gather, Truett, 
Sheena, and their employees continue to give back to the community that 
has supported Ludlow's over the past two decades.
  I am pleased to recognize Truett and Sheena Pershy and the entire 
Ludlow's Steakhouse team for their dedication to Corydon and Wayne 
County. I look forward to seeing their continued success and wish them 
the very best in the years ahead.●
```
