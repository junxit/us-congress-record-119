# EXPANDING ACCESS TO CAPITAL FOR RURAL JOB CREATORS ACT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 1190

```
         EXPANDING ACCESS TO CAPITAL FOR RURAL JOB CREATORS ACT

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs be discharged from 
further consideration of H.R. 1190 and the Senate proceed to its 
immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1190) to amend the Securities Exchange Act of 
     1934 to expand access to capital for rural-area small 
     businesses, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1190) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
