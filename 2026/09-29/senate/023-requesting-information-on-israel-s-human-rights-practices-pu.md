# REQUESTING INFORMATION ON ISRAEL'S HUMAN RIGHTS PRACTICES PURSUANT TO SECTION 502B(c) OF THE FOREIGN ASSISTANCE ACT OF 1961--Motion to Discharge

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 852

```
 REQUESTING INFORMATION ON ISRAEL'S HUMAN RIGHTS PRACTICES PURSUANT TO 
   SECTION 502B(c) OF THE FOREIGN ASSISTANCE ACT OF 1961--Motion to 
                               Discharge

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 2304(c) and in 
accordance with section 502B(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961, 
I move to discharge the Committee on Foreign Relations from further 
consideration of S. Res. 852.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read as follows:

       Motion to discharge from the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations, S. Res. 852, a resolution requesting information 
     on Israel's human rights practices pursuant to section 
     502B(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961.

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays are ordered.
  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, I want to thank my colleague from 
Vermont Senator Welch, thank Senator Sanders, who was here earlier, 
Senator Kaine, and all of our colleagues who have cosponsored a very 
important resolution demanding accountability--demanding accountability 
in the killings of nine American citizens on the West Bank in just the 
last 4 years. And as both the Biden administration and the Trump 
administration have said, if we don't get accountability, then these 
killings will continue with impunity.
  So this is about saving American citizens' lives. It is also a 
recognition that if we can't get accountability for American citizens, 
imagine how hard it is for a Palestinian to get accountability for 
human rights abuses committed against them--and about the Americans 
being currently detained in Israeli military prisons and the hundreds 
of Palestinian kids being detained as we speak.
  So this is a resolution that simply requires a report--a report on 
what the U.S. Government is doing to protect its own citizens. I would 
hope that every Member of this body would support a resolution that 
does that and calls for a review of the human rights situation and 
conditions on the West Bank.
  So with that, Mr. President, I would ask that the previously 
scheduled vote begin immediately.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                      Vote on Motion to Discharge

  The question is on agreeing to the motion to discharge.
  The yeas and nays were previously ordered.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Louisiana (Mr. Cassidy) and the Senator from Alaska (Mr. 
Sullivan).
```
