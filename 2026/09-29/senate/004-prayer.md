# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. This morning, I am happy to introduce Dr. J. 
Blair Blackburn, president of East Texas Baptist University, from 
Marshall, TX, and he will lead us in prayer this morning.
  The guest Chaplain offered the following prayer:
  Join me in prayer.
  Dear Heavenly Father, Creator of Heaven and Earth, author and 
perfecter of our faith, To You, O Lord, not to us--to You--be the glory 
and honor.
  And Father, we come to You this morning with hearts full of 
gratitude. We recognize that You are sovereign over every nation, every 
authority, and every generation. God, in a world that is turning their 
face from You, may the United States of America be a people who seeks 
Your face.
  We lift before You these esteemed Members of the U.S. Senate and 
entrust their service to our Nation unto Your providence. Grant them 
discerning hearts and eyes attentive to Your work in America and around 
the world. In the weight and the complexity of their responsibilities, 
Father, remind our Senators that true leadership ultimately is an act 
of stewardship. Shape the passion of their hearts and establish the 
work of their hands to serve others with humility, with integrity, and, 
Father, with love.
  Lord, convict our congressional leaders, President Trump, Vice 
President Vance, the Cabinet, and our Supreme Court Justices to be 
instruments of Your peace. As it has been stated, where there is 
division, cultivate reconciliation; where there is disagreement, kindle 
charity; where there is uncertainty, grant wisdom; and where there is 
despair, Father, awaken hope. But where there is darkness, let Your 
light prevail. Help these leaders of our Nation seek to understand 
others and, Father, to serve rather than to be served and to lead with 
grace and mercy.
  Surround these leaders with wise and faithful people who will 
strengthen them, Father, when they are weary, counsel them, Lord, when 
they are uncertain, and guide them toward whatever is true and noble, 
whatever is right and pure, Father, whatever is lovely and admirable.
  In the midst of our differences, Lord, remind us that our deepest 
hope is not found in political power, human institutions, or earthly 
kingdoms, but in You, God, whose kingdom will never end.
  I offer this prayer in the Name above every name, the King of Kings 
and Lord of Lords, my Savior and Lord, Jesus Christ. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
