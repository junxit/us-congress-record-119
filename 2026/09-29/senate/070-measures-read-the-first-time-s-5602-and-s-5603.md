# MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 5602 AND S. 5603

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 5602, S. 5603

```
           MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 5602 AND S. 5603

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I understand that there are two bills at 
the desk, and I ask for their first reading en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bills by title for the 
first time.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 5602) to prohibit the use of Federal funds for 
     the planning and construction of any structure or monument at 
     Memorial

[[Page S5184]]

     Circle at Columbia Island/Lady Bird Johnson Park in the 
     District of Columbia.
       A bill (S. 5603) to prohibit the demolition of Presidential 
     memorials.

  Mr. THUNE. I now ask for a second reading, and I object to my own 
request, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection having been heard, the bills 
will receive their second reading on the next legislative day.
```
