# SENATE RESOLUTION 921--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE THAT THE ADVERTISEMENTS PROMOTING PRESIDENT DONALD TRUMP THAT AIRED IN SEPTEMBER 2026, WHICH INCLUDED THE DIRECT LANGUAGE "PAID FOR BY THE...

**Measures:** S.Res. 921

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 921--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE THAT THE 
ADVERTISEMENTS PROMOTING PRESIDENT DONALD TRUMP THAT AIRED IN SEPTEMBER 
    2026, WHICH INCLUDED THE DIRECT LANGUAGE ``PAID FOR BY THE U.S. 
  GOVERNMENT,'' SHOULD NOT BE PAID FOR BY THE UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

  Ms. HASSAN submitted the following resolution; which was referred to 
the Committee on Appropriations:

                              S. Res. 921

       Resolved, That it is the sense of the Senate that the 
     advertisements promoting President Donald Trump that aired in 
     September 2026, which included the direct language ``paid for 
     by the U.S. Government,'' should not be paid for by the 
     United States government.
```
