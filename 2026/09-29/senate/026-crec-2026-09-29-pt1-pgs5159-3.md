                        TRIBUTE TO JOHN McKENIZE

● Mr. BANKS. Madam President, I rise today to recognize John 
McKenzie, who will retire from his role as the President and CEO of the 
Indiana Credit Union League at the end of this year. Throughout his 
career, John has led many key initiatives to benefit Indiana's credit 
unions, their leaders, their employees, and the 2.8 million credit 
union members in Indiana.
  His work has involved strengthening the credit unions' advocacy 
effectiveness, including the 1991 battle to preserve the independence 
of the National Credit Union Share Insurance Fund, the 1998 effort to 
protect field of membership expansion for Federal credit unions, 
protecting Federal tax exemption and interchange income, and State-
level work to help pass legislation to expand State-chartered credit 
union field of membership options.
  The Indiana Credit Union League's service capability and commitment 
to collaboration have grown during John's tenure as a result of many 
efforts, including building a card services program and establishing a 
self-insured association health plan, both of which have provided 
millions of dollars in total cost savings to participating credit 
unions and, at the same time, built the organization's financial 
strength. He was also at the forefront of developing the State's shared 
branching network, where the league continues to provide managerial and 
operational support for 300 locations statewide.
  John came to the league in 1986 after working as executive vice 
president of the State's corporate credit union, arriving at a time 
when the organization had under $1 million in retained earnings, and he 
has overseen the growth to nearly $40 million, reinforcing the 
financial strength of the league and its ability to support Hoosier 
credit unions.
  His other contributions to the credit union system have included 
leading fundraising efforts among credit unions to establish the 
Indiana Military Veterans Hall of Fame and to serve on that board, 
serving on the Credit Union National Association Board for the benefit 
of credit unions across the country, and working with the World Council 
of Credit Unions to host representatives from around the world, which 
resulted in receiving the Distinguished Service Award.
  I am glad that, on October 2, 2026, John will be inducted into the 
Indiana Credit Union Hall of Fame.
  He will retire from the Indiana Credit Union League at the end of 
this year, after 40 years of legacy building that will benefit Indiana 
credit unions, the league, and credit union members long into the 
future.●
