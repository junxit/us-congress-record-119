# TRIBUTE TO ALBEY BROCK

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ALBEY BROCK

● Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I rise today to honor County 
Judge-Executive Albey Brock for his 19 years of service to the people 
of Bell County, KY. Since 2007, Albey has presided over Bell County's 
Fiscal Court, working closely with constituents on local initiatives 
while creating and administering the county budget. He is a proud 
eastern Kentuckian whose longtime dedication and service to the region 
warrants recognition.
  Over the years, I have worked with Albey on numerous initiatives to 
ensure eastern Kentuckians have access to critical infrastructure. 
During the pandemic, I partnered with Albey to secure Federal funding 
for the Middlesboro-Bell County Airport which sustained the airport's 
daily operations, protected jobs, and offset additional costs. We also 
worked together on conservation efforts to secure Fern Lake so the 
people of Middlesboro and residents throughout the county have access 
to a significant water source and attraction at Cumberland Gap National 
Park.
  Our work didn't stop there. Albey and I also worked together to 
secure Pineville Hospital's future when it threatened to be closed. 
Through all these partnerships and collaborative efforts, Albey 
repeatedly demonstrated his commitment to the future of his community 
and its economic interests.
  In the time I have known him, Albey has been a fierce advocate for 
eastern Kentucky. From his advocacy efforts at the national level on 
behalf of coal miners to making sure his constituents benefit from 
local economic development, he has a keen eye for the human impact of 
policy decisions. As a county judge-executive, this skill is one of the 
most important, and I applaud his impressive dedication to his 
neighbors.
  At the same time, he has worked to promote eastern Kentucky economic 
development, showing up when it matters. This includes the Mountain 
Laurel Festival--Kentucky's oldest, continuous festival--held every 
year in Bell County. I have been able to attend on several occasions, 
and it is always good to see Albey out in the community. Not only does 
he show up, but Albey also knows how to deliver results for the people 
he represents, and I am grateful for his hard work.
  Through it all, Albey has made decisions with consideration for his 
immediate community and worked to improve not just his county, but the 
region at large as well. As his time leading Bell County comes to an 
end, I want to take a moment today to honor his service. I wish him the 
best in this next chapter of his life, and I am sure he looks forward 
to spending more time with his wife Bitha as they enjoy time with their 
children and grandchildren.●

                          ____________________
```
