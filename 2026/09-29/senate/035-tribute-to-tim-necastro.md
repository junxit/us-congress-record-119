# TRIBUTE TO TIM NeCASTRO

**Speaking:** McCormick, David (M001243) [R-PA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO TIM NeCASTRO

● Mr. McCORMICK. Madam President, I rise today to honor Tim 
NeCastro on the occasion of his retirement as president and chief 
executive officer of Erie Insurance, effective December 31, 2026, 
capping a distinguished 30-year career with the company.
  An Erie native and graduate of Cathedral Preparatory School and 
Gannon University, Mr. NeCastro joined Erie Insurance in 1996 following 
his work at the Plastek Group and Ernst & Young. He rose through roles 
in finance, services, and sales, led the company's expansion into 
Kentucky in 2014. He was named president and CEO in 2016. Under his 
leadership, Erie Insurance grew to nearly $13 billion in premium and 
more than 7 million policies in force across 12 States and the District 
of Columbia, celebrated the company's 100th anniversary in 2025, and 
remains the largest employer and only Fortune 500 company headquartered 
in Erie County, PA.
  Mr. NeCastro's impact extends well beyond the company he led. As 
founding board president of the Erie Downtown Development Corporation 
and board chairman of Infinite Erie, he has helped drive more than a 
billion dollars in transformational investment in his

[[Page S5163]]

hometown. He has also served the community through leadership roles 
with the Mazzini Charitable Foundation, the Erie Community Foundation, 
Team Pennsylvania, the American Property Casualty Insurance 
Association, the Pennsylvania Society, and the Robert Irvine 
Foundation.
  Alongside his wife Lisa, Mr. NeCastro has been a devoted supporter of 
causes across the region, leading the campaign that raised $18.5 
million to renovate and expand the expERIEnce Children's Museum and 
currently cochairing United Way of Erie County's Uniting for Education 
endowment campaign to support local students. Following his retirement 
as CEO, he will continue to serve the Erie community as president of 
the Erie Insurance Foundation.
  On behalf of the people of Pennsylvania, I congratulate Tim NeCastro 
on a remarkable career and thank him for three decades of service to 
Erie Insurance, his tireless work to revitalize his hometown, and his 
lasting commitment to Pennsylvania. I wish him and Lisa the very best 
in retirement.●
```
