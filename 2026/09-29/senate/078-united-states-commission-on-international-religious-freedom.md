# UNITED STATES COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 1744

```
      UNITED STATES COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM 
                      REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Foreign Relations be discharged from further consideration 
of H.R. 1744 and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1744) to extend and authorize annual 
     appropriations for the United States Commission on 
     International Religious Freedom through December 31, 2028.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
considered read a third time and passed and that the motion to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1744) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
