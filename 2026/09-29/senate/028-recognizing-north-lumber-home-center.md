# RECOGNIZING NORTH LUMBER & HOME CENTER

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                 RECOGNIZING NORTH LUMBER & HOME CENTER

● Ms. ERNST. Madam President, as chair of the Senate Committee 
on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize and 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize 
North Lumber & Home Center of Decatur County, IA, as Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  For more than four decades, North Lumber & Home Center has helped 
provide the materials and supplies that keep homes, farms, and 
businesses across southern Iowa running. Sally Dykes and her family 
purchased North Lumber from its previous owners in 1980. The family has 
since grown North Lumber into North Lumber & Home Center, expanding its 
inventory and operations to better serve customers throughout Decatur 
County and the surrounding communities.
  Today, Sally runs the business alongside her son Tony Dykes. The 
mother and son duo and their five North Lumber employees have continued 
to find ways to expand the business under Sally's ownership. North 
Lumber moved into a larger building 5 years ago, remodeling the space 
while keeping the original building for additional storage. In 2025, 
the business expanded again with the construction of 60-by-104 storage 
building to accommodate its growing inventory. In 2002, the family 
opened Southern Iowa Building Center in Lamoni, which also employs five 
people. In recognition of their contribution to the area, the two 
businesses were jointly named the Decatur County Business of the Year 
in 2023.
  For Sally and Tony, serving southern Iowa extends well beyond the 
lumberyard. North Lumber & Home Center supports local school 
basketball, football, and baseball programs, as well as Central Trail 
Future Farmers of America, the Boy Scouts, performing arts boosters, 
after-prom events, Little League, and career and technology education 
at Central Decatur Schools. The business has also supported the Decatur 
County Sheriff's National Night Out, the Leon and Grand River rodeos, 
the Leon Queen Contest, local cancer fundraising efforts, and Friends 
of Decatur County Hospital. Their generosity has even reached beyond 
Iowa, including a donation supporting a Lamoni livestock relief effort 
bound for Oklahoma in March 2026.
  I am pleased to recognize Sally, Tony, and the teams at North Lumber 
& Home Center and Southern Iowa Building Center for their generosity 
and commitment to helping Decatur County grow. I look forward to their 
continued success and wish them the very best in the years 
ahead.●
```
