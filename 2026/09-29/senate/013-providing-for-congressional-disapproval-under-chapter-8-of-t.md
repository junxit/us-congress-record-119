# PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL UNDER CHAPTER 8 OF TITLE 5, UNITED STATES CODE, OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE & MEDICAID SERVICES OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN...

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI], Tillis, Thomas (T000476) [R-NC], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 197

```
  PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL UNDER CHAPTER 8 OF TITLE 5, 
UNITED STATES CODE, OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE & 
   MEDICAID SERVICES OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES 
RELATING TO ``PATIENT PROTECTION AND AFFORDABLE CARE ACT, HHS NOTICE OF 
 BENEFIT AND PAYMENT PARAMETERS FOR 2027; AND BASIC HEALTH PROGRAM''--
                           Motion to Proceed

  Ms. BALDWIN. Madam President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 456, 
S.J. Res. 197.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 456, S.J. Res. 197, a 
     joint resolution providing for congressional disapproval 
     under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services of 
     the Department of Health and Human Services relating to 
     ``Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act, HHS Notice of 
     Benefit and Payment Parameters for 2027; and Basic Health 
     Program''.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There will now be 10 minutes of debate equally 
divided.
  Ms. BALDWIN. Madam President, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in stopping President Trump from terminating healthcare for up 
to 2 million Americans on the Affordable Care Act.
  Let's back up for a second. President Trump has made it clear that he 
does not like the Affordable Care Act. He has vowed and tried to repeal 
it for over a decade with absolutely no plan to replace it. That would 
leave 23 million Americans without insurance.
  We are talking about hourly employees and contractors in the gig 
economy. We are talking about small business owners on our Main 
Streets. And we are talking about the one in four farmers who are 
already being pummeled from every side by higher costs brought on by 
President Trump.
  Americans banded together and, so far, we have stopped President 
Trump and congressional Republicans from completely repealing the 
Affordable Care Act. But that hasn't stopped the President from doing 
everything he can to chip away at, and hollow out, marketplace 
coverage.
  Republicans and President Trump refuse to keep tax credits that help 
working families afford their coverage. This alone is causing premiums 
to double for 22 million Americans and has pushed more than 2\1/2\ 
million Americans off their care because they simply could not afford 
it.
  They have slashed the funding by 90 percent for navigators, the very 
experts who help people find affordable health insurance.
  Just last week the Trump administration kicked hundreds of thousands

[[Page S5140]]

of Americans off their insurance with no details as to why.
  That brings us to this rule that undermines Americans' healthcare, 
jacks up cost, terminates coverage for millions more Americans. 
President Trump's policy will promote catastrophic health plans with 
extremely high deductibles and out-of-pocket costs, luring Americans 
into selecting plans that cover next to nothing when they get sick or 
injured. We call these junk plans.
  The effort will also loosen physician network requirements, leaving 
Americans with fewer providers who will take their insurance. And it 
will allow insurers to offer nonnetwork plans to make it easier for 
insurers to raise out-of-pocket costs, sell new kinds of junk insurance 
coverage, increase deductibles, cover fewer services, and kick more 
providers out of network.
  The result will be more Americans buying worse care that does not 
cover necessary services, from routine checkups to life-threatening 
emergencies. It will leave working families holding the bag for medical 
expenses.
  The Trump administration itself says that this will kick 2 million 
Americans off their Affordable Care Act coverage while jacking up costs 
on millions more. Benefits could be reduced for tens of millions of 
Americans, meaning folks could lose access to coverage for addiction 
treatment, hearing aids, or other critical services. Plus, the rule 
would pile on redtape, increasing the paperwork burden of nearly 5 
million Americans.
  Americans understand what a lifeline this program is for a cancer 
patient, Americans with disabilities, working families, and 
entrepreneurs. On behalf of the 23 million Americans who rely on the 
Affordable Care Act for their health insurance, we cannot allow this 
administration to continue the attack, raise their costs, and undermine 
their ability to access quality care.
  Right now, Americans are unequivocal: they cannot bear the high costs 
of life. The war in Iran is causing gas and grocery prices to 
skyrocket, our farmers are struggling from needless trade wars, and now 
the President is raising the costs of healthcare for millions of 
Americans.
  I urge my colleagues to stand with the vast majority of Americans who 
support the Affordable Care Act and stop this misguided rule that would 
jeopardize care for millions of Americans.
  I yield the floor.
  Mr. TILLIS. Madam President, Republicans yield back all time.
```
