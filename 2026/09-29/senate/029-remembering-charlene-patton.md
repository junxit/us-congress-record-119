# REMEMBERING CHARLENE PATTON

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS]

```
                      REMEMBERING CHARLENE PATTON

● Mr. MORAN. Madam President, I rise today to honor Kansan 
Charlene Patton, a tireless advocate for American agriculture who 
passed away on September 20, 2026.
  As a U.S. Senator representing a farm State, I often find myself 
educating my colleagues and other officials in Washington, DC, about 
the vital role agriculture plays in our economy, our communities, and 
our tables. Agricultural advocacy is an important responsibility, and 
few Kansans fulfilled that mission more fully than Charlene Patton.
  As we mourn her passing, my thoughts are with her family, friends, 
colleagues, and the many Kansans whose lives she touched.
  For more than 50 years, Charlene dedicated her career to advancing 
nutrition education and promoting the critical role of agriculture. 
From her service with the Kansas Department of Agriculture to her work 
with the Kansas Soybean Commission and the Kansas Wheat Commission, she 
became one of Kansas agriculture's most recognizable and trusted 
voices. She shared her expertise through media appearances, educational 
programs, cookbooks, and her contribution in developing the ``From the 
Land of Kansas'' trademark program.
  Her morning segment, ``Cooking with Charlene,'' was viewed by many 
Kansans over the years. For more than five decades, her demonstrations 
aired on WIBW-TV, where she combined practical cooking advice with 
lessons about Kansas agriculture, helping viewers better understand the 
connection between the food on their tables and the farmers who produce 
it.
  Through her work and television appearances on WIBW-TV, KAKE-TV and 
KOAM-TV, she shared recipes, nutritional education and agricultural 
insights with audiences across the State. Her work brought Kansas 
agriculture into kitchens, making it relatable and accessible to 
generations of Kansans.
  Her passion for agriculture, food, and community service was rooted 
in her experience with 4-H. As both a member and later an adult 
volunteer serving Jefferson County, Shawnee County, and the State 
organization, she dedicated her time to help develop future generations 
of food and nutritional leaders.
  Charlene's legacy extends far beyond the recipes she shared or the 
programs she championed. She helped generations of Kansans better 
understand the value of Agriculture, encouraged healthy family meals, 
and helped increase appreciation for the hard-working producers who 
feed our Nation.
  On behalf of the U.S. Senate, I am grateful for Ms. Patton's lifetime 
of service, advocacy, and dedication to Kansas agriculture. Her 
contributions will continue to be felt through the communities she 
strengthened, the lives she touched, and the lasting impact she leaves 
on the State of Kansas.●
```
