# CLAIMING AGE CLARITY ACT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 5284

```
                        CLAIMING AGE CLARITY ACT

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Finance be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 
5284 and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5284) to require the Social Security 
     Administration to make changes to the social security 
     terminology used in the rules, regulation, guidance, or other 
     materials of the Administration.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
considered read a third time and passed and that the motion to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 5284) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
