# TRIBUTE TO GARY MOORE

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO GARY MOORE

● Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I rise today to honor my 
friend Gary Moore, county judge-executive of Boone County, KY, and 
former president of the Kentucky Association of Counties. Gary was 
first elected in 1998, and after 27 years of service, he will be 
retiring with a long list of achievements deserving of recognition.
  Gary's exposure to public service began at a young age. His father 
served as Pendleton County Sheriff, inspiring Gary with his love for 
helping others. It is clear to me that Gary adopted his father's work 
ethic and commitment to his community.
  This commitment extended beyond the borders of Boone County to the 
entire Commonwealth. I was proud to work alongside Gary when he served 
as president of the Kentucky Association of Counties, helping chart a 
course for the future of Kentucky. It was also my privilege to 
administer Gary's oath of office when he became president of the 
National Association of Counties.
  On the regional level, Gary completed a term as president of the 
Ohio-Kentucky-Indiana Regional Council of Governments. These roles are 
far more than titles; they reflect dedication to collaboration at the 
State, regional, and national levels and are a testament to the 
leadership skills I have long known Gary to possess. Inside Boone 
County, he worked diligently to protect the health and prosperity of 
his community, which includes his work fighting the opioid epidemic 
that has plagued the lives of so many of our neighbors. I am grateful 
to have had him as a partner in this fight, and we continue to make 
progress each and every day.
  As one of the fastest growing counties in the Commonwealth, Boone 
County has faced increasing demands on its transportation 
infrastructure. Recognizing these needs, we worked together to deliver 
meaningful improvements. I was proud to help in this effort by securing 
Federal investments to improve interstate highways 75 and 71, providing 
commuters with much-needed updates. Gary has also worked tirelessly 
with community members to improve the local road system, particularly 
along the Limaburg Road Corridor. These infrastructure projects 
strengthened access to the Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky Airport, 
facilitating further economic development while enhancing safety for 
hard-working Kentuckians who drive to work every day. There is no doubt 
Judge Moore has left Boone County better than he found it, and I am 
thankful for his dedication.
  A lifelong Kentuckian and a fine public servant, Gary has spent his 
career in service to his community and to our Commonwealth at large. 
Today, I honor his work and wish him the best as he enjoys retirement 
alongside his wife Linda, spending time with their children and 
grandchildren.●

                          ____________________
```
