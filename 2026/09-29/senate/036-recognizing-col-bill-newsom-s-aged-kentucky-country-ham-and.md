# RECOGNIZING COL. BILL NEWSOM'S AGED KENTUCKY COUNTRY HAM AND NEWSOM'S OLD MILL STORE

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
 RECOGNIZING COL. BILL NEWSOM'S AGED KENTUCKY COUNTRY HAM AND NEWSOM'S 
                             OLD MILL STORE

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, as a member of the Senate Committee 
on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, I enjoy highlighting Kentucky 
businesses that represent the very best of the American entrepreneurial 
spirit. Today, I am proud to recognize Col. Bill Newsom's Aged Kentucky 
Country Ham and Newsom's Old Mill Store of Princeton, KY.
  Western Kentucky has a tradition of doing things the old-fashioned 
way, and you would be hard-pressed to find a better example than 
Newsom's Old Mill Store in Princeton. The shop opened its doors on New 
Year's Day back in 1917, founded by H.C. Newsom. More than a century 
later, it is still right where it started--serving the local community 
and curing some of the finest hams in the country.
  Today, the shop is run by H.C.'s granddaughter Nancy Newsom Mahaffey, 
though most folks around Caldwell County simply know her as The Ham 
Lady. Nancy learned the trade directly from her father Colonel Bill 
Newsom, and she hasn't varied the original blueprint. In an era where 
most food production is rushed, Newsom's still does it by hand: pork, 
salt, brown sugar, slow hickory smoke, and the patience to let the 
seasonings do the heavy lifting.
  In 2009, one of Nancy's 2-year-aged hams was invited to hang in Spain 
at the famous Jamon Museum, the first and only American country ham 
ever given that distinction. Their dry-cured ham and prosciutto have 
fed guests at Presidential inaugurations and earned praise from food 
critics across the country.
  When you walk into the store at 208 East Main Street today it feels 
like stepping back in time. The shelves are packed with local sorghum, 
homemade chow-chow, jams, and candies. More importantly, when you call 
to ask a question or place an order, Nancy and her team still pick up 
the phone themselves. Her guiding rule has always been simple: ``We 
make service personal.''
  Congratulations to Nancy Newsom Mahaffey and everyone at Newsom's Old 
Mill Store. Thank you for keeping a great Kentucky tradition alive, and 
I wish you many more years of success.●

                          ____________________
```
