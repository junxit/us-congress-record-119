# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Eternal God, into Your hands we commend our longtime colleague, 
Congressman Bill Posey, in deepest gratitude that You allowed us to 
share and serve together in the journey of life, in service to this 
country, and in the work of finding common ground for the common good.
  Thank You for Bill's principled and pragmatic approach to the issues 
he devotedly pursued on behalf of his constituents. Even more, we thank 
You for his example of what it means to remain strongly and 
passionately committed to the people You called him to serve.
  Even while his death brings forth many tributes and remembrances of 
his public service, we pay special mind to those who loved him 
privately and personally. Grant that Katie, the love of his life; his 
daughters, Cathi and Pamela; and their families may always know of Your 
presence in his absence. Grant them an awareness of Your abiding love 
through the outpouring of condolences and the indwelling grace of their 
many memories.
  Truly, O God, Representative Bill Posey has fought the good fight. He 
has finished the race, and he has kept the faith.
  Grant him now the crown of righteousness from his Lord, the righteous 
judge, who now praises him for his good and faithful service.
  In Your redeeming name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
