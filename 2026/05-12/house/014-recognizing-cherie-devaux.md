# RECOGNIZING CHERIE DeVAUX

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                       RECOGNIZING CHERIE DeVAUX

  (Mr. McGARVEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, nothing matches the pageantry and 
excitement of the Kentucky Derby.
  For 2 weeks, Louisvillians prepare for the most exciting 2 minutes in 
sports, and this year's derby was one of the best yet.
  As the horses rounded the fourth turn, 23-1 long shot Golden Tempo 
came from the back of the pack, ridden by Jose Ortiz, to finish first, 
charging into the record books and the history books in the process 
because Golden Tempo wasn't the only one to beat the odds this derby.
  In fact, when he crossed the finish line, trainer Cherie DeVaux 
officially became the first woman trainer to ever win the Kentucky 
Derby. Think about this: 152 derbies; 2,053 horses have made it to the 
starting gates; only 18 of them have been trained by women; and, now, 1 
is in the winner's circle.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Cherie on behalf of all Louisvillians, 
Kentuckians, and horse boys and girls the world over. I thank her and 
wish her the best of luck in this year's Belmont.

                          ____________________
```
