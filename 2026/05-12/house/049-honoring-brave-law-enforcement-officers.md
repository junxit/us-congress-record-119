# HONORING BRAVE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                HONORING BRAVE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the brave 
law enforcement officers who have made the ultimate sacrifice while 
serving and protecting our communities.
  Dedicated officers put their lives on the line every single day. 
Their selflessness and determination to keep us safe serve as a 
powerful reminder of the risk they face in the line of duty.
  We remember their names and the legacy they leave behind, and their 
memories will forever be etched in our hearts and minds.
  Let us stand united in gratitude ensuring that their sacrifices are 
never forgotten.

                          ____________________
```
