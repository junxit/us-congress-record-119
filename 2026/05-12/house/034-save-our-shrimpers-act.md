financing foreign shrimp farms, and for other purposes, as amended, on

pass the bill, as amended.

  Mses. ESCOBAR, MOORE of Wisconsin, and Mr. JACKSON of Illinois 
changed their vote from ``yea'' to ``nay.''
  Mr. JACKSON of Texas, Ms. SANCHEZ, Messrs. PALLONE, DAVIS of

the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The title of the bill was amended so as to read: ``A bill to require 
the United States Executive Directors at the international financial 
institutions to oppose certain projects involving shrimp production.''.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
