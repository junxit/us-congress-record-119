# TRUMP ACHIEVES DEREGULATION

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                      TRUMP ACHIEVES DEREGULATION

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am grateful that 
President Donald Trump has achieved deregulation, created jobs, and 
strengthened opportunities for American families and new graduates.
  In the first 100 days, President Trump achieved his goal by reducing 
regulations. This goal continues as, last month, House Republicans, led 
by Speaker   Mike Johnson, passed two bills that refocused on 
deregulation, improved infrastructure, lowering costs for families, and 
choosing results over radicalism. Promises made, promises kept.
  Superstars of deregulation are: Education Secretary Linda McMahon, 
EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin, Energy Secretary Chris Wright, and NRC 
Commissioner David Wright.
  Mr. Speaker, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength through 
revealing war criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump, 
providing satellite targeting information to kill Americans and 
Israelis.
  Courageous Ukrainians provide whistles to children at night to help 
in recovery efforts as war criminal Putin bombs their homes.
```
