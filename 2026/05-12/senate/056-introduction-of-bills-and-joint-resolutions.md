**Measures:** S. 4488, S. 4489, S. 4490, S. 4491, S. 4492, S. 4493, S. 4494, S. 4495, S. 4496, S. 4497, S. 4498, S. 4499, S. 4500, S. 4501, S. 4502, S. 4503, S. 4504, S. 4505

              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. CORTEZ MASTO (for herself and Mr. Young):
       S. 4488. A bill to amend title 23, United States Code, to 
     include school bus route safety in the safe routes to school 
     program, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. DAINES (for himself and Mr. Sheehy):
       S. 4489. A bill to authorize the Dry-Redwater Regional 
     Water Authority System in the State of Montana, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mrs. MURRAY (for herself, Mr. Wyden, Mr. Van Hollen, 
             Mr. Booker, and Ms. Alsobrooks):
       S. 4490. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide a tax on the assets of trusts, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. SHEEHY (for himself and Mrs. Shaheen):
       S. 4491. A bill to enhance diplomatic engagement on 
     international biotechnology and biosecurity matters; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Mr. VAN HOLLEN (for himself, Mr. Moran, Ms. 
             Klobuchar, and Mr. Tillis):
       S. 4492. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide matching payments for ABLE account contributions 
     by certain individuals, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. VAN HOLLEN (for himself, Mr. Moran, Ms. 
             Klobuchar, and Mr. Tillis):
       S. 4493. A bill to clarify the use of direct deposit for 
     contributions to ABLE programs; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. DURBIN (for himself, Mr. Blumenthal, Ms. 
             Duckworth, and Mr. Van Hollen):
       S. 4494. A bill to prioritize funding for an expanded and 
     sustained national investment in biomedical research; to the 
     Committee on Appropriations.
           By Mr. MARKEY (for himself and Mr. Merkley):
       S. 4495. A bill to require reports on the dangers posed by 
     nuclear reactors in areas that might experience armed 
     conflict; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Ms. ALSOBROOKS (for herself and Mr. Justice):
       S. 4496. A bill to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 
     to provide for additional uses of funds for grants to 
     strengthen historically Black colleges and universities, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND (for herself and Mr. Cruz):
       S. 4497. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide tax relief relating to public safety officers' 
     death benefits, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Mr. MORAN (for himself, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Tillis, 
             and Ms. Klobuchar):
       S. 4498. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to make expiring ABLE provisions permanent, improve 
     accessibility and education for families, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. TILLIS (for himself and Mr. Hickenlooper):
       S. 4499. A bill to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     require the Secretary of

     Health and Human Services to expand and enhance the National 
     Healthcare Safety Network to establish real time infectious 
     disease and public health surveillance capabilities across 
     nursing homes in the United States, and for other purposes.
           By Mr. KENNEDY (for himself and Mr. Booker):
       S. 4500. A bill to direct the Attorney General to establish

     violent incident clearance and technology investigative

           By Mr. CORNYN:
       S. 4501. A bill to bar aliens from admission to the United

     Judiciary.
           By Mr. OSSOFF:
       S. 4502. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986

           By Mrs. MURRAY (for herself, Ms. Murkowski, Ms.

             Padilla):
       S. 4504. A bill to improve the full-service community 
     school program, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Ms. ERNST (for herself, Mr. Padilla, Mr. Barrasso,
