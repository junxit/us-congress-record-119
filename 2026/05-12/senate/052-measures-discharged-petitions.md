# MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITIONS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 188

```
                     MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITIONS

       We, the undersigned Senator, in accordance with chapter 8 
     of title 5, United States Code, hereby direct that the Senate 
     Committee on Environment and Public Works be discharged of 
     further consideration of S.J. Res. 188, a joint resolution 
     providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``National 
     Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: Coal- and 
     Oil-Fired Electric Utility Steam Generating Units: Final 
     Repeal,'' and, further, that the resolution be immediately 
     placed upon the legislative Calendar under General Orders.

           S. King, Jr., Richard Blumenthal, Martin Heinrich,

           Bernard Sanders, Corey A. Booker, Mark R. Warner, 
           Raphael G. Warnock, Andy Kim, Charles E. Schumer, Amy 
           Klobuchar, Mazie K. Hirono, Alex Padilla, Lisa Blunt 
           Rochester, Mark Kelly, Gary C. Peters, Richard J.
```
