              RECOGNIZING GREEN REAL ESTATE & AUCTION CO.

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Green 
Real Estate & Auction Co. of Sac County, IA, as the Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  In 1872, Col. Festus J. Green emigrated from Somersetshire, England. 
At just 14 years old, he started building a career in farming, masonry, 
and auctioneering. In 1894, Col. F.J. Green & Son was officially 
founded and laid the groundwork for what would eventually become known 
as Green Real Estate & Auction Co. This five-generation family business 
is rooted in hard work, entrepreneurship, and a commitment to serving 
rural Iowa communities.
  Over the past 132 years, Green Real Estate & Auction Co. has grown 
alongside Iowa's agricultural economy while maintaining its family-
centered values and exceptional customer service. Today, under the 
leadership of Jim Green, a fifth-generation auctioneer and real estate 
broker, the business continues to provide auctioneering, both farmland 
and residential real estate services, and farm consignment to clients 
across western Iowa. The company has become a trusted resource for 
families, farmers, and businesses navigating property sales, estate 
transitions, and agricultural transactions. With a team of under a 
dozen employees, the business supports local jobs while contributing to 
the vibrancy of Sac City and the broader Sac County community.
  In addition to its longstanding success, Green Real Estate & Auction 
Co. reflects the strong, civic-minded spirit that defines Iowa small 
businesses. The Green family supports local initiatives, including 
participation in county fair activities that remain central to the 
region's agricultural traditions. Beyond Sac County, Green Real Estate 
has active membership in the National Auctioneers Association, Iowa 
Auctioneers Association, and the United Counties Board of Realtors, 
demonstrating their continued involvement in the industry. This 
commitment to local and national engagement demonstrates Green Real 
Estate's dedication to entrepreneurship and economic growth in Sac 
County.
  The Greens' business also remains active with local charitable 
efforts. The business supports Pheasants Forever, local schools, and 
health-related community benefits. Green Real Estate has also held 
auctions benefiting the local 4-H program and other charitable causes 
throughout Sac County. Even as the business has expanded across 
generations, the Green family remains committed to preserving the 
personal service, trust, and community relationships that have defined 
the company for more than a century.
  I am pleased to recognize Jim Green, along with the entire team at 
Green Real Estate & Auction Co., for their dedication to Sac City and 
Sac County. I look forward to their continued success and wish them the 
very best in the years ahead.●
