# HONORING MORGAN G. HOBEN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING MORGAN G. HOBEN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
remarkable servant, Mrs. Morgan Hoben.
  Mrs. Morgan Hoben, a native of Natchez, Mississippi, is a graduate of 
Natchez-Adams High School, Class of 2002.
  With nearly a decade of service in the Natchez-High School District 
and two years in the classroom, she brings both experience and passion 
to her role as a high school teacher of Journalism, Oral Communication, 
and Debate. Mrs. Hoben's educational journey reflects her deep 
commitment to learning and growth. She is a graduate of Copiah-Lincoln 
Community College (Natchez Campus), The University, where she earned 
advanced credentials in education.
  Her diverse academic background informs her dynamic teaching style 
and her ability to connect with students across disciplines. In the 
classroom, Mrs. Hoben is known for creating engaging, real-world 
learning experiences that empower students to find their voice, think 
critically, and communicate with confidence. She is especially passion 
about developing student leaders through journalism and debate, while 
also serving as a strong advocate for the arts and creative expression.
  Beyond the classroom, Mrs. Hoben has a long-standing history of 
service to others and is an active member of several professional and 
community organizations. Her work reflects a deep belief in the power 
of education to transform lives an strengthen communities. Mrs. Hoben 
is married to Jason Hoben, and together they are the proud parents of 
two sons, Jack and Michael.
  She remains committed to uplifting her students, her school, and her 
hometown of Natchez through education, service, and advocacy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Morgan 
Hoben for her actions and achievements in the Natchez, Mississippi 
community.

                          ____________________
```
