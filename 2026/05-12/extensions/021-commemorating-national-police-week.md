# COMMEMORATING NATIONAL POLICE WEEK

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                   COMMEMORATING NATIONAL POLICE WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to commemorate 
National Police Week.
  As we honor the courage, sacrifice, and unwavering dedication of our 
Nation's law enforcement officers, we pay special tribute to those who 
made the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty. Their selfless service 
and devotion to protecting our communities will never be forgotten.
  As a Representative from Pennsylvania, I am especially honored to 
recognize the 19 fallen officers from our Commonwealth whose names were 
added this year to the National Law Enforcement Officers Memorial Wall. 
Their commitment to serve and protect stood above all personal 
interest, reflecting the very best of our Nation.
  In my district, we are fortunate to be served by exceptional law 
enforcement professionals. Recently, I had the opportunity to meet with 
Blair County Sheriff Jim Ott, whose leadership in coordinating 
Pennsylvania's annual law enforcement memorial service in Hollidaysburg 
demonstrates the deep respect owed to those who have fallen in the line 
of duty. His example reminds us that honoring these heroes is not 
simply a responsibility--it is a solemn privilege.
  I offer my enduring gratitude and appreciation--this week and every 
week--for every man and woman who wears the badge.

                          ____________________
```
