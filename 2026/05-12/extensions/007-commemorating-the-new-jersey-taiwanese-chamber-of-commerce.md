# COMMEMORATING THE NEW JERSEY TAIWANESE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
       COMMEMORATING THE NEW JERSEY TAIWANESE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the New Jersey 
Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce, which has supported Taiwanese American 
entrepreneurs and professionals throughout New Jersey. As a member of 
the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of North America, the New Jersey 
Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce remains steadfast in their commitment to 
serving Taiwanese professionals across the Garden State by promoting 
networking, resource sharing, and community engagement opportunities.
  I extend my warm congratulations to the New Jersey Taiwanese Chamber 
of Commerce as they celebrate their Annual Gala and President Handover 
Ceremony. This esteemed non-profit organization has made significant 
strides in promoting trade and commerce throughout New Jersey by 
strengthening economic ties between the United States and Taiwan. 
Moreover, the New Jersey Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce commitment to 
community service is evident through their active participation in 
local cultural events and their ongoing support for education.
  The New Jersey Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce is well known for their 
charitable contributions, annual conventions, and community festivals 
at Bridgewater Commons in Bridgewater, Somerset County. In addition, 
the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of North America engages the next 
generation of business leaders through youth empowerment activities 
within their Junior Chapter, placing a strong emphasis on mentorship 
and career development.
  This organization provides its members with valuable opportunities to 
exchange business insights, technology updates, and management 
experiences. Through a variety of initiatives, including large-scale 
gatherings for entrepreneurs throughout the Greater New York-New Jersey 
area, the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of North America fosters 
connections and celebrates the many accomplishments of the community.
  The New Jersey Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce has consistently 
demonstrated a strong commitment to serving Taiwanese Americans here in 
New Jersey. I am confident that their impact and involvement will 
continue to strengthen the community for years to come.

                          ____________________
```
