# HONORING LEELANAU COUNTY CLERK MICHELLE CROCKER FOR 30 YEARS OF DISTINGUISHED SERVICE

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
    HONORING LEELANAU COUNTY CLERK MICHELLE CROCKER FOR 30 YEARS OF 
                         DISTINGUISHED SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and to recognize Michelle 
Crocker, the longest serving county clerk currently in office in the 
state of Michigan, as she marks the 30th anniversary of her swearing in 
as Leelanau County Clerk.
  Since assuming office in 1996, Michelle Crocker has demonstrated a 
steadfast commitment to public service and the people of Leelanau 
County. Her time as county clerk reflects not just longevity, but an 
unwavering dedication to the responsibilities entrusted to her office. 
Her career in public service is not limited to her time as county 
clerk: in fact, she has spent 45 years serving in local government 
starting with Clerk Dorothy Wunderlich in 1981, making her the longest 
serving employee in county government. Throughout her distinguished 
career, she has helped guide Leelanau County through significant 
changes, including the conversion of county financial systems from 
paper spreadsheets to computerized financial programs. Her leadership 
has ensured that local government continues to operate efficiently and 
transparently.
  Michelle Crocker's service has not gone unnoticed. In 2021, she was 
presented with a special tribute by the Michigan State Legislature for 
her steadfast leadership through unprecedented challenges and evolving 
expectations in local government. Her ability to meet the demands of 
public service with professionalism and a genuine commitment to others 
sets a strong example for public officials across our state.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Michelle Crocker for her 
lasting contributions to Leelanau County and the State of Michigan. On 
behalf of Michigan's 1st Congressional District, I extend my sincere 
appreciation for her extraordinary service as she celebrates this 
remarkable milestone.

                          ____________________
```
