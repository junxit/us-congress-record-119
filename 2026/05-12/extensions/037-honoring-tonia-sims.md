# HONORING TONIA SIMS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                          HONORING TONIA SIMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Mrs. Tonia Sims, a visionary event producer, creative 
director, and entrepreneur whose work has elevated the standard of 
excellence in the Mississippi Delta and beyond.
  As the founder and driving force behind TLS Weddings & Special 
Events, Mrs. Sims has distinguished herself as a leader in the event 
production industry, known for curating sophisticated, experience-
driven celebrations that blend modern elegance with cultural 
authenticity. Through her keen eye for luxury aesthetics and her 
unwavering commitment to client experience, she has transformed 
countless weddings, milestone celebrations and large-scale social 
events into unforgettable moments marked by precision, creativity, and 
intentional design.
  Under her leaderships the TLS brand has expanded beyond traditional 
event services to include signature event series, high profile day 
parties, and immersive social experiences that attract diverse 
audiences and set a new benchmark for elevated entertainment in the 
region. Her productions ranging from upscale cocktail festivals to 
themed experiential gatherings are widely recognized for their vibrant 
energy, premium quality, and attention to detail.
  Mrs. Sims' contributions extend well beyond her professional 
achievements. She has demonstrated a deep and ongoing commitment to 
community engagement and youth empowerment. Through the TLS Delta 
Entrepreneurial Scholarship, her organization awarded $10,000 in 
scholarships in 2025 to aspiring young entrepreneurs and is poised to 
continue this impactful initiative. Additionally, TLS provides 
scholarships to local high school seniors, reinforcing her dedication 
to education, economic growth, and the development of future leaders.
  Her excellence and influence have earned recognition from respected 
organizations and institutions, including the Delta Business Journal, 
Higher Purpose Co., Alpha Kappa Alpha

[[Page E436]]

Sorority, Inc., and the City of Greenville. These honors reflect not 
only her business success but also her meaningful contributions to the 
broader community.
  At the heart of Mrs. Sims' journey is her strong foundation of faith 
and family. She credits her success to her faith in God, the support of 
her dedicated team, and the unwavering encouragement of her husband, 
Larnelle Bush, and their children, Kylon, Kaci, and Kelby. Together, 
they embody the values of perseverance, service, and excellence that 
continue to guide her work. Mrs. Sims has established TLS as more than 
an event brand--it is a platform connection, celebration, and 
opportunity.
  Through innovation, professionalism, and a commitment to purpose, she 
continues to raise the bar for what is possible in her field.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mrs. Tonia 
Sims for her outstanding contributions to entrepreneurship, her 
community, and the advancement of creative excellence. Her work serves 
as an inspiration, and her impact will undoubtedly continue to be felt 
for years to come.

                          ____________________
```
