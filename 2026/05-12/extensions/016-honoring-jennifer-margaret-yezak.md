# HONORING JENNIFER MARGARET YEZAK

**Speaking:** McCaul, Michael T. (M001157) [R-TX]

```
                    HONORING JENNIFER MARGARET YEZAK

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL T. McCAUL

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. McCAUL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Jennifer Margaret 
Yezak for more than 40 years of extraordinary contributions to 
agriculture, the State of Texas, and our Nation. A proud graduate of 
Texas A&M University, Jennifer's career reflects deep expertise in 
federal policy and public administration.
  From serving as a legislative assistant to U.S. Senators to her 
leadership as Deputy Commissioner of the Colorado Department of 
Agriculture, Jennifer has consistently championed the needs of rural 
America. Her distinguished federal service includes senior roles at the 
U.S. Department of Agriculture, where she served as Senior Advisor to 
the Secretary and Director of External and Intergovernmental Affairs, 
strengthening vital ties between the White House, Congress, and state 
officials.
  Earlier in her career, she advocated for all 50 state agriculture 
departments at the National Association of State Departments of 
Agriculture and held key roles within the Texas Department of 
Agriculture and the Texas House Committee on Agriculture and Livestock.
  Throughout her distinguished tenure, Jennifer has been an 
indispensable asset to my office, serving as a vital bridge between 
complex federal policy and the practical needs of our agricultural 
community. Her profound expertise is matched only by her graciousness; 
she is an accomplished professional who consistently exceeded the call 
of duty to support my staff, the university, and the countless Texans 
who depend on her guidance. Jennifer's dedication has left an 
unforgettable mark on all who have had the privilege of working 
alongside her.
  Beyond her professional accolades--including the 2017 Texas 
Agricultural Lifetime Leadership Distinguished Alumna Award--Jennifer 
remains deeply rooted in her community and her Polish heritage. Growing 
up in a family that founded the Bremond Press, she carries forward a 
legacy of civic engagement through her active support of local 
leadership and the annual Polish Pickle Run. Jennifer has ``planted 
trees'' through her career that will provide

[[Page E431]]

shade for generations of Texas Aggies, farmers, and ranchers.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Jennifer's exceptional 
service and wishing her continued success in all her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
