# HONORING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF BOB WALSH

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
                HONORING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF BOB WALSH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 12, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Bob Walsh, a 
distinguished member of the Bernardsville community, as he celebrates 
his 100th birthday--a milestone that reflects a century of service, 
character, and devotion to country and community.
  Bob Walsh is a proud veteran of the United States Marine Corps, 
having served honorably as a radio telephone operator and radar 
operator in the Pacific Theater during World War II. In answering our 
Nation's call, he demonstrated the courage, duty, and sacrifice that 
define the American servicemember. His exemplary service earned him the 
American Campaign Medal, the Asiatic-Pacific Campaign Medal with one 
Bronze Star, the Army of Occupation Medal, and the World War II Victory 
Medal.
  Following the war, Bob Walsh continued to embody the highest ideals 
of citizenship. Through his integrity, kindness, and quiet leadership, 
he has earned the admiration of all who know him. He remains an active 
and proud member of the Bernardsville VFW Memorial Post 7858, 
continuing a lifetime of commitment to fellow veterans and community 
members.
  Bob Walsh's life stands as a testament to the spirit of the Greatest 
Generation--a generation whose strength of character and unwavering 
patriotism shaped our Nation. His story inspires all of us and serves 
as a reminder of the enduring values that strengthen our communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Bob 
Walsh's 100 years of life, honoring his distinguished military service, 
and expressing our profound gratitude for his contributions to our 
Nation. We extend our warmest wishes to him and his family on this 
remarkable occasion.
```
