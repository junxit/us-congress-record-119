# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O Lord, our God, to the faithful, You show Yourself faithful; to the 
blameless, You show Yourself blameless; to the pure, You show Yourself 
pure.
  Would that we remain faithful today that we may see how You remain 
steadfast in Your love for us. Would that we prove blameless today that 
we may understand how Your hand in our lives is just and purposeful. 
Would that we remain pure of thought, word, and deed, that we may 
reflect Your virtue.
  Then remind us that when we perceive You to be capricious, or 
contrary, or cruel, it is the deception in our own souls that obscures 
our understanding of Your grace and mercy in our lives.
  Humbly we approach You in Your righteousness. Do not let our pride 
endure Your judgment.
  In Your perfect name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
