                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BURCHETT:
       H.R. 11128. A bill to require people who manufacture for 
     sale, sell, introduce or deliver for introduction in 
     interstate commerce, or import into the United States 
     clothing or shoes to disclose any hazardous chemical in such 
     clothing and shoes, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Energy and Commerce.

       H.R. 11129. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of

     Committee on Ways and Means.

     employees, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Education and Workforce.

     murder more consistently, to provide sound capital sentencing

     judgments subject to United States Supreme Court review, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

       H.R. 11133. A bill to require the Secretary of Veterans

     other purposes; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
