# RECOGNIZING THE DETROIT PEOPLE'S PLATFORM AND EQUITABLE DETROIT COALITION FOR TEN YEARS OF COMMUNITY BENEFITS ORDINANCE ORGANIZING

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE DETROIT PEOPLE'S PLATFORM AND EQUITABLE DETROIT 
   COALITION FOR TEN YEARS OF COMMUNITY BENEFITS ORDINANCE ORGANIZING

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Detroit People's 
Platform and Equitable Detroit Coalition as they celebrate ten years of 
the city's Community Benefits Ordinance (CBO) giving Detroiters a voice 
in major development projects. In 2016, thanks to the tireless 
organizing and advocacy of countless people working together under the 
banner of the Detroit People's Platform and Equitable Detroit 
Coalition, more than 100,000 Detroiters voted to establish the 
country's first citywide community benefits agreement ordinance.
  In the ensuing decade, thousands of Detroiters have participated in 
CBO meetings and hundreds of residents have served on Neighborhood 
Advisory Councils that give voice to community concerns when 
significant public incentives are offered to developers. The Detroit 
People's Platform and Equitable Detroit Coalition have organized 
residents on a project-by-project basis to secure stronger community 
benefits packages, and this advocacy has resulted in the Detroit City 
Council approving several amendments to strengthen the CBO.
  As the work to build thriving communities for all continues, we are 
enormously grateful to the visionary leaders, organizers, and activists 
who helped establish Detroit's CBO a decade ago. The city of Detroit is 
stronger as a result of the fight for community benefits as Detroiters 
joined together to advance equity and Justice.
```
