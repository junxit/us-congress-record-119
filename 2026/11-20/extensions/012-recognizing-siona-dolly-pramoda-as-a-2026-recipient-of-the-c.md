# RECOGNIZING SIONA "DOLLY" PRAMODA AS A 2026 RECIPIENT OF THE CONGRESSIONAL AWARD GOLD MEDAL

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
    RECOGNIZING SIONA ``DOLLY'' PRAMODA AS A 2026 RECIPIENT OF THE 
                     CONGRESSIONAL AWARD GOLD MEDAL

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Siona ``Dolly'' 
Pramoda, a 2026 recipient of the Congressional Award Gold Medal for the 
District of Puerto Rico.
  The United States Congress established the Congressional Award in 
1979 through Public Law 96-114 to recognize initiative, service, and 
achievement among young Americans. Created as a bipartisan effort in 
both the House of Representatives and the Senate, the program provides 
young people with an opportunity to develop the values of service, 
personal growth. physical fitness, and citizenship through meaningful, 
character-building experiences.
  The Congressional Award is a non-partisan, voluntary, and non-
competitive program in which participants set and pursue goals across 
four areas: Voluntary Public Service, Personal Development, Physical 
Fitness, and Expedition/Exploration. Through these experiences, 
participants are encouraged to challenge themselves, serve others, and 
develop skills that will benefit them throughout their lives. The Gold 
Medal, the highest level of recognition offered through the 
Congressional Award program, reflects the dedication and perseverance 
required to pursue and achieve meaningful goals in each of these areas.
  A graduate and valedictorian of The Baldwin School of Puerto Rico's 
Class of 2024, Dolly has demonstrated an outstanding commitment to 
academic excellence, leadership, and community service. As Co-Founder 
and Global President of SafeTeensOnline, she has helped advance 
cybersecurity awareness among more than five million students across 
five countries. Her dedication to empowering young people has earned 
her national recognition, including being named to Forbes' 30 Under 30 
Class of 2024 and receiving the White House's Girls Leading Change 
honor. Her accomplishments reflect a remarkable commitment to using 
education and innovation to make a meaningful difference in the lives 
of others.
  It is my privilege to recognize Dolly for earning the Congressional 
Award Gold Medal and to congratulate her on this achievement. Her 
accomplishment reflects the values at the heart of this program and the 
importance of providing young people with opportunities to grow, serve, 
and prepare for the future.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Dolly 
Pramoda on earning the Congressional Award Gold Medal and wishing her 
continued success in her future endeavors.
```
