# RECOGNIZING MACKEY BENTLEY FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
 RECOGNIZING MACKEY BENTLEY FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE 
                           OF REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mackey Bentley for 
his outstanding service to the constituents of New York's 12th 
Congressional District.
  A son of Tennessee, Mackey graduated from Tennessee State University 
with a bachelor's degree in political science and holds a master's 
degree in public administration from Indiana University. He was first 
able to utilize his passion for helping people and the governmental 
skills he acquired through his studies serving as a Constituent Liaison 
in the District Office of Representative Jim Cooper during his last 
term in office. He continued this work moving to New York in 
Assemblymember Danny O'Donnell's office as his Communications Director 
prior to joining my office in November 2024 as a Community Liaison.
  Mackey quickly deepened his knowledge of the local concerns of my 
constituents in the district, proving himself to be a leader on housing 
and infrastructure issues. He has adeptly worked with community groups 
and our partners in local government to navigate the tensions of 
needing to drastically improve crumbling housing infrastructure while 
centering the stated desires of the residents of Fulton & Elliot-
Cheslea New York City Housing Authority (NYCHA) Houses that are 
undergoing a Rental Assistance Demonstration (RAD) conversion. He has 
advocated to increase funding in wrap-around services for the homeless, 
as well as for resources for our arts community, which contributes to 
the fabric of our city as a cultural capital of the world.
  He has also skillfully represented my transportation interests in 
community meetings. Together we worked to ensure Route 9A remained an 
at-grade urban boulevard, and as a green space for New Yorkers to enjoy 
safely and connect us to our beloved waterfront. Because events in the 
district do not always adhere neatly to a 9-5 schedule, I thank Mackey 
for enthusiastically attending a myriad of community board meetings, 
neighborhood task forces and advisory councils, and town halls that 
occurred during the evening and over the weekends.
  After I announced my decision not to seek re-election, Mackey worked 
diligently to organize my physical files, spanning over three decades 
of my Congressional career to ensure they could be properly archived 
and accessible to historians and researchers for generations to come.
  Mackey's desire to better understand legislation that can best 
improve the lives of our constituents was always in the foreground of 
his work. He is a solutions-oriented team player, stepping up in ways 
that went above and beyond his defined role whenever he anticipated an 
unmet need in the office. His insights gleaned from working in a 
Southern Congressional office provided him with a unique and insightful 
perspective on political strategy that enhanced my office's community 
relations team.
  His steady, grounded personality coupled with his affable demeanor 
made Mackey a

[[Page E1273]]

true asset to my office. I wish him luck on his next endeavor and hope 
he continues to enjoy exploring New York, especially the Bushwick 
neighborhood where he lives with his wife Juliette Lefaivre Bentley and 
their pets Moony and Wilma.
```
