# CELEBRATING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF AMERICAN FEDERATION OF TEACHERS' LOCAL 1650

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
 CELEBRATING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF AMERICAN FEDERATION OF TEACHERS' 
                               LOCAL 1650

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 60th Anniversary 
Celebration of American Federation of Teachers' (AFT) Local 1650, 
celebrating six decades of dedication to faculty advocacy, collective 
bargaining, and the advancement of higher education in our community.
  Chartered in 1966 at Henry Ford Community College, AFT Local 1650 has 
represented fall-time faculty and established a meaningful voice in 
college governance. Through its efforts, the organization has secured 
fair contracts, improved compensation and benefits, and strengthened 
professional standards for education.
  Over the years, AFT Local 1650 has demonstrated a strong commitment 
to both faculty and students by supporting quality instruction and 
equitable working conditions. Its work has contributed to a 
collaborative academic environment and the continued success of Henry 
Ford Community College. It is impossible to honor the work of Local 
1650 without recognizing John McDonald, who served as President of the 
union for 43 years until his passing in 2021. Under McDonald's 
leadership, Local 1650 negotiated strong contracts featuring shared 
governance, community service, wages, post tenure review, distance 
education, intellectual property, and professional development.
  With the celebration of this milestone, Michigan's 12th Congressional 
District honors the lasting impact of AFT Local 1650 and John McDonald 
and expresses our appreciation for its continued service to our 
community and dedication to educational excellence.
```
