# RECOGNIZING PAM WRIGHT'S RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
                  RECOGNIZING PAM WRIGHT'S RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Pam Wright 
as she retires after 32 years of dedicated service to the people and 
businesses of the Grand Strand.
  Throughout her career with the Greater Myrtle Beach Collaborative and 
the Myrtle Beach Area Chamber of Commerce, Pam has worked tirelessly to 
support local businesses, foster meaningful relationships, and 
strengthen our region's economy. Her efforts have connected business 
owners with the resources and opportunities they need to succeed while 
encouraging collaboration throughout the Grand Strand.
  Pam's dedication and accomplishments have earned her national 
recognition, including two Lifetime Achievement Awards and 14 
membership sales recognitions from the Association of Chamber of 
Commerce Executives. In 2025, she was also honored with the Carolinas 
Association of Chamber of Commerce Executives' 30 Years of Service 
Award, recognizing her longstanding contributions to the chamber 
profession.
  A graduate of Leadership Grand Strand Class 36, Pam has demonstrated 
a continued commitment to leadership and community development. Her 
contributions earned her nominations for the program's Ann DeBock 
Leadership Award in both 2025 and 2026, reflecting the respect and 
admiration of those who have had the privilege of working alongside 
her.
  Pam's commitment to the Grand Strand extends well beyond her 
professional responsibilities. Throughout her career, she has 
generously devoted her time to numerous charitable organizations, 
including United Way, the American Cancer Society, the Alzheimer's 
Association, and Rotary International. As an active member of Midtown 
Vineyard Church, she has also helped welcome international exchange 
workers to our community through the church's All Nations Cafe.
  Even as Pam enters this next chapter of her life, her dedication to 
serving others will continue through her involvement in the Greater 
Myrtle Beach Collaborative's Ambassador Program. Her willingness to 
remain engaged in the community speaks to the genuine passion for 
service that has defined her career.
  Pam's decades of service reflect a deep commitment to the people and 
businesses that make the Grand Strand such a special place.

[[Page E1271]]

Her hard work, generosity, and dedication have made a lasting impact on 
our community. I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Pam on 
her remarkable career and wishing her all the best in her well-deserved 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
