# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF WESTERN GOLF & COUNTRY CLUB

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
     HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF WESTERN GOLF & COUNTRY CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Western Golf & Country Club 
on the occasion of its 100th anniversary, as they celebrate a century 
of excellence, tradition, and community in Redford Township, Michigan.
  Founded in 1926, the Club is known as the ``Club of Good Fellowship'' 
and has long stood as a premier, member-owned institution. Its course, 
designed by Donald Ross, reflects a legacy of craftsmanship, while its 
culture reflects a commitment to camaraderie and sportsmanship. 
Generations of members have gathered there to build friendships, host 
community events, and celebrate important milestones.
  The Club's history shows both vision and resilience. It grew from 
farmland into a vibrant recreational community with diverse amenities. 
With remarkable resilience following the 2018 clubhouse fire, the club 
reopened its doors quickly in 2021 with a renewed commitment to 
preserving and carrying forward its enduring spirit.
  As Western Golf & Country Club marks its 100th anniversary, this 
milestone stands as a testament to a century of fellowship, tradition, 
and community. I commend its members and leadership for preserving this 
legacy and celebrating a remarkable 100 years of impact.
```
