# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF GLADYS PEGGY NOBLE

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF GLADYS PEGGY NOBLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of Mother 
Peggy Noble, an inspirational leader and a champion for economic 
justice for working families. Born in Port Huron, Michigan, Mother 
Peggy was the oldest of 10 children. She was a product of the Detroit 
Public Schools, earning an Associate of Arts from Henry Ford College, a 
Bachelor of Social Work from Wayne State University, and a Master of 
Social Work from Wayne State University.
  For more than 60 years, she had been a community activist in the City 
of Detroit and dedicated her time to numerous non-profit organizations, 
the City of Detroit, and the State of Michigan. She was seen as a 
community mother who always applied her knowledge and expertise as a 
social worker and therapist to help serve our families for many years. 
Mother Peggy Noble was also the President of the College Park Community 
Association, a founding member of the Northwest Detroit Democratic 
Club, and a proud precinct delegate. Additionally, she served as a 
community board member for the 8th Precinct of Detroit Police 
Department.
  Mother Noble dedicated her life to serving the College Park 
neighborhood. She was a tireless organizer who supported her neighbors, 
advocated for children, and brought people together to strengthen her 
community. Among her many efforts, she was especially proud of her work 
encouraging Detroiters to ``Get Out to Vote,'' leading voter 
registration initiatives and organizing opportunities for residents to 
meet candidates running for local, state, and national office. In 
recognition of her unwavering commitment to civic engagement, she was 
awarded the Michigan Democratic Party's Volunteer of the Year Award in 
2024.
  Mother Noble believed deeply that real change is built through 
relationships, advocacy, and showing up for your community. As a mentor 
to many young community mothers, she was a fierce and unwavering 
advocate for families--standing up for her neighborhood and fighting 
for fairness in Michigan's broken auto insurance system. Detroit is 
stronger because of her voice, her leadership, and her steadfast belief 
in people. We carry her spirit forward in how we organize, how we 
protect one another, and how we continue the fight for a city that 
truly works for all of us.
  Mother Noble's service has left an undeniable mark on our city. Her 
legacy lives on through the lives she touched, the efforts she led, and 
the generations she inspired to lead with courage and compassion. May 
her memory continue to be a blessing to her family, friends, and the 
city she so deeply loved. My thoughts are with Rochelle, Eugene, 
Kimberly, Jaimi, Asante, and all of Peggy's loved ones during this 
difficult time. May she rest in power and love.

                          ____________________
```
