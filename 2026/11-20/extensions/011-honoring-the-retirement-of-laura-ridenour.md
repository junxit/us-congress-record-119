# HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF LAURA RIDENOUR

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
               HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF LAURA RIDENOUR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and honor Deputy Chief 
Laura Ridenour of the

[[Page E1272]]

Dearborn Fire Department as she retires from 24 years of distinguished 
service to the people of Dearborn, Michigan.
  Deputy Chief Ridenour began her career with the Dearborn Fire 
Department in 2002 as a probationary firefighter. Through dedication, 
expertise, and steadfast commitment to public safety, she rose through 
the ranks--Firefighter I in 2003, Firefighter II in 2006, Fire 
Inspector in 2007, Assistant Fire Marshal in 2012, and later that same 
year, Fire Marshal. In 2022, she achieved the rank of Deputy Chief, 
becoming the highest-ranking woman in the department's 114-year 
history.
  As one of the longest-serving Fire Marshals in Dearborn's history, 
Deputy Chief Ridenour earned regional respect for her deep knowledge of 
fire codes, her leadership in inspections and enforcement, and her 
unwavering focus on prevention. Upon her promotion to Deputy Chief, she 
assumed a central leadership role--serving as the department's 
spokesperson, financial officer, and a key administrator while 
continuing to oversee all fire-prevention operations. Her colleagues 
consistently recognized her professionalism, collaborative spirit, and 
ability to guide complex initiatives with clarity and purpose.
  Michigan's 12th Congressional District thanks Deputy Chief Laura 
Ridenour for her 24 years of service, leadership, and dedication. We 
wish her every success and fulfillment as she begins this next chapter 
in her retirement.

                          ____________________
```
