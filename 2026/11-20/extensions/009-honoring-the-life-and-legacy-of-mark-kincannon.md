# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MARK KINCANNON

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
             HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MARK KINCANNON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of Mark 
Kincannon, a devoted public servant, athlete, and ``family man.''
  Mark was born on July 2, 1957, in Ypsilanti, Michigan to Robert 
Kincannon and Frances Mae (Ford) Kincannon who taught him the 
importance of education and public service. While attending Harry S. 
Truman High School in Taylor, Michigan, Mark began a long athletic 
career where his unparalleled talents in football and track and field 
led to him setting several school records that still stand today. He 
continued his education at Cleary College, where he received a Bachelor 
of Arts and later earned a Master of Business Administration at the 
University of Phoenix. Mark was a devout Christian and his teaching 
taught him to love thy neighbor, as he spent much of his time 
reflecting on the graciousness of God--often citing scripture, and 
practicing daily devotions.
  While balancing school, Mark worked as an electrician for General 
Motors and later retired after a 35-year career. He also worked for 
multiple LLCs, Office of the Mayor in Detroit, volunteered for the 
Michigan State Representatives, and Unifi Aviation (a partner of Delta 
Airlines). With all this work experience, at heart, Mark is best known 
to have been a true family man. He loved spending time with his 
children and creating new memories with his grandchildren and great-
granddaughter. Mark's mission in life was to spread joy and love to 
everyone in his community. He touched many lives. His advice, kindness, 
jokes, and infectious laugh will live in us forever. Mark Kincannon 
will be deeply missed but not forgotten.
  We extend our love to his mother, wife, siblings, children, 
grandchildren, and friends as we celebrate his life. We are committed 
to carrying his infectious spirit of joy in the community he loved so 
dearly. May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
