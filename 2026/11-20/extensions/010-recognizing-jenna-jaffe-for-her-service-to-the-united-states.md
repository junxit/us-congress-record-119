# RECOGNIZING JENNA JAFFE FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
 RECOGNIZING JENNA JAFFE FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jenna Jaffe for 
her 11 years of outstanding service to the constituents of New York's 
12th Congressional District.
  Jenna came to New York to pursue a career in public service and 
advocacy after receiving her undergraduate degree from UMass Amherst in 
Political Science in 2014. She joined my district office in 2016 as a 
Community Liaison, focusing on local Lower Manhattan issues. However, 
her direct work with community members uncovered her passion for direct 
service and pivoted her role to a full-time caseworker within a year. 
She found her calling with immigration casework and became my 
Immigration Specialist in 2020.
  Jenna's unshakeable dedication to immigration advocacy is guided by 
her conviction that Americans are born all over the world, every single 
day. Over the course of the past decade, she has lived up to this 
belief by facilitating the approvals of thousands of visas, green 
cards, and related benefits. Any time I refer a constituent with an 
immigration issue to Jenna, I know that she will go above and beyond to 
ensure the best possible outcome, armed with the integrity that is the 
cornerstone of her work.
  Jenna's advocacy on behalf of crisis-driven populations goes far 
beyond the scope of her defined job responsibilities. During the Biden 
administration, she worked with the State Department to develop and 
operationalize a secure process for evacuating vulnerable Afghan allies 
to the United States. She frequently collaborates with the House 
Judiciary Committee's Counsel and has made significant contributions to 
my office's strategy around immigration policy.
  Jenna is a recognized subject matter expert in her field, routinely 
training and mentoring other immigration caseworkers throughout the 
country. She has built a robust network of partnerships across New 
York's immigrant advocacy community.
  As Jenna moves on to the next step in her career, I thank her for her 
dedicated service to the people of New York's 12th Congressional 
District. I am confident that she will continue to be a fierce advocate 
for immigrants in the United States, centering her values at the 
forefront of whatever she does next.
```
