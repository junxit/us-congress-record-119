# RECOGNIZING TERESA GRIMES FOR HER EXCELLENT SERVICE TO SOUTHWEST ALABAMA

**Speaking:** Sewell, Terri A. (S001185) [D-AL]

```
   RECOGNIZING TERESA GRIMES FOR HER EXCELLENT SERVICE TO SOUTHWEST 
                                ALABAMA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TERRI A. SEWELL

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. SEWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Teresa Grimes, on 
this National Rural Health Day, for her selection as Alabama's 2026 
Community Star by the National Organization of State Offices of Rural 
Health. Having long focused on the challenges facing rural healthcare 
providers, I know how much local leadership matters.
  Under Teresa's leadership, the Washington County Hospital and Nursing 
Home has strengthened its facilities, including an expanded emergency 
department and new Legacy Hall dedicated in July 2022. Teresa and the 
Hospital and Nursing Home have also received numerous awards and 
performance honors for their service in Washington County, including a 
Chartis Performance Leadership Award, a PRC Excellence Award for 
quality of care, and recognition by Newsweek among America's best 
nursing homes. The impact of Teresa's leadership is felt far beyond 
Washington County.
  Teresa has represented Southwest Alabama on the Alabama Hospital 
Association's Board of Trustees and has served on the Alabama Board of 
Examiners of Nursing Home Administrators. Her selection as Alabama's 
2026 Community Star by the National Organization of State Offices of 
Rural Health speaks to her excellence.
  Established in 1995, the National Organization of State Offices of 
Rural Health supports the Nation's 50 State Offices of Rural Health in 
improving access to quality healthcare through leadership development, 
education, advocacy, and partnerships. Its annual Community Star 
Recognition Program, established in 2015, highlights individuals and 
organizations making a meaningful difference in rural communities. The 
National Organization of State Offices of Rural Health deserves our 
thanks for bringing national attention to these local leaders and the 
essential work they do.
  Teresa Grimes serves as the Chief Executive Officer of the Washington 
County Hospital and Nursing Home in Chatom, Alabama, an institution 
that has served Washington County since 1953. The Washington County 
Hospital and Nursing Home includes a 25-bed critical access hospital 
and an 88-bed nursing facility, providing essential services to 
patients, residents, and families in Southwest Alabama.
  On a personal note, I was proud to help secure $1 million in grant 
funding for the Washington County Health Care Authority for equipment 
and facility improvements in 2022. As I welcome Washington County into 
my district in the 120th Congress, I will continue to work to 
strengthen rural healthcare access and lower costs for patients and 
families.
  On behalf of Alabama's 7th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in celebrating the selection of Teresa Grimes as 
Alabama's 2026 Community Star by the National Organization of State 
Offices of Rural Health.
```
