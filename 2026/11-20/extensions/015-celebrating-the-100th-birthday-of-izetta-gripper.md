# CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF IZETTA GRIPPER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
            CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF IZETTA GRIPPER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Izetta Willingham Gripper 
on the occasion of her 100th birthday, celebrating a century of faith, 
family, resilience, and extraordinary service to her community.
  Born April 11, 1926, in Winnsboro, South Carolina, Mrs. Gripper moved 
to Michigan in 1949 and became a proud Detroit resident for over 50 
years. She married Willie James Gripper, Sr. in 1950, and together they 
raised five children, building a remarkable legacy that now includes 11 
grandchildren and nine great-grandchildren.
  A faithful member of Friendship Baptist Church since 1950, Mrs. 
Gripper has served in numerous roles, including Mission Outreach, 
choir, and community service ministries. She was also active in PTAs, 
block clubs, and as a dedicated election poll worker. A talented 
homemaker, seamstress, and caterer, she enriched countless lives 
through her generosity, strength, and grace.
  As she celebrates this extraordinary milestone, we honor her enduring 
legacy, profound impact, and truly remarkable life, and extend our 
heartfelt wishes for continued joy and happiness. Michigan's 12th 
Congressional District honors lzetta Gripper for her commitment to 
faith and service in celebration of her 100th birthday. I thank her for 
her lifetime of wisdom, guidance, and her remarkable influence on our 
community and district.
```
