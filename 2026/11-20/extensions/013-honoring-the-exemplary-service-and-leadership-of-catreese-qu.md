# HONORING THE EXEMPLARY SERVICE AND LEADERSHIP OF CATREESE QUALLS

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
    HONORING THE EXEMPLARY SERVICE AND LEADERSHIP OF CATREESE QUALLS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the extraordinary 
contributions of Catreese Qualls, a visionary leader whose career spans 
more than two decades of advocacy and empowerment in Metro Detroit.
  Ms. Qualls has long served as a pillar of the Schoolcraft College 
community, where her mentorship and heritage-based educational programs 
have fostered a profound sense of belonging for culturally diverse 
student groups. Her strategic guidance as an advisor to Arab Women 
United (AWU) catalyzed the organization's growth from a student club 
into a premier regional non-profit, now encompassing four university 
chapters and over 250 active members.
  A champion of educational equity, Ms. Qualls spearheaded the ``Little 
Free Library'' initiative, placing more than 3,000 books into the hands 
of underprivileged students to bridge the literacy gap. Furthermore, as 
the founder of Lotus & Sage Leadership Development Training & 
Consulting LLC, she has equipped over 500 women with ESL training and 
workforce readiness skills essential for economic independence. Her 
tireless efforts in civic engagement and her recognition as the 2026 
Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging (DEIB) Change Maker by the 
Livonia Westland Chamber of Commerce stand as a testament to her 
enduring impact. Michigan's 12th District recognizes Catreese Qualls 
for her heart for the people and her unwavering dedication to the 
community.
```
