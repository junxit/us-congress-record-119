# RECOGNIZING ZAMAN INTERNATIONAL

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                    RECOGNIZING ZAMAN INTERNATIONAL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Zaman International as it 
celebrates its 30th Anniversary of service to the people of Inkster and 
the surrounding communities. For three decades, Zaman has empowered 
families to break cycles of intergenerational poverty with dignity, 
hope, and compassion.
  Since its founding in 1996, Zaman International has remained 
committed to meeting the needs of families facing economic hardship, 
providing them with the tools and support necessary to build self-
directed futures. What began as a grassroots effort has evolved into a 
multifaceted 501(c)(3) organization offering services that address both 
short and long-term barriers to economic stability. Its ``Campus of 
Hope''--an innovative hub that integrates basic needs assistance, 
educational programming, and vocational training--brings together 
wraparound support under one roof, ensuring that women and children can 
pursue economic independence in a safe, nurturing environment.
  Zaman's impact has also cultivated a culture of compassion and 
empowerment that uplifts entire communities. By centering dignity and 
respect in every interaction, Zaman has built trust with families who 
have historically been underserved or overlooked. As Zaman 
International marks this milestone anniversary, its legacy is clear: 
thousands of lives transformed, countless families supported, and a 
community made stronger through its presence.
```
