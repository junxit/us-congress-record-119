# HONORING BRISTOL SANTA HOUSE

**Speaking:** Amo, Gabe (A000380) [D-RI]

```
                      HONORING BRISTOL SANTA HOUSE

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. GABE AMO

                            of rhode island

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Mr. AMO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Bristol Santa House 
and Mr. Michael Rielly for their dedication to bringing joy, kindness, 
and the holiday spirit to families throughout Rhode Island's 1st 
Congressional District.
  Each year, the Bristol Santa House provides free, safe, and 
accessible visits with Santa, allowing families from all walks of life 
to take part in this cherished holiday tradition. The organization also 
offers dedicated private visits for children and adults with 
disabilities and their families, creating a welcoming and inclusive 
environment where everyone can experience the magic of the holiday 
season. This year, the Bristol Santa House will distribute more than 
2,000 free toys and treats to visitors.
  The Bristol Santa House and its dedicated North Pole delegate, 
Michael Rielly, have served the community for nearly five decades. In 
1979, Rhode Island's distinguished Senator Claiborne Pell recognized 
Michael's grandfather, James D. Rielly, in the Congressional Record for 
his years of service and his dedication to children and adults with 
disabilities. Today, Michael continues that legacy through his work 
with the Bristol Santa House, bringing happiness to new generations of 
Rhode Islanders and ensuring that his grandfather's spirit of 
compassion lives on. The Bristol Santa House is a wonderful example of 
the generosity and community spirit that make Rhode Island so special. 
Its commitment to inclusion and service reflects the true meaning of 
the holiday season.
  On behalf of the people of Rhode Island's 1st Congressional District, 
I am honored to congratulate the Bristol Santa House and Michael Rielly 
for their continued dedication to our community. I thank them for 
preserving this meaningful holiday tradition and for the lasting joy 
they bring to so many Rhode Islanders.

                          ____________________
```
