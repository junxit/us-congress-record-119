# RECOGNIZING KELSEY CINA FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
 RECOGNIZING KELSEY CINA FOR HER SERVICE TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 20, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kelsey Cina for 
her outstanding service to the constituents of New York's 12th 
Congressional District.
  Kelsey joined my office as a Caseworker in the Summer of 2022, right 
after she completed a Master of Social Work from the Silver School of 
Social Work at New York University. She also holds an undergraduate 
degree in Social Work from the University of Vermont.
  Her passion for both social work and advocating for others started in 
her elementary school years. She found her voice by encouraging her 
school's administration to create more time and space for her 
classmates to collectively grieve the heartbreaking shooting in Sandy 
Hook that happened just a few towns over from her native Weston, 
Connecticut. Additionally, after experiencing a tragic loss, she worked 
with a social worker who made such an indelible and positive impact on 
her life, she knew the only way to pay her back was to become a social 
worker herself.
  Armed with these personal experiences and expertise in incorporating 
micro social work skills in a macro environment, Kelsey eagerly dove 
into constituent casework. We can see the tangible effects of Kelsey's 
work as she directly improves the lives of New York's 12th 
Congressional District's residents by helping them successfully 
navigate the web of federal agencies, from ensuring they get their full 
tax refund from the IRS, to reuniting families separated for years by 
expediting visa requests, and to overseeing our district's military 
nominations process for training our future leaders.
  Each day serving as a congressional caseworker has its own peaks and 
valleys and sometimes one unexpectedly finds themselves putting their 
own safety on the line. Such was the case for Kelsey when she 
patriotically stood up to federal officers seeking unauthorized entry 
to my New York district office.
  Kelsey brings her warm, creative, and curious personality to both her 
work and our office culture, making her a true asset to our team. She 
obtained her License in Social Work and supervises social work students 
at the University of Vermont, enriching their internship experiences in 
various placements including Senator Bernie Sanders' office. Always a 
nurturer, in her free time Kelsey enjoys tending to her house plants 
and volunteering in her local Brooklyn community garden.
  I wish Kelsey the best of luck wherever she finds herself next in her 
career and thank her for commendable work in my office.

                          ____________________
```
