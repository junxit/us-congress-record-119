# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2026, THROUGH MONDAY, NOVEMBER 30, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2026, THROUGH MONDAY, NOVEMBER 30, 
                                  2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned to then convene 
for pro forma session only, with no business being conducted on the 
following dates and times: Tuesday, November 24 at 3 p.m. and Friday, 
November 27 at 12 noon; further, that when the Senate adjourns on 
Friday, November 27, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on Monday, 
November 30; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; finally, at 5:30 
p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session and execute the order 
with respect to the Reardon nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
