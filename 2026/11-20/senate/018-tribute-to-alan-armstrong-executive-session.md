# Tribute to Alan Armstrong (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK], Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

```
                       Tribute to Alan Armstrong

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, my friend Alan Armstrong, on January 1 
of this year, was expecting to retire this year, and, instead, he spent 
250 days serving our State and the Nation here in the U.S. Senate. He 
made yet another sacrifice for our country, of so many that he has made 
over his lifetime.
  His family sacrificed a tremendous amount for him to be able to be 
here. He sacrificed a lot to be able to be here, and he got a 
tremendous amount done in a very short time period.
  So I just want to be able to rise today and to be able to thank Alan 
and his family for the sacrifices that they made to be able to be here, 
the dedication and the focus that he had on permitting reform to be 
able to make sure that the one thing that he could push to get done got 
done. But, thankfully, he was able to get a lot more done, as well, in 
relationships and in tasking legislation to be able to help move our 
country forward.
  So my State will be long-term grateful and our Nation will be 
grateful as we continue to be able to advance in

[[Page S5832]]

construction projects and so many things in the future. So we are 
grateful in Oklahoma for Alan's sacrifice and for his family's 
sacrifice as well.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Alaska.
  Ms. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, also very briefly, there has been much 
said about the diligence of this short-term, but probably most 
effective, Senator in 250 days, when it comes to his ability to focus 
in on permitting and to really see that success come to fruition.
  But the one thing that you did not say was the heart that you have 
shown for the American Indian people within the State of Oklahoma, your 
care and true consideration when it comes to Tribal issues.
  You have been probably one of the most reliable members of our Indian 
Affairs Committee during your short tenure here, but there also you 
have been equally, equally effective. And I am just deeply grateful for 
the passion you have shown in that area, in addition to your ability to 
accomplish big things in a short period of time.
  Best wishes to you.
  I yield the floor.
```
