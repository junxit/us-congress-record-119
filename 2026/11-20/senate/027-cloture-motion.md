# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 967, Daniel Lewis Ballou, of 
     Kentucky, to be United States District Judge for the Eastern 
     District of Kentucky.
         John Thune, Jim Banks, Tom Cotton, John R. Curtis, James 
           Lankford, Tim Sheehy, John Barrasso, Mike Lee, Mike 
           Rounds, Rick Scott of Florida, Kevin Cramer, Mike 
           Crapo, Bernie Moreno, John Boozman, Cindy Hyde-Smith, 
           Darline Graham, Cynthia M. Lummis.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. By unanimous consent, the mandatory quorum 
call has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of Daniel Lewis Ballou, of Kentucky, to be United States 
District Judge for the Eastern District of Kentucky, shall be brought 
to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt), the Senator from Louisiana (Mr. 
Cassidy), the Senator from Texas (Mr. Cornyn), the Senator from 
Kentucky (Mr. McConnell), and the Senator from Alabama (Mr. 
Tuberville).
  Mr. SCHUMER. I announce that the Senator from Connecticut (Mr.
```
