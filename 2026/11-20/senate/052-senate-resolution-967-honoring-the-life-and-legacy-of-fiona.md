# SENATE RESOLUTION 967--HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF FIONA THE HIPPOPOTAMUS OF THE CINCINNATI ZOO AND BOTANICAL GARDEN, AND EXPRESSING THE GRATITUDE OF THE SENATE TO THE STAFF OF THE CINCINNATI...

**Measures:** S.Res. 967

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

   SENATE RESOLUTION 967--HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF FIONA THE 
HIPPOPOTAMUS OF THE CINCINNATI ZOO AND BOTANICAL GARDEN, AND EXPRESSING 
  THE GRATITUDE OF THE SENATE TO THE STAFF OF THE CINCINNATI ZOO FOR 
                THEIR EXTRAORDINARY CARE AND DEDICATION

  Mr. MORENO  (for himself and Mr. Husted) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 967

       Whereas Fiona, a Nile hippopotamus at the Cincinnati Zoo 
     and Botanical Garden, was born in January 2017, 6 weeks 
     premature and weighing only 29 pounds, the smallest premature 
     hippopotamus known to survive in human care;
       Whereas Fiona overcame extraordinary odds from the day of 
     her birth, growing into a robust animal who captured the 
     hearts of millions of people around the world through her 
     resilience, personality, and daily updates shared by the 
     Cincinnati Zoo;
       Whereas Fiona became an icon of the State of Ohio, 
     inspiring children and families, generating widespread 
     interest in wildlife and conservation, and serving as a 
     beloved ambassador for her species;
       Whereas Fiona would have turned 10 years old in January 
     2027, and in her nearly 10 years of life she brought joy, 
     wonder, and a sense of shared community to Ohioans and people 
     across the United States;
       Whereas, on November 8, 2026, Fiona accidentally ingested a 
     knit hat that entered her habitat, creating a serious medical 
     emergency;
       Whereas the veterinarians, animal keepers, and entire 
     animal care team at the Cincinnati Zoo and Botanical Garden 
     worked tirelessly and heroically around the clock, employing 
     every available medical and supportive measure in an effort 
     to save Fiona's life;
       Whereas, despite those dedicated efforts, Fiona passed away 
     on November 19, 2026, leaving a profound sense of loss among 
     her Cincinnati Zoo family and the countless people who loved 
     her; and
       Whereas the staff of the Cincinnati Zoo demonstrated the 
     highest standards of professional excellence, compassion, and 
     commitment to animal welfare throughout Fiona's life and in 
     her final days: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) honors the life and enduring legacy of Fiona the 
     hippopotamus of the Cincinnati Zoo and Botanical Garden, 
     whose story of resilience and joy enriched the lives of 
     millions;

[[Page S5844]]

       (2) expresses deep gratitude and appreciation to the 
     veterinarians, keepers, and entire staff of the Cincinnati 
     Zoo and Botanical Garden for their tireless work, 
     professional excellence, and compassionate care of Fiona 
     throughout her life and during her final medical emergency;
       (3) extends heartfelt condolences to the Cincinnati Zoo 
     family and to all those who were touched by Fiona's life; and
       (4) recognizes Fiona as a lasting symbol of the spirit and 
     heart of the State of Ohio.
```
