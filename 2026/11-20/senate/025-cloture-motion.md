# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 970, Kyle Frederick Reardon, of 
     Alaska, to be United States District Judge for the District 
     of Alaska.
         John Thune, Jim Banks, Tom Cotton, John R. Curtis, Bill 
           Cassidy, James Lankford, Tim Sheehy, John Barrasso, 
           Mike Lee, Mike Rounds, Rick Scott of Florida, Kevin 
           Cramer, Mike Crapo, Bernie Moreno, John Boozman, Cindy 
           Hyde-Smith, Darline Graham, Lisa Murkowski.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. By unanimous consent, the mandatory quorum 
call has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of Kyle Frederick Reardon, of Alaska, to be United States 
District Judge for the District of Alaska, shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt), the Senator from Louisiana (Mr. 
Cassidy), the Senator from Texas (Mr. Cornyn), and the Senator from 
Alabama (Mr. Tuberville).
```
