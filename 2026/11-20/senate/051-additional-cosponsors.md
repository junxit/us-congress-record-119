# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 1227, S. 1763, S. 3508, S. 3940, S. 4160, S. 5218, S. 5289, S. 5608, S. 5793

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1227

  At the request of Mr. Markey, the name of the Senator from Idaho (Mr. 
Risch) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1227, a bill to require the 
Administrator of the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services and the 
Commissioner of Social Security to review and simplify the processes, 
procedures, forms, and communications for family caregivers to assist 
individuals in establishing eligibility for, enrolling in, and 
maintaining and utilizing coverage and benefits under the Medicare, 
Medicaid, CHIP, and Social Security programs respectively, and for 
other purposes.


                                S. 1763

  At the request of Mr. Young, the name of the Senator from Arizona 
(Mr. Kelly) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1763, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the 7-year recovery 
period for motorsports entertainment complexes.


                                S. 3508

  At the request of Mr. Schiff, his name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
3508, a bill to require the transfer of regulatory control of certain 
munitions exports from the Department of Commerce to the Department of 
State, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3940

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the name of the Senator from Hawaii 
(Ms. Hirono) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3940, a bill to amend the 
Community Development Banking and Financial Institutions Act of 1994 to 
require the Secretary of the Treasury to testify before Congress, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 4160

  At the request of Mr. Schiff, the name of the Senator from Nevada 
(Ms. Rosen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4160, a bill to amend the 
Commodity Exchange Act to prohibit certain event contracts involving 
sports and casino-style games.


                                S. 5218

  At the request of Mr. Ossoff, his name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
5218, a bill to amend the Agricultural Research, Extension, and 
Education Reform Act of 1998 to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to 
establish a program under which the Secretary will award competitive 
grants to eligible entities for the purpose of establishing and 
enhancing farming and ranching opportunities for veterans.


                                S. 5289

  At the request of Mr. Cruz, the name of the Senator from Pennsylvania 
(Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5289, a bill to protect 
the right to the free exercise of religion.


                                S. 5608

  At the request of Mr. Crapo, the name of the Senator from California 
(Mr. Padilla) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5608, a bill to extend the 
Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 5793

  At the request of Mrs. Shaheen, the name of the Senator from Rhode 
Island (Mr. Whitehouse) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5793, a bill to 
help prevent suicide by giving people more control over their own 
lethal means purchase decisions, through the creation of a voluntary 
purchase delay database.

                          ____________________
```
