# Oversight (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                               Oversight

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I want to start on oversight and start by 
speaking briefly about Congress' important oversight responsibilities.
  Congress' investigative and oversight powers are essential to its 
constitutional responsibility to legislate, oversee the execution of 
Federal laws, and to preserve the system of checks and balances. Those 
powers include the authority to compel testimony and documents in 
furtherance of legitimate legislative purposes.
  The Constitution's speech or debate clause safeguards legislative 
independence and preserves the separation of powers by protecting 
Members of Congress and their staff from executive or judicial 
interference in investigative activities within the legislative sphere. 
Attempts to chill congressional inquiry or to deter Members from 
carrying out their constitutional oversight responsibilities undermine 
the carefully constructed system of checks and balances enshrined in 
our constitutional system.
```
