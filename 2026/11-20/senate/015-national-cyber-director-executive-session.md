# National Cyber Director (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                        National Cyber Director

  Mr. President, on Trump firing the National Cyber Security Director, 
the American people want to know what is behind all the recent outages, 
but Trump just said:

       [R]elax. Everything's good.

  He is in never-never land. His head is so far in the sand that he 
doesn't know which side is up.
  Donald, don't tell Americans to relax. Tell them the truth--the truth 
about what is causing these disruptions.
  Trump keeps saying these outages are just business as usual, that 
they are isolated incidents and his administration isn't responsible. 
If these outages are just business as usual, then why did Trump just 
fire National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross?
  When the Trump administration is reluctant to explain a disaster, the 
safe bet is always that their incompetence is to blame. Incompetence 
rules in this administration because Trump doesn't care about hiring 
competent people. He hires people who suck up to him, who help him make 
money, give him nice seats at the table, flatter him. That is all. That 
is what is running our country these days, and that is why Trump was so 
resoundingly beaten in last Tuesday's election.
  Sean Cairncross was a political hack without a lick of cyber security 
experience. We need a National Cyber Director who actually knows how to 
keep America safe and our critical systems secure. Instead of another 
politically connected crony, we should have hard-working cyber security 
experts take the lead. If only Trump and Elon Musk hadn't purged so 
many of them.
  It is clear these outages are anything but normal, which is why I 
call again on Trump and DNI Jay Clayton to assemble a ``red team'' of 
trained intelligence professionals to get to the bottom of what is 
going on and report their findings to Congress by December 1.
  ``Relax'' isn't a cyber security strategy. Americans need answers.
```
