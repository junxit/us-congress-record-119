# TRIBUTE TO GEORGE McGILL

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO GEORGE McGILL 

● Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. President, I rise to honor the tremendous 
service of George McGill as he completes his tenure as the mayor of my 
hometown: Fort Smith, AR.
  I have been honored to know Mayor McGill for decades as a 
businessman, State legislator, community leader, and, ultimately, as a 
dear friend.
  He is a fellow Arkansas Razorback who earned a bachelor of science in 
education and a master of business administration and eventually went 
on to operate a successful insurance company while giving back to the 
place he grew up. He was eventually elected to represent the community 
in the Arkansas House of Representatives for three terms where he had 
the opportunity to focus on issues he cares deeply about, including 
hunger relief, higher education, veterans, and the protection of the 
elderly, children, and families.
  During his time in the State legislature, he earned the respect of 
his peers and held several leadership positions, including as Deputy 
Speaker Pro-Tempore of the Arkansas House of Representatives.
  McGill became mayor of Fort Smith in August 2019, the first African-
American elected to the role in the city's 184-year history. Under his 
leadership, the city has embraced new opportunities, addressed 
challenges, and cultivated a reputation as a leading defense community, 
as well as a great place to raise a family and do business.
  Before he was an entrepreneur or community volunteer, McGill served 
as a second lieutenant in the U.S. Army during the Vietnam war. When he 
was interviewed for the Library of Congress Veteran's History Project, 
McGill talked about how his service shaped his character, instilling in 
him the values of discipline and hard work, and a sense of patriotism 
he has carried throughout his life.
  Although he has received countless honors throughout his career, I 
know the honor that matters most to him is knowing how many young 
people he has mentored and inspired through his efforts in the Fort 
Smith school system and community organizations he created. He has not 
just led from meeting rooms; he has also led in classrooms and hallways 
and neighborhoods, showing up for young people and providing them with 
tangible skills and opportunities.
  I am grateful for Mayor McGill's reliable counsel and friendship over 
the years. As he retires from his current role at city hall, he can be 
proud of the many ways his leadership and example will continue to 
impact Fort Smith for generations to come.●

                          ____________________
```
