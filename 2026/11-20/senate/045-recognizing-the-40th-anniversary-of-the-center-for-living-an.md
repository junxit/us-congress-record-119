 RECOGNIZING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE CENTER FOR LIVING AND LEARNING

● Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize a 
milestone for one of Middle Tennessee's great treasures: the Center for 
Living and Learning. This past October marked 40 years since this 
nonprofit residential treatment facility first opened its doors on a 
30-acre farm in Franklin to help those suffering from serious mental 
illness. Since then, the center has provided a safe, encouraging 
environment for vulnerable Tennesseans, empowering them to reclaim 
their lives and thrive in their communities.
  Founded in 1986 by the Lackey family to care for their brother 
Donald, the center has become a trusted home for Tennesseans living 
with schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, bipolar disorder, 
anxiety, depression, and posttraumatic stress disorder. By combining 
residential, vocational, and psychiatric programs, the facility 
provides comprehensive care, ensuring residents have the treatment and 
skills they need to move forward. From job training and working on the 
farm to music classes and recreational outings, residents gain a 
renewed sense of independence.
  Cofounder and executive director Fran Clippard has said that the work 
of the center is about ``meeting the person where their needs are.'' 
For four decades, it has done exactly that--transforming countless 
lives for the better and serving as a blessing for the entire Franklin 
community. I congratulate Fran, the Lackey family, and the center's 
dedicated staff on this milestone, and I wish them many more years of 
service to Tennessee.●
