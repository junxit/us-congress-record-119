# Trump Administration (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Armstrong, Alan (A000383) [R-OK], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                          Trump Administration

  Mr. President, on Trump adviser corruption, every single day, 
Americans get another whiff of the Trump administration's rancid 
corruption. We just learned that Trump's former Communications Director 
Jason Miller has been peddling his connections to the White House for 
millions and millions of dollars.
  Lobbying on behalf of foreign governments, Jason Miller made more 
than $15 million in the past year under this administration--more than 
he had made in the previous 7 years combined. That is not a 
coincidence. Miller didn't win the lottery. He didn't strike gold. 
Miller sold his connections to the President and to top administration 
officials as a registered foreign agent.
  This is how governing works in Trump's America. You have to know a 
guy who knows a guy, and if you can pay that guy serious cash, you 
might just get the President's ear.
  Recently, Trump has brought Jason Miller back into the fold as an 
unpaid special government employee, as if we needed another one roaming 
the halls of the White House. There are so many incompetent, money-
grubbing people.
  Americans are tired of the reek of corruption coming from their 
capital. And it is not just the stench that bothers Americans; it is 
the fact that while billionaires, foreign governments, special 
interests, and Donald Trump are cashing in, working families are being 
left behind.
  When our Democratic majority takes charge, Democrats are ready to 
throw this system of unbridled corruption into the trash once and for 
all.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. ARMSTRONG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. ARMSTRONG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to complete my 
remarks prior to the scheduled rollcall vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
