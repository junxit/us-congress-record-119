# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 5814, S. 5815, S. 5816, S. 5817, S. 5818, S. 5819, S. 5820

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. KING (for himself and Ms. Murkowski):
       S. 5814. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to qualify homeless youth and veterans who are full-time 
     students for purposes of the low-income housing tax credit; 
     to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. WARNER (for himself, Ms. Collins, Mr. Padilla, 
             and Mr. McConnell):
       S. 5815. A bill to convert the program of public financing 
     of presidential election campaigns to a program for providing 
     grants to States for enhancing the security of election 
     systems, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Rules 
     and Administration.
           By Mr. SCHATZ (for himself and Ms. Hirono):
       S. 5816. A bill to amend the Agriculture Improvement Act of 
     2018 with respect to micro-grants for food security, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. SCHIFF:
       S. 5817. A bill to require the Environmental Protection 
     Agency to carry out a study to better understand the 
     environmental impacts of wildland fires, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. BUDD (for himself, Mr. Hickenlooper, Mr. Wicker, 
             Mr. Moran, and Mr. Kim):
       S. 5818. A bill to authorize an extension of a program to 
     permit screened passengers and their property arriving on 
     direct flights or flight segments originating at certain 
     foreign last point of departure airports to continue on 
     additional flights or flight segments originating in the 
     United States without additional screening, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Mr. YOUNG (for himself, Mr. Coons, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
             Warnock, Mr. Cruz, Mr. Kim, Mrs. Blackburn, Mr. 
             Hickenlooper, Mr. Lankford, Mr. Booker, Mr. Crapo, 
             and Ms. Blunt Rochester):
       S. 5819. A bill to amend title 28, United States Code, to 
     authorize additional judges for the circuit and district 
     courts, to require a study on the composition of certain 
     judicial circuits, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. KENNEDY (for himself, Mr. Lankford, Ms. Ernst, 
             and Mr. Peters):

[[Page S5843]]

  

       S. 5820. A bill to establish Governmentwide requirements 
     for pre-payment fraud prevention actions, to provide the U.S. 
     Treasury appropriate data resources, to facilitate 
     participation in Governmentwide anti-fraud data sharing, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
```
