# NOMINATION OF MICHAEL LoSAPIO

**Speaking:** Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
                     NOMINATION OF MICHAEL LoSAPIO 

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I give notice of my intent to object to any 
unanimous consent agreement regarding the nomination of Michael 
LoSapio, of New Jersey, to be Director of the Cybersecurity and 
Infrastructure Security Agency, Department of Homeland Security.
  LoSapio currently serves as the chief information security officer at 
Palantir, where he has worked for nearly 12 years. I have long been 
concerned about Palantir's work to facilitate the Trump-Vance 
administration's abuse of government data and would have concerns with 
any Palantir executive leading the Nation's leading cyber defense 
agency.
  But for a person who would be in charge of protecting American 
elections, LoSapio's answers on the 2020 election and the insurrection 
on January 6 at his nomination hearing are disqualifying. He refused to 
answer who won the Presidential race in 2020. And he stated that the 
January 6 insurrection was not an attack.
  Let's be clear: The insurrection on January 6 was an attack 
instigated by Donald Trump in an attempt to undo the results of a 
democratic election. In one of our democracy's darkest days, a horde of 
violent domestic terrorists trampled over our Capitol. As much as this 
administration wants to sweep the insurrection on January 6 under the 
rug, history will always remember the officers who were assaulted and 
the lives that were lost because of the events on that day. I will not 
stand by idly while Donald Trump tries to put an insurrectionist 
Palantir executive in charge of our Nation's cyber security and 
election security.

                          ____________________
```
