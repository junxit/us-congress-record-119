# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 967, S.Res. 968, S.Res. 969, S.Res. 970, S.Res. 971, S.Res. 972, S.Res. 973, S.Res. 974

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. MORENO (for himself and Mr. Husted):
       S. Res. 967. A resolution honoring the life and legacy of 
     Fiona the hippopotamus of the Cincinnati Zoo and Botanical 
     Garden, and expressing the gratitude of the Senate to the 
     staff of the Cincinnati Zoo for their extraordinary care and 
     dedication; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. FETTERMAN (for himself and Mr. McCormick):
       S. Res. 968. A resolution congratulating the Gannon 
     University men's basketball team on their victory in the 69th 
     National Collegiate Athletic Association Division II men's 
     basketball national championship; to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Ms. MURKOWSKI (for herself, Mr. Schatz, Mr. 
             Sullivan, Ms. Alsobrooks, Mr. Daines, Ms. Baldwin, 
             Ms. Lummis, Mr. Bennet, Mr. Tillis, Mr. Blumenthal, 
             Mr. Hoeven, Ms. Cortez Masto, Mrs. Fischer, Mrs. 
             Gillibrand, Mr. Rounds, Mr. Heinrich, Mr. Crapo, Mr. 
             Hickenlooper, Mr. King, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Lujan, Mr. 
             Merkley, Mr. Padilla, Ms. Rosen, Ms. Smith, Ms. 
             Warren, Mrs. Murray, Mr. Van Hollen, Ms. Cantwell, 
             Mr. Gallego, Mr. Schumer, Mr. Risch, Mr. Wyden, Mr. 
             Cramer, Mr. Lankford, Mr. Ricketts, Mr. Durbin, Ms. 
             Hirono, Mr. Moran, Mr. Markey, and Mr. Booker):
       S. Res. 969. A resolution recognizing National Native 
     American Heritage Month and celebrating the heritages and 
     cultures of Native Americans and the contributions of Native 
     Americans to the United States; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. CRUZ (for himself and Mr. Cornyn):
       S. Res. 970. A resolution commending and congratulating 
     Texas Southmost College for 100 years of service to the State 
     of Texas and the United States; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. WARNOCK (for himself, Mr. Grassley, Mr. 
             Marshall, Mrs. Blackburn, Mr. Risch, Mr. Crapo, Mrs. 
             Capito, Mr. Lankford, Ms. Collins, Mr. Barrasso, Ms. 
             Cortez Masto, Mr. Durbin, Ms. Hirono, Mr. Lujan, Mr. 
             Merkley, Mr. Murphy, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Booker):
       S. Res. 971. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of November 8, 2026, as ``National First-
     Generation College Celebration Day''; considered and agreed 
     to.
           By Mrs. SHAHEEN (for herself and Ms. Collins):
       S. Res. 972. A resolution designating November 2026 as 
     ``American Diabetes Month''; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Ms. ROSEN (for herself, Mr. Barrasso, Ms. Baldwin, 
             and Mrs. Fischer):
       S. Res. 973. A resolution designating November 2026 as 
     ``National Hospice and Palliative Care Month''; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. WARNOCK (for himself and Mrs. Capito):
       S. Res. 974. A resolution designating November 29, 2026, as 
     ``Drive Safer Sunday''; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
