# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Precious Lord, we come before You today as empty vessels in need of 
Your filling. Fill us with Your power, strengthen us by Your might, and 
renew our lawmakers in spirit, mind, and heart. Give them a fervent 
love for You that inspires them to honor You through their service.
  Lord, keep them steadfast in faith, firm in their convictions, and 
unwavering in their trust in You. Even when confronted with mountains 
that seem impossible to move, permit them to know that You are able to 
enable them and empower them to do what no one can do without Your 
power.
  Help them daily to feel the unfailing influence and assurance of Your 
love and give them the courage, wisdom, and perseverance to overcome 
every obstacle before them.
  May their lives and leadership reflect Your goodness, grace, and 
glory.
  We pray in Your omniscient Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
