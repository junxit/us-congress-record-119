# AI Chips (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                AI Chips

  Mr. President, now on Trump giving AI chips to China, Trump needs to 
realize that the only reason he received such a warm welcome from the 
Chinese Communist Party this week was because he is doling out our most 
advanced AI chips to China like they are party favors. The House 
released confidential information yesterday revealing that Trump's 
Commerce Department approved the sale of over 800,000--800,000--of our 
cutting-edge AI chips to Chinese companies in just the first 6 months 
since Trump loosened export controls.
  That is not even the worst of it. The Trump administration knowingly 
approved the sale of tens of thousands of these chips to companies with 
ties to China's People's Liberation Army. Trump's Commerce Department 
even approved the sale of tens of thousands of NVIDIA chips to a 
company affiliated with the very same Chinese spy who honeypotted 
Jensen Huang. Keep in mind, Trump and Lutnick knew this woman was a spy 
ahead of time and still rushed to approve the sale of this sensitive 
technology to her companies.
  It is sort of cash-and-carry over there in the White House, in the 
Commerce Department. They don't care about our security. Trump and 
Lutnick sold out America's national security to make a quick buck for 
themselves and their billionaire buddies.
  Every time we think Trump couldn't get any more corrupt, be any more 
of a money-grubbing President, he finds a new way to debase himself. 
Trump cares more about the tens of millions of dollars he has in NVIDIA 
stock than about keeping our country safe and our economy competitive.
  These chips are helping the PLA advance its military objectives and 
Chinese tech companies get an edge on AI, quantum computing, and other 
industries critical to national security and competitiveness.
  Did Trump press Xi on any of this? Of course not. Trump was too 
distracted by the seating chart and the CCP's money and probably 
distracted by the millions of dollars he, Lutnick, and others make. It 
is so gross.
  Trump clearly can't be trusted to protect this vital technology, 
which is why I urge Congress to pass the AI OVERWATCH Act, the MATCH 
Act, and the Chip Security Act to keep our advanced AI chips out of the 
clutches of the CCP. Xi gets our chips and manufacturing equipment; 
Trump gets a seat at CCP's head table--and who knows how much cash--and 
America gets screwed. Congress can't let that stand.
```
