# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Lisa Wink Schultz, Office of the Senate Chaplain, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Gracious Lord, in the wilderness when You saw the hungry crowds, Your 
Son looked upon them with compassion and provided for their needs.
  Today, we pray for the Members of the House of Representatives. Grant 
them hearts that are moved by that same compassion. Help them to see 
the people they have been called to serve--each created in Your image 
and deeply loved by You.
  May they seek not only what is politically expedient, but what is 
just and for the common good.
  May all who serve here be instruments of Your peace, reflecting 
compassion to a Nation in need.
  We pray in Your loving name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
