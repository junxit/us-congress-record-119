# TRIBUTE TO MARK EISELE

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO MARK EISELE

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, at the 114th Wyoming State Fair, Senator 
Lummis and I will have the pleasure of introducing Mark Eisele as a 
2026 inductee to the Wyoming Agriculture Hall of Fame.
  Mark Eisele comes from a long line of Wyoming ranchers, dating back 
generations. However, Mark built his livestock operation on his own 
from the ground up.
  Mark has long been an iconic leader for Wyoming agriculture. His 
career

[[Page S4406]]

led him to the historic King Ranch near Cheyenne. This is where he 
first worked as an employee before acquiring the historic ranch.
  Today, Mark and his wife Trudy, along with their three children, 
raise Red and Black Angus cow/calf pairs and retained yearlings through 
branded beef programs. The family also manages a custom haying 
operation and harvests alfalfa and native grass hay.
  Throughout his career, Mark has valued maintaining healthy 
rangelands. He has long believed that a healthy rangeland is the 
foundation of a successful cattle operation. Through Mark's leadership, 
the King Ranch has partnered with the U.S. Department of Agriculture's 
(USDA) Agricultural Research Service Rangeland Resources and Systems 
Research Unit for over 20 years.
  Mark has led the King Ranch to embrace research and innovation 
through projects on livestock production, economic efficiency, flexible 
stocking strategies for erratic and variable precipitation, adaptive 
grazing management for poisonous plants, targeted grazing for invasive 
cheatgrass, soil carbon responses to grazing management, and habitat 
conservation.
  The Eisele family's stewardship efforts have earned widespread 
recognition. They received the Wyoming Stock Growers Association 
Environmental Stewardship Award and the Leopold Conservation Award in 
2015.
  More recently, the ranch received the 2023 Kurt Bucholz Conservation 
Award. This was followed by the 2025 Chuck Jarecki Rancher Land 
Stewardship Award presented by the International Society for Range 
Management.
  Along with receiving many accolades, Mark has committed his career to 
serving the agriculture industry as an advocate. Mark has been a 
longtime member of the Wyoming Stock Growers Association and served as 
the president from 2011 to 2013. After years of committee work within 
the National Cattlemen's Beef Association, Mark was elected president 
for the 2024 to 2025 term. He has become one of the Nation's leading 
voices for beef producers.
  Mark's dedication and service to Wyoming's agriculture industry makes 
him an outstanding choice for the Wyoming Agriculture Hall of Fame. It 
is a great honor to participate in Mark's induction. I congratulate 
Mark Eisele as a 2026 inductee into the Wyoming Agriculture Hall of 
Fame.

                          ____________________
```
