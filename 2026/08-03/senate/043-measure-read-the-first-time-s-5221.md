**Measures:** S. 5221

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 5221

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the
```
