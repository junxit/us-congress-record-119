# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, Father of mercy, light, and justice, we acknowledge 
that we are in Your hands. Lord, we ask for Your mercy upon this 
legislative body; let Your Holy Spirit guide them with wisdom, 
discernment, and courage.
  Prosper the work You have called them to do that their decisions may 
serve the welfare of all people. Strengthen them in times of 
difficulty, and keep them faithful in their responsibilities, inspiring 
them to protect and defend the Constitution of the United States.
  May all that is done in this Chamber reflect Your wisdom, honor, and 
love. We pray in your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
