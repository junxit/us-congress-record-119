# TRIBUTE TO DAVID BEDFORD

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO DAVID BEDFORD

● Mr. TUBERVILLE. Mr. President, when it comes to the military, 
there is no such thing as working 9 to 5. You are on call 24 hours a 
day, 365 days a year. For U.S. Army Chief Warrant Officer David Bedford 
of Wilmer, he still has this mentality about serving others.
  A Mobile County native, David learned about military service from his 
father, who was a World War II veteran and Purple Heart recipient. In 
1969, David followed his father's lead and enlisted to train as an Army 
pilot. After completing his training at Fort Rucker, David deployed to 
Vietnam in 1971, where he flew Huey helicopters more than 1,500 hours 
for resupply and transportation missions. He describes his most 
satisfying missions as those where he brought mail to troops on the 
frontlines, a small way to help put a

[[Page S4407]]

smile on their faces during a very dark time.
  David attributes his safe return home to the many prayers of his 
wife, mother, family, and friends. He had a special gift waiting for 
him when he came back to Alabama: a baby girl who was born while he was 
deployed overseas. David and his wife Theresa had two more daughters 
and raised their family in Mobile County while he finished his degree 
at the University of South Alabama. After graduating, David began 
working in the county school system, eventually becoming a counselor 
and helping students navigate life's challenges.
  David saw his job as more than just a 9 to 5, but as a way to invest 
in the lives of his students and help prepare them for the real world. 
Both David and Theresa have found a multitude of ways to partner 
together and serve the surrounding community. Through their involvement 
in the Retired and Senior Volunteer Program--otherwise known as RSVP--
they frequently volunteer at community events and help seniors in the 
area prosper. David and Theresa are also members of the Christian 
Motorcycles Association, where they use their love of motorcycles to 
share their faith with other veterans. He also is a member of the 
Patriot Guard Riders, supporting Gold Star families and reminding them 
that they are not alone.
  David's generosity of his time has truly made a difference that spans 
far beyond our State. It is my honor to recognize David Bedford as the 
August Veteran of the Month.●

                          ____________________
```
