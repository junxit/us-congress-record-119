# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 986, S. 1032, S. 1245, S. 1289, S. 1308, S. 1538, S. 1677, S. 1814, S. 1906, S. 1907, S. 3131, S. 3637, S. 3697, S. 3799, S. 3938, S. 4379, S. 4395, S. 4429, S. 4469, S. 4784, S. 4931, S. 5033, S. 5036, S. 5048, S. 5190, S. 5200, S. 5201

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 986

  At the request of Mr. Kaine, the name of the Senator from Hawaii (Mr. 
Schatz) was added as a cosponsor of S.

[[Page S4408]]

986, a bill to address and take action to prevent bullying and 
harassment of students.


                                S. 1032

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1032, a bill to 
amend title 10, United States Code, to provide for concurrent receipt 
of veterans' disability compensation and retired pay for disability 
retirees with combat-related disabilities, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1245

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from New 
York (Mrs. Gillibrand) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1245, a bill to 
amend title 38, United States Code, to expand health care and benefits 
from the Department of Veterans Affairs for military sexual trauma, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 1289

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the names of the Senator from Utah 
(Mr. Curtis), the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer), the Senator 
from Georgia (Mr. Warnock) and the Senator from Delaware (Ms. Blunt 
Rochester) were added as cosponsors of S. 1289, a bill to require the 
Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in commemoration of the 25th 
anniversary of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks on the United 
States and to support programs at the National September 11 Memorial 
and Museum at the World Trade Center.


                                S. 1308

  At the request of Mr. Tillis, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1308, a 
bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the criteria for 
approval of certain independent study programs for purposes of the 
educational assistance programs of the Department of Veterans Affairs.


                                S. 1538

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from Rhode 
Island (Mr. Whitehouse) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1538, a bill to 
amend the Animal Welfare Act to expand and improve the enforcement 
capabilities of the Attorney General, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1677

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the name of the Senator from 
Washington (Mrs. Murray) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1677, a bill to 
provide health insurance benefits for outpatient and inpatient items 
and services related to the diagnosis and treatment of a congenital 
anomaly or birth defect.


                                S. 1814

  At the request of Mr. Whitehouse, the name of the Senator from New 
Mexico (Mr. Lujan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1814, a bill to amend 
title 28, United States Code, to provide for a code of conduct for 
justices of the Supreme Court of the United States, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 1906

  At the request of Mr. Marshall, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Ms. Slotkin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1906, a bill to amend the 
Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act with respect to the regulation of 
zootechnical animal food substances.


                                S. 1907

  At the request of Mr. Marshall, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Ms. Slotkin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1907, a bill to amend the 
Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act to provide for a 
consistent definition for plant biostimulants.


                                S. 3131

  At the request of Mr. Schiff, the name of the Senator from California 
(Mr. Padilla) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3131, a bill to provide 
for the inclusion on the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Wall of the names of 
the lost crew members of the USS Frank E. Evans killed on June 3, 1969.


                                S. 3637

  At the request of Mr. Marshall, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Ms. Slotkin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3637, a bill to amend the 
National Agricultural Research, Extension, and Teaching Policy Act of 
1977 to reauthorize the Agriculture Advanced Research and Development 
Authority, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3697

  At the request of Mrs. Hyde-Smith, the names of the Senator from 
South Carolina (Mr. Scott) and the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. 
McCormick) were added as cosponsors of S. 3697, a bill to amend the 
Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to prohibit the approval of new 
abortion drugs, to prohibit investigational use exemptions for abortion 
drugs, and to impose additional regulatory requirements with respect to 
previously approved abortion drugs, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3799

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the name of the Senator from 
Georgia (Mr. Ossoff) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3799, a bill to 
amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize the Healthy Start 
Initiative.


                                S. 3938

  At the request of Mr. King, the name of the Senator from Idaho (Mr. 
Crapo) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3938, a bill to amend title 10, 
United States Code, to authorize representatives of veterans service 
organizations to participate in presentations to promote certain 
benefits available to veterans during preseparation counseling under 
the Transition Assistance Program of the Department of Defense, and for 
other purposes.


                                S. 4379

  At the request of Mr. Kennedy, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Ms. Blunt Rochester) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4379, a bill to 
require the Secretary of State and the Secretary of Defense to jointly 
submit a report on efforts by the Government of the Russian Federation 
to violate the religious freedoms of the people of Ukraine, to require 
the President to impose all applicable sanctions with respect to 
foreign persons determined to have engaged in such efforts, and for 
other purposes.


                                S. 4395

  At the request of Mr. McCormick, the names of the Senator from 
Montana (Mr. Daines) and the Senator from Hawaii (Ms. Hirono) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 4395, a bill to reauthorize the Terrorism 
Risk Insurance Act of 2002, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4429

  At the request of Mr. Moreno, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4429, a bill to 
prohibit the importation, manufacture, sale, resale, or introduction 
into interstate commerce in the United States of connected vehicles and 
related software and hardware associated with foreign adversaries.


                                S. 4469

  At the request of Mr. McCormick, the name of the Senator from Ohio 
(Mr. Moreno) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4469, a bill to amend the 
Commodity Exchange Act to modify provisions relating to event

                                S. 4931

  At the request of Mr. Lee, the name of the Senator from Wyoming (Ms. 
Lummis) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4931, a bill to amend the Clean 
Air Act to preserve consumer vehicle choice, protect the electric grid, 
and impose limits on regulations under that Act, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 5033

  At the request of Mr. Schiff, the name of the Senator from New Jersey 
(Mr. Kim) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5033, a bill to amend title 5, 
United States Code, to create a right of public access to certain 
records relating to the courts of the United States, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 5036

(Mr. Kim) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5036, a bill to amend title

                                S. 5190

                                S. 5200

  At the request of Mr. Fetterman, the name of the Senator from New

                                S. 5201

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the names of the Senator from
```
