# REMEMBERING RICHARD ALLEN "RICK" BALL

**Speaking:** Marshall, Roger (M001198) [R-KS]

```
                REMEMBERING RICHARD ALLEN ``RICK'' BALL

● Mr. MARSHALL. Mr. President, today we honor the life and 
legacy of Richard Allen ``Rick'' Ball of Great Bend, KS. Rick was a 
respected businessman, dedicated community leader, and devoted family 
man whose service left a lasting impact on those around him.
  For decades, Rick served the Great Bend community as a trusted 
certified public accountant and partner at Adams, Brown, Beran & Ball. 
He helped local businesses and organizations succeed through his sound 
advice and steady guidance. His leadership earned him recognition as 
the 2012 Great Bend Citizen of the Year and the 2008 U.S. Small 
Business Administration Financial Services Champion of the Year.
  However, Rick's dedication extended well beyond his professional 
career. He gave generously of his time through the Great Bend Chamber 
of Commerce, United Way, the Golden Belt Community Foundation, and many 
other local organizations. He also enjoyed hunting, gardening, and 
golfing, and he was known for his kindness and willingness to help 
others.
  Though he will be greatly missed, Rick's legacy lives on through his 
wife Lois, his children and grandchildren, and the many people whose 
lives he touched. His example will continue to inspire the Great Bend 
community for years to come.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Richard Allen ``Rick'' 
Ball and in offering our prayers and sympathies to his family, friends, 
and the entire Great Bend community.●

                          ____________________
```
