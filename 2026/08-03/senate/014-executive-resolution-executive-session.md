# EXECUTIVE RESOLUTION (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S.Res. 817

```
                          EXECUTIVE RESOLUTION

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I move to proceed to executive session to 
consider the en bloc nominations listed in S. Res. 817.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to the provisions of S. Res. 817, the 
nominations listed therein are pending en bloc.
```
