# EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

```
                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and were referred as 
indicated:

        EC-4269. A communication from the President of the United 
     States, transmitting, pursuant to law, a report of the 
     continuation of the national emergency with respect to 
     Lebanon that was declared in Executive Order 13441 of August 
     1, 2007; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban 
     Affairs.
        EC-4270. A communication from the Chief Regulatory 
     Officer, Citizenship and Immigration Services, Department of 
     Homeland Security, transmitting, pursuant to law, the report 
     of a rule entitled ``Affirmative Asylum Referrals Without 
     Interview'' (RIN1615-AD18) received in the Office of the 
     President of the Senate on July 29, 2026; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
        EC-4271. A communication from the Section Chief, Internal 
     Revenue Service, Department of the Treasury, transmitting, 
     pursuant to law, the report of a rule entitled ``Relief for 
     foreign participating member associations of FIFA from having 
     to file a Form 990 for years in which their only US source 
     income is related to participation in the 2026 FIFA World 
     Cup'' (Rev. Proc. 2026-28) received in the Office of the 
     President of the Senate on July 30, 2026; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
```
