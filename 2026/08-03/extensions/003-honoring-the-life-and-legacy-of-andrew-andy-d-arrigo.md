# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ANDREW "ANDY" D'ARRIGO

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

```
        HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ANDREW ``ANDY'' D'ARRIGO

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, August 3, 2026

  Ms. LOFGREN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Andrew ``Andy'' D'Arrigo, whose vision, leadership, and dedication 
to agriculture helped shape the Salinas Valley into the agricultural 
heart of our Nation. His passing marks the loss of one of California's 
most respected agricultural leaders, whose influence will endure for 
generations.
  Following his service in the United States Navy during World War II 
and graduation from the University of California, Davis, Andrew 
``Andy'' D'Arrigo returned to D'Arrigo Bros. Company. As President of 
D'Arrigo Bros. Company of California, he led the business by 
transforming it into one of the world's premier grower-shippers of 
fresh produce. His leadership created thousands of jobs, strengthened 
the regional economy, and elevated California agriculture on the global 
stage.
  Andrew ``Andy'' D'Arrigo believed that strong communities are 
essential to a thriving agricultural industry. Through the Andrew and 
Phyllis D'Arrigo Charitable Foundation and his personal philanthropy, 
he supported healthcare, education, youth development, and community 
organizations throughout Monterey County, helping improve the lives of 
countless families and reinforcing the strength of the Salinas Valley.
  Mr. Speaker, Andrew ``Andy'' D'Arrigo leaves behind an extraordinay 
legacy of agricultural innovation, economic leadership, and community 
service. His life's work strengthened the Salinas Valley, advanced 
California agriculture, and demonstrated the lasting impact of 
visionary leadership rooted in service to others.
  Therefore, it is with profound respect that I ask my colleagues to 
join me in honoring the life of Andrew ``Andy'' D'Arrigo. On behalf of 
the people of California's 18th Congressional District, I extend our 
deepest condolences to the D'Arrigo family and celebrate a life whose 
enduring contributions will remain integral to the Salinas Valley's 
agricultural heritage and community for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
