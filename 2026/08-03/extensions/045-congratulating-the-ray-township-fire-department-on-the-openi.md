# CONGRATULATING THE RAY TOWNSHIP FIRE DEPARTMENT ON THE OPENING OF ITS NEW FIRE STATION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 CONGRATULATING THE RAY TOWNSHIP FIRE DEPARTMENT ON THE OPENING OF ITS 
                            NEW FIRE STATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, August 3, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Ray Township 
Fire Department as it celebrates the commissioning of its new Fire 
Engine No. 1 and the ribbon cutting of its new fire station.
  Since its establishment in 1975, the Ray Township Fire Department has 
proudly served the community. For more than five decades, its dedicated 
firefighters have answered every call with courage, professionalism, 
and an unwavering commitment to protecting the lives and property of 
the residents of Ray Township. Their selfless service and willingness 
to respond at a moment's notice exemplify the very best of public 
service.
  The opening of this new fire station marks a significant investment 
in the safety and future of the community. Replacing the department's 
original station built in the 1970s, the modern facility features 
expanded vehicle bays, administrative offices, firefighter living 
quarters, a fitness area, and a dedicated training room. These 
improvements will enhance the department's readiness, strengthen 
emergency response capabilities, and ensure firefighters have the 
resources they need to protect the community for generations to come.
  The commissioning of the department's new Fire Engine No. 1 further 
reflects Ray Township's commitment to maintaining a well-equipped fire 
service. This new fire engine will help ensure firefighters can 
continue responding quickly and effectively to emergencies while 
meeting the evolving needs of a growing community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the Ray 
Township Fire Department on this remarkable milestone. On behalf of 
Michigan's 9th Congressional District, I extend my sincere to gratitude 
to the firefighters, officers, and all those whose vision and 
dedication made this achievement possible. I wish the Ray Township Fire 
Department continued success as it faithfully serves and protects the 
residents of Ray Township for many years to come.
```
