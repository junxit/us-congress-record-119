# HONORING CAROLINE KUHN

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                         HONORING CAROLINE KUHN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, August 3, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Caroline Kuhn for 
her outstanding service and many contributions to Virginia's 1st 
Congressional District and to our Nation.
  Caroline joined my office in January 2025 after serving on the House 
Committee on Ways and Means. Prior to coming to Washington, she earned 
both her Bachelor of Arts in Political Science and her Master of Public 
Administration from Texas A&M University, where she demonstrated an 
early commitment to public service and leadership.
  During her time in my office, Caroline worked her way from Staff 
Assistant to Legislative Aide, earning each new responsibility through 
her dedication, professionalism, and exceptional work ethic. She played 
a vital role in ensuring our office provided outstanding constituent 
service and helped manage one of the strongest constituent 
correspondence and mail programs in the Commonwealth of Virginia and 
the Congress.
  Caroline approached every task with thoughtfulness, kindness, and 
selflessness. No matter the challenge, she consistently rose to the 
occasion with a positive attitude and unwavering commitment to serving 
the people of Virginia's 1st District. She was also a trusted mentor to 
interns and staff alike, generously sharing her knowledge and helping 
cultivate a collaborative and supportive office culture. Her 
leadership, integrity, and willingness to invest in others have left a 
lasting mark on my team.
  I extend my sincere gratitude to Caroline for her dedicated service 
over the past year and a half. She has been an invaluable member of my 
staff, and while we will miss her greatly, I am confident she will 
continue to excel as she begins the next chapter of her career with the 
House Committee on Natural Resources. I wish her every success in this 
new role and look forward to seeing all she will accomplish in the 
years ahead.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Caroline 
Kuhn for her dedicated service to Virginia's 1st Congressional District 
and to our Nation. May God bless Caroline as she embarks on this 
exciting new chapter, and may she continue to serve our country with 
the same excellence and integrity that have defined her time in my 
office.

                          ____________________
```
