# RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS VETERAN WARRANT OFFICER MATTHEW D. ZAMMIT AS AUGUST 2026 VETERAN OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS VETERAN WARRANT OFFICER MATTHEW 
             D. ZAMMIT AS AUGUST 2026 VETERAN OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, August 3, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Warrant Officer 
Matthew D. Zammit for his distinguished service in the United States 
Marine Corps and his continued dedication to his family and community. 
It is my privilege to honor him as our August 2026 Veteran of the 
Month.
  Warrant Officer Zammit enlisted in the United States Marine Corps in 
1983. After graduating from recruit training at Marine Corps Recruit 
Depot Parris Island, South Carolina, he completed Infantry Weapons 
Repair School in Aberdeen, Maryland, beginning a remarkable 22-year 
military career.
  Throughout his service, Warrant Officer Zammit held assignments in 
California, Hawaii, Spain, Morocco, Ecuador, Japan, and North Carolina. 
He served as a Maine Security Guard protecting American embassies 
overseas before becoming an Ordnance Inspector with the 2nd Marine 
Division. In 1999, he was promoted to Warrant Officer and reassigned as 
the Lead G-4 Inspection Team Officer for small arms and artillery for 
the 2nd Marine Division, II Marine Expeditionary Force, where he 
demonstrated exceptional leadership and technical expertise.
  His outstanding service was recognized with numerous honors, 
including three Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medals, six Good 
Conduct Medals, two National Defense Service Medals, the Global War on 
Terrorism Service Medal, Meritorious Unit Commendations, and multiple 
expert marksmanship awards.
  Following his retirement from the Marine Corps in 2005, Warrant 
Officer Zammit continued serving our Nation in civilian roles 
supporting the United States Army. Over the next two decades, he 
supported military operations at Fort Bragg, Detroit Arsenal, and Fort 
Hood, while deploying to Iraq, Afghanistan, and Kuwait. His continued 
service earned additional recognition, including the NATO Medal, 
Superior Civilian Award, and a Two-Star Letter of Appreciation.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Warrant Officer Zammit has 
remained committed to serving his community. He earned a bachelor's 
degree in psychology and two master's degrees in organizational 
management, supply chain, and life coaching. He later shared his 
passion for mentorship by coaching high school cross-country and track 
and field athletes.
  Together with his wife, Captain Marisol Zammit, a fellow United 
States Marine Corps veteran, he has raised four children while 
continuing to exemplify the values of service, faith, and leadership. 
Today, Warrant Officer Zammit and his family reside in China Township, 
where they remain active in their church and community.
  Mr. Speaker, Warrant Officer Matthew D. Zammit has dedicated his life 
to serving our Nation and his community. Through his military service 
and continued commitment to others, he exemplifies the values of honor, 
courage, and commitment. It is my great honor to recognize his service 
and accomplishments by naming him our August 2026 Veteran of the Month.
```
