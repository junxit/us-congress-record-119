# HONORING THE TOWN OF ARBUCKLE, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING THE TOWN OF ARBUCKLE, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, August 3, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
town of Arbuckle, California, on the sesquicentennial anniversary of 
its founding, and to recognize its rich history and community spirit. 
This celebration honors the generations of residents, civic leaders, 
and organizations that have shaped the community's unique character, 
agricultural heritage, vibrant identity, and forward-looking values.
  The town of Arbuckle was founded in 1875 following the arrival of the 
railroad in the Sacramento Valley. Arbuckle has played an important 
role in the agricultural, cultural, and economic development of Colusa 
County. The year 2026 marks the 150th anniversary of the first 
jollification of Arbuckle, and the community will celebrate this 
historic milestone, honoring the generations of residents, businesses, 
farmers, and community organizations that have contributed to the 
town's enduring legacy.
  Since its founding, Arbuckle has served as an agricultural center in 
the Sacramento Valley and has fostered a strong tradition of 
volunteerism, civic engagement, and community service. The town was 
named after pioneer and rancher Tacitus Ryland Arbuckle, who donated 
land for the railroad right-of-way, depot, and townsite, helping 
establish the foundation for the community's growth. This community has 
supported schools, local businesses, parks, youth programs, veterans' 
memorials, and numerous annual events that strengthen community bonds 
and preserve local heritage. Arbuckle residents have consistently 
demonstrated resilience, generosity, and commitment to improving the 
quality of life for present and future generations.
  Arbuckle is widely recognized for its strong sense of community, 
agricultural heritage, volunteer spirit, and small-town character. 
Generations of families have called Arbuckle home, contributing to a 
tradition of neighborliness, civic pride, and stewardship. The 
community's enduring commitment to preserving its history while 
embracing future opportunities exemplifies the values that have 
sustained Arbuckle for 150 years.
  Mr. Speaker, the town of Arbuckle has had a profound impact on our 
community and our city. Therefore, it is fitting and proper that we 
honor the city's sesquicentennial anniversary here today as they 
celebrate this milestone.
```
