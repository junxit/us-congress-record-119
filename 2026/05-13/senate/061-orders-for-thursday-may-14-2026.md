# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 14, 2026

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S.Res. 526, S.Res. 690

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 14, 2026

  Mr. MORENO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Thursday, May 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motion with respect to the 
nominations to be considered en bloc pursuant to S. Res. 690 ripen at 
1:45 p.m. tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MORENO. For the information of my colleagues, they should expect 
a vote in relation to S. Res. 526 at noon tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
