# TRIBUTE TO HAYES HANSEN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO HAYES HANSEN

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Hayes Hansen, an intern 
in

[[Page S2273]]

my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has done for me, 
my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several weeks.
  Hayes is a graduate of Custer County High School in Miles City, MT, 
and a recent graduate of Black Hills State University in Spearfish, SD, 
having earned degrees in political science and international studies. 
This fall, Hayes plans to attend the University of Nebraska College of 
Law in Lincoln, NE. Hayes is a dedicated worker who has been committed 
to getting the most out of her experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Hayes for all the fine 
work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
