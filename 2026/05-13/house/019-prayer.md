# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O Lord, our guardian, guide, and stay, transform the thoughts of our 
minds, that what comes from our mouths will reflect our love for You 
and our desire to serve You well in this place.
  Guide our thinking throughout the day, in our comings and goings, our 
interviews, and exchanges, that what we perceive in these encounters 
would be seasoned with Your grace and that how we choose to respond 
would be leavened with Your mercy.
  Stay our tongues, that we would not be quick to retort with 
unwholesome talk, but harness our passions, that they would be governed 
with kindness, directed toward the building up of others, according to 
their needs, and offered for the benefit of all those who bear witness 
to our words.
  Lord, may the words of our mouths and the meditations of our hearts 
be acceptable in Your sight. We pray this day in the strength of Your 
name, O Lord, our rock and our redeemer.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
