# RECOGNIZING MARTIN COUNTY WRIGHT FLIGHT FLY DAY

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
            RECOGNIZING MARTIN COUNTY WRIGHT FLIGHT FLY DAY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, dreams are taking flight in 
Martin County, North Carolina.
  The Wright Flight Fly Day rewards sixth graders from across the 
county for academic success, leadership, and positive choices by giving 
them an opportunity to see their home community in an airplane from the 
skies above.
  Through a powerful partnership between the local schools, sheriff's 
office, and airport commission, students are connected to real-world 
opportunities in aviation, engineering, science, technology, and public 
service.
  We are working to keep the American Dream within reach.
  Wright Flight reminds young people across eastern North Carolina that 
their hopes and dreams are achievable and their future can soar even 
higher.
```
