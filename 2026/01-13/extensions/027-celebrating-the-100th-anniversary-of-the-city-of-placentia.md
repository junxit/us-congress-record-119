# CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE CITY OF PLACENTIA

**Speaking:** Tran, Derek (T000491) [D-CA]

```
       CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE CITY OF PLACENTIA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DEREK TRAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 13, 2026

  Mr. TRAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate a truly momentous 
milestone as the City of Placentia celebrates its 100th Anniversary. 
For a century, Placentia has stood as a vibrant and resilient community 
in Orange County, enriched by its history, strengthened by its 
diversity, and united by a shared commitment to progress.
  Founded in 1926, Placentia grew from its agricultural roots into a 
thriving suburban city while preserving the values that have long 
defined it: hard work, civic pride, and a deep sense of community. From 
its early citrus groves to the dynamic neighborhoods, schools, and 
businesses that define the city today, Placentia's story is one of 
thoughtful growth and enduring opportunity.
  Over the past 100 years, generations of residents, civic leaders, 
educators, public servants, and business owners have contributed to 
Placentia's success. Their dedication has fostered a city known for 
strong public institutions, safe neighborhoods, and a commitment to 
education, innovation, and community engagement. Placentia's ability to 
adapt while honoring its past reflects the very best of the American 
spirit.
  As Placentia marks its centennial, we also look forward to the next 
generation of leaders, families, and entrepreneurs who will continue 
shaping the city's future. With ongoing investments in infrastructure, 
public safety, education, and economic development, Placentia is well-
positioned to thrive for the next hundred years and beyond.
  On this historic occasion, I extend my heartfelt congratulations to 
the residents of Placentia and express my deepest appreciation to all 
who have contributed to the city's growth and success. It is an honor 
to represent this remarkable community in Congress, and I am proud to 
recognize the City of Placentia's 100 years of history, service, and 
achievement.

                          ____________________
```
