# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7026

**Measures:** H.R. 7026

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 7026.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, 
     including its powers over the federal budget, oversight of 
     the executive branch, and the authority to make all laws 
     necessary and proper for carrying out those powers.
```
