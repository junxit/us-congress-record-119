# Text of Senate Amendment 4195

**Speaking:** Cruz, Ted (C001098) [R-TX]

```
  SA 4195. Mr. CRUZ submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the joint resolution S.J. Res. 98, to direct the removal of 
United States Armed Forces from hostilities within or against Venezuela 
that have not been authorized by Congress; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the end of section 1, add the following:
       (5) Members of the United States Armed Forces and 
     intelligence community, and all those involved in the 
     operations within Venezuela related to the January 3, 2026, 
     capture of Nicolas Maduro, including President Donald J. 
     Trump, should be commended for their efforts in a successful 
     mission.
                                 ______
```
