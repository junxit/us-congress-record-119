# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, strong to save, whose arms have bound the restless 
waves, we praise You for Your magnificent love that awakens us each 
day.
  Thank You for lawmakers who strive to please You. Lord, continue to 
guide their feet and teach them Your wisdom.
  In these turbulent times, show Yourself strong on behalf of those who 
love You. Solve the riddles that confound us. Confuse those who seek to 
hinder Your providence. And give Your strength to those who would like 
to throw in the towel. Bring order to a world that often seems to spin 
out of control. Please comfort all who mourn.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
