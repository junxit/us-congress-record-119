# Text of Senate Amendment 4215

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 4215. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 6938, making consolidated appropriations for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title II of division A, insert 
     the following:
       Sec. 223.  None of the funds made available to the 
     Executive Office for Immigration Review under this title may 
     be used for immigration judges who facilitate any arrest by 
     U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement officers or agents 
     at any courthouse.
                                 ______
```
