# Text of Senate Amendment 6712

**Speaking:** Britt, Katie Boyd (B001319) [R-AL]

```
  SA 6712. Mrs. BRITT (for Ms. Graham (for herself and Mr. Blumenthal)) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by Mrs. Britt to the 
bill H.R. 5334, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow 
early childhood educators to take the educator expense deduction, and 
for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``An Act to impose sanctions 
     and other measures with respect to the Russian Federation, as 
     championed by the late Senator Lindsey O. Graham, and for 
     other purposes.''.
                                 ______
```
