# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God of infinite goodness, confirm Your past mercies by 
empowering us to be faithful to Your commands.
  Lord, help our lawmakers this day to use their understanding, time, 
and talents to do what You desire. May our Senators desire to please 
You with faithful service as You rule their hearts, guiding their 
thoughts, words, and works. Enable them to fulfill their duty to love 
You with all their heart, mind, and strength. Take possession of their 
desires and order their steps by the power of Your loving providence. 
Pour down Your blessings upon them, that they may ever promote liberty, 
justice, and faith.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
