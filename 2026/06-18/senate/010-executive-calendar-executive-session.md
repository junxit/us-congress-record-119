# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schmitt, Eric (S001227) [R-MO]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. SCHMITT. Mr. President, I ask that the Senate proceed to 
executive session and the Chair execute the order with respect to the 
Holding nomination at 1:45 p.m. today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to executive session and resume consideration of the following 
nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of George 
Holding, of North Carolina, to be United States Director of the 
European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Oklahoma.
```
