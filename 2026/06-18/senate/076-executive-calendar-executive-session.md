  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations en 
bloc: Calendar No. 750 through 756; and all nominations on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, and Navy, with 
the exception of PN681-4; PN835-2; PN925; Matthew T. Anderson, PN969;

that the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the 
table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be 
in order to any of the nominations; and that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
