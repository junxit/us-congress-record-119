# HONORING THE CAREER OF MS. SURABHI DABIR

**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

```
                HONORING THE CAREER OF MS. SURABHI DABIR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 4, 2026

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
career and public service of Ms. Surabhi Dabir, a respected policy 
leader and dedicated advocate for housing and community development 
across our Nation.
  For more than three decades, Ms. Dabir has worked at the intersection 
of federal policy, community engagement, and economic opportunity, 
helping to shape programs that have strengthened rural, Tribal, and 
underserved communities.
  From her leadership roles at the Housing Assistance Council and 
Enterprise Community Partners, to her service at the U.S. Department of 
Agriculture's Rural Housing Service, she has consistently translated 
complex policy into real results for families in need.
  During her tenure at USDA, Ms. Dabir served as a trusted advisor to 
senior leadership and Members of Congress, helping to guide billions of 
dollars in housing and infrastructure investments, advance Farm Bill 
priorities, and expand access to affordable homeownership.
  Her work has supported disaster recovery efforts, strengthened rural 
economies, and ensured that communities across the country have the 
tools to thrive.
  Ms. Dabir has been invaluable to my office as well--twice. First as a 
USDA fellow and now in her current role as a Legislative Assistant, Ms. 
Dabir fought hard for rural services, and especially for colonias.
  Beyond her professional accomplishments, Ms. Dabir's commitment to 
service is grounded in family. She is supported by her husband, Dr. 
Jeremy Blum, and their sons, Arjun and Anand, who share in this moment 
of recognition.
  Mr. Speaker, Ms. Surabhi Dabir's career reflects a deep and enduring 
commitment to public service, innovation, and opportunity. Her impact 
will be felt for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
