# HONORING THE CAREER OF MR. JIMMY CORNELIUS

**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

```
               HONORING THE CAREER OF MR. JIMMY CORNELIUS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 4, 2026

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the extraordinary 
service and enduring legacy of Mr. Jimmy Cornelius, a distinguished 
American whose life reflects a deep and unwavering commitment to our 
nation and community.
  Mr. Cornelius devoted more than 27 years to the United States Air 
Force, serving with honor in both Security Police and the legal field, 
and retiring as a Senior Master Sergeant. His military career was 
marked by leadership, excellence, and valor, supporting missions across 
the globe.
  Following his service in uniform, he continued his dedication through 
nearly 17 years in civil service with the Office of the Staff Judge 
Advocate at Joint Base San Antonio-Randolph, bringing his total to an 
exceptional 44 years of service to our country.
  Beyond his professional achievements, Mr. Cornelius has made a 
profound impact on Randolph Field ISD, where he has served as a trustee 
since 2011, including multiple years as board president. His leadership 
has strengthened educational opportunities for military-connected 
students and families, ensuring they have the tools to succeed.
  Mr. Cornelius' life is a testament to duty, integrity, and selfless 
service. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his remarkable 
contributions and wishing him the very best in his well-earned 
retirement.
```
