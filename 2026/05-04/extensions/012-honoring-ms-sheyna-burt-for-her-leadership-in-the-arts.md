# HONORING MS. SHEYNA BURT FOR HER LEADERSHIP IN THE ARTS

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
        HONORING MS. SHEYNA BURT FOR HER LEADERSHIP IN THE ARTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 4, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize Ms. 
Sheyna Burt, an accomplished attorney, President of Youth Orchestras of 
Prince William, dedicated violinist, and most notably a community 
leader. It is my privilege to recognize her on the occasion of An 
Evening Above the Clouds, a celebration marking 16 years of uplifting 
our community through the Hylton Performing Arts Center.
  More than 25 years ago, Ms. Burt began her musical journey as a 
student in the Prince William County Public Schools' strings program. 
What began as a childhood hobby grew into a lifelong passion and 
purpose. Today as President of the Youth Orchestras of Prince William 
and the Old Bridge Chamber Orchestra, she remains committed to sharing 
the arts and ensuring that generations to come have access to those 
same opportunities as she did.
  Through her leadership both on stage and in public forums, she has 
helped shape a more inclusive and vibrant cultural landscape, expanded 
access to music education, and advocated for the importance of the 
arts.
  Her work extends well beyond performance. Through her legal 
expertise, Ms. Burt has supported nonprofit organizations in advancing 
their missions.
  Through her leadership with the Hylton Performing Arts Center as a 
board member, board chair, and now immediate past chair--she has helped 
to facilitate an environment where artists and audiences can connect, 
thrive, and be inspired.
  For more than 25 years, Ms. Burt has made a lasting impact on our 
community through her vision, her advocacy, and her unwavering 
dedication to others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Ms. Burt, a 
true champion of the arts and a leader who will impact generations to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
