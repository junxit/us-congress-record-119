# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SHERIFF DALE "FROG" FORD

**Speaking:** Comer, James (C001108) [R-KY]

```
       HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SHERIFF DALE ``FROG'' FORD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAMES COMER

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 4, 2026

  Mr. COMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Sheriff Dale

[[Page E414]]

``Frog'' Ford, a devoted public servant, respected law enforcement 
officer, and cherished member of the Monroe County community.
  Sheriff Ford dedicated his life to serving and protecting the people 
of his hometown. For forty-eight years he wore the badge with pride, 
integrity, and unwavering sense of duty. Over the course of his 
distinguished career, he served the citizens of Tompkinsville as Chief 
of Police for ten years and later as Sheriff of Monroe County for 
twelve years. In every role he held Sheriff Ford earned the trust and 
respect of those he served through his steady leadership, fairness, and 
deep commitment to his community.
  I offer my sincerest condolences to his wife of twenty-one years, 
Ronda, and his children Kimberly, Chris, and Sean, and his stepchildren 
Shelby, and Alex, and his eight grandchildren. Sheriff Ford's service, 
courage, and leadership have set an example for future generations, of 
law enforcement officers. With his passing, Monroe County has lost a 
beloved and legendary figure who will not be forgotten.

                          ____________________
```
