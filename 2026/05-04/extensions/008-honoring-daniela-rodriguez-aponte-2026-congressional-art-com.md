# HONORING DANIELA RODRIGUEZ APONTE, 2026 CONGRESSIONAL ART COMPETITION WINNER FOR PUERTO RICO

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
 HONORING DANIELA RODRIGUEZ APONTE, 2026 CONGRESSIONAL ART COMPETITION 
                         WINNER FOR PUERTO RICO

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 4, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
achievement of the winner of the 2026 Congressional Art Competition in 
Puerto Rico, a nationwide initiative that encourages high school 
students to explore art, innovation, and cultural expression.
  This year, students were invited to reimagine our island through the 
creativity, storytelling, and design principles of video games. Among 
many talented participants, one student distinguished herself through 
originality, strong thematic relevance, and technical excellence.
  I am proud to recognize Daniela Rodriguez Aponte, a 12th grade 
student at Escuela Especializada Central de Artes Visuales in San Juan, 
as this year's winner. Her piece, titled ``Level Up: Boriken Dreams,'' 
is a digital illustration that portrays Puerto Rican youth immersed in 
a vibrant, arcade-like setting, engaging with a game that reflects 
their identity, environment, and aspirations. Through its composition 
and use of color, the work captures both imagination and cultural 
pride, offering a compelling interpretation of the competition's theme.
  This artwork reflects not only technical excellence, but also a 
thoughtful message about how younger generations engage with culture, 
technology, and community. It is a clear demonstration of how art can 
serve as a powerful language to communicate ideas and inspire 
reflection.
  Daniela's achievement is a testament to the creativity, discipline, 
and talent that distinguishes Puerto Rico's young artists. It also 
underscores the importance of arts education in fostering innovation 
and expression, and in strengthening the cultural fabric of our 
communities.
  On behalf of the people of Puerto Rico, I extend my sincere 
congratulations to Daniela Rodriguez Aponte for this remarkable 
accomplishment. Her work represents the creativity, determination, and 
excellence that we strive to cultivate in all students.

                          ____________________
```
