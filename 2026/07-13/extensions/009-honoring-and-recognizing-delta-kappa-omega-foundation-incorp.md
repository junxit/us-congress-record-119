# HONORING AND RECOGNIZING DELTA KAPPA OMEGA FOUNDATION, INCORPORATED

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
  HONORING AND RECOGNIZING DELTA KAPPA OMEGA FOUNDATION, INCORPORATED

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
opening of the Delta Kappa Omega/Providence Community Service Center in 
Tallahassee, Florida, which will continue to help improve the lives of 
people of Leon County.
  The community center is the culmination of years of dedication and 
commitment by the Delta Kappa Omega Foundation, Incorporated, providing 
academic scholarships to deserving students through its Annual 
Gentlemen Sampler fundraising event and providing financial support to 
the under-served through its partnership with local social services 
agencies; and through countless other programs and projects.
  The organization worked with many agencies in the community, 
including the City of Tallahassee, the Community Redevelopment Agency 
and the Providence Neighborhood Association, to build the Delta Kappa 
Omega/Providence Community Service Center.
  Delta Kappa Omega Foundation, Incorporated, was established in Leon 
County in October of 1996. For the past 16 years, the organization has 
been committed to providing service that enhances the education, civic 
and social well-being of the citizens of Leon County and surrounding 
communities.

[[Page E669]]

  The members of Delta Kappa Omega Foundation, Incorporated have 
demonstrated how the strength, talents and commitment of women working 
together, to implement the ideals of community service, can change a 
community for the better and improve the quality of life.
  Please join me in honoring and recognizing the outstanding 
accomplishments of Delta Kappa Omega Foundation, Incorporated, and its 
long-standing legacy of service to this community.

                          ____________________
```
