# HONORING THE LIFE OF SUSAN LASCOLETTE

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF SUSAN LASCOLETTE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
life of Susan Lascolette, a cherished member of our community. Susan 
was an extraordinarily dedicated individual who worked tirelessly and 
persistently to make her country, commonwealth, and Nation a better 
place.
  Susan had an impressive career as a Senior Insurance Underwriter with 
notable firms such as Maryland Casualty, Security Insurance, and 
Markel; not to mention Susan's significant civic contributions, where 
she served as District No. 1 Supervisor for Goochland County from 2012 
to 2024 and was past President of the Goochland Republican Women. Susan 
was also an active member of the Goochland Republican Committee and 
served as member of the State Central Committee of the Republican Party 
of Virginia from 2016 representing the 7th and 5th Districts.
  Her devotion to service was not only evident in her professional 
life, but in her personal life as well. Susan was deeply involved in 
the community, volunteering whenever she could, helping support causes 
such as Meals on Wheels, Goochland Cares, and the Goochland Fire 
Auxiliary. Susan's kindness touched everyone she met, and she leaves 
behind a legacy full of compassion and devotion.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in celebrating Susan's incredible life. Our community is 
eternally grateful for everything she has done.

                          ____________________
```
