# COMMEMORATING THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE KNIGHTS OF COLUMBUS MARQUETTE COUNCIL 588

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
    COMMEMORATING THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE KNIGHTS OF COLUMBUS 
                         MARQUETTE COUNCIL 588

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 125th 
anniversary of the Knights of Columbus Marquette Council 588. For more 
than a century, Marquette Council 588 has faithfully served three 
Sussex County parishes in New Jersey: Our Lady of the Lake in Sparta, 
St. Thomas of Aquin in Ogdensburg, and Immaculate Conception in 
Franklin.
  Established in 1901, Marquette Council 588 stands among the oldest 
Knights of Columbus councils in both New Jersey and the United States. 
Their name honors Father Jacques Marquette, the French priest and 
explorer.
  Throughout its 125-year history, Marquette Council 588 has played an 
integral role in many behind-the-scenes activities within the parishes, 
and beyond. The Council's unwavering commitment is evident in its 
support for families in need, assistance to veterans, engagement with 
youth, and dedication to charitable service. Members consistently serve 
their neighbors, regardless of background or faith, embodying the 
principles of Charity, Unity, and Fraternity.
  The Knights actively live out the Beatitudes through both word and 
deed. Recent initiatives, such as delivering 3,000 Thanksgiving turkeys 
to families across Sussex County, collecting nearly 1,000 coats for 
those confronting winter's chill, and organizing numerous food drives 
clearly reflect their spirit of selfless service.
  As we celebrate Marquette Council 588's remarkable milestone, we 
extend our gratitude for 125 years of dedicated service to Sussex 
County and the State of New Jersey. I sincerely wish them continued 
success for the next 125 years and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
