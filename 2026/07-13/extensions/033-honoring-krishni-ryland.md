# HONORING KRISHNI RYLAND

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING KRISHNI RYLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Ms. Krishni Ryland as she celebrates a remarkable season of 
achievement marked by three significant milestones: earning a Master of 
Arts in Teaching degree in Secondary Education, publishing her first 
book, and beginning her teaching career as a Career, Technical, and 
Agricultural Education educator.
  After faithfully serving our nation for 20 years in the United States 
Army, Ms. Ryland retired from military service and embarked upon a new 
journey centered on education, faith, and service to others. Her 
commitment to personal growth and lifelong learning is evident in her 
impressive academic accomplishments. Most recently, she entered a 
Master of Arts in Teaching degree in Secondary Education from Liberty 
University. Prior to this achievement, she earned a Master of Arts in 
Teaching degree in Secondary Education from Liberty University. Prior 
to this achievement, she earned bachelor's degrees in Business 
Administration and Religion, demonstrating her dedication to both 
professional excellence and spiritual development.
  In a addition to completing her master's degree, Ms. Ryland has 
become a published author with the release of her book, Did God Really 
Say That? Unlearning What We Thought Was Scripture. Through this work, 
she encourages readers to deepen their understanding of biblical 
teachings and challenges them to examine commonly held beliefs through 
the lens of Scripture. Continuing her mission of service, Ms. Ryland 
will begin her teaching career in the fall of 2026 as a Career, 
Technical, and Agricultural Education educator. In this role, she will 
have the opportunity to inspire, mentor, and equip the next generation 
of leaders and professionals. Her transition from military service to 
the classroom reflects her enduring commitment to empowering others and 
strengthening communities through education.
  Ms. Ryland is also the founder of Speak Life Outreach Ministries, a 
nonprofit organization dedicated to encouraging, uplifting, and 
transforming lives through faith-based outreach and biblical teaching. 
Through her ministry, she has touched countless individuals by sharing 
messages of hope, purpose, and spiritual growth.
  A proud daughter of John and Shirley Meeks of Yazoo City, 
Mississippi, Ms. Ryland's accomplishments are a testament to the values 
of faith, perseverance, service, and dedication instilled in her 
throughout her life.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join in congratulating Krishni 
Ryland on these extraordinary achievements and in celebrating her 
continued contributions to education, faith, and public service. Her 
life and work serve as an inspiration to all.
```
