# RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF SUMMIT HIGH SCHOOL LACROSSE COACH JIM DAVIDSON

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF SUMMIT HIGH SCHOOL LACROSSE COACH JIM 
                                DAVIDSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Coach Jim Davidson 
of Summit, New Jersey, upon his retirement from Summit High School 
men's lacrosse team.
  Coach Davidson began his journey with Summit High School's lacrosse 
team in 1986 and became head coach in 1999. Drawing on his extensive 
athletic background in basketball and football, as well as his 
outstanding leadership capabilities, he transformed the program into a 
nationally recognized powerhouse. Throughout the past 27 years, Coach 
Davidson has led his team to 14 state titles, two Tournament of 
Champions crowns, and a remarkable 68-game winning streak, and most 
recently, a ranking as the top public school in the country for men's 
lacrosse.
  While some may remember his tenure as the fourth most winningest 
men's lacrosse coach in state history, his true legacy lies in the 
foundation of what he built at Summit. Coach Davidson established a 
program rooted in repetition and structure. Preparation was exemplary 
and standards did not bend regardless of talent or age group. Coach 
Davidson played a pivotal role in growing and sustaining youth interest 
in the high school program, ensuring its lasting success.
  By instilling discipline and fostering a culture of respect, he 
cultivated generations of dedicated student-athletes. While Coach 
Davidson's retirement marking the end of an era in New Jersey lacrosse, 
his influence extends beyond wins and losses. Coach Davidson is admired 
for his class, humility, and respect, earning recognition from coaches 
across the state for his significant contributions to the sport and his 
influence on countless young athletes.
  Mr. Speaker, as we celebrate Coach Jim Davidson's enduring impact and 
legacy, I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring him for his 
steadfast commitment to Summit High School men's lacrosse team.
```
