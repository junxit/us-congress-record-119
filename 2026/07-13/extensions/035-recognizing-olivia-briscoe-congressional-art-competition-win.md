# RECOGNIZING OLIVIA BRISCOE, CONGRESSIONAL ART COMPETITION WINNER

**Speaking:** Houchin, Erin (H001093) [R-IN]

```
    RECOGNIZING OLIVIA BRISCOE, CONGRESSIONAL ART COMPETITION WINNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIN HOUCHIN

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mrs. HOUCHIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a remarkably 
talented young artist, Olivia Briscoe, a senior at Salem High School, 
for winning first place in the 2026 Congressional Art Competition for 
Indiana's 9th Congressional District.
  Each spring, Members of Congress sponsor the Congressional Art 
Competition to encourage and celebrate the artistic talents of high 
school students across the country. The competition provides young 
artists with the opportunity to showcase their work while representing 
their home districts on a national stage.
  Olivia's winning acrylic painting, Teal Flowers, stood out for its 
exceptional detail and creativity, in recognition of her achievement, 
her artwork will be displayed for one year in the United States Capitol 
alongside winning pieces from congressional districts across the 
Nation. Her painting is also featured on the House of Representatives' 
Congressional Art Competition website, where it can be viewed by people 
across the country.
  This accomplishment not only reflects Olivia's talent and dedication, 
but also the support and encouragement of her teachers, family, and the 
Salem High School community.
  I congratulate Olivia on her outstanding achievement. I wish her 
continued success in all her future endeavors, and I thank her for 
representing Indiana's 9th Congressional District with creativity and 
excellence.

                          ____________________
```
