# HONORING BILLIE JOE FRAZIER

```
                      HONORING BILLIE JOE FRAZIER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to honor a remarkable 
servant, Mr. Billie Joe Frazier.
  Billie Joe Frazier is a graduate of Sadie V. Thompson High School. He 
attended Jackson State University before enlisting in the U.S. Navy, 
where he served from 1971 to 1974. He later joined the Natchez Police 
Department.
  Mr. Frazier began his career with the Natchez Police Department in 
1976, becoming one of the first 15 African Americans to serve as a 
police officer in the City of Natchez. During his 32 years of service, 
he held several leadership positions, including Patrol Supervisor and 
Supervisor of Jail Administration and Records.
  Throughout his career, Mr. Frazier demonstrated courage, 
professionalism, and a deep commitment to his community. He was 
recognized as a community hero for rescuing two women trapped in a 
vehicle during a flood. He also survived being shot in the line of 
duty, yet continued to serve with dedication and resilience.
  Mr. Frazier is married to Shelly ``Shirley'' Fitzgerald Frazier, and 
they have three daughters: Jarita Frazier-King, Courtney Frazier, and 
Skylar King-Frazier. They also have one son, Keimon B. Frazier, along 
with four beautiful grandchildren and one great-granddaughter, 
affectionately known as their ``chocolate drop.'' Mr. Frazier is a 
deacon at Greater Mt. Sinai and currently serves as an alderman for the 
City of Natchez.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Billie 
Joe Frazier of Natchez, Mississippi, for his actions, achievements, and 
dedicated service to the Natchez community.
```
