# COMMEMORATING THE RETIREMENT OF SUMMIT FIRE CHIEF ERIC EVERS

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
      COMMEMORATING THE RETIREMENT OF SUMMIT FIRE CHIEF ERIC EVERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the retirement of 
Summit Fire Chief Eric Evers, who has served the Summit Fire Department 
with distinction for an extraordinary 38 years. Chief Evers' steadfast 
dedication, leadership, and commitment to the safety and well-being of 
Summit residents are worthy of the highest commendation.
  Chief Evers began his journey with the department as a volunteer 
firefighter in 1987, quickly rising through the ranks to become a 
career firefighter in 1990. Over the years, he served in multiple 
command roles, including Lieutenant, Battalion Chief, Deputy Chief, and 
Summit Fire Official, culminating in his appointment as Fire Chief in 
2015. Throughout his tenure, Chief Evers consistently demonstrated 
professionalism, courage, and a steadfast focus on public safety.
  Chief Evers' impact is evident in the collaborative partnerships 
forged with the Summit Police Department, Summit Volunteer First Aid 
Squad, and numerous local, county, state, and federal agencies. Chief 
Evers played an integral role in leading emergency operations and 
ensuring residents remained informed about fire safety. As Deputy 
Emergency Management Coordinator and Operations Chief for the National 
Fallen Firefighters Foundation Memorial Weekend, he brought national 
recognition to Summit's commitment to excellence.
  Chief Evers leaves behind a legacy defined by significant 
achievements. Among these, his work to prioritize staffing, expand the 
department by hiring additional firefighters, and champion the 
completion of Summit's new, state-of-the-art fire station are 
especially notable. The new facility, equipped with advanced 
decontamination systems, enhanced training areas, and upgraded living 
spaces, stands as a testament to Chief Evers' vision and determination 
to protect both firefighters and Summit residents.
  Throughout his career, Chief Evers received multiple valor awards, 
reflecting his unwavering dedication and heroism. His leadership 
inspired those around them and set a standard for bravery, 
professionalism, and service that will resonate throughout the 
community for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring Chief Eric 
Evers on a well-earned retirement. We extend our best wishes for 
continued success and happiness in the years ahead. Summit is safer, 
stronger, and better because of Chief Evers' remarkable leadership and 
steadfast commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
