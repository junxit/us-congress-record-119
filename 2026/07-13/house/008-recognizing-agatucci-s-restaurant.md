# RECOGNIZING AGATUCCI'S RESTAURANT

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
                              {time}  1215
                   RECOGNIZING AGATUCCI'S RESTAURANT

  (Mr. LaHood of Illinois was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Agatucci's 
Restaurant in Peoria, Illinois, as it approaches an extraordinary 
milestone of 100 years in business.
  This family-owned restaurant has been a cornerstone of central 
Illinois since 1926, representing the enduring spirit of 
entrepreneurship, family, and community that defines the heart of 
Peoria.
  Founded in 1926 by Vincenzo ``James'' Agatucci, Agatucci's Restaurant 
began as a small grocery store before becoming one of Peoria's most 
beloved family-owned restaurants.
  During the Great Depression, James transformed his grocery store into 
a restaurant to serve the community, later obtaining liquor license 
number 13, now the oldest continually owned license in Peoria.
  The Agatucci family has built a tradition of hard work, tremendous 
hospitality, and excellence. From introducing pizza to Peoria after 
World War II to expanding into the frozen pizza and sauce market, 
Agatucci's has continued to bring people together over delicious food 
and warm hospitality.
  Agatucci's is proudly operated by the fourth generation of the 
Agatucci family. As the restaurant enters its 10th decade, Danny and 
Tony Agatucci remain committed to the same values that built their 
family's legacy.
  As we commemorate this incredible milestone, I recognize Danny and 
Tony Agatucci, their fathers whom they learned from, and the entire 
Agatucci family for their dedication, entrepreneurial spirit, and 
lasting contribution to Peoria.
  Congratulations on 100 years of family, food, and community. Best 
wishes for the next 100 years.

                          ____________________
```
