# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy and wise God, grant our hearts wisdom. May we receive Your Word 
and heed Your instruction, that what we intend for this day would 
prosper in accordance with Your will.
  May we trust in You, O Lord, that we would bear the fruit of the 
guidance You provide as we labor in this place.
  Grant us, as well, the gift of discernment, and the support of Your 
strength and encouragement, so that, as challenging as it may seem, we 
would speak Your truth and do what is right in Your sight.
  From the fountain of life that You graciously provide, may we learn 
prudence. Then may the meditations of our hearts and the words of our 
mouths prove judicious and useful, keeping us from the influence of our 
folly.
  Today, may the lives we live and the words we speak be led by the 
hand of Your instruction, that we may teach others of Your wisdom and 
will.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
