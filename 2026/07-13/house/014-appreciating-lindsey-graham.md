# APPRECIATING LINDSEY GRAHAM

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                      APPRECIATING LINDSEY GRAHAM

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, South Carolinians are 
shocked and saddened to hear of the passing of Senator Lindsey Graham.
  Senator Graham enthusiastically served the people of the Palmetto 
State and America as a public servant and veteran. As a patriot, 
tireless for peace through strength, he successfully challenged 
totalitarians.
  President Donald Trump memorialized Graham as one of the greatest 
people and Senators he has ever known.
  The New York Post analyzed that ``all Americans of good will should 
be inspired to keep up his lifelong fights for freedom, decency, and 
justice.''
  The Wall Street Journal editorialized that the death of Lindsey 
Graham ``is a loss for the country and the cause of global freedom.''
  All three generations of the Wilson family extend deepest sympathy 
with our thoughts and prayers for the family led by his sister, Darline 
Graham Nordone, of Lexington.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops. Trump is reinstituting peace 
through strength, revealing war criminal Putin lies, and insulting 
Trump and mocking Trump, which inspired Senator Graham to successfully 
achieve legislative sanctioning of war criminal Putin.

                          ____________________
```
