# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1424

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, I move that the House do 
now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 7 o'clock and 14 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 1424, 
the House adjourned until tomorrow, Tuesday, July 14, 2026, at 10 a.m., 
for morning-hour debate and noon for legislative business, as a further 
mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable Senator Lindsey 
Graham.
```
