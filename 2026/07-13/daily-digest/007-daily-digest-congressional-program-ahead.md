                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of July 14 through July 17, 2026

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of the nomination of 
Matthew A. Schwartz, of New York, to be United States Circuit Judge for 
the Second Circuit, post-cloture, and vote on confirmation thereon at 
12:00 noon.
  At 2:30 p.m., Senate will swear-in Senator-designate Darline Graham, 
of South Carolina.

  Following which, Senate will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on 
the motion to proceed to consideration of S. 4784, National Defense 
Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2027.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
