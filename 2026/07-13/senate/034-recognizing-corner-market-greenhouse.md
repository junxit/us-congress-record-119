                 RECOGNIZING CORNER MARKET & GREENHOUSE

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Corner 
Market & Greenhouse of Fayette County, IA, as the Senate Small Business 
of the Week.
  Founded in 1994 by Paul and Linda Manske, Corner Market & Greenhouse 
was established to provide families in Oelwein with a community-focused 
store to meet every gardening need. Carrying seasonal flowers, plants, 
and decor, Paul and Linda aspire to bring life to every Iowan's 
backyard.
  The Manske family relocated to Oelwein in 1989 when Paul's job 
transferred him there. The family began the venture by waking up early 
in the morning and selling goods at farmers' markets. After countless 
early mornings bringing their products to people across the State, the 
Manske family decided to open their own storefront, drawing people to 
them. The family grew the business from a modest farm stand into the 
beloved local institution it is today.
  For over 32 years, Paul and Linda have shown up, demonstrating the 
entrepreneurial drive rooted in Iowa values. Corner Market & Greenhouse 
is one of Paul and Linda's greatest passions, prompting the family to 
joke, it

[[Page S3737]]

is like one of their children. Since its establishment, the whole 
Manske family has contributed to the business, including their sons 
Byron and Travis, and now their grandchildren. The couple hopes their 
business will be passed down through their family for generations to 
come.
  The family has demonstrated true grit and resilience during their 
time as small business owners, exemplified by their family motto ``work 
smarter, not harder.'' A devastating fire in April of 2004 destroyed 
Corner Market & Greenhouse, but the family persevered. They rebuilt the 
business from the ground up and continue modernizing greenhouses by 
staying ahead of agricultural technology, allowing 55,000 plants to be 
transplanted daily. Unlike most establishments, Corner Market & 
Greenhouse's modernized facility is fully open to the public. Paul and 
Linda have grown their business through a successful collaboration with 
other Iowa businesses, while continuing to think of ways to expand 
their own.
  Corner Market & Greenhouse is renowned for ``small town hospitality 
and big city variety,'' with many customers praising the establishment 
for its fair prices, agricultural expertise, and welcoming customer 
service. The business strives to curate unique items for customers and 
has emerged as a must-visit destination, drawing in travelers from 
across the Midwest.
  The Manskes have built a business that generously serves the 
community by hosting student groups for greenhouse tours. Moreover, the 
establishment donated flowers for storefronts in downtown Oelwein when 
the local municipality discontinued supplying decorative plants for the 
town. The establishment is also a longstanding member of the Oelwein 
Chamber and Area Development, OCAD. Over the past three decades, the 
Manske family has continued to better Oelwein and Iowa through their 
innovative approach to their business.
  It is my pleasure to recognize Paul, Linda, Byron, and Travis Manske 
at Corner Market & Greenhouse for their enduring commitment to Fayette 
County. I look forward to their ongoing success and wish them the very 
best in the years ahead.●
