# HONORING SCOTT O'DELL

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
                         HONORING SCOTT O'DELL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 17, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Mr. Scott O'Dell on 
being named New Teacher of the Year by the Michigan Association of 
Career and Technical Education (MI ACTE). This distinguished honor 
reflects his exceptional leadership in the classroom and his steadfast 
commitment to preparing the next generation of aviation mechanics.
  In just over two years at the Northwest Education Services Career 
Tech Center, Mr. O'Dell has quickly established himself as a leader in 
Michigan's Career and Technical Education community. Prior to entering 
the classroom, he worked in the aviation industry training new 
employees. It was there that he discovered his passion for teaching. In 
2023, he became North Ed's first instructor of the Aviation Maintenance 
program, bringing hands-on, real-world experience directly to his 
students.
  His expertise in aviation mechanics fosters a classroom environment 
where theory meets real-world practice, an impact that Assistant 
Superintendent Pat Lamb has noted significantly enhances student 
engagement and success. His dedication helps ensure that Michigan's 
workforce remains strong and that young men and women are well prepared 
for meaningful, high-demand careers.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Mr. Scott O'Dell for 
earning the distinction of New Teacher of the Year from the Michigan 
Association of Career and Technical Education. On behalf of Michigan's 
1st Congressional District, I congratulate him on this outstanding 
achievement and thank him for his commitment to shaping the future of 
our skilled workforce.

                          ____________________
```
