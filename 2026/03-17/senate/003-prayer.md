# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, who knows every one of our deepest desires, even our hurts are 
not hidden from You. We rejoice that we are Your children. Thank You 
for saving us from unseen traps and dangers. Help us to live so that we 
will inspire generations yet to come.
  As Senators do the work of freedom today, may they work with a sense 
of history. Give them the courage to make decisions that will 
strengthen our Nation for the storms ahead.
  Lord, keep them from the pitfalls that nurture divisions and unite 
them in their efforts to find common ground.
  And, Lord, comfort all who mourn throughout our world.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
