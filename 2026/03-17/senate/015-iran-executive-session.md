# Iran (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  Iran

  Mr. President, on Iran, each day brings more evidence that Donald 
Trump has no idea what he is doing in Iran. Yesterday, the President 
effectively admitted his administration badly miscalculated Iran's 
responses to his attacks.
  As he put it:

       They hit Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE, and Kuwait. Nobody 
     expected it. We were shocked. They fought back.

  Comments like that show that Donald Trump is in over his head.
  The old saying goes: If you break it, you buy it.
  Well, Donald Trump, you broke it badly, and now you are trying to get 
out of paying the price.
  The Strait of Hormuz is effectively shut down. Oil prices continue to 
rise. Yesterday, we learned that about 200 American troops have now 
been injured in this conflict. Thirteen servicemembers have made the 
ultimate sacrifice. We pray for them, their families, and for everyone 
in harm's way.

[[Page S1064]]

  But, again, I repeat to Donald Trump: If you break it, you buy it.
  Donald Trump broke it badly, and now he is trying to get out of 
paying the price.
  Donald Trump promised this would be over in a matter of days or 
weeks. Well, we are now in week 3. There is still no sign this war is 
close to ending. In fact, despite severe blows, most reporting suggests 
Iran's regime remains intact and has hardened rather than collapsed.
  This war needs to come to an end. In the meantime, I repeat what I 
said last week: Donald Trump's Cabinet must come before the Senate. We 
need public hearings. We need to hear from Secretary Rubio, 
Secretary Hegseth, and others under oath, and answer tough questions. 
They need to answer some very basic questions: Why are we at war? What 
is the objective? Will there be American boots on the ground? How 
exactly do they plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz? What is the exit 
strategy?

  Right now, we do not have answers to questions that should be easy 
for any competent administration to answer, and that should alarm every 
one of us.
```
