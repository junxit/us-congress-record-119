                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 673, Anna St. John, of Louisiana, 
     to be United States District Judge for the Eastern District 
     of Louisiana.

nomination of Anna St. John, of Louisiana, to be United States District 
Judge for the Eastern District of Louisiana, shall be brought to a 
close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
