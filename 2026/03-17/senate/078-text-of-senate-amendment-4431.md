# Text of Senate Amendment 4431

**Speaking:** Gallego, Ruben (G000574) [D-AZ]

```
  SA 4431. Mr. GALLEGO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory 
Committee on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. REQUIREMENTS RELATING TO MAIL VOTER REGISTRATION.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, the 
     amendments made by subsections (d) and (h) of section 2 shall 
     not take effect until the date on which the national average 
     price of gasoline is less than $3 per gallon.
                                 ______
```
