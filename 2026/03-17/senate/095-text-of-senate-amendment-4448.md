# Text of Senate Amendment 4448

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4448. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee 
on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. DOCUMENTARY PROOF OF UNITED STATES CITIZENSHIP 
                   REQUIRED FOR FIREARM TRANSFERS.

       Section 5812(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is 
     amended by striking ``and his photograph'' and inserting ``, 
     his photograph, and documentary proof of United States 
     citizenship, as defined in section 3 of the National Voter 
     Registration Act of 1993 (52 U.S.C. 20502)''.
```
