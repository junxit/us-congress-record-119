# Text of Senate Amendment 4435

**Speaking:** Gallego, Ruben (G000574) [D-AZ]

```
  SA 4435. Mr. GALLEGO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory 
Committee on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. TERMINATION OF INCREASED TARIFF-RATE QUOTA ON BEEF 
                   IMPORTED FROM ARGENTINA.

       On and after the date of the enactment of this Act--
       (1) the increase in the tariff-rate quota on beef imported 
     from Argentina proclaimed by the President in Proclamation 
     11010 (91 Fed. Reg. 7107) shall have no force or effect; and
       (2) the tariff-rate quota on such beef in effect on 
     February 5, 2026, shall apply.
                                 ______
```
