# Text of Senate Amendment 4413

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4413. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee 
on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. DOCUMENTARY PROOF OF UNITED STATES CITIZENSHIP 
                   REQUIRED FOR ASSAULT WEAPON PURCHASES.

       Section 922 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by 
     adding at the end the following:
       ``(aa) Documentary Proof of United States Citizenship 
     Required for Assault Weapon Purchases.--It shall be unlawful 
     for any person to sell or otherwise dispose of an assault 
     weapon to any individual unless the individual presents 
     documentary proof of United States citizenship, as defined in 
     section 3 of the National Voter Registration Act of 1993 (52 
     U.S.C. 20502).''.
                                 ______
```
