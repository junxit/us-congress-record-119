# Energy Costs (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Ricketts, Pete (R000618) [R-NE], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                              Energy Costs

  Mr. President, finally, on energy costs, later today, Senators 
Heinrich, Whitehouse, and I will release the most comprehensive Senate 
report to date on Donald Trump's broken promise to lower energy costs. 
This is the third major pillar in Democrats' yearlong effort to present 
a lower cost agenda to the American people. Lowering costs will remain 
our North Star all year long.
  Donald Trump might think affordability is a hoax. He might be fixated 
on foreign wars. But Democrats understand that the cost of living is 
the No. 1 issue on people's minds.
  When it comes to energy prices, the story is very simple: Donald 
Trump promised he would cut the price of energy in half in his first 
year. Well, 14 months have passed, and not only are energy prices up, 
they are rising roughly three times the rate of inflation, and that is 
not sustainable.
  Our report explains in detail why this is happening. Donald Trump 
chose to help his friends in Big Oil out instead of helping American 
families lower their energy bills. He has waged war on clean energy--
the cheapest and fastest source to build and get electrons on the 
grid--and now Americans are paying the price.
  He cut clean energy because many contributors--Harold Hamm--hate 
clean energy because they know it is the future, not the oil and gas 
that they claim should be the only route to go. When he was running for 
office, Donald Trump promised oil executives at Mar-a-Lago--Harold Hamm 
among them--that if they gave him $1 billion for his election, he would 
give them a great deal in return. Now he is making good on that 
bargain.
  His so-called Big Beautiful Bill has destroyed clean energy jobs 
across the country and raised taxes on clean energy. He has weaponized 
the Federal Government to undermine 35 clean energy projects that could 
have powered 14 million homes and supported 170,000 jobs. At the same 
time, Trump's war with Iran has pushed up the cost of gasoline and 
diesel. His tariffs have raised the cost of material needed to produce 
energy here at home.
  So after more than a year, the pattern is very clear: Donald Trump 
has systemically destroyed investments in energy production, favored 
Big Oil over working families, and raised costs on households by 
killing the cheapest and fastest sources of new energy--solar, wind, 
and battery storage.
  The American people are paying the price for Trump's broken promises.
  I thank Senators Heinrich and Whitehouse for their tremendous work on 
this report. We will have more to say later today and in the weeks 
ahead.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Nebraska.
  Mr. RICKETTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for up 
to 5 minutes prior to the scheduled rollcall vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
