# SAVE America Act (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                            SAVE America Act

  Mr. President, today, we are kicking off an extended debate on the 
SAVE America Act, a package of commonsense measures united around two 
themes: protecting our elections and protecting our youth.
  You are going to hear me use the adjective ``commonsense'' a lot in 
this debate because if there is any word that is suitable to describe 
the measures we are considering, it is that. If there is anything 
essential to the integrity of elections, it is ensuring that those who 
are registered to vote are eligible to vote and that those who show up 
to vote at polling places are who they say they are.
  And how do you do that? Well, by requiring that Americans show proof 
of citizenship when they register to vote and that they show photo ID 
at polling places. That is what the SAVE America Act would do. It is 
just common sense. And polls show that the American people 
overwhelmingly agree. But to hear Democrats talk, you would think that 
demonstrating citizenship or showing a photo ID is an intolerable 
burden.
  And yet I haven't heard Democrats complaining about the thousand 
other circumstances in which we require photo ID in this country. 
Democrats are aware that Americans have to show photo ID to get on a 
plane--right?--and to start a new job and when they head to the 
doctor's office and to rent a car and to stay in a hotel. Heck, if you 
want to get a library card, you have to show a photo ID. Do Democrats 
remember their 2024 convention, when attendees had to show a photo ID 
to get in? The senior Democrat Senator from Georgia required photo ID 
to get into his recent rally.

  Do Democrats hear themselves? Are they going to start boycotting 
libraries and airplanes and hotels for requiring a photo ID? Are they 
going to explain why they are perfectly justified in requiring photo ID 
for their political rallies while it is somehow an intolerable burden 
to ask people to show their photo ID when they go to vote?
  I expect they won't. They will simply continue with their mindless 
partisan opposition to commonsense ID requirements for Federal 
elections.
  But let's hit pause on that topic for a minute and go to another 
issue where common sense and the Democrat Party have parted ways, and 
that is on the subject of transgender ideology. In their haste to 
embrace medical transgender ideology, Democrats have put themselves in 
the absurd position of supporting the hijacking of women and girls' 
sports by biological males. Yep; that is right. The party that would 
portray itself as a defender of women has put itself on the record as 
willing to sacrifice women's athletic opportunities for the sake of 
biological males.
  Now, no one should need an explanation of what the presence of 
biological males in women's sports costs women. At the most basic 
level, women are losing out on a slot on the team. Every roster place 
that goes to a male is a roster place that doesn't go to a female. And 
for women who are on the roster, well, the presence of a biological 
male can and frequently does rob them of opportunities to excel.
  Biologically, men possess physical advantages when it comes to 
sports--things like greater muscle mass and greater aerobic power--
which means that, by and large, men are likely to dominate in women's 
sports, taking wins and medal opportunities away from women.

[[Page S1061]]

  In an August 2024 United Nations report on violence against women and 
girls, they had this to say about the results of men participating in 
women's sports:

       According to information received, by 30 March 2024, over 
     600 female athletes in more than 400 competitions have lost 
     more than 890 medals in 29 different sports.

  Over 600 female athletes in more than 400 competitions have lost more 
than 800 medals in 29 different sports.
  The SAVE America Act would ensure that men are not allowed to hijack 
women's sporting opportunities. Yet, once again, we expect my Democrat 
colleagues to oppose keeping women's sports for women.
  The idea that a female high school athlete might lose out on a 
college scholarship because her achievements were overshadowed by those 
of a biological male on her team is bad enough, but an even more 
dangerous consequence of the embrace of transgender ideology is the 
children who have become victims of irreversible gender transition 
procedures. And when I say ``children,'' I mean children: young men and 
women who are not old enough to vote, who are years away from being 
able to legally drink, who still need parental permission to take 
medicine at school.
  The New York Times reports:

       A review of hospital data from 2016 through 2020 identified 
     about 3,600 patients aged 12 to 18 who had received gender-
     related surgery.

  Aged 12 to 18. Can the Presiding Officer imagine that? Can the 
Presiding Officer imagine allowing a child not yet out of middle school 
to receive a medical procedure that will result in permanent mutilation 
of part of her body?
  This madness has to end. Multiple European countries have acted to 
rein in gender transition procedures for minors and so have a number of 
U.S. States. The SAVE America Act would extend protection from these 
procedures to all children, whatever State they live in.
  Commonsense measures: require proof of citizenship to register to 
vote, require a photo ID at the polls, protect women's sports from 
being hijacked by biological males, and protect teenagers from life-
altering, irreversible gender transition procedures--measures, I might 
add again, supported by a majority of Americans and measures we expect 
my Democrat colleagues to oppose.
  It is unfortunate that common sense is not very common anymore in the 
Democratic Party.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The majority whip.
```
