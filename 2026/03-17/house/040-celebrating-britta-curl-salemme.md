# CELEBRATING BRITTA CURL-SALEMME

**Speaking:** Fedorchak, Julie (F000482) [R-ND]

```
                    CELEBRATING BRITTA CURL-SALEMME

  (Mrs. FEDORCHAK asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. FEDORCHAK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate a proud 
North Dakotan, Britta Curl-Salemme, and the entire USA women's hockey 
team on their thrilling overtime victory against our neighbors to the 
north.
  North Dakotans know Britta well. She represents the very best of our 
State: hard work, grit, and team spirit. From the rinks of North Dakota 
to the world stage, she has carried those values with pride.
  Britta's dedication and leadership helped deliver a gold medal moment 
our country is still celebrating.
  I recognize the people who helped make this moment possible: her 
parents, Bill and Gretchen, and her siblings who have supported her 
every step of the way. North Dakota could not be more proud of Britta. 
We know she is gold through and through.

                          ____________________
```
