# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 6084, H.R. 7661, H.R. 7890, H.R. 7891, H.R. 7892, H.R. 7893, H.R. 7894

```
Committee Meetings
AMERICAN INDIAN AND ALASKA NATIVE PUBLIC WITNESS DAY (DAY 1, MORNING 
SESSION)
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies held a hearing entitled ``American Indian and Alaska 
Native Public Witness Day (Day 1, Morning Session)''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. CAPITOL POLICE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Legislative Branch held a 
budget hearing on the U.S. Capitol Police. Testimony was heard from 
Michael G. Sullivan, Chief, U.S. Capitol Police.
MEMBER DAY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Legislative Branch held a 
hearing entitled ``Member Day''. Testimony was heard from 
Representative Arrington and public witnesses.
NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Agencies held an oversight hearing on 
the National Institutes of Health. Testimony was heard from Jay 
Bhattacharya, M.D., Director, National Institutes of Health, Department 
of Health and Human Services.
HEARING--AMERICAN INDIAN AND ALASKA NATIVE PUBLIC WITNESS (DAY 1, 
AFTERNOON SESSION)
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies held a hearing entitled ``Hearing--American Indian and 
Alaska Native Public Witness (Day 1, Afternoon Session)''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.

[[Page D284]]


APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Legislative Branch held a 
budget hearing on the U.S. House of Representatives. Testimony was 
heard from the following U.S. House of Representatives officials: Kevin 
F. McCumber, Clerk; William P. McFarland, Sergeant at Arms; Anne M. 
Dressendorfer Binsted, Acting Chief Administrative Officer; Matthew 
Berry, General Counsel; Christen Stevenson, Inspector General; Brian 
Lindsey, Law Revision Counsel; and Warren Burke, Legislative Counsel.
U.S. MILITARY POSTURE AND NATIONAL SECURITY CHALLENGES IN NORTH AND 
SOUTH AMERICA
Committee on Armed Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``U.S. Military Posture and National Security Challenges in North and 
South America''. Testimony was heard from the following Department of 
Defense officials: General Francis Donovan, Commander, U.S. Southern 
Command; General Gregory Guillot, Commander, U.S. Northern Command and 
North American Aerospace Defense Command; and Joseph Humire, Performing 
the Duties of Assistant Secretary of Defense for Homeland Defense and 
Americas Security Affairs, Office of the Secretary of Defense.
FISCAL YEAR 27 STRATEGIC FORCES POSTURE HEARING
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces held a 
hearing entitled ``FY27 Strategic Forces Posture Hearing''. Testimony 
was heard from the following Department of Defense officials: Marc 
Berkowitz, Assistant Secretary of Defense for Space Policy, Office of 
the Secretary of Defense; Admiral Richard Correll, Commander, U.S. 
Strategic Command; General Gregory Guillot, Commander, U.S. Northern 
Command and North American Aerospace Defense Command; Robert Kadlec, 
Assistant Secretary of Defense for Nuclear Deterrence, Chemical, and 
Biological Defense, Office of the Secretary of Defense; and General 
Stephen Whiting, Commander, U.S. Space Command.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Education and Workforce: Full Committee held a markup on 
H.R. 7894, the ``Truman Scholarship Clean House Act''; H.R. 7890, the 
``Science of Reading Act of 2026''; H.R. 7891, the ``Student Aid Fraud 
Oversight and Accountability Act of 2026''; H.R. 7892, the ``No Aid for 
Ghost Students Act of 2026''; H.R. 7893, the ``FAFSA Verification 
Efficiency Act''; H.R. 7661, the ``Stop the Sexualization of Children 
Act''; and H.R. 6084, the ``ERISA Litigation Reform Act''. H.R. 7894, 
H.R. 7890, H.R. 7891, H.R. 7892, H.R. 7893, H.R. 7661, and H.R. 6084 
were ordered reported, as amended.
WINTER STORM FERN LESSONS: SUPPLYING RELIABLE POWER TO MEET PEAK DEMAND
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Energy held a hearing 
entitled ``Winter Storm Fern Lessons: Supplying Reliable Power to Meet 
Peak Demand''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
PROTECTING PATIENTS AND SAFEGUARDING TAXPAYER DOLLARS: THE ROLE OF 
CENTERS FOR MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES IN COMBATTING MEDICARE AND 
MEDICAID FRAUD
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a hearing entitled ``Protecting Patients and 
Safeguarding Taxpayer Dollars: The Role of CMS in Combatting Medicare 
and Medicaid Fraud''. Testimony was heard from Kimberly Brandt, Deputy 
Administrator and Chief Operating Officer, U.S. Centers for Medicare 
and Medicaid Services, Department of Health and Human Services.
UPDATING AMERICA'S FINANCIAL PRIVACY FRAMEWORK FOR THE 21ST CENTURY
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Updating America's Financial Privacy Framework for the 21st 
Century''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
REFORMING AMERICA'S DEFENSE SALES
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Reforming America's Defense Sales''. Testimony was heard from Stanley 
L. Brown, Senior Bureau Official, Bureau of Political-Military Affairs, 
Department of State; Michael P. Duffy, Under Secretary of War for 
Acquisition and Distrainment, Department of War; and Michael F. Miller, 
Director, Defense Security Cooperation Agency, Department of Defense.
WASTE, FRAUD, AND ABUSE IN FOREIGN ASSISTANCE: LESSONS LEARNED AND 
CHARTING A PATH FORWARD
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Oversight and Intelligence Subcommittee 
held a hearing entitled ``Waste, Fraud, and Abuse in Foreign 
Assistance: Lessons Learned and Charting a Path Forward''. Testimony 
was heard from Adam Kaplan, Deputy Inspector General, Office of the 
Inspector General, U.S. Agency for International Development; and

[[Page D285]]

Elates Love-Grayer, Director, International Affairs and Trade, 
Government Accountability Office.
DEEPSEEK AND UNITREE ROBOTICS: EXAMINING THE NATIONAL SECURITY RISKS OF 
PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, ROBOTICS, AND 
AUTONOMOUS TECHNOLOGIES AND BUILDING A SECURE U.S. TECHNOLOGY BASE
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Cybersecurity and 
Infrastructure Protection held a hearing entitled ``DeepSeek and 
Unitree Robotics: Examining the National Security Risks of PRC 
Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and Autonomous Technologies and 
Building a Secure U.S. Technology Base''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
PIER PRESSURE: REGULATION AND COMPETITION IN MARITIME SHIPPING
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on the Administrative State, 
Regulatory Reform, and Antitrust held a hearing entitled ``Pier 
Pressure: Regulation and Competition in Maritime Shipping''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
OVERSIGHT OF THE U.S. POSTAL SERVICE: THE FINANCIAL FUTURE UNDER 
POSTMASTER GENERAL STEINER
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on 
Government Operations held a hearing entitled ``Oversight of the U.S. 
Postal Service: The Financial Future Under Postmaster General 
Steiner''. Testimony was heard from David Martini, Director, Physical 
Infrastructure, U.S. Government Accountability Office; and David 
Steiner, Postmaster General, U.S. Postal Service.
FORCE DESIGN OR FORCE IN DECLINE: REVIEWING READINESS
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Subcommittee on Coast 
Guard and Maritime Transportation held a hearing entitled ``Force 
Design or Force in Decline: Reviewing Readiness''. Testimony was heard 
from Admiral Thomas G. Allan, Jr., Vice Commandant, U.S. Coast Guard.
ADVANCING AMERICA'S INTERESTS AT THE WORLD TRADE ORGANIZATION'S 14TH 
MINISTERIAL CONFERENCE
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Trade held a hearing 
entitled ``Advancing America's Interests at the World Trade 
Organization's 14th Ministerial Conference''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
   No joint committee meetings were held.
```
