# SENATE RESOLUTION 888--CONGRATULATING THE PASEO VERDE LITTLE LEAGUE BASEBALL TEAM ON WINNING THE 2026 LITTLE LEAGUE WORLD SERIES UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP

**Measures:** S.Res. 888

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 888--CONGRATULATING THE PASEO VERDE LITTLE LEAGUE 
  BASEBALL TEAM ON WINNING THE 2026 LITTLE LEAGUE WORLD SERIES UNITED 
                          STATES CHAMPIONSHIP

  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO (for herself and Ms. Rosen) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 888

       Whereas, on August 29, 2026, the Paseo Verde Little League 
     of Henderson, Nevada, representing the Mountain Region, 
     defeated the Hamilton West Side Little League of Ohio in the 
     United States Championship Game of the 2026 Little League 
     World Series;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League earned its place in 
     the 2026 Little League World Series after winning the Nevada 
     State Championship and advancing through the Mountain Region 
     Tournament undefeated;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League defeated Billings Big 
     Sky Little League of Montana, 9-2, in the Mountain Region 
     Championship Game to earn its second appearance in the Little 
     League World Series in 3 years;
       Whereas, in Williamsport, the Paseo Verde Little League 
     opened its World Series tournament with a dramatic 6-5 
     victory over the Hamilton West Side Little League of Ohio, 
     overcoming a 3-run deficit and winning on a walk-off single 
     by Hudson Cowley;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League continued its 
     remarkable run by defeating the Soundview Little League of 
     Washington, 5-0, advancing further in the tournament;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League then defeated the 
     Davenport Northwest Little League of Iowa, 14-6, in the 
     United States semifinal, exploding for 9 runs in the sixth 
     inning and progressing to the United States Championship Game 
     undefeated;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League again beat the 
     Hamilton West Side Little League of Ohio, 12-2, clinching the 
     United States title for a second straight year for Nevada;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League players each 
     contributed to the team's United States championship and 
     demonstrated exceptional skill, determination, teamwork, and 
     resilience throughout their championship run;
       Whereas manager Cory Edwards, the coaches, volunteers, 
     parents, families, and supporters have all played an 
     essential role in developing young athletes and building a 
     culture of character, confidence, and teamwork within Paseo 
     Verde Little League;
       Whereas the Paseo Verde Little League has become a source 
     of tremendous pride for Henderson, Southern Nevada, and the 
     entire State of Nevada through its outstanding performance on 
     the national stage; and
       Whereas the 2026 Paseo Verde Little League team has 
     inspired young athletes throughout Nevada and demonstrated 
     that hard work, perseverance, and collaboration can carry a 
     community to the highest levels of youth baseball: Now, 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate congratulates and honors the 
     Paseo Verde Little League of Henderson, Nevada, on winning 
     the 2026 Little League World Series United States 
     Championship and commends the players, coaches, families, 
     volunteers, and supporters for their extraordinary 
     achievement.

                          ____________________
```
