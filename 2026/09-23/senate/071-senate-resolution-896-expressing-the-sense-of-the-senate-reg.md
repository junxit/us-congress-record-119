# SENATE RESOLUTION 896--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE REGARDING THE IMPORTANCE OF RELIGIOUS FREEDOM, AUTHORITATIVE TEXTS, AND TRANSPARENCY IN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS

**Measures:** S.Res. 896

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 896--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE REGARDING THE 
IMPORTANCE OF RELIGIOUS FREEDOM, AUTHORITATIVE TEXTS, AND TRANSPARENCY 
                   IN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS

  Mr. LANKFORD (for himself and Mr. Coons) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 896

       Whereas freedom of religion is a fundamental right;
       Whereas the First Amendment to the Constitution of the 
     United States stipulates that ``Congress shall make no law 
     respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the 
     free exercise thereof.'';
       Whereas religious freedom, including the freedom of every 
     person to have a faith, live their faith, change their faith, 
     or have no faith at all, is a bedrock liberty and a central 
     protection secured by the First Amendment to the Constitution 
     of the United States;
       Whereas freedom of religion is a foundational element of 
     democracy, human rights, and the rule of law in the United 
     States and abroad;
       Whereas bias or discrimination against any religious group 
     undermines equal treatment, weakens public trust, and harms a 
     free and just society; and
       Whereas artificial intelligence systems increasingly 
     influence public understanding and democratic discourse, 
     making transparency and the fair treatment of religious 
     viewpoints essential to informed civic engagement: Now, 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) affirms the importance of religious freedom as a 
     bedrock and central constitutional liberty for every person;
       (2) recognizes that bias or discrimination against any 
     religious group, person of faith, person who changes their 
     faith, or person who has no faith at all is harmful to a free 
     and just society;
       (3) encourages all organizations that design, develop, or 
     operate artificial intelligence systems to adopt clear and 
     consistently applied standards that uphold universal 
     principles of religious freedom and transparency;
       (4) supports the development and deployment of artificial 
     intelligence systems that provide responses to religious 
     questions that are thorough and fairly representative of 
     relevant beliefs, texts, traditions, and perspectives; and
       (5) commends the people of the United States to consult 
     primary sources and religious leaders when seeking to 
     understand religious beliefs and traditions, including sacred 
     texts and other authoritative writings of those traditions.
```
