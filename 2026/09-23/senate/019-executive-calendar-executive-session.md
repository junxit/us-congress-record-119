# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Ms. LUMMIS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: 
Calendar No. 918 and Calendar No. 919 and only PN769-2 Wade E. Arnold 
on the Secretary's desk in the Coast Guard; that the nominations be 
confirmed, the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions 
be in order to any of the nominations; that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume 
legislative session.

  The nominations considered and confirmed are as follows:
```
