# RECOGNIZING ALLEGHENY COUNTY AIRPORT

**Speaking:** McCormick, David (M001243) [R-PA]

```
                  RECOGNIZING ALLEGHENY COUNTY AIRPORT

● Mr. McCORMICK. Madam President, I rise today to honor 
Allegheny County Airport, known to Pennsylvanians as AGC, as it 
celebrates 95 years of service to the National Airspace System, to 
southwestern Pennsylvania, and to our Nation.
  Ninety-five years ago, on September 11, 1931, Allegheny County 
Airport opened as the Nation's third-largest commercial airport and one 
of the first modern air facilities built with a paved runway system. 
That bold beginning set the tone for nearly a century of leadership in 
American aviation.
  Today, AGC continues to serve as a premier gateway for business and 
general aviation. As an FAA-designated General Aviation Reliever 
Airport within the National Plan of Integrated Airport Systems, it 
preserves critical airspace efficiency for Pittsburgh International 
Airport while supporting the economic vitality of the region, including 
during major national events such as the 2025 U.S. Open and the 2026 
NFL Draft.
  AGC's role extends well beyond commerce. Nearly 20 percent of all 
flights at the airport are dedicated to lifesaving patient care through 
STAT MedEvac operations, making it an indispensable partner in 
emergency air medical logistics and time-sensitive organ transport 
across the region.
  The airport has also long served as a center of aerospace education 
and workforce development. It is home to the accredited Pittsburgh 
Institute of Aeronautics and an ATP Flight School, both of which 
prepare the next generation of certified technicians and commercial 
pilots to serve our Nation's skies.
  Over the course of its 95-year history, Allegheny County Airport has 
facilitated countless critical VIP and executive movements, including 
historic visits by Presidents Harry S. Truman, Jimmy Carter, George W. 
Bush, Barack Obama, Donald Trump, and Joe Biden.
  On behalf of the people of Pennsylvania, I congratulate Allegheny 
County Airport on this remarkable milestone and thank its leadership, 
employees, and partners for 95 years of service, safety, and 
excellence. I look forward to its continued success for decades to 
come.●
```
