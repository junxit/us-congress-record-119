# RECOGNIZING COPPERHEAD GUN & RANGE

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
                   RECOGNIZING COPPERHEAD GUN & RANGE

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, as a member of the Senate Small 
Business Committee, I seek to recognize outstanding Kentucky small 
businesses that exemplify the American entrepreneurial spirit. It is my 
privilege to recognize Copperhead Gun & Range of Prestonsburg, KY, as a 
Senate Small Business of the Week.
  Founded in 2014 by Bill Conley, Copperhead Gun & Range began in 
Martin, KY, with a mission to provide eastern Kentucky with a locally 
owned source for firearms, ammunition, and shooting accessories while 
promoting responsible firearm ownership. What began as a small firearms 
retailer has since grown into one of the region's premier destinations 
for shooting sports, firearms education, and training.
  The business later transitioned to Brian Nelson, who saw an 
opportunity to expand Copperhead. In 2016, Copperhead opened a larger 
facility in Prestonsburg featuring an indoor shooting range and 
expanded retail operations. Aaron Scott took responsibility for 
managing the new location and overseeing its day-to-day operations, 
beginning nearly a decade of leadership and growth.
  Under Aaron's management, Copperhead expanded its customer base, 
products, and services while developing a reputation for knowledgeable 
service and expertise. In 2025, after years of helping build the 
business, Aaron purchased Copperhead from Brian and became its owner 
and CEO. Today, Copperhead's business has grown to approximately three 
times its earlier size while remaining locally and family owned.
  Copperhead is now a full-service firearms retailer and indoor 
shooting range serving customers throughout eastern Kentucky. In 
addition to firearms, ammunition, optics, accessories, and related 
products, Copperhead emphasizes education and responsible firearm 
ownership. The business provides concealed carry certification classes, 
firearms safety instruction, hunter education, and specialized training 
opportunities.
  Following the devastating floods that struck eastern Kentucky in July 
2022, Copperhead became an official Floyd County donation collection 
site, helping gather essential supplies for families who had lost homes 
and belongings.
  When flooding again struck Floyd County in February 2025, Copperhead 
did more than collect donations. The business provided specialized 
military-style vehicles and personnel to assist the Floyd County 
Sheriff's Office with emergency response efforts. Working alongside 
local first responders, Copperhead's team helped evacuate more than 100 
residents from flooded areas and transported critical emergency 
equipment where conventional vehicles could not safely travel.
  Copperhead's commitment to its customers and community has not gone 
unnoticed. The business has been recognized as ``Best in the 
Mountains'' by the Floyd County Chronicle, reflecting the reputation 
Aaron and his team have built among the people they serve. Small 
businesses like Copperhead Gun & Range demonstrate the important role 
locally owned businesses play in their communities. They create jobs, 
develop expertise, invest locally, and, when their neighbors are in 
need, answer the call.
  Congratulations to Aaron Scott and the entire team at Copperhead Gun 
& Range. I wish them continued success.●
```
