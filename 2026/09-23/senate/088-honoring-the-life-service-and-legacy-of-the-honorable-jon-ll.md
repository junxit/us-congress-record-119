# HONORING THE LIFE, SERVICE, AND LEGACY OF THE HONORABLE JON LLEWELLYN KYL, FORMER SENATOR FOR THE STATE OF ARIZONA

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 897

```
      HONORING THE LIFE, SERVICE, AND LEGACY OF THE HONORABLE JON 
         LLEWELLYN KYL, FORMER SENATOR FOR THE STATE OF ARIZONA

  Ms. LUMMIS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 897, submitted earlier today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:
       A resolution (S. Res. 897) honoring the life, service, and 
     legacy of the Honorable Jon Llewellyn Kyl, former Senator for 
     the State of Arizona.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Ms. LUMMIS. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to, 
the preamble be agreed to, and that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 897) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')
```
