# REMEMBERING MATHIAS TOEBBEN

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
                      REMEMBERING MATHIAS TOEBBEN

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Mathias ``Matth'' Toebben, a respected northern Kentucky 
developer, businessman, community leader, and friend to countless 
people throughout the Commonwealth of Kentucky who passed away on 
August 3, 2026. His passing marks the end of a long and distinguished 
life dedicated to his family, his work, and the continued growth and 
prosperity of the community he called home.
  Throughout his 95 years, Matth demonstrated the values that have long 
defined northern Kentucky: hard work, perseverance, entrepreneurship, 
and a commitment to building something better for the generations that 
would follow.
  As a developer, he understood that building a community meant far 
more than constructing buildings or developing property. It meant 
creating places where families could live, businesses could grow, and 
future generations could prosper. His vision and investment helped 
contribute to the growth and transformation of northern Kentucky over 
the course of many decades.
  For those fortunate enough to know Matth, he will be remembered as a 
man of character and determination--a man who understood the value of a 
handshake, the importance of keeping one's word, and the responsibility 
that comes with success. His life serves as an example of what can be 
accomplished through vision, hard work, and an unwavering commitment to 
one's community.
  Northern Kentucky today is a thriving region with a growing economy 
and a strong sense of community. Matth played an important role in 
making that possible. His contributions are woven into the story of 
northern Kentucky's development, and his influence will continue to be 
felt long after his passing.

[[Page S4917]]

  At the age of 95, Matth leaves behind a legacy measured not only in 
what he built, but in the people and communities that were strengthened 
by his life's work. His passing is a profound loss to his family, his 
friends, his colleagues, and the many people throughout northern 
Kentucky who had the privilege of knowing him.
  I extend my deepest condolences to the Toebben family. May they find 
comfort in the memories of a life well lived and in knowing that 
Mathias' contributions to northern Kentucky will endure for generations 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
