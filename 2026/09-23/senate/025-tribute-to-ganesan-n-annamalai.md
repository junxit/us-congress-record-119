# TRIBUTE TO GANESAN N. ANNAMALAI

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO GANESAN N. ANNAMALAI

  Mrs. SHAHEEN. Madam President, I rise today to honor Mr. Ganesan N. 
Annamalai and recognize his contributions to our country as an 
exceptional public servant in the Department of the Air Force.
  As an Air Force Senate Liaison Officer from October 2023 to August 
2026, Ganesan performed his duties with distinction, supporting the 
118th and 119th

[[Page S4914]]

U.S. Congresses. With roots in both Nashua, NH, and Austin, TX, Ganesan 
is a graduate of George Washington University and Johns Hopkins 
University and has served as a civilian in the Department of the Air 
Force for more than 8 years. He has demonstrated exceptional and 
unrivaled leadership in service to the U.S. Government throughout his 
career.
  In his role as Air Force liaison officer to the Senate, Ganesan 
advised Department of the Air Force senior leaders and helped develop 
strategic engagement opportunities to advance U.S. Air Force and U.S. 
Space Force priorities. He facilitated seamless collaboration on behalf 
of the Department of the Air Force across 62 congressional offices, 
serving as the principal Air Force liaison to 34 Senators and their 
respective staff. Ganesan supported delegations for Members of Congress 
and staffers to showcase Air Force equities in the United States and 
abroad, including to the Reagan National Defense Forum and the Paris 
Air Show. Through these interactions, he strengthened international 
collaboration and cultivated relationships crucial for advancing shared 
defense priorities. Most notably, Ganesan's strategic support was vital 
to the successful execution of more than 280 engagements for senior 
Department of the Air Force leadership. This included coordinating 43 
posture hearings and managing the critical confirmation activities for 
the 27th Secretary of the Air Force, the 24th Chief of Staff of the Air 
Force, and the 3rd Chief of Space Operations. Ganesan's direct 
involvement and stewardship enabled Members of Congress to make 
informed decisions about Air Force oversight, funding and resources.
  Ganesan's leadership and composure were instrumental during his 
detail to the White House Situation Room as a duty officer from 
September 2024 through August 2025. He meticulously documented 
developments during the initial hours of emerging crises, providing 
real-time analysis directly to the President and other senior 
government officials. He led his team in convening urgent calls and 
providing robust intelligence to the Nation's most senior officials, 
exhibiting remarkable poise under extreme pressure. His efforts were 
central to supporting the National Security Council across two 
administrations, directly enabling the execution of 496 high-level 
phone calls, 23 official trips, and 29 critical policy meetings.
  I ask my colleagues and all Americans to join me in recognizing my 
fellow Granite Stater Ganesan Annamalai for his many contributions to 
the safety and security of our great country. We wish him all the best 
in the next chapter of his life.

                          ____________________
```
