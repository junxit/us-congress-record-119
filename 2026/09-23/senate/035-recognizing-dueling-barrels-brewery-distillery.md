# RECOGNIZING DUELING BARRELS BREWERY & DISTILLERY

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
            RECOGNIZING DUELING BARRELS BREWERY & DISTILLERY

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, as a member of the Senate Small 
Business Committee, I seek to recognize outstanding Kentucky small 
businesses that exemplify the American entrepreneurial spirit. 
Therefore, it is my privilege to recognize Dueling Barrels Brewery & 
Distillery of Pikeville, KY, as a Senate Small Business of the Week.
  Located in the heart of downtown Pikeville, Dueling Barrels Brewery & 
Distillery was founded through the vision of the late Dr. Pearse Lyons 
and his wife Deirdre Lyons. Natives of Ireland, the Lyonses saw 
similarities between the mountains and people of their homeland and 
those of eastern Kentucky. Their appreciation for the region inspired 
an investment in downtown Pikeville intended to create jobs, attract 
visitors, and celebrate Appalachian culture through Kentucky's 
longstanding traditions of brewing and distilling. Although Dr. Lyons 
passed away before Dueling Barrels opened its doors in 2018, the 
business became an important part of downtown Pikeville and the 
region's growing tourism economy.
  In 2025, Kendall and Shannon Wright, owners of Brothers Wright 
Distilling Company, acquired Dueling Barrels. Already established in 
Kentucky's bourbon industry, the Wrights saw an opportunity to preserve 
what had been created in Pikeville while building upon it. Since 
acquiring the business, they have expanded its offerings, added a stage 
showcasing local musicians, enhanced the kitchen and food menu, and 
renovated the upstairs into a private event venue.
  Today, Dueling Barrels combines handcrafted spirits and locally 
brewed beer with a full-service restaurant, live entertainment, events, 
and Kentucky hospitality. It draws visitors to Pikeville while 
providing residents with a gathering place in the heart of their 
community.
  The Wrights' commitment to eastern Kentucky extends beyond Dueling 
Barrels. Brothers Wright Distilling is bringing together two industries 
deeply connected to Kentucky's identity: coal and bourbon. The company 
ages bourbon underground in a former eastern Kentucky coal mine, where 
the naturally stable environment provides distinctive conditions for 
barrel aging. The Wrights are working to transform the abandoned mine 
into a tourist destination where visitors can experience Appalachia's 
coal history while learning about Kentucky bourbon.
  Their work has earned recognition within the craft spirits industry. 
Brothers Wright Distilling's Mary's Coal Mine Moonshine received a 
silver medal at the 2024 American Craft Spirits Association Judging of 
American Craft Spirits. They are also a craft member of the Kentucky 
Distillers' Association.
  Small businesses like Dueling Barrels Brewery & Distillery embody the 
determination, creativity, and community spirit that make Kentucky 
special. Kendall and Shannon Wright are investing in the future of 
eastern Kentucky, supporting their neighbors, and finding innovative 
ways to celebrate Appalachia's heritage while creating new economic 
opportunities.
  Congratulations to the entire team at Dueling Barrels Brewery & 
Distillery. I wish them continued success, and I am proud to recognize 
this great Kentucky business.●
```
