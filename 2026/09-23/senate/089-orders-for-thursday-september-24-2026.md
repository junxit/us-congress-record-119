# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S. 4668

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026

  Ms. LUMMIS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10:00 
a.m. on Thursday, September 24; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume 
consideration of the substitute amendment No. 6776 to S. 4668 
postcloture; further, that postcloture time be expired at 10:45 a.m. 
and the Senate vote on adoption of amendment No. 6776.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
