# RECOGNIZING VISIONCORPS

**Speaking:** McCormick, David (M001243) [R-PA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING VISIONCORPS

● Mr. McCORMICK. Madam President, I rise today to honor 
VisionCorps of Lancaster, PA, as it celebrates its 100th anniversary of 
service to individuals who are blind or vision impaired.
  VisionCorps was founded in 1926 by a group of local businessmen 
committed to helping veterans blinded during the First World War, and 
others who were blind, learn a trade. What began as a small workshop 
teaching skills such as basket weaving and chair caning has grown, over 
a century, into a comprehensive rehabilitation and support services 
organization serving thousands of Pennsylvanians.
  Today, VisionCorps provides low vision occupational therapy, safe 
travel training, assistive technology instruction, employment services, 
and independent living support to help individuals who are blind or 
vision impaired overcome barriers and live fuller, more independent 
lives. Serving communities across southeastern and south-central 
Pennsylvania, the organization has also built meaningful employment 
pathways for people who are blind, including through its long-standing 
partnership with the National Industries for the Blind.
  For a century, VisionCorps has stayed true to its founding purpose: 
empowering individuals who are blind or vision impaired to attain 
independence. On behalf of the people of Pennsylvania, I congratulate 
VisionCorps on its 100th anniversary and thank its staff, volunteers, 
and supporters for a century of changing lives.●
```
