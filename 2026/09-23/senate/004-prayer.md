# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, bless our Senators. Give them the strength to do what 
they cannot do on their own and the grace to become what they cannot be 
without You. Lord, guide them toward decisions that advance the common 
good, strengthen our communities, and protect the vulnerable wherever 
they are in the world. May lawmakers encourage one another to move 
toward the shared goal of unity, cooperation, and civility. When the 
way forward is uncertain, give them wisdom. When they are weary, give 
them strength. When they face challenges beyond their abilities, remind 
them that You are their help and refuge.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
