# TRIBUTE TO MONTANA EDUCATORS

**Speaking:** Sheehy, Tim (S001232) [R-MT]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO MONTANA EDUCATORS

● Mr. SHEEHY. Madam President, as the school year gets underway, 
it is my honor to pay tribute to all our Montana educators.
  As we gather in the Senate today, Montana teachers are standing in 
front of classrooms of all sizes, from high school auditoriums in 
Billings to one-room schools serving our most rural communities. They 
show up every day with a profound commitment to their students acting 
as mentors, advisors, and friends who build character and confidence 
from a young age. From Sidney to Hamilton, our educators ensure every 
Montana child receives a quality education.
  A teacher's true success is not measured by headlines; it is found in 
everyday breakthroughs. It happens when a struggling student is 
convinced to stay in school, or when an adult believes in a child for 
the very first time. These victories may often go unseen, but they 
quietly shape the trajectory of a student's life.
  With so much asked of our schools today, it is worth taking a moment 
to

[[Page S4918]]

honor the educators leading Montana's classrooms. Through every 
instruction, kind word, and life lesson, they shape the future leaders 
of our state and country, and they truly deserve our recognition and 
thanks.●

                          ____________________
```
