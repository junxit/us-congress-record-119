# TRIBUTE TO ADMIRAL JENNIFER S. COUTURE

**Speaking:** Kaine, Tim (K000384) [D-VA]

```
                 TRIBUTE TO ADMIRAL JENNIFER S. COUTURE

  Mr. KAINE. Madam President, I rise today to congratulate Rear Admiral 
Jennifer S. Courture on her retirement from the U.S. Navy and to honor 
her for more than 31 years of distinguished service to our Nation.
  Throughout her extraordinary career, Rear Admiral Couture has 
exemplified the highest ideals of leadership, professionalism, and 
public service. A native of Vineland, NJ, who spent much of her naval 
career in Virginia, she was commissioned through the Naval Reserve 
Officers Training Corps program at George Washington University in 1995 
and went on to serve with distinction across multiple sea, shore, and 
flag assignments.
  At sea, Rear Admiral Couture served aboard USS Wasp, USS Saipan, USS 
Elrod, USS Leyte Gulf, and USS Porter. She commanded USS Kauffman and 
Destroyer Squadron 28, deploying twice as Sea Combat Commander with the 
Harry S. Truman Carrier Strike Group, one of the few women to command a 
carrier strike group in Navy history, highlighting her role as a 
trailblazer for women in the armed forces.
  Her leadership style has focused on empowering sailors and fostering 
a culture of excellence within her command. Her operational service 
included support for Operations Joint Endeavor, Active Endeavor, Iraqi 
Freedom, Enduring Freedom, and Inherent Resolve.
  Ashore, she held key leadership and policy roles that strengthened 
the Navy's people, readiness, and operational effectiveness, including 
service with Commander, U.S. Atlantic Fleet; Naval Surface Force 
Atlantic; U.S. Joint Forces Command; the Center for a New American 
Security; and the Office of the Chief of Naval Personnel. Furthermore, 
Rear Admiral Couture completed studies at the U.S. Naval War College 
and the Joint Forces Staff College and earned a master of arts degree 
from Old Dominion University and a doctor of education in 
Organizational leadership from Northeastern University.
  As a flag officer, Rear Admiral Couture continued to lead at the 
highest levels, serving as Commander, Naval Service Training Command; 
Commander, Carrier Strike Group Eleven; and Director, Military 
Personnel Plans and Policy, OPNAV N13. In each role, she advanced the 
development, readiness, and success of sailors and naval forces 
entrusted to her leadership.
  Rear Admiral Couture's career reflects a sustained commitment to 
mission, mentorship, innovation, and the people who make the U.S. Navy 
strong. Her accomplishments--including numerous personal, unit, and 
service awards, among them the Legion of Merit--stand as a testament to 
her devotion to country and to the sailors she has led and inspired.
  As she concludes more than three decades of honorable service, I 
congratulate her on an exceptional naval career and wish her and her 
family fair winds and following seas in retirement.

                          ____________________
```
