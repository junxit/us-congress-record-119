# REMEMBERING JAMES BRUEGGEMANN

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
                     REMEMBERING JAMES BRUEGGEMANN

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, I rise today to honor the life and 
memory of James Edward Joseph Brueggemann, a devoted husband, father, 
grandfather, businessman, and man of deep faith who passed away on 
August 15, 2026.
  James, known affectionately as ``Jim'' to those who knew him, was 
born on February 22, 1960, to Bernard and Rita Brueggemann. He was the 
third of their six children. Jim was raised in a family that instilled 
in him a strong Catholic faith, a profound sense of responsibility, and 
an enduring commitment to family and hard work.
  Those values became hallmarks of Jim's life. He devoted himself to 
his family and to the family business Bavarian Trucking, where he 
carried forward the work ethic and sense of duty that had been passed 
down to him by his parents.
  Jim's life was defined not simply by his work, but by the people he 
loved. He was a devoted husband to his wife Maria, a loving father, and 
a proud grandfather. He is survived by Maria, their 16 children, 46 
grandchildren and counting, and his siblings.
  His faith was central to the way Jim lived his life. Even as his 
health declined, he remained devoted to the Catholic faith. His family 
recounts that shortly before his passing, Jim went to Confession, 
received Holy Communion, the Apostolic Blessing, and Extreme Unction. 
Later, he told his wife, ``I am ready to go.'' Jim passed away the 
following morning, surrounded by his wife, children, siblings, and 
loved ones.
  The story of Jim Brueggemann is ultimately a story of faith, family, 
perseverance, and service. He leaves behind a large and loving family 
whose lives were shaped by his example and whose memories of him will 
endure for generations.
  Northern Kentucky has lost a respected member of its community, but 
Jim's greatest legacy will not be measured by a business or an 
accomplishment. It will be measured by the family he loved, the values 
he lived, the faith he cherished, and the example he provided to those 
who had the privilege of knowing him.
  I extend my deepest condolences to Maria Brueggemann, Jim's children 
and grandchildren, his brothers and sister, and all of his extended 
family and friends.●

                          ____________________
```
