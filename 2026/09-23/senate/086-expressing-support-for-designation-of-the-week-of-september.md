# EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR DESIGNATION OF THE WEEK OF SEPTEMBER 13 THROUGH 19, 2026, AS "NATIONAL ADULT EDUCATION AND FAMILY LITERACY WEEK"

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 863

```
      EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR DESIGNATION OF THE WEEK OF SEPTEMBER 
     13 THROUGH 19, 2026, AS ``NATIONAL ADULT EDUCATION AND FAMILY 
                            LITERACY WEEK''

  Ms. LUMMIS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions be discharged from 
further consideration and the Senate now proceed to S. Res. 863.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:
       A resolution (S. Res. 863) expressing support for 
     designation of the week of September 13 through 19, 2026, as 
     ``National Adult Education and Family Literacy Week''.
  There being no objection, the committee was discharged and the Senate 
proceeded to consider the resolution.
  Ms. LUMMIS. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to, 
the preamble be agreed to, and that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 863) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in the Record of 
September 17, 2026, under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
