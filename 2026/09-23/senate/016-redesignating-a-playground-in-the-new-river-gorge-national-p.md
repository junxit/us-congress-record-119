# REDESIGNATING A PLAYGROUND IN THE NEW RIVER GORGE NATIONAL PARK AND PRESERVE IN THE STATE OF WEST VIRGINIA AS THE "HEARTS OF GOLD PLAYGROUND"

**Speaking:** Justice, James C. (J000312) [R-WV]

**Measures:** S. 2933

```
  REDESIGNATING A PLAYGROUND IN THE NEW RIVER GORGE NATIONAL PARK AND 
    PRESERVE IN THE STATE OF WEST VIRGINIA AS THE ``HEARTS OF GOLD 
                              PLAYGROUND''

  Mr. JUSTICE. Mr. President, you know, we are dealing with some tough 
stuff, aren't we, all over the place. Whether it be the war in Iran or 
whatever it may be, we deal with tough stuff here every day, every 
single day.
  But just imagine this one. Imagine coming home to one of your 
children and knowing that today--today--you got the prognosis and they 
have childhood cancer. It can't get any tougher than that. No matter 
what any of us say, it can't possibly get any tougher than that.
  I would tell you that my bill, S. 2933, with Senator Capito and 
Congressman Miller, that we have all jumped on to try to support in 
every way--it deals about something that is so simple, so, so 
elementary today.
  You know, in my State, we have an unbelievable national park in the 
New River Gorge, and they have a playground there. All we are doing is 
asking just this: that we rename that playground for all those 
children--all those children--that absolutely have cancer and are 
fighting for their lives in every way and all the unbelievable families 
that are really hurting.
  The name we want it to be is ``Hearts of Gold Playground.'' It seems 
so simple, because hearts of gold--think about this: the little gold 
ribbon. It

[[Page S4902]]

stands for something that right now is going on all across this country 
and all across the world.
  This doesn't have anything to do with swings and slides and 
everything; it has to do with just absolutely what should touch all of 
our hearts. Cancer is unthinkable. We have made strides that are off 
the chart, and every day that goes by, we are a day closer to 
absolutely curing cancer forever more and eradicating it from our 
lives. Won't it be an incredible day?
  With all that being said, the great part about this is we don't have 
to have food fights, do we? We don't have to pick sides between 
Republicans and Democrats. We only absolutely need to move forward on 
goodness--real, real, live goodness.
  When I was a Governor not long ago, I had the opportunity in 
September--I think it was on September 13, 2023. I had the opportunity 
to make in West Virginia a proclamation--you know, that was just a 
followup to what we already had--make in West Virginia a Childhood 
Cancer Awareness Month in the month of September.
  You know, we signed into legislation a thing that absolutely had a 
new license plate--that had on this license plate ``West Virginia, Cure 
Childhood Cancer.''
  We have done some good things, but today--today--let's get on with 
unanimous consent to doing something really, really special that maybe 
a few--that maybe a lot will be there and absolutely feel really good 
about the moment we stood up for them in every way.
  God bless all those that are fighting this dreaded disease, all the 
great families, and all those incredible children.
  Mr. President, with that being said, notwithstanding rule XXII, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the immediate 
consideration of Calendar No. 645, S. 2933.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2933) to redesignate a playground in the New 
     River Gorge National Park and Preserve in the State of West 
     Virginia as the ``Hearts of Gold Playground: In Honor of West 
     Virginia Children and Families Impacted by Childhood 
     Cancer''.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. JUSTICE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read 
a third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (S. 2933) was ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, 
was read the third time, and passed as follows:

                                S. 2933

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. REDESIGNATION OF THE ``HEARTS OF GOLD PLAYGROUND: 
                   IN HONOR OF WEST VIRGINIA CHILDREN AND FAMILIES 
                   IMPACTED BY CHILDHOOD CANCER'' IN THE NEW RIVER 
                   GORGE NATIONAL PARK AND PRESERVE, WEST 
                   VIRGINIA.

       (a) Redesignation.--The primary playground located in the 
     Grandview area south of the amphitheater at New River Gorge 
     National Park and Preserve in the State of West Virginia 
     shall be known and designated as the ``Hearts of Gold 
     Playground: In Honor of West Virginia Children and Families 
     Impacted by Childhood Cancer''.
       (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, 
     document, paper, or other record of the United States to the 
     playground referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to 
     be a reference to the ``Hearts of Gold Playground: In Honor 
     of West Virginia Children and Families Impacted by Childhood 
     Cancer''.
  Mr. JUSTICE. How about that? Good stuff.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Tennessee.
```
