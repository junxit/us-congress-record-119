# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 226, S. 1750, S. 3859, S. 4053, S. 5354

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
U.S. CENTRAL COMMAND
Committee on Armed Services: Committee received a closed briefing on an 
update on the United States Central Command area of operations from 
Admiral Charles B. Cooper II, USN, Commander, United States Central 
Command, Department of Defense.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Armed Services: Committee ordered favorably reported 6,859 
military nominations in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, and 
Space Force.
CYBER OPERATIONS
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Cybersecurity received a 
closed briefing on cyber operations and readiness for the second and 
third quarters of Fiscal Year 2026 as well as an update on election 
security from Katherine E. Sutton, Assistant Secretary for Cyber 
Policy, Rear Admiral Ronald A. Foy, USN, Director for Operations, J3, 
and Brigadier General Kayle M. Stevens, USAF, Director for 
Intelligence, J2, both of United States Cyber Command, and Brigadier 
General Mark McGill, USAF, Deputy Director for Global Operations, J3, 
Joint Staff, all of the Department of Defense.
NUCLEAR WASTE MANAGEMENT
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine updating America's nuclear waste management policy, 
after receiving testimony from Lake Barrett, American Nuclear Society 
Nuclear Waste Policy Task Force, Washington, D.C.; Katrina McMurrian, 
Nuclear Waste Strategy Coalition, Franklin, Tennessee; and Kathryn D. 
Huff, University of Wisconsin Madison Department of Nuclear Engineering 
and Engineering Physics, Madison.
WRONGFUL DETENTIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee received a closed briefing on 
combating and deterring wrongful detentions, focusing on an update on 
United States efforts from Dustin Stewart, Deputy Special Presidential 
Envoy for Hostage Affairs, Department of State.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Committee did 
not agree to the request to direct Senate Legal Counsel to file an 
application seeking immunity.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Indian Affairs: Committee ordered favorably reported S. 
5354, to reauthorize the Native American Housing Assistance and Self-
Determination Act of 1996, with an amendment.
LEGISLATION
Committee on Indian Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
S. 1750, to reaffirm the applicability of the Indian Reorganization Act 
to the Poarch Band of Creek Indians, S. 3859, to amend the Catawba 
Indian Tribe of South Carolina Land Claims Settlement Act of 1993 with 
respect to future membership in the Catawba Indian Nation, S. 4053, to 
take certain land in the State of California into trust for the benefit 
of the Pechanga Band of Indians, and H.R. 226, to take certain Federal 
lands in Tennessee into trust for the benefit of the Eastern Band of 
Cherokee Indians, after receiving testimony from Bryan Mercier, 
Director, Bureau of Indian Affairs, Department of the Interior; 
Stephanie Bryan, Poarch Band of Creek Indians, Atmore, Alabama; 
Patricia Leach, Catawba Indian Nation, Rock Hill, South Carolina; Mark 
Macarro, Pechanga Band of Indians, Temecula, California; and Michell 
Hicks, Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians, Cherokee, North Carolina.
WOMEN IN SPORTS
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
standing up for women in sports, focusing on ensuring opportunity, 
fairness and safety for female athletes, after receiving testimony from 
Kimberly Shasby Jones, The Independent Council on Women's Sport, 
Darien, Connecticut; William Bock, III, Kroger Gardis & Regas, LLP, 
Indianapolis, Indiana; and Carly Smolak, San Diego, California.
AI SURVEILLANCE NETWORK
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism 
concluded a hearing to examine flock's nationwide AI surveillance 
network, after receiving testimony from Alasdair Whitney, The Institute 
for Justice, Chicago, Illinois; Ross Teeple, Pinal County Sheriff's 
Office, Arizona; Chad A. Marlow, American Civil Liberties Union, New 
York, New York; Benn Jordan, Atlanta, Georgia; and Lindsey Isaacs, Palm 
Coast, Florida.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of Maria Lopez, of Florida, to be Chairman of 
the Board of Veterans' Appeals, Department of Veterans Affairs, and 
Robert R. Fleck, of Pennsylvania, David M. Jones, of Virginia, Daniel 
F. Rendleman, of Illinois, and James Lewis Quinn, of Virginia, who was 
introduced by

[[Page D932]]

Senator Cassidy, each to be a Judge of the United States Court of 
Appeals for Veterans Claims, after the nominees testified and answered 
questions in their own behalf.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
GENERIC DRUG MANUFACTURING
Special Committee on Aging: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
leveling the playing field, focusing on restoring America's edge in 
generic drug manufacturing, after receiving testimony from Kurt 
Orlofski, PAI Pharma, Parsippany, New Jersey; Christine Baeder, Apotex 
USA and LATAM, Weston, Florida; and Mihir Torsekar, Coalition for a 
Prosperous America, Washington, D.C.
```
