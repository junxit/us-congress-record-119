# HONORING THE SERVICE OF KERSANDRA "SARGE" BROOKS

```
           HONORING THE SERVICE OF KERSANDRA ``SARGE'' BROOKS

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 30, 2026

  Ms. WASSERRMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the service of 
retired Army Sergeant Kersandra Elaine Brooks of Fort Lauderdale, 
Florida.
  Sergeant (E-5) Kersandra Elaine Brooks, also known as ``Sarge,'' 
enlisted in the United States Army in 1979. During her service, she 
provided support for Operation Desert Shield and Operation Desert Storm 
and served with the United States Customs Service on the ``Drug War'' 
team. ``Sarge'' acquired vital skills through military operations 
specialist training, where she was trained in administration, 
telecommunications, signal corps, mortuary services, and ``Top 
Driver.'' She has received numerous awards, including the National 
Defense Service Medal, the Southwest Asia Service Medal, and the Expert 
Shooter Badge with M16 Rifle Bar, a U.S. military marksmanship 
decoration awarded to personnel who achieve the highest level of 
shooting proficiency.
  After valiantly serving in the United States Army for 21 years, 
``Sarge'' was honorably discharged in 2000.
  Sergeant Brooks attended Sheridan Vocational Law Enforcement School 
and earned a bachelor's degree from Broward Community College. She 
remains active in the community, serving and helping others with 
compassion and grace. As a veteran, she works to provide other veterans 
with information and resources to assist them and their families. As a 
40-year breast cancer survivor, she helps to raise awareness and funds 
for a variety of breast cancer and other charitable organizations. 
``Sarge'' has one daughter, Michelle, and three grandchildren: 
Demariyah, Demiyah, and Denorah.
  Truly an inspiration, ``Sarge'' is known for her kind and generous 
spirit and her legacy of service to our country and community. I am 
fortunate and proud to call Kersandra ``Sarge'' Brooks my dear friend, 
and I ask all who know and love ``Sarge'' to join me in wishing her a 
very happy, healthy, and heartfelt 70th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
