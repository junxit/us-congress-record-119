# HONORING BRANDON BRACALE-LLEWELLYN AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR LGBTQ+ PRIDE

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING BRANDON BRACALE-LLEWELLYN AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                        FLORIDA FOR LGBTQ+ PRIDE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 30, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Brandon Bracale-Llewellyn as a 
Distinguished Leader in Central Florida for LGBTQ+ Pride Month. Brandon 
Bracale-Llewellyn is a respected business owner, community leader, and 
advocate whose dedication to Central Florida's LGBTQ+ community has 
made a lasting impact throughout the region. Born in Hershey, 
Pennsylvania, Brandon is the owner of Savoy Orlando, one of Central 
Florida's most prominent LGBTQ+ entertainment venues and a longstanding 
gathering place that has fostered community, inclusion, and support for 
countless individuals over the years.
  Through both his personal philanthropy and leadership as a business 
owner, Brandon has donated significant time, resources, and financial 
support to numerous nonprofit organizations serving the LGBTQ+ 
community and beyond. His commitment to public service extends through 
his volunteer leadership, including serving as president of the Orange 
County Rainbow Democrats and currently serving on the Board of 
Directors for the LGBT+ Center Orlando.
  In recognition of his dedication to advocacy and community 
engagement, Brandon was honored with the prestigious Chase Smith Award 
in 2019, recognizing his outstanding contributions to equality, civic 
involvement, and public service.
  Beyond his professional and community accomplishments, Brandon values 
spending time with family in Pennsylvania and enjoys traveling to 
Mexico with his partner, Alan. His commitment to service, leadership, 
and community building continues to strengthen Central Florida and 
inspire those around him.
```
