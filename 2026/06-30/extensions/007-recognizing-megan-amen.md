# RECOGNIZING MEGAN AMEN

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                         RECOGNIZING MEGAN AMEN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 30, 2026

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Megan Amen, for her service to the country and the 2nd District of 
South Carolina.
  Throughout her four-year time in the office, Megan has been an 
invaluable member of our team, focused on the challenging 
responsibility of keeping my daily and long-term schedule running 
smoothly.
  She also had the added duties of overseeing a legislative portfolio 
covering several important issue areas, including her work in 
supporting Armed Services legislation.
  Megan graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree in dance and political 
science from Rutgers University, then followed up, with a Master of 
Arts degree in international relations from Arizona State University.
  Though a native of Arizona, Megan will always be fondly regarded as 
an honorary South Carolinian for her commitment to SC-02.
  Megan will be missed, but we wish her well in her new duties serving 
with the USO, which is such a valued institution for service members 
and families worldwide.

                          ____________________
```
