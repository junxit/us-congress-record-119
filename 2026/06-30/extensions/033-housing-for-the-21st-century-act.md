**Speaking:** Waters, Maxine (W000187) [D-CA]

  Ms. WATERS. Madam Speaker, the 21st Century Road to Housing Act is 
historic legislation that will improve housing affordability and access 
in this country for millions of Americans. This legislation would not 
have been possible without the contributions of the minority 
professional staff of the Committee, who worked for months with 
advocacy groups, Members of Congress, and other stakeholders on 
drafting and perfecting this legislation. Their staff work was 
essential to the success of this legislation.
