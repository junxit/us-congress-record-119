# CONGRATULATING DR. THOMAS MCKEE ON EARNING THE 2026 YOOPER SUPER OF THE YEAR AWARD

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
CONGRATULATING DR. THOMAS MCKEE ON EARNING THE 2026 YOOPER SUPER OF THE 
                               YEAR AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 30, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Dr. Thomas McKee for 
earning the 2026 Yooper Super of the Year Award, an honor that reflects 
his outstanding leadership of Rudyard Area Schools.
  Each year, this award is presented to a superintendent who 
exemplifies excellence in public education and demonstrates a deep 
commitment to students, educators, and community stakeholders across 
the Upper Peninsula. Nominated by his peers, Dr. McKee was formally 
recognized for his outstanding service and leadership on April 29, 
2026.
  Under his guidance, Rudyard Area Schools have experienced meaningful 
improvements to facilities and infrastructure, reinforcing the strength 
and identity of the district. Supporters of Dr. McKee have highlighted 
how he has transformed the culture of education throughout the 
community through steady leadership, increased expectations, and care 
for students and staff. His leadership has not only strengthened public 
education in Rudyard, but has helped advance priorities for rural 
sustainability, equitable school funding, and special education 
services across the Upper Peninsula.
  Dr. McKee's dedication to public education helped cultivate a school 
system defined by pride, purpose, and positivity. His work stands as a 
model for educational leadership across Michigan and our Nation.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Dr. Thomas McKee on this 
well-deserved honor. On behalf of Michigan's 1st Congressional 
District, I thank him for enduring contributions to the students, 
families, and educators of Rudyard Area Schools and the Upper 
Peninsula.
```
