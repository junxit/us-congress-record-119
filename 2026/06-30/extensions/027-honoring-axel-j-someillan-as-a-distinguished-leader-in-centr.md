# HONORING AXEL J SOMEILLAN AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR LGBTQ+ PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING AXEL J SOMEILLAN AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                         FOR LGBTQ+ PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 30, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Axel J Someillan as a 
Distinguished Leader in Central Florida for LGBTQ+ Pride Month. Axel J 
Someillan is a renowned photographer known for his exceptional ability 
to capture the essence of his subjects through a distinctive and 
evocative style. With a career spanning over several years, Axel has 
established himself as a prominent figure in the world of photography, 
celebrated for his keen eye for detail and his ability to tell 
compelling visual stories.
  Axel's portfolio encompasses a wide range of photographic genres, 
including portraiture, fashion, commercial, and fine art photography. 
His work is characterized by its striking composition, innovative use 
of light and shadow, and a profound understanding of color and texture. 
Whether working with models, celebrities, or everyday individuals, Axel 
brings out the unique personality and emotion of his subjects, creating 
images that resonate deeply with viewers.
  Throughout his career, Axel J Someillan has collaborated with 
prestigious clients and publications, earning acclaim for his 
creativity and professionalism. His photographs have been featured in 
leading magazines, advertising campaigns, and all exhibitions, 
garnering numerous awards and accolades.
  Axel's passion for photography extends beyond his commercial work; he 
is also dedicated to exploring personal projects that push the 
boundaries of the medium. Through these endeavors, he continues to 
evolve as an artist, experimenting with new techniques and concepts to 
expand his artistic vision.
  Committed to sharing his knowledge and inspiring the next generation 
of photographers, Axel frequently conducts workshops and lectures, 
where he offers insights into his creative process and the technical 
aspects of his craft. His contributions to the field of photography 
have made a lasting impact, solidifying his reputation as a visionary 
and influential artist.
```
