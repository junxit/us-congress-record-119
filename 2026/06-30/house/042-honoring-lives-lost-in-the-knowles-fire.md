# HONORING LIVES LOST IN THE KNOWLES FIRE

**Speaking:** Hurd, Jeff (H001100) [R-CO]

```
                HONORING LIVES LOST IN THE KNOWLES FIRE

  (Mr. HURD of Colorado asked and was given permission to address the

[[Page H4335]]

House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor three brave 
wildland firefighters who gave their lives protecting the people of 
Colorado.
  Last Friday, Emily Barker, Nick Hutcherson, and Sydney Watson were 
responding to the Knowles fire in western Colorado as members of the 
Rifle Helitack crew. They answered the call to protect others, and in 
doing so gave their lives.
  Emily was from Clinton Township, Michigan. Nick was from Glendale, 
Arizona. Sydney was from Warrior, Alabama. They came from across our 
country to serve in Colorado on some of the most challenging terrain in 
the American West.
  This is what wildland firefighters do. They go where the need is 
greatest: into the smoke, into the canyons, and into danger so our 
communities can remain safe.
  Today we thank Emily, Nick, and Sydney not only for how they died, 
but for how they lived: with courage, selflessness, and a commitment to 
serving others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to keep their families, crewmates, 
and every firefighter still on the line in our thoughts and prayers.
```
