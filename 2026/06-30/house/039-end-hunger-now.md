# END HUNGER NOW

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
                             END HUNGER NOW

  (Mr. McGOVERN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, WIC provides pregnant and postpartum moms 
and their babies with healthy food. It increases nutrition, reduces 
premature births, and improves access to healthcare for moms and 
babies.
  That is why, Mr. Speaker, I find it unconscionable that Republicans 
in this Chamber passed a bill earlier this month that slashes funding 
for the program by $200 million. The bill takes away money for 
nutritious fruits and vegetables and would force WIC providers to turn 
away eligible families for the first time in 30 years.
  Then, 1 week later, Republicans gave $70 billion to make a slush fund 
for ICE and CBP. When it comes to giving $200 million, that is less 
than 0.1 percent of that number, to hungry kids, Republicans said that 
we ran out of money.
  What the hell is wrong with these people, Mr. Speaker?
  I thought they wanted to make America healthy again. You can't be 
healthy if you are hungry, Mr. Speaker, and the Republican Agriculture 
appropriations bill will make hunger worse.
  I will fight tooth and nail for the Senate to restore the fruit and 
vegetable voucher and reverse these cuts to WIC. Let's work together to 
actually make America healthy and end hunger now.

                          ____________________
```
