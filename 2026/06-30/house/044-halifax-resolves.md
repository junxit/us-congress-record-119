# HALIFAX RESOLVES

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                            HALIFAX RESOLVES

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, before there was the 
Declaration of Independence, there was Halifax, North Carolina.
  More than 250 years ago, 83 delegates gathered in Halifax with the 
courage to take a bold stand. Through the Halifax Resolves, the first 
Colony officially called for independence from Great Britain. That is 
why North Carolina proudly carries the title ``First in Freedom.''
  The journey of American independence traveled through eastern North 
Carolina, where brave individuals believed freedom was worth the risk. 
Their courage helped change the course of history.
  As America prepares to celebrate 250 years, let's remember that every 
generation has a responsibility to protect the freedoms they fought to 
secure.
  May God bless our wonderful country, the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
