# HONORING ROBERT STEPHENSON

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
                       HONORING ROBERT STEPHENSON

  (Mr. FITZPATRICK asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Robert 
Stephenson, a proud member of the Greatest Generation, as he celebrated 
his 101st birthday on June 14th, 2026.
  Born in Chicago in 1925, Robert came of age during the Great 
Depression, developing the values of hard work and perseverance that 
would guide his life.
  At 17 years old, he enlisted in the United States Navy during World 
War II, where he helped manage personnel and aerial mapping records.
  Following his military service, Robert studied accounting at the 
University of Pennsylvania. In 1964, he founded R.L. Stephenson, 
Incorporated.
  He shared 75 years of marriage with his wife, Helene. He remains as 
active today, at 101 years old, as he ever has been.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in wishing Robert 
Stephenson a very happy 101st birthday and in honoring his century plus 
of service to the American spirit that he embodies.
```
