# RECOGNIZING SABRINA ANAYA

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Adelita S. (G000606) [D-AZ]

```
                       RECOGNIZING SABRINA ANAYA

  (Mrs. GRIJALVA asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
extraordinary young woman from Yuma, Arizona, Kofa High School senior 
Sabrina Anaya.
  Sabrina distinguished herself both in the classroom and on the field, 
earning the title of Kofa High School valedictorian while being named 
Yuma's Female Athlete of the Year.
  A remarkable three-sport athlete, Sabrina excelled in cross-country, 
soccer, and track and field. She was named the Yuma Sun Girls Cross-
Country Runner of the Year, earned Defensive Player of the Year honors 
in soccer, and dominated distance events in track, winning the 800-
meter, 1600-meter, and 3200-meter races.
  However, what makes Sabrina's accomplishments so impressive is not 
just her athletic talent or academic excellence. It is her dedication, 
discipline, and determination to succeed in every area. She has shown 
what is possible through hard work, perseverance, and a commitment to 
excellence.
  Sabrina represents the very best of Yuma and Arizona. I congratulate 
her on this well-deserved recognition and wish her continued success at 
Arizona State University and in her studies in electrical engineering.
  To Sabrina, I say: ``Congratulations''; ``felicidades.''

                          ____________________
```
