# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, whose mercy is great unto the heavens, help us to do 
what is right. May we not forget that You are the judge of the Earth 
and that we are accountable to You. Lord, protect us from our enemies 
for we place our trust in You.
  Sustain our Senators. Surround them with the shield of Your divine 
favor as they seek to live for Your glory. Hasten the day when Your 
just rule shall fill the Earth with health, life, and peace.
  Be exalted, O God, above the heavens. Let Your glory be over the 
Earth.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
