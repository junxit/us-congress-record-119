                            MORNING BUSINESS

                          EXECUTIVE RESOLUTION

  Mrs. SHAHEEN. Mr. President, while I voted no on S. Res. 690, which 
calls for an up or down vote on 49 nominations, en bloc, I would have 
voted yes on the following nominations if I had the opportunity to vote 
on each nomination separately:

       1. Cal. #615--Andrew Benson, of Maine, to be United States 
     Attorney for the District of Maine for the term of four 
     years.
