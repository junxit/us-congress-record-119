                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                  Week of May 19 through May 22, 2026

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will resume consideration of the nomination of 
Sheria Akins Clarke, of South Carolina, to be United States District 
Judge for the District of South Carolina, and vote on the motion to 
invoke cloture thereon at 11:45 a.m. If cloture is invoked on the 
nomination, Senate will vote on confirmation thereon at 2:15 p.m.
  Following disposition of the nomination of Sheria Akins Clarke, 
Senate will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of 
Evan Rikhye, of the Virgin Islands, to be Judge for the District Court 
of the Virgin Islands for a term of ten years.
  Senate expects to vote on or in relation to S. J. Res. 185, 
Hostilities Within or Against the Islamic Republic of Iran during 
Tuesday's session of the Senate.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
