# HONORING THE MEMORY OF KERRY ALLEN McDANIEL

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
              HONORING THE MEMORY OF KERRY ALLEN McDANIEL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 26, 2026

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of Kerry 
Allen McDaniel of Horse Cave, Kentucky.
  Kerry Allen McDaniel dedicated his life to public service and the 
betterment of the Commonwealth of Kentucky through a distinguished 
career spanning decades. For 27 years, he served with the Kentucky 
Department of Environmental Protection, where he played an integral 
role in advancing and enforcing regulations across air, waste, and 
water systems. His breadth of expertise and commitment to protecting 
Kentucky's natural resources made him a respected leader in his field 
and a trusted steward of the environment.
  Following his retirement from state service, Mr. McDaniel continued 
his commitment to his community by serving as the Emergency Management 
Director and Solid Waste Coordinator for Hart County. In these roles, 
he worked tirelessly to ensure the safety, preparedness, and 
environmental well-being of local residents. During the early days of 
the COVID-19 pandemic, Kerry was among the first frontline workers in 
Hart County and the surrounding region, working tirelessly to secure 
and distribute critical personal protective equipment and ensuring the 
community remained as safe and protected as possible. Known for his 
generosity, leadership, and tireless work ethic, Kerry McDaniel leaves 
behind a lasting legacy defined by service to others and a steadfast 
commitment to making Kentucky a better place for future generations.
```
