# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Rachel Landers Vaagenes, Capitol Hill Presbyterian Church, 
Washington, D.C. offered the following prayer:
  Our dear Lord God, we pause this week to remember. All faiths call 
the faithful to look back and remember, in order to give us a vision of 
what can be. We are called to remember who we are, to remember whose we 
are; to remember our various calling; and to remember those whom we are 
connected to in this life on Earth.
  And so as servant leaders of this United States, we unplug and 
disengage from the busyness that is always demanding and distracting 
and dividing, and we take time to remember. To remember the words of 
our Founders calling out to us from above, inscribed on the ceiling of 
this very Chamber: ``E Pluribus unum''; ``Out of many, one.''
  May this moment of remembrance and prayer be a time where we honor 
the many who sacrificed themselves for the many. And may this moment 
inspire us to seek a future of selflessness, and a vision of prosperity 
and liberty that is wedded to the prosperity and liberty of the many. 
May our work be toward a unity that remembers the many, challenging as 
that may be. With the past, present, and future in our hearts, we lift 
up this prayer in hope.
  May it be so.
  Amen.
```
