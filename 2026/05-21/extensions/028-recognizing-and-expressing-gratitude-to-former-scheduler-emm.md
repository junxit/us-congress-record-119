# RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO FORMER SCHEDULER EMMA WILSON

**Speaking:** Jackson, Ronny (J000304) [R-TX]

```
  RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO FORMER SCHEDULER EMMA WILSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RONNY JACKSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. JACKSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
express my sincere gratitude to my former Scheduler, Emma Wilson of 
Bakersfield, California, for her dedicated service and steadfast 
commitment to the people of Texas' 13th Congressional District. In a 
role that demands precision, poise, and tireless attention to detail, 
Emma ensured that our office operated efficiently and effectively each 
and every day. Her professionalism and organizational excellence played 
a vital role in supporting my ability to represent and serve my 
constituents.
  Emma is a distinguished graduate of Auburn University's Harbert 
College of Business, where she majored in Finance and Marketing. A 
member of the Honors College, she earned Dean's List recognition every 
semester and was the recipient of numerous academic scholarships for 
her excellence, leadership, and character.
  Emma discovered her passion for public service during her internship 
in the personal office of House Armed Services Committee Chairman Mike 
Rogers. During her time on Capitol Hill, she developed a deep 
appreciation for the legislative process and serving the American 
people. That passion ultimately led her back to Washington, D.C., where 
she joined my team.
  As the Scheduler in my office, Emma managed a complex schedule with 
remarkable efficiency and poise. She balanced the priorities of the 
district, stakeholder meetings, committee responsibilities, and 
constituent engagements with steady professionalism. Her thoughtful 
precision, strong communication skills, and unwavering positive 
attitude made her an indispensable member of Team Jackson.
  Today, Emma continues her public service as a Scheduling Coordinator 
in the Executive Office of President Donald J. Trump. I am confident 
that her strong work ethic, careful attention to detail, and dedication 
to service have continued to make her an invaluable asset for the 
President.
  While Emma has moved into a new role, her impact on my office and on 
the people of Texas' 13th Congressional District continues to be felt 
in the work we do every day. On behalf of myself, my staff, and the 
constituents I proudly serve, I want to thank Emma Wilson for her loyal 
service, her commitment to excellence, and her patriotic spirit. I wish 
her the very best as she continues her journey in public service.

                          ____________________
```
