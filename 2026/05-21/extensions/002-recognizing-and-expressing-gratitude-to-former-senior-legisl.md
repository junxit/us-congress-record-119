# RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO FORMER SENIOR LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT KATIE WALLACH

**Speaking:** Jackson, Ronny (J000304) [R-TX]

```
   RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO FORMER SENIOR LEGISLATIVE 
                        ASSISTANT KATIE WALLACH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RONNY JACKSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. JACKSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
express my sincere gratitude to my former Senior Legislative Assistant, 
Katie Wallach of Irving, Texas, for her exceptional service, steadfast 
dedication, and unwavering commitment to the people of Texas' 13th 
Congressional District. From the earliest days of my tenure in 
Congress, Katie has been a cornerstone of my team. Her sharp policy 
insight, institutional knowledge, and tireless work ethic helped shape 
my legislative strategy and ensured that my constituents received the 
strong, principled representation they deserve.
  Katie's commitment to public service began long before she joined my 
office. She pursued her passion for government at the University of 
Dallas, where she majored in Politics. Throughout college, she 
strengthened her experience in politics serving in both congressional 
and state government offices, including with Representative Pete 
Sessions, the Texas General Land Office, and numerous campaigns always 
demonstrating initiative, professionalism, and a deep respect for the 
institutions she served.
  In May 2021, Katie joined my office as a Staff Assistant. It did not 
take long for her talent and determination to stand out. Through earned 
promotions, she advanced from Legislative Correspondent to Legislative 
Aide, Legislative Assistant, and ultimately Senior Legislative 
Assistant.
  As Senior Legislative Assistant, Katie led my policy efforts on the 
House Foreign Affairs Committee. Her strategic insight and procedural 
expertise were instrumental in advancing my legislative priorities. 
Most notably, she played a critical role in helping secure the passage 
and enactment of my first bill being signed into law--the Maintaining 
American Superiority by Improving Export Control Transparency Act. This 
achievement stands as a testament to her diligence, persistence, and 
command of the legislative process. Her ability to navigate both the 
substance and the strategy of Congress made her an indispensable member 
of Team Jackson.
  Today, Katie continues her distinguished service as a Special 
Assistant to the Assistant Secretary of War for Legislative Affairs. In 
this role, she will continue supporting our warfighters, strengthening 
our national defense, and advancing policies that put America First.
  Although Katie has moved into a new role, her impact on my office and 
on the people of Texas' 13th Congressional District will be felt for 
years to come. On behalf of myself, my staff, and the constituents I 
proudly serve, I want to thank Katie Wallach for her years of loyal 
service, her commitment to excellence, and her unwavering patriotic 
spirit. I wish her nothing but success as she continues serving the 
American people.

                          ____________________
```
