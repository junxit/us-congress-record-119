# RECOGNIZING JASON AND ALEX RHEINWALD

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                  RECOGNIZING JASON AND ALEX RHEINWALD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Jewish American 
Heritage Month to recognize two outstanding community leaders and 
preservers of Rochester's Jewish culinary heritage: Jason and Alex 
Rheinwald.
  Jason and Alex Rheinwald are the co-owners of Fox's Deli, a nearly 
century-old Rochester institution that has long served as a cornerstone 
of the region's Jewish community and cultural life. After taking over 
the deli several years ago, the brothers committed themselves to 
preserving its traditional Jewish deli identity while thoughtfully 
modernizing and expanding the business for a new generation.
  Under their leadership, Fox's Deli has continued to serve as a 
gathering place for Rochester families and a living connection to the 
city's historic Jewish community. Their stewardship of this important 
institution earned Fox's recognition by New York State's Historic 
Business Preservation Registry, reflecting both the deli's cultural 
significance and the Rheinwald brothers' dedication to preserving its 
legacy.
  In recent years, Jason and Alex further expanded Rochester's Jewish 
culinary traditions through the opening of The Bagel Shop By Fox's on 
Park Avenue, bringing traditional hand-rolled New York-style bagels and 
Jewish deli culture to new audiences while maintaining the quality and 
sense of community that define Fox's.
  Jason and Alex Rheinwald's work reflects a deep and enduring 
commitment to preserving Rochester's Jewish heritage, supporting local 
business, and strengthening community connections. During Jewish 
American Heritage Month, it is especially fitting that we honor 
individuals who have gone above and beyond to preserve shared cultural 
traditions and enrich our communities. I am proud to recognize them and 
extend my deepest gratitude for their continued service.
```
