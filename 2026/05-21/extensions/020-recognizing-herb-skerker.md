# RECOGNIZING HERB SKERKER

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING HERB SKERKER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Jewish American 
Heritage Month to recognize a dedicated community leader and volunteer 
from Rochester, New York: Herb Skerker.
  For decades, Herb Skerker has been a respected and active leader 
within Rochester's Jewish community through his longstanding 
involvement with Temple B'rith Kodesh, one of the region's historic 
Reform congregations. Through his service and leadership, he has helped 
strengthen Jewish communal life in Rochester across generations, 
supporting the synagogue's religious, cultural, and philanthropic 
initiatives.
  Herb's commitment to Jewish education, congregational leadership, and 
preserving the traditions of Rochester's Jewish institutions has earned 
him the admiration and gratitude of countless members of the community. 
In addition to his work with Temple B'rith Kodesh, he has supported 
broader charitable and civic efforts that reflect his deep dedication 
to service and community engagement.
  Widely regarded as a thoughtful and devoted presence within Rochester 
Jewish life, Herb has consistently contributed his time, leadership, 
and energy to ensuring the continued vitality of the local Jewish 
community. His example demonstrates the profound impact that dedicated 
volunteerism and faithful service can have on generations of families 
and congregants.
  Herb Skerker's service reflects a deep and enduring commitment to 
faith, leadership, and Jewish communal life. During Jewish American 
Heritage Month, it is especially fitting that we honor individuals who 
have gone above and beyond to strengthen our communities and preserve 
shared cultural heritage. I am proud to recognize him and extend my 
deepest gratitude for his continued service.

                          ____________________
```
