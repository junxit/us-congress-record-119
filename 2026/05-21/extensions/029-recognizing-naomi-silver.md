# RECOGNIZING NAOMI SILVER

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING NAOMI SILVER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Jewish American 
Heritage Month to recognize an exceptional civic and sports leader from 
Rochester, New York: Naomi Silver.
  Naomi Silver serves as President and Chief Executive Officer of the 
Rochester Red Wings and Rochester Community Baseball, where her 
leadership has helped strengthen one of Rochester's most beloved 
institutions. Throughout her groundbreaking career in professional 
baseball, she has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to civic 
engagement, philanthropy, and youth development, carrying forward the 
community-centered legacy established by her father, Morrie Silver.
  In addition to her accomplishments in professional sports, Naomi has 
long been deeply involved in Rochester's Jewish community through 
leadership and volunteer service with organizations including the 
Jewish Federation of Greater Rochester and Hillel Community Day School. 
Her dedication to strengthening educational, cultural, and nonprofit 
institutions has had a lasting impact across the region.
  Widely respected throughout Rochester for both her professional 
leadership and her service to others, Naomi has become an important 
advocate for community-centered leadership and civic responsibility. 
Her work reflects the values of service, inclusion, and compassion that 
continue to strengthen the Rochester community.
  Naomi Silver's career reflects a deep and enduring commitment to 
civic leadership, philanthropy, and Jewish communal life. During Jewish 
American Heritage Month, it is especially fitting that we honor 
individuals who have gone above and beyond to strengthen our 
communities and preserve shared cultural heritage. I am proud to 
recognize her and extend my deepest gratitude for her continued 
service.
```
