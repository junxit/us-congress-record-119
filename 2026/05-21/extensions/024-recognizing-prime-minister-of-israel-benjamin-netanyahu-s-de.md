# RECOGNIZING PRIME MINISTER OF ISRAEL BENJAMIN NETANYAHU'S DEFENSE OF THE DRUZE COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Hamadeh, Abraham J. (H001098) [R-AZ]

```
 RECOGNIZING PRIME MINISTER OF ISRAEL BENJAMIN NETANYAHU'S DEFENSE OF 
                          THE DRUZE COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ABRAHAM J. HAMADEH

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Prime 
Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, who, in July 2025, demonstrated resolute 
leadership and moral clarity through his actions to defend the Druze 
community in Syria during a period of escalating violence and 
instability. As Druze villages and families faced mounting threats in 
southern Syria, Netanyahu made clear that Israel would not remain 
silent or indifferent while innocent people--particularly a community 
bound to Israel by generations of friendship and shared sacrifice--were 
placed in danger. His commitment reflected not only strategic 
leadership, but also a deep recognition of the historic bond between 
the Jewish and Druze peoples, a relationship built on loyalty, courage, 
honor, and mutual respect.
  For decades, the Druze community has played an integral role in the 
fabric of Israeli society, serving with distinction in the Israel 
Defense Forces, contributing to public life, and standing shoulder to 
shoulder with the people of Israel in moments of both triumph and 
hardship. Netanyahu's decision to act in defense of Druze civilians in 
Syria sent a powerful message to the world: true allies do not abandon 
one another in times of crisis. His leadership underscored the 
principle that protecting vulnerable communities and confronting 
extremism are essential to preserving regional stability and human 
dignity.
  At a time when much of the international community hesitated, 
Netanyahu's actions reflected conviction, strength, and a willingness 
to uphold Israel's longstanding commitment to the safety and security 
of the Druze people. In standing with the Druze during one of their 
most difficult moments, Prime Minister Netanyahu reinforced a legacy of 
partnership, solidarity, and unwavering support that will not be 
forgotten.
```
