# HONORING THE SERVICE OF CAPTAIN MICHAEL D. BISHOP

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
           HONORING THE SERVICE OF CAPTAIN MICHAEL D. BISHOP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 21, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Captain Michael D. Bishop, U.S. Navy Commanding Officer, for 
his distinguished military service.
  Captain Bishop has served in the U.S. Navy since his enlistment in 
1991 and has built an exceptional career marked by operational 
excellence, leadership, and dedication to the development of future 
servicemembers. His first role was with the ``World Watchers'' of VQ-1 
at Naval Air Station Agana in Guam. He was later selected for the 
Enlisted Commissioning Program and graduated summa cum laude from Ohio 
State University, where he earned a bachelor's degree in political 
science.
  Over the course of his service, Captain Bishop has accumulated more 
than 3,000 pilot flight hours and over 7,500 hours in military 
aircraft. His career reflects extraordinary dedication, 
professionalism, and patriotism, and he

[[Page E480]]

continues to exemplify the very best of the United States Navy.
  Captain Bishop has served in numerous operational and training 
assignments, earning recognition for his aviation expertise and 
leadership. Since assuming command at Kingsville Naval Air Station in 
2024, he has overseen the training of future Navy, Marine Corps, and 
Allied tactical pilots through Training Air Wing TWO (TW-2). Under his 
leadership, the installation has continued to strengthen its 
relationship with the Kingsville community while maintaining the 
highest standards of military readiness and instruction.
  Captain Bishop's guidance has helped shape the next generation of 
military aviators. Drawing on his operational and instructional 
expertise, he will continue to drive leadership excellence at NAS 
Kingsville during his tenure.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the 34th Congressional District of Texas, I 
congratulate Captain Bishop on his many achievements and thank him for 
his continued service. His leadership serves as an example for 
servicemembers across South Texas and the Nation.
```
