# HONORING DEPUTY JAMES MARK WINDSOR

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
                   HONORING DEPUTY JAMES MARK WINDSOR

● Mr. TUBERVILLE. Mr. President, 31 years ago Alabama lost a 
legend, Randolph County Deputy James Mark Windsor, who was killed at 
the young age of 28 years old in the line of duty on May 6, 1995. 
Deputy Windsor answered the call to serve his community with courage 
and humility, dedicating himself to protecting the people of Randolph 
County and the great State of Alabama. East Alabama is undoubtedly a 
better, safer place for families in our great State because of the work 
of Deputy Windsor. Though his time in law enforcement was tragically 
brief, his impact and sacrifice will never be forgotten.
  Deputy Windsor served as a reserve deputy for 2 years before 
attending the police academy and becoming an active deputy with the 
Randolph County Sheriff's Office. After only 4 months as a full-time 
deputy, he made the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty. His 
willingness to step forward and serve speaks volumes about his 
character, sense of duty, and commitment to others.
  As the anniversary of his passing comes around each year, it is 
important that we remember Deputy Windsor's life and impact that he 
continues to make in his community. The people of Alabama remain 
forever indebted to him and to all law enforcement officers who put 
themselves in harm's way in service. His bravery lives on in the lives 
of his family and friends.
  My wife Suzanne and I extend our heartfelt prayers to Deputy 
Windsor's family, friends, fellow deputies, and all those who knew and 
loved him as they celebrate the legacy Deputy Windsor has left behind. 
May they find comfort in knowing that his sacrifice will never be 
forgotten, and may God continue to bless the memory of Deputy James 
Mark Windsor.●

                          ____________________
```
