# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 736, Kathleen S. Lane, of Montana, 
     to be United States District Judge for the District of 
     Montana.
         John Thune, Ted Budd, Thom Tillis, John Barrasso, Tim 
           Sheehy, Joni Ernst, Jon Husted, Katie Boyd Britt, David 
           McCormick, Mike Rounds, John Boozman, Bill Cassidy, 
           Rick Scott of Florida, Josh Hawley, Cynthia M. Lummis, 
           Kevin Cramer, Steve Daines.
```
