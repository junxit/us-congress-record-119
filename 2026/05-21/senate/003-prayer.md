# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Maker of the earth, sea, and sky, speak to our hearts today that we 
may cling to things that cannot fail. Speak to our lawmakers that they 
may embrace Your purposes and do Your will. Give them strength for the 
work before them in that they may not become weary in well-doing, 
realizing that, in due season, the harvest will come.
  And, God, we also ask You to bless this land we love. Defend it from 
the forces that seek to destroy our freedoms. May its citizens never 
forget that righteousness exalts a nation but that sin is an equal 
opportunity destroyer.
  And, Lord, let there be peace on Earth, and let it begin with us.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
