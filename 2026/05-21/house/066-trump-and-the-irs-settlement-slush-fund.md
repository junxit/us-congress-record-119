```
                TRUMP AND THE IRS SETTLEMENT/SLUSH FUND

  (Ms. BALINT asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)

corrupt slush fund to give money to his allies, including the violent 
attackers who beat police officers on January 6.

Department of Justice to make a deal with the other agency to drop your 
ridiculous lawsuit that you brought in the first place in exchange for

taking our money, taxpayer dollars, to give to his friends, his allies, 
and his foot soldiers for their craven loyalty.
  Former Capitol Police Officers have already filed a lawsuit to stop 
it. I am cosponsoring a bill to put an end to this disgusting, illegal 
corruption.
  Let's be clear: Congress on both sides of the aisle must say ``no'' 
to this President.
```
