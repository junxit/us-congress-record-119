# THANKING MOLLY CUTRONA, MY CHIEF OF STAFF

**Speaking:** Amodei, Mark E. (A000369) [R-NV]

```
               THANKING MOLLY CUTRONA, MY CHIEF OF STAFF

  (Mr. Amodei of Nevada was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. AMODEI. Mr. Speaker, some may be surprised to see me standing on 
the House floor today, including the person this speech is about. I 
felt it was necessary.
  Today is a bittersweet day. For my longtime Chief of Staff, Molly 
Cutrona, it is her last day on Capitol Hill. Unfortunately, we are 
losing her talent, her leadership, and her feisty energy to the private 
sector.
  Although she is a native of Nevada's redheaded stepchild's neighbor, 
California--no offense to my colleagues from there who don't hold her 
in the same regard that we do--we consider her, after her stint in this 
office, an honorary, born-again Nevadan.
  Molly started in my office 8 years ago as my legislative director. 
Over those 8 years, I have watched her grow both professionally and 
personally, getting married, and welcoming beautiful twins into the 
world.
  However, Mr. Speaker, don't be fooled. I have experienced plenty of 
bullying from her disguised as advising. I will say this once, and only 
once: Some of it may have been deserved.
  Those 8 years aren't defined by her antics. She will be remembered in 
these Halls for her ambition, loyalty, creativity, confidence, and 
absolute dedication to her colleagues and to the people of Nevada's 
Second District.
  We are losing one of the best in the business. It has been an honor 
to work alongside her, and we have no doubt that she will continue to 
set a phenomenal example for her daughters, Claire and Coco.
  Molly, from the bottom of my heart, thank you for everything. With C-
SPAN as my witness: I apologize for allowing Ken, your successor, to 
rearrange your office.

                          ____________________
```
