# CELEBRATING BELLEFONTE HIGH SCHOOL AND MIDDLE SCHOOL MAJORETTE TEAMS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
  CELEBRATING BELLEFONTE HIGH SCHOOL AND MIDDLE SCHOOL MAJORETTE TEAMS

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 
the Bellefonte High School and Middle School majorette teams, which 
both secured multiple State titles at the Pennsylvania State Baton 
Twirling Championships.
  At the competition, both the high school and middle school teams were 
named the 2026 Pennsylvania Open and State Halftime Show Twirl Team 
winners.
  Both programs have a history of winning area and State championships, 
and their recent success is a direct reflection of their dedication and 
hard work over the past year.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend Bellefonte High School majorette captain 
Breanna Dickey, who has been selected as the next featured twirler at 
Indiana

[[Page H3713]]

University. This upcoming football season, Breanna will lead the 
university's marching band on the field.
  She first picked up a baton at age 4 and has won multiple State and 
national twirling titles and has competed internationally.
  I congratulate Breanna.
  Mr. Speaker, I applaud the high school and middle school majorettes 
for their great achievement. I thank them for representing Bellefonte 
and the local community so well.

                          ____________________
```
