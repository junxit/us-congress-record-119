# HONORING DANIEL G. WUCHTER

**Speaking:** Mackenzie, Ryan (M001230) [R-PA]

```
                       HONORING DANIEL G. WUCHTER

  (Mr. MACKENZIE asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MACKENZIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Daniel G. Wuchter, Sr., a longtime entrepreneur and the owner of the 
Allentown Fairgrounds Farmers Market.
  For decades, Dan helped shape one of the Lehigh Valley's most beloved 
institutions. His connection to the farmers market stretched back to 
its first opening in 1955, when he and his brother first operated a 
stand before building a business that grew into a lifelong commitment 
to the market and the community it served.
  As owner of the farmers market, Dan's hard work helped transform it 
into a gathering place that generations of families have come to know 
and love. Those who knew Dan remember not only his determination, but 
also his generosity and love for the Lehigh Valley.
  We extend our condolences to his wife, Laurie; his family; friends; 
and all those who knew him.

                          ____________________
```
