# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Father Andrew Aziz, Holy Apostolic Catholic Assyrian Church 
of the East, Gilbert, Arizona, offered the following prayer:
  In the name of the Father, and the Son, and the holy spirit, one God, 
Amen.

[[Page H3683]]

  Heavenly Father, we stand before You, acknowledging Your sovereign 
wisdom and mercy. We come to You at this hour asking You to bless and 
strengthen our civil servants as they labor for our country and its 
citizens.
  Let their deliberations be inspired by love, compassion, and justice 
for all; serving the common good with integrity and a heart for the 
needs of every citizen, without distinction of race or creed.
  In all things, O Lord, remind us of our dependence on Your divine 
grace. Father, we ask You to bless our land, which has been a beacon of 
hope and a refuge for the oppressed and the marginalized. Grant freedom 
to the captive, relief to the suffering, and help us all to construct a 
better and safer tomorrow for our future generations of Americans.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
