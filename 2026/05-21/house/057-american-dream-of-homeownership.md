# AMERICAN DREAM OF HOMEOWNERSHIP

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
                    AMERICAN DREAM OF HOMEOWNERSHIP

  (Mr. NUNN of Iowa asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, together, in a bipartisan fashion, 
this Chamber passed the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, the most 
significant update to rural housing programs in decades.
  I fought to make sure my Rural Housing Service Reform Act was front 
and center in this package, because in rural Iowa, the Federal programs 
meant to help families buy, repair, or keep their homes are running on 
technology that is almost as old as the internet itself. Families are 
paying the price.
  Iowa families are waiting, in some cases up to a year, for a U.S. 
Department of Agriculture's housing loan while interest rates change 
and prices

[[Page H3714]]

continue to rise. Those essential home repairs are capped so low they 
won't even cover a new roof or plumbing repairs.
  My legislation fixes this. It modernizes the broken USDA system, 
doubles the home repair loan limits, and preserves affordable housing 
that would otherwise disappear.
  I am proud this reflects the true American Dream of homeownership. In 
fact, I worked across the aisle with my very good friend Ranking Member 
Emanuel Cleaver to write these reforms.
  If a Democrat from Kansas City will drive all the way to Iowa to help 
build a home, you know this is truly a bipartisan effort. Because of 
this bill, our families are in a better spot.
  Now, I am ready to go to work with the Senate and the President to 
get this signed.

                          ____________________
```
