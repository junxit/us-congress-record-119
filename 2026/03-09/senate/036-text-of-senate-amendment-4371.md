# Text of Senate Amendment 4371

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
  SA 4371. Mr. TUBERVILLE submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the supply of 
housing in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. DEFINITION OF COVERED MULTIFAMILY DWELLINGS.

       Section 804(f)(7) of the Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 
     3604(f)(7)) is amended by striking ``4'' each place that term 
     appears and inserting ``20''.
                                 ______
```
