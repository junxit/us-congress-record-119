# Text of Senate Amendment 4378

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6644

```
  SA 4378. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by

[[Page S933]]

him to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the supply of housing in 
America, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. EXCLUSION OF HUD-VASH VOUCHERS FROM PROJECT-BASED 
                   ASSISTANCE CAP.

       Section 8(o)(19) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 
     (42 U.S.C. 1437f(o)(19)) is amended by adding at the end the 
     following:
       ``(E) Percentage limitation for project-based assistance.--
     The percentage limitation under paragraph (13)(B) shall not 
     apply to assistance provided under this paragraph.''.
                                 ______
```
