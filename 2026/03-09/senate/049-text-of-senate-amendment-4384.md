# Text of Senate Amendment 4384

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 4384. Mr. PADILLA (for himself and Mr. Cramer) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill 
to increase the supply of housing in America, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. _____. AGENT MEMBERSHIP.

        Section 304(b)(2) of the Federal Credit Union Act (12 
     U.S.C. 1795c(b)(2)) is amended by striking ``all those credit 
     unions'' and inserting ``any such credit unions''.
                                 ______
```
