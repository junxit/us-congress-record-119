# National Speech and Debate Education Day (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                National Speech and Debate Education Day

  On another subject, Mr. President, since 2016, the senior Senator 
from Delaware and I have led the Senate to pass a resolution to 
recognize National Speech and Debate Education Day. This year, our 
bipartisan resolution designated March 6 as National Speech and Debate 
Education Day, marking the 10th anniversary of celebration of this 
important occasion.
  However, it isn't just 10 days but much longer. The organization 
governing these programs--the National Speech & Debate Association--has 
been around much longer. The National Speech & Debate Association, 
which happens to be based in the heart of America, which is right there 
in my home State of Iowa, in West Des Moines, IA--the National Speech & 
Debate Association has served its members for over 100 years.
  For over a century, the National Speech & Debate Association has 
worked to equip young people with the skills to research thoroughly, to 
construct sound arguments, to listen actively, and to respond 
thoughtfully. These are not just competition skills; they are life 
skills.
  Particularly when we have so much partisanship in our country and at 
a time when our public discourse can feel increasingly divided, 
programs like these matter more than ever. Speech and debate education 
teaches students that strong arguments are built on evidence; that 
persuasion requires preparation; and that progress begins, first of 
all, by just listening.
  As we celebrate the 10th anniversary of the National Speech and 
Debate Education Day, we honor not only an organization with a proud 
100-year-or-more history but also generations of students who have 
stepped up to the podium, found their voice, and strengthened our 
democracy in the process.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Budd). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
