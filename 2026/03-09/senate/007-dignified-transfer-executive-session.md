# Dignified Transfer (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                           Dignified Transfer

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, over the weekend, I was honored to 
attend a very solemn but dignified transfer of the remains at the Dover 
Air Force Base along with President Trump, Vice President Vance, 
Secretary Hegseth, and the entire Iowa congressional delegation. We 
paid our respects to the seven fallen heroes. Barbara and I continue to 
pray for the safety of our brave military.
```
