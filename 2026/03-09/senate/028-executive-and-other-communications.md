# EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

```
                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communication was laid before the Senate, together with 
accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and was referred as 
indicated:

        EC-2923. A communication from the President of the United 
     States to the President pro tempore of the United States 
     Senate, transmitting, consistent with the War Powers 
     Resolution, a report relative to military action taken on 
     March 6, 2026, against the facilities of narco-terrorists 
     affiliated with a designated terrorist organization; this 
     action was taken at the invitation of, with the consent of, 
     and in partnership with the Government of the Republic of 
     Ecuador; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
```
