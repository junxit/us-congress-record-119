# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord, as we begin today's session, we ask that Your Spirit would 
guide the deliberations of our lawmakers. Move in their hearts, guiding 
their thoughts and intentions to noble ends. May they hear Your voice, 
embrace Your wisdom, and trust Your guidance. Use them to lead the 
world into a new era of freedom and peace. Lord, help our Nation's 
leaders to stand tall for righteousness. Bind them together through the 
power of prayer as they intercede for one another during these 
dangerous times.
  And, Lord, protect our military personnel in harm's way.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
