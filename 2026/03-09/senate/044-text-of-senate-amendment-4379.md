# Text of Senate Amendment 4379

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 4379. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the supply of housing 
in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. GAO STUDY.

       Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this 
     Act, the Comptroller General of the United States shall 
     conduct a study and submit to Congress recommendations on--
       (1) the required income calculations and certifications 
     across Federal housing assistance programs and other related 
     social assistance programs; and
       (2) ways to streamline the process and reduce the burden of 
     those calculations and certifications.
                                 ______
```
