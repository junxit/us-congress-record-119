# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2657, H.R. 3149, H.R. 7257, H.R. 7258, H.R. 7266, H.R. 7272, H.R. 7305, H.R. 7757

```
Committee Meetings
LIBRARY OF CONGRESS AND GOVERNMENT PUBLISHING OFFICE
Committee on Appropriations: On Friday, March 6, 2026, Subcommittee on 
Legislative Branch held a budget hearing on the Library of Congress and 
Government Publishing Office. Testimony was heard from Robert R. 
Newlen, Acting Librarian of Congress; and Hugh N. Halpern, Director, 
U.S. Government Publishing Office.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: On Thursday, March 5, 2026, Full 
Committee held a markup on H.R. 7757, the ``Kids Internet Digital 
Safety Act''; H.R. 2657, the ``Sammy's Law''; H.R. 3149, the ``App 
Store Accountability Act''; H.R. 7257, the ``Securing Community 
Upgrades for a Resilient Grid Act''; H.R. 7305, the ``Energy Threat 
Analysis Center Act of 2026''; H.R. 7258, the ``Energy Emergency 
Leadership Act''; H.R. 7266, the ``Rural and Municipal Utility 
Cybersecurity Act''; and H.R. 7272, the ``Pipeline Cybersecurity 
Preparedness Act''. H.R. 7757, H.R. 2657, H.R. 3149, H.R. 7257, and 
H.R. 7305 were ordered reported, as amended. H.R. 7258, H.R. 7266, and 
H.R. 7272 were ordered reported, without amendment.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
