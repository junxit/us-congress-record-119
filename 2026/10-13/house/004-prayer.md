# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O Lord of sea and sky, who carved the ocean floors and set the waters 
in their place, who stills storms to a whisper and hushes the waves of 
the sea, watch over all those who this day sail these vast oceans in 
their service to our Nation and the United States Navy.
  Even as we celebrate 251 years of the Navy's noble history, may each 
sailor who stands the watch today be assured of Your protection and 
strengthened in their resolve by the promise of Your sure defense.
  Wherever they find themselves this day, with the USS George W. Bush 
Carrier Strike Group or the USS Boxer Amphibious Ready Group in the 
Arabian Gulf, on the destroyers and support ships operating in the 
Indian Ocean, the Red Sea, and the Mediterranean, God of all creation, 
watch over them.
  May both chart and compass guide them and Your hand lead them through 
the tempests and shoals they encounter.
  Then, as You have granted the USS Lincoln and all who have gone down 
to the sea in ships safe passage home to the waiting arms of family and 
friends, so may each of us anticipate the reward of Your embrace when 
we have faithfully served You in the days You have given us in this 
place, defending the freedoms You have bestowed on this country.
  In Your eternal name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
