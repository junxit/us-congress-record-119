                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BAIRD:
       H.R. 10795. A bill to authorize the establishment of a 
     United States-Sudan Agriculture and Minerals partnership, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 10796. A bill to direct the Secretary of Defense to 
     designate an executive agent responsible for synchronizing 
     cooperative efforts between the United States and Israel, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Armed Services, and 
     in addition to the Committee on Foreign Affairs, for a period 
     to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case 
     for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 10797. A bill to direct the Secretary of the Air Force 
     to carry out a program to provide for the safe, long-range 
     aerial transport of individuals known to be infected, or 
     suspected of infection, by high-consequence infectious 
     diseases; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. CORREA (for himself and Ms. Dexter):
       H.R. 10798. A bill to establish a grant program for funding 
     silicosis treatment and education, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. GROTHMAN:
       H.R. 10799. A bill to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to modernize the wage required for exempt H-1B workers, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. LATIMER:
       H.R. 10800. A bill to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to establish a process for designating a safe third 
     country and review of safe third country determinations, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 10801. A bill to direct the Secretary of the Treasury 
     to carry out a program under which Federal funds for 
     elementary and secondary education are either provided 
     directly to parents to cover certain educational expenses or 
     approved by parents for distribution to the public school 
     their child attends, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Education and Workforce, and in addition to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means, for a period to be subsequently determined 
     by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of such 
     provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the committee 
     concerned.
           By Mr. OLSZEWSKI (for himself and Ms. McBride):
       H.R. 10802. A bill to direct heads of Federal agencies to 
     support increased access to voting, and for other purposes; 
     to the Committee on House Administration, and in addition to 
     the Committees on Oversight and Government Reform, Science, 
     Space, and Technology, Armed Services, and the Judiciary, for 
     a period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in 
     each case for consideration of such provisions as fall within 
     the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mrs. RAMIREZ (for herself, Ms. Tlaib, Mrs. Grijalva, 
             and Mr. McGovern):
       H.R. 10803. A bill to abolish the Department of Homeland 
     Security and establish a Department of Community Safety, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Homeland Security, 
     and in addition to the Committees on Oversight and Government 
     Reform, the Judiciary, Transportation and Infrastructure, 
     Rules, Foreign Affairs, Financial Services, Armed Services, 
     Intelligence (Permanent Select), Education and Workforce, 
     Appropriations, Ways and Means, and Energy and Commerce, for 
     a period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in 
     each case for consideration of such provisions as fall within 
     the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. RILEY of New York:
       H.R. 10804. A bill to establish penalties for the failure 
     of investor-owned electric utilities to meet State 
     performance targets; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. SCHNEIDER:
       H.R. 10805. A bill to amend the Communications Act of 1934 
     to prohibit schools and public libraries that receive 
     universal service support from blocking internet access to 
     lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer resources, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 10806. A bill to amend title XVIII of the Social 
     Security Act to decrease fraud related to home health 
     agencies in Medicare, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Ways and Means, and in addition to the 
     Committees on Energy and Commerce, and the Judiciary, for a 
     period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each 
     case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Ms. TLAIB (for herself, Ms. Garcia of Texas, Ms. Lee 
             of Pennsylvania, Ms. Omar, Mrs. Ramirez, Ms. 
             Velazquez, and Mrs. Watson Coleman):
       H.R. 10807. A bill to prohibit the use of automated mass 
     surveillance systems and associated components, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Oversight and Government 
     Reform, and in addition to the Committee on the Judiciary, 
     for a period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in 
     each case for consideration of such provisions as fall within 
     the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. VAN EPPS:
       H.R. 10808. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to allow additional contributions to a health savings 
     account in the case of the birth or adoption of a child, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
           By Mr. VINDMAN:
       H.R. 10809. A bill to amend the Federal Power Act to 
     protect ratepayers from costs relating to the electric loads 
     of data centers and high-density computing facilities; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Ms. KAPTUR (for herself and Mr. Latta):
       H. Res. 1624. A resolution recognizing the 110th 
     anniversary of the Nation's First Presidential library, the 
     Rutherford B. Hayes Presidential Library & Museums; to the 
     Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
