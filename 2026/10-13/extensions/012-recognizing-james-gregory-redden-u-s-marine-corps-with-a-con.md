# RECOGNIZING JAMES GREGORY REDDEN, U.S. MARINE CORPS, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
      RECOGNIZING JAMES GREGORY REDDEN, U.S. MARINE CORPS, WITH A 
                   CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor James Gregory Redden 
of Taylor, Texas, by presenting him with a Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. His devotion to our Nation through his military service 
and his continued commitment to his community service reflect the 
pinnacle of American exceptionalism.
  Greg Redden enlisted in the United States Marine Corps in October 
1984 and reported for basic training in San Diego with Platoon 1105 in 
1985. Following continued training at El Toro USMC Air Station and 
Tustin MCAS, Greg was assigned to Marine Aircraft Group 24 at the 
Marine Corps Air Station in Kaneohe Bay, Hawaii, where he worked with 
VMFA 235 and HMH 364 and received his Collateral Duty Inspector 
certification. During his time in K-Bay, he had two temporary 
deployments to 29 Palms Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center, where he 
was the lead for the night shift ground support for aircraft repairs. 
He also had one temporary deployment to Top Gun, where he provided 
ground support for the flightline.
  Greg was then assigned to the Marine Corps Air Station Futenma in 
Okinawa from January to August 1988. Following this, he returned to 
Hawaii until November 1989, when he received the highest security 
clearance by the Marine Corps and was reassigned to Quantico, Virginia, 
and was assigned to Marine One. He traveled with President George H.W. 
Bush on numerous deployments as part of the maintenance support team 
for the President's helicopters. While in Quantico, he was also on the 
Osprey test team and was the lead for maintenance of the group support 
equipment.
  Having been honorably discharged from the USMC in April 1992, Greg 
turned to public service, working with the City of Austin from 1997 to 
2016. Greg moved to Taylor, Texas, in August 2014, where his community 
involvement began to soar. He has been an active member of the American 
Legion Post since 2018, serving as Service Officer since 2023. When the 
American Legion needed a temporary post due to renovations, Greg 
volunteered his home.
  Greg joined the Rotary Club of Taylor in April 2022 and served as its 
president in 2024-2025. He still helps lead the club, volunteering his 
time at the Shepherd's Heart food pantry, working with DAP grants, 
serving on the scholarship review team, and organizing the annual golf 
tournament and several other fundraising events.
  He has been a member of the Taylor Rodeo Association since 2023, 
serving in a variety of organizational and fundraising roles. He has 
also been a member of the Taylor Chamber of Commerce since 2019, where 
he has served as an honorary ambassador and vice chair of the Chamber 
golf tournament. He has now served on the Taylor City Council since he 
took his oath of office in May 2025.
  Greg is a co-owner of Veteran Storage LLC in Taylor, and he is a 
devout member of the Fellowship Church, where he is a greeter on Sunday 
mornings.
  It is with great honor and respect that I present United States 
Marine Corps E4 James Gregory Redden with a Congressional Veteran 
Commendation from the 17th Congressional District of Texas. His love of 
his God and his country serve as an inspiration to us all.
```
