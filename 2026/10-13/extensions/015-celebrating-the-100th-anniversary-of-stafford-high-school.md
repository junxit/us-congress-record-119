# CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF STAFFORD HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
       CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF STAFFORD HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
100th anniversary of Stafford High School in Stafford County, Virginia, 
and to honor a century of academic excellence, school spirit, and 
unwavering commitment to educating generations of young Virginians.
  Since opening its doors in 1926, Stafford High School has been more 
than an institution of learning. It has been a cornerstone of the 
Stafford community, where lifelong friendships are forged, dreams take 
shape, and young people discover their potential. Through its 
commitment to education, leadership, and service, the school has helped 
prepare generations of students to pursue their ambitions and make 
meaningful contributions to their communities.
  From its classrooms to athletic fields, and its performing arts 
programs and extracurricular activities, Stafford High School has 
cultivated a proud tradition of achievement and opportunity. Its 
success reflects the dedication of countless educators, administrators, 
staff, and families who have invested their time, talents, and hearts 
into the success of every student.
  In recognition of this anniversary, Stafford High School teachers, 
with generous support from the Stafford Education Foundation, created a 
commemorative mural celebrating the school's centennial. This lasting 
tribute beautifully reflects the pride, dedication, and community 
spirit that have defined Stafford High School for generations.
  As we celebrate this extraordinary achievement, we must also look 
toward the future. As leaders, our responsibility is to ensure that 
every student who walks through Stafford High School's doors has the 
opportunity, resources, and encouragement to pursue their dreams and 
build a brighter future.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Stafford High School on its centennial anniversary and extending our 
heartfelt appreciation to the educators, administrators, families, and 
alumni who have sustained its remarkable legacy. May the next 100 years 
bring continued excellence, opportunity, and enduring Tribe pride.

                          ____________________
```
