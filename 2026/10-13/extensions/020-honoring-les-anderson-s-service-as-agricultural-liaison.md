# HONORING LES ANDERSON'S SERVICE AS AGRICULTURAL LIAISON

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
        HONORING LES ANDERSON'S SERVICE AS AGRICULTURAL LIAISON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Les 
Anderson for over six years of service with my team as an Agricultural 
Liaison.
  Les has been a key advisor on agriculture issues and truly knows what 
is most important to Minnesota farmers. Les first joined my team after 
serving as the President of the Minnesota Corn Growers Association. He 
farms in Cannon Falls, Minnesota and grows corn and soybeans.
  Les is the longest serving employee in my district office. He has 
been steadfast in his commitment to our mission and never fails to make 
me laugh. His positive attitude and compassion are qualities that will 
serve him well in the future.
  I know Les will continue advocating on behalf of Minnesota's farmers. 
I wish him the best in his future.

                          ____________________
```
