# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF STEPHANIE FULTON SANDS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
         HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF STEPHANIE FULTON SANDS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of Ms. Stephanie Fulton Sands, a woman of unwavering faith 
and dedication to her community. I include in the Record the following 
proclamation:

       Whereas, Stephanie Elaine Fulton was born on January 12, 
     1960, in Miami, Florida, to the late Nathan ``Big Bro'' 
     Fulton and Marion L. Spotford. As the eldest grandchild and 
     firstborn, she brought immense joy to her family and all who 
     knew her; and
       Whereas, Stephanie was a proud product of Miami-Dade County 
     Public Schools, graduating from Miami Carol City Senior High 
     School in 1978. She furthered her education at Barry 
     University, where she earned an Associate's Degree, which 
     fueled her professional pursuits; and
       Whereas, Stephanie accepted the Lord Jesus Christ as her 
     personal savior at an early age and was a faithful member of 
     Antioch Missionary Baptist Church of Carol City, where she 
     also lent her beautiful voice as a member of the choir; and
       Whereas, Stephanie demonstrated remarkable professional 
     achievements, having worked with the Department of Children 
     and Families (HRS) and in the fields of finance and real 
     estate. Most notably, she dedicated her life to serving as 
     Chief of Administration for the Trayvon Martin Foundation, 
     tirelessly working to reduce gun violence and uplift families 
     through holistic support, S.T.E.M. education, and mentoring 
     for women and minorities; and
       Whereas, Stephanie's commitment to service extended to her 
     recent membership with Top Ladies of Distinction (TLOD), 
     where she continued her passion for making a positive impact 
     on the world; and
       Whereas, Stephanie's life was a testament to selflessness, 
     kindness, and a deep commitment to her family, friends, and 
     community. Whether through her professional work or personal 
     relationships, she touched countless lives with her 
     generosity and compassion, Her infectious warmth made her a 
     beloved member of every community she was part of; and
       Whereas, Ms. Stephanie Fulton Sands left behind to carry on 
     her legacy and hold on to loving memories are her two 
     daughters, Shakisha Harris and Shantanett Sands; one son, 
     Darion Sands; four grandchildren Keymani Harris, Oxley 
     Christiani, III, Chrisette Christiani and Cornelius Miller, 
     Jr.; parents, Marion and Ted Evans; siblings, Ronald Fulton, 
     Mark Fulton, Sybrina Fulton, Yolanda Evans, and Tousha Hicks; 
     nephews, Jahvaris Fulton and Ronquavis Fulton; aunts and 
     uncles, Yvonne Greene (Jacob), Rhudine Wiley (Charlie), Erma 
     President (Allen), Wadell Owens and Eleanor Owens; sister/
     cousin, Penelope Samuel; past partner for over 30 years, 
     Gregory Sands; and a host cousins and friends. She was 
     preceded in death by her beloved father, Nathan Fulton; 
     great-grandmother and grandmother, Leona ``Big Mama'' Tim and 
     Nettie ``Nana'' Spotford; aunt, Leona Johnson; and nephew, 
     Trayvon Martin, who all welcomed her with open arms into 
     eternity; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to recognize the late Stephanie Fulton Sands, and 
     extend my heartfelt sympathy.

                          ____________________
```
