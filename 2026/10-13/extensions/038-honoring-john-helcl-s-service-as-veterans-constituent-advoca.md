# HONORING JOHN HELCL'S SERVICE AS VETERANS CONSTITUENT ADVOCATE

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
     HONORING JOHN HELCL'S SERVICE AS VETERANS CONSTITUENT ADVOCATE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate John 
Helcl for his one year of service with my office.
  John is my office's Veterans Constituent Advocate and he serves 
active duty and veteran constituents in my district. John first joined 
my team after losing his job with the VA due to the Trump 
Administration's sweeping layoffs. He spoke at a press conference I 
hosted and bravely shared his story. I was proud to have John join my 
team. In fact, John's son Jack wants to work in my office as an intern. 
He's 13 years old but eager to follow in his father's footsteps.
  John is an army veteran and spent most of his career in public 
safety, working for both the state and federal governments. His 
resilience and dedication will only benefit him in future endeavors. He 
is currently pursuing a Master's Degree at Hamline University.

                          ____________________
```
