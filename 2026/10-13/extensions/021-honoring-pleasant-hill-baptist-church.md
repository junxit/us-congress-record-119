# HONORING PLEASANT HILL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING PLEASANT HILL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pleasant Hill 
Baptist Church of Shreveport, Louisiana--an enduring pillar of faith, 
fellowship, and community uplift whose legacy spans more than one 
hundred years. Established in 1916 under the name New Zion Baptist 
Church, and reorganized in 1917 as Pleasant Hill Baptist Church, this 
historic congregation has served as a spiritual home for generations of 
families, shaping the religious and civic landscape of the Shreveport 
community.
  From its earliest beginnings, Pleasant Hill Baptist Church has stood 
as far more than a place of Sunday worship. It became a sanctuary where 
individuals and families could strengthen their Christian faith, find 
encouragement in times of hardship, and work together in service and 
fellowship. Through seasons of change--both within the community and 
across the nation--the congregation remained firmly anchored in its 
commitment to Christian principles, spiritual development, and 
compassionate outreach. Its consistency in mission reflects the 
enduring strength and perseverance of historically rooted African 
American churches throughout Louisiana and the South.
  As the congregation grew in size and ministry, Pleasant Hill Baptist 
Church experienced periods of expansion, relocation, and renewal. Each 
transition marked another chapter in the church's unfolding story, 
propelled by members who were determined to preserve the ministry 
entrusted to them by earlier generations. Pastors, ministers, deacons, 
trustees, ministry leaders, and lay members all contributed their time, 
talents, resources, and leadership, ensuring that Pleasant Hill 
remained a

[[Page E1074]]

place where worship, learning, and service could flourish. Their 
collective dedication allowed the church to continue building 
opportunities for future generations to gather, grow, and serve.
  Throughout its more than one hundred years of ministry, Pleasant Hill 
Baptist Church has maintained a strong emphasis on Christian education, 
biblical teaching, and spiritual growth. Its worship services, prayer 
meetings, Bible study programs, youth ministries, and outreach 
initiatives have helped nurture the spiritual and practical needs of 
countless individuals and families. The church has long recognized that 
ministry extends beyond the sanctuary walls. As such, it has been an 
active force for good in the Shreveport community--supporting families, 
uplifting neighborhoods, and embodying the call to love and serve 
others with sincerity and compassion.
  The continued presence and longevity of Pleasant Hill Baptist Church 
highlight the vital role that historic African American congregations 
have played in strengthening communities through faith, leadership, 
fellowship, and advocacy. For more than a century, Pleasant Hill has 
remained a place where people could come together during moments of 
celebration and times of struggle--offering comfort, stability, and 
hope to all who entered its doors. Its story is intertwined with the 
broader narrative of Shreveport's religious, cultural, and civic 
history.
  Today, Pleasant Hill Baptist Church continues its historic mission 
under the leadership of Rev. Reginald G. Reid, Sr., who became the 
congregation's 11th pastor in 2022. Under Pastor Reid's leadership, 
Pleasant Hill remains committed to honoring the legacy of the pastors 
and members who came before him while boldly preparing for the future 
of ministry and service. His vision has helped strengthen the 
congregation's spiritual foundation, expand its outreach efforts, and 
reinforce its role as a vital institution in the community.
  More than a century after its founding, Pleasant Hill Baptist Church 
stands as a testament to faith, perseverance, fellowship, and 
generational commitment. From its beginnings as New Zion Baptist Church 
in 1916 to the thriving ministry it represents today, the church's 
enduring presence is a tribute to the men and women who faithfully 
supported its mission and to a congregation determined to carry that 
legacy forward for generations yet to come. I urge my colleagues to 
join me in honoring Pleasant Hill's extraordinary legacy.
```
