# HONORING THE MEMORY OF IAN MASSEY

**Speaking:** Leger Fernandez, Teresa (L000273) [D-NM]

```
                   HONORING THE MEMORY OF IAN MASSEY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. TERESA LEGER FERNANDEZ

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. LEGER FERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the memory of Ian 
Massey, an Electric Power Lineman for Farmer's Electric Cooperative in 
Clovis, New Mexico. Tragically, Ian succumbed to injuries sustained 
while attempting to restore power to rural residents of Curry County 
during damaging rain storms on September 25, 2026.
  Ian began his career as a lineman in 2011 after graduating from 
Portales High School. He cared deeply for his coworkers and friends and 
was always willing to lend a helping hand.
  Ian knew no strangers and gave freely of himself to people around 
him. Ian's legacy will be one of love, friendship, faith, and family. 
His love of family filled their home with laughter, energy, joy, and an 
unwavering commitment to their wellbeing.
  It is often stated that Electrical Linemen are the essential and 
often unsung heroes who climb toward the danger of storms, darkness, 
and extreme weather to keep our modern world powered. They work in 
freezing ice, pouring rain, and brutal summer heat waves. Their type of 
work requires long hours, missed holidays, and family dinners.
  This is the legacy Ian leaves for us to remember. A dedicated worker, 
family man, friend, and outstanding member of the community who was 
ready and steady whenever the call came.
```
