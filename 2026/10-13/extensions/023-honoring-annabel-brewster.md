# HONORING ANNABEL BREWSTER

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                       HONORING ANNABEL BREWSTER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor Annabel Brewster, a community advocate in South Florida, 
entrepreneur, neighbor and friend as she celebrates her 70th birthday. 
I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Annabel Brewster migrated to the United States in 
     the early 1970s from Nigeria, and she has embodied the work 
     of an Unsung Hero ever since. She has constantly contributed 
     to the Nigerian-American and South Florida community to 
     fulfill her passion for justice and equality; and
       Whereas, Ms. Brewster founded the Africa-USA Chamber of 
     Commerce to promote trade partnerships and build bridges 
     between Africa and Florida. The Chamber grew to represent 
     over 50 members from businesses to local government agencies. 
     With the support of the Broward Economic Development Center, 
     the Chamber held a successful trade conference, titled: 
     Africa-Myths & Realities--which was attended by senior trade 
     representatives from several African nations as well as 
     representatives of more than 75 businesses and organizations; 
     and
       Whereas, Ms. Brewster holds many degrees and certificates 
     which includes: a Bachelor of Science, a Master of Business 
     Administration, and an Advanced Professional Certificate 
     (APC) from New York University, as well as a Certified 
     Financial Planner (CFP) designation from Adelphi University. 
     Additionally, she holds a Florida real estate salesman 
     license. Annabel founded MGN Healthcare Services (MGN) to 
     provide much needed home health care services to the 
     vulnerable elderly and developmentally delayed populations in 
     Miami-Dade County and subsequently expanded into Broward 
     County. At almost the age of 60, she earned her nursing 
     degree and was certified as a Registered Nurse; and
       Whereas, Ms. Brewster's concern for at-risk children 
     prompted her to found Mainstreaming Plus, an after-school 
     program for middle-school at-risk kids. Mainstreaming Plus 
     served over 100 students within 6 years in the early to late 
     1990s. The program turned ``D'' students into ``A'' and ``B'' 
     students; and
       Whereas, due to Ms. Brewster's nurture of the Nigerian 
     community, she has been dubbed ``Area mother'' because of her 
     passionate involvement in political awareness, cultural 
     celebrations and varying activities and causes; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to recognize Ms. Annabel Brewster for her commitment, 
     dedication and service to our community and our country.

                          ____________________
```
