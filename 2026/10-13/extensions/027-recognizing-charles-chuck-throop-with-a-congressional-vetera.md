# RECOGNIZING CHARLES "CHUCK" THROOP WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
   RECOGNIZING CHARLES ``CHUCK'' THROOP WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Charles ``Chuck'' 
Throop of Hutto, Texas, by presenting him with a Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. His service to our country and dedication to serving his 
community truly represent the best of the best in our country.
  Charles Throop's primary occupational specialty was as a computer 
systems analyst with the United States Air Force. His service with the 
1137th USAF Special Activities Squadron is particularly noteworthy. 
During the Vietnam era, special activities squadrons supported highly 
sensitive communications, intelligence, command-and-control, and 
classified operations.
  Chuck's decorations and awards include Air Force Commendation Medal, 
National Defense Service Medal, Vietnam Service Medal, Republic of 
Vietnam Campaign Medal, Air Force Outstanding Unit Award, and 
Presidential Unit Citation.
  After seven years of honorable service in the Air Force, Chuck turned 
to the community, which he serves with the utmost dedication to this 
day in Hutto and the surrounding areas. Over the last year and a half, 
Chuck has served as the volunteer leader of the City of Hutto's 
Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging Commission's Senior Citizen 
Working Group. Chuck personally sought the assistance of churches, 
nonprofits, civic groups, and other related community partners.
  Chuck expanded his efforts beyond Hutto by attending regional 
conferences and meetings. Chuck partnered with the Hutto American 
Legion to secure a permanent meeting spot for the Senior Citizen 
Working Group. Chuck provided his own resources and time to events like 
Bingo night and several other social activities.
  He has personally staffed booths at resource fairs. recruiting 
volunteers and encouraging community participation. Not only in his 
efforts does Chuck encourage this participation, but by following 
Chuck, residents have been positively influenced to become informed, 
participate in the system, and contribute to their community.
  Chuck embodies what citizen leadership looks like, Through his 
discipline, optimism, humility, and infectious sense of humor, he has 
brought people together, built lasting friendships, and created a model 
for grassroots civic engagement that continues to inspire others.
  It is with great honor and respect that I present United States Air 
Force Special Activities Squadron Staff Sergeant E-4 Charles ``Chuck'' 
Throop with a Congressional Veteran Commendation from the 17th 
Congressional District of Texas. His community leadership reflects the 
same values of dedicated service that his military service did decades 
ago.
```
