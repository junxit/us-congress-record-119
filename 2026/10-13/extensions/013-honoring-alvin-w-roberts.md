# HONORING ALVIN W. ROBERTS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                       HONORING ALVIN W. ROBERTS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Alvin 
W. Roberts, a Vietnam Combat Veteran. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas, Mr. Alvin W. Roberts has dedicated over 45 years 
     of his life to serving veterans, disabled, elderly, and 
     children, championing and spearheading numerous commendable 
     and notable projects for the Vietnam Veterans of America, 
     Inc., locally, statewide, and nationally; and
       Whereas, Mr. Alvin W. Roberts, as a Life Member of Vietnam 
     Veterans of America, he has consistently gone above and 
     beyond in recruiting and retaining members, implementing 
     innovative systems to enhance youth activities, community 
     engagement, and Americanism programs; and
       Whereas, Mr. Roberts has held numerous positions within 
     Vietnam Veterans of America. Inc., including Vice President 
     of Chapter 1125, showcasing his commitment to the 
     organization's goals and objectives; and
       Whereas, Mr, Roberts played a pivotal role in the 
     establishment and success of the INVEST (Innovate and 
     Navigate Veterans' Employment Via Support and Training) 
     program, connecting veterans with disabilities to training 
     programs, support services, and jobs, setting an exemplary 
     standard for community service; and
       Whereas, Mr. Roberts is a role model and mentor within the 
     Miami-Dade County School System and has instilled Values of 
     Citizenship in the minds of many of our young people with 
     positive outcomes. Mr. Roberts' affiliation with Big 
     Brothers/Big Sisters of Miami and the 5,000 Role Models of 
     Excellence Programs has benefitted inner-city and at-risk 
     youth within the Tri-County Communities, of Miami-Dade, 
     Broward, and Palm Beach; and
       Whereas, he is a former employee, serving more than 35 
     years, in the Miami VA Healthcare System Office of 
     Administration as a Lead Medical Support Assistant. During 
     his tenure as a Federal Employee of the Miami VA, he was the 
     recipient of South Florida Federal Executive Board Federal 
     Employee of the Year in the Category of Administration and 
     again, he was the overall Federal Employee of the Year 
     Recipient In Service To The Community; and
       Whereas, Mr. Roberts has received numerous local, State, 
     and National awards for his outstanding service to the Miami-
     Dade County community. He has received awards from the 
     esteemed VVA FSC Member of the Year and VVA National Member 
     of the Year, awarded under the Past Presidency of Jerry 
     Klein. Notably, he received a Proclamation in 2019 from State 
     Legislator James Bush, underscoring the widespread 
     recognition of his impactful contributions. Vice President 
     Roberts has been a recipient of the Spirit of ADA, 
     acknowledging his instrumental role in the success of the 
     INVEST program; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     recognize Alvin W. Roberts for his outstanding service, 
     leadership, and dedication to veterans, the community, and 
     the mission of Vietnam Veterans of America. Inc.

                          ____________________
```
