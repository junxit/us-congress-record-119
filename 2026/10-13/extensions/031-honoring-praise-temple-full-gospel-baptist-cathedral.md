# HONORING PRAISE TEMPLE FULL GOSPEL BAPTIST CATHEDRAL

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
          HONORING PRAISE TEMPLE FULL GOSPEL BAPTIST CATHEDRAL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend and recognize Praise 
Temple Full Gospel Baptist Cathedral of Shreveport, Louisiana--an 
extraordinary ministry that has stood as a beacon of spiritual 
vitality, community service, and transformative leadership for more 
than thirty years. Founded on March 24, 1992, when Rev. Larry L. 
Brandon and six dedicated believers organized what was then Praise 
Temple Missionary Baptist Church, the congregation began humbly with 
prayer services and Bible study in a private home in Bossier City. 
These small gatherings reflected a deep hunger for spiritual fellowship 
and a shared belief that God was preparing them for a greater work.
  On April 19, 1992, the members held their first worship service at 
Midway Christian Church on Midway Avenue in Shreveport. As attendance 
flourished and the ministry quickly outgrew its space, the congregation 
moved through several temporary locations before finding a permanent 
home on Greenwood Road in 1996. This relocation marked a pivotal moment 
in the church's development, symbolizing both its growing membership 
and expanding vision for outreach in the Shreveport-Bossier community.
  In May 1997, the church embraced a new identity as Praise Temple Full 
Gospel Baptist Church following its affiliation with the Full Gospel 
Baptist Church Fellowship. This alignment brought renewed emphasis on 
spiritual empowerment, holistic ministry, and dynamic worship. As the 
ministry broadened in scope, the congregation later adopted the name 
Praise Temple Full Gospel Baptist Cathedral, reflecting its expanding 
regional influence and its increasing role as a spiritual center for 
thousands of believers.
  Praise Temple has long been recognized not only for its powerful 
worship and preaching but also for its unwavering commitment to 
community service. The church played a critical role during Hurricanes 
Katrina and Rita, mobilizing volunteers, sheltering displaced families, 
providing hot meals, clothing, transportation assistance, counseling, 
and financial support. lts response during these crises demonstrated 
the heart of its ministry--meeting people at their point of need and 
extending the love of Christ far beyond the sanctuary walls.
  Under the leadership of Bishop L. Lawrence Brandon, who now serves as 
Senior Pastor and CEO, Praise Temple continues to grow in membership, 
ministry impact, and regional visibility. Bishop Brandon's visionary 
guidance

has expanded the church's outreach, strengthened its presence in the 
Shreveport-Bossier area, and nurtured a spiritually vibrant 
congregation committed to worship, discipleship, and service. Through 
community initiatives, media outreach, youth engagement, and missions, 
Praise Temple remains a powerful force for hope, healing, and 
transformation.
  Mr. Speaker, Praise Temple Full Gospel Baptist Cathedral stands today 
as a testimony to what faith, perseverance, and dedicated leadership 
can accomplish. From a small prayer gathering in a living room to a 
thriving cathedral serving thousands, Praise Temple's story is a 
reminder of the remarkable things that happen when people follow God's 
call and serve their community with compassion. I urge my colleagues to 
join me in recognizing this exceptional ministry and its enduring 
impact on the Shreveport-Bossier community and beyond.
```
