# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF OFFICER STEVEN CORELY

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
         HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF OFFICER STEVEN CORELY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of a truly remarkable individual, Officer Steven Corley. I 
include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Steven Frederick Corley's journey began on 
     September 8, 1972, in Miami, Florida, born to Frederick 
     ``Freddie'' Corley and Priscilla Bush. He was a product of 
     Miami-Dade County Schools as a graduate of Miami Norland 
     Senior High's 1990 class. After graduating, he served in the 
     United States Army; and
       Whereas, Officer Corley got his start in law enforcement 
     with the Florida Department of Corrections from 1996 to 1998. 
     He went on to start a career with the Miami Dade Police 
     Department in 1998 working in the Carol City District. Years 
     later, he became a part of the Strategic & Specialized 
     Investigations Bureau in 2007. His final assignment was with 
     the Street Terror Offender Program (STOP). He was able to 
     investigate some of the county's most violent crimes. This 
     work led him to receive numerous awards; and
       Whereas, Officer Corley leaves to mourn his wife of 19 
     years, Trinese Corley; Children, Ryan Corley, Queenesha 
     Corley, and Queneetra Corley; Grandchildren: Jasmine Corley 
     and Martravius Smikle, Jr.; Mother: Priscilla Bush; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to recognize the late Officer Steven Corley, 
     celebrating his remarkable life and legacy of service, 
     leadership, and brotherhood, and committing to honor his 
     memory by continuing to uphold the principles for which he so 
     passionately advocated.

                          ____________________
```
