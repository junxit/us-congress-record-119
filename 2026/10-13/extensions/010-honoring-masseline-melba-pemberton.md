# HONORING MASSELINE MELBA PEMBERTON

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                   HONORING MASSELINE MELBA PEMBERTON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor the late Mrs. Masseline Melba Pemberton, a faithful servant of 
God, a community activist, a loving mother and grandmother, as well as 
a dutiful member of Bethany Seventh-day Adventist Church, I include in 
the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Mrs. Pemberton devoted herself to providing food 
     and clothing for the poor and homeless; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Pemberton, a member of the King's Daughters, 
     carried God's Word to the elderly through her missionary work 
     at nursing homes and senior citizen centers; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Pemberton was an inspiration to and strong 
     supporter of young women and men in South Florida and around 
     the country; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Pemberton was a constant presence in inner-
     city communities pointing out the needs and working to 
     improve the quality of life for all South Floridians; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Pemberton was an active member of her church 
     Bethany Seventh-day Adventist Church, where she held various 
     positions including, Deaconess, Teacher for the Youth Sabbath 
     School and Vacation Bible School; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend the late Mrs. Masseline Melba Pemberton for her 
     unwavering commitment and faithful service to our community, 
     to her family and friends, and to her church.

                          ____________________
```
