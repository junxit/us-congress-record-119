# HONORING MARTIN ROY EDMONDS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                      HONORING MARTIN ROY EDMONDS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Martin Roy 
Edmonds. I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Incorporated was 
     founded upon the principles of Brotherhood, Scholarship, and 
     Service, these values are exemplified by the extraordinary 
     life and contributions of Brother Martin Roy Edmonds; and
       Whereas, Martin Roy Edmonds was born on October 19, 1958, 
     in Bronx, New York, to Samuel and Lillie Edmonds, who 
     instilled in him a deep appreciation for Black history, 
     culture, and the pursuit of knowledge, shaping him into a man 
     of integrity and service; and
       Whereas, his educational journey led him to attend Florida 
     Memorial College (FMC), where he earned a Bachelor of Arts 
     degree in Business Management with a minor in Computer 
     Science, while actively contributing to the campus community 
     through roles such as treasurer of the freshman and sophomore 
     classes, editor of the student newsletter, and President of 
     the National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHC) at FMC: and
       Whereas, on December 6, 1980, Mr. Edmonds was initiated 
     into Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Incorporated through the Beta 
     Pi Chapter at FMC, where he demonstrated exemplary 
     leadership. serving as chapter president, vice president, and 
     business manager, among other roles, helping his chapter to 
     earn accolades such as ``Organization of the Year'' and 
     ``State and Regional Chapter of the Year''; and
       Whereas, throughout his career, Brother Edmonds continued 
     to embody the values of Phi Beta Sigma by remaining actively 
     involved with the fraternity, serving as a collegian advisor, 
     chartering the Rho Sigma Chapter's Sigma Beta Club, and 
     holding various leadership positions at the chapter, state, 
     and regional levels, including Southern Regional Director, 
     Chairman of the Regional Directors Council, and Deputy Chief 
     of Staff to the Honorable Chris V. Rey, International 
     President of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc.; and
       Whereas, Mr. Edmonds has made significant contributions to 
     his community through his involvement in numerous programs, 
     including SATAP (Sigma Against Teenage Pregnancy), Project 
     SADA (Sigma Against Domestic Abuse), Black Dollar Day, and 
     Voter Registration Drives, as well as his leadership in 
     creating two non-profit organizations, The Blue Dove 
     Community Foundation, Inc. and The TMB Charitable Foundation, 
     Inc., which have raised funds and provided critical support 
     to youth, collegiate brothers, and underserved communities; 
     and
       Whereas, his professional career in Miami-Dade County's 
     Information Technology Department spanned 37 years, where he 
     demonstrated leadership in technical support, cybersecurity, 
     and education, providing mentorship to youth and contributing 
     to the community's technological advancement; and
       Whereas, Brother Edmonds has served in leadership roles 
     within numerous organizations, including as President of the 
     National Pan-Hellenic Council of Miami, Worshipful Master of 
     San Salvador Lodge No. 603, and Guardian Ad Litem, advocating 
     for the rights and well-being of vulnerable children; and
       Whereas, his tireless efforts, unrelenting dedication, and 
     passion for service have been recognized with numerous 
     honors, including Gold and Platinum Life Membership in Phi 
     Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc., induction into the Florida 
     Sigmas Hall of Fame, and recognition as the 2023 Collegiate 
     Chapter Advisor of the Year at Florida International 
     University; and now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That I, Frederica S. Wilson, a Member of the 
     United States House of Representatives, representing the 24th 
     Congressional District of Florida, do hereby recognize and 
     celebrate Martin Roy Edmonds for his distinguished service, 
     unwavering commitment to the principles of Phi Beta Sigma 
     Fraternity, Incorporated, and his invaluable contributions to 
     the betterment of society.
```
