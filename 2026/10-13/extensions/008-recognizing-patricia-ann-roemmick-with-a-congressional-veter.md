# RECOGNIZING PATRICIA ANN ROEMMICK, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
    RECOGNIZING PATRICIA ANN ROEMMICK, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to present Patricia Ann Roemmick of 
China Spring, Texas, with a 2026 Congressional Veteran Commendation. 
Patricia's honorable military service and decades of dedication to her 
community, young people, and fellow veterans reflect the selflessness, 
compassion, and commitment that exemplify the very best of our Texas 
values.
  Patricia entered the United States Navy in October 1964 and served 
honorably through October 1967, attaining the rank of Communications 
Yeoman, CYN3 (E-4), Patricia served at the U.S. Naval Training Center 
in Bainbridge, Maryland, and at Naval Air Station Whiting Field in 
Milton, Florida. Patricia's responsibilities included serving as a PBX 
Operator, Telegraphic Operator, and Radioman.
  As computers were beginning to be incorporated into military 
operations, Patricia received additional training at a nearby college, 
which ultimately provided her with classified access to the Command 
Center. In that role, Patricia supported missions that included both 
successful and tragic Apollo missions, as well as numerous Navy flying 
units operating from Whiting Field.
  Patricia served during a particularly demanding period at Whiting 
Field, where Navy aviation training involved significant risks. 
Patricia's attention to detail and dedication to her duties contributed 
to the station being recognized as an Albany AESC Tributary of the 
Month after handling more than 1,000 messages without a single 
violation. Patricia's commanding officer specifically recognized her 
performance, pride in her work, and contribution to the station's 
achievement. Patricia received an Honorable Discharge in 1967 and was 
awarded the National Defense Service Medal.
  Patricia's commitment to service continued after her military career. 
Following her discharge, Patricia and her husband, Gary, moved to 
Arlington, Texas, where she worked in marketing for the Marriott hotel 
chain for eleven years. In 1984, they moved to China Spring, where 
Patricia became a teacher's aide for the local school district. During 
her time with China Spring schools, she actively supported the PTA, 
athletic programs, 4-H, Future Farmers of America, and Future 
Homemakers of America. Patricia's dedication to the school community 
was formally recognized with a Certificate of Appreciation for 
outstanding service and contributions to China Spring Elementary 
School.
  Patricia has also devoted countless hours to supporting young people 
and strengthening her community. Beginning in 2018, she became involved 
with the China Spring Young Farmers Fund, helping raise money to assist 
young farmers with livestock shows and state meetings and supporting 
efforts to build the China Spring Agriculture Barn. Patricia and her 
husband have remained active members of the China Spring Athletic 
Booster Club and have volunteered with Meals on Wheels, including 
stocking shelves for Christmas deliveries and providing financial 
support.
  Through the China Spring Civics Club, Patricia has supported numerous 
community initiatives, including preservation of the China Spring 
Tabernacle historical site, China Spring Cares, Isaiah 117 House, 
student memorial scholarships, Christmas Families, Meals on Wheels, and 
fundraisers for the China Spring Volunteer Fire Department. Patricia 
has also collected blankets for the blind at Doris Miller Medical 
Center and blankets for distribution during Veterans Day parades. 
Patricia's service has extended to Brookdale Nursing Home, where she 
has helped with Valentine's Day activities and distributed lap blankets 
to military veterans residing there.
  Patricia's dedication to veterans remains an important part of her 
service today. Through

[[Page E1070]]

the Heart of Texas Behavioral Health Network's Waco Veterans One-Stop, 
Patricia volunteers at the front desk, welcomes and assists veterans 
and other visitors, helps with meals and special events such as 
Thanksgiving, Veterans Day, and Memorial Day, participates in the 
Veterans Day Parade, and delivers lunches to residents at Ridgecrest 
Retirement Home. Patricia's continued willingness to serve veterans and 
those in need demonstrates that her commitment to service did not end 
with her military discharge.
  Patricia and her husband, Gary, are the proud parents of two adult 
children, Robin and Jason. Throughout her life, Patricia has 
consistently demonstrated a willingness to give her time, talents, and 
resources to others. From her honorable service in the United States 
Navy to her work with children and schools, support for agriculture and 
community organizations, and continued service to veterans, she has 
built a legacy defined by compassion, reliability, and community 
spirit.
  It is this lifetime of honorable military service, volunteerism, and 
devotion to her community, young people, and fellow veterans that I 
proudly commend Patricia Ann Roemmick of China Spring, Texas, to her 
friends, family, community, and country. Patricia's continued 
commitment to serving others embodies the spirit of the Congressional 
Veteran Commendation, and it is my honor to recognize her with the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation from the 17th Congressional District 
of Texas.
```
