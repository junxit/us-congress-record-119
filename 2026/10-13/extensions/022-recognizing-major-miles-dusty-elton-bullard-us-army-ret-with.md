# RECOGNIZING MAJOR MILES "DUSTY" ELTON BULLARD, US ARMY, (RET.), WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
RECOGNIZING MAJOR MILES ``DUSTY'' ELTON BULLARD, US ARMY, (RET.), WITH 
                  A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to present Major Miles 
``Dusty'' Bullard, U.S. Army, of Pflugerville, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. Dusty's career and dedication to 
his community upon retirement exemplify that love of country and 
neighbor upon which America was founded.
  Born in Hammond, Indiana, Dusty joined the United States Army in 
1966. Dusty began basic training at Fort Campbell, Kentucky, and then 
headed to Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, for heavy equipment and 
demolition training. He was then assigned to an engineer unit at Fort 
Lewis, Washington, with 6th Army, and then to Fort Meade, Maryland, 
with 1st Army. He received orders for Vietnam in April 1968, arriving 
in June of that year.
  Dusty was assigned to the 20th Engineer Brigade, 544th CS Company, 
and 94th Engineer Detachment, where he worked on various construction 
projects as well as airfield repairs, and was promoted to Sergeant E-5. 
His last job was as a Night Operations Sergeant, and after a 16-month 
tour, he returned to Indiana. Dusty served in the U.S. Army Reserve and 
Army National Guard until 1993.
  In 1990, Dusty was called to active duty for Desert Shield/Desert 
Storm and was assigned to Headquarters, 3rd Army, at Fort McPherson, 
Georgia. They shipped to Saudi Arabia, and he was assigned to 
Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 22nd Support Command, Theater 
Army. Dusty's 90-day tour lasted 10 \1/2\ months, during which he 
worked with Logistics Engineer Operations as a Construction and 
Operations Staff Engineer Officer. For this work, Dusty was awarded the 
Bronze Star for meritorious service and was promoted to Major.
  After his retirement two years later, Dusty's service continued, now 
as a dutiful citizen. Dusty is a charter member and officer of Kerlin-
Lyerly American Legion Post 154 and is also an officer of Lone Star 
Chapter 4 of the Disabled American Veterans. He is a member of the VFW 
and is an active and instrumental member of the newly formed 
Pflugerville Veterans Advisory Taskforce.
  Dusty has assisted with taking veterans to and from VA clinics in 
Austin and Cedar Park. As a certified Service Officer with the DAV, he 
assisted several veterans with claims assistance. Dusty is very active 
in supporting the two local JROTC programs at Hendrickson and Connally 
High Schools. Furthermore, he supports the Pflugerville Police 
Department's Operation Blue Santa, a community program providing toys 
and gifts to those in need.
  Dusty lost his beautiful wife of over 40 years, Janice, on August 
20th of this year. He has six children: Aaron, Dustin, Chad, Christeen, 
Yvonne, and Danette, two of whom, Christeen and Yvonne, are veterans 
themselves. Miles has 10 grandchildren and two great-grandchildren. 
Dusty is a member of First Baptist Church in Pflugerville.
  It is with great honor and respect that I present United States Army 
Major Miles ``Dusty'' Bullard with a Congressional Veteran Commendation 
from the 17th Congressional District of Texas. His longstanding and 
devout commitment to the United States of America typifies patriotism 
and service.
```
