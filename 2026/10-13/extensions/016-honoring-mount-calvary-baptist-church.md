# HONORING MOUNT CALVARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING MOUNT CALVARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mount Calvary Baptist 
Church of Mansfield, Louisiana, a valued congregation and community 
institution located at 329 Porter Street. Under the leadership of 
Pastor Williams and the dedicated service of its members, Mount Calvary 
Baptist Church continues to provide worship, fellowship, and ministry 
to the people of Mansfield and DeSoto Parish.
  The historical roots of the original Mount Calvary Baptist Church are 
connected to the early-twentieth-century movement to establish African 
American Baptist congregations in communities where such spaces were 
needed. Across generations, the church has developed as a 
multigenerational and multicultural beacon, welcoming people from many 
walks of life and seeking to strengthen the bonds of faith and 
community.
  The name ``Mount Calvary'' honors Calvary, or Golgotha, the biblical 
hill outside Jerusalem where Jesus Christ was crucified. This name 
reflects the church's emphasis on the sacrifice, salvation, grace, and 
redemption that form the foundation of its Christian faith.
  Mount Calvary Baptist Church has also demonstrated faithful 
stewardship through its commitment to improving its facilities. During 
a period of leadership transition, including major improvements 
advanced under Pastor Williams, the congregation undertook Phase I 
renovations focused on the lobby and main sanctuary. These efforts 
included hand-cleaning the historic interior brick walls, updating the 
structural paint, and installing a new pulpit.
  The congregation completed the $87,000 Phase I renovation project 
entirely debt-free, reflecting careful planning, shared commitment, and 
responsible financial stewardship. This accomplishment stands as a 
testament to the generosity and dedication of Mount Calvary Baptist 
Church's members and supporters.
  Mount Calvary Baptist Church is grounded in fundamental, Bible-based 
Baptist doctrines. The church affirms one true and living God, 
eternally existing in three persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy 
Spirit and teaches that salvation is the free gift of divine grace 
received through faith in Jesus Christ, apart from human works.
  The church recognizes the Old and New Testaments as the inspired and 
infallible Word of God and observes the two ordinances of the Christian 
faith: believers' baptism by full immersion and the Lord's Supper. It 
also affirms the local church as an autonomous body of baptized 
believers called to carry out the Great Commission.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Pastor 
Williams and the faithful congregation of Mount Calvary Baptist Church 
for their enduring commitment to worship, stewardship, ministry, and 
community fellowship. May Mount Calvary Baptist Church continue to 
serve as a place of faith, welcome, and hope for the people of 
Mansfield and for generations to come.
```
