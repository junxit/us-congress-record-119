# RECOGNIZING GARY DEAN ROEMMICK, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
     RECOGNIZING GARY DEAN ROEMMICK, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to present Gary Dean Roemmick of 
China Spring, Texas, with a 2026 Congressional Veteran Commendation. 
Gary's honorable military service, decades of professional leadership, 
and continued commitment to serving his community and fellow veterans 
reflect the hard work, generosity, and dedication that exemplify the 
very best of our East Texas values.
  Gary entered the United States Navy in December 1965 and served 
through 1971, attaining the rank of Aviation Mechanic, ADR3, E-4. 
Gary's duty stations included San Diego, California; Naval Air Station 
Millington, Tennessee; and Naval Air Station Whiting Field in Milton, 
Florida. As a Plane Captain with the VT-2 Training Squadron, Gary was 
responsible for pre-flight and post-flight inspections and servicing of 
T-28 Trojan aircraft. Gary oversaw thousands of takeoffs and landings 
and coordinated necessary followup maintenance. Gary's responsibilities 
also included assisting with the recovery of several flight training 
accidents in which officers in training were lost. Gary received an 
honorable discharge in 1967 and continued serving in the Inactive 
Reserve until 1971 and his military service was recognized with the 
National Defense Service Medal and Good Conduct Medal.
  Following his military service, Gary began a long and successful 
career in the private sector. Gary worked for Delta Airlines at Dallas 
Love Field as a Ramp Service Agent before joining Payless Cashway, Inc. 
in 1972. Beginning in floor sales, Gary advanced into management and 
ultimately served as a Store Manager for many years. In 1984, Gary 
transferred to Waco, where he continued as a Payless Cashways Store 
Manager until 2001. Gary's management skills and dedication were 
recognized through twelve Gold and Silver Achievement Awards during his 
career with the company. Gary later served as Store Manager for Waco 
Insulation Supply Company from 2001 to 2008.
  Gary's commitment to service became especially evident after he and 
his wife, Patricia, established roots in the China Spring community. 
Gary has been actively involved with the China Spring Lions Club, 
participating in fundraisers, the Fall Festival, school scholarship 
programs, and other community service projects. Gary has also served 
with the China Spring Youth Association as a board member, equipment 
manager, and youth athletics coach, as well as supporting the China 
Spring Athletic Booster Club and Future Farmers of America Boosters, 
where he helped students with animals and livestock shows.
  Since 2023, Gary has continued his service to others through his 
volunteer work with the Waco Veterans One-Stop, sponsored by the Heart 
of Texas Behavioral Health Network. Gary has become a dependable source 
of assistance for local veterans, helping prepare, serve, and clean up 
after weekly and special occasion meals. Gary has delivered meals to 
veterans at Ridgecrest Retirement and Assisted Living and has provided 
transportation for veterans traveling to and from the Veterans One-Stop 
to meet with counselors and Veteran Service Officers. Gary also 
coordinates free haircuts for veterans and, when needed, has 
volunteered to mow the yards of veterans who require assistance.
  Gary's service extends to special events and activities designed to 
strengthen the veteran community. Gary has helped organize and support 
Thanksgiving, Christmas, Veterans Day, and Memorial Day meals at the

[[Page E1073]]

Veterans One-Stop and played an active role in a fishing tournament 
that brought together disabled and able-bodied veterans for fellowship 
and recreation. Gary's willingness to assist wherever needed has made 
him a trusted and valued volunteer, with a simple philosophy: when 
veterans need assistance, Gary steps up.
  Gary's lifetime of service was further recognized when he received a 
custom-made Quilt of Valor honoring his military and community service. 
From his years serving his country in the United States Navy to his 
decades of professional leadership and his continued volunteer work 
supporting veterans, young people, schools, and neighbors, Gary has 
consistently demonstrated a willingness to give his time and talents to 
others.
  It is this lifetime of honorable military service, leadership, 
volunteerism, and devotion to his community and fellow veterans that I 
proudly commend Gary Dean Roemmick of China Spring, Texas, to his 
friends, family, community, and country. His continued commitment to 
serving others embodies the spirit of the Congressional Veteran 
Commendation, and it is my honor to recognize him with the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation from the 17th Congressional District 
of Texas.
```
