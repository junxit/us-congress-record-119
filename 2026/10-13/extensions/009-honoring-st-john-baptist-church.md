# HONORING ST. JOHN BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                    HONORING ST. JOHN BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor St. John Baptist 
Church of Mansfield, Louisiana, a historic congregation and valued 
community institution located at 900 Johnson Street. Under the 
leadership of Rev. Isiah D. Anderson, Sr., St. John Baptist Church 
continues to provide worship, fellowship, and community ministry to the 
people of Mansfield and DeSoto Parish.
  The congregation's roots extend to the late nineteenth century. 
Historical records, including William Hicks's A History of Louisiana 
Negro Baptists, document baptisms taking place at the Mansfield 
congregation as early as 1878, underscoring the church's longstanding 
presence and significance in the community.
  St. John Baptist Church was also shaped by the influential ministry 
of Elder and Bishop I. A. Carter. During his years of service to the 
Mansfield church in the late 1800s, he directed the construction of 
several houses of worship and baptized hundreds of individuals, laying 
a strong spiritual foundation for future generations.
  For nearly 150 years, St. John Baptist Church has embodied the faith, 
perseverance, and dedication of the people it serves. Despite the 
passage of time and changing circumstances, the congregation has 
remained devoted to worship and to offering a spiritual home where 
individuals and families can come together, deepen their faith, and 
encourage one another.
  The story of St. John Baptist Church further demonstrates the 
essential role religious institutions play in preserving community 
identity. Its enduring record of worship, baptism, leadership, and 
congregational fellowship reflects the ways churches have strengthened 
families and nurtured hope in communities across Louisiana.
  Today, St. John Baptist Church continues its ministry from its 
Johnson Street location through worship services and community 
outreach. The congregation also shares its message through its official 
website and St. John Baptist Church Facebook page, extending its 
ministry to members and supporters both near and far.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Rev. Isiah 
D. Anderson, Sr. and the devoted congregation of St. John Baptist 
Church for their lasting contributions through worship, leadership, 
service, and community ministry. May St. John Baptist Church continue 
to offer faith, fellowship, and strength to the people of Mansfield and 
to generations yet to come.
```
