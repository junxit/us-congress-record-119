# HONORING MATTHEW BUELTEL'S SERVICE AS SENIOR OUTREACH REPRESENTATIVE

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
  HONORING MATTHEW BUELTEL'S SERVICE AS SENIOR OUTREACH REPRESENTATIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate 
Matthew Bueltel for an amazing two years with my office, first as an 
intern and most recently as my Senior Outreach Representative.
  Matthew first joined my team as an intern in 2024 and came on full-
time in 2025. He is a self-starter and is always willing to go the 
extra mile. Since coming on board, Matthew has helped to organize over 
30 events in the district and throughout this Congress, has attended 
over 50 events out in the community. His positivity, proactivity and 
relationship building skills have been assets in his role.
  As a Minnesota native, Matthew cares deeply about local politics and 
this is shown through his work. I know his approachable nature and 
adaptability will serve him well in his next chapter.
  I look forward to seeing what Matthew does next and I wish him the 
best in his future endeavors.
```
