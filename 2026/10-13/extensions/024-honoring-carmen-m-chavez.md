# HONORING CARMEN M. CHAVEZ

**Speaking:** Vargas, Juan (V000130) [D-CA]

```
                       HONORING CARMEN M. CHAVEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JUAN VARGAS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. VARGAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Carmen M. Chavez, 
Esq., Executive Director of Casa Cornelia Law Center, and recognize her 
as our October Constituent of the Month.
  For over three decades, Carmen has been dedicated to serving some of 
the most vulnerable members of our San Diego community. Carmen began 
her career with Casa Cornelia in 1995 as a volunteer. She went on to 
serve as a paralegal, law clerk, attorney, and Associate Director 
before becoming Executive Director in 2007.
  Throughout her career, Carmen has worked tirelessly to advance access 
to justice and ensure that individuals who otherwise could not afford 
legal representation have an opportunity

[[Page E1075]]

to understand and exercise their rights under the law. Under her 
leadership, Casa Cornelia has continued its mission of providing pro 
bono legal services to asylum seekers, unaccompanied children, 
survivors of human trafficking and domestic violence, and other victims 
of serious crimes.
  Her leadership extends well beyond the courtroom. Those who have 
worked alongside Carmen describe her efforts to build a culture of 
service that brings together attorneys, law students, interpreters, 
translators, community partners, and volunteers around the principle of 
justice with compassion. In 2025 alone, Casa Cornelia assisted more 
than 2,500 individuals from 91 countries, providing services across 
more than 58 languages.
  Carmen's contributions to our region have also been recognized 
throughout the community. In 2020, the City of San Diego proclaimed 
November 17th as ``Carmen M. Chavez, Esq.'s 25th Anniversary at Casa 
Cornelia Law Center Day'' in recognition of her years of service. Most 
recently, she was recognized as a recipient of a 2026 Prebys Leadership 
Award.
  Carmen has devoted her professional life to serving others, 
strengthening access to justice, and upholding the dignity of 
individuals facing extraordinarily difficult circumstances. I am 
honored to recognize Carmen for her compassion, integrity, and 
sustained commitment to the San Diego community.
```
