# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF OFFICER ARTHUR MARTINEAU

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
        HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF OFFICER ARTHUR MARTINEAU

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of Officer Arthur Martineau. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas, Arthur Walter ``Big Art'' Martineau was born on 
     October 31, 1970, in Brooklyn, New York, and moved to Miami 
     Beach in the 1980s, where he began a lifelong mission of 
     giving back to his community; and
       Whereas, Arthur Martineau, driven by the experiences of his 
     youth in Brooklyn and the absence of police presence in his 
     neighborhood, embarked on a distinguished law enforcement 
     career in 1997 with the City of Miami Beach Police 
     Department, earning a reputation for unwavering dedication to 
     public service and community engagement; and
       Whereas, Officer Martineau was appointed Executive Director 
     of the Miami Beach Police Athletic League (PAL) in 2010, 
     where he worked tirelessly to prevent juvenile delinquency 
     and provide opportunities for young people in Miami Beach, 
     creating transformative programs that changed countless lives 
     for the better; and
       Whereas, Arthur Martineau honorably served his country as a 
     proud veteran of the United States Air Force and pursued his 
     education with great determination, earning a bachelor's 
     degree in criminal justice and law enforcement administration 
     from the Union Institute and University: and
       Whereas, throughout his distinguished career, Officer 
     Martineau received numerous accolades, including the City of 
     Miami Beach Employee of the Year award in 2018, and on May 
     25th of that year, the Mayor and City Commission of Miami 
     Beach declared it ``Officer Art and Deborah Martineau Day'' 
     in recognition of his exemplary dedication to his community; 
     and
       Whereas, Officer Martineau's leadership and vision extended 
     beyond his professional responsibilities, mentoring aspiring 
     law enforcement officers, coaching youth football, and 
     helping numerous teenagers secure full scholarships to 
     prestigious universities, leaving an indelible impact on the 
     lives of those he served; and
       Whereas, Officer Martineau was known for his charm, wit, 
     and love of life, embracing simple pleasures such as oatmeal 
     cookies, family vacations to Jamaica, movies, and cheering 
     for his beloved New York Jets; and
       Whereas, despite being diagnosed with esophageal cancer in 
     September 2023, Officer Martineau demonstrated extraordinary 
     courage and resilience, continuing his role as Executive 
     Director of Miami Beach PAL and serving his community with 
     steadfast passion and dedication until his passing on January 
     23, 2025; and
       Whereas, Officer Martineau leaves behind his devoted wife, 
     Officer Deborah Martineau, who continues to serve the Miami 
     Beach Police Department with distinction, their five beloved 
     children--Antrena Martineau, Nichole Doty, Demetrius Smith, 
     Alexis Martineau, and Arthur Martineau, III--and a vast 
     network of friends, colleagues, and mentees who were 
     profoundly impacted by his wisdom, kindness, and unwavering 
     commitment to improving the lives of others; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of

[[Page E1078]]

     Florida, am honored to recognize the life of Officer Arthur 
     Walter ``Big Art'' Martineau will forever inspire those who 
     follow in his footsteps to serve with honor, integrity, and 
     love for their community.

                          ____________________
```
