# HONORING MAMIE L. CUTLER

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                        HONORING MAMIE L. CUTLER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor the late Mrs. Mamie L. Cutler, a faithful servant of God, a 
community activist, a loving mother and grandmother, as well as a 
dutiful member of the Liberty City Church of Christ. I include in the 
Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Mrs. Cutler gave willingly and often to the 
     children of the Scott Projects,

[[Page E1069]]

     providing them hot meals and her famous frozen cups at her 
     family business, Cutler Frozen Cup House; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Cutler was a devoted member of the Liberty 
     City Church of Christ for 75 years, and one of its first 
     worshipers; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Cutler served on the church's Mothers Board 
     providing leadership and inspiration to the congregation; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Cutler believed in a strong tight-knit 
     community--neighbor looking after neighbor, which is evident 
     by her 41 year-long friendship with her non-English speaking 
     neighbor; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend the late Mrs. Mamie L. Cutler for her unwavering 
     commitment and faithful service to our community, to her 
     family and friends, and to her church.

                          ____________________
```
