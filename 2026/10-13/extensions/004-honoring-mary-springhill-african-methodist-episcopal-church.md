# HONORING MARY SPRINGHILL AFRICAN METHODIST EPISCOPAL CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
      HONORING MARY SPRINGHILL AFRICAN METHODIST EPISCOPAL CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mary Springhill 
African Methodist Episcopal Church of Shreveport, Louisiana, a historic 
congregation and steadfast community institution located at 4426 
Linwood Avenue. Under the leadership of Dr. Lorenzo Neal and the 
Central North Louisiana Conference within the 8th Episcopal District of 
the African Methodist Episcopal Church, Mary Springhill AME continues 
to serve as a beacon of faith, fellowship, worship, and community 
engagement.
  Mary Springhill AME Church recently celebrated its 127th church 
anniversary in late 2025, reflecting a history that reaches back to 
approximately 1898. For more than a century, the congregation has 
provided generations of worshippers with a spiritual home where 
individuals and families can gather, grow in faith, and support one 
another through life's joys and challenges.
  The designation ``African Methodist Episcopal'' carries profound 
historical meaning. The denomination was organized by people of African 
descent in response to racial discrimination within the white Methodist 
Episcopal Church and was legally established in 1816 by Richard Allen 
in Philadelphia. ``Methodist'' reflects the church's doctrinal roots, 
while ``Episcopal'' describes its governance by bishops.
  The name ``Mary Springhill`` also reflects the ways in which African 
American congregations have preserved faith, family, and local history 
in their names. ``Springhill`` may refer to the location of an original 
church site, a natural landmark, or a historic community settlement, 
while ``Mary'' may honor a biblical figure or an important person 
connected to the congregation's founding and heritage.
  As a traditional AME congregation, Mary Springhill is grounded in a 
Wesleyan-Arminian theological tradition. The church affirms the Holy 
Trinity--God the Father, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Ghost--as one 
eternal God, as well as the true resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  Mary Springhill AME also upholds the Holy Scriptures, accepting the 
canonical books of the Old and New Testaments as containing all things 
necessary for human salvation. Its ministry emphasizes grace, 
forgiveness, inclusivity, and the opportunity for individuals to 
receive second chances through the teachings of Jesus Christ.
  The congregation further demonstrates that faith is expressed through 
action. Through community service, social empowerment, outreach, 
traditional hymns, contemporary worship, and youth ministries, Mary 
Springhill AME seeks to strengthen families, encourage young people, 
and respond to the needs of the Shreveport community.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring Dr. 
Lorenzo Neal and the faithful congregation of Mary Springhill African 
Methodist Episcopal Church for more than a century of worship, 
perseverance, service, and Christian witness. May Mary Springhill AME 
continue to be a source of hope, spiritual growth, community 
empowerment, and fellowship for the people of Shreveport and for 
generations to come.
```
