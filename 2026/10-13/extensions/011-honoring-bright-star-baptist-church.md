# HONORING BRIGHT STAR BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING BRIGHT STAR BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Bright Star Baptist 
Church of Shreveport, Louisiana, an enduring institution of Christian 
faith, worship, fellowship, and community service for more than a 
century. Founded in 1922, the church has remained committed to 
providing a spiritual home where individuals and families can worship 
God, strengthen their faith, build meaningful relationships, and serve 
others, grounded in its mission of loving God, loving one another, and 
loving those who are lost.
  Since its establishment, Bright Star has been built on a strong 
foundation of faith and Christian service. Through worship, prayer, 
biblical teaching, fellowship, outreach, and service, the congregation 
has provided spiritual guidance and encouragement while responding to 
the needs of individuals and families throughout the surrounding 
community. For more than 100 years, generations of families have 
entered its doors to worship, pray, learn, celebrate, grieve, and find 
encouragement, and through periods of social, economic, and cultural 
change the church has remained a place of stability, fellowship, and 
spiritual support.
  Worship has remained at the heart of Bright Star's ministry. Through 
regular services, prayer, preaching, music, and Christian fellowship, 
members have grown spiritually and formed a strong sense of community. 
Bible study and Christian education have likewise equipped members with 
the spiritual foundation to meet the challenges of everyday life and 
have prepared future generations to become leaders in the church and 
community. Prayer has been another foundation of the congregation, 
giving members strength and encouragement in moments of joy and in 
times of hardship.
  The church's ministry has consistently extended beyond its worship 
services. Bright Star has shown a commitment to outreach and community 
involvement by seeking opportunities to meet practical needs and assist 
individuals and families, including community clothing giveaways that 
provide tangible support to its neighbors. These efforts reflect the 
church's belief that Christian ministry includes both sharing the 
message of faith and demonstrating that faith through service, and they 
continue the tradition of African American churches and other faith 
institutions in Shreveport that have served as centers of worship while 
also providing support, leadership, fellowship, education, and 
assistance.
  For many families, Bright Star has been much more than a place to 
attend church services. It has been a spiritual home where 
relationships are formed, families are strengthened, and generations 
have celebrated baptisms, marriages, anniversaries, and accomplishments 
and gathered for homegoing services. The church's longevity is a 
testament to the pastors, ministers, deacons, ministry leaders, 
musicians, teachers, volunteers, families, and faithful members who 
have given their time, talents, prayers, and resources to sustain its 
ministry for those who follow.
  An important chapter in the church's modern history began in 1997, 
when Pastor Patrick L.

[[Page E1071]]

Dennis began serving as pastor. His tenure, now spanning multiple 
decades, has given the congregation continuity in spiritual leadership 
as it entered a new century of ministry. Under his leadership, Bright 
Star has continued to emphasize worship, Bible study, prayer, Christian 
fellowship, outreach, and involvement in the community, building on the 
work of previous generations while focusing on the spiritual 
development of its members and the needs of the people it is called to 
serve.
  Today, Bright Star Baptist Church remains committed to worship, Bible 
study, prayer, fellowship, evangelism, outreach, and service, and 
continues to seek opportunities to make a positive difference in the 
lives of individuals and families throughout Shreveport. As new 
generations become part of the congregation, they inherit a legacy of 
faith, sacrifice, perseverance, and service, and the responsibility of 
carrying that mission forward through Christian education, the 
development of future leaders, and continued outreach.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Bright Star 
Baptist Church and its congregation for more than a century of faith 
and service to the people of Shreveport. Guided by its mission of 
loving God, loving one another, and loving those who are lost, may 
Bright Star continue to stand as a beacon of faith, hope, fellowship, 
and service for generations to come.
```
