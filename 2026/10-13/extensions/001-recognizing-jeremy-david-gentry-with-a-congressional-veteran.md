# RECOGNIZING JEREMY DAVID GENTRY WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
     RECOGNIZING JEREMY DAVID GENTRY WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to present Jeremy David Gentry of 
Groveton, Texas, with a Congressional Veteran Commendation. Jeremy 
Gentry is a proud United States Army Veteran and represents the very 
best of what it means to serve one's country and one's community.
  A native of Deep East Texas, Jeremy was born and raised in the 
community of Friday, Texas, where his family has deep roots. At just 18 
years old, Jeremy answered the call to serve his country. He served in 
the United States Army from 1995 until 2004, including service during 
the Global War on Terror. His military service is distinguished by both 
dedication and achievement. Among his honors are multiple Army 
Commendation Medals, Army Achievement Medals, the Army Good Conduct 
Medal, the National Defense Service Medal, the Global War on Terrorism 
Medal, and the Armed Forces Service Medal.
  Jeremy's commitment to service did not end when he returned home. 
After completing his military service, Jeremy earned his bachelor's 
degree, teaching certification, and began a career dedicated to 
educating young people in Groveton. For approximately 17 years, he has 
served Groveton ISD as an educator and today serves as the district 
librarian while continuing to teach college courses to Groveton High 
School students.
  Jeremy has also devoted 17 years to the school's University 
Interscholastic League program and has served as the High Schools 
Coordinator since 2023. Under his leadership, Groveton students have 
achieved an extraordinary four state championships, an impressive 
accomplishment for a small rural school district. In 2026, Jeremy was 
recognized with the UIL Sponsor Excellence Award. His dedication to his 
students extends far beyond the classroom. Jeremy routinely gives his 
own time to help students succeed, including working with them before 
and after school and using his lunch breaks to provide additional 
instruction and tutoring. He has worked to improve the school library 
and has found books, furniture, games, and other resources for students 
without placing an additional financial burden on the school district. 
When he saw that a ramp used by special education students needed 
repair, Jeremy took the initiative to secure funding and, together with 
his students, rebuilt the ramp. He also led efforts to improve the 
appearance of Groveton High School by creating a flower bed and helping 
make the school a more welcoming place.
  Alongside his daughter, Jeremy established an annual book drive so 
children could select books from the elementary library to take home 
and read at no cost. He also helped establish a community book stand at 
a local doctor's office, allowing people to borrow and return books on 
an honor system. His service to others has likewise been evident 
through his church and his community. For 16 years, Jeremy served as a 
youth ministry leader and worship leader at his local Baptist church. 
He remains involved in the church's music ministry today.
  Those who know Jeremy describe him not only as an accomplished 
veteran, educator, and community volunteer, but as a person of 
character, integrity, humility, and compassion. His superintendent, Jim 
Dillard, described Jeremy as someone who consistently goes above and 
beyond for his students and colleagues. After working with educators 
across five school districts and in numerous roles, Superintendent 
Dillard called Jeremy his ``most valuable player.'' That is a powerful 
testament to a life dedicated to serving others
  The Congressional Veteran Commendation is intended to recognize 
veterans whose service continues long after their military careers have 
ended. Jeremy is an outstanding example of that tradition. He answered 
the call of his country as a young man. When he returned home, he 
continued answering the call of his community--through education, 
mentorship, faith, volunteerism, and an unwavering commitment to the 
young people of Groveton and Trinity County. Jeremy has spent his life 
planting what one nominator described as ``little seeds of hope and 
positivity'' wherever he goes. Those seeds have grown into stronger 
students, a stronger school, and a stronger community.
  I am proud to recognize Jeremy Gentry for his military service to our 
Nation and, just as importantly, for the many years of service he has 
given to the people of Deep East Texas. On behalf of the people of 
Texas' 17th Congressional District, I thank Jeremy for his service, his 
sacrifice, and his continued commitment to others. Mr. Speaker, please 
join me in congratulating Jeremy Gentry on receiving the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation.
  May God bless Jeremy Gentry, his family, the great state of Texas, 
and the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
