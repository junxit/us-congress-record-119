# HONORING CATHEDRAL OF SAINT JOHN BERCHMANS

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING CATHEDRAL OF SAINT JOHN BERCHMANS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

   Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Cathedral of 
Saint John Berchmans of Shreveport, Louisiana, one of the city's 
historic religious institutions and an enduring center of Catholic 
faith, education, worship, and community service. Established in 1902, 
the parish traces its beginnings to Jesuit priests who came to serve 
the growing Catholic population of Shreveport. In the more than 120 
years since, Saint John Berchmans has grown from a local parish and 
school into the spiritual center of the Roman Catholic Diocese of 
Shreveport.
  The parish was founded during a period of significant growth in 
Shreveport, as new families made the city their home and the need for 
institutions of worship, education, and community life grew. The Jesuit 
priests responded by establishing a parish where families could gather 
for Mass, receive the sacraments, educate their children in the 
Catholic tradition, and build fellowship. The original parish included 
both a church and a school on Texas Avenue, and from its earliest years 
the close relationship between worship and education has been a 
defining characteristic of Saint John Berchmans.
  As Shreveport developed, so did the parish, and increasing membership 
created the need for a larger and more permanent church. In 1927, the 
cornerstone of the present church was laid, and the building was 
completed and consecrated in 1929. It stands as a testament to the 
faith, planning, generosity, and sacrifice of the parishioners and 
church leaders who envisioned a permanent spiritual home for 
generations of Catholics. Its historic design, sacred artwork, stained 
glass, and religious symbols reflect the rich heritage of the Catholic 
faith and serve as a visible reminder of the clergy, parishioners, 
families, and educators who have built and preserved this community.
  For generations, Saint John Berchmans has been a spiritual home where 
Catholics have celebrated baptisms, First Communions, confirmations, 
marriages, ordinations, anniversaries, funerals, and other milestones 
of faith and family life, binding the Cathedral to the personal 
histories of countless families throughout Shreveport and Northwest 
Louisiana. Its ministry has also extended beyond worship through 
charitable outreach, religious education, pastoral care, family 
ministries, and service initiatives that address both the spiritual and 
practical needs of individuals and families. In keeping with the 
Catholic tradition, education and faith formation have helped prepare 
young people to become responsible members of their churches, families, 
professions, and communities.
  A major milestone came in 1986 with the establishment of the Roman 
Catholic Diocese of Shreveport, when Saint John Berchmans was elevated 
to the status of cathedral. As the cathedral church of the diocese, it 
became the spiritual seat of the Catholic Church in Northwest Louisiana 
and a central gathering place for major diocesan celebrations, 
liturgies, ordinations, and ceremonies. This role extended the 
Cathedral's reach beyond its own parish and made it a spiritual home 
for Catholics from communities throughout the region.
  The Cathedral has been strengthened throughout its history by 
generations of priests, deacons, religious educators, teachers, parish 
staff, ministry leaders, musicians, volunteers, families, and 
parishioners. Through decades of change in Shreveport, in times of 
prosperity and uncertainty, celebration and hardship, it has remained a 
consistent spiritual presence where individuals can seek prayer, 
guidance, fellowship, and a deeper relationship with God.
  Today, the Cathedral of Saint John Berchmans continues its historic 
mission under the leadership of Reverend Raney Johnson, Rector. It 
remains committed to reverent worship, Catholic education, faith 
formation, pastoral care, community outreach, and service, while 
fulfilling its responsibility as the cathedral church of the Diocese of 
Shreveport. It is both a treasured historic landmark and a living 
community of faith, welcoming parishioners, visitors, students, 
families, clergy, and members of the wider community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the Cathedral 
of Saint John Berchmans and its parish family for more than 120 years 
of faith, education, and service to the people of Shreveport and 
Northwest Louisiana. May the Cathedral continue to honor the vision of 
those who established the parish in 1902 and serve generations yet to 
come.
```
