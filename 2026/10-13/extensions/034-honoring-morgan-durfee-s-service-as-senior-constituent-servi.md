# HONORING MORGAN DURFEE'S SERVICE AS SENIOR CONSTITUENT SERVICES MANAGER

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
HONORING MORGAN DURFEE'S SERVICE AS SENIOR CONSTITUENT SERVICES MANAGER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and thank Morgan 
Durfee for one year of service with my office.
  Morgan serves as the Senior Constituent Services Manager in my 
office. He is a dedicated public servant and serves the constituents of 
Minnesota's Second Congressional District with empathy and sincerity. 
Morgan spent two years at the Minnesota State Legislature before 
joining my team.
  This Congress alone, Morgan has opened over 100 cases and helped the 
team to deliver $5.6 million back to constituents. His meticulousness 
and dedication to casework was critical during Operation Metro Surge, 
where he worked to support over 20 constituents with ICE.
  I have full confidence that Morgan will continue to give back to 
Minnesotans in his next career step. I wish him the best in his future 
endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
