# RECOGNIZING LIEUTENANT COLONEL MICHAEL JACOB SLAVENS, U.S. AIR FORCE, (RET.), WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
 RECOGNIZING LIEUTENANT COLONEL MICHAEL JACOB SLAVENS, U.S. AIR FORCE, 
           (RET.), WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to present retired Lieutenant 
Colonel Michael Jacob Slavens, USAF (Ret.), of Waco, Texas, with a 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. Michael's distinguished 22-year 
military career, exceptional leadership, and continued dedication to 
veterans, students, and his community reflect the duty, honor, and 
commitment that exemplify the very best of our Texas values.
  Michael graduated from Baylor University and Baylor Air Force ROTC in 
1973 and entered Air Force pilot training at Webb Air Force Base, 
Texas. Michael subsequently completed KC-135A pilot training and began 
his career as a co-pilot with the 46th Air Refueling Squadron at K.I. 
Sawyer Air Force Base, Michigan. Michael later completed the Strategic 
Air Command Combat Training Course as a distinguished graduate and 
served with the 410th Bombardment Wing as Chief of KC-135 Emergency War 
Order Operations. In that position, Michael trained more than 150 
combat aircrew members and staff in emergency war procedures supporting 
nuclear and contingency operations, Michael's performance earned his 
unit three consecutive Outstanding ratings from the Strategic Air 
Command Inspector General, the highest rating given by the command.
  Michael went on to serve with the 39th Tactical Group at Incirlik Air 
Base, Turkey, where he excelled during United States Air Force-Europe 
(USAFE) and NATO inspections and served in critical emergency 
operations positions. Michael later attended Air Command and Staff 
College at Maxwell Air Force Base before joining the Strategic Air 
Command Operations Staff, where he worked to improve command center 
operations. During his final Air Force assignment with the Air Force 
Combat Operations Staff, Michael served in several senior operational 
positions, including Senior Air Force Duty Officer, Deputy Chief of 
Current Operations, Chief of the Headquarters Air Force Current 
Operations Team, and Deputy Chief of the Air Force Operations Support 
Center.
  Throughout his military career, Michael was repeatedly recognized for 
his leadership, technical expertise, judgment, and ability to perform 
under pressure. Michael's innovative work included developing a 21st 
century technology upgrade designed to improve situational awareness 
for the Air Force Chief of Staff. That effort subsequently became a 
Department of Defense prototype, demonstrating his ability to identify 
operational challenges and develop innovative solutions that enhanced 
national defense capabilities. Michael's military service was 
recognized with numerous decorations and awards, including three 
Meritorious Service Medals, Air Force Outstanding Unit and 
Organizational Excellence Awards, the Combat Readiness Medal. National 
Defense Service Medal, Overseas Service Ribbons, Air Force Longevity 
Service Award, and Air Force Training Ribbon.
  Michael also pursued extensive education throughout his military 
career and beyond. Michael earned a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology from 
Baylor University and a Master of Science in Public Administration from 
Troy State University. Michael's professional military education 
included Squadron Officers School, Air Command and Staff College, and 
Air War College.
  After retiring from active military service in 1996 and completing a 
subsequent career as a Department of Defense contractor, Michael 
relocated to Waco in 2016 and quickly became deeply involved in the 
community. In 2017, Michael joined the Heart of Texas Chapter of the 
Military Officers Association of America. Within a year, he was 
selected to serve on the chapter's Board of Directors and subsequently 
served as Second Vice President, First Vice President, and President in 
2022 and 2023. Under his leadership, Michael helped expand the 
chapter's support for veterans' service organizations while maintaining 
scholarship assistance for veterans and their dependents and supporting 
JROTC programs and Baylor ROTC.
  Michael's leadership has had a measurable impact on veteran 
organizations in the Waco area. During his presidency, the chapter 
substantially increased its financial support for veteran service 
organizations and secured additional MOAA Community Outreach Grants 
benefiting Veterans One Stop and the James Connally Flight of the Order 
of Daedalians. In 2026, Michael was again asked to serve as chapter 
president. Michael's contributions have also included editorial and 
website work for the chapter, efforts that helped the organization 
receive the highest-level national MOAA recognition for its newsletter 
and website. Michael's service was further recognized when he was named 
a ``Local Hero'' during Veterans Appreciation Day at Baylor Ballpark.
  Michael's commitment to education and young people is equally 
significant. He serves on the Baylor ROTC Detachment 810 alumni 
scholarship evaluation committee, participates in Baylor's Pro-Sales 
Evaluation Program by providing students with evaluations and critiques 
of their presentations, and works with Baylor's Lifelong Learning 
Committee to identify and coordinate future educational programs. 
Through these efforts, Michael continues to mentor students and 
encourage the next generation of leaders. Michael's life demonstrates 
that service does not end with military retirement.
  From his distinguished career as an Air Force pilot and senior 
operations officer to his continued leadership on behalf of veterans, 
support for ROTC and JROTC programs, and service to students within the 
broader Waco community, Michael has consistently answered the call to 
serve. Michael's record reflects not only professional excellence, but 
also a lifelong commitment to the principle of ``Duty, Honor, Country--
Always Serving.''
  It is this extraordinary record of military service, leadership, 
innovation, mentorship, and continued devotion to veterans and his 
community that I proudly commend Lieutenant Colonel Michael Jacob 
Slavens of Waco, Texas, to his friends, family, community, and country. 
Michael's lifelong commitment to service embodies the spirit of the 
Congressional Veteran Commendation, and it is my honor to recognize him 
with the 2026 Congressional Veteran Commendation from the 17th 
Congressional District of Texas.
```
