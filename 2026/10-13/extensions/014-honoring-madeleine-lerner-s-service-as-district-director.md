# HONORING MADELEINE LERNER'S SERVICE AS DISTRICT DIRECTOR

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
        HONORING MADELEINE LERNER'S SERVICE AS DISTRICT DIRECTOR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and thank Madeleine 
Lerner for five years of service with my office, most recently as my 
District Director.
  Madeleine began her work with my office as a Community Liaison, 
supporting relationship building and stakeholder engagement efforts. 
She was later promoted to Senior Community Liaison, Director of 
Community Engagement, Deputy District Director and ultimately, District 
Director.
  During her time as District Director, Madeleine strengthened our 
casework operation, leading to over $11 million returned to 
constituents. She also steered the district team through the 
uncertainty of Operation Metro Surge and worked diligently to support 
Minnesotans facing the unimaginable. I'll always be grateful for the 
work Madeleine did in traveling with me to El Paso to support a 
constituent and work for their release from ICE detention.
  Madeleine leads with empathy and I know this trait will serve her 
well in the future. I've always appreciated Madeleine's dedication to

[[Page E1072]]

the work and the relationships she was able to build in the community. 
I've valued Madeleine's advice and counsel over the years and wish her 
the best in her future.

                          ____________________
```
