# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROES FEED MY STRAVING CHILDREN

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
      HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROES FEED MY STRAVING CHILDREN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Home 
Heroes, the volunteers at Feed My Starving Children, an organization 
working to combat childhood hunger by providing nutritious meals to 
children across over 70 countries. The only permanent site in the Lone 
Star State is right here in North Texas and is one of just eight 
nationwide.
  With the help of dedicated volunteers and supporters, meals are hand-
packed and distributed to children across the world. Each box contains 
216 meals, allowing every volunteer shift to make a meaningful impact.
  On North Texas Giving Day, the organization raised funds to provide 
over 400,000 meals, which is enough to feed 1,107 kids a daily meal for 
a full year. I thank the staff, volunteers, and supporters who are 
fighting to end childhood hunger and making a difference right here in 
North Texas.

                          ____________________
```
