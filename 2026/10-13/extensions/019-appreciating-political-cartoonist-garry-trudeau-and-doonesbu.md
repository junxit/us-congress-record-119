# APPRECIATING POLITICAL CARTOONIST GARRY TRUDEAU AND DOONESBURY

**Speaking:** Cohen, Steve (C001068) [D-TN]

```
     APPRECIATING POLITICAL CARTOONIST GARRY TRUDEAU AND DOONESBURY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE COHEN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in appreciation for the 
political cartoonist Garry Trudeau, creator of the Doonesbury comic 
strip that has chronicled politics and American history with humor and 
wit for nearly 56 years.
  Mr. Trudeau has graciously permitted me to reprint his work in my 
weekly e-newsletter for the past several years, and I want to thank him 
here publicly for the privilege. I've been a Doonesbury fan since the 
early 1970s and have followed the careers of his many witty 
characters--Mike, Mark, Zonker, B.D. and Boopsie, among others, and 
real-life political figures--in the pages of The Washington Post. My 
hometown paper in Memphis suspended publication of Doonesbury long ago.
  Mr. Trudeau, a 1970 graduate of Yale University, was a valued 
political commentator even before he won the Pulitzer Prize for 
editorial cartooning in 1975, the first comic strip artist to receive 
that distinction. He has been a Pulitzer finalist another three times, 
and has received other national and international accolades, including 
the George Polk Award for a lifetime's achievement in 2015. Doonesbury 
was called ``one of the great intellectual/artistic accomplishments of 
the past half century'' in 2010 by Eric Alterman in The Nation.
  It is a pleasure to wake up on Sunday mornings to see his wife, Jane 
Pauley, the longtime host of the ``CBS News Sunday Morning'' show. The 
two are icons of my generation. As I approach retirement from Congress, 
I wish them both well.

                          ____________________
```
