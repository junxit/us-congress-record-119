# HONORING ILHAN ABDULLAHI'S SERVICE AS STAFF ASSISTANT/CONSTITUENT ADVOCATE

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
   HONORING ILHAN ABDULLAHI'S SERVICE AS STAFF ASSISTANT/CONSTITUENT 
                                ADVOCATE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate and thank llhan 
Abdullahi for her over one year of service with my office.
  Ilhan is my district office Staff Assistant/Constituent Advocate and 
she joined the team after serving as an intern in 2025. This Congress 
alone, Ilhan has opened over 120 cases and helped the team to deliver 
$5.6 million back to constituents. She singlehandedly returned over 
$500,000 to a single constituent this year.
  Ilhan's positive attitude, collaborative nature and dedication to 
serving the community will serve her well in the future. I'll always be 
proud to have been an early part of llhan's career and I wish her the 
best in her future.
```
