# HONORING MARIE BOSWELL

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING MARIE BOSWELL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and congratulate 
Ms. Marie Boswell on her retirement from the Department of Juvenile 
Justice after 47 years of distinguished service with the State of 
Florida. I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Ms. Boswell dedicated her life to serving others 
     very early in her professional career. In 1971, upon 
     graduating from the esteemed Miami Northwestern Senior High 
     School, Ms. Boswell began working for the Florida Office of 
     Vocational Rehabilitation where she assisted people with 
     disabilities find meaningful careers to enhance their 
     independence; and
       Whereas, in 1975, Ms. Boswell continued pursuing her career 
     in public interest work when she joined the Florida 
     Department of Health and Rehabilitative Services. There, Ms. 
     Boswell discovered her passion for assisting youth and 
     families by ensuring their needs were met and connecting them 
     with support services; and
       Whereas, Ms. Boswell committed to changing the perilous 
     trajectory of troubled youth when she joined the Florida 
     Department of Juvenile Justice in 1994. As a Delinquency 
     Prevention Specialist with the Office of Prevention and 
     Victim Services, Ms. Boswell advised and implemented juvenile 
     justice programs to address the emerging needs of Florida's 
     at-risk youth in various judicial circuits throughout South 
     Florida; and
       Whereas, Marie Boswell's passion for children and her 
     commitment to their safety and success is truly admirable. 
     The State of Florida owes a debt of gratitude for the 47 
     years she has invested in uplifting her community; and
       Whereas, Marie Boswell now looks forward to spending more 
     quality time with her loved ones following retirement, which 
     includes her beloved two children and two precious 
     grandchildren.
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to recognize the legacy of a loyal public servant, a 
     great mother, and a phenomenal woman--Ms. Marie Boswell.

                          ____________________
```
