# RECOGNIZING COLONEL WILLIAM "BILL" DALE BAUER, USMC, (RET.), WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
 RECOGNIZING COLONEL WILLIAM ``BILL'' DALE BAUER, USMC, (RET.), WITH A 
                   CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to present Colonel William ``Bill'' 
Dale Bauer of Waco, Texas, with a 2026 Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. Bill's distinguished military career in the United States 
Marine Corps, a continued service to veterans, his community and 
lifelong commitment to our nation exemplify the character, leadership, 
and dedication worthy of this recognition.
  Born in Pensacola, Florida, in 1936, Bill grew up in a proud military 
family. Bill's father, Lieutenant Colonel Harold W. Bauer, a graduate 
of the United States Naval Academy and a World War II Medal of Honor 
recipient, served as a lifelong inspiration. Bill followed in his 
father's footsteps, receiving a presidential appointment to the Naval 
Academy and graduating with the Class of 1958. Bill was commissioned as 
a Marine officer and earned his wings as a Marine fighter pilot in 
1960, beginning a distinguished 27-year career that culminated in his 
attaining the rank of Colonel.
  Bill's military service included some of the most demanding 
assignments of his generation. During the Vietnam War, Bill flew 110 
combat missions in the F-4 Phantom from 1967 to 1968. Bill served as an 
Air Liaison Officer to the 1st Marine Regiment during the Battle of HuI 
in the TIt Offensive and later at Khe Sanh. Throughout his career, Bill 
held significant leadership positions, including commanding Marine 
Fighter Attack Training Squadron 101 in Yuma, Arizona, Marine Aircraft 
Group 15 in Japan, and the Marine Aviation Detachment at the Naval Air 
Test Center in Patuxent River, Maryland.
  Bill continued to distinguish himself through advanced military 
education and professional achievement. Bill attended the Navy Test 
Pilot School in 1970 and received the Empire Test Pilot School Award 
for the top final exercise. Bill later attended the Naval War College, 
where he earned the Stephen B. Luce Award for outstanding performance. 
Bill's extraordinary military service was recognized with numerous 
awards and decorations, including the Legion of Merit, Distinguished 
Flying Cross, Bronze Star Medal, Air Medal with eight awards, Vietnam 
Cross of Gallantry, Combat Action Ribbon, Navy Commendation Medal, 
Presidential Unit Citation with two awards, and other unit and service 
commendations. Bill retired from the Marine Corps in 1985 after 27 
years of service.
  Bill's commitment to service did not end with his military 
retirement. Bill went on to a successful second career as a financial 
advisor with Merrill Lynch, ultimately serving as a First Vice 
President and Branch Manager before retiring in 2014. In this role, 
Bill found fulfillment in helping others achieve financial security, 
continuing the leadership and service that had defined his military 
career.
  Even at the age of 90, Bill continues to serve his country and 
community. Bill has volunteered on the Northern Service Academy Board 
for Congressman Pete Sessions, helping evaluate and select young men 
and

[[Page E1079]]

women who aspire to serve and protect our nation. Bill's continued 
involvement demonstrates that, although he may be retired from active 
military service, he remains deeply committed to preparing the next 
generation of Americans for lives of service.
  Bill has also devoted decades to his local community and fellow 
veterans. Bill served as President of the Waco Chapter of the Military 
Officers Association of America and helped strengthen the 
organization's presence by establishing a consistent meeting location 
at the Brazos Club. Bill has served as President of the Westside Waco 
Rotary Club, as a board member of the American Red Cross, and on the 
Board of Directors of Ridgewood Country Club. Bill's commitment to 
service has also been reflected through his longtime involvement in the 
Episcopal Church, where he served in numerous leadership and volunteer 
roles.
  Bill's life is ultimately defined not simply by the honors he has 
received, but by the way he has continued to lead, mentor, and serve 
others. From his distinguished service as a Marine aviator and combat 
veteran to his ongoing work supporting veterans, his community, and 
future generations of military leaders, Bill has demonstrated an 
unwavering sense of duty and devotion to country.
  It is this extraordinary record of military service, leadership, 
volunteerism, and continued commitment to his fellow Americans that I 
proudly commend Colonel William ``Bill'' Dale Bauer of Texas to his 
friends, family, community, and country. It is my honor to recognize 
him with the 2026 Congressional Veteran Commendation from the 17th 
Congressional District of Texas.
```
