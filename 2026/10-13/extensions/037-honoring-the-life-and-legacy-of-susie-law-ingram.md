# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SUSIE LAW INGRAM

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SUSIE LAW INGRAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 13, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of a truly remarkable individual, Susie Law Ingram, a woman 
of unwavering faith and dedication to her community. I include in the 
Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Susie Law Ingram was born in Georgia on March 3, 
     1954, to the late Emmett and Gussie Law. She was the youngest 
     of nine children; and
       Whereas, at a young age, Susie and her family relocated to 
     Miami, Florida. She was a product of the Dade County Public 
     School system, attending Miami Jackson High School. It was at 
     Miami Jackson where she met the love of her life, Mr. Richard 
     Ingram. Shortly after graduating, the two were wed at the 
     young age of 17 and 18. To this union, four boys were born: 
     Richard D., Fedrick, Randy and Zach; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Ingram had a true love for the arts. While in 
     high school, she was part of the drama club and the choir. 
     She was also a member of the poetry club which led to the 
     composition of short stories, plays and ultimately the 
     publication of her book ``God Lives Here, Too.''; and
       Whereas, inspired by her sons Fedrick and Randy, Mrs. 
     Ingram went on to finish her high school diploma and obtain a 
     degree in elementary education, in her 50's, from Miami Dade 
     College; and
       Whereas, she was a devout Christian and an advocate for 
     social activism, Mrs. Ingram participated in events on local, 
     state, and national levels to promote voter registration, 
     advancing black people, and education. Later in life, Mrs. 
     Ingram and her husband worked together in the private nursing 
     industry. Ultimately, they transitioned into transportation 
     becoming bus drivers and members of the Transport Workers 
     Union; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Ingram leaves behind a loving family, 
     including her devoted husband Richard; her four sons, Richard 
     D. (Lynette), Fedrick, Randy (Jamila) and Zach; 10 
     grandchildren, Dannielle, Niliyah, Elan, Erin, Jaliyah, 
     D'Quan, Randy, Jr., Duane, Jr., Jordan, and Jaren; 2 sisters, 
     Emma Law and Lowrine Jenkins; 2 brothers, Emmit, Jr. and 
     Johnnie. She also leaves a host of nieces, nephews, 
     relatives, friends, and church members, all of whom will 
     forever cherish her memory; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to recognize the late Susie Ingram, and extend my 
     heartfelt sympathy.

                          ____________________
```
