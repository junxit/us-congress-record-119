# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Great is Your faithfulness, O God our creator. We seek You, O Lord. 
Be good to those whose hope is in You. We wait for You, perhaps not as 
patiently as we ought. We know You are our portion.
  Summer and winter, springtime and harvest, the seasons of our lives 
and the times we endure are all in Your hand. May we call this to mind 
in these days. In this hope, may we see the new mercies You provide for 
us, and may we seek to bear witness to Your never-ending faithfulness.
  In the enduring peace found in You, we offer both ourselves and our 
prayers to You in Your sovereign name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
