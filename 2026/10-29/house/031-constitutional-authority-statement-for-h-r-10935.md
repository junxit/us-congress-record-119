# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10935

**Measures:** H.R. 10935

```
           By Ms. LOFGREN:
       H.R. 10935.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2, provides Congress with the 
     power to ``dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory and other Property 
     belonging to the United States.''
       The Supreme Court has described this power to be ``without 
     limitations,'' holding that ``Congress may constitutionally 
     limit the disposition of the public domain to a manner 
     consistent with its views of public policy.'' See United 
     States v. San Francisco, 310 U.S. 16, 29 (1940).
```
