# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE JOHN CARTER OF TEXAS, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE JOHN CARTER OF TEXAS, MEMBER OF 
                                CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable   John Carter of Texas, Member of 
Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, October 28, 2026.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you formally, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I, John Carter, United States Representative for the 
     31st Congressional District of Texas, have been served with a 
     subpoena for documents issued by the District Court of Bell 
     County, Texas.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is not 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
     Hon. John Carter.

                          ____________________
```
