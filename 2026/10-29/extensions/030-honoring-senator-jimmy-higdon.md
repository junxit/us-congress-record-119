# HONORING SENATOR JIMMY HIGDON

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
                     HONORING SENATOR JIMMY HIGDON

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. BARR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor a true public servant, 
Kentucky State Senator Jimmy Higdon. His home is in Lebanon, Kentucky, 
where he was born on July 15, 1953.
  Senator Higdon graduated from Marion County High School and Morehead 
State University. He served in the United State Army Reserves from 1976 
to 1984. After college, Higdon returned to Lebanon and became a partner 
in Key Market grocery store, which later became Higdon's Foodtown. The 
store, a small independent grocery, closed in 2011.
  Higdon was first elected to the Kentucky House of Representatives in 
2002 to represent the 24th District. He served for 4 terms. He was then 
elected to the Kentucky Senate in a 2009 special election and was 
elected four more times in District 14. In the Senate, he served as 
chair of the Senate Veterans, Military Affairs, and Public Protection 
Committee and the Senate Transportation Committee. Senator Higdon was 
chosen by his colleagues as the Republican majority whip and as 
president pro tempore.
  A well-respected leader, Senator Higdon is retiring from elected 
office at the end of his term in 2026 after 24 years of service to the 
people of Kentucky. He is a tremendous public servant and an 
inspiration to us all. I appreciate his dedication to the people that 
he represents in Frankfort and I wish him and his wife Jane a wonderful 
retirement. It is my honor to recognize Senator Jimmy Higdon before the 
United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
