# HONORING STAFF SERGEANT DANIEL "DAN" PALENSKI ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

```
 HONORING STAFF SERGEANT DANIEL ``DAN'' PALENSKI ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Staff Sergeant 
Daniel ``Dan'' Palenski, a World War II veteran and member of Veterans 
of Foreign Wars Springfield Post 7327, as he celebrates his 100th 
birthday. Dan's remarkable life reflects the courage, patriotism, and 
dedication that define the Greatest Generation.
  During World War II, Dan answered his country's call and served as an 
infantry soldier in the Pacific Theater. His military service earned 
him the Asiatic-Pacific Campaign Medal and the World War II Victory 
Medal. Alongside millions of his fellow Americans, Dan helped secure 
victory in one of the most consequential conflicts in our Nation's 
history. His contributions remain an enduring part of our country's 
legacy.
  Today, Dan is a valued member of Springfield's veterans' community 
through VFW Post 7327. His continued connection to fellow veterans 
reflects the bonds forged through shared experiences and a commitment 
to one another. Dan represents a generation whose courage and sacrifice 
helped shape the world we know today.
  As we celebrate Dan's 100th birthday, we also recognize the 
importance of preserving the stories of those who lived through World 
War II. Their firsthand experiences offer younger generations an 
invaluable understanding of our Nation's history and the sacrifices 
made to defend our freedoms. I commend VFW Springfield Post 7327 for 
its longstanding commitment to honoring veterans, supporting military 
families, and strengthening the Springfield community.
  As the Representative of Virginia's 11th Congressional District, I am 
proud to recognize Dan and the many World War II veterans who have made 
Fairfax County their home. Their experiences remind us that history is 
not simply preserved in museums and textbooks, but lives in the stories 
of our neighbors, friends, and family members.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Staff 
Sergeant Daniel ``Dan'' Palenski on his 100th birthday, expressing our 
Nation's gratitude for his contributions, and wishing him continued 
health, happiness, and many more years surrounded by family and 
friends.

                          ____________________
```
