# RECOGNIZING MR. ROGER SPITZ

```
                      RECOGNIZING MR. ROGER SPITZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Roger Spitz of 
the Carlisle Area Sertoma Club. Sertoma--``Service to Mankind''--is one 
of the oldest service clubs in our Nation, and provides critical 
resources to those with hearing problems, The Carlisle Area Sertoma 
Club was chartered in May 1998; since then, it has given nearly 
$100,000 in scholarships to local high school students seeking to study 
audiology, speech pathology, or special education. The Club generously 
provides fire alarms and a baseball camp for the deaf/hard of hearing, 
and hearing amplifiers to those who are unable to afford hearing aids.
  As president of the Carlisle Area Sertoma Club, Roger was 
instrumental in developing and advancing the Feel the Danger Accessible 
Fire Safety Equipment Program, designed to help individuals who face 
barriers in recognizing and responding to fire emergencies, and 
providing life-saving assistance like bed-shaker smoke alarms, fire 
safety training, and other alerting devices for individuals who may 
have trouble responding to traditional fire alarms. For the more than 
one million Pennsylvanians who live with deafness and hearing loss, 
Feel the Danger has helped place hundreds of alarms and safety devices 
in homes and businesses in our communities.
  Roger's leadership has helped expand and grow the Feel the Danger 
program and increased attention from local are safety leaders, the 
State Fire Commissioner, and other federal and state officials. He's 
motivated by the simple belief that every individual, regardless of 
age, hearing ability, or physical limitations, deserves an effective 
warning and the best possible opportunity to respond during a fire 
emergency.
  Mr. Speaker, I'm honored and privileged to recognize and thank Roger 
Spitz, the Feel the Danger Program, and the Carlisle Area Sertoma Club 
on their critical work, tireless service, and attention to our most 
vulnerable. Their service above self sets the standard by which all 
should be judged.
```
