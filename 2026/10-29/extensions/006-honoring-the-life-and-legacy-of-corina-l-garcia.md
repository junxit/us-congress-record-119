# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF CORINA L. GARCIA

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF CORINA L. GARCIA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Mrs. Corina L. Garcia, a remarkable Cuban-American educator whose 
unwavering commitment to freedom and service left a lasting impact on 
generations of students and families throughout South Florida.
  Born on November 7, 1926, in Havana, Cuba, Mrs. Garcia dedicated her 
life to educating others. As a teacher in her homeland, she refused to 
compromise her principles or participate in the Castro regime's efforts 
to indoctrinate children with communist ideology. For standing firm in 
her convictions, she was declared an enemy of the Cuban regime.
  In 1961, Mrs. Garcia made the courageous decision to flee communist 
Cuba with her two young children. Like countless Cuban exiles who 
helped build our South Florida community, she arrived with her 
determination, her values, and her belief in the American Dream.
  Rather than allowing the hardships of exile to define her future, 
Mrs. Garcia continued her education, earning a bachelor's degree from 
the University of Miami and a master's degree in School Administration 
from Florida International University.
  For the next 44 years, she devoted herself to the children of Miami-
Dade County, teaching Spanish, developing innovative reading programs, 
and inspiring generations of students. Her dedication extended beyond 
academics, as she championed programs that promoted kindness, good 
manners, and respect for others. To her friends and colleagues, she was 
affectionately known as ``Mother Teresa,'' a reflection of her 
extraordinary generosity and willingness to lend a helping hand to 
anyone in need.
  After an incredible 58 years of teaching in Cuba and the United 
States, Mrs. Garcia retired in 2013, leaving behind a legacy that 
continues to live on through the countless lives she touched. Mrs. 
Garcia's story is a testament to the courage of the Cuban exile 
community and the enduring promise of America.
  Although Mrs. Garcia is no longer with us, her contributions to South 
Florida will never be forgotten. May her memory forever be a blessing.
```
