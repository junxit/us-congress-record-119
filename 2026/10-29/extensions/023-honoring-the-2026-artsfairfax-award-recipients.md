# HONORING THE 2026 ARTSFAIRFAX AWARD RECIPIENTS

```
             HONORING THE 2026 ARTSFAIRFAX AWARD RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the recipients 
of the 2026 ArtsFairfax Awards, which celebrate individuals and 
organizations whose creativity, generosity, and leadership have 
strengthened the arts community in Fairfax County. This year's 
honorees--Bruce L. Berlage, Karen and Jim Cleveland, the Southeast 
Fairfax Development Corporation, and Thomas Beveridge--have made 
lasting contributions to the cultural life of Northern Virginia.
  Bruce L. Berlage, recipient of the Jinx Hazel Award, has demonstrated 
an extraordinary commitment to expanding access to the arts. Through 
his longtime support of McLean Project for the Arts and his leadership 
in establishing the new MPA Berlage Arts & Education Center, Bruce has 
helped create a welcoming space for artistic expression, education, and 
community engagement. His generosity and dedication will benefit 
generations of Fairfax County residents.
  Karen and Jim Cleveland, recipients of the Philanthropy Award, have 
spent decades enriching the Reston community through their leadership 
and support of the arts. From their involvement with the Tephra 
Institute of Contemporary Art and Public Art Reston to their broader 
civic and philanthropic contributions, they have helped fulfill 
Reston's founding vision of a vibrant, inclusive community where the 
arts are central to everyday life.
  The Southeast Fairfax Development Corporation, recipient of the 
Innovation Award, has demonstrated how public art can strengthen 
neighborhoods and support economic revitalization. Through its murals 
and the innovative ``Faces of One'' sculpture initiative along Richmond 
Highway, the organization has transformed public spaces, celebrated 
local history, and brought residents, businesses, and visitors together 
through art.
  Thomas Beveridge, recipient of the Achievement Award, has dedicated 
his distinguished career to enriching the musical life of our region. A 
gifted composer, conductor, and vocalist, Thomas founded the New 
Dominion Chorale in 1991, creating opportunities for singers of all 
backgrounds to participate in exceptional choral performances. His more 
than 700 compositions and arrangements, along with his commitment to 
music education and community outreach, have left an enduring mark on 
Northern Virginia's cultural landscape.
  I also commend ArtsFairfax for its continued leadership in supporting 
local artists, arts organizations, and cultural programming. Through 
grants, professional development, and public art initiatives, 
ArtsFairfax helps ensure that creativity remains an essential part

[[Page E1140]]

of our community's identity and economic vitality.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
2026 ArtsFairfax Awards recipients and thanking them for their 
remarkable contributions. Their work reminds us that the arts do more 
than enrich our lives--they bring neighbors together, inspire future 
generations, and help make Fairfax County an extraordinary place to 
call home.
```
