# HONORING STEVEN E. WOODARD, SR. WITH THE UNSUNG HERO AWARD

```
       HONORING STEVEN E. WOODARD, SR. WITH THE UNSUNG HERO AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Steven E. Woodard, 
Sr. as a recipient of the Unsung Hero Award and to honor his 
longstanding commitment to quietly serving fellow veterans and the 
Cleveland community.
  Mr. Woodard is a United States Marine Corps veteran whose dedication 
to his fellow servicemembers began while he was still in uniform. 
Following his first deployment in 2005, he made a practice of checking 
in with fellow servicemembers and veterans to ensure they were doing 
well mentally, emotionally, physically, and personally. What began as 
simple acts of concern developed into more than two decades of 
consistent mentorship, advocacy, and support.
  Since 2016, Mr. Woodard has personally assisted approximately 56 
veterans with navigating the Department of Veterans Affairs service-
connected disability claims process. He has helped veterans better 
understand the process, identify necessary information and resources, 
and connect with organizations capable of providing additional 
assistance.
  His commitment does not end when paperwork is submitted. Mr. Woodard 
follows up, checks on veterans' progress, listens to their concerns, 
and remains available throughout the process. These conversations 
frequently extend beyond veterans benefits to mental and emotional 
wellbeing, physical health, employment, housing, family relationships, 
and the challenges associated with transitioning to civilian life.
  Mr. Woodard understands that sometimes the most meaningful act of 
service is simply ensuring that another veteran knows someone is there. 
He has built relationships based on trust, listening without judgment 
and connecting veterans and their families with professional, 
governmental, and community resources when additional assistance is 
needed.
  His commitment to service also extends throughout the broader 
community. From 2009 through 2013, Mr. Woodard served with the Vision 
of Angels Youth Foundation, beginning as a volunteer and ultimately 
becoming President of its Board. He has also volunteered extensively 
through Philippi Missionary Baptist Church, including overseeing its 
Safety and Security Team and helping provide a safe environment for 
members and visitors.
  Across each of these roles, a consistent pattern defines Mr. 
Woodard's service: when he identifies someone who needs help, he makes 
himself available. Whether checking on a fellow veteran, helping 
someone navigate the VA system, mentoring young people, serving his 
church, or connecting a family with community resources, he performs 
the work that is necessary regardless of whether anyone is watching.
  The Unsung Hero Award honors veterans whose continued service happens 
far from the spotlight--the individuals who make the phone calls, 
conduct the follow-ups, listen when someone is struggling, and perform 
the often-unrecognized work that holds communities together. Mr. 
Woodard embodies that spirit.
  For more than twenty years, he has demonstrated that service does not 
end when the uniform comes off. He continues to show up for fellow 
veterans and his community, not for recognition, but because he 
believes those around him should never have to face their challenges 
alone.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Steven 
E. Woodard, Sr. on receiving the Unsung Hero Award and in thanking him 
for his service to our Nation and for the countless acts of mentorship, 
advocacy, compassion, and service he has quietly provided to veterans 
and families throughout Northeast Ohio.
```
