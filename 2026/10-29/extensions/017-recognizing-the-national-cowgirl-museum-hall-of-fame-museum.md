# RECOGNIZING THE NATIONAL COWGIRL MUSEUM & HALL OF FAME MUSEUM ON THEIR FACILITY EXPANSION

```
RECOGNIZING THE NATIONAL COWGIRL MUSEUM & HALL OF FAME MUSEUM ON THEIR 
                           FACILITY EXPANSION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
National Cowgirl Museum & Hall of Fame for its upcoming expansion in 
Fort Worth's Cultural District. This $19 million, 16,000-square-foot 
expansion represents a significant investment in preserving and 
celebrating the stories of the remarkable women who helped shape the 
American West.
  Since its founding, the National Cowgirl Museum & Hall of Fame has 
served as a one-of-a-kind institution dedicated to honoring women of 
the West who exemplify courage, resilience, independence, and 
pioneering spirit. This expansion will allow the Museum to further its 
mission while creating new opportunities for visitors to experience the 
history and heritage of the American West.
  The expanded Museum will feature a striking new entrance, new gallery 
and exhibition spaces, an expanded event space, and a new fashion 
gallery highlighting the evolution of Western style. One of the most 
unique additions will be a fully functioning carousel, designed and 
crafted by renowned artisans, serving as a centerpiece for visitors of 
all ages.
  This project is more than an expansion of a building--it is an 
investment in Fort Worth's cultural heritage and in the generations of 
women whose courage and determination helped define the West. By 
creating new spaces for education, storytelling, and community 
engagement, the Museum will ensure that these important stories 
continue to educate and inspire visitors for generations to come.
  With the grand opening scheduled for November 7, 2026, this expansion 
marks an exciting new chapter for an institution that has become an 
important part of Fort Worth and the broader Western community.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize the National Cowgirl Museum & Hall of Fame for its continued 
dedication to preserving our Western heritage and celebrating the 
extraordinary women who have shaped it. I congratulate the Museum on 
this remarkable expansion and wish it continued success for many years 
to come.
```
