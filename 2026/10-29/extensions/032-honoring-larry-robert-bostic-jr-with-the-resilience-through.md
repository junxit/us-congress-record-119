# HONORING LARRY ROBERT BOSTIC, JR. WITH THE RESILIENCE THROUGH SERVICE AWARD

```
 HONORING LARRY ROBERT BOSTIC, JR. WITH THE RESILIENCE THROUGH SERVICE 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Larry Robert 
Bostic, Jr. as a recipient of the Resilience Through Service Award and 
to honor his extraordinary perseverance, leadership, and continued 
dedication to improving the lives of veterans and individuals with 
disabilities.
  Mr. Bostic served approximately six years on active duty in the 
United States Army from 1984 to 1990. Following his honorable 
discharge, he continued his commitment to public service through a 
distinguished career in law enforcement and federal investigations, 
serving as a police officer, detective, criminal investigator, and 
Supervisory Special Agent with several federal agencies.
  In 2015, Mr. Bostic experienced a life-altering spinal cord injury 
that resulted in significant paralysis. Rather than allowing adversity 
to define his future, he demonstrated remarkable determination in his 
recovery and transformed his personal challenges into a renewed 
commitment to helping others facing similar circumstances.
  Mr. Bostic became deeply involved with the Buckeye Chapter of 
Paralyzed Veterans of America, progressing from Board Member to Vice 
President, President, and ultimately National Director. Through his 
leadership, he has advocated for veterans with disabilities, mentored 
fellow veterans, and helped individuals navigate resources for adaptive 
equipment, accessible housing, and disability benefits.
  His commitment extends to promoting accessibility and adaptive 
recreation. Through his involvement with the Americans with 
Disabilities Act Resource Group at the Louis Stokes Cleveland VA 
Medical Center and the Ohio THRIVE Through Tennis Foundation, he has 
helped individuals with disabilities regain confidence, independence, 
and opportunities for meaningful participation in their communities. He 
has also competed in wheelchair games, earning more than 70 medals, 
trophies, and plaques.
  The Resilience Through Service Award honors veterans who demonstrate 
extraordinary perseverance in the face of adversity while continuing to 
serve and uplift others. Mr. Bostic embodies these ideals through his 
determination, compassion, and unwavering commitment to transforming 
personal challenges into opportunities for meaningful change.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Larry 
Robert Bostic, Jr. on receiving the Resilience Through Service Award 
and in thanking him for his service to our Nation, his remarkable 
resilience, and his continued dedication to empowering veterans and 
individuals with disabilities throughout Ohio.
```
