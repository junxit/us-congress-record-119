# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN SCOTT GODDARD

```
                   RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN SCOTT GODDARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor California Highway 
Patrol Captain Scott Goddard on nearly three decades of outstanding 
service to the people of California and to congratulate him on his 
retirement. Captain Goddard began his service with the California 
Highway Patrol in 1997 as an officer serving the Fresno region. He then 
served in assignments throughout the Central Valley and Southern 
California. Before becoming Captain, he held several regional 
leadership assignments, including a role on the multijurisdictional 
Kings County Major Crimes Task Force, which investigates complex gang, 
narcotics, and other major violent crimes. Over the course of his 
career, he was promoted through the ranks of Sergeant, Lieutenant, and 
eventually became Captain of the California Highway Patrol Visalia Area 
Office in 2023.
  As Captain, the Visalia area office underwent a regional safety 
traffic campaign that reduced crashes caused by impaired driving. 
Through this effort, he took a proactive approach and combined public 
education with enforcement, working closely with partner agencies and 
the community. Captain Goddard also helped prepare the next generation 
of officers. On the College of the Sequoias Administration of Justice 
Advisory Board, he worked with leaders from the Tulare-Kings Counties 
Police Academy, the College of the Sequoias Police Department, the 
California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, and the 
College of the Sequoias faculty. Together, they influenced the courses 
and academy training that future officers receive.
  For decades, Captain Goddard led with skill and dedication, 
protecting the countless communities he served and helping shape the

[[Page E1134]]

success of future law enforcement officers across the state. Today, the 
Central Valley is safer because of his service to the California 
Highway Patrol.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in thanking Captain Scott Goddard for his devoted service to 
the Central Valley. I extend my best wishes to him, his wife Melanie, 
and their daughters Lauren and Hailey in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
