# HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE OF MS. MARILYN EZZY ASHCRAFT, MAYOR OF ALAMEDA

```
  HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE OF MS. MARILYN EZZY ASHCRAFT, MAYOR OF 
                                ALAMEDA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LATEEFAH SIMON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. SIMON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a friend, dedicated 
public servant, and a committed leader here in the East Bay--Ms. 
Marilyn Ezzy Ashcraft, the mayor of Alameda, California.
  Born in San Francisco and raised on the island of Alameda, Mayor 
Ashcraft attended local public schools, including Edison Elementary and 
Alameda High School. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from the 
University of California, Davis, and began her career as a Probation 
Officer in Plumas County before pursuing her Juris Doctor at the Santa 
Clara University School of Law.
  Mayor Ashcraft's commitment to public service began long before she 
held elected office. In 2000, she co-chaired a successful ballot 
measure campaign to build Alameda's Main Library and renovate the 
City's two branch libraries. In 2002, she led a successful campaign to 
establish a new healthcare district and parcel tax to keep Alameda 
Hospital open. She subsequently served on the City of Alameda's 
Planning Board, Economic Development Commission, and Alameda Hospital 
Board of Directors. In 2012, Mayor Ashcraft made history as the first 
Arab American elected to the Alameda City Council. She served on the 
Council, including two years as Vice Mayor, before becoming Alameda's 
first Arab American mayor in 2018. Reelected in 2022, she continued her 
commitment to building a more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable 
community.
  Throughout her tenure, Mayor Ashcraft has championed initiatives to 
improve the quality of life for Alameda residents. Under her 
leadership, the City established the CARE Team, a mental health crisis 
response program that connects residents in crisis with trained 
professionals; adopted a Housing Element to address the community's 
housing needs; and advanced the redevelopment of Alameda Point, 
including the construction of supportive housing for formerly unhoused 
residents. Mayor Ashcraft also helped guide Alameda through the COVID-
19 pandemic.
  Mayor Ashcraft has been a strong advocate for climate action, clean 
energy, and environmental sustainability. Her leadership earned 
national recognition in 2026 when the United States Conference of 
Mayors presented her with the 2026 Mayors' Climate Protection Award for 
advancing decarbonization. She has supported investments in bicycle and 
pedestrian infrastructure, policies accelerating the transition to 
zero-emission technologies, and efforts to attract innovative 
businesses to Alameda.
  Mayor Ashcraft's commitment to public service extends well beyond 
Alameda's shores. As a member of the Metropolitan Transportation 
Commission and as Vice Chair of the Alameda County Transportation 
Commission, she has advocated for expanded public transportation and 
improved regional connectivity. Her leadership also includes service on 
the League of California Cities Board of Directors, its Housing, 
Community, and Economic Development Policy Committee, and its East Bay 
Division Executive Board. She also represents Alameda at the 
Association of Bay Area Governments and is a past president of the 
Alameda County Mayors Conference.
  It has been a privilege to work with Mayor Ashcraft during my time in 
Congress. I am grateful for her friendship, partnership, and dedication 
to the people of Alameda and the East Bay. As she concludes her tenure 
as mayor, I extend my heartfelt appreciation for her decades of service 
and the lasting contributions she has made to her community. I wish 
Mayor Ashcraft and her family all the best as they begin this next 
chapter.
```
