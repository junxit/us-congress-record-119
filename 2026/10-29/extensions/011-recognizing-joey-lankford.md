# RECOGNIZING JOEY LANKFORD

```
                       RECOGNIZING JOEY LANKFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Joey Lankford 
on the occasion of his retirement from the Bedford Fire Department 
after 46 years of dedicated service to our community.
  Joey's passion for public service began at a young age, inspired by 
his father, Mason Lankford, who served as the first Fire Marshal in 
Tarrant County. Following in his father's footsteps, Joey began 
volunteering with the Bedford Fire Department in 1978 before officially 
joining the department in 1980. Throughout his distinguished career, 
Joey served in numerous roles, including Firefighter, Paramedic, 
Engineer, Lieutenant, Investigator, and Deputy Chief and Fire Marshal. 
For the last 16 years of his career, Joey served as Deputy Chief and 
Fire Marshal, carrying on the title his father once held and leading 
with the same commitment to keeping North Texas families safe.
  Beyond the Bedford Fire Department, Joey served as a field and 
training paramedic with Daniel EMS Fort Worth, a flight paramedic with 
CareFlite Air Medical, and an instructor at Tarrant County College. 
After earning his Texas peace officer license in 1997, he expanded his 
service into arson investigations and worked with the Bureau of 
Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives as an Accelerant Detection 
Canine Handler alongside his canine partners, Billie and Rio, from 1999 
to 2014. He also served as a Task Force Officer and Special Deputy from 
2000 to 2026.
  Joey has also dedicated his time to training future first responders 
through various fire and law enforcement training programs. Since 2015, 
he has served as a family escort during the National Fallen 
Firefighters Foundation Memorial Weekend, honoring firefighters who 
made the ultimate sacrifice.
  As Joey begins his well-deserved retirement. I congratulate him on an 
exceptional 46-year career and thank him for his dedicated service to 
the Bedford Fire Department and North Texas community. His lasting 
contributions will continue to inspire future generations.

                          ____________________
```
