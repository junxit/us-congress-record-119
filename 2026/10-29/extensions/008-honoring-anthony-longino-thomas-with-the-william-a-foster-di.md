# HONORING ANTHONY LONGINO-THOMAS WITH THE WILLIAM A. FOSTER DISTINGUISHED SERVICE AWARD

```
      HONORING ANTHONY LONGINO-THOMAS WITH THE WILLIAM A. FOSTER 
                      DISTINGUISHED SERVICE AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Anthony ``Tony'' 
Longino-Thomas as a recipient of the William A. Foster Distinguished 
Service Award and to honor his four decades of service to our Nation 
and the communities of Northeast Ohio.
  Mr. Longino-Thomas began his military career in 1986 when he joined 
the United States Air Force. He served on active duty until 1996, 
including service during the Gulf War and in Eskan Village, Saudi 
Arabia. His commitment to our Nation continued after active duty 
through service in the United States Air Force Reserve from 1996 to 
2002 and the United States Navy Reserve from 2002 to 2011.
  Mr. Longino-Thomas carried that commitment to service into his 
civilian career. In 1996, he joined the Solon Police Department, where 
he served for 20 years and became the first Black police officer in the 
department's history. Following his retirement from the Solon Police 
Department in 2016, he continued his career in public safety by joining 
the Beachwood Police Department as a School Resource Officer.
  In that role, Mr. Longino-Thomas has served not only as a public 
safety professional but also as a mentor and trusted presence for 
students, families, and educators. His work reflects a commitment to 
building relationships with young people and strengthening the 
communities in which they live and learn.
  His dedication to public service extends even further. Since 2024, 
Mr. Longino-Thomas has served as a member of Bedford City Council, 
bringing decades of experience in military and public service to his 
role as an elected representative.
  Mr. Longino-Thomas has also remained active in organizations 
dedicated to service and fellowship. He is a charter member of the Ohio 
Chapter of Mu Beta Phi Military Fraternity and a member of El Hasa 
Temple No. 28. For more than two decades, he has also supported young 
athletes and the sport of track and field, serving as a registered Ohio 
high school track and field official since 2001 and as a USA Track & 
Field registered official since 2010.
  The William A. Foster Distinguished Service Award recognizes a record 
of extraordinary and sustained service. Mr. Longino-Thomas has 
demonstrated that commitment throughout his life. From serving our 
country in uniform, to protecting Northeast Ohio communities, mentoring 
young people, participating in civic organizations, and serving in 
elected office, he has continually answered the call to serve.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Anthony 
``Tony'' Longino-Thomas on receiving the William A. Foster 
Distinguished Service Award and in thanking him for his distinguished 
military service and decades of continued service to Northeast Ohio.
```
