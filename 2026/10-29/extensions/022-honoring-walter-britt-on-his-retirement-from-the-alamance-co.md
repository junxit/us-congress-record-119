# HONORING WALTER BRITT ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE ALAMANCE COUNTY COMMUNITY YMCA

```
   HONORING WALTER BRITT ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE ALAMANCE COUNTY 
                             COMMUNITY YMCA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. Walter Britt on a 
distinguished career as Chief Executive Officer of the Alamance County 
Community YMCA. He retires after 28 years of leadership and more than 
40 years of service in the YMCA movement.
  Mr. Britt joined the Alamance County Community YMCA in 1998 as its 
fifth Executive Director. That year, the Y raised $500,000 through a 
capital campaign to renovate its facilities and purchase new equipment. 
Throughout his tenure, Mr. Britt worked alongside dedicated staff and 
community partners to build on that investment, expand programs, and 
strengthen the YMCA's service to Alamance County.
  Mr. Britt's service to Alamance County has been a calling rooted in 
his faith and commitment to others. I am thankful for his leadership 
which has enriched the lives of generations of families. I know I speak 
for our entire community in thanking him for his decades of dedicated 
service and wishing him all the best in his next chapter.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in honoring Mr. Walter Britt on his 
retirement and thanking him for his outstanding service to the Alamance 
County Community YMCA and the people of North Carolina.
```
