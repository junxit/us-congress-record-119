# HONORING MARLENE BLUM FOR HER SERVICE TO FAIRFAX COUNTY

```
        HONORING MARLENE BLUM FOR HER SERVICE TO FAIRFAX COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Marlene Blum 
for more than 40 years of dedicated public service and volunteer 
leadership in Fairfax County, particularly her commitment to improving 
the health and well-being of our community.
  Throughout her decades of service, Marlene has been a tireless 
advocate for accessible, high-quality health care and the needs of 
Fairfax County residents. Her tireless leadership and genuine 
dedication to Fairfax residents have helped strengthen the County's 
approach to public health and ensure that the voices of residents 
remain central to important decisions affecting their care.
  As Chair of the Fairfax County Health Care Advisory Board, Marlene 
has played an important role in advancing conversations about health 
care access and the challenges facing our community. Her thoughtful 
guidance and commitment to public service have earned her the respect 
and appreciation of colleagues, community leaders, and residents 
throughout Fairfax County.
  Marlene's decades of service have led to her well-deserved 
recognition throughout Fairfax County. She was named Fairfax County 
Citizen of the Year for her contributions to education, healthcare, and 
human services. She also received the Lawrence V. Fowler Award, 
recognizing her outstanding volunteer service on County boards and 
commissions. These honors speak to the lasting impact of her work and 
the respect she has earned throughout our community.
  Beyond her professional accomplishments, Marlene's genuine dedication 
to helping others has set an inspiring example for future generations 
of community leaders. Her willingness to devote her time and talents to 
improving the lives of her neighbors reflects the very best of civic 
engagement and public service here in Fairfax County.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Marlene Blum 
for her more than 40 years of service in Fairfax County. Her 
contributions have left a lasting impact on our community, and I wish 
her all the best as she begins this next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
