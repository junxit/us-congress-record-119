# HONORING MARIAN GIBSON ON THE OCCASION OF HER RETIREMENT

```
        HONORING MARIAN GIBSON ON THE OCCASION OF HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Marian Gibson, 
a dedicated public servant and resident of Vienna, Virginia, on the 
occasion of her retirement from the Transportation Security 
Administration on October 31, 2026. Throughout her distinguished career 
in federal service, Marian has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to 
strengthening our federal institutions, supporting the work of 
Congress, and advancing the safety and security of the American people.
  Since joining TSA in January 2017, Marian has served as Deputy 
Assistant Administrator for Legislative Affairs, playing a critical 
role in advancing the agency's priorities before Congress. She has also 
served as Acting Assistant Administrator for Legislative Affairs, 
providing steady leadership during periods of transition. In these 
roles, Marian has worked closely with the House and Senate 
Appropriations Committees to secure congressional consideration of 
TSA's budget, programs, and policies.
  Marian began her career on Capitol Hill as a Legislative 
Correspondent for Senator John Heinz before serving as a Legislative 
Assistant to Representative E. Clay Shaw, Jr. She later held senior 
congressional affairs positions at the Department of Transportation and 
served as Director of Legislative Affairs for the U.S. Railroad 
Retirement Board. In 2009, she joined the Department of Homeland 
Security, where she held key positions in appropriations and budget 
management. Her expertise also earned her a fellowship with the Senate 
Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee, where she 
provided legislative analysis and guidance on homeland security 
appropriations and agency management.
  Throughout her career, Marian has earned a reputation for 
professionalism, sound judgment, and a deep understanding of the 
legislative process. Her decades of public service reflect the 
essential contributions of career federal employees whose expertise and 
dedication strengthen our government. Beyond her professional 
accomplishments, Marian is a valued member of our Northern Virginia 
community. She holds degrees from Emory University and Marymount 
University and resides in Vienna, where she and her husband have raised 
their four children.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Marian 
Gibson on her well-earned retirement and thanking her for her many 
years of distinguished service to our Nation. Her career exemplifies 
the integrity, expertise, and commitment to public service that make 
our federal workforce so exceptional. I wish Marian and her family 
continued happiness and fulfillment as she begins this next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
