# TRIBUTE TO THOMAS F. BALISTRERI

```
                    TRIBUTE TO THOMAS F. BALISTRERI

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GWEN MOORE

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. MOORE of Wisconsin. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart 
to honor the life and legacy of Thomas F. Balistreri of Milwaukee, 
Wisconsin, who passed away on August 30, 2026, at the age of 86.
  Born on December 12, 1939, and raised on Milwaukee's East Side in a 
close-knit Italian American community, Thomas Balistreri dedicated his 
life to two callings that would come to define him: service to our 
Nation and service to our children.
  After graduating from Marquette University High School and earning 
his degree in Communications from St. Norbert College, where he was 
commissioned through Army ROTC, Thomas answered the call to serve his 
country. He served on active duty in the United States Army Signal 
Corps from 1962 to 1965, stationed with U.S. Army Headquarters Europe 
in Heidelberg, Germany.
  Upon returning home to Milwaukee, Tom began a remarkable 37-year 
career with Milwaukee Public Schools, the district I am proud to 
represent. He began as a classroom teacher and rose to serve as 
principal of Bay View High School and, later, Rufus King High School, 
an International Baccalaureate school and one of the finest public high 
schools in our State. Under his leadership, Rufus King twice earned the 
U.S. Department of Education's National Blue Ribbon School Award and 
was recognized nationally, including a feature on NBC's Dateline. Tom 
was twice named Principal of the Year. He established Italian language 
instruction at Rufus King--the first of its kind in a Milwaukee high 
school--building a legacy of language education that continues to 
benefit Milwaukee students today. Following his retirement from the 
principal's office in 2001, Tom continued to serve our city's students 
as an At-Large member of the Milwaukee Board of School Directors.
  Tom was a proud son of Milwaukee's Italian American community. He 
served as president of the Milwaukee Chapter of UNICO, sat on the Board 
of Directors of the Italian Community Center, and was a devoted member 
of St. Rita's Catholic Church. An advocate for his fellow veterans, he 
remained active in the American Legion and the Veterans of Foreign 
Wars, and for many years he volunteered as a United States Naval 
Academy Blue and Gold Officer, helping guide young Wisconsinites toward 
futures in service to our country. He also served on my Service 
Academies Selection Board.
  Above all, Tom was a beloved husband, father, and grandfather. He is 
survived by his wife, Barbara; his seven children, Thomas, Anthony, 
Maria, Andrew, Victoria, Christina, and Michael; his seven 
grandchildren; and his sister, Rose.
  Tom's life was a testament to the power of uplifting others through 
selfless service, compassionate leadership, and an unwavering 
commitment to making a difference. Throughout every chapter of his 
life, he led with courage, humility, and a deep sense of responsibility 
to the greater good of his community and his nation. As principal, he 
understood that education was about more than what happened inside the 
classroom; it was about inspiring young people to recognize their 
potential and creating opportunities for every student to succeed. 
Through his leadership, he helped shape not only the communities he 
served, but also the futures of students, families, and educators whose 
lives he touched.
  I am exceptionally grateful for the life Tom lived and the example he 
set. He leaves behind a lasting legacy that will be carried forward for 
generations, not only through the family he loved so dearly, but also 
through the countless lives that were changed by the opportunities he 
created, the doors he opened, the communities he strengthened, and the 
friends he stood by. Milwaukee is better because of him, and his spirit 
will continue to inspire all who were fortunate enough to know him, as 
well as future generations who will benefit from the lasting impact of 
his work.
  Mr. Speaker, Thomas F. Balistreri gave his life to service--first in 
uniform, defending our Nation, and then in our classrooms, shaping 
generations of Milwaukee students. Our city, our schools, and our 
Nation are stronger because of his devotion. On behalf of the people of 
Wisconsin's 4th Congressional District, I extend my deepest condolences 
to the Balistreri family, and I ask my colleague to join me in honoring 
his memory and his extraordinary legacy of service.

                          ____________________
```
