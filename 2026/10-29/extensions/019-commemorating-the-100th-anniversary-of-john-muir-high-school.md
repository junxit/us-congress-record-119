# COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF JOHN MUIR HIGH SCHOOL

```
      COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF JOHN MUIR HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and commend the 
distinguished John Muir High School on their 100th Anniversary.
  Today, John Muir High School serves more than 1,000 students of all 
backgrounds, providing an education that fosters success in academics, 
professional development, and community leadership.
  John Muir High School is the only Early College in the Pasadena 
Unified School District. Through its renowned early college curriculum, 
Muir lowers the barriers to higher education while giving students 
opportunities to explore their passions and grow into the leaders of 
the future.
  Over the decades, John Muir High School has produced generations of 
leaders and innovators, including authors, politicians, artists, civil 
rights activists, and Olympic medalists. This remarkable list of 
alumni, including American icons like Jackie Robinson and Octavia 
Butler, reflects Muir's long tradition of providing a supportive 
learning environment that empowers the next generation to reach their 
full potential.
  In January 2025, the Eaton Fire destroyed the homes of 200 current 
Muir students and staff. Yet through perseverance, John Muir High 
School has been able to rebuild and recover. Students, teachers, and 
families remained resilient and supported one another through one of 
the most challenging periods for the Pasadena and Altadena communities. 
This spirit embodies what it truly means to be a Mustang.
  That spirit does not appear only in moments of crisis. It is built 
every day in the classroom. At John Muir High School, students are 
encouraged to set high expectations and work hard, which is reflected 
in a culture of mentorship and academic excellence. Faculty and staff 
are committed to building strong relationships with students and 
connecting them to community resources to support their success in 
college, future careers, and beyond.
  The 100th anniversary of John Muir High School is an incredible 
milestone, and I am proud to honor the legacy and impact left on 
students who have been given the tools they need to learn, grow, and 
succeed.

                          ____________________
```
