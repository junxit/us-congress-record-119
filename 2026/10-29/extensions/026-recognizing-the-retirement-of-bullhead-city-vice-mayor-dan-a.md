# RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF BULLHEAD CITY VICE MAYOR DAN ALFONZO

```
   RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF BULLHEAD CITY VICE MAYOR DAN ALFONZO

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PAUL A. GOSAR

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. GOSAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
retirement of Bullhead City Vice Mayor Dan Alfonzo following a long and 
distinguished career in public service.
  Before being elected vice mayor in August 2022, Vice Mayor Alfonzo 
built a distinguished career as an attorney, author, writer, and 
businessman. Before attending law school, he served our nation during 
the Vietnam War and was honorably discharged on three separate 
occasions. After earning his Juris Doctor, he went on to practice 
corporate law, work with government agencies, and serve as in-house 
counsel for small businesses.
  Over four decades of legal practice, Vice Mayor Alfonzo gained 
extensive experience in government contracting, procurement, 
litigation, and appeals. These experiences gave him firsthand insight 
into how government operates and how it can be abused by self-
interested actors.
  As a writer, publisher, author, and member of the Orange County Peace 
Officers Association, Vice Mayor Alfonzo understands the importance of 
engaged citizens and accountable government. Recognizing that local 
government must operate in accordance with our constitutional 
principles and serve the interests

[[Page E1141]]

of its citizens, he became increasingly involved in his community. He 
consistently supported local businesses, youth and family programs, 
veterans, first responders, nonprofits, and cherished community 
traditions. Through these efforts, he demonstrated the character, 
integrity, and courage necessary to serve the people of Bullhead City.
  I especially commend Vice Mayor Alfonzo for his service as vice chair 
of the Bullhead Metropolitan Planning Organization Executive Board. In 
this role, he helped foster regional collaboration in Mohave County and 
advance long-range transportation planning for Bullhead City residents. 
Situated across the Colorado River from Laughlin, Nevada, Bullhead City 
depends on safe, efficient, and affordable infrastructure to connect 
residents, businesses, and visitors. These transportation networks are 
essential to the region's continued prosperity and allow visitors from 
Las Vegas, Laughlin, Lake Havasu City, Kingman, and neighboring 
communities to experience all that Bullhead City has to offer.
  Vice Mayor Alfonzo's military service, professional accomplishments, 
public service, and deep community involvement reflect a lifelong 
commitment to his fellow Americans and the principles that make our 
nation great. His leadership has helped strengthen my district, and I 
am proud to recognize his many contributions as he concludes this 
chapter of public service.
  Mr. Speaker, while his retirement marks the end of an important era 
for Bullhead City, Vice Mayor Alfonzo leaves behind a legacy of 
service, patriotism, and civic responsibility. His life exemplifies the 
enduring American ideal that dedication, hard work, and virtue earn the 
respect and gratitude of one's community. I thank Dan Alfonzo for his 
service to our country and the people of Bullhead City, and I wish him 
and his family all the best in retirement.

                          ____________________
```
