# RECOGNIZING MR. CRISTIAN GAGINSKY FOR MORE THAN TWO DECADES OF DIPLOMATIC SERVICE

```
    RECOGNIZING MR. CRISTIAN GAGINSKY FOR MORE THAN TWO DECADES OF 
                           DIPLOMATIC SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. ADERHOLT. Mr. Speaker, I am privileged today to recognize and 
honor Mr. Cristian Gaginsky upon the conclusion of more than two 
decades of dedicated service to the partnership between the United 
States and Romania.
  Mr. Gaginksy's career stands as a testament to unwavering commitment, 
strategic insight, and steadfast devotion to strengthening an important 
alliance between our nations.
  He served as a dedicated diplomat during some of the most 
consequential moments of the 21st century. From the aftermath of the 
September 11 attacks to NATO enlargement, the global financial crisis, 
Russia's invasion of Ukraine, and recent developments in the Middle 
East, he has worked tirelessly to maintain the enduring success of 
U.S.-Romania cooperation.
  Mr. Gaginsky's work has helped shape a partnership rooted in shared 
values, mutual trust, and common economic and national security 
interests.
  Born and educated in Romania, Cristian began his American diplomatic 
service in 2000 as Head of Consular and Legal Affairs. During his first 
tour, he coordinated critical bilateral efforts in the aftermath of 9/
11, supported U.S. operations in Afghanistan and Iraq, and helped 
secure Romania's designation as a market economy.
  He played a central role in advancing Romania's NATO accession and 
supported historic White House engagements that strengthened the 
foundation of the U.S.-Romania partnership.
  In 2006, he began his second U.S. tour in Political and Congressional 
Affairs, contributing to the 2008 NATO Summit in Bucharest, helping 
establish the American Romanian Business Council, and supporting the 
efforts that shaped the modern Visa Waiver framework.
  From 2012 to 2015, he brought his diplomatic experience into the 
private sector as Manager of Government and Legal Affairs for Ford 
Motor Company Romania, where he protected a major U.S. investment, 
strengthened competitiveness conditions, and implemented enhanced 
compliance systems across Ford's Romanian operations.
  Then in 2015, he returned to Washington for his third and longest 
diplomatic assignment as Deputy Chief of Mission. He strengthened 
homeland security and counter terrorism cooperation, supported 
Romania's progress toward non-immigrant Visa Waiver designation, and 
helped orchestrate U.S.-Romania support for Ukraine.
  Across all these roles, Mr. Gaginsky advised American and Romanian 
CEOs on how to calibrate expectations, strategy, and pursuit of 
business plans and priorities to achieve successful outcomes. His work 
at the intersection of diplomacy, security, and economic development 
has deepened a partnership that continues to advance peace and 
prosperity.
  As the longest-serving European diplomat in Washington, he leaves a 
legacy defined by

[[Page E1137]]

professionalism, humility, and a profound respect for the United 
States. I am honored to have had the privilege to get to know Cristian 
Gaginsky during his many years of service and to call him a friend. He 
will be greatly missed by the diplomatic community as well as many of 
us on Capitol Hill, I wish Cristian, his wife, Cristina, and their 
children, Matei, Vlad, and Martha, all the best.
```
