# RECOGNIZING SUDDEN INFANT DEATH SYNDROME AWARENESS MONTH

```
        RECOGNIZING SUDDEN INFANT DEATH SYNDROME AWARENESS MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to join parents and medical 
professionals across the country in recognizing October as Sudden 
Infant Death Syndrome Awareness Month.
  Sudden Infant Death Syndrome, or SIDS, is defined as the unexpected 
death of a baby under one year of age. Hundreds of families in this 
country experience the devastating loss of a child to SIDS each year. 
Even more tragically, most of those lost to SIDS are thought to be 
healthy. The cause of SIDS remains unknown.
  I am grateful to the many researchers, health care providers, non-
profit volunteers, and others who promote awareness of SIDS and work to 
end preventable infant deaths. I am especially gratified by the efforts 
of the SPE Foundation of Elida, Ohio, founded by Hunter Jolliff and 
named in honor of his cousin, Stetson Patrick Etzkorn. Ever since 
Stetson's death due to SIDS at the age of three months, Hunter and his 
family have supported other families who have experienced infant death 
and taught parents about safe sleep practices for babies. They have 
channeled their family's grief into powerful efforts to help others.
  Mr. Speaker, as we mark SIDS Awareness Month, I invite my colleagues 
to join me in paying tribute to the innocent young lives taken by SIDS, 
in offering our comfort and support to their loved ones, and in 
highlighting the important work of all who devote their lives to 
improving infant sleep safety.
```
