# RECOGNIZING STEPHEN MATHER LEGACY DAY

```
                 RECOGNIZING STEPHEN MATHER LEGACY DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES A. HIMES

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. HIMES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Stephen Mather 
Legacy Day, observed on October 29, 2026, at The Mather Homestead in 
Darien, Connecticut, and to honor the man whose vision shaped America's 
national parks.
  Stephen Tyng Mather led the campaign that persuaded Congress to 
create the National Park Service in 1916. From 1917 to 1929, he served 
as the agency's inaugural director, organizing its workforce, 
establishing the conservation principles by which we still abide, and 
promoting appreciation for public lands. His family homestead in 
Darien, now a National Historic Landmark, is a fitting tribute to his 
life's work of preserving lands for future generations to enjoy.
  On Stephen Mather Legacy Day, a bronze memorial plaque honoring 
Mather will be installed at the Homestead. The same plaque stands in 
national parks and monuments across our country, and it bears the 
words:
  ``He laid the foundation of the National Park Service, defining and 
establishing the policies under which its areas shall be developed and 
conserved unimpaired for future generations. There will never come an 
end to the good that he has done.''
  I commend The Mather Homestead Foundation for preserving this 
landmark and sharing Mather's story with new generations. Mr. Speaker, 
I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Stephen Mather Legacy Day 
and expressing gratitude for a man who dedicated himself to protecting 
our country's natural beauty and ensuring that all Americans could 
enjoy it.
```
