# HONORING THE WORK OF COUNCILMEMBER PATTI COMERFORD

```
           HONORING THE WORK OF COUNCILMEMBER PATTI COMERFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JUAN CISCOMANI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the work of Town of 
Marana Councilmember Patti Comerford, who has dedicated 24 years to 
faithfully serving the people of Marana.
  Before coming to Arizona, Patti was a community service officer in 
Los Angeles County for 10 years. This experience helped shape her 
ability to listen well to the needs of those around her, a skill which 
she still excels in.
  With the desire to give her son a better childhood, Patti and her 
family moved to the agricultural community of Marana.
  Since 1994, Patti and her family have lived in Marana's Continental 
Ranch, where she has served on the HOA's Activities and Finance 
committees. Patti has also been a member of the Marana Optimist Club 
and served as President of the Marana High School Band Boosters.
  Patti was also the founder of the Continental Ranch Little League and 
served on the Little League District Board.
  Her dedication to the people of Marana doesn't end there. Patti 
served on the Marana Planning and Zoning Commission for seven years, 
where she also spent time as the chair.
  First elected to the Marana Town Council in 2001, Patti has served as 
a councilmember for three terms. From 2011 to 2013, she also served as 
Marana's vice mayor.
  Throughout her time in office, Patti has prioritized a good 
relationship between law enforcement and the Marana community, critical 
water rights, and town growth.
  With Patti's retirement from the Marana Town Council this fall, I 
want to express my gratitude and appreciation for her continued 
commitment to public service, and the impact she has made on Marana.
  Patti, has been a wonderful community member, councilmember, and 
friend. Marana wouldn't be the same without her.
  Congratulations to Patti on a successful career, and I wish her the 
best of luck for what's next.
```
