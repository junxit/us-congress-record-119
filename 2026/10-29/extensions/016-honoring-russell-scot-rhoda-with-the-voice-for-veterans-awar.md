# HONORING RUSSELL SCOT RHODA WITH THE VOICE FOR VETERANS AWARD

```
     HONORING RUSSELL SCOT RHODA WITH THE VOICE FOR VETERANS AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 29, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Russell Scot Rhoda 
as a recipient of the Voice for Veterans Award and to honor his 
continued advocacy on behalf of veterans and their families throughout 
Northeast Ohio.
  Mr. Rhoda is a United States Army veteran who has continued his 
commitment to service by working to improve the lives of fellow 
veterans, particularly those experiencing homelessness, aging veterans, 
veterans with disabilities, and their families.
  As President of Veteran Housing Solution, Mr. Rhoda has worked to 
address one of the most fundamental needs facing veterans: access to 
safe, stable, and accessible housing. His advocacy goes beyond 
identifying challenges. He works directly to develop solutions that 
allow veterans to live with greater security, independence, and 
dignity.
  Through his leadership, Veteran Housing Solution has acquired and 
rehabilitated homes in the City of Cleveland and utilized community 
partnerships and volunteer resources to support construction and 
improvements. His work has also connected veterans and their families 
with furniture, support services, credit assistance, and other 
resources necessary to establish and maintain stable housing.
  Mr. Rhoda has been particularly committed to ensuring that the homes 
of aging veterans and veterans with disabilities meet their individual 
needs. His efforts have included making homes wheelchair accessible, 
installing ramps and walk-in showers, replacing standard fixtures with 
accessible alternatives, lowering shelving, helping veterans obtain 
wheelchairs, coordinating wheelchair maintenance, and pursuing grants 
to support these improvements.
  The Voice for Veterans Award recognizes individuals who stand at the 
forefront of

[[Page E1138]]

change for our veterans--those who identify where systems fall short, 
advocate for better services and opportunities, and remain committed 
until meaningful solutions are achieved. Mr. Rhoda exemplifies that 
mission through his hands-on approach to veteran's advocacy and his 
determination to ensure that those who served our Nation have the 
resources and support they deserve.
  His work demonstrates that advocacy is not only about raising 
awareness. It is about translating the needs of veterans into action 
and creating tangible improvements in their everyday lives.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Russell 
Scot Rhoda on receiving the Voice for Veterans Award and in thanking 
him for his service to our Nation and his continued commitment to 
advocating for veterans and their families throughout Northeast Ohio.
```
