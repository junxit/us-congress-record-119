# TRIBUTE TO ALICE NORMENT

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ALICE NORMENT

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Alice for her hard work as an intern in my 
Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and contributions to my 
office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Alice is a native of North Carolina. She is currently a junior at The 
Madeira School in McLean, VA. She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, 
which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of 
her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last several 
months.
  I want to thank Alice for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
