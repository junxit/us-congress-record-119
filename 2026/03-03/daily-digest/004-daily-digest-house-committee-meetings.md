# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 7567, H.R. 7744

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Agriculture: Full Committee began a markup on H.R. 7567, 
the ``Farm, Food, and National Security Act of 2026''.
DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2026
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 7744, the 
``Department of Homeland Security Appropriations Act, 2026''. The 
Committee granted, by a record vote of 7-4, a rule providing for 
consideration of H.R. 7744, the Department of Homeland Security 
Appropriations Act, 2026, under a closed rule. The rule waives all 
points of order against consideration of the bill. The rule provides 
that the bill shall be considered as read. The rule waives all points 
of order against provisions in the bill. The rule provides one hour of 
general debate equally divided and controlled by the chair and ranking 
minority member of the Committee on Appropriations or their respective 
designees. Finally, the rule provides one motion to recommit. Testimony 
was heard from Chairman Cole and Representative DeLauro.

Joint Meetings
LEGISLATIVE PRESENTATIONS
Joint Hearing: Senate Committee on Veterans' Affairs concluded a joint 
hearing with the House Committee on Veterans' Affairs to examine the 
legislative presentation of Veterans of Foreign Wars, Student Veterans 
of America, Jewish War Veterans of the U.S.A., Blinded Veterans 
Association, Fleet Reserve Association, Minority Veterans of America, 
National Association of County Veterans Service Officers, Iraq and 
Afghanistan Veterans of America, after receiving testimony from Carol 
Whitmore, Ryan Gallucci, Kristina Keenan, Michael Figlioli, and Jason 
Johns, all of Veterans of Foreign Wars of the U.S.; Tammy Barlet, 
Students Veterans of America; Scott P. Stevens CWO4, USA (Ret.), Jewish 
War Veterans of the U.S.A.; Lea Rowe, Blinded Veterans Association; 
Richard Fetro, Fleet Reserve Association; Colonel Lorry M. Fenner, USAF 
(Ret.), Minority Veterans of America; Andrew Tangen, National 
Association of County Veterans Service Officers; and Kyleanne Hunter, 
Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans of America.
```
