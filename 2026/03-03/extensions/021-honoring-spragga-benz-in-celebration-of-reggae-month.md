# HONORING SPRAGGA BENZ IN CELEBRATION OF REGGAE MONTH

**Speaking:** Cherfilus-McCormick, Sheila (C001127) [D-FL]

```
          HONORING SPRAGGA BENZ IN CELEBRATION OF REGGAE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 3, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Spragga 
Benz in celebration of Reggae Month. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas, reggae and dancehall music represent powerful 
     expressions of Jamaican culture, resilience, and global 
     influence, celebrated annually during Reggae Month as a 
     tribute to the artists who have shaped and elevated the genre 
     worldwide; and
       Whereas, Spragga Benz, born Carlton Errington Grant in 
     Kingston, Jamaica, has been a dynamic force in dancehall 
     music since the early 1990s, earning respect for his lyrical 
     skill, versatility, and enduring contribution to the 
     evolution of Jamaican sound; and
       Whereas, from his breakout success with ``Love Mi Gun'' to 
     chart-topping hits such as ``She Nuh Ready Yet,'' and 
     acclaimed albums including Jack it Up, Fully Loaded Shotta 
     Culture, and Chiliagon, which topped the Billboard Reggae 
     Albums chart, Spragga Benz has consistently demonstrated 
     artistic excellence and innovation; and
       Whereas, his collaborations with internationally renowned 
     artists across genres have expanded the reach of reggae and 
     dancehall, reinforcing Jamaica's cultural impact on the 
     global music landscape; and
       Whereas, beyond music, Spragga Benz has shown a deep 
     commitment to community upliftment through initiatives such 
     as the Stay in School Program and the Carlisle Foundation, 
     supporting youth development and opportunity in Jamaica; and
       Whereas, his legacy reflects perseverance, cultural pride, 
     and a dedication to using music as a platform for 
     empowerment, storytelling, and social influence; and 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That we, Florida's 20th Congressional District, 
     proudly honor Spragga Benz in celebration of Reggae Month, 
     recognizing his outstanding contributions to reggae and 
     dancehall music, his cultural influence, and his commitment 
     to community empowerment.

                          ____________________
```
