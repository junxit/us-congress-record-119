# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9242

**Measures:** H.R. 9242

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 9242.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact the No American Left 
     Behind Act pursuant to Article 1, Section 8 of the 
     Constitution, including its powers to provide for the common 
     defense, make rules for the government and regulation of the 
     Armed Forces, and make all laws necessary and proper for 
     carrying those powers into execution.
```
