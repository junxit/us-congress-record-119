# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Eternal Lord, satisfy our deepest desires with Your love. Quench our 
thirst for understanding with Your wisdom, for You alone can provide 
for our need for discernment. Only You can appease our appetite for 
truth.
  Help us to see how futile it is to think that we can be contented 
with wealth, power, and position.
  Help us to understand the vanity of clinging to today's worries and 
pursuing fleeting successes.
  Turn our eyes from the pursuit of vainglory and fix them upon Your 
glory, the glory of Your eternity.
  In our time, in this place, may we instead pursue a lifelong response 
of obedience to Your guidance and direction.
  On this day, in every moment, may we instead follow in faith Your 
perfect will, for in You alone do we find all that we seek.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
