# ZEROING OUT MONETARY BENEFITS IMPROPERLY EXPENDED ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 8467

```
         ZEROING OUT MONETARY BENEFITS IMPROPERLY EXPENDED ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Schmidt). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the unfinished business is the question on suspending the rules and 
passing the bill (H.R. 8467) to reform the Payment Integrity 
Information Act of 2019 to ensure executive agencies focus on fraud 
prevention, and for other purposes, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. Gill) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
