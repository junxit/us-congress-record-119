# RECOGNIZING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2026 NORTHERN VIRGINIA LEADERSHIP AWARDS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2026 NORTHERN VIRGINIA LEADERSHIP 
                                 AWARDS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 2026 recipients 
of the Northern Virginia Leadership Awards presented by Leadership 
Fairfax.
  Leadership Fairfax is a nonprofit corporation whose mission is to 
inspire, connect, develop, and engage the next generation of leaders in 
Northern Virginia, so that they are prepared to serve and strengthen 
our community. Over 2,500 alumni from their programs have become part 
of a fast-growing network of civic and business leaders in the region.
  Each year, Leadership Fairfax recognizes individuals or organizations 
that have demonstrated exceptional leadership and made extraordinary 
contributions to our community. I include in the Record the following 
names of the 2026 Northern Virginia Leadership Awards recipients:

       The Non-Profit Organization Leadership Award was presented 
     to Dyslexic Edge, a nonprofit devoted to helping all students 
     become a success because of their dyslexia, devoting time 
     daily to recognizing, celebrating, and developing the gifts 
     of each child instructed.
       The Non-Profit Individual Leadership Award was presented to 
     Joe Meyer, the Executive Director and CEO of Shelter House. 
     Joe has put tremendous work forward to managing six major 
     shelters in Northern Virginia, establishing strategies to end 
     homelessness and domestic violence.
       The Regional Leadership Award went to Imam Mohamed Magid, 
     Resident Scholar at the ADAMS Center. At ADAMS, Imam Magid 
     plays a vital role in guiding the community through 
     education, spiritual growth, and advocacy. His work has 
     empowered individuals and families across generations, and 
     his efforts to foster inclusivity and unity have earned him 
     deep respect across diverse communities.
       The Trustee Leadership Award was presented to Sylvia 
     Lambert-Woodard, the President and CEO of Pathway Homes. Her 
     visionary leadership and steadfast commitment to community, 
     stability, and collaboration across Northern Virginia have 
     made a meaningful and lasting impact on our region--and 
     continue to inspire those she serves.
       The Educational Leadership Award will be presented to Mrs. 
     Elizabeth Scheurer, Vice Principal of Herndon Elementary 
     School. Her visionary leadership and unwavering dedication to 
     fostering community, stability, and collaboration throughout 
     Northern Virginia have created a lasting impact on our region 
     and continue to inspire those around her.
       The Youth Organization Leadership Award was presented to 
     LAZERA 1 Brotherhood Empowered Teen Leadership Team. This 
     Youth-Led program focuses on reducing the impact and exposure 
     to trauma among youth aged 12-19 through cultural enrichment 
     and civic engagement activities.
       The Youth Individual Leadership Award was presented to 
     Tucker Wright, Ambassador of Homestretch. At just 16 years 
     old, Tucker Wright demonstrated extraordinary leadership and 
     compassion through eight years of dedicated service to 
     families experiencing homelessness.
       The Corporate Leadership Award was presented to You've Got 
     Maids of Northern Virginia. Their teams work with precision 
     and pride, following a proven process perfected over 40 
     years. Their leadership and dedication continue to inspire 
     our community.

  Mr. Speaker, the contributions of these individuals and organizations 
are one of the reasons why Fairfax is such a sought-after community in 
which to live and work, and this year's honorees highlight the legacy 
of Leadership Fairfax in preparing our community's future leaders to 
address the challenges we face. I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating these honorees and thanking them for their service to 
Northern Virginia.
```
