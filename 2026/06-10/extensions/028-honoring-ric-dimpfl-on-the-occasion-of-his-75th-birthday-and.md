# HONORING RIC DIMPFL ON THE OCCASION OF HIS 75TH BIRTHDAY AND OVER 50 YEARS OF DEDICATION TO THE VOLUNTEER FIRE SERVICE

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
 HONORING RIC DIMPFL ON THE OCCASION OF HIS 75TH BIRTHDAY AND OVER 50 
           YEARS OF DEDICATION TO THE VOLUNTEER FIRE SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ric Dimpfl on the 
occasion of his 75th birthday and to recognize a lifetime of 
extraordinary service to his community and the fire service. For over 
50 years, Ric has proudly served with the Hamburg Volunteer Fire 
Department, demonstrating an unwavering commitment to protecting the 
lives and property of others.
  Beyond his service as a firefighter, Ric has been a tireless advocate 
for emergency responders throughout New York State. In 1991, he co-
founded The Firefighter newspaper, providing a respected voice for the 
fire service community. He has also spent decades working with elected 
officials to advance legislation and policies that strengthen emergency 
services and support first responders.
  Ric's lasting impact on the fire service was recognized in 2025 when 
he was inducted into the Erie County Fire Wall of Honor. This 
distinction reflects not only his decades of service but also his 
leadership, advocacy, and dedication to improving the fire service for 
future generations. He is supported and celebrated by his wife, Sharon, 
as well as his children and grandchildren, who share in the pride of 
his remarkable legacy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in honoring Ric Dimpfl on his 75th birthday and thanking him 
for his extraordinary contributions to the Hamburg Volunteer Fire 
Department, Erie County, and the State of New York. His commitment to 
service, community, and family stands as an enduring example to all.
```
