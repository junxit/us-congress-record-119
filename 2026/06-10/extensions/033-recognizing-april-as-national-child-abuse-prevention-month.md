# RECOGNIZING APRIL AS NATIONAL CHILD ABUSE PREVENTION MONTH

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
       RECOGNIZING APRIL AS NATIONAL CHILD ABUSE PREVENTION MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize April 2026 as 
National Child Abuse Prevention Month and to reaffirm our shared 
commitment to protecting children, supporting families, and 
strengthening communities across our nation.
  Since 1983, National Child Abuse Prevention Month has served as a 
reminder of our collective responsibility to ensure that every child 
grows up in a safe, stable, and nurturing environment. Preventing abuse 
and neglect requires a coordinated effort among families, educators, 
healthcare providers, law enforcement, and community organizations 
working together to support children and intervene when help is needed.
  In communities across Northern Virginia, dedicated professionals and 
volunteers are working every day to protect children and support 
survivors. Their efforts help ensure that children are treated with 
dignity and compassion, and that families have access to the resources 
they need during difficult times.
  In Virginia's 11th Congressional District, organizations like 
SafeSpot Children's Advocacy

[[Page E562]]

Center play a vital role in this work. SafeSpot partners with law 
enforcement, child protective services, and other community 
organizations to support children and families navigating the trauma of 
abuse. Through trauma-informed forensic interviews, advocacy services, 
and therapy, organizations like SafeSpot help reduce the burden on 
children while ensuring they receive the care and support they need to 
begin the healing process.
  Child Abuse Prevention Month also highlights the importance of 
prevention through education, awareness, and early intervention. By 
investing in families and strengthening support systems, we can reduce 
risk factors and create environments where children can thrive.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing National 
Child Abuse Prevention Month and in supporting the individuals and 
organizations working tirelessly to protect children and promote safe, 
healthy communities.

                          ____________________
```
