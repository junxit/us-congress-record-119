# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ALFIERO RANIERO GIAMBERARDINO

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
     HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ALFIERO RANIERO GIAMBERARDINO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Mr. Alfiero Raniero Giamberardino, whose dedication to his 
craft, his family, and his community left a lasting impact on all who 
knew him.
  Born in L'Aquila, Italy, Mr. Giamberardino carried with him a strong 
work ethic and deep appreciation for family throughout his life. After 
coming to the United States, he built a distinguished career through 
the International Union of Painters and Allied Trades in New York City, 
where he developed a reputation for skill, professionalism, and pride 
in his work.
  Over the course of his career, Mr. Giamberardino contributed to 
projects across New York City, including some of its most recognizable 
landmarks. His craftsmanship and leadership earned him the respect of 
colleagues throughout the trade and eventually

[[Page E555]]

led him to establish his own company, Almar Painting and Decorating Co. 
Through decades of work, he built a successful business, and a 
reputation defined by integrity, quality, and commitment to others.
  While he took great pride in his profession, those closest to Mr. 
Giamberardino remember him most for his warmth, generosity, and larger-
than-life personality. He had a deep love for music, especially opera, 
and found joy in gardening, collecting, and sharing stories with family 
and friends. Whether welcoming guests into his home or spending time 
with loved ones, he brought energy, humor, and kindness to every room 
he entered.
  Above all else, Mr. Giamberardino was devoted to his family. He 
shared a loving marriage with his wife of 61 years, Anna Maria Romita, 
and took immense pride in his son Marco, daughter-in-law Andrea, and 
grandchildren Lorenzo and Alessandra. His love for his family was 
evident in every part of his life and remained at the center of who he 
was.
  Alfiero Giamberardino's story reflects the contributions of so many 
immigrants who helped build communities across our country through hard 
work, perseverance, and devotion to family. His legacy lives on through 
the lives he touched, the family he cherished, and the lasting example 
he set for future generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the life of 
Alfiero Raniero Giamberardino and in extending our condolences to his 
family and loved ones.
```
