# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF MS. CAROLINE SAYERS

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF MS. CAROLINE SAYERS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ZACHARY NUNN

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
exceptional individual and valued member of my team, Ms. Caroline 
Sayers, as she concludes nearly three years of dedicated service to 
Iowa's 3rd Congressional District.
  A proud native of Georgia and devoted University of Georgia Bulldogs 
fan, Caroline and I have shared a friendly rivalry since her first day 
on Team Nunn. While her Bulldogs wear red and black and mine wear Drake 
blue, our shared commitment to hard work, teamwork, and service quickly 
made Caroline an invaluable member of our team.
  As our Senior Policy Advisor, Caroline became one of the most trusted 
and respected voices on our team. She led our work on some of the most 
consequential and complex financial services issues before Congress, 
including housing, banking, cryptocurrency, and artificial 
intelligence. Her expertise, diligence, and strategic leadership helped 
advance four pieces of legislation through the House this Congress 
alone.
  Earlier this year, Caroline joined me on a congressional delegation 
trip to Taiwan, where we engaged key allies and partners to strengthen 
our national security, expand economic opportunities for Iowa 
producers, and

[[Page E559]]

reinforce America's commitment to a free and open Indo-Pacific.
  Many of Caroline's contributions happened behind the scenes. Anyone 
who has watched a Financial Services Committee hearing knows I 
occasionally request a prop or visual aid with very little notice. Time 
and again, Caroline was the one tasked with making it happen--and 
somehow, she always delivered.
  Beyond her impressive policy achievements, Caroline is a mentor to 
younger staff, a trusted colleague, and a steady presence in our 
office. Her professionalism, good humor, and unwavering commitment to 
public service left a lasting mark on Team Nunn and the constituents we 
serve.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Ms. Caroline 
Sayers for her outstanding service to Iowa's 3rd Congressional 
District. We are deeply grateful for her contributions, and we wish 
Caroline every success in the next chapter of her career and service.

                          ____________________
```
