# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MRS. SHIRLEY GRABER

**Speaking:** Smith, Adrian (S001172) [R-NE]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MRS. SHIRLEY GRABER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ADRIAN SMITH

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. SMITH of Nebraska. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Mrs. Shirley 
Graber of McCook, Nebraska, and wish her a very happy 100th birthday. 
Reaching a century of life is a truly extraordinary milestone and I 
hope Mrs. Graber's day be filled with treasured memories, the love of 
family, and the appreciation of friends near and far.

                          ____________________
```
