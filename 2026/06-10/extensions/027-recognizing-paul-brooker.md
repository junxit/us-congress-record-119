# RECOGNIZING PAUL BROOKER

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING PAUL BROOKER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
young man from Rochester, New York, whose dedication to leadership, 
service, and personal excellence has earned him admission to the United 
States Military Academy at West Point: Paul Brooker.
  Throughout his time in high school, Paul distinguished himself 
through his integrity, commitment, and seriousness of purpose. 
Respected by both his peers and teachers, he consistently demonstrated 
a willingness to serve others, whether as a supportive classmate, a 
trusted friend, or a captain of his cross-country team. His 
accomplishments reflect not only a strong work ethic, but also a deep 
commitment to leadership and service.
  Drawn to West Point's enduring mission of developing leaders of 
character committed to the service of our Nation, Paul recognized in 
the Academy an institution that reflected his own values and 
aspirations. The principles of duty, honor, and selfless service 
resonated deeply with him, as did the opportunity to challenge himself 
academically and physically while preparing for a life of leadership.
  Mr. Speaker, I am confident that Paul Brooker will make the Rochester 
community proud as he embarks on this next chapter of his life. I 
congratulate him on this remarkable achievement and extend my very best 
wishes for continued success at the United States Military Academy and 
beyond.

                          ____________________
```
