# CONGRATULATING SCOTT CLARK

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
                       CONGRATULATING SCOTT CLARK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to offer my heartfelt 
congratulations to Scott Clark on receiving the Skill, Integrity, and 
Responsibility (SIR) Award.
  On June 6, 2026, I had the pleasure of joining Scott at the 2026 
Annual Associated General Contractors of Georgia Convention and can 
confidently say no one is more deserving.
  The SIR award is given to the individual who exemplifies the values 
embraced through outstanding service, industry leadership, and 
involvement in the community. Scott embodies each of these qualities.
  Throughout his distinguished career, Scott has served in many 
different roles, eventually leading to his current role as Chief 
Executive Officer at RW Allen Construction.
  His commitment to excellence and passion for developing future 
leaders will leave a long-lasting impact on the construction industry 
in Georgia and across the Nation.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Scott is also a devoted 
family man and man of faith. I congratulate my friend Scott, his wife 
Jennifer, and their two children on this well-deserved recognition and 
honor.

                          ____________________
```
