  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate this year's 
winners of the Congressional Award.
  The Congressional Award honors those who strive to be part of 
something bigger than themselves. With this award, Congress recognizes 
young Americans who devote their free time to an important activity. It 
could be taking on a public service project, pursuing a hobby or new 
skill, starting a physical fitness program, or going on an outdoor 
expedition.
  Participants set goals in these areas, and how they do in meeting 
them determines the level of award they receive. Just as important, 
they receive the sense of pride that comes with fulfilling a mission. 
The program, established in 1979, is a great way to motivate young 
people to develop the building blocks of good citizenship.
  I congratulate each of the award's winners.
  I include in the Record a list of this year's Congressional Award 
Gold Medalists from around the country, including two recipients from
