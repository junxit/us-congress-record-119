# RECOGNIZING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF VIENNA LITTLE LEAGUE

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF VIENNA LITTLE LEAGUE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a remarkable 
milestone in our Northern Virginia community, the 75th anniversary of 
Vienna Little League.
  For seventy-five years, Vienna Little League has served as a 
cornerstone of youth development in the Town of Vienna, providing 
generations of children with the opportunity to learn the game of 
baseball while building character, confidence, and lifelong 
friendships. Since its founding, the league has embodied the very best 
of community spirit, bringing together families, volunteers, and local 
leaders in support of our youth.

[[Page E556]]

  Vienna Little League has played an essential role in shaping young 
lives both on and off the field. Through its commitment to teamwork, 
sportsmanship, and perseverance, the league has instilled values that 
extend far beyond the game itself. Thousands of players have come 
through its program, many of whom carry forward the lessons they 
learned into their schools, careers, and communities.
  In addition to its longstanding tradition, Vienna Little League 
continues to achieve excellence at the highest levels of youth 
baseball. In 2022, its 9-11U All-Star team captured the Southeast 
Regional Title for the first time in over fifty years, and in 2023, its 
8-11-Year-Old team once again brought home the Southeast Regional 
Title. These accomplishments reflect not only athletic talent, but also 
the strength of the league's coaching, mentorship. and community 
support.
  Equally impressive is the continued vitality of the league itself. 
Even in an era where travel baseball has become increasingly prevalent, 
Vienna Little League remains a thriving and accessible program, with 
nearly 1,000 players registered for the 2026 spring season. This 
remarkable level of participation is a testament to the enduring value 
of community-based athletics and the strong sense of belonging that 
Vienna Little League fosters.
  At the heart of Vienna Little League's success is its dedicated 
network of volunteers, including coaches, board members, umpires, and 
parents, who generously give their time and energy year after year. 
Their commitment ensures that each season provides a safe, inclusive, 
and enriching environment where every child has the opportunity to grow 
and succeed.
  The 75th anniversary of Vienna Little League is not only a 
celebration of its history, but also a testament to its enduring impact 
on the community. It reflects decades of hard work, community 
investment, and a shared belief in the importance of youth engagement 
through athletics. Above all, it represents an extraordinary community 
that has been built around the game, one that continues to bring pride 
to the Town of Vienna.
  The anniversary celebration brings together past and present players, 
families, and community members to honor the league's legacy and look 
ahead to its continued success. It is an opportunity to reflect on the 
countless memories made on the fields of Vienna and to recognize the 
individuals who have contributed to the league's lasting success.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Vienna 
Little League on its 75th anniversary, and in wishing the league 
success this season. Its legacy of service, achievement, and community 
building has left an indelible mark on Northern Virginia, and its 
continued success will no doubt inspire generations to come.
```
