# RECOGNIZING BAPS SHRI SWAMINARAYAN MANDIR OF BOYNTON BEACH ON ITS 25TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Krishnamoorthi, Raja (K000391) [D-IL]

```
RECOGNIZING BAPS SHRI SWAMINARAYAN MANDIR OF BOYNTON BEACH ON ITS 25TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. RAJA KRISHNAMOORTHI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize BAPS Shri 
Swaminarayan Nlandir of Boynton Beach on the occasion of its 25th 
Anniversary, known as Rajat Jayanti, and to honor its longstanding 
contributions to the spiritual, cultural, and civic life of South 
Florida.
  Since its inauguration in June 2001 as the first traditional Hindu 
temple in Palm Beach County, BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir has served 
as a center of faith, learning, and community for thousands of families 
throughout the region. For twenty-five years, the Mandir has advanced 
the values of devotion, unity, compassion, and selfless service while 
preserving and promoting Hindu culture, heritage, and traditions. 
Through its religious and educational programs, it has fostered 
spiritual growth and strengthened bonds across generations.
  The impact of the Mandir extends well beyond its walls. Dedicated 
volunteers have organized food drives, health fairs, walkathons, 
disaster relief initiatives, and youth leadership programs that have 
benefited countless individuals throughout South Florida. The Mandir 
has also promoted interfaith understanding and civic engagement by 
welcoming people of all backgrounds to learn about and experience Hindu 
culture, helping to build a stronger and more connected community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing BAPS Shri 
Swaminarayan Mandir of Boynton Beach as it celebrates its 25th 
Anniversary. Its enduring commitment to faith, service, and community 
has enriched the cultural fabric of South Florida, and its 
contributions will continue to inspire future generations.
```
