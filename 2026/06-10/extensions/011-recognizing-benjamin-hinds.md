# RECOGNIZING BENJAMIN HINDS

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BENJAMIN HINDS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
young man from Rochester, New York, whose commitment to leadership, 
service, and personal excellence has earned him admission to the United 
States Coast Guard Academy: Benjamin Hinds.
  Inspired by a proud family tradition of military service, Benjamin 
has long been guided by the values of duty, honor, and commitment to 
country. Following in the footsteps of both his father and grandfather, 
he developed a deep appreciation for disciplined leadership and a 
desire to dedicate himself to a cause greater than himself. His 
acceptance to the United States Coast Guard Academy represents not only 
a remarkable personal achievement, but also the continuation of a 
family legacy rooted in service to our Nation.
  Throughout his time at McQuaid Jesuit High School, Benjamin 
distinguished himself as a diligent student, supportive teammate, and 
respected leader among his peers. Known for his generosity and 
unwavering dedication, he consistently sought opportunities to help 
others and lead by example. Whether encouraging his teammates from the 
pool deck, assisting fellow students in the classroom, or dedicating 
his time to the betterment of his community, Benjamin demonstrated the 
character, integrity, and sense of responsibility that define future 
leaders in military service.
  Mr. Speaker, I am confident that Benjamin Hinds will continue to make 
the Rochester community proud as he embarks on his journey at the 
United States Coast Guard Academy. I congratulate him on this 
remarkable achievement and extend my very best wishes for continued 
success in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
