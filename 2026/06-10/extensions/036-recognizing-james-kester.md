# RECOGNIZING JAMES KESTER

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JAMES KESTER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an exceptional 
young man from Rochester, New York, whose dedication to leadership, 
service, and academic excellence has earned him admission to the United 
States Air Force Academy: James Kester.
  Throughout his years at Fairport High School, James distinguished 
himself as a leader both in the classroom and in his community. As an 
Eagle Scout, he dedicated countless hours to serving others, 
demonstrating a commitment to teamwork, responsibility, and selfless 
service. He further developed those leadership skills as captain of his 
football team, where he learned the importance of setting an example, 
supporting his teammates, and fostering success through collective 
effort. Balancing these responsibilities with an outstanding academic 
record, James consistently embodied the values of hard work, integrity, 
and perseverance.
  For James, a commitment to service is deeply rooted in family 
tradition. Coming from a five-generation military family, he was 
inspired from an early age by the example of those who answered the 
call to serve our Nation. Drawn to the Air Force Academy's mission of 
developing leaders of character, he recognized an opportunity to 
continue that legacy while preparing himself for a lifetime of service. 
His dedication to duty, leadership, and country makes him exceptionally 
well-suited for the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead.
  Mr. Speaker, I am confident that James Kester will continue to make 
the Rochester community proud as he embarks on his journey at the 
United States Air Force Academy. I congratulate him on this remarkable 
achievement and extend my very best wishes for continued success in the 
years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
