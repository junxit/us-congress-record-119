# RECOGNIZING AUGUSTINE GALAMBOS

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                     RECOGNIZING AUGUSTINE GALAMBOS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an exceptional 
young man from Rochester, New York, whose courage, resilience, and 
commitment to service have earned him admission to the United States 
Naval Academy: Augustine Galambos.
  At a pivotal moment in his life, Augustine demonstrated remarkable 
bravery when his brother was involved in a life-threatening climbing 
accident. Faced with an extraordinary and frightening situation, he 
remained calm under pressure, acted decisively, and helped ensure his 
brother's safety. His composure, courage, and ability to respond 
effectively in a moment of crisis revealed a maturity and strength of 
character far beyond his years.
  Rather than allowing that experience to define him, Augustine used it 
as inspiration to pursue a path dedicated to service and leadership. 
Drawn to the challenges and responsibilities that accompany military 
service, he sought an institution that would further develop his 
character while preparing him to lead in times of uncertainty. The 
United States Naval Academy provides that opportunity, offering a 
rigorous environment where his determination, discipline, and sense of 
duty will continue to grow.
  Mr. Speaker, I am confident that Augustine Galambos will continue to 
distinguish himself as a Midshipman and future leader in service to our 
Nation. On behalf of the Rochester community, I congratulate him on 
this remarkable achievement and extend my very best wishes for 
continued success at the United States Naval Academy and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
