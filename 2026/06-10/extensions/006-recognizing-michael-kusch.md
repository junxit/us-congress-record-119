# RECOGNIZING MICHAEL KUSCH

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MICHAEL KUSCH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
young man from Rochester, New York, whose leadership, dedication, and 
commitment to service have earned him admission to the United States 
Merchant Marine Academy: Michael Kusch.
  Throughout his time at Pittsford Mendon High School, Michael 
distinguished himself through academic excellence, athletic 
achievement, and a steadfast commitment to leadership. Balancing a 
rigorous course load with the responsibilities of serving as captain of 
the varsity swim team, he developed the discipline, perseverance, and 
teamwork necessary to excel in every endeavor. Whether in the 
classroom, in competition, or within his community, Michael 
consistently demonstrated resilience and a willingness to place the 
needs of others before his own.
  Michael's desire to serve our Nation has been shaped by meaningful 
experiences both inside and outside of school. Through his 
participation in Monroe County's Teen Police Academy and his volunteer 
work with Teens for Veterans, he gained a deeper appreciation for 
public service, civic responsibility, and the sacrifices made by those 
who protect our freedoms. These experiences strengthened his commitment 
to leadership and inspired him to pursue a future dedicated to 
safeguarding the security and prosperity of our Nation.
  Mr. Speaker, I am confident that Michael Kusch will continue to make 
the Rochester community proud as he embarks on this next chapter of his 
life. I congratulate him on this remarkable achievement and extend my 
very best wishes for continued success at the United States Merchant 
Marine Academy and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
