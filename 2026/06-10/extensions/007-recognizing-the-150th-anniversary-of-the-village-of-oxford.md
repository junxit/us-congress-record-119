# RECOGNIZING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE VILLAGE OF OXFORD

```
       RECOGNIZING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE VILLAGE OF OXFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mrs. McClain. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Village of 
Oxford on its 150th anniversary. This milestone is a testament to the 
generations of people who have helped make Oxford the remarkable place 
it is today.
  For 150 years, Oxford has been a place where neighbors look out for 
one another, families put down roots, and local businesses help drive 
the community forward. Its motto, ``Small Done Right,'' perfectly 
captures the pride, hard work, and strong sense of community that has 
defined Oxford for a century and a half and will continue to shape its 
future for years to come.
  From supporting local businesses and a vibrant downtown to investing 
in families and community institutions, Oxford has continued to grow 
while preserving the character that makes it special. As we celebrate 
this anniversary, we honor the history of the village and the people 
who have shaped it, while also looking forward to the bright future 
ahead.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
Village of Oxford on its 150th anniversary and wishing its residents 
many more years of success. Oxford's story is a reminder that strong 
towns are shaped by generations of hardworking people who care deeply 
about the place they call home.

                          ____________________
```
