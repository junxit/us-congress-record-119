# RECOGNIZING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF GOTTESMAN RTW ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF GOTTESMAN RTW ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 60th anniversary 
of Gottesman RTW Academy, and to honor the long-standing service of the 
Head of School, Dr. Moshe Vaknin. For six decades, the Academy has 
provided the Jewish community of Morris and Sussex Counties with a 
strong foundation in both general and Judaic Studies, pairing academic 
rigor with core Jewish values.
  Founded in 1967, the Academy offers education from infant care 
through eighth grade and is accredited by the New Jersey Association of 
Independent Schools. Its commitment to individualized learning, 
critical thinking, and community leadership has made it a central 
institution within New Jersey's Jewish educational landscape.
  For twenty years, Dr. Vaknin, the Academy's Head of School, has 
advanced this mission through steadfast dedication to students, 
families, and the broader community. After immigrating from Israel in 
1990, Dr. Vaknin built a career grounded in service, faith, and 
education. His leadership has strengthened the Academy's focus on 
cultivating the academic, social, and emotional growth of every 
student.

[[Page E554]]

  Today, we honor both the Academy's enduring impact and Dr. Vaknin's 
exceptional contributions. May Gottesman RTW Academy continue to uphold 
its tradition of excellence and service for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
