# RECOGNIZING THE AWARDEES OF THE 2026 ANNUAL PRIVILEGE AWARDS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE AWARDEES OF THE 2026 ANNUAL PRIVILEGE AWARDS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the recipients 
of the 2026 Annual Privilege Awards, presented by the Bernie L. Bates 
Foundation, an organization dedicated to strengthening communities 
across Fairfax County through education, mentorship, and service.
  Now in its fifth year, the Annual Privilege Awards honor outstanding 
residents whose time, leadership, and generosity have significantly 
supported nonprofit organizations and uplifted communities throughout 
Northern Virginia.
  This award is given to those who embody the spirit of the late 
Colonel Marion ``Barney'' Barnwell, United States Army, Retired, and 
Lord Fairfax, 2018. He was a community leader and advocate known for 
his longstanding commitment to civic engagement and public service in 
Northern Virginia. He was actively involved with the Bernie L. Bates 
Foundation, supporting efforts that promote education, mentorship, and 
opportunities for young people. Through his leadership and volunteer 
work, Barnwell helped strengthen community partnerships and advance 
initiatives that encourage service and leadership among local 
residents.
  This year's honorees exemplify the spirit of service and the ``pay it 
forward'' mentality that the program was created to celebrate. I 
include in the Record the following names of the 2026 Annual Privilege 
Awards recipients:

       Dawnielle Feucht, INOVA Mount Vernon Hospital. Dawnielle 
     Feucht is recognized for

[[Page E557]]

     her commitment to compassionate healthcare and community 
     service through her work at INOVA Mount Vernon Hospital. Her 
     dedication to improving patient care and supporting the 
     wellbeing of Fairfax County residents reflects the highest 
     ideals of service in the healthcare profession.
       Kim-Thoa Faxon, Fort Belvoir Chapel. Kim-Thoa Faxon is 
     honored for her leadership and service through the Fort 
     Belvoir Chapel, where she has worked to strengthen spiritual 
     support and community engagement for military families and 
     residents in the region. Her efforts help foster connection, 
     compassion, and resilience within the community.
       Alicia Washington, National Pan-Hellenic Council of 
     Northern Virginia. Alicia Washington is recognized for her 
     leadership within the National Pan-Hellenic Council of 
     Northern Virginia, where she has advanced civic engagement, 
     service initiatives, and opportunities for collaboration 
     among historically Black Greek-letter organizations. Her work 
     has strengthened community partnerships and expanded service 
     efforts across the region.
       Scott E. Brideau, Rebuilding Together Arlington/Fairfax/
     Falls Church, Inc. Scott Brideau is honored for his 
     dedication to improving the lives of local residents through 
     Rebuilding Together Arlington/Fairfax/Falls Church. Through 
     volunteer leadership and community collaboration, he has 
     helped advance critical home repair and revitalization 
     projects that ensure safe and stable housing for vulnerable 
     families.
       Meg Turkson, Riverside Elementary School. Meg Turkson is 
     recognized for her outstanding service to students and 
     families at Riverside Elementary School. Through her 
     commitment to education and mentorship, she has helped create 
     opportunities for young people to succeed academically and 
     grow as engaged members of their community.

  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
recipients of the 2026 Annual Privilege Awards for their extraordinary 
commitment to service in Fairfax County. Their leadership, generosity, 
and dedication strengthen our communities and embody the very best of 
Northern Virginia.
```
