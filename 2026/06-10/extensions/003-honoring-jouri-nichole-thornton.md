# HONORING JOURI NICHOLE THORNTON

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                    HONORING JOURI NICHOLE THORNTON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 10, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Jouri Nichole Thornton, an outstanding student leader and 
scholar from Jackson, Mississippi, whose academic excellence, 
leadership, and service exemplify the very best of our Nation's young 
leaders.
  Ms. Thornton is a graduating senior at Mississippi Valley State 
University, where she is pursuing a degree in Business Administration 
with a concentration in Accounting. Throughout her academic career, she 
has distinguished herself as a dedicated scholar and an influential 
leader on campus.
  She currently serves as President of the Student Government 
Association, where she has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to 
advocating for her fellow students and strengthening campus engagement. 
In addition, she serves as President of the National Society of 
Leadership and Success and President of Delta Mu Delta International 
Honor Society in Business, further highlighting her dedication to 
academic achievement and leadership development.
  In Spring 2026, Ms. Thornton was initiated into the Psi Gamma Chapter 
of Beta Sorority, Incorporated, continuing a proud tradition of service 
and scholarship. She has also served as a Thurgood Marshall College 
Fund Yard Ambassador, representing her institution while promoting 
educational opportunity and excellence.
  Her academic accomplishments are equally impressive. Ms. Thornton was 
named Outstanding Scholar for the Business Administration Department 
for the 2023 to 2024 academic year, a testament to her hard work, 
discipline, and pursuit of excellence. Beyond the classroom, she 
contributes to the cultural life of the university as an active member 
of the university choir.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Jouri 
Nichole Thornton's achievements as they reflect a deep commitment to 
leadership, scholarship, and service. Her future is exceedingly bright, 
and there is no doubt she will continue to make meaningful 
contributions to her community and our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
