# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we praise Your holy Name. Forgive us for the times when 
our fear has been greater than our faith, when worries have 
overshadowed our trust, and when we have looked more at our 
circumstances than at Your promises. Strengthen our hearts and renew 
our confidence in You, knowing that You are able to do more than we can 
ask or imagine.
  Lord, remind our lawmakers that You are the same God who has guided 
Your people through every step, the same God who makes a way when there 
seems to be no way, and the same God whose grace is sufficient for 
every trial they face. When burdens grow heavy, teach our Senators to 
cast their cares on You, trusting that You care for them.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
