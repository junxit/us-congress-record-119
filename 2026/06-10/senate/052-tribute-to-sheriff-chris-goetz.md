# TRIBUTE TO SHERIFF CHRIS GOETZ

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                     TRIBUTE TO SHERIFF CHRIS GOETZ

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, with my colleague Senator Mike 
Crapo, we honor Sheriff Chris Goetz of Idaho as he announces his 
retirement after more than 17 years as sheriff and nearly three decades 
of service with the Clearwater County Sheriff's Office.
  Throughout his career, Sheriff Goetz has been a pillar of our 
community, exemplifying dedication and leadership and earning the 
respect of colleagues and residents alike. He is a lifetime member of 
the Western States Sheriffs Association and has served on the Idaho 
Sheriffs Association Board of Directors since 2010. Among his many 
notable accomplishments, Sheriff Goetz is one of only three sheriffs in 
Idaho history to serve as president of the Idaho Sheriff's Association 
twice. This unique distinction further demonstrates his commitment to 
public safety, the people of Clearwater County, and Idaho.
  Sheriff Goetz's contributions to his community extend far beyond law 
enforcement. He is a past president of the Idaho Association of 
Counties and has volunteered countless hours to the Sunnyside Rural 
Fire District, for

[[Page S2735]]

which he serves as assistant fire chief. Sheriff Goetz is also the 
president of the Clearwater County 4-H Leaders Council and a member of 
the 4-H Livestock Sale Committee and the Weippe Rodeo Association.
  As Sheriff Goetz begins this next chapter, we extend our sincere 
appreciation for his impressive and heroic tenure in public service. He 
has undoubtedly made a profound impact on Clearwater County, and his 
dedication to keeping our citizens safe will leave a legacy that is 
felt across Idaho for generations to come.
  On behalf of the people of Idaho, we wish Sheriff Goetz many happy 
years in retirement filled with family, new adventures, and continued 
contributions to the Gem State.●
```
