# Economy (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                Economy

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, this morning's inflation numbers reaffirm 
what the American people already know: Trumpflation is getting worse 
and worse, with no end in sight. Trumpflation hit 4.2 percent last 
month, a 3-year high. Another month of Trump's illegal Iran war, 
another month of Trump's tariffs, another month of Republican control 
of Congress, and what does America get? A new alltime high for 
Trumpflation.
  Just about everything is getting more expensive. In the past year, 
the price of fresh produce has gone up 6 percent; meat, 7.6; 
electricity, 6; airfare, 26.7; and gasoline by a staggering 45-percent 
increase.
  Is it any wonder that polling this week found that 70 percent--70 
percent--of American voters disapprove of Trump's handling of the cost 
of living? That number includes a whole lot of Republicans.

  Donald Trump promised to lower costs on day one, but nearly 17 months 
since taking office, he and his MAGA majorities in Congress haven't 
lifted a finger to lower Americans' costs.
  But let's be clear. Republicans aren't just failing to address the 
affordability crisis; they are making it worse--actively making it 
worse. Republicans are driving up costs with their expensive tariff 
tax, with their disastrous war with Iran that is leading to 
recordbreaking gas prices, with their trillion-dollar cut to 
healthcare, and with the rest of this radical, hard-right agenda that 
so misserves the American people.
  Trump's tariffs are costing American families more than $1,700 a year 
on average. American families have had to pay an additional $750 on 
average during these past few months just to meet skyrocketing costs of 
gasoline because of the Iran war.
  Instead of focusing on helping Americans weather this dire financial 
moment, Republicans are helping Donald Trump get even richer. As 
Americans' costs go up, Donald Trump keeps putting his hand into the 
till to make money for himself and his family.
  Republicans are more concerned with Trump's golden ballroom than with 
helping Americans afford housing. Republicans would rather give 
taxpayer-funded payouts to Trump and his cronies through his MAGA slush 
fund than help Americans put food on the table. Republicans continue to 
fight for Trump and his corruption rather than lower costs for working 
families.
```
