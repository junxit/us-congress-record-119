# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 2, S.Con.Res. 33

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2025, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on June 9, 2026, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 2. An act to provide for reconciliation pursuant to 
     title II of S. Con. Res 33.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2025, 
the enrolled bill was signed on June 9, 2026, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, by the Acting President pro tempore (Mr. Curtis).
```
