# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 4744

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

**Measures:** S. 4744

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 4744

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk, and I ask for its first reading, S. 4744.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 4744) to amend titles 10 and 38, United States 
     Code, and other Federal laws, to improve benefits for 
     veterans and the administration of the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs.

  Mr. CORNYN. I now ask for a second reading and, in order to place the 
bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection having been heard, the bill will 
receive a second reading on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
