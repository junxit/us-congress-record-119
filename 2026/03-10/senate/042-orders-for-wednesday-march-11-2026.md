# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 11, 2026

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6644

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 11, 2026

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, March 11; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 343, H.R. 6644, Housing for the 21st 
Century Act; further, that all time during recess, adjournment, and 
leader remarks count postcloture on substitute amendment No. 4308 to 
Calendar No. 343, H.R. 6644.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
