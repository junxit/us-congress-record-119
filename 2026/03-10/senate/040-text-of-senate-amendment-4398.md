# Text of Senate Amendment 4398

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

```
  SA 4398. Mr. KENNEDY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 4308 proposed by Mr. Scott of South Carolina (for 
himself and Ms. Warren) to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the 
supply of housing in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 205(b)(2)(A)(ii)(II), strike items (aa) and (bb) 
     and insert the following:
       (aa) the difference in the number of housing units, as of 
     the third quarter of the preceding fiscal year, in the 
     jurisdiction of the eligible recipient, as calculated by the 
     Secretary; and
       (bb) the difference in the number of housing units, as of 
     the third quarter of the preceding fiscal year, in the 
     jurisdictions of all eligible recipients that receive a bonus 
     amount under this paragraph, as calculated by the Secretary.

                          ____________________
```
