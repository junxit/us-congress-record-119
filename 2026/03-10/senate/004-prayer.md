# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of mercy, we trust Your power and will not be afraid. In these 
best and worst of times, give us the peace that comes from confidence 
in Your providential powers. Bless our lawmakers. When they feel 
pessimistic, remind them that You are able to keep them from stumbling 
or slipping. You are their gracious and merciful God--slow to anger, 
quick to hear, and ready to rescue. Provide them with shelter for 
life's storms.
  And, Lord, be a refuge for our military servicemembers and a comfort 
for the families of the fallen.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
