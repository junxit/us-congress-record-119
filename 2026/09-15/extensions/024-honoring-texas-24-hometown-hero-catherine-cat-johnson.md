# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CATHERINE "CAT" JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CATHERINE ``CAT'' JOHNSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 15, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Catherine ``Cat'' Johnson, a Southlake native and a Shelton 
School alumna, who recently began her journey at Texas Tech University. 
She has earned national recognition for her leadership and service to 
our North Texas community.
  This summer, Cat created take-home meal kits that ensured the 
children would have access to healthy meals over the weekend if their 
families were suffering from financial hardships. Cat worked with GRACE 
in Grapevine to bring her project, ``Feed our Kids Weekend Edition'' to 
life. This project led to her earning the Girl Scouts Gold Award, the 
highest honor in Girl Scouting from the Girl Scouts of Texas Oklahoma 
Plains. In recognition for her impact in the community in helping 
address childhood hunger she received a $5,000 national scholarship 
from Girl Scouts of the USA.
  I congratulate Cat on this recognition for her service to our 
community and wish her the best in her year ahead at Texas Tech 
University.

                          ____________________
```
