# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:

[[Page H5637]]

  This is the day that You, O Lord, have made, and we are graced to 
receive it. Your gift is beyond measure, and our hearts are filled with 
gratitude for Your loving-kindness today and always.
  Grant then that we use this day meaningfully, rejoicing in the 
opportunities You set before us, glad in the knowledge that You have 
entrusted us with the stewardship of the gift of life and offered us 
the privilege of serving You in the work we do here in this United 
States House of Representatives.
  Let us then consider the time we are given to be not our own, but 
Yours. Make every minute serve as our response to You. Ensure that 
every conversation we have reflects our love for You. May every task 
completed glorify You that all may see that what is marvelous in our 
eyes is, in fact, what You, O Lord, have accomplished in the generosity 
of the grace and mercy You have shown to us.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
