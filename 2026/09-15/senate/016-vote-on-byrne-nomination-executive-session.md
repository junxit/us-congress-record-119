# Vote on Byrne Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                        Vote on Byrne Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Byrne nomination?
  Mr. MORENO. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the 
Senator from Kentucky (Mr. McConnell).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Delaware (Mr. Coons) and 
the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) are necessarily absent.
  The result was announced--yeas 52, nays 45, as follows:
```
