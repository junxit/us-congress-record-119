**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** S. 4668

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Curtis). Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair 
lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will 
state.

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate,

     for, student athletes and to promote fair competition among 
     intercollegiate athletics, and for other purposes.
```
