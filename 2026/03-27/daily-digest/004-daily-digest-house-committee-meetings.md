Committee Meetings
Homeland Security and Further Additional Continuing Appropriations Act,

Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on Senate amendment 
to H.R. 7147, the ``Homeland Security and Further Additional Continuing 
Appropriations Act, 2026'' [Further Additional Continuing 
Appropriations Act, 2026]. The Committee granted, by a record vote of 
8-4, a rule that provides that the House shall be considered to have 
taken from the Speaker's table, H.R. 7147, the ``Homeland Security and 
Further Additional Continuing Appropriations Act, 2026'' [Further 
Additional Continuing Appropriations Act, 2026], with the Senate 
amendment

[[Page D351]]

thereto, and to have concurred in the Senate amendment with an 
amendment consisting of the text of Rules Committee Print 119-21. 
Testimony was heard from Chairman Cole and Representative DeLauro.

  No joint committee meetings were held.
