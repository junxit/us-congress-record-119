# BILLS PRESSENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 227, H.R. 837, H.R. 952, H.R. 1721, H.R. 2388, H.R. 2481, H.R. 3620, H.R. 4307, H.R. 4467, H.R. 6380, H.R. 7022, H.R. 7831

```
                   BILLS PRESSENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on October 14, 
2026, the following bills were presented to the President of the United 
States for approval:

       H.R. 4467. To modify the boundary of the Vicksburg National 
     Military Park in the State of Mississippi, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2481. To require online dating service providers to 
     provide fraud ban notifications to online dating service 
     members, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2388. To take certain Federal land in the State of 
     Washington into trust for the Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 1721. To direct the Secretary of Commerce to conduct a 
     study on the feasibility of manufacturing in the United 
     States products for critical infrastructure sectors, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 952. To convey the reversionary interest of the United 
     States in certain land in Sacramento, California.

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, further reported that on 
October 15, 2026, the following bills were presented to the President 
of the United States for approval:

       H.R. 7831. To amend the Mineral Leasing Act to extend the 
     period of time during which the Secretary of the Interior is 
     required to collect a fee for each new application for a 
     permit to drill, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 7022. To provide for the transmission of emergency 
     alerts by satellite, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 6380. To establish the Chiricahua National Park in the 
     State of Arizona as a unit of the National Park System, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 4307. To direct the Secretary of Labor to train 
     certain employees of Department of Labor how to effectively 
     detect and assist law enforcement in preventing human 
     trafficking during the course of their official duties, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 3620. To provide for the conveyance of certain 
     property to the Southcentral Foundation located in Anchorage, 
     Alaska, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 837. To require the Secretary of Agriculture to convey 
     the Pleasant Valley Ranger District Administrative Site to 
     Gila County, Arizona.
       H.R. 227. To allow a period in which members of the clergy 
     may revoke their exemption form Social Security coverage, and 
     for other purposes.
```
