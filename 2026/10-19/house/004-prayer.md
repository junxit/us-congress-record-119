# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God, our judge, show us Your mercy and grant us Your peace this day. 
Judge between the nations and arbitrate among Your peoples, for these 
days are rife with discord around the world, among countrymen, and 
within our own communities.
  Beat the swords of war in Israel and Palestine, Iran and the Middle 
East, Russia and Ukraine into plowshares that they would prepare the 
soil for accord among enemies.
  Forge the spears of violence into tools of mediation that the fires 
of discontent would find no fuel and anger would no longer claim the 
lives of innocent people, especially schoolchildren.
  Settle the disputes among us wherever and however they are incited. 
Show us how we are to live. Let us know the goodness that is ours when 
we seek to dwell in harmony.
  Bestow Your blessing of peace on us this day, as we pray in the power 
of Your name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
