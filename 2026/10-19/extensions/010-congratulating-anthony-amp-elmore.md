# CONGRATULATING ANTHONY "AMP" ELMORE

**Speaking:** Cohen, Steve (C001068) [D-TN]

```
                 CONGRATULATING ANTHONY ``AMP'' ELMORE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE COHEN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Anthony ``Amp'' 
Elmore for his work in cultural diplomacy through the creation of the 
Tom Mboya Legacy District in the historic Black Memphis community of 
Orange Mound, which will occur on October 24, 2026--the anniversary of 
the founding of the United Nations in 1945. Mboya was a Kenyan labor 
leader with ties to the American labor movement and major American 
Civil Rights leaders and was assassinated in 1969. Mr. Elmore, a karate 
kick-boxing champion and documentary film maker, has been involved in 
bridging American-Kenyan relations for decades and founded the African 
Cultural Embassy in Orange Mound.
  Mr. Mboya met with then-Senator John F. Kennedy during his campaign 
for the presidency in 1960. Mr. Mboya urged Kenndey to support efforts 
to bring hundreds of East African foreign exchange students to the 
United States, eventually including Barack Obama, Sr., the future 
president's father. Mr. Elmore notes in preparation for the founding of 
the Mboya Legacy District later this month that Dr. Martin Luther King, 
Jr. delivered his ``I Have a Dream'' speech in August of 1963, 
President Kennedy, a promoter of the African independence movement in 
Kenya, was assassinated in November of 1963, and the nation of Kenya 
was founded in December of 1963, linking historic elements of the 
United States-Kenya relationship. He says that the founding of Kenya 
was an extension of the American Civil Rights movement. Mr. Elmore was 
also instrumental in forging ties between Orange Mound's Cherokee 
Elementary School and the Arya Steve Cohen Center for Cultural 
Diplomacy founded in 2024 in Kisumu, Kenya. I have long admired Mr. 
Elmore's work bridging United States relations with Kenya and wish him 
well as he embarks on this new chapter.

                          ____________________
```
