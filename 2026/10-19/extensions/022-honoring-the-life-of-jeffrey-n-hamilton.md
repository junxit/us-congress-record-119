# HONORING THE LIFE OF JEFFREY N. HAMILTON

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                HONORING THE LIFE OF JEFFREY N. HAMILTON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to honor the life of the late 
Pastor Jeffrey N. Hamilton, a faithful servant of God, an advocate for 
education, community activist, and devoted husband, father, 
grandfather, brother, and friend. I include in the Record the following 
proclamation:

       Whereas, in 1991, as a member of Mt. Tabor Missionary 
     Baptist Church, Pastor Jeffrey Hamilton was called into the 
     ministry. After moving his membership to Mt. Olive Missionary 
     Baptist Church, he was ordained to teach the word of God and 
     went on to serve as the youth minister at Greater Peace 
     Missionary Baptist Church. In 2006, he was called to pastor 
     at Shady Grove Missionary Baptist Church and then went on to 
     establish his own church, the New Life Christian Worship 
     Center Family in Miami's Liberty City; and
       Whereas Pastor Hamilton was considered a beacon of light by 
     his congregation and his community, and was affectionately 
     called ``Coach Ham'' by the thousands of children he worked 
     with through numerous community organizations including the 
     Children of Inmates and The Drug Free Coalition, the Miami 
     Dade County School Board, the Healthy Mind Healthy Heart 
     Program, the Hosanna Community Baptist Church Foundation and 
     the 5000 Role Models of Excellence Project; and
       Whereas Pastor Hamilton believed that education is an 
     essential building block to success and, as a strong believer 
     in the expression ``practice what you preach,'' went back to 
     school to further his education and pursed a Master's Degree 
     in Divinity and Special Education from St. Thomas University 
     and from Nova University; and
       Whereas Pastor Hamilton's passion for students was 
     unmatched and he touched the lives of elementary, middle and 
     high school students across Miami-Dade; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to pay tribute to the late Pastor Jeffrey Hamilton, 
     for his passion and devotion to God, his church, to our 
     students and community.

                          ____________________
```
