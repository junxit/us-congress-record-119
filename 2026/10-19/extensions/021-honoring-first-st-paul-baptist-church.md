# HONORING FIRST ST. PAUL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING FIRST ST. PAUL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor First St. Paul Baptist 
Church, located in the historic Bayhills community near Bunkie, 
Louisiana. The church traces its origins to the era of slavery and is 
recognized in its congregational history as the oldest African American 
church in Avoyelles Parish. Its beginnings are associated with the 
former Leinster Plantation, where enslaved African Americans gathered 
to worship, often in secret under the cover of darkness. That 
determination to worship together has marked the church ever since.
  Although the church's exact founding date is unknown, a deed dated 
October 23, 1875, documents the conveyance of approximately two acres 
of land for a church, a schoolhouse, and a burial ground. Together, 
they show the church was a place to worship, to learn, and to lay loved 
ones to rest. Under its first pastor, Reverend Patrick Harris, the 
congregation established a lasting foundation of faith and fellowship 
during the transition from slavery to freedom, becoming a source of 
stability and hope for families building new lives.
  Destructive storms struck in 1885 and again in 1945, but First St. 
Paul persevered, rebuilt its house of worship, and continued its 
ministry. After the 1945 storm, Amazon Baptist Church welcomed the 
congregation until its building could be restored, a sign of the 
fellowship among the area's churches.
  The church's influence extended throughout the area as descendants of 
its founding families helped establish and serve other congregations, 
including Second St. Paul Baptist Church, Amazon Baptist Church, and 
First Union Baptist Church. In this way, First St. Paul has 
strengthened the faith community of Bunkie and the surrounding area 
well beyond its own walls.
  Today, under the leadership of Reverend John Calbert, First St. Paul 
continues its more than 160-year legacy of faith, resilience, and 
service to the Bayhills and greater Bunkie communities. Mr. Speaker, I 
ask my colleagues to join me in honoring First St. Paul Baptist Church 
and its congregation for their enduring faith and service to the people 
of Bunkie. May First St. Paul continue to strengthen the faith 
community of Bunkie for generations to come.
```
