# RECOGNIZING THE HISTORY AND SERVICE OF FIRST BAPTIST CHURCH OF BLACKSBURG

**Speaking:** Norman, Ralph (N000190) [R-SC]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE HISTORY AND SERVICE OF FIRST BAPTIST CHURCH OF 
                               BLACKSBURG

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RALPH NORMAN

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. NORMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize First Baptist 
Church of Blacksburg, South Carolina, for nearly 150 years of ministry.
  First Baptist Church of Blacksburg was organized on December 4, 1876, 
as Berea Baptist Church. It grew out of Antioch Baptist Church, which 
originated from Buffalo Baptist Church, one of the oldest churches in 
South Carolina. Reverend W.L. Brown served as its first pastor and led 
a congregation of fifty. The original church building stood near the 
corner of Highway 29 and Mountain Street, where it served not only as a 
place of worship but also as a school and meeting hall.
  As the congregation grew, church leaders began planning for a new 
facility. In 1909, B. J. Gold, J.F. Cline, R.A. Westbrooks, Mary Earle, 
and Ed Turner formed a committee to find a suitable location. After an 
initial land donation fell through, the church purchased property at 
the corner of Cherokee and Rutherford Streets for $500 under the 
leadership of Reverend C.W. Payseur. Construction began in the spring 
of 1912, and the first service was held that June. With the move to its 
new building, the congregation adopted the name First Baptist Church of 
Blacksburg.
  Over the decades, First Baptist Church of Blacksburg has continued to 
grow and adapt to meet the needs of its congregation and the 
surrounding community. As the church expanded, it invested in its 
facilities and created more opportunities for worship, fellowship, and 
community activities. These efforts reflect the commitment of 
generations of members who have worked to sustain the church's ministry 
and ensure it remains an important part of life in Blacksburg. Through 
periods of growth and change, the congregation has remained dedicated 
to sharing the Gospel and providing a place where people can gather in 
faith and fellowship.
  In January 2025, First Baptist Church of Blacksburg called Dr. Wayne 
Brown as its 31st pastor. Dr. Brown is the first pastor in the church's 
history to have been raised in the congregation, ordained there, and 
called to lead it. His appointment is a meaningful milestone for the 
church and reflects the strong ties it has maintained with the 
community across generations. As the congregation looks to the future, 
it does so with a history shaped by faithful leadership and the 
continued commitment of its members.
  As First Baptist Church of Blacksburg approaches its 150th 
anniversary this December, its history serves as a reminder of the 
lasting contributions that local churches make to their communities. 
For nearly a century and a half, the congregation has brought people 
together through worship, fellowship, and service. Its continued 
presence in Blacksburg reflects the faith, generosity, and dedication 
of those who have helped build and sustain its ministry. The church's 
legacy is not only found in its history but also in the generations of 
people whose lives have been touched by its work.
  I congratulate First Baptist Church of Blacksburg on nearly 150 years 
of ministry and commend its congregation for its longstanding 
commitment to the people of Blacksburg and the surrounding community. I 
am grateful for the role the church has played in South Carolina and 
pray for God's continued blessings on its congregation and its ministry 
for generations to come.
```
