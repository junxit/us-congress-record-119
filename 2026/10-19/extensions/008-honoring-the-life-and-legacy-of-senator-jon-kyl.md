# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SENATOR JON KYL

**Speaking:** Ciscomani, Juan (C001133) [R-AZ]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SENATOR JON KYL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JUAN CISCOMANI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the incredible life 
and legacy of Jon Kyl, a towering figure in the history of Arizona, who 
sadly passed on September 17, 2026.
  Among the many thoughtful, forceful leaders our state has benefited 
from since joining the union in 1912, Jon Kyl was in a class all his 
own. His accomplishments as a member of this body and as a Senator are 
the stuff of legend. The Arizona that my family and seven and a half 
million others call home is a state that Jon Kyl helped shape.
  Born in Nebraska and raised in Iowa, he moved west, looking forward 
to the future ahead of him. Jon Kyl earned his bachelor's degree from 
the University of Arizona in 1964. This year, 2026, marks the 60th 
anniversary of his graduation from the University of Arizona law 
school. What he has achieved since then is an inspiration to anyone who 
aspires to a career in public service.
  Over the course of his 26 years in office, Jon Kyl forged a 
reputation as a tireless workhorse and a principled leader. He also was 
a formidable campaigner, an articulate messenger and a fierce debater. 
Unlike me and many of my colleagues, Jon Kyl never lost an election.
  His experience, judgment, and common sense are why Arizonans 
entrusted him with the responsibility to be our advocate in Washington. 
These are the same reasons I trusted him also. Among the very first I 
ever cast as a naturalized American, was a vote for Jon Kyl as United 
States Senator in 2006.
  Senator Kyl set a high standard for representing Arizona in Congress. 
I believe everyone who knows him can agree that he is the truest 
embodiment of the word ``statesman.'' He always prioritized the long-
term good of our country over party or personal gain, and he did so 
with a moral compass that was spot-on.
  Senator Kyl had a significant impact on me throughout the years. He 
was generous with his time even before I was elected, and after I 
became a Member of Congress, he gave me invaluable advice that went 
beyond the job. He shared with me how to take care of my family while 
working for the people of Arizona, words of wisdom which I still hold 
to.
  I was extremely honored at the opportunity to speak on his life and 
legacy at the University of Arizona's inaugural Kyl Institute for 
National Security Forum in January. To know such a man as Jon Kyl was a 
privilege. I was proud to share how he impacted me and countless 
others, along with this amazing state we call home.
  Senator Kyl had an indelible influence in Arizona, and I am proud to 
have witnessed the ways in which he served so faithfully. I thank him 
for all the work he did. The great state of Arizona would not be the 
same today without it.
  In regard to his wife Caryll, his children and grandchildren, and all 
his loved ones and close friends, I am truly sorry for their loss. May 
my dear friend rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
