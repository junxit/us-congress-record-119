# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF MR. MIKE WALLACE

**Speaking:** Norman, Ralph (N000190) [R-SC]

```
              RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF MR. MIKE WALLACE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RALPH NORMAN

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. NORMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Mike Wallace 
for his many years of service to South Carolina. Through 28 years of 
work with the York Baptist Association, Mike has helped strengthen 
local churches, I support missions, and bring people together through 
faith and service.
  Mike was born and raised in Charleston, South Carolina, before moving 
to York County in 1993. He answered his call to ministry during his 
freshman year at North Greenville University in 1980. He continued his 
education at Baptist College of Charleston and later earned a Master of 
Divinity from Southwestern Theological Seminary. These experiences 
helped prepare him for a life dedicated to ministry and serving others.
  As Mike began his work in ministry, he came to recognize a particular 
calling to missions. While serving as an associate pastor, he led his 
church on nine mission trips and witnessed firsthand the difference 
these efforts could make in people's lives. Seeing individuals and 
communities grow through these experiences helped shape his path and 
ultimately led him to dedicate his career to mission development and 
outreach.
  In 1998, Mike joined the York Baptist Association as Mission 
Development Director, helping local churches carry out their missions 
and reach more people through the Gospel. He later became Missions 
Strategist in 2016, a role that allowed him to deepen his understanding 
of York County and the needs of its communities. Through this work, he 
helped churches build connections, strengthen their outreach, and 
fulfill their shared mission of making disciples and reaching people 
for Christ. His years of service reflect a commitment to helping local 
congregations make a meaningful difference in the lives of those around 
them.
  Throughout his career, Mike has demonstrated that service is about 
more than holding a position. It is about building relationships, 
supporting others, and remaining committed to a purpose greater than 
oneself. His work has left a lasting mark on the York Baptist 
Association and the broader community. Through his leadership and 
dedication, he has helped strengthen the churches and relationships 
that will continue this work for years to come.
  Mike's commitment to faith and service has also been supported by his 
family. He and his wife, Tammy, have been married for more than 40 
years and have raised two sons, Michael and Joshua. Together, they have 
built a family grounded in faith, service, and commitment to others. 
Their support has been an important part of Mike's life and the work he 
has carried out throughout his career.
  I congratulate Mike Wallace on his retirement and thank him for 28 
years of dedicated service to the York Baptist Association and the 
people of South Carolina. His commitment to ministry, missions, and his 
community leaves a legacy that will continue to benefit others for 
generations to come.
```
