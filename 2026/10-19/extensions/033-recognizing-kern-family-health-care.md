# RECOGNIZING KERN FAMILY HEALTH CARE

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
                  RECOGNIZING KERN FAMILY HEALTH CARE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kern Family 
Health Care as it marks 30 years of providing quality health care to 
the people of Kern County.
  On July 1, 1996, the plan was established to ensure that working 
families and vulnerable residents in the Central Valley could see a 
doctor close to home. Three decades later, Kern Family Health Care 
serves as the county's only public and locally governed health plan, 
serving nearly 400,000 residents across Kern County.
  As the needs of the community have continued to increase, the 
quantity and quality of the services offered by Kern Family have as 
well. Beyond traditional medical coverage, it offers behavioral health 
services, maternity and pediatric care, chronic disease management, 
transportation services, and even programs that help patients find 
stable housing. In 2026, Kern Family Health Care extended its reach to 
seniors by beginning to serve dual-eligible Medicare and Medi-Cal 
members.
  The plan has also invested millions of dollars in the community, 
supporting local clinics, hospitals, and thousands of jobs. It hosts 
and sponsors a wide variety of events throughout Kern, including 
landmark regional health summits and career fairs that recruit local 
healthcare professionals.
  The dedicated work of Kern Family's employees, its governing board, 
physicians, and community partners have made a lasting change in the 
lives of countless people. Chief Executive Officer Emily Duran has also 
played an integral role in the success of the organization, 
contributing over 25 years of experience in healthcare and social 
support services. Together, these individuals have made this milestone 
possible.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in congratulating Kern Family Health Care on 30 years of 
providing attentive, quality health care to hundreds of thousands of 
individuals in need across Kern County. I wish them continued success 
for decades to come.
```
