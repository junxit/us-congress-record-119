# HONORING SALEM BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                     HONORING SALEM BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Salem Baptist Church 
of Bunkie, Louisiana, which marks its 120th anniversary this year. The 
church was founded on March 6, 1906, by Reverend Albert Luther Hayward, 
Sr., with four members gathered in his home. The congregation purchased 
property later that year and constructed its first house of worship in 
1910. Throughout its 120-year history, Salem has contributed 
significantly to the spiritual, educational, and social development of 
Bunkie's African American community, and it has been a steady presence 
in the faith community of Bunkie from its earliest days.
  During segregation, Salem served as a location for public school 
classes, as documented in the history of Bunkie Colored-Carver High 
School. By opening its doors to students, the church helped make 
education possible for the children of its community. Salem also hosted 
a community meeting organized by Principal Forest Paul Augustine during 
his 1945 to 1956 administration, which led to the establishment of the 
Community School Club. The club's efforts raised more than $1,700 
toward the construction of a school auditorium, showing how the church 
served as a gathering place where neighbors came together to improve 
their schools.
  In February 1942, Salem organized and sponsored Boy Scout Troop No. 
106 under Scoutmaster A.J. Fisher, providing leadership and citizenship 
opportunities for African American boys. This service to young people 
reflects the church's belief that its ministry extends beyond Sunday 
worship to the development of future community leaders.
  Salem's commitment to community advancement continued through the 
expansion of its educational and outreach ministries, including the 
construction of an educational facility in 1981. During the pastorate 
of Reverend James L. Sampson, the Albert L. Hayward Community 
Development Corporation was established to promote community 
improvement and development, carrying the name of the church's founder 
into the work of community building.
  Under the leadership of Reverend Greg Jackson, pastor since 2006, 
Salem continues its mission of Christian education, youth development, 
community outreach, and spiritual service. Today, the church remains an 
enduring institution in the religious and community life of Bunkie, and 
its history of service continues to strengthen the families and 
congregations around it.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Salem Baptist 
Church and its congregation on 120 years of faith, education, and 
service to the people of Bunkie. May Salem continue to strengthen the 
faith community of Bunkie for generations to come.
```
