# HONORING BETH SHOLOM TEMPLE ON 90 YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
           HONORING BETH SHOLOM TEMPLE ON 90 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Beth 
Sholom Temple of Fredericksburg, Virginia, as it celebrates 90 years of 
faithful service, steadfast leadership, and unwavering commitment to 
the people of Fredericksburg and the Commonwealth of Virginia.
  Since its founding on May 16, 1936, Beth Sholom Temple has 
thoughtfully served and welcomed the Community of Greater 
Fredericksburg. The Temple and its community have remained a beacon of 
love and acceptance for not just the members, but also the community 
around them.
  The temple has contentiously served as a reliable and comforting 
gathering place for groups like the Virginia Youth of Beth Sholom and 
it has remained committed to educating both children and adults in the 
Jewish tradition. The temple's identity is deeply rooted in helping and 
caring for those in the community who are in need. For years, the local 
community has been blessed with Beth Sholom's Social Action volunteers 
who serve and help the greater Fredericksburg area in the Jewish 
tradition of ``tikkun olam'' (repairing the world). From weekly bread 
donations and Thanksgiving boxes for the homeless to a dedicated Caring 
Committee that supports members in times of need, the temple fully 
embodies its commitment to serving others.
  Selflessness does not describe the members of Beth Sholom well 
enough. In times without a Rabbi, the Ladies Committee organized the 
religious school, continued fundraising events, and maintained the 
Temple's financial records, allowing their members to continue to serve 
the community around them.
  Under the kind and ambitious early leadership of Rabbi Ulrich Steuer, 
the temple community built its first location in Fredericksburg, 
cementing a legacy of acceptance and generosity. What began as a small 
group of Jewish families who met in the Parish House of a local 
Episcopal Church, is now a diverse and inclusive community that 
supports everyone's unique spiritual journey and has a thriving Jewish 
community in Virginia. All who experience the joy and kindness of Beth 
Sholom understand what it means for a group of strangers to become a 
family.
  I want to honor and thank Beth Sholom's leadership and every member 
of the Temple on this remarkable 90-year milestone. As the temple 
celebrates ``90 Years of Light'', let this be a reminder that the 
occasion is about more than an anniversary. It is a celebration of the 
people, traditions, and shared experiences that have shaped the 
temple's story and will carry it forward for generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Beth 
Sholom Temple for its enduring faith, exceptional service, and profound 
contributions to the Fredericksburg community.
```
