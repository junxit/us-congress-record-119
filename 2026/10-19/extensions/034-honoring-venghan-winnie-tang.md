# HONORING VENGHAN "WINNIE" TANG

```
                    HONORING VENGHAN ``WINNIE'' TANG

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Ms. Wilson of FLORIDA. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District, of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor Ms. Venghan 
``Winnie'' Tang, a dedicated community advocate with a powerful voice 
who has made significant contributions to South Florida and around the 
country. I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas Ms. Tang is a deeply committed activist who has 
     devoted her professional career to promoting the interests 
     and well-being of the Asian Pacific American community;
       Whereas, Ms. Tang has enriched the lives of countless South 
     Floridians by administering afterschool tutoring programs, 
     encouraging voter registration and census participation, and 
     advocating for immigration reform, legal and civil rights, 
     homeless reform, healthcare, and women's affairs;
       Whereas Ms. Tang was the first Asian American to serve on 
     the Miami-Dade County Democratic Executive Committee, and has 
     organized voter registration drives and ``Get Out the Vote'' 
     campaigns in the Asian community;
       Whereas, Ms. Tang's excellence in leadership, advocacy, 
     non-profit work, and community service has been recognized by 
     various organizations through awards, accomplishments, and 
     appointments, including chairing

[[Page E1105]]

     the Asian-American Steering Committee which led to the 
     creation of the Asian-American Advisory Board in Miami-Dade 
     County, being the first Chinese American woman to receive the 
     Miami-Dade County Park & Recreation Department's in the 
     Company of Women Award in 2002, receiving the Florida 
     Minority Reinvestment Community Coalition's Minority Advocacy 
     Award in 2006, and receiving the Inaugural Honors Award 
     Saluting South Florida's 21st Century Educators by Miami 
     Gardens Excellence in Education Council (MGEEC) in 2013; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend Ms. Venghan ``Winnie'' Tang for her years of 
     commitment and faithful service to our community.
```
