# HONORING KAREN ARONOWITZ

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                        HONORING KAREN ARONOWITZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District, of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor retiring 
United Teachers of Dade, Union President Karen Aronowitz for her 
leadership and dedication to our students, teachers, parents and 
schools. I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas Karen Aronowitz served three terms as President of 
     United Teachers of Dade (UTD), represented 32,000 teachers 
     and education support professionals, led successful reform 
     initiatives, including the School Improvement Zone for 
     underperforming schools within the Miami-Dade County Public 
     Schools system, which served as a national model for improved 
     school performance;
       Whereas, Mrs. Aronowitz created professional improvement 
     and development programs for educators through the 
     establishment UTD's Education Summit and the UTD Teaching 
     Excellence Foundation;
       Whereas, Mrs. Aronowitz has been featured in numerous 
     national publications, such as The Wall Street Journal and 
     Who's Who among American Teachers, for her work and research 
     in education, for which she has also received several awards 
     including the Jewish Museum of Florida's ``Breaking the Glass 
     Ceiling Award, the Monty Trainer Chairman's Award for 
     Speaking UP for Public Education and honored as a ``Trail 
     Blazer for Education by the Bethune-Cookman University Miami-
     Dade Alumni Association;
       Whereas, Mrs. Aronowitz serves on the American Federation 
     of Teachers Executive Council, is a member of AFT's Human 
     Rights and Community Relations Committee and Democracy 
     Committee, serves on the United Way of Miami-Dade Public 
     Policy Committee and is a member of the Miami-Chapter of the 
     NAACP; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend Karen Aronowitz for her dedication and commitment to 
     our students, our teachers, our parents and our schools.

                          ____________________
```
