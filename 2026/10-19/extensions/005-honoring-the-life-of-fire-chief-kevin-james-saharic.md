# HONORING THE LIFE OF FIRE CHIEF KEVIN JAMES SAHARIC

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE OF FIRE CHIEF KEVIN JAMES SAHARIC

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Fire Chief 
Kevin James Saharic of Lebanon Borough, New Jersey, who devoted more 
than 45 years to serving others through the fire and rescue service. 
His extraordinary dedication to the safety of our community earned the 
admiration and respect of all who knew him.
  Kevin Saharic was born on January 16, 1964, in Red Bank, New Jersey, 
to Alexander ``Lex'' and Hazel (Minaldi) Saharic. He spent his early 
childhood in Sea Bright before moving to Lebanon in 1974, where he 
remained a lifelong resident.
  Kevin was the beloved spouse of Michele Kimberly Saharic. High school 
sweethearts, they married in 1985 and recently celebrated 40 years of 
marriage, which is a testament to their enduring love and partnership. 
Their daughters, Marlaina and Ali, and grandchildren, Blake, Matthew, 
Owen, Hazel, and Carter, were their pride and joy.
  Kevin dedicated his life to protecting others. He joined the Lebanon 
Fire Company in 1980 and served as Chief for 23 years. At the time of 
his passing, he served as Captain and Public Service Director for the 
Borough of Lebanon. Kevin was also a member of the Hunterdon County 
Fire Chiefs Association, a founding member of the FAST Team in 
Hunterdon County and the North Hunterdon Fire Alliance (Squad 51), and 
Secretary for the New Jersey State Firemen's Relief Association.
  Kevin led with integrity and distinction. He received the Medal of 
Valor for rescuing a downed firefighter and a civilian during a fire. 
In 2024, Kevin was honored as Grand Marshal of the Lebanon Borough 
Fourth of July Parade in recognition of their countless contributions 
to the community. Throughout his distinguished career, Kevin 
consistently placed the safety of others before his own.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Kevin 
Saharic's life and unparalleled civic commitment. We are profoundly 
grateful for his decades of dedicated service. I extend my sincere 
condolences to Kevin's family and friends as they mourn his loss.
```
