# HONORING RICHARD M. "DICK" TRECKELO ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Yakym, Rudy (Y000067) [R-IN]

```
      HONORING RICHARD M. ``DICK'' TRECKELO ON HIS 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RUDY YAKYM, III

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Richard M. ``Dick'' 
Treckelo of Elkhart, Indiana. He is a veteran, a lifelong Hoosier, and 
a man who has given a century to the community he loves. On October 22, 
2026, Dick will turn 100.
  Dick was a college student at the University of Michigan when his 
country called and he answered. He set his studies aside and served in 
the United States Army Air Forces in 1945 and 1946, as World War II 
came to a close, earning the designation of sharpshooter. He did what 
his generation did. He served, he came home, and he got to work.
  Home was always Elkhart. Dick finished his degrees in Ann Arbor, then 
came back to practice law beside his father, Frank, a respected 
attorney who later served as City Judge and Elkhart Superior Court 
Judge. Dick built a practice of his own and later became a partner at 
Barnes & Thornburg, guiding local families and businesses through four 
decades of growth. He did not chase a bigger city. He chose to build 
his life and his career right here.
  That is who Dick is. Steady. Humble. Loyal to the people and the 
place that raised him.
  He met his wife, Anne, on a blind date in 1954. They shared nearly 65 
years of marriage and raised two daughters, Marla and Mary. Today, Dick 
is a proud grandfather of five and great-grandfather of six, a family 
that carries his example forward.
  Service to country. Devotion to family. A lifetime of quiet, faithful 
work in his hometown. Dick Treckelo embodies the very best of Indiana's 
2nd District.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Dick for his 
service and wishing him a very happy 100th birthday.
```
