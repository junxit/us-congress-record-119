# HONORING COMMUNITY CHAPEL CHURCH OF GOD IN CHRIST

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
           HONORING COMMUNITY CHAPEL CHURCH OF GOD IN CHRIST

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Community Chapel 
Church of God in Christ of Opelousas, Louisiana, which has spent 38 
years in worship, evangelistic outreach, and service to the people of 
its community. The church was organized in July 1988 by Reverend Frank 
Ford, family, and friends, who found a building at 770 S. Oak Street in 
Opelousas suitable for worship. The congregation held its first worship 
experience there in August 1988, and in September 1988, Reverend Ford 
was appointed pastor of Community Chapel Church of God in Christ.
  With the approval and favor of God, the church's growth was 
encouraging and inspiring. The congregation worshiped at 770 S. Oak 
Street until April 1991, when it purchased a worship center at 1606 
College Avenue in Opelousas. In that new home, continued growth has 
inspired and encouraged the congregation to worship God and carry on 
its evangelistic outreach, bringing more families into the life of the 
church and adding to the strength of the faith community of Opelousas.
  Over 38 years, the Community Chapel family has built a family life 
center and purchased properties in the area. These investments reflect 
a church that has put down roots in Opelousas and has planned for the 
future, giving the congregation more space to gather, worship, and 
serve.
  The church's motto, ``people helping people,'' captures its approach 
to ministry and its history of service. In 38 years, Community Chapel 
has touched the lives of many people, both in the city of Opelousas and 
beyond it. A church that lives out that motto strengthens families, 
supports its neighbors, and encourages other believers by example, and 
it has become an important part of the faith community of Opelousas.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Community 
Chapel Church of God in Christ and its congregation for 38 years of 
faith, outreach, and service to the people of Opelousas. The church 
continues to grow and to experience the blessings of God, and I wish it 
many more years of ministry and community service.
```
