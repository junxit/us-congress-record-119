# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MICHEAL ALLEN ALEXANDER

**Speaking:** Veasey, Marc A. (V000131) [D-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MICHEAL ALLEN ALEXANDER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARC A. VEASEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. VEASEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life and 
legacy of Micheal Allen Alexander, a devoted husband, beloved family 
member, and faithful servant of God.
  At a very young age, Micheal committed his life to Christ at Victory 
Missionary Original Baptist Church in Dallas, Texas. He was ordained as 
a deacon, where he served faithfully, demonstrating deep commitment to 
his faith and to serving others.
  A proud graduate of L.G. Pinkston High School in Dallas, Micheal 
distinguished himself academically and musically. He was an 
accomplished trumpet player in the school band and graduated with 
honors as President of the Class of 1968. His talent was evident from 
an early age and remained hallmarks of his life. Later, he pursued a 
career in the United States Postal Service, working in the local 
Network Distribution Center in Dallas for over 30 years before 
retiring, where he met his current wife, Mrs. Annie Alexander.
  Micheal shared his God-given gifts and talents as a member of 
Victory's male chorus and sanctuary choir. He sang with the Eddie Clark 
and the Voices of Resurrection Community Choir and was a member of the 
quartet Roy Jones and the Sensational Jubilees. Micheal was known for 
his faith, kindness, musical gifts, and his commitment to his community 
touched the lives of many.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the life and 
legacy of Micheal Allen Alexander. His memory will continue to be 
cherished by his wife, family, friends, church family, colleagues, and 
the Dallas community.
```
