# HONORING DR. ABRAHAM ABUCHOWSKI

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
                    HONORING DR. ABRAHAM ABUCHOWSKI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Dr. Abraham Abuchowski for his 
extraordinary contributions to Lebanon Township, New Jersey, and his 
lifesaving leadership in drug development and delivery.
  For nearly six years, Dr. Abuchowski has served on the Lebanon 
Township Committee, helping oversee the food pantry, senior care, and 
veterans' assistance programs.
  After earning a bachelor's degree and Ph.D. in biochemistry from 
Rutgers University, Dr. Abuchowski transformed drug development through 
innovations such as Oncaspar, a treatment for childhood leukemia, and 
PEGylation, a technology that can make medications safer, longer 
lasting, and more affordable. Through Enzon, he helped advance New 
Jersey's biotechnology industry and expand access to essential 
pharmaceuticals. Dr. Abuchowski also served as the inaugural president 
of what is now BioNJ, New Jersey's first biotechnology organization.
  Today, Dr. Abuchowski maintains a farm in Lebanon where he cares for 
chickens, ducks, and dogs. He is a licensed pilot who enjoys 
woodworking and the discipline of martial arts.
  Despite his remarkable achievements, he remains notably humble, once 
saying simply, ``I live very well.'' When colleagues in municipal 
government learned about his scientific contributions, they were 
surprised to discover how much this local farmer had achieved.
  Dr. Abuchowski has never been one to seek recognition, yet he could 
not be more deserving. He has dedicated his life to serving others and 
is astoundingly accomplished. The people of Lebanon Township, the State 
of New Jersey, and our Nation owe him a debt of gratitude. I invite my 
colleagues to join me in commemorating Dr. Abraham Abuchowski.

                          ____________________
```
