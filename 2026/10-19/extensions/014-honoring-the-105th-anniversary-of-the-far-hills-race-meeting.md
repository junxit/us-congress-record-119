# HONORING THE 105TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FAR HILLS RACE MEETING

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
      HONORING THE 105TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FAR HILLS RACE MEETING

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the 105th anniversary of the 
Far Hills Race Meeting, known locally as the Hunt, in Far Hills, New 
Jersey. This premier social, charitable, and sporting event that has 
raised an astounding $22 million to support local health-care 
organizations.
  The Far Hills Race Meeting began in 1870 as the Essex Hunt, a fox 
hunting event in Montclair, New Jersey in 1870. In 1913, its organizers 
incorporated as the Essex Fox Hounds and established the Farmer's Day 
Race Meeting to thank the farmers and landowners who permitted them to 
hunt on their property. The event included the New Jersey Hunt Cup 
Steeplechase, a race that remains part of the Far Hills Race Meeting 
today.
  In 1915, the event moved to the Grant B. Schley estate, now known as 
Moorland Farm, where the races have been held ever since. Today, the 
Hunt hosts the Grand National Steeplechase, the most prestigious race 
in American steeple chasing.
  With coverage from major sports outlets, the Far Hills Race Meeting 
continues to promote charity and community while introducing steeple 
chasing to audiences worldwide.
  I invite my colleagues to join me in commemorating the Hunt's 105th 
anniversary and wish the event continued success for many years to 
come.
```
