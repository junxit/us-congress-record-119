# THANKING POLITICAL CARTOONIST BILL DAY

**Speaking:** Cohen, Steve (C001068) [D-TN]

```
                 THANKING POLITICAL CARTOONIST BILL DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE COHEN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and send appreciation 
to the political

[[Page E1099]]

cartoonist Bill Day, who has permitted me to reprint his work in my 
weekly newsletter for the past several years. Bill is a particularly 
gifted artist and satirist with whom I became familiar with when he 
worked for my hometown newspaper, the Memphis Commercial Appeal. He is 
perhaps best known as an antagonist of the National Rifle Association, 
frequently rebuking it for preventing any form of sensible gun-
ownership or gun-use restrictions. Bill won the Robert F. Kennedy 
Journalism Award for editorial cartooning in 1985 and 2010 and the 
National Cartoonist Society award for the best editorial cartoon in 
1996. I look forward to his prodigious work product each week, which 
often skewers the Trump Administration's aberrant gaffes with nuanced 
humor. I want to thank Bill Day publicly for allowing me to showcase 
his clever insights and wish him well as he continues his ingenious 
work publicly exposing the lunatic politics of our time.

                          ____________________
```
