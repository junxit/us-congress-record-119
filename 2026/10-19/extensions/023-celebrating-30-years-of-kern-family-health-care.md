# CELEBRATING 30 YEARS OF KERN FAMILY HEALTH CARE

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
            CELEBRATING 30 YEARS OF KERN FAMILY HEALTH CARE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 30 years of 
outstanding service by Kern Family Health Care.
  Established on July 1, 1996, Kern Family Health Care was created to 
provide Medi-Cal coverage to Kern County residents. Within two years, 
it began enrolling Healthy Families Program members, and by 2006, its 
total enrollment exceeded 100,000. Today, it is the largest managed 
care health plan in Kern County providing Medi-Cal and Medicare 
coverage to thousands of local residents.
  Guided by a mission to improve member health through an integrated 
managed healthcare delivery system, Kern Family Health Care's service 
is built on four pillars: its members, its employees, its providers, 
and above all, the community.
  It benefits from the leadership of Chief Executive Officer Emily 
Duran, whose more than 25 years of experience in healthcare and social 
support services have strengthened the organization's impact, earning 
NCQA Health Plan and Health Equity Accreditations, and a place on 
Fortune's 2025 Best Workplaces in Health Care list.
  Kern Family Health Care became a central figure in our community 
through its advocacy and its work strengthening local health care 
systems. It serves seniors and people with disabilities; it launched a 
Health Homes program for members with multiple chronic conditions; and 
it began providing Medicare Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) 
coverage to those eligible for both Medi-Cal and Medicare. Today, Kern 
Family Health Care serves approximately 85 percent of all Medi-Cal 
enrollees in Kern and continues working to improve the health and well-
being of all members in its care.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating the Kern Family Health 
Care team for the past 30 years of care, advocacy, and excellence in 
serving the people of Kern County.
```
