# HONORING RAY MOU

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                            HONORING RAY MOU

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ray Mou. I 
include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Mr. Ray Mou, Director General of Taipei Economic 
     and Cultural Office (TECO), in Miami since September 2010, 
     has overseen trade relations between Florida and Taiwan that 
     have improved by 20 percent with an average bilateral trade 
     volume of more than $817 Million;
       Whereas, Mr. Mou has worked closely with the Taiwanese 
     Chambers of Commerce in Florida to explore and develop 
     business opportunities, such as trade missions between Miami 
     entrepreneurs and Taiwan;
       Whereas, Mr. Mou has worked to strengthen relations between 
     Taiwan and Miami political and business leaders by 
     coordinating annual visits;
       Whereas, Mr. Mou has worked to foster a relationship 
     between 32 universities to promote language and cultural 
     learning programs offered at the Taiwan Academy;
       Whereas, Mr. Mou has taken an active role in Haiti's 
     reconstruction after the earthquake by taking part in 
     humanitarian aid projects of the international charity NGO 
     ``Food for the Poor,''
       Whereas, Mr. Mou has worked to educate South Floridians 
     about Asian culture through Chinese gourmet cuisines, 
     traditional artistries and Taiwanese products;
       Whereas, Mr, Mou has spent the past three years working to 
     promote a strong and lasting business relationship not just 
     in South Florida but across the state; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida--
     commend Mr, Ray Mou, Director General of Miami's Taipei 
     Economic and Cultural Office, for his commitment to our 
     community's economic, educational and humanitarian growth

                          ____________________
```
