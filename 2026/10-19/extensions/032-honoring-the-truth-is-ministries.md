# HONORING THE TRUTH IS MINISTRIES

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                    HONORING THE TRUTH IS MINISTRIES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 19, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor The Truth Is 
Ministries of Opelousas, Louisiana, a church founded on love, 
compassion, and the belief that ministry must reach beyond the walls of 
the church to touch the everyday lives of families. The ministry was 
born from a vision placed in the hearts of Pastor Easton Shelvin and 
Lady Kizzy Shelvin while Pastor Shelvin was serving as pastor of Little 
Zion Baptist Church in Port Barre, Louisiana. It began as an outreach 
ministry with a mission to restore and strengthen families, and it was 
formally established as a church in 2009.
  In its early years, Pastor Shelvin and Lady Kizzy hosted youth 
concerts and family fun days in parks in several communities. Their 
special concern for underserved children led to the Toys for Tots 
Christmas concerts, at which national recording artist Paul Porter 
joined the ministry as bicycles, toys, money, and gift certificates 
were given to children and families. As the ministry served young 
people, its leaders saw another urgent need in strengthening marriages, 
and they began Marital Couples Night Outs and marriage seminars that 
gave couples encouragement, fellowship, and practical support. Many 
marriages experienced healing and restoration.
  The outreach ministry then needed a spiritual home where families 
could worship together and where fellowship would be rooted in love and 
the Word of God. With the help and support of their parents and family, 
Pastor Shelvin and Lady Kizzy established The Truth Is Ministries as a 
church. The first worship service was held at the Opelousas City North 
Park Civic Center, where much of the ministry's outreach had begun. A 
year later, the congregation moved to Highway 749, where it occupied 
two locations over five years, a season of growth and development. 
During this time, the ministry celebrated its first spiritual son, 
Christopher Lazard, who accepted his prophetic calling and later 
established Spirit of Elijah Life Institute Church. The church also 
endured difficult seasons, when some people walked away, yet the 
ministry continued to love them, and in time some returned.
  In 2015, The Truth Is Ministries moved to its present home at 619 
West Vine Street in Opelousas. United around a vision of restoration, 
the church family transfonned a building considered to have no value 
into a place of worship, hope, and community service. Pastor Shelvin 
often uses the restored sanctuary as a living illustration of the 
ministry's calling: ``If we can come together and restore a building 
considered to have no value, then we can come together and restore 
people whom the world has treated as though they have no value.'' The 
church has worked to put that conviction into action by preaching the 
Gospel, serving families, embracing those who have been overlooked, and 
believing that every life has God-given worth.
  For more than a decade, lives have been blessed, strengthened, and 
restored through the Word of God preached by Pastor Shelvin and the 
faithful service of Lady Kizzy Shelvin and the entire church family. 
The church lovingly remembers those who labored with it and have gone 
on to glory. The Truth Is Ministries has been a pillar of strength in 
Opelousas by guiding underserved children toward adulthood through 
faith, supporting families, and showing, in its own words, that it is 
``not a building'' but a people joined together by faith, love, and a 
shared commitment to restoration.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring The Truth Is 
Ministries and its congregation for their faith, compassion, and 
service to the families of Opelousas. May the ministry continue to 
restore and strengthen families, and to show that every life has God-
given worth, for generatIons to come.
```
