# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Carlton Gerrell, Tennessee Valley Community Church, Paris, 
Tennessee, offered the following prayer:
  Our dearest and most gracious Heavenly Father, I thank You for the 
opportunity to come today and pray in the Capital of our government, 
Father, that You set apart many years ago. I pray today, Father, for 
our leaders, our President, our Congressmen, and our Senators that, 
Father, we will, as people, submit to the authority and pray for them 
as You commanded in 1 Peter and in Romans. Father, they deserve all 
honor and glory, and I pray for their wisdom, their strength, and their 
discernment, Father, to lead us in a way that honors and glorifies You.
  Father, bless this time together, that You give them complete wisdom 
and earnest desire to glorify You, Father, the Lord of the universe, 
the Lord of all creation, and the Lord over this Nation.
  These things we pray in Your most precious Heavenly Son's name, the 
almighty name of Jesus.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
