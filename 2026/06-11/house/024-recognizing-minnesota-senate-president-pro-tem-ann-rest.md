# RECOGNIZING MINNESOTA SENATE PRESIDENT PRO TEM ANN REST

**Speaking:** Omar, Ilhan (O000173) [D-MN]

```
        RECOGNIZING MINNESOTA SENATE PRESIDENT PRO TEM ANN REST

  (Ms. OMAR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Minnesota Senate 
President Pro Tempore Ann Rest for over 40 years of service in the 
Minnesota Legislature.
  Senator Rest led with cogovernance, often holding listening sessions 
at Frankie's Chicago Style Pizza in New Hope where she heard directly 
from community members and let their voices lead her policymaking.
  She made history as the first and only legislator to chair the Taxes 
Committee in both the State house and the senate where she shaped the 
largest tax cut package in State history.
  That landmark legislation invested more than $1.5 billion in a child 
tax credit fund and included over $105 million to expand affordable 
housing in our State.
  I have been incredibly grateful for her steadfast leadership and 
unwavering commitment to bettering the lives of all Minnesotans, and I 
wish her the very best in her retirement.
  She has made an incredible mark on my life and the lives of so many 
young Minnesota legislators, and I am so grateful for her.

                          ____________________
```
