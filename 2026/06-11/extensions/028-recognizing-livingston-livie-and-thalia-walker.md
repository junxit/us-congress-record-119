# RECOGNIZING LIVINGSTON "LIVIE" AND THALIA WALKER

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
           RECOGNIZING LIVINGSTON ``LIVIE'' AND THALIA WALKER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Caribbean American 
Heritage Month to recognize two outstanding community leaders and 
entrepreneurs from Rochester, New York: Livingston ``Livie'' and Thalia 
Walker.
  For more than two decades, Livie and Thalia have enriched the 
Rochester community by sharing the vibrant culture, traditions, and 
hospitality of their native Jamaica through Livie's Jamaican 
Restaurant. What began as a small neighborhood business has grown into 
a beloved local institution, serving not only authentic Caribbean 
cuisine but also as a gathering place where residents can experience 
and celebrate the richness of Caribbean heritage.
  In 2005, the Walkers opened Livie's Imports Market on Chili Avenue in 
Rochester's 19th Ward, bringing a unique cultural offering to the 
community. Through years of hard work, dedication, and entrepreneurial 
spirit, the business expanded and ultimately found its current home in 
Gates. Throughout that growth, Livie and Thalia remained committed to 
preserving and sharing their heritage while fostering meaningful 
connections among people from all backgrounds.
  Their contributions extend far beyond their restaurant. Through their 
leadership and community engagement, they have created opportunities 
for cultural exchange and community celebration. As organizers of the 
annual Family Fun Day in Genesee Valley Park, they bring together 
families from across the region through live reggae music, sporting 
events, authentic Caribbean cuisine, and fellowship, strengthening the 
bonds that unite our diverse community.
  Livingston and Thalia Walker exemplify the entrepreneurial spirit, 
cultural pride, and commitment to community that have long enriched 
both Rochester and our Nation. Their dedication to sharing Caribbean 
culture, supporting local families, and fostering a sense of belonging 
has left a lasting impact on countless lives. It is with great pride 
that I recognize Livingston ``Livie'' Walker and Thalia Walker during 
Caribbean American Heritage Month and extend my sincere gratitude for 
their many contributions to the Rochester community.
```
