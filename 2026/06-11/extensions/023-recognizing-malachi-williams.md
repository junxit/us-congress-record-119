# RECOGNIZING MALACHI WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING MALACHI WILLIAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Caribbean American 
Heritage Month to pay tribute to a dedicated civil servant from 
Rochester, New York: Malachi Williams.
  Throughout his life, Malachi has exemplified the values of hard work, 
perseverance, and service to others. He began his career as a custodian 
at the Jewish Nursing Home before dedicating eighteen years of service 
to the City of Rochester. Through his work, he earned the respect of 
colleagues and community members alike, carrying out his 
responsibilities with diligence, humility, and an unwavering commitment 
to those around him.
  Even after retiring in 1998, Malachi's strong work ethic remained 
unchanged. Never one to remain idle, he embarked on a second career as 
a full-time taxi driver, continuing to serve members of the Rochester 
community for many years. Today, he finds joy in tending his backyard 
garden, cultivating fresh fruits and vegetables that he generously 
shares with family and friends, a reflection of the kindness and 
generosity that have defined his life.
  This June, Malachi celebrates a remarkable milestone: his 100th 
birthday. Reaching a century of life is an extraordinary achievement, 
and one made all the more meaningful by the positive impact he has had 
on countless individuals throughout those years. His dedication to 
family, commitment to service, and generosity of spirit have made him 
an inspiration to all who have had the privilege of knowing him.
  It is with great pride that I recognize Malachi Williams during 
Caribbean American Heritage Month and on the occasion of his 100th 
birthday. His life stands as a testament to the enduring values of 
service, perseverance, and community. On behalf of the Rochester 
community, I extend my heartfelt congratulations and best wishes as he 
celebrates this extraordinary milestone.

                          ____________________
```
