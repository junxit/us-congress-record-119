# RECOGNIZING THE 2026 FAIRFAX COUNTY VOLUNTEER SERVICE AWARDS RECIPIENTS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
RECOGNIZING THE 2026 FAIRFAX COUNTY VOLUNTEER SERVICE AWARDS RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Volunteer 
Fairfax and express my sincere appreciation to recipients of the 2026 
Annual Fairfax County Volunteer Service Awards.
  Established 50 years ago, Volunteer Fairfax matches the skills and 
interests of thousands of volunteers with the needs of local non-profit 
organizations. The success of this model and its impact on delivery of 
the services needed is beyond question; Volunteer Fairfax has been 
rated as one of the most effective community service organizations in 
the Nation.
  Each year, Volunteer Fairfax selects a few exceptional individuals, 
groups, or organizations to receive special recognition. I include in 
the Record the following names of the 2026 Fairfax County Volunteer 
Service Awards honorees:

       Barbara Pearson: Braddock District Champion
       Great Falls Trailblazers: Dranesville Community Champion
       Leah Lamba-Skidmore: Franconia District Champion
       Rosa Simon: Hunter Mill District Champion
       Nancy Moy: Mason District Champion
       Mary Paden: Mount Vernon District Champion
       Greater Oakton Community Association: Lauren Crum & Shelley 
     Deutch: Providence District Champion
       Arnie Daxe: Springfield District Champion
       Evan Braff: Sully District Champion
       Craig Dubishar: Gerald E. ``Gerry'' Connolly Lifetime 
     Achievement Award

  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me in commending Volunteer 
Fairfax for its decades of outstanding community service. I 
congratulate the recipients of the 2026 Fairfax County Volunteer 
Service Awards and thank them and the thousands of other local 
volunteers for their incredible contributions to our community. Their 
selfless dedication is worthy of our highest praise and is one of the 
main reasons that our community is often ranked as one of the best 
places in the country to live, work, and raise a family.

                          ____________________
```
