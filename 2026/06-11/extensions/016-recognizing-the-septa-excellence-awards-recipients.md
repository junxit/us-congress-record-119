# RECOGNIZING THE SEPTA EXCELLENCE AWARDS RECIPIENTS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```

special education across Fairfax County Public Schools.

students of all abilities, ensuring every child has the opportunity to 
thrive.
```
