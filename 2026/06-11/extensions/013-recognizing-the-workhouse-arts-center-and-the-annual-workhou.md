# RECOGNIZING THE WORKHOUSE ARTS CENTER AND THE ANNUAL WORKHOUSE BALL

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE WORKHOUSE ARTS CENTER AND THE ANNUAL WORKHOUSE BALL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Workhouse 
Arts Center and celebrate the annual Workhouse Ball, an event that 
highlights the importance of the arts and the lasting impact the 
Workhouse has had on Northern Virginia.
  The Workhouse Arts Center has become an important gathering place for 
artists, educators, performers, and residents from across our region. 
The Workhouse creates opportunities for people of all backgrounds to 
experience creativity and engage with the arts in meaningful ways.
  The annual Workhouse Ball serves as both a celebration of the arts 
and a reflection of the strong community that supports them. By 
bringing together artists, local leaders, businesses, and supporters 
from across Northern Virginia, the event highlights the important role 
cultural institutions play in strengthening community connections and 
enriching the quality of life throughout our region.
  The arts are an essential component to any vibrant and healthy 
community. They inspire creativity, encourage dialogue, preserve 
culture, and bring people together through shared experiences. 
Institutions like the Workhouse Arts Center help ensure that the arts 
remain accessible and vibrant within our community while supporting 
local artists and fostering the next generation of creative talent.
  I would also like to recognize the staff, volunteers, artists, and 
supporters whose dedication has helped the Workhouse Arts Center grow 
into one of Northern Virginia's leading cultural institutions. Their 
work continues to expand opportunities for artistic expression and 
community engagement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
Workhouse Arts Center and celebrating the annual Workhouse Ball and its 
contributions to the cultural life of Northern Virginia.
```
