# RECOGNIZING THE FAIRFAX CONNECTOR MAINTENANCE TEAM'S STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
RECOGNIZING THE FAIRFAX CONNECTOR MAINTENANCE TEAM'S STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
outstanding achievement of the Fairfax Connector maintenance team and 
to congratulate them on securing first place at the 2026 Virginia State 
Bus Roadeo.
  Technicians Jerry Salgado, Matthew Bender, and Martin Lopez earned 
top honors at this year's statewide competition, marking the fifth 
consecutive year that Fairfax Connector has claimed the championship. 
The Virginia State Bus Roadeo brings together transit professionals 
from across the Commonwealth to test their skills in real world 
scenarios, including vehicle inspection and troubleshooting challenges.
  This continued success speaks to the high level of expertise, 
preparation, and consistency demonstrated by this team. Their 
performance reflects both the technical knowledge and attention to 
detail required to maintain a safe and reliable public transit system.
  As a result of their first-place finish, the Fairfax Connector 
maintenance team will represent the Commonwealth of Virginia at the 
American Public Transportation Association International Bus Roadeo 
this month in Salt Lake City, Utah. This event brings together top 
transit maintenance teams from across North America for a premier 
showcase of technical skill and precision.
  While their work often takes place behind the scenes, the impact of 
their efforts is felt every day by the thousands of riders who rely on 
Fairfax Connector. This achievement offers

[[Page E566]]

a well-earned opportunity to celebrate their skill, dedication, and the 
vital role they play in keeping our community moving.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Jerry, 
Salgado, Matthew Bender, and Martin Lopez on their state championship 
and in wishing them continued success as they represent Virginia on the 
international stage.

                          ____________________
```
