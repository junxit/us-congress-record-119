# RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF LIEUTENANT COLONEL JOSE MANUEL PIZARRO MATOS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF LIEUTENANT COLONEL JOSE MANUEL PIZARRO 
                                 MATOS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lieutenant 
Colonel Jose Manuel Pizarro Matos upon his retirement from the United 
States Army after 37 years of distinguished service to our Nation.
  Throughout his remarkable and decorated career spanning nearly four 
decades, Lieutenant Colonel Pizarro Mates dedicated himself to 
advancing military medicine, protecting the health of our service 
members, and strengthening the Army Medical Department through 
scientific research and innovation. His work has directly contributed 
to the readiness and well-being of the men and women who serve our 
Nation.
  A deeply respected scientist and Army officer, Lieutenant Colonel 
Pizarro Matos earned national recognition within his field through 
decades of research, mentorship, and professional achievement. His 
contributions include extensive scientific publications, oversight of 
major research programs, and expert guidance to countless military and 
civilian professionals whose work continues to benefit the Armed Forces 
and the broader medical community.
  His service has been recognized through numerous military awards and 
honors, including the Order of Military Medical Merit, multiple 
Meritorious Service Medals, and designation as a leader in Army medical 
research and development. These accomplishments reflect a career 
defined by genuine excellence, integrity, and an unwavering commitment 
to serving others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Lieutenant Colonel Jose Manuel Pizarro Matos on his retirement and in 
thanking him for his extraordinary service to the United States Army 
and our Nation.
```
