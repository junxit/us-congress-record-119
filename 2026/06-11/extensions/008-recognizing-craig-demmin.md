# RECOGNIZING CRAIG DEMMIN

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING CRAIG DEMMIN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Caribbean American 
Heritage Month to pay tribute to an outstanding athlete and leader from 
Rochester, New York: Craig Demmin.
  Born in Trinidad and Tobago, Craig Demmin distinguished himself 
through an exceptional soccer career that spanned both the national and 
international stages. As a member of the Trinidad and Tobago National 
Senior Team, he earned more than twenty-five international caps, 
representing his country with pride and distinction. His professional 
career took him across the globe, from Scotland to Tampa, before 
ultimately bringing him to Rochester, where he would leave a lasting 
impact both on and off the field.
  During his tenure with the Rochester Rhinos, Craig established 
himself as one of the most respected players in franchise history. A 
former team captain and four-time Team Most Valuable Player, he 
exemplified leadership and excellence in competition. His achievements 
earned the admiration of teammates, coaches, and fans alike, while his 
character and sportsmanship made him a role model throughout the 
Rochester community.
  Beyond his athletic accomplishments, Craig has remained deeply 
committed to serving others. He has devoted countless hours to 
mentoring young athletes and supporting local soccer programs, sharing 
his knowledge and experience with the next generation. His generosity 
extends far beyond the soccer field. For more than a decade, Craig has 
sponsored numerous families during the holiday season and throughout 
the year, providing support and encouragement to those in need.
  Craig Demmin's dedication to athletics, mentorship, and community 
service exemplifies the values of generosity and perseverance that have 
long enriched the Rochester community. It is with great pride that I 
recognize Craig Demmin during Caribbean American Heritage Month and 
thank him for the lasting impact he continues to make on the lives of 
others.
```
