# RECOGNIZING CLAY OSBORNE

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING CLAY OSBORNE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Caribbean American 
Heritage Month to recognize a dedicated leader from Rochester, New 
York: Clay Osborne.
  Born in Panama, Clay Osborne has built a distinguished career 
characterized by leadership, service, and a commitment to helping 
others succeed. As Founder and President of True Insights Consulting, 
he has earned widespread respect for his expertise in executive 
coaching, leadership development, and organizational effectiveness. 
Drawing upon decades of experience across the corporate and 
governmental sectors, Clay has devoted his professional life to 
empowering individuals and strengthening organizations.
  Beyond his professional achievements, Clay has demonstrated an 
unwavering dedication to the Rochester community. His extensive civic 
involvement includes service as Chair of the Board of ESL Federal 
Credit Union and the Center for Governmental Research. He is also a 
founding member of the Sigma Pi Phi Foundation and a charter member of 
the Rochester Area Community Foundation's African American Giving 
Circle, reflecting his longstanding commitment to expanding opportunity 
and fostering community engagement.
  Clay's leadership extends to numerous organizations dedicated to 
developing future leaders and promoting inclusive excellence. Through 
his service on the advisory board of the Parent Leadership Training 
Institute and as Chair of the Workforce Diversity Network, he has 
worked tirelessly to advance diversity, equity, and inclusion while 
creating pathways for individuals to thrive in their personal and 
professional lives.
  Clay Osborne's contributions to the Rochester community exemplify the 
values of leadership, service, and perseverance that have enriched our 
Nation. His dedication to uplifting others and strengthening the 
institutions that serve our communities has left a lasting and 
meaningful impact. It is with great pride that I recognize Clay Osborne 
during Caribbean American Heritage Month and extend my sincere 
gratitude for his many contributions to Rochester and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
