# RECOGNIZING THE DERBY SMOKE ON THE PLAINS BBQ AND MUSIC FESTIVAL'S 15TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Estes, Ron (E000298) [R-KS]

```
RECOGNIZING THE DERBY SMOKE ON THE PLAINS BBQ AND MUSIC FESTIVAL'S 15TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RON ESTES

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. ESTES. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 15th anniversary of 
the Derby Smoke on the Plains BBQ and Music Festival. This weekend, the 
residents of Derby, in my home district of Kansas, will be celebrating 
their 15th annual Smoke on the Plains BBQ and Music Festival.
  This event is more than just good food and good music, though that 
would be reason enough to celebrate. It is an event that brings 
together the local community and BBQ masters from across the region, 
with families and friends of all ages finding various ways to enjoy 
life together.
  Located in High Park, the festival also showcases a premier gathering 
place for Kansas families. It truly represents an active city with 
amenities that improve the quality of life for its residents.
  I am honored to have this exciting festival in Kansas, and 
celebrating their 15th year in 2026 is extra special. As Derby 
celebrates the 15th annual Smoke on the Plains, our Nation is preparing 
to celebrate the 250th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of 
Independence. A lot has changed over the past quarter of a millennium, 
but I imagine that our Founding Fathers would be pleased to see the 
patriotism and community pride that is taking place in Derby.
  While this year is sure to be exciting, I look forward to seeing this 
festival grow for many years to come. I encourage all Kansans to 
celebrate the 15th anniversary of Derby Smoke on the Plains as we 
prepare for America's semiquincentennial in less than a month.
```
