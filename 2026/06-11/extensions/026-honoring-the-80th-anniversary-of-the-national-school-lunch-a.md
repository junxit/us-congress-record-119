# HONORING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NATIONAL SCHOOL LUNCH ACT

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

```
     HONORING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NATIONAL SCHOOL LUNCH ACT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM WALBERG

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, 80 years ago this month, the National 
School Lunch Act was signed into law--and for eight decades, this 
program has delivered on a simple promise: no child should sit in a 
classroom too hungry to learn.
  As Chairman of the Education and Workforce Committee, I am proud of 
what Congress has done to strengthen school nutrition. President Trump 
signed the Whole Milk for Healthy Kids Act into law--returning whole 
and 2 percent milk to school cafeterias for the first time in over a 
decade, supporting Michigan's dairy farmers and giving Michigan's 
parents and children exactly what they deserve: real nutrition and real 
choice.
  Secretary Rollins and this administration also understand that good 
nutrition is the foundation of student success--in the classroom, in 
the community, and in life.
  In Michigan's 5th District and across this great nation, nearly 30 
million children are served school meals every school day. This 
administration is committed to making sure those meals are nutritious, 
locally sourced, and worthy of the children who receive them.
  On this 80th anniversary, I am proud to stand with President Trump 
and Secretary Rollins in ensuring our young people have the building 
blocks for a healthy future.

                          ____________________
```
