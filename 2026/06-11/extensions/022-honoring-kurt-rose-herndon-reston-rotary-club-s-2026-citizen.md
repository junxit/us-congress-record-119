# HONORING KURT ROSE, HERNDON-RESTON ROTARY CLUB'S 2026 CITIZEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
 HONORING KURT ROSE, HERNDON-RESTON ROTARY CLUB'S 2026 CITIZEN OF THE 
                                  YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
member of our Northern Virginia community, Kurt Rose, on the occasion 
of being named the 2026 Citizen of the Year by the Rotary Club of 
Herndon-Reston.
  For decades, Kurt Rose has exemplified the very best of public 
service through his unwavering commitment to children, families, and 
community organizations throughout the Herndon and Reston areas. His 
leadership reflects the Rotary ideal of ``Service Above Self,'' and his 
impact can be felt across countless lives in our region.
  In 2005, Kurt helped bring the Dolly Parton Imagination Library to 
the Herndon-Reston community, a transformative initiative that provides 
free books each month to young children from birth to age five. Through 
this effort, thousands of local children have gained access to books in 
their homes, fostering early literacy and a lifelong love of reading.
  Kurt is also the co-founder of Aspen Jewelry Designs, where he has 
demonstrated that business success and community service can go hand in 
hand. Through his work, he has supported numerous charitable 
initiatives and strengthened local partnerships. In addition, he co-
founded the Ro-Sigle Community Giving Foundation, which raises funds 
and awareness to expand access to literacy resources for children and 
families in need.
  Beyond these accomplishments, Kurt has generously given his time and 
support to organizations such as Kids R First, Cornerstones, the South 
Lakes High School PTSA Food Pantry, the South Lakes and Herndon High 
School Bands, the Reston YMCA, and the Reston Museum's Annual Cardboard 
Boat Regatta. He has also served as a dedicated board member of the 
Reston Historic Trust & Museum, helping to preserve the unique heritage 
of the Reston community.
  The Citizen of the Year distinction awarded by the Rotary Club of 
Herndon-Reston carries deep significance within the community. Each 
year, this honor is bestowed upon an individual whose service, 
leadership, and compassion have made a lasting and measurable impact on 
the lives of others. It is a recognition not only of professional 
achievement, but of a sustained commitment to uplifting neighbors, 
strengthening families, and building a more connected and resilient 
community.
  The annual celebration brings together community leaders, local 
organizations, families, and friends to reflect on the values that 
define Herndon and Reston: service, generosity, and civic engagement. 
It serves as a powerful reminder of the role that local leadership 
plays in addressing community needs and inspiring others to contribute 
their time and talents in meaningful ways.
  Kurt Rose's selection as the 2026 honoree reflects a lifetime of 
dedication to these ideals.

[[Page E570]]

His work in advancing childhood literacy, supporting local nonprofits, 
and fostering community partnerships embodies the very spirit of this 
award. Through both his professional endeavors and volunteer service, 
he has set a standard of leadership that continues to inspire those 
around him.
  On April 26, 2026, the Rotary Club of Herndon-Reston honored Kurt 
Rose at the Reston Community Center, celebrating a lifetime of service 
that has strengthened our community and uplifted those most in need.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Kurt 
Rose on this well-deserved recognition. His leadership, generosity, and 
steadfast dedication to others serve as an inspiration to us all and 
embody the very spirit of community that defines Northern Virginia.
```
