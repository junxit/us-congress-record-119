# RECOGNIZING NOELLE MCKINNON'S RETIREMENT

```
                RECOGNIZING NOELLE MCKINNON'S RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE EZELL

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2026

  Mr. EZELL Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Noelle McKinnon of Jones County, Mississippi, on her retirement after 
more than four decades of dedicated public service.
  Noelle began her career in 1985 as a Legal Assistant and Office 
Manager for attorney Franklin C. McKenzie, Jr., where she oversaw the 
day-to-day operations of a busy law practice and managed everything 
from legal filings to financial administration.
  In 1994, she began serving as the Certified Court Administrator for 
Mississippi's 19th Chancery Court District, a position she has held 
with distinction for more than 30 years. In that role, she managed 
court operations, worked closely with local governments, law 
enforcement, the Administrative Office of Courts, and the public, and 
played a vital role in ensuring the efficient administration of justice 
throughout Jones and Wayne Counties.
  Throughout her career, Noelle has been a respected leader in the 
judicial community, serving on numerous committees within the 
Mississippi County Administrators Association, including as President, 
and as the Lieutenant Governor's appointee to the Judicial Advisory 
Study Committee.
  The professionalism, dedication, and commitment to public service 
have left a lasting impact on Mississippi's judicial system and the 
countless people she has served over the years.
  As she begins this well-earned retirement, I join her family, 
friends, colleagues, and the people of Mississippi in thanking Noelle 
McKinnon for her many years of service and wishing her all the best in 
the years ahead.
```
