# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4744

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 4744

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4744

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 4744) to amend titles 10 and 38, United States 
     Code, and other Federal laws, to improve benefits for 
     veterans and the administration of the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs.

  Mr. THUNE. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
