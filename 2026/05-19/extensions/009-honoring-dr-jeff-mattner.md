# HONORING DR. JEFF MATTNER

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
                       HONORING DR. JEFF MATTNER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES R. BAIRD

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 19, 2026

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Dr. Jeff 
Mattner from Martinsville, Indiana, a devoted educator whose impact on 
education will be felt for years to come. For 14 years, Dr. Mattner 
served with the Association of Christian Schools International as 
Senior Director of the Central Division, supporting more than 600 
schools across 18 states with wisdom, integrity, and a servant's heart.
  Throughout his decades-long career as a teacher, principal, 
superintendent, and trusted advisor, he demonstrated an unwavering 
commitment to advancing educational opportunity and strengthening 
communities through faith-based education. Guided by his faith, Dr. 
Mattner also engaged in advocacy efforts that advanced the mission of 
Christian schools. His legacy endures in the countless students, 
educators, and families he influenced through his service, and my 
thoughts and prayers are with his family. May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
