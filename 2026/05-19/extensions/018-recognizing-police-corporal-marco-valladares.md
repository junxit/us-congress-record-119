# RECOGNIZING POLICE CORPORAL MARCO VALLADARES

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING POLICE CORPORAL MARCO VALLADARES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 19, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Police Corporal 
Marco Valladares for his outstanding service to the City of Euless as 
he retires after more than three decades in law enforcement and a 
lifetime dedicated to serving others.
  Before joining the Euless Police Department, Corporal Valladares 
proudly served in the United States Marine Corps from 1982 to 1990 as a 
RADAR Technician on the F-4 Phantom Jet Fighter, earning an Honorable 
Discharge for his service to our Nation.
  Throughout his distinguished career with the Euless Police 
Department, Corporal Valladares served in numerous roles, including 
Patrol Officer, Gang Officer, SWAT Officer, Bicycle Patrol Officer, 
Field Training Officer, Criminal Investigator, and School Resource 
Officer. Promoted to Police Corporal in 1993, he served with 
professionalism and integrity for more than 30 years.
  Among his many contributions, Corporal Valladares established the 
Euless Bicycle Patrol Program, strengthening relationships between 
officers and the community through neighborhood policing efforts. He 
also served on the Euless SWAT Team for more than a decade and later 
became a SWAT Hostage Negotiator and Criminal Investigator, bringing 
steady leadership to some of the department's most difficult 
situations.
  Since 2012, Corporal Valladares has served as the School Resource 
Officer at Central Junior High School, where he became a trusted mentor 
and role model to countless students and families in the Euless 
community.
  A dedicated public servant and lifelong learner, Corporal Valladares 
earned his Bachelor of Arts Degree in Criminal Justice, Summa Cum 
Laude, from Columbia College and obtained numerous advanced 
certifications throughout his career, including his Texas Master Peace 
Officer License.
  I thank Police Corporal Marco Valladares for his leadership and 
unwavering commitment to protecting and serving others and wish him all 
the best in his well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
