# HONORING DR. GONZALO SALAZAR'S SERVICE TO LOS FRESNOS CISD

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
       HONORING DR. GONZALO SALAZAR'S SERVICE TO LOS FRESNOS CISD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 19, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
commend Dr. Gonzalo Salazar for his dedication to the students, 
educators, and families of Los Fresnos Consolidated Independent School 
District (CISD).
  Over the course of his distinguished career, Dr. Salazar has served 
in numerous leadership roles within the Los Fresnos CISD, culminating 
in his tenure as Superintendent beginning in 2006. From his early 
service as Assistant Principal at Lopez-Riggins Elementary to his 
leadership as Principal of Palmer-Laakso Elementary and Olmito 
Elementary, Dr. Salazar consistently worked to expand opportunities and 
improve outcomes for students.
  Under his leadership, campuses earned recognition for college 
readiness from the Texas Education Agency, reflecting his commitment to 
equipping students with the tools they need to succeed. As 
Superintendent, Dr. Salazar prioritized educator support through 
professional development and innovative leadership initiatives, 
strengthening the district as a whole and fostering a culture of 
excellence. In recognition of these efforts, Dr. Salazar was named 
Region One Superintendent of the Year in 2024, reflecting his vision, 
leadership, and unwavering commitment to educational achievement.
  Dr. Salazar's career stands as a great example of the impact someone 
can have on an entire community. His legacy will live on in the success 
of the countless students and families he has served throughout South 
Texas.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas's 34th Congressional 
District, I commend Dr. Salazar for his exemplary service and extend my 
deepest gratitude for his contributions to the Los Fresnos community 
and beyond.
```
