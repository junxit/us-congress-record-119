# RECOGNIZING CHIEF JIM MEEK FOR 50 YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
           RECOGNIZING CHIEF JIM MEEK FOR 50 YEARS OF SERVICE

  (Mr. Joyce of Pennsylvania was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Jim Meek 
for 50 years of dedicated service as a volunteer with the Blue Ridge 
Fire and Rescue Company in Blue Ridge Summit, Pennsylvania.
  Over the years, Chief Meek has served the fire department in multiple 
active service and administrative roles, including fire chief for 25 
years.
  Chief Meek has been praised as a significant role model and mentor 
for the department's first responders.
  His 50-year commitment to public safety and the protection of 
property is an inspiration to constituents throughout the 13th 
Congressional District.
  For his bravery and selfless service, I express gratitude to Chief 
Meek for his enduring commitment to protecting communities, and I 
extend my best wishes to him in all future endeavors.


             Recognizing Blair County Conservation District

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Blair 
County Conservation District for 60 years of service as an advocate for 
natural resources and the county's agricultural community.
  Established in 1966, the Conservation District focuses on the 
protection and the preservation of Blair County's natural resources.
  With pride, I join in a yearlong commemoration of the Conservation 
District's milestone anniversary. For six decades, the district has 
played an essential role in supporting our communities through 
conservation programs that help to preserve Blair County's rich natural 
heritage for future generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I express gratitude to Conservation District Manager 
Donna Fisher, her board of directors, and the professional staff for 
expanding upon their distinguished legacy.


          Recognizing Brechbill & Helman Construction Company

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Brechbill & Helman Construction Company of Chambersburg, Pennsylvania, 
for 60 years of success as an industry leader.
  Founded in 1966 by Darrell Brechbill and John Helman, the company 
originated with three employees but possessed a vision that transformed 
them into a thriving construction business.
  Over the past six decades, the company has expanded its expertise 
across a wide range of projects.
  Brechbill & Helman have created a legacy of spaces that are created 
to strengthen the community and support businesses. Join me in 
congratulating company founders Darrell Brechbill and John Helman, Barb 
Runyon, and Matt Dice on this milestone anniversary.


         Honoring the Life and Public Service of Joseph Metzgar

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and 
public service of Joe Metzgar, who recently passed away in Altoona, 
Pennsylvania.
  For over 20 years, Joe served as Logan Township supervisor. Many have 
noted that Joe could put a smile on your face even during intense 
discussions at meetings.
  A proud United States Air Force veteran, Joe has been described as an 
honest and fair supervisor who took a commonsense approach to all 
issues.
  In addition, Joe was a field auditor for the Pennsylvania Department 
of Public Welfare.
  Mr. Speaker, as we pause to remember Joe's legacy of service, we also 
keep his entire family in our prayers. Join me in honoring the life of 
Joe Metzgar, a dedicated husband, father, and grandfather, who always 
had the best interests of Logan Township in his heart.
```
