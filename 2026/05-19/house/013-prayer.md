# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of all blessings and giver of every good gift, You need nothing 
from us, yet You desire that, in receiving the bounty of Your grace, we 
would want to give back to You and to Your creation out of our genuine 
love and appreciation that we have for You.
  In some ways, our gifts seem so small, even inconsequential, when 
compared to the vastness of Your boundless generosity. Give us 
confidence that You know our hearts and that You desire that we give 
from the pureness within us, whatever we have, no matter how 
insignificant it may feel. May anything we present to You reflect our 
best selves.
  When we use our gifts of time, energy, and talents here in this 
place, keep us from seeing them as a means to garner Your favor. Break 
the spirit of competition within us that feeds our pride and causes us 
to seek the enhancement of our reputation vice the benefit of those 
around us.
  May we set aside our short-sighted ambitions, and instead look for 
ways to work together to invest all that we value into the advancement 
of Your eternal kingdom.
  Holy Lord, receive these our gifts and use them according to Your 
will.
  We pray because of all that is ours when we do so in Your name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
