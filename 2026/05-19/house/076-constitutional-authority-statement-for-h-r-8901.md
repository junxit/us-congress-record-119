# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 8901

**Measures:** H.R. 8901

```
           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
       H.R. 8901.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the 
     United States, including the powers to provide for the common 
     defense, regulate commerce with foreign nations, make all 
     laws necessary and proper for carrying into execution the 
     foregoing powers, and exercise authority over the expenditure 
     of federal funds and conditions attached to federally funded 
     research awards.
```
