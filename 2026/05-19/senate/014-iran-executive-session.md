# Iran (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  Iran

  Mr. President, on Iran, yesterday, Donald Trump once again displayed 
the vicious cycle he has trapped America in with his disastrous war in 
Iran. Within hours, he went from threatening to blow Iran into oblivion 
to saying that a peace deal was imminent. This he does within 6 hours 
of one another.
  Can anyone believe this guy? Whether you are a world leader, an 
American citizen, or anything in between, can you believe him? He keeps 
making it up. He doesn't care about the truth. And what is worse is, he 
thinks you don't care about the truth--that he can lie, lie, lie.
  First--first--he threatens to blow Iran into smithereens, and 6 hours 
later, he says a peace deal is imminent. We know it is bull. Everyone 
knows it is bull.
  But maybe people don't realize the consequences of that bull--how 
disastrous this war is and how it turns America's attention, or at 
least Trump's attention and his followers' attention, to this 
disastrous war, rather than lowering their costs.
  We have seen Trump follow the same pattern in Iran again and again. 
Trump threatens, Trump retreats, then threatens again--rinse and 
repeat. That is what he does. Trump is practicing ``Groundhog Day'' 
diplomacy, smashing the clock over and over again like Bill Murray does 
in the movie. Trump is no closer to ending this war, no closer to 
bringing down the skyrocketing costs of this war, no closer to getting 
our troops out of harm's way.
  Senate Republicans must not continue to allow Trump to remain stuck 
in this never-ending loop of threats and failed negotiations. I urge 
Senate Republicans to support Democrats' War Powers Resolution to 
withdraw our troops from hostilities with Iran, which we are bringing 
to the floor for an eighth vote--an eighth vote--later today.
  Republicans, instead of perpetuating Trump's reckless cycle, could 
help break it by supporting our War Powers Act to end the war. That 
would be doing what the American people need and want.
```
