# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Curtis, John R. (C001114) [R-UT]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. CURTIS. Mr. President, I have six requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the 
Majority and Minority Leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


                      COMMITTEE ON ARMED SERVICES

  The Committee on Armed Services is authorized to meet in closed and 
open session during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, 
at 9:30 a.m.


           COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE, SCIENCE, AND TRANSPORTATION

  The Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation is authorized 
to meet during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, at 2 
p.m., to conduct a subcommittee hearing.


        COMMITTEE ON HOMELAND SECURITY AND GOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS

  The Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs is 
authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, May 19, 
2026, at 8 a.m., to conduct a business meeting.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, at 2:30 p.m., to 
conduct a hearing.


                    SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  The Select Committee on Intelligence is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, at 3 p.m., to conduct a 
closed briefing.


           SUBCOMMITTEE ON EMERGING THREATS AND CAPABILITIES

  The Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities of the 
Committee on Armed Services is authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, at 3 p.m., to conduct a hearing.

                          ____________________
```
