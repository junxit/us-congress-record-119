# TRIBUTE TO MARGARET DOUD

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MARGARET DOUD

● Mr. PETERS. Mr. President, I rise today to honor Margaret 
Doud, the longtime, highly regarded mayor of Mackinac Island. Mayor 
Doud has made an immeasurable impact on Mackinac Island as she has 
faithfully led the city over the past 51 years, and it is a privilege 
to recognize her here today as we celebrate her 52nd term, and her 
status as the longest serving mayor in the United States.
  Mayor Doud is a native of Mackinac Island, a small island in the 
Straits of Mackinac home to roughly 500 full-time residents. A former 
teacher and current owner of the iconic Windermere Hotel, she almost 
did not run for office when she was first approached, believing she 
would not get elected. The following year in 1975, she won her first 
election--and 51 more since then. On Mackinac Island, mayors only serve 
1-year terms, which means she has had to run for reelection for all her 
years of service. Her ability to have won reelection for the past 51 
years--receiving 100 percent of the votes just this year--is a 
testament to the trust she has earned from her community.
  While Mackinac Island may only have 500-full time residents, it is 
also one of the busiest and most visited destinations in the State of 
Michigan. Every summer, over 1 million visitors come to island to visit 
its historic sites, enjoy beautiful views, and shop at the many small 
businesses. Through it all, Mayor Doud has been steadfast in her 
commitment to making the island a safe, thriving area for both 
residents and visitors alike. Her fierce dedication to her community, 
whether it be its tourism industry, its small businesses, or its unique 
car-free status, is more than deserving of recognition.
  I cannot understate the impact that Mayor Doud has had on Mackinac 
Island and the State of Michigan over the last 51 years. As the mayor 
of Mackinac Island, her compassion, dedication, and strategic vision 
have led to its great success, and I know she will continue to serve 
her community in the highest regard. For this reason, I am honored 
today to recognize the great achievements of Mayor Doud. Please join me 
in celebrating her long, storied career leading Mackinac 
Island.●

                          ____________________
```
