# 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF TEMPLE KNESSET ISRAEL

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
               100TH ANNIVERSARY OF TEMPLE KNESSET ISRAEL

● Mr. SCHIFF. Mr. President, today I rise to honor and celebrate 
Temple Knesset Israel in Los Angeles for its milestone 100th 
anniversary.
  Founded in 1926, Temple Knesset Israel has been a beacon of light for 
its community; a congregation that has shown an ability to grow in the 
modern world and build strong foundations for future generations. One 
of the oldest congregations in Los Angeles, Temple Knesset Israel has 
focused on giving its members spiritual connection and cultural 
awareness.
  Fostering strong ties to its visitors, the temple has been a 
cornerstone of empowering all religions and ethnicities. A place where 
everyone is welcome, Temple Knesset Israel has been a powerful force of 
good.
  The temple has always been a welcoming home for those in need. 
Comprised of congregants from many ethnic groups, Temple Knesset Israel 
is proud to be a congregation focused on inclusivity and community. Its 
continued efforts to educate its members and bring together those of 
all walks of life has made the congregation a critical part of the Los 
Angeles community.
  Temple Knesset Israel's spiritual leaders Rabbi Robert Elias and 
Cantor Lee Greenberg have been pillars of spiritual guidance and 
continuity throughout the years. Maintaining stability in trying times, 
these leaders have been a centerpiece for calm and perseverance.
  I ask all members to join me in acknowledging the importance of 
Temple Knesset Israel. Its impact on the Los Angeles region cannot be 
overstated, and for 100 years, it has worked to bring together the 
Jewish community with immensely positive impact.●
```
