# Center for American Progress (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                      Center for American Progress

  Mr. President, on a speech I am giving at the Center for American 
Progress on election protection: Later today, I will speak at the 
Center for American Progress' IDEAS Conference to detail Democrats' 
plans to protect our elections from MAGA interference.
  Donald Trump is escalating his attacks on our elections because he 
knows his agenda is unpopular. He knows he is going to lose the 
elections, and so he wants to throw the election, to cheat. Senate 
Democrats are not waiting until after the damage is done. I am 
launching Senate Democrats' election protection task force to prevent, 
monitor, and challenge any malfeasance from Donald Trump and the far 
right before, during, and after our elections in November. Our task 
force is bringing together 11 Senators and some of the sharpest 
election experts in the country--people like Marc Elias, Eric Holder, 
Vanita Gupta, Norm Eisen, Skye Perryman, and others--as well as a 
variety of election protection organizations to safeguard our 
democracy. I am looking forward to detailing some of our task force's 
upcoming plans, and I encourage everyone to tune in.
```
