                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 470

  At the request of Mrs. Hyde-Smith, the name of the Senator from 
Tennessee (Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 470, a bill 
to amend the CARES Act to remove a requirement on lessors to provide 
notice to vacate, and for other purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Armstrong, his name was added as a cosponsor of 
S. 630, a bill to authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to make 
payments to the Quapaw Nation and certain members of the Quapaw Nation 
in accordance with the recommendation of the United States Court of 
Federal Claims, and for other purposes.

Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 823, a bill 
to create intergovernmental coordination between State, local, Tribal,

other purposes.
