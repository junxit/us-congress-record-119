# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF D&D INGREDIENTS

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

```
          CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF D&D INGREDIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 22, 2026

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the contributions of the 
founders, owners, and employees of D&D Ingredients of Delphos, Ohio, as 
they mark fifty years in business.
  Founded in 1976 by Doc and Dorothy Miller, D&D Ingredients is one of 
the region's most prominent animal nutrition companies. Through their 
integrity and hard work, commitment to delivering high-quality 
products, and dedication to meeting the needs of their customers, the 
Millers formed the foundation for steady growth that has made D&D a 
major force in the animal feed and pet food space. The company now 
employs 156 and serves customers across the globe.
  Five years ago, D&D became a part of the California-based Swanson 
Family of Companies, allowing D&D to serve new markets while 
maintaining the family-friendly culture and values that have shaped the 
company since its founding.
  Mr. Speaker, D&D will hold an open house on Saturday to commemorate 
this milestone. On behalf of the people of Ohio's 4th Congressional 
District, I am proud to join in the accolades to the men and women of 
D&D Ingredients as they celebrate fifty years of outstanding service to 
their customers. They have my very best wishes for continued excellence 
in the decades to come.
```
