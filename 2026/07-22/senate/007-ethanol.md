# ETHANOL

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                ETHANOL

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, once again, I am here to educate my 
colleagues about the necessity for passing the ethanol issue of E15 in 
this Congress.
  As Iowa families are going on their summer vacations and people visit 
or pass through my home State, we are seeing the evidence in Iowa that 
drivers are demanding E15 with their pocketbooks.
  New data from the Iowa Department of Revenue shows Iowa drivers 
purchased 47 million gallons of E15 in May. This is a new monthly 
record. That puts E15 on track to be about 50 percent of all gasoline 
sales in Iowa in 2026, up from 27 percent last year.
  Drivers know that E15 delivers lower costs for families, saving them 
up to 40 cents per gallon. This can be hundreds of dollars per year, 
giving working families more money for groceries, housing, and 
necessities.
  When Iowans are allowed to make choices at the pump, more are 
choosing E15. The growth of E15 in Iowa shows that American consumers 
want this chance to save money. This is another reason why the Senate 
must pass year-round E15, putting $14 billion more in the pockets of 
farmers with the increased price of corn.

                          ____________________
```
