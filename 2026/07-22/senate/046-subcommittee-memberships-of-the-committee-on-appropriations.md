  SUBCOMMITTEE MEMBERSHIPS OF THE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE 
                             119TH CONGRESS

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the revised 
memberships on the 12 subcommittees of the Committee on Appropriations 
for the 119th Congress be printed in the Record. The new assignments 
reflect the service on the committee of Senator Darline Graham, who was 
selected to fill the vacancy created by the passing of former Senator 
Lindsey Graham, as well as other revisions related to the change in 
committee membership.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:
