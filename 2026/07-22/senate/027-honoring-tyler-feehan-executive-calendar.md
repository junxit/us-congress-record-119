# Honoring Tyler Feehan (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Schatz, Brian (S001194) [D-HI]

```
                         Honoring Tyler Feehan

  Mr. SCHATZ. Mr. President, I am heartbroken on behalf of everybody in

[[Page S4209]]

Hawaii and everybody across this country about the death of 1LT Tyler 
Feehan of Ewa Beach, HI, who was among those killed in Jordan this past 
weekend while serving our country. His courage, his dedication, and his 
unwavering commitment to duty reflect the very best of our Armed 
Forces. We extend our deepest condolences to his family, his loved 
ones, and to his fellow servicemembers.
```
