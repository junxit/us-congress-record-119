# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, source of all goodness, use our lawmakers today for 
Your glory. Make them undaunted people, striving to know Your will and 
experience Your power.
  Lord, provide them with exactly what they need to accomplish Your 
purposes. May they receive Heaven's approval for their faithful service 
to You and country.
  Lord, transform their complex problems with solutions from Your 
throne.
  We commit the work of this day to You, receiving Your strength to 
honor Your Name.
  We pray in Your glorious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
