# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 180

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Ms. LUMMIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, notwithstanding 
rule XXII, that the postcloture time with respect to the Traynor 
nomination be expired, and the Senate vote on confirmation of the 
nomination immediately following disposition of the motion to discharge 
S.J. Res. 180, or at 12 noon if no motion is made; further, that it be 
in order for Senator Van Hollen or his designee to make a motion to 
discharge S.J. Res. 180 from the Foreign Relations Committee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
