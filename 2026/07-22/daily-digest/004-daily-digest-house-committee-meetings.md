Committee Meetings
FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING TESTIMONY FROM CHIEF TOM SCHULTZ, U.S. 
FOREST SERVICE
Committee on Agriculture: Subcommittee on Forestry and Horticulture 
held a hearing entitled ``For the Purpose of Receiving Testimony from 
Chief Tom Schultz, U.S. Forest Service''. Testimony was heard from Tom 
Schultz, Chief, U.S. Forest Service, Department of Agriculture.
FUNDING LAPSES: ANALYZING SHUTDOWN REFORM
Committee on Appropriations: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Funding Lapses: Analyzing Shutdown Reform''. Testimony was heard from 
Senator Lankford, Representative McClintock, and a public witness.
BROKEN TRUST: HOW THE BIDEN-HARRIS DEPARTMENT OF LABOR LEAKED 
CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Workforce 
Protections held a hearing entitled ``Broken Trust: How the Biden-
Harris DOL Leaked Confidential Information''. Testimony was heard from 
Anthony P. D'Esposito, Inspector General, Department of Labor.
LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS TO STRENGTHEN CONSUMER PROTECTION IN A CHANGING 
MARKETPLACE
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Commerce, 
Manufacturing, and Trade held a hearing entitled ``Legislative 
Proposals to Strengthen Consumer Protection in a Changing 
Marketplace''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LEGISLATIVE HEARING ON PROTECTING COMMUNICATIONS NETWORKS AND IMPROVING 
CONNECTIVITY
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Communications and 
Technology held a hearing entitled ``Legislative Hearing on Protecting 
Communications Networks and Improving Connectivity''. Testimony was 
heard from Andrew Von Ah, Director, Physical Infrastructure, Government 
Accountability Office.
UNITED NATIONS ACCOUNTABILITY AND REFORM: ADVANCING AN AMERICA FIRST 
FOREIGN POLICY THROUGH STRATEGIC DIPLOMACY AND BURDEN SHARING
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``United Nations Accountability and Reform: Advancing an America First 
Foreign Policy Through Strategic Diplomacy and Burden Sharing''. 
Testimony was heard from Mike Waltz, Representative of the United 
States of America to the United Nations, Department of State; and Jeff 
Bartos, Representative of the United States of America to the United 
Nations, United Nations Management and Reform, Department of State.
BUILDING A NEW REGIONAL SECURITY ARCHITECTURE: THE NEXT PHASE OF THE 
ABRAHAM ACCORDS
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Middle East and North Africa Subcommittee 
held a hearing entitled ``Building a New Regional Security 
Architecture: The Next Phase of the Abraham Accords''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
CHARTING A NEW COURSE: COUNTERING CHINA'S DOMINANCE IN GLOBAL 
SHIPBUILDING
Committee on Foreign Affairs: East Asia and the Pacific Subcommittee 
held a hearing entitled ``Charting a New Course: Countering China's 
Dominance in Global Shipbuilding''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.

MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee concluded a markup on H.R. 
1939, to require the imposition of sanctions with respect to conflict 
in Sudan, and for other purposes; H.R. 9087, to direct the Secretary of 
State to take actions with respect to certain foreign affairs matters; 
and H.R. 9086, to amend the Foreign Service Act of 1980 to modify and 
improve that Act. H.R. 1939 and H.R. 9087 were ordered reported, as 
amended. H.R. 9086 was ordered reported, without amendment.
OVERSIGHT OF THE SMITHSONIAN: RADICAL REVISIONISM AT THE NATIONAL 
MUSEUM OF AMERICAN HISTORY
Committee on House Administration: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Oversight of the Smithsonian: Radical Revisionism at the 
National Museum of American History''. Testimony was heard from 
Elizabeth MacMillan Director, National Museum of American History; and 
public witnesses.
FALSE NARRATIVES SURROUNDING CONDITIONS AT THE DELANEY HALL IMMIGRATION 
AND CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT DETENTION CENTER
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Oversight held a hearing 
entitled ``False Narratives Surrounding Conditions at the Delaney Hall 
ICE Detention Center''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 
309, the ``National Law Enforcement Officers Remembrance, Support and 
Community Outreach Act''; H.R. 4219, the ``National Wildlife Refuge 
System Invasive Species Strike Team Act of 2025''; H.R. 4931, the

for other purposes; and H.R. 1693, to redesignate the National Historic

Cubin National Historic Trails Interpretive Center''. H.R. 8454, H.R. 
8121, H.R. 6021, H.R. 5063, H.R. 4931, H.R. 4219, and H.R. 309 were 
ordered reported, as amended. H.R. 1693 was ordered reported, without
