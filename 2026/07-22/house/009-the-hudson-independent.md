# THE HUDSON INDEPENDENT

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                         THE HUDSON INDEPENDENT

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I want to salute the tremendous community 
contribution of The Hudson Independent, an online publication that 
chronicles the lives and stories of people, organizations, governments, 
schools, businesses, and the like, in the municipalities that border 
the Hudson River in Westchester County in New York: Ardsley, Dobbs 
Ferry, Irvington, Hastings-on-Hudson, Tarrytown, and Sleepy Hollow.
  The team that does this reporting every day is led by Editor in Chief 
Barrett Seaman and Editor Rick Pezzullo, both experienced journalists 
with long resumes of achievement. The senior management group President 
and Chairman Walter Montgomery and colleagues: Robert Kimmel, Dean 
Gallea, Paula Etzel, and Michael Richards, have guided The Indy, as it 
is known and loved, from its founding in 2006.
  The calendar of local events captures all of the many varied 
activities of the river towns and featured stories on the

[[Page H5028]]

issues and the personalities of the area are given full attention at a 
time when hyperlocal news is disappearing from view.
  From the floor of the U.S. House of Representatives, we salute The 
Hudson Independent for its commitment to fair and accurate journalism 
and the communities of Westchester, the river towns, their people, and 
their stories.

                          ____________________
```
