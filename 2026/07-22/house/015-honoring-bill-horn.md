# HONORING BILL HORN

**Speaking:** Issa, Darrell (I000056) [R-CA]

```
                           HONORING BILL HORN

  (Mr. ISSA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. ISSA. I thank the Speaker for noting that disparaging the 
President is not appropriate for this House.
  Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and the contribution of 
Bill Horn, a man who gave first his service to the country in Vietnam 
and then went on to be one of the longest serving county supervisors in 
San Diego County.
  Bill Horn wanted more for his family, and he worked hard to do it. 
While being a county supervisor full time, he also ran successful 
agriculture.

[[Page H5029]]

  Mr. Speaker, in his retirement, he did more philanthropic work around 
the world at his own expense than people will ever know. The unsung 
hero of Vietnam, our county supervisor, a man of simple means and no 
ambition, did so much for those who needed him. He will be honored and 
missed.

                          ____________________
```
