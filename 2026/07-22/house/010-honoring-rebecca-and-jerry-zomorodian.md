# HONORING REBECCA AND JERRY ZOMORODIAN

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING REBECCA AND JERRY ZOMORODIAN

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor Jerry Zomorodian and 
his late wife, Rebecca, Orange County residents who were deeply 
involved in our community.
  Jerry was born in Iran and later came to the United States in 1983 
where he met Rebecca. They were married for 43 years before her passing 
in 2025. Through hard work, the two of them built a life together, 
owning and operating a small Persian restaurant. They were successful 
while staying true to their values.
  Jerry is proud to be an American. Rebecca was a devoted wife, loving 
mother, advocate for breast cancer awareness, and a philanthropist who 
helped her community.
  Rebecca courageously fought cancer for many years before it 
ultimately took her life. She was deeply involved in her community and 
will be missed by her family, friends, and all of us.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to recognize the life and legacy of 
Rebecca Zomorodian and honor the way she and Jerry helped our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
