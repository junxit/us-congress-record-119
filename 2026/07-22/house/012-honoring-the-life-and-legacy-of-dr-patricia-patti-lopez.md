# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. PATRICIA "PATTI" LOPEZ

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Adelita S. (G000606) [D-AZ]

```
      HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. PATRICIA ``PATTI'' LOPEZ

  (Mrs. GRIJALVA asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the extraordinary 
life and legacy of Dr. Patricia ``Patti'' Lopez, a dedicated educator, 
mentor, and advocate whose life's work transformed the lives of 
countless children and families in southern Arizona.
  For more than three decades, Dr. Lopez served Tucson's students as a 
teacher, principal, district leader, deputy superintendent, and chief 
academic officer for Tucson Unified School District. As a former TUSD 
governing board member, I primarily remember Dr. Lopez for her bright 
spirit, dedication, compassion, instincts, and smarts.
  After her distinguished career in education, she continued her 
mission as executive director of Angel Heart Pajama Project, helping 
provide more than 73,000 children experiencing crises with new pajamas, 
books, and the comfort of knowing that they were seen, valued, and 
loved.
  Dr. Lopez believed that even the simplest acts of kindness could 
change a child's life. Her compassion, integrity, and unwavering 
commitment inspired volunteers, strengthened communities, and brought 
hope to thousands of children when they needed it most.
  May we honor Dr. Patti Lopez by carrying forward her example of 
service and her belief that every child deserves comfort, dignity, and 
hope.
  ``Muchisimas gracias''; ``many thanks'', Patti. ``Para todos, 
descansa en paz''; ``for all, rest in peace.''

                          ____________________
```
