# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy One, who prompts us to conform to Your will, shine in the hearts 
of our lawmakers, providing them with Your wisdom. Lord, purify the 
desires of their hearts, leading them beside the still waters of 
contemplation, thanksgiving, and restoration. Embolden them to become 
instruments of Your saving power, which comforts, challenges, and 
confronts. Remove from them anything that hinders them from being 
instruments of Your peace, bringing light where there is darkness, hope 
where there is despair, and peace where there is conflict.
  As You keep our Senators faithful to their calling in these sometimes 
chaotic, confusing, and challenging times, may Your peace guard their 
hearts.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
