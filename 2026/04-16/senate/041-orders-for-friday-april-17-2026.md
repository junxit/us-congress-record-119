# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 17, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 17, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 10 a.m. 
on Friday, April 17; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate be in a period 
of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
