# RECOGNIZING TEMPLE BETH SHOLOM

**Speaking:** Rosen, Jacky (R000608) [D-NV]

```
                     RECOGNIZING TEMPLE BETH SHOLOM

● Ms. ROSEN. Mr. President, it is my honor to rise today to pay 
tribute to Temple Beth Sholom of Las Vegas, as the synagogue and its 
members celebrate their 80th anniversary during 2026. In 1946, the 
first Jewish congregation was established in southern Nevada, meeting 
and worshipping at the Las Vegas Jewish Community Center. As the city 
of Las Vegas grew in the 1950s, so too did the Jewish community, 
leading to the construction of the first Temple building, which gave 
the community a new home to worship in. The congregation formally named 
it Temple Beth Sholom. Now, 80 years later at its current location in 
Summerlin, Temple Beth Sholom remains a vibrant and active Jewish 
congregation hosting daily minyan services and weekly Shabbat services, 
as well as celebrating life cycle events throughout the year. Education 
is also a key pillar of Temple Beth Sholom's service to the community, 
with classroom learning for children of all ages, and active USY and 
Kadima youth programs as well. More importantly, Temple Beth Sholom 
serves the Las Vegas Jewish Community through hosting various 
educational and social events throughout the year, welcoming all of the 
southern Nevada community to participate.
  To Rabbi Felipe Goodman, musical director DJ Sinai, board president 
Elliot Blut, and all of the Temple Beth Sholom congregation and team 
that keep it running, I join citizens across Nevada and the Nation in 
sending our sincere congratulations on 80 years of Temple Beth Sholom 
being a pillar of the Jewish community in southern Nevada and wish them 
many more years of success.●

                          ____________________
```
