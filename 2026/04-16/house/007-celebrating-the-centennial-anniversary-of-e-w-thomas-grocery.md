# CELEBRATING THE CENTENNIAL ANNIVERSARY OF E.W. THOMAS GROCERY STORE AND DMV

**Speaking:** McGuire, John J. (M001239) [R-VA]

```
CELEBRATING THE CENTENNIAL ANNIVERSARY OF E.W. THOMAS GROCERY STORE AND 
                                  DMV

  (Mr. McGUIRE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGUIRE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the centennial 
anniversary of E.W. Thomas Grocery Store and DMV in Fluvanna County in 
Virginia's Fifth Congressional District.
  Since their founding, this family-owned store has withstood the test 
of time.
  Through generations, E.W. Thomas has served as more than just a 
grocery store: providing local jobs, supporting families, delivering 
DMV services in a rural area, and fostering goodwill in the community.
  Today, the Thomas name remains a cornerstone of the store and the 
community it serves.
  As E.W. Thomas Grocery Store marks its 100th anniversary, we 
recognize not just a business, but a living symbol of resilience, 
service, and the enduring spirit of rural America.
```
