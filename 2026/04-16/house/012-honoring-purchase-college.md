# HONORING PURCHASE COLLEGE

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                              {time}  1010
                       HONORING PURCHASE COLLEGE

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Purchase College of 
the State University of New York system, also known as SUNY Purchase.
  Purchase College is a top 10 public college nationally recognized for 
its arts programs and its rigorous programs in the liberal arts and 
sciences. As the only 4-year public college in Westchester County, SUNY 
Purchase College delivers a high-quality, affordable education and 
supports first-generation student success.
  Purchase College harbors graduates who thrive on the world stage as 
actors, musicians, filmmakers, and artists, and they have meaningful 
careers as psychologists, nonprofit leaders, doctors, scientists, 
lawyers, researchers, and educators.
  This is a testament to the programs available to the students and the 
resources provided to alumni long after graduation day. They have just 
announced a special partnership with Rockland Community College.
  I am pleased to recognize SUNY Purchase College for the work they do 
for their students and the wider community.
```
