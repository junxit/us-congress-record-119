# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 8353

**Measures:** H.R. 8353

```
           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 8353.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, 
     including the power to enforce protections for individual 
     liberties implicated by fees or penalties tied to religious 
     participation.
```
