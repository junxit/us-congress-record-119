# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2283, H.R. 4219, H.R. 5999, H.R. 6001, H.R. 6251, H.R. 6444, H.R. 6652, H.R. 6848, H.R. 6993, H.R. 7288, H.R. 8195

```
Committee Meetings
FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING TESTIMONY FROM THE HONORABLE MICHAEL S. 
SELIG, CHAIRMAN, U.S. COMMODITY FUTURES TRADING COMMISSION
Committee on Agriculture: Full Committee held a hearing entitled ``For 
the Purpose of Receiving Testimony from the Honorable Michael S. Selig, 
Chairman, U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission''. Testimony was 
heard from Michael S. Selig, Chairman, U.S. Commodity Futures Trading 
Commission.
APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. ARMY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Defense held a budget 
hearing on the U.S. Army. Testimony was heard from Daniel P. Driscoll, 
Secretary, Department of the Army; and General Christopher C. LaNeve, 
Vice Chief of the Army and Acting Chief of Staff of the Army.
APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. ARMY CORPS OF ENGINEERS AND BUREAU OF RECLAMATION
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Energy and Water 
Development and Related Agencies held a budget hearing on the U.S. Army 
Corps of Engineers and Bureau of Reclamation. Testimony was heard from 
Lieutenant General William H. Graham, Jr., Chief of Engineers and 
Commanding General, U.S. Army of Corps Engineers; Adam R. Telle, 
Assistant Secretary, Army for Civil Works; and Andrea Travnicek, 
Assistant Secretary for Water and Science, Department of the Interior.
APPROPRIATIONS--DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY; U.S. CUSTOMS AND 
BORDER PROTECTION, U.S. IMMIGRATION AND CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT, U.S. 
CITIZEN AND IMMIGRATION SERVICES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Homeland Security held a 
budget hearing on the Department of Homeland Security: U.S. Customs and 
Border Protection, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement, and U.S. 
Citizenship and Immigration Services. Testimony was heard from the 
following Department of Homeland Security officials: Joseph Edlow, 
Director, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services; Todd Lyons, Acting 
Director, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement; and Rodney Scott, 
Commissioner, U.S. Customs and Border Protection.

APPROPRIATIONS--DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural 
Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies held a 
budget hearing on the Department of Agriculture. Testimony was heard 
from Brooke Rollins, Secretary, Department of Agriculture.
APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. TRADE REPRESENTATIVE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Commerce, Justice, 
Science, and Related Agencies held a budget hearing on the Office of 
the U.S. Trade Representative. Testimony was heard from Jamieson Greer, 
Ambassador, U.S. Trade Representative.
APPROPRIATIONS--DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY: CYBERSECURITY AND 
INFRASTRUCTURE SECURITY AGENCY, TRANSPORTATION SECURITY ADMINISTRATION, 
U.S. COAST GUARD, U.S. SECRET SERVICE, AND FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT 
AGENCY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Homeland Security held a 
budget hearing on the Department of Homeland Security: Cybersecurity 
and Infrastructure Security Agency, Transportation Security 
Administration, U.S. Coast Guard, U.S. Secret Service, and Federal 
Emergency Management Agency. Testimony was heard from the following 
Department of Homeland Security officials: Nick Andersen, Acting 
Director, Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency; Sean 
Curran, Director, U.S. Secret Service; Karen Evans, Acting 
Administrator, Federal Emergency Management Agency; Admiral Kevin 
Lunday, Commandant, U.S. Coast Guard; and Ha Nguyen McNeill, Acting 
Administrator, Transportation Security Administration.
APPROPRIATIONS--DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Agencies held a budget hearing on the 
Department of Health and Human Services. Testimony was heard from 
Robert F. Kennedy, Jr., Secretary, Department of Health and Human 
Services.
APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. FOREST SERVICE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies held a budget hearing on the U.S. Forest Service. 
Testimony was heard from Tom Schultz, Chief, U.S. Forest Service, 
Department of Agriculture.
DEFENSE INTELLIGENCE ENTERPRISE--CHALLENGES, PRIORITIES, AND RESOURCING 
FOR FISCAL YEAR 2027
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Intelligence and Special 
Operations held a hearing entitled ``Defense Intelligence Enterprise--
Challenges, Priorities, and Resourcing for Fiscal Year 2027''. 
Testimony was heard from the following Department of Defense officials: 
Bradley D. Hansell, Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and 
Security, Office of the Secretary of Defense; General Joshua M. Rudd, 
Director and Commander, National Security Agency and U.S. Cyber 
Command; Lieutenant General James H. Adams III, Director, Defense 
Intelligence Agency; and Lieutenant General Michele Bredenkamp, 
Director, National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency.
EXAMINING THE POLICIES AND PRIORITIES OF THE EMPLOYEE BENEFITS SECURITY 
ADMINISTRATION
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Health, 
Employment, Labor, and Pensions held a hearing entitled ``Examining the 
Policies and Priorities of the Employee Benefits Security 
Administration''. Testimony was heard from Daniel Aronowitz, Assistant 
Secretary, Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department of 
Labor.
THE FISCAL YEAR 2027 DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY BUDGET
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Energy held a hearing 
entitled ``The Fiscal Year 2027 Department of Energy Budget''. 
Testimony was heard from Chris Wright, Secretary, Department of Energy.
PROMOTING ACCESS TO CREDIT FOR EVERYDAY AMERICANS
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Financial Institutions 
held a hearing entitled ``Promoting Access to Credit for Everyday 
Americans''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LATIN AMERICA AFTER THE FALL OF MADURO
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Western Hemisphere Subcommittee held a 
hearing entitled ``Latin America After the Fall of Maduro''. Testimony 
was heard from Michael Kozak, Senior Bureau Official, Bureau of Western 
Hemisphere Affairs, Department of State.
OVERSIGHT WITH SECRETARIES OF STATE: LIST MAINTENANCE AND ELIGIBILITY 
VERIFICATION
Committee on House Administration: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Oversight with Secretaries

[[Page D402]]

of State: List Maintenance and Eligibility Verification''. Testimony 
was heard from Scott Schwab, Secretary of State, Kansas; and Steve 
Simon, Secretary of State, Minnesota.
THE HUMAN TOLL OF SANCTUARY POLICIES: STORIES FROM VICTIMS AND FAMILIES
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Oversight held a hearing 
entitled ``The Human Toll of Sanctuary Policies: Stories from Victims 
and Families''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Water, Wildlife and 
Fisheries held a hearing on H.R. 4219, the ``National Wildlife Refuge 
System Invasive Species Strike Team Act of 2025''; H.R. 6251, to amend 
the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 to allow importation of polar 
bear trophies taken in sport hunts in Canada before the date the polar 
bear was determined to be a threatened species under the Endangered 
Species Act of 1973; H.R. 7288, the ``Western South Dakota Water Supply 
Project Feasibility Study Act''; and H.R. 8195, the ``Responsible 
Cormorant Management and Control Act of 2026''. Testimony was heard 
from Representatives Begich and Johnson of South Dakota; David Miko, 
Acting Deputy Director for Operations, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, 
Department of the Interior; and public witnesses.
SUBSURFACE SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY: AMERICAN ENERGY AND MINERAL 
DOMINANCE
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Subcommittee on Energy 
held a hearing entitled ``Subsurface Science and Technology: American 
Energy and Mineral Dominance''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Health held a markup on 
H.R. 2283, the ``Recognizing Community Organizations for Veteran 
Engagement and Recovery Act''; H.R. 6993, the ``BEACON Act of 2026''; 
H.R. 6652, the ``U.S. Vets of the FAS Act''; H.R. 6444, the ``Blast 
Overpressure Research and Mitigation Task Force Act''; H.R. 5999, to 
amend title 38, United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Veterans 
Affairs to furnish an opioid antagonist to a veteran without requiring 
a prescription or copayment; H.R. 6001, the ``Veterans with ALS 
Reporting Act''; and H.R. 6848, the ``Whole Health for Veterans Act''. 
H.R. 2283, H.R. 6993, H.R. 6652, H.R. 6444, H.R. 5999 were forwarded to 
the full Committee, as amended. H.R. 6001 and H.R. 6848 were forwarded 
to the Full Committee, without amendment.
BOUNCE HOUSES, DRONES, AND MASSAGE CHAIRS: A REVIEW OF VA'S PURCHASE 
HISTORY IN THE VETERAN READINESS AND EMPLOYMENT PROGRAM
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Economic Opportunity 
held a hearing entitled ``Bounce Houses, Drones, and Massage Chairs: A 
Review of VA's Purchase History in the Veteran Readiness and Employment 
Program''. Testimony was heard from Margarita Devlin, Principal Deputy 
Undersecretary for Benefits, Veterans Benefits Administration, 
Department of Veterans Affairs; and a public witness.
HEARING WITH HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES SECRETARY ROBERT F. KENNEDY, JR.
Committee on Ways and Means: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Hearing with Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy, 
Jr.''. Testimony was heard from Robert F. Kennedy, Jr., Secretary, 
Department of Health and Human Services.
FISCAL YEAR 2027 BUDGET REQUEST FOR THE NATIONAL RECONNAISSANCE OFFICE, 
THE NATIONAL GEOSPATIAL-INTELLIGENCE AGENCY, AND THE UNITED STATES 
SPACE FORCE
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Subcommittee on Defense 
Intelligence and Overhead Architecture held a hearing entitled ``Fiscal 
Year 2027 Budget Request for the National Reconnaissance Office, the 
National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, and the United States Space 
Force''. Testimony was heard from the following Department of Defense 
officials: Christopher Scolese, Director, National Reconnaissance 
Office; Lieutenant General Michelle Bredenkamp, Director, National 
Geospatial-Intelligence Agency; and Brian Shannon, Director, Program 
Integration, U.S. Space Force. This hearing was closed.
FISCAL YEAR 2027 BUDGET REQUEST FOR THE FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION 
AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Subcommittee on the 
National Intelligence Enterprise held a hearing entitled ``Fiscal Year 
2027 Budget Request for the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the 
Department of Homeland Security.'' This hearing was closed.
```
