# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O Lord, our God, throughout Scripture, You have shown us the way we 
are to go and the paths we should take. Blessed are those, You have 
said, whose way is blameless, who walk in Your law.
  But too often, we are inclined to wander, prone to leave the God we 
say we love. Confident in our own sense of direction, lured by the 
appeal of immediate pleasure, our hearts turn from You. We become lost 
in the distractions of our own devising and lose sight of the blessings 
You provide.
  God, show us mercy. In our faithlessness, remind us that You are 
always faithful. Then may we turn our eyes back to You, seek You with 
our whole heart, and direct our lives in accordance with Your law. May 
we abide in Your righteous presence and respond to Your steadfast love 
in the living of these days.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
