                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the 
following enrolled bills were signed by Speaker pro tempore Houchin on 
Wednesday, October 14, 2026:
  H.R. 227, to allow a period in which members of the clergy may revoke 
their exemption from Social Security coverage, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 837, to require the Secretary of Agriculture to convey the 
Pleasant Valley Ranger District Administrative Site to Gila County, 
Arizona;
  H.R. 997, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to conform to 
the intent of the Internal Revenue Service Restructuring and Reform Act 
of 1998, as set forth in the joint explanatory statement of the 
committee of conference accompanying Conference Report 105-599, that 
the National Taxpayer Advocate be able to hire and consult counsel as 
appropriate;

[[Page H6044]]

  H.R. 1190, to amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to expand 
access to capital for rural-area small businesses, and for other 
purposes;
  H.R. 1550, to redesignate Saratoga National Historical Park as 
Saratoga National Battlefield Park;
  H.R. 1703, to amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to clarify 
payment rules for manual wheelchairs under part B of the Medicare 
program;
  H.R. 1744, to extend and authorize annual appropriations for the 
United States Commission on International Religious Freedom through 
December 31, 2028;
  H.R. 1945, to designate the America's National Churchill Museum 
National Historic Landmark, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 3620, to provide for the conveyance of certain property to the 
Southcentral Foundation located in Anchorage, Alaska, and for other 
purposes;
  H.R. 4307, to direct the Secretary of Labor to train certain 
employees of Department of Labor how to effectively detect and assist 
law enforcement in preventing human trafficking during the course of 
their official duties, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 5235, to amend title 41, United States Code, to prohibit minimum 
educational requirements for proposed contractor personnel in certain 
contract solicitations, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 5284, to require the Social Security Administration to make 
changes to the social security terminology used in the rules, 
regulation, guidance, or other materials of the Administration;
  H.R. 6380, to redesignate the Chiricahua National Monument in the 
State of Arizona as Chiricahua National Park, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 7022, to provide for the transmission of emergency alerts by 
satellite, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 7730, to amend title 11, United States Code, to modify certain 
bankruptcy eligibility requirements, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 7831, to amend the Mineral Leasing Act to extend the period of 
time during which the Secretary of the Interior is required to collect 
a fee for each new application for a permit to drill, and for other 
purposes;
  H.R. 8205, to amend the Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for 
ALS Act to reauthorize the provisions of such Act through fiscal year 
2031, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 8352, to authorize peace officer standards and training agencies 
to access criminal history records, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 9496, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to postpone tax 
deadlines and reimburse paid late fees for United States nationals who 
are unlawfully or wrongfully detained or held hostage abroad, and for 
other purposes;
  H.R. 10159, to authorize the Secretary of the Army to convey to the 
State of North Carolina a certain parcel of real property located at 
Fort Bragg, North Carolina, on which the State shall construct a 
veterans' State home, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 10167, to direct the Secretary of the Treasury to stop minting 
the penny, to require cash transactions to be rounded up or down to the 
nearest 5 cents, and for other purposes.
