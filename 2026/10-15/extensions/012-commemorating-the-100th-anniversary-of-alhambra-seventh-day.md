# COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ALHAMBRA SEVENTH-DAY ADVENTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
 COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ALHAMBRA SEVENTH-DAY ADVENTIST 
                                 CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate the distinguished 
Alhambra Seventh-Day Adventist Church on its 100th anniversary.
  Founded in 1926, Alhambra Seventh-Day Adventist Church has grown to a 
congregation of more than 400 members who are dedicated to sharing 
hope, supporting health and wellness, and meeting the needs of others. 
Guided by these principles, the church welcomes all individuals, 
offering opportunities to build meaningful relationships and find a 
sense of belonging.
  Through community outreach, members of the Alhambra Seventh-Day 
Adventist Church strive to bring comfort to the sick and assist those 
facing hardships. Whether through hosting community health events, 
offering meals to members and neighbors, providing education and family 
programming, or by caring for and supporting vulnerable members of the 
community, Alhambra SDA Church embodies the values of loving one's 
neighbor.
  The centennial is an occasion not only to celebrate the history of 
Alhambra Seventh-Day Adventist Church, but also to recognize the 
dedication of the pastors, leaders, members, volunteers, and supporters 
who have helped sustain its mission for decades. Their contributions 
have made a positive impact on the lives of congregation members and 
the Alhambra community.
  I am proud to recognize Alhambra Seventh-Day Adventist Church on 
reaching this incredible milestone. Through caring for the sick, 
providing for the needy, and creating a congregation that welcomes all, 
the Alhambra SDA Church's impact will continue to be felt by our 
community for many years to come.
```
