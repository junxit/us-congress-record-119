# RECOGNIZING CORPORAL TOM GENE FORDYCE, U.S. MARINE (RET.), WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
   RECOGNIZING CORPORAL TOM GENE FORDYCE, U.S. MARINE (RET.), WITH A 
                   CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to present Corporal Tom Gene 
Fordyce of Huntsville, Texas, with a 2026 Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. His life of service reflects the hard work, dedication, 
and commitment that exemplify the very best of our East Texas values.
  Tom Fordyce entered active duty at Camp Pendleton, California, in 
June 1966 at the age of eighteen. He served with Company C, 1st 
Battalion, 26th Regiment, 3rd Division of the Marine Corps. During the 
Vietnam War, Tom served with honor and heroism at Khe Sanh, where he 
sustained a severe injury to his left arm that rendered it permanently 
unusable. His military service instilled in him a resilience and 
commitment to mission and purpose that he has carried throughout his 
life.
  Tom served two years overseas in combat and earned numerous military 
honors, including the Purple Heart Medal, Vietnam Service Medal, 
Vietnam Campaign Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Rifle 
Sharpshooter Badge, Good Conduct Medal, and Combat Action Ribbon. He 
completed Infantry Training and the Anti-Tank Assaultman's Course in 
1966, followed by Combat Marksmanship training in 1968. Following his 
military service, Tom graduated from Sam Houston State University in 
1972 and went on to build a distinguished career with the Texas 
Department of Criminal Justice spanning more than three decades. He 
retired in 2005 as Department Head of the Agriculture and Minerals 
Division. That same year, he was appointed by Governor Rick Perry to 
serve as a board member of the Trinity River Authority, a position he 
held for many years. Tom also served as a Commissioner for the Texas 
Board of Pardons and Paroles and currently serves as a member of the 
Texas Board of Criminal Justice, having been appointed by Governor Greg 
Abbott.
  Tom's commitment to veterans is equally remarkable. In 2010, he 
founded the Warrior Banquet in conjunction with the Mighty Oaks 
Foundation, where he currently serves as Chief of Special Projects and 
a board member. Through this work, he helps advance the organization's 
faith-based mission of providing peer-to-peer mentoring and recovery 
programs for combat veterans and military families dealing with PTSD.
  Tom has also devoted himself to strengthening his hometown of 
Huntsville and supporting its youth. In 2014, he was named the 
Huntsville Item's Unsung Hero in recognition of his persistent 
involvement with veterans and the community, as well as his support of 
local youth in agriculture. Throughout the years, Tom and his family 
have continued to give back to their community and encourage others to 
help make Huntsville a better place. His community service has included 
leadership and board roles with the H.E.A.R.T.S. Veterans Museum, 
Walker County Fair Association, Lone Survivor Foundation, and 
Huntsville Veterans Advisory Council.
  As a Marine, Tom was guided and shaped into the man we know today. 
Behind every great man, however, is a great woman, and Tom has been 
married to Sharon for 32 years. Together, they are the proud parents of 
four adult children: Stephanie, Michael, Lauren, and Tyler. The love 
and inspiration of his family, along with God's blessings, have helped 
shape Tom into a neighbor, hero, and trusted source of wise counsel to 
countless citizens and elected officials throughout Huntsville.
  It is this lifetime of hard work, service, leadership, and devotion 
to his community, veterans, and its youth that I proudly commend Tom 
Gene Fordyce of Huntsville, Texas, to his friends, family, community, 
and country. It is my honor to recognize him with the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation.
```
