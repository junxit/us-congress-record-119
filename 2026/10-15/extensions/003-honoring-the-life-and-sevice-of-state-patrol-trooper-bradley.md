# HONORING THE LIFE AND SEVICE OF STATE PATROL TROOPER BRADLEY ALLAN RITGER

```
  HONORING THE LIFE AND SEVICE OF STATE PATROL TROOPER BRADLEY ALLAN 
                                 RITGER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DERRICK VAN ORDEN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. VAN ORDEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
service of State Patrol Trooper Bradley Allan Ritger.
  Bradley was born January 2, 1997, in Menomonie, Wisconsin. He enjoyed 
many parts of life growing up in Western Wisconsin. Bradley loved 
swimming, hunting, fishing, LEGO, traveling up north, and enjoying the 
company of his brothers and cousins. Bradley's analytical mind led him 
to seek out challenge in the world of grand strategy while also 
displaying appreciation for the arts in classic literature.
  Bradley attended Wildlands High School. He is well remembered by his 
peers for his intelligence and humor. Bradley would lead an active 
life, competing and representing his school in both football and 
wrestling. Bradley would graduate high school and go on to attend 
Chippewa Valley Technical College. From there, he would begin his law 
enforcement training.
  Bradley graduated from the Wisconsin State Patrol Academy on June 30, 
2022, as a member of the 67th Recruit Class. Serving the people of Dunn 
County, he became a certified general law enforcement instructor, 
mobile field force team member and squad leader, and salvage vehicle 
instructor.
  Bradley was a man of strong faith, conviction, and character. Those 
closest to him knew him as thoughtful, quick-witted, and deeply loyal 
to the people he loved. As the eldest of seven brothers, he naturally 
took on the role of a leader and protector, always looking out for 
those around him.
  Bradley is survived by his wife, Caitlyn; his daughters, Candace and 
Alice; his parents, Jeffrey Ritger (Katelyn) and Sarah Ritger 
(Dickinsen); and his six brothers, Ethan (Mackenzie), Aiden (Mara), 
Jeffrey, II, Noah, Nolan, and Joseph; along with his niece, Iris, and 
nephews, Zachariah and Cedric.
  Bradley will be remembered not only for his service to the State of 
Wisconsin, but for the husband, father, son, brother, friend, and 
neighbor he was to so many. His commitment to serving others, devotion 
to his family, and steadfast values leave behind a legacy worthy of 
recognition.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that Bradley Allan Ritger and his contributions to 
my state and the Nation be in the Record for posterity.
```
