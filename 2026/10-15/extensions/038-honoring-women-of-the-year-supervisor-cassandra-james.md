# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: SUPERVISOR CASSANDRA JAMES

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
         HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: SUPERVISOR CASSANDRA JAMES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Supervisor Cassandra James as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. 
Representing Greater Vallejo as Supervisor for Solano County's First 
District, Cassandra exemplifies leadership grounded in equity and 
inclusion. Contributing more than a decade of experience working to 
improve housing stability and strengthen safety-net programs and 
continues to serve as a tireless advocate for California's most 
vulnerable communities.
  Cassandra began her advocacy career with the San Francisco Youth 
Commission, where she championed economic justice for historically 
marginalized communities. She continues to draw on this expertise to 
advance policies that amplify community voices throughout Solano County 
and ensure that people have a meaningful role in decisions that affect 
their lives. Her years as a member of the Service Employees 
International Union have further strengthened her commitment to 
advocacy and community agency. Cassandra has also held countless 
leadership roles in community organizations, serving on the board of 
Vallejo Together, as Vice Chair of the Vallejo Housing and Community 
Development Commission, and as Membership Committee Chair for the 
Solano County League of Women Voters. Throughout her career, Cassandra 
has demonstrated a passion for educating and empowering our community, 
serving as a champion for unhoused, formerly incarcerated, low-income, 
and others whose voices are often overlooked.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Cassandra James for her 
positive contributions to our community.
```
