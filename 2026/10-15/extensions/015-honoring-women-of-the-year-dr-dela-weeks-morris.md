# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DR. DELA WEEKS-MORRIS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DR. DELA WEEKS-MORRIS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Dr. Dela Weeks-Morris as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. For more 
than 25 years, Dr. Morris has strengthened our community through 
education, civic engagement, and public service. As Chair and Co-Chair 
of the Vallejo Human Relations Commission, she has worked tirelessly to 
foster meaningful relationships and engagement among residents, 
community groups, and city officials.
  Through her service on the Vallejo Police Chief's Advisory Committee, 
she has advocated for public safety while instilling community trust in 
law enforcement. Education has long been a cornerstone of Dr. Morris's 
work. After earning her doctorate in education, she dedicated her 
career to expanding educational opportunities, serving as a college 
instructor, graduate coach, and K-12 educational leader. Today, Dr. 
Morris manages a public assistance program focused on empowering low-
income students and helping them to realize their educational goals.
  Dr. Morris's commitment to diversity and inclusion is equally 
commendable. During her tenure at PG&E, she led the Black Employee 
Resource Group and received the President's Diversity Champion Award in 
recognition of her commitment to mentorship and community mobilization. 
She continues her service through leadership roles in the NAACP Oakland 
Branch, Mount Calvary Baptist Church, the Northern California UNCF 
Scholarship Planning Committee, and the National Panhellenic Council of 
Solano County. Her life of service epitomizes compassion, commitment, 
and humility. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Dr. Dela Weeks-
Morris for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
