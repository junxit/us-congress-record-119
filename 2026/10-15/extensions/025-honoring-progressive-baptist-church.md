# HONORING PROGRESSIVE BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING PROGRESSIVE BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Progressive Baptist 
Church of Lafayette, Louisiana, a steadfast beacon of faith, hope, and 
community service located at 2001 East Simcoe Street. Founded in 1925 
by Brothers and Sisters Warren Hall, W.O. Brian R. Anderson, and Henry 
Sias, Progressive Baptist Church has served generations of worshippers 
and community members with dedication and purpose.
  Shortly after its organization, Reverend A.C. Marshall was called to 
serve as the church's first pastor. He led a congregation of nine 
members, and a small, white-framed church was constructed in 1926, 
marking a new era of growth and stability. Progressive Baptist Church 
later became affiliated with the Seventh District Missionary Baptist 
Association, and Reverend Marshall faithfully served the congregation 
for 20 years.
  Following Reverend Marshall's tenure, Reverend Alex, Reverend J.J. 
Webster, Reverend E.D. Rideaux, Reverend C.R. Steward, and Reverend 
Freddie Banks served in succession, continuing the church's mission of 
spreading the Gospel and strengthening its ministry. Reverend Raymond 
Smith later became pastor and oversaw a period of remarkable growth and 
development, including the expansion and renovation of the church 
building in 1953, the purchase of property for future development, the 
construction of a fellowship hall, and the acquisition of land for a 
church cemetery.
  Following Reverend Smith's pastorate, Pastor Lloyd Joiner, Jr., was 
called to lead Progressive Baptist Church, ushering in an extraordinary 
era of growth, vision, and ministry. Under Pastor Joiner's dynamic 
leadership, the current sanctuary was constructed, and the church 
completed numerous renovations and improvements, including the Lloyd 
Joiner, Jr. Educational Building, which provides space for Christian 
education and ministries, as well as a daycare facility serving 
families in the community.
  Pastor Joiner's commitment to community engagement also led to the 
establishment of the Progressive Community Outreach Center. The center 
serves the congregation and the wider Lafayette community by hosting 
health screenings, a GED program, sports events, financial literacy 
classes, grief share meetings, a summer enrichment program, and a 
testing center--all with the mission of sharing the love and Word of 
God.
  Under Pastor Joiner's continued vision, United Theological Seminary 
and Bible College established an extension campus on the grounds of 
Progressive Baptist Church, training men and women from various 
Christian denominations for leadership within their churches, 
communities, and beyond. After 44 years of faithful service, Pastor 
Lloyd Joiner retired and now serves honorably as Pastor Emeritus.
  On October 15, 2023, Dr. Chad E. Nora was elected as the next pastor 
of Progressive Baptist Church. Since assuming leadership, Pastor Chad 
has emphasized three core focuses: strong biblical teaching; a loving, 
sincere church community; and caring, effective, and meaningful 
outreach. He has overseen multiple facility upgrades, modernized church 
operations through digital innovation, introduced new ministry 
initiatives, and established the church's official vision, mission, and 
slogan, reflecting a commitment to unity, purpose, tradition, and 
progress.
  In 2025, Progressive Baptist Church celebrated its centennial, 
honoring the vision, perseverance, and faith of generations past while 
looking forward with hope and purpose to the future.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Pastor 
Chad E. Nora and the faithful congregation of Progressive Baptist 
Church for a century of worship, ministry, Christian education, and 
community outreach. May Progressive Baptist Church continue to serve as 
a beacon of faith and hope for the people of Lafayette and beyond for 
generations to come.
```
