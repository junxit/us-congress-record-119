# CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BAKER FUNERAL HOME

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
      CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BAKER FUNERAL HOME

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Baker 
Funeral Home on the extraordinary occasion of its 100th anniversary.
  For a full century, Baker Funeral Home has been a trusted part of the 
Fort Worth community, providing comfort and compassionate service to 
families during some of life's most difficult moments. Founded in 1917, 
the funeral home has grown alongside the community it serves while 
remaining committed to the values of family, faith, service, and 
perseverance. For generations, families have entrusted Baker Funeral 
Home with the care of their loved ones, knowing they will be treated 
with dignity, compassion, and respect.
  The Baker family's impact extends far beyond their work as funeral 
professionals. Over the past 100 years, they have created businesses, 
supported community institutions, provided essential services, and 
helped open doors of opportunity for generations of Fort Worth 
residents. Their longstanding presence is woven into the history and 
character of the community.
  What makes this milestone especially meaningful is the continuity of 
the Baker family's legacy. Multiple generations have dedicated 
themselves to the funeral home and to the families who rely on its 
services. Through periods of tremendous change in Fort Worth, the Baker 
family has remained a constant presence, demonstrating the lasting 
impact that a family-owned business can have when it is rooted in 
service to its community.
  Reaching a 100-year milestone is a remarkable achievement for any 
organization. Reaching it while remaining family-owned and deeply 
committed to the people it serves is an especially meaningful 
accomplishment.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize the Baker Funeral Home for their 100th Anniversary. I 
congratulate them on this exciting milestone and wish them continued 
success in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
