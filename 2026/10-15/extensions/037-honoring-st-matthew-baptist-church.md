# HONORING ST. MATTHEW BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING ST. MATTHEW BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor St. Matthew Baptist 
Church of Alexandria, Louisiana, which was organized on October 5, 
1869, and has served its community with faith and devotion for more 
than 150 years. Born from a vision and an open heart, St. Matthew built 
its first facility on Sunny Island under the leadership of the late 
Reverend Jake Johnson. The building was large enough to hold three rows 
of pews, and the congregation also built a parsonage for its pastor and 
established burial grounds where many of its forebears are laid to 
rest. The congregation worshiped at that location for 66 years, and in 
that time the church became a place where families worshiped, raised 
their children, and laid their loved ones to rest.
  In 1935, rising river waters forced St. Matthew to relocate. The Odd 
Fellows and Ruth's, a local fraternal organization whose members were 
also members of the church, dedicated a half-acre of land for a second 
church home. That gift shows how closely St. Matthew's story is tied to 
the people around it, and the church has long drawn strength from that 
spirit of community partnership. By 1984, the church roll recorded 187 
members, and it continued to grow, adding to the strength of the faith 
community of Alexandria.
  Beyond worship, St. Matthew served as an educational center. The 
lodge allowed the church to use its hall for school, which became known 
as Luckett Elementary School and soon became the educational center for 
the whole community. By giving children a place to learn, the church 
and its neighbors showed that faith and service to the community go 
together, and that investment in education is an investment in the 
future. This history of service reaches well beyond the walls of the 
sanctuary and remains part of the church's legacy in Alexandria.
  St. Matthew has been guided by devoted pastors, including Reverend 
Jake Johnson, Reverend William Young, Reverend Robert Cleveland, 
Reverend P.H. McGruder, Reverend Dave D. Smith, and Pastor Emeritus 
Reverend Joe S. Green. Reverend Frank Jackson serves today as Senior 
Pastor. Each generation of pastoral leadership has helped carry the 
congregation forward, preaching and teaching the gospel and nurturing a 
church family that has stood as a pillar of the faith community in 
Alexandria.
  In May 1985, the church obtained 10 acres of land where it stands 
today. Since that time, God has blessed St. Matthew with rapid growth 
in its ministries and membership. The church remains dedicated to 
preaching and teaching the gospel, firmly standing on the word of God, 
with the mission of ``Making Disciples.'' That mission shapes the 
church's impact on the faith community of Alexandria, as it helps 
believers grow in faith and prepares them to serve others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring St. Matthew 
and its congregation for more than 150 years of faith, perseverance, 
and service to the people of Alexandria. May St. Matthew continue to 
make disciples,

strengthen the faith community, and serve its neighbors for generations 
to come.
```
