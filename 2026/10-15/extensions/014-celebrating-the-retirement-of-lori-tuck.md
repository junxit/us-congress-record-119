# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF LORI TUCK

**Speaking:** Burchett, Tim (B001309) [R-TN]

```
                CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF LORI TUCK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. TIM BURCHETT

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. BURCHETT. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the exceptional career of 
news anchor Lori Tucker. For 30 years, she has dedicated her career to 
informing the people of East Tennessee. After stints as a reporter and 
anchor in San Antonio, Houston, and Little Rock, Lori settled in 
Knoxville. For those of us who live in East Tennessee, she is 
recognizable as the face of Channel 6's nightly news broadcasts.
  Over several decades, she has been notable for doing good through her 
reporting. She founded the ``Project Grad Telethon'' to raise money for 
college and trade-school bound students, for which she won the ``Caught 
Doing Right for Kids'' Award. Lori has also been praised for her 
medical reporting, specifically her educational stories on cancer. 
Along with her evening news team, she was the recipient of the Edward 
R. Murrow Award.
  Outside of her work, Lori is a member of the Executive Women's 
Association of Knoxville and graduate of the Leadership Knoxville Class 
of 1998.
  I wish her well in her much-deserved retirement, where she no doubt 
will enjoy spending time with her husband Steve, her two daughters, and 
her grandchildren.

                          ____________________
```
