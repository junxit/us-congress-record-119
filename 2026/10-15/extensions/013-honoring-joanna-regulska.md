# HONORING JOANNA REGULSKA

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                        HONORING JOANNA REGULSKA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
contributions of Joanna Regulska, upon her retirement from the role of 
Vice Provost and Dean of Global Affairs and distinguished professor of 
gender, sexuality and women's studies at the University of California, 
Davis.
  Regulska has led large-scale programs that transform institutions, 
establish collaborative partnerships and substantially improve globally 
focused services for over 30 years. She was appointed as the first 
Chief Global Strategist at the University of California, Davis (UC 
Davis) on September 1, 2025. She also oversees Global Affairs at UC 
Davis where she strives to provide all students with global learning 
experiences through a Provost's Priority of Global Education for All. 
She engages faculty, staff and students with the United Nations 
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) as part of UC Davis's commitment 
to solving global challenges. Regulska has helped promote understanding 
and address global inequities for all students.
  Regulska serves on the board of directors for the National 
Association of Foreign Student Advisers (NAFSA): Association of 
International Educators as the Vice President for public policy and 
practice. In 2022, she was honored with the Charles Klasek Award for 
outstanding service in the field of international higher education from 
the Association of international Education Administrators. In 2021, 
Regulska was named an inaugural member of the National Academy for 
International Education, the first learned society of its kind made up 
of distinguished leaders from around the world. In 2019, she was 
honored with the first-ever Senior International Officer Award from the 
Institute of International Education.
  Regulska's work at Global Affairs has helped UC Davis attain several 
prominent awards. In 2020, UC Davis earned the Association of Public 
and Land-Grant Universities' (APLU) Platinum Institutional Award for 
Global Learning, Research and Engagement and NAFSA's Senator Paul Simon 
Award for Comprehensive Campus Internationalization. In 2025, UC Davis 
received the Innovative Excellence in Internationalization Award from 
the Association of International Education Administrators.
  Regulska's research concentrates on women's political activism, 
grassroots mobilization, decentralization, democracy and 
democratization, with a focus on Europe and the Caucasus. She is the 
author and co-author of

[[Page E1085]]

nine books and over 100 articles and chapters, including her most 
recent book, Global Goals, Global Education: Advancing the United 
Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Her research has been supported 
by the National Science Foundation, European Commission, Ford 
Foundation, American Council of Learned Societies, Andrew Mellon 
Foundation and other funding agencies. In recognition of her 
exceptional career, Regulska received the 2022 Outstanding Achievement 
Award from the Association for Women in Slavic Studies.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Joanna Regulska for her dedicated service to our 
community at the University of California, Davis, Therefore, it is 
fitting and proper that we honor her today.
```
