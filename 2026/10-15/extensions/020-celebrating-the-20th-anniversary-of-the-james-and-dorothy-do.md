# CELEBRATING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE JAMES AND DOROTHY DOSS HERITAGE AND CULTURE CENTER

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
CELEBRATING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE JAMES AND DOROTHY DOSS HERITAGE 
                           AND CULTURE CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 20th 
anniversary of the James and Dorothy Doss Heritage and Culture Center 
in Weatherford, Texas.
  For two decades, the Doss Heritage and Culture Center has served as a 
gathering place where the history and traditions of Parker County can 
be discovered, appreciated, and passed on to future generations. What 
began as an effort to provide greater space for the county's growing 
historical collections has developed into a vibrant cultural 
institution that welcomes thousands of visitors each year.
  The exhibits at the Doss highlight the individuals, communities, 
industries, and events that have helped shape Parker County. Community 
learning and family activities are offered to make local history 
accessible and interesting to visitors of all ages. From historic 
artifacts and pioneer-era structures to Western art, music, and 
cultural celebrations, the center provides a unique connection to the 
past.
  The Doss also plays an important role in bringing people together. 
Events such as the annual Pioneer Heritage Fest draw visitors from 
across Texas and beyond, while concerts, educational programs, 
children's activities, and partnerships with local organizations 
strengthen the center's role in the community.
  This milestone would not be possible without the dedication of the 
staff, volunteers, board members, donors, and countless community 
supporters who have recognized the importance of preserving Parker 
County's story. Their efforts ensure that future generations will have 
the opportunity to learn from and appreciate the people and traditions 
that came before them.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize the James and Dorothy Doss Heritage and Cultural Center and 
everyone who helped make possible its enduring contribution to 
Weatherford, Parker County, and the broader Texas community. May its 
next chapter be filled with continued growth, discovery, and a lasting 
commitment to preserving the history that makes this community special.
```
