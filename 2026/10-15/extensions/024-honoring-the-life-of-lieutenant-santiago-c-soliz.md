# HONORING THE LIFE OF LIEUTENANT SANTIAGO C. SOLIZ

**Speaking:** De La Cruz, Monica (D000594) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE OF LIEUTENANT SANTIAGO C. SOLIZ

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MONICA DE LA CRUZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Ms. De La CRUZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
extraordinary legacy of Lieutenant Santiago C. Soliz, a cherished 
family man, proud veteran, and devoted public servant from Alice, 
Texas.
  A proud graduate of Alice High School, Lt. Soliz went on to answer 
the call of duty through his service in the United States Army. Upon 
returning home, he chose to continue serving his community, dedicating 
more than three decades of service to the Alice Police Department.
  Beyond his long and distinguished career, Lt. Soliz was the heart and 
foundation of his family. Alongside his beloved wife, Belen, they built 
a home filled with love for their children, Juan Carlos and Monica, and 
grandchildren, Mason and Karley.
  Lt. Soliz lived a life defined by strength, selfless service, and 
love for his family and community.
```
