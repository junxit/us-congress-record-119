# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: NANCY DUNN

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: NANCY DUNN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Nancy Dunn as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Nancy served as an 
educator for the Fairfield-Suisun Unified School District for 25 years, 
teaching both elementary and middle school students. During this time, 
she built strong positive relationships with families and the 
community, fostering an environment that improved student success. 
Nancy also served as the President of the Fairfield-Suisun Unified 
Teachers Association for six years, including during the COVID-19 
Pandemic, where she assisted hundreds of teachers as they educated 
students through distanced learning. In this leadership role, she 
worked to strengthen collaboration between educators and administrators 
to advance student achievement.
  Following her teaching career, Nancy continued her commitment to 
advocating for educational improvements and was elected to serve on the 
Vacaville Unified School District Governing Board. In this role, Nancy 
improved retention rates, enhanced transparency and accountability 
mechanisms, and focused on stabilizing the School District's finances. 
She also served on the Vacaville Library Commission during its 
expansion of the Vacaville Cultural Center Library, highlighting the 
dedication Nancy has to the education of students of all ages.
  Through her decades of service as an educator, advocate, and public 
servant, Nancy has helped strengthen local schools and expand 
educational opportunities for her community. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and 
recognize Nancy Dunn for her lasting contributions to education within 
our community.
```
