# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: PATRICIA HUNTER

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: PATRICIA HUNTER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Patricia Hunter 
as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year 
for her outstanding leadership, civic engagement, and dedication to the 
Vallejo community. As president of the NAACP Vallejo Branch, Patricia 
has strengthened community partnerships, improved communications, and 
encouraged greater participation in the organization's work. Previously 
serving as youth chair, she established Youth Unit 1742 in 2020, 
engaging more than 60 young people and helping sustain youth 
programming during the COVID-19 pandemic. Patricia has helped organize 
celebrations honoring local civil rights leaders and expanded 
opportunities for young people to learn Vallejo's history and fostered 
dialogue with city leaders on issues affecting residents.
  Her commitment to public participation also includes organizing voter 
outreach and supporting youth involvement in elections. Her service 
extends to the African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church, where she has 
held numerous leadership roles in Christian education, youth ministry, 
and lay organizations at local and regional levels. She also founded 
Thoughtful Thursday, a women's fellowship that grew beyond California 
during the pandemic.
  Through her work with Black Women Organized for Political Action and 
the National Council of Negro Women, Patricia continues to champion 
community empowerment and meaningful civic involvement. On behalf of 
the people of California's 8th Congressional District, I am proud to 
honor Patricia Hunter for her steadfast service, inspiring leadership, 
and lasting contributions to our community.
```
