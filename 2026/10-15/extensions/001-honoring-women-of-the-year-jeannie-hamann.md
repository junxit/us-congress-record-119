# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: JEANNIE HAMANN

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: JEANNIE HAMANN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jeannie Hamann 
as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year 
for her exceptional service to cancer patients and the families 
throughout our community. For more than 25 years, Jeannie has turned 
compassion into action.
  As co-founder, president, and executive director of the Tim & Jeannie 
Hamann Foundation, she has helped provide more than $2 million in 
direct financial assistance to over 600 cancer patients across Solano 
and neighboring counties. The foundation helps families afford housing, 
groceries, transportation, utilities, and medical expenses during 
treatment. Jeannie's commitment began with the Hamann's Hearts for a 
Cure Relay for Life team in 1998. What started with grassroots 
fundraising grew into community events, including an annual dinner and 
auction supported by dedicated volunteers and local businesses. Her own 
breast cancer diagnosis in 2016 deepened her understanding of the 
challenges patients face and strengthened her resolve to help.
  Jeannie has also been a respected real estate professional since 
1987. She has served as president of the Solano Association of Realtors 
and the Benicia Downtown Business Association, contributing decades of 
leadership to her community. Through personal hardship and 
extraordinary generosity, Jeannie has remained a source of strength and 
hope for countless families. On behalf of the people of California's 
8th Congressional District, I am proud to honor Jeannie Hamann for her 
compassion, resilience, and enduring commitment to serving others.
```
