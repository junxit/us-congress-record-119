# HONORING SECRETARY DANIEL DRISCOLL

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
                   HONORING SECRETARY DANIEL DRISCOLL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Daniel Driscoll 
for his service as Secretary of the Army and to honor his years of 
dedication to the defense of our Nation.
  A North Carolina native, Driscoll hails from a long line of 
servicemembers who valiantly answered the call to defend America. His 
grandfather served in World War II and his father served in the Vietnam 
War. Following in their footsteps, Driscoll commissioned in the U.S. 
Army, deploying in 2009 in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom. He was 
awarded the Army Commendation Medal, Ranger Tab, and Combat Action 
Badge for his service.
  After his time in active duty, Driscoll went to law school and held 
numerous positions in the private sector. On December 4, 2024, 
President Donald Trump appointed Driscoll as Secretary of the Army. 
Secretary Driscoll worked tirelessly to modernize the force and ensure 
our warfighters had the tools and support they needed to get the job 
done.
  As Fort Bragg's Congressman, I want to thank Driscoll especially for 
the improvements he helped champion at the base. Driscoll visited Fort 
Bragg often during his tenure, and he supported the installation to 
improve readiness, soldier lethality, and build a better quality of 
life for our servicemembers and their families.
  One legacy Driscoll leaves behind is the selection of Fort Bragg to 
participate in the Army program to build and operate nuclear 
microreactors on military bases. This means Fort Bragg will have the 
secure, sustainable, and safe energy our warfighters need to train, 
deploy, and win. For years, I helped push for this capability, but it 
would not have happened without Driscoll's leadership.
  Driscoll is a great soldier and great American patriot who put duty, 
our national security, and the wellbeing of our warfighters above all 
else. I am proud to call Secretary Driscoll my friend and am grateful 
for his service to our Nation.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in thanking Daniel Driscoll for 
serving as Secretary of the Army and for his years of distinguished 
service to the United States.
```
