# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: IVONNE MALAVE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: IVONNE MALAVE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of callifornia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Ivonne Malave as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. For 35 years, Ivonne 
has worked diligently as an advocate for youth, families, and 
underserved communities in both Solano and Contra Costa County. Her 
career has spanned from Juvenile Probation, Group Homes, and numerous 
community organizations, demonstrating her commitment to service, 
empowerment, and mentorship in our community.
  For the past 16 years, Ivonne has partnered with multiple nonprofit 
agencies throughout the district and has served as a Recreation Aide 
with the City of Richmond's Community Service Department. In these 
roles, Ivonne has exhibited her devotion to the cause of uplifting our 
youth and preparing them for success in the future. In addition to 
these roles, Ivonne educates the youth in our community on job 
readiness. She ensures our young adults are exposed to a wide range of 
trades and careers, and are equipped with essential skills for 
workforce success.
  Beyond Ivonne's service to our community in her professional 
capacity, she remains a symbol of joy in our community and works to 
connect with our community's youth. She frequently takes young people 
to sporting events or on outdoor excursions, which builds their self-
efficacy through teamwork, outdoor skills, and shared experiences.
  Ivonne Malave has consistently demonstrated her commitment to 
empowering and mentoring our youth, and has acted with both diligence 
and selflessness in her commitment to improving outcomes for young 
people in our district. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure and honor to recognize Ivonne 
Malave for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
