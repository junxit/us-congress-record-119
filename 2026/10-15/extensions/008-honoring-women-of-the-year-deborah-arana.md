# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DEBORAH ARANA

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DEBORAH ARANA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Deborah Arana as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As the owner and lead 
teacher at Simply for Kids Daycare, Deborah has distinguished herself 
as an exceptional caregiver, mentoring young children with patience, 
compassion, and dedication. She consistently goes above and beyond for 
her students, nourishing each child with homecooked meals, celebrating 
their birthdays, and coordinating enriching field trips. Deborah 
prepares her students for a life of civic engagement, instilling in 
each one the importance of cooperation, responsibility, and respect.
  Deborah has also demonstrated remarkable perseverance, leading a 
small business through multiple community-wide crises that have placed 
a major strain on caregivers. Despite these challenges, Deborah remains 
committed to providing a safe, nurturing, and supportive environment 
for the children entrusted to her care. We celebrate Deborah for her 
tremendous contributions to uplifting families across our community, 
and for the vital role she plays in caring for and educating the next 
generation.
  Deborah's impact in the community extends far beyond the children who 
walk through her doors. Her unwavering commitment to education, 
service, and civic responsibility serves as an exemplary model for 
childcare providers and organizations across our community. On behalf 
of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my 
pleasure to honor and recognize Deborah Arana for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
