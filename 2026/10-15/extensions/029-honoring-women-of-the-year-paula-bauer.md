# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: PAULA BAUER

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: PAULA BAUER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Paula Bauer as 
one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year for 
her exceptional leadership, environmental advocacy, and longstanding 
commitment to civic engagement in Vallejo and throughout Solano County. 
For decades, Paula has dedicated her time and talents to strengthening 
her community.
  A longtime leader of the United Democrats, Solano County's oldest 
Democratic club, she has served in numerous leadership roles, helping 
sustain the organization and encouraging residents to participate in 
the democratic process. She currently serves on the Solano County 
Democratic Central Committee, where she continues her commitment to 
public service. Paula's passion for environmental justice has made a 
lasting impact on Vallejo. As an executive board member of Fresh Air 
Vallejo, she has worked to raise awareness of environmental concerns, 
encourage community participation, and advocate for responsible 
development. Her leadership has helped bring residents together to 
address important issues affecting health, safety, and future of their 
community.
  Paula also serves on the Solano County Planning Commission, 
representing District 2, where she brings her experience as an attorney 
and community advocacy to important land-use and development decisions. 
Known for her dependability, compassion, and willingness to mentor 
others, Paula exemplifies the power of grassroots leadership and 
meaningful public participation. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, I am proud to honor Paula 
Bauer for her outstanding service and enduring contributions to our 
community.
```
