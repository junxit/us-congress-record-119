# HONORING MEGAN PHILBROOK FOR BEING NAMED NEW HAMPSHIRE'S 2026 TEACHER OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Pappas, Chris (P000614) [D-NH]

```
 HONORING MEGAN PHILBROOK FOR BEING NAMED NEW HAMPSHIRE'S 2026 TEACHER 
                              OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHRIS PAPPAS

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of New Hampshire's 
2026 Teacher of the Year, Megan Philbrook. For 11 years, Megan has 
dedicated her life to education centered around creating a substantive 
impact on the lives of middle-school students who come through her 
classroom. Through her creativity, passion for pedagogy, and enthusiasm 
to lift students and co-educators alike, she embodies all of what it 
means to be an amazing teacher.
  Currently, Megan wears her heart on her sleeve at Andover Elementary/
Middle School, where she has taught fifth through eighth grade history 
since 2017. Since she graduated from Wheaton in 2014, she has always 
cared deeply about her students. In the classroom, her inquiry-based 
teaching style strives to immerse students in the material through 
hands-on activities such as model construction and historical 
reenactments to show them how fun history can be. Megan always has a 
smile on her face and is not only ready to teach her students, but is 
also ready to learn from them.
  The care and dedication Megan has for her community extends beyond 
the material. Megan's infectious passion for education shows itself 
every day through her mission to help teachers and students find joy in 
their work. For Megan, to make learning magical is not reserved just 
for students; it is for teachers too. Going forth, her goal will be to 
connect teachers with underused resources such as grants, professional 
development opportunities, and local partnerships to provide students 
with purposeful learning experiences.
  On behalf of the constituents of New Hampshire's 1st Congressional 
District, I congratulate Megan on being named New Hampshire's 2026 
Teacher of the Year. I wish Megan, her family, and her students all the 
best in the years to come as she continues inspiring the next 
generation of Granite Staters.

                          ____________________
```
