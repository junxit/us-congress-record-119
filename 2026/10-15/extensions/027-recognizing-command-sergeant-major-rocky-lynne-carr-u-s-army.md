# RECOGNIZING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR ROCKY LYNNE CARR, U.S. ARMY (RET.), WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
RECOGNIZING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR ROCKY LYNNE CARR, U.S. ARMY (RET.), 
               WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Command Sergeant 
Major Rocky Lynne Carr, a distinguished United States Army veteran and 
a resident of Lufkin, Texas, who has devoted more than three decades of 
his life to serving our Nation and his fellow Americans.
  Command Sergeant Major Carr served 31 years in the United States 
Army, culminating his career as the 17th Command Sergeant Major of the 
Military Surface Deployment and Distribution Command, the Army Service 
Component Command to the United States Transportation Command. 
Throughout his distinguished career, he served in positions of 
increasing responsibility and leadership, from leading Soldiers at the 
company level to serving as a senior enlisted leader for major Army 
organizations in the United States, Europe, and around the world.
  His military service included two deployments to Iraq, two 
deployments to Bosnia, and a deployment to Kuwait. His service in these 
demanding environments required exceptional leadership, discipline, and 
dedication to the Soldiers under his command. Among his many military 
decorations and awards, Command Sergeant Major Carr received the Bronze 
Star Medal twice for his service during deployments to Iraq, as well as 
multiple awards recognizing his leadership, achievement, and 
meritorious service.

[[Page E1089]]

  Throughout his career, Rocky understood that leadership was about 
more than accomplishing a mission. It was about taking care of people. 
He placed a strong emphasis on developing future leaders, building 
cohesive teams, maintaining readiness, and supporting Soldiers, 
civilians, and military families. His commitment to mentorship and 
professional development was further demonstrated through his 
involvement with the Sergeant Morales Club and his efforts to develop 
generations of non-commissioned officers.
  After retiring from active military service in March 2022, Command 
Sergeant Major Carr continued his commitment to serving others. Rather 
than stepping away from service, he brought his decades of military 
leadership and organizational experience home to East Texas.
  Today, he serves without compensation as the Co-Founder and Vice 
President/Chief Operating Officer of Make it Happen Missions, a 
nonprofit organization dedicated to connecting volunteers with 
individuals and communities facing genuine needs. Through this 
organization, he helps organize volunteers, establish community 
partnerships, coordinate service projects, secure resources, and turn 
identified needs into tangible results.
  In just the last two years, Make it Happen Missions has generated 
more than $320,000 in cost avoidance for East Texas families, churches, 
and nonprofits, while coordinating more than 5,000 volunteer hours 
involving 487 volunteers and 109 collaborations with community 
organizations.
  One example of this service is the organization's partnership with 
the Texas Ramp Project, through which Command Sergeant Major Carr and 
fellow volunteers have helped construct accessibility ramps for elderly 
residents and individuals with disabilities. These projects provide 
safer access to homes and help individuals maintain their independence 
and dignity.
  His community service extends well beyond ramp construction. He has 
helped with home repairs, assistance to veterans, support for elderly 
and disabled residents, community cleanups, emergency assistance, and 
efforts supporting underserved communities across East Texas. Since 
2024, he has personally devoted countless hours to directing, planning, 
and coordinating these efforts.
  Rocky has also helped transform a severely deteriorated warehouse 
into a functional headquarters and operations center for Make it Happen 
Missions. Through volunteer labor, donated resources, community 
partnerships, and careful stewardship, he helped create a facility 
where volunteers can be trained, equipped, and dispatched to meet the 
needs of their communities.
  Mr. Speaker, Rocky represents the very best traditions of military 
and community service. His commitment to others did not end when he 
retired from the Army. Instead, he carried the values of leadership, 
discipline, teamwork, compassion, and service from his military career 
into a new mission--serving the people of East Texas.
  His 31 years of military service, combat deployments, senior enlisted 
leadership, commitment to mentoring Soldiers, and continued dedication 
to his community are worthy of recognition.
  It is my honor to recognize Command Sergeant Major Rocky L. Carr as a 
recipient of the 2026 Congressional Veteran Commendation for the 17th 
Congressional District of Texas. I commend him for his extraordinary 
record of service to our Nation and for continuing to make a meaningful 
difference in the lives of his fellow East Texans.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Command 
Sergeant Major Rocky L. Carr for his distinguished military service and 
his continued commitment to serving others.
```
