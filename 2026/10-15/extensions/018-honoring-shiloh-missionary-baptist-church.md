# HONORING SHILOH MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING SHILOH MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Shiloh Missionary 
Baptist Church of Port Allen, Louisiana, a longstanding congregation 
and community institution located at 950 7th Street. Under the 
leadership of Reverend Dr. Raymond E. Allen, Sr., Shiloh Missionary 
Baptist Church continues to serve its congregation and surrounding 
community through worship, ministry, education, and outreach.
  Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church has a long history in the Baton 
Rouge community, with limited records dating to before 1899. In 1899, 
the church was reorganized under the direction of the Fourth District 
Baptist Association, and Reverend A. A. Ginn was elected pastor. In 
1900, land was purchased from the Benevolent Society to establish a 
permanent church home, and the church was officially chartered.
  Over the following decades, Shiloh experienced continued growth under 
the leadership of several pastors. In 1965, the original church 
building was demolished, and a new structure was constructed. The 
congregation returned to its present site on January 23, 1966, and paid 
the mortgage in full in less than 10 years. The church also expanded 
its property by acquiring land for a cemetery, additional parking, and 
future development.
  In 1991, Reverend Alvin L. Green, Sr., became pastor and emphasized 
local missions, community outreach, and Christian education. Following 
his passing in 2000, Reverend Raymond Allen, Sr., was elected pastor on 
July 3, 2001. Under his continued leadership, the church experienced 
increased attendance, membership growth, and expanded ministries.
  In 2011, Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church began planning for a new 
fellowship hall to provide additional space for worship, ministry, 
education, and community activities. This effort reflects the 
congregation's enduring commitment to meeting the needs of its members 
and strengthening its service to the surrounding community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Reverend 
Dr. Raymond E. Allen, Sr., and the faithful congregation of Shiloh 
Missionary Baptist Church for their enduring commitment to worship, 
ministry, Christian education, and community outreach. May Shiloh 
Missionary Baptist Church continue to serve as a beacon of faith and 
hope for the people of Port Allen and West Baton Rouge Parish for 
generations to come.
```
