# CONGRATULATING THE WEATHERFORD CHAMBER OF COMMERCE ON THE CONSTRUCTION OF THEIR NEW BUILDING

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
CONGRATULATING THE WEATHERFORD CHAMBER OF COMMERCE ON THE CONSTRUCTION 
                         OF THEIR NEW BUILDING

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
Weatherford Chamber of Commerce and the construction of its new 
facility in Weatherford, Texas, which represents an important 
investment in the continued growth and economic strength of Weatherford 
and Parker County.
  For generations, the Chamber has served as an advocate and resource 
for local businesses, helping strengthen connections among business 
leaders, entrepreneurs, community organizations, and residents. As 
Weatherford and the surrounding region continue to experience 
significant growth, the Chamber's new building will provide additional 
space and resources to support the evolving needs of the business 
community and create new opportunities for collaboration and economic 
development throughout Parker County.
  The new Chamber facility will be an important addition to the 
Weatherford community, creating a dedicated space where businesses and 
community leaders can connect, exchange ideas, and build lasting 
partnerships. Its development comes at a meaningful time for Parker 
County, as continued population and business growth bring new 
opportunities to the region. By expanding its physical presence and 
resources, the Chamber will be well positioned to welcome new 
businesses, strengthen existing enterprises, and contribute to the 
continued prosperity of the community for years to come.
  I commend the Weatherford Chamber of Commerce, its leadership, 
members, partners, and supporters for their commitment to this project 
and to the future of the community. This new facility is a tangible 
symbol of confidence in Weatherford and Parker County and will serve as 
a lasting investment in the businesses, families, and communities that 
call the region home.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize the Weatherford Chamber of Commerce on its new facility. I 
wish the Chamber continued growth and success into the future.

                          ____________________
```
