# HONORING NEW SCOTT OLLY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING NEW SCOTT OLLY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor New Scott Olly Baptist 
Church of Alexandria, Louisiana--an enduring institution of faith, 
fellowship, and community service that has stood as a spiritual anchor 
in Rapides Parish for nearly six decades. Formally organized on January 
26, 1965, as a Non-Profit Religious Corporation under Louisiana law, 
New Scott Olly Baptist Church has grown from its early beginnings into 
a vibrant and beloved congregation deeply rooted in Scripture-centered 
worship and service to others.
  From its founding, New Scott Olly Baptist Church embraced the Baptist 
traditions of believer's baptism, congregational governance, and 
worship grounded in the Word of God. Over the years, the congregation 
developed a blended worship experience, incorporating both traditional 
hymns and contemporary praise, reflecting the diverse expressions of 
faith within its fellowship. Its ministries for children and youth have 
played a central role in nurturing the next generation, providing a 
place where young people can grow spiritually, build community, and 
learn the importance of service.
  More than a gathering place for Sunday worship, New Scott Olly 
Baptist Church has long functioned as a community hub--a place where 
individuals and families find support, spiritual guidance, and 
meaningful connection. Bible study groups, prayer meetings, and 
outreach ministries have shaped the church's identity as one committed 
to holistic ministry. The church's location in the heart of Alexandria 
makes it accessible to lingtime residents and newcomers alike, and its 
doors remain open to all who seek fellowship, hope, and a deeper 
understanding of God's Word.
  The impact of New Scott Olly Baptist Church extends well beyond its 
sanctuary walls. For decades, the church has provided a moral and 
spiritual presence in Rapides Parish, supporting community 
partnerships, participating in local outreach programs, and offering 
assistance to those facing economic, social, or personal challenges. 
Whether through food drives, youth mentorship, community events, or 
support for struggling families, the church has demonstrated unwavering 
dedication to serving its neighbors--embodying the Biblical can to love 
one another in word and deed.
  Today, New Scott Olly Baptist Church continues its forward-looking 
ministry under the leadership of Reverend Lynell Smith, who has guided 
the congregation with steadfast devotion and a passion for outreach. 
Under Reverend Smith's leadership, the church remains committed to 
spiritual growth, congregational unity, and community engagement. His 
vision has strengthened the church's mission and reaffirmed the 
commitment of its members to live out their faith through service, 
compassion, and worship.
  Mr. Speaker, after more than 60 years of ministry, New Scott Olly 
Baptist Church stands as a testament to the power of faith, resilience, 
and community. Its enduring presence in Alexandria reflects generations 
of believers who dedicated their time, talents, and hearts to the work 
of the church. As it continues to grow and serve, New Scott Olly 
Baptist Church remains a beacon of hope and a vital part of the 
spiritual and civic life of Rapides Parish. I urge my colleagues to 
join me in honoring New Scott Olly Baptist Church for its dedication to 
the community, and the spreading of the Word of God.
```
