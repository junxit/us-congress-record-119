# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: HELEN INTINTOLI

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: HELEN INTINTOLI

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Helen Intintoli 
as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of

[[Page E1087]]

the Year. Helen's commitment to local culture was recognized in 2009 
when the Vaca Arts Council honored her with a special award for 
individuals who ``go the extra mile for local culture.'' That 
distinction reflects what those who know Helen understand: she builds, 
nurtures, and sustains the artistic life of her community.
  Helen retired from Solano Community College and continues to work 
with students through music at the college and throughout the 
community. One of her most enduring legacies is the Madrigal Dinner 
events, blending music, dancing, storytelling, and theatrical artistry 
while inspiring students and delighting audiences.
  Helen served as Choir Director at St. Vincent Ferrer Church for 24 
years, encouraging singers to grow while maintaining a spirit of 
warmth, inclusion, and excellence. Her love for music is a lived 
mission. She and her ensemble, Well Tempered Voices, practiced for many 
years at her home, released two CDs and traveled to Japan to perform.
  Helen joined Soroptimist in 2005, helping to improve the lives of 
women and girls locally and around the world. She served in several 
leadership roles, including twice as President.
  Helen embodies excellence, perseverance, and service to others. She 
has spent her life strengthening community through music, culture, 
mentorship, and kindness. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor Helen Intintoli for 
her outstanding contributions to our community.
```
