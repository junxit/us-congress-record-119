# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: MELISSA EDWARDS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: MELISSA EDWARDS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Melissa Edwards 
as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. 
Melissa is recognized for her extraordinary dedication to youth, 
community service, and the people of Vallejo, California. Melissa has 
devoted decades to supporting young people in her community, she taught 
kids about wildlife at Marine World and later worked for the Child 
Support Division, ensuring parents were fulfilling their 
responsibilities to their children.
  Melissa has spent a number of hours volunteering with the local Boy 
Scouts and continues to spend a few weeks every year planning a 
curriculum in addition to facilitating the fun science activities for 
the Scouts Day camps. Melissa has supported the Cal Poly Maritime 
Academy in Vallejo by providing home cooked meals for the students and 
has taught many kitchen skills and cooking classes for the youth. 
Through her business ``Enchanted Cottage Kitchen'', Melissa was one of 
the first home kitchen that was certified through Solano County, paving 
the way for future home kitchen businesses.
  Beyond these contributions, Melissa has played an instrumental role 
in sustaining the Vallejo Sports Hall of Fame, helping preserve the 
history of athletics in Vallejo and ensuring future generations receive 
the support they deserve for local sports programs. She has spent her 
life strengthening the community through support, teaching, resources 
and kindness. On behalf of the people of California's eighth 
Congressional District, I am pleased to honor Melissa Edwards for her 
unwavering commitment to service and her lasting contributions to the 
city of Vallejo.

                          ____________________
```
