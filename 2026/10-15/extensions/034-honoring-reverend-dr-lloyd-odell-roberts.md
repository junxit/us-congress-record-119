# HONORING REVEREND DR. LLOYD ODELL ROBERTS

**Speaking:** Beyer, Donald S. (B001292) [D-VA]

```
               HONORING REVEREND DR. LLOYD ODELL ROBERTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. DONALD S. BEYER, JR.

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. BEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Reverend Dr. Lloyd Odell 
Roberts, he was a man of unwavering faith, devoted service, steadfast 
leadership, and deep love for his family and community. His life, which 
spanned more than nine decades, was marked by a commitment to serving 
God, his country, his church, and those around him. He leaves behind a 
legacy that will continue to speak through the many lives he touched.
  Rev. Roberts proudly served his country for 20 years in the United 
States Air Force. His military career reflected the discipline and 
dedication that would characterize every chapter of his life. He 
retired honorably in 1972 with the rank of Master Sergeant.
  Following his military service, Rev. Roberts continued his commitment 
to public service as a Capitol Police Officer. During his tenure, he 
had the distinction of serving under the Honorable Thurgood Marshall, 
the first African American Justice of the United States Supreme Court.
  Whether in uniform or in service to his community, Rev. Roberts 
understood that leadership was not simply a position, it was a 
responsibility.
  Rev. Roberts was united in marriage to Barbara Lee Chisman whom he 
shared three children with. His family remained one of the greatest 
sources of meaning and joy throughout his life.
  Throughout his life, Rev. Roberts served several churches in Northern 
Region and surrounding region, including years of service as Pastor of 
Ebenezer Baptist Church in Alexandria, Virginia.
  His ministry extended well beyond the walls of the local church. Rev. 
Roberts served faithfully in numerous leadership positions, including 
First Vice Moderator, member of the Baptist Center Board, and teacher 
within the Northern Virginia Baptist Association. He believed that the 
church had a responsibility to meet people where they were and to serve 
the broader community.
  Rev. Roberts remained connected to the church and to the faith that 
had guided him throughout his life. Whenever possible, he faithfully 
attended First Baptist Church of Glenarden, maintaining a spiritual 
connection to the congregation that had welcomed him and his family 
nearly six decades earlier and where he had first answered the call to 
preach.
  Rev. Dr. Lloyd Odell Roberts departed this life peacefully on Sunday, 
May 24, 2026, at the age of 94.
  His journey carried him from Williamsburg, Virginia, to the United 
States Air Force, to public service, and ultimately to a lifetime of 
pastoral ministry. Along the way, he was a husband, father, 
grandfather, pastor, teacher, mentor, community leader, and faithful 
servant of God.
  Rev. Dr. Lloyd Odell Roberts lived a life of purpose, served with 
distinction, led with conviction, and walked faithfully in his calling.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Rev. Roberts and his 
enduring legacy.

                          ____________________
```
