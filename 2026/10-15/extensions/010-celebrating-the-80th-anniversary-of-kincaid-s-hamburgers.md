# CELEBRATING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF KINCAID'S HAMBURGERS

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
        CELEBRATING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF KINCAID'S HAMBURGERS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 80th 
Annviersary of Kincaid's Halnburgers in Fort Worth Texas. This 
remarkable milestone reflects not just a successful business but a 
cornerstone of community. Since Charles Kincaid opened his grocery 
store in 1946. Kincaid's has grown from a neighborhood business into a 
Fort Worth institution. For eight decades, it has welcomed generations 
of families, friends, and visitors, becoming a cherished part of the 
city's character and culture.
  The iconic Kincaid's hamburger was developed in the 1960s by Ownyel 
Robert ``O.R.'' Gentry, the butcher at Charles Kincaid's Grocery & 
Market. As the first grocery store in the area to feature a specialty 
fullservice meat depamnent. Kincaid's provided O.R. Gentry with access 
to high-quality beef, inspiring him to begin grilling hamburgers that 
would become a beloved Fort Worth tradition. What began as a local 
favorite soon gained widespread acclaim, and in the early 1980s. 
Kincaid's was recognized among the nation's best hamburger restaurants.
  Kincaid's also represents a remarkable legacy of family 
entrepreneurship. O.R. Gentry eventually purchased Kincaid's, which was 
later passed down to his son, Ron. Today, Ron's sons, Jonathan and 
Christian, continue that legacy, guiding Kincaid's into its eighth 
decade while preserving the qualities that have made it a beloved Fort 
Worth establishment.
  Celebrating an 80th anniversary is an extraordinary accomplishment, 
particularly for a family-owned business that has remained so closely 
connected to the cornmunity it serves. This milestone reflects the 
dedication of the Gentry family, the employees who have contributed to 
Kincaid's success, and the generations of customers who have made the 
restaurant a part of their lives.
  For 80 years. Kincaid's has been a place where people gather, 
celebrate, and share in one of the simple pleasures that brings a 
community together. Its enduring success is a testament to the strength 
of its family legacy and the special relationship it has built with the 
people of Fort Worth.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas. I congratulate 
the Gentry family, cheir employees, and everyone who has contributed to 
Kincaid's remarkable 80-year history. May this cornerstone of Fort 
Worth tradition continue to thrive for many generations to come.
```
