# HONORING IMPACT CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                         HONORING IMPACT CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 15, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Impact Church of 
Shreveport, Louisiana, which has grown from a newly established 
ministry in the late 1990s into an institution of worship, spiritual 
development, leadership, and community service. The church traces its 
beginnings to September 11, 1998, when Pastor Rasby G. Mason II 
received the call to organize what was originally known as Temple of 
Deliverance Full Gospel Baptist Church, built on a vision of reaching 
people, transforming lives, and making a meaningful difference in the 
community.
  Only 2 days later, on September 13, 1998, Temple of Deliverance held 
its first worship service at the Clarion Hotel on 70th Street in 
Shreveport. Ninety-one people attended, and 52 became members, giving 
the new ministry a strong foundation. Without a permanent facility, the 
congregation showed that a church's strength lies in the people who 
believe in its mission. As attendance grew, services moved to Louisiana 
State University Shreveport, and in May 1999; less than a year after 
that first service, the ministry moved into its own location on East 
70th Street. That permanent home allowed the church to expand its 
worship, ministries, leadership development, outreach, and programs for 
families, and to become more closely connected to the surrounding 
Shreveport community.
  In 2012, the church adopted the name Impact Church, reflecting an 
expanded vision of making a positive and lasting impact on individuals, 
families, communities, and ultimately the world. The name is tied to 
the ministry's guiding vision of ``impacting the world one person at a 
time,'' the belief that meaningful change begins with individuals, and 
that strengthening one person's faith, encouraging one family, 
developing one leader, or helping one person in difficulty can reach 
outward into neighborhoods, workplaces, and communities. Central to the 
church's identity is a culture of love and grace, in which people from 
different backgrounds and life experiences can find acceptance, 
spiritual direction, and opportunities for personal growth. Impact 
Church also identifies as a deliverance ministry, maintaining the 
spiritual foundation established when it was organized as Temple of 
Deliverance, and seeks through worship, preaching, prayer, and biblical 
instruction to help people overcome personal and spiritual challenges.
  Worship remains central to the life of Impact Church, where members 
receive biblical teaching, pray, and build fellowship. The church has 
also made leadership development a priority, encouraging members to 
strengthen their gifts and talents so they can be positive influences 
in their families, careers, churches, and communities. Its focus on 
families reflects the conviction that strong families build strong 
communities, and the church offers spiritual guidance, encouragement, 
and resources to help people of all ages and backgrounds navigate 
everyday life.
  Impact Church's commitment to service extends beyond its worship. 
Through community outreach, the congregation seeks to provide practical 
assistance and encouragement to people both within and outside its 
membership, in the belief that ministry is measured not only by what 
happens during a worship service but by what members do for their 
neighbors when they leave the sanctuary. The ministry has grown through 
the commitment of its pastors, ministry leaders, volunteers, families, 
and members, who have invested their time, talents, resources, and 
faith to turn the vision of 1998 into a ministry that serves the 
Shreveport community.
  Today, Impact Church continues its ministry under the leadership of 
Bishop Rasby G. Mason II, Apostle and Founder, whose leadership remains 
connected to the vision that inspired the original congregation. Pastor 
Jacqueline Mason serves as Site Pastor, providing pastoral leadership 
to the congregation and supporting its emphasis on worship, 
discipleship, family, leadership development, and community engagement. 
Together with the church's ministry leaders and members, they continue 
to build on the foundation established more than a quarter-century ago.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Impact Church 
and its congregation for more than 25 years of vision, faith, and 
service to the people of Shreveport. May Impact Church continue to 
create a culture of love and grace, support families, develop leaders, 
serve the community, and transform lives for generations to come.
```
