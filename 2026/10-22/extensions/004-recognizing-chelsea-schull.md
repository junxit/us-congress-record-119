# RECOGNIZING CHELSEA SCHULL

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                       RECOGNIZING CHELSEA SCHULL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Chelsea Schull, of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, for her eight years 
of dedication and service to the State of South Dakota. Chelsea served 
as the State Operations Manager, overseeing event coordination, the 
Service Academy nomination process, and the Vietnam Veteran 
Commemorative Pinning program, among other duties.
  Under her leadership, my office saw year-over-year increases in the 
number of South Dakota students applying for nomination to a United 
States Service Academy. She handled these applicants with 
professionalism and ushered the prospective military leaders through 
the process.
  Chelsea's appreciation and respect for the South Dakota veteran 
community was unwavering. Her coordination of the Vietnam Veteran 
Commemorative Pinning program resulted in nearly 1,600 South Dakota 
Vietnam-era veterans receiving the long-awaited recognition they 
deserved.
  Hundreds of South Dakotans had their issues with federal agencies 
resolved because of Chelsea's work and she has consistently stepped up 
in times of crisis to assist South Dakotans abroad during conflict. 
Chelsea organized more than 5,000 stakeholder meetings during her time 
and planned and executed more than 200 events, tours, and town halls.
  I also want to recognize Chelsea's family including her husband Kyle, 
and children Wyatt, Gavin, and Reagan.

[[Page E1108]]

  Chelsea has been a valuable member of my team, and I am greatly 
appreciative of her service to the people of South Dakota. Her 
commitment to public service is commendable.

                          ____________________
```
