# RECOGNIZING REID RASMUSSEN

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                       RECOGNIZING REID RASMUSSEN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Reid Rasmussen, of Canton, South Dakota, for his eight years of 
dedication and service to the State of South Dakota. Reid served as a 
Constituent Services Representative and was our team's primary contact 
for agricultural outreach, among other duties.
  During his time, Reid completed nearly 1,200 cases, helping South 
Dakotans navigate challenges with federal agencies. He became my 
office's point person for agriculture and ATF related cases.
  He maintained superior constituent services throughout changing and 
difficult political climates. His dedication to supporting constituents 
is truly commendable.
  Reid also managed the Sioux Falls district office internship program, 
serving as a mentor and advisor to students working in the office. His 
mentorship and guidance to these young professionals further represents 
his commitment to the future.
  I would also like to recognize his incredible wife, Elise.
  I am grateful for Reid's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for his commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
