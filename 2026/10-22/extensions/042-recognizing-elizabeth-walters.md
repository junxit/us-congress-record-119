# RECOGNIZING ELIZABETH WALTERS

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                     RECOGNIZING ELIZABETH WALTERS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Elizabeth Walters, of Washington, D.C., for her eight years of 
dedication and service to the State of South Dakota. Elizabeth served 
as the Deputy Chief of Staff and Legislative Director in my office, 
following her early roles as Legislative Correspondent and Legislative 
Assistant.
  Elizabeth established herself as a senior leader on our team and as a 
thoughtful policy advisor from the start, garnering respect from her 
colleagues for her thoughtfulness and sound leadership.
  She has successfully spearheaded legislative efforts within the House 
Select Committee on China, House Transportation and Infrastructure 
Committee, and numerous other areas including veterans' affairs. Her 
portfolio also included appropriations, defense, foreign affairs, 
oversight, rural water, science, supply chain, and tax.
  She routinely established my office's leadership on legislative 
priorities including Keep the Nine, the gross vehicle weight pilot 
program, tribal trust land, and other supply chain-related legislation. 
She was successful in reaching out to other Member offices to increase 
co-sponsorship of these priorities.
  Elizabeth shows up to work every day having done the necessary 
research and is always prepared and insightful in her legislative 
guidance.
  I would also like to recognize her husband, Tristan.
  I am grateful for Elizabeth's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
