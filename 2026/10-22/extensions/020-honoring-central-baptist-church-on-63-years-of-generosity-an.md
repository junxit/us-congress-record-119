# HONORING CENTRAL BAPTIST CHURCH ON 63 YEARS OF GENEROSITY AND GLOBAL MISSIONS

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
 HONORING CENTRAL BAPTIST CHURCH ON 63 YEARS OF GENEROSITY AND GLOBAL 
                                MISSIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Central 
Baptist Church in Woodbridge, Virginia, as it celebrates 63 years of 
faithful service, steadfast leadership, and unwavering commitment to 
the people of Woodbridge and the Commonwealth of Virginia.
  In 1963, under the caring leadership of Pastor Harry Owens, the 
congregation built their first permanent home in Woodbridge, planting 
roots in a place where it could serve the community around it. From 
those early days, the church's generosity and kindness have shaped what 
it truly means to serve.
  Central Baptist Church and their members are recognizable by their 
distinct dedication to making the world a better place. The mission of 
the church is to go out into the community and provide proactive 
assistance to those most in need, instead of waiting for others to come 
to them first. This kind of selflessness is what makes Central Baptist 
a pillar of love and fellowship in the greater Woodbridge area.
  It is no coincidence that as we celebrate this great milestone, 
Central Baptist is also celebrating their annual Missions Month, which 
highlights the inspiring mission work that the church participates in. 
Additionally, the calling to help others has transcended the borders of 
the Commonwealth as Central Baptist brings their mission work abroad, 
spreading the love and kindness of Virginians to the rest of the world. 
Consistently, the church demonstrates generosity to members of the 
community who are most at risk and provides meaningful assistance to 
all who need it.
  I want to personally congratulate Pastor Brad Weniger, Sr. and his 
family for their unwavering devotion to this church and for building 
Central Baptist's Bus Ministry into the largest of its kind in the 
area. I also want to thank the entire congregation for the endless 
support in the Virginia community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Central 
Baptist Church for its continued commitment and lasting contributions 
to Woodbridge and the Commonwealth.
```
