# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BRIDGE OF THE GODS

**Speaking:** Dexter, Maxine (D000635) [D-OR]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BRIDGE OF THE GODS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MAXINE DEXTER

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 100th 
anniversary of the Bridge of the Gods in Cascade Locks, a defining 
landmark connecting Oregon and Washington across the Columbia River 
Gorge.
  Spanning 1,858 feet and soaring 140 feet above the water, this steel-
truss cantilever structure has become an important transportation link 
used by residents, visitors, cyclists, and Pacific Crest Trail hikers.
  The bridge's distinctive name is a reference to Klickitat oral 
histories of a natural land bridge that once crossed the Columbia River 
Gorge following the massive 15th-century Bonneville Landslide. The 
modern bridge was built near the upstream end of the former crossing 
and opened on October 26, 1926. It became an immediate economic asset, 
allowing drivers to make a 100-mile loop along both the scenic 
Evergreen and Columbia River highways.
  As the Bridge of the Gods approaches its century of service to the 
Gorge, I join the community of Cascade Locks in celebrating all the 
connections it has helped foster in the region.
```
