# RECOGNIZING JONATHON SUNDET

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                      RECOGNIZING JONATHON SUNDET

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Jonathon Sundet of Brookings, South Dakota, for his years of 
service to the people of South Dakota. Jonathon has served many roles 
in my office starting as an intern, joining the team full time as Staff 
Assistant, and was later promoted to Legislative Correspondent. He now 
serves as a Legislative Assistant on my policy team.
  Jonathon made the office's communications with constituents and 
recognizing the public's accomplishments a real strength. His work on 
constituent correspondence grew each year. In 2024 he drafted six 
policy letters. In 2025 he drafted 50 congratulatory letters, 55 
legislative correspondence letters, and seven additional legislative 
items. Those numbers have only grown in 2026.
  In 2025, he wrote a dozen Congressional Record statements to honor 
outstanding South Dakotans, the most my office has ever submitted in a 
single year. Jonathon also improved the office's core services. He 
formalized and digitized our flag request tracking, and to promote the 
Congressional App Challenge, he reached out to 353 high school and 
middle school staff members.
  He consistently goes above and beyond for South Dakotans. In one 
case, he arranged for a flag to be flown over the Capitol and shipped 
to Rapid City on the same day it was requested, when the process 
usually takes about a month. His work directly resulted in my office 
receiving the Democracy Award for Best Constituent Experience in D.C. 
from the Congressional Management Foundation.
  I am grateful for Jonathon's service to the people of South Dakota 
and for his commitment to public service.
```
