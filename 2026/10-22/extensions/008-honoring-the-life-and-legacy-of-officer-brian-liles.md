# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF OFFICER BRIAN LILES

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF OFFICER BRIAN LILES

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today along with my colleague, 
Representative Vince Fong, to honor the life and legacy of California 
Highway Patrol Officer Brian Liles. For more than 16 years, Officer 
Liles served the people of California with honor, dedication, kindness, 
and an unwavering commitment to helping others.
  A Central Valley native, Officer Liles was born in Dinuba, 
California. He graduated from the California Highway Patrol Academy in 
2010 and began a career dedicated to keeping Californians safe. He 
served the communities of San Jose, Hayward, and Fresno before 
transferring to Visalia in 2019.
  During every shift, Officer Liles put on his uniform and patrolled 
our roads with dedication to keeping travelers safe, answering the

[[Page E1109]]

call no matter the danger presented. His service made a lasting 
difference in the lives of countless Californians, and our Central 
Valley communities are safer today because of his dedication. On 
September 28, 2026, Officer Liles made the ultimate sacrifice, losing 
his life in the line of duty while patrolling Highway 99.
  Officer Liles is survived by his wife, two children, parents, and two 
sisters. Our prayers and well-wishes are with his family, loved ones, 
and fellow California Highway Patrol Officers as they navigate this 
tremendous loss. Those who knew Officer Liles remember him as a humble, 
kind, and outstanding officer who was always willing to lend a helping 
hand to his neighbors. His commitment to serving others extended far 
beyond his badge and uniform.
  Mr. Speaker, Representative Fong and I ask our colleagues in the 
House of Representatives to join us in honoring the life of California 
Highway Patrol Officer Brian Liles. We are deeply grateful for his 
years of service and the sacrifice he made to protect the people of 
California. His commitment and dedication to the state of California 
and the people of the Central Valley will never be forgotten.

                          ____________________
```
