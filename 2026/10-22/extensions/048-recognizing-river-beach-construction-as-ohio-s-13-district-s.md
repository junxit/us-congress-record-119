# RECOGNIZING RIVER BEACH CONSTRUCTION AS OHIO'S 13 DISTRICT'S CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
 RECOGNIZING RIVER BEACH CONSTRUCTION AS OHIO'S 13 DISTRICT'S CHAMPION 
                              OF THE WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise to today recognize the crew at River 
Reach Construction in Barberton as Ohio's 13 District's Champion of the 
Week.
  This summer, workers heard cries coming from inside one of the 
company's excavators. Operating supervisor Chase Guello followed the 
sound and freed a tiny kitten trapped at the bottom of the machine.
  Unable to locate the kitten's mother, the crew turned to a local 
foster family to help nurse her back to health. They named their newest 
coworker Komatsu, or ``Sue'' for short, after the brand of excavator 
where she was found. After about two weeks in foster care, Sue returned 
to the crew that rescued her. Sue now has a permanent home with the 
company's shop mechanic, Jared, and his family. Once she is old enough, 
she will return to River Reach as the crew's resident shop cat, already 
outfitted with her own tiny safety vest and hard hat.
  It is because of community members like the crew at River Reach 
Construction that I call my district the ``Birthplace of Champions.''
```
