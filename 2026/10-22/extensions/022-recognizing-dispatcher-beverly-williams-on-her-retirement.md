# RECOGNIZING DISPATCHER BEVERLY WILLIAMS ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING DISPATCHER BEVERLY WILLIAMS ON HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dispatcher 
Beverly Williams on the occasion of her retirement from the Euless 
Police Department, after more than 24 years of dedicated service in law 
enforcement and public safety.
  Beverly began her career on July 18, 2002, as a records clerk with 
the Abilene Police Department, where she served until January 16, 2005. 
She later joined the Euless Police Department on January 8, 2007, as a 
dispatcher, a position she has faithfully held for nearly two decades. 
Throughout her career, Beverly has played an important role in 
supporting law enforcement officers, coordinating emergency responses, 
and serving the residents of her community.
  Beverly's dedication and professionalism have earned her numerous 
recognitions throughout her career. She was named the City of Euless 
Employee of the Month in January 2016 and May 2023 and was nominated 
for Civilian of the Year in both 2008 and 2014. She also received the 
Civilian Distinguished Service Award in 2011, the Silent Hero 
Certificate of Achievement in 2010, and the Communication Center Unit 
Citation in 2014. These honors, along with her many letters of 
commendation, reflect her commitment to excellence and the lasting 
impact of her service.
  As Beverly begins her well-deserved retirement, I want to 
congratulate her on an exceptional career and thank her for her many 
years of dedicated service to the Euless Police Department and our 
community.
```
