# HONORING KATHLEEN GROGAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING KATHLEEN GROGAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Kathleen Grogan as 
one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. 
Kathleen has made a profound impact in the Solano County community as a 
volunteer for the non-profit organization End 68 Hours of Hunger for 
more than five years.
  Each week, she directs a group of volunteers to pack 500 bags of food 
for children from schools in Fairfield, Vacaville, and Dixon. Because 
of their hard work and her passionate leadership, the organization 
provided over 16,000 bags of food during the 2025 to 2026 school year. 
End 68 hours of Hunger addresses the roughly 68 hours between Friday's 
school lunch and Monday's school breakfast. The program is 100 percent 
volunteer based with 95 percent of the funds going directly to 
elementary school students in need. It is the only chapter in 
California and west of the Mississippi River.
  In 2016, Kathleen visited three schools in Uganda, touching the lives 
of more than 1,500 children. She helped raise more than $5,000 for 
pumps that carry water from wells to

[[Page E1111]]

schools for cooking, bathing, and gardens that help feed students. In 
2018, she raised another $5,000 for water sanitation systems, bringing 
safe drinking water to the schools and the local hospital.
  Kathleen's leadership and generosity have fed children, brought clean 
water to communities, and inspired others to volunteer. On behalf of 
the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my 
pleasure to honor and recognize Kathleen Grogan for her extraordinary 
dedication to serving children and families, both in our local 
communities and around the world.

                          ____________________
```
