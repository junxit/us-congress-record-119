# RECOGNIZING AIRWOMAN JAYUANA TYLER AS OHIO'S 13TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
    RECOGNIZING AIRWOMAN JAYUANA TYLER AS OHIO'S 13TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                    DISTRICT'S CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Airwoman Jayuana 
Tyler as Ohio's 13th Congressional District's Champion of the Week. 
This recognition honors her remarkable accomplishments, her dedicated 
service to our country, and her many contributions to her community, 
and celebrates her outstanding service in the United States Air Force.
  After her three-year deployment, Jayuana Tyler returned to Akron in 
September 2026. Appearing as a ``guest speaker'' at her sister's 
school, she surprised her sister and the rest of her family, a moment 
they had long awaited. The reunion was a joyful homecoming for a family 
who had missed her presence at every holiday, birthday, and milestone 
while she was away.
  Airwoman Jayuana Tyler's excellence and impact started at a young 
age. After graduating from McKinley High School in 2019 with a 3.9 GPA, 
Tyler enlisted in the Airforce. In 2021, Tyler was named the 75th Air 
Base Wing Airman of the Year. This selection is reserved for those who 
demonstrate exceptional leadership, work ethic, and dedication to 
mission readiness. She was selected by leadership out of a highly 
competitive field of military and civilian personnel at Hill Air Force 
Base.
  After graduating from Airman Leadership School, Jayuana Tyler went on 
to win the Levitow Award, the highest honor in Air Force Professional 
Military Education, celebrating her placement in the top 1 percent of 
her class upon graduation in 2022. The award highlights Tyler's 
academic excellence, hard work, and determination.
  More recently, Jayuana Tyler was coined and personally recognized by 
Air Force Chief of Staff General Kenneth S. Wilsbach, the highest-
ranking member of the Air Force, a distinction given only to those who 
exemplify outstanding service and commitment. As an aviation resource 
manager, she is responsible for critical squadron functions that keep 
aircrews qualified and mission-ready, work that directly supports our 
national defense.
  I congratulate Jayuana Tyler. Her accomplishments and service to our 
Nation are a reflection of strength of Ohio's 13th District, and they 
exemplify why I am proud to call it the ``Birthplace of Champions.''
```
