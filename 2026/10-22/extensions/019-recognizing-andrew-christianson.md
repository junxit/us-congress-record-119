# RECOGNIZING ANDREW CHRISTIANSON

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                    RECOGNIZING ANDREW CHRISTIANSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Andrew

[[Page E1112]]

Christianson, of Tea, South Dakota, for eight years of dedication to my 
team and 16 years of service to the people of South Dakota. Andrew has 
served as my Chief of Staff since my first day in Congress. Under his 
leadership, my team has consistently punched above its weight.
  Andrew guided an office that saw 20 bills signed into law, with a 
dozen more passing the House of Representatives. That work earned 
national recognition. The Center for Effective Lawmaking ranked me the 
most effective House Republican on agriculture issues in two Congresses 
and on Native American issues in the 118th Congress. It also ranked me 
in the top five on transportation and public lands. In 2022, the 
American Farm Bureau Federation honored me with its Golden Plow Award.
  Andrew made sure our commitment to South Dakotans was just as strong. 
The Congressional Management Foundation recognized my office with three 
Democracy Awards: Constituent Accountability and Accessibility in 2021, 
Constituent Service in 2023, and Constituent Experience in D.C. in 
2025.
  These achievements belong to the whole team--a team built and 
maintained by Andrew. My congressional office experienced turnover much 
lower than the average House office, which is a direct result of 
Andrew's collaborative management style and positive attitude.
  I also want to recognize Andrew's family including his wife Andrea 
and children Stevie and Woodrow.
  I am grateful for his years of service to the people of South Dakota 
and for his commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
