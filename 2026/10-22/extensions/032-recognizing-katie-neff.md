# RECOGNIZING KATIE NEFF

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                         RECOGNIZING KATIE NEFF

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Katie Neff, of Keystone, South Dakota, for her eight years of 
dedication and service to my congressional office, and her total of 11 
years of service to the State of South Dakota. Katie served as the West 
River Director, providing leadership to my Rapid City office and 
representation to western South Dakota.
  Katie excelled at listening to the concerns of western South Dakota 
and representing my office in this region of the state. She was heavily 
involved in community functions and served as a voice for the office.
  During her time, Katie completed more than 400 cases, helping South 
Dakotans navigate challenges with federal agencies.
  She provided expert insights on forestry, timber, and federal lands 
policy issues. I am grateful for her guidance on these issues.
  I would also like to recognize her husband, Kwinn.
  I am grateful for Katie's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
