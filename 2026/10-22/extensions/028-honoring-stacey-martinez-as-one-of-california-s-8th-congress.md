# HONORING STACEY MARTINEZ AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING STACEY MARTINEZ AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Stacey Martinez as 
one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. A 
Vallejo native, Stacey has dedicated nearly three decades to supporting 
individuals with developmental disabilities and their families. 
Beginning her career as a Respite Care Provider in 1998, she has 
demonstrated an unwavering commitment to empowering individuals to live 
independently and lead fulfilling lives.
  Stacey currently serves as Executive Director of The Arc-Solano. Her 
career reflects a deep commitment to inclusion, dignity, and 
opportunity for all. Beyond her professional accomplishments, Stacey is 
an active community leader. She serves on the North Bay Regional 
Centers' Vendor Advisory Committee, participates in the California 
Conference of Executives of the Arc of California, and serves on the 
NBRC Board of Directors' Public Policy Committee.
  Stacey's dedication extends to numerous local organizations, 
including Soroptimist International of Vallejo, the Cave Language 
Academy PTA, 100+ Women Who Care Solano County, Leadership Vallejo and 
the Vallejo Chamber of Commerce. Through her leadership and service, 
Stacey has made a lasting impact on individuals with disabilities, 
their families, and the broader Vallejo community. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor and recognize Stacey Martinez for her outstanding contributions 
to our community.

                          ____________________
```
