# RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES NAVY SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER DONALD JONES AS OCTOBER 2026 VETERAN OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES NAVY SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER DONALD JONES 
                  AS OCTOBER 2026 VETERAN OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Senior Chief 
Petty Officer Donald Jones for his service in the United States Navy 
and his work supporting fellow veterans in Sandusky. It is my privilege 
to honor him as our October 2026 Veteran of the Month.
  Senior Chief Jones enlisted in the Navy in 1977 after graduating from 
high school, beginning what would become a 32-year military career 
spanning active duty and reserve service. After serving aboard two 
warships and completing shore assignments in Norfolk, Virginia, and San 
Diego, California, he left active duty in 1987 to spend more time with 
his wife and three young children. He later took a job at a Michigan 
foundry and moved his family to Sanilac County.
  After ten years in the civilian workforce, Senior Chief Jones joined 
the Navy Reserve and served with a Seabee battalion at Selfridge Air 
National Guard Base. In 2003, he returned to active duty. Over the next 
16 years, he served aboard four additional ships and spent three years 
instructing fellow sailors at a naval base in Groton, Connecticut.
  Alongside his Navy assignments, he earned a bachelor's degree in 
business management and graduated from the Navy's Senior Enlisted 
Academy in Newport, Rhode Island. He also served aboard the USS 
Enterprise during her final deployment before the historic ship was 
retired. His final assignment was as an Assistant Supply Officer at 
Camp David, the presidential retreat.
  Senior Chief Jones received numerous military honors, including two 
Joint Service Commendation Medals, four Navy Commendation Medals, and 
three Navy Achievement Medals.
  After retiring from the Navy, he earned a second bachelor's degree in 
World History. He and his wife returned to Sandusky to be near their 
children and grandchildren. He now serves as the Financial Officer for 
American Legion Post 369.
  Mr. Speaker, Senior Chief Jones' dedication to serving others extends 
well beyond his years in uniform. I am proud to name him our October 
2026 Veteran of the Month.
```
