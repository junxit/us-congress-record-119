# HONORING THE LIFE OF MR. HARRY EPPLE

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF MR. HARRY EPPLE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Mr. Harry 
Epple of Bernards Township, New Jersey, who celebrated his 100th 
birthday in September. Born on September 6, 1926, Mr. Epple 
demonstrated exceptional character and patriotism throughout his life. 
He served as an officer in the United States Navy during World War II, 
earning numerous decorations yet remaining modest, always considering 
it a privilege to serve his country.
  In 1946, Mr. Epple met his beloved wife Betty during a game of 
shuffleboard, marking the beginning of a 75-year marriage built on 
faith, joy, and kindness. Their children, Michael and Colleen; 
grandchildren Kathleen, Allison, and Kayla; and great-grandson Brandon 
continue to uphold the values instilled by Harry and Betty.
  Mr. Epple was a respected and cherished member of Bernards Township, 
where last year a parade was held in his honor. He shared his wisdom 
and passion for his country, inspiring all who knew him.
  We owe our deepest gratitude to Mr. Epple and his fellow servicemen 
and women for their profound sacrifices. As he celebrated his 
centennial birthday, we were reminded of his legacy of service, 
patriotism, and resilience. I invite my colleagues to join me in 
recognizing this extraordinary milestone and life.
```
