# COMMEMORATING THE 90TH ANNIVERSARY OF JET PROPULSION LABORATORY

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
    COMMEMORATING THE 90TH ANNIVERSARY OF JET PROPULSION LABORATORY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to commemorate the 90th anniversary of 
the establishment of the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) and recognize 
the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) for its successful 
stewardship of JPL since 1936. I include in the Record the following 
proclamation:

       Whereas, since founding the Jet Propulsion Laboratory in 
     1936 following a series of bold and pioneering experiments in 
     rocket propulsion, the California Institute of Technology has 
     stewarded the Laboratory into becoming a center of excellence 
     in robotic space exploration, providing deep and enduring 
     contributions to the Nation while enriching NASA's 
     technological and human capital;
       Whereas, under Caltech's management and scientific 
     leadership, JPL has played a central role in many of the 
     United States' most historic space missions including Voyager 
     to Europa Clipper, having designed, built, and landed every 
     successful U.S. rover on Mars, and laying the foundation for 
     American preeminence in the solar system;
       Whereas, for 90 years, Caltech and JPL have together 
     delivered an unmatched scientific rate of return, advancing 
     high-precision gravity science, groundbreaking radar imaging 
     and interferometry systems, revolutionary detectors for 
     observing Earth and other planets, and orbiting telescopes 
     that observe our universe;
       Whereas, for 90 years, Caltech and JPL have played an 
     outsized role in launching and accelerating the growth of the 
     aerospace industry; advancing microelectronics, robotics and 
     deep technological breakthroughs; and serving as a source of 
     pride and inspiration for the State of California and the 
     American people; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Congressmember Judy 
     Chu, representing California's 28th Congressional District, 
     do hereby:
       (1) Recognize and commend the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, 
     the California Institute of Technology, and all of the 
     scientists, engineers, researchers, educators, and business 
     leaders whose dedication to JPL has advanced the Nation's 
     scientific and technological leadership;
       (2) Congratulate Caltech on 90 years of leadership and 
     stewardship of the Jet Propulsion Laboratory;
       (3) Recognize the innumerable technological breakthroughs, 
     revolutionary scientific discoveries, and daring exploration 
     that have characterized JPL's first ninety years; and
       (4) Express gratitude to Caltech for its partnership with 
     NASA and its service to the United States and global 
     scientific community.

                          ____________________
```
