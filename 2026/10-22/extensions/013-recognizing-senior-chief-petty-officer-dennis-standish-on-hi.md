# RECOGNIZING SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER DENNIS STANDISH ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
     RECOGNIZING SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER DENNIS STANDISH ON HIS 
                               RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Senior Chief 
Petty Officer Dennis Standish on the occasion of his retirement from 
the United States Navy after 26 years of honorable service to our 
Nation.
  Senior Chief Standish began his military career in February 1996, 
completing recruit training at Great Lakes, Illinois, followed by 
submarine and technical training in Groton, Connecticut. He was 
subsequently assigned to USS Tucson (SSN 770), where he completed 
multiple deployments throughout the Pacific, earned his Submarine 
Warfare qualification, and participated in critical undersea 
operations.
  Following his active-duty service, Senior Chief Standish continued 
his commitment to our Nation through the Navy Reserve. Throughout his 
distinguished career, he supported Naval Special Warfare, submarine 
operations, and theater undersea warfare missions across the Pacific, 
Middle East, and Indian Ocean. His service included supporting counter-
piracy operations aboard a Turkish warship and assisting with the rapid 
restoration of communications capabilities aboard USS Porter (DDG 78) 
following an at-sea collision.
  In recognition of his leadership and dedication, he was promoted to 
Chief Petty Officer in September 2018 and Senior Chief Petty Officer in 
July 2021. His final assignment with Navy Reserve Commander, Submarine 
Force Atlantic Headquarters, in Norfolk, Virginia, marked the 
conclusion of a remarkable career dedicated to strengthening our 
Nation's naval readiness and security.
  Senior Chief Standish's 26 years of service reflect an unwavering 
commitment to duty, exceptional leadership, and devotion to the United 
States Navy. His contributions to our Nation's defense and undersea 
warfare capabilities have left a lasting impact on those he served 
alongside and the missions he supported.
  I would like to congratulate Senior Chief Petty Officer Dennis 
Standish on his well-deserved retirement and express my sincere 
gratitude for his 26 years of dedicated service to our great Nation. I 
wish him all the best as he begins this next chapter of his life.

                          ____________________
```
