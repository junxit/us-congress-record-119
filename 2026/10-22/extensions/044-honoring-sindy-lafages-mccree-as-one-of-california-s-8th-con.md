# HONORING SINDY LAFAGES-McCREE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
HONORING SINDY LAFAGES-McCREE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sindy Lafages-
McCree as one of

[[Page E1119]]

California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. A devoted 
educator and advocate, Sindy has spent more than two decades 
strengthening early childhood education throughout Solano County.
  Originally from Guadeloupe, Sindy is the founder of Les Petits 
Papillons French Family Day Care and Les Grand Papillons French 
Preschool. Drawing on her fluency in French, Creole, and English, she 
has created vibrant, multicultural learning environments that support 
young children and their families. Her commitment to early childhood 
education extends well beyond the classroom. Sindy played a leading 
role in reinstating the Solano-Napa Chapter of the California 
Association for the Education of Young Children and now serves as 
Chapter President. In this role, she works to expand professional 
development and continuing education opportunities for early childhood 
professionals throughout Solano and Napa Counties.
  Sindy has also shared her expertise at the state and national level, 
presenting at multiple California Association for the Education of 
Young Children conferences, the 2025 California Child Care Resource & 
Referral Network Conference, and the 2026 National Association for 
Family Child Care Conference. She further supports local educators as a 
consultant, providing training in childcare business administration and 
helping strengthen program quality. Through her dedication to children, 
families, and educators, Sindy continues to advance educational 
excellence across the region. On behalf of the people of California's 
8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor Sindy Lafages-
McCree for her many contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
