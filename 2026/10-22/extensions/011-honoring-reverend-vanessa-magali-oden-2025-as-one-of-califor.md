# HONORING REVEREND VANESSA MAGALI ODEN 2025 AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
 HONORING REVEREND VANESSA MAGALI ODEN 2025 AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH 
               CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Reverend Vanessa 
Magali Oden as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women 
of the Year. The first Spanish-speaking administrator at Elite Public 
Schools in Vallejo, where she has served both as Vice Principal and 
Assistant Director of Multilingual Affairs, Reverend Oden has broken 
down many barriers to education for bilingual students. To ensure that 
every voice is heard, she has engaged extensively with students, their 
families, and the surrounding communities.
  Reverend Oden has helped pave the way to academic success for many 
students through her efforts. Following the damage caused in Puerto 
Rico by Hurricane Maria, she and her ministry led water relief efforts 
for those impacted. They have assisted in many other crises, from 
providing aid to women and children in nations impacted by conflict to 
helping supplying food to displaced families on the border of Colombia 
and Venezuela. During the Pandemic, she also organized online 
counseling for women across the globe while also assisting during AIDS 
crises in Mexico, Uganda, and Rwanda.
  A champion of service, Reverend Oden has spearheaded efforts to feed 
local unhoused individuals and families while emphasizing unity through 
multilingual outreach. Her work has improved the lives of many in 
Solano County, uplifting those most vulnerable and strengthening our 
communities. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Reverend Vanessa 
Magali Oden for her extraordinary commitment to education, humanitarian 
service, and uplifting vulnerable communities both at home and around 
the world.
```
