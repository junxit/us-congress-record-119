# HONORING RYAN P. HAYGOOD FOR HIS DEDICATION TO CIVIL RIGHTS AND SOCIAL JUSTICE

**Speaking:** Mejia, Analilia (M001246) [D-NJ]

```
HONORING RYAN P. HAYGOOD FOR HIS DEDICATION TO CIVIL RIGHTS AND SOCIAL 
                                JUSTICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ANALILIA MEJIA

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Ryan P. 
Haygood for his extraordinary contributions to civil rights, racial 
justice, and his efforts to advance equality throughout New Jersey and 
across our Nation.
  As President and Chief Executive Officer of the New Jersey Institute 
for Social Justice, Ryan led efforts to address systemic inequality and 
strengthen communities throughout our state. Under his leadership, the 
Institute advanced measures to ensure all communities do more than 
survive, they thrive. Ryan tirelessly advocated for New Jersey's $15 
minimum wage, automatic voter registration, and the restoration of 
voting rights to approximately 83,000 residents on probation or parole. 
His advocacy contributed to ending prison-based gerrymandering and 
expanded opportunities for historically underserved communities.
  Ryan's leadership has transformed New Jersey. He advanced critical 
criminal justice reforms, including efforts to close New Jersey's youth 
prisons and expanded community-based alternatives to incarceration. 
Under his leadership, the New Jersey Institute for Social Justice has 
worked to address racial disparities in housing and economic 
opportunity, advocated for greater access to homeownership, and 
established the New Jersey Reparations Council in order to examine the 
lasting impacts of slavery and systemic discrimination.
  Ryan Haygood is no stranger to the fight for justice. Prior to 
leading the Institute, Ryan served as Deputy Director of Litigation at 
the NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, working on landmark civil 
rights cases defending voting rights and challenging discriminatory 
practices. Today, he continues his commitment to justice as President 
and Chief Executive Officer of the much-lauded Southern Poverty Law 
Center.
  Today I rise to celebrate Ryan Haygood's remarkable contributions to 
our state and to uplift his unwavering commitment to equality, 
opportunity, and justice. His leadership and advocacy will strengthen 
our democracy and will help build a more just and equitable future for 
all.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ryan P. 
Haygood for his extraordinary service to New Jersey and our Nation. His 
leadership and advocacy have strengthened our democracy and helped 
build a more just and equitable future for all.
```
