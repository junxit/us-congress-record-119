# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF ALLYSON AVES

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF ALLYSON AVES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ZACHARY NUNN

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding public servant and valued member of my team, Ms. Allyson 
Aves, as she concludes nearly a year and a half of dedicated service to 
Iowa's 3rd Congressional District. Allyson first joined Team Nunn as an 
intern and quickly demonstrated that no task was too big and no 
challenge too great. Through her hard work and dedication, she rose 
through the ranks from Staff Assistant to Legislative Correspondent, 
taking on new responsibilities and eventually leading the very 
internship program where her own Capitol Hill journey began.
  One of Allyson's defining moments came during a late-night Farm Bill 
markup; an experience she called her ``Super Bowl.'' While most Iowans 
were sound asleep, Allyson was on Capitol Hill until 2 a.m., watching 
the legislative process unfold and helping secure 21 provisions in a 
bill she proudly took home with a signed copy to remember. Her passion 
for Iowa agriculture extended to our fight for nationwide, year-round 
E15 and our unofficial campaign to ``Make American Butter Great 
Again.''
  Of course, Allyson made her mark on Team Nunn in other ways, too. She 
earned the nickname ``Crash'' for her adventures navigating Washington 
traffic, finding creative parking solutions, making sure I arrived at 
my next meeting on time, and even asserting her official federal status 
to deter a would-be car-thief and saving one D.C.-driven and ``gently 
used'' Jeep Grand Cherokee from a near certain grand theft auto. 
Allyson approached every drive with the same determination she brought 
to the rest of her job. Her car may have collected a few battle scars 
along the way, but Allyson never let that slow her down.
  Allyson also proved willing to tackle even the most unconventional 
assignments, including a memorable mission to secure a pie at the 
Congressional Gourmet Gala. When her plan to sneak it out in a 
colleague's purse fell through, Allyson adapted and simply carried it 
out herself--a fitting example of her resourcefulness and 
determination. But beyond the humor and memorable stories, what truly 
distinguishes Allyson is her selflessness. She's the colleague who 
stays late to finish an assignment, offers a ride home after a long 
day, and gives up her weekend to help a teammate move into a new 
apartment. No matter how demanding the day or difficult the task, 
Allyson consistently puts others first and approaches every 
responsibility with a commitment to serving her colleagues and the 
people of Iowa. That same spirit made Allyson an exceptional mentor and 
leader. When she took charge of our internship program, she welcomed 
new interns with patience, enthusiasm, and a genuine desire to help 
them succeed. Under her guidance, young people who arrived on Capitol 
Hill with little experience gained the confidence and skills to provide 
valuable contributions to our team.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Ms. Allyson 
Aves for her outstanding service to Iowa's 3rd Congressional District. 
Her work ethic, leadership, humor, and unwavering commitment to others 
have left a lasting impression on Team Nunn. While we will miss having 
her on our team, I am excited to see her carry those same qualities 
into her next role with the Senate Agriculture Committee. I have no 
doubt Allyson will continue to serve with distinction, and I wish her 
every success in this next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
