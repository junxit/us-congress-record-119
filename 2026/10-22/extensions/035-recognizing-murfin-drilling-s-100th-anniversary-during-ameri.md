# RECOGNIZING MURFIN DRILLING'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY DURING AMERICA'S SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Estes, Ron (E000298) [R-KS]

```
   RECOGNIZING MURFIN DRILLING'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY DURING AMERICA'S 
                           SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RON ESTES

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. ESTES. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Murfin Drilling on the 
occasion of its 100th anniversary. In 1926, I.W. Murfin founded the 
business that would become a powerhouse in oil and gas exploration, 
production, and drilling in Kansas. It has expanded during the last 
century, but has maintained its roots in the Murfin family, which now 
includes the fourth generation of the family working in the company.
  Murfin Drilling helps maintain Kansas' dominance in energy 
production, benefiting this critical industry for the Sunflower State 
and the region. But the Murfin family has not been content with just 
oil and gas, as the business has diversified over the years, creating a 
positive impact on the community. Thanks to the solid foundation of its 
founder and the family members who have dedicated their lives to 
fostering this successful locally owned company, Murfin Drilling is 
recognized as a leading oil and gas company in the Nation.
  While this milestone for Murfin Drilling is impressive, it is even 
more meaningful that it coincides with the 250th anniversary of the 
adoption of the Declaration of Independence. Energy production has been 
an important part of our Nation's story throughout the last century, 
thanks to Kansas businesses like Murfin. As we celebrate 250 years and 
look toward the next 250 years, I have full confidence that Murfin 
Drilling will remain a critical part of our Nation's story.
  I am grateful Murfin Drilling has called Wichita home for 100 years 
and continues to invest in the people of south-central Kansas. On 
behalf of the 4th Congressional District of Kansas, I congratulate 
them.

                          ____________________
```
