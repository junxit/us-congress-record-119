# RECOGNIZING THE SOUTH CAROLINA MILITARY VETERANS HALL OF FAME

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE SOUTH CAROLINA MILITARY VETERANS HALL OF FAME

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend the South 
Carolina Military Veterans Hall of Fame for its dedication to honoring 
the generations of South Carolinians whose courage, sacrifice, and 
service have defended our Nation and preserved our freedoms. As America 
celebrates the 250th anniversary of our independence, we are reminded 
of South Carolina's proud tradition of military service, dating back to 
the founding of our Republic. From the Patriots who fought at 
Sullivan's Island, Kings Mountain, and Cowpens to the brave men and 
women serving in uniform today, South Carolinians have answered our 
Nation's call for two and a half centuries.
  The South Carolina Military Veterans Hall of Fame serves to preserve 
this remarkable legacy by recognizing distinguished veterans and 
sharing their stories with future generations. Through its commitment 
to honoring those who have served, the Hall of Fame reminds us that the 
freedoms we enjoy are made possible by the courage and sacrifice of our 
servicemembers and their families. I commend the Hall of Fame, its 
Board of Directors, and its inductees for their steadfast dedication to 
preserving South Carolina's rich military heritage and ensuring that 
the contributions of our veterans are never forgotten.
  Mr. Speaker, the South Carolina Military Veterans Hall of Fame is a 
fitting tribute to the men and women who have served our State and 
Nation with honor and distinction. I ask that my colleagues join me in 
recognizing this outstanding organization and expressing our deepest 
gratitude to the veterans and military families whose sacrifices have 
safeguarded our freedoms.
```
