# RECOGNIZING CAPSTONE HEALTH'S 25TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Aderholt, Robert B. (A000055) [R-AL]

```
             RECOGNIZING CAPSTONE HEALTH'S 25TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. ADERHOLT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Capstone Health on the occasion of its 25th Anniversary of serving the 
people of Alabama's 4th Congressional District and rural communities in 
Alabama.
  For 25 years, Capstone Health has remained committed to providing 
quality, accessible healthcare to rural communities. Since opening its 
doors in 2001, Capstone Health has served more than 40,000 individuals 
throughout Walker and Winston counties. What began a nurse-managed 
center in an office trailer with a mission to meet the healthcare needs 
of its community, has turned into a multi-location system of care.
  Today, Capstone Health provides access to affordable, high-quality 
services including primary care, pediatrics, women's health, men's 
health, behavioral health, pharmacy, optometry, and two schoolbased 
health centers.
  Capstone Health has earned recognition from the United States 
Department of Health and Human Services for its contributions to 
community healthcare and received a New Access Point grant in 
recognition of its commitment to expanding access to quality care. 
These honors reflect their leadership team's continued dedication to 
meeting the healthcare needs of rural communities in Northwest Alabama.
  Through investments in new facilities and expanded school-based 
health services, including Hilltop Health at Carbon Hill Schools, 
Capstone Health has continued to expand its services and reach into 
communities such as Nauvoo and Carbon Hill.
  The Nauvoo Capstone Rural Health Clinic provides expanded access to 
primary and behavioral healthcare, allows room to bring on an 
additional primary care provider, offers a dedicated Telehealth suite 
for when on-site providers are not available, and includes ADA-
compliant access to pharmacy services. These services are especially 
critical in a rural area like Nauvoo, where health care options are few 
and far between. Within a 30-mile radius, this clinic may be the only 
source of consistent, community-based care.
  As Capstone Health reflects on 25 years of service, its legacy tells 
a story of an unwavering commitment to the communities it serves. From 
a single nurse-managed center to an expanding network of healthcare 
services and locations, Capstone Health continues to build on its 
mission: improving access, advancing quality care, and strengthening 
the health of the communities it serves.
  On behalf of the people of Alabama's 4th Congressional District, I 
extend my heartfelt congratulations to Capstone Health for 25 years of 
serving health patients, healthy families, and healthy communities.
  I commend the staff, leadership, especially David Jones, the 
Executive Director of Capstone Health--and all of their community 
partners for their dedication to healthier patients, stronger families, 
and healthier communities, and I wish them continued success for many 
years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Capstone 
Health for years of excellence, dedication, and unwavering commitment 
to the community.
```
