# HONORING CLIFFORD E. PINKNEY, OHIO VETERANS HALL OF FAME INDUCTEE

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

```
   HONORING CLIFFORD E. PINKNEY, OHIO VETERANS HALL OF FAME INDUCTEE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Clifford E. Pinkney, who 
will be inducted into the Ohio Veterans Hall of Fame during a ceremony 
in Dublin on November 12, 2026, as part of its 2026 class.
  Clifford E. Pinkney's life and career are a model for all who would 
devote themselves to public service. Following his time in the Army, he 
joined the Cuyahoga County Sheriffs Office, where he rose through the 
ranks to become sheriff in 2015. He earned the acclaim of his peers 
throughout the Nation for spearheading the creation of inter-agency 
task forces to combat organized crime and human trafficking. Those who 
served under him praise his honesty, trustworthiness, and integrity.
  Aside from his official duties, Mr. Pinkney devoted a large part of 
his life to mentoring youth. For more than two decades, he was a youth 
basketball coach, inspiring young people to achieve their goals through 
teamwork. He also worked as an official for USA Boxing.
  Mr. Speaker, selection for the Hall of Fame is a high honor accorded 
to no more than twenty Ohioans each year. To be considered, individuals 
must not only serve our Nation honorably in the military but also 
reflect the high value of service to others in their post-military 
careers. I am pleased to join in the accolades for Clifford Pinkney and 
his outstanding record of service as he will be inducted into the Ohio 
Veterans Hall of Fame.

                          ____________________
```
