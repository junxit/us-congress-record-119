# HONORING RONALD JEFFREY "JEFF" BAKER WITH THE NEW GUARD AWARD

**Speaking:** Brown, Shontel M. (B001313) [D-OH]

```
    HONORING RONALD JEFFREY ``JEFF'' BAKER WITH THE NEW GUARD AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ronald Jeffrey 
``Jeff'' Baker as a recipient of the New Guard Award and to honor his 
service to our Nation and his continued commitment to mentoring and 
supporting young people in our community.
  Mr. Baker is a United States Army veteran who has carried the values 
of service, leadership, and responsibility into his civilian life. 
Through his work with young people, he has demonstrated that service to 
others can continue long after military service has concluded. As a 
coach and mentor, Mr. Baker has dedicated his time to encouraging young 
men to work hard, believe in themselves, and strive to reach their full 
potential. Through intramural sports and basketball, he has developed 
positive relationships with the young people he mentors and has become 
a consistent and supportive presence in their lives.
  Mr. Baker's commitment extends well beyond his responsibilities as a 
coach. He personally helps ensure that the young men he mentors are 
able to participate by picking them up and transporting them to 
basketball practices and other activities. His willingness to invest 
his own time and effort demonstrates the personal commitment he has 
made to their success. For some of these young men, Mr. Baker has also 
become an important male role model. His mentorship provides 
encouragement, guidance, and stability to young people who may not 
otherwise have that support readily available. By showing up 
consistently and leading by example, he helps teach lessons of 
discipline, responsibility, teamwork, and perseverance that extend far 
beyond the basketball court.
  The New Guard Award recognizes veterans who continue their legacy of 
service by investing in the next generation. It honors those who use 
mentorship, education, coaching, advocacy, and community service to 
help young people develop confidence, leadership, discipline, and 
commitment to their communities.
  Mr. Baker embodies the purpose of this award through his dedication 
to the young people he serves. His example reminds us that mentorship 
can have a lasting impact and that sometimes the most meaningful form 
of leadership is simply being there, consistently, for someone who 
needs guidance and encouragement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Ronald 
Jeffrey Baker on receiving the New Guard Award and in thanking him for 
his service to our Nation and his continued investment in the next 
generation of leaders in Northeast Ohio.
```
