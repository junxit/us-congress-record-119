# RECOGNIZING DANICA ALLMER

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                       RECOGNIZING DANICA ALLMER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Danica Allmer, of Hill City, South Dakota, for her eight years of 
dedication and service to my congressional office, and her more than 17 
total years of service to the State of South Dakota. Danica served as 
my Constituent Services Representative and Special Projects 
Coordinator.
  As a senior case worker, Danica helped more than 900 South Dakotans 
navigate challenges with federal agencies.
  She organized my Level Up Youth Conferences to inspire the next 
generation of engaged citizens. These conferences brought high school 
students across the state together for important lessons on civic 
responsibility and leadership.
  She also led the Congressional App Challenge program, providing 
recognition for students in technology, and the Angels in Adoption 
program, which recognizes incredible South Dakota families who have 
fostered or adopted children.
  I would also like to recognize her husband, Derek, and children, 
Dakota and Drew.
  I am grateful for Danica's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
