# RECOGNIZING COURTNEY HEITKAMP

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                     RECOGNIZING COURTNEY HEITKAMP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Courtney Heitkamp, of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, for her eight 
years of dedication and service to the State of South Dakota. Courtney 
served as the Deputy Chief of Staff and State Director, leading my in-
state team and serving as a trusted advisor.
  Her leadership developed and maintained exceptional team stability in 
the South Dakota district offices. She became a mentor to many staff 
members and provided a strategic vision for the state operation, 
including exceptional constituent services and engagement programs.
  Courtney's strategic thinking was critical in establishing my offices 
procedures, operations, and programs. She established the bipartisan, 
bicameral Israel casework coordination channel, which grew to a 250-
particant limit.
  She executed major district events including trade and tariff events, 
town halls, district work period schedules, and policy roundtables 
which provided immense value to South Dakotans.
  She was an effective bridge between the South Dakota and Washington, 
D.C. offices and encouraged improved collaboration and coordination 
across the team. In addition to leading a strong team, Courtney's 
successfully balanced increasingly complex communication and management 
duties.
  I am grateful for Courtney's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.
```
