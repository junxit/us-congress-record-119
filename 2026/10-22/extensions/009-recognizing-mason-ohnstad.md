# RECOGNIZING MASON OHNSTAD

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MASON OHNSTAD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Mason Ohnstad, of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, for more than three 
years of dedication and service to the people of South Dakota. Mason 
began as an intern in my office in 2023. He went on to serve as 
Legislative Correspondent and Legislative Assistant, and he now serves 
as my Legislative Director.
  My policy team has relied heavily on Mason's work and judgment. He 
staffed my work on the House Committee on Agriculture, which is vitally 
important to South Dakota. In that role, he met with farmers, ranchers, 
and constituents across the state as he worked diligently to find 
solutions to their problems. In the 119th Congress, I have served as 
Chairman of the Subcommittee on Commodity Markets, Digital Assets, and 
Rural Development. Mason quickly became the go-to staffer among his 
peers on digital assets and cryptocurrency policy. My success as 
Chairman was in large part thanks to Mason's hard work and skill in 
navigating a complex legislative landscape. When the House Committee on 
Agriculture passed the Farm Bill in March 2026, Mason secured 11 of my 
bills in its text, along with a dozen more of my policy priorities.
  Mason's work extended beyond agriculture. His advocacy to the United 
States Postal Service helped stop a Processing and Distribution Center 
from being moved out of South Dakota. He organized a roundtable where 
constituents could share their concerns about mail service, and he 
persistently followed up with the Postal Service to hold it 
accountable. When Mason joined my office as Legislative Correspondent, 
there was a backlog of over 100 letters which he diligently worked 
through in a month.
  I am grateful for Mason's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for his commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
