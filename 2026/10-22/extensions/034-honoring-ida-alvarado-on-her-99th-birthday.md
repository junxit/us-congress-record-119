# HONORING IDA ALVARADO ON HER 99TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Garcia, Sylvia R. (G000587) [D-TX]

```
               HONORING IDA ALVARADO ON HER 99TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SYLVIA R. GARCIA

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. GARCIA of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ida Alvarado, 
a beloved member of Houston's East End community, as she celebrated her 
99th birthday on October 19, 2026.
  For nearly a century, Ida has shown us what it means to work hard, 
care for your family, and give back to your community. She and her late 
husband, Frank, raised five children and taught them the importance of 
faith and helping others.
  Ida has never been one to sit on the sidelines. She and Frank never 
missed an election, and they passed that sense of civic duty on to 
their children. From working early voting for Harris County to 
volunteering for her daughter Carol's campaigns, Ida has spent decades 
helping her neighbors make their voices heard.
  And Ida knows how to enjoy life, too. Whether catching up with her 
compadres at Mason Park or cheering on her Houston Astros, she has 
always made time for the people and things she loves.
  Ida is the kind of person who makes the East End so special. She has 
spent her life looking out for her family and her neighbors, and that 
is something worth celebrating. I join her loved ones in wishing her a 
very happy 99th birthday and thanking her for the examples she has set 
for generations.

                          ____________________
```
