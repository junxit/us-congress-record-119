# CELEBRATING THE FIRST ANNIVERSARY OF THE AD ASTRA ADVOCACY CENTER

**Speaking:** Davids, Sharice (D000629) [D-KS]

```
   CELEBRATING THE FIRST ANNIVERSARY OF THE AD ASTRA ADVOCACY CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the 
Ad Astra Advocacy Center on its one-year anniversary. Since opening in 
2025, Ad Astra Advocacy has provided coordinated care and assistance 
for children and adults with disabilities who have experienced abuse or 
neglect. I am honored to recognize this critical work in the district. 
Franklin and Miami County, Kansas, now have a resource to ensure no 
child will face abuse alone.
  Over the past year, dedicated professionals provided a safe and 
supportive environment for over 80 children, their families, and other 
survivors of trauma. Ad Astra Advocacy works diligently to bring local 
stakeholders, law enforcement, advocates, and mental health 
professionals to not only children who have experienced abuse but their 
families as well. Through coordinated care, these families are 
supported through every step of the healing journey. Their dedication 
to the safety of our community is most admirable.
  Led by Executive Director Skylor Yeager, Ad Astra Advocacy is working 
to fill in the gaps for rural families seeking critical care during a 
vulnerable time. That is why I nominated the Ad Astra Advocacy Center 
for the 2026 Angels in Adoption Honoree award. I am proud to represent 
such an important organization, and I am eager to see what they do 
next. I applaud this important milestone and wish Skylor and her team 
many more years of success.
  As we celebrate the first anniversary of the Ad Astra Advocacy 
Center, please join me in recognizing and honoring the remarkable 
achievements of this organization. Together, we can build a safer and 
brighter future for the children of Kansas' 3rd District.

                          ____________________
```
