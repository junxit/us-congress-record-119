# HONORING MARIA DEL ROCIO MARTINEZ LUNA AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING MARIA DEL ROCIO MARTINEZ LUNA AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH 
               CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Maria del Rocio 
Martinez Luna as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women 
of the Year. Maria is a dedicated community leader and longtime 
advocate for Richmond's 23rd Street corridor whose life reflects 
resilience, perseverance, and an unwavering commitment to service.
  Born in Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico, Maria immigrated to the United 
States with her mother and sister and was raised in a single-parent 
household. She witnessed her mother work tirelessly to support their 
family while navigating the uncertainty of their immigration status. 
Despite these challenges, Maria remained committed to her education 
and, in 2009, became the first person in her family to graduate from 
high school. For the past 13 years, Maria has worked at Rigo's Auto 
Sales, where her dedication and leadership helped her become Secretary 
of the 23rd Street Merchants Association. In this role, she works 
closely with merchants, residents, community organizations, and public 
agencies to strengthen one of Richmond's most important cultural and 
commercial corridors. Maria helps organize major community events, 
including Richmond's annual Cinco de Mayo Festival, she has also 
assisted with immigration forums that provide residents with reliable 
information about their rights and available protections, drawing on 
her own experience to help immigrant families feel informed and 
supported. Her commitment extends to public safety. Maria works with 
local merchants, residents, the Richmond Police Department, and City 
partners to address traffic, speeding, pedestrian safety, parking, and 
other neighborhood concerns. Much of Maria's work takes place behind 
the scenes and her leadership strengthens businesses, supports 
immigrant families, and brings people together. On behalf of the people 
of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor 
Maria del Rocio Martinez Luna for her outstanding contributions to our 
community.
```
