# RECOGNIZING KRISTEN BLAKELY

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                      RECOGNIZING KRISTEN BLAKELY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
thank Kristen Blakely of Royal, Illinois, for her five years of 
dedication and service to the people of South Dakota. Kristen joined my 
office in October 2021 as Legislative Correspondent and Press 
Assistant. She was later promoted to Press Secretary and now serves as 
my Communications Director.
  Kristen has driven my office's communications, often with a lean 
team. During the 2023 debt limit negotiations, she single-handedly 
handled press inquiries, including an in-studio Sunday morning show 
appearance. That year she also relaunched my weekly column as ``The Big 
Three'' and rebuilt the office's mass communications plan.
  In 2024, Kristen placed my op-ed in The Washington Post and 
strengthened relationships with South Dakota media outlets. She did 
this while running the communications shop alone through an intense 
stretch that included my spearheading digital assets legislation and 
committee passage of the Farm Bill.
  In 2025, she secured national coverage of my America Works Act and my 
Military Service Academy legislation. One social media thread she 
produced on the One Big Beautiful Bill Act drew 3.4 million views. 
During the 43-day government shutdown, she booked 41 interviews, 26 of 
them in the first week.
  I also want to recognize Kristen's family including her husband Kyser 
and son Royal.
  I am grateful for Kristen's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
