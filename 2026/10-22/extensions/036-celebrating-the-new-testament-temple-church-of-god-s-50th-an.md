# CELEBRATING THE NEW TESTAMENT TEMPLE CHURCH OF GOD'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
 CELEBRATING THE NEW TESTAMENT TEMPLE CHURCH OF GOD'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 50 
years of faith, dedication, and community of the New Testament Temple 
Church of God, a place of worship which has made a lasting impact on 
the Bronx.
  Established in 1976 by Reverend Albert Edwards and his family, the 
church began with a small group of Jamaican immigrants who gathered for 
worship in a Bronx home. From those modest beginnings, New Testament 
Temple has grown into a thriving congregation whose commitment to 
serving others has remained at the heart of its mission.
  Under Bishop Paul Peart, the congregation has grown from 
approximately 35 members to reaching more than 1,000 individuals, even 
including those who participate in worship online. Throughout this 
growth, the church has remained dedicated to strengthening its 
community and ensuring that those facing hardship are not forgotten.
  In 2002, Bishop Peart founded Good Neighbors Community Outreach 
Agency, extending the church's work beyond its congregation. Through 
educational opportunities, food distribution, healthcare referrals, 
immigration assistance, and programs supporting young people, the 
organization has helped countless Bronx residents' access essential 
resources and support.
  During the COVID-19 pandemic, the church expanded its food pantry 
operations from serving 200 families weekly to more than 2,000. At a 
time when many families struggled so much just to meet their basic 
needs, New Testament Temple provided a source of stability and relief. 
The church's dedication has continued ever since, including welcoming 
asylum seekers and helping to provide them with meals, clothing, and 
other necessities.
  The history of New Testament Temple Church of God is a testament to 
what can be accomplished when a community comes together with a shared 
purpose. For half a century, its members have demonstrated that the 
strength of a congregation lies not simply in its size, but in the 
lives it touches and the neighbors it serves.
  I congratulate Bishop Paul Peart, the Edwards family, and the entire 
New Testament Temple Church of God congregation on this historic 
milestone. Their 50 years of dedication have left an indelible mark on 
the Bronx, and I look forward to seeing their continued contributions 
for generations to come.
```
