# HONORING DR. MONIQUE SIMS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
  HONORING DR. MONIQUE SIMS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Monique Sims as 
one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year for 
her outstanding leadership in public health, maternal care, and 
community service. For more than 25 years, Dr. Sims has worked to 
improve the health of mothers, infants, and families, particularly in 
underserved communities.
  She led the Solano County African American Community Baby Shower for 
21 years, helping address disparities in maternal and infant health. 
Through her work in lactation education, she has trained nurses, 
doulas, health educators, and community members, while mentoring 
aspiring dietitians and lactation consultants. Dr. Sims has served as 
WIC manager, a California State WIC lactation trainer, and a long-term 
member of the Solano County Black Infant Health Advisory Board. She has 
also collaborated with Stanford Medical Center on community discussions 
about health disparities and helped develop breastfeeding education for 
Touro University students.
  Beyond healthcare, Dr. Sims collaborates with Revival Center to 
provide essential supplies and support families. She founded the Melvin 
Thomson Center Walking Club in North Vallejo and offers free line 
dancing classes that bring neighbors together through movement and 
fellowship. Recognized for her advocacy and service, Dr. Sims continues 
to inspire others through her mentorship, compassion, and commitment to 
equitable care. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, I am proud to honor Dr. Monique Sims for her 
extraordinary contributions in our community.

                          ____________________
```
