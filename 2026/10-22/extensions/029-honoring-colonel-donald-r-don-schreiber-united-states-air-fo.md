# HONORING COLONEL DONALD R. "DON" SCHREIBER, UNITED STATES AIR FORCE, RETIRED, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
HONORING COLONEL DONALD R. ``DON'' SCHREIBER, UNITED STATES AIR FORCE, 
           RETIRED, WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Colonel Donald 
``Don'' R. Schreiber of Waco, Texas, with a 2026 Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. Don's distinguished career in the United States Air 
Force, years of service to fellow veterans and the Waco community, and 
lifelong commitment to our Nation exemplify the character, leadership, 
and dedication worthy of this recognition.
  Born in Seattle, Washington, and raised on Whidbey Island by a 
devoted mother after the early loss of Don's father, Don attended 
Washington State University and joined Air Force ROTC. Encouraged by a 
trainer who noted that Don had the best eyesight of all the applicants, 
Don pursued a pilot slot and excelled in every screening. Don was 
commissioned in 1964 and completed pilot training at Vance Air Force 
Base in Oklahoma, beginning a distinguished 28-year career that 
culminated in Don's attaining the rank of Colonel.
  Don's military service included some of the most demanding 
assignments of his generation. After flying the RF-4C reconnaissance 
jet across Europe from RAF Alconbury in England, Don volunteered for 
Vietnam. From 1967 to 1968, Don flew 100 combat missions over North 
Vietnam from Udorn, Thailand, as part of one of the youngest aircrews 
in Southeast Asia. On November 19, 1967, Don flew a low-level 
reconnaissance mission over Hanoi through heavy surface-to-air missile 
fire, earning the Silver Star. Don later flew the KC-135 tanker and the 
U-2 reconnaissance aircraft, supporting intelligence missions around 
the world during the Cold War. Throughout his career, Don held 
significant leadership positions, including commanding the U-2 
detachment at Osan Air Base in Korea, serving as Deputy Director of the 
Strategic Air Command Reconnaissance Center, commanding the U-2, SR-71, 
and T-38 training squadron at Beale Air Force Base in California, and 
serving as Vice Wing Commander and then Wing Commander at Beale.
  Don continued to distinguish through advanced military education and 
professional achievement. Don attended the Air Force Command and Staff 
College and earned a master's degree from Troy State University, later 
completing Air War College. Don also served as a staff officer with the 
Air Force Technical Applications Center, focusing on airborne nuclear 
detection systems. Don's extraordinary military service was recognized 
with numerous awards and decorations, including the Legion of Merit, 
Silver Star, Distinguished Flying Cross with three Oak Leaf Clusters, 
Air Medal with sixteen Oak Leaf Clusters, and other unit and service 
commendations. In 1989, Don became the commander of Air Force ROTC at 
Baylor University and retired from the Air Force in 1992 after 28 years 
of service.
  Don's commitment to service did not end with military retirement. Don 
went on to a second career as a professor in the Marketing and 
Management Department of Baylor University's business school, teaching 
for 26 years before retiring in 2016. In this role, Don found 
fulfillment in preparing generations of young Americans for 
professional success, continuing the leadership and service that had 
defined Don's military career.
  Don's family has been central to this life of service. Married since 
December 1968, Don and his wife raised three daughters, Laura, Kirsten, 
and Erin, through many moves and long deployments. All three daughters 
graduated from Baylor University, and together Don and his wife are now 
proud grandparents of thirteen grandchildren. Don's family exemplifies 
the sacrifice and strength of the military families who stand behind 
every servicemember.
  Don has also devoted more than three decades to the local community 
and fellow veterans. Since arriving in Waco in 1989, Don has served as 
a past President of the Military Officers Association of America, a 
past Flight Captain of the Daedalian Society, and a past President of 
the Baylor Bear Foundation. Don helped organize the third Red River 
Rats convention and serves as a member and usher of First United 
Methodist Church. Don's commitment to service has been reflected in 
countless volunteer roles that have strengthened Central Texas.
  Don's life is ultimately defined not simply by the honors received, 
but by the way he has continued to lead, mentor, and serve others. From 
distinguished service as an Air Force aviator and combat veteran to 
years of teaching, volunteering, and supporting fellow veterans, Don 
has demonstrated an unwavering sense of duty and devotion to country.
  It is this extraordinary record of military service, leadership, 
teaching, volunteerism, and continued commitment to fellow Americans 
that I proudly commend Colonel Donald R. ``Don'' Schreiber of Waco, 
Texas to friends, family, community, and country. It is my honor to 
recognize Don with the 2026 Congressional Veteran Commendation from the 
17th Congressional District of Texas.
```
