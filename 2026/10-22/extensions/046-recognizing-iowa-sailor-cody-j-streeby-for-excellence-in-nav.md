# RECOGNIZING IOWA SAILOR CODY J. STREEBY FOR EXCELLENCE IN NAVY RECRUITING

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
    RECOGNIZING IOWA SAILOR CODY J. STREEBY FOR EXCELLENCE IN NAVY 
                               RECRUITING

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ZACHARY NUNN

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Construction 
Electrician 2nd Class Cody J. Streeby of Eldon, Iowa, for being named 
Navy Recruiting Command's Junior Recruiter of the Quarter for the third 
quarter of Fiscal Year 2026, and for his leadership in preparing the 
next generation of American Sailors.
  As a 20-year Air Force veteran, I know our military is only as strong 
as the men and women willing to answer the call to serve. Cody comes 
from a family that has answered it for generations. His grandparents, 
his father, his uncles and aunt, his older brother, and his brother-in-
law all served in uniform. Cody carries that tradition forward by 
helping other young Americans find their own path to serving our 
Nation. Before he became a recruiter, Cody spent nine and a half years 
on active duty in the Navy. He deployed aboard the guided-missile 
cruiser USS Chancellorsville and served at United States Fleet Forces 
Command. His path to recruiting began after a severe vehicle accident 
during transfer leave placed him on a humanitarian assignment. As he 
recovered, Cody chose recruiting duty and turned a personal setback 
into a new mission: bringing future Sailors into the Navy.
  Today, Cody leads Navy Recruiting Station Southridge in Des Moines as 
its recruiter-in-charge, and over the past two and a half years his 
leadership has brought the station top regional honors. Under his 
direction, NRS Southridge earned three Medium Station of the Month 
honors, Medium Station of the Quarter, and Fiscal Year 2026 Medium 
Station of the Year. Cody was named Fiscal Year 2026 Recruiter-in-
Charge of the Year and has twice been named Junior Sailor of the 
Quarter. This quarter, that record earned him national recognition from 
Navy Recruiting Command.
  Every young American Cody helps enlist is one more Sailor ready to 
defend our freedom

[[Page E1120]]

and strengthen our national security. Beyond his recruiting duties, he 
gives back by coordinating and taking part in local blood drives and by 
volunteering as a judge at high school Junior Reserve Officers' 
Training Corps drill meets.
  Mr. Speaker, our Navy needs leaders who put service before self and 
inspire others to do the same. Cody is one of those leaders, and Iowa 
is proud to call him one of our own. I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating Construction Electrician 2nd Class Cody J. Streeby on 
being named Navy Recruiting Command's Junior Recruiter of the Quarter, 
and in thanking him for his dedicated service to our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
