# RECOGNIZING MYRA PAIGE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 24TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Carbajal, Salud O. (C001112) [D-CA]

```
   RECOGNIZING MYRA PAIGE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 24TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SALUD O. CARBAJAL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. CARBAJAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Myra Paige as 
one of California's 24th Congressional District's Women of the Year. 
Each year, I have the honor of recognizing women on the Central Coast 
whose leadership, service, and dedication have left a lasting impact on 
our community through the Women of the Year Award. This year, it is my 
pleasure to recognize Myra Paige for her advocacy, public service, and 
commitment to defending Democratic values across the Central Coast and 
nationwide.
  Myra has devoted ten years of service to Indivisible Santa Barbara 
(ISB), a local non-profit committed to defending our democracy against 
further democratic backsliding and dismantling the influence of 
oligarchs in our community. She currently serves as the President of 
ISB and co-leads Commit to Democracy Santa Barbara (CTD), one of the 
most highly regarded postcard-writing campaigns in our nation. Under 
Myra's leadership, 500 volunteers send up to 10,000 postcards monthly 
to support critical Congressional and Senatorial campaigns nationwide.
  Myra joined Indivisible Santa Barbara after a profoundly moving 
event: the 2016 election night. As the daughter of a Holocaust 
survivor, she felt a deep-seated recognition that history can repeat 
itself. Instead of surrendering to despair and despondency, Myra 
converted that fear into concrete action. Through ISB, Myra mobilized 
the community against authoritarianism by spearheading a pivotal town 
hall with Congressman Carbajal, California State Senator Pro Tem 
Monique Limon, and Assemblymember Gregg Hart, organized the ``No 
Kings'' rallies, and led weekly demonstrations focused on protecting 
one another and fortifying our local safety net.
  To scale this impact, Myra built lasting local coalitions, notably 
``The Summit,'' uniting the Democratic Women of Santa Barbara County, 
the Fund for Santa Barbara, Planned Parenthood Central Coast, and 
Commit to Democracy SB to align political strategy and amplify 
collective action. Myra also strengthened partnerships with 805 
UndocuFund and the Immigrant Legal Defense Center (ILDC), fortifying 
defenses for the local immigrant community against persistent attacks. 
Among her most notable achievements, Myra successfully advocated 
against ICE activity on the Santa Barbara City College (SBCC) campus. 
Her direct engagement with the SBCC Board of Trustees led to a 
unanimous vote in January 2026 prohibiting ICE from entering campus 
grounds.
  Beyond her leadership with ISB and CTD, Myra serves as a Board Member 
of Planned Parenthood Central Coast and was previously honored as the 
Democratic Woman of the Year by the Democratic Women of Santa Barbara 
County. I am proud to recognize Myra Paige for her extraordinary record 
of leadership, advocacy, and service advancing Democratic change in our 
Central Coast community and beyond.
```
