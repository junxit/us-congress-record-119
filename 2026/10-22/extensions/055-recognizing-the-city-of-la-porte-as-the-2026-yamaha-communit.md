# RECOGNIZING THE CITY OF LA PORTE AS THE 2026 YAMAHA COMMUNITY OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Mrvan, Frank J. (M001214) [D-IN]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE CITY OF LA PORTE AS THE 2026 YAMAHA COMMUNITY OF THE 
                                  YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. FRANK J. MRVAN

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. MRVAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the City of La 
Porte, Indiana, on being named the 2026 Yamaha Community of the Year by 
the Indiana Chamber of Commerce.
  Each year, the Indiana Chamber of Commerce recognizes an outstanding 
community for its efforts to enhance the quality of life for residents 
while creating opportunities for the success of its local businesses. 
This year, the Chamber highlighted La Porte's efforts toward that goal, 
under the leadership of Mayor Tom Dermody and local government leaders, 
in collaboration with its many business, educational, and nonprofit 
partners.
  La Porte is demonstrating what can be accomplished when government, 
businesses, community organizations, and residents share a vision and 
collectively act to turn a vision into reality. This honor is 
especially meaningful as it recognizes more than economic growth or new 
development--it recognizes a community working together to set a 
foundation for a stronger future for its residents, to create 
opportunities for local businesses, and to preserve the quality of life 
that makes La Porte a place to call home.
  We see this commitment in the investments being made in the local 
economy, the development of new housing, the revitalization of downtown 
and surrounding neighborhoods, and the continued investments in parks 
and public spaces that bring people together. These efforts are 
important because economic development is about more than attracting 
investments, it is also about creating good-paying jobs and 
opportunities for working families, supporting small business, and 
ensuring the next generation has opportunities to build their future 
here in Northwest Indiana.
  I congratulate Mayor Dermody, his administration, the La Porte City 
Council, and all economic development partners, business leaders, 
community organizations, and everyone whose dedicated efforts have led 
to this honor and contribute to La Porte's success. La Porte's 
recognition is a testament to what can be achieved when a community 
embraces its potential.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you and all my distinguished colleagues join 
me in commending the City of La Porte on its well-deserved recognition 
as the Indiana Chamber of Commerce's 2026 Yamaha Community of the Year.
```
