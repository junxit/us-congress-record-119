# RECOGNIZING THE DRAGON DREAM TEAM AS OHIO'S 13TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE DRAGON DREAM TEAM AS OHIO'S 13TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                    DISTRICT'S CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Dragon Dream 
Team, Ohio's first and only breast cancer survivor dragon boat team, as 
Ohio's 13th Congressional District's Champion of the Week.
  Based in Akron and founded in 2007, the Dragon Dream Team was created 
to show that an active, fulfilling life is possible after a breast 
cancer diagnosis. Through teamwork, perseverance, and a shared 
commitment to supporting one another, these athletes have built a 
community that extends far beyond the water.
  Recently, the team represented Northeast Ohio on the international 
stage at the 2026 International Breast Cancer Paddlers' Commission 
Participatory Dragon Boat Festival in France. Competing alongside 
approximately 4,500 breast cancer survivor paddlers from 25 countries, 
the team finished second overall in the world in the 1,000-meter race, 
and first place among United States teams.
  Beyond their athletic achievements, these remarkable women support 
breast cancer survivors throughout Northeast Ohio through community 
outreach, education, and service.
  Their accomplishments demonstrate the power of resilience, 
camaraderie, and the human spirit. The team is a shining example of why 
Ohio's 13th Congressional District is known as the ``Birthplace of 
Champions.'' I am proud to recognize the Dragon Dream Team for 
representing our community with distinction and inspiring survivors 
everywhere.

                          ____________________
```
