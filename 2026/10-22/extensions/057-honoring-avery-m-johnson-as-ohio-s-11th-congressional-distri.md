# HONORING AVERY M. JOHNSON AS OHIO'S 11TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S VETERAN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Brown, Shontel M. (B001313) [D-OH]

```
   HONORING AVERY M. JOHNSON AS OHIO'S 11TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S 
                          VETERAN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Avery M. Johnson as 
Ohio's 11th Congressional District's Veteran of the Year and to honor 
his distinguished record of service to our Nation, our veterans, and 
the communities of Northeast Ohio.
  Mr. Johnson served honorably in the United States Army from 1982 to 
1992, including during the Persian Gulf War, before continuing his 
service in the Ohio Army National Guard from 1993 to 2005. As a Platoon 
Sergeant, he served in positions of significant responsibility, 
including during the response to the September 11, 2001, terrorist 
attacks and during a deployment in support of the Iraq War from 2003 to 
2004.
  During his deployment to Iraq, Mr. Johnson served as a Platoon 
Sergeant and Police Station Commander, overseeing critical 
responsibilities that included VIP escort missions, force protection, 
and the training of Iraqi police forces. His leadership, sacrifice, and 
distinguished military service earned him the Bronze Star Medal for 
combat operations in Iraq and the Ohio Distinguished Service Medal.
  Mr. Johnson's commitment to service did not end when he took off the 
uniform. He brought the same dedication, leadership, and sense of 
responsibility that defined his military career back home to Northeast 
Ohio.
  Throughout his civilian life, Mr. Johnson has worked to strengthen 
his community through housing advocacy, public health education, 
neighborhood improvement, and support for fellow veterans. He helped 
lead residents of East 102nd Street in Garfield Heights in advocating 
for major infrastructure improvements to address years of neighborhood 
flooding. He has also continued to serve as an advocate for veterans, 
helping fellow servicemembers access the benefits they earned and 
remaining active in the American Legion.
  His commitment to public service also led him to the Garfield Heights 
City Council. Mr. Johnson was appointed to Council in 2021 and 
subsequently elected by the residents he serves, in 2023, his 
colleagues selected him to serve as President of City Council, making 
him the first African American to hold that position since Garfield 
Heights was founded in 1919. He continues to serve the residents of 
Ward 4 and the broader Garfield Heights community.
  The Ohio 11th Congressional District Veteran of the Year Award 
recognizes a veteran whose service did not end with military service 
but instead evolved into a continued commitment to others. Mr. Johnson 
embodies that principle. He has served our country in uniform and has 
continued to serve here at home by mentoring, advocating, leading, and 
strengthening his community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Avery 
M. Johnson on being named the Ohio 11th Congressional District Veteran 
of the Year and in thanking him for a lifetime of service to our Nation 
and the people of Northeast Ohio.
```
