# RECOGNIZING HUDSON TENN FOR HIS BRAVERY IN SAVING HIS GRANDMOTHER'S LIFE

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
  RECOGNIZING HUDSON TENN FOR HIS BRAVERY IN SAVING HIS GRANDMOTHER'S 
                                  LIFE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Hudson Tenn, of 
St. Clair County, Michigan, for his extraordinary courage and quick 
thinking in helping his grandmother during a medical emergency. At just 
five years old, Hudson demonstrated remarkable bravery when faced with 
a frightening and unexpected situation.
  When Hudson's grandmother suddenly collapsed and lost consciousness, 
he knew he needed to find help. Despite his young age, Hudson took 
action, bringing his three-year-old sister along as he ran down the 
road to find somebody who could help. His quick thinking led neighbors 
back to his grandmother, allowing them to contact emergency responders 
and get her the medical attention she needed.
  Hudson's actions exemplify the values of courage, compassion, and 
responsibility. Even in a moment of uncertainty, he remained determined 
to help his grandmother while looking out for his younger sister. His 
remarkable response served as a powerful reminder that heroism is not 
defined by age, but by the willingness to act when someone is in need.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Hudson 
Tenn for his bravery, quick thinking, and compassion.
```
