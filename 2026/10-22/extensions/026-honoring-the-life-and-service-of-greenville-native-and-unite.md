# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF GREENVILLE NATIVE AND UNITED STATES MARINE AUSTIN "ZADE" ABDELNABI

**Speaking:** Timmons, William R. (T000480) [R-SC]

```
 HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF GREENVILLE NATIVE AND UNITED STATES 
                    MARINE AUSTIN ``ZADE'' ABDELNABI

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. WILLIAM R. TIMMONS IV

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. TIMMONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and service 
of Austin ``Zade'' Abdelnabi, a United States Marine and Las Vegas 
Metropolitan Police Officer whose life was tragically taken in the line 
of duty on August 4, 2026.
  Zade was a proud son of Upstate South Carolina, raised in the Greer 
and Greenville area and a graduate of Blue Ridge High School. Before 
answering the call to serve our Nation as a United States Marine, he 
spent his early years in the community he called home. His commitment 
to service would continue beyond his military career, leading him to 
join the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department in 2023, where he 
dedicated himself to protecting and serving the community.
  Although Zade served the people of Nevada, South Carolina was always 
home. His roots remained firmly planted in the Upstate, where much of 
his family still resides. The community that helped shape the man he 
became continued to be an important part of his story, and his loss has 
been deeply felt in both South Carolina and Nevada.
  Zade embodied the values of honor, courage, integrity, and sacrifice. 
Through his service in the United States Marine Corps and his work in 
law enforcement, he demonstrated a commitment to something greater than 
himself. He answered the call to serve his country as a Marine and 
continued that calling as a police officer, placing himself in the 
service of others until his final watch.
  On August 4, 2026, Zade was killed in the line of duty while 
protecting the community he faithfully served. His loss is deeply felt 
by his fellow officers, those who served alongside him in the Marine 
Corps, his loved ones, and the Greenville community he called home. The 
outpouring of love and support from across South Carolina and beyond 
stands as a testament to the impact of his life and service.
  Zade is survived by his wife, Sage Abdelnabi; his father, Ghaleb 
Abdelnabi; his mother, Amy Harvey; his stepmother, Sana Masoud; his 
brother, Daniel (Ryan) Abdelnabi; and his sister, Janae Koury. His 
family now carries the weight of an unimaginable loss. Behind the 
uniform was a husband, son, brother, and friend whose life meant far 
more than the titles he held or the duties he performed.
  Zade will be remembered not only for his service to our Nation and 
his sacrifice in protecting the public, but for the life he lived and 
the people he loved. His dedication to duty, devotion to his family, 
and willingness to serve others leaves behind a legacy worthy of 
recognition.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that Austin ``Zade'' Abdelnabi and his 
contributions to our Nation and the communities he served be entered 
into the Record for posterity.
```
