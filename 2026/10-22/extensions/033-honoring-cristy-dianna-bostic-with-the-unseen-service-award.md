# HONORING CRISTY DIANNA BOSTIC WITH THE UNSEEN SERVICE AWARD

**Speaking:** Brown, Shontel M. (B001313) [D-OH]

```
      HONORING CRISTY DIANNA BOSTIC WITH THE UNSEEN SERVICE AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Cristy Dianna Bostic as 
a recipient of the Unseen Service Award and to honor her extraordinary 
dedication as a military and veteran caregiver, devoted spouse, and 
steadfast supporter of veterans throughout Northeast Ohio.
  The Unseen Service Award recognizes the spouses, caregivers, Gold 
Star families, and military families whose sacrifices are too often 
made outside of public view. These individuals remind us that service 
to our Nation is not carried by those in uniform alone. Behind our 
servicemembers and veterans are families who provide care, stability, 
encouragement, and strength through some of life's most difficult 
circumstances. Mrs. Bostic exemplifies that spirit of service.
  Cristy and her husband, United States Army veteran Larry Robert 
Bostic, Jr., have been married since 1998. Throughout their marriage, 
she supported her husband through the demands of his career, frequent 
moves, extensive travel, and significant health challenges, while also 
caring for their family.
  Their lives changed dramatically in 2015 when Mr. Bostic suffered a 
spinal cord injury that resulted in quadriplegia. During months of 
hospitalization, rehabilitation, and the difficult adjustment that 
followed, Mrs. Bostic remained steadfastly by his side.
  As her husband adapted to life with a significant disability, Mrs. 
Bostic assumed the responsibilities of a long-term caregiver. She has 
assisted with personal care, meals, transportation, household 
responsibilities, medication management, appointments, and the many 
activities of daily living that her husband can no longer independently 
perform. Just as importantly, she has provided the encouragement and 
emotional support necessary for him to continue living an active and 
purposeful life.
  Her support has enabled Mr. Bostic to participate in adaptive sports, 
mentor fellow veterans, advocate for individuals with spinal cord 
injuries, and serve in leadership roles within the Paralyzed Veterans 
of America. Behind those accomplishments has been a caregiver willing 
to drive countless miles, spend hours at meetings and events, and 
provide the daily support necessary to make that continued service 
possible.
  Yet Mrs. Bostic's compassion has never been limited to her own 
family. She has become a familiar and supportive presence among other 
veterans and individuals with disabilities, assisting participants at 
adaptive sporting events and offering encouragement wherever it is 
needed. She has also volunteered alongside her husband visiting 
veterans, including those receiving long-term care and treatment in the 
Spinal Cord Injury Unit at the Louis Stokes Cleveland VA Medical 
Center.

[[Page E1116]]

  The Unseen Service Award honors those whose sacrifices may not come 
with a uniform, rank, or medal, but whose service is indispensable. 
Caregiving requires patience, resilience, compassion, and sacrifice, 
often without recognition and without a day truly off.
  Mrs. Bostic represents that quiet strength. Her years of unwavering 
support have allowed her husband not simply to confront life after a 
catastrophic injury, but to continue serving others. In doing so, she 
has become a source of support to the broader veterans community 
herself.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Cristy 
Dianna Bostic on receiving the Unseen Service Award and in recognizing 
her years of sacrifice, caregiving, volunteerism, and unwavering 
commitment to her family and to veterans throughout Northeast Ohio.
```
