# HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF QUAKERTOWN FIRE COMPANY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
        HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF QUAKERTOWN FIRE COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the 75th anniversary of the 
Quakertown Fire Company in Pittstown, New Jersey. For 75 years, the 
Quakertown Fire Company has dutifully served our community. Its storied 
legacy of protecting Franklin Township reflects the steadfast emergency 
services its volunteers continue to provide today.
  Founded in 1914 and reorganized as a combination fire and EMS 
department in 1951, Quakertown Fire Company serves all of Franklin 
Township and portions of Union Township and Alexandria Township in 
Hunterdon County.
  Over their many years, the responsibility and capabilities of 
Quakertown Fire Company have greatly increased. From answering 65 calls 
a year in the mid-1980s to 767 calls in 2022, their services are needed 
now more than ever.
  Today, the Quakertown Fire Company is led by Chief Bradley Patkochis. 
Through the leadership and tireless dedication of every volunteer who 
has served over the past 75 years, the company continues its proud 
legacy and works to ensure the safety of Franklin Township and beyond.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in commemorating the 
75th anniversary of the Quakertown Fire Company and in wishing its 
members continued success for many years to come.
```
