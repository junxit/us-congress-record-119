# RECOGNIZING CHARLIE LANDER AS OHIO'S 13TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
  RECOGNIZING CHARLIE LANDER AS OHIO'S 13TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S 
                          CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Charlie Landers as 
Ohio's 13th Congressional District's Champion of the Week. This 
recognition honors his dedication to our district, his remarkable 
accomplishments, and his ongoing contributions to the community.
  Charlie Landers is a 16-year-old high school student at Western 
Reserve Academy in Hudson, Ohio. After reading Plato's ``Republic,'' he 
was inspired to dive deeper into the realm of human ethics and 
technology. With the rise of Artificial Intelligence (A.I.), Landers 
seeks to educate those around him about cyber safety, especially 
seniors. As part of his initiative, Landers has created and hosted his 
own workshops across the district.
  During his series of workshops, he creates an environment that is 
open, friendly, personable, and welcoming, while simultaneously 
educational and informative. Landers believes it is important to take 
care of each other, as well as stay informed and share our knowledge in 
order to crack down on scams.
  It was reported that last year, seniors in the United States lost 
more than $4.8 billion from more than 147,000 complaints. Nearly 25,000 
occurred in Ohio, according to the FBI. At only 16, Charlie Landers 
took action to combat these issues by teaching people around him how to 
spot scams, fighting against the misuses of A.I., and saving community 
members from losing money.
  This recognition honors his commitment to education, helping those 
around him, and his character. Landers thanks his educators, family, 
and friends for being his support system and encouraging him to create 
his workshop.
  I congratulate Charlie Landers. His accomplishments and commitment to 
our district are a reflection of the strength of Ohio's 13th District, 
and they exemplify why I am proud to call it the ``Birthplace of 
Champions.''

                          ____________________
```
