# RECOGNIZING SONIA BEHRMAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 24TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Carbajal, Salud O. (C001112) [D-CA]

```
  RECOGNIZING SONIA BEHRMAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 24TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SALUD O. CARBAJAL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 22, 2026

  Mr. CARBAJAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sonia Behrman as 
one of California's 24th Congressional District's Women of the Year. 
Each year, I have the privilege of honoring women on the Central Coast 
who have made a meaningful difference in our community through the 
Women of the Year Award. This year, it is my pleasure to recognize 
Sonia Behrman of Carpinteria, California, for her strength, compassion, 
and commitment to helping others.
  Born and raised in Carpinteria, Sonia is the daughter of Aurora, a 
first-generation immigrant from Mexico City, and John, whose family is 
from Jalisco. Sonia learned from an early age the importance of family, 
hard work, and perseverance. As a young mother, she faced difficult 
circumstances and made the decision to build a new future for herself 
and her family. With the support of Domestic Violence Solutions and 
Transition House, she found the strength to move forward and create a 
life centered around caring for others.
  As a single mother, Sonia earned her RN from Santa Barbara City 
College and built a career in nursing, working in chemical dependency 
care, neonatal intensive care, and the operating room. Her commitment 
to caring for others later led her to foster care, where she and her 
current husband opened their home to medically fragile infants and 
adopted a baby girl.
  Sonia has become a strong advocate for foster youth. She organizes a 
major annual fundraiser for Angel's Foster Care in Santa Barbara, 
bringing together more than 200 guests each year. She also supports the 
nonprofit Foster Love and travels to Disneyland to chaperone foster 
youth for a day in the park. She serves on the Board of Directors for 
the Westmont College Foundation and has participated in a medical 
mission in Guatemala with Helps International.
  Each Christmas, Sonia prepares backpacks filled with essentials and 
personally distributes them to people experiencing homelessness. She 
has built her life around caring for others, whether through nursing, 
foster care, community service, or simply being there for a friend. She 
exemplifies service as a way of life.
  It is with great pride that I honor Sonia Behrman as Woman of the 
Year. Her strength, generosity, and commitment to service have made a 
lasting difference in the lives of so many people. I ask my colleagues 
to join me in recognizing Sonia for all she does for the community and 
for the compassion she continues to bring to the Central Coast of 
California.
```
