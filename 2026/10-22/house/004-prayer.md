# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, we want to know You. We want to know what You want of us, 
what You are doing in our lives, and what we mean to You.
  Help us to see that to know You is to keep Your commands and to allow 
Your Word to shape our lives.
  In obeying Your Word, may we come to understand more fully Your 
perfect love for us.
  Create in us a desire to follow You, for our lives should be more 
than lives of obedience but of character. Show us what it means to 
pattern our lives according to Your will, that we would be humble, 
intentional, and selfless.
  Then let us, in our daily walk, not hold on to hatred or resentment, 
bitterness or acrimony, lest we become so focused on these dark 
thoughts that we ourselves are blinded and others cannot see Your light 
in our lives.
  Sure of Your truth and pardoned by Your mercy, may we offer ourselves 
to Your leading today and in the days to come.
  In the light of Your truth and in the strength of Your name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
