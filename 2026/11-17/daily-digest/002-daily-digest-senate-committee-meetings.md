# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine the nominations of Nathan Anonick, of Virginia, 
who was introduced by Senator Boozman, and Matthew Rowland, of the 
District of Columbia, who was introduced by Senator Klobuchar, both to 
be a Commissioner of the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, after 
the nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
NOMINATION
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the nomination of Hung Cao, of Virginia, to be Secretary of the Navy, 
Department of Defense, after the nominee, who was introduced by 
Representative Rob Wittman, testified and answered questions in his own 
behalf.
U.S.-SAUDI ARABIA NUCLEAR COOPERATION
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee received a closed briefing on 
United States-Saudi Arabia Agreement on civil nuclear cooperation from 
Donald Blome, Assistant Secretary of State, Bureau of Near Eastern 
Affairs; and Richard Goorevich, Assistant Deputy Administrator for Non-
Proliferation and Arms Control, National Nuclear Security 
Administration, Department of Energy.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee ordered favorably reported the 
nominations of Robert E. Long, Jr., to be United States District Judge 
for the Middle District of Florida, Peter M. Mansfield, to be United 
States District Judge for the Eastern District of Louisiana, Jesus A. 
Osete, to be United States District Judge for the Western District of 
Missouri, Stephen Husk, of Virginia, to be a Commissioner of the United 
States Parole Commission, and Samuel King Lee, to be United States 
Attorney for the Eastern District of Tennessee, Gretchen C.F. Shappert, 
of Virginia, to be United States Attorney for the District of Guam and 
concurrently United States Attorney for the District of the Northern 
Mariana Islands, Robert Troester, to be United States Attorney for the 
Western District of Oklahoma, Reid McAlister Creswell, to be United 
States Marshal for the District of South Carolina, James Christopher 
Edge, to be United States Marshal for the Western District of North 
Carolina, Christopher Walter Inabinett, to be United States Marshal for 
the Middle District of Alabama, and James Richard Jackson, to be United 
States Marshal for the Eastern District of Texas, all of the Department 
of Justice.
PRESCRIPTION DRUG COSTS
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
how competition and transparency lowers prescription drug costs, after 
receiving testimony from Merith Basey, Patients For Affordable Drugs 
Now, Washington. D.C.; Alex Oshmyansky, Cost Plus Drugs, Dallas, Texas; 
Matthew Pitlick, Iowa Pharmacy Association, Urbandale, Iowa; Neeraj 
Sood, University of Southern California Sol Price School of Public 
Policy, Los Angeles, California; and Karriem S. Watson, UI Health Mile 
Square Health Center, Chicago, Illinois.
ROBLOX AND ONLINE GAMING
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism 
concluded a hearing to examine Roblox and online gaming, focusing on 
digital playgrounds endangering America's children, after receiving 
testimony from Michael Schlep and Woody

[[Page D1034]]

Doe, both of Texas; Khari James, National Center on Sexual 
Exploitation, Tallahassee, Florida; and Myriam Gilles, Northwestern 
Pritzker School of Law, Chicago, Illinois.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Rules and Administration: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of Ashley Stow, of Texas, and Andrew Woodson, 
of Virginia, both to be a Member of the Federal Election Commission, 
after the nominees, who were both introduced by Senator Hagerty, 
testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee received a closed briefing 
on certain intelligence matters from officials of the intelligence 
community.
```
