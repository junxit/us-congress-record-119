# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 40

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-21. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Puerto Rico 
     House of Representatives, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 40, supporting the efforts of the Corps of 
     Cadets and Retirement Officers of Puerto Rico Association, 
     urging that the Medal of Honor be awarded posthumously to 
     Sergeant First Class Jorge Otero Barreto, who for his acts of 
     valor and conspicuous gallantry in the Vietnam War, is known 
     as the ``Puerto Rican Rambo''; and for other related 
     purposes; which was referred to the Committee on Veterans' 
     Affairs.
```
