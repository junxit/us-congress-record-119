# RECOGNIZING CHESTER KIRBY

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING CHESTER KIRBY

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the heroic 
actions of Chester Kirby of Waynesville, Georgia, who helped save three 
lives from a fire on February 12, 2025.
  On a Wednesday afternoon, when a brush fire spread out of control, 
Chester raced toward the flames engulfing his neighbors' mobile home. 
When he arrived, the flames were growing, and the three people inside 
had no idea of the danger they faced.
  Showing extraordinary courage and selflessness, Chester pounded on 
the door and shouted to alert his neighbors. After two of the occupants 
were helped to safety, Chester rushed inside the burning home, found 
Robert Graham, who was wheelchair bound, and carried him out of the 
flames. Because of him, all three occupants escaped the fire with their 
lives.
  If you ask Chester, he will tell you it was just one of those things 
you do when you have to do it.
  When danger came knocking, he put the lives of others before his own, 
and that is heroism.
  I thank Chester. He is a role model to us all and the kind of 
neighbor we would all be lucky to have.

                          ____________________
```
