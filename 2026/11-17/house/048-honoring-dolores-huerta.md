# HONORING DOLORES HUERTA

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Adelita S. (G000606) [D-AZ]

```
                        HONORING DOLORES HUERTA

  (Mrs. GRIJALVA asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Dolores 
Huerta, an icon, a trailblazer, and a lifelong champion for justice. As 
the cofounder of the United Farm Workers, she dedicated her life to 
fighting for workers' rights, civil rights, and dignity for all.
  Dolores was the voice for communities too often unheard and unseen. 
She was our ``warrior''; ``guerrera,'' fearless, determined, and 
committed to the causes and the people she loved.
  In 1972 when people told her ``no, you can't''; ``no se puede,'' she 
answered ``yes, we can''; ``si se puede.'' More than five decades 
later, those words continue to inspire.
  Dolores was also a dear friend of my dad's and our entire family. She 
stood with us through some of our happiest and hardest moments. I will 
always remember her compassion and leadership.
  As a Latina, I know that generations of women, workers, and leaders, 
including myself, stand on the shoulders of Dolores Huerta. She opened 
doors and broke barriers so others could follow.
  Her legacy is one of courage, resilience, hope, and action. The best 
way to honor her is to continue to fight for the justice she dedicated 
her life to.
  May she rest in power. ``Live long, Dolores Huerta''; ``Que viva, 
Dolores Huerta.''

                          ____________________
```
