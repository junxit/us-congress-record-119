# HONORING THE LIVES AND LEGACIES OF CONGRESSMAN TIM HOLDEN AND STATE REPRESENTATIVE BOB ALLEN

**Speaking:** Meuser, Daniel (M001204) [R-PA]

```
  HONORING THE LIVES AND LEGACIES OF CONGRESSMAN TIM HOLDEN AND STATE 
                        REPRESENTATIVE BOB ALLEN

  (Mr. Meuser of Pennsylvania was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. MEUSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with sincere sympathy and 
condolences to honor the lives and legacies of two exceptional public 
servants from Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania, Congressman Tim Holden, 
who served in this body from 1993 to 2013, and, as well, Pennsylvania 
State Representative Bob Allen, both of whom passed away this weekend.
  Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania, has lost two outstanding leaders who 
dedicated their lives to public service and made tremendous 
contributions to our region.
  I knew Tim Holden for many, many years, and he was always a good, 
fair, and decent man. He was just a great guy. He was well-liked and 
widely respected, including by those on both sides of the political 
aisle.
  Before being elected to Congress in 1992, Tim served our Schuylkill 
County community as Schuylkill County Sheriff. He went on to become a 
highly respected Member of this body and one of the original Blue Dog 
Democrats. He believed in working across the aisle, finding common 
ground, and putting the interests of his constituents and his country 
first.
  After leaving Congress, Tim continued his public service in the 
Pennsylvania State government as director of the Liquor Control Board. 
He was always accessible and willing to pick up the phone any time. It 
was always a pleasure to see Tim out and about in Schuylkill County, 
and when you did, Mr. Speaker, you could always count on a great 
conversation.
  Sadly, Mr. Speaker, we also lost former State Representative Bob 
Allen, who also served as mayor of Pottsville and was a true pillar in 
the Schuylkill County community. Bob was a good friend and a good man 
who was widely respected throughout our region. He dedicated his life 
to making Schuylkill County a better place and accomplished a great 
deal for the people he represented.
  He was also deeply committed to strengthening Pennsylvania and was 
someone lawmakers and community leaders, including myself, turned to 
regularly for his counsel, friendship, and support. Bob made a real 
difference in Schuylkill County, and his contributions to our community 
will never be forgotten.
  Mr. Speaker, Tim Holden and Bob Allen represented different parties. 
Tim was a Democrat, and Bob was a Republican, but they shared a 
friendship and a commitment to public service, a love for Schuylkill 
County, a love for Pennsylvania and for our country, and had a genuine 
desire to make life better for the people they represented.
  They are great examples of the type of leadership that we all 
respect. Both men served our region with distinction, and their 
legacies will live on in the communities they worked so hard to 
improve.
  Our entire community mourns the loss of these two public servants, 
and I extend my most sincere condolences and prayers to the Holden 
family and the Allen family, and their many friends and colleagues 
throughout Schuylkill County, throughout Pennsylvania, and throughout 
our entire Nation.
```
