# REMEMBERING JEFF STEPP

**Speaking:** Moore, Tim (M001236) [R-NC]

```
                         REMEMBERING JEFF STEPP

  (Mr. MOORE of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MOORE of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and memory of John Jeffrey ``Jeff'' Stepp, who was born on 
September 12, 1956, in Gaston County, North Carolina.
  After high school, Jeff served in the United States Marine Corps from 
1974 until 1976. He went on to pursue a career in law enforcement, 
first with the Gastonia Police Department, and then with the North 
Carolina Highway Patrol, where he served there as a field training 
officer and academy instructor before retiring as a master trooper in 
2004.
  After retirement, Jeff continued helping others through Bethlehem 
Community Church, where he helped establish the Stepp of Faith 
Ministry, which provided medical equipment to those in need, and 
organized blood drives in our community.
  Jeff believed true character is measured not by recognition, but by 
the difference we make in the lives of others. His life was a testament 
to courage, integrity, compassion, and faith.
  Mr. Speaker, as we mourn his passing, may we remember Jeff Stepp for 
his legacy of protecting others and lending a helping hand. Our prayers 
are with his family and friends.

                          ____________________
```
