# RECOGNIZING THE RESTAURANT, SO. FOX

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                  RECOGNIZING THE RESTAURANT, SO. FOX

  (Mr. Carter of Georgia was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
restaurant, So. Fox, and congratulate them for their recognition in The 
New York Times as one of the top 50 restaurants in the United States.
  Appearing on this list is a prestigious honor, with the list holding 
national appeal and driving an increase in tourism to the area. The 
restaurant is in Atlanta, Georgia, opened in May of 2026, and has since 
risen to popularity not only locally but nationally as well.
  The process to choose a restaurant for the list involves 16 scouts 
traveling across the country and determining, without ranking, the top 
spots. So. Fox was able to win this spot out of 538 restaurants in 38 
States across the continental United States. So. Fox continues to 
represent Georgia hospitality and excellence across the Nation.
  I wish nothing but the best for So. Fox and its owners going forward.


                       Recognizing Lainie Barrow

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Miss Lainie Barrow of Rincon, Georgia, on her success in 
winning Miss USA National Preteen on her first try. This pageant is 
made to recognize young girls for academics, leadership, and community 
service.
  Lainie had never competed in a pageant before this one. She attends 
Ebenezer Middle School and will spend the next year as an ambassador 
for the program, representing her own platform, Lainie's Wild Hearts.
  Barrow spends her time cheerleading, performing in her school band, 
and giving back through 4-H Club and Beta Club.
  Barrow has expressed interest in continuing her pageant career and 
one day becoming Miss America.
  I want to extend congratulations to Miss Lainie Barrow and thank her 
for representing the best of Georgia.

    Recognizing Georgia Military Veterans Hall of Fame Class of 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Georgia Military Veterans Hall of Fame Class of 2026.
  This brave class of men and women gave everything to defend our 
Nation's freedom and have made the people of Georgia incredibly proud 
through their hard work and sacrifice. These inductees come from all 
over our Nation's military branches and have served Georgia and the 
Nation for many years.

  Those inducted into the Georgia Military Veterans Hall of Fame have 
shown countless acts of heroism and service and have gone above and 
beyond the call of duty in service to our Nation. The work these men 
and women have done in protecting our Nation's freedom is a debt we can 
never repay.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate all of them on their induction, and I 
thank them for their fine service to our country.


                       Recognizing Ariana Roddini

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ariana 
Roddini for her retirement from the Federal Law Enforcement Training 
Center, FLETC.
  Ms. Roddini will retire on December 31 from her position as the 
associate director for training operations.
  Her service in law enforcement originally began with her career as a 
fraud investigator for the New York City Department of Health in 1994.
  Ms. Roddini has overseen all training operations at FLETC training 
sites in Georgia, New Mexico, South Carolina, and Maryland, as well as 
international, State, local, Tribal, and territorial training 
throughout the United States and the world.
  Prior to her appointment as the associate director, Ms. Roddini 
served FLETC as the assistant director for the training management 
operations directorate. In that role, she provided oversight and 
strategic direction to six training and administrative divisions across 
FLETC's four training sites.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Ms. Roddini for her service to Georgia and our 
entire Nation.
```
