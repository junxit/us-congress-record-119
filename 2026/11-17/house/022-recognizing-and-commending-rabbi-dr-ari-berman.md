# RECOGNIZING AND COMMENDING RABBI DR. ARI BERMAN

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
            RECOGNIZING AND COMMENDING RABBI DR. ARI BERMAN

  (Ms. FOXX asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)

[[Page H6236]]

  

  Ms. FOXX. Mr. Speaker, I also rise to recognize Rabbi Dr. Ari Berman, 
the president of Yeshiva University, who is serving as the House guest 
chaplain.
  When I was chair of the House Committee on Education and Workforce, I 
had the pleasure of meeting Rabbi Berman for the first time. His 
leadership at Yeshiva University and the university's commitment to 
academic excellence anchored by Jewish and American values should be 
recognized and commended.
  Yeshiva University fosters curiosity and critical thinking for a life 
of professional achievement, personal religious growth, and the 
contribution to the Jewish people, Israel, and the world.
  Mr. Speaker, that is a very important mission and one that I know 
Rabbi Berman remains steadfast in supporting and nourishing for all of 
the university's students, faculty, and staff, both past and present.
  I thank Rabbi Berman for his work and his continued leadership. May 
he and his family be blessed today, tomorrow, and always.

                          ____________________
```
