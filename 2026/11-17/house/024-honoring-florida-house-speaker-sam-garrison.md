# HONORING FLORIDA HOUSE SPEAKER SAM GARRISON

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
                              {time}  1410
              HONORING FLORIDA HOUSE SPEAKER SAM GARRISON

  (Mr. BEAN of Florida asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, there is a reason they call it the 
free State of Florida. We value individual liberty, support free 
enterprise, and believe government should serve the people. Today, my 
good friend Sam Garrison takes the gavel of the speaker of the Florida 
House of Representatives to carry those principles forward.
  Sam calls Clay County home, right in the middle of Florida's Fourth 
Congressional District. We are proud to see one of our own entrusted 
with this tremendous responsibility.
  Sam understands that leadership means listening, standing by your 
principles, and keeping the people you serve at the heart of every 
decision.
  We congratulate also his wife, Gayle; and their daughters, Jane and 
Mary Beth. This special day belongs to them too.
  My voting responsibilities keep me here on this floor in Washington, 
but Abby and I are cheering them on and wish we could be with them in 
Tallahassee.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Sam. May God grant him wisdom and 
strength as he leads our great State.

                          ____________________
```
