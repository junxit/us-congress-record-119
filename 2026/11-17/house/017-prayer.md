# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rabbi Ari Berman, Yeshiva University, New York, New York, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty God, at the dawn of creation, You endowed humanity with its 
most sacred gift: the imprint of Your divine image.
  Upon this truth our Nation was founded and this House established: to 
protect human dignity and give voice to the people whose freedoms flow 
from this divine inheritance.
  Lord, help us hear the needs behind those voices. There is a hunger 
in this land not only for economic security, but for belonging and 
shared purpose.
  Bless the Members of this House with an intimate sympathy for the 
struggles and aspirations of their constituents.
  Help this House unleash the power of our shared humanity, reject the 
political expediency of hate, and uplift all whom they represent.
  Grant us the wisdom of the prophets of Israel who taught us to 
cherish our blessings and place them in service to others.
  For we serve You most when we serve each other best.
  May this be Thy will.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
