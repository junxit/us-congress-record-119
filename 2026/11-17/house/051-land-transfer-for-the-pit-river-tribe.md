# LAND TRANSFER FOR THE PIT RIVER TRIBE

**Speaking:** Gallagher, James (G000607) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2400

```
                 LAND TRANSFER FOR THE PIT RIVER TRIBE

  (Mr. GALLAGHER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GALLAGHER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in strong support of H.R. 
2400, the Pit River Land Transfer Act of 2026, as amended by the 
Senate.
  This legislation is straightforward. It transfers approximately 557 
acres of Federal land in Shasta County into trust for the benefit of 
the Pit River Tribe, one of California's first people.
  Known as the Four Corners property, this land holds deep cultural and 
historical significance to the Tribe.
  The Pit River Tribe has worked for decades with perseverance to 
reclaim this ancestral land.
  Despite too many years of Federal delays, I stand here today, and I 
hope you will join me to make it right. They have waited long enough.
  This is also a responsibly and carefully written transfer. Existing 
rights will remain protected, while approximately 87 acres, containing 
transportation corridors and other permitted uses, will remain outside 
the transfer.
  This legislation is deeply personal both to me and the Tribe. 
Congressman Doug LaMalfa championed this bill. He understood what this 
land meant to the Pit River Tribe and its people. I have been proud to 
carry this bill forward on Doug's behalf.
  I am also grateful to Senators Padilla and Schiff for working across 
party lines to advance it through the Senate.
  Returning this land has never been a partisan issue. It is about 
honoring a longstanding commitment and correcting years of Federal 
delay.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to pass this bill.

                          ____________________
```
