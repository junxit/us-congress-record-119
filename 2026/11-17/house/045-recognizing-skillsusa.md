# RECOGNIZING SKILLSUSA

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                         RECOGNIZING SKILLSUSA

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, for more than 60 years, 
SkillsUSA has prepared students for trade, technical, and skilled 
service jobs. This organization has helped young men and women succeed 
in today's modern economy.
  Career and technical education is a strategic asset and an 
opportunity for students to learn tangible skills for the jobs of 
tomorrow. Members of SkillsUSA are the next leaders of our workforce 
and an investment in our future.
  As co-chair of the Career and Technical Education Caucus and a senior 
member of the House Education and Workforce Committee, I was pleased to 
see a U.S. team compete at the WorldSkills Competition in September.
  Mr. Speaker, Alexis Squyres of Pennsylvania was one of eight students 
selected to represent SkillsUSA at the WorldSkills Competition in 
Shanghai. I congratulate Alexis on this achievement and thank him for 
representing the Commonwealth.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to support career and technical 
education. Together, we can continue to champion legislation that will 
build the workforce to keep our country competitive for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I applaud and congratulate these students for 
representing our country so well at WorldSkills.

                          ____________________
```
