# CELEBRATING AMERICA'S HISTORY

**Speaking:** Haridopolos, Mike (H001099) [R-FL]

```
                     CELEBRATING AMERICA'S HISTORY

  (Mr. Haridopolos of Florida was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, as we celebrate America's 250th 
anniversary, we are reminded of the incredible history, sacrifices, and 
founding principles that make our Nation the greatest country in the 
world.
  That is why I am proud to join my colleague, Congresswoman Jennifer 
McClellan of Virginia, in introducing the bipartisan National American 
History and Founders Month Act.
  This legislation would establish a permanent national observance 
dedicated to educating Americans about our Nation's founding, the 
remarkable individuals who shaped our Nation, and the freedoms we enjoy 
today.
  As a former history teacher, I know firsthand how important it is for 
the next generation and all Americans to learn the incredible story of 
America. As we look ahead to the next 250 years, we must ensure that 
future generations understand and appreciate the freedoms that make 
America so special.


                Celebrating Future of American Aerospace

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight the exciting 
showcase of American innovation right here on Florida's Space Coast.

  I was proud to join leaders from across the Nation, including 
President Trump and NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman, among others in 
aerospace and aviation at Kennedy Space Center for the first inaugural 
Cape Week showcase, celebrating the past, present, and future of 
American flight and space exploration.
  In honor of our Nation's 250th anniversary, the event brought 
together innovators, engineers, astronauts, and industry leaders to 
highlight the technologies shaping the next generation of aerospace. 
From advanced aircraft and autonomous vehicles to the next frontier of 
space exploration, Cape Week demonstrated what is possible when 
American ingenuity and innovation lead the way.
  There is no better place to host an event than Florida's Space Coast, 
where generations of Americans have pushed the boundaries of what is 
possible. Our region is at the center of American aerospace, and I look 
forward to seeing the next generation of breakthroughs take place right 
there.


          Recognizing Walter Marshall as Veteran of the Month

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Walter 
Marshall, a proud veteran from Brevard County and our Veteran of the 
Month.
  Mr. Marshall served our Nation honorably in the United States Navy 
for 6 years as a rescue swimmer, taking on a role that required 
courage, discipline, and a commitment to protecting others.
  During his service, he supported search and rescue missions in the 
Navy's 6th Fleet throughout the Mediterranean, helping safeguard 
American personnel and those in need at sea. His service earned him the 
National Defense Service Medal, recognizing his contribution to our 
Nation's defense.
  Rescue swimmers put themselves in harm's way to save others, 
embodying the very best of the selflessness and dedication for those 
who wear the Nation's uniform.
  Today, we thank Walter Marshall for his service, his sacrifice, and 
his commitment to protecting his fellow Americans.


      Honoring Miguel Antonio Pabon, Jr., as Veteran of the Month

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Miguel 
Antonio Pabon, Jr., a proud veteran of Florida's Space Coast, and our 
October Veteran of the Month.
  Mr. Pabon dedicated 10 years to serving our Nation, including 8 years 
in the United States Marine Corps and 2 years in the Florida Army 
National Guard, ultimately achieving the rank of sergeant.
  During his military career, he served as a maintenance administrator 
and recruiter, supporting our Armed Forces and helping prepare the next 
generation of Americans for military service.
  His dedication earned him several honors, including the Good Conduct 
Medal, Global War on Terrorism Service Medal, Global War on Terrorism 
Expeditionary Medal, and the National Defense Service Medal. His 
service across two branches of our Armed Forces reflect a lasting 
commitment to duty, discipline, and defending our Nation.
  Today, we thank Miguel Antonio Pabon, Jr., for his dedicated service 
to our military, his patriotism, and his continued example of what 
Americans are all about.
```
