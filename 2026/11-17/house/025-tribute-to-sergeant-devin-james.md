# TRIBUTE TO SERGEANT DEVIN JAMES

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO SERGEANT DEVIN JAMES

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise with a heavy heart 
to pay special tribute to Sergeant Devin James of the Greene County 
Sheriff's Office.
  Sergeant James was killed in the line of duty on November 12 while 
attempting to assist with a vehicle pursuit.
  He had been with the Greene County Sheriff's Office for 5 years.
  Sergeant James was well loved and represented the best in law 
enforcement.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Sergeant Devin James and 
extend my deepest condolences to his family, his colleagues at the 
Greene County Sheriff's Office, and to the Greene County community.

                          ____________________
```
