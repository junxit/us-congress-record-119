# RECOGNIZING ELIZABETH SOTO

**Speaking:** Ramirez, Delia C. (R000617) [D-IL]

```
                       RECOGNIZING ELIZABETH SOTO

  (Mrs. Ramirez of Illinois was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize United States 
Army veteran and Illinois' Third Congressional District's very own 
Elizabeth Soto for her unwavering commitment to empowering our veterans 
and our community.
  Born and raised in Chicagoland, Elizabeth is as diverse as our 
beloved city, with a rich blend of Mexican, French, and American roots. 
For 9 years, she proudly served in the Illinois Army National Guard, 
from 2004 to 2013, where she developed the values of integrity, 
resilience, leadership, and service that continue to guide her today.
  Elizabeth currently serves as the superintendent of the Veterans 
Assistance Commission of Cook County, making history as the first 
Latina and the first woman to hold the position in the organization's 
more than 90-year history.
  There, Elizabeth has worked to modernize veterans services, 
strengthen government and community partnerships, and meet the evolving 
needs of our veterans. As a valued member of my veterans working group, 
Elizabeth brings her experience, dedication, and firsthand 
understanding of the veteran community to ensure that we honor our 
veterans with action.

  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, I 
commend Elizabeth Soto for her meaningful and lasting impact on the 
veteran community and the residents of Cook County.


                      Recognizing Richard Wojewnik

  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize U.S. Marine 
Corps veteran Richard Wojewnik for his service, leadership, and 
dedication to his community, family, and country.
  Richard embodies dedication to public service, with over 40 years of 
service across his military career and employment with the United 
States Postal Service.
  While Richard has a dedicated service record as a special duty marine 
security guard at the Hong Kong Consulate General from 1981 through 
1983 and a Marine Corps Reserve member in Operation Desert Shield and 
Operation Desert Storm, Richard's heart for service extends beyond his 
career.
  As a member of American Legion Post 335 and Veterans of Foreign Wars 
Post 5979, Richard was instrumental in founding the Chicagoland 
Combined Veterans Museum, keeping veterans' stories alive and honoring 
their service and courage.
  Richard is a passionate advocate and a mentor to young people. He 
served on our district's Service Academy Nominations Committee. He 
wanted to make sure that today, on this House

[[Page H6231]]

floor, I noted that his story would not be complete without the 
recognition of the support, love, care, sacrifice, and dedication of 
his beloved wife of 43 years, Luz.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, I 
am honored to commend Richard for his commitment to service and to 
honoring the dedication and sacrifice of our veterans. May we honor his 
service with action, always.

                              {time}  1215


                     Recognizing Charles Henderson

  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Charles 
Henderson, my constituent, a historian, a member of my veterans working 
group, and a warrior for peace and social justice.
  The son of a Mexican-born mother and African-American father, Charles 
grew up in Chicago, where he witnessed firsthand the systemic barriers 
affecting marginalized communities. These experiences shaped his 
commitment to equity, advocacy, and community empowerment.
  After returning from service in Afghanistan and South Korea, Charles 
became a fierce advocate of ensuring that veterans receive the benefits 
they were promised and deserve, including access to mental health care 
and safe, stable housing. Most notable, Charles was instrumental in 
ensuring Emilio Aguirre, a World War II U.S. Army veteran, Bronze Star 
recipient, and former prisoner of war received a proper burial after 
his death in the 1995 Chicago heat wave. Charles dedicated 5 years to 
uncovering Aguirre's story and ensuring that he received the honor he 
deserved.
  Today, Charles is a vocal activist, participating in Veterans for 
Peace, where he advocates for an end to these forever wars and 
campaigns of death and destruction.
  On behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, I am proud to 
commend Charles Henderson for his unwavering commitment to veterans' 
rights, equity, and community care. I thank Charles. His name has now 
been permanently entered into the Congressional Record. We will, 
together, build a more peaceful world.
```
