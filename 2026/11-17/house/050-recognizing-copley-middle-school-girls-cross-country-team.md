# RECOGNIZING COPLEY MIDDLE SCHOOL GIRLS' CROSS-COUNTRY TEAM

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
       RECOGNIZING COPLEY MIDDLE SCHOOL GIRLS' CROSS-COUNTRY TEAM

  (Mrs. SYKES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1

[[Page H6253]]

minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize the Copley Middle 
School girls' cross-country team as the Champion of the Week for Ohio's 
13th Congressional District.
  The Copley Middle School girls' cross-country team recently earned 
first-place finish in the middle school cross-country State 
championship. This recognition honors their extraordinary achievement, 
perseverance, and unwavering commitment to excellence.
  Demonstrating outstanding athletic skill at the State championship, 
three runners secured positions within the top 15. Emerson Capper 
finished in first place, Emma Jones placed second, and Mary Crawford 
achieved seventh overall.
  This is an extraordinary end to an unprecedented season where the 
team finished either first or second at every single meet and 
invitational that they competed in this year.
  I extend my sincere congratulations to Coach Amy Renner and the 
entire Copley Middle School girls' cross-country team for their well-
deserved recognition and exceptional season.
  Girls' and women's sports produce next year's leaders, business 
leaders, businessowners, trailblazers, and Congresswomen. They 
certainly prove why Ohio's 13th Congressional District is known the 
birthplace of champions.

                          ____________________
```
