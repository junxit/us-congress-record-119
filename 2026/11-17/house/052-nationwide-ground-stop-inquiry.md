# NATIONWIDE GROUND STOP INQUIRY

```
                              {time}  1920
                     NATIONWIDE GROUND STOP INQUIRY

  (Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, the FAA's nationwide ground stop 
last Friday has yet to receive the appropriate level of action 
necessary for the chaos and disruption caused to millions of travelers 
and the corollary issues of public safety and agency accountability.
  The Transportation Secretary Duffy has yet to properly account for 
this disruption and, instead, has blamed Congress and lack of 
infrastructure dollars.
  I spent part of my career as a telecom attorney. I believe the buck 
stops with the people who oversee the FAA system upgrades, that they 
make sure they are experienced network engineers who are strategically 
and methodically coordinating how new software or AI features interface 
with analog systems, and that there is proper oversight of outside 
contractors. It is called responsible government.
  Yes, it is our job in Congress to legislate, appropriate, and provide 
oversight of the agencies and their activities. It is our job to get 
the facts right. This is why Speaker Johnson should launch an inquiry 
into this disruption.
  Last month, I also led a colleague letter to Secretary Duffy and FAA 
demanding details related to the testing that they did on new AI-
enabled smart systems for airports.
  The FAA rushed to install these systems after only 40-plus days. This 
is why we must, together, seek more answers, transparency, and 
accountability. It is not a bridge too far.

                          ____________________
```
