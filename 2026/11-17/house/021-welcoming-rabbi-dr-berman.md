# WELCOMING RABBI DR. BERMAN

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
                       WELCOMING RABBI DR. BERMAN

  (Ms. STEFANIK asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, it is a true honor to welcome my friend, 
Rabbi Dr. Ari Berman, as our guest chaplain today.
  Rabbi Dr. Berman serves as president of Yeshiva University, the 
premier Jewish university in the United States, based in my home State 
of New York.
  Ari Berman was born and raised in Queens and graduated from the 
Marsha Stern Talmudical Academy in 1987. He graduated magna cum laude 
from Yeshiva University and received his rabbinical ordination at the 
Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Theological Seminary. He went on to serve as a 
rabbi at the Jewish Center of Manhattan beginning in 1994 before 
becoming its lead rabbi in 2000.
  He earned a master's degree in medieval Jewish philosophy from 
Bernard Revel Graduate School. After moving to Israel in 2008, he 
earned a doctorate from Hebrew University in Jerusalem.
  Rabbi Dr. Berman was chosen as the fifth president of Yeshiva, with 
his tenure beginning in June 2017. Under his leadership, the university 
launched 20-plus new graduate and STEM-focused degree programs, 
achieved over 100 percent enrollment growth, and completed a historic 
landmark campaign advancing scholarships, faculty, facilities, and 
innovation.
  He has been recognized as a global leader in education, religion, and 
morality. He founded the ACE Commission on Faith-Based Colleges and 
Universities, led the first-ever delegation of university presidents on 
the March of the Living to Auschwitz, and following the tragic events 
of October 7, founded a coalition of more than 100 university 
presidents united against terrorism and anti-Semitism.
  He delivered the benediction at the inaugural ceremony in the U.S. 
Capitol for President Trump in January 2025. He leads with moral 
clarity, and it is an honor to welcome him here today.

                          ____________________
```
