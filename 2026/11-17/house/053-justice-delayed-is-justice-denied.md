# JUSTICE DELAYED IS JUSTICE DENIED

**Speaking:** Pressley, Ayanna (P000617) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 40

```
                   JUSTICE DELAYED IS JUSTICE DENIED

  (Ms. PRESSLEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Ms. PRESSLEY. Mr. Speaker, this week we honor a bridge between 
generations and recommit ourselves to the work of repair.
  That history runs through Massachusetts. State Senator Bill Owens 
drafted reparations legislation that helped provide a model for 
Congressman John Conyers' Federal effort.
  On November 20, 1989, Congressman Conyers introduced the Federal 
reparations commission bill that would evolve into H.R. 40.
  Today, I am honored to carry H.R. 40 and am clear-eyed about our 
responsibility to advance reparations.
  This movement did not begin in Washington. It was carried here by 
organizers, advocates, and lawmakers who refuse to accept that justice 
for Black people must always wait.
  Same fight. New generation. An obligation still unmet.
  This is about confronting the injustices of our past and repairing 
the harm that endures in Black folks' housing, school funding, 
healthcare, and the ability to build and pass on wealth.
  Mr. Speaker, justice delayed is justice denied, and we will not back 
down in our pursuit of healing and reparative justice.
  Same fight. New generation.

                          ____________________
```
