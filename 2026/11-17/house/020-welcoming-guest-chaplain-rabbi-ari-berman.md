# WELCOMING GUEST CHAPLAIN RABBI ARI BERMAN

**Speaking:** Espaillat, Adriano (E000297) [D-NY]

```
               WELCOMING GUEST CHAPLAIN RABBI ARI BERMAN

  (Mr. ESPAILLAT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. ESPAILLAT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today after literally traveling 
through several continents to be here today and welcome and commend our 
guest chaplain, Rabbi Dr. Ari Berman, the president of Yeshiva 
University, and to thank him for opening the people's House in prayer.
  For nearly a century, Yeshiva has called Washington Heights home. Its 
Wilf Campus sits on the Amsterdam Avenue hill of Washington Heights, in 
the heart of the neighborhood where I grew up.
  In the decades since, the Heights has served as the cradle of Jewish-
Latino relations in America. Dominicans, Puerto Ricans, Colombians, 
Mexicans, and Jewish New Yorkers have stood side by side in this 
community, protecting our neighborhoods and forging new opportunities 
for our sons, daughters, and grandchildren.
  The rabbi's public service and leadership at Yeshiva University, his 
alma mater, stands as an embodiment of this cycle and credo.
  I thank Rabbi Berman for joining us in prayer today and for his daily 
efforts to foster opportunity, unity, build bonds, and celebrate our 
shared history.

                          ____________________
```
