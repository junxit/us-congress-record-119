# NATIVE AMERICAN MONTH

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                              {time}  1910
                         NATIVE AMERICAN MONTH

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Native American 
Heritage Month. This month, we honor the culture, contributions, and 
resilience of the more than 575 Tribal nations and native communities 
across the country.
  The historical legacy of Native Americans in the Hudson Valley of New 
York is reflected throughout Westchester County and the Bronx as the 
names of cities from Tuckahoe to Mamaroneck honor the indigenous people 
that first stewarded our land.
  Today, New York is home to over 100,000 Native Americans who 
contribute to our vibrant and diverse community.
  But let us not forget the work that remains. We must continue to 
speak up for the Native American community and their enduring battle 
for sovereignty and self-determination. We will not let the 
contributions of Native Americans in our country be erased.

                          ____________________
```
