# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 1665, H.R. 3495, H.R. 4464, H.R. 5267, H.R. 5525, H.R. 8801, H.R. 9607, H.R. 9608

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. WALBERG: Committee on Education and Workforce. H.R. 
     9608. A bill to ensure the Department of Health and Human 
     Services will manage family engagement programs for 
     elementary and secondary education, and for other purposes; 
     with an amendment (Rept. 119-879). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WALBERG: Committee on Education and Workforce. H.R. 
     9607. A bill to ensure the Department of Labor will manage 
     programs within the Office of Career, Technical, and Adult 
     Education, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 
     119-880). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mrs. HOUCHIN: Committee on Rules. H. Res. 1665. A 
     resolution providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     3495) to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to 
     clarify the definition of employee as it relates to direct 
     sellers and real estate agents, and for other purposes, and 
     providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5267) to 
     preserve the franchise business model (Rept. 119-881). 
     Referred to the House Calendar.
       Mr. ARRINGTON: Committee on the Budget. H.R. 4464. A bill 
     to amend the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 respecting the 
     scoring of preventive health savings (Rept. 119-882). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. COMER: Committee on Oversight and Government Reform. 
     H.R. 5525. A bill to repeal the authority of the District of 
     Columbia to use automated traffic enforcement systems and to 
     erect signage prohibiting right turns at a red traffic 
     signal, with an amendment (Rept. 119-883). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. COMER: Committee on Oversight and Government Reform. 
     H.R. 8801. A bill to prohibit a congestion toll in the 
     District of Columbia, with an amendment (Rept. 119-884). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
```
