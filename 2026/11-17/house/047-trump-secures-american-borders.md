# TRUMP SECURES AMERICAN BORDERS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                     TRUMP SECURES AMERICAN BORDERS

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, under the leadership of 
President Donald Trump and House Republicans, led by Speaker   Mike 
Johnson, America has the most secure border in history. Illegal 
crossings have dropped to record lows with 17 consecutive months of 
zero illegal alien crossings, keeping American families safe and 
protecting American taxpayer dollars for the truly needy.
  The Council of Economic Advisers calculates the average lifetime 
taxpayer cost of an illegal alien in America is over $165,000.
  If the extreme leftist Democrats prevail to reopen the southern 
border with mass amnesty, spending would explode, diverting assistance 
from deserving Americans.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops. Trump is reinstituting peace 
through strength, revealing war criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump 
and mocking Trump, shamefully providing assistance to Iran to kill 
Americans abroad and now here in Washington with dirty bombs.
  Congratulations, New York Post, for 225 years of service, America's 
oldest newspaper, promoting fair and free journalism in the best 
tradition of freedom of the press.

                          ____________________
```
