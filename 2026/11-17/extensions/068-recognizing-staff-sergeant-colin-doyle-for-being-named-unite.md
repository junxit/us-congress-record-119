# RECOGNIZING STAFF SERGEANT COLIN DOYLE FOR BEING NAMED UNITED STATES ARMY NCO OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Boyle, Brendan F. (B001296) [D-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING STAFF SERGEANT COLIN DOYLE FOR BEING NAMED UNITED STATES 
                          ARMY NCO OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRENDAN F. BOYLE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. BOYLE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Staff Sergeant Colin Doyle of the 3rd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment 
for being named the United States Army's Noncommissioned Officer (NCO) 
of the year at the 2026 Association of the United States Army (AUSA) 
Annual Meeting and Exposition in Washington, D.C. on October 12, 2026.
  Staff Sergeant Doyle is from Glenside, Pennsylvania and graduated 
from La Salle College High School in Wyndmoor, Pennsylvania. He would 
then go on to be assigned to the 75th Ranger Regiment of the United 
States Army.
  The NCO of the Year award was presented to Staff Sergeant Doyle by 
the U.S. Army Vice Chief of Staff General Christopher LaNeve and 
Sergeant Major of the Army Michael Weimer. It honors Doyle for his 
exceptional performance at the intense competition held at Fort Benning 
in Georgia on September 11 to September 19, 2026. The competition 
featured 11 squads, each representing their respective major Army 
commands. They competed in a variety of events, including a formal 
board, fitness assessments, weapons qualification, familiarization 
lanes, an 18.6-mile night march, and obstacle courses.
  In addition to being honored as the NCO of the Year, Staff Sergeant 
Doyle was also the squad leader of the squad that represented the Army 
Rangers and was named Squad of the Year. In a statement, Staff Sergeant 
Doyle characterized the 75th Ranger Regiment as ``one-of-a-kind'' and 
described the unit's focus on being prepared and their mindset that 
competition strengthens each individual.
  Mr. Speaker, it is altogether fitting that the House recognizes Staff 
Sergeant Doyle for this incredible achievement.
```
