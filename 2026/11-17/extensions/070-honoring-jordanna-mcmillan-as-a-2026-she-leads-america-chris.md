# HONORING JORDANNA McMILLAN AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING JORDANNA McMILLAN AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN 
                             OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jordanna 
McMillan as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction 
award recipient.
  Jordanna McMillan serves as U.S. Executive Director of the Israel 
Allies Foundation, which equips a global network of legislators whose 
support for Israel is rooted in faith. She has served with the 
organization since its first international office opened in Washington, 
D.C., in 2011.
  Jordanna works with the United States Congress, hosting Capitol Hill 
events, coordinating meetings with visiting leaders, and helping 
organize an annual advocacy day that brings hundreds of rabbis and 
pastors to Washington. She speaks at international conferences for 
legislators and leads congressional and state legislator delegations to 
Israel, including visits to Judea and Samaria.
  Raised near Atlanta in a home and church supportive of Israel, 
Jordanna first visited the country in 2004 during the Second Intifada. 
Beginning in 2007, she lived in Jerusalem for nearly two years, 
studying Modern Hebrew and Middle Eastern Studies at Hebrew 
University's Rothberg International School while serving with the 
International Christian Embassy Jerusalem.
  During that time, she helped lead young adult Christians on service 
tours, connecting participants with biblical sites and preparing them 
to advocate for Israel.
  A summa cum laude graduate of Southeastern University, Jordanna's 
service reflects her commitment to faith, family, relationships, and 
strengthening understanding and support for Israel.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
