# RECOGNIZING SERGEANT MAJOR JEFFREY SCOTT HOWARD (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
         RECOGNIZING SERGEANT MAJOR JEFFREY SCOTT HOWARD (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Sergeant Major Jeffrey Scott Howard 
(Ret.) of Belton has committed his life to uplifting service members, 
veterans, and his community, both in uniform and as a civilian. His 
enduring passion for others demonstrates the finest traditions of the 
United States Army, and it is my distinct honor to recognize him with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation.
  A graduate of Airborne, Pathfinder, and Air Assault schools, earning 
Honor Graduate distinction in the latter two, SGM Howard served as a 
Rifleman, Pathfinder Platoon Sergeant for the 17th Combat Aviation 
Group, and Operations Sergeant Major for the 1st Battalion, 75th 
Infantry Regiment. His achievements include selection as the 1983 U.S. 
Army Infantry Center Instructor of the Year, the AUSA Leadership Award, 
and service as the first Department of the Army Inspector General 
Sergeant Major.
  Following his military career, Mr. Howard demonstrates his dedication 
to community service as a Precinct Chair and Election Judge in Bell 
County and has been actively involved in legislative matters. He also 
serves as President of the Temple Gun Club where he provides leadership 
to encourage responsible firearm ownership and safety. Prior to moving 
to Belton, Mr. Howard served as a volunteer County Veteran Service 
Officer, helping veterans receive the assistance they earned through 
their service.
  Sergeant Major Jeffrey Scott Howard exemplifies the highest 
traditions of service. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his 
outstanding achievements and dedication to our Nation and community.
```
