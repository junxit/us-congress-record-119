# HONORING MERCEDES VIANA SCHLAPP AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 HONORING MERCEDES VIANA SCHLAPP AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN 
                          WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mercedes Viana 
Schlapp as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction 
award recipient.
  Mercedes Viana Schlapp is a political and policy expert, media 
commentator, and Senior Fellow at CPAC, where she manages policy 
centers addressing national and intentional issues. She helped launch 
the CPAC Center for Combating Human Trafficking, the CPAC Center for 
Combating Antisemitism, and CPAC Unidos, a Hispanic grassroots 
coalition.
  Alongside her husband, Matt, Mercedes develops international 
partnerships through CPAC conferences, grassroots organizing, and 
coalition building. Her work has included meetings with leaders in 
Argentina, Israel, El Salvador, Brazil, Mexico, and other nations, 
advancing freedom and America First policies.
  Mercedes co-hosts Newsmax's The Right Squad and CPAC Now: America 
Uncanceled. She previously served as a weekly host on WMAL's morning 
radio show.
  During the Trump administration, she served as Assistant to the 
President and Senior Advisor for Strategic Communications. She managed 
the communications team, advised on messaging, policy, and scheduling, 
and served as a White House spokeswoman. She coordinated communications 
and policy rollouts with White House offices and Cabinet officials.
  In 2001, Mercedes served as White House Director of Specialty Media, 
communicating in English and Spanish and developing strategies for the 
Office of Media Affairs.
  Her career reflects service in public policy, government 
communications, broadcasting, and building relationships across 
international communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
