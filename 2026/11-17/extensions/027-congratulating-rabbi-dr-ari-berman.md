# CONGRATULATING RABBI DR. ARI BERMAN

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
                  CONGRATULATING RABBI DR. ARI BERMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor Rabbi Dr. Ari Berman, President of Yeshiva University, and to 
congratulate him on the tremendous honor of delivering the opening 
prayer on the floor of the United States House of Representatives.
  Since becoming Yeshiva University's fifth president in 2017, Rabbi 
Berman has transformed the institution, expanded academic 
opportunities, launched more than 20 new graduate degree programs, and 
strengthened its commitment to academic excellence and moral 
leadership.
  His contributions extend far beyond YU. In the aftermath of October 
7, 2023, Rabbi Berman helped establish a coalition of more than 100 
university presidents standing together against terrorism and 
antisemitism. He has also led fellow university leaders on the March of 
the Living to Auschwitz, ensuring that the lessons of history are never 
forgotten.
  Rabbi Berman exemplifies the importance of faith, education, service, 
and moral courage. His leadership reminds us that higher education must 
do more than cultivate knowledge; it must also cultivate character and 
humanity
  As a Representative of the Bronx, home to a vibrant Jewish community 
and so many Yeshiva University students and alumni, I am particularly 
proud to recognize his contributions.
  I congratulate Rabbi Berman on this well-deserved honor and thank him 
for bringing his wisdom and faith to this chamber.
```
