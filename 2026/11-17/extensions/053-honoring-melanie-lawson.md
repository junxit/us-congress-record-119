# HONORING MELANIE LAWSON

**Speaking:** Menefee, Christian D. (M001245) [D-TX]

```
                        HONORING MELANIE LAWSON

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. CHRISTIAN D. MENEFEE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. MENEFEE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Melanie Lawson, an 
award-winning journalist and trusted voice of Houston, on the occasion 
of her induction into the Silver Circle at the 2026 Lone Star Emmy 
Awards. The Silver Circle is one of the highest honors bestowed by the 
National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences.
  A native Houstonian, Ms. Lawson grew up in Third Ward. She earned her 
undergraduate degree from Princeton University and a joint law and 
journalism degree from Columbia University, then spent three years 
practicing First Amendment law on Wall Street before returning to 
Houston.
  For more than 40 years at ABC13, Ms. Lawson anchored ``Live at 5'' 
and ``Eyewitness News at 11 a.m.`` and hosted ``Crossroads,'' a public 
affairs program on race, politics, faith, and community. She covered 
the election of the city's first Black mayor and first woman mayor, 
Hurricane Harvey, and the deadly Texas ice storm, and she sat down with 
presidents, poets, and icons from Maya Angelou to Aretha Franklin.
  Her work has earned her three Emmy Awards, the YWCA's Outstanding 
Women of the Year recognition, and lifetime achievement honors from the 
Houston Press Club, Houston Community College, Women in Film and 
Television, and the Houston Association of Black Journalists. In 2026, 
the National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences inducted Ms. 
Lawson into the Silver Circle, an honor reserved for television 
professionals with careers of 25 years or more.
  Ms. Lawson's service reaches far beyond the newsroom. She has served 
on Princeton University's Board of Trustees and has lent her time to 
the Houston Ballet, SHAPE Community Center, the Children's Defense 
Fund, Volunteer Houston, and the Houston Association of Black 
Journalists. In December 2025, she awarded several scholarships to the 
next generation of journalists, ensuring that young storytellers will 
have the same opportunity she was given.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas, I congratulate Melanie 
Lawson on her induction into the Silver Circle at the 2026 Lone Star 
Emmy Awards, commend her extraordinary career, and extend my deepest 
gratitude for more than four decades of trusted service to Houston.

                          ____________________
```
