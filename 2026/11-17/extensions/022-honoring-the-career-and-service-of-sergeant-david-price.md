# HONORING THE CAREER AND SERVICE OF SERGEANT DAVID PRICE

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
        HONORING THE CAREER AND SERVICE OF SERGEANT DAVID PRICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES R. BAIRD

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sergeant David Price. 
Sergeant Price is retiring from the Indiana State Police after 31 
years.
  Price began his career after graduating from the recruit academy in 
1995, assigned to LaGrange County in the Fort Wayne District. During 
his time with the ISP, he has served in the Jasper District, and 
Crawford, Perry, and Spencer Counties.
  Price served in many capacities throughout his career but is known 
for his time with the Indiana State Police Emergency Response Team, 
(SWAT), and serving as Detective and Investigative Squad Leader at the 
Jasper Post. He was also a founding member of the Indiana State Police 
Cyber Crime Unit, helping Indiana utilize new digital forensics with 
the increase of cybercrimes in the digital age.
  I thank Sergeant Price for his valued service to the Indiana State 
Police. I congratulate him on 31 years, and I wish him good luck in 
this next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
