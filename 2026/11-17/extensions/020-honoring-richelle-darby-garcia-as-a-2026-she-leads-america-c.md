# HONORING RICHELLE DARBY GARCIA AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 HONORING RICHELLE DARBY GARCIA AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN 
                          WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Richelle Darby 
Garcia as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction award 
recipient.
  Richelle Darby Garcia serves as Senior Prophet and ``Warship'' Leader 
at The Impact Center in Lawton, Oklahoma, alongside her husband, Ray 
Garcia. She disciples the worship team, cultivating devotion to God and 
music rooted in intimacy with the Holy Spirit.
  Of Oklahoma's Kiowa and Comanche Tribes, a direct descendant of 
Hunting Horse and the last scout of General Custer and Big Bow, 
Richelle is the daughter of LouAnn Tippeconnie Darby and Evangelist 
Geri Darby of the Darby Indian Singers, the first Native American 
gospel singers to appear on Hee Haw and the Grand Ole Opry. Continuing 
this musical and ministry legacy, Richelle received Native American 
Music Awards nominations in 2022 and 2024. Through songwriting, worship 
leadership, and travel with RezAmerica to reservations, she shares the 
gospel, encourages spiritual breakthrough, and brings hope to Native 
American communities.
  Her public service includes 23 years with Indian Health Services and 
work as Office Administrator for the Kiowa Tribe Legislature. She 
serves as Chaplain for the Kiowa Tribe Police Department, participates 
in the Kiowa Chapter of the Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and 
Indigenous People, and works as a tribal election worker.
  In 2026, Richelle became Co-Leader of She Leads Native America, 
furthering her commitment to honoring indigenous women, strengthening 
faith, and encouraging others to fulfill their God-given calling.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
