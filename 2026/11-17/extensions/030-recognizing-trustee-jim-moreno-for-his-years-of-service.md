# RECOGNIZING TRUSTEE JIM MORENO FOR HIS YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
        RECOGNIZING TRUSTEE JIM MORENO FOR HIS YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jim Moreno, a 
retiring Board of Trustee Member, committed student advocate, and a 
pillar of the higher education community here in Orange County.
  For the past two decades, Jim has served as a dedicated Trustee, 
where, under his leadership, he oversaw our local colleges grow and 
graduate or transfer over 165,000 students to universities or go on to 
work good-paying jobs that contribute to our communities back home. He 
never stopped believing in the power of education, and his efforts 
evidently reflect that.
  Jim is a long-time Orange County resident and proud product of the 
Community College system, which sparked his motivation to give back to 
the very communities that have shaped him. Jim understands the needs of 
our students and has worked diligently to support them and their 
families by streamlining their financial aid process. Notably, our 
local Veterans depend on financial aid to pursue their community 
college education.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Jim for all that he has 
done for our higher education system here in Orange County and across 
the California community. He left a legacy of transformative impact 
that championed student success, workforce development, and access to 
higher education. That work should never go unnoticed.
  I thank Jim for his continued commitment to our students, employees, 
and the communities he serves back home. Esther and I wish him the best 
in his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
