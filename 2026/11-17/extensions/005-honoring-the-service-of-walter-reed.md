# HONORING THE SERVICE OF WALTER REED

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

```
                  HONORING THE SERVICE OF WALTER REED

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM WALBERG

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to recognize Walter Reed 
of Clarklake as the Veteran of the Month for November and honor him for 
his distinguished service on behalf of a grateful Nation.
  Walter graduated from Michigan State University's ROTC program in 
1962 and went on to serve as an Armor Officer in the United States 
Army, ultimately attaining the rank of Major. His military career took 
him to Germany and later to Vietnam, where he completed two tours of 
duty. During his time in Vietnam, Walter also played an important role 
in coordinating logistics for the peace talks and negotiations. He 
concluded his active-duty military career at Fort Knox, Kentucky, 
before returning home to Michigan and continuing his service in the 
Army Reserves.
  For his bravery and contributions to our Nation, Walter was awarded 
the Purple Heart and 5 Bronze Star medals. Following his active-duty 
service, Walter continued supporting the United States military through 
his work at General Dynamics Land Systems, where he led the development 
and delivery of programs throughout the United States and Europe until 
his retirement. His commitment to our Nation's defense extended well 
beyond his years in uniform, reflecting a lifelong dedication to 
supporting the men and women who serve.
  Since returning to Michigan, Walter has remained deeply involved in 
his local community. He is an active member of the Lions Club, where he 
continues to support annual fundraising efforts to ensure the 
organization has the resources necessary to serve others. He is also 
involved with Vietnam Veterans in Jackson and regularly visits local 
high schools to share his military experiences with students, helping 
younger generations better understand the sacrifices of those who 
served during the Vietnam War. Additionally, Walter maintains close 
relationships with the members of the Army unit he commanded in 
Vietnam, continuing to attend their annual reunions and remain an 
active part of their lives. Today, he resides at his family's homestead 
in Clarklake, which has been in his family for more than 150 years.
  Walter Reed's faithful service and continued commitment to both our 
country's armed services and Michigan's local communities should serve 
as an inspiration to us all. May others know of my high regard for 
Walter, as well as my deep gratitude for his lasting contributions to 
the Nation we love.
```
