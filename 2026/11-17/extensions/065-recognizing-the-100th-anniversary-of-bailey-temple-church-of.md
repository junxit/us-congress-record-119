# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAILEY TEMPLE CHURCH OF GOD IN CHRIST

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAILEY TEMPLE CHURCH OF GOD IN 
                                 CHRIST

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and celebrate the 100th 
anniversary of Bailey Temple Church of God in Christ, a historic 
institution that has served as a beacon of faith and community in the 
City of Detroit.
  Founded in 1926, Bailey Temple Church of God in Christ has faithfully 
served generations of Detroit residents. For a century, Bailey Temple 
has helped shape the religious and cultural landscape of Detroit. The 
church is deeply connected to the legacy of gospel music through Dr. 
Mattie Moss Clark and the world-renowned Clark sisters: Karen, Jackie, 
Twinkie, and Denise. Bailey Temple has been home to respected faith 
leaders, including Bishop John Sheard, Bishop John Drew Sheard, and 
Bishop Ethan Sheard, whose ministries have strengthened the Church of 
God in Christ and communities in and beyond Detroit.
  Under the leadership of Elder Randall L. Greenwood, Bailey Temple 
Church of God in Christ continues its community service and engagement. 
I am honored to recognize Bailey Temple Church of God in Christ for 100 
years of faithful service to our community and its continuing 
dedication to strengthening our community for generations to come.
```
