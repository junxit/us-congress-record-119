# CELEBRATING THE TREMONT FIST BUMPING VETERANS

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
             CELEBRATING THE TREMONT FIST BUMPING VETERANS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Tremont Fist 
Bumping Veterans, a group of dedicated local veterans who have spent 
nearly a decade welcoming students to school while fostering a spirit 
of patriotism, service, and community throughout Tremont Illinois.
  The group began in August 2017, when Rick Otey and five fellow 
veterans started visiting Tremont Grade School every Thursday morning 
to welcome students with a friendly fist bump. Since then, the group 
has grown to 22 members and has expanded its weekly visits to include 
Tremont Middle School and High School. Comprised of primarily Vietnam-
era veterans, the group finds the warm reception they receive from the 
students especially meaningful, offering a sense of appreciation and 
belonging that many did not experience upon returning home from the 
war.
  Beyond their weekly visits, the Fist Bumping Veterans also sponsor 
Christmas toy drives, march in the homecoming parade, conduct flag 
retirement ceremonies, and give flag folding demonstrations at Tremont 
basketball games. Next year, they will help bring the Vietnam Traveling 
Memorial Wall to Tremont City Park, furthering their mission of 
bringing the community together and fostering patriotism and respect 
for America's veterans.
  In all they do, the Tremont Fist Bumping Veterans exemplify the 
patriotism, service, and community that make central Illinois such a 
special place. I am proud to recognize the Fist Bumping Veterans for 
their outstanding contributions to the Tremont community, and I thank 
them for their continued service to our Nation.
```
