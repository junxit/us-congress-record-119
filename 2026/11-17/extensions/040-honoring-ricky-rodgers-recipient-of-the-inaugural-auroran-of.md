# HONORING RICKY RODGERS, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
 HONORING RICKY RODGERS, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ricky Rodgers of 
Aurora, Illinois, recipient of the Auroran of Action Award presented by 
the Aurora Actionaires. For more than two decades, Ricky has dedicated 
himself to mentoring young people, celebrating African American 
heritage, and strengthening the community he proudly serves.
  In 1999, Ricky helped found African American Men of Unity (AAMOU), a 
nonprofit rooted in the belief that every young person deserves the 
opportunity to recognize their worth and fulfill their potential. As 
Executive Director, he has provided mentorship, positive role models, 
and programs focused on leadership, academic success, job readiness, 
and life skills. His work encourages young people to overcome 
challenges, believe in themselves, and strive for greatness.
  Ricky also recognizes the importance of bringing families together 
through shared traditions. For more than twenty-five years, AAMOU's 
Juneteenth and Kwanzaa celebrations have offered Aurora residents 
meaningful opportunities to honor African American history, celebrate 
cultural heritage, and pass cherished traditions to younger 
generations. These gatherings foster pride, connection, and a sense of 
belonging throughout our community.
  Through AAMOU's annual Back-to-School Bash, Ricky and fellow 
volunteers help children begin the school year with confidence. By 
providing school supplies, free haircuts, and other resources, they 
ease the burden on families while reminding students that their 
community believes in them and wants them to succeed.
  His contributions have been recognized by organizations including the 
NAACP, Quad County Urban League, East Aurora School District, and the 
City of Aurora. He has also been named a Cultural Champion by the Roots 
of Aurora. In 2014, AAMOU's Rites of Passage program was highlighted as 
a mentoring model for the country, and the organization received the 
Daily Herald's ABBE Award for Outstanding Not-for-Profit.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Ricky Rodgers for receiving the 
Auroran of Action Award. I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking 
Ricky for his steadfast dedication to Aurora and for the opportunities 
he continues to create for its young people and families.
```
