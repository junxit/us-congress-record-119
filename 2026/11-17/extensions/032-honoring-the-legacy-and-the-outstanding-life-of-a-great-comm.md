# HONORING THE LEGACY AND THE OUTSTANDING LIFE OF A GREAT COMMUNITY LEADER RAMCHAND GOHUMAL SADHWANI

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
   HONORING THE LEGACY AND THE OUTSTANDING LIFE OF A GREAT COMMUNITY 
                    LEADER RAMCHAND GOHUMAL SADHWANI

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
life of Mr. Ramchand Gohumal Sadhwani, born on December 12, 1935, in 
Sindh, British India, which is now a Province in Pakistan. He recently 
passed away on May 14, 2024. Upon his traveling to our country, Mr. 
Sadhwani faced many trials and tribulations during his lifetime. He did 
not let those factors slow him down, as hard work, determination, 
resilience, and many other qualities helped Mr. Sadhwani build a life 
filled with lots of love and purpose. He never failed to share with 
anyone he met throughout his lifetime.
  Mr. Sadhwani was preceded in death by his loving wife, Sajni 
Sadhwani; his daughter, Nanda Bulchandani; his two brothers, Murlidhar 
G. Sadhwani and Vashi Sadhwani; and his parents, Gohumal and Lilawanti 
Sadhwani.
  Mr. Sadhwani was a widower whose tenacity guided him while he raised 
his five children. His sacrifices and nurturing instilled important 
values within each of his children that paved the way for their own 
successes. His children witnessed and experienced his resilience, 
building a sense of inner strength. Mr. Sadhwani is survived by four of 
his children, Raju, Veena, Hoori, and Harish; his daughters-in-law, 
Rita and Nikita; his three sons-in-law, Shankar, Ishwar, and Pritam; 
and many grand and great-grandchildren. He also leaves a host of 
nieces, nephews, cousins, and friends, all inspired by his love, 
tenacity, and wisdom.
  Mr. Sadhwani made New Orleans, Louisiana; the 2nd Congressional 
District, his home for nearly 50 years. His children, grandchildren, 
great-grandchildren, as well as their close friends were showered by 
Mr. Sadhwani's affection, as he brought life, joy, and inspiration to 
those who had the fortune of being a part of his life. I am humbled to 
say

[[Page E1224]]

I too, was showered with love and wisdom. This further validates the 
need to memorialize Mr. Sadhwani as a kind, strong man with unwavering 
love for his family, loved ones, and those in his life. His legacy will 
forever live on through the countless lives he touched. I am honored to 
document this life well-lived.
```
