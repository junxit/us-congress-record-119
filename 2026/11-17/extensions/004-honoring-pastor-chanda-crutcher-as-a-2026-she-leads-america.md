# HONORING PASTOR CHANDA CRUTCHER AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 HONORING PASTOR CHANDA CRUTCHER AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN 
                          WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pastor Chanda 
Crutcher as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction 
award recipient.
  Pastor Chanda Crutcher is a ministry leader, social innovator, and 
civic servant dedicated to strengthening communities through faith, 
relationships, and purposeful service.
  A Baton Rouge native based in Huntsville, Alabama, Chanda serves as 
Founding Chief Visionary Officer of The Legacy Center, advancing aging 
services, advocacy, guardianship, and community care. Alabama's first 
Registered Guardian, she helped establish the state's first Advocacy 
Guardianship and Elder Mediation office in partnership with the Probate 
Court of Madison County.
  Appointed a Huntsville Housing Authority Commissioner by Mayor Tommy 
Battle, Chanda participates in the federal Choice Neighborhoods 
Initiative, supporting neighborhood transformation and generational 
stability.
  As Atlantic District Supervisor for The Foursquare Church, Chanda 
provides leadership across 21 states, approximately 300 congregations, 
and 1,500 credentialed ministers. The district's congregations worship 
in more than 13 languages, reflecting the diversity of the communities 
they serve. She also serves as a global partner liaison for Africa.
  Chanda's leadership is rooted in her relationship with Jesus Christ 
and the conviction that people matter more than programs or politics. 
Her work emphasizes relationships, dignity, and service that creates a 
legacy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
