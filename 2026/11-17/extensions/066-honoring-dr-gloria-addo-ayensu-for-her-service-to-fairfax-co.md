# HONORING DR. GLORIA ADDO-AYENSU FOR HER SERVICE TO FAIRFAX COUNTY

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
   HONORING DR. GLORIA ADDO-AYENSU FOR HER SERVICE TO FAIRFAX COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Gloria Addo-
Ayensu for her decades of service to public health in Fairfax County, 
Virginia.
  Dr. Addo-Ayensu joined the Fairfax County Health Department in 1999 
and became its director in 2003. For more than two decades,

[[Page E1233]]

she has worked to protect the health of our residents and strengthen 
the county's public health system.
   Under her leadership, the Health Department earned national 
accreditation, improved its emergency preparedness capabilities, and 
expanded partnerships to serve Fairfax County's diverse population. She 
championed health equity and worked to ensure that residents across our 
community could access the information and services they needed.
  Her expertise proved especially valuable during the COVID-19 
pandemic. As Fairfax County confronted an unprecedented public health 
emergency, Dr. Addo-Ayensu guided the county's response, coordinated 
with local and state partners, and helped residents navigate rapidly 
changing circumstances. Her steady guidance helped protect our 
community during a period of extraordinary uncertainty.
  I had the privilege of working closely with Dr. Addo-Ayensu during my 
service on the Fairfax County Board of Supervisors. I saw firsthand her 
thoughtful approach to difficult decisions, her dedication to our 
residents, and her commitment to the public health professionals who 
serve them.
  Her legacy extends beyond the emergencies she helped manage. She 
strengthened the institutions, partnerships, and workforce that Fairfax 
County will rely on for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Dr. 
Gloria Addo-Ayensu on her retirement and thanking her for her decades 
of distinguished service. Her work has made Fairfax County healthier, 
safer, and better prepared for the future. I wish her and her family 
all the best in this well-deserved next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
