# HONORING NANCY McDANIEL AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING NANCY McDANIEL AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF 
                              DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nancy McDaniel 
as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction award 
recipient.
  Nancy McDaniel serves as President and Chief Executive Officer of 
Aglow International, a global Christian organization serving women and 
men in more than 170 nations. Founded in 1967, Aglow connects 
communities through prayer, discipleship, leadership development, 
evangelism, and outreach.
  An experienced ministry leader, speaker, and author, Nancy is 
dedicated to equipping individuals to develop their gifts, strengthen 
their faith, and serve their communities. Her leadership emphasizes 
biblical principles, personal integrity, and cooperation among 
believers across cultural and national boundaries.
  From 1999 to 2025, Nancy served as Aglow International's Prayer 
Director, providing direction for its worldwide prayer movement and 
developing coordinated strategies supporting the organization's 
mission. Her service helped unite participants around shared 
commitments to faith, service, and the needs of communities worldwide.
  Having ministered in more than 50 nations, Nancy brings extensive 
international experience to her leadership. She is the author of 
Prayer: Heaven's Open Door, which explores prayer and a personal 
relationship with God.
  Her husband, Dave McDaniel directs Men of Aglow International. 
Together, they teach and minister internationally, encouraging 
reconciliation between men and women and their shared participation in 
Christian service.
  Nancy's career reflects a sustained commitment to spiritual 
leadership, developing others, and strengthening communities through 
faith and service.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.

                          ____________________
```
