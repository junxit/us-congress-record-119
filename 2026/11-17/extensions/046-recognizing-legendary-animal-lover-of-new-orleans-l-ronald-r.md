# RECOGNIZING LEGENDARY ANIMAL LOVER OF NEW ORLEANS L. RONALD "RON" FORMAN

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
  RECOGNIZING LEGENDARY ANIMAL LOVER OF NEW ORLEANS L. RONALD ``RON'' 
                                 FORMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a 
phenomenal New Orleans native son, veteran, a true cultural and 
environmental leader, the original zookeeper of Louisiana, and friend; 
it is with great joy that I recognize a New Orleans living legend, L. 
Ronald ``Ron'' Forman, as the City of New Orleans prepares to honor 
Forman for all his contributions to the cultural landscape of New 
Orleans and the entire state of Louisiana.
  Throughout the course of his lengthy career, Ron earned the deep 
respect and affection of New Orleanians--as a charismatic and beloved 
leader. However, prior to his leadership in nature conservancy, Ron 
served for two years in the United States Army, and his service to our 
Nation did not stop after his service in the Army.
  Ron began his career caring for animals in an equally impactful and 
humbler role. He began as an administrative analyst in 1972 and less 
than two years later he earned the position of Deputy Director of 
Audubon Park & Zoological Garden. Only four years later, in 1977, Ron 
took on a new challenge and his current title as President and Chief 
Executive Officer of the entire Audubon Nature Institute. Ever since, 
Ron has been a stalwart defender of our unique natural and cultural 
landscape.
  Under Ron's leadership who was charged with either closing the Zoo or 
``fixing it up,'' Ron helped lead the transformation of the Audubon Zoo 
into the Audubon Nature Institute, which includes a family of museums, 
parks, and state-of-the-art conservation initiatives dedicated to 
making a positive impact on nature and wildlife. The Audubon Nature 
Institute includes Audubon Zoo, Audubon Park in Uptown New Orleans, 
Audubon Aquarium and sectarium, the Woldenberg Riverfront Park in 
Downtown New Orleans, Audubon Louisiana Nature Center in New Orleans 
East, and Audubon Wilderness Park and Freeport--McMoRan Audubon Species 
Survival Center on the West Bank of New Orleans in the lower coast of 
Algiers, Audubon Aquarium Rescue, and Gulf United for Lasting Fisheries 
(G.U.L.F.).
  Ron's work does not stop; however, his passion for animals and 
Audubon Park began at the age of 10, when Ron took a job helping tie up 
the swan-shaped paddle boats in the Audubon Park lagoon. More recent 
examples demonstrating his love for our natural landscape include the 
multi-million dollar transformation of the Audubon Aquarium and Audubon 
Insectarium, and currently he steams forward on his work to create 
Governor Nicholls and Esplanade Wharves public parks that will open the 
Mississippi River as the front porch of the City of New Orleans with an 
additional two and a half miles of contiguous open park space.
  Additionally to his work in nature conservancy under the Audubon 
Nature Institute, Ron has shared his passion, skills, and knowledge to 
other boards and organizations, such as: Board of Directors for New 
Orleans & Company; former Chairman of Louisiana Stadium and Exposition 
District; served on the Executive Committee of the New Orleans 
Metropolitan Convention & Visitors Bureau; Board of Directors of the 
American Association of Museums and the Advisory Committee of Chimp 
Haven; was chairman of the Louisiana Nature Conservancy; worked as a 
partner with Marlin Perkins; and launched the Business Council of New 
Orleans and the River Region and Greater New Orleans, Inc.
  Ron has indeed worked to protect Louisiana's world-renowned natural 
landscape and he has received the awards to prove it, including Woodrow 
Wilson Award, Gambit Magazine's ``New Orleanian of the Year,'' New 
Orleans Magazine's ``Man of the Decade,'' Ella Brennan Lifetime 
Achievement in Hospitality Award, and a myriad of awards and honors.
  Ron has proven to have an unrivaled ability to raise funds and 
execute major projects

[[Page E1228]]

which have changed our city and state in incalculable beneficial ways. 
His strength through Katrina, an oil spill, and a global pandemic 
amongst many other crises has been an inspiration and a true testament 
to his resilience and in turn our city. Today, I rise to honor Forman's 
exemplary, inimitable, and unparalleled leadership in our city, state, 
region and country, which has significantly contributed to making New 
Orleans an international tourist destination, brought billions of 
dollars in economic impact to our community, earned national 
recognition, conducted groundbreaking scientific research, and had a 
profound conservation impact for our planet, our country and our city. 
I congratulate Ron on 52 years of dedicated and future thinking 
leadership; his work will be greatly missed but his impact will be felt 
for generations to come.
```
