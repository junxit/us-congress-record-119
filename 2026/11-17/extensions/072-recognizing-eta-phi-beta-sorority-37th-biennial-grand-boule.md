# RECOGNIZING ETA PHI BETA SORORITY 37TH BIENNIAL GRAND BOULE

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
      RECOGNIZING ETA PHI BETA SORORITY 37TH BIENNIAL GRAND BOULE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Eta Phi Beta for their 
37th Biennial Youth Conference in our beautiful community of Detroit.
  Eta Phi Beta Sorority, Incorporated was founded on October 4, 1942, 
by 11 exceptional women who attended the Lewis Business College in 
Detroit. The Sorority was later incorporated in 1943 and has since 
sustained great growth and expanded to multiple chapters spanning 
multiple different regions across the Nation. Eta Phi Beta has led 
numerous initiatives and centered its mission on helping its members 
pursue higher scholastic goals, develop personally, and serve through 
community service. Guided by its founding purpose, Eta Phi Beta 
Sorority has led communities through its efforts to empower women and 
uplift disadvantaged families. The Sorority has expanded its reach 
through impactful programs such as the ``Bee-Ettes'' youth group, which 
offers students opportunities to participate in personal development 
workshops, which include cross-generational mentorship with Sorority 
members. These efforts are critical in their work to make sure families 
are equipped with wraparound support to help them thrive.
  Today, Eta Phi Beta Sorority, Incorporated stands as a testament to 
the enduring power of leadership, compassion, and collective action. 
Its legacy is measured not only by its decades of service, but also by 
the countless lives it has touched and the opportunities it has created 
for future generations. As the Sorority continues its mission, all 
programs and projects remain consistent with the intention of its 
selfless motto: to do the work, ``Not for Ourselves, But for Others.'' 
The 12th Congressional District is proud to recognize Eta Phi Beta and 
wishes them well in their continued pursuits.
```
