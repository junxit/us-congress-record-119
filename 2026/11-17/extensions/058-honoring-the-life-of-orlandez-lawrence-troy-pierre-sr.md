# HONORING THE LIFE OF ORLANDEZ LAWRENCE "TROY" PIERRE, SR.

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
      HONORING THE LIFE OF ORLANDEZ LAWRENCE ``TROY'' PIERRE, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
of a phenomenal leader and friend, who was a fixture in Greater New 
Orleans' transit system for Jefferson Parish, Orlandez Lawrence 
``Troy'' Pierre, Sr. He was born on October 13, 1962, in New Orleans, 
Louisiana to the late Ernest J. Pierre, Sr. and Leola DeVore Harris.
  He passed away on Saturday, November 22, 2025. His journey was one of 
passion, dedication, and unwavering commitment to the betterment of 
society. His tireless efforts promulgating the soul and spirit of the 
Greater New Orleans area touched countless lives, as his memory will 
continue to inspire future generations. Orlandez was a proud 1980 
graduate of L.B. Landry High School and later attended Southern 
University A&M in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, where he was a member of the 
Southern University Marching Band (``Human Jukebox'').
  A dear friend and my proud social brother, we both served as members 
of the Jugs Social Club, Inc., proudly parading in the streets of 
Algiers during the annual Mardi Gras Season with the Krewe of NOMTOC, 
as he served as Parade Captain.
  Orlandez lived and breathed New Orleans. His contributions, although 
unseen, are essential to the culture of Greater New Orleans and the 
successes of its transportation network. Orlandez was a beacon of light 
in every endeavor he undertook. As is made evident by his service to 
his community, Orlandez was the epitome of a devoted husband, father, 
and grandfather, always putting the needs of his family first. He 
approached everything with grace, integrity, and determination to make 
a positive impact. Beyond his roles in transportation, Orlandez was 
deeply involved in his community. His compassion knew no bounds, and he 
approached every act of service with humility and empathy. Orlandez's 
dedication to his community was truly exemplary, his efforts made a 
tangible difference in the lives of so many, and he was a good friend 
who will be deeply missed.
  Orlandez leaves behind a legacy of love, service, and devotion. In 
his personal life, Orlandez was a source of strength and support to 
those closest to him, always offering a listening ear and a word of 
encouragement.

[[Page E1231]]

His care and generosity created a welcoming space for all who knew him, 
and his home was a place of laughter, love, and connection. Orlandez's 
ability to balance his many roles was a testament to his exceptional 
character, and his commitment to those he cared about. As we reflect on 
Orlandez's life, let us remember his as a role model for us all. His 
dedication to excellence, his commitment to service. and his unwavering 
compassion serve as an inspiration for us all to strive for a better 
world. His legacy will live on through the countless lives he touched 
and the positive impact he had on our community.
  May his memory be a blessing to us all.

                          ____________________
```
