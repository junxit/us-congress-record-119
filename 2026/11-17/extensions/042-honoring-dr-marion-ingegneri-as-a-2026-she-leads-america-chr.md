# HONORING DR. MARION INGEGNERI AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
  HONORING DR. MARION INGEGNERI AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN 
                          WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Marion 
Ingegneri, M.A., Th.D. (h.c.) as a 2026 She Leads America Christian 
Woman of Distinction award recipient.
  Dr. Marion Ingegneri is a visionary Christian leader, entrepreneur, 
revivalist, and strategist whose ministry has empowered women and 
leaders around the world.
  Ordained with the Foursquare Church in 1980, Marion later served as 
Vice Chair of its Board of Directors. She founded Grace North Church in 
Phoenix, Arizona, growing it from six people into a thriving ministry 
across three locations.
  As Global Director of the Day of Prayer for the Peace of Jerusalem 
with Eagles' Wings Ministries, she mobilizes prayer across nations. She 
is Founder and President of the Ministry Leader Network, equipping 
leaders through development and publishing opportunities. She also 
founded Essential Conferences for Women in 2010 and Women in Ministry 
Leadership Cohorts in 2014, initiatives that have helped thousands grow 
in leadership and purpose.
  Her influence extends throughout the Americas, Europe, Africa, the 
South Pacific, and the Middle East, including Israel. She authored 
Choose Life and serves as editor-in-chief and contributing author of 
Leading Clean. She earned a Master of Arts in Strategic Leadership from 
Life Pacific University and received an honorary Doctor of Theology 
from Life Point Christian University.
  Her honors include the B. Derone Robinson Leadership Excellence Award 
(2019), Life Pacific University's Kingdom Leadership Award (2026), and 
Foursquare's Harold Helms Living Legacy Award (2026). Her decades of 
service continue to inspire emerging generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
