# HONORING NICK MARTIN FOR HIS OUTSTANDING SERVICE

**Speaking:** DelBene, Suzan K. (D000617) [D-WA]

```
            HONORING NICK MARTIN FOR HIS OUTSTANDING SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SUZAN K. DelBENE

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. DelBENE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and thank Nick Martin 
for serving as my Communications Director for almost 7 years.
  Nick joined my office at the outset of the COVID-19 pandemic and 
jumped headfirst into navigating unprecedented challenges. He is 
incredibly skilled at handling all aspects of a comprehensive 
communications strategy, especially finding creative ways to tell 
stories that not enough people are paying attention to.
  Nick has been an exceptional leader helping to steer our office 
through many different chapters. From my time serving as chair of the 
New Democrat Coalition to leading the DCCC, he has been by my side as a 
reliable, consistent member of my team. I am deeply grateful for all 
the hard work, countless hours, and late nights he has spent advancing 
our shared goal of making this country a better place and fighting for 
the values we hold dear.
  Nick's tenure as my Communications Director has been a resounding 
success. He has always been a strong leader, not only guiding 
communications but serving as an invaluable member of Team DelBene. 
Although he and his baked goods will be greatly missed, I am beyond 
excited to see all of the wonderful things he will accomplish as a 
Chief of Staff for Representative Robert Menendez. I wish him all the 
best in this next chapter and will forever be rooting for his success.

                          ____________________
```
