# HONORING JEN LILLEY AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
  HONORING JEN LILLEY AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF 
                              DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jen Lilley as a 
2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction award recipient.
  Jen Lilley is an actress, singer, author, and advocate with more than 
75 film and television credits. Her work includes The Artist, winner of 
five Academy Awards; regular roles on Days of Our Lives and General 
Hospital; and starring performances in more than a dozen holiday films 
for Hallmark Channel and Great American Family. She portrays Gloria 
Stewart in the Jimmy Stewart biopic, Jimmy.
  Known for her versatility in drama and comedy, Jen has worked with 
artists including Clint Eastwood, Burt Reynolds, and Robin Williams. 
Her performances reflect a commitment to storytelling and connecting 
with audiences.
  An accomplished recording artist, Jen released a debut Christmas 
album that reached number one on Amazon's Jazz chart. She is the author 
of Wake Up Your Faith, an Amazon number one New Release before 
publication, and Called to Foster? An Honest Guide to Getting Started. 
Her writing offers encouragement and practical guidance informed by her 
faith and experience.
  Jen's service extends beyond entertainment to advocacy for children 
and families in crisis. Having fostered and adopted through the United 
States foster care system, she brings firsthand understanding to the 
needs of vulnerable children. Through nonprofit board service and 
ambassador roles, she supports organizations serving children and 
families.
  Her career and charitable work reflect a sustained commitment to 
creativity, compassion, faith, and strengthening communities through 
service.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
