# RECOGNIZING TERESA GRIMES

**Speaking:** Moore, Barry (M001212) [R-AL]

```
                       RECOGNIZING TERESA GRIMES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BARRY MOORE

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. MOORE of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and celebrate 
Teresa Grimes, the CEO of Washington County Hospital and Nursing Home 
in Chatom, Alabama, on her selection as Alabama's 2026 Community Star 
by the National Organization of State Offices of Rural Health. This 
national recognition honors a leader whose service reflects the 
dedication, compassion, and resourcefulness that sustain health care in 
rural America.
  Established in 1995, the National Organization of State Offices of 
Rural Health (NOSORH) supports the Nation's 50 State Offices of Rural 
Health in improving access to quality health care. NOSORH helps rural 
communities strengthen the institutions and services on which their 
residents depend through education, leadership development, and 
advocacy.
  NOSORH established an annual Community Star Recognition program to 
help recognize the unsung heroes in rural communities who work to 
provide health services to their residents. The program honors those 
that exemplify innovation, collaboration, and service to others.
  Teresa Grimes embodies these characteristics in her leadership of the 
Washington County Hospital and Nursing Home, an institution that has 
served thousands across the Southwest Alabama region since 1953. Under 
her guidance, the hospital has made important improvements in its 
facilities, including an expanded emergency department and new Legacy 
Hall which was dedicated in July 2022. These investments reflect a 
commitment to meeting the needs of patients and nursing home residents 
while strengthening the community's health care infrastructure.
  Ms. Grimes's service extends well beyond her own institution. She has 
represented southwest Alabama on the Alabama Hospital Association's 
Board of Trustees and served on the Alabama Board of Examiners of 
Nursing Home Administrators, bringing a rural perspective to statewide 
health care discussions. In 2026, she was inducted into the Washington 
County Hall of Fame, and the facility she leads has earned national 
recognition for quality of care.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Teresa 
Grimes on her selection as Alabama's 2026 Community Star. We thank her 
and the entire Washington County Hospital and Nursing Home team for 
their leadership, compassion, and commitment to the people of rural 
Alabama, and we wish them continued success.
```
