# CELEBRATING MISSION SAN JUAN CAPISTRANO'S 250TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
      CELEBRATING MISSION SAN JUAN CAPISTRANO'S 250TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 250th 
Anniversary of Mission San Juan Capistrano, the ``Jewel of the 
California Missions,'' for its beauty, history, and cultural 
significance.
  Mission San Juan Capistrano was established by Father Junipero Serra 
on November 1, 1776, as the seventh among 21 California missions 
founded by the Spanish, and is considered the birthplace of Orange 
County, California.
  Mission San Juan Capistrano was built by indigenous hands and was a 
center for agriculture, industry, education, and religion. The 
Mission's role has evolved over the centuries and today serves as a 
historical landmark reflecting California's multicultural history, 
including Native American, Spanish, Mexican, and European heritage.
  Mission San Juan Capistrano continues the ``bell ringing'' tradition 
every year on eight distinct dates, including on November 1st for 
Founder's Day, and is committed to promoting and nurturing an 
appreciation for history while providing a place of faith, renewal, and 
celebration.
  Mission San Juan Capistrano celebrates the miracle of the Swallows 
annually on March 19th, St. Joseph's Day, welcoming visitors from 
around the world to witness the migration of the swallow from Goya, 
Argentina, to San Juan Capistrano.
  As the Mission commemorates its semiquincentennial, I commemorate the 
Mission Preservation Foundation for their work to preserve Mission San 
Juan Capistrano's historic and cultural heritage for generations to 
come.
```
