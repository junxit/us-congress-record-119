# HONORING THE SERVICE OF MADELEINE CHANDLER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
               HONORING THE SERVICE OF MADELEINE CHANDLER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Madeleine Chandler for her 
extraordinary life of service and unwavering dedication to her family 
and community.
  Born in Normandy, France, on July 20, 1924, Madeleine overcame 
tremendous hardship during her childhood and served as a nurse caring 
for wounded soldiers during World War II. During this unprecedented 
work her compassion and strength brought comfort to all she encountered 
and was a beacon of light in such difficult times. After the war, 
Madeleine married United States Army Sergeant Bert Chandler came to the 
United States as one of the many French war brides seeking to build a 
new life. She became a proud United States citizen in 1949 and has 
called Redford, Michigan home for more than seven decades.
  Throughout her life, Madeleine devoted herself to raising her family, 
serving as a Girl Scouts leader, supporting local charitable efforts, 
and giving generously to children and families in need through 
countless donations of clothing, toys, and other essentials.
  At 102 years old, Madeleine continues to inspire everyone she meets 
with her kindness and joyful spirit. Her remarkable journey stands as a 
testament to courage and perseverance. The 12th Congressional district 
is honored to recognize Madeleine Chandler for her many years of 
service and wish her continued health, happiness, and the love of her 
family for years to come.
```
