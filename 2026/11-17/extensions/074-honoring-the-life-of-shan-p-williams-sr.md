# HONORING THE LIFE OF SHAN P. WILLIAMS, SR.

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE OF SHAN P. WILLIAMS, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
of a phenomenal leader and friend, who was a fixture in New Orleans' 
educational system for decades, Shan P. Williams, Sr. Born on October 
1, 1948, in New Orleans, Louisiana, Shan's journey was one of passion, 
dedication, and unwavering commitment to the betterment of society. He 
passed away on November 1, 2025, in New Orleans, leaving behind a 
legacy of love, service, and devotion. His tireless efforts and 
compassionate spirit touched countless lives, and his memory will 
continue to inspire future generations. Shan faithfully served as 
President of the Professional Administrators of New Orleans Public 
Schools, Inc., and the Orleans Parish School Board as a human resources 
specialist, interim athletic director, and principal. His 
contributions, although often unseen, are essential to New Orleans' 
educational fabric, learning environment, and culture.
  Shan was a beacon of light in every endeavor he undertook. He was a 
proud member of both Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc., the Zulu Social 
Aid & Pleasure Club, and honored to serve as Grand Marshall of NOMTOC 
in 2023. Shan was the epitome of a devoted husband, father, and 
grandfather, always putting the needs of his family first. He 
approached everything with grace, integrity, and determination to make 
a positive impact. Beyond his roles in education, Shan was deeply 
involved in his community. His compassion knew no bounds, and he 
approached every act of service with humility and empathy. Shan's 
dedication to his community was truly exemplary, and his efforts made a 
tangible difference in the lives of so many.
  In his personal life, Shan was a source of strength and support to 
those closest to him, always offering a listening ear and a word of 
encouragement. His care and generosity created a welcoming space for 
all who knew him, and his home was a place of laughter, love, and 
connection. Shan's ability to balance his many roles was a testament to 
his exceptional character, and his commitment to those he cared about. 
As we reflect on Shan's life, let us remember his as a role model for 
us all. His dedication to excellence, his commitment to service, and 
his unwavering compassion serve as an inspiration for us all to strive 
for a better world. His legacy will live on through the countless lives 
he touched and the positive impact he had on our community.
  May his memory be a blessing to us all.

                          ____________________
```
