# RECOGNIZING KATHY ERVIN

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING KATHY ERVIN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ms. Kathy Ervin 
for being awarded the 2026 Cattlewoman of the Year by the Kern County 
Cattlewomen's Association.
  For decades, Kathy Ervin has been an integral part of the California 
cattle industry. She first began running her own grazing operation in 
Glennville, California in 1993. Since then, she has helped dozens of 
local cattle ranchers with feeding and branding, acting as a mentor to 
so many in the region. Her love of animals has been with her since 
childhood when she learned to ride, harness, and drive a team of burros 
in California's Central Valley. Later, she channeled this passion into 
her work with the Kern County Cattlewomen's Association.
  As a member of the Board of Directors for the Kern County 
Cattlewomen, Kathy has helped with dozens of events, programs, and 
initiatives. Whether it was planning community farm events or setting 
up for the annual rodeo, she has devoted her time to helping the people 
of Kern County engage with the cattle industry. She has brought 
knowledge, skill, and experience to the Board, and she even serves as a 
delegate for the nationwide Cattlewomen's Association.
  Kathy's service extends far beyond the Cattlewomen's Association. She 
served in local California hospitals and worked to support children and 
families as an Investigating Family Support Officer for the Kern County 
District Attorney's Office for over 25 years. She was also a Reserve 
Deputy Sheriff for 13 years, serving in the mounted unit for many of 
them. Since 1981, she has also devoted much of her time to the 
Greenhorn Mountains Veterans Association alongside her husband, Dennis.
  Today, Kathy lives on a small ranch with her horses, chickens, 
cattle, and her dog, Jack. She continues to serve her community in many 
different roles, and her work throughout the community is a model of 
public service.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in congratulating Ms. Kathy Ervin on being named Kern County's 
2026 Cattlewoman of the Year. Her dedication to agriculture, education, 
and service to the community embodies the values that define the 
Central Valley.
```
