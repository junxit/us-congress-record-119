# RECOGNIZING DAVID GREENGRASS FOR HIS DISTINGUISHED SERVICE TO THE U.S. HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

```
RECOGNIZING DAVID GREENGRASS FOR HIS DISTINGUISHED SERVICE TO THE U.S. 
                        HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. NADLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank David Greengrass for 
his more than twenty years of outstanding service to the House of 
Representatives and the House Judiciary Committee.
  David has a long and distinguished career working on Capitol Hill. He 
has a sterling reputation as a trusted advisor, a clear and articulate 
writer, and an expert in parliamentary procedure.
  David is currently the Parliamentarian of the House Judiciary 
Committee Democratic Staff, and my Senior Counsel serving in my office 
as my de facto Legislative Director.
  During David's junior year at Wesleyan University, he spent a 
semester in Washington, D.C. through American University. As part of 
his program, he interned in my office--a natural fit since he was a 
constituent, having grown up on the Upper West Side of Manhattan.
  His hard work and positive attitude impressed my staff so much that 
after he graduated in 1998, he was asked to return to my office in a 
full-time position as Legislative Correspondent. His second day on the 
job coincided with the delivery of Ken Starr's Special Counsel report, 
which launched the drive to impeach President Bill Clinton. The volume 
of mail and phone calls received during that period was extraordinary, 
but David managed to keep us on track responding to constituents about 
impeachment and any number of other issues of importance to my 
district.
  David later rose to become a Legislative Assistant, covering a broad 
portfolio of issues, including health care and issues related to 
veterans and senior citizens, among others. Among his notable 
successes, he led the efforts to increase funding for the Housing 
Opportunities for Persons with AIDS, or HOPWA, program, through 
bipartisan sign-on letters, negotiations with the Appropriations 
Committee, and by passing several amendments on the House Floor, even 
while in the Minority. He also handled arts issues, which is 
particularly important to my district, home to the Broadway theater 
community, numerous world-class museums, a thriving film and television 
industry, and many prominent visual arts galleries.
  After the devastating attacks of September 11th, David worked 
directly on our efforts to help rebuild New York, including 
participating in early discussions on creating a memorial to the lives 
lost on 9/11 and working to ensure that small businesses and residents 
in Lower Manhattan who received federal financial assistance in the 
months after the attacks were treated fairly under the tax laws. He was 
also a key member of our appropriations team and helped secure millions 
of dollars for local community projects in my district.
  In 2004, David left my office to attend American University's 
Washington College of Law, where he earned his J.D. After law school, 
David worked as an Associate at Clifford Chance before returning to the 
Hill in 2009 as Senior Legislative Assistant, Counsel, and eventually 
Legislative Director to Congressman Steve Cohen. During his more than 
five years in the office, David handled a busy legislative portfolio 
focused primarily on Congressman Cohen's work related to the Judiciary 
Committee. Much of his work involved criminal justice matters, with an 
emphasis on reducing racial disparities, including legislation he 
drafted to provide for expungement of certain non-violent offenses. 
David also led Congressman Cohen's efforts to end the War on Drugs and 
to reform the Nation's marijuana laws.
  In 2015, when I became the Ranking Member of the Judiciary 
Committee's Subcommittee on Courts, Intellectual Property,

[[Page E1221]]

and the Internet, I had the opportunity to bring David back to my 
personal office and to the Judiciary Committee staff, where he 
continues to serve today. As Democratic Counsel to me and to Full 
Committee Ranking Member John Conyers on the Subcommittee, David 
prepared hearings and markups on a range of intellectual property 
matters and a variety of issues related to the federal courts. Among 
the major efforts he was involved in was legislation to reform the 
patent litigation system and enactment of the Music Modernization Act 
and the Defend Trade Secrets Act.
  While serving as Counsel on the Subcommittee, David simultaneously 
worked in my personal office handling all other matters related to the 
Judiciary Committee and effortlessly balanced these two demanding 
roles.
  David played a major role in helping to elect me Ranking Member and 
then Chairman of the House Judiciary Committee in 2017 and 2019 
respectively. Once I was elected Ranking Member, I promoted him to 
Senior Counsel and eventually Parliamentarian of the Judiciary 
Committee.
  As Senior Counsel, David coordinated preparation of hearings, 
markups, and floor activity across the entire Committee, including 
editing memos, arranging briefings, and managing the paper flow to 
ensure that I was always prepared. He also served as a liaison to my 
personal office and ensured that the interests of my district were 
always represented in the Committee. As part of this role, David edited 
or drafted nearly every statement that I delivered in Committee or on 
the House Floor on Judiciary matters. This could number up to a dozen 
in any given week, but I always trusted that by the time a draft 
statement reached my desk I would barely need to add a comma because 
David understands my voice so well.
  As any regular viewer of C-SPAN can tell you, David has been by my 
side (or right behind me) for hundreds of hearings and markups over the 
years, helping to manage--and even choreograph--the proceedings so that 
they ran smoothly. In that time, he became an expert in parliamentary 
procedure, knowing how to help defuse a particularly tense debate or to 
ensure that both the Majority and the Minority were treated fairly 
under the rules. That is why I added the role of Parliamentarian to his 
responsibilities, and he continues to serve in that role today under 
Ranking Member Raskin.
  David has a profound understanding of the art of the possible. He 
knows when to fight and when to let it go. He has been by my side 
during many difficult markups in Committee. He understands the rules, 
knows what is fair, and makes sure everyone gets heard. He is deeply 
committed to democratic values and policies, and, at the same time, he 
vigorously defends the rights of Members of the other party to express 
their views. He deeply believes that if we properly follow the rules, 
we can ensure that everyone is treated fairly.
  David is the ultimate reasonable man. He is levelheaded, rational, 
logical, and he can clearly explain both sides of every issue. This is 
a true asset, because I know when I listen to David, I am getting the 
full picture of the issue. He is always well-prepared and he helps me 
understand not just the merits of the issue, but also where the other 
side stands. This leads not to knee jerk reactions and quick decisions 
but carefully considered positions and decisions that stand the test of 
time.
  David is an absolute workhorse. He powers through 4-hour full 
committee hearings and 10+ hour markups with poise and professionalism. 
He writes statements and legal memos late into the night and ensures 
that every Judiciary Committee statement I make is written in my voice 
with my values and beliefs properly represented. I trust him implicitly 
and am proud to have worked with him so closely on Judiciary Committee 
matters since I first became the Ranking Member in 2017.
  David stepped up in my final year in the House to become my senior 
legislative counsel/legislative director. He also works closely with my 
staff on the Subcommittee on the Administrative State, Regulatory 
Reform, and Antitrust to advance my work as Ranking Member of the 
Subcommittee taking on mega-mergers and preserving competition, 
protecting consumers against corporate greed, and fighting against 
efforts to dismantle the regulatory process.
  Despite a demanding workload, David has always made time for the 
things most important to him in life, such as the New York Yankees, the 
Capitol Hill Chorale, in which he has sung for more than 25 years, and 
of course, his friends and family.
   I would particularly like to thank his wife, Jessica Reimelt, for 
supporting David so steadfastly over his decades of service. Twenty 
years of working on Capitol Hill translates into thousands of late 
nights, hundreds of missed family dinners, and way too much time spent 
away from family. Jessica has supported David as he has pursued a 
career in public service trying to make the world a better place for 
millions of people all over the country. I also want to thank David's 
son, Ben Greengrass, for sharing his father with the Congress, the 
Committee, and the country. David shared with our staff many, many 
memories of his life growing up in D.C., going to school, and playing 
sports. David's love for his family is as profound as it is obvious to 
everyone who knows David well.
  As my career comes to an end on Capitol Hill, I wish David the best 
of luck in his future endeavors and thank him for his service to me, to 
the Committee, to the Congress, to New Yorkers, and to the Nation.
```
