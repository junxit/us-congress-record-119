# RECOGNIZING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF EAST BRUNSWICK FAST BREAK BASKETBALL

**Speaking:** Watson Coleman, Bonnie (W000822) [D-NJ]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF EAST BRUNSWICK FAST BREAK 
                               BASKETBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BONNIE WATSON COLEMAN

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. WATSON COLEMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
congratulate East Brunswick Fast Break Basketball as it celebrates 30 
years of dedication to youth athletics, sportsmanship, and community 
service.
  For three decades, this outstanding organization has provided young 
athletes throughout East Brunswick and the surrounding communities with 
opportunities to develop their athletic abilities, build confidence, 
learn the value of teamwork, and experience the lifelong benefits of 
being part of something greater than themselves.
  Founded 30 years ago by Matthew Korten and Todd Levy, East Brunswick 
Fast Break Basketball was built on a vision of creating a positive and 
inclusive environment where local youth could learn the fundamentals of 
basketball, develop lasting friendships, and embrace the values of 
leadership, discipline, and sportsmanship both on and off the court.
  What began as a local initiative by two dedicated community leaders 
has grown into one of Middlesex County's most valued youth sports 
organizations. Over the past three decades, Fast Break has served 
thousands of young athletes, made possible by generations of dedicated 
volunteer administrators, coaches, referees, families, and community 
supporters who have generously contributed their time and talents.
  The organization's commitment to inclusion and service extends beyond 
traditional youth basketball through its Fast Break Buddy Ball program, 
which provides children and young

[[Page E1230]]

adults with disabilities the opportunity to experience the joy of 
basketball, develop their abilities, and become part of a supportive 
team. Together, Fast Break Basketball and Fast Break Buddy Ball have 
earned numerous awards and recognition for their outstanding 
programming and dedication to volunteerism.
  Through their vision, leadership, and unwavering commitment to youth 
development, Matthew Korten, Todd Levy, and the entire Fast Break 
family have made an enduring contribution to the East Brunswick 
community. Their legacy extends far beyond the basketball court, 
touching the lives of thousands of young people and strengthening the 
community they proudly serve.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Matthew 
Korten and Todd Levy for their vision and dedication in founding East 
Brunswick Fast Break Basketball, and congratulating the entire Fast 
Break organization on 30 remarkable years of service, leadership, and 
commitment to our youth and community.
```
