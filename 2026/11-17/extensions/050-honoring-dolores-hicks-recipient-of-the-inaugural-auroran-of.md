# HONORING DOLORES HICKS, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
 HONORING DOLORES HICKS, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dolores Hicks of 
Aurora, Illinois, recipient of the Auroran of Action Award presented by 
the Aurora Actionaires. She is a trailblazer, healthcare professional, 
and devoted public servant whose compassion, strength, and commitment 
to others have made a lasting difference in our community. 
Affectionately known as ``Mother Hicks,'' she has spent more than five 
decades caring for others, breaking barriers, and helping make Aurora a 
better place for generations of families.
  In 1969, Mrs. Hicks made history by becoming the first Black nurse at 
what is now Mercy Medical Center in Aurora. At a time when 
opportunities for African American healthcare professionals were 
limited, she helped pave the way for those who would follow. During her 
25 years at Mercy Hospital and more than four decades in healthcare, 
including her service with VNA Health Care and CCS Medical, she 
provided compassionate care to countless patients and families. Her 
career is a testament not only to her professional dedication, but also 
to her courage, perseverance, and genuine concern for the well-being of 
others.
  Beyond her historic career in nursing, Mrs. Hicks has been a pillar 
of community leadership and civic progress. As an elected Trustee for 
Aurora Township, she has been a trusted voice for residents, advocating 
for working families, senior citizens, and young people. In this role, 
she chairs the Aurora Township Senior Advisory Committee and serves on 
the Senior Services Advisory Council, using her platform to champion 
the needs of older adults. Her dedication to the next generation is 
equally profound, demonstrated by her service as an appointed member of 
the Township Youth Advisory Board. Whether helping someone navigate a 
difficult situation, offering encouragement, or standing up for those 
in

[[Page E1229]]

need, she has approached her responsibilities with the same compassion 
and determination that defined her nursing career.
  Her unwavering commitment to civic duty and social progress has 
earned her numerous local accolades, including a Kane County Black 
History Month proclamation alongside her daughter, Dr. Peggy Hicks; the 
Mayor's Influence and Excellence Award in recognition of outstanding 
community advocacy, civic leadership, and community impact; an honorary 
Street Dedication designating the section of North Park Avenue as 
``Dolores Hicks Way''; and a City of Aurora Proclamation designating 
her date of birth as an official day of recognition within the 
municipality.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Dolores Hicks on receiving the 
Auroran of Action Award for her exceptional civic contributions, 
community empowerment efforts, and outstanding personal dedication to 
the residents of Aurora. I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking 
Dolores for her lifetime of dedication to the people of Aurora. She 
embodies the very best of the American spirit: resilient, 
compassionate, and deeply committed to the service of others.
```
