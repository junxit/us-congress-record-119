# HONORING CHANCELLOR JOHN SPENCER SHARP

**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

```
                 HONORING CHANCELLOR JOHN SPENCER SHARP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Chancellor John 
Spencer Sharp, a distinguished public servant, respected leader in 
higher education, and proud Texan whose decades of service have helped 
shape the future of our great state.
  A graduate of Texas A&M University, Sharp began his career in public 
service with a commitment to serving the people of Texas. In 1979, he 
was elected to the Texas House of Representatives, where he served 
until 1987. He continued his public service as a member of the Texas 
Railroad Commission from 1987 to 1991, before being elected Comptroller 
of Public Accounts of Texas in 1991.
  As Comptroller, Sharp served until 1999, overseeing the state's 
finances and promoting responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars. 
Following his time in elected office, he continued contributing to 
Texas through the private sector, becoming a principal in the Austin 
office of Ryan & Company. In 2005, he was appointed chairman of the 
Texas Tax Reform Commission, where he helped examine and improve the 
state's tax structure.
  In 2011, Sharp was appointed the 14th Chancellor of the Texas A&M 
University System. Over the next 14 years, he led one of the Nation's 
largest university systems, advancing its commitment to higher 
education, research, innovation, and public service. Under his 
leadership, the system continued to grow and expand opportunities for 
students while strengthening its contributions to communities 
throughout Texas. He retired from the chancellorship in 2025, leaving 
behind a lasting legacy of leadership and progress.
  Throughout his career, Sharp has demonstrated an unwavering 
commitment to public service and the people of Texas. Whether serving 
in the Legislature, overseeing state finances, shaping tax policy, or 
leading one of our state's most respected university systems, he has 
worked to strengthen Texas and create opportunities for future 
generations.
  His decades of dedication have most recently been recognized with the 
prestigious Stewardship of Texas Values Award, honoring his leadership 
and commitment to the principles and traditions that make Texas strong. 
This recognition is a fitting tribute to a lifetime of service to our 
state and its people.
  Mr. Speaker, John Sharp's career reflects the best of public service. 
His leadership, dedication, and contributions to Texas government and 
higher education have left a lasting impact on our state. I 
congratulate him on receiving the Stewardship of Texas Values Award and 
thank him for his many years of service to the people of Texas.
```
