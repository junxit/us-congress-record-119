# RECOGNIZING STERLING COLE, JR.

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING STERLING COLE, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Sterling 
Cole, Jr. on being awarded the 2026 Cattleman of the Year by the Kern 
County Cattlemen's Association.
  Born into a long line of cattleman in Bakersfield, California, 
Sterling developed a passion for cattle ranching at an early age. He 
was raised in the agricultural community of Delano, California, where 
he learned the business from his father. As a student, he joined Future 
Farmers of America and raised 40 steers. The student project earned him 
FFA Farmer of the Year before he graduated from Bakersfield College.
  Dedication to public service is a key characteristic of the Cole 
family. Sterling's grandfather helped lead Delano as one of its first 
city councilmen when the city was incorporated in 1915. His father 
served as mayor and his mother also served as mayor pro tem. Sterling 
has followed his family's example and has been a strong supporter of 
local organizations, including youth initiatives like 4-H and the Club 
of Arroyo Grande. He is also involved in the community as a member of 
the Moose Club and a decades-long supporter of the Delano Elks Club.
  Sterling's support for his community has not gone unnoticed. In 2026, 
the Delano Harvest Holidays Association named Sterling and his wife, 
Janie, as Agriculture Grand Marshals. Today, Sterling still works in 
the business he entered as a young man. He took the family ranch and 
doubled its size and herd, strengthening the family's legacy and 
Delano's agricultural economy
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in congratulating Mr. Sterling Cole, Jr. on being named Kern 
County's 2026 Cattleman of the Year. He has been responsible for 
feeding countless families, training young farmers, and serving his 
community. Kern County is stronger because of his dedication to his 
community, and I wish him continued success.
```
