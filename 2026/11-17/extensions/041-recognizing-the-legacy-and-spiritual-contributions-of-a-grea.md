# RECOGNIZING THE LEGACY AND SPIRITUAL CONTRIBUTIONS OF A GREAT COMMUNITY LEADER, BISHOP J. DOUGLAS WILEY

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
RECOGNIZING THE LEGACY AND SPIRITUAL CONTRIBUTIONS OF A GREAT COMMUNITY 
                    LEADER, BISHOP J. DOUGLAS WILEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr, Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding gentleman and New Orleans icon, Bishop J. Douglas Wiley. He 
was born in Detroit, Michigan. Bishop Wiley was born into a Christian 
home and heard the call to preach the Word of God at the age of 7.
  He took an active part in school activities and student leadership 
and won numerous awards. In 1979, he graduated from John Wesley College 
with a bachelor's in arts theology.
  In 1981, Bishop J. Douglas Wiley was named Senior Pastor of Greater 
Morning Star Missionary Baptist Church in Mount Clemens, Michigan. In 
recognition of his achievements

[[Page E1226]]

at this Church, the Union Baptist Theological Seminary of Birmingham, 
Alabama, bestowed upon Pastor Wiley, the Doctor of Divinity Degree in 
1983.
  After 12 years at the Greater Morning Star Church, Bishop and Mrs. 
Lisa Wiley began the Life Center Cathedral in New Orleans, Louisiana. 
Over the years the Church has been instrumental in establishing 32 
ministries that reach out to all aspects of the Greater New Orleans 
Community, including Men's and Women's Ministries, witnessing teams, 
youth camps, tutorial programs, clothing and food distribution, and 
more.
  In January 1994, Pastor Wiley was consecrated to the office of Bishop 
in the Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International, where he 
served as executive treasurer for 17 years. Bishop Wiley lives in the 
New Orleans Area with his wife Elder Lisa. They have three children and 
three grandchildren.
  The Greater New Orleans Community is blessed to have Bishop Wiley as 
a spiritual leader to so many. I am proud to call him my friend for 
over 25 years. What a blessing to have a spiritual friend, a Bishop, 
and prayer warrior throughout my political career. I am forever 
grateful for his friendship, love, and guidance as I serve the 
Constituents of Louisiana's 2nd. I offer him a sincere, heartfelt thank 
you, for all that he does in spiritual leadership, and may he see many 
more years serving individuals across the country.
```
