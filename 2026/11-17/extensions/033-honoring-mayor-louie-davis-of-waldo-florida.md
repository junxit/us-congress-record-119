# HONORING MAYOR LOUIE DAVIS OF WALDO, FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
              HONORING MAYOR LOUIE DAVIS OF WALDO, FLORIDA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Louie Davis, who is 
stepping down as Mayor of Waldo, Florida, after more than four decades 
in elected office.
  Louie's life of public service began in 1969, when he joined the 
Waldo area fire department as a volunteer. Fifteen years later, the 
people of Waldo asked him to serve in a new way. They elected him in 
1984, and he has served as a council member and mayor ever since. The 
Florida League of Cities has honored him for that remarkable record.
  Waldo may be small, yet under Louie its voice carried well beyond the 
city limits. He brought Waldo's priorities to Tallahassee and to 
Washington, and he chaired the North Central Florida Regional Planning 
Council. The relationships he cultivated with state legislators saved 
Waldo real money. In a town like Waldo, that is what good stewardship 
looks like.
  When Louie announced he was stepping down, he gave the credit to the 
people of Waldo. ``We are not a rich town,'' he said, ``but we have a 
lot of hard-working citizens.'' Louie has always been one of them. Our 
Republic draws its strength from towns like Waldo and from the 
neighbors who step forward when their town needs them. Louie first 
stepped forward with the fire department. For 57 years, Waldo has been 
able to count on him.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Mayor Louie 
Davis for a lifetime of service to Waldo. On behalf of Florida's 3rd 
Congressional District, I thank Louie. Waldo is stronger for every year 
he gave it, and I wish him the very best in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
