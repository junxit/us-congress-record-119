# CELEBRATING DOROTHY L. VINSON'S 90TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
             CELEBRATING DOROTHY L. VINSON'S 90TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the incredible life of 
Ms. Dorothy L. Vinson as she turns 90 years old.
  Born in Georgia, Dorothy L. Vinson moved to Detroit, Michigan, more 
than 55 years ago, beginning a lifelong journey rooted in resilience, 
family, and community. Detroit became the vital setting where she built 
her enduring legacy as a devoted mother, grandmother, and community 
pillar. Professionally, she dedicated decades of service as a 
seamstress for General Motors (GM) until her retirement, demonstrating 
a strong work ethic and perseverance. Her loyalty and discipline led to 
quiet yet significant contributions to one of America's most iconic 
industries. Known as a kind-hearted giver, Ms. Vinson embodies 
compassion and selflessness.
  The 12th Congressional District proudly honors Ms. Dorothy L. Vinson 
as she celebrates her 90th birthday. We extend our deepest gratitude 
for her vision and courage, recognizing a life built on resilience, 
family, and community. Her legacy of love, discipline, and generosity 
continues to inspire everyone around her.
```
