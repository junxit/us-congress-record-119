# RECOGNIZING WOODY LARSON, 2026 SUNBELT AG EXPO SOUTHEASTERN FARMER OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
 RECOGNIZING WOODY LARSON, 2026 SUNBELT AG EXPO SOUTHEASTERN FARMER OF 
                                THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Woody Larson of 
Okeechobee, Florida, the 2026 Sunbelt Ag Expo Southeastern Farmer of 
the Year.
  Woody bested state winners from six other Southeastern states. In 
2005, his father, Red, won the same honor. Twenty-one years apart, Red 
and Woody became the first father and son to earn the Southeastern 
title.
  Red bought his first cow with money from his paper route. After 
flying in World War II, he started a small dairy on rented land, and 
Woody grew up at his father's side. After earning a dairy science 
degree at the University of Florida in 1973, Woody came home for good. 
By the late 1980s, under Woody's management, Red's small dairy had 
grown to more than 12,000 milking cows.
  In 1987, the state gave Okeechobee's dairy farmers a hard choice: 
clean up or leave. Many took a buyout and left. The Larsons stayed and 
got to work. Woody brought new water quality practices to his farms and 
helped write Florida's dairy water quality manual, giving other dairy 
farmers a path to follow.
  Woody's leadership reached well beyond his own fence line. He helped 
create a dairy check-off that has generated more than $9 million for 
research and youth programs at the University of Florida, back home in 
Gainesville. For more than 50 years, he has provided dairy calves to 
young 4-H and FFA exhibitors, cultivating the next generation of 
Florida agriculture one calf at a time.
  In 1972, Woody married his high school sweetheart, Grace. Together 
they founded Family Tree Enterprises so their children, Travis, Jacob, 
and Ginger, could carry the family's legacy forward. Woody's motto is 
``Stay and Do.'' He stayed, and he did.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Woody Larson and the 
entire Larson family on an honor two generations in the making.
```
