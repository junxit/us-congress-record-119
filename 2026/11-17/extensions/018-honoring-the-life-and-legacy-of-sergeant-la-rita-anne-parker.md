# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SERGEANT LA RITA ANNE PARKER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
      HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SERGEANT LA RITA ANNE PARKER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of 
Sergeant La Rita Anne Parker, as her family, loved ones, and community 
gather in both grief and profound gratitude for the blessing of her 
time with us.
  Sergeant La Rita Anne Parker proudly served our Nation in the United 
States Army for over 11 years, earning numerous distinctions that 
reflected her unwavering dedication,

[[Page E1220]]

and discipline. Her commitment to service did not end when she hung up 
her uniform. She went on to serve another 18 years with the United 
States Postal Service, continuing the work that keeps our communities 
connected.
  Sergeant Parker was truly defined not by her career of service, but 
by the extraordinary generosity that shaped every corner of her life. 
After witnessing a miracle in her own family following a life-saving 
operation, she quietly made the selfless decision to give the gift of 
life to others. Her love moved outward and instinctively, without ever 
seeking applause. Her kindness was a way of life.
  Those blessed to know her describe her as loving, giving, and strong. 
Sergeant Parker's legacy is not measured only through years or titles, 
but in the immeasurable impact of a woman who poured love, kindness, 
and service into her community every single day. The 12th Congressional 
District honors her remarkable life and extends our sincerest 
condolences to her family, friends, and all who carry her memory in 
their hearts. May her spirit continue to inspire the generosity, 
courage, and compassion that defined her beautiful life.

                          ____________________
```
