# HONORING THE SERVICE AND RETIREMENT OF BATTALION CHIEF ERIC ARNOLD

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
   HONORING THE SERVICE AND RETIREMENT OF BATTALION CHIEF ERIC ARNOLD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Battalion Chief Eric Arnold 
as he retires after 25 years of heroic public service. Chief Arnold has 
protected our community with profound bravery, steady leadership, and 
an unwavering commitment to saving lives.
  Chief Arnold first joined the department on May 8, 2001, as a 
Firefighter 1. Over the next two and a half decades, his leadership 
capability and technical expertise propelled him steadily through the 
ranks. He earned promotions to Firefighter 2 in 2005, Fire Engineer in 
2011, Lieutenant in 2017, and Fire Captain in 2019, before culminating 
his career as Battalion Chief on December 24, 2023. Along the way, he 
consistently expanded his skill set, graduating from the Staff and 
Command School and securing advanced certifications for the Special 
Operations Team.
  Chief Arnold's exemplary service is reflected in a remarkably 
decorated array of accolades. His life-saving instincts earned him the 
prestigious Fire Chief Life Saving Award three times in 2001, 2002, and 
2010, alongside a Meritorious Service Award in 2021. A consummate team 
player, he was a staple of highly decorated crews, earning six 
Meritorious Company Commendations, a Vaccination Center Award, and a 
long history of Safe Driving and Perfect Attendance honors.
  On behalf of Michigan's 12th Congressional District, we thank 
Battalion Chief Eric Arnold for his 25 years of courageous, life-saving 
service. As he retires on June 2, 2026, he leaves behind a lasting 
legacy of integrity, mentorship, and profound public safety. We wish 
him a happy, healthy, and well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
