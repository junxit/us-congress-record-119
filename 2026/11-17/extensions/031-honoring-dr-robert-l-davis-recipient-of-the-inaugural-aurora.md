# HONORING DR. ROBERT L. DAVIS, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
  HONORING DR. ROBERT L. DAVIS, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF 
                              ACTION AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Dr. Robert L. Davis of 
Aurora, Illinois, recipient of the Auroran of Action Award presented by 
the Aurora Actionaires. For more than four decades, Dr. Davis has 
enriched our community through music, ministry, mentorship, and a 
steadfast commitment to serving others.
  Born and raised in Aurora, Dr. Davis graduated from East Aurora High 
School in 1983 and pursued studies in music and theology. Called to 
ministry as a teenager, he built a remarkable career as a pastor, 
gospel recording artist, songwriter, and community leader while 
remaining deeply connected to his community.
  In 1994, Dr. Davis joined Cynthia N. Miller in leading the Voices for 
Excellence Aurora Community Choir as Musical Director and Vice 
President. Through the choir, he helped bring neighbors together, 
celebrated musical traditions, and encouraged young performers to 
develop their talents. His dedication to community music has also 
included leadership at Main Baptist Church, where he returned in 2022 
as Pastor of Worship and Arts and Minister of Music.
  Dr. Davis's musical influence extends beyond Aurora. His compositions 
have been recorded by the Church of God in Christ National Mass Choir, 
and he has worked with celebrated gospel artists including the Clark 
Sisters, Reverend James Moore, and Donnie McClurkin. His ministry has 
reached audiences internationally, yet his commitment to nurturing 
talent at home has remained central to his work.
  In 2005, Dr. Davis founded Inner Court Worship Cathedral in Aurora, 
continuing a lifetime of pastoral leadership. Through worship, 
mentorship, and community engagement, he has offered encouragement to 
individuals and families and helped create spaces where people feel 
welcomed, supported, and inspired.
  Dr. Davis especially deserves recognition not only because of his 
musical ability, but also because of his willingness to share it. 
Whether guiding a young musician, leading a congregation, or bringing 
people together in song, he has shown how faith, generosity, and 
service can strengthen a community.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Dr. Robert L. Davis for 
receiving the Auroran of Action Award. I ask my colleagues to join me 
in thanking Dr. Davis for his contributions and the inspiration he 
continues to provide to generations of Aurorans.
```
