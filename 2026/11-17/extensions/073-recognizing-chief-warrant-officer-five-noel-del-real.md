# RECOGNIZING CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER FIVE NOEL DEL REAL

**Speaking:** Vasquez, Gabe (V000136) [D-NM]

```
          RECOGNIZING CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER FIVE NOEL DEL REAL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GABE VASQUEZ

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. VASQUEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Chief Warrant 
Officer Five (CW5) Noel Del Real, who recently retired after serving as 
the Command Chief Warrant Officer and Chief Innovation Officer for the 
32nd Army Air and Missile Defense Command (AAMDC) at Fort Bliss.
  Noel enlisted in the United States Army as a 14T, Patriot Launching 
Station Enhanced Operator/Maintainer, and he was appointed as an Air 
Defense Artillery Warrant Officer in 2007 following graduation from the 
Warrant Officer Candidate School. He stepped into his role as the 
Command Chief Warrant Officer in October 2022.
  Noel has shown a strong commitment to academic excellence and 
education, which is reflected in the multiple degrees and 
certifications he has earned. He holds a Doctor of Education from the 
University of Southern California, a Master of Engineering from the 
University of Kansas, and a Master of Arts and Bachelor of Arts in 
Homeland Security from American Military University. His technical 
certifications include graduation from the Patriot Top Gun course, the 
Air Defense Artillery Fire Control Officer (ADAFCO) course, and the 
Fundamentals of Acquisitions Management course. Additionally, he has 
completed all levels of Warrant Officer Professional Military Education 
and was selected and completed the Command and General Staff Course 
(CGSC).
  Throughout his career, Noel has held numerous leadership positions 
and taken on a variety of roles, showcasing his dedication to service 
and supporting fellow servicemembers. His leadership and operational 
assignments include: Army Air and Missile Defense Command (AAMDC) 
Standardization Officer of 32d AAMDC, in Fort Bliss, Texas; Brigade 
Standardization Officer of the 11th Air Defense Artillery (ADA) Brigade 
also in Fort Bliss, Texas; Battalion Standardization Officer of the 1st 
Battalion, 62d ADA, in Fort Hood, Texas; Tactical Director in Fort 
Hood, Texas; Tactical Control Officer at Kadena Air Base in Japan; and 
Platoon Sergeant and Squad Leader of 3d Battalion, 2d ADA, in Fort 
Bliss, Texas.
  His key staff and institutional assignments include: Chief of War 
Plans, 32d AAMDC in Fort Bliss, Texas; AAMDC Air Defense Artillery Fire 
Control Officer (ADAFCO), 32d AAMDC, in Fort Bliss Texas; Chief of the 
Warrant Officer Education System (WOES), in Fort Sill, Oklahoma; 140E/
140K Warrant Officer Advanced Course (WOAC) Manager, in Fort Sill, 
Oklahoma; ADAFCO Course Manager in Fort Sill, Oklahoma; Brigade ADAFCO, 
69th ADA Brigade in Fort Hood, Texas; Maintenance Officer at Kadena Air 
Base in Japan; and Patriot Crew Member of the 1st Battalion, 43d ADA, 
6th Cavalry.
  Noel's commitment to serving our Nation is demonstrated in the 
numerous awards and honors he has earned and his distinguished military 
record. His awards and decorations include the Legion of Merit, the 
Bronze Star Medal, the Meritorious Service Medal with five oak leaf 
clusters, the Army Commendation medal with ``R'' device and four oak 
leaf clusters, the Army Achievement Medal with four oak leaf clusters, 
the Meritorious Unit Commendation, the Army Good Conduct Medal, the 
National Defense Service Medal, the Global War on Terrorism 
Expeditionary Medal, the Global War on Terrorism Service Medal, the 
Korea Defense Service Medal, the Army Service Ribbon, and the Overseas 
Service Ribbon with Numeral 7. Additionally, he is authorized to wear 
the Combat Action Badge, the Master Space Badge, the Senior Instructor 
Badge, and the Basic Recruiter Badge. He is also a recipient of the 
Ancient Order of Saint Barbara.
  Noel is from El Paso, Texas, and he was raised by a hardworking, 
single mother, Marisela Del Real. He is a first-generation American and 
was enrolled in English as a Second Language (ESL) classes until the 
3rd grade because he did not know English. He is married to Mercedes 
Del Real, who is from Austin, Texas, and they have four wonderful 
children together; Mia, Jacob, Camila, and Benjamin.
  On behalf of the people of New Mexico's 2nd Congressional District, I 
would like to thank Chief Warrant Officer Five Noel Del Real for his 
outstanding service, commitment to protecting Americans, and leadership 
to young servicemembers. We are thankful for his years of hard work and 
sacrifice and wish him a happy retirement.
```
