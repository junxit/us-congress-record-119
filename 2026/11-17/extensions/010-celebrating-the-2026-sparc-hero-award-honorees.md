# CELEBRATING THE 2026 SPARC HERO AWARD HONOREES

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
             CELEBRATING THE 2026 SPARC HERO AWARD HONOREES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
distinguished honorees of the fourth annual An Evening to SPARCle Gala. 
This year's celebration recognizes Fairfax County Board of Supervisors 
Chairman Jeffrey C. McKay, Elevance Health/Anthem, and Hannah Irsfeld, 
Esq., as recipients of the SPARC Hero Award, along with a special 
tribute to SPARC Board Chair Ellen Dyke for her outstanding leadership 
and service.
  For two decades, Specially Adapted Resource Centers (SPARC) has 
worked to improve the lives of adults with severe and multiple 
disabilities throughout Northern Virginia. By providing individualized 
support, opportunities for personal development, and meaningful 
community engagement, SPARC addresses a critical gap in services for 
individuals who have ages out of the K-12 special education system. I 
include in the Record the following 2026 SPARC Hero Award honorees:

       Chairman Jeffrey C. McKay, recipient of the 2026 SPARC Hero 
     Award, has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to building a 
     more inclusive Fairfax County. Throughout his public service, 
     Chairman McKay has championed policies that promote 
     accessibility, equity, and opportunity for residents of all 
     abilities. His leadership reflects the belief that every 
     individual deserves the opportunity to participate fully in 
     community life, and his support for SPARC exemplifies the 
     importance of strong partnerships between local government 
     and community organizations.
       Elevance Health/Anthem, recipient of the 2026 SPARC Hero 
     Award, has demonstrated its commitment to disability 
     inclusion through its partnership with SPARC and support for 
     initiatives that advance awareness, advocacy. and access to 
     services. As the presenting sponsor of SPARC's 2026 A Call to 
     Conscience advocacy summit, Elevance Health helped bring 
     together community leaders, policymakers, advocates, and 
     families to address the challenges facing adults with 
     significant disabilities. Its contributions demonstrate the 
     meaningful role that corporate partners can play in 
     strengthening communities and expanding opportunities for 
     those who need them most.
       Hannah Irsfeld, Esq., recipient of the 2026 SPARC Hero 
     Award, has distinguished herself through her dedication to 
     disability rights and her service as Vice Chair of SPARC's 
     Board. As a disability civil rights attorney, Hannah has 
     brought invaluable legal expertise to the organization's 
     advocacy efforts, including research into Virginia's 
     obligations under the Americans with Disabilities Act and 
     federal disability rights protections. Her work has helped 
     strengthen SPARC's efforts to advance community-based 
     services and ensure that adults with significant disabilities 
     receive the dignity. respect, and opportunities they deserve.
       Ellen Dyke, recipient of a special tribute for her 
     leadership and service as SPARC Board Chair, has helped guide 
     the organization's mission and strengthen its commitment to 
     the individuals and families it serves. Through her 
     dedication, compassion, and service, Ellen has helped foster 
     partnerships, advance SPARC's advocacy efforts, and support 
     an organization that provides a vital sense of community and 
     belonging to adults with severe and multiple disabilities.

  This gala began with a simple wish from SPARC participants: an 
opportunity to dress up, share a wonderful meal, dance with friends, 
and celebrate alongside their community. That wish has grown into an 
annual tradition that brings together families, volunteers, business 
leaders, public officials, and advocates in celebration of inclusion 
and the inherent dignity of every individual.
  The contributions of this year's honorees reflect the values at the 
heart of SPARC's mission: compassion, service, advocacy, and the belief 
that every person deserves a meaningful place in our community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Chairman Jeffrey C. McKay, Elevance Health/Anthem, and Hannah Irsfeld, 
Esq., on receiving the 2026 SPARC Hero Awards, and in recognizing Ellen 
Dyke for her exceptional leadership and service. I thank each of them 
for their dedication to SPARC and for helping build a more inclusive 
Northern Virginia where every individual has the opportunity to thrive.
```
