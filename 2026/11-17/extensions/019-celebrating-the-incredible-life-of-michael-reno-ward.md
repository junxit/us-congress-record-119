# CELEBRATING THE INCREDIBLE LIFE OF MICHAEL RENO WARD

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
          CELEBRATING THE INCREDIBLE LIFE OF MICHAEL RENO WARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
life of Michael Reno Ward. Louisiana music suffered a tremendous loss 
with the passing of the jazz violinist. He passed away unexpectantly on 
Monday, December 15, 2025, at the age of 60. Michael was a world-
renowned jazz violinist; born in San Antonio; however, adopted by the 
city of New Orleans.
  Michael, a Southern University and A&M College alumnus, was a world-
traveled jazz musician who performed at nightclubs, festivals, and 
other events, considered a cultural staple in the New Orleans and Baton 
Rouge communities. His influence helped expand the violin's role in 
jazz and contemporary Black music spaces.
  He was a gifted violinist who helped shape the culture and soul of 
New Orleans. Michael Ward was an outstanding musician, a loving husband 
and father, and my dear friend. He was a gifted violinist who helped 
shape the culture and soul of New Orleans. Michael was also part of a 
family music trio, All Strings Attached, with his sons--a true 
testament to his passion for both family and his art. He and his music 
will be deeply missed but will forever live on in the spirit of our 
city.
  Though deeply rooted in the community, he was known not only for his 
musicianship, but for mentoring and inspiring the next generation, his 
family added. Two of his sons, Shaun and Cortes Ward, carry on his 
musical legacy. He performed alongside them as part of a trio called 
All Strings Attached.
  Michael leaves to cherish his memories with his beloved wife, Lenzie 
LeBeauf-Ward and their two sons, Cortes and Cameron Ward; daughter 
Brittany Hynson (Favyon); sons Shaun (Blair), Michael, Jr. (Razine), 
Azariah Ward, and Devante Lacy; and 7 grandchildren. He is preceded in 
death by his mother Carol Ward, daughter Gabrielle Hynson, and grandson 
Rashad Ward.
  Everyone he touched with his violin was able to celebrate the full 
presence of the music and everyone in it. May my dear friend rest in 
peace.
```
