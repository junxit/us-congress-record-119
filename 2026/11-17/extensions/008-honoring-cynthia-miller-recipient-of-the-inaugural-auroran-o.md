# HONORING CYNTHIA MILLER, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
 HONORING CYNTHIA MILLER, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Cynthia N. Miller 
of Aurora, Illinois, recipient of the Auroran of Action Award presented 
by the Aurora Actionaires. For more than four decades, Mrs. Miller has 
been a devoted community leader, advocate, and mentor whose compassion, 
generosity, and commitment to service have made a lasting difference in 
the lives of countless Aurora residents.
  Throughout her life, Mrs. Miller has worked to bring people together, 
create opportunities, and ensure that families have access to the 
resources and support they need to thrive. As the inaugural Chairwoman 
of Aurora's African American Heritage Advisory Board, she helped 
establish a foundation for celebrating African American history, 
culture, and civic engagement. She also serves as President of the 
Aurora African American Health Coalition, helping provide free health 
services, screenings, and education to underserved residents.
  Her commitment to community extends into education, the arts, and 
public service. Nearly 40 years ago, Mrs. Miller co-founded Voices for 
Excellence Aurora Community Choir, creating a space where music, 
mentorship, and fellowship could bring generations together. She has 
also served as an Aurora Civil Service Commissioner, chaired the East 
Aurora School District 131 Advisory Council, and advised the Aurora 
Urban League.
  Professionally, Mrs. Miller dedicated more than thirty years to 
education and social services, including leadership roles with the 
Illinois Department of Corrections and the Kane County Employment and 
Training Agency. Her work reflected her belief in helping individuals 
overcome challenges and build better futures.
  Her contributions have earned numerous honors, including the Lifetime 
Achievement Award from Aurora Mayor Richard C. Irvin, the YWCA Woman of 
the Year Award, and the Illinois State Community Service Employee of 
the Year Award. Yet her greatest legacy is reflected in the people she 
has encouraged, supported, and inspired along the way.
  Affectionately known as ``Momma Miller,'' she has become a trusted 
source of wisdom, kindness, and encouragement for generations of Aurora 
residents. Through her involvement with Main Baptist Church and Delta 
Sigma Theta Sorority, Incorporated, she continues to demonstrate that 
meaningful leadership begins with caring for others.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Cynthia Miller on receiving the 
Auroran of Action Award. I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking 
Mrs. Miller for her extraordinary contributions and the legacy she 
continues to build for future generations.
```
