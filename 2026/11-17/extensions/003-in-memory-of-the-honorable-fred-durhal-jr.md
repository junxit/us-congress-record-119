# IN MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE FRED DURHAL, JR.

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
              IN MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE FRED DURHAL, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of my 
dear friend and my former colleague, State Representative Fred Durhal, 
Jr. who was known for his unwavering dedication to public service, his 
deep love for his community, and his tireless efforts to improve the 
lives of those around him.
  Rep. Durhal was a proud product of the Detroit Public Schools and 
later earned a bachelor's degree in political science from Wayne State 
University and pursued additional studies in public administration at 
Phoenix University. Rep. Durhal's professional experiences touched many 
spheres in our community. He served in the Michigan State House as the 
Dean Emeritus of the Michigan House of Representatives, having served 
from 2008 to 2014, holding several titles, including the Chair Emeritus 
of the Michigan Legislative Black Caucus. Rep. Durhal also served our 
community well, recently with the Detroit Rescue Mission Ministries and 
formerly as a community resource specialist with the Michigan Economic 
Development Corporation and served under a number of public officials, 
including Detroit Mayor Coleman Young, Congresswoman Barbara Rose 
Collins, State Senator Jackie Vaughn, and Wayne County Commissioner 
Bernard Parker.
  Rep. Durhal championed issues that directly impacted his 
constituents, such as equity in education, addressing housing 
disparities, and increasing economic opportunities for Detroit's 
working-class families. Rep. Durhal was an advocate for the city's most 
vulnerable populations. Rep. Durhal was a trailblazer when it came to 
defining what public service truly is, from his advocacy for working 
families, to his dedication to mentoring the next generation of 
leaders, Fred truly helped shape our communities and uplift countless 
lives. He was my dear friend and colleague in the Michigan State 
Legislature. In his very own words, he said that ``public service isn't 
just about passing laws--it's about making life better for the people 
we represent. It's about lifting up the voices of those who have been 
silenced for too long''.

[[Page E1216]]

  Rep. Durhal was a fighter and took each day as an opportunity to do 
more. Even in his final days, he fought with the same courage and 
tenacity. I join our community in mourning his passing during this 
difficult time and send my deepest condolences to his family, children, 
and all who were touched by his life. His legacy will continue to live 
on in the city he loved so dearly. May he rest in power.

                          ____________________
```
