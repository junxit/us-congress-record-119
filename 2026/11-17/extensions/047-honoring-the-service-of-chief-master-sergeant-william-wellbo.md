# HONORING THE SERVICE OF CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT WILLIAM WELLBORNE

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
    HONORING THE SERVICE OF CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT WILLIAM WELLBORNE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the remarkable legacy and 
service of Chief Master Sergeant William Welborne, a World War II 
veteran and community leader. Today we look to honor his service and 
celebrate his 93rd birthday.
  Mr. Welborne's remarkable and heroic service figures prominently in 
our Nation's historic Tuskegee Airmen, which were the first African 
American military pilots and support personnel in the United States 
Armed Forces in 1948. With his dedication and commitment, he made an 
indelible mark in history, not only as a line chief with the Tuskegee 
Airmen but also as a true American hero. His service to our Nation 
started from January 1948 to February 1981 in the Air Corps. William 
served our Nation both in the Korean and Vietnam Wars; his service is 
nothing short of extraordinary. Beyond his service to our Nation, 
William is most proud of his service to the Tuskegee Airmen Detroit 
Chapter, where he currently resides on the Board of Directors, 
overseeing the organization in its vital pursuit of preserving the 
legacy of the Tuskegee Airmen by educating and uplifting new 
generations of leaders in aviation from underserved backgrounds.
  As one of the few remaining Tuskegee Airmen, William tirelessly works 
to keep the legacy of the Tuskegee Airmen alive and remembered. Please 
join me in recognizing Chief Master Sergeant William Welborne on this 
celebration of his lifelong service to our country and community. The 
12th Congressional District wishes Mr. Welborne a very happy 93rd 
birthday, as he is surrounded by the love and warm wishes of his family 
and friends.

                          ____________________
```
