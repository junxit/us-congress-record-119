# HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE OF JAMES W. "JIM" LANCASTER

**Speaking:** Westerman, Bruce (W000821) [R-AR]

```
       HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE OF JAMES W. ``JIM'' LANCASTER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BRUCE WESTERMAN

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. WESTERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the remarkable 
public service of Mr. James W. ``Jim'' Lancaster of Sheridan, Arkansas. 
Jim has dedicated much of his life to serving his community, including 
an incredible 42 years on the Sheridan City Council. He also served 
three consecutive terms in the Arkansas House of Representatives and 
three years on the Grant County Quorum Court. Today, he continues his 
commitment to Sheridan as Chairman of the Planning Commission.
  Born and raised in Sheridan, Jim graduated from Sheridan High School 
in 1955 and earned a degree in Industrial Engineering from the 
University of Arkansas at Monticello in 1959. Before retiring, he 
enjoyed a career in engineering and management with Arkansas Louisiana 
Gas Company and Rockwell International Corporation. He and his wife, 
Betty, have one child, three grandchildren, and one great-grandchild.
  Jim's contributions to Sheridan span nearly six decades. He was 
serving on the City Council when the city adopted its original zoning 
regulations in 1968, and today he continues to help shape Sheridan's 
future. His experience, historical knowledge, and dedication to his 
hometown are truly remarkable.
  Jim Lancaster represents the very best of public service and is a 
shining example of what it means to serve one's community with 
dedication and genuine love for home. His decades of commitment to 
Sheridan, Grant County, and the State of Arkansas have left a lasting 
mark on our communities. I commend him for his extraordinary service 
and thank him for his continued dedication to the people of Arkansas.

                          ____________________
```
