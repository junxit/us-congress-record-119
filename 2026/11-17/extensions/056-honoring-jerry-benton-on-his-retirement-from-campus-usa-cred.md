# HONORING JERRY BENTON ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM CAMPUS USA CREDIT UNION

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
  HONORING JERRY BENTON ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM CAMPUS USA CREDIT UNION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Jerry Benton, who 
will retire at the close of this year after more than 27 years of 
service to CAMPUS USA Credit Union in Gainesville, Florida.
  Jerry has devoted more than 40 years of his career to credit unions. 
A graduate of Millikin University and a former certified public 
accountant, he served on the management team at Decatur Earthmover 
Credit Union in Illinois before joining CAMPUS in 1999 as Chief 
Operating Officer.
  In 2014, Jerry was named President and CEO. He inherited a credit 
union of about 70,000 members and $1.2 billion in assets. He leaves 
behind one that serves more than 180,000 members and holds more than $4 
billion in assets. Along the way, CAMPUS opened new service centers and 
extended its reach from 12 Florida counties to 25.
  Behind those figures is a principle Jerry kept at the center of his 
work. A credit union belongs to its members, and he led CAMPUS for the 
people who own it. He built a culture that puts people first. That 
culture may be his most enduring contribution, and it will serve CAMPUS 
well under Jeff Thieman, who succeeds him as CEO on January 1.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Jerry Benton 
for a career devoted to the members of CAMPUS and to North Central 
Florida. On behalf of Florida's 3rd Congressional District, I thank 
Jerry for all he has built. I wish him every happiness in retirement.

                          ____________________
```
