# CELEBRATING THE CAREER OF MR. CLINTON O. ROBINSON

**Speaking:** Davids, Sharice (D000629) [D-KS]

```
           CELEBRATING THE CAREER OF MR. CLINTON O. ROBINSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
career of Mr. Clinton O. Robinson, the Associate Vice President and 
Director of State and Local Government Affairs at Black and Veatch.
  Mr. Robinson is retiring after a remarkable 42-year career at Black 
and Veatch, including

[[Page E1225]]

serving as a civil engineer, project manager, and valued collaborator 
with local, state, and federal governments as the Director of State and 
Local Government Affairs. Mr. Robinson's illustrious career has made 
him an invaluable advocate on issues relating to water, energy, 
telecommunications, infrastructure, and how these core tenets of day-
to-day life impact Kansas City and surrounding communities.
  Mr. Robinson has also been an invaluable resource to me and my 
office. He was present for one of the first official meetings I took as 
a Member of Congress, and he remained a steadfast voice for consistent 
and robust financing of infrastructure. I know he was as excited as I 
was when the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act was passed into 
law--a generational investment into the roads, highways, bridges, and 
rail infrastructure of our country.
  Mr. Robinson has worked to address critical issues tactfully, 
pragmatically, and with humility. Mr. Robinson's professional expertise 
would be exemplary on its own, but that only encompasses a fraction of 
how he gave back to his community. As President of the Blue Valley 
School District School Board, Mr. Robinson led Kansas' fourth largest 
school district with integrity. Under his leadership, he co-founded and 
led the implementation of the Blue Valley Center for Advanced 
Professional Studies, a program allowing high school students the 
opportunity to gain unique, applicable expertise in some of our 
Nation's most in-demand career fields, Mr. Robinson also served in 
leadership roles for Safehome, a local non-profit organization serving 
survivors of domestic violence, and at Youthfriends, a school-based 
mentoring program connecting adult role models with students to build 
confidence and encourage academic success.
  The dedication Mr. Robinson has for leaving his community a better 
place than he found it, both personally and professionally, is felt by 
everyone who meets him.
  Please join me in extending my sincere congratulations to Mr. 
Robinson on his exceptional career and profound impact on the Kansas 
City region. I wish Mr. Robinson an enjoyable, well-deserved 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
