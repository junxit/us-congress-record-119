# HONORING CYNTHIA LATIMER, RECIPIENT OF THE TORCH AWARD (EXCELLENCE IN EDUCATION)

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
 HONORING CYNTHIA LATIMER, RECIPIENT OF THE TORCH AWARD (EXCELLENCE IN 
                               EDUCATION)

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Cynthia Latimer, 
recipient of The Torch Award (Excellence in Education) presented by the 
National Coalition of 100 Black Women, Inc., Aurora, Illinois 
Metropolitan Chapter. This award honors a Black woman who has 
championed education and academic excellence, and Ms. Latimer 
exemplifies this commitment through her distinguished career and 
lifelong dedication to students, educators, and our community.
  Ms. Latimer's career in Aurora's public schools included service as a 
special education teacher, principal, and district administrator, 
culminating in her role as Assistant Superintendent of Teaching and 
Learning for West Aurora School District 129. Throughout her career, 
she championed students' diverse learning needs and advanced 
initiatives in curriculum, special education, early childhood 
education, English language learning, family engagement, and 
professional development, ensuring every student has the opportunity, 
support, and resources to reach their full potential.
  Her commitment to education extends beyond the classroom. Through her 
leadership with the Illinois Education Association and the National 
Education Association, she advocated for educators and policies that 
strengthen public education. As a member of the Illinois Congressional 
Contact Team, she also worked directly with members of Congress and 
their staff on issues affecting schools, education funding, and 
employee rights.
  Following her retirement in 2014, she continued mentoring educators 
and administrators, consulting with school districts, and advocating 
for public education. In 2019, Governor J.B. Pritzker appointed her to 
the Illinois State Board of Education, further extending her service to 
Illinois students and families.
  Ms. Latimer understands that leadership is not simply about the 
positions one holds, but about lifting others up, opening doors, and 
helping people recognize their own potential. Inspired by the values 
her parents instilled in her, she continues to serve her community 
through the Aurora Actionaires and Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, 
Incorporated.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Cynthia Latimer for her 
extraordinary dedication to education and public service. I ask my 
colleagues to join me in thanking her for all she has given to Aurora 
and for the inspiration she continues to provide to future generations. 
Her legacy reminds us that the true measure of educational leadership 
is found not only in the progress we achieve today, but also in the 
opportunities we create for generations to come.
```
