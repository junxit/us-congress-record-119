# HONORING THE LIFE OF HIGH CHIEF SAMANA SEMO VEAVEA

**Speaking:** Radewagen, Aumua Amata Coleman (R000600) [R-AS]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE OF HIGH CHIEF SAMANA SEMO VEAVEA

                                 ______
                                 

                   HON. AUMUA AMATA COLEMAN RADEWAGEN

                           of american samoa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. RADEWAGEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of High 
Chief Samana Semo Veavea who passed away on October 19, 2026. He was a 
beloved member of the American Samoa community, and while his 
unexpected passing leaves a void, he also leaves behind a legacy of 
commitment to our Samoan ideals of service, family, leadership, and 
faith.
  A U.S. Army veteran, Veavea had a storied military career spanning 
several decades after which he returned to our island home ready to 
serve his islands with the same ethos with which he served his country. 
He was the Senior Army Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps 
instructor, where he mentored countless young cadets instilling in them 
the virtues of leadership and service.
  A leader and public servant, he was director of the Department of 
Parks and Recreation during the Togiola-Faoa administration and then 
later served as director of the Department of Homeland Security during 
both the Lolo-Lemanu administration and the Lemanu-La`apui 
administration.
  Always involved in one way or another even outside of his career, he 
served on the board of directors of the Boys & Girls Club of American 
Samoa. Through his example, he helped to develop the next generation of 
leaders in American Samoa.
  Samana was not only a respected member of our community, but also a 
fellow candidate in the 2026 race for Congress, sharing in the 
democratic process and the responsibility of serving the people of 
American Samoa. His participation in the election reflected his 
willingness to step forward and contribute to the future of our beloved 
territory.
  We honor his legacy and thank him and his family for their public 
service that continues to benefit the territory to this day. As we 
mourn his passing, may we remember his life with respect and offer 
comfort and strength to all those grieving this loss. My thoughts and 
prayers are with his family during this difficult time.
  His funeral service is scheduled for November 26th after which he 
will be transported on December 4th to his final resting place at 
Veteran State Memorial Cemetery in Hawaii. May the Lord bring peace to 
his loved ones and may Samana Semo Veavea rest in eternal peace.
```
