# RECOGNIZING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF LIEN DOAN HUNG VUONG, TROOP 612 AND PACK 612

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
RECOGNIZING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF LIEN DOAN HUNG VUONG, TROOP 612 AND 
                                PACK 612

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 30th 
anniversary of Lien Doan Hung Vuong, Troop 612 and Pack 612, and 
celebrate three decades of dedication to youth development, community 
service, and cultural heritage in Fairfax County.
  Founded on December 6, 1996, with just five scouts and two leaders, 
Lien Doan Hung Vuong has grown into a thriving scouting organization 
that provides young people with opportunities to develop leadership 
skills and serve their communities. Through camping, outdoor 
activities, and educational programs, the organization has helped 
generations of scouts develop the confidence and character to become 
engaged members of their communities.
  Throughout its history, Lien Doan Hung Vuong has demonstrated a 
strong commitment to service and scouting excellence. The organization 
has received the Quality Unit Award numerous times and helped many 
young people achieve the prestigious rank of Eagle Scout. These 
accomplishments reflect the dedication of its volunteer leaders and 
families, whose continued support has been essential to the 
organization's success.
  Lien Doan Hung Vuong plays an important role in preserving and 
celebrating Vietnamese American heritage. Established by Vietnamese 
American families, the organization honors the Hung Kings through its 
name and shares Vietnamese traditions through cultural performances and 
educational activities. Its Lion Dance Team has become a meaningful 
part of community celebrations, helping connect younger generations 
with their heritage while sharing these traditions with neighbors 
across Northern Virginia.
  As Lien Doan Hung Vuong celebrates this milestone, we also recognize 
the countless parents, volunteers, and community supporters who have 
helped sustain its mission over the past three decades. Their 
commitment has created a welcoming environment where young people can 
learn the importance of service while taking pride in their cultural 
identity.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Lien 
Doan Hung Vuong, Troop 612 and Pack 612, on their 30th anniversary and 
thanking the scouts, leaders, and families who have contributed to its 
success. I wish the organization continued growth and many more years 
of service to the Fairfax County community.
```
