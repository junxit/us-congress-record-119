# HONORING THE LEGACY OF MODERN TIMES

**Speaking:** Omar, Ilhan (O000173) [D-MN]

```
                  HONORING THE LEGACY OF MODERN TIMES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ILHAN OMAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Modern Times in Minneapolis 
for their exemplary contributions and dedication to serving our 
community during Operation Metro Surge, and for embodying the values of 
solidarity and mutual aid as they demonstrate a new model for non-
profit restaurants.
  For more than 15 years, Modern Times has been a mainstay in South 
Minneapolis. Those who know the establishment know that staff have 
always seen their restaurant as more than just a place to sell food. 
During Operation Metro Surge in 2026, while federal agents brought 
panic and chaos, Modern Times changed its business strategy. Instead of 
running like an ordinary restaurant, they decided to operate on a 
donation-basis with the objective of feeding hungry people without 
regard to their financial situation. By breaking societal and 
capitalistic norms, they used their resources to provide support to 
those who needed it most.
  To support this effort, Modern Times accepted donations from whomever 
felt capable. Unsurprisingly, contributions poured in from Minneapolis 
and places all across the country, which made it possible for the 
restaurant to serve thousands of meals to people who were on the 
frontlines resisting fascism. Owner Dylan Alverson saw this initiative 
as a quick response to the occupation, as well as a learning experience 
for small businesses.
  As their motto declared, Modern Times was working to ``stop 
monetizing humanity, one meal at a time.'' During a period when 
countless families were feeling lonely and exposed, Modern Times 
presented them with a warm meal, a friendly place, and the knowledge 
that neighbors would stand by one another in times of crisis. Not only 
is this reflective of selflessness and solidarity from the owner and 
staff, but it shows how the Minneapolis community came together to 
support one another. It is my honor to pay tribute to Modern Times and 
their accomplishments.
```
