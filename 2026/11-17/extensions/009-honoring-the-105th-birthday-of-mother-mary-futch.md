# HONORING THE 105TH BIRTHDAY OF MOTHER MARY FUTCH

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
            HONORING THE 105TH BIRTHDAY OF MOTHER MARY FUTCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and celebrate the life of Ms. 
Mary Futch, a long-time resident of Detroit, as she celebrates her 
105th birthday.
  Ms. Futch was born and raised in Kosciusko, Mississippi, but she is 
proud to call Detroit her home. In fact, she has lived at the same 
address on Maxwell Street for more than 60 years, the longest living 
resident on the street. Married to the late Ollie Futch, Ms. Futch was 
a devoted wife and mother of ten children. Ms. Futch, or ``Momma 
Futch,'' is well-known in her neighborhood and church community at Mt. 
Zion Missionary Baptist Church.
  Please join me in wishing Mother Futch the happiest of birthdays, 
surrounded by the love and warm wishes of her family and friends.

                          ____________________
```
