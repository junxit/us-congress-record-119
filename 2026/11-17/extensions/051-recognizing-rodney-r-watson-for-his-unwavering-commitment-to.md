# RECOGNIZING RODNEY R. WATSON FOR HIS UNWAVERING COMMITMENT TO LOUISIANA'S EDUCATION SYSTEM

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
     RECOGNIZING RODNEY R. WATSON FOR HIS UNWAVERING COMMITMENT TO 
                      LOUISIANA'S EDUCATION SYSTEM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
celebrate the heroic commitment to the state of Louisiana's education 
system of Mr. Rodney R. Watson. Mr. Watson has served the education 
system of Louisiana for over 32 years, serving educators in Louisiana's 
2nd Congressional District, of which I proudly represent. He is 
retiring after a decade in the role of Executive Director of the 
Louisiana Retired Teachers Association, a membership organization 
representing the interests and concerns of the retired educators of the 
state of Louisiana.
  In 1993, Mr. Watson joined the staff of the Department of Education, 
where he served as Program Manager for the Job Training Partnership 
Act, Project Facilitator for the Louisiana Content Standards 
Development Project, and Director for the Division of School Standards, 
Accountability and Assistance.
  In 2008, Mr. Watson served as Interim Superintendent of the Caddo 
Parish School System, before returning to the Department of Education 
as the Assistant Superintendent of the Office of Educator Support.
  Mr. Watson has held teaching positions in the faculties of the 
Massachusetts College of Liberal Arts and Tulane University, holding 
membership in many professional organizations, including the Louisiana 
and American Societies of Association Executives and the Association of 
State Retired Teacher Executives, where he served as President and 
meritoriously supported the Units of District 1, President, Officers, 
and Members.
  The Louisiana Retired Teachers Association is alive and well and I am 
proud of that. It is ingrained in the people of Louisiana who nurture 
the education system of Louisiana. I offer a thank you to Mr. Rodney R. 
Watson for his dedicated service as he receives the honorable tribute 
on his retirement at the statewide LRTA Fall Meeting. I congratulate 
him.

                          ____________________
```
