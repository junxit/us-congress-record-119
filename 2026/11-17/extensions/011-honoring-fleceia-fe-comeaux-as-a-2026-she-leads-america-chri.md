# HONORING FLECEIA "FE" COMEAUX AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 HONORING FLECEIA ``FE'' COMEAUX AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN 
                          WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Fleceia ``Fe'' 
Comeaux as a 2026

[[Page E1218]]

She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction award recipient.
  Fe Comeaux has dedicated 27 years to the Fellowship of Christian 
Athletes, serving as South Houston FCA Area Director. She also serves 
as Coordinator of Chaplains for the WNBA, chaplain for the Houston 
Dash, and co-chaplain for the U.S. Women's National Soccer Team. 
Through these roles, she supports athletes and chaplains, helping them 
influence others through faith.
  A former two-sport athlete at the University of Houston, Fe earned 
four varsity letters in basketball and one in soccer. Her athletic 
experience, alongside 11 years as a high school teacher and varsity 
basketball coach, gives her an understanding of the pressures and 
opportunities within competitive sports.
  Fe speaks nationally to audiences ranging from junior high students 
to professional athletes. She has mentored Olympians, coaches, and 
athletes from diverse backgrounds, encouraging them to recognize their 
God-given gifts, develop character, and use their talents to glorify 
God.
  Committed to building foundations of faith, Fe helps athletes 
discover their identity beyond performance and confidently pursue God's 
calling. Her ministry equips individuals to embrace their potential, 
walk boldly in their purpose, and make a positive difference in the 
lives of others. She remains committed to developing leaders whose 
influence reflects Christ on and off the field.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
