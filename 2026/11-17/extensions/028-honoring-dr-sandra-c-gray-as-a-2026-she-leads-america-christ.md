# HONORING DR. SANDRA C. GRAY AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING DR. SANDRA C. GRAY AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN 
                             OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Sandra C. 
Gray as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction award 
recipient.
  Dr. Sandra C. Gray served Asbury University for 30 years as a 
business professor, department chair, provost, and president, serving 
as president from 2007 to 2019. Throughout her service, she 
demonstrated academic excellence, deep faith, and a commitment to 
developing leaders.
  During her presidency, Dr. Gray guided a period of significant 
growth, including the largest capital campaign in Asbury's history, its 
transition from college to university status, and the expansion of 
academic programs and campus facilities. Her leadership strengthened 
the institution's mission and fostered an environment where scholarship 
and Christian faith flourished together.
  A trusted advisor, community leader, and advocate for Christian 
higher education, Dr. Gray has influenced students, educators, and 
institutions throughout Kentucky, across the United States, and 
internationally. Her work reflects a dedication to preparing students 
for lives of service, integrity, and purpose.
  Since retiring from the presidency, she has continued serving on 
multiple boards and sharing her experience as a speaker and consultant 
in higher education and the Church. Her commitment to mentoring and 
leadership development continues to encourage the next generation.
  Dr. Gray's career exemplifies sustained service to education, 
community, and faith, leaving a legacy of opportunity, encouragement, 
and achievement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
