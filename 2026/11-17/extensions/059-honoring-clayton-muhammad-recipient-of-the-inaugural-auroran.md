# HONORING CLAYTON MUHAMMAD, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
HONORING CLAYTON MUHAMMAD, RECIPIENT OF THE INAUGURAL AURORAN OF ACTION 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Clayton Muhammad as 
the inaugural recipient of the Aurora Actionaires' Auroran of Action 
Award, recognizing his exceptional leadership, distinguished career, 
and profound contributions to the Aurora community.
  Born and raised in Aurora, Mr. Muhammad has established himself as 
one of the city's most influential voices and dedicated advocates. An 
accomplished educator, award-winning communications and media 
professional, and civic leader, he has spent more than 25 years 
championing educational opportunity, developing future leaders, and 
strengthening the community he proudly calls home.
  Affectionately known as ``The Voice of Aurora,'' Mr. Muhammad has 
served as a trusted spokesperson and emcee for more than 1,000 events. 
His distinguished career includes serving as Senior Advisor to Aurora 
Mayor Richard C. Irvin, Chief Engagement Officer and Chief Equity 
Officer for the City of Aurora, and spokesperson for East Aurora School 
District 131. He currently serves as Vice President of Community 
Engagement and External Affairs at Waubonsee Community College.
  Among his many accomplishments, one of his most meaningful is the 
founding of Boys II Men, Inc. in 2002, transforming a period of 
violence in Aurora into an opportunity to inspire and empower young 
men. Through mentorship and leadership development, the organization 
has shaped hundreds of lives, producing college graduates, accomplished 
professionals, and elected officials. His impactful leadership is 
further reflected in Aurora's award-winning CHANGE Reform Initiative.
  Mr. Muhammad's extraordinary contributions have earned him more than 
100 awards and recognitions, including the City of Aurora's African 
American Leader of the Year Award, Kane County Citizen of the Year 
Award, Aur-City United's Mentor of the Year Award, Alpha Kappa Alpha 
Sorority's Central Region Community Service Award, and his beloved 
Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity's Midwest Community Leader of the Year 
Award.
  Clayton Muhammad exemplifies the highest ideals of excellence and 
servant leadership. His enduring legacy is evident not only in his 
professional accomplishments but also in the countless lives he has 
touched through his unwavering dedication to uplifting others.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Clayton Muhammad on receiving 
the inaugural Auroran of Action Award. I ask my colleagues to join me 
in thanking him not only for all that he has accomplished, but for the 
extraordinary person he is and the countless ways he continues to make 
a difference in the lives of others. His life and work remind us that 
the greatest measure of leadership is not simply what we accomplish, 
but the difference we make in the lives of others.
```
