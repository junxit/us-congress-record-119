# HONORING WENDY GRIFFITH AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING WENDY GRIFFITH AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF 
                              DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Wendy Griffith 
as a 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction award 
recipient.
  Wendy Griffith is a journalist, author, and speaker with nearly 40 
years in television news, including 27 years with the Christian 
Broadcasting Network. Known for her reporting centered on faith, she 
co-hosted The 700 Club alongside the late Dr. Pat Robertson.
  A West Virginia native, Wendy earned a Bachelor of Science in 
Journalism from West Virginia University. She began her television 
career in 1987 in Yuma, Arizona, and served as an anchor and reporter 
in markets nationwide, including her hometown of Charleston, West 
Virginia.
  Joining CBN in 1999 as Congressional Correspondent, Wendy covered 
national stories, including President Bill Clinton's impeachment trial. 
She later relocated to CBN's Virginia Beach headquarters, focusing on 
stories of faith and spiritual significance.
  Wendy has authored three books: Praying the News: Your Prayers Are 
More Powerful Than You Know; You Are a Prize to Be Won! Don't Settle 
for Less than God's Best; and You Didn't Miss It! God's Best Is Worth 
the Wait. Drawing from her experience of marrying for the first time in 
her 50s, she encourages others to trust God's timing.
  An adventurer, Wendy summited Tanzania's Mount Kilimanjaro in 2014, 
reflecting the perseverance and faith she shares through her 
journalism, writing, and speaking.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
