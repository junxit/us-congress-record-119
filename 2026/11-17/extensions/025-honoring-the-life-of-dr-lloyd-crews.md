# HONORING THE LIFE OF DR. LLOYD CREWS

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF DR. LLOYD CREWS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 17, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the incredible life and 
legacy of Former Councilman Dr. Lloyd Crews, whose life was rooted in 
faith, family, and service.
  As a proud product of the Detroit Public Schools; graduating from 
Cass Technical High School, Dr. Crews earned both a Bachelor of Arts 
and master's degree in political science from Clark Atlanta University. 
His commitment to service led him to pursue a Ph.D. in Educational 
Leadership and Policy Studies from Wayne State University. In addition 
to his education, Dr. Crews was a lifelong member to the Greater Christ 
Baptist Church, and a proud member of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc.
  Dr. Crews' leadership extended far beyond his many titles. He was a 
passionate advocate for diversity, equity, and inclusion, leading the 
initiative of coordinating Oakland County Community College's first Dr. 
Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Convocation. Additionally, Dr. Lloyd's 
proudest accomplishment at OCC was founding the OCC Man Up program--a 
mentorship initiative designed to uplift young men through education, 
leadership, and purpose.
  Those who knew Lloyd will remember his constant radiant smile, his 
easy laughter, and his boundless compassion. Our thoughts and prayers 
are with his loving wife, Stacey Crews; his beloved son, Jamier; and 
his parents, Lloyd Walker and Charlie Marie Crews. His spirit will 
continue to shine through every life he touched and every community he 
helped strengthen.

                          ____________________
```
