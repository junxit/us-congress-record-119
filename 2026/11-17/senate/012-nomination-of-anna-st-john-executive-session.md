# Nomination of Anna St. John (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                      Nomination of Anna St. John

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, today, the Senate will vote to confirm 
Anna St. John to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit.
  Judge St. John's record on the bench is short. She began her tenure 
as a district judge in April of this year. At the time of her 
nomination to the Fifth Circuit, she had not presided over any trials. 
But she is being quickly promoted because of her record as a lawyer, 
where she repeatedly challenged class-action settlements and litigated 
politically charged issues.
  In November 2021, as Congress considered legislation ending forced 
arbitration in cases of workplace sexual assault and harassment, Judge 
St. John testified in support of compelling abuse survivors into forced 
arbitration. The reality is that forced arbitration denies sexual 
assault survivors a day in court and shields misconduct from public 
view.
  Former Fox News journalist Gretchen Carlson, who strongly advocated 
for the legislation, publicly opposed Judge St. John's district court 
nomination.
  Ms. Carlson wrote: ``While women around the country bravely shared 
the urgency of eradicating forced arbitration, St. John used her power 
and influence to gaslight these women and the public. She downplayed 
their stories and minimized their pain.''
  I oppose Judge St. John's nomination, and I ask my colleagues to join 
me.
```
