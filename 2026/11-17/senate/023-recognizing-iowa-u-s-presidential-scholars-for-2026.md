# RECOGNIZING IOWA U.S. PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLARS FOR 2026

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

          RECOGNIZING IOWA U.S. PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLARS FOR 2026

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, it is my privilege to recognize 
three outstanding students from Iowa who have been named U.S. 
Presidential Scholars for 2026: Christopher Kenneth Gebhardt of 
Guttenberg, a student at Clayton Ridge High School; Jackson Wallace 
Andrew Lampe of Dubuque, a student at Wahlert High School; and Vienna 
B. Rossmnanith of Ames, a student at Ames High School.
  The U.S. Presidential Scholars Program was established to recognize 
and honor some of our Nation's most distinguished graduating high 
school seniors. This prestigious honor recognizes students for their 
exceptional academic achievement, leadership, and contributions to 
their communities.
  I am proud to see these students from across our State recognized for 
their impressive accomplishments. Their achievements reflect the 
outstanding educational opportunities available in Iowa, the support 
and commitment of their families and teachers, and the promise of 
Iowa's next generation of leaders.
  I congratulate Christopher, Jackson, and Vienna on receiving this 
well-deserved honor. I am confident they will continue to make their 
home towns, schools, and families proud, and I look forward to 
following their accomplishments in the years to come.●
```
