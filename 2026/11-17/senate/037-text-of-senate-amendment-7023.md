# Text of Senate Amendment 7023

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 7023. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 61, line 2 of the amendment, strike ``or''.
       On page 61, line 7 of the amendment, strike the period and 
     insert ``; or''.
       On page 61 of the amendment, between lines 7 and 8, insert 
     the following:
       ``(vii) a shortage of qualified personnel within the 
     Federal agency responsible for preparing or reviewing the 
     environmental document or issuing the authorization that 
     materially impairs the ability of the agency to meet the 
     relevant deadline established under paragraph (1) or (2).
```
