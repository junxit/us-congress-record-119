# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S.Res. 959

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. HUSTED. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions 
of S. Res. 959.
  There being no objection, as a further mark of respect for the late 
John D. ``Jay'' Rockefeller, IV, former Senator from West Virginia, the 
Senate, at 6:08 p.m., adjourned until Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 
10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
