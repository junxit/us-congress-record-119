# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 2026

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2347

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 2026

  Mr. HUSTED. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, November 18; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume 
consideration of H.R. 2347; further, that all postcloture time expire 
on substitute amendment No. 6883 at 11:45 a.m.; and if Senator Merkley 
or his designee raises a point of order on substitute amendment No. 
6883, the Senate vote in relation to the point of order at 11:45 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
