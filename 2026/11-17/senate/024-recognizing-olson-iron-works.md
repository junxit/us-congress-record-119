# RECOGNIZING OLSON IRON WORKS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING OLSON IRON WORKS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, I am pleased to recognize Olson Iron 
Works of Greene, County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the Week.
  Shane Olson's passion for building inspired him to open Olson Iron 
Works. Shane discovered a love for hands-on work at a young age when he 
learned how to weld from his father and shop teacher. At the age of 17, 
Shane enlisted in the Marine Corps, where he continued building his 
skills by working on AV-8B Harriers, lifted trucks, and different types 
of gas, diesel, and turbine-powered equipment. After leaving the 
Marines, Shane returned to Iowa where his wife Alisa suggested that he 
turn his passion into a small business. In 2012, Shane bought and 
remodeled an old barn and started Olson Iron Works as a part-time 
venture. During the day, Shane helped build and repair equipment for 
local farmers. At night, Shane worked full-time at a factory, where he 
continued to strengthen his welding skills by repairing machines. Shane 
decided he wanted to do more for his country, which led him to join the 
Iowa Air National Guard as a firefighter. There, he was able to put his 
welding and fabricating skills to use by developing protective 
equipment during his deployments. Shane continued serving as a 
firefighter for 20 years and worked in the shop on his days off. 
Eventually, Shane shifted his focus to Olson Iron Works, making it his 
full-time job in 2018.
  What began as a small operation in a tiny barn has grown into a 
14,000-square-foot shop with a team of four employees including Shane's 
wife, Alisa, who handles logistical matters like financing and 
contracting for the shop.
  Shane also continues what he considers his ``artistic'' roots by 
crafting ornamental garden pieces out of metal. Whether it is a 
monogram, firepit, basketball hoop, or a custom item, Shane and his 
team ensure that their customers receive the highest quality products. 
That quality keeps customers coming back, as 80 percent of Olson Iron 
Works' customers are repeat buyers.
  In addition to running a small business, Shane remains an active 
member of the veteran community. Olson Iron Works regularly makes and 
donates metal flags honoring deceased veterans and fallen first 
responders in the area. Additionally, Shane is a lifetime member of the 
American Legion and VFW, where he often volunteers with the Legion's 
post in Churdan, IA, and the VFW post in Jefferson. Furthermore, Shane 
has previously served on the Greene County Conservation Board and 
Greene County Veterans Affairs Commission. Today, Shane is also a 
member of his local school board. Through these acts of service, Shane 
has demonstrated that he cares deeply for his fellow Iowans and the 
veteran community.

[[Page S5721]]

  I am pleased to recognize Shane and the employees of Olson Iron Works 
for their commitment to serving Greene County. I look forward to their 
continued success and wish them the very best in the years 
ahead.●
```
