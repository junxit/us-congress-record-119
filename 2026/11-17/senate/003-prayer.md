# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, do not forget our lawmakers. In the midst of the 
difficult challenges they face and the weighty responsibilities they 
carry, remember them in Your mercy. As they struggle to protect 
freedom, defend the principles upon which this Nation was founded, and 
secure a better future for generations yet to come, turn Your ear 
toward them and answer their prayers.
  When their spirits grow weary, renew their strength. When uncertainty 
clouds their judgment, grant them clarity. When discouragement 
threatens their commitment, restore their optimism and remind them that 
nothing is impossible for You.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
