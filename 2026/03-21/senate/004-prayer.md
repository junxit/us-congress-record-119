# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty God, our shelter from life's storms, give to the Members of 
this body a faith strong enough to face the tempest of our time.
  Lord, strengthen them to confront with courage the challenges that 
come, knowing that Your purposes, providence, and power will prevail. 
Give Your Senators a constancy of purpose that will motivate them to do 
Your will.
  Rule as sovereign Lord in this Chamber, guiding the deliberations, 
debates, and decisions.
  And, Lord, let there be peace on Earth. We pray in Your sublime Name. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
