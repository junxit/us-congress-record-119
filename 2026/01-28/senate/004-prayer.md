# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Answer us, O God, when we call. Be gracious to us and hear our 
prayers. Look on our Nation with favor for Your promises are sure. 
Lord, we thank You that so many of our Nation's Founders put their 
trust in You. Make us worthy of this Godly heritage. May Your presence 
on Capitol Hill today so influence our Senators that the thoughts they 
think, the words they speak, and the work they do will honor You. Lord, 
don't be far from us but continue to be our hope for the years to come. 
Help us also to remember how You have sustained us in the past as You 
continue to provide for our daily needs.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
