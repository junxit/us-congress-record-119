# RECOGNIZING SURVIVAL GARDEN SEEDS

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                   RECOGNIZING SURVIVAL GARDEN SEEDS

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, as a member and former chairman of 
the Senate Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each month 
I recognize and celebrate the American entrepreneurial spirit by 
highlighting the success of a small business in my home State of Idaho. 
Today, I am proud to honor Survival Garden Seeds in Rigby as the Idaho 
Small Business of the Month for January 2026.
  Jason Spahr and Daryl Ward started Survival Garden Seeds in April 
2020 as a way to combat supply chain disruptions and food shortages. 
The small family business was inspired by the Victory Garden movement 
of World Wars I and II, which encouraged people to grow food at home to 
support their communities. As the business grew over the following 
years, Jason and his wife Arianne assumed leadership, transforming it 
into the Idaho-based operation it is today. Through Survival Garden 
Seeds, the couple and their team aim to help people become more self-
reliant through gardening.
  Starting with just 30 seed varieties in a small facility, Jason and 
his family handled all packaging and shipping for incoming orders. 
Thanks to their hard work and long hours, Survival Garden Seeds has 
grown to 23 employees and now offers over 400 different kinds of seeds.
  In the 5 years since its founding, Survival Garden Seeds has evolved 
into a fully domestic manufacturing business, designing and building 
its own seed-packing equipment and managing production, packaging, and 
distribution under one roof. Their in-house process has helped secure 
partnerships with companies like Walmart and Amazon, fueling business 
growth, job creation, and greater supply chain reliability.
  Survival Garden Seeds places a strong focus on using education to 
promote gardening techniques for long-term self-reliance. Jason, 
Arianne, and the team partner with local food banks, community gardens, 
and missions in Africa to teach sustainable gardening practices. They 
also regularly offer gardening and homesteading classes to Idahoans 
throughout the Rigby area.
  Congratulations to Survival Garden Seeds and its team on being 
recognized as the Idaho Small Business of the Month for January 2026. 
Your dedication to excellence makes Idaho proud, and I look forward to 
your continued growth and success.●
```
