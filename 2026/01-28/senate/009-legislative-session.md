                       Working Families Tax Cuts

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, this past Monday was the start of tax 
filing season. Now, I know most Americans aren't usually excited about 
the start of tax season, but this year, there is less reason for dread 
because thanks to the Working Families Tax Cuts, most Americans are 
going to see a lower tax bill. That means bigger tax refunds for a lot 
of Americans, as much as $1,000 more than last year, according to some 
analysts.
  The Working Families Tax Cuts is literally putting more money in 
Americans' pockets. As the American people file their tax returns this 
year, they will see the numerous ways the Working Families Tax Cuts 
benefit them.
  Thanks to this legislation, Americans can count on more money in 
their pockets for the long term. For most Americans, tax relief starts 
with their tax rate and the standard deduction, which over 90 percent 
of taxpayers in this country claim.
  In 2017, Republicans lowered tax rates across the board and just 
about doubled the standard deduction. In our landmark bill last year, 
Republicans made that relief permanent, and then we raised the standard 
deduction even further.
  On top of that, we created a bonus deduction for seniors, an 
additional

[[Page S302]]

$6,000 deduction to reduce the tax burden of more than 30 million 
Americans over the age of 65.
  Then there is the significant tax relief we delivered for families. 
In 2017, Republicans doubled--doubled--the child tax credit. Last year, 
we raised it even further to $2,200 per child. And then, we linked the 
child tax credit to inflation, which means its value will keep pace 
with families' actual costs, and again that is also permanent.
  Parents will also have the opportunity to open new investment 
accounts for their newborns when they file their taxes this season. 
Those accounts provide another tax advantage option for parents to 
invest in their children's future. The money can be used after kids 
turn 18 for their education, to start a business, to buy their first 
home; in short, to take advantage of opportunities available to them 
and get a head start on their American dream.
  Those are retroactive to 2025. So parents who had a baby last year 
can apply for one of these accounts when they file their taxes this 
season. Hard-working Americans are the ones who benefit from 
Republicans' landmark tax relief bill.
  Perhaps the biggest boost goes to those Americans who earn tips and 
overtime pay. Waiters, busboys, hairstylists and barbers, Uber, Lyft, 
and taxi drivers are among the hard-working Americans who will be able 
to deduct their tips on their taxes this year. That will reduce their 
tax bills and let them keep a lot more of the money they earn.
  The same is true of workers who put in overtime throughout the year. 
Police officers, firefighters, paramedics, and nurses work important 
jobs, and that often means they have to work extra hours during the 
week or come in on holidays. Well, thanks to the Working Families Tax 
Cuts, they will be able to keep more of the overtime pay they earn for 
that extra time away from their loved ones.
  I don't have to remind anybody about the inflation crisis started by 
Democrats 5 years ago. Higher prices have affected everybody. But 
relief is on the way. Affordability starts with more money in your 
pocket, and that is exactly what the Working Families Tax Cuts 
delivers.
  Whether you are just getting started or you are raising a family or 
you are trying to enjoy your golden years, the Working Families Tax 
Cuts has something for you. Republicans wrote this bill with hard-
working Americans in mind, and that is who is going to see the benefits 
this tax season and in the years to come.
