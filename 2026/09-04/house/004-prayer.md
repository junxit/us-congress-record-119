# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of our lives, the source of our strength, we commit our work to 
You. May the sweat of our brows and the calluses on our hands, the 
hours spent and the sacrifices made, prove our commitment to the tasks 
You have set before us.
  All that we strive for, all that we hope to accomplish, we surrender 
to Your will and entrust to Your keeping.
  Then may Your blessing be upon our efforts, that by Your grace they 
would be in concert with Your enduring plan for Your people. Open our 
eyes to see what success looks like: the welfare of the people we have 
been called to serve, the soundness of the communities and the country 
in which we live, and the flourishing of the families we love.
  With renewed dedication to our labor and rekindled love for You, we 
offer ourselves and our prayers to You in the power of Your sovereign 
name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
