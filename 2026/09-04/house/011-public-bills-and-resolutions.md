# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 1512, H.Res. 1513, H.R. 10298, H.R. 10299, H.R. 10300, H.R. 10301, H.R. 10302, H.R. 10303

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. FOSTER (for himself, Mr. Fitzpatrick, Ms. Adams, 
             Mr. Bishop, Mr. Carson, Ms. Clarke of New York, Ms. 
             Craig, Mr. Figures, Mr. Garcia of Illinois, and Ms. 
             Norton):
       H.R. 10298. A bill to amend the Higher Education Act of 
     1965 to allow certain payments made by public service 
     employees to qualify for public service repayment, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Education and Workforce.
           By Mr. KUSTOFF (for himself, Mr. Rose, and Mr. Van 
             Epps):
       H.R. 10299. A bill to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 83 East Park Street in 
     Alamo, Tennessee, as the ``Kenneth Nolen Davis Post Office''; 
     to the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
           By Mr. McGOVERN:
       H.R. 10300. A bill to foster accountability for digital 
     content providers; to the Committee on the Judiciary, and in 
     addition to the Committee on Energy and Commerce, for a 
     period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each 
     case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. NORCROSS:
       H.R. 10301. A bill to award a Congressional Gold Medal to 
     Peter McGuire in recognition of his contributions to the 
     American Labor Movement; to the Committee on Financial 
     Services, and in addition to the Committee on House 
     Administration, for a period to be subsequently determined by 
     the Speaker, in each case for consideration of such 
     provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the committee 
     concerned.
           By Mr. PETERS (for himself and Mr. Evans of Colorado):
       H.R. 10302. A bill to amend the Federal Power Act with 
     respect to the coordination of Federal authorizations for 
     transmission facilities, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Ms. POU (for herself, Mr. Bresnahan, and Ms. 
             Scholten):
       H.R. 10303. A bill to direct the Administrator of the 
     Federal Aviation Administration to establish a flight deck 
     training program for certain employees of the Federal 
     Aviation Administration, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
           By Mrs. BICE (for herself, Ms. Houlahan, Mr. Womack, 
             and Ms. Elfreth):
       H. Res. 1512. A resolution celebrating the 50th anniversary 
     of the admission of women to the United States service 
     academies and honoring the pioneering women of the Class of 
     1980; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania (for himself and Mr. 
             Panetta):
       H. Res. 1513. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of the week beginning on September 6, 2026, as 
     ``Celebrate Community Week''; to the Committee on Oversight 
     and Government Reform.

                          ____________________
```
