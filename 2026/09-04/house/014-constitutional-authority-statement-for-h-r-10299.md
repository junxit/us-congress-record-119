# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10299

**Measures:** H.R. 10299

```
           By Mr. KUSTOFF:
       H.R. 10299.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to establish post offices, as 
     enumerated in Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United 
     States Constitution.
```
