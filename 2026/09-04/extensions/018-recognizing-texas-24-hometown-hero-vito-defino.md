# RECOGNIZING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO VITO DEFINO

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             RECOGNIZING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO VITO DEFINO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize our Texas-24 
Hometown Hero, Bedford resident and World War II veteran, Vito Defino. 
Vito was drafted for World War II only one month after graduating high 
school. When given the choice between the Army Air Corps or Navy, he 
chose to fly, serving at various bases across the country as a right 
wing gunner on the B-29 Superfortress.
  Throughout his time, Vito was promoted to Corporal and then Sergeant 
in only 14 months. During WWII, he met Mary, his future wife, who was 
working in military intelligence, and they were both honorably 
discharged on the same day.
  Following WWII, Vito earned his degree in education, while working 
evenings at General Electric. Throughout his career, Vito poured into 
the students with various roles as a math teacher, physical education 
coach, and later a vice principal for 30 years.
  Vito is currently 100 years old and lives in Texas with his daughter, 
Dee, and her family. I thank Vito Defino for his service to our Nation 
and for being an inspiration to those around him.

                          ____________________
```
