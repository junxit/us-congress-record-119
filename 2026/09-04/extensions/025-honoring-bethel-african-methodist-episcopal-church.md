# HONORING BETHEL AFRICAN METHODIST EPISCOPAL CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
           HONORING BETHEL AFRICAN METHODIST EPISCOPAL CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a great church in my 
District, Bethel African Methodist Episcopal Church, located on South 
Blvd in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Established in 1867 by free Black men 
during the Reconstruction era, Bethel AME Church stands as one of the 
oldest pillars of faith and community in South Baton Rouge.
  The church's founding in the aftermath of emancipation marked a 
powerful milestone in the history of African American civic leadership. 
Throughout the turbulent years of Jim Crow and the civil rights 
movement, Bethel AME Church served as a sanctuary for hope, resilience, 
and justice, providing a forum for community advocacy and spiritual 
upliftment.
  Bethel's significance within the AME denomination is longstanding, 
including hosting the unveiling of a U.S. Postal Service Black Heritage 
stamp in 2016 to honor AME founder Richard Allen and serving as a proud 
site for the Louisiana AME Annual Conference. These moments underscore 
the church's vital role in preserving and celebrating the heritage of 
the AME tradition.
  Today, Bethel AME Church continues its legacy of service through 
ongoing outreach, including food distribution programs that support 
families in need across Baton Rouge. Its commitment to compassion and 
community remains unwavering, reflecting the enduring spirit of those 
who founded it more than 150 years ago.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues and fellow citizens to recognize 
Bethel African Methodist Episcopal Church for its historic leadership, 
dedication to faith, and steadfast service to South Baton Rouge. May 
its legacy of worship, resilience, and outreach inspire generations to 
come.
```
