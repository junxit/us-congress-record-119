# HONORING THE LIFE OF MARVIN LEONARD WOODALL

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE OF MARVIN LEONARD WOODALL

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Marvin Leonard Woodall of Doylestown, Pennsylvania, who 
passed away peacefully at the age of 88.
  Marvin Woodall was a distinguished business leader, innovator, 
philanthropist, and devoted family man whose remarkable career and 
service to his community left an enduring impact on countless lives.
  A graduate of the University of Oklahoma, Marvin began his 
professional career with Procter & Gamble before joining Johnson & 
Johnson in 1963. During his 37-year career with the company, he 
excelled in 19 positions and ultimately served as President of Johnson 
& Johnson Interventional Systems. In that role, he was a driving force 
behind the development of the first coronary stent, a groundbreaking 
advancement that transformed the field of cardiology and improved the 
lives of millions of people around the world.
  Following his retirement, Marvin continued his leadership in the 
medical community. His vision and generosity helped establish the 
Woodall Center for Heart and Vascular Care at Doylestown Hospital. He 
served as CEO of the Cardiovascular Research Foundation, president of 
the Ann Silverman Community Health Clinic, and on the boards of 
numerous medical device companies.
  Marvin was equally devoted to his community. After settling in 
Doylestown in 1985, he and his beloved wife, Dee Ann, became active 
members of Doylestown Presbyterian Church, where Marvin served as an 
elder. He generously gave his time and leadership to organizations 
throughout Eastern Pennsylvania, including the Heritage Conservancy, 
Bucks County Playhouse Artists, Bucks County Herald Foundation, Bucks 
County Symphony Orchestra, Bucks County Choral Society, Village 
Improvement Association, Network of Victim Assistance, Americans for 
Native Americans, and the Mercer and Michener Museums.
  Marvin will be remembered for his warmth, curiosity, and zest for 
life. An avid golfer, he enjoyed playing at Doylestown Country Club and 
courses around the world. He loved jazz, theater, and cheering on the 
Oklahoma Sooners. Above all, he cherished his family and the life he 
built with Dee Ann, his beloved wife of 65 years.
  On behalf of the United States Congress, I extend my deepest 
condolences to the Woodall family and join them in celebrating the life 
of a man whose leadership, innovation, generosity, and devotion to his 
community touched lives far beyond his own. May Marvin Woodall's memory 
continue to inspire all who had the privilege of knowing him.
```
