# RECOGNIZING STOUT STREET FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                  RECOGNIZING STOUT STREET FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Stout 
Street Foundation on their 50th anniversary.
  Fifty years ago, the Stout Street Foundation opened its doors as a 
safe, supportive home for Coloradans battling addiction. For the past 
five decades, it has provided hope, stability, and a real pathway to 
lasting recovery. What started as a small recovery house in Denver has 
grown into one of Colorado's most trusted long-term residential 
treatment communities, offering recovery services, workforce 
development, counseling, and peer support to individuals working to 
rebuild their lives.
  Today, the Foundation serves more than 400 individuals each year 
through its therapeutic community model, which emphasizes 
accountability, life skills development, and lasting personal growth. 
Over the last 50 years, its work has helped over 10,000 Coloradans 
achieve lasting sobriety, reconnect with their families, and return to 
their communities with renewed purpose and genuine opportunity.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate the Stout Street Foundation on its 50th 
anniversary and wish the organization continued success in its 
important work for many years to come.
```
