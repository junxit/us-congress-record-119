# HONORING ELEVATE CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                        HONORING ELEVATE CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Elevate Church, 
located in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Founded in 2013 and currently led by 
Apostle Keith Richard, Elevate Church is a non-denominational ministry 
dedicated to building the body of Christ by connecting individuals with 
their faith and helping them grow in their relationship with Christ.
  Apostle Richard answered the call to pastor in 2010, and together 
with Lady Tanesha Richard, brought their vision for Elevate Church to 
life in 2012. Since its inception, the church has focused on equipping 
its members to serve through their God-given gifts, talents, and 
skills. Elevate Church's mission is evident in its commitment to 
ministry, outreach, and missions, encouraging its congregation to 
become servant leaders within the community.
  Elevate Church continues to pursue its vision of welcoming the 
community and training people to live out God's purpose. The church 
provides opportunities for members to share the love of Christ, both 
locally and around the world, making a meaningful impact on the lives 
of individuals in East Baton Rouge Parish and beyond.
  Mr. Speaker, I encourage my colleagues to join me as we celebrate 
Elevate Church, the pastor, and the dedicated congregation for over a 
decade of outstanding service to the Baton Rouge area. Their legacy of 
faith, service, and leadership continues to inspire and uplift families 
throughout East Baton Rouge Parish, making a lasting difference in the 
community.
```
