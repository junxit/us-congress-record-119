# RECOGNIZING DANIEL ROSALES

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING DANIEL ROSALES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Daniel Rosales 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Daniel has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Daniel, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Daniel's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Arvada West High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Daniel Rosales on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
