# HONORING ST. MARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                    HONORING ST. MARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an outstanding church 
in my District, St. Mary Baptist Church, for serving as a fixture in 
Baton Rouge, Louisiana since its founding in 1929. From its humble 
beginnings in a wood-framed cottage, the church grew steadily, 
establishing its first sanctuary and expanding into a vibrant campus 
that now anchors the neighborhood.
  Over the last several decades, St. Mary Baptist Church has evolved 
into a major institution, offering impactful ministries that meet

[[Page E872]]

the needs of Baton Rouge families. Its prison outreach program extends 
hope and support to those incarcerated, while dedicated nursing-home 
ministries bring comfort to elders throughout the community. In 2004, 
the church launched the Clothes Closet, providing essential clothing to 
neighbors in need and strengthening its tradition of practical service.
  Today Reverend Conway Knighton helps carry on the mission of 
promoting positive change and advocating for justice by collaborating 
with local organizations, such as Together Baton Rouge, to address 
issues affecting the community.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues and fellow citizens to recognize 
St. Mary Baptist Church, Reverend Knighton, and the congregation for 
nearly a century of hope, compassion, and service. Its enduring legacy 
reflects a steadfast commitment to uplifting families and neighborhoods 
across Baton Rouge, making a lasting impact that will inspire 
generations to come.
```
