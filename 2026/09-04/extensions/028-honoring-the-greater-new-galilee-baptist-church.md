# HONORING THE GREATER NEW GALILEE BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
            HONORING THE GREATER NEW GALILEE BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor The Greater New 
Galilee Baptist Church, a cornerstone of faith and service in the 
Scotlandville community of Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Founded in the 
1960s, and currently led by Pastor Dr. Mark A. Litt, Sr., this 
remarkable church has continually uplifted generations of families.
  Greater New Galilee Baptist Church has been a pillar of the 
Scotlandville community for generations, with its roots on Wilbur 
Street going back to the 1960s. Since 1967, when the congregation 
secured property and financed the construction of its church and 
educational building, the church has served as a beacon of hope, 
providing a lasting center for

[[Page E873]]

worship, education, and community life. Its commitment to spiritual 
growth, fellowship, and outreach has shaped the identity of 
Scotlandville, making the church a trusted haven for those seeking 
guidance, support, and a sense of belonging.
  Through the establishment of the C.N. Burrell, Sr. Christian Academy, 
GNGBC expanded its impact by offering early education and childcare. 
This faith-based institution continues to nurture children and 
families, providing an environment where young minds can learn, grow, 
and flourish. Thereby strengthening the bonds of community and ensuring 
a legacy of faith-driven education in Baton Rouge.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in recognizing The 
Greater New Galilee Baptist Church for its unwavering dedication to 
service, education, and spiritual guidance. May Pastor Litt and the 
congregation continue to inspire, nurture, and empower families, 
sustaining the church's treasured traditions and vital role in 
community life for generations yet to come.
```
