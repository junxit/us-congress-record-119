# RECOGNIZING ROYAL FAMILY KIDS ARVADA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                  RECOGNIZING ROYAL FAMILY KIDS ARVADA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Royal Family 
Kids from Arvada, Colorado for winning the 2026 Golden Rotary Ethics in 
Business Award for a not-for-profit organization.
  Royal Family Kids Arvada is a subchapter of For The Children, a 
national nonprofit that has served at-risk foster youth since 1985. In 
their unrelenting support for children who have experienced relational 
trauma in the foster care system or abuse, Royal Family Kids Arvada is 
driven by the mission to ensure every ``kid from hard places'' is 
treated well.
  Their commitment to responsible stewardship, financial transparency, 
trauma-informed care, and long-term mentorship serves both their donors 
and the healthy development of

[[Page E870]]

each child they serve. Supporting at-risk youth in our community is 
such critical work. As someone who faced many challenges growing up, I 
know my life is on a much different path because of the adults and 
teachers in Jefferson County who cared about me and invested in me as a 
teen.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to thank Royal Family Kids Arvada for all they do to 
support at-risk youth in Jefferson County.

                          ____________________
```
