# RECOGNIZING JAKE CANTER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JAKE CANTER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jake Canter, 
born in Evergreen, Colorado, for representing the United States in the 
Men's Snowboard Slopestyle at the 2026 Winter Olympic Games in Milan, 
Italy and congratulate him on his winning of the bronze medal.
  In 2016, Jake experienced a traumatic brain injury that caused him to 
go into a six-day coma with a 20 percent survival rate. Jake had to 
relearn how to walk and talk and ultimately returned to snowboarding in 
2017. With hard work and community support, he ended up earning the 
Junior Jam title at the 2018 U.S. Open and at just 15 years old Jake 
competed in the inaugural 2019 Knuckle Huck at X Games Aspen, earning a 
silver medal. Jake went on to win a bronze medal at the 2022 Laax Open 
and represented the USA at the 2023 FIS Snowboard World Championships 
in Bakuriani, Georgia. And in 2026, Jake made his Olympic debut in 
Milan and won bronze.
  Jake Canter's story is one of resilience in the face of challenges. 
Not only did he overcome the obstacles but found growth and is now 
inspiring other Coloradans to do the same.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Jake Canter for winning the bronze medal 
and representing Colorado as part of the 2026 Winter U.S. Olympic Team 
at the Milan Olympics.

                          ____________________
```
