# HONORING SACRED HEART OF JESUS

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                     HONORING SACRED HEART OF JESUS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sacred Heart of Jesus, 
an extraordinary church in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Founded as a mission 
in 1924 and established as a parish in 1928, Sacred Heart of Jesus has 
played a pivotal role in the spiritual life of the city's Italian 
immigrant community, providing them with a welcoming place to worship 
in their native language under the guidance of its first pastor, Father 
Dominic Blasco, a native of Sicily.
  Sacred Heart of Jesus made history in 1929 by opening Baton Rouge's 
first parochial school built by a church parish, laying the foundation 
for a legacy of Catholic education that continues to shape generations. 
In 1942, the parish dedicated its magnificent present church, a 
testament to the generosity and resilience of its parishioners during 
World War II. The church is renowned for its Byzantine- and Sicilian-
inspired architecture, stunning murals by Benedictine monk Dom Gregory 
DeWit, and exquisite stained glass crafted by Emil Frei Glass Studios.
  Today, under the leadership of Reverend Joshua D. Johnson, Sacred 
Heart of Jesus stands as a cherished Baton Rouge landmark and a symbol 
of unity, cultural heritage, and faith. Its enduring presence continues 
to inspire and uplift families throughout East Baton

[[Page E871]]

Rouge Parish, preserving a rich tradition that honors both its 
immigrant roots and its commitment to service.
  Mr. Speaker, I encourage my colleagues to recognize Sacred Heart of 
Jesus, Reverend Johnson, and the devoted congregation for their 
remarkable contributions to Baton Rouge. The church's legacy as a 
beacon of hope, education, and community will continue to make a 
lasting impact for generations to come.
```
