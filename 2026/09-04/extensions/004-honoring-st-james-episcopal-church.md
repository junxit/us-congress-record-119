# HONORING ST. JAMES EPISCOPAL CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING ST. JAMES EPISCOPAL CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor St. James Episcopal 
Church, founded in 1844 and presently led by Reverend Christopher 
Duncan. This venerable house of worship stands as a striking example of 
Gothic Revival architecture, with its graceful spire and stained-glass 
windows enduring as a cherished landmark in Baton Rouge for more than 
180 years.
  St. James Episcopal Church's remarkable history is marked by 
resilience, having survived devastating tornadoes, outbreaks of yellow 
fever, the Battle of Baton Rouge, Civil War occupation, and years of 
Reconstruction. Through each challenge, the church not only preserved 
its structure but deepened its role as a haven of faith and hope for 
countless families.
  For generations, St. James Episcopal Church has fostered unity, 
compassion. and spiritual guidance, offering a sanctuary for worship 
and community service. Its outreach efforts and steadfast ministry have 
uplifted parishioners and neighbors alike, providing comfort in times 
of crisis and celebration during moments of joy.
  The church's enduring presence is a testament to the unwavering 
spirit of its congregation and the rich heritage it represents within 
Baton Rouge and beyond. Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to honor 
Reverend Duncan and the faithful members of St. James Episcopal Church 
for their extraordinary commitment and lasting contributions. May this 
historic church continue to inspire, serve, and bless all who enter its 
doors for generations to come.
```
