# HONORING LIVING FAITH CHRISTIAN CENTER

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING LIVING FAITH CHRISTIAN CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Living Faith Christian 
Center, as we celebrate its service to the Baton Rouge community for 
several decades. Founded in 1986 and currently led by Bishop Raymond 
Johnson, Living Faith Christian Center has been unwavering in its 
mission to help people enjoy the life promised by Jesus in John 10:10. 
Grounded in the integrity of God's Word, the church believes that every 
Christian can live a victorious and successful life through faith.
  The church has remained committed to serving, reaching, and touching 
lives with the message of hope and God's love. Each week their music 
ministry leads them into the presence of God. The ministry team, 
consisting of their deacon, ushers, ministers, greeters, TV crew, youth 
and nursery, and others work together to ensure that all have an 
opportunity to receive God's best. Bishop Johnson's unique teaching 
style reaches all generations, making Living Faith a dynamic church 
with a vision to see God's blessings realized for all.
  Living Faith Christian Center's outreach extends far beyond its 
walls, serving the community through a variety of ministries. These 
include the Teaching of the Word, television and radio ministry, tapes 
and books, men's and women's fellowships, nursing home faith, prison 
ministries, LFCC Youth Ministry, marriage fellowships, singles 
ministry, and alcohol and drug ministry. The church's dedication to 
being a blessing to the body of Christ in each walk of life is evident 
in its compassionate service and commitment to sharing God's love.
  Mr. Speaker, I encourage my colleagues to honor Living Faith 
Christian Center, Bishop Raymond Johnson, and the devoted members for 
their outstanding contributions to Baton Rouge. Their legacy of faith, 
hope, and service continues to inspire and uplift families throughout 
East Baton Rouge Parish, making a lasting difference in countless 
lives.
```
