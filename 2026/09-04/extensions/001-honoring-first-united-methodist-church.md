# HONORING FIRST UNITED METHODIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING FIRST UNITED METHODIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor First United Methodist 
Church, a venerable institution whose roots reach back to 1834, making 
it one of Baton Rouge's oldest and most cherished congregations. 
Currently led by Reverend Brady Whitton, this historic church has 
served as a beacon of faith and community for nearly two centuries.
  Its enduring red brick sanctuary on North Blvd stands as a testament 
to the spirit and resilience of generations who have gathered within 
its walls, weathering the trials of the Civil War era and blossoming 
into a thriving Sunday School of 500 members by 1912 and moved into its 
present downtown sanctuary, in 1926. Perhaps most significant, the 
church chose to remain downtown as other congregations moved toward 
newer areas of the city, preserving its place as a longstanding 
downtown institution.
  Throughout its long history, First United Methodist Church has 
continually responded to the evolving needs of Baton Rouge, pioneering 
radio and television ministry, establishing robust youth programs, and 
creating centers for adults and early childhood education. The church's 
commitment to inclusivity and outreach has led to the creation of 
vibrant children's ministries and innovative neighborhood service 
programs.
  Today, the church's legacy is renewed through heartfelt outreach 
initiatives such as Revive225 home repairs, the Love Your Neighbor van 
providing aid to unhoused residents, community meals, and a host of 
ministries that nurture and uplift the surrounding neighborhood. For 
nearly 200 years, First United Methodist Church has been more than a 
place of worship, it has been a steadfast ally, a gathering place, and 
a wellspring of hope for all who enter its doors.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in honoring Reverend 
Whitton and the dedicated congregation of First United Methodist Church 
for their extraordinary commitment to faith, service, education. and 
the enduring spirit of community. May this historic church continue to 
inspire, serve, and bless Baton Rouge for generations to come.
```
