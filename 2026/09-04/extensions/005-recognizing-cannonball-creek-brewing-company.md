# RECOGNIZING CANNONBALL CREEK BREWING COMPANY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
              RECOGNIZING CANNONBALL CREEK BREWING COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Cannonball 
Creek Brewing Company of Golden, Colorado, for receiving both gold and 
silver medals at the Beer World Cup, designating their Project Alpha 81 
and Mindbender brews as world-class.
  The World Beer Cup, otherwise known as the ``Olympics of Beer,'' 
evaluates beers from around the world and recognizes the top brewers 
and their beers, drawing 8,166 entries from 50 countries. Beyond their 
medal, the Cannonball Creek Brewing Company won in the most competitive 
category, known as West Coast-style IPA.
  Small businesses like Cannonball Creek Brewing Company help drive 
Golden's local economy and our state's economy. For both natives and 
members of Golden's thriving tourism industry, Cannonball Creek Brewing 
Company has provided excellent service and beers for over 20 years, 
contributing to Colorado's leadership in producing outstanding craft 
brews.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate the Cannonball Creek Brewing Company on 
receiving a gold and silver medal at the World Beer Cup and thank them 
for their contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
