# HONORING MARJORIE P. McCRAW

**Speaking:** Keating, William R. (K000375) [D-MA]

```
                      HONORING MARJORIE P. McCRAW

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. WILLIAM R. KEATING

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. KEATING. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Marjorie P. McGraw as 
she celebrates her 95th birthday.
  Born on September 7, 1931, Marjorie has devoted her life to 
philanthropy, compassion, and community service. Alongside her late 
husband, John McGraw, Marjorie has demonstrated extraordinary 
generosity and civic leadership through the Elizabeth B. McGraw 
Foundation, supporting organizations that strengthen the health, 
welfare, and future of the Cape Cod community
  Marjorie first met John, the love of her life, in 1951. They married 
in December later that year in Jacksonville, Florida. Together, they 
raised their six children, John, Lee, Mark. Scott, Lynne, and Brian in 
New Jersey, while also spending significant time in Osterville, 
Massachusetts; Gulf Stream, Florida; and New York City. As avid antique 
collectors, Marjorie and John welcomed many tours of their Osterville 
home over the years.
  Through her ongoing philanthropy, Marjorie has served as a 
transformational benefactor to the Boys and Girls Club of Cape Cod, 
helping to provide safe spaces, mentorship, and educational 
opportunities for generations of young people. Her extraordinary 
support for Cape Cod Healthcare has improved access to quality care and 
the well-being of countless community members across the region. As an 
animal activist, Marjorie has rescued and raised many Bichons Frise, 
and with her daughter Lynne, their volunteer work and enduring 
commitment to the MSPCA Cape Cod reflect a deep passion for animal 
welfare.
  On September 7, 2026, Marjorie will celebrate her 95th birthday 
surrounded by family and loved ones. Through her compassion, 
generosity, and unwavering commitment to helping others, Marjorie has 
established a legacy of kindness and service that will enhance the 
lives of families throughout the Cape Cod community for many years to 
come.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to honor Marjorie McGraw, and I ask that my 
colleagues join me in celebrating this momentous occasion and wishing 
Marjorie many more years of health and happiness.

                          ____________________
```
