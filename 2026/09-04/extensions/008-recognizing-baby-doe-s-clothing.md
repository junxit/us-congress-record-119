# RECOGNIZING BABY DOE'S CLOTHING

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                    RECOGNIZING BABY DOE'S CLOTHING

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Baby Doe's 
Clothing from Golden, Colorado, for winning the 2026 Golden Rotary 
Club's Ethics in Business Award for a for-profit organization.
  For over two and a half decades, Baby Doe's Clothing has supported 
the Golden community through volunteer efforts, donated goods, and by 
fundraising for local organizations. Named after Colorado infamous 
historical figure Elizabeth ``Baby Doe'' Tabor, their organization 
sells a variety of women's clothing and accessories in downtown Golden. 
Baby Doe's Clothing stands as a model business promoting the best 
interest of Golden--recycling their waste, prioritizing transparency 
with customers, and donating excess inventory to local non-profits 
supporting low-income and previously incarcerated women.
  Baby Doe's Clothing owner, Lynn Conrad and original founder Nancy 
Mason, have also gone above and beyond for our community by 
volunteering their time with the Golden Chamber of Commerce and for 
cherished community events like the Golden Fine Arts Festival. They 
also contribute as community sponsors for the organizations GoFarm and 
the Golden Civic Foundation. In the words of her award nominee, Lynn is 
``all about Golden'' and ``has lived in, worked in, and contributed to 
Golden for over two decades.''
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to recognize Baby Doe's Clothing for all they do for our 
community.
```
