# RECOGNIZING THE COLORADO PASSPORT AGENCY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE COLORADO PASSPORT AGENCY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Colorado 
Passport Agency on its 20th anniversary.
  Since opening on September 29, 2005, the Colorado Passport Agency has 
been assisting constituents in Colorado, Nebraska, and Wyoming with 
their urgent travel needs. The Colorado Passport Agency serves as a 
vital lifeline for travelers facing urgent situations. Since 2023, the 
Colorado Passport Agency has processed nearly 1.3 million passport 
applications on-site through 2025.
  During the unexpected surge of passport applications in 2023, our 
office relied heavily on the Colorado Passport Agency, and they worked 
tirelessly to assist our constituents and my team. Through my office 
alone, the agency has assisted hundreds of constituents--helping them 
attend weddings abroad, reunite with loved ones, and navigate 
unexpected travel emergencies with confidence and peace of mind.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate the Colorado Passport Office on its 20th 
anniversary and thank them for their contribution to our community.
```
