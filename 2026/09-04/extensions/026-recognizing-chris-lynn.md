# RECOGNIZING CHRIS LYNN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING CHRIS LYNN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Chris Lynn 
for his years of service at Seniors Resource Center and to congratulate 
him on his retirement.
  Chris Lynn has dedicated his career to treating older adults with 
dignity, respect, and the support they deserve, making a lasting 
difference across Colorado. As President and CEO of Seniors Resource 
Center, he has been a compassionate and thoughtful leader, guiding one 
of the Jefferson County community's most impactful senior services 
organizations.
  Since 1978, the organization has built a legacy of being a 
comprehensive, community-based provider delivering in-home care, adult 
day programming, care management, and other essential services to 
thousands of seniors and their families. Chris's leadership reflects a 
deeply personal investment in ensuring older adults are supported with 
independence, stability, and access to the resources they need to 
thrive.
  Chris has been a strong voice in Colorado on issues such as unstable 
funding, service accessibility, and the growing demand for aging 
services, consistently emphasizing the real human impact behind policy 
decisions. He serves our community in many capacities, including on 
regional advisory bodies like the Jefferson County Commission on Aging 
and the Denver Regional Council on Governments. He also supports 
mission-driven organizations such as the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, 
the Colorado Multiple Sclerosis Society, and the Courageous Faces 
Foundation. Through advocacy and action, Chris has never lost sight of 
the challenges faced by seniors, especially those on fixed incomes, 
while helping expand essential services such as food distribution and 
coordinated care.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to recognize Mr. Chris Lynn and thank him for his service 
to our community.

                          ____________________
```
