# HONORING COLONEL AMY S. JOHANNSEN

**Speaking:** Sorensen, Eric (S001225) [D-IL]

```
                   HONORING COLONEL AMY S. JOHANNSEN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC SORENSEN

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. SORENSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Colonel Amy S. 
Johannsen of Morrison, Illinois, to mark the conclusion of an 
exceptional 40-year career in the United States Air Force. As Commander 
of the 934th Maintenance Group. Minneapolis Joint Air Reserve Station 
in Minnesota, Colonel Johannsen has led 288 AGR, Reserve, and civilian 
personnel across 17 Air Force Specialty Codes, overseeing critical 
maintenance operations on eight C-130H aircraft on combat airlift 
missions.
  Colonel Johannsen's service reflects the Air Force's ``Aim High. . . 
Fly-Fight-Win'' motto. She supported Desert Shield, Desert Storm, and 
multiple deployments throughout Southwest Asia, including operations in 
Qatar in support of the Global War on Terrorism. Freedom's Sentry, and 
Freedom's Sentinel. Her selection to the TFI AMC-led Tanker Task Force 
under Brigadier General Fountaine further underscores her exceptional 
expertise and trust within the service.
  Colonel Johannsen's distinguished decorations include the Meritorious 
Service Medal with five oak leaf clusters, the Air and Space 
Commendation Medal, the Air and Space Achievement Medal, the Joint 
Meritorious Unit Award, and the Air and Space Outstanding Unit Award 
with two oak leaf clusters.
  Mr. Speaker, Colonel Amy Johannsen's career reflects the highest 
ideals of service, leadership, and commitment to our country. I join 
her family, colleagues, and grateful Nation in congratulating her on 
her well-earned retirement and thanking her for four decades of 
selfless service to the United States.

                          ____________________
```
