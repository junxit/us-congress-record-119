                     RECOGNIZING NASHELY LEON MATOS

                                 ______

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nashely Leon

at Arvada West High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Nashely Leon Matos on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
