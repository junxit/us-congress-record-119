# HONORING FIRST LITTLE ROCK MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
          HONORING FIRST LITTLE ROCK MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor First Little Rock 
Missionary Baptist Church, a historic African American congregation 
located on South 17th Street in the heart of South Baton Rouge, 
Louisiana. National Register documentation notes that Little Rock 
Church existed at the site as early as the 1910's, with the present 
church building reconstructed in 1963. First Little Rock has played a 
vital role in the spiritual and social life of South Baton Rouge for 
more than a century.
  The church's impact extends far beyond worship, reflecting its deep 
commitment to community and civic engagement. During the segregation 
era, Reverend Willie K. Brooks, a distinguished pastor and leader, 
organized efforts to provide Black children with recreational 
opportunities, establishing the United Negro Recreation Association in 
1945 and raising funds for a safe place to swim. This legacy of 
advocacy and service embodies the church's dedication to uplifting and 
empowering the Baton Rouge community.
  Under the current leadership of Pastor Dr. James Riley; First Little 
Rock Missionary Baptist Church remains a steadfast anchor in South 
Baton Rouge. Its pastors continue to participate in civic and public 
events, ensuring the church's enduring presence in the wider community. 
Through more than a century of ministry, First Little Rock's history 
reflects the vital role that Black churches have played in shaping 
Baton Rouge's religious, social, and civic development.
  Mr. Speaker, I encourage my colleagues to recognize First Little Rock 
Missionary Baptist Church, Pastor Dr. James Riley, and the dedicated 
congregation for their remarkable contributions to Baton Rouge. Their 
legacy of faith, service, and community leadership continues to inspire 
and uplift families throughout East Baton Rouge Parish, making a 
lasting difference for generations to come.
```
