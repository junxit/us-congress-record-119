# HONORING ST. JOHN'S UNITED METHODIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING ST. JOHN'S UNITED METHODIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor St. John's United 
Methodist Church, located in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Since its founding 
in 1961, St. John's has been a beacon of faith and compassion for East 
Baton Rouge Parish and currently led by Reverend Lane Cotton Winn.
  The church's impact goes far beyond its sanctuary, with its 
Shepherd's Market food pantry launching in 2012 and quickly becoming a 
lifeline for families facing hunger. Within its first two years, 
Shepherd's Market served 1,421 families and distributed over 430,000 
pounds of food, eventually expanding to serve about 420 families each 
month. In addition to food distribution, St. John's provides ESL 
classes and assistance with Medicaid applications, empowering neighbors 
to overcome barriers and access vital resources.
  During the devastating Baton Rouge floods of 2016, St. John's Family 
Life Center transformed into an emergency base for disaster-response 
teams including Team Rubicon, IsraAID, AmeriCorps, FEMA Corps, and Team 
Canada. The church played a critical role in coordinating relief 
efforts and offering refuge to flood victims, demonstrating its 
unwavering commitment to serving the community during times of crisis.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to honor St. John's United 
Methodist Church for its outstanding service, leadership, and 
compassion. Its legacy as a resource, refuge, and place of worship 
continues to make a lasting difference in Baton Rouge, inspiring 
generations to serve others and uplift their neighbors.
```
