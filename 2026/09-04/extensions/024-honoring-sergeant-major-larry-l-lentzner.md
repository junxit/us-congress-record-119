# HONORING SERGEANT MAJOR LARRY L. LENTZNER

**Speaking:** Gooden, Lance (G000589) [R-TX]

```
               HONORING SERGEANT MAJOR LARRY L. LENTZNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LANCE GOODEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 4, 2026

  Mr. GOODEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Sergeant 
Major Larry L. Lentzner, a United States Marine whose extraordinary 
courage, sacrifice, and devotion to duty during the Vietnam War 
exemplify the highest ideals of the United States Marine Corps.
  While serving with Hotel Company, 2nd Battalion, 1st Marines near the 
Que Son Mountains in the Republic of Vietnam, then Staff Sergeant 
Lentzner and his fellow Marines were ambushed by a North Vietnamese 
Army force that significantly outnumbered their unit. Staff Sergeant 
Lentzner was struck by enemy fire and suffered devastating wounds to 
both legs.
  Despite his injuries, he continued returning fire and directing his 
Marines, even as he periodically lost consciousness. Staff Sergeant 
Lentzner underwent multiple surgeries and extensive reconstruction of 
both legs. Determined to return to duty, he defied his medical 
prognosis and taught himself to walk again. Sergeant Major Lentzner 
would go on to honorably serve our Nation and the Marine Corps for 
another 25 years.
  It is my privilege to recognize Sergeant Major Lentzner for his 
distinguished service to the United States. I extend our deepest 
gratitude to him, and I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his 
extraordinary service and sacrifice.

                          ____________________
```
