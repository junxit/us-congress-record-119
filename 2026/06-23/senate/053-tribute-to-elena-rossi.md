# TRIBUTE TO ELENA ROSSI

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ELENA ROSSI

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Elena Rossi, an intern in 
my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.
  Elena is a graduate of Georgetown Visitation Preparatory School in 
Washington, DC. Currently, she is attending Boston College in Chestnut 
Hill, MA, where she is pursuing a degree in economics. She is a hard 
worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.

[[Page S3053]]

  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Elena for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
