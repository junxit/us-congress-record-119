# DOBBS V. JACKSON WOMEN'S HEALTH ORGANIZATION

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
              DOBBS V. JACKSON WOMEN'S HEALTH ORGANIZATION

  Mr. SCHUMER. Finally, Mr. President, on our spotlight forum on the 
Dobbs decision, later today, we will join Senator Murray and other 
Senate Democrats for a spotlight forum on Republicans' extreme war on 
women's reproductive freedom ahead of the fourth anniversary of the 
Dobbs decision.
  Donald Trump may want to brush his extreme effort to curtail women's 
reproductive rights under the rug, but we won't let him.
  On the campaign trail in 2024, Donald Trump said that women ``won't 
be thinking about abortion'' if he got elected, but the truth is that 
they have to think about it more than ever before because of this awful 
decision.
  Ever since the Dobbs decision 4 years ago, Republicans have been 
working around the clock to restrict access to abortion care. So today, 
we are going to hear from people who have been forced to live with the 
consequences of Republicans' radical assault on women's reproductive 
freedom, and we will discuss how we are fighting back. We are going to 
remind Donald Trump that women haven't stopped thinking about his 
efforts to take away their rights; they are mad as hell.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). The majority whip.

                          ____________________
```
