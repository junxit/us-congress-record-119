# TRIBUTE TO FRANCIES POULOS

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO FRANCIES POULOS

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, today I have the honor to recognize 
Francies Poulos as Montanan of the Month.
  Francies Poulos is a veteran from Billings who exemplifies the spirit 
of America through service and sacrifice.
  As a young woman, Francies joined the Women's Army Corps during World 
War II, demonstrating an extraordinary love of country and commitment 
to duty as part of the generation that trailblazed new roles for women 
in the armed forces. During her time in service, she met the love of 
her life Nick, whom she married in 1945.
  Later on, Francies earned college degrees in accounting and in law. 
After moving back to Montana in the 1990s, she and her husband 
dedicated their time to helping turn neglected Billings homes into 
spaces that renters could afford. Outside of her work life, Francies is 
an artist, poet, photographer, and author.
  This June, Francies celebrated her 107th birthday, an incredible 
milestone achievement. I commend her for her service, her civic-minded 
attitude, and selfless contributions to Montana. It is my distinct 
honor to recognize Francies Poulos as Montanan of the Month.●

                          ____________________
```
