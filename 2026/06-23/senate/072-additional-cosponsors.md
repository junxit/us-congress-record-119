                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 178

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the name of the Senator from Kansas (Mr. 
Marshall) was added as a cosponsor of S. 178, a bill to amend title XIX 
of the Social Security Act and the Public Health Service Act to improve 
the reporting of abortion data to the Centers for Disease Control and 
Prevention, and for other purposes.

XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to apply financial assistance 
towards the cost-sharing requirements of health insurance plans, and 
for other purposes.
