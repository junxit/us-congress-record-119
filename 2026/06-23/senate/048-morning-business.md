                       TRIBUTE TO ERIK GUNDERSON

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Erik Gunderson, an intern

weeks.

Currently, he is attending the University of Notre Dame in Notre Dame, 
IN, where he is pursuing degrees in global affairs and political 
science. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Erik for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.
