# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF REVEREND JOHN L. GIBSON, SR.

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
      HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF REVEREND JOHN L. GIBSON, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor and remember Reverend 
John L. Gibson, Sr. of Peoria, Illinois, a beloved pastor, gifted 
psalmist, community leader, and devoted servant. Reverend Gibson passed 
away peacefully at his home on May 27, 2026, at the age of 86, leaving 
behind a lasting legacy of faith, ministry, and service.
  Born on July 21, 1939, in Leland, Mississippi, Reverend Gibson 
answered the call to ministry and went on to faithfully serve Rising 
Sun Baptist Church, later Rising Sun Christian Church, for 48 years. 
Under his leadership, the church grew spiritually and physically, 
including the construction of its 850-seat building on West Charter Oak 
Road in Peoria, Illinois.
  Reverend Gibson was known as a powerful preacher and gifted psalmist 
whose voice uplifted countless people. Beyond the pulpit, he was a 
mentor, counselor, and pillar of the community who dedicated his life 
to strengthening others through faith, compassion, and service.
  Above all, Reverend Gibson was a devoted husband, father, 
grandfather, and spiritual leader. He leaves behind his loving wife, 
Gigi M. Hamilton-Gibson, along with many family members, church 
members, and spiritual sons and daughters. Reverend Gibson will be 
deeply missed and remembered for his ministry, music, and enduring love 
for his community.

                          ____________________
```
