# RECOGNIZING MARY MARGARET MAULE FOR RECEIVING THE ATHENA LEADERSHIP AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
  RECOGNIZING MARY MARGARET MAULE FOR RECEIVING THE ATHENA LEADERSHIP 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mary Margaret 
Maule for receiving the ATHENA Leadership Award from the Crystal Lake 
Chamber of Commerce. This distinguished award honors individuals who 
have demonstrated excellence, creativity, and initiative in their 
professional endeavors.
  Mary Margaret exemplifies what it means to be a community leader. 
With extensive experience across business, entrepreneurship, government 
affairs, and nonprofit leadership, she has used her unique skills and 
expertise to lead important community initiatives and mentor countless 
community leaders and professionals.
  During her tenure as President of the Crystal Lake Chamber of 
Commerce, Mary Margaret played a pivotal role in strengthening 
collaboration between local chambers and elected officials, thereby 
promoting greater awareness of the impact of public policy on small 
businesses. She also partnered with community stakeholders to address 
workforce development needs in McHenry County, helping to establish a 
high school-based program designed to cultivate a talent pipeline and 
support regional economic growth in the manufacturing sector.
  Mary Margaret is currently leading Turning Point--Domestic Violence 
Services through a period of transition, where she has refocused 
organizational messaging on hope and transformation for survivors. 
Under her leadership, the organization has introduced new programs and 
expanded opportunities for professional development and empowerment for 
its predominantly female staff. Her commitment to service is further 
reflected in her work as a board member for Big Brothers Big Sisters 
and CASA of McHenry County, helping significantly expand their capacity 
to serve those in need.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to represent such a dedicated community 
leader, and I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mary Margaret 
Maule for her outstanding service to the people of McHenry County.
```
