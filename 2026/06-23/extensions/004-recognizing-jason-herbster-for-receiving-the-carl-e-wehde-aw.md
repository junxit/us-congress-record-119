# RECOGNIZING JASON HERBSTER FOR RECEIVING THE CARL E. WEHDE AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
    RECOGNIZING JASON HERBSTER FOR RECEIVING THE CARL E. WEHDE AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jason Herbster for 
receiving the esteemed Carl E. Wehde Award. This award, presented in 
memory of the late Mayor Carl E. Wehde, recognizes individuals who 
embody the spirit of service, leadership, and dedication

[[Page E602]]

to improving the quality of life in the greater Crystal Lake area.
  As the Executive Director of the Crystal Lake Park District, Jason 
has played a crucial role in ensuring that the community is a desirable 
place to live, work, and play. During his more than 35-year career in 
parks and recreation, he has spent more than 15 years with the Crystal 
Lake Park District, overseeing 42 parks and 1,400 acres of land. He 
also served for 15 years as a board member for the Northern Illinois 
Special Recreation Association (NISRA) and several years as a Chamber 
Ambassador, directly contributing to the growth and success of the 
community.
  Under Jason's leadership, the Crystal Lake Park District has been 
recognized as a top workplace for teens in McHenry County, named a 
Distinguished Agency Mentor by the Illinois Association of Park 
Districts, and honored repeatedly by the Government Finance Officers 
Association. Jason's strategic vision for major community facilities, 
contributions to countless community events, and dedication to the 
Crystal Lake community are truly commendable.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to represent the community of Crystal Lake, 
and I ask my colleagues to join me in acknowledging Jason Herbster for 
his selfless service.

                          ____________________
```
