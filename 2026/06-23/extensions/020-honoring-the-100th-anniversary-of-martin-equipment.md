# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF MARTIN EQUIPMENT

```
           HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF MARTIN EQUIPMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize the 100th anniversary 
of Martin Equipment. For a century, Martin Equipment has served as a 
trusted family business in Central Illinois, supporting contractors, 
businesses, farmers, forestry professionals, and communities across the 
Midwest.
  Founded in 1926 as Sam F. Martin Implement in Roanoke, Illinois, 
Martin Equipment began with a commitment to hard work, customer 
service, and meeting the needs of local communities. While the company 
has grown over the past 100 years, its dedication to integrity, 
service, and family leadership has remained constant.
  Today, Martin Equipment is headquartered in Goodfield, Illinois, and 
operates as a fourth-generation, family- and woman-owned John Deere 
Construction and Forestry dealer under the leadership of President and 
CEO DeLene Martin Bane, the great-granddaughter of the company's 
founder. With nine locations across Illinois, Iowa, and Missouri, and 
approximately 250 employees, Martin Equipment continues to play an 
important role in the region's construction, forestry, agriculture, and 
infrastructure industries.
  As we celebrate 100 years of Martin Equipment, we honor its proud 
history and the generations of the Martin family, employees, customers, 
and community partners who have contributed to its success. I am proud 
to recognize this outstanding Illinois business and ask my colleagues 
to join me in congratulating Martin Equipment on 100 years of 
leadership, integrity, and service.
```
