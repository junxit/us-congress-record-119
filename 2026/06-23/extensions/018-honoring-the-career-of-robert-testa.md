# HONORING THE CAREER OF ROBERT TESTA

**Speaking:** Courtney, Joe (C001069) [D-CT]

```
                  HONORING THE CAREER OF ROBERT TESTA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOE COURTNEY

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  Mr. COURTNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the commendable 
career of Mr. Robert Testa as he retires this month after 11 years of 
dedicated service as Assistant Superintendent of Vernon Public Schools. 
Vernon Public Schools is an outstanding public education system 
benefiting from the tireless work

[[Page E606]]

of teachers, staff, and leadership. Thanks to Mr. Testa's 
contributions, Vernon Public Schools have been able to provide students 
with the support and opportunities needed to nourish academically.
  Mr. Testa has led an impressive career reflecting a deep commitment 
to Vernon. He began his time with Vernon Public Schools serving as 
Assistant Principal at Center Road School. Later, he worked as 
Principal of Lake Street School for eight years. He also acted as the 
Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math (STEM) Coordinator for the 
entire district. Mr. Testa's final role as a dedicated public servant 
to Vernon Public Schools was as Assistant Superintendent. With an 
impressive history of leadership already under his belt, Mr. Testa 
sought further education in executive leadership at the University of 
Connecticut to foster innovation in Vernon public Schools.
  As a leader in education nationwide, Connecticut public schools rely 
on committed staff and leadership who deeply value and prioritize 
student success. As an integral part of the administrative team, Mr. 
Testa has undertaken a pivotal role to ensure teachers have the 
training and resources necessary to ensure student success. The hard 
work of Vernon Public Schools administrators has earned them national 
recognition, including being honored as a DALI (District Administration 
Leadership Institute) District of Distinction in December 2025.
  Vernon Public Schools is no stranger to being acknowledged for its 
achievements--such as the Northeast and Skinner Road Schools being 
named a Blue-Ribbon honoree in 2020 and 2023, respectively. Moreover, 
Vernon is home to several ``Schools of Distinction'': Maple Street 
School, Northeast School, Lake Street School and Skinner Road School.
  Mr. Speaker, as the Member serving the 2nd District of Connecticut, I 
laud Mr. Testa's deep dedication to the Vernon community, especially to 
educators and students. On behalf of the U.S. House of Representatives 
and the American people, I express deep gratitude for Mr. Testa's 
service to bolstering public education and advocating for students. I 
join Mr. Testa's family, friends, and colleagues in wishing him all the 
best in his well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
