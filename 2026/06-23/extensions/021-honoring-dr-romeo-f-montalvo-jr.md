# HONORING DR. ROMEO F. MONTALVO, JR.

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
                  HONORING DR. ROMEO F. MONTALVO, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor the remarkable career of Dr. Romeo F. Montalvo, Jr., as he 
celebrates an extraordinary career spanning 48 years of practicing 
pediatrics in Brownsville, Texas.
  For nearly five decades, Dr. Montalvo has shown immense dedication, 
kindness, and compassion to the many families he has cared for in South 
Texas. Through his unwavering commitment to patient care, he has 
brought both children and parents closer together, building trust and 
helping families navigate some of life's most important milestones.
  Beyond his work as a physician, he has cultivated a dedicated team of 
healthcare professionals who share his commitment to excellence and 
compassionate service. Dr. Montalvo has served as an example of what 
genuine service to others looks like. Medicine was never simply a 
profession for him, but his life's calling. He always recognized the 
needs of others and exceeded expectations to provide comfort and 
exceptional care.
  Every life he touched has set off a chain of moments, milestones, and 
memories that extend far beyond the walls of his practice. Dr. Montalvo 
served multiple generations of families, watching children grow and 
develop into parents, professionals, and community leaders. His 
contributions have strengthened the health of our community and 
improved the lives of countless South Texans.
  Alongside his professional accomplishments, he has built a loving 
family, sharing nearly 30 years with his wife, Linda.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas' 34th Congressional 
District, I am proud to recognize Dr. Romeo F. Montalvo, Jr., for his 
extraordinary life of service, kindness, and attentive care. His legacy 
serves as a powerful example of the difference one person can make 
through a career devoted to service. I wish him a happy and well-
deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
