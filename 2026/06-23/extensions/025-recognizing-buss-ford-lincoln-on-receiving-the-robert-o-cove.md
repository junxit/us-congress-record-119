# RECOGNIZING BUSS FORD LINCOLN ON RECEIVING THE ROBERT O. COVEY AWARD

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
  RECOGNIZING BUSS FORD LINCOLN ON RECEIVING THE ROBERT O. COVEY AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 23, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Buss Ford Lincoln 
for receiving the Robert O. Covey Award for embodying the spirit of 
American ingenuity and community engagement. For decades, Buss Ford 
Lincoln has exemplified what it means to be a pillar of the local 
business community through its commitment to civic leadership, 
community development. and dedication to local partnerships.
  Buss Ford Lincoln's impact extends far beyond the showroom floor. 
Through thoughtful donations to regional infrastructure and education 
initiatives, Buss Ford Lincoln has

[[Page E608]]

helped rebuild the McHenry Theatre, provided lighting for the Ryan Buss 
Zone Skate Park, supported baseball and softball leagues, and sponsored 
the McHenry Distinguished Warrior Graduate Program scholarship. 
Additionally, Buss Ford Lincoln created the ``Buss Challenger League.'' 
a free program that gives children with disabilities the opportunity to 
learn and play baseball.
  Known for its exceptional customer service and community engagement, 
Buss Ford Lincoln understands that giving back to the community is a 
key part of a successful business. By consistently investing its time 
and resources in Crystal Lake, it has made the community a better place 
to live, work, and play.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to represent the community of Crystal Lake, 
and I ask my colleagues to join me in acknowledging Buss Ford Lincoln 
for its impactful contributions to the community.

                          ____________________
```
