# REMEMBERING EIGHT INDIVIDUALS LOST IN B-52 STRATOFORTRESS CRASH AT EDWARDS AIR FORCE BASE

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA], Whitesides, George (W000830) [D-CA]

```
  REMEMBERING EIGHT INDIVIDUALS LOST IN B-52 STRATOFORTRESS CRASH AT 
                         EDWARDS AIR FORCE BASE

  (Mr. FONG asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, our hearts are heavy as we honor and remember 
the eight individuals lost in the B-52 Stratofortress crash at Edwards 
Air Force Base on Monday, June 15, in Kern County, California:
  Colonel Gregory Watson
  Lieutenant Colonel Gabriel Estrella
  Retired Lieutenant Colonel Miles Middleton
  Major Alexander Davis
  Major Robert Dee
  Major Brad Hovey
  Jeromy Smith
  Christopher Rischar
  These brave men dedicated their lives to this Nation's defense, and 
their sacrifice will never be forgotten.
  Behind each of these heroes are family, friends, and loved ones 
facing an unimaginable loss. They represent the very best of our 
community and our country: Americans willing to give everything in 
defense of the United States of America.
  I ask our Nation to join us in lifting up the Edwards community and 
everyone grieving this devastating loss.
  May God grant them strength and peace during this difficult time. May 
He forever bless the memory of those we lost.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield to the gentleman from California (Mr. 
Whitesides), my colleague and friend.
  Mr. WHITESIDES. Mr. Speaker, I join my colleague in solemn 
remembrance today, standing here on behalf of the Edwards Air Force 
Base community and all those across the Nation who feel this loss so 
profoundly.
  The eight crewmembers who lost their lives last week were heroes. 
They were performing work that was essential for the national security 
of our great country, work that is deeply woven into the fabric and 
history of the Antelope Valley.
  Our prayers are with their families, friends, and loved ones.
  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I ask that the House observe a moment of 
silence for these eight great Americans.

                          ____________________
```
