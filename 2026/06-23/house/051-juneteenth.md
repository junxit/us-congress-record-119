# JUNETEENTH

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                               JUNETEENTH

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, Juneteenth reminds us of 
the importance of strength, faith, and perseverance.
  Across eastern North Carolina, from our smallest towns to our most 
rural communities, we celebrated Juneteenth, honored history, and 
remembered the generations who kept believing in a brighter future.
  Juneteenth reminds us that we must keep the American Dream within 
reach for everyone, which is possible when we stand together, work 
together, and believe together. We must remain committed to building 
communities where every child, every family, and every neighbor has the 
opportunity to succeed.
  It is up to us to allow freedom to ring.

                          ____________________
```
