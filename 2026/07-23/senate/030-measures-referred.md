# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 89, H.Con.Res. 113, H.R. 7283, H.R. 8884

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 7283. An act to amend title 41, United States Code, to 
     amend the time period for certain acquisition-related dollar 
     thresholds; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 8884. An act to amend title II of the Social Security 
     Act to reauthorize demonstration authority for the disability 
     insurance program; to the Committee on Finance.

  The following concurrent resolutions were read, and referred as 
indicated:

       H. Con. Res. 89. Concurrent resolution directing the 
     President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers 
     Resolution, to remove United States Armed Forces from 
     hostilities with Iran; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
       H. Con. Res. 113. Concurrent resolution establishing the 
     congressional budget for the United States Government for 
     fiscal year 2027 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary 
     levels for fiscal years 2028 through 2036; to the Committee 
     on the Budget.

                          ____________________
```
