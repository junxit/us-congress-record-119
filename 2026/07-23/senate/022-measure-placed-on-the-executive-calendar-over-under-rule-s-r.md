# MEASURE PLACED ON THE EXECUTIVE CALENDAR OVER/UNDER RULE--S. Res. 817

**Speaking:** Armstrong, Alan (A000383) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 817

```
 MEASURE PLACED ON THE EXECUTIVE CALENDAR OVER/UNDER RULE--S. Res. 817

  Mr. ARMSTRONG. Mr. President, I send an executive resolution to the 
desk for the consideration of certain nominations en bloc and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title 
for the information of the Senate.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       An executive resolution (S. Res. 817) authorizing the en 
     bloc consideration in Executive Session of certain 
     nominations on the Executive Calendar.

  Mr. ARMSTRONG. In order to place the executive resolution on the 
calendar, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection having been heard, the executive 
resolution will lie over 1 day.

                          ____________________
```
