                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 142

  At the request of Mr. Barrasso, the name of the Senator from 
California (Mr. Padilla) was added as a cosponsor of S. 142, a bill to 
award a Congressional Gold Medal to wildland firefighters in 
recognition of their strength, resiliency, sacrifice, and service to 
protect the forests, grasslands, and communities of the United States, 
and for other purposes.


                                 S. 185

  At the request of Mr. Tillis, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 185, a bill to 
provide a civil remedy for individuals harmed by sanctuary jurisdiction 
policies, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 304

  At the request of Ms. Graham, her name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
304, a bill to amend section 301 of the Immigration and Nationality Act 
to clarify those classes of individuals born in the United States who 
are nationals and citizens of the United States at birth.


                                 S. 494

  At the request of Mr. Schmitt, the name of the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Bennet) was added as a cosponsor of S. 494, a bill to establish a 
national plan to coordinate research on epilepsy, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 1169

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the name of the Senator from Indiana 
(Mr. Banks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1169, a bill to prohibit 
State excise taxes on firearms and ammunition manufacturers and 
dealers.


                                S. 1517

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1517, a bill to 
include sexual assault and aggravated sexual violence in the definition 
of aggravated felony under the Immigration and Nationality Act in order 
to expedite the removal of aliens convicted of any such crime.


                                S. 1541

  At the request of Mr. Kelly, the name of the Senator from Nevada (Ms. 
Rosen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1541, a bill to support the 
national defense and economic security of the United States by 
supporting vessels, ports, and shipyards of the United States and the 
U.S. maritime workforce.


                                S. 1952

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1952, a bill to 
amend title 18, United States Code, to establish a criminal prohibition 
on the public release of the name of a Federal law enforcement officer 
with the intent to obstruct a criminal investigation or immigration 
enforcement operation.


                                S. 2223

  At the request of Mr. Young, the names of the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Hickenlooper) and the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Moreno) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 2223, a bill to amend the Small Business Investment 
Act of 1958 to increase the amount that may be invested in small 
business investment companies.


                                S. 2229

  At the request of Mr. Sullivan, the names of the Senator from 
Connecticut (Mr. Blumenthal) and the Senator from Illinois (Ms. 
Duckworth) were added as cosponsors of S. 2229, a bill to require the 
Secretary of the Treasury to mint a coin in recognition of the Foreign 
Service of the United States and its contribution to United States 
diplomacy.


                                S. 3054

  At the request of Mr. Cornyn, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3054, a bill to 
amend the William Wilberforce Trafficking Victims Protection 
Reauthorization Act of 2008 and the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to 
enhance efforts to combat the trafficking of children.


                                S. 3085

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the name of the Senator from Indiana 
(Mr. Banks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3085, a bill to ensure that 
certain operations, functions, and services of the Federal Government

continue during a lapse in appropriations.


                                S. 3103

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the names of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Mr. Scott) and the Senator from Colorado (Mr. Bennet) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 3103, a bill to authorize the extension of 
nondiscriminatory treatment (normal trade relations treatment) to

                                S. 3197

  At the request of Mr. Lee, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3197, a bill to 
amend title 18, United States Code, to establish a rebuttable 
presumption that a defendant who is not a United States citizen or 
lawful permanent resident poses a danger to the community and a serious 
risk of flight, for purposes of determining whether to release or 
detain the defendant pending trial.


                                S. 3267

                                S. 3314

                                S. 3589

  At the request of Mr. Banks, the name of the Senator from Virginia

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the name of the Senator from South
