# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF AURORA FASTPRINT

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
          CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF AURORA FASTPRINT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 23, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Aurora Fastprint 
as it celebrates its 50th anniversary, marking half a century as a 
family-owned staple of the Aurora community.
  Founded in 1976 by Tom Bartlett, Aurora Fastprint has provided high-
quality, full-service printing services to businesses, nonprofit 
organizations, schools, municipalities, and residents throughout the 
Fox River Valley. Whether showcasing the work of a local poet in its 
storefront windows during Poetry Month or sponsoring a holiday food 
drive for the Aurora Area Interfaith Food Pantry, Aurora Fastprint has 
consistently demonstrated its commitment to the community. Its 
storefront in Aurora's historic downtown district has become home to a 
notable and respected local business.
  Over the last 50 years, Aurora Fastprint has demonstrated remarkable 
resilience and innovation. As technology and the needs of the community 
have evolved, Aurora Fastprint has adapted and evolved too. By 
expanding beyond traditional printing services to offer graphic design, 
digital printing, and Every Door Direct Mail service, the company has 
adapted to the changing needs of its clients. Now, in its second 
generation of ownership under Bartlett's daughter Kim Woodin, Aurora 
Fastprint has grown alongside and supported the community it serves.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud that Aurora Fastprint calls my district home. 
Its remarkable 50-year history is a testament to the company's enduring 
commitment to service, reliability, innovation, and the Aurora 
community. I am confident that Aurora Fastprint will continue to thrive 
for generations to come.
```
