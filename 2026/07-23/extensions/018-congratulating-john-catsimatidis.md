# CONGRATULATING JOHN CATSIMATIDIS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                    CONGRATULATING JOHN CATSIMATIDIS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 23, 2026

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate 
John Catsimatidis on being named honorary chairman of Unleash 
Prosperity.
  Unleash Prosperity is dedicated to educating policy makers and the 
public about policies that have been proven to maximize economic growth 
and equitable prosperity in America and globally.
  Mr. Catsimatidis has been a steadfast supporter of this organization 
for many years, and this well-deserved recognition reflects his 
commitment to entrepreneurship, economic growth, and opportunity.
  In 2019, he purchased WABC, a New York talk radio show which has 
since become the

[[Page E737]]

most listened to AM radio station in America--a testament to his hard 
work.
  I extend my sincere congratulations to him, and I wish him continued 
success in his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
