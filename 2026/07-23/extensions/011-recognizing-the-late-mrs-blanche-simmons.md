# RECOGNIZING THE LATE MRS. BLANCHE SIMMONS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
               RECOGNIZING THE LATE MRS. BLANCHE SIMMONS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 23, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize the late Mrs. Blanche 
Simmons, a faithful servant of God, beloved wife, mother and friend. I 
include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Mrs. Simmons was a strong supporter of parent 
     teacher associations and other school activities that 
     encouraged children to ensure their growth and development as 
     productive citizens;
       Whereas, Mrs. Simmons, who knew the value of an education 
     and service to one's country, saw many of her of children 
     graduate from colleges and universities, become educators and 
     lawyers and serve in the United States Army;
       Whereas, Mrs. Simmons was an active member of Salter's 
     Chapel African Methodist Episcopal Church in Miami, Florida;
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a Member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     proud to recognize the late Mrs. Blanche Simmons for her 
     commitment to God and her church, and for her dedication and 
     devotion to her family, friends and our community.

                          ____________________
```
