# AFFORDABILITY FOR FAMILIES

```
                       AFFORDABILITY FOR FAMILIES

  (Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, as families across Maryland's 
Sixth Congressional District prepare for a new school year, they are 
facing yet another hit to their wallets. Parents will be spending an 
average of $485 extra per child on back-to-school costs, nearly 12 
percent more than last year.
  I am a mom of four daughters, and I have lived through various stages 
of raising a family, buying backpacks, new sports shoes, equipment, and 
back-to-school supplies, even getting them off to college.
  Today, my youngest will be at college, and I know that these costs 
continue after high school, including how many families are struggling 
with affordability of college and career and technical training.
  Families shouldn't have to struggle to choose between paying for 
childcare and school supplies. That is why I have launched a costs down 
agenda to focus on new ways to address accessible daycare, fortify the 
child income tax credit, further food co-ops, prohibit algorithmic 
pricing of consumer goods and groceries, and end nonsensical tariffs 
which drive up costs.
  In Maryland, we leave no one behind and we shouldn't in Congress, 
either. Team Maryland is working at Federal, State, and local levels to 
mitigate costs and address these costs, and we need to do it in 
Congress, as well.
  We need partnership to support working families.

                          ____________________
```
