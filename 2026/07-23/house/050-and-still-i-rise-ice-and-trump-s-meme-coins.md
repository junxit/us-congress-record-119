# AND STILL I RISE: ICE AND TRUMP'S MEME COINS

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

```
              AND STILL I RISE: ICE AND TRUMP'S MEME COINS

  (Under the Speaker's announced policy of January 3, 2025, Mr. Green 
of Texas was recognized for 60 minutes as the designee of the minority 
leader.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, and still I rise. And I rise today 
as I always did, censured but not silenced, a very proud, unbossed, 
unbought, liberated, and unelected Democrat.
  And I rise today to bring two things to the attention of the House 
and to the people of the United States of America. The first is a brief 
update on ICE, and the second is an explanation related to crypto crony 
capitalism.
  Let us begin. With reference to ICE, Mr. Speaker, we had a briefing 
just yesterday, and at that briefing some things were discovered and 
other things were uncovered. What we found, of course, we knew, and 
that is that there was not a body camera on the ICE officers who took 
the life of Mr. Lorenzo Salgado Araujo.
  Officers did not have body cameras on, and there was not a dash 
camera in the vehicle. The video that we have seen was taken by some 
public device, and that video, of course, has given us reason to 
understand that Mr. Salgado Araujo was not committing what I perceive 
as an infraction related to the law. He was not.
  The evidence that we heard yesterday gave us good reason to believe 
that there would be body cameras. And these body cameras would be in 
place on all officers who are performing this type of activity where 
they are taking people into custody or desire to take

[[Page H5193]]

people into custody for various reasons associated with immigration, 
that these officers will all have body cameras.
  I brought up the question of dash cameras, and they indicated to me 
that that would be given consideration. So I am very proud that the 
persons who were available indicated that they would not only give us 
the body cameras in 60 days, but they would also consider dash cameras 
in the vehicle itself.
  I think these things will help not only the public but also the 
officers because the officers can immediately have the evidence to 
exonerate themselves when there is some question as to whether or not 
the officers have committed an infraction of the law.
  I am very proud to know these things will happen, but there will be a 
follow up to make sure they do happen.
  I am also proud to tell you that tomorrow there will be in Houston, 
Texas, a forum, a forum wherein we will have an opportunity to have a 
discussion about these issues associated with Mr. Lorenzo Salgado 
Araujo's death. We will have an opportunity to ask questions of people 
who can give us more information.
  These things are important.
  Finally, on this question of ICE, I would only make this point that 
we will get the body cameras. I believe we will get the dash cameras in 
time.
  Here is what I know: I know that we will have them because lives were 
lost. And I regret that it has taken the loss of lives--the loss of 
lives--to have us do what ICE should have been asking us to do. We 
should not have been asking that these things be done. One would think 
that the officers would say: These body cameras are going to benefit 
us, would you please, Congress, give us body cameras? That was not the 
case.
  And because it was not the case and because Mr. Pretti lost his life, 
Ms. Good lost her life, Mr. Salgado Araujo lost his life, and we have a 
person in Maine who lost life, these four persons, all of these things 
occurring within this year, these issues, these persons losing their 
lives will now give us the opportunity to have body cameras.
  It is a disgrace, a shame, and a sin to have to conclude that lives 
have to be lost for us to do the right thing. I want the world to know 
that I will never, ever stop the challenge that is before us to make 
sure that ICE is an institution, as it were, an organization, if you 
will, that will treat people fairly and justly.
  Not all of the ICE officers are engaged in illegal activities, and 
some of the activities are illegal when they engage in extrajudicial 
killings, but not all of the officers are doing this, so I am not 
painting everybody with the same brush.
  I am saying that enough of them are doing it for us to demand some 
additional reforms with ICE. Of course, we are still demanding that 
they have uniforms that are indicative of the work that they do, that 
their names be displayed on those uniforms, and that they not enter 
property without a proper warrant. These are the things that we would 
just take for granted that the Constitution would afford us and would 
be recognized by officers of the law.
  I promise you, Mr. Speaker, I am going to stay in this fight, and we 
are going to get positive results.
  Now, moving on to the crony crypto capitalism. For those who do not 
know, the President has a meme coin, and the President's meme coin has 
done quite well.
  The President's meme coin, the dollar sign Trump meme coin, that meme 
coin has made $1.64 million per day over a 1-year period of time. Over 
that same 1-year period of time, nearly 1 million people lost $3.8 
billion. Let me repeat that: Over that same period of time, nearly 1 
million people lost $3.8 billion.
  How did this happen?
  It happened because the meme coin that the President has is a part of 
a Ponzi scheme. A Ponzi scheme is a means by which a person can 
convince people that they should buy something that does not have any 
worth except for the purchase of that thing itself, which is nothing in 
this case. Mr. Speaker, you purchase nothing with the belief that 
someone is willing to pay more for nothing than you paid for nothing, 
and, as a result, the value is increased for you if you paid nothing at 
a lower price than someone else is going to pay for this same thing for 
nothing.
  The President has sold nothing to nearly 1 million people who lost 
$3.8 billion. The President made $1.74 million per day on the deal, 
pumped it up, took his $1.74 million per day and left nearly 1 million 
people to lose $3.8 billion. That, Mr. Speaker, is the President's 
Ponzi scheme.
  One would ask: How can this be?
  It exists because we have a President who lives without the law by 
causing certain persons to believe that things should be legal that 
clearly have not been and should not be.
  Ponzi schemes have been illegal in this country. The President has 
managed to get his persons who are in charge to indicate that this is 
not illegal, that this Ponzi scheme is not, and others still are. I am 
mentioning this to you, Mr. Speaker, because this scheme, this whole 
scam, is having an impact on capitalism.
  There are people who are now starting to see this as a part of 
capitalism, the Ponzi scheme, a means of making money on what is called 
a free market. They believe that the free market is designed in certain 
places to benefit very wealthy people.

                              {time}  1130

  They have breaks in benefits from capitalism by having laws passed to 
benefit them and by engaging in things such as this Ponzi scheme that 
benefit them. In a sense, this is harming the concept of capitalism. 
One should be able to understand why some people are looking at other 
options. If this is the way we are going to manage capitalism, by 
having your cronies do quite well while millions of others are taken 
advantage of, this is one example of why people would say: Maybe this 
is not working well enough, and we will try something else.
  My hope is that we can correct these things, especially this thing 
associated with the President and his Ponzi scheme. My hope is that we 
can correct these things and let people see that truly we can have an 
economic order that treats people with the kind of justice that they 
deserve. This is an unjust system, the Ponzi scheme, and we have to 
bring justice to it. Probably the best way to bring justice to it is 
going to be to see this President walk away and then, thereafter, do 
some serious investigations, serious hearings, and get to the bottom of 
things, not only associated with the Ponzi scheme but other things, as 
well.
  I regret that some people are not going to get their money back. My 
hope is that there would be some way to reverse it, but my belief is 
that a good many people are just going to suffer and will not get their 
money back.
  I also know this: That, as I am speaking now--and I am telling people 
this is the case--the Ponzi scheme continues. People are going to buy 
into nothing and expect to get something.
  You have been noticed. Please understand that the President is just 
taking advantage of you. He is. Not only is he doing it in this area, 
there are many others. I am just talking about this today. I will talk 
about others at a later time. He is taking advantage of you. You can 
stop it by not buying into this Ponzi scheme.
  Capitalism is taking a hit, and it is for many reasons but the chief 
reason, I think, being the President who is doing things to aid and 
abet his friends, allowing people to make contributions and then have 
certain benefits. A President who clearly has lived beneath the law and 
above the law. Beneath it in the sense that he doesn't believe it 
exists so he pushes it aside. Above it in the sense that he doesn't get 
charged for his criminality the same way other people would be charged 
if they were doing similar things.
  My belief is that we have time to cure some of the harm, but time is 
not on our side. Every day the President continues to find new ways to 
take advantage of the economic order that has been in place in this 
country since its inception, for the most part.
  I would only say to you this: One of the remedies that we have 
available to us is one that I will pursue. I said we needed to wait 
until we could get control of the House, the Senate, and the 
Presidency, yes.

[[Page H5194]]

  People want to know: What are you going to do now? I have said, and I 
continue to say, that we are in a countdown to impeachment. My desire 
will be to do this: When next we are in session, for all to know, 
Articles of Impeachment will be filed. The countdown will continue, but 
it will become that which will be brought to the floor of the House of 
Representatives when next we are in session. When next the House of 
Representatives is in session, I will take the Articles over to the 
clerk, they will be filed, and at some time thereafter--a reasonable 
amount of time thereafter, to give Members a chance to see exactly what 
I have filed, although I plan to publish the articles before they are 
filed so people will know what we are going to file--file these 
Articles of Impeachment.
  That will give them plenty of time to prepare the defense of, well, 
it is too soon. The Senate won't take up the articles seriously. You 
don't have enough senators there to convict. Let's see, what else will 
people say? Always, whatever it is, is not impeachable until maybe they 
decide that they want to file Articles, and then it becomes an 
impeachable offense.
  Let's be reminded of what President Ford said: An impeachable 
offense--meaning impeachment--is whatever Congress says at a given 
time. Whatever given Congress says at a given time. It is not 
appealable. It is not something that will require you to report how you 
came to your conclusions. Really, it is a matter of conscience for me, 
and I hope for others, as well.
  We will file the Articles of Impeachment. We will notice them prior 
to them being filed. People can start preparing now your arguments for 
why you can't support the Articles of Impeachment. Here is what I do 
know: Posterity is not going to look favorably upon us for allowing a 
President to commit impeachable offenses and do nothing.
  Of course, someone would say, but we will do something when we are in 
the majority. Now, that is another interesting question, as I close. I 
have been out on the campaign trail, and I hear the people talking 
about all of these things that they are going to do when they get to 
Congress. Well, I have seen at least one example of the person getting 
to Congress and concluding: I will be doing something next year when we 
have the majority.
  Run on doing something great now but get here and realize that you 
don't have the power to do what you thought, so you will do it next 
year.
  Well, I am going to do things now. I am not going to wait until next 
year. I am a right-now kind of guy. As a right-now kind of person, I 
will bring the Articles of Impeachment.
  Mr. Speaker, this is what the Constitution requires. It is what I 
will do because, again, I am an unbought, unbossed, unafraid, 
unelected, liberated Democrat.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield back the balance of my time.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to refrain from 
engaging in personalities toward the President.
```
