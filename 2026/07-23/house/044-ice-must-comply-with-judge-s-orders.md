# ICE MUST COMPLY WITH JUDGE'S ORDERS

**Speaking:** Kamlager-Dove, Sydney (K000400) [D-CA]

```
                  ICE MUST COMPLY WITH JUDGE'S ORDERS

  (Ms. KAMLAGER-DOVE asked and was given permission to address the

[[Page H5192]]

House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAMLAGER-DOVE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to once again demand 
that ICE immediately provide my constituent, Carlitos Ricardo Parias, 
urgently needed medical care.
  Carlitos is a father, husband, and volunteer in our community who was 
even recognized by city council for his service.
  Last October, ICE shot Carlitos near his elbow. He received initial 
care at a hospital, but since being transferred to the Adelanto ICE 
facility in December, ICE has denied him proper care for the injury 
they caused.
  I visited Carlitos in late May. His hand was purple, his fingers were 
atrophied, and he is experiencing headaches and nerve pain. I am 
worried that he may have a bone infection and that his arm could be 
amputated.
  Carlitos urgently needs medical attention, and many more at Adelanto 
do, too.
  Last week, a Federal judge ordered sweeping changes to improve 
Adelanto's conditions for detainees. It is time for ICE to comply with 
this judge's orders and end this cruelty at Adelanto and nationwide.

                          ____________________
```
