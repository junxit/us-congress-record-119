# SALUTING 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF KELLY'S SEA LEVEL

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
             SALUTING 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF KELLY'S SEA LEVEL

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, today I want to salute the 50th anniversary 
celebration of an institution in my home community of Rye, Kelly's Sea 
Level.
  A big shindig is planned for the month of August, a little later on, 
at this iconic restaurant and bar that has welcomed locals and visitors 
since 1976.
  The McGuire family, Jerry and Martha and their kids, are beloved in 
the community, and they are at Kelly's most every night with their 
families and friends watching TV sports, the Yankees, Giants, Rangers, 
Knicks, even the Mets. They also are outdoors in the fall to watch the 
Rye Garnets high school football games.
  Visiting Kelly's is to be at home. For the long-term locals from Rye, 
Harrison, Port Chester, Greenwich--the financiers, the college kids 
home for the holidays, hardworking firefighters, and the occasional 
Congressman--they all come to Kelly's each week.
  We know the menu, the bartenders, the waitstaff, and all of those 
little things that the locals know, like the two-seat table in the back 
known as The Oak Room.
  It is Jerry and Martha who give Kelly's its heart and soul. We all 
are amazed that we have reached 50 years.
  From the floor of the U.S. House of Representatives, we salute the 
McGuire family and Kelly's Sea Level on its 50th year of serving the 
thirsty and hungry people of Rye and the surrounding communities.

                          ____________________
```
