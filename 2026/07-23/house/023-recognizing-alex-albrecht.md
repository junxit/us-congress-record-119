# RECOGNIZING ALEX ALBRECHT

**Speaking:** Biggs, Sheri (B001325) [R-SC]

```
                       RECOGNIZING ALEX ALBRECHT

  (Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, every day law enforcement 
officers across this country put on the badge, putting their lives on 
the line.
  Anderson County Sheriff's Deputy, Alex Albrecht, experienced that 
reality firsthand. During what began as a routine traffic stop with 
another officer, Deputy Albrecht responded to a call for backup and was 
shot in the line of duty while serving his community.
  By the grace of God, he survived, has recovered from his injuries, 
and continues to embody the courage and resilience that define 
America's law enforcement officers.
  Deputy Albrecht's story reminds us that there is no such thing as a 
routine day in law enforcement. Every traffic stop, every call for 
service, and every shift carries risk. Yet, our deputies and police 
officers answer that call regardless, putting others before themselves.
  For the people across South Carolina, men and women like Deputy 
Albrecht represent the very best of public service.
  May God continue to bless Deputy Albrecht, his family, and every law 
enforcement officer who faithfully serves our communities.

                          ____________________
```
