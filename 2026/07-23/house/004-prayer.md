# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Ever-present God, there is no place we can go from Your spirit. There 
is no place we find ourselves where Your presence is not with us.
  Abide with us as the week ends and as we take our leave from this 
place. May the work of these last several months receive Your blessing, 
and may the decisions in the days and weeks ahead of us remain in Your 
keeping.
  We pray Your wisdom in our efforts to pursue the responsibilities You 
have laid upon us, and we ask for Your care and protection in times of 
rest and rejuvenation.
  Lord, go before us and prepare the way, so that we will be ready to 
respond to Your guidance wherever You lead us. Lord, be with us; 
encourage and embolden us, so that we may be capable of meeting 
whatever chores or challenges we encounter. Never leave or forsake us, 
that we will always know that we are where You want us to be, as we 
seek to serve both You and this country faithfully.
  In the sure and certain power of Your name, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
