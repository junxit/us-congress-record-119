# CELEBRATING DISABILITY PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Stansbury, Melanie A. (S001218) [D-NM]

```
                   CELEBRATING DISABILITY PRIDE MONTH

  (Ms. STANSBURY asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. STANSBURY. Mr. Speaker, happy Disability Pride Month, which 
originated in the passage of the Americans with Disabilities Act.
  This month and every month, we celebrate the strength, resilience, 
and leadership of New Mexicans living with disabilities in our State 
and across the country.
  They have fought for accessible schools, reliable transportation, 
inclusive workplaces, and the freedom to live with dignity, self-
determination, and joy.
  I thank our leaders, advocates, and communities for their work.
  To every New Mexican living with a disability, their voice, their 
advocacy, and their leadership matter. They belong in every classroom, 
every workplace, every community, and at every seat at every table.
  We will continue to fight and celebrate alongside them, not just this 
month but every month, to build a New Mexico and a world where every 
person can live with dignity, independence, and pride.

                          ____________________
```
