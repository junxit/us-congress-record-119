# CELEBRATING DORETHEA BRANCH ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
           CELEBRATING DORETHEA BRANCH ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the 
remarkable life of Dorethea Branch of La Grange, North Carolina, who 
recently celebrated her 100th birthday.
  She has lived a life defined by faith, service, and a steadfast 
commitment to her community.
  As a social worker, educator, businessowner, and civic leader, she 
broke barriers, including becoming the first African-American member of 
the board of trustees at UNC Health Lenoir.
  Through decades of volunteer service and leadership, she strengthened 
lives and opened doors for others.
  I wish Dorethea Branch a joyful 100th birthday and thank her for a 
century of faith, love, and service that continues to inspire eastern 
North Carolina and our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
