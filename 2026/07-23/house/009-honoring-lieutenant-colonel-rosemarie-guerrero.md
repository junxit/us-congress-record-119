# HONORING LIEUTENANT COLONEL ROSEMARIE GUERRERO

**Speaking:** Garcia, Jesus G. "Chuy" (G000586) [D-IL]

```
             HONORING LIEUTENANT COLONEL ROSEMARIE GUERRERO

  (Mr. GARCIA of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GARCIA of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Lieutenant 
Colonel Rosemarie Guerrero of the United States National Guard as she 
retires after an extraordinary career dedicated to serving our Nation.
  Lieutenant Colonel Guerrero started her brilliant career in 1989, 
when she enlisted in the Illinois Air National Guard. In her current 
role as Cyber Warfare Branch Chief, she oversees a $205 million Cyber 
Mission Force portfolio and provides direct guidance on mobilization, 
training, resourcing, and posturing for the Air National Guard Cyber 
Mission Forces.
  At a time when the Pentagon faces more than 10 million cyber 
intrusion attempts every single day, her work defending our country 
against cyberattacks has never been more important.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Lieutenant Colonel Guerrero for her decades of 
leadership, sacrifice, and service. I wish her a happy and well-
deserved retirement.
```
