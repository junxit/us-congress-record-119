# RECOGNIZING SCOTT WINSLOW

**Speaking:** Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

```
                       RECOGNIZING SCOTT WINSLOW

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD FINSTAD

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 14, 2026

  Mr. FINSTAD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Scott Winslow of 
Fountain, Minnesota, for his recent appointment to the Minnesota USDA 
Farm Service Agency State Committee.
  Scott is a fifth-generation farmer in Fillmore County, where he 
raises corn, soybeans, hogs, and beef cattle. He understands firsthand 
the challenges that family farmers and producers across southern 
Minnesota are facing, and he brings that real-world experience to his 
service.
  Scott has long been a leader in our agricultural community, serving 
on the Fillmore County Farm Bureau Board, the Fillmore County Pork 
Producers Board, and previously on the Minnesota FSA State Committee. 
He also currently serves on the U.S. Grains Council Trade Team and the 
Minnesota Corn Research and Promotion Council, helping promote 
Minnesota agriculture at home and abroad.
  It was an honor to nominate Scott to serve on the FSA State 
Committee. I congratulate him on this appointment and thank him for his 
continued service to Minnesota agriculture.

                          ____________________
```
