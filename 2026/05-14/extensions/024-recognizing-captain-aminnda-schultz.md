# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN AMINNDA SCHULTZ

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                  RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN AMINNDA SCHULTZ

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 14, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pasco County 
Fire Rescue Captain Aminnda Schultz for her exceptional leadership, 
compassion, and unwavering dedication to public service.
  Captain Schultz is a shining example of pride, ownership, and 
wholehearted commitment within the fire service. Her leadership is felt 
not only by those who work alongside her, but also throughout the 
community she serves. She consistently leads with compassion, humility, 
and a genuine commitment to lifting others up--qualities that define 
the very best of public safety professionals.
  Over the past year, Captain Schultz has been nominated multiple times 
for departmental recognition, each reflecting her willingness to go 
above and beyond the call of duty. Whether representing Pasco County 
Fire Rescue through her service with the Honor Guard, volunteering 
countless hours at Safety Town, supporting the Benevolent Association, 
or engaging with the community at public events, she consistently 
places others before herself. Her positivity, generosity, and steadfast 
dedication to her fellow firefighters are evident in everything she 
does.
  Captain Schultz leads by example, fostering a culture of 
professionalism, teamwork, and service that strengthens both her 
department and the broader community. These qualities reflect not only 
her commitment to excellence in the fire service, but also her 
character and her deep sense of responsibility to those she serves. She 
embodies the spirit of service, honor, and dedication that this 
recognition is intended to celebrate.
  I commend Captain Aminnda Schultz for her outstanding service to the 
residents of Florida's 12th Congressional District and proudly

[[Page E451]]

recognize her as the 2026 First Responder of the Year. Her leadership, 
compassion, and commitment to others make her truly deserving of this 
honor.

                          ____________________
```
