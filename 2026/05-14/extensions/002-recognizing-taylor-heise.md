# RECOGNIZING TAYLOR HEISE

**Speaking:** Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

```
                        RECOGNIZING TAYLOR HEISE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD FINSTAD

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 14, 2026

  Mr. FINSTAD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to Olympic gold 
medalist and southern Minnesota native, Taylor Heise, on her remarkable 
performance at the 2026 Winter Olympic Games in Milano Cortina, Italy.
  In her very first Olympic appearance, Taylor not only helped lead 
Team USA to a gold medal but delivered at the biggest moments--
including the assist on the game-winning overtime goal that secured 
victory over Canada.
  Earlier this year, Taylor's hometown of Lake City declared February 
19th as ``Taylor Heise Day,'' recognizing her historic achievement, the 
years of perseverance, early-morning practices, and the family and 
mentors who helped her reach this moment. Her grandfather Kenny, who 
once drove her to Red Wing every day so she could play, said it best: 
``Taylor, you work hard and it paid off.''
  Taylor's grit, talent, and heart have inspired fans at home in 
Minnesota and across the country. She reminded all of us what it means 
to pursue a dream with unwavering determination.
  I congratulate Taylor on her extraordinary 2026 Olympic performance. 
Her family, friends, teammates, and fellow southern Minnesotans could 
not be prouder of her.

                          ____________________
```
