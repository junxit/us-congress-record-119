# CONGRATULATING THE DWYER SCHOOL OF IRISH DANCE

```
             CONGRATULATING THE DWYER SCHOOL OF IRISH DANCE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL R. TURNER

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 14, 2026

  Mr. TURNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Dwyer 
School of Irish Dance following its outstanding performance at the 2026 
World Irish Dancing Championships in Chicago. Representing Central Ohio 
and the Miami Valley on an international stage, these talented young 
dancers demonstrated extraordinary discipline, artistry, and commitment 
while competing among the best Irish dancers in the world. Their 
success reflects the dedication of the dancers, instructors, and 
families who continue to strengthen and celebrate Irish culture in our 
communities.
  I would especially like to recognize Liam Rardon of Centerville, Mia 
Duvall of Bellbrook, Madison King of Centerville, Knighton Ambrosius of 
Dayton, Aidan Dwyer of Centerville, and Sean Dwyer of Centerville for 
their impressive individual accomplishments at the championships. In 
addition, the Dwyer School's ceili and team dancers earned exceptional 
international recognition, with the Subminor Mixed Team placing second 
in the world and the Senior Mixed Team placing third in the world in 
their respective categories. The school's dance drama team also earned 
second place for its creative performance honoring aviation pioneer 
Douglas ``Wrong Way'' Conigan. Their tribute to ``the Flying Irishman'' 
also served as a meaningful celebration of Dayton's proud legacy as the 
Birthplace of Aviation and home of the Wright Brothers.
  These accomplishments reflect the hard work and commitment of the 
dancers, instructors, and families who support the Dwyer School of 
Irish Dance. I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating them on 
their success and for the pride they have brought to the Miami Valley 
and the State of Ohio.

                          ____________________
```
