# RECOGNIZING FIREFIGHTER/INSPECTOR JOHN BEEBE

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
              RECOGNIZING FIREFIGHTER/INSPECTOR JOHN BEEBE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 14, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
contributions of Port Richey Firefighter/Inspector John Beebe for his 
exceptional dedication, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to 
public safety and municipal service.
  Firefighter/Inspector Beebe has consistently demonstrated a level of 
initiative and work ethic that reflects great credit upon himself and 
the City of Port Richey. In addition to his core responsibilities, he 
plays a vital role in ensuring the safety and reliability of the city's 
fire protection infrastructure through the testing and maintenance of 
every fire hydrant within the municipal water system. Beyond these 
essential duties, Firefighter/Inspector Beebe actively supports 
emergency operations, assisting with everyday calls for service, brush 
fires, and emergency boat responses in the Gulf. His willingness to 
respond wherever needed underscores his deep commitment to protecting 
both residents and visitors alike.
  Firefighter/Inspector Beebe has also elevated the city's fire 
inspection program by ensuring that every business is inspected 
annually, strengthening public safety and compliance across the 
community. In coordination with the building department, he further 
assists in tracking business tax receipts, helping to ensure that local 
businesses remain properly

[[Page E447]]

registered and in compliance with city requirements. Although assigned 
to the fire department, Firefighter/Inspector Beebe is widely 
recognized for his willingness to assist every department within the 
City of Port Richey. He is a dependable team player who consistently 
offers thoughtful feedback, constructive ideas, and collaborative 
support wherever it is needed.
  Equally important, Firefighter/Inspector Beebe approaches every task 
with a positive attitude and a steady smile, regardless of the 
challenge at hand. His professionalism, optimism, and dedication make 
him a valued member of the city workforce and a model of public 
service.
  I commend Firefighter/Inspector John Beebe for his outstanding 
service to the residents of Florida's 12th Congressional District and 
proudly recognize him as a 2026 First Responder of the Year. His 
commitment, versatility, and positive spirit exemplify the very best of 
public service.

                          ____________________
```
