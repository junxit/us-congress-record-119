# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Lord, our God, wake us up. Open our ears to listen and align our 
wills to obey Your instructions.
  You have placed us here. We do not serve because of our efforts 
alone, but You have seen fit to place this mantle of responsibility on 
our shoulders. We stand ready to receive Your orders.
  Train our tongues that we may know how to encourage and sustain those 
who need our advocacy and not burden or weigh down the weary who have 
lost all hope.
  Rein in our will, that we may not rebel against Your leading in the 
living of these days. May we not turn away from the direction You would 
have us go in our service to Your people.
  Even when we find ourselves in the dark, uncertain of what lies 
ahead, even when there is no clear light to illuminate our path, show 
us that we should not just trust in You, but that You call us to lean 
on You to lead the way.
  Sovereign God, we yield to Your leadership and pray in the authority 
of Your name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
