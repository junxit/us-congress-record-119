# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 8836

**Measures:** H.R. 8836

```
           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 8836.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to regulate commerce with foreign 
     nations and among the several States, to establish a uniform 
     rule of naturalization, and to make all laws necessary and 
     proper for carrying into execution such powers, pursuant to 
     Article I, Section 8, Clauses 3, 4, and 18 of the 
     Constitution of the United States.
```
