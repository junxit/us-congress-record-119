# Waiving Quorum Call (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Schmitt, Eric (S001227) [R-MO]

**Measures:** S.Res. 690

```
                          Waiving Quorum Call

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to waive the 
mandatory quorum call with respect to the cloture motion on the 
nominations en bloc pursuant to S. Res. 690.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHMITT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
