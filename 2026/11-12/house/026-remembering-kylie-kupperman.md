# REMEMBERING KYLIE KUPPERMAN

**Speaking:** Gottheimer, Josh (G000583) [D-NJ]

```
                      REMEMBERING KYLIE KUPPERMAN

  (Mr. GOTTHEIMER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GOTTHEIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to remember a very special young 
woman who none of us will ever forget and who left behind a lasting 
legacy, Kylie Kupperman. Tomorrow would have been her 21st birthday.
  Her parents, Hayley and Adam, are very dear friends of mine. I had 
the privilege of knowing Kylie from the day she was born. Just weeks 
before her fourth birthday, Kylie was diagnosed with medulloblastoma, a 
rare and fast-growing pediatric brain tumor. She courageously beat it 
the first time, but it came back years later.
  Smiley Kylie's smile never left her face, as you can see, no matter 
what was thrown at her. She got into her first-choice college, the 
University of Delaware, to study fashion design. She was 19 years old 
when she passed away.
  To honor the impact she had on all of us and her favorite color, 
pink, I introduced a resolution designating her birthday, November 13, 
tomorrow, as Kylie's Day. I will continue to fight to pass the 
bipartisan Kylie's Law to ensure pediatric cancer patients have a seat 
at the table.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in supporting that 
legislation. May Kylie's memory be a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
