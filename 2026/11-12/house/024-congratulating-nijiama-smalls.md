# CONGRATULATING NIJIAMA SMALLS

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
                     CONGRATULATING NIJIAMA SMALLS

  (Mr. VINDMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Ms. Nijiama Smalls 
on being named president of the Prince William County NAACP.
  A former vice president of the organization, Nijiama brings a strong 
record of leadership and advocacy to her new role. She is also the 
executive director and founder of The Black Girl Healing Project, a 
nonprofit dedicated to providing healing resources and supportive 
spaces for Black women and girls navigating emotional wounds and 
systemic trauma.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend Ms. Nijiama Smalls for her commitment to 
service and look forward to seeing the positive impact of her 
leadership on the Prince William County community and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
