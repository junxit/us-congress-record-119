# HONORING OUR MEN AND WOMEN IN UNIFORM

**Speaking:** Salinas, Andrea (S001226) [D-OR]

```
                 HONORING OUR MEN AND WOMEN IN UNIFORM

  (Ms. SALINAS asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. SALINAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the brave men and 
women who have worn our Nation's uniform and sacrificed so much to 
defend our freedoms.
  As the daughter of a Vietnam veteran, I know firsthand the sacrifices 
our servicemembers and their families make in service to our country.
  My father taught me the values of duty, sacrifice, and service to 
something greater than yourself, but today, we are failing to live up 
to our promise to those who served. The Trump administration's policies 
are making matters worse.
  Last year alone, the VA lost roughly 30,000 workers, including 
thousands of nurses, doctors, and staff responsible for processing 
veterans benefits. Today, VA facilities across the country are being 
forced to do more with less, leaving dedicated healthcare professionals 
stretched thin.
  Our veterans answered the call to our Nation. Now, it is our turn to 
answer the call to serve them. By passing the Major Richard Star Act 
today, we are taking a step in the right direction.
  Mr. Speaker, we owe our veterans the care, benefits, and dignity they 
have earned.

                          ____________________
```
