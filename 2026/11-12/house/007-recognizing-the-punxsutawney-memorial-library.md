# RECOGNIZING THE PUNXSUTAWNEY MEMORIAL LIBRARY

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE PUNXSUTAWNEY MEMORIAL LIBRARY

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the Punxsutawney Memorial Library for achieving gold star status from 
the Pennsylvania Library Association.
  This achievement reflects the library's commitment to education and 
community services.
  This status is part of the Pennsylvania Forward initiative, a 
statewide effort to help libraries provide resources and information 
that support people.
  Pennsylvania Forward recognizes that libraries can position people 
for successful careers through reading. The program offers books 
centered on basic literacy, civics, health, and finances.
  Mr. Speaker, libraries are an integral part of our communities. They 
create a love for learning. They teach children essential skills, and 
they provide a sense of community for many seniors.
  I thank all the librarians and staff across Pennsylvania who serve 
children, families, students, and seniors every day.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend the Punxsutawney Memorial Library on this 
accomplishment. I look forward to learning more about their success in 
the future.
```
