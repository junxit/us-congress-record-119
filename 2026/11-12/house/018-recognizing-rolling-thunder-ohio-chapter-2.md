# RECOGNIZING ROLLING THUNDER OHIO CHAPTER 2

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
               RECOGNIZING ROLLING THUNDER OHIO CHAPTER 2

  (Mrs. SYKES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize Rolling Thunder 
Ohio Chapter 2 as the Champion of the Week for Ohio's 13th 
Congressional District.
  The organization was recently named Summit County's 2026 Veteran of 
the Year. This recognition honors the organization's continued 
commitment to supporting those who have served our Nation and ensuring 
their sacrifices are never forgotten.
  Through its work to keep the POW-MIA mission alive, Rolling Thunder 
raises awareness for the servicemembers who never made it home and the 
families who continue to await answers.
  Just as importantly, the organization works year-round to support 
local veterans and make a meaningful difference in the lives of those 
who have worn our Nation's uniform.
  As Veterans Day reminds us, honoring our veterans must extend beyond 
ceremonies and holidays. It means showing up, lending a helping hand, 
and making sure those who serve receive the recognition, support, and 
gratitude they deserve and they have earned.
  I am grateful for Rolling Thunder Ohio Chapter 2 and the meaningful 
impact its members continue to make through Summit County and our 
entire community. Congratulations again on this well-deserved 
recognition, and may God bless and keep our troops safe.

                          ____________________
```
