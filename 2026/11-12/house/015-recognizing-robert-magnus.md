# RECOGNIZING ROBERT MAGNUS

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING ROBERT MAGNUS

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize retired 
United States Marine Corps General Robert Magnus and wish to 
congratulate him on his induction into the Georgia Military Veterans 
Hall of Fame.
  Mr. Magnus has a long and impressive career in our Nation's Armed 
Forces and has dedicated decades of his life to serving and protecting 
the American people.
  Mr. Magnus served more than 39 years in our Nation's military and was 
the last Active-Duty Marine Corps officer who had served during the 
Vietnam war.
  Mr. Magnus has received countless awards and accolades over the 
years, having earned every one of them in his fine service to his 
country.
  In his time after leaving the Armed Forces, Mr. Magnus has served as 
a director and as a consultant on a wide spectrum of organizations and 
international businesses.
  Mr. Magnus remains dedicated to the military community and veterans 
across the Nation. His commitment to serving this Nation is unwavering, 
and we are proud to have Mr. Magnus and his wife, Meredith, as fellow 
members of our Georgia community.

                          ____________________
```
