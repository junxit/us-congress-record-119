# RECOGNIZING YUBA-SUTTER VETERANS DAY PARADE

**Speaking:** Gallagher, James (G000607) [R-CA]

```
              RECOGNIZING YUBA-SUTTER VETERANS DAY PARADE

  (Mr. GALLAGHER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GALLAGHER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Yuba-Sutter 
Veterans Day Parade and the neighbors who have kept this tradition 
going for 24 years.
  Every Veterans Day, families gather in downtown Marysville to thank 
the men and women who have served our great country. Local veterans' 
groups come alongside the community, and servicemembers come from 
nearby Beale Air Force Base to attend the parade.
  Volunteers put on this parade, and local donations keep it free for 
groups to participate.
  It takes a lot of work to make this great event happen every year, 
and I am grateful for the amazing volunteers who make it happen.
  Families from all around come out and enjoy the parade, but mostly 
what we do is we say thank you to our veterans who have sacrificed so 
much for our country. It is something we should do every day, not just 
on Veterans Day.
  This year's grand marshal is Marine Corps veteran Mike Fitzgerald. 
Mike has spent years helping organize this parade, and I am glad our 
community gets to recognize him for his service.
  To our veterans across Yuba-Sutter, I thank you for serving our 
country. When families line the streets of Marysville on Veterans Day, 
they are there for you.

                          ____________________
```
