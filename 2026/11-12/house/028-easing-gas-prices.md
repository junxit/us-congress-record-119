# EASING GAS PRICES

**Speaking:** Tokuda, Jill N. (T000487) [D-HI]

```
                           EASING GAS PRICES

  (Ms. TOKUDA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. TOKUDA. Mr. Speaker, families can't fill their gas tanks with 
hopes and promises.
  Last week, Americans spoke loud and clear: lower costs, end the war, 
and put working families first. I hear the strain from families in 
Hawaii and across the country.
  Since this reckless war in Iran began, gas prices have surged. In 
Hawaii, a gallon now averages $5.63, 26 percent higher than last year 
and more than a dollar above the national average. That means harder, 
impossible choices between getting to work, paying for rent, keeping 
the lights on, and putting food on the table.
  We must end this war and reopen the Strait of Hormuz, but families 
need relief now. We have good bills before Congress that can ease the 
pain at the pump. Suspending the Federal gas tax would immediately save 
drivers 18 cents a gallon.
  Mr. Speaker, the need is urgent. The bills are here. Let's act now 
and give families some room to breathe.

                          ____________________
```
