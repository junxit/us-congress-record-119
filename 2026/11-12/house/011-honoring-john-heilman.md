# HONORING JOHN HEILMAN

**Speaking:** Friedman, Laura (F000483) [D-CA]

```
                         HONORING JOHN HEILMAN

  (Ms. FRIEDMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. FRIEDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor John Heilman, one of 
the founders and the mayor of West Hollywood, California, who is 
retiring after nearly 40 years on the city council.
  In 1984, John was elected to West Hollywood's very first city 
council. He helped to build the city from day one, and he is one of the 
longest serving openly gay elected officials in the United States of 
America.
  In 1985, in the early years of the AIDS crisis, John helped to write 
one of the Nation's very first laws banning discrimination against 
people living with HIV and AIDS. He helped to create one of the 
country's first domestic partnership registries, extending recognition 
and benefits to same-sex couples and senior residents. He cofounded the 
West Hollywood Community Housing Corporation to build affordable 
housing.
  His colleagues have chosen him to lead as mayor again and again, most 
recently just this past year. Along the way, he spent decades teaching 
law at Whittier, Southwestern, USC, and UCLA, mentoring generations of 
lawyers. In his final term, he is donating his entire city council 
salary to community organizations.
  As a former mayor myself, I know how much of this work happens far 
from the cameras and without public accolades. John has been doing this 
work without thanks for four decades, and I thank him for all of his 
work and service.

                          ____________________
```
