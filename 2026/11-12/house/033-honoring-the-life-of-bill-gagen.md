# HONORING THE LIFE OF BILL GAGEN

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                    HONORING THE LIFE OF BILL GAGEN

  (Mr. DeSAULNIER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of 
Bill Gagen, a distinguished attorney and community advocate in my 
district in the San Francisco Bay area.
  As a young man, Bill walked 54 miles in the historic Selma marches 
with Dr. King and saw his speech in Montgomery, Alabama.
  After graduating from the University of California Berkeley Law 
School and serving in the U.S. Army, Bill worked as a deputy district 
attorney in Contra Costa County. He later became a criminal defense 
attorney, taking on many young clients who needed a second chance.
  Bill also served his community on local boards, including the De La 
Salle High School Board of Trustees, the Danville Fire Protection 
District, and many other nonprofits.
  Sadly, Bill passed away in September at the age of 83. He will be 
fondly remembered for his quick wit and his dedication to his community 
and this country.
  Please join me in honoring the life and service of Bill Gagen.

                          ____________________
```
