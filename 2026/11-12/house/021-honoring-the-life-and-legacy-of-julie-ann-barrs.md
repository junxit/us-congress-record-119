# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JULIE ANN BARRS

**Speaking:** Clyde, Andrew S. (C001116) [R-GA]

```
                              {time}  1110
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JULIE ANN BARRS

  (Mr. CLYDE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CLYDE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Julie Ann Barrs, a constituent from Ellijay, Georgia, who tragically 
passed away during last week's historic flood that ravaged north 
Georgia.
  Julie was born in Atlanta and spent most of her life in neighboring 
Cobb County. She worked up through the ranks of the Cobb County 
Sheriff's Office, where she became the agency's first female SWAT team 
member. Additionally, Julie worked with the U.S. Marshals on human 
trafficking operations.
  After moving to Ellijay, Julie took up work at Pilgrim's processing 
plant and was a faithful member of Hope Lutheran Church. Her passion 
for music, the outdoors, and her beloved dogs was contagious.
  Julie was a Georgian of incredible accomplishment with a tender heart 
and a mindset of serving those around her. I send my deepest 
condolences to her friends and loved ones, including her brother, Jeff, 
during this time of tragedy.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all take comfort in the finished work of Christ 
and the words of Psalm 34:18: ``The Lord is close to the brokenhearted 
and saves those who are crushed in spirit.''
```
