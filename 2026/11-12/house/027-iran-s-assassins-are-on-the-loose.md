# IRAN'S ASSASSINS ARE ON THE LOOSE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                   IRAN'S ASSASSINS ARE ON THE LOOSE

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, The Wall Street Journal is 
correct today. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's move from his home to 
a U.S. military base following Iranian threats on his life is part of a 
much larger story. Iran's regime has been hunting top U.S. officials.
  It was true before the current war, and nobody could expect that a 
deal to end the war would make it go away. On Tuesday, the FBI added 
Shahram Poursafi, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps operative 
accused of plotting to murder John Bolton, to its most wanted list.
  Additionally, the same day, the Justice Department indicted Mohammad 
Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi, alleging he helped direct the September 
plot against the U.S. bombers at the U.K.'s Fairford air base in the 
United Kingdom.
  Additionally, these threats are serious, and Mr. Bolton isn't the 
only one to face them, as former Secretary of State Mike Pompeo and 
U.S. Special Representative for Iran Brian Hook can attest. U.S. 
enforcement has broken up other Iranian murder-for-hire plots against 
President Trump.

  This month, the Iranian regime put up a billboard in Tehran 
announcing: ``We Will Kill Trump.''
  We are in a war America did not choose of dictators with rule of gun 
invading democracies with rule of law. When the regime in Tehran 
chants, ``Death to Israel. Death to America,'' they mean it.

                          ____________________
```
