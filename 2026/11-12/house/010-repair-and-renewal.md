# REPAIR AND RENEWAL

**Speaking:** Dean, Madeleine (D000631) [D-PA]

```
                              {time}  0910
                           REPAIR AND RENEWAL

  (Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, last week, the American people 
issued a resounding rejection of the chaos, corruption, and cruelty of 
the current administration: a reckless war with Iran, an erratic trade 
war, inhumane immigration tactics, ripping apart our social safety net, 
and degrading our institutions and civil servants.
  All of this has sent the cost of living for your family and mine 
soaring. All the while, the President has enriched himself and his 
family to the tune of more than $2 billion.
  Last week, the American people said no more. They gave Democrats a 
historic majority, a historic chance to lead, and a charge to bring the 
cost of gas and groceries down, to expand homeownership, to end wars, 
and to tell Americans that when they get sick, they will get care.
  Americans rejected the rot and rubble of this administration, and 
they embraced the hope for our government that we can and should work 
for them. I believe that, too.
  Now is the time to repair and to renew our public trust in American 
institutions and our faith for a brighter tomorrow, for the people.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Wittman). Members are reminded to 
refrain from engaging in personalities toward the President.

                          ____________________
```
