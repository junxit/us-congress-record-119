# FIGHTING FOR A MORE JUST AMERICA

**Speaking:** Pressley, Ayanna (P000617) [D-MA]

```
                    FIGHTING FOR A MORE JUST AMERICA

  (Ms. PRESSLEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)

[[Page H6203]]

  

  Ms. PRESSLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today for every person who dreams 
of a better tomorrow. I rise for every person who ran, voted, and 
organized to deliver a mandate for bold, transformative change, from 
Massachusetts to Texas to Mississippi, for those who turned hope into 
action.
  Mr. Speaker, it is so important that we fight for a more just 
America. It is what we owe to every person who calls this country home.
  We need a more just America where one job is enough and no one goes 
bankrupt when they get sick. We need a more just America where Black 
men grow old, Black women birth safely, and children live, learn, and 
play without fear. We need a more just America where people don't have 
to choose between rent, medical bills, and groceries.
  Some say we are asking for too much, that we should work with the 
very Republicans who have used their power to exact harm, but 
incrementalism is a recipe for failure. If we moderate our aspirations, 
we cost people their lives and livelihoods.
  Mr. Speaker, the Democrats must meet the people's cries with action. 
We cannot let money or special interests dictate our fight. We are 
accountable to the people who sent us here, period.
  This is the people's House, and we must advance the people's agenda. 
With the supermajority on the way, it is time we move exactly like 
that.

                          ____________________
```
