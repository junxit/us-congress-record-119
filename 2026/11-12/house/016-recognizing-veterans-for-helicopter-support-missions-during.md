# RECOGNIZING VETERANS FOR HELICOPTER SUPPORT MISSIONS DURING VIETNAM

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 5939

```
  RECOGNIZING VETERANS FOR HELICOPTER SUPPORT MISSIONS DURING VIETNAM

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize 
the Marine Corps and Navy veterans who performed helicopter support 
missions during Vietnam.
  The 1st Marine Aircraft Wing missions flown between 1962 and 1975 
transported troops, cargo, and about 189,000 wounded and sick Marines 
and sailors to medical facilities.
  These missions took them into dangerous combat zones to rescue fellow 
servicemembers and bring them to safety.
  H.R. 5939, the USMC Helicopter Support in Vietnam Congressional Gold 
Medal Act, properly recognizes these remarkable veterans.
  We must not forget their bravery.

                          ____________________
```
