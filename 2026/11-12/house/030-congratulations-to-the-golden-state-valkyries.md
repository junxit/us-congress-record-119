# CONGRATULATIONS TO THE GOLDEN STATE VALKYRIES

**Speaking:** Simon, Lateefah (S001231) [D-CA]

```
             CONGRATULATIONS TO THE GOLDEN STATE VALKYRIES

  (Ms. SIMON asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. SIMON. Mr. Speaker, I rise in honor, in support, and in 
congratulations to the Golden State Valkyries, champions of the WNBA.
  On Halloween night, they beat the Atlanta Dream 68-63 in game 7 and 
won their first championship.
  This team is just 2 years old. No expansion team in WNBA history has 
ever won the championship faster.
  The Valks train in Oakland, California, my district. When they play 
and when they train, they exude such joy, such skill, and beautiful 
sisterhood.
  In a time where Black, Brown, and queer people are under attack by 
their own government, the Valks have made ``Ballhalla'' a place where 
our neighbors feel loved and safe.
  Congratulations to Ohemaa, the general manager of the Valks; Coach 
Natalie; Gabby Williams, our MVP; and the entire organization.
  So much love to the Bay for supporting these women. Play ball. Go 
Valks.

                          ____________________
```
