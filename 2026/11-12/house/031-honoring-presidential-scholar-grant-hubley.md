# HONORING PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLAR GRANT HUBLEY

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
               HONORING PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLAR GRANT HUBLEY

  (Ms. KAPTUR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Grant Hubley on being 
named a U.S. Presidential Scholar in Career and Technical Education 
from the State of Ohio.
  A graduate of Anthony Wayne High School in Waterville, Ohio, Grant is 
the first-ever Career and Technical Education scholar from northwest 
Ohio and the first Presidential Scholar of any kind from Ohio's Ninth 
Congressional District since 2013.
  This highly competitive award recognizes our Nation's most 
distinguished high school seniors.
  This accomplishment is not only a testament to Grant's dedication but 
also to the caliber of teachers and staff who help educate our 
northwest Ohio students day in and day out, year after year.
  Northwest Ohio is so very privileged to have dedicated teachers that 
tirelessly serve our youth and pave the way for our students to succeed 
and achieve in all they do.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Grant Hubley on this tremendous 
achievement. I wish him the best of luck as he continues his studies at 
Ohio University. He represents northwest Ohio so well, and he certainly 
has a bright, constructive future ahead.

                          ____________________
```
