# HOLDING CONGRESS ACCOUNTABLE

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
                      HOLDING CONGRESS ACCOUNTABLE

  (Mr. HERNANDEZ asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, the American people gave a mandate to 
this Congress, a mandate to focus on affordability and accountability.
  As Puerto Rico's Representative in Congress, I will fight for our 
island's priorities within this mandate.
  Let's fix the Medicaid funding gap. Let's fix the nutritional 
assistance funding gap. Let's fix the island's energy cost crisis. 
Let's hold the crooks accountable here in Washington and in Puerto Rico 
as well.
  Mr. Speaker, I also welcome the students from Wesleyan Academy who 
are visiting Washington here today. This is their House, and I am here 
to assist them and assist the people of Puerto Rico as well.

                          ____________________
```
