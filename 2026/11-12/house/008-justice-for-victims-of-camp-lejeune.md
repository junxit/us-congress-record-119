# JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF CAMP LEJEUNE

**Speaking:** Ross, Deborah K. (R000305) [D-NC]

```
                  JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF CAMP LEJEUNE

  (Ms. ROSS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. ROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to demand justice for the 
veterans and families affected by water contamination at Camp Lejeune.
  It has been over 40 years since toxic water was discovered on the 
grounds of Camp Lejeune, but many victims are still waiting for our 
government to do right by them.
  Too many have been left waiting for help that never arrived. Some 
have passed away without ever seeing their claims adjudicated.
  These men and women served our country honorably, only to suffer from 
the devastating illnesses caused by one of the worst toxic water 
contamination disasters in the U.S.
  Thousands of Camp Lejeune claims remain trapped in a backlog in a 
single judicial district, leaving veterans and their families waiting 
for years for the justice they were promised.
  Just last month, our President decided to dangle the fate of their 
futures in front of them as a political ploy.
  It is disrespectful. It is disturbing, and it is time to put politics 
aside and deliver swift justice and fair compensation to Camp Lejeune 
victims.

                          ____________________
```
