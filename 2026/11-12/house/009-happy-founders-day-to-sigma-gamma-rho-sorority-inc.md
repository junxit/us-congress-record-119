# HAPPY FOUNDERS DAY TO SIGMA GAMMA RHO SORORITY, INC.

**Speaking:** Kelly, Robin L. (K000385) [D-IL]

```
          HAPPY FOUNDERS DAY TO SIGMA GAMMA RHO SORORITY, INC.

  (Ms. KELLY of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KELLY of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I would like to wish my sorority, 
Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Incorporated, led by International Grand 
Basileus Harris, a very happy Founders Day.

[[Page H6194]]

  Mr. Speaker, 104 years ago, seven fearless Black educators at Butler 
University gathered in segregated Indianapolis, Indiana, to lay the 
foundation for the first and only Divine Nine sorority founded on a 
predominantly White campus, with a legacy of community enrichment, 
education, and Black excellence forever echoed by the words: ``greater 
service, greater progress.''
  Sigma Gamma Rho is home to many firsts--the first Black woman 
journalist allowed in the White House, the first Black woman CPA, and 
the first African-American person to win an Oscar.
  From the Halls of Capitol Hill, I salute my former colleagues, 
Corrine Brown and the first Black woman mayor of Oakland, Barbara Lee, 
my mentor and my shero.
  On another note, I want to salute Commander Jay Beck and his 11 line 
brothers for becoming men of Omega Psi Phi on this day 4 years ago 
through Chi Lambda Lambda. I wish a happy queversary to those 
gentlemen.

                          ____________________
```
