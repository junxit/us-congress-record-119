# RECOGNIZING VIRGINIA UNIVERSITY OF LYNCHBURG

**Speaking:** McGuire, John J. (M001239) [R-VA]

```
              RECOGNIZING VIRGINIA UNIVERSITY OF LYNCHBURG

  (Mr. McGUIRE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. McGUIRE. Mr. Speaker, I seek to recognize an amazing educational 
institute in my district.
  The Virginia University of Lynchburg is a historic African-American 
institution founded in 1886 to educate ministers, missionaries, and 
public school teachers.

[[Page H6202]]

  Rooted in Christian heritage, VUL provides students with an 
environment that combines liberal arts education with spiritual and 
trade development.
  The university seeks to recognize the potential in every student and 
equips them to become capable leaders and scholars.
  VUL emphasizes Christian service, academic excellence, critical 
thinking, communication, and service to others.
  For many years, the university has operated teaching locations 
throughout Virginia, continuing its historic missionary spirit of 
serving the community.
  I am proud to represent such institutions who are devoted to the 
betterment of both faith and community in their scholars.
```
