# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Loving God, we pray Your protection over us this day. Guard us from 
anything or anyone who would steal our affection for You.
  It is so easy to get caught up in the business and the busyness of 
the day that our own schedules steal the time and attention we owe to 
You.
  Things happen, assaulting our sense of certainty and obscuring our 
clarity, that we fail to see You in the midst of it all guiding us 
through.
  Fear, doubt, and cynicism cloud our spirits and undermine our desire 
to remain faithful to Your will for our lives.
  Easy answers and lucrative options lure us away from standing fast by 
Your precepts when bearing the strain of hard choices and difficult 
decisions.
  Heavenly Father, as children dear to You, on this day and in the 
living of our lives, may we keep away from anything that dares to take 
Your place in our hearts.
  To You, the one true God, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
