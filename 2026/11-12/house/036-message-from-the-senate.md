# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 2434, S. 3589, S. 3720, S. 3923, H.R. 4930, H.R. 8552

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment bills of the 
House of the following titles:

       H.R. 4930. An ACT to expand the sharing of information with 
     respect to suspected violations of intellectual property 
     rights in trade.
       H.R. 8552. An ACT to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2026, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the surviviors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed bills of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2434. An ACT to amend the Dayton Aviation Heritage 
     Preservation Act of 1992 to adjust the boundary of the Dayton 
     Aviation Heritage National Historical Park, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 3589. An ACT to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 
     to provide students with disabilities and their families with 
     access to critical information needed to select the right 
     college and succeed once enrolled.

       S. 3720. An ACT to amend the National Trails System Act to 
     direct the Secretary of the Interior to conduct a study on 
     the feasibility of designating the Bay Area Ridge as a 
     national scenic trail, and for other purposes.
       S. 3923. An ACT to improve the weather research of the 
     National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, support 
     improvements in weather forecasting and prediction and expand 
     commercial opportunities for the provision of weather data.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 93-642, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, appoints the following Senator 
to be a member of the Board of Trustees of the Harry S. Truman 
Scholarship Foundation:
  The Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Kim) vice the Senator from Hawaii 
(Mr. Schatz).
  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 119-60, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader, appoints the following 
individual to serve as a member of the Commission on the National 
Defense Strategy:
  Carrie Filipetti of the District of Columbia.
  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 96-114, as 
amended, the Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader appoints the 
following individual to the Congressional Award Board:
  Andi Fouberg of South Dakota.

                          ____________________
```
