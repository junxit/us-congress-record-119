# RECOGNIZING FOOD ACCESS PROGRAMS

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                    RECOGNIZING FOOD ACCESS PROGRAMS

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight the Food Access 
in Community Health Centers Act.
  Amid rising costs and unprecedented cuts to SNAP and Medicaid, 
emergency feeding operations and community health centers provide 
critical support to countless Americans. That is why I am proud to have 
introduced this legislation, alongside Congresswoman Kelly of Illinois, 
to implement programs that couple community health centers with 
emergency feeding operations across the country.
  We know that food access is directly linked to health outcomes, and 
this bill aims to address the food insecurity that patients can face 
while seeking essential care. I commend all the incredible 
organizations, including Feeding Westchester and Westchester Community 
Health Center, that have already established strong partnerships like 
these.
  Mr. Speaker, I look forward to seeing food access programs 
implemented at community health centers across the country.
```
