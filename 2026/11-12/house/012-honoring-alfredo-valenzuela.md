# HONORING ALFREDO VALENZUELA

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Adelita S. (G000606) [D-AZ]

```
                      HONORING ALFREDO VALENZUELA

  (Mrs. GRIJALVA asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Alfredo 
Valenzuela, a recipient of the Ray Davies Lifetime Humanitarian 
Achievement Award from the Educational Enrichment Foundation.
  Dr. Valenzuela is one of the most deserving people to receive this 
award. For over 40 years, Dr. V has dedicated his life to nurturing 
young people through mariachi music, preserving culture, building 
confidence through song and performance, and inspiring generations of 
students across southern Arizona, including all of his own children, 
who now lead mariachi groups of their own.
  I know firsthand about the impact of his life's work. Dr. V was my 
guitar teacher when I was 7 years old, and I am proud to count myself 
among his former students, as are my children.
  A few years ago, our community gathered outside Davis-Romero 
Bilingual School to celebrate the legacy and unveil a beautiful statue 
in his honor. Seeing generations of students come together, including 
those now leading their own mariachi programs, was a powerful testament 
to his vision and dedication.
  When I spoke to Dr. V, he shared how much joy it brings him to watch 
his ``little eagles,'' ``aguilitas,'' grow year after year. His eyes 
filled with tears, and it was clear just how deeply he loves our 
children.
  ``Our thanks,'' ``muchisimas gracias,'' to Dr. V for his vision, his 
passion, and his extraordinary contributions. I thank him for inspiring 
generations of children.
  I give my love to Dr. V and offer ``congrats,'' ``felicidades,'' on 
this well-deserved honor.

                          ____________________
```
