# PROTECTING PUBLIC LANDS FROM DATA CENTERS

**Speaking:** Dexter, Maxine (D000635) [D-OR]

```
               PROTECTING PUBLIC LANDS FROM DATA CENTERS

  (Ms. DEXTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, how many times do Oregonians have to say it? 
Our public lands are not for sale. Oregon has some of the most 
beautiful and pristine public lands in the Nation.
  Last year, Republicans tried to sell our public lands to the highest 
bidder. Oregonians raised hell.
  Now, Interior Secretary Doug Burgum wants to sell off public lands so 
corporations can build data centers, destroying them for good.
  Oregon has had enough.
  This week, I introduced the Protecting Our Public Lands from Data 
Centers Act to stop new data centers from being built on Federal public 
lands.
  We cannot allow billionaires and corporations to destroy these 
treasured spaces where we take our kids camping, where we cast a line 
to fish, and where we hike with our families.
  These are places generations of Americans have explored, cared for, 
and passed down. These public lands are treasured and must be protected 
for generations to come. I am fighting to protect every acre. Stay 
loud.

                          ____________________
```
