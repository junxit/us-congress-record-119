# HONORING ANTONIO GUTIERREZ

**Speaking:** Ramirez, Delia C. (R000617) [D-IL]

```
                              {time}  1120
                       HONORING ANTONIO GUTIERREZ

  (Mrs. Ramirez asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Antonio Gutierrez, 
the

[[Page H6204]]

embodiment of a good neighbor, for his work to defend our communities 
from displacement and deportations.
  For over a decade, Antonio has organized in our communities to 
protect immigrants, to ensure tenants' rights against eviction and 
unfair housing practices, and to dismantle the for-profit mass 
detention system.
  In their current role at Organized Communities Against Deportations, 
OCAD, as DHS terrorized our communities during Midway Blitz, Antonio 
helped activate observers, secure resources for Chicago's undocumented 
community, connect immigrants to legal resources, and so much more.
  Antonio inspires me to fight for pathways to citizenship for all. And 
when we fight, we win.
  On behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, I am proud to 
commend Antonio Gutierrez for pushing us to be unafraid and to stand up 
for one another and for our shared humanity.
  On this day, Antonio's name has been entered officially into the 
Congressional Record.

                          ____________________
```
