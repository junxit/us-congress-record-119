# RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO DEFENSE FELLOW CAPTAIN CRYSTAL YOUNG

**Speaking:** Jackson, Ronny (J000304) [R-TX]

```
RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO DEFENSE FELLOW CAPTAIN CRYSTAL 
                                 YOUNG

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. RONNY JACKSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. JACKSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
express my sincere gratitude to Captain Crystal Young of the United 
States Marine Corps for her outstanding work and service to the people 
of Texas' 13th Congressional District while serving as my Defense 
Fellow during 2026. Captain Young has been an exceptional addition to 
my office, bringing unparalleled military experience, a strong work 
ethic, and a deep understanding of the challenges facing our service 
members.
  Captain Young joined my office with an impressive educational 
background and extraordinary experience from her distinguished Marine 
Corps career, which proved invaluable in advancing my legislative 
priorities on the House Armed Services Committee. Crystal began her 
military career in 2013, serving as an aviation maintenance technician 
before earning her commission through the Marine Corps Enlisted 
Commissioning Program in 2018. She earned her Bachelor of Science 
degree in Aviation Management from Southern Illinois University and a 
Master of Arts degree in Management from the University of Alabama. She 
is currently pursuing a Master of Public Policy degree at George Mason 
University as part of the Marine Corps Congressional Fellowship 
Program, continuing her commitment to developing the knowledge and 
skills neccessary to best serve our nation.
  Throughout her career, Captain Young has served with distinction in 
aviation maintenance, logistics, operational planning, and leadership 
assignments across the Marine Corps. Her experience includes serving as 
Assistant Operations Officer with Marine Aviation Logistics Squadron 
41; Motor Transportation Platoon Commander, Maintenance Management 
Officer, and Service Company Operations Officer with 9th Communication 
Battalion; and Series Commander at Marine Corps Recruit Depot San 
Diego. She also deployed in support of the 24th Marine Expeditionary 
Unit aboard the USS Bataan and served as the Strategic Mobility Officer 
for the MAGTF Deployment and Distribution Operations Center with Task 
Force 51/5th Marine Expeditionary Brigade. These assignments gave 
Crystal firsthand insight into the demands placed on our service 
members and the importance of ensuring they have the training, 
equipment, and logistical support they need to successfully accomplish 
their missions and safeguard American interests.
  That experience proved particularly valuable as Crystal worked to 
advance my national defense priorities in Congress. Her understanding 
of military readiness, force deployment, and operational planning 
helped inform my work on the House Armed Services Committee. Crystal 
played an important role in advancing key provisions and securing 
funding for Texas' 13th Congressional District in the House of 
Representatives' Fiscal Year 2027 National Defense Authorization Act 
and Defense Appropriations bill. Her work has helped secure critical 
capabilities for our warfighters, protect skilled defense workforce 
opportunities in my district, and strengthen America's military for 
generations to come. Her contributions this year have been a testament 
to the value of having experienced military leaders to help shape the 
policies that affect our armed forces.
  While Crystal's professional accomplishments speak for themselves, I 
have also appreciated the character she brought to our team. Her 
integrity, dependability, and commitment to the mission have made her a 
trusted member of my staff. She approaches her responsibilities with 
the same dedication she has demonstrated throughout her Marine Corps 
career, always mindful of the men and women who depend on the decisions 
made here in Washington.
  On behalf of myself, my staff, and the constituents I proudly serve, 
I extend my sincere appreciation to Captain Crystal Young for her 
service to my office and our great Nation. I am grateful for her 
contributions and wish her nothing but continued success as she 
advances her career in the United States Marine Corps.
```
