# RESHORING AMERICAN MANUFACTURING ACT OF 2026

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 9718

```
              RESHORING AMERICAN MANUFACTURING ACT OF 2026

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Ms. STEVENS. Mr. Speaker, helping American manufacturers bring 
production home is a bipartisan goal that should unite this Congress 
and the next. H.R. 9718, the Reshoring American Manufacturing Act of 
2026, is a welcome first step toward that goal, helping small 
businesses find resources and domestic partners.
  Connecting businesses with assistance is valuable, but its 
effectiveness depends on the resources available. Congress must build 
on the RAM Act by ensuring those resources address the financial and 
technological barriers to domestic production.
  That need is particularly pressing in critical materials. China's 
dominance in processing and refining leaves Michigan's automotive 
industry and manufacturers nationwide vulnerable to supply disruptions 
and economic pressures. Building competitive domestic capacity requires 
access to capital and investment in innovation.
  My Unearth America's Future Act would provide that support through 
loans and loan guarantees, investment and production tax credits, 
research and demonstration programs, and a national supply chain 
center. These tools would help expand domestic processing, refining, 
and recycling capabilities, supporting American jobs and reducing 
dependence on China.
  I welcome bipartisan support for the RAM Act. I urge my colleagues to 
build on that commitment by advancing the Unearth America's Future 
Act--strengthening the resources manufacturers need to bring production 
home.
```
