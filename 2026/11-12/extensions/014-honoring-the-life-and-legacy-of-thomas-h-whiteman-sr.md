# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF THOMAS H. WHITEMAN, SR.

**Speaking:** Huizenga, Bill (H001058) [R-MI]

```
        HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF THOMAS H. WHITEMAN, SR.

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. BILL HUIZENGA

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. HUIZENGA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Thomas H. Whiteman, Sr., a devoted husband, father, grandfather, and 
United States Army veteran who passed away on November 7, 2026, at the 
age of 85.
  Born on June 24, 1941, in Gary, Indiana, to James and Ruby Whiteman, 
Tom graduated from La Paz High School in 1961. He went on to serve our 
Nation in the United States Army during the Vietnam War, where he 
worked as a soldier, welder, and blacksmith, earning the rank of 
Sergeant before receiving an honorable discharge.
  On April 15, 1966, Tom married Maxine Norton, beginning a marriage 
that would span more than 60 years. Together, they raised two sons, 
Thomas, Jr. and Todd, and built a family rooted in faith, hard work, 
and service to others.
  Throughout his life, Tom demonstrated remarkable mechanical ability 
and an entrepreneurial spirit. He and Maxine operated Tom's Mobile 
Service before he began his career as a millwright, working at Allied 
Products and later Bendix. During his 32 years at Bendix, Tom served as 
a steward and trustee of United Auto Workers Local 9, advocating for 
his fellow workers until his retirement in 2006.
  Tom's commitment to service extended far beyond his military career. 
A longtime member of Beaver Creek Wesleyan Church, he generously 
volunteered his talents to repair, build, and maintain whatever his 
church family needed. Known for his compassion, resourcefulness, and 
selflessness, Tom was a man who preferred helping others to receiving 
recognition.
  Tom is survived by his beloved wife, Maxine; his sons, Thomas, Jr. 
and Todd; and his grandchildren. His son Todd serves as my Chief of 
Staff, and I have had the privilege of witnessing firsthand the 
dedication to service that continues through the Whiteman family.
  Mr. Speaker, Thomas Whiteman exemplified the values of faith, family, 
hard work, and service to country. His life reminds us that a lasting 
legacy is built not only through great accomplishments, but through the 
countless acts of kindness and generosity that strengthen our families 
and communities.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Sergeant Thomas H. 
Whiteman, Sr., thanking him for his service to our Nation, and 
extending our deepest condolences to Maxine, Todd, and the entire 
Whiteman family. May God bless his memory and comfort all those who 
mourn his passing.
```
