# RECOGNIZING EITAN ALPERSTEIN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING EITAN ALPERSTEIN

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Eitan Alperstein of Denver, Colorado, for earning the Runner Up Award 
in the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.
  Eitan built CivicLens, an app that helps users find reputable sources 
and information on how local and state governments are tackling certain 
public issues. This app, which helps facilitate and encourage civic 
engagement by everyday people, is an impressive example of Eitan's 
determination, hard work, and advanced STEM knowledge base.
  I congratulate Eitan Alperstein for his success in the Congressional 
App Challenge. I am certain he will continue to show the same 
dedication and strength of character in all his future endeavors. I 
look forward to seeing all he will accomplish.

                          ____________________
```
