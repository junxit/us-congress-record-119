# RECOGNIZING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2026 BEST OF BRADDOCK AWARDS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2026 BEST OF BRADDOCK AWARDS

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 2026 
recipients of the Best of Braddock Awards, an annual tradition in 
Virginia's 11th Congressional District that celebrates the residents, 
volunteers, public servants, students, and organizations who strengthen 
the Braddock District of Fairfax County through extraordinary service 
and civic leadership. I include in the Record the following names of 
the 2026 Best of Braddock Award recipients:

       Mary Keeser, 2026 Braddock District Citizen of the Year. 
     Mary has dedicated her life to serving veterans, protecting 
     our parks, and strengthening our neighborhoods. A fourth-
     generation Army veteran, she founded the National Capital 
     Veterans Service Foundation and America's Adopt A Soldier to 
     support service members and veterans. As President of Friends 
     of Lake Accotink Park, she has also led community improvement 
     and conservation efforts, inspiring neighbors to volunteer 
     and give back. Her compassion, generosity, and tireless 
     commitment to helping others embody the very best of public 
     service.
       Minn Vo, 2026 Braddock District Young Person of the Year. 
     Minn has demonstrated extraordinary leadership through his 
     founding of Hands in Hope, a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to 
     advancing health equity and supporting underserved 
     communities. Since establishing the organization in December 
     2025, he has mobilized more than 250 young volunteers to 
     provide essential supplies and resources to over 1,500 
     individuals. His efforts have expanded from Fairfax County to 
     communities nationwide and internationally, empowering young 
     people to become leaders in service and public health.

[[Page E1208]]

       David Vo, 2026 Civic Engagement Award. David has devoted 
     himself to making government and community resources more 
     accessible to residents throughout the Braddock District. As 
     a Legislative Aide to Delegate Vivian Watts, he assists 
     constituents in navigating government services, while his 
     volunteer work supports immigrant families, individuals with 
     disabilities, and underserved communities. Through language 
     assistance, voter outreach, youth mentorship, and civic 
     organizing, David has helped strengthen community 
     participation and ensure more residents have a voice in the 
     decisions affecting their lives.
       Rob Nellis, 2026 Neighborhood Enhancement or Beautification 
     Award. Rob has demonstrated outstanding volunteer leadership 
     through his efforts to improve the Kings Park neighborhood 
     and promote environmental stewardship. He spearheaded the 
     creation of a neighborhood compost collection program, 
     developed an interactive map for the community's annual yard 
     sale, and contributed to local environmental advocacy through 
     the Kings Park Climate Committee. His initiative and 
     dedication have strengthened neighborhood connections and 
     encouraged residents to take an active role in improving 
     their community.
       PFC Farhad Zia, 2026 Most ``Can Do'' Public Employee Award. 
     As a member of the West Springfield Neighborhood Patrol Unit, 
     PFC Zia has demonstrated exceptional investigative skill, 
     leadership, and dedication to public safety. Working 
     alongside the newly established Warrant Squad, he has played 
     a critical role in locating and apprehending individuals 
     wanted for serious offenses, while mentoring fellow officers 
     and strengthening investigative operations. His expertise in 
     securing and executing search warrants has contributed to 
     significant law enforcement successes and helped keep Fairfax 
     County families safe.
       Kings Park West Soccer League, 2026 Club or Organization 
     Making a Difference in the Braddock District Award. The Kings 
     Park West Soccer League has provided an inclusive and 
     welcoming environment for young athletes to develop their 
     skills, build friendships, and learn the importance of 
     teamwork. Under the volunteer leadership of Commissioner 
     David Fick, with contributions from former Commissioner Kevin 
     Hough and Kenneth Keane, the league has expanded its programs 
     to serve more families and include middle school students. 
     Their dedication has made youth athletics more accessible and 
     strengthened the sense of community throughout the Braddock 
     District.

  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 2026 
Best of Braddock Award recipients for their exceptional contributions 
to the Braddock District and Fairfax County. Their dedication, 
leadership, and compassion embody the very best of Virginia's 11th 
Congressional District and serve as an example to communities across 
our Nation.
```
