# RECOGNIZING ADARSH SHANKARLING

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING ADARSH SHANKARLING

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Adarsh Shankarling of Broomfield, Colorado, for earning the Best in 
Show Award in the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.
  Adarsh built the Volentara Volunteer Training Platform, an app that 
provides online training courses for hospital volunteers. He has 
already implemented the app to train volunteers at St. Anthony North 
Hospital in Westminster, Colorado, showing Adarsh's commitment to 
serving his community in addition to his determination, hard work, and 
impressive knowledge base in the STEM field.
  I congratulate Adarsh Shankarling for his success in the 
Congressional App Challenge. I am certain he will continue to show the 
same dedication and strength of character in all his future endeavors. 
I look forward to seeing all he will accomplish.

                          ____________________
```
