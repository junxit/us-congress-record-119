# HONORING DODDIE CAVE AND RUTH HARTMAN FOR THEIR OUTSTANDING SERVICE TO THE RAVENSWORTH FARM COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
HONORING DODDIE CAVE AND RUTH HARTMAN FOR THEIR OUTSTANDING SERVICE TO 
                     THE RAVENSWORTH FARM COMMUNITY

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Doddie Cave and 
Ruth Hartman, two extraordinary residents of Ravensworth Farm in 
Springfield, Virginia, who were recently honored with Outstanding 
Citizen Awards by the Ravensworth Farm Civic Association. For more than 
six decades, these remarkable women have dedicated their time and 
talents to strengthening their neighborhood and serving the Northern 
Virginia community.
  Doddie Cave moved to Ravensworth Farm with her husband, Pat, in 1963 
and has been a pillar of the community ever since. Throughout the 
years, she has volunteered in local schools, supported youth scouting 
programs, served as a swim team representative for seven years, and 
helped organize neighborhood events. As an active member of the 
Ravensworth Farm Garden Club, she helped beautify the community and 
support charitable initiatives. She also served as a neighborhood block 
captain for 20 years, assisted with the Ravensworth Farmer newsletter, 
and worked as an election official for decades. As Doddie and her 
family prepare to leave Ravensworth Farm after 63 years, they leave 
behind an extraordinary legacy of service, friendship, and community 
spirit.
  Ruth Hartman has demonstrated that same unwavering commitment to 
service since moving to Ravensworth Farm with her late husband, Ken, in 
1964. Ruth has served as a room mother, PTA volunteer, Girl Scout 
leader, and civic association corresponding secretary. She helped 
organize the community's 50th anniversary celebration and continues to 
serve as a neighborhood block captain. Her dedication extends well 
beyond Ravensworth Farm. Ruth served as an election official for more 
than 70 elections, earning recognition from Fairfax County for her 
exceptional service. She also volunteered for 28 years at Fairfax 
Hospital and has devoted countless hours to her church, where she has 
served as both an elder and a deacon.
  As a former resident of Ravensworth Farm, I know firsthand what makes 
this neighborhood special. Its strong sense of community is built upon 
the generosity, dedication, and neighborly spirit of residents like 
Doddie and Ruth. Their decades of volunteerism have enriched the lives 
of generations of families and helped make Ravensworth Farm a place 
where neighbors truly care for one another.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Doddie 
Cave and Ruth Hartman on receiving the Outstanding Citizen Awards and 
thanking them for their remarkable contributions to Ravensworth Farm 
and Northern Virginia. Their lifelong commitment to service exemplifies 
the very best of our community, and I wish them both continued 
happiness and fulfillment in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
