# RECOGNIZING JOANNA F. "JODY" JONSSON FOR WORKING TO IMPROVE PEOPLE'S LIVES THROUGH SUCCESSFUL INVESTING

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

```
RECOGNIZING JOANNA F. ``JODY'' JONSSON FOR WORKING TO IMPROVE PEOPLE'S 
                   LIVES THROUGH SUCCESSFUL INVESTING

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. BRAD SHERMAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. SHERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Joanna F. 
``Jody'' Jonsson, Vice Chair of Capital Group, who will retire at the 
end of this year after 36 years with the firm. Capital Group is not 
only headquartered, but was also founded 95 years ago, in my hometown 
of Los Angeles.
  Jody joined Capital Group in 1990 as an investment research analyst 
in its San Francisco office and went on to become a portfolio manager 
and, ultimately, principal investment officer of Capital Group's New 
Perspective Fund. She invested through nearly every kind of market, 
from the dot-com era to the Global Financial Crisis to the pandemic, 
and became known for rigorous research and for staying calm when 
markets were not. Along the way, she met with countless company 
management teams, government officials, and regulators around the 
world, bringing a global perspective shaped by her years of living and 
working in Geneva and London. Barron's named her one of the 100 Most 
Influential Women in U.S.

[[Page E1204]]

Finance, and she has been recognized by Forbes among the women 
investors who excelled in 2020.
  As Vice Chair of Capital Group, President of Capital Research and 
Management Company and a member of the firm's Management Committee, 
Jody helped shape the firm's Long-term Strategic Plan and was a trusted 
mentor to many of the firm's investors and client professionals. She 
has also been a steadfast advocate for active investing and for 
investors broadly, a message she brought to this House in June when she 
testified before the Financial Services Committee's Subcommittee on 
Capital Markets on the future of how America invests.
  Throughout her career, Jody paired investing prowess, sound judgment, 
and quiet humility with a deep commitment to Capital Group's mission of 
improving people's lives through successful investing and genuine care 
for her fellow associates. Colleagues regard her as having built one of 
the most respected careers in the firm's history. I ask the House to 
join me in honoring Jody Jonsson for her many years of dedication to 
America's investors and in extending to her and her family our very 
best wishes as she begins her retirement.

                          ____________________
```
