# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to cast my vote concerning the 
impeachment of Secretary of War Pete Hegseth. Had I been present, I 
would have voted in support of Secretary Hegseth and against these 
impeachment efforts.
  The Constitution grants Congress the authority to impeach civil 
officers for treason, bribery, or other high crimes and misdemeanors. 
Policy disagreements do not meet that constitutional threshold. 
Impeachment is a serious constitutional responsibility that should be 
exercised sparingly, not rushed through without hearings, public 
testimony, or due process.
  If House Democrats have demonstrated anything since last Tuesday, it 
is that they are more interested in pursuing impeachment of President 
Trump and members of his Cabinet than delivering meaningful results for 
the American people.
```
