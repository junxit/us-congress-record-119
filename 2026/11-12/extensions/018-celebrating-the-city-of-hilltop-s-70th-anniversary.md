# CELEBRATING THE CITY OF HILLTOP'S 70TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Omar, Ilhan (O000173) [D-MN]

```
           CELEBRATING THE CITY OF HILLTOP'S 70TH ANNIVERSARY

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. ILHAN OMAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the City of Hilltop, 
Minnesota, on the occasion of its 70th anniversary. Hilltop is a 
community that has shown the value of resilience, civic participation, 
and how the collective strength of neighbors can create a better 
future.
  Before its founding in 1956, Hilltop was home to many manufactured 
housing parks as the demand for affordable mobile homes increased by 
the end of WWII. To keep local control and develop their community 
during the fast-paced growth of the Twin-Cities, Hilltop grew from a 
small, manufactured housing community into one of Minnesota's unique 
municipalities.
  Enclaved in the larger City of Columbia Heights, Hilltop covers about 
1/10th of a square mile and is home to roughly 957 residents. The 
quaint town's motto, ``Little City with a Big Heart,'' truly 
encapsulates the essence of the city, as it demonstrates how its impact 
is measured not by population or size, but rather the strength of its 
residents. Hilltop has consistently remained dedicated to developing a 
city that connects people through events, local initiatives, and day-
to-day acts of being a good neighbor.
  Hilltop's steadfast leader embodies this motto every day. Mayor Terry 
Wiggin's passion for the city is all too apparent in his warm smile and 
his fierce advocacy for his constituents. I am proud to have worked in 
collaboration with Mayor Wiggin to secure $1,850,000 in Community 
Project Funding for Hilltop's new 6,000-foot Emergency Tornado Shelter.
  It is an honor to congratulate the City of Hilltop on its 70th 
anniversary. Hilltop has and will continue to cultivate a sense of 
belonging and community pride. After seven decades, the City of Hilltop 
remains a welcoming environment for its residents and visitors.
```
