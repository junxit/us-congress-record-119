# CELEBRATING THE KOREAN SERVICE CENTER'S 35TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Omar, Ilhan (O000173) [D-MN]

```
        CELEBRATING THE KOREAN SERVICE CENTER'S 35TH ANNIVERSARY

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. ILHAN OMAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to recognize the Korean Service 
Center (KSC), on the occasion of their 35th anniversary. The KSC has 
been instrumental in providing essential culturally responsive services 
to Korean and other immigrant-Americans since its founding in 1989.
  Located in the heart of Minneapolis' Cedar Riverside community--the 
same neighborhood I grew up in--the Korean Service Center was 
established when a Minneapolis Public Housing Authority (MPHA) intern 
identified a gap in Korean immigrants receiving essential social 
services. What initially began as an adjustment program for new Korean 
immigrants to learn English and take naturalization classes, has 
expanded over decades to provide a variety of holistic programs and 
support ranging from new parent education to caregiver consultations 
and support.
  Their presence in the community has built long-lasting trust and 
relationships with the very people they serve; and in 2019, after a 
horrific fire in the MPHA's Cedar High Apartment building, the Korean 
Service Center staff were immediately on the scene along with first 
responders to assist those impacted--that is a testament to their 
unwavering commitment to community.
  As an immigrant, I know how important cultural community 
organizations like the Korean Service Center are. These centers serve 
as the first point of contact for families who are seeking resources 
but may not know where to start. They provide community enrichment, 
social services and 24-hour customized living services both in seniors' 
own home and in their Grace Place facility--which I had the honor of 
visiting in 2023. During my visit, I saw first-hand the incredible 
services they provide to ensure that Korean immigrants can live 
independent and healthy lives without facing cultural or language 
barriers. This has earned them recognitions such as the 2016 Circle of 
Excellence Award from the Minnesota Department of Human Services.
  In Minnesota, we care for our communities, and it is evidenced in 
organizations like the Korean Service Center. Mr. Speaker, I ask that 
my colleagues join me in celebrating the KSC's 35th anniversary.
```
