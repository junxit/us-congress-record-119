# RECOGNIZING BROOKE MONTOYA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BROOKE MONTOYA

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Brooke Montoya 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Brooke has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Brooke, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Brooke's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Warren Tech North and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Brooke Montoya on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
