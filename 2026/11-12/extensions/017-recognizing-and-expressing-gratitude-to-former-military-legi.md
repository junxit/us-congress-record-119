# RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO FORMER MILITARY LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT ASHER THOMPSON

**Speaking:** Jackson, Ronny (J000304) [R-TX]

```
  RECOGNIZING AND EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO FORMER MILITARY LEGISLATIVE 
                        ASSISTANT ASHER THOMPSON

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. RONNY JACKSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. JACKSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
express my sincere gratitude to my former Military Legislative 
Assistant, Asher Thompson of Fishers, Indiana, for his exceptional 
service, steadfast dedication, and unwavering commitment to the people 
of Texas' 13th Congressional District. His depth of knowledge for 
national security policy, thoughtful judgment, and relentless work 
ethic made substantial contributions to my legislative efforts and 
ensured that my constituents received strong representation in 
Congress.
  Asher's passion for public service began long before he joined my 
office. He received his bachelor's degree in political science and 
government from Ohio Christian University, where he was a member of the 
baseball team and served as Student Body President. After college, 
Asher moved to Washington, D.C. and began working as a Government 
Relations Assistant with the Heritage Foundation, where he worked to 
advance foreign and defense policy. Later, Asher joined Heritage Action 
for America as a Grassroots Assistant, helping to increase the 
engagement of conservatives across the nation on key issues concerning 
energy, national security, election integrity, and immigration.
  In April 2025, Asher joined my office as Legislative Correspondent, 
where he supported my work on the House Armed Services Committee and 
the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence. He quickly 
distinguished himself through his thoroughness, proactiveness, and 
ability to convert ideas into deliverable work products under tight 
deadlines. In January of 2026, Asher was promoted to Military 
Legislative Assistant.
  As Military Legislative Assistant, Asher led my policy initiatives on 
the House Armed Services Committee, playing a critical role in helping 
secure major wins in the FY27 National Defense Authorization Act. Asher 
was essential in advancing legislative language that supports the 
communities and defense industrial base in my district, especially 
Sheppard Air Force Base, Bell Textron, and Pantex. Additionally, Asher 
was vital in helping to deliver key policy priorities for the Trump 
administration, including renaming the Department of Defense as the 
Department of War, establishing an America First Arms Transfer 
Strategy, and accelerating the development and deployment of next-
generation sUAS and C-UAS technologies for America's warfighters. With 
66 provisions included in the final House version of the bill, this 
achievement stands as a testament to his diligence, persistence, and 
deep understanding of the legislative process, which will help 
strengthen American national security interests around the world.
  Today, Asher continues his distinguished service as a Confidential 
Assistant to the Deputy Secretary of War. In this role, he will 
continue supporting our warfighters, strengthening our national 
defense, and advancing President Donald J. Trump's ``Peace Through 
Strength'' policy agenda.
  Although Asher has moved into a new role, his impact on my office and 
on the people of Texas' 13th Congressional District will be felt for 
years to come. On behalf of myself, my staff, and the constituents I 
proudly serve, I want to thank Asher Thompson for his loyal service, 
his commitment to excellence, and his dedication to the America First 
mission. I wish him nothing but success as he continues serving the 
American people.

                          ____________________
```
