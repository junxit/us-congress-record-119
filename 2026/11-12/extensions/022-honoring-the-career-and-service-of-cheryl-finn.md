# HONORING THE CAREER AND SERVICE OF CHERYL FINN

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
             HONORING THE CAREER AND SERVICE OF CHERYL FINN

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. JAMES P. McGOVERN

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker. I rise today in recognition of Cheryl Finn 
of Uxbridge, Massachusetts, for her remarkable career as a paramedic, 
educator, volunteer firefighter, and leader of global humanitarian 
relief after 46 years of extraordinary service.
  After earning her Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) license in 1979 
and her Paramedic Certificate in 1985, Cheryl quickly became a pioneer 
for women working in the Emergency Medical Services field. As one of 
the first women to work at the Uxbridge Fire Department, the Bay State 
Ambulance in Worcester, and the Auburn Fire Department ambulance, her 
strides as an EMT and paramedic blazed a trail for many other women who 
have followed.
  Cheryl has been a dedicated paramedic and mentor for decades. She 
served as the Services Director for the Milford Hospital Paramedic 
Service as well as the Program Coordinator and Chair of the 
Quinsigamond Community College Paramedic Program, a position she held 
for 25 years. During that entire time, she continued to work as a 
paramedic, treating thousands of patients while training and mentoring 
hundreds of EMTs and paramedics.
  Borders never got in the way of Cheryl's determination to help 
people. Guided by her deep faith, Cheryl has both participated in and 
led various trips conducting international medical work. She has 
provided humanitarian aid in Tanzania, Zanzibar, Colombia, Guatemala, 
the Dominican Republic, Haiti, Mexico, and other countries across the 
world. While she was not stationed abroad, Cheryl rallied members of 
her community to support families in other nations, including 
organizing a food drive for children in Haiti.
  The common thread throughout all the work Cheryl has done is her 
unyielding devotion to helping others. Over the course of her career, 
Cheryl helped save and improve countless lives all over the world. 
Whether it is volunteering as a firefighter in Central Massachusetts or 
spending six years working in Mexico with the Benedictines, Cheryl 
serves as a reminder of the good we can do in the world and sets a 
shining example for all of us to follow.
  All while doing this vital work, Cheryl and her late husband Charles 
also raised four children--Mary, Theresa, Sarah, and Andrew--who share 
in their mother's deep sense of faith and public service.
  On behalf of the United States Congress and the people of 
Massachusetts' 2nd Congressional District, it is my great honor and 
privilege to recognize Cheryl Finn and to thank her for her dedication, 
altruism, and service to our community.
```
