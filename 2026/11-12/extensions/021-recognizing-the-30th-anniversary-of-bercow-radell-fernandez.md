# RECOGNIZING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF BERCOW RADELL FERNANDEZ LARKIN & TAPANES, PLLC

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF BERCOW RADELL FERNANDEZ LARKIN & 
                             TAPANES, PLLC

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
30th anniversary of Bercow Radell Fernandez Larkin & Tapanes, PLLC, a 
distinguished law firm that has played a meaningful role in the growth 
and development of Miami-Dade County and South Florida.
  Founded in 1996 by Jeffrey Bercow and Michael Radell, the firm was 
established with a vision to provide thoughtful, solutions-oriented 
legal counsel in the highly complex field of land use and zoning law. 
Over the past three decades, the firm has grown into one of the 
region's leading practices, with its continued success guided by a 
strong leadership team that includes shareholders Ben Fernandez, 
Michael Larkin, and Melissa Tapanes Llahues.
  Through its work, Bercow Radell Fernandez Larkin & Tapanes has 
contributed to shaping the built environment of our community, often 
behind the scenes, supporting responsible growth, advancing economic 
development, and helping bring to fruition projects that strengthen our 
local economy and enhance the quality of life for residents throughout 
Miami-Dade County.
  With deep roots in our community, the firm has worked collaboratively 
with local governments, planners, and stakeholders to navigate complex 
regulatory processes and promote thoughtful, sustainable development 
across South Florida. Its contributions have helped position our region 
as a vibrant, resilient, and globally competitive community.
  In addition to its professional accomplishments, the firm and its 
attorneys have demonstrated a longstanding commitment to civic 
engagement and public service, reflecting the values that make Miami-
Dade County strong.
  As Bercow Radell Fernandez Larkin & Tapanes, PLLC celebrates its 30th 
anniversary, I am proud to recognize the firm's founders, shareholders, 
and entire team for their unwavering dedication to excellence, their 
lasting contributions to Miami-Dade County, and their continued 
commitment to serving our community. I congratulate the firm on this 
remarkable milestone and wish it continued success for many years to 
come.
```
