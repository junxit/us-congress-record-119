# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ED BOLEN

**Speaking:** Estes, Ron (E000298) [R-KS]

```
                HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ED BOLEN

                                  _____
                                 

                             HON. RON ESTES

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. ESTES. Mr, Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of Ed 
Bolen, who passed away earlier this week. Ed was a Kansas native, and 
to the fullest extent, he embodied the character and spirit of the 
Sunflower State throughout his life and career in aviation.
  Ed's story began in Salina, Kansas, where his father served on the 
board of the Salina Regional Airport. From an early age, aviation was 
an important part of Ed's family and life. After completing his 
undergraduate degree at the University of Kansas, as well as earning a 
law degree and a Master of Laws in taxation, Ed dove into aviation 
policy in the United States Senate.
  On Capitol Hill, he advised U.S. Senator Nancy Kassebaum (R-KS) on 
aviation policy. Ed was instrumental in negotiating the passage of one 
of the most important and comprehensive aviation bills of our 
generation, the General Aviation Revitalization Act of 1994. Ed's work 
on this legislation enabled Wichita aviation manufacturers to continue 
to innovate, grow, and lead. And this was just the beginning of his 
career in aviation.
  Ed also served as majority General Counsel to what is now the Senate 
Committee on Health, Education, Labor and Pensions. He went on to 
advocate for America's general aviation planemakers as President of the 
General Aviation Manufacturers Association (GAMA). In 2004, he 
transitioned to lead the National Business Aviation Association (NBAA) 
as President and CEO.
  For over 25 years, he was a steady, balanced voice for the business 
aviation sector. His stewardship shaped U.S. leadership in aviation and 
the Air Capital of the World. He led NBAA with humility, integrity, and 
quiet confidence, making him one of the most effective voices for 
aviation at every stage of his career. Just this week, Ed was inducted 
into the Kansas Aviation Hall of Fame.
  Kansans like Ed Bolen make the Sunflower State unlike any other. He 
was a devoted husband, father, friend, and advocate. Mr. Speaker, on 
behalf of the people of Kansas' 4th Congressional District, Susan and I 
join his family, friends, and the aviation community in sharing our 
condolences for this loss. As we celebrate Ed's life and legacy, his 
family, friends, and colleagues will remain in our prayers.
```
