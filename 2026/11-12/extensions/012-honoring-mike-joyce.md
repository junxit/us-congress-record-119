# HONORING MIKE JOYCE

**Speaking:** Lynch, Stephen F. (L000562) [D-MA]

```
                          HONORING MIKE JOYCE

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. STEPHEN F. LYNCH

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. LYNCH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mike Joyce and 
recognize his longstanding commitment to Boys & Girls Clubs of 
Dorchester. Boys & Girls Clubs of Dorchester have been a supportive and 
inclusive haven for youth in Dorchester for over 51 years.
  It is my honor to bring to the floor this evening the gratitude of 
thousands of families from my District and across the region who are 
grateful beyond words for Mike Joyce's service to Boys & Girls Clubs of 
Dorchester over the past 46 years.

[[Page E1206]]

  Mike Joyce began working for the Boys & Girls Clubs of Dorchester in 
1979, when he was just 14 years old. Throughout his time at the Clubs, 
Mike has been a dedicated mentor and leader, serving as Vice President 
of Programming, Program Director, Athletic Director, and now as the 
Senior Vice President of Operations.
  Mike has had a profound influence on numerous individuals within the 
community. He is viewed as a role model for the Club's youth, staff, 
and alumni. Through his passion for mentoring youth and active 
involvement in the community, Mike has cultivated a culture of 
positivity and excellence across the Club's three clubhouses and soon 
to be four.
  Often working behind the scenes and after hours, Mike Joyce 
consistently goes the extra mile to ensure no task is left unfinished. 
Mike leads by example and sets a standard that inspires everyone to 
work to their full potential. Through his consistent guidance and 
encouragement, Mike has left a lasting impression on the lives of 
thousands. His dedication has significantly impacted the character and 
success of the Club as a whole.
  Mr. Speaker, Mike Joyce, has made countless efforts to build more 
secure and caring environments for children. Through programs such as 
Safe Summer Streets, Mike has made Boston's Dorchester neighborhood a 
safer and more welcoming place for youth. Thanks to leaders like Mike 
Joyce, teens in Dorchester not only have a space to stay late into the 
evening, but also have access to reliable transportation, nutritious 
meals, and enriching activities.
  As a long-time member and leader of the Northeast New England Area 
Program Council of Boys & Girls Clubs of America, Mike has extensive 
experience in youth mentorship and programming. His work motivates 
others to be both compassionate and effective leaders for young 
individuals. Mike has been a key figure in fostering collaboration and 
partnerships among Boys & Girls Clubs from across the Nation, 
contributing to improved programs for at-risk youth.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mike Joyce and his 
years of service to Boys & Girls Clubs in Dorchester. It is my distinct 
privilege to acknowledge Mike's unwavering commitment to serving the 
youth and being an inspirational visionary for all. I thank Mike for 
preserving and honoring the legacy of Boys & Girls Clubs of Dorchester 
for generations to come.
```
