# RECOGNIZING SHERIFF JEFF LEIKAUF

**Speaking:** McClintock, Tom (M001177) [R-CA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING SHERIFF JEFF LEIKAUF

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. TOM McCLINTOCK

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2026

  Mr. McCLINTOCK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jeff Leikauf 
for his 36-year career with the El Dorado County Sheriff's Office as a 
Correctional Officer. Jeff began his career with the El Dorado County 
Sheriff's Office on October 20, 1990, as a correctional officer. Over 
the course of 36 years, he has served in numerous roles across the 
Custody and Operations Divisions, including School Resource Officer and 
General Investigations Detective. In 2012, he was promoted to Sergeant, 
serving in Patrol, the Special Enforcement Detail, Narcotics, and Court 
Services. In 2017, he was promoted to Lieutenant, overseeing the 
Support Services Unit before returning to the Operations Division.
  On January 3, 2023, Jeff was sworn in as the 33rd Sheriff of El 
Dorado County. Throughout his career, he has served in or overseen 
nearly every unit within the Sheriff's Office, including SWAT, the 
Honor Guard, the Gang Unit, Explosive Ordnance Disposal, and the 
Sheriff's Training and Explorers programs. His additional service as a 
Weaponless Defense Instructor, Training Officer, and member of the 
Community Emergency Response Team reflects his longstanding commitment 
to preparing and protecting his community.
  Beyond his professional responsibilities, Jeff has remained deeply 
involved in the El Dorado County community. He is a dedicated coach and 
advocate for youth sports and actively participates in several civic 
and community organizations focused on improving the quality of life 
for residents.
  Jeff and his wife, Jenny, reside in Cameron Park, where they have 
built a family together over 31 years of marriage and raised four 
children. As Sheriff, Jeff's goal remains to serve the people of El 
Dorado County and help ensure it remains a safe place to live, work, 
play, and raise a family.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Sheriff 
Jeff Leikauf for his 36 years of service, his commitment to public 
safety, and his dedication to the people of El Dorado County. His 
career reflects a longstanding commitment to his community, and I 
congratulate him on his service and leadership.
```
