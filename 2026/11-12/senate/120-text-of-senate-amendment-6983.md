# Text of Senate Amendment 6983

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 6983. Mr. HEINRICH (for Mr. Welch) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

        Beginning on page 135, strike line 19 and all that follows 
     through page 136, line 2, and insert the following:
       ``(B) Requirement.--Clauses (i) through (iv) of 
     subparagraph (A) shall only apply to a general permit being 
     reissued pursuant to that subparagraph if--
       ``(i) the general permit does not--

       ``(I) narrow or eliminate preconstruction requirements; or
       ``(II) raise the threshold for such preconstruction 
     requirements; or

       ``(ii) the Secretary has carried out an independent and 
     publicly available review of the general permit and 
     determined that the impacts to human health and the 
     environment are minimal.''.

```
