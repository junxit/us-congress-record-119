# Text of Senate Amendment 6943

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6943. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 17, line 20 of the amendment, insert ``, and 
     assistance described in subparagraph (D)'' after ``Code''.

       On page 23 of the amendment, between lines 13 and 14, 
     insert the following:
       ``(D) Anti-circumvention for immigration enforcement and 
     border agencies.--
       ``(i) Definition of covered agency.--In this subparagraph, 
     the term `covered agency' means--

       ``(I) U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement, or any 
     successor office or agency;
       ``(II) U.S. Customs and Border Protection, or any successor 
     office or agency; and
       ``(III) the Department of Homeland Security or any other 
     Federal agency, to the extent that the agency--

       ``(aa) acts on behalf of an agency described in subclause 
     (I) or (II); or
       ``(bb) provides funding or assistance for the benefit of 
     such an agency.
       ``(ii) Inclusion.--Notwithstanding subparagraph 
     (B)(i)(III), the term `major Federal action' includes a 
     proposed agency action of a covered agency to provide 
     funding, a grant, a loan, a loan guarantee, a contract, a 
     cooperative agreement, an intergovernmental agreement, a 
     lease, or any other form of Federal assistance or arrangement 
     to a State, Tribal, or local government or a private entity 
     for a project or undertaking if any of the following applies:

       ``(I) Congressionally directed funds.--The funds are 
     appropriated or otherwise made available by an Act of 
     Congress that specifically designates the funds for--

       ``(aa) that project or undertaking; or
       ``(bb) a specific category of facilities, operations, or 
     activities of a covered agency.

       ``(II) Substitute for direct agency action.--The project or 
     undertaking will carry out a function, mission, or activity 
     that a covered agency is authorized or required by law to 
     perform and would otherwise carry out directly, including 
     through a facility owned or operated by a covered agency.
       ``(III) Covered agency use or benefit.--The primary 
     intended use, occupant, or beneficiary of the completed 
     project or undertaking, or of its ongoing operation, is a 
     covered agency or a program administered by a covered agency.
       ``(IV) Covered agency direction.--A covered agency--

       ``(aa) selects, approves, or directs the location, design, 
     scope, capacity, or operating requirements of the project or 
     undertaking; or
       ``(bb) retains authority to approve, condition, or 
     terminate its operation.
       ``(iii) Prohibition on structuring to avoid review.--A 
     covered agency may not structure, divide, delegate, or 
     characterize a proposed agency action for the purpose or with 
     the effect of avoiding the requirements of this Act for an 
     action that would otherwise be a major Federal action, 
     including by transferring funds or responsibility to a non-
     Federal entity or contracting with a private entity.
       ``(iv) Renewals, extensions, and modifications.--This 
     subparagraph applies to a proposed agency action of a covered 
     agency to renew, extend, expand, or materially modify a 
     contract, agreement, or form of assistance described in 
     clause (ii), including an arrangement entered into before the 
     date of enactment of this subparagraph, if the renewal, 
     extension, expansion, or modification would meet any of the 
     conditions described in subclauses (I) through (IV) of that 
     clause.
       ``(v) Written determination.--

       ``(I) In general.--With respect to a proposed agency action 
     of a covered agency, the determination under subparagraph (A) 
     shall be consistent with this subparagraph.
       ``(II) Requirement.--A determination by a covered agency 
     under subparagraph (A) that a proposed action of a covered 
     agency is not a major Federal action described in clause (ii) 
     shall--

       ``(aa) be made in writing;
       ``(bb) state the basis for the determination with reference 
     to each of subclauses (I) through (IV) of clause (ii); and
       ``(cc) be made publicly available before the covered agency 
     takes the action.
       Beginning on page 71 of the amendment, strike line 20 and 
     all that follows through page 72, line 4, and insert the 
     following:
       ``(a) Definition of Claim.--
       ``(1) In general.--In this section, the term `claim' means 
     a claim that--
       ``(A) is filed on or after the date of enactment of the 
     Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026; and
       ``(B) seeks judicial review under any provision of Federal 
     law of an authorization based on a claim that an 
     environmental review failed to be properly conducted pursuant 
     to the requirements of this Act.
       ``(2) Exclusion.--In this section, the term `claim' does 
     not include a claim that seeks judicial review of a project, 
     action, or facility proposed, funded, operated, approved, 
     permitted, or contracted by or on behalf of U.S. Immigration 
     and Customs Enforcement or U.S. Customs and Border Protection 
     for immigration enforcement or detention purposes.
```
