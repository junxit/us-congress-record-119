# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious and loving God, we are utterly dependent upon You for 
everything. We could not breathe a breath, think a thought, or move a 
muscle without Your moment-by-moment provision. Every blessing we enjoy 
is a testimony to Your goodness, and every opportunity we receive is an 
expression of Your grace. Today, we echo the words of the psalmist: 
Bless the Lord, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless His Holy 
Name. Bless the Lord, O my soul and forget not all His benefits. 
Continue to guide our lawmakers. Keep their hearts grateful, even amid 
challenges, disappointments, and setbacks. May they cultivate hearts of 
thanksgiving and demonstrate through their words, attitudes, and 
actions a genuine appreciation for Your goodness.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
