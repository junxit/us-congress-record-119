# Text of Senate Amendment 6979

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 6979. Mr. HEINRICH (for Mr. Welch) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 176 of the amendment, strike line 1 and 
     all that follows through page 178, line 2, and insert the 
     following:

       (III) reasonable attorney fees and costs of litigation, as 
     the court determines appropriate; and
       (IV) any costs associated with the applicable delay in the 
     construction or operation of the covered project or action, 
     as the court determines appropriate.

       (ii) Savings clause.--Nothing in this subparagraph limits 
     or supersedes any existing equitable authority of a court to 
     grant any available form of temporary or injunctive relief 
     against the Federal department or agency.
       (iii) Motion for temporary or preliminary relief.--
     Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, on a 
     motion for temporary or preliminary relief, the reviewing 
     court shall not consider available cost recovery under 
     subclause (III) or (IV) of clause (i) in considering whether 
     harm to the holder of a Federal authorization or permit is 
     irreparable.
                                 ______
```
