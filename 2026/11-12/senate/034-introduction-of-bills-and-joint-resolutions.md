# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 5717, S. 5718

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. DUCKWORTH (for herself and Mr. Durbin):
       S. 5717. A bill to amend title 36, United States Code, to 
     move the place of incorporation and domicile of the National 
     Woman's Relief Corps to Illinois, to move the principal 
     office of such Corps to Murphysboro, Illinois, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mrs. FISCHER (for Ms. BLUNT ROCHESTER (for herself 
             and Mrs. Fischer)):
       S. 5718. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code, with 
     respect to restroom access for certain drivers, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.

                          ____________________
```
