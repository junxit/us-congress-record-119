# Text of Senate Amendment 6940

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6940. Mr. PADILLA (for Mr. Schiff) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

        Strike section 2251 of the amendment and insert the 
     following:

     SEC. 2251. OFFSHORE ENERGY.

       (a) Leases, Easements, and Rights-of-way.--Section 8 of the 
     Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1337) is 
     amended--
       (1) in subsection (m)--
       (A) by striking ``of this Act'';
       (B) by striking ``(m) All'' and inserting the following:
       ``(m) Disposition of Revenues.--
       ``(1) In general.--All''; and
       (C) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(2) No refund.--Except as otherwise provided in this Act, 
     the Secretary may not refund any amounts deposited in the 
     Treasury at any time under paragraph (1).''; and
       (2) in subsection (p)--
       (A) in paragraph (4)--
       (i) in subparagraph (H), by inserting ``, including any 
     decision of the Secretary regarding such a lease, easement, 
     or right-of-way'' after ``subsection'';
       (ii) in subparagraph (I), by striking ``prevention of 
     interference with reasonable uses'' and inserting 
     ``prevention of unreasonable interference with other uses''; 
     and
       (iii) in subparagraph (J)--

       (I) in clause (i), by striking ``and'' at the end;
       (II) in clause (ii), by adding ``and'' after the semicolon 
     at the end; and
       (III) by adding at the end the following:

       ``(iii) the interests of affected States in energy 
     produced, transported, stored, or transmitted as a result of 
     activities on the outer Continental Shelf;'';
       (B) by striking paragraph (7) and inserting the following:
       ``(7) Coordination and consultation with affected state and 
     local governments.--The Secretary shall provide for ongoing 
     coordination and consultation with the Governor of any State 
     or the executive of any local government that may be affected 
     by--
       ``(A) a lease, easement, or right-of-way under this 
     subsection; or
       ``(B) any decision of the Secretary regarding such a lease, 
     easement, or right-of-way.'';
       (C) by striking paragraph (10) and inserting the following:
       ``(10) Applicability.--
       ``(A) In general.--Except as provided in subparagraph (B), 
     this subsection does not apply to any area on the outer 
     Continental Shelf within the exterior boundaries of any unit 
     of the National Park System, the National Wildlife Refuge 
     System, the National Marine Sanctuary System, or any National 
     Monument.
       ``(B) Exception.--Notwithstanding subparagraph (A), the 
     Secretary, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce 
     under section 304(d) of the National Marine Sanctuaries Act 
     (16 U.S.C. 1434(d)), may grant rights-of-way on the outer 
     Continental Shelf within units of the National Marine 
     Sanctuary System for the transmission of electricity.''; and
       (D) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(11) Duration of permits in marine sanctuaries.--
     Notwithstanding section 310(c)(2) of the National Marine 
     Sanctuaries Act (16 U.S.C. 1441(c)(2)), any permit or 
     authorization granted under that Act that authorizes the 
     installation, operation, or maintenance of electric 
     transmission cables on a right-of-way granted by the 
     Secretary described in paragraph (10)(B) shall be issued for 
     a term equal to the duration of the right-of-way granted by 
     the Secretary.''.
       (b) Clarification of Remedies.--Section 23 of the Outer 
     Continental Shelf Lands Act ( 43 U.S.C. 1349) is amended--
       (1) in subsection (a), by striking paragraph (5) and 
     inserting the following:
       ``(5) Remedies.--
       ``(A) In general.--In issuing a final order in any action 
     brought pursuant to subsection (a)(1) or (c), a court may 
     grant such relief, including compensatory damages, 
     declaratory or injunctive relief, disgorgement of excess 
     payments, and restitution, as the court determines to be 
     necessary--
       ``(i) to compel compliance with this Act;
       ``(ii) to preserve the rights of affected persons;
       ``(iii) to restore affected persons to the status in 
     existence before the applicable violation; and
       ``(iv) to deter additional violations of this Act.
       ``(B) Costs.--A court described in subparagraph (A) may 
     award costs of litigation, including reasonable attorney and 
     expert witness fees, to any party, as the court determines to 
     be appropriate.
       ``(C) Bonds and securities.--In any case in which a 
     temporary restraining order or preliminary injunction is 
     sought in an action brought pursuant to subsection (a)(1) or 
     (c), a court may require the filing of a bond or equivalent 
     security in an amount sufficient to compensate for any loss 
     or damage suffered, in accordance with the Federal Rules of 
     Civil Procedure.''; and
       (2) in subsection (c)(1), by striking ``Appeal'' and 
     inserting ``Appeals''.
       (c) Clarification of Purpose.--Section 3(4) of the Outer 
     Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1332(4)) is amended by 
     striking subparagraph (C) and inserting the following:
       ``(C) such States, and affected units of local government 
     within those States, are entitled to an ongoing opportunity 
     to participate, to the extent consistent with the national 
     interest, in the policy, planning, and operational decisions 
     made by the Federal Government relating to--
       ``(i) exploration for, and development and production of, 
     minerals of the outer Continental Shelf; or
       ``(ii) production, transportation, storage, or transmission 
     of energy from activities on the outer Continental Shelf;''.
       (d) Administration of Leasing.--Section 5 of the Outer 
     Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1334) is amended--
       (1) in subsection (a)(2)--
       (A) in subparagraph (A), by striking the subparagraph 
     designation and all that follows through ``that--'' in the 
     matter preceding clause (i) and inserting the following:
       ``(A) that such a cancellation may occur at any time, if, 
     after a hearing at which all affected parties, including all 
     lessees and all affected States, have an opportunity to 
     participate, the Secretary determines that--''; and
       (B) in subparagraph (B), by striking ``lessee;'' and 
     inserting ``lessee, if appropriate; and'';
       (2) in subsection (h)--
       (A) by striking ``(h) The'' and inserting the following:
       ``(h) Notification.--
       ``(1) In general.--The''; and
       (B) in paragraph (1) (as so designated), by striking ``of 
     such action'' and all that follows through ``recommend'' and 
     inserting the following: ``of that action.
       ``(2) Action by secretary.--After receiving a notification 
     under paragraph (1), the Secretary--
       ``(A) shall notify, and continue to consult with, the 
     Governor of each affected State; and
       ``(B) may recommend''; and
       (3) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(k) Cancellations or Suspensions on Threat of Harm.--Any 
     cancellation or suspension ordered by the Secretary that is 
     predicated on a threat of serious irreparable or immediate 
     harm or damage, or on an imminent threat of serious or 
     irreparable harm or damage, requires a finding by the 
     Secretary of particularized harm that can only be feasibly 
     averted by suspension of on-lease activities.''.
       (e) Savings Clause.--Nothing in this section or an 
     amendment made by this section modifies the limitations 
     described in section 50265(b)(2) of Public Law 117-169 (43 
     U.S.C. 3006(b)(2)).
                                 ______
```
