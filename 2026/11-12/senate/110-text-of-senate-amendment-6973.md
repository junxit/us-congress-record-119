# Text of Senate Amendment 6973

**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL]

```
  SA 6973. Mr. SCOTT of Florida (for himself and Mrs. Moody) submitted 
an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by 
Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and 
Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, other than 
punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or sexual 
contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        In section 1204, add at the end the following:
       (c) Compliance With Biological Opinion and Reinstatement of 
     Approval of Florida State Dredged and Fill Permitting 
     Program.--

[[Page S5653]]

       (1) Definitions.--In this subsection:
       (A) Agency action.--
       (i) In general.--The term ``Agency Action'' means the 
     approval by the Administrator of the Environmental Protection 
     Agency of the request of the State of Florida for the 
     assumption by the State of Florida of the administration and 
     permitting of a State permit program under section 404 of the 
     Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1344), as 
     described in the notice of the Environmental Protection 
     Agency entitled ``EPA's Approval of Florida's Clean Water Act 
     Section 404 Assumption Request'' (85 Fed. Reg. 83553 
     (December 22, 2020)).
       (ii) Inclusion.--The term ``Agency Action'' includes other 
     activities.
       (B) Biological opinion.--
       (i) In general.--The term ``Biological Opinion'' means the 
     programmatic biological opinion issued by the United States 
     Fish and Wildlife Service entitled ``Programmatic Biological 
     Opinion for Environmental Protection Agency's Approval of 
     FDEP's Assumption of the Administration of the Dredge and 
     Fill Permitting Program under Section 404 of the Clean Water 
     Act'' (FWS Log Nos. 04E00000-2021-F-0001 and 04E00000-2021-B-
     0001) and dated November 17, 2020.
       (ii) Inclusion.--The term ``Biological Opinion'' includes 
     the incidental take statement described on pages 69 through 
     73 of the programmatic biological opinion described in clause 
     (i).
       (C) Other activities.--The term ``other activities'' has 
     the meaning given the term in the glossary of the Biological 
     Opinion.
       (2) Compliance with biological opinion.--For purposes of 
     the Agency Action--
       (A) the Biological Opinion is deemed to be compliant with, 
     and compliance with the Biological Opinion is deemed to be 
     compliance with, the requirements of the Endangered Species 
     Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq.); and
       (B) no additional consultation with the United States Fish 
     and Wildlife Service or the National Marine Fisheries Service 
     under section 7(a) of that Act (16 U.S.C. 1536(a)) is 
     required.
       (3) Reinstatement of agency action.--Notwithstanding any 
     other provision of law or any court order vacating or setting 
     aside the Agency Action, the Agency Action is reinstated and 
     shall have full force and effect beginning on the date of 
     enactment of this Act.
```
