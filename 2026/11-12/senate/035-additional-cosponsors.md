# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 1335, S. 1532, H.R. 2347, S. 3525, S. 3940, S. 3958, S. 4134, S. 4545, S. 4784, S. 5463, S. 5547, S. 5552

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1335

  At the request of Mr. Tillis, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1335, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude debt held by certain 
insurance companies from capital assets and to extend capital loss 
carryovers for such companies from 5 years to 10 years.


                                S. 1532

  At the request of Mr. Crapo, the name of the Senator from Illinois 
(Ms. Duckworth) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1532, a bill to amend 
the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to modify the railroad track 
maintenance credit.


                                S. 3525

  At the request of Mr. Marshall, the name of the Senator from Utah 
(Mr. Curtis) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3525, a bill to preserve 
the franchise business model.


                                S. 3940

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the name of the Senator from Hawaii 
(Mr. Schatz) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3940, a bill to amend the 
Community Development Banking and Financial Institutions Act of 1994 to 
require the Secretary of the Treasury to testify before Congress, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 3958

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the names of the Senator from 
Massachusetts (Ms. Warren), the Senator from Hawaii (Ms. Hirono) and 
the Senator from Illinois (Ms. Duckworth) were added as cosponsors of 
S. 3958, a bill to amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to 
transition Puerto Rico to the supplemental nutrition assistance 
program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4134

  At the request of Mr. Ricketts, the name of the Senator from Missouri 
(Mr. Schmitt) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4134, a bill to amend 
chapter 131 of title 5, United States Code, to require certain 
restrictions on stocks for Members of Congress and their spouses and 
dependents, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4545

  At the request of Mr. Schatz, the names of the Senator from New 
Jersey (Mr. Kim) and the Senator from Illinois (Ms. Duckworth) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 4545, a bill to amend the America COMPETES 
Act to establish certain scientific integrity policies for Federal 
agencies that fund, conduct, or oversee scientific research, and for 
other purposes.


                                S. 5463

  At the request of Mr. Wicker, the name of the Senator from Montana 
(Mr. Sheehy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5463, a bill to amend title 
36, United States Code, to request the President to designate a month 
each year as National American History and Founders Month.


                                S. 5547

  At the request of Mr. Scott of Florida, the name of the Senator from 
Florida (Mrs. Moody) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5547, a bill to add 
the Department of State and the Department of Homeland Security to the 
list of permitted disclosures for the National Center for Missing and 
Exploited Children, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5552

  At the request of Mr. Scott of South Carolina, the names of the 
Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt) and the Senator from Illinois (Mr. 
Durbin) were added as cosponsors of S. 5552, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide a tax credit for American film 
and television productions, and for other purposes.


                           AMENDMENT NO. 6591

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of amendment No. 6591 
intended to be proposed to S. 4784, an original bill to authorize 
appropriations for fiscal year 2027 for military activities of the 
Department of Defense, for military construction, and for defense 
activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.


                           AMENDMENT NO. 6854

  At the request of Ms. Murkowski, the names of the Senator from 
Arizona (Mr. Gallego), the Senator from Washington (Ms. Cantwell) and 
the Senator from New Mexico (Mr. Lujan) were added as cosponsors of 
amendment No. 6854 intended to be proposed to H.R. 2347, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact.


                           AMENDMENT NO. 6865

  At the request of Mr. Merkley, the names of the Senator from 
Washington (Ms. Cantwell) and the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Wyden) were 
added as cosponsors of amendment No. 6865 intended to be proposed to 
H.R. 2347, a bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact.


                           AMENDMENT NO. 6871

  At the request of Mr. Merkley, the names of the Senator from 
Washington (Ms. Cantwell) and the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Wyden) were 
added as cosponsors of amendment No. 6871 intended to be proposed to 
H.R. 2347, a bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on

                           AMENDMENT NO. 6882
```
