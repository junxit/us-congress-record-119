# Text of Senate Amendment 6949

**Speaking:** Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

```
  SA 6949. Mr. BENNET submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       On page 376, strike lines 3 through 14 and insert the 
     following:
       ``(2) Authorization.--Notwithstanding subsections (a) and 
     (b), where public land is needed by the proprietor of a lode 
     or placer claim for operations in connection with any lode or 
     placer claim within the proposed plan of operations, the 
     proprietor may locate and include within the plan of 
     operations as many valid mill site claims under this 
     subsection as are reasonably necessary for its operations.
                                 ______
```
