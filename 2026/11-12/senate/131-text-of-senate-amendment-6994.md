# Text of Senate Amendment 6994

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 6994. Ms. CANTWELL (for herself, Mr. Wyden, and Mrs. Murray) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        Beginning on page 145 of the amendment, strike line 21 and 
     all that follows through page 146, line 16, and insert the 
     following:

[[Page S5657]]

       ``(2) Limitations on assumptions.--The Secretary may not 
     assign the consultation responsibilities of the Secretary 
     described in paragraph (4)(A) to any State for a project or 
     agency action (as referred to in section 7) that--
       ``(A) exceeds the boundaries of the State;
       ``(B) may affect a species--
       ``(i) the range of which extends beyond the boundaries of 
     the State, including a species that occurs in, or migrates 
     through, Federal waters; or
       ``(ii) for which the Secretary shares responsibility under 
     this Act with another head of another Federal agency; or
       ``(C) affects in any way Indian country (as defined in 
     section 1151 of title 18, United States Code) or the treaty 
     or Trust-protected resources of an Indian Tribe (as defined 
     in section 4 of the Indian Self-Determination and Education 
     Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 5304)).
                                 ______
```
