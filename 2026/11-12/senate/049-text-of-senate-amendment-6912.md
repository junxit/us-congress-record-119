# Text of Senate Amendment 6912

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6912. Mr. KIM submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       On page 198, between lines 18 and 19, insert the following:
       (d) Disclosures.--Notwithstanding any other provision of 
     law, a developer of, or applicant for a permit for, a 
     specific type of covered project shall not be eligible to 
     receive from the Federal Government, in an action under 
     subsection (c) or otherwise, any damages relating to a 
     Federal permitting decision relating to that project unless 
     the developer or applicant first discloses to the public and, 
     if applicable, to the court--
       (1) the ultimate beneficial owners of the developer or 
     applicant;
       (2) all financial stakeholders in the developer or 
     applicant;
       (3) all third-party payment arrangements relating to, 
     contingent on, or that may be affected by the outcome of the 
     permitting decision or any action relating to the permitting 
     decision, including the payment or receipt of such damages; 
     and
       (4) any known financial interests involving Federal 
     officials connected to the permitting decision or any action 
     (including any settlement) relating to the permitting 
     decision in--
       (A) the developer or applicant; or
       (B) the outcome of the permitting decision or any action 
     (including any settlement) relating to the permitting 
     decision.
                                 ______
```
