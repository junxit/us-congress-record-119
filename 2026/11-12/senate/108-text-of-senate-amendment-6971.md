# Text of Senate Amendment 6971

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 6971. Mr. HEINRICH (for Mr. Wyden (for himself, Mr. Merkley, and 
Ms. Cantwell)) submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       At the end of title II of division B, add the following:

Subtitle H--Prohibition of Construction of Data Centers on Federal Land

     SEC. 2271. PROHIBITION OF CONSTRUCTION OF DATA CENTERS ON 
                   FEDERAL LAND.

       (a) Definitions.--In this section:
       (1) Data center.--The term ``data center'' has the meaning 
     given the term in section 453(a) of the Energy Independence 
     and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 17112(a)).
       (2) Federal land.--
       (A) In general.--The term ``Federal land'' means--
       (i) National Forest System land; and
       (ii) land managed by the Secretary of the Interior.
       (B) Exclusions.--The term ``Federal land'' does not 
     include--
       (i) any land located within the boundaries of an Indian 
     reservation, pueblo, or rancheria; or
       (ii) any land not located within the boundaries of an 
     Indian reservation, pueblo, or rancheria, the title to which 
     is held--

       (I) in trust by the United States for the benefit of an 
     Indian Tribe or an individual Indian;
       (II) by an Indian Tribe or an individual Indian, subject to 
     restriction against alienation under laws of the United 
     States; or
       (III) by a dependent Indian community.

       (3) Indian tribe.--The term ``Indian Tribe'' has the 
     meaning given the term in section 4 of the Indian Self-
     Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 5304).
       (4) National forest system.--The term ``National Forest 
     System'' has the meaning given the term in section 11(a) of 
     the Forest and Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning Act of 
     1974 (16 U.S.C. 1609(a)).
       (b) Prohibition on Construction of Data Centers.--
     Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the construction 
     of data centers on Federal land is prohibited.
       (c) Reversionary Clause Required for Federal Land 
     Conveyances.--Any conveyance of Federal land occurring on or 
     after the date of enactment of this Act shall include a 
     reversionary clause to ensure that the conveyed Federal land 
     shall revert to the United States if the conveyed Federal 
     land is used for a data center, unless the use of the 
     conveyed Federal land for a data center is specifically 
     authorized by an Act of Congress.
```
