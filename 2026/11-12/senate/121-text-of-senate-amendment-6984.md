# Text of Senate Amendment 6984

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS]

```
  SA 6984. Mr. MORAN (for himself, Mr. Grassley, and Mr. Schmitt) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 242, strike line 2 and insert the following: the 
     siting of the applicable facility.
       ``(6) Land restoration plans.--
       ``(A) In general.--To preserve, mitigate, and restore 
     affected property, including farmland, ranchland, and Tribal 
     land, under this section, the Commission shall require an 
     applicant for a permit under subsection (b)--

[[Page S5656]]

       ``(i) to file a land restoration plan with the Commission 
     and provide a copy to each affected landowner;
       ``(ii) to conduct onsite inspections to determine 
     compliance with the land restoration plan; and
       ``(iii) to comply with all requirements of the land 
     restoration plan as approved by the Commission.
       ``(B) Corrective action.--If the holder of a permit issued 
     under subsection (b) fails to comply with the requirements of 
     this paragraph, the Commission shall issue an order requiring 
     corrective action, the costs of which shall be borne by the 
     permit holder.
       ``(C) Savings provision.--Nothing in this paragraph 
     precludes measures or requirements for the protection, 
     mitigation, or restoration of affected property that differ 
     from those prescribed in this paragraph.''.
```
