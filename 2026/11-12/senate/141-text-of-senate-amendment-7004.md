# Text of Senate Amendment 7004

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 7004. Mr. HEINRICH (for Ms. Smith) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 169 of the amendment, strike line 22 and 
     all that follows through page 172, line 2, and insert the 
     following:
       (1) Covered project or action.--
       (A) In general.--The term ``covered project or action'' 
     means a project or action with a non-Federal project sponsor 
     that has secured all necessary Federal authorizations or 
     permits for construction or operation.
       (B) Exclusion.--The term ``covered project or action'' does 
     not include initial authorizations or rights to develop where 
     additional approvals, authorizations, or permits are required 
     for construction or operation.
       (2) Federal authorization or permit.--The term ``Federal 
     authorization or permit'' means an authorization, permit, 
     record of decision, right-of-way, finding of no significant 
     impact, verification, extension, construction and operations 
     plan, biological opinion, incidental take statement, 
     concurrence, variance, departure, determination, or any other 
     similar approval or order that is necessary, including any 
     determination by a Federal department or agency that 
     additional authorizations or permits are not necessary, for 
     the construction or operation at normal capacity of a 
     project.
       (b) Maintenance of Authorizations and Permits.--
       (1) In general.--For covered projects or actions, a Federal 
     department or agency shall not take any new action that would 
     revoke, rescind, withdraw, terminate, suspend, vacate, 
     remand, reopen, materially amend, or materially alter a 
     Federal authorization or permit in effect on or after 
     November 6, 2026, or take any other action to interfere with 
     or prevent the construction or operation at normal capacity 
     of a project that has secured all necessary Federal 
     authorizations and permits, unless--
       (A) the Federal department or agency takes such action in 
     response to an order by a court of competent jurisdiction;
       (B) the holder of the Federal authorization or permit has 
     materially breached the terms of the Federal authorization or 
     permit or otherwise materially violated applicable law;
       (C) the Federal authorization or permit was obtained 
     through fraud, intentional concealment, or material 
     misrepresentation;
       (D) such action is necessary to prevent immediate, 
     specific, urgent, substantial, and proximate harm or damage 
     to life, property, human health, the environment, national 
     security, or defense that is based on new information that 
     was not considered in the underlying environmental review 
     process or final agency action for the Federal authorization 
     or permit;

[[Page S5666]]

       (E) the Federal department or agency has received a request 
     from the holder of the Federal authorization or permit for 
     the covered project or action to take such action;
       (F) such action is taken pursuant to any applicable law or 
     regulation; or
       (G) the Federal authorization or permit was issued in 
     violation of, in conflict with, ultra vires of, or in ways 
     not permitted by, Federal law or regulations, as determined 
     by the issuing Federal department or agency.
```
