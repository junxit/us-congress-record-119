# Text of Senate Amendment 7008

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 7008. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 141 of the amendment, strike line 10 and 
     all that follows through page 142, line 2, and insert the 
     following:
       ``(2)(A) The terms `best scientific and commercial data 
     available' and `best scientific data available' mean 
     scientific information, including traditional ecological 
     knowledge, that is available to the Secretary and is 
     accurate, reliable, and relevant to the matter under 
     consideration, including information derived from peer-
     reviewed research, agency studies and monitoring, 
     scientifically credible data and analyses, expert judgment, 
     and other scientifically valid sources.
       ``(B) In determining whether scientific information is 
     accurate, reliable, and relevant under subparagraph (A), the 
     Secretary shall consider--
       ``(i) the extent to which the information accurately 
     estimates, identifies, or describes the condition, process, 
     or relationship at issue;
       ``(ii) the appropriateness and transparency of the 
     scientific methods, data, assumptions, and analyses used;
       ``(iii) the consistency of the information with established 
     scientific principles and the extent to which uncertainty, 
     limitations, and conflicting evidence are disclosed;
       ``(iv) the relevance of the information to the ecological, 
     geographic, spatial, and temporal scales of the decision; and
       ``(v) the degree to which the information has been 
     independently reviewed, tested, corroborated, or otherwise 
     subjected to scientific scrutiny.''; and
       Beginning on page 144 of the amendment, strike line 21 and 
     all that follows through page 161, line 22.
       On page 164, line 3 of the amendment, strike ``2031--'' and 
     all that follows through ``Act.'' on line 7 and insert ``2031 
     to carry out section 7 of the Endangered Species Act of 1973 
     (16 U.S.C. 1536).''.

                          ____________________
```
