# RECOGNIZING THE 2026 STARS OF MISSISSIPPI AUTOMOTIVE MANUFACTURING

**Speaking:** Wicker, Roger F. (W000437) [R-MS]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE 2026 STARS OF MISSISSIPPI AUTOMOTIVE MANUFACTURING

● Mr. WICKER. Madam President, the Mississippi Automotive 
Manufacturing Association has recently named its 2026 Stars of 
Mississippi Automotive Manufacturing, and I wish to recognize the 
honorees in the Congressional Record today.
  The award recognizes exceptional frontline employees. They must 
exemplify professionalism and be dedicated to continued improvements. 
Recipients are known for their leadership skills and technical 
expertise, setting a high bar of excellence across Mississippi's 
automotive manufacturing industry.
  For meeting these requirements and for inspiring others to do the 
same, the Mississippi Automotive Manufacturers Association honored the 
following individuals:

       Taner Douell, Chris Magee, Robin Ryan, and Nicole Tyler of 
     Nissan North America in Canton; Grant Feathers of CITE 
     Armored, Inc. in Holly Springs; Stephen Brown, Kiana Gayden, 
     LaMorris Reynolds, and Daniel Rios of Continental Tire the 
     Americas in Clinton; Thomas Hilderbrant, Millie Smith, and 
     Jeff Sparks of Toyota Motor Manufacturing, Mississippi in 
     Blue Springs; Felesha Neal, Brad Richardson, and Blake 
     Swafford of PACCAR Engine Company in Columbus; and Jerry 
     Smith and Levi Sumrall of Hol-Mac Corporation, Pac Mac in Bay 
     Springs.

  These men and women exemplify the skill, integrity, and dedication 
that define Mississippi's automotive manufacturing workforce. Their 
efforts strengthen our State's leadership in the Southern automotive 
industry, and they uphold the highest standards of excellence and 
innovation. It is thanks to exemplary individuals like these that 
automotive companies continue investing in Mississippi.●
```
