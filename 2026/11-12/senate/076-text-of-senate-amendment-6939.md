# Text of Senate Amendment 6939

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6939. Mr. PADILLA (for Mr. Schiff) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

       At the end of section 1402, add the following:
       (d) Applicability.--This section does not apply to any 
     specific type of covered project

[[Page S5618]]

     for which the Federal Government has been found by a Federal 
     court to have violated applicable law when the Federal 
     Government provided a Federal authorization or permit for the 
     specific type of covered project.
                                 ______
```
