# Text of Senate Amendment 6932

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6932. Mr. BOOKER (for himself and Ms. Alsobrooks) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. 
Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. 
Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, other than punitive 
damages, received on account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 181 of the amendment, between lines 4 and 5, insert 
     the following:
       (5) Exclusion.--Paragraph (1) does not apply to a Federal 
     authorization or permit that was issued--
       (A) in violation of applicable Federal law in effect at the 
     time of issuance; or
       (B) under emergency procedures.
                                 ______
```
