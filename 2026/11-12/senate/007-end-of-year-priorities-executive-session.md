# End-of-Year Priorities (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         End-of-Year Priorities

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, with the 2026 midterm elections behind 
us, Congress must finish its work for this Congress. On my agenda for 
Iowans in the coming weeks, first of all, confirming President Trump's 
remaining judicial nominees to lifetime appointments on the Federal 
bench, and that is a responsibility that falls on my shoulders as 
chairman of the Judiciary Committee.
  And then obviously, we know it costs money to shut the government 
down. It costs taxpayers money to open the government up. So keeping 
the government open beyond the short-term funding bill that expires on 
December 11 ought to be high on the agenda.
  And then passing the National Defense Authorization Act which we have 
done for 65 years in a row, and that legislation includes stuff out of 
my Judiciary Committee that protects kids who are in danger of the 
things that are online.
  And then enacting a farm bill that contains year-round, nationwide 
E15, ethanol, and addresses high input costs that farmers face.
  And then passing commonsense permitting reform to unlock the American 
infrastructure and reduce government redtape, and that permitting bill 
is the subject of debate right now on the floor of the U.S. Senate.
  Then lastly, something that I have been involved in for decades--to 
emphasize the responsibilities that we have to maintain rural 
healthcare. That would include reauthorizing the Medicare-Dependent and 
Low-Volume Hospital Programs, the Rural Community Hospital 
Demonstration Program, and a Medicare payment floor for rural state 
doctors and clinics.
  As we look to the 120th Congress, I personally congratulated the new 
Members of the Iowa Federal delegation on their elections. I look 
forward to working with all of those new Members on behalf of the 
people of Iowa. The next Congress has a lot to accomplish, and I am 
ready to work with these new Members and the Members of both parties to 
get things done.
  Affordability was front and center in this election and must be the 
bipartisan focus next year. Any serious affordability agenda will need 
to focus on fiscal sanity and reduce waste, fraud, and abuse in Federal 
programs. Our national debt now stands at levels we have not seen since 
the end of World War II while Social Security is projected to become 
insolvent in just 6 years and Medicare shortly thereafter.
  We must find a bipartisan solution to a very real fiscal crisis that 
has been staring Congress in the face for too long of a time.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
