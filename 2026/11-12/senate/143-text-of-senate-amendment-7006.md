# Text of Senate Amendment 7006

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS]

```
  SA 7006. Mr. MORAN (for himself, Mr. Grassley, and Mr. Schmitt) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 242, strike line 2 and insert the following:
     the siting of the applicable facility.
       ``(6) Land restoration.--
       ``(A) In general.--In acting on applications for permits 
     issued under subsection (b), the Commission shall apply 
     practices and procedures related to the preservation and 
     restoration of affected property, including farmland, 
     ranchland, and Tribal land, and mitigation of impacts to such 
     property, that are no less protective than the practices and 
     procedures that the Commission applies when reviewing 
     applications under section 7 of the Natural Gas Act (15 
     U.S.C. 717f).
       ``(B) Corrective action.--If the holder of a permit issued 
     under subsection (b) fails to comply with any requirement or 
     condition imposed by the Commission that is related to the 
     preservation or restoration of, or mitigation of impacts to, 
     affected property, the Commission may issue an order 
     requiring corrective action, the costs of which shall be 
     borne by the permit holder.
       ``(C) Savings provision.--Nothing in this paragraph 
     precludes measures or requirements for the protection, 
     mitigation, or restoration of affected property that differ 
     from those prescribed in this paragraph.''.
```
