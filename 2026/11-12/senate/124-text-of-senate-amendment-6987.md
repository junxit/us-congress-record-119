# Text of Senate Amendment 6987

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 6987. Ms. CANTWELL (for herself, Mr. Wyden, and Mrs. Murray) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place in title II of division A, insert 
     the following:

     SEC. 12__. RULE OF CONSTRUCTION FOR INDIAN TRIBES.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of law, nothing in this 
     title or an amendment made by this title affects the 
     authority of a Tribal government to impose and implement 
     approved water quality standards with respect to waters on a 
     reservation under the jurisdiction of that Tribal government.
                                 ______
```
