# Text of Senate Amendment 6915

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6915. Mr. KIM (for himself, Mr. Booker, and Ms. Alsobrooks) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        On page 17 of the amendment, between lines 4 and 5, insert 
     the following:
       ``(B) Inclusion.--The term `major Federal action' includes 
     any proposed agency action that involves retrofitting a 
     facility or building a facility for detention purposes.
       On page 17, line 5 of the amendment, strike ``(B)'' and 
     insert ``(C)''.
       On page 23, line 4 of the amendment, strike ``(C)'' and 
     insert ``(D)''.
       On page 23, line 6 of the amendment, strike ``(B)'' and 
     insert ``(C)''.
       Beginning on page 71 of the amendment, strike line 20 and 
     all that follows through page 72, line 4, and insert the 
     following:
       ``(a) Definition of Claim.--
       ``(1) In general.--In this section, the term `claim' means 
     a claim that--
       ``(A) is filed on or after the date of enactment of the 
     Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026; and
       ``(B) seeks judicial review under any provision of Federal 
     law of an authorization based on a claim that an 
     environmental review failed to be properly conducted pursuant 
     to the requirements of this Act.
       ``(2) Exclusion.--In this section, the term `claim' does 
     not include a claim that seeks review of a proposed agency 
     action that involves retrofitting a facility or building a 
     facility to be used by the Department of Homeland Security 
     for immigration detention or immigration processing purposes.
                                 ______
```
