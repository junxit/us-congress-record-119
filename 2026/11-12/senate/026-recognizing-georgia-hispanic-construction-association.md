# RECOGNIZING GEORGIA HISPANIC CONSTRUCTION ASSOCIATION

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

         RECOGNIZING GEORGIA HISPANIC CONSTRUCTION ASSOCIATION

● Mr. OSSOFF. Madam President, I commend and congratulate the 
Georgia Hispanic Construction Association GHCA, on their 15 years of 
service to the Hispanic business and construction community in the 
State of Georgia.
  This Saturday, November 14, the GHCA will hold its annal gala and 
kick off its 15-year anniversary celebration, and I join executive 
director Gabriela Matute, the board of directors, staff, and supporters 
in celebrating this exciting anniversary.
  Established in 2012, the GHCA has worked to promote a vibrant 
construction industry in which Georgia's Hispanic business are 
respected, empowered, and have access to opportunities to thrive.
  Their work aims to provide educational and business development 
resources to construction firms in Georgia, supporting Hispanic 
entrepreneurs, creating jobs, and fostering the success of the Hispanic 
construction community.
  As Georgia's senior U.S. Senator, I congratulate and commend the 
Georgia Hispanic Construction Association for their 15 years of devoted 
service to the State of Georgia.●
```
