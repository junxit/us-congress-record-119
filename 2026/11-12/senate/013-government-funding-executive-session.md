# Government Funding (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Tillis, Thomas (T000476) [R-NC], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                           Government Funding

  Next, Mr. President, with regard to government funding, Republicans 
are committed to keeping this government open. Government funding runs 
out in less than a month. It is the responsibility of Congress to fund 
the government. Republicans want to keep the government open, pay our 
troops, and protect essential services.
  There are now some Democrats who are threatening to once again shut 
down the government. They intend to do it right before Christmas. The 
last thing this country needs is another painful, chaotic, Democrat-
caused government shutdown. For families who live paycheck to paycheck, 
including military families, a government shutdown means more strain, 
more stress, and more suffering by the American people. This has to 
end.
  I have heard Senator Schumer come to this floor and talk endlessly 
about chaos. Nothing would be more chaotic than a decision by Schumer 
and the Democrats to once again shut down the government.
  Mr. President, the American people sent a clear message last Tuesday. 
Republicans are listening. We are committed to regaining their trust, 
and we will do it by delivering results.
  The Senate has important work to do in these last 52 days. 
Republicans are going to stand strong, united, and energized on behalf 
of the American people.
  Thank you.
  I yield the floor.
  Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. TILLIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Hagerty). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
