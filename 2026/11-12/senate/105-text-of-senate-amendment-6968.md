# Text of Senate Amendment 6968

**Speaking:** Markey, Edward J. (M000133) [D-MA]

```
  SA 6968. Mr. MARKEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

        On page 345, between lines 2 and 3, insert the following:
       (3) Interagency consultation.--In developing the Data 
     Center Community Toolkit under paragraph (1), the Secretary 
     shall consult relevant Federal agencies in selecting and 
     verifying information and resources included.
       (4) Public comment.--In developing the Data Center 
     Community Toolkit under paragraph (1), the Secretary shall 
     solicit comments from the public to guide the types of 
     resources and information that should be included.
                                 ______
```
