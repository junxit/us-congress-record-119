# Text of Senate Amendment 6967

**Speaking:** Markey, Edward J. (M000133) [D-MA]

```
  SA 6967. Mr. MARKEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

        At the end of section 2117, add the following:
       (g) Environmental Impacts.--
       (1) Definitions.--In this subsection:
       (A) Administrator.--The term ``Administrator'' means the 
     Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.
       (B) Data center.--The term ``data center'' means any 
     facility, or group of facilities with the same owner in the 
     same utility area that--
       (i) primarily contains electronic equipment used to host 
     information and information systems accessed by other systems 
     or by users on other devices both in and outside of the State 
     in which the facility or group of facilities is located;
       (ii) is a free-standing structure or a facility within a 
     larger structure that uses environmental control equipment to 
     maintain the proper conditions for the operation of 
     electronic equipment;
       (iii) has an energy demand greater than 50 megawatts; and
       (iv) meets such other criteria as the Federal 
     Communications Commission determines to be appropriate for 
     purposes of this section, including anticircumvention 
     provisions.
       (C) Data center corporation.--The term ``data center 
     corporation'' means any corporation, joint-stock company, 
     partnership, association, business trust, or organized group 
     of persons, whether incorporated or not, or a receiver or 
     trustee of such, that owns or proposes to own a data center.
       (2) Certificate requirements for data centers.--
       (A) Definition.--In this paragraph, the term ``Commission'' 
     means the Federal Communications Commission.
       (B) Certificate required.--
       (i) In general.--Before proposed construction activities 
     may begin with respect to a data center, the data center 
     corporation with respect to the data center shall be required 
     to obtain from the Commission a certificate certifying that 
     the construction of the data center will not harm the public 
     interest.
       (ii) Evaluation of application.--

       (I) In general.--With respect to an application for a 
     certificate described in clause (i), the Commission shall 
     evaluate the potential impacts of the applicable data center 
     on--

       (aa) air quality, including local, upstream, and downstream 
     emissions of air pollutants;
       (bb) water quality, including toxic and priority pollutant 
     concentration levels, temperature, turbidity, and nutrient 
     levels;
       (cc) the local and watershed-level availability and cost of 
     water, including the impacts on wastewater services;
       (dd) the average levels of noise and light within 2 miles 
     of the proposed site of the data center;
       (ee) energy costs and electricity system reliability, 
     particularly for residential and commercial ratepayers within 
     the same regional electricity market as the data center, as 
     determined by the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission;
       (ff) the local ecosystem and wildlife;
       (gg) the local economy, including through the offering of 
     public subsidies and tax exemptions;
       (hh) community well-being, including the establishment of 
     community benefits agreements; and
       (ii) jobs, particularly the creation of full-time jobs with 
     wages that are not less than the prevailing wage rates for 
     the class in the applicable locality.

       (II) Consultation.--In conducting an evaluation required 
     under subclause (I), the Commission shall consult with all 
     relevant Federal, State, local, Tribal, and regional 
     authorities (as determined based on the location of the 
     applicable data center), including--

       (aa) the Environmental Protection Agency, the Department of 
     Energy, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, and other 
     Federal agencies determined appropriate by the Commission;
       (bb) local, State, and Tribal--
       (AA) environmental protection agencies;
       (BB) zoning boards;
       (CC) siting and permitting authorities;
       (DD) energy and economic development agencies; and

[[Page S5651]]

       (EE) consumer advocates;
       (cc) State public utility commissions;
       (dd) Transmission Organizations, as defined in section 3 of 
     the Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. 796);
       (ee) the regional commission or committee that represents 
     the interests of States with respect to the relevant regional 
     electricity market; and
       (ff) other relevant Federal, State, local, Tribal, and 
     regional authorities, as determined appropriate by the 
     Commission.
       (iii) Determination.--The Commission shall issue a 
     certificate described in clause (i) if, after conducting the 
     evaluation required under clause (ii), the Commission 
     determines that the proposed data center will not harm the 
     public interest.
       (iv) Ongoing compliance; enforcement.--After issuing a 
     certificate under clause (i) with respect to a data center, 
     the Commission--

       (I) shall, not later than 2 years after issuing that 
     certificate, and once every 2 years thereafter--

       (aa) assess whether the data center remains in compliance 
     with that certificate, as determined by evaluating the data 
     center pursuant to the requirements under clause (ii); and
       (bb) as part of the assessment required under item (aa), 
     establish a publicly noticed and accessible process through 
     which affected members of the public may submit documentation 
     or provide comment with respect to the certificate; and

       (II) if the Commission, during an assessment conducted 
     under subclause (I) of this clause, determines that a data 
     center does not remain in compliance with that certificate, 
     as determined as described in subclause (I)(aa) of this 
     clause, may take action and pursue remedies to ensure that 
     the data center remains in compliance, including by imposing 
     fines that are proportionate to the duration and degree by 
     which the data center is not in compliance.

       (C) Savings clause.--Nothing in this paragraph shall 
     preempt or render void any State or local regulation relating 
     to the construction or operation of a data center.
       (3) Identifying air pollution and disparate impacts of data 
     center development and investing in community response.--
       (A) In general.--The Administrator may--
       (i) pursuant to subsections (b) through (e) of section 103 
     of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7403), make grants, enter 
     into contracts, or carry out other activities described in 
     that section to identify, monitor, and increase community 
     response capabilities against environmental or public health 
     harms from changes in air quality associated with the 
     construction and operation of data centers and associated 
     energy infrastructure, with a priority for activities that 
     serve communities with greater cumulative environmental or 
     public health burdens; and
       (ii) make grants and carry out other activities with 
     respect to the changes in air quality described in paragraph 
     (1) authorized under section 105 of the Clean Air Act (42 
     U.S.C. 7405).
       (B) Authorization of appropriations.--
       (i) In general.--In addition to any other amounts 
     authorized to be appropriated to carry out sections 103 and 
     105 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7403, 7405), there are 
     authorized to be appropriated to the Administrator for each 
     of fiscal years 2027 through 2031, to remain available until 
     expended--

       (I) $50,000,000 to carry out subparagraph (A)(i); and
       (II) $50,000,000 to carry out subparagraph (A)(ii).

       (ii) Administrative expenses.--Of the amounts made 
     available under subclauses (I) and (II) of clause (i) in a 
     fiscal year, the Administrator shall reserve 5 percent for 
     the administrative costs necessary to carry out the 
     activities funded under those subclauses.
       (4) Water quality monitoring.--
       (A) In general.--The Administrator may--
       (i) in addition to the purposes for which grants may be 
     provided under section 106 of the Federal Water Pollution 
     Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1256), make additional grants to, or 
     enter into cooperative agreements or contracts with, entities 
     eligible to receive grants under that section for water 
     quality monitoring and water pollution control related to 
     existing or expected impacts on water systems occurring as a 
     result of the construction and operation of data centers and 
     associated energy infrastructure; and
       (ii) make grants to the entities described in clause (i) to 
     obtain or contract for technical assistance relating to the 
     purposes described in that clause.
       (B) Authorization of appropriations.--In addition to any 
     other amounts authorized to be appropriated to carry out 
     section 106 of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 
     U.S.C. 1256), there are authorized to be appropriated to the 
     Administrator for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031, to 
     remain available until expended--
       (i) $100,000,000 to carry out subparagraph (A)(i); and
       (ii) $30,000,000 to carry out subparagraph (A)(ii).
       (5) Federal resources for mitigation of noise pollution.--
       (A) Study.--
       (i) In general.--The Administrator shall carry out a study 
     on the health impacts of noise from data centers and on-site 
     energy generation, including an examination of--

       (I) the selection of noise measurement methodologies by 
     relevant State and local agencies with respect to that noise;
       (II) the threshold at which health impacts from that noise 
     is felt;
       (III) the effectiveness of various noise abatement 
     methodologies used at data centers and on-site energy 
     generators; and
       (IV) new and emerging noise abatement methodologies that 
     may, or have the potential to, mitigate the extent of noise 
     pollution impacts from data centers and on-site energy 
     generation.

       (ii) Report.--

       (I) In general.--Not later than 2 years after the date of 
     enactment of this Act, the Administrator shall submit to 
     Congress a report describing the results of the study 
     conducted under clause (i).
       (II) Requirements.--The report required under subclause (I) 
     shall include specific recommendations on new measures that 
     can be implemented to mitigate the impact of data centers and 
     on-site energy generation on surrounding communities.

       (B) Grants under the quiet communities program.--Section 14 
     of the Noise Control Act of 1972 (42 U.S.C. 4913) is 
     amended--
       (i) in subsection (b)--

       (I) in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking 
     ``but not limited to'';
       (II) in paragraph (2), by striking ``sections 6, 7, and 8 
     of this Act'' and inserting ``section 6 or 8, or section 
     44715 of title 49, United States Code''; and
       (III) by redesignating paragraphs (1) through (5) as 
     subparagraphs (A) through (E), respectively, and indenting 
     appropriately;

       (ii) in subsection (c)--

       (I) in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking 
     ``but not be limited to'' and inserting ``in accordance with 
     Federal authority pursuant to this Act to regulate sources of 
     noise in interstate commerce'';
       (II) in paragraph (1)--

       (aa) in subparagraph (C), by striking ``and'' at the end;
       (bb) by redesignating subparagraphs (A) through (D) as 
     clauses (i) through (iv), respectively, and indenting 
     appropriately; and
       (cc) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(v) establishing and implementing training programs on 
     use of noise abatement equipment; and
       ``(vi) implementing noise abatement plans;'';

       (III) by striking the undesignated matter following 
     paragraph (5); and
       (IV) by redesignating paragraphs (1) through (5) as 
     subparagraphs (A) through (E), respectively, and indenting 
     appropriately;

       (iii) by redesignating subsections (a) through (g) as 
     paragraphs (1) through (7), respectively, and indenting 
     appropriately;
       (iv) in the matter preceding paragraph (1) (as so 
     redesignated), by striking ``To promote'' and inserting the 
     following:
       ``(a) In General.--To promote''; and
       (v) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(b) Authorization of Appropriations.--There is authorized 
     to be appropriated to carry out this section $20,000,000 for 
     each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031, to remain available 
     until expended.''.
       (6) State and local capacity grants to address data center 
     environmental impacts.--
       (A) Definitions.--In this paragraph:
       (i) Cumulative impact.--The term ``cumulative impact'', 
     with respect to pollution impact, means the impact on the 
     environment resulting from the incremental impact of the 
     action when added to other past, present, and reasonably 
     foreseeable future actions.
       (ii) Eligible entity.--The term ``eligible entity'' means a 
     partnership between--

       (I) a State, Tribal, or local government; and
       (II) a community-based nonprofit organization.

       (iii) Program.--The term ``program'' means the program 
     established under subparagraph (B).
       (B) Establishment.--The Administrator shall establish a 
     program to award grants to eligible entities to carry out 
     activities that benefit disadvantaged communities, as 
     determined by the Administrator, in accordance with this 
     paragraph.
       (C) Grants.--
       (i) In general.--The Administrator shall award grants under 
     the program to eligible entities to carry out activities 
     described in clause (ii).
       (ii) Eligible activities.--An eligible entity receiving a 
     grant under the program may use funds from that grant for--

       (I) community-led evaluation, monitoring, prevention, and 
     remediation of air, water, and noise pollution impact, 
     including cumulative impacts, with an emphasis on projects 
     that prioritize evaluation, monitoring, prevention, and 
     remediation of cumulative impacts;
       (II) efforts to strengthen local enforcement and technical 
     capacity for inspectors, engineers, and permitting and 
     compliance staff with relevant State, Tribal, and local 
     environmental agencies; and
       (III) facilitating engagement of disadvantaged communities, 
     as determined by the Administrator, in State, Tribal, local, 
     and intergovernmental zoning, permitting, and regulatory 
     decisions, including through strategies that increase access 
     across languages, abilities, and media platforms.

       (iii) Term.--The term of a grant under the program shall be 
     up to 3 years.
       (D) Technical assistance.--The Administrator shall provide 
     technical assistance to eligible entities with respect to 
     grants awarded under the program.
       (E) Authorization of appropriations.--
       (i) In general.--There is authorized to be appropriated to 
     the Administrator for each

[[Page S5652]]

     of fiscal years 2027 through 2031, to remain available until 
     expended--

       (I) $560,000,000 to award grants under subparagraph (C); 
     and
       (II) $40,000,000 to provide technical assistance under 
     subparagraph (D).

       (ii) Administrative costs.--Of the amounts made available 
     under clause (i) in a fiscal year, the Administrator shall 
     reserve 7 percent to carry out this paragraph.
```
