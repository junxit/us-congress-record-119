# Order of Procedure (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           Order of Procedure

  Mr. MORENO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following the 
remarks of Senator Britt, the Senate vote on confirmation of the Cook 
nomination; further, notwithstanding rule XXII, if cloture is invoked 
on the Domenico nomination, all postcloture time be expired and the 
Senate immediately vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the St. John 
nomination; further, if cloture is invoked on the St. John nomination, 
all postcloture time be expired, and notwithstanding rule XXII, the 
Senate vote on the confirmation of the Domenico and St. John 
nominations, at a time to be determined by the majority leader in 
consultation with the Democrat leader, no earlier than Monday, November 
16; further, if confirmed, the motions to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table and the President be immediately notified of 
the Senate's actions; finally, the cloture motions filed during 
Wednesday's session of the Senate ripen at 5:30 p.m. on Monday, 
November 16.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
