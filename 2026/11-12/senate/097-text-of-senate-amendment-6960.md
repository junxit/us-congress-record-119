# Text of Senate Amendment 6960

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 6960. Mr. HEINRICH (for Mr. King) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

        On page 6 of the amendment, strike lines 12 through 15 and 
     insert the following:
       ``(1) Agency cerpo.--The term `agency CERPO' means--
       ``(A) with respect to a key permitting agency for which 1 
     or more agency CERPOs have been designated under section 
     41002(b)(2)(A)(iii)(I) of the FAST Act (42 U.S.C. 4370m-
     1(b)(2)(A)(iii)(I)), each agency CERPO so designated; and
       ``(B) with respect to any other key permitting agency, the 
     chief environmental review and permitting officer of the key 
     permitting agency, as designated by the head of the key 
     permitting agency.
                                 ______
```
