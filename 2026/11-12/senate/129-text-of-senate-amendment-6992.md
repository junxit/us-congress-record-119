# Text of Senate Amendment 6992

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 6992. Ms. CANTWELL (for herself and Ms. Murkowski) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. 
Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. 
Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, other than punitive 
damages, received on account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the end of section 1204, add the following:
       (c) EPA Final Determinations.--Notwithstanding any other 
     provision of law, each determination of the Administrator of 
     the Environmental Protection Agency under section 404(c) of 
     the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1344(c)) 
     made during the period that begins on January 1, 2023, and 
     ends on the date of enactment of this Act shall have the 
     force and effect of law.
                                 ______
```
