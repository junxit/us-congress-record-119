# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2026

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2347

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2026

  Mr. HUSTED. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, November 16; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to executive 
session and resume consideration of the Domenico nomination; further, 
that at 5:30 p.m., the Senate execute the order in relation to the 
Domenico nomination; if the nomination is confirmed, the Senate resume 
legislative session and vote on the motion to invoke cloture on 
substitute amendment No. 6883 to H.R. 2347.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
