# Text of Senate Amendment 6942

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6942. Mr. PADILLA (for himself, Mr. Gallego, Mr. Lujan, Mr. 
Hickenlooper, and Ms. Rosen) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito 
(for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill 
H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from 
gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts

[[Page S5619]]

or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 483, line 23, strike ``clause (ii)'' and insert 
     ``clauses (ii) and (iii)''.
       On page 484, line 17, insert ``and clause (iii)'' after 
     ``(II)''.
       On page 485, between lines 18 and 19, insert the following:
       ``(iii) Application.--Clauses (i) and (ii) shall not apply 
     to a claim that is brought by an Indian tribe or Native 
     Hawaiian Organization based on harm to 1 or more properties 
     of traditional religious or cultural importance.
```
