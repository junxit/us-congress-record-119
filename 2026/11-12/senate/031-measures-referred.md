# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1013, H.R. 2102, H.R. 5549, H.R. 9084, H.R. 9718, H.R. 10238, H.R. 10329, H.R. 10353, H.R. 10355, H.R. 10371, H.R. 10375

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1013. An act to amend the Federal securities laws to 
     enhance 403(b) plans, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
       H.R. 2102. An act to amend title 10, United States Code, to 
     provide for concurrent receipt of veterans' disability 
     compensation and retired pay for disability retirees with 
     combat-related disabilities, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
       H.R. 5549. An act to amend the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to 
     provide for more efficient hearings on nuclear facility 
     construction applications, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Environment and Public Works.
       H.R. 9084. An act to increase transparency relating to the 
     Department of Energy's authorizations of certain nuclear 
     facilities; to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 9718. An act to require the Administrator of the Small 
     Business Administration to maintain a website for small 
     business concerns relating to onshoring manufacturing 
     capacity, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Small 
     Business and Entrepreneurship.
       H.R. 10238. An act to require the Administrator of the 
     Small Business Administration to disseminate to small 
     business concerns certain information and resources relating 
     to cybersecurity matters, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship.
       H.R. 10329. An act to require the Administrator of the 
     Small Business Administration to disseminate information on 
     foreign adversary conduct that poses material harm to small 
     business concerns, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Small Business and Entrepreneurship.
       H.R. 10353. An act to amend the Small Business Investment 
     Act of 1958 to require a quarterly report on small business 
     investment companies, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship.
       H.R. 10355. An act to require the Administrator of the 
     Small Business Administration to establish procedures for 
     consistency of certain disaster loan outreach materials, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Small Business and 
     Entrepreneurship.
       H.R. 10371. An act to direct the Associate Administrator 
     for Veterans Business Development of the Small Business 
     Administration to ensure compliance with certain reporting 
     requirements, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Small Business and Entrepreneurship.

[[Page S5590]]

  

       H.R. 10375. An act to amend the Small Business Investment 
     Act of 1958 to improve the loan guaranty program, enhance the 
     ability of small manufacturers to access affordable capital, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Small Business 
     and Entrepreneurship.

                          ____________________
```
