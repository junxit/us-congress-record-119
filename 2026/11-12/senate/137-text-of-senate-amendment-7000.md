# Text of Senate Amendment 7000

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

```
  SA 7000. Ms. MURKOWSKI (for herself, Mr. Schatz, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. 
Gallego, and Mr. Lujan) submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

        On page 491 of the amendment, strike lines 17 through 22 
     and insert the following:
       ``For each of fiscal years 2027 through 2036, there is 
     authorized to be appropriated, from the unappropriated 
     balance of the Historic Preservation Fund on September 30, 
     2026, or the general fund of the Treasury, $400,000,000 for 
     deposit in the Historic Preservation Fund, from which the 
     Secretary shall provide grants to Tribal Historic 
     Preservation Officers and other capacity grants to carry out 
     this division, including grants administered pursuant to 
     section 302907 for Indian tribes and Native Hawaiian 
     organizations to establish, enhance, or expand their capacity 
     to carry out their responsibilities under this division.''.
```
