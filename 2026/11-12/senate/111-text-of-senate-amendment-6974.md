# Text of Senate Amendment 6974

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

```
  SA 6974. Mr. HEINRICH (for Mr. Kelly (for himself, Mr. Schiff, Mr. 
Padilla, and Mr. Gallego)) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito 
(for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill 
H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from 
gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

        On page 181 of the amendment, strike lines 5 through 7 and 
     insert the following:
       (c) Savings Clause.--Nothing in this section--
       (1) limits or modifies any authority under any applicable 
     law, compact, or treaty; or
       (2) creates any right or entitlement to the delivery, 
     release, or availability of a specific quantity of water.
                                 ______
```
