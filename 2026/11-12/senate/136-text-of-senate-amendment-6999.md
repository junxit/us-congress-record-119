# Text of Senate Amendment 6999

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

```
  SA 6999. Ms. MURKOWSKI (for herself, Mr. Schatz, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. 
Gallego, Mr. Lujan, and Mr. Hickenlooper) submitted an amendment 
intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for 
Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) 
to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
exclude from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, 
received on account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 461, strike line 8 and all that follows 
     through page 464, line 13, and insert the following:

[[Page S5663]]

       ``(ix) the continuation, operation, maintenance, or post-
     approval implementation of a project, activity, or program 
     after the determination has been made under subsection (c), 
     except that a subsequent discrete decision of a Federal 
     agency to amend or materially modify a determination made 
     under subsection (c) is itself an undertaking, to the extent 
     provided in this section.
       ``(C) Rule of construction with respect to indian tribes 
     and native hawaiian organizations.--
       ``(i) In general.--Notwithstanding subparagraph (A) and 
     clauses (i) and (ii) of subparagraph (B), a project, 
     activity, or program receiving Federal financial assistance 
     shall be considered an undertaking for purposes of this 
     section if--

       ``(I) the Federal agency providing the financial assistance 
     has discretionary authority to approve, condition, or modify 
     the use of the assistance for the project, activity, or 
     program; and
       ``(II) based on reasonably available information, including 
     information provided by an Indian tribe or Native Hawaiian 
     organization, the Federal agency knows or has reason to 
     believe that the project, activity, or program may affect a 
     property of traditional religious and cultural importance to 
     an Indian tribe or Native Hawaiian organization.

       ``(ii) Determination.--

       ``(I) In general.--The determination under clause (i)(II) 
     shall be made as part of the initial determination of the 
     Federal agency of whether a project, activity, or program 
     constitutes an undertaking under subsection (c)(2).
       ``(II) Information.--In making such a determination under 
     clause (i)(II), the Federal agency shall seek information 
     from any Indian tribe or Native Hawaiian organization 
     reasonably identified as attaching religious and cultural 
     significance to properties in the geographic area potentially 
     affected.

       ``(iii) Limitation.--Nothing in this subparagraph--

       ``(I) requires a separate archaeological survey, formal 
     determination of eligibility for inclusion in the National 
     Register, or other identification effort solely for purposes 
     of determining whether a project, activity, or program 
     constitutes an undertaking under clause (i);
       ``(II) requires a Federal agency to determine whether a 
     project, activity, or program will have an adverse effect on 
     a historic property before determining whether the project, 
     activity, or program constitutes an undertaking under clause 
     (i);
       ``(III) alters the requirements applicable to the 
     identification and evaluation of historic properties or 
     consultation with Indian tribes or Native Hawaiian 
     organizations after a project, activity, or program is 
     determined to constitute an undertaking; or
       ``(IV)(aa) extends the deadline for determining whether a 
     project, activity, or program constitutes an undertaking 
     under subsection (c)(2); or
       ``(bb) requires the Federal agency to delay the 
     determination pending the receipt of information from an 
     Indian tribe or Native Hawaiian organization.''; and

       (4) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(c) Consultation.--
       ``(1) In general.--The responsibilities of a Federal agency 
     under this section shall be limited to historic properties 
     within the area of potential effects identified by the 
     Federal agency.
       ``(2) Initiation and report.--
       ``(A) Determination.--
       ``(i) In general.--Not later than 30 days after the date of 
     receipt of a request for review of a proposed project, 
     activity, or program under this section, the applicable 
     Federal official shall determine whether the proposed 
     project, activity, or program is an undertaking subject to 
     the requirements of this section.
       ``(ii) Potential effect.--If an applicable Federal official 
     determines that the applicable undertaking is a type of 
     activity that does not have the potential to cause any effect 
     on a historic property (assuming a historic property is 
     present), the applicable Federal official shall have no 
     further obligation under this section.
       ``(iii) Applicability of other provisions.--Subject to 
     paragraph (7)(B)(ii), if the review of an undertaking is 
     governed by a Federal agency program alternative established 
     under section 800.14 of title 36, Code of Federal Regulations 
     (or a successor regulation), or this section, including an 
     alternate procedure, programmatic agreement, exempted 
     category, standard treatment, or program comment, the 
     applicable Federal official shall carry out this paragraph in 
     accordance with that program alternative, subject to 
     paragraph (4), subparagraphs (A) and (B) of paragraph (5), 
     and paragraph (6).
       ``(B) Report on undertaking.--
       ``(i) In general.--If the applicable Federal official 
     determines under subparagraph (A) that the proposed project, 
     activity, or program is an undertaking, the applicable 
     Federal official shall prepare a report that includes--

       ``(I) a determination regarding the area of potential 
     effects for the undertaking;
       ``(II) identification of any historic properties within the 
     area of potential effects; and
       ``(III) description of the adverse effects, if any, of the 
     undertaking on historic properties within the area of 
     potential effects.

       ``(ii) Mandatory solicitation of information.--In making a 
     determination under subparagraph (A)(i), the applicable 
     Federal official shall seek, discuss, as appropriate, and 
     incorporate, as applicable, information from each applicable 
     State Historic Preservation Officer, Tribal Historic 
     Preservation Officer, Indian tribe, or Native Hawaiian 
     organization, local government, and project, activity, or 
     program applicant, if the information is communicated to the 
     applicable Federal official not later than 30 days after the 
     date on which the Federal official submits a request for such 
     information.
```
