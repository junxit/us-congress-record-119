**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

  SA 6962. Mr. HEINRICH (for Mr. King) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. WAIVER AND LIMITATION FOR CERTAIN WIND PROJECTS.

       (a) Waiver.--Congress waives the requirement to obtain any 
     approval, authorization, permit, mitigation agreement, or 
     similar document from any Federal agency for the wind 
     projects described in subsection (c).
       (b) Limitation.--No Federal agency may take any action to 
     stop the construction or operation at full capacity of any 
     wind project described in subsection (c) without first 
     obtaining an order from the United States Court of Appeals 
     for the District of Columbia Circuit.
