# Text of Senate Amendment 6934

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
  SA 6934. Mr. VAN HOLLEN submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito 
(for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill 
H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from 
gross income any damages, other than punitive damages,

[[Page S5615]]

received on account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 2107, add at the end the following:
       (c) Curtailment of Computational Loads.--
       (1) Definitions.--In this subsection:
       (A) Commission.--The term ``Commission'' means the Federal 
     Energy Regulatory Commission.
       (B) Computational load.--The term ``computational load'' 
     means the load of a computational load site.
       (C) Computational load operator; computational load owner; 
     computational load site.--The terms ``computational load 
     operator'', ``computational load owner'', and ``computational 
     load site'' have the meanings given those terms in subsection 
     (a)(2).
       (D) Independent system operator; regional transmission 
     organization.--The terms ``Independent System Operator'' and 
     ``Regional Transmission Organization'' have the meanings 
     given those terms in section 3 of the Federal Power Act (16 
     U.S.C. 796).
       (2) Curtailment.--Not later than 90 days after the date of 
     enactment of this Act, the Commission shall initiate 
     investigations under section 206 of the Federal Power Act (16 
     U.S.C. 824e) to ensure that Regional Transmission 
     Organizations and Independent System Operators have processes 
     and procedures in their open access transmission tariffs to 
     curtail computational loads before reasonably foreseeable 
     energy or operating reserve shortages trigger shortage or 
     scarcity pricing under the applicable tariff, and not later 
     than the commencement of an energy emergency alert level EEA 
     1 (as defined by the North American Electric Reliability 
     Corporation) or a higher energy emergency alert level event 
     applicable to the affected area, if the computational load 
     has failed to bring sufficient new generation to the grid to 
     meet its peak demand, plus a reserve margin, to address 
     reliability and affordability impacts on other grid users.
       (3) Resource adequacy procurements.--A Regional 
     Transmission Organization or Independent System Operator 
     shall not include a computational load subject to curtailment 
     under paragraph (2) in any Commission-jurisdictional resource 
     adequacy procurement, including a capacity market, beyond the 
     quantity of energy demanded by the computational load that 
     can be met by new supply brought to the procurement on behalf 
     of that computational load.
       (4) Compensation.--
       (A) In general.--The Commission shall determine whether to 
     compensate, and if so, set the level of compensation for, any 
     computational load owner or computational load operator for 
     computational load curtailed under paragraph (2), to ensure 
     just and reasonable rates.
       (B) Limitation.--No compensation provided under 
     subparagraph (A) may be derived from ratepayers other than 
     computational load owners and computational load operators.
       (5) Application of certain requirements.--Any tariff 
     approved under this subsection is subject to the requirements 
     of sections 205 and 206 of the Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. 
     824d, 824e) that all rates, charges, terms, and conditions be 
     just and reasonable and not unduly discriminatory or 
     preferential.
       (6) Requirement.--As soon as practicable, and not later 
     than 270 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the 
     Commission shall establish replacement rates, terms, and 
     conditions that are consistent with the requirements of 
     paragraphs (2) through (5).
```
