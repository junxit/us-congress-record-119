# Text of Senate Amendment 6995

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 6995. Ms. CANTWELL (for herself and Mr. Wyden) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. 
Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. 
Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, other than punitive 
damages, received on account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 1401, add at the end the following:
       (d) Exclusion of Certain Federal Land in Alaska.--
       (1) Definitions.--In this subsection:
       (A) Arctic national wildlife refuge.--
       (i) In general.--The term ``Arctic National Wildlife 
     Refuge'' means the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge designated 
     under section 303(2) of the Alaska National Interest Lands 
     Conservation Act (Public Law 96-487; 94 Stat. 2390).
       (ii) Inclusion.--The term ``Arctic National Wildlife 
     Refuge'' includes the Coastal Plain (as defined in section 
     20001(a) of Public Law 115-97 (16 U.S.C. 3143 note)).
       (B) Covered activity.--The term ``covered activity'' means 
     any project, action, or activity, including any lease sale, 
     lease, exploration, seismic survey, road, pipeline, or right-
     of-way, that is located wholly or partially on Federal land 
     within--
       (i) the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge; or
       (ii) the National Petroleum Reserve in Alaska.
       (C) National petroleum reserve in alaska.--The term 
     ``National Petroleum Reserve in Alaska'' means the National 
     Petroleum Reserve in Alaska designated under section 102 of 
     the Naval Petroleum Reserves Production Act of 1976 (42 
     U.S.C. 6502).
       (2) Exclusion.--Subsection (b) shall not apply to--
       (A) any Federal authorization or permit, lease, or other 
     Federal approval for a covered activity; or
       (B) any action by a Federal department or agency with 
     respect to a covered activity.
       (3) Savings clause.--Nothing in this subsection shall limit 
     the authority of any Federal department or agency under any 
     other provision of law with respect to a covered activity.
```
