# Nomination of Gregory Carl Cook (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Britt, Katie Boyd (B001319) [R-AL], Moreno, Bernie (M001242) [R-OH]

```
                    Nomination of Gregory Carl Cook

  Mrs. BRITT. Mr. President, I am proud to speak today in support of 
Justice Greg Cook of the Alabama Supreme Court to serve as a Federal 
district judge in the Northern District of Alabama. Justice Cook is 
beyond qualified to serve in this role.
  When you look at his resume, starting from growing up in the Shoals 
where he cemented core values of faith, family, and freedom, going on 
into Duke University and then Harvard Law School, and then having a 
very distinguished career in public service, which includes being a 
captain of the U.S. Air Force--in everything that Greg has done in 
life, he has done it to the best of his ability.
  He has given it his whole heart, and he has made sure to put the 
needs of the State and country first.
  Since serving in the Air Force, he became a distinguished partner at 
one of our State's top law firms where he was elected after that to be 
an associate justice of the Alabama Supreme Court in 2022. And this is 
actually when I got to know Greg. We traveled around the State together 
on the campaign trail. I got to watch him interact with people. I got 
to know he and his wife Kimberly, their love for the Lord, their love 
for their family, and Greg's love for the law.
  Greg wrote the Alabama civil procedure, ``Rules of Civil Procedure'' 
book, which is quite thick. Greg would take it out around as he talked 
to what he would hope to be, the people he was able to serve on the 
Alabama Supreme Court, the citizens of the great State of Alabama.
  And he would always tell them the one thing that they could count on 
from him is not only his tireless hard work but that he would be boring 
and predictable. And what he meant by that was he was going to follow 
the law. The words that were in that book, that would be what guided 
him--the Constitution, and knowing that he was not there to create 
laws, he was there to interpret them.
  More important than his many accolades, I am really proud to call him 
and his wife friends. He truly embodies the very best of the great 
State of Alabama.
  I have every faith and confidence that he will use everything in his 
being to make sure that he serves the people of the Northern District 
and this Nation to the very best of his ability.
  I want to thank President Trump for Justice Cook's nomination, and I 
want to urge my colleagues to support him in the upcoming vote.
  This is the exact kind of person we want to see on the Federal bench. 
This is a good day for the State of Alabama and a great one for our 
Nation.
  I yield the floor.
```
