# Text of Senate Amendment 6920

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 6920. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 170 of the amendment, line 5, strike ``an'' and 
     insert ``a final and publicly disclosed''.
       On page 170 of the amendment, between lines 14 and 15, 
     insert the following:
       (3) Project or action.--The term ``project or action'' 
     means an instance of a specific type of covered project under 
     section 1402(b)(4).
       On page 172 of the amendment, line 1, insert ``, including 
     application of new rulemaking,'' after ``such action''.
       On page 181 of the amendment, between lines 7 and 8, insert 
     the following:
       (d) Prohibition on Permit Payoffs.--Notwithstanding any 
     other provision of law, no funds shall be available to pay 
     compromise settlements of claims alleging unlawful 
     termination of a Federal authorization or permit.
       On page 188 of the amendment, line 13, insert ``, based on 
     the date a Federal department or agency determines an 
     application is complete,'' after ``deadline''.
       On page 188 of the amendment, line 17, insert ``, if 
     determined applicable by a Federal department or agency'' 
     before the semicolon.
       On page 188 of the amendment, line 20, insert ``, if 
     determined applicable by a Federal department or agency'' 
     before the semicolon.
       On page 199 of the amendment, between lines 12 and 13, 
     insert the following:
       (3) Project.--The term ``project'' means an instance of a 
     specific type of covered project under section 1402(b)(4).
                                 ______
```
