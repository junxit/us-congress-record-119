# Text of Senate Amendment 6916

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 6916. Mr. MERKLEY (for himself, Mr. Markey, Mr. Schiff, Ms. 
Duckworth, and Mr. Wyden) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito 
(for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill 
H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from 
gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. PROHIBITION TO CARRY OUT CERTAIN PROJECTS.

       (a) In General.--Nothing in this Act provides the 
     authorization of Congress, including the issuance of any 
     permit or regulatory approval, to carry out any project 
     described in subsection (b) and no Federal funds or private 
     donations may be used for such projects absent express 
     authorization of Congress after the date of enactment of this 
     Act.
       (b) Projects Described.--The projects referred to in 
     subsection (a) are each project that relates to the 
     following:
       (1) The East Wing Modernization.
       (2) The Triumphal Arch at Memorial Circle.
       (3) The East Potomac Golf Links.
                                 ______
```
