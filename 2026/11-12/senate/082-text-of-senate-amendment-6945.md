# Text of Senate Amendment 6945

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6945. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       In section 1401(b)(4)(F) of the amendment, add at the end 
     the following:
       (iii) Applicability of limitation.--Notwithstanding any 
     other provision of law, the limitation on payments described 
     in clause (ii) shall apply to any claim brought pursuant to 
     applicable law that--

       (I) the United States Court of Federal Claims would have 
     jurisdiction; and
       (II) arises from the termination of--

       (aa) a Federal authorization or permit; or
       (bb) a Federal lease.
                                 ______
```
