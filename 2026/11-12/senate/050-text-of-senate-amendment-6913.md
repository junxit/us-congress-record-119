# Text of Senate Amendment 6913

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6913. Mr. KIM (for himself, Mr. Booker, and Ms. Alsobrooks) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        Beginning on page 71 of the amendment, strike line 22 and 
     all that follows through page 72, line 4, and insert the 
     following:
       ``(1) is filed on or after the date of enactment of the 
     Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026;
       ``(2) seeks judicial review under any provision of Federal 
     law of an authorization based on a claim that an 
     environmental review failed to be properly conducted pursuant 
     to the requirements of this Act; and
       ``(3) is not asserted in an amended pleading in an action 
     that was originally filed prior to the date of enactment of 
     the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026.
                                 ______
```
