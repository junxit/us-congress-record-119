[[Page S5648]]

than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or sexual 
contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. WAIVER AND LIMITATION FOR CERTAIN WIND PROJECTS.

     approval, authorization, permit, mitigation agreement, or 
     similar document from any Federal agency for the wind 
     projects described in subsection (c).
