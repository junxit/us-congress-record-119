# Text of Senate Amendment 6933

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
  SA 6933. Mr. VAN HOLLEN (for himself, Ms. Alsobrooks, Mr. Coons, and 
Ms. Blunt Rochester) submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       On page 181 of the amendment, between lines 4 and 5, insert 
     the following:
       (5) Voluntary remand.--
       (A) In general.--For a project that has a Federal 
     authorization or permit on the date of enactment of this Act, 
     notwithstanding any outstanding authorizations or permits, a 
     court shall only grant a remand requested by a Federal 
     department or agency that is pending as of November 11, 2026, 
     or is substantially similar to a remand request pending on 
     November 11, 2026, for the same project, if the court finds 
     that the Federal department or agency has established grounds 
     for action under subparagraph (B), (C), or (D) of that 
     paragraph.
       (B) Clarification.--An order granting a remand under 
     subparagraph (A) shall not, by itself, constitute a court 
     order requiring action under paragraph (1)(A).
                                 ______
```
