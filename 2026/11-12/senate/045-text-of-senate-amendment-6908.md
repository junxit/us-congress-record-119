# Text of Senate Amendment 6908

**Speaking:** Hagerty, Bill (H000601) [R-TN]

```
  SA 6908. Mr. HAGERTY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 249, strike lines 5 through 17 and insert the 
     following:
       ``(1) ERCOT and tva.--This section shall not apply within--
       ``(A) the area referred to in section 212(k)(2)(A); or
       ``(B) the area described in section 15d(a) of the Tennessee 
     Valley Authority Act of 1933 (16 U.S.C. 831n-4(a)).
       ``(2) Other utilities.--
       ``(A) In general.--For the purposes of this section, the 
     Commission shall have jurisdiction over all transmitting 
     utilities, including transmitting utilities described in 
     section 201(f), but excluding any ERCOT utility (as defined 
     in section 212(k)(2)(B)), the Tennessee Valley Authority 
     (within the area described in section 15d(a) of the Tennessee 
     Valley Authority Act of 1933 (16 U.S.C. 831n-4(a))), and any 
     exempt transmitting utility (as defined in section 225(a)) 
     that does not voluntarily subject itself to Commission 
     jurisdiction for the purposes of this section.
       Beginning on page 261, strike line 10 and all that follows 
     through age 262, line 2, and insert the following:
       ``(B) Exclusions.--The term `transmission planning region' 
     does not include--
       ``(i) the Electric Reliability Council of Texas or the 
     region served by members of the Electric Reliability Council 
     of Texas; or
       ``(ii) the Tennessee Valley Authority or the area described 
     in section 15d(a) of the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 
     1933 (16 U.S.C. 831n-4(a)).
       ``(b) Jurisdiction.--
       ``(1) ERCOT and tva.--This section shall not apply within--
       ``(A) the area referred to in section 212(k)(2)(A); or
       ``(B) the area described in section 15d(a) of the Tennessee 
     Valley Authority Act of 1933 (16 U.S.C. 831n-4(a)).
       ``(2) Other utilities.--
       ``(A) In general.--For the purposes of this section, the 
     Commission shall have jurisdiction over all transmitting 
     utilities, including transmitting utilities described in 
     section 201(f), but excluding any ERCOT utility (as defined 
     in section 212(k)(2)(B)), the Tennessee Valley Authority 
     (within the area described in section 15d(a) of the Tennessee 
     Valley Authority Act of 1933 (16 U.S.C. 831n-4(a))), and any 
     exempt transmitting utility that does not voluntarily subject 
     itself to Commission jurisdiction for the purposes of this 
     section.
                                 ______
```
