# Text of Senate Amendment 6944

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6944. Mr. PADILLA (for himself and Mr. Schiff) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. 
Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. 
Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, other than punitive 
damages, received on account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 170, between lines 14 and 15, insert the following:
       (3) New action; any other action.--The terms ``new action'' 
     and ``any other action'' do not include an action taken 
     pursuant to any applicable law (including regulations), 
     including the discretionary or nondiscretionary issuance, 
     application, or enforcement of statutes or regulations.
       On page 171, line 20, insert ``or'' after the semicolon.
       On page 171, line 24, strike ``; or'' and insert a period.
       On page 172, strike lines 1 and 2.
       On page 173, line 24, strike ``(F)'' and insert ``(E)''.
                                 ______
```
