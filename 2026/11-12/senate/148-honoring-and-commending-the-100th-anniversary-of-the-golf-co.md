# HONORING AND COMMENDING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE GOLF COURSE SUPERINTENDENTS ASSOCIATION OF AMERICA

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S.Res. 834

```
   HONORING AND COMMENDING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE GOLF COURSE 
                 SUPERINTENDENTS ASSOCIATION OF AMERICA

  Mr. HUSTED. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on the Judiciary be discharged from further consideration and 
the Senate now proceed to S. Res. 834.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 834) honoring and commending the 
     100th anniversary of the Golf Course Superintendents 
     Association of America.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the resolution.
  Mr. HUSTED. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to, 
the preamble be agreed to, and that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 834) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in the Record of 
August 6, 2026, under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
