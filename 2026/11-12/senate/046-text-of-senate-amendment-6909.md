# Text of Senate Amendment 6909

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
  SA 6909. Mr. BARRASSO (for Mr. Cornyn (for himself and Mr. Padilla)) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title I of division B, insert 
     the following:

     SEC. 21__. REAUTHORIZATION.

       Section 40101 of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act 
     (42 U.S.C. 18711) is amended--
       (1) in subsection (i)(1), by striking ``2026'' and 
     inserting ``2031''; and
       (2) in subsection (j), by striking ``2022 through 2026'' 
     and inserting ``2027 through 2031''.
                                 ______
```
