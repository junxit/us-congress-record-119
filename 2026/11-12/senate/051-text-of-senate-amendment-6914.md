# Text of Senate Amendment 6914

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6914. Mr. KIM submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       In section 2107, add at the end the following:
       (c) Electric Bill Transparency.--Each electric utility (as 
     defined in section 3 of the Public Utility Regulatory 
     Policies Act of 1978 (16 U.S.C. 2602)) shall include on each 
     incremental bill statement provided to any customer of the 
     electric utility a clear breakdown of the costs and other 
     charges included in that statement, which shall separately 
     identify--
       (1) costs attributable to supply;
       (2) costs attributable to transmission;
       (3) costs attributable to distribution; and
       (4) other charges.
                                 ______
```
