# Text of Senate Amendment 6969

**Speaking:** Markey, Edward J. (M000133) [D-MA]

```
  SA 6969. Mr. MARKEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

        On page 345, strike lines 4 through 21 and insert the 
     following:
       (1) In general.--At the request of a State, local, or 
     Tribal government, the Secretary shall--
       (A) request the applicable computational load site 
     developer to provide to the Secretary information, data, 
     analyses, models, assumptions, estimates, projections, and 
     other materials relating to the proposed computational load 
     site;
       (B) independently assess that information and those 
     materials, including by evaluating--
       (i) the reasonableness of the assumptions and methodologies 
     used;
       (ii) the reliability of the data;
       (iii) whether estimates and projections are adequately 
     supported by the information provided; and
       (iv) the technical reasonableness of the conclusions 
     reached; and
       (C) after receiving and assessing relevant information, the 
     Secretary shall--
       (i) transmit such information back to the applicable State, 
     local, or Tribal government; and
       (ii) make publicly available any information not considered 
     confidential.
                                 ______
```
