# Text of Senate Amendment 6997

**Speaking:** Duckworth, Tammy (D000622) [D-IL]

```
  SA 6997. Ms. DUCKWORTH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 249, strike line 23 and all that follows 
     through page 256, line 2, and insert the following:
       (j) Land Restoration.--Section 216 of the Federal Power Act 
     (16 U.S.C. 824p) is amended--
       (1) by redesignating subsections (j) and (k) as subsections 
     (k) and (l), respectively; and
       (2) by inserting after subsection (i) the following:
       ``(j) Land Restoration.--
       ``(1) In general.--Not later than 180 days after the date 
     of enactment of the Bipartisan American Affordability and 
     Jobs Act of 2026, the Commission, in consultation with the 
     Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of the Interior, 
     affected States and Indian Tribes, and relevant stakeholders, 
     shall issue rules establishing standards for the restoration 
     of affected property during and after construction of 
     electric transmission facilities, including--
       ``(A) standards relating to--
       ``(i) topsoil separation and replacement;
       ``(ii) temporary and permanent repair to drain tile;
       ``(iii) removal of rocks and debris from the right-of-way;
       ``(iv) restoration of areas of soil compaction;
       ``(v) restoration of terraces, waterways, and other erosion 
     control structures;
       ``(vi) revegetation of untilled land;
       ``(vii) future installation of drain tile or soil 
     conservation structures;
       ``(viii) restoration of land slope and contour;
       ``(ix) restoration of areas used for field entrances and 
     temporary roads;
       ``(x) construction in wet conditions; and
       ``(xi) designation of a point of contact for affected 
     landowner inquires or claims; and
       ``(B) any other standard that the Commission determines is 
     necessary to ensure that affected landowners are able to use 
     affected property to as similar an extent as possible to the 
     use of that property before the construction of an electric 
     transmission facility.
       ``(2) Inspections.--
       ``(A) In general.--The Commission shall require, and 
     establish procedures for the conduct of, an on-site 
     inspection for compliance with the standards established 
     under paragraph (1).
       ``(B) Requirement.--An on-site inspection under 
     subparagraph (A) shall be conducted by an independent 
     professional engineer that--
       ``(i) is familiar with the standards established under 
     paragraph (1); and
       ``(ii) does not have a conflict of interest with respect to 
     the inspection.
       ``(C) Cost.--The cost of an inspection under subparagraph 
     (A) shall be borne by the holder of the permit for the 
     applicable electric transmission facility.
       ``(3) Remedies.--
       ``(A) Initial notice and corrective action.--
       ``(i) In general.--If, pursuant to an inspection under 
     paragraph (2), an inspector finds a violation of a standard 
     established under paragraph (1), a land restoration plan 
     described in paragraph (4), or an independent

[[Page S5661]]

     agreement on land restoration, the inspector conducting the 
     inspection shall--

       ``(I) give oral notice of the violation, followed by a 
     written notice, to the holder of the permit for the 
     applicable electric transmission facility; and
       ``(II) order that permit holder to take corrective action 
     to come into compliance and remedy the violation, the cost of 
     which shall be borne by that permit holder.

       ``(ii) Cessation of construction activity.--If an 
     inspection under paragraph (2) occurs during the construction 
     of an electric transmission facility and the inspector finds 
     a violation of a standard established under paragraph (1), a 
     land restoration plan described in paragraph (4), or an 
     independent agreement on land restoration, the inspector may 
     issue an order to temporarily halt construction activity 
     until the permit holder remedies the violation.
       ``(B) Further action.--
       ``(i) In general.--If, after receiving notice of a 
     violation under subparagraph (A)(i), a permit holder does not 
     remedy the violation identified in the notice, the Commission 
     shall issue an order requiring corrective action to be taken, 
     including a timeline for completion.
       ``(ii) Timeline extension.--The Commission may extend or 
     alter a timeline established under clause (i) if the permit 
     holder is actively cooperating with the Commission--

       ``(I) to remedy the violation identified in the notice 
     provided under subparagraph (A)(i); and
       ``(II) to take the corrective action identified in the 
     order issued under clause (i).

       ``(iii) Further remedies.--If a permit holder fails to 
     comply with an order requiring corrective action under clause 
     (i) within the timeline established by the Commission (as 
     extended or altered under clause (ii), if applicable), the 
     Commission--

       ``(I) may revoke the permit; and
       ``(II) shall impose a civil penalty equal to the sum 
     obtained by adding--

       ``(aa) $100,000; and
       ``(bb) the reasonable cost, as determined by the 
     Commission, in consultation with the affected landowners, of 
     the land remediation activities necessary to remedy the 
     violation identified in the notice provided under 
     subparagraph (A)(i).
       ``(C) Distribution of civil penalty; affected landowner 
     compensation.--The amount of any civil penalty collected 
     under subparagraph (B)(iii)(II) shall be distributed as 
     follows:
       ``(i) The amount described in item (aa) of that 
     subparagraph shall be retained by the Commission.
       ``(ii) The amount described in item (bb) of that 
     subparagraph shall be provided proportionately to affected 
     landowners based on the estimated cost of necessary land 
     remediation activities on the affected property of each 
     affected landowner.
       ``(D) Judicial review.--
       ``(i) In general.--An affected landowner alleging a 
     violation of any requirement of this subsection, including 
     violation of a standard established under paragraph (1), an 
     inspection requirement under paragraph (2), or an order 
     issued under paragraph (3), may bring a civil action against 
     the permit holder in--

       ``(I) the district court of the United States for the 
     judicial district in which the affected property is located; 
     or
       ``(II) the appropriate court of the State in which the 
     affected property is located.

       ``(ii) Remedies.--In an action under clause (i), a court 
     may order all appropriate relief, including injunctive relief 
     and compensatory, punitive, and incidental damages, including 
     attorney fees.
       ``(4) Land restoration plans.--
       ``(A) In general.--An applicant for a construction permit 
     under subsection (b) shall file with the application for that 
     permit under subsection (c) a written land restoration plan 
     describing how the construction activity will meet the 
     standards established under paragraph (1).
       ``(B) Copies of land restoration plan.--An applicant 
     described in subparagraph (A) shall provide a copy of the 
     land restoration plan described in that subparagraph to all 
     affected landowners.
       ``(5) Independent land restoration agreements.--
     Notwithstanding the standards established under paragraph 
     (1), an applicant for a construction permit under subsection 
     (b) may, in lieu of or in addition to issuing a land 
     restoration plan under paragraph (4), enter into written 
     independent land restoration agreements with affected 
     landowners.
       ``(6) Inspections under independent land restoration 
     agreements.--If an inspection under paragraph (2) is to occur 
     at a construction site for which an independent land 
     restoration agreement exists as described in paragraph (5), a 
     copy of the independent land restoration agreement shall be 
     provided to the inspector.''.

     SEC. 2102. RECONDUCTORING AND OTHER ACTIVITIES IN EXISTING 
                   RIGHTS-OF-WAY.

       (a) Categorical Exclusion for Reconductoring.--The Federal 
     Power Act is amended by inserting after section 216 (16 
     U.S.C. 824p) the following:

     ``SEC. 216A. GRID CAPACITY EXPANSION IN EXISTING RIGHTS-OF-
                   WAY.

       ``(a) Definitions.--In this section:
       ``(1) Applicable electrical standard.--The term `applicable 
     electrical standard' includes--
       ``(A) a reliability standard (as defined in section 
     215(a)); and
       ``(B) an applicable clause of the National Electrical 
     Safety Code published by the Institute of Electrical and 
     Electronics Engineers Standards Association.
       ``(2) Covered area.--The term `covered area' means an 
     area--
       ``(A) within an existing right-of-way; or
       ``(B) within the minimum additional area adjacent to an 
     existing right-of-way, provided that the total additional 
     area is not more than \1/4\ mile from the boundary of the 
     right-of-way, that is necessary--
       ``(i) to comply with applicable electrical standards; or
       ``(ii) to complete an activity described in subsection (b).
       ``(3) Covered facility.--The term `covered facility' means 
     an existing electric transmission or distribution facility or 
     associated infrastructure (including an electrical 
     substation) that is located within a covered area.
       ``(b) Exemptions.--
       ``(1) In general.--Activities to maintain or increase the 
     transmission capacity of the electric grid within a covered 
     area, and any Federal agency action authorizing such 
     activities shall not be considered to be--
       ``(A) a major Federal action for purposes of the National 
     Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.); or
       ``(B) an undertaking for purposes of section 306108 of 
     title 54, United States Code.
       ``(2) Inclusions.--The activities described in paragraph 
     (1) include--
       ``(A) any repair, maintenance, replacement, upgrade, 
     rebuilding, modification, optimization, minor relocation of, 
     or addition to, a covered facility, including--
       ``(i) reconductoring;
       ``(ii) an increase in operating voltage;
       ``(iii) a circuit addition or adjustment;
       ``(iv) installation of advanced transmission technologies 
     (as defined in section 1223(a) of the Energy Policy Act of 
     2005 (42 U.S.C. 16422(a))); and
       ``(v) installation of grid-enhancing advanced transmission 
     technologies (as defined in section 232(a));
       ``(B) any minimum widening of an existing right-of-way 
     associated with an activity described in subparagraph (A) 
     that--
       ``(i) is necessary to comply with applicable electrical 
     standards; or
       ``(ii) remains within a covered area;
       ``(C) an addition of energy storage at or near a covered 
     facility; and
       ``(D) the addition of a parallel circuit within an existing 
     right-of-way.''.
       (b) Interstate Natural Gas Pipeline Modernization Within 
     Existing Corridors.--Section 7 of the Natural Gas Act (15 
     U.S.C. 717f) is amended by adding at the end the following:
       ``(i) Covered Pipeline Activities.--
       ``(1) Definitions.--In this subsection:
       ``(A) Covered activity.--The term `covered activity' means 
     any repair, maintenance, replacement, upgrade, rebuilding, 
     modification, optimization, minor relocation, looping, 
     compression, capacity-enhancement project, or addition to a 
     covered facility.
       ``(B) Covered area.--The term `covered area' means--
       ``(i) an existing right-of-way; and
       ``(ii) the footprint of original construction previously 
     authorized and environmentally reviewed under the National 
     Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.), 
     together with any minimum additional area adjacent to an 
     existing right-of-way, provided that the total additional 
     area is not more than \1/4\ mile from the boundary of the 
     right-of-way, that is necessary to complete a covered 
     activity.
       ``(C) Covered facility.--The term `covered facility' means 
     an existing interstate natural gas pipeline or associated 
     infrastructure, including a compressor station, meter 
     station, regulator station, valve site, launcher, receiver, 
     storage-related facility, or similar facility located within 
     a covered area.
       ``(2) Environmental review.--A covered activity and any 
     Federal agency action authorizing that covered activity--
       ``(A) shall not be considered a major Federal action under 
     the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 U.S.C. 4321 
     et seq.) if the covered activity remains within a covered 
     area; and

[[Page S5662]]

       ``(B) shall not be considered an undertaking for purposes 
     of section 306108 of title 54, United States Code, if the 
     covered activity remains within a covered area.
       ``(3) Rule of construction.--Nothing in this subsection 
     affects the applicability of any requirement under--
       ``(A) the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 
     1251 et seq.) (commonly known as the `Clean Water Act');
       ``(B) the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 et seq.); or
       ``(C) any other applicable Federal law not expressly 
     addressed by this subsection.''.
```
