# Text of Senate Amendment 6937

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 6937. Mr. PADILLA (for Mr. Schiff) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 141 of the amendment, strike line 12 and 
     all that follows through page 142, line 2, and insert the 
     following:
       ``(A) mean all relevant scientific and commercial 
     information, including traditional Tribal ecological 
     knowledge, available at the time of the agency action; and
       ``(B) include data, analyses, conceptual and numerical 
     models, and model results that--
       ``(i) account for known or potential sources of error; and
       ``(ii) are applied--

       ``(I) using accepted principles, methods, tools, and 
     professional standards of practice; and
       ``(II) consistent with the goals and purposes of this 
     Act.''; and

                                 ______
```
