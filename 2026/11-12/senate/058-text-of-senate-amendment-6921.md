# Text of Senate Amendment 6921

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 6921. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for 
herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 
2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross 
income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of 
any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 18, line 7, insert ``, excluding financial 
     assistance provided for the construction of a data center'' 
     after ``action''.
       On page 20, line 25, insert ``(excluding lifeline 
     infrastructure that directly ties to a data center)'' after 
     ``20 percent''.
       On page 136, between lines 2 and 3, insert the following:
       (c) Permits for Dredged or Fill Material.--Notwithstanding 
     paragraph (3)(B) of section 404(e) of the Federal Water 
     Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1344(e)) (as added by 
     subsection (b)(3)), the Secretary of the Army, acting through 
     the Chief of Engineers, may only consider a discharge of 
     dredged or fill material with respect to a data center to be 
     a minimal adverse environmental effect if the discharge is 
     into less than \1/2\ acre of navigable waters.
       On page 202, between lines 19 and 20, insert the following:

     SEC. 1404. DATA CENTER AND ASSOCIATED INFRASTRUCTURE 
                   EXCLUSION.

       Sections 1401 through 1403, and section 3(18)(B) of the 
     National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (as amended by 
     section 1102(a)), shall not apply to--
       (1) any Federal authorization or permit, lease, or other 
     Federal approval for a data center or associated 
     infrastructure; or
       (2) any action by a Federal department or agency with 
     respect to a data center or associated infrastructure.
       On page 235, between lines 3 and 4, insert the following:

     SEC. 1603. CLEAN AIR ACT REGULATION.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a gas turbine 
     used as a power source for a data center shall be considered 
     a stationary source under the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 
     et seq.).

     system facility; qualifying BTM DES facility.--
       ``(A) In general.--The term `qualifying behind-the-meter 
     distributed energy system facility' or `qualifying BTM DES 
     facility' means a facility that--
       ``(i) is an energy storage system (including a battery 
     energy storage system), a fuel cell system, a generator, or 
     other resource--

       ``(I) that can produce electricity within 15 minutes; and
       ``(II) the output of which can be responsive to the signal 
     of the system operator;

       ``(ii) is located behind the meter of a retail customer of 
     a distribution utility (as defined in section 217(a));
       ``(iii) has a rated capacity of not less than 5 kilowatts 
     and not more than 5 megawatts; and
       ``(iv) maintains metering and telemetry capabilities that--

       ``(I) are sufficient to dispatch the facility, account for 
     withdrawals from and injections to the grid, and communicate 
     all relevant operational data from the facility; and
       ``(II) are not required to include equipment owned or 
     installed by the distribution utility (as so defined), and 
     may include the use of device-level equipment, including 
     inverters.

       ``(B) Gas generators.--For purposes of subparagraph 
     (A)(iii), all gas generators located behind the meter of a 
     single retail customer described in subparagraph (A)(ii) 
     shall be considered to be a single resource the rated 
     capacity of which consists of the total aggregated rated 
     capacity of those generators.
```
