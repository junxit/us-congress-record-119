# Text of Senate Amendment 6931

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6931. Mr. BOOKER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, 
Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       In section 1401(b)(4), strike subparagraph (F) and insert 
     the following:
       (F) Source of funds.--
       (i) In general.--Amounts awarded under subparagraph (D)--

       (I) shall be paid from the fund described in section 1304 
     of title 31, United States Code; and
       (II) shall not be subject to the availability of 
     appropriations.

       (ii) Prohibition of use of federal funds.--No Federal 
     funds, including amounts from the fund described in section 
     1304 of title 31, United States Code, may be used to pay for 
     a settlement of a claim brought under this paragraph.
                                 ______
```
