# Text of Senate Amendment 6926

**Speaking:** Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

```
  SA 6926. Mr. BENNET (for Mr. Lujan (for himself and Mr. Bennet)) 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6883 
proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. 
Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the bill H.R. 2347, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, 
other than punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 456, strike lines 1 through 6 and insert the 
     following:
       ``(i) any visual, atmospheric, or audible element, unless 
     the visual, atmospheric, or audible element would have--

       ``(I) a direct impact on a historic property that would 
     significantly diminish the eligible characteristics of the 
     historic property; or
       ``(II) an indirect impact on a historic property identified 
     by an Indian tribe under subsection (c)(2)(B)(ii);

                                 ______
```
