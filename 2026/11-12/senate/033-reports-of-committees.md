# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 90, S. 140, H.R. 331, S. 465, S. 789, S. 1254, S. 2262, H.R. 2306, S. 2970, S. 3371, S. 3524, S. 3527, S. 4116, S. 4410, S. 4846, S. 4870, S. 5352, S. 5359, H.R. 5729, H.R. 7618

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 90. A bill to prohibit the use of funds by the Secretary 
     of the Interior to finalize and implement certain travel 
     management plans in the State of Utah.
       S. 140. A bill to address the forest health crisis on the 
     National Forest System and public lands, and for other 
     purposes.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, without amendment:
       H.R. 331. An act to amend the Aquifer Recharge Flexibility 
     Act to clarify a provision relating to conveyances for 
     aquifer recharge purposes.
       S. 465. A bill to require the Federal Energy Regulatory 
     Commission to reform the interconnection queue process for 
     the prioritization and approval of certain projects, and for 
     other purposes.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 789. A bill to require reports on critical mineral and 
     rare earth element resources around the world and a strategy 
     for the development of advanced mining, refining, separation, 
     and processing technologies, and for other purposes.
       S. 1254. A bill to amend the Aquifer Recharge Flexibility 
     Act to clarify a provision relating to conveyances for 
     aquifer recharge purposes.
       S. 2262. A bill to amend the Federal Land Policy and 
     Management Act of 1976 to clarify the nature of public 
     investment for purposes of certain rulemaking, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2306. An act to reauthorize the Adams Memorial 
     Commission, and for other purposes.
       S. 2970. A bill to authorize the use of off-highway 
     vehicles in certain areas of the Capitol Reef National Park, 
     Utah.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment:
       S. 3371. A bill to designate the Museum of the Blind 
     People's Movement in Baltimore, Maryland, as the ``National 
     Museum of the Blind People's Movement''.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 3524. A bill to amend title 54, United States Code, to 
     modify certain cost-sharing requirements for grant programs 
     under the American Battlefield Protection Program, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 3527. A bill to release from wilderness study area 
     designation certain land in the State of Montana, to improve 
     the management of that land, and for other purposes.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment:
       S. 4116. A bill to advance next-generation geothermal 
     electricity generation demonstration projects in new regions, 
     and for other purposes.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, without amendment:
       S. 4410. A bill to amend the Mineral Leasing Act to provide 
     for the payment of bonus payments of certain coal leases 
     issued under that Act.
       S. 4846. A bill to amend the United States-Mexico 
     Transboundary Aquifer Assessment Act to reauthorize the 
     United States-Mexico transboundary aquifer assessment 
     program, and for other purposes.

       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 4870. A bill to amend the Infrastructure Investment and 
     Jobs Act to reauthorize the Earth Mapping Resources 
     Initiative, and for other purposes.
       S. 5352. A bill to amend the Water Infrastructure 
     Improvements for the Nation Act and the Water Desalination 
     Act of 1996 to reauthorize certain desalination programs, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. 5359. A bill to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to 
     convey to Pennington County, South Dakota, certain National 
     Forest System land.
       By Mr. LEE, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, without amendment:
       H.R. 5729. An act to authorize direct emergency acquisition 
     flexibilities for the National Park Service for the purpose 
     of retaining, vetting, approving, and expediting contractor 
     approval for the clearing, rebuilding, maintenance, and 
     improvement of the Grand Canyon grounds that have been 
     impacted by the Dragon Bravo Fire.
       H.R. 7618. An act to amend title 54, United States Code, to

     under the American Battlefield Protection Program, and for
```
