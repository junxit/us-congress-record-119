# Text of Senate Amendment 5107

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
  SA 5107. Mr. DURBIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 33, setting forth the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2026 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal 
years 2027 through 2035; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the end of title III, add the following:

     SEC. 3___. DEFICIT-NEUTRAL RESERVE FUND RELATING TO 
                   PREVENTING CRYPTOCURRENCY ATM FRAUD.

       The Chairman of the Committee on the Budget of the Senate 
     may revise the allocations of a committee or committees, 
     aggregates, and other appropriate levels in this resolution, 
     and make adjustments to the pay-as-you-go ledger, for one or 
     more bills, joint resolutions, amendments, amendments between 
     the Houses, motions, or conference reports relating to 
     preventing fraud at virtual currency kiosks and provide law 
     enforcement the tools to crack down on virtual currency kiosk 
     scams by requiring virtual currency kiosk operators to 
     register virtual currency kiosks with the Financial Crimes 
     Enforcement Network of the Department of Treasury, provide 
     disclosures and fraud warning on all virtual currency kiosks, 
     provide consumer acknowledgement of disclosures on all 
     virtual currency kiosks, appoint a compliance officer, 
     provide print receipts at all virtual currency kiosks, use

[[Page S1992]]

     blockchain analytics, refund the full transaction for new 
     customers and fees for existing customers for any 
     fraudulently induced transaction, impose daily transaction 
     limits of $1,000 for new customers and $2,000 for existing 
     customers, provide live customer service, and communicate 
     with law enforcement about fraudulent transactions, by the 
     amounts provided in such legislation for those purposes, 
     provided that such legislation would not increase the deficit 
     over the period of the total of fiscal years 2026 through 
     2035.
                                 ______
```
