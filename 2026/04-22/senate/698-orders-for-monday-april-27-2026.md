# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 27, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 27, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, April 27; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed and the Senate proceed to executive 
session and resume consideration of Calendar No. 670, Robert Cekada; 
further, that the cloture motion with respect to the nomination ripen 
at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
