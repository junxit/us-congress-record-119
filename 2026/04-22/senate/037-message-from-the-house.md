# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 1020, H.R. 2319, H.R. 3419, H.R. 5201

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:18 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, without amendment:

       S. 1020. An act to require the Federal Energy Regulatory 
     Commission to extend the time period during which licensees 
     are required to commence construction of certain hydropower 
     projects.

  The message also announced that the House has passed the following 
bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 2319. An act to direct the Secretary of Health and 
     Human Services to conduct a review to evaluate the status of 
     research on lung cancer in women and underserved populations, 
     and for other purposes.
       H..R. 2493. An act to reauthorize certain programs 
     regarding rural health care.
       H.R. 3419. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     reauthorize the telehealth network and telehealth resource 
     centers grant programs.
       H.R. 5201. An act to direct the Federal Communications 
     Commission to publish a report on implementation of the 
     Kari's Law Act of 2017, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
