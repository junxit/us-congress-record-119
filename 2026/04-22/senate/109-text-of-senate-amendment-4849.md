# Text of Senate Amendment 4849

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
  SA 4849. Mrs. SHAHEEN (for herself, Ms. Hassan, Mr. Kaine, Mr. Kelly, 
Mr. Gallego, and Mr. Welch) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 33, setting 
forth the congressional budget for the United States Government for 
fiscal year 2026 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for 
fiscal years 2027 through 2035; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       At the end of title III, add the following:

     SEC. 3003. DEFICIT-NEUTRAL RESERVE FUND RELATING TO LIMITING 
                   THE ESTABLISHMENT OF NEW U.S. IMMIGRATION AND 
                   CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT FACILITIES WITHOUT STATE 
                   AND LOCAL CONSENT.

       The Chairman of the Committee on the Budget of the Senate 
     may revise the allocations of a committee or committees, 
     aggregates, and other appropriate levels in this resolution, 
     and make adjustments to the pay-as-you-go ledger, for one or 
     more bills, joint resolutions, amendments, amendments between 
     the Houses, motions, or conference reports relating to 
     limiting the establishment of new U.S. Immigration and 
     Customs Enforcement detention or processing facilities 
     without State and local consent, which may include initiating 
     a public comment period, requiring a signed written agreement 
     with appropriate local government officials and the Governor 
     of the State in which such new facility will be located, and 
     issuing advanced congressional notification, by the amounts 
     provided in such legislation for those purposes, provided 
     that such legislation would not increase the deficit over 
     either the period of the total of fiscal years 2026 through 
     2030 or the period of the total of fiscal years 2026 through 
     2035.
                                 ______
```
