# Text of Senate Amendment 5052

**Speaking:** Lujan, Ben Ray (L000570) [D-NM]

```
  SA 5052. Mr. LUJAN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 33, setting forth the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2026 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal 
years 2027 through 2035; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the end of title III, add the following:

     SEC. 3___. DEFICIT-NEUTRAL RESERVE FUND RELATING TO HELPING 
                   LOW-INCOME AND MIDDLE-INCOME HOUSEHOLDS SAVE ON 
                   ENERGY EFFICIENCY IMPROVEMENTS.

       The Chairman of the Committee on the Budget of the Senate 
     may revise the allocations of a committee or committees, 
     aggregates, and other appropriate levels in this resolution, 
     and make adjustments to the pay-as-you-go ledger, for one or 
     more bills, joint resolutions, amendments, amendments between 
     the Houses, motions, or conference reports relating to 
     providing rebates, grants, or low-interest financing to help 
     low- and middle-income households, including in rural, 
     Tribal, and underserved communities, access energy efficiency 
     upgrades, including weatherization, heating, ventilation, and 
     air conditioning replacement, insulation, and efficient 
     appliances, to reduce energy burden and lower utility costs, 
     which rebates, grants, or low-interest financing shall be 
     provided in complement to existing Federal programs such as 
     the weatherization assistance program, by the amounts 
     provided in such legislation for those purposes, provided 
     that such legislation would not increase the deficit over the 
     period of the total of fiscal years 2026 through 2035.
                                 ______
```
