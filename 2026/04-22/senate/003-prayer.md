# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, this day our hearts rise up to meet You, and our lips 
extol Your wonderful works in the Earth. Great is Your faithfulness. 
Today, may Your peace go with our lawmakers wherever You lead them, 
enabling them to feel the wonder of Your sacred presence, power, and 
peace. Lord, give them the wisdom to find true life in serving others, 
becoming Your representatives to our Nation and world. Inspire the 
hearts of our Senators to obey Your precepts as they discover the 
delight of doing Your will. Help them to see that their well-being is 
inextricably bound to the well-being of their neighbors. Empower them 
to share compassion, patience, and courage.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
