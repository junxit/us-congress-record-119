                  TRIBUTE TO DuSHUN L. SCARBROUGH, SR.

● Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding Arkansan, DuShun L. Scarbrough, Sr., who recently received 
two distinguished national honors that reflect his unwavering 
commitment to service, leadership, and community empowerment.
  We were tremendously proud to learn Scarbrough had earned the 2026 
Beloved Community Civic Leadership Award by The King Center, a 
nonprofit steward of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr.'s legacy led by Dr. 
Bernice A. King. This accolade honors individuals whose work reflects a 
deep commitment to advancing justice, fostering unity, and serving 
others in the footsteps of MLK Jr.
  Also this year, he became only the second Arkansan to receive the 
Freedom Flame Award from the organizers of the Selma Bridge Crossing 
Jubilee, an annual event that commemorates the legacy of the Selma to 
Montgomery voting rights marches. This distinction celebrates leaders 
who carry forward the enduring spirit of courage, sacrifice, and 
meaningful social change.
  These recognitions are a testament to Scarbrough's lifelong 
dedication to uplifting others. Raised in Little Rock by his 
grandmother and aunts, he chose a path of purpose and perseverance, 
becoming a positive force in his community and an advocate for 
opportunity and inclusion. He graduated from Philander Smith College 
and pursued further education before returning to Arkansas, yet has 
remained steadfast in advancing the ideals championed by Dr. King while 
ensuring that personal, professional, and civic opportunity remain 
accessible to all people, regardless of race, creed, color, or 
background.
  Scarbrough's service as the executive director of the Arkansas Martin 
Luther King Jr. Commission has consistently reflected his belief in 
this mission and a dedication to uplift and invest in his community, as 
well as our entire State. His efforts have activated others, encouraged 
new ideas and inspired the next generation to make a lasting impact. In 
fact, thanks to his vision and guidance, The Natural State now hosts 
the largest service day nationwide in honor of the life of MLK Jr.
  Scarbrough follows in the footsteps of exceptional Arkansans, 
including Dr. Maya Angelou, whose legacies are hallmarks of progress 
and positive leadership that shape countless lives.
  DuShun Scarbrough, Sr.'s story is one of resilience, faith, and 
determination. His message is simple but powerful: work hard, stay 
focused, and do not be afraid to create new paths where none exist. I 
commend all his extraordinary achievements and the example he sets. 
Arkansas is proud of his many contributions, and our Nation is stronger 
because of his service.●
