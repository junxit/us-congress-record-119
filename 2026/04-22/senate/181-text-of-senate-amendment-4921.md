# Text of Senate Amendment 4921

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

```
  SA 4921. Ms. HIRONO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
her to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 33, setting forth the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2026 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal 
years 2027 through 2035; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the end of title III, add the following:

     SEC. 3___. DEFICIT-NEUTRAL RESERVE FUND RELATING TO ENSURING 
                   ROBUST FUNDING AND FOOD SUPPLY FOR FOOD BANKS 
                   AND PROTECTING FUNDING USED TO PURCHASE FOOD 
                   FOR DISTRIBUTION DURING EMERGENCIES.

       The Chairman of the Committee on the Budget of the Senate 
     may revise the allocations of a committee or committees, 
     aggregates, and other appropriate levels in this

[[Page S1967]]

     resolution, and make adjustments to the pay-as-you-go ledger, 
     for one or more bills, joint resolutions, amendments, 
     amendments between the Houses, motions, or conference reports 
     relating to ensuring food banks have robust funding and food 
     supply and protecting funding used to purchase food for 
     distribution during emergencies by the amounts provided in 
     such legislation for those purposes, provided that such 
     legislation would not increase the deficit over the period of 
     the total of fiscal years 2026 through 2035.
                                 ______
```
