# Text of Senate Amendment 5138

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

```
  SA 5138. Mr. WHITEHOUSE submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 33, setting 
forth the congressional budget for the United States Government for 
fiscal year 2026 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for 
fiscal years 2027 through 2035; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

               TITLE V--POLICY STATEMENTS IN BOTH HOUSES

     SEC. 5001. POLICY STATEMENT REGARDING THE INVESTIGATION OF 
                   THREATS AGAINST FEDERAL JUDGES AND THEIR 
                   FAMILIES.

       It is the policy of this concurrent resolution that no 
     Federal funds be obligated or expended by the Department of 
     Justice until the Attorney General of the United States 
     provides to Congress a written certification confirming that 
     the Department of Justice has ongoing investigations of 
     threats against Federal judges and the family members of 
     Federal judges for evidence of coordination that may 
     constitute a criminal conspiracy, enterprise, or 
     solicitation.
                                 ______
```
