# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 670, Robert Cekada, of Florida, to 
     be Director, Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and 
     Explosives.
         John Thune, Tom Cotton, Tim Sheehy, John Barrasso, Jon A. 
           Husted, Tommy Tuberville, Jim Justice, Cindy Hyde-
           Smith, Roger F. Wicker, Mike Rounds, Steve Daines, 
           Marsha Blackburn, David McCormick, John Cornyn, Thom 
           Tillis, James Lankford, John R. Curtis.

                          ____________________
```
