# Text of Senate Amendment 5298

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 5298. Ms. CANTWELL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 33, setting forth the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2026 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal 
years 2027 through 2035; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place in title IV, insert the following:

     SEC. 41__. CONGRESSIONAL BUDGET OFFICE STUDY ON TRANSPARENCY 
                   OF SIZE OF ICE AND CBP FUNDING IN THE 119TH 
                   CONGRESS.

       Not later than 30 days after the date on which this 
     resolution is agreed to, the Congressional Budget Office 
     shall submit to Congress, and make publicly available, a 
     report that makes the amounts made available for U.S. 
     Immigration and Customs Enforcement and U.S. Customs and 
     Border Protection during the 119th Congress easier to 
     understand and communicate by equating such amounts to the 
     number of days programs funded by the Highway Trust Fund 
     could be funded if an equal amount of funding were deposited 
     in the Highway Trust Fund.
                                 ______
```
