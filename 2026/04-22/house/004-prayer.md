# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Make us mindful, O Lord, of Your presence in this place and in every 
corner of our lives. And if, in discovering You in our midst, we find 
ourselves apprehensive or ashamed, remind us of Your love that allows 
us to approach You just as we are, with all our faults and foibles. 
Your perfect love casts out all reason to fear You. Make us perfect in 
Your love.
  At the same time, let us not confuse Your favor as blanket approval 
for anything we may be inclined to say or do. As righteous as we might 
think we are, may we hold on to a healthy fear of the standard of 
holiness by which You measure us.
  In this fear may we find wisdom--a wisdom that acknowledges our utter 
dependence on Your grace and guidance in the time You have given us, 
and our responsibility for the authority You have entrusted to us.
  Grant us, then, the knowledge that leads to insight, that the 
effectiveness of this day will be multiplied by Your counsel, and the 
work we set out to do would reveal Your merciful hand in our lives.
  In the boldness You grant us to call on Your name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
