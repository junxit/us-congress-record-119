# CELEBRATING 25 YEARS OF SALINA PREGNANCY SERVICE CENTER

**Speaking:** Mann, Tracey (M000871) [R-KS]

```
        CELEBRATING 25 YEARS OF SALINA PREGNANCY SERVICE CENTER

  (Mr. MANN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MANN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a meaningful 
milestone in my hometown of Salina, Kansas: the 25th anniversary of the 
Salina Pregnancy Service Center.
  For 25 years, this organization has served women, families, and 
unborn children across our community with compassion, conviction, and 
care. As someone who is pro-life and who believes that life begins at 
conception, I am grateful for organizations like PSC that put those 
beliefs into action every day.
  The Salina Pregnancy Service Center provides life-affirming medical 
care, education, and counseling. They walk alongside mothers with 
compassion, offering real support that meets both physical and 
emotional needs. Just as important, they offer hope and guidance rooted 
in faith, reminding every person who walks through their doors that 
they are not alone.
  Their mission is clear. They offer the love of Christ to those facing 
a pregnancy decision while helping promote decisions that strengthen 
Kansas families.
  Congratulations on 25 years of service. I look forward to seeing your 
continued impact in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
