# RECOGNIZING Z'S PIZZA & DINER

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING Z'S PIZZA & DINER

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Z's 
Pizza & Diner of Bremer County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Founded in early 1972, Z's Pizza & Diner of Waverly, IA, which was 
formerly named Z's Restaurant & Lounge, is a family-owned and family-
style restaurant built on hard work and commitment to quality. For more 
than five decades, Z's Pizza has served as a gathering place for 
families, friends, and neighbors, earning a reputation for outstanding 
hospitality and made-from-scratch food. Z's is especially known for its 
handcrafted pizzas, featuring dough and sauce recipes passed down 
through generations, including an Iowa staple: taco pizza. With a 
dedicated team of 23 employees, the restaurant continues to play an 
important role in Waverly's local economy.
  Christopher and Joy Carney, the current owners of Z's Pizza & Diner, 
purchased the restaurant from Christopher's uncle Dean Carney in 2018. 
Since then, they have worked diligently to preserve the restaurant's 
long-standing traditions while thoughtfully guiding it through new 
challenges and opportunities. During the pandemic, the Carneys made the 
difficult decision to relocate from its longtime Waterloo location to 
its current home in downtown Waverly. After 47 years in Waterloo, Z's 
proudly reopened in Waverly in 2022, marking a new chapter in its 
history. Their leadership, resilience, and commitment to customer 
service ensured that Z's remains a trusted and cherished cornerstone of 
the community.
  Since opening at its current location, Z's has continued to grow. In 
2025, the business was awarded Main Street Iowa's Open 4 Business 
Grant, which supports small businesses investing in new equipment, 
technology, and expanded services to better meet customers' needs. In 
addition to its restaurant operations, Z's also offers catering 
services for family gatherings, corporate events, and community 
celebrations, further extending its impact throughout Bremer County.
  Z's Pizza is a proud member of the Waverly Chamber of Commerce and 
the Main Street Iowa Program and remains deeply committed to giving 
back. In addition to launching a charitable initiative offering free 
meal coupons for individuals experiencing food insecurity, the 
restaurant regularly hosts and supports community events and gatherings 
to bring neighbors together. Through accessible online ordering and 
catering services, Z's Pizza continues to serve local families and 
organizations, reflecting the Carney family's belief that small 
businesses have a responsibility to care for their neighbors and 
strengthen the community they call home.
  It is my honor to recognize Christopher and Joy Carney and the entire 
Z's Pizza & Diner team for their outstanding work and dedication to 
their community. I look forward to their continued success and wish 
them the very best in the years ahead.●
```
