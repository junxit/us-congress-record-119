# TRIBUTE TO KENT EIKENBERRY

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       TRIBUTE TO KENT EIKENBERRY

● Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. President, I rise today to honor and 
recognize the distinguished service of Kent Eikenberry, president and 
chief executive officer of the Northwest Arkansas Food Bank, upon his 
retirement after a decade of exemplary leadership.
  Kent has been a driving force behind the food bank's mission to fight 
hunger and food insecurity throughout Arkansas. Under his guidance, the 
organization strengthened partnerships with local farmers, businesses, 
faith communities, and volunteers, expanding its reach and improving 
its ability to deliver nutritious food to those in need. His steady 
hand and thoughtful vision helped the food bank grow into a cornerstone 
of support for families facing difficult times.
  Arkansas is stronger because of Kent's service. His example reminds 
us of the difference one individual can make in a community. As he 
begins a well-earned retirement, the impact of his leadership will 
endure for years to come in the lives of those he helped and his 
tireless effort to grow the food bank.
  I am grateful for Kent's friendship and dedication to advocating for 
greater food security. On behalf of all Arkansans, I extend my deepest 
gratitude to Kent Eikenberry and his wife Diana for their lasting 
contributions and wish their family every happiness in the years 
ahead.●
```
