# RECOGNIZING THE COFFEE GROUNDS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING THE COFFEE GROUNDS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor The 
Coffee Grounds of Monroe County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of 
the Week.
  Founded in October 2023, The Coffee Grounds is a family-owned, 
veteran-owned small business created to serve the Albia community. For 
years, residents shared that they wanted a convenient place to grab a 
quality cup of coffee early in the morning on their way to work. That 
message was echoed through Main Street Albia and within the Chamber of 
Commerce, where community members consistently expressed hope for a 
drive-through coffee option. Listening closely to their neighbors, two 
local couples, Tyler and Jenny Boley and Wes and Randell Aeschliman, 
came together to turn that dream into a reality for Monroe County.
  Today, The Coffee Grounds operates as both a drive-through and walk-
in coffee shop, opening early every day of the week to serve customers 
before the workday begins. The business offers a wide variety of 
beverages and treats, including coffee, espresso drinks, teas, energy 
drinks, smoothies, lemonades, and sweet breakfast items. The Coffee 
Grounds proudly sources its espresso and coffee beans from producers in 
Iowa and features baked goods prepared fresh by local Iowa bakers. With 
a team of seven employees, The Coffee Grounds has quickly become a 
daily stop for commuters, families, and neighbors, while contributing 
to the local economy of Monroe County.
  The owners of The Coffee Grounds are deeply invested in their 
community and exemplify the spirit of local leadership. Tyler Boley is 
a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and serves as president of Welcome Home 
Soldier, an organization dedicated to honoring veterans and supporting 
military families. All four owners are actively involved in 
volunteering with organizations such as the Chamber of Commerce, Main 
Street Albia, youth sports programs, the volunteer fire department, and 
the Rotary Club, where Wes serves as president. Beyond the coffee shop, 
the owners also operate additional local businesses, including 
insurance and real estate enterprises. Through these efforts, they 
continue to invest in the long-term vitality of Main Street Albia while 
preserving the character of their community and creating new 
opportunities for residents.
  In just a short time, The Coffee Grounds has become more than a place 
to grab a cup of coffee, it has become a gathering point that reflects 
the values of service and community connection. By listening to their 
neighbors and investing back into Albia, the owners have strengthened 
Main Street and demonstrated the lasting impact small businesses can 
have in rural communities.
  I am pleased to recognize the Boleys, the Aeschlimans, and the entire 
team at The Coffee Grounds for their dedication to Monroe County. I 
look forward to their continued success and wish them the very best in 
the years ahead.●

                          ____________________
```
