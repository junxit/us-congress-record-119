# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Lord, our Redeemer, abide with our Senators through the passing 
hours of another day. Give them an optimism, unity, and purpose so that 
they will not forget how You enabled them to surmount obstacles in the 
past. Lord, strengthen them to stand firm for those good and eternal 
values that keep a nation strong. Remind them that You are the pilot of 
their lives who can guide them to a desired destination.
  Let discretion, understanding, and wisdom protect them from the 
forces of evil. Save them from becoming legends in their own minds as 
You keep them humble enough to see their need of You.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
