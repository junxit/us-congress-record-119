# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 28, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 7148

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 28, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 11 a.m. 
on Wednesday, January 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume the 
motion to proceed to Calendar No. 302, H.R. 7148; further, that the 
Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly 
conference meetings.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
