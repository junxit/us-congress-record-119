# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, we thank You for all who serve in the House of 
Representatives and for their willingness to offer themselves in 
service and leadership to our country. Continue to grace them with 
discerning minds, humble hearts, and composed spirits as they work 
together for the good of the American people. Sustain them in their 
service, bless their families and staffs, and keep our Nation under 
Your watchful care.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
