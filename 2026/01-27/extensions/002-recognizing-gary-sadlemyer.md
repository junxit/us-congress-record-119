# RECOGNIZING GARY SADLEMYER

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                       RECOGNIZING GARY SADLEMYER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE FLOOD

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 27, 2026

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a pillar of 
Nebraska broadcasting, Gary Sadlemyer, who, after nearly five decades 
behind the microphone at KFAB News Radio, has officially retired.
  Since first stepping into KFAB's studio in 1976, Gary's signature 
voice has been trusted by generations of listeners.
  From morning news to Nebraska football broadcasts, Gary's 
professionalism, wit, and warmth made him not just a radio host, but a 
friend to his audiences.
  Gary's commitment to excellence earned him induction into the 
Nebraska Broadcasters Association's Hall of Fame in 2011. Simply put: 
Gary's impact on the people of Nebraska will endure long after his 
final sign-off.
  As he steps into this next chapter, Gary will enjoy more time with 
his kids and grandkids.
  Today, I join Nebraskans everywhere in thanking Gary Sadlemyer for 
his decades of commitment to our state as a superb broadcaster and I 
wish him all the best in his well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
