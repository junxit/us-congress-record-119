# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, we ask that You would shower our Senators today with 
Your bountiful blessings. Without Your guidance, we are all like ships 
without rudders, tossed by every wind of opinion and circumstance, but 
when You are our captain and counselor, You establish our course and 
enable us to accomplish Your good purposes.
  Incline the hearts of our lawmakers toward that which promotes peace, 
liberty, and goodness. Quiet every voice that would lead us away from 
unity. Inspire our legislators with genuine compassion, steadfast 
courage, and servant-hearted leadership.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
