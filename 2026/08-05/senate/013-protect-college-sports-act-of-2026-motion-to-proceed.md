# PROTECT COLLEGE SPORTS ACT OF 2026--Motion to Proceed

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 4668

```
         PROTECT COLLEGE SPORTS ACT OF 2026--Motion to Proceed

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 449, S. 
4668.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 449, S. 4668, a bill to 
     protect the name, image, and likeness rights of, and provide

     competition among intercollegiate athletics, and for other 
     purposes.

motion.

                             Cloture Motion

     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate,

     proceed to Calendar No. 449, S. 4668, a bill to protect the 
     name, image, and likeness rights of, and provide protections 
     for, student athletes and to promote fair competition among 
     intercollegiate athletics, and for other purposes.
         John Thune, Ted Cruz, Cynthia M. Lummis, Eric Schmitt,
```
