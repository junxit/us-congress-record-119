# Trump Administration (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                          Trump Administration

  Mr. President, now Trump is going to Nevada today to tout his 
disastrous economic record. His going is an insult to every Nevadan 
reeling from higher costs.
  If there is one place where Americans know how to spot a losing hand, 
it is Las Vegas, Donald Trump. They see your losing hand. Nevadans know 
better than anyone that Trump's economy has gone bust. Does Trump want 
to brag about how Nevadans are paying 4 bucks and 80 cents for a gallon 
of gasoline because of his war? Will Trump tout how more than 36,000 
Nevadans have lost their health coverage because Trump cut a trillion 
dollars from our healthcare system to give billionaires another tax 
break? Nope.
  While grocery prices continue rising, will Trump explain to the more 
than 78,000 hungry Nevadans who have lost food assistance that his 
policies are working exactly as intended? Or maybe Trump wants to thump 
his chest about the $3,100 extra Nevadans are spending because of 
Trump's failed policies that have wiped out any extra tax refund they 
saw this year.

  Trump can't bluff his way out of the economic disaster he has 
unleashed on the American people. Nope. He can have all the bluff and 
bravado and lies he wants. It is not going to wash because people are 
experiencing the hurt that Trump has inflicted on them.
```
