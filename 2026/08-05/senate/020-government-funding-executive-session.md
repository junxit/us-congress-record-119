# Government Funding (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           Government Funding

  Mr. President, on the CR, Senate Democrats have been unequivocal: We 
want to work across the aisle to keep the government open. The CR gives 
us the maneuvering room we need to hash out a budget that makes life 
more affordable for the American people and reins in the Trump 
administration's chaos and corruption.
  Throughout these negotiations, Senate Democrats are fighting for 
working families. And thanks to Senator Patty Murray's efforts, we have 
fended off Trump's harmful effort to hold hostage Federal grants that 
would support communities stretching from one end of the Nation to the 
other.
  Trump has this amazing philosophy: If you are a red State, you 
deserve the taxpayers' money. If you are a blue State, you don't. And 
he totally skews our grant-giving to help his friends and to punish his 
enemies when it has no basis in law, fact, justice, fairness.
  The American people are counting on Congress to come together and 
deliver, and that is what Senate Democrats are fighting to do every 
day.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
