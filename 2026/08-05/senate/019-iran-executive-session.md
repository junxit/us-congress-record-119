# Iran (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  Iran

  Mr. President, on Iran, Trump seems to think that opening his mouth 
and opening the Strait of Hormuz are the same thing. Once again, Trump 
is making big claims that a deal to reopen the strait is imminent. How 
many times have we heard that?
  Whenever Trump says there is a deal, it means one of two things: 
Either the strait is still staying closed or it opens for a couple of 
days and then it closes again. None of his deals have come to fruition, 
and gas prices continue to rise.
  At this time, there are rumors he is willing to give up even more 
control of the strait to Iran to open. If that is true, Donald Trump 
has put himself in such a difficult position in Iran, blundering his 
way into a war with no goal, with no preparation, with no future.
  He is stuck. He is going to try to get out of it, but mark my words, 
he will surrender to Iran, in a sense. He will have to sue for peace, 
and the Iranians will emerge stronger and America weaker. It is his 
only way out. Once again, Trump is doing all of these things.
  Which one will it be this time, Donald, when it comes to the strait? 
How many days after you say the strait will open up will it close 
again? When are you going to get out of the way and let real diplomats 
negotiate an end to the war that ensures the free flow of shipping 
through the strait?
  Americans can't fuel their cars on wishful thinking and empty 
promises, and Senate Democrats have now forced 13 votes on the Iran War 
Powers Resolutions to end Trump's disastrous war. We will keep forcing 
votes as long as it takes until Senate Republicans help us bring our 
troops home, end this war, and bring costs down.
```
