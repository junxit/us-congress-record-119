# Nomination of Todd Blanche (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       Nomination of Todd Blanche

  Now, Mr. President, on a separate matter, Senate Republicans are 
confirming individuals who will keep the American people safe.
  Last week, I met with Todd Blanche. He has been nominated to be the 
next Attorney General of the United States. I support him. He clearly 
is the right man for the job, and it is time for the Senate to move 
forward and confirm him.
  Todd Blanche has led an impressive legal career. He belongs in this 
job. He brings to the job a decade of experience at the very Department 
of Justice. That includes his current service as the Acting Attorney 
General. Under his leadership, the Department of Justice is focusing 
once again on its core mission, and that is the mission of fighting 
crime and upholding the rule of law.
  Today, Americans are safer. Murder rates have fallen dramatically 
under this administration. So have deaths from drug overdoses. And Mr. 
Blanche has fought forcefully for the safety and security of the 
American people.
  Today, the Department is prosecuting drug cartels. It is dismantling 
gangs of violent criminals. Mr. Blanche has also been a key leader in 
Republicans' war against fraud. He helped establish a new Division in 
the Department of Justice to do exactly just that, and it is already, 
today, succeeding.
  Mr. Blanche knows what it takes to lead the Department of Justice. It 
takes integrity. It takes accountability. And he is going to lead with 
both of those characteristics. That is why his nomination is supported 
by former Department of Justice officials, by so many attorneys general 
from States, and by law enforcement.
  Former Attorney General Bill Barr is one such supporter. He recently 
praised Todd Blanche in an editorial in the Wall Street Journal. He 
said:

       Throughout his career [Todd Blanche] has shown the personal 
     determination, discipline and thick skin [that the] position 
     demands.

  That is exactly right. Todd Blanche is dedicated to the rule of law. 
He is fearless, he is focused, and he is fair. As Attorney General, he 
is going to provide candid advice even when it is not easy. He is going 
to stand up for the United States, and he will keep America safe and 
protect the American people. It is time to confirm him as Attorney 
General.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. PADILLA. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. PADILLA. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to deliver up to 
15 minutes of remarks before we proceed to the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
