# REMEMBERING MICHAEL JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI]

```
                      REMEMBERING MICHAEL JOHNSON

  Ms. BALDWIN. Mr. President, today I rise to recognize the life and 
legacy of Michael Johnson, a respected community leader, a dedicated 
advocate for young people, and a champion for opportunity throughout 
Wisconsin. Mr. Johnson's leadership, vision, and unwavering commitment 
to service transformed countless lives and helped build a stronger 
future for children and families across Dane County. His passing is a 
tremendous loss to the Madison community, Dane County, the State of 
Wisconsin, and to all those fortunate enough to know him.
  Mr. Johnson was raised in Chicago, where he developed an early 
understanding of the challenges facing many young people and families. 
Those experiences shaped a lifelong commitment to creating 
opportunities for others. He believed deeply that every child deserves 
the chance to succeed, regardless of circumstance, and he dedicated his 
career to making that belief a reality.
  Before coming to Wisconsin, Mr. Johnson worked with youth-serving 
organizations in Chicago, Philadelphia, and St. Louis. In 2010, he 
became president and chief executive officer of the Boys & Girls Clubs 
of Dane County. Over the next 16 years, he transformed the organization 
into one of the region's most impactful nonprofit institutions. Under 
his leadership, the Boys & Girls Clubs expanded its reach, strengthened 
partnerships throughout the community, and increased its capacity to 
serve young people and their families.
  Mr. Johnson understood that helping young people succeed required 
more than afterschool programs alone. He championed initiatives focused 
on education, workforce development, mentorship, and career readiness. 
Through partnerships with schools, businesses, and community 
organizations, Mr. Johnson helped create opportunities for students to 
pursue higher education, develop professional skills, and prepare for 
successful futures. Thousands of young people benefited from programs 
and services made possible through his vision and determination.
  Among Mr. Johnson's most significant accomplishments was his vision 
for the McKenzie Regional Workforce Center. Recognizing both the 
growing demand for skilled trades workers and the need to create 
pathways to economic mobility for young people, he led the effort to 
develop a first-of-its-kind workforce training center in Dane County. 
Through years of planning, partnership-building, and fundraising, Mr. 
Johnson transformed an ambitious idea into reality. Today, the center 
provides hands-on training, industry certifications, and career 
preparation in high-demand skilled trades, creating opportunities for 
young people to build family-sustaining careers while helping address 
workforce shortages throughout the region. Mr. Johnson viewed the 
center not simply as a building, but as an investment in the future of 
Wisconsin's youth and a model for how communities can expand 
opportunity through innovation and collaboration.
  Mr. Johnson also understood the importance of ensuring that young 
people see themselves reflected in the history and institutions around 
them. He played a leading role in the effort to establish the Vel 
Phillips Memorial Statue on the grounds of the Wisconsin State Capitol. 
Through his leadership and fundraising efforts, supporters from across 
Wisconsin came together to create a lasting tribute to Vel Phillips, a 
pioneering civil rights leader, jurist, and public servant whose 
groundbreaking achievements opened doors for generations of 
Wisconsinites. The unveiling of the statue marked a historic milestone 
for the State of Wisconsin and reflected Mr. Johnson's belief that 
honoring the accomplishments of trailblazing leaders can inspire future 
generations to pursue lives of service and leadership.
  Mr. Johnson's impact extended far beyond the Boys & Girls Clubs. He 
became one of Madison's most trusted civic leaders and strongest 
advocates for building a more inclusive community. He brought together 
leaders from education, business, government, and nonprofit 
organizations to address challenges facing local families. Whether 
working to expand educational opportunities, strengthen neighborhoods, 
or create pathways to economic success, Mr. Johnson had a unique 
ability to unite people around a common purpose.
  Those who knew Mr. Johnson often spoke of his gift for connecting 
with others. He believed in meeting people where they were and helping 
them realize their potential. He served as a mentor to countless young 
people and emerging leaders, offering encouragement, guidance, and 
support. His leadership was grounded in the belief that meaningful 
change comes from investing in people and empowering them to succeed.
  While Mr. Johnson received many honors throughout his career, his 
greatest achievement was the lasting impact he made on the lives of 
others. The students who graduated, the families who found support, and 
the community leaders he inspired all stand as a testament to his 
life's work. His legacy can be seen throughout Dane County in the 
opportunities he helped create and the futures he helped shape.
  Mr. Johnson was also a devoted husband, father, and family man. He 
carried the same compassion, integrity, and commitment into his 
personal life that he demonstrated in his professional career. His 
family, friends, colleagues, and community will remember him not only 
for what he accomplished, but for the kindness, optimism, and 
generosity he shared with others.
  Mr. Johnson devoted his life to expanding opportunity and 
strengthening communities. His leadership helped countless young people 
imagine a brighter future and gave them the tools to pursue it. As we 
honor his life and mourn his passing, we celebrate a legacy of service 
that will continue to inspire generations to come. I am proud to join 
communities across Wisconsin in recognizing Michael Johnson's 
extraordinary contributions and the enduring legacy of his life's work.

                          ____________________
```
