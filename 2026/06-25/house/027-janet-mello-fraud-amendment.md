# JANET MELLO FRAUD AMENDMENT

**Speaking:** Rose, John W. (R000612) [R-TN]

```
                      JANET MELLO FRAUD AMENDMENT

  (Mr. ROSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. ROSE. Mr. Speaker, I have introduced an amendment to the National 
Defense Authorization Act addressing the massive Janet Mello fraud 
scandal at Fort Sam Houston and the broader pattern of abuse it has 
exposed.
  This amendment simply requires a public, comprehensive report on how 
more than $100 million meant for the 4-H Military Partnership Grant 
program was stolen over several years. What red flags were missed? What 
reforms will prevent this from ever happening again?
  It has already passed the House twice, most recently by a vote of 
430-3, only to be stripped out by the Senate in their infinite wisdom 
for reasons known only to the Almighty. Meanwhile, yet another Fort Sam 
Houston

[[Page H4253]]

official has been charged with stealing over $500,000 from Army family 
programs.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge the Rules Committee to make my amendment in order 
for the third year.

                          ____________________
```
