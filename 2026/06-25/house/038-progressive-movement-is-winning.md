# PROGRESSIVE MOVEMENT IS WINNING

**Speaking:** Khanna, Ro (K000389) [D-CA]

```
                    PROGRESSIVE MOVEMENT IS WINNING

  (Mr. KHANNA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KHANNA. Mr. Speaker, the progressive movement is winning across 
the country, from the heart of New York to Michigan to Maine.
  The people are saying no to foreign wars, and they are saying no to 
genocide in Gaza. They are saying no to the unfair and lopsided economy 
that has allowed a few people to hoard extreme wealth and power, and 
they are saying yes to Medicare for All, to unions for all, to 
childcare for all, to making sure that we have a housing guarantee for 
kids by the time they are 35 if they work hard.
  Now, some of the oligarchs don't like it. Elon Musk has been 
threatening to sue me or jail me, but I will tell you what: They should 
judge us by those who are attacking us.
  We will not be intimidated. We stand for transforming this country 
for the working class over the billionaire class.

                          ____________________
```
