# HONORING GUADALUPE TOWN MANAGER JEFF KULAGA'S PUBLIC SERVICE CAREER

**Speaking:** Ansari, Yassamin (A000381) [D-AZ]

```
  HONORING GUADALUPE TOWN MANAGER JEFF KULAGA'S PUBLIC SERVICE CAREER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. YASSAMIN ANSARI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Ms. ANSARI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Jeff 
Kulaga upon his retirement as Town Manager of the Town of Guadalupe, 
Arizona, and to thank him for more than three decades of distinguished 
public service to communities across our state.
  Since his unanimous appointment as Town Manager and Town Clerk in 
2017, Jeff Kulaga has served the people of Guadalupe with integrity, 
professionalism, and an unwavering commitment to community improvement. 
In addition to overseeing the Town's day-to-day operations and 
implementing Town Council policy, he has taken on numerous 
responsibilities including serving as Guadalupe's Chief Financial 
Officer, Zoning Administrator, Business License Administrator, Cemetery 
Administrator, and Community Development Director. Under his 
leadership, the Town has continued to advance opportunities for 
residents while responsibly managing an annual budget of more than $11 
million.
  Jeff's career in public service spans more than 30 years in Arizona 
municipal government. Prior to his time in Guadalupe, he served as 
Assistant City Manager for the City of Tempe and held multiple 
leadership roles in Mesa and Scottsdale, including Government Relations 
Director and Executive Assistant to the Mayor. Throughout each chapter 
of his career, he has earned the respect of colleagues, elected 
officials, and community members alike for his thoughtful leadership 
and collaborative approach to governance.
  Our office has had the privilege of working closely with Jeff on 
projects that directly improve the quality of life for Guadalupe 
residents. Most notably, we were proud to partner with the Town to help 
secure $2 million for the refurbishment of Biehn Colony Park, an 
investment that will help preserve and revitalize an important 
community space for generations to come. Jeff's advocacy for Guadalupe 
and his ability to bring partners together played an instrumental role 
in advancing this effort.
  A proud ``Double Devil,'' Jeff earned both his Bachelor of Science 
degree and Master's degree in Public Administration from Arizona State 
University. He has remained deeply engaged in civic and professional 
organizations throughout his career, including the Arizona City and 
County Management Association, the Arizona Municipal Clerks 
Association, and the Urban Land Institute.
  Jeff Kulaga's career reflects the very best of public service: steady 
leadership, institutional knowledge, and a deep belief in serving 
others. On behalf of Arizona's 3rd Congressional District, I extend my 
sincere gratitude for his years of service and wish him, his family, 
and loved ones continued success and fulfillment in this next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
