# HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY FIRE & RESCUE DEPARTMENT VALOR AWARD RECIPIENTS

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY FIRE & RESCUE DEPARTMENT VALOR AWARD 
                               RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Prince William County Fire & Rescue Department Valor Award recipients, 
recognizing the extraordinary bravery and selfless service demonstrated 
by the department's firefighters, paramedics, and rescue personnel. 
These awards reflect the courageous actions of the dedicated men and 
women who put themselves in harm's way to protect and serve the 
community in its most critical moments.
  As we mark the 40th anniversary of the Valor Awards, we are proud to 
celebrate this tradition that has stood as the highest recognition of 
courage within the Prince William County Fire & Rescue Department. 
Since inception, these awards have honored generations of first 
responders who answered the call when it mattered most.
  Each recipient exemplifies true valor, facing danger without 
hesitation, providing life-saving aid, and prioritizing the safety of 
others in the most challenging emergencies. This commitment to saving 
lives is further strengthened by the recent launch of the Fire and 
Rescue System's new Advanced Care Field Operations, a groundbreaking 
initiative that delivers blood administration directly at the scene of 
critical trauma emergencies.
  Whether responding to fires, medical emergencies, or natural 
disasters, these individuals demonstrate an unyielding commitment to 
preserving life and property. Their professionalism and readiness in 
the face of uncertainty set an example for us all and serve as a 
reminder of the impact that first responders have on the lives of those 
they protect. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring 
the Prince William County Fire & Rescue team for their remarkable 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
