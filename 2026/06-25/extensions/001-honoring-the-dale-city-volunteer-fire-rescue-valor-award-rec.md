# HONORING THE DALE CITY VOLUNTEER FIRE & RESCUE VALOR AWARD RECIPIENTS

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
 HONORING THE DALE CITY VOLUNTEER FIRE & RESCUE VALOR AWARD RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Dale City Volunteer Fire & Rescue Valor Award recipients, recognizing 
the extraordinary bravery and selfless service demonstrated by the 
department's firefighters, paramedics, and rescue personnel. These 
awards reflect the courageous actions of the dedicated men and women 
who put themselves in harm's way to protect and serve the community in 
its most critical moments.
  As we mark the 40th anniversary of the Valor Awards, we are proud to 
celebrate this tradition that has stood as the highest recognition of 
courage within the Dale City Volunteer Fire & Rescue. Since inception, 
these awards have honored generations of first responders who answered 
the call when it mattered most.
  Each recipient exemplifies true valor, facing danger without 
hesitation, providing life-saving aid, and prioritizing the safety of 
others in the most challenging emergencies. Their courage and 
compassion strengthen the bonds within our community, fostering trust, 
resilience, and a shared sense of security. Today, we honor their 
collective bravery and commitment to public service.
  Whether responding to fires, medical emergencies, or natural 
disasters, these individuals demonstrate an unyielding commitment to 
preserving life and property. Their professionalism and readiness in 
the face of uncertainty set an example for us all and serve as a 
reminder of the impact that first responders have on the lives of those 
they protect. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring 
the Dale City Volunteer Fire & Rescue team for their remarkable 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
