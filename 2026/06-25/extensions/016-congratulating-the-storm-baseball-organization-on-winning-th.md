# CONGRATULATING THE STORM BASEBALL ORGANIZATION ON WINNING THE 2026 LEGION BASEBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
  CONGRATULATING THE STORM BASEBALL ORGANIZATION ON WINNING THE 2026 
                      LEGION BASEBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the SBO--
Storm Baseball Organization on winning the 2026 Legion Baseball 
Championship of Puerto Rico. This victory represents the dedication, 
discipline, and teamwork of outstanding young athletes, their coaching 
staff, families, volunteers, and sponsors.
  Legion Baseball stands as one of the most significant youth programs 
of the American Legion. For over a century, it has developed young 
athletes not merely as ballplayers, but as responsible citizens and 
future leaders. Through this program, young men learn discipline, 
teamwork, respect, civic responsibility, and a commitment to excellence 
that extends far beyond the baseball field.
  I recognize Manager Alexis Ortiz and Coaches Jovany Santiago, Pedro 
Perez, Dalwin Roman Rodriguez, and Christian Lopez, whose leadership, 
expertise, and commitment to developing these young men made this 
championship possible. I also honor Pedro J. Rodriguez Oquendo of 
American Legion Post 129, whose sponsorship and support strengthened 
this organization.
  The championship was earned by nineteen exceptional young athletes 
and I include in the Record their names:

       Carlos Colon, Yadriel Delgado, Elimelec Flores, Diego 
     Lopez, Ken Luis Lopez, Lorenzo Melendez, David Orellano, Joel 
     Orellano, Ian Perez, Andres Quintana, Alexander Rivera, 
     Carlos Rodriguez, Dereck Rodriguez, Xavier Rovira, Dereck 
     Soto, Victor Tirado, Jeddriel Villegas, Sebastian Villegas, 
     and Marcos Acevedo.

  Their perseverance, competitive spirit, and representation of Puerto 
Rico reflected the true meaning of sportsmanship and pride.
  Legion Baseball also offers our veterans from all eras the 
opportunity to continue serving by investing in youth. Through their 
participation, veterans strengthen the bonds between generations and 
reaffirm The American Legion's mission: serving veterans, families, 
youth, and our Nation.
  I urge all citizens of Puerto Rico to recognize the importance of 
programs like Legion Baseball, which unite young athletes, veterans, 
families, and communities. These young champions represent the future 
of our island and the enduring spirit of excellence and service.

                          ____________________
```
