# RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LAKE ORION LIONS CLUB JUBILEE

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LAKE ORION LIONS CLUB JUBILEE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Lake Orion 
Lions Club Jubilee and the lasting tradition it represents for the 
Village of Lake Orion. This beloved celebration is a testament to the 
strong community spirit and pride that have brought residents together 
for the last 50 years.
  Starting in 1976, the Lake Orion Lions Club created the Jubilee as 
part of the community's celebration of the Nation's Bicentennial. What 
began as a special commemorative event has grown into one of the Lions 
Club's largest fundraisers and a cherished annual summer tradition for 
the Lake Orion community.
  As our Nation celebrates its 250th anniversary, the Jubilee serves as 
an example of how communities across America continue to honor their 
history while building traditions that bring people together.
  For generations, the Jubilee has served as a gathering place where 
families, friends, and neighbors come together to celebrate the 
community they call home. The Jubilee remains a cherished tradition 
that brings the Lake Orion community together each year in celebration 
of its shared history and strong sense of community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the Lake 
Orion Jubilee and congratulating the Lake Orion Lions Club, volunteers, 
and residents for sustaining this remarkable community tradition.
```
