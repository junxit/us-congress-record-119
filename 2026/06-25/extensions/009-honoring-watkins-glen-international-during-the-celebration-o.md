# HONORING WATKINS GLEN INTERNATIONAL DURING THE CELEBRATION OF AMERICA'S 250TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
HONORING WATKINS GLEN INTERNATIONAL DURING THE CELEBRATION OF AMERICA'S 
                           250TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Watkins Glen 
International, one of America's most iconic motorsports venues, for its 
enduring contributions to our Nation's sporting heritage as we 
celebrate the 250th anniversary of the United States of America.
  For generations, Watkins Glen International has welcomed visitors 
from across the country and around the world, serving as both an 
economic engine for the region it serves and a source of pride for New 
Yorkers. Since the earliest days of road racing in Watkins Glen, The 
Glen has become synonymous with excellence, competition, and 
innovation, earning its place among the most celebrated racing 
facilities in North America.
  As our Nation commemorates America 250, Watkins Glen International 
continues to bring people together through a shared appreciation for 
competition, tradition, and community. Beyond the excitement on the 
track, The Glen serves as a gathering place where families create 
lasting memories, local businesses thrive, and visitors experience the 
very best of what our region has to offer.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in recognizing Watkins Glen International for its longstanding 
contributions to New York and our Nation, and for helping unite 
communities during the celebration of America's 250th anniversary. Its 
legacy of excellence, hospitality, and community engagement stands as a 
testament to the enduring American spirit.
```
