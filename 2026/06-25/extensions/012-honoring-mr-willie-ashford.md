# HONORING MR. WILLIE ASHFORD

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                      HONORING MR. WILLIE ASHFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Mr. Willie Ashford of Jackson, Mississippi, for being named 
the 2026 National Waste and Recycling Association Residential Driver of 
the Year.
  Mr. Ashford has distinguished himself by exceptional service 
professionalism, and dedication to the waste and recycling industry. 
His selection as the 2026 National Waste and Recycling Residential 
Driver of the Year is prestigious national honor that recognizes 
excellence among more than 100,000 drivers employed by waste and 
recycling companies across the United States.
  Committed to serving the residents of Mississippi, Mr. Ashford 
provides essential waste collection services to approximately 6,000 
homes each week in Jackson and the surrounding communities. His work 
plays a vital role in maintaining clean, safe, and healthy 
neighborhoods for thousands of Mississippi families.
  In addition to his daily responsibilities, Mr. Ashford has 
demonstrated outstanding leadership in the workplace by serving as a 
top trainer for new drivers at his location. Through mentorship and 
hands-on instruction, he helps prepare the next generation of skilled 
and safety-conscious professionals, ensuring that high standards of 
service continue across the industry.
   Beyond his professional accomplishments, Mr. Ashford has invested in 
the lives of young people by coaching youth sports in his community. As 
a coach and role model, he has provided guidance, encouragement, and 
support to countless young athletics and their families.
  Mr. Ashford's success has also been strengthened by the support of 
his wife, Chantry Ashford, whose partnership and a encouragement have 
contributed to his many achievements.
  Mr. Speaker, today we honor Mr. Willie Ashford for his remarkable 
accomplishment and for being named the 2026 National Waste and 
Recycling Association Residential Driver of the Year.
   I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing his outstanding 
service and congratulating him on this well-deserved national honor.
```
