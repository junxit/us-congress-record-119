# RECOGNIZING GARY CHAPMAN FOR BEING NAMED THE DICKINSON COUNTY VETERAN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
 RECOGNIZING GARY CHAPMAN FOR BEING NAMED THE DICKINSON COUNTY VETERAN 
                              OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Mr. Gary Chapman for 
being named Dickinson County Veteran of the Year. This well-deserved 
honor reflects his steadfast dedication to his fellow Servicemembers, 
the communities throughout Michigan's Upper Peninsula, and our Nation.
  Gary served our Nation honorably from July 22, 1970, to July 21, 
1973, with service in the Vietnam War from 1971 to 1973. Throughout his 
time in uniform, Gary earned numerous awards and decorations, including 
the National Defense Service Medal, the Vietnam Service Medal with one 
Bronze Service Star, the Republic of Vietnam Campaign Medal, the 
Marksmanship Badge, and two Overseas Service Bars. Following his 
honorable discharge, Gary has continued serving his community for three 
decades as a custodian and bus driver for the Breitung Township School 
District.
  Gary's dedication to service did not end when he hung up the uniform. 
For more than 40 years, he has faithfully volunteered with American 
Legion Post No. 363, where he has served as Adjutant. Recognizing the 
importance of investing in our Nation's youth, he created the first 
ever Sons of the Legion for Post No. 363 in the early 2000's and 
continues to host its monthly meetings at his home. Every year he plans 
and organizes the Kingsford Memorial Day Program at the Quinnesec 
Cemetery and proudly volunteers countless hours selling Poppies during 
the American Legion fundraiser event.
  His efforts exemplify some of the very best American values: 
selflessness, determination, and a steadfast commitment to community.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in recognizing Mr. Gary Chapman for 
being named Dickinson County Veteran of the Year. I thank him for his 
enduring commitment to Veterans, his community, and our great Nation.
```
