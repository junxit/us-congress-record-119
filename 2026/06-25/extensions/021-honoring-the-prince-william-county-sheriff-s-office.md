# HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
          HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Prince William County Sheriff's Office and to recognize one of its 
distinguished members who has received a Merit Award: Deputy Michelle 
Hand. While today we recognize Deputy Hand, this award reflects the 
commitment to duty, professionalism, and public safety that defines the 
entire Sheriff's Office.
  As we mark the 40th anniversary of the Valor Awards, we are proud to 
celebrate this tradition that has stood as the highest recognition of 
courage within the Prince William County Sheriff's Office. Since 
inception, these awards have honored generations of first responders 
who answered the call when it mattered most.
  Whether in the courtroom, on transport duty, or assisting during 
critical incidents, members of the Sheriff's Office play a vital role 
in maintaining the safety and order of our community.
  Today, we recognize the extraordinary actions of Deputy Hand, whose 
bravery and dedication serve as powerful examples of the values upheld 
by the Prince William County Sheriff's Office. Her service reminds us 
that heroism is not measured by numbers, but by the impact one person 
can have through a single courageous act.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the Prince 
William County Sheriff's Office for their remarkable contributions to 
our community.

                          ____________________
```
