# HONORING THE VETERANS ASSISTANCE COMMISSION OF LAKE COUNTY'S 100-YEAR ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Schneider, Bradley Scott (S001190) [D-IL]

```
 HONORING THE VETERANS ASSISTANCE COMMISSION OF LAKE COUNTY'S 100-YEAR 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. BRADLEY SCOTT SCHNEIDER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. SCHNEIDER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Veterans 
Assistance Commission of Lake County (VACLC) as it celebrates 100 years 
of service to the veterans and families of Lake County, Illinois.
  Founded in 1926, the VACLC came into being when local veterans 
recognized the need for a centralized organization dedicated to serving 
veterans and their families. For a century, the VACLC has worked 
tirelessly to ensure that the men and women who served our Nation 
receive the benefits, resources, and support they have earned through 
their sacrifice. What began as a single service office has become a 
well-respected veteran's service organization in Illinois and has aided 
veterans from all eras and conflicts.
  Today, the Commission offers a broad range of essential services, 
including disability claims advocacy, financial assistance, burial 
benefits, and survivors' benefits. The VACLC also provides professional 
representation for veterans navigating the claims and appeals process, 
ensuring that those who have served our country receive the benefits 
which they have earned and deserve. Notably, the VACLC provides these 
services free of charge as part of the Commission's unwavering 
commitment to serving veterans and their families.
  The Commission's continued success is due in large part to the 
dedication of its leadership and staff. Led by Executive Director 
Andrew Tangen since 2017, the VACLC has expanded its impact and 
strengthened its service to veterans throughout Lake County. A United 
States Navy veteran who served eight deployments and attained the rank 
of Lieutenant Commander, Mr. Tangen has brought his extensive military, 
legal, and professional experience to the Commission. His leadership 
has positioned the organization for continued success in serving the 
growing needs of today's veterans.
  Working closely with local governments, community organizations, and 
veteran advocates throughout Lake County, the VACLC continues to ensure 
that no veteran is left behind.
  I am especially proud of the Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake 
County's partnership with my office. Every year the VACLC has joined me 
honoring our local veterans at my annual Veterans Day Breakfast and has 
partnered with my staff to help share our community's gratitude through 
our annual Valentines for Veterans program. Their commitment to our 
veterans extends far beyond the many services they provide.
  Mr. Speaker, the Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake County 
embodies the values of service, dedication, and community that make my 
district a desired home for so many veterans. As the VACLC celebrates 
their centennial anniversary, I congratulate its leadership, staff, 
volunteers, and clients on this remarkable milestone and thank them for 
100 years of outstanding service to our veterans. I look forward to 
seeing the Commission continue its important mission for many years to 
come.
```
