# HONORING THE OCCOQUAN POLICE DEPARTMENT

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
                HONORING THE OCCOQUAN POLICE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 25, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Occoquan Police Department and to recognize one of its distinguished 
members who has received a Communications Award: Police Officer Alex 
Budd. While today we recognize Officer Budd, this award reflects the 
commitment to duty, professionalism, and public safety that defines the 
entire department.
  As we mark the 40th anniversary of the Valor Awards, we are proud to 
celebrate this tradition that has stood as the highest recognition of 
courage within the Occoquan Police Department. Since inception, this 
recognition honors generations of first responders who answered the 
call when it mattered most.
  The impact of such bravery resonates throughout the community. Each 
courageous act, whether confronting immediate threats, providing aid in 
moments of crisis, or responding calmly under pressure, demonstrates a 
profound commitment to the well-being of others. These actions go 
beyond traditional law enforcement; they embody public service at its 
highest level.
  By recognizing the Occoquan Police Department and Officer Budd today, 
we not only celebrate their courage, but also the influence that 
dedicated public service can have on a community. Their heroism 
strengthens the community, setting a powerful example of what it means 
to serve with integrity, compassion, and resilience.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the Occoquan 
Police Department for their remarkable contributions to our community. 
I also want to personally thank Officer Budd for going above and beyond 
in his service to others.

                          ____________________
```
