# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Sovereign Lord, we want to be holy, set apart for You, to glorify You 
in the work that we do and by the lives we lead. Almighty Lord, we want 
to be in fellowship with You, faithful to You, to shine forth Your 
truth in every thought that enters our minds and each word that comes 
from our mouths throughout this day.
  In offering our prayers to You, may we realize that to hope for Your 
answer, we cannot behave as we like, without any regard for Your desire 
for righteousness. We cannot twist truth and rely on clever deceptions 
and not give attention to the light of truth to which You hold us each 
accountable.
  On this day, may we prove rigorous in holding fast to our honesty, 
committed to maintaining our integrity, and constant in our moral 
inventory. Thus, may our lives be a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing 
to You and worthy of Your intentions for this day.
  In view of Your mercy and in the hope of Your grace, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
