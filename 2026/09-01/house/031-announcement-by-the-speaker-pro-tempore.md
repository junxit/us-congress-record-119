# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 1499, H.R. 6500

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Goldman of Texas). Proceedings will 
resume on questions previously postponed. Votes will be taken in the 
following order:
  Motion to suspend the rules and concur in the Senate amendments to 
H.R. 6500;
  Ordering the previous question on House Resolution 1499; and
  Adoption of House Resolution 1499, if ordered.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, remaining electronic votes will be 
conducted as 5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
