# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DR. SABRINA ROBERTSON

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DR. SABRINA ROBERTSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Dr. Sabrina Robertson as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Sabrina 
has dedicated over 25 years of her career working directly with 
adolescent girls in substitute care, veterans in transitional housing, 
justice-impacted individuals, families involved with courts, and 
vulnerable patients in behavioral health systems. Sabrina is a trauma-
informed therapist and delivers emotional support across various 
community settings. Her role as a doctor spans across Solano and Contra 
Costa counties, reflecting a commitment that crosses geographic 
boundaries and serves multiple populations. She mentors Master of 
Social Work students and associate clinicians, emphasizing integrity, 
cultural humility, accountability, and excellence.
  She challenges new professionals to fully embrace their 
responsibilities and models best practices in her field. Dr. Robertson 
also facilitates a weekly court-approved anger management program and 
provides parenting education focused on healthy development and co-
parenting. She has also shared her knowledge by authoring The Pain of 
My Inheritance: My Mother's Wound, a book about healing and 
restoration. Sabrina is valued not only for her formal achievements but 
also for her everyday impact on those she supports, mentors, and 
inspires.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Dr. Sabrina Robertson for her 
positive contributions to our community.
```
