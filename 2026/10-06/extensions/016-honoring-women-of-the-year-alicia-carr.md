# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ALICIA CARR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ALICIA CARR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Alicia Carr as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. For nearly 20 years, 
Alicia has dedicated her time and talents to serving others throughout 
Solano County. A military spouse and longtime community volunteer, she 
has worked to promote health, cultural pride, and fellowship among 
seniors, military families, and members of the Filipino-American (Fil-
Am) community.
  Alicia began volunteering at the Fairfield Senior Center in 2006, 
assisting with senior activities and participating in line-dancing 
classes. After being encouraged by her instructor to help lead the 
class, she eventually stepped into that role and later took over the 
program following his passing, continuing in his memory. Since joining 
the Filipino American Community of Fairfield, Suisun, and Vacaville, 
Inc. in 2017, Alicia has volunteered weekly as a line-dancing 
instructor while organizing cultural performances, fundraisers, and 
large community events. Beyond her work as an instructor, Alicia has 
served as Assistant Secretary, Secretary, and currently as a member of 
the Board of Directors. Her fellow members describe her as a dependable 
leader and the person they turn to when something needs to get done.
  Through her many roles, Alicia's service has positively impacted 
Filipino-American families, military families, and seniors throughout 
Solano County. Alicia's unwavering commitment to the community brought 
people together and strengthened connections, fostering a greater sense 
of unity and belonging.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Alicia Carr for her positive 
contributions to our community.
```
