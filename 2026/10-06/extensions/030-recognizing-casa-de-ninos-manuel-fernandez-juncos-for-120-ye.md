# RECOGNIZING CASA DE NINOS MANUEL FERNANDEZ JUNCOS FOR 120 YEARS OF SERVICE TO PUERTO RICO'S CHILDREN AND YOUTH

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
  RECOGNIZING CASA DE NINOS MANUEL FERNANDEZ JUNCOS FOR 120 YEARS OF 
              SERVICE TO PUERTO RICO'S CHILDREN AND YOUTH

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Casa 
de Ninos Manuel Fernandez Juncos as it commemorates its 120th 
anniversary of service to the children and youth of Puerto Rico.
  Founded in 1906 in San Juan, Puerto Rico, under the leadership of the 
distinguished educator Don Manuel Fernandez Juncos, the institution was 
established to provide shelter, protection, and guidance to vulnerable 
children who lacked stable homes and opportunities. What began as El 
Refugio de Ninos Desamparados evolved into Casa de Ninos Manuel 
Fernandez Juncos, a nonprofit organization that has transformed the 
lives of generations of young people for more than a century.
  Today, Casa de Ninos Manuel Fernandez Juncos provides preventive and 
therapeutic services to children, adolescents, and young adults who 
have experienced abuse, abandonment, or neglect. Through educational, 
rehabilitative, and counseling programs led by a dedicated team of 
professionals, the organization helps young people develop the skills, 
values, and confidence necessary to build productive and meaningful 
lives.
  Throughout its history, Casa de Ninos Manuel Fernandez Juncos has 
remained steadfast in its mission to provide a safe and supportive 
environment where young people can overcome adversity and reach their 
full potential. By addressing both immediate needs and long-term 
development, the organization has strengthened communities and created 
opportunities for countless young people across Puerto Rico.
  For 120 years, its commitment to education, protection, and personal 
development has made Casa de Ninos Manuel Fernandez Juncos an enduring 
source of hope for Puerto Rico's youth.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Casa de 
Ninos Manuel Fernandez Juncos on the occasion of its 120th anniversary 
and in commending its remarkable legacy of service and continued 
dedication to improving the lives of Puerto Rico's children and youth.
```
