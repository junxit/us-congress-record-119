# RECOGNIZING AVIATION ORDNANCEMAN THIRD CLASS PETTY OFFICER JANYSSA LILYBETH ESPINOZA

**Speaking:** De La Cruz, Monica (D000594) [R-TX]

```
  RECOGNIZING AVIATION ORDNANCEMAN THIRD CLASS PETTY OFFICER JANYSSA 
                           LILYBETH ESPINOZA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MONICA DE LA CRUZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. De La CRUZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Aviation 
Ordnanceman Third Class Petty Officer Janyssa Lilybeth Espinoza for her 
exceptional service in the United States Navy.
  Raised in Texas, Petty Officer Espinoza graduated from Veterans 
Memorial High School before enlisting in the Navy at age 19. She 
proudly represents our community as the first woman in her family to 
serve our nation in the military.
  As an Aviation Ordnanceman aboard the USS George Washington and the 
USS Abraham Lincoln, she carries out critical flight deck operations, 
handling weapons and supporting our F-18 and F-35 aircraft under 
demanding conditions.
  Especially during her extended ten-month deployment at sea aboard the 
USS Abraham Lincoln, Petty Officer Espinoza showed herself to be a true 
leader, stepping up during the most challenging of times to support her 
fellow sailors while working long hours thousands of miles away from 
her loved ones. Through it all, she remained steadfast in her love of 
country and remained focused on the mission at hand.
  Petty Officer Espinoza exemplifies the courage, sacrifice, and 
dedication that define our Armed Forces. I thank Petty Officer Espinoza 
for her selfless devotion to our Nation, and I wish her fair winds and 
following seas.

                          ____________________
```
