# HONORING THE 59TH ANNIVERSARY OF MIAMI'S BIG FIVE CLUB

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
         HONORING THE 59TH ANNIVERSARY OF MIAMI'S BIG FIVE CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Miami's historic 
Big Five Club as it celebrates its 59th anniversary and nearly six 
decades of service to South Florida's Cuban-American community.
  The story of the Big Five is inseparable from the story of the Cuban 
exile community in Miami. Founded by Cuban exiles under the leadership 
of Dr. Manuel R. Morales, the Club brought together families associated 
with five of Havana's great social institutions: the Havana Yacht Club, 
Vedado Tennis Club, Casino Espanol, Havana Biltmore Yacht and Country 
Club, and Miramar Yacht Club.
  Forced to flee their homeland following the rise of the communist 
dictatorship, these families rebuilt their lives in Miami while 
preserving the traditions, values, and sense of community they brought 
from Cuba. From its earliest days, the Big Five has stood for God, 
country, family, Cuban traditions, freedom, and democracy. For 
generations, it has been a place where Cuban families could celebrate 
our culture, play sports, enjoy music and dominoes, build lifelong 
friendships, and pass our traditions from one generation to the next.
  For me, this anniversary is also deeply personal. My father and my 
family were members of the Big Five for decades, and like so many Cuban 
families in Miami, we shared countless moments there with friends and 
loved ones. The Club became part of the fabric of the Cuban exiles 
community.
  The Big Five represents far more than a social club. It represents an 
exile generation that arrived in America having lost its homeland but 
refused to lose its identity. They rebuilt their lives, raised their 
families, strengthened Miami, and kept alive their love for a free 
Cuba.
  It is my privilege to congratulate the leadership, members, families, 
and friends of the Big Five Club on its 59th anniversary. May the Big 
Five continue to serve our community for generations to come.
```
