# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MYLES TURNER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MYLES TURNER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize our Texas-24 
Hometown Hero, Myles Turner. Myles is a former Euless Trinity 
basketball superstar, who has a legacy of giving back to his community. 
The Milwaukee Bucks Center recently announced that he's pledged $2 
million over the next 20 years to the Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD athletic 
department, a donation that will support every middle and high school 
in the area.
  Over the next 20 years, the funds will help students get to the next 
level, which Myles clarifies can have a variety of meanings, not just 
professional sports. Turner encourages students to pursue their 
passion, whatever it may be. To support this message, he also gifted 
Trinity's choir program with a grand piano.
  In his honor, Trinity High School will name the new arena the Myles 
Turner Fieldhouse, where Turner will host basketball camps, practices, 
and clinics for Work as Role Models, his nonprofit.
  I thank Myles for his consistent care and support of our North Texas 
community and for being an inspiration to all.

                          ____________________
```
