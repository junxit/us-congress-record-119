# HONORING SYDNEY ROBERT STEWART

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING SYDNEY ROBERT STEWART

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Pastor S. 
Robert Stewart as he is consecrated to Bishop S. Robert Stewart.
  Pastor Sydney Robert Stewart has been an integral part of the Miami 
Gardens community for over four decades, actively contributing to its 
development and progress. For 27 years, Pastor Stewart has led the 
congregation of Pentecostal Tabernacle International in Miami, Florida. 
His impact extends beyond his role as a pastor, as he has been actively 
involved in effecting positive change through his passion and 
commitment to serving others.
  Pastor Stewart's tireless work and dedication towards the betterment 
of the community has resulted in a remarkable achievement. He has 
managed to unite pastors from different denominations, despite their 
varying religious affiliations, to come together and collaborate 
towards a common goal. This collaboration has led to the formation of 
the Miami Gardens Pastors Lifting Up Services (MGPLUS), which aims to 
uplift and serve the community. Through his leadership, Pastor Stewart 
has shown that despite our differences, we can come together for the 
greater good and make a positive impact in our city.
  Over the last 25 years, under the leadership of Pastor Stewart, 
Pentecostal Tabernacle has led a series of initiatives that have 
brought immense support and resources to the people of this dynamic 
community. These programs have played a vital role in improving the 
lives of countless individuals and families, providing them with the 
tools and resources necessary to thrive and succeed.
  Among the many programs the Ministry offers are General Educational 
Development (GED) classes, which allow individuals to complete their 
high school education and receive a diploma. The ministry also provides 
life skills training and workshops, which equip individuals with the 
necessary tools to navigate the complexities of daily life. The 
Ministry also recognizes the importance of business skills in today's 
economy and provides training and workshops to individuals seeking to 
acquire or enhance their business acumen. Another vital program of the 
ministry is the CARE Health Campaign and Community Health Fair, which 
provides free health screenings, as well as mental and behavioral 
health screenings, interventions, and referrals. The Ministry also 
offers support to individuals experiencing grief with the CARE for 
Grief Groups and the Uplift Summer Grief Camp, which served children 
who have experienced the loss of a loved one to death and incarceration 
for the past three years.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring Bishop S. Robert Stewart for 
all he has achieved.
```
