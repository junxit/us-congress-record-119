# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: NWAEZE NMEREOLE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: NWAEZE NMEREOLE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Nwaeze Nmereole as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. A devoted 
advocate for others, Nwaeze has transformed the lives of individuals 
and families throughout the Nigerian, African, and broader immigrant 
communities of Contra Costa County and the greater Bay Area.
  One of Nwaeze's greatest passions is education and empowerment. She 
voluntarily tutors individuals preparing for the GED examination, 
assists prospective Licensed Vocational Nurses with licensing 
examinations, and teaches basic computer skills to help others pursue 
employment and become more self-sufficient. She is equally committed to 
mentoring young people, encouraging them to pursue their education, 
develop job skills, achieve financial independence, and build 
productive futures.
  Through her work, Nwaeze has become a trusted resource for 
immigrants, elderly residents, and families experiencing hardship. She 
helps newly arrived immigrants obtain driver's licenses, open bank 
accounts, enroll in English as a Second Language programs, and navigate 
the U.S. immigration process. She also assists elderly community 
members with transportation to medical care, obtaining prescriptions, 
and purchasing groceries. Nwaeze has also opened her home to 
individuals experiencing homelessness, providing temporary shelter 
until they can regain stability.
  Nwaeze has quietly transformed lives without seeking recognition, 
guided by her faith, compassion, and belief that everyone deserves 
dignity and an opportunity to succeed. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor 
Nwaeze Nmereole for her outstanding service and positive contributions 
to our community.
```
