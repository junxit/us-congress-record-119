# HONORING CHRISTIAN LOVE BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING CHRISTIAN LOVE BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Christian Love Baptist 
Church of Alexandria, Louisiana, an enduring institution of faith, 
service, and community fellowship whose roots run deep in Rapides 
Parish. Established in 1954 under the leadership of the late Rev. Bert 
Collins, Christian Love began as a small but devoted congregation 
committed to sharing the Gospel and fostering Christian unity. At the 
same time, nearby Gethsemane Baptist Church, organized and pastored by 
the late Rev. Charlie Jackson, was growing in its own ministry 
outreach. Both churches served as spiritual anchors for African 
American families in Alexandria during a period of significant cultural 
change and community development.
  In 1963, these two congregations, Christian Love Baptist Church and 
Gethsemane Baptist Church, joined together in a historic merger that 
united their memberships, ministries, and visions for the future. It is 
not easy for two churches, each with its own history, pastor, and 
traditions, to become one, and the willingness of both congregations to 
do so reflects a deep commitment to unity and to the larger mission of 
serving the people of Alexandria. The merger created a single 
congregation with expanded capacity for worship, fellowship, and 
service, and it laid a powerful foundation for decades of spiritual 
growth, community involvement, and renewed commitment to uplifting the 
people of Alexandria.
  The church entered a new chapter of organizational stability on 
January 4, 1982, when it was formally incorporated as Christian Love 
Baptist Church of Alexandria, Inc., a nonprofit religious corporation 
under Louisiana law, recognized as a tax-exempt organization. Local 
members often describe Christian Love as a welcoming haven, a place 
defined by compassion, encouragement, and unwavering faith. Its 
ministries emphasize Christian love in action, offering programs that 
support spiritual development, family life, and communal support.
  For generations, Christian Love Baptist Church has upheld its 
reputation as a center of love, faith, and service within the 
Alexandria community. It has been a place where families have come 
together to worship, where believers have been encouraged to grow in 
their faith, and where neighbors have found compassion and support in 
times of need. This long history of service is central to the church's 
identity: it is a congregation that has understood from its earliest 
days that the Gospel is lived out not only on Sunday morning, but in 
the care that its members show for one another and for the community 
around them.
  The impact of Christian Love extends well beyond its own membership. 
As a congregation formed from two historic churches, it carries forward 
the legacies of its founding pastors and stands as a model of unity 
within the faith community of Alexandria, showing what can be 
accomplished when believers set aside differences and work toward a 
common purpose. Its example strengthens the broader faith community, 
standing alongside other churches in the shared work of nurturing 
faith, supporting families, and serving those in need.
  Today, Christian Love Baptist Church stands not only as a testament 
to its rich history but also as a vibrant, forward-looking congregation 
dedicated to worship, discipleship, and outreach, to transforming 
lives, strengthening families, and shining the light of Christ 
throughout Central Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join 
me in honoring the legacy built by Christian Love Baptist Church and 
its dedicated congregation, and I wish the church continued strength 
and blessing in the years to come.
```
