# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CHRISTINA STRAWBRIDGE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CHRISTINA STRAWBRIDGE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Christina 
Strawbridge as one of

[[Page E1037]]

California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. For more 
than three decades, Christina has demonstrated an extraordinary 
commitment to strengthening the City of Benicia through public service, 
small-business leadership, historic preservation, and community 
involvement. Christina owned and operated a successful downtown Benicia 
business for more than twenty years, contributing to the vitality of 
the city's historic downtown while mentoring and supporting fellow 
entrepreneurs. She later served two terms on Benicia's City Council, 
where she championed economic development, historic preservation, and 
initiatives that improved residents' quality of life.
  Among her most significant accomplishments was helping lead efforts 
to protect Benicia's two State Parks from closure during California's 
budget crisis. Her advocacy helped preserve important public spaces and 
historic resources, including the Benicia State Capitol, Fischer-Hanlon 
House, and Benicia State Recreation Area. Christina also played an 
important role in revitalizing the Southern Pacific Railroad Depot, 
helping transform the historic landmark into a community gathering 
place.
  As publisher of Benicia Magazine, Christina highlighted the people, 
businesses, artists, and organizations that make Benicia unique. She is 
also a founding board member and current President of Benicia Main 
Street, where she supports small businesses, promotes tourism, 
organizes community events, and works to preserve downtown Benicia's 
character. Christina's decades of leadership have strengthened 
Benicia's economy, preserved its history, protected public spaces, and 
inspired others to serve.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor Christina Strawbridge for her outstanding 
contributions to our community.
```
