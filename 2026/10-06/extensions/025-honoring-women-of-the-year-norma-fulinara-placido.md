# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: NORMA FULINARA PLACIDO

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: NORMA FULINARA PLACIDO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Norma Fulinara Placido as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Originally 
born in the Philippines, Norma earned a Bachelor of Science in 
elementary education before immigrating to the United States to join 
her husband, who was serving in the U.S. Navy.
  In 1971, she studied Information Technology and Data Processing at 
Peralta College in Oakland, California, and went on to build a 32-year 
career. A lifelong advocate for public service and leadership, Norma 
currently serves as President of the Vallejo Sister City

[[Page E1039]]

Association and Sister City Commissioner, promoting cultural exchange 
and community partnerships. She also served as President of the 
Filipino Community of Solano County, Inc. for 20 years, leading 
fundraising efforts for cultural, charitable, and community development 
initiatives.
  Throughout her service, Norma has held leadership positions with 
numerous other community organizations, including the Solano County 
Fair Association and others throughout Northern California. Her 
dedication has earned her numerous honors, including being named one of 
the 100 Most Influential Filipina Women in the World by the Filipina 
Women's Network and the first-ever Outstanding Filipino American Women 
Award, among several other leadership, volunteer, and community service 
awards. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Norma Fulinara 
Placido for her positive contributions to our community.
```
