# HONORING WAYNE A. JONES

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                        HONORING WAYNE A. JONES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Wayne A. 
Jones, an outstanding public servant as he starts his new journey as 
the first black Police Chief of the City of Miami Beach.
  Chief Jones was born and raised in Nassau. Bahamas. He lived between 
the Bahamas and Miami as an adolescent. He earned a Master of Science 
degree in Public Administration from Florida international University, 
a Bachelors degree in Political Science and Public Administration from 
Florida Memorial University, and an Associate's Degree in Professional 
Piloting and Technology from Miami-Dade College. He is also a licensed 
pilot. Chief Jones completed the Federal Bureau of Investigation's Law 
Enforcement Executive Development Seminar and the Police Executive 
Research Forum's Senior Management Institute for Police program.
  Chief Jones has a career that spans nearly three decades. He began 
with the City of Miami Beach Police Department in 1996 as a patrol 
officer and has progressively ascended through the ranks. Chief Jones 
has worked in multiple department areas, including Patrol Operations, 
Special Operations, Community Affairs, Internal Affairs, and Criminal 
Investigations.
  During his tenure, Chief Jones created the department's human 
trafficking unit and developed strong partnerships with the State 
Attorney's Human Trafficking taskforce. This unit leads Miami-Dade 
County in investigations and arrests for sex trafficking offenses. As a 
sergeant in community affairs, he developed the blueprint for MBPD's 
nationally recognized Homeless Resource Program. As a Major, he crafted 
a strategy to boost the recruitment of women police officers, which 
resulted in an increase of female officers from 12 to 18 percent of all 
new hires. Chief Jones' mission is to prioritize women in leadership.
  Chief Jones is an active member of several nationally recognized 
organizations, such as the International Chiefs of Police and The 
National Organization of Black Law Enforcement Executives. Mr. Speaker, 
please join me in honoring Chief Wayne A. Jones for all he has 
achieved.
```
