# HONORING GOOD HOPE BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING GOOD HOPE BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Good Hope Baptist 
Church, located at 114 C.L. Bradford Street in Pineville, Louisiana, a 
congregation that has served the Pineville area, and the greater 
Alexandria community across the Red River, for more than a century. 
Established in 1907 as a place of worship rooted in the Baptist 
tradition, Good Hope's mission is to be a community of believers 
empowered by the Holy Spirit to love God, love all people, and make 
disciples.
  Good Hope began in the early 20th century as a small congregation 
meeting in a modest setting. Over the decades, it grew through the 
dedication of its members, expanding its worship services, outreach 
programs, and community involvement. From the beginning, the values of 
faith, service, and fellowship shaped the life of the church, and a 
long history of service has remained central to its identity ever 
since.
  That history of service is the heart of Good Hope's story. The 
church's mission to love all people has been lived out through members 
who take part in local events, charitable work, and spiritual support 
for their neighbors. For generations, Good Hope has been a place where 
families have found worship, encouragement, and help in times of need, 
and

[[Page E1043]]

where the call to discipleship has been understood to include caring 
for the community beyond the church's walls.
  In 2017, the church celebrated its 110th anniversary with a special 
program documented in a photo book, Good Hope Baptist Church 110th, by 
Michael R. Maffet. The anniversary project featured historical images, 
program details, and reflections on the church's journey, highlighting 
its role in the Pineville community and its enduring commitment to 
spiritual growth and service.
  Today, under the leadership of Pastor Joseph K. Lewis and First Lady 
Tricia Lewis, Good Hope Baptist Church continues to uphold its founding 
mission. The church also reaches beyond its walls through social media 
and video content, including its YouTube channel, which shares worship 
services, events, and community updates, allowing the congregation's 
ministry to reach members and neighbors wherever they are.
  The impact of Good Hope extends well beyond its own membership. As a 
longstanding congregation in the Pineville and Alexandria area, it 
contributes to the strength of the broader faith community, standing 
alongside other churches in the shared work of nurturing faith, 
supporting families, and building a community grounded in compassion. 
Its long history reflects resilience, faithfulness, and a deep 
connection to the people it serves, and its example continues to 
inspire those who worship there and those who look to it as a model of 
faithful service.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Good Hope 
Baptist Church, Pastor Joseph K. Lewis, First Lady Tricia Lewis, and 
the dedicated congregation for nearly 120 years of steady growth, 
community engagement, and commitment to Christian discipleship and 
service. May Good Hope continue to be a vital part of the spiritual and 
social life of Pineville, Alexandria, and the surrounding communities 
for generations to come.
```
