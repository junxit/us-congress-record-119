# HONORING TIMOTHY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                    HONORING TIMOTHY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Timothy Baptist Church 
of Alexandria, Louisiana, a congregation with deep roots in the 
community that trace back to 1880, when the church was founded in 
Latanier, Louisiana. Timothy began as a small congregation in a rural 
setting and grew through the dedication of its early members and 
leaders. For more than 140 years, it has been a steady presence in the 
lives of its members and an important part of the faith community of 
Alexandria.
  What has distinguished Timothy Baptist Church throughout its history 
is its willingness to adapt in order to best serve its members. Over 
the decades, as the congregation expanded and the community changed, 
the church relocated several times. Each move reflected both the growth 
of the church and the shifting needs of its members, and each 
demonstrated a congregation that understood that its mission was to go 
where its people were and to meet them where they were. A church that 
began in a rural community in 1880 and found its home in Alexandria is 
a living example of a congregation that has grown and changed without 
losing sight of its purpose.
  In 1965, the church was officially chartered and incorporated, a 
formal step in its organizational history that placed the congregation 
on firm footing for the future. The congregation's perseverance and 
faithfulness then led to the construction of its current building, 
which was dedicated in 1968. That building became a central place of 
worship and community gathering, symbolizing the dedication of the 
church's founders and congregants and giving generations of families a 
permanent home for worship, fellowship, and celebration.
  Throughout its history, Timothy Baptist Church has been led by 
committed pastors, including Rev. George L. Price, Sr., who emphasized 
sharing the goodness of God and transforming the world through faith 
and service. The church's mission has consistently focused on fostering 
Kingdom-minded disciples through the teachings of Jesus Christ and the 
inspiration of the holy spirit, a mission that looks outward as well as 
inward, equipping believers to carry their faith into their families, 
their workplaces, and their neighborhoods.
  Today, Timothy Baptist Church continues to serve as a spiritual and 
community hub in Alexandria, maintaining its mission to empower the 
next generation of Christians while preserving its rich heritage. By 
honoring its past while preparing its young people to lead, the church 
strengthens not only its own congregation but the faith community of 
Alexandria as a whole, setting an example of how a church can remain 
rooted in tradition while responding to the needs of each new 
generation.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Timothy 
Baptist Church and its dedicated congregation for more than 140 years 
of faith, perseverance, and service to the people of Alexandria. May 
Timothy continue to adapt, to nurture disciples, and to strengthen its 
community for generations to come.
```
