# HONORING TIMOTHY BELCHER

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                        HONORING TIMOTHY BELCHER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Deputy 
Chief Timothy Belcher for his stellar and philanthropic contributions 
to the 5000 Role Models of Excellence Project.
  Frederick Douglass once said, ``it is easier to build strong children 
than to repair broken men.'' Thank you so much for believing in our 
mission of interrupting the school to prison pipeline and to guiding 
our young boys along a cheerfully charted path and sending them to 
college.

[[Page E1036]]

  You are investing in the children of our community, and they will be 
the benefactors of your investment for generations to come. You will 
always be a part of us, and we will always be a part of you. Thank you 
for all that you do on behalf of our children.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring Deputy Chief Timothy Belcher, 
a 2023 Role Model Wall Man with a generous philanthropic spirit, and I 
ask my colleagues from the 117th Congress to join me in honoring this 
special circle of brotherhood.

                          ____________________
```
