# HONORING MT. ZION MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING MT. ZION MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mt. Zion Missionary 
Baptist Church of Alexandria, Louisiana, a strong and enduring faith 
community rooted in Christian belief and guided by the principles of 
the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Founded upon a commitment to spiritual 
development and sincere worship, the church has long served as a place 
where individuals and families can encounter God, grow in grace, and 
walk faithfully in their Christian journey. With Baptist teachings as 
its foundation, Mt. Zion emphasizes sound doctrine, heartfelt 
preaching, and a worship experience centered on glorifying God.
  Recognizing the vital importance of nurturing spiritual maturity, the 
church offers a range of ministries designed to support believers at 
every stage of life. Regular worship services, Bible study 
opportunities, prayer gatherings, and small-group discipleship help 
members strengthen their understanding of Scripture and draw nearer to 
God. These ministries serve not only as teaching tools but also as 
avenues for fellowship, encouragement, and personal transformation, as 
members seek to live out their faith inside and outside the church 
walls.
  In addition to its internal ministries, Mt. Zion is deeply committed 
to serving the broader community. Through outreach programs, service 
initiatives, and partnerships that address practical needs, the church 
strives to embody Christ's love in meaningful and tangible ways. 
Whether providing support to families, encouraging youth, ministering 
to seniors, or responding to the needs of the underserved, Mt.

[[Page E1041]]

Zion has been a steady and compassionate presence in the lives of 
countless individuals across Central Louisiana. This long history of 
service reflects the church's understanding that faith is not only 
professed on Sunday, but lived out each day in care for one's 
neighbors.
  The impact of Mt. Zion extends well beyond its own membership. As a 
congregation of Alexandria, it contributes to the strength of the 
broader faith community, standing alongside other churches in the 
shared work of nurturing faith, supporting families, and meeting the 
needs of those who are struggling. Its commitment to sound teaching and 
to practical service sets an example for the many who worship there and 
for those in the community who look to the church for guidance, 
encouragement, and help in times of need.
  Above all, Mt. Zion Missionary Baptist Church seeks to be a beacon of 
hope, love, and service, shining the light of Christ in a world that 
needs encouragement and spiritual grounding. The church remains 
steadfast in its mission to uplift the community, equip believers, and 
extend the message of salvation with unwavering faith. As it moves 
forward, Mt. Zion continues to embrace its calling to serve, inspire, 
and transform lives through the unchanging truth of God's Word.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in acknowledging Mt. 
Zion Missionary Baptist Church and its dedicated congregation for its 
endearing legacy of spreading the Word of God and its commitment to the 
Alexandria and Central Louisiana community. May Mt. Zion continue to be 
a source of faith, hope, and service for generations to come.
```
