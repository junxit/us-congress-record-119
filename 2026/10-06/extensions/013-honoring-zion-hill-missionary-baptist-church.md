# HONORING ZION HILL MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING ZION HILL MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the historic and 
enduring legacy of Zion Hill Missionary Baptist Church, a steadfast 
pillar of faith and community in Central Louisiana and the Alexandria 
area for more than a century. The church traces its beginnings to 1925, 
when its first sanctuary was constructed on land generously donated by 
Rev. Hall and his wife. Rooted in a strong foundation of Christian 
believers, the congregation persevered through trials, challenges, and 
the uncertain conditions of its early years. Their resilience, anchored 
in faith, established Zion Hill as a spiritual refuge committed to 
worship, service, and the advancement of God's Kingdom.
  In 1996, a significant new chapter began when Dr. Joshua Joy Dara, 
Sr., moved to Central Louisiana and accepted the call to serve as 
pastor. His inspiring leadership, dynamic preaching, and passion for 
teaching the Word of God brought renewed growth and vitality to the 
congregation. Under Dr. Dara's guidance, Zion Hill experienced 
unprecedented expansion, growing from a modest membership of 75 to more 
than 3,500 worshipers. His ministry has touched countless lives, 
drawing individuals and families into deeper fellowship and spiritual 
maturity.
  Today, Zion Hill stands as one of the region's most vibrant and 
visionary congregations, operating more than 50 ministries that address 
spiritual, educational, and social needs across the community. These 
ministries reflect the church's commitment to serving the whole person, 
mind, body, and spirit. Whether through youth programs, outreach 
initiatives. Christian education, or service-focused projects, Zion 
Hill continues to uplift its congregation and its surrounding 
neighborhoods with compassion and purpose.
  The church's long history of service is central to its identity. A 
congregation that began with a small group of believers on land donated 
by a pastor and his wife has grown into one that serves thousands, and 
it has done so by remaining true to the idea that faith is meant to be 
lived out in service to others. By meeting needs that are spiritual, 
educational, and social, Zion Hill has become a place where families 
find not only worship, but also guidance, encouragement, and practical 
support.
  The church's reach extends far beyond its physical walls. Zion Hill's 
worship services are broadcast weekly on multiple television stations 
throughout Louisiana and shared across numerous social media platforms, 
allowing the message of hope to reach national and international 
audiences. Through this expanded digital presence, Zion Hill continues 
its mission of spreading the Gospel and connecting believers from every 
walk of life, including those who are unable to join in person.
  The impact of Zion Hill on the faith community of Alexandria and 
Central Louisiana is significant. With a membership in the thousands 
and more than 50 ministries, it stands alongside the region's other 
churches in the shared work of nurturing faith, supporting families, 
and serving those in need, and it serves as a model of what a growing, 
service-minded congregation can accomplish. Its example encourages 
other congregations and inspires the many individuals and families who 
have found a spiritual home within its walls.
  Mr. Speaker, Zion Hill Missionary Baptist Church remains a beacon of 
light, steadfast devotion, and transformative ministry in Central 
Louisiana, faithfully carrying forward its calling to serve God and His 
people. I urge my colleagues to join me in honoring Zion Hill, Dr. 
Joshua Joy Dara, Sr., and the dedicated congregation for more than a 
century of spreading the Word of God and serving the community.
```
