# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: RAMI MUTH

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: RAMI MUTH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Rami Muth as she is honored as one of California's 8th 
Congressional District's Women of the Year. A devoted educator and 
community leader, Rami has spent decades expanding opportunities for 
students and encouraging civic engagement throughout Solano and Contra 
Costa Counties.
  Rami devoted 40 years to public education, serving in the Benicia, 
Fairfield-Suisun, and Martinez Unified School Districts. Her commitment 
to students and excellence in teaching earned her recognition as Mentor 
Teacher of the Year in 1995 and Solano Teacher of the Year in 1997. She 
later served as Director of Curriculum and, from 2008 until her 
retirement in 2016, as Superintendent of the Martinez Unified School 
District. In that role, she worked to strengthen student achievement 
while building meaningful partnerships among schools, families, and the 
broader community.
  Rami's commitment to education and service has continued well beyond 
her retirement. Since 2016, she has served on the Executive Board of 
#hersmile, a nonprofit supporting healing and resiliency in the face of 
adversity. She also serves as a consultant and curriculum director for 
C4L, an online school that provides adults facing barriers to 
traditional education with a pathway to a high school diploma.
  Her dedication to civic engagement is equally strong. Since 2020, 
Rami has served on the board of the League of Women Voters of Solano 
County and currently serves as co-president. Through this work, she has 
encouraged young people to become informed and engaged citizens, 
including helping launch a student essay contest inviting students to 
reflect on ``What Democracy Means to Me.''
  Through her lifelong dedication to education, service, and civic 
participation, Rami continues to open doors for others and strengthen 
the communities she serves.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor Rami Muth for her many contributions to our 
community.
```
