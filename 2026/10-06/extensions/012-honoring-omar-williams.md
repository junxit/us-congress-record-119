# HONORING OMAR WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING OMAR WILLIAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. WILSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Omar Williams as he is 
the newly appointed pastor of Pentecostal Tabernacle.
  Pastor Omar Williams is a native of St. James, Jamaica. He was born 
on April 6, 1979, in White Hall, St. Elizabeth as the sixth of seven 
children of Reginald (deceased 2010) and Agatha (now Abigail) Williams.
  Pastor Williams earned a Diploma in Education from Sam Sharpe 
Teachers' College; a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from the 
International University of the Caribbean, a Master of Arts in Pastoral 
Care from Fordham University; and is currently pursuing a Doctor of 
Ministry degree in Ministry Leadership at Southeastern University.
  His parents introduced him to the church when they became members of 
Kingsway

[[Page E1035]]

Tabernacle (KWT) United Pentecostal Church of Jamaica (UPCJ) in 1981 
under the leadership of Pastor Ambrose Hind. He made his personal 
commitment to Jesus Christ in 1991 when he was baptized with the Holy 
Spirit and was subsequently baptized in water.
  While residing in New York, Pastor Williams was an active member of 
the New York Metro District, of the United Pentecostal Church 
International (UPCI). On June 10, 2017, Mr. Williams was ordained to 
the ministry by the (UPCI) during the New York Metro District Annual 
Conference held in Old Westbury, New York.
  Since January 1, 2022, Pastor Williams has been serving as the 
Assistant Pastor for Pentecostal Tabernacle Miami (Pentab) under the 
leadership of Pastor S. Robert Stewart. He has been providing general 
and spiritual oversight to the ministry of Pentab under the guidance of 
Pastor Stewart and in collaboration with the board of elders, associate 
pastor, ministers, auxiliaries, employed staff, and volunteers. He has 
been functioning as Pentab's primary Bible teacher and preacher, 
responsible for the regular Bible studies and ministry of the church.
  In June 2023, the baton of pastoral leadership was passed from Pastor 
Stewart to Pastor Omar Williams. Pastor Williams and his wife Lady Dr. 
Michell Williams are honored to carry the legacy of Pentecostal 
Tabernacle International forward for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring Pastor Omar A. Williams for 
all he has achieved.
```
