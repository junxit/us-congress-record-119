# HONORING LEE "FREEZY" PRINCE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING LEE ``FREEZY'' PRINCE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Lee 
``Freezy'' Prince, a visionary music manager, astute entrepreneur, 
dedicated youth advocate, and proud product of Carol City High School 
whose multifaceted contributions to the entertainment industry, 
business community, and youth development have made him an exemplary 
role model and community leader.
  Lee Prince has built an extraordinary career as one of the music 
industry's most respected managers and entrepreneurs. As the long-time 
manager and business partner of international superstar Flo Rida, 
Freezy has been instrumental in cultivating one of the most successful 
careers in contemporary music. In a pivotal strategic decision, Freezy, 
along with A&R executive Mike Caren, persuaded Flo Rida to expand his 
artistic vision beyond Miami-focused music and instead focus on larger, 
universal themes with worldwide appeal, This calculated gamble paid off 
magnificently. Under Freezy's management through Strong Arm Management, 
Flo Rida has sold over 100 million records worldwide, with hits like 
``Low,'' ``Right Round,'' ``Good Feeling,'' ``Wild Ones,'' ``Whistle,'' 
and ``My House'' dominating charts across continents.
  As Chief Executive Officer of IMG Strong Arm, Freezy oversees overall 
company operations, including strategic planning, financial management, 
talent acquisition, and business development. His innovative approach 
to marketing and management has propelled Flo Rida to achieve numerous 
chart-topping hits and international acclaim.
  Beyond artist management, Freezy has demonstrated exceptional 
business acumen through strategic real estate investments transforming 
his community. In 2023, Freezy and Flo Rida made history by purchasing 
the Cloverleaf Shopping Plaza in Miami Gardens for $10 million, making 
it Miami Gardens' first entirely Black-owned shopping plaza--a 
milestone achievement in a city where Black residents comprise the 
majority yet historically lacked ownership of commercial real estate. 
Under Freezy and Flo Rida's ownership, the plaza has undergone 
significant improvements. Tenants have praised the transformation, 
noting enhanced security, better maintenance, and responsive 
management.
  Freezy's business portfolio also includes Studio 183, a popular 
entertainment hub, as well as investments in The Licking restaurant and 
Bar Idol. These strategic acquisitions demonstrate Freezy's commitment 
to creating economic opportunities and building wealth within the Black 
community.
  Mr. Speaker, while Freezy's business achievements are impressive, his 
most profound impact lies in his dedication to youth development and 
community service. Freezy co-founded Big Dreams for Kids with Flo Rida, 
a charitable organization encompassing multiple programs including the 
Florida Youth Football League, Strong Arm Elite Track Club, and FYFL 
Cheerleaders.
  The Florida Youth Football League has become one of the premier youth 
football organizations in South Florida, offering competitive football 
and cheerleading programs across 22 clubs throughout Broward County. 
The league provides structured athletic competition while emphasizing 
character development, academic achievement, teamwork, and leadership 
skills.
  Strong Arm Elite Track Club provides young athletes with high-level 
training in track and field. The program has produced numerous 
accomplished athletes who have competed at district, regional, state, 
and national levels. Known for his exceptional ability to develop 
talent, Freezy has personally aided scores of students in securing 
college scholarships and opportunities to pursue their dreams through 
athletics.
  Freezy's dedication to youth is exemplified through his personal 
involvement as a coach for his son in both the Florida Youth Football 
League and Strong Arm Elite Track Club. In 2024, Lee Prince, Jr. won 
the Florida High School Athletic Association Class 4A State 
Championship in the 100-meter dash, claiming the title of Florida State 
Champion. He also placed second in the 200-meter dash at the state 
championships, earning recognition as the Broward County 4A-3A Boys 
Track Athlete of the Year. As a running back for the St. Thomas Aquinas 
Raiders football team, Lee Prince, Jr. has also won a state football 
championship.
  Mr. Speaker, Freezy's commitment to community extends far beyond 
youth athletics. Every Thanksgiving, Freezy feeds community members, 
ensuring families in need have proper holiday meals. During Christmas, 
he provides toys to children. Throughout the year, Freezy actively 
supports worthwhile causes and charitable initiatives.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Lee ``Freezy'' Prince for 
his outstanding contributions to the music industry, his strategic 
business investments creating economic opportunities in Miami Gardens, 
and his profound dedication to youth development. Lee ``Freezy'' Prince 
is a true asset to Miami-Dade County, the State of Florida, and our 
Nation.
```
