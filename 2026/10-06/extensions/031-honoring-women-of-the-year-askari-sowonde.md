# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ASKARI SOWONDE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ASKARI SOWONDE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Askari Sowonde 
as she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. A longtime Vallejo resident, Askari has dedicated 
decades to strengthening her community through the arts, education, and 
civic engagement. Throughout her years in Vallejo, Askari has created 
opportunities for residents to come together, celebrate culture, and 
engage with important community issues. Through her production company, 
Askari Sowonde Productions, she has organized festivals, concerts, 
youth programs, and community events that have enriched Vallejo and 
fostered greater connection among its residents.
  One of Askari's most enduring contributions is the Annual Vallejo 
Community Kwanzaa Celebration. For more than 20 years, she has helped 
sustain this important local tradition, which remains Vallejo's only 
communitywide Kwanzaa celebration. Her work has also included Black 
History Month programs, art showcases, peace rallies, and events for 
children and families.
  Askari has also contributed to Vallejo through public service. She 
served on the City of Vallejo's Commission on Culture and the Arts, 
where she supported programs that expanded cultural and artistic 
opportunities, and with the Vallejo Naval & Historical Museum, helping 
preserve and share the city's diverse history. In recognition of her 
longstanding commitment to the community, the Vallejo Chapter of the 
National Council of Negro Women presented her with its Woman in Action 
Award in 2019.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to recognize Askari Sowonde for her decades of 
service, leadership, and dedication to the Vallejo community.
```
