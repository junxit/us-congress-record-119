# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CARMEN SLACK

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CARMEN SLACK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Carmen Slack as 
she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. Carmen has demonstrated a longstanding commitment to 
strengthening and celebrating the arts throughout Vallejo and Solano 
County. Through her leadership in the local arts community, she has 
helped create opportunities that bring residents together while 
ensuring that artists have meaningful spaces to share their talents and 
creativity.
  Carmen has been instrumental in supporting arts programming, grant 
writing, free public events, and the fiscal sponsorship of cultural 
activities throughout the year. She has also played an important role 
in the continued success of the Vallejo Art Walk, one of the city's 
longest-running cultural events. Through these efforts, local artists 
are given opportunities to showcase their work while residents are 
invited to gather, connect, and experience Vallejo's vibrant artistic 
community.
  As Director of the SoIano County Art Council, Carmen has continued to 
expand access to the arts and encourage broad participation across the 
community. Her work has helped ensure that local artists are supported, 
represented, and included in the cultural life of the region.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor Carmen Slack for her dedication to the arts 
and her many contributions to Vallejo and Solano County.

                          ____________________
```
