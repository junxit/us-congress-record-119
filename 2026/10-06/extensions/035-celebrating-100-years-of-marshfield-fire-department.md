# CELEBRATING 100 YEARS OF MARSHFIELD FIRE DEPARTMENT

**Speaking:** Alford, Mark (A000379) [R-MO]

```
          CELEBRATING 100 YEARS OF MARSHFIELD FIRE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK ALFORD

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. ALFORD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Marshfield 
Fire Protection District and congratulate Chief Michael Taylor, the 
department's firefighters and personnel, and the entire Marshfield 
community on this historic 100-year milestone.
  The Marshfield Fire Department was established on July 26, 1926, by a 
dedicated group of citizens who answered the call to protect their 
neighbors. For more than 80 years, volunteer firefighters carried that 
mission forward, responding to emergencies with courage, skill, and an 
unwavering commitment to their community.
  The Marshfield Fire Protection District was created in 2007 and 
continued to operate as an all-volunteer organization. As Marshfield 
grew and emergency-service demands increased, the district hired its 
first full-time firefighters in 2021. In 2023, it transitioned to a 24-
hour, three-shift staffing model to strengthen its emergency response 
capabilities.
  Today, 11 career and 44 volunteer firefighters serve side by side 
under Chief Taylor's leadership. Although the department has evolved to 
meet the community's changing needs, it's foundation remains unchanged: 
neighbors protecting neighbors. Its volunteers remain an essential part 
of the district, working alongside career personnel to protect the 
residents and visitors of Marshfield.
  Last month, the community marked this historic anniversary through 
traditions that honored the generations of firefighters who have shaped 
the department. A bell-ringing ceremony paid tribute to those who 
served faithfully and have since passed away, while the ceremonial 
passing of an axe from retired firefighters to the department's cadets 
symbolized the transfer of responsibility, knowledge, and commitment to 
the next generation.
  For a century, Marshfield's firefighters have carried forward a proud 
tradition of courage

[[Page E1042]]

and service. Their history belongs not only to those who built the 
department, but also to those who serve today and those preparing to 
answer the call tomorrow.
  Please join me, in congratulating Chief Taylor, the firefighters and 
personnel of the Marshfield Fire Protection District, and the 
Marshfield community on 100 years of service, and in honoring every 
firefighter, past and present, who has helped protect this community.
```
