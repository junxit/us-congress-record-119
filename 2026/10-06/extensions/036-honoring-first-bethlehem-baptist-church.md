# HONORING FIRST BETHLEHEM BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                HONORING FIRST BETHLEHEM BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor First Bethlehem 
Baptist Church of Alexandria, Louisiana, a longstanding and beloved 
institution in the local community. Founded in 1875, this historic 
church has played a vital role in the spiritual and social life of its 
members for more than 150 years. Situated in the heart of Alexandria, 
the church is a beacon of faith and hope for its congregation and the 
surrounding area.
  The church's rich history is rooted in the African-American community 
of Rapides Parish. From its humble beginnings as a small gathering of 
worshippers to its present-day status as a thriving congregation. First 
Bethlehem has weathered many challenges and triumphs, and through it 
all, it has remained steadfast in its commitment to serving God and 
ministering to the needs of its members. That unbroken history of 
service, stretching across more than a century and a half of change, is 
the foundation on which the church stands today.
  Over the years, First Bethlehem has expanded its outreach to include 
a wide range of programs and services designed to benefit the community 
at large. From youth ministries to senior programs, the church offers 
something for people of every age and background, caring for the 
youngest members of the congregation and honoring its elders alike. In 
addition to regular worship services, the church hosts community 
events, Bible studies, and social gatherings that bring people together 
in fellowship and unity, making the church a place where neighbors 
meet, families connect, and the bonds of community are strengthened.
  The leadership of First Bethlehem is dedicated to guiding and 
shepherding the congregation with wisdom and compassion. Under the 
guidance of Pastor John Smith, the church has grown and thrived, 
continuing to spread the message of God's love and grace to all who 
enter its doors. Pastor Smith's vision for the church is one of 
inclusion, diversity, and empowerment, as he leads the congregation in 
worship, service, and outreach.
  First Bethlehem's historic building and steeple are central landmarks 
in the Alexandria community, a symbol of faith and hope to all who pass 
by. Its choir and worship team lead powerful and uplifting services 
that inspire all who attend, and the church welcomes both long-time 
members and newcomers seeking spiritual guidance.
  The impact of First Bethlehem extends well beyond its own membership. 
As one of the longstanding congregations of Alexandria, and one with 
deep roots in the African-American community, it contributes to the 
strength of the broader faith community, standing alongside other 
churches in the shared work of nurturing faith, supporting families, 
and serving neighbors in need. Its example of steady, faithful service 
through good times and hard times continues to inspire those who 
worship there and those who look to it as a model of what a committed 
congregation can accomplish.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating First 
Bethlehem Baptist Church, Pastor John Smith, and the dedicated 
congregation for more than 150 years of faith, service, and fellowship 
to the Alexandria community. May First Bethlehem continue to be a 
pillar of strength and a light of hope in Alexandria for generations to 
come.
```
