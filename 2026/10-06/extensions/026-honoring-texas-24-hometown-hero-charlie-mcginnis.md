# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHARLIE McGINNIS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHARLIE McGINNIS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Charlie McGinnis. He is a freshman at St. Mark's School of Texas. 
When deciding his Eagle Scout project, he knew where to go. Just two 
years before, Charlie's older brother, James, completed his project at 
Juliette Fowler Communities. This inspired Charlie to pursue a project 
that would help make life easier for seniors living in the community.
  Charlie led a team, with fellow scouts from Troop 82 and his family, 
to complete a designated shared-ride station. The facility explained 
that due to the size of the campus, drivers have been having difficulty 
locating residents. The structure allows for easier pick-up, as well as 
provides a shaded area for residents to sit. A resident also added that 
the area can be used to watch games at the park.
  I thank Charlie McGinnis for his dedication to the North Texas 
community while on his path to becoming an Eagle Scout.
```
