# RECOGNIZING THE GRAFE FAMILY

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING THE GRAFE FAMILY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Grafe family 
and their remarkable legacy of farming, perseverance, and dedication to 
South Florida agriculture.
  The Grafe family's farming tradition began in 1890, when Emil Moritz 
Grafe immigrated from Germany through Ellis Island and began farming on 
Staten Island, New York. As the country changed, so did the family. In 
1919, they moved south and purchased land in Miami before establishing 
a home and flower farm in South Dade in 1925.
  For generations, the Grafe family continued to grow and adapt, 
farming vegetables and fruit before building a successful cut-flower 
business. In 1975, Herbert Grafe III established Grafe's Cut Flowers, 
eventually growing nearly 30 acres of flowers that were sold throughout 
Miami and shipped across the country.
  After increased competition from imported flowers forced the business 
to close in 1998, Herbert continued his agricultural career at Knaus 
Berry Farm until his retirement in 2024. Today, he carries on the 
family tradition by tending his lychee grove.
  For more than 130 years, the Grafe family has remained rooted in 
agriculture. Their story represents the hard work, resilience, and 
pioneering spirit that have helped shape South Dade and our 
agricultural community. It is my privilege to recognize the Grafe 
family and honor their generations of contributions to South Florida 
agriculture.
```
