# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ANJURLE DUCHAUSSEE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
             HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ANJURLE DUCHAUSSEE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Anjurle Duchaussee as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Through 
profound youth advocacy, years of civic volunteerism, and steadfast 
public service across Solano County, Anjurle exemplifies the absolute 
best of community leadership and compassion. As a dedicated volunteer 
with CASA (Court Appointed Special Advocates) of Solano County, Anjurle 
champions the mission of supporting Fairfield's underserved youth, 
families, and seniors. She is consistently the first to arrive and the 
last to leave, spearheading the logistics and physical setup for 
critical community initiatives.
  Anjurle also serves as the Administrative Lead for the Fairfield Fire 
Department, where colleagues and leadership view her as the 
foundational glue holding department operations together. When the 
department faced the heartbreaking loss of a firefighter who died in 
the line of duty, Anjurle stepped forward with tireless compassion to 
support donation drives and support networks for the family and agency, 
earning formal recognition and deep gratitude from the Fairfield 
Firefighters Association.
  Beyond her extensive public service and emergency management 
contributions, Anjurle is a loving mother and grandmother whose daily 
life is defined by quiet generosity and selfless devotion to others. 
She represents the best of Solano County and California's Eighth 
Congressional District and has fundamentally strengthened the safety, 
well-being, and heart of our community.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Anjurle Duchaussee for her 
positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
