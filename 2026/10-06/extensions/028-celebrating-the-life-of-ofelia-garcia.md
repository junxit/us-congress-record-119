# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF OFELIA GARCIA

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
                 CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF OFELIA GARCIA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 
the 105th birthday of Ms. Ofelia Garcia, whose life has been defined by 
perseverance, joy, and devotion to her family.
  Ms. Garcia was born in 1921 in San Pedro, Tamaulipas, Mexico, where 
she demonstrated great strength and determination from an early age. 
After the loss of both her parents, she was raised by her aunt. To 
support her family, she and her brother, Jose, traveled to Big Springs, 
Texas, to pick cotton, often carrying sacks heavier than she was.
  On October 7, 1950, Ms. Garcia married Leonel Garcia Sanchez. 
Together, they raised five children--Ruben, Salvador, Bertha, Leonel 
Jr., and Dora Ella--in Rancho La Palanga, Tamaulipas, Mexico. Her 
dedication to her family has remained a constant throughout her life, 
reflected in the care she provided for her ailing aunt while her 
husband worked in Mercedes, Texas.
  Ms. Garcia's strong work ethic is a true example of the American 
spirit. After becoming a U.S. Citizen in 1967, she and her family moved 
across the country in search of work, traveling to Ohio to pick produce 
and later to Michigan to work in a packing shed.
  Ms. Garcia is known for the joy she brings. Music has always been an 
important part of her life, whether she is doing chores, gardening, or 
dancing, while her sense of humor fills the room with laughter. She 
brings such warmth and positive spirit, touching everyone fortunate 
enough to know her.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas's 34th Congressional 
District, I am proud to honor Ofelia Garcia for a lifetime of love and 
devotion to her family. She is a source of pride and inspiration to all 
who know her, and I am honored to recognize her remarkable life.
```
