# HONORING PILGRIM MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING PILGRIM MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Pilgrim Missionary 
Baptist Church of Alexandria, Louisiana, affectionately known 
throughout Alexandria and the surrounding region as ``The Friendly 
Church.'' Since its founding in 1955, Pilgrim has served as a pillar of 
faith, fellowship, and community. Located at 410 Solomon Street in the 
heart of Alexandria, the church has long stood as a spiritual anchor 
for families seeking a Christ-centered place of worship rooted in the 
African American Baptist tradition. Today, the church is led by Pastor 
Tom Hamilton, Jr.
  The legacy of Pilgrim Missionary Baptist Church is closely tied to 
the ministry of Rev. Tom Hamilton, whose pastoral journey began in 
1987. Before shepherding the Pilgrim congregation, he ministered at New 
Zion Missionary Baptist Church in Colfax for 13 years and at Sunlight 
Baptist Church in Pineville for 7 years. His strong biblical teaching, 
compassionate leadership, and heart for service played a central role 
in strengthening the church's foundation, and under pastoral guidance 
Pilgrim has grown spiritually and numerically, building a strong 
tradition of worship, discipleship, and community presence.
  Throughout its history, Pilgrim has committed itself to fostering 
spiritual formation and community engagement. The church's mission, to 
build disciples, nurture relationships, and extend the love of Christ 
through service, has shaped every aspect of its ministry. Through 
weekly worship services, Bible studies, prayer gatherings, and 
community programs, Pilgrim gives its members opportunities to deepen 
their faith while also serving the broader Alexandria community. Its 
outreach efforts, including annual back-to-school supply drives and 
various youth initiatives, reflect its belief that ministry extends 
beyond the sanctuary and into the neighborhoods it serves.
  That history of service is at the heart of Pilgrim's identity. For 
more than 70 years, the church has been a place where families find a 
warm welcome, where young people are encouraged and supported, and 
where neighbors know they can turn for prayer and help. Supplying 
school materials to children preparing for a new school year, and 
investing in the lives of youth, are expressions of a congregation that 
understands its calling to lift up the community around it, and they 
are the reason Pilgrim has earned its reputation as a community-
centered church.
  The impact of Pilgrim extends well beyond its own membership. As a 
longstanding congregation in Alexandria, rooted in the African American 
Baptist tradition, it contributes to the strength of the broader faith 
community, standing alongside other churches in the shared work of 
nurturing faith, supporting families, and serving those in need. Its 
longstanding engagement in civic life and its dedication to uplifting 
families and supporting youth make it a beacon of hope in Alexandria 
and a model of what a welcoming, service-minded congregation can be.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in honoring Pilgrim 
Missionary Baptist

[[Page E1044]]

Church, Pastor Tom Hamilton, Jr., and the dedicated congregation for 
more than 70 years of faith, fellowship, and service to the Alexandria 
community. May Pilgrim continue forward in its ministry, rooted in 
faith and guided by tradition, for generations to come.
```
