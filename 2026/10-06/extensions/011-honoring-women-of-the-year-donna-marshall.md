# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DONNA MARSHALL

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DONNA MARSHALL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 6, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Donna Marshall as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. For nearly 
three decades, Donna has served the Benicia community as a special 
education teacher and dedicated volunteer with numerous organizations, 
including Benicia Reads, Solano County Adopt-a-Poll, and the Lions 
Vision Clinic. In her current role as Co-Director of the Community Meal 
at St. Paul's Episcopal Church, Donna exemplifies compassion and 
leadership through her work to address food insecurity and social 
isolation. By recruiting and training volunteers, coordinating meals, 
securing donations, and fundraising, Donna has helped grow the program 
from serving approximately 100 meals once a week to providing roughly 
550 meals over two days each week. The Community Meal also distributes 
groceries and thousands of pounds of food each month to individuals and 
families in need. Donna has built meaningful partnerships with local 
organizations, businesses, and food providers that help sustain these 
efforts. Just as importantly, she has fostered an environment where 
every guest is treated with dignity, compassion, and respect.
  Donna's service extends throughout Benicia. As Treasurer of the 
Benicia Kiwanis Foundation, she helps support scholarships, literacy 
programs, food assistance, and other local initiatives. Her dedication 
has previously been recognized with a Community Service Award from the 
Kiwanis Club of Benicia. Donna has demonstrated a steadfast commitment 
to serving others and building a stronger, more caring community.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Donna Marshall for her 
outstanding service and positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
