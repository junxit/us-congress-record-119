# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Zack Randles, Waterfront Church, Washington, D.C., offered the 
following prayer:
  Awesome, almighty God, we come before You today on behalf of the 
Members of this body, their staff teams, their families, and our 
country. Every 2 years around this time, emotions are high and many 
feel like their hope and their future hang in the balance. Every issue, 
big and small, is magnified, and the prospect of coming up short can 
take you to a place of great fear or even despair.
  But our hope and our future, our peace of mind and our joy are too 
valuable to be entrusted to our efforts and our government alone. We 
remember the words of Psalm 40:4-5: ``Blessed is the man who makes the 
Lord his trust, who does not turn to the proud, to those who go astray 
after a lie. You have multiplied, O Lord my God, Your wondrous deeds 
and Your thoughts toward us; none can compare with You. I will proclaim 
and tell of them, yet they are more than can be told.''
  For whatever is ahead, we trust You, O Lord. For whoever will sit in 
these seats, we know You will prepare them for the moment. For any 
difficult or murky situations we will encounter, we believe You will 
provide in abundance. Give us confidence today to trust You with what 
You have already written. Also, bless and protect our troops.
  We ask this in the name of Jesus.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
