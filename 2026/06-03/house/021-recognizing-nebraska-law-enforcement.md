# RECOGNIZING NEBRASKA LAW ENFORCEMENT

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                  RECOGNIZING NEBRASKA LAW ENFORCEMENT

  (Mr. FLOOD asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FLOOD. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize the heroes of Nebraska 
law enforcement.
  Last Friday, as I was participating in the annual La Vista Days 
celebration, the Papillion Police Department responded to an active 
shooter at a shopping center just down the road.
  Officers located the suspect and began a pursuit that ended in a busy 
parking lot nearby, where the officers opened fire. The threat was 
neutralized, and any injuries to the public or law enforcement did not 
happen.
  When the community faced an armed gunman, the men and women of 
Papillion P.D., La Vista P.D., and the Sarpy County Sheriff's Office 
ran towards danger. Because of their bravery, everybody went home safe.
  Their courage is a testament to the sacrifice law enforcement 
officers make every day they put on the uniform. I am proud to back the 
blue, and I thank those serving in law enforcement for keeping us safe.

                          ____________________
```
