# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9129

**Measures:** H.R. 9129

```
            By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
        H.R. 9129.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress derives its authority to enact this legislation 
     from Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (the Commerce Clause), 
     which grants Congress the power to regulate Commerce with 
     foreign Nations, as this bill regulates the importation and 
     commercial use of robotic systems manufactured by entities 
     subject to the ownership, control, or direction of the 
     People's Republic of China.
```
