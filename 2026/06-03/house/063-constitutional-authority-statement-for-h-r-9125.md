# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9125

**Measures:** H.R. 9125

```
           By Ms. JACOBS:
       H.R. 9125.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which grants Congress the power to regulate interstate 
     commerce and make all laws necessary and proper for carrying 
     into execution its enumerated powers.
```
