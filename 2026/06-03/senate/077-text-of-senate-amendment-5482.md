# Text of Senate Amendment 5482

**Speaking:** Hickenlooper, John W. (H000273) [D-CO]

```
  SA 5482. Mr. HICKENLOOPER submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  No funds made available by this title may be 
     obligated or expended unless the Department of Homeland 
     Security rescinds all restrictions limiting Members of 
     Congress and their staff from exercising their constitutional 
     oversight authority by visiting immigration detention 
     facilities.
                                 ______
```
