# PARTY PRIORITIES

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                            PARTY PRIORITIES

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, this week, with the contrast between the 
two parties in bright lights, Republicans are consumed by Donald 
Trump's priorities: his corrupt slush fund, his gilded ballroom, his 
rogue police force, his reckless war with Iran, and his refusal to 
contain the rising costs Americans face every week. Democrats are 
focused on the American people's priorities: lowering costs, bringing 
stability, and fighting back against this administration's rampant--
rampant--corruption.
  At a time when families are struggling and looking for answers, that 
contrast matters. Republicans are fighting for Trump. Democrats are 
fighting for the American people. And we will keep driving that 
contrast every day, on every issue.
```
