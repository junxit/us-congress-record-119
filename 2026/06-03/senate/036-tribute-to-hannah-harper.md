# TRIBUTE TO HANNAH HARPER

**Speaking:** Schmitt, Eric (S001227) [R-MO]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO HANNAH HARPER

● Mr. SCHMITT. Mr. President, today, I rise to honor Hannah 
Harper of Willow Springs, MO. Since she was a child, the Harper family 
has been firmly immersed in the American staples of bluegrass and folk 
music, and this background has helped mold her into a truly remarkable 
vocalist. A lifelong Missourian with deep roots in the Ozarks, she has 
balanced raising a family with her rapidly growing musical career. Her 
ability to address real life topics through her art, such as the 
challenges and blessing of parenthood through her heartfelt song 
``String Cheese,'' is truly inspiring.
  Recently, Hannah won the 24th season of American Idol. I have no 
doubt that she has a bright future ahead of her and will continue to 
bring our home State's unique musical heritage to the national stage. 
Hannah Harper has represented the Show-Me-State very well, and I am 
proud to call her a fellow Missourian and parent to three wonderful 
children. I wish her all the best, both in her family life, as well as 
in her career.●

                          ____________________
```
