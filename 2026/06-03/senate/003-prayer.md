# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Lord our God, restorer of the joy of those who find You, we praise 
Your holy Name.
  Today, strengthen our Senators. Infuse them with integrity, humility, 
and courage. Empower them to disagree without being disagreeable, as 
they seek to protect our constitutional Republic. May their primary aim 
be to please You. Remove from them clouds of frustration as they strive 
to seize those opportunities that best serve the interests of freedom.
  Lord, instruct and teach them in the way You desire them to go.
  And, Lord, bring peace to our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
