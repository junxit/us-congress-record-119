# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MR. SPENCER LEAK, JR.

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MR. SPENCER LEAK, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANNY K. DAVIS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 3, 2026

  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the life and 
legacy of Mr. Spencer Leak, Jr., who passed away a few days ago 
unexpectedly at the young age of 56.
  Mr. Spencer Leak, Jr. was a member of one of Chicago's most prominent 
families, the Leak family. He was following in the footsteps of his 
grandfather, Reverend A.R. Leak, and his father, the iconic Spencer 
Leak.
  Spencer, Jr. was Vice President of Leak and Sons Funeral Home and a 
respected business and civic leader whose life carried forward a family 
tradition that has endured for three generations, staying steadfast to 
the belief that every family is entitled to honor their loved ones with 
dignity, care, and respect regardless of financial circumstances. 
Through his dedication to the funeral services profession, he was 
defined by service compassion and unwavering commitment to the people 
of Chicago.
  Mr. Spencer Leak, Jr. was rooted in family; he was a champion for 
Cook County's business community and a generous mentor to aspiring 
entrepreneurs. In many instances he would share his history as a 
working adolescent within the family business--answering phones, 
washing limousines and carrying out countless assignments that helped 
shape his character and prepare him to continue a proud family legacy.

[[Page E521]]

  Alongside his parents and siblings, Spencer offered comfort and 
guidance during some of life's challenging times, earning the trust and 
admiration of families and communities throughout Chicago and beyond. 
Beyond his professional accomplishments, Spencer worked actively on 
civic and community issues. He served selflessly on my 7th 
Congressional District Health Sciences Public Policy working group 
where he used his expertise and insights to help shape educational 
initiatives into public action for funeral service professionals, 
families, and the community as a whole. His voice was invaluable and 
his commitment to serving others never wavered. His contributions to 
our city, our district, and the funeral service profession will not be 
forgotten.
  Spencer Leak, Jr. leaves behind a legacy of leadership, compassion, 
community, and stewardship that will continue to inspire generations. 
Our lives through Spencer, Jr., have been touched with compassionate 
greatness. May he rest in peace.
```
