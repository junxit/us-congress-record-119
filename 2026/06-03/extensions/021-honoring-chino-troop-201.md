# HONORING CHINO TROOP 201

**Speaking:** Torres, Norma J. (T000474) [D-CA]

```
                        HONORING CHINO TROOP 201

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NORMA J. TORRES

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 3, 2026

  Mrs. TORRES of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Chino 
Troop 201 as it celebrates its 100th anniversary.
  Troop 201 was founded in 1926 by Vernon Decker, Bob Brady, and Ernest 
Soper. The three partnered with the American Legion to launch Chino's 
first Scout troop, making it the oldest Scouts troop in my district.
  Troop 201's first Scoutmaster was Jon Cooley. In the early days of 
the troop's founding, the troop did not yet have a Scout house. Members 
of Troop 201 held their early meetings at the ``Thimble Club,'' a local 
spot in Chino.
  In 1935, the City of Chino recognized the growth of the organization 
and worked with the troop to help its development. The city left a 
piece of land for the troop to use as a home. The Scout house gave the 
troop a stable place to meet and a secure area to store gear, 
guaranteeing that scouts in Chino had a roof over their heads.
  Troop 201's founders, sponsors, and the City of Chino have supported 
the community centered around Chino Rotary Club. For the past 100 
years, the troop has provided young leaders with opportunities for 
adventure, service, and character development, shaping them during key 
transformative years.
  In 1941, Troop 201 partnered with the Chino Rotary Club, uniting in 
their commitment to promoting community, leadership, and service. Since 
then, scouts in this troop have contributed thousands of hours to 
community service, outdoor education, and leadership training.
  The current organization leadership consists of Patricia Cox, the 
Chartered Organization Representative, Maritza Sanchez, Troop Committee 
Chair, and Jeffrey Baker, the current Scoutmaster. Under the direction 
of organization leaders and dedicated parents, families gather at the 
John Anderson Scout Center every Monday night for regular meetings.
  Beyond weekly meetings, an outdoor adventure is offered each month in 
addition to four major events each year. Scouts can choose between 
canoeing down the Colorado River, backpacking and fishing by the 
Eastern Sierra Mountain Range, developing skills on a desert trip that 
offers training on rifle shootings, rappelling, and rocketry, or 
attending an intensive summer camp focused on advancement and skill-
building.
  This year, a special fifth major event will be offered: Troop 201's 
Centennial Celebration. The celebration will honor all scouts who have 
shared fond memories with the organization for more than 100 years, in 
addition to more than 125 Eagle Scouts from Troop 201 who have 
contributed to more than 600 community service hours each year.
  In celebration of the 100th anniversary of Chino Rotary Club Troop 
201, it is my privilege to honor such a remarkable organization. Its 
impact on our young leaders in Chino and beyond is worthy of the 
highest commendation.
```
