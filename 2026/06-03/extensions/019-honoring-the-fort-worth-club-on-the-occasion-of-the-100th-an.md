# HONORING THE FORT WORTH CLUB ON THE OCCASION OF THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ITS HISTORIC SEVENTH STREET LOCATION

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING THE FORT WORTH CLUB ON THE OCCASION OF THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY 
                OF ITS HISTORIC SEVENTH STREET LOCATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 3, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor the Fort Worth Club on the occasion of the 100th anniversary of 
its historic Seventh Street location in Fort Worth. This remarkable 
milestone marks a century of service as one of the premier social, 
civic, and business institutions in North Texas.
  Since the completion of its construction, the Fort Worth Club has 
stood as a distinguished gathering place for leaders in business, 
govemment, and public life. Over the decades, its halls have welcomed 
notable figures including Franklin D. Roosevelt, James H. Doolittle, 
and countless civic leaders, athletes, and prominent members of the 
community.
  The Fort Worth Club has served not only as a venue for fellowship and 
commerce, but also as a cornerstone of civic engagement and cultural 
tradition. The building is home to the Davey O'Brien Foundation Hall of 
Fame and annually hosts the prestigious Davey O'Brien Award, 
recognizing studen-athletes who exemplify excellence in teamwork, 
sportsmanship, leadership, and academics.
  In addition, the Club continues to provide a home for a wide range of 
businesses and organizations, including distinguished law firms, energy 
companies, technology enterprises, nonprofit organizations, and public 
offices, all of which contribute to the continued growth and vitality 
of the Fort Worth community.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
the Fort Worth Club on 100 years at its Seventh Street location and in 
recognizing its enduring legacy of leadership, service, and commitment 
to the people of Fort Worth and the State of Texas.
```
