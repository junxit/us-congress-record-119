# HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE AND RETIREMENT OF TIMOTHY T. BOYDE

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
     HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE AND RETIREMENT OF TIMOTHY T. BOYDE

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 3, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker. I rise to recognize Timothy T. Boyde for 
his career of

[[Page E516]]

public service spanning four decades of dedication to local government. 
While his distinguished career spanned three states, it is his decade 
of leadership in Allegany County, New York that I wish to highlight 
today. Since joining the County Administrator's Office in 2016, Tim has 
brought steady leadership, sound judgment, and an unwavering commitment 
to the people of Allegany County. Through times of challenge and 
opportunity alike, he helped guide county government with 
professionalism, integrity, and a deep respect for the taxpayers he 
served.
  Tim's career has been defined by a commitment to effective 
government. He understood that public service is not about recognition, 
but about results. Throughout his tenure in Allegany County, he worked 
tirelessly to strengthen county operations, promote fiscal 
responsibility, improve efficiency, and support initiatives that 
enhanced the quality of life for residents. His ability to bring people 
together, solve complex problems, and navigate difficult decisions 
earned the respect of elected officials, department leaders, employees, 
and community partners alike. Just as importantly, he invested in those 
around him, mentoring colleagues and helping cultivate the next 
generation of public servants.
  As Tim closes this remarkable chapter of service, we reflect not only 
on his accomplishments, but on the example he set. His career stands as 
a testament to the vital role that dedicated public servants play in 
strengthening our communities and ensuring that government works for 
the people it serves. On behalf of the constituents of New York's 23rd 
Congressional District, and the taxpayers of Allegany County, I extend 
my sincere gratitude and best wishes in his retirement. I ask this body 
to rise and join me in recognizing a life of service.

                          ____________________
```
