# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 978

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 7 o'clock and 10 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 978, 
the House adjourned until tomorrow, Thursday, January 8, 2026, at 10 
a.m. for morning-hour debate and noon for legislative business, as a 
further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable Doug 
LaMalfa.
  Thereupon (at 7 o'clock and 10 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, Thursday, January 8, 2026, 
at 10 a.m. for morning-hour debate.
```
