# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine the nominations of Gregory Ibach, of Nebraska, to 
be Under Secretary of Agriculture for Trade and Foreign Agricultural 
Affairs, who was introduced by Senator Fischer, and Carl Bednarski, of 
Michigan, who was introduced by Senator Slotkin, John Grunewald II, of

and answered questions in their own behalf.

Member of the Federal Communications Commission, and Joshua Shoemaker, 
of Virginia, to be Chief Financial Officer, and Robert H. Steinau, of

Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine the nomination of Justin Schwab, of the District of 
Columbia, to be an Assistant Administrator, Office of Air and 
Radiation, Environmental Protection Agency, after the nominee testified 
and answered questions in his own behalf.
NOMINATIONS

Counsel, both of the Department of the Treasury, and Christopher 
Abbott, of Virginia, to be an Assistant Secretary of Commerce, after 
the nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
