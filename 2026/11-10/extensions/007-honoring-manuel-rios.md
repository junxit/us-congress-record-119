# HONORING MANUEL RIOS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING MANUEL RIOS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Manuel 
Rios for being named as the Napa County Farm Bureau Member of the Year 
at the annual Love of the Land celebration. The Farm Bureau recognizes 
his outstanding contributions to Napa Valley, his leadership in our 
wine community, and his longstanding commitment to serving our 
agricultural community.
  Beginning at age 10, he worked alongside his father in Napa Valley 
vineyards, developing a deep understanding of the land and the care 
required to produce exceptional wine grapes. Driven by a desire to 
expand his knowledge, Manuel completed viticulture courses at Napa 
Valley College and pursued vineyard management opportunities throughout 
California, including Lake, Mendocino, Solano, Santa Barbara, and 
Sonoma counties. His professional experience eventually took him 
internationally to Australia and Argentina, where he managed vineyards 
for large corporate operations and gained valuable perspectives on 
viticulture and agricultural management.
  In 1998, Manuel returned to Napa Valley and founded Rios Farming 
Company, LLC. In 2005, Manuel expanded his agricultural endeavors into 
winemaking, establishing Rios Wine Company. Today, Rios Wine Company 
produces approximately 3,500 cases annually, with a winery located in 
the Aetna Springs area of northern Napa County. Manuel has 
distinguished himself as a pioneering Hispanic agricultural 
entrepreneur, respected vineyard management professional, and dedicated 
community leader.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Manuel has demonstrated an 
unwavering commitment to agricultural advocacy, farmworker 
representation, and community service. As a former President and 
current Board Member of the Napa County Farm Bureau, Manuel has helped 
protect the interests of local farmers, vineyard owners, and 
agricultural businesses. He is a trusted representative of the 
agricultural community, helping bridge communication between growers, 
farmworkers, and community leaders. His service as a Board Member of 
the Napa County Housing Commission and support for Napa's farmworker 
centers reflects his broader commitment to addressing housing needs and 
improving opportunities for those who live and work in the region.
  His contributions also extend to the next generation of agricultural 
leaders. Through ongoing donations, sponsorships, and participation in 
community events, Manuel supports Napa County 4-H, Future Farmers of 
America (FFA), and initiatives that encourage young people to pursue 
agricultural education and careers.
  Mr. Speaker, Manuel Rios has a legacy that is defined by his lasting 
commitment to Napa Valley's agricultural heritage, its workforce, and 
the future of the community he calls home. Therefore, it is fitting and 
proper that we honor my friend Manny Rios here today.
```
