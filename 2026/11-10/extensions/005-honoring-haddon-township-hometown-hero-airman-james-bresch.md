# HONORING HADDON TOWNSHIP HOMETOWN HERO AIRMAN JAMES BRESCH

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
       HONORING HADDON TOWNSHIP HOMETOWN HERO AIRMAN JAMES BRESCH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and commend Airman 
James Bresch. On Thursday, September 10, 2026, family, friends, and 
members of the community gathered at Haddon Township High School in 
Haddon Township, New Jersey to honor and recognize Airman James Bresch 
for his service to both his community and the United States of America.
  Originally born in Bridgeton, New Jersey, Airman James Bresch 
enlisted in the United States Navy on November 29, 1961. James was 
stationed at the Naval Base in Mayport, Florida where he served on CVA-
42, also known as the USS Franklin D. Roosevelt. During his time in 
active duty, Airman James Bresch was deployed to the Mediterranean Sea, 
where he served on one of two carriers' tasked with maintaining United 
States naval capabilities in the region during the Cuban Missile 
Crisis. While serving on the USS Franklin D. Roosevelt, James served on 
the flight deck, helping move, launch, and recover naval aircraft. On 
November 23, 1963, James Bresch was honorably discharged from the 
United States Navy, achieving the rank of Airman E-3.
  Following his active-duty service, James invested in his community by 
volunteering with Little League, Girls Softball and Township Soccer. He 
raised his daughters Tricia and Michelle and his son James alongside 
his wife Pat, and for 36 and a half years he worked as a Lab Technician 
for E.I. Dupont, earning several awards throughout his career for his 
individual efforts.
  Today, Airman James Bresch has been a resident of Haddon Township for 
58 years and shares immense pride in his family. His Grandson, Joey 
Zuccarelli, following a nomination from Congressman Donald Norcross, 
graduated 24th in a class of 1,107 students at the United States 
Military Academy at West Point prior to earning a full ride to Harvard 
to earn his master's degree. His granddaughter Kaci Broadhurst is a 
current high school student with plans to also apply to West Point in 
the coming years, following in the footsteps of her family's long 
history of service.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask you to join me in honoring and commending Airman 
James Bresch, a citizen dedicated to both his country and community, 
and who has thoroughly served them both to the best of their ability.
```
