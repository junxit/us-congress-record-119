# RECOGNIZING LEGENDARY NEW ORLEANIANS BISHOP PAUL SYLVESTER MORTON, PASTOR EMERITUS AND DR. DEBRA BROWN "PASTOR D" MORTON

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
  RECOGNIZING LEGENDARY NEW ORLEANIANS BISHOP PAUL SYLVESTER MORTON, 
        PASTOR EMERITUS AND DR. DEBRA BROWN ``PASTOR D'' MORTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a 
phenomenal New Orleans couple, Bishop Paul Sylvester Morton and Dr. 
Debra Brown ``Pastor D'' Morton, true spiritual and community leaders, 
friends, and mentors to many across Louisiana's 2nd Congressional 
District and the entire world.
  Bishop Paul S. Morton was born in Windsor Ontario, Canada. Born into 
a Christian family, his father pastored two congregations, one in 
Windsor, Ontario and the other in Detroit, Michigan. In 1972, he moved 
to New Orleans, Louisiana. Not long after, he became the Senior Pastor 
of the Greater Saint Stephen Missionary Baptist Church. Shortly after 
his appointment as senior pastor of Greater St. Stephen, Morton married 
the former Debra Brown. He served faithfully there for 33 years. Under 
his leadership, it became a ``Full Gospel'' Baptist Church, expanding 
to three areas of the city, maximizing at seven services per Sunday.
  Greater Saint Stephen Missionary Baptist Church was founded in 1937 
by 69 faithful members and was pastored by Reverend Arthur Armstead 
Gundy. A few distinguished founding members included the late Mrs. 
Senobia Scott, grandmother of our esteemed former co-pastor, Dr. Debra 
Brown Morton; as well as the late Mrs. Edmonia Nelson Rouselle, well 
known educator and mother of New Orleans' own the late William ``Bill'' 
Rouselle, Jr., two courageous women, astute enough to create this 
remarkable House of Worship. After Reverend Gundy's passing, the 
church's legacy was then led by Reverend Percy Simpson, Jr., Bishop 
Paul S. Morton, Sr., and Dr. Debra B. Morton. Each of these inspiring 
and devoted leaders have incorporated programs to highlight the 
intersection of religious and community support.
  New Orleans native, Dr. Debra B. Morton is a charismatic leader, 
author, visionary, and entrepreneur who served diligently alongside her 
husband, Bishop Paul S. Morton, as Senior Pastor of Greater St. Stephen 
Full Gospel Baptist Church in New Orleans, Louisiana and Co-Pastor of 
Changing A Generation Full Gospel Baptist Church in Atlanta, Georgia, 
two internationally renowned and thriving ministries.
  Co-Pastor Morton serves as the General Overseer of the women's 
division and the Daughters of The Promise of the Full Gospel Baptist 
Church. Dr. Morton's sphere of influence profoundly expanded far beyond 
local pastorate in 1987 when she founded the Greater Women of 
Excellence Ministry (WOE). As the founder of WOE, Dr. Morton nurtured 
thousands of women and hosted motivational conferences annually for 
over 15 years.
  In 1993, Debra B. Morton was ordained as an elder and earned the 
ground-breaking distinction as one of the first ordained female elders 
to preach in a Baptist pulpit. It was her gregarious personality that 
garnered her local and national popularity with her television and 
radio talk shows, ``Morning Coffee'' where discussions of everyday life 
were infused with spiritual application. It was then Dr. Morton 
proclaimed, ``Jesus is better than a cup of coffee; He is Lord.''
  A Stellar Award nominee and James Cleveland award winner, Dr. Morton 
has gone on to executive produce an album and many songs, author 
several books, launch a fragrance line, open a troubled teen girls' 
home and assist her husband rebuild and expand a great ministry. Of all 
her many accomplishments, Dr. Morton takes greatest pride in these: her 
personal relationship with Jesus Christ, her role as a wife, mother of 
three children--Jasmine (Tyron), Paul, Jr. (Kortni), and Christiann 
(Julian)--and grandmother of Elvin, Jr., London Paul, Jakai, Paul, III, 
Peyton, Julian, Jr., ``Princess'' Reign, and little Sage (the newest 
edition). Her eternal goal is to tear down the kingdom of darkness as 
she prophetically brings light to all who will hear her. Their son 
Paul, Jr. later became a Grammy Award-winning musician under the name 
PJ Morton.
  During their ministry at Greater St. Stephen Full Gospel Baptist 
Church, the Morton's established the Full Gospel Baptist Church 
Fellowship which initially began as a Charismatic Baptist movement 
within the National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc. Pastor Morton was 
consecrated into the episcopacy in March 1993 by George Augustus 
Stallings in New Orleans. The movement became its own separate 
denomination in 1994.
  By 1997, Greater St. Stephen grew to 18,000 members in 3 locations 
under his pastorate. In November 1993, they, along with J. Delano 
Ellis, Wilbert Sterling McKinley, and Roy E. Brown, established the 
Joint College of African American Pentecostal Bishops. In 2005, the 
Morton's founded Changing A Generation Full Gospel Baptist Church in 
Metro Atlanta. In 2013, Bishop Paul Morton announced his intent to 
retire as Presiding Bishop of the Full Gospel Baptist Church 
Fellowship. Two years later, in 2015, he retired from his office as 
leader of Full Gospel.
  To reflect the new vision of the ministry in 1933, Bishop Morton and 
Dr. Debra B. Morton established the Full Gospel Baptist Church 
Fellowship International with Dr. Morton as the mother of Fellowship 
and Visionary for the Women. Dr. Debra ``Pastor D'' B. Morton was 
Senior Pastor for 13 years and recently transitioned to Pastor 
Emeritus/Overseer. The mantle has been passed down to her son-in-law, 
Dr. Tyron Delbert Robinson and her eldest daughter, First Lady, Elder 
Jasmine K. Morton Robinson.
  Greater St. Stephen Full Gospel Baptist Church has continuously 
persevered through the homegoings of Pastors Gundy and Simpson, natural 
disasters including deadly storms like Hurricane Katrina, a devastating 
fire of the ministry's original Uptown location, and most recently the 
Coronavirus. They have played a vital role within the community and 
have dedicated themselves to spiritually uplifting the community and 
its congregation. I am proud to be a member and to have a sacred space 
within its walls. I am especially proud to say my Dear Mother, Mrs. 
Eartha Flemings Carter, prayerfully served this Congregation as an 
ordained Elder. There are deep roots in my family at Greater St. 
Stephen Full Gospel Baptist Church.
  I am honored to recognize Pastor Emeritus Paul Sylvester Morton and 
Dr. Debra ``Pastor D'' Morton and the entire Greater St. Stephen Full 
Gospel Baptist Church Family, affectionally known as my spiritual home. 
We are blessed to have experienced their vision and leadership 
throughout the entire Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship 
International, and for their commitment and service in honoring and 
uplifting God in our shared community. I thank them for so proudly 
shining the light of our Lord upon the Greater New Orleans Community 
and the entire World.
```
