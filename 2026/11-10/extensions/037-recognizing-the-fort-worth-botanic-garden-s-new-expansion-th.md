# RECOGNIZING THE FORT WORTH BOTANIC GARDEN'S NEW EXPANSION, THE BAKER MARTIN FAMILY GARDEN

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE FORT WORTH BOTANIC GARDEN'S NEW EXPANSION, THE BAKER 
                          MARTIN FAMILY GARDEN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Fort 
Worth Botanic Garden's expansion, the Baker Martin Family Garden, an 
exciting new addition that will provide Fort Worth families with 
opportunities to learn, explore, and connect with nature.
  The two-acre garden is the first major expansion of the Botanic 
Garden's 20-year Master Plan and marks an important milestone and a new 
chapter in the continued growth of this treasured Fort Worth 
institution. Designed with children and families in mind, the garden 
will feature a variety of interactive spaces that encourage 
exploration, creativity, and appreciation for the natural world, while 
providing accessible experiences for all visitors.
  With the garden scheduled to offer early access in November 2026 and 
hold its grand opening in March 2027, this project will further 
strengthen the Fort Worth Botanic Garden's role as a destination for 
education, conservation, and community engagement.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize the Fort Worth Botanic Garden and everyone who helped make 
the Baker Martin Family Garden possible. I congratulate them on this 
exciting milestone and wish them continued success in the years to 
come.
```
