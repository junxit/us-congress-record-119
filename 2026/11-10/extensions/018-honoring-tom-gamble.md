# HONORING TOM GAMBLE

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING TOM GAMBLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Tom 
Gamble for being named as the Napa County Farm Bureau Agriculturist of 
the Year at the annual Love of the Land celebration. The Farm Bureau 
recognizes his exceptional contributions to Napa Valley agriculture, 
longstanding leadership in agricultural preservation, and commitment to 
environmental stewardship.
  Tom Gamble grew up in Napa Valley as a first-generation vigneron and 
third-generation member of a family whose agricultural roots in Napa 
County date back to 1916. His upbringing was deeply connected to the 
family's farming and ranching operations, where he developed an 
appreciation for agriculture through experiences picking walnuts, 
irrigating alfalfa, and working cattle on family ranchland. His father, 
George Gamble, was a longtime Berryessa-area cattle rancher who began 
ranching in the region in 1960. George received the Napa County Farm 
Bureau's Love of the Land recognition as Agriculturist of the Year in 
2009.
  Tom's agricultural career began in 1981 when he purchased his first 
vineyard while attending the University of California, Davis. He 
developed extensive experience across viticulture, cattle ranching, and 
agricultural business management. In 1999, Tom and his brother

[[Page E1182]]

Jim Gamble co-founded Napa Free Range Beef, one of Napa County's first 
grass-finished beef operations. Alongside his cousin Launce Gamble, Tom 
continues to co-manage family ranchland and leased vineyards. In 2005, 
Tom and his wife, Colette, founded their winery, now known as Gamble 
Estates. Today, Gamble Estates produces wines from certified organic 
vineyards in Oakville, Rutherford, Mount Veeder, and Yountville. The 
winery's approach emphasizes soil health, ecological balance, and the 
preservation of each vineyard site's unique characteristics.
  Throughout his career, Tom has demonstrated a commitment to 
protecting the region's agricultural resources, advancing sustainable 
farming practices, and ensuring the long-term viability of local 
agriculture. As a longtime leader within the Napa County Farm Bureau, 
Tom served nearly two decades on its Board of Directors, including 
terms as President and Vice President. He played an important role in 
local land-use policy, particularly through his involvement in the 
development of the 2008 Napa County General Plan. His environmental 
contributions include involvement with the Napa River Restoration 
Project and Fish Friendly Farming, supporting efforts to improve 
watershed health, restore natural habitats, and encourage 
environmentally responsible agricultural practices.
  Mr. Speaker, Tom Gamble has demonstrated the ability to integrate 
agricultural tradition with innovative farming practices, building 
businesses that reflect both his family's heritage and his vision for 
the future of Napa Valley agriculture. Therefore, it is fitting and 
proper that we honor him here today.
```
