# REPUBLICAN VOTE TO BLOCK SEC. HEGSETH'S IMPEACHMENT

**Speaking:** McCollum, Betty (M001143) [D-MN]

```
          REPUBLICAN VOTE TO BLOCK SEC. HEGSETH'S IMPEACHMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETTY McCOLLUM

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Ms. McCOLLUM. Mr. Speaker, today, House Republicans voted to refer 
articles of impeachment against Secretary of Defense Hegseth to the 
Judiciary Committee instead of allowing a vote on the floor. This vote 
is another attempt by Republicans to shield Secretary Hegseth from the 
accountability Americans deserve. Since Hegseth's nomination by 
President Trump, I have stated concerns that he was ill-prepared and 
not fit to serve as Secretary of Defense, and questioned Trump's 
judgement in nominating him. Sadly, these concerns were justified.
  Mr. Hegseth swore an oath to support and defend the Constitution of 
the United States, he has willfully disregarded his responsibility to 
faithfully execute the laws of the United States. His removal is long 
overdue, but Republicans have once again refused to hold him 
accountable. Less than three months after his confirmation by the U.S. 
Senate, I called on Secretary Hegseth to resign for mishandling 
classified information when he shared war plans on a phone messaging 
application. His tenure has been marked by chaos and cruelty, which 
impacts our servicemembers most directly.
  Secretary Hegseth's prosecution of the War in Iran is clearly in 
contravention of the War Powers Resolution of 1973. Congress has 
neither declared war, nor passed statutory authorization, and the 
United States was not attacked by Iran. Secretary Hegseth's actions 
have left our servicemembers dangerously vulnerable and placed the 
United States in an expanding regional conflict. Hegseth has 
systemically dismantled the office and systems designed to mitigate 
civilian harm in hostilities, and that he stated were ``stupid rules of 
engagement.'' His willful disregard for U.S. laws is a clear 
demonstration that he is unfit to serve. His reckless actions have 
contributed to the horrific killing of civilians in Iran, including at 
least 120 children. Secretary Hegseth does not get to choose which 
parts of U.S. law he wants to follow.
  From the beginning, I've said that Secretary Hegseth was unfit to 
lead our men and women in uniform. As Secretary of Defense, he has made 
America less safe through his chaos and gross incompetence. 
Republican's refusal to stand up for our Constitution and the rule of 
law is disgusting. When Democrats take the majority in the 120th 
Congress, we will hold Secretary Hegseth accountable for his illegal 
actions.
```
