# CELEBRATING GEORGE AND CAROL LAW'S 70TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
      CELEBRATING GEORGE AND CAROL LAW'S 70TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate George and Carol 
Law on the occasion of their 70th wedding anniversary.
  The Laws married on September 29, 1956, in Chillicothe, Ohio. On this 
joyous occasion, we celebrate 70 years of partnership, commitment, and 
love. They celebrated this incredible milestone surrounded by their 
friends, family, and loved ones at the Encinitas Community Center.
  On this special occasion, I extend my heartfelt congratulations and 
best wishes for continued health, happiness, and cherished memories.

                          ____________________
```
