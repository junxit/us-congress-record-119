# RECOGNIZING L.E. JONES COMPANY AS THE 2026 VETERAN BUSINESS OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
RECOGNIZING L.E. JONES COMPANY AS THE 2026 VETERAN BUSINESS OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize L.E. Jones 
Company of Menominee, Michigan as the recipient of the 2026

[[Page E1181]]

Upper Peninsula Michigan Works! Veteran Business of the Year Award. 
This honor reflects the company's longstanding commitment to hiring 
Veterans and giving those who have worn the uniform of our Nation the 
chance to build meaningful careers.
  Founded in 1941, L.E. Jones is a world leader in the manufacture of 
valve seat inserts for high-performance engines and other critical 
engine components. Its products keep America moving, supporting 
transportation, agriculture, construction, maritime operations, and 
aviation. Through technical expertise and a steadfast commitment to 
quality, the company has earned a reputation for excellence while 
strengthening our Nation's industrial base and the Upper Peninsula's 
workforce.
  Just as important is the company's dedication to employing Veterans. 
L.E. Jones understands that those who have served bring discipline, 
leadership, and technical skill that cannot be taught in a classroom. 
Consider U.S. military Veteran Christopher Cadeau, who spent a decade 
in shipbuilding engineering. With help from Upper Peninsula Michigan 
Works!, Veterans' Employment Services, and dedicated workforce 
professionals, Christopher received career guidance and resume support 
that connected him with L.E. Jones. The company saw the value of his 
experience and hired him as a full-time machinist. That is how it 
should work: a Veteran's skills put to use, and an employer made 
stronger for it.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating L.E. 
Jones Company on being named the 2026 Upper Peninsula Michigan Works! 
Veteran Business of the Year. I commend its leadership for recognizing 
the value of our Veterans, and I thank the entire L.E. Jones team for 
helping those who served our Nation find meaningful work and continue 
contributing to their communities.

                          ____________________
```
