# HONORING AND CELEBRATING THE 103RD ANNIVERSARY OF FORT WORTH FIRE STATION NO. 18

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING AND CELEBRATING THE 103RD ANNIVERSARY OF FORT WORTH FIRE 
                             STATION NO. 18

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Fort 
Worth Fire Station No. 18 in celebration of its 103rd anniversary.
  Since opening in 1923, Fire Station No. 18 has been a fixture of Fort 
Worth, serving generations of residents in our community. Its presence 
on Camp Bowie Boulevard reflects a century of change throughout the 
city, as neighborhoods have grown and the needs of residents have 
evolved. Despite these changes, the station's purpose has remained 
constant: protecting lives and property when emergencies arise.
  The work carried out from Station No. 18 represents the dedication 
and readiness required of those who serve in the fire service. 
Firefighters regularly face unpredictable and dangerous situations to 
assist their fellow Texans, often responding when others are 
experiencing some of their most difficult moments. Their commitment to 
public safety is an essential part of the strength and security of our 
communities.
  Station No. 18 also stands as an example of how a community can 
preserve its history while continuing to meet modern needs. The efforts 
to retain the historic building reflect the value residents place on 
the places that connect them to their neighborhood's past. By 
maintaining its historic character while accommodating the demands of 
contemporary firefighting, the station continues to serve both a 
practical purpose and a lasting place in Fort Worth's local history.
  This anniversary provides an opportunity to recognize the 
firefighters, department personnel, and community members who have 
contributed to the station's legacy. Across generations, their work and 
support have helped sustain an institution dedicated to public service. 
Their contributions remind us that a fire station's history is measured 
not only in years, but also in the lives protected and the trust built 
with the community it serves.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I commend the 
Fort Worth Fire Department and the personnel associated with Station 
No. 18 for their commitment to protecting our community. I congratulate 
the station on this historic milestone and extend my appreciation to 
all those who have contributed to its century of service.
```
