# RECOGNIZING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS DALE WAYNE TREADWAY, U.S. ARMY (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
 RECOGNIZING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS DALE WAYNE TREADWAY, U.S. ARMY (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Sergeant First 
Class Dale Wayne Treadway, U.S. Army (Ret.) of Copperas Cove, Texas, as 
a recipient of the Congressional Veteran Commendation. His decades of 
service to our Nation and community reflect a lifelong commitment to 
serving others.
  Mr. Treadway served in the United States Army for more than 20 years, 
retiring as a Sergeant First Class. Throughout his military career, he 
served in assignments across the United States and Germany, with 
deployments to Iraq, Bosnia, and Herzegovina. He also helped 
orchestrate the Base Realignment and Closure relocation of the 
Directorate of Training and Doctrine from Fort Bliss to Fort Sill. In 
recognition of his service, he received numerous honors, including the 
Meritorious Service Medal, Army Commendation Medal, Iraq Campaign Medal 
with Two Campaign Stars, and the Air Assault Badge.
  Following his military service, Mr. Treadway continued his commitment 
to public service in Copperas Cove. He served six years on the Copperas 
Cove Planning and Zoning Commission, including as Chairman. Mr. 
Treadway also served on the Subdivision Technical Advisory Committee, 
Bell County Water Control and Improvement District No. 1 Board of 
Directors, Charter Review Committee, and Board of Zoning Adjustments. 
He further served as Chairman of the Coryell Central Appraisal District 
and President of the Central Texas District of the Texas Water 
Utilities Association. In 2024, Mr. Treadway was elected to the 
Copperas Cove City Council, Place 5, and was reelected to a full term 
in 2025.
  I am honored to recognize Sergeant First Class Dale Wayne Treadway 
(Ret.) for his distinguished military service and continued dedication 
to his community and congratulate him on receiving the Congressional 
Veteran Commendation.
```
