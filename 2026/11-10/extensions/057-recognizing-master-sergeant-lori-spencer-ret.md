# RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT LORI SPENCER (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
            RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT LORI SPENCER (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Master Sergeant Lori Spencer (Ret.) 
of Killeen has committed her life to uplifting service members, 
veterans, and their loved ones. Her enduring passion for others 
demonstrates the finest traditions of the United States Army, and it is 
my distinct honor to recognize her with a Congressional Veteran 
Commendation.
  Over her distinguished military career, Master Sergeant Spencer 
served across the U.S., Korea, and Germany, earning decorations 
including the Meritorious Service Medal (MSM), Army Commendation Medal 
(ARCOM), Army Achievement Medal (AAM), and the National Defense Service 
Medals. Honored as the Fort Hood CSM Elijah King Honorary Retiree of 
the Year, she dedicated decades of service to her country both at home 
and abroad.
  Today, Ms. Spencer actively supports veterans and military families 
across Central Texas through guidance, referrals, and advocacy. She 
serves with the Fort Hood Retiree Council, helps coordinate the annual 
Retiree Days Golf Tournament, and is a dedicated advocate for veterans 
throughout the community through several local and national Veterans 
Service Organizations.
  Master Sergeant Lori Spencer (Ret.) represents the premier of Central 
Texas through her unwavering dedication and hard work. I am forever 
grateful for Ms. Spencer's service and ask my colleagues to join me in 
extending our warmest congratulations on her exceptional achievements.

                          ____________________
```
