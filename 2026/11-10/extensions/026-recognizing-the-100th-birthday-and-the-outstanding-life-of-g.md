# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY AND THE OUTSTANDING LIFE OF GREAT COMMUNITY LEADER ELVA MAE DEROUEN-MURRAY

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY AND THE OUTSTANDING LIFE OF GREAT 
                COMMUNITY LEADER ELVA MAE DEROUEN-MURRAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mrs. 
Elva Mae Derouen-Murray as she celebrates her 100th Birthday. She was 
reared in Weeks, Louisiana and is currently residing in New Iberia, 
Louisiana. She was born May 9, 1924, the year President Calvin Coolidge 
(30th) was elected and has survived the Great Depression of 1929 to 
1939. She is one of five children born to Armand and Virginia Pennier-
Derouen, and sister to Rose, Mable, Norman, and Lorena. She has 
witnessed the election of 16 Presidents of the United States and 
experienced Black Tuesday, when she witnessed the Wall Street Crash of 
1929 to 1932. Other notables born in 1924 includes Activist and Author 
James Baldwin, President Jimmy Carter, Representative Shirley Chisholm, 
and Actress Cicely Tyson.
  A courageous woman, Mrs. Derouen-Murray lived through World War II 
(1929 to 1945); the Cold War (1946 to 1991); the Korean War (1950 to 
1953); and Vietnam War (1959 to 1975). She has lived through the Civil 
Rights Movement; the 1963 March on Washington; the death of Reverend 
Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. (1968); witnessed the televised swearing-in 
and death of President John F. Kennedy, Sr. (1968); witnessed the 
swearing-in ceremony of the 44th and first Black President of the 
United States, The Honorable Barack Obama; and survived the Covid-19 
Pandemic of 2020.
  A loyal and dedicated Democrat, Mrs. Derouen-Murray has worked behind 
the scenes in political campaigns of former Senator Mary Landrieu, 
Representative Cleo Fields, and a host of local races in St. Mary 
Parish, including Police Juror, City Council, School Board, and Mayoral 
races. As a strong believer in family values, Mrs. Derouen-Murray 
survived her beloved husband, the late Damon Murray. To that marriage 
five beautiful children were born--Mary Ann, Morrial, Ronald, Dwayne, 
and Troy.
  I take pride and pleasure in joining the many families, friends, and 
members of the Religious and Local Community at Large in congratulating 
Mrs. Elva Mae Derouen-Murray on 100 Years of Life.
  The 2nd Congressional District of Louisiana congratulates Mrs. Elva 
Mae Derouen-Murray on celebrating 100 Years of Life, a very loving 
person who cares more about others than she does for herself, who 
provided selfless dedication, devoted leadership, and faithful service 
to the St. Mary Parish Community. I wish her continued health, 
happiness, and success in the years to come.
```
