# RECOGNIZING KYLEIGH DEVER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING KYLEIGH DEVER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kyleigh Dever 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Kyleigh has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Kyleigh, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Kyleigh's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Pomona High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Kyleigh Dever on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
