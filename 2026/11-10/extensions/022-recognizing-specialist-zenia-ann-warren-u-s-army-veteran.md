# RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST ZENIA ANN WARREN, U.S. ARMY VETERAN

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST ZENIA ANN WARREN, U.S. ARMY VETERAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Specialist 
Zenia Ann Warren, U.S. Army Veteran, of Killeen, Texas, as a recipient 
of the Congressional Veteran Commendation. Her military service came 
during a significant period of change for women serving in the Army, 
and her dedication to serving her country and community has continued 
throughout her life.
  Ms. Warren served in the United States Army as a Medical Specialist. 
During her military career, she served at Fort McClellan, Fort Sam 
Houston, Fort Hood, Fort Wainwright, and Fort Greely. Among her most 
significant memories was the integration of the Women's Army Corps 
(WAC) into the U.S. Army, and she was among the first women to able to 
qualify with the M16. Her military service earned her the National 
Defense Service Medal and Expert Shooting Badge.
  Following her military service, Ms. Warren continued her commitment 
to public and community service. She has held numerous leadership 
positions with the Texas Federation of Republican Women, including 
serving as President in 2024 and 2025, and continues her involvement 
with the National Federation of Republican Women. Her civic service has 
included serving as an Election Judge, Election Clerk, Deputy Voter 
Registrar, Precinct Chair, and Vice Chair of the Bell County Republican 
Party. She has also supported scholarship opportunities for graduating 
high school seniors and initiatives benefiting veterans and active-duty 
servicemembers. Her leadership earned her the National Federation of 
Republican Women's Ronald Reagan Leadership Award.
  I commend Specialist Zenia Ann Warren for her service to our Nation 
and her continued dedication to her community and congratulate her on 
receiving the Congressional Veteran Commendation.
```
