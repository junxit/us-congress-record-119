# RECOGNIZING MR. DAVID VALDEZ

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
                      RECOGNIZING MR. DAVID VALDEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. David 
Valdez of Georgetown, Texas. Mr. Valdez has committed his life to 
uplifting service members, veterans, and his community, both in uniform 
and as a civilian. His enduring passion for others demonstrates the 
finest traditions of the United States Air Force, and it is my distinct 
honor to recognize him with a Congressional Veteran Commendation.
  Mr. Valdez served as a Sergeant in the U.S. Air Force and trained as 
a photographer, later earning a B.A. in journalism from the University 
of Maryland, College Park. Over his distinguished career, he served as 
Chief Official White House Photographer and Director of the White House 
Press Office under President George H.W. Bush. After working with 
President Bush, Valdez spent eight years with The Walt Disney Company 
and subsequently dedicated another decade of service to the U.S. 
government.
  Throughout his service and career, he earned national acclaim as an 
icon of American photography. He's used that incredible skill to give 
back to his community. Mr. Valdez initiated a project to take portraits 
of Soldiers and their families at Fort Hood. He is also the founder of 
the Texas Photography Festival and Chairman of the Georgetown Arts and 
Culture Board.
  David Valdez represents the very best of Central Texas through his 
unwavering dedication and hard work. His work will continue to share a 
direct look at some of America's most iconic moments, and I ask my 
colleagues to join me in extending our warmest congratulations to him 
on his exceptional achievement.

                          ____________________
```
