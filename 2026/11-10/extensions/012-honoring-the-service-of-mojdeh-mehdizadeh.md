# HONORING THE SERVICE OF MOJDEH MEHDIZADEH

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING THE SERVICE OF MOJDEH MEHDIZADEH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Chancellor 
Mojdeh Mehdizadeh for her retirement after 39 years of service with 
Contra Costa Community College District (4CD), including 25 years spent 
in executive leadership roles. She was selected as the 10th permanent 
chancellor of 4CD in January 2024, after being appointed Interim 
Chancellor in February 2022. Her commitment to 4CD is driven by a 
mission to transform lives through education and creating an 
environment where every student feels they belong.
  Ms. Mehdizadeh began her career as a student worker with 4CD while 
completing her undergrad requirements at Diablo Valley College. She 
came to understand and appreciate how important community college is 
for preparing students to enter the workforce and become engaged 
members of the community. She earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer 
Information Systems from San Francisco State University and a Master of 
Arts in Organizational and Intercultural Communications from California 
State University, East Bay. While working toward her degrees, she 
continued a career with 4CD, serving as an adjunct faculty member, 
College Research Coordinator, Technology Applications Manger, President 
of Contra Costa College, and Vice Chancellor of Technology Systems. 
Additionally, in her role as Executive Vice Chancellor of Education and 
Technology, she worked across the district to strengthen educational 
programs, student services, workforce and economic development, 
international education, distance education, research and planning, and 
grants. In her role as Chancellor, Mojdeh was responsible for 
assessing, planning, organizing, and evaluating 4CD's resources to meet 
students' educational needs and the needs of the community. As 
Chancellor, Mojdeh has guided the development of a District Strategic 
Plan that serves as the foundation for the colleges' Educational 
Strategic Plans, cultivated future leaders by relaunching the 4CD 
Leadership Institute and implementing the Faculty Diversity Internship 
Program, and ensured 4CD's financial stability throughout the years.
  In addition to her roles at 4CD, Mojdeh is active in state and 
federal organizations focused on advancing equity in public education. 
She was elected CEO of the Board of the Community College League of 
California and serves on the boards of East Bay Leadership Council, the 
Kennedy-King Memorial Scholarship Committee, RCF Connects, and Richmond 
Promise. She has been a true leader in advancing educational 
opportunities for all students, regardless of social or economic 
background.
  Please join me in honoring Mojdeh Mehdizadeh for her decades of 
outstanding service and congratulating her on her well-deserved 
retirement.
```
