# RECOGNIZING GOODYEAR, ARIZONA'S 80TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

```
            RECOGNIZING GOODYEAR, ARIZONA'S 80TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PAUL A. GOSAR

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. GOSAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
the City of Goodyear, Arizona, for its 80th anniversary as an 
incorporated community.
  The story and history of Goodyear is uniquely Arizonan: originally 
purchased by President Paul W. Litchfield of the Goodyear Tire & Rubber 
Company in 1917, Goodyear began its life as a small community built 
around a 16,000-acre Egyptian long-fiber cotton farm. This farm would 
not only help remedy cotton shortages during the First World War; it 
would help produce fabric meshes for Goodyear's 1918 Pneumatic Cord 
Tire--the first widely produced pneumatic tire in the world.
  During the Second World War, Goodyear played another important role 
in Arizona's wider efforts to support America's struggle against 
tyranny; Goodyear hosted the Naval Air Facility, an auxiliary airfield 
for Luke

[[Page E1186]]

Airforce Base, and an aircraft manufacturing plant that provided 
crucial parts for the B-29 Superfortress and other iconic bombers. 
These early chapters in Goodyear's history established the town's 
traditions of hard work, innovation, patriotism, and perseverance. 
After the war ended, Goodyear was finally incorporated on November 16, 
1946.
  At the time, Goodyear, Arizona only consisted of 151 homes, 250 
apartments, and a handful of local businesses catering to the nearby 
airfields and manufacturing plant. But with eight decades of sustained 
growth, Goodyear has flourished; slowly becoming one of Arizona's most 
dynamic communities with approximately 125,000 residents. Goodyear is a 
proud part of Phoenix's West Valley, providing prosperity, opportunity, 
beauty, and hope to all who call the Valley of the Sun their home. 
Since 1985, Goodyear has been one of Arizona's major cities. Since 
Goodyear was designated an official city, generations of families, 
entrepreneurs, farmers, veterans, workers, and community leaders have 
worked together to shape its continued success going into the future.
  As the proud Representative of Goodyear in Arizona's 9th 
Congressional District, I am honored to recognize this important 
milestone. The city's continued growth and prosperity are a true 
testament not only to the time, talents, and energy of those who built 
it; it is a testament to Goodyear's belief that all Arizonans must work 
together to make Arizona the greatest state in our great Nation.
  I extend my sincere congratulations to Mayor Joe Pizzillo, the 
members of Goodyear City Council, all city employees, community 
organizations, local businesses, and, most importantly, to the citizens 
of Goodyear on 80 years of achievement.
  May the next 80 years bring continued opportunity, prosperity, and 
success to this blossoming Arizona community. Happy 80th anniversary, 
Goodyear.
```
