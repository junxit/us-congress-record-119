# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF SOUTH JERSEY PORT CORPORATION

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
    HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF SOUTH JERSEY PORT CORPORATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 100th 
Anniversary of South Jersey Port Corporation. On Thursday, October 15, 
2026, colleagues, friends, and members of the community gathered at the 
Balzano Marine Terminal in Camden, New Jersey to commemorate the 100th 
anniversary of the South Jersey Port Corporation.
  Established in 1925 by the New Jersey State Legislature and 
originally designated as the South Jersey Port District and Commission, 
the entity expanded significantly throughout the 1940s and 1960s to 
support the region's growing industrial needs. Renamed the South Jersey 
Port Corporation in 1968, it has since remained a cornerstone of 
economic vitality in Southern New Jersey and the broader Delaware River 
Region, which was most recently exemplified by the opening of the 
Paulsboro Marine Terminal in 2017.
  For a century, the South Jersey Port Corporation has served as a 
catalyst for economic development across seven New Jersey counties. 
Created to support a broad range of industries, the corporation today 
sustains thousands of well-paying union jobs across sectors including 
steel, forest products, project cargo, and manufacturing. The 
commodities moving through its terminals supply essential raw materials 
and semi-finished goods that underpin heavy manufacturing and 
construction throughout the United States.
  At present, the South Jersey Port Corporation stands as a vital 
gateway linking global markets with the Mid-Atlantic and the North 
American heartland. For more than 100 years, it has been a trusted 
leader in bulk, breakbulk, and project cargo, facilitating efficient 
trade flows with partners across Africa, Asia, South America, and 
Europe while providing immediate access to more than 38 million 
American consumers. The corporation continues to advance key 
initiatives; among them enhanced rail connectivity, cleaner and more 
efficient equipment, modernized terminal infrastructure, and new 
avenues for cargo expansion, while maintaining strong union employment 
and fostering workforce development.
  Mr. Speaker, I respectfully request that you join me in recognizing 
and commending the 100th anniversary of the South Jersey Port 
Corporation, an institution that has long demonstrated its dedication 
to both our Nation and the Southern New Jersey region.
```
