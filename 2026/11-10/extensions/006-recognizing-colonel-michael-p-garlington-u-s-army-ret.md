# RECOGNIZING COLONEL MICHAEL P. GARLINGTON, U.S. ARMY (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
      RECOGNIZING COLONEL MICHAEL P. GARLINGTON, U.S. ARMY (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Colonel Michael P. Garlington, U.S. 
Army (Ret.) of Round Rock, Texas, has dedicated nearly three decades to 
serving our Nation and has continued that commitment long after leaving 
military service. From beginning his Army career as an enlisted Soldier 
to retiring as a Colonel, his life has been marked by service to his 
country, fellow veterans, and community.
  Colonel Garlington served in the United States Army for nearly 28 
years, beginning his career as an enlisted infantryman before becoming 
a cavalry officer and later a logistics officer. His service took him 
across the United States and overseas to Germany, Bosnia, Tajikistan, 
Iraq, and Afghanistan, and included assignments with the 3rd Armored 
Cavalry Regiment, Joint Staff, and Crane Army Ammunition Activity. He 
served in Operation Iraqi Freedom and other missions abroad before 
retiring as a Colonel in 2019. His military honors include the Defense 
Superior Service Medal, Legion of Merit, two Bronze Star Medals, six 
Meritorious Service Medals, and numerous other decorations.
  Following his retirement, Colonel Garlington continued serving 
veterans, military families, and his Central Texas community. As a 
founding developer and organizer of Leading2Win, he has helped mentor 
transitioning service members, recruited volunteers through IBM's 
Veteran Business Resource Group, and secured more than $20,000 in IBM 
support for the organization. He also serves as Vice President for 
Community Partner Outreach for the Association of the United States 
Army's Texas Capital Area Chapter and has supported the Ride On Center 
for Kids and Veterans through volunteer service. Beyond the veteran 
community, he has dedicated hundreds of volunteer hours to Round Rock 
High School programs and was selected as the guest speaker for Cedar 
Park's 2026 Memorial Day observance.
  I am honored to recognize Colonel Michael P. Garlington for his 
distinguished service to our Nation, fellow veterans, and community and 
congratulate him on receiving the Congressional Veteran Commendation. 
His continued dedication to serving others reflects the same commitment 
to service that defined his Army career.
```
