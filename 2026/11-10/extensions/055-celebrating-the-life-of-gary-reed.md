# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF GARY REED

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                   CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF GARY REED

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today along with my colleague, 
Representative David G. Valadao, to honor the life and legacy of Gary 
Reed, a dedicated educator and advocate for the Porterville community 
over the past five decades. His numerous contributions have helped 
expand higher education access in the Central Valley and strengthened 
the role of community colleges across California's rural communities.
  Mr. Reed stayed close to home for much of his education, receiving 
his associate's degree in business administration from Porterville 
College in 1966, and attending Fresno State to obtain his bachelor's 
degree in 1969. He began his career as an educator in Ventura County in 
the early 1970s, receiving the Ventura Outstanding Educator of the Year 
award in 1975 for his efforts. Though he briefly left his role as an 
educator to help his father run Reed & Reed Enterprises in the late 
1970s, he returned to earn his master's degree in school management 
from the University of La Vern in 1979.
  Mr. Reed's contributions to the Porterville area are not solely 
education based. He spent large portions of the 1980s committing 
himself to public service and helping his community. He selflessly 
served on the Tulare County Retirement and Pension Board, the Sierra 
View District Hospital Board, the Tulare County Board of Supervisors, 
the Cal State Bakersfield Advisory Board, the Porterville Education 
Foundation, the Porterville Chamber of Commerce, and as Director of the 
Porterville Fair, among numerous other roles.
  Mr. Reed was named President of the Tulare Kings Art Council and 
Porterville Area Business Association, and in 1993, he partnered with 
Mark Shoemaker and Jeffrey Brookshire to create Reed, Shoemaker, 
Brookshire Inc., an investment brokerage business that expanded into a 
national, multi-million-dollar company. These experiences helped him 
provide oversight for the Foundation's California Community Colleges 
Scholarship, where he grew their endowment by 76 million dollars and 
helped them reach 3,500 community college students annually.
  For more than 15 years, Mr. Reed served on the Foundation for 
California Community Colleges' Board of Director. He continued to serve 
on the Foundation's Investment Advisory Committee until his passing in 
August 2026.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in honoring Gary Reed 
for a lifetime of service to his community, his impact as an educator, 
businessman, and community champion, and his ceaseless work for the 
people of Porterville.
```
