# HONORING DR. RAVI PATEL

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                        HONORING DR. RAVI PATEL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Ravi Patel for 
40 years of dedicated service and exemplary care to the people of Kern 
County through the Comprehensive Blood & Cancer Center (CBCC).
  Dr. Patel dedicated his career to caring for others long before the 
CBCC was created. He first earned his medical degree from Gujarat 
University's Government Medical College in India, before immigrating to 
the United States alongside his wife, Dr. Naina Patel, to complete his 
residency at Illinois' Ravenswood Hospital. He subsequently pursued a 
fellowship in hematology and oncology at Loyola University's Stritch 
School of Medicine, then moved to Bakersfield, California with Naina in 
1984, establishing the CBCC later that year.
  Under his leadership, what began as a 1,200-square-foot office with a 
single employee has grown into one of the largest freestanding cancer 
centers in the Nation, treating more than a quarter million patients 
since its founding. As the directing manager, Dr. Patel personally 
cared for many of the CBCC's patients and served as the principal 
investigator for numerous clinical research studies. Throughout his 
tenure, he also partnered with pharmaceutical companies and academic 
research institutions to ensure that cancer patients in Kern County had 
access to the best treatments available. Today, the CBCC stands as a 
pillar of cancer research nationwide, collaborating with UCLA's Jonsson 
Comprehensive Cancer Center to advance breakthrough, FDA-approved 
treatments that have changed the way cancer is treated around the 
world.
  Notably, Dr. Patel was named an Associate Clinical Professor of 
Medicine at UCLA for his work with the Jonsson Center and also worked 
closely with the Residency Program in Internal Medicine at the Kern 
County Medical Center to expand the resources and opportunities 
available to young residents in the Central Valley. Furthermore, 25 
years after establishing the CBCC, Dr. Patel and Naina created the Ravi 
and Naina Patel Foundation in Kern County to give back following their 
success. Through this foundation, Dr. Patel has worked tirelessly 
promoting basic education, proper nutrition, secure housing, and a 
healthier environment for some of our most underserved communities.
  As he steps away from CBCC, Dr. Patel entrusts its future to Dignity 
Health, a long-time partner. Reflecting on their collaboration, he 
stated, ``Since the beginning of our partnership in 2012, I've felt 
deep assurance that our patients are in the best of hands with Dignity 
Health.'' Yet, as countless patients have affirmed, nothing can replace 
the personal impact Dr. Patel has had on Kern County through his 
compassion, dedication, and holistic approach to care.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Dr. 
Ravi Patel for 40 years of extraordinary research, treatment, and 
service to the people of Kern County. His legacy will endure for 
generations to come.
```
