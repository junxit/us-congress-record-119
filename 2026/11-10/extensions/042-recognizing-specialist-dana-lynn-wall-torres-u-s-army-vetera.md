# RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST DANA LYNN WALL TORRES, U.S. ARMY VETERAN

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
    RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST DANA LYNN WALL TORRES, U.S. ARMY VETERAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Specialist Dana Lynn Wall Torres, U.S. Army Veteran, of Copperas Cove, 
Texas, as a recipient of the Congressional Veteran Commendation. Her 
service to our Nation and continued dedication to the Central Texas 
community reflect her commitment to helping others.
  Ms. Wall Torres served in the United States Army for more than six 
years as a Motor Transport Operator, attaining the rank of Specialist. 
During her military career, she served in assignments across the United 
States and overseas, including service in Operation Iraqi Freedom and 
Operation Enduring Freedom. She also participated in a humanitarian 
mission in Guatemala, helping deliver equipment, medical supplies, and 
food to communities recovering from Hurricane Stan. In recognition of 
her service, she received numerous honors, including the Army 
Commendation Medal, three Army Achievement Medals, two Army Good 
Conduct Medals, the Iraq Campaign Medal, and Afghanistan Campaign 
Medal.
  Following her military service, Ms. Wall Torres continued her 
commitment to serving others in Central Texas. She helped establish and 
now leads D&S Cuddling Critters, which provides animal-assisted 
emotional support and wellness programs for veterans, active-duty 
servicemembers, first responders, seniors, and other members of the 
community. In only three years, the organization expanded to offer five 
services while overseeing 11 community programs. She also helped 
establish Market with a Mission, which distributed more than 58,000 
pounds of food to more than 5,800 households during its first four-
month cycle. Ms. Wall Torres also developed SAFE Central Texas, a 
community network focused on suicide-prevention awareness and 
connecting residents with resources.
  I am honored to recognize Specialist Dana Lynn Wall Torres for her 
service to our Nation and community and congratulate her on receiving 
the Congressional Veteran Commendation.
```
