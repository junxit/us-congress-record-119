# RECOGNIZING COMMANDER ED ROY RILEY, U.S. NAVY (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
          RECOGNIZING COMMANDER ED ROY RILEY, U.S. NAVY (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize 
Commander Ed Roy Riley, U.S. Navy (Ret.) of Florence, Texas, as a 
recipient of the Congressional Veteran Commendation. From serving as an 
enlisted aircrewman and rescue swimmer to becoming an F-14 fighter 
pilot and retiring as a Commander, his career reflects decades of 
dedicated service to our Nation.
  Commander Riley served in the United States Navy from 1970 to 1994. 
During the first phase of his career, he served as a helicopter 
crewman, operating airborne sonar and anti-submarine sensors and 
serving as a rescue swimmer, After earning his degree from the 
University of Washington, he became an officer and F-14 fighter pilot 
and later served as a test pilot. He accumulated more than 2,000 hours 
in the F-14 and completed more than 450 arrested carrier landings. 
During the Iranian Hostage Crisis, he served aboard the recovery 
carrier involved in the rescue attempt and was awarded the Navy 
Expeditionary Medal.
  Following his military career, Commander Riley remained involved in 
the defense and veteran communities. He worked as a test pilot and test 
director at Northrop Grumman and later established C-Dragon Consulting. 
After returning to Texas, he established American Legion Post 354 in 
Florence in 2022 and has served as its Commander since its inception. 
Under his leadership, the post has supported local students, veterans, 
and community events while helping maintain the Florence Veterans 
Memorial. In recognition of his continued service, Commander Riley was 
named Florence's 2025 Citizen of the Year.
  I commend Commander Ed Roy Riley for his many years of service, both 
in uniform and in his community, and congratulate him on receiving the 
Congressional Veteran Commendation.
```
