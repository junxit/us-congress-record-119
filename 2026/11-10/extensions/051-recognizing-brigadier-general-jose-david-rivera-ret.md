# RECOGNIZING BRIGADIER GENERAL JOSE DAVID RIVERA (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
         RECOGNIZING BRIGADIER GENERAL JOSE DAVID RIVERA (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Brigadier General Jose David Rivera 
(Ret.) of Salado, Texas has committed his life to uplifting service 
members, veterans, and their loved ones, both in uniform and as a 
civilian. His enduring passion for others demonstrates the finest 
traditions of the United States Army, and it is my distinct honor to 
recognize him with a Congressional Veteran Commendation.
  A graduate of the United States Military Academy at West Point, 
General Rivera also holds a Master of Strategic Studies from the U.S. 
Army War College. His combat and operational deployments span Iraq, 
Afghanistan, Israel, and Kosovo. During his service as brigade 
commander, he led three concurrent Texas missions; border security, 
COVID-19 vaccinations, and civil disturbance support. His restructuring 
of the brigade into three task forces ensured all missions succeeded. 
His service culminated as Director of Operations, Plans, Training and 
Exercises at U.S. Army Central, responsible for coordinating the 
operations of 12,000 Soldiers across 147 units in the Middle East and 
Central Asia.
  Throughout his 30 years of service, he earned the Army Distinguished 
Service Medal, two Bronze Stars, Defense Superior Service Medal, and 
the Combat Infantryman Badge. Today, he translates this expertise into 
civilian impact as a principal coach with Arrowhead Leadership 
Consultants, while actively supporting local veteran organizations and 
neurodiversity.
  Brigadier General Jose David Rivera represents the best of Central 
Texas through his unwavering dedication and hard work. I am forever 
grateful for General Rivera's service and wish him continued success in 
all future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
