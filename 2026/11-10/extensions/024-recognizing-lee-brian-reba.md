# RECOGNIZING LEE BRIAN REBA

**Speaking:** Norton, Eleanor Holmes (N000147) [D-DC]

```
                       RECOGNIZING LEE BRIAN REBA

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. ELEANOR HOLMES NORTON

                      of the district of columbia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Ms. NORTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Lee Brian Reba, a dear 
friend and a third-generation Washingtonian whose more than four 
decades of professional achievement, public service and civic 
leadership have made a lasting and meaningful contribution to the 
District of Columbia and our Nation.
  Mr. Reba is a respected leader in the events and protocol profession, 
with a record of creating, producing and managing innovative, large-
scale and high-visibility events. His professional accomplishments 
reflect not only extraordinary organizational and managerial abilities, 
but also the diplomacy, sound judgment, discretion, creativity and 
meticulous attention to detail essential to representing institutions 
and public officials.
  Mr. Reba has worked in federal and local government, higher 
education, civic affairs and community engagement. He currently serves 
as Senior Advisor for University Events and Protocol in the Office of 
the President at the University of the District of Columbia (UDC). 
Through his leadership and expertise, he has helped ensure that UDC is 
represented with distinction, professionalism and an unwavering 
commitment to the highest standards of protocol.
  Prior to his distinguished career in higher education, Mr. Reba 
served in the D.C. and federal governments. He served as Deputy Chief 
of Staff to the Chairman of the D.C. Council and held a position in the 
Office of the Secretary within the Executive Office of the D.C. Mayor. 
He was appointed to a Schedule C position at the U.S. Department of 
Health and Human Services, where he served under Secretary Donna E. 
Shalala during the Clinton administration.
  Mr. Reba's commitment to his local community is equally noteworthy. 
Elected to serve as Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner for Single 
Member District 3C01 in Woodley Park, he dedicated 15 years to 
representing residents, addressing community concerns and advancing the 
quality of life in his neighborhood and throughout D.C.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Lee Brian 
Reba and in expressing our appreciation for his extraordinary record of 
service and lasting contributions to the people and institutions of the 
District of Columbia and the United States.
```
