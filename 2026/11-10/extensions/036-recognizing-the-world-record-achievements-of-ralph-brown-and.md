# RECOGNIZING THE WORLD RECORD ACHIEVEMENTS OF RALPH BROWN AND ROBERT BROWN

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE WORLD RECORD ACHIEVEMENTS OF RALPH BROWN AND ROBERT 
                                 BROWN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
achievements and the ambition of two of my constituents, Ralph Brown 
and his brother, Robert Brown.
  Ralph is a proud United States Marine Corps Veteran whose commitment 
to the armed services did not end when he transitioned back into 
civilian life. Outside of running his local business, Ralph has devoted 
much of his time to honor our Nation's wounded warriors through the 
passion for maritime adventure he shares with his brother. In 2009, the 
brothers completed a nearly-three-month long journey across the 
Atlantic Ocean in a 21-foot flats boat. In 2011, that journey was 
recognized as the world record for using the smallest powerboat ever to 
complete the crossing. On that voyage, they proudly hoisted the 
American flag as they traversed over 7000 miles of open ocean between 
Tampa, Florida and their destination of Hamburg, Germany. Before their 
departure, they dedicated that journey to the memory of the American 
heroes who made the ultimate sacrifice during Operation Eagle Claw and 
raised funds for several Wounded Warrior Organizations along the way.
  I join Ralph and Robert in remembering those soldiers, and in 
remembering all our Nation's fallen heroes. We must never forget those 
servicemembers who have given their lives to defend others, protect the 
homeland, and uphold the American way of life. Ralph and Robert's 
journey reminds us all that we have a solemn responsibility to honor 
the sacrifices of our Veterans, to tell their stories, and to support 
those who return home wounded.
  Ralph and Robert represent the best of bold American entrepreneurship 
and unwavering patriotism. I thank Ralph for his service in the Marine 
Corps, and I wish Ralph and Robert the best in their next ambitious 
project to honor our heroes and promote American maritime innovation 
right at home in my District in Hudson, Florida.
```
