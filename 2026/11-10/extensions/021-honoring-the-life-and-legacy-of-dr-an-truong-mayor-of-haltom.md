# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. AN TRUONG, MAYOR OF HALTOM CITY, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. AN TRUONG, MAYOR OF HALTOM CITY, 
                                 TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Dr. An Truong, Mayor of Haltom City, Texas, who passed away 
on October 16, 2026, at the age of 78.
  Dr. Truong's journey from Vietnam to North Texas was one of 
perseverance, dedication, and a deep commitment to serving others. 
After immigrating to the United States at age 26, he built a life 
centered on public service, beginning with the Fort Worth Police 
Department before spending 37 years as an investigator with the Tarrant 
County District Attorney's Office. Throughout his career, he devoted 
himself to helping others and protecting the community he called home.
  His dedication to service extended beyond his professional career and 
into local leadership. After serving on the Haltom City Council, Dr. 
Truong was elected mayor in 2019. As the first Vietnamese American 
mayor in Tarrant County, he made history while representing the growing 
diversity and shared aspirations of the North Texas community. His 
journey serves as a reminder of the meaningful contributions 
individuals can make when they dedicate themselves to the places and 
people around them.
  Dr. Truong's legacy is defined not only by the positions he held, but 
also by the years he gave to his community. From his work in law 
enforcement to his leadership in Haltom City, he demonstrated a lasting 
commitment to public service. His life reflects the importance of those 
who step forward to serve, leaders who take responsibility for their 
communities, and individuals who dedicate their time and talents to 
making a difference in the lives of others.
  As we remember Dr. Truong, we recognize a life devoted to service and 
the community he worked so hard to support. His passing is a profound 
loss for his family, friends, colleagues, and the residents of Haltom 
City. We extend our heartfelt condolences to all who knew him and are 
grateful for the lasting contributions he made to North Texas.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I honor Dr. An 
Truong's life and extend my deepest sympathies to his loved ones and 
the Haltom City community. May his years of service be remembered with 
gratitude, and may his legacy continue to inspire others to serve their 
communities with the same dedication and care.
```
