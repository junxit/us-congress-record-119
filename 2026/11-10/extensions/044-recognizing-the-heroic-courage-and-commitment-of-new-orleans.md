# RECOGNIZING THE HEROIC COURAGE AND COMMITMENT OF NEW ORLEANS POLICE OFFICER JOSEPH RODRIGUE

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE HEROIC COURAGE AND COMMITMENT OF NEW ORLEANS POLICE 
                        OFFICER JOSEPH RODRIGUE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to humbly honor 
and celebrate the heroic courage and commitment of New Orleans Police 
Officer Joseph Rodrigue. Officer Rodrigue has served the citizens of 
New Orleans, Louisiana for 10 years. He currently serves as a Senior 
Police Officer in the 3rd District Unit.
  On Wednesday, January 1, 2025, a man drove a pickup into crowds 
celebrating the New Year on Bourbon Street early killing 14 people. 
President Biden said he was ``inspired by ISIS.'' This unfortunate 
event occurred near the intersection of Canal and Bourbon Streets in 
the city's historic French Quarter, the heart of the Louisiana tourism 
industry and a driver of the state's economy. This attack was clearly 
intended to harm many more people. This terrorist used a 6,000-pound 
truck as a weapon and then opened fire. He had planted explosives, 
which he intended to detonate.
  He sped his rented truck around a police car that was blocking an 
entrance to Bourbon Street at about 3:15 a.m. Wednesday morning and 
struck several people along the street before crashing the truck into 
an obstacle setup in the street. He then opened fire and died in a 
shootout with police. Two of those injured in the attack were police 
officers. Investigators confirmed they found what appeared to be 
improvised explosive devices in the truck.
  ``This man was trying to run over as many people as he possibly 
could,'' the New Orleans police superintendent, Anne Kirkpatrick, said 
in a news conference Wednesday. ``He was hellbent on creating the 
carnage and the damage that he did.''
  The actions of Officer Joseph Rodrigue and his fellow officers on New 
Year's Day highlight the profound impact of the NOPD's dedication to 
public safety. His teamwork, professionalism, and courage under fire 
are a reminder of the daily sacrifices made by law enforcement officers 
to protect the lives of others.
  The officers didn't have a chance to make actual tactical decisions 
amongst themselves. They acted as a team. Some of these men and women 
didn't work together ever before, many from different jurisdictions and 
departments. But the individual officers who were closer to the scene, 
who acted on a threat and eliminated the threat, did everything that 
you would expect them to do and more, especially under the 
circumstances. At least 57 others were injured, including Officer 
Rodrigue, receiving a gunshot wound to his left leg, requiring multiple 
surgeries. Another police officer was wounded and is also recovering 
from his injuries.
  Throughout numerous interviews, each of them expressed the same 
sentiment--they were simply doing what they believed was expected of 
them when they joined the New Orleans Police Department. Officer Joseph 
Rodrigue's bravery is a testament to his character and his commitment 
to serving and protecting this city and its visitors.
  The only reason there wasn't a greater loss of life was because of 
the quick work of the New Orleans Police Department. We have all heard 
stories of police officers and responders in other cities freezing in 
the face of a crisis--not Officer Rodrigue. He ran to danger and 
quickly neutralized it before the attacker could cause more harm. For 
this reason, I recognize Officer Joseph Rodrigue, a true hero saving 
countless lives. I humbly thank him for his courageous efforts and 
unwavering service as a member of the New Orleans Police Department.
```
