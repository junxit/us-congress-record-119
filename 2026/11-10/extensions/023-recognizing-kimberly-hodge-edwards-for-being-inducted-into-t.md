# RECOGNIZING KIMBERLY HODGE EDWARDS FOR BEING INDUCTED INTO THE 2026 WARRENSVILLE HEIGHTS HIGH SCHOOL ALUMNI HALL OF FAME

**Speaking:** Brown, Shontel M. (B001313) [D-OH]

```
  RECOGNIZING KIMBERLY HODGE EDWARDS FOR BEING INDUCTED INTO THE 2026 
          WARRENSVILLE HEIGHTS HIGH SCHOOL ALUMNI HALL OF FAME

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SHONTEL M. BROWN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kimberly Hodge 
Edwards on the occasion of her induction into the 2026 Warrensville 
Heights High School Alumni Hall of Fame. This distinction honors a 
lifetime resident of Warrensville Heights whose career has been defined 
by dedicated service to children, families, individuals with 
disabilities, and the broader community.
  Kimberly earned her bachelor's degree from Urbana University and her 
Master of Science in Social Administration from Case Western Reserve 
University. Prior to joining my office, she devoted more than 25 years 
to social services, supporting children and families affected by abuse 
and neglect, working to reunify families, and assisting children in 
finding permanent homes through successful adoptions. Her career 
reflects a sustained commitment to protecting vulnerable individuals 
and strengthening families. For 18 years, Kimberly also worked to help 
individuals with disabilities obtain meaningful employment and greater 
independence, while ensuring their health and safety. Through this 
work, she demonstrated the importance of providing individuals with the 
resources, advocacy, and opportunities necessary to live with dignity 
and participate fully in their communities.
  Kimberly further distinguished herself through 22 years of service as 
a member of Warrensville Heights City Council. Her extensive tenure in 
local government reflects her longstanding commitment to the city in 
which she was raised and continues to call home. It was during this 
period of public service that Kimberly and I served together on City 
Council, beginning a professional relationship

[[Page E1184]]

grounded in a shared commitment to serving the residents of 
Warrensville Heights.
  Today, as Senior Advisor, Kimberly continues her public service 
through her work my office, where she also served as Deputy Chief of 
Staff and District Director and continues to provide valuable 
leadership and institutional knowledge in service to the people of 
Ohio's 11th Congressional District.
  Her induction into the 2026 Warrensville Heights High School Alumni 
Hall of Fame is a fitting recognition of a career and life dedicated to 
public service. Kimberly's record of professional achievement, 
community involvement, and commitment to those in need reflects the 
values of leadership, service, and responsibility that this honor 
represents. I congratulate Kimberly Hodge Edwards on this well-deserved 
recognition and commend her for more than two decades of dedicated 
service to the Warrensville Heights community and the people she has 
served throughout her career.
```
