# HONORING BOROUGH OF STRATFORD HOMETOWN HERO DR. EMILY J. BALOG

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
     HONORING BOROUGH OF STRATFORD HOMETOWN HERO DR. EMILY J. BALOG

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and commend Dr. 
Emily J. Balog. On Wednesday, October 21, 2026, family, friends, and 
members of the community gathered at Sterling High School to honor and 
recognize Dr. Emily J. Balog for her dedicated service to both her 
community and the United States of America.
  Born in Mount Laurel and raised in Laurel Springs, New Jersey, Dr. 
Emily Balog attended Sterling High School, where she graduated early in 
2002. Immediately following her graduation, she matriculated to Camden 
County College, earning 55 credits before transferring to Thomas 
Jefferson University. While at Thomas Jefferson University, Dr.

[[Page E1183]]

Balog earned a bachelor's degree in 2006 and a master's degree in 
occupational therapy in 2007. During her service in the Air National 
Guard, she also attended the Community College of the Air Force, where 
she earned an Associate of Applied Science in Disaster Preparedness. In 
2018, she was selected as a recipient of the distinguished Pat Tillman 
Scholarship, and in 2021, Dr. Balog completed her Ph.D. in 
Translational Health Science.
  Prior to her more than 20-year career in Occupational Therapy, Dr. 
Balog served at McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst Air Force Base in Burlington 
County. She enlisted in the Air National Guard in 2002, rising to the 
rank of Staff Sergeant before being honorably discharged in 2008. That 
same year, she was deployed to Iraq in support of Operation Enduring 
Freedom. As a member of the Emergency Disaster Preparedness Unit--most 
notably recognized under the designation C.B.R.N., standing for 
Chemical, Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear--she provided lectures, 
led training exercises, and developed simulated emergency response 
scenarios during peacetime. While deployed, she oversaw Plans and 
Operations, working to identify disaster points, create decontamination 
plans, and coordinate mission-critical activities for her fellow 
servicemembers.
  Dr. Balog graduated at the top of her class in both basic training 
and the Army's Chemical School. Her distinguished service earned her 
multiple commendations, including the Global War on Terrorism 
Expeditionary Medal, Global War on Terrorism Service Medal, and 
National Defense Service Medal, among others. She is the daughter of a 
Vietnam veteran and the granddaughter of a World War II Air Force 
pilot.
  Today, Dr. Balog has resided in the Borough of Stratford for 18 
years. She is married to her husband, Matt, also a Sterling High School 
alumnus and a veteran of the United States Army. Together, they are 
raising two children: Reed. a 9th-grade ROTC student at Sterling High 
School, and Ivy, a 7th grader who aspires to one day serve in law 
enforcement. Dr. Balog has provided comprehensive care to geriatric 
patients throughout her two decades as an Occupational Therapist and 
currently serves as a research scientist at Rowan-Virtua School of 
Osteopathic Medicine. She remains deeply involved in her community 
through volunteer work in New Jersey and Philadelphia with 
organizations such as Heroic Gardens, Action Tank, and the Sterling 
High School Board of Education. She was also a lifetime member of the 
Stratford VFW prior to its closure following the COVID-19 pandemic.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask you to join me in honoring and commending Dr. 
Emily J. Balog, a citizen who has dedicated herself wholeheartedly to 
her country and her community, and who has served both with exceptional 
commitment and distinction.
```
