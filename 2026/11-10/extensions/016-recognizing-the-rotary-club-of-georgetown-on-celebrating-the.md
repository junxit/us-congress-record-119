# RECOGNIZING THE ROTARY CLUB OF GEORGETOWN ON CELEBRATING THE 10TH ANNUAL FIELD OF HONOR

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE ROTARY CLUB OF GEORGETOWN ON CELEBRATING THE 10TH 
                         ANNUAL FIELD OF HONOR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Rotary Club of Georgetown as it celebrates the10th Annual Field of 
Honor, an event that honors veterans, active-duty and reserve military 
members, and first responders throughout Central Texas.
  Chartered on February 28, 1962, the Rotary Club of Georgetown has 
served the community for more than six decades through service 
projects, scholarships, and support for local organizations. In 
recognition of the many veterans and first responders in Central Texas, 
the Club established its first Field of Honor at San Gabriel Park in 
2017. Jeanne Cox was instrumental in bringing the Field of Honor to the 
Rotary Club and helping establish what has become an annual Georgetown 
tradition.
  The first Field of Honor featured 1,200 full-size American flags 
honoring veterans, service members, first responders, and other local 
heroes. Since then, the event has grown into a cherished community 
event, drawing thousands of visitors and supporting organizations that 
serve veterans and first responders. To date, more than $270,000 has 
been awarded to over 50 organizations.
  This year marks the 10th Anniversary of the Field of Honor and 
America's 250th birthday. A display of 2,026 American flags will honor 
those who have served while reflecting the patriotism and gratitude 
that have defined this Georgetown tradition for the past decade.
  I am honored to congratulate Jeanne Cox and the Rotary Club of 
Georgetown on the 10th Anniversary of the Field of Honor. I commend the 
volunteers and community members whose dedication has made this event 
possible and thank them for continuing to honor those who serve and 
protect our Nation.
```
