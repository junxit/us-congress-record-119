# CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BOYS & GIRLS CLUB OF GREATER TARRANT COUNTY

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
 CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BOYS & GIRLS CLUB OF GREATER 
                             TARRANT COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Boys 
& Girls Clubs of Greater Tarrant County as it celebrates its 100th 
anniversary in 2026.
  Founded in Fort Worth in 1926, the organization has spent a century 
supporting young people and families throughout North Texas. Over the 
decades, the organization has continued to serve its community while 
responding to the evolving needs of local youth.
  Through educational programs, recreational activities, and 
opportunities to develop leadership and practical skills, Boys & Girls 
Clubs of Greater Tarrant County helps to prepare young people for 
future success. Its work highlights the value of investing in children 
and teens by connecting them with resources, guidance, and 
opportunities to discover their strengths and pursue their goals.
  Under the leadership of President and CEO Daphne Barlow, the 
organization continues its work across North Texas, reaching young 
people through its club locations, school-based programs, and mobile 
services. Its commitment to youth development reflects the 
contributions of the staff members, volunteers, families, and community 
partners who help make these programs possible.
  This centennial also serves as an opportunity to recognize the 
generations of leaders, mentors, staff members, volunteers, and 
community supporters whose dedication has shaped the organization's 
history. Many incredible people have helped sustain its mission through 
a century of growth and change, leaving a lasting mark on the young 
people and families they have served. Their legacy lives on in the 
opportunities created for future generations and in the continued 
commitment to strengthening communities throughout North Texas.
  The organization's centennial celebration provides an opportunity to 
honor a century of service and recognize the generations of young 
people whose lives have been shaped by their experiences with the club. 
Through initiatives such as the Century Circle, the organization is 
also encouraging continued community support to sustain its mission in 
the years ahead.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
the Boys & Girls Clubs of Greater Tarrant County on this remarkable 
100-year milestone. I commend the organization for its longstanding 
commitment to helping young people grow, learn, and prepare for the 
future, and I wish it continued success throughout its next century of 
service.
```
