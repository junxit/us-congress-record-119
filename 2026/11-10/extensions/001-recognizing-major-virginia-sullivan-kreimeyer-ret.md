# RECOGNIZING MAJOR VIRGINIA SULLIVAN KREIMEYER (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
          RECOGNIZING MAJOR VIRGINIA SULLIVAN KREIMEYER (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, Major Virginia Sullivan Kreimeyer 
(Ret.) of Cedar Park, Texas has dedicated her life to serving our 
Nation and community, both in uniform and as a civilian. Her lifelong 
commitment to others embodies the highest ideals of the United States 
Air Force, and it is my distinct honor to recognize her with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation.
  Enlisting in the U.S. Air Force, Major Kreimeyer had a remarkable 
military career, establishing the first multi-force newspaper and 
assisting in the recovery of Commerce Secretary Brown's 1996 plane 
crash in Croatia. Following her honorable service in the Air Force, she 
seamlessly translated her military expertise into a lifetime of 
civilian impact across Central Texas.
  Today, Ms. Kreimeyer's commitment to faith, family, freedom and 
community is evident in all she does. She leads efforts to support 
military families, including Treats for Troops and Spouses Appreciation 
Lunches for those stationed in Camp Mabry. In addition, after founding 
the Leander Area Republican Women's club, she leads the organization in 
honoring the fallen and recognizing veterans through Wreaths Across 
America, Memorial Day observances, and Veterans Day ceremonies. She 
also implemented LARW's Constitution Scholarship Program for high 
school seniors, helping to inspire the next generation.
  Major Virginia Sullivan Kreimeyer (Ret.) represents the finest of 
Central Texas through her unwavering dedication, leadership, and public 
service. I am deeply grateful for her service to our country and the 
younger generation and ask my colleagues to join me in honoring her 
exceptional lifetime achievements.

                          ____________________
```
