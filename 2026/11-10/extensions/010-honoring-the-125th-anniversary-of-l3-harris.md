# HONORING THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF L3 HARRIS

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
              HONORING THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF L3 HARRIS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 125th 
anniversary of L3 Harris. On Thursday, October 1, 2026, colleagues, 
partners, and community leaders gathered to honor the unwavering 
commitment of L3Harris Technologies to the City of Camden, New Jersey 
and commemorate 125 years of distinguished innovation in service to 
national security, aerospace, defense communications, and global 
technology. This historic occasion celebrates not only a remarkable 
corporate legacy, but also a profound and enduring relationship with 
the city whose people and talent have shaped and continue to shape the 
company's contributions to our Nation and the world.
  Founded in 1895 in Niles, Ohio, L3Harris traces its heritage through 
generations of American ingenuity and technological advancement. In 
Camden, that legacy dates to 1901 where it began with the Victor 
Talking Machine Company, founded by Eldridge Reeves Johnson. The 
company transformed how the world experienced music and culture. The 
iconic image of Nipper the dog listening to ``His Master's Voice,'' 
conceived and brought to life in the City of Camden, stands as a 
symbol, of creativity, craftsmanship, and global influence born right 
here in this great ``City Invincible.''
  In 1929, Victor was acquired by RCA, ushering in a new era of 
innovation in communication technology. Camden became a center of 
excellence during the Space Age when engineers and workers developed 
communications technology for NASA's Apollo missions, including systems 
used from Apollo 7 through Apollo 17. Communications equipment built in 
Camden in part enabled Neil Armstrong and the Apollo 11 crew to speak 
to the world from the lunar surface, a testament to the skill and 
dedication of the company's workforce and global enduring impact. 
Through the successive chapters of corporate leadership, the Camden 
facility's legacy remained a beacon of progress, from the stewardship 
of General Electric to Lockheed Martin, before becoming L3Harris in 
2019.

[[Page E1180]]

  Today, the L3 Harris Camden workforce continues this tradition by 
developing and testing advanced defense communications including anti-
jam technology, resilient command-and-control systems, and encryption. 
Their work honors more than a century of commitment to excellence and 
dedication to the American people, supporting our service members and 
contributing to the vitality of the City of Camden.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the House join me in recognizing and honoring 
L3Harris Technologies on its 125th anniversary in the City of Camden, 
New Jersey. For more than a century, L3Harris has exemplified 
innovation, strengthened national security, and demonstrated 
extraordinary service to communities at home and abroad. We commend not 
only their enduring legacy but also its profound relationship with the 
City of Camden and lasting impact for generations to come.
```
