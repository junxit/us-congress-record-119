# CELEBRATING THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FORT WORTH PUBLIC LIBRARY

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
   CELEBRATING THE 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FORT WORTH PUBLIC LIBRARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
celebrate the 125th Anniversary of the Fort Worth Public Library.
  The Fort Worth Public Library opened its doors in 1901, with nearly 
7,000 books serving a city of approximately 27,000 residents. What 
began as an effort led by local women committed to expanding access to 
knowledge has grown into a citywide library system serving a population 
of more than one million. This remarkable history reflects Fort Worth's 
growth and the enduring value residents place on education, literacy, 
and public resources.
  For generations, the Fort Worth Public Library has provided residents 
with far more than just books. Its branches offer opportunities for 
learning, access to technology, educational programming, and spaces 
where people of all ages can connect with their community. By helping 
residents explore new interests, develop skills, and access 
information, the library continues to play an important role in 
strengthening families and expanding educational opportunities 
throughout Fort Worth.
  Throughout its 125-year history, the Fort Worth Public Library has 
helped make knowledge and learning accessible to residents from all 
walks of life. From introducing children to the joy of reading to 
providing adults with resources for personal and professional growth, 
the library serves as a place where curiosity is encouraged and new 
opportunities begin. Its enduring presence reflects the importance of 
investing in education and ensuring that residents have the tools to 
learn, grow, and contribute to their communities.
  This anniversary also provides an opportunity to recognize the 
librarians, staff members, volunteers, civic leaders, and community 
members whose dedication has sustained the library throughout its 
history. Their efforts have helped preserve its legacy while allowing 
it to evolve alongside the community it serves.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
the Fort Worth Public Library on 125 years of service. I commend its 
continued commitment to education, literacy, and community enrichment, 
and I look forward to seeing its positive impact continue for 
generations to come.
```
