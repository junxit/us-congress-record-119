# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF WILLIAM E. "BILL" GAGEN, JR.

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
    HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF WILLIAM E. ``BILL'' GAGEN, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life and 
service of William (Bill) Gagen, Jr. for his outstanding advocacy and 
decades of exemplary work as a trial lawyer in Contra Costa County.
  Throughout his life, Bill used his skills to advocate for others and 
serve his community. He developed an interest in public speaking at a 
young age, joining Bellarmine College Preparatory's speech and debate 
team, where he specialized in impromptu speeches and earned a national 
title in radio broadcasting. While attending Georgetown University, 
Bill completed three years in the ROTC program and served on student 
council. He also participated in the historic Selma Marches, walking 54 
miles in total and observing Martin Luther King, Jr.'s speech in 
Montgomery. After graduation, Bill spent summer in Niger with Operation 
Crossroads Africa, where he and fellow volunteers helped build a 
community center. He then began attending Boalt Hall law school at UC 
Berkeley where he met his future wife, Marianne McDaniel. Bill passed 
the bar exam then went on active duty with the U.S. Army. During his 
time in the Army, he taught public speaking skills to military 
officers.
  Bill started his legal career as a Deputy District Attorney in Contra 
Costa County and prosecuted ten felony cases in his first 18 months. He 
went on to join Edward Merrill in Danville, which later became the 
Gagen Mccoy law firm. He became known as ``one of the best-known 
criminal defense lawyers in Northern California'' for his exceptional 
work. He took on very notable cases, but nearly a third of his clients 
were adolescents. Bill fought for young people who needed a second 
chance and believed that everyone deserved a fair trial. His 
professionalism and strong sense of justice led him to be named a Top 
25 attorney by the San Francisco Chronicle, and a Top 100 Lawyer by 
California Super Lawyer. He was also elected to the American College of 
Trial Lawyers, served as president of the Contra Costa Bar Association, 
and taught as an adjunct professor at John F. Kennedy University 
College of Law. Further, he co-authored the California Criminal 
Discovery and contributed to the Continuing Education of the Bar 
practice guides.
  Beyond his many professional achievements, Bill was passionate about 
giving back to his community. He served on multiple boards, including 
the Board of Trustees for De La Salle High School, the Danville Fire 
Protection District, the Eugene O'Neill Foundation, Diablo Bank, and 
the Boalt Hall Alumni Association.
  Bill was devoted to his family and was known for his warmth, quick 
wit, and sense of humor. In his free time, he enjoyed spending time 
with his wife, three sons, and four grandchildren. He loved traveling 
with his wife, Marianne--the couple traveled to Botswana, the 
Galapagos, Indonesia, and more. However, one of Bill's favorite places 
was his cabin in Bear Valley, California, where he enjoyed taking part 
in the annual Bear Valley Music Festival, of which he was a generous 
supporter.
  Sadly, Bill passed away on September 17, 2026 at the age of 83. He 
will be remembered for his passionate advocacy, candor, and 
compassionate nature. Please join me in honoring William E. Gagen, Jr. 
for his remarkable impact in our community.
```
