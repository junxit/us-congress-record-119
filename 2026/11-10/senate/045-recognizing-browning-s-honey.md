# RECOGNIZING BROWNING'S HONEY

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                      RECOGNIZING BROWNING'S HONEY

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, as a member and former chairman of 
the Senate Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each month 
I recognize and celebrate the American entrepreneurial spirit by 
highlighting the success of a small business in my home State of Idaho. 
Today, I am proud to honor Browning's Honey in Idaho Falls as the Idaho 
Small Business of the Month for October 2026.
  Established in 1921, Browning's Honey is a fifth-generation family 
business settled deep in the Idaho Falls community. For more than a 
century, the Browning family has remained committed to beekeeping, 
honey production, and agricultural pollination. Through their quality 
and innovation, the family has raised healthy bees, run productive 
farms, and built a beloved local business.
  Today, Merle, Tom, James, and Zac Browning own the business. Their 
hard work has earned Browning's Honey a positive reputation beyond 
Idaho. As the business has grown, the Brownings have maintained their 
values of caring for bees, responsible stewardship, and pride in their 
high-grade honey. What began as an apiary expanded into a thriving 
business, with a gift shop offering honey, natural skincare products, 
artisan foods, and more. Known as the ``Best Little Boutique in Idaho 
Falls,'' Browning's Honey has become a staple of eastern Idaho and a 
reflection of the family businesses that built our State.
  Beyond honey, the Brownings invest in research to improve honey bee 
health and strengthen the long-term sustainability of beekeeping and 
agriculture. They also cofounded the Bee & Butterfly Habitat Fund and 
Project Apis to establish and support habitats for essential 
pollinators like honey bees and butterflies. Through educational visits 
and community events at their Idaho locations, the Browning family 
continues to teach Idahoans about the vital role honey bees play in 
agriculture and how we can help them thrive.
  Congratulations to the Browning's Honey team on being recognized as 
the Idaho Small Business of the Month for October 2026. Your dedication 
to excellence makes Idaho proud, and I look forward to your continued 
growth and success.●
```
