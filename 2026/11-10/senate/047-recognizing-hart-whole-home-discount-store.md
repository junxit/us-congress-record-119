# RECOGNIZING HART WHOLE HOME DISCOUNT STORE

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
               RECOGNIZING HART WHOLE HOME DISCOUNT STORE

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, Idaho's veterans made great 
sacrifices for our freedoms, and we owe them our immense gratitude. 
Many who returned from service continue to be mission-driven, highly 
skilled, dedicated to success, and have opened small businesses vital 
to the Gem State. This month, I am proud to recognize the veteran-owned 
small businesses that make Idaho's communities and our economy thrive. 
As a member and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small 
Business and Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor Hart Whole Home in 
Twin Falls as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of the Month for November 
2026.
  Jon Leninger joined the U.S. Army in 2008 and graduated from basic 
training on the honor list. During his nearly 9 years of service, Jon 
rose to the rank of sergeant and earned numerous medals. While deployed 
to Afghanistan, he also helped train soldiers in weapons systems, 
communications, and hand-to-hand combat. After 8 years and 10 months of 
service, Jon medically retired as a disabled veteran.
  Following his time in the military, Jon moved to the Magic Valley and 
worked as a cement truck driver. When his wife approached him with the 
idea of opening a family-run antique store, Jon jumped at the 
opportunity to spending more time with his family. In 2021, the 
Leningers opened Acquired Treasures in Kimberly.
  After 3 years, Jon expanded beyond antiques and collectibles to 
include furniture and household goods and moved to a larger storefront 
in Twin Falls. With this, Acquired Treasures rebranded to become the 
Hart Whole Home Discount Store, named for their youngest daughter 
Torryn Hart.
  For the Leningers, Hart Whole Home is about more than helping 
customers furnish their homes; it is an opportunity to give back to the 
Magic Valley community. Hart Whole Home regularly supports local causes 
and nonprofits, including the Sportsman for Fish and Wildlife, the 
Brotherhood of the 5th annual spaghetti dinner, Empty Bowl fundraisers, 
the Amazing Grace Cowpie event, as well as benefits for local schools.
  Thank you, Jon Leiniger and the Leiniger family, for your service to 
our country, and congratulations to Hart Whole Home Discount Store for 
being selected as an Idaho Small Business of the Month for November 
2026. You are an outstanding example of the immeasurable impact 
veterans have in communities across Idaho and the U.S. You make the Gem 
State proud, and I look forward to your continued success.●
```
