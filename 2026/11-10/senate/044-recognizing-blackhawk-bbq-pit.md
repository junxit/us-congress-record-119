# RECOGNIZING BLACKHAWK BBQ PIT

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                     RECOGNIZING BLACKHAWK BBQ PIT

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, Idaho's veterans made great 
sacrifices for our freedoms, and we owe them our immense gratitude. 
Many who returned from service continue to be mission-driven, highly 
skilled, dedicated to success, and have opened small businesses vital 
to the Gem State. This month, I am proud to recognize the veteran-owned 
small businesses that make Idaho's communities and our economy thrive. 
As a member and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small 
Business and Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor Blackhawk BBQ Pit 
in Blackfoot as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of the Month for 
November 2026.
  A Blackfoot native, Nic Transtrum enlisted in the U.S. Army January 
2001, becoming a UH-60 Blackhawk mechanic and crew chief. Following the 
terrorist attacks on September 11, 2001, Nic Transtrum's commitment to 
defend our great Nation and serve grew even stronger as he was accepted 
to flight school, became a warrant officer, and flew 79 combat missions 
during his deployment to Iraq.
  As a pilot, Nic carried a strong sense of responsibility for his 
aircraft, his crew, his mission, the soldiers counting on him, and for 
the freedoms of every American citizen. That duty left a lasting impact 
on Nic. After a hard landing near the end of his deployment, Nic 
suffered from serious spinal injuries that permanently grounded him, 
and he was medically retired from the Army in 2008.
  In 2019, Nic bought a barbeque trailer and launched Blackhawk BBQ Pit 
to introduce Southern mobile barbecue culture to eastern Idaho. The 
trailer, originally purchased to work a few summer events, quickly 
became a multi-faceted operation, including catering, a brick-and-
mortar restaurant, and expanding into Utah. As Blackhawk BBQ Pit grew, 
Nic felt many of the aspects of the military he had been missing 
return: mission, teamwork, family, service, and a connection to the 
freedoms he had once served to protect.
  While his once little family venture has become a thriving business, 
Nic maintains the same values. He coined the motto: ``LIVE FREE. EAT 
BBQ.'' This statement reflects not only how customers are meant to 
enjoy Blackhawk's food but is also ingrained in the business culture. 
Blackhawk BBQ Pit proudly employs veterans, who bring a strong 
commitment to country and service, and Nic actively works to honor 
veterans, first responders, and police throughout the community.
  Thank you, Nic Transtrum, for your service to our country, and 
congratulations to Blackhawk BBQ Pit for being selected as an Idaho 
Small Business of the Month for November 2026. You are an outstanding 
example of the immeasurable impact veterans have in communities across 
Idaho and the U.S. You make the Gem State proud, and I look forward to 
your continued success.●
```
