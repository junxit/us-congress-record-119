# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3978, H.R. 8552, H.R. 9612, H.R. 9613, H.R. 9614

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered by 
Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3978. An act to amend the definition of ``production 
     facility'' in the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to exclude an 
     equipment or device capable of reprocessing spent nuclear 
     fuel in a manner that does not separate plutonium from other 
     transuranic elements, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 8552. An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2026, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 9612. An act to amend the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to 
     update the licensing procedures for uranium enrichment 
     facilities to enable the timely, safe deployment of such 
     facilities, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 9613. An act to update the role of the Advisory 
     Committee on Reactor Safeguards in the licensing and 
     oversight of nuclear reactor facilities, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 9614. An act to amend the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to 
     provide compensation authority for certain career appointees, 
     and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to 2 U.S.C. 1151(a), and the 
order of the House of January 3, 2025, the Speaker appoints the 
following Member on the part of the House of Representatives to the 
Board of Trustees of the Congressional Office for International 
Leadership: Mr. Case of Hawaii.
  The message further announced that pursuant to 20 U.S.C. 1011c, and 
the

[[Page S5410]]

order of the House of January 3, 2025, the Speaker appoints the 
following individuals on the part of the House of Representatives to 
the National Advisory Committee on Institutional Quality and Integrity 
for a term of 6 years: Upon the recommendation of the Minority Leader: 
Mr. Robert Shireman of Oakland, California and Ms. Dominique Baker of 
Wilmington, Delaware.
```
