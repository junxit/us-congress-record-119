# RECOGNIZING J-DOG

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                           RECOGNIZING J-DOG

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, Idaho's veterans made great 
sacrifices for our freedoms, and we owe them our immense gratitude. 
Many who returned from service continue to be mission-driven, highly 
skilled, dedicated to success, and have opened small businesses vital 
to the Gem State. This month, I am proud to recognize the veteran-owned 
small businesses that make Idaho's communities and our economy thrive. 
As a member and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small 
Business and Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor J-Dog in Coeur 
d'Alene as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of the Month for November 
2026.
  John Merwald joined the U.S. Marine Corps in 1978, serving for 10 
years in the infantry and as a combat engineer across two deployments. 
After leaving the military, John continued his service through the 
Spokane Veterans Stand Down, the Department of Veterans Affairs' post-
9/11 transition team, and veterans' employment programs with the Idaho 
and Spokane Departments of Labor. Today, he serves on the boards of the 
Idaho Veterans Assistance League, North Idaho Stand

[[Page S5409]]

Down, and the Kootenai County Veterans Council.
  In 2020, John and his wife partnered with J-Dog Junk Removal & 
Hauling to expand their services into north Idaho. From commercial 
demolition to emergency response for fire- and water-damaged 
properties, John and J-Dog work to meet the needs of local communities 
throughout the region.
  J-Dog is a veteran- and military-family-owned operation. In their 6 
years of business, J-Dog North Idaho has grown from employing 3 
veterans to 38. John attributes their success to the team's focus on 
providing opportunities for veterans, military personnel, first 
responders, and their families.
  Thank you, John Merwald, for your service to our country, and 
congratulations to J-Dog for being selected as an Idaho Small Business 
of the Month for November 2026. You are an outstanding example of the 
immeasurable impact veterans have in communities across Idaho and the 
U.S. You make the Gem State proud, and I look forward to your continued 
success.●
```
