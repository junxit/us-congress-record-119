# RECOGNIZING RANGE 208

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                         RECOGNIZING RANGE 208

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, Idaho's veterans made great 
sacrifices for our freedoms, and we owe them our immense gratitude. 
Many who returned from service continue to be mission-driven, highly 
skilled, dedicated to success, and have opened small businesses vital 
to the Gem State. This month, I am proud to recognize the veteran-owned 
small businesses that make Idaho's communities and our economy thrive. 
As a member and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small 
Business and Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor Range 208 in 
Lewiston as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of the Month for November 
2026.
  Joe Alicea enlisted in the U.S. Marine Corps in April 2000, serving 
for 8 years and completing two deployments. His extraordinary service 
earned him numerous honors, including the Presidential Unit Citation, 
National Defense Service Medal, Combat Action Ribbon, and Rifle and 
Pistol Expert Badges. He served as an artillery surveyor and later to 
survey information chief, a position typically held by higher ranking 
Marines.
  In 2025, Joe purchased Diamondback Shooting Range and renamed it to 
Range 208. Tributes to Joe's service and the Armed Forces are 
incorporated throughout the business. Its logo honors his time in the 
Marine Corps, depicting the 1st Marine Division's ``Blue Diamond'' and 
the six stars of Orion's Belt and Sword. For Joe, the stars represent 
both his identity as a warrior and his mission of building a community 
of trained, responsible citizens.
  Range 208 is the only indoor shooting range and training academy 
withing 100 miles of the Lewis-Clark Valley, and Joe is proud to 
welcome clients of all skill levels and experiences. He offers 
everything from introductory courses to defensive pistol training, as 
well as one-on-one instruction. As a brother to four sisters and a 
father to three daughters, Joe is particularly committed to helping 
women develop the knowledge and confidence to handle firearms safely. 
Range 208 offers many classes designed specifically for women, which 
have become their largest clientele group.
  Joe and his team also believe strongly in giving back to their 
community. This October, Range 208 hosted Aim for Advocacy, a month-
long target challenge competition. to find the best local shot. 
Proceeds raised during the event were donated to the Two Rivers United 
Child Advocacy Center in Lewiston, which works with local law 
enforcement, child protective services, prosecutors, medical 
professionals, and therapists to investigate reports of child abuse and 
support children after they have experienced violence.
  Thank you, Joe Alicea, for your service to our country, and 
congratulations to Range 208 for being selected as an Idaho Small 
Business of the Month for November 2026. You are an outstanding example 
of the immeasurable impact veterans have in communities across Idaho 
and the U.S. You make the Gem State proud, and I look forward to your 
continued success.●

                          ____________________
```
