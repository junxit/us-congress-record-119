# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 945, Gregory Carl Cook, of Alabama, 
     to be United States District Judge for the Northern District 
     of Alabama.
         John Thune, Thom Tillis, Roger Marshall, Ted Budd, John 
           Cornyn, Marsha Blackburn, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Bernie 
           Moreno, Mike Rounds, Shelley Moore Capito, John 
           Barrasso, James Lankford, Mike Crapo, John Hoeven, John 
           Boozman, Chuck Grassley, Cynthia M. Lummis.

                          ____________________
```
