                 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF HOMEWOOD, ALABAMA

● Mrs. BRITT. Madam President, I rise today to recognize the 
city of Homewood, AL, as it celebrates its 100th anniversary on October 
29, 2026. For a century, Homewood has exemplified the spirit of 
community, civic pride, and local leadership that makes Alabama such a 
special place to call home.
  This centennial is an opportunity to celebrate not only the city's 
history, but also the generations of people whose dedication, hard 
work, and vision have shaped Homewood into the thriving city it is 
today.
  Homewood's story began on October 29, 1926, when the communities of 
Edgewood, Rosedale, and Grove Park came together to form a new city in 
Shades Valley, just south of Red Mountain and neighboring Birmingham. 
Their decision reflected a shared commitment to building a community 
that could serve its residents and provide a strong foundation for 
future generations.
  Over the decades, Homewood has grown and evolved while maintaining 
the distinct character that makes it such a treasured part of the 
Birmingham metropolitan area. The city's neighborhoods, local 
businesses, parks, and public spaces reflect a deep sense of belonging 
and a commitment to preserving the qualities that make a community feel 
like home.
  Education has been an important part of that legacy. In 1970, 
Homewood established its own independent school system, demonstrating 
the community's commitment to investing in its children. Today, 
Homewood's schools continue to play an essential role in preparing the 
next generation for success and giving them the tools to succeed and 
achieve their American dream.
  As we celebrate this milestone, we recognize that a city's legacy is 
measured not simply by years, but also by the people who give it 
meaning. We honor the founders who envisioned Homewood a century ago, 
the generations who built and sustained it, and the residents, 
educators, public servants, business owners, first responders, and 
leaders who continue to contribute to its success.
  I also rise to recognize the importance of preserving Homewood's 
history while embracing the opportunities and responsibilities that lie 
ahead. The next century will bring new challenges

and new possibilities, and the same community spirit that guided 
Homewood's founding will remain essential to its future. As its 
residents gather to commemorate 100 years, I am proud to join them in 
celebrating a storied history and looking forward to the next century 
of progress.
  I am honored to recognize the city of Homewood, AL, as it celebrates 
a century of community, opportunity, and history. May the city of 
Homewood continue to prosper, may its neighborhoods remain places of 
connection and opportunity, and may the next 100 years bring continued 
growth, enduring friendships, and new achievements that take this 
remarkable city to even greater heights.●
