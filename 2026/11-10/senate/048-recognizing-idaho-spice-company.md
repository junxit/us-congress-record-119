# RECOGNIZING IDAHO SPICE COMPANY

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                    RECOGNIZING IDAHO SPICE COMPANY

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, Idaho's veterans made great 
sacrifices for our freedoms, and we owe them our immense gratitude. 
Many who returned from service continue to be mission-driven, highly 
skilled, dedicated to success, and have opened small businesses vital 
to the Gem State. This month, I am proud to recognize the veteran-owned 
small businesses that make Idaho's communities and our economy thrive. 
As a member and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small 
Business and Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor Idaho Spice 
Company in Caldwell as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of the Month for 
November 2026.
  Craig Smith joined the U.S. Army during the first Gulf War, where he 
trained as a laboratory technician. Throughout his 10 years of service, 
Craig earned numerous medals and awards, including the Army 
Commendation Medal and Good Conduct Medal. After leaving the military, 
Craig continued his service by working at the Boise Veterans 
Administration for a decade.
  Idaho Spice Company began around the Smith kitchen table, as Craig 
and his wife Katie started cooking meals in bulk as an affordable and 
tasty way to feed their family of eight. Craig discovered his passion 
for experimenting with different spices and ingredients to create 
delicious flavors the whole family could enjoy. As his interest in 
cooking grew, Craig left his job at the VA to train to be a chef. In 
2019, Idaho Spice Company made its first appearance at the Caldwell 
Farmers Market.
  Originally offering six spice blends, Idaho Spice Company now has 
more than 30 unique blends in a variety of sizes, along with a wide 
selection of spices, teas, and sauces. In November 2023, the Smiths 
opened their first storefront in Caldwell, creating a welcoming place 
for the community to discover new flavors. Through hands-on classes, 
Idaho Spice Company helps customers learn how to use their products and 
feel more confident in their own kitchens.
  Craig and Katie place an emphasis on supporting the community that 
has helped build their business. Idaho Spice Company regularly 
participates in local fundraisers, donates food baskets and gift boxes 
to schools and nonprofits, and works alongside Vallivue FFA and the 
College of Idaho's annual Go Purple Steak Feed.
  Thank you, Craig Smith, for your service to our country, and 
congratulations to Idaho Spice Company for being selected as an Idaho 
Small Business of the Month for November 2026. You are an outstanding 
example of the immeasurable impact veterans have in communities across 
Idaho and the U.S. You make the Gem State proud, and I look forward to 
your continued success.●
```
