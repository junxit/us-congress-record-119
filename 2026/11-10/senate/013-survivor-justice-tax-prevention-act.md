# SURVIVOR JUSTICE TAX PREVENTION ACT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 2347

```
                  SURVIVOR JUSTICE TAX PREVENTION ACT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Lummis). The clerk will report the bill by 
title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2347) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to exclude from gross income any damages, other than 
     punitive damages, received on account of any sexual acts or 
     sexual contact.


                   Recognition of the Majority Leader

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.


                           Amendment No. 6883

  (Purpose: In the nature of a substitute.)
  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I call up substitute amendment No. 6883.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       The Senator from South Dakota [Mr. Thune], for Mrs. CAPITO, 
     proposes an amendment numbered 6883.

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask that the reading be dispensed with.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  (The amendment is printed in today's Record under ``Text of 
Amendments.'')
  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask for the yeas and nays on the 
amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays are ordered.


                Amendment No. 6884 to Amendment No. 6883

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I have an amendment at the desk to 
substitute amendment No. 6883.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       The Senator from South Dakota [Mr. Thune] proposes an 
     amendment numbered 6884 to amendment No. 6883.

  Mr. THUNE. I ask the reading be dispensed with.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment is as follows:

                     (Purpose: To improve the bill)

       At the end add the following.
       ``This Act shall take effect 1 day after the date of 
     enactment.''
  Mr. THUNE. I ask for the yeas and nays on the amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays are ordered.


                Amendment No. 6885 to Amendment No. 6884

desk.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment is as follows:
```
