# TRIBUTE TO DR. TUCKER PATTERSON

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO DR. TUCKER PATTERSON

  Mr. BOOZMAN. Madam President, I rise today to recognize Dr. Tucker 
Patterson upon his retirement as Director of the Food and Drug 
Administration's (FDA) National Center for Toxicological Research 
(NCTR) in Jefferson, AR, and congratulate him on his distinguished 
career of public service.
  For more than three decades, Dr. Patterson has worked to advance 
scientific understanding, protect public health and serve others. He 
first joined the FDA in 1992 as an ORISE fellow at NCTR. After spending 
3 years with the State of Arkansas, he returned to NCTR in 2001 as a 
research biologist and steadily advanced through positions of 
increasing leadership and responsibility. He was appointed deputy 
director for research in 2020 and has served as Center Director since 
March 2022, providing steady leadership and helping advance NCTR's 
important mission.
  Throughout his career, Dr. Patterson has made significant 
contributions to the field of toxicology. The research he conducted in 
neuroscience and neurotoxicology has helped address complex scientific 
challenges and strengthened the FDA's ability to evaluate the safety of 
regulated products. His contributions have earned recognition from FDA 
leadership, including for his work with the Pediatric Anesthesia 
Research Group, the Commissioner's Fellowship Program Evaluation Group 
and the FDA One Health initiative. With more than 140 scientific 
publications to his credit, Dr. Patterson has made a lasting impact on 
the scientific community.
  Beyond his work at NCTR, Dr. Patterson has played an important role 
in advancing international collaboration through the Global Coalition 
for Regulatory Science Research and the Global Summit on Regulatory 
Science. Most recently, he served as chairman of the FDA-led 
international consortium focused on applying emerging technologies and 
big data to strengthen regulatory science and food and drug safety.
  Just as important as his professional accomplishments is Dr. 
Patterson's commitment to giving back. He has volunteered his expertise 
on medical missions to underserved communities around the world, 
helping provide vision screenings and eyeglasses to those in need. Here 
at home, he has supported the next generation of Natural State 
scientists through his involvement with the Arkansas Research Alliance, 
mentoring young professionals and helping them pursue opportunities in 
scientific research.
  Dr. Patterson has built a career that reflects hard work, dedication, 
and a commitment to serving others. His leadership has served an 
important institution in Arkansas incredibly well, advanced scientific 
inquiry, and inspired those who have had the opportunity to work 
alongside him. We are fortunate to have someone of his caliber 
contributing to our State and making a difference across the Nation.
  On behalf of the people of Arkansas, I congratulate Dr. Tucker 
Patterson on his retirement and thank him for his more than 30 years of 
public service. I wish him and his family all the best in this well-
deserved next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
