# TRIBUTE TO BENJAMIN PINEIRO

**Speaking:** Hassan, Margaret Wood (H001076) [D-NH]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO BENJAMIN PINEIRO

● Ms. HASSAN. Madam President, I am honored to recognize 
Benjamin Pineiro of Salem as October's Granite Stater of the Month for 
his efforts to spread kindness and make a meaningful difference in the 
lives of others while working at ScrubaDub.
  When Benjamin began working at ScrubaDub, a car wash in Salem, he 
wanted to find a way to brighten customers' days. He thought back to 
his grandfather, who used to make him smile by drawing cartoon 
characters like Mickey Mouse. Hoping to do the same for others, 
Benjamin spent 3 months perfecting his own Mickey Mouse drawing before 
he began drawing the character on customers' windows as they entered 
the car wash. Through a simple act of creativity and kindness, Benjamin 
showed that a car wash doesn't have to be an ordinary thing; he found 
ways to help children who were running an errand with their parents 
have a bit more fun. Whether greeting regular visitors, striking up 
conversations, or drawing beloved cartoon characters on car windows, 
Benjamin went out of his way to make every customer feel welcome and 
appreciated.
  Benjamin understands that even the smallest of gestures can make a 
meaningful difference in someone's day. Over the years, he formed 
special relationships with customers, including a young boy and his 
mother who visited the car wash regularly. Benjamin's drawings and 
friendly greetings became a highlight of their visits, and he was 
deeply moved to learn how much his kindness meant to the family. For 
Benjamin, these interactions were about more than making customers 
smile. He recognized that everyone faces challenges, and he hoped that 
even a brief moment of kindness could make someone's day a little 
easier.
  Benjamin has since left ScrubaDub to pursue his passion for music. He 
now works one-on-one with children as a drum instructor, helping them 
develop their musical abilities, build confidence, and express their 
creativity. He brings the same compassion and enthusiasm to his lessons 
that he brought to the car wash, creating a welcoming environment where 
his students feel supported and encouraged.
  From making drawings at ScrubaDub to the way he approaches teaching 
music, Benjamin has shown that even the smallest acts of kindness can 
have a lasting impact. His commitment to finding ways to brighten the 
lives of those around him is why I am proud to name him October's 
Granite Stater of the Month.●

                          ____________________
```
