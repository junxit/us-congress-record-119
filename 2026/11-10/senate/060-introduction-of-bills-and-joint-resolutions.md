# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 5696, S. 5697, S. 5698, S. 5699, S. 5700, S. 5701, S. 5702, S. 5703, S. 5704, S. 5705, S. 5706, S. 5707, S. 5708, S. 5709, S. 5710, S. 5711, S. 5712, S. 5713, S. 5714, S. 5715

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. DUCKWORTH (for herself, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. 
             King, Mr. Sanders, Mr. Kelly, Mr. Welch, Mr. Bennet, 
             Mrs. Murray, Mr. Fetterman, Mr. Wyden, Ms. Cantwell, 
             Ms. Alsobrooks, and Mr. Van Hollen):
       S. 5696. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     improve presumptions of toxic exposure, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Mr. LANKFORD (for himself and Mr. Peters):
       S. 5697. A bill to ensure that Federal agencies rely on the 
     best reasonably available scientific, technical, demographic, 
     economic, and statistical information and evidence to 
     develop, issue or inform the public of the nature and bases 
     of Federal agency rules and guidance, and for other purposes; 
     to the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental 
     Affairs.
           By Mr. KING (for himself and Ms. Warren):
       S. 5698. A bill to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     protect the integrity of the United States Preventive 
     Services Task Force, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Ms. BALDWIN (for herself and Mrs. Gillibrand):
       S. 5699. A bill to provide greater support for 
     grandfamilies and older caregiver relatives; to the Committee 
     on Finance.
           By Ms. BALDWIN (for herself, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. 
             Merkley, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Reed, Ms. Duckworth, Mr. 
             Kim, Mr. Booker, Mr. Markey, and Mrs. Gillibrand):
       S. 5700. A bill to amend title XVIII of the Social Security 
     Act to provide for an option for any citizen or permanent 
     resident of the United States age 50 to 64 to buy into 
     Medicare; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. BOOKER (for himself, Mr. Husted, and Ms. 
             Collins):
       S. 5701. A bill to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     include certain Federal positions within the definition of 
     law enforcement officer for retirement purposes, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
           By Mr. BOOKER (for himself, Ms. Hirono, and Mr. 
             Durbin):
       S. 5702. A bill to improve maternal health policies in 
     correctional facilities, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Ms. ALSOBROOKS (for herself and Mr. Tillis):
       S. 5703. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     establish a program for the provision of support services to 
     borrowers using housing loan benefits administered by the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Ms. ROSEN (for herself and Mrs. Fischer):
       S. 5704. A bill to require the Secretary of Health and 
     Human Services to improve the detection, prevention, and 
     treatment of behavioral health issues among public safety 
     officers, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Health, 
     Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. MARKEY:
       S. 5705. A bill authorizing grants to support long-term 
     resiliency for archival institutions, libraries, and museums 
     in order to provide continuity and access to covered records; 
     to the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental 
     Affairs.
           By Mr. MARKEY (for himself, Mr. Merkley, Mr. Sanders, 
             Ms. Warren, and Mr. Welch):
       S. 5706. A bill to amend the Bank Holding Company Act of 
     1956 and the Financial Stability Act of 2010 to require a 
     reduction of financial sector emissions to protect financial 
     stability, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
           By Mr. YOUNG (for himself and Mr. Booker):
       S. 5707. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to establish that employers may offer employees a choice 
     among tax-favored employer contributions; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Mr. CRUZ:
       S. 5708. A bill to designate the community-based outpatient 
     clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Victoria, 
     Texas, as the ``Master Sergeant Roy P. Benavidez VA Clinic''; 
     to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND:
       S. 5709. A bill to require the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to conduct an epidemiological study of certain health 
     effects that have been experienced by women veterans, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Mr. CORNYN (for himself, Ms. Hassan, Mr. Tillis, Mr. 
             Kaine, and Ms. Collins):
       S. 5710. A bill to support local educational agencies in 
     addressing the student mental health crisis; to the Committee 
     on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. BOOZMAN (for himself and Mr. Padilla):
       S. 5711. A bill to allow the Secretary of the Interior to 
     enter into contracts or agreements with eligible entities to 
     carry out habitat management activities in units of the 
     National Wildlife Refuge System, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. WELCH (for himself and Mr. Crapo):
       S. 5712. A bill to require reporting by the Department of 
     Defense on the use of open-air burn pits, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. BOOKER (for himself and Mr. Kim):
       S. 5713. A bill to protect employees from discrimination 
     based on family caregiver responsibilities, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
           By Mr. DURBIN (for himself and Mr. Lee):
       S. 5714. A bill to subject professional baseball clubs to 
     the antitrust laws; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. PADILLA (for himself, Mr. Blumenthal, and Ms. 
             Warren):
       S. 5715. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, and 
     the United States Housing Act of 1937, to make certain 
     improvements to the supported housing program for veterans 
     commonly known as ``HUD-VASH'', and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
```
