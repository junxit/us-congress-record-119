# TRIBUTE TO TIM GATZ

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO TIM GATZ

● Mr. LANKFORD. Madam President, I rise today to honor the 
retirement announcement of Tim Gatz as the Oklahoma Secretary of 
Transportation and executive director of the Oklahoma Department of 
Transportation, ODOT. Tim has been the lead expert on transportation in 
Oklahoma for years and

[[Page S5406]]

has garnered national honors and respect from his many peers and 
colleagues. Under his leadership, Oklahoma's transportation system has 
improved drastically, and millions of Oklahomans benefited from his 
extensive career as a public servant.
  A native Oklahoman, Tim graduated from Oklahoma State University in 
1989 with a degree in landscape architecture. Upon graduation, Tim 
began his career at ODOT in 1990 as a drafting technician, and what 
began as an entry level position transformed into a tremendous career 
of public service. Tim worked at ODOT his entire professional career 
and displayed his passion for transportation, his leadership ability, 
and his hard work and dedication to the people of Oklahoma.
  Tim Gatz was appointed as executive director of the Oklahoma 
Department of Transportation, ODOT, and secretary of transportation in 
2019 and was subsequently reappointed in 2024. Prior to these roles, 
Tim served as the executive director of the Oklahoma Turnpike 
Authority, deputy director of ODOT, director of administration at ODOT, 
and director of capital programs. His career spanned 6 Governors, 19 
different State legislatures, and 6 prior ODOT directors. He has been a 
trusted adviser to numerous Federal, State, and local officials. He 
even testified before the Senate Environment and Public Works 
Transportation and Infrastructure Subcommittee in May 2017.
  In his 37 years at ODOT, Tim worked on countless transportation 
projects that have contributed to the State's highway system, 
railroads, and waterways. Among his accomplishments, Tim helped lead 
the State from 49th in the Nation for bridge conditions in 2004 to 4th 
in the Nation in 2025. He was instrumental in the development of ODOT's 
8-Year construction work plan and is credited with streamlining many 
project delivery processes at ODOT during his transportation career.
  Tim is a registered professional landscape architect and has received 
several honors throughout his career, including the Governor's Public 
Service Award and the Federal Highway Administration's Partners in 
Quality Award. He has been actively involved in several national and 
State commissions and associations, including the United States and 
State of Oklahoma Route 66 Centennial Commissions, the International 
Bridge, Tunnel and Turnpike Association, and the American Society of 
Landscape Architects. He is also a member of the board of directors of 
the American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials 
where he serves as chairman of the committee on funding and finance.
  Tim's leadership in transportation will be sorely missed by future 
generations of Oklahomans. Transportation projects can often be a slow, 
turtle-paced process that can be frustrating for the traveling public. 
However, infrastructure is one of the few government functions that can 
be seen directly and is the first impression for most Americans. Tim 
has done a tremendous job serving the people of Oklahoma by 
transforming the State's transportation network. Oklahoma's highways, 
railroads, and waterways are better today because of Tim Gatz.
  Tim currently resides in El Reno, OK, with his wife Sandy. They have 
two sons--Dillon, and spouse Meagan, Trace, and spouse Rachel--as well 
as four grandchildren and twins on the way. He is looking forward to 
spending more time with family and working on projects around the 
house. Tim, thank you for your leadership and friendship.●
```
