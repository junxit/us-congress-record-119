# TRIBUTE TO ISAAC BELDEN

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ISAAC BELDEN

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, the Gem State is made strong by 
the hard-working, dedicated, and selfless actions of Idahoans. Across 
our State, hundreds of thousands of veterans, first responders, 
volunteers, and public servants go above and beyond for the communities 
they call home. These people are pillars of our State and are essential 
to keeping Idaho the best place to live, work, and raise a family. 
Through my Idaho Service Spotlight, I recognize these exceptional 
Idahoans whose service and leadership have made a lasting impact on the 
Gem State. Today, I am pleased to recognize Isaac Belden as an Idaho 
Service Spotlight recipient for the Month of October 2026.
  Isaac served 12 years as a combat engineer in the Idaho Army National 
Guard, including a deployment to Iraq in support of Operation Iraqi 
Freedom. Isaac's service shaped his leadership style, instilling in him 
a strong sense of discipline, accountability, and a relentless focus on 
the mission at hand. After retiring from the military, Isaac, like many 
veterans, found himself missing the structure, brotherhood, and sense 
of purpose that had defined his identity.
  Isaac's path led him to discover a new purpose in business and 
entrepreneurship. He quickly recognized the large number of veterans 
who faced similar struggles following military service. That 
realization inspired Isaac to create his nonprofit, the Veteran 
Entrepreneur Alliance, in 2019.
  The Veteran Entrepreneur Alliance is dedicated to helping veterans 
and military spouses navigate their return to civilian life and offer 
assistance in their entrepreneurial endeavors. Recognizing that many 
veterans have a vision but may not have access to the necessary 
resources to make that vision a reality, Isaac works to fill that gap. 
Under his leadership, VEA has grown into a statewide organization that 
provides veterans and military spouses with business education, 
mentorship, and resources at no cost.
  Since its founding, VEA has served more than 1,500 veterans across 
the Gem State, helping to launch more than 700 veteran-owned small 
businesses. Isaac's dedicated work through the Veteran Entrepreneur 
Alliance is a continuation of his life's mission of serving others.
  On behalf of the people of Idaho, I thank and congratulate Isaac for 
being selected as an Idaho Service Spotlight recipient for the Month of 
October 2026. You reflect the very best of our State, and Idaho is 
stronger because of your actions.●
```
