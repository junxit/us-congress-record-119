# TRIBUTE TO MIKE THOMASON

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MIKE THOMASON

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, I rise today to recognize Mike 
Thomason, a remarkable Idahoan whose dedication to his community has 
made a significant impact on the Northwest. As Mike enters a well-
deserved retirement, I extend my sincere gratitude for his strong 
leadership as commissioner for the Port of Lewiston and congratulate 
him on a lifetime of faithful service.
  Mike began his career in the electric and natural gas industry in 
1978. For 33 years, he helped Avista, a leading supplier of energy to 
the region, power local businesses and households. Mike worked his way 
up the ladder and served in a variety of roles, from government 
relations to strategic corporate philanthropy, before retiring in March 
2011.
  Retirement, however, did not last long. In January 2013, he was 
appointed as a commissioner of the Port of Lewiston. As commissioner, 
Mike played an important role in advancing economic development in the 
Lewis-Clark Valley. His focus on attracting businesses, creating jobs, 
and bringing the first passenger cruise line to Idaho, has left an 
enduring mark on the Gem State's only seaport.
  Mike's work to promote the Port and the Lewis-Clark Valley community 
was far-reaching. In addition to his duties as commissioner, he has 
served on the boards of the Lewis-Clark Chamber of Commerce, St. Joseph 
Regional Medical Center, and Valley Vision.
  As Mike enters this next chapter, his leadership, care, and 
dedication will continue to shape the future of the Lewis-Clark Valley 
for many years to come. Today, I join many Idahoans in extending my 
deepest appreciation to Mike Thomason for his many years of devoted 
service.●

                          ____________________
```
