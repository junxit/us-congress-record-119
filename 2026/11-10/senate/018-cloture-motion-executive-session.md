# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 901, Daniel Desmond Domenico, of 
     Colorado, to be United States Circuit Judge for the Tenth 
     Circuit.
         John Thune, Tim Sheehy, John Barrasso, Pete Ricketts, 
           Thom Tillis, Chuck Grassley, Kevin Cramer, Ted Budd, 
           Shelley Moore Capito, Cynthia M. Lummis, Marsha 
           Blackburn, Tim Scott of South Carolina, Bill Hagerty, 
           Jim Banks, John R. Curtis, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Joni 
           Ernst.

                          ____________________
```
