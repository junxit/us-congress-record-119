# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, who in Your wisdom so numbered and ordered our days 
that we must walk by faith and not by sight, increase our trust in Your 
sovereign care.
  As the U.S. Senate turns to a new chapter in its history, remind our 
lawmakers that no challenge is beyond Your power and no circumstance 
beyond Your ability to redeem.
  Lord, grant our Senators a faith that triumphs over despair, a love 
that triumphs over hate, and a courage that triumphs over fear and 
faintheartedness. Strengthen their resolve, sharpen their judgment, and 
deepen their commitment to the common good. Empower them to be bold and 
faithful participants in the awesome responsibility of preserving our 
Nation's strength, advancing its promise, and safeguarding the freedoms 
entrusted to us.
  May their decisions be guided by wisdom, their actions tempered by 
compassion, and their leadership sustained by an abiding commitment to 
justice and the well-being of all our people.
  We offer this prayer in Your great and Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
