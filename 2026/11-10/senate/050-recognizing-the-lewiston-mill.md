# RECOGNIZING THE LEWISTON MILL

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                     RECOGNIZING THE LEWISTON MILL

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, I rise today to celebrate the 
Idaho Forest Group's Lewiston Mill and its 100 years of operation. 
Located adjacent to the Clearwater River, the Lewiston Mill has a long 
history of lumber production and remains a pillar of north central 
Idaho's economy.
  The Lewiston Mill is part of Idaho Forest Group, a family-founded 
business headquartered in Coeur d'Alene that has grown into one of the 
Nation's largest lumber producers. With seven sawmills and customers 
around North America, Idaho Forest Group produces more than 1 billion 
board feet each year, much of which comes from its Lewiston facility.
  The Lewiston Mill was one of the first large mills in the Northwest 
when it was built in the early 1920s. Spanning 180 acres, the sawmill 
has evolved alongside Idaho's timber industry. Over a 10-year period 
that began in 2012, the mill underwent major improvements, including 
two new saw lines and a log merchandising and sorting line, 
strengthening the facility's ability to provide high-quality Idaho 
lumber. Today, the mill can produce more than 300 million board feet 
each year.
  The Lewiston Mill's impact extends well beyond lumber production. It 
is a driver of the local economy, provides jobs, and plays a key role 
in North American lumber supply chains. Through an agreement with 
Clearwater Paper, the mill also supplies wood chips and sawdust that 
are vital to paper production at the Lewiston Pulp Mill.
  For 100 years, the Lewiston Mill has helped put Idaho timber to work. 
Its history is a reminder that the forest products industry remains a 
cornerstone of the north central Idaho economy, supporting families, 
rural communities, and generations of skilled Idaho workers.
  Congratulations to Idaho Forest Group and the Lewiston Mill on 100 
years of operation. I look forward to the continued success of this 
essential mill and its contributions to Idaho.●
```
