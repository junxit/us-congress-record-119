# TRIBUTE TO JACQUE BURT

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JACQUE BURT

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, the Gem State is made strong by 
the hard-working, dedicated, and selfless actions of Idahoans. Across 
our State, hundreds of thousands of veterans, first responders, 
volunteers, and public servants go above and beyond for the communities 
they call home. These people are pillars of our State and are essential 
to keeping Idaho the best place to live, work, and raise a family. 
Through my Idaho Service Spotlight, I recognize these exceptional 
Idahoans whose service and leadership have made a lasting impact on the 
Gem State. Today, I am pleased to recognize Jacque Burt as an Idaho 
Service Spotlight recipient for the Month of October 2026.
  For more than two decades, Jacque has dedicated her life to serving 
children and families in foster care. Her journey began in 2000, when 
she became a foster parent. For more than 17 years, she welcomed 
infants, teenagers, siblings, and children from all paths of life into 
her home. Along the way, Jacque adopted children of her own from foster 
care and found great joy in helping children and families successfully 
reunify.
  Jacque's journey as a foster mother eventually led her to the Idaho 
Foster & Adoptive Parent Association, where she currently serves as 
president. In January 2020, Jacque started The Village, a foster care 
closet in Blackfoot that initially served 10 children each month. With 
a small grant, a handful of volunteers, and a belief that children 
entering foster care deserve more, The Village grew into a regional hub 
for foster families across 16 counties in southeast Idaho. Today, The 
Village serves 125 children each month, from newborns to young adults.
  Jacque's focus is centered on easing the trauma children in foster 
care can experience, building their self-esteem, and surrounding them 
with love. During her time as a foster mother, Jacque witnessed the 
significant impact unfamiliar environments had on a child in foster 
care. She also noticed the tremendous difference between receiving 
hand-me-downs and giving a child the opportunity to choose something 
for themselves. At The Village, children are encouraged to do just that 
by selecting the clothing, toys, and personal items they would enjoy 
the most and taking them home.
  The Village runs entirely on volunteers and community support, 
ensuring

children can shop at no cost to families or caregivers. This year, The 
Village relocated to a larger facility, allowing it to expand its

resources for grandparents or family members who are raising children 
without the child's parents. The Village's mission is also growing. 
Soon, Jacque and the team will offer resources to support young people 
preparing to age out of foster care and take the next step toward

  On behalf of the people of Idaho, I thank and congratulate Jacque for 
being selected as an Idaho Service Spotlight recipient for the Month of 
October 2026. You reflect the very best of our State, and Idaho is 
stronger because of your actions.●
```
