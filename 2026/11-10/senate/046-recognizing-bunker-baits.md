# RECOGNIZING BUNKER BAITS

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                        RECOGNIZING BUNKER BAITS

● Mr. RISCH. Madam President, Idaho's veterans made great 
sacrifices for our freedoms, and we owe them our immense gratitude. 
Many who returned from service continue to be mission-driven, highly 
skilled, dedicated to success, and have opened small businesses vital 
to the Gem State. This month, I am proud to recognize the veteran-owned 
small businesses that make Idaho's communities and our economy thrive. 
As a member and former chairman of the Senate Committee on Small

[[Page S5408]]

Business and Entrepreneurship, I am pleased to honor Bunker Baits in 
Kuna as one of Idaho's Small Businesses of the Month for November 2026.
  Andrew Myers proudly served our Nation for 13 years as a U.S. Army 
airborne infantryman, deploying to Iraq and Afghanistan before serving 
at the Joint Readiness Training Center at Fort Polk. After his medical 
retirement, as Andrew recovered from spinal surgery, a friend 
introduced him to making fishing baits as a creative outlet and a 
therapeutic way to find purpose in his next chapter.
  In 2021, Andrew turned that passion into Bunker Baits. A one-man 
operation run out of Andrew's garage, known as The Bunker, Bunker Baits 
manufactures soft plastic lures for bass fishing. Andrew started the 
business as an offshoot of his YouTube channel, Vet Bros Fishing, which 
he launched with his brother in 2019.
  Andrew's goal in growing his business is to provide high-quality, 
handmade baits for anglers and serve the veteran community. While 
transitioning to civilian life, Andrew found tranquility in the process 
of making baits, and he believes other veterans could experience the 
same benefits. His vision is to build a brotherhood and inspire 
employment opportunities for fellow veterans. Andrew hopes to move to a 
larger facility soon, allowing Bunker Baits to hire and mentor more 
veterans and help them build successful lives beyond military service.
  Thank you, Andrew Myers, for your service to our country, and 
congratulations to Bunker Baits for being selected as an Idaho Small 
Business of the Month for November 2026. You are an outstanding example 
of the immeasurable impact veterans have in communities across Idaho 
and the U.S. You make the Gem State proud, and I look forward to your 
continued success.●
```
