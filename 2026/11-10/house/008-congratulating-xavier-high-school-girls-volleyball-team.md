# CONGRATULATING XAVIER HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS' VOLLEYBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Wied, Tony (W000829) [R-WI]

```
        CONGRATULATING XAVIER HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS' VOLLEYBALL TEAM

  (Mr. Wied of Wisconsin was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Xavier High 
School girls' volleyball team from Appleton, Wisconsin, on winning the 
2026 WIAA Division 2 State championship.
  This marks the program's third State championship since 2022, an 
incredible accomplishment that reflects the dedication and hard work of 
these talented young athletes.
  Throughout the season, the Hawks showcased the teamwork, 
determination, and perseverance needed to compete at the highest level. 
This accomplishment is a reflection of their countless hours of 
practice, dedication, and hard work.
  I congratulate Xavier athletic director and head coach, Luke 
Herriges, and the entire team on such an incredible season and 
accomplishment.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
Xavier Hawks on this outstanding achievement. They made their school, 
the Appleton community, and all of northeastern Wisconsin incredibly 
proud. I wish these young athletes continued success in all of their 
future endeavors.


                       Recognizing Jeanne Stangel

  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jeanne Stangel, 
president and CEO of Curative Connections in Green Bay, who will retire 
this December after more than 35 years of dedicated service to 
northeastern Wisconsin.
  Throughout her long career, Jeanne has held leadership positions with 
The Salvation Army, Notre Dame Academy, and UW-Green Bay. Most 
recently, she led Curative Connections for 5 years, where she 
spearheaded a $5.3 million campaign that brought the world-class 
Yesteryear Village to life. This unique dementia care setting recreates 
a neighborhood from the 1960s and 1970s to bring comfort, familiarity, 
and cherished memories to those living with dementia.
  Through her leadership, Jeanne strengthened an organization that 
serves 1,600 individuals each week and brought nine other nonprofits 
together to establish an advocacy group that promotes independence and 
a better quality of life for older adults and individuals with 
disabilities across northeastern Wisconsin.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Jeanne 
Stangel on an outstanding career and thanking her for her decades of 
unwavering service. I wish her and her family all the very best as she 
begins her well-deserved retirement.


              Recognizing Greg Lexow and Kristin Banister

  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize paramedics Greg 
Lexow and Kristin Banister of the Marinette County Joint Special 
Response Team, who were recently recognized by the Marinette County 
Sheriff's Office and received the Legion of Merit award for their 
lifesaving actions during a tragic accident.
  Earlier this year, Greg and Kristin responded to an emergency 
involving an individual who had suffered a self-inflicted gunshot 
wound. Working under extraordinary circumstances, they provided 
immediate lifesaving medical care that stabilized the individual and 
ultimately saved their life.
  In moments like these, every second matters. Greg and Kristin 
demonstrated the skill, courage, and composure necessary to respond to 
an emergency. Their actions are an incredible example of our first 
responders' dedication and the difference they make in their 
communities every single day.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Greg 
Lexow and Kristin Banister on this well-deserved recognition and to 
thank them for their heroic actions and dedicated service to Marinette 
County and northeastern Wisconsin.
```
