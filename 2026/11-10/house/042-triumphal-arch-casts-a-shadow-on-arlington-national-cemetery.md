# TRIUMPHAL ARCH CASTS A SHADOW ON ARLINGTON NATIONAL CEMETERY

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
      TRIUMPHAL ARCH CASTS A SHADOW ON ARLINGTON NATIONAL CEMETERY

  (Ms. KAPTUR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, this Veterans Day week, I rise today to 
object to President Trump's poorly conceived plans for a triumphal arch 
that would cast a shadow over the sacred grounds of Arlington National 
Cemetery.
  The President's proposal has not been properly reviewed by 
architectural specialists. The proposed 250-foot-tall arch would break 
up the beauty and carefully constructed site lines from the Lincoln 
Memorial to Arlington National Cemetery.
  Further, this unnecessary and ostentatious gold-covered arch would 
cast shadows over the final resting place of 75,000 U.S. 
servicemembers. It would block the entrance.
  It would also impose shadows over the President John F. Kennedy 
Memorial, the Military Women's Memorial, and the grave sites of three 
Supreme Court Justices. One of the largest stretches of shadow will 
block light to the white marble markers of World War I and World War II 
veterans.
  The arch will also cast a shadow on the D.C. side of the river 
shadowing the Vietnam Veterans, the Lincoln, and the Korean War 
Veterans Memorials.
  Veterans interred at Arlington National Cemetery deserve respect. 
They deserve their final resting places to be treated with dignity. The 
President should reconsider this ill-suited monstrosity. Our veterans 
deserve so much better.
  Perhaps because there is no veteran in the Trump's immediate family 
there

Cemetery.
```
