# THANKING EMPORIA, KANSAS

**Speaking:** Schmidt, Derek (S001228) [R-KS]

```
                        THANKING EMPORIA, KANSAS

  (Mr. SCHMIDT asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SCHMIDT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Emporia, Kansas, the 
founding city of Veterans Day.
  In 1953, local resident Alvin J. King, moved by the loss of his 
nephew in World War II, believed our Nation should honor all veterans, 
not only those who served in the World War I.
  That November 11, Emporia hosted the first All Veterans Day, setting 
an example for our Nation.
  Then-Congressman Ed Rees of Emporia carried Mr. King's vision to 
Washington.
  In 1954, President Dwight D. Eisenhower, another notable Kansan, 
signed the bill establishing Veterans Day.
  This week, the Lyon County History Center in Emporia unveiled a 
plaque honoring Alvin King's leadership in the renaming of Armistice 
Day as Veterans Day. The plaque will be located at the site of Mr. 
King's former shoe store on Commercial Street in downtown Emporia, a 
fitting tribute to the man whose vision helped create our national 
tradition.
  Congress by concurrent resolution in 2003 recognized Emporia's role, 
and its legacy lives on every November 11.
  On this eve of Veterans Day, I thank Emporia and its citizens for 
their leadership and extend my deepest gratitude to all of America's 
veterans. We owe them a debt of gratitude that can never be fully 
repaid.

                          ____________________
```
