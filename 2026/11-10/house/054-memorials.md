# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 20, H.J.Res. 28, H.J.Res. 45

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-140. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Alaska, relative to House Joint Resolution 
     No. 20, encouraging the United States Congress to establish 
     hiring goals for apprentices and veteran apprentices; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
       ML-141. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of California, relative to Assembly Joint Resolution 
     No. 232, declaring the week of September 13 to September 19, 
     2026, inclusive, as Direct Support Professional Recognition 
     Week in California; to the Committee on Education and 
     Workforce.
       ML-142. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Alaska, relative to House Joint Resolution No. 45, urging the 
     United States Congress to fully fund the Individuals with 
     Disabilities Education Act; to the Committee on Education and 
     Workforce.
       ML-143. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of California, relative to Assembly Joint Resolution 
     No. 27, calling on the President of the United States to 
     immediately submit a supplemental disaster declaration to the 
     United States Congress to unlock funding for disaster aid and 
     also call upon the United States Congress to approve 
     supplemental aid to support the County of Los Angeles, 
     regardless of whether it receives a request from the 
     president; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.
       ML-144. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Alaska, relative to House Joint Resolution No. 28, calling on 
     the United States Congress to pass the Kids Online Safety 
     Act; jointly to the Committees on Energy and Commerce and 
     Education and Workforce.
       ML-145. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of California, relative to Assembly Joint Resolution 
     No. 9, urging the President of the United States and Congress 
     of the United States to immediately restore full and 
     consistent funding and staffing for the National Park 
     Service; jointly to the Committees on Natural Resources and 
     Agriculture.
       ML-146. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of California, relative to Assembly Joint Resolution 
     No. 29, declaring the Legislature's opposition to any federal 
     executive action that restricts, burdens, or interferes with 
     California's vote by mail system and would call upon the 
     United States Congress to reject any federal legislation that 
     would restrict or eliminate vote by mail options and to 
     protect the constitutional authority of states to administer 
     their own elections; jointly to the Committees on the 
     Judiciary, Oversight and Government Reform, Ways and Means, 
     and House Administration.

                          ____________________
```
