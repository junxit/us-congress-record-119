# HONORING MOUNT VERNON BOYS AND GIRLS CLUB

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
               HONORING MOUNT VERNON BOYS AND GIRLS CLUB

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Mount Vernon Boys 
and Girls Club.
  Serving more than 1,400 young people every day, this exceptional 
organization remains a cornerstone in the Mount Vernon community. By 
offering engaging academic programs, creative electives, and workforce 
development training, the Boys and Girls Club provides young people 
with the tools they need to succeed in and outside of the classroom.
  Beyond these programs, the club offers a safe, welcoming gathering 
place where members can discover new talents, evolve as students, and 
pursue their goals.
  I commend the leadership, staff, and mentors at the Boys and Girls 
Club of Mount Vernon for their dedication, and I look forward to seeing 
the impact its members will make in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
