# AMERICAN PEOPLE SENT A MESSAGE

**Speaking:** Dexter, Maxine (D000635) [D-OR]

```
                              {time}  1830
                     AMERICAN PEOPLE SENT A MESSAGE

  (Ms. DEXTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise because last week the American people 
sent a resounding message. They rejected a government that operates 
with impunity backed by a rubber-stamped Republican Congress.
  They rejected forever wars.
  Today, House Republicans had the chance to show they heard that 
message. Instead, they voted to bury Articles of Impeachment against 
Pete Hegseth.
  Two months in, he incompetently and illegally texted war plans to a 
group chat with a journalist in it.
  He has waged a war on his own ranks by purging trans servicemembers 
and firing women commanders.
  Then came his illegal war on Iran. On day one, an American strike hit 
a girl's school. More than 150 people died, mostly children.
  Our country deserves a leader, not lip service from a former morning 
TV host.
  Republicans may have buried this vote today, but in January, a new 
Congress answers to the American people.
  Pete Hegseth will answer to it as well.

                          ____________________
```
