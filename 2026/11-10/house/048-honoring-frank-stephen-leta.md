# HONORING FRANK STEPHEN LETA

**Speaking:** Onder, Robert F. (O000177) [R-MO]

```
                              {time}  1840
                      HONORING FRANK STEPHEN LETA

  (Mr. ONDER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. ONDER. Mr. Speaker, today, I honor the extraordinary life of my 
friend Frank Stephen Leta, who passed away on August 5 at the age of 
95.
  The son of Italian immigrants, Frank served our country in the Air 
Force. He married the love of his life, Toni, at Corpus Christi 
Catholic Church in St. Louis in 1952, a marriage that would span 74 
years. Through hard work, he had success in selling sewing machines.
  In 1965, Frank decided to take a chance on a small and relatively 
unknown Japanese motorcycle company named Honda and later became the 
first Honda automotive dealer in the Midwest. His business grew into 
Frank Leta Automotive Group which today operates five dealerships 
throughout Missouri.

[[Page H6137]]

  Frank's business accomplishments were impressive, but it is his 
generosity, faith, and dedication to his family that we remember most. 
Frank was the father of 3, grandfather to 12, and great-grandfather to 
23. He is deeply missed by his family, friends, and fellow parishioners 
at St. Gianna Catholic Church, of which I am one.
  May Frank's soul, and the souls of all the faithfully departed, 
through the mercy of God rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
