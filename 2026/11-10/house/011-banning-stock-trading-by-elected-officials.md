# BANNING STOCK TRADING BY ELECTED OFFICIALS

**Speaking:** Magaziner, Seth (M001223) [D-RI]

```
                              {time}  1030
               BANNING STOCK TRADING BY ELECTED OFFICIALS

  (Mr. Magaziner of Rhode Island was recognized to address the House 
for 5 minutes.)
  Mr. MAGAZINER. Mr. Speaker, last week, the American people spoke loud 
and clear. They want an end to the corruption that has ruined 
Washington.
  We have a mandate for change, and that starts with finally, at long 
last, banning Members of Congress and the President from trading 
stocks. The American people know that when elected officials are 
allowed to trade off of inside information, when they get rich off of 
their positions, they aren't going to do what is best for the country.
  The time has come. Our next Speaker, Hakeem Jeffries, has committed 
to putting it on the floor next year, as has Leader Schumer in the 
Senate.
  More than 100 candidates for Congress ran television ads calling for 
a congressional stock trading ban in the last election. Most 
importantly, the American people are demanding that we do this. The 
time has come.
  This bill must be strong. It must fully ban buying, owning, and 
selling individual stocks altogether, and it must include the President 
of the United States, whose net worth has grown by $3 billion in the 
last 2 years and who has traded more stocks than every Member of 
Congress combined.
  We have a good bill ready to go to end this practice once and for 
all, a bill that I worked on last year with Ranking Member  Joe 
Morelle, Representatives Ocasio-Cortez and Jayapal, and our Republican 
partners Chip Roy and Brian Fitzpatrick, among many others. Under the 
leadership of Speaker Jeffries, we will be ready to move swiftly and 
deliver.
  Banning stock trading in Congress and the White House must be part of 
a larger effort to root out corruption here in Washington. The 
corruption leads politicians to turn a blind eye at corporate greed 
that drives up prices and makes life unaffordable. The American people 
have waited long enough. It is time.


                         Priorities in Congress

  Mr. MAGAZINER. Mr. Speaker, last week, the American people gave us an 
assignment. They want change. They are tired of a Washington that works 
for the wealthy and well-connected, while everyone else falls further 
behind.
  They told us exactly what they want. They want access to healthcare 
that they can actually afford. They want to heat their homes this 
winter and put food on their table free of the burden of the 
President's tariffs. They want to keep our country out of unnecessary 
wars, like the war in Iran that is driving up gas prices. They also 
want an end to the corruption that rigs the system in favor of the 
wealthy and big corporations at the expense of everybody else.

[[Page H6119]]

  More than anything else, they want us to be bold. They don't want 
messaging bills or half measures. They don't just want us to reverse 
the bad Trump policies from the last 2 years and call it a day. The 
American people want and deserve something better.
  In the upcoming 120th Congress, let's commit to doing big things. 
Let's take on the entrenched interests so that no child goes to bed 
hungry, no one is denied access to the lifesaving care they need, and 
no President or Congressman is allowed to profit off of their positions 
in office.
  Let's build a country where everyone who is willing to work hard can 
build a good life for themselves and where the middle class and our 
standard of living in America are once again the envy of the world. 
Let's build a country that is not just a playground for billionaires, 
but a land of opportunity for everyone. This government, this country, 
belongs to the people, and the people are about to get it back.

                          ____________________
```
