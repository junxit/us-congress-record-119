# WELCOMING BYRON "MICHAEL" THOMAS, JR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                WELCOMING BYRON ``MICHAEL'' THOMAS, JR.

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, on October 6, 2026, 
Lexington County, South Carolina welcomed the birth of Byron 
``Michael'' Thomas, Jr., of Cayce.
  Baby Thomas is the newest addition to the family of Byron and Rebecca 
Thomas and sister Mary Blythe, who lovingly arrived into the world in 
February of last year.
  Born at 9:26 p.m., weighing 8 pounds, and measuring 19 inches high, 
young Michael enters a loving home with extended family and friends, 
including his proud grandparents Jeff and Vicky Thomas, Lana Thomas, 
and Bruce and Nancy Pope.
  I am grateful for his father Byron, who is a dedicated, longtime 
staff member in the West Columbia congressional office serving as 
director of outreach and simultaneously as a city of Cayce councilman.
  Byron ``Michael'' Thomas, Jr., is blessed to be born into a home that 
will foster and guide him to a bright and fulfilling future.
  On behalf of my wife, Roxanne, and our family and staff and my son, 
the Governor-elect of South Carolina Alan Wilson, I extend the warmest 
congratulations to Byron, Rebecca, and Mary Blythe for the arrival of 
this blessing.
  I am grateful for the courageous service of Secretary of War Pete 
Hegseth to defeat Tehran which chants: Death to Israel, death to 
America. We must win.

                          ____________________
```
