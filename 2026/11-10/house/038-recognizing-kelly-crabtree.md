# RECOGNIZING KELLY CRABTREE

**Speaking:** Landsman, Greg (L000601) [D-OH]

```
                       RECOGNIZING KELLY CRABTREE

  (Mr. LANDSMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kelly Crabtree, 
a constituent who proudly represented southwest Ohio at the Special 
Olympics USA Games this year.
  It was Kelly's first time competing on a national stage. She joined 
just three other Special Olympic swimmers from Ohio to compete against 
athletes from all 50 States. She swam the 50-meter breaststroke and the 
100-meter freestyle. After qualifying for the finals in both events, 
Kelly secured a fourth place finish in the 100-meter freestyle and a 
second place finish in the 50-meter breaststroke, bringing home silver 
for Ohio.
  Beyond the competition, Kelly said, ``My favorite part about the 
games was meeting other people from different States.''
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Kelly on this incredible achievement. Her 
hard work and dedication have made southwest Ohio very proud.
```
