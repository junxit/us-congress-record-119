# END HUNGER NOW

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
                             END HUNGER NOW

  (Mr. McGOVERN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, I recently hosted an affordability 
roundtable in my hometown of Worcester to hear directly from 
constituents who are doing everything right but are still getting 
squeezed by the Trump administration's reckless economic policies that 
favor the well-off and the well-connected over working people.
  Just yesterday, the Trump administration doubled down on its 
heartlessness with the announcement of an intentional policy change to 
kick another 6.3 million Americans off of nutrition assistance. Let's 
remember that is on top of the more than 5.5 million people who have 
already been kicked off of SNAP by Republicans' big, ugly bill, and the 
worst of the cuts haven't even hit yet.
  The cruelest part of all of this is it is children who will be hurt 
the most. Tens of thousands of children will lose free and reduced-
price meals, summer meal benefits, and even WIC due to nothing more 
than onerous paperwork burdens.
  Last week, the American people made their verdict on Trump very 
clear. I look forward to fighting back against this unconscionable 
policy. Let us all come together and end hunger now.

                          ____________________
```
