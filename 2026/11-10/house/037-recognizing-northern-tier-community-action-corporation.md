# RECOGNIZING NORTHERN TIER COMMUNITY ACTION CORPORATION

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
         RECOGNIZING NORTHERN TIER COMMUNITY ACTION CORPORATION

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the Northern Tier Community Action Corporation, which celebrated its 
60th anniversary last month.
  For decades, this nonprofit has served men, women, and children 
across northern Pennsylvania and has helped countless people start 
their journey toward achieving the American Dream.
  Mr. Speaker, this organization covers more than 3,000 square miles, 
and this year, their staff assisted almost 3,000 people. I applaud them 
for this historic achievement.
  That is thousands of people who have access to new opportunities and 
thousands of Pennsylvanians who have achieved their goals. As a senior 
member of the House Education and Workforce Committee, I applaud the 
group for connecting men and women with employment and ensuring 
children have the resources necessary to thrive in school.
  This group's dedication and expertise will strengthen our local 
communities for years to come.

[[Page H6135]]

  Mr. Speaker, I thank Northern Tier Community Action Corporation for 
their commitment to the region, and I congratulate them on this 
milestone.

                          ____________________
```
