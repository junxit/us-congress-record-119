# IMPEACH SECRETARY HEGSETH

**Speaking:** Jacobs, Sara (J000305) [D-CA]

```
                       IMPEACH SECRETARY HEGSETH

  (Ms. JACOBS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. JACOBS. Mr. Speaker, I am so proud to represent San Diego, the 
biggest military community in the country.
  It is my community, my neighbors and friends, who have been shipped 
off to fight Secretary Hegseth's reckless war in Iran, so I take it 
personally when our leaders are cavalier with our servicemembers' 
lives.
  I take it personally when our leaders ask our servicemembers to take 
illegal action in Iran, Venezuela, and Yemen.
  I took it personally when my constituents aboard the USS Abraham 
Lincoln spent 10 months at sea, sometimes without enough food to eat, 
hot water to take a shower, and sufficient access to healthcare.
  It is my constituents who are still there fighting this war, and what 
has it all been for? We are not any safer or more secure.
  Look at what has been done in our name: a girls' elementary school 
bombed in Minab and hundreds of people murdered in boats in the 
Caribbean and the eastern Pacific.
  This isn't strength. It is recklessness. It is illegal. Someone 
should be held accountable. We should start with Secretary Pete 
Hegseth.
  Mr. Speaker, my vote today to impeach him isn't political. It is 
personal, and it is for the servicemembers who I represent.

                          ____________________
```
