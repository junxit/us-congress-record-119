# CAPITOL THEATRE CELEBRATES 100 YEAR ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
            CAPITOL THEATRE CELEBRATES 100 YEAR ANNIVERSARY

  (Mr. Latimer of New York was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Capitol Theatre 
in Port Chester. They are celebrating their 100th anniversary this 
year.
  Over the years, they have drawn thousands of visitors each year, and 
the Capitol Theatre has been a cornerstone of Port Chester's cultural 
identity for generations and their downtown rebirth in the last decade.
  The Capitol Theatre has welcomed incredible performers in its 
centurylong history, including the Grateful Dead, Pink Floyd, David 
Bowie, Janis Joplin, The Rolling Stones, and countless others.
  The theatre's #TheCapCares community initiatives have supported local 
nonprofit organizations, volunteer opportunities, and charitable 
partnerships, extending its impact far beyond pure entertainment.
  The Capitol Theatre remains a gathering place for music, culture, and 
community to join together and to create lasting memories for all. Here 
is to the next 100 years.


                   Celebrating Albanian Independence

  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, on November 28, 1912, Albanians declared 
their freedom from the Ottoman Empire.
  In the century since, Albania and its people have persevered through 
two world wars, Communist rule, and uncertainty of the post-Soviet era.
  Through this adversity, they still demonstrated self-determination in 
preserving their identity and their culture. It is a strength that 
continues to sustain Albania through present-day challenges while 
inspiring deep pride in its vibrant heritage.
  At home, I am proud to represent the vibrant Albanian-American 
community across Westchester and the Bronx. In Congress, serving on the 
House Foreign Affairs Committee, I will continue working to strengthen 
the bond between our two nations and to honor our strong NATO 
alliances.
  ``Happy Independence Day''; ``Gezuar Diten e Pavaresise.'' That is 
the best I can do. Congratulations.


             Honoring the Boys & Girls Club of New Rochelle

  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor the valuable work of 
the Boys & Girls Club of New Rochelle.
  With two clubhouses and a dozen partner schools across southern 
Westchester, the Boys & Girls Club of New Rochelle positively impacts 
more than 7,000 students across our communities.
  In New York, over 300,000 students are left unattended every day 
between 3 p.m. and 6 p.m. The Boys & Girls Club's fantastic staff and 
volunteers make the most of this critical time by providing students 
with educational after-school programs, electives, and workplace 
training.
  These programs reinforce important classroom lessons, give students a 
healthy space to express themselves, and provide a safe place to go 
outside of school for those who might not otherwise have it.
  I commend the work that the Boys & Girls Club of New Rochelle has 
accomplished and look forward to witnessing the positive change its 
members will make on the world to come in the years ahead.
```
