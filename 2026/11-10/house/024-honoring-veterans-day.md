# HONORING VETERANS DAY

**Speaking:** Whitesides, George (W000830) [D-CA]

```
                         HONORING VETERANS DAY

  (Mr. WHITESIDES asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WHITESIDES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Veterans Day 
and to honor the veterans who have served our country and the 
sacrifices they and their families have made.
  Our responsibility to those who have served does not end when they 
take off the uniform. We must make sure that they have the healthcare, 
the housing,

[[Page H6122]]

and the support they need when they come home.
  I see that commitment firsthand across my district, from the new VA 
clinic in Lancaster to groups like Blue Star Mothers, our VFW and 
American Legion posts, Coffee4Vets, Vets 4 Veterans, and the many 
veterans service organizations that support those who have served.
  In Congress, I will keep fighting to deliver on our promise by 
demanding House leadership pass the Major Richard Star Act and get it 
signed into law, ensuring that combat-injured veterans receive the full 
retirement benefits that they have earned.
  I thank all those who have served. They deserve more than our 
gratitude. They deserve our continued commitment.

                          ____________________
```
