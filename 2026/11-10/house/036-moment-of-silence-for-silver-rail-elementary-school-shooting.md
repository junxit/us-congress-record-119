# MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR SILVER RAIL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL SHOOTING

**Speaking:** Bynum, Janelle S. (B001326) [D-OR]

```
      MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR SILVER RAIL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL SHOOTING

  (Ms. BYNUM asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. BYNUM. Mr. Speaker, it has been almost a month since we lost our 
babies in the Silver Rail Elementary School shooting in Bend.
  While the news outlets have since packed up and gone home, our 
community is still grieving and holding on tightly to the memories of 
our babies that we lost.
  Thanksgiving is just around the corner, and our families are staring 
down the holiday outfits that won't be worn, the pieces of pie that 
will go untouched, and the place cards beside the chairs that won't be 
filled.
  My heart is with our families, our students, our educators, and all 
of Oregon.
  To our babies that we lost and their families, I am with you. 
Everyone is with you.
  I know we can't keep saying good-bye to our children like this. Our 
babies deserve to feel safe at school, at home, and in our communities. 
They deserve better, Mr. Speaker. I promise to continue to fight to 
protect all of America's children.
  Mr. Speaker, in this Chamber I ask that you join me in a moment of 
silence to remember everyone who was lost.
  Let us keep watch over our babies.

                          ____________________
```
