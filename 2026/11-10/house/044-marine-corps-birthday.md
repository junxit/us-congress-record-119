# MARINE CORPS BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                         MARINE CORPS BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I wish the United States 
Marine Corps a very happy 251st birthday. They have been answering our 
Nation's call for over two-and-a-half centuries.
  Eastern North Carolina is extremely proud of Marine Corps Air Station 
Cherry Point, Marine Corps Air Station New River, and Marine Corps Base 
Camp Lejeune, as well as the many generations of marines and their 
families who have lived there.
  I thank all marines, past and present, for their courage, dedication, 
and service to our country. Happy birthday to the United States Marine 
Corps. Semper fi.

                          ____________________
```
