# RECOGNIZING DR. WALTER BOYNTON

**Speaking:** Elfreth, Sarah (E000301) [D-MD]

```
                     RECOGNIZING DR. WALTER BOYNTON

  (Ms. ELFRETH asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. ELFRETH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of an award-winning 
environmentalist and Chesapeake Bay champion, Dr. Walter Boynton.
  For over 50 years, Dr. Boynton's research has been foundational to 
our scientific knowledge of the bay and the policies that will preserve 
its watershed.
  In 1983, Dr. Boynton helped design, implement, and institutionalize 
the Chesapeake Bay Monitoring Program, widely considered one of the 
best in the world, which still tracks key statistics for the bay today.
  Dr. Boynton's research team was also the first to identify excess 
nutrients as the cause of death for underwater grasses and their impact 
on keystone species in the greater bay.
  Dr. Boynton's work has immensely impacted our beloved watershed and 
watersheds worldwide. The Chesapeake Bay is large and complex. It is 
thanks to his work and researchers like him that we will be able to 
protect this national treasure and unique ecosystem for generations to 
come.
```
