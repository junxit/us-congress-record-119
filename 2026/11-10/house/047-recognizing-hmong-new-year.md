# RECOGNIZING HMONG NEW YEAR

**Speaking:** Grothman, Glenn (G000576) [R-WI]

```
                       RECOGNIZING HMONG NEW YEAR

  (Mr. GROTHMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GROTHMAN. Mr. Speaker, over the weekend I, one more time, had an 
enjoyable time attending the new year's ceremony of the Hmong community 
in Appleton, Wisconsin. I think the week before that I attended a 
similar ceremony in Oshkosh, and in 2 more weeks I will attend a 
ceremony in Sheboygan, Wisconsin.
  Wisconsin is the home of the third largest Hmong population in the 
country, and I am proud to represent the Hmong.
  The Hmong, of course, came to the United States after they helped the 
United States in the war against communism, which we fought in Vietnam.
  They and their children have grown throughout Wisconsin and are an 
economic boon to our community, as well as providing a strong moral 
example to all Americans as you look at their family values, which are 
clearly greater than the native born.
  In any event, I would like to take this opportunity to say happy new 
year to all the Hmong in the Sixth Congressional District.

                          ____________________
```
