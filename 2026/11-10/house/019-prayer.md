# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty Father, whose command is over all and whose love never 
fails, we pray Your blessing on the United States Marine Corps this day 
as they celebrate their 251st birthday. O Lord, You have been their 
rock. Having trained their hands for war and their fingers for battle, 
You have been steadfast in Your love for them, their fortress, their 
stronghold, and their deliverer.
  Wherever these brave men and women find themselves today, in the air, 
on land and sea, deployed in the Middle East and the Indo-Pacific, 
standing watch at American embassies, and supporting regional security 
across the globe, be their shield and sure defense.
  Guard their families and loved ones in their absence. Preserve the 
bond of their love that it will sustain them in these days.
  May each of us give thanks for the sacrifices these marines make each 
day so that we may enjoy the freedoms they defend. Into Your care we 
commend these soldiers of the sea, these few and proud men and women.
  May they be assured of Your faithfulness even as they remain semper 
fidelis, always faithful, to You and to these United States.
  In Your strong name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
