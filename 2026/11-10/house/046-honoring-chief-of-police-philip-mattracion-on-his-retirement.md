# HONORING CHIEF OF POLICE PHILIP MATTRACION ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Riley, Josh (R000622) [D-NY]

```
      HONORING CHIEF OF POLICE PHILIP MATTRACION ON HIS RETIREMENT

  (Mr. RILEY of New York asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. RILEY of New York. Mr. Speaker, Ellenville is a small village 
nestled in the valley between the Catskills and the Gunks. It is home 
to the Blueberry Festival, the Borscht Belt festival, and my wife's 
favorite breakfast sandwiches at Morning Sunshine. But what really 
makes Ellenville special is its people. People like Ellenville Police 
Chief Philip Mattracion, who is retiring from the Ellenville Police 
Department after a distinguished 40-year career.
  Service and patriotism run deep in Chief Mattracion's family. The 
grandson of a World War II veteran who put his life on the line to 
defend America, the son of a Navy medic killed in action in Vietnam, 
Chief Mattracion learned at a young age what it means to serve your 
community and your country.
  That is what he has been doing for his entire life. He rose through 
the ranks from narcotics detective to police chief, a role in which he 
has served with honor and distinction for the past 23 years.
  That would be a life full of service for most, but Chief Mattracion 
has always gone above and beyond, serving also as president of the 
Ellenville School Board.
  During my time with Chief Mattracion, whether at Underground Coffee 
or in the halls of Ellenville's schools, the thing that shines through 
most clearly is how much he loves the community he serves, admires the 
police officers he leads, and cares for the students wearing Blue 
Devils blue.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Chief Mattracion on his retirement and 
wish him all the very best in his next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
