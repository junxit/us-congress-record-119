# THE VOTERS HAVE SPOKEN

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
                         THE VOTERS HAVE SPOKEN

  (Mr. Walkinshaw of Virginia was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Madam Speaker, 14 months ago, I stood in this well 
and took the oath of office. In my first remarks to this House, I 
warned that my community stood on the leading edge of the damage the 
Trump administration's agenda would do to the country. Federal workers 
in my district were being fired by a chainsaw-wielding billionaire, and 
families were already paying more for groceries and healthcare.
  I asked this Chamber to take that warning seriously. My Republican 
colleagues booed, jeered, and then went back to their business, cutting 
healthcare and funding child detention.
  Since then, tariffs have shown up at every checkout counter in 
America. DOGE cuts have closed offices and delayed services that folks 
across the Nation rely on. In February, the President took us into war 
with Iran that Congress never authorized. Our servicemembers have paid 
for that decision with their lives, and American families are paying at 
the pump.
  Last Tuesday, voters rendered their verdict, and it was unmistakable. 
They told us to end the tariffs. They told us to end the war. They told 
us to reverse the healthcare cuts. They told us to end the efforts to 
traumatize our VA nurses and park rangers.
  In January, the new Congress will get to work. Democrats are ready to 
reverse the cuts and repeal the tariffs that act as a tax on every 
household, to bring our servicemembers home, and to reduce prices and 
increase paychecks.
  Madam Speaker, the American people are tired of watching insiders 
cash in. Four days before his inauguration, a foreign leader bought 
nearly one-half of the President's family's crypto company for $500 
million. Six days before the administration closed a deal that could 
put up to $1.6 billion of American taxpayer financing behind a tungsten 
mine in Kazakhstan, the President's sons bought a stake in it. The no-
bid contract for the Reflecting Pool went to the President's donor, and 
the pool turned green.
  The price of beef skyrockets, and Republicans say: Pick a different 
protein. The American people spent this year picking different proteins 
while the well-connected lined up at the trough. For working families, 
this Congress has meant higher prices and ICE-inflicted chaos in their 
communities. For those close to the administration, it has been a gold 
rush.
  The voters have now told this Congress that it fell far short. The 
next Congress can, must, and will do better.

                          ____________________
```
