# RECOGNIZING INTERNATIONAL CJD AWARENESS DAY

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
              RECOGNIZING INTERNATIONAL CJD AWARENESS DAY

  (Mr. VALADAO asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, this Thursday is International Creutzfeldt-
Jakob Disease, or CJD, Awareness Day.
  I will take a moment to raise awareness about this rare brain 
disease. CJD causes a rapid decline in a person's cognitive health and 
often leads to death within a few months or 1 year following the onset 
of symptoms. While there are approximately 600 new cases in the United 
States each year, this condition sadly does not have a cure.
  In 2018, I was personally impacted by the loss of a dear friend and 
mentor, the former mayor of Bakersfield and founder of Hall Ambulance 
Service, Harvey L. Hall.
  Harvey Hall was a compassionate man with great love for life, his 
family, the Central Valley, and the Nation. He was also a beloved 
member of our community and the longest-serving mayor in Bakersfield 
history.
  Sadly, Mayor Hall lost his life just 2 weeks after his CJD diagnosis. 
While we don't have a cure for this disease, raising awareness for CJD 
brings us closer to breakthroughs in treatments, faster diagnostics, 
and vital support that caregivers need.
  Together, we can bring hope to those living with this devastating 
disease and the families who have lost loved ones to CJD.

                          ____________________
```
