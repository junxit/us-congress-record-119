# REMEMBERING BRANDON EICH

**Speaking:** Biggs, Sheri (B001325) [R-SC]

```
                        REMEMBERING BRANDON EICH

  (Mrs. Biggs of South Carolina was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Pickens Police Department Reserve Officer Brandon Eich. He led a life 
of service, sacrifice, and dedication.
  Brandon was more than a constituent. He was a friend and a vibrant, 
energetic leader in our community with a bold vision. My heart 
continues to grieve for his sweet wife, Lisa, and his children and 
grandchildren.
  South Carolina's Third District is full of passionate and selfless 
first responders who answer the call when most wouldn't dare. Those who 
had the pleasure of knowing Brandon know that he exemplified service 
and had a deep passion for supporting law enforcement.
  From providing security at local football games to leading the 
NextGen youth program to his work with the Pickens Police Department 
and The Stillwater Group, which he founded, his impact in our community 
will be felt for years to come.
  I am reminded of one of the verses read at his celebration of life, 
Psalms 23:1-3: ``The Lord is my shepherd, I shall not want. He maketh 
me to lie down in green pastures; he leadeth me beside the still 
waters.''

  God bless the memory of Brandon Eich and his sweet family.


                 Celebrating WCRS Radio and Anne Eller

  Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
a notable celebration of accomplishment in public media. This year, 
WCRS Radio, known as the Voice of Greenwood, celebrated 85 years of 
community service and broadcasting over the airwaves.
  Behind that voice is the charismatic Ms. Anne Eller and her trusty 
sidekick, Penny.
  Through her work, she entertains, advocates, and connects the people 
of Greenwood to the leaders who represent them and provides informative 
programming.
  Anne has spent decades covering local, State, and national issues, 
playing a pivotal role in communicating the issues facing our State and 
Nation.
  She has received numerous STAR Awards, Radio Show of the Year, and 
commands the longest running and award-winning program in Greenwood, 
``Meet Me at the Diner.''
  Beyond her career in radio, Anne has dedicated much of her time to 
community service, including food drives and holiday toy drives for 
local schools.
  I thank my friend, Anne Eller, at WCRS Radio for her dedication and 
service to the people of Greenwood.


                       Recognizing Mary Franklin

  Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor a wonderful constituent, Mrs. Mary Franklin, for her 105th 
birthday.
  Mrs. Mary has led an extraordinary and inspiring life. Raised during 
the Great Depression, she graduated high school at just 15 years old, 
beginning her career at 16.
  She raised two wonderful children, Gayle and Julius Jr. From there, 
her family grew, blessing Mrs. Mary with four grandchildren and seven 
great-grandchildren.
  Beyond her dedication to family and career, Mrs. Mary shares her 
musical talent by playing piano and singing in the choir at St. John 
United Methodist Church in Sumter, South Carolina. Even at 105 years 
old, she still regularly harmonizes with her friends and family, a 
beautiful testament to the joyful spirit she carries with her.
  On November 16, Mrs. Mary will be surrounded by family and friends as 
they celebrate 105 years of life filled with love, service, and 
cherished memories. Happy 105th birthday, Mrs. Mary Franklin.

                          ____________________
```
