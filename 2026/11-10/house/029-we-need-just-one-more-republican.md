# WE NEED JUST ONE MORE REPUBLICAN

**Speaking:** Garcia, Sylvia R. (G000587) [D-TX]

```
                              {time}  1210
                    WE NEED JUST ONE MORE REPUBLICAN

  (Ms. GARCIA of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. GARCIA of Texas. Mr. Speaker, we need just one Republican to sign 
my discharge petition to force a vote on the American Dream and Promise 
Act, just one more. We have 217 signatures. We need one more.
  Dreamers, temporary protected status holders, and deferred enforced 
departure holders are having their lives turned upside-down by Trump's 
mass deportation machine. It is cruel and unjust, but Congress can do 
something about it, and they can do it now.
  Three of my Republican colleagues have already signed. They recognize 
this is commonsense legislation that the American people support. The 
majority of Americans support the Dream Act. Even Donald Trump himself 
admitted his deportation policies have been too tough. Now he says he 
is willing to work with Democrats on immigration reform.
  Mr. President, here is your chance. Tell one House Republican to sign 
our discharge petition. Let's bring this bill to a vote and work 
together to get started on immigration reform.

                          ____________________
```
