                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. CLOUD:
       H.R. 10971. A bill to amend title 46, United States Code, 
     to apply an exemption to certain floating dry docks in 
     Ingleside, Texas; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.
           By Mr. CLOUD:
       H.R. 10972. A bill to require the Secretary of Defense to 
     carry out an operational pilot program under the Hybrid Space 
     Architecture initiative to evaluate the use of commercially 
     available orbital data center services and space-based cloud 
     computing capabilities relevant to national security space 
     and joint mission requirements, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. CLOUD:
       H.R. 10973. A bill to designate the community-based 
     outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in 
     Victoria, Texas, as the ``Master Sergeant Roy P. Benavidez VA 
     Clinic''; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Ms. UNDERWOOD:
       H.R. 10974. A bill to rescind appropriations provided to 
     U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement and U.S. Customs and 
     Border Protection by the One, Big, Beautiful Bill Act, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary, and in 
     addition to the Committees on Ways and Means, and Homeland 
     Security, for a period to be subsequently determined by the 
     Speaker, in each case for consideration of such provisions as 
     fall within the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Ms. BONAMICI (for herself, Mr. Garcia of Illinois, 
             Ms. Salinas, Ms. Tlaib, Ms. Pettersen, Ms. Jayapal, 
             Mr. Huffman, Mr. Casar, and Mr. Vargas):

     include retailers of manufactured or modular homes in the 
     definition of mortgage originator, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Financial Services.
           By Mr. BURCHETT:
       H.R. 10976. A bill to establish a limitation on the use of 
     Federal funds for the construction and operation of data

       H.R. 10978. A bill to amend the Small Business Act to

     Business.
           By Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas (for herself, Mr. Joyce of 
             Ohio, Ms. Lofgren, Mr. Turner of Ohio, and Ms.

       H.R. 10980. A bill to amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster 
     Relief and Emergency Assistance Act with respect to the 
     implementation of building codes, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER (for himself and Mr. Lawler):
       H.R. 10981. A bill to direct the Secretary of Commerce to

     intelligence models, and for other purposes; to the Committee

     Intelligence (Permanent Select), for a period to be 
     subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 10982. A bill to prohibit the availability of Federal

           By Mr. VINDMAN:

           By Mr. MASSIE:
       H. Res. 1655. A resolution impeaching Peter Brian Hegseth,

           By Mrs. LUNA (for herself and Mr. Burchett):
       H. Res. 1656. A resolution providing for consideration of

     prohibit Members of Congress, the President, Vice President, 
     and their spouses and dependents from owning or trading 
     stocks, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Rules.
