# RECOGNIZING ELLEN STEIN

**Speaking:** Hurd, Jeff (H001100) [R-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING ELLEN STEIN

  (Mr. HURD of Colorado asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ellen 
Stein as she steps away from the opinion pages of The Durango Herald 
and The Journal.
  Ellen first joined The Durango Herald in 2016 as an editorial writer 
working alongside longtime editorial page editor Bill Roberts. She 
returned to the job in September 2024 calling it the only one she ever 
would have gone back to.
  Community newspapers help a region think through the issues that 
shape daily life. Ellen brought seriousness, curiosity, and a strong 
sense of responsibility to that work.
  One of her greatest contributions was making room for other voices. 
She welcomed community members onto the page, including people who 
disagreed with her and listened when they challenged her views. That 
openness strengthened the conversation and earned readers' trust.
  A strong community depends on people being willing to share their 
ideas and on someone willing to make room for them.
  I thank Ellen for her years of service to southwest Colorado and wish 
her the very best in the next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
