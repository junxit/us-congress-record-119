# END WAR IN IRAN

**Speaking:** Barragan, Nanette Diaz (B001300) [D-CA]

```
                            END WAR IN IRAN

  (Ms. Barragan of California was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Ms. BARRAGAN. Mr. Speaker, on Sunday, President Trump went on ``60 
Minutes.'' When he spoke about the war with Iran, he said: ``You know, 
when you call it a war, it's not a war. If you look at what's happened 
with Iran in the last 6 months, 5 months, 6 months, we haven't lost one 
soldier.''
  Mr. Speaker, the President's statement is wrong, dead wrong.
  Mr. Speaker, 23 American servicemembers have died because of 
President Trump's war of choice with Iran, and at least 866 have been 
wounded. These are not just numbers. These are Americans who put on a 
uniform, served their country, and had families waiting for them to 
come home.
  Sergeant Declan Coady was a 20-year-old college student at Drake 
University and an Eagle Scout. He could have avoided deployment because 
he was enrolled in ROTC, but he chose to serve overseas. His sister 
remembered him as the most amazing brother and son their family could 
have asked for.
  Private First Class Isabella Gonzales was just 19 years old when she 
was killed in July. She graduated from high school only last year, and 
her friends remembered her as someone who loved dancing and made people 
laugh. They were counting down the days until she could come home to 
visit. Instead, they gathered to mourn her.
  There are so many others. Let's honor them:
  Captain Cody Khork
  Sergeant First Class Noah Tietjens
  Master Sergeant Class Nicole Amor
  Major Jeffrey O'Brien
  Chief Warrant Officer Robert Marzan
  Staff Sergeant Benjamin Pennington
  Major John Klinner
  Major Ariana Savino
  Technical Sergeant Ashley Pruitt
  Captain Seth Koval
  Captain Curtis Angst
  Master Sergeant Tyler Simmons
  Major Sorffly Davius
  Captain Gabriel Edwards
  Captain Tyler James Feehan
  Staff Sergeant Angel Sarah Rampersad
  Staff Sergeant Michael Swinton
  Petty Officer First Class Sarai Ramirez-Galvan
  Major Bianca Wilkerson
  Mr. Speaker, these were 21 lives cut short--21.
  Two Air Force servicemembers also died in October in nonhostile 
incidents while supporting Iran war operations. The Defense Department 
still has not publicly released their names.
  President Trump cannot erase the sacrifices of our troops just 
because it doesn't fit his narrative of his war, and the damage from 
this war goes beyond the loss of lives.
  What else doesn't fit his narrative is that American families 
continue to pay for his war every day. Diesel prices have climbed above 
$6 a gallon nationwide. Farmers are paying more to grow and harvest our 
food. Truckers are paying more to deliver that food and countless other 
goods that Americans rely on every day. Those higher costs are being 
passed on to families across the country.
  Gas prices are up, too. Trump and Pete Hegseth's war of choice has 
cost the average household $856 on gas and

[[Page H6118]]

more than $2,000 overall. Families are paying more just to get to work, 
take their kids to school, and live their lives. Meanwhile, Trump's oil 
and gas stocks have grown as much as $35.8 million, and Big Oil is 
raking in record profits.
  Mr. Speaker, our troops have sacrificed enough. American families 
have paid enough. President Trump needs to stop pretending this war has 
no cost, take responsibility, and bring our troops home.
  House Democrats will continue to fight to end his reckless war of 
choice in Iran and to bring down the cost of groceries and gas.
  Let's end this war.

                          ____________________
```
