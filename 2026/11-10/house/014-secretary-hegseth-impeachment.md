# SECRETARY HEGSETH IMPEACHMENT

**Speaking:** Houlahan, Chrissy (H001085) [D-PA]

```
                     SECRETARY HEGSETH IMPEACHMENT

  (Ms. Houlahan of Pennsylvania was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Ms. HOULAHAN. Madam Speaker, I deliver this speech today because 
Secretary Hegseth has earned it. Dare I say, he merits it.
  I rise today in support of the Articles of Impeachment that have been 
brought against Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth by my colleague, 
Congressman   Thomas Massie, because

[[Page H6120]]

since the day he was nominated, I have been clear, Secretary Hegseth is 
not qualified for this job.
  Unfortunately for our Nation and our people, he has literally 
``Signaled'' and doubled down on that incompetence ever since day one.
  Because of Secretary Hegseth, our country is weaker materially and 
morally, and far more isolated than we ever have been as a Nation, as 
an ally, or as a force for good in this world.
  Because of Secretary Hegseth, our allies around the globe have lost 
faith in us, no longer trusting us with vital information or 
intelligence or economic partnerships.
  Because of Secretary Hegseth, our men and women in uniform are 
deprived of responsible, intelligent, and skilled leadership, and more 
than 50 percent of them are indeed considering separating from the 
military early because of his relentless culture wars, misogyny, 
racism, and ideological poisoning.
  Because of Secretary Hegseth, we are all dealing with the 
consequences of a reckless, expensive, and deadly war with no end in 
sight.
  Let's dig in a little bit more on that. It is a war that has us 
paying more at the gas pump and at the grocery store, a war that has 
destroyed 244 military base structures and 81 military aircraft, a war 
that has drained nearly all of our most critical munition stockpiles, 
leaving us vastly more vulnerable to our adversaries.
  It is a war he claims only cost the American taxpayers $47 billion so 
far, yet we all know it has cost us so much more. Specifically, this 
war has left 865 servicemembers wounded and has claimed 23 
servicemembers' lives, people who will never come back to their 
families. Though, in true Hegseth fashion, today, this morning, he 
reported only 18 killed in action during his press performance.
  Instead of explaining himself to the American people, Secretary 
Hegseth dragged his feet, obfuscated, and refused to tell us where our 
dollars are being spent, where our missiles are being fired, and which 
of our servicemembers will not come home from a war that he started.
  He has done so while preening and peacocking all over the world in 
pushup contests and PT stunts, using our servicemembers as props, and 
he has done so while requesting another trillion dollars of taxpayer 
money to fund this military and this Pentagon.
  Hegseth's projection of power is a mirage and one that we, the 
American people, see through. He is not a serious man. He is not a 
capable man. Our Nation deserves a real Secretary of Defense who will 
not be laughed out of every room in the Pentagon, in Brussels, and in 
Congress.
  The absurdity continued with his press conference just this morning 
devoted, clearly, to an audience of one. He wants to pretend that 
calling out his failures, his incompetence, and his zealotry is an 
attack on our men and women in uniform, which is a colossal insult.
  Our troops and their families serve our country with more dignity, 
strength, patriotism, and skill than Secretary Hegseth has ever been 
able to demonstrate.
  I push for his resignation or removal because he has earned it and 
because of my deep reverence for our servicemembers.

                              {time}  1045

  Secretary Hegseth wants to poison this impeachment process with the 
very same partisan hostility that he has injected into our military, 
but we will not allow him to do so. We will keep up this fight because 
he earned it and because it is the right thing for our troops, it is 
the right thing for our military families, and it is the right thing 
for our country.

                          ____________________
```
