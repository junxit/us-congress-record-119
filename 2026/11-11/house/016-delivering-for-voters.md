# DELIVERING FOR VOTERS

**Speaking:** Hoyle, Val T. (H001094) [D-OR]

```
                         DELIVERING FOR VOTERS

  (Ms. HOYLE of Oregon asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. HOYLE of Oregon. Mr. Speaker, I believe that one of the greatest 
acts of treason has been the effort to undermine the faith in our 
electoral system in order to disenfranchise eligible voters in this 
country, but these attacks have failed miserably.
  In Oregon, we vote by mail, and we had a 61-percent voter turnout, 
and almost 2 million ballots were cast. Across the country, 49 percent 
of eligible Americans voted in the midterms, which is the second-
highest rate in a century, just behind the 2018 midterms.
  The President tried to stop the Postal Service from delivering mail 
ballots. He tried to cut public safety funding from States if they 
refused unconstitutional overreach. He pressured Republican statehouses 
to aggressively gerrymander congressional districts so that he could 
stay in power.
  Last week, the American people responded to his authoritarian 
overreach, and now it is time for us to deliver.
  People want to be able to afford gas and groceries. They want us to 
stop engaging in illegal, immoral wars overseas. They want a path to 
the middle class when we have greater income inequality than we have 
ever had.
  It is time that we fight. The American people have made their voices 
heard.

                          ____________________
```
