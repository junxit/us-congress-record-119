**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                   AMERICA GROWING UNDER REPUBLICANS

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, under the progrowth agenda 
of President Donald Trump and House Republicans, led by Speaker   Mike 
Johnson, America grew at an annual rate of 3.4 percent in the third 
quarter, the strongest pace in a year.
  President Trump has an America First agenda delivering, as promised, 
historic levels of investment and faster growth, creating jobs.
  Third quarter achievements are:
  Private investment increased by 9.4 percent.
  Business investment has grown by 13 percent.
  Consumer spending grew by 3.8 percent.
  South Carolina will continue record growth with Governor-elect Alan 
Wilson and Lieutenant Governor Mike Reichenbach, and all other 
statewide State officials are Republicans.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops. Trump is reinstituting peace 
through strength, revealing war criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump, 
mocking Trump, with Putin supporting the regime in Yemen, achieving 
death to Israel, death to America. President Vucic of Serbia has been 
defeated in an election last week, an ally of Putin.
  As a father grateful for four servicemembers, on this Veterans Day, I 
am grateful for all of these men and women who have served our greatest 
country in uniform.
  God bless our troops. God bless our veterans.
```
