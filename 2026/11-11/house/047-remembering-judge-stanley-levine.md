# REMEMBERING JUDGE STANLEY LEVINE

**Speaking:** Stutzman, Marlin A. (S001188) [R-IN]

```
                    REMEMBERING JUDGE STANLEY LEVINE

  (Mr. STUTZMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. STUTZMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Judge Stanley Levine of Fort Wayne, Indiana, who passed away last 
month at the age of 88.
  Judge Levine is remembered in many ways for his service to his 
community and his country. Beginning in 1998, he served 20 years on the 
Allen County Superior Court where he was highly respected for his 
integrity and his quick wit. He also served his country in the Air 
National Guard at the 122nd Fighter Wing in Fort Wayne, Indiana.
  Outside of these accomplishments, Judge Levine gave back to his 
community through his work with Heartland Sings, where he shared his 
love of music and singing.
  Judge Levine's life is an example to all Hoosiers on how to truly 
serve others in everything that we do.
  My prayers go out to Judge Levine's family. May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
