# HONORING THE MEMORY OF WALLACE D. MALONE, JR.

**Speaking:** Palmer, Gary J. (P000609) [R-AL]

```
             HONORING THE MEMORY OF WALLACE D. MALONE, JR.

  (Mr. PALMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. PALMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of my 
friend, Wallace D. Malone, Jr.
  Malone was born August 3, 1936, in Dothan, the son of Wallace D. 
Malone, Sr., and Alice Mae Dee Malone.
  Wallace earned his bachelor's degree in business in 1957 from the 
University of Alabama. He earned an MBA degree in 1959 from the Wharton 
School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania.
  I first met Wallace in the early 1990s when he was chairman and CEO 
of SouthTrust Bank, which he founded. Wallace was a legendary figure in 
banking, building SouthTrust into the largest financial organization in 
Alabama and one of America's Fortune 500 companies.
  His family entered banking over 100 years ago in one office in the 
heart of Dothan, Alabama.
  Wallace was inducted into the Alabama Academy of Honor in 1986. In 
1996, The Birmingham News named Wallace CEO of the Year, and in 2001, 
Wallace was inducted into the Alabama Business Hall of Fame.
  Malone invested his time and treasure in his community and State. He 
served on numerous boards supporting healthcare, business, and numerous 
nonprofits, as well as serving on the Federal Reserve Branch in 
Birmingham. He also supported education through the Malone Family 
Foundation, and he was one of the early and longtime supporters of the 
Alabama Family Alliance, later the Alabama Policy Institute, the think 
tank that I had the honor of leading.
  Wallace was, first and foremost, a man of faith and a family man. He 
and his wife, Ocllo, were married 48 years, parents to 6 children, 17 
grandchildren, and 9 great-grandchildren.
  Wallace was a dear friend and counselor to me and to so many others. 
He was always generous in sharing his insights and wisdom, and I always 
enjoyed my time with him. He was truly one of a kind.

                          ____________________
```
