# SBIC REPORTING MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2026

**Speaking:** Williams, Roger (W000816) [R-TX], Velazquez, Nydia M. (V000081) [D-NY], Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 10353

```
                SBIC REPORTING MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2026

  Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I move to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 10353) to amend the Small Business Investment Act 
of 1958 to require a quarterly report on small business investment 
companies, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The text of the bill is as follows:

                               H.R. 10353

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

       This Act may be cited as the ``SBIC Reporting Modernization 
     Act of 2026''.

     SEC. 2. QUARTERLY SMALL BUSINESS INVESTMENT COMPANY 
                   PERFORMANCE REPORT.

       Section 308(g) of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 
     (15 U.S.C. 687(g)) is amended by adding at the end the 
     following new paragraph:
       ``(4) Quarterly Report.--
       ``(A) In general.--Beginning on the date that is 90 days 
     after the date of the enactment of this paragraph, and 
     quarterly thereafter, the Administrator shall publish on a 
     webpage of the Administration a report on small business 
     investment companies licensed under this title.
       ``(B) Contents.--Each report required by this paragraph 
     shall include, for the period covered by the report, the 
     following:
       ``(i) The information described in paragraph (3)(A).
       ``(ii) The number of applicants approved by the 
     Administrator to proceed to the final licensing phase under 
     section 107.300(b) of title 13, Code of Federal Regulations 
     (or a similar successor regulation).
       ``(iii) The number of applicants received the approval 
     described in clause (ii) and met the requirements of section 
     302(a).
       ``(iv) The dollar value of debentures issued under this 
     title.
       ``(v) The number of jobs created or sustained due to 
     investments made by small business investment companies.''.

  Mr. Speaker, I rise today in strong support of H.R. 10353, the SBIC 
Reporting Modernization Act of 2026, introduced by Representatives 
Finstad and Morrison both from the great State of Minnesota.
  The SBA's Small Business Investment Company program helps American 
small businesses access the capital they need to grow, and it is 
working.
  The SBIC program closed the last fiscal year with a record $600 
billion in combination private capital and SBA leverage.
  When a program is helping deliver that kind of investment, Congress 
needs timely information to understand its performance and ensure it 
continues to serve small businesses effectively.
  Rather than waiting for an annual report, this legislation requires 
the SBA to provide quarterly updates on licensing, capital deployed, 
and the employment landscape. Quarterly reporting means better 
information; better, stronger oversight; and a greater ability to build 
on what is working.
  This bill makes a successful program stronger and ensures Congress 
has the information needed to help in delivering for Main Street 
America.
  I urge my colleagues to support H.R. 10353. Mr. Speaker, I reserve 
the balance of my time.

  Ms. VELAZQUEZ. Mr. Speaker, I yield myself such time as I may

  Mr. Speaker, I rise in support of H.R. 10353, the SBIC Reporting 
Modernization Act of 2026.
  The Small Business Investment Company program is administered by the 
SBA to help increase the flow of private capital to small businesses. 
SBICs combine private funds and funds borrowed with an SBA guarantee to 
provide capital to small businesses that have the potential to generate 
outsized job growth and economic impact.
  SBICs make a meaningful impact on Main Street. In fiscal year 2024, 
they provided approximately $7.2 billion in capital to over 1,000 
businesses.
  Timely information from the SBA is key to ensuring the effective 
operation of the SBIC program. In order for this committee to carry out 
oversight, we need the latest data.
  In fact, the SBA is required by statute to provide a full and 
detailed description of the SBIC program in its annual report to 
Congress. Historically, quarterly updates on the program have been 
published by the SBA.
  Unfortunately, despite my urging, the SBA has not issued a complete 
report since fiscal year 2024.
  This bill will require the SBA to publish quarterly reports. This 
report will provide important information, including data on SBIC 
licenses, the debentures issued under the program, and the jobs created 
and sustained through SBIC investment in small businesses.
  Requiring this report will ensure Congress has the latest data to 
monitor and address challenges in the SBIC program as they arise.
  Mr. Speaker, I applaud Representatives Finstad and Morrison for 
working together on this legislation. I ask all my colleagues to 
support it, and I reserve the balance of my time.
```
