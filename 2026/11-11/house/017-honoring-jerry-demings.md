# HONORING JERRY DEMINGS

**Speaking:** Frost, Maxwell (F000476) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING JERRY DEMINGS

  (Mr. FROST asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Orange County Mayor 
Jerry Demings and his 46 years of public service.
  From becoming Orlando's first Black police chief, then to Orange 
County's first Black sheriff, and then Orange County's first Black 
mayor, Mayor Demings has broken barriers throughout his career.
  One of the most powerful things about public service is that the 
impact does not end when your time in service does. It lives on through 
the lives you have touched and the work that you have done.
  Mayor Demings has helped guide Orange County through some of our most 
difficult moments, but he has also celebrated our greatest 
achievements. It has been a privilege working alongside him, hosting 
townhalls together across the district, and bringing together Federal 
and local dollars to tackle the most pressing challenges facing our 
community and our home.
  Mayor Demings has given 46 years of his life to serving the people of 
our home, and our region is better because of him.

  I am beyond grateful for his friendship and the example he has set 
for all of us as we carry this work forward.
  God bless Mayor Demings, and of course, God bless the Honorable 
Congresswoman Val Demings.
  Mr. Speaker, I wish Mayor Demings all the best in his next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
