# EXTENDING GRATITUDE FOR OUR SERVICEMEMBERS

**Speaking:** Morrison, Kelly (M001234) [D-MN]

```
               EXTENDING GRATITUDE FOR OUR SERVICEMEMBERS

  (Ms. MORRISON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. MORRISON. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize all of the brave 
men and women who put on the uniform to serve our country. I am proud 
to say that Minnesota is home to nearly 300,000 veterans.
  I rise to extend my deepest gratitude to every veteran and 
servicemember who has given so much for our freedom and our democracy. 
Their sacrifice is a reminder of the patriotism, courage, and 
selflessness that defines our Armed Forces.
  We are forever indebted to them for their service.
  I also want to extend my gratitude to every veteran's family. My 
husband is an Army combat veteran, and we both come from long lines of 
military service, so I understand the enormous sacrifice that families 
make in service to our country.
  When servicemembers put their lives on the line, it is our duty and 
our sacred obligation to uphold the contract we made with them and 
ensure that they and their families get the support and care they 
earned through their service.
  A grateful nation honors them every day--and especially today on 
Veterans Day--for their service and their sacrifice.

                          ____________________
```
