# CALLING FOR ENERGY PRICE RELIEF

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                    CALLING FOR ENERGY PRICE RELIEF

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, as we approach the winter months and the 
weather gets colder, millions of Americans will see the cost of heating 
their homes rise amidst the President's war in Iran.
  According to the Joint Economic Committee, the cost of home heating 
oil in New York is estimated to increase by nearly 32 percent this 
winter.
  At the same time, President Trump has repeatedly called for 
eliminating the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program, or LIHEAP, a 
popular program that helps hardworking families afford to heat their 
homes.
  Instead of delivering real price relief for the American people, 
President Trump's war in Iran, his tariffs, and his reckless cuts to 
social safety net programs are decimating family budgets as we descend 
into the winter season.
  Congress cannot leave these families out in the cold. I implore my 
colleagues on both sides of the aisle to deliver real energy price 
relief before families are forced to choose between heating their homes 
and putting food on their table.

                          ____________________
```
