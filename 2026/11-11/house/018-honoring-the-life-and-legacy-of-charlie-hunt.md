# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF CHARLIE HUNT

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF CHARLIE HUNT

  (Mr. BEAN of Florida asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Charlie Hunt of Green Cove Springs, Florida, who went home to 
be with the Lord at the age of 85.
  For more than 35 years, Charlie faithfully served as pastor of Black 
Pond Baptist Church in Clay County, Florida.
  Mr. Speaker, he was more than just a preacher. He was a shepherd, 
mentor, and friend who brought comfort to families, visited the sick, 
and shared the love of Jesus with countless people.
  Charlie leaves behind Mary, his beloved wife of 64 years; daughters 
Susan and Beth; grandchildren Michael Worley, Courtney Hoard, Luke 
Hunt, Jake Childers, Lauren Markey, and Connor Markey; and five great-
grandchildren.
  To Mary and the entire Hunt family, I extend my deepest condolences. 
Charlie fought the good fight, finished the race, and kept the faith.
  Well done, good and faithful servant.

                          ____________________
```
