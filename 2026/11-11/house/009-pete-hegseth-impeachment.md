# PETE HEGSETH IMPEACHMENT

**Speaking:** Quigley, Mike (Q000023) [D-IL]

```
                        PETE HEGSETH IMPEACHMENT

  (Mr. QUIGLEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, the House may have failed to impeach Pete 
Hegseth this week, but the fight isn't over. In 54 days, Democrats will 
have the majority. We will hold Pete Hegseth accountable.
  Mr. Speaker, 21 American servicemembers have lost their lives in

[[Page H6150]]

Trump's war with Iran, a war not authorized by Congress.
  He oversaw more than 200 killings and unlawful military strikes in 
the Caribbean.
  He dismantled safeguards designed to prevent civilian casualties, 
leading to the deaths of 120 Iranian schoolchildren.
  He purged high-ranking military officials because of their race, 
gender, and for daring to disagree with the administration.
  On Veterans Day, our obligation is to protect those who serve. That 
means demanding accountability from those entrusted with their lives 
and refusing to let Pete Hegseth escape responsibility.

                          ____________________
```
