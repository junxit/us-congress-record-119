# END ICE RAIDS

**Speaking:** Rivas, Luz M. (R000620) [D-CA]

```
                             END ICE RAIDS

  (Ms. RIVAS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. RIVAS. Mr. Speaker, last week, the American people sent a 
resounding message that they want lower costs and ICE out of our 
streets.
  How does the Trump administration respond? They responded by ignoring 
the will of the people and naming Stephen Miller, the architect of 
Trump's cruel ICE raids, as White House Chief of Staff.
  For nearly 2 years, Stephen Miller has been the driving force behind 
Trump's ICE raids that have torn apart communities, kidnapped our 
neighbors, and murdered innocent people.
  In the weeks leading up to last week's election, ICE raids in the San 
Fernando Valley and across California increased in an attempt to 
silence people from using their voices. However, our communities 
refused to be silenced, and we sent a clear message that we want ICE 
out of our neighborhoods.
  Under Stephen Miller, ICE is violating the fundamental rights and 
safety of individuals across the San Fernando Valley to carry out 
Donald Trump's anti-immigrant agenda.
  I am calling for Stephen Miller to be fired and for ICE to be 
abolished.

                          ____________________
```
