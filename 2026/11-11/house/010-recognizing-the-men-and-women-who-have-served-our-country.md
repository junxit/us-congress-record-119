# RECOGNIZING THE MEN AND WOMEN WHO HAVE SERVED OUR COUNTRY

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE MEN AND WOMEN WHO HAVE SERVED OUR COUNTRY

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the men and women who served our country to protect our freedom.
  Today, we honor Americans who have served in the Army, Navy, Air 
Force, Marines, Coast Guard, Space Force, National Guard, and Reserves.
  Veterans Day is an opportunity to unite and thank past servicemembers 
for defending our Nation against threats around the world.
  There are more than 15 million veterans in the United States, 
including nearly 700,000 in my home State of Pennsylvania.
  As elected officials, we have a responsibility to meet the needs of 
our veterans and their families, ensuring they succeed during the 
transition to civilian life.
  As a proud Army dad, I know the sacrifices our veterans make, and I 
am honored to stand with them, not just today but every day.
  Mr. Speaker, I take a point of personal privilege to wish my son, 
Logan Thompson, who just retired after 22 years in the Army, a happy 
birthday. Today is his 40th birthday. Happy birthday to Logan.
  Our veterans have given so much out of a deep love for their country, 
and I offer my sincerest gratitude to all of them and their families.
  God bless our military, and God bless the United States.

                          ____________________
```
