# NEW GOVERNOR IN DEEP RED TEXAS

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

```
                     NEW GOVERNOR IN DEEP RED TEXAS

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, deep in the heart of red Texas, a 
miracle has been given birth, and that miracle has caused a female to 
be selected to be the next speaker of the Texas House. It is 
significant, Mr. Speaker, because when I was there to hear the 
announcement, I was in the room where they have the pictures of all of 
the prior speakers of the Texas House, and they have something in 
common: They were all men, and they were all White.
  Mr. Speaker, the next speaker of the Texas House of Representatives 
will be a woman, and she will be a Black woman.
  God bless the United States of America. Deep in the heart of Texas, 
the Governor will be a female, the Lieutenant Governor will be a 
female, and the speaker of the house will be a female.

                          ____________________
```
