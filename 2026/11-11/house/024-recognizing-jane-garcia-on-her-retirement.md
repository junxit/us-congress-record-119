# RECOGNIZING JANE GARCIA ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
               RECOGNIZING JANE GARCIA ON HER RETIREMENT

  (Mr. DeSAULNIER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jane Garcia as 
she retires from her role as the chief executive officer of La Clinica.
  Jane is a friend and a valued member of the community I represent in 
the San Francisco Bay Area.
  Since Jane assumed her role in 1982, La Clinica has provided 
culturally sensitive care to over 86,000 patients in the east bay of 
the San Francisco Bay Area.
  An advocate for accessible healthcare, Jane has served on numerous 
boards and is president of the California Primary Care Association.
  Among the many accolades that Jane's work has earned her, she was 
inducted into the Alameda County Women's Hall of Fame. She has played 
an invaluable role in La Clinica's ability to help marginalized 
communities around the bay area and give them access to quality 
healthcare.
  Please join me in congratulating my friend, Jane Garcia, a valued 
member of our community and a valued American, on her well-earned 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
