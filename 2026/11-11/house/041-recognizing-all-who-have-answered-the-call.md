# RECOGNIZING ALL WHO HAVE ANSWERED THE CALL

**Speaking:** Messmer, Mark B. (M001233) [R-IN]

```
               RECOGNIZING ALL WHO HAVE ANSWERED THE CALL

  (Mr. MESSMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MESSMER. Mr. Speaker, on this day set aside to honor America's 
veterans, I rise to recognize the brave men and women who have worn the 
uniform of the United States Armed Forces.
  During difficult times of war, generations of Hoosiers from Indiana's 
Eighth District have answered the call and left their families, their 
communities, and the comforts of home to defend our country and protect 
the freedoms we hold dear.
  Our responsibility to the needs of those great heroes does not end 
when their Active-Duty service is over. I join my colleagues in making 
sure that every United States veteran will always receive the benefits, 
the healthcare, and all the support they have earned and deserve.
  We honor and thank every Hoosier and every American who has served 
our grateful Nation, today and every day, for their courageous 
sacrifice. May God bless our beloved veterans and their families, and 
may God bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
