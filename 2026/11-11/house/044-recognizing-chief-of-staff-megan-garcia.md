# RECOGNIZING CHIEF OF STAFF MEGAN GARCIA

**Speaking:** Balint, Becca (B001318) [D-VT]

```
                RECOGNIZING CHIEF OF STAFF MEGAN GARCIA

  (Ms. BALINT asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. BALINT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my Chief of Staff, 
Megan Garcia, and thank her for 4 incredible years.
  Megan was my very first hire in Congress, and we have been through a 
lot together. We built our office from the ground up, survived some 
very long days, and tackled more than a few unexpected challenges along 
the way.
  Megan brought to this work nearly a decade of experience on the Hill 
and experience as a foundation and nonprofit leader. She brought 
strategy, an incredible work ethic, and a whole lot of heart to the 
work. She has been my right hand, my sounding board, and, importantly, 
someone who will always tell me what I need to hear whether I want to 
hear it or not.
  We have shared a lot of core values, including our belief that the 
work that we do for the people deserves everything we have got.
  I thank Megan for everything. This office is better because of her, 
and I am better because of her. I wish Megan all the best on her next 
adventure. The lady will be missed.

                          ____________________
```
