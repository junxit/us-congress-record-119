# HONORING OFELIA GUERRA

**Speaking:** Ramirez, Delia C. (R000617) [D-IL]

```
                              {time}  1120
                         HONORING OFELIA GUERRA

  (Mrs. RAMIREZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Ofelia Guerra, a fierce 
protector of our communities, our children, and our culture.
  Ofelia is the founder and executive director of Ballet Folklorico de 
Chicago, the Midwest's largest Mexican folklorico school, serving more 
than 400 students annually.
  A lifelong Chicagoan, raised by her immigrant parents from Mexico and 
Cuba, she founded Ballet Folklorico de Chicago to create an accessible 
space for young people and families to connect with their cultural 
heritage through dance and community activities.
  Under Ofelia's leadership, Ballet Folklorico de Chicago has grown 
into a leading cultural institution, creating youth leadership and 
employment opportunities, expanding access to the arts, and awarding 21 
college scholarships to date.
  On behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, I am proud to 
commend Ofelia Guerra for her commitment to preserving cultural 
heritage and strengthening communities through the arts. I thank 
Ofelia. ``Congratulations''; ``felicitaciones.''
```
