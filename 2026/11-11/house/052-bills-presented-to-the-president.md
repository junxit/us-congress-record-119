# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 997, H.R. 1703

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 9, 
2026, the following bills were present to the President of the United 
States for approval:

       H.R. 1703. To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act 
     to clarify payment rules for manual wheelchairs under part B 
     of the Medicare program.
       H.R. 997. To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     conform to the intent of the Internal Revenue Service 
     Restructuring and Reform Act of 1998, as set forth in the 
     joint explanatory statement of the committee of conference 
     accompanying Conference Report 105-599, that the National 
     Taxpayer Advocate be able to hire and consult counsel as 
     appropriate.

                          ____________________
```
