# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of justice and peace, mercy and love, at this 11th hour on this 
11th day of the 11th month, we pause in prayer to You in gratitude for 
their service, giving thanks for the countless men and women, many of 
them our own family members and friends, who willingly risked their 
lives in our Nation's conflicts to protect and defend the freedom and 
democracy of these United States.
  Greater love has no one than these Americans who demonstrated the 
full measure of devotion by their willingness to lay down their lives 
for their countrymen. There is no greater sacrifice than that which our 
veterans have made in service to this Nation.
  Heal the wounds of war that still plague them, both visible and 
invisible. Apply Your balm of comfort on the battle scars that yet 
riddle their bodies and their souls. Grant them the peace they strove 
for as they reckon their spirits with the untold cost of their service.
  And inspire us not just to thank them for their service, but to 
welcome their stories, to seek their wisdom, and to value the 
contributions they continue to make for the betterment of our 
communities.
  God, with lasting gratitude, we honor the brave who have allowed us 
to live in this land of the free.
  In Your eternal name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
