# CONGRESSIONAL KIDNEY CAUCUS HONORS TROY ZIMMERMAN

**Speaking:** Miller, Carol D. (M001205) [R-WV]

```
           CONGRESSIONAL KIDNEY CAUCUS HONORS TROY ZIMMERMAN

  (Mrs. MILLER of West Virginia asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. MILLER of West Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today as chair of 
the Congressional Kidney Caucus to honor a true champion for kidney 
patients across the country, Troy Zimmerman.

[[Page H6173]]

  Troy has spent decades in government relations, most recently at the 
National Kidney Foundation, fighting tirelessly for the millions of 
Americans living with kidney disease.
  His commitment is personal. Troy has a family history of polycystic 
kidney disease, and he has carried that understanding into every policy 
fight, every hearing, and every conversation with Members of this body.
  Long before his time with the National Kidney Foundation, Troy cut 
his teeth right here on Capitol Hill where he also met his wife, Susan, 
before continuing his advocacy work across the healthcare lobbying 
world.
  Now, after decades spent serving patients and their families, Troy is 
retiring from the National Kidney Foundation.
  Troy is more than a colleague. To those of us who have worked with 
him, he is a dear friend and a tireless advocate for those in need.
  On behalf of the Congressional Kidney Caucus and patients everywhere, 
I wish Troy a fulfilling and joyful retirement. He has earned it.

                          ____________________
```
