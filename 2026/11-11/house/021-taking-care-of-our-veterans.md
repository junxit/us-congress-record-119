# TAKING CARE OF OUR VETERANS

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                      TAKING CARE OF OUR VETERANS

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)

[[Page H6152]]

  

  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I pay special tribute to 
the marines and their families who unknowingly were exposed to 
contaminated drinking water at Camp Lejeune.
  These families lived, worked, and raised children on the base for 
years, not realizing that their drinking water could make them sick.
  As a veteran, I believe we have a sacred obligation to these 
servicemembers and their families to right this wrong. Veterans Day is 
a reminder of why we should take care of every veteran and their family 
members who have been affected and in the most efficient and timely 
manner possible.
  It is not only the right thing to do, Mr. Speaker. It is the human 
thing to do.

                          ____________________
```
