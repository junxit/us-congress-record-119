# HONORING DR. DAN DOORNINK

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
                              {time}  1720
                       HONORING DR. DAN DOORNINK

  (Mr. NEWHOUSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of Dr. 
Dan Doornink. Both on and off the field, Dr. Dan represented the best 
of our community in central Washington.
  I first met Dan on the football field in high school where he played 
for the Wapato Wolves. Dan went on to play for the Washington State 
University Cougars and, most famously, for the Seattle Seahawks.
  After an extraordinary football career--``Mr. Third Down,'' as many 
fans called him--Dan returned to central Washington and served his 
community for over 30 years as a physician. While known nationally for 
his talent, central Washington will also remember him for his 
dedication to the community that raised him and the many decades of 
service and fond memories that he provided.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in Congress to join me in offering 
thoughts and prayers for his family, friends, and loved ones during 
this difficult time.

                          ____________________
```
