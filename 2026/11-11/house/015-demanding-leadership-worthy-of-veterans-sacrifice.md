# DEMANDING LEADERSHIP WORTHY OF VETERANS' SACRIFICE

**Speaking:** Horsford, Steven (H001066) [D-NV]

```
           DEMANDING LEADERSHIP WORTHY OF VETERANS' SACRIFICE

  (Mr. HORSFORD asked and was given permission to address the House

[[Page H6151]]

for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HORSFORD. Mr. Speaker, our constituents put their faith in us to 
show up.
  Yesterday, a personal emergency delayed my arrival to the House 
floor, and I missed an important vote. I take full responsibility. The 
accountability I demand of others applies to me, too.
  I would have voted ``no'' on the Republican motion to refer so that 
the House could proceed to a vote on Secretary Hegseth's impeachment.
  I am submitting that position for the Congressional Record now.
  On Veterans Day, we honor those who served by defending our 
Constitution and demanding leadership worthy of their sacrifice.
  Hegseth should be impeached. Americans deserve answers. No Secretary 
of Defense is above the law.
  Republicans may have delayed accountability, but they have not ended 
it. My constituents expect me to confront the affordability crisis, 
take on corruption, and hold this administration accountable. They 
deserve action, and I intend to deliver.
  May God bless our troops, our veterans, and their families, and may 
God bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
