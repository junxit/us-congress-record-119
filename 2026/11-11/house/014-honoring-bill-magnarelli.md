# HONORING BILL MAGNARELLI

**Speaking:** Mannion, John W. (M001231) [D-NY]

```
                        HONORING BILL MAGNARELLI

  (Mr. MANNION asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MANNION. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor New York State 
Assemblyman Bill Magnarelli, a veteran himself, as he prepares to leave 
office after 14 terms and 28 remarkable years.
  Born and raised in Syracuse, Bill is a model public servant, 
delivering for our businesses, schools, libraries, and first 
responders.
  He invested in our roads and bridges and helped to not only restore 
but, dare I say, save our beloved Landmark Theatre. He brought the 
Central New York Veterans Parade and Expo back to life in 2008 and has 
led it ever since.
  I was privileged to work with Bill in Albany to give Syracuse Hancock 
International Airport its own police force, New York State's first new 
police force in nearly 50 years.
  Bill has been a mentor and friend to me and to so many others. I 
thank Bill for representing Syracuse and central New York with such 
dedication and honor. I wish him and his family all the best.

                          ____________________
```
