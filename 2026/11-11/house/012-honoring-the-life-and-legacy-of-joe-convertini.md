# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JOE CONVERTINI

**Speaking:** Tenney, Claudia (T000478) [R-NY]

```
                              {time}  1110
             HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF JOE CONVERTINI

  (Ms. TENNEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. TENNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize tomorrow, November 12, 
as Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease, or CJD, Awareness Day.
  I also rise to honor Joe Convertini, a lifelong resident of Cortland, 
New York, and former constituent, who passed away from CJD this past 
August.
  If you knew Joe, you were truly blessed. I was privileged to get to 
know Joe through his daughter Julia, who served on my staff in the New 
York State Assembly.
  Joe was a devoted husband to his wife of 38 years, Kellyann; a caring 
brother to his younger brother, Phil; and the most wonderful father to 
his three beautiful girls, Victoria, Julia, and Sophia.
  Joe lived his life with grace, kindness, and integrity. He loved his 
home. He loved God. He loved his family with every fiber of his being.
  On August 1, Joe's life was cut short by CJD, a disease that most 
Americans have probably never heard of. This rare, rapidly progressive 
brain disease is not only terminal, but it is almost impossible to 
detect. The initial symptoms are subtle and hardly noticeable before it 
suddenly materializes into a devastating neurological decline at 
remarkable speed.
  For a month, his family was forced to watch helplessly as Joe slipped 
away, but the Convertini family's experience is not an anomaly. It is 
the cruel reality for many families with a loved one afflicted by this 
disease.
  While there is currently no treatment or cure, the CJD Foundation and 
various other patient groups are working tirelessly to change that. 
Joe's legacy will live on through his family's determination to fight 
for a cure. May his memory move us to act.
  Mr. Speaker, may God bless Joe and his family.

                          ____________________
```
