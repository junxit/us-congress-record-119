# MANDATE FOR CHANGE

**Speaking:** Amo, Gabe (A000380) [D-RI]

```
                           MANDATE FOR CHANGE

  (Mr. AMO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. AMO. Mr. Speaker, the people have spoken, and they want the Iran 
war over and accountability for the leaders who led us down this costly 
path.
  However, Republicans refused. When given the chance to stand with the 
people, they once again chose to stand with Trump, his illegal war of 
choice with Iran, and more chaos and more confusion.
  There have been 23 American servicemembers killed. Hundreds more have 
been injured, and families here at home are paying the price, from the 
gas pump to the grocery store to the heating oil tank.
  The Pentagon is not a television studio for the Secretary to build up 
his public image of wartime leader. It is past time to end this illegal 
war, bring down costs, and hold Hegseth accountable.
  While we haven't moved forward on action to remove Hegseth yet, you 
better believe my inspiring new colleagues and my current colleagues 
here in the Congress will be prepared to deliver on the American 
people's mandate for change.

                          ____________________
```
