# HONORING RON CANESTRARI

**Speaking:** Tonko, Paul (T000469) [D-NY]

```
                        HONORING RON CANESTRARI

  (Mr. TONKO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TONKO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a respected and revered 
titan in New York State politics and a true public servant who always 
fought on behalf of his community, my friend Ron Canestrari.

[[Page H6172]]

  I was heartbroken to hear of Ron's passing this week. He was an 
invaluable friend, advocate, and mentor during our time working 
together in the New York State Assembly. He further served our capital 
region as the Assembly majority leader and as a longtime mayor of 
Cohoes.
  Public service was a natural calling for Ron. He believed 
wholeheartedly in the role of government to better people's lives, and 
he embodied that mission by leading with compassion, intelligence, and 
a relentless commitment to his community across his nearly 40 years of 
service.
  Ron's spirit and heart will forever be remembered by myself and all 
of those who have had the honor of working alongside him.
  Mr. Speaker, I request that we honor his memory through this legacy 
in the House and also to acknowledge that he may rest comfortably in 
the arms of his Creator.

                          ____________________
```
