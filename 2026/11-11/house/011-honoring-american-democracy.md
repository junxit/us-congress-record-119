# HONORING AMERICAN DEMOCRACY

**Speaking:** Mejia, Analilia (M001246) [D-NJ]

```
                      HONORING AMERICAN DEMOCRACY

  (Ms. MEJIA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. MEJIA. Mr. Speaker, I rise in honor of American democracy, never 
designed to be perfect, but completely designed to be perfectible, a 
representative system in which true strength lies not in the absence of 
conflict, but in the peaceful, structured way in which we resolve it.
  As a true patriot, I assert and uphold that the power of this 
governing body begins and ends with its citizens.
  Our government was designed to operate strictly by the consent of the 
governed. The ultimate authority rests in the hands of we the people.
  For many, it seems as if we have lost our way. It is our collective 
duty to ensure that we find our way back.
  Like many new Members taking my first steps in this great 
institution, I recognize the tremendous privilege and responsibility we 
have committed ourselves to. May we always earn the trust and 
confidence of our fellow Americans, and may we be remembered for 
upholding justice and liberty for all overall.

                          ____________________
```
