# RECOGNIZING AALIYAH URIOSTE

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING AALIYAH URIOSTE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Aaliyah Urioste 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Aaliyah has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Aaliyah, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Aaliyah's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Three Creeks K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Aaliyah Urioste on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
