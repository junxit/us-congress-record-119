# COMMEMORATING MATTHEW ARTHUR'S SERVICE TO MARYLAND'S 1ST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
COMMEMORATING MATTHEW ARTHUR'S SERVICE TO MARYLAND'S 1ST CONGRESSIONAL 
                                DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. HARRIS of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and 
congratulate my Senior Legislative Assistant, Matthew Arthur, for his 
years of selfless service. Matthew has been a loyal and dedicated staff 
member who has worked tirelessly for constituents in Maryland's 1st 
Congressional District. The commitment Matthew has demonstrated has 
been exceptional and praiseworthy.
  Mr. Arthur rose through the ranks of my office, beginning as Staff 
Assistant and working his way up to serve as my Senior Legislative 
Assistant, overseeing my healthcare portfolio, and many other issues. 
He has consistently and faithfully worked to ensure my legislative 
agenda was met. He has been an extremely valuable team member, always 
willing to take on challenging roles, or to help wherever is needed.
  It is important that Representatives recognize outstanding staff such 
as Matthew. Often, the work of these staffers goes unnoticed, but is 
nonetheless critical in maintaining the functions of our government.
  Matthew is a true patriot, and Maryland's 1st Congressional District 
undoubtedly owes him a great debt of gratitude. He will be missed.
  I thank Matthew, and wish him well in his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
