# RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES AIR FORCE CAPTAIN DAVID "PEDRO" MORENO

**Speaking:** Rutherford, John H. (R000609) [R-FL]

```
   RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES AIR FORCE CAPTAIN DAVID ``PEDRO'' MORENO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JOHN H. RUTHERFORD

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. RUTHERFORD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Captain David 
``Pedro'' Moreno, who retires on Veterans Day, November 11, 2026, after 
more than 23 years of honorable service in the United States Air Force 
and the Iowa Air National Guard.
  Captain Moreno began his career as a Tactical Air Control Party 
Airman and completed five combat deployments--three to Afghanistan and 
two to Iraq. After those years on the battlefield, he spent eight years 
as a heating, ventilation, and air conditioning craftsman (HVAC), 
including supporting recovery efforts at Tyndall Air Force Base in 
Panama City, Florida after Hurricane Michael, a Category 5 storm, 
struck the installation in 2018. Captain Moreno was one of the first 
recalled to the installation after landfall to aid with cleanup. In his 
spare time, he helped veterans with roof repairs and tree removal.
  Captain Moreno later earned his commission and fulfilled a lifelong 
dream of becoming a military aviator. As a combat plans officer and MQ-
9 Reaper pilot with the Iowa Air National Guard in Des Moines, he flew 
103 combat missions and 46 combat-support missions, logging 549 flight 
hours while providing armed overwatch and real time intelligence.
  His military honors include the Bronze Star Medal, the Air Force 
Combat Action Medal, and multiple Aerial Achievement Medals. Included 
with his Commendation Medal Citation was his contribution to a combat 
search-and-rescue mission, his leadership during a power failure and 
severe weather, and his dedicated efforts to train and mentor fellow 
Airmen.
  Captain Moreno is a credit to the United States Air Force and I wish 
him only the best in his retirement.
```
