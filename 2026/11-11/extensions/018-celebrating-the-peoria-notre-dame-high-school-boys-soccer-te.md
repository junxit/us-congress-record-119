# CELEBRATING THE PEORIA NOTRE DAME HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' SOCCER TEAM

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
    CELEBRATING THE PEORIA NOTRE DAME HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' SOCCER TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Peoria Notre 
Dame Boys' Soccer Team, who won the Illinois High School Association 
Class 2A State Championship on November 7, 2026. This marks the 
program's sixth state championship and further solidifies its place 
among the premier soccer programs in Illinois.
  The Irish secured the title in a 1-0 win over the Crystal Lake South 
Gators. Finishing with an impressive 22-1-3 record and a 19-match 
unbeaten streak, Peoria Notre Dame demonstrated exceptional skill, 
determination, discipline, and teamwork throughout the season.
  With their victory, the Irish now hold the second-most state 
championships in Illinois history and are tied for the most appearances 
in state championship matches. Beyond these accomplishments, Peoria 
Notre Dame also holds state records for sectional championships, state 
finals appearances, and state trophies. Much of this success is a 
testament to the leadership and dedication of Head Coach Mike Bare, who 
has spent almost two decades with the Irish, earning five of six state 
titles and producing numerous All-State selections, All-Americans, and 
NCAA Division 1 players.
  I am proud to represent the Peoria Notre Dame community and commend 
the players, coaches, families, and supporters who contributed to this 
outstanding accomplishment. As the Irish celebrate another historic 
season, I congratulate Coach Bare and the Peoria Notre Dame team on 
their remarkable achievement and wish the program continued success in 
the years to come.
```
