# RECOGNIZING AVEN WILLIFORD

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING AVEN WILLIFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Aven Williford 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Aven has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Aven, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Aven's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Three Creeks K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Aven Williford on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
