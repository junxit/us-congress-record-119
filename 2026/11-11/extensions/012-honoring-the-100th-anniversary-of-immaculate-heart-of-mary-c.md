# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF IMMACULATE HEART OF MARY CATHOLIC CHURCH

**Speaking:** Garcia, Sylvia R. (G000587) [D-TX]

```
  HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF IMMACULATE HEART OF MARY CATHOLIC 
                                 CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SYLVIA R. GARCIA

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Ms. GARCIA of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 100th 
anniversary of Immaculate Heart of Mary Catholic Church in Houston's 
Magnolia Park neighborhood and celebrate a century of faith, family, 
and service to our community.
  Founded in 1926 by the Missionary Oblates of Mary Immaculate, 
Immaculate Heart of Mary has been a cornerstone of Houston's Mexican 
American community for generations. What began as a small gathering of 
families grew into a beloved church that has welcomed hardworking 
families seeking faith, fellowship, and a place to call home.
  For 100 years, this church has been part of the fabric of Magnolia 
Park. It is where families have celebrated baptisms, weddings, and 
life's most important milestones, where neighbors have come together in 
difficult times, and where our traditions and values have been passed 
down to our children and grandchildren.
  As the parish celebrated this special milestone on November 8, 2026, 
I want to congratulate Father Lucio, the clergy, parishioners, and all 
the families whose faith and dedication have kept this church strong 
for a century.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the 
Immaculate Heart of Mary Catholic Church and its 100 years of service 
to our community. May God continue to bless this parish and the 
families who call it home for many years to come.
```
