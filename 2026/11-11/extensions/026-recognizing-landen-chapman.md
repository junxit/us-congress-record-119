# RECOGNIZING LANDEN CHAPMAN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING LANDEN CHAPMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Landen Chapman 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Landen has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Landen, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Landen's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Two Roads Charter School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Landen Chapman on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
