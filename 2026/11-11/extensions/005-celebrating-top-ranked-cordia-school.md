# CELEBRATING TOP-RANKED CORDIA SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Rogers, Harold (R000395) [R-KY]

```
                  CELEBRATING TOP-RANKED CORDIA SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HAROLD ROGERS

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Kentucky. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to 
the students, teachers, and staff of Cordia School in Knott County, 
Kentucky, which has been named the top-rated high school in the 
Commonwealth of Kentucky in the Kentucky Department of Education's 
2025-2026 school accountability results. This remarkable accomplishment 
is a source of tremendous pride for our region.
  Nestled along Lotts Creek in the mountains of Knott County, Cordia 
School has served Appalachian families for more than 90 years. In 1933, 
Alice Slone, a Knott County native who had been sent to Cleveland, 
Ohio, by Alice Lloyd to further her education, came home to the 
mountains to teach and founded the Lotts Creek Community School. A year 
later she opened Cordia High School, bringing the community together 
around a common goal: education. Nearly a century later, Cordia's 
students are carrying that mission forward in extraordinary fashion.
  The numbers speak for themselves. Cordia's tenth graders posted 
proficiency rates of 71 percent in mathematics and 88 percent in 
reading, far above the statewide averages of 40 percent and 49 percent. 
In earning the top spot, this school of fewer than 200 students 
finished ahead of some of the largest and most celebrated high schools 
in the Commonwealth.
  The road to the top has not been easy. Like so many communities in 
southern and eastern Kentucky, the families of Lotts Creek have endured 
economic hardship, and many are still rebuilding from the devastating 
floods that struck our region in July 2022. Yet in the face of those 
challenges, the students of this small mountain school outperformed 
every high school in Kentucky. Their success shows that academic 
excellence is not determined by geography, family income or the size of 
a school. It is the result of hard work, dedicated teachers and a 
community that refuses to give up on its children.
  I commend Principal Deanna Wicker, the faculty and staff, and the 
Knott County School District for their unwavering commitment to these 
students. I also applaud the parents and families who have stood behind 
them every step of the way. Above all, I congratulate the students 
themselves. They have shown young people across Eastern Kentucky that 
their hard work matters, and that extraordinary things can come from 
even the smallest mountain communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Cordia School 
for its outstanding achievement. These students have earned their place 
among the best in Kentucky, and I wish them continued success in the 
years to come.
```
