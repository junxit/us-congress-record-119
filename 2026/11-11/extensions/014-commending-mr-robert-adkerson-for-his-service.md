# COMMENDING MR. ROBERT ADKERSON FOR HIS SERVICE

**Speaking:** Loudermilk, Barry (L000583) [R-GA]

```
             COMMENDING MR. ROBERT ADKERSON FOR HIS SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BARRY LOUDERMILK

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. LOUDERMILK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend 
Mr. Robert Adkerson, who served as my Chief of Staff in my 
Congressional office for over 11 years.
  Prior to his joining the team and helping manage my campaign in 2014, 
Rob was a stalwart in Georgia's Conservative movement. He was known for 
his leadership at the grassroots level and consistently demonstrated a 
commitment to restoring freedom and liberty, the primary qualities I 
believed made him uniquely qualified to later lead my Congressional 
staff.
  As Chief of Staff, Rob exemplified our mission of upholding the 
original ideals of our Constitution and representing and serving the 
people of the 11th Congressional District with integrity and respect.
  Beyond serving on staff, Rob has been a friend to me and my family 
and a steadfast colleague who stood by me through some of the most 
challenging times. He was there with me during the domestic terrorist 
attack against the Republican Congressional Baseball team in 2017, and 
he was there time and again as we navigated threats against me, my 
family, and my staff.
  Rob's passion for the American ideal and our founding principles has 
remained his priority. Over the course of his time working with me in 
my Congressional office, from 2015 to 2026, Rob engaged with 
constituents and stakeholders from all walks of life and across the 
political spectrum.
  I greatly appreciate his service in the office and to the 
constituents of Georgia's 11th Congressional District. I believe that 
whatever Rob chooses to do in the future, he will undoubtedly continue 
working to ensure we have a community, state, and Nation that is free, 
safe, and full of opportunity; and I am grateful for the time he spent 
as a valued member of our office family.
  God speed, and I thank Rob for his years of service.

                          ____________________
```
