# COMMENDING THE STAFF OF LOUISIANA'S 6TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
     COMMENDING THE STAFF OF LOUISIANA'S 6TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend the staff of 
Louisiana's 6th Congressional District for their exceptional service 
during the 119th Congress. Through long days, demanding deadlines, and 
the constant needs of the people we serve, they have shown the hard 
work, dedication, and commitment to constituents that define public 
service at its best.
  This team has served the people of Louisiana from Washington, D.C., 
and from district offices in Baton Rouge, Opelousas, Shreveport, and 
Lafayette. Whether they were reviewing legislation, preparing for 
hearings and

[[Page E1198]]

votes, meeting with stakeholders, answering calls, helping a family 
navigate a federal agency, or engaging with residents in their own 
communities, each member of this staff has kept one goal in mind: to 
serve the constituents of the 6th District with excellence, compassion, 
and respect.
  This staff also holds a unique distinction. Every member of the team 
is from Louisiana. They know our parishes, our neighborhoods, our 
churches, our schools, and our families because they are part of them. 
They took great pride in serving their home state, and that pride 
showed in the care they brought to every task. For them, this work was 
never only a job. It was an opportunity to give back to the place that 
raised them.
  In the Washington, D.C. office, I recognize Chief of Staff, Cheredith 
Rhone; Legislative Directors, Matt DeVille and Evan Ashy; Scheduler, 
Destinee Carmouche; Legislative Assistants, Joseph Coleman and Kyle 
Pierre; Legislative Aides, Christian Vavasseur and Darian McCorvey; 
Staff Assistant, Christopher Braggs; and Press Secretaries, Sydney 
Broome and Calla Duggan.
  In the Baton Rouge district office, I recognize District and 
Communications Director, Shalyric Self; Senior Advisor and Director of 
Member and Stakeholder Engagement, Ben Jeffers; Director of Faith and 
Community Outreach, Johnny Anderson; Field Representative and 
Caseworker, Carlette Dunn; and District Receptionist, Sydney LaFleur.
  I also recognize LaVonya Malveaux of the Opelousas office; Regional 
Representatives, Signorah Thomas and Quinton Aught of the Shreveport 
office; and Reggie Guillory of the Lafayette office.
  I further recognize Field Representative, Nick Campbell; Special 
Assistant, Roderick Scott; Student Engagement Coordinator, Peighton 
Ashley; and Staff Assistant, Kennedi Washington for their service to 
the 6th District.
  Mr. Speaker, the success of any congressional office rests on the 
people behind the scenes who answer the call day after day.
  I am deeply grateful to each member of this team for their service, 
their sacrifice, and their love for the people of Louisiana. I ask my 
colleagues to join me in commending the staff of Louisiana's 6th 
Congressional District for a job well done during the 119th Congress.

                          ____________________
```
