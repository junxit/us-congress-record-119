# HONORING DALLAS BERGL FOR A LIFETIME OF SERVICE TO INDIANA

**Speaking:** Yakym, Rudy (Y000067) [R-IN]

```
       HONORING DALLAS BERGL FOR A LIFETIME OF SERVICE TO INDIANA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RUDY YAKYM III

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dallas Bergl, President 
and CEO of INOVA Federal Credit Union, who has spent his career helping 
the people of Northern Indiana build better lives. In recognition of 
that work, Dallas was recently awarded the Sagamore of the Wabash, one 
of Indiana's highest honors.
  For more than 25 years as INOVA's President and CEO, Dallas has 
focused on helping Hoosier families and small businesses thrive. INOVA 
is still headquartered in downtown Elkhart, where it has been for more 
than 80 years, and under Dallas's leadership it opened a new branch in 
Mishawaka in 2023.
  Dallas started at INOVA as a collections manager and rose to become 
President and CEO in 1999. Before that, he honorably served our Nation 
in the United States Army Infantry and the United States Army Honor 
Guard at Fort Myer, Virginia, where he took part in President Ronald 
Reagan's Presidential Escort and received numerous military honors.
  Dallas never stopped investing in Indiana. He carried the voice of 
Hoosier credit unions to the boards of the Indiana Credit Union League, 
the Credit Union National Association, and the World Council of Credit 
Unions. He served as the City of Elkhart's ambassador to China. In 
2018, he helped organize a delegation of Hoosiers who traveled to 
Dominica to assist credit unions after a devastating hurricane. His 
community has honored him with the Key to the City of Elkhart and 
induction into the Indiana Credit Union Hall of Fame.
  Mr. Speaker, Dallas Bergl has set an example of service, integrity, 
and a genuine commitment to improving the lives of Hoosiers. As he 
prepares to conclude his career in early 2027, and on behalf of both 
the United States Congress and the people of Indiana's 2nd 
Congressional District, I am proud to honor his incredible achievements 
and extend to him our best wishes for the next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
