# HONORING THE SERVICE OF ROBIN VOS

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

```
                   HONORING THE SERVICE OF ROBIN VOS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BRYAN STEIL

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the career and service 
of Speaker of the Wisconsin Assembly, Robin Vos. Robin began his public 
service as a student representative on the Universities of Wisconsin 
Board of Regents from 1989 to 1991. He later served as a Racine County 
Board Supervisor in 1994 before being elected to represent Wisconsin's 
63rd Assembly District. First elected in 2004, Robin represented the 
63rd District for more than two decades and continued his service 
representing the 33rd District beginning in 2025.
  Robin has been a steadfast advocate for the people of southeastern 
Wisconsin and a leading voice on issues that strengthen our state's 
economy, communities, and future. In 2013, Robin was elected the 75th 
Speaker of the Wisconsin State Assembly, becoming the longest-serving 
Speaker in Wisconsin history. Throughout his tenure, he played a 
central role in shaping state policy providing multiple rounds of tax 
relief for Wisconsin families, advancing beneficial economic reforms, 
and leading to numerous state budget surpluses. His Speaker's Task 
Forces have developed important legislation on topics including mental 
health, rural schools, Alzheimer's and dementia, urban education, water 
quality, suicide prevention, and foster care. Robin previously served 
as President of the National Conference of State Legislatures (NCSL) 
from 2019 to 2021 and later in 2023. He is the first elected official 
from Wisconsin to hold this leadership post.
  As a lifelong resident of Racine County, Robin has remained deeply 
connected to the communities he represents. Whether meeting with 
constituents, supporting local organizations, or working to bring 
resources and opportunities to southeastern Wisconsin, he has 
consistently demonstrated a commitment to serving his neighbors and 
improving the quality of life for the people of his district and the 
people of our state.
  I thank Robin for his leadership, commitment to Wisconsin, and his 
many years of service. His contributions to our state will have a 
lasting impact.
```
