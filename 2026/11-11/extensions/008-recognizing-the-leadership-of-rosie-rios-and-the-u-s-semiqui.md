# RECOGNIZING THE LEADERSHIP OF ROSIE RIOS AND THE U.S. SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL COMMISSION

**Speaking:** Watson Coleman, Bonnie (W000822) [D-NJ]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE LEADERSHIP OF ROSIE RIOS AND THE U.S. 
                     SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL COMMISSION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BONNIE WATSON COLEMAN

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mrs. WATSON COLEMAN. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the Congressional 
commissioners, I rise today along with my colleagues, Representatives 
Robert Aderholt, Dwight Evans, and Maria Elvira Salazar, to honor and 
recognize the extraordinary leadership of Rosie Rios, Chair of the 
United States Semiquincentennial Commission (America250).
  For the past four years, Chair Rios has guided our Nation's official 
planning commission with vision, dedication, and an unwavering 
commitment to bringing Americans together. Entrusted with leading the 
Nation's commemoration of the 250th anniversary of American 
independence, she has embraced the monumental responsibility of 
ensuring that this historic occasion reflects the people, communities, 
and ideals that define our great Nation. Under her leadership, the 
Commission has worked to engage Americans across all fifty states, five 
territories, and the District of Columbia in reflecting on our shared 
history, celebrating our Nation's achievements, and looking toward its 
future.
  Throughout her tenure, Chair Rios has overseen an ambitious range of 
national initiatives designed to inspire civic engagement, promote 
historical education, and encourage community participation. Through 
initiatives such as Our American Story, the America's Field Trip 
student contest, historical exhibitions, artistic tributes like the 
America250 National Birthday Cake, and the America250 Time Capsule, the 
Commission has helped bring the spirit of the Semiquincentennial to 
communities across the country. Her leadership has been instrumental in 
advancing a commemoration that honors our Nation's founding while 
encouraging Americans of all backgrounds to participate in this 
celebration.
  Mr. Speaker, such a historic undertaking could not succeed without a 
dedicated team. We would also like to express our deep gratitude to the 
fellow Commissioners of the United States Semiquincentennial 
Commission: Robert Brady, Reginald Browne, Tonic Burgos, Kellyanne 
Conaway, Val Crofts, Joe Crowley, Daniel DiLella, Cathy Gillespie, Noah 
Griffin, Jr., Wilfred M. McClay, Lucas Morel, James Rane, Jack 
Schlossberg, Paul Tetreault. and Lynn Forney Young. Their bipartisan 
collaboration, tireless efforts, and shared dedication have been 
essential in shaping a meaningful and successful celebration worthy of 
our Nation's historic milestone.
  As Members of Congress, we recognize the tremendous responsibility 
entrusted to Chair Rios and her fellow Commissioners in preparing our 
nation for this once-in-a-generation commemoration. Their collective 
efforts have helped lay the foundation for a historic celebration that 
will inspire Americans for generatIons to come.
  Mr. Speaker, we extend our sincere appreciation to Chair Rosie Rios 
and all the Commissioners for their service and dedication to the 
United States of America. We are proud to join together in recognizing 
their contributions to this historic milestone in our Nation's history.
```
