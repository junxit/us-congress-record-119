# CELEBRATING KENNETH "KENNY" MOORE'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Bost, Mike (B001295) [R-IL]

```
          CELEBRATING KENNETH ``KENNY'' MOORE'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. MIKE BOST

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. BOST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Mr. Kenneth 
``Kenny'' Moore's 100th birthday, and a life lived richly in service to 
his country, community, family, and friends.
  Kenny was born in Murphysboro, Illinois, on November 6, 1926, to 
Robert and Laura Moore. It has been said growing up during the

[[Page E1197]]

Great Depression left a lasting impact on many Americans; Kenny was no 
different.
  In 1944, at the age of 18, Kenny joined the United States Navy. He 
served as a Seaman First Class on the USS PC-822, escorting ships from 
open sea to port off the coast of Panama.
  When Kenny was discharged following the war, he returned to 
Murphysboro, where he met and married the love of his life, Wilma 
Reeves, in 1949. Shortly after marrying Wilma, Kenny started a land 
clearing and excavation business in town. In 1958, the couple purchased 
a small farm south of Murphysboro where they raised their children.
  Kenny and Wilma had a blessed marriage of 76 years. He and Wilma 
raised six children--Beverly, Warren, Roger, Callie, Angela, and Mark--
in their loving home. Their children would bring them eight 
grandchildren and 13 great-grandchildren, who Kenny and Wilma loved 
dearly.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in celebrating Kenneth Moore's legacy and 
service as we make the milestone of his 100th birthday. I pray he and 
his loved ones have a wonderful time celebrating a century of great 
memories.

                          ____________________
```
