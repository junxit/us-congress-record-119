# RECOGNIZING KMI SPACE ON THE ANNIVERSARY OF ITS HISTORIC ORBITAL CAPTURE DEMONSTRATION

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
   RECOGNIZING KMI SPACE ON THE ANNIVERSARY OF ITS HISTORIC ORBITAL 
                         CAPTURE DEMONSTRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 11, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize KMI Space, a 
space logistics company founded and based in Marquette, Michigan, on 
the occasion of its anniversary of their groundbreaking orbital capture 
demonstration performed on November 15, 2024. KMI's achievement was a 
historic milestone for American space capabilities and a reminder of 
the ingenuity Michigan businesses bring to our Nation's aerospace 
industry.
  On November 4, 2024, KMI's REACCH orbital capture technology launched 
to the International Space Station. 11 days later, astronaut Sunita 
Williams installed the technology onto an Astrobee free-flying robot in 
the station's Japanese Experiment Module, where it successfully 
captured a free-flying test object on its first calibration run. Over 
six sessions, station crew members completed 172 capture cycles, 
proving the technology's potential to advance operations in orbit and 
future space logistics.
  With this accomplishment, KMI became the first private company to 
repeatedly capture objects in space and the first commercial entity to 
capture an unprepared object in orbit. After more than 200 days aboard 
the station,

[[Page E1195]]

REACCH returned to Earth in May 2025 aboard SpaceX's Dragon CRS-32 
cargo spacecraft. These demonstrations are an important step toward 
future free-flying space missions and expanded capabilities in orbit.
  Achievements like this do not happen by chance. They are the product 
of disciplined preparation, technical expertise, and a team that runs 
toward hard problems. KMI shows that innovative small businesses in 
Northern Michigan and the Upper Peninsula can make real contributions 
to American technological advancement and help keep our Nation ahead in 
space.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating KMI on 
this historic accomplishment. I thank their team for representing 
Michigan businesses and innovation so well on a national scale.
```
