# Text of Senate Amendment 6892

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

```
  SA 6892. Mr. KENNEDY (for himself and Mr. Cassidy) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. DEFINITION OF THE TERM ``COASTAL POLITICAL 
                   SUBDIVISION'' UNDER THE GULF OF MEXICO ENERGY 
                   SECURITY ACT OF 2006.

       Section 102(10)(A) of the Gulf of Mexico Energy Security 
     Act of 2006 (43 U.S.C. 1331 note; Public Law 109-432) is 
     amended by striking ``as of the date of enactment of this 
     Act'' and inserting ``, as delineated on the date of 
     enactment of the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs 
     Act of 2026 in the management program of the Gulf producing 
     State approved by the Secretary of Commerce under section 306 
     of that Act (16 U.S.C. 1455)''.
                                 ______
```
