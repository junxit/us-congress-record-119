# SENATE RESOLUTION 958--RECOGNIZING THE WEEK OF NOVEMBER 9 THROUGH NOVEMBER 13, 2026, AS "NATIONAL VETERANS SMALL BUSINESS WEEK"

**Measures:** S.Res. 958

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 958--RECOGNIZING THE WEEK OF NOVEMBER 9 THROUGH 
    NOVEMBER 13, 2026, AS ``NATIONAL VETERANS SMALL BUSINESS WEEK''

  Ms. ERNST (for herself, Ms. Duckworth, Mr. Justice, Ms. Alsobrooks, 
Mr. Risch, Mr. Hickenlooper, Ms. Collins, Ms. Smith, Mr. Crapo, Mrs. 
Shaheen, Mr. Rounds, Ms. Hirono, Mr. McConnell, Ms. Rosen, Mr. Budd, 
Mr. Schiff, Mr. Lee, Ms. Cortez Masto, Mr. Cassidy, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. 
Kennedy, Mr. Reed, Mr. Tuberville, Mr. Booker, Mr. Tillis, Mr. Cramer, 
Mr. Scott of Florida, Mr. Curtis, Mr. Moran, Mr. Daines, Mrs. Fischer, 
Mr. Young, Mr. Hoeven, Mrs. Britt, Mr. Boozman, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. 
Scott of South Carolina, Mr. Lankford, Mr. Wicker, and Mr. Hawley) 
submitted the following resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 958

       Whereas the Armed Forces of the United States train 
     individuals with the skills, discipline, and leadership 
     necessary to establish and operate a successful business;
       Whereas there are 1,651,542 veteran-owned small businesses 
     in the United States, employing 3,245,316 individuals;
       Whereas veteran-owned small businesses make up 5.3 percent 
     of all businesses in the United States;
       Whereas veteran-owned small businesses account for more 
     than $952,000,000,000 in total sales every year;
       Whereas the Small Business Administration hosts events 
     honoring National Veterans Small Business Week from November 
     9 through November 13, 2026;
       Whereas the Committee on Small Business and 
     Entrepreneurship of the Senate celebrates National Veterans 
     Small Business Week during the week of November 9 through 
     November 13, 2026; and
       Whereas the week of November 9 through November 13, 2026, 
     would be an appropriate time to celebrate National Veterans 
     Small Business Week: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) recognizes the week of November 9 through November 13, 
     2026, as ``National Veterans Small Business Week'';
       (2) supports the goals and ideals of National Veterans 
     Small Business Week;
       (3) recognizes the importance of creating policies that 
     promote a business-friendly environment for small business 
     owners that is free of unnecessary and burdensome regulations 
     and red tape; and
       (4) expresses appreciation for the continued service to the 
     United States by the veterans of the United States through 
     small business ownership and entrepreneurship.

                          ____________________
```
