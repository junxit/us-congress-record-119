# SENATE RESOLUTION 956--HONORING THE SERVICE AND SACRIFICE OF THE VETERANS OF THE UNITED STATES ON VETERANS DAY, 2026

**Measures:** S.Res. 956

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

   SENATE RESOLUTION 956--HONORING THE SERVICE AND SACRIFICE OF THE 
          VETERANS OF THE UNITED STATES ON VETERANS DAY, 2026

  Mrs. MOODY submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 956

       Whereas President Woodrow Wilson declared November 11, 
     1919, as the first observance of Armistice Day, on the one-
     year anniversary of the end of hostilities in World War I;
       Whereas, in 1938, Congress passed a law to establish 
     November 11 annually as Armistice Day to honor World War I 
     veterans;
       Whereas, following heroic actions from servicemembers in 
     World War II and the Korean War, Congress passed Public Law 
     83-380 in 1954, to change Armistice Day to Veterans Day;
       Whereas Veterans Day honors all who have served in the 
     United States military during war or peacetime;
       Whereas the Department of Veterans Affairs indicates that 
     since the founding of the country, more than 41,000,000 
     people of the United States have earned the title of veteran;
       Whereas the Department of Veterans Affairs reports that, as 
     of the date of the introduction of this resolution, 
     approximately 17,300,000 veterans are living;
       Whereas veterans have kept the darkness of evil from 
     extinguishing the blessed light of freedom;
       Whereas veterans have gone forward to fight kaisers, 
     commissars, fascists, Nazis, and terrorists to preserve the 
     freedoms of the United States;
       Whereas the families of the veterans also sacrifice greatly 
     for the United States;
       Whereas veterans must continue to receive the services and 
     support they rightfully deserve; and
       Whereas veterans service organizations and other groups 
     provide critical advocacy on behalf of the veterans of the 
     United States: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) honors the service and sacrifice of the veterans of the 
     United States; and
       (2) calls on the people of the United States to observe 
     Veterans Day in recognition of the heroic actions of veterans 
     to protect the United States and allow freedom to endure.
```
