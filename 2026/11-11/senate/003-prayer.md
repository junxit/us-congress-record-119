# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, who surrounds us with angels who excel in strength. We 
thank You for this day set aside to honor those who have served our 
Nation in the U.S. Armed Forces.
  We remember with gratitude those who served in times of war and 
peace, those who stood firm with courage when challenges and sacrifices 
were required, those who helped to protect our freedom.
  Bless our veterans. Heal those who carry physical and emotional 
wounds. Comfort grieving families. And strengthen those who continue to 
bear the burdens of military service.
  May our appreciation be reflected not merely in our words but in our 
willingness to serve others, defend what is right, and become 
instruments of Your peace.
  Surround our veterans and their families with Your grace and reward 
their sacrifices with the assurance that they are remembered and 
appreciated.
  We pray in the Name of the Prince of Peace. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
