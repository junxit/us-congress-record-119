# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 956, S.Res. 957, S.Res. 958

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mrs. MOODY:
       S. Res. 956. A resolution honoring the service and 
     sacrifice of the veterans of the United States on Veterans 
     Day, 2026; considered and agreed to.
           By Mrs. SHAHEEN (for herself, Mr. Cotton, Mr. 
             Blumenthal, Ms. Hirono, Mr. Sullivan, Mr. Budd, Mr. 
             Hickenlooper, Mr. Sheehy, Mr. Booker, Ms. Cortez 
             Masto, Ms. Baldwin, Mr. Schmitt, Mr. Scott of 
             Florida, Ms. Rosen, Ms. Warren, Mr. Bennet, Mr. 
             Boozman, and Mr. Fetterman):
       S. Res. 957. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of November 15, 2026, as ``National Warrior Call 
     Day'' and recognizing the importance of connecting members of 
     the Armed Forces and veterans in the United States to support 
     structures necessary to transition from the battlefield, 
     especially peer-to-peer connection; considered and agreed to.
           By Ms. ERNST (for herself, Ms. Duckworth, Mr. Justice, 
             Ms. Alsobrooks, Mr. Risch, Mr. Hickenlooper, Ms. 
             Collins, Ms. Smith, Mr. Crapo, Mrs. Shaheen, Mr. 
             Rounds, Ms. Hirono, Mr. McConnell, Ms. Rosen, Mr. 
             Budd, Mr. Schiff, Mr. Lee, Ms. Cortez Masto, Mr. 
             Cassidy, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Reed, Mr. 
             Tuberville, Mr. Booker, Mr. Tillis, Mr. Cramer, Mr. 
             Scott of Florida, Mr. Curtis, Mr. Moran, Mr. Daines, 
             Mrs. Fischer, Mr. Young, Mr. Hoeven, Mrs. Britt, Mr. 
             Boozman, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Scott of South Carolina, 
             Mr. Lankford, Mr. Wicker, and Mr. Hawley):
       S. Res. 958. A resolution recognizing the week of November 
     9 through November 13, 2026, as ``National Veterans Small 
     Business Week''; considered and agreed to.
```
