# Text of Senate Amendment 6893

**Speaking:** Smith, Tina (S001203) [D-MN]

```
  SA 6893. Ms. SMITH (for herself and Ms. Klobuchar) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by her to the bill H.R. 2347, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income 
any damages, other than punitive damages, received on account of any 
sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       On page 242, strike lines 3 through 10 and insert the 
     following:
       (d) Rights-of-Way.--Section 216(e) of the Federal Power Act 
     (16 U.S.C. 824p(e)) is amended--
       (1) in paragraph (1), by striking ``or a State'';
       (2) in paragraph (3), by striking ``shall conform'' and all 
     that follows through the period at the end and inserting 
     ``shall be in accordance with rule 71.1 of the Federal Rules 
     of Civil Procedure.''; and
       (3) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(5) Any proceeding under this subsection shall be subject 
     to all applicable landowner protections afforded by the State 
     in which the applicable affected property is located, and 
     nothing in this section overrides or otherwise affects any 
     protection, including any substantive or procedural right, 
     requirement, or other protection, available or otherwise 
     applicable to an affected landowner under State law before, 
     during, or after the proceeding.''.
                                 ______
```
