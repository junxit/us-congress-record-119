# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 2448, S. 3301, S. 3329, S. 4348, S. 5685

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 2448

  At the request of Mr. Kelly, the name of the Senator from Illinois 
(Ms. Duckworth) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2448, a bill to amend 
title 10, United States Code, to improve dependent coverage under the 
TRICARE Young Adult Program.


                                S. 3301

  At the request of Mr. Kelly, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Lujan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3301, a bill to prohibit 
purchases of certain semiconductor manufacturing equipment from foreign 
entities of concern or subsidiaries of foreign entities of concern, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 3329

  At the request of Ms. Warren, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3329, a bill to amend 
title 38, United States Code, to recognize and honor the service of 
individuals who served in the United States Cadet Nurse Corps during 
World War II, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4348

  At the request of Mr. Banks, the name of the Senator from Montana 
(Mr. Sheehy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4348, a bill to repeal the 
90/10 rule as it pertains to proprietary schools under title IV of the 
Higher Education Act of 1965.


                                S. 5685

  At the request of Ms. Warren, the name of the Senator from Oregon 
(Mr. Merkley) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5685, a bill to require 
the disclosure of supervisory records of certain financial institutions 
that relate to Jeffrey Epstein, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
