# Text of Senate Amendment 4339

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
  SA 4339. Mr. MERKLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 4308 proposed by Mr. Scott of South Carolina (for 
himself and Ms. Warren) to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the 
supply of housing in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 301, between lines 6 and 7, insert the following:
       (g) Disallowance of Certain Deductions for Large 
     Institutional Investors.--
       (1) Mortgage interest.--
       (A) In general.--Section 163 of the Internal Revenue Code 
     of 1986 is amended by redesignating subsection (n) as 
     subsection (o) and by inserting after subsection (m) the 
     following new subsection:
       ``(n) No Deduction for Large Institutional Investors.--No 
     deduction shall be allowed under this chapter with respect to 
     interest paid or accrued by a large institutional investor 
     (as defined in section 901(a)(4) of the 21st Century ROAD to 
     Housing Act).''.
       (2) Depreciation.--Section 167 of the Internal Revenue Code 
     of 1986 is amended by redesignating subsection (i) as 
     subsection (j) and by inserting after subsection (h) the 
     following new subsection:
       ``(i) No Deduction for Large Institutional Investors.--No 
     deduction shall be allowed under this section with respect to 
     any property held by a large institutional investor (as 
     defined in section 901(a)(4) of the 21st Century ROAD to 
     Housing Act).''.
       (3) Effective date.--The amendments made by this subsection 
     shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date that is 
     5 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
                                 ______
```
