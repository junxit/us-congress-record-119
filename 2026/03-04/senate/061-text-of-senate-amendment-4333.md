# Text of Senate Amendment 4333

**Speaking:** Slotkin, Elissa (S001208) [D-MI]

```
  SA 4333. Ms. SLOTKIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the supply of housing 
in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. EXPANSION OF DEFENSE PRODUCTION ACT OF 1950 TO 
                   ADDRESS HOUSING.

       The Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. 4501 et seq.) 
     is amended--
       (1) in section 2(a)(5) (50 U.S.C. 4502(a)(5)), by inserting 
     ``and residential construction and rehabilitation'' after 
     ``domestic energy''; and
       (2) in section 702(14) (50 U.S.C. 4552(14)), by inserting 
     ``, housing,'' after ``programs for military''.
                                 ______
```
