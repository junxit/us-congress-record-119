# Text of Senate Amendment 4326

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  SA 4326. Mr. SCHIFF (for himself and Ms. Ernst) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill 
to increase the supply of housing in America, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. BASE AND OVERTIME WAGES ASSOCIATED WITH REPAIR, 
                   RESTORATION AND REPLACEMENT OF DAMAGED 
                   FACILITIES.

       Section 406(a)(2)(D) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster 
     Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5172(a)(2)(D)) 
     is amended to read as follows:
       ``(D) base and overtime wages for extra hires utilized for 
     substantial damage determinations occurring within 180 days 
     after the major disaster is declared or, for all other 
     activities to facilitate the implementation and enforcement 
     of adopted building codes, for a period of not more than 2 
     years after a date selected by the recipient of the relevant 
     contribution.''.
                                 ______
```
