                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations en 
bloc: Calendar No. 657 through Calendar No. 660, Calendar No. 661 with 
the exception of Col. Thomas M. Siverts, Calendar No. 662, and Calendar 
No. 663, and all nominations on the Secretary's desk with the exception 
of PN681 Dustin B. Elliott, PN681 Vincent J. Noble, and PN835 Thomas P. 
MacNeil; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the motions to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
any of the nominations; that the President be immediately notified of 
the Senate's action; and that the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
