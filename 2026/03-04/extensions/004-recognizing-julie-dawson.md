# RECOGNIZING JULIE DAWSON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JULIE DAWSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 4, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Julie Dawson of 
Arvada, Colorado, for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2025 
Community Champion Award.
  Julie is the publisher for Best Version Media and is an irreplaceable 
cornerstone of the Arvada community. Julie champions small business 
leaders by building powerful connections that turn collaboration into 
real momentum. Her commitment to our community is exemplified by her 
monthly networking happy hours, which provide business owners with a 
valuable platform to build meaningful relationships and access key 
opportunities and resources. The Community Champion Award honors a 
person who demonstrates an exceptional commitment to serving the Arvada 
community, and Julie's dedication to empowering local businesses makes 
her truly deserving of this recognition.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Julie Dawson on receiving this award and 
thank her for her contributions to the Arvada community.
```
