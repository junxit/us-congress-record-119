# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 204, S. 601, S. 815, S. 933, H.R. 952, S. 1135, H.R. 1276, H.R. 1829, S. 1981, S. 2102, S. 2787, S. 3004, S. 3493, S. 3527, S. 3923

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
CURRENT READINESS OF THE JOINT FORCE
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Readiness and Management 
Support, concluded a hearing to examine the current readiness of the 
Joint Force after receiving testimony from General Christopher C. 
LaNeve, USA, Vice Chief of Staff of the Army, Admiral James W. Kilby, 
USN, Vice Chief of Naval Operations, General Bradford J. Gering, USMC, 
Assistant Commandant of the Marine Corps, General John D. Lamontagne, 
USAF, Vice Chief of Staff of the Air Force, and General Shawn N. 
Bratton, USSF, Vice Chief of Space Operations, all of the Department of 
Defense; and Diana C. Maurer, Director, Defense Capabilities and 
Management, Government Accountability Office.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee ordered 
favorably reported the following business items:
  S. 933, to authorize programs for the National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration for fiscal year 2025, with an amendment in the nature of 
a substitute; and
  S. 3923, to improve the weather research of the National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration, support improvements in weather forecasting 
and prediction, and expand commercial opportunities for the provision 
of weather data, with amendments.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: Committee ordered favorably 
reported the following business items:
  S. 601, to remove restrictions from a parcel of land in Paducah, 
Kentucky, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  H.R. 1276, to remove restrictions from a parcel of land in Paducah, 
Kentucky;
  S. 815, to designate the outdoor amphitheater at the Blue Ridge Music 
Center in Galax, Virginia, as the ``Rick Boucher Amphitheater'';
  S. 1135, to amend the National Trails System Act to direct the 
Secretary of the Interior to conduct a study on the feasibility of 
designating the Bonneville Shoreline Trail, with an amendment in the 
nature of a substitute, and an amendment to the title;
  S. 1981, to require the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of 
the Interior to utilize grazing for wildfire risk reduction, with an 
amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 2102, to establish the Ralph David Abernathy, Sr., National 
Historic Site;
  S. 2787, to amend the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 
to ensure that ranchers who have grazing agreements on national 
grasslands are treated the same as permittees on other Federal land, 
with an amendment;
  S. 3004, to direct the Secretary of the Interior to convey certain 
Bureau of Land Management land to the city of Price, Utah, with an 
amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 3493, to provide for the conveyance of certain Federal land in 
Carson City, Nevada, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 3527, to release from wilderness study area designation certain 
land in the State of Montana, to improve the management of that land, 
with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  H.R. 204, to require that the Secretary of Agriculture and the 
Secretary of the Interior submit accurate reports regarding hazardous 
fuels reduction activities;
  H.R. 952, to convey the reversionary interest of the United States in 
certain land in Sacramento, California;
  H.R. 1829, to require the Secretary of Agriculture to convey certain 
lands within the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, with an amendment 
in the nature of a substitute; and
  The nominations of Stevan Pearce, of New Mexico, to be Director of 
the Bureau of Land Management, David LaCerte, of Louisiana, to be a 
Member of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, and Kyle Haustveit, 
of North Dakota, to be Under Secretary of Energy.

BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee ordered favorably 
reported the nomination of the nomination of Douglas Weaver, of 
Maryland, to be a Member of the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
TOXIC SUBSTANCES CONTROL ACT FEE REAUTHORIZATION AND IMPROVEMENT ACT
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine a discussion draft of an original bill entitled, 
``the Toxic Substances Control Act Fee Reauthorization and Improvement 
Act of 2026'', after receiving testimony from Richard E. Engler, 
Bergeson and Campbell, P.C., on behalf of the Coalition for Chemical 
Innovations, David Isaacs, Semiconductor Industry Association, and 
Michal Ilana Freedhoff, Holland and Knight LLP, all of Washington, D.C.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
