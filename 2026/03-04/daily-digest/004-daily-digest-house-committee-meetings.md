# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2071, H.R. 2827, H.R. 4171, H.R. 6162, H.R. 6955, H.R. 7065, H.R. 7127, H.R. 7567, H.R. 7688

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Agriculture: Full Committee continued a markup on H.R. 
7567, the ``Farm, Food, and National Security Act of 2026''.
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, TRADE AND FOREIGN AGRICULTURAL AFFAIRS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural 
Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies held an 
oversight hearing on the Department of Agriculture, Trade and Foreign 
Agricultural Affairs. Testimony was heard from Luke Lindberg, Under 
Secretary for Trade and Foreign Agricultural Affairs, Department of 
Agriculture.
PREPARING FOR THE FUTURE: AMERICA'S COMMUNITY COLLEGES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled 
``Preparing for the Future: America's Community Colleges''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
SPEED TO SCALE: REVITALIZING THE DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL BASE
Committee on Armed Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Speed to Scale: Revitalizing the Defense Industrial Base''. Testimony 
was heard from Michael P. Duffey, Under Secretary of Defense for 
Acquisition and Sustainment, Department of Defense.
ENERGY, INSTALLATIONS, AND ENVIRONMENT UPDATE
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Readiness held a hearing 
entitled ``Energy, Installations, and Environment Update''. Testimony 
was heard from Dale Marks, Assistant Secretary of Defense for Energy, 
Installations, and Environment, Department of Defense; Jordan Gillis, 
Assistant Secretary of the Army for Installations, Energy, and 
Environment, Department of the Army; Brendan Rogers, Assistant 
Secretary of the Navy for Energy, Installations, and Environment, 
Department of the Navy; and Michael Borders, Assistant Secretary of the 
Air Force for Energy, Installations, and Environment, Department of the 
Air Force.
BUILDING AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-READY AMERICA: STRENGTHENING 
EMPLOYER-LED TRAINING
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Higher Education 
and Workforce Development held a hearing entitled ``Building an AI-
Ready America: Strengthening Employer-Led Training''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
AMERICA'S ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE: AUTHORIZING PIPELINE SAFETY
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Energy held a hearing 
entitled ``America's Energy Infrastructure: Authorizing Pipeline 
Safety''. Testimony was heard from Paul J. Roberti, Administrator, 
Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department of 
Transportation.
READY FOR REUSE: LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS TO UNLEASH THE POTENTIAL OF 
AMERICA'S BROWNFIELDS SITES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Environment held a 
hearing entitled ``Ready for Reuse: Legislative Proposals to Unleash 
the Potential of America's Brownfields Sites''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 
2071, the ``Save our Shrimpers Act''; H.R. 4171, the ``Small 
Entrepreneurs' Empowerment and Development Act of 2025''; H.R. 6955, 
the ``Main Street Capital Access Act''; H.R. 7127, the ``Restoring the 
Secondary Trading Market Act''; and H.R. 7688, the ``DPA Modernization 
Act of 2026''. H.R. 2071, H.R. 4171, H.R. 6955, H.R. 7127, and H.R. 
7688 were ordered reported, as amended.
HIGHWAY SAFETY UNDER THREAT: EXAMINING NON-DOMICILED COMMERCIAL 
DRIVER'S LICENSE ISSUANCE TO ILLEGAL ALIENS
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Oversight, 
Investigations, and Accountability held a hearing entitled ``Highway 
Safety Under Threat: Examining Non-Domiciled CDL Issuance to Illegal

[[Page D245]]

Aliens''. Testimony was heard from Tim Tipton, Commissioner, Department 
of Public Safety, Oklahoma; Richard R. Del Toro, Jr., Sheriff, St. 
Lucie County, Florida; and a public witness.
OVERSIGHT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY
Committee on the Judiciary: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Oversight of the Department of Homeland Security''. Testimony was 
heard from Kristi Noem, Secretary, Department of Homeland Security.
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Indian and Insular 
Affairs held a hearing on H.R. 2827, to provide for the equitable 
settlement of certain Indian land disputes regarding land in Illinois, 
and for other purposes; H.R. 6162, the ``Albuquerque Indian School Act 
of 2025''; and H.R. 7065, the ``Seneca Nation Law Enforcement 
Efficiency Act''. Testimony was heard from Chairman Cole, and 
Representatives Stansbury and Langworthy; Bryan Mercier, Director, 
Bureau of Indian Affairs, Department of the Interior; and public 
witnesses.
OVERSIGHT OF THE MIGRATORY BIRD TREATY ACT
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Water, Wildlife and 
Fisheries held a hearing entitled ``Oversight of the Migratory Bird 
Treaty Act''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
OVERSIGHT OF FRAUD AND MISUSE OF FEDERAL FUNDS IN MINNESOTA: PART II
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Full Committee held a 
hearing entitled ``Oversight of Fraud and Misuse of Federal Funds in 
Minnesota: Part II''. Testimony was heard from Keith Ellison, Attorney 
General, Minnesota; and Tim Walz, Governor, Minnesota.
THE GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION: EXAMINING THE FUTURE OF FEDERAL 
REAL ESTATE MANAGEMENT TO REDUCE COSTS FOR THE TAXPAYER
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Subcommittee on 
Economic Development, Public Buildings, and Emergency Management held a 
hearing entitled ``The General Services Administration: Examining the 
Future of Federal Real Estate Management to Reduce Costs for the 
Taxpayer''. Testimony was heard from Edward C. Forst, Administrator, 
General Services Administration.
DELIVERING FOR VETERANS AND CAREGIVERS: YEAR ONE OF THE DOLE ACT
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a hearing entitled ``Delivering for Veterans and 
Caregivers: Year One of the Dole Act''. Testimony was heard from Thomas 
O'Toole, Acting Assistant Under Secretary for Health for Clinical 
Services, Veterans Health Administration, Department of Veterans 
Affairs; and Sharon Silas, Director, Health Care, Government 
Accountability Office.
HEARING WITH INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, FRANK J. 
BISIGNANO
Committee on Ways and Means: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Hearing with Internal Revenue Service Chief Executive Officer, Frank 
J. Bisignano''. Testimony was heard from Frank J. Bisignano, Chief 
Executive Officer, Internal Revenue Service, Department of the 
Treasury.
COUNTERINTELLIGENCE SCREENING, VETTING, AND WATCH LISTING
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Full Committee held a 
hearing entitled ``CI Screening, Vetting, and Watch Listing''. 
Testimony was heard from Lauren Elinsky, Assistant Director, Mission 
Capabilities Directorate, National Counterintelligence and Security 
Center, Office of the Director of National Intelligence; John McHenry, 
National Security Agency, Department of Defense; Steve McQueen, 
Assistant Director, Threats Screening Center, Federal Bureau of 
Investigation, Department of Justice; Roman Rozhavsky, Assistant 
Director, Counterintelligence and Espionage Division, Federal Bureau of 
Investigation, Department of Justice; David Tomlinson, Assistant 
Director, Counterintelligence Directorate, National Counterintelligence 
and Security Center, Office of the Director of National Intelligence; 
and David Williams, National Security Agency, Department of Defense. 
This hearing was closed.

Joint Meetings

hearing with the House Committee on Veterans' Affairs to examine the 
legislative presentation of the American Legion, Paralyzed Veterans of 
America, AMVETS, National Association of State Directors of Veterans 
Affairs, Wounded Warrior Project, Tragedy Assistance Program for

CHINA'S INFILTRATION AND COERCION IN EUROPE
```
