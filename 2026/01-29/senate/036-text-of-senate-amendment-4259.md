# Text of Senate Amendment 4259

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4259. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 7148, making further consolidated appropriations 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 1132, line 21, strike ``$316,295,000'' and insert 
     ``$387,795''.
       On page 1133, line 14, insert before the period at the end 
     the following: ``: Provided further, That notwithstanding the 
     previous provisos, $42,900,000 shall be made available for 
     the Office of Civil Rights and Civil Liberties and 
     $28,600,000 shall be made available for the Office of the 
     Immigration Detention Ombudsman and such amounts shall be 
     obligated to restore the staff levels set forth in the 
     Department of Homeland Security Appropriations Act, 2024 
     (division C of Public Law 118-47)''.
                                 ______
```
