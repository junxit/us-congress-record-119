# Text of Senate Amendment 4263

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4263. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 7148, making further consolidated appropriations 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 1235, between lines 21 and 22, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 555.  All of the terms, conditions, purposes, and 
     amounts provided in the explanatory statement accompanying 
     this division, including the amounts for programs,

[[Page S384]]

     projects, and activities beginning on page 118 of the 
     explanatory statement, shall have the force and effect of 
     law, except that the language set forth in House Report 119-
     173 shall carry no legal force and effect.
                                 ______
```
