# Text of Senate Amendment 4261

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4261. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 7148, making further consolidated appropriations 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 1235, between lines 21 and 22, insert the following
       Sec. 555.  None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act or by any other Act may be used by the 
     Department of Homeland Security to support immigration 
     enforcement operations carried out by U.S. Immigration and 
     Customs Enforcement or U.S. Customs and Border Protection if 
     any Department of Homeland Security employee fails to 
     cooperate with an investigation by an appropriate State or 
     local official or agency relating to the use of force by any 
     employee of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement or U.S. 
     Customs and Border Protection.
                                 ______
```
