# Text of Senate Amendment 4265

**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL]

```
  SA 4265. Mr. SCOTT of Florida submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill H.R. 7148, making further consolidated 
appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in the matter preceding division 
     A, insert the following:

     SEC. __. STRIKE OF EARMARKS.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, none of 
     the funds provided under any division of this Act may be used 
     for Congressionally Directed Spending projects.
                                 ______
```
