# Text of Senate Amendment 4279

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
  SA 4279. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 7148, making further consolidated appropriations 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 1176, after line 17, add the following:
       Sec. 239.  No funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act or by any other Act may be used to 
     carry out any civil arrest or apprehension of any noncitizen, 
     including immigration enforcement actions authorized under 
     section 287 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 
     1357), without a judicial warrant issued by a Federal 
     district court or magistrate judge.
```
