# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of compassion, love, and mercy, You have reconciled us to 
yourself, generously and patiently offering us opportunity after 
opportunity to return to Your embrace.
  On this day, may we draw ever closer to You, to do Your will and not 
pursue our own desires, to strive to speak Your truth and not to bend 
our words to suit our story, and to serve as examples of faith and not 
as those paralyzed by fear.
  Soften our hardened hearts and ease our anxious minds that we would 
obey Your commandments and trust in Your provision. Guide us on the 
path You would have us go. Show us again the depth of Your steadfast 
and abiding love.
  May we respond with gratitude and delight, living this day and every 
day, reflecting the grace of Your unconditional love and sharing the 
peace of Your divine nature.
  In Your eternal name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
