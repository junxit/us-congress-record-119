# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2026

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2026

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on 
Tuesday, February 24; that following the prayer and pledge, Senator 
Kaine be recognized to deliver Washington's Farewell Address, as 
provided under the previous order; that following his remarks, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, that the cloture motion filed on February 12 
ripen at 5 p.m.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
