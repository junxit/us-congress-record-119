# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 3893, S. 3894

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. LEE (for himself, Mr. Durbin, Mr. Cramer, and 
             Ms. Hirono):
       S. 3893. A bill to amend the Foreign Intelligence 
     Surveillance Act of 1978 to reauthorize and reform certain 
     authorities and to provide greater transparency and 
     oversight; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mrs. SHAHEEN (for herself and Ms. Hassan):
       S. 3894. A bill to prohibit the Department of Homeland 
     Security from constructing, acquiring, renovating, or 
     operating any new processing site or detention center without 
     providing a mechanism for public comments regarding such 
     activity, entering into a signed, written agreement with 
     appropriate State and local officials, and providing Congress 
     with advance notice of such activity; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
