                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and were referred as 
indicated:

        EC-2775. A communication from the President of the United 
     States, transmitting, pursuant to law, a report of the 
     continuation of the national emergency that was originally 
     declared in Proclamation 6867 of March 1, 1996, with respect 
     to Cuba; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
        EC-2776. A message from the President of the United 
     States, transmitting, pursuant to law, a report of the 
     continuation of the national emergency that was originally 
     declared in Executive Order 13660 of March 6, 2014, with 
     respect to Ukraine; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
     Urban Affairs.
        EC-2777. A message from the President of the United 
     States, transmitting, pursuant to law, a report of the 
     continuation of the national emergency that was originally 
     declared in Executive Order 13692 of March 8, 2015, with 
     respect to the situation in Venezuela; to the Committee on 
     Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
