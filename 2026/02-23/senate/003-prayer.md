# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Creator God, by Your will, we came into being, and at Your command, 
we shall one day transition into eternity.
  Let Your Spirit lead our Senators today. May they increase in 
humility, integrity, and faith so that each day they will approach 
nearer to Your likeness. Lord, help them to offer themselves afresh to 
be used in Your service.
  Show them Your ways, guide them on Your path, and empower them to 
obey Your precepts. Give them wisdom when perplexed, vigor when 
discouraged, and guidance when they seek Your will.
  And, Lord, we thank You for the courage, commitment, and compassion 
in the life and legacy of Jesse Jackson. We pray in Your merciful name. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
