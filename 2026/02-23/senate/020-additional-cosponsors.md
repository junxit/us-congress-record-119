# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 1552, S. 1594, S. 1720, S. 1973, S. 2503, S. 3837

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1552

  At the request of Mr. Cotton, the names of the Senator from Illinois 
(Ms. Duckworth) and the Senator from Alabama (Mr. Tuberville) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 1552, a bill to promote and protect from 
discrimination living organ donors.


                                S. 1594

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from 
Delaware (Mr. Coons) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1594, a bill to 
amend the Lacey Act Amendments of 1981 to prohibit certain activities 
involving prohibited primate species, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1720

  At the request of Mr. Cassidy, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Ms. Slotkin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1720, a bill to amend 
title XIX of the Social Security Act to remove the Medicaid coverage 
exclusion for inmates in custody pending disposition of charges, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 1973

  At the request of Mr. Cassidy, the name of the Senator from Illinois 
(Ms. Duckworth) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1973, a bill to amend 
title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide for the coordination 
of programs to prevent and treat obesity, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2503

  At the request of Mr. Cruz, the names of the Senator from Utah (Mr. 
Curtis), the Senator from Maryland (Mr. Van Hollen), the Senator from 
Vermont (Mr. Welch) and the Senator from Delaware (Ms. Blunt Rochester) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 2503, a bill to require all aircraft to 
be equipped with Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast In, to 
improve aviation safety, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3837

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the name of the Senator from Iowa (Mr. 
Grassley) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3837, a bill to designate the 
clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Des Moines, Iowa, as 
the ``Staff Sergeant Edgar Torres-Tovar VA Clinic'' and to designate 
the community-based outpatient clinic of the Department in 
Marshalltown, Iowa, as the ``Staff Sergeant William Nathaniel Howard VA 
Clinic''.

                          ____________________
```
