  Democrats will not stand for it. And, in fact, the new tariff regime 
that

                       Tribute to Roger S. Penske

  Mr. MORENO. Mr. President, I rise today with deep personal gratitude 
and profound admiration to honor a legendary American, Roger S. Penske. 
This past Friday, February 20, marked his 89th birthday.
  Born in Shaker Heights, OH, Roger Penske has made America better and 
has touched the lives of millions of Americans in an extraordinarily 
positive way. As we celebrate this occasion, we also mark the 60th 
anniversary of Team Penske, the racing dynasty he founded at the age of 
29 in February of 1966. Team Penske has defined excellence, innovation, 
and triumph in motor sports, unlike any other racing program in U.S. 
history.
  Roger's journey is truly inspiring. At the age of 21, he became a 
champion driver, capturing the SCCA national title. He competed in 
Formula 1. He competed in NASCAR, and, as I just mentioned, he competed 
in the SCCA.
  In 1961, he was named the Sports Illustrated Driver of the Year. His 
success on the track earned him the enduring nickname of ``The 
Captain'' for his unmatched strategic mind and competitive fire.
  After an incredible racing career, he turned his attention to 
building Team Penske into one of the most successful teams in the 
history of any professional sport. The results are truly staggering. 
Let's go through them: a Formula 1 victory, three Daytona 500 wins, a 
record-setting 20 Indianapolis 500 victories.
  You can see that photograph here--20 times won the Indianapolis 500. 
By the way, it is incredible to win it once; to win it 20 times is 
unheard of.
  Forty-eight national championships, over 700 pole positions, more 
than 650 major wins across INDYCAR, NASCAR, and IMSA, along with other 
racing series--this includes victories in the 24 Hours of Daytona, the 
12 hours of Sebring, along with a win in Australia's legendary Bathurst 
1000 race.
  More than 100 drivers have raced for Team Penske over the course of 
six decades, includes legends such as Mark Donohue, Rick Mears, Rusty 
Wallace, and Al Unser.
  Just recently, Roger bought and revitalized the Indianapolis Motor 
Speedway, which hosts the single largest 1-day sporting event on the 
planet, the Indy 500. This acquisition assured this iconic venue's 
future for generations of fans.
  And those are just Roger Penske's accomplishments on the racetrack. 
He leads Penske Corporation, a global powerhouse in transportation, 
logistics, and retail automotive, employing tens of thousands of 
Americans, with operations in every single State. Penske Automotive 
Group is the largest automobile dealership owner on Earth, selling more 
new vehicles in more places than any other single organization. It also 
owns the single largest automobile dealership on the planet, Longo 
Toyota, which sells almost 20,000 new vehicles in a single location per 
year.
  In 2019, Roger's extraordinary contributions earned him the 
Presidential Medal of Freedom, the Nation's highest civilian honor, 
given to only 674 Americans in our entire history. This award 
celebrated not just his dominance in racing and industry but his 
embodiment of American entrepreneurship, patriotism, and excellence--
qualities that continue to shine brightly.
  And, fittingly, Roger's legacy ties directly into our Nation's 
upcoming milestone. As part of America's 250th anniversary this year, 
he is spearheading the Freedom 250 DC Grand Prix, a groundbreaking 
INDYCAR street race around the National Mall and our iconic monuments 
on August 21 to 23. This historic, first ever motor race in our 
Nation's Capital will showcase the majesty of this great city, honor 
our independence, and highlight the innovation and pride of American 
motor sports. It is a fitting tribute to a man whose life has been 
dedicated to speed, strategy, and service to this great country.
  Roger Penske is more than a racer, more than a businessman, more than 
a philanthropist supporting education, veterans, and communities in 
Ohio and beyond. He is the best of what America represents: grit, 
integrity, and an unyielding belief in doing things the right way.
  My own connection to Roger Penske goes back two decades. I bought my 
first automobile dealership from him, a pivotal moment in my life. That 
taught me firsthand the values that underpin the Penske way: Effort 
equals results.
  I have gotten to know Roger Penske, I have gotten to know his family, 
I have gotten to know his company, and I have gotten to know his team. 
They are utterly amazing, and his attention to detail is impossible to 
capture with words.
  It is why George Costanza, in a memorable Seinfeld episode, was 
declared as ``most definitely not Penske material.'' But in fairness to 
George Costanza, that is a high bar that many of us would find hard to 
achieve.
  As the U.S. Senator from Ohio, his birthplace, and someone whose path 
was forever changed by him, I am honored to salute Roger Penske on his 
89th birthday and the 60th anniversary of Team Penske.
  Thank you, Captain, for your friendship, for your inspiration, and 
for showing all of us what it means to win with class and with heart.
  Happy birthday, Roger. Here is to many more victories on and off the 
track.
  I yield the floor.

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I rise today alongside my companion and 
friend and colleague, the senior Senator from Ohio, to honor and 
recognize a great American, and that great American would be Roger 
Penske.
  Last week, Roger celebrated his 89th birthday. Team Penske celebrated 
its 60th year. Both Roger and Team Penske have very much of which to be 
proud; yet Roger is going to be the last person ever to discuss his 
remarkable achievements.
  His guiding principle is ``effort equals results.'' Now, that is a 
phrase those of us from Wyoming understand very well. ``Effort equals 
results'' simply means that a hard day's work equals real rewards. It 
also means extra efforts yield exceptional results. Honesty, hard work, 
and integrity are going to get you far in life, and Roger Penske 
continues to go the full distance.
  Now, as a teenager, Roger got into the car business--buying and 
fixing up old cars. Cars were his calling. Even during college, at 
Lehigh University in Pennsylvania, he was successfully racing 
automobiles. He had a successful racing career, a successful career as 
an owner of a fleet of car dealerships, and a successful career helming 
Penske Truck Rental. He employs tens of thousands of Americans, and he 
employs people in all 50 States. The Penske name is synonymous with 
success, with excellence, and with moving America forward. Effort 
equals results.
  As Roger grew more and more successful in business, racing was always 
on his mind. So, 60 years ago this month, at the age of 29, in 1966, 
Team Penske was born. Racing is where Roger's passion comes alive. Team 
Penske debuted at the 24 Hours of Daytona. They haven't hit the brakes 
in 60 years. To use a racing term, Roger is still ``standing on the gas 
pedal.''
  Roger is known in racing as the Captain, and the Captain's leadership 
has made Team Penske the most successful racing team in automobile 
history. Their drivers have had more than 600 victories all across 
various forms of racing. Team Penske has won a record-setting 20 
Indianapolis 500 races and, this year, a historic three-peat at the 24 
Hours of Daytona. Effort equals results.
  Just like race car driving itself, the Penske name is woven into the 
fabric

celebrations. In this historic year, Roger Penske is spearheading the
