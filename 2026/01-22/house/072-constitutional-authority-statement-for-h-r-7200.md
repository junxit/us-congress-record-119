# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7200

**Measures:** H.R. 7200

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
        H.R. 7200.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, clause 3 of the United States 
     Constitution (To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes); and 
     Article I, section 8, clause 4 of the United States 
     Constitution (To establish an uniform Rule of Naturalization, 
     and uniform Laws on the subject of Bankruptcies throughout 
     the United States).
```
