# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 1014

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Proceedings will resume on questions 
previously postponed. Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Ordering the previous question on House Resolution 1014 and on the 
amendment offered by the gentlewoman from North Carolina;
  Adoption of the amendment offered by the gentlewoman from North 
Carolina, if ordered; and
  Adoption of House Resolution 1014, if ordered.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, remaining electronic votes will be 
conducted as 5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
