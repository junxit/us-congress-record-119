# END HUNGER NOW

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
                             END HUNGER NOW

  (Mr. McGOVERN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, local grocers play a pivotal role in 
improving access to nutritious food. That is why I joined my dear 
friend and colleague, Congresswoman Ayanna Pressley, and State and 
local leaders on a visit to El Valle de la Sultana Market in Everett, 
Massachusetts, last Friday as part of my statewide End Hunger Now Tour.
  Victor and Martha Gutierrez and Audriana Perdomo own La Sultana 
markets, which have been providing cultural staples to the people of 
Everett, east Boston, and Somerville for 30 years. We visited the 
newest location in Everett, which was stocked with delicious and 
culturally appropriate foods like fresh produce and meat and heard 
directly from people who shop at the store for groceries.
  Their message was loud and clear. Skyrocketing food prices and cuts 
to SNAP are impacting their ability to feed their families. Their 
powerful stories provide me further motivation to fight Trump policies 
that are driving up the cost of food and to once and for all end hunger 
now.

                          ____________________
```
