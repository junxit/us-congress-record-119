# REPEAL SECTION 230

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 7045

```
                           REPEAL SECTION 230

  (Mr. PATRONIS asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to urge my colleagues to 
support H.R. 7045, the PROTECT Act, because it is time to repeal 
section 230 and hold Big Tech accountable for the harm being done to 
our children.
  As a father of two young boys, I refuse to stand by and allow harm to 
happen to our children when there are no consequences, and these 
companies walk away without responsibility.
  Big Tech has become a digital fentanyl that is slowly killing our 
children. It is pushing parents to the sidelines. It is acting as 
therapists. It is replacing relationships with robots. This must stop.
  Parents are fighting a system designed to replace them. Algorithms 
and chatbots fuel addiction, depression, self-harm, and even suicide, 
all while these companies hide behind section 230.
  When social media leads our kids to suicidal thoughts, our children 
believe they are better off gone, and no one is held responsible. This 
is a deeply broken system.
  I demand accountability. I am standing up for our children, and I am 
saying that the double standard ends now.

                          ____________________
```
