# HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ANCHOR BAY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
  HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ANCHOR BAY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 22, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate 
the 50th Anniversary of the Anchor Bay Chamber of Commerce, 
commemorating five decades of service, connection, and community growth 
in the Anchor Bay region.
  Founded in 1976, the Anchor Bay Chamber of Commerce has remained 
steadfast in its mission to support local businesses, foster 
collaboration, and enhance the quality of life throughout the 
community. Over the past half century, the Chamber has grown into a 
trusted resource and a strong voice for business, providing meaningful 
networking opportunities, advocacy, and community events that bring 
people together and strengthen local ties.
  Today, with more than 425 members, the Anchor Bay Chamber of Commerce 
continues to embody its guiding principle to ``Anchor Your Business in 
the Community.'' Through partnerships, leadership development, and 
innovation, the Chamber plays a vital role in promoting economic 
vitality while reinforcing the importance of community engagement.
  As the Anchor Bay Chamber of Commerce marks this significant 
milestone, we honor its rich history and dedicated leadership while 
looking ahead to the next 50 years of growth and opportunity. The 
Chamber's continued commitment to strengthening both business and 
community serves as a model of excellence and civic responsibility.
  Mr. Speaker, I extend my sincere congratulations to the Anchor Bay 
Chamber of Commerce, its members, staff, and ambassadors on this 
remarkable achievement, and I wish them continued success in the years 
to come.
```
