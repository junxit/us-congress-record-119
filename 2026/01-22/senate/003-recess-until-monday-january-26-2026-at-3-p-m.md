# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 26, 2026, AT 3 P.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 26, 2026, AT 3 P.M.

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands in recess until 3 p.m. on Monday, January 26, 2026.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 16 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, January 26, 2026, at 3 p.m.
```
