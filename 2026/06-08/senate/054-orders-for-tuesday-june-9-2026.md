# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 9, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 9, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Tuesday, June 9; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, that the 
Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. tomorrow for the weekly 
conference meetings; and that the Senate proceed to executive session 
to execute the order on the Mattivi nomination at 2:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
