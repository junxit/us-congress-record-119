# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we give our hearts to You, for You are good and Your 
mercies endure forever. You rule the day and the night. You are our 
hope for the years to come.
  Lord, we thank You for Your faithfulness, Your guidance, and Your 
unfailing love. Strengthen our lawmakers when they are weak, give them 
wisdom when they are uncertain, and give them peace in every season of 
their lives. Help them to trust You with their cares, to walk in Your 
ways, and to reflect Your goodness to those around them. Lord, bless 
their families and all who are in need of Your grace. May Your presence 
comfort our Senators and Your truth lead them each day.
  And, Lord, thank You for our new summer 2026 page class.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
