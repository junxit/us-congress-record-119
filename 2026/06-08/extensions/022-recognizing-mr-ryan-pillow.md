# RECOGNIZING MR. RYAN PILLOW

**Speaking:** Moylan, James C. (M001219) [R-GU]

```
                      RECOGNIZING MR. RYAN PILLOW

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JAMES C. MOYLAN

                                of guam

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2026

  Mr. MOYLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor a 
remarkable public servant whose career has been defined by courage, 
leadership, and an unwavering commitment to protecting others--Mr. Ryan 
Douglas Pillow--upon his retirement after more than 26 years of 
combined military and federal civilian service to the United States.
  For over two decades, Ryan Pillow has served at Andersen Air Force 
Base on Guam as a federal firefighter with the Department of the Air 
Force, ultimately rising to the position of Assistant Fire Chief of 
Health and Safety.
  Before beginning his civilian career, Mr. Pillow proudly served four 
years in the United States Navy, continuing a lifetime dedicated to 
serving and protecting our Nation.
  Throughout his distinguished career, Ryan Pillow has been entrusted 
with some of the most challenging and high-risk responsibilities 
imaginable. As an Incident Commander and emergency response leader, he 
has overseen operations involving strategic military aircraft, critical 
infrastructure, and life-threatening emergencies under some of the most 
demanding conditions.
  His operational experience includes leading emergency responses 
involving B-1 , B-2, and B-52 strategic bombers--some of the most 
critical assets in our Nation's defense posture. He also led emergency 
operations involving fighter aircraft incidents, including assisting in 
the emergency egress of a fighter pilot and managing high-risk airfield 
emergencies involving F-18 aircraft.
  But his service extended far beyond the runway. Mr. Pillow also led 
life-saving technical rescue operations, including over-the-cliff 
rescues, structural fire emergencies, and water rescue missions. During 
major typhoon events impacting Guam, he served as Incident Commander, 
helping ensure the protection of personnel, mission-essential 
infrastructure, and critical defense capabilities during some of the 
island's most dangerous conditions.
  And during the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, he once again 
demonstrated steady leadership by helping manage initial emergency 
response operations aimed at protecting personnel and establishing 
effective containment measures during an unprecedented national crisis.
  These responsibilities required not only technical expertise, but 
calm under pressure, decisive leadership, and complete trust from the 
men and women he served alongside. Throughout his career, Ryan Pillow 
earned that trust time and time again.
  In addition to his operational leadership, he played a critical role 
in strengthening firefighter health and safety programs, improving 
emergency response readiness, and fostering mutual aid partnerships 
that enhanced regional emergency response capabilities throughout Guam.
  His extraordinary service has been recognized through numerous 
distinguished honors,

[[Page E541]]

including the Navy Life-Saving Award, the Exemplary Civilian Service 
Medal, the Air Force Achievement Award, and recognition as Fire Officer 
of the Year.
  But beyond the awards and titles, Mr. Pillow represents something 
larger. He represents the thousands of military and civilian first 
responders who work behind the scenes every single day to protect 
lives, defend critical infrastructure, and ensure mission readiness in 
one of the most strategically important regions in the world.
  The people of Guam understand deeply the importance of those who 
stand ready in moments of crisis. Whether responding to aircraft 
emergencies, natural disasters, fires, or national emergencies, 
professionals like Ryan Pillow provide the steady leadership and 
courage that keep our communities and our servicemembers safe. His 
career reflects the very best traditions of public service: duty before 
self, leadership under pressure, and a lifelong commitment to 
protecting others.
  As he retires after more than 26 years of honorable service, we 
recognize not only the accomplishments of an exceptional firefighter 
and leader, but the legacy of a man who dedicated his career to serving 
something greater than himself.
  On behalf of the people of Guam and a grateful Nation, I extend my 
deepest gratitude to Mr. Ryan Douglas Pillow for his extraordinary 
service and sacrifice. I also extend appreciation to his family, whose 
support undoubtedly made this long and distinguished career possible.
  We wish Ryan Pillow the very best in this next chapter of life and 
thank him for the lasting impact he has made on Guam and our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
