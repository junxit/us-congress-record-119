# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF HISTORIC ROUTE 66

**Speaking:** Smith, Jason (S001195) [R-MO]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF HISTORIC ROUTE 66

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. JASON SMITH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2026

  Mr. SMITH of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 100th 
anniversary of Route 66 and its special connection to Rolla, Missouri. 
Known as ``The Mother Road,'' Route 66 has long been central to Rolla's 
identity as a transportation hub, strengthened by its location between 
St. Louis and Springfield.
  Route 66 was first paved through Rolla in 1928, earning the city the 
distinction of being the ``Inaugural City'' of the final pavement 
project. Originally running through downtown, Route 66 brought new life 
to the area through increased commerce and tourism. As automobile 
travel expanded in the 1940s, the route became a symbol of American 
prosperity, fueling significant economic growth in Rolla as new 
businesses emerged to serve travelers.
  Though Route 66 was eventually replaced by Interstate 44, portions of 
the original roadway remain in Rolla and across the country, preserving 
this historic landmark for future generations. Today, Rolla proudly 
celebrates its Route 66 heritage through preserved historic areas, 
local attractions, and community events like the annual Route 66 
Summerfest. In recognition of this milestone, the Mayor of Rolla has 
proclaimed 2026 the ``Route 66 Centennial Year,'' honoring the city's 
longstanding role along the route.
  It is my privilege to recognize the enduring connection between Route 
66 and the City of Rolla, and I ask my colleagues to join me in 
commemorating this iconic piece of American history.
```
