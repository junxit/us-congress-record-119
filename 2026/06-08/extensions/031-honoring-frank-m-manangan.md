# HONORING FRANK M. MANANGAN

**Speaking:** Moylan, James C. (M001219) [R-GU]

```
                       HONORING FRANK M. MANANGAN

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JAMES C. MOYLAN

                                of guam

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2026

  Mr. MOYLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a remarkable 
member of our Guam community, Mr. Frank M. Manangan, who recently 
celebrated an extraordinary milestone--his 100th birthday.
  A century of life is a rare and remarkable achievement. It is a 
testament not only to longevity, but to a life filled with purpose, 
perseverance, and dedication to others. Today, we honor Mr. Manangan 
not simply because he has reached the age of one hundred, but because 
of the legacy he has built throughout those one hundred years.
  Mr. Manangan was born on April 2, 1925, in San Antonio, Zambales, 
Philippines. Raised in humble surroundings, he learned early in life 
the values of hard work, discipline, and service. After graduating from 
high school, he joined the local police force, proudly serving his 
country and community with honor. Even as a young man, he demonstrated 
a commitment to helping others and fulfilling responsibilities larger 
than himself.
  Like many who came before him, Mr. Manangan eventually sought new 
opportunities and a brighter future. He made the journey to Guam 
carrying with him determination, resilience, and a strong work ethic 
that would define the rest of his life. He arrived ready to work, ready 
to provide, and ready to build a future for his family.
  Over the years, he contributed to Guam in many ways. He worked as a 
carpenter, applying his skill and craftsmanship to help build and 
maintain the community around him. He worked in warehouse operations 
and later transitioned into data entry. No matter the position, he 
approached every job with diligence, humility, and pride. His 
willingness to work hard and adapt throughout different stages of life 
speaks to the character that has earned him the respect of so many.
  But perhaps Mr. Manangan's greatest contribution was the family he 
helped build.
  Together with his beloved wife, he raised ten children. As the sole 
provider for his family, he worked tirelessly--often holding two jobs 
at the same time--to ensure that his children had the necessities of 
life and the opportunity to pursue their own dreams. The sacrifices he 
made were countless. Like so many parents, he placed the needs of his 
family above his own, day after day and year after year. Through his 
example, he taught his children the values of perseverance, 
responsibility, and unconditional love.
  Those who know Mr. Manangan know that his legacy extends far beyond 
his work ethic. They know him as a man who brought people together.
  One of his greatest passions was cooking. With ten children to feed, 
he learned how to create delicious meals while making the most of every 
dollar. His home was often filled with the aromas of traditional 
Filipino dishes, and over time his pancit became a favorite among 
family members and friends alike. It became a staple at gatherings and 
celebrations, where loved ones would eagerly look forward to enjoying 
one of his signature dishes. Through food, he expressed care, 
generosity, and a deep love for those around him.
  It is often said that a person's true wealth is measured not by what 
they possess, but by the lives they touch. By that measure, Mr. 
Manangan's life has been extraordinarily rich. His children, 
grandchildren, great-grandchildren, relatives, friends, and neighbors 
have all benefited from the example he has set and the values he has 
passed down through generations.
  Today, as a centenarian, Mr. Manangan stands as a living reminder of 
the power of hard work, sacrifice, faith, and family. His story 
reflects the experiences of so many who came to Guam seeking 
opportunity and who, through determination and perseverance, helped 
strengthen our island community. Yet his story is uniquely his own--a 
story marked by service, devotion, and an enduring commitment to those 
he loved most.
  On behalf of the people of Guam and the United States House of 
Representatives, I extend my deepest congratulations to Mr. Frank M. 
Manangan on reaching this extraordinary milestone. We thank him for a 
lifetime of contributions, for the family he has raised, and for the 
example he continues to provide for future generations.
  Happy 100th Birthday to Mr. Frank M. Manangan. May he continue to be 
blessed with good health, happiness, and the love of family and 
friends.

                          ____________________
```
