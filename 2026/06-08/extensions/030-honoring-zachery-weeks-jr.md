# HONORING ZACHERY WEEKS, JR.

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                      HONORING ZACHERY WEEKS, JR.

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and congratulate Zackery Weeks, Jr., an outstanding member of the 
Humphreys County High School Class of 2026, whose academic excellence, 
leadership, and dedication have distinguished him as one of the 
brightest young scholars in his community.
  Zackery has earned the title of Humphreys County Star Student, a 
recognition that reflects his exceptional academic achievement and 
commitment to excellence. With an impressive 32 on the ACT and 1400 on 
the SAT, Zackery stands among the top-performing students in the 
region. His accomplishments in the classroom demonstrate not only 
intellectual ability, but also discipline, perseverance, and a strong 
work ethic.
  Beyond academics, Zackery has shown leadership and character through 
his service as Chess Captain, where he has led with strategy, poise, 
and critical thinking. These qualities will serve him well as he 
continues his educational journey.
  Zackery's hard work has resulted in extraordinary opportunities. He 
has received more than $5.2 million in scholarship offers and more than 
80 college acceptances. This fall, he will attend The University of 
Southern Mississippi on a full-ride scholarship, where he will continue 
building on his record of success while proudly representing Humphreys 
County.
  Zackery is the beloved son of Lakisha Hinnant and Zackery Weeks, Sr., 
and the proud grandson of Pastor Kevin and Laura Hinnant of Belzoni, 
Mississippi; Felicia Allen of Selma, North Carolina; and the late 
Bronica Lane. He is also admired by his younger siblings, Serenity and 
Victory Weeks, who look up to him with pride and excitement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Zackery 
Weeks, Jr. for his remarkable achievements. His dedication, faith, and 
determination are a source of pride to his family, his school, and the 
entire Humphreys County community. I look forward to seeing all that he 
will accomplish in the years ahead.
```
