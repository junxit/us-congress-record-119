# RECOGNIZING MAY AS HUNTINGTON'S DISEASE AWARENESS MONTH

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

```
        RECOGNIZING MAY AS HUNTINGTON'S DISEASE AWARENESS MONTH

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2026

  Ms. STEVENS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize May as 
Huntington's Disease Awareness Month. As representatives in Congress, 
we hold the tremendous responsibility of representing our constituents 
bravely battling this condition.
  Huntington's Disease (HD) is a fatal genetic disorder--with no 
treatment that slows or stops the progression--that causes the 
breakdown of nerve cells in the brain. For over 30 years, we as a 
Nation have honored this month by ``going blue'' in support of our 
loved ones with Huntington's Disease. In that time, we have identified 
root causes, defined metrics for early detection, and had revolutionary 
breakthroughs in treatment. We have come a long way, but we have much 
more to accomplish in this fight.
  Given the genetic nature of Huntington's Disease, it often impacts 
families for generations. Christina, one of my constituents, has 
endured the loss of her grandmother and the pain of watching her father 
and aunt battle this disease. All too familiar with the uncertainty 
this disease brings, she is committed to breaking the cycle and has 
become an advocate for all Americans with HD.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to represent and amplify the fighting 
spirit of the Huntington's Disease community in Congress. I call upon 
my colleagues to join Christina and myself in committing to greater 
federal support for brave Americans with Huntington's Disease.

                          ____________________
```
