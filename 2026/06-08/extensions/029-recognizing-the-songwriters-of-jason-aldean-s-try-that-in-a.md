# RECOGNIZING THE SONGWRITERS OF JASON ALDEAN'S "TRY THAT IN A SMALL TOWN"

**Speaking:** Smith, Jason (S001195) [R-MO]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE SONGWRITERS OF JASON ALDEAN'S ``TRY THAT IN A SMALL 
                                 TOWN''

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. JASON SMITH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2026

  Mr. SMITH of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
songwriters of Jason Aldean's hit song ``Try That in a Small Town.'' 
Neil Thrasher, Kurt Allison, Tully Kennedy, and Kelley Lovelace created 
a song that deeply resonated with millions of Americans, highlighting 
the all-American spirit of patriotism and resilience found in small 
town USA.
  When ``Try That in a Small Town'' was released in 2023, the song made 
waves across the country, eventually reaching No. 1 on the all-genre 
Billboard Hot 100 chart and No. 1 on the Billboard Hot Country Songs 
chart. At a moment when division and hate seemed to dominate discourse, 
this song sparked conversations about American patriotism when we need 
it most. Thrasher, Allison, Kennedy, and Lovelace used their 
storytelling talents to bring people together across generations.
  Beyond the success of this single song, Neil Thrasher, Kurt Allison, 
Tully Kennedy, and Kelley Lovelace have each built careers that have 
left a true mark on modern country music. Their music has climbed 
charts and become an integral part of the all-American way of life. 
More importantly, each of them brings a deep appreciation for honest 
storytelling that captures the voices and values of our great Nation.
  Together, these four songwriters embody the heart of American 
patriotism. Their collaboration on ``Try That in a Small Town'' 
reflects the same integrity and passion that define this country. Their 
songwriting embodies what country music does best--tell the truth about 
who rural communities are and what we stand for.

                          ____________________
```
