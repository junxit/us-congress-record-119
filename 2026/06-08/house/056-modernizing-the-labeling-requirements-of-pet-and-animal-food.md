# MODERNIZING THE LABELING REQUIREMENTS OF PET AND ANIMAL FOOD

**Speaking:** Schmidt, Derek (S001228) [R-KS]

```
      MODERNIZING THE LABELING REQUIREMENTS OF PET AND ANIMAL FOOD

  (Mr. SCHMIDT asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)

[[Page H3978]]

  

  Mr. SCHMIDT. Mr. Speaker, I am grateful that the House last week 
approved the FY27 agriculture appropriations bill, the second FY27 
appropriations bill brought to the floor under regular order.
  The bill we passed contained the PURR Act, which is a bill I have 
championed to modernize the labeling requirements of pet and animal 
food.
  The Kansas Second District is the pet and animal food capital of 
America, employing nearly 20,000 people in all aspects of the industry, 
from nutrition research and development to production.
  Overall, the animal feed and pet food industries annually contribute 
nearly $100 billion in value to the U.S. economy and more than 750,000 
jobs.
  Modernizing the labeling requirements on these products will improve 
efficiency and reduce compliance costs for pet food manufacturers. It 
will strengthen the industry. This will have a direct benefit for the 
affordability of pet food for consumers and animal feed for our farmers 
and ranchers.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank the Appropriations Committee for including this 
important measure from my district in the bill. I look forward to the 
Senate advancing it and the President signing it into law.
```
