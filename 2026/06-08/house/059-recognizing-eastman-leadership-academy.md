# RECOGNIZING EASTMAN LEADERSHIP ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                 RECOGNIZING EASTMAN LEADERSHIP ACADEMY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, it is a proud history, a 
new beginning, and a brighter future for eastern North Carolina.
  After closing in 2009, the historic Eastman School in Halifax County 
is reopening its doors as Eastman Leadership Academy. From its humble 
beginnings in 1894, serving generations of families, to closing due to 
consolidation, Eastman's story has always been about community.
  Soon, students will fill these hallways again: learning, growing, and 
writing the next chapter of this incredible legacy.
  With the dedicated vision of Superintendent Cunningham, the Halifax 
County Board of Education, and the Board of Commissioners, the school 
will serve students in kindergarten through eighth grade.
  Eastman's reopening is a reminder that when communities invest in 
education, they are investing in new opportunities, leadership, and the 
future.
```
