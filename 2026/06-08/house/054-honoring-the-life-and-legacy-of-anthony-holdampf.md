# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ANTHONY HOLDAMPF

**Speaking:** Lawler, Michael (L000599) [R-NY]

```
                              {time}  1920
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ANTHONY HOLDAMPF

  (Mr. LAWLER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LAWLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Anthony Holdampf, a longtime firefighter, veteran, and public servant 
who passed away on May 30 at the age of 88.
  Tony was a proud veteran of the 101st Airborne Division and dedicated 
decades of his life to serving others, first in uniform and then 
through a distinguished 37-year career with the FDNY.
  During the FDNY's war years of the 1960s and 70s, Tony served on the 
front lines with Engine 43 and Ladder 19 in the south Bronx. He later 
became a charter member of the Tactical Control Unit 712, an elite unit 
assigned to some of the city's most dangerous fires.
  After retiring from the FDNY, Tony continued serving his community as 
a life member of the Volunteer Hose Company No. 2 in West Haverstraw 
and was honored with the Rockland County Fireman of the Year award.
  His commitment to the fire service inspired future generations, 
including his son, David, and his son-in-law, Robert, both FDNY 
firefighters.
  Whether helping a neighbor, supporting his fellow firefighters, or 
serving his community, Tony always answered the call. He embodied the 
very best of service, courage, and selflessness. My thoughts and 
prayers are with his beloved wife, Mary Ellen; his children; 
grandchildren; and all who had the privilege of knowing him.
```
