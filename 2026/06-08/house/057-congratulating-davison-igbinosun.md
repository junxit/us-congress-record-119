# CONGRATULATING DAVISON IGBINOSUN

**Speaking:** McIver, LaMonica (M001229) [D-NJ]

```
                    CONGRATULATING DAVISON IGBINOSUN

  (Mrs. McIVER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Davison Igbinosun 
for being selected by the Buffalo Bills with the 62nd pick in the 2026 
NFL draft.
  Davison is a graduate of Union High School and was New Jersey's top 
recruit in the 2022 high school football class. Davison began his 
college football journey at Ole Miss before transferring to Ohio State. 
There he developed into one of the Big Ten's top defensive backs and a 
pivotal player on one of America's strongest college football defenses.
  In his three seasons at Ohio State, Davison started 42 of 43 games. 
He was a First-Team All-Big Ten selection in 2025.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of New Jersey's 10th Congressional District, I 
congratulate Davison as he continues to represent Union High School and 
embarks on his professional football career.

                          ____________________
```
