# FIGHTING ISLAMOPHOBIA

```
                         FIGHTING ISLAMOPHOBIA

  (Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today as it was only 18 
days ago when two gunmen opened fire at the Islamic Center of San 
Diego, killing three, injuring one, and inflicting terror on not just a 
community but to Americans everywhere.
  Since taking office, I have met with religious communities across my 
incredibly diverse Sixth District of Maryland, including many of our 
Islamic community centers. My Muslim neighbors not only fear this 
violence spreading to Maryland but are concerned they must prepare for 
it.
  Today, I share their concerns and a request to fight Islamophobia in 
this body. Sadly, one of my Republican colleagues recently did tweet: 
Dogs were preferable to Muslims. It was very reprehensible.
  I for one believe we must fight all targeted violence or hateful 
intimidation or religion or classification, be it Islamophobia, anti-
Semitism, racist targeting, or anti-LGBTQ.
  Curiously, the House GOP leadership has remained silent as some 
support the Sharia Free Caucus where 66 congressional Members advanced 
legislation that singles out Muslim communities saying they threaten 
the U.S. system in our country. It is not only untrue but counters our 
freedom of religion. Our Muslim communities must feel that they are 
treated with respect. Hate has no place in our Congress.

                          ____________________
```
