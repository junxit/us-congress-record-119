# BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 140, H.R. 8322

```
          BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on April 17, 
2026, the following bill and joint resolution were presented to the 
President of the United States for approval:

       H.J. Res. 140. Providing for congressional disapproval 
     under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating to Public 
     Land Order No. 7917 for Withdrawal of Federal Lands; Cook, 
     Lake, and Saint Louis Counties, MN.
       H.R. 8322. To amend the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 to 
     extend the authorities of title VII of the Foreign 
     Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 through April 30, 2026, 
     and for other purposes.
```
