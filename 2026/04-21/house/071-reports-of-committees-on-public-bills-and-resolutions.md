# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 151, H.R. 7567

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. COMER: Committee on Oversight and Government Reform. 
     H.R. 151. A bill to require a citizenship question on the 
     decennial census, to require reporting on certain census 
     statistics, and to modify apportionment of Representatives to 
     be based on United States citizens instead of all persons; 
     with an amendment (Rept. 119-619). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania: Committee on Agriculture. 
     H.R. 7567. A bill to provide for the reform and continuation 
     of agricultural and other programs of the Department of 
     Agriculture through fiscal year 2031, and for other purposes; 
     with an amendment (Rept. 119-620). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
```
