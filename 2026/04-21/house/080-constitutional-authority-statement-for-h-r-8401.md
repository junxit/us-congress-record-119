# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 8401

**Measures:** H.R. 8401

```
           By Mr. BEGICH:
       H.R. 8401.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following provisions of the Constitution of the United 
     States:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 (the Commerce Clause), which 
     grants Congress the authority to regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, among the several States, and with Indian 
     Tribes. This legislation amends the Marine Mammal Protection 
     Act of 1972 to regulate the transport, purchase, sale, and 
     export of certain sea otter pelts and products, activities 
     that constitute interstate, tribal, and foreign commerce.
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 (the Necessary and Proper 
     Clause), which authorizes Congress to make all laws necessary 
     and proper for carrying into execution the foregoing powers, 
     including the regulation of wildlife-related commerce and 
     subsistence uses under federal law.
```
