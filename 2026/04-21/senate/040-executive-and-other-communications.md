# EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

```
                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and were referred as 
indicated:

        EC-3186. A communication from the Secretary of Education, 
     transmitting, pursuant to law, the report of a rule entitled 
     ``Final Priority and Definitions-Secretary's Supplemental 
     Priority and Definitions on Advancing Artificial Intelligence 
     in Education'' (34 CFR Part 75) received in the Office of the 
     President pro tempore of the Senate; to the Committee on 
     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
        EC-3187. A communication from the Secretary of Education, 
     transmitting, pursuant to law, the report of a rule entitled 
     ``Final Priority and Definitions-Secretary's Supplemental 
     Priority and Definitions on Career Pathways and Workforce 
     Readiness'' (34 CFR Part 75) received in the Office of the 
     President pro tempore of the Senate; to the Committee on 
     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
        EC-3188. A communication from the United States Trade 
     Representative, transmitting, pursuant to law, a six-month 
     periodic report on the national emergency that was declared 
     in Executive Order 14257 with respect to regulating imports 
     with a reciprocal tariff to rectify trade practices that 
     contribute to large and persistent annual United States goods 
     trade deficits; to the Committee on Finance.
        EC-3189. A communication from the Chairman and Chief 
     Executive Officer, Farm Credit Administration, transmitting, 
     pursuant to law, the Administration's Strategic Plan for 
     fiscal years 2026 through 2030; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.
        EC-3190. A communication from the Director, Congressional 
     Affairs, Nuclear Material Safety and Safeguards, 
     transmitting, pursuant to law, the report of a rule entitled 
     ``The Sunset Rule-Aircraft Impact Assessment'' (RIN3150-AL63) 
     received in the Office of the President of the Senate on 
     April 15, 2026; to the Committee on Environment and Public 
     Works.

                          ____________________
```
