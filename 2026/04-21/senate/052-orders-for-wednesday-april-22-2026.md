# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 22, 2026

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 33

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 22, 2026

  Ms. LUMMIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, April 22; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 376, S. Con. Res. 33, with 32 hours of 
debate remaining on the budget resolution, equally divided.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
