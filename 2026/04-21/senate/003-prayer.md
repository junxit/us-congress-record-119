# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Today's opening prayer will be offered by Dr. 
Chad Balthrop, executive pastor at First Baptist Church Owasso, from 
Owasso, OK.
  The guest Chaplain offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Heavenly Father, You are the powerful controller of all things, and 
we praise You. You have made us a nation of laws. We believe words on 
paper are enough to manage behavior. Yet, experience demonstrates that 
words alone frequently fall flat. So we need You to do something in us 
and through us that can't possibly be explained because of us. We seek 
Your forgiveness and the mercy found in the life, death, and 
resurrection of Jesus. Write Your word on our hearts and in the heart 
of our Nation. Begin with these lawmakers. I am so thankful for the 
sacrifices they make to serve people. They are instruments of Your 
justice. Their decisions are in Your hands. May they be wise and 
faithful. Give them courage to walk humbly with You and one another. In 
our disagreements, grant us the gift of repentance and capacity for 
forgiveness. Let them be the people who enter a room and peace follows 
after.
  Bless their families with Your presence. Bless those who serve by 
their side with skill and integrity. We long to be a nation that lights 
the world for generations. So we entrust our lives to You and choose to 
use who we are and what we have for the benefit of others.
  In Jesus' Name, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
