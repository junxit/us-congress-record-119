# HONORING MIGUEL ALVARADO AND THE GUADALUPE CAR SHOW

**Speaking:** Ansari, Yassamin (A000381) [D-AZ]

```
          HONORING MIGUEL ALVARADO AND THE GUADALUPE CAR SHOW

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. YASSAMIN ANSARI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 21, 2026

  Ms. ANSARI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an extraordinary 
community leader, Miguel Alvarado, of Guadalupe, Arizona. For decades, 
Miguel has exemplified what it means to serve others, not for 
recognition, but out of a deep and abiding commitment to his neighbors. 
His work reflects the very best of community leadership: consistent, 
compassionate, and rooted in bringing people together.
  For 25 years, Miguel has led the Guadalupe Car Show, an event that 
has become a cherished tradition and a source of pride for the 
community. Beyond celebrating culture and craftsmanship, the event has 
served as a unifying force, bringing together families, local 
organizations, and residents from all walks of life. Through this work, 
Miguel has helped strengthen the social fabric of Guadalupe year after 
year.
  Miguel's commitment extends far beyond a single event. For over 15 
years, he has organized a Back to School event providing free food, 
haircuts, backpacks, and entertainment to families preparing for the 
school year. He has also dedicated more than 15 years to hosting a 
Christmas celebration for local families, ensuring children experience 
the joy of the holiday season through gifts, meals, and community 
connection. Additionally, for over a decade, Miguel has helped provide 
more than 50 turkeys annually to families in need during the 
Thanksgiving season, ensuring that no neighbor is left behind.
  None of this work happens alone. Miguel's leadership has inspired a 
network of support throughout Guadalupe, including his car club, The 
Intimidations, and countless community members who rally behind his 
efforts. His impact is measured not only in years of service,

[[Page E349]]

but in the lives he has touched and uplifted. It is my honor to 
recognize Miguel Alvarado for his unwavering dedication and to thank 
him for being a pillar of the Guadalupe community.
```
