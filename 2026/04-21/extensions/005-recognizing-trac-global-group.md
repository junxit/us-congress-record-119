# RECOGNIZING TRAC GLOBAL GROUP

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                     RECOGNIZING TRAC GLOBAL GROUP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 21, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize TRAC Global 
Group for its commitment to economic development, international 
partnership, and cultural exchange, and to commend its growing 
investment in North Texas. I include in the Record the following 
proclamation in recognition of it:

       Whereas, TRAC Global Group is a global developer and 
     investor headquartered in South Korea with a strong record of 
     success in international real estate and infrastructure 
     development, and is expanding its presence in North Texas; 
     and
       Whereas, under the leadership of Chairman Chong Mun, a 
     distinguished Korean American entrepreneur, TRAC Global Group 
     has demonstrated visionary leadership and has played a 
     meaningful role in strengthening economic and cultural ties 
     between the United States and South Korea; and
       Whereas, the company has successfully collaborated with 
     global partners. financial institutions, and public entities 
     on major residential and commercial developments, showcasing 
     its ability to execute transformative, large-scale projects; 
     and
       Whereas, TRAC Global Group has proposed the K-Wave Cluster 
     Project as a multi-phase development in the Dallas-Fort Worth 
     region, designed to serve as a hub for Korean lifestyle, 
     integrating food, beauty, entertainment, culture, and 
     technology into one destination; and
       Whereas, this project is expected to generate significant 
     economic impact by creating jobs, supporting small 
     businesses, encouraging workforce development, and 
     contributing to long-term regional growth; and
       Whereas, the project will further position North Texas as a 
     center for innovation, international collaboration, and 
     cultural exchange, strengthening the relationship between 
     local communities and global partners; and
       Whereas, TRAC Global Group's commitment to innovation, 
     sustainability, and economic vitality reflects a shared 
     vision for continued growth and opportunity in the region; 
     and
       Now, therefore, I recognize TRAC Global Group for its 
     contributions to economic development, cultural exchange, and 
     international partnership, and commend its leadership for its 
     investment in the North Texas community. I extend my best 
     wishes for continued success in its future endeavors and for 
     the positive impact it will bring to the region and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
