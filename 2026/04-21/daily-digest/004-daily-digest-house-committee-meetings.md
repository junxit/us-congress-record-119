# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 425, H.R. 941, H.R. 1501, H.R. 1687, H.R. 3756, H.R. 4290, H.R. 5911, H.R. 5929, H.R. 6162, H.R. 7618, H.R. 8210, H.R. 8286, H.R. 8290

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Appropriations: Full Committee began a markup on the 
Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Bill, Fiscal Year 2027; and the Financial Services and 
General Government Appropriations Bill, Fiscal Year 2027; and the 
report on the Interim Suballocations of the Budget Allocations for FY 
2027.
CYBER POSTURE OF THE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Cyber, Information 
Technologies, and Innovation held a hearing entitled ``Cyber Posture of 
the Department of Defense''. Testimony was heard from Katherine Sutton, 
Assistant Secretary of Defense for Cyber Policy and Principal Cyber 
Advisor to the Secretary of Defense, Office of the Secretary of 
Defense; and General Joshua Rudd, U.S. Army, Commander, U.S. Cyber 
Command; Director, National Security Agency/Chief, Central Security 
Service; Department of Defense.
BUSINESS MEETING; MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Education and Workforce: Full Committee held a business 
meeting to approve subcommittee assignments; and markup on H.R. 8210, 
the ``A Stronger Workforce for America Act of 2026''. Subcommittee 
assignments were approved. H.R. 8210 was ordered reported, as amended.
THE FISCAL YEAR 2027 DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES BUDGET
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``The Fiscal Year 2027 Department of Health and Human Services 
Budget''. Testimony was heard from Robert F. Kennedy, Jr., Secretary, 
Department of Health and Human Services.
SATELLITE AND TELECOMMUNICATIONS STREAMLINING ACT: MODERNIZING 
SATELLITE LICENSING FOR THE FINAL FRONTIER
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Communications and 
Technology held a hearing entitled ``SAT Streamlining Act: Modernizing 
Satellite Licensing for the Final Frontier''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
ETHICS COMMITTEE SANCTIONS HEARING
Committee on Ethics: Full Committee held a hearing entitled ``Ethics 
Committee Sanctions Hearing''.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 
941, the ``Small LENDER Act''; H.R. 8286, the ``Exchange Rate 
Accountability Act of 2026''; H.R. 8290, the ``Protecting Americans' 
Retirement Savings From Politics Act''; and H.R. 425, the ``Repealing 
Big Brother Overreach Act''. H.R. 941, H.R. 8286, H.R. 8290, and H.R. 
425 were ordered reported, as amended.

[[Page D423]]

ONLINE SCAMS, CRYPTO FRAUD, AND DIGITAL EXTORTION: AN EXAMINATION OF 
HOW TRANSNATIONAL CRIMINAL NETWORKS TARGET AMERICANS
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Border Security and 
Enforcement; and Subcommittee on Cybersecurity and Infrastructure 
Protection held a joint hearing entitled ``Online Scams, Crypto Fraud, 
and Digital Extortion: An Examination of How Transnational Criminal 
Networks Target Americans''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
PROTECTING U.S. LEADERSHIP IN CODES DEVELOPMENT AND ENHANCING PUBLIC 
ACCESS
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Courts, Intellectual 
Property, Artificial Intelligence, and the Internet held a hearing 
entitled ``Protecting U.S. Leadership in Codes Development and 
Enhancing Public Access''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 
1501, the ``Protecting Domestic Mining Act of 2025''; H.R. 5929, the 
``Critical Minerals Supply Chain Resiliency Act''; H.R. 1687, the 
``CLEAN Act''; H.R. 3756, the ``FISH Act of 2025''; H.R. 4290, the 
``Downwinder Commemoration Act of 2025''; H.R. 5911, the ``Crystal 
Reservoir Conveyance Act''; H.R. 6162, the ``Albuquerque Indian School 
Act of 2025''; and H.R. 7618, the ``American Battlefield Protection 
Program Amendments Act of 2026''. H.R. 7618, H.R. 1501, H.R. 5929, H.R. 
1687, H.R. 3756, H.R. 4290, H.R. 5911, and H.R. 6162 were ordered 
reported, as amended.
ROBOTS MADE IN AMERICA: ADVANCING U.S. LEADERSHIP IN MANUFACTURING AND 
AUTOMATION
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Subcommittee on Research 
and Technology held a hearing entitled ``Robots Made in America: 
Advancing U.S. Leadership in Manufacturing and Automation''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
INDEPENDENT WORK, REAL OPPORTUNITY: THE GIG ECONOMY AND THE FUTURE OF 
ENTREPRENEURSHIP
Committee on Small Business: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Independent Work, Real Opportunity: The Gig Economy and the Future of 
Entrepreneurship''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
EXAMINING VETERANS AFFAIRS BENEFITS: PENSION AND FIDUCIARY, AND 
VETERANS AFFAIRS LIFE INSURANCE OPTIONS
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Disability Assistance 
and Memorial Affairs held a hearing on ``Examining VA Benefits: Pension 
and Fiduciary, and VA Life Insurance Options''. Testimony was heard 
from Tim Sirhal, Acting Principal Deputy Under Secretary for Benefits, 
Veterans Benefits Administration, Department of Veterans Affairs; and 
public witnesses.
PROTECTING PATIENTS AND TAXPAYERS: CRACKING DOWN ON MEDICARE FRAUD
Committee on Ways and Means: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Protecting Patients and Taxpayers: Cracking Down on Medicare Fraud''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
FISCAL YEAR 2027 BUDGET HEARING FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Subcommittee on the 
National Intelligence Enterprise held a hearing entitled ``Fiscal Year 
2027 Budget Hearing for the Department of Energy''. Testimony was heard 
from Sandy Willis, Acting Director, Office of Intelligence and 
Counterintelligence, Department of Energy. This hearing was closed.
FISCAL YEAR 2027 BUDGET REQUEST FOR THE CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE AGENCY
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Subcommittee on the Central 
Intelligence Agency held a hearing entitled ``Fiscal Year 2027 Budget 
Request for the Central Intelligence Agency''. Testimony was heard from 
John Ratcliffe, Director, Central Intelligence Agency. This hearing was 
closed.

Joint Meetings
IRAN'S SUPPORT FOR RUSSIA
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe: Commission concluded 
a hearing to examine Iran's support for Russia and lessons learned from 
Ukraine, after receiving testimony from Ilan Berman, American Foreign 
Policy Council; Benham Ben Taleblu, Foundation for the Defense of 
Democracies; and Aaron Zelin, Washington Institute for Near East 
Policy.
```
