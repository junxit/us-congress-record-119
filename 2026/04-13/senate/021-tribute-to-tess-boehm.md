# TRIBUTE TO TESS BOEHM

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO TESS BOEHM

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Tess Boehm for her hard work as an intern in 
my Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and contributions to 
my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming. Tess is originally from 
San Luis Obispo, CA. Tess attends the Texas A&M Bush School of 
Government and Public Service where she is pursuing a master's degree 
in national security and intelligence. She has demonstrated a strong 
work ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in

her great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Tess for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.
```
