# Pope Leo XIV (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                              Pope Leo XIV

  Mr. President, on Pope Leo, last night, Donald Trump reached a new 
low when he insulted Pope Leo--the first American Pope--by calling him, 
among other things, weak on crime and too liberal.
  What is going on in that man's head?
  Trump said this in response to the Pope's comments during Easter 
Sunday, calling for peace. It is the same Donald Trump who has called 
the Bible his favorite book on the campaign trail but can't name a 
single verse, who sells a Trump-branded ``Presidential Edition'' of the 
Bible made in China for $100, and who we all remember held up a Bible 
in front of St. John's Episcopal Church here in DC after ordering 
protestors for racial justice to be tear-gassed. Maybe Trump has 
forgotten what is written in that Bible or, more likely, he never knew 
it in the first place.
  Trump followed up his criticism of Pope Leo by posting an AI-
generated image of himself as a Christlike figure. Can you believe 
this? Can he get any lower?
  This sort of behavior makes a mockery of millions of Christian 
Americans, many of whom voted for Trump and who fervently believe that 
Jesus Christ is the Son of God. More broadly than that, it is a 
disgusting affront to anybody who expects an American President to lead 
with dignity.
  Trump's defenders, no doubt, will manage to find a way to hand-wave 
or ignore this latest temper tantrum--his blindly loyal and devoutly 
Catholic Vice President included.
  Can you imagine, if a Democrat had done this, the outcry we would be 
hearing?
  But, really, how much clearer could it be that the despised villains 
we read about in the New and Old Testaments--power-drunk and violent 
authoritarians like the Babylonian King Nebuchadnezzar or King Herod of 
Judea--bear many parallels to Donald Trump, who--let me repeat--has 
viciously attacked Pope Leo for calling for peace in the world.
  If King Herod had had a Truth Social account in the first century, I 
think he would have probably described Jesus Christ, who saved the 
penitent thief crucified alongside him, as weak on crime as well.
  The shortest verse in the Bible is ``Jesus wept.'' Maybe Donald Trump 
can manage to remember that one.
  (Mrs. BRITT assumed the Chair.)
```
