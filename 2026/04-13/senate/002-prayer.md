# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the fountain of our hope, we thank You that, although we 
plan our course, You determine the success of our journey. In a world 
of turbulence, trials, and turmoil, guide our feet; hold our hands.
  Lord, inspire our lawmakers to commit their plans to You, trusting 
the unfolding of Your loving providence. May they not depend only on 
persistent self-reliance but draw upon the supernatural strength and 
wisdom You desire to give them. We praise You for the many amazing 
gifts You provide for their pilgrimage.
  Today, fill this Chamber with Your presence and power.
  We pray in Your awesome Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
