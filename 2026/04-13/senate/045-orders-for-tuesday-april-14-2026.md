# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 14, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 14, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Tuesday, April 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to executive 
session and resume consideration of Executive Calendar No. 672, John 
Shepherd; further, that at 11:30 a.m., the Senate vote on confirmation 
of the Shepherd nomination and following disposition of the nomination, 
the Senate vote on the motion to invoke cloture on Calendar No. 674, 
Christopher Wolfe, and if cloture is invoked on the Wolfe nomination, 
the Senate recess following the cloture vote until 2:15 p.m. to allow 
for the weekly conference meetings and the Senate vote on confirmation 
of the Wolfe nomination at 2:15 p.m.; and, finally, that if any 
nominations are confirmed during Tuesday's session of the Senate, the 
motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table and 
the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
