# RECOGNIZING MILL CREEK CAFE

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING MILL CREEK CAFE

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Mill 
Creek Cafe of Cedar County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Founded in 2010 by Jamie Wilhau and Marcia Syring, Mill Creek Cafe is 
a welcoming, locally owned spot where the Clarence community gathers 
over quality, home-cooked meals. Over the years, this woman-owned small 
business has grown into a hub for families, friends, and neighbors, 
known for its consistency, hospitality, and made-from-scratch dishes.
  The cafe offers a wide variety of dining options on a rotating 6-week 
menu, including sandwiches, soups, salads, and desserts with daily 
specials displayed on a restored schoolhouse chalkboard. Out of the 
many delicious choices, in typical Iowa fashion, the tenderloin is 
easily the crowd favorite.
  In addition to their work at Mill Creek Cafe, Jamie and Marcia also 
run an event management business together at their Highpoint Event 
Center venue that includes catering services and on-site overnight 
accommodation. They are actively engaged members of Clarence Main 
Street and the Cedar County Economic Development Group, where they 
support local economic growth and strengthen the small business 
community by hosting fundraising meals that benefit local businesses 
and schools. Through this involvement, they continue to invest in the 
long-term vitality of Main Street while preserving the character of 
their community by hosting fundraising meals that benefit local 
businesses and schools.
  Mill Creek Cafe has earned statewide recognition, representing Cedar 
County on a list of 99 Must Try Iowa Restaurants, as well as the Warren 
Coleman Honorary Award from the Stanwood Lions Club for their 
exceptional catering services. Beyond accolades for their dining 
service, Jamie and Marcia also received the Friends of Iowa Renovation 
Award for their event venue.
  Run solely by Jamie and Marica from the beginning, their business 
supports the home-style dining needs of Iowans while contributing to 
the vibrancy of Clarence and the broader Cedar County area. Mill Creek 
Cafe exemplifies the spirit of American entrepreneurship through its 
commitment to service and community.
  I am pleased to recognize Jamie Wilhau and Marcia Syring from Mill 
Street Cafe for their dedication to Cedar County. I look forward to 
their continued success and wish them the very best in the years 
ahead.●
```
