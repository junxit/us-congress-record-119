                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                Week of April 14 through April 17, 2026

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of the nomination of 
John Thomas Shepherd, of Arkansas, to be United States District Judge 
for the Western District of Arkansas, with a vote on confirmation 
thereon at 11:30 a.m. Following which, Senate will vote on the motion 
to invoke cloture on the nomination of Christopher R. Wolfe, of Texas,

confirmation of the nomination of Christopher R. Wolfe, of Texas, to be 
United States District Judge for the Western District of Texas.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
