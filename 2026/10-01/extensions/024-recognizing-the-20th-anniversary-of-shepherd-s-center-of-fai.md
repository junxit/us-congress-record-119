# RECOGNIZING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF SHEPHERD'S CENTER OF FAIRFAX-BURKE

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF SHEPHERD'S CENTER OF FAIRFAX-BURKE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Shepherd's 
Center of Fairfax-Burke on its anniversary, marking more than 20 years 
of dedicated service to the older adult community of Fairfax, Burke, 
and South County, Virginia.
  Shepherd's Center of Fairfax-Burke is a nonprofit organization 
founded in 2006 with the mission of helping older adults in the 
Fairfax-Burke area live their lives independently, with purpose and 
dignity. Under the leadership of Executive Director Ms. Beth Kilgore-
Robinson, the organization has provided invaluable free services, 
including transportation, social, educational, and enrichment programs 
for older adults throughout our community.
  In the past year alone, Shepherd's Center recorded 10.385 client 
contacts, hosted 132 fitness classes with more than 5,000 attendees, 
and provided nearly 3,000 no-cost rides to older adults across the 
Fairfax, Burke, and South County communities. These numbers reflect the 
immense impact of the organization's work and the difference these 
services have made in the lives of older adults. For more than two 
decades, through their volunteering and programs Shepherd's Center has 
worked tirelessly to improve quality of life for older adults in our 
community by creating opportunities for them to stay active, 
independent, and most importantly connected to the community around 
them. The continued impact of Shepherd's Center is a testament to the 
dedication of its staff, volunteers, and supporters.
  I am proud to have supported and stood alongside this remarkable 
organization over the years, and I am grateful for the countless hours 
of service that Shepherd's Center and its volunteers have given to the 
people of Northern Virginia.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Executive Director Ms. Beth Kilgore-Robinson and all of the members of 
the Shepherd's Center of Fairfax-Burke on its 20th anniversary of 
service to the people of Northern Virginia.
```
