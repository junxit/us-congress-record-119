# CELEBRATING SHERIFF KEVIN L. SUGG

```
                   CELEBRATING SHERIFF KEVIN L. SUGG

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Sheriff Kevin L. 
Sugg, who has dedicated nearly four decades to serving and protecting 
his community.
  For 39 years, Sheriff Sugg has answered the call to serve. Whether as 
an officer, patrolman, deputy, corporal, or sheriff, he has devoted his 
career to keeping the people of Houston County and Tennessee safe. 
Sheriff Sugg's record of service is extraordinary. Over the course of 
his career, he received the Life Saving Medal, was inducted into the 
Police Chief Hall of Fame, received the Medal of Bravery from the City 
of Alamo and the Medal of Valor from Crockett County, and was 
recognized with the 911 Institute's ``Going Beyond the Call of Duty'' 
Award. Each honor reflects his courage, dedication, and commitment to 
public service.
  Sheriff Sugg's lifetime of service has included multiple lifesaving 
actions, including the heroic rescue of three children from a burning

[[Page E1001]]

building. During severe weather, he volunteered to deliver food and 
medication to elderly residents and veterans. Throughout his career, he 
shared his knowledge and experience with the next generation of law 
enforcement officers as an instructor at TLETA and the New Sheriffs 
School.
  Yet, a career in public service is defined by more than moments of 
heroism. It is also defined by faithfulness in the little things--the 
everyday responsibilities that rarely make headlines but make an 
enormous difference to the people you serve. Sheriff Sugg understood 
that.
  Under his leadership, careful budgeting resulted in seven consecutive 
years without an audit finding. He oversaw the installation of a new 
roof at the jail and critical repairs to its sewer system. He also 
secured patrol vehicles and new equipment for the Sheriffs Office 
without additional cost to taxpayers.
  These accomplishments may not always earn a medal, but they represent 
an essential part of effective public service. They demonstrate Sheriff 
Sugg's commitment to responsible stewardship, fiscal accountability, 
and ensuring that the resources entrusted to his office were used 
wisely.
  Above all, Sheriff Sugg never lost sight of the people he served. He 
treated the people he served and the men and women who served alongside 
him, with compassion, kindness, and respect. In doing so, he embodied 
the very best of Tennessee's tradition as the Volunteer State.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Sheriff Kevin L. Sugg on his retirement 
and thank him for 39 years of faithful service to Houston County and 
the State of Tennessee. His courage has saved lives, his leadership has 
strengthened his community, and his example will continue to inspire 
the law enforcement officers who follow in his footsteps. I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing Sheriff Sugg for his distinguished 
career and in wishing him and his family all the best in this next 
chapter.
  May God bless him and his family, and may God bless all those who put 
on the badge and answer the call to serve.

                          ____________________
```
