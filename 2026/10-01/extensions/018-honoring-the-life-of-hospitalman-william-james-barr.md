# HONORING THE LIFE OF HOSPITALMAN WILLIAM JAMES BARR

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE OF HOSPITALMAN WILLIAM JAMES BARR

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an American hero 
and a member of Pennsylvania's 1st Congressional District, Hospitalman 
William James Barr, United States Navy, and to recognize his service, 
sacrifice, and enduring memory in the hearts of his family, his 
community, and our Nation.
  William James Barr was a young man from Warminster, Pennsylvania, who 
answered the call to serve his country during the Vietnam War. After 
graduating from William Tennent High School in 1966, William went on to 
serve his fellow Americans as a Navy Corpsman.
  In 1968, at just 19 years old, Hospitalman Barr made the ultimate 
sacrifice while serving in Vietnam. Though his life was tragically cut 
short, his service and sacrifice ensured that his name would never be 
forgotten.
  Today, decades after his passing, the people of Bucks County are 
honoring William in a lasting and meaningful way. Bucks County Bridge 
No. 140 on Valley Road, also known as Kemper Park Bridge, will be 
dedicated in his honor.
  This dedication is more than the name of a bridge. It is a reminder 
to everyone who crosses it of a young Pennsylvanian who stepped forward 
to serve his country and gave his life in defense of others. It is a 
reminder that freedom comes at a cost, and that we have a 
responsibility to remember those who paid that price.
  William's story also reminds us of the importance of a community 
coming together to preserve the memory of its heroes. Through this 
bridge dedication, future generations will have the opportunity to know 
his name and to understand the sacrifice of his service.
  On behalf of the United States House of Representatives and the 
people of Pennsylvania's 1st Congressional District, I extend my 
deepest gratitude to Hospitalman William James Barr for his service and 
sacrifice to our Nation. I also extend my appreciation to his family, 
friends, veterans, and the members of the Bucks County community who 
have worked to ensure that his memory continues to live on. May we 
always remember William James Barr, a son of Bucks County and a hero of 
the United States Navy.

                          ____________________
```
