# HONORING RACHEL SHOEMAKE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING RACHEL SHOEMAKE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Rachel Shoemake as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Rachel, a 
member of IBEW Local Union 302, is a true champion for women in the 
trades. As the first Business Agent of her local, she has broken down 
countless barriers and paved the way for other women to follow. Rachel 
is highly respected not only by her colleagues and peers, but also by 
the countless tradeswomen and tradesmen who have had the privilege of 
knowing and working alongside her. A leader by example, she has 
consistently demonstrated what it means to support, encourage, and 
uplift others. Rachel has also consistently shown up for The 
Boilermaker's Women at Work Committee, generously sharing her time, 
experience, and mentorship to inspire others to pursue leadership 
within the trades. Rachel's commitment to advancing opportunities for 
women and her willingness to help others succeed make her an 
exceptional mentor and role model to women in the trades throughout the 
Bay Area. Moreover, her relentless advocacy has not only inspired 
individual growth but has also contributed to a more inclusive and 
supportive environment within the entire industry. I commend Rachel for 
her exceptional service, tireless contributions, and unwavering 
dedication to the community, serving as an inspiration to others. On

[[Page E1003]]

behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is 
my pleasure to honor and recognize Rachel Shoemake for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
