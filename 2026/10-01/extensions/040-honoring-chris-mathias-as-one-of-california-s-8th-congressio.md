# HONORING CHRIS MATHIAS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
    HONORING CHRIS MATHIAS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Chris Mathias as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Chris, who founded the 
Indivisible group in El Sobrante, has been an incredible leader and 
advocate within her community.
  Through her tireless efforts, she has built a vibrant and engaged 
community in West County, bringing people together around a shared 
commitment to civic participation and strengthening democracy. Through 
organizing community events, demonstrations, and meetings, she has 
guided the community with her leadership, passion, and commitment. Her 
desire to better our Nation and support the well-being of all 
communities serves as a steadfast example for those who follow.
  She has also continually supported marginalized populations, 
advocating for their voices while working closely with national, state, 
and local Indivisible groups. She has fostered collaboration and 
created meaningful connections among organizations and community 
members.
  Her dedication to the strengthening of the Contra Costa community has 
made a meaningful difference in the lives of many residents. On behalf 
of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my 
pleasure to honor and recognize Chris Mathias for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
