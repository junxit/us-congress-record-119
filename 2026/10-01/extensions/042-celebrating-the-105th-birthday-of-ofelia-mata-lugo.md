# CELEBRATING THE 105TH BIRTHDAY OF OFELIA MATA LUGO

**Speaking:** De La Cruz, Monica (D000594) [R-TX]

```
           CELEBRATING THE 105TH BIRTHDAY OF OFELIA MATA LUGO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MONICA DE LA CRUZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. DE LA CRUZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the incredible 
105th birthday of Mrs. Ofelia Mata Lugo.
  Born in 1921, Ofelia's life is a beautiful stow of strength, faith, 
and family. As a young woman, she worked tirelessly harvesting crops 
before settling in Mercedes with her husband, Leonel, to raise their 
family. Through every chapter of her journey, including achieving her 
lifelong dream of becoming a United States citizen in 1967, her spirit 
has always shone bright.
  Today, Ofelia is the proud mother of five children, a grandmother of 
13, and a great-grandmother of 24. Her life has been filled with 
countless moments of joy, from cheering on the Mercedes Tigers and the 
Dallas Cowboys, to standing proudly beside her son, Leonel, Jr., as he 
retired from the United States Army.
  Ofelia remains a shining example of the American dream and the 
strength of our South Texas heritage. I congratulate her on 105 years.

                          ____________________
```
