# HONORING THE LIFE OF ELDER RUBEN ALEXANDER COX, JR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE OF ELDER RUBEN ALEXANDER COX, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District, of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor the life of 
the late Elder Ruben Alexander Cox, Jr., a man of God, a revered 
spiritual and religious leader, and a beloved community servant. I 
include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, the late Elder Ruben Alexander Cox, Jr., at an 
     early age, was ordained to the gospel ministry, committed to 
     the work and calling of God, and served as a minister for 
     more than 50 years;
       Whereas Elder Cox dedicated his life to spreading God's 
     message of hope and love, he became a spiritual blessing to 
     many;
       Whereas Elder Cox gave abundantly to charity and to his 
     community throughout his lifetime;
       Whereas Elder Cox was an advocate for community and social 
     change, and one of the communities' unsung heroes;
       Whereas Elder Cox built his life around faith, family and 
     friends;
       Whereas Elder Cox, even in retirement, continued to work 
     with the youth in his community, mentoring and guiding them 
     to become selfless members of society;
       Whereas Elder Cox was a devoted father, husband, and friend 
     who always had a wise sermon for anyone who would listen; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend the late Elder Ruben Alexander Cox, Jr. for his 
     dedication to his family, his church, our youth and our 
     community.

                          ____________________
```
