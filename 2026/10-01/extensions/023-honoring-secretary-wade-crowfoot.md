# HONORING SECRETARY WADE CROWFOOT

**Speaking:** Ruiz, Raul (R000599) [D-CA]

```
                    HONORING SECRETARY WADE CROWFOOT

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RAUL RUIZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. RUIZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Secretary Wade Crowfoot, 
a true partner to our communities and a friend to my office, on 
receiving the Minerva Hoyt California Desert Conservation Award from 
the Joshua Tree National Park Association.
  Since 2019, Secretary Crowfoot has served as California's Natural 
Resources Secretary and as a member of Governor Newsom's cabinet. He 
leads an agency of more than 25,000 employees dedicated to protecting 
our forests, rivers, coast, and wildlife, and to advancing a clean 
energy future. He is driving California's commitment to conserve 30 
percent of its land and coastal waters by 2030, and he has opened a new 
era of partnership with California Native American Tribes.
  His commitment to the environment spans a career. He served in 
Governor Jerry Brown's administration, led the Water Foundation to 
build water resilience across the West, and helped shape California's 
landmark climate policies at the Environmental Defense Fund.
  Secretary Crowfoot was a key partner in establishing the Chuckwalla 
National Monument. His leadership, alongside Tribal leaders and 
community organizations, helped protect lands of deep cultural and 
environmental value for generations to come.
  He also worked with me as a state and federal partner on Salton Sea 
restoration. From the Species Conservation Habitat Project to its 
expansion, we joined together at the October 2024 groundbreaking to 
highlight new investment in restoration.
  Secretary Crowfoot has supported a balanced approach to our desert: 
protecting culturally and environmentally significant lands while 
preserving room for renewable energy and transmission. That vision 
shaped the Chuckwalla effort and the Desert Renewable Energy 
Conservation Plan.
  He visited our communities many times, listened, and showed up. Our 
Tribal leaders and community partners consider him a friend, and so do 
I.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Secretary Crowfoot on this well-deserved 
honor. I thank him for his partnership and for his commitment to our 
desert.
```
