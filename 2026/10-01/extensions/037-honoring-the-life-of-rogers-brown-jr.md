# HONORING THE LIFE OF ROGERS BROWN, JR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF ROGERS BROWN, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to honor the life of the late Mr. 
Rogers Brown, Jr., a beloved husband, father, and friend, who was also 
gifted with extraordinary culinary skills that led to a stellar career 
as a master chef and restaurant inspector. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas Mr. Brown's love of cooking and culinary talents 
     led to the title of Chef and a long and rewarding career with 
     Amtrak; and
       Whereas Mr. Brown's culinary career was followed by a 
     decade's long career as an Inspector of Hotels and 
     Restaurants for the State of Florida--a job he held until his 
     retirement: and
       Whereas Mr. Brown bravely served his country as a soldier 
     in the United States Army; and
       Whereas Mr. Brown was a kind and gentle man who loved to 
     travel and share tales of his adventures with family members 
     and friends; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     recognize the late Mr. Rogers Brown, Jr., for his dedication 
     and commitment to his family and for his service to our 
     community, state, and country.

                          ____________________
```
