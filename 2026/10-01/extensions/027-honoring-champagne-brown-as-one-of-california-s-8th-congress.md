# HONORING CHAMPAGNE BROWN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING CHAMPAGNE BROWN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Champagne Brown as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Shaped by 
her upbringing in South Central Los Angeles and her Louisiana roots, 
she has dedicated her career to equity, civil rights, and uplifting 
marginalized and underserved communities in Solano County. In her 
professional work, she manages a demanding caseload, providing direct 
services to individuals facing extreme poverty and deep systemic 
barriers. Beyond her career, Champagne is a devoted labor leader within 
SEIU 1021, where she advocates for workers and supports the union's 
mission to build safe, healthy, and equitable communities across 
Northern California. She is also a respected leader in California 
politics, serving as the District Director for Black Women Organized 
for Political Action (BWOPA) in Contra Costa and Solano Counties, 
Regional Director for the CADEM Black Caucus, and co-chair of the 
Democratic Party of Contra Costa County (DPCCC) Club Development 
Committee. As the founder of Black Girls Politickin, Champagne has 
helped build political power for historically underrepresented 
communities and has played a key strategic role in historic campaigns. 
Her extraordinary contributions have earned her top honors, including 
the California Black Women's Collective ``Trailblazer Hall of Fame 
Award for Labor,'' the BWOPA ``Ella Hill Hutch Award,'' and the DPCCC 
``Volunteer of the Year.'' On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize 
Champagne Brown for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
