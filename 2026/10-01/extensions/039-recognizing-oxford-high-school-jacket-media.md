# RECOGNIZING OXFORD HIGH SCHOOL JACKET MEDIA

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
              RECOGNIZING OXFORD HIGH SCHOOL JACKET MEDIA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Oxford 
High School Jacket Media.
  The Oxford High School Jacket Media program has become an important 
part of broadcasting and preserving the special events of the City of 
Oxford and giving students the unique opportunity to have hands-on 
experience in the field. The program has given the students the 
opportunity to create an online viewing source for a variety of special 
occasions locally.
  Oxford High School Jacket Media also produces ``The Buzz,'' a 
broadcast specially designed for OHS students while school is in 
session. The group has been successful and professional and was chosen 
to assist ESPN when the network came to Choccolocco Park to broadcast 
games as well as producing livestreams of the Rubato International 
Piano Competition from the Oxford Performing Arts Center since its 
inception in 2022 seen across the globe.
  The livestreams they set up created the chance for them to partner 
with Alabama Public Television to create a 90-minute documentary, 
``Rubato: Behind the Keys,'' for APT, which premiered on Sunday, 
September 20,

[[Page E1009]]

2026, before being made available to public television stations 
nationwide.
  I join Mayor Craft and the Oxford City Council in celebrating 
September 20, 2026, as Oxford High School Jacket Media Day and 
encourage all our citizens to tune in and support the work of these 
young people.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing and 
celebrating the extraordinary dedication and work of the Oxford High 
School Jacket Media.
```
