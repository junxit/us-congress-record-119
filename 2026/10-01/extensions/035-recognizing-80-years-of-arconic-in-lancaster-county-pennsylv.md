# RECOGNIZING 80 YEARS OF ARCONIC IN LANCASTER COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
   RECOGNIZING 80 YEARS OF ARCONIC IN LANCASTER COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Arconic for 80 years of 
manufacturing and economic impact in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania.
  Arconic Lancaster Operations began in 1946 with New Holland Metals 
Co., a small aluminum producer that, after acquisitions and name 
changes, became a part of what is now Arconic.
  Arconic's 1.3 million-square-foot facility is a cornerstone of the 
county's manufacturing economy, employing over 875 people and serving 
400 customers in North America across various markets and sectors. The 
7,000 active products produced in Lancaster are incorporated into 
vehicles, household appliances, architectural building systems, and 
many other products used across the continent.
  Beyond its economic contributions, Arconic has had a demonstrable, 
positive impact on the Lancaster community. Through the Arconic 
Foundation, it has invested millions of dollars into local nonprofits 
and schools to support basic needs, education, and manufacturing 
workforce development.

[[Page E1008]]

  The success of Arconic Lancaster Operations has made Arconic a 
leading aluminum producer across the world and a standard-bearer for 
Pennsylvania manufacturing. I am grateful for Arconic Lancaster 
Operations and its place in our community over the past 80 years. I 
thank the employees who have made these accomplishments possible, and I 
wish the company continued success.
```
