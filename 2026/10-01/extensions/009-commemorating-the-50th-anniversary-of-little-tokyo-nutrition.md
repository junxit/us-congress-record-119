# COMMEMORATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF LITTLE TOKYO NUTRITION SERVICES

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
 COMMEMORATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF LITTLE TOKYO NUTRITION SERVICES

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and congratulate the 
distinguished Little Tokyo Nutrition Services on its 50th anniversary
  Founded in 1976, Little Tokyo Nutrition Services was created to 
provide for the well-being of Little Tokyo's senior citizens. For the 
past half century, Little Tokyo Nutrition Services has grown into an 
invaluable resource for our community, dedicated to reducing food 
insecurity for seniors in the Little Tokyo and Boyle Heights 
neighborhoods of Los Angeles.
  Little Tokyo Nutrition Services is a pillar of our community, serving 
more than 100 affordable meals each day to senior citizens who are 
living on limited income, or unable to prepare meals or shop for 
groceries independently. In addition to providing meals on site, Little 
Tokyo Nutrition Services delivers approximately 80 meals directly to 
homebound seniors throughout the community so that those who are most 
vulnerable continue to have access to nutritious food, ensuring no one 
is left hungry simply because they cannot leave their home.
  Little Tokyo Nutrition Services addresses social isolation and 
promotes access to supportive services by creating a welcoming and 
supportive environment where seniors can foster lasting connections and 
a sense of belonging. Through regular social gatherings and activities, 
Little Tokyo Nutrition Services helps seniors remain actively engaged 
with their community.
  Beyond nutrition and supportive services, Little Tokyo Nutrition 
Services helps seniors navigate the everyday challenges of modern life. 
Whether they need assistance with technology or transportation to their 
medical appointments, Little Tokyo Nutrition Services has ensured that 
our seniors can access the essential services and support they need.
  I am proud to recognize Little Tokyo Nutrition Services on this 
incredible milestone and congratulate the organization on 50 years of 
serving seniors.
```
