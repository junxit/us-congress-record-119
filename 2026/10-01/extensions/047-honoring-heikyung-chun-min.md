# HONORING HEIKYUNG CHUN MIN

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

```
                       HONORING HEIKYUNG CHUN MIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. LOFGREN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, as Chair of and on behalf of 
the California Democratic delegation, to offer sincere condolences to 
our colleague, Representative Dave Min on the passing of his mother, 
Heikyung Chun Min of Palo Alto.
  Ms. Chun Min was born and raised in South Korea. After attending 
Seoul National University for a Bachelor of Science in chemistry, she 
was chosen for a scholarship program to study in the United States at 
Brown University. She completed her post-doctoral fellowships at 
Cornell University.
  In 1979, Ms. Chun Min and her family moved to California and were one 
of the first engineers and executives in Silicon Valley's semiconductor 
industry. Her hard work and dedication led her to senior positions at 
Fairchild Semiconductor and National Semiconductor.
  In addition to her successful engineering career, Ms. Chun Min was a 
caring mother to her two children and her five grandchildren. She was 
also a longtime volunteer at Gunn High School, where both of her sons 
attended.
  Ms. Chun Min led an extraordinary life and her dedication to 
innovation in Silicon Valley. May her memory continue to comfort those 
fortunate enough to know her. She was loved and respected by her 
community and her family.
```
