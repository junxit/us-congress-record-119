# CELEBRATING EAST TOLEDO FAMILY CENTER'S 125TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
       CELEBRATING EAST TOLEDO FAMILY CENTER'S 125TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARCY KAPTUR

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate and honor East 
Toledo Family Center on this occasion of its 125th anniversary. Founded 
in the summer of 1901 by Reverend H. Hoover, the center first began as 
an organization to help immigrants to Ohio adjust to their new lives in 
the United States. Thanks to decades of community support and 
engagement, the center has grown from the family-run Neighborhood 
Association to a thriving hub that serves families in all stages of 
life through preschool programs, athletic programming, community 
organizing, and senior events.
  If not the first of its kind in Northwest Ohio, the East Toledo 
Family Center is certainly among the longest-tenured and most well-
known. It nurtured children through playground spaces and citizenship 
classes in the beginning of the 20th century. It uplifted the spirits 
of the downtrodden when the Great Depression struck our Nation. It 
brought a sense of community to families returning to everyday life 
after World War II. It reinvigorated seniors by expanding its offerings 
to a Senior Center in the 1980s. It offered early education 
opportunities with the opening of a Head Start program at the turn of 
the new millennium. Today, it continues to invest in our community in 
many of these same ways and more.
  The Center's resilience and commitment to the citizens of East Toledo 
through over a century of war, economic hardship, and societal shifts 
stands as an example to us all. It is an example of how to serve others 
and an example of when to ask for help. Cherished as a place that 
``help(s) people help themselves,'' the center flourishes with the help 
of many dedicated staff members and volunteers.
  In seeking to strengthen and serve our community one life at a time, 
the East Toledo Family Center has steadily changed the lives of 
countless Toledoans for generations. Please join me, and our entire 
Northwest Ohio community, in honoring and celebrating ETFC and their 
125 tremendous years of service. May the next 125 years bring continued 
prosperity and a sense of belonging to the many lives it will touch. 
Onward.
```
