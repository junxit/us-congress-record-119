# RECOGNIZING INNOVATIONS IN BRAIN CANCER RESEARCH

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
            RECOGNIZING INNOVATIONS IN BRAIN CANCER RESEARCH

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an important 
advancement for patients and families confronting brain cancer. The 
United States Food and Drug Administration has approved new technology 
to help physicians differentiate recurrent or progressive glioma from 
treatment-related change, in conjunction with other diagnostic 
evaluations, in adults and pediatric patients one month of age and 
older.
  Gliomas are among the most challenging cancers we face, and 
determining whether a patient's cancer has returned or progressed can 
be particularly difficult following treatment. This new imaging option 
can provide physicians with additional information at a critical point 
in a patient's care.
  I commend the scientists, clinicians, advocates, and innovators whose 
work contributed to this achievement, and above all, the patients and 
families who continue to inspire progress against cancer.
  As Co-Chair of the Congressional Cancer Caucus and lead sponsor of 
the BRAIN Act, I remain committed to advancing policies that support 
medical innovation, strengthen our Nation's cancer research and care 
infrastructure, and help ensure that patients benefit from advances in 
cancer detection, imaging, and treatment.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate all those who helped make this important 
milestone possible and look forward to celebrating future innovations 
in brain cancer research.
```
