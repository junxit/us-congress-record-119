# HONORING SUE DUNCAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
HONORING SUE DUNCAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S 
                           WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Sue Duncan as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a dedicated leader 
within her community, Sue has worked tirelessly to advance wildlife 
safety and preparedness in Contra Costa County. Through her work with 
Make El Cerrito Fire Safe, the El Cerrito Fire Safe Council, and the 
Firewise USA El Cerrito 1 community, Sue has helped transform wildfire 
preparedness from a seasonal public-safety message to a sustained 
neighborhood responsibility.
  In 2018, Sue founded Make El Cerrito Fire Safe to organize her 
neighbors around the growing threat of wildfires. Since then, she has 
worked closely with city officials, public safety agencies, and 
community members to promote fire-fuel reduction, evacuation planning, 
and emergency preparedness. Her advocacy has helped El Cerrito secure 
more than $1.6 million in outside funding for critical fire-safety 
tools.
  Sue's commitment demonstrates the meaningful impact that dedicated 
community leadership and engagement can have on public safety. She has 
helped establish a strong sense of trust between residents and their 
public-safety agencies through thoughtful dialogue and by translating 
fire-prevention guidance into actions residents can understand and 
implement. Her devotion to protecting the safety of her community does 
not go unnoticed. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Sue 
Duncan for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
