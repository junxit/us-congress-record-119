# HONORING RUTH ELLIS-MYERS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                       HONORING RUTH ELLIS-MYERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to honor Ruth Ellis-Myers, who 
retired after 28 years of distinguished service with Miami-Dade County 
government. I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Mrs. Ellis-Myers began her career with Miami-Dade 
     County's former Department of Building and Zoning in 1985 as 
     an Enforcement Officer 1 and was soon promoted to a 
     supervisory position and later promoted to Administrative 
     Assistant with the former Developmental Impact Committee; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Ellis-Myers, who had worked hard and climbed 
     the ladder within her department but after a merger found 
     herself back where she started, took it as a challenge and 
     ultimately worked her way back into a supervisory role within 
     the county's new Planning and Zoning Department; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Ellis-Myers took advantage of an opportunity 
     of a lifetime by participating in the Miami-Dade County 
     Management Trainee 2003 to 2004 program and was promoted to 
     Assistant to the Director of the Department of Procurement 
     Management; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Ellis-Myers started her career with the 
     county with a bachelor's degree in business administration 
     and later earned two Master's degrees in Public 
     Administration and Library and Information Science; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend retiring Miami-Dade County Administrator Ruth Ellis-
     Myers for her dedication and commitment to her profession and 
     for 28 years of service to our community.

                          ____________________
```
