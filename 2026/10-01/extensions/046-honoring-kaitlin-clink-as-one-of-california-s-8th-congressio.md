# HONORING KAITLIN CLINK AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
    HONORING KAITLIN CLINK AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Kaitlin Clink as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a community leader, 
Kaitlin has distinguished herself by bridging high-level international 
human coordination with direct, grassroots public service. For the past 
ten years, Kaitlin has served as a Board Member and Managing Director 
of Edfu Foundation Inc., where she has coordinated civil society 
participation across all five sessions of the United Nations Permanent 
Forum on People of African Descent, helping advance global policy 
dialogue. Since 2018, she has also helped organize events through the 
UN Commission on the Status of Women, advancing maternal health, 
nutrition security, and technological equity for women and girls. 
Kaitlin's commitment to service extends far beyond her international 
work. As Executive Director of the Bay Area Street Pantry, she has 
spent nearly a decade leading weekly food distributions at Melrose 
Library, providing fresh, nutritious groceries to hundreds of families 
each week. She has also led youth food distributions at Fremont High 
School and mobilized emergency food assistance for Coast Guard families 
and federal workers during government shutdowns. Kaitlin also overcame 
significant medical challenges throughout her youth, rather than 
allowing these experiences to define her limitations, she turned them 
into an unwavering commitment to serving and advocating for others 
facing vulnerability and hardship. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and 
recognize Kaitlin Clink for her positive contributions to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
