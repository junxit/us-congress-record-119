# HONORING SERGEANT THOMAS PHILIP HIXON

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                 HONORING SERGEANT THOMAS PHILIP HIXON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to honor Miami-Dade Police 
Sergeant Thomas Philip Hixon, who retired after 25 years of outstanding 
service to our community and to young men, as a mentor with the 5000 
Role Models of Excellence Project. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas, Sergeant Hixon, a highly skilled and well-
     respected law enforcement officer, rose from the ranks of 
     police officer to investigator and to supervisor over his 25-
     year career; and
       Whereas Sergeant Hixon was assigned to the Homicide Bureau, 
     Robbery Bureau, the Public Housing and Urban Development 
     Unit, as well as the Carol City and Intracoastal District, 
     where he investigated thousands of criminal cases that led to 
     the successful prosecution of scores of defendants; and
       Whereas Sergeant Hixon has received more than 80 letters of 
     commendation. was the recipient of the 2004 United States 
     Attorney's Office Law Enforcement Officer of the Year Award, 
     and has received numerous other accolades, including the 
     Employee Excellence Award and Officer of the Month Award; and
       Whereas Sergeant Hixon has worked to make Miami-Dade County 
     safer for residents and visitors, and has helped to improve 
     the lives of the less fortunate; and
       Whereas Sergeant Hixon is a dedicated mentor with the 5000 
     Role Models of Excellence Project, working to improve the 
     odds for at-risk young; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend retiring Miami-Dade County Police Sergeant Thomas 
     Philip Hixon for his dedication, commitment and untiring 
     service to our community.
```
