# IN MEMORY OF WALLACE MEEKS

**Speaking:** Fuller, Clay (F000485) [R-GA]

```
                       IN MEMORY OF WALLACE MEEKS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLAY FULLER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. FULLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in memory of Wallace Meeks. 
Wallace Meeks lived a life centered around the people he loved most: 
his family and his community.
  A proud Northwest Georgian, Wallace called LaFayette home. Above all 
else, he was a devoted family man. He and his wife, Judy, shared 58 
years of marriage and built a family that became his greatest legacy. 
Together, they raised three sons, Wally, Stacey, and Anthony. Wallace 
was also a proud grandfather and great-grandfather who was blessed to 
watch his family grow across generations.
  The strength of a community comes from people who work hard, care for 
their neighbors, and put their families first. Wallace represented 
those values throughout his life, and his memory will live on through 
the many people who knew and loved him.
  I am grateful for Wallace's life and the example he leaves behind. 
Mr. Speaker, please join me in keeping Judy, the entire Meeks family, 
and all those who loved Wallace in your prayers during this difficult 
time.
  May God bless the Meeks family and bring them comfort in the days 
ahead.

                          ____________________
```
