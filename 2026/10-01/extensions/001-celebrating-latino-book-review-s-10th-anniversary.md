# CELEBRATING LATINO BOOK REVIEW'S 10TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Castro, Joaquin (C001091) [D-TX]

```
           CELEBRATING LATINO BOOK REVIEW'S 10TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOAQUIN CASTRO

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. CASTRO of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Latino 
Book Review's 10th Anniversary. For the last 10 years, Latino Book 
Review has worked diligently to make sure Latino voices have their 
rightful place among the Nation's great contributors to arts and 
literature across the United States.
  Latino Book Review was founded in 2016 with the purpose of elevating 
the voices of our Nation's Latino communities. In offering a forum for 
book reviews, news and scholarly articles, podcasts, art and more, they 
provide audiences with a reverent celebration of Latino cultural 
heritage.
  In their first decade, Latino Book Review has achieved remarkable 
accomplishments. They have included contributions from over 500 
authors, provided students in higher education from across the country 
with the opportunity to publish their work, and gave the recipients of 
nationally acclaimed awards the chance to share their journey. In their 
first 10 years, across their platforms, they have promoted Latino 
contributions to our Nation's cultural landscape to over 100,000 
individuals.
  Not only does this anniversary mark the recognition of the growing 
legacy of the organization, but it also celebrates Latino Book Review's 
relocation to San Antonio, Texas. It is a tremendous honor to welcome 
the organization to a city whose identity is deeply rooted in an 
immense pride in its Latino culture.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in celebration of Latino Book Review's 
10th Anniversary. May they continue to shed light upon the stories, 
voices, and images that define our Nation's Latino communities.
```
