# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF LEATHERWOOD BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF LEATHERWOOD BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
100th anniversary of Leatherwood Baptist Church in Calhoun County, 
Alabama.
  One century ago, seven members organized a church in the community 
blacksmith shop. As the church grew, services were held in a tent and 
later moved to the community general store. In 1942, the church 
purchased land beside the general store and built an auditorium.
  Over the years, multiple additions occurred and by the 1970s, more 
land was acquired and a large sanctuary, a fellowship hall, and 
educational facilities were built. In December of 1990, the church was 
destroyed by a fire, but reconstruction happened rapidly. In less than 
a year, a dedication for the new church was held.
  To meet the growing ministry and membership needs, in 2006, a new 
Worship Center opened, and renovations and new construction led to a 
new Children's Ministry wing and Youth Ministry building.
  Just three years ago, a new Children's Building was built, and 
classrooms were repurposed for the senior adults. Pastor Phil 
Winningham, Associate Pastor Scott Yaikow and the congregation 
officially celebrated this milestone on September 20, 2026, with 
historical displays, a special service and a fellowship meal.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing and 
celebrating the 100th Anniversary of Leatherwood Baptist Church.
```
