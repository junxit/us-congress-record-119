# RECOGNIZING THE CAREER ACHIEVEMENTS AND IMPACTFUL LEADERSHIP OF DR. DAVID J. SKORTON

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE CAREER ACHIEVEMENTS AND IMPACTFUL LEADERSHIP OF DR. 
                            DAVID J. SKORTON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Dr. David 
J. Skorton, who has announced his retirement as President and CEO of 
the Association of American Medical Colleges effective in June 2027.
  Since his appointment in 2019, Dr. Skorton's leadership at the AAMC 
has been marked by a deep commitment to advancing academic medicine, 
strengthening the physician workforce, training the next generation of 
caregivers, and improving the health of patients and communities across 
the country.
  Dr. Skorton believes that the best path forward for medical 
practitioners is to possess a clear-eyed view of existing challenges 
and recognize that more must be done to improve the health of people 
everywhere.
  Early during his tenure, Dr. Skorton guided AAMC through the COVID-19 
pandemic, helping to bring attention to shortages of personal 
protective equipment in hospitals and testing shortages in academic 
laboratories.
  Prior to joining AAMC, Dr. Skorton was the 13th Secretary of the 
Smithsonian Institution and president of both Cornell University and 
the University of Iowa. As Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution, 
Dr. Skorton oversaw 19 museums, 21 libraries, the National Zoo, 
numerous research centers, and several education units and centers.
  Dr. Skorton's background as a physician and researcher provided the 
foundation for his career as an academician and university 
administrator. As a leader in higher education, Dr. Skorton set a tone 
of accessibility, consultation, and compassion.
  An avid musician who plays the flute and saxophone, Dr. Skorton is an 
ardent and nationally recognized supporter of the arts and humanities.
  On behalf of constituents throughout the 13th Congressional District, 
I express gratitude to Dr. Skorton for his distinguished and impactful 
contributions to society and extend to him best wishes for a wonderful 
retirement.
```
