# HISPANIC WOMEN'S DAY IN FLORIDA AND MIAMI-DADE COUNTY

**Speaking:** Salazar, Maria Elvira (S000168) [R-FL]

```
         HISPANIC WOMEN'S DAY IN FLORIDA AND MIAMI-DADE COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. MARIA ELVIRA SALAZAR

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. SALAZAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Camara de 
Comercio de Mujeres en Estados Unidos de America as it celebrates its 
second anniversary alongside Hispanic Women's Day on October 2, 2026.
  There is no better place to celebrate entrepreneurship than in Miami. 
Small businesses are an essential part of our local economy. According 
to the United States Small Business Administration, small businesses 
accounted for 98.5 percent of employers and 53.6 percent of employment 
in the Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area in 2022. 
These businesses create jobs, provide essential goods and services, and 
help drive the continued economic growth of South Florida.
  Women have become an essential part of this landscape, with more than 
21,000 women-owned businesses throughout Miami-Dade. Women are not 
simply participating in Miami's entrepreneurial growth--they are 
helping drive it.
  In celebrating its second anniversary and Hispanic Women's Day on 
October 2, 2026, Camara de Comercio de Mujeres en Estados Unidos de 
America provides us with an opportunity to recognize the leadership, 
influence, and contributions of Hispanic women in business. Through 
this work, Camara de Comercio helps women entrepreneurs strengthen 
their businesses and contribute to the prosperity of the communities 
they call home.
  As the proud representative of Florida's 27th Congressional District, 
the heart of Miami, I am honored to celebrate the women whose vision 
and hard work strengthen our local economy and inspire the next 
generation of entrepreneurs.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Camara 
de Comercio de Mujeres en Estados Unidos de America on its second 
anniversary and for its incredible contributions to our communities in 
South Florida. May we continue to recognize the women who turn ideas 
into businesses, businesses into opportunities, and opportunities into 
a stronger future for Miami and our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
