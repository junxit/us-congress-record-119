# HONORING JAMELA SMITH-FOLDS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
 HONORING JAMELA SMITH-FOLDS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Jamela Smith-Folds as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Jamela 
Smith-Folds is an exceptional member of the West Contra Costa Unified 
School District (WCCUSD) Board of Trustees, and she has proven herself 
an extraordinary leader in her 15-year career in education. A pillar of 
our community, her deep commitment to student success is rooted in her 
own remarkable journey.
  As a child, Jamela Smith-Folds struggled with reading until a single 
teacher recognized her potential and invested time in her igniting her 
lifelong passion for learning, This experience shaped her core 
philosophy every child deserves to be seen, taught, and championed. She 
has served as a WCCUSD classroom teacher, homeschool educator, devoted 
parent

[[Page E1002]]

volunteer, and leader of numerous school organizations. From coaching 
at Hercules Middle School to advocating for families, she has always 
focused on uplifting others.
  In 2020, her dedication culminated in her election to the WCCUSD 
School Board, where she has found one of her greatest joys serving the 
community as Trustee. Since taking office, Trustee Smith-Folds has 
distinguished herself by an unwavering, uncompromising commitment to 
equity and justice. She played a pivotal role in establishing the 
school district's equity policies, leading with both heart and backbone 
to ensure education systems serve all students equitably. On behalf of 
the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my 
pleasure to honor and recognize Jamela Smith-Folds for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
