# HONORING STACY WAYNE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
     HONORING STACY WAYNE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Stacy Wayne as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As principal of El 
Cerrito High School, Stacy has worked to foster a strong, safe, and 
high-performing environment for both students and staff. From the 
beginning, Stacy has brought her innovative vision to El Cerrito High 
while collaborating with students and faculty members. A committed 
leader, she has brought new ideas and dynamic energy to the El Cerrito 
community, elevating opportunities for academic success while 
prioritizing student well-being and school culture.
  A frequent face at school events, from athletics to theater, Stacy is 
dedicated to ensuring that every student feels supported in their 
extracurricular pursuits. She has grown large amounts of school spirit, 
rallying students to support their peers. She further works with 
faculty and staff, ensuring active collaboration among teachers to 
foster a school culture that prioritizes education and student success 
while making students feel seen and valued.
  The cohesive, encouraging environment she has cultivated at El 
Cerrito High School has benefited students, their families, and faculty 
alike. Stacy's dedication to student success, support for faculty, and 
outstanding service to the El Cerrito community do not go unnoticed. On 
behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is 
my pleasure to honor and recognize Stacy Wayne for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
