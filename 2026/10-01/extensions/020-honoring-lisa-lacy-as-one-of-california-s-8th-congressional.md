# HONORING LISA LACY AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
HONORING LISA LACY AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S 
                           WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of California

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Lisa Lacy as she is honored as one of California's 8th 
Congressional District's Women of the Year. Lisa is a dedicated member 
of the Antioch community who works tirelessly to protect and care for 
the local feral cat population of Antioch. Starting her days in the 
early hours of the morning, Lisa works to trap, transport, house, and 
medicate feral cats across Antioch. She has worked to prepare formerly 
feral cats for their new lives in loving homes through trap, neuter, 
and return efforts. Lisa has helped to create a more humane environment 
for our feline friends and those who enjoy their company.
  To educate and empower her fellow Antioch community members, Lisa 
created the Scruffy to Fluffy information page. Community members can 
find adoptable cats, as well as information on how best to deal with 
those that are feral. Many community members have turned to her after 
receiving litters that are too large for them to manage, with Lisa 
helping to arrange adoptions and other methods of care. Her work has 
benefited both 4-legged and human members of Antioch's community, and 
her dedication does not go unnoticed. Her willingness to give her time 
and energy to a cause that affects both animals and residents reflects 
the very best qualities of community service. On behalf of the people 
of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor 
and recognize Lisa Lacy for her positive contributions to our 
community.
```
