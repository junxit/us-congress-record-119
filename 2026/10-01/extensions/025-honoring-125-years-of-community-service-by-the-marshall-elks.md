# HONORING 125 YEARS OF COMMUNITY SERVICE BY THE MARSHALL ELKS LODGE NO. 683

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
HONORING 125 YEARS OF COMMUNITY SERVICE BY THE MARSHALL ELKS LODGE NO. 
                                  683

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Marshall Elks Lodge No. 
683 as it celebrates 125 years of service to the people of Marshall, 
Harrison County, and communities across East Texas.
  Chartered on April 19, 1901, the Lodge built its first home in 1903 
on North Washington Street, now a Recorded Texas Historic Landmark. 
Over the years, Elk's members have given more than $12 million back to 
the community through charitable giving, service projects, and 
volunteer effort. Behind that number are the things East Texans 
remember: a holiday meal on the table, help arriving after a disaster, 
a scholarship for a student headed to college, and a blood drive that 
helped a stranger.
  The Lodge has long stood with our veterans and first responders. It 
invests in young people through the Hoop Shoot Free Throw Contest and 
drug awareness programs, and it supports Texas Elks Children's Services 
and Special Grant Programs for children with special needs. In 2016, 
the Marshall Economic Development Corporation and the Greater Marshall 
Chamber of Commerce honored the Lodge as Non-Profit Organization of the 
Year.
  For 125 years, generations of members have given their time, their 
talents, and their treasure, guided by the four principles of Elkdom: 
charity, justice, brotherly love, and fidelity. That is the East Texas 
way of caring for a neighbor.
  I thank every member, past and present, and their families. May God 
bless the members of Marshall Elks Lodge No. 683, and may He continue 
to bless East Texas and the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
