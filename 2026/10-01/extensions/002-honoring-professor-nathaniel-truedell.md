# HONORING PROFESSOR NATHANIEL TRUEDELL

**Speaking:** Carson, Andre (C001072) [D-IN]

```
                 HONORING PROFESSOR NATHANIEL TRUEDELL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANDRE CARSON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. CARSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Professor Nathaniel 
Truedell, a devoted minister of music, educator, and mentor, in 
celebration of his 50th year of music ministry at Jones Tabernacle 
A.M.E. Zion Church in Indianapolis, Indiana.
  For half a century, Professor Truedell has lifted the hearts of the 
Jones Tabernacle family through praise and worship. His devotion, 
discipline, and dedication have made him a cornerstone of the 
congregation. Through changing times, technological shifts, and 
pastoral transitions, he has remained a steady and stabilizing 
presence. He has always turned the spotlight and applause away from 
himself and toward the glory of God.
  Professor Truedell's influence reaches well beyond the church walls, 
leading the Broad Ripple High School Gospel Choir and the Ebony 
Majestic Choir at Indiana State University for years. 50 years ago, he 
founded the community choir Sounds of Aquarius. He also taught at Broad 
Ripple High School and Washington Middle School, where he shaped the 
lives of countless students--myself included--through his teaching of 
theatre, drama, and music.
  Those who know Professor Truedell describe him as humble, kind, 
generous, patient, and always striving for excellence. He is a leader, 
a teacher, and a mentor whose impact is felt by the many people he has 
served throughout our community.
  Mr. Speaker, Professor Nathaniel Truedell has spent 50 years using 
music to serve God and uplift others. As Jones Tabernacle A.M.E. Zion 
Church gathers to celebrate his ministry, I ask my colleagues to join 
me in honoring Professor Truedell's faithful service. musical 
excellence, and lasting legacy.

                          ____________________
```
