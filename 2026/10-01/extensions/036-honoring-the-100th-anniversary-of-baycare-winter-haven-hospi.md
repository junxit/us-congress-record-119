# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAYCARE WINTER HAVEN HOSPITAL

**Speaking:** Franklin, Scott (F000472) [R-FL]

```
    HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAYCARE WINTER HAVEN HOSPITAL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SCOTT FRANKLIN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. FRANKLIN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor BayCare 
Winter Haven Hospital as it celebrates 100 years of serving the people 
of Winter Haven, Polk County, and Central Florida.
  Since opening its doors on October 2, 1926, Baycare Winter Haven 
Hospital has grown alongside the community it serves. For a century, 
generations of families have turned to the hospital for care during 
some of the most important and difficult moments of their lives.
  Throughout its history, Baycare Winter Haven Hospital has continued 
to expand and adapt to meet the changing health care needs of our 
region. The hospital has helped lead advancements in emergency care, 
cardiovascular medicine, orthopedics, surgical services, and maternity 
care while maintaining its focus on providing high-quality care close 
to home.
  That commitment has also extended beyond the walls of the hospital. 
In 1967, Baycare Winter Haven Hospital established the Center for 
Behavioral Health, the first community mental health center in the 
Nation developed with federal funding to support the 
deinstitutionalization of children and adults living with mental 
illness. Nearly six decades later, the Center continues to serve our 
community.
  More recently, Baycare Winter Haven Hospital partnered with Florida 
State University to establish a family medicine residency program in 
2020, helping address the shortage of physicians in our region and 
train the next generation of medical professionals.
  None of these accomplishments would have been possible without the 
physicians, nurses, medical professionals, administrators, volunteers 
and supporters who have dedicated themselves to caring for their 
neighbors. Their service over the past century has touched countless 
lives and helped make Baycare Winter Haven Hospital an important part 
of the Polk County community.
  Mr. Speaker, I again congratulate BayCare Winter Haven Hospital on 
its centennial and thank all those, past and present, who have 
contributed to 100 years of service to Central Florida. On behalf of 
Florida's 18th Congressional District, I wish the entire Baycare Winter 
Haven Hospital community continued success as it begins its second 
century of caring for the people of our region.
```
