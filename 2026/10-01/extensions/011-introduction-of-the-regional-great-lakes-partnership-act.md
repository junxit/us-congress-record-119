# INTRODUCTION OF THE REGIONAL GREAT LAKES PARTNERSHIP ACT

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

**Measures:** S. 4573, H.R. 10639

```
        INTRODUCTION OF THE REGIONAL GREAT LAKES PARTNERSHIP ACT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARCY KAPTUR

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today along with my colleague, 
Representative Tim Walberg, and fellow co-chairs of the bipartisan 
Congressional Great Lakes Task Force, in introducing H.R. 10639, the 
Regional Great Lakes Partnership Act. This bill designates the Great 
Lakes Commission as the ``Regional Great Lakes Partnership'' for 
purposes of participation in the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
Administration's (NOAA) Regional Ocean Partnerships Program.
  The Regional Ocean Partnerships Program, authorized by Division J, 
the Oceans and Atmosphere Division, of the Fiscal Year 2023 National 
Defense Authorization Act (P.L. 117-263), provides support to regional 
organizations voluntarily convened by Governors to coordinate 
interstate water and coastal management, data sharing, and 
environmental planning. In the three years since its creation, the 
Program has successfully supported four regional groups spanning the 
East, West, and Gulf coasts of the United States mainland.
  However, the Great Lakes Region has not yet been included in the 
Program due to the absence of a designated regional organization to 
receive the Federal funds and facilitate interstate coordination. By 
formally adding the Great Lakes Commission through this legislation, 
Congress can increase Federal support for research and conservation 
work vital to the eight-State Great Lakes Region and the world's third-
largest economy. Importantly, this bill leverages an existing, well-
established working body rather than creating a new entity, thereby 
prioritizing efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and continued investment 
in healthy watersheds, resilient coastlines and working waterfronts, 
and economic vitality across the Great Lakes Region.
  This bill, H.R. 10639, is a companion to S. 4573, which was 
introduced earlier this year by the senior Senators from Michigan and 
Ohio, Senator Gary Peters and Senator Bernie Moreno, and which the 
Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation, ordered to 
be favorably reported to the full Senate on September 16, 2026.
  I look forward to working with my colleagues to advance this bill 
into law and urge the leadership of the Committee on Natural Resources 
to move it forward in the legislative process.
```
