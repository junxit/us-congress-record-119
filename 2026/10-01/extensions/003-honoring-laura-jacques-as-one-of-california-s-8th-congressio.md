# HONORING LAURA JACQUES AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
    HONORING LAURA JACQUES AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Laura Jacques as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Laura is a dedicated 
member of the Antioch Historical Society and the Woman's Club of 
Antioch. In her role with the Historical Society, Laura has played a 
central role in organizing and running several community events, 
including the city's 150th anniversary and the relocation of the Red 
Caboose. For every event, Laura goes above and beyond, including 
helping produce a commemorative coin, a book documenting Antioch's 
history, and a celebratory barbecue. As a part of Antioch's Women's 
Club, Laura has worked tirelessly to transform her local community. She 
has dedicated her efforts to enhancing the Women's Club's outdoor 
space, while also spearheading the early stages of a new mural on the 
building that will celebrate local history and art. In addition to 
directly supervising the construction of the space, Laura was 
instrumental in the project's fundraising. Across all her roles, Laura 
consistently drives positive changes in her community through her 
leadership, sense of civic responsibility, and commitment to 
excellence. Her efforts have benefited Antioch community members of all 
ages, and her dedication to supporting the community is truly 
commendable and does not go unnoticed. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and 
recognize Laura Jacques for her positive contributions to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
