# RECOGNIZING CLAY COUNTY'S AMERICA 250 CELEBRATION

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
           RECOGNIZING CLAY COUNTY'S AMERICA 250 CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Clay County's 
upcoming America250 celebration. 250 years ago, America was born from a 
revolutionary idea: that government gets its power from the people. 
That idea became the United States of America. This year, our Nation 
celebrates its 250th anniversary. Back home in Clay County, Florida, we 
are marking this historic milestone by remembering what we inherited 
and recommitting ourselves to passing it on.
  On October 14, in celebration of America's 250th anniversary, Clay 
County leaders will gather at the Historic Triangle in Green Cove 
Springs to sign our America 250 proclamation at exactly 2:50 in the 
afternoon.
  October 14 has special meaning. On that day in 1774, the First 
Continental Congress adopted the Declaration and Resolves, another 
important step on the road to July 4, 1776.
  Mr. Speaker, freedom does not run on autopilot. Every generation must 
teach it, defend it, and pass it on. Clay County is answering that 
call.
  On October 14, Clay County will not simply remember American history. 
We will add a page to it. May God bless Clay County, and may God 
continue to bless the United States of America.
  I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       WHEREAS, on July 4, 1776, our nation's founders declared 
     that all people are created equal and are endowed with the 
     unalienable rights of life, liberty, and the pursuit of 
     happiness, establishing the principles that continue to guide 
     the United States of America; and
       WHEREAS, for 250 years, generations of Americans have 
     defended those ideals through service, sacrifice, innovation, 
     civic engagement, and an unwavering commitment to self-
     government; and
       WHEREAS, Clay County has played an important role in our 
     nation's history, from its early settlements along the St. 
     Johns River to its enduring support of agriculture, military 
     service, conservation, commerce, and the freedoms that define 
     our American way of life; and
       WHEREAS, the people of Clay County have built strong 
     communities rooted in faith, family, hard work, and public 
     service, creating a legacy that reflects the very principles 
     upon which our Nation was founded; and
       WHEREAS, the 250th anniversary of the United States offers 
     an opportunity to honor the courage and vision of those who 
     came before us while recognizing our shared responsibility to 
     preserve our history, protect our freedoms, and strengthen 
     our communities for future generations; and
       WHEREAS, Clay County is proud to commemorate America's 
     Semiquincentennial through educational programs, community 
     celebrations, historic preservation efforts, and the 
     establishment of a permanent Charters of Freedom display, 
     creating a lasting tribute to the Declaration of 
     Independence, the United States Constitution, and the Bill of 
     Rights for residents and visitors alike; and
       WHEREAS, this anniversary reminds us that the American 
     story is still being written, and that each generation has 
     the privilege and responsibility to safeguard liberty, 
     encourage civic participation, and inspire those who will 
     follow us; and
       NOW, THEREFORE, WE, the undersigned elected officials of 
     Clay County, together with our state and federal legislative 
     partners, do hereby proclaim our shared commitment to 
     honoring the 250th Anniversary of the United States of 
     America.
       We encourage all residents to reflect upon our nation's 
     history, celebrate the enduring ideals of freedom and 
     democracy, recognize the sacrifices made to preserve them, 
     and work together to ensure that the blessings of liberty 
     continue for generations to come.
       IN WITNESS WHEREOF, we have hereunto set our hands this 
     14th day of October, in Clay County, Florida, in celebration 
     of America 250 and in recognition of our enduring commitment 
     to our Nation, our community, and the generations yet to 
     come.

                          ____________________
```
