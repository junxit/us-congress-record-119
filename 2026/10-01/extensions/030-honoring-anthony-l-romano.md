# HONORING ANTHONY L. ROMANO

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M001226) [D-NJ]

```
                       HONORING ANTHONY L. ROMANO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT MENENDEZ

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. MENENDEZ of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the outstanding contributions of my dear friend, Anthony L. Romano.
  Commissioner Romano has dedicated his life to public service, law 
enforcement, education, and the communities of Hudson County and 
especially his hometown of Hoboken. His longstanding commitment to 
serving others has made a lasting impact of Hudson County and the 
residents he proudly represents.
  Commissioner Romano served the City of Hoboken for 32 years as a 
member of the Police Department, rising to the rank of Captain. Since 
his election to the Hudson County Board of Commissioners in 2009, he 
has continued his commitment to public service by championing 
investments in local infrastructure, public spaces, and community 
resources. His efforts have helped improve county roads and parks and 
secure funding for the Hoboken Historical Museum, Hoboken Public 
Library, and Hoboken Cove Walkway.
  Commissioner Romano has also worked to strengthen county government 
and expand opportunities for Hudson County residents. He sponsored an 
ethics reform package requiring annual ethics training for county 
employees, supported the creation of the County's minority and women 
enterprise office, and helped advance rehabilitation and reentry 
initiatives. Beyond his official duties, his long-standing involvement 
with civic and community organizations reflects his deep commitment to 
the people he represents.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Commissioner Romano for his 
exemplary public service and his unwavering dedication to Hudson 
County. His contributions have left and indelible mark on Hoboken, 
Jersey City, and the residents he proudly serves.
```
