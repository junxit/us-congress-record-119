# HONORING THE 120TH ANNIVERSARY OF DONALDSON FARMS

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
           HONORING THE 120TH ANNIVERSARY OF DONALDSON FARMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 120th 
anniversary of Donaldson Farms in Hackettstown, New Jersey, and to 
honor the remarkable legacy this family-owned operation has built over 
six generations.
  Founded as a dairy farm in 1906, after the family traded a house in 
East Orange for a humble plot of land, Donaldson Farms has grown into a 
cherished Warren County institution while remaining deeply rooted in 
its agricultural heritage.
  At the heart of the business is the Donaldsons' enduring commitment 
to high-quality farming, exceptional service, and meaningful 
contributions to their neighbors and the broader community.
  Today, the farm welcomes as many as 7,000 guests each weekend to 
enjoy its farmers market, site tours, apple-picking activities, 
nursery, and greenhouse. These offerings provide families with 
opportunities to connect with agriculture, experience the beauty of the 
Garden State, and create lasting memories together.
  As the Donaldsons celebrate this extraordinary milestone, they also 
remember and honor Lewis Donaldson, the third-generation owner and 
beloved family member whose generosity and integrity continue to 
inspire the family. It is in his loving memory that the

[[Page E1007]]

Donaldsons carry forward a proud tradition of service and dedication.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in commemorating the 
120th anniversary of Donaldson Farms, an exemplary representation of 
New Jersey's agricultural excellence and community spirit. Their twelve 
decades in business stand as a testament to their perseverance, 
commitment, and love for farming. I extend my warmest congratulations 
to the entire Donaldson family and wish them continued success for many 
years to come.

                          ____________________
```
