# HONORING CHRISTINE DEAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
   HONORING CHRISTINE DEAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Christine Dean as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Christine 
has dedicated decades to serving the Martinez community through civic, 
historical, public-safety, and remembrance efforts. A longtime member 
of the Martinez Historical Society Board, Christine has successfully 
chaired its popular Home Tour, coordinating homeowners, sponsors, and 
volunteers. She has also supported Martinez's national Night Out for 
many years, helping strengthen police-community relationships and 
neighborhood connections.
  Christine's most remarkable achievement is her decades-long 
stewardship of the Yuba City Bus Tragedy Remembrance Ceremony, a 
profound event honoring the 28 children and their teacher who passed 
away in 1976. She has organized remembrance ceremonies for nearly 50 
years, ensuring that families, first responders, survivors, and 
community members have a place to gather and reflect. Christine's 
lifelong commitment to Martinez reflects exceptional compassion, 
leadership, and dedication to preserving history, strengthening 
community, and honoring those who have shaped it. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor and recognize Christine Dean for her positive contributions to 
our community.
```
