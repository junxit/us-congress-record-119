# RECOGNIZING DWIGHT AND MARTHA SCHAR

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

```
                  RECOGNIZING DWIGHT AND MARTHA SCHAR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 1, 2026

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the exceptional 
philanthropy of Dwight and Martha Schar as Ohio's Ashland University 
celebrates yet another milestone in the couple's generosity to the 
school.
  An Ohio native, Dwight Schar graduated from Ashland University (then 
known as Ashland College) in 1964, majoring in education. He then began 
his teaching career while also selling houses on the weekends, which 
soon became the basis of his next career. He joined Ryan Homes full 
time in 1968, leading the company's acquisition and development work in 
Ohio and two other states.
  In 1973, Dwight took on a corporate role for Ryan Homes, serving as a 
vice president in the Washington area. Seven years later, he founded 
NVHomes, a Virginia-based homebuilder that has become one of the 
Nation's leaders in home construction. NVHomes was acquired by Ryan 
Homes in 1987; Dwight continued to lead the company (renamed NVR)

[[Page E1000]]

after the acquisition, serving as chief executive officer into 2005 and 
as chairman of the board until 2022.
  For decades, the Schars have supported Ashland University's programs 
and facility development, contributing more than $60 million in all. 
Buildings in the College of Education and the College of Nursing bear 
the Schar name, as do campus athletic and dining facilities--all made 
possible because of the couple's commitment to the school.
  Tomorrow, October 2, 2026, students, administrators, and supporters 
of Ashland University will gather at the newly christened Schar Plaza 
to mark the opening of three newly renovated residence halls. Funding 
for these renovations came from the Schars' recent $20 million gift to 
Ashland University--the largest gift it has ever received.
  On behalf of the people of Ohio's 4th Congressional District, I offer 
my sincere thanks to Dwight and Martha Schar for their inspirational, 
generous support of higher education at Ashland University. Their 
contributions continue to transform the Ashland campus, enhancing the 
educational experience for current students and attracting potential 
students for generations to come.
```
