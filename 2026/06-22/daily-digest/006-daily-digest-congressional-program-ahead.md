                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of June 23 through June 26, 2026

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will be in a period of morning business.
  At 2:15 p.m., Senate will begin consideration of the nomination of 
Darrell Owens, of Pennsylvania, to be U.S. Representative to the

Committee on Foreign Relations from H. Con. Res. 86, Hostilities with 
Iran, with a vote on adoption of the concurrent resolution.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
