# SENATE RESOLUTION 952--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE PHILIP A. HART SENATE OFFICE BUILDING FOR THE COMMEMORATION AND REUNIFICATION OF ALEXANDER CALDER'S MOUNTAINS AND CLOUDS ON NOVEMBER...

**Measures:** S.Res. 952

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

SENATE RESOLUTION 952--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE PHILIP 
A. HART SENATE OFFICE BUILDING FOR THE COMMEMORATION AND REUNIFICATION 
    OF ALEXANDER CALDER'S MOUNTAINS AND CLOUDS ON NOVEMBER 18, 2026

  Mr. PADILLA (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 952

       Whereas the Philip A. Hart Senate Office Building (referred 
     to in this preamble as the ``Hart Building'') is home to 
     Alexander Calder's Mountains and Clouds, one of the most 
     significant pieces of modern art on the Capitol Grounds;
       Whereas Alexander Calder's Mountains and Clouds was 
     commissioned for the Hart Building and conceived as an 
     integral part of the building's architecture and public 
     spaces;
       Whereas Alexander Calder's design was selected by the 
     Architect of the Capitol in 1975 and the plans were presented 
     in November 1976, with installation completed in November 
     1986;
       Whereas Alexander Calder (1898-1976) was one of America's 
     greatest sculptors of the 20th century, known for his 
     invention of the suspended moving sculpture, with Mountains 
     and Clouds representing his final work;
       Whereas the Clouds were removed and have been separated 
     from the Mountains since 2014, leaving the Senate and the 
     public without the artwork as it was originally conceived and 
     experienced; and
       Whereas the historic refabrication and reinstallation of 
     the Clouds in 2026 as part of Alexander Calder's 
     extraordinary Mountains and Clouds in the Hart Building was 
     made possible through the collaboration and commitment over 
     the past 10 years by the Architect of the Capitol, the 
     Secretary of the Senate, the Senate Commission on Art, the 
     Committee on Appropriations of the Senate, the Committee on 
     Rules and Administration of the Senate, and several current 
     and former Senators, as well as the exceptional dedication 
     and generous support of the Calder Foundation: Now, 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE HART SENATE OFFICE 
                   BUILDING FOR COMMEMORATION OF THE REUNIFICATION 
                   OF ALEXANDER CALDER'S MOUNTAINS AND CLOUDS.

       The atrium in the Philip A. Hart Senate Office Building is 
     authorized to be used on November 18, 2026, for the 
     commemoration of the reunification of Alexander Calder's 
     Mountains and Clouds.
```
