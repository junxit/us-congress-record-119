# BIPARTISAN AMERICAN AFFORDABILITY AND JOBS ACT

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

**Measures:** S. 5653

```
             BIPARTISAN AMERICAN AFFORDABILITY AND JOBS ACT

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, today I voted no on cloture on the 
motion to proceed to the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs 
Act, S. 5653. Permitting reform is needed to fully unlock the clean 
energy transition--and the good-paying jobs and affordable energy that 
it will bring--and I appreciate the effort that has gone into this 
bill. In reviewing it, I have focused on its potential to expand clean 
and affordable energy and the need to preserve critical protections for 
Maryland communities and the Chesapeake Bay. As it stands, this bill 
falls short of balancing those trade-offs, undercutting longstanding 
environmental protections without doing enough to protect the Maryland 
Offshore Wind project from the Trump administration's continued 
attacks.
  The bill's changes to the Clean Water Act are particularly 
concerning, going well beyond what is necessary to speed up permitting 
of critical energy infrastructure and hamstringing Maryland's ability 
to effectively protect its waters and the health of the Chesapeake 
Bay--and may actually result in a two-tiered State and Federal system 
that causes additional delay. Language in the bill on project certainty 
is meant to put a stop to the Trump administration's attacks on 
renewable energy projects, but it fails to provide key protections for 
Maryland's Offshore Wind project--the State's largest renewable energy 
project which would provide affordable, reliable electricity to over 
600,000 homes. Additionally, the bill's limitations on judicial review 
go too far, especially to the National Historic Preservation Act and 
the National Environmental Policy Act, restricting the ability of 
communities to meaningfully weigh in on projects that will impact them.
  We need permitting reform that makes targeted changes to address real 
problems while protecting the health of communities and the 
environment. As it currently stands, this bill needs major improvements 
to meet that mark. I will be introducing targeted amendments to address 
my concerns with the bill and will continue to push for a robust 
amendment process to improve it.
```
