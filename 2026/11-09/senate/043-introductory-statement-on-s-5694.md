# Introductory Statement on S. 5694

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 5694

```
      By Mr. PADILLA (for himself and Mr. Schiff):
  S. 5694. A bill to take certain land in the State of California into 
trust for the benefit of the Barona Group of Capitan Grande Band of 
Mission Indians of the Barona Reservation, California, and for other 
purposes; to the Committee on Indian Affairs.
  Mr. PADILLA. Mr. President, I rise today to introduce a bill to 
transfer a total of 847 acres of Federal land in San Diego County, CA, 
currently administered by the Bureau of Land Management, BLM, and the 
U.S. Forest Service, USFS, to be held in trust for the Barona Band 
Mission Indians.
  The transfer of these three parcels would unify the Tribe with their 
ancestral home, which has since been fragmented, to allow for land 
management consistent with the Tribe's cultural traditions and 
practices. The Tribe has also worked with BLM and USFS to preserve 
existing rights-of-way, public recreational access and public trails, 
emergency access for Federal personnel, and existing water rights and 
service agreements.
  The Tribe was forced out of their first federally established 
reservation when the city of San Diego bought a portion of the Capitan 
Grande Reservation from the Federal Government to build a reservoir. 
After the reservation was flooded, the Tribe purchased the Barona 
property in 1932 to create their current reservation. The Barona Tribe 
and the Viejas Band of Kumeyaay Indians now share a joint-trust patent 
to jointly manage the remaining 15,000 acres of the Capitan Grande 
Reservation surrounding the reservoir.
  The proposed parcels in this bill are adjacent to the existing 7,500 
acre Barona Indian Reservation near Ramona and have deep cultural and 
historical significance to the Tribe. Adding this land into trust would 
act as a bridge--connecting the land between the Barona Indian 
Reservation and the Capitan Grande Reservation. This legislation would 
allow the Tribe to better protect sacred sites, traditional use areas, 
and cultural resources on ancestral land.
  I want to thank Senator Schiff for cosponsoring this legislation and 
Representative Darrell Issa for introducing companion legislation in 
the House.
```
