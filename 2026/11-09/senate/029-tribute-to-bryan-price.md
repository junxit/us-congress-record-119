# TRIBUTE TO BRYAN PRICE

**Speaking:** Young, Todd (Y000064) [R-IN]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO BRYAN PRICE

● Mr. YOUNG. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize Bryan 
Price, who will retire from his role as the president and CEO of the 
Indiana University Credit Union in April 2027.
  Bryan has been with IU Credit Union for more than 40 years, spending 
the last 20 as its president and CEO. Throughout his career, Bryan has 
provided leadership that has benefited the credit union's 72,000 
members, touching communities throughout the State. In addition to his 
contributions to the IU Credit Union, Bryan worked on behalf of 
Indiana's more than 2.8 million credit union members through service on 
the board of Indiana's Department of Financial Institutions, the 
Consumer Financial Protection Bureau Credit Union Advisory Council, the 
Chicago Federal Reserve Bank Community Depository Institution Advisory 
Council, and the board of the Indiana Credit Union League.
  Bryan has provided leadership with exceptional vision, integrity, and 
an unwavering commitment to improving the financial well-being of 
Indiana families and communities. Under his stewardship, IU Credit 
Union has grown into one of Indiana's largest and most respected credit 
unions while remaining steadfast in its cooperative, member-focused 
mission. He has consistently championed the belief that a credit 
union's purpose extends beyond banking: that it exists to strengthen 
the community it serves. His leadership has inspired employees, 
community partners, and fellow credit union leaders to embrace that 
same philosophy of service.
  His leadership has fostered a culture of service and community 
investment that has produced meaningful and lasting benefits for 
Hoosiers. This has included the credit union contributing more than 
$300,000 annually to community sponsorships and charitable initiatives; 
awarding more than $47,000 annually in scholarships to students 
pursuing higher education; supporting United Way campaigns, affordable 
housing initiatives, financial literacy programs, and numerous 
nonprofit organizations throughout south-central Indiana; and investing 
in partnerships that improve educational opportunities, economic 
development, and the overall quality of life for Hoosier communities.
  Bryan has also distinguished himself as one of Indiana's most 
effective credit union advocates. He has worked with legislators and 
regulators at the State and Federal levels to help shape public policy 
and improve consumer access to affordable financial services. In 
recognition of these accomplishments and more, he was inducted into the 
Indiana Credit Union Hall of Fame on October 2 of this year.
  Bryan's legacy will be measured not only by the growth and success of 
the organizations he led; it will also be measured by the countless 
lives he has positively impacted through his commitment to public 
service, financial empowerment, education, and community investment. He 
embodies the leadership qualities that others look up to, and his 
contributions have left a lasting mark on his credit union, community, 
colleagues, and everyone who benefited because of his dedication to the 
cooperative principle of people helping people.●

                          ____________________
```
