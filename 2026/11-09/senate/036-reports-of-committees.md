# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 933, S. 2378, S. 3226, S. 4448, S. 4691

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. CRUZ, from the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 933. A bill to authorize programs for the National 
     Aeronautics and Space Administration for fiscal year 2025, 
     and for other purposes (Rept. No. 119-156).
       S. 2378. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     establish funds for investments in aviation security 
     checkpoint technology, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 119-
     157).
       S. 4448. A bill to require the Assistant Secretary of 
     Commerce for Communications and Information to create tools 
     for tracking the progress of grant recipients under the 
     Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment Program, to require 
     the Assistant Secretary to help executive agencies improve 
     compliance with the statutory deadline for processing 
     communications use applications, and for other purposes 
     (Rept. No. 119-158).
       By Mr. CRUZ, from the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation, without amendment:
       S. 4691. A bill to support improved awareness and 
     coordination of Federal communications resources during 
     disasters and emergencies, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 
     119-159).
       By Mr. PAUL, from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 3226. A bill to provide for the creation of the missing 
     Armed Forces and civilian personnel Records Collection at the 
     National Archives, to require the expeditious public 
     transmission to the Archivist and public disclosure of 
     missing Armed Forces and civilian personnel records, and for 
     other purposes.
```
