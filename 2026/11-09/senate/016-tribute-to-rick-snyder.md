# TRIBUTE TO RICK SNYDER

**Speaking:** Banks, Jim (B001299) [R-IN]

```

                         TRIBUTE TO RICK SNYDER

● Mr. BANKS. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize Rick 
Snyder, who recently announced he will retire from his role as the 
president of the Indianapolis Fraternal Order of Police at the end of 
his current term. Rick has led Indianapolis Lodge 86 of the Fraternal 
Order of Police with faith and integrity for more than 12 years, 
representing more than 3,000 active and retired members from 10 
different law enforcement agencies in central Indiana. He has also 
served Hoosiers as a law enforcement officer for more than three 
decades.
  As the longest serving president in Indianapolis FOP's history to 
date, Rick Snyder has been a fierce and trustworthy advocate for 
members of the Indy Metro PD and he has led Indianapolis FOP to a 
strong position of national leadership.
  Under Rick's leadership, Indianapolis police officers have received 
better salaries and equipment, and their working conditions have 
improved. Rick has been a strong advocate of ``tough on crime'' 
policies that keep Indianapolis residents safe, and he has stood by his 
members, supporting legal protections that allow law enforcement 
officers to perform their duties in good faith and without fear of 
retribution. Rick has consistently set the example as a spiritual 
leader for Indianapolis police officers and law enforcement officials 
around the country as chaplain for the National FOP.
  It isn't surprising that National FOP president Patrick Yates 
affirmed Rick's strong leadership, saying ``when lodges across the 
country call me about ideas, I always tell them they should look at 
what the Indianapolis FOP is doing.''
  I am proud of the strong legacy of public service Rick is leaving, 
and I look forward to working with Bobby Ferguson, who was elected to 
succeed Rick as the next Indianapolis FOP president just last 
week.●
```
