# TRIBUTE TO STEVE PENDERY

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO STEVE PENDERY

● Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I rise today to pay tribute to a 
good friend of mine and a devoted public servant to the people of 
Campbell County, KY, County Judge-Executive Steve Pendery. Steve is 
wrapping up his service after 28 years at the helm leading Campbell 
County, and I ask my colleagues to please join me in applauding his 
efforts to improve the lives of his fellow residents and help usher in 
an era of economic growth and success for the region.
  A lifelong resident of Campbell County, Steve built his career 
working in the insurance industry. His professional roots cemented in 
him a resolve that making a difference through hard work is possible 
and service to others is important. Since being elected county judge-
executive, Steve has accrued numerous accolades and accomplishments on 
behalf of Campbell County residents.
  Steve's wins for Campbell County have all come from his dedication to 
common sense, fiscally responsible government. Judge Pendery helped 
expand fiber optic cable across the county to keep residents connected, 
and he worked hard to expand water service to many households. He even 
managed to eliminate park fees and lower property taxes for residents 
while maintaining the highest quality local government services.
  In addition, Steve's focus on Campbell County's infrastructure has 
been vital for commuters throughout the region and for managing 
increased economic growth. His core belief in running a small, 
efficient government focused on providing top-tier, superior services 
for the people has worked. All who are familiar with Campbell County 
can see the impact Steve has made on the community, and I am grateful 
for his work and service that has benefited so many Kentuckians.
  Over the years, I have been honored to work together with Steve to 
fight the opioid epidemic that has plagued the lives of so many of our 
neighbors across the Commonwealth. Through understanding the issue and 
collaborating with people in the community, Judge Pendery has been a 
great partner in this work which has led to a significant reduction in 
fatal overdoses over the last several years in Campbell County. I am 
glad to have had Steve on the ground in northern Kentucky pushing 
forward on the frontlines of this issue.
  Steve's invaluable leadership over nearly three decades leaves behind 
an enduring legacy that will benefit Campbell County and the 
Commonwealth of Kentucky for many generations to come. I know that his 
wife Dana and his children Chase and Allie are incredibly proud of him, 
and I look forward to seeing what comes next for him in this next 
chapter of his life. So once again, I will ask my colleagues to join me 
in honoring Judge-Executive Steve Pendery for his distinguished record 
of public service to Campbell County and the Commonwealth of 
Kentucky.●

                          ____________________
```
