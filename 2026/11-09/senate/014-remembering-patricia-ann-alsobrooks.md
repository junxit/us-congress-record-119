# REMEMBERING PATRICIA ANN ALSOBROOKS

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
                  REMEMBERING PATRICIA ANN ALSOBROOKS

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, I rise today to honor the life and 
memory of Patricia Ann Alsobrooks, a beloved mother, wife, and woman of 
remarkable strength and compassion who dedicated her life to supporting 
those around her.
  Patricia embodied hard work, perseverance, and selflessness. 
Throughout her life, she showed an unwavering commitment to her family 
and a willingness to sacrifice for the well-being of others. In her 
career as a receptionist, she understood the importance of 
determination and creating opportunities for those she loved. Her 
generosity, kindness, and quiet strength were reflected in the many 
ways she cared for and supported her loved ones.
  Patricia's life was a testament to the lasting impact of love and 
dedication. She believed in encouraging others to pursue their dreams 
and never hesitated to offer her support. Through her actions, she 
demonstrated that a person's influence is measured not only by their 
accomplishments but also by the lives they touch and the opportunities 
they help create for others.
  Patricia's devotion to her family was especially evident in the 
values she instilled in her daughters Kim Alsobrooks and our colleague 
Angela Alsobrooks. Through her example, she taught them the importance 
of hard work, compassion, and perseverance. My heart goes out to 
Senator Alsobrooks, her sister Kim, her daughter Alex, their entire 
family during this difficult time. I hope they find comfort in the 
cherished memories they shared with Patricia and the love and support 
of those around them.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the life of Patricia Ann 
Alsobrooks and celebrating the legacy of strength, love, and kindness 
she leaves behind.

                          ____________________
```
