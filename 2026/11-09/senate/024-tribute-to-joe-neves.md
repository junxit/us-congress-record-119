# TRIBUTE TO JOE NEVES

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO JOE NEVES

● Mr. SCHIFF. Mr. President, I rise today to pay tribute to 
Kings County Supervisor Joe Neves on his retirement. Supervisor Neves 
was first elected to serve Kings County in 1994, and during his last 
year of service in 2026, he was the longest serving seated county 
supervisor in California.
  Supervisor Neves was born in Hanford, CA, and grew up working at his 
family's farming operation, which included diversified farming, beef 
production, and trucking of agriculture commodities. He worked on his 
family farm throughout high school and college. He is a first-
generation graduate from West Hills Community College, University of 
Phoenix, and Brandman University. In 1979 he became a volunteer fireman 
at the Stratford Fire Station and soon after became the manager of the 
Stratford Public Utilities District.
  At age 37, Supervisor Neves was elected to the Kings County Board of 
Supervisors, where he would go on to serve for nearly three decades. 
Supervisor Neves' service can be defined by his commitment to his 
community. His work in establishing a 40,000-acre solar zone in Kings 
County helped bridge the gap between farmers with fallowed land and the 
State's renewable energy goals. As the chairman of the Kings County 
Area Public Transit Agency, Supervisor Neves oversaw a $31 million 
investment into the county through the

[[Page S5349]]

construction of a public transportation hub, supporting rural 
communities' access to local resources. Through his work, Supervisor 
Neves made investments in the Central Valley that will continue to 
serve constituents in the future.
  Upon retiring, Supervisor Neves was awarded the 2025 Agricultural 
Supporter of the Year from the Lemoore Chamber of Commerce. This 
recognition speaks to his local advocacy for California farmers who 
sustain our economy. He has also been honored as the Lemoore Citizen of 
the Year and was awarded the Tachi Tribe Award, reflecting the respect 
he has garnered with local stakeholders. Supervisor Neves is often seen 
barbequing at local events, announcing sports games, or even as 
dressing up as Santa Claus with his wife Kathy as Mrs. Claus. Even in 
the small things, Supervisor Neves has found a way to make a positive 
impact in Kings County.
  I ask all Members to join me in recognizing Supervisor Joe Neves on 
his retirement after 30 years of service. His impact on the Kings 
County community will be enjoyed and remembered for years to 
come.●
```
