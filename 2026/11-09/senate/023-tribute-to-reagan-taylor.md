# TRIBUTE TO REAGAN TAYLOR

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO REAGAN TAYLOR

● Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I rise today to pay tribute to a 
true patriot and devoted Kentuckian who has served the great people of 
Madison County, KY, for the last 11 years as county judge-executive. As 
Reagan Taylor wraps up his time leading Madison County's local 
government, I would like to ask my colleagues to please join me in 
applauding his dedicated service to the people of Richmond, Berea, and 
the surrounding areas during a time of tremendous growth and success 
for the region.
  After an impressive career in business, Reagan decided it was time to 
give back to the community that raised him and run for office. Reagan 
was first elected in 2014, and his tenure has been marked by a vision 
of efficient and effective government that invests taxpayer dollars 
with honest stewardship. Judge Taylor and I share a strong belief that 
when you invest in your constituents, it pays off in dividends. And 
most of the growth that Madison County has experienced over the last 11 
years is, no doubt, thanks to and because of Judge Taylor's leadership.
  I have had the pleasure of working with Judge Taylor over the years 
on securing additional Federal funding for Madison County, and our most 
significant collaboration has been working on the Blue Grass Army 
Depot, BGAD. After decades of effort, in 2023, we were proud to 
celebrate the safe destruction of the remaining chemical weapons stored 
at BGAD. Reagan and I have worked together to support the 
installation's future as hundreds of constituents rely on this 
installation as a place of employment. As warfare adapts and our Armed 
Forces continue to evolve, Reagan and I have also worked tirelessly to 
keep BGAD in the conversation, making sure it has the necessary funding 
to continue providing the military with vital resources.
  I have been proud to help secure hundreds of millions of dollars in 
additional Federal funding through the appropriations process for BGAD 
to expand the manufacturing and production process of energetics at the 
installation. I am thankful for Reagan's support and his advocacy 
efforts on behalf of the Blue Grass Army Depot and residents of Madison 
County who support its mission. His work is admirable and his trusted 
counsel has been critical for our work together to modernize BGAD.
  Judge Taylor has been a great representative on behalf of the people 
of Madison County, and I am honored to have worked with him to provide 
for our constituents. It is difficult to find someone who has done as 
much as he has for so many hard-working Kentuckians. I know that 
whatever Reagan decides to do next, his exemplary leadership skills 
will serve him well in this new chapter of his life. I wish Reagan, his 
wife Kelly, their children the best, and I thank them for their time 
and devotion to Madison County.●

                          ____________________
```
