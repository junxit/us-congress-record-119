# TRIBUTE TO CHRISTOPHER CARLILE

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO CHRISTOPHER CARLILE

● Mr. TUBERVILLE. Mr. President, transitioning from military to 
civilian life can be difficult for many of our veterans. For U.S. Army 
COL Chris Carlile of Enterprise, he has combined many of his passions 
and experiences to make this transition easier for Alabama's heroes. 
Chris is a sixth-generation Army veteran who spent more than 36 years 
protecting our country across the globe.
  He held some of the most critical military positions in the post-9/11 
era, including serving as a nuclear strike adviser to President George 
W. Bush, where he was entrusted with carrying the nuclear football. He 
also oversaw thousands of soldiers for numerous missions throughout his 
Army tenure.

[[Page S5350]]

Though this was a heavy responsibility, Chris said it was the soldiers 
that deserve the recognition.
  After retiring from the military, Chris and his wife Sandra returned 
to the Wiregrass where they first met when he was a young pilot in 
training. He became involved in the Wiregrass veterans' community and 
now helps veterans apply for their benefits as a veterans' service 
officer with Disabled American Vets, Post No. 9. Chris also serves as 
the chairman of the executive committee of the Army Aviation Museum 
Foundation, where he helps tell the story of Fort Rucker's role in Army 
aviation to more than 100,000 visitors every year. His passion for Army 
aviation also shines through his involvement with the Friends of Army 
Aviation, where he has coordinated public nationwide air shows, funeral 
honor flights, displays for community service, and helicopter ride 
events with their Huey choppers. They made Alabama proud with their 
flights during the Army's 250th birthday parade in Washington, DC. 
Chris is also a Harvard-educated cattleman and is part of the Coffee 
County Cattlemen's Association, where he mentors other veterans who are 
wanting to transition from the military to the ag industry.
  We have a lot to thank Chris for when it comes to our military 
security and our national food security. It is my honor to recognize 
him as the October Veteran of the Month.●
```
