# RECOGNIZING THE GOOSE

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                         RECOGNIZING THE GOOSE

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the U.S. Senate 
Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize 
an outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize The 
Goose of Dallas County, IA, as the Senate small Business of the Week.
  The idea for The Goose began when the Merkle family recognized the 
need for a community space in Van Meter. In 2025, the family came 
across a vacant building for sale while on a run. Nickie and Rob Merkle 
saw an opportunity to build a business and serve their community, so 
they decided to purchase the space and open an ice cream shop. Nickie 
and Rob envisioned their shop as a place where locals could gather, 
encourage one another, and enjoy life's simple pleasures. To emphasize 
community even more, the Merkles named the shop ``The Goose,'' 
symbolizing the tendency for geese to fly together and lift each other 
up along the way. The Goose opened in July 2026. Following a successful 
summer season, Nickie and Rob expanded the menu to include coffee to 
better serve the community through the winter months.
  Though new, The Goose already plays a key role in Van Meter. Under 
Nickie and Rob's leadership, it has flourished into a vibrant social 
hub that hosts various community events. Their vision has become 
reality. To further engage with the community, The Goose partnered with 
a local elementary school to showcase student designs on its coffee cup 
sleeves and in the ''Goose Gallery'' on the store's walls. The shop 
also stays open late after large community events like high school 
homecoming and Music in The Park, so locals can grab a bite. The Goose 
sources its ice cream from Hiland Dairy and offers customers a wide 
range of flavors. The coffee menu features lattes, cold brew, and 
specialty drinks. Both coffee and ice cream orders can be placed online 
or in-store. As the Merkles continue to grow their business and serve 
others, they credit the success of The Goose to the community and staff 
who have supported them along the way.
  I am pleased to recognize the Merkles and the employees of The Goose 
for their commitment to community building and involvement in Dallas 
County. I look forward to their continued success and wish them the 
very best in the years ahead.●
```
