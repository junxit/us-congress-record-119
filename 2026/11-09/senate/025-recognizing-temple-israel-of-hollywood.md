# RECOGNIZING TEMPLE ISRAEL OF HOLLYWOOD

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
                 RECOGNIZING TEMPLE ISRAEL OF HOLLYWOOD

● Mr. SCHIFF. Mr. President, today I rise to honor and celebrate 
Temple Israel of Hollywood as it commemorates its 100th anniversary.
  Founded in 1926 by seven families, Temple Israel of Hollywood has 
been a cornerstone of the Los Angeles Jewish community for a century. 
What began as a small congregation has grown into a beautiful community 
that provides a place of worship, education, and service, bringing 
generations of families together through their shared faith and 
commitment to tradition and justice.
  For 100 years, Temple Israel of Hollywood has worked to preserve 
Jewish traditions while embracing the changing needs of its community 
members. Through its Religious School, Bay-Nimoy Early Education 
Center, and Briskin Elementary School, the congregation has educated 
generations of young people and helped foster a lasting connection to 
Jewish identity and values.
  Temple Israel of Hollywood's commitment to service extends far beyond 
its congregation. From raising funds following the 1928 St. Francis Dam 
disaster to supporting relief efforts after Hurricane Katrina, the 
temple has continually answered the call to help those in need. Its 
longstanding tradition of providing Christmas dinners and monthly 
lunches to vulnerable populations reflects its dedication to improve 
the human condition across communities.
  The temple has also played an important role in advancing social 
change. In the 1960s, Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., and Harry Belafonte 
spoke from its sanctuary. Throughout its history, the congregation has 
championed civil rights, supported underserved communities, and built 
meaningful relationships across religious traditions. Its founding of 
Big Sunday in 1999, a single-day community service initiative, further 
reflects its enduring commitment to bringing people together through 
service.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the impact and 
importance of Temple Israel of Hollywood. For a century, the 
congregation has enriched the Los Angeles community through its 
dedication to Jewish tradition, thought, and practice. I congratulate 
Temple Israel of Hollywood on this remarkable milestone and wish the 
congregation continued success for the next 100 years and 
beyond.●
```
