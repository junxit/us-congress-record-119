# REMEMBERING DAVID HOLLISTER

**Speaking:** Slotkin, Elissa (S001208) [D-MI]

```
                      REMEMBERING DAVID HOLLISTER

● Ms. SLOTKIN. Mr. President, it is my honor to recognize the 
life and public service of the late David C. Hollister, the esteemed 
former mayor of Lansing, MI.
  Mr. Hollister passed away earlier this year at the age of 84, leaving 
behind a remarkable career of passionate public service to Michigan. He 
transformed our State for the better, especially our capital city, for 
generations of Michiganders.
  Born in Kalamazoo in 1942 and raised in Battle Creek, Mr. Hollister 
studied at Kellogg Community College and Michigan State University. 
While studying at MSU, he was inspired by a speech Rev. Martin Luther 
King, Jr., gave, as well as a chance meeting with him. This compelled 
Mr. Hollister to travel to Mississippi to volunteer as a teacher on the 
frontlines of the civil rights movement. These life-changing 
experiences encouraged him toward public service.
  A common thread throughout Mr. Hollister's life was his commitment to 
peace, social justice, equality, education, and human rights. His 
philosophy was called ``VP3.'' First, you need a positive vision to 
help people. Then, you need the three ``Ps'': a plan to get there, 
partners who are truly committed to the vision, and perseverance to get 
it done. This was the foundation of his five decades of public service, 
which were marked by integrity, consistency, and passion for a better 
Michigan.
  Mr. Hollister first served Michigan as a teacher in Durand and 
Lansing. He saw firsthand how many of his students suffered because 
they did not have enough food at home or their families could not pay 
their bills. These experiences drove him to run for elected office. He 
served on the Ingham County Board of Commissioners and then nearly two 
decades in the Michigan House of Representatives. Chairing the 
appropriations subcommittee on social services for one decade, Mr. 
Hollister was a staunch advocate for helping families get the 
healthcare, food, wages, and resources they needed to stay healthy. He 
helped establish the Greater Lansing Food Alliance, now known as the 
Greater Lansing Food Bank, a lifeline providing food to families in 
need.
  Mr. Hollister is most revered for his leadership in revitalizing 
Lansing into the incredible city that Michiganders enjoy and love 
today. He believed to his core that Lansing was the best city in the 
world. When he spoke about his positive vision for the city, he was 
passionate and excited; and Lansing elected him three times as mayor to 
carry it out, starting in 1993.
  When you drive down Michigan Avenue, you see a beautiful baseball 
stadium and a sea of restaurants, shops, and apartments. Much of that 
is because of Mr. Hollister. He helped establish the beloved Lansing 
Lugnuts, a minor league baseball team that helped revitalize Lansing's 
downtown.
  He also helped strengthen Lansing's economy. When General Motors 
announced plans in 1996 to close plants in the region, Mr. Hollister 
created a grassroots coalition of labor unions, business leaders, State 
officials, and community advocates from both sides of the aisle to keep 
the business in town. Their efforts were successful, saving thousands 
of good-paying jobs and ensuring Lansing's economy was set up for 
success for decades to come. He also helped bring new industries to the 
region that are still thriving today.
  For his tenacious and strong-willed service to Lansing, the capital's 
city hall now bears his name. Mr. Hollister also served at Michigan 
State University and as the director of the Michigan Department of 
Labor and Economic Growth under former Gov. Jennifer Granholm.
  Mr. Hollister was a champion for Lansing who will be dearly missed by 
our State. He was also a proud and caring husband, father, grandfather, 
friend, and mentor.
  As we celebrate David Hollister's life, it is my honor to appreciate 
his lifetime of getting things done to help Michigan families and 
children, especially in our State's wonderful capital city.●
```
