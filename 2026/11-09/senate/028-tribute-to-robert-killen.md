# TRIBUTE TO ROBERT KILLEN

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ROBERT KILLEN

● Mr. TUBERVILLE. Mr. President, whether or not you grow up 
planning to join the military, any time in the uniform has a way of 
sticking with you for many years. For U.S. Navy Petty Officer Third 
Class Robert Killen of Huntsville, he has carried the same conviction 
to protect those around him even after his time in the Navy as a 
firefighter and police officer.
  Growing up in Florida, Robert was drawn to the Navy a few years after 
he graduated from high school. He went on to serve for 7 years during 
1989 to 1995 and deployed during both the Gulf War and Operations 
Desert Shield and Desert Storm. Part of his responsibilities on the 
Navy ships included aircraft rescue firefighting, a passion that has 
remained at the forefront of his life all these years later.
  Eventually, Robert moved to Huntsville where he met and married his 
wife Christy. And they have built quite a life for themselves in the 
Rocket City. As the oldest graduate of both the policy and fire 
academies, Robert began his career in public safety at the Huntsville 
International Airport as both a police officer and firefighter. For the 
last 15 years, he has kept travelers safe from threats they likely 
won't ever know they faced.
  In the last few years, he has gone the extra mile by getting 
certified and bringing in a Crisis Intervention Team--CIT--to the 
airport's public safety operations. Along with his critical fire and 
police work, his CIT training keeps travelers even more safe from a 
potential mental health crisis. Not to mention, outside of his 24-hour 
shifts at the airport, Robert also serves as a sheriff with the Madison 
County Sheriff's Department. He is truly keeping north Alabamians safe 
on multiple fronts.
  Robert also uses his position to help the most vulnerable among us. 
Every year around the holidays, he participates in a ``shop-with-a-
cop'' program, where officers take underprivileged children to pick out 
Christmas gifts they might otherwise not receive. He also drives the 
aircraft rescue and firefighting truck in the Huntsville City Christmas 
Parade every year.
  Robert and his wife have two children: Cannon, who followed in his 
dad's footsteps and also works at the airport, and Kendall, who is 
currently pursuing her college degree. As Thanksgiving approaches, I 
hope we all take some time to express our thankfulness for the men and 
women, like Robert, who have given their time and resources to keeping 
us safe.
  It is my honor to recognize him as the November Veteran of the 
Month.●

                          ____________________
```
