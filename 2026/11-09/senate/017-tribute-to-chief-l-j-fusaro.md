# TRIBUTE TO CHIEF L.J. FUSARO

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO CHIEF L.J. FUSARO

● Mr. BLUMENTHAL. Mr. President, I rise today to celebrate the 
distinguished career of Chief L.J. Fusaro. Chief Fusaro is retiring 
after giving 32 years of dedicated service to his community and 
providing stellar leadership within Connecticut's law enforcement 
organizations.
  Chief Fusaro graduated from The Citadel Military College of South 
Carolina with a bachelor of arts in political science in 1992 and then 
received a master of science in criminal justice administration from 
Western New England College in 1999. He joined the Connecticut State 
Police as a deputy commanding officer for the troop and district 
commands unit in 2004.
  Chief Fusaro immediately became a leader within the Connecticut law 
enforcement community, helping to manage criminal investigations, 
oversee operational planning and staffing, and coordinating with 
prosecutors, municipal leaders, and other regional stakeholders on 
investigations and public safety initiatives. He undertook many 
additional leadership assignments, including becoming a major crime 
squad commander and a troop commander.
  Chief Fusaro's considerable leadership skills were quickly recognized 
when he was made a commanding officer of the casino operations and 
licensing unit, where he was responsible for coordinating and directing 
law enforcement and regulatory oversight activities involving two of 
the largest casino operations in the world. Chief Fusaro would go on to 
become commanding officer of the emergency services unit, providing 
critical guidance to a variety of specialized units and collaborating 
directly with Federal agencies and other security partners, as well as 
the director of the Connecticut Intelligence Center, where he was 
responsible for overseeing statewide intelligence, counterterrorism, 
and critical infrastructure protection programs.
  After his distinguished career with the Connecticut State Police, 
Chief Fusaro joined the town of Groton as the chief of police and 
emergency management director. In these roles, Chief Fusaro was the 
executive leader of a full-service municipal public safety organization 
responsible for law enforcement operations, emergency management, risk 
mitigation, strategic planning, and community safety initiatives.
  Chief Fusaro's distinguished career has been rightfully recognized 
with several awards and honors. He is the recipient of the 
Distinguished Chief Award from the Police Commissioners Association of 
Connecticut, as well as the FBI Director's Recognition for Service 
during his leadership of the Sandy Hook response. Chief Fusaro has also 
held leadership positions in several professional organizations, 
including being president of both the Connecticut Police Chiefs 
Association and the New England Association of Chiefs of Police.
  Chief Fusaro's remarkable career is a testament to his dedication to 
public service and genuine commitment to his community. He has no doubt 
affected the lives of so many Connecticut residents and helped to make 
our State a safer place. I hope that my colleagues join me in 
celebrating Chief Fusaro's career and wish him the best in his next 
chapter.●
```
