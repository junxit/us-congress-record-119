# H.R. 2347

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2347

```
                               H.R. 2347

  Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, tonight, I voted that the Senate proceed 
to debate H.R. 2347.
  The bill intends to accelerate construction of energy transmission 
and clean energy infrastructure in order to lower energy prices for 
American families and accelerate the transition to renewable energy.
  While I believe the bill merits Senate debate, I reserve judgement on 
whether it merits passage pending upcoming debate and amendment.

                          ____________________
```
