# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 5691, S. 5692, S. 5693, S. 5694, S. 5695

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. SMITH (for herself and Mr. Rounds):
       S. 5691. A bill to authorize certain death-related 
     commemorative uses of the Peace Corps seal, emblem, logo, and 
     name; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Mr. SANDERS (for himself and Mr. Merkley):
       S. 5692. A bill to prohibit the use of automatic license 
     plate readers by the Federal Government without explicit 
     statutory authorization, to withhold grants awarded by 
     certain agencies from State and local governments that use 
     automatic license plate readers, and to prohibit the use of 
     Federal funds to purchase automatic license plate readers; to 
     the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Ms. ROSEN (for herself and Mr. Curtis):
       S. 5693. A bill to amend section 453 of title 37, United 
     States Code, to provide for the reimbursement of costs of 
     long commutes for members of the Armed Forces assigned to 
     remote or isolated installations in areas without affordable 
     housing; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. PADILLA (for himself and Mr. Schiff):
       S. 5694. A bill to take certain land in the State of 
     California into trust for the benefit of the Barona Group of 
     Capitan Grande Band of Mission Indians of the Barona 
     Reservation, California, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Indian Affairs.
           By Mrs. BLACKBURN:
       S. 5695. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to eliminate duplicative and misguided enforcement units of 
     the Internal Revenue Service; to the Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```
