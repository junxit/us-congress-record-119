# RECOGNIZING TOP NOTCH FIREWORKS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING TOP NOTCH FIREWORKS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the U.S. Senate 
Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize 
an outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize Top 
Notch Fireworks of Union County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of 
the Week.
  Located in Creston, IA, Top Notch Fireworks has served southwest Iowa 
communities for nearly a decade, as a family-owned and operated company 
specializing in consumer-grade retail and professional fireworks 
displays. Brandon and MaKenzi Vonk founded the business in 2017, 
although Brandon's interest began much earlier. In the early 2000s, 
when Brandon was in college, the sale of fireworks was not permitted in 
Iowa. To pursue his interest, Brandon spent time selling fireworks in 
Missouri.
  In 2017, Iowa passed legislation allowing for the sale of fireworks. 
That same year, Brandon and MaKenzi founded Top Notch Fireworks in a 
30-by-30-foot tent. Soon after, the couple moved to a permanent space 
off Route 34 where they continue to operate out of today. The Vonks run 
this seasonal business alongside 10 dedicated employees. While they are 
only open for a portion of the year, the Top Notch team is committed to 
honoring the traditions and excitement that come with fireworks 
displays.
  After years of success, Top Notch Fireworks has built a reputation 
for safety and expertise throughout Iowa. Top Notch's commitment to 
safety and quality is why so many communities in the area have chosen 
the business for their fireworks needs. Top Notch provides 14 
communities in southeast Iowa with their annual Fourth of July 
fireworks shows. The business is also trusted with fireworks displays 
at county fairs, high school football games, weddings. and more.
  Top Notch Fireworks regularly donates products and proceeds to local 
causes. The business hosts the Wishes Fund, a raffle that raises money 
for a community member in need. But Top Notch does not stop there: 
Every Creston High School home game features fireworks to enhance the 
festivities. Top Notch Fireworks can also be found at the annual 
Cromwell Harvest Fest. The Vonks also donate to local sports teams, 
police, and fire departments. As an important fixture of the community, 
Top Notch Fireworks has been voted a Best of Southwest Iowa business 
for the past 3 years. The Vonks credit their team and community for Top 
Notch Fireworks' success and continue to view their achievement as an 
opportunity to give back.
  I am pleased to recognize Brandon, MaKenzi, and the entire Top Notch 
Fireworks team for their commitment to safety, quality, and community. 
I look forward to their continued success and wish them the very best 
in the years ahead.●

                          ____________________
```
