# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 221, S. 5682, S. 5683, S. 5684, S. 5685, S. 5686, S. 5687, S. 5688, S. 5689, S. 5690

```
     INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING ADJOURNMENT

  On October 15, 2026, under the authority of the order of the Senate 
of September 30, 2026, the following bills and joint resolutions were 
introduced, read the first and second times by unanimous consent, and 
referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SCOTT of Florida:
       S. 5682. A bill to amend the Foreign Agents Registration 
     Act of 1938, as amended to clarify that foreign government-
     owned media operating in the United States must register as a 
     foreign agent; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Ms. ROSEN (for herself and Mr. Lankford):
       S. 5683. A bill to require the development of a best 
     practices guide for individuals seeking to establish and 
     operate a Family Child Care home on a military installation; 
     to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. SCOTT of Florida:
       S. 5684. A bill to require the debarment of any Federal 
     contractor who improperly discloses personally identifiable 
     information, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Ms. WARREN (for herself, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Lujan, 
             and Mr. Wyden):
       S. 5685. A bill to require the disclosure of supervisory 
     records of certain financial institutions that relate to 
     Jeffrey Epstein, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
           By Mr. KELLY (for himself and Mr. Coons):
       S. 5686. A bill to prohibit recording or broadcasting 
     military executions and to prohibit the use of Federal funds 
     for public executions; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. BOOKER:
       S. 5687. A bill to codify the process for petitions for 
     executive clemency, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. MORENO:
       S. 5688. A bill to amend the Mineral Leasing Act for 
     Acquired Lands and the Mineral Leasing Act to increase the 
     State share of oil and gas leasing revenue from acquired 
     lands, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Natural Resources.
           By Mr. MORENO:
       S. 5689. A bill to prohibit Federal and State incentives 
     for the development or commercial operations of data centers; 
     to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. WARNOCK (for himself and Mr. Scott of South 
             Carolina):
       S. 5690. A bill to provide for the replacement of the 
     plaque at Lincoln Memorial commemorating the ``I Have a 
     Dream'' speech of Martin Luther King, Jr; to the Committee on 
     Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. MERKLEY (for himself and Mr. Markey):
       S.J. Res. 221. A joint resolution providing for 
     congressional disapproval of the agreement for civilian 
     nuclear cooperation between the United States and the Kingdom 
     of Saudi Arabia pursuant to section 123 of the Atomic Energy 
     Act of 1954; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
```
