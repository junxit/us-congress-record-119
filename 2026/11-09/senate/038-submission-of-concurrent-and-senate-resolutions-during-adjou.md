**Measures:** S.Res. 950, S.Res. 951

```
   SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS DURING ADJOURNMENT

  On October 15, 2026, under the authority of the order of the Senate 
of September 30, 2026, the following concurrent resolutions and Senate 
resolutions were read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

             Mr. Hickenlooper, Mrs. Shaheen, Mr. Curtis, Mr. 
             Whitehouse, Mrs. Capito, Mr. King, and Mr. Sullivan):
       S. Res. 950. A resolution designating the week of September 
     14 through September 18, 2026, as ``National Clean Energy 
     Week''; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mrs. HYDE-SMITH (for herself and Mr. Ossoff):
       S. Res. 951. A resolution designating September 2026 as 
     ``National Infant Mortality Awareness Month'', raising 
     awareness of infant mortality, and increasing efforts to 
     reduce infant mortality; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
