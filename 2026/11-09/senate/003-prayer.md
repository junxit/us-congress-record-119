# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who orders our steps and guides us into the future, we 
seek Your blessings upon the U.S. Senate during this season of 
transition. We commend to Your care those Senators who will be leaving 
and those who will be joining this legislative body. Grant them wisdom, 
courage, and grace as they embrace new responsibilities and 
opportunities for service. Lord, unite us in our commitment to the 
common good and inspire us to ensure that the best days of this great 
Republic are guided because of Your power. Strengthen us to be salt and 
light to our generation as we seek to do Your will on Earth as it is 
done in Heaven. Remind us that our ultimate hope rests neither in the 
donkey nor the elephant, but in the lamb of God who is our peace, our 
redemption, and our everlasting hope.
  We pray in our Redeemer's Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
