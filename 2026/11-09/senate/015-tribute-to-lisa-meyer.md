# TRIBUTE TO LISA MEYER

**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO LISA MEYER

  Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Mr. President, I am honored to recognize Lisa 
Meyer's incredible service to our State and our Nation and to 
congratulate her on her retirement. Lisa is the definition of a public 
servant, dedicating her 42-year career to serving the people of 
Florida. She taught English before working in State government. Lisa 
was an integral part of the operation, setting a standard of excellence 
in everything she did. When I began my first term as Florida's 
Governor, I asked Lisa to continue serving and join our team, and I am 
so grateful she agreed. Since then, Lisa has been central to our team's 
success. When I became Florida's Senator, she was one of my first 
hires. Throughout her time in my office as Governor and Senator, Lisa 
helped Floridians anytime and anywhere get the assistance they needed 
as my constituent services director. She led our casework team, and 
together, they always went above and beyond to deliver for Florida 
families. Anyone who knows Lisa will tell you she holds herself to an 
unmatched standard of excellence, which is why, under her leadership, 
our team won the Congressional Management Foundation's Democracy Award 
for Outstanding Constituent Services in 2022. If you have met Lisa, it 
is easy to see why the honor fell on our team. Lisa does not work for 
awards, but she does award-winning

[[Page S5346]]

work. Driven by her faith that she witnesses in the way she lives, Lisa 
always leaves Floridians better off than when she meets them. I am 
truly sad to see her expertise and institutional knowledge leave our 
team, but I am beyond grateful for the countless hours she has given to 
communities in Florida in her various roles in my office and her 
community. She is a hard worker, a talented leader, and a trusted 
friend. I wish her my warmest congratulations on her retirement.

                          ____________________
```
