# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H. RES. 1247

**Speaking:** Takano, Mark (T000472) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1247

```
  NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES 
               FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H. RES. 1247

  Mr. TAKANO. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, I rise 
to provide notice of my intent to offer a motion to discharge the 
Committee on Rules from further consideration of H. Res. 1247.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Taylor). Did the gentleman sign the 
petition?
  Mr. TAKANO. I did, Mr. Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, a motion 
to discharge is privileged only at a time designated by the Speaker 
within 2 legislative days after the intent to offer such motion is 
offered.
  The Chair will entertain the gentleman's motion at that time.

                          ____________________
```
