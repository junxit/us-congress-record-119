# RECOGNIZING JEFF TECH TRAINING PROGRAM

```
                              {time}  1910
                 RECOGNIZING JEFF TECH TRAINING PROGRAM

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the new Jeff 
Tech Farm to Refrigerator Training Program which was launched earlier 
this year.
  This innovative curriculum provides educational opportunities for 
students who are committed to strengthening our country's agriculture 
industry.
  The program is a solution to the current shortage of meat processing 
professionals, and it will provide students with hands-on experience 
and a strong foundation in the science of meat cutting.
  As the chairman of the House Committee on Agriculture, I am pleased 
to see the advancements Jeff Tech is making to ensure Pennsylvania 
producers have access to dependable meat processors.
  These students will support our supply chains and will keep beef and 
pork upon our dinner tables. I commend the school and all those who 
were involved with planning this new curriculum. This program is 
another example of how Jeff Tech prepares its students for high-demand 
jobs.
  Mr. Speaker, as co-chair of the Career and Technical Education 
Caucus, I am confident this curriculum will strengthen our workforce 
and local communities for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Jeff Tech.

                          ____________________
```
