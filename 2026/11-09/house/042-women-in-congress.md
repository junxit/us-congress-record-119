# WOMEN IN CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Stansbury, Melanie A. (S001218) [D-NM]

```
                           WOMEN IN CONGRESS

  (Ms. STANSBURY asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. STANSBURY. Mr. Speaker, we are back, and tonight we are here with 
huge news. Not only did we win back the House, we won it with women. We 
set as our goal this election to win back the House with at least 100 
women. And as of tonight, we announce that we have won with 120 
Democratic women taking seats in the House this next Congress.
  These women are historymakers. They are blasting through barriers and 
glass ceilings, blowing through the roof of our goals, making history 
with their bodies and with their elections in the Senate, in the House, 
and in our Governor's houses.
  Tonight we celebrate. Tonight we get back to work. Tonight we fight 
for the American people. Tonight we say to America: We are back, and we 
won back the House with women.
  Are you guys ready for this? Let's do this.

                          ____________________
```
