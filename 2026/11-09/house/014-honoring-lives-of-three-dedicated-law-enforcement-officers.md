# HONORING LIVES OF THREE DEDICATED LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS

**Speaking:** Baumgartner, Michael (B001322) [R-WA]

```
       HONORING LIVES OF THREE DEDICATED LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS

  (Mr. Baumgartner of Washington was recognized to address the House 
for 5 minutes.)
  Mr. BAUMGARTNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to 
honor the lives and legacies of three heroes of public service from 
eastern Washington who made our community better and safer, three 
tremendous heroes who we have lost: Spokane County Deputy Mason 
Acheson, Spokane County Deputy Parker Perry, and Whitman County Sheriff 
Brett Myers.
  On November 3, Deputies Acheson and Perry lost their lives after 
responding to a domestic violence call in Spangle, Washington. Both men 
put themselves in harm's way to protect others, making the ultimate 
sacrifice in service to our community.
  I had the privilege of knowing Mason personally. We lifted weights 
together at our local CrossFit gym. He was wonderful. His wife is also 
wonderful and was even my children's first piano teacher. This loss 
hits especially close to home. Similarly, Deputy Parker Perry was known 
as a wonderful individual.
  We also remember Sheriff Brett Myers, who passed away on October 7 at 
just 56 years old, following a battle with a rare neurological disease. 
After 24 years as Whitman County Sheriff, Brett leaves behind an 
extraordinary legacy of leadership, integrity, and service. My own 
parents knew Brett as a friend, as did many families across our region.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask this body to join me in extending our deepest 
condolences, love, and prayers to the families of these three men in 
the Spokane and Whitman County Sheriff's offices. It has been a very, 
very tough time for law enforcement in our area.
  I ask everyone in eastern Washington to please reach out to all law 
enforcement and let them know how appreciated they are. These men's 
colleagues and brothers do not get the opportunity to stand down from a 
line of protection to grieve as they should be able to, and the least 
that all of us can do is to reach out and make sure they know they are 
appreciated.

         Honoring Life and Legacy of Deer Park Mayor Tim Verzal

  Mr. BAUMGARTNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life, 
service, and legacy of Deer Park Mayor Tim Verzal, a beloved public 
servant and community leader who passed away peacefully in mid-
September at the age of 73 following a battle with pancreatic cancer.
  Tim's journey in public service was built on a foundation of hard 
work, dedication, and a deep love for his community. After a successful 
career in local construction, where he developed an extensive, 
practical knowledge of roads, water, and sewer systems, he channeled 
his skills into local government. He served 4 years on the Deer Park 
City Council before being elected mayor in 2017, earning the trust of 
his community and going on to win reelection twice more.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in sending our deepest 
condolences to Mayor Verzal's family and the entire community of Deer 
Park. May we honor his years of selfless dedication, hold his legacy 
close, and remember the incredible impact he made on eastern 
Washington.


          Spokane County Commissioner Mary Brooks' Retirement

  Mr. BAUMGARTNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate former 
Spokane County Commissioner Mary Brooks on her recent retirement and to 
honor her 9 years of exceptional service on the Board of Commissioners.
  Since joining the board in 2017, Mary has been a steadfast champion 
for the people of Spokane County. Serving as chair of the board for 5 
consecutive years and as president of the Washington State Association 
of Counties, she led with courage, vision, and, most of all, a 
collaborative spirit, particularly during the very, very difficult 
times of COVID.
  During her tenure, Mary prioritized fiscal responsibility while 
delivering critical investments in public safety, local infrastructure, 
vibrant parks, and nationally recognized health programs.
  Mary's leadership has left an indelible mark on Spokane County, 
leaving behind safer streets, stronger families, and a higher quality 
of life for her residents and beyond.
  I extend my heartfelt gratitude to Mary for her nearly decade of 
exemplary leadership. As she prepares for her next chapter, Spokane 
County is undeniably stronger for her service. On behalf of the United 
States Congress, I congratulate Mary and wish her nothing but the best.
```
