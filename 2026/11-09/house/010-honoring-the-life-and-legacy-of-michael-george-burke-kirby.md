# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MICHAEL GEORGE BURKE KIRBY

**Speaking:** Beyer, Donald S. (B001292) [D-VA]

```
       HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MICHAEL GEORGE BURKE KIRBY

  (Mr. Beyer of Virginia was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. BEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
the late Michael George Burke Kirby of Falls Church, Virginia, who 
passed away in September at the age of 81.
  Mike dedicated over 50 years of his life to public service, politics, 
and the arts, leaving a lasting mark on his community and the many 
people he mentored throughout his career.
  Mike spent nearly 20 years on Capitol Hill, serving the office of my 
predecessor, Congressman Jim Moran of Virginia, as well as 
Representatives Bob Matsui and Glenn Anderson of California. His work 
spanned transportation, military logistics, energy, and environmental 
policy. He later served for more than 25 years as the congressional 
liaison for the National Endowment for the Arts, bringing his deep love 
for the arts to his work in public service.
  Beyond his professional career, Mike was a fixture in local politics 
here in Northern Virginia. He served as chair of the Fairfax County 
Democratic Committee and remained active in local political organizing 
for decades. Whether helping guide the committee's work, supporting 
fellow members, or encouraging young people pursuing careers in public 
service, Mike was committed to uplifting his community and making sure 
that those around him succeeded.
  Though Mike spent nearly five decades in Northern Virginia, Mike 
never lost his connection to his hometown of Chicago. A proud Irish 
American and a devoted Chicago Cubs fan, he cherished folk and 
traditional arts, particularly Irish music and dance. He shared his 
experiences, travels, and lifelong engagement in politics with friends 
and colleagues throughout his life.
  Mike will be remembered for his dedication to public service, his 
enthusiasm for the arts, and his generosity toward those of us who were 
fortunate enough to know him.
  Mr. Speaker, I extend my deep condolences to his wife, Anne, and his 
many friends and colleagues. While Mike will be deeply missed, his 
contributions to Northern Virginia and the impact he made as a 
colleague, friend, mentor, and family member will never be forgotten.
```
