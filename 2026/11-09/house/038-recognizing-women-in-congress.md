# RECOGNIZING WOMEN IN CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
                     RECOGNIZING WOMEN IN CONGRESS

  (Ms. KAPTUR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and celebrate the women who 
have fought tirelessly to make their voices heard in this very Chamber.
  When I first arrived in Congress, there were only 24 women. Today, 
there are 150 women serving across the House and Senate. Now, I look 
forward to serving as one of at least 115 record-breaking members, and 
counting, of the Democratic Women's Caucus.
  For over a century, women have pushed against the economic and social 
barriers that once kept them out of the halls of power. Since Jeannette 
Rankin became the first woman elected to Congress in 1916, women have 
championed human rights, civil rights, expanded economic opportunity, 
strengthened national security, and led critical committees that shape 
the education, healthcare, and future of our Nation.
  After last week's historic election, it is evident that our progress 
is undeniable. However, it is also unfinished. To young women watching 
today: Your place is here. Your voice matters. The road ahead may not 
always be easy. Know that generations of women before us have paved the 
way.
  America will not stop. We are a nation of progress. Let us continue 
to fight for equal representation, for policies that uplift working 
women and families, and for a future where no one questions whether a 
woman belongs in the highest office. Together, we all strive for 
generations to come, never constrained by a glass ceiling.

                          ____________________
```
