                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. CARTER of Louisiana (for himself and Mr. 
             Luttrell):
       H.R. 10961. A bill to direct the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to report on the Warrior Training Advancement Course

     Veterans' Affairs.

     duration of lunch periods, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Education and Workforce.
