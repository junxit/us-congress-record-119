# RECOGNIZING NATIONAL BREAD MONTH

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING NATIONAL BREAD MONTH

  (Mr. Joyce of Pennsylvania was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize 
November as National Bread Month and to honor the dedicated men and 
women of America's commercial baking industry who work tirelessly every 
day to feed our Nation.
  As we enter the holiday season, families across Pennsylvania and 
throughout our country are turning their focus towards gathering around 
the table together, and America's baking industry helps make these 
celebrations so memorable.
  The baking industry also strengthens our community, and Martin's 
Famous Potato Rolls is a shining example of that. Founded in 
Chambersburg, this family-owned business has grown its reach around the 
world while remaining true to its roots.
  Under the leadership of president Tony Martin, executive vice 
president Joe Martin, guided by the legacy of their parents, Jim and 
Donna Martin, the company continues to invest in the people of central 
Pennsylvania.
  As a doctor, I know that nutrition must be accessible and affordable. 
Bread is a vital, nutrient-dense staple that delivers nearly 40 percent 
of the dietary fiber in the American diet, along with essential 
vitamins and nutrients.
  As co-chair of the Congressional Baking Caucus, my responsibility is 
to ensure that the voices of our bakers, our millers, and our farm 
families are heard here in the Halls of Congress.
  Join me in recognizing November as National Bread Month.


                       Recognizing Tom Caulfield

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, it is with respect that I 
stand to recognize Tom Caulfield for a truly inspiring personal and 
professional commitment to our Nation's veterans and to their families.
  Tom has dedicated his career to serving veterans, individuals with 
disabilities, and their families through his workforce development and 
his mentorship.
  A United States Army Vietnam-era veteran, Tom is the founder and CEO 
of Johnstown-based Veteran Community Initiatives, known as VCI. Under 
Tom's leadership, VCI has expanded its facility and its service 
offerings to create a centralized hub for veterans' assistance.
  Tom's dedication and advocacy has shaped VCI into a leading resource 
for veterans and their families throughout the region, touching the 
lives of more than 15,000 veterans and family members since the 
organization's founding in 1993.
  As Tom prepares for his retirement at the end of 2026, I express my 
personal appreciation for his remarkable legacy of commitment to 
strengthening our veteran community.


                   Recognizing Volunteer Firefighters

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, during my time in the 
district throughout the month of October, I visited firefighters at 
multiple stations that serve the 13th Congressional District of 
Pennsylvania.
  Repeatedly, firefighters at all volunteer stations have told me that 
their greatest challenges relate to recruitment and to funding.
  According to FEMA, nearly 97 percent of the Commonwealth's 1,800 
registered fire departments are staffed fully or partly by volunteers. 
This is the third highest percentage in the United States.
  Since 2008, the number of volunteers nationwide has decreased by 
approximately 25 percent, while call volume at the same time has risen 
by 70 percent.
  These statistics deepen the appreciation for volunteers like Brent 
Strasbaugh who serves as president and firefighter with the Cashtown 
Community Fire Department in Adams County. Over 30 years of volunteer 
service, Brent estimates that he has responded to more than 2,000 calls 
while serving in numerous frontline and administrative positions.
  To all volunteer firefighters who dedicate their lives to the 
protection of life and the preservation of property throughout the 13th 
Congressional District, I express my heartfelt thanks.
```
