# RECOGNIZING HENLEY JERNIGAN

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING HENLEY JERNIGAN

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Henley 
Jernigan for her great accomplishment in winning the Miss Elementary 
America Kindergarten title.
  The competition took place in Little Rock, Arkansas, where Henley 
competed against competitors from all around the country. It is the 
first major scholarship pageant she has ever entered.
  Winning the crown means Henley will spend the year traveling across 
the United States promoting the pageant system and her personal 
platform, Henley's Kindness Club.
  Little Henley is already quite proud of her Georgia roots and wanted 
to make sure she shared credit with the people who got her to the 
national stage, saying a big thank you to all her family and friends 
back home in Vidalia, Georgia.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Henley on her great victory and hope that 
she continues to make Georgia proud.

                          ____________________
```
