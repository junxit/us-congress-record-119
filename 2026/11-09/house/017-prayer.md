# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Receive us, O Most Holy God, into Your presence. Embrace us in Your 
welcome as we return to You. Having endured challenges that have tested 
our spirits, some of us enjoying victories and others enduring 
disheartening defeats, we turn to You that You would lay Your hand on 
our lives.
  May we emerge from the fire of trial as refined silver. May we yield 
ourselves to the demands before us that we would be tested as gold is 
tested. May each of us call upon Your name and may we listen for Your 
Word.

[[Page H6088]]

  Remind us that the opinions of our dissenters and the accolades of 
our supporters are only noise. Answer us clearly with Your voice, that 
we would understand above all else that You have called us to be Your 
people in this place.
  And may we, in response, before any word we speak or task we 
undertake, acknowledge that You, O Lord, are our God.
  Into Your care we commend the work that lies before us. And in the 
power of Your name, we entrust our hopes and our prayers for these 
days.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
