     NUCLEAR REFUEL (RECYCLING EFFICIENT FUELS UTILIZING EXPEDITED 
                             LICENSING) ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 3978) to amend the definition of ``production 
facility'' in the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to exclude an equipment or 
device capable of reprocessing spent nuclear fuel in a manner that does 
not separate plutonium from other transuranic elements, and for other 
purposes, as amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

gentleman from Ohio (Mr. Latta) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, as amended.
