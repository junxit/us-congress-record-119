# VETERANS DAY

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                              VETERANS DAY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I honor those who served 
our country in uniform.
  Our freedom is protected by many ordinary citizens: our neighbors, 
our friends, and our families who have responded to our country's call.
  Many of them left the comfort of home to stand guard throughout the 
world to protect us from harm.
  Mr. Speaker, we celebrate Veterans Day not just as another holiday 
but as a time to recognize the incredible dedication and sacrifice made 
by those who have served.
  Veterans are strong, resilient, and dedicated individuals even long 
after their service to our country. Veterans also make great 
contributions to our communities as leaders, mentors, and neighbors.

[[Page H6110]]

  To all of our veterans who have served across generations, I thank 
you with all of my heart.

                          ____________________
```
