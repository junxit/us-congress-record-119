# NO AMNESTY FOR ILLEGALS

**Speaking:** Grothman, Glenn (G000576) [R-WI]

```
                        NO AMNESTY FOR ILLEGALS

  (Mr. GROTHMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GROTHMAN. Mr. Speaker, we have had a lot in the paper as we have 
over the last few years about immigration policy, and I was going to 
give a speech, but I thought I would refer to another gentleman, 
President Donald Trump, when he said speaking about those who want 
amnesty: Amnesty is suicide for Republicans. Not one of those 12 
million who broke our laws will vote Republican. Obama is laughing at 
the GOP. That is what President Trump said prior to the time he was 
elected President largely on that issue.
  We obviously should have some illegal immigrants take priority over 
others in removing them. For example, those who are on welfare should 
be removed first. But under no circumstances should the law become: You 
get in here, you hang around for a year and don't break another law, 
and we will allow you in. That would be suicide for the United States.
  Already one-sixth of the people in this country are foreign born, 
something we haven't had since at least Civil War times. So I beg every 
decisionmaker in the District of Columbia to respect Mr. Trump's words 
and not grant any more amnesty to illegals.

                          ____________________
```
