# RECOGNIZING VETERANS

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                          RECOGNIZING VETERANS

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, this Wednesday, on Veterans Day, we join 
together to honor the service and the sacrifice of over 17.6 million 
American veterans living in the United States.
  Answering the call to service requires a profound sense of sacrifice, 
duty, and enduring commitment, and we are grateful.
  Our Nation must honor these servicemembers with that same commitment, 
not only with praise and proclamations, but by caring for them when 
they return and in the years that follow.
  I hear from veterans in New York who are struggling to keep food on 
the table, find stable housing, or receive timely, effective healthcare 
from the VA. That is why I implore this body to ensure that those who 
served our country are provided with the care they earned and those 
that made the ultimate sacrifice are returned home to be honored with 
dignity.
  Mr. Speaker, from the floor of the House of Representatives, I thank 
all the servicemen and -women for keeping our country safe.

                          ____________________
```
