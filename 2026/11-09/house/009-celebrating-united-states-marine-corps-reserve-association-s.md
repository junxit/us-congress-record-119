# CELEBRATING UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS RESERVE ASSOCIATION'S CENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
CELEBRATING UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS RESERVE ASSOCIATION'S CENTENNIAL

  (Mr. Bergman of Michigan was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, yesterday was the National Veterans Parade.
  In fact, two of the three Vietnam veterans still remaining in 
Congress were there. My colleague sitting right in front of me,  Jim 
Baird from Indiana, rode in the parade aboard one of his weapons 
trucks, if you will, with some of his colleagues.
  It was a beautiful parade on a beautiful day across the street from 
the National Archives, but the real stars of the parade, I believe, 
were all of the young groups of marching bands, twirlers, Junior ROTC 
groups, and the organizations that support veterans and their families 
in so many ways.
  It was just a great day to be out there on The Mall yesterday, and I 
would suggest putting it on our calendars for next year because it will 
be that same Sunday here in D.C. prior to the celebration of Veterans 
Day.
  Veterans Day is November 11. Remember that Veterans Day is a 
celebration. We celebrate those who served. We celebrate who we are and 
how we remain a country because of our veterans' service.

  November 10, tomorrow, for those of you in the Marine Corps, is the 
Marine Corps' 251st birthday. We have a lot of things going on here.
  Mr. Speaker, this tie happens to be the colors of the Marine Corps, 
in case anybody knows, but the idea is to celebrate all of the veterans 
and their families who have served over time.
  It is my honor, though, today specifically, to honor an organization 
that I have been a part of for a long time, since I was still in 
uniform. I am honoring the 100th anniversary of the United States 
Marine Corps Reserve Association.
  Mr. Speaker, I will read the following commendation:
  ``In the year of our Lord Two Thousand and Twenty Six.
  ``United States Marine Corps Reserve Association 100th Anniversary.
  ``Whereas, President Woodrow Wilson signed the naval appropriations 
act on August 29, 1916, officially establishing the Marine Corps 
Reserve; and
  ``Whereas, Major General Melvin Maas, United States Marine Corps 
Reserve, later founded the United States Marine Corps Reserve 
Association to

[[Page H6085]]

foster readiness, professional development, and advocacy for the 
Reserve Force; and
  ``Whereas, Major General Maas, while serving in the United States 
House of Representatives, became a leading voice in Congress for 
national defense and the strategic importance of a Ready Reserve Corps; 
and
  ``Whereas, for a century, the Marine Corps Reserve Association has 
promoted the interests of the United States Marine Corps and the Marine 
Corps Reserve, helping to advance and preserve the security and defense 
of our Nation; and
  ``Whereas, today, the association represents Active, Reserve, and 
veteran members, tirelessly advocating for servicemembers, veterans, 
and their families on Capitol Hill and within local communities; and
  ``Whereas, in keeping with its foundational vision, the Marine Corps 
Reserve Association continues to champion legislative initiatives, 
partner with organizations like the Semper Fi & America's Fund, and 
support charitable and relief efforts across the country;
  ``Now, therefore, I, Congressman Jack Bergman, on behalf of the 
United States House of Representatives, do hereby commend the United 
States Marine Corps Reserve Association and join in celebrating their 
landmark 100th anniversary.
  ``In testimony whereof, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the 
seal of the United States House of Representatives to be affixed this 
9th day of November 2026.''
```
