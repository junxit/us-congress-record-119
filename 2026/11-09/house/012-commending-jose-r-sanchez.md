# COMMENDING JOSE R. SANCHEZ

**Speaking:** Ramirez, Delia C. (R000617) [D-IL]

```
                              {time}  1230
                       COMMENDING JOSE R. SANCHEZ

  (Mrs. Ramirez of Illinois was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, in honor of Latino heritage, I rise today 
to recognize my constituent, Jose R. Sanchez, for his inspiring 
dedication and his work ethic that reminds us that when Latinos set a 
goal, we make it happen.
  Jose Sanchez is a licensed clinical social worker with over 30 years 
of experience as a healthcare executive. In 2010, Sanchez became the 
only Latino hospital CEO in Illinois, managing what was known as the 
Norwegian American Hospital, which faced strong financial hardship and 
needed to rebuild the trust within the community.
  After seeing declining health in the community of Humboldt Park, he 
knew that, in his own words, ``the hospital had to be proactive, 
embrace the community, and form relationships'' and ``partnerships'' 
within the community.
  Under his leadership and vision, the Norwegian American Hospital, 
today known as Humboldt Park Health, has realized financial stability, 
extending care in our community; driven employment and economic 
development; enhanced the quality of care through partnerships; 
modernized systems; and helped address prominent health needs in 
Illinois' Third Congressional District.
  In 2024, Humboldt Park Health became the very first hospital in the 
Midwest and the 13th in the Nation to achieve health equity 
certification from the Joint Commission. In 2025, Mr. Sanchez helped 
open Humboldt Park Health's new wellness center, the first phase of a 
broader community health campus focused on wellness, accessibility, and 
empowerment.
  For Jose Sanchez, healthcare and managing a hospital is more than 
just treating patients. For him, for Jose, it is about building a 
healthier, more prosperous community.
  On behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, it is my honor 
to commend Jose R. Sanchez for his lifetime of leadership, vision, and 
a commitment to health equity and wellness. It is why, today, it is my 
honor to enter Jose Sanchez' name permanently into the Congressional 
Record. ``Congratulations''; ``Felicitaciones,'' Jose.


                   Honoring Marien Casillas Pabellon

  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Marien Casillas 
Pabellon, a proud Puerto Rican and organizer for our shared dignity and 
worker solidarity who reminds us to always put our faith into action.
  Born in Puerto Rico to a working class, politically engaged Baptist 
reverend and a librarian, Marien began her life as an organizer early 
on. At 21, she started working for the Seafarers International Union of 
Puerto Rico, where she led bargaining fights, handled grievances and 
mediation, developed workshops, and ran successful campaigns for 
workers' rights.
  In 2005, Marien moved to New Jersey to pursue a master's degree in 
labor and employment relations. During this time, she continued 
fighting for worker rights. In 2009, she became the executive director 
of New Labor, where she led efforts to offer Latino immigrants a voice 
in their workplace and community. Under Marien's leadership, New Labor 
became a leader in organizing, mobilizing thousands of immigrants to 
fight for workplace rights and healthcare in one of New Jersey's 
largest and most successful Workers Memorial Day actions.
  Marien now serves as the executive director of the West Suburban 
Action Project, also known as PASO, where she leads initiatives, 
advocating for immigrant communities, focusing on healthcare access, 
tenant rights, and addressing labor injustices. As we confronted 
Operation Midway Blitz over a year ago, Marien lent her voice and her 
organizing background to protect our neighbors from the Department of 
Homeland Security's terror and demand justice for Silverio Villegas 
Gonzalez, who was killed by ICE agents last September.
  Throughout her career, she has dedicated herself to community and 
labor organizing and coauthored: ``The Worker Center Handbook: A 
Practical Guide to Starting and Building the New Labor Movement.''
  It is my honor, on behalf of Illinois' Third Congressional District, 
to honor Marien Casillas Pabellon for her commitment to worker rights, 
including the rights of immigrant workers, and a reminder that worker 
solidarity will, in fact, protect us from fascist attacks.
  I thank Marien for embodying the values of community, compassion, and 
service. It is my honor to commend her and to permanently enter her 
name into the Congressional Record.
```
