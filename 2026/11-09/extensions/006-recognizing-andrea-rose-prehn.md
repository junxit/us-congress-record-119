# RECOGNIZING ANDREA ROSE-PREHN

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                     RECOGNIZING ANDREA ROSE-PREHN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and thank Andrea Rose-Prehn, of Aberdeen, South Dakota, for her six 
years of dedication and service to the State of South Dakota. Andrea 
served as the Northeast Area Director, providing exceptional service 
and representation to the northeast region of the state.
  Andrea is a skilled casework representative who has helped more than 
600 South Dakotans navigate challenges with federal agencies. In her 
time, she showcased an incredible ability to assist constituents with 
tax-related issues, as well as urgent immigration matters.
  She successfully worked with the United States Citizen and 
Immigration Service to aid Uniting For Ukraine families in South Dakota 
to expedite extensions for parole and work authorizations.
  Andrea was an incredible representative of my office, running a one-
person operation out of Aberdeen. She sought opportunities to be 
involved in the community, joining many government affairs and 
community leadership meetings.
  I'd also like to recognize her sons, Max and AJ.
  I'm grateful for Andrea's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.

                          ____________________
```
