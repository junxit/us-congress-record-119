# RECOGNIZING AIMEE HOYLE

**Speaking:** Johnson, Dusty (J000301) [R-SD]

```
                        RECOGNIZING AIMEE HOYLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and thank Aimee Hoyle, of Rapid City, South Dakota, for her eight years 
of dedication and service to my congressional office, and her more than 
13 years of service to the State of South Dakota. Aimee primarily 
served as our Casework Manager and Tribal Relations Coordinator and 
also spent time early on as my Northeast Regional Director.
  Among her achievements, Aimee's work as Casework Manager allowed our 
office to receive a Congressional Management Foundation award for 
Outstanding Constituent Services. Her leadership in managing a timely 
and effective casework program has helped thousands of South Dakotans.
  She personally helped nearly 1,000 South Dakotans navigate issues 
with federal agencies. Her specialty in this space allowed her to be a 
go-to expert in nearly any casework scenario.
  She also served as an in-state tribal liaison, connecting policy from 
Washington, D.C. to South Dakota's tribal communities.
  I'd also like to recognize her husband Travis, son Oliver, and step-
daughter Gabriela.
  I'm grateful for Aimee's years of service to the people of South 
Dakota and for her commitment to public service.
```
