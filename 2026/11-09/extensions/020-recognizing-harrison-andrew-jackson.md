# RECOGNIZING HARRISON ANDREW JACKSON

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
                  RECOGNIZING HARRISON ANDREW JACKSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
young man from Mayo, Florida, Harrison Andrew Jackson, who has been 
named a 2026 U.S. Presidential Scholar in Career and Technical 
Education.
  Harrison graduated from Lafayette High School as salutatorian of the 
Class of 2026. He is one of just 161 students nationwide to receive 
this prestigious honor this year. Only 20 were recognized for career 
and technical education, and Harrison is one of just four Presidential 
Scholars from the state of Florida.
  In high school, Harrison was active in FFA and Future Business 
Leaders of America. He also took on agricultural scientific research, 
and that work gave him the chance to represent his community and our 
state at the national level.

[[Page E1170]]

  Harrison is now a freshman at the University of Florida, where he 
studies Food and Resource Economics and keeps building on his passion 
for agriculture.
  Mr. Speaker, Harrison grew up in rural Lafayette County and earned 
one of the highest honors our country gives a high school student. That 
says a lot about him, and it says a lot about the families and teachers 
in our small towns who help students like him succeed. Our farms and 
ranches need young people like Harrison, and he gives me real 
confidence in what comes next. North Central Florida is proud of him.
  On behalf of Florida's 3rd Congressional District, I congratulate 
Harrison Andrew Jackson on this well-deserved achievement, and I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing him.

                          ____________________
```
