# RECOGNIZING ANSLEY BOYLAN

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                       RECOGNIZING ANSLEY BOYLAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ansley Boylan for her 
service to the House Committee on Energy and Commerce.
  A graduate of Baylor University, Ansley began her career on Capitol 
Hill in 2017 in the office of Representative Jeb Hensarling (TX-05), 
serving in operations roles in his office and later in that of 
Representative Ken Buck (CO-04). I got to know AnsIey when she served 
as Operations Director for Representative Larry Bucshon(IN-08) and was 
impressed by her warmth and attention to detail. When I became Chairman 
of the House Committee on Energy and Commerce in January 2025, I was 
very grateful that Ansley joined our team as Director of Operations. 
She quickly became a trusted member of our team and a steady presence 
for all the Members of the Committee and staff.
  After nearly a decade of service to the United States House of 
Representatives, Ansley will soon depart the House Committee on Energy 
and Commerce to pursue a new role in the private sector. I thank her 
for her commitment to the Committee, the Congress, and our country. I 
wish her all the best alongside her husband, Matt, son Hank, and their 
growing family.

                          ____________________
```
