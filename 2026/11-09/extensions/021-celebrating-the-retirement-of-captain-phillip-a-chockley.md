# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF CAPTAIN PHILLIP A. CHOCKLEY

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
       CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF CAPTAIN PHILLIP A. CHOCKLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the retirement of 
Captain Phillip A. Chockley from the United States Navy after 23 years 
of honorable service.
  A native of Shelbyville, Tennessee, Captain Chockley earned a 
Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy and Political Science from the 
University of Tennessee at Martin, a Juris Doctor from the University 
of Tennessee, and a Master of Laws in International and National 
Security Law from Columbia University. He received his commission in 
October 2002 through the Student Program. After being admitted to 
practice law in Tennessee in 2003, Captain Chockley attended Officer 
Indoctrination and Naval Justice School before beginning his career as 
a Navy judge advocate.
  Captain Chockley's first duty station was the Trial Service Office 
East in Norfolk, VA, where he served as a command services attorney and 
trial counsel. Next, he served as the Deputy Director of Command 
Services, Assistant Staff Judge Advocate for Command, Navy Region 
Europe, and lastly Senior Trial Counsel in the Regional Legal Service 
office of Europe and Southwest Asia, located in Naples, Italy.
  In 2009, Captain Chockley deployed to Baghdad, Iraq, in support of 
Operation Iraqi Freedom. There, he served as the Officer in Charge of 
the Central Criminal Court of Iraq Liaison Office. After returning from 
Iraq, he assumed duty as Flag Secretary to the Deputy Judge Advocate 
General of the Navy. Captain Chockley next served as Deputy Fleet Judge 
Advocate at U.S. Third Fleet, providing operational and administrative 
legal support to the Commander on a variety of issues facing the fleet. 
After concluding his tour at Third Fleet, Captain Chockley transferred 
to Norfolk, Virginia, where he served as Staff Judge Advocate to the 
Eisenhower Carrier Strike Group, deploying to the Fifth and Sixth Fleet 
areas of operations in support of Operation Inherent Resolve. From 
Norfolk, Captain Chockley and his family returned to Europe, where he 
served as an Assistant Legal Counsel and eventually Chief of 
Operational Law at U.S. Africa Command in Stuttgart, Germany.
  Following duty at AFRICOM, Captain Chockley served as a judge 
advocate assignment officer (LCDR Detailer) at PERS-4416 in Millington, 
Tennessee, before moving to the Washington, D.C. area to serve as Legal 
Counsel to the Chief of Naval Personnel/DCNO N1 and, ultimately, Legal 
Counsel to the Vice Chief of Naval Operations.
  Captain Chockley's individual service medals include the Legion of 
Merit (Gold Star in Lieu of Second Award), Defense Meritorious Service 
Medal (Bronze Oak Leaf in Lieu of Second Award), the Meritorious 
Service Medal (Gold Star in Lieu of Fourth Award), the Navy 
Commendation Medal, and the Navy Achievement Medal (Gold Star in Lieu 
of Second Award).
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me in celebrating the 
retirement of Captain Phillip A. Chockley from the United States Navy. 
Captain Chockley spent two decades serving our country with bravery and 
honor. I thank Captain Chockley for his years of service, and I wish 
him the best in retirement.
```
