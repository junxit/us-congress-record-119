# RECOGNIZING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MINNESOTA MUSLIMS FOR LIFE BLOOD DRIVE

**Speaking:** Morrison, Kelly (M001234) [D-MN]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MINNESOTA MUSLIMS FOR LIFE 
                              BLOOD DRIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. KELLY MORRISON

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Ms. MORRISON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Minnesota 
Chapter of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community of Minnesota for its 
continued dedication to service, compassion, and unity through the 
annual Muslims for Life Blood Drive, commemorating the anniversary of 
the September 11 terrorist attacks.
  Since 2011, the Muslims for Life campaign has honored the victims of 
September 11 by bringing communities together to donate blood and help 
save lives. In partnership with the American Red Cross, the Ahmadiyya 
Muslim Community has organized blood drives across the country, 
including at the Minnesota State Capitol. Through these efforts, the 
community has transformed a day of national tragedy into an opportunity 
for community, service, and remembrance.
  The Muslims for Life campaign reminds us that even in the face of 
devastation and division, we can find common ground through acts of 
kindness and service to others. On behalf of the people of Minnesota, I 
thank the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community, its volunteers, and all those who 
have participated in this meaningful tradition.
```
