# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF DR. HERBERT H. ROWE, JR. PASTOR, UPPERROOM BIBLE CHURCH

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
  CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF DR. HERBERT H. ROWE, JR. PASTOR, UPPERROOM 
                              BIBLE CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
life of Dr. Herbert H. Rowe, Jr. He was born March 26, 1957, in New 
Orleans, Louisiana and peacefully transitioned to be with the Lord 
surrounded by his wife and family on February 9, 2024. Pastor Rowe was 
preceded in death by his parents, Herbert H. Rowe, Sr., and Ora D. 
Rowe. He is survived by his loving and devoted wife, Dr. Carol Guillory 
Rowe; his devoted son, Caleb Jordan Rowe; and Chelsi Watson Rowe. He is 
also survived by his sisters, Patsy Rowe Brown (Charles), Carolyn Rowe 
Treaudo (Roland); and brothers Reginald Rowe (Amelia); and, Oliver 
Tarvin (Diana). Additionally, he is remembered by a host of nieces, 
nephews, cousins, aunts, and uncles, all touched by his wisdom and 
love.
  A dedicated servant of God and visionary leader, as a child 
matriculating through New Orleans public schools, he attended and 
graduated from Walter L. Cohen High School and continued his 
educational journey through Xavier University of Louisiana, where he 
cultivated a strong foundation for his life's work. While attending 
Xavier University, he pledged Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. in the 
fall of 1976. He also received his Doctor of Divinity from Faith Impact 
Bible College.
  Born Roman Catholic, Dr. Rowe received revelation of living and 
forgiving God for himself as his then girlfriend, Carol Guillory, 
convinced him to attend a church service where he received Christ as 
his savior. Carol Guillory and Dr. Herbert Rowe joined in holy 
matrimony on October 29, 1988. In 1995, the beloved couple welcomed 
their son, Caleb Jordan Rowe, bringing added joy to their loving 
family.
  Dr. Rowe was employed at United Parcel Service (UPS) for over 15 
years, where he exemplified dedication in his professional and 
spiritual pursuits. Departing from his management position at UPS, his 
journey as a minister of the gospel began in 1993 leading one of the 
greatest and strongest faith driven ministries. The greatness of his 
tenure in ministry allowed for authentic mentorship as well as 
relationships with Dr. Leroy Thompson, Kenneth Copeland, Kenneth E. 
Hagan, Drs. IV and Bridget Hillard, Drs. Mike and DeeDee Freeman, and 
ultimately his father in the gospel, became Dr. Frederick K. C. Price 
for over 25 years. As a board member of The Fellowship of International 
Word of Faith Ministries, now The Fellowship, Dr. Rowe made significant 
contributions to the global faith community under the leadership of 
Apostle Frederick K. C. Price and Apostle Michael A. Freeman.
  Alongside his wife, he founded the Faith Impact Bible Institute, The 
Upperroom Bible Church and Preschool and Academy, and Faith Impact TV 
and Radio Ministries. The Faith Impact TV Broadcast, spanning locally, 
nationally, and worldwide, stands as a testament to Dr. Rowe's enduring 
vision.
  For more than three decades, he passionately preached the message of 
faith, establishing The Upperroom Bible Church--one church with 
multiple locations--a testament to his vision of reaching and teaching 
people how to believe and receive from God on purpose. His impact on 
faith, service and family will forever resonate in the hearts of those 
he touched and inspired. Pastor Rowe dedicated his life to ``mending 
broken people.'' Dr. Rowe's legacy extends through his beloved family. 
He leaves behind his cherished wife, Dr. Carol Guillory Rowe, who stood 
by his side in their shared commitment to faith and service. His only 
son, Caleb Jordan Rowe, and a daughter by love, Chelsi Watson Rowe, 
both carry forward the family's legacy of love and devotion.
  The man, the legacy, will never be forgotten. His transition to 
Heaven leaves a void in the hearts of those who knew him, but his 
legacy lives on through the family he leaves behind. His impact on 
faith, service, and family will forever be remembered and celebrated by 
those who continue to carry his torch of love and devotion. I am 
honored to say he was my dear friend and mentor. May my friend rest 
well. His legacy will live on forever.
```
