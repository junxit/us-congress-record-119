# HONORING ARMANDO JOSE RIVAS HERRERA FOR HIS SERVICE TO CENTRAL FLORIDA AS A FIREFIGHTER

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING ARMANDO JOSE RIVAS HERRERA FOR HIS SERVICE TO CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                            AS A FIREFIGHTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, a career firefighter, paramedic, instructor, 
and special operations team member, Armando Jose Rivas Herrera has 
dedicated his professional life to ensuring that first responders are 
better prepared, more capable, and safer when responding to 
emergencies.
  He began his service in Caracas, Venezuela, where he worked as an 
emergency nurse, paramedic, disaster-response professional, and 
instructor. These experiences shaped his commitment to training, 
leadership, and the belief that preparation saves lives.
  Today, Armando serves in Florida's fire service, where he actively 
supports fire suppression, emergency medical services, special 
operations, and firefighter development. As an instructor and Field 
Training Officer, he has helped prepare countless firefighters to 
respond effectively in high-risk environments. His commitment to 
excellence was recognized when he was named Fire Instructor of the Year 
for 2025.
  In 2022, Armando founded Fire Rescue Group, an organization dedicated 
to expanding access to high-quality firefighter training and emergency-
response education. Through this effort, he has provided instruction, 
consulting, and mentorship to firefighters and emergency-response 
organizations across the United States and numerous countries around 
the world, fostering international collaboration and the exchange of 
lifesaving knowledge.
  Armando has also demonstrated a deep commitment to humanitarian 
service, helping coordinate support and emergency resources for first 
responders affected by the 2026 earthquake in Venezuela.
```
