# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF MARGARET ORR, METEOROLOGIST AT WDSU, CHANNEL 6

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
  CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF MARGARET ORR, METEOROLOGIST AT WDSU, 
                               CHANNEL 6

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Margaret 
Orr, a New Orleans native and icon, and chief meteorologist, as she is 
set to retire after 45 years on the air. Margaret joined WDSU News in 
1979 as a general assignment reporter. She became co-host of the 
``Breakfast Edition'' with reporter Bill Stanley, and later co-host of 
the ``World's Fair Show'' with Alec Gifford. For more than 20 years, 
Margaret has co-hosted the Children's Hospital Telethon with Norman 
Robinson helping to raise millions of dollars for Children's Hospital.
  Margaret grew up in New Orleans where she graduated from Louise S. 
McGehee High School, and Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge.
  In 1965, when Hurricane Betsy blew the roof off her childhood home, 
she was mesmerized by her view of the sky when the eye of the hurricane 
passed overhead. That kicked off a lifelong obsession with weather 
events--and solidified a remarkable ability to always look for the 
positive in virtually any scenario. As her fascination with weather 
grew, she completed the Mississippi State Broadcast Meteorology program 
with flying colors. Officially bitten by the news bug, she graduated in 
1975 and moved up to Charleston, South Carolina and got a job working 
at a TV station as a receptionist. In the evenings, she tagged along 
with the news crew and learned how to use equipment so she could film 
segments. Eventually, a position opened, and she worked there for a few 
years before relocating to Baton Rouge, and then eventually getting a 
call from WDSU in New Orleans.
  In 2009, she began forecasting weather for the 5, 6 and 10 p.m. news 
after many years on the morning news. Margaret Orr is a meteorologist 
who gained prominence for her work at WDSU-TV, an NBC affiliate in New 
Orleans, Louisiana. She has been with the station for several years, 
providing weather forecasts and coverage of significant weather events, 
including hurricanes. Orr is known for her expertise and dedication to 
keeping viewers informed and safe during severe weather situations in 
the Gulf Coast region.
  Over the years, she would become a trusted source of news and 
weather, helping guide the city through some of its toughest times, 
including Hurricane Katrina. While most of the station evacuated, a 
small skeleton crew, which included reporters Roop Raj, Dan Thomas, 
Helena Moreno and Orr, remained behind. As conditions worsened, they 
were forced to leave the TV set and seek shelter at the Hilton New 
Orleans Riverside Hotel. Orr, meanwhile, was desperate for the storm to 
blow over so she could get outside and start relaying what she saw to 
her viewers.
  Through her conversational, enthusiastic reporting style, she's 
become a larger-than-life local celebrity--characterized by her 
signature red hair and blazers as bright as her fun-loving personality.
  Whether it was as a weather forecaster, a colleague or a friend, Orr 
has consistently been there for her community with genuine care. It has 
not been uncommon for Orr to become emotional, wearing her heart on her 
sleeve, as she urges viewers to heed her warnings. Margaret is a member 
of both the American Meteorology Society and the National Weather 
Association. She has Seals of Approval from both. Margaret has also won 
first-place awards from the Associated Press and the New Orleans Press 
Club for breaking weather.
  When Margaret is not at work, she is likely tweeting about the 
weather, something she plans to continue after retirement. You can 
follow her on Twitter by following @margaretorr. She tweets the latest 
forecast and during severe weather, she gives the latest warnings to 
keep you safe.
  Margaret is married with three children and a dog named Bleu. Bleu is 
royalty--he was King of Barkus. Margaret likes to garden, paint, read, 
swim and bike.
  On this day I wish her many happy days during her retirement. A 
special salute as she sends happiness outside of the newsroom.
```
