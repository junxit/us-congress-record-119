# RECOGNIZING TYLER HOAGLAND

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING TYLER HOAGLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tyler Hoagland 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Tyler has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Tyler, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Tyler's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Oberon Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Tyler Hoagland on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
