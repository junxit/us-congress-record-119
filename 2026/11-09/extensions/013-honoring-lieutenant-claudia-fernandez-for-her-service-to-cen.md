# HONORING LIEUTENANT CLAUDIA FERNANDEZ FOR HER SERVICE TO CENTRAL FLORIDA AS A FIREFIGHTER

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING LIEUTENANT CLAUDIA FERNANDEZ FOR HER SERVICE TO CENTRAL 
                        FLORIDA AS A FIREFIGHTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, for 26 years, Lieutenant Claudia Fernandez has 
faithfully served the residents of Orange County, advancing through the 
ranks from Firefighter to Engineer and ultimately to Lieutenant. Since 
2021, she has served as the department's Behavioral Health Coordinator, 
helping firefighters and their families navigate some of life's most 
challenging moments.
  Lieutenant Fernandez has been instrumental in strengthening Orange 
County Fire Rescue's Peer Support Program, earning the trust and 
respect of her colleagues through her compassion, professionalism, and 
commitment to firefighter wellness. Her efforts have helped create a 
culture in which first responders are empowered to seek support and 
care for their mental health.
  Her distinguished service has been recognized with numerous honors, 
including a Life Saving Award, Fire Officer of the Year, a National 
Association of Counties Achievement Award, and the Outstanding Training 
Contribution Award. She was also selected as a speaker at the 2024 
Center for Public Safety Excellence Conference, where she shared her 
expertise in behavioral health and peer support.
  What stands out most about Lieutenant Fernandez is her desire to help 
other departments build strong support systems for their firefighters 
and families. Through her leadership, she has made a lasting impact not 
only in Orange County but throughout the fire service community.
```
