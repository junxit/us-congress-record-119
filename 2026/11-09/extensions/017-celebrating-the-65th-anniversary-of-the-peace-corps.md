# CELEBRATING THE 65TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE PEACE CORPS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
          CELEBRATING THE 65TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE PEACE CORPS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 65th 
anniversary of the Peace Corps and its enduring commitment to putting 
service to work for communities around the world.
  On March 1, 1961, President John F. Kennedy issued his historic call 
to service and established the Peace Corps, launching a new era of 
American volunteerism abroad. Over the past 65 years, nearly 250,000 
Americans have served as Peace Corps volunteers in 144 countries, 
fostering people-to-people diplomacy in areas such as education, 
health, agriculture, environment, youth in development, and community 
economic development.
  My wife, Patti, and I heard the call of President Kennedy and decided 
to begin our marriage serving together as Peace Corps volunteers in 
Ethiopia. Both Patti and I credit our careers in public service to the 
hard work and rewarding service we experienced all those years ago in 
Ethiopia.
  Today, the Peace Corps continues to exemplify its slogan, ``the 
toughest job you'll ever love,'' building sustainable partnerships and 
strengthening communities around the world. It has enhanced global 
perceptions of the United States by demonstrating American values 
through service, humility, cultural exchange, and partnership rather 
than through political or military means.
  Service in the Peace Corps has inspired generations of Americans to 
pursue careers in public service, education, economic development, 
diplomacy, and health, creating a lasting pipeline of globally minded 
civic leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate this momentous 65th 
anniversary of the Peace Corps. I rise to honor the more than 250,000 
individuals who have served as Peace Corps volunteers for their 
dedication to peace, friendship, and community-driven development. I 
commend the Peace Corps' enduring mission to put purpose, passion, and 
skills to work for America and the world.
```
