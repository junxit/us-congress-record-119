# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF DARLINGTON COUNTY CHIEF DEPUTY CLIFTON J. HOWLE

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF DARLINGTON COUNTY CHIEF DEPUTY CLIFTON 
                                J. HOWLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RUSSELL FRY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and service of 
Darlington County Chief Deputy Clifton J. Howle, a devoted law 
enforcement officer and public servant who spent more than three 
decades serving the people of South Carolina.
  Chief Deputy Howle began his law enforcement career in 1993 with the 
South Carolina Department of Probation, Parole and Pardon Services. 
During his many years with the agency, he served in several leadership 
positions, including as an agent in charge and assistant deputy 
director for field operations. After retiring from the department in 
2022, his commitment to public service continued as he served as a 
municipal judge in both Jefferson and McBee.

[[Page E1169]]

  In 2025, Chief Deputy Howle joined Sheriff Michael August's 
administration as Chief Deputy of the Darlington County Sheriff's 
Office, bringing with him decades of experience and leadership. His 
colleagues remembered him not only for his service, but also for the 
way he treated those around him with kindness, dignity, and respect. 
His peers described him as more than a colleague and leader, but as 
someone who was deeply cared for by those who had the privilege of 
serving alongside him.
  Chief Deputy Howle dedicated his career to protecting his neighbors, 
strengthening his community, and upholding the responsibilities 
entrusted to him. His many years of service leave a lasting legacy 
throughout Darlington County and across our state. I ask that my 
colleagues join me in keeping his family, friends, and all those whose 
lives he touched in our thoughts and prayers.

                          ____________________
```
