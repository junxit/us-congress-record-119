# HONORING THE INDIANAPOLIS (IN) CHAPTER OF THE LINKS, INCORPORATED

**Speaking:** Carson, Andre (C001072) [D-IN]

```
   HONORING THE INDIANAPOLIS (IN) CHAPTER OF THE LINKS, INCORPORATED

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANDRE CARSON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. CARSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Indianapolis (IN) 
Chapter of The Links, Incorporated, in celebration of its 75th 
anniversary of friendship, service, and commitment to the Indianapolis 
community.
  The Links, Incorporated was established in Philadelphia in 1946 and 
currently has approximately 17,000 members in 302 chapters across the 
United States, the Bahamas, and the United Kingdom. Founded in 1951, 
the Indianapolis Chapter has spent three-quarters of a century 
transforming our community through civic, educational, and cultural 
service.
  Over the decades, the Chapter has supported local organizations such 
as the Phyllis Wheatley YWCA, Flanner House, the Alpha Home, the Madam 
Walker Legacy Center, and UNCF. Its Celebrity Barbeque and Grille 
celebrated minority achievement while raising nearly $300,000 for 
community programs over the past 30 years. The Links are well-known in 
our community through their work to uplift others. They host health 
fairs, raise funds for and award scholarships, and promote civic 
engagement through voter registration drives and a weekly ``Community 
Link'' segment on WISH-TV. The Chapter's commitment to the arts 
continues through LIMELIGHT, a benefit concert featuring violinist, 
trumpeter, and composer Justin S. Kinchen alongside the Indianapolis 
Chamber Orchestra. Proceeds support the Chapter's programs, including 
My Sheep, a year-long series of workshops for middle and high school 
girls in Indianapolis.
  Mr. Speaker, as the Indianapolis (IN) Chapter of The Links, 
Incorporated gathers on November 15th at the Indiana Roof Ballroom for 
its 75th Anniversary Gala, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring 
its dedicated service and its enduring commitment to excellence, 
service, and friendship.
```
