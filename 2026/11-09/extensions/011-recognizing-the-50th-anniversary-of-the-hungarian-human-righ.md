# RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE HUNGARIAN HUMAN RIGHTS FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE HUNGARIAN HUMAN RIGHTS 
                               FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. HARRIS of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
50th anniversary of the Hungarian Human Rights Foundation.
  For five decades, the Hungarian Human Rights Foundation has 
demonstrated what can be accomplished when people are willing to stand 
up for human dignity, defend fundamental rights, and make sure that the 
voices of minority communities are heard.
  What began as a small group of dedicated advocates grew into an 
organization with a lasting presence in Washington and an international 
reputation for documenting human rights abuses and bringing them to the 
attention of policymakers. Over the years, the Foundation has testified 
before Congress, worked with American policymakers, and helped ensure 
that the rights of Hungarian communities in Romania, Slovakia, Serbia, 
Ukraine, and elsewhere remained part of the international conversation. 
Its work was particularly important during the Cold War, when reliable 
information about the treatment of Hungarian minorities behind the Iron 
Curtain was often difficult to obtain.
  That history is a reminder that defending human rights is not 
something that can be taken for granted. It requires people willing to 
speak up, even when doing so is dangerous.
  As we celebrate 50 years of the Hungarian Human Rights Foundation, we 
should recognize the generations of leaders, volunteers, and advocates 
who built this organization and carried its mission forward. And we 
should look toward the next 50 years with the same determination.
  Congratulations to the Hungarian Human Rights Foundation on this 
remarkable milestone. I thank them for their service to the Hungarian 
people, to the Hungarian-American community, and to the cause of human 
rights.
  As a proud Hungarian-American, I am honored to join in celebrating 
the 50th anniversary of the Hungarian Human Rights Foundation.
  Congratulations.

                          ____________________
```
