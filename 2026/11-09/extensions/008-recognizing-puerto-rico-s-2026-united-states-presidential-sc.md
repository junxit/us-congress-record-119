# RECOGNIZING PUERTO RICO'S 2026 UNITED STATES PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLARS

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
   RECOGNIZING PUERTO RICO'S 2026 UNITED STATES PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLARS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend 
three exceptional students from Puerto Rico who have been named 2026 
United States Presidential Scholars.
  The U.S. Presidential Scholars Program is one of the Nation's highest 
honors for graduating high school seniors. It recognizes not only 
outstanding performance on college entrance examinations, but also 
exceptional scholarship and a demonstrated commitment to community. 
This year, only 161 high school seniors across the United States 
received this distinction, and it is with great pride that I include in 
the Record the names of the following Presidential Scholars from Puerto 
Rico:

       Francisco Javier Ferre of Guaynabo, Colegio San Ignacio de 
     Loyola;
       Mardi Victoria Roques Colon of San Juan, St. John's School; 
     and
       Ricardo Antonio Rovira of Guaynabo, Colegio San Ignacio de 
     Loyola.
  Each of these students has shown a deep commitment to developing 
Puerto Rico's next generation of leaders and to serving their peers 
through a range of initiatives. Whether through student government or 
through research and academic projects, their work has been guided by a 
common purpose: to strengthen their communities and to serve them with 
conviction, determination, and excellence.
  Francisco, Mardi, and Ricardo have not only met that standard of 
excellence but exceeded it. I am honored by their achievements and by 
what they represent for the youth of Puerto Rico.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating these 
remarkable students. May their success serve as an inspiration to 
others.

                          ____________________
```
