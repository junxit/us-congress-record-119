# HONORING GARRISON COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR RYAN JEFFERS

```
         HONORING GARRISON COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR RYAN JEFFERS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Command Sergeant Major 
Jeffers, a soldier whose more than two decades of service reflect the 
very best of the United States Army and the noncommissioned officer 
corps.
  Born in Chatsworth, Georgia, Command Sergeant Major Jeffers entered 
the Army on June 1, 2000, and completed One Station Unit Training with 
B Company, 2nd Battalion, 54th Infantry at Fort Benning, Georgia. From 
there he built a career defined by leadership at every level of the 
infantry.
  At Fort Drum, New York, with the 2nd Battalion, 14th Infantry, he 
served in every role from machine gunner to team leader and deployed to 
Kosovo and Afghanistan. At Fort Campbell, Kentucky, with the 1st 
Battalion, 187th Infantry, he served as a team leader, squad leader, 
and platoon sergeant and completed three combat tours in Iraq. He then 
trained the next generation of soldiers as a Senior Drill Sergeant at 
Fort Knox, Kentucky.
  Returning to Fort Campbell, he deployed to Afghanistan with the 1st 
Squadron, 33rd Cavalry, and later served at 101st Airborne Division 
Headquarters as a Senior Operations Sergeant, deploying to Liberia and 
Iraq. As a First Sergeant in the 1st Battalion, 506th Infantry 
Regiment, he led soldiers in B Company, D Company, and Headquarters 
Company for 30 months, including a deployment to Ghana in support of 
Regionally Aligned Forces-Africa.
  Command Sergeant Major Jeffers also invested in the Army's future 
leaders. At the 101st Noncommissioned Officers Academy, he served as a 
Master Leader Course facilitator and Basic Leader Course branch chief. 
He then served as Operations Sergeant Major of the 2nd Battalion, 327th 
Infantry Regiment, before returning to the 1st Battalion, 506th 
Infantry Regiment as its Command Sergeant Major from June 2022 to 
September 2024. In 2023, he deployed with the battalion to Estonia in 
support of Operation European Assure, Deter and Reinforce, helping 
strengthen the NATO alliance.
  His decorations reflect a career of valor and sustained excellence. 
They include the Bronze Star Medal with Oak Leaf Cluster, the 
Meritorious Service Medal with three Oak Leaf Clusters, the Joint 
Service Commendation Medal, the Army Commendation Medal with four Oak 
Leaf Clusters, and the Meritorious Unit Citation. He wears the Combat 
Infantryman Badge, the Expert Infantryman Badge, the Drill Sergeant 
Badge, and the Parachutist and Air Assault Badges. He is a graduate of 
the United States Army Sergeants Major Academy and holds both a 
bachelor's and a master's degree in organizational leadership and 
management.
  Behind every soldier is a family that serves alongside them. Command 
Sergeant Major Jeffers is married to his wife, Cara, and is the proud 
father of Mackenzie and identical twin sons, Sebastian and Thatcher. 
Their sacrifices, through years of deployments and moves, deserve our 
deep gratitude.
  Mr. Speaker, Command Sergeant Major Jeffers has led from the front, 
trained those who follow, and carried the Army's standards across 
multiple continents and combat zones. I ask my colleagues to join me in 
thanking him and his family for their service to our Nation and every 
success and joy as they celebrate his well-earned retirement in the 
years ahead.
```
