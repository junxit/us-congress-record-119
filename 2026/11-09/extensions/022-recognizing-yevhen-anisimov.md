# RECOGNIZING YEVHEN ANISIMOV

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING YEVHEN ANISIMOV

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Yevhen Anisimov 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Yevhen has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Yevhen, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Yevhen's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Oberon Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Yevhen Anisimov on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
