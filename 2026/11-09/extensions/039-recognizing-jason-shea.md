# RECOGNIZING JASON SHEA

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

```
                         RECOGNIZING JASON SHEA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FRANK PALLONE, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to Jason Alan 
Shea, who has served on the frontline of coastal resiliency development 
in his role of project manager for the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers 
(USACE) New York District. Jason is leaving this role of distinguished 
service with several accomplished projects directly protecting the 
coastline of the 6th Congressional District. Jason holds more than 30 
years of experience delivering complex federal resources, including 
flood-risk-management and disaster response initiatives. Jason not only 
displays exceptional technical leadership, public service, and team 
performance--he sets the standard.
  Earlier in his career, he managed water-resource studies for the 
USACE Los Angeles District and practiced civil engineering consulting. 
Jason served as a Supervisory Civil Engineer with the USACE New York 
District, where he led planning and watershed programs, supervised 
staff across multiple disciplines and grade levels, and managed major 
Civil Works studies and post-disaster recovery efforts. He held 
temporary GS-14 leadership assignments as Branch Chief for the Sandy 
Planning Branch and as an HQUSACE North Atlantic Division Regional 
Integration Team Planner. It is undeniable that excellence in USACE 
operations nationwide are products of Jason's countless mentorship and 
career involvement.
  By the numbers, Jason has had oversight success in leading 
multidisciplinary Project Delivery Teams through the design, planning, 
and construction of high-profile infrastructure projects valued at up 
to $1 billion. Together, with Jason's instrumental partnership, Sea 
Bright's coastal renourishment and feasibility reevaluation effort has 
transformed the longevity of the Sea Bright coastline. A project my 
constituents fiercely support. His portfolio also includes the Passaic 
Tidal Coastal Project in Newark; the Minish Park Coastal Erosion 
Project; and multi-agency offshore borrow-area engineering and 
permitting studies. Jason has also served as Project Manager for Flood 
Plain Management Services, Planning Assistance to States, and Special 
Investigations programs. It is incredible to work with someone with a 
multifaceted work ethic.
  In times of national crisis, Jason has served as a national-level 
emergency manager supporting FEMA under Emergency Support Function No. 
3. He has served as the Lead ESF No. 3 Representative at the FEMA 
Region 2 Regional Response Coordination Center and has deployed 
nationwide as a Deputy or Acting Team Leader, responsible for all USACE 
Planning and Response Teams on a national disaster.
  I will always appreciate Jason's incredible support during Hurricane 
Sandy. The teams covering debris removal, emergency power, temporary 
housing, infrastructure assessment, and restoration of critical public 
facilities exceeded expectations under Jason's leadership. Over time, 
Jason Shea has been recognized as Commander's Supervisor of the Year, 
Project Delivery Team of the Year, Planning Team of the Year, and 
multiple Humanitarian and Civilian Service Commendation Medals. These 
recognitions are truly well deserved
  Mr. Speaker, it should go without saying that the American people, 
New Jerseyans, and the people of the 6th Congressional District have 
been well served by Jason Shea. Even though he is moving on from his 
current role as Project Manager, I wish him best in his future 
endeavors and know that whatever path he chooses will be a successful 
one.
```
