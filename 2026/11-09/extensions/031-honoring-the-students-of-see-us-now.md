# HONORING THE STUDENTS OF SEE US NOW

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                  HONORING THE STUDENTS OF SEE US NOW

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of National 
Adoption Month to honor the students of See Us Now, a student-led 
nonprofit organization dedicated to advocating for the safety, dignity, 
and future of children in foster care. See Us Now began in Florida when 
a troubling discovery inspired a young person to act. After 
volunteering with a group home for foster children, Charissa Wagner 
learned that children in need of safe and stable housing were living in 
tents. Charissa and her mother, Kari Wagner, responded with compassion 
and determination. Together with a committed team of teenagers, they 
established an organization to bring greater visibility to children in 
foster care and ensure their needs are heard. These young advocates 
have brought their message to Washington, meeting with Members of 
Congress to raise awareness of the foster housing shortage and 
encourage meaningful solutions. They helped build support for the Find 
and Protect Foster Youth Act, versions of which passed the House and 
Senate in 2024. Through their advocacy for Missing and Trafficked 
Foster Children Awareness Week, they have also drawn attention to the 
vulnerability of children who go missing from foster care and the 
urgent need to protect them from exploitation.
  Additionally, these students have advocated for the Ensuring Medicaid 
Continuity for Children in Foster Care Act, legislation I have been 
proud to lead. Their efforts underscore the importance of ensuring that 
children in foster care maintain access to the health care and support 
they need. National Adoption Month reminds us that every child deserves 
the safety, stability, and unconditional love of a permanent family. 
More than 330,000 children are in foster care across our country, and 
more than 46,000 are legally free to be adopted. Behind each of these 
numbers is a child with individual hopes, talents, and dreams. The 
students of See Us Now are helping ensure these children are seen, 
heard, and remembered. Their work demonstrates the profound difference 
young people can make when they turn compassion into action. Through 
persistence and leadership, they have elevated the voices of vulnerable 
children and encouraged others to share in the responsibility of 
supporting them.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Charissa 
Wagner, Kari Wagner, and the students of See Us Now for their 
dedication to children in foster care. I would like to give special 
recognition to the founding members of See Us Now: Bryce Wagner, Kobe 
Deng, and Gabriel Lopez, who also serve as Lead Advocates. I further 
recognize the organization's other Lead Advocates: Nishant Shah, Ella 
Bradley, Adele Bradley, Izzy Stokes, Bryce Nolt, Noah Martinez, Nikos 
Tziotis, and Shalen Shah; and its members: Alexandra Ohman, Aarush 
Kumar, Micah Goldstein, Ian Clearwater, and Charleigh Whelan. During 
National Adoption Month, I encourage all Americans to consider how they 
can help, whether through adoption, fostering, or supporting the 
families and organizations that care for these children. I commend 
these young leaders for their service and wish them continued success 
in this important mission.
```
