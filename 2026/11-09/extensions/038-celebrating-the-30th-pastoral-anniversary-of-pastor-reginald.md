# CELEBRATING THE 30TH PASTORAL ANNIVERSARY OF PASTOR REGINALD NICHOLAS, SR.

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
CELEBRATING THE 30TH PASTORAL ANNIVERSARY OF PASTOR REGINALD NICHOLAS, 
                                  SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate three 
decades of faithful, phenomenal leadership from an outstanding New 
Orleans Minister, Pastor Reginald Nicholas, Sr. Pastor Reginald 
Nicholas, Sr. is the 12th Presiding Pastor of the Olive Branch Baptist 
Church and was born on April 12, 1968, to the late Arthur Matthews and 
Irma Nicholas. Pastor Nicholas was reared by his grandparents, Herbert 
and Francis Wallace. He accepted Christ on April 12, 1968, and was 
baptized at St. Paul Missionary Baptist Church by his late great uncle, 
Pastor Haywood Custer.
  In 1980, he became a proud member of New Jerusalem Baptist Church 
under the leadership of Pastor Emeritus George Davis Sr. While at New 
Jerusalem, Pastor Nicholas served under the leadership of Pastor George 
Davis Sr. It was there he grew in the grace of God while serving as 
Director of Brotherhood and Foreign Mission; Co-Chairman of the Deacon 
Board; Sunday School and Bible Study Teacher. In August 1986, he was 
called to the Ministry. On October 12, 1986, Pastor Nicholas preached 
his first sermon, ``Never Look Down on a Man, unless you are Picking 
Him Up''. He was ordained on July 30, 1988. Pastor Nicholas received a 
Bachelor of Theology, Master of Biblical Studies, Graduate Specialist, 
and Doctor of Theology from Christian Bible College of Louisiana.
  On Thursday, June 30, 1994, he was selected to Pastor Olive Branch 
Baptist and installed on July 15, 1994, by the Westside Missionary 
Baptist Association. Pastor Nicholas serves faithfully and untiringly 
as a dedicated Pastor, ministering to all the needs of this 
congregation, visiting the sick, attending to widows, and ministering 
to those in prison.
  Pastor Nicholas is a community-oriented leader. In January 1995, he 
organized the 1st Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. March in Algiers, 
inviting two of Dr. King's nephews, Derrick and Vernon C. King.
  In 2000, Pastor Reginald Nicholas, Sr., the late Pastors Arthur 
Wardsworth, and Randy Stevenson organized WSN Ministries, Inc., a 
family ministry designed to help strengthen families and teach them to 
deal with different issues biblically.

[[Page E1175]]

  In January 2004, the Olive Branch Baptist Church called Pastor to 
full-time ministry, retiring him from the Jefferson Parish Sheriff's 
Office after 15 years of dedicated service. He caringly challenges his 
flock to launch out into the deep as he leads them to new and exciting 
Spiritual heights. He conducts a 7-week Spiritual Boot Camp Class 
offered to new members and available to all members, which lays the 
foundation for Spiritual growth, including several workshops, Bible 
institutes, revivals, and conferences. Under Pastor Nicholas' dynamic 
leadership towards ``BUILDING A WINNING TEAM,'' he has the Deacon and 
Deaconess Ministry along with the Brotherhood and Sisterhood 
Ministries, an active Youth Ministry, a Ministry of Music, a Media 
Ministry, a Ushers Ministry, a Married Couples Ministry. ``THE CHURCH 
WHERE THE CHRISTIANS ARE LABORERS TOGETHER WITH GOD.'' 1Corinthians 
3:9. Pastor Nicholas also holds office in Louisiana Home & Foreign 
Missions Baptist Convention (Vice President-at-Large) as well as the 
National Baptist Convention of America International, Inc. (Deputy 
Secretary to Haiti).
  In July 2006, Pastor Nicholas was appointed Chairman of the Foreign 
Mission Board of Louisiana Home and Foreign Missions Baptist 
Convention, Inc. As the former chairman of the Foreign Mission Board, 
he led that convention to the top in foreign mission giving nationwide. 
Olive Branch Baptist Church is one of the leading churches in foreign 
mission giving statewide. He has made five trips to Ghana, West Africa, 
and nine to Haiti.
  In October 2008, the Olive Branch Baptist Church had a note-shredding 
ceremony and became debt-free. The parishioners retired a $700,000 
Church Note with a celebration. On October 18, 2009, we had a 
Celebratory Service celebrating the Street Name Change from Odeon 
Avenue to Branch Joseph Avenue.
  The Olive Branch Community Development Corporation (OBCDC) was formed 
under his leadership, and it serves the community once a month with a 
Hot Meal through our Share A Meal Ministry. In January 2008, Pastor 
Nicholas began a Men & Boys Ministry on the 1st Sunday of each month. 
In May 2008, he was appointed to the Trustee Board of Christian Bible 
College. On April 15, 2009, he was elected as the 2nd President of 
Cornerstone Missionary Baptist General Association. He was installed on 
May 23, 2010, and re-elected to a second term as Cornerstone Missionary 
Baptist General Association President.
  In October 2018, he became the Vice President-At-Large of the 
Louisiana Home and Foreign Missions Baptist Convention, Inc. In 2019, 
when the nation was hit with COVID-19, Pastor Nicholas, along with the 
help of his sons Reginald, Jr. and Justin Hughes, would go live on 
social media sites where he preached and taught from his home.
  He has four spiritual sons who are pastoring: Rev. William P. Butler, 
Sr. (April 2009)--Rock of Ages B. C. of Morristown, Tennessee; Rev. 
Larry P. Steib (March 26, 2010)--Mt. Pilgrim 4th Baptist Church, 
Algiers, Louisiana; Rev. Mat E. Turner, I (September 26, 2010)--Greater 
Old Zion Missionary Baptist Church, New Orleans, Louisiana; and Rev. 
Gregory Ussin (March 10, 2014)--Ebenezer Baptist Church, Houston, 
Texas.
  Pastor Nicholas is a Visionary, leading this ministry to higher 
heights and greater depths in our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
  On June 12, 1982, Pastor Reginald Nicholas, Sr. entered into Holy 
Matrimony with Eulalie Jacqueline Neale. Three precious children were 
born into this union: Reginald, Jr., LaDonna Michelle, and Jeremy 
Marquel Nicholas. They are blessed with a daughter-in-love, Erica L. 
Nicholas. He is the proud grandfather of Reginald, III, Morgann, 
Emerson, Jazmine, and Eric. He also serves as a surrogate father to 
April Green and a surrogate grandfather to Christian.
  The Greater New Orleans Community is blessed to have him as a 
spiritual leader to so many. I am proud to call him my friend. I am 
forever grateful for his friendship, love, and guidance, and I will 
carry his direction as I serve the Constituents of LA02. I congratulate 
him on 30 years of spiritual leadership, and may he see many more years 
serving individuals across the country.

                          ____________________
```
