# HONORING FIREFIGHTER ANDRE CASTANON, ENGINEER CORBIN SMITH, LIEUTENANT JACOB ECKERT, AND BATTALION CHIEF WALTER EVERSON FOR THEIR LIFESAVING ACTIONS ON DECEMBER 22, 2024

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```

 JACOB ECKERT, AND BATTALION CHIEF WALTER EVERSON FOR THEIR LIFESAVING 
                      ACTIONS ON DECEMBER 22, 2024

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, on the evening of December 22, 2024, crews 
responded to a heavily involved barn fire with reports of an occupant 
trapped inside an upstairs apartment. During rescue efforts, a 
catastrophic structural collapse trapped two firefighters beneath 
burning debris, transforming an already dangerous fire into a 
firefighter rescue operation.
  In the critical moments that followed, these four firefighters 
demonstrated extraordinary professionalism and composure under 
pressure. Anticipating the evolving conditions, Engineer Corbin Smith 
established a second charged hose line that would prove essential when 
the collapse occurred. Firefighter Andre Castanon quickly recognized 
the danger and immediately placed water on the collapse area, helping 
control fire conditions and creating precious time for rescue efforts.
  Simultaneously, Lieutenant Jacob Eckert and Battalion Chief Walter 
Everson provided calm and decisive leadership amid rapidly changing 
conditions. Despite active fire, an unstable structure, and two trapped 
firefighters, they coordinated rescue operations and guided responding 
crews through one of the most challenging situations firefighters can 
encounter.
  Thanks to the preparation, training, teamwork, and leadership 
displayed that night, both trapped firefighters were successfully 
rescued without significant injury.
  What could have become a devastating tragedy instead became a 
powerful testament to the values of the fire service: courage, 
selflessness, professionalism, and brotherhood.
```
