# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF VERONICA LEE ANN DOWNS-DORSEY

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
         CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF VERONICA LEE ANN DOWNS-DORSEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
celebrate the life of Veronica Lee Ann Downs-Dorsey, a phenomenal 
leader and friend, where she was immersed in Christian principles, 
music, and love--the foundation for her life and ministry. I was 
honored to have Veronica represent the State of Louisiana's and its 2nd 
Congressional District at the 2023 Annual Legislative Conference 
Congressional Black Caucus Gospel Extravaganza, the first Catholic 
Choir to ever participate in 30 years. Born May 23, 1956, to Virgil and 
Ruby Downs. She was raised in a loving Christian home filled with 
warmth, discipline, and encouragement from strong role models, 
including her grandmother, Mrs. Emma Dupree, and other achievers who 
inspired her to reach her full potential. Her educational journey began 
at Martinez Kindergarten, where she was immersed in Christian 
principles, music, and love--the foundation for her life and ministry. 
As a faithful member of St. Monica Catholic Church, her musical talent 
blossomed early. At just 14 years old, she directed her first choir, 
The Golden Emeralds, at Union Bethel African Methodist Episcopal Church 
in New Orleans. A proud graduate of McDonogh 35 Senior High School, 
Class of 1974, Veronica went on to earn her Bachelor of Music Education 
from Xavier University and her Master of Music Education from 
Southeastern Louisiana University in Hammond.
  To portray Veronica, affectionately known as ``Ms. Vee,'' one would 
describe her as a lively blend of purpose, passion, and praise. She was 
a performer, soloist, director, instructor, arranger, organist, 
pianist, mentor, and friend--all united by her steadfast faith and love 
for music. For 40 years, she served in the Orleans Parish School 
System, inspiring generations as the Vocal Music Educator at McDonogh 
No. 35 Senior High School. Under her leadership, The Singing Roneagles 
became a symbol of excellence, recognized as the first high school 
gospel choir in New Orleans and the first to perform at the New Orleans 
Jazz & Heritage Festival. Their performances in the Gospel Tent became 
an annual tradition, and the choir received numerous awards, including 
OffBeat Magazine's Best of the Beat Award for Best Gospel Group three 
consecutive years (2014 to 2016).
  Beyond the classroom, Veronica's ministry touched churches and 
communities throughout New Orleans and beyond. She faithfully served as 
Choir Director at St. Monica Catholic Church, later leading The Voices 
of Peter Claver at St. Peter Claver Catholic Church. She also directed 
the New Orleans Gospel Soul Children, the Vee-Ro Singers. She was an 
accompanist for The New Orleans Black Chorale, Tyrone Foster and the 
Arc Singers, The Davell Crawford Singers, and the New Orleans Chapter 
of the Gospel Music Workshop of America, Inc. Her leadership extended 
to the Archdiocese of New Orleans Mass Gospel Choir, where she served 
as Co-Director. Veronica's dedication earned her numerous awards and 
honors, including the 2015 NOLA Gospel Awards Trailblazer Award, the 
2016 New Orleans City Council Women's History Month Recognition, and 
the Order of St. Louis Medallion (1999). She was also a proud member of 
Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc., Omicron Lambda Omega Chapter, sharing 
sisterhood and service with grace. She was a dedicated member of the 
Mystic Krewe of Femme Fatale, where she established and led the Voices 
of MKFF. Veronica's music and ministry carried her around the world--
from Norway and Martinique to Mexico City, where she shared her 
extraordinary gifts with audiences abroad.
  Veronica was united in sacred marriage to Wardell Anthony Dorsey, and 
together they raised their beloved daughter, Veronique Lahdell Dorsey, 
who continues her mother's legacy of excellence and faith. Veronica's 
life was a testament to God's grace. She allowed the Lord to use her as 
a vessel of joy, strength, and light through music and education. Her 
passion for young people and her belief in the transformative power of 
song touched countless lives.

part of her life through her spiritual journey. Veronica Lee Ann Downs-
Dorsey--a faithful servant, a gifted musician, a devoted teacher, and a 
radiant light in the kingdom of God. A Truly Gifted Woman of God. May
```
