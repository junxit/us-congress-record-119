# RECOGNIZING THE HIGHLANDS LIONS CLUB 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE HIGHLANDS LIONS CLUB 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FRANK PALLONE, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate the 
Highlands Lions Club as it commemorates 100 years of service this year. 
The volunteers and community members will gather for a luncheon and 
recognition ceremony to honor the service organization's past and 
future. The Highlands Lions Club has been an integral part of the local 
community since its establishment and continues to uphold its mission 
to provide residents with community betterment and support.
  On October 28, 1926, the Highlands Lions Club officially chartered, 
making it one of New Jersey's earliest Lions chapters. Around the 
world, the Lions were part of an international movement for vision and 
blindness prevention following Helen Keller's influential 1925 address. 
This organization's first event debuted at Bahrs Landing and drew over 
100 attendees, including Lions District Governor Carl Banwart. The 
night featured speeches, musical performances, and installation of the 
original officer slate. From the 1930s to the 1950s, recurring 
fundraising events and themed social gatherings formed lasting 
community traditions. Today, the Highlands Lions Club continues to have 
a special social, recreational, and charitable presence in Highlands.
  Throughout its history, various service projects by the Highlands 
Lions Club's longstanding members have had meaningful influence on 
local civic development. In 2007, the Monmouth County Board officially 
dcsignatcd ``Frank C. White Day'' in recognition of Mr. White's 
contributions as previous President and District Governor of Lions Club 
International. Mr. White notably secured a significant Lions Clubs 
International matching grant that provided educational materials to be 
accessible to Lions network chapters nationwide.
  For one hundred years, the Highlands Lions Club has developed 
community programs aligned with the global organization's key mission 
to serve others. This includes supporting local students with 
educational scholarships, holding annual holiday donation collections, 
funding eye exams and screenings for children, distributing eyeglasses 
worldwide, and engaging in youth mentorship to the Highlands Leos.
  Mr. Speaker, once again, please join me in marking the 100th 
anniversary of Highlands Lions Club. Their continued service is truly 
deserving of this body's recognition.
```
