# HONORING CHIEF JUANITA "LORI" McDONALD FOR HER SERVICE TO CENTRAL FLORIDA AS A FIREFIGHTER

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
  HONORING CHIEF JUANITA ``LORI'' McDONALD FOR HER SERVICE TO CENTRAL 
                        FLORIDA AS A FIREFIGHTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, for more than 31 years as a firefighter and 
paramedic, including nearly 28 years with the Orlando Fire Department, 
Chief Juanita ``Lori'' McDonald has distinguished herself as a 
trailblazer and mentor. As the longest-serving female firefighter in 
the department's history, she has helped pave the way for future 
generations of women in the fire service.
  Throughout her career, Chief McDonald has demonstrated an unwavering 
commitment to serving both her community and her fellow firefighters. 
In addition to her leadership as a District Chief, she has served on 
numerous Orlando Fire Department committees, contributed to promotional 
and training programs,

[[Page E1172]]

and held leadership roles with Orlando Professional Firefighters Local 
1365.
  Chief McDonald's impact extends far beyond Orlando. As Planning 
Manager for Florida Task Force 4 Urban Search and Rescue, she has 
supported disaster response efforts, including deployments following 
Hurricane Idalia and major flooding events. She has also dedicated 
countless hours to firefighter education as an instructor and full-time 
faculty member at Seminole State College, helping prepare the next 
generation of emergency responders.
  Among her many accomplishments, Chief McDonald founded ``20 Strong,'' 
a mentorship and support network for women firefighters within the 
department, creating opportunities for encouragement, professional 
development, and leadership.
```
