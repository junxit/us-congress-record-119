# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we thank You for a new year with all its hurdles, 
hardships, and hopes. We pause to express our gratitude, for You have 
been better to us than we deserve. Lord, You have been bread for our 
hunger, water for our thirst, and hope for our times of despair. Guide 
our lawmakers during these turbulent times until Your will is done on 
Earth, even as it is done in Heaven. Give them wisdom to discern sanity 
from derangement, truth from falsehood, and light from darkness.
  We pray in Your marvelous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
