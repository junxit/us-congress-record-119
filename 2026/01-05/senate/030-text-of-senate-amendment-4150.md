# Text of Senate Amendment 4150

**Speaking:** Gallego, Ruben (G000574) [D-AZ]

```
  SA 4150. Mr. GALLEGO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 4016, making appropriations for the Department 
of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be obligated or expended for the 
     use of military force, the conduct of hostilities, or the 
     preparation for war against or with respect to Greenland.

                          ____________________
```
