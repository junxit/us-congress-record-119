# Text of Senate Amendment 4148

**Speaking:** Marshall, Roger (M001198) [R-KS]

```
  SA 4148. Mr. MARSHALL (for Mr. Scott of Florida) proposed an 
amendment to the bill S. 320, to authorize the Earthquake Hazards 
Reduction Act of 1977, and for other purposes; as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

     SEC. 10. FUNDING CONTROLS.

       Section 12 of the Earthquake Hazards Reduction Act of 1977 
     (42 U.S.C. 7706), as amended by section 9, is further amended 
     by adding at the end the following:
       ``(e) Funding.--Funds used by an agency to carry out this 
     Act may, as provided in advance in appropriations Acts, only 
     come from amounts authorized to be appropriated to that 
     agency.
       ``(f) Deficit Reduction.--Any amount appropriated to an 
     account of a Federal agency for the Federal agency to carry 
     out a responsibility under this Act that is cancelled 
     pursuant to section 1552(a) of title 31, United States Code, 
     shall be transferred to the general fund of the Treasury and 
     be applied to deficit reduction.''.
```
