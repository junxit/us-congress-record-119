# REAFFIRMING THE PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE PACIFIC ISLANDS FORUM AND THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Case, Ed (C001055) [D-HI]

```
 REAFFIRMING THE PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE PACIFIC ISLANDS FORUM AND THE 
                             UNITED STATES

                                 ______
                                 

                              HON. ED CASE

                               of hawaii

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. CASE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in support of my resolution 
reaffirming the enduring partnership between the Pacific Islands Forum 
(PIF) and its eighteen members and the United States toward stability, 
prosperity and peace in the Pacific, and expressing the sense of the 
House of Representatives that the United States will continue to remain 
a strong, reliable and active partner in advancing a peaceful, 
resilient and prosperous Blue Pacific.
  The PIF is the Pacific Islands region's premier political and 
economic policy organization, bringing together 18 member countries and 
four associate members to advance shared priorities across the Blue 
Pacific. The Forum provides an important platform for Pacific Island 
countries and territories to work collectively on regional security, 
disaster resilience, economic development, ocean governance and other 
issues that directly affect the people of the Pacific.
  The United States has longstanding ties with the PIF and the Pacific 
Island countries it represents. The United States has been a PIF 
Dialogue Partner since 1989, and our relationship is grounded in shared 
interests and longstanding connections across the Pacific. The United 
States is itself a Pacific Nation, home to millions of people with 
Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander heritage, and our Pacific 
territories of Guam and American Samoa are Associate Members of the 
PIF. We also share close relationships with the Freely Associated 
States through the Compacts of Free Association.
  Our interests in the Pacific are clear. The United States and the PIF 
share a commitment to maintaining a peaceful and secure region, 
strengthening economic opportunity, responding to natural disasters, 
protecting our oceans and supporting a free and open Pacific. Strong 
engagement with the PIF is an important part of advancing these shared 
priorities while respecting Pacific leadership and the sovereignty of 
Pacific Island nations.
  One of the PIF's principal annual gatherings is the PIF Leaders 
Meeting, where Pacific

[[Page E813]]

leaders and Dialogue Partners come together to discuss regional 
priorities and set the Forum's agenda for the year ahead. The 55th PIP 
Leaders Meeting will take place in Palau from August 30 to September 4. 
Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau is also expected to 
participate, making this an important opportunity to demonstrate 
continued U.S. engagement with the region.
  This resolution reaffirms the United States' commitment to the PIF 
and encourages continued high-level engagement with Pacific regional 
institutions. It also supports Pacific-led implementation of the PIF's 
2050 Strategy for the Blue Pacific Continent and calls for continued 
cooperation on regional security, disaster resilience, economic 
development, ocean stewardship, technology and connectivity and other 
shared priorities.
  The United States has a strong interest in a peaceful, resilient and 
prosperous Pacific, and the PIF is an essential partner in achieving 
that goal. I urge my colleagues to support this bipartisan resolution 
and reaffirm that the United States will remain a strong, reliable and 
active partner to the Pacific Islands Forum and the people of the Blue 
Pacific.
```
