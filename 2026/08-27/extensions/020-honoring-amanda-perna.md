# HONORING AMANDA PERNA

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING AMANDA PERNA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Ms. LOIS FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor my 
constituent, Amanda Perna--an accomplished fashion designer, author, 
and community leader--and to recognize her as a finalist in the Verizon 
Small Business Super Pitch Competition. Amanda was one of only four 
finalists to compete in the Super Pitch and win tickets to the World 
Cup Final.
  In 2012, Amanda founded House of Perna, a sustainable fashion brand 
based in Delray Beach. Over the past fourteen years, she has built one 
of South Florida's most recognizable independent fashion labels through 
creativity, entrepreneurship, and an unwavering commitment to giving 
back. Under her leadership, House of Perna has supported more than 250 
nonprofit organizations through charitable donations, mentorship, 
educational workshops, fundraising initiatives, and community 
partnerships. From advancing literacy and youth development to 
empowering women, supporting healthcare initiatives, and promoting the 
arts, Amanda has used her business as a powerful force for positive 
change throughout Palm Beach County.
  Amanda's dedication to serving others extends far beyond her 
business. She serves on the boards of the YMCA of South Palm Beach 
County, Achievement Centers for Children & Families, and the Delray 
Beach Chamber Advisory Board, reflecting her belief that opportunity, 
education, and creativity should be accessible to everyone. During the 
COVID-19 pandemic, Amanda partnered with Hearst Publications to create 
free sewing tutorials and patterns that helped people make protective 
face coverings. She also serves as co-chair of Laugh with the Library, 
raising critical funds to expand literacy programs, and chairs Making 
Strides Against Breast Cancer, inspiring community support for women 
and families affected by breast cancer.
  Amanda exemplifies the very best of community leadership through her 
compassion, generosity, and entrepreneurial spirit. She has 
demonstrated that business success and civic engagement go hand-in-
hand, creating lasting opportunities for others while strengthening the 
fabric of our community.
  Please join me in recognizing Amanda Perna for her outstanding 
contributions to Palm Beach County. I thank Amanda for her 
extraordinary service to South Florida and wish her continued success 
in all her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
