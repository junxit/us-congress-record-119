# HONORING "EL COMANDANTE" ANDRES HERNANDEZ

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING ``EL COMANDANTE'' ANDRES HERNANDEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize ``El Comandante'' 
Andres Hernandez for his extraordinary achievements as a community 
leader and band instructor for the youth of Santa Ana, California.
  Andres's story began in the Mexican state of Morelos, in the city of 
Zapatec. At just 8 years old, Andres became a flag bearer for his 
school, where he would later work his way up to joining the esteemed 
Benito Juarez High School marching band, and become commander or orders 
or ``El Comandante de Ordenes'' for a neighboring school in the same 
municipality.
  Andres's hard work and determination paid off, laying the foundation 
for him to become a marching band instructor for young folks. In 1998, 
Andres marked his formal beginnings as an instructor, where he taught 
the children of Cuahetomoc, Juan Jacobo Rousseau, and Benito Juarez 
respectively. Always striving for more, Andres formed the first 
marching band of the municipal police and the first ``Banda De Guerra'' 
for the municipality of Juarez Morelos.
  In June of 2009, Andres founded his own marching band in my home 
district, ``Banda de Guerra Osos de California''. This Santa Ana band 
prides itself on fostering cultural appreciation for the next 
generations of Mexican and Hispanic Americans. Under his leadership as 
Comandante, Banda de Guerra Osos has participated in various civic 
events across the Golden State, receiving well-deserved recognitions 
from the City of Santa Ana, Anaheim, and even countries across Latino-
America. Andres's diligent work as a community leader caught my own 
eye, leading me to congressionally recognize him for his 
accomplishments.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Comandante Andres for 
his exemplary leadership and mentorship for the next generations. Our 
community thanks Comandante Andres for leading by example.
```
