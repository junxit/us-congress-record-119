# HONORING THE LIFE OF ALEXANDER "XANDER" TIMOTHY RIEXINGER

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
      HONORING THE LIFE OF ALEXANDER ``XANDER'' TIMOTHY RIEXINGER

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
memory of Alexander ``Xander'' Timothy Riexinger of Lower Saucon 
Township, Pennsylvania, whose passing has brought profound sadness to 
his family, friends, and the entire community.
  Xander's passing is a heartbreaking loss for those who knew and loved 
him. For those who knew Xander, they might recall his deep appreciation 
of motorcycles and a passion for the open road. The memories of those 
rides and shared experiences will remain with those who loved him.
  Xander was also an Eagle Scout with BSA Troop 27, an accomplishment 
that reflected his character, dedication, and commitment to service. 
The values he developed through Scouting were a meaningful part of the 
person he became and the life he shared with those around him.
  Mr. Speaker, Xander's life touched so many of the people around him, 
and his memory will live on through the family and friends who knew him 
best. In times of loss, we are reminded of the importance of standing 
together and supporting our neighbors.
  On behalf of myself and the people of Pennsylvania's 1st 
Congressional District, I extend my deepest condolences to Xander's 
family, friends, and all who mourn his passing. May they find comfort 
in their memories of him and strength in one another.

                          ____________________
```
