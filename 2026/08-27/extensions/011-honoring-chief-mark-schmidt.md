# HONORING CHIEF MARK SCHMIDT

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
                      HONORING CHIEF MARK SCHMIDT

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Chief Mark 
Schmidt and recognize an extraordinary career spanning more than 40 
years in law enforcement, including nearly three decades as Chief of 
Police of the Upper Makefield Police Department.
  Since becoming Chief in 1997, Chief Schmidt has transformed and 
strengthened the department through his vision, leadership, and 
unwavering commitment to excellence. Under his leadership, the 
department doubled in size, growing from 9 to 18 officers, constructed 
state-of-the-art headquarters, and continually modernized its 
operations though advanced technology, equipment, and training.
  Among Chief Schmidt's proudest accomplishments was leading the 
department to PLEAC accreditation in 2020. This prestigious designation 
reflects the department's commitment to the highest standards of 
professionalism, accountability, and excellence in law enforcement. The 
department has proudly maintained that accreditation ever since.
  Chief Schmidt has also been a steadfast advocate for the professional 
development of his officers, He has encouraged advanced training and 
higher education, and under his leadership, numerous officers have 
earned master's degrees while continuing to serve their community.

[[Page E810]]

  His commitment to law enforcement has extended far beyond Upper 
Makefield. Chief Schmidt served for more than 20 years as Secretary of 
the Bucks County Police Chiefs association and more than 20 years as 
President of the Bucks County Police Association, in addition to 
serving on three committees. Through these roles, he has helped 
strengthen and advance the law enforcement throughout Bucks County.
  Chief Schmidt's remarkable career is a testament to his dedication, 
vision, and commitment to public service. For more than four decades, 
he has made lasting contributions to the Upper Makefield community and 
all entrusted to his care.
  We are proud to recognize Chief Mark Schmidt and congratulate him on 
a truly extraordinary career and wish him all the best during this new 
chapter.
```
