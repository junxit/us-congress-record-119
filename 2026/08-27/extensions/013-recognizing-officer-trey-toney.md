# RECOGNIZING OFFICER TREY TONEY

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                     RECOGNIZING OFFICER TREY TONEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Officer Trey 
Toney on his retirement from the Grapevine Police Department after 25 
years of service. With decades in law enforcement, Officer Toney has 
demonstrated an unwavering commitment to public safety and serving our 
North Texas community.
  Officer Toney began his law enforcement career in 1994 with the 
Texarkana Police Department, where he served as a Patrol Officer before 
joining the Housing Projects and Gang Unit. In 2001, he joined the 
Grapevine Police Department and went on to serve the community in 
numerous capacities throughout his distinguished career.
  During his time with the Grapevine Police Department, Officer Toney 
served in Patrol and spent a decade in the Motor Unit before 
transitioning to Commercial Vehicle Enforcement, where he developed 
specialized expertise and worked to ensure the safety of Grapevine's 
roadways. In 2023, he transitioned into a new role as a School Resource 
Officer, continuing his commitment to public service by fostering 
positive relationships between law enforcement and students, staff, and 
families.
  Over the past two years, Officer Toney has served at Grapevine 
Elementary, where his daily commitment to safety and building trust has 
made a lasting impact on the school community. I thank Officer Toney 
for his decades of service in protecting and serving others and wish 
him all the best as he begins his well-earned retirement.
```
