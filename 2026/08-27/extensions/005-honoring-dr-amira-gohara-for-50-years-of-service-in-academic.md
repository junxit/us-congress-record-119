# HONORING DR. AMIRA GOHARA FOR 50 YEARS OF SERVICE IN ACADEMIC MEDICINE

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
 HONORING DR. AMIRA GOHARA FOR 50 YEARS OF SERVICE IN ACADEMIC MEDICINE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARCY KAPTUR

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Dr. 
Amira Gohara as she, along with our entire Northwest Ohio community, 
celebrates a remarkable 50 years of service to the University of Toledo 
and to the greater world of medicine and pathology.
  Dr. Gohara's medical and scientific training spans three continents. 
She earned her Doctor of Medicine at Cairo University in Cairo, Egypt, 
and completed residencies in clinical pathology with Egypt's Ministry 
of Health, Oldham General Hospital in the United Kingdom, and with the 
Medical University of Ohio in Toledo. With special training in 
immunology from the Medical University of Ohio and in renal pathology 
at the University of Chicago, she is triple board certified in anatomic 
pathology, clinical pathology, and immunopathology.
  Equally impressive is Dr. Gohara's dedication to teaching and 
mentoring the next generation of medical professionals at the 
University of Toledo's College of Medicine and Life Sciences. She has 
served as Program Director of the Pathology Residency Program, Director 
of the Medical Student Fellowship, Director of the Pathology Medical 
Student Clerkship, and Medical Director of the Pathologists' Assistant 
Program, among other roles guiding students through their medical 
education. One measure of her impact is her 36 Golden Apple Awards for 
Teaching Excellence, an unprecedented number of recognitions for her 
commitment to shaping her students' futures. As her students and 
mentees continue in their careers, they carry forward Dr. Gohara's 
enduring legacy.
  Dr. Gohara has also taken on significant leadership roles within the 
University of Toledo, including Dean and Provost of the former Medical 
College of Ohio (MCO), Interim President of MCO, Dean Emrita of MCO, 
Associate Dean for Faculty Affairs, Assistant Dean for Student Affairs, 
and Chair of the Department of Pathology. Beyond her institutional 
leadership, she has been a steadfast champion for medical education, a 
dedicated advocate for the health of our Northwest Ohio community, and 
a trailblazer for women in medicine. These roles reflect not only her 
professional excellence but also her authentic investment in the 
wellbeing of her students and colleagues alike.
  This month, the University of Toledo's College of Medicine and Life 
Sciences and its Department of Pathology hosted a Festschrift Symposium 
in her honor. A Festschrift is considered among the highest honors in 
academia, bringing together distinguished colleagues, former students, 
and scholars to celebrate the lasting influence of a respected educator 
and leader--and certainly, Dr. Gohara is deserving of such distinction.
  As a physician, pathologist, and educator, Dr. Amira Gohara has 
shaped the lives of countless individuals--from patients and students 
to fellow medical professionals and academics--and has left an 
indelible mark on the culture of academic medicine in Toledo and 
beyond. She is a remarkable woman and a humanitarian in every sense of 
the word, quietly, faithfully, compassionately pursuing change.
```
