# HONORING THE LIFE OF JOVANA KARNOVIC

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF JOVANA KARNOVIC

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Jovana 
Karnovic--a distinguished researcher whose life and work shaped the 
academic communities that have had the esteemed privilege of working 
with her. Jovana strived to make the world we live in a fairer and 
better place, dedicating her 35 years of life to continuous work, 
learning, and diligent research. Beyond Jovana's impressive 
professional and academic achievements, she was someone you could rely 
on. Jovana was not only a dedicated academic and researcher, but a 
loving partner, colleague, and friend to all those whom she left an 
impact on.
  At the age of 18, Jovana left her life in Northern Serbia and moved 
to the United States for a chance to achieve her American Dream. Jovana 
went on to attend Fullerton College, where she not only left a lasting 
presence on those around her but chose to thrive academically. Jovana 
left such an impact that, in 2012, she was named the college's top 
speaker and honored with a commemorative plaque in recognition of her 
lasting achievements. Jovana graduated from Fullerton college with 
honors.
  Following her graduation from Fullerton College, Jovana continued to 
fulfill her desire to learn, earning her BS, MS, and PHD degrees 
respectively. Soon after, Jovana founded `Reshaping Work', where she 
connected thousands of professionals and established 100+ partnerships 
around the globe. Jovana's legacy continues in the work and 
achievements that she poured her heart into, and the countless lives 
that she has touched. Jovana will be remembered as a shining light, who 
made it

[[Page E812]]

her mission to make those around her feel seen, uplifted, and inspired.
  Jovana's memory will live with us all. We thank her for a life filled 
with compassion, a willingness to learn, and dedication for helping 
those around her.
```
