# REMEMBERING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DALE GRIFFIN

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
            REMEMBERING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DALE GRIFFIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD G. DAVIS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life 
and legacy of Nash County EMS Director Dale Griffin, who passed away on 
August 8, 2026. I join the Nash County community in mourning Dale's 
passing and in remembering his decades of dedicated service to the 
people of Nash County.
  Dale dedicated nearly four decades of his life to emergency medical 
services and to caring for the people of Nash County. He joined Nash 
County EMS as a paramedic in 1988 and went on to serve as a training 
program manager and operations officer before becoming EMS Director in 
2023. Throughout his career, Dale demonstrated an unwavering commitment 
to his profession and to the people he served.
  Dale was much more than a dedicated EMS professional. He was a 
leader, mentor, and friend to generations of emergency medical 
professionals. He believed in those he worked alongside, invested in 
their growth, and stood beside them through some of their most 
difficult moments. He was known for his leadership, kindness, humor, 
and genuine love for others, which left a lasting impression on Nash 
County EMS and the broader emergency services community. The careers he 
helped shape, the patients he served, and the colleagues he mentored 
are a lasting part of his legacy.
  Dale's passing is deeply felt in Nash County and by all those who had 
the privilege of knowing him. I extend my deepest condolences to his 
wife, Julie; his daughters, Harlee and Grace; his entire family; and 
his Nash County EMS family during this difficult time. I hope they find 
comfort in knowing that Dale's life of service touched countless lives 
and that his legacy will continue through the people and community he 
served so faithfully.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the life and 
legacy of Dale Griffin and in keeping his loved ones, colleagues, and 
friends in their thoughts and prayers. His commitment to serving others 
will not be forgotten, and his legacy will continue to live on in the 
Nash County EMS community he devoted so much of his life to.
```
