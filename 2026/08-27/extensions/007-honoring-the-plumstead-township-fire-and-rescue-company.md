# HONORING THE PLUMSTEAD TOWNSHIP FIRE AND RESCUE COMPANY

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
        HONORING THE PLUMSTEAD TOWNSHIP FIRE AND RESCUE COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an important 
new chapter in the proud tradition of service in Plumstead Township, 
Bucks County, Pennsylvania.
  Last week, Plumstead Township welcomed its first career firefighters: 
Rachel Brick, Benjamin Morris, Daniel Pfeiffer, and Harley Turner, as 
the community takes an important step toward strengthening its fire and 
rescue services.
  For generations, Plumstead residents have relied on volunteer 
firefighters who answered the call to protect their neighbors. The 
addition of a career crew does not replace that tradition, it builds 
upon it, giving the community another layer of dependable emergency 
response while working alongside the Township's dedicated volunteers.
  This moment also held personal significance, as lifelong Plumstead 
resident Judge Jeffrey G. Trauger administered the oath. His late 
mother, Lois Hunsberger Trauger, served with the Plumsteadville 
Volunteer Fire Company Auxiliary.
  Mr. Speaker, moments like this remind us that public safety is 
ultimately about people willing to step forward and serve others. I 
congratulate Plumstead Township's first career firefighters and thank 
them, Chief William Weigman, Public Safety Director David Mettin, and 
the Township's volunteer firefighters for their commitment to keeping 
their neighbors safe.
  I also want to recognize the Plumstead Township Board of Supervisors 
for their leadership and commitment to strengthening public safety, 
including Chair Matthew Given, Vice Chair Kenneth Lichtenstein, Members 
Jennifer Moroney, Daniel Hilferty, and Gregory Bankos, as well as 
Township Manager Stacey Mulholland. Their leadership, along with the 
dedication of the Township's public safety professionals and 
volunteers, has helped make this important milestone possible.

[[Page E809]]

  I am proud to recognize this milestone and wish these firefighters a 
long and honorable career of service to the Plumstead community.

                          ____________________
```
