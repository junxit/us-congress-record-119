# RECOGNIZING THE COSUMNES RIVER COLLEGE ATHLETICS DEPARTMENT'S 55TH YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Matsui, Doris O. (M001163) [D-CA]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE COSUMNES RIVER COLLEGE ATHLETICS DEPARTMENT'S 55TH 
                            YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DORIS O. MATSUI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Ms. MATSUI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Cosumnes River 
College (CRC) Athletics Department as it celebrates 55 years of 
athletic excellence.
  File CRC Athletic Program has become a model of academic and athletic 
achievement in the Sacramento region and across California's community 
college system.
  Competing in one of the most demanding conferences in the state, CRC 
student-athletes have distinguished themselves not only through their 
performance in competition, but through their leadership, character, 
and service to the campus and community they represent.
  The 2025-26 academic year stands among the most successful in the 
departments history. Eight of CRC's ten athletic programs earned 
postseason berths, and the softball program advanced to the 2026 3C2A 
State Championship. The CRC Athletics Department earned twenty-five 
All-Conference selections and three All-American honors. The 
achievements on the field were matched in the classroom, where the 
department posted a cumulative 3.401 grade-point average: the highest 
in CRC Athletics history. One hundred and eight student athletes earned 
the Scholar-Athlete recognition, including thirty-six who achieved a 
perfect 4.0 grade-point average overall.
  Under the leadership of Athletic Director Collin Pregliasco, CRC 
Athletics has built a culture in which academic excellence and top-
notch-on-the-field performance go hand in hand. Early mornings, long 
bus rides and countless hours of training have shaped CRC student 
athletes to embody the discipline, accountability, and perseverance 
that define Hawks tradition and generational success.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite all my colleagues to join me in honoring the 
Cosumnes River College Athletics Department on fifty-five years of 
excellence. We celebrate the student-athletes, coaches and staff whose 
dedication continues to build a legacy of triumph, leadership, and 
opportunity for generations to come.
```
