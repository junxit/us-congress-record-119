# HONORING COLONEL BRUCE BREDLOW

**Speaking:** Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

```
                     HONORING COLONEL BRUCE BREDLOW

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD FINSTAD

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Mr. FINSTAD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Col. Bruce Bredlow 
for more than 30 years of dedicated service to our country. As Col. 
Bredlow enters retirement, I want to personally thank him for all he 
has done and sacrificed in service to the American people throughout 
his decorated military career.
  Born and raised in Champlin, Minnesota, Col. Bredlow enlisted in the 
Army in 1991, and graduated from the Mankato State University ROTC 
program as a Distinguished Military Graduate. In 1997, he was 
commissioned as a Second Lieutenant in the Air Defense Artillery.
  At Fort Bliss, Texas, Col. Bredlow served as a platoon leader and 
battery executive officer in 3-2 ADA, 31st ADA Brigade and later 
commanded Delta Battery, 1-7 ADA, 108th ADA Brigade and Headquarters, 
and Headquarters Battery, 108th ADA Brigade.
  Throughout his career, Col. Bredlow served the United States at bases 
around the world, including Japan, Saudi Arabia, Italy, and Germany. 
His roles included Air and Missile Defense Commander, Joint Operations 
Center Watch Officer, and Brigade Commander. He is now retiring as ADA 
Assistant Commandant for the Army at Fort Sill, Oklahoma.
  He was recognized for this distinguished service with numerous awards 
and decorations, including the Legion of Merit, Bronze Star Medal, 
Defense Meritorious Service Medal, Meritorious Service Medal, Army 
Commendation Medal, the Army Achievement Medal, the Army Basic Space 
Badge, and the parachutist badge.
  I wish all the best to Col. Bredlow in his retirement and many happy 
years to come with his loved ones. Our state and Nation will forever be 
grateful for his service and dedication to his fellow Americans.

                          ____________________
```
