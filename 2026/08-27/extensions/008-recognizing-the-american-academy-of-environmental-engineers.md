# RECOGNIZING THE AMERICAN ACADEMY OF ENVIRONMENTAL ENGINEERS AND SCIENTISTS

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE AMERICAN ACADEMY OF ENVIRONMENTAL ENGINEERS AND 
                               SCIENTISTS

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, August 27, 2026

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
American Academy of Environmental Engineers and Scientists (AAEES) for 
its leadership in advancing environmental engineering and science 
across our country.
  The AAEES is the premier professional organization dedicated to 
recognizing excellence in environmental engineering and science while 
promoting the highest standards of ethics, innovation, and public 
service. The AAEES's commitment to advancing technical expertise in 
this field has strengthened the professions that safeguard our Nation's 
public health, natural resources, and environmental quality.
  Environmental engineers and scientists play a vital role in 
addressing many of the most pressing challenges facing our Nation. 
Their work supports the protection of drinking water supplies, improves 
air quality, advances sustainable infrastructure, remediates 
contaminated land and water, strengthens community resilience, and 
develops innovative solutions to emerging environmental challenges. 
Their expertise helps ensure that communities across the United States 
have access to safe water, healthy ecosystems, and infrastructure that 
supports economic growth while protecting the environment.
  As our Nation continues to invest in resilient infrastructure, clean 
water systems, environmental stewardship, and technological innovation, 
the contributions of environmental engineers and scientists have never 
been more important.
  Today, I commend the American Academy of Environmental Engineers and 
Scientists for its enduring contributions to the engineering and 
scientific professions and for its unwavering commitment to excellence, 
innovation, and service. I extend my sincere appreciation for the 
Academy for their dedication to advancing the environmental professions 
and improving the quality of life for all Americans.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the American Academy of 
Environmental Engineers and Scientists for its remarkable achievements 
and in wishing the Academy continued success in fulfilling its 
important mission to promote continual improvement in environmental 
engineering and science education and practice.

                          ____________________
```
