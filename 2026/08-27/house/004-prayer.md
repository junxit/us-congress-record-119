# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Charles Trullols, Catholic Information Center, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the holy spirit.
  Amen.
  God Almighty and Eternal Father, source of every good gift, we come 
before You in gratitude.
  Bless and guide the Members of this Chamber, and all those who during 
these weeks return to their communities to listen to the people they 
serve. Grant them open ears and humble hearts, that their encounters 
with their neighbors may deepen their understanding and bear fruit in 
wise laws and faithful public service.
  As our Nation looks toward another season of choosing its 
Representatives, temper ambition with honesty and rivalry with respect. 
May those who seek to serve remember that, beyond parties and political 
divisions, every person in every district is first and foremost a child 
of God.
  Holy spirit, fill our lawmakers with wisdom and courage. May their 
work strengthen the bonds that hold us together as one people, and may 
this country remain, under Your providence, a beacon of liberty, 
justice, and hope for generations to come.
  Through Christ our Lord.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
