# Text of Senate Amendment 4729

**Speaking:** Welch, Peter (W000800) [D-VT]

```
  SA 4729. Mr. WELCH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1383, to establish the Veterans Advisory Committee 
on Equal Access, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. DHS CONTRACT INFORMATION.

       Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this 
     Act, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall submit to 
     Congress a copy of each contract and subcontract associated 
     with an advertising campaign of the Department of Homeland 
     Security entered into on or after January 20, 2025.
                                 ______
```
