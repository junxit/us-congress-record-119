# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, look beyond our mistakes and see our needs. Remind us 
that we are created in Your likeness and our hearts are restless 
without the gift of Your peace.
  Lord, speak to our lawmakers today and teach them to listen, through 
wars and rumors of wars, for Your still, small voice. Guide them to 
learn the language of prayer and daily experience its power in their 
lives.
  May they be calm when You would have them listen, grateful when You 
would have them blessed, and obedient when You would have them act. 
Make them always eager to receive directions from You.
  And, Lord, be with those throughout our Nation and world who feel the 
pain of grief.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
