# HONORING HERBERT DIXON

**Speaking:** Franklin, Scott (F000472) [R-FL]

```
                         HONORING HERBERT DIXON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SCOTT FRANKLIN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. SCOTT FRANKLIN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of Herbert Dixon, a legendary golfer and longtime 
Bartow resident who passed away at the remarkable age of 106.
  Herbert's love for golf began early. He started caddying at the 
Bartow Golf Course at just 14 years old and taught himself the game by 
practicing on the course at night during segregation.
  He went on to become a pioneer in professional golf, winning 63 
tournaments in the United Golf Association, including the National 
Negro Open Championship. Herbert also served our country in the U.S. 
Air Force and earned recognition in multiple halls of fame.
  As a lifelong golfer myself, I can certainly appreciate a man who 
spent more than nine decades around the game. Herbert was still hitting 
his age into his later years and became a true legend in Bartow and 
throughout the golf community.
  Living to 106 is remarkable enough. Doing it while leaving behind a 
legacy like Herbert's is something truly special.
  My condolences are with his family, friends and all those who knew 
him. May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
