# RECOGNIZING JAYLYNN ROBINSON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING JAYLYNN ROBINSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jaylynn 
Robinson for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Jaylynn has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Jaylynn, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Jaylynn's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Jaylynn Robinson on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
