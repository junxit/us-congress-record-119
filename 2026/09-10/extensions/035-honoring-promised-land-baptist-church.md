# HONORING PROMISED LAND BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING PROMISED LAND BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Promised Land Baptist 
Church, a congregation whose history began on Saturday, October 2, 
1976, under the inspired guidance of the late Reverend Frank Herbert, 
serving then as Assistant Pastor. The church's earliest meetings and 
services were spirit-filled and held at the home of Brother Wallace and 
Sister Gloria Moore on Lewis Street. The founding Deacons included 
Brothers William Blacher, Essix Bradley, Robert Carter, Cary Harrison, 
Johnnie Herbert, Eddie Lee, and Wallace Moore. Brother Essix Bradley 
and Brother Wallace Moore were elected Treasurer and Assistant 
Treasurer, respectively. Sister Helen Grant served as Financial 
Secretary, and Sister Sarah Times as Recording Secretary.
  The first Holy Communion Service was observed on the second Sunday, 
October 10, 1976, at the home of Reverend Frank and Sister Rosa Herbert 
on Avenue C. Following this blessed observance, preparations were made 
for services to be held at the Masonic Hall on Rosenwald Road. Though 
the membership was small, love and unity flourished, and the church 
developed and progressed rapidly.
  On October 17, 1976, the church charter was recorded, and Promised 
Land Baptist Church was formally constituted.
  Soon thereafter, the church building located at 519 Crane Street was 
acquired and declared its home. On the first Sunday in November 1976, 
the initial service commenced. One week later, on November 14, 1976, 
the Lord's Supper was commemorated under the guidance of Reverend Eddie 
Sulcer, Jr. Shortly after, he was elected as the first Pastor of the 
church. During his tenure, the church site was renovated, land was 
purchased, the building was paid for, and--most importantly--many souls 
were saved.
  In 1987, Reverend Perry Wrights, Jr. was elected and continues to 
serve faithfully as Pastor. Under his leadership, a new energy 
revitalized Promised Land, and a renewed commitment by the congregation 
allowed the church to look toward the future with hope, growth, and 
spiritual renewal. The membership matured tremendously, and the church 
prospered.
  While located at 519 Crane Street, the church celebrated numerous 
goals and milestones, including purchasing additional land, 
refurbishing the sanctuary, and renovating the parking lot. Victories 
in stewardship--spiritual and financial--were abundant, and for this, 
the congregation gives thanks to God.
  In 1996, Sister Helen Grant retired due to health concerns, and 
Sister Belinda Hamilton succeeded her as Financial Secretary. Sister 
Rose Palmer Morris was appointed Recording Secretary. During elections, 
Sister Hamilton was reaffirmed as Financial Secretary, Sister Morris as 
Recording Secretary, Brother William Blacher, Sr. as Chairman of the 
Deacon Ministry, and Brother Robert Carter as Treasurer.
  Due to unforeseen circumstances, the congregation left the former 
location. Under the direction of Pastor Wrights, the church was blessed 
with a new sanctuary at 7234 Plank Road, where it has worshiped since 
November 1999. With more space and continued dedication, the church has 
grown tremendously. The congregation gives all honor and praise to God, 
recognizing His goodness is beyond measure.
  On February 20, 2004, Brother William Blacher, Sr. went home to be 
with the Lord. Brother Abraham Barnes was appointed as Spokesman for 
the Deacon Ministry. God also called home beloved Recording Secretary 
Sister Rose Palmer Morris. On April 8, 2008, Brother Robert Carter was 
called from labor to reward, and Brother Kenny Leatherman was appointed 
Treasurer. These pioneers faithfully worked without hesitation and with 
pure determination for God's Kingdom, loving both their church and 
Pastor deeply.
  Promised Land continues to look forward to the future, recognizing 
there is much work to accomplish. Every new member is encouraged to 
join in the mission of reaching and teaching all whom the Lord sends. 
God has charted a bold course for Promised Land, and the congregation 
prays His blessings continue to pour out.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Promised 
Land Baptist Church. It continues to succeed--`Because He Lives'--and 
because the gifts of the church family, while not equal, are united in 
faith and purpose.
```
