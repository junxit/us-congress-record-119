# RECOGNIZING ZF AXLE DRIVES EMPLOYEES FOR THEIR EXCEPTIONAL RESPONSE TO A MEDICAL EMERGENCY

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
RECOGNIZING ZF AXLE DRIVES EMPLOYEES FOR THEIR EXCEPTIONAL RESPONSE TO 
                          A MEDICAL EMERGENCY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Scarlett Davis, Matthew De Vriese, Kenneth Alcorn, Keith Dromowicz, 
Steven Gerald Snider, David Causby, and Jesse Smith for their 
exceptional response to a medical emergency at ZF Axle Drives in 
Marysville, Michigan. Their quick thinking, teamwork, and compassion 
made a meaningful difference when a fellow employee needed help.
  These heroes did not hesitate. Each played a crucial role in keeping 
the response moving quickly and smoothly, from identifying the 
emergency to preparing for and briefing first responders on the 
situation.
  Their calmness under the pressure of an immensely difficult situation 
exemplifies the values of teamwork, safety, compassion, and leadership 
that define Michigan's workplace culture. Moments like these remind us 
that our greatest strength is our people.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing each of 
these heroic individuals. Their actions certainly saved a life, and I 
am proud to honor them for demonstrating the very best of ZF Axle 
Drives and Michigan as a whole.

                          ____________________
```
