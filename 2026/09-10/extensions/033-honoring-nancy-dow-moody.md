# HONORING NANCY DOW MOODY

**Speaking:** Huffman, Jared (H001068) [D-CA]

```
                        HONORING NANCY DOW MOODY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JARED HUFFMAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. HUFFMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Nancy Dow 
Moody and her decades of extraordinary public service as a nonprofit 
leader as she retires from Lifehouse.
  Nancy's distinguished career spans more than 40 years in service to 
people with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Nancy joined 
Lifehouse as Director of Services in 1992, bringing with her years of 
experience in managing programs that support the disability services 
community. In 2005, she was promoted to Vice President and Chief 
Operating Officer, and in 2008, she assumed the role of President and 
CEO.
  Over the course of her tenure, Nancy guided Lifehouse through 
transformational growth and inclusion. She expanded services, 
modernized systems, and developed pioneering programs that improved 
quality of life for thousands of individuals and families. Nancy 
championed affordable housing solutions for Lifehouse clients, 
collaborated on the development of innovative senior living programs, 
and helped launch the first Supported Living Program in the State of 
California. Under her leadership, Lifehouse also spearheaded the Marin 
Autism Collaborative, a key resource linking families, local agencies, 
and service providers.
  Nancy is admired for her steady leadership, thoughtful decision-
making, and genuine commitment to Lifehouse's mission, always drawing 
upon her extensive background and academic achievements to inform her 
leadership. She earned her Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Boston 
State College and a Master of Arts in Non-Profit Organizational 
Management from the University of San Francisco, where she graduated 
cum laude as a Merit Scholar. In 2019, she completed the Strategic 
Perspectives in Nonprofit Management Program at Harvard Business 
School, bringing nationally recognized best practices back to 
Lifehouse.
  Mr. Speaker, it has been my honor and privilege to work with Nancy. I 
can personally

[[Page E896]]

attest to how her leadership has transformed and enriched the lives of 
innumerable families throughout the Bay Area including mine. Please 
join me in expressing our profound gratitude for her remarkable 
contributions and in extending our warmest wishes for a richly deserved 
and fulfilling retirement.
```
