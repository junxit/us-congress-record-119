# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF KEVIN RICHARDSON

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
         HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF KEVIN RICHARDSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Chief Kevin Richardson, the longtime Coppell Fire Department 
Chief. Earlier this year, he officially retired after years of public 
service to our North Texas community. Chief Richardson Joined the 
department in 1993 and since his promotion to Fire Chief in 2006, the 
fire department has been performing at an unprecedented level of 
excellence.
  Under his leadership, two new fire stations were built, the 
department achieved and maintained the highest Public Protection 
Classification rating from the Insurance Services Office, and every 
firefighter in the department became certified as an emergency medical 
technician. As Coppell experienced rapid growth, Chief Richardson 
ensured the fire department grew stronger. The Coppell mayor, city 
council members, and firefighters have expressed their immense praise 
for him.
  I thank Chief Richardson for his leadership of an exceptional fire 
department and decades of public service. His work has saved lives and 
kept the Coppell community safe. We wish him the very best in his well-
deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
