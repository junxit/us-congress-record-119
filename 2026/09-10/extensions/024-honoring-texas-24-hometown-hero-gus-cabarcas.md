# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO GUS CABARCAS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO GUS CABARCAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Gus Cabarcas, a veteran who served as a Combat

[[Page E894]]

Medic in the U.S. Army at Fort Hood before continuing his career of 
service as a Sergeant with the NYPD. After over two decades in public 
service, Gus brought his passion for helping others to Combined Arms 
where he served as the North Texas Regional Coordinator and has been an 
Organizer for Veteran In Business.
  During his time at Combined Arms, Gus worked hard to serve veterans 
in our North Texas community and connect them with the resources to 
successfully transition and thrive after military service.
  Through his leadership and dedication, he helped strengthen the 
veterans community across the region. I thank Gus for his continued 
impact and unwavering support for our veteran community. His dedication 
is a shining example for us all.

                          ____________________
```
