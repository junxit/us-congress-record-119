# RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES AIR FORCE VETERAN COMMAND CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT MAXWELL GRINDSTAFF AS SEPTEMBER 2026 VETERAN OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
   RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES AIR FORCE VETERAN COMMAND CHIEF MASTER 
   SERGEANT MAXWELL GRINDSTAFF AS SEPTEMBER 2026 VETERAN OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Command Chief 
Master Sergeant Maxwell Grindstaff for his distinguished service in the 
United States Air Force and his community. It is my privilege to honor 
him as our September 2026 Veteran of the Month.
  CMSgt Grindstaff enlisted in the United States Air Force in 1987. He 
began his career in Security Forces before retraining in communications 
and computer operations. Throughout his military career, he served in 
Michigan, the United Kingdom, New York, North Dakota, California, 
Missouri, Texas, Utah, Ohio, and Afghanistan, taking on increasingly 
senior leadership roles.
  In 2010, CMSgt Grindstaff served as Command Chief in Kabul, 
Afghanistan. He later served as Command Chief at Hill Air Force Base in 
Utah and at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in Ohio before becoming 
Command Chief at the United States Air Force Academy in Colorado in 
2014. He retired in 2017 after three decades of distinguished service, 
earning numerous honors including the Legion of Merit, Bronze Star, and 
Meritorious Service Medals.
  Today, he continues his service with the Department of the Air Force 
in a civilian capacity, leading industry engagement and stakeholder 
management to support major Air Force modernization efforts. CMSgt 
Grindstaff is a family man and an active member of his community. He 
and his wife, Millie, are proud Lapeer natives who have raised three 
daughters and welcomed five grandchildren.
  Mr. Speaker, Command Chief Master Sergeant Maxwell Grindstaff has 
dedicated his life to serving our nation and community. Through his 
military service, leadership, and ongoing commitment to the community, 
he exemplifies honor, courage, and selfless service. It is my great 
honor to recognize his service and accomplishments by naming him our 
September 2026 Veteran of the Month.
```
