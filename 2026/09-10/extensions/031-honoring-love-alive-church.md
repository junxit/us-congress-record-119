# HONORING LOVE ALIVE CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                       HONORING LOVE ALIVE CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a great church 
in my district, Love Alive Church, located at 4517 Lassen Drive in 
Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Founded in 2013 by Pastor Ronaldo Hardy, Love 
Alive Church was envisioned as a vibrant, progressive, and multi-ethnic 
congregation where the next generation could grow in their faith. 
Pastor Hardy and his team dreamed of electrifying worship, a dynamic 
presentation, and a church family that would reflect the diversity of 
Baton Rouge. They felt the calling to plant this church because their 
generation needed a fresh and engaging spiritual home.
  After a year of preparation filled with both challenges and 
excitement, the initial launch planned for September 2014 was 
postponed, as they waited for God's timing. On January 11, 2015, Love 
Alive Church officially opened its doors at the AMC Theater near the 
Mall of Louisiana. For 14 months, the congregation worshiped there, 
witnessing God's presence and guidance in every season.
  Soon after, God opened the door for the church to move into a 
building on Jones Creek, where Love Alive began to grow rapidly. The 
congregation doubled in size for four consecutive years, expanding from 
one to four services. In 2019, the Lord blessed Love Alive Church with 
its permanent home on Lassen Drive, where it remains today.
  Since settling in its new home, Love Alive Church has continued to 
flourish as a place of passionate worship, prophetic teaching, and 
committed discipleship. At the heart of every ministry and outreach is 
the mission to help people find family, discover purpose, and change 
the world. Each person who joins Love Alive contributes to this ongoing 
story, helping carry forward a legacy of faith and community for future 
generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in applauding Love Alive 
Church for over a decade of trustworthy service, and in wishing Pastor 
Ronaldo Hardy and the congregation continued blessings as they pursue 
their mission and impact lives for years to come.
```
