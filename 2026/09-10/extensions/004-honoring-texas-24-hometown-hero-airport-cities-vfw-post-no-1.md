# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO AIRPORT CITIES VFW POST NO. 10454

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO AIRPORT CITIES VFW POST NO. 10454

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Airport Cities VFW Post No. 10454. They have proudly served 
veterans and families across Grapevine, Colleyville, Southlake, and 
Keller for over 50 years. Chartered on October 14, 1975, with 64 
charter members, and now grown into a thriving organization of nearly 
300 members.
  For the past five decades, Post 10454 has faithfully served the 
community through civic involvement, volunteer service and charitable 
contributions, and has provided stability and bonds of friendship that 
have strengthened the Cities of Grapevine, Colleyville, Southlake and 
Keller, and Texas. The Post has ensured its presence at the annual 
Bluebonnet Hills Memorial Day Remembrance Service.
  We thank them for their unwavering service and support of our North 
Texas community for over 50 years.
```
