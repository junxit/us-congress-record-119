# RECOGNIZING BLUE WATER DEVELOPMENTAL HOUSING ON ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
  RECOGNIZING BLUE WATER DEVELOPMENTAL HOUSING ON ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Blue 
Water Developmental Housing as it celebrates 50 years of providing 
life-changing residential and community-based services to people with 
intellectual and developmental disabilities across Michigan's Blue 
Water region.
  For five decades, Blue Water Developmental Housing has provided a 
safe, supportive place where individuals can live with greater 
independence. Today, across St. Clair and Macomb counties, the 
organization has grown to include 13 group homes and 19 assisted living 
homes, often referred to as ``Homes for Your Own''.
  However, Blue Water Developmental Housing offers far more than a 
place to live. It has created an environment where members are 
empowered to become active and valued participants in their 
communities. Its dedicated staff and caregivers demonstrate unwavering 
compassion every day, helping those live more independently and 
providing invaluable support to their families.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Blue 
Water Developmental Housing on this remarkable milestone. Its mission 
reflects the very best of our communities. Every person who has 
contributed to that mission has made a lasting difference in the lives 
of countless individuals and families, and we thank them for their 
service.

                          ____________________
```
