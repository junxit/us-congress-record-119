# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MAGDA A. HERNANDEZ

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MAGDA A. HERNANDEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Magda Hernandez, she has served as Irving ISD Superintendent for 
the past eight years and announced her retirement in December 2026. 
With eight years leading Irving ISD and 34 years in public education, 
her career reflects a deep commitment to our North Texas students and 
schools. Hernandez began her journey in Irving ISD as a bilingual aide 
and went on to serve in many roles from a teacher, to Assistant 
Principal, and District Administrator, before becoming the district's 
first female Superintendent. Her leadership has made a lasting, 
positive impact on public education in the Irving community and beyond.
  During her time as Superintendent, Hernandez has remained focused on 
helping students succeed and continuing to move the district forward. 
She has received numerous awards recognizing her outstanding career. 
Even after becoming eligible for retirement, Hernandez chose to 
continue serving, wanting to help the district continue improving. She 
has described her time at Irving ISD as one of the greatest honors of 
her life. We thank Magda Hernandez for her commitment to our community 
and the legacy she leaves behind.
```
