# HONORING FIRST SERGEANT JERRY WOOTEN

**Speaking:** Franklin, Scott (F000472) [R-FL]

```
                  HONORING FIRST SERGEANT JERRY WOOTEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SCOTT FRANKLIN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. SCOTT FRANKLIN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of First Sergeant Jerry Wooten, who dedicated decades 
of his life to serving our country and shaping the next generation of 
leaders.
  First Sergeant Wooten served 20 years in the United States Army 
before continuing his service as the JROTC instructor at Mulberry High 
School for more than two decades.
  To generations of cadets, he was known simply as ``First Sergeant.'' 
He taught his students the same values that guided his own life: 
discipline, integrity, service and leadership. Those lessons stayed 
with them as they went on to serve in the military, attend college, 
build careers and give back to their communities.
  First Sergeant Wooten lived a life of service, and our community is 
better because of the example he set.
  My condolences are with his wife, Carol, their two children and all 
those whose lives he touched.
  First Sergeant stood the watch faithfully. May he rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
