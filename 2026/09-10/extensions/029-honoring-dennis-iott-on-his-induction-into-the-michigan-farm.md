# HONORING DENNIS IOTT ON HIS INDUCTION INTO THE MICHIGAN FARMERS HALL OF FAME

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
HONORING DENNIS IOTT ON HIS INDUCTION INTO THE MICHIGAN FARMERS HALL OF 
                                  FAME

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dennis Iott of 
Kalkaska County on his induction into the Michigan Farmers Hall of Fame 
and to honor his more than four decades of dedication to Michigan 
agriculture.
  For 45 years, Dennis has worked to build Iott Seed Farms into one of 
Michigan's premier agricultural operations. Under his leadership, the 
farm has nearly tripled its seed potato production and now grows 
approximately 27 million pounds of seed potatoes each year, making Iott 
Seed Farms the largest supplier of seed potatoes in Michigan. His 
commitment to excellence has earned him recognition as the 2026 Frito-
Lay Seed Supplier of the Year, the 2025 Kalkaska Business of the Year, 
and the 2024 Michigan Association of Conservation Districts 
Conservation Farm of the Year.
  Dennis's impact extends well beyond his own farm. As President of 
Potato Growers of Michigan and Chairman of the Kalkaska Conservation 
District Board of Directors, he has been a steadfast advocate for 
Michigan farmers and for responsible stewardship of our natural 
resources. He has worked with Michigan State University researchers, 
welcomed students and policymakers to his farm, and participated in 
field trials that advance agricultural practices strengthening both 
productivity and conservation. He has also been a consistent voice in 
discussions surrounding Michigan's water resources, advocating for 
sound science, responsible management, and continued access to water 
for farmers.
  Perhaps most importantly, Dennis has invested in the people who will 
carry Michigan agriculture forward. Through mentorship, agricultural 
education, and years of service to farming organizations and his 
community, he has generously shared his knowledge with fellow farmers, 
students, researchers, and the next generation of agricultural leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Dennis on this well-deserved honor, thank 
him for his decades of service, and wish him and his family continued 
success for many growing seasons to come.
```
