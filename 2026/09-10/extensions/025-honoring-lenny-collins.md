# HONORING LENNY COLLINS

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
                         HONORING LENNY COLLINS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Lenny Collins, a Vietnam War veteran, Marine, husband, and devoted 
member of the Pensacola community.
  Lenny dedicated his life to making sure the sacrifices of our 
veterans, and especially those who never came home, would never be 
forgotten. After visiting the Moving Wall in 1987, Lenny was inspired 
to bring a permanent memorial to Pensacola. Through years of 
determination, he turned that vision into Wall South in 1992, which 
ultimately became the Veterans Memorial Park we know today.
  But Lenny did not do this alone. By his side throughout this journey 
was his beloved wife, Gloria. For 57 years, Lenny and Gloria shared a 
life dedicated not only to one another, but to their fellow veterans 
and the community they called home. Together, they helped build a 
lasting place of remembrance that has touched countless families and 
welcomed more than 100,000 visitors each year.
  Lenny understood the weight of a name on the Wall and the pain 
carried by those who served in Vietnam. He took that personal 
experience and turned it into something meaningful for an entire 
community. Because of him, generations of Northwest Floridians will 
have a place to honor our fallen, remember their sacrifice, and teach 
their children that freedom is never free.
  Lenny leaves behind a tremendous legacy of service, love of country, 
and love for his community. His impact will live on every time a 
veteran visits Wall South, every time a family pauses at one of the 
memorials, and every time a young person learns about the sacrifices 
made to protect our freedom.
  My deepest condolences are with Gloria, their daughters, their 
family, and all of Lenny's friends and fellow Marines. I hope they find 
comfort knowing that Lenny's life made a lasting difference in the 
lives of so many.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Lenny Collins 
and remembering a Marine who spent his life ensuring that America would 
never forget those who gave everything for our country.
```
