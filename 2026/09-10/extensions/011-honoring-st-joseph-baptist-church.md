# HONORING ST. JOSEPH BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING ST. JOSEPH BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor St. Joseph Baptist 
Church. The late Rev. Henry Roan Sr. organized St. Joseph on October 8, 
1927. The first building of worship was in a frame house located in the 
Zee-Zee Gardens neighborhood. The late Randolph Gilmore was the 
Chairman of the Deacon Board; the late Josephine Hudson served as 
Mother, and Alice Scott was Secretary. In the year 1939, the church 
moved from Zee-Zee Gardens to a location at 2424 Tennessee Street.
  During the same year, Rev. Roan's health began to fail, and he could 
not serve the church as he desired and had to give up the pastoral 
ship. Rev. L. B. Stevenson was called to succeed Rev. Roan in 1939. The 
Senior Choir was organized on October 8, 1939, and the Senior Usher 
Board in 1940 under the leadership of Rev. Stevenson who served until 
1948.
  On October 8, 1948, Rev. Q. T. Ellis was elected pastor and served 
diligently until 1953. In October 1953, the congregation elected Rev. 
Isaiah Mitchell as Pastor. Under the leadership of Rev. Mitchell, St. 
Joseph underwent tremendous spiritual and physical growth. On September 
30, 1962, the Church was dedicated as the Greater St. Joseph's Baptist 
Church.
  Rev. Mitchell served St. Joseph well for 34 years and fought hard for 
beautification, growth in membership, and stability. In 1985, when his 
health began to fail and would not allow him to travel from St. 
Francisville to Baton Rouge early on Sunday mornings, Rev. Joseph 
Davis, Bro. Dewitt Gray, and Bro. Donald Ford carried on for him.
  On December 26, 1985, Bro. Donald Ford was elected Assistant Pastor 
and carried on untiringly until February 16, 1987, when Dr. Lionel Lee, 
Sr., was elected pastor. Dr. Lee accepted the pastoral ship cautiously 
yet prayerfully asking God for guidance and a blueprint. Under his 
leadership, St. Joseph made many noteworthy and significant 
achievements. Rev. Lee served as Pastor for fourteen years and moved 
St. Joseph and its body of members onto a mark of higher calling.
  On July 25, 2001, Rev. Lionel Lee departed this life. He faithfully 
preached the Word of God and preached the whole counsel of God, which 
necessitated many hours of Bible Study and memorization.
  On March 23, 2002, the St. Joseph Baptist Church Family elected Rev. 
Donald Grim as their new Pastor. Rev. Grim was installed on April 21, 
2002, and presently remains as Pastor of the St. Joseph Baptist Church.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring St. Joseph 
Baptist Church for nearly a century of faithful service, spiritual 
leadership, and dedication to the community. May its pastor, 
leadership, and congregation continue to receive blessings as they 
build on the church's legacy and pursue its future with confidence.
```
