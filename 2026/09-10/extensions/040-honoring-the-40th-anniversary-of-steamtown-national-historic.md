# HONORING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF STEAMTOWN NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE

**Speaking:** Bresnahan, Robert P. (B001327) [R-PA]

```
   HONORING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF STEAMTOWN NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ROBERT P. BRESNAHAN, JR.

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. BRESNAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
proclamation honoring the 40th Anniversary of Steamtown National 
Historic Site:
  Whereas, established by Congress on October 30, 1986, Steamtown 
National Historic Site occupies a portion of the former Delaware, 
Lackawanna & Western Railroad yard, connecting the site directly to the 
railroad history that helped build Scranton and shape the industrial 
heritage of Northeastern Pennsylvania; and
  Whereas, Steamtown preserves an extraordinary collection of historic 
locomotives, passenger cars, freight equipment, archives, and railroad 
artifacts, providing visitors with a unique opportunity to experience 
the craftsmanship and human stories behind an era that transformed 
American industry, commerce, transportation, and communities; and
  Whereas, Steamtown's mission is to further public understanding and 
appreciation of the role steam railroading played in the development of 
the United States, and as the only site within the National Park System 
where the story of steam railroading and the people who made it 
possible is told, Steamtown serves as an important center for 
preservation, education, and appreciation of America's industrial 
heritage; and
  Whereas, the anniversary provides an opportunity to honor the 
generations of railroad workers whose skill and dedication powered 
America's growth, as well as the preservationists, National Park 
Service employees, volunteers, and community members who have worked to 
ensure that their stories continue to be shared with future 
generations;
  Now therefore, I, Rob Bresnahan, Jr., Member of Congress, do hereby 
recognize and congratulate Steamtown National Historic Site on the 
occasion of its 40th Anniversary, honoring four decades of preserving 
America's railroad heritage, educating visitors, and celebrating the 
enduring legacy of railroading and the people who helped build our 
Nation.

                          ____________________
```
