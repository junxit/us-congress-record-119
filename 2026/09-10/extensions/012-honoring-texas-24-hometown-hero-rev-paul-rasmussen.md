# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO REV. PAUL RASMUSSEN

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
          HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO REV. PAUL RASMUSSEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Rev. Paul Rasmussen, who has served as not only a pastor at 
Highland Park United Methodist Church, but as a steady presence and 
trusted leader for thousands of families across our North Texas 
community for over 25 years.
  A fourth-generation United Methodist minister and graduate of SMU's 
Perkins School of Theology, Rev. Rasmussen's faith-based leadership 
helped guide one of Dallas's largest congregations.
  Even as he transitions from being Senior Pastor, Rev. Rasmussen's 
message continues to inspire. Through decades of sermons, mentorship, 
and service, Rev. Rasmussen has left a lasting imprint on our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
