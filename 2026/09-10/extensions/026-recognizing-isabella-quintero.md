# RECOGNIZING ISABELLA QUINTERO

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING ISABELLA QUINTERO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Isabella 
Quintero for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Isabella has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Isabella, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Isabella's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning 
of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Isabella Quintero on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
