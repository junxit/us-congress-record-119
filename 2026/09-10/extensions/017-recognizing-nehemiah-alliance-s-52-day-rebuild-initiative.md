# RECOGNIZING NEHEMIAH ALLIANCE'S 52-DAY REBUILD INITIATIVE

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
       RECOGNIZING NEHEMIAH ALLIANCE'S 52-DAY REBUILD INITIATIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Nehemiah 
Alliance's 52-Day Rebuild Initiative which begins on sundown September 
10th and lasts until sundown on November 1st.
  Modeled after the Biblical account in the Book of Nehemiah where 
Nehemiah rebuilt Jerusalem's walls in 52 days, the Rebuild Initiative 
is a national spiritual movement that calls on people of faith to unify 
in extended prayer, fasting, repentance, and intercession for the 
renewal of the United States and the rebuilding of its spiritual walls.
  There is no better time to call for taking up this task than the 
250th anniversary of our nation's founding. As we continue celebrating 
the 250th birthday of our great country, I am reminded of the great 
fortune we are all afforded from our First Amendment, especially our 
Freedom of Religion.
  As the highest-ranking woman in Congress, and as a person of deep 
faith, I wake up every day determined to fight for these continued 
freedoms.
  May God Bless all of those who are participating in this Initiative, 
and may God continue to Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
