# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MOFFITT SERVICES

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MOFFITT SERVICES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Moffitt Services, out of Fort Worth, who stepped up this January 
when the winter storm brought dangerous conditions to North Texas.
  Working around the clock, Moffitt crews navigated icy roads to 
deliver fuel for generators, along with water and other critical 
supplies to nursing homes, hospitals, and organizations in need across 
the region. Their efforts helped ensure generators stayed running and 
essential services remained online for patients and residents who rely 
on them.
  Their work didn't stop when the ice melted. As conditions continued 
to improve across North Texas, Moffitt Services shifted to assisting 
with cleanup efforts, helping to remove fallen trees and storm debris.
```
