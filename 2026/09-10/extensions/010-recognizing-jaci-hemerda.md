                        RECOGNIZING JACI HEMERDA

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jaci Hemerda

  Jaci has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Jaci, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Jaci's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
