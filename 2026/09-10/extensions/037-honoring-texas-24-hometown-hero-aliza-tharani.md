# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ALIZA THARANI

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ALIZA THARANI

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Aliza Tharani. Aliza is a Euless native who combines academic 
excellence with a strong interest in science. After graduating at the 
top of her class from Colleyville Heritage High School, she began her 
undergraduate studies at Johns Hopkins University, where she is 
pursuing a degree in Neuroscience.
  Aliza has served as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in both the 
Mysore Lab, studying brain pathways, and the Jantzie Lab in 
Neurosurgery. She has also shadowed in transplant nephrology at UT 
Southwestern, gaining valuable exposure to patient-centered care.
  In addition to her academic work, Aliza has given back to the 
community by volunteering with A Place to Talk (suicide-prevention peer 
support), serving as a First Aid Attendant with the Jubilee Monument 
Corp., and participating as a National Alzheimer's Buddies volunteer.
  Recognized for her achievements, Aliza is an ISEF Finalist, a 
National Honor Society Scholarship Semifinalist, and the recipient of 
multiple awards. Through her scholarship and service, she reflects the 
values of Euless and serves as a positive example for our community.
```
