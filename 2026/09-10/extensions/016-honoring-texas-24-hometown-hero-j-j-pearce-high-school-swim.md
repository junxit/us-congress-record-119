# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO J.J. PEARCE HIGH SCHOOL SWIM TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO J.J. PEARCE HIGH SCHOOL SWIM TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, the J.J. Pearce High School swim team. They demonstrated the 
power of community by rallying behind a mission to support their 
custodian Van Hua Le. The team learned that Le was experiencing health 
issues which left him out of work for weeks, and they felt called to 
use their talents to support him.
  To help, the swim team organized a Swim-A-Thon, inviting friends, 
families, and neighbors to pledge donations for laps completed. By the 
end of the event, they had swam over 4,000 meters and collected $18,000 
for their custodian. Their kindness did not stop there. Afterwards, the 
team gifted Van Hua Le with a new car and bicycle to assist with his 
six mile commute and set up a GoFundMe campaign for him as well.
  I thank the J.J. Pearce High School swim team for showing North Texas 
what it means to lift one another up in difficult times. Their 
compassion inspires us all.

                          ____________________
```
