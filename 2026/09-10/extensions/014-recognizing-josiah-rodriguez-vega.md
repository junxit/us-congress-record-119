# RECOGNIZING JOSIAH RODRIGUEZ VEGA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                   RECOGNIZING JOSIAH RODRIGUEZ VEGA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Josiah 
Rodriguez Vega for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors 
for Youth Award.
  Josiah has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Josiah, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Josiah's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Josiah Rodriguez Vega on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
