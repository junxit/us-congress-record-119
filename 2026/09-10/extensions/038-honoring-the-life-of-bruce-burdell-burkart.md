# HONORING THE LIFE OF BRUCE BURDELL BURKART

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE OF BRUCE BURDELL BURKART

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Bruce Burdell Burkart of Doylestown, Pennsylvania, who passed 
away on August 27, 2026, at the age of 94.
  Born and raised in Doylestown, Bruce dedicated his life to his 
family, his community, and his country. A graduate of North Wales High 
School, he was a talented multi-sport athlete and remained connected to 
his alma mater throughout his life. He later served in the United 
States Army during the Korean War before building a nearly 27-year 
career with Georgia-Pacific.
  Bruce's commitment to public service was extraordinary. He served as 
New Britain Borough's first building inspector, tax collector for 24 
years, a member of Borough Council, Judge of Elections, and Borough 
Historian. His service extended well beyond elected and appointed 
positions, as he helped preserve local landmarks, supported community 
projects, volunteered at the Mercer Museum, a longtime Red Cross blood 
donor, and Meals on Wheels advocate. Following his beloved wife's 
passing, he continued his service in her honor.
  Bruce's wit, generosity, and willingness to lend a hand left a 
lasting impression on those around him. In recognition of his family's 
contributions to the community, New Britain Borough Council renamed 
Borough Hall as Burkart Hall in 2013.
  Above all, Bruce cherished his family. On behalf of the United States 
Congress, I extend my deepest condolences to the Burkart family and 
join them in honoring a life distinguished by military service, civic 
leadership, generosity, and devotion to family. Bruce's contributions 
to his community will endure, and his memory will remain a cherished 
part of the lives he touched.
```
