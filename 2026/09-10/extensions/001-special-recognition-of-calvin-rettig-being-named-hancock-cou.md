# SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF CALVIN RETTIG BEING NAMED HANCOCK COUNTY VETERAN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF CALVIN RETTIG BEING NAMED HANCOCK COUNTY VETERAN 
                              OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
SSgt. Calvin ``Cal'' Rettig, who has been named Hancock County Veteran 
of the Year. This honor recognizes Cal's decades of service to his 
family, community, and the Nation.
  Cal was drafted into the U.S. Army in 1944, where he trained for a 
frontline unit. He was tasked with detecting the enemy and relaying 
intelligence back to the artillery. His unit was initially sent to the 
Philippines to begin night patrols. Following the bombing of Japan, his 
group occupied Tokyo, where Cal served on security and police patrol as 
a staff sergeant until his release in 1946.
  Cal married his wife, Bernice, on the day he returned home. The two 
of them had met in 1941 and dated until he left for the war. They were 
married for sixty-five years. They raised three children, and their 
family has since grown to include twenty grandchildren and twenty-five 
great-grandchildren.
  After his discharge, Cal built two full careers. From 1948 to 1966, 
he managed the Farm Bureau lumber yard. After the lumber yard, he spent 
the next twenty-two years at Whirlpool as a mold setter. He also farmed 
340 acres throughout both careers.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating SSgt. 
Rettig on being named the Hancock County Veteran of the Year. On behalf 
of the people of the Fifth Congressional District of Ohio, I recognize 
the devotion and compassion that Cal has for his community, and I thank 
him for his service to our Nation.
```
