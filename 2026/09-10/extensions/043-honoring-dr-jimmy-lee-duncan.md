# HONORING DR. JIMMY LEE DUNCAN

**Speaking:** McDowell, Addison P. (M001240) [R-NC]

```
                     HONORING DR. JIMMY LEE DUNCAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ADDISON P. McDOWELL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. McDOWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Jimmy Lee 
Duncan, a resident of Salisbury, North Carolina, whose life has been 
defined by service to our Nation and to the people of Rowan County.
  Dr. Duncan began his distinguished career of service in the United 
States Army, where he served for more than two decades, including 
during the Vietnam War with the 101st Airborne Division, before 
retiring as a Lieutenant Colonel His devotion to serving others didn't 
end with his military retirement.
  Dr. Duncan continued that legacy at home through decades of 
leadership in his community. Alongside his wife, Linda, and daughter, 
Susan, he founded Project Light, Rowan County's first full-time anti-
human trafficking agency. Through their vision, courage, and 
compassion, Project Light has grown into a regional force to accelerate 
the fight against human trafficking.
  On behalf of North Carolina's 6th District, I thank Dr. Duncan and 
his family for a lifetime of service and an enduring legacy that will 
continue to strengthen our community for generations to come.
```
