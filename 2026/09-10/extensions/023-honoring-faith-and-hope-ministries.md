# HONORING FAITH AND HOPE MINISTRIES

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING FAITH AND HOPE MINISTRIES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Faith and Hope 
Ministries, located at 9943 E. Tampa Dr. in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, 
East Baton Rouge Parish. Founded in 2007 by Rev. Felton J. Thomas, Sr., 
Faith and Hope Ministries has faithfully served its community for 9 
years, a number symbolizing divine completeness and the fulfillment of 
God's promise. In 2007, God gave Pastor Thomas a vision for the 
ministry that would unfold over ten years. In 2017, the Lord revealed 
it was time for the vision to come to fruition, leading to the search 
for a church home among nearly 100 buildings.
  From this extensive list, only two calls were returned: one 
discouraging, and one from Ms. Marie Reado at Any Occasion Banquet 
Hall, who graciously offered her space. On May 7, 2017, Faith and Hope 
Ministries officially opened its doors there at 10565 Florida Blvd. 
After 6 months, Sister Michelle Scott introduced the ministry to its 
second home at 1913 North St., where the first service was held on 
October 8, 2017.
  The onset of the Coronavirus pandemic in 2020 brought unprecedented 
challenges, mandating a halt to congregational worship in March. 
Despite this, the church continued to fellowship by phone, remaining 
encouraged and steadfast in faith. When churches were able to reopen in 
June 2020 with social distancing, Faith and Hope Ministries joyfully 
reunited in worship, persevering even as many churches, businesses, and 
families could not. In November 2021, circumstances led to the farewell 
of the second church home, but the congregation adapted by returning to 
the phone line for Sunday services and Thursday Bible Studies.
  In December, Pastor Thomas, after meditating and expressing the 
desires of his heart to God, received a call from Mr. Eddie Vosper, 
bringing the ministry back to worship at 2127 Beaumont Dr. on January 
2, 2022. Since its inception, Faith and Hope Ministries has ordained 
deacons, deaconesses, teachers, and evangelists, baptized many, 
distributed school supplies yearly, launched a feeding program for the 
homeless, established a clothes closet, participated in the Black 
Voters Matters initiative to educate and assist community members in 
voting, canvassed communities to spread voter knowledge, provided 
transportation to polling places, implemented prayer services, and 
partnered with Southern University to offer workshops aimed at crime 
prevention.
  Beyond Louisiana, the ministry's ``Georgia crew'' feeds the homeless, 
distributes school supplies, provides gas stipends, and passes out 
clothing to those in need. Currently, Faith and Hope Ministries counts 
80 members, not including adopted members, faithful visitors, friends 
who support the ministry, or those joining by phone from across the 
United States. Through continual prayer and faith, the ministry is 
poised for growth so that God's word will reach even more people.
  As previously stated, the number nine represents completeness and 
fulfillment of God's promise, and Faith and Hope Ministries stands as a 
testament to that promise. The congregation looks forward with 
excitement to the next steps, having witnessed countless miracles since 
the ministry's inception. To all who hear my voice, whether present 
physically or joining by phone, be prepared to witness the miracles God 
will bring forth from this place and to share in our prayer by 
attending.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me honoring Faith and Hope 
Ministries Church as we celebrate almost 10 years of faithful service, 
and in wishing its pastor and congregation continued blessings for the 
years to come.
```
