# HONORING NEW PHILADELPHIA BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                HONORING NEW PHILADELPHIA BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the rich 
history and enduring legacy of New Philadelphia Baptist Church, a 
pillar of faith, family, and community in Slaughter, Louisiana.
  In 1960, a dedicated group of community members--Marshall Sanford, 
Mary L. Turner, Lucille Cain, Nimrod Sanford, Fannie Franklin, 
Henrietta Rogers, Beatrice Sanford, David Sanford, Ada Jones, and 
Elizabeth Fields--donated 1.5 acres of land at 9027 Sanford Lane for 
the creation of a house of worship. Their generosity laid the 
foundation for what was originally called Philadelphia Baptist Church 
No. 2, later known as New Philadelphia Baptist Church.
  Founded in that same year by Solomon Franklin and Nimrod Sanford, the 
church's first pastor was Reverend D.E. Major, who faithfully led the 
congregation from 1960 until his retirement in December 2006. Under 
Reverend Dr. Major's leadership, New Philadelphia established numerous 
ministries, installed an indoor baptismal pool, remodeled the choir 
stand, and constructed an annex that included a pastor's study, kitchen 
and dining area, bathroom, and nursery or classroom space. The church 
also purchased a 16-passenger van to ensure members could attend 
services and church functions.
  The Trustee and Deacon Boards in the church's early years were 
comprised of Brothers Marshall ``Dude'' Henderson, James Holliday, 
Johnnie Hughes, Alonzo Sanford, Henry Sanford, Johnny Sanford, Marshall 
Sanford, Purley Sanford, Robert ``Bud'' Sanford, Cleveland ``C.L.'' 
Spann, H. Wallace, Eugene Watson, and Henry Williams Sister Celestine 
W. Sanford served as Church Clerk, and Sister Rosella Davis served as 
Assistant Clerk.
  In March 2006, the church received an additional .42 acres of land 
donated by Deborah, Leslie, and Jeanelle Schmidt. Following Reverend 
Dr. Major's retirement, he served as Pastor Emeritus until 2012.
  In December 2006, Reverend Theodore Major became the anointed pastor 
of New Philadelphia Baptist Church. His tenure has led to the 
construction of a new pastor's study, the expansion and revitalization 
of various ministries--including the Sisterhood, now the Women's 
Ministry--and the establishment of a Community Educational Organization 
and a building fund. In February 2008, Deacon Freddie Holliday sold .38 
acres to the church, bringing the total campus area to 2.30 acres. 
Additional accomplishments include the construction of two new 
bathrooms at the church entrance, the establishment of a Youth Usher 
Ministry (revived in 2026), and the founding of a Male Chorus.
  In 2017, New Philadelphia Baptist Church was officially registered 
with the Secretary of State as New Philadelphia Baptist Church of 
Slaughter. The following year, the church expanded again, adding an 
administrative office and the Dr. D. E. Major Learning Center, 
connected by Sanford Hall. In 2022, the church updated the flooring in 
the Sanctuary, Annex, and Kitchen, and purchased a new copy machine and 
computer. The next year brought the addition of a new piano and 
television monitors. In 2025, the church received a fresh coat of paint 
and purchased a new organ.
  New Philadelphia Baptist Church is proudly affiliated with the 
Louisiana Baptist Convention, the East Feliciana Ministers Conference, 
and the National Baptist Convention USA, Inc.
  For more than six decades, New Philadelphia Baptist Church has grown 
in membership, ministries, and outreach--remaining firmly rooted on the 
land donated in faith and love in 1960. Today, it continues to serve as 
a Christian family united in purpose, committed to spiritual growth, 
and devoted to the principles upon which it was founded.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing New 
Philadelphia Baptist Church for its outstanding contributions to the 
community and for its steadfast mission of worship, service, and 
fellowship.
```
