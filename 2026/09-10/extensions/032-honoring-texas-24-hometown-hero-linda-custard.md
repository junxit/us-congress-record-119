# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO LINDA CUSTARD

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO LINDA CUSTARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Linda Custard. She demonstrated generosity and support for our 
North Texas community, and her alma mater, Southern Methodist 
University (SMU). Her life reflected a deep commitment to the arts, 
education, and civic engagement. She sadly passed away earlier this 
year, on January 22, 2026. Custard began at SMU as a speech arts 
student before earning her undergraduate degree from Mills College and 
later returning to SMU to complete her MBA at the Cox School of 
Business. She assumed several leadership roles, including serving on 
the SMU Board of Trustees. She also played a key role in advancing the 
Meadows School of the Arts and the Meadows Museum, contributing 
meaningfully to the university and the wider community.
  Custard was committed to helping initiatives that advanced the arts 
and education. She received awards recognizing her leadership and 
philanthropy at SMU, including the Cox School of Business Distinguished 
Alumni Award, the SMU Students' Association's Outstanding Trustee 
Award, the SMU Distinguished Alumni Award, and the Mustang Award. She 
was known for her dedication, influence, and passion for giving back.
  We thank Linda Pitts Custard for her service to our North Texas 
community and the lasting impression she leaves behind.

                          ____________________
```
