# HONORING DONALDSON CHAPEL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                HONORING DONALDSON CHAPEL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 10, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Donaldson Chapel, a 
historic and steadfast institution of faith whose beginnings trace back 
to the early years of the twentieth century in the community now known 
as Easy Town.
  In the early 1900s, a group of believers conceived the idea of 
establishing a church that would serve as a spiritual home for 
generations to come. The congregation's first pioneer and organizer, 
Reverend Blackman K. Donaldson, formally established the church on July 
21, 1912, naming the new temple Donaldson

[[Page E898]]

Chapel. For 53 years, Reverend Donaldson faithfully shepherded the 
church, guiding its growth and shaping its role as a pillar in the 
community. His passing in July 1966 left a devoted body of worshipers 
committed to carrying forward the vision he had nurtured.
  In April 1967, Reverend Freddie Williams of Port Allen, Louisiana, 
was called to pastor Donaldson Chapel. During his determined and 
hardworking tenure, the congregation paid off the church mortgage--an 
achievement that strengthened the church's foundation both spiritually 
and financially. Reverend Williams served until 1970, when he was 
called to his eternal reward.
  On August 4, 1970, Reverend Charles T. Smith was elected pastor, 
initiating a new era of spiritual and numerical growth. Under his 
leadership, the church embarked on two major remodeling projects, first 
in 1972 and then in 1986, which resulted in new furniture, a new organ, 
and the purchase of a church van. Reverend Smith also pastored the 
Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church, and as Shiloh continued to grow, he 
made the thoughtful decision to serve one church full time. In May 
1997, Reverend Smith concluded his distinguished ministry at Donaldson 
Chapel.
  In July 1997, a familiar face assumed leadership. Assistant Pastor 
Tommie N. Gipson, Jr., was elected full-time pastor, continuing the 
legacy of spiritual strength fostered by Pastor Smith. Under Pastor 
Gipson's leadership, Donaldson Chapel entered the modern era with 
significant physical and ministry-related expansions. The parking lots 
were paved, four lots were purchased, and three houses were transformed 
into educational facilities. Additional improvements included roof 
repairs, new microphones, and enhanced computer systems for the media 
ministry. Today, Donaldson Chapel stands as a thriving congregation 
with more than 500 members.
  Pastor Gipson has ensured that Donaldson Chapel remains deeply 
committed to serving its community. With a strong emphasis on 
education, children, youth, discipleship, and worship, he has 
implemented a children's nursery, youth church, children's church, and 
elevated Sunday School as a top priority. He also established adult 
ministries such as the course of study, prison ministry, and clothes 
closet, among other programs. Under his leadership, the church has 
become a consistent supporter of Park Elementary School as well as 
numerous civic and religious organizations.
  Looking toward the future, Pastor Gipson and the Deacon Ministry 
enlisted the expertise of local architect Merald Poche to design a 
concept for forthcoming Donaldson Chapel facilities. Their forward-
looking vision seeks to introduce 21st century buildings that will not 
only accommodate a growing congregation but also stand as a testament 
to God's continued movement--breaking chains and performing miracles, 
one by one.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Donaldson 
Chapel for more than a century of faithful service, spiritual 
leadership, and community dedication. May its pastor, leadership, and 
congregation continue to be blessed as they honor their past and boldly 
pursue the future.
```
