# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 1520

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 13 of rule I and H. Res. 
1520, the House stands adjourned until noon on Monday next for morning-
hour debate, and 2 p.m. for legislative business, as a further mark of 
respect for the victims of the terrorist attack.
  Thereupon (at 3 o'clock and 33 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until Monday, September 14, 2026, at noon 
for morning hour debate as a further mark of respect for the victims of 
the terrorist attack.
```
