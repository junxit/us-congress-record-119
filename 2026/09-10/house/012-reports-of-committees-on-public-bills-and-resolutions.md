```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. STEIL: Committee on House Administration. H.R. 9360. A 
     bill to sunset the Advisory Committee on the Records of 
     Congress, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 
     119-808). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. STEIL: Committee on House Administration. H.R. 9342. A 
     bill to revise certain authorities of the Government 
     Publishing Office, and for other purposes; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 119-809, Pt. 1). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 4931. A 
     bill to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to extend 
     certain leases within units of the National Park System 
     without opening the lease to bidding; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 119-810). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2317. A 
     bill to provide for transfer of ownership of certain Federal 
     lands in northern Nevada, to authorize the disposal of 
     certain Federal lands in northern Nevada for economic 
     development, to promote conservation in northern Nevada, and 
     for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-811, Pt. 1). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. GARBARINO: Committee on Homeland Security. H.R. 9327. A 
     bill to establish in U.S. Customs and Border Protection a 
     pilot program to adopt dogs from local animal shelters to be 
     trained as support dogs, and for other purposes; (Rept. 119-
     812). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. GARBARINO: Committee on Homeland Security. H.R. 9394. A 
     bill to require the Department of Homeland Security to 
     conduct an exercise related to agroterrorism, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 119-813). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GUTHRIE: Committee on Energy and Commerce. H.R. 9340. A 
     bill to amend the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 
     1978 to establish a Federal standard relating to the recovery 
     of the full, incremental costs of upgrades that serve large-
     load customers, and for other purposes; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 119-814). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.
```
