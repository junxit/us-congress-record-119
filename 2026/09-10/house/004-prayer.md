# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  We give You thanks, good and gracious God, for the blessings of this 
day and for the privilege of caring for this Nation that we call home. 
Watch over and protect our Representatives in Congress, who have been 
entrusted with serving our country and her people. Bless their families 
and staffs and all who labor in this Capitol. Sustain them in their 
work, keep them mindful of those they serve, and hold us ever in Your 
loving care.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
