# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, 
offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, known to us in countless ways and times without number, 
we turn to You that in Your light we might see light.
  Lord, so much pain, grief, and despair exists in our world. Give us 
Your balm, comfort, and hope. As our lawmakers work, help them to see 
the role they can play in doing Your will on Earth. As they become more 
aware of Your presence, may their lives experience the splendor, 
wisdom, and strength that You alone can give. May they not forget that 
You are still their mighty fortress, a bulwark never failing.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
