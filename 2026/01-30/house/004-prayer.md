# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  To You, O Lord, we pray for mercy. In our prosperity, we assume that 
nothing bad will come our way. In our tranquility, we believe that we 
will never be shaken.
  How easily we receive Your favor. Like a mountain that cannot be 
moved, we stand assured that You have endowed us with strength and 
majesty.
  Have pity on us, O God, when it seems that You have hidden Your face 
from us. Hear us when we cry to You for mercy. Without You beside us, 
to guide us through the times of want and weakness, we are terrified. 
Deprived of Your loving-kindness, we know nothing but dismay and 
despair.
  To You, O Lord, we cry for mercy, that even in our worst moments, we 
would feel secure in Your compassion, hopeful in Your deliverance, and 
unwavering in the strength that is found only in You.
  In Your strong and gracious name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
