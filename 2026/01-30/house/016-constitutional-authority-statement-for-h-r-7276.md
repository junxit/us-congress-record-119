# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7276

**Measures:** H.R. 7276

```
           By Mr. AMODEI of Nevada:
       H.R. 7276.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The consitutional authority to impose tariffs is found in 
     Section 8, Article I of the United States Constitution, which 
     grants Congress the power to levy taxes, duties, imposts, and 
     excises.
```
