# Text of Senate Amendment 4295

**Speaking:** Welch, Peter (W000800) [D-VT]

```
  SA 4295. Mr. WELCH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 7148, making further consolidated appropriations 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 1176, after line 17, add the following:
       Sec. 239.  The Director of U.S. Immigration and Customs 
     Enforcement (ICE) shall republish ICE Directive 19009 in full 
     without redactions to reestablish mandatory procedures and 
     protocols concerning firearms and the use of force by ICE 
     employees.
```
