# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, from whom we come, to whom we belong, and in whose 
service is our peace, may Your Kingdom come. Lord, use our lawmakers to 
do Your will on Earth as it is done in Heaven. Create in them 
courageous hearts that will be undaunted by fear, unconquered by 
adversity, and unstained by sin.
  Provide our Senators with the wisdom to remember Your words in 
Matthew 7:12:

       Therefore, whatever you want people to do to you, do also 
     to them.

  Strengthen our lawmakers to lift downcast lives, hopeless hearts, and 
grieving spirits.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
