# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, February 2; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate be in a period 
of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each; finally, that notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture 
motion with respect to Calendar No. 610, David Fowlkes, ripen at 5:30 
p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
