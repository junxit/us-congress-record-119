# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 221, H.Res. 1646, H.Res. 1647, H.R. 10952, H.R. 10953

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BAUMGARTNER:
       H.R. 10952. A bill to promote forest health and reduce 
     wildfire risk by prioritizing forest management through 
     stewardship in high-risk firesheds, providing tax incentives 
     for biomass energy production, and supporting economic 
     utilization of forest products, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Agriculture, and in addition to the 
     Committees on Ways and Means, and Natural Resources, for a 
     period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each 
     case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mr. KEAN:
       H.R. 10953. A bill to direct the Secretary of Heath and 
     Human Services to establish and carry out a program for 
     making allotments to States to pay for the cost of carrying 
     out the State Ombudsman Program to serve as a resource to 
     provide information and support to individuals with 
     developmental disabilities and their families; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce, and in addition to the 
     Committee on Education and Workforce, for a period to be 
     subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Ms. SCHOLTEN:
       H.J. Res. 221. A joint resolution proposing an amendment to 
     the Constitution of the United States giving Congress power 
     to regulate campaign contributions for Federal elections; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. CARTER of Louisiana (for himself, Mr. Menendez, 
             Mr. McCormick, Mr. Lieu, Mr. Dunn of Florida, Mr. 
             Jackson of Illinois, Mrs. Miller of West Virginia, 
             and Mr. Mullin):
       H. Res. 1646. A resolution supporting the recognition of 
     November as ``Bladder Health Awareness Month'', supporting 
     efforts to increase public education, reduce stigma, and 
     improve access to care for bladder health conditions, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Ms. DEAN of Pennsylvania (for herself and Mr. 
             Fitzpatrick):
       H. Res. 1647. A resolution expressing support for the 
     recognition of the week of November 1 through November 7, 
     2026, as ``Drowsy Driving Prevention Week''; to the Committee 
     on Transportation and Infrastructure.

                          ____________________
```
