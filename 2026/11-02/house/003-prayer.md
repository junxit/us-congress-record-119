# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. John Lado, St. Peter's Catholic Church, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, You are an all-provident father. We thank You 
for including us in Your wonderful design. As we open this session, 
although not burdened with the full weight of legislative work, may our 
hearts and minds be imbued with Your wisdom that we may continue our 
mission to establish peace and justice for all people.
  May Your spirit guide us in our deliberations and in our watchful 
care of this great Nation which we call our home. Bless all of us and 
never let us stray from Your attentive and fatherly care nor from Your 
compassionate love. We ask this in the name of the Lord.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
