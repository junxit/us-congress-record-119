# RECOGNIZING RED SILO COFFEE ROASTERS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                  RECOGNIZING RED SILO COFFEE ROASTERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Red Silo Coffee 
Roasters of Arvada, Colorado for receiving the 2026 Outstanding Small 
Business Award from the Arvada Economic Development Association. Each 
year, this award recognizes an Arvada small business that stands apart 
for its job growth, innovation, community involvement, outreach, and 
more.
  Founders Greg Pierce and Shelley Flood, who had worked together on 
construction and design projects for more than 20 years, chose Arvada 
as the home for their first craft restaurant and roastery: Red Silo 
Coffee Roasters. Since its founding, Red Silo has grown from a single 
Arvada storefront into four locations, with continued expansion 
creating local jobs across its cafe, roasting, and production 
operations. Red Silo is set apart by its commitment to developing its 
employees, sourcing directly from farmers, and small-batch roasting. 
Red Silo has also expanded its local production with an artisan ice 
cream creamery featuring natural, organic, from-scratch ingredients. 
Red Silo embodies the innovation, hard work, and community spirit that 
make small businesses thrive.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Red Silo Coffee Roasters for receiving the 
2026 Outstanding Small Business Award and thank them for their 
contributions to the Arvada community.
```
