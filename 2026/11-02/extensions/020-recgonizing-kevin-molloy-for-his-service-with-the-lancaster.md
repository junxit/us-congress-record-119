# RECGONIZING KEVIN MOLLOY FOR HIS SERVICE WITH THE LANCASTER COUNTY CONVENTION CENTER AUTHORITY

```
  RECGONIZING KEVIN MOLLOY FOR HIS SERVICE WITH THE LANCASTER COUNTY 
                      CONVENTION CENTER AUTHORITY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr, SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I would like to recognize Kevin R. Molloy, 
who has served as executive director of the Lancaster County Convention 
Center Authority (LCCCA) since 2008.
  Kevin Molloy, who previously worked for authorities and groups across 
the country, became executive director of the LCCCA in May 2008. He 
joined the authority when the convention center was still under 
construction and he oversaw its opening in June 2009.
  Under Kevin's leadership. the Lancaster County Convention Center has 
been at the heart of the city of Lancaster's economic and cultural 
life. Over a hundred thousand people from across the country attend 
events there annually, ranging from consumer shows and sporting events 
to concerts and science fiction conventions. In turn, the convention 
center generates millions of dollars in economic impact for the city, 
county, and commonwealth and provides jobs for over 300 people. Kevin 
also oversaw the expansion of the Lancaster County Convention Center to 
include a new event space, the Commons on Vine, and worked with 
LancasterHistory to develop the Thaddeus Stevens and Lydia Hamilton 
Smith Center for History and Democracy.
  The Lancaster County Convention Center has played an important role 
in revitalizing the City of Lancaster in large part thanks to Kevin 
Molloy's leadership as executive director of the LCCCA. I thank Kevin 
for his hard work and service to the community and wish him the best in 
his next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
