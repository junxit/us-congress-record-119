# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF GLORIA MAE GURLEA

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
          RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF GLORIA MAE GURLEA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Gloria Mae Gurlea, a 
lifelong daughter of Michigan's Upper Peninsula, as she celebrates her 
100th birthday on October 16, 2026. Gloria has spent a century living 
by the values of faith, family, service, and devotion to our Nation.
  Born in Escanaba in 1926 to Maurice and Katherine Connor, Gloria grew 
up alongside her sister and best friend, Faye, in Turin and later New 
Swanzy. She graduated from Gwinn High School in 1944. In 1949, Gloria 
answered the call to serve her country and enlisted in the United 
States Navy, completing basic training at the Great Lakes Naval 
Training Center.
  Gloria's first assignment brought her to Washington, D.C., where she 
served as a Cryptologic Technician 2nd Class at the Naval Operations 
Center on Nebraska Avenue during the Korean War. While stationed in 
Washington, she met Corporal Victor Gurlea, whom she married in 1951.
  Following her honorable discharge, Gloria spent the next two decades 
traveling the world alongside Victor as he continued his career in the 
United States Army. Together, they raised two sons, Timothy and Paul, 
building a family grounded in service and resilience.
  Following Victor's service in Vietnam, the Gurlea family returned 
home to the Upper Peninsula in 1969 and settled once again in New 
Swanzy. Today, Gloria remains part of that same community as a resident 
of Mill Creek Assisted Living in Marquette.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Gloria 
Mae Gurlea on this remarkable milestone and thanking her for her 
service to our Nation. May she and her family continue to be blessed 
for many years to come.
```
