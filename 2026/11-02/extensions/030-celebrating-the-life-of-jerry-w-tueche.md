# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF JERRY W. TUECHE

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
                CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF JERRY W. TUECHE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life of Jerry W. Tueche 
of Manheim Township, who passed away on September 30, 2026.
  Mr. Jerry Tueche was a father to Michele, a grandfather to Christian, 
Weston, and Taylor, and a husband to Marjorie for 57 years prior to her 
passing in 2018.
  Though he first began his career in different sectors, Jerry's 
professional calling was in public service. From 1971 to 2005, he 
served the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania in various capacities, 
including roles within the Governor's Office, the Department of 
Commerce, the State Treasury, and the Auditor General's Office. He also 
returned to Western Pennsylvania and served as County Manager for 
Fayette County. Even after his retirement and move to Manheim Township, 
Jerry continued serving his community. He served as Manheim Township 
Tax Collector and Treasurer and spent many years as Vice-Chairperson of 
Manheim Township's Civil Service Commission. He was also active in the 
local Republican committee, assisted in local political campaigns, and 
was an ardent volunteer for civic organizations like the Rotary Club.
  Jerry Tueche lived an exciting and full life, and his impact on both 
our community and Pennsylvania cannot be understated. I am glad to have 
known him. In recognizing and celebrating his life, I offer prayers of 
comfort to his family during this time.
```
