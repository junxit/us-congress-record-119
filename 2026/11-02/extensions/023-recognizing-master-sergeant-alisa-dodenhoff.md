# RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT ALISA DODENHOFF

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT ALISA DODENHOFF

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Air Force veteran, 
Master Sergeant Alisa Dodenhoff of Midlothian, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. Her dedication to our Nation, 
fellow service members, and community exemplifies the highest ideals of 
service and leadership.
  Master Sergeant Dodenhoff began her Air Force career in November 
2011, serving on active duty until November 2017 before continuing her 
service in the Air Force Reserve. Throughout her career, she had 
deployments in support of Operations Enduring Freedom and Operation 
Inherent Resolve. Master Sergeant Dodenhoffs military honors include 
the Air and Space Commendation Medal, Meritorious Unit Award with three 
Oak Leaf Clusters, Air and Space Outstanding Unit Award, and Military 
Outstanding Volunteer Service Medal, among numerous other awards and 
commendations.
  Following her military service, Master Sergeant Dodenhoff continued 
her commitment to servant leadership through mentorship, community 
involvement, and support for veterans, military families, students, and 
aspiring leaders throughout North Texas. She actively coaches and 
mentors students at Arlington ISD, helping young people build 
confidence, develop leadership skills, and prepare for their futures. 
She also serves as a leader with the DFW Professional Women's 
Networking Group, where she empowers women through mentorship, 
professional development, and meaningful community connections. Master 
Sergeant Dodenhoff remains dedicated to supporting veterans and 
military families through her involvement with the Veteran Tickets 
Foundation, helping create opportunities for veterans and their 
families to attend events and be recognized for their service. Her 
commitment to community and economic development is further 
demonstrated through her service on the Board of Directors of the 
Midlothian Chamber of Commerce, where she contributes to the growth and 
strength of the local business community.
  Master Sergeant Dodenhoff's dedication to serving her Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. I commend her remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for her steadfast commitment to our veterans, 
military families, students, and the North Texas community.
```
