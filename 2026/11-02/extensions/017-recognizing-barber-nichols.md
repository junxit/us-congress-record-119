# RECOGNIZING BARBER-NICHOLS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BARBER-NICHOLS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr Speaker, I rise today to recognize Barber-Nichols 
of Arvada, Colorado for receiving the 2026 Outstanding Large Employer 
Award from the Arvada Economic Development Association. Each year, this 
award recognizes a large employer that stands apart for their job 
growth, innovation, community involvement, outreach, and more.
  Barber-Nichols exemplifies outstanding corporate leadership through 
its commitment to workforce development, innovation, and connection to 
the Arvada community. The company is helping build the next generation 
of skilled workers through its apprenticeship program with Warren Tech, 
Manufacturing Day tours, student visits, and hands-on STEM 
opportunities. Young people gain firsthand exposure to machining and 
engineering work that supports critical applications in aerospace, 
defense, energy, and naval systems. Barber-Nichols also invests in the 
Arvada community by supporting local schools, outdoor education, and 
holiday food and toy drives. I am grateful for Barber-Nichols' 
continued dedication to employees, education, and the Arvada community.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Barber-Nichols for receiving the 2026 
Outstanding Large Employer Award and thank them for their contributions 
to the Arvada community.

                          ____________________
```
