# HONORING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS GARY GLADUE

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
               HONORING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS GARY GLADUE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sergeant First 
Class Gary Gladue for his distinguished service to our Nation and his 
continued dedication to Veterans and the community of Menominee, 
Michigan.
  Sergeant First Class Gladue's commitment to service did not end when 
he left the military. Today, he continues that work in his community as 
Commander of American Legion Post 146 in Menominee and Adjutant of VFW 
Post 1887. Gary dedicates his time to supporting fellow Veterans, 
bringing them together, and making sure their service is remembered and 
recognized.
  Gary's dedication to his fellow Veterans is evident in the countless 
ways he continues to serve. As a volunteer driver for the Disabled 
American Veterans, he frequently makes the nearly two-and-a-half-hour 
round trip to transport local Veterans to the Oscar G. Johnson VA 
Medical Center in Iron Mountain, ensuring they can access the care and 
benefits they have earned. Through the Military 20 Year Club, Gary 
helps provide military funeral honors, support community events, and 
award scholarships to local students. He also volunteers with UP Health 
System Home Care and Hospice, where he visits Veterans nearing the end 
of their lives, listens to their stories, and provides companionship 
when it matters most.
  Outside of his work with Veterans organizations, Gary is always 
willing to lend a helping hand to a neighbor or fellow Veteran in need. 
He recently accepted two donated mobility chairs and is repairing them 
so they can be provided to Veterans who need them. He and fellow 
volunteers also delivered a donated lift chair to a disabled Veteran in 
Stephenson, Michigan. His wife, Lisa, is often by his side in these 
efforts, and together they have made helping their neighbors a regular 
part of their daily lives.
  Mr. Speaker, let us join in recognizing Sergeant First Class Gary 
Gladue for his lifetime of service to our Nation, our Veterans, and his 
community.
```
