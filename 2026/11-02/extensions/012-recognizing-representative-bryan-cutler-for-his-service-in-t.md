# RECOGNIZING REPRESENTATIVE BRYAN CUTLER FOR HIS SERVICE IN THE PENNSYLVANIA GENERAL ASSEMBLY

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
    RECOGNIZING REPRESENTATIVE BRYAN CUTLER FOR HIS SERVICE IN THE 
                     PENNSYLVANIA GENERAL ASSEMBLY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and congratulate 
Representative Bryan Cutler on his retirement from the Pennsylvania 
General Assembly.
  Representative Cutler has served ten terms as the Representative for 
the 100th Legislative District in southern Lancaster County. From June 
2020 through January 2023, he served as the 139th Speaker of the House, 
having previously served as Majority Leader, and in the 2023-2024 
legislative session he served as Minority Leader.
  A native of Peach Bottom, Representative Cutler faced adversity from 
an early age when both of his parents were diagnosed with ALS, a 
disease that would eventually claim both of their lives. He also served 
as their caregiver. After graduating from Solanco High School, he 
became an X-ray technologist, then graduated from Lebanon Valley 
College with a degree in health care administration. Later he earned 
his law degree from Widener Law School and practiced law before being 
elected to the Pennsylvania House of Representatives in 2006.
  During his time in office, Representative Cutler has established 
himself as a leader in reducing fraud and abuse within the welfare 
system, including through his efforts to detect and prevent fraud in 
the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP), and as a 
champion of health care issues. Legislation Representative Cutler 
helped to advance eliminated redundancies in hospital accreditation 
procedures, funded new trauma centers in rural Pennsylvania, broke down 
barriers in medical records law, and established the Pennsylvania 
Health Insurance Exchange Authority, the state-based health insurance 
exchange and reinsurance program.
  Representative Bryan Cutler has spent the past twenty years serving 
the residents of southern Lancaster County and working for the benefit 
of all Pennsylvanians. I thank him for his positive impact, 
congratulate him on his well-earned retirement, and wish him and his 
family the best in his next chapter.
```
