# RECOGNIZING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS ANGELO WINFIELD

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            RECOGNIZING SERGEANT FIRST CLASS ANGELO WINFIELD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th District of Texas, the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to Sergeant 
First Class Angelo Winfield, United States Army, of Hurst, Texas, for 
his honorable service in the Armed Forces of the United States of 
America and for his continued service to his community.
  Sergeant First Class Winfield served honorably in the United States 
Army and Army Reserve for more than twenty-two years, from 1981 to 
2003. Rising through the enlisted ranks, he held leadership positions 
in medical administration, operational readiness, training, and 
personnel support. His assignments included the 351st Surgical 
Hospital, the 877th Quartermaster Company, and overseas service in El 
Salvador. He also assumed responsibilities in unit mobilization, 
safety, and family readiness, demonstrating versatility and dedication 
to his fellow soldiers.
  At the 351st Surgical Hospital, Sergeant First Class Winfield earned 
recognition for improving patient administration, strengthening 
readiness, and contributing to outstanding command inspection results. 
His decorations include multiple Army Achievement Medals, the Army 
Reserve Components Achievement Medal, and the Army Overseas Service 
Ribbon, alongside numerous commendations. He also received recognition 
for his volunteer support of soldiers and families through the Family 
Readiness Group.
  Following retirement, Sergeant First Class Winfield continued serving 
others as an information technology consultant and business owner, 
donating networking equipment to local nonprofits. He served on a 
homeless shelter's executive board, volunteered as a Big Brother 
mentor, and welcomed children into his home as a foster parent. He also 
coached YMCA basketball, supported Scouting, mentored high school 
students, and helped secure a playground grant for a charter school, 
where he assisted with construction.
  His dedication continues through longtime volunteer service at Union 
Gospel Mission of Tarrant County, where he serves meals and supports 
individuals experiencing homelessness. As a church elder, he also 
provides leadership and mentorship through his church's men's ministry. 
His compassion and willingness to serve continue to strengthen families 
and communities across North Texas.
  Sergeant First Class Winfield's honorable military service and 
lifelong commitment to supporting fellow service members, strengthening 
families, and uplifting those in need reflect the highest traditions of 
the United States Army and bring great credit upon himself, the State 
of Texas, and the United States of America.
```
