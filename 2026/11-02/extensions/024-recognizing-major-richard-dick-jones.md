# RECOGNIZING MAJOR RICHARD "DICK" JONES

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                RECOGNIZING MAJOR RICHARD ``DICK'' JONES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th District of Texas, the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to Major Richard 
Jones, United States Air Force, of Grapevine, Texas, for his honorable 
service in the Armed Forces of the United States of America and for his 
continued service to his community.
  Major Jones served with distinction in the United States Air Force 
for twenty years, beginning as an enlisted Airman before earning his 
commission and becoming a military aviator. He accumulated 
approximately 10,000 flight hours and served as an Operations Officer 
with the 1605th Air Base Wing under Military Airlift Command. His 
decorations include the Air Force Commendation Medal, National Defense 
Service Medal with one bronze service star, Air Force Good Conduct 
Medal, Armed Forces Expeditionary Medal, and Air Force Outstanding Unit 
Award with one oak leaf cluster. He retired as a Major in 1972.
  Following his retirement, Major Jones devoted decades to civic 
leadership and volunteerism in Grapevine. He served on the Executive 
Board of the Grapevine Chamber of

[[Page E1151]]

Commerce, worked as a Chamber Ambassador, and graduated from Leadership 
Grapevine. Alongside his wife, Paula, he volunteered at city festivals, 
including co-chairing the 2010 Grapevine Main Street Days Festival. As 
an original member of the Grapevine Wine Pouring Society, he also 
helped promote the Texas wine industry through community events.
  A longtime member and Paul Harris Fellow of the Grapevine Rotary 
Club, Major Jones has supported civic initiatives and international 
fellowship. As an original member of Grapevine's Sister Cities program, 
he fostered relationships with communities in Mexico and Austria. He 
also served for years as a volunteer docent at the Grapevine History 
Museum and, alongside Paula, was named a Lifetime Member of the 
Grapevine Historical Society in 2024.
  Major Jones has continued supporting the military community through 
the Military Officers Association of America and the Order of 
Daedalians, where he served as Scholarship Chairman for at least five 
years, recognizing local high school ROTC students. His decades of 
service have strengthened Grapevine and inspired his family to follow 
his example.
  Major Jones's distinguished military career and lifelong dedication 
to his community, fellow veterans, and future generations reflect the 
highest traditions of the United States Air Force and bring great 
credit upon himself, the State of Texas, and the United States of 
America.
```
