# RECOGNIZING FLARE

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                           RECOGNIZING FLARE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Flourish in 
Life through Art and Resources for Endometriosis (FLARE) for their work 
making the realities of endometriosis visible through patient-created 
artwork, healthcare education, research, and advocacy.
  In September of 2026, FLARE will hold their inaugural Art Exhibit in 
New York City, bringing awareness to a chronic illness that continues 
to be one of the most under-recognized medical conditions in the world. 
The exhibit will make endometriosis visible by presenting it through 
the eyes of artists experiencing the real symptoms and life disruptions 
that chronic pain causes.
  Chelsea Hardesty, a health policy advocate and artist from Lakewood 
in Colorado's 7th Congressional District, has worked tirelessly to 
connect local leadership with the growing national movement to improve 
access to endometriosis education and healthcare. Her dedication to 
advocacy supports patients with endometriosis and clinicians across the 
United States.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to recognize FLARE for their work bringing hope to 
endometriosis patients nationwide.
```
