# RECOGNIZING JOE HENGSTLER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING JOE HENGSTLER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Joe Hengstler 
of Arvada, Colorado, for receiving the 2026 Community Spirit Award from 
the Arvada Economic Development Association. Each year, this award 
recognizes an individual whose outstanding service to the community has 
helped define and shape Arvada's character.
  As the Executive Director of the Olde Town Arvada Business 
Improvement District from 2019 until his passing on September 23, 2025, 
Joe was the driving force behind Olde Town's revitalization and was 
affectionately known as its ``Mayor.'' Through his leadership, he 
expanded tourism, secured grants, and fostered regional partnerships 
that elevated Olde Town as a model for downtown revitalization in 
Colorado and beyond. Joe's dedication, creativity, and steady 
leadership were vital to ensuring Olde Town's vibrancy and economic 
vitality for years to come.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Joe Hengstler for receiving the 2026 
Community Spirit Award and thank him for his contributions to the 
Arvada community.

                          ____________________
```
