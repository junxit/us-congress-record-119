# RECOGNIZING SARAH KINZER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING SARAH KINZER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mountain 
Resource Center Chief Executive Officer Sarah Kinzer for her 
distinguished leadership and service to the Conifer and Foothills area.
  For over seven years, Sarah has led the Mountain Resource Center by 
strengthening and expanding the resources the organization provided 
throughout the Covid pandemic and in recent years marked by drastically 
rising costs and instability with funding sources. Under her 
leadership, the Mountain Resource Center launched the Mobile Market in 
2021 and a fully licensed, award-winning preschool in 2024. Beyond the 
crucial services Sarah has helped expand, she has also left the 
Mountain Resource Center in a more stable position with its building 
mortgage fully paid off, allowing the organization to remain active in 
Conifer for years to come.
  In an unincorporated, rural community like Conifer without local 
elected municipal officials, leaders like Sarah are even more critical 
in building community and providing services for our most vulnerable 
neighbors. The Mountain Resource Center's services, including food 
resources, case management, veteran support, and community building, 
are especially needed in rural areas with many senior residents, like 
Conifer. Sarah and the Mountain Resource Center have been an invaluable 
partner as my office has worked to engage with and serve our 
constituents ``up the hill.''
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to thank Sarah for her dedication to our community. I wish 
her the best of luck in her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
