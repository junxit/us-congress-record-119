# RECOGNIZING OLDE TOWN PICKIN' PARLOR

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                  RECOGNIZING OLDE TOWN PICKIN' PARLOR

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Olde Town 
Pickin' Parlor of Arvada, Colorado for receiving the 2026 Outstanding 
Retailer Award from the Arvada Economic Development Association. Each 
year, this award recognizes an Arvada retail business that stands apart 
in their job growth, innovation, community involvement, outreach, and 
more.
  Since its founding in 1991, Olde Town Pickin' Parlor has been one of 
the most beloved independent music shops in Colorado and a destination 
for acoustic instrument enthusiasts from across the region. For more 
than three decades, Olde Town Pickin' Parlor has welcomed musicians and 
instrument enthusiasts from across the region with exceptional service, 
expertise, and warm hospitality. Owner Kit and his knowledgeable staff 
take the time to help every customer, while the shop's luthiers have 
earned a reputation for performing remarkable work restoring and 
repairing instruments. I am grateful for their contributions to the 
Arvada music and retail community.

[[Page E1147]]

  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Olde Town Pickin' Parlor for receiving the 
2026 Outstanding Retailer Award and for their contributions to the 
Arvada community.

                          ____________________
```
