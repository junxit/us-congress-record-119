# RECOGNIZING LAURIE MIGNONE FOR HER 22 YEAR OF DISTINGUISHED PUBLIC SERVICE

**Speaking:** DeLauro, Rosa L. (D000216) [D-CT]

```
  RECOGNIZING LAURIE MIGNONE FOR HER 22 YEAR OF DISTINGUISHED PUBLIC 
                                SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROSA L. DeLAURO

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. DeLAURO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Laurie Mignone for 22 years of distinguished service to the federal 
government, including 11 years as a senior staffer on the House 
Committee on Appropriations.
  After earning a master's degree in public health from Emory 
University, Laurie began her federal career as a Presidential 
Management Fellow at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention 
(CDC), the crown jewel of the Nation's public health system. In 2006, 
she came to the Committee on Appropriations for the first time--as a 
detailee on loan from CDC. During her initial stint on the committee, 
Laurie laid out a career path that would bring her full circle, many 
years later, to return to the committee as a senior staffer in charge 
of public health policy and global health policy.
  Laurie's career exemplifies a life of public service. Over the last 
22 years, she has served in senior roles across the federal government, 
including positions at the Department of Health and Human Services, the 
Office of Management and Budget, and the National Institutes of Health. 
She was the first woman to serve as the senior budget officer at the 
National Cancer Institute--the oldest and largest institute at NIH--
where she was responsible for a budget of more than $6 billion.
  In 2012, Laurie returned full-time to the Appropriations Committee to 
serve on the staff of the Surveys & Investigations team, known as S&I, 
a nonpartisan unit responsible for conducting oversight, drafting 
investigative reports, and providing expert recommendations to the 
committee. During her time on the S&I staff, she led confidential 
program evaluations of a wide range of federal programs at the request 
of the committee's leadership.
  I am proud to say, in 2019, I brought Laurie to the subcommittee on 
Labor, HHS, and Education, where she has served with distinction for 
the past eight years. Laurie has been the driving force behind many 
achievements in public health programs during her time on the 
subcommittee--including the establishment of the public health data 
initiative in 2019 and the public health infrastructure program in 
2022. In 2020, Laurie was instrumental in developing five emergency 
supplemental appropriations packages to respond to the COVID-19 
pandemic, including billions of dollars for the development and 
distribution of the COVID-19 vaccine.
  In 2023, Laurie expanded her role on the Committee on Appropriations 
to include a staff position on the State and Foreign Operations 
subcommittee, where she has focused on improving the coordination of 
global health programs across government silos, especially in the area 
of global health security. Laurie also extended her expertise to 
improve operations at the State Department and USAID, as well as 
oversight over international financial institutions.
  I thank Laurie for her tireless public service--which often included 
long days, weekends, and holidays--as she dedicated more than 20 years 
to improving the health and well-being of millions of Americans, as 
well as the health and well-being of the most impoverished communities 
across the globe. I also thank her husband and son, Joel and Jake 
Parriott, for the sacrifices they made--those weekends, holidays, and 
family vacations--to support Laurie and her important work on behalf of 
the committee. Laurie will be sorely missed.
  I congratulate Laurie on a distinguished career in public service and 
wish her the best in her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
