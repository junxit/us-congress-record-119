# RECOGNIZING FIRST LIEUTENANT CARMEN GIFFORD

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING FIRST LIEUTENANT CARMEN GIFFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Army veteran, First 
Lieutenant Carmen Gifford of Corsicana, Texas, with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. Her dedication to our country, fellow service 
members, and community is a shining example of courage, leadership, and 
service.
  Lieutenant Gifford served in the U.S. Army from June 14, 2016 to 
October 20, 2019, as an Infantry Officer. After graduating from Officer 
Candidate School in 2016 as the Distinguished Leadership Graduate, she 
became the eighth woman to join the U.S. Army Infantry. As part of the 
Army's integration of women into Infantry units, she helped establish 
the first female-integrated infantry unit at Fort Hood with the 2nd 
Battalion, 7th Cavalry Regiment, 1st Armored Brigade Combat Team, 1st 
Cavalry Division. In 2018, she was selected as one of approximately 25 
service members from across the Armed Forces to represent the United 
States on the Joint Armed Forces Women's Rugby Team.
  Lieutenant Gifford's military honors include the Army Achievement 
Medal, Korea Defense Service Medal, Parachutist Badge, and numerous 
other awards and commendations.
  Following her honorable discharge from the Army in 2019, Lieutenant 
Gifford continued her commitment to service and leadership. She 
continues to mentor veterans and recently separated service members as 
they transition to civilian life. She also serves her community as an 
Intermediate Peace Officer and Arson Investigator and volunteers at 
community events in Wilmer and Irving. In addition, she supports 
Parkland Burn Camp, which provides free summer camp opportunities for 
children who have experienced severe burn injuries.

[[Page E1148]]

Her commitment to public service has been recognized with a Medal of 
Commendation from the Irving Fire Department.
  Lieutenant Gifford's dedication to serving her Nation and community 
exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and civic 
leadership. I commend her remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for her steadfast commitment to our Nation, her 
fellow service members, and the communities she continues to serve.
```
