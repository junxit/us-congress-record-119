# RECOGNIZING MASTER GUNNERY SERGEANT BRENT DORROUGH

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
           RECOGNIZING MASTER GUNNERY SERGEANT BRENT DORROUGH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired U.S. Marine 
Corps veteran, Master Gunnery Sergeant Brent Dorrough of Ovilla, Texas, 
with a Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to 
our country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  Throughout his 30 years of service, MGySgt Dorrough held numerous 
leadership positions before eventually retiring on August 31, 2022. He 
deployed in areas such as Iraq in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom, 
Afghanistan in support of Operation Enduring Freedom, as well as 
assignments throughout Somalia, Bosnia, Haiti, Cuba, Okinawa, Japan, 
South Korea, Norway, Hungary, Georgia, Germany, West Africa, and 
Djibouti. MGySgt Dorrough's military decorations include the Legion of 
Merit, Defense Meritorious Service Medal, and numerous other individual 
and unit awards.
  Following his retirement from the Marine Corps, MGySgt Dorrough 
continued his commitment to serving others through public service. In 
January 2024, he was appointed by 378th District Judge William Wallace 
to establish and lead the Ellis County Veterans' Treatment Court, 
building the program from the ground up and expanding it into a 
regional resource serving veterans from Ellis, Hill, Henderson, 
Johnson, and Navarro counties. His work has led to speaking 
opportunities on Veterans' Treatment Courts and services throughout 
Texas, and he was selected by the Governor's Office of Court 
Administration to serve as the state's first Veterans' Treatment Court 
Trainer. MGySgt Dorrough also serves his community as an HOA President, 
Cub Scout Den Leader, Marine Corps League member, Toys for Tots 
volunteer, Community Champion and founding member of the Unite Ellis 
Military Community Team, and board member of Unite Ellis.
  MIGySgt Dorrough's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation, our 
veterans, and our community.
```
