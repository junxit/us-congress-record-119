# RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST 5TH CLASS DAVID SALINAS

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
             RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST 5TH CLASS DAVID SALINAS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Army veteran 
Specialist 5th Class David Salinas of Ennis, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our 
country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  During his military service from 1970 to 1972, Specialist Salinas 
served with the 101st Airborne Division during the Vietnam War. After 
completing technical training as a certified and licensed aircraft 
mechanic, he began his military career in a stateside transportation 
unit, operating a variety of military vehicles. He later deployed to 
South Vietnam, where he served as the Non-Commissioned Officer in 
Charge of an aircraft electrical shop supporting a CH-47 Chinook 
assault support helicopter unit. Following his return from Vietnam, 
Specialist Salinas continued his aviation career as an aircraft 
mechanic and flight crew chief for a government contractor at the 
Nevada Test Site. He received numerous military decorations including a 
Bronze Star for Meritorious Achievement, the Vietnam Service Medal, the 
Republic of Vietnam Service Medal, the National Defense Service Medal, 
and numerous other military honors.
  Following his military service, Specialist Salinas continued his 
commitment to serving others through extensive volunteer work 
supporting veterans, children, families, and individuals in need. He 
currently serves as Vice President of the Hero Services Coalition, Vice 
Chairman of the Ellis County Veterans Celebration, and a Toys for Tots 
Committee Member. He previously served as a founding board member of 
The Heritage of Heroes and has been active with Lions Club 
International for many years, serving twice as Secretary and once as 
Program Chairman. Specialist Salinas' commitment to serving others 
through his Christian faith is reflected in his volunteer work 
supporting hospice patients and serving his church as a greeter, 
audiovisual operator, and security volunteer. He has also served as a 
Big Brothers of America mentor, providing guidance and encouragement to 
a young boy who had lost his father.
  Specialist Salinas's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation, our 
veterans, and our community.
```
