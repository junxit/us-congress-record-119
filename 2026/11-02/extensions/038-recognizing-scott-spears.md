# RECOGNIZING SCOTT SPEARS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING SCOTT SPEARS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Scott Spears 
for receiving the 2026 Entrepreneurial Spirit Award from the Arvada 
Economic Development Association. Each year, this award recognizes 
individuals who demonstrate the traits of an exemplary entrepreneur, 
including their leadership, innovation, determination, and community 
spirit.
  Scott is a born-and-raised Arvadan and the owner of five distinctive 
local businesses: Scrumptious, School House, Super Zoom Bang Bang, 
Secret Level, and Sock. What makes Scott's business model distinctive 
is his ability to identify unmet community needs and create highly 
curated, one-of-a-kind concepts to fit those needs. From specialty 
sweets and whiskey, to toys and socks, each business offers a unique 
experience rooted in the community. School House Kitchen and Libations, 
housed in Arvada's original 1882 schoolhouse, offers more than 2,000 
whiskeys, 50 beers, and Arvada's first rooftop patio. Scott's 
leadership extends beyond business, serving as Vice President of the 
Business Improvement District and serving on the Board of Directors for 
Historic Olde Town Arvada.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Scott Spears for receiving the 2026 
Entrepreneurial Spirit Award and thank him for his contributions to the 
Arvada community.

                          ____________________
```
