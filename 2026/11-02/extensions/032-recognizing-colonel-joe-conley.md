# RECOGNIZING COLONEL JOE CONLEY

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                     RECOGNIZING COLONEL JOE CONLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired Army veteran 
Colonel Joe Conley of Mansfield, Texas, with a Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our country and community is 
a shining example of honor and duty.
  Colonel Conley served 27 years, retiring in 2006. Throughout his 
distinguished career, he commanded at the company, battalion, and 
brigade levels and served at multiple locations including Camp Casey 
and Yongsan in Korea, Honduras, the Pentagon, and the Defense Contract 
Management Agency in Alexandria, Virginia. Colonel Conley led teams 
responsible for ensuring U.S. service members had access to critical 
weaponry and munitions.

[[Page E1153]]

  Colonel Conley's military honors include the Legion of Merit, 
Meritorious Service Medal, Joint Service, Commendation Medal, Army 
Commendation Medal, and the Army Achievement Medal, among numerous 
other awards and commendations.
  Following his military retirement, Colonel Conley continued his 
commitment to serving and mentoring his community. For 15 years, he 
served as the Senior Army Instructor for the JROTC program at Molina 
High School in Dallas, where he mentored and motivated students to 
become responsible and engaged citizens. He also serves as an Associate 
Minister at Bethlehem Baptist Church in Mansfield where he serves on 
the church's Veterans Ministry Board, supporting veteran seminars, food 
pantry initiatives, meet-and-greets, and veterans' trips. Additionally, 
he serves on the Mansfield Veterans Memorial and Tribute Foundation, 
supporting local veterans through programs including the Field of Flags 
and veteran benefits seminars.
  Colonel Conley's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation, our 
veterans, and the Mansfield community.

                          ____________________
```
