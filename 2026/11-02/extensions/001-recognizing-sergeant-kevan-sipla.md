# RECOGNIZING SERGEANT KEVAN SIPLA

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                    RECOGNIZING SERGEANT KEVAN SIPLA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Army veteran, 
Sergeant Kevan Sipla of Ennis, Texas, with a Congressional Veteran 
Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our country and community is 
a shining example of honor and duty.
  During his military service, Sergeant Sipla served as a Sniper 
Section Leader with Comanche Company, 1st Squadron, 2nd Cavalry 
Regiment. His assignments included Fort Lewis, Washington, Germany, and 
his deployment to Iraq, where he participated in the Battle of Sadr 
City in the spring of 2008. As a sniper, Sergeant Sipla and his team 
were recognized for their expertise in countering improvised explosive 
device emplacement teams and their extensive knowledge of the area of 
operations. During combat operations in Sadr City, his team provided 
overwatch and suppressive fire in support of U.S. Navy SEALs. Sergeant 
Sipla's military decorations include the Purple Heart, two Army 
Commendation Medals, the Army Achievement Medal, and numerous other 
military honors and decorations.
  Following his military service, he continued his commitment to 
serving others through leadership, financial mentorship, and efforts to 
combat food insecurity throughout Ellis County and surrounding 
communities. Under his leadership as Lead at Manna House Outreach in 
Midlothian, food waste decreased from approximately 50 percent to 2 
percent, while the number of individuals served increased from just 
over 500 to more than 1,000 per month. These efforts helped Manna House 
become one of the top five food pantries among approximately 500 
supported by the North Texas Food Bank. Sergeant Sipla also serves as a 
financial coach, helping individuals develop budgets, reduce debt, and 
build long-term financial stability. His community outreach extends 
through partnerships with organizations across the region, including 
The Heights, Cares Services in Waxahachie, North Ellis County Outreach, 
and the Midlothian Senior Center, among others
  Sergeant Sipla's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation, our 
veterans, and families throughout Ellis County and beyond.
```
