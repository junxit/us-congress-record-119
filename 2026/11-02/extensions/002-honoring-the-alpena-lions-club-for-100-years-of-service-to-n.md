# HONORING THE ALPENA LIONS CLUB FOR 100 YEARS OF SERVICE TO NORTHERN MICHIGAN AND THE WORLD

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
  HONORING THE ALPENA LIONS CLUB FOR 100 YEARS OF SERVICE TO NORTHERN 
                         MICHIGAN AND THE WORLD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize the Alpena 
Lions Club as they celebrate 100 years of dedicated service to the 
community of Alpena, Michigan and the humanitarian mission of Lions 
International.
  The Alpena Lions Club's story is one of a community that has chosen, 
decade after decade, to show up for its neighbors. Since 1926, 
generations of volunteers have dedicated their time and effort to the 
club's mission of serving their community, meeting humanitarian needs, 
encouraging peace, and promoting international understanding. As a 
proud affiliate of Lions Club International, the global leader in 
community and humanitarian service, the Alpena Lions Club has 
demonstrated that the spirit of service knows no borders.
  Currently led by President Audra Smith, the club has stayed true to 
their mission across all 100 years, working every day to improve the 
lives of Northern Michiganders. By providing free health screenings, 
organizing food drives, supporting the visually impaired, assisting 
families affected by childhood illnesses, and advocating for the 
responsible stewardship of our natural resources, the club embodies the 
Northern Michigan values of hard work, generosity, and looking out for 
your fellow man. From small acts of kindness to providing support in 
areas of disaster, the Alpena Lions Club is living proof that ordinary 
people can do extraordinary things, and that even one person, answering 
the call to serve, can make a difference.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the Alpena Lions Club for a 
century of dedicated service to Alpena, to the country, and to the 
world. On behalf of Michigan's 1st Congressional District, I thank 
President Smith and all the club's members for their tireless efforts, 
and wish them continued success in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
