# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN JOE HILL

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                      RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN JOE HILL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired U.S. Navy 
veteran Captain Joe Hill of Corsicana, Texas, with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our country and 
community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  Throughout his 35 years of military service, Captain Hill served at 
installations and aboard ships throughout the United States and abroad 
including the USS Blue, USS Gallatin, and USS Pyro. He completed two 
tours in Vietnam and later served during Operation Desert Storm before 
retiring in February 1992. His military decorations include the Navy 
Meritorious Service Medal, Navy and Marine Corps Commendation Medal, 
the Battle Efficiency Ribbon and numerous other awards, medals and 
commendations.
  Following his retirement from the Navy, Captain Hill continued his 
commitment to public service through federal, state, and community 
leadership. As a FEMA Intergovernmental Affairs Officer, he helped 
communities recover from Hurricanes Katrina, Rita, Gustav, and Ike by 
coordinating and tracking the restoration of critical public 
facilities, including schools, hospitals, and first responder stations. 
He later served as a Field Representative for Congressman Charles 
Boustany of Louisiana and on the Service Academy Selection Boards for 
Senator David Vitter and Congressman Boustany.
  Captain Hill has dedicated decades to community service, particularly 
in support of veterans, youth, and education. He served as Vice Chair 
and a founding board member of the Louisiana Military Hall of Fame and 
Museum, he founded the Southwest Louisiana Literacy Coalition, 
established the first Junior Navy ROTC program at LaGrange High School 
in Lake Charles, and co-founded the Wise County Veterans Memorial Park 
and United Way of Wise County. His contributions have been recognized 
with the National Daughters of the American Revolution Founders Medal 
for Patriotism and Wise County Citizen of the Year. He currently serves 
as Mayor of Corsicana, continuing his lifelong commitment to public 
service.
  Captain Hill's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation and our 
community.
```
