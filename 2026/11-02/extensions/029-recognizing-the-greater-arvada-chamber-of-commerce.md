# RECOGNIZING THE GREATER ARVADA CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
           RECOGNIZING THE GREATER ARVADA CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Greater 
Arvada Chamber of Commerce for receiving the 2026 Community Partnership 
Award from the Arvada Economic Development Association. Each year, this 
award recognizes an organization that supports economic development 
efforts and community outreach.
  The Greater Arvada Chamber has strengthened the regional business 
environment in recent years through B.O.L.D. 2026, promoting economic 
resiliency through talent, housing, childcare, advocacy, and 
entrepreneurship. In 2024, it launched the Business Resource Center, 
which serves as a hub for supporting training, collaboration, and 
entrepreneurship. The Chamber has invested in workforce development by 
supporting Career Hubs in schools and partnering with education and 
workforce leaders. The Chamber has also raised more than $22,000 for 
the ``No Neighbor Hungry'' campaign and continues to promote civic 
engagement and community dialogue through public forums.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate the Greater Arvada Chamber of Commerce for 
receiving the 2026 Community Partnership Award and thank them for their 
contributions to the Arvada community.
```
