# RECOGNIZING THE QUARRYVILLE LIONS CLUB FOR A CENTURY OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE QUARRYVILLE LIONS CLUB FOR A CENTURY OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize the 
Quarryville Lions Club for one hundred years of service to the local 
community.
  The Quarryville Lions Club was chartered on September 10, 1926 with 
twenty-six members. Since its establishment, the club has taken the 
Lions Clubs International motto of ``We Serve'' to heart. Its members 
organize veteran recognition efforts, run community events like the 
Huffnagle Park concert series, help those who are visually impaired, 
and support local organizations such as food banks.
  The Quarryville Lions Club has also played a key role in promoting 
the Lions across the county. Having been sponsored by the Lancaster 
Lions Club when it was founded, Quarryville has sponsored or 
cosponsored five other nearby Lions clubs.
  The Quarryville Lions Club is one of 49,000 clubs across 200 
countries and regions, and Lions Clubs International boasts 1.4 million 
members.
  As the Quarryville Lions Club celebrates this significant milestone, 
I thank the club and its members for their positive impact. Their 
contributions have made both Quarryville and Pennsylvania's 11th 
Congressional District a better place, and I wish them another one 
hundred years of success.
```
