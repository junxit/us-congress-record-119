# HONORING GARY KAI'S SERVICE TO HAWAII'S BUSINESS COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Tokuda, Jill N. (T000487) [D-HI]

```
       HONORING GARY KAI'S SERVICE TO HAWAII'S BUSINESS COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JILL N. TOKUDA

                               of hawaii

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. TOKUDA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Gary Kai as he concludes his 17 years of dedicated service as Executive 
Director of the Hawaii Business Roundtable. His departure marks an 
important milestone for the Roundtable and offers an opportunity to 
recognize a career defined by leadership, service, and a lasting 
commitment to Hawaii's economic and community well-being. Since joining 
the Hawaii Business Roundtable in 2009, Gary has helped bring business 
leaders together around a shared vision for a stronger, more resilient, 
and more prosperous Hawaii. Through his leadership, he has encouraged 
thoughtful dialogue and built bridges, realizing that the challenges 
facing our state require a willingness to work together and create 
opportunities for generations to come. His contributions have 
strengthened the Roundtable and reinforced the important role Hawaii's 
business community plays in the well-being of our state.
  Gary's impact on Hawaii, however, extends well beyond his years at 
the Business Roundtable. Before taking on this role, he built a 
distinguished career in banking, finance, and real estate development 
serving as Vice President of Towne Development of Hawaii Inc., General 
Manager of HomeStreet Bank, Senior Vice President at First Hawaiian 
Bank, and President and Chief Operating Officer of Finance Factors Ltd. 
Across these positions, Gary developed a deep understanding of Hawaii's 
economy, its local businesses, and the people whose livelihoods depend 
on them. His experience and leadership have made him a trusted voice in 
Hawaii's business community and a valued contributor to the state's 
economic progress. Beyond Gary's professional accomplishments, his 
legacy also includes the relationships he has built, the colleagues he 
has supported, and the spirit of collaboration he has encouraged 
throughout his career. His contributions reflect a deep appreciation 
for the people and communities that make Hawaii unique and a belief 
that our state's long-term success depends on investing in both its 
economic vitality and the well-being of its people.
  As Gary concludes his tenure with the Roundtable, his contributions 
will continue to be felt through the work of the organization, the 
relationships he has fostered, and the broader businesses community he 
has helped strengthen. His legacy is one of meaningful service and a 
sustained commitment to a better future for Hawaii.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Gary Kai for his 17 
years of dedicated leadership and service to the Hawaii Business 
Roundtable and for the broader contributions he has made throughout his 
distinguished career. He has our sincere gratitude for his service to 
Hawaii and our aloha, for continued success, happiness, and fulfillment 
in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
