# RECOGNIZING ELECTRICIAN'S MATE FIRST CLASS (SS) JOHN WALKER

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
      RECOGNIZING ELECTRICIAN'S MATE FIRST CLASS (SS) JOHN WALKER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Navy veteran, 
Electrician's Mate First Class (SS) John Walker of Corsicana, Texas, 
with a Congressional Veteran Commendation. His technical expertise, 
dedication to his fellow service members, and continued commitment to 
educating and strengthening his community are a shining example of 
honor and service.
  EM1(SS) Walker served in the U.S. Navy from November 1994 to December 
2004, including aboard the USS Jacksonville (SSN-699) and at Pearl 
Harbor Naval Shipyard and Intermediate Maintenance Facility. While 
aboard the USS Jacksonville, he was recognized for his technical 
expertise and played a critical role in completing emergent repairs 
that allowed the submarine to remain on station and fulfill its 
operational commitments. At Pearl Harbor Naval Shipyard, he helped 
transform the Sailor training and certification program, reducing 
training time from more than three years to approximately six to nine 
months. He also contributed to engineering improvements that 
strengthened the shipyard's ability to support nuclear-powered vessels. 
His military honors include Navy Achievement Medals, the Battle ``E'' 
Ribbon, Good Conduct Medals, National Defense Service Medals, Sea 
Service Deployment Ribbons, and numerous other awards and 
commendations.
  Following his military service, EM1(SS) Walker continued serving his 
community through education and workforce development. At Navarro 
College, he taught Petroleum Technology and Industrial Maintenance, 
helping more than 200 students develop skills for careers in the 
skilled trades. He later served as the Corsicana Center Manager for 
Workforce Solutions of North Central Texas, where he helped individuals 
overcome employment barriers and return to the workforce. Under his 
leadership, the Corsicana Center was recognized as the North Central 
Texas Workforce Solutions Center of the Year.

[[Page E1149]]

  EM1(SS) Walker's dedication to serving his Nation and community 
exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and civic 
leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation and the 
communities he continues to serve.
```
