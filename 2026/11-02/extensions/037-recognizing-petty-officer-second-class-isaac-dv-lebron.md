# RECOGNIZING PETTY OFFICER SECOND CLASS ISAAC "DV" LEBRON

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING PETTY OFFICER SECOND CLASS ISAAC ``DV'' LEBRON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Navy veteran Petty 
Officer Second Class Isaac ``DV'' Lebron of Waxahachie, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our 
country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  During his first eight years of active-duty service in the U.S. Navy, 
Petty Officer Lebron served aboard the USS Peleliu and with Inshore 
Boat Unit 16 in Kuwait. He supported operations in the Global War on 
Terrorism, including Operation Enduring Freedom and Operation Iraqi 
Freedom. Following his initial active-duty service, he continued 
serving in the Navy Reserve and was recalled to active duty in support 
of these operations. Petty Officer Lebron's military decorations 
include the Navy Unit Commendation, Navy ``E'' Ribbon, Armed Forces 
Expeditionary Medal, Sea Service Deployment Ribbons, Humanitarian 
Service Medal, and numerous other awards and decorations.
  Following his military service, Petty Officer Lebron continued his 
commitment to serving others through veteran advocacy, mentorship, and 
community outreach throughout Ellis County. After years of advocating 
for the establishment of the Ellis County Veterans' Treatment Court, he 
now serves as a volunteer mentor, helping fellow veterans navigate 
challenges, access VA resources, and find support and encouragement. 
For approximately six years, he has hosted The Ellis Talker, a podcast 
highlighting local leaders, organizations, and issues affecting the 
community, including veterans' well-being, health and wellness, and 
civic engagement. Petty Officer Lebron has also volunteered at 
Stonegate Church in Midlothian for more than eight years, serving on 
the Coffee Team making his ``combat brew'' for the congregation, 
assisting with special events, praying with and encouraging others, and 
helping people in need. Beyond his formal volunteer commitments, he has 
personally assisted veterans and neighbors by providing groceries, 
essential supplies, disability resources and other needs. In 
recognition of his civic engagement and community service, the City of 
Waxahachie named him an Honorary City Council Member in June 2025.
  Petty Officer Lebron's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation 
and community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service 
and civic leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend 
my sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation, our 
veterans, and the Ellis County community.
```
