# RECOGNIZING ELISAIA SUALUA MA'ILO

**Speaking:** Radewagen, Aumua Amata Coleman (R000600) [R-AS]

```
                   RECOGNIZING ELISAIA SUALUA MA'ILO

                                 ______
                                 

                   HON. AUMUA AMATA COLEMAN RADEWAGEN

                           of american samoa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mrs. RADEWAGEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 2026 
American Samoa Territorial Teacher of the Year, Elisaia Sualua Ma'ilo. 
Elisaia Sualua Ma'ilo teaches 11th grade United States history and 
Advanced Placement (AP) United States history at Tafuna High School in 
Pago Pago, American Samoa. With the ethos that ``we rise by lifting 
others.'' Elisaia has worked to make history meaningful and accessible 
to his students while emphasizing service, leadership, and cultural 
identity. That dedication to serving and uplifting his community has 
extended beyond the classroom.
  He founded the Adopt-A-Student Academic Intervention Program, an 
initiative designed to support students who were failing multiple 
courses through teacher mentoring, tutoring, parent engagement, and 
academic intervention. The program helped students successfully recover 
failed courses and was subsequently adopted by the American Samoa 
Department of Education as a Credit Recovery Program across the 
island's public high schools. He served in professional leadership 
roles, including PLC leadership and school improvement efforts, while 
advocating for literacy, student achievement, teacher collaboration, 
and equitable educational opportunities for our students. He has been a 
steering committee member for our Warrior Literacy Program at THS and 
is currently part of the leadership team as program coordinator. One of 
Elisaia's stated goals is to perpetuate the Samoan culture through the 
arts. He is a music festival coach, speech festival coach and National 
History Day coach at Tafuna, where he also has led the Professional 
Learning Community Team for the past four years. He volunteers with the 
Moso'oi Festival of Arts and sings in the American Samoa Arts Council 
Choir. He is also a member of the youth group at Our Lady of the Rosary 
Church, which helps the community through cleanups and by donating to 
local charities. Elisaia was officially recognized by our local leaders 
including the American Samoan Fono. This serves as an affirmation of 
the importance of the educators who dedicate their lives to the 
children and future of American Samoa.
  I hope that this recognition serves as inspiration for aspiring 
teachers and a reminder that the work of an educator is ultimately an 
investment in the future of our people.
```
