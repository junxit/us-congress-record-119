# CONGRATULATING BRYN KROSSE ON WINNING THE PENNSYLVANIA GIRLS' GOLF STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
  CONGRATULATING BRYN KROSSE ON WINNING THE PENNSYLVANIA GIRLS' GOLF 
                           STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Bryn Krosse of 
York, Pennsylvania, and congratulate her on winning the Pennsylvania 
Interscholastic Athletic Association (PIAA) Class 3A Girls' Golf State 
Championship.
  The Dallastown Area High School freshman capped off an outstanding 
season with her victory at the PIAA Class 3A State Championship in 
State College. Bryn completed an impressive sweep of titles this 
season, winning league and district championships before claiming the 
state title. With her victory, she became York County's first PIAA golf 
state champion in seven years.
  Despite being just a freshman, Bryn has spent much of her life around 
the game of golf. Inspired by her older brothers, who are also golfers, 
she developed a passion for the sport at a young age. Bryn credits 
their guidance and encouragement with helping her develop the 
determination and mental toughness needed to succeed on the golf 
course.
  Bryn's accomplishments extend well beyond high school competition. 
She regularly competes in junior golf tournaments across the country, 
gaining valuable experience against some of the nation's top young 
golfers. This past summer, she won the American Junior Golf 
Association's Junior Chicago All-Star Championship in Aurora, Illinois, 
adding another impressive achievement to her growing list of 
accomplishments.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Pennsylvania's 11th Congressional District, 
I congratulate Bryn on this remarkable achievement. Her state 
championship victory is a testament to her hard work, dedication, and 
commitment to excellence. I wish her continued success as she pursues 
her passion for golf and look forward to seeing all that she 
accomplishes in the years ahead.
```
