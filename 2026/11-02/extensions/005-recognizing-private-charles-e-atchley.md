# RECOGNIZING PRIVATE CHARLES E. ATCHLEY

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                 RECOGNIZING PRIVATE CHARLES E. ATCHLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Army veteran, 
Private Charles E. Atchley of Waxahachie, Texas with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our country and 
community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  Private Atchley volunteered for military service the day he turned 
18, reporting to the draft office in Lancaster, Texas. He served in the 
U.S. Army with Company A, 290th Regiment 75th Infantry Division during 
WWII. During his service, Private Atchley deployed to Europe and 
participated in the Battle of the Bulge and the Colmar Pocket campaign. 
Charles experienced combat for the first time during the German attack 
on December 16, 1944. Serving in severe winter conditions, he and his 
fellow soldiers held their position, helped drive back enemy forces, 
and supported the Allied advance toward the Rhine. After sustaining 
severe frostbite, Private Atchley was hospitalized in Marseille, 
France, and later received an honorary medical discharge.
  Following his honorable discharge, he continued his commitment to 
serving and supporting the veteran community. Private Atchley remained 
actively involved with the 75th Infantry Division Association, 
regularly attending reunions and maintaining lasting connections with 
fellow veterans. He also continues to participate in veteran events 
throughout Ellis County, helping preserve the history, memories, and 
legacy of those who served alongside him during World War II.
  Private Atchley's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of service and sacrifice. 
I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my sincere 
appreciation for his steadfast commitment to honoring and supporting 
our Nation's service members and veterans.
```
