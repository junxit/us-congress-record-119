# RECOGNIZING PEYTON WEBB

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING PEYTON WEBB

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Peyton Webb for 
earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Peyton has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Peyton, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Peyton's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Wheat Ridge High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Peyton Webb on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
