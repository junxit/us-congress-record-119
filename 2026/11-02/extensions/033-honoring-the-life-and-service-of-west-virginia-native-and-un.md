# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF WEST VIRGINIA NATIVE AND UNITED STATES ARMY VETERAN CORPORAL CLOVIS EUGENE FERGUSON

**Speaking:** Moore, Riley M. (M001235) [R-WV]

```
HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF WEST VIRGINIA NATIVE AND UNITED STATES 
              ARMY VETERAN CORPORAL CLOVIS EUGENE FERGUSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RILEY M. MOORE

                            of west virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. MOORE of West Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and service of Corporal Clovis Eugene Ferguson, a United States 
Army Veteran and beloved member of the Salt Rock Elementary community.
  Born and raised in West Virginia, Cpl. Ferguson joined the United 
States Army to serve his country during the Vietnam War soon after 
graduating from Barboursville High School. After his military service, 
Cpl. Ferguson and his wife, Connie, settled in Salt Rock, West 
Virginia, where he was a career member of the Plumbers and Pipefitters 
Local 521.
  Together, they had two children, Amy and Jason, were respected 
members of their local church, and generous neighbors to many in the 
Salt Rock community. Cpl. Ferguson coached basketball, softball, 
baseball, and soccer. He was especially dedicated to the children and 
faculty of Salt Rock Elementary, where he shared joy with the students 
through his annual tradition of dressing up as Elvis for Halloween and 
Santa Claus for Christmas. He also dedicated countless hours to sharing 
his knowledge and practical wisdom with students.
  The principal of Salt Rock Elementary School wrote that Cpl. Ferguson 
was ``especially proud of his service to the United States of America'' 
and that his love for our great Nation was evident in the way he lived. 
He will be remembered as a familiar face, trusted friend, and source of 
joy, and his legacy will continue to live on through his family and the 
students whom he loved and mentored.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that Cpl. Ferguson and his contributions to our 
Nation and his communities be honored for his service.
```
