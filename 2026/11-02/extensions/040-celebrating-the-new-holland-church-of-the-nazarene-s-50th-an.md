# CELEBRATING THE NEW HOLLAND CHURCH OF THE NAZARENE'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
 CELEBRATING THE NEW HOLLAND CHURCH OF THE NAZARENE'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate and recognize New 
Holland Church of the Nazarene for 50 years of faith, love, and service 
to the community.
  New Holland Church of the Nazarene was founded on July 18, 1976, as a 
church plant of the Ephrata Church of the Nazarene. The new 
congregation included 17 charter members and 7 children, including 
Reverend Kenneth Mingledorff and his wife, Jan. Since construction of 
the new church had not yet begun, the congregation met at the local Odd 
Fellows Hall until the building was finished the following year. The 
new building brought both the New Holland congregation and KinderHaus 
Daycare, now KinderHaus Child Care Learning Center, under one roof. 
KinderHaus was established by the Ephrata Church of the Nazarene in 
1975, and Jan Mingledorff took over as director of the daycare when the 
New Holland congregation was founded a year later.
  In the past 50 years, New Holland Church of the Nazarene has become 
central to the local community. The church itself is a testament to 
this, as the congregation has hundreds of members and the building has 
gone through three different construction phases. In tandem with 
KinderHaus, the church has provided a half-century of high-quality 
childcare. New Holland Church of the Nazarene also hosts its own 
basketball and cheer programs, assists with National Day of Prayer 
events, and partners with local nonprofits for food and clothing 
distribution.
  I congratulate New Holland Church of the Nazarene on its 50th 
anniversary. I wish the congregation continued success in serving the 
community and look forward to the next 50 years.
```
