# RECOGNIZING LIEUTENANT COLONEL CURTIS CHASTAIN

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             RECOGNIZING LIEUTENANT COLONEL CURTIS CHASTAIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th District of Texas, the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to Lieutenant 
Colonel Curtis Chastain, United States Air Force, of Fort Worth, Texas, 
for his honorable service in the Armed Forces of the United States of 
America and for his continued service to his community.
  Lieutenant Colonel Chastain served with distinction in the United 
States Air Force for more than three decades, including active duty, 
Reserve, and Air National Guard service. Following in the footsteps of 
his late father, a fighter test pilot and astronaut who died in an 
aviation accident when Curtis was nine, he became a C-141 Starlifter 
transport and instructor pilot. He supported operations during Desert 
Storm, conflicts in Panama and Bosnia, operations in Somalia and 
Afghanistan, and multiple classified missions. He also served with the 
238th Air Support Operations Squadron, contributing to specialized air-
ground operations and mission readiness. After initially retiring in 
2006, he returned to the Mississippi Air National Guard and served 
until October 2018.
  His numerous decorations include the Meritorious Service Medal, 
Aerial Achievement Medal, two Air Force Commendation Medals, Air Force 
Achievement Medal, three Armed Forces Expeditionary Medals, National 
Defense Service Medal with one bronze service star, Southwest Asia 
Service Medal with three bronze service stars, Antarctica Service 
Medal, and Kuwait Liberation Medal.
  Throughout his military career and beyond, Lieutenant Colonel 
Chastain remained committed to aviation and education. As an Air Force 
Academy Liaison Officer for ten years, he mentored aspiring cadets and 
helped numerous students earn appointments to the Academy. He also 
guided future aviators as an international airline captain, test pilot, 
and flight instructor.
  His dedication to young people extends throughout North Texas. He 
served as Vice President of the Northwest Independent School District 
PTA, a substitute teacher, and Chairman of its Watch D.O.G.S. program. 
He has volunteered with Pilots for Kids, supporting hospitalized 
children, and the Big Brothers Association; taught firearms and hunter 
safety; and supported wildlife conservation through the Texas Bighorn 
Society.
  Lieutenant Colonel Chastain's distinguished military career and 
enduring commitment to mentorship, education, and community service 
reflect the highest traditions of the United States Air Force and bring 
great credit upon himself, the State of Texas, and the United States of 
America.
```
