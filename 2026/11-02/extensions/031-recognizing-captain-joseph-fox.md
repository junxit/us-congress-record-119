# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN JOSEPH FOX

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                     RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN JOSEPH FOX

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th District of Texas, the 2026 
Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to Captain 
Joseph Fox, United States Army, of Dallas, Texas, for his honorable 
service in the Armed Forces of the United States of America and for his 
continued service to his community.
  Captain Fox graduated from the United States Military Academy at West 
Point in 1974 and served honorably in the United States Army for five 
years as a Field Artillery Officer. Throughout his military career, he 
held numerous leadership positions, including Company Executive 
Officer, Assistant Brigade Personnel Officer, Company Commander, and 
Aide-de-Camp to both the Deputy Commanding General and Commanding 
General at Fort Dix, New Jersey. His exemplary performance and 
dedication to duty earned him the National Defense Service Medal and 
the Meritorious Service Medal.
  Following his military service, Captain Fox built a distinguished 
career in labor relations, human resources, and employment law, serving 
in leadership positions with major corporations and law firms. For more 
than two decades, he has served as Vice President and Chief Counsel of 
Global Labor and Employment Law for Celanese Corporation. Alongside his 
professional accomplishments, he has remained deeply committed to 
supporting veterans and military families, leading the company's 
Veterans Employee Resource Group and subsequently serving as its Dallas 
Site sponsor. Through these efforts, he has coordinated charitable 
support for numerous veteran-focused organizations, including Defenders 
of Freedom, the Boots to Heels program, and Paws of Honor.
  Captain Fox's dedication to those who serve extends throughout the 
North Texas community. For more than fifteen years, he has been an 
active member of the Rockwall Band of Brothers and Sisters, supporting 
initiatives such as Carry The Load and the Special Operations Warrior 
Foundation. He has also served on Congressional Service Academy 
Nomination Boards for three Members of Congress, helping identify and 
encourage the next generation of military leaders. Through his 
mentorship of students pursuing ROTC, direct commissioning, and 
appointments to the United States Service Academies, he continues to 
instill the principles of duty, honor, and service that have guided his 
own life.
  Captain Fox's distinguished military service and enduring commitment 
to veterans, military families, and future generations reflect the 
highest traditions of the United State Army and bring great credit upon 
himself, the State of Texas, and the United States of America.
```
