# RECOGNIZING ALEX ADKISSON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING ALEX ADKISSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Alex Adkisson, 
a teacher at Green Mountain High School from Lakewood, Colorado, for 
being named a finalist for 2027 Colorado Teacher of the Year by the 
Colorado Department of Education.
  In our community, Mr. Adkisson has a reputation for going above and 
beyond to engage his students in mathematics, construction, 
engineering, and business. He has long fostered a learning environment 
where students can apply what they learn to real-world situations, 
develop practical skills, and explore new possibilities for their 
future.
  Mr. Adkisson was previously recognized for his outstanding teaching 
with the 2023 Harbor Freight Tools for Schools $100,000 Teaching Grand 
Prize. Since then, Mr. Adkisson has continued to excel and his teaching 
style continues to inspire fellow educators, students, and the 
community alike.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, I 
am honored to recognize Alex Adkisson for his service to his students 
and community.

                          ____________________
```
