# HONORING GORAN RANNEFORS

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
                        HONORING GORAN RANNEFORS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 2, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize former Swedish 
diplomat and business leader Goran Rannefors on the occasion of his 
retirement and to commend his decades of service to Sweden, the United 
States, and the international community.
  As Co-Chairs of the House Friends of Sweden Caucus, it is an honor to 
recognize Mr. Rannefors for his contributions to strengthening the 
longstanding friendship between our two nations and advancing the value 
of educational and cultural exchange.
  Following his service in the Swedish Armed Forces, Mr. Rannefors held 
leadership positions in Sweden's private sector before being appointed 
Sweden's Trade Commissioner to the United States in 1986. He served in 
that position for nearly a decade, helping strengthen economic ties 
between Sweden and America. In 1994, Mr. Rannefors joined Education 
First (EF), beginning more than three decades of leadership with an 
organization dedicated to international education and cultural 
exchange. During his tenure, EF expanded its reach around the world, 
creating opportunities for students to travel, study, learn English, 
and experience different cultures. His leadership also helped establish 
Hult International Business School and expand EF's international 
educational programs, including its au pair program.
  Over the course of his career with EF, Mr. Rannefors helped create 
opportunities for more than one million students around the world. He 
has also continued his commitment to service through the Cultural Care 
Kids First Foundation, which supports children in need around the 
globe.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in thanking Mr. Rannefors for his many 
years of service and wish him and his family the very best in 
retirement. His work has benefited countless Swedish and American 
families and helped demonstrate the enduring value of friendship, 
education, and cultural exchange.
```
