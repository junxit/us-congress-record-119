# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Grassley).
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, November 2, 2026.
     To the Senate:
  Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the Standing Rules of 
the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Mike Lee, a Senator from the 
State of Utah, to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Chuck Grassley,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. LEE thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```
