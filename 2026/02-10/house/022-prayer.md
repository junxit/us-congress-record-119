# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy Lord, make our love increase. Reach into our hearts and minds 
and inspire us to let go of our animosity and disdain for the people 
and situations that disturb us. Replace our distrustful attitudes with 
a growing reliance on You, and deepen our faith that we would believe 
Your hand is at work in our lives and in every soul and every 
circumstance. Then, as we find ourselves growing in care and concern, 
may we overflow in our desire to love and then find within ourselves 
the willingness to love others even as You have loved us.
  Strengthen our hearts as well. For as motivated as we may be one day 
to do the right thing, to stay on the right path, to think and speak in 
the way You would have us do, it doesn't take but one irritating 
encounter, one misunderstood comment, one difficult morning to find 
ourselves out of sync with Your desire for the day and for our lives.
  Infuse us this day with strength and purity that we may appear 
blameless in Your sight, confident in Your mercy, and upheld by Your 
steadfast love.
  In Your strong and gracious name we pray.
  Amen.
```
