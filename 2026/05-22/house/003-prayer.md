# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy and eternal Lord, before the mountains were born or You brought 
forth the whole world, from everlasting to everlasting, You are God. 
You are the one who holds our past, our present, and our future in the 
embrace of Your eternal arms.
  On this Memorial Day weekend, may this certainty bring comfort to all 
who grieve loved ones lost to the ravages of war and to the sacrifices 
required in defense of this country.
  We pray Your blessing on the memories of those--many without fuss or 
fanfare--who have stood in the gap between the things we value most: 
freedom, peace, family, and nation, and those forces that threaten 
their safety and security.
  Freedom is not free, and these men and women, who have worn the cloth 
of our Nation in every clime and place, paid the ultimate price. May 
Your presence, even in the shadow of their death, have sustained them 
and granted them peace to the last.
  May their example of selfless service for the greater good of this 
country inspire in each of us a deeper gratitude, a sincere humility, 
and a renewed commitment to You and to these United States.
  It is in Your strong and sacred name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
