# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9011

**Measures:** H.R. 9011

```
            By Mr. BELL:
        H.R. 9011.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States, including the authority to dispose of and regulate 
     Federal property under the Property Clause (Article IV 
     Section 3, Clause 2), and the authority to provide for the 
     general welfare of the United States.
```
