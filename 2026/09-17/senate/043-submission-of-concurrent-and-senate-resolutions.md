# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 862, S.Res. 863, S.Res. 864, S.Res. 865, S.Res. 866, S.Res. 867, S.Res. 868, S.Res. 869

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mrs. BLACKBURN (for herself, Mr. Hagerty, Ms. Cortez 
             Masto, Ms. Smith, Ms. Collins, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. 
             Hickenlooper, Mr. Lujan, Mr. Heinrich, Ms. Lummis, 
             Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Justice, Mr. Blumenthal, Mrs. 
             Hyde-Smith, Mrs. Gillibrand, Mr. Schiff, and Mr. 
             Ricketts):
       S. Res. 862. A resolution honoring the life and legacy of 
     Dolly Parton, a daughter of Tennessee whose extraordinary 
     contributions to music, philanthropy, literacy, education, 
     health, conservation, and culture enriched the lives of 
     countless Americans; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. REED (for himself, Ms. Collins, Mr. King, Mr. 
             Whitehouse, Mr. Blumenthal, and Mr. Durbin):
       S. Res. 863. A resolution expressing support for 
     designation of the week of September 13 through 19, 2026, as 
     ``National Adult Education and Family Literacy Week''; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. WHITEHOUSE (for himself, Mr. Cassidy, Ms. 
             Alsobrooks, Ms. Baldwin, Mr. Blumenthal, Ms. Blunt 
             Rochester, Mr. Booker, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Coons, Ms. 
             Hassan, Ms. Hirono, Mr. Kaine, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Kim, 
             Mr. King, Mr. Markey, Mr. Merkley, Mr. Murphy, Mrs. 
             Murray, Mr. Padilla, Mr. Reed, Mr. Schatz, Mr. 
             Schiff, Mrs. Shaheen, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Warner, Ms. 
             Warren, Mr. Wicker, and Mr. Wyden):
       S. Res. 864. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of the week of September 19 through September 26, 
     2026, as ``National Estuaries Week''; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. MARKEY (for himself, Mr. Budd, Mr. Schiff, and 
             Mr. Curtis):
       S. Res. 865. A resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Senate that the People's Republic of China has engaged in the 
     arbitrary or wrongful detention of American academics and has 
     otherwise harmed the prospects for scientific collaboration 
     and people-to-people exchanges between the United States and 
     the People's Republic of China; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.
           By Mr. RICKETTS (for himself and Mr. Schiff):
       S. Res. 866. A resolution supporting the designation of 
     October 2026 as ``Substance Use & Misuse Prevention Month'' 
     to raise awareness of substance use and misuse in the United 
     States; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
           By Mr. HUSTED (for himself and Mr. Lujan):
       S. Res. 867. A resolution designating the week of September 
     14 through September 20, 2026, as ``Interscholastic Athletic 
     Administration Week''; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. MERKLEY (for himself, Ms. Baldwin, Mr. 
             Blumenthal, Mr. Booker, Mr. Coons, Ms. Duckworth, Mr. 
             Fetterman, Mrs. Gillibrand, Ms. Hirono, Mr. Kaine, 
             Mr. Markey, Mrs. Murray, Mr. Padilla, Mr. Sanders, 
             Mr. Schatz, Mr. Schiff, Ms. Smith, Ms. Warren, Mr. 
             Whitehouse, and Mr. Wyden):
       S. Res. 868. A resolution recognizing September 20, 2026, 
     as ``National LGBTQ+ Servicemembers and Veterans Day''; to 
     the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Mrs. MOODY:
       S. Res. 869. A resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Senate that the third Friday of September should be 
     recognized as ``National POW/MIA Recognition Day''; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
```
