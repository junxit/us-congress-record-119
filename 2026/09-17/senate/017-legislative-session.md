# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 4668

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I move to proceed to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.


                           Amendment No. 6776

  (Purpose: In the nature of a substitute.)
  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I call up Cruz-Cantwell substitute 
amendment No. 6776.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       The Senator from South Dakota [Mr. Thune], for Mr. Cruz, 
     proposes an amendment numbered 6776.

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask the reading be dispensed with.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  (The amendment is printed in today's Record under ``Text of 
Amendments.'')
  Mr. THUNE. I ask for the yeas and nays on my amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays are ordered.


                Amendment No. 6777 to Amendment No. 6776

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I have an amendment at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       The Senator from South Dakota [Mr. Thune] proposes an 
     amendment numbered 6777 to amendment No. 6776.

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the reading 
of the amendment be dispensed with.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment is as follows:

                     (Purpose: To improve the bill)

       At the end add the following.
       ``This Act shall take effect 1 day after the date of 
     enactment.''
  Mr. THUNE. I ask for the yeas and nays on my amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays are ordered.


                Amendment No. 6778 to Amendment No. 6777

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I have a second-degree amendment at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       The Senator from South Dakota [Mr. Thune] proposes an 
     amendment numbered 6778 to amendment No. 6777.

  Mr. THUNE. I ask unanimous consent that the reading of the amendment 
be dispensed with.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment is as follows:

                     (Purpose: To improve the bill)

       Strike ``1 day'' and insert ``2 days''.

                     (Purpose: To improve the bill)

       At the end add the following.
       ``This Act shall take effect 3 days after the date of 
     enactment.''
  Mr. THUNE. I ask for the yeas and nays on the amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:
```
