# MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITION

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 213

```
                      MEASURES DISCHARGED PETITION

                          (September 17, 2026)

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with chapter 8 
     of title 5, United States Code, hereby direct that the Senate 
     Committee on Judiciary be discharged of further consideration 
     of S.J. Res. 213, a resolution on providing for congressional 
     disapproval of the rule submitted by the U.S. Immigration and 
     Customs Enforcement of the Department of Homeland Security 
     relating to ``Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission 
     and an Extension of Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic 
     Students, Exchange Visitors, and Representatives of Foreign 
     Information Media'', and, further, that the resolution be 
     immediately placed upon the Legislative Calendar under 
     General Orders.
         Richard J. Durbin, Mazie K. Hirono, Tina Smith, Catherine 
           Cortez Masto, Alex Padilla, Cory A. Booker, Kirsten E. 
           Gillibrand, Lisa Blunt Rochester, Martin Heinrich, Amy

           Murray, Bernard Sanders, Jeanne Shaheen, Richard 
           Blumenthal, Michael F. Bennet, Tim Kaine, Tammy 
           Baldwin, Sheldon Whitehouse, Andy Kim, Peter Welch, 
           John W. Hickenlooper, Adam B. Schiff, Tammy Duckworth, 
           Christopher A. Coons, Ron Wyden, Elizabeth Warren, Jeff 
           Merkley, Christopher Murphy, Edward J. Markey, Maria
```
