# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 210, S. 364, S. 537, S. 1031, S. 1677, S. 1715, S. 2019, S. 2903, S. 3265, S. 3332, S. 3387, S. 3605, S. 4395, S. 4411, S. 4429, S. 4775, S. 4784, S. 4814, S. 5028, S. 5043, S. 5259, S. 5288, S. 5336, S. 5371

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 364

  At the request of Mr. Crapo, the names of the Senator from Ohio (Mr. 
Moreno) and the Senator from Indiana (Mr. Young) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 364, a bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
to remove silencers from the definition of firearms, and for other 
purposes.


                                 S. 537

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the name of the Senator from Indiana 
(Mr. Young) was added as a cosponsor of S. 537, a bill to prohibit the 
Secretary of the Interior and the Secretary of Agriculture from 
prohibiting the use of lead ammunition or tackle on certain Federal 
land or water under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Interior 
and the Secretary of Agriculture, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1031

  At the request of Mr. Tillis, the name of the Senator from Montana 
(Mr. Daines) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1031, a bill to amend Title 
XVIII of the Social Security Act to create a Radiation Oncology Case 
Rate Value Based Payment Program exempt from budget neutrality 
adjustment requirements, and to amend section 1128A of title XI of the 
Social Security Act to create a new statutory exception for the 
provision of free or discounted transportation for radiation oncology 
patients to receive radiation therapy services.


                                S. 1677

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the name of the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Hickenlooper) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1677, a bill to 
provide health insurance benefits for outpatient and inpatient items 
and services related to the diagnosis and treatment of a congenital 
anomaly or birth defect.


                                S. 1715

  At the request of Mr. Hagerty, the names of the Senator from Ohio 
(Mr. Moreno) and the Senator from Indiana (Mr. Young) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 1715, a bill to prohibit payment card networks and 
covered entities from requiring the use of or assigning merchant 
category codes that distinguish a firearms retailer from a general 
merchandise retailer or sporting goods retailer, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 2019

  At the request of Mr. Crapo, the name of the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mrs. Fischer) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2019, a bill to establish 
a Task Force for Recognizing and Averting Payment Scams, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 2903

  At the request of Ms. Murkowski, the name of the Senator from 
Wisconsin (Ms. Baldwin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2903, a bill to 
amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to require a 
group health plan or health insurance coverage offered in connection 
with such a plan to provide an exceptions process for any medication 
step therapy protocol, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3265

  At the request of Mr. Cassidy, the name of the Senator from Ohio (Mr. 
Husted) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3265, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to improve and enhance the work 
opportunity tax credit, to encourage longer-service employment, and to 
modernize the credit to make it more effective as a hiring incentive 
for targeted workers, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3332

  At the request of Mr. Cornyn, the name of the Senator from Montana 
(Mr. Sheehy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3332, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the exclusion of gain from 
the sale of a principal residence, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3387

  At the request of Mr. Gallego, the name of the Senator from Virginia 
(Mr. Warner) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3387, a bill to prohibit 
certain uses of automated decision systems to inform individualized 
prices, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3605

  At the request of Ms. Graham, her name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
3605, a bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow a 
portion of general business credit carryforwards to be transferred by 
certain taxpayers affected by federally declared disasters and other 
incidents.

                                S. 4395

Risk Insurance Act of 2002, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4411

  At the request of Mr. Markey, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Heinrich) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4411, a bill to clarify 
eligibility for small business loans, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4429

  At the request of Mr. Moreno, the name of the Senator from Minnesota

the importation, manufacture, sale, resale, or introduction into 
interstate commerce in the United States of connected vehicles and 
related software and hardware associated with foreign adversaries.


                                S. 4775

  At the request of Mr. Cornyn, the name of the Senator from Ohio (Mr. 
Moreno) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4775, a bill to amend the 
Protection of Lawful Commerce in Arms Act to clarify liability 
protections for firearms and associated manufacturers and retailers, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 4814

  At the request of Mr. Markey, the name of the Senator from Georgia 
(Mr. Ossoff) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4814, a bill to require the 
Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Haiti for temporary 
protected status.


                                S. 5028

  At the request of Mr. Husted, the names of the Senator from Florida 
(Mrs. Moody), the Senator from Nebraska (Mr. Ricketts), the Senator 
from Kansas (Mr. Marshall) and the Senator from Louisiana (Mr. Kennedy) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 5028, a bill to amend the Public Utility 
Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 to establish a Federal standard 
relating to the recovery of the full, incremental costs of upgrades 
that serve large-load customers, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5043

  At the request of Mr. Budd, the names of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) and the Senator from Alabama (Mr. Tuberville) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 5043, a bill to direct the Food and Drug 
Administration to prevent the importation of counterfeit, unapproved, 
misbranded, or adulterated drugs from the People's Republic of China or 
other foreign countries, to establish enhanced safeguards for imported 
drug products, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5259

  At the request of Mr. Hagerty, the name of the Senator from Ohio (Mr. 
Moreno) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5259, a bill to prohibit 
sanctuary jurisdictions from receiving community development block 
grants, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5288

  At the request of Mr. Schatz, the name of the Senator from Arizona 
(Mr. Kelly) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5288, a bill to restore 
press access to the Pentagon, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5336

  At the request of Mrs. Fischer, the name of the Senator from Kansas 
(Mr. Moran) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5336, a bill to amend title 
49, United States Code, to prohibit liability at common law for failure 
to manufacture or equip a motor vehicle to an extent that exceeds 
applicable motor vehicle safety standards, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5371

  At the request of Mr. Lee, the name of the Senator from Missouri (Mr. 
Hawley) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5371, a bill to close loopholes 
in the immigration laws that serve as incentives to aliens to attempt 
to enter the United States unlawfully, and for other purposes.


                             S.J. RES. 210

  At the request of Mr. Moreno, the name of the Senator from Texas (Mr. 
Cruz) was added as a cosponsor of S.J. Res. 210, a joint resolution 
providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
Protection Agency relating ``California State Nonroad Engine Pollution 
Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft Regulations; Notice of 
Decision''.


                           AMENDMENT NO. 5903

  At the request of Ms. Duckworth, the name of the Senator from 
Tennessee (Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of amendment No. 
5903 intended to be proposed to S. 4784, an original bill to authorize 
appropriations for fiscal year 2027 for military activities of the 
Department of Defense, for military construction, and for defense 
activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.
```
