# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 49

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-47. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the General 
     Assembly of the State of South Carolina, relative to 
     Concurrent Resolution H. 3008, requesting the Congress of the 
     United States call a convention of the states to propose 
     amendments to the Constitution of the United States; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
       ML-48. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of Connecticut, relative to Substitute House Joint 
     Resolution No. 49, Resolution Act No. 25-1, rescinding 
     previous applications of the General Assembly requesting the 
     Congress of the United States call a convention of the states 
     to propose amendments to the Constitution of the United 
     States; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
