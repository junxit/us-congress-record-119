# HONORING SETH NESMITH

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                         HONORING SETH NESMITH

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Seth 
NeSmith, a registered nurse at Atrium Health Navicent, who has been 
named one of the 2025 Hospital Heroes recipients by the Georgia 
Hospital Association.
  This award recognizes the top three hospital employees or volunteers 
from GHA-member hospitals who dedicate their time to improving both 
their organization and the world around them.
  Mr. NeSmith has impressively received a registered nurse degree, 
bachelor of science in nursing, stroke certified registered nurse 
credential, and critical care registered nurse certifications.
  Mr. NeSmith was also awarded last year's Atrium Health Navicent's 
DAISY Award for noticing a vehicle stuck deep in a ditch, pulling the 
victim out, and providing immediate further care.
  Mr. NeSmith's leadership is vital for shaping the next generation of 
healthcare professionals who will go on to serve communities across 
Georgia. This recognition speaks to his extensive skills and expertise 
in nursing.
  We appreciate Mr. NeSmith's commitment to hard work, compassion for 
everyone around him, and going above and beyond for his patients.

                          ____________________
```
