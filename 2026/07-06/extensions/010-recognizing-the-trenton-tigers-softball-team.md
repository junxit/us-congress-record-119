# RECOGNIZING THE TRENTON TIGERS SOFTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
              RECOGNIZING THE TRENTON TIGERS SOFTBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, July 6, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
group of young women from Florida's 3rd Congressional District, the 
Trenton Tigers softball team, on capturing their third consecutive 
FHSAA Rural State Championship.
  This championship wasn't handed to them. It was earned through 
countless early mornings, late night practices, discipline, and a 
commitment to one another that defines what being a champion is all 
about.
  The Trenton Tigers softball team has represented their school and 
Florida's 3rd District with grit, resilience, and determination. 
Through relentless work, unwavering discipline, and an unbreakable bond 
as teammates, they have shown that the heart of a champion isn't 
measured by the size of a school or a town, but by the determination to 
outwork and dominate every challenge placed before them.
  I also want to give special recognition to Addison Allaire, who was 
recently named 2026 Florida Gatorade Player of the Year. This 
prestigious honor reflects her leadership, work ethic, and commitment 
to excellence in the classroom and on the field. Addison's 
accomplishments embody the very best of Florida student-athletes, and 
this recognition is well deserved.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Florida's 3rd Congressional District, I 
want to congratulate Addison and the Trenton Tigers softball team on 
another outstanding season. She has inspired young female athletes 
across our district by showing what hard work and heart can build. Go 
Tigers.

                          ____________________
```
