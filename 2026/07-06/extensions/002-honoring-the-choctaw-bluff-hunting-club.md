# HONORING THE CHOCTAW BLUFF HUNTING CLUB

```
                HONORING THE CHOCTAW BLUFF HUNTING CLUB

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, July 6, 2026

  Mr. Hudson. Mr. Speaker, on July 4, 2026, the Stimpson family 
celebrated the 100th anniversary of the Choctaw Bluff Hunting Club 
founded by Fred T. Stimpson in 1926.
  Fred was one of Alabama's most influential early conservation 
leaders, especially in the areas of wildlife restoration forestry, and 
habitat protection. Fred served on Alabama's first Department of 
Conservation and Natural Resources Conservation Advisory Board. He 
helped lead the Alabama Wildlife Federation and ultimately established 
the Fred T. Stimpson Special Opportunity Area which provides protected 
lands for deer, turkey and other species in Clarke County, Alabama.
  Today, Choctaw Bluff provides the Stimpson family and the club's 
members with the opportunity to hunt, socialize and preserve their core 
values. These values include the love of family, the practice of faith 
and the protection of land, which will be passed down for generations.
  I am proud to call the Stimpson family my friends, and I hope Choctaw 
Bluff along with its values will continue for 100 more years, and 
beyond.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in honoring The Choctaw Bluff 
Hunting Club.
```
