# RECOGNIZING JOE CIRULLI

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JOE CIRULLI

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, July 6, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
leader from our Gainesville Community, Joe Cirulli, on his induction 
into the Health and Fitness Association Hall of Fame.
  For decades, Joe has been a pioneer in the health and fitness 
industry, demonstrating that true leadership extends far beyond the 
walls of a gym. As the founder of Gainesville Health & Fitness, he has 
built more than a successful business. He has cultivated a culture that 
inspires people to live healthier, stronger, and more connected lives.
  Joe's expertise, vision, and servant leadership have transformed the 
way countless individuals think about health and wellness. His 
commitment to investing in people, strengthening communities, and 
leading by example has left a lasting impact on Gainesville and the 
Nation.
  This well-deserved honor recognizes a lifetime of innovation, 
dedication, and service. Joe has shown that when you lead with purpose 
and put people first, you can influence lives for generations.
  Congratulations to Joe on this remarkable achievement. Florida's 3rd 
District is proud to call him one of our own, and we thank him for his 
continued commitment to making our communities healthier and stronger.

                          ____________________
```
