# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN MICHELLE WALKER-CRAWFORD

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
              RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN MICHELLE WALKER-CRAWFORD

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, July 6, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
leader from Florida's 3rd Congressional District, Michelle Walker-
Crawford, on her well-deserved induction into the Florida FFA Hall of 
Fame.
  This prestigious honor reflects a lifetime of dedication to 
agricultural education, student leadership, and the values that make 
our rural communities so strong. Through her unwavering commitment to 
the Florida FFA, Michelle has helped shape her generations of young 
leaders, equipping them with the skills, confidence, and character to 
succeed both inside and outside the classroom.
  Her passion for education and her steadfast belief in the next 
generation has left an enduring mark on Florida FFA. The countless 
students she has mentored and inspired will carry her legacy forward 
for years to come.
  Michelle's impact extends far beyond any one chapter or classroom. 
She has strengthened the future of agriculture in our state by 
investing in the people who will lead it, and her service is a shining 
example of what it means to lead with purpose.
  Congratulations to Michelle on this incredible achievement. I thank 
her for her years of service, her dedication to Florida's students, and 
for making Florida's 3rd District proud.

                          ____________________
```
