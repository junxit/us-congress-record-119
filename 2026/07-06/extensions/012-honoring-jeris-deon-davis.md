# HONORING JERIS DEON DAVIS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                       HONORING JERIS DEON DAVIS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, July 6, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of Jeris Deon Davis of Shaw, Mississippi, and to 
recognize his dedicated service to the citizens of Mississippi through 
his distinguished career with the Mississippi Bureau of Investigation.
  Throughout his career in law enforcement, Mr. Davis demonstrated 
professionalism, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to justice. As 
a member of the Mississippi Bureau of Investigation, he played a vital 
role in protecting communities, supporting criminal investigations, and 
upholding the laws of our state. Through hard work, dedication, and 
exemplary leadership, he rose through the ranks, serving as a 
Lieutenant before being promoted to Branch Director at the Mississippi 
Bureau of Investigation. His commitment to ensuring the safety and 
well-being of Mississippi's citizens earned him the respect and 
admiration of colleagues, community leaders, and residents alike.
  A native of Shaw, Mississippi, Mr. Davis exemplified the values of 
hard work, service, and leadership. His career reflected a steadfast 
commitment to making a positive difference in the lives of others and 
strengthening the communities he served. Through his dedication and 
sacrifice, he helped make Mississippi a safer place for countless 
families.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Mr. Davis served as a role 
model for young people throughout the Mississippi Delta, demonstrating 
the importance of discipline, responsibility, and public service. His 
contributions left a lasting impact on both the law enforcement 
community and the citizens of Mississippi.
  Although he is no longer with us, the legacy of Jeris Deon Davis 
lives on through the lives he touched, the communities he served, and 
the example he set for future generations. His commitment to justice, 
service, and excellence will not be forgotten.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the memory of 
Jeris Deon Davis for his outstanding service, dedication, and 
contributions to the State of Mississippi. We extend our deepest 
condolences to his family, friends, colleagues, and all who mourn his 
passing. May they find comfort in knowing that his life was one of 
purpose, service, and lasting impact.
  His legacy of leadership, integrity, and commitment to justice will 
continue to inspire all who knew him and those who follow in his 
footsteps.

                          ____________________
```
