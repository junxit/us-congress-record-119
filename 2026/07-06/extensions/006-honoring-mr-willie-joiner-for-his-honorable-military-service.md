# HONORING MR. WILLIE JOINER FOR HIS HONORABLE MILITARY SERVICE AND LIFETIME OF PERSEVERANCE

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
   HONORING MR. WILLIE JOINER FOR HIS HONORABLE MILITARY SERVICE AND 
                        LIFETIME OF PERSEVERANCE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, July 6, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Mr. Willie Joiner, a proud veteran of the United States Army, 
whose courageous service during the Vietnam War and lifelong 
perseverance embody the very best of the American spirit.
  Mr. Joiner was drafted into the United States Army in 1968 and, 
following completion of basic training, answered our nation's call by 
serving a full tour of duty in the Vietnam War. He faithfully fulfilled 
his responsibilities with honor and distinction before receiving an 
honorable discharge in 1972. Like so many veterans of his generation, 
Mr. Joiner returned home carrying the unseen burdens of war while 
continuing to live a life marked by dignity, resilience, and quiet 
strength.
  A resident of Dundee, Mississippi, Mr. Joiner's story began in Tunica 
County, where he was raised as one of fifteen children born to Frank, 
Sr. and Gennie V. Joiner. Growing up in a modest three-bedroom home on 
a plantation, the Joiner family faced many hardships. Without modern 
conveniences and with limited resources, Mr. Joiner worked alongside 
his parents and siblings as a sharecropper, helping provide for his 
family through hard work and determination.
  Despite these humble beginnings, Mr. Joiner answered the call to 
defend the freedoms we cherish. His service reflects the sacrifices 
made by countless young Americans who left their homes to serve a 
nation they loved, often at great personal cost.
  Today, Mr. Joiner continues to face serious medical challenges 
related to his military service. His courage in confronting these 
challenges serves as another testament to his enduring character. As a 
grateful nation, we owe our veterans not only our appreciation but also 
our unwavering commitment to ensuring they receive the care, support, 
and respect they have earned through their service.
  Mr. Joiner's legacy also lives on through the enduring values of the 
Joiner family. In 2024, the family established The Joiner Place, a 
family heritage and learning center dedicated to preserving their 
history and inspiring future generations through the principles of 
faith, humility, service, generosity, forgiveness, and thankfulness. 
Their mission reminds us that strong families and shared history 
strengthen our communities and our nation.
  I also commend the efforts to preserve the Joiner family's legacy 
through the designation of Joiner Road in honor of Frank, Sr. and 
Gennie V. Joiner, recognizing the lasting contributions of a family 
whose story reflects the perseverance and resilience of rural 
Mississippi.
  Mr. Speaker, it is with profound respect and gratitude that I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Willie Joiner for his 
honorable military service, his steadfast commitment to family and 
country, and the example he continues to set for future generations. 
May we never forget his sacrifices, and may God continue to bless Mr. 
Willie Joiner, his family, our veterans, and the United States of 
America.
```
