# TRIBUTE TO MADISON SPEAR

**Speaking:** Sheehy, Tim (S001232) [R-MT]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MADISON SPEAR

● Mr. SHEEHY. Mr. President, I would like to take a moment to 
express my appreciation to Madison Spear for her hard work as an intern 
in my Missoula, MT, office this spring.
  Madison is a native of Missoula, MT. She currently owns her own 
photography and bookkeeping businesses. She attended University of 
Montana where she obtained her associate of arts degree. Madison has 
demonstrated a strong work ethic, and her efforts have been key to our 
mission to serve the people of Montana.
  I want to thank Madison for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It has been a pleasure having her as part 
of our team this spring, and I wish her all the best as she continues 
to build a bright future for herself.●

                          ____________________
```
