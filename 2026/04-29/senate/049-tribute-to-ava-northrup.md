# TRIBUTE TO AVA NORTHRUP

**Speaking:** Sheehy, Tim (S001232) [R-MT]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO AVA NORTHRUP

● Mr. SHEEHY. Mr. President, I would like to take a moment to 
express my appreciation to Ava Northrup for her hard work as an intern 
in my Bozeman, MT, office this spring.
  Ava is a native of Leavenworth, WA. She currently works as a wildland 
firefighter for the U.S. Forest Service and obtained a degree in 
political science from Montana State University. Ava has demonstrated a 
strong work ethic, and her efforts have been key to our mission to 
serve the people of Montana.
  I want to thank Ava for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It has been a pleasure having her as part of our 
team this spring, and I wish her all the best as she continues to build 
a bright future for herself.●

                          ____________________
```
