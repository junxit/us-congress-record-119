# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1755, H.R. 2488, H.R. 6099, H.R. 7287, H.R. 7331, H.R. 7343, H.R. 7432, H.R. 7463, H.R. 7515, H.R. 7529, H.R. 7655, H.R. 7809, H.R. 7995, H.R. 8107, H.R. 8193, H.R. 8225, H.R. 8259, H.R. 8312, H.R. 8340, H.R. 8428, H.R. 8462, H.R. 8463, H.R. 8464, H.R. 8466, H.R. 8467

Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Appropriations: Full Committee held a markup on the 
Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, Related 
Agencies Appropriations Bill, FY 2027. The Agriculture, Rural 
Development, Food and Drug Administration, Related Agencies 
Appropriations Bill, FY 2027, was ordered reported, as amended.
DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE FISCAL YEAR 2027 BUDGET REQUEST
Committee on Armed Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Department of Defense Fiscal Year 2027 Budget Request''. Testimony 
was heard from General Dan Caine, Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of 
Staff, Department of Defense; and Pete Hegseth, Secretary of Defense, 
Department of Defense.
SPEECH OR SILENCE? THE FUTURE OF THE FIRST AMENDMENT IN HIGHER 
EDUCATION
Committee on Education and Workforce: Subcommittee on Higher Education 
and Workforce Development held a hearing entitled ``Speech or Silence? 
The Future of the First Amendment in Higher Education''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
AI AND THE GRID: MEETING GROWING POWER DEMAND WHILE PROTECTING 
RATEPAYERS
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Energy held a hearing 
entitled ``AI and the Grid: Meeting Growing Power Demand While 
Protecting Ratepayers''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
HEALTHIER AMERICA: LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS ON THE REGULATION AND 
OVERSIGHT OF FOOD
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``Healthier America: Legislative Proposals on the Regulation 
and Oversight of Food''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
EXAMINING DERIVATIVES' ROLE IN THE TREASURY MARKET
Committee on Financial Services: Task Force on Monetary Policy, 
Treasury Market Resilience, and Economic Prosperity held a hearing 
entitled ``Examining Derivatives' Role in the Treasury Market''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
ORBITS OF INFLUENCE: EMERGING THREATS TO U.S. SPACE SECURITY AND 
FOREIGN POLICY IMPLICATIONS
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Europe Subcommittee held a hearing 
entitled ``Orbits of Influence: Emerging Threats to U.S. Space Security 
and Foreign Policy Implications''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.

[[Page D464]]


U.S. ACCOUNTABILITY AT THE UNITED NATIONS: CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES 
FOR REFORM
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Oversight and Intelligence Subcommittee 
held a hearing entitled ``U.S. Accountability at the United Nations: 
Challenges and Opportunities for Reform''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
DATA CENTERS, TELECOMMUNICATIONS NETWORKS, AND SPACE-BASED SYSTEMS: 
MODERNIZING THE DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY'S SECTOR RISK 
MANAGEMENT AGENCY ROLE FOR THE COMMUNICATIONS AND IT SECTORS
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Cybersecurity and 
Infrastructure Protection held a hearing entitled ``Data Centers, 
Telecommunications Networks, and Space-Based Systems: Modernizing DHS's 
SRMA Role for the Communications and IT Sectors''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
PEACE OF MIND: STRENGTHENING VICTIM PROTECTIONS UNDER KAYLEIGH'S LAW
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Crime and Federal 
Government Surveillance held a hearing entitled ``Peace of Mind: 
Strengthening Victim Protections Under Kayleigh's Law''. Testimony was 
heard from Suzette Martinez Valladares, State Senator, California; and 
public witnesses.
POWERING THE 21ST CENTURY WITH AMERICAN COPPER
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Energy and Mineral 
Resources held a hearing entitled ``Powering the 21st Century with 
American Copper''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Water, Wildlife and 
Fisheries held a hearing on H.R. 7287, the ``Lewis and Clark Regional 
Water System Expansion Feasibility Study Act''; H.R. 7331, the ``Dakota 
Mainstem Water Supply Project Feasibility Study Act''; H.R. 7515, to 
direct the Secretary of the Interior to convey to the Nisqually Indian 
Tribe the Clear Creek Hatchery infrastructure; H.R. 8259, the 
``Reclamation Project Consultation Improvement Act of 2026''. Testimony 
was heard from Representatives Johnson of South Dakota, Strickland, and 
Bentz; and public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Full Committee held a 
markup on H.R. 8463, the ``Pre-Payment Fraud Prevention and Treasury 
Data Access Act''; H.R. 8464, the ``Stopping Fraudulent Payments Act''; 
H.R. 8312, the ``Fraud Prevention and Accountability Act''; H.R. 8428, 
the ``Federal Fraud Prevention Workforce Training Act''; H.R. 8467, the 
``Zeroing Out Monetary Benefits Improperly Expended Act''; H.R. 8466, 
the ``Taxpayer Resources Used in Emergencies Accountability Act''; H.R. 
8340, the ``Taxpayer Funds Oversight and Accountability Act''; H.R. 
1755, the ``Timely and Accurate Benefits Act''; H.R. 8107, the 
``Government Audit and Accountability of Federally Funded State-
Administered Programs Act''; H.R. 8193, to designate the facility of 
the United States Postal Service located at 189 East Main Street in 
Xenia, Ohio, as the ``Gilman 'Gil' Whitney Post Office Building''; H.R. 
2488, to designate the facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 3817 Marysville Boulevard in Sacramento, California, as the 
``Grantland Johnson Post Office''; H.R. 6099, to designate the facility 
of the United States Postal Service located at 2200 South Salina Street 
in Syracuse, New York, as the ``Wallie Howard Jr. Post Office 
Building''; H.R. 7809, to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 201 East Grant Avenue in Georgetown, Ohio, as 
the ``Ulysses S. Grant Post Office Building''; and H.R. 8225, to 
designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
111 South Tremont Street in Tremonton, Utah, as the ``Sorensen-Estrada 
Post Office''. H.R. 8463, H.R. 8464, H.R. 8312, H.R. 8428, H.R. 8467, 
H.R. 8466, H.R. 8340, H.R. 1755, H.R. 8107, and H.R. 8193 were ordered 
reported, as amended. H.R. 2488, H.R. 6099, H.R. 7809, and H.R. 8225 
were ordered reported, without amendment.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Full Committee held a 
markup on H.R. 8462, the ``National Quantum Initiative Reauthorization 
Act''. H.R. 8462 was ordered reported, as amended.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Ways and Means: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 7432, 
the ``Foster Youth Housing Opportunity Act''; H.R. 7463, the ``Foster 
Youth Postsecondary Education Access and Success Act''; H.R. 7343, the 
``Foster Youth Workforce Opportunity Act''; H.R. 7529, the ``Fresh 
Starts for Foster Youth Act''; H.R. 7655, the ``Support for Expectant 
and Parenting Foster Youth Act''; and H.R. 7995, the ``Chafee 
Opportunities for New Networks and Existing Connection Trust Act''. 
H.R. 7432, H.R. 7463, H.R. 7343, H.R. 7529, H.R. 7655, and H.R. 7995 
were ordered reported, as amended.
