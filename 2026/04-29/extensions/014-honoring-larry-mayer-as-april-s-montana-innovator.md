# HONORING LARRY MAYER AS APRIL'S "MONTANA INNOVATOR"

**Speaking:** Downing, Troy (D000634) [R-MT]

```
         HONORING LARRY MAYER AS APRIL'S ``MONTANA INNOVATOR''

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. TROY DOWNING

                               of montana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 29, 2026

  Mr. DOWNING. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Larry Mayer as the 
``Montana Innovator'' for the month of April. In honor of America's 
250th birthday, Montana's congressional delegation is recognizing 
exceptional individuals who embody the spirit of our great state and 
Nation. This month, I am proud to highlight Montana's rich communities 
and the power of visual storytelling by celebrating a dedicated 
photojournalist who has spent decades documenting the people, places, 
and events that define the Treasure State: Larry Mayer.
  Larry Mayer, a photographer with The Billings Gazette, has worked in 
photojournalism for nearly 50 years, capturing and preserving the 
stories of communities across Montana. Having worked with Larry during 
my time in Congress and previously as Montana State Auditor, I have 
come to admire his calm demeanor, kindness, and ability to connect with 
everyone.
  Larry's commitment to photojournalism drew international attention 
when he identified the Chinese spy balloon as it crossed over Montana. 
Capturing both everyday moments and historic events is part of the very 
fabric of our state. With Larry Mayer behind the lens, those stories 
will continue to be told for generations to come.
  It is my honor to recognize him this April, alongside eleven other 
individuals who represent what it means to be a Montanan.

                          ____________________
```
