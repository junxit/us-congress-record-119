# RECOGNIZING EVELYN LUNA AVALOS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING EVELYN LUNA AVALOS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 29, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Evelyn Luna 
Avalos for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Evelyn has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Evelyn, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Evelyn's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Jefferson Jr./Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Evelyn Luna Avalos on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
