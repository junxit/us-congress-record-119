# HONORING THE LIFE OF MARY ANNE MULLANY

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF MARY ANNE MULLANY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 29, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life of Mary Anne Mullany of Ridley Park, Pennsylvania, who passed away 
recently at the age of 94.
  A graduate of the Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania School 
of Nursing, she went on to receive her Bachelor of Science in Nursing 
from Neumann University before beginning her more than 40-year career 
as a Registered Nurse, including 20 years in the Neonatal Intensive 
Care Nursery at the Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania.
  Throughout her time at the Hospital of the University of 
Pennsylvania, Mary worked to care for the most vulnerable. She spent 
long nights on her feet monitoring vital signs, carrying out life-
saving procedures, holding children during their first and most fragile 
days, and comforting families as they navigated the challenges of 
caring for a child born prematurely.
  Mary cherished her six children, eight grandchildren, and two great-
grandchildren, and dedicated her life to caring for others.
  She was a lifelong learner. Her passion for history and travel took 
her from Israel and Spain to the National Parks of the American West, 
always by the side of those she loved. She enjoyed attending the Opera, 
going to Phillies games, and solving New York Times crossword puzzles 
while sitting in her garden alongside her daughter, Mary.
  Today, please join me in remembering Mary Anne Mullany--a nurse, a 
mother, and a friend to all she knew.

                          ____________________
```
