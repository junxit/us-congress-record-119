# RECOGNIZING ASHLEY BECK

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING ASHLEY BECK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 29, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ashley Beck for 
earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Ashley has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Ashley, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Ashley's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Drake Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Ashley Beck on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
