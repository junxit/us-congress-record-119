# RECOGNIZING ALDIN MEDNOLUCANIN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING ALDIN MEDNOLUCANIN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 29, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Aldin 
Mednolucanin for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Aldin has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Aldin, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Aldin's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Arvada West High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Aldin Mednolucanin on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
