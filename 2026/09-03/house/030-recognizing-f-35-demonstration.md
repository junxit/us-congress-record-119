# RECOGNIZING F-35 DEMONSTRATION

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                     RECOGNIZING F-35 DEMONSTRATION

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, exciting news: Lockheed 
Martin brought an F-35 Lightning II cockpit demonstrator to LCC's 
Senator Jim Perry Aviation Center for Excellence at the North Carolina 
Global TransPark.

[[Page H5491]]

  Flying this simulator with Lieutenant Governor Rachel Hunt was a 
powerful reminder that the future of American aviation and our national 
defense is stronger because of eastern North Carolina.
  The F-35 is central to the modernization of Marine Corps aviation, 
and Marine Corps Air Station Cherry Point is preparing to support F-35 
squadrons as part of this transformation.
  Such investment strengthens our national security while supporting 
skilled jobs, innovation, and economic opportunity.
  We are extremely proud of our Marines and proud that eastern North 
Carolina continues to play a vital role in our Nation's defense.
  

                          ____________________
```
