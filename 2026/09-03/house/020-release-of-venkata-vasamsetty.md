# RELEASE OF VENKATA VASAMSETTY

**Speaking:** Ross, Deborah K. (R000305) [D-NC]

```
                              {time}  1040
                     RELEASE OF VENKATA VASAMSETTY

  (Ms. ROSS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. ROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight the release of 
Venkata Vasamsetty from ICE custody.
  Ms. Vasamsetty is a Wake County substitute teacher, a mother, and a 
grandmother of U.S. citizens, who has lived in this country legally for 
27 years with no criminal record.
  Last month, ICE arrested her and wrongfully detained her. By every 
measure, she is an asset to our community and poses no threat. Under 
this administration, thousands of other law-abiding immigrants just 
like her are being held in detention facilities, often without access 
to proper medical care and humane living conditions.
  It is wrong to use the American taxpayers' dollars to detain people 
like Ms. Vasamsetty.
  We must continue to speak out against ICE's cruel and unlawful 
actions. We cannot allow this administration to continue detaining our 
neighbors and terrorizing our communities.

                          ____________________
```
