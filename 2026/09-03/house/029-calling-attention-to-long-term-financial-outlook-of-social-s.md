# CALLING ATTENTION TO LONG-TERM FINANCIAL OUTLOOK OF SOCIAL SECURITY

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
  CALLING ATTENTION TO LONG-TERM FINANCIAL OUTLOOK OF SOCIAL SECURITY

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to call attention to the long-
term financial outlook of Social Security.
  According to the Social Security Administration, the Social Security 
trust fund will be able to pay full benefits only until the end of 
2032.
  Social Security is a lifeline that lifts seniors, widows, and 
dependent children out of poverty.
  

  If Congress does not act to fix this problem, over 3.3 million New 
Yorkers will be affected by future benefit cuts, with the average 
retiree in New York seeing their benefits reduced by more than $500.
  Inaction will only perpetuate an inevitable spike in poverty if 
Congress fails to act to ensure Social Security's long-term solvency.
  I implore my colleagues to come together in this moral moment to save 
Social Security before we subject our constituents to a reduction in 
their hard-earned benefits.

                          ____________________
```
