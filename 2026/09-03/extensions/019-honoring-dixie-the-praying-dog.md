# HONORING DIXIE THE PRAYING DOG

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
                     HONORING DIXIE THE PRAYING DOG

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES R. BAIRD

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a great Hoosier canine 
that has brought comfort and joy to thousands of people across our 
great Nation.
  Dixie the Praying Dog is best known for her therapy work serving 
veterans and children.
  She has brought joy to almost every Indy Honor Flight, visited VA 
Hospitals, military ceremonies, nursing homes, schools, disaster 
response sites, etc.
  Thousands of veterans and their family members have met Dixie and had 
the opportunity to see Dixie ``pray,'' bark, and receive pets.
  Alongside her owner, Brian Calvert, Dixie has become a powerful 
symbol of patriotism and unconditional love, proving that extraordinary 
acts of service can come from the most unexpected heroes.
  Dixie's simple act of laying her paws together has become an 
unforgettable image of hope for people facing life's greatest 
challenges. I thank Brian and Dixie for bringing comfort to people 
everywhere they go.

                          ____________________
```
