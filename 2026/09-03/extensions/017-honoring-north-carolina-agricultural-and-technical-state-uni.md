# HONORING NORTH CAROLINA AGRICULTURAL AND TECHNICAL STATE UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
  HONORING NORTH CAROLINA AGRICULTURAL AND TECHNICAL STATE UNIVERSITY

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the anniversaries of 
North Carolina Agricultural and Technical State University's (N.C. A&T) 
100th homecoming and the 125th football season for the Aggies.
  The Aggies' homecoming is known as ``The Greatest Homecoming on 
Earth.'' Each fall, tens of thousands of N.C. A&T alumni join students, 
faculty, and Aggie fans in a weeklong homecoming celebration. The 
university traces its homecoming tradition to 1926, when Aggies began 
gathering around football, fellowship, and alumni engagement. The 100th 
anniversary homecoming celebration includes a parade, an alumni 
concert, a stepshow, a coronation, a tailgate, and a game against Elon 
University on October 31, 2026.
  The centennial celebration also coincides with the 125th season of 
Aggie football, which dates to 1901 when N.C. A&T played its first game 
against Livingstone College. There have been many great moments in the 
team's history including: 11 Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference 
championships and undefeated season in 2017. N.C. A&T has also produced 
39 NFL players, including one hall of famer and 3 Superbowl champions.
  I am proud to support such a talented and historic program. I am 
inspired by what N.C. A&T accomplished and I cannot wait to see what 
they will achieve in the next 125 years.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today honoring the North Carolina 
Agricultural and Technical State University on its 100th homecoming and 
125th football season.
```
