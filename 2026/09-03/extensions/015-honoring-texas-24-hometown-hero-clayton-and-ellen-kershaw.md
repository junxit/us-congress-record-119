# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CLAYTON AND ELLEN KERSHAW

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CLAYTON AND ELLEN KERSHAW

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker. I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Clayton and Ellen Kershaw, who have been named the 2026 
University Park Citizens of the Year in recognition of their commitment 
to serving others and making a lasting impact on their community and 
beyond.
  This award honors University Park residents who enhance the city's 
quality of life, often through service, generosity, and leadership. 
Since the launch of Kershaw's Challenge in 2011, the Kershaw's have 
supported vulnerable and at-risk children in Dallas, Los Angeles, 
Zambia, and the Dominican Republic by providing hope, resources, and 
opportunities for a brighter future. Through Kershaw's Challenge, the 
couple has used their platform to empower communities and create 
opportunities for children and families in need.
  Clayton, a graduate of Highland Park High School and a former pitcher 
for the Los Angeles Dodgers, has also been recognized for his 
humanitarian efforts with the Roberto Clemente Award. Together, Clayton 
and Ellen continue to inspire others through their dedication to 
creating meaningful change.
```
