# HONORING THE OLDEST MAN ALIVE IN THE UNITED STATES, MR. MAJOR JEWEL McDONALD

**Speaking:** Guest, Michael (G000591) [R-MS]

```
  HONORING THE OLDEST MAN ALIVE IN THE UNITED STATES, MR. MAJOR JEWEL 
                                McDONALD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL GUEST

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. GUEST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the nearly 111 years of 
Mr. Major Jewel McDonald of Louisville, Mississippi. Born in 1915, Mr. 
McDonald has witnessed generations of history in his lifetime. Mr. 
McDonald has lived through two world wars, twenty different Presidents 
going back to President

[[Page E860]]

Woodrow Wilson, six members of the 3rd Congressional District of 
Mississippi going back to Congressman Benjamin G. Humphreys, and has 
witnessed events such as the roaring 20's, the space race, the civil 
rights movement, the fall of the Berlin Wall, and the rise of modern 
technology. Mr. McDonald shares a birth year with famous singer Frank 
Sinatra and fellow Mississippian Muddy Waters.
  Mr. McDonald has lived a wholesome life with family and his 
community, working hard and investing in a career with The Kroger 
Company. In the 1980's, after a stint in Ohio, Mr. McDonald moved back 
to Louisville to live in his home community.
  Mr. McDonald was recognized as the oldest living man in America 
following the passing of Edwin ``Ed'' George Martin of Lehigh Acres, 
Florida, on July 24, 2026, at the age of 111 years and 159 days. On 
November 1, 2026, Mr. McDonald will celebrate his 111th birthday. He 
will be turning 111 on November 1st. I am honored to recognize Mr. 
McDonald for his long life and amazing legacy.
  I wish Mr. McDonald the best and I am honored to represent him in the 
United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
