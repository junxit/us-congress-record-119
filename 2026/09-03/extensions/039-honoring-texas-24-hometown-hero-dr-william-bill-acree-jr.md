# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DR. WILLIAM "BILL" ACREE. JR.

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DR. WILLIAM ``BILL'' ACREE. JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Dr. William ``Bill'' Acree, Jr. for his longstanding dedication 
to mentoring student researchers. Dr. Acree has been named the 2026 
Council on Undergraduate Research (CUR) Goldwater Scholars Faculty 
Mentor Award recipient.
  A professor of chemistry at the University of North Texas (UNT) and a 
UNT faculty member since 1988, Dr. Acree has combined decades of 
research with an unwavering commitment to student mentorship and 
fostering scientific excellence. He is a Fellow of the Royal Society of 
Chemistry and the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry and 
previously served as co-editor of the Journal of Chemical 
Thermodynamics. He has mentored more than 250 undergraduate and high 
school students, helping them earn prestigious honors, including the 
Goldwater Scholarship, and inspiring them to pursue impactful careers 
in STEM and beyond.
  Dr. Acree's dedication to student success and advancing undergraduate 
research reflects the lasting impact of a true mentor. I congratulate 
him on this well-deserved honor.

                          ____________________
```
