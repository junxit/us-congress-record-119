# CELEBRATING CLAY HIGH SCHOOL'S CENTENNIAL ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
         CELEBRATING CLAY HIGH SCHOOL'S CENTENNIAL ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARCY KAPTUR

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Clay High School 
as they mark their Centennial Anniversary. Part of the Oregon City 
School District, Clay High School opened its doors to its first class 
of freshmen on September 13, 1926.
  Public education is a partnership between students, educators, 
families, and the communities they call home. The students in the 
classroom today are the leaders who will change the world tomorrow. For 
a century, Clay High School has helped prepare generations of young 
people to meet that responsibility, with nearly 23,000 graduates 
receiving their diplomas since the school first opened its doors.
  Named for Jeremiah Clay, on whose farm the school was built, Clay 
High School has grown from a small school serving its first freshman 
class into a thriving, comprehensive high school. The school seeks to 
develop and care for the whole student, offering a range of technical 
programs, honors courses, Advanced Placement courses, and a College 
Credit Plus Program. Its 27 varsity sports teams and numerous 
extracurricular activities give its students a place to connect, grow, 
and learn outside of the classroom. Through its commitment to rigorous 
and relevant education, strong relationships, and a culture of 
excellence and growth, Clay continues to prepare students to become 
engaged, adaptable citizens.
  Please join me in offering heartfelt congratulations to the teachers, 
staff, and community members who have invested in the success of Clay 
High School, its many students, and the entire City of Oregon. This 
milestone reflects a century of dedication and commitment by countless 
individuals. As Clay High School celebrates 100 years of tradition and 
achievement, I am so proud of the work being done to expand educational 
opportunities for young people in Oregon, and across Lucas County and 
look forward to the many accomplishments still to come.
  Go Eagles. Onward.

                          ____________________
```
