# HONORING GREATER KING DAVID BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING GREATER KING DAVID BAPTIST CHURCH

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a faithful 
congregation in my District, Greater King David Baptist Church in Baton 
Rouge, Louisiana, as it marks more than a century of worship, Christian 
education, evangelism, and community service.
  Greater King David Baptist Church was established in 1922 after a 
division within New Light Baptist Church. Originally located on Crane 
Street, the church began with approximately thirty-two members and was 
led by its founding pastor, Reverend Willie Wheeler. In 1925, the 
congregation adopted the name King David Baptist Church to distinguish 
it from another church with the same name. As the congregation grew, a 
larger church building was completed on Crane Street in 1947, followed 
by the construction of an educational building.
  In 1973, the church began construction of a new facility to 
accommodate its growing membership and expanding educational programs. 
The new church on Blount Avenue was completed and consecrated in 1975, 
at which time the congregation officially adopted the name Greater King 
David Baptist Church. The church continued to expand its ministries and 
community outreach, including establishing an Inner-City location on 
Harry Drive in 1995. Greater King David Baptist Church has maintained a 
longstanding commitment to worship, Christian education, evangelism, 
and community service in Baton Rouge.
  Greater King David Baptist Church is led today by Reverend Dr. John 
E. Montgomery.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Greater 
King David Baptist Church for more than a century of faithful service, 
and in wishing its pastor and congregation continued blessings in the 
years to come.
```
