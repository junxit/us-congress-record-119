# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CORPORAL JOSHUA SWISHER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
        HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CORPORAL JOSHUA SWISHER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Southlake Police Department Corporal Joshua Swisher. He was 
recently honored with the Meritorious Conduct Award, the department's 
second-highest honor, recognizing his exceptional courage and 
lifesaving actions in the line of duty.
  Corporal Swisher was recognized for his response to a vehicle fire on 
State Highway 114, where he helped rescue a driver trapped inside a 
burning vehicle. After the car caught fire, Corporal Swisher acted 
quickly, breaking the window and pulling the driver to safety before 
the car became fully engulfed in flames.
  His bravery and commitment to protecting others reflect the 
dedication and service our first responders provide to our communities 
every day. I thank Corporal Joshua Swisher for his courage and service 
to North Texas.

                          ____________________
```
