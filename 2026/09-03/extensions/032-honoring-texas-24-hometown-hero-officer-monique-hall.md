# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO OFFICER MONIQUE HALL

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
          HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO OFFICER MONIQUE HALL

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Officer Monique Hall. She has dedicated over 25 years to 
protecting, supporting, and uplifting the Bedford community. She 
currently works for the Bedford Police Department's Behavioral 
Intervention Unit, serving as an advocate for victims of domestic 
violence while helping connect them with the resources they need. Her 
advocacy work is part of broader efforts in Northeast Tarrant County to 
prevent domestic violence and reduce repeat offenses through proactIve 
intervention programs.
  Since joining the Bedford Police Department in 1999, Officer Hall has 
served as a Public Service Officer, Patrol Officer, and School Resource 
Officer. She is also a member of the department's Hostage Negotiations 
Team and Crisis/Peer Support Team.
  Through her compassion, dedication, and commitment to helping others, 
Officer Hall has made a lasting impact on their lives. I congratulate 
Monique on this well-deserved recognition.

                          ____________________
```
