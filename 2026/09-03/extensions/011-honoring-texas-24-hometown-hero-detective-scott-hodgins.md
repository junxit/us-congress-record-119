# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DETECTIVE SCOTT HODGINS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
        HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DETECTIVE SCOTT HODGINS

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero. Detective Scott Hodgins, who recently retired from years of 
dedicated service to the Irving Police Department.
  Detective Hodgins began as a Patrol Officer before transitioning to a 
Detective in the Crimes Against Children Unit. In that role, he helped 
protect the most vulnerable members of society and help bring victims 
to justice. During his time, he earned a Certificate of Merit Bar, and 
hosted a podcast called ``The Case Reopened'', where he examined cold 
cases with a detective's insight.
  I thank Detective Hodgins for his service to the Irving Police 
Department and helping keep North Texas safe.

                          ____________________
```
