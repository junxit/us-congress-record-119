# HONORING THE LIFE OF JOHN FRANCIS "JACK" HOUTON

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE OF JOHN FRANCIS ``JACK'' HOUTON

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. JAMES P. McGOVERN

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of John 
Francis ``Jack'' Houton--a proud Bostonian, Navy veteran, attorney, 
longtime public servant, devoted family man, and my friend.
  I had the privilege of knowing Jack for many years. We shared Red Sox 
games, stories about Boston politics, and plenty of conversations about 
public service. He was part of a generation of Massachusetts public 
servants who believed deeply in government as a force for good--and who 
understood that serving others was never about getting credit.
  Jack was born and raised in Dorchester, the son of Irish immigrants 
from County Donegal, Ireland. He became the first in his family to 
attend college, graduating from Boston College before earning a 
commission as an officer in the United States Navy. He served six years 
on active duty and later retired from the Naval Reserve as a Lieutenant 
Commander.
  When his time in uniform ended, his service continued. Jack went on 
to earn his law degree and build a distinguished career in 
Massachusetts public life. He served as Special Assistant to Boston 
Mayor John F. Collins, worked with Boston's arts community, served as 
Clerk of the Massachusetts House Committee on Rules, and later as an 
Assistant Attorney General under Robert H. Quinn.
  Jack's contribution to his community extended far beyond any job 
title. Throughout his life, he remained involved in civic affairs, 
libraries, historic preservation, theater, charitable organizations, 
and the arts. He was endlessly curious, deeply engaged, and always 
ready for a conversation about Boston, politics, Ireland, literature, 
the Red Sox, or the sea.
  What I will remember most is the man himself: his warmth, humor, his 
wisdom, and his friendship. Above all, Jack loved his family. He is 
survived by his beloved wife, Ellen; his eight

[[Page E858]]

children; fourteen grandchildren; his sister, Roseleen; and a large 
extended family.
  Massachusetts is stronger because of people like Jack Houton--people 
who spend their lives quietly showing up, serving their communities, 
and leaving things a little better than they found them.
  I extend my deepest condolences to Jack's family and all who loved 
him. I thank my friend Jack for a life of service, friendship, and 
purpose.
  Fair winds and following seas, my friend.

                          ____________________
```
