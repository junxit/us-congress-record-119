# HONORING INTERDENOMINATIONAL FAITH ASSEMBLY

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING INTERDENOMINATIONAL FAITH ASSEMBLY

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a steadfast 
congregation in my district, Interdenominational Faith Assembly in 
Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and to recognize its many years of prayer, 
perseverance, and service to the people of East Baton Rouge Parish.
  The journey of Interdenominational Faith Assembly began with a few 
faithful and determined saints known as the Prayer Band. Founded by 
Pastor Jim and Edith Howard, the Prayer Band held services in Brother 
and Sister Howard's home on Maplewood Drive. The Prayer Band continued 
services in the homes of many others within the membership.
  As the Prayer Band grew, houses could no longer accommodate the 
multitude of hungry souls searching for the Word. The journey

[[Page E865]]

continued to Rhodes United Funeral Home, located on Scenic Highway, and 
then to Alpine Chapel on Chestnut Street. The need for a larger edifice 
arose. Later God directed their steps, as they were inspired by divine 
wisdom to seek an even larger edifice, and Interdenominational Faith 
Assembly at 3006 Fuqua came to fruition.
  God did not stop there. Hundreds of people packed the little red 
brick church on the corner of Fuqua, and the need for an even larger 
edifice existed. Through a divine Word of Wisdom to the church, God 
said, ``It's still not big enough, I'm going to increase it. I am in 
the midst of my people and I am going to bless them. I will take them 
higher.'' The manifestation was fulfilled in October 1989. The body of 
Interdenominational Faith Assembly moved to the new sanctuary at 5050 
Greenwell Street, where the congregation now worships God in spirit and 
in truth.
  Interdenominational Faith Assembly is led today by Pastor Guevara 
Johnson.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in acknowledging 
Interdenominational Faith Assembly for its years of faithful service, 
and in wishing its pastor and congregation every blessing in the years 
to come.
```
