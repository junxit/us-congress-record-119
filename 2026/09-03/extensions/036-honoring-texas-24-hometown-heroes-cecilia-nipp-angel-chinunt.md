# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROES CECILIA NIPP, ANGEL CHINUNTDET, AND PHOEBE BUTLER OF URSULINE ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROES CECILIA NIPP, ANGEL CHINUNTDET, AND 
                   PHOEBE BUTLER OF URSULINE ACADEMY

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Heroes, Cecilia Nipp, Angel Chinuntdet, and Phoebe Butler, three 
educators from Dallas who are representing North Texas on one of the 
world's biggest stages. Cecilia Nipp, Angel Chinuntdet, and Phoebe 
Butler of Ursuline Academy were selected as FIFA World Cup 2026 Host 
City Ambassadors after a highly competitive process that drew more than 
33,000 applicants for just over 5,000 volunteer positions.
  Stationed at the FIFA Fan Festival at Fair Park, they are helping 
create a welcoming experience for visitors from around the world. For 
them, the event is about more than volunteering, it is a chance to 
share their love for the community and showcase the hospitality that 
defines North Texas. Their dedication to service extends beyond their 
professional roles as they inspire others through leadership, cultural 
connection, and community pride.
  I thank Cecilia, Angel, and Phoebe, for representing North Texas 
through their service, leadership, and warm welcome to fans from around 
the world.
```
