# HONORING UNITED CHRISTIAN FAITH MINISTRIES

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING UNITED CHRISTIAN FAITH MINISTRIES

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate a thriving 
ministry in my district, United Christian Faith Ministries in Baton 
Rouge, Louisiana, as it observes twenty-five years of worship, 
outreach, and service to the people of East Baton Rouge Parish.
  After relocating from Atlanta to Baton Rouge, Mark A. Ellis, Pastor 
and Founder, received the vision for United Christian Faith Ministries 
in June 2001. God instructed Pastor Ellis to reach out to the 
community, preach the Gospel, and teach the Word of God with simplicity 
and understanding. In August 2001, the ministry was formed with only 
seven members. United Christian Faith Ministries held its first worship 
service at the Ramada Inn, previously located in Baton Rouge, 
Louisiana.
  Over the years, God has blessed United Christian Faith Ministries 
tremendously and has allowed the ministry to touch many lives within 
this community. With over twenty-five ministries established, its focus 
continues to be on outreach, worship, fellowship, ministry, and 
service. Its membership continues to grow expediently. The ministry has 
grown from the seven members in 2001 to currently over two thousand 
members, and many souls have been added to the body of Christ. To God 
be the glory.
  The congregation yet remains dependent upon God and His infinite 
wisdom as it continues to build the kingdom of God and celebrate Jesus. 
The Lord is continually sharing His presence with United Christian 
Faith Ministries and, by His sovereign leadership, adding to this 
ministry daily. Great is God's faithfulness.
  In August of 2017, United Christian Faith Ministries cut the ribbon 
on the state-of-the-art United Center. This facility was built to house 
the many discipleship opportunities the ministry offers.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating United 
Christian Faith Ministries on the occasion of its twenty-fifth 
anniversary, and in wishing its pastor and congregation God's continued 
favor in the years ahead.
```
