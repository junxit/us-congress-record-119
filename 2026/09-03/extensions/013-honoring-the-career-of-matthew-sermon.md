# HONORING THE CAREER OF MATTHEW SERMON

**Speaking:** Courtney, Joe (C001069) [D-CT]

```
                 HONORING THE CAREER OF MATTHEW SERMON

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. JOE COURTNEY

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. COURTNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the distinguished 
career of Mr. Matthew Sermon, who is retiring today, Thursday, 
September 3, 2026, after nearly 30 years of stellar federal service.
  Mr. Sermon began his extraordinary career as a midshipman at the 
United States Naval Academy. After graduation from Annapolis, he was 
commissioned as a Surface Warfare Officer and nuclear engineering 
officer. During his time at sea, he was deployed to the Middle East 
during Operation Enduring Freedom and Operation Iraqi Freedom, 
accumulating an impeccable record in uniform.
  After discharging from active duty, Matt continued his public service 
with the Navy as a civilian in 2005, focused on revitalizing America's 
shipbuilding industrial base. He passionately understood that, for the 
sake of our maritime Nation, the decline of our shipbuilding workforce, 
infrastructure, and supply chain, which occurred in the wake of the end 
of the Cold War, needed to be reversed.
  In that cause, he has made critical contributions to build and 
support ships, amphibs, destroyers, and most specially submarines. 
Specifically, these contributions were made while holding various 
program manager and leadership roles in the Support Ships, Boats, and 
Craft Programs the International Fleet Support office, for the Zumwalt 
Class Destroyer, and for the Columbia Class Submarine program. Within 
these positions, Mr. Sermon managed billions of dollars in funding and 
hundreds of personnel, all of which was guided by his commitment to the 
technological advancement and unilateral preeminence of our Nation's 
Navy.
  Mr. Speaker, Matt Sermon is a household name within Eastern 
Connecticut's substantial submarine industrial base. Starting in 2019, 
as Congress increased funding for the undersea fleet, he managed 
investment in our submarine suppliers, workforce, and shipyards.
  Year in and year out, over my 12 years as Chairman and/or Ranking 
Member of the Seapower and Projection Forces subcommittee, Matt has 
been a most trusted partner--and leader--in executing Congress' 
constitutional duty to ``provide and maintain a Navy''.
  Mr. Sermon completes his more than two decades of civil service as 
the Executive Director for the Director Reporting Portfolio Manager, 
Submarines, or DRPMI Subs. His work, alongside Vice Admiral Gaucher, is 
focused on taking the production of submarine tonnage to record high 
levels. His dedication and service are admirable and deserving of 
special recognition. As his time in public service draws to a close, I 
want to personally attest to the fact that tens of thousands of new, 
young, energetic metal trades workers, designers, and engineers are 
today enjoying life-changing careers because of Matt's devoted, 
talented leadership.
  I ask that the House join me in celebrating the career of Mr. Matthew 
Sermon, and in wishing all the best to his family.
```
