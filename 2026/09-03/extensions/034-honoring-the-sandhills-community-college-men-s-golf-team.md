# HONORING THE SANDHILLS COMMUNITY COLLEGE MEN'S GOLF TEAM

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
        HONORING THE SANDHILLS COMMUNITY COLLEGE MEN'S GOLF TEAM

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Sandhills 
Community College men's golf team for capturing their sixth consecutive 
NJCAA Division III National Championship.
  On June 5, 2026, at Chautauqua Golf Club, the Sandhills Flyers 
secured another championship on the national stage, beating the runner 
up by 42 strokes.
  This tremendous achievement is a testament to the team's unity, 
discipline, and outstanding work ethic. I would like to especially 
congratulate Sebastian Haug, Kip Melen, Borden Beaver, and Trace 
Wallace for all finishing inside the top 13, individually earning NJCAA 
All-American honors.
  I know I speak for our whole community in offering my most heartfelt 
congratulations to the entire championship team. They have all made our 
community proud.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today honoring the Sandhills Flyers.

                          ____________________
```
