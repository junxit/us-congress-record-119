# CONGRATULATING WEST LAMPETER TOWNSHIP POLICE CHIEF BRIAN WICZKOWSKI ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
CONGRATULATING WEST LAMPETER TOWNSHIP POLICE CHIEF BRIAN WICZKOWSKI ON 
                             HIS RETIREMENT

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I am pleased to recognize and congratulate 
Brian Wiczkowski on his retirement as Police Chief for West Lampeter 
Township after 36 years in law enforcement.
  After graduating from Millersville University with a degree in 
secondary education, Brian Wiczkowski joined the Lancaster City Police 
Department in 1990. In his time with Lancaster City, he served in the 
Patrol Division, the Criminal Investigation Division, and was an 
original member of the Community Policing Program, and he led those 
efforts when the city established its own Community Policing Department 
in 1999. He also worked closely with the county's Behavioral Health/
Disabilities Service to assign a Crisis Intervention employee to the 
department.
  After advancing through the ranks, Brian was promoted to Captain of 
the Patrol Division in 2008 before accepting the position of Chief of 
Police in West Lampeter in 2014. As chief, he led the West Lampeter 
Township Police Department to become fully accredited through the 
Pennsylvania Law Enforcement Accreditation Program helped implement the 
School Resource Officer position in Lampeter-Strasburg School District, 
and established a co-responder program to assist those suffering from 
drug addiction and mental health issues.
  After 36 years in law enforcement, Chief Wiczkowski ends a storied 
career of positive impact and service to the local community. I thank 
Chief Wiczkowski for his service and wish him the best in retirement.
```
