# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ANQUINETTE HILL

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ANQUINETTE HILL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Arbor Creek Elementary School teacher, Anquinette Hill. Mrs. Hill 
was recently named Region 11 Elementary Teacher of the Year.
  Mrs. Hill has dedicated over 16 years to teaching, pouring into 
students and staff alike. She is known for always having a smile, and 
going above and beyond to provide new STEAM-based projects and 
opportunities to students.
  Nominees submitted a professional portfolio that included a resume, 
professional biography, and essays describing their instructional 
philosophy, contributions to school culture, community engagement, 
leadership experiences, and vision for the teaching profession. This 
award puts Mrs. Hill in the running for the State of Texas Teacher of 
the Year, which will be announced within the coming weeks.
  I congratulate Mrs. Hill on her recognition and for serving students 
across North Texas.

                          ____________________
```
