# HONORING CALEIGH CONLIN

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
                        HONORING CALEIGH CONLIN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. RICHARD HUDSON --

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Caleigh Conlin for 
winning the American Heroes Student Art Contest.
  Caleigh represented North Carolina as the high school division winner 
for her piece ``Carrollton's Ride.'' Her artwork honors the Founding 
Father, Charles Carroll of Carrollton. Caleigh chose to depict Charles 
Carroll to celebrate America 250 because of the religious connection 
she shares with Carroll, who was the only Catholic to sign the 
Declaration of Independence.
  This tremendous achievement is not her first competition win. She has 
entered various local competitions, trained under local artists, and 
two years ago won the Cambell House Youth Art Contest. I am excited to 
see how she will continue to succeed in the future.
  I know I speak for our entire community in offering my most heartfelt 
congratulations to Caleigh Conlin. She is an exceptional artist and has 
made our community proud.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today celebrating Caleigh Conlin.

                          ____________________
```
