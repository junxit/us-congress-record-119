# HONORING PAM WILSON ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

```
                 HONORING PAM WILSON ON HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARCY KAPTUR

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate Pam 
Wilson on the occasion of her retirement. As the Senior Vice President 
of Long-Term Care at the Area Office on Aging of Northwestern Ohio, Pam 
has lovingly and dutifully served older adults in Northwest Ohio for 
over four decades.
  Tasked with connecting older adults to the care services they need, 
Pam took her role in stride, always going the extra mile for those who 
leaned on her. Most remarkably, she secured home care services for 
nearly 3,000 older adults annually across a ten-county area in 
Northwest Ohio. These are individuals who qualify for nursing home care 
but were instead able to remain in the comfort and safety of their own 
homes. Through these services, Pam provided a sense of security and 
dignity to countless seniors, along with their families and caregivers. 
The program represented one of the most extraordinary and successful 
Medicaid Waiver home care programs in the entire region.
  A true wizard on the complexities of Medicare and Medicaid, Pam 
expertly navigated the systems to secure the care necessary for 
numerous individuals. She was pivotal in establishing and negotiating 
contracts with several managed care companies to ensure all recipients 
were cared for to the highest level. Her service did not end with our 
region's seniors--Pam was also a tremendous mentor to hundreds of 
nurses and social workers, and she provided support services to 
countless caregivers. She organized and led the first-ever Caregiver 
Support Program, creating a safe space and sense of relief for a group 
that is often overlooked. Her leadership in establishing high standards 
and ethical practices had a profound impact on the many nurses and 
social workers she trained. That impact that will only continue to grow 
as her mentees continue in the field of aging.
  Pam's quiet capability, calm assurance, and caring heart, along with 
her professional excellence, made her a well-respected member of the 
aging network right from the start. Her legacy is carried forth in 
policy, precedence, and leadership throughout gerontology and among her 
colleagues and our community.
  Please join me and our entire Northwest Ohio community in thanking 
Pam Wilson for her tireless service and in wishing her well in 
retirement. Pam's steadfast care and immense knowledge will be sorely 
missed by all she worked alongside and all whose lives she has touched. 
May her retirement bring her the same joy and peace that she brought to 
so many others during her time with the Area Office on Aging.
```
