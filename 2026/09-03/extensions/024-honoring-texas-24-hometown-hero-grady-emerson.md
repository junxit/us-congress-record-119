# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO GRADY EMERSON

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO GRADY EMERSON

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Fort Worth Christian's Grady Emerson, who was the second overall 
pick in the 2026 MLB Draft, selected by the Tampa Bay Rays. During an 
impressive senior season, he was named Gatorade National Player of the 
Year and Baseball America's High School Player of the Year, leading 
Fort Worth Christian to the TAPPS Division II state championship game. 
He became the fourth Dallas-Fort Worth area high school player selected 
in the top 10 of the MLB Draft this century. Additionally, he became 
the second high school player in the history of USA Baseball's Golden 
Spikes Award to be named a semifinalist, joining MLB superstar Bobby 
Witt, Jr.
  Grady Emerson also made history as the first player to compete on 
multiple USA Baseball 15U and 18U National Teams, earning three gold 
medals. Recognized for his exceptional hitting, athleticism, and 
overall skill, he is regarded as one of the top high school prospects 
in recent years
  I congratulate Grady. North Texas is cheering him on in his next 
chapter.

                          ____________________
```
