# HONORING THE TERRY SANFORD HIGH SCHOOL GOLF TEAM

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
            HONORING THE TERRY SANFORD HIGH SCHOOL GOLF TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 3, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Terry Sanford High 
School Golf Team on winning the 2026 Professional Golf Association 
(PGA) National High School Golf Invitational in Pinehurst, North 
Carolina.
  Terry Sanford High School competed against every No. 1 ranked team 
from all 50 states and territories to accomplish this tremendous feat. 
After combining for a team score of 22 shots under par through two 
rounds, Terry Sanford prevailed victorious 12 strokes ahead of the 
second-place team. This is the first North Carolina team in tournament 
history to be crowned national champions.
  This tremendous achievement is a testament to the team's unity, 
discipline, and outstanding work ethic. I know I speak for our entire 
community in offering my most heartfelt congratulations to the entire 
team. They have all made our community proud.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in celebrating the Terry Sanford 
High School Golf Team.
```
