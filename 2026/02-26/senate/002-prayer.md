# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God of time and eternity, we come to You not because we are 
perfect, but because we also need Your mercy, wisdom, and love. By Your 
grace, we are able to triumph over evil, living no longer for ourselves 
alone, but for You.
  Give our Senators a vision of the goal that produces righteousness, 
unity, and justice. Use our lawmakers to bring understanding, peace, 
and patience to Capitol Hill. Empower them to serve the less fortunate, 
to bear the burdens of freedom, and to labor for Your glory.
  And, Lord, bless those who are going through this season of lent. We 
pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
