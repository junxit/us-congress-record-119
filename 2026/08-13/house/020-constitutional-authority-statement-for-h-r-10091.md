# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10091

**Measures:** H.R. 10091

```
           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 10091.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 (the Postal Clause, 
     granting the power `To establish Post Offices and post 
     Roads') and Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (the Commerce 
     Clause, granting the power to regulate commerce among the 
     several States) of the United States Constitution.
```
