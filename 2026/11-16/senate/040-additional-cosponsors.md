                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1705

  At the request of Mr. Cotton, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Ms. Blunt Rochester) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1705, a bill to 
require the Secretary of Commerce to issue standards with respect to 
chip security mechanisms for integrated circuit products, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 1748

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the name of the Senator from Oregon 
(Mr. Merkley) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1748, a bill to protect 
the safety of children on the internet.


                                S. 1866

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the name of the Senator from Arizona 
(Mr. Kelly) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1866, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to reauthorize and improve the National 
Breast and Cervical Cancer Early Detection Program for fiscal years 
2026 through 2030, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2195

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. Fetterman) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2195, a 
bill to award a Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to the brave 
women who served in World War II as members of the U.S. Army Nurse 
Corps and U.S. Navy Nurse Corps.


                                S. 2937

  At the request of Mr. Durbin, the name of the Senator from Virginia 
(Mr. Kaine) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2937, a bill to establish 
legal standards for advanced artificial intelligence products.


                                S. 3492

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the names of the Senator from 
Delaware (Mr. Coons), the Senator from Colorado (Mr. Bennet), the 
Senator from Oregon (Mr. Merkley), the Senator from Delaware (Ms. Blunt 
Rochester), the Senator from Maryland (Mr. Van Hollen), the Senator 
from New Mexico (Mr. Heinrich) and the Senator from Colorado (Mr. 
Hickenlooper) were added as cosponsors of S. 3492, a bill to amend 
titles XVIII and XIX of the Social Security Act to require skilled 
nursing facilities, nursing facilities, intermediate care facilities 
for the intellectually disabled, and inpatient rehabilitation 
facilities to permit essential caregivers access during any period in 
which regular visitation is restricted.


                                S. 4709

  At the request of Mr. Ricketts, the name of the Senator from 
California (Mr. Padilla) was added as a cosponsor

  At the request of Mr. Kim, the name of the Senator from Delaware (Ms. 
Blunt Rochester) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5037, a bill to amend 
title XIX of the Social Security Act to make all children eligible for 
Medicaid from birth until age 26, to require States to automatically 
enroll children and young adults under age 26 in the State Medicaid 
program, and for other purposes.

States Code, to improve the maintenance, alteration, and construction

                                S. 5390

  At the request of Mr. Schumer, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Ms. Blunt Rochester) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5390, a bill to 
terminate and refund certain duties imposed by the President, and for 
other purposes.


                                S. 5497

  At the request of Mr. King, the name of the Senator from Pennsylvania 
(Mr. Fetterman) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5497, a bill to amend 
title 38, United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Veterans 
Affairs to reimburse State homes for the cost of, or to furnish to 
State homes, certain costly medications provided to veterans who 
receive nursing home care in such State homes, and for other purposes.


                                S. 5593

  At the request of Mr. Schmitt, the names of the Senator from Montana 
(Mr. Sheehy) and the Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Budd) were added 
as cosponsors of S. 5593, a bill to prevent the removal of children for 
sex-rejecting interventions.


                                S. 5600

  At the request of Mr. Kim, the name of the Senator from California 
(Mr. Padilla) was added as a cosponsor of S. 5600, a bill to 
reauthorize the Traumatic Brain Injury program.


                                S. 5608

  At the request of Mr. Crapo, the names of the Senator from Alaska 
(Ms. Murkowski), the Senator from Alaska (Mr. Sullivan) and the Senator
