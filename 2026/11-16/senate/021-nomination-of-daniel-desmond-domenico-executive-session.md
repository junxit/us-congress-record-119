# Nomination of Daniel Desmond Domenico (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Markey, Edward J. (M000133) [D-MA]

```
                 Nomination of Daniel Desmond Domenico

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, today, the Senate will vote to confirm 
Daniel Domenico to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit.

[[Page S5679]]

  Judge Domenico's record rubberstamping President Trump's unlawful 
mass deportation policies as a district court judge makes him unfit for 
the Tenth Circuit. I have no reason to think he will set aside his 
blind loyalty to the President if confirmed.
  Prior to his hearing, Judge Domenico had ruled in favor of the Trump 
administration's controversial mandatory detention policy 100 percent 
of the time. He is an outlier not only in the District of Colorado, but 
also among judges across the country.
  In at least 26,200 cases in which Federal district judges have ruled 
on ICE's mandatory detention policy, more than 21,950 were rulings 
against the Trump administration, including the vast majority of 
rulings by Trump-appointed judges.
  Judge Domenico's willingness to greenlight the President's harmful 
immigration policies is alarming as this administration continues to 
undermine the rights and safety of immigrants.
  I will oppose his nomination, and I urge my colleagues to do the 
same.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Oklahoma.
```
