# TRIBUTE TO SHERIFF MIKE MILSTEAD

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO SHERIFF MIKE MILSTEAD

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I have the privilege of recognizing 
Sheriff Mike Milstead, who will soon retire from his position as 
Minnehaha County Sheriff in South Dakota. Mike's outstanding leadership 
and unwavering dedication to protecting his community have strengthened 
public safety and made a lasting impact across Minnehaha County.
  Mike was born and raised in our great State of South Dakota. The son 
of highway patrolman Jim Milstead, Mike grew up in a law enforcement 
family. He and his wife Rhonda have two children and make their home in 
Sioux Falls, SD.
  Mike has spent his life serving South Dakotans, starting his public 
service career very young. He joined the Sioux Falls Police Explorer 
Program as a ninth grader in 1969, and he began his career with the 
Sioux Falls Police Department in 1973. After 24 years of service, he 
was elected as Minnehaha County sheriff in 1997. Voters have reelected 
him six times without opposition, allowing him to serve in this role 
for nearly 30 years. As Minnehaha County sheriff, Mike has led an 
operation that serves seven South Dakota cities, the South Dakota Air 
National Guard Base, and the Minnehaha County Jail. The sheriff's 
department employs over 240 staff members and provides full-service 
patrol, investigations, warrant, and civil divisions. While in office, 
Mike established a Chaplaincy Program, School Resource Officer Program, 
and South Dakota Salutes--a nonprofit supporting first responders, 
Active-Duty military personnel, and veterans.
  Mike has served on the executive board of the Midwest High Intensity 
Drug Trafficking Area since 2003 and is the longest serving member in 
the organization's history. While working on the executive board, he 
has also served as vice chair, chair of the fiscal planning committee, 
and chair of the executive board. Additionally, Mike served on the 
National Sheriff Association's Drug Enforcement and Homeland Security/
Global Policing Affairs committees and is active with the U.S. 
Attorney's Anti-Terrorism Task Force and the FBI's Joint Terrorism Task 
Force.
  Through his many responsibilities, Mike has focused on building 
strong relationships between the sheriff's department and the 
communities it serves. His distinguished law enforcement career clearly 
shows his commitment to public service and his dedication to serving 
and protecting his neighbors.
  As Mike embarks on a well-earned retirement, I want to extend my 
heartfelt gratitude for his leadership and commitment to South Dakota. 
While he may be stepping away from the daily demands of the sheriff's 
office, the programs he established, partnerships he strengthened, and 
lives he touched will leave a legacy throughout Minnehaha County and 
South Dakota. I pray that God blesses Mike, Rhonda, and their family in 
the days ahead.

                          ____________________
```
