# SNAP (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  SNAP

  Madam President, finally, on SNAP cuts, Trump just won't let up on 
his campaign to make it harder for American families to feed 
themselves. That is so sickening, so troublesome, so mean.
  Last week, the Trump administration proposed a new rule that would 
kick more than 6 million Americans off food stamps and stop nearly 2 
million children--children--from getting free school lunch meals. What 
the Trump administration is doing is rationing, not reform.
  Keep in mind, Trump already booted 5.5 million Americans off of food 
assistance to pay for tax cuts for himself and his fellow billionaires.

[[Page S5675]]

  This is the administration in a nutshell: empty promises on 
affordability, empty dinner plates in homes across the country, and 
empty lunch trays for millions and millions of kids.
  Groceries cost more than ever because of Trump's disastrous war and 
his costly tariffs, but Trump somehow thinks that now is the optimal 
time to pull the rug out from vulnerable families. The people most at 
risk of losing these vital food benefits thanks to this new rule are 
working families, many with children; seniors; and people with 
disabilities.
  As we begin this holiday season, we should be making it easier for 
families to put food on the table, not harder. Trump could ease 
families' anguish at the grocery store with the snap of his fingers. 
Instead, he is telling Americans: Tighten your belts.
  Senate Democrats are going to fight to help ensure no family has to 
pinch pennies to feed their families. We are going to fight Trump's 
food cuts, Trumps cuts to food stamps, his tariffs, his war, and all 
other policies that are stretching Americans thin at the grocery store.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Mississippi.
```
