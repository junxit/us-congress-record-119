# TRIBUTE TO JOHN HEILMAN

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JOHN HEILMAN

● Mr. SCHIFF. Mr. President. I rise today with my colleague 
Senator Alex Padilla to pay tribute to Mayor John Heilman in 
recognition of his extraordinary four decades of public service and 
leadership to the city of West Hollywood.
  Mayor Heilman has been an integral part of West Hollywood since its 
very beginning. He was active in the movement to incorporate the city 
and was elected to its first city council in November 1984 and was 
selected as West Hollywood's second mayor in 1985. Since that time, he 
has served numerous terms as mayor, including in 1990, 1995, 1999, 
2001, 2006, 2010, 2017, and most recently in 2026. Mayor Heilman is one 
of the longest serving openly gay elected officials in the United 
States. In December 2025, he was inducted into the Victory Fund's 
LGBTQ+ Political Hall of Fame in recognition of four decades of 
leadership that helped establish West Hollywood as a global center for 
LGBTQ+ rights and equality.
  Throughout his tenure, Mayor Heilman has been an advocate for 
affordable housing and tenant protections. Mr. Heilman helped to 
develop the city's landmark rent stabilization ordinance and its 
inclusionary housing policy, making sure that new residential 
developments include housing opportunities for low- and middle-income 
residents. Additionally, he cofounded the West Hollywood Community 
Housing Corporation, which develops affordable housing in West 
Hollywood and surrounding neighborhoods.
  Mayor Heilman's steadfast leadership has been central to West 
Hollywood's longstanding commitment to civil rights and equality. He 
drafted the city's landmark ordinance prohibiting HIV and AIDS 
discrimination and received the U.S. Conference of Mayors' Award for 
his leadership during the AIDS crisis. He also established the senior 
advisory board, the Russian-speaking advisory board, and the city's 
annual women's leadership conference, ensuring that residents from 
across the community have a voice in local government.
  His impact can also be seen throughout the physical and economic 
landscape of West Hollywood. Mayor Heilman championed the development 
of the West Hollywood Library, which opened in 2011, and has worked 
closely with the city's business community throughout his tenure. He 
was a founder of what is now Visit West Hollywood and helped establish 
business improvement districts serving both the iconic Sunset Strip and 
the city's Design District.
  Beyond his service to the city, Mayor Heilman has remained committed 
to advocacy and education. He serves on the board of Outright 
International, which advances the rights of LGBTQ+ people around the 
world, and the Jacaranda Foundation, which supports children orphaned 
by AIDS in Malawi. As an educator for nearly four decades, he has 
taught many generations of future attorneys at Southwestern Law School, 
University of Southern California Gould School of Law, Whittier Law 
School, and University of California, Los Angeles School of Law.
  I ask all Members to join me and Senator Padilla in recognizing Mayor 
John Heilman for more than four decades of distinguished public service 
and his unwavering commitment to equality for all.●
```
