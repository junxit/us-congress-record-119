# Federal Aviation Administration (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    Federal Aviation Administration

  Madam President, on the nationwide FAA outage, we just saw only the 
third nationwide FAA outage in American history, and we still haven't 
received any clear answer as to how it happened.
  Millions of Americans were deterred. Hundreds of thousands of 
Americans slept on airport floors over the weekend--we also have the 
pictures--because the Trump administration has been asleep at the 
wheel.
  Trump is responding to this disaster the same way he does to every 
other disaster. He just points fingers away from himself.
  This morning, Trump said this about the 17,000 flights canceled on 
his watch this weekend:

       I don't take any responsibility.

  What? So much for ``the buck stops here.'' Apparently, in this 
administration, the buck doesn't stop in the Oval Office; it gets 
rebooked, rerouted, and sent as far away from Trump as possible.
  Trump is now asking for $50 billion, despite sitting on most of the 
$12.5 billion that Congress gave him last year--so typical of this 
incompetent administration. They have money. They don't use it. And 
then they come back and say: Give us more money.
  Well, we want to give them all the money they need, but they have to 
use it and know how to implement it, and they don't.
  Mr. President Trump, what happened to the $12.5 billion we gave you, 
even though you said you need more money?
  If Trump spent a fraction of the attention and resources he devotes 
to his personal vanity projects on ensuring that Americans can fly 
safely and reliably, this outage might never have happened.
  If this administration refuses to take responsibility, Congress has 
to push for accountability through hearings, investigations, and every 
tool we have at our disposal.
```
