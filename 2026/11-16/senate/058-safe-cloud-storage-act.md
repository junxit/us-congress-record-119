# SAFE CLOUD STORAGE ACT

**Speaking:** Ricketts, Pete (R000618) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 7834

```
                         SAFE CLOUD STORAGE ACT

  Mr. RICKETTS. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
immediate consideration of H.R. 7834, which was received from the 
House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 7834) to limit liability for certain entities 
     storing child sexual abuse material for law enforcement 
     agencies, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. RICKETTS. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered 
read a third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 7834) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
