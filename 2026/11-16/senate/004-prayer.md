# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal and gracious God, You have taught us that Your Will for our 
lives is to give thanks in every circumstance. Continue to fill our 
lawmakers with a spirit of gratitude, reminding them that thanksgiving 
is not dependent upon favorable circumstances but flows from the hearts 
that recognize Your goodness and grace.
  May they regard the privilege of belonging to Your family as one of 
life's greatest blessings and a continual reason for gratitude. May 
this privilege inspire them to serve others with humility, lead with 
compassion, and conduct themselves with integrity.
  With everything that is within us, may we bless Your Holy Name, 
acknowledge Your abundant goodness, and live each day with hearts 
overflowing with gratitude.
  We pray in Your mighty and matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
