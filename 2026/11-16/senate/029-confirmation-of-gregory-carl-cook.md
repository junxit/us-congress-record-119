# CONFIRMATION OF GREGORY CARL COOK

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                   CONFIRMATION OF GREGORY CARL COOK

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, last week, the Senate voted to confirm 
Gregory Carl Cook to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District 
of Alabama.
  While campaigning for his seat on the Alabama Supreme Court, Justice 
Cook went the extra mile to highlight his hyperpartisan, conservative 
credentials. He repeatedly identified himself as, ``Trump-Tough.''
  Following the results of the 2024 election, he expressed ``wonderful 
relief'' and said, ``the great national nightmare is over.''
  In notes from another campaign speech, Justice Cook wrote in all 
caps, ``ANY REPUBLICAN IS BETTER THAN ANY DEMOCRAT.''
  I asked him what he meant by these statements. Like other Trump 
nominees who have been asked to account for their hyperpartisan 
language, Justice Cook deflected and claimed his statements were taken 
out of context.
  That is cold comfort to litigants who have different political views 
than Justice Cook. They have no reason to believe that this nominee 
will treat them fairly.
  For those reasons, I oppose Justice Cook's nomination to the Federal 
bench.

                          ____________________
```
