# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 5719, S. 5720, S. 5721, S. 5722, S. 5723

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. WICKER (for himself, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Schmitt, 
             and Mr. Coons):
       S. 5719. A bill to amend title XIX of the Social Security 
     Act to clarify that whole genome and whole exome sequencing 
     for children with certain medical needs is covered under the 
     Medicaid program; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. BLUMENTHAL (for himself, Mrs. Britt, and Mr. 
             Booker):
       S. 5720. A bill to amend title 9 of the United States Code 
     with respect to arbitration; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Mr. WARNER:
       S. 5721. A bill to require Federal agency heads to submit 
     an annual assessment on staffing needs of information 
     technology contracting officers and contracting officer's 
     representatives, to require the heads to submit a plan on 
     information technology acquisition workforce staffing and 
     development using the assessments, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND (for herself, Mr. Merkley, Ms. 
             Rosen, Mr. King, Mr. Wyden, Ms. Blunt Rochester, Mr. 
             Reed, Mr. Coons, Mr. Durbin, Mr. Kaine, Mrs. Murray, 
             Mr. Peters, Mr. Booker, Ms. Cantwell, Ms. Duckworth, 
             and Mr. Gallego):
       S. 5722. A bill to amend the definition of professional 
     degree in the Higher Education Act of 1965; to the Committee 
     on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. HAWLEY (for himself and Mr. Durbin):
       S. 5723. A bill to amend title 9, United States Code, with 
     respect to arbitration of disputes involving child cyber 
     injuries and child sexual exploitation; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
