# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2026

**Speaking:** Ricketts, Pete (R000618) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 2347

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2026

  Mr. RICKETTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it

[[Page S5702]]

stand adjourned until 10 a.m. on Tuesday, November 17; that following 
the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the date, morning business be closed, 
and the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 2347; further, that the 
Senate recess from 12:30 until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly 
conference meetings; and that at 2:15 p.m., the Senate proceed to 
executive session to execute the order in relation to the St. John 
nomination; that following disposition of the nomination, the Senate 
resume legislative session; finally, that all time during the Senate 
recess, morning business, and leader remarks count postcloture on 
substitute amendment No. 6838.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
