# AI Chips (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                AI Chips

  Madam President, now, on closing the AI chip loophole, the Trump 
administration might finally--finally--be waking up to the absolute 
mess they created by giving the Chinese Communist Party greater access 
to our most advanced AI chips. The rule the Department of Commerce 
announced today could be a meaningful step toward countering AI chip 
smuggling and limiting China's remote access to this critical 
technology--but only if the rule is enforced.
  Even if the administration decides to enforce the new rule, it still, 
of course, has critical gaps. The only way to close these loopholes and 
close them for good, once and for all, is for Congress to pass the AI 
OVERWATCH Act, the MATCH Act, and the Chip Security Act, as well as 
other bipartisan legislation--this is all bipartisan--to stop China 
from getting our tech.
  My colleagues and I have been advocating for these bills and pushing 
Trump to get tougher on Chinese export controls for years. This 
administration has shown time and time again that it is completely 
disinterested in enforcing export controls.
  Instead of protecting our national security, Howard Lutnick is trying 
to protect the bottom line of his family business, which has billions 
of dollars tied up in some of these foreign data center projects.
  Let's not forget that Trump holds tens of millions of dollars in 
Nvidia stock and stands to make immense profits if he lets China 
continue access to these chips, despite the huge damage it does to our 
country in the short and long term. Even now, Trump is still making 
excuses for Jensen Huang after his salacious espionage scandal.
  That is right, America. Both Trump and Lutnick have a vested 
financial interest in selling out our national security to China.
  Talk about the kind of corruption that America is talking about from 
one end of the country to the other and is so disgusted with this 
administration about. Here is another glaring, glaring example. Trump 
and Lutnick continue to protect their billionaire buddies, even after 
giving away the store to the Chinese military and hurting our national 
security in a very, very harmful way.
  Now, Trump is off on another trip to China to meet with President Xi. 
Will Trump be tough with Xi on these major national security concerns? 
I don't hold my breath. If past is prologue, all he wants is another 
photo op, and he says something that is meaningless or even in direct 
contradiction to what happened at the meeting.
  Congress needs to stop Trump from taking our country in this 
dangerous direction. We must--we must--pass bipartisan export control 
bills. And, crucially, we need to bring Jensen Huang and top Trump 
administration officials before Congress to answer why these export 
controls were loosened in the first place.
```
