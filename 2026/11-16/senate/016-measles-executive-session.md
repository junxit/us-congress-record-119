# Measles (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                Measles

  Madam President, today, there is so much bad news because of the 
incompetence and the corruption of this administration.
  On losing the measles elimination status, it took Trump and R.F.K. 
less than 2 years to undo the decades of work that went into 
eliminating measles in America. Over the weekend, it was announced the 
United States lost its measles elimination status, a public health 
achievement that we were so proud to have secured over two decades ago. 
The resurgence of this dangerous virus is a direct consequence of the 
pathological stupidity plaguing this administration.
  Measles cases in the United States hit a 35-year high in 2026, and 
this year isn't even over yet. That is a disgrace, something we should 
be ashamed of.
  Measles cases are increasing. The danger to kids is increasing. 
Thousands are falling ill and some are even dying because the Trump 
administration undermined vaccine and public health infrastructure, 
spewed vaccine misinformation, purged doctors and public health experts 
responsible for combating this disease, and cut billions of dollars off 
in public health grants.
  The Trump administration has traded in all of the miracles of modern 
medicine for conspiracy theories and snake oil remedies, and the 
American people are paying for Trump's recklessness with their lives. 
The cruelty of this administration is on full display.
  Trump and R.F.K., Jr., weren't content with kicking millions of 
Americans off their health insurance to pay for tax cuts for 
billionaires. Now, they are letting preventable disease spread, and 
Americans are more vulnerable than ever.
  The Trump administration will have to answer to Congress and the 
American people about how it could let this public health crisis get so 
out of hand.
```
