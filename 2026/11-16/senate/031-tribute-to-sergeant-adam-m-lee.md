# TRIBUTE TO SERGEANT ADAM M. LEE

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                    TRIBUTE TO SERGEANT ADAM M. LEE

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, today I have the honor to recognize 
SGT Adam M. Lee as Montanan of the Month.
  Adam serves as a combat medic specialist with the Montana National 
Guard. Back in March, while was volunteering as a Life Flight paramedic 
in California, where his training and dedication to his duties were 
exemplified in a critical moment.
  When two hikers fell hundreds of feet from a trail near Tahquitz Rock 
in Idyllwild, Adam came to the rescue. Entering hazardous terrain in 
subfreezing temperatures, Adam and several rescue members administered 
aid and protected the injured hikers for days in the worsening weather.
  Adam, along with other rescuers, eventually evacuated the hikers and 
brought them to safety. Adam is commended for his exemplary performance 
of qualities of Montana guardsmen and embodying the command of ``The 
Montana Way.'' SGT Adam M. Lee received the Soldier's Medal during a 
ceremony at the Belgrade Armory in August for his heroic actions and 
saving the lives of the critically injured hikers. Lee continues to 
serve both his State and community and is pursuing a career as a 
physician assistant.
  I am thrilled to recognize SGT Adam M. Lee for his dedication to 
training and preparation, his heroism, and his selfless actions to help 
protect and defend our citizens with the utmost dedication.●
```
