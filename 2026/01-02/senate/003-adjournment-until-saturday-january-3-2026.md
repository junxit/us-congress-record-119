# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL SATURDAY, JANUARY 3, 2026

```
              ADJOURNMENT UNTIL SATURDAY, JANUARY 3, 2026

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 12 noon on Saturday, January 3, 2026 and 
will reconvene for the 2nd session of the 119th Congress.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 3 and 40 seconds p.m., adjourned until 
Saturday, January 3, 2026, at 12 noon.
```
