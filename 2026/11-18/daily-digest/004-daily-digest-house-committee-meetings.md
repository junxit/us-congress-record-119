WORLDWIDE THREATS TO THE HOMELAND: 25 YEARS AFTER 9/11
Committee on Homeland Security: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Worldwide Threats to the Homeland: 25 Years After 9/11''. Testimony 
was heard from Markwayne Mullin, Secretary, Department of Homeland 
Security; Jay Clayton, Director of National Intelligence, Office of the 
Director of National Intelligence; and Christopher G. Raia, Deputy 
Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation, Department of Justice.

OVERSIGHT OF THE BUREAU OF ALCOHOL, TOBACCO, FIREARMS AND EXPLOSIVES' 
OUT-OF-BUSINESS RECORDS RETENTION PRACTICES
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Subcommittee on Federal 
Law Enforcement held a hearing entitled ``Oversight of the Bureau of 
Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives' Out-of-Business Records 
Retention Practices''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
PROTECTING MAIN STREET, SECURING AMERICA: EXAMINING CYBERSECURITY 
CHALLENGES FOR SMALL BUSINESS CONTRACTOR
Committee on Small Business: Subcommittee on Contracting and 
Infrastructure held a hearing entitled ``Protecting Main Street, 
Securing America: Examining Cybersecurity Challenges for Small Business 
Contractors''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
PSYCHEDELIC-ASSISTED THERAPIES FOR VETERANS: THE PATH FORWARD
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``Psychedelic-Assisted Therapies for Veterans: The Path 
Forward''. Testimony was heard from Andrew Gradison, Senior Advisor, 
Veterans Health Administration, Department of Veterans Affairs; and 
public witnesses.
