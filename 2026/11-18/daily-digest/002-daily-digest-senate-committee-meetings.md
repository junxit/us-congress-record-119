# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 69, H.R. 226, S. 2586, S. 2883, H.R. 2916, S. 3301, S. 3475, S. 3573, S. 3741, S. 3859, S. 3954, S. 4053, S. 4256, H.R. 4463, S. 5251, S. 5332, S. 5508, S. 5663, H.R. 5682, S. 5718

Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
SOCIAL SECURITY
Committee on the Budget: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
reality of Social Security, after receiving testimony from Veronique de 
Rugy, George Mason University Mercatus Center, Arlington, Virginia; and 
Jason J. Fichtner, LIMRA Retirement Income Institute, and Kathryn Anne 
Edwards, National Academy of Social Insurance, both of Washington, D.C.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee ordered 
favorably reported the following business items:
  S. 69, to amend the Communications Act of 1934 to address 
governmental interference in content moderation decisions by providers 
of interactive computer services, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  S. 2586, to require the Administrator of the National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration to establish an assessment program for 
commercial-scale offshore aquaculture through demonstration projects, 
to establish Aquaculture Centers of Excellence, to support aquaculture 
workforce development and working waterfronts, with an amendment in the 
nature of a substitute;
  S. 2883, to establish an East Coast Bivalve Research Task Force, with 
an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 3301, to prohibit purchases of certain semiconductor manufacturing 
equipment from foreign entities of concern or subsidiaries of foreign 
entities of concern, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 3741, to require the Secretary of Commerce to promulgate 
regulations to improve nucleic acid synthesis security, with an 
amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 3954, to terminate the ability of eligible telecommunications 
carriers in certain States to use State eligibility determination 
processes in place of the National Verifier to determine the 
eligibility of consumers for Lifeline service, with an amendment in the 
nature of a substitute;
  S. 4256, to amend title 49, United States Code, to establish 
acceptable formats for presentation of airman certificates to the 
Federal Aviation Administration, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  S. 5251, to modify the termination of the aviation consumer 
protection advisory committee;
  S. 5332, to direct the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
Administration to maintain the Cooperative Institute Program, with an 
amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 5508, to establish a public-private working group to develop 
cybersecurity best practices for telecommunications carriers and 
related supply chain participants, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  S. 5663, to establish a program at the National Science Foundation to 
fund research awards to reproduce federally funded research, with an 
amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 5718, to amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to 
restroom access for certain drivers, with an amendment in the nature of 
a substitute; and
  The nominations of Leslie Becera, of Virginia, to be an Assistant 
Secretary of Transportation, Danielle Thumann Severs, of North 
Carolina, to be a Member of the Federal Communications Commission, 
Joshua Shoemaker, of Virginia, to be Chief Financial Officer, and 
Robert Steinau, of Maryland, to be Inspector General, both of the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and a promotion list in 
the Coast Guard.

VA ACCOUNTABILITY AND TRANSPARENCY
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine VA accountability and transparency, focusing on examining the 
current and future path of VA's electronic health record modernization 
program, after receiving testimony from Neil C. Evans, Program 
Executive Director, Electronic Health Record Modernization, and Chief 
Officer, Office of Connected Care, Department of Veterans Affairs; 
Carol C. Harris, Director, Information Technology and Cybersecurity, 
Government Accountability Office; and Mike Sicilia, and Seema Verma, 
Oracle, Austin, Texas.
NOMINATIONS

Counsel, and Joseph Weirsky, of Virginia, to be Director of the 
National Counterterrorism Center, who was introduced by Senator Daines, 
both of the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, after the 
nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
SAFETY NET
Special Committee on Aging: Committee concluded a hearing to examine a 
bridge, not a barrier, focusing on reforming the safety net to reward 
work, after receiving testimony from Joshua Bandoch, The Foundation for 
Research on Equal Opportunity, Arlington, Virginia; Greg R. Lawson, The 
Buckeye Institute, Columbus, Ohio; and Lelaine Bigelow, Georgetown Law 
Center on Poverty and Inequality, Washington, D.C.
