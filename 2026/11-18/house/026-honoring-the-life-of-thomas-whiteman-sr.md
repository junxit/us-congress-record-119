# HONORING THE LIFE OF THOMAS WHITEMAN, SR.

**Speaking:** Huizenga, Bill (H001058) [R-MI]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE OF THOMAS WHITEMAN, SR.

  (Mr. HUIZENGA asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HUIZENGA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Thomas 
Whiteman, Sr., a Vietnam veteran, devoted husband, father, and 
grandfather, who passed away November 7 at the age of 85.
  Tom proudly served our Nation in the United States Army, earning the 
rank of sergeant, but his commitment to service did not end when he 
took off the uniform.
  Whether working with his hands, helping a neighbor, or serving his 
church, Tom lived a life defined by generosity, humility, and faith.
  For 60 years, he shared that life with his beloved wife, Maxine, 
raising a family and building a legacy that will endure for 
generations.
  How do I know? Well, his son Todd serves as my chief of staff, and I 
know how deeply this loss is felt not only by his family but by the 
entire community.
  Maxine, Todd, Tommy, and the entire Whiteman family have heartfelt 
condolences and prayers from me, Natalie, and all of Team Huizenga. May 
God bless Tom Whiteman, and may his family find comfort in a life so 
faithfully lived.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Tom Whiteman for being a good and faithful 
servant. May he rest well.

                          ____________________
```
