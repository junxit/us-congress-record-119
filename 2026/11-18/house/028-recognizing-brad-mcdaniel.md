# RECOGNIZING BRAD McDANIEL

**Speaking:** Stutzman, Marlin A. (S001188) [R-IN]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BRAD McDANIEL

  (Mr. STUTZMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. STUTZMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my district 
director in Indiana's Third District, Brad McDaniel, for over 25 years 
of service in the U.S. Army.
  Since enlisting in 1995, Brad has continued to pour his time and his 
talents into serving our Nation.
  In 2004, Brad supported his former Army Reserve unit while they were 
deployed to Afghanistan, and he later deployed to support operations in 
Kuwait in 2008.
  His leadership also extends to investing in the next generation of 
soldiers, including my son, through overseeing five instructor 
battalions.
  From career counselor to first sergeant to command sergeant major, 
Brad has demonstrated excellence at every level. His drive to serve 
others, lead well, and make a difference sets an example to all of us.
  Mr. Speaker, Brad's service to our country is extremely admirable, 
and I congratulate him on his retirement. I thank him for his 
friendship and for his service to the constituents in the Third 
District in Indiana. I also thank him for his service to our Nation 
while serving in the greatest military in the world, and I wish him all 
the best. God bless.

                          ____________________
```
