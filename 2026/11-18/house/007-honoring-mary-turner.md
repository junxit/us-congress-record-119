# HONORING MARY TURNER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                          HONORING MARY TURNER

  (Ms. Tlaib of Michigan was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor my dear friend Mary Turner.
  Mary was an icon and our angel in southwest Detroit. She was a social 
worker for almost four decades. She had known my two sons since their 
birth.
  With all the advocacy and social work and her showing up for so many 
immigrant families, she was always the calm in our storms. She was the 
community mother who shaped the mother and advocate that I am today.
  Whenever a resident reached out in need of basic necessities like 
food or shelter, Mary Turner would work late into the night because she 
couldn't rest knowing that a family was going to go hungry or be out in 
the cold. She poured so much love into our families and community in 
southwest Detroit.
  Mary centered the work that she did on her faith. I still remember 
taking her to Mass at 8 o'clock in the morning, as she would sit there 
and listen to the Spanish Mass, smile, and hold my hand.
  Helping others was a deeply serious calling for Mary, and she was 
relentless in her pursuit to connect people with resources that they 
needed.
  Mr. Speaker, her life wasn't easy. She struggled with her health, but 
she always supported her neighbors and showed up over and over again.
  Mr. Speaker, I will miss her dearly, but I know she will continue to 
look after our families and our communities.


                Strengthening Environmental Protections

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, the House must vote down the so-called 
permitting reform bill.
  The bill is 417 pages of bad ideas and empty promises written in 
secrecy and shoved down our throats. Instead of listening to the 
people, Congress sells out to the tech bros and big polluters.
  This dirty deal would speed up construction of data centers. Here in 
Michigan, I am telling you, it is a ``hell no'' to data centers. It 
will also speed up gas pipeline projects.
  I was raised in southwest Detroit. I know very well what it feels 
like to be ignored by my own government as big polluters poison our 
water and our air. It used to smell like rotten eggs when playing 
outside and going into my home. I watched my friends get asthma and 
so many neighbors get cancer.

  Now, I work in Congress and have the people's movement behind us. I 
am bringing the voices of Tribal nations and people in Detroit and 
throughout Wayne County and Oakland County. Those climate justice 
groups are saying to us: No--no to this awful, awful giveaway to 
corporate polluters.
  We should be strengthening our environmental protections, not tearing 
them down. We deserve to breathe clean air.
  Mr. Speaker, we shouldn't be begging our government for the ability 
to breathe clean air and to drink clean water, so we need to vote this 
dirty bill down.


                    Protecting Lebanese TPS Holders

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, temporary protected status for Lebanon is set 
to expire this month unless it is extended by the Trump administration.
  Without temporary protected status, our neighbors are at risk of 
being deported to Lebanon, where the ongoing illegal Israeli invasion 
and ethnic cleansing campaign has already killed 4,000 people this 
year.
  I introduced the Lebanon TPS Act because the safety of our neighbors 
cannot be left up to the whims of Trump and Stephen Miller.
  The U.S.-backed invasion of Lebanon displaced more than 1.2 million 
people from their homes. They are doing the same thing in Lebanon that 
they did in Gaza. That is over 20 percent of the entire Lebanese 
population.
  Look at this. This is Bint Jbeil, where the majority of many of our 
families come from in the 12th Congressional District. These are 
satellite images of Bint Jbeil, a Lebanese city with tens of thousands 
of people and the ancestral home for many families from my district, 
going back generations. Bint Jbeil is just one of the cities that is 
being systematically bulldozed and destroyed by the Israeli military.
  Mr. Speaker, to tear Lebanese TPS holders from our communities and 
send them back to suffer from these war crimes is unacceptable. We must 
protect TPS and end U.S. support for Israeli war crimes.
```
