# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 959, H.R. 5254, H.R. 8545

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Warner, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate agreed to the following resolution:

                              S. Res. 959

       Whereas John Davison ``Jay'' Rockefeller, born in New York 
     City, came to West Virginia in 1964 as a Volunteers in 
     Service to America worker;
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller, rather than return to New York, 
     embraced West Virginia and dedicated his public life to 
     serving the State;
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller was first elected to the West 
     Virginia House of Delegates in 1966, representing Kanawha 
     County, and then elected as the 22nd West Virginia Secretary 
     of State in 1968, serving from 1969 to 1973;
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller served as President of West 
     Virginia Wesleyan College from 1973 to 1975;
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller served as Governor of West Virginia 
     from 1977 to 1985;
       Whereas, in 1984, Jay Rockefeller was elected to the United 
     States Senate, serving West Virginia in the Senate until 
     2015;
       Whereas, during his tenure in the Senate, Jay Rockefeller 
     Chaired the Committee on Veterans' Affairs of the Senate, the 
     Select Committee on Intelligence of the Senate, and the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the 
     Senate;
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller was a tireless advocate for the 
     people of West Virginia, championing health care for 
     children, miners, and veterans, and increasing economic 
     development across the State;
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller, in memory of his mother, who 
     suffered from Alzheimer's disease, created what is now the 
     Rockefeller Neuroscience Institute at West Virginia 
     University, a cutting-edge research and medical treatment 
     center dedicated to advanced neurological care, mental health 
     treatment, and education; and
       Whereas Jay Rockefeller died on November 6, 2026, at the 
     age of 89, leaving behind his wife Sharon, 4 children John D. 
     Rockefeller V, Valerie Rockefeller, Charles Rockefeller, and 
     Justin Aldrich Rockefeller, and 9 grandchildren: Now, 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That--
       (1) the Senate--
       (A) has heard with profound sorrow and deep regret the 
     announcement of the death of the Honorable John Davison 
     ``Jay'' Rockefeller IV, former member of the Senate; and
       (B) respectfully requests that the Secretary of the 
     Senate--
       (i) communicate this resolution to the House of 
     Representatives; and
       (ii) transmit an enrolled copy of this resolution to the 
     family of the deceased; and
       (2) when the Senate adjourns today, it stand adjourned as a 
     further mark of respect to the memory of the Honorable John 
     Davis ``Jay'' Rockefeller.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed without 
amendment bills of the House of the following titles:

       H.R. 5254. An act to authorize the Secretary of the 
     Interior to enter into an agreement with the Gateway Arch 
     Park Foundation to host private events in Gateway Arch 
     National Park buildings, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 8545. An act to provide for the transfer of 
     administrative jurisdiction over certain Federal land in the 
     State of California, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 114-255, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader, appoints the following 
individuals to serve as members of the Health Information Technology 
Advisory Committee:
  Dr. Roxana Lupu of South Dakota.
  Dr. Patrick Woodard of South Dakota.
  The message also announced that pursuant to the provisions of Public 
Law 106-398, as amended by Public Law 108-7, the Chair, on behalf of 
the Majority Leader, and in consultation with the Chairmen of the 
Senate Committee on Armed Services and the Senate Committee on Finance, 
announces the appointement of the following individual to serve as a 
member of the United States-China Economic and Security Review 
Commission:
  Keith Krach of the District of Columbia.
```
