# REMEMBERING TIM HOLDEN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                         REMEMBERING TIM HOLDEN

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to remember 
and honor my former colleague and friend, Congressman Tim Holden of 
Pennsylvania, who passed away Sunday at his home.
  Tim was a proud son of Schuylkill County. He never forgot where he 
came from or the people he represented. During his 20 years in 
Congress, he was a tireless advocate for his district, particularly 
working families, rural communities, and Pennsylvania's agriculture 
industry.
  Tim understood that our responsibility as Members of Congress is not 
to serve a political party, but to serve the people who sent us here. 
He believed in working across the aisle, building relationships, and 
finding common ground.
  He once suggested to an incoming Congressman from the opposite party 
that serving on the House Agriculture Committee would be a smart move 
for best serving his congressional district. That was Tim, and I 
appreciated his advice. He put good representation ahead of partisan 
politics.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Pennsylvania, I extend my 
deepest condolences to Tim's family, friends, and anyone who had the 
privilege of knowing him.
  Tim Holden leaves behind a legacy of service, civility, and 
bipartisan friendship. May God bless Congressman Holden.

                          ____________________
```
