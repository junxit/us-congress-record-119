# IMPEACHING THE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

```
                        IMPEACHING THE PRESIDENT

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, and still I rise to say that if a 
person robs a bank and has an epiphanous moment, a road to Damascus 
experience, if you will, and is converted, becomes one of God's best 
people, that person will still be prosecuted for robbing the bank.
  If a President takes our country to war without the consent of 
Congress and continues that war without the consent of Congress, the 
President must be impeached. If we do not impeach this President, it 
would be a stain on this Congress, but it would also give future 
Presidents the belief that they can take the country to war without the 
consent of Congress.
  And still I rise, and I say, the President will be impeached.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to refrain from 
engaging in personalities toward the President.

                          ____________________
```
