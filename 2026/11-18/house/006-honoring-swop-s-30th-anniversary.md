# HONORING SWOP'S 30TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Garcia, Jesus G. "Chuy" (G000586) [D-IL]

```
                    HONORING SWOP'S 30TH ANNIVERSARY

  (Mr. Garcia of Illinois was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. GARCIA of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the Southwest 
Organizing Project's 30 years of work and commitment to our community.
  Since 1996, the Southwest Organizing Project has been fighting 
structural racism, organizing and creating spaces and opportunities for 
families by building connections and developing leadership to empower 
adults and young people of color.
  The Southwest Organizing Project came to life to address the 
increasing diversity of Southwest Chicago and to ensure that old and 
new residents had a voice in the decisions that impact their 
neighborhoods.
  Through efforts like the Parent Mentor Program, the Communities 
Partnering 4 Peace, and the Community Learning Partnership, the 
Southwest Organizing Project supports parents, helps create safer 
neighborhoods, and develops pathways to higher education for 
historically underserved communities.
  I was proud to secure $1 million in Community Project Funding in 2022 
to support their Reclaiming Southwest Chicago project aimed at creating 
quality, affordable housing for families on the Southwest Side.
  Mr. Speaker, in the 30 years since its foundation, the Southwest 
Organizing Project's work continues to center in capacity building, 
connections, and, most of all, community.


        Celebrating 10th Anniversary of Back of the Yards Coffee

  Mr. GARCIA of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise in celebration of Back of 
the Yards Coffee's 10-year anniversary.
  Founded in 2016 by Back of the Yards native Jesse Iniguez, this 
establishment in my district is invested in the present and future of 
our community.
  Jesse's vision for this coffee shop came at a time when the 
neighborhood was too often defined by violence and hardship, but Jesse 
recognized his neighborhood's potential and wanted to create a place 
where people could come together and enjoy a great cup of coffee while 
building community rooted in culture.
  Since then, Back of the Yards Coffee's model has always been to hire 
locally and support the local economy, while also creating 
relationships with farmers in Latin America to honor the taste of our 
homelands, committing to being environmentally responsible, 
and investing in giving back to the community through neighborhood 
initiatives and intentional senior and youth programming.

[[Page H6260]]

  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Back of the Yards Coffee on their 10-year 
anniversary, and here is to 10 more years of great coffee and 
community.


                Recognizing La Casa del Pueblo Taqueria

  Mr. GARCIA of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a small 
business that has brought tradition and good food to my neighborhood, 
La Casa del Pueblo Taqueria. The name translates into ``The People's 
House Taqueria.''
  For 64 years, it has served Mexican food in a place that feels like 
home in Pilsen. This restaurant, first opened by Martha Saucedo and her 
mom, Maria Sanchez, began in the 1960s inside a store with only one 
table.
  As immigrants began to arrive to work in the factories in the Pilsen 
area, the restaurant grew into the full storefront that we know today.
  After Christmas, La Casa del Pueblo will begin a new chapter under 
the name Del Pueblo Tacos, Tamales y Mas in Berwyn, where they plan to 
expand their tamale-making production while continuing to offer their 
customers' favorites, like menudo, enchiladas, or caldo de res. I am 
certain that their homemade flavors will continue to bring success and 
build memories for all customers.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank La Casa del Pueblo for bringing a piece of home 
to my district.
```
