# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy Lord, our heads bow before You. Our eyes look toward You. Our 
hearts yield unto You, and You give us the blessings we hope for and 
the provisions we need. You open Your hand and satisfy the desires of 
every living thing.
  May we then hope to be blessed with discernment and wisdom. May 
justice and mercy be the provisions we receive in answer to our need. 
May Your hand touch not just our lives but the lives of all of those 
around us. Attend to their requests.
  We are humbled by Your righteousness. May we strive to live lives 
worthy of Your holy purpose.
  We are honored by Your faithfulness. May we endeavor to remain 
committed to honesty and integrity in every aspect of our being.
  Lord, be near to us this day as we call on You from the truth You 
have planted in our souls.
  Greatly do we praise You. Humbly do we beseech You. Hear our prayers 
this day, O Lord.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
