# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 4930, H.R. 8552

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 17, 
2026, the following bills were presented to the President of the United 
States for approval:

       H.R. 8552. To increase, effective as of December 1, 2026, 
     the rates of compensation for veterans with service-connected 
     disabilities and the rates of dependency and indemnity 
     compensation for the survivors of certain disabled veterans, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4930. To expand the sharing of information with 
     respect to suspected violations of intellectual property 
     rights in trade.
```
