                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the

           By Mr. FONG (for himself and Mr. Gottheimer):
       H.R. 11055. A bill to amend the Homeland Security Act of 
     2002 to establish within the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure 
     Security Agency a pilot program to be known as the ``BRIDGE 
     AI Mission Program'' to recruit and manage artificial 
     intelligence and emerging technology professionals on a 
     temporary basis to support the core mission of the Agency, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Homeland 
     Security.

     Security enforcement actions, and for other purposes; to the

       H.R. 11058. A bill to require a school that receives 
     support under the E-Rate program of the Federal

     Commerce.

     the Judiciary.

       H.R. 11063. A bill to direct the Nuclear Regulatory
