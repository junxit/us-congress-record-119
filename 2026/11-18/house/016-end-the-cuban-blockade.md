# END THE CUBAN BLOCKADE

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
                         END THE CUBAN BLOCKADE

  (Mr. McGOVERN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, Cuba is our neighbor. Our histories are 
intertwined. Our futures are, too.
  The 10 million Cubans on the island are just like us. They go to 
school and work, raise families, and live their lives. They crave 
freedom and justice.
  The Cuban people are suffering hardships which U.S. taxpayers are 
paying to make worse. The cruel and illegal U.S. blockade is creating a 
severe humanitarian crisis, with food scarcity and endless blackouts.
  The Trump administration is trying to starve them out like Leningrad 
in 1942 or Gaza in slow motion. We hear of plans for military attack, 
an Iran war in the Caribbean.
  Cuba belongs to the Cuban people, not to Marco Rubio's cabal of 
ideologues. Rubio, the viceroy of Venezuela, wants to be the caudillo 
of Cuba.
  True liberation demands that we drop the U.S. embargo, whose 60 years 
of inefficacy is matched only by its cruelty.
  If Trump and Rubio want to invade Cuba, they must come to Congress 
and seek its approval. They must explain to the American people, who 
just voted resoundingly against endless war, why they must pay for 
another.
  Trade and diplomacy, not war, will liberate the people of Cuba. My 
message to Trump and Rubio is: Back off. Let the Cuban people decide 
their own future.

                          ____________________
```
