# COLLEGE STUDENTS FACING HOUSING INSECURITY

**Speaking:** Dexter, Maxine (D000635) [D-OR]

```
               COLLEGE STUDENTS FACING HOUSING INSECURITY

  (Ms. DEXTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, roughly one in seven college students 
experienced homelessness in 2023, with nearly half facing housing 
insecurity.
  The most basic needs a human has are food and shelter. A student 
cannot learn if hungry or unhoused.
  I introduced the Postsecondary Attainment Through Housing Supports 
Demonstration Act to directly address this challenge and help ensure 
our students have what they need to be successful.
  This bill builds partnerships between nonprofit housing providers, 
colleges, and universities to ensure students have a stable place to 
live. It puts housing first and pairs it with the wraparound services a 
student needs to succeed.
  Mr. Speaker, I am a first-generation college graduate. Affordable 
housing and affordable education opened the door to my American Dream. 
Young people across this country must also have access to that 
opportunity.
  To our students: I will keep fighting for you and your ability to 
thrive. Stay loud.

                          ____________________
```
