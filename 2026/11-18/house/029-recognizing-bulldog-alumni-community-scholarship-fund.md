# RECOGNIZING BULLDOG ALUMNI COMMUNITY SCHOLARSHIP FUND

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
         RECOGNIZING BULLDOG ALUMNI COMMUNITY SCHOLARSHIP FUND

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize the 
Bulldog Alumni Community Scholarship Fund, a Rocky Mount, North 
Carolina-based organization that is helping students attend college and 
prepare for life after college.
  The cost of higher education is becoming out of reach for so many 
families in eastern North Carolina. Scholarships can make the 
difference between students continuing their education or putting their 
dreams on hold.
  What makes this organization so special is that it goes beyond 
providing scholarships to students. The Bulldog Alumni Community 
Scholarship Fund also provides students with professional mentoring to 
assist with their career exploration, helping them search for 
internships and preparing them for life after college.
  Mr. Speaker, young people deserve every opportunity to succeed.
```
