# CELEBRATING SMALL BUSINESS SATURDAY

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                  CELEBRATING SMALL BUSINESS SATURDAY

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, as we approach Thanksgiving and the holiday 
season, I rise to celebrate Small Business Saturday, which is an 
opportunity to celebrate the heart and soul of our community, our local 
small businesses.
  Westchester and the Bronx are home to some of the most successful, 
passionate, and hardworking businessowners in the Nation. From Co-Op 
City to White Plains, these local businesses are driving economic 
development and giving back to the community by organizing community 
events and sponsoring youth sports teams. We salute our local Chambers 
of Commerce for their exceptional work working for our local 
businesses.
  In the days following Thanksgiving, we are flooded with ads for sales 
from large corporate retailers. It is imperative that we continue to 
support the hardworking entrepreneurs in our own backyard.
  On Small Business Saturday, I encourage my constituents and Americans 
everywhere to take into consideration not just what you buy but from 
whom you are buying.

                          ____________________
```
