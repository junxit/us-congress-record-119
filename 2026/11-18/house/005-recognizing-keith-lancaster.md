# RECOGNIZING KEITH LANCASTER

**Speaking:** Rose, John W. (R000612) [R-TN]

```
                      RECOGNIZING KEITH LANCASTER

  (Mr. Rose of Tennessee was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. ROSE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a great Tennessean and 
faithful servant of the Lord, Goodlettsville resident Keith Lancaster, 
a constituent of mine in Tennessee's Sixth Congressional District.
  Growing up in Paris, Tennessee, Mr. Lancaster grew up in a community 
where musical fellowship was essential to his daily life. In 1976, Mr. 
Lancaster felt a calling to dedicate his life to worship ministries. In 
1982, he founded Acappella Ministries to establish a vehicle to spread 
spiritual hope globally through the unmatched versatility of the human 
voice.
  From his base in Goodlettsville, he has traveled our Nation and 
internationally as a musicianary to cultivate congregational singing, 
train worship leaders, and foster unified community expression through 
song.
  Over the last five decades, he has contributed hundreds of songs sung 
in church congregations worldwide, including ``Lead Me to Rest,'' 
``Walking That Line,'' and ``Abba Father.'' There is no doubt that Mr. 
Lancaster has inspired many new voices in the worship scene.
  Mr. Speaker, it is an honor to represent a citizen of such profound 
faith and enduring generosity. I ask my colleagues to join me in 
celebrating the 50-year ministry of Keith Lancaster and wishing him, 
his family, and Acappella Ministries continued blessings in their 
future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
