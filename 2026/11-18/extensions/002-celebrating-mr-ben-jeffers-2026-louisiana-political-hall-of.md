# CELEBRATING MR. BEN JEFFERS, 2026 LOUISIANA POLITICAL HALL OF FAME INDUCTEE

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
  CELEBRATING MR. BEN JEFFERS, 2026 LOUISIANA POLITICAL HALL OF FAME 
                                INDUCTEE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an 
incredible man, someone I met at the early start of my political career 
upon my graduation from Xavier University of Louisiana, Mr. Ben 
Jeffers. He has devoted more than five decades to public service at the 
national, state, and local levels. A resident of Baton Rouge, he is a 
graduate of Southern University, where he earned a Bachelor of Science 
in business.
  A lifelong Democrat, Jeffers has been a prominent leader within the 
party at both the state and national levels. He served as Chair of the 
Louisiana Democratic Party, as a member of the Democratic National 
Committee and its Executive Committee, and as Chair of the Democratic 
National Committee Southern Caucus, helping shape political engagement 
and voter outreach across the region.
  In 1978, Jeffers founded Ben Jeffers, Inc., a successful management 
and marketing consulting firm, and he currently serves as Senior 
Advisor to United States Representative Cleo Fields. His public service 
spans all levels of government. At the municipal level, he served as 
Personnel Administrator for the City of Baton Rouge. At the state 
level, he held key roles including Acting Director of New Orleans 
Charity Hospital, Undersecretary of the Louisiana Department of Health 
and Hospitals, Director of Human Services, and Chief of Staff to 
Governor Edwin Edwards. He later served on the Louisiana Tax Commission 
by appointment of Governor John Bel Edwards.
  Nationally, Jeffers served as Regional Director of the United States 
Department of Health and Human Services during the Carter 
Administration and as a congressional aide to Representative Edwin 
Edwards. He also held leadership roles in journalism and has been 
active in civic and cultural organizations throughout Louisiana.
  A United States Marine Corps veteran, Jeffers attained the rank of 
sergeant and later ran statewide for Louisiana Secretary of State. He 
and his wife, Salomia Lawson Jeffers, have one son and are active 
members of Mount Zion First Baptist Church in Baton Rouge.
  I thank the Louisiana Political Museum and Hall of Fame Foundation 
for assuring his legacy continues.
```
