# HONORING MEDICAL CITY WEATHERFORD ON EARNING THE 2026 HEALTHGRADES AMERICA'S BEST HOSPITALS FOR SPECIALTY CARE AWARD

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
  HONORING MEDICAL CITY WEATHERFORD ON EARNING THE 2026 HEALTHGRADES 
           AMERICA'S BEST HOSPITALS FOR SPECIALTY CARE AWARD

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Medical 
City Weatherford for receiving the 2026 Patient Safety Excellence Award 
from Healthgrades, a distinguished national honor recognizing hospitals 
that demonstrate exceptional performance in protecting the well-being 
of their patients.
  This recognition places Medical City Weatherford among the top five 
percent of hospitals in the United States for patient safety, an 
achievement reserved for a select group of healthcare institutions 
across the country. Additionally, the hospital was recognized for its 
specialty-care performance, placing it among the top 10 percent of 
hospitals nationwide. These distinctions underscore the exceptional 
standard of care required to earn such national recognition and reflect 
the hospital's continued commitment to patient safety and clinical 
excellence.
  These accomplishments are made possible by the physicians, nurses, 
administrators, and support staff whose dedication ensures patients and 
families throughout Parker County and the surrounding communities 
receive quality care. Their commitment to maintaining the highest 
standards of safety and service makes Medical City Weatherford an 
invaluable resource to our community.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
Medical City Weatherford on receiving the 2026 Patient Safety 
Excellence Award and its additional national recognition. This 
achievement is a testament to the outstanding work of its healthcare 
professionals, and we are grateful for their continued dedication to 
the health and well-being of our community.

                          ____________________
```
