# HONORING JOHNATHAN BARNES FOR BEING NAMED THE 2026 DRIVER OF THE YEAR BY THE AUTO HAULERS ASSOCIATION OF AMERICA

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING JOHNATHAN BARNES FOR BEING NAMED THE 2026 DRIVER OF THE YEAR 
               BY THE AUTO HAULERS ASSOCIATION OF AMERICA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Johnathan Barnes of Blue Mound, Texas, for being named the 2026 Driver 
of the Year by the Auto Haulers Association of America. This 
distinguished honor recognizes Mr. Barnes' exceptional professionalism, 
dedication to safety, and outstanding commitment to excellence 
throughout his career in vehicle transportation.
  As a vehicle transporter at Carvana's Blue Mound location for more 
than eight years, Mr. Barnes has established an extraordinary record of 
safe driving, logging more than 1.34 million miles without a 
preventable accident. His achievement reflects the discipline, 
preparation, and attention to detail he brings to his work each day. 
This is why he was recognized for his remarkable accomplishments at the 
Auto Haulers Association of America's Annual Fall Expo, where he 
received this prestigious industry honor.
  Mr. Barnes's commitment to service and responsibility is rooted in 
his 15 years of military service. He served three years active duty in 
field artillery at Fort Hood before continuing his service as a 
mechanic in the Texas Army National Guard. During his 2008 to 2009 
deployment to Kuwait, Iraq, and Afghanistan, he led a 12-soldier 
vehicle recovery team and later helped prepare fellow soldiers for 
their own deployments. He concluded his military career in 2015 with 
the rank of staff sergeant. His dedication to service continues within 
his family, as his son currently serves in the military and his 
daughter previously served for four years.
  Before joining Carvana, Mr. Barnes worked as a bridge inspector for 
the Texas Department of Transportation. Throughout his career, he has 
carried forward the same sense of responsibility, attention to detail, 
and respect for safety that defined his military service. Known for his 
willingness to look out for those around him on the road, he 
exemplifies the professionalism and care that strengthen the 
transportation industry.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
Johnathan Barnes on being named the 2026 Auto Haulers Association of 
America Driver of the Year. His remarkable safety record, distinguished 
military service, and continued dedication to his profession represent 
the very best of our community. We are proud to recognize his 
accomplishments and celebrate this well-deserved honor.
```
