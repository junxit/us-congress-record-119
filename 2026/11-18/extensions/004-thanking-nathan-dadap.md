# THANKING NATHAN DADAP

**Speaking:** Mullin, Kevin (M001225) [D-CA]

```
                         THANKING NATHAN DADAP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. KEVIN MULLIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. MULLIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to express my gratitude to Nathan 
Dadap, a valued member of my staff here in Washington, D.C., since 
2024. He joined us after a stint in the Senate. Before he came to 
Capitol Hill, Nathan worked at the Environmental Protection Agency's 
regional office while residing in a portion of my district in San 
Francisco.
  Throughout his time in my office, Nathan has lent his expertise, 
kindness, and good humor to my team. Nathan has been instrumental in 
helping me write and amend legislation, conduct oversight, and push the 
policies that are a priority for my district, particularly addressing 
climate change, extreme weather, sea-level rise, grid resilience, and 
our transition to clean energy. These are issues of great national 
importance of which the San Francisco Bay Area is at the forefront. 
Nathan understands the urgency, and he has worked hard to meet the 
moment.
  Nathan has also helped me write numerous pieces of related 
legislation, including the Battery and Regenerative Braking Act, 
Earthquake Resilience Act, Federal Flood Risk Management Act, Forging 
Ahead on Rail Electrification (FARE) Act, Groundwater Rise and

[[Page E1238]]

Infrastructure Preparedness Act, Methane Removal Research and 
Innovation Act, Reasonable Adjustments To Energy Schedules (RATES) Act, 
Resilient Emergency Alert Communications and Training (REACT) Act, 
Reliable Emergency Alert Delivery Integrity (READI) Act, and several 
more. The breadth of his contributions speaks to his impressive ability 
to work across complex sectors to build consensus. He has been able to 
build diverse coalitions around smart solutions to a range of large-
scale problems we face as a Nation.
  I speak for my entire team in both D.C. and San Mateo when I say we 
will miss Nathan's daily presence, but we look forward to seeing where 
his career takes him. He--and Ophelia, Arthur, and Emily--will always 
be members of Team Mullin.
  As he departs my office this week, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
thanking Nathan for his service to California's 15th Congressional 
District, this institution, and the country.

                          ____________________
```
