# HONORING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2025 AND 2026 FAIRFAX COUNTY CITIZEN OF THE YEAR AWARDS

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
HONORING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2025 AND 2026 FAIRFAX COUNTY CITIZEN OF 
                            THE YEAR AWARDS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the recipients of 
the 2025 and 2026 Fairfax County Citizen of the Year Awards, presented 
by the Fairfax County Federation of Citizens Associations at its 73rd 
Annual Citizen of the Year Awards Luncheon.
  For more than seven decades, the Fairfax Federation has celebrated 
residents whose volunteerism, leadership, and dedication to public 
service have strengthened our community. These awards reflect a simple 
truth about Fairfax County: Our greatest strength is the people who 
give their time and talents to make life better for their neighbors.
  I am proud to congratulate Jim Hall, the 2025 Citizen of the Year, 
and Daniel Palenski, the 2026 Citizen of the Year. Both exemplify the 
commitment to service that makes Fairfax County such an extraordinary 
place to live.
  I also congratulate the recipients of the 2025 Citations of Merit, 
Lauren Crum and Shelley Deutch, and the 2026 recipients, Tilly 
Blanding, Debbie Kilpatrick, and Matt Bell. Their contributions remind 
us that meaningful change often begins with neighbors who see a need 
and step forward to help.
  This year's celebration also recognizes recipients of the Gerry 
Connolly Special Gratitude Award: The Honorable Ken Plum for 2025, and 
Carol A. Hawn, Virginia Diamond, and the Honorable Dick Saslaw for 
2026.
  These awards hold special meaning for me. I had the privilege of 
working alongside my predecessor and friend, Representative Gerry 
Connolly, and witnessed firsthand his unwavering commitment to Fairfax 
County. Gerry understood that public service was about more than 
holding office. It was about showing up, listening to people, and 
working every day to improve their lives.
  As a former president of the Fairfax Federation, Gerry knew the vital 
role civic associations and community volunteers play in shaping our 
county. It is fitting that an award bearing his name recognizes 
individuals who carry forward his spirit of service.
  I want to thank the Fairfax County Federation of Citizens 
Associations for its continued work to strengthen civic engagement and 
recognize those who make a difference in our neighborhoods.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
Fairfax County Citizen of the year award recipients. Their generosity, 
leadership, and dedication to others embody the very best of our 
community and inspire the next generation of Fairfax County leaders.
```
