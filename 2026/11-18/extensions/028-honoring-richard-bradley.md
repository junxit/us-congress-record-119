# HONORING RICHARD BRADLEY

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING RICHARD BRADLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Mr. Richard Bradley of Jackson, Mississippi, an entrepreneur, 
workforce-development leader, real estate developer, former collegiate 
coach, and community advocate whose work has created opportunities for 
individuals and families throughout his hometown.
  A native of Jackson, Mr. Bradley has spent more than two decades 
working in business development, real estate, construction, workforce 
training, automotive services, hospitality, and community 
revitalization. He currently serves as President of Metro Booming 
Training Academy and A3 Consulting, CEO of U-Do-It Auto Garage, Founder 
and CEO of Metropolitan Bar and Grill, known as MBAR, and Senior 
Project Manager for PDT Logistics.
  Mr. Bradley is a proud graduate of Jackson State University, where he 
played basketball for the Tigers and earned a Bachelor of Business 
Administration in 2001 and a Master of Science in Education in 2010. He 
later returned to Jackson State as an assistant coach for the women's 
basketball program, mentoring student-athletes both on and off the 
court. In 2023, he was selected as Grand Marshal of Jackson State 
University's Homecoming Parade, and in 2025, he was inducted into the 
Jackson State University Athletics Hall of Fame.
  Through Metro Booming Training Academy, Mr. Bradley has helped expand 
workforce-development opportunities in skilled trades, automotive 
technology, construction, manufacturing, clean energy, and other high-
demand industries. Under his leadership, the Academy has trained more 
than 370 students while building partnerships with employers, 
educational institutions, government agencies, and community 
organizations.
  Mr. Bradley is also the visionary developer behind Jackson Yards; and 
approximately $30 million mixed-use economic and community-development 
project planned for downtown Jackson. The project seeks to combine 
workforce education, housing, business development, automotive and 
industrial training, community programming, retail opportunities, and 
neighborhood revitalization.
  His commitment to service extends beyond his businesses. Mr. Bradley 
has supported student mentorship, scholarships, school-supply drives, 
meal distributions, neighborhood cleanups, youth programs, university 
fundraising, and other community initiatives. He also serves on the 
board of the Mississippi Urban League and is a member of Kappa Alpha 
Psi Fraternity, Incorporated, and the American Association of Blacks in 
Energy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. 
Richard Bradley for his commitment to workforce development, 
entrepreneurship, education, and service to the people of Jackson and 
the State of Mississippi.
```
