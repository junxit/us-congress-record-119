# HONORING WAVERLY HARRIS, LANDRI BENTON, AMBER VALENCIA, AND AUBREY GRAY OF WEATHERFORD HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
HONORING WAVERLY HARRIS, LANDRI BENTON, AMBER VALENCIA, AND AUBREY GRAY 
                       OF WEATHERFORD HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Waverly 
Harris, Landri Benton, Amber Valencia, and Aubrey Gray of Weatherford 
High School for earning first-place honors in their respective 
divisions at the 2026 National Future Farmers of America Agriscience 
Fair. As freshmen, these four students achieved the remarkable 
distinction of becoming national champions, bringing well-deserved 
recognition to Weatherford FFA and the 12th Congressional District of 
Texas.
  Competing as two teams, these four students presented research 
projects exploring important issues in agricultural science. 
Agriculture remains a cornerstone of our community, and the next 
generation of students who seek to advance and strengthen the industry 
will play an important role in its continued success.
  Waverly Harris and Landri Benton partnered on ``Rinse Risk: An 
Analysis of Different Washing Methods on Bacterial Growth of Fresh 
Produce,'' investigating the effectiveness of common produce-washing 
methods in reducing bacterial contamination and promoting food safety.
  Amber Valencia and Aubrey Gray collaborated on ``Teaching Through the 
Tough Days: Mental Health and Coping Strategies in Agricultural Science 
Classrooms,'' examining the demands placed on agricultural educators 
and the challenges affecting their well-being. Their work brought 
attention to the importance of supporting the educators who play an 
essential role in preparing future generations of agricultural leaders.
  What makes these accomplishments especially remarkable is that all 
four students achieved this distinction as freshmen. Early in their 
high school careers, they have already demonstrated the initiative, 
knowledge, and determination to compete at the national level. Their 
success reflects the strength of Weatherford's agricultural education 
program and serves as an inspiring example of what young students can 
accomplish through hard work, curiosity, and collaboration.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
Waverly Harris, Landri Benton, Amber Valencia, and Aubrey Gray on this 
distinguished achievement. Their success brings pride to Weatherford 
High School and our community, and I wish them continued success. I 
look forward to seeing all they will accomplish in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
