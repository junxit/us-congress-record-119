# CONGRATULING WOODFORD COUNTY SHERIFF MATTHEW SMITH ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
  CONGRATULING WOODFORD COUNTY SHERIFF MATTHEW SMITH ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sheriff Matthew L. 
Smith for his more than four decades of distinguished service in law 
enforcement to the people of Woodford County, Illinois. Throughout his 
career, Sheriff Smith demonstrated exceptional leadership and a deep 
commitment to public safety, community service, and the wellbeing of 
those he has been sworn to protect.
  Sheriff Smith began his career with the Woodford County Sheriffs 
Office in 1984 as a part-time dispatcher. Over the following decades, 
he advanced into numerous roles, serving as a Corrections Deputy, 
Patrol Deputy, Operations Captain, and Chief Deputy. His extensive 
career also included opportunities to serve with departments across 
Woodford County, including Spring Bay, Eureka, Metamora, and Bayview 
Gardens. Ultimately, his decades of experience culminated in his last 
12 years of service as the Woodford County Sheriff.
  Beyond his work in law enforcement, Sheriff Smith has remained deeply 
involved in his community. He served on the boards of the Eureka Fire 
Protection District and Saint Mary's Parish Credit Union. Additionally, 
he spent more than 22 years as the chairman of the Woodford County 
Emergency Telephone System Board. In all these roles, he has 
demonstrated immense leadership, professionalism, and a commitment to 
the residents of Woodford County.
  As Sheriff Smith prepares to retire after an extraordinary career in 
public service, I wish him and his family all the best in the years to 
come. His dedication, loyalty, and selfless service have made a lasting 
impact on Woodford County and set a precedent of excellence for the 
Sheriff's Office.
```
