# HONORING MR. JAMES LEWIS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING MR. JAMES LEWIS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and congratulate Mr. James Lewis on his appointment as vice president 
of the Mississippi Athletic Administrators' Association (MAAA) for the 
2026 to 2027 school year. His selection reflects a steadfast commitment 
to advancing educational athletics and serving student-athletes across 
the state of Mississippi.
  Since 2022, Mr. Lewis has served as the Athletic Director for the 
Vicksburg Warren School District, where he has demonstrated exceptional 
leadership, professionalism, and a deep dedication to the success and 
well-being of student-athletes. Through his service, he has worked 
tirelessly to strengthen athletic programs while fostering 
opportunities for young people to develop leadership, discipline, 
teamwork, and character.
  The Mississippi Athletic Administrators' Association plays a vital 
role in supporting athletic administrators through professional 
development, policy guidance, and collaboration on issues affecting 
interscholastic athletics. Having served on the association's planning 
committee for the past two years, Mr. Lewis has already contributed 
significantly to its mission. As vice president, he will now serve on 
the executive board while continuing his responsibilities with the 
Vicksburg Warren School District.
  Mr. Lewis will help guide professional development initiatives, 
support the association's annual conference, and assist athletic 
administrators throughout Mississippi in navigating the evolving 
landscape of high school athletics. His forward-thinking approach and 
commitment to preparing schools for legislative and policy changes will 
benefit students, educators, and communities across our state.
  Mr. James Lewis exemplifies servant leadership through his 
willingness to share his expertise and advocate for the continued 
success of Mississippi's athletic programs. His dedication to ensuring 
that student-athletes remain at the center of every decision serves as 
an inspiration to his colleagues and reflects the highest ideals of 
educational leadership.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. James 
Lewis for this well-deserved appointment and in commending him for his 
outstanding service to the Vicksburg Warren School District, the 
Mississippi

[[Page E1249]]

Athletic Administrators' Association, and the countless student 
athletes who will benefit from his leadership.
```
