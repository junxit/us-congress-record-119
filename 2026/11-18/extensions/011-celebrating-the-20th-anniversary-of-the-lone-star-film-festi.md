# CELEBRATING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LONE STAR FILM FESTIVAL IN FORT WORTH, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
CELEBRATING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LONE STAR FILM FESTIVAL IN FORT 
                              WORTH, TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 20th 
anniversary of the Lone Star Film Festival and its enduring 
contributions to the arts, culture, and filmmaking community of Fort 
Worth, Texas.
  Two decades ago, when Fort Worth's independent film scene was still 
in its early stages, a group of passionate film enthusiasts established 
the Lone Star Film Festival with a simple but meaningful mission: to 
bring exceptional independent cinema to local audiences. What began as 
an effort to introduce residents to new voices and perspectives in 
filmmaking has grown into an annual celebration connecting Fort Worth 
audiences with emerging Texas talent and independent storytellers from 
around the world.
  Presented by the Fort Worth Film Commission, the Lone Star Film 
Festival has become an important platform for filmmakers to showcase 
their work, reach new audiences, and gain recognition for their 
creativity. Over the years, the festival has continued to elevate 
emerging artists while introducing audiences to innovative storytelling 
and diverse perspectives. Its 20th anniversary celebration features 
more than 70 short films from around the world, nearly half of which 
are from Texas, demonstrating the remarkable talent and continued 
growth of the state's filmmaking community.
  Reaching this milestone is a testament to the dedication of the Lone 
Star Film Society, its leadership, filmmakers, volunteers, and 
supporters who have sustained the festival through changing times and 
challenges. Beyond bringing independent cinema to Fort Worth, the 
festival provides filmmakers with opportunities to celebrate their 
work, connect with audiences, and find encouragement to continue 
pursuing their craft. It also invites residents to discover new 
stories, engage with the creative process, and take pride in a festival 
that belongs to their community.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I congratulate 
the Lone Star Film Society and everyone who has contributed to the Lone 
Star Film Festival's 20 years of success. May this milestone serve as a 
celebration of its remarkable legacy and a foundation for many more 
years of creativity, storytelling, and cultural enrichment in Fort 
Worth.
```
