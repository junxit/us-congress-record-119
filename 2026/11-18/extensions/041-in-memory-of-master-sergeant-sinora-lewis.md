# IN MEMORY OF MASTER SERGEANT SINORA LEWIS

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
               IN MEMORY OF MASTER SERGEANT SINORA LEWIS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the incredible legacy of Mrs. 
Sinora H. Lewis, affectionately known as ``Nora,'' who was a retired 
Master Sergeant, Registered Nurse, and devoted family woman.
  Born in 1944 in West Virginia, Sinora ``Nora'' Lewis was raised by 
her grandmother, whose community service shaped Sinora's lifelong 
mission to leave the world better than she found it. From a young age, 
she embraced leadership, managing household responsibilities and 
cooking for a large family. This early discipline transitioned 
seamlessly into her professional life as she completed Nurse's Aide 
training and joined the United States Army Reserve.
  Sinora's military and medical careers were defined by excellence and 
a spirit of advocacy. She served 23 years in the Army Reserve, 
including tours during the Persian Gulf War and Desert Storm, 
eventually earning the rank of Master Sergeant. Parallel to her 
military service, she dedicated 33 years to the James H. Quillen VA 
Medical Center. A lifelong learner, she advanced her career by earning 
her RN from East Tennessee State University, later using her experience 
to mentor single mothers into the nursing profession.
  Beyond her clinical work, Sinora was a formidable political 
strategist and organizer. She was a pillar of the Democratic Women of 
Sullivan County and played a vital role in the 2012 election of 
President Barack Obama as a regional office lead. Known for her ``tough 
love,'' honesty, and directness, Sinora's legacy is one of unwavering 
devotion to justice, education, and the care of her fellow veterans.
```
