# HONORING MRS. GLADYS JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                      HONORING MRS. GLADYS JOHNSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mrs. 
Gladys Johnson of Tallahatchie County, Mississippi.
  Mrs. Johnson is a proud native of Tallahatchie County, the daughter 
of the late Mr. Howard Jenkins and Mrs. Arether Jenkins. A graduate of 
West Tallahatchie High School, she pursued higher education at 
Northwest

[[Page E1241]]

Mississippi Community College (Cosmetology studies); Coahoma Community 
College (A.A., Early Childhood Education, 1999; Elementary Education, 
2000); and Mississippi Valley State University, where she earned her 
B.S. in Early Childhood Education, cum laude, in 2018. Her academic 
record includes honors, awards, and recognition for scholastic 
achievement.
  Mrs. Johnson's professional life reflects a sustained commitment to 
community service and education. Early roles included Secretary and 
Community Service Representative at Glendora Economic and Community 
Development, Inc., and Chancery Clerk Assistant at the Tallahatchie 
County Courthouse. She provided caregiving services with Total 
Healthcare and served the Mississippi Department of Human Services from 
2000 to 2016, dedicating sixteen years to family support and child 
welfare. She later worked as an ES Specialist with the West 
Tallahatchie School District and held a position with the Federal 
Department of the Treasury. Today she serves as a Career Navigator 
Coordinator, guiding participants toward educational, workforce, and 
personal goals through workforce development and community services.
  Since 2015, Mrs. Johnson has also served as a Funeral Service 
Assistant, offering compassionate support to families during times of 
loss. Her service to children and families was recognized by the West 
Tallahatchie School District when she was named Parent of the Year.
  Faith and church service are central to Mrs. Johnson's life. She is a 
Prayer Team member and part of the Sisterhood at St. Luke Missionary 
Baptist Church, and she has been actively involved with Zion Traveler 
Missionary Baptist Church--serving as a guest speaker and on multiple 
committees including Trip Advisors, Church Decorating, Anniversary, 
Pastor's Aid, and Youth. Her church work has both shaped and showcased 
her gifts of encouragement, creativity, and leadership.
  Mrs. Johnson is also an entrepreneurially minded stylist with a 
passion for interior and fashion styling. She creates memorable 
settings for weddings, parties, and church events, and nurtures the 
vision for her creative business, Gee's Creations & Decor.
  Guided by faith, Mrs. Johnson believes in practical service: ``Go 
into the vineyard and work--there's always room for willing workers.'' 
Her life exemplifies perseverance, lifelong learning, compassion, and a 
resolve to make room for others to be seen, heard, and trusted. She 
credits God for every opportunity and aims to be remembered for the 
lives she has touched rather than titles held.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mrs. 
Gladys Johnson for her decades of faithful service to her community, 
her commitment to education and families, and her abiding faith.
```
