# HONORING RABBI DR. ARI BERMAN

**Speaking:** Gottheimer, Josh (G000583) [D-NJ]

```
                     HONORING RABBI DR. ARI BERMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOSH GOTTHEIMER

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GOTTHEIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to thank Rabbi Dr. Ari Berman, 
president of Yeshiva University, for the prayer he just offered this 
chamber, and for his incredible work as a leader in higher education.
  Under his leadership, Yeshiva University has more than doubled its 
enrollment, launched over 20 new graduate and STEM programs, and 
expanded scholarships and programming for students.
  What truly sets Rabbi Berman apart is his moral clarity and his 
action to combat antisemitism. After October 7, as antisemitism surged 
on campuses across America, he didn't wait for someone else to act. He 
built a coalition of more than 100 university presidents to stand 
against terrorism and hate and led the first-ever delegation of 
university presidents to Auschwitz. He has also established a Center 
for Holocaust Studies and a Center for Values and Leadership to educate 
future leaders and combat historical misinformation.
  Rabbi Berman believes education must build character, courage, and 
conscience, not just intellect. We need that now more than ever.
  I thank Rabbi Berman for his prayer, his leadership, and for showing 
a generation of students what it means to stand up and speak out.

                          ____________________
```
