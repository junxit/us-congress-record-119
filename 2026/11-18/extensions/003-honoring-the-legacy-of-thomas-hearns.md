# HONORING THE LEGACY OF THOMAS HEARNS

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                  HONORING THE LEGACY OF THOMAS HEARNS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the incredible legacy of 
Thomas Hearns, a devoted public servant and accomplished athlete. Above 
all, a loving father centering his life around his family.
  Born on October 18, 1958, in Grand Junction, Tennessee to Lois 
Hearns, Thomas moved with his family to Detroit, Michigan at age five. 
While growing up in Detroit, Thomas discovered boxing at the legendary 
``Kronk Gym,'' where under the guidance of trainer Emanuel Steward, he 
developed into one of the greatest fighters in boxing history. His 
amateur record of 155-8 led to a professional career that would make 
him the first boxer in history to win world titles in five weight 
divisions. He was famously known as the Hitman, because of his punching 
power and legendary battles with Marvin Hagler, Roberto Duran, and Iran 
Barkley. He was named Fighter of the Year in 1980 and 1984, and 
inducted into the International Boxing Hall of Fame in 2012. Thomas 
credited Emanuel Steward and the Kronk Gym with shaping him. ``I would 
have never been an eight-time world champion if it wasn't for Kronk, 
and for my main man, Emanuel Steward.
  Thomas remains a towering figure in boxing and beyond, respected not 
only for his greatness in the ring but for the character he carries 
outside of it. We extend our deepest love and appreciation to his 
family, friends. and the boxing community as we honor and celebrate his 
extraordinary life and ongoing influence. We are committed to carrying 
his legacy forward and ensuring his impact continues to inspire future 
generations.
```
