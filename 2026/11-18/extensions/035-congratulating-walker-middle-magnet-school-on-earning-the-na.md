# CONGRATULATING WALKER MIDDLE MAGNET SCHOOL ON EARNING THE NATIONAL GOLD RIBBON DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
CONGRATULATING WALKER MIDDLE MAGNET SCHOOL ON EARNING THE NATIONAL GOLD 
                           RIBBON DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Principal 
Sarah Caldwell, the school leadership team, faculty, staff, and 
students of Walker Middle Magnet School in Odessa, Florida, on being 
named a 2026 National Gold Ribbon School in Service-Learning by Youth 
Service America. Walker is one of just 50 schools nationwide to earn 
this distinction. This recognition reflects the work of educators who 
connect classroom lessons to needs in the community and give students a 
voice in deciding how to help. Through service-learning, students have 
the opportunity to identify challenges, work together on solutions, and 
see what their efforts can accomplish. In the process, they learn to 
listen to others, take responsibility for their work, and lead with 
compassion.
  I commend Principal Caldwell and her team for making service a part 
of the school's culture. I also congratulate Superintendent Van Ayres 
and Hillsborough County Public Schools for supporting educators who 
help young people become active, engaged citizens. May the students of 
Walker Middle Magnet School receive thanks for putting what they learn 
in the classroom to work for their neighbors. They have given our 
community one more reason to be proud of them.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
entire Walker Middle Magnet School community and Hillsborough County 
Public Schools on this well-deserved national recognition.

                          ____________________
```
