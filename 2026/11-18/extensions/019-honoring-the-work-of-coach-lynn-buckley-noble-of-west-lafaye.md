# HONORING THE WORK OF COACH LYNN BUCKLEY NOBLE OF WEST LAFAYETTE

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
    HONORING THE WORK OF COACH LYNN BUCKLEY NOBLE OF WEST LAFAYETTE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES R. BAIRD

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lynn Buckley Noble 
of West Lafayette for her work with Special Olympics athletes in 
Tippecanoe County.
  Noble coaches golf as well, and helps athletes train to run 5Ks. Her 
work starts well before race day. Alongside helping athletes prepare, 
she raises money to cover their entry fees. When an athlete has put in 
the miles, she wants to see them at the starting line.
  Coach Buckley Noble also uses her fluency in sign language to welcome 
even more athletes into Special Olympics programs. Whether she is 
coaching, teaching, or fundraising, she understands what it takes for 
each athlete to participate.
  I thank Coach Noble for giving every Hoosier the opportunity to 
compete and to be part of a team, no matter who they are.
```
