# CELEBRATING THE 85TH BIRTHDAY OF MRS. SHARLENE KAREN CLARK

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
       CELEBRATING THE 85TH BIRTHDAY OF MRS. SHARLENE KAREN CLARK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the extraordinary life of 
Mrs. Sharlene Karen Clark as she turns 85 years old.
  A daughter of Detroit born in 1940, Mrs. Clark stands as a testament 
to the resilience and pride of her city. Following 42 years of marriage 
to her beloved husband, Bill Clark, she has embraced her role as 
matriarch with grace and wisdom. Her three children--Brenda, Eric, and 
Darrin--were raised within the rich fabric of the Detroit community and 
have since seen their own children, Austin, Summer, and Olivia, reach 
the milestone of being first-generation college graduates. Mrs. Clark 
is cherished by her family for her sharp wit and generous heart. Her 
life's work is reflected in the success of her descendants and the 
remarkable legacy of devotion she continues to uphold.
  The 12th Congressional District proudly honors Mrs. Sharlene Karen 
Clark as she celebrates her 85th birthday. We extend our deepest 
gratitude for her legacy of love and commitment, which continues to 
inspire everyone around her, and we hope that this year around the sun 
is the best one yet.

                          ____________________
```
