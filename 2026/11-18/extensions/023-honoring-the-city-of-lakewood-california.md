# HONORING THE CITY OF LAKEWOOD, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING THE CITY OF LAKEWOOD, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Lakewood, a city I will unfortunately no longer represent in the 
120th Congress.
  Lakewood is a beautiful city with strong community engagement, active 
businesses, and a dedicated city government. As a bordering city to 
Long Beach, where I served as Mayor of for eight years, Lakewood is a 
place I have appreciated a close partnership and a shared sense of 
community for a long time.
  There are many local service clubs that are very active in the 
community, such as the Soroptimist International of Lakewood/Long 
Beach, the Rotary Club of Lakewood, the Lakewood Jaycees, and more. The 
City of Lakewood and the Rotary Club of Lakewood have even partnered 
together to create Project Shepard. With roots deep in the city, each 
of these service groups demonstrates the dedicated efforts made to 
build and support the community.
  Community members are always eager to attend the variety of events 
hosted in the city such as the Pan American Fiesta, Lakewood's Lemonade 
Day, the Lakewood Run, Lakewood's Youth Hall of Fame awards, the Earth 
Walk, and summer Concerts in the Park. Whether hosted by the city or by 
a local organization, the residents are always enthusiastic to show up 
and support each other. Lakewood is also known for its abundance of 
trees and dedication to urban forestry have also earned the city a 
national designation as Tree City USA by the Arbor Day Foundation.
  The City of Lakewood also has a strong commitment to building 
connections between the community and first responders. Local law 
enforcement hosts regular events such as Coffee or Donuts with a Cop, 
National Night Out, tours of the Lakewood Sheriff's Station, and Public 
Safety Expos. There is great emphasis on public safety appreciation 
with the city hosting an annual Award of Valor to show appreciation for 
first responders and residents who acted heroically.
  During my time as Lakewood's representative, I had the pleasure of 
visiting NGHBRS Kitchen for lunch, as well as visiting both Mayfair 
High School and Artesia High School to meet students and tour the 
campuses. I was also able to host a roundtable for small business 
organizations at Monte Verde Park that fostered important conversations 
with the business community.
  Lastly, it was a pleasure to work with the dedicated city staff and 
councilmembers throughout the annual processes for Community Project 
Funding requests. First, we worked together to secure $850,000 for the 
Palms Park Community Center Improvement Project that will remove 
blight, improve energy efficiency, and renovate the restrooms to ensure 
ADA accessibility. Second, we worked to secure $1,031,000 to provide 
law enforcement with technologies for enhanced public safety. These 
community projects will positively impact the city for years to come, 
and I am thrilled to have been part of the process.
  It was a privilege to partner with the City of Lakewood, local small 
businesses, and residents throughout these past four years to 
contribute to the strong and growing fabric of the community. I am 
honored to have represented this amazing city in Congress.
```
