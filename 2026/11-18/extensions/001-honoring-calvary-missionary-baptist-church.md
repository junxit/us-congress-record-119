# HONORING CALVARY MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
               HONORING CALVARY MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Calvary Missionary Baptist Church of Midnight, Mississippi, and to 
honor its longstanding history of faith, fellowship, and service.
  The story of Calvary Missionary Baptist Church began in 1881, when a 
group of believers organized a church approximately two miles south of 
Silver City, Mississippi, in a small community know as Mill Port. 
Without a permanent church building, the congregation initially 
gathered for worship in a brush arbor. A one-room building was later 
constructed to serve as the congregation's first church home.
  Under the leadership of its first pastor, Reverend Whitting, the 
congregation continued to grow. The church later relocated to Midnight, 
Mississippi, where another one-room building was constructed. By 1895, 
under leadership of Reverend G.P. Phillips, the congregation was 
working toward the construction of a larger place of worship while 
continuing to carry the name Calvary.
  Over the generations, Calvary has been strengthened by dedicated 
pastors, deacons, church mothers, trustees, officers, and members who 
have given their time and talents to sustain its ministry. The church 
has also grown physically alongside its congregation. During the latter 
half of the 20th century, members undertook projects that included new 
sanctuary pews, improvements to the church interior and exterior, and 
work on the church parking area. Under the leadership of Reverend R.L. 
Reed, the church added a fellowship hall and kitchen, further expanding 
its ability to serve its members and community.
  Calvary's history continued into 21st century under the leadership of 
pastors including Reverend Ronald Woodruff, Reverend Orlando Franklin, 
Reverend Alan Blake, Sr., and Reverend Tuwana Earl Ray. Throughout 
these transitions, generations of church leaders and members continued 
the work begun by the congregation's founders.
  From its humble beginnings in 1881 to the present day, Calvary 
Missionary Baptist Church has remained committed to its community and 
its Christian mission. The church describes its calling as being a 
``beacon of light and hope'' and continuing to spread the good news of 
the Gospel.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Calvary 
Missionary Baptist Church for its enduring legacy and for the 
generations of men and women whose faith, service, and dedication have 
sustained this historic congregation. I extend my best wishes to the 
entire Calvary church family as they celebrate their remarkable history 
and look toward the future.
```
