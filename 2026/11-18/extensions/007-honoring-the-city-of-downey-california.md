# HONORING THE CITY OF DOWNEY, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE CITY OF DOWNEY, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Downey, a city I have represented in Congress for four years but 
will sadly no longer represent in the 120th Congress.
  Downey is an amazing city that has an incredibly strong sense of 
community, a flourishing economy, and a lively city government. I am 
grateful for their partnership, the positive changes we were able to 
make together, and the relationships we built.
  Downey's sense of service is highlighted by its many clubs and 
organizations that are very active in the community, such as the 
American Legion Post No. 270, Gangs Out of Downey, the Downey Rose 
Float Association, the Soroptimist International of Downey, the Rotary 
Club of Downey, the Kiwanis Club of Downey, as well as the Downey 
Coordinating Council, and many more.
  Community members are always enthusiastic to attend the variety of 
events hosted in the city such as Downey Pride, the TLC Family Resource 
Center's annual 5k run, volunteer clean-ups known as Keep Downey 
Beautiful, the Special Needs Awareness and Resource Fair, the Downey 
Chamber of Commerce's Annual Christmas Parade, and so many more. 
Whether hosted by the city or by a local organization, the residents 
are always ready to show their support.
  The City of Downey is home to the Columbia Memorial Space Center, a 
museum that commemorates the astronauts whose lives were lost on 
February 1, 2003 in the Space Shuttle Columbia's tragedy. It also 
highlights Southern California's aerospace history with an interactive 
science hub that hosts a variety of events from workshops to astronomy 
nights, and it will soon be home to the 122 feet long and 35 feet tall 
mock-up of the historic Inspiration space shuttle.
  During my time as Downey's representative, I had the pleasure of 
visiting small businesses such as Mariachi Bakery, Oh Comadre Candles, 
Fernando's Restaurant for a Peruvian lunch, and Pieloon for some 
Thanksgiving

[[Page E1239]]

pies. I also got to tour the Downey Historical Society and host a 
townhall for local elected officials.
  Lastly, it was an honor to work with city staff and councilmembers 
throughout the processes for Community Project Funding requests. First, 
we worked together to secure $850,000 for a Community Empowerment Hub 
at the Downey Family YMCA that will provide various amenities and 
programs to thousands of members, many of whom are from underserved 
communities. Second, we secured $1,000,000 for Downtown Downey 
renovations that will transform a city-owned parking lot into a public 
space with walkways, seating areas, landscaping, and recreational 
activities. Third, we worked to secure $250,000 for upgrades to the 
public restrooms at Rio San Gabriel Park that will ensure 
accessibility, sanitation, and comfort for park visitors.
  It was a pleasure to partner with the City of Downey, local 
organizations, small businesses, and residents throughout these past 
four years to contribute to the strong and growing fabric of the 
community. I am honored to have represented this amazing city in 
Congress.
```
