# HONORING AKEEM THOMAS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                         HONORING AKEEM THOMAS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Akeem Thomas, professionally known as Akeem Ali, an artist, 
entertainer, veteran, and native of Jackson, Mississippi, whose work 
reflects the service creativity, and cultural contributions of the 
State of Mississippi.
  Mississippi has made enduring contributions to American music and 
culture. From blues and gospel to soul, rhythm and blues, and hip-hop, 
the state has produced artists whose work has helped shape the Nation's 
artistic landscape. Mr. Thomas stands among a new generation of 
Mississippi creatives whose talent and determination continue that 
legacy.
  Prior to and alongside his career in entertainment, Mr. Thomas served 
as a member of the Army National Guard. His military service 
demonstrates a commitment to duty, discipline, and service to country. 
These same qualities are reflected in his professional journey as an 
artists who has continued to pursue his craft with focus, perseverance, 
and purpose.
  As Akeem Ali, Mr. Thomas gained wider recognition through his 
breakout success ``Keemy Casanova,'' which introduced national 
audiences to his distinctive style, lyricism, and creative vision. 
Through projects including Rollin, Mack in the Day, Texture Tapes, and 
D.E.A.D., he has continued to build a body of work that reflects 
originality, performance, and a deep connection to the musical 
traditions that shaped him.
  Mr. Thomas' contributions extend beyond his individual 
accomplishments. He has acknowledged and paid respect to the 
Mississippi rappers, musicians, and entertainers who came before him, 
recognizing the foundation they established for future artists from the 
state. In doing so, he helps preserve and elevate Mississippi's place 
within the broader history of American music and entertainment.
  He has also used his platform to support and uplift his peers in the 
entertainment industry. As his work has reached broader audiences, he 
has helped bring attention to other Mississippi creatives and 
introduced fellow artists to opportunities on a larger stage. His 
commitment to collaboration reflects the importance of community, 
mentorship, and shared progress within the arts.
  Though his career has carried his work into larger creative spaces, 
Mr. Thomas continues to represent Jackson and Mississippi with 
distinction. His journey reflects the ability of Mississippi artists to 
contribute meaningfully to national culture while remaining connected 
to the communities and traditions that helped shape them.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Akeem 
Thomas, professionally known as Akeem Ali, for his service to our 
Nation, his contributions to music and entertainment, and his role in 
advancing the cultural legacy of Jackson and the State of Mississippi.
```
