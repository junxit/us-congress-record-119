# HONORING REPRESENTATIVE TRENEE McGEE AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA YOUNG CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING REPRESENTATIVE TRENEE McGEE AS A 2026 SHE LEADS AMERICA YOUNG 
                     CHRISTIAN WOMAN OF DISTINCTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Representative 
Trenee McGee as a 2026 She Leads America Young Christian Woman of 
Distinction Award recipient.
  Representative Trenee McGee serves Connecticut's 116th House 
District, representing West Haven. Elected in December 2021, she became 
the youngest Black woman elected to the Connecticut General Assembly. 
Her public service combines legislative advocacy, community engagement, 
and a commitment to the well-being of women and families.
  Before entering state government, Trenee served on the West Haven 
City Council as its youngest member. She brings experience as a 
speaker, advocate, and artist, engaging audiences nationally and 
internationally.
  Trenee earned a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Acting from Marymount 
Manhattan College and a certificate from the Umbra Institute in 
Perugia, Italy. Beginning her performing career in television 
commercials at age three, she developed a commitment to artistic 
expression and education.
  In 2020, she established TDM Productions & Acting Studio to nurture 
emerging artists and encourage their creative development. Her work 
connects the arts with opportunities for personal growth and community 
participation.
  A Whole Life advocate, Trenee champions support for women, children, 
and families. She joined the board of Pro-Black, Pro-Life in 2021 and 
identifies with Democrats for Life.
  Through public office, advocacy, and the arts, Trenee continues 
encouraging civic involvement, developing young talent, and fostering 
dialogue about the dignity and value of life.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this 
outstanding woman of distinction.
```
