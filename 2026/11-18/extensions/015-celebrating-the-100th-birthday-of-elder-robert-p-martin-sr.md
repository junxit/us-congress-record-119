# CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF ELDER ROBERT P. MARTIN, SR.

**Speaking:** Carter, Troy A. (C001125) [D-LA]

```
     CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF ELDER ROBERT P. MARTIN, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY A. CARTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with great pride 
and deep gratitude to recognize a remarkable milestone in the life of a 
respected elder, spiritual leader, and treasured member of our New 
Orleans community, Elder Robert P. Martin, Sr., who celebrated his 
100th birthday on April 12, 2026.
  A century of life is a rare and extraordinary blessing. It represents 
not only longevity, but a lifetime of faith, wisdom, perseverance, and 
service to others.
  Elder Martin was born in 1926, a time when America and the world 
looked very different. That year, Calvin Coolidge served as President 
of the United States, the Nation was in the height of the Roaring 
Twenties, and the average annual wage was about $1,200. A gallon of 
gasoline cost about twenty cents, and a new home averaged roughly 
$7,700.
  It was also a time when the cultural heartbeat of America was being 
shaped by the powerful sounds of jazz emerging from New Orleans, led by 
musical pioneers such as Louis Armstrong. While the Nation experienced 
growth and change, African Americans in the South continued to endure 
the harsh realities of segregation and inequality.
  From those humble beginnings, Elder Martin's life would span some of 
the most defining moments in modern history. Over the past one hundred 
years, he has witnessed the Great Depression, World War II, the Civil 
Rights Movement, the rise of modern technology, and historic progress 
in the struggle for justice and equality.
  Through every chapter of this remarkable century, Elder Martin has 
remained steadfast in his faith, his character, and his devotion to his 
community. As an Elder and respected spiritual leader, he has helped 
guide generations with wisdom, humility, and a quiet strength that has 
uplifted all who have had the privilege to know him. His life reflects 
the enduring values of faith, family, service, and perseverance.
  The Scriptures remind us of the blessing of long-life lived-in 
righteousness. As it is written in Psalm 92:14: ``They shall still bear 
fruit in old age; they shall be fresh and flourishing.'' Indeed, the 
life of Elder Robert P. Martin, Sr. stands as living proof of that 
promise.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize Elder Robert P. 
Martin, Sr. as he celebrates this extraordinary milestone of 100 years. 
His life is a testament to faith, resilience, and the power of a life 
well lived.
  On behalf of the people of Louisiana's 2nd Congressional District, I 
offer my heartfelt congratulations and deepest appreciation for his 
century of wisdom, leadership, and inspiration.
  May God continue to bless Elder Martin, his family, and all those who 
gather to celebrate this historic and joyous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
