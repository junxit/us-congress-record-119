# CONGRATULING JIM SCHIELEIN FOR HIS DISTINGUISHED PUBLIC SERVICE TO LEE COUNTY

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
CONGRATULING JIM SCHIELEIN FOR HIS DISTINGUISHED PUBLIC SERVICE TO LEE 
                                 COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize Jim Schielein for 
his decades of distinguished advocacy and public service to the people 
of Lee County, Illinois. Throughout his career, Jim has demonstrated 
exceptional leadership and a deep commitment to strengthening his 
community, supporting local agriculture, and serving the people of Lee 
County.
  A fifth-generation Illinois farmer, Jim was raised in Chillicothe 
before moving to Dixon in 1985 to run his family's farm operation in 
Lee County. His dedication to agriculture extended far beyond the farm; 
however, and he went on to serve as the President of the Lee County 
Farm Bureau from 1998 to 2000 and on the Illinois Farm Bureau Board of 
Directors from 2000 to 2010. Eventually, his decades of agricultural 
advocacy and service earned him the Lee County Farm Bureau 
Distinguished Service Award, recognizing his longstanding leadership 
among Illinois farmers.
  Jim has also dedicated much of his life to public service. He served 
as the South Dixon Township Supervisor and as a member of both the 
Dixon Public Schools Board of Education and the Lee County Board. Since 
joining the Lee County Board in 2018, Jim served on numerous 
committees, including serving as Chairman of the Finance Committee. 
Through

[[Page E1248]]

this work, he has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to improving 
his community and representing the interests of his fellow residents.
  As Jim prepares to conclude his service on the Lee County Board on 
December 7, 2026, I extend my gratitude for his years of thoughtful 
service and many contributions to Lee County and communities across 
Illinois. His decades of leadership and dedication to public service 
have made a lasting impact on his community, and I wish him and his 
family all the best in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
