# HONORING JEROME TIMMONS, JR.

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                      HONORING JEROME TIMMONS, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
remarkable public servant, Jerome Timmons, Jr.
  Jerome is a seasoned law enforcement professional, instructor, public 
servant, community leader, and devoted father whose career has been 
defined by service, leadership, perseverance, and commitment to others.
  Jerome was born in Montgomery, Alabama, and raised in St. Mary's, 
Georgia. He developed an early appreciation for discipline, 
responsibility, and public service. He attended Alabama State 
University, where he earned a bachelor's degree in criminal justice, 
laying the educational foundation for a career dedicated to protecting 
and serving others.
  In 2003, Jerome graduated from the Federal Law Enforcement Training 
Center (FLETC) and began his career as a United States Park Ranger. He 
served as a Park Ranger until 2015. After more than a decade in federal 
law enforcement, Jerome pursued another lifelong dream--becoming a 
firefighter. He ultimately returned to what he considers his first 
professional love, law enforcement, continuing his career with the 
Adams County Sheriff's Office and rising to the rank of Lieutenant.
  His journey has also included significant personal adversity. In 
2018, Jerome was diagnosed with Cutaneous T-cell Lymphoma (CTCL). He 
underwent radiation treatment and continued chemotherapy as part of his 
care.
  Throughout that journey, he continued fulfilling his responsibilities 
to his family, profession, and community. The experience strengthened 
the importance he places on perseverance, purpose, family, and making 
the most of every opportunity to positively affect the lives of others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Jerome 
Timmons, Jr. for his service, perseverance, and achievements in the 
Natchez, Mississippi, community.
```
