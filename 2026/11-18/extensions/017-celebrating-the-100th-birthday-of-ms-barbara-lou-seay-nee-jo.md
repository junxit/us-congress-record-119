# CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MS. BARBARA LOU SEAY (NEE JONES)

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
   CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MS. BARBARA LOU SEAY (NEE JONES)

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and celebrate the life of Ms. 
Barbara Lou Seay on the occasion of her 100th birthday, a milestone 
that reflects a century of faith, resilience, and service to family and 
community.
  Born on December 30, 1925, in Detroit's Conant Gardens neighborhood, 
Barbara Lou Seay (nee Jones) was the fourth of nine siblings. She can 
trace her lineage back five generations to an emancipated enslaved 
woman, Rebecca Howlett, who lived on the River Plantation in Chester 
County, Virginia.
  A graduate of Pershing High School, Barbara built a life grounded in 
hard work, faith, and love. She married Precious B. Seay, a World War 
II veteran, in 1947, and together they raised seven children. After the 
tragic loss of her husband in 1963, Barbara persevered, working in food 
service at Harper Hospital and Detroit Public Schools while continuing 
to raise her family.
  A devoted member of City Temple Seventh-day Adventist Church, Barbara 
served her congregation as a choir member, deaconess, funeral 
coordinator, and hospitality committee member. Her home was a place of 
refuge, music, and fellowship, and despite limited resources, she 
ensured that all seven of her children had the opportunity to attend 
college.
  Barbara Lou Seay's life is a testament to faith, sacrifice, and the 
power of community. It is my honor to recognize her 100th birthday and 
the 12th Congressional District celebrates

[[Page E1242]]

a matriarch whose legacy continues to inspire generations.
```
