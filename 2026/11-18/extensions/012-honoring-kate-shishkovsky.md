# HONORING KATE SHISHKOVSKY

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                       HONORING KATE SHISHKOVSKY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the work and service of 
Livonia Democratic Club President Kate Shishkovsky, who has served her 
community to help promote democratic values and progressive policies 
with the goal of making a more welcoming and equitable community in 
Livonia.
  A nurse, attorney, and lifelong Democrat, Kate has spent her life in 
service to her community. Beyond her professional achievements, she has 
contributed years of volunteer work and serving with integrity. Her 
passion to protect voting rights led her to work at local election 
polls for years. While raising her two daughters, Kate balanced a 
career as an attorney before returning to school to follow her passion 
for nursing.
  As President of the Livonia Democratic Club, she channeled that same 
drive into the community, growing the organization into one of the 
largest and most active in Michigan. Motivated by the 2016 election, 
Kate transformed her concern into local action. Inheriting a Livonia 
Democratic Club at low participation--with only 45 members and minimal 
activity--she saw a vital opportunity for revitalization. Through a 
year of tireless recruitment and collaborative organizing, Kate and her 
team turned a small circle into a formidable local force.
  Under Kate's leadership, the LDC became a powerhouse, securing 
historic victories for Livonia's first female mayor, Maureen Miller 
Brosnan, and the city's first Muslim Congresswoman. Kate's service has 
left an indelible mark on Michigan's 12th Congressional District.
  We celebrate her incredible career, thank her for her vision, and 
wish her the very best in this next chapter of retirement.

                          ____________________
```
