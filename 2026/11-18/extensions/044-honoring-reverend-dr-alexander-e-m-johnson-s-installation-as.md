# HONORING REVEREND DR. ALEXANDER E.M. JOHNSON'S INSTALLATION AS PASTOR OF BRENTWOOD BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Menefee, Christian D. (M001245) [D-TX]

```
 HONORING REVEREND DR. ALEXANDER E.M. JOHNSON'S INSTALLATION AS PASTOR 
                      OF BRENTWOOD BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. CHRISTIAN D. MENEFEE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. MENEFEE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Reverend Dr. 
Alexander E.M. Johnson on the occasion of his installation as Pastor of 
the historic Brentwood Baptist Church in Houston, Texas.
  A native Houstonian, Dr. Johnson earned a Bachelor of Arts in 
Religion and African American Studies from Harvard College, a Master of 
Divinity from Princeton Theological Seminary, and a Doctor of Ministry 
in Transformational Leadership from Boston University.
  Dr. Johnson answers the call as the Pastor of Brentwood Baptist 
Church, following the 46 years of outstanding leadership of Pastor 
Emeritus Dr. Joe Samuel Ratliff. He spent 13 years as Executive Pastor 
and Director of Operations at Wheeler Avenue Baptist Church under the 
leadership of Dr. Marcus D. Cosby. He also serves as a Visiting Scholar 
in the African American Studies Department at the University of 
Houston, where he teaches courses on the Black Church in America, Black 
Liberation Theology, and Black Leadership.
  His service reaches beyond the pulpit and the classroom. Dr. Johnson 
serves as Board Chair of the City of Houston's Independent Police 
Oversight Board, to which he was appointed by the late Congressman 
during his time as Mayor of Houston, Sylvester Turner, and sits on the 
Harris County Public Defender's Board. He is a member of the Harvard 
Alumni Association, Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Incorporated, and a 
Life Member of the National Association for the Advancement of Colored 
People (NAACP).
  Rooted in a firm belief in the transformative power of God's love, 
Dr. Johnson seeks to share that love for the edification and 
transformation of all God's children as the newly elected Pastor of 
Brentwood Baptist Church. The Houston community is blessed to have a 
shepherd of his faith, scholarship, and character lead the congregation 
at Brentwood Baptist Church.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas, I congratulate 
Reverend Dr. Alexander E.M. Johnson on his installation, honor the 
legacy of Dr. Ratliff, and extend my best wishes to the Brentwood 
Baptist Church family as they begin this new chapter.
```
