# HONORING THE CITY OF BELLFLOWER, CALIFORNIA

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING THE CITY OF BELLFLOWER, CALIFORNIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City 
of Bellflower, California, a city I will sadly no longer represent in 
the 120th Congress.
  Bellflower is a beautiful city with a very strong sense of community, 
highly engaged service organizations and small businesses, and a 
committed city government. It has been a pleasure to partner with all 
of them.
  The real sense of community is evident from Bellflower's many active 
local service clubs and organizations, such as Caring Connections, the 
Rotary Club of Bellflower, the Bellflower Lions Clubs, the Kiwanis Club 
of Bellflower, and the Bellflower Gardening Club. They are all 
incredibly engaged, always working to build community and support each 
other. These service groups illustrate the dedicated efforts made to 
build and support the community.
  Community members are always eager to attend the variety of events 
hosted in the city such as car shows, holiday tree lightings, Summer 
Streetfests concerts, Community Walks, performances by the Bellflower 
Symphony Orchestra and the Bellflower Civic Chorus, National Night 
Outs, a number of family-oriented events, and so many more. Whether 
hosted by the city or by a local organization, these events are widely 
attended and are met with great support from residents.
  One landmark in the city is beautiful Downtown Bellflower and its 
variety of restaurants, boutiques, unique shops and kiosks, and one of 
my favorite comic bookstores, Metropolis Comics. The Bellflower Chamber 
of Commerce is very active in bringing small businesses together from 
across the city and the

[[Page E1246]]

region to network, learn, grow, and support each other. There is a 
strong emphasis on shopping locally, which is crucial for these small 
businesses to thrive.
  Downtown Bellflower is also home to the Los Angeles County Fire 
Museum which displays historic equipment, educates visitors on the 
history of LA County Fire, and houses Engine 23, the City's first fire 
engine from the 1920s. The City of Bellflower will soon be home to a 
Metro Station for the Southeast Gateway Line, a new light rail that 
will connect Southeast Los Angeles. This project is monumental and will 
provide greater access to public transportation, and I am thrilled to 
continue to support its construction.
  During my time as Bellflower's representative, I had the pleasure of 
visiting small businesses such as Madres Brunch and Yummi Yummi 
Bionicos. I have also made a number of visits to the Southeast Gateway 
Line's future station as I work to highlighting the importance of 
public transportation
  Lastly, it was a pleasure to work with the dedicated City staff and 
councilmembers throughout the annual processes for Community Project 
Funding requests. First, we worked together to secure $870,000 for 
public safety updates to facilities throughout the City of Bellflower, 
with an emphasis on the city's parks. Then, we worked to secure 
$250,000 for the Simms Park Community Center Renovation Project that 
will make several critical renovations to the facility, such as 
transforming the existing auditorium into a multipurpose room, 
modernizing the kitchen, ensuring ADA compliance with the restrooms, 
and so much more.
  Together, these community projects will contribute to building a 
safer and more connected community, and I am honored to have been part 
of the process.
  It was a privilege to partner with the City of Bellflower, highlight 
various small businesses, and engage with residents throughout these 
past four years to contribute to the strong and growing fabric of the 
community. I am honored to have represented this amazing city in 
Congress.
```
