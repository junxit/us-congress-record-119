# HONORING BEAR MARSH MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
             HONORING BEAR MARSH MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 18, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Bear Marsh Missionary Baptist Church of Carrollton, Mississippi, and to 
honor its longstanding history and contributions to the African 
American community of Carroll County.
  Bear Marsh Missionary Baptist Church holds a distinguished place in 
Carroll County history as the first Black church established in the 
county. Its founding represented an important milestone in the 
development of an independent African American faith community and 
provided a place where Black families could worship, fellowship, teach 
their children, and build a stronger community together.
  For generations, Bear Marsh Missionary Baptist Church has served as 
more than a house of worship. The church has been a gathering place for 
families, a source of spiritual encouragement, and an institution that 
has helped preserve the history, traditions, and faith of the African 
American community in Carroll County.
  The legacy of Bear Marsh is reflected in the generations of families 
who have passed through its doors and in the continued commitment of 
its congregation to faith, fellowship, and community. Its history 
reminds us of the important role Black churches have played throughout 
Mississippi in strengthening families, nurturing leadership, and 
sustaining communities through times of both hardship and progress.
  Today, I commend the members and leaders of Bear Marsh Missionary 
Baptist Church for preserving this important legacy. I am proud to 
recognize Bear Marsh for its historic place in Carroll County and for 
the enduring contributions it has made to the people it has served.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Bear Marsh 
Missionary Baptist Church and celebrating its historic legacy and 
continued service to the Carroll County Community.
```
