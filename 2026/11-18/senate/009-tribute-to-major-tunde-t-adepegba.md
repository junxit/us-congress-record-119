# TRIBUTE TO MAJOR TUNDE T. ADEPEGBA

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

```
                   TRIBUTE TO MAJOR TUNDE T. ADEPEGBA

  Mr. PETERS. Madam President, I rise today to recognize MAJ Tunde T. 
Adepegba for his distinguished service to the U.S. Army and his 
contributions to the U.S. Senate and our Nation's defense. Major 
Adepegba has consistently demonstrated leadership, professionalism, and 
commitment to service that exemplifies the finest traditions of our 
Armed Forces.
  Major Adepegba commissioned through the Reserve Officer Training 
Corps in 2011 as a military police officer. His Army career has 
included service as the aide-de-camp to the Deputy Commanding General 
of U.S. Army Recruiting Command and commander of the 58th Military 
Police Company at Schofield Barracks, HI, where he led 163 military law 
enforcement professionals and deployed a platoon in support of 
Operation Orient Shield, the largest annual bilateral military field 
training exercise between the United States and Japan.
  I had the privilege of working closely with Major Adepegba when he 
served in my office as an Army congressional fellow from 2024 to 2025. 
During his fellowship, he handled national security and defense issues 
and supported my work on the Senate Armed Services Committee and Senate 
Appropriations Committee. His operational experience, legal background, 
and understanding of

[[Page S5755]]

the Army provided valuable perspective to my office as we worked on 
issues affecting our servicemembers, military installations, and 
national security. His professionalism, sound judgment, and ability to 
translate complex defense requirements into the legislative process 
made him a valued member of my team.
  Following his fellowship, Major Adepegba continued his service on 
Capitol Hill as an Army budget congressional liaison in the U.S. 
Senate. He brings to this work an impressive academic background, 
including a bachelor of arts in political science from Niagara 
University, a juris doctor from Roger Williams University School of 
Law, and a master of arts in legislative affairs from The George 
Washington University.
  Major Adepegba's service would not have been possible without the 
steadfast support and sacrifice of his family. His wife Basannya and 
their sons Lutalo and Mutebi shared in the long hours and demands 
associated with his military and congressional service. I am grateful 
for their sacrifice and for the support they provided Major Adepegba 
throughout his time serving my office and the U.S. Senate.
  It is my privilege to recognize Major Adepegba for his distinguished 
service to the Army, Congress, and our Nation. I extend my sincere 
appreciation to Tunde, Basannya, Lutalo, and Mutebi for their continued 
commitment and sacrifice, and I wish the Adepegba family continued 
success in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
