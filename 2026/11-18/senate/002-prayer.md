# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who rules the raging of the sea and stills the storms, 
hear the prayers of our lawmakers. May their prayers deepen their faith 
in You, strengthen their commitment to justice, renew their devotion to 
freedom, and enlarge their compassion for all those they are entrusted 
to serve.
  Lord, grant them the wisdom to rise above division, the humility to 
listen to one another, and the courage to pursue what is right, rather 
than merely what is expedient. Remind them that the common good is 
greater than individual interests and that public service is a sacred 
trust. May the righteousness that exults a nation be reflected in their 
deliberations, decisions, and conduct.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
