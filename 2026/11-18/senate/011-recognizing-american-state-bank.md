                    RECOGNIZING AMERICAN STATE BANK

● Ms. ERNST. Madam President, as chair of the U.S. Senate 
Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize 
an outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize 
American State Bank of Clarke County, IA, as the Senate Small Business 
of the Week.
  In 1987, Osceola, IA, lost its primary banking institution amid 
difficult economic conditions in the Midwest. The failed bank left 
behind $8.6 million in assets and $7.7 million in deposits. American 
State Bank was founded to help restore local banking services to the 
community. The Schipper, Kale, Reed, Emary, and Garnet families came 
together to acquire the bank, ensuring residents would have a choice in 
where to do their banking. On April 24, 1987, American State Bank was 
officially opened in Osceola, after assuming the deposits and acquiring 
the assets of the failed bank.
  Each of the five families brought their own expertise ranging from 
banking and agriculture to hospitality, entrepreneurship, and retail. 
The bank was built on several core values: honesty, integrity, 
commitment to employees and customers, sound banking, and support for 
the local community. Those principles inspired the business's motto, 
``What a Bank Should Be,'' and continue to guide its work today.
  As it grew, American State Bank opened a second location in downtown 
Osceola in 1990 through the acquisition of another local bank. Since 
then, it has expanded to Winterset and Lamoni. Today, four of the five 
founding families still own the bank, which has grown to 36 full time 
employees led by CEO Kevin Klemesrud. Kevin has led the bank for 16 
years, succeeding founding CEO Jim Schipper, who served for its first 
20 years. While agricultural lending remains its primary focus, the 
bank also offers personal and business banking, investments, insurance, 
and financial education.
  Beyond its banking services, American State Bank is deeply involved 
in the community. The bank makes annual donations to the Future Farmers 
of America and 4-H club, helping children pursue a career in 
agriculture. It has also helped fund childcare facilities, school 
events, and the development of a recreational center. Additionally, the 
bank rewards employees for volunteering and matches those incentives 
with donations to charities of their choice. Last year, employees 
volunteered for a total of 2,644 hours, demonstrating the business' 
continued commitment to the community as it approaches its 40th 
anniversary.
  I am pleased to recognize the five founding families and employees of 
American State Bank for their dedication to serving and strengthening 
communities across southern Iowa. I look forward to their continued 
success and wish them the very best in the years ahead.●
