# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE '

**Measures:** H.R. 2400, H.R. 7954, H.R. 8195, H.R. 8483, H.R. 9237, H.R. 9269, H.R. 9600, H.R. 9785, H.R. 10136

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE '

  At 4:05 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 7954. An act to assist Tribal governments in the 
     management of buffalo and buffalo habitat and the 
     reestablishment of buffalo on Indian land.
       H.R. 8195. An act to require the Secretary of the Interior 
     to develop regional management frameworks for the take of 
     double-crested cormorants.
       H.R. 8483. An act to take certain land in the State of 
     California into trust for the benefit of the Barona Group of 
     Capitan Grande Band of Mission Indians of the Barona 
     Reservation, California, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 9237. An act to amend titles 10 and 38, United States 
     Code, and other Federal laws, to improve benefits for 
     veterans and the administration of the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs.
       H.R. 9269. An act to extend the authorization of the 
     African American Civil Rights Network.
       H.R. 9600. An act to approve the location of a 
     commemorative work for Thomas Paine, America's indispensable 
     and unsung Founding Father.
       H.R. 9785. An act to direct the Secretary of the Interior 
     to conduct studies to determine the feasibility of 
     constructing projects to supply municipal, rural, and 
     industrial water in certain parts of the Missouri River 
     basin, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 10136. An act to require the Secretary of Agriculture 
     to convey the Crossett Experimental Forest to the State of 
     Arkansas, and for other purposes.

  The message further announced that the House agreed to the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2400) to take certain Federal land in 
the State of California into trust for the benefit of the Pit River 
Tribe.
```
