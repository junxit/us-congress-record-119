# Text of Senate Amendment 7026

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
  SA 7026. Mr. VAN HOLLEN submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6933 submitted by Mr. Van Hollen (for himself, 
Ms. Alsobrooks, Mr. Coons, and Ms. Blunt Rochester) and intended to be 
proposed to the amendment SA 6883 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mrs. 
Capito (for herself, Mr. Lee, Mr. Whitehouse, and Mr. Heinrich)) to the 
bill H.R. 2347, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
from gross income any damages, other than punitive damages, received on 
account of any sexual acts or sexual contact; which was ordered to lie 
on the table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 1 of the amendment, strike line 3 and all 
     that follows through page 2, line 9, and insert the 
     following:
       (5) Voluntary remand.--
       (A) In general.--For a project that has a Federal 
     authorization or permit on the date of enactment of this Act, 
     notwithstanding any outstanding authorizations or permits, a 
     court shall only grant a remand requested by a Federal 
     department or agency that is pending as of November 11, 2026, 
     or is substantially similar to a remand request pending on 
     November 11, 2026, for the same project, if the court finds 
     that the Federal department or agency has established grounds 
     for action under subparagraph (B), (C), or (D) of paragraph 
     (1).
       (B) Clarifications.--
       (i) Action.--An order granting a remand under subparagraph 
     (A) shall not, by itself, constitute a court order requiring 
     action under paragraph (1)(A).
       (ii) Special rule.--Nothing in subparagraph (A) affects the 
     application of the special rule described in paragraph 
     (4)(E).
                                 ______
```
