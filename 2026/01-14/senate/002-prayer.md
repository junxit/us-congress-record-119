# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God, You have blessed us beyond our deserving, enabling our 
Nation to continue to strive to be the land of the free and the home of 
the brave. Remind us once more that freedom isn't free.
  Inspire our national leaders to be people of diligence, integrity, 
and prayer. May they claim Your promise in II Chronicles 7:14: that if 
they humble themselves, pray, repent, and seek You earnestly, You will 
hear their prayers, forgive their transgressions, and heal our land.
  And, Lord, guide the fall 2025 page class in all of their tomorrows.
  We pray in Your magnificent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
