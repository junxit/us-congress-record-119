# TRUST VERSUS WASTE

**Speaking:** Shreve, Jefferson (S001229) [R-IN]

```
                           TRUST VERSUS WASTE

  (Mr. SHREVE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SHREVE. Mr. Speaker, across my home State of Indiana, I hear the 
same thing time and again. People work hard. They pay their taxes, and 
they expect Washington to treat their dollars with respect.
  Hoosier hospitality means that we help neighbors who truly need it, 
but fraud turns this virtue into a target, and hardworking Hoosiers 
foot the bill.
  This is not left versus right. It is honesty versus the dishonest, 
trust versus waste. For every dollar stolen by fraud, it is a dollar we 
cannot spend on public safety, on our national security, or on helping 
working families get ahead.
  We owe taxpayers accountability, stronger oversight, and closed 
loopholes that invite abuse.
  Protect the taxpayers. Protect the programs, and punish the scammers.

                          ____________________
```
