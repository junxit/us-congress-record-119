# CONGRATULATING WPIAL CHAMPIONS

**Speaking:** Deluzio, Christopher R. (D000530) [D-PA]

```
                     CONGRATULATING WPIAL CHAMPIONS

  (Mr. DELUZIO asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DELUZIO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate and congratulate 
the many high school teams in Pennsylvania's 17th Congressional 
District that won their WPIAL championships this fall season. Those 
champions are as follows:
  In football, they are Aliquippa, Avonworth, and Seton LaSalle.
  In boys' soccer, they are Fox Chapel, West Allegheny, and Deer Lakes.
  In girls' soccer, they are North Allegheny and Fox Chapel.
  In girls' tennis, they are Shady Side Academy and Quaker Valley.
  In girls' volleyball, they are Beaver, Avonworth, and Eden Christian.
  In boys' golf, they are South Fayette and Shady Side Academy.
  In field hockey, they are Pine-Richland, Fox Chapel, and Shady Side 
Academy.
  In boys' cross-country, they are Quaker Valley.
  In girls' cross-country, they are Shady Side Academy.
  Mr. Speaker, I say it a lot: We make champions in western 
Pennsylvania.
  I congratulate all the winners. They make us proud at home.
```
