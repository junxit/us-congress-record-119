# TRUMP AND REPUBLICANS WINNING FOR FAMILIES

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
               TRUMP AND REPUBLICANS WINNING FOR FAMILIES

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, led by Republicans and 
signed into law by President Donald Trump, the Working Families Tax 
Cuts is reversing harmful Biden-era regulations and putting more money 
in the pockets of American families and creating jobs.
  The law is delivering bigger paychecks, lower costs, and real results 
for hardworking families, including: expanding childcare access, 
permanently expanding the child tax credit; supporting American jobs, 
with tax breaks for loans on ``Made in America'' cars; keeping the 
border secure, protecting American families with more Border Patrol 
agents; 4-year low gas prices; reversing Biden-era regulations; 
modernizing air traffic control; no tax on tips, overtime, and Social 
Security.
  President Trump and House Republicans are producing real results, led 
by Speaker   Mike Johnson.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump, with Putin 
defending mass murderer Khomeini as Donald Trump courageously 
encourages the people of Iran for freedom.
```
