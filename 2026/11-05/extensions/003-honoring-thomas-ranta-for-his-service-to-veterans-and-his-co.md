# HONORING THOMAS RANTA FOR HIS SERVICE TO VETERANS AND HIS COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
  HONORING THOMAS RANTA FOR HIS SERVICE TO VETERANS AND HIS COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Thomas Ranta for being 
named the 2026 Marquette County Veteran of the Year. I thank him for 
his decades of dedicated service to our Nation, our Veterans, and the 
Marquette County community.
  Tom learned the importance of helping Veterans from his father, a man 
who proudly wore the uniform during World War II. After spending 13 
years in the United States Army and National Guard, Tom continued his 
commitment to Veterans through his work with the American Legion and 
other Veterans organizations.
  Tom's involvement in his community began with his own experience as a 
1967 American Legion Boys State delegate. He later became the Boys 
State Chairman for ten years, helping send 56 young men to the Boys 
State program and preparing them for the experience.
  Tom has dedicated countless hours to honoring our Nation's Veterans. 
Since 2010, he has spearheaded the ``Flags Over School Program,'' 
coordinating Veterans Day programs at nine schools and two nursing 
homes. Each year, he leads efforts to display 143 American flags at 
Harlow Park and coordinates the placement of approximately 1,400 flags 
at Veterans' graves in Park and Holy Cross Cemeteries. For nearly a 
decade, Tom has coordinated Memorial Day ceremonies at both cemeteries. 
After learning that some residents at the Jacobetti Home for Veterans 
could not attend, Tom established a separate Memorial Day ceremony 
there so Veterans and their families could participate in honoring 
those who made the ultimate sacrifice.
  Tom has held numerous leadership positions with American Legion Post 
44, including Post Commander and Boys State Chairman, and is also a 
member of VFW Post 6 and the Marquette County Veterans Alliance. He 
also served as a Lieutenant with the Michigan State Police for 33 
years.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Thomas Ranta. I thank him 
for a lifetime of dedication to our Nation, our Veterans, and the State 
of Michigan.
```
