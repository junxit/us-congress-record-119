# RECOGNIZING JORGE ALVAREZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING JORGE ALVAREZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jorge Alvarez 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Jorge has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Jorge, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Jorge's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Mandalay Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Jorge Alvarez on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
