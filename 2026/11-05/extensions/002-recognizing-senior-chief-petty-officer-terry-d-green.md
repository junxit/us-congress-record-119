# RECOGNIZING SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER TERRY D. GREEN

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
         RECOGNIZING SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER TERRY D. GREEN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired Navy veteran, 
Senior Chief Petty Officer Terry D. Green of Waxahachie, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our 
country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  HMCS (FMF) Green served 27 years in the U.S. Navy as a Hospital 
Corpsman, including 20 years supporting the U.S. Marine Corps in 
infantry and training commands. He completed multiple deployments in 
support of Operations Iraqi Freedom and Enduring Freedom and served 15 
years as an Independent Duty Corpsman. Throughout his career, HMCS 
(FMF) Green held many positions including a Fleet Marine Force Warfare 
Specialist and Curriculum Developer for the 1st Marine Division Combat 
Trauma Management Course. He also served as a Tactical Combat Casualty 
Care (TCCC) and Tactical Emergency Casualty Care (TECC) instructor and 
subject matter expert, contributing to the initial adoption of TCCC as 
the standard of care across Marine Corps operational forces.
  HMCS (FMF) Green received numerous ribbons, awards and commendations 
including the Meritorious Service Medal and Navy and Marine Corps 
Commendation Medals.
  He continues his commitment to service through mentorship, education, 
and support for veterans and first responders. As the sole Navy 
Corpsman member of the Waxahachie Marine Corps League, HMCS (FMF) Green 
helps lead the annual Marine Corps Ball, preserving Marine Corps and 
Naval traditions. As a founding volunteer mentor with the Ellis County 
Veterans Treatment Court, he now serves as Mentor Coordinator, 
strengthening the program's mentorship efforts. HMCS (FMF) Green also 
serves on the Ellis County Sheriff's Office Citizens Training Advisory 
Council and volunteers as a Stop the Bleed, Tactical Combat Casualty 
Care, and Tactical Emergency Casualty Care instructor, providing free 
lifesaving trauma training to local law enforcement and first 
responders.
  HMCS (FMF) Green's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and 
civic leadership. His continued dedication to veterans, first 
responders, and the broader community reflects a lasting commitment to 
service and leadership.
```
