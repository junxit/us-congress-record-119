# RECOGNIZING TRACY Y. JOHNSON AS OKALOOSA SUPPORT STAFFER OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
  RECOGNIZING TRACY Y. JOHNSON AS OKALOOSA SUPPORT STAFFER OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tracy Y. Johnson 
for being named Okaloosa Support Staffer of the Year in recognition of 
her outstanding dedication and leadership.
  Tracy exemplifies professionalism and a passion for serving others. 
Through her extensive training in early childhood education, staff 
leadership, and child development, she has demonstrated an unwavering 
commitment to professional growth and the well-being of children and 
families.
  Her numerous certifications and credentials, including her Child 
Development Associate Credential and Florida DCF Director Credential, 
further reflect her dedication to excellence and continuous learning.
  Tracy's contributions, leadership, and commitment to those she serves 
make her truly deserving of this recognition. I congratulate her on 
this well-earned honor and thank her for the positive difference she 
continues to make in her community.
  I congratulate Tracy Y. Johnson on being named Staffer of the Year.

                          ____________________
```
