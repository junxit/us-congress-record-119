# RECOGNIZING THE 103RD ANNIVERSARY OF THE FOUNDING OF THE REPUBLIC OF TURKIYE

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 103RD ANNIVERSARY OF THE FOUNDING OF THE REPUBLIC OF 
                                TURKIYE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 103rd 
anniversary of the founding of the Republic of Turkiye and to extend my 
congratulations to the people of Turkiye and to Turkish Americans in 
Michigan and across the United States.
  On October 29, 1923, Mustafa Kemal Ataturk proclaimed the Republic of 
Turkiye, launching a new era of national sovereignty, modernization, 
and reform. Over a century later, Turkiye still sits at the crossroads 
of Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, a location of real strategic 
consequence.
  The relationship between our two nations rests on diplomatic, 
economic, cultural, and security cooperation. Those ties have endured 
for generations, and they continue to open doors for greater 
collaboration and mutual understanding.
  Turkiye is a steadfast NATO ally, anchoring the Alliance's 
southeastern flank. In 74 years of membership, Turkiye has stood 
shoulder to shoulder with the United States and our allies, and in 2026 
it hosted the NATO Summit in Ankara. Turkiye remains central to 
collective defense, regional security, and the pursuit of peace.
  That friendship is strengthened by the Turkish American community. 
Across Michigan, Turkish American physicians, educators, scientists, 
engineers, entrepreneurs, artists, and civic leaders make our 
communities stronger and add to the economic and cultural vitality of 
our state.
  As Turkish Americans gather to celebrate Republic Day, we honor their 
heritage, their accomplishments, and their commitment to building 
stronger ties between the American and Turkish people.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in congratulating the people of Turkiye 
and Turkish Americans in Michigan and across the country on the 103rd 
anniversary of the Republic of Turkiye. May our friendship continue to 
grow, grounded in mutual respect, shared prosperity, and an unwavering 
commitment to peace and security.
```
