# HONORING THE GOLDEN STATE VALKYRIES

**Speaking:** Pelosi, Nancy (P000197) [D-CA]

```
                  HONORING THE GOLDEN STATE VALKYRIES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. NANCY PELOSI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Ms. PELOSI. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate the Golden State 
Valkyries on winning the 2026 WNBA Championship and bringing a new 
banner home to the San Francisco Bay Area. With a thrilling Game 7 
victory over the Atlanta Dream at Chase Center, the Valkyries made 
history by capturing the title in just their second season. Their 
energy, determination and resilience have made our City and the entire 
Bay Area proud.
  As a fan since their first tip-off, I have been thrilled to watch the 
Valkyries soar--and to join the devoted fans whose enthusiasm has made 
Ballhalla a safe and magical home for this team and its supporters. 
Together, the players, owners, coaches and staff have built a 
championship team that inspires countless young women to pursue their 
dreams and advances the proud legacy of women on the court and women in 
sports.
  This championship belongs, too, to the community that embraced our 
Valkyries from the beginning, painted the community with bursts of 
lavender and cheered the team on every step of the way. On behalf of 
San Francisco, I congratulate our new WNBA champions on this historic 
achievement. Go Valkyries.

                          ____________________
```
