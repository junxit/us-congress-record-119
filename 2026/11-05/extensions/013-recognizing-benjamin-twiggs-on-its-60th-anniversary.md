# RECOGNIZING BENJAMIN TWIGGS ON ITS 60TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
          RECOGNIZING BENJAMIN TWIGGS ON ITS 60TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Benjamin Twiggs 
of Traverse City, Michigan, as it celebrates its 60th anniversary, and 
to commend its enduring commitment to Michigan's cherry industry and 
the farming families who sustain it.
  Founded in 1966, Benjamin Twiggs has become a fixture in Traverse 
City, a community known for its rich agricultural heritage and its tart 
cherries. In 2020, fifth-generation fruit farmer Leisa Eckerle Hankins 
acquired the store with a clear mission: to strengthen the connection 
between local agriculture and the customers who support it. She later 
learned that her father had invested in the company at its founding, 
more than five decades earlier. Her family's story with the business 
had come full circle.
  Today, Leisa and her family continue to build on that legacy by 
promoting Michigan-grown cherries, teaching visitors about the region's 
agricultural traditions, and standing behind local farmers. Through its 
products and outreach, Benjamin Twiggs shows the hard work of 
Michigan's agricultural community and carries the value of our state's 
cherry industry well beyond the orchard. Leisa has also helped 
strengthen the industry by establishing a cherry grower alliance that 
brings together more than 100 growers around their shared future.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Benjamin Twiggs and the Eckerle Hankins 
family on 60 years of business and service to Michigan's cherry 
industry. I commend their dedication to our state's agricultural 
heritage, and I thank them for supporting the farmers, families, and 
communities that make Northern Michigan such a special place to call 
home.
```
