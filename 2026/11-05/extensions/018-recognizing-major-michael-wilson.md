# RECOGNIZING MAJOR MICHAEL WILSON

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                    RECOGNIZING MAJOR MICHAEL WILSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired U.S. Air Force 
veteran Major Michael Wilson of Midlothian, Texas, with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. His distinguished military service and continued 
commitment to public service and the North Texas community are a 
shining example of honor and duty. Major Wilson honorably served in the 
U.S. Air Force from January 1999 to March 2019, completing a 
distinguished 20-year career in both active duty and the Air Force 
Reserve. He progressed from enlisted Airman to commissioned officer and 
ultimately commanded the 301st Security Forces Squadron. His military 
honors include the Meritorious Service Medal, Air Force Commendation 
Medal, Air Force Achievement Medal, and numerous other military honors.
  Among the most significant experiences of Major Wilson's military 
career was serving during the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks, an 
experience that influenced his decision to become an officer and 
ultimately deploy to the Middle East. During his deployment to Saudi 
Arabia as an Operations Officer and Deputy Commander, he worked 
alongside U.S. and Saudi security forces and Explosive Ordnance 
Disposal personnel to safely destroy errant Tomahawk missiles, for 
which members of the team received military recognition.
  Following his military service, Major Wilson continued his commitment 
to public service through a distinguished career with the Arlington 
Police Department, serving as an officer and detective until his 
retirement in 2024. He was nominated for Officer of the Year six 
consecutive years and continues serving the community as a business 
owner, educator, mentor, executive pastor, and police academy 
instructor. As the owner and CEO of Blue Mint Thai & Asian Cuisine and 
Velocity Investing Group, he creates jobs, supports veterans and 
military families, and develops future leaders. He also mentors 
Arlington ISD entrepreneurship students, teaches future law enforcement 
officers, and supports veteran and military organizations throughout 
North Texas.
  Major Wilson's dedication to serving his Nation and community 
exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and civic 
leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation, our 
veterans, and the North Texas community.
```
