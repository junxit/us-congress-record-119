# RECOGNIZING SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER RICHARD BARRETT

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
         RECOGNIZING SENIOR CHIEF PETTY OFFICER RICHARD BARRETT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired U.S. Navy 
veteran Senior Chief Petty Officer Richard Barrett of Ferris, Texas, 
with a Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to 
our country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  SKCS Barrett served in the U.S. Navy from 1972 until his retirement 
in 2004, where he held various leadership roles including Senior Chief 
Petty Officer for the 9th Naval Construction Regiment, Supply Officer, 
Leading Seaman for the 1st Division, and ship's helmsman aboard the USS 
George Dewey. Throughout his service, SKCS Barrett served during the 
Cold War, Vietnam Era, and Gulf War eras, including Operations Desert 
Shield and Desert Storm. He was awarded many military medals, awards 
and commendations including the Navy Commendation Medal.
  Following his military service, SKCS Barrett continued his commitment 
to serving others through leadership in his community. He served as 
Mayor, Mayor Pro Tem, and Alderman for the City of Ferris, Texas, and 
contributed to the community through his service on the Ferris Planning 
and Zoning Board, Ferris Library Board, and Lions Club. During his time 
in community leadership, he helped advance projects that benefited 
generations of Ferris residents, including the development of a golf 
course, a new library, and improvements to local baseball fields. 
Today, SKCS Barrett continues his commitment to the next generation by 
serving as an Instructional Aide for middle school students at Palmer 
ISD.
  SKCS Barrett's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and 
community exemplifies the highest traditions of service and sacrifice. 
I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my sincere 
appreciation for his steadfast commitment to honoring and supporting 
our Nation and community.

                          ____________________
```
