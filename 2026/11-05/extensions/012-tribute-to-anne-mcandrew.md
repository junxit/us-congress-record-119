# TRIBUTE TO ANNE McANDREW

**Speaking:** Calvert, Ken (C000059) [R-CA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ANNE McANDREW

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KEN CALVERT

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. CALVERT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the outstanding 
service and dedication of Ms. Anne McAndrew, a distinguished career 
civil servant who has been instrumental in the financial stewardship 
and advancing the strategic priorities of the Department of War (DoW). 
Throughout her remarkable career spanning over three decades. Ms. 
McAndrew has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to service and to 
our Nation. She has been an invaluable asset to the House 
Appropriations Subcommittee on Defense in our oversight of the defense 
budget and our support for our brave men and women in uniform.
  Ms. McAndrew has over 30 years of federal government experience, 
including more than 20 years in the Senior Executive Service, starting 
her career in the Navy's financial management community serving at the 
Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Navy (Financial Management), 
Naval Air Systems Command, Naval Training Systems Center, and Naval 
Training Center. In 2021, during a presidential transition, she served 
as acting Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller) and during another 
presidential transition in 2025, she performed the duties of the Deputy 
Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller), demonstrating her commitment 
to technical leadership and the trust that she has built with the 
senior leadership of the Department and the country.
  Earlier in her career, Ms. McAndrew served as the Director of the 
Military Personnel and Construction within the Office of the Under 
Secretary of War for Comptroller OUSW(C). She was responsible for all 
fiscal matters regarding the Department's military pay and benefits, 
physical infrastructure on military installations, and global basing 
matters, including overseas posture. Before this assignment, Ms. 
McAndrew served as the Associate Director for Personnel and Health 
Care, where she was responsible for all financial matters related to 
military and civilian compensation, as well as for operation and 
maintenance programs for the Reserve Components.
  Ms. McAndrew's career has been marked by hands-on experience at all 
levels of the DoW, from field activity to major command, Service 
headquarters' staff, and the Office of the Secretary of War. A 
testament to her experience and trust is that she authored the initial 
supplemental requests for the Global War

[[Page E1160]]

on Terrorism following the September 11th terrorist attacks and was 
involved in the recent supplemental request related to the 
contingencies in Iran.
  Ms. McAndrew currently serves as the Deputy Comptroller for Program/
Budget in the OUSW(C). She is the most senior DoW career financial 
manager and advisor, responsible for budget policy, budget formulation, 
congressional budget justification, funds allocation, program 
execution, cost control, and evaluation of the entire Department budget 
totaling more than $1 trillion and incredible complexity. Ms. McAndrew 
holds a Master of Business Administration degree and has received three 
Presidential Rank Awards, two Distinguished and one Meritorious, as 
well as multiple Exceptional Civilian Service Awards and Special Act 
Awards throughout her career.
  I join my colleagues today in acknowledging the extraordinary hard 
work and selfless service of this outstanding patriot. I thank her for 
over thirty years of dedicated service and experience, for her 
unwavering commitment to the Department of War, and for her work on 
behalf of our brave men and women in uniform.

                          ____________________
```
