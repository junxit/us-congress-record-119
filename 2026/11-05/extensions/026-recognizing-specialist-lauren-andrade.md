# RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST LAUREN ANDRADE

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                 RECOGNIZING SPECIALIST LAUREN ANDRADE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Army National Guard 
veteran, Specialist Lauren Andrade of Waxahachie, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. Her dedication to our Nation's 
veterans and her continued service to the community are a shining 
example of honor, compassion, and duty.
  Specialist Andrade served in the U.S. Army National Guard from 
February 2005 to February 2011, including a deployment to Stryker Base 
at Camp Cropper in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom. She received 
numerous military decorations which include the Army Commendation 
Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Global War on Terrorism Service 
Medal, Iraq Campaign Medal with one Bronze Service Star, Army Service 
Ribbon, and Armed Forces Reserve Medal with M Device. While deployed 
overseas, Specialist Andrade witnessed firsthand the hardships faced by 
fellow service members, including personal and financial challenges. 
These experiences gave her a lasting understanding that for many 
veterans, the challenges of service continue long after returning home. 
These challenges include homelessness, financial hardship, family 
instability, and the difficult transition to civilian life.
  Following her military service, Specialist Andrade dedicated herself 
to supporting veterans and their families as the Founder and Executive 
Director of Serenity Veterans Village, a nonprofit organization serving 
homeless, at-risk, and struggling veterans, veteran families, and 
surviving spouses. Through the organization, she provides critical 
assistance including housing, food, clothing, transportation, 
identification documents, employment resources, medical care, and 
connections to other essential services. Serenity Veterans Village also 
provides meals, outreach events, holiday assistance, and ongoing 
support to help veterans rebuild their lives and achieve greater 
stability and independence. Through her leadership, advocacy, and 
personal dedication, Specialist Andrade continues to ensure that 
veterans and their families receive the support, dignity, and 
opportunities they need to move forward.
  Specialist Andrade's dedication to serving her Nation and community 
exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and civic 
leadership. I commend her remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for her steadfast commitment to our Nation's 
veterans, their families, and the Waxahachie community.
```
