# COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ALTADENA LIBRARY DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
  COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ALTADENA LIBRARY DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and commend the 
distinguished Altadena Library District on its 100th Anniversary.
  Established in 1926 as an independent library district, the Altadena 
Libraries were founded on the principle that residents deserve a 
locally governed library that understands and responds to their needs. 
For a century, it has grown into a cherished institution, serving more 
than 41,000 of our neighbors to help them stay informed and access 
quality resources and services.
  Beyond their collection of more than 60,000 books, the Altadena 
Libraries offer far more than access to reading materials. They also 
provide free services to help residents gain independence and 
participate more fully in community life. Through its adult literacy 
program and English as a Second Language (ESL) classes, the libraries 
help residents build their reading and writing skills providing better 
opportunities in pursuing their education and employment.
  As a vital resource for Altadenans, the Libraries are also committed 
to serving our neighbors of all ages. For our youth, the Altadena 
Libraries lay the foundation for lifelong

[[Page E1158]]

reading and learning. From providing Early Literacy Library Kits and 
hosting Storytimes to offering a Teen Volunteer Program, the Libraries 
help youth of all ages develop the essential skills needed to grow into 
successful readers and engaged members of our community.
  When the Eaton Fire devastated the lives and homes of our neighbors 
in January 2025, the Altadena Libraries ensured that our community 
didn't have to face recovery alone. Thanks to the hard work of the 
district staff, the Altadena Main Library reopened less than two months 
after the fire. Since reopening, they have been a hub for residents to 
receive critical recovery resources and find comfort among other 
survivors. Through it all, the Libraries have stayed true to their 
mission to serve Altadena.
  The 100th anniversary of the Altadena Libraries is an incredible 
milestone, and I am proud to honor the legacy of this institution and 
its impact in Altadena and the surrounding region. Because of its 
services, members of our community have been given the tools needed to 
foster literacy, expand access to knowledge, and enrich their lives.
```
