# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF GORDON CORNWELL

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF GORDON CORNWELL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Gordon Cornwell, a respected architect, community leader, and 
longtime resident of Traverse City, Michigan. Through his professional 
work, civic involvement, and commitment to preserving the beauty of the 
Grand Traverse area, Gordon left a lasting mark on the community he 
called home.
  After earning his bachelor's and master's degrees in architecture 
from Syracuse University and serving as a Lieutenant in the United 
States Navy during World War II, Gordon and his wife, Jean, moved to 
Traverse City in 1947. There he established his own architectural 
practice and began a career that would span hundreds of projects 
throughout Northern Michigan. His designs included Traverse City 
Central High School, West Junior High, St. Francis High School, the 
City-County Building, and numerous churches, hospitals, and residences.
  Gordon's commitment to Traverse City extended far beyond his 
profession. He served 35 years on the Traverse City Planning Commission 
and played an instrumental role in drafting the city's original zoning 
ordinance. He was a founding member of the Traverse Area Development 
Fund, now known as the Traverse Area Community Foundation, and served 
as a board member of the Traverse City Chamber of Commerce, where he 
chaired its downtown development committee. Gordon also served for many 
years on the board of Child and Family Services of Northwest Michigan 
and was a member of Kiwanis for six decades. Through these roles, 
Gordon demonstrated a steadfast commitment to strengthening local 
institutions, supporting families, and ensuring Traverse City remained 
a welcoming place to live and work.
  Gordon also understood the importance of preserving the natural 
beauty and recreational opportunities that make Northern Michigan 
special. As a founding member of the Grand Traverse Yacht Club and 
Grand Traverse Ski Club, he helped foster a lasting tradition of 
outdoor recreation in the region. His support for community sailing 
reflected his desire to share his love of Grand Traverse Bay with 
future generations. Above all, Gordon will be remembered for his 
devotion to his family, his faith, and his neighbors. He and Jean 
raised four children in Traverse City and built a life rooted in 
service, integrity, and an appreciation for the community around them. 
His warm smile, ready wit, and universal acceptance of others touched 
the lives of those who knew him.
  Mr. Speaker, I extend my heartfelt condolences to Jean, their family, 
and all who mourn Gordon's passing. I thank Gordon Cornwell for his 
decades of service and his lasting commitment to the people and 
community of Northern Michigan.
```
