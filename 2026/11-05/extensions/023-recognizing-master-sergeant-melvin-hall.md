# RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT MELVIN HALL

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT MELVIN HALL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired U.S. Marine 
Corps veteran Master Sergeant Melvin Hall of Red Oak, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our 
country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  Throughout his 23 years in the U.S. Marine Corps, MSgt Hall served in 
locations around the world including Japan, South Korea, the 
Philippines, Australia, Cuba, the Bahamas, and Hungary. In October 
1979, while assigned to Weapons Company, 2nd Battalion, 4th Marines, 
MSgt Hall survived the Camp Fuji fire in Japan, which claimed the lives 
of 13 Marines from his company. Following his escape from the burning 
barracks, he assisted injured and displaced Marines as survivors were 
gathered and transported for medical treatment. This experience left a 
lasting impact on his life and reinforced his commitment to 
responsibility, compassion, and service to others. MSgt Hall received 
numerous military awards and commendations including the Navy 
Achievement Medal and Meritorious Unit Commendation.
  Following his military retirement, MSgt Hall continued his commitment 
to service through economic development, local government, and 
veterans' initiatives. As an educator at Red Oak High School, he taught 
computer maintenance while serving as a mentor to his students. Former 
students credit him with helping them build confidence, overcome 
obstacles, and prepare for adulthood, extending his impact far beyond 
the classroom. His dedication to students earned him recognition as 
Texas State Technical College Teacher of the Year. MSgt Hall has also 
supported entrepreneurs and rural communities through economic 
development, he has served on the Pecan Hill City Council and community 
boards, and supported veterans through initiatives such as the UNT 
Dallas Veteran Success Center and Warriors Canvas.
  MSgt Hall's distinguished military service, dedication to mentoring 
future generations, and continued commitment to veterans and his 
community reflect the highest traditions of military service and civic 
leadership. His lifelong devotion to helping others and strengthening 
his community is a testament to the enduring impact of service beyond 
the uniform.
```
