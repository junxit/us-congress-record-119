# RECOGNIZING KEIRA DONALDSON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                    in the house of representatives

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Keira Donaldson

  Keira has overcome many challenges along her journey to success,

work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Keira's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Mandalay Middle School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Keira Donaldson on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
