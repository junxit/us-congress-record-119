# RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT ROBERT BELL

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                RECOGNIZING MASTER SERGEANT ROBERT BELL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize retired U.S. Marine 
Corps veteran Master Sergeant Robert Bell of Ennis, Texas, with a 
Congressional Veteran Commendation. His unwavering commitment to our 
country and community is a shining example of honor and duty.
  MSgt Bell served in the U.S. Marine Corps for 19 years with multiple 
deployments to areas including Okinawa, Iraq and Afghanistan. He served 
in leadership roles including leading a four-Marine fire team to 
oversee the training and operations of an infantry battalion of more 
than 800 Marines and Sailors. During his 2007 deployment to Iraq as 
Infantry Platoon Sergeant, MSgt Bell's platoon experienced frequent 
firefights and despite the opportunity to be medically evacuated, he 
remained with his platoon and continued to lead Marines through the 
remainder of their operation. He medically retired from the Wounded 
Warrior Battalion after 19 years, due to wounds received in Iraq. MSgt 
Bell was awarded over 40 medals, awards and commendations including the 
Navy and Marine Corps Commendation Medal with Combat Distinguishing 
Device (Combat ``V'').
  Following his military service, MSgt Bell continued his commitment to 
serving veterans and his community. In 2017, he founded the Ellis 
County Marine Corps League Detachment 1452, which remains active today. 
In 2020, MSgt Bell established the Ellis County Toys for Tots campaign 
where he continues to serve as Campaign Coordinator, helping provide 
Christmas gifts to thousands of local children each year. In 2021, he 
helped preserve the Ellis County Veterans Celebration and now serves as 
Chairman of its Planning Committee and in 2022, he helped establish the 
Heroes Services Coalition, which now serves as the parent organization 
for the celebration. Today, MSgt Bell continues to expand opportunities 
to honor and serve veterans, including through the planned 2027 
Covenant of Remembrance and Service Memorial Day ceremony.
  MSgt Bell's unwavering dedication to serving his Nation and community 
exemplifies the highest traditions of service and sacrifice. I commend 
his remarkable contributions and extend my sincere appreciation for his 
steadfast commitment to honoring and supporting our Nation and 
community.
```
