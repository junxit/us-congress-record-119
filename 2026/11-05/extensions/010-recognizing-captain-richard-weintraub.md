# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN RICHARD WEINTRAUB

**Speaking:** Ellzey, Jake (E000071) [R-TX]

```
                 RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN RICHARD WEINTRAUB

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAKE ELLZEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 5, 2026

  Mr. ELLZEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize U.S. Air Force veteran 
Captain Richard Weintraub of Mansfield, Texas, with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. His distinguished military service and continued 
commitment to public service are a shining example of honor and duty.
  During his military service, Captain Weintraub served from October 
1959 to October 1968 at James Connally Air Force Base, Mather Air Force 
Base, Castle Air Force Base, March Air Force Base, and Andersen Air 
Force Base in Guam. During the Cuban Missile Crisis in October 1962, he 
participated in several 26-hour airborne alert and air-sampling 
missions off the northern coast of the Soviet Union. He also flew 
nuclear alert missions as part of Operation Chrome Dome and completed 
rotations at Andersen Air Force Base in Guam. He later served as an 
intelligence officer and concluded his military career overseeing 
tactical fighter wing maintenance at Homestead Air Force Base.
  His military honors include the Cold War Commendation and recognition 
for completing 2,000 hours aboard the B-52.
  Following his military service, Captain Weintraub continued his 
commitment to public service through appointments by the Arlington City 
Council to the Environmental Commission and Historical Commission, 
where he contributed to the preservation and advancement of his 
community.
  Captain Weintraub's dedication to serving his Nation and community 
exemplifies the highest traditions of military service and civic 
leadership. I commend his remarkable contributions and extend my 
sincere appreciation for his steadfast commitment to our Nation and the 
communities he continues to serve.
```
