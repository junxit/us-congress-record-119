# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 1648, H.Res. 1649, H.R. 10954, H.R. 10955, H.R. 10956, H.R. 10957, H.R. 10958, H.R. 10959, H.R. 10960

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mrs. BIGGS of South Carolina (for herself and Mr. 
             Cloud):
       H.R. 10954. A bill to amend the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act to deem an abortion drug misbranded unless it 
     contains a ``Do Not Flush'' warning label and to require a 
     catch kit, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Commerce.
           By Ms. CHU (for herself, Ms. Moore of Wisconsin, Mr. 
             Evans of Pennsylvania, Mr. Gomez, Ms. Sewell, Ms. 
             Tlaib, Ms. Tokuda, Mr. Thanedar, Ms. Norton, Ms. 
             Crockett, and Mrs. Foushee):
       H.R. 10955. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to modify the age limitations on eligibility for the 
     earned income tax credit; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
           By Mr. KENNEDY of Utah:
       H.R. 10956. A bill to amend the Indian Health Care 
     Improvement Act to allow Indian Health Service scholarship 
     and loan recipients to fulfill service obligations through 
     half-time clinical practice, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources, and in addition to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce, for a period to be 
     subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 10957. A bill to direct the Administrator of the 
     Environmental Protection Agency to study and report on anti-
     microplastic strategies, and to establish a pilot program to 
     demonstrate and evaluate anti-microplastic strategies; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce, and in addition to the 
     Committees on Transportation and Infrastructure, and Science, 
     Space, and Technology, for a period to be subsequently 
     determined by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of 
     such provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the 
     committee concerned.
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 10958. A bill to require companies that sell or offer 
     for sale clothing to disclose the estimated mass of 
     microplastic in such clothing, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Ms. SALINAS (for herself and Mr. Mackenzie):
       H.R. 10959. A bill to amend the Patient Protection and 
     Affordable Care Act to require Exchanges to provide for 
     special enrollment periods related to changes in plan 
     provider networks; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. WALKINSHAW:
       H.R. 10960. A bill to provide authority to convert certain 
     employees serving under time-limited appointments to career 
     or career-conditional appointments in the civil service, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Government Reform.
           By Mr. BLAIR:
       H. Res. 1648. A resolution requiring Members of the House 
     of Representatives to post monthly activity reports on their 
     official public websites, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on House Administration.
           By Mr. MORELLE:
       H. Res. 1649. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of the first full week of November as ``National 
     Animal Shelter Appreciation Week``; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture. -

                          ____________________
```
