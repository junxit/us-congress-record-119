# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  All-knowing and all-powerful God, there is nothing that You don't 
already know, nothing over which You are not sovereign. We commend unto 
You our unknowing--our inability to see what these days will portend 
for this country and the governing of this Nation. We appeal to You in 
our humanity--with our limited perspective and our dependence on Your 
wisdom to guide us in our lives.
  As children we sang easily that You have the whole world in Your 
hands. Inspire us to see--wherever we stand--that You still do. Our 
past, our present, and our future are laid bare before You.
  May we bear up under the accounting to which You will hold us. In our 
response to uncertainty, may we trust in Your wisdom. In our living 
into these times of change, may we yield ourselves to Your direction.
  Search us, God, and know our hearts. Lead us into the way You would 
have us go and into the lives You would have us lead.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.

                          ____________________
```
