# RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```
             RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
resignation from the House of Representatives:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 December 8, 2025, Washington, DC.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Enclosed is my letter of retirement 
     sent to Georgia Governor Brian Kemp. I will leave the House 
     of Representatives effective January 5, 2026, at 11:59 pm 
     EST.
       It has been my honor and privilege to serve the people of 
     Georgia's 14th congressional district.
           Sincerely,
                                           Marjorie Taylor Greene,
     Member of Congress.
                                  ____

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 December 8, 2025, Washington, DC.
     Governer Brian Kemp,
     The Office of the Governor,
     Atlanta, GA.
       Dear Governor Kemp: I am writing to notify you of my 
     retirement as a Member of the United States House of 
     Representatives for the 14th Congressional District of 
     Georgia effective January 5, 2026, at 11:59 pm EST.
       It has been my honor and privilege to serve the people of 
     Georgia's 14th congressional district.
           Sincerely,
                                            Majorie Taylor Greene,
                                               Member of Congress.

     

                          ____________________
```
