# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty God, sanctify this moment, laden as it is with our 
expectations and intentions for the coming year. With all its promise 
and all its pressure, take the beginning of this second half of the 
119th Congress and gather it into Your divine design. Prepare us for 
the way You would have us go.
  Raise the valleys that would pull us down into incivility and 
discord. Make low the mountains that inspire us toward prerogative and 
privilege. Make level the rough ground that is inherent to the 
democratic process. Make plain the rugged moments of confusion when so 
much is at stake in the decisions that will be made here.
  Then reveal Your desire for this body and for this country in this 
time. May all strive to see it together and work as one body to effect 
Your divine will.
  In Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
