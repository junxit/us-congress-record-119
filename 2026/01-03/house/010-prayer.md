# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy and eternal God, we commend to You this past year and pray Your 
blessing over all our efforts.
  Should we be consumed with pride over our accomplishments, humble us, 
for nothing we do is outside of Your sovereign plan. Should we be 
overwhelmed with frustration, believing our best attempts have been 
thwarted, raise us up, for all things, when done in good faith, will 
find reward in the fullness of time.
  All of this--our successes, our failures, our achievements, our 
losses--we commend to You. For any glory we gain is like the flowers of 
the field, and any defeat we suffer is like the grass that withers, but 
Your Word, O Lord, endures forever.
  Speak then Your final Word over the first half of the 119th Congress, 
that we would know the might of Your righteousness and the mercy of 
Your reward.
  All this we ask by Your endless grace and in Your matchless name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
