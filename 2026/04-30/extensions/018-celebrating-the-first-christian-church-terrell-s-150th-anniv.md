# CELEBRATING THE FIRST CHRISTIAN CHURCH TERRELL'S 150TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Gooden, Lance (G000589) [R-TX]

```
   CELEBRATING THE FIRST CHRISTIAN CHURCH TERRELL'S 150TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LANCE GOODEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 30, 2026

  Mr. GOODEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 150th 
anniversary of the First Christian Church (Disciples of Christ) in my 
hometown of Terrell, Texas. Founded in 1875 in borrowed quarters, this 
congregation has been a local institution of faith for over a century.
  The church constructed a simple sanctuary on land purchased from R.A. 
Terrell in 1881, solidifying its presence and influence in my 
community. Although initially bare, the land was quickly developed. A 
tall frame building sufficient to accommodate the growing congregation 
was erected at the corner of Adelaide and Asylum in 1895. This 
building, ornamented with a bell tower and spire, stained glass 
windows, and a baptistery, became a symbol of unwavering faith. The 
present brick parsonage was built next door in 1958, replacing the old 
frame structure. In 1988, the Charles E. Mull Christian Family Center 
was dedicated, providing a multi-use space for classrooms, offices, a 
conference room, and athletic facilities.
  I congratulate and thank the clergy and staff on this milestone--
Pastor Craig Rutherford, Lisa Cross, Mary Kay Cole, Jerry Vick, and 
Donna Best. The First Christian Church of Terrell will continue to be a 
source of faith, service, and strength for generations to come.
```
