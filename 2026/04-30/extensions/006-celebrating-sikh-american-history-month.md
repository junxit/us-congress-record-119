# CELEBRATING SIKH AMERICAN HISTORY MONTH

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
                CELEBRATING SIKH AMERICAN HISTORY MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 30, 2026

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, as a proud member of the American Sikh 
Congressional Caucus, I rise today with deep pride and gratitude to 
celebrate Sikh History Month and to strongly support a resolution 
recognizing the history, culture, heritage, and immense contributions 
of the Sikh American community.
  Sikhism, founded over 500 years ago by Guru Nanak Dev Ji, is built on 
three unbreakable pillars: meditating on the Divine; earning an honest 
living through tireless work; and sharing generously with others. These 
core principles of equality, social justice, selfless service (seva), 
and human dignity continue to guide Sikh Americans as they overcome 
adversity and strengthen our Nation.
  In California's Central Valley, Sikh and Punjabi farmers proudly 
wearing the turban form the backbone of American agriculture. They 
produce more than 90 percent of our peaches, nearly two-thirds of our 
prunes, and vast harvests of almonds, walnuts, and other crops that 
feed America from coast to coast. From the fields to our military, 
boardrooms, classrooms, and humanitarian efforts, Sikh Americans 
exemplify entrepreneurship, civic leadership, and unwavering 
patriotism.
  Their gurdwaras serve as beacons of compassion, offering free meals 
to every person in need proving that no one eats alone and no one goes 
hungry. This month is an opportunity to highlight Sikh identity and 
articles of faith, promotes education and cultural events, and 
reaffirms America's sacred commitment to religious liberty and 
pluralism for all.
  I congratulate our Sikh brothers and sisters on Sikh History Month.

                          ____________________
```
