# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, who has been the hope, refuge, and joy of many 
generations, thank You for giving us the power to seek You. We praise 
You for Your promise that those who keep on seeking will find.
  Lord, inspire our lawmakers to seek Your wisdom in order to be guided 
by Your loving providence. Give them a clearer vision of Your truth, a 
great faith in Your might, and a deeper assurance of Your love. Teach 
them to work and not to ask for any reward except that of knowing they 
are doing Your will. Do for them immeasurably, abundantly, above all 
that they can ask or imagine.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
