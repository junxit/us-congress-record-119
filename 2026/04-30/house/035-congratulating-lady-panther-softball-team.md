# CONGRATULATING LADY PANTHER SOFTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Comer, James (C001108) [R-KY]

```
                              {time}  1650
               CONGRATULATING LADY PANTHER SOFTBALL TEAM

  (Mr. COMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. COMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the biggest high school 
sports achievement in the history of Cumberland County, Kentucky.
  Congratulations to the Cumberland County Lady Panther softball team 
on their recent victory in the 2026 All A Classic State Championship.
  The Lady Panther victory marks the very first State championship won 
by any sports team in the history of Cumberland County High School.
  Cumberland County is one of Kentucky's most rural counties, with a 
population of just over 6,000 residents. Cumberland Countians were so 
proud of their softball team, they lined the streets of Burkesville, 
Kentucky, for a massive parade honoring their hometown heroes.
  Congratulations again to the State champion Cumberland County Lady 
Panther softball team.
```
