# HONORING EVA WOODBY

**Speaking:** Kim, Young (K000397) [R-CA]

```
                          HONORING EVA WOODBY

  (Mrs. KIM asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. KIM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor my dear friend, Eva 
Woodby, who recently passed away, and I send my deepest condolences to 
her devoted husband, Alex Yu, and family.
  Eva lived the American Dream. She came to the United States as a 
student with nothing and worked tirelessly to build a very successful 
business. No matter how much she achieved, she never stopped giving 
back or lifting others up.

[[Page H3299]]

  Even when her business struggled during COVID and as she battled 
late-stage cancer, Eva never gave up. She faced every challenge with 
strength, dignity, and determination. Her life was not defined by 
hardship, but by courage and resilience. She showed all of us what it 
means to keep moving forward, no matter the odds.
  I am very grateful for Eva's friendship, and I will strive to live up 
to the example she set every single day.

                          ____________________
```
