# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rabbi Peter Stein, Temple B'rith Kodesh, Rochester, New York, offered 
the following prayer:
  Source of blessing, I pray that today will bring us closer to a world 
of justice and peace. God, watch over the Members of this Congress. May 
You be blessed with wisdom and courage. May Your debates and decisions 
create safety and success, dignity and opportunity, for all who call 
our country home.
  Lead them with a spirit of discernment, humanity, and restraint. 
Guide them with a commitment to the common good.
  I pray for Your loved ones, that they remain safe and You remain in 
their nourishing embrace.
  As You do Your work on Capitol Hill, may Your leadership stand on the 
values of compassion and respect, making this place a beacon of light 
that extends from sea to shining sea. May You remain steadfast in the 
pursuit of the vision of a nation of liberty and justice for all, a 
home for all who yearn to breathe free.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
