# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2424

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2424

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2424

  Mr. LaHOOD. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 2424, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative LaMalfa of California, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Illinois?
  There was no objection.
```
