# HONORING IDAHO GOVERNOR DIRK KEMPTHORNE

**Speaking:** Fulcher, Russ (F000469) [R-ID]

```
                HONORING IDAHO GOVERNOR DIRK KEMPTHORNE

  (Mr. FULCHER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FULCHER. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to stand today to remember the 
life and legacy of a dear friend and fellow Idahoan, Dirk Kempthorne.
  Dirk was a trusted leader and a devoted public servant. Throughout 
his distinguished career, he served as mayor of Boise, Idaho's 
Governor, United States Senator, and Secretary of the Interior.
  In each role, Dirk worked tirelessly to strengthen our communities, 
promote responsible stewardship, and expand opportunity for Idaho 
families.
  His leadership helped support job growth, drive commerce, and invest 
in infrastructure across the Gem State. He was also instrumental in the 
commissioning of the USS Idaho nuclear submarine, championing support 
for its crew and their families.
  Beyond his many accomplishments, Dirk was a loving husband, father, 
and grandfather. He was just a good man.
  Rest in peace, my friend.

                          ____________________
```
