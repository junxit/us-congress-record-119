# NO WAREHOUSING OF HUMAN BEINGS IN INDUSTRIAL ICE DETENTION CENTERS

**Speaking:** Morrison, Kelly (M001234) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 8494

```
   NO WAREHOUSING OF HUMAN BEINGS IN INDUSTRIAL ICE DETENTION CENTERS

  (Ms. MORRISON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. MORRISON. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to speak on the importance of 
the Ban Warehouse Detention Act.
  The Trump administration wants to spend $40 billion for detention 
centers

[[Page H3327]]

to hold human beings. ICE is confining people in windowless rooms with 
metal bunk beds, a few metal tables, and toilets. This is where they 
stay all day and all night, sometimes for months.
  Trump is planning to give $40 billion in taxpayer dollars to his 
megadonors and other private contractors to build and run more ICE 
detention centers that are literally killing people.
  We must pass H.R. 8494, the Ban Warehouse Detention Act, because 
Americans across the country are standing up and saying, we don't want 
this.
  From New Hampshire to New York, from Oklahoma to Tennessee, to my 
home State of Minnesota, when Americans find out about ICE's plans to 
buy massive industrial warehouses to hold nearly 100,000 human beings, 
they are flooding their city council meetings, putting pressure on 
their local officials, and demanding that these contracts be canceled. 
Across red States and blue States alike, Americans are saying that this 
is not what they want and this is not who we should be.

                          ____________________
```
