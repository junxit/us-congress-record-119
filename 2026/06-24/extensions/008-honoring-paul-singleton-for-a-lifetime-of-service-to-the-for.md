# HONORING PAUL SINGLETON FOR A LIFETIME OF SERVICE TO THE FORT WALTON BEACH COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
 HONORING PAUL SINGLETON FOR A LIFETIME OF SERVICE TO THE FORT WALTON 
                            BEACH COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 24, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a truly 
remarkable American, Mr. Paul Singleton, as he celebrates two 
extraordinary milestones: his 93rd birthday and his 2,000th event as a 
Chamber Ambassador for the Greater Fort Walton Beach Chamber of 
Commerce.
  For many in Okaloosa County, Paul is a familiar face at community 
gatherings and Chamber events. Behind that familiar face, however, is a 
lifetime defined by service, leadership, and an unwavering commitment 
to others.
  Paul served our Nation with distinction for 32 years in the United 
States Air Force, rising

[[Page E613]]

to the rank of Lieutenant Colonel. His military career took him around 
the world, including assignments supporting Special Operations and 
service during the Vietnam War. Like so many of his generation, Paul 
answered the call to serve and carried that sense of duty long after 
hanging up the uniform.
  Retirement did not slow him down. Instead, Paul dedicated himself to 
serving the community he calls home. Since becoming a Chamber 
Ambassador in 1984, he has been one of Northwest Florida's most devoted 
volunteers, welcoming new businesses, supporting local entrepreneurs, 
and helping strengthen the community that makes our region so special.
  Attending 2,000 Chamber events is a remarkable achievement. It 
represents thousands of handshakes, countless volunteer hours, and more 
than four decades spent investing in the success of others. It is a 
testament to a lifetime of showing up, lending a hand, and making a 
difference.
  At 93 years old, Paul continues to embody the values of integrity, 
humility, and service. His example reminds us that lasting impact is 
not measured by titles or recognition, but by a steadfast commitment to 
helping others and strengthening the communities we cherish.
  On behalf of Florida's 1st Congressional District, I am proud to 
congratulate Paul Singleton on his 93rd birthday and this extraordinary 
milestone. Northwest Florida is stronger because of his service, and 
countless lives have been enriched by his generosity and dedication.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Paul Singleton for a 
lifetime of service to our Nation, our community, and the people of 
Northwest Florida.

                          ____________________
```
