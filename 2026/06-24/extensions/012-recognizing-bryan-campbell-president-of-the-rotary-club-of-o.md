# RECOGNIZING BRYAN CAMPBELL, PRESIDENT OF THE ROTARY CLUB OF ORANGE PARK SUNRISE

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
RECOGNIZING BRYAN CAMPBELL, PRESIDENT OF THE ROTARY CLUB OF ORANGE PARK 
                                SUNRISE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 24, 2026

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Bryan 
Campbell as he will be sworn in as President of the Rotary Club of 
Orange Park Sunrise in Clay County, Florida, on June 27, 2026.
  Bryan has been a Rotarian since 2012, living the club motto of 
``Service Above Self'' through more than a decade of work in community 
service, youth development, food security, health, wellness, and local 
philanthropy. As President, he will lead with a clear goal: ensuring 
the Rotary Club of Orange Park Sunrise strengthens Clay County through 
service, collaboration, and leadership.
  Bryan's service extends far beyond Rotary. As my District Director, 
he works every day to connect the people of Northeast Florida with 
their federal government, solve problems for constituents, and 
strengthen relationships across our communities.
  Before joining my office, Bryan led major healthcare organizations in 
Colorado and Florida. He helped author LifeBridge, a physician wellness 
program used by more than 30 medical societies nationwide to address 
physician burnout, depression, and suicide. He also created the Blue 
Paisley Ribbon to recognize National Thyroid Awareness Month.
  Earlier in his career, Bryan was an Emmy Award-winning journalist, 
bringing the same commitment to public service that would later define 
his work in healthcare advocacy, nonprofit leadership, and constituent 
service.
  Bryan has served as President of the American Association of Medical 
Society Executives, Chair of the Council of Florida Medical Society 
Executives, Chairman of Jacksonville's Mayor's Council for Fitness and 
Wellbeing, Chairman of the Clay County Charter Review Commission, 
Director of Challenge Enterprises, and President of North Florida PRSA.
  In every chapter, Bryan has brought people together, strengthened 
institutions, and turned service into impact. Bryan is joined in this 
milestone by his wife, Sarah; his children, Lauren and Nathan; and his 
grandson, Lincoln.
  On behalf of the people of Florida's 4th Congressional District, I 
congratulate Bryan Campbell on his installation as President of the 
Rotary Club of Orange Park Sunrise, and I thank him for his service to 
our community, our state, and our Nation.
```
