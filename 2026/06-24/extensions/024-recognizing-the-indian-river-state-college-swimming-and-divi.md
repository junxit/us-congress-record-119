# RECOGNIZING THE INDIAN RIVER STATE COLLEGE SWIMMING AND DIVING PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Mast, Brian J. (M001199) [R-FL]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE INDIAN RIVER STATE COLLEGE SWIMMING AND DIVING PROGRAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRIAN J. MAST

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 24, 2026

  Mr. MAST. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Indian River State 
College (IRSC) Swimming and Diving program. IRSC has demonstrated 
unmatched dominance in collegiate athletics through discipline, 
teamwork, and a sustained culture of excellence. The men's team secured 
its 52nd consecutive NJCAA National Championship, the longest active 
title streak in collegiate sports history, while the women's team 
earned its 48th national title, completing a historic sweep by winning 
every event at the 2026 championship meet.
  At the 2026 NJCAA National Championships in Fort Pierce, Florida, 
athletes delivered record-breaking performances, including Marcus 
Johnson's two national records in breaststroke events, and record-
setting relay swims by Noah Smith, Johnson, Zack Gresham, and Oliver 
Nell.
  Under Head Coach Sion Brinn--an alumnus, Olympian, and 13-time 
national championship coach--the program continues to embody excellence 
and community leadership. Serving over 24,000 students, Indian River 
State College inspires its community and represents Fort Pierce with 
distinction nationwide.
  I am pleased to extend the highest congratulations to the men's and 
women's Swimming and Diving teams, Coach Brinn and staff, President 
Timothy E. Moore, and all student-athletes who have contributed to this 
extraordinary legacy.
  These achievements stand as a source of pride for Florida's 21st 
Congressional District and the Nation, reflecting the power of 
teamwork, dedication, and the pursuit of greatness.
```
