# CELEBRATING THE GREATER POMPANO BEACH CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
       CELEBRATING THE GREATER POMPANO BEACH CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 24, 2026

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the grand 
opening and ribbon cutting of the new office for the Greater Pompano 
Beach Chamber of Commerce and recognize the organization on 79 years of 
dedicated service to the businesses, residents, and communities of 
Broward County, Florida.
  Founded in 1947, the Greater Pompano Beach Chamber of Commerce has 
served as a cornerstone of economic growth and community development 
throughout the greater Pompano Beach area. For nearly eight decades, 
the Chamber has united business leaders, entrepreneurs, professionals, 
and community stakeholders in a shared mission to promote civic 
engagement, strengthen commerce, and enhance the quality of life for 
all who call this region home.
  The Chamber has also demonstrated commitment to regional 
collaboration and growth through the acquisition of the Margate Chamber 
of Commerce in 2016 and the Lighthouse Point Chamber of Commerce in 
2018. These efforts expanded its reach and strengthened its ability to 
serve businesses and communities throughout northeast Broward County. I 
thank the Chamber's Chief Executive Officer Evencia A. Janvier for her 
outstanding leadership and dedication, her commitment to fostering 
business growth, community engagement, and economic development 
continuing to make a lasting impact throughout the region. Today, the 
Chamber represents more than 700 businesses and professionals who work 
collectively to advance the economic prosperity of the community. 
Through collaboration, advocacy, networking, and leadership, the 
Chamber demonstrates the power of a united business community. Its 
efforts have fostered opportunities for growth, innovation, and 
investment while creating a stronger and more resilient local economy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
Greater Pompano Beach Chamber of Commerce for its outstanding service, 
leadership, and commitment to economic and community development. On 
behalf of Florida's 23rd Congressional District, I extend my sincere 
congratulations and best wishes for continued success in the years 
ahead.
```
