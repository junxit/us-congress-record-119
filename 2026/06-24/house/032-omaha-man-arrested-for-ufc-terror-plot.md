# OMAHA MAN ARRESTED FOR UFC TERROR PLOT

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                 OMAHA MAN ARRESTED FOR UFC TERROR PLOT

  (Mr. FLOOD asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, the arrest of a man in Omaha accused of 
leading the plot to attack the White House's UFC event this month is a 
stark reminder about the threats that face our Nation.
  According to DHS, the suspect entered the U.S. illegally and 
allegedly sought to make the attack `` . . . as deadly as we can get.''
  This case underscores the importance of securing our borders, 
enforcing our immigration laws, and remaining vigilant against those 
who seek political violence rather than political debate.
  I am grateful to the Omaha FBI, working together with the Nebraska 
State Patrol, who arrested this alleged ringleader before lives were 
put at risk. Their professionalism and commitment to help keep our 
communities safe happens every day.
  Regardless of ideology or political affiliation, political violence 
is never acceptable. We must reject extremism, promote civil discourse, 
and work together to lower the temperature of political rhetoric.
  I thank our law enforcement officers once again for making this 
arrest.

                          ____________________
```
