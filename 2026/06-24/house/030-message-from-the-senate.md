# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 86

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has agreed to without amendment a concurrent 
Resolution of the House of the following title:

       H. Con. Res. 86. Concurrent Resolution directing the 
     President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers 
     Resolution, to remove United States Armed Forces from 
     hostilities with Iran.
```
