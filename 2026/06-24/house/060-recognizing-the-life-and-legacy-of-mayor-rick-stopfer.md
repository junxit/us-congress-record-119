# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MAYOR RICK STOPFER

**Speaking:** Veasey, Marc A. (V000131) [D-TX]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MAYOR RICK STOPFER

  (Mr. VEASEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VEASEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the distinguished 
service of my good friend, Rick Stopfer, whose more than three decades 
of dedication to the city of Irving has left a lasting and meaningful 
impact on the community he proudly serves.
  He was elected to the city council in 1998. Rick has demonstrated a 
steady commitment to public service. He has held so many key leadership 
roles. In addition to mayor, he served as mayor pro tem for the 
council. He was reelected to his third term in May of 2023. He has 
really helped the city of Irving grow. Anyone who has been to the city 
and has seen Las Colinas and seen all the Fortune 1500 companies that 
have decided to make Irving their home over the last decade, it is 
absolutely amazing.
  In addition to everything that he has done for Irving, he has also 
served on the Dallas Area Rapid Transit board and other regional bodies 
that reflect a broader vision for the entire Metroplex.
  Rick is also a person who is deeply rooted in the Irving community. 
He is involved in so many different civic organizations, and he is just 
really a great guy. Alongside with his wife of more than 40 years, 
Melanie, he has really built an amazing legacy in the city of Irving.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Rick on everything he has done for the 
city.

                          ____________________
```
