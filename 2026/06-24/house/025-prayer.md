# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy Lord, You have called us to You and chosen us as Your own. You 
have spoken to us through Your word and having heard Your voice, may we 
respond to You with our whole selves.
  May every aspect of our being reflect a deep desire to live into the 
grace You have shown us, a grace that has led us to this place to serve 
this Nation at this time in its history.
  May the work we do to govern and lead this country be established in 
Your divine guidance and sustained by our faith in You.
  May even the little things that seem inconsequential and yet are 
essential to the success of our efforts be prompted by our love for 
You.
  May our endurance through the monumental and the mundane be inspired 
and upheld by the hope that is found only in You.
  God, the author, redeemer, and sustainer of our lives, we entrust the 
living of this day to Your keeping. In Your enduring name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
