# RECOGNIZING DARRELL OWENS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                              {time}  1720
                       RECOGNIZING DARRELL OWENS

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Mr. Darrell Owens, who will serve as U.S. representative to the 
Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe.
  Ambassador Owens, a proud Erie, Pennsylvania, native, began working 
on Capitol Hill in the early 2000s and joined my office during my first 
term. As my military legislative assistant, Ambassador Owens put his 
head down and got to work on day one, advancing our legislative 
priorities and making sure that we met the needs of our constituents 
and servicemembers.
  Ambassador Owens knows what servicemembers need because he stood with 
them. Along with his time in Washington, D.C., he served as a 
commissioned officer in the United States Army Reserve. He knows what 
it means to meet people right where they are.
  Prior to his nomination, he served as a Senior Adviser in the Office 
of Congressional and Legislative Affairs at the U.S. Department of 
Veterans Affairs. Ambassador Owens has remained grounded in his 
commitment to service and his love for country.
  Mr. Speaker, Ambassador Owens is exactly the type of person we want 
representing the United States overseas. May God bless him, and I wish 
him the very best.

                          ____________________
```
