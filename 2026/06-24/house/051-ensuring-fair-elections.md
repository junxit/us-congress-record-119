# ENSURING FAIR ELECTIONS

**Speaking:** Stutzman, Marlin A. (S001188) [R-IN]

```
                        ENSURING FAIR ELECTIONS

  (Mr. STUTZMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. STUTZMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in strong support of the SAVE 
America Act introduced by Representative Roy and Senator Lee.
  In a few weeks, the United States will celebrate its 250th 
anniversary. One thing that has sustained American democracy since its 
founding is elections. Our Founders created a government by the people 
and for the people, and it is imperative that our elections continue to 
represent the views of American citizens only.
  The SAVE America Act will do exactly that: ensure that our country's 
elections are decided only by American citizens and not foreign 
nationals.
  Indiana first enacted voter ID laws in 2005, and it is time the rest 
of the country did the same. Over 80 percent of Americans support voter 
ID and showing proof of citizenship to vote.
  Mr. Speaker, the Senate cannot keep stalling on this commonsense 
election reform. Senate colleagues must restore the talking filibuster 
and pass the SAVE America Act as soon as possible. Our country's future 
depends on it.

                          ____________________
```
