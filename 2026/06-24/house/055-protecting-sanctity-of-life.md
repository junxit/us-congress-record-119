# PROTECTING SANCTITY OF LIFE

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Mike (K000403) [R-UT]

```
                      PROTECTING SANCTITY OF LIFE

  (Mr. KENNEDY of Utah asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. KENNEDY of Utah. Mr. Speaker, 4 years ago this week, the Supreme 
Court ruled in Dobbs that the Constitution contains no right to an 
abortion.
  The Court returned this question to the people and their elected 
representatives. That was the right decision.
  I came to Congress after years as a physician. Firsthand, I have seen 
the heartbeat of an infant on an ultrasound. I have placed newborns in 
the arms of their mothers. Protecting life was my job, and I take that 
same duty with me onto this floor.
  Because of the Dobbs decision, the States and all of us are made free 
to uphold our duty and protect the sanctity of life.
  There are children in my home State of Utah and across this country, 
as well as my family, who are growing up today because of those who 
fought for them. They are the future of this country, and we are better 
for having them in it.
  Mr. Speaker, we owe every mother and child a country that welcomes 
and continues to protect life.

                          ____________________
```
