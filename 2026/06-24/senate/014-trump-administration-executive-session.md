# Trump Administration (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                          Trump Administration

  Mr. President, now Trump's visit to the Capitol. Seeing Trump come to 
the Capitol today to meet Senate Republicans is like watching the clown 
car pull up to the circus, made even more so by Trump's canceling of 
the bill signing, which I will talk more about in a minute.
  When Trump comes to the Hill today, he will find the MAGA Senate 
majority in meltdown. They are one big, hot mess--that is what the 
Republican Senate is right now. Republicans are wringing each other's 
necks. They can't govern their own conference of 53 people, let alone a 
country of hundreds of millions.
  After being doublecrossed, blindsided, and embarrassed by Trump, 
Senate Republicans can either keep bending the knee to the President or 
start demanding answers on behalf of the American people.
  The reason that Republicans are such a hot mess is obvious. Trump's 
views are totally out of touch with the American people's views, and 
Republicans are afraid to tell him he is wrong.
  Speak up. Stand up to Trump. Tell him what you think, Republicans. 
That might do your party some good. It might even do Trump some good if 
he would ever listen.
  Americans want to know what Republicans are doing to bring down 
costs. Americans want to know how Trump plans to end his disastrous war 
with Iran. Americans want to know whether the GOP will continue going 
along with Trump's all-out assault on voting rights with legislation 
like the SAVE Act.
  If my colleagues across the aisle have any sense at all, they ought 
to grill Trump on affordability, on his failed war with Iran, on his 
effort to hold American national security hostage, and all his other 
policies that are hurting the American people.
  When they emerge from their meeting, will Republicans have told Trump 
the truth? Will they speak truth to power? Will they do their job? Or 
will they just bend in obedience to him and get more frustrated, more 
angry, and more ineffective than they have ever been?
```
