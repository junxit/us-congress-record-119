# Nomination of Michael J. Hendershot (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI], Slotkin, Elissa (S001208) [D-MI]

```
                  Nomination of Michael J. Hendershot

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, today, the Senate will vote to confirm 
Michael Hendershot to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District 
of Ohio.
  Mr. Hendershot's record reflects his partisanship. In the Ohio 
Attorney General's Office, he defended the State's laws restricting 
abortion access. He continued to defend the State even after Ohio 
voters enshrined reproductive rights in the Ohio Constitution in 2023.
  He also represented Ohio officials against challenges to the State's 
gerrymandered State legislative and congressional maps as they were 
struck down five times by the Ohio Supreme Court for violating the Ohio 
Constitution.
  Finally, Mr. Hendershot is yet another Trump judicial nominee who is 
an election denier. In November 2020, he joined an amicus brief to the 
U.S. Supreme Court asking the Court to grant cert and reverse the 
underlying decision of the Pennsylvania Supreme Court that allowed for 
the counting of absentee ballots received after election day.
  I oppose Mr. Hendershot's nomination and ask my colleagues to join 
me.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Rhode Island.
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the vote 
scheduled for 5:30 be allowed to begin immediately.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
