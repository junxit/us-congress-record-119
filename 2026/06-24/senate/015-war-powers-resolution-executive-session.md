# War Powers Resolution (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                         War Powers Resolution

  Mr. President, on the Iran War Powers Resolution, the successful 
passage of our War Powers Resolution yesterday reasserts what the 
American people have been saying for months: Trump's reckless war with 
Iran must end.
  For the first time, this resolution has passed both Chambers of 
Congress and does not require the President's signature. The message 
from the only branch of government with the power to declare war, the 
Congress, could not be more clear: The Trump administration must 
withdraw United States forces from hostilities in Iran. Both Houses 
have now spoken and said just that. Every day that Trump refuses to get 
our troops out of harm's way, the pressure mounts on him and the Senate 
Republicans who continue to go along with this failed war.
  Trump said our War Powers Resolution to end his war is 
``meaningless.'' Trump thinks Americans' frustrations with his historic 
blunder are meaningless too. Trump complained that our resolution 
``just made my job more difficult.''
  Donald Trump, our job is not to make your job less difficult. Our job 
is to make the American people's lives less difficult, especially after 
all you have done to make them more difficult.
  We don't care about making Trump's life easier. We care about making 
Americans' lives easier by ending this disastrous, costly war. Trump 
still has no idea of how much more difficult this war has made life for 
the American people with 13 lost servicemembers and hundreds getting 
hurt. Everyone is paying hundreds of dollars more for extra gasoline. 
Everyone is enduring increasing inflation and costs going up. And now, 
he is asking the American people to foot the $100 billion bill for his 
war? Americans don't even know if they will get anything in return 
because Trump refuses to reveal his deal with Iran.
  What we do know is that Iran took Trump to the cleaners. Everything 
is worse off: gas prices, the deficit, America's prestige in the world. 
Iran gets hundreds of billions of dollars for reconstruction, they get 
sanctions relief, they get tighter control over the Strait of Hormuz 
and much more. The Congress and the American people have spoken after 
yesterday. Trump needs to end this disaster of a war immediately.


                    21st Century ROAD to Housing Act

  Now, Mr. President, on the housing bill, Trump's clown car gets more 
and more laughable and ridiculous. Here is what he just did: He 
canceled his bill signing on the housing bill. It is utterly amazing. 
Trump is running away from one of the very few accomplishments that 
could actually help the American people. Any time there is an 
opportunity for Trump to help the American people, he runs the other 
way. He comes up with some idiot thing in his head that this may hurt 
this one or help this one in his pettiness with his enemies, and he 
throws up roadblocks.
  The bipartisan housing bill is an accomplishment that the American 
people want, are proud of, and need. Trump runs away from it. He is not 
going to sign it. He is petulant, he is angry, and he looks ridiculous 
in canceling it just 2 hours before he is supposed to sign it, saying 
we should do the SAVE Act instead.
  How does the SAVE Act lower Americans' housing costs? It doesn't. How 
does the SAVE Act do anything good? It doesn't. It is a vicious, nasty 
piece of material, and probably, in Trump's ridiculous mind, he thinks: 
Oh, I don't want to sign the housing bill because it might detract from 
the SAVE Act.
  The SAVE Act, not the SAVE bill--the SAVE Act will not pass, Donald 
Trump. Get on board with this housing bill. Don't veto it, because it 
is so important to the American people. The passage of our bipartisan 
housing bill--it was bipartisan--ought to remind the Nation how 
government can deliver when we put aside the partisan chaos we see 
coming out of the White House every day.
  Listen to me, Trump. Sign this thing. Get something good done for the 
American people for once. The American people are counting on us to 
make life more affordable, and lowering housing costs is absolutely 
essential to that effort.
  The ROAD to Housing puts our country on the right track towards 
fixing the housing shortage that drives up costs for the American 
people. Our bill streamlines local zoning and permitting. It provides 
new flexible funding to expand supply. It supports manufactured and 
modular housing. It helps convert old office buildings into new 
housing, and it stops institutional investors from crowding Americans 
out of the housing market.
  Our legislation is going to help build more, faster, better, cheaper 
housing and build everywhere so we can get more homes on the market.
  What is wrong with that, Donald Trump? Why do you run away from 
signing it when it will be so beneficial to the American people? Trump, 
the housing crisis in America is too severe to let partisanship get in 
the way of commonsense solutions.
  The ROAD to Housing is an important step toward ending the 
affordability crisis, and it must not be the last. We will keep at it, 
particularly if we regain the majority. But now, for once, Trump should 
sign it and stop running away from it and stop making such a fool of 
himself.
  I thank Senator Warren for her leadership on this bill, as well as 
all of the members of the Banking Committee and everyone else who 
helped to make the ROAD to Housing a success. It looks like, even if 
Trump were to try to veto it, there will be probably enough votes in 
both Houses to override that veto.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority whip.
```
