**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sheehy). Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair 
lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will 
state.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 776, Matthew A. Schwartz, of New 
     York, to be United States Circuit Judge for the Second 
     Circuit.

Circuit Judge for the Second Circuit, shall be brought to a close?
