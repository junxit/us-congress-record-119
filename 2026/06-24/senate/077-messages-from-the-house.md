# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 629, H.R. 826, H.R. 4238, H.R. 6644, H.R. 7396, H.R. 8879, H.R. 8880, H.R. 8881, H.R. 8882

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:03 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agreed to the amendment of the Senate to the amendment of the House to 
the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 6644) to increase the 
supply of Housing in America, and for other purposes.
  At 2:16 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, without amendment:

       S. 629. An act to amend the Agricultural Credit Act of 1978 
     to remove barriers to agricultural producers in accessing 
     funds to carry out emergency measures under the emergency 
     conservation program, and for other purposes.

  The message further announced that the House has passed the following 
bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 826. An act to require the Inspector General of the 
     Small Business Administration to submit a quarterly report on 
     fraud relating to certain COVID-19 loans.
       H.R. 4238. An act to improve accountability in the disaster 
     loan program of the Small Business Administration, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 7396. An act to establish an Office of Native American 
     Affairs within the Small Business Administration, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 8879. An act to amend the Small Business Act to 
     require a report on small business concern participation in a 
     covered contracting programs, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 8880. An act to require the Comptroller General to 
     evaluate Federal cybersecurity assistance to small business 
     concerns, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 8881. An act to amend the Small Business Act to 
     require the Administrator of the Small Business 
     Administration to report on the use of artificial 
     intelligence and machine learning by the Administration, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 8882. An act to amend the Small Business Economic 
     Policy Act of 1980 to examine how the competitiveness of 
     small businesses is affected by the enforcement of Federal 
     antitrust laws, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
