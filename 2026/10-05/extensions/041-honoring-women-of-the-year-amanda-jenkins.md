# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: AMANDA JENKINS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: AMANDA JENKINS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Amanda Jenkins as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a 
devoted and resilient member of the community who has endured eight 
years without stable housing, Amanda works for Contra Costa County's 
Coordinate Outreach Referral and Engagement (CORE) program to provide 
resources to people experiencing homelessness in the Richmond area. 
Amanda, a mother of five and grandmother of seven, originally faced 
homelessness after she made the difficult decision to leave an abusive 
relationship. Despite facing difficult circumstances, Amanda remained 
committed to supporting the people around her, establishing the Castro 
encampment, protecting her neighbors, and sharing resources with her 
community. She has also remained a fierce advocate for her community, 
sparking key dialogues between government agencies, homelessness task 
forces, and people living in unhoused communities. Today, through her 
work for the CORE program, Amanda engages with and helps stabilize 
people experiencing unsheltered homelessness through consistent street 
outreach. She is also attending school to become a Community Health 
Worker. Amanda is not defined by the years she spent without housing, 
she is defined by the community she protected, the trust she built, the 
promise she kept, the people she continues to serve, and the hope her 
life now gives to others. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Amanda 
Jenkins for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
