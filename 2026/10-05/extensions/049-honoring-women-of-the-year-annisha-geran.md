# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ANNISHA GERAN

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ANNISHA GERAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Annisha

[[Page E1027]]

Geran as she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional 
District's Women of the Year, Annisha Geran, a devoted mother of four 
and an involved community member, inspires those around her. Driven by 
her dedication to her children's and the community's education, Annisha 
has remained actively engaged in the Pittsburg Unified School District 
(PUSD). She has served as vice president on the school site council at 
Pittsburg High School for the past two terms and has participated in 
many PUSD parent classes, including PIQE, Serenamente, and Parent 
Project. She also founded Parent Appreciation Night at Pittsburg High 
School, now in its third year.
  As a working mother, Annisha has also led by example, earning a 
Bachelor of Arts in Social Science from Chico State, where she earned 
the Billie Jackson Award honoring the top African American Scholar at 
the university. Also, as an alumna of Los Medanos College, she received 
the college's Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., Emerging Leadership Award in 
2025, highlighting the impact of her advocacy and leadership throughout 
Pittsburg.
  Annisha has transformed personal hardship into fuel and a commitment 
to uplifting and empowering others. Her perseverance is inspiring and 
encourages others to seek support and accomplish their goals. Annisha 
is a role model in her community and a true symbol of both strength and 
determination. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize 
Annisha Geran for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
