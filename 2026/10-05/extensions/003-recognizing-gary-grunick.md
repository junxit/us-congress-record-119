# RECOGNIZING GARY GRUNICK

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING GARY GRUNICK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GRAVES. Mr. Speaker, I proudly rise today to recognize Gary 
Grunick for his distinguished career of service to our Nation and 
community. Gary served more than twenty years in the United States Air 
Force, attained the rank of Colonel, and is a veteran of the Persian 
Gulf War. As a Judge Advocate, he served as both a prosecutor and 
defense attorney, Staff Judge Advocate, and Chief of Civil Law for the 
United States Air Forces in Europe. He later served as a military 
appellate judge on the Air Force Court of Military Review in 
Washington, D.C., where he authored more than 150 opinions.
  Since retiring from the Air Force, Gary has continued his commitment 
to service through his legal practice and community involvement. He 
served as an Executive Board Trustee for LifeWay Christian Resources 
and as Chairman of the Lincoln County Republican Central Committee. In 
2023, Gary joined my Military Academy Nominations Board, where he 
interviews prospective candidates and provides recommendations as I 
consider nominations to our Nation's service academies. Through this 
work, he helps identify and encourage the next generation of military 
leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Gary 
Grunick for his decades of dedicated service and his continued 
commitment to our Nation's young men and women. His service to the 
United States, the state of Missouri, and the people of Missouri's 
Sixth Congressional District is deeply appreciated, and I am proud to 
represent him in the United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
