# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: MARY ANN DAWKINS-PADIGELA

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
         HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: MARY ANN DAWKINS-PADIGELA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Mary Ann Dawkins-Padigela as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Mary Ann 
is a dignified U.S. Air Force veteran who continues to serve her 
community as President of the East Bay Chapter of the California 
Association of Marriage and Family Therapists. Under her exceptional 
leadership, the East Bay chapter has expanded to over 700 members by 
cultivating an inclusive work environment where pre-licensed and 
licensed therapists feel respected, supported, and valued. Mary Ann has 
dedicated herself to bringing people together through meaningful 
collaborations and turning ambitious ideas into tangible results. From 
organizing and overseeing numerous initiatives to leading continuing 
education workshops and community networking gatherings, Mary Ann 
approaches every endeavor with professionalism, creativity, and an 
unwavering commitment to excellence. Her ability to unite people from 
diverse professional and cultural backgrounds and to translate complex 
clinical concepts into practical, accessible community solutions is a 
rare quality that her team and community deeply appreciate. She not 
only prioritizes excellence and inclusivity, but also strongly 
advocates for under-resourced and systemically marginalized 
communities. Mary Ann is the epitome of ideal community leadership and 
advocacy, and her hard work does not go unnoticed. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor and recognize Mary Ann Dawkins-Padigela for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
