# RECOGNIZING TYLER REED

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING TYLER REED

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GRAVES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Tyler Reed for his 
commitment to service and leadership. After graduating high school, 
Tyler attended the United States Military Academy and served our Nation 
in the United States Army. Following his military service, he joined 
Reed Automotive, his family's business, where he has distinguished 
himself as a successful businessman and community leader.
  Tyler continues to serve his community through my Military Academy 
Nominations Board, where he interviews prospective candidates and 
provides recommendations as I consider nominations to our Nation's 
service academies. As a husband and father, Tyler exemplifies the 
values of service, leadership, and dedication while helping prepare the 
next generation of military leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Tyler Reed 
for his dedication and hard work throughout the Military Academy 
nominations process. His service to the United States, the state of 
Missouri, and the people of Missouri's Sixth Congressional District is 
deeply appreciated, and I am proud to represent him in the United 
States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
