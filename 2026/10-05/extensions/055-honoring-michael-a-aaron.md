# HONORING MICHAEL A. AARON

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
                       HONORING MICHAEL A. AARON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Michael A. Aaron 
for more than 30 years of fire and rescue service, serving the people 
of Northwest Florida and along the Gulf Coast.
  Since beginning his career in 1994, Michael has answered the call to 
serve in volunteer county, municipal, and federal fire-service roles. 
For more than three decades, he has put others before himself and 
helped protect the communities he calls home.
  Michael recently earned his Chief Fire Officer designation from the 
Commission on Professional Credentialing and will graduate from 
Columbia Southern University on October 23 with a Bachelor of Science 
in Fire Department Administration, graduating Summa Cum Laude with a 
4.0 GPA.
  I am proud to recognize Michael for a career built on service, public 
safety, sacrifice, and a commitment to his fellow Americans. We owe a 
debt of gratitude to men and women like Michael who choose to serve and 
protect their communities.
  I thank Michael for 30 years of answering the call.

                          ____________________
```
