# TRIBUTE TO GAIL MAHNKE

**Speaking:** Calvert, Ken (C000059) [R-CA]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO GAIL MAHNKE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KEN CALVERT

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. CALVERT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the extraordinary 
life and enduring legacy of Gail Mahnke, who passed away on September 
17, 2026, after sustaining a traumatic brain injury on September 12 in 
Gillette, Wyoming. Gail was doing what she truly loved--spending the 
day with her beloved daughter, Kady, and competing in the barrel races. 
True to the strength, courage, and determination with which she lived 
her life, Gail fought valiantly until the very end.
  Gail was born and raised in Casper, Wyoming, the beloved daughter of 
Don and Mary Lou Mahnke, both of whom were well-established and 
respected members of Casper's medical community. Her Wyoming upbringing 
helped shape the woman she would become. From an early age, Gail 
developed a deep appreciation for the outdoors, often sharing cherished 
stories of hunting alongside her father. It was also during those 
formative years that her lifelong love of horses began, along with the 
extraordinary compassion she showed toward animals of every kind. 
Whether caring for a horse or tending to one of the many creatures and 
critters that crossed her path, Gail possessed a natural instinct to 
protect and nurture.
  From an early age, Gail knew that she wanted to devote her life to 
helping animals. She completed her undergraduate studies at the 
University of Wyoming before owning her Doctor of Veterinary Medicine 
degree from Colorado State University in Fort Collins. Gail went on to 
found the Casper Animal Medical Center, where she built a highly 
successful veterinary practice and served the Casper community for more 
than 30 years. In the later years of her career, she sold the practice, 
allowing her to pursue the many other dreams and adventures that still 
called to her.
  While successfully building and managing her veterinary practice, 
Gail raised her beloved daughter, Kady, and became a guardian to her 
three nephews, Nick, Josh, and Isaac. Hers was a busy and welcoming 
household, filled with children to raise, schools and activities to 
manage, countless animals to care for, and friends who were always made 
to feel at home. Even with the demands of her family and career, Gail 
remained deeply committed to animal welfare and charitable service. 
Whether through the rodeo community or by quietly helping friends, 
family members, and animals in need, she gave generously of her time, 
expertise, and resources.
  Gail loved to travel and often found ways to combine her journeys 
with the pursuits she treasured most--horseback riding, scuba diving, 
Yoga and continuing her professional education through seminars around 
the world. One of the most special lumen of Gail's life after 
retirement was watching her daughter, Kady, marry Jarrett in her 
beloved Cora, Wyoming.
  In 2021, she met Evan Matzner, beginning what would become an 
enduring relationship and business partnership. Together, they shared a 
passion for real estate, travel, adventure, and creating a life filled 
with meaningful experiences. They purchased a beautiful home in San 
Pedro, Belize, and made treasured memories in the places closest to 
their hearts, including Cora, Wyoming; Custer, South Dakota; 
Scottsdale, Arizona; Belize; and all the wonderful places in between. 
Evan will remain forever grateful for the life they shared and the 
precious time they were given together.
  Gail leaves behind an extraordinary legacy and an enduring impression 
upon the countless lives she touched. Her strength, generosity, 
compassion, and love for her family, friends, and animals will not be 
forgotten. She will be forever loved and deeply missed by Evan, her 
family, her many lifelong friends, and all those fortunate enough to 
have known her.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the 
remarkable life of Gail Mahnke and in extending our deepest condolences 
to all who knew and loved her.
```
