# HONORING ELEANOR MARSHALL

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
                       HONORING ELEANOR MARSHALL

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor constituent 
Eleanor Marshall, and congratulate her on reaching an incredible 
milestone, her 100th birthday. Her remarkable life reflects faith, 
family, hard work, and dedication to those around her.
  Born in Philadelphia on October 1, 1926, Eleanor built a life 
centered on her family and community. She was married to Chester E. 
Marshall Jr., a World War II U.S. Navy veteran, and together they 
raised three children: Chester F. Marshall III, a Vietnam-era U.S. Air 
Force veteran; Thomas George Marshall; and Laraine N. Smyrl.
  Eleanor's greatest legacy is her family, which has grown to include 
eight grandchildren and twelve great-grandchildren. Her family's proud 
tradition of military service, including that of her husband and son, 
is a testament to the values of dedication and patriotism she helped 
instill in those she loved.
  Throughout her life, Eleanor demonstrated a strong work ethic, 
spending many years working at the Stetson Hat Company. Outside of 
work, she was an avid bowler who enjoyed the camaraderie and 
friendships that came with the sport.
  As Eleanor celebrates her 100th birthday, we honor a life defined by 
family, perseverance, hard work, and cherished relationships. Her 
legacy lives on through the many generations of her family and all 
those fortunate enough to know her.

                          ____________________
```
