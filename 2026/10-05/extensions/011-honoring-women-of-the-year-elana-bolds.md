# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ELANA BOLDS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ELANA BOLDS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Elana ``Lana'' Bolds as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a 
community leader, Elana cares for her neighbors, helps families in 
need, and makes herself available whenever people call. Since 1995, 
Elana has organized community events, talent shows, youth 
opportunities, and other activities that give children positive 
opportunities to connect and thrive. She has worked in community 
centers, schools, and programs, helping young people understand the 
consequences of their choices. More than an event organizer, she 
creates a true sense of community in her neighborhood, making children 
feel seen, protected, celebrated, and connected. Elana is also a 
funeral singer, where she witnessed far too many children from her 
neighborhood lose their lives to violence. Twenty years ago, she 
channeled her grief and concern into establishing the annual ``Put the 
Guns Down'' anti-violence event. Through her organization, Visions of 
Hope, she continues to bring families, youth, service providers, 
community leaders, and public safety partners together to address 
bullying and violence prevention. Elana's commitment to her community 
has endured through personal loss, the COVID-19 pandemic, and other 
hardships. While she has mourned children she once worked with, her 
guidance and unwavering presence have helped many others grow into 
adulthood. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Elana ``Lana'' Bolds 
for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
