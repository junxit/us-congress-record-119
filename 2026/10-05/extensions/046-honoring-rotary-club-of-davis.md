# HONORING ROTARY CLUB OF DAVIS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                     HONORING ROTARY CLUB OF DAVIS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Rotary 
Club of Davis on their centennial anniversary as they celebrate 100 
years of community service.

[[Page E1026]]

  At the time of the Rotary Club of Davis's founding in 1926, the City 
of Davis, California only had population of approximately 2,000 people. 
Of those residents, 25 business owners, University of California staff, 
and farmers joined together to form a Rotary Club that aimed to promote 
fellowship and provide community service. Four of the Davis Rotary's 
founding members had previously served on the first Davis City Council.
  The Rotary Club of Davis is chartered under Rotary International, 
which began in 1905 as an all-male organization. By the mid-1970s, many 
members of Davis Rotary Club were among those urging Rotary 
International to change that policy. When Rotary International finally 
did so in 1987, Rotary Club of Davis was among the first chapters to 
admit women to their membership. Since then, many women have served in 
club leadership, including current president Donna Prebys.
  Rotary Club of Davis is committed to community service. The club's 
first community project was the Davis Boy Scout cabin which was 
constructed in 1927 and maintained through the year 2000. In its early 
decades, the club made grants to individuals in the community who 
needed assistance with healthcare costs and maintained a loan program 
for international students at the university. The club landscaped the 
Davis High School campus in the 1970s, built a stage at the local Pence 
Gallery in the 1980s, and helped with projects at the University of 
California Davis Arboretum and North Davis Ponds in the 1990s.
  Today, the club provides volunteers and financial support for worthy 
causes such as Meals on Wheels, the Yolo Food Bank, the Yolo Crisis 
Nursery, Yolo County Children's Alliance, Cameron's Place, Empower 
Yolo, and the Salvation Army. Through their annual Big Night 
fundraising dinner, which began in 2000, the Rotary Club of Davis has 
raised over $1 million dedicated to Yolo County organizations working 
to prevent child abuse.
  The Davis Rotary supports the work of the Rotary Foundation and its 
projects worldwide. They have partnered with other Rotary Clubs on a 
host of projects, including the City of Davis's Sister City, Uman, 
Ukraine, and most recently, the Vacaville Rotary Club's project to 
build water wells in a remote village in Peru. They have supported 
worthy causes with Sahaya International in Africa and The Wardrobe in 
Ukraine.
  Mr. Speaker, Rotary Club of Davis has dedicated 100 years of service 
to our Davis community. Therefore, it is fitting and proper that we 
honor the Rotary Club here today.
```
