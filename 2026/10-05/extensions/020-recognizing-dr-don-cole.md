# RECOGNIZING DR. DON COLE

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING DR. DON COLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GRAVES. Mr. Speaker, I proudly rise today to recognize Dr. Don 
Cole, a remarkable man who has exemplified the finest qualities of 
citizenship, leadership, and service through his years in the United 
States Navy and his continued service to our community. Dr. Cole served 
our nation in the United States Navy, where he also earned his medical 
degree and began a distinguished career dedicated to caring for others.
  Dr. Cole continues his commitment to service through his work on my 
Military Academy Nominations Board, where he interviews prospective 
candidates and provides thoughtful recommendations as I consider 
nominations to our nation's service academies. A proud husband and 
father, Dr. Cole embodies the values of service, dedication, and 
leadership while helping identify the next generation of military 
leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I proudly ask my colleagues to join me in commending Dr. 
Don Cole for his dedication and hard work throughout the Military 
Academy nominations process. His service to the United States, the 
state of Missouri, and the people of Missouri's Sixth Congressional 
District is deeply appreciated, and I am proud to represent him in the 
United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
