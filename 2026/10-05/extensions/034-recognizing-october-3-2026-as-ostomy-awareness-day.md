# RECOGNIZING OCTOBER 3, 2026, AS OSTOMY AWARENESS DAY

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
          RECOGNIZING OCTOBER 3, 2026, AS OSTOMY AWARENESS DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Ostomy Awareness Day was observed on Saturday, 
October 3, 2026. An ostomy can be a difficult and deeply personal 
journey, but for many individuals, it is also a second chance at life. 
Ostomy surgery can provide a path forward for people facing serious 
illnesses and conditions, including colorectal cancer, inflammatory 
bowel disease, traumatic injuries because of domestic abuse or military 
engagement, and any other number of medical challenges. These 
procedures can be life-saving and life-restoring, allowing individuals 
to return to their families, their workplaces, their communities, and 
the activities they love.
  Yet too often, people living with an ostomy face misunderstanding, 
stigma, and unnecessary barriers. Ostomy Awareness Day provides an 
important opportunity to educate the public, dispel misconceptions, and 
remind us that an ostomy does not define a person or diminish the 
quality of a life.
  In Florida, approximately 55,000 people are living with an ostomy. 
They are our neighbors, friends, family members, coworkers, veterans, 
caregivers, students, and community leaders. Their experiences 
demonstrate the strength and resilience of people who have overcome 
significant medical challenges and continue to live full and meaningful 
lives.
  I also want to recognize the families, caregivers, physicians, nurses 
and certified ostomy nurse specialists, and other health care 
professionals who provide essential care and support to those living 
with an ostomy. Their compassion and expertise make an enormous 
difference in helping patients adjust to life after surgery and thrive.
  This year's Ostomy Awareness Day theme, ``One Community--Stronger 
Together,'' is a fitting reminder that no one should have to navigate 
life with an ostomy alone. Awareness, education, peer support, and 
access to quality care can help ensure that every person who needs an 
ostomy has the resources and support necessary to live with confidence 
and dignity.
  On Ostomy Awareness Day, we celebrate the resilience of the ostomy 
community and recognize the medical advances and dedicated 
professionals that have made these life-saving surgeries possible. Most 
importantly, we affirm that an ostomy is not the end of a person's 
story--it can be the beginning of a healthier and more hopeful chapter.
  I am proud to stand with the Florida ostomy community and with ostomy 
communities across our Nation. I encourage my colleagues and all 
Americans to join me in raising awareness, breaking down stigma. and 
celebrating those whose lives have been saved and restored through 
ostomy surgery.
```
