# HONORING LARCENIA J. BULLARD

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                      HONORING LARCENIA J. BULLARD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor one of Florida's most respected and heralded political leaders, 
Senator Larcenia J. Bullard, as she and her family celebrate her 
influential political career as a member of both the Florida State 
House of Representative and the Florida State Senate.
  Senator Larcenia J. Bullard began her career as a Florida policymaker 
in 1992, when she was elected to the Florida House of Representatives 
for District 118; the first woman ever elected to the post. After 
serving as a Representative for eight years, she was elected to the 
Florida Senate by District 39 in 2002. She was reelected in 2004 and in 
2008.
  The Florida State Senator successfully passed important legislation, 
like the Magic City Children's Zone, the Good Samaritan Act, and the 
State Facilities/Emergency Shelters Bill. Senator Bullard has also 
served as the Vice Chair of the Agricultural and Transportation 
committees.
  Senator Bullard is also the matriarch of an esteemed family of 
Florida Legislators, which is unprecedented. Her husband is former 
State Representative Edward B. Bullard, who represented District 118 
from 2000 to 2008. The family tradition of public service continues 
with their son Dwight Bullard, who was elected as District 118's 
representative in 2008. Senator Bullard has two other children; Vincent 
and Edwina. They have three grandchildren.
  Senator Larcenia J. Bullard was born and raised in Allendale, South 
Carolina. She relocated to Florida in 1980, where she obtained her 
Master's Degree from Nova Southeastern University and began to work as 
a teacher in the Miami-Dade Public School System.
  I am pleased to honor Senator Larcenia J. Bullard of Florida District 
39, for her unwavering dedication and commitment to her constituents, 
her community and her state.
```
