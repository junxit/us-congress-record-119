# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ANNIE KING-MEREDITH

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
            HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ANNIE KING-MEREDITH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Annie King-Meredith as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. A devoted 
member of the Richmond community, Annie has dutifully served her 
community in the variety of roles that she has held in her six decades 
of service. She spent her career working in the defense industry as an 
engineer and project manager and has been deeply involved in local 
governance and neighborhood initiatives in Contra Costa County 
throughout her life. Annie has served the North Richmond Municipal 
Advisory Council, the Contra Costa County Neighborhood Services, the 
Shields-Reid Neighborhood Council, and many other organizations. 
Throughout all of these roles, Annie has demonstrated her dedication to 
the goal of community planning and city development, as she aims to 
give back to the communities that raised her. Annie has also dedicated 
her time to the causes of environmental justice, as she calls attention 
to egregious environmental hazards, and helps her local community 
receive more resources and services. In addition to these civic 
contributions, Annie is also a talented artist; she frequently uses her 
artistic skills to both demonstrate the beauty of humanity, and to call 
out injustices. By demonstrating her unwavering service and commitment 
to environmental justice, Annie embodies what it means to give back to 
your community. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Annie 
King-Meredith for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
