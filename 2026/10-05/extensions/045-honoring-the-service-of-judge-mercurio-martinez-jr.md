# HONORING THE SERVICE OF JUDGE MERCURIO MARTINEZ, JR.

**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

```
          HONORING THE SERVICE OF JUDGE MERCURIO MARTINEZ, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Judge Mercurio 
Martinez, Jr. of Laredo, Texas, on his retirement from the Laredo 
College Board of Trustees after more than six decades of service to 
higher education and the Laredo community.
  Judge Martinez began his career in the classroom. He served as a 
full-time instructor at Laredo Junior College from 1959 to 1968, 
returned as a part-time instructor in the spring semesters of 1976 and 
1978, and taught Business Administration full time from 1982 until June 
1987.
  Altogether, he gave roughly 15 years to instruction at the 
institution now known as Laredo College.
  His service to public education continued on the Laredo Independent 
School District Board, where he served from June 1973 to March 1977. 
During part of that time, the LISD Board also governed Laredo Junior 
College.
  Judge Martinez was first elected to the Laredo College Board of 
Trustees in 1988 and served 24 years on that board across multiple 
terms.
  As a trustee, he helped strengthen the college's financial footing 
and its ability to serve students across Webb County.
  His public service extended well beyond education. He served on the 
Laredo City Council from 1978 to 1991 and as Webb County Judge from 
1991 to 2002, leading one of the largest counties in Texas.
  Mr. Speaker, few people have shaped a community's schools and 
colleges as long or as directly as Judge Martinez.
  On behalf of the 28th District of Texas, I thank him and his family 
for six decades of service to Laredo, and I wish him the very best in 
his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
