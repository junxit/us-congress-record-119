# HONORING THE LIFE OF KENTUCKY STATE TROOPER HAYDEN PHILLIPS

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
      HONORING THE LIFE OF KENTUCKY STATE TROOPER HAYDEN PHILLIPS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, the Kentucky Congressional Delegation 
mourns the loss of Kentucky State Trooper Hayden Phillips, who was 
tragically killed in the line of duty on the afternoon of September 28, 
2026.
  Trooper Phillips leaves behind a loving wife, Jordan; three young 
sons, Kason, Knox, and Konnor; loving parents, Homer and Robin: 
dedicated colleagues in law enforcement; and a Commonwealth that will 
forever be indebted to him for his sacrifice.
  A native of South Central Kentucky, Hayden Phillips dedicated his 
life serving the people of Kentucky, first as a volunteer firefighter 
and EMT, then as an officer with the Glasgow Police Department and 
Russell Springs Police Department. According to his family, Trooper 
Phillip's lifelong dream was to serve our Commonwealth as a Kentucky 
State Trooper, a dream he ultimately achieved, joining the force in 
2023.
  Trooper Phillips was assigned to Kentucky State Police Post 3, 
selflessly serving the citizens of Allen, Barren, Butler, Edmonson, 
Hart, Logan, Simpson, and Warren Counties. Our community was no doubt a 
better place because of Trooper Phillips' willingness to protect and 
defend his fellow Kentuckians.
  In the few short days since his passing, Kentuckians from all walks 
of life have come together to express their heartfelt sympathy and 
mourn the loss of this Kentucky hero. The Commonwealth of Kentucky and 
the United States of America will forever be grateful to Trooper Hayden 
Phillips for making the ultimate sacrifice to protect our community. 
His family, friends, and loved ones will always remain in our prayers.

                          ____________________
```
