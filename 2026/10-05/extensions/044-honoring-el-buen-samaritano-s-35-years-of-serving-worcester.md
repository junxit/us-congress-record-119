# HONORING EL BUEN SAMARITANO'S 35 YEARS OF SERVING WORCESTER

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
      HONORING EL BUEN SAMARITANO'S 35 YEARS OF SERVING WORCESTER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JAMES P. McGOVERN

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, this year, El Buen Samaritano Food Program 
is celebrating 35 years of serving Worcester, Massachusetts, as a key 
leader in the fight to end hunger and build a more resilient community.
  El Buen Samaritano was founded in 1991 by Maria and Osiris Reyes, two 
incredible anti-hunger champions who wanted their neighbors to have 
enough to eat. They established a food pantry to provide members of our 
community with access to emergency food when times are tough. Thirty-
five years later, the food pantry continues to provide families, 
seniors, and all people facing food insecurity with weekly access to 
nutritious food.
  But EBS is so much more than a food pantry; they offer comprehensive 
support services to provide people with clothing, job opportunities, 
and health care services. EBS also provides toys for kids to ensure 
everyone can have a happy holiday season.
  Maria and Osiris' passion for serving others inspired their daughter, 
Mari Gonzalez, to join the movement. Mari now serves as Executive 
Director of El Buen Samaritano Food Program, where she continues to 
provide families in Worcester with better access to basic needs. Mari 
has also become a leading voice in Worcester advocating for a world 
where all people thrive.
  El Buen Samaritano's work is rooted in love, compassion, and 
kindness. At the heart of EBS is the idea that every person should be 
treated with dignity. My wife Lisa and I have had the honor of 
volunteering with the incredible team at El Buen Samaritano, and their 
impact on the over 35,000 families who they have served is clear.
  Maria, Osiris, and Mari's decades of leadership have improved our 
city in unimaginable ways. I'm grateful for their tremendous 
contributions and look forward to continuing to partner with them as we 
fight for a future where the needs of all people are met and no person 
goes hungry.
```
