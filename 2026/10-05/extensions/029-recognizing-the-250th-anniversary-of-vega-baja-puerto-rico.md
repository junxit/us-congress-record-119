# RECOGNIZING THE 250TH ANNIVERSARY OF VEGA BAJA, PUERTO RICO

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE 250TH ANNIVERSARY OF VEGA BAJA, PUERTO RICO

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the municipality of 
Vega Baja, Puerto Rico, as it celebrates its 250th anniversary.
  Vega Baja was officially founded on October 7, 1776, thanks to the 
vision of Don Antonio Viera and the generosity of Don Manuel Negron 
Benitez, who provided the land to establish the town. It is a 
remarkable coincidence of history that Vega Baja shares its founding 
year with our Nation, making this semi-quincentennial an especially 
profound milestone for both.
  Over the past two and a half centuries, the city has been a 
cornerstone of Puerto Rico's development. It grew into an agricultural 
powerhouse, known for its sugarcane and citrus. Later, in 1873, the 
establishment of the Central San Vicente, the island's first sugarcane 
refinery, transformed the local economy and cemented Vega Baja's legacy 
in our island's industrial history.
  But the true heart of Vega Baja lies in its people. Walking through 
its historic downtown, you can feel the deep cultural identity of a 
community that has raised generations of educators, artists, athletes, 
and public servants who have made Puerto Rico proud. This year 2026, 
the 69th edition of the National Puerto Rican Day Parade was dedicated 
to Vega Baja, affectionately known as the ``Ciudad del Melao Melao,'' 
in commemoration of this 250th anniversary.
  Reaching 250 years is no small feat, and it reminds us of the 
critical role of sound public administration. Simply put, no city 
endures and thrives for two and a half centuries without dedicated 
administrators and leaders who consistently prioritize the well-being 
of their citizens.
  I invite my colleagues to join me in congratulating Mayor Marcos Cruz 
Molina and the thousands of proud vegabajenos on this historic 
occasion. Here's to the next 250 years of progress and prosperity for 
Vega Baja.

                          ____________________
```
