# HONORING 165 YEARS OF McDONALD STUDIO IN SOUTH BEND

**Speaking:** Yakym, Rudy (Y000067) [R-IN]

```
          HONORING 165 YEARS OF McDONALD STUDIO IN SOUTH BEND

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RUDY YAKYM III

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize McDonald Studio of 
South Bend, Indiana, the longest-operating photography studio in our 
state, and the Patnaude family, who have owned it for 80 years.
  For 165 years, McDonald Studio has captured timeless moments for the 
people of South Bend. Weddings. Senior pictures. Baby pictures. Family 
portraits. Over the past half-century, generations of South Bend high 
school seniors sat for their senior pictures at McDonald Studio. If you 
grew up in South Bend, chances are McDonald Studio took your picture, 
or your parents', or your grandparents'.
  The story begins in 1861, when a young photographer named Albert 
McDonald opened a picture studio in South Bend. That was the year the 
Civil War began, and four years before South Bend was incorporated as a 
city. Photography itself was barely two decades old. Albert ran the 
studio for more than three decades. His son, Edwin, and other owners 
carried it forward in the years that followed. Through every change, 
the McDonald name carried on.
  In 1946, Raymond F. Patnaude and his wife, Beulah, bought McDonald 
Studio. Their son, David, and his wife, Ann, joined the business in 
1975, more than 50 years ago.
  McDonald Studio endured the Civil War, the Great Depression, and two 
World Wars. It lasted through the arrival of color film, Polaroids, and 
digital photography. But one thing never changed: capturing the moments 
that matter most to South Bend families.
  The studio's mission is to promote value, beauty, expression, and 
timeless memories to share with family and friends, For more than 160 
years, McDonald Studio has done exactly that.
  In September, David and Ann announced they are ready to retire. 
Whatever comes next for the business, McDonald Studio's legacy is

[[Page E1023]]

secure. It hangs on the walls of Hoosier homes across our community.
  Mr. Speaker, a photograph freezes a moment in time. For 165 years, 
McDonald Studio has made sure South Bend's most important moments were 
never lost. On behalf of Indiana's Second Congressional District, I 
congratulate McDonald Studio on 165 remarkable years and thank the 
Patnaude family for preserving the moments that matter most to Hoosier 
families.
```
