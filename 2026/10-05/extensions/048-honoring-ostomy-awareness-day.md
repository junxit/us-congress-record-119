# HONORING OSTOMY AWARENESS DAY

**Speaking:** Keating, William R. (K000375) [D-MA]

```
                     HONORING OSTOMY AWARENESS DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. WILLIAM R. KEATING

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. KEATING. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Ostomy 
Awareness Day.
  An ostomy can be a challenging and deeply personal journey, but for 
many, it is also a life-saving and life-restoring procedure. Whether 
facing colorectal cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, traumatic 
injuries, or other serious health challenges, ostomy surgery offers a 
path forward, restoring hope and enabling people to return to their 
families, workplaces, and communities.
  In Massachusetts, approximately 17,500 people have an ostomy. They 
are our neighbors, friends, family members, coworkers, veterans, 
students, caregivers, and community leaders. Their journeys reflect 
remarkable strength and resilience, overcoming adversity and 
significant medical challenges to lead full and meaningful lives.
  Too often, people living with an ostomy face stigma, 
misunderstanding, and unnecessary barriers. Ostomy Awareness Day 
provides an important opportunity to educate the public, dispel 
misconceptions, remind us that an ostomy does not define a person or 
diminish their quality of life, and recognize the families, caregivers, 
clinicians, certified ostomy nurse specialists, and other health care 
professionals who provide essential care and support. Their compassion 
and expertise are pivotal in helping people adjust to life after 
surgery and thrive.
  This year, Ostomy Awareness Day will be observed on October 3, 2026. 
We celebrate the resilience of the ostomy community and honor the 
dedicated professionals and medical advancements that make these 
lifesaving surgeries possible. Most importantly, we recognize that an 
ostomy is not the end of a person's story; it can mark the beginning of 
a healthier and more hopeful chapter. This year's theme, ``One 
Community--Stronger Together,'' reminds us that no one should have to 
navigate life with an ostomy alone. Awareness, education, peer support, 
and access to quality care are essential to ensure that every person 
with an ostomy can live with confidence and dignity.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to honor the ostomy community, and I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing October 3, 2026, as Ostomy 
Awareness Day.
```
