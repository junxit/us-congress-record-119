# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ROCIO HERNANDEZ

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ROCIO HERNANDEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Dr. Rocio

[[Page E1029]]

Hernandez as she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional 
District's Women of the Year. Dr. Rocio ``Cio'' Hernandez is a mental 
health clinician, public health leader, educator, and philanthropist 
whose three decades of work center on service to children, families, 
and communities. She connects individual healing with community 
leadership, advancing accessibility, inclusion, and opportunities for 
individuals to build resilience for themselves and others. Her public 
service includes leadership on the First 5 Children and Families 
Commissions in Contra Costa and Marin counties, where she advocates for 
young children and the systems that support them. She has helped lead 
the Novato Teen Clinic, Young Moms Group, Cio's Angels, and Binational 
Health Week. As a bilingual public information officer, producer, and 
host of CONNECT!, Rocio expanded access to mental health, family 
resources, and public services. Through teaching, clinical practice, 
and community engagement, she has remained committed to reducing stigma 
and helping people feel safe seeking support. As founder, president, 
and CEO of The Be it Foundation, she furthers her work through 
scholarships, emergency assistance, and behavioral health workforce 
development. She also founded Got This! Inc. and Levelation 
International, which promotes resilience and leadership development. 
She is also a generous friend and colleague, often working quietly 
behind the scenes to build a healthier community. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor and recognize Dr. Rocio Hernandez for her positive contributions 
to our community.
```
