# HONORING THE 2026 USA BIOLYMPIAD GOLD MEDALISTS

```
            HONORING THE 2026 USA BIOLYMPIAD GOLD MEDALISTS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ADAM SMITH

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. SMITH. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the outstanding 2026 USA 
Biolympiad (USABO) team and to recognize and congratulate the Gold 
Medal recipients from Washington's Ninth Congressional district: Emma 
Li (Lakeside School), Seattle, Washington and Kevin Wu (BASIS 
Independent), Bellevue, Washington.
  From an initial pool of nearly 12,800 registered students from 46 
states, these Gold Medalists earned the right to represent the United 
States as the USA Biolympiad Team at the International Biology Olympiad 
(IBO), a worldwide competition involving student teams from over 
seventy countries. After completing various practical and theoretical 
exams designed to test students' critical thinking in laboratory skills 
and biological reasoning, all four members on the USA team were awarded 
a Gold Medal at the 37th International Biolympiad. This incredible 
achievement speaks to the hard work and dedication of our nation's 
finest young high school scholars who will go on to pursue careers in 
science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM).
  I thank the Center for Excellence in Education for sponsoring the 
USABO, and I wish Emma and Kevin all the best in their future 
endeavors. Congratulations to them on their win.
```
