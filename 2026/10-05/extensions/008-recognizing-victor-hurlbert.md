# RECOGNIZING VICTOR HURLBERT

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING VICTOR HURLBERT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GRAVES. Mr. Speaker, I proudly rise today to recognize Victor 
Hurlbert, a remarkable man who has exemplified the finest qualities of 
citizenship, leadership, and service. After graduating from Oak Park 
High School, Victor attended the United States Naval Academy and served 
in the United States Navy as an intelligence officer. Following his 
military service, he became a financial advisor before joining Hurlbert 
CPS LLC, his family's business. Since 2019, Victor has served as 
Auditor of Clay County, Missouri, and he recently returned to military 
service as an intelligence officer in the United States Army Reserve.
  Victor also continues his commitment to service through my Military 
Academy Nominations Board, where he interviews prospective candidates 
and provides thoughtful recommendations as I consider nominations to 
our Nation's service academies. Through his military and public 
service, Victor has demonstrated a deep understanding of leadership, 
citizenship, and duty.
  Mr. Speaker, I proudly ask my colleagues to join me in commending 
Victor Hurlbert for his dedication and hard work throughout the 
Military Academy nominations process. His service to the United States, 
the state of Missouri, Clay County and the people of Missouri's Sixth 
Congressional District is deeply appreciated, and I am proud to 
represent him in the United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
