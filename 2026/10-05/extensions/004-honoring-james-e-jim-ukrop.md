# HONORING JAMES E. "JIM" UKROP

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                    HONORING JAMES E. ``JIM'' UKROP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
incredible life of Mr. James E. ``Jim'' Ukrop, a cherished member of 
the Richmond community. He was a shining example of what it meant to be 
a businessman, philanthropist, servant leader, and Virginian.
  Jim earned a bachelor's degree in business administration from 
William & Mary and began helping his family run Ukrop's Supermarket. 
Alongside his brother Bobby, he helped grow the family business from 
one store to over two dozen locations across Virginia. Their stores 
were known for incredible customer service, even carrying patrons' 
groceries to their cars. For Jim and his family, it was all about 
treating others the way you would want to be treated. In December of 
2009, the family announced it would sell its 25 supermarkets to Giant-
Carlisle. However, Jim's service didn't stop with this sale but rather 
took on a new form. After the sale, he created New Richmond Ventures, a 
venture capital firm that raised money for local startup businesses. 
Jim cared deeply about the Richmond area, its community members, and 
young entrepreneurs to whom he was devoted to helping.
  Jim was also a proud and dedicated William & Mary alumnus, serving as 
a secretary of the William & Mary Board of Visitors, as president of 
the Alumni Association Board, and as a member of the William & Mary 
Foundation. Additionally, he was awarded the Alumni Medallion in 1981, 
the highest honor given by the Alumni Association. Jim generously gave 
his time, talent, and resources. He was known throughout the William & 
Mary community for his optimism, pragmatism, and unwavering focus on 
excellence.
  His life was nothing short of extraordinary and he represented the 
values that make our Commonwealth so great. He spent his life serving 
others and worked alongside fellow Virginians to make his hometown a 
better place. Jim's joyful attitude, generosity, and kind spirit will 
truly be missed. Mr. Speaker, I ask that you and my colleagues join me 
in honoring and celebrating the life of Mr. James E. ``Jim'' Ukrop. I 
pray for his eternal peace and strength for his loved ones during this 
time.
```
