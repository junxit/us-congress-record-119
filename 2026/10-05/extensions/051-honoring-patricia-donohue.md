# HONORING PATRICIA DONOHUE

**Speaking:** Beyer, Donald S. (B001292) [D-VA]

```
                       HONORING PATRICIA DONOHUE

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. DONALD S. BEYER, JR.

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. BEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Ms. Patricia Donohue for 
her extensive service across both the public and private sectors. Ms. 
Donohue is the Emerging Technology Program Manager within the Office of 
the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Installations, Energy and 
Environment. She is responsible for identifying meaningful technology 
pilot opportunities and driving resilience, sustainability, and 
innovation for U.S. Army building systems, energy and water 
infrastructure, microgrids, and energy storage. In this role, she has 
successfully steered Research and Development resources for critical 
Army strategic initiatives implementation.
  With over thirty years of cradle-to-grave engineering and 
construction management experience, Ms. Donohue has built a career on 
transforming complex technical challenges into programs that safeguard 
our installations, secure our energy and water infrastructure, and 
mitigate both physical and cyber risks. She actively represents the 
Army on several interagency working groups, helping to develop policy 
and guidance, and collaborates with national laboratories on matters of 
national concern. Her steadfast leadership has included portfolio 
performance reviews, oversight of national programs, construction 
management of major system projects, and the inspection and operation 
of facilities. Her technical experience encompasses the development of 
vertical and horizontal construction projects, energy management, 
hydrogeological and geotechnical investigations, and environmental 
assessments. Her previous work includes establishing centers of 
expertise in Microgrids, Building Commissioning, and stormwater 
management site development during her time with the U.S. Army Corps of 
Engineers.
  I hope my colleagues will join me in honoring my constituent, Ms. 
Donohue, for her remarkable career and contributions to the Army as a 
skilled engineer with a commitment to public service.
```
