# HONORING KAISER PERMANENTE NAPA MEDICAL OFFICES

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
            HONORING KAISER PERMANENTE NAPA MEDICAL OFFICES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
Kaiser Permanente Napa Medical Offices on their 75th Anniversary as 
they celebrate decades of high quality, compassionate care and 
commitment to the health and well-being of Napa County.
  Kaiser Permanente is the nation's largest non-profit integrated 
health care system, bringing physicians, care teams, hospitals, 
pharmacies, and health plans together to provide coordinated, high 
quality and affordable care. Kaiser Permanente is nationally recognized 
for excellence in prevention, specialty care and clinical outcomes, 
supported by advanced medical research, innovation and leading Centers 
of Excellence. Guided by its mission to improve the health of its 
members and the communities it serves, Kaiser Permanente invests in 
programs that expand access to care, address social drivers of health 
and advance community well-being.
  Kaiser Permanente began caring for Napa families in 1951 and today 
serves approximately 45,000 members throughout Napa Valley and the 
surrounding region. Napa Medical Offices have provided generations of 
local families with compassionate, coordinated care, offering primary, 
specialty, behavioral health, pharmacy, laboratory, diagnostic and 
other essential services in one convenient location.
  Kaiser Permanente and the Trefethen family share a significant 
historical connection through Eugene ``Gene'' Trefethen, a trusted 
leader within the Kaiser enterprises who played an influential role in 
shaping the partnership between Kaiser Foundation Health Plan and its 
medical groups. Eugene Trefethen's leadership helped strengthen the 
integrated health care model that enabled Kaiser Permanente to grow 
into the nationally respected health system it is today, creating a 
lasting legacy that connects two important chapters of Napa Valley and 
California history.
  Kaiser Permanente has demonstrated its enduring commitment to Napa 
County through community partnerships, volunteerism, and more than $15 
million in investments supporting the health and well-being of local 
residents as of 2026.
  Mr. Speaker, Kaiser Permanente has dedicated 75 years of service to 
Napa County with Eugene Trefethen's lasting contributions. Therefore, 
it is fitting and proper that we honor the Kaiser Permanente Napa 
Offices here today.
```
