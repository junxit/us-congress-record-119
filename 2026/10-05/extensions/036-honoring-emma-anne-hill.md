# HONORING EMMA ANNE HILL

**Speaking:** Moore, Blake D. (M001213) [R-UT]

```
                        HONORING EMMA ANNE HILL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BLAKE D. MOORE

                                of utah

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. MOORE of Utah. Mr. Speaker, today I honor the life of my 
constituent, Emma Anne Hill, who sadly passed away on September 29, 
2026, at 25 years old. Emma was born with a congenital heart defect, 
known as pulmonary atresia. When she was born, doctors expected she 
would not live through the month, but Emma decided that wasn't enough 
for her. Emma spent her 25 years fighting through multiple surgeries, 
heart failure, pain, and challenges most Americans could not imagine, 
but she also spent her years determined to bring joy to her family and 
friends.
  My heart is with Emma's parents, Casey and Kysha; her siblings, 
Porter, Claire, Adam, and Jane; and the community of people who love 
and have supported Emma and her family over the last 25 years. I am 
comforted to know that they will all be together again one day.
  Emma was a friend to every person she met, spreading happiness, 
friendship, and joy--what her father has called as ``the Emma way.'' In 
her honor, I encourage all of us to be more like Emma.

                          ____________________
```
