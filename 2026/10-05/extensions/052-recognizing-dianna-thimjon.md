# RECOGNIZING DiANNA THIMJON

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING DiANNA THIMJON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GRAVES. Mr. Speaker, I proudly rise today to recognize DiAnna 
Thimjon, a remarkable woman who has exemplified the finest qualities of 
citizenship, leadership, and service. After graduating high school, 
DiAnna attended the United States Military Academy and served our 
Nation in the United States Army as an intelligence officer. Following 
her military service, she entered the private sector, applying the 
leadership and analytical skills she developed at West Point to help 
businesses thrive. She currently serves as an Executive Partner at 
Garner, a leading information and technology research company.
  DiAnna continues her commitment to service through my Military 
Academy Nominations Board, where she interviews prospective candidates 
and provides thoughtful recommendations as I consider nominations to 
our nation's service academies. A proud wife and mother, DiAnna has 
instilled the values of service and leadership in her children. Her 
son, Benjamin, has followed in her footsteps by serving our country.
  Mr. Speaker, I proudly ask my colleagues to join me in commending 
DiAnna Thimjon for her dedication and hard work throughout the Military 
Academy nominations process. Her service to the United States, the 
state of Missouri, and the people of Missouri's Sixth Congressional 
District is deeply appreciated, and I am proud to represent her in the 
United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
