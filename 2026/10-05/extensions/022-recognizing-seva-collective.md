# RECOGNIZING SEVA COLLECTIVE

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING SEVA COLLECTIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize SEVA Collective, a 
nonprofit based in the City of Santa Ana, for their diligent efforts in 
supporting our communities in need.
  Since its inception, SEVA Collective has served over 5.5 million 
meals, establishing methods to tackle food insecurity for communities 
across Orange County and Southern California. Through a strong 
commitment to selfless service, SEVA Collective regularly runs food 
drives and provides food for our neighbors in need, year-round, rain or 
shine.
  Beyond their efforts in tackling food insecurity, SEVA Collective 
also believes in inspiring the next generation of leaders. SEVA 
Collective also offers programming focused on youth development, having 
led 30+ graduates in developing the skills needed to become strong, 
competent, and committed leaders.
  Always striving to find ways to help others, SEVA Collective has also 
established a free medical clinic that operates during their regular 
food distributions. This clinic further supports our neighbors in need, 
providing basic access to medical tests and offering free medical 
consultations for all those who come. With the establishment of this 
clinic, SEVA Collective has been able to help our neighbors catch 
health issues that would have otherwise gone undetected.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing SEVA Collective for 
their hard work and selfless service in helping those in need and 
inspiring action through committed leadership. We thank SEVA Collective 
for all that they have done to support the health and wellness of our 
neighbors back home. All of us here in Orange County wish them success 
as they continue to serve our communities for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
