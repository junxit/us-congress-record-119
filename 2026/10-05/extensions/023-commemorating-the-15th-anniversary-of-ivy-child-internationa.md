# COMMEMORATING THE 15TH ANNIVERSARY OF IVY CHILD INTERNATIONAL

**Speaking:** Trahan, Lori (T000482) [D-MA]

```
     COMMEMORATING THE 15TH ANNIVERSARY OF IVY CHILD INTERNATIONAL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LORI TRAHAN

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mrs. TRAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 15th 
Anniversary of Ivy Child International, a Massachusetts-based nonprofit 
organization devoted to promoting wellness, mindfulness, and 
culturally-sensitive education to children and communities. Ivy Child 
International has a mission to cultivate resilience and leadership 
through mindfulness, with a priority towards children who have the 
fewest resources. Their values of implementing evidence-based research, 
developing deep-rooted relationships, and authentic commitment to their 
community partners are to be admired. In the last fifteen years, Ivy 
Child International has developed over three hundred fifty programs, 
serving nearly four hundred thousand program participants.
  At the heart of Ivy Child International's work is the belief that 
every child deserves the opportunity and support to thrive. Its 
programs

[[Page E1019]]

provide children with practical tools to manage stress, strengthen 
emotional well-being, and develop the confidence and resilience needed 
to succeed. Just as importantly, Ivy Child recognizes that effective 
programs must reflect the cultures, languages, and experiences of the 
communities they serve. By bringing together educators, families, and 
community partners, Ivy Child has helped create supportive environments 
where young people can learn, grow, and reach their full potential.
  Children are the future of our communities; through their many 
programs and partnerships, Ivy Child has demonstrated how empowering 
youths can positively impact families and entire communities. They have 
received recognition from the Massachusetts Department of Public Health 
as an ``Essential Agent of Change'' and have received various honors 
and accolades across the Commonwealth, including being recognized as a 
top Mindful Nonprofit. They employ a wide range of talent to promote a 
multilingual and multicultural education for our children--preparing 
them for a world that is increasingly connected. I am grateful for the 
work they do and look forward to their continued growth and 
contributions within our communities.

                          ____________________
```
