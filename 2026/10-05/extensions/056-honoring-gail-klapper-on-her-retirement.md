# HONORING GAIL KLAPPER ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Hurd, Jeff (H001100) [R-CO]

```
                HONORING GAIL KLAPPER ON HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JEFF HURD

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Gail Klapper 
for her remarkable tenure leading the Colorado Forum.
  The Forum is a nonpartisan group of 75 to 80 CEOs and community 
leaders from across Colorado who take on some of the most difficult 
issues facing our state--from energy development and environmental 
protection, to water, health care, and education.
  What makes the Forum unusual is that it takes a position only when 
its members reach unanimity. That requires more than talking points. It 
requires serious analysis, honest disagreement, and a willingness to 
work toward practical solutions. Under Gail's leadership, that approach 
made the Colorado Forum a trusted resource for policymakers on both 
sides of the aisle.
  I had the privilege of serving as a member of the Forum and seeing 
Gail's leadership firsthand. She had a rare ability to bring people 
with different perspectives together and keep them focused on solving 
problems.
  Gail did more than convene leaders. She helped build a culture of 
thoughtful, principled civic leadership in Colorado.
  On behalf of Colorado's Third District and the entire State of 
Colorado, I thank Gail for her service and congratulate her on an 
extraordinary career.

                          ____________________
```
