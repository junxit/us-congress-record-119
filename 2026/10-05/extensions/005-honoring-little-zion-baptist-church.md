# HONORING LITTLE ZION BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING LITTLE ZION BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Little Zion Baptist 
Church, a congregation that has stood for more than 130 years

[[Page E1014]]

and gives glory to God for its longevity. The original Little Zion 
Baptist Church was erected in the community of Brookstown in 1894 under 
the leadership of Rev. Wesley Smith, with a building committee that 
included the late Brother Thomas Haynes and the late Brother Whitney.
  In 1924, the Brookstown sanctuary was destroyed by fire, a loss that 
led to the purchase of a new site on Edwin Street under the leadership 
of the late Rev. Beverly White, Sr. The building committee for the new 
church consisted of the late Brother Joseph White, Brother Jim Austin, 
and Brother Sunny Wade. By 1951, as the membership continued to grow, 
the congregation recognized that a renovation of the church was 
essential, and a building committee was established to meet the needs 
of its members. That renovation was completed under the leadership of 
the late Rev. R.W. Ball, who was later led to relocate Little Zion to 
74th Avenue in the Banks Community, where the church stands today.
  When Rev. Ball retired in 1963, Rev. Morris Jackson, Jr. was elected 
pastor the following year and went on to serve Little Zion for 44 
years, guiding the congregation through four decades of change. Upon 
his retirement in 2008, his son, Marcus Jackson, was elected as Little 
Zion's fifth pastor, and he continues to serve today. Pastor Jackson 
has led Little Zion as a value-driven church that stands on the 
principles of faith, hope, and love, an approach that appreciates the 
assets each member brings to the church, the community, and the 
Kingdom.
  Over its many years of existence, Little Zion has survived many 
challenges, from the loss of its first sanctuary to fire to the changes 
of more than a century. As the congregation grows into the image of 
Christ, it continues to influence its church, its families, its 
communities, and the world, spreading the gospel throughout all 
nations. The spirit and wisdom of its past have well equipped Little 
Zion to carry on a great legacy, and the congregation gives thanks to 
God for the dedication and faithfulness of its pastors and members 
throughout the years. and for the fact that, in its own words, it is 
``still standing.''
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating Little 
Zion Baptist Church, Pastor Marcus Jackson, and the dedicated 
congregation for more than 130 years of faith, resilience, and service. 
May Little Zion continue to stand for generation to come.
```
