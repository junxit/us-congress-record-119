# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: SHASA CURL

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: SHASA CURL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Shasa Curl as 
she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. Shasa has dedicated more than two decades as City 
Manager of the city of Richmond, leaving behind a storied career in 
public service and a lasting legacy in the City of Richmond.
  Shasa recently concluded her career as Richmond City Manager after 
decades of strengthening the community she served. Beginning her public 
service career with the Redevelopment Agency, Shasa drew on her 
expertise in urban development to help secure over $300 million in new 
grant funding for infrastructure, housing, and education. In 
partnership with the Richmond Fund for Children and Youth, Shasa helped 
allocate $9 million for youth-serving organizations in the 2026 fiscal 
year alone. Shasa's leadership strengthened Richmond's financial 
position, increasing the city's reserves to 21 percent and removing 
Richmond from the State auditor's high-risk designation. Under her 
leadership, Richmond recorded its lowest homicide rate in 2025. 
Throughout her career, she built strong partnerships with community 
stakeholders and City Councils to advance Richmond's long-term 
interests. Shasa is a dedicated public servant whose leadership, 
expertise, and commitment to Richmond have left an everlasting legacy. 
On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it 
is my pleasure to honor and recognize Shasa Curl for her positive 
contributions to our community.
```
