# HONORING MARCELINA "MARCI" PINA-CHRISTIAN

**Speaking:** Keating, William R. (K000375) [D-MA]

```
              HONORING MARCELINA ``MARCI'' PINA-CHRISTIAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. WILLIAM R. KEATING

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. KEATING. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Marcelina 
``Marci'' Pina-Christian and her years of service to New Bedford 
community.
  After earning a degree in business administration from the University 
of Massachusetts Amherst, Marci first began her career in Rhode Island 
as an insurance adjuster before returning to New Bedford, where she 
discovered an appreciation for community engagement at the YWCA. For 
many years, Marci went on to excel in the private sector, becoming the 
first woman of color to lead the customer service department at Main 
Street Textiles. She later followed her passion for education, teaching 
English at Keith Middle School for five years.
  In 2007, Marci joined the City of New Bedford's Department of 
Community Services as a human services coordinator, later rising to the 
role of program director. For nearly two decades, Marci led initiatives 
advancing human rights, health awareness, education, neighborhood 
engagement, cultural programming, and community outreach. She played a 
pivotal role in transforming the city's Human Relations Commission into 
the Human Rights Commission, establishing Human Rights Day, 
implementing New Bedford's official Juneteenth commemoration, and was a 
driving force behind the city's annual Martin Luther King Jr., Day 
program.
  As a certified mediator, Marci led the Face to Face Mediation 
Program, offering free services to court litigants and community 
members. She also used these skills to moderate community meetings and 
public forums, helping residents find common ground and move difficult 
conversations forward. As host of ``Neighborhood Update,'' New 
Bedford's longest-running local cable program. Marci uplifted community 
voices and connected residents with local services and reliable 
information, which proved especially helpful during the COVID-19 
pandemic.
  The impact of Marci's leadership can be felt throughout the 
Commonwealth. She previously served on Attorney General Maura Healey's 
Advisory Council on Race and Equity and currently serves on Governor 
Healey's Advisory

[[Page E1017]]

Council on Black Empowerment. In honor of her late husband, musician 
and educator Armsted Christian, Marci established a committee that 
raised $50,000 to create the Armsted Christian Endowed Scholarship at 
Berklee College of Music, which has awarded six scholarships to date.
  Marci believes that the greatest measure of leadership is not the 
positions held or the programs created, but rather one's ability to 
prepare others to carry the work forward. After 19 years of service, 
Marci retired from the New Bedford Department of Community Services 
earlier this summer, leaving a legacy defined by her commitment to 
strengthening civic institutions, connecting neighbors to vital 
resources, and mentoring and inspiring the next generation to lead.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to honor Marci Pina-Christian, and I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing her enduring service and the 
opportunities she has created for the people of New Bedford and the 
Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
```
