# HONORING THE MEMORY OF JUAN MANUEL ESCOBAR

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
               HONORING THE MEMORY OF JUAN MANUEL ESCOBAR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
remarkable life of Juan Manuel Escobar, a native South Texan whose life 
was defined by integrity, valor, and an unwavering commitment to 
helping others.
  Mr. Escobar was a proud veteran of the United States Marine Corps. He 
enlisted at age 19 and served proudly with the 1st Marine Division in 
Vietnam. His dedication and strong character earned him selection as a 
Marine body bearer and member of the Presidential Honor Guard. These 
qualities continued to define his service in theater, where he was 
wounded in action and awarded the Purple Heart in recognition of his 
courage and sacrifice.
  After his honorable discharge, Mr. Escobar continued his education at 
Pan American University before beginning a career as a teacher and 
football coach at Rio Grande City High School. In the classroom and on 
the field, he became a dedicated mentor and role model for countless 
young people.
  Mr. Escobar carried that same sense of duty into a 25-year career 
with the U.S. Department of Homeland Security. While in this role, he 
took on critical assignments, including serving as the Patrol Agent in 
Charge of the Kingsville Station and as a Senior Special Agent with the 
Organized Crime Drug Enforcement Task Force.
  Mr. Escobar continued serving his community through elected office, 
representing District 43 in the Texas House of Representatives, and 
later as Kleberg County Judge.
  Beyond the positions he held, Mr. Escobar remained a devoted husband 
and father. His life was guided by a commitment to family, mentorship, 
and a desire to make a difference in the lives of those around him.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas's 34th Congressional 
District, I am proud to honor Juan Manuel Escobar for his extraordinary 
life of service, sacrifice, and excellence. South Texas is better place 
because of him, and I am thankful for the lasting mark he left on our 
community.
```
