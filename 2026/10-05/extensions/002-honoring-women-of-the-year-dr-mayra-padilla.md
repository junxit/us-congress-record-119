# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DR. MAYRA PADILLA

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
             HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DR. MAYRA PADILLA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Dr. Mayra Padilla as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a 
lifelong resident of Contra Costa County, Mayra has committed herself 
to leadership, service, equity, and educational opportunity in our 
community. A graduate of Richmond High School, Mayra completed her 
undergraduate education at Contra Costa College and U.C. Davis, where 
she earned a degree in applied physics, later earning a doctorate in 
neuroscience at U.C. Berkeley. After building a career in neuroscience 
at the San Francisco Brain Institute, SRI International, and Sands 
Research, Inc., Mayra returned to Contra Costa College to serve as the 
director of the Hispanic Serving Institution Science, Technology, 
Engineering, and Math grant. She now serves as Dean of Institutional 
Effectiveness and Equity at Contra Costa College. In addition to her 
work with Contra Costa College, Mayra serves the community through 
roles with the East Bay Center for Performing Arts and the Research and 
Planning Group for California Community Colleges. Mayra's work has 
benefited Contra Costa College students and others across the state, 
and her dedication to the community does not go unnoticed. Her efforts 
have strengthened those around her and helped create a more connected, 
supportive, and thriving community. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and 
recognize Dr. Mayra Padilla for her positive contributions to our 
community.
```
