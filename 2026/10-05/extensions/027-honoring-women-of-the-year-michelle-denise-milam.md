# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: MICHELLE DENISE MILAM

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: MICHELLE DENISE MILAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Michelle Denise Milam as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Michelle 
Milam has devoted her career to making Richmond a safer, stronger, and 
more compassionate community.
  A Richmond native, she began her public service as a teenager with 
the East Bay Public Safety Corridor Partnership, working to prevent gun 
violence and building the foundation for a career uniting government 
and community. She later served as a field representative for the 
California State Assembly and as a mayoral aide and spokesperson in the 
Richmond Mayor's Office.
  As Crime Prevention Manager for the City of Richmond Police 
Department, Michelle's work reaches far beyond traditional crime 
prevention. She helped secure nearly $30 million with city, county, 
state, federal, and community partners to expand outreach, shelter, 
treatment, encampment resolution, and pathways to housing.
  Michelle also serves as President of the Richmond Public Library 
Foundation, is a member of the Richmond Rotary Club, and is active in 
St. Paul African Methodist Episcopal Church. Grounded in family and 
community resilience, Michelle leads with compassion, persistence, and 
creativity, consistently bringing people together to strengthen 
Richmond. Her work serves as an inspiration to others and demonstrates 
the meaningful impact that one dedicated individual can have. On behalf 
of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my 
pleasure to honor and recognize Michelle Milam for her positive 
contributions to our community.
```
