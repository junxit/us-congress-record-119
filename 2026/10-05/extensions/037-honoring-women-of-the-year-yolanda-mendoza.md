# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: YOLANDA MENDOZA

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: YOLANDA MENDOZA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 5, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Yolanda Mendoza as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a 
member of the local Latina Center, she has dedicated her time to 
empowering women, supporting families, and supporting our community. 
Meaningful change does not happen overnight, it happens after years of 
hard work and dedication. For the past 13 years, Yolanda has shown up 
for her community with compassion and care for those around her, and 
she didn't give up until she created real change. She has been a 
problem solver for those she serves, committed to connecting people 
with resources, navigating their challenges, and building trust with 
local families in Richmond, Antioch, Roseville, and Bay Point, making a 
difference for anyone she encounters. Her reach extends far beyond the 
district, as she's shown up across California, Mexico, South America, 
and Spain with compassion and a willingness to help. She not only 
embodies what it means to be a community leader but also sets others up 
to do the same. Yolanda's commitment to her community reflects the best 
traits of a leader, as she consistently shows dedication to empowering 
those around her. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize 
Yolanda Mendoza for her positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
