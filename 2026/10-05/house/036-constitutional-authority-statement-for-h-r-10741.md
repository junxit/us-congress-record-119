# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10741

**Measures:** H.R. 10741

```
           By Mr. KHANNA:
       H.R. 10741.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 1 of section 8 of article I of the 
     Constitution of the United States, Congress has the power to 
     enact this legislation to provide for the common defense and 
     general welfare of the United States.
```
