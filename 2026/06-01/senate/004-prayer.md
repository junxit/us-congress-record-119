# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God, let Your glory be over all the Earth. Thank You for 
Your faithfulness that endures forever.
  Today, give our lawmakers steadfast hearts that they may honor You. 
Give them the wisdom, courage, and commitment to strive to do Your 
will. May their debates and discussions be characterized by civility.
  Lord, lead our Senators throughout life's changing scenes, providing 
them with strength for every challenge. Remind them of the importance 
of reverential awe, which is the beginning of wisdom.
  Lord, grant us all competence, reverence, and humility for the living 
of these days.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
