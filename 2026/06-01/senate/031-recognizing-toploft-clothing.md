                      RECOGNIZING TOPLOFT CLOTHING

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Toploft 
Clothing of Carroll County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Founded in 2014 by Stephanie Hackfort, Toploft Clothing began as an 
online women's clothing boutique built on Stephanie's passion for 
fashion and her desire to create a flexible business model balancing 
entrepreneurship with family life. What started as a small online 
retail operation has grown into a thriving business serving customers 
throughout Iowa and beyond.
  Toploft opened its first physical location in 2015 before moving to 
the current downtown Carroll location in 2019. After the COVID-19 
pandemic, the business pivoted from boutique apparel to custom 
clothing, branded merchandise, and webstore services tailored to serve 
businesses, schools, sports teams, and community organizations. Today, 
the company works with more than 300 organizations nationwide and ships 
to customers in 48 States.
  Toploft Clothing has established itself as a trusted provider of 
custom apparel and branded merchandise solutions while maintaining the 
personalized customer service and entrepreneurial spirit that helped 
launch the business over a decade ago. Their specialty ``always open'' 
services help organizations strengthen their branding and community 
presence through customized products and merchandise solutions. 
Supported by a dedicated team of six employees and the occasional 
family member that jumps in when an extra hand is needed, Toploft 
contributes to local economic activity while upholding the innovation 
and flexibility required to remain competitive in an evolving retail 
marketplace.
  Beyond its business operations, Toploft Clothing is actively engaged 
in the local community. All the folks on the management team are 
current or former members of the Carroll Chamber of Commerce. Stephanie 
currently serves as the chamber retail committee leader, her husband 
Matt is the chamber agriculture representative, and the business' chief 
operating officer is chamber president. Through participation in 
chamber initiatives, community events, and merchandise collaborations, 
Toploft demonstrates an ongoing commitment to promoting local 
businesses and fostering community pride.
  Additionally, the business is committed to supporting charitable 
endeavors. In 2025, the team helped raise $28,000 for nonprofit 
organizations, medical needs, local sports activities, and school 
events. In recognition of its growth and impact, Toploft Clothing was 
honored with the 2025 Carroll Chamber of Commerce New Business Award.
  I am pleased to recognize Stephanie and Matt Hackfort, along with the 
entire team at Toploft Clothing, for their dedication to Carroll County 
and customers across the United States. I look forward to their 
continued success and wish them the very best in the years 
ahead.●
