# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 4644, S. 4645, S. 4646, S. 4647, S. 4648, S. 4649

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SCHIFF (for himself, Mr. Kelly, and Ms. 
             Slotkin):
       S. 4644. A bill to provide for limitations on judgements, 
     awards, and compromise settlements under section 1304 of 
     title 31, United States Code; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Mr. SCHIFF (for himself and Mr. Kelly):
       S. 4645. A bill to provide for limitations on judgments, 
     awards, and compromise settlements under section 1304 of 
     title 31, United States Code; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Mr. BLUMENTHAL (for himself, Mr. King, Ms. 
             Klobuchar, Ms. Baldwin, Mr. Lujan, Mr. Bennet, Mr. 
             Whitehouse, Mr. Sanders, Mr. Gallego, Ms. Rosen, Mr. 
             Kelly, Mrs. Shaheen, and Mr. Welch):
       S. 4646. A bill to reinstate criminal penalties for persons 
     charging veterans unauthorized fees relating to claims for 
     benefits under the laws administered by the Secretary of 
     Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Veterans' Affairs.
           By Ms. KLOBUCHAR (for herself and Ms. Smith):
       S. 4647. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide an income tax credit for eldercare expenses; to 
     the Committee on Finance.
           By Ms. WARREN (for herself and Mr. Grassley):
       S. 4648. A bill to improve transparency with respect to 
     foreign influence on Department of Defense contractors; to 
     the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. LEE (for himself and Mr. Wyden):
       S. 4649. A bill to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     update the privacy protections for electronic communications 
     information that is stored by third-party service providers 
     in order to protect consumer privacy interests while meeting 
     law enforcement needs, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
