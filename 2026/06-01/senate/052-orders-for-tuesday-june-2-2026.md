# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 2, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 2, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Tuesday, June 2; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to executive 
session to resume consideration of the Lane nomination; further, that 
at 11:15 a.m., the Senate execute the order in relation to the Lane 
nomination; that following the disposition of the cloture motion with 
respect to the Kuhlman nomination, the Senate recess for the weekly 
conference meetings until 2:15 p.m.; and that if cloture is invoked on 
the Kuhlman nomination, the Senate vote on confirmation of the 
nomination at 2:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
