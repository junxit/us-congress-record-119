                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                  Week of June 2 through June 5, 2026

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of the nomination of 
Kathleen S. Lane, of Montana, to be United States District Judge for 
the District of Montana, post-cloture, and vote on confirmation thereon 
at 11:15 a.m.
  Following disposition of the nomination of Kathleen S. Lane, Senate 
will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Jeffrey 
M. Kuhlman, of Kansas, to be United States District Judge for the 
District of Kansas. If cloture is invoked on the nomination, Senate 
will vote on confirmation thereon at 2:15 p.m.

  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
