# CELEBRATING 15 YEARS OF LEADERSHIP FOR YONKERS MAYOR MIKE SPANO

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
    CELEBRATING 15 YEARS OF LEADERSHIP FOR YONKERS MAYOR MIKE SPANO

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Yonkers Mayor Mike 
Spano as he continues into the 15th year of his leadership as chief 
executive of Westchester's largest city and the third largest city in 
the State of New York, Yonkers.
  Mayor Spano is the longest currently serving mayor of a major city in 
New York State, guiding a municipality of over 200,000 residents and 
running the largest police department, fire department, sanitation 
department, parks and recreation programs, senior citizen centers, 
every area of municipal services, and more.
  Mike and I served side by side in the Westchester County Board of 
Legislators, and we worked together in the New York State Assembly.
  As mayor of the largest local government, we worked closely together 
during my tenure as Westchester County Executive on a myriad of 
programs, from reducing crime to improving green spaces for all to 
enjoy.
  Mayor Spano has shepherded the improvement of Yonkers' public 
education programs with higher graduation rates and new school 
construction. He has shepherded an economic development boom in the 
city, including, among other things, an expansion of the film industry, 
giving Yonkers the nickname of Hollywood on the Hudson. Mayor Spano has 
overseen the expansion of housing, both affordable and market rate, 
attracting new residents.
  From the floor of the House of Representatives, I salute a leader who 
proves that government can still work and achieve for everyone when led 
by a true leader.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Mike Spano for his service.

                          ____________________
```
