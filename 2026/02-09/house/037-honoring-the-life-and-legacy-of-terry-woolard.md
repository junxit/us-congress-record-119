# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF TERRY WOOLARD

**Speaking:** McIver, LaMonica (M001229) [D-NJ]

```
             HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF TERRY WOOLARD

  (Mrs. McIVER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of 
Principal Terry Woolard, a dedicated leader in Essex County education 
for 34 years.
  Terry served for decades as an educator in New Jersey. In the South 
Orange-Maplewood School District, he was first the director of 
information technology, then acting principal of the Alternative School 
at Montrose, and later an assistant principal at Columbia High School.
  Terry was also the president of the American School Counselor 
Association for many years. In his most recent position, he was the 
principal of Hillside High School.
  When he passed away in December, Mr. Woolard left a lasting legacy in 
each school community and organization where he served. Mr. Woolard 
believed deeply in the potential of every student and was known for his 
unwavering care for others.
  We are also grateful for his contributions to the district and the 
impact left on the lives of New Jersey students.
```
