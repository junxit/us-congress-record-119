# CELEBRATING SPARKY'S DINER

**Speaking:** Lawler, Michael (L000599) [R-NY]

```
                              {time}  1920
                       CELEBRATING SPARKY'S DINER

  (Mr. LAWLER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LAWLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate a true Hudson 
Valley institution, Sparky's in Garnerville, New York, as it marks its 
50th anniversary on February 13, 2026.
  Since 1976, Sparky's has become more than a place to grab breakfast 
or lunch. It is a gathering place for generations of families, friends, 
and neighbors across Haverstraw and Rockland County. Opened by Louie 
and Jeannie Nicolakis, Sparky's was built on hard, long hours, and a 
simple but powerful idea: to create a place where everyone feels 
welcome. Louie and Jeannie invested everything they had into this 
vision, and the community has been better for it ever since.
  After Louie's passing, Jeannie carried that legacy forward, devoting 
her life to serving her customers and giving back to the community, 
eventually being recognized with New York State's Empire Award in 2019 
for her community contributions, one of the highest honors for a small 
business.
  For 50 years, Sparky's has been a place where everyone is treated 
like family, where stories are shared over coffee, and kindness is 
always on the menu.
  I congratulate Jeannie and everyone at Sparky's Diner on this 
incredible milestone and wish them many more years of success serving 
the Hudson Valley, and I look forward to coming

[[Page H2085]]

back for another coffee, eggs, bacon, and toast.

                          ____________________
```
