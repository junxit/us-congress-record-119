# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we apologize when we fail to see the Imago Dei, Your 
image, in every human being. We apologize when we refuse to love our 
enemies, to bless those who curse us, and to pray for those who 
despitefully use us. We apologize when we forget that You have made of 
one blood all the nations of the Earth.
  Create in us clean hearts, O God, as you use our lawmakers for Your 
glory.
  We pray in the Name of Him who was abused, despised, and rejected by 
humanity. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
