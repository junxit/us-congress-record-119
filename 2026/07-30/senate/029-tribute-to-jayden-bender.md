# TRIBUTE TO JAYDEN BENDER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JAYDEN BENDER

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jayden Bender, an intern 
in my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work she has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
months.
  Jayden is a graduate of Britton-Hecla High School in Britton, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of South Dakota in 
Vermillion, SD, where she is majoring in legal studies with a minor in 
policial science. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jayden for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
