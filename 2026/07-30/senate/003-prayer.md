# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Today's opening prayer will be offered by 
Pastor Max Lucado, Pastor of the Oak Hills Church, San Antonio, TX.
  The guest Chaplain offered the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, we thank You for the privilege of prayer. We ask 
that You receive our praise and heed our requests.
  We praise You for the abundant blessings of health, healing, and 
hope. We thank You for the grace that saves us and Your sovereign hand 
that protects us.
  On this day, I thank You specifically for the service of our dear 
friend Senator John Cornyn. You have blessed him with integrity, 
wisdom, and the heart of a servant. As he enters a new chapter, we 
request Your favor to be upon him and Sandy.
  Let those who serve You in this chamber do exactly that--serve You as 
they serve this country. Fill them with humility, discernment, and 
strength.
  May all of our lives bring You honor.
  And may we be found faithful when You return to take us home.
  In the name of the One who saves, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
