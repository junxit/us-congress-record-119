# Economy (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 5183

```
                                Economy

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, today, the disastrous Trump economy 
delivered another one-two gut punch to the American people: Growth is 
down. Costs are up.
  The American people don't need government reports and economists to 
tell

[[Page S4355]]

them how sluggish Trump's economy is or how high costs are. Americans 
see it at the gas pump. They see it at the grocery store. They see it 
whenever they pay the rent or for heating or for electricity.
  Trump, on the other hand, has no clue just how deep in the dumps his 
economy is. He keeps calling higher costs a ``hoax'' and affordability 
a ``made-up word.''
  In the meantime, Trump has done nothing but make matters worse with 
his reckless and irresponsible war, with his chaotic tariffs, and with 
his crusade against American energy and assault on healthcare.
  And every day, while Trump's failed Presidency makes life harder for 
American families, where does he spend his time and energy? Enriching 
himself.
  Democrats aren't going to let this one-two economic punch turn into a 
knockout blow. We are going to keep fighting to bring down Americans' 
costs and bring an end to Trump's failed agenda that is driving those 
costs up and up and up.
  (The remarks of Mr. Schumer pertaining to the introduction of S. 5183 
are printed in today's Record under ``Statements on Introduced Bills 
and Joint Resolutions.'')
```
