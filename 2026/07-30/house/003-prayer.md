# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Who is a God like You? You before whom mountains melt like wax before 
a fire and valleys split apart like water rushing down a slope. Who is 
a God like You who chooses to gather the people of Your own creation 
like sheep in a pen, a flock in its pasture, providing Your shelter and 
protection? Who is a God like You who pardons our sins and forgives our 
transgressions? Who is a God like You? You do not stay angry forever, 
but You delight to show us mercy.
  There is no God like You.
  In humility and gratitude, we offer ourselves and our work unto You 
that You, who are like no other, will be glorified.
  We pray these prayers in the name that is above all other names.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
