# HONORING MISS ADDISON HUMPHREYS FOR DISTINGUISHED ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENT AND LEADERSHIP

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
HONORING MISS ADDISON HUMPHREYS FOR DISTINGUISHED ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENT 
                             AND LEADERSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Miss Addison Humphreys of McAllen, Texas, a member of the McAllen High 
School Class of 2027, whose academic excellence, leadership, and 
service reflect the very best of South Texas.
  Miss Humphreys ranks among the top four percent of her graduating 
class, which has more than 470 students, and has pursued a rigorous 
course of study in preparation for a future in medicine. As State 
President of Texas HOSA--Future Health Professionals, she represents 
more than 51,000 students across 1,570 chapters and is the first 
student in McAllen Independent School District and Hidalgo County 
history to hold both area and state HOSA office.
  Her commitment to service extends far beyond the classroom. She 
founded Books2Border, a youth-led literacy initiative that has 
distributed more than 5,500 books and 20 laptops, established five 
community libraries, rebuilt school libraries in Reynosa, Tamaulipas, 
Mexico, and expanded educational opportunities for more than 2,500 
students in Mexico.
  Overall, Miss Humphreys has also completed more than 1,300 documented 
hours of community service. Through DHR Health's LASER program, she is 
researching childhood obesity and Type II diabetes risk in the Rio 
Grande Valley, has been selected to present her work internationally, 
and has helped educate more than 2,500 students about stroke 
recognition.
  South Texas is home to countless young people with big dreams and a 
desire to make a difference. Miss Humphreys is a fine example of that 
promise, reflecting the hard work, compassion, and spirit of service 
that define our communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Miss Addison Humphreys for her 
distinguished academic achievement, leadership, and service, and I wish 
her continued success in the years ahead. She has a bright future ahead 
of her and has made South Texas proud.
```
