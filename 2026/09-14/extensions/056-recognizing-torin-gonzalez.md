# RECOGNIZING TORIN GONZALEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING TORIN GONZALEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Torin Gonzalez 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Torin has overcome many challenges along their journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Torin, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Torin's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Jefferson Jr/Sr High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Torin Gonzalez on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
