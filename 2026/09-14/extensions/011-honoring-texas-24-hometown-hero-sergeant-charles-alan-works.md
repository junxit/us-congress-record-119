# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO SERGEANT CHARLES "ALAN" WORKS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO SERGEANT CHARLES ``ALAN'' WORKS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Sergeant Charles ``Alan'' Works. He dedicated his life to public 
service, beginning his law enforcement career in 2007 with the Denton 
County Sheriff's Office. He later served with the Tarrant County 
College Police Department from 2013 to 2020, continuing his commitment 
to protect and serve. He joined the DFW Airport Police Department in 
2020, and was promoted to sergeant in 2022.

[[Page E902]]

  Sergeant Works was known by colleagues and travelers alike for his 
cowboy hat and the constant smile on his face. We are grateful for his 
years of service to Denton, Tarrant County, and DFW. We will remember 
Sergeant Works for the lasting impact he made through his dedication 
for serving our North Texas Community.

                          ____________________
```
