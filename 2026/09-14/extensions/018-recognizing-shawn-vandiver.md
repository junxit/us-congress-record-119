# RECOGNIZING SHAWN VANDIVER

**Speaking:** Peters, Scott H. (P000608) [D-CA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING SHAWN VANDIVER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SCOTT H. PETERS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. PETERS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today along with my colleagues, 
Representative Vargas, Representative Levin, and Representative Jacobs, 
to recognize Shawn VanDiver, an exemplary San Diegan for his unwavering 
service on behalf of Afghan allies who stood with our servicemembers 
through two decades of war.
  Shawn VanDiver is the founder and president of AfghanEvac. Shawn 
built this grassroots organization into a nationwide coalition 
dedicated to ensuring that our Nation keeps its promises.
  Since the Taliban took over Afghanistan in 2021, Shawn has brought 
together veterans, advocates, nonprofits, and government 
representatives to help our Afghan partners reach safety. His work has 
helped reunite families who are now rebuilding their lives in the 
United States.
  Shawn is a Navy veteran. This service gave him the clear view that 
our military is more capable because of its alliances, and when he re-
joined civilian life, he brought this insight to San Diego's civic 
consciousness.
  Shawn's commitment to fairness and trustworthiness exemplifies our 
Nation's foundational ideals. The inspiration for his non-stop energy 
and advocacy is his commitment to our allies: when people risk their 
lives in support of our country and servicemembers, our commitment to 
them does not end when that war does.
  As we continue to commemorate the 25th anniversary of the September 
11, 2001, attacks, which kicked off our involvement in Afghanistan, and 
the fifth anniversary of our withdrawal, every single one of us in this 
chamber should remember the countless Afghans who risked their lives to 
support our servicemembers. Our commitment to their safety is not only 
the right thing to do, but also the story we tell the world about 
ourselves. America, at its core, is a Nation of decent people that 
looks out for its allies. I thank my friend Shawn for always reminding 
us of this basic fact and I thank him for refusing to give up.

                          ____________________
```
