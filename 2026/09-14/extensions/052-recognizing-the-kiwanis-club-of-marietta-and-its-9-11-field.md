# RECOGNIZING THE KIWANIS CLUB OF MARIETTA AND ITS 9/11 FIELD OF FLAGS MEMORIAL AT KENNESAW MOUNTAIN NATIONAL BATTLEFIELD PARK

**Speaking:** Loudermilk, Barry (L000583) [R-GA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE KIWANIS CLUB OF MARIETTA AND ITS 9/11 FIELD OF FLAGS 
        MEMORIAL AT KENNESAW MOUNTAIN NATIONAL BATTLEFIELD PARK

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BARRY LOUDERMILK

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. LOUDERMILK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Kiwanis 
Club of Marietta and its 9/11 Field of Flags Memorial at Kennesaw 
Mountain National BattlefIeld Park, as our Nation marks the 25th 
anniversary of the terrorist attacks of September 11, 2001.
  First organized by the Kiwanis Club of Marietta in 2002, the Field of 
Flags has become a solemn tradition of remembrance in our community. 
This year marks the sixth installation of the memorial, with 2,977 
American flags placed on the grounds of Kennesaw Mountain National 
Battlefield Park, each representing an individual life lost on 
September 11, 2001.
  The memorial is made possible through the efforts of members of the 
community who gather to carry and place the flags. On September 11, 
2026, the Kiwanis Club will hold its 25th Anniversary Memorial Service, 
during which the names of all 2,977 victims will be read aloud. The 
flags will remain on display through September 19 as a continuing 
tribute to those whose lives were taken.
  For more than two decades, the Field of Flags has brought the 
Marietta community together to reflect upon the values of service, 
sacrifice, and unity. This tradition seeks to ensure that younger 
generations understand the significance of September 11, 2001 and 
remember the men, women, and children who were lost, as well as the 
courage demonstrated by first responders, servicemembers, and private 
citizens throughout our Nation.
  Chartered in 1930, the Kiwanis Club of Marietta has a longstanding 
tradition of service to children, families, and the broader community. 
I commend its members, volunteers, community partners, and all those 
whose continued support has sustained the Field of Flags and preserved 
this important tradition of remembrance.
  In further recognition of this solemn anniversary, an American flag 
was flown over the United States Capitol on September 11, 2026, at my 
request, in honor of the 2,977 lives lost on September 11, 2001, and in 
recognition of the Kiwanis Club of Marietta's 9/11 Field of Flags 
Memorial at Kennesaw Mountain National BattlefIeld Park.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the memory of 
the 2,977 lives lost on September 11, 2001, and in recognizing the 
Kiwanis Club of Marietta for its enduring commitment to ensuring that 
our community and our Nation never forget.
```
