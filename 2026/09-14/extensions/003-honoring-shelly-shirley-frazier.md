# HONORING SHELLY "SHIRLEY" FRAZIER

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                  HONORING SHELLY ``SHIRLEY'' FRAZIER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
remarkable public servant, Shelly ``Shirley'' Frazier.
  Shelly Frazier is a 1973 graduate of North Natchez High School. She 
continued her education at Meadow-Draughon Business College in New 
Orleans, Louisiana, where she earned a degree in business 
administration in 1974.
  Shelly began her career with AT&T, where she worked for 32 years 
before retiring in 2010. During her career, her work took her to 
several locations, including Jackson, Mississippi; Pensacola, Florida; 
New Orleans, Louisiana; and Atlanta, Georgia. Her career reflected a 
strong work ethic, professionalism, and commitment to excellence.
  Her commitment to service continued well beyond her professional 
career. Mrs. Frazier is currently serving her third term as the 
District 4 Election Commissioner for Adams County, where she continues 
to serve her community through the administration of local elections. 
She is also a lifetime member of the NAACP and has faithfully served as 
secretary for more than 15 years.
  Shelly Frazier is married to Billie Joe Frazier, and together they 
have three daughters, Jarita Frazier-King, Courtney Frazier, and Skylar 
King-Frazier, and one son, Keimon B. Frazier. They also cherish four 
grandchildren and one great-granddaughter, affectionately known as 
their ``chocolate drop.''
  Mrs. Frazier has also played an important role in preserving her 
family's history, culture, and culinary traditions. Alongside her 
mother and daughters, she helped launch the Natchez Heritage School of 
Cooking, a family business dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich 
foodways and cultural heritage of Natchez and the surrounding region.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Shelly 
``Shirley'' Frazier of Natchez, Mississippi, for her achievements and 
dedicated service to the Natchez-Adams County community.
```
