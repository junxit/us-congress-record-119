# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MINNESOTA REPRESENTATIVE KAREN CLARK

**Speaking:** Omar, Ilhan (O000173) [D-MN]

```
  HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MINNESOTA REPRESENTATIVE KAREN CLARK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ILHAN OMAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the incredible life 
and legacy of Minnesota Representative Karen Clark.
  Ms. Clark was first elected to the Minnesota House of Representatives 
in 1980, serving 19 consecutive terms until her retirement in 2018. In 
addition to her 38 years of public service in the House, Ms. Clark also 
served the Minnesota community as a nurse and an educator. She later 
co-founded the Women's Environmental Institute with her wife of 35 
years, Jacquelyn Zita, which is an organization dedicated to fighting 
for environmental justice through education, advocacy, research, and 
more.
  Karen Clark was the first openly LGBTQ+ candidate to run for and win 
a seat in the Minnesota legislature, and she was the longest serving 
openly lesbian member to serve in a State legislature in the United 
States. She chaired numerous subcommittees regarding economic justice 
and development, empowering the constituents she represented in the 
lowest income district in the state. Ms. Clark also served on Governor 
Rudy Perpich's Task Force on Lesbian and Gay Minnesotans in 1990, later 
playing a crucial role in passing an amendment to the Minnesota Human 
Rights Act that protected LGBTQ+ Minnesotans from discrimination in 
housing, employment, and education, cementing Minnesota as the first 
state in the Nation to include protections based on gender identity. 
Two decades later, Clark led the bipartisan effort to legalize same-sex 
marriage in Minnesota, a landmark accomplishment for LGBTQ+ rights in 
the state. In that same year, former President Barack Obama honored 
Clark as a Harvey Milk Champion of Change.
  Karen Clark was also known for her steadfast dedication to 
championing environmental justice and socioeconomic justice, especially 
for lower income residents, Indigenous Americans, immigrants, and 
communities of color. Even after her retirement from public office, she 
was regularly spearheading fights for progress at the Capitol and in 
her community.
  Karen Clark was an advocate for all Minnesotans, and she was deeply 
beloved by all who knew her. To say that Clark was a trailblazer is an 
understatement--Minnesota would not be the state it is today without 
her tremendous service. I proudly represent the same Minneapolis 
neighborhoods she served, and I am honored to carry on the legacy she 
built to uplift these communities. Her impact will be celebrated for 
generations to come, as her passing serves as a reminder to us all that 
every one of us can change the world by caring for our community. 
Please join me in honoring Karen Clark and her monumental legacy.
```
