# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO SOUTHLAKE CARROLL DRAGONS VOLLEYBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO SOUTHLAKE CARROLL DRAGONS VOLLEYBALL 
                                  TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Southlake Carroll Dragons Volleyball team, as they made history, 
claiming their first-ever Class 6A Division II State Championship in a 
five-set victory over Austin High School. This was their first finals 
appearance in 27 years and this time, they secured the long awaited 
title!
  Congratulations to the Dragons and their head coach, Leslie Jackson, 
for an unforgettable season and for bringing home a historic state 
championship to Southlake.

                          ____________________
```
