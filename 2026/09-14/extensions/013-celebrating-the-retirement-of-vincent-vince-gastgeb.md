# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF VINCENT "VINCE" GASTGEB

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
        CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF VINCENT ``VINCE'' GASTGEB

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
celebrate the retirement of Mr. Vincent ``Vince'' Gastgeb, a dedicated 
public servant and friend to Western Pennsylvania.
  Vince's career has spanned both the public and private sectors, with 
a lasting commitment to our Commonwealth. Following Allegheny County's 
adoption of its Home Rule Charter in 1999, Vince was elected to 
represent District 5 on the County's first-ever Council. He was 
reelected to four consecutive terms and served on the County's Budget 
and Finance Committee. In 2012 and again in 2022, Vince was appointed 
to the Pennsylvania Banking and Securities Commission, where he helped 
advance financial literacy and education across Pennsylvania.
  Most recently, Vince served as the Chief Corporate and Government 
Affairs Officer for the Allegheny County Airport Authority, overseeing 
legislative and government affairs, grant funding, and corporate and 
airline sales. During his tenure, the Authority secured air service to 
three transatlantic destinations, funded its new Cargo 4 building, 
broke ground on two buildings at the Neighborhood 91 additive 
manufacturing campus, and strengthened partnerships across the region.
  Vince was a vital part of building the new terminal at Pittsburgh 
International Airport, which opened in November 2025. This was the 
culmination of more than a decade of work to reimagine aviation in 
Western Pennsylvania.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Vince 
Gastgeb on his retirement and thanking him for a career dedicated to 
serving Western Pennsylvania.
```
