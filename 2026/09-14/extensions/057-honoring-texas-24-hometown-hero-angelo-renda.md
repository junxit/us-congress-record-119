# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ANGELO RENDA

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ANGELO RENDA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Angelo Renda. He is a three-year letterman and one of the most 
accomplished quarterbacks in Southlake Carroll history. He guided the 
Dragons to back-to-back District 4-6A championships and has a 
remarkable 28-1 record. His junior year he helped lead Southlake 
Carroll to a 6A Division II UIL Texas State runner-up finish.
  Angelo is the all-time passing yards leader in Carroll Dragons 
history, finishing his high school career with 524 completions for 
8,197 yards and 93 touchdowns.
  We can't wait to watch his journey continue at the University of 
Pittsburgh, and to see him succeed with Panthers like he has with the 
Dragons.

                          ____________________
```
