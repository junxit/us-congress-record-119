# HONORING NEW GIDEON BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING NEW GIDEON BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge New Gideon 
Baptist Church of Baton Rouge, Louisiana, founded on December 24, 1886, 
under an oak tree at Perkins Road and Pickett Street. A congregation of 
eight believers assembled under the leadership of Reverend Ronald 
Fields, who faithfully pastored from 1886 until 1907. The early decades 
of the church saw steady growth, including an increase in membership 
under Reverend John J. Johnson, who served from 1908 to 1923. From 1924 
to 1948, Reverend Fred C. Matthews, Sr. led the congregation, during 
which time church property was sold and services were temporarily held 
at a Methodist Church on Valley Street. In 1946, property on Balis 
Drive was purchased, and by 1947 groundbreaking for a new structure 
commenced. Although construction initially halted, New Gideon advanced 
steadfastly through faith and perseverance.
  In 1949, Reverend Oscar Livous became pastor, completing the new 
church facility and serving until 1959. Reverend John David Lands then 
led the church for 33 years, guiding major expansions including the 
enlargement and remodeling of the second building--construction 
completed in March 1965. Under Reverend Lands's leadership, the 
congregation continued to grow, reaching a capacity that necessitated a 
larger facility. In 1983, adjacent property was purchased and 
groundbreaking for a new sanctuary was held. In 1984, the current New 
Gideon Baptist Church building was completed. Reverend Lands 
transitioned to be with the Lord on August 1, 1992, marking the end of 
a profoundly impactful era.
  Reverend Joseph J. Mitchell was elected pastor in April 1993 and 
served faithfully until September 29, 1999. In 2000, Reverend Thomas N. 
Bessix began his tenure, leading the church until May 2015. Under 
Pastor Bessix's leadership, New Gideon experienced tremendous growth in 
membership and ministry, establishing comprehensive Christian Education 
initiatives including Noon Day Bible Study, Fulfillment Hour, New 
Members Orientation, and annual leadership conferences. A major 
milestone came in 2006 with the groundbreaking of the Education Life 
Center (ELC) during the 120th Church Anniversary. The ELC dedication 
weekend took place May 9, 2008, to May 11, 2008. Pastor Bessix also 
championed modern evangelism, establishing New Gideon's presence across 
the worldwide web through its website, Facebook, Twitter, and 
livestream broadcasting to expand its reach in sharing the Gospel.
  Mr. Speaker, God blessed New Gideon Baptist Church on February 7, 
2016, with the election of its eighth pastor, Reverend Brandon D. 
Collins, whose preaching and leadership transformed lives until his 
departure in 2025. On October 9, 2025, Reverend Thomas N. Bessix was 
once again elected Senior Pastor and installed on November 23, 2025. 
His empowered leadership marks another milestone in the legacy of New 
Gideon Baptist Church, continuing its mission to uplift the community, 
expand ministry, and faithfully proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ. I 
ask my colleagues to join me in commemorating New Gideon Baptist Church 
for more than a century of spiritual devotion, perseverance, and 
service to God's people.
```
