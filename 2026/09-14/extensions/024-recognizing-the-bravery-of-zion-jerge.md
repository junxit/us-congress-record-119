# RECOGNIZING THE BRAVERY OF ZION JERGE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                 RECOGNIZING THE BRAVERY OF ZION JERGE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize Zion Jerge 
for his heroic acts of bravery. When Zion was faced with a life-
threatening situation, he acted quickly and decisively to courageously 
rescue a father and son from drowning, saving their lives. He 
demonstrates the strength and care that should inspire us all to act 
with care for our neighbors and community.
  As a Cadet at the Cal Poly Maritime Academy, Zion exemplifies 
qualities of perseverance, leadership, and readiness, which align with 
the Academy's values, and which are needed for success in the Maritime 
industry. I am confident that Zion will carry these qualities forward 
as he continues his journey and distinguished career in the Maritime 
industry.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
please accept my sincere gratitude for his brave and honorable service 
as well as my best wishes for the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
