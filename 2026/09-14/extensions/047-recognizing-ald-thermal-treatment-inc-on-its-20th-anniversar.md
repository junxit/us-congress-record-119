# RECOGNIZING ALD THERMAL TREATMENT, INC. ON ITS 20TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
    RECOGNIZING ALD THERMAL TREATMENT, INC. ON ITS 20TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor ALD 
Thermal Treatment, Inc. of Port Huron, Michigan, as it celebrates 20 
years of excellence in manufacturing and heat treatment services.
  For two decades, ALD Thermal Treatment has been an important part of 
Port Huron's manufacturing community. Since opening its doors, its Port 
Huron facility has processed more than 500 million parts, with not a 
single one returned from the field--a remarkable record and a testament 
to its quality and precision.
  Its standard of excellence has earned the General Motors Supplier 
Quality Excellence Award in 11 of the past 12 years, including becoming 
the first heat treatment services company to receive the award in 2014.
  ALD Thermal Treatment also invests in the next generation of Michigan 
manufacturers by hosting Manufacturing Day events for high school 
students and offering internships to students studying materials 
science and metallurgy. Several employees have been with the company 
since it opened 20 years ago, reflecting the strong workplace culture 
that has helped make the company successful.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating ALD 
Thermal Treatment, Inc. on its 20th anniversary. I am proud to 
recognize its commitment to quality, safety, and Michigan 
manufacturing, and I wish them continued success for many years to 
come.
```
