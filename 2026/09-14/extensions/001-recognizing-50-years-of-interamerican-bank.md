# RECOGNIZING 50 YEARS OF INTERAMERICAN BANK

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
               RECOGNIZING 50 YEARS OF INTERAMERICAN BANK

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Interamerican 
Bank as it celebrates 50 years of service to South Florida. Founded in 
Miami-Dade County on September 10, 1976, by Agustin C. Velasco, 
Interamerican Bank represents a remarkable story of faith, family, 
perseverance, and the American Dream.
  Mr. Velasco fled the communist Castro regime in 1961 with his wife, 
Nieves, and their two young children, Felisa and Agustin, Jr. Like so 
many Cuban exiles, he arrived in Miami determined to rebuild his life 
in freedom. He worked in factories, started his own business, and just 
15 years after arriving in the United States, founded Interamerican 
Bank.
  For five decades, Interamerican Bank has remained a family-owned 
community bank dedicated to serving the people and businesses of South 
Florida. Today, the Bank operates five branches, including its Hialeah 
branch, which proudly celebrates its 30th anniversary this year.
  The legacy of Agustin C. Velasco, Sr. continues through three 
generations of the Velasco family. His son, Agustin Velasco, Jr., 
serves as President and Chief Executive Officer, while his son-in-law, 
Ibrahim Pacheco, has provided nearly three decades of leadership as 
Chairman of the Board. His granddaughters, Isis Pacheco Velasco, 
Christie Velasco, and Lindsie Velasco, are also helping carry this 
proud family tradition into the future.
  Interamerican Bank is more than a South Florida institution, its 
story embodies the promise of our great Nation: that those who flee 
tyranny for freedom can work hard, build something lasting, and create 
opportunities for generations to come. I congratulate Interamerican 
Bank, the Velasco family, and its entire team on 50 years of service 
and wishing them continued success for many years to come.
```
