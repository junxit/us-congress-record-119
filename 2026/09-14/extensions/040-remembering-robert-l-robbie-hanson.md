# REMEMBERING ROBERT L. "ROBBIE" HANSON

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
                REMEMBERING ROBERT L. ``ROBBIE'' HANSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. HARRIS of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
life and service of Robert L. ``Robbie'' Hanson, who passed away on 
September 6, 2026.
  Robbie Hanson was a member of the Cambridge, Maryland community for 
many decades and embodied public service throughout his career. Upon 
graduating with the North Dorchester Class of 1971, he enlisted in the 
United States Air Force Security Forces where he served in South-East 
Asia during the Vietnam War. Robbie continued his Air Force career 
serving in the Air Force Reserves at Dover Air Force Base in the 512th 
Weapons Systems Security Forces.
  Robbie transitioned into a distinguished career with Maryland Parole 
and Probation, spanning 34 years. He also served as The City 
Commissioner for the Fifth Ward in Cambridge, Chair of the Dorchester 
County Planning and Zoning Commission, and served on multiple terms in 
the Maryland Veterans Commission. Robbie was co-founder of the 
Dorchester County Public Safety and Treatment Network and a member of 
the Dorchester County Local Management Board for Child and Family 
Services. A proud veteran, Robbie was a member of the American Legion 
Post 91, a life member of the VFW Post 7460 and a life member of the 
Vietnam Veterans Chapter 0648.
  His dedication to serving others has left a legacy in Cambridge. 
Robbie was a true patriot, and Maryland's First Congressional District 
undoubtedly owes him a great debt of gratitude. I thank Robbie for his 
contributions and selfless service. May the memories of Robbie bring 
comfort and peace to his family, loved ones and others.

                          ____________________
```
