# HONORING ELM GROVE BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING ELM GROVE BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a cherished 
congregation in my district, Elm Grove Baptist Church in Baton Rouge, 
Louisiana, as it celebrates a century of worship, Christian education, 
and service to the Eden Park community.
  Elm Grove Baptist Church was founded around June 1925 in the home of 
George and Carrie Walks on North 38th Street, then known as Orange 
Street, in the Eden Park community of Baton Rouge. Since its founding, 
the church has been committed to worship, Christian education, 
community service, and outreach.
  The church experienced significant growth under the leadership of 
Reverend J.R. Williams, who served as pastor from 1952 to 2000. During 
his tenure, Elm Grove established membership training programs, 
stewardship revivals, mission schools, interdenominational and 
interracial services, a child development center, and missions work in 
Liberia, West Africa. The congregation also relocated to its present 
site and constructed a new sanctuary and education building.
  Following Reverend Williams's retirement and passing in 2000, 
Reverend Errol K. Domingue became the church's seventh pastor. Today, 
Elm Grove Baptist Church continues its longstanding commitment to 
worship, Bible study, Christian education, ministry, and service to the 
Eden Park and Baton Rouge communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Elm Grove 
Baptist Church for a century of faithful service, and in wishing its 
pastor and congregation continued blessings in the years to come.
```
