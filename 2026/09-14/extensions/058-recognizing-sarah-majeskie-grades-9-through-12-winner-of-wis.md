# RECOGNIZING SARAH MAJESKIE, GRADES 9 THROUGH 12 WINNER OF WISCONSIN'S 6TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT 2026 STUDENT ESSAY CONTEST

**Speaking:** Grothman, Glenn (G000576) [R-WI]

```

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GLENN GROTHMAN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. GROTHMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sarah Majeskie, 
the winner of the ninth through twelfth grade division of my 2026 
Student Essay Contest, and to commend this outstanding young person 
from Wisconsin's 6th Congressional District for her thoughtful 
reflections on our country and system of government.
  Sarah, a tenth-grade student at Berlin High School, was selected as 
the winner for her essay reflecting on the importance of America's 
constitutional republic, representative government, individual rights, 
and the freedoms protected by our Constitution.
  I am proud to recognize Sarah for the time, effort, and thought she 
put into her essay. Her work demonstrates an appreciation for the 
responsibilities of citizenship and the principles that have helped 
make our nation strong. I congratulate her on this achievement and am 
pleased to include in the Record her winning essay:


             Sarah Majeskie--Berlin High School, 10th Grade

       If you ask Americans what type of government we have, many 
     will say we live in a democracy. While it is true our 
     government uses democratic principles such as voting for our 
     leaders, we do not operate under a democracy. Instead, the 
     United States government is a Constitutional Republic, a form 
     of government that I along with many other Anrericans value 
     and believe is the best way to provide our nation with a 
     strong future.
       Before we as American citizens can value our Constitutional 
     Republic and give it the respect it deserves, we must first 
     understand it. Although people may appreciate something 
     without fully comprehending it, to hold it to a high value 
     they must understand what it is and what it means to them.
       A Constitutional Republic is a form of government where 
     citizens elect representatives to make decisions on their 
     behalf, governed by a constitution that sets forth 
     fundamental principles and laws of the state and limits 
     government power. Unlike a monarchy, where leaders inherit 
     power, a dictatorship where citizens live in fear because 
     power is in the hands of a select few, or authoritarianism 
     where freedoms are stripped from people, our constitution 
     protects our individual rights which sets us apart.
       For any governing leader to be elected, the Constitutional 
     Republic directs that any eligible citizen has the ability to 
     vote, regardless of race, gender, or financial background. 
     This ensures that the government must follow the Constitution 
     while allowing citizens to choose those who represent them. 
     Our Constitutional Republic differs from pure democratic 
     systems that operate solely on majority rule. Our government 
     gives those in smaller rural communities as strong a voice as 
     those in bigger urban communities.
       In our Constitutional Republic nobody agrees 100% with each 
     other. However because of the way our country is run, we can 
     all vote for who we believe will uphold what is right. We can 
     choose who will represent everyone's individual voice. We do 
     not use pure democracy which follows majority rule, rather we 
     follow the constitution which protects our individual rights 
     as voters.
       I value the opportunities being a citizen of the United 
     States provides. We have the chance for new beginnings, new 
     opportunities, and a new way of living. As Americans, we can 
     express our opinions, choose who we want to support, and vote 
     for our future. Whether it is voting for the new alderperson 
     in a tiny town of 2,000, or casting a ballot for the 
     Commander in Chief itself, we are given the opportunity to 
     choose who we think best represents the American people in 
     regions across the nation instead of the first born child of 
     a king, or whoever possesses the most brute force.
       Another thing I value is that as a woman, I will be able to 
     vote when I am of voting age. There are no hoops for women to 
     jump through and I value that every person no matter what has 
     the right to vote, and our vote really matters. As I vote for 
     one candidate, someone else votes for another, but no matter 
     who is elected, our constitution ensures the power is divided 
     across the country differently so that power remains in the 
     hands of the people.
       One thing I value the most under our constitution is our 
     religious liberties. The ability to practice my religion 
     including choosing my way to worship, pray, and share my 
     beliefs is a right I value because I know many others do not 
     have this privilege. It is my hope for our future that no 
     matter who runs our country, our schools, or even our 
     hospitals, that everyone may be free to practice any religion 
     they choose, supported by the constitution.
       As America continues to be a melting pot of cultures and 
     backgrounds, I hope that our Constitutional Republic can 
     continue to provide fair representation and equal protection 
     under law to all citizens. Having a diverse country where 
     everyone has the right to life, liberty, and the pursuit of 
     happiness is my ultimate wish for our future.
```
