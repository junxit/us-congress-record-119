# HONORING UNITED STATES ARMY SPECIALIST JAMES MICHAEL YOHN, SR.

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
     HONORING UNITED STATES ARMY SPECIALIST JAMES MICHAEL YOHN, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend and honor the life of 
United States Army Specialist James Michael Yohn, Sr., who was killed 
in action (KIA) while bravely serving our Nation with the 1st Squadron, 
3rd Armored Cavalry Regiment in Mosul, Iraq.
  Specialist Yohn dedicated his life to selfless service--both in and 
out of uniform. He was an athlete from a very young age, played 
baseball and basketball at a high level, and enjoyed fishing--saying 
that it was a calming way for him to stop and reflect in peace near the 
tranquility of the water.
  Before enlisting in the United States Army, Specialist Yohn served 
with the Highspire Volunteer Fire Department for eight years. While 
serving with distinction for the benefit and safety of his community, 
he also loved being on the fire engine and making people laugh while 
performing his duties.
  After the terror attacks on September 11, 2001, Specialist Yohn told 
his mother that he wanted to do something to protect our Nation from 
present and future barbaric acts of terrorism. Five years later, he 
enlisted in the Army and attended Basic Training at Fort

[[Page E913]]

Knox, Kentucky. After graduating, he was stationed at Fort Hood, Texas, 
where he met his future wife, Amber.
  Shortly after deploying to Iraq in October 2007, he and Amber 
discovered that they were expecting their first child. He returned home 
for 30 days to make plans for their future and redeployed to Iraq in 
February 2008.
  On June 25, 2008, Specialist Yohn and two other Troopers of the 3rd 
Armored Cavalry Regiment's 1st Squadron were headed back to a combat 
outpost following a route-protection mission. Their vehicle was the 
convoy lead that provided security for the rest of the platoon when 
they struck a roadside bomb that tragically killed all three 
Cavalrymen.
  Specialist Yohn was 25 years-old when terrorists cowardly took his 
life. He was so excited about becoming a father. He was a devoted 
husband and family man who had a way of making others smile without 
even trying. He was an excellent Trooper who took pride in his work and 
loved the life of spirit of not only being a Soldier but being around 
other Soldiers. This year, 2026, marks 18 years since Specialist Yohn 
was killed in action (KIA). He was the first Fallen in the Global War 
on Terror to hail from Dauphin County, and his memory endures through 
his loving wife, Amber, and the son who proudly carries his namesake--
James Michael Yohn, Jr.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored and privileged to recognize Specialist 
James Yohn, who not only selflessly and eagerly served our Nation with 
distinction in the United States Army, but defended those who may never 
even know him, that they might live free and peacefully because of his 
sacrifices. His tireless dedication, loyalty, patriotism, 
professionalism, and devotion are a great credit to himself, our 
communities, the United States Army, and the United States of America, 
and set the standard by which all should be judged. May he Rest in 
God's Peace until we ``. . . meet again on the High Ground.''
```
