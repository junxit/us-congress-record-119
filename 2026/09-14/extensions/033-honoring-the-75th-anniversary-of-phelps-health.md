# HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF PHELPS HEALTH

```
             HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF PHELPS HEALTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JASON SMITH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. SMITH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 75th anniversary of 
Phelps Health --one of Southeast Missouri's oldest rural hospitals--and 
its commitment to serving the people of Missouri's 8th Congressional 
District.
  Phelps Health was opened in 1951 as Phelps County Memorial Hospital, 
serving over 30 patients in just the first three days. What started as 
a small, 63-bed facility now boasts cutting-edge technology, ever-
expanding services, and partnerships with facilities across the state.
  For the past 75 years, Phelps Health and its staff have shown their 
devotion to the people of Phelps County, acting as examples of 
innovation, compassion, and service. This dedication is shown through 
their mission to reimagine rural healthcare and create lasting 
solutions to consistent challenges.
  As Phelps Health celebrates this remarkable milestone, it looks to 
the next 75 years of prioritizing modernization and innovation. The 
staff and volunteers of this facility have shown time and time again 
how valuable a dedication to selfless service can be to a community. 
Their mission is simple: provide high-quality, accessible healthcare 
through empathy, hard work, and a devotion to helping folks in this 
region.
  It is an honor and a privilege to recognize Phelps Health on this 
historic anniversary, and I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating the staff, volunteers, and community members who have 
made this historic moment possible.
```
