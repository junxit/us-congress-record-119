# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO LIGHT THE WORLD GIVING MACHINE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
     HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO LIGHT THE WORLD GIVING MACHINE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Light the World Giving Machine, which returned to North Texas 
this past holiday season, turning generosity into action across our 
communities. These red vending machines allow donors to give meaningful 
items, like warm clothing, meals, medical supplies, and even livestock, 
to local and global charities serving those most in need.
  The Giving Machines ensure that 100 percent of every donation goes 
directly to charity, with all operating costs covered. In 2024, Giving 
Machines placed in three North Texas cities helped raise more than 
$600,000, proving once again that when North Texans come together, the 
impact is powerful and far-reaching.
```
