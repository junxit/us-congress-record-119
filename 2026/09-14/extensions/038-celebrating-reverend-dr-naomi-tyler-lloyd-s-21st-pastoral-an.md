# CELEBRATING REVEREND DR. NAOMI TYLER-LLOYD'S 21ST PASTORAL ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
 CELEBRATING REVEREND DR. NAOMI TYLER-LLOYD'S 21ST PASTORAL ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Reverend 
Dr. Naomi Tyler-Lloyd as she celebrates her 21st Pastoral Anniversary 
at Trinity Baptist Church in the Bronx.
  For more than two decades, Pastor Naomi has faithfully led her 
congregation with compassion, wisdom, and an unwavering commitment to 
serving both God and her community.

[[Page E909]]

Her journey to ministry reflects a lifelong dedication to education and 
service. Before answering God's call, she built a distinguished career 
as a counselor and educator, using her gifts to uplift others and 
strengthen families.
  Since becoming Pastor of Trinity Baptist Church in 2005, Reverend Dr. 
Tyler-Lloyd has guided her congregation with grace and vision while 
extending her ministry far beyond the walls of the church. Through her 
leadership in the Baptist World Alliance, she has helped strengthen 
communities of faith across the globe and has continued to teach and 
preach the Gospel both nationally and internationally.
  Today, we celebrate not only 21 years of faithful pastoral 
leadership, but a lifetime devoted to faith, service, and the 
empowerment of others. On behalf of New York's 15th Congressional 
District, I congratulate Reverend Dr. Naomi Tyler-Lloyd on this 
remarkable milestone and thank her for her enduring contributions to 
the Bronx and beyond. May God continue to bless her ministry for many 
years to come.

                          ____________________
```
