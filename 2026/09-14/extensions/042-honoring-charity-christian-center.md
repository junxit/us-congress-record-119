# HONORING CHARITY CHRISTIAN CENTER

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING CHARITY CHRISTIAN CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend a dedicated ministry 
in my district, Charity Christian Center in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, as 
it marks four decades of worship, outreach, and service to the people 
of East Baton Rouge Parish.
  Charity Christian Center was formed upon a vision that the Lord gave 
Pastor Don L. Bradford in 1985. After several months of home Bible 
study and a miraculous trip to a Kenneth Hagin Camp Meeting that same 
year, the Lord birthed Charity Christian Center into the pastor's 
spirit.
  On Sunday, December 17, 1992, Charity Christian Center held its first 
service in its new location on West McKinley Street. In August of the 
following year, Kingdom Kids Daycare Center was established at the new 
location, along with the Victorious Living Radio Ministry, a Clothing 
Distribution Center, and the Charity Christian Center Summer Day Camp.
  On October 22, 2010, under the appointment of the Holy Spirit and the 
advisement of their pastors and spiritual parents, Drs. Phillip and 
Brenda Goudeaux of Calvary Christian Center in Sacramento, California, 
Dr. Linda Bradford was officially appointed Pastor of Charity Christian 
Center, and Dr. Don Bradford was officially appointed Bishop and 
Overseer.
  Throughout the years, Charity Christian Center has continued to go, 
grow, and multiply. Though trials have come, its members have continued 
to press on, and in October 2011, Charity Christian Center was once 
again blessed to purchase a new church facility on O'Neal Lane, which 
includes an annex building currently housing a daycare and Don Bradford 
Bible College classrooms.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Charity 
Christian Center for four decades of faithful service, and in wishing 
its pastors and congregation God's continued favor in the years ahead.
```
