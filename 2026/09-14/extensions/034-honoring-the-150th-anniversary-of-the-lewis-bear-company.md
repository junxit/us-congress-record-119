# HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LEWIS BEAR COMPANY

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
        HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LEWIS BEAR COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a true Northwest 
Florida institution, The Lewis Bear Company, which is celebrating 150 
years of serving Northwest Florida.
  For generations, the Bear family has been part of the fabric of the 
Panhandle. In 1876, Lewis Bear, opened a small grocery and chandlery 
shop on South Palafox Street. What he started 150 years ago has grown 
into a family business that continues to serve Northwest Florida today. 
That kind of longevity does not happen by accident. It takes hard work 
and a commitment to the community you call home.
  The Lewis Bear Company has grown and changed with Northwest Florida, 
but it has never lost sight of its roots. Today, five generations 
later, the company employs hundreds of Floridians and serves thousands 
of businesses throughout the region.
  But the Bear family's legacy is about much more than business. They 
have consistently given their time, resources, and energy to 
organizations across our community, helping make Northwest Florida a 
better place.
  As someone who is proud to serve Northwest Florida, I know how much 
companies like Lewis Bear mean to our region. They are a reminder that 
when a family builds a business, they are not just building something 
for themselves, they are building something for the community and for 
the generations that follow.
  For 150 years, the Lewis Bear Company has been a part of the story of 
Northwest Florida. I congratulate the Bear family and everyone who has 
been part of this incredible legacy on this remarkable milestone. I 
look forward to seeing the Lewis Bear Company continue its tradition of 
serving our community for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
