# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO BARRETT KNETSCH

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO BARRETT KNETSCH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Barrett Knetsch, a Euless native, who has been giving back to our 
North Texas community since the age of four, when he first shaved his 
head to raise money for pediatric cancer charities. Now at sixteen, he 
has continued this tradition for twelve consecutive years and has 
raised more than $100,000 to support children battling cancer.
  Currently a student at St. Mark's School of Texas, Barrett 
participates in student council, choir, and varsity sports. Yet, he 
still finds time to rally his friends, family, and local businesses 
around this important cause. He also extends his commitment to service 
by volunteering at the Euless Community Center, helping at local 
Habitat for Humanity construction projects, and assisting at local food 
distribution centers.
  Barrett is setting a wonderful example for all of North Texas. His 
commitment shows how one person's determination can uplift an entire 
community.

                          ____________________
```
