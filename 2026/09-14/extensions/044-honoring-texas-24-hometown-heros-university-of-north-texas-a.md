# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS UNIVERSITY OF NORTH TEXAS AT DALLAS AND NORTH TEXAS FOOD BANK

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS UNIVERSITY OF NORTH TEXAS AT DALLAS 
                       AND NORTH TEXAS FOOD BANK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Heros, the

[[Page E910]]

University of North Texas at Dallas, in partnership with the North 
Texas Food Bank for their annual Thanksgiving distribution. This past 
year, they brought vital support to our community. Thanks to dedicated 
student volunteers, nearly 1,000 North Texas families received food to 
help them prepare for the holiday. Each family was provided a turkey, 
approximately 60 pounds of groceries, and thermal clothing for the 
colder months ahead.
  We are very grateful for the volunteers who serve our neighbors with 
compassion. North Texans continue to show up for one another, and 
events like this highlight the generosity of our community year after 
year.

                          ____________________
```
