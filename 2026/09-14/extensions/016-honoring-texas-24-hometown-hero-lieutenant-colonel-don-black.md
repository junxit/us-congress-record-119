# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO LIEUTENANT COLONEL DON BLACK MUNSON

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
  HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO LIEUTENANT COLONEL DON BLACK MUNSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Lieutenant Colonel Don Black Munson, as he lived a life dedicated 
to serving our country. He was commissioned as an officer upon 
graduating from Tulane University in 1960 as a Distinguished Military 
Graduate. Over the course of a 22-year Army career, he served in 
numerous leadership roles, including with the 82nd Airborne Division, 
Special Forces, and the 1st Cavalry Division. During the Vietnam War, 
he commanded C Company, 1st Battalion, 7th Cavalry, and later served as 
Operations Officer for the 2nd Battalion, 7th Cavalry.
  He earned the Silver Star for capturing three enemy combatants under 
fire and the Distinguished Flying Cross for heroism in extracting 
stranded soldiers under intense enemy attack. Lieutenant Colonel 
Munson's decorations also include two Bronze Stars for Valor, eleven 
Air Medals, five Meritorious Service Medals, and the Purple Heart, as 
well as the Master Combat Infantryman Badge, Master Parachutist Badge, 
and Pathfinder Badge.
  His dedication to service did not end with his military career. A 
longtime member and former commander of the Military Order of World 
Wars. We thank him for bravely serving our country and our community 
for many years.

                          ____________________
```
