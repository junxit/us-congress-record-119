# HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF PRAIRIE VIEW A&M UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Menefee, Christian D. (M001245) [D-TX]

```
     HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF PRAIRIE VIEW A&M UNIVERSITY

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. CHRISTIAN D. MENEFEE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. MENEFEE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Prairie View A&M 
University, on the occasion of its 150th anniversary celebration for 
its outstanding contributions to higher education.
  Prairie View A&M began as an Agricultural and Mechanical College for 
the ``colored youth'' with just 8 students. Today, it stands as Texas' 
second oldest public institution and a leading Carnegie-classified R2 
research university, enrolling more than 10,000 proud Productive 
Panthers.
  As an exemplary Historically Black College and University, Prairie 
View A&M is home to numerous distinguished research centers and 
institutes, including the National Center for Infrastructure Technology 
and the Texas Juvenile Crime Prevention Center. Throughout its history, 
the University has expanded access to higher education, particularly 
for underrepresented communities, by fostering an environment of 
growth, academic achievement, and innovation.
  Under the visionary leadership of President Dr. Tomikia P. LeGrande, 
Prairie View A&M has launched its ``Journey to Eminence'' plan, an 
ambitious effort to elevate the institution as a premier public 
historically Black university and a national model for student success, 
with an emphasis on research excellence, increased graduation rates, 
and strong post-graduate outcomes.
  Prairie View A&M's legacy stands as a testament to the power of 
access, opportunity, and academic excellence. Its impact will continue 
to shape generations of leaders for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas, I commend Prairie View 
A&M University on this remarkable milestone and extend my deepest 
gratitude for 150 years of service to students, to Texas, and to the 
Nation.
```
