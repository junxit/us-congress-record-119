# RECOGNIZING THE HARRISBURG HAVOC WOMEN'S TACKLE FOOTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE HARRISBURG HAVOC WOMEN'S TACKLE FOOTBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize members of the Harrisburg 
Havoc Women's Tackle Football Team for their participation in the 2026 
Women's Football Alliance (WFA) All-American Bowl.
  On July 25, 2026, the WPA hosted the All-American Bowl at the Tom 
Benson Hall of Fame Stadium in Canton, Ohio, where the best of the 
League's National Conference and American Conference faced off during 
the WFA ``Championship Weekend.'' Several members of the Havoc were 
selected to participate in this historic event to represent 
Pennsylvania on the National stage. Four Havoc players were selected to 
earn first-team honors: Leah Hutchinson, Kaeli Thomas, Ashley Robinson, 
and Elizabeth Smith (who is unable to participate due to her attendance 
at United States Army Basic Training). Earning second-team honors from 
the Havoc are Angela Deamer, Patience Waters, Samantha Moyer, Bri 
Swanson, Melissa Fawcett, and Millie Mosley.
  In addition to the players, Harrisburg Havoc Coach DeQuan Ware also 
was selected for the WFA All-Pro Team, where he worked alongside 
coaches from across the United States while proudly representing the 
Harrisburg Havoc and Pennsylvania in the All-American Bowl.
  The Havoc's selections to these prestigious positions showcase the 
dedication and commitment of these athletes on and off the field. These 
women have spent hours training, mentoring, competing, and serving as 
positive role models for their fellow teammates and other young women 
hoping to play at their level.
  Mr. Speaker, as the sport of women's tackle football continues to 
grow, I am proud that the 10th Congressional District is home to 
exceptional athletes who inspire others with their dedication, work 
ethic, and performance. I am honored to recognize the Harrisburg Havoc 
Football Team for their valiant efforts and performance in the All-
American Bowl and look forward to their future successes.

                          ____________________
```
