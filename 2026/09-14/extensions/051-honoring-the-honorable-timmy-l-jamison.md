# HONORING THE HONORABLE TIMMY L. JAMISON

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                HONORING THE HONORABLE TIMMY L. JAMISON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Alderman Timmy L. Jamison of Marks, Mississippi, a devoted husband, 
father, grandfather, businessman, and longtime public servant whose 
life of hard work and compassion has lifted his community.
  Timmy Jamison was born to James and Annie Jamison of Marks, 
Mississippi. From the age of six, he learned the value of work 
alongside his father--plowing gardens, driving tractors, and helping 
neighbors keep their plots and farms productive. Those early years 
shaped a lifelong ethic of service, responsibility, and care for 
others.
  Timmy graduated from Quitman County High School in 1990. He married 
Sandra Jamison, and together they raised five children and now cherish 
seven grandschildren. A small-business owner, Timmy, has been the owner 
and operator of his trucking company since 2015, providing steady work 
and dependable service throughout the region.
  For decades Timmy has stepped forward to serve his neighbors in 
elected office. He has served as alderman at-large since 1997--apart 
from one term--and continues in this role today. Whether handling 
routine municipal matters or helping a neighbor in crisis, Timmy has 
always put people first.
  Timmy's life is defined by compassion in action. He is known across 
Marks for listening, for rolling up his sleeves, and for treating 
everyone with diginity. Neighbors turn to him for practical help, wise 
counsel, and the steady presence of a servant-leader who believes 
public service is about improving the lives of others.

[[Page E912]]

  Mr. Speaker, Alderman Timmy Jamison's dedication to family, 
enterprise, and community exemplifies the best of small-town America. I 
ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing him for his decades of 
faithful service to Marks, Mississipi, and for the compassion and 
perservance he brings to every task.
```
