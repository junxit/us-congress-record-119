# RECOGNIZING THE IMPORTANCE OF ARKANSAS AND NATIONAL TRUCK DRIVERS

**Speaking:** Crawford, Eric A. "Rick" (C001087) [R-AR]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE IMPORTANCE OF ARKANSAS AND NATIONAL TRUCK DRIVERS

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ERIC A. ``RICK'' CRAWFORD

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. CRAWFORD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the millions of 
professional truck drivers who serve the American public each and every 
day.
  Truck drivers transport billions of dollars' worth of goods across 
our country, delivering essential products that Americans rely on every 
day. These men and women are the backbone of our Nation's economy. 
Without their dedication and tireless work, our communities would not 
have access to the goods and services they depend on.
  These drivers have committed their careers to serving others and 
ensuring that our Nation's supply chain continues to move. However, 
that responsibility does not come without its fair share of challenges. 
Truck drivers face long hours on the road, time away from their 
families, demanding work conditions, and the constant responsibility of 
safely delivering the goods our Nation depends on.
  These challenges are felt by truckers across our country, including 
the thousands of hardworking men and women who call Arkansas home. I 
want to applaud the associations in my state and across our Nation that 
represent the needs of truck drivers. Associations such as the Arkansas 
Trucking Association advocate every day on behalf of these men and 
women and the trucking companies that employ them. Their work has 
helped make Arkansas a national hub for trucking.
  I also want to acknowledge the thousands of trucking companies and 
their drivers based in Arkansas for the hard work they do for my 
constituents. Companies such as J.B. Hunt and Maverick Transportation 
employ hardworking drivers who help deliver goods not only throughout 
Arkansas, but the entire Nation.
  As Vice Chair of the Transportation and Infrastructure Committee, I 
am proud to be a champion of several key pieces of legislation this 
Congress aimed at strengthening the trucking industry, improving 
safety, and making sure our Nation's truck drivers are heard and 
supported. I will continue to advocate for our truckers and their 
needs.
  As we recognize these hardworking men and women during National Truck 
Driver Appreciation Week, we must remember that our economy depends on 
them. Without our truck drivers, our economy would come to a 
standstill, our shelves would go empty, and our communities lose access 
to the goods they rely on every day.
  I want to thank the millions of truck drivers across our country, and 
especially those who call Arkansas home, for keeping America moving.

                          ____________________
```
