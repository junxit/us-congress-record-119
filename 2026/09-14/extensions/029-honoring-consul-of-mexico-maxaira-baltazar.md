# HONORING CONSUL OF MEXICO, MAXAIRA BALTAZAR

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING CONSUL OF MEXICO, MAXAIRA BALTAZAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Consul of Mexico, 
Maxaira Baltazar, for her 20 years of leadership and experience in 
consular affairs and bilateral relations.
  Maxaira first began her career in Del Rio, Texas, where served as 
Consul for Protection and Legal Affairs, later being assigned to serve 
as Consul of Mexico for the Mexican Embassy in the Czech Republic.
  Upon her return to the United States in 2015, Maxaira completed a 
specialization in U.S. Legal Studies at The University of New Mexico's 
School of Law. Always striving for more, she went on to collaborate 
with the Federal Public Defender's Office and the criminal, family, and 
child legal divisions of New Mexico's Second Judicial District Court.
  As part of her specialization, she went on to develop guidelines for 
ways to assist nationals in child custody-related matters.
  Throughout her career, Maxaira has served the U.S. Department of 
State, where she took part in a U.S.-Mexico Consular Exchange program 
dedicated to providing international assistance for Mexican nationals 
during the tumultuous suspension of travel period caused by the COVID-
19 pandemic, drafting guidance for Mexican missions abroad during times 
of crisis.
  Most recently, Maxaira has served as Counselor for House Affairs in 
the Congressional Relations Office at the Mexican Embassy in the U.S. 
Now, Maxaira has assumed her current post as Consul of Mexico in Santa 
Ana, serving the Mexican-American communities in my home District and 
beyond.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in commending Maxaira for her 20 years 
of dedicated leadership and experience. We thank Maxaira for all that 
she does to protect, inform, and inspire our Nation's Mexican-American 
communities.
```
