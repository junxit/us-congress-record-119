# CELEBRATING THE 30TH ORDINATION ANNIVERSARY OF FATHER JIMMY DRENNAN

**Speaking:** Castro, Joaquin (C001091) [D-TX]

```
  CELEBRATING THE 30TH ORDINATION ANNIVERSARY OF FATHER JIMMY DRENNAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOAQUIN CASTRO

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. CASTRO of Texas. Mr. Speaker, today I rise in celebration of the 
30th Anniversary of the Ordination of Father Jimmy Drennan. A leader 
guided by grace, hope and compassion. Father Jimmy's commitment to 
service embodies an unyielding dedication to ensuring the spiritual 
growth of our San Antonio community.
  Father Jimmy's life of service has taken many roles throughout his 
life. As a student at

[[Page E908]]

Highlands High School in San Antonio, he served as the school's mascot 
for three years, encouraging joy and community among his peers. He also 
served in the Student Council and as Class Vice-President his senior 
year,
  Outside of school, Father Jimmy developed a love for the Roman 
Catholic faith by the following the powerful examples of his mother, 
Anne Drennan, and his grandmother Cecil. At the age of 17, he made the 
Sacrament of Baptism at St. Margaret Mary Catholic Church, the same 
parish where he currently serves as Pastor.
  As a young adult, Father Jimmy's commitment to service took the form 
of a police officer with the San Antonio Police Department from 1985 to 
1991. After seven years as an officer, he felt his calling to the 
Priesthood and entered the Assumption Seminary. On May 10, 1996, Father 
Jimmy was ordained by the Archdiocese of San Antonio.
  Father Jimmy's time as a priest has seen him take multiple roles 
throughout the archdiocese. He has served as pastor of St. Paul 
Catholic Church, St, Joseph Catholic Church--Honey Creek, and St. 
Margaret Mary Catholic Church. He has also held administrative roles 
across the archdiocese. He serves as the Spiritual Director for the 
First Responder focused Shields of Christ ACTS Teams and was the 
Chaplain to former San Antonio Police Chief William McManus.
  Father Jimmy has also dedicated himself to serving the San Antonio 
community. He is a Board Member for Project Quest, a workforce 
development organization. For over thirty years, he has been a trusted 
leader with the COPS-Metro Alliance. He is also an outspoken advocate 
for the immigrant communities.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in celebration of a pastoral career 
dedicated to serving one's community. For over thirty years, Father 
Jimmy has been a source of hope and inspiration to many, and I am 
grateful for all he has done for our San Antonio community.
```
