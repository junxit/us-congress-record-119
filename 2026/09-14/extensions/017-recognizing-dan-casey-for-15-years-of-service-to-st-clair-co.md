# RECOGNIZING DAN CASEY FOR 15 YEARS OF SERVICE TO ST. CLAIR COUNTY

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
   RECOGNIZING DAN CASEY FOR 15 YEARS OF SERVICE TO ST. CLAIR COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Dan 
Casey for 15 years of dedicated service to St. Clair County as the 
leader of the Economic Development Alliance of St. Clair County.
  Mr. Casey joined the EDA as CEO in 2011 after previously serving in 
economic development roles with the cities of Southfield and Rochester 
Hills. He truly built a career defined by strengthening Michigan 
communities and creating opportunities for businesses and residents.
  During his tenure with the EDA, Mr. Casey helped guide St. Clair 
County through significant economic challenges, including the recovery 
from the 2009 recession and the COVID-19 pandemic. Under his 
leadership, the EDA helped attract the Magna Electric Vehicle Solutions 
project to the long-vacant St. Clair Industrial Park, strengthen local 
downtowns, and support entrepreneurs through small business programs.
  After 15 years with the EDA, Mr. Casey retired as CEO on September 1, 
2026. While he has stepped away from his position, he plans to remain 
active in the community through board service and devote more time to 
writing.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Dan Casey 
for his service to St. Clair County. I am proud to honor his dedication 
to economic development, small businesses, and the communities he has 
worked to strengthen throughout his career, and I wish him all the best 
in his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
