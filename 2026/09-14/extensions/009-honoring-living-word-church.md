# HONORING LIVING WORD CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                      HONORING LIVING WORD CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Living Word Church 
(LWC) of Baton Rouge, a ministry birthed in prayer and sustained by 
prayer. On Wednesday, January 1, 1997, under the direction of the Holy 
Spirit, Living Word Church held its first service at the Prince Murat 
Hotel, formerly located on Nicholson Drive. Since that first meeting, 
God has added to the church in both numbers and spiritual maturity, 
abundantly blessing the ministry as it endeavors to fulfill its vision 
and God-breathed mission.
  Living Word Church is a place of praise and thanksgiving, filled with 
a congregation that acknowledges the miracle-working power of the 
Almighty God. In May 1998, the church rented a facility located on 
Nicholson Drive, where services were held for three years. In February 
2001, LWC purchased another building on Nicholson Drive. God blessed 
the ministry with individuals in the local body who oversaw the 
renovation process, along with able men of God who assisted. The church 
held its first Sunday service in the new location on Easter Sunday, 
April 15. 2001.
  Out of a love for all people, LWC sponsored several community 
outreaches, including Hallelujah Nights, prayer breakfasts, health 
fairs, food and clothing distribution, Thanksgiving baskets, Christmas 
gifts, a church community basketball team, and other outreach 
activities. The ministry thrived on Nicholson Drive for fourteen years.
  On April 6, 2015, a new facility was purchased on N. Sherwood Forest 
Drive. This location encompasses 7.95 acres of land and includes the 
main sanctuary, fellowship hall, a baseball field, and a gymnasium. LWC 
stands strong because of the unity among its leaders and membership. 
The Word of God goes forth in each service, and God continues to do 
great things as the ministry grows, knowing that greater things are 
still to come.
  On November 19, 2025, Living Word Church expanded its commitment to 
serving the community through the opening of the Living Word Church 
Food Pantry. This ministry was created to meet the needs of individuals 
and families by providing food and support to those experiencing food 
insecurity. The opening of the food pantry represents another 
expression of pure love for God and commitment to serving His people.
  The leadership and members of Living Word Church praise God for all 
that has been accomplished. The ministry has flourished since its 
humble beginnings at the Prince Murat Hotel. The favor, grace, and 
presence of God manifest there. LWC is a place of love, worship, and 
anointing, where lives are continually transformed through the Word of 
God.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Living Word 
Church for its steadfast faith, service, and spiritual impact on the 
Baton Rouge community, and in wishing its leadership and congregation 
continued blessings in the years to come.
```
