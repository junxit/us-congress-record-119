# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ANEESH MAXUMDER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ANEESH MAXUMDER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Aneesh Maxumder. He is a Euless native and current student at 
Harvard University, where he continues to build on his strong 
commitment to public service. His interest in public service began at a 
young age, when his family regularly volunteered in our community, 
including at food drives for Grapevine Relief & Community Exchange. He 
continued his dedication to serving North Texas by participating in our 
Congressional Youth Advisory Program, where students across Texas-24 
learn about public service and strengthen their leadership skills.
  After witnessing friends struggle to access the resources needed to 
pursue medical careers, Aneesh launched HealthPath Horizons to connect 
students with the tools they need to enter the healthcare field. 
Through this nonprofit, he provides presentations, workshops, and open-
access materials to underrepresented students. To date, HealthPath 
Horizons has directly empowered more than 300 underserved students to 
pursue the medical careers they aspire to.
  Aneesh's leadership and desire to help others makes him a shining 
example of the remarkable students from North Texas. We look forward to 
watching him empower others to explore their own dream careers.

                          ____________________
```
