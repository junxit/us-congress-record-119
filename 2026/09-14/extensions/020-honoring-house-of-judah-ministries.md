# HONORING HOUSE OF JUDAH MINISTRIES

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING HOUSE OF JUDAH MINISTRIES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor House of Judah 
Ministries. It is an interdenominational Christian church founded in 
Baton Rouge, Louisiana, in 1999 by its Founder and Senior Pastor, 
Apostle Dr. Lawrence D. Hardy. From its inception, the ministry has 
been guided by a clear and enduring mission: ``Touching Lives and 
Meeting Needs Through God's Eyes.''
  From its earliest days, House of Judah Ministries has remained 
committed to fulfilling God's purpose by reaching people from all walks 
of life. Its vision is to lead change, equip, and empower God's people, 
and make a positive and lasting impact in the communities it serves. 
Over the years, the ministry has extended its reach far beyond the four 
walls of the church through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, 
discipleship, spiritual development, community outreach, and 
compassionate service to individuals and families.
  Throughout its history, House of Judah Ministries has developed and 
supported a wide range of ministries and areas of service, including 
Worship and Praise Ministry, Children's Ministry, Youth and Young Adult 
Ministry, Men's and Women's Ministries, Evangelism and Community 
Outreach, Prison Ministry and Reintegration Support, Health and

[[Page E904]]

Wellness Ministry, Economic and Financial Empowerment, Marriage and 
Family Ministry, Prayer and Intercessory Ministry, Discipleship and 
Biblical Education, Media and Technology Ministry, and Missionary and 
Community-Service Initiatives.
  Throughout its history, The House of Judah Ministries has worshipped 
and ministered from several locations throughout Baton Rouge. As the 
ministry continued to grow and respond to the needs of the community, 
it relocated to accommodate its evolving vision and mission. Its 
current place of worship is located on Anselmo Lane, Baton Rouge, 
Louisiana, where it continues to provide a place for worship, biblical 
instruction, spiritual growth, and community connection.
  Under the leadership of Apostle Dr. Lawrence D. Hardy, the influence 
of The House of Judah Ministries has extended well beyond its local 
congregation. Apostle Hardy is recognized as a pastor, teacher, 
apostle, prophet, entrepreneur, and spiritual father. Through his 
ministry and leadership, he has touched churches, civic organizations, 
government leaders, and communities throughout Louisiana and beyond. In 
2026, the Louisiana Senate formally commended Apostle Hardy for his 
exceptional leadership and spiritual guidance during the Louisiana 
Legislature's Regular Session.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to recognize the House of Judah 
Ministries for remaining faithful to its founding mission. Through 
changing seasons, expanding ministries, new locations, and unwavering 
commitment to community service, the church continues to stand as a 
place of worship, healing, empowerment, restoration, and spiritual 
growth. Its history is marked by lives touched, families strengthened, 
disciples developed, and communities served. As it looks toward the 
future, The House of Judah Ministries remains committed to honoring its 
foundation, embracing its God-given vision, and continuing its mission: 
``Touching Lives and Meeting Needs Through God's Eyes.''
```
