# RECOGNIZING ABIGAIL ROTH, GRADES 6 THROUGH 8 WINNER OF WISCONSIN'S 6TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT 2026 STUDENT ESSAY CONTEST

**Speaking:** Grothman, Glenn (G000576) [R-WI]

```
RECOGNIZING ABIGAIL ROTH, GRADES 6 THROUGH 8 WINNER OF WISCONSIN'S 6TH 
           CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT 2026 STUDENT ESSAY CONTEST

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GLENN GROTHMAN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. GROTHMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Abigail Roth, 
the winner of the sixth through eighth grade division of my 2026 
Student Essay Contest, and to commend this outstanding young person 
from Wisconsin's 6th Congressional District for her thoughtful 
reflections on our country and system government.
  Abigail, a seventh-grade student at Neenah Middle School, was 
selected as the winner for her essay on the importance of an informed 
and educated citizenry, strong communities, civic engagement, and the 
ability of Americans to work together to improve our country.
  I am proud to recognize Abigail for the time, effort, and thought she 
put into her essay. Her work demonstrates an appreciation for the 
responsibilities of citizenship and the principles that have helped 
make our nation strong. I congratulate her on this achievement and am 
pleased to include in the Record her winning essay:

             Abigail Roth--Neenah Middle School, 7th Grade

       I believe America's strength comes from our nation's 
     ability to grow and move forward with the engagement and 
     attention of the citizens. To realize America's strength, the 
     citizens must be well informed and educated. Those citizens 
     create communities that generate trust between their 
     neighbors to influence policy. When those communities unite, 
     America has a system in place to change the fate of our 
     governmental system for the better. This system can create 
     long lasting change in America, improving the country for 
     future generations.
       In order for America to grow and move forward, the public 
     must be well informed and well educated. Everyone that has 
     the right to vote should have access to education. Education 
     creates a solid base of knowledge and skills necessary to 
     promote individual civic responsibility. Civic responsibility 
     is important because it leads to engagement with our country, 
     necessary for a strong democracy and nation. Without the 
     ability to read the ballot, how can we expect anyone to be 
     engaged with democratic process? Next, having a well informed 
     public that knows what is going on in the nation is very 
     important. It's not enough that citizens can read, it is that 
     they do read, and learn about issues in their government that 
     they care about. With robust information, a well informed 
     public enlivens democracy to grow and move forward, because 
     people can make better decisions about what is happening in 
     their country. To have a well informed public, reliable 
     sources of information must be available. Newspapers, news 
     channels, and government reports that adhere to standards 
     which promote reliability and trustworthiness are valuable. 
     These information sources create an environment where good 
     faith debate and discussion can occur. America's strength is 
     tied to its citizen's ability to constructively debate the 
     issues of the moment.
       Secondly, America derives strength from the trust built 
     through engaged communities. When citizens are engaged with 
     the issues that are affecting their community they can unite 
     and come together to improve. When a community solves a 
     problem they build trust by talking to each other and working 
     together to fix the problem. People get involved in their 
     community by joining events, talking to neighbors and joining 
     clubs in their community. Anything that requires multiple 
     people to interact in a community creates familiarity and 
     trust. The government especially requires familiarity and 
     trust from its constituents. As people work with each other 
     to address issues in their community, those same people grow 
     to become influential parts of the government. Whether they 
     submit public comments, letters to their representatives or 
     running for local office, these people exert influence on 
     governance that leads to improvement. This process of well-
     informed, and educated citizens engaging with the issues of 
     their community keeps growing until a chain of trust bonds 
     every person. America derives strength from the trust through 
     communities that come together and unite.
       Lastly, we have built a system of government that can 
     improve upon itself. When we have a well informed and well 
     educated public we can create good ideas. These good ideas 
     will be tested by the community through discussion and debate 
     to identify and refine them into great ideas. Then those 
     great ideas will be carried up from local to state to federal 
     politicians who can deploy them to help the nation. An 
     example of this is the 19th amendment that gave women the 
     right to vote. Over the span of about 70 years, the idea of 
     women's suffrage spread grew from novel, to good, to great, 
     until finally being ratified in 1920. In other forms of 
     government, like the British monarchy from which the USA 
     revolted, citizens' ideas take a back seat to what the leader 
     wants. But in this nation we have created a system where 
     things can change and be better.
       Overall, America's strength comes from a public being well 
     informed. That public must belong to communities that create 
     trust and generate great ideas. Those great ideas then inform 
     the governmental system that adapts to become better. To make 
     America stronger, citizens must obtain good education, keep 
     themselves well informed, build community to discuss and 
     debate how America can improve, and engage in the political 
     process to see their ideas come into law. America's strength 
     will improve if we work together to ensure that all citizens 
     can partake in this country's growth.
```
