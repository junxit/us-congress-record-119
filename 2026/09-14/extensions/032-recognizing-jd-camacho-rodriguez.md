# RECOGNIZING JD CAMACHO RODRIGUEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                    RECOGNIZING JD CAMACHO RODRIGUEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize JD Camacho 
Rodriguez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  JD has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like JD, develop crucial skills and a work 
ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a 
testament to JD's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Jefferson Jr/Sr High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate JD Camacho Rodriguez on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
