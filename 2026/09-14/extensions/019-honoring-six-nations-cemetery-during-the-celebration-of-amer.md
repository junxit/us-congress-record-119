# HONORING SIX NATIONS CEMETERY DURING THE CELEBRATION OF AMERICA'S 250TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
HONORING SIX NATIONS CEMETERY DURING THE CELEBRATION OF AMERICA'S 250TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Six Nations 
Cemetery in the Town of Orange, Schuyler County, and the community 
members who have worked to restore and preserve this historic site as 
we celebrate the 250th anniversary of the United States of America.
  Six Nations Cemetery is an important part of our region's history and 
serves as a lasting reminder of the generations of Americans who came 
before us. The dedication of plaques honoring veterans buried at the 
cemetery provides a meaningful opportunity to recognize their service 
and sacrifice while ensuring their contributions are remembered by 
future generations.
  Today's dedication and community picnic also demonstrate the 
importance of coming together to celebrate our shared history and the 
communities that make our Nation strong. As we mark America's 250th 
anniversary, events like this give us an opportunity to reflect on our 
past while looking toward the future.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in recognizing Six Nations Cemetery, its veterans, and the 
dedicated community members who have worked to preserve this important 
piece of our local history. Their efforts are a fitting tribute to our 
Nation's history and the enduring American spirit.
```
