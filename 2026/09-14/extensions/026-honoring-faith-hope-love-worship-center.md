# HONORING FAITH, HOPE & LOVE WORSHIP CENTER

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING FAITH, HOPE & LOVE WORSHIP CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge Faith, Hope & 
Love Worship Center, a church with deep spiritual roots in St. 
Francisville, Louisiana. Established in 1985 under the leadership of 
Bishop Dwight D. Pate, the ministry grew in strength and fellowship. In 
July 1989, Ronald E. Hardy, Sr. was installed as pastor of what was 
then known as Faith, Hope and Love Fellowship, beginning a new chapter 
of leadership and vision within the congregation.
  In 2002, God spoke to Bishop Dwight D. Pate to release Faith, Hope & 
Love to Pastor Ronald Hardy, Sr., prompting a formal celebration and 
prophetic proclamation of 2002 as `The Year of the Double.' During a 
revival later that year, God spoke through a prophet, declaring that an 
extended work was required. God instructed Pastor Hardy to establish a 
second location for the ministry in Baton Rouge, sharing that there was 
a harvest of family, friends, and others who needed to be reached. 
Following divine direction, Pastor Hardy was led to a building on 
Wooddale Boulevard.
  On December 25, 2002--Christmas Day--Faith, Hope & Love of Baton 
Rouge opened, marking a historic expansion of the ministry. Faith, Hope 
& Love Fellowship became Faith, Hope and Love Worship Center: one 
church in two locations. Soon afterwards, land was purchased and a new 
church facility was built on Choctaw Drive. With this expansion, the 
ministry embraced its mission to reach families and communities across 
both cities with a message of hope, faith, and transformational love.
  Mr. Speaker, Faith, Hope & Love Worship Center is a multifaceted 
church dedicated to bringing restoration to the lives of God's people. 
Its ministry is grounded in introducing individuals to Christ and 
teaching them to live the abundant life through the Word of God. As 
Faith, Hope & Love continues to grow and fulfill its mission, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring this remarkable church for its 
decades of service, spiritual leadership, and dedication to 
strengthening communities in both St. Francisville and Baton Rouge.
```
