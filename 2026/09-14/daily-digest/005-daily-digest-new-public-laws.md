# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S. 858, H.R. 1276, H.R. 2069, H.R. 2196, H.R. 5366

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D716)
  S. 858, to authorize the National Medal of Honor Museum Foundation to 
establish a commemorative work on the National Mall to honor the 
extraordinary acts of valor, selfless service, and sacrifice displayed 
by Medal of Honor recipients. Signed on September 10, 2026. (Public Law 
119-104)
  H.R. 1276, to remove restrictions from a parcel of land in Paducah, 
Kentucky. Signed on September 11, 2026. (Public Law 119-105)
  H.R. 2069, to amend the Federal Funding Accountability and 
Transparency Act of 2006 to ensure that other transaction agreements 
are reported to USAspending.gov. Signed on September 11, 2026. (Public 
Law 119-106)
  H.R. 2196, to provide for an extension of the legislative authority 
of the National Emergency Medical Services Memorial Foundation to 
establish a commemorative work in the District of Columbia and its 
environs. Signed on September 11, 2026. (Public Law 119-107)
  H.R. 5366, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to codify and 
extend the rules for personal casualty losses arising from major 
disasters and the rules for the exclusion from gross income of 
compensation for losses or damages resulting from certain wildfires. 
Signed on September 11, 2026. (Public Law 119-108)
```
