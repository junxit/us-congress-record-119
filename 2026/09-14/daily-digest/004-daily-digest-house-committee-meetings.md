# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 210, H.J.Res. 213, H.R. 5334, H.R. 9576, H.R. 10326

```
Committee Meetings
PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE 
ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY RELATING TO CALIFORNIA STATE NONROAD 
ENGINE POLLUTION CONTROL STANDARDS; OCEAN-GOING VESSELS AT-BERTH; 
NOTICE OF DECISION; PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE 
ISSUED BY THE ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY RELATING TO THE 
CALIFORNIA STATE NONROAD ENGINE POLLUTION CONTROL STANDARDS; COMMERCIAL 
HARBOR CRAFT REGULATIONS; NOTICE OF DECISION; NATIONAL FRAUD 
ENFORCEMENT DIVISION ACT OF 2026; PREVENTING RIP-OFFS AND OBTAINING 
OVERSIGHT OF FUNDS ACT; SENATE AMENDMENTS TO THE LINDSEY O. GRAHAM 
SANCTIONING RUSSIA AND IRAN ACT OF 2026
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.J. Res. 210, 
providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
Protection Agency relating to ``California State Nonroad Engine 
Pollution Control Standards; Ocean-Going Vessels At-Berth; Notice of 
Decision''; H.J. Res. 213, providing for congressional disapproval 
under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule issued by 
the Environmental Protection Agency relating to the ``California State 
Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft 
Regulations; Notice of Decision''; H.R. 9576, the ``National Fraud 
Enforcement Division Act of 2026''; H.R. 10326, the ``Preventing Rip-
offs and Obtaining Oversight of Funds Act''; and the Senate amendments 
to H.R. 5334, the ``Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act 
of 2026''. The Committee granted, by a record vote of 7-3, a rule 
providing for consideration of H.R. 9576, the ``National Fraud 
Enforcement Division Act of 2026'', H.R. 10326, the ``PROOF Act'', H.J. 
Res. 210, Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
Protection Agency relating to ``California State Nonroad Engine 
Pollution Control Standards; Ocean-Going Vessels At-Berth; Notice of 
Decision'', H.J. Res. 213, Providing for congressional disapproval 
under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule issued by 
the Environmental Protection Agency relating to the ``California State 
Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft 
Regulations; Notice of Decision'', and the Senate amendments to H.R. 
5334, the ``Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 
2026''. The rule provides for consideration of H.R. 9576, the 
``National Fraud Enforcement Division Act of 2026'', under a closed 
rule. The rule waives all points of order against consideration of the 
bill. The rule provides that an amendment in the nature of a substitute 
consisting of the text of Rules Committee Print 119-41 shall be 
considered as adopted and the bill, as amended, shall be considered as 
read. The rule waives all points of order against provisions in the 
bill, as amended. The rule provides one hour of general debate equally 
divided and controlled by the chair and ranking minority member of the 
Committee on the Judiciary or their respective designees. The rule 
provides one motion to recommit. The rule further provides for 
consideration of H.R. 10326, the ``PROOF Act'', under a closed rule. 
The rule waives all points of order against consideration of the bill. 
The rule provides that the bill shall be considered as read. The rule 
waives all points of order against provisions in the bill. The rule 
provides one hour of general debate equally divided and controlled by 
the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on the Judiciary 
or their respective designees. The rule provides one motion to 
recommit. The rule further provides for consideration of H.J. Res. 210, 
Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
Protection Agency relating to ``California State Nonroad Engine 
Pollution Control Standards; Ocean-Going Vessels At-Berth; Notice of 
Decision'', under a closed rule. The rule waives all points of order 
against consideration of the joint resolution. The rule provides that 
the joint resolution shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the joint resolution. The rule 
provides one hour of general debate equally divided and controlled by 
the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and 
Commerce or their respective designees. The rule provides one motion to 
recommit. The rule further provides for consideration of H.J. Res. 213, 
Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule issued by the Environmental Protection 
Agency relating to the ``California State Nonroad Engine Pollution 
Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft Regulations; Notice of 
Decision'', under a closed rule. The rule waives all points of order 
against consideration of the joint resolution. The rule provides that 
the joint resolution shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the joint resolution. The rule 
provides one hour of general debate equally divided and controlled by 
the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and 
Commerce or their respective designees. The rule provides one motion to 
recommit. The rule further provides for the consideration of the Senate 
amendments to H.R. 5334, the ``Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and 
Iran Act of 2026''. The rule makes in order a single motion offered by 
the chair of the Committee on Foreign Affairs or his designee that the 
House concur in the Senate amendments to H.R. 5334. The rule waives all 
points of order against consideration of the Senate amendments and the 
motion. The rule provides that the Senate amendments and the motion 
shall be considered as read. Finally, the rule provides one hour of 
debate on the motion equally divided and controlled by the chair and 
ranking minority member of the Committee on Foreign Affairs or their 
respective designees. Testimony was heard from Representatives Palmer, 
Pallone, Cline, Raskin, McCaul, Meeks, and Hoyer.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
