# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty and eternal God, thank You for hearing and answering our 
prayers. You are the high and lofty one. Yet You have chosen to dwell 
with those who have contrite and humble hearts.
  We ask that you dwell with our lawmakers. May they meditate on Your 
ways and be guided by the light of Your word.
  Lord, give them wisdom to seek Your help in times of trouble and 
beyond. Remind them of all that You have already done, particularly the 
ways You carried and sustained this Nation through September 11, 2001. 
May those memories give them confidence that the best is yet to come.
  Lord, do for them immeasurably, abundantly above all that they can 
ask or imagine.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
