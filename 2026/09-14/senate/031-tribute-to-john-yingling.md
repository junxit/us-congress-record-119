# TRIBUTE TO JOHN YINGLING

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JOHN YINGLING

  Ms. BALDWIN. Mr. President, today I rise to honor John Yingling on 
his retirement after his distinguished service as chair of the 
Wisconsin State Fair Park Board. During his tenure, Mr. Yingling helped 
lead one of the most transformative periods in Wisconsin State Fair 
history, helping create lasting memories for millions of fairgoers.
  In 2011, then-Wisconsin Governor Scott Walker appointed Mr. Yingling 
to serve on the Wisconsin State Fair Park Board. During his time as 
chair, the State fair expanded year-round operations, hosted the 
largest Clydesdale horse show in North America, and welcomed INDYCAR 
racing back to the historic Milwaukee Mile. These accomplishments, 
along with many others, have strengthened the State fair and helped 
spur investment in the greater Milwaukee community.
  When Mr. Yingling became chair, the State fair faced significant 
financial challenges. He was instrumental in helping move the fair from 
the red into the black, and under his leadership, those gains were 
reinvested in the fair and the community.
  Mr. Yingling also helped foster innovation and creativity that have 
become hallmarks of the Wisconsin State Fair. Through the annual 
Sporkies and Drinkies competitions, State Fair vendors compete to 
create unique foods and drinks that attract visitors from across 
Wisconsin and beyond.
  As we celebrate the 175th anniversary of the Wisconsin State Fair, I 
am pleased to recognize Mr. Yingling's distinguished service and his 
many contributions to the people of Wisconsin. His leadership has 
helped ensure that the State fair remains a treasured Wisconsin 
tradition for generations to come.
  I congratulate Mr. Yingling on his retirement and wish him all the 
best.
```
