# TRIBUTE TO BOB AND CELIA KIRKWOOD

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                   TRIBUTE TO BOB AND CELIA KIRKWOOD

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I rise today in celebration of Bob and 
Celia Kirkwood, the Boys and Girls Club of Central Wyoming 2026 
honorees.
  Since 1969, the Boys and Girls Club of Central Wyoming has been 
inspiring young people to reach their full potential. Local boys and 
girls participate in educational and athletic programs that provide 
enrichment and help them become productive, responsible and caring 
citizens. From leadership development to a focus on healthy lifestyles, 
the club provides top-notch opportunities for each member.
  For over 55 years, the Boys and Girls Club of Central Wyoming has 
connected youth with dedicated volunteers and mentors. The club serves 
over 13,000 youth each year across its four-county territory, helping 
each member become their best self. Each year, at its annual breakfast, 
the organization highlights an individual or couple for their 
commitment to giving back to the community. This week, they will honor 
Bob and Celia Kirkwood with this prestigious recognition.
  On Wednesday, September 16, 2026, the Boys and Girls Club of Central 
Wyoming will host their annual awards and recognition breakfast in 
Casper. They will honor the Kirkwoods for their unwavering dedication 
to the Casper community. Bob and Celia's commitment to the youth of 
Natrona County is certainly worth celebrating. It is fitting that the 
Boys and Girl Club of Central Wyoming is publicly recognizing their 
legacy of service and strong investment in our next generation of 
leaders.
  Bob and Celia have been married for 47 years and are the proud 
parents of Michael Kirkwood, Tom and Cassie Kirkwood and grandparents 
to Josie. They center their life on faith and are proud that Wyoming 
has always been home.
  In 1982, Bob joined his father's business Kirkwood Oil and Gas. 
Alongside his brother Steve, they have diversified the company to 
include oil and gas operations, pipeline management, and real estate. 
He remains an industry advocate and trusted adviser to many around the 
State.
  Celia focused her time on raising their sons. From volunteering 
throughout their education to serving as a Boys and Girls Club Youth of 
the Year judge, she is committed to giving back to those who need it 
most. Celia shines behind the scenes, making sure to concentrate on 
faith, family, and community service.
  Beyond the Boys and Girls Club, Bob and Celia passionately support 
Saint Anthony Tri-Parish Catholic School, the Wyoming Rescue Mission, 
and True Care. Our community is fortunate that, where there is a need, 
the Kirkwoods are willing to step up to help their friends and 
neighbors.
  Bob and Celia are guided by their faith. They encourage the members 
of the Boys and Girls Club to ``make sure the path you are on leads to 
the life you truly want and do not be afraid to adjust along the way. 
Remember, God knows best; trust Him.''
  For decades, the couple has led by example and have generously given 
back to the community they love so much. The Kirkwoods live their 
mission of meeting real needs and building lasting impact through 
consistent, practical action.
  The Boys and Girls Club of Central Wyoming is instrumental in 
providing young people with a safe, nurturing, and inspiring space to 
develop and learn. The opportunities afforded to members allow them to 
become the future leaders we need. These impressive goals would not be 
possible without the passion and generosity of Bob and Celia. They are 
exemplary role models of selfless leadership.
  It is with great honor that I recognize this incredible couple and 
their ongoing dedication to Casper. I encourage my colleagues to join 
me in celebrating Bob and Celia Kirkwood for everything they have done 
and continue to do to make Casper a better place for all.

                          ____________________
```
