# TRIBUTE TO JENNIFER ANDERSON

**Speaking:** Duckworth, Tammy (D000622) [D-IL]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                      TRIBUTE TO JENNIFER ANDERSON

● Ms. DUCKWORTH. Mr. President, along with my colleague Senator 
Mike Crapo, we join in honoring Jennifer Anderson, registered 
dietitian, for her commitment to children's health. We thank her for 
sharing about her work with the Senate Moms group.
  As the founder of Kids Eat in Color and the CEO of BetterBites, 
Jennifer Anderson supports parents navigating the challenges of feeding 
their children well by sharing education, strategies, and resources 
focused on nutrition and eating. She also works with healthcare 
providers by offering resources and an

[[Page S4654]]

accredited continuing education workshop to prevent and treat Avoidant/
Restrictive Food Intake Disorder and extreme picky eating in patients.
  Jennifer's career of over 20 years has focused on nutrition and 
public health. She earned a master of science in public health from the 
Johns Hopkins School of Public Health and worked as a nutritionist 
information specialist and consultant at the U.S. Department of 
Agriculture. After utilizing her experience to navigate her own 
children's growth struggles and extreme picky eating, she dedicated her 
expertise to supporting other parents.
  Our staff lead the Senate Moms group, a bipartisan group of more than 
200 moms employed by the U.S. Senate. The group's regular meetings and 
internal resources in the Senate help working moms feel united and 
supported in navigating the Senate workplace and raising children in 
the Washington, DC, area.
  Thank you, Jennifer, for your inspiring work that empowers families 
and healthcare professionals to advance children's health and 
nutrition. You have encouraged the many moms who help keep the U.S. 
Senate functioning day-to-day. We wish you the best in all the future 
offers you.●
```
