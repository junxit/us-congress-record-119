# CONGRATULATING LIEUTENANT JOHN SWANDRAK

**Speaking:** McIver, LaMonica (M001229) [D-NJ]

```
                CONGRATULATING LIEUTENANT JOHN SWANDRAK

  (Mrs. McIVER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Lieutenant John 
Swandrak on 30 years of service to the Cranford Police Department and 
wish Lieutenant Swandrak the best on his retirement.
  His decades of service in patrol, community policing, and the 
detective bureau allowed him to build invaluable relationships and 
strengthen the bond between the police department and the Cranford 
community.
  That was demonstrated in profound ways, particularly during his son 
Nicholas' battle with leukemia. Cranford Police Department, Cranford 
Fire Department, and countless neighbors rallied around the Swandrak 
family reflecting the same support and compassion he consistently 
showed others.
  Lieutenant Swandrak is foundational to the fabric of Cranford, and he 
is leaving a legacy of service, fairness, and true community 
partnership for all of New Jersey to follow.
  On behalf of the New Jersey 10th District, I congratulate Lieutenant 
Swandrak on a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
