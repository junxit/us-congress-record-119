Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Health held a markup 
on H.R. 5880, the ``Fight Illicit Pill Presses Act''; H.R. 7184, the 
``PRESS Act''; and H.R. 8005, the ``Stop Pills That Kill Act''. H.R. 
8005 was forwarded to the full Committee, as amended. H.R. 5880 and 
H.R. 7184 were forwarded to the full Committee, without amendment.
MAINTAINING AMERICA'S LEADERSHIP IN BIOMEDICAL INNOVATION: FOOD AND 
DRUG ADMINISTRATION'S ROLE IN ADVANCING U.S. DRUG DEVELOPMENT
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Health held a hearing 
entitled ``Maintaining America's Leadership in Biomedical Innovation: 
FDA's Role in Advancing U.S. Drug Development''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
THE SEMI-ANNUAL REPORT OF THE BUREAU OF CONSUMER FINANCIAL PROTECTION
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``The Semi-Annual Report of the Bureau of Consumer Financial 
Protection''. Testimony was heard from Russell Vought, Acting Director, 
Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, Federal Reserve System.
ENDING SUPPLY CHAIN DEPENDENCY: ALIGNING TOOLS, CAPITAL, AND 
PARTNERSHIPS
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Ending Supply Chain Dependency: Aligning Tools, Capital, and 
Partnerships''. Testimony was heard from Ben Black, Chief Executive 
Officer, U.S. International Development Finance Corporation; Thomas R. 
Hardym, Deputy Director and Chief Operating Officer, U.S. Trade and 
Development Agency; and Dan Petrie, Acting Chief of Staff, Millennium 
Challenge Corporation.
A NEW BEGINNING FOR COLOMBIA
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Western Hemisphere Subcommittee held a 
hearing entitled ``A New Beginning for Colombia''. Testimony was heard 
from

[[Page D715]]

Deputy Assistant Secretary for South America, Bureau of Western

ELECTION OBSERVATION: BEST PRACTICES AND CONGRESS'S ROLE
Committee on House Administration: Subcommittee on Elections held a 
hearing entitled ``Election Observation: Best Practices and Congress's 
Role''. Testimony was heard from Paige Gallagher, Legislative Attorney, 
Congressional Research Service, Library of Congress; Lance Reed, 
Director, Summit County Board of Elections, Ohio; and Wesley Wilcox, 
Supervisor of Elections, Marion County Florida.

726, the ``Crow Tribe Water Rights Settlement Amendments Act of 2025'';

Act of 2025''; H.R. 2406, the ``National Oceanic and Atmospheric

H.R. 2827, and H.R. 2406 were ordered reported, without amendment. H.R. 
6893, H.R. 2317, H.R. 9436, H.R. 726, and H.R. 6251 were ordered 
reported, as amended.

Government Accountability Office; and public witnesses.
A REVIEW OF THE OFFICE OF SPACE COMMERCE'S MISSION AUTHORIZATION

witnesses.
