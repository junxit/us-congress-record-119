# RECOGNIZING MR. JOHNNIE L. WILLIAMS, JR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                RECOGNIZING MR. JOHNNIE L. WILLIAMS, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida,

[[Page E695]]

I rise today to recognize and honor Mr. Johnnie L. Williams, Jr., one 
of Miami's first Black business owners. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation in his honor:

       Whereas, Mr. Johnnie L. Williams, Jr., served Miami-Dade 
     County with integrity and commitment to protecting and 
     serving its citizens for over 40 years;
       Whereas, Mr. Williams was bound by his beliefs in equality, 
     fairness and justice for African Americans, employees of 
     Miami-Dade County, students and families. He generously gave 
     and supported various charitable organizations, including 
     little league teams in Opa locka, Miami Gardens and Liberty 
     City;
       Whereas, Mr. Williams worked tirelessly to ``balance the 
     scales of justice.'' and ensure the safety of our youth;
       Whereas, Mr. Williams was one of the first Black business 
     owners in Miami-Dade County, running an NFL Skycap and 
     Porters business;
       Whereas, Mr. Williams was a member and advocate of the 
     Miami-Dade Chamber of Commerce and served as the President of 
     the Orange Ridge Corporation;
       Whereas, Mr. Williams served in the United States Navy, and 
     is a veteran of World War II; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     honor the life and accomplishments of Mr. Johnnie L. 
     Williams, Jr.

                          ____________________
```
