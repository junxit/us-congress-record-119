# COMMENDING THE LATE ELDER RUBEN ALEXANDER COX, JR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
           COMMENDING THE LATE ELDER RUBEN ALEXANDER COX, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to commend the late Elder 
Ruben Alexander Cox, Jr. and I include in the Record the following 
proclamation in his honor:

       Whereas, the late Elder Ruben Alexander Cox, Jr., at an 
     early age, was ordained to the gospel ministry, committed to 
     the work and calling of God and served as a ministry for over 
     50 years;
       Whereas, Elder Cox dedicated his life to preaching and 
     teaching the word of God, and by teaching messages of hope 
     and love became a spiritual blessing to many;
       Whereas, Elder Cox gave abundantly to charity and to his 
     community throughout his lifetime;
       Whereas, Elder Cox was an advocate for community and social 
     change, and one of the community's unsung heroes:
       Whereas, Elder Cox built his life around faith, family and 
     friends;
       Whereas, Elder Cox, even in retirement, continued to work 
     with the youth in his community, mentoring and guiding them 
     to become selfless members of society;
       Whereas, Elder Cox was a devoted father and husband who 
     always had a wise sermon for anyone who would listen; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United

[[Page E692]]

     States House of Representatives, representing the 24th 
     Congressional District of Florida, commend the late Elder 
     Ruben Alexander Cox, Jr. for his dedication to his family, 
     his church, our youth and our community.

                          ____________________
```
