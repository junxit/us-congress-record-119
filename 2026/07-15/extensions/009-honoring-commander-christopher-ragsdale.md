# HONORING COMMANDER CHRISTOPHER RAGSDALE

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                HONORING COMMANDER CHRISTOPHER RAGSDALE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Commander Christopher Ragsdale on the occasion of his taking command of 
the USS Lenah Sutcliffe Higbee. This milestone is a testament to his 
professionalism and unyielding commitment to serving our Nation. 
Advancing to this level of leadership reflects both the trust placed in 
him by the United States Navy and the exceptional standard he has 
continuously demonstrated throughout his career.
  During his time serving as my Department of Defense Legislative 
Fellow, Commander

[[Page E693]]

Ragsdale distinguished himself as a professional of the highest 
caliber. Commander Ragsdale has judgement that is unmatched and a 
commitment to excellence that is second to none. These traits made him 
an invaluable asset to our team. Whether navigating complex national 
security challenges or offering thoughtful insight on matters affecting 
our Armed Forces, Commander Ragsdale consistently exhibited a level of 
competence and character that set him apart.
  It is no surprise to those of us who had the privilege of working 
alongside him that he has now taken command of the USS Lenah Sutcliffe 
Higbee. This achievement reflects not only his incredible abilities, 
but also his unbreakable dedication to serving our Nation.
  While we miss his presence in our office, we take extraordinary pride 
in knowing that he will continue to do great things wherever he goes. 
The United States Navy, and our country, are stronger because of 
leaders like Commander Ragsdale. Mr. Speaker, I ask that you join me in 
recognizing Commander Christopher Ragsdale for his service to the 
Nation. I pray for his continued success in all that lies ahead.

                          ____________________
```
