# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF DUININCK

**Speaking:** Emmer, Tom (E000294) [R-MN]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF DUININCK

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM EMMER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Mr. EMMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
100th anniversary of Duininck, a fourth-generation, family-owned 
construction company that has been serving communities in Minnesota and 
around the country with integrity for a century. It is my privilege to 
congratulate the Duininck family, the employees of Duininck, and all 
who have contributed to this remarkable legacy on 100 years in 
business.
  Founded in Prinsburg, Minnesota, it all started with two brothers and 
their road grading enterprise in 1926. With workhorses and iron 
graders, their pioneering spirit laid the foundation for their future 
success. Two years later, the third Duininck brother joined their team, 
solidifying this first-generation partnership that would build a 
lasting legacy.
  In the 1950s, seven sons joined the business to continue the family 
venture. Soon after, they expanded further regionally, supporting 
President Dwight D. Eisenhower's Federal-Aid Highway Act to invest in 
America's interstate highway system. Duininck's work on these major 
projects established its reputation for excellence in heavy highway and 
civil construction.
  In 1985, Duininck expanded its operation to Texas for highway and 
government airfield construction. Fifty courageous families moved from 
Prinsburg, Minnesota to Texas to launch this new venture. Many of those 
original families still live there and work at Duininck to this day.
  In 1996, Duininck expanded its Minnesota branch and strengthened its 
regional presence. By 2003, the grandchildren of the original founders 
had fully assumed ownership of the business. In 2026, the fourth 
generation of owners joined the company, helping continue this family 
legacy.
  Duininck's guiding principles of treating people well, respecting the 
communities it serves, and doing the work the right way have not only 
been the foundation of its success, but have positively impacted every 
community its work has touched. Duininck has created jobs and supported 
our economy for generations, strengthening the communities it calls 
home.
  From Minnesota and throughout the Midwest to Texas, Duininck has 
built a legacy of strength. It is an honor to recognize Duininck for 
its lasting impact in Minnesota on the occasion of its 100th 
anniversary. I join Duininck's leadership, employees, and customers in 
celebrating this historic achievement as we look ahead to the success 
of many generations to come.
```
