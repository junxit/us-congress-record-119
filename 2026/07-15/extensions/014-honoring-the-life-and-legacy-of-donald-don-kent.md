# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DONALD "DON" KENT

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DONALD ``DON'' KENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to 
honor the life and legacy of my dear friend Donald ``Don'' Kent, a 
devoted husband, father, grandfather, great-grandfather, Marine, 
entrepreneur, artist, and faithful servant whose life reflected the 
values of faith, family, and service.
  Born in Norfolk, Virginia, Don lived a life defined by integrity and 
hard work. He proudly served our Nation in the United States Marine 
Corps, where he developed the discipline and resilience that guided him 
throughout his life. Following his military service, he attended the 
University of Maryland before embarking on a successful career as an 
entrepreneur.
  Don also dedicated 25 years to his passion for art. Alongside his 
beloved wife, Jo Ann, he traveled the country sharing his artwork and 
touching countless lives through his creativity, warmth, and kindness. 
I am proud to have his paintings hanging in my home and office, daily 
reminders of his talent and of our friendship. Known for his quick wit 
and generous spirit, Don lived out his faith through humility, 
compassion, and service to others.
  Our families grew close through our sons, who have been best friends 
for years, and I came to know Don not only as a talented artist but as 
a man of remarkable warmth and character. His friendship was a gift to 
me and my family, and his loss is felt deeply.
  Don Kent's legacy of service, generosity, and unwavering love will 
live on through his family, friends, and all who were blessed to know 
him. I extend my deepest condolences to his loved ones as they honor 
his remarkable life.

                          ____________________
```
