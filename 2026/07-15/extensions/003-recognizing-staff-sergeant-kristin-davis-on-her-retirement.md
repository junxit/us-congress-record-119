# RECOGNIZING STAFF SERGEANT KRISTIN DAVIS ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING STAFF SERGEANT KRISTIN DAVIS ON HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Staff 
Sergeant Kristin Davis of the Burnet County Sheriff's Office for her 
dedicated service to the people of Burnet County and Central Texas, on 
her retirement.
  For more than 22 years, Staff Sergeant Davis has served her community 
with professionalism, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to public 
safety. She began her career with the Burnet County Sheriff's Office at 
just 19 years old as a dispatcher. Over the years, she has served in 
nearly every facet of the department, including as a jailer, patrol 
deputy, patrol corporal, Criminal Investigations Division investigator, 
and Administrative Staff Sergeant. Her career is a testament to her 
work ethic and her willingness to serve wherever she was needed.
  Those who know her best often say she grew up in the Burnet County 
Sheriff's Office, and after more than two decades of service, it is 
easy to see why. She has dedicated her career to protecting her 
community and supporting those around her.
  Mr. Speaker, public servants like Staff Sergeant Kristin Davis make 
our communities stronger and safer. Her leadership, professionalism, 
and dedication have earned the respect of her colleagues and the 
gratitude of the people she serves.
  On behalf of the people of Texas's 31st Congressional District, I 
thank Staff Sergeant Davis for her years of faithful service and 
congratulate her on this well-deserved recognition. I wish her all the 
best in the years to come and thank her for the example she has set for 
others who choose a life of public service.

                          ____________________
```
