# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SERGEANT JAMES DAWSON

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
         HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF SERGEANT JAMES DAWSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 15, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Sergeant James Dawson, a proud World War II veteran whose 
courage, selflessness, and devotion to our Nation exemplified the 
highest ideals of American service.
  Born in Higgins, Texas, on March 21, 1921, Sergeant James Dawson 
answered the call to serve during World War II with unwavering 
dedication. His extraordinary bravery earned him the World War II 
Victory Medal after he courageously saved the lives of two fellow 
servicemembers, placing their safety above his own in the face of grave 
danger. Sergeant James Dawson embodied what it means to be an American. 
Through his sacrifice, courage, and steadfast commitment to others, he 
demonstrated the values that have long defined our Nation's Greatest 
Generation. His actions serve as a lasting reminder of the selfless 
service and heroism displayed by so many who fought to preserve the 
freedoms we enjoy today.
  On behalf of the 31st Congressional District of Texas, I extend my 
deepest gratitude for Sergeant James Dawson's faithful service to our 
country and honor the remarkable life he lived. May his legacy of 
courage, patriotism, and sacrifice continue to inspire generations to 
come.
```
