# SAVE Act (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                SAVE Act

  Now, let me talk a little bit about the SAVE Act. Republicans keep 
screwing around in reconciliation with Trump's voter suppression SAVE 
Act. Republicans should know by now that it ain't going to fly with us, 
with the courts, or with the American people. I will say it as many 
times as it takes: The SAVE Act is dead on arrival here in the Senate.
  I don't care how Republicans try to package their plan to resurrect 
the old ghost of Jim Crowe. We will kill it.
  Trump's blatant effort to strip Americans of their voting rights is 
withering in the courts, too. Yesterday--just yesterday--Trump lost his 
14th and 15th cases trying to demand state voter rolls.
  Hey, Donald, what you are doing is illegal and unconstitutional, and 
the courts are making that very clear.
  Rightwing Republicans like the Senator from Utah can bluster all they 
want, but the SAVE Act is not going to become law. Trump will have to 
face all the voters, not just the ones he wants, in November.
  And the mess the Republicans find themselves in right now won't go 
away until they realize just how futile this effort to disenfranchise 
millions of Americans is.
  Instead, Trump has bragged on national TV that he plans to force a 
shutdown if he doesn't get his way on the SAVE Act. Trump said: 
Republicans have to pass SAVE, otherwise we are going to have a 
shutdown in November.
  Hear that, Republicans? You hear that, Americans? You hear that, 
voters?
  Trump says he is going to shut down the government to take away 
Americans' voting rights. Are Republicans going to go along with that?
  I remind Donald again--as I did yesterday--last time you bragged 
about shutting down the government with Nancy and me in the Oval Office 
didn't come out too good for you, Donald. This one will come out even 
worse.
  So I hope Republicans hear this. Trump says he is going to shut down 
the government so he can take away Americans' voting rights. How do you 
think that is going to go over in this country?
  Democrats aren't saying we want a shutdown; Trump is. Republicans 
ought to do themselves a favor and bury the SAVE Act once and for all.
```
