# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, Sovereign over this Nation and Lord of our lives, our 
desire is to glorify You through faithful service to our country. Grant 
us an earnest spirit and steadfast purpose in our work today.
  Lord, help us to know Your will, and having known it, to desire it 
above our own. Teach us to say what we mean and to mean what we say, 
speaking with wisdom, integrity, and praise. Grant our lawmakers 
resoluteness, intentionality, and courage. Quiet every distraction so 
that they may listen attentively to Your voice and speak with 
conviction and compassion.
  We pray in Your righteous and holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
