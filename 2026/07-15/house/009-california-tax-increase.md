# CALIFORNIA TAX INCREASE

**Speaking:** Kiley, Kevin (K000401) [I-CA]

```
                        CALIFORNIA TAX INCREASE

  (Mr. KILEY of California asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. KILEY of California. Mr. Speaker, many Californians were shocked 
to

[[Page H4472]]

learn that our legislature and Governor have just imposed what is being 
called the largest tax increase in State history, which is saying quite 
a lot in a State that already has the highest taxes in the country.
  Included in that is a massive new healthcare tax that is estimated to 
raise the healthcare premiums for many Californians by hundreds of 
dollars. This is something that our State simply cannot afford. When we 
already have the highest cost of living in the country, this is the 
last thing that our families need.
  What is more, it violates, at the very least, the spirit if not the 
letter of Proposition 35, passed by voters to limit precisely this kind 
of a tax increase.
  Fortunately, there is a way we can fight back. This new change has to 
be approved by regulators at the Federal level, and so myself and my 
colleagues are asking HHS--and we will be conveying this in a letter--
specifically to block the implementation of this plan, to block this 
tax proposal so that Californians do not suffer another blow to both 
our democracy and our cost of living.

                          ____________________
```
