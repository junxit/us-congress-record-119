# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 54, S. 1078, H.R. 1514, S. 1604

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-108. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Kansas, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution 
     No. 1604, requesting the Congress of the United States call a 
     convention of the states to propose amendments to the 
     Constitution of the United States; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
       ML-109. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 54, 
     to support H.R. 1514 and S. 1078 of the 119th Congress, 
     entitled the ``Mississippi River Basin Fishery Commission 
     Act''; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
