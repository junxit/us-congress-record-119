# HONORING OFFICE INTERNS

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                              {time}  1720
                        HONORING OFFICE INTERNS

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, as I have recognized the excellent staff 
members who were working with me in this term, may I also recognize and 
thank the young people, college students, and recent college graduates 
who have labored in the inglorious role of intern doing day-to-day 
grunt work necessary but often boring.
  They have given months of their young lives on my behalf, and I am 
grateful. They are: Isabel Tenney, Anna Barlis, Piper Genkin, Mariana 
Ortega, Sebastian Rojas, Miles Genkin, Krisna Rivera, Tim Manson, and 
Tommy Cole.
  Mr. Speaker, I trust this experience was a good one and that in the 
years to come, they will achieve great professional and personal 
success.

                          ____________________
```
