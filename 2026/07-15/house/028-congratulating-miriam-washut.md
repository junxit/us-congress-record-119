# CONGRATULATING MIRIAM WASHUT

**Speaking:** Hageman, Harriet M. (H001096) [R-WY]

```
                      CONGRATULATING MIRIAM WASHUT

  (Ms. HAGEMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. HAGEMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
high school senior Miriam Washut of Lander on winning the grand prize 
in the Presidential 1776 Award.
  This national civics contest challenges high school students across 
the country on their knowledge of the Founders, the Revolutionary War, 
and the documents that delivered the birth of our Republic.
  Miriam demonstrated her superior knowledge of America's founding at 
every stage of this national competition, earning her a well-earned 
prize of a $150,000 scholarship and a trip to the Oval Office where she 
told the President her plans to attend Wyoming Catholic College.
  Mr. Speaker, 250 years after the Declaration of Independence was 
signed, students like Miriam show the promise of our revolutionary 
principles carried on by the next generation for another 250 years.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Miriam for making Wyoming proud with her 
profound interest in America's founding.
```
