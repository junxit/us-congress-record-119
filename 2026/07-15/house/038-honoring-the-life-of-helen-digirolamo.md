# HONORING THE LIFE OF HELEN DiGIROLAMO

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF HELEN DiGIROLAMO

  (Mr. FITZPATRICK asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, it is with profound sadness that I rise 
today to honor the life and legacy of Helen DiGirolamo of Bensalem, 
Bucks County, Pennsylvania, who we sadly lost on July 10, 2026, at the 
age of 98.
  Born in Philadelphia in 1928, Helen was a cherished member of the St. 
Ephrem Church in Bensalem for 55 years, where she rooted her life in 
faith, in service, and in her community.
  In her younger years, she loved bowling. Throughout her life, she was 
known for her exceptional cooking, especially during the holiday 
gatherings with her family surrounding her at the table.
  Helen was preceded in death by her beloved husband of 45 years, 
Eugene, and their daughter, Janice. She is survived by her children, 
Gene and Joanne; her son-in-law, Michael; seven grandchildren; and 11 
great-grandchildren.
  Mr. Speaker, her son, Gene, was so good to her in all of her years, 
particularly her later years, when every single Sunday he dedicated to 
spending time with her.
  Helen DiGirolamo will be deeply missed, but her spirit will live on 
in the family and the community she so dearly loved.

                          ____________________
```
