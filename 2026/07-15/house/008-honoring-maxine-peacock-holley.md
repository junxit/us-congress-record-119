# HONORING MAXINE PEACOCK HOLLEY

**Speaking:** Moore, Tim (M001236) [R-NC]

```
                     HONORING MAXINE PEACOCK HOLLEY

  (Mr. MOORE of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MOORE of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor the 
life and memory of Maxine Peacock Holley, a devoted public servant of 
Benson, North Carolina, who passed away at the age of 82.
  Maxine dedicated her life to serving others as a teacher's aide, a 
magistrate judge, and a Benson town commissioner. She was a 
trailblazer. She was the first woman to chair the Benson Housing 
Authority and made history as Johnston County's first successful write-
in candidate elected to public office.
  With deep faith, Maxine showed what it means to love your neighbor. 
Even while battling cancer, she continued to serve with grace and an 
unwavering commitment to others.
  Above all, Maxine was devoted to her family. She was a proud mother 
to Reggie, Nikita, and Shawn, and she instilled in them that same 
spirit of service.
  Mr. Speaker, as we mourn her passing, we also celebrate a life well 
lived: a life of faith, a life of service, and a life of selflessness. 
I ask my colleagues to join me in remembering her and thanking her for 
her public service.

                          ____________________
```
