# CONGRATULATING INDIANA FFA STATE OFFICERS

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
               CONGRATULATING INDIANA FFA STATE OFFICERS

  (Mr. BAIRD asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate two State FFA officers 
from the Fourth District who were recently elected at the Indiana State 
convention.
  FFA is the largest U.S. career and technical student organization. 
FFA is rooted in farming and preparing youth for careers in 
agribusiness, science, veterinary medicine, and using hands-on learning 
techniques.
  Katie McClain from Carroll High School was elected as secretary. She 
is the granddaughter of a former Indiana State representative, Rich 
McClain, who I had the privilege of serving with.
  She is the 2026 Lilly Scholar winner, which has a full ride to Purdue 
University. Katie was the president of her local FFA chapter at Carroll 
and the district vice president last year.
  Jessie Frazee from Fountain Central High School was elected as the 
Northern Region vice president. Jessie was the local FFA president at 
Fountain Central High and the Fourth District president this past year. 
Jessie is the first Fountain FFA chapter member to become a State 
officer.
  Congratulations to these two amazing Hoosiers. I think they are in 
the audience. If they are, I ask that they please raise their hand or 
stand.
  I congratulate them.

                          ____________________
```
