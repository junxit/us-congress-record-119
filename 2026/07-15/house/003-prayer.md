# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, it is good to give thanks to You as we start this day. O 
Lord most high, we pause to give praise to Your name. This morning, may 
we begin our work declaring Your steadfast love so that by day's end we 
will bear witness to Your faithfulness.
  Establish us upon Your sure foundation. Orient us to Your presence in 
our everyday affairs and to Your engagement in every aspect of our 
lives, that we may realize that there is nothing that escapes Your 
attention. You have Your hand on all that transpires in this room.
  Where there is uncertainty, may we look to You for guidance. Where 
there is misunderstanding, may we open our hearts to Your wisdom. Where 
there is falsehood, reveal Your truth. Where there is disagreement, 
supply us with words of reconciliation, and where there is animosity, 
show us how to invite Your peace into our discourse.
  Lord, You alone are upright. May we stand today on the rock of Your 
righteousness and pray in the strength of Your name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
