# HONORING OFFICER EDGAR SALAZAR

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
                     HONORING OFFICER EDGAR SALAZAR

  (Mr. NEWHOUSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor Moses Lake Police 
Officer Edgar Salazar for his public service and congratulate him on 
being named the 2026 Hometown Hero by the U.S. Attorney's Office for 
the Eastern District of Washington.
  This well-deserved award is in recognition of Officer Salazar's 
heroic actions at the Gorge Amphitheater Campground in 2023, when he 
put his life on the line and helped to prevent a mass shooting.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Officer Salazar for the remarkable courage he 
displayed that day in saving dozens of lives and for his continued 
service to the Moses Lake community. He represents the best in law 
enforcement and we are all grateful for his service.

                          ____________________
```
