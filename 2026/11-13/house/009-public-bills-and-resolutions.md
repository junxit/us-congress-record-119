# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 11033, H.R. 11034, H.R. 11035, H.R. 11036

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
       H.R. 11033. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to include qualified domestic film or television 
     production as a qualified production activity for purposes of 
     the special allowance for qualified production property; to 
     the Committee on Ways and Means.
           By Mrs. FLETCHER (for herself and Mr. Weber of Texas):
       H.R. 11034. A bill to establish the National Fish and 
     Wildlife Foundation Coastal State Fund; to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.
           By Mr. LARSEN of Washington:
       H.R. 11035. A bill to amend the Public Health Service Act 
     to direct the Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting 
     through the Assistant Secretary for Mental Health and 
     Substance Use, to award grants to support community-based 
     programs for harm reduction services for individuals who use 
     substances; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. WALKINSHAW:
       H.R. 11036. A bill to require Federal agency heads to 
     submit an annual assessment on staffing needs of information 
     technology contracting officers and contracting officer's 
     representatives, to require the heads to submit a plan on 
     information technology acquisition workforce staffing and 
     development using the assessments, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.

                          ____________________
```
