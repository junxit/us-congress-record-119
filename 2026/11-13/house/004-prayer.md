# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  O God, our provider, let this be the day that we seek You. May we 
cease our own pursuits and pursue the righteousness that You desire 
from us.
  Today, may we show our faithfulness to You in the way we love one 
another: family members, friends, strangers, and even our enemies. May 
we strive to demonstrate that love which You have so mercifully shown 
to us.
  Then, we ask for the reward of a life lived in You, where our hunger 
for decency and civility is satisfied, where our thirst for integrity 
and honesty is quenched.
  Bless us this day and reveal to us that all that we seek and 
everything we desire is found in You.
  In Your sure and certain name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
