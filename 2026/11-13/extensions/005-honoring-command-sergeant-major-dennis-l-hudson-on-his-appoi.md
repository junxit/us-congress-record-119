# HONORING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR DENNIS L. HUDSON ON HIS APPOINTMENT AS GARRISON COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR OF FORT BRAGG

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
HONORING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR DENNIS L. HUDSON ON HIS APPOINTMENT AS 
             GARRISON COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR OF FORT BRAGG

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 13, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Command 
Sergeant Major (CSM) Dennis Hudson on his appointment as Garrison 
Command Sergeant Major of Fort Bragg.
  It is an honor to welcome CSM Hudson back to Fort Bragg, the home of 
the Airborne and Special Operations Forces, and the largest military 
installation in the world. CSM Hudson began his military career after 
enlisting in the U.S. Army in July 1998, attending training at Fort 
Benning, Georgia. Throughout his career, CSM Hudson was assigned to the 
101st Team, 4th Ranger Training Battalion, 2nd Infantry Division, 
Defense POW/MIA Accounting Agency, Combined Joint Task Force--Operation 
Inherent Resolve, and the 82nd Airborne Division, among others.
  CSM Hudson deployed to Iraq and Afghanistan four times and has been 
decorated for his service, including receiving the Bronze Star Medal, 
Purple Heart, and Defense Meritorious Service Medal with one Oak Leaf. 
Throughout his career, he also earned the Ranger Tab, Combat 
Infantryman Badge, Expert Infantryman Badge, Pathfinder Badge, Senior 
Parachutist Badge, and the Air Assault Badge. His diverse military 
education includes Combat Life Saver Course, Basic Leader Course, 
Senior Tactical Instructor Course, and Brigade CSM Pre-Command Course.
  CSM Hudson's devotion to our country is admirable and his appointment 
is well-earned. I wish him continued success and look forward to 
celebrating his future achievements.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today to congratulate CSM Hudson and 
thank him for his dedication and continued service to our country.
```
