# HONORING THE FIRST AFRICAN METHODIST EPISCOPAL ZION CHURCH

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

```
       HONORING THE FIRST AFRICAN METHODIST EPISCOPAL ZION CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 13, 2026

  Ms. LOFGREN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the public service of the 
First African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church of San Jose, CA, as it 
celebrates 162 years of service to our community.
  The African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church, the Mother Church, was 
founded in New York City in October 1796 and came to be known as the 
``Freedom Church'' for its unwavering commitment to the dignity and the 
emancipation of Black Americans. Its legacy of justice, empowerment, 
and faith has shaped countless lives across generations.
  Founded in San Jose in 1864, the First American Methodist Episcopal 
Zion Church has carried forward this profound legacy. Recognized as the 
oldest Black church in San Jose, it has stood as a historic institution 
devoted to advancing equity, nurturing community, and strengthening the 
moral and spiritual fabric of the city.
  For more than a century and a half, the First African Methodist 
Episcopal Zion Church has provided service, guidance, fellowship, 
stewardship, educational support, economic assistance, and spiritual 
encouragement to the people of San Jose. The church has served as a 
welcoming and steadfast pillar of community life, opening its doors to 
all.
  On November 15, 2026, the church will celebrate its 162nd 
anniversary. This milestone commemorates not only its enduring presence 
but also its ongoing efforts to strengthen, support, and uplift the San 
Jose community.
  The First African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church has stood for 
devotion, perseverance, and civic leadership. Through its outreach and 
its enduring dedication to justice and service, the church has enriched 
countless lives. Its longstanding contributions will continue to be 
honored with deep gratitude and appreciation throughout the San Jose 
community.
```
