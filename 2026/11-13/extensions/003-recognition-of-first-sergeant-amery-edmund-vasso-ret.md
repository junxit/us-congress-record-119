# RECOGNITION OF FIRST SERGEANT AMERY EDMUND VASSO (RET.)

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
        RECOGNITION OF FIRST SERGEANT AMERY EDMUND VASSO (RET.)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 13, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize First 
Sergeant Amery Edmund Vasso (Ret.) of Liberty Hill, Texas, as a 
recipient of the Congressional Veteran Commendation. His decades of 
service to our Nation, fellow veterans, and his community reflect a 
lifelong commitment to serving others.
  Mr. Vasso served in the United States Army for more than 23 years, 
retiring as a First Sergeant. Throughout his military career, he served 
in assignments across the United States and overseas, including service 
in Operation Desert Storm and Operation Iraqi Freedom. He also attended 
the United States Sergeants Major Academy and served with the 
Asymmetric Warfare Group, supporting its mission to advise combat 
forces. In recognition of his distinguished service, he received 
numerous honors, including the Legion of Merit, Meritorious Service 
Medal, Army Commendation Medal, and Combat Action Badge.
  Following his retirement from the Army, Mr. Vasso continued his 
commitment to service. He spent 14 years as a volunteer firefighter, 
traveled to Haiti and Nicaragua to help drill freshwater wells, and 
served with the All-Stars Project/Development School for Youth and All 
Veterans Memorial. He has been especially involved with the American 
Legion, serving as District and County Commander and Post Commander. 
His efforts have supported Operation Stand Down, the Temple Veterans 
Home, and the Williamson County Veterans Services office. He also 
raised $47,600 over three years to support scholarship programs for 
American Legion Post 174 and VFW Post 8587 in Georgetown, Texas.
  I am honored to recognize First Sergeant Amery Edmund Vasso for his 
service to our Nation and community and congratulate him on receiving 
the Congressional Veteran Commendation.
```
