# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MERL CRAFT

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MERL CRAFT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 13, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today along with my colleague, 
Congressman DeSaulnier, to recognize and celebrate the life of Marilyn 
``Merl'' Craft. Merl's life and legacy are immeasurable. A lifelong 
resident of Pittsburg, Merl served the community as a mayor, 
councilmember, City Recreation Supervisor, and volunteer.
  Over the course of nearly 50 years, Merl dedicated herself to the 
community of Pittsburg, working to build partnerships between 
government, community, and faith-based organizations. Merl tirelessly 
sought to empower local youth, strengthen families, and to connect 
residents with resources and opportunities. As a councilmember and 
mayor, Merl secured investment in the local community, notably in 
programs such as My Brother's Keeper Pittsburg, the Pittsburg Dream 
Courts, the Pittsburg Senior Center, and the Pittsburg Marina Community 
Center. Each project reflected Merl's lifelong goal of creating safe 
community spaces where people could stay active, engage with others, 
and build lasting connections.
  Beyond her service in local government, Merl founded Open 
Opportunities, a nonprofit dedicated to providing young adults with 
mentoring and career training through the Future Build Pre-Apprentice 
Training Program. Merl's work played a key role in expanding 
opportunities for women in the building trades. Across all her 
endeavors, Merl embodied the belief that there is no greater honor than 
serving others.
  Merl's love for and dedication to the Pittsburg community changed 
lives across the city. Please join us in extending our heartfelt 
condolences to Merl's family and to all those whose lives she touched. 
We thank Merl for her service, her leadership, and her unwavering 
commitment to the people of Pittsburg. Her story will continue to 
inspire generations to come, and she will never be forgotten.

                          ____________________
```
