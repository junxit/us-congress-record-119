# HONORING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR DANIEL SCOTT

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
              HONORING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR DANIEL SCOTT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 13, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Daniel Scott for 
his service as Garrison Command Sergeant Major (CSM) at Fort Bragg, 
North Carolina.
  CSM Scott enlisted in the U.S. Army in May 1997 as an Infantryman. 
CSM Scott went on to serve in the 10th Mountain Division, 25th Light 
Infantry Division, 101st Airborne Division and the 82nd Airborne 
Division. During this period, he deployed to Bosnia-Herzegovina and 
Afghanistan three times.
  In recognition of his service, Scott was awarded the Bronze Star 
Medal, Defense Meritorious Service Medal, Joint Service Commendation 
Medal, Army Achievement Medal, Afghanistan Campaign Medal, and Global 
War on Terrorism Medal among other honors.
  As Fort Bragg's Congressman, I have had the pleasure of working side 
by side with CSM Scott during his time at Fort Bragg. Under his 
leadership, Fort Bragg advanced its readiness, safety and operational 
effectiveness. Through his professionalism and dedication, he improved 
the quality of life for those who live, work, and serve on the 
installation.
  CSM Scott is a great soldier and great American who put duty, our 
national security, and the wellbeing of our warfighters above all else. 
I am extremely grateful for his service to our nation.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in thanking Command Sergeant Major 
Daniel Scott for his years of distinguished service to the United 
States.
```
