# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2026, THROUGH MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 3633

```
ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2026, THROUGH MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 
                                  2026

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate adjourns on Thursday, September 10, it stand adjourned until 3 
p.m. on Monday, September 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
at 5:30 p.m., the cloture motion with respect to the Byrne nomination 
ripen; finally, notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motion with 
respect to the motion to proceed to Calendar No. 423, H.R. 3633 ripen 
on Tuesday, September 15, at 2:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
