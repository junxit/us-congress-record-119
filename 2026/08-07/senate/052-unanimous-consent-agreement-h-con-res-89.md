# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H. CON. RES. 89

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 89

```
              UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H. CON. RES. 89

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, at a time to be determined by the majority 
leader in concurrence with the Democratic leader, no later than Friday, 
October 2, H. Con Res. 89 be discharged from the Committee on Foreign 
Relations and the Senate proceed to its consideration; further, there 
be 6 hours of debate only, with time divided between the leaders or 
their designees on the concurrent resolution and that following the use 
or yielding back of that time, the Senate vote on adoption of the 
concurrent resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
