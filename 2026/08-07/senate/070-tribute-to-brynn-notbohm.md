# TRIBUTE TO BRYNN NOTBOHM

**Speaking:** Sheehy, Tim (S001232) [R-MT]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BRYNN NOTBOHM

● Mr. SHEEHY. Madam President, I would like to take a moment to 
express my appreciation to Brynn Notbohm for her hard work as an intern 
in my Billings office this summer.

for me and my staff. It has been a pleasure having her as part of our 
team this summer, and I wish her all the best as she continues to build 
a bright future for herself.●
```
