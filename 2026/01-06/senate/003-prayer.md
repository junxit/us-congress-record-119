# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our hope in times of praise and privation, guide our lawmakers 
on each step of their pilgrimage. Make them supreme in compassion, 
mercy, and love. Give them fellowship with You, one another, and their 
constituents. Bring our Senators more and more into oneness with You, 
fellowship with Your suffering, and obedience to Your commands. Fill 
them with the spirit of Your peace. In their weakness, give strength. 
In their anxiety, give serenity. In their discouragement, give 
optimism.
  Lord, we thank You for the police who helped defend the Capitol on 
January 6, 2021. Continue to strengthen and protect them in all of 
their tomorrows.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
