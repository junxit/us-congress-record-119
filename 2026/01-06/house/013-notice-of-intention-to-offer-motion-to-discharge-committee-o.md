# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 780

**Speaking:** Jeffries, Hakeem S. (J000294) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 780

```
  NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES 
           FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 780

  Mr. JEFFRIES. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, I rise 
to provide notice of my intent to offer a motion to discharge the 
Committee on Rules from the further consideration of House Resolution 
780.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Does did the gentleman sign the petition?
  Mr. JEFFRIES. Yes, Mr. Speaker, I did.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, a motion 
to discharge is privileged only at a time designated by the Speaker 
within 2 legislative days after the intent to offer such motion is 
offered.
  The Chair will entertain the gentleman's motion at that time.

                          ____________________
```
