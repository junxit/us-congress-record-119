# RECOGNIZING LINDER FARM NETWORK

**Speaking:** Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

```
                    RECOGNIZING LINDER FARM NETWORK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD FINSTAD

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. FINSTAD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Linder Farm 
Network--a true staple of our southern Minnesota agricultural 
community--and congratulate them on celebrating 50 years on air.
  For decades, the Linder Farm Network has been a trusted voice for 
farmers, producers, and rural families across corn and soybean country.
  What started as a vision by Lynn Ketelsen and the Linder family on 
four local stations has grown into a network of 39 stations covering 89 
counties.
  Through the last half century of planting seasons, harvests, market 
swings, and industry changes, Linder Farm Network has been in trucks 
and tractor cabs with southern Minnesota producers, delivering the 
critical market data, weather updates, and agricultural programming 
that farm country relies on.
  The dedication of Lynn and the Linder team has earned them the top-
rated farm network in the state and I'm proud to commend them on fifty 
years of service to keeping our rural communities connected.
  I congratulate the entire team at Linder Farm Network on celebrating 
this incredible milestone. Wishing them all continued success for the 
next 50 years and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
