# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CORPORAL LEE HIGGINBOTHAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CORPORAL LEE HIGGINBOTHAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Corporal Lee Higginbotham. He is an Army Veteran with over 13 
years of service who now serves as a corporal with the Dallas Veterans 
Affairs Police Department.

[[Page E950]]

  He was in the right place at the right time, stopping to fuel up his 
car, when he noticed a small crowd gathered and quickly realized a 
child was in distress. Without hesitation, he jumped into action, 
performing the Heimlich maneuver and ultimately saving the life of a 
choking 2-year-old.
  His heroic actions are a powerful reminder of the courage, training, 
and compassion of the officers who protect our community every day. I 
thank Corporal Higginbotham not only for his quick thinking and bravery 
in this moment, but for his years of military service and his continued 
dedication to keeping North Texas safe.

                          ____________________
```
