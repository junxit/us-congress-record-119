# HONORING THE FORT WORTH BOTANIC GARDEN ON THEIR PRESTIGIOUS ACCREDITATION FROM THE AMERICAN ALLIANCE OF MUSEUMS

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
      HONORING THE FORT WORTH BOTANIC GARDEN ON THEIR PRESTIGIOUS 
          ACCREDITATION FROM THE AMERICAN ALLIANCE OF MUSEUMS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Fort 
Worth Botanic Garden for achieving accreditation from the American 
Alliance of Museums, a prestigious distinction representing the highest 
standards of excellence for museums across the Nation.
  This accomplishment is a testament to the dedication, expertise, and 
years of effort from the leadership, staff, volunteers, and community 
partners at the Fort Worth Botanic Garden. The accreditation process is 
highly rigorous, with only a select few museums nationwide meeting the 
criteria necessary to receive this distinguished honor.
  The Fort Worth Botanic Garden has been designated as a specialized 
museum for its outstanding work in preserving living plant collections, 
advancing conservation initiatives, and offering educational programs 
that foster a deeper appreciation for the natural world. In partnership 
with the Botanical Research Institute of Texas, the Fort Worth Botanic 
Garden continues to support impactful research while providing 
opportunities for discovery and engagement throughout the community.
  This achievement reflects the Fort Worth Botanic Garden's important 
role as both a cultural landmark and an educational resource for Fort 
Worth and beyond. President and CEO Patrick Newman, along with the 
entire Fort Worth Botanic Garden team, have helped cultivate an 
institution that protects natural resources, inspires future 
generations, and creates meaningful experiences for visitors from 
across the globe.
  With this accreditation, the Fort Worth Botanic Garden joins the 
distinguished group of Fort Worth institutions in the cultural 
district, including the Amon Carter Museum of American Art, the Kimbell 
Art Museum, the Modern Art Museum of Fort Worth, and the Sid Richardson 
Museum, which have each earned this esteemed recognition.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
congratulate the Fort Worth Botanic Gardens on this incredible 
achievement.

                          ____________________
```
