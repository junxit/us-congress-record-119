# CONGRATULATING FIRE CHIEF RAYMOND HILL ON HIS RETIREMENT FOLLOWING 30 YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
 CONGRATULATING FIRE CHIEF RAYMOND HILL ON HIS RETIREMENT FOLLOWING 30 
                            YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Fort Worth Fire Chief Raymond Hill on his retirement 
following more than 30 years of dedicated service to the City of Fort 
Worth and its residents. Chief Hill began his career with the Fort 
Worth Fire Department in December 1994 as a firefighter and steadily 
advanced through each rank. Over the past three decades, he has led 
critical operations, including firefighter training, emergency 
management, health and wellness, fire apparatus services, and the Fire 
Alarm Office.
  In October 2025, the Fort Worth City Council unanimously appointed 
Chief Hill as interim fire chief. During his tenure, he guided the 
department through its transition to full responsibility for emergency 
medical services and helped reduce average Emergency Medical Services 
response times by approximately 32 percent over the past year.
  Chief Hill's career is a testament to the value of public service, 
steady leadership, and a deep commitment to the community. For more 
than three decades, he has dedicated himself to protecting the people 
of Fort Worth and supporting the firefighters who serve alongside him.
  As Chief Hill begins this next chapter, I extend my sincere gratitude 
for his years of service and sacrifice Fort Worth is stronger because 
of his leadership, and his contributions to the community will be 
remembered for years to come.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
congratulate Chief Raymond Hill and wish him and his family happiness 
and all the best in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
