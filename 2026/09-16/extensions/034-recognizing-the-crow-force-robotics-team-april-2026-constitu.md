# RECOGNIZING THE CROW FORCE ROBOTICS TEAM, APRIL 2026 CONSTITUENTS OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE CROW FORCE ROBOTICS TEAM, APRIL 2026 CONSTITUENTS OF 
                               THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Crow Force Robotics 
Team, Team No. 10809, from Sage Creek High School in Carlsbad, 
California, as my April 2026 Constituents of the Month.
  These 19 students are proving that when young people are given the 
tools, mentorship, and encouragement to pursue their passions, 
extraordinary things happen. Placing first among 84 teams in the San 
Diego region and advancing to the FIRST Tech Challenge World 
Championships in Houston are remarkable achievements, and this is the 
first time a Carlsbad Unified School District team has reached this 
level of competition on placement.
  Crow Force is not just building robots. They are building the next 
generation of engineers, problem-solvers, and leaders. Their commitment 
to community outreach, mentoring younger students, and spreading a love 
of STEM makes them role models well beyond the competition floor.
  I wish the entire team the best of luck at the World Championships, 
and I know they will represent Carlsbad and our district with pride.

                          ____________________
```
