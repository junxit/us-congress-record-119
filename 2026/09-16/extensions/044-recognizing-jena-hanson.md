# RECOGNIZING JENA HANSON

**Speaking:** Finstad, Brad (F000475) [R-MN]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JENA HANSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD FINSTAD

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. FINSTAD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 1st 
District's own Jena Hanson, the newly crowned 73rd Princess Kay of the 
Milky Way.
  Princess Kay of the Milky Way serves as the goodwill ambassador for 
our Minnesota dairy farmers. Selected through a three-phase process, 
candidates compete throughout the year with hopes of being selected as 
a finalist prior to the Minnesota State Fair.
  Jena, from Owatonna and representing Steele County, was announced as 
the 2026 winner and will spend this next year helping connect dairy 
farmers with consumers across the state. She even had her likeness 
carved into a 90-pound block of butter displayed at the Great Minnesota 
Get-Together.
  The crowning of Princess Kay is a special tradition for the Finstad 
family, as my niece was the 61st Princess Kay and my wife served 
Watonwan County Dairy Princess.
  I congratulate Jena. I know she will represent both the 1st District 
and our dairy farmers well in this special role. Southern Minnesota and 
I are proud of her accomplishments.

                          ____________________
```
