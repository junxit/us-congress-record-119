# RECOGNIZING MICHAELLE JINNETTE, JULY 2026 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
   RECOGNIZING MICHAELLE JINNETTE, JULY 2026 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Michaelle Jinnette, a 
resident of Encinitas, as my Constituent of the Month for July 2026.
  When Michaelle's son Tristan was diagnosed with an ultra-rare genetic 
condition, she and her husband, Kevin, refused to accept that there was 
nothing to be done. Instead, they founded the Cure KCNH1 Foundation and 
set out to build related scientific research and inspire hope that did 
not yet exist for families like theirs.
  Michaelle's determination has connected families across the globe and 
helped launch new research into a disorder shared by fewer than 100 
children worldwide. She has shown that one parent's love, paired with 
tireless resolve, can move the frontiers of medicine forward.
  I urge my colleagues to join me in celebrating Michaelle Jinnette, 
and in thanking her for the strength, compassion, and leadership she 
brings to families facing rare disease everywhere.

                          ____________________
```
