# RECOGNIZING NATALIE HELLMANN

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING NATALIE HELLMANN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Natalie Hellmann for 
her service to the House of Representatives.
  A native of Alexandria, Virginia, Natalie graduated with a Bachelor 
of Arts in Business Management from Rollins College. After serving in 
multiple internships for Majority Whip Steve Scalise, Majority Leader 
Kevin McCarthy, and Senator Marco Rubio, her career on Capitol Hill has 
taken her to the offices of Representatives David P. Joyce (OH-14), 
Scott Franklin (FL-15), and Earl L. ``Buddy'' Carter (GA-01). Shortly 
after I became Chairman of the House Committee on Energy and Commerce, 
Natalie joined my staff serving as a Professional Staff Member on the 
Subcommittee on Commerce, Manufacturing, and Trade. In this role she 
quickly became a trusted advisor on issues related to Name, Image, and 
Likeness (NIL) legislation, collegiate and professional sports policy, 
and semiconductor manufacturing policy, among others.
  Natalie will soon depart the House Committee on Energy and Commerce 
to move to the West Coast. I wish her the best in her future endeavors 
and thank Natalie for her service to the United States House of 
Representatives, the Energy and Commerce Committee, and the American 
people.

                          ____________________
```
