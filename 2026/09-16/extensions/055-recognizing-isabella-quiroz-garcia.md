# RECOGNIZING ISABELLA QUIROZ GARCIA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                   RECOGNIZING ISABELLA QUIROZ GARCIA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Isabella Quiroz 
Garcia for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Isabella has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Isabella, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Isabella's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Jefferson Jr/Sr High School and is clearly just the 
beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Isabella Quiroz Garcia on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
