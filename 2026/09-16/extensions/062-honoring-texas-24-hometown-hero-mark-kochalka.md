# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MARK KOCHALKA

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO MARK KOCHALKA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Mark Kochalka. He has dedicated his life to service, with more 
than 25 years of distinguished service in the United States Navy. Mark 
new as a P-3C instructor pilot and Mission Commander, deploying 
multiple times to the Middle East. During his final tour in Iraq and 
Afghanistan, he led a special forces drone unit. His leadership earned 
recognition from both his peers and superiors.
  Today, Mark continues to serve North Texas in his role at Boeing; his 
work ensures that our service members have the technology they need to 
complete their missions and return home safely. Outside of work, he is 
a devoted husband, father, and active community member in Midway Hills.
  I thank Mark for his lifelong commitment to duty, leadership, and 
commitment to making North Texas stronger.

                          ____________________
```
