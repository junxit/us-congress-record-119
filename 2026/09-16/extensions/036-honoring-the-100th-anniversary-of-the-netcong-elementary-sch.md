# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NETCONG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
    HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NETCONG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Netcong Elementary 
School in Netcong, New Jersey as it celebrates its 100th anniversary. 
This remarkable milestone reflects a century of dedication to educating 
numerous generations of Netcong residents.
  Throughout these years, Netcong Elementary School has remained 
committed to its mission of nurturing the children of Netcong. Its 
teachers and faculty have continually inspired students, fostering a 
lifelong love of learning, a strong sense of community, and the 
confidence to shape their futures.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in commemorating Netcong 
Elementary School. We warmly congratulate the board, administration, 
staff, students, families, and all Netcong residents on this special 
occasion and celebrate the enduring strength and spirit of this 
invaluable educational institution.

                          ____________________
```
