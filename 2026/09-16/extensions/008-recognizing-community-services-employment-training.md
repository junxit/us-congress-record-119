# RECOGNIZING COMMUNITY SERVICES EMPLOYMENT TRAINING

```
           RECOGNIZING COMMUNITY SERVICES EMPLOYMENT TRAINING

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. VALADOA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Community 
Services Employment Training (CSET) on its 50th anniversary and to 
celebrate years of dedicated service to the people of Tulare County.

[[Page E937]]

  Founded in 1976 to address the War on Poverty, CSET was established 
to provide underserved families with the resources, skills, and 
opportunities they need to build better lives. Since then, CSET has 
remained committed to that mission while expanding its services to meet 
the evolving needs of Tulare County residents.
  Through its Employment Connection Locations, CSET has helped 
strengthen and develop the local workforce by offering job search 
assistance, career planning, vocational training scholarships, and 
other supportive workforce initiatives. The organization has also 
provided vital support to underserved community members through energy 
assistance and free tax preparation programs. These services help 
families afford essential heating, water, and electricity costs while 
ensuring they can file their annual tax returns at no expense.
  Over the past five decades, CSET's impact has been recognized through 
a wide variety of grant awards that have helped expand its ability to 
serve the community. In 2022, CSET received millions of dollars in 
grant funding from the Bezos Day 1 Families Fund to support its efforts 
to address homelessness and assist families in need. That same year, 
the U.S. Department of Labor's OSHA Susan Harwood Training Grant 
Program awarded CSET additional funding to expand its workplace safety 
education. The assistance received through this program has equipped 
vulnerable workers, temporary workers, and workers in high-hazard 
industries with the training needed to make workplaces around Tulare 
County safer and healthier.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in congratulating Community Services Employment Training on 50 
years of dedicated service to Tulare County. Through its diverse 
community initiatives and unwavering commitment to creating lasting 
change, CSET continues to make a meaningful difference in the lives of 
Central Valley families and truly embodies its core values of 
belonging, hope, growth, and connection.
```
