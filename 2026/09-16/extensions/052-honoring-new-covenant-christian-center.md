# HONORING NEW COVENANT CHRISTIAN CENTER

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING NEW COVENANT CHRISTIAN CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor New Covenant Christian 
Center and its founding Pastor, Michael Smith, for more than two 
decades of faithful ministry born from a divine vision and carried 
forward through years of prayer, sacrifice, and service to the people 
of Baker and Baton Rouge, Louisiana.
  On December 28, 2002, at 4:00 a.m., God awakened Pastor Smith and 
began to speak to him concerning a vision, instructing him to locate 
the scriptures that would become the foundation of his ministry and 
giving him the name New Covenant Christian Center. Pastor Smith carried 
this vision quietly, trusting that it was ordained for an appointed 
time. Remarkably, several anointed men and women of God who did not 
personally know Pastor Smith spoke prophetically about this very 
vision, and on December 8, 2001, his own pastor, Bishop Mary Alexander, 
likewise revealed the vision to him during a worship service, 
confirming what God had already spoken.
  In July 2003, Pastor Smith relocated to his home state of Louisiana, 
where God revealed that this would be the place where New Covenant 
Christian Center would be born. Throughout August 2003, Pastor Smith 
entered a season of spiritual fasting and consecration, seeking 
instruction, and it was during this time that God revealed to him the 
why, the how, and the when of the ministry to come. On Wednesday, 
October 1, 2003, Pastor Smith held the first Bible study meeting at the 
Cathedral of Faith on Oswego Street in Baton Rouge, gathered with 
family members and a few close friends. The following week, the meeting 
moved to the House of God Church on Paige Street in Baton Rouge, where 
it continued to grow as many were saved, set free, delivered, and 
changed by God's Word. On February 1, 2004, the ministry held its first 
official worship service on Main Street in Baker, Louisiana.
  As the congregation grew in faith and in number, New Covenant 
Christian Center acquired five acres of land in Baker on September 2, 
2005, for its future church home, and that land was paid in full by 
February 2006. On January 15, 2008, the ministry closed on a 14,000-
square-foot building on East Myrtle Avenue in Baker, which would be 
renovated to become its new sanctuary. Renovations began on May 5, 
2008, and on Sunday, April 5, 2009, the congregation held its first 
official service in the new sanctuary, where Pastor Smith delivered the 
anointed and powerful message, ``He Brought Us!!''
  New Covenant Christian Center has continued to expand its reach in 
the years since. On January 10, 2010, the ministry launched the Victory 
& Hope Television Broadcast, airing every Sunday on WBRL at 7:00 a.m. 
and extending the ministry's message well beyond its sanctuary walls. 
Recognizing the growing demand for Pastor Smith's anointed leadership, 
the ministry expanded its borders to the greater Baton Rouge area on 
October 17, 2010, with the birth of Jericho International Ministries, 
its second location.
  From a vision given in the middle of the night to a thriving ministry 
with two locations and a growing television broadcast, New Covenant 
Christian Center and Jericho International Ministries stand as a 
testament to what can happen when faith and obedience meet divine 
purpose. True to their calling, this ministry remains committed to 
leading lives unto Jesus Christ, reaching the lost, and increasing the 
Kingdom of God.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Pastor 
Michael Smith, New Covenant Christian Center, and Jericho International 
Ministries for more than two decades of vision, faith, and devoted 
service to the people of Baker, Baton Rouge, and the surrounding 
Louisiana community. May God continue to grace this ministry with His 
favor and anointing for many years to come.

                          ____________________
```
