# HONORING OFFICER DEVIN JONES ON BEING NAMED 2026 LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER OF THE YEAR BY THE AMERICAN LEGION DEPARTMENT OF TEXAS

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING OFFICER DEVIN JONES ON BEING NAMED 2026 LAW ENFORCEMENT 
     OFFICER OF THE YEAR BY THE AMERICAN LEGION DEPARTMENT OF TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize K-9 
Officer Devin Jones of the Weatherford Police Department for being 
named the 2026 Law Enforcement Officer of the Year by the American 
Legion Department of Texas.
  This distinguished honor recognizes law enforcement officers who 
demonstrate exceptional service, professional achievement, community 
involvement, and a commitment to duty beyond the expectations of their 
profession. Nominated by American Legion Post 163, Officer Jones has 
exemplified these qualities and more through his dedicated service to 
the citizens of Weatherford and Parker County.
  As a member of the Weatherford Police Department's K-9 Unit, Officer 
Jones has consistently demonstrated professionalism, integrity, and an 
unwavering commitment to public safety. His dedication to protecting 
the community and serving with distinction has earned him the respect 
of his fellow officers and the community he serves.
  Presented during the American Legion Department of Texas Annual 
Convention, this award reflects the high standards of one of our 
nation's most respected veterans organizations and underscores the 
significance of Officer Jones' accomplishments.
  Officer Jones' exemplary service has brought great credit to the 
Weatherford Police Department and the law enforcement profession. His 
dedication serves as an inspiration to those who wear the badge.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I extend my 
sincere congratulations to Officer Devin Jones on this well-deserved 
recognition and wish him continued success in his service to the people 
of Texas.

                          ____________________
```
