# ADVANCING WATER RESEARCH AND COLLABORATION ACT OF 2025

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 7889

```
         ADVANCING WATER RESEARCH AND COLLABORATION ACT OF 2025

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 14, 2026

  Ms. LOFGREN. Mr. Speaker, I support H.R. 7889, a bill to reauthorize 
the water resources research and technology institutes program.
  Over the last four decades, the Water Resources Research Act Program, 
administered by the United States Geological Survey (USGS), has 
supported a nationwide network of 54 Water Resources Research 
Institutes and Centers serving every state, the District of Columbia, 
Puerto Rico, the United States Virgin Islands, and Guam. Through this 
federal-state partnership, the program uses competitive grants to 
support water-resources research, technology transfer, information 
sharing, and the training of the next generation of water scientists 
and engineers.
  This work is increasingly important as communities across my district 
and the country face growing water-resource challenges. The USGS's 
first comprehensive national assessment of water availability found 
that every region in the lower 48 states faces limitations to water 
availability in at least one aspect of water quantity, quality, or 
flow. These challenges can create barriers to equitable access to clean 
water.
  At the same time, we must ensure that we have the workforce needed to 
meet these challenges. The USGS anticipates a shortage of science and 
technology professionals and recognizes that increasing stress on our 
Nation's water resources will require highly trained and skilled 
professionals in water resources and management.
  This is why I am encouraging all my colleagues, both in the House and 
the Senate, to support the Advancing Water Research and Collaboration 
Act of 2025. As Ranking Member of the Committee on Science, Space, and 
Technology. I can attest that reauthorizing this program and ensuring 
that we continue to invest in the environmental research, innovation, 
and workforce development needed to address water resource challenges 
are critical to our country's prosperity and quality of life.
```
