# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS FORT WORTH FIRE DEPARTMENT AND FORT WORTH POLICE DEPARTMENT

```
 HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS FORT WORTH FIRE DEPARTMENT AND FORT 
                        WORTH POLICE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms, VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Heros, the Fort Worth Fire Department and the Fort Worth Police 
Department. When devastating

[[Page E949]]

floods struck the Texas Hill Country over the July 4th weekend in 2025, 
the Fort Worth Fire Department and the Fort Worth Police Department 
didn't hesitate to answer the call for help. Organizing a large-scale 
donation drive across 45 fire and police stations, they mobilized the 
community to collect needed supplies for flood victims.
  For days, these dedicated teams gathered, sorted, and packaged 
thousands of donated goods, going above and beyond to ensure only the 
most requested and useful items made it to the families most in need. 
Their coordination, care, and sense of duty during this difficult time 
speak volumes about their commitment to service.
  I thank the Fort Worth Fire Department and the Fort Worth Police for 
stepping up in a time of need. Their actions reflect the very best of 
public service. I thank them for all they do for our North Texas 
community, and now, for those in the Hill Country too.

                          ____________________
```
