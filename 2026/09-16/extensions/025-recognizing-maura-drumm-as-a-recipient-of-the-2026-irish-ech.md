# RECOGNIZING MAURA DRUMM AS A RECIPIENT OF THE 2026 IRISH ECHO LABOR AWARD

**Speaking:** Boyle, Brendan F. (B001296) [D-PA]

```
  RECOGNIZING MAURA DRUMM AS A RECIPIENT OF THE 2026 IRISH ECHO LABOR 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRENDAN F. BOYLE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. BOYLE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Maura Drumm on being recognized as a 2026 Irish Echo Labor Awards 
honoree for her outstanding work as Media Coordinator and 
Communications Project Manager for the International Brotherhood of 
Teamsters. I am proud to say that

[[Page E942]]

Maura was born and raised in my district in Northeast Philadelphia, 
Pennsylvania.
  Maura earned a bachelor's degree from the Catholic University of 
America in 2017 and a master's degree from American University in 2020. 
Not long after graduation, Maura began working at the United States 
Department of Agriculture and at the Biden White House. During her time 
in the Biden Administration, she worked in both the Press Office and 
the White House Council on Environmental Quality (CEQ). At CEQ, Maura 
and her team advanced President Biden's goal of ensuring that the 
federal government's transition to clean energy and sustainable 
operations created and supported good-paying union jobs.
  Following in the footsteps of her father and grandfather, Maura 
became a proud third-generation Teamster last July when she took on her 
current role with the union. Since joining the Teamsters, Maura's hard 
work, kindness, and commitment to the labor movement have been 
recognized and appreciated by all in the workplace. Those important 
traits are exactly what propelled her to becoming a 2026 recipient of 
the Irish Echo Labor Awards.
  The Irish Echo--our Nation's oldest Irish American newspaper--
established the Labor Awards to recognize exceptional Irish American 
contributors to the United States labor movement. I can think of few 
individuals more deserving of this honor than Maura, who is joining 
remarkable company in receiving it. I know her parents, Christopher and 
Maureen, and her sisters, Bridget, Caitlin, and Erin, are proud.
  Mr. Speaker, it is altogether fitting that the House recognizes Maura 
Drumm for this incredible achievement.

                          ____________________
```
