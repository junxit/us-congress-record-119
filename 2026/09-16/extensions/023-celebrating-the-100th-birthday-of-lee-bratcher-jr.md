# CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF LEE BRATCHER, JR.

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
          CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF LEE BRATCHER, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to wish a very happy 100th 
birthday to a remarkable Kentuckian and United States Army veteran, Lee 
Bratcher, Jr.
  Born on September 16, 1926, in Halt County, Kentucky, Lee was the 
fourth of five children born to Roberta and Lee Bratcher, Sr. Growing 
up in rural Kentucky during the height of the Great Depression, Lee 
learned the value of hard work and responsibility at an early age. 
After completing eighth grade and while working on his family's farm, 
Lee registered for the Selective Service in 1943.
  At the age of 19, Lee was drafted into the United States Army in 
November 1945 to serve during World War II. After completing various 
trainings in preparation for his deployment, Lee went on to be 
stationed in Europe, primarily in Germany, as the War ended and the 
process of rebuilding the European continent began. After two years of 
dedicated military service, Lee returned home to the Commonwealth, 
marrying Betty Jean Reynolds on November 5, 1949. Together, they built 
a life centered around faith and community.
  Faith has been at the center of Lee's life, helping to guide and 
shape the spiritual life of those in his community. Over the years, he 
has served as a deacon, treasurer, and member of the building and 
pastor search committees at Lonoke Baptist Church in Horse Cave, 
Kentucky. Shortly after his return from Europe, Lee and his wife 
purchased their first farm in Hart County, growing tobacco and raising 
cattle. As a 30-year member of the Hart County Farm Bureau Board of 
Directors, Lee has held the role of president on three separate 
occasions, helping to shape agriculture policy in the county and 
Commonwealth.
  On October 7, 2019, Lee faced the heartbreaking loss of his wife, 
Betty. Their commitment to each other was evident throughout their 
lives, allowing them to be just one month shy of their 70th wedding 
anniversary. Her memory lives on in Lee and their three children, Pam, 
James, and Don. For 100 years, Lee Bratcher, Jr. has represented the 
very best of the American spirit. I wish Lee a happy 100th birthday and 
I thank him for a century of service to his family, his community, and 
our Nation.
```
