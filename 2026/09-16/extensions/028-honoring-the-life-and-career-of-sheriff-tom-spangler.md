# HONORING THE LIFE AND CAREER OF SHERIFF TOM SPANGLER

**Speaking:** Burchett, Tim (B001309) [R-TN]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND CAREER OF SHERIFF TOM SPANGLER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. TIM BURCHETT

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. BURCHETT. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor the life and career 
of former Knox County Sheriff Tom ``Spanky'' Spangler. A native of Knox 
County, Sheriff Spangler dedicated his entire life to public service.
  After following in his father's footsteps and joining the United 
States Air Force, Sheriff Spangler returned to Knox County in 1980 and 
joined the Sheriff's Office. During his career on the force, he held 
several positions from patrol officer to heading the narcotics 
division. On top of this, Sheriff Spangler earned his diploma from the 
FBI National Academy and started the Knox County Sheriff's aviation 
program. He also held certifications for operating both helicopters and 
fixed-wing planes.
  Following his retirement in 2009, Sheriff Spangler stayed active in 
the community as a member of the Clear Springs Baptist Church in 
Corryton. However, it was not long before he got back into service, 
being asked to serve as the Assistant Chief of Training for the Blount 
County Sheriff's Office. So, it was no surprise to anyone when he was 
easily elected twice, both in 2018 and 2022, as Knox County Sheriff.
  Tom is survived by his wife, Linda, their two daughters and sons-in-
law, and multiple grandchildren. He will be remembered for his life-
long commitment to law enforcement and unwavering support for East 
Tennessee.
```
