# RECOGNIZING THE BIG FLATS FIRE DEPARTMENT ON THE OCCASION OF ITS 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
RECOGNIZING THE BIG FLATS FIRE DEPARTMENT ON THE OCCASION OF ITS 100TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Big Flats Fire 
Department on the occasion of its 100th anniversary. For a century, the 
department has stood as a pillar of service, dedication, and commitment 
to the Big Flats community.
  Since its founding in 1926, the Big Flats Fire Department has played 
an essential role in protecting the lives and property of residents 
throughout the Southern Tier. Generations of firefighters have answered 
the call, responding to emergencies and providing critical assistance 
to their neighbors in times of need.
  The department's century of service reflects the courage and 
selflessness of its members, both past and present. Their willingness 
to volunteer their time and put the safety of others first represents 
the very best of our communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in recognizing the Big Flats Fire Department on its 100th 
anniversary and in extending our sincere gratitude to its members, past 
and present, for their century of dedicated service. Their commitment 
to protecting the Big Flats community is worthy of our highest 
appreciation.

                          ____________________
```
