# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ZACCHARY L. KNOLL

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF ZACCHARY L. KNOLL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life of Zacchary L. 
Knoll of Clay Township, who passed away after a brief illness on 
December 8, 2025, at the age of 42.
  Mr. Zacchary Knoll, or Zac, was a husband to Teal and father to 
Barrett. He shared 8 years of marriage with his wife and was the proud 
father of their 5-year-old son.
  He had a strong presence in the community where he worked for Denver 
Borough for a total of 18 years. He started as a Laborer Ill in the 
Department of Public Works and was promoted to Director of Public Works 
in 2022, a role in which he oversaw the borough's water, sewer, and 
roadway systems. In this capacity he contributed greatly to the health, 
safety, and wellbeing of the community and was an engaging, positive 
ambassador for the borough.
  In January 2026, Denver Borough Council forever memorialized Zac by 
renaming the baseball field at the North 4th Street Playground as the 
Zac Knoll Field, a testament to how beloved he was in the community.
  We are grateful to Zacchary Knoll for his service to Denver Borough. 
As we celebrate his life, we offer prayers of comfort to his wife and 
son.
```
