# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF FORMER WEST LAFAYETTE POLICE CHIEF DAN MARVIN

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF FORMER WEST LAFAYETTE POLICE CHIEF DAN 
                                 MARVIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES R. BAIRD

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor former West Lafayette 
Police Chief Dan Marvin, who passed away recently at the age of 62.
  Dan Marvin was an exemplary leader in the West Lafayette community, 
dedicating 30 years to its police force, including nine as chief.
  During his time on the force, Dan was selected as president of the 
Indiana Association of Chiefs of Police, served on multiple special FBI 
task forces, and was awarded the Sagamore of the Wabash, issued by the 
Governor of Indiana.
  Dan was an avid outdoorsman. He loved fishing for walleye and pike. 
He also hunted whitetail deer and moose. Being a son, husband, father, 
and grandfather meant everything to him. Dan's love for his family knew 
no bounds. However, most important in his life was a love for his 
Savior, Jesus Christ.
  May Dan rest in peace. I thank him, sincerely, for a life well lived.

                          ____________________
```
