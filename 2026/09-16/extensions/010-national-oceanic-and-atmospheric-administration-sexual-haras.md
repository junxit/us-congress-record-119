# NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION SEXUAL HARASSMENT AND ASSAULT PREVENTION IMPROVEMENTS ACT OF 2025

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2406

  Ms. LOFGREN. Mr. Speaker, I support H.R. 2406. This bipartisan bill 
strengthens protections against sexual assault and harassment at the 
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).
  The researchers, forecasters, engineers, and all supporting personnel 
at NOAA must be able to conduct their essential work without fear of 
assault. The mission of NOAA, which also houses the National Weather 
Service, is to understand and predict our changing environment, share 
that information with the public, and manage our marine ecosystems and 
resources. As the Ranking Member of the Science, Space, and Technology 
Committee, I understand the importance and necessity of our federal 
research enterprise and our need to protect it. In order to meet that 
critical mission, it's the research at NOAA that underscores the 
successfulness of its forecasts, warnings, and other products and 
services. The quality of the research is threatened when its workers do 
not feel safe.
  The NOAA sexual harassment Prevention Act led by Congresswoman 
Bonamici, another member of the Science Committee, helps create a safe 
workplace for NOAA personnel. The bill sets to expand the coverage of 
NOAA's sexual harassment prevention and response policy, provide a 
secure reporting structure for victims, and improve the ability for the 
agency to enforce the prohibition of assault and intimidation.
  I encourage my colleagues on both sides of the aisle, in the House 
and the Senate, to support this legislation to prevent sexual 
harassment and assault at NOAA.
