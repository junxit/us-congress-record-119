# RECOGNIZING HIS EXCELLENCY NGUYEN QUOC DZUNG, AMBASSADOR OF THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM TO THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Hamadeh, Abraham J. (H001098) [R-AZ]

```
    RECOGNIZING HIS EXCELLENCY NGUYEN QUOC DZUNG, AMBASSADOR OF THE 
           SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM TO THE UNITED STATES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ABRAHAM J. HAMADEH

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize His 
Excellency Ambassador Nguyen Quoc Dzung on the occasion of the 81st 
National Day of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the conclusion of 
his term as Ambassador to the United States.
  The history of the United States and Vietnam is one of the most 
remarkable stories of reconciliation in modern diplomacy. Once divided 
by a devastating war, our two nations have spent the past three decades 
building a relationship grounded in dialogue, mutual respect, and a 
shared commitment to a more peaceful and prosperous future. Since the 
normalization of diplomatic relations in 1995, the United States and 
Vietnam have developed a broad partnership spanning trade, investment, 
education, science and technology, security, and people-to-people ties.
  Ambassador Dzung served in Washington during a particularly important 
chapter in that relationship. In September 2023, the United States and 
Vietnam elevated our relationship to a Comprehensive Strategic 
Partnership--the highest level of Vietnam's diplomatic partnerships. 
This historic step reflected how far our two countries have come and 
created new opportunities for cooperation in areas ranging from 
economic engagement and resilient supply chains to semiconductors, 
technology, education, energy, and security.
  During his tenure, Ambassador Dzung helped strengthen the foundation 
of this partnership and advance the many connections that bring our 
countries closer together. He has engaged with leaders in government, 
academia, and the private sector across the United States, helping 
identify new opportunities for cooperation.
  He has also represented an important chapter in the continuing work 
of reconciliation between the American and Vietnamese peoples. Our 
countries have made significant progress in addressing the legacies of 
war, including accounting for missing servicemembers, remediating 
former military sites, and clearing unexploded ordnance. These efforts 
demonstrate that even the most difficult chapters of our shared history 
can become a foundation for greater understanding and cooperation.
  As Ambassador Dzung concludes his tenure in Washington, we recognize 
his service at an important moment in the history of United States-
Vietnam relations. His tenure has coincided with a period in which two 
former adversaries have continued to build a relationship defined by 
cooperation, opportunity, and an enduring commitment to a better 
future.
  On behalf of the United States Congress, I thank Ambassador Dzung for 
his service, his friendship, and his contributions to strengthening the 
bonds between the American and Vietnamese peoples. We wish him every 
success in his future endeavors and look forward to continuing the 
remarkable partnership that our two nations have built together.
```
