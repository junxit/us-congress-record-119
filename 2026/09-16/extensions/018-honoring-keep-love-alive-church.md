# HONORING KEEP LOVE ALIVE CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                    HONORING KEEP LOVE ALIVE CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Keep Love Alive 
Church, located in the Chapel of Oak Park Plaza retirement facility in 
Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and to recognize the vital ministry it has 
provided to some of our community's most treasured, yet often 
overlooked, residents for the past 16 years.
  Since its founding in 2010, Keep Love Alive Church has ministered 
mostly to the elderly and retired, faithfully serving the residents of 
Oak Park Plaza and, by extension, one of the most neglected and 
underserved populations in Baton Rouge: those living in retirement 
facilities and nursing homes. For far too many residents of such 
facilities, opportunities for spiritual fellowship and worship can 
become increasingly difficult to access as age and mobility change 
their daily lives. Keep Love Alive Church was built to meet that need 
directly, bringing the Word of God, fellowship, and ministry to those 
who might otherwise be unable to receive it.
  From the very beginning, Reverend Michael Collins has served as 
Pastor of Keep Love Alive Church, and for 16 years he has answered 
God's calling to serve this population with faithfulness and joy. 
Pastor Collins is supported in this mission by two associate ministers, 
who work alongside him to ensure that the congregation's approximately 
twenty-five members receive the spiritual care, worship, and fellowship 
they deserve. It is evident that God placed upon Pastor Collins's heart 
and spirit a deep and abiding commitment to this ministry, and he 
continues to serve it not out of obligation, but with genuine happiness 
and responsiveness to his calling.
  The members of Keep Love Alive Church represent generations of faith, 
wisdom, and life experience, and this body should recognize that their 
contributions did not end upon entering a retirement facility. These 
are individuals who spent lifetimes raising families, building careers, 
serving their communities, and living out their faith long before Keep 
Love Alive Church ever came into existence. In their later years, they 
continue to embody that same faith, gathering in worship and fellowship 
as an active, living congregation rather than a forgotten population. 
Keep Love Alive Church honors that lifetime of faith simply by ensuring 
it does not go unrecognized or unattended in this later season of life.
  Ministries such as Keep Love Alive Church remind us that the elderly 
and retired members of our community are not a population to be set 
aside, but a population still very much in need of, and deserving of, 
active spiritual care, fellowship, and love. Through their steadfast 
presence at Oak Park Plaza, Pastor Collins, his associate ministers, 
and the congregation of Keep Love Alive Church have shown Baton Rouge 
what it truly means to keep love alive for those who may need it most.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Keep Love 
Alive Church, Pastor Michael Collins, and the congregation for 16 years 
of faithful ministry to the elderly and retired residents of Baton 
Rouge. May this ministry continue to be a source of comfort, dignity, 
and love for many years to come.
```
