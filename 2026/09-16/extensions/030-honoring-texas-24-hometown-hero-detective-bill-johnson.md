# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DETECTIVE BILL JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
         HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DETECTIVE BILL JOHNSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Detective Bill Johnson. Detective Johnson spent over 20 years 
with the New York City Police Department. He had been working in his 
new role as the detective-investigator for the Fugitive Enforcement 
Division for a few months when the attacks on September 11, 2001 
occurred.
  That morning, Johnson got off his night shift only hours before the 
attack. He didn't wait to be called back into work, he went straight to 
the station to offer help. For the next few weeks, he worked 
tirelessly, serving in various roles, including efforts at Ground Zero 
looking through ``the pile'' for survivors and reporting missing loved 
ones. He was part of history, as everyone came together and he 
witnessed the city change. Johnson's efforts continued for four months 
as he worked in the morgue helping identify victims and recovering DNA. 
His time spent in hazardous conditions from the debris led to his and 
other responders' cancer diagnoses in subsequent years.
  Johnson moved from New York to Southlake in 2019. 25 years later, 
Johnson is the featured speaker at Southlake's Remembrance Ceremony, 
where he will share his story and continue to honor the legacy of those 
who lost their lives on that day, and those who lost battles in the 
years following. I thank Bill Johnson for his incredible heroism on one 
of America's darkest days.
```
