# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ROXBURY POLICE DEPARTMENT

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
      HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ROXBURY POLICE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Roxbury Police 
Department in Roxbury Township, Morris County, New Jersey, as it marks 
its 100th anniversary. This is a remarkable milestone reflecting a 
century of unwavering service and dedication to the good people of 
Succasunna, Ledgewood, Landing, Kenvil, Port Morris, and Berkshire 
Valley within Roxbury.
  Established in 1926, the Department began its journey under the 
leadership of its first Police Chief, Fred W. Plumstead. Chief 
Plumstead served with distinction until he was tragically killed in the 
line of duty less than three years after his appointment. His sacrifice 
remains a lasting part of the Department's history and serves as a 
powerful reminder of the courage and selflessness displayed by those 
who serve.
  For a hundred years, the Roxbury Police Department has been steadfast 
in its mission to protect the residents of Roxbury Township. Through 
generations, officers have consistently answered the call in times of 
need, providing both security and leadership.
  Throughout its history, two core principles have guided the 
Department: the belief that the people of Roxbury Township deserve 
dedicated and reliable protection, and the confidence that future 
generations of officers will continue to serve with courage, 
professionalism, and compassion.
  From its earliest days under Chief Plumstead to the officers serving 
today, the Roxbury Police Department has demonstrated an unwavering 
commitment to the safety and well-being of the community
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in commemorating the 
Roxbury Police Department. We extend our heartfelt congratulations to 
its members on this significant occasion and celebrate the strength and 
spirit that define this essential community organization.
```
