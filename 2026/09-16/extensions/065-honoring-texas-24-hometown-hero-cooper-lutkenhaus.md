# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO COOPER LUTKENHAUS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO COOPER LUTKENHAUS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Cooper Lutkenhaus. At just 16 years old, Cooper made history by 
breaking the U18 world record in the 800-meter with a time of 1:42.27 
at the USA Track and Field Outdoor Championships in Eugene, Oregon.
  His final 100-meter split and silver medal finish stunned even the 
most seasoned athletes and commentators, as he split an incredible 
12.48 seconds in the final 100 meters to close the gap.
  I thank Cooper for inspiring the next generation and showing the 
world what the Lone Star State is made of. He continues to represent 
North Texas well, and we cannot wait to keep watching his journey.

                          ____________________
```
