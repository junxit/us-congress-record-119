# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ERIC WEINBERG

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ERIC WEINBERG

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown Hero, Eric 
Weinberg, an AP and Honors student at Highland Park High School, who 
excels in the classroom and on the court as a varsity tennis player. 
His dedication to service has earned him the Presidential Gold Award 
and Congressional Bronze Award.
  Eric has continued to serve North Texas as a graduate of our 2024 to 
2025 Congressional Youth Advisory Council, as well as the University 
Park Youth Advisory Commission, and other community organizations. This 
summer, he was one of only two students from Texas selected for the FBI 
National Academy Associates Youth Leadership Program in Quantico.
  Eric is already leading by example, balancing academics, athletics, 
and service. His leadership and commitment to serving others make him a 
true standout in North Texas.

                          ____________________
```
