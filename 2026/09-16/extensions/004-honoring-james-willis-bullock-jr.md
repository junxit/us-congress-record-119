# HONORING JAMES WILLIS BULLOCK, JR.

**Speaking:** Ivey, Glenn (I000058) [D-MD]

```
                   HONORING JAMES WILLIS BULLOCK, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GLENN IVEY

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. IVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor James Willis Bullock, 
Jr., who is turning 98 years old today. Mr. Bullock, Jr. served our 
country honorably as a member of the Air Force, Flight 3056, Squadron 
3727, as a District of Columbia Social Services Counselor and 
Representative, and conducted the District's Traffic Alcohol Program. 
Mr. Bullock, Jr.'s service ensured the American public's safety and 
well-being.
  Born on September 16, 1928, in Durham, North Carolina, Mr. Bullock, 
Jr. went on to attend North Carolina A&T as a member of JROTC, then 
bravely enlisted in the Air Force. Mr. Bullock was assigned to Air 
Force Flight 3056, Squadron 3727, our Nation's last solely African-
American unit. His service and membership to Squadron 3727 instilled 
Mr. Bullock's place in our Nation's history, and made him a symbol of 
desegregation and equal rights. His actions remind us of the sacrifice 
many Black veterans made and continue to make. After 20 years of 
distinguished service, Mr. Bullock ultimately earned the Air Force 
Commendation Medal.
  Following his military service, Mr. Bullock, Jr. continued serving 
the community as a Social Services Counselor for the Government of the 
District of Columbia, Department of Public Welfare, SSA Children's 
Center. His ability to succeed in this role led to his appointment as 
Social Services Representative at Maple Glen D.C. Children's Center, 
where he cared and provided for nearly 100 children. The selflessness 
exemplified by Mr. Bullock earned him a Certificate from the District 
of Columbia.
  Even after this record of accomplishments, Mr. Bullock, Jr., trusted 
by the public, was selected to conduct the District of Columbia's 
Traffic Alcohol Program. After three years of dedication to this role, 
Mr. Bullock, Jr. assisted 50 individuals through the program, 
furthering our Nation's public safety.
```
