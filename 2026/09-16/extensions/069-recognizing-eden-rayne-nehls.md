# RECOGNIZING EDEN RAYNE NEHLS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING EDEN RAYNE NEHLS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Eden Rayne 
Nehls for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Eden Rayne has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Eden Rayne, develop crucial skills 
and a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Eden Rayne's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Jefferson Junior/Senior High School and is clearly just 
the beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Eden Rayne Nehls on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
