# HONORING THE 10TH ANNIVERSARY OF ALIENTO

**Speaking:** Ansari, Yassamin (A000381) [D-AZ]

```
                HONORING THE 10TH ANNIVERSARY OF ALIENTO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. YASSAMIN ANSARI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. ANSARI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 10th 
anniversary of Aliento and to honor its visionary founder, Reyna 
Montoya.
  For a decade, Aliento has stood as a beacon of hope, healing, and 
empowerment for immigrant youth and families across Arizona. Founded in 
2016 by Reyna Montoya, Aliento was built on the belief that every young 
person deserves not only opportunity but also a safe space to be seen, 
heard, and supported. Through its work, the organization has 
transformed countless lives, turning pain into purpose and fear into 
community.
  Aliento's mission extends beyond advocacy. It has created a holistic 
model of support that addresses mental health, leadership development, 
and civic engagement for undocumented and mixed-status families. 
Through programs centered on storytelling, healing circles, and youth 
leadership, Aliento has helped young people find their voice and step 
into their power as leaders in their communities. In doing so, the 
organization has helped redefine what it means to belong in our Nation.
  Over the past ten years, Aliento has also played a critical role in 
shaping a more compassionate and inclusive Arizona. From organizing 
community events and advocacy campaigns to uplifting the stories of 
immigrant families, the organization has built bridges of understanding 
and solidarity across diverse communities. Its impact is measured not 
only in the programs delivered, but in the resilience it has nurtured 
and the futures it has helped make possible.

[[Page E945]]

  This milestone is a testament to Reyna Montoya's extraordinary 
leadership. Her courage, vision, and unwavering commitment have 
inspired a movement rooted in dignity, healing, and justice. Through 
Aliento, she has cultivated a generation of young leaders who are not 
only advocating for change, but embodying it.
  It is my honor to recognize Aliento on its 10th anniversary and 
commend Reyna Montoya for her profound contributions. May their work 
continue to uplift, empower, and inspire for many years to come.

                          ____________________
```
