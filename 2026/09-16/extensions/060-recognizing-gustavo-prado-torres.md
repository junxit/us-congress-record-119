# RECOGNIZING GUSTAVO PRADO TORRES

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                    RECOGNIZING GUSTAVO PRADO TORRES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Gustavo Prado 
Torres for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Gustavo has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Gustavo, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Gustavo's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Jefferson Jr/Sr High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Gustavo Prado Torres on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
