# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MATTHEW A. "MATT" LEWIS

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
       HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MATTHEW A. ``MATT'' LEWIS

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Matthew A. ``Matt'' Lewis, a beloved member of the Owego 
community who passed away on September 6, 2026, surrounded by his 
loving family.
  Matt was a longtime radio broadcaster with WEBO, where he became a 
familiar voice to generations of listeners throughout the region. 
Through his work behind the microphone, he connected with local 
athletes, community leaders, and neighbors, helping tell the stories 
that make our communities special. He also shared his love of racing as 
an announcer at racetracks throughout New York and Pennsylvania.
  Beyond broadcasting, Matt dedicated countless hours to supporting the 
Boy Scouts of America. As a Scoutmaster with Troop 60, he helped grow 
the troop from just five boys to more than 100 registered Scouts. His 
commitment to mentorship, service, and community left a lasting impact 
on the young people he guided.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in honoring the life of Matthew Lewis and extending our deepest 
condolences to his wife, Cobi, his children, his grandchildren, and all 
who knew him. His dedication and familiar voice will be missed 
throughout Owego and the Southern Tier.

                          ____________________
```
