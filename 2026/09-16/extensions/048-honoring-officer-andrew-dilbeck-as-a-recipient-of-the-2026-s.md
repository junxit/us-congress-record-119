# HONORING OFFICER ANDREW DILBECK AS A RECIPIENT OF THE 2026 STATE OF TEXAS COMMISSION ON LAW ENFORCEMENT'S PROFESSIONAL ACHIEVEMENT AWARD

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
  HONORING OFFICER ANDREW DILBECK AS A RECIPIENT OF THE 2026 STATE OF 
  TEXAS COMMISSION ON LAW ENFORCEMENT'S PROFESSIONAL ACHIEVEMENT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Officer 
Andrew Dilbeck of the Fort Worth Police Department upon his receipt of 
the 2026 State of Texas Law Enforcement Achievement Award for 
Professional Achievement, presented by the Texas Commission on Law 
Enforcement.
  This prestigious recognition honors Officer Dilbeck's exceptional 
dedication to public service, outstanding professional performance, and 
unwavering commitment to protecting the citizens of Fort Worth and 
communities throughout North Texas. His service reflects the highest 
ideals of the law enforcement profession.
  As a member of the Fort Worth Police Department's Sex Offender 
Registration and Tracking Unit (S.O.R.T.), Officer Dilbeck has 
strengthened partnerships with community organizations, enhancing the 
department's ability to locate and monitor registered sex offenders 
experiencing homelessness within a rapidly growing metropolitan area.
  During the first eight months of 2025, Officer Dilbeck assisted in 
apprehending of 182 felony fugitives wanted on S.O.R.T. warrants. His 
outstanding performance earned him selection as a Task Force Officer 
with the United States Marshals Service, where he also serves on the 
North Texas Fugitive Task Force Echo Team, working alongside federal, 
state, and local law enforcement agencies to apprehend dangerous 
fugitives across more than 100 counties.
  Despite these demanding responsibilities, Officer Dilbeck remains 
committed to monitoring registered sex offenders throughout the region. 
His investigative efforts and testimony in both state and federal 
courts have helped secure lengthy prison sentences for numerous 
offenders, strengthening public safety across Texas.
  Officer Dilbeck's professionalism, integrity, and dedication to 
justice have distinguished him as an exceptional public servant and

[[Page E947]]

brought honor to the Fort Worth Police Department.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I extend my 
sincere congratulations to Officer Andrew Dilbeck on this well-deserved 
honor and wish him continued success in his service to the people of 
Texas.
```
