# HONORING THE 200TH ANNIVERSARY OF FOUNTAIN COUNTY INDIANA

**Speaking:** Baird, James R. (B001307) [R-IN]

```
       HONORING THE 200TH ANNIVERSARY OF FOUNTAIN COUNTY INDIANA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES R. BAIRD

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 200th 
anniversary of Fountain County Indiana. Fountain was formed along the 
Wabash River on April 1st, 1826. Two hundred years later, Fountain has 
grown into a thriving community with a rich history and culture.
  A beautiful Art Deco courthouse sits on the square in Fountain. Known 
for gorgeous murals lining its hallways and depicting the settlement of 
western Indiana, it really is a masterpiece. Another historic spot is 
the Cades Mill Covered Bridge, the oldest covered bridge in Indiana.
  The steel industry has been a longstanding presence in Fountain for 
over 100 years through the company Harrison Steel.
  Bricking is another industry in Fountain. Bricks from the county have 
stood up over 25 Purdue University buildings and helped pave the 
Indianapolis 500 track, famously monikered as the ``Brickyard.'' 
Fountain County truly has played a remarkable role in our state's 
history.
  I congratulate everyone from Fountain County on their bicentennial.

                          ____________________
```
