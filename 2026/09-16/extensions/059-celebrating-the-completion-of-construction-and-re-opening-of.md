# CELEBRATING THE COMPLETION OF CONSTRUCTION AND RE-OPENING OF AZLE JUNIOR HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
   CELEBRATING THE COMPLETION OF CONSTRUCTION AND RE-OPENING OF AZLE 
                           JUNIOR HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
opening of the newly constructed Azle Junior High School in Azle, 
Texas.
  The completion of this state-of-the-art campus marks an important 
milestone for the Azle Independent School District and the entire Azle 
community. Designed to serve future generations of students, the new 
junior high school reflects the district's unwavering commitment to 
providing a safe, innovative, and enriching learning environment where 
students can thrive.
  The new Azle Junior High School represents a new era of educational 
opportunity for the students of Azle. This exceptional campus will 
empower young Texans to develop the knowledge, skills, and character 
needed to succeed both in the classroom and beyond. Through the 
dedication of district leadership, educators, staff, parents, and 
community members, the school will foster academic excellence, 
encourage personal growth, and prepare students to become the next 
generation of leaders, innovators, and engaged citizens.
  On behalf of Texas' 12th Congressional District, I am honored to 
recognize the opening of the new Azle Junior High School and commend 
everyone whose hard work and commitment made this achievement possible. 
This remarkable new campus will provide generations of students with 
the opportunity to reach their fullest potential. I extend my heartfelt 
congratulations to the Azle Independent School District as it 
celebrates this exciting milestone, and I wish its students, faculty, 
and staff continued success for years to come.

                          ____________________
```
