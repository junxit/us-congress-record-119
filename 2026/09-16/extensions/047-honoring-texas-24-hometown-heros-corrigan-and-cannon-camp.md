# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS CORRIGAN AND CANNON CAMP

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS CORRIGAN AND CANNON CAMP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Heros, Corrigan and Cannon Camp, 9-year-old twins from Fort Worth, who 
reminded all of us what it means to lead with compassion and action. 
After devastating floods hit Central Texas and damaged Camp Mystic, 
where Corrigan had just spent time this summer, they felt called to 
help.
  With determined spirits, the siblings set up a lemonade and cookie 
stand in their neighborhood. By the end of the day they had raised over 
$5,000, donating every cent to Camp Mystic to support recovery efforts 
and honor the lives lost. They even sold ``Tweety Cookies,'' a special 
Camp Mystic tradition, in tribute to owner and Executive Director Dick 
Eastland, who lost his life saving campers during the flood.
  I thank Corrigan and Cannon for showing Texas-24 what it means to 
serve others with faith, kindness, and courage. Their generosity shines 
brightly.
```
