# REMEMBERING O.J. BRIGANCE

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
                       REMEMBERING O.J. BRIGANCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. STENY H. HOYER

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, some of the most inspiring stories we 
encounter come from sports. Underdogs winning from behind. Teams 
finding unity in common purpose and setback being met with resolve. 
They lift us up and help us see more clearly the values that matter 
most in our lives.
  One of the most resonant and inspiring stories for Marylanders has 
been the journey of O.J. Brigance. O.J. was a football star. He helped 
lead the Baltimore Ravens to victory in the Super Bowl in 2001.
  Though he only spent one season in Baltimore as a player, O.J. made 
it his home, and after he retired, he returned to the Ravens in 2004 as 
Director of Player Development. That could have been the end of his 
story. But, in 2007 O.J. was diagnosed with A.L.S. A.L.S., of course, 
is a terrible, fatal disease, for which there is not yet a cure, in 
spite of so much noble effort by medical researchers and doctors. Only 
half of patients live more than three years beyond their diagnosis. 
Fewer than 20 percent survive beyond a decade.
  O.J. was among the very few who made it nineteen years. What did he 
do with those years? He became an advocate and a leader in the fight 
against A.L.S, He brought the same spark and drive from his playing 
career to the work of raising money for research. O.J. set an example 
of resolve. He showed an underdog spirit every day. He helped inspire 
so many to the common purpose of finding a cure.
  Mr. Speaker, O.J. Brigance lost his battle with A.L.S. in August. 
Marylanders mourn his passing, and I hope the House will join me in 
paying tribute to an inspiring leader, who showed us all what it means 
to work hard and persevere--both on and off the football field.
  I extend my condolences to his beloved wife Chanda and the extended 
Brigance family.

                          ____________________
```
