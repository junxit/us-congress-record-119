# RECOGNIZING NICK AND CHERYL DEAN, AUGUST 2026 CO-CONSTITUENTS OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
 RECOGNIZING NICK AND CHERYL DEAN, AUGUST 2026 CO-CONSTITUENTS OF THE 
                                 MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Nick and Cheryl Dean, 
residents of Encinitas, as my Co-Constituents of the Month for August 
2026.
  For more than a decade, Nick and Cheryl have used the power of film 
to bring the wonders of the ocean, and the dangers facing it, into 
homes around the world. Their dedication took them across four 
continents over six years to document the North Atlantic right whale, a 
species with fewer than 400 animals left on Earth.
  Nick's background as a research scientist and Cheryl's gift for 
communicating science to the public have made them uniquely effective 
storytellers and advocates. Their foundation's work protecting the 
right whale and defending critical conservation laws reflects a deep 
commitment to our planet's future.
  I urge my colleagues to join me in celebrating Nick and Cheryl Dean 
for their extraordinary contributions to ocean conservation and 
environmental storytelling.
```
