# RECOGNIZING ESMERALDA GONZALEZ AND DAVID ONTIVEROS, JUNE 2026 CO- CONSTITUENTS OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
   RECOGNIZING ESMERALDA GONZALEZ AND DAVID ONTIVEROS, JUNE 2026 CO-
                       CONSTITUENTS OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Esmeralda Gonzalez and 
David Ontiveros, residents of Oceanside, as my Co-Constituents of the 
Month for June 2026.
  Esmeralda and David represent the very best of what community means. 
They do not wait to be asked; they simply show up, roll up their 
sleeves, and give everything they have to the people around them. 
Through their years of service with Pride by the Beach, they have 
helped build something that is more than an event. It is a testament to 
belonging.
  David's commitment to the greater good and Esmeralda's steadfast 
leadership remind us that strong communities are built by one dedicated 
person at a time.
  I urge my colleagues to join me in celebrating Esmeralda Gonzalez and 
David Ontiveros, and in thanking them for the service they bring to 
North County San Diego and to our entire community.

                          ____________________
```
