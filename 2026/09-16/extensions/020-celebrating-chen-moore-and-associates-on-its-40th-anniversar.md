# CELEBRATING CHEN MOORE AND ASSOCIATES ON ITS 40TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
     CELEBRATING CHEN MOORE AND ASSOCIATES ON ITS 40TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Chen Moore and 
Associates (Chen Moore) on the occasion of its 40th anniversary. 
Founded in November 1986 and headquartered in Fort Lauderdale, Chen 
Moore has grown into a respected multidisciplinary firm providing 
engineering, planning, environmental science, landscape architecture, 
site development, and project delivery services throughout Florida and 
beyond. With 13 offices across Florida, along with offices in Atlanta, 
Knoxville, and Raleigh, the firm has completed hundreds of projects 
while demonstrating a strong commitment to technical excellence, 
environmental stewardship, and sustainable and resilient communities.
  Chen Moore's success reflects the leadership and service of its 
founder, Dr. Ben Chen, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Peter 
Moore, and President Jose L. Acosta. Each has made significant 
contributions to the engineering profession and the South Florida 
community through professional leadership, mentorship, philanthropy, 
and nonprofit service.
  As part of its 40th anniversary, Chen Moore partnered with the ASCE 
Florida Section and the Florida Engineering Foundation, Inc. to 
establish the Chen Moore Scholarship Fund, supporting the next 
generation of engineering students.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Chen Moore and 
Associates on four decades of service, innovation, and community 
commitment, and I extend my best wishes to its entire team for many 
more years of continued success.
```
