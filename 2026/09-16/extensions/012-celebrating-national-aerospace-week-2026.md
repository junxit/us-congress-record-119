# CELEBRATING NATIONAL AEROSPACE WEEK 2026

**Speaking:** Estes, Ron (E000298) [R-KS]

```
                CELEBRATING NATIONAL AEROSPACE WEEK 2026

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RON ESTES

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. ESTES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize National Aerospace 
Week. There is no better way to celebrate this week each year than to 
highlight how aerospace is woven into Kansas' past, present, and 
future.
  For more than a century, Kansas' aerospace trailblazers have 
propelled the United States to the forefront of aviation. Aviation is 
central to the history and identity of Wichita and south central 
Kansas. Nearly 100 years ago in 1928, Wichita was designated as the 
``Air Capital of the World,'' thanks to aviation pioneers like Clyde 
Cessna, Olive Ann Beech, and Lloyd Stearman.
  Since then, aviation has continued to flourish here, and hardworking 
Kansans have worked tirelessly to design and manufacture the airplanes 
and components that fly much of our world. Most everyone from Kansas, 
and especially Kansas' 4th Congressional District, has a connection to 
aviation. From the production lines in Wichita to the shop floors in 
small towns across our state, Kansas is home to the fifth largest 
aerospace workforce in the country.
  The industry supports over 100,000 American jobs that added over $20 
billion to our state's GDP last year alone. The economic impact can be 
seen across our state's A&D suppliers who manufacture the parts and 
components that keep our skies safer and connected.
  As co-chair of the House Aerospace Caucus, I am proud to represent 
the Air Capital in Washington. I am humbled to represent the people 
that help build the technologies keeping American aerospace at the 
forefront of innovation and safety each day.
```
