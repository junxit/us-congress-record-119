# RECOGNIZING JESSICA DONLON

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                       RECOGNIZING JESSICA DONLON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jessica Donlon 
for her service to the House Committee on Energy and Commerce.
  A double graduate of the University of Notre Dame as both an 
undergrad and student of the law, Jessica began her career on Capitol 
Hill in 2010 as an investigations counsel on the House Committee on 
Oversight and Government reform for Chairman Darrell Issa, rising 
through the ranks to a Senior Counsel role.
  Since then, Jessica's service to the American people has included 
working in several senior roles under the leadership of seven different 
Chairs across three different House congressional committees, Oversight 
and Government Reform, Small Business, and Energy and Commerce and as 
Deputy General Counsel in President Trump's Office of Management and 
Budget, Executive Office of the President. In January 2025, when I 
became Chairman of the House Committee on Energy and Commerce, Jessica 
joined my team as General Counsel where she quickly became a trusted 
advisor who has been key to managing the extensive responsibilities of 
the committee.
  Her work was essential to managing intricate and complex procedural 
requirements, advancing important oversight and investigative 
priorities, and fighting to advance sound policy through our 
legislative agenda, including ensuring President Trump's Working 
Families Tax Cuts bill was signed into law, along with dozens of other 
bills. We could not have succeeded this Congress without her sound 
judgment and steady hand.
  Jessica will soon depart the House Committee on Energy and Commerce 
after 16 years of service in the federal government to continue her 
career in private practice. I thank her for her commitment to the 
Committee, the Congress, and the American people, and I wish her the 
best in her future with her husband Steve and her three children, Cora, 
Luke, and A.J.

                          ____________________
```
