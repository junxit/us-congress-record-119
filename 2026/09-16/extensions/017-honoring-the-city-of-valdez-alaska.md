# HONORING THE CITY OF VALDEZ, ALASKA

**Speaking:** Begich, Nicholas J. (B001323) [R-AK]

```
                  HONORING THE CITY OF VALDEZ, ALASKA

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS J. BEGICH III

                               of alaska

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. BEGICH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the City of Valdez on 
its 125th Anniversary. This anniversary marks an important milestone, 
one that allows the Nation to reflect on both the pioneer values of 
Valdez and the great Alaskan spirit. Founded in 1898 as a debarkation 
point for those seeking fortune in the Klondike goldfields, residents 
voted to formalize their city and built the foundation of a community.
  On Good Friday in 1964, a 9.2 magnitude earthquake, the most powerful 
ever recorded in North American history, struck the region and 
unleashed devastating tsunamis that claimed 33 lives and destroyed the 
town itself. Valdez did not throw in the towel but instead relocated, 
rebuilt, and returned stronger than before. In Alaska, we speak of 
people forged by long winters and hard resolve, and Valdez embodies 
that spirit of grit.
  In the 1970s, Valdez became the southern terminus of the Trans-Alaska 
Pipeline System and the largest crude oil port in North America. Then, 
on Good Friday in 1989, the grounding of the Exxon Valdez on Bligh Reef 
brought yet another challenge to the people of Valdez. The people 
responded and following that tragedy, Valdez became a world leader in 
oil spill preparedness and response.
  Today, Valdez stands as a city built by hardworking men and women, 
representative of the unyielding tenacity of those who call it home. On 
behalf of the people of Alaska, I congratulate the City of Valdez on 
125 years of perseverance, resourcefulness, and resolve.
  For Alaska.
```
