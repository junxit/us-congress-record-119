# RECOGNIZING SERGEANT JOHN BANNER OF THE WHITE SETTLEMENT POLICE DEPARTMENT FOR HIS EXCEPTIONAL VALOR

**Speaking:** Goldman, Craig A. (G000601) [R-TX]

```
    RECOGNIZING SERGEANT JOHN BANNER OF THE WHITE SETTLEMENT POLICE 
                  DEPARTMENT FOR HIS EXCEPTIONAL VALOR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sergeant 
John Banner of the White Settlement Police Department for receiving the 
State of Texas Law Enforcement Achievement Award for Valor from the 
Texas Commission on Law Enforcement.
  This distinguished honor recognizes Sergeant Banner's courage, 
professionalism, and dedication to protecting the community. His 
actions represent the highest standards of service demonstrated by law 
enforcement officers across Texas, and our Nation.
  Sergeant Banner displayed extraordinary bravery while responding to a 
suspicious vehicle call that quickly escalated into a dangerous 
situation. Through his calm approach and efforts to de-escalate the 
encounter, Sergeant Banner was able to work alongside fellow officers 
to safely resolve the incident and take the suspect into custody.
  The investigation later revealed that the suspect had traveled across 
state lines with plans to harm estranged relatives and carry out an 
attack on a local church. Without question, Sergeant Banner's 
unwavering comrnitment to serve and protect a potentially devastating 
tragedy was prevented.
  On behalf of Texas' 12th Congressional District, I am honored to 
congratulate Sergeant John Banner and commend him, along with the 
entire White Settlement Police Department, for their continued bravery, 
dedication, and commitment to protecting and serving our community.
```
