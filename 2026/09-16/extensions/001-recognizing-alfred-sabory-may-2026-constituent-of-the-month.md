# RECOGNIZING ALFRED SABORY, MAY 2026 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
      RECOGNIZING ALFRED SABORY, MAY 2026 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and recognize Alfred Sabory, 
a 101-year-old World War II Veteran, as my Constituent of the Month for 
May 2026.
  Alfred Sabory is a man who has embraced every chapter of his life 
with purpose--and he shows no signs of slowing down. When duty called, 
he stepped forward to defend our Nation against the forces of fascism 
and the Axis powers, and he stayed in the fight long after he could 
have come home. For many, service in a world war would define a 
lifetime.
  Yet after his extraordinary bravery abroad, he devoted himself to 
educating the next generation here at home. He stands as one of the 
finest examples of a life given in service to others.
  From safeguarding liberty on foreign soil to inspiring young people 
in the classroom to championing the well-being of our seniors, Alfred 
Sabory has spent more than a century strengthening this Nation. I urge 
my colleagues to join me in paying tribute to this exceptional 
American.

                          ____________________
```
