# HONORING THE DISTINGUISHED CAREER OF STATE SENATOR LELA ALSTON

**Speaking:** Ansari, Yassamin (A000381) [D-AZ]

```
     HONORING THE DISTINGUISHED CAREER OF STATE SENATOR LELA ALSTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. YASSAMIN ANSARI

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2026

  Ms. ANSARI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
remarkable public service career of State Senator Lela Alston upon her 
retirement from the Arizona Legislature after decades of dedicated 
service to the people of Arizona.
  For more than four decades, State Senator Alston has been a steadfast 
advocate for her constituents and a respected leader within Arizona's 
government. Throughout her tenure in both the Arizona House of 
Representatives and the Arizona State Senate, she earned a reputation 
for integrity, pragmatism, and an unwavering commitment to improving 
the lives of Arizona families.
  Representing communities across central Phoenix, currently 
Legislative District 5, State Senator Alston has consistently 
championed policies that expand opportunity, strengthen public 
education, support working families, and protect vulnerable 
populations. Her leadership has been defined by a deep understanding of 
the challenges facing her constituents and a determination to bring 
people together in pursuit of practical solutions.
  State Senator Alston's impact extends far beyond the legislation she 
helped enact. As one of Arizona's most respected public servants, she 
has mentored countless elected officials, community leaders, and public 
servants who have followed in her footsteps. Her dedication to public 
service, her ability to build consensus, and her commitment to treating 
all people with dignity and respect have left an enduring mark on 
Arizona's civic life.
  Throughout her career, State Senator Alston demonstrated that 
effective leadership is rooted in service, compassion, and a genuine 
commitment to the public good. Her voice has been a powerful force for 
her community, and her contributions have helped shape Arizona for the 
better. As she concludes her distinguished legislative career, we 
celebrate not only her many accomplishments, but also the example she 
has set for future generations of public servants. The people of 
Arizona have been well served by her leadership, and her legacy will 
continue to be felt for years to come.
  On behalf of Arizona's 3rd Congressional District and the United 
States House of Representatives, I extend my deepest gratitude to State 
Senator Lela Alston for her extraordinary

[[Page E938]]

service to the State of Arizona. I congratulate her on her retirement 
and wish her continued happiness, health, and success in the years 
ahead.

                          ____________________
```
