# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10420

**Measures:** H.R. 10420

```
           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 10420.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the United States 
     Constitution, which provides Congress the power to provide 
     for the general welfare, and Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, 
     which provides Congress the power to make all laws necessary 
     and proper for carrying into execution its enumerated powers.
```
