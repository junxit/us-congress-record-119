# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, we cannot fathom Your mysteries. We cannot probe the limits 
of Your being, for they are higher than the heavens above and deeper 
than the depths below.
  And yet You allow us, in the finitude of our lives and the brevity of 
our days, to receive the wideness of Your mercy, which is wider than 
the sea. You offer us the kindness of Your justice, which is more than 
freedom from our transgressions.
  For Your love, O God, is broader than we can measure. Your heart is 
eternally and wonderfully kind.
  In this moment, may we, with open minds and contrite hearts, receive 
the bounty of Your mercy and submit ourselves to the greatness of Your 
sovereignty, that we may, as servants of the Most High God, honor You 
in all that we do and say this day and in all the days You have 
numbered for us.
  In the light of Your word and in the power of Your name, we dare 
pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
