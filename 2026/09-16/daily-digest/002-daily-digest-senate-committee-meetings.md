Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Committee ordered 
favorably reported an original bill entitled, ``Agricultural Act of 
2026''.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee ordered 
favorably reported the following business items:
  S. 3467, to reauthorize the Virginia Graeme Baker Pool and Spa Safety 
Act, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 3929, to require the Administrator of the Federal Aviation 
Administration to acquire and install certified airborne position 
reference tools at air traffic control towers, with an amendment in the 
nature of a substitute;
  S. 4573, to designate the Great Lakes Commission as a Regional Great 
Lakes Partnership, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 4749, to amend the Communications Act of 1934 to create a Federal 
cause of action to address jawboning, with an amendment in the nature 
of a substitute;
  S. 4938, to reduce trawl gear impacts on bycatch and seafloor habitat 
in the Bering Sea, Aleutian Islands, and Gulf of Alaska, to establish 
gear performance standards, seafloor contact detection, and salmon 
excluder requirements, to improve Council transparency and 
participation, to prioritize ecosystem analyses, to modernize 
electronic monitoring, to prohibit unsustainable foreign seafood 
imports, and to establish a Bycatch Mitigation and Habitat Protection 
Assistance Fund, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 4950, to amend the National Telecommunications and Information 
Administration Organization Act to codify the Institute for 
Telecommunication Sciences, to direct the Assistant Secretary of 
Commerce for Communications and Information to establish an initiative 
to support the development of emergency communication and tracking 
technologies, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute;
  S. 5188, to amend the Professional Boxing Safety Act of 1996 to 
establish requirements for unified boxing organizations and to further 
enhance the well-being of professional boxers, with an amendment in the 
nature of a substitute;
  S. 5224, to improve training for airport drivers in the movement area 
at commercial service airports through the establishment of a task 
force and the use of technology, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute;
  H.R. 7022, to provide for the transmission of emergency alerts by 
satellite, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute; and
  Promotions in the Coast Guard.

BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee ordered favorably 
reported the following business items:
  S. 5045, to amend the Clean Air Act to modify the handling of air 
quality monitoring with respect to prescribed fires;
  S. 3135, to require the Administrator of the Environmental Protection 
Agency to authorize manufacturers of certain vehicles to suspend engine 
derate or shutdown functions in prolonged cold weather conditions, with

  S. 5249, to amend the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to align the 
licensing of uranium enrichment facilities with other fuel cycle 
facilities under that Act, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute; and
  21 Committee resolutions to approve prospectuses from the General 
Services Administration.

Goodman III, of Florida, a Career Member of the Senior Foreign Service,

the Department of State, after the nominees testified and answered 
questions in their own behalf.

NOMINATIONS

concluded a hearing to examine the nominations of Gerard Klomp, of

Committee on Indian Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
housing needs in native communities, including S. 5354, to reauthorize

Assistant Secretary for Native American Programs, Department of Housing 
and Urban Development; Alicia Puente Cackley, Government Accountability 
Office; and Bryan Warner, Cherokee Nation, Tahlequah, Oklahoma.

the nominations of Daniel Lewis Ballou, to be United States District 
Judge for the Eastern District of Kentucky, who was introduced by 
Senator Paul, Richard Wesley Bennett, to be United States District 
Judge for the Southern District of Texas, Courtney Leigh Coker, to be 
United States District Judge for the Northern District of Texas, Kyle 
Frederick Reardon, to be United States District Judge for the District 
of Alaska, who was introduced by Senator Murkowski, and Trevor Scott 
Pemberton, to be United States District Judge for the Northern District 
of Oklahoma, and Zachary Paul West, to be United States District Judge 
for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, who were both introduced by 
Senator Lankford, after the nominees testified and answered questions

receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
MENOPAUSE CARE GAP IN AMERICA
Special Committee on Aging: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the menopause care gap in America, after receiving testimony from 
Suzanne Fenske, Women's Health Ambassador, HHS Office on Women's 
Health; Jean Wactawski-Wende, University at Buffalo School of Public 
Health and Health Professions, Buffalo, New York; Lynne M. Coslett-
Charlton, American College of Obstetricians & Gynecologists, Pittston, 
Pennsylvania; and Jennifer Weiss-Wolf, NYU School of Law, New York, New 
York.
