# RECOGNIZING THE HAITIAN HERITAGE MUSEUM

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE HAITIAN HERITAGE MUSEUM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th district, of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor South 
Florida's Haitian Heritage Museum. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas, the Haitian Heritage Museum is an integral part of 
     our community, embodying and preserving Haiti's rich culture 
     and heritage;
       Whereas, the Haitian Heritage Museum has given Haitian art 
     a permanent and prominent place in South Florida's art 
     community;
       Whereas, the Haitian Heritage Museum's scholarship program 
     provides deserving students an opportunity to further their 
     education and perfect their talent as artists;
       Whereas, the Haitian Heritage Museum will honor community 
     leaders who have made extraordinary contributions to Haitian 
     heritage and culture during its 9th Annual Discover Haiti 
     Gala on Saturday, February 23rd;
       Whereas, the Haitian Heritage Museum will present the 
     Father Jean Juste Visionary Award, named after the visionary 
     leader who advocated for human rights and social justice on 
     behalf of Haiti's poor;
       Whereas, Father Jean Juste is widely regarded as the 
     ``Godfather'' of Miami's Little Haiti community; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida:
       Recognize the tremendous contributions of the Haitian 
     Heritage Museum to South Florida:

[[Page E688]]

       Affirm the importance of promoting and preserving Haitian 
     art and culture; and
       Honor the recipients of the Father Jean Juste Visionary 
     Award.

                          ____________________
```
