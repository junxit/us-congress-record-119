# CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF DIVERSIFIED ENERGY

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
         CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF DIVERSIFIED ENERGY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the 25th 
anniversary of Diversified Energy. Diversified Energy was founded by 
its current Chief Executive Officer, Rusty Hutson in 2001. Under 
Rusty's continued leadership, Diversified Energy has become an industry 
name in the management and production of low-decline natural gas and 
oil assets.
  Diversified Energy's success can be attributed to its foundational 
business model of long-term growth. Its focus on low decline production 
has led to the extension of well life, reduced the need for new surface 
disruption, and maximized the value of their existing assets.
  Additionally, Diversified Energy has distinguished itself in its 
reduction of methane emission. By implementing continuous monitoring, 
advanced leak detection, and proactive well retirement programs, the 
company has lowered its methane intensity across all operations. 
Diversified Energy's strategy and implementation of these standards 
demonstrate a discipled plan that prioritizes excellence and 
environmental care.
  Above all else, safety will always remain the top priority of 
Diversified Energy. They have established a strong culture of safety 
through routine training, accountability, and continuous improvement. 
Through the implementation of rigorous operational standards, 
Diversified Energy ensures that its employees, contractors, and 
surrounding communities remain safe and protected.
  Mr. Speaker, Diversified Energy's success can be attributed to its 
core values of safety,

[[Page E682]]

long-term planning, and effort to reduce environmental impact. I 
commend Rusty for his success, and I celebrate their accomplishment of 
25 years of service. I look forward to their continued efforts to 
strengthen American energy.

                          ____________________
```
