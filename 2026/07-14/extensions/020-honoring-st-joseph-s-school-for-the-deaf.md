# HONORING ST. JOSEPH'S SCHOOL FOR THE DEAF

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
               HONORING ST. JOSEPH'S SCHOOL FOR THE DEAF

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor St. 
Joseph's School for the Deaf in the Bronx, an institution rooted in 
faith, resilience, and excellence since 1869.
  For over 150 years, this school has empowered deaf and hard-of-
hearing students through a bilingual education in American Sign 
Language and English, fostering critical thinking, independence, and 
strong leadership qualities.
  Under the leadership of Mark Patrick, the faculty ensures that every 
student receives the attention and resources they need to thrive 
academically and socially. As a neighbor in the Bronx, I have witnessed 
firsthand the vital role St. Joseph's plays--not just as a school, but 
as a community pillar whose mission enriches families and uplifts our 
borough.
  Today, we celebrate St. Joseph's dedication to unlocking the 
potential of every child, building confident learners, and advancing 
inclusive education for generations. I thank the faculty, staff, 
families, and students for their tireless commitment and extraordinary 
spirit.
  Please join me in recognizing St. Joseph's School for the Deaf for 
its service to our community and in supporting its future success.

                          ____________________
```
