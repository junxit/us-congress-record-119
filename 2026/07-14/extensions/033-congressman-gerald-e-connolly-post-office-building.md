# CONGRESSMAN GERALD E. CONNOLLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
          CONGRESSMAN GERALD E. CONNOLLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                         HON. MICHAEL R. TURNER

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 13, 2026

  Mr. TURNER. Mr. Speaker, I am proud to support and cosponsor 
legislation designating a postal facility in Fairfax, Virginia in honor 
of our late colleague, and my dear friend, Congressman Gerry Connolly.
  Gerry was a devoted public servant who spent over three decades 
serving the people of Virginia and our Nation with unmatched dedication 
and purpose. He began his career in public service as a member of the 
Fairfax County Board of Supervisors in 1995, and served five years as 
its Chairman, from 2004 to 2009. As Chair of the ten-member board, 
Gerry balanced a budget of $4.5 billion and managed a county that, 
based on its size, would make it the Nation's thirteenth largest city, 
twelfth largest school district, and sixth largest office market.
  Gerry Connolly went on to serve the people of the 11th Congressional 
District of Virginia in

[[Page E687]]

this House from 2009 until 2025. He was recognized by the University of 
Virginia's Center for Effective Lawmaking ``as being the most effective 
member of the House in advancing policies pertaining to government 
operations'' during the 117th Congress, and was also cited as ``being 
the most effective lawmaker in the House overall'' among all Members, 
both Republican and Democrat.
  Gerry understood that the best way to get things done for his 
community and for his country was to work with people from both sides 
of the aisle. We served on the House Oversight and Government Reform 
Committee, where I was proud to partner with him on postal reform 
issues, and on legislation protecting the rights of our federal 
workforce.
  We both had the honor of serving as president of the U.S. Delegation 
to the NATO Parliamentary Assembly. There was no stronger champion in 
our NATO Parliamentary Assembly than Gerry Connolly for the importance 
of NATO, which he called ``the indispensable bulwark for democracy 
itself.''
  Gerry's impact on the Commonwealth of Virginia and his country will 
not soon be forgotten. His legacy will live on for years to come, both 
in his work and in the many lives he touched. I was honored to serve 
with him in this chamber, and it was an even greater honor to call him 
my friend.
  It is appropriate that we honor Gerry in the community he served by 
naming this post office as a tribute to that all he did to make his 
Congressional district, and our Nation, a better place. May his life 
and memory be a blessing to us all.
```
