# RECOGNIZING THE 35TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SULLY DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE 35TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SULLY DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 35th 
Anniversary of the Sully District, the westernmost supervisor district 
in Fairfax County, Virginia, which includes communities such as 
Centreville, Chantilly, Oak Hill, Oakton, and surrounding 
neighborhoods.
  Established following the 1990 Census, the Sully District has grown 
into a vibrant and diverse community that reflects both the history and 
future of Northern Virginia. Over the past three and a half decades, 
the district has experienced significant growth and development while 
maintaining a strong sense of community and commitment to quality of 
life for its residents.
  The Sully District has long been shaped by thoughtful leadership at 
the local level. The district's first supervisor, Michael Frey, served 
from its creation through his retirement after more than two decades in 
office. During his tenure, he helped guide the district through a 
period of rapid population growth and transformation, playing a key 
role in advancing infrastructure, expanding public safety services, 
supporting youth programs, and preserving community assets. His 
leadership helped establish the foundation upon which the Sully 
District continues to thrive today.
  Today, the Sully District is represented by Supervisor Kathy Smith, 
who continues to build on that legacy through her tireless service to 
the residents of the Sully District. Her leadership reflects an ongoing 
commitment to meeting the needs of a growing community while ensuring 
that residents remain connected to local government and services.
  As the Sully District marks its 35th anniversary, residents come 
together to celebrate not only its history but also the people and 
partnerships that have contributed to its growth and success. This 
milestone reflects decades of dedication to building a community where 
families can live, work, and thrive.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the 
Sully District on its 35th anniversary and in recognizing its continued 
contributions to Fairfax County and the Commonwealth of Virginia.
```
