# RECOGNIZING MAY AS MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS MONTH

**Speaking:** Walkinshaw, James R. (W000831) [D-VA]

```
            RECOGNIZING MAY AS MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JAMES R. WALKINSHAW

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize May 2026 as 
Mental Health Awareness Month and to reaffirm our shared commitment to 
raising awareness, reducing stigma, and providing compassionate support 
for those affected by mental health challenges.
  Since 1949, Mental Health Awareness Month has served as an 
opportunity to increase understanding and encourage early support for 
those living with mental health conditions. It also highlights the 
importance of making care accessible to individuals and families across 
the country.
  Mental health is a critical part of overall well-being. It shapes how 
people navigate daily life, respond to challenges, and connect with 
others. Each year, millions of Americans experience mental health 
conditions, yet many still face obstacles when seeking care.
  In communities across Northern Virginia, dedicated providers, 
advocates, and organizations are working every day to expand access to 
care and support those in need. Their efforts help ensure that 
individuals and families receive the services, understanding, and 
respect they deserve.
  Mental Health Awareness Month also reminds us of the importance of 
building strong, supportive communities where individuals feel safe 
seeking help. By promoting awareness and investing in mental health 
services, we can help ensure that no one faces these challenges alone.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing May 2026 
as Mental Health Awareness Month and in supporting continued efforts to 
improve mental health care, expand access to services, and promote the 
well-being of individuals and families across our Nation.
```
