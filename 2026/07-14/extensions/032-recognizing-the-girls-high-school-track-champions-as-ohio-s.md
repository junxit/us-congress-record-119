# RECOGNIZING THE GIRLS HIGH SCHOOL TRACK CHAMPIONS AS OHIO'S 13TH DISTRICT CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE GIRLS HIGH SCHOOL TRACK CHAMPIONS AS OHIO'S 13TH 
                     DISTRICT CHAMPION OF THE WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize six extraordinary 
high school athletes as Ohio's 13th Congressional District's Champions 
of the Week.
  Last month, these young women demonstrated excellence, determination, 
and perseverance on Ohio's biggest high school track and field stage.
  Jackson High School junior Gabby Whalen soared to a Division I state 
championship in the girls pole vault, showcasing the hard work and 
dedication that have made her one of Ohio's premier athletes.
  The Buchtel High School girls 800-meter relay team of Jada Davis, 
Joslynne Bushner, Devonnah Smith, and Miniya Mitchell captured a state 
championship through teamwork, discipline, and an unwavering commitment 
to one another.
  Their victory is a testament to the proud tradition of excellence at 
Buchtel and was just 0.2 seconds shy of the state record.
  Finally, Tallmadge High School senior Allison Hay closed out her 
remarkable high school career by winning the Division II state 
championship in the 800 meters, an accomplishment that reflects years 
of dedication and perseverance.
  These young women have represented their schools, families, and 
communities with distinction. Their achievements remind us why Ohio's 
13th Congressional District continues to be the ``Birthplace of 
Champions.''
  I congratulate Gabby, Jada, Joslynne, Devonnah, Miniya, and Allison 
on their outstanding accomplishments and proudly recognize them as the 
Champions of the Week for Ohio's 13th Congressional District.

                          ____________________
```
