# CONGRATULATING COACH DAN GRAYBILL ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE MANHEIM TOWNSHIP SWIMMING TEAM

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
 CONGRATULATING COACH DAN GRAYBILL ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE MANHEIM 
                         TOWNSHIP SWIMMING TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate and recognize Coach 
Dan Graybill on his retirement after 40 years of dedication to the 
Manheim Township High School Blue Streaks swimming team.
  Coach Dan Graybill led the Blue Streaks boys' program for 40 years 
and guided the girls' program for 39 years. Under his leadership as 
head coach, the girls team secured 15 Lancaster-Lebanon League team 
titles, while the boys team earned 11 titles. Together, the Blue 
Streaks amassed a total of 31 section crowns during his tenure.
  Beyond his work at Manheim Township High School, Coach Graybill also 
contributed to the sport through coaching positions at the Lancaster 
YMCA and Overlook, a team in the Lancaster County Summer Swim League.
  Coach Graybill has left a profound impact on the Manheim Township 
swimming community. I congratulate Dan Graybill on his well-deserved 
retirement, and I wish him the best wherever the future may lead him.

                          ____________________
```
