# HONORING THE SERVICE OF TONY ACEVEDO

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
                  HONORING THE SERVICE OF TONY ACEVEDO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 14, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the remarkable career and dedicated service of Mr. Tony Acevedo of the 
Memorial Service Detachment in Corpus Christi, Texas.
  Mr. Acevedo has devoted his life to serving both his country and his 
community. Drafted to the United States Army in 1972, he bravely served 
in Vietnam with the 3rd Brigade, 1st Cavalry Division, and the 525th 
Military Intelligence Expeditionary Brigade. His efforts in 
transporting soldiers, equipment, supplies, and classified messages 
were essential to advancing the efforts of the U.S. military.
  After leaving the service in 1973, Mr. Acevedo earned his bachelor's 
degree in criminal justice at the University of Texas Pan American and 
valiantly continued his service as a police officer with the Corpus 
Christi Police Department, where he served for over 30 years, including 
18 years as a hostage negotiator. Throughout his law enforcement 
career, he has demonstrated extraordinary courage and an unwavering 
commitment to protecting the people of South Texas.
  After retiring from the force, Mr. Acevedo joined the Memorial 
Service Detachment Honor Guard, where he participated in more than 
4,150 military burial ceremonies, laying U.S. servicemen and women to 
rest with honor and respect. His dedication has brought comfort and 
support to countless military families during some of their most 
difficult moments.
  Mr. Speaker, throughout his life, Mr. Acevedo has displayed a clear 
dedication to public service to our Nation and the Corpus Christi 
community. His decades of selfless service exemplify the very best of 
public dedication and patriotism which deserves to be recognized.
```
