# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2140, H.R. 3194, H.R. 3978, H.R. 5549, H.R. 9084, H.R. 9317, H.R. 9497, H.R. 9612, H.R. 9613, H.R. 9614, H.R. 9615, H.R. 9616, H.R. 9617, H.R. 9618

```
Committee Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--SUPREME COURT OF THE UNITED STATES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Financial Services and 
General Government held a budget hearing on the Supreme Court of the 
United States. Testimony was heard from Elena Kagan, Associate Justice, 
Supreme Court of the United States; and Amy Coney Barrett, Associate 
Justice, Supreme Court of the United States.
U.S. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Financial Services and 
General Government held an oversight hearing on the U.S. Small Business 
Administration. Testimony was heard from Kelly Loeffler, Administrator, 
U.S. Small Business Administration.

[[Page D705]]


TRAINING ACTIVISTS, NOT PHYSICIANS: THE IMPACT OF DIVERSITY, EQUITY, 
AND INCLUSION ON MEDICAL SCHOOLS
Committee on Education and Workforce: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Training Activists, Not Physicians: The Impact of DEI on 
Medical Schools''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Energy held a markup 
on H.R. 3978, the ``Nuclear REFUEL Act''; H.R. 5549, the ``Efficient 
Nuclear Licensing Hearings Act''; H.R. 9084, the ``Department of Energy 
Nuclear Transparency Act''; H.R. 9612, the ``American Enrichment 
Deployment Act''; H.R. 9613, the ``Nuclear Advisory Committee Reform 
Act''; and H.R. 9614, the ``NRC Staff Pay Alignment Act''. H.R. 9612, 
H.R. 5549, H.R. 9613, and H.R. 9614 were forwarded to the full 
Committee, without amendment. H.R. 3978 and H.R. 9084 were forwarded to 
the full Committee, as amended.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Environment held a 
markup on H.R. 2140, the ``Diesel Emissions Reduction Act of 2025''; 
H.R. 3194, the ``Lifting Overburdensome Commerce Obstructions and 
Motives Act''; H.R. 9317, the ``Buses Utilizing Safety and 
Environmental Standards Act''; H.R. 9615, the ``Battery Recycling for 
America's Competitive Economy Act''; H.R. 9616, the ``Environmental 
Monitoring and Remediation Technology Assessment Imitative Act of 
2026''; H.R. 9617, the ``Coordinating and Harnessing America's Recovery 
of Minerals Act''; and H.R. 9618, the ``Diesel Engine Flexibility 
Act''. H.R. 2140, H.R. 3194, H.R. 9317, H.R. 9616, and H.R. 9617 were 
forwarded to the full Committee, without amendment. H.R. 9615 and H.R. 
9618 were forwarded to the full Committee, as amended.
THE FEDERAL RESERVE'S SEMI-ANNUAL MONETARY POLICY REPORT
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``The Federal Reserve's Semi-Annual Monetary Policy Report''. Testimony 
was heard from Kevin M. Warsh, Chairman, Board of Governors of the 
Federal Reserve System.
FISCAL YEAR 27 BUREAU OF INDUSTRY AND SECURITY BUDGET: THE ARTIFICIAL 
INTELLIGENCE ARMS RACE AND THE INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATIONS 
TECHNOLOGY AND SERVICES OFFICE
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``FY27 BIS Budget: the AI Arms Race and the ICTS Office''. Testimony 
was heard from Jeffrey Kessler, Under Secretary of Commerce for 
Industry and Security, Department of Commerce.
MORE BANG FOR THE BUCK: ALIGNING COMMERCIAL DIPLOMACY BETWEEN STATE AND 
COMMERCE
Committee on Foreign Affairs: East Asia and the Pacific Subcommittee; 
and South and Central Asia Subcommittee held a joint hearing entitled 
``More Bang for the Buck: Aligning Commercial Diplomacy Between State 
and Commerce''. Testimony was heard from David L. Fogel, Assistant 
Secretary, Commerce and Director General of the United States and 
Foreign Commercial Service, International Trade Administration, 
Department of Commerce; and Caleb Orr, Assistant Secretary, Bureau of 
Economic, Energy, and Business Affairs, Department of State.
U.S. ECONOMIC SECURITY AND INVESTMENT ABROAD: ASSESSING ECONOMIC 
SECURITY ZONES
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Oversight and Intelligence Subcommittee 
held a hearing entitled ``U.S. Economic Security and Investment Abroad: 
Assessing Economic Security Zones''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGIES AND INITIATIVES TO TACKLE THE MISSING AND 
MURDERED INDIGENOUS PEOPLES CRISIS IN INDIAN COUNTRY
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations; and Subcommittee on Indian and Insular Affairs held a 
joint hearing entitled ``Innovative Technologies and Initiatives to 
Tackle the MMIP Crisis in Indian Country''. Testimony was heard from 
Charles Addington, Principal Director, Office of Justice Services, 
Bureau of Indian Affairs, Department of the Interior; and public 
witnesses.
COMBATING DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION IN AMERICAN INSTITUTIONS
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Task Force on Defending 
Constitutional Rights and Exposing Institutional Abuses held a hearing 
entitled ``Combating DEI in American Institutions''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ON MAIN STREET: HOW ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IS 
SHAPING THE FUTURE OF SMALL BUSINESS
Committee on Small Business: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``AI on Main Street: How AI is Shaping the Future of Small Business''. 
Testimony

[[Page D706]]

was heard from Jeffrey Kessler, Under Secretary of Commerce for 
Industry and Security, Department of Commerce.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Full Committee held a 
markup on updated Subcommittee Rosters due to changes on the Majority 
side; General Services Administration Capital Investment and Leasing 
Program Resolutions; and H.R. 9497, the ``Water Resources Development 
Act of 2026''. Updated Subcommittee Rosters were approved. Two General 
Services Administration Capital Investment and Leasing Program 
Resolutions were agreed to. H.R. 9497 was ordered reported, as amended.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
