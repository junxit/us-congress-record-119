# CONGRATULATING TANQUE VERDE HIGH SCHOOL SOFTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Ciscomani, Juan (C001133) [R-AZ]

```
         CONGRATULATING TANQUE VERDE HIGH SCHOOL SOFTBALL TEAM

  (Mr. Ciscomani of Arizona was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Tanque Verde 
High School softball team on winning the 2026 3A State championship 
title.
  The Hawks earned the title by defeating Yuma Catholic by a 10-0 
score.
  This victory marks the team's first-ever championship, an 
accomplishment that is exciting and worth celebrating.
  During the championship game, Tanque Verde added their first run on 
the board in the third inning, before adding six runs in the fourth, 
one in the fifth, and two more in the sixth.
  Junior pitcher Claire Achilles was named the Class 3A MVP after 
pitching six innings, allowing a hit and recording 13 strikeouts.
  Additionally, sophomores Mikaela Jackson and Ella Haggard each had 
three hits during the game.
  The Hawks gave all they had and came out on top. I am so proud of 
them.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Coach Mark Stevens, the whole team, and 
everyone who made this win possible. Their hard work has made their 
school and our community proud. I know the Hawks will enjoy their 
victory. They have earned it, and here is to another

[[Page H4417]]

successful season and many, many more.


      Congratulating Salpointe Catholic High School Softball Team

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Salpointe 
Catholic High School softball team on winning the 2026 4A State 
championship title.
  This victory marks the team's seventh title in the last nine years.
  Confident in the team's ability to win this year's title, Salpointe 
Coach Tricia Sztan and her staff ordered shirts with the slogan 
``#TakeItBack2026'' written on the back.
  At the beginning of the fifth inning, senior second baseman Alyssa 
Montano led with a single and advanced to second with the help of 
senior pitcher-outfielder Soli Ramos.
  Then Catherine ``Peaches'' Sztan hit an RBI single to center field 
that scored Montano and tied up the game at 2.
  The Lancers finished the fifth inning with three runs after an RBI 
single by senior catcher Sarah Camp and RBI double by senior first 
baseman CC Moraga with two outs which put the Lancers ahead 4-2.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Coach Sztan, the whole team, and everyone 
who made this win possible. Their hard work has made their school and 
our community very proud. I know the Lancers will enjoy their victory. 
They have earned it, and here is to another successful season and many, 
many more.


      Congratulating the Canyon Del Oro High School Softball Team

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Canyon 
del Oro High School softball team on winning the 2026 5A State 
championship title.
  After three runner-up finishes in the past five seasons, the Dorados 
came out on top this year with a score of 4-3 in the championship game, 
finishing the season 29-6.
  This victory marks the team's first State title since 2017. It is 
also Coach Kelly Fowler's third title since starting at CDO.
  During the game, junior first baseman Emma Shaffer scored a three-run 
home run at the top of the first inning, putting the Dorados up 3-0 
early in the game.
  The game was tied up 3-3 for a short time, but the tie was broken 
after Shaffer forced a walk to load the bases and freshman second 
baseman Brooklyn Benavides moved the score to 4-3.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Coach Kelly Fowler, the whole team, and 
everyone who made this win possible. Their hard work has made their 
school and our community very proud. I know the Dorados will enjoy 
their victory. They have earned it, and here is to another successful 
season and many, many more.
```
