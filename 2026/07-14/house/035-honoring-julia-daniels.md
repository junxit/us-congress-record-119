# HONORING JULIA DANIELS

**Speaking:** Ross, Deborah K. (R000305) [D-NC]

```
                         HONORING JULIA DANIELS

  (Ms. ROSS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. ROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Julia Daniels, a remarkable North Carolinian whose generosity, 
leadership, and love for our State touched countless lives.
  Born in New Bern and raised in Raleigh, Julia dedicated herself to 
preserving and enriching North Carolina's culture and civic 
institutions. She was a champion of the arts, history, and education, 
helping strengthen organizations, including the North Carolina Museum 
of Art and the North Carolina Historical Commission.
  Julia was often called a professional volunteer, but her impact went 
far beyond that title. To many, Julia was the embodiment of grace and 
service. She opened her heart to causes she believed in, inspiring 
generations through her kindness and civic spirit.
  As we mourn her passing, we also celebrate an exceptional life and a 
legacy that will endure across North Carolina for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
