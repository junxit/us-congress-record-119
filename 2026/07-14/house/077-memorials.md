# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 9, H.J.Res. 727

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-106. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Utah, relative to House Concurrent Resolution 
     9, urging federal assistance in stabilizing and restoring the 
     Great Salt Lake; jointly to the Committees on Natural 
     Resources and Transportation and Infrastructure.
       ML-107. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Tennessee, relative to House Joint Resolution 
     727, urging the United States Congress to enact legislation 
     to bring home USS Intrepid sailors entombed in Libya; jointly 
     to the Committees on Veterans' Affairs and Armed Services.
```
