# HONORING LATAVIA STURDIVANT

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                      HONORING LATAVIA STURDIVANT

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Latavia Sturdivant, a 
disability advocate, Yonkers resident, New York City Schools educator, 
author, Lehman College professor, and now Ms. Wheelchair America 2026.
  Ms. Wheelchair America was established on the principle of giving a 
platform to women who use wheelchairs, showing that they are more than 
their disability. The pageant crowns their winner based on advocacy, 
leadership, and achievements.
  This title could not reflect Ms. Sturdivant more. Her passion and 
accomplishments are noted and rooted in resilience, service, and 
passion for breaking barriers for wheelchair users. Her motto: ``Don't 
pay attention to what we roll in, pay attention to how we roll'' speaks 
to her will to be a voice in the community.
  Mr. Speaker, she is an inspiration to all of us, and from the floor 
of the House of Representatives, I extend my congratulations to Latavia 
on this remarkable accomplishment.
```
