# RECOGNIZING AND HONORING MINNESOTA STATE SENATOR STEVE CWODZINSKI

**Speaking:** Morrison, Kelly (M001234) [D-MN]

```
   RECOGNIZING AND HONORING MINNESOTA STATE SENATOR STEVE CWODZINSKI

  (Ms. MORRISON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. MORRISON. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize and honor 
Minnesota State Senator Steve Cwodzinski for his lifelong dedication to 
education, civic engagement, and public service.
  Senator Cwodzinski began his career in public service as a public 
school teacher. For over 30 years, he inspired students to become 
informed and engaged citizens.
  Senator Cwodzinski brought that same passion for public service to 
the Minnesota State Senate where he was first elected in 2016. Drawing 
on his background as an educator, he became a leading advocate for 
students and teachers across Minnesota.
  In addition to his championing of students and educators, he is a 
passionate advocate for protecting the Boundary Waters and chief author 
of legislation for permanent protection of Minnesota's crown jewel.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Senator Cwodzinski is a 
devoted husband to his wife, Patti; a proud father of two children; and 
grandfather of two grandchildren. His love of family, community, and 
country has shaped every aspect of his life and service.
  On behalf of the people of Minnesota, I extend my sincere gratitude 
for his years of dedicated service and wish him all the best in his 
future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
