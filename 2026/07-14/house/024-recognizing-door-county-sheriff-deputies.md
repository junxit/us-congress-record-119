# RECOGNIZING DOOR COUNTY SHERIFF DEPUTIES

**Speaking:** Wied, Tony (W000829) [R-WI]

```
                RECOGNIZING DOOR COUNTY SHERIFF DEPUTIES

  (Mr. Wied of Wisconsin was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sergeant Thomas 
Lemke and Deputy Triston Beauchamp of the Door County Sheriff's Office, 
along with Gary Robert Rasheed Daughtery, for their actions that helped 
save a driver's life following a serious crash.
  The crash occurred after the driver experienced a medical emergency, 
causing his vehicle to leave the roadway. Without hesitation, Mr. 
Daughtery stopped to help and removed the driver from the vehicle so he 
could receive immediate care.
  Sergeant Lemke and Deputy Beauchamp quickly began CPR and used an AED 
until ambulance crews arrived to begin patient care.
  Their decisive response and teamwork played a critical role in giving 
the driver a chance to survive. Faced with a life-or-death emergency, 
each of these individuals stepped forward, without hesitation, and did 
everything within their power to save a life.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in commending their 
courage and expressing our profound gratitude for their service to the 
people of Door County in northeastern Wisconsin.


                  Recognizing Pagel's Ponderosa Dairy

  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pagel's Ponderosa 
Dairy as it celebrates its 80th anniversary.
  Founded in 1946 by Carl and Garnet Pagel, Pagel's Ponderosa Dairy 
began with just eight milking cows on their family homestead in 
Kewaunee County. What started as a small family farm has grown across 
generations into one of northeastern Wisconsin's most respected dairy 
operations.
  Over the last 80 years, the Pagel family has helped strengthen 
Wisconsin's proud dairy tradition. Under their stewardship, Pagel's 
Ponderosa currently milks thousands of cows, employs more than 150 
people, and remains a leader in northeastern Wisconsin's agriculture 
and dairy industries.
  The Pagel family continues to maintain a strong commitment to caring 
for their animals, land, employees, and their community, reflecting the 
very best of northeastern Wisconsin agriculture.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Pagel's 
Ponderosa Dairy on 80 years of hard work and service to the Kewaunee 
community in northeastern Wisconsin. I wish them continued success for 
many generations to come.


                         Honoring Dave Schmidt

  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Dave Schmidt, a dedicated servant of the Clintonville community.
  Dave sadly passed away on June 8 after a courageous battle with 
cancer. For many years, he gave his time and energy to the Clintonville 
Area Food Pantry, helping make sure local families had food, support, 
and someone willing to answer the call when they needed help.
  Dave was not someone who served for praise or recognition. He simply 
saw a need, and he stepped forward. In recognition of that legacy, the 
Clintonville Area Food Pantry will now be known as Dave's Pantry.
  This name is a fitting tribute to a man whose kindness touched 
countless lives across northeastern Wisconsin.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join the people of northeastern 
Wisconsin and me in thanking Dave Schmidt for his life of service. My 
prayers are with his family, his friends, and those who knew and loved 
him.


               Congratulating Deacon Mike Vander Bloomen

  Mr. WIED. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Deacon Mike 
Vander Bloomen on his retirement from the parish staff at Our Lady of 
Lourdes Catholic Church and for his 25 years of faithful service.
  Deacon Vander Bloomen began serving in Our Lady of Lourdes in 2001 
and was ordained in 2003. Since then, whether visiting the elderly, 
offering guidance through troubling times, or using personal stories to 
connect with parishioners, he has supported countless people and helped 
strengthen their faith.
  Although he is retiring from the parish staff, Deacon Vander Bloomen 
will continue preaching and lending a hand at Our Lady of Lourdes where 
he can. His willingness to continue serving speaks to his love for his 
faith and his dedication to the community that he has served for 25 
years.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Deacon Mike Vander Bloomen on his many 
years of service and wish him all the best in his well-deserved 
retirement.
```
