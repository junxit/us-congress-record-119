# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION AND INFRASTRUCTURE

```
      COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION AND 
                             INFRASTRUCTURE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the chair of the Committee on Transportation and 
Infrastructure, which was read and referred to the Committee on 
Appropriations:

         Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure, House of 
           Representatives,
                                    Washington, DC, July 14, 2026.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: On July 14, 2026, pursuant to section 
     3307 of Title 40, United States Code, the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure met in open session, with a 
     quorum being present, to consider two resolutions included in 
     the General Services Administration's Capital Investment and 
     Leasing Programs.
       I have enclosed copies of the resolutions adopted by the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure on July 14, 
     2026.
           Sincerely,
                                                       Sam Graves,
         Chairman, Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
       Enclosures.

                          Committee Resolution


           alteration--optimization program various buildings

       Resolved by the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure of the United States House of Representatives, 
     that pursuant to 40 U.S.C. Sec. 3307, appropriations are 
     authorized for the reconfiguration and renovation of core 
     assets under the U.S. General Services Administrations's 
     (GSA) jurisdiction, custody, or control to support efforts to 
     optimize space configuration and performance; deliver the 
     best value in real estate to GSA's customer agencies across 
     the government; reduce the government's footprint; and 
     achieve significant annual cost savings for taxpayers 
     consistent with the spend plan provided to the Committe on 
     December 3, 2025, dated October 2025, at a total cost of 
     $254,710,000, a prospectus for which is attached to and 
     included as part of this resolution. This resolution amends 
     the Committee resolution adopted by the Committee on July 15, 
     2025 by providing additional authorization of $104,710,000.
       Provided, that General Services Administration shall not 
     delegate to any agency the authority granted by this 
     resolution.
       Provided further, an updated Expenditure Plan be submitted 
     to the Committee prior to expenditure of any funds.
       Provided further, projects funded shall demonstrate a clear 
     connection to long-term footprint reduction, including but 
     not limited to sale, disposal, relinquishment, or substantial 
     reductions in rentable square footage or financial costs.
       Provided further, projects funded conform with the 
     requirements established pursuant to the Thomas R. Carper 
     Water Resources Development Act of 2024 (P.L. 118-272), 
     including building utilization requirements and notification 
     of milestones to the Committee.
       Provided further, not later than 30 calendar days after the 
     date on which a request from

     Transportation and Infrastructure of the House of the 
     Representatives is received by the Administrator of General

                          Committee Resolution


           alteration--optimization program various buildings

       Resolved by the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure of the United States House of Representatives, 
     that pursuant to 40 U.S.C. Sec. 3307, appropriations are 
     authorized for the reconfiguration and renovation of core 
     assets under the U.S. General Services Administration's (GSA) 
     jurisdiction, custody, or control to support efforts to 
     optimize space configuration and performance; deliver the 
     best value in real estate to GSA's customer agencies across 
     the government; reduce the government's footprint; and 
     achieve significant annual cost savings for taxpayers related 
     to the relocation of the Department of Energy (DOE) 
     headquarters in Washington, D.C. at a total cost of 
     $215,553,000, a prospectus for which is attached to and 
     included as part of this resolution.
       Provided, that General Services Administration shall not 
     delegate to any agency the authority granted by this 
     resolution.
       Provided further, that should the actual costs associated 
     with the relocation of the DOE headquarters be less than the 
     total cost authorized, the Administrator of General Services 
     may expend excess authorized funds on the additional 
     optimization projects only if an Expenditure Plan is 
     submitted to the Committee prior to expenditure of any funds.
       Provided further, projects funded shall demonstrate a clear 
     connection to long-term footprint reduction, including but 
     not limited to sale, disposal, relinquishment, or substantial 
     reductions in rentable square footage or financial costs.
       Provided further, projects funded conform with the 
     requirements established pursuant to the Thomas R. Carper 
     Water Resources Development Act of 2024 (P.L. 118-272), 
     including building utilization requirements and notification 
     of milestones to the Committee.
       Provided further, not later than 30 calendar days after the 
     date on which a request from the Chair or Ranking Member of 
     the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure of the 
     House of the Representatives is received by the Administrator 
     of General Services, the Administrator shall provide such 
     Member a response in writing that provides any information 
     requested regarding the program.
```
