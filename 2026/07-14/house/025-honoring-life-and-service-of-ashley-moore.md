# HONORING LIFE AND SERVICE OF ASHLEY MOORE

**Speaking:** Moore, Tim (M001236) [R-NC]

```
                              {time}  1130
               HONORING LIFE AND SERVICE OF ASHLEY MOORE

  (Mr. Moore of North Carolina was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Mr. MOORE of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today I rise on a sad 
occasion but to honor the life and the service of Ashley Moore of my 
home county, Cleveland County, North Carolina, who tragically passed 
away just one day after celebrating her 37th birthday.
  On July 6 of this year while Ashley was a paramedic with the 
Cleveland County EMS, while she and her team were responding to an 
emergency, an impaired driver struck the ambulance from behind, 
injuring the patient, another paramedic, and unfortunately, killing 
Ashley.
  Ashley served Cleveland County for 9 years as a paramedic, a field 
training officer, and an EMS instructor. She dedicated her life to 
caring for others with compassion and professionalism.
  Every day, first responders put themselves in harm's way to protect 
their communities, and Ashley embodied the very best of that calling, 
putting the needs of others before her own until her very final 
moments.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House join me in praying 
that God will bring comfort and strength to Ashley's family and her 
fellow first responders during this time.
```
