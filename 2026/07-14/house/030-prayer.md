# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Bless us, O Lord, this day as we place our trust in You. Sure of Your 
guidance and hopeful for Your favor, we put our confidence in You.
  Let our faith in You be our source of strength. Like a tree planted 
by the water, may everything that is within us reach to You to sustain 
us in the work set before us.
  When the heat of argument or the intensity of debate becomes too 
much, may we draw from Your living waters the refreshment and calm of 
Your quiet presence.
  When our worries and frustrations inflame our words and ignite our 
anger, may we find coolness in the streams of Your peace and 
righteousness.
  Lord, search our hearts that they would be found faithful. Examine 
our minds that they would prove blameless. Reward us according to our 
conduct this day and judge us according to the fruit of our deeds.
  We appeal to Your mercy and pray in Your holy name with gratitude for 
Your undeserved grace.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
