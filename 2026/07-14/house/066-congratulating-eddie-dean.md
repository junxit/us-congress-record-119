# CONGRATULATING EDDIE DEAN

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
                       CONGRATULATING EDDIE DEAN

  (Mr. VINDMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Eddie Dean on 
his induction into the inaugural class of Madison County High School's 
Athletic Hall of Fame.
  Eddie is a legendary football coach who led the Madison County 
program from 1967 to 2005. His impressive record includes winning three 
State championships.
  Through Eddie's devotion to coaching, he has built a legacy of hard 
work, but what makes him so deserving of this recognition is not just 
what he accomplished on the field, but the time he took to better his 
players off of it. He instilled in them a sense of community, 
discipline, and integrity.
  Eddie's leadership and unwavering commitment to this program are 
truly deserving of this recognition, and I am honored to name him this 
week's Constituent of the Week. I congratulate him and thank him for 
all he does for these young athletes.

                          ____________________
```
