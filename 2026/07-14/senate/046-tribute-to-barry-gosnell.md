# TRIBUTE TO BARRY GOSNELL

**Speaking:** Britt, Katie Boyd (B001319) [R-AL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BARRY GOSNELL

● Mrs. BRITT. Mr. President, I am honored to recognize Mr. Barry 
Gosnell, an exceptional public servant, business leader, and innovator 
whose more than four decades of leadership in government and industry 
have strengthened our national defense, advanced technological 
innovation, and fostered economic growth throughout the Tennessee 
Valley and beyond.
  A longtime leader at Redstone Arsenal, Mr. Gosnell devoted 28 years 
of civilian service to advancing engineering excellence, innovation, 
and mission effectiveness in support of the American warfighter. Most 
recently serving as deputy director of the Software Engineering 
Directorate, he helped lead one of the Army's most vital technical 
organizations through a period of remarkable growth and transformation. 
As a principal architect of that success, Mr. Gosnell played a central 
role in the directorate's 40-percent increase in business revenue, 
reaching $1.1 billion in fiscal year 2012, while the organization 
expanded to more than 3,000 government and contract engineers 
supporting some of our Nation's most important defense programs.
  Before serving as deputy director, Mr. Gosnell led the Acquisition 
and Technology Engineering Division for 14 years and earned a 
reputation as a

[[Page S3934]]

trusted and highly capable executive leader. During his tenure, he 
oversaw the reassignment of engineers and technical personnel to 
establish two new divisions, strengthening organizational capability 
and increasing government engineering capacity by 15 percent. Over the 
course of his leadership, he simultaneously served in multiple 
demanding roles, including deputy director, fire support division 
chief, head of special projects, and oversight of critical business 
management functions, reflecting the confidence his superiors and 
colleagues consistently placed in his leadership.
  Following his distinguished government career, Mr. Gosnell continued 
his service to the defense community through his leadership at COLSA 
Corporation. As chief executive officer, he has overseen one of the 
most successful periods in the company's history. Under his guidance, 
COLSA achieved record growth, generating more than $400 million in new 
revenue during 2024 and 2025 alone. Despite significant industry-wide 
challenges resulting from Federal contract reviews and reductions in 
professional services spending, COLSA remained resilient and well-
positioned for continued success. Mr. Gosnell fostered a culture of 
collaboration, accountability, and open communication, empowering 
leaders throughout the organization to contribute ideas and pursue 
innovative solutions. He strengthened strategic partnerships across the 
Huntsville and Eglin defense communities, resulting in increased prime 
contract awards and valuable teaming opportunities. Recognizing the 
importance of disciplined growth, he established rigorous business 
development processes that enhanced corporate performance and 
maintained a strong pipeline of future opportunities. He also helped 
position COLSA at the forefront of emerging technologies by leading the 
company's expansion into uncrewed systems, assembling highly skilled 
teams and helping bring innovative commercial drone technologies to 
market. Throughout his tenure, he remained steadfastly focused on long-
term growth while ensuring that every decision reflected the four 
values established by COLSA's founder, Dr. Frank J. Collazo: teamwork, 
integrity, people, and customer satisfaction.
  Mr. Gosnell's career has been defined by innovation and forward-
thinking leadership. He played a pivotal role in advancing the use of 
game-based technologies for military training and recruiting, helping 
bring to life the groundbreaking America's Army program, which reached 
more than 10 million registered users and became one of the most widely 
recognized online military engagement tools in the world. His vision in 
leveraging emerging technology helped modernize how the Army 
communicates with and inspires future generations of servicemembers.
  His contributions to cyber security and systems engineering have 
likewise left a lasting mark. Mr. Gosnell was instrumental in 
developing Software Engineering Directorate capabilities to secure 
critical operational and training systems against evolving cyber 
threats, strengthening the Army's information assurance posture during 
a period of rapidly increasing digital risk. He also played a central 
role in expanding systems integration laboratory concepts to aviation 
platforms, enabling improved interoperability, enhanced testing, and 
significant cost savings across multiple Army airframes, including the 
AH-64 Apache, UH-60 Black Hawk, CH-47 Chinook, and other mission-
critical platforms.
  Throughout his distinguished career, Mr. Gosnell consistently 
demonstrated an unwavering commitment to strengthening America's 
defense capabilities while fostering technical excellence within 
government and industry alike. His work in missile defense systems 
testing, aviation interoperability, cyber security, business 
leadership, and organizational development has had an enduring impact, 
directly supporting the readiness of our armed forces and the strength 
of Alabama's defense industrial base.
  I am pleased to join my colleagues and the citizens of Alabama in 
expressing our sincere gratitude to Mr. Barry Gosnell for his 
extraordinary leadership, innovation, and faithful service to the U.S. 
Army, the defense community, and our Nation. His career stands as a 
testament to excellence in public service, principled leadership, and a 
steadfast commitment to those he serves. Mr. Gosnell is truly an 
outstanding American.●
```
