# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

```
                    ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. If the Senator-designate will now present 
herself at the desk, the Chair will administer the oath of office.
  The Senator-designate, Darline Graham, escorted by Mr. Scott of South 
Carolina and Mrs. Britt, advanced to the desk of the President pro 
tempore; the oath prescribed by law was administered to her by the 
President pro tempore; and she subscribed to the oath in the Official 
Oath Book.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Congratulations, Senator.
  (Applause, Senators rising.)

                          ____________________
```
