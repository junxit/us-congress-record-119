# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious and almighty God, source of all wisdom, righteousness, and 
truth, we come before You with humble hearts, acknowledging our need 
for Your guidance. Lord, we pray for the Members of this legislative 
body. Grant them wisdom to discern what is right, courage to do what is 
just, and humility to seek Your direction in every decision. Inspire 
them to think Your thoughts, listen for Your guidance, and faithfully 
serve the people in their care. Remind them that true strength and 
lasting hope are found in Your sovereign power. Sustain them in Your 
mighty hand. Bless them with unity, integrity, and compassion. Lord, 
guide their work for the good of our Nation and the well-being of all 
people.
  We ask these things in Your holy and all-knowing Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
