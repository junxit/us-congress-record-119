# TRIBUTE TO DASHAUN BROWN, RYAN GILLESPIE, AND GIUSEPPE MARTURANO

**Speaking:** Tillis, Thomas (T000476) [R-NC]

```
    TRIBUTE TO DASHAUN BROWN, RYAN GILLESPIE, AND GIUSEPPE MARTURANO

● Mr. TILLIS. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize Dashaun 
Brown, Ryan Gillespie, and Giuseppe Marturano for their heroic actions 
on May 21, 2026. While playing soccer on Wrightsville Beach, NC, Mr. 
Brown, Mr. Gillespie, and Mr. Marturano heard cries for help. As Mr. 
Marturano called 911, Mr. Brown and Mr. Gillespie ran into the water to 
rescue Nicole Woods and her nephew.
  As the current continued to pull Ms. Woods and her nephew farther out 
to sea, Mr. Brown and Mr. Gillespie fought through rough surf and 
reached them. Ms. Woods was able to push her nephew toward the two men, 
and within minutes, first responders arrived and

[[Page S3935]]

brought both Ms. Woods and her nephew to shore.
  Thanks to their bravery and the rapid response of first responders, 
an otherwise tragic outcome was averted. Their actions reflect the very 
best of our great State, and I am proud to recognize these young men 
today.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Dashaun Brown, Ryan 
Gillespie, and Giuseppe Marturano for their extraordinary heroism and 
in expressing our deepest gratitude for their service to others in a 
moment of urgent need.●

                          ____________________
```
