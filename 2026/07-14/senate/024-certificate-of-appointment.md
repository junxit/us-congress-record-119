# CERTIFICATE OF APPOINTMENT

```
                       CERTIFICATE OF APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair lays before the Senate the 
certificate of appointment to fill the vacancy created by the death of 
Lindsey O. Graham of South Carolina. The certificate, the Chair is 
advised, is in the form suggested by the Senate.
  If there is no objection, the reading of the certificate will be 
waived, and it will be printed in full in the Record.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:

                        State of South Carolina


                       Certificate of Appointment

     To the President of the Senate of the United States:
       This is to certify that, pursuant to the power vested in me 
     by the Constitution of the United States and the laws of the 
     State of South Carolina, I, Henry Dargan McMaster, the 
     governor of said State, do hereby appoint Darline Graham a 
     Senator from said State to represent said State in the Senate 
     of the United States until the vacancy therein caused by the 
     death of Lindsey O. Graham, is filled by election as provided 
     by law.
       Witness: His excellency our governor Henry Dargan McMaster, 
     and our seal hereto affixed at the State Capitol this 13th 
     day of July, in the year of our Lord 2026.
           By the Governor:
                                                Henry D. McMaster,
                                                         Governor.
                                                     Mark Hammond,
                                               Secretary of State.
     [State Seal Affixed]

                          ____________________
```
