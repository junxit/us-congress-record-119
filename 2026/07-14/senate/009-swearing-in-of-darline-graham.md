# SWEARING IN OF DARLINE GRAHAM

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     SWEARING IN OF DARLINE GRAHAM

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, before I begin my remarks this morning, I 
want to offer a word of welcome to the new Senator from South Carolina.
  Later today, we will swear in Darline Graham to fill the seat of her 
brother and our friend Lindsey Graham. I know Darline will carry on 
Lindsey's tireless service for South Carolina, and I look forward to 
serving with her.

                          ____________________
```
