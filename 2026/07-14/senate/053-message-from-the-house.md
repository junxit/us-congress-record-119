# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Res. 1424, H.R. 3106, H.R. 3535, H.R. 6332, H.R. 7574, H.R. 7809, H.R. 8168, H.R. 8535, H.R. 8738, H.R. 8770, H.R. 8874, H.R. 8897, H.R. 9328, H.R. 9388, H.R. 9391

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:39 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3106. An act to require the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security to conduct a collective response to a terrorism 
     exercise that includes the management of cascading effects on 
     critical infrastructure during times of extreme cold weather, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3535. An act to amend the Federal Election Campaign 
     Act of 1971 to apply the prohibition against contributions 
     and donations by foreign nationals in connection with 
     elections to contributions or donations in connection with 
     ballot initiatives and referenda.
       H.R. 6332. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 10660 Page Avenue in 
     Fairfax, Virginia, as the ``Congressman Gerald E. Connolly 
     Post Office Building''.
       H.R. 7574. An act to provide for the reorganization and 
     realignment of the Engagement, Liaison, and Outreach Office 
     of the Department of Homeland Security, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 7809. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 201 East Grant Avenue in 
     Georgetown, Ohio, as the ``Ulysses S. Grant Post Office 
     Building''.
       H.R. 8168. An act to require an assessment of terrorism 
     threats to the United States posed by foreign terrorist 
     organizations and Specially Designated Global Terrorists 
     present in countries that are major non-NATO allies, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 8535. An act to direct the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security to develop performance metrics relating to the 
     detection, deterrence, and seizure of illicit fentanyl, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 8738. An act to amend the Federal Election Campaign 
     Act of 1971 to require electronic report filing for persons 
     making certain electioneering communications and to permit 
     political committees to make disbursements other than by 
     check.
       H.R. 8770. An act to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     establish funds for investments in aviation security, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 8874. An act to amend the James M. Inhofe National 
     Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal year 2023 to require 
     recurring reports on the transition of jurisdictions no 
     longer eligible for Urban Area Security Initiative funding, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 8897. An act to improve travel for American families, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 9328. An act to improve travel for active-duty 
     military personnel and accompanying family, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 9388. An act to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     authorize an extension of a program to permit screened 
     passengers and their property arriving on direct flights or 
     flight segments originating at certain foreign last point of 
     departure airports to continue on additional flights or 
     flight segments originating in the United States without 
     additional screening, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 9391. An act to authorize an extension and expansion 
     of the reimbursable screening

[[Page S3936]]

     services program of the Transportation Security 
     Administration, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the House agreed to the following 
resolution:

       H. Res. 1424. Resolution relative to the death of the 
     Honorable Lindsey Graham, a Senator from the State of South 
     Carolina.

                          ____________________
```
