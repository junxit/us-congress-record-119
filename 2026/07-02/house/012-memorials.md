# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 54, S.Res. 125, S.Res. 245, S. 1078, H.R. 1114

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-91. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to Senate Resolution No. 
     125, urging and requesting the United States Navy 
     expeditiously execute a transparent and competitive MUSV 
     acquisition strategy to provide a stable production forecast 
     to prevent the loss of high-tech talent and industrial 
     capacity; to the Committee on Armed Services.
       ML-92. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 19, 
     supporting federal efforts to eliminate the U.S. Department 
     of Education and urges the United States Congress to fully 
     cooperate with these efforts; to the Committee on Education 
     and Workforce.
       ML-93. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 20, 
     supporting federal efforts to eliminate the U.S. Department 
     of Education and urges the United States Congress to fully 
     cooperate with these efforts; to the Committee on Education 
     and Workforce.
       ML-94. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 11, 
     urges the United States Congress to pass and fully fund 
     legislation that fulfills the federal commitment to provide 
     up to 40% of the national average per-pupil expenditure for 
     the excess cost of educating children with disabilities under 
     IDEA; to the Committee on Education and Workforce.
       ML-95. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 10, 
     urging the President of the United States, members of the 
     United States Congress, the Secretary of the United States 
     Department of Agriculture, and the Secretary of the United 
     States Department of Health and Human Services to prohibit 
     the inclusion of artificial sweeteners, specifically 
     aspartame, acesulfame potassium, advantame, neotame, 
     saccharin, and sucralose, in all school meal programs 
     administered under federal law; to the Committee on Education 
     and Workforce.
       ML-96. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 54, 
     memorializing the Congress of the United States to support 
     H.R. 1114 and S. 1078 of the 119th Congress, entitled the 
     ``Mississippi River Basin Fishery Commission Act''; to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
       ML-97. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to house Joint Memorial No. 22, 
     supporting and urging the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to 
     curtail, or allow curtailment of, the number of pelicans 
     within Idaho; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
       ML-98. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 21, 
     urging the U.S. Department of War and the Defense Industrial 
     Base Consortium to award grant moneys to American Tungsten 
     for the development of the IMA mine project; to the Committee 
     on Natural Resources.
       ML-99. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 14, 
     urging the United States Congress, the Department of the 
     Interior, and the Bureau of Reclamation to cooperate with the 
     State of Idaho to move forward immediately with fast-track 
     research on the feasibility and desirability of small 
     reservoir raises at Minidoka Dam, Jackson Lake, and American 
     Falls Dam, support planning and design work for new medium-
     sized reservoirs across eastern Idaho, facilitate creative 
     financing options through federal funds and public-private 
     partnerships, assist in the completion of an updated basin 
     study in the next two to three years to identify priority 
     projects; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
       ML-100. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 9, 
     urging the Congress of the United States to, as expeditiously 
     as possible, enact legislation reaffirming that FLPMA is the 
     proper and primary avenue to facilitate effective federal

[[Page H4370]]

     land management and disposal, including by land exchange; to 
     the Committee on Natural Resources.
       ML-101. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the Commonwealth 
     of Pennsylvania, relative to Senate Resolution No. 245, 
     supporting the bipartisan Federal Veterans Traumatic Brain 
     Injury Adaptive Care Opportunities Nationwide Act; to the 
     Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       ML-102. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 16, 
     supports the on-going efforts of the Idaho Department of 
     Insurance to protect Idaho Medicare beneficiaries and promote 
     a stable and fair insurance marketplace; jointly to the 
     Committees on Energy and Commerce and Ways and Means.
       ML-103. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Idaho, relative to House Joint Memorial No. 13, 
     affirms the controlling standard announced by the Supreme 
     Court in Sackett v. Environmental Protection Agency that 
     requires an indistinguishable surface connection to exist for 
     waters adjacent to ``waters of the United States'', which are 
     relatively permanent, standing, or continuously flowing 
     bodies of water, to be subject to the Clean Water Act and 
     federal regulation and jurisdiction, and the Legislature 
     opposes any federal assertion of jurisdiction inconsistent 
     with that standard; jointly to the Committees on Energy and 
     Commerce and Transportation and Infrastructure.

                          ____________________
```
