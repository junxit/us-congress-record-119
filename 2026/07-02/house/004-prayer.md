# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Minister Andy Brewster, Jefferson Avenue Church of Christ, 
Cookeville, Tennessee, offered the following prayer:
  Father God, thank You for this day, for our families, friends, and 
the freedoms we enjoy in our Nation. Thank You for these 
Representatives and their willingness to serve. Bless their families 
and each of them individually. Help all of us to recognize Your 
blessings in our lives.
  Please be with the matters under consideration in this Chamber. We 
pray that the conversation is both civil and productive. Father, we 
pray that these lawmakers will seek justice, love kindness, and walk 
humbly with You, O God.
  We pray and we thank You for both our Armed Forces and our civil 
servants on the local, State, and Federal levels.
  As our Nation celebrates 250 years, let us look back and thank You 
and look forward to follow You. We pray for our leaders. We pray for 
peace. We praise You, O God. Let justice roll and freedom ring.
  In Jesus name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
