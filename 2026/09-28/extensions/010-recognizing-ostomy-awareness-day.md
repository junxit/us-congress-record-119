# RECOGNIZING OSTOMY AWARENESS DAY

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING OSTOMY AWARENESS DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Ostomy Awareness Day, which will be observed on Saturday, October 3, 
2026.
  An ostomy can be a difficult and deeply personal journey, but for 
many, it is also a second chance at life. Ostomy surgery can provide a 
path forward for individuals facing serious illnesses and conditions, 
including colorectal cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, traumatic 
injuries, and other medical challenges. These procedures can be 
lifesaving and allow individuals to return to their families, 
workplaces, communities, and the activities they love.
  Yet too often, people living with an ostomy face misunderstanding, 
stigma, and unnecessary barriers. Ostomy Awareness Day provides an 
opportunity to educate the public, dispel misconceptions, and remind us 
that an ostomy does not define a person or diminish their quality of 
life.
  In Pennsylvania, approximately 32,500 people are living with an 
ostomy. Their stories demonstrate the strength of those who continue to 
live full and meaningful lives after significant medical challenges.
  Families, caregivers, physicians, nurses, certified ostomy nurse 
specialists, and other health care professionals also deserve 
recognition for the care and support they provide to those living with 
an ostomy.
  On Ostomy Awareness Day, we celebrate the resilience of the ostomy 
community and recognize the medical advances and dedicated 
professionals who make these life-saving surgeries possible.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing October 3, 2026, as Ostomy 
Awareness Day.

                          ____________________
```
