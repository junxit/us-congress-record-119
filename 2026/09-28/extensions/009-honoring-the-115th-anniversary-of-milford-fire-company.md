# HONORING THE 115TH ANNIVERSARY OF MILFORD FIRE COMPANY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
         HONORING THE 115TH ANNIVERSARY OF MILFORD FIRE COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the 115th anniversary of the 
Milford Fire Company in Milford, New Jersey. For more than a century, 
the Milford Fire Company has dutifully served our community. Its 
storied legacy of protecting Milford reflects the steadfast emergency 
services its volunteers continue to provide today.
  In November 1911, 14 volunteers organized the Milford Hose Company 
No. 1. William Balliet was elected as its first foreman, a title later 
changed to chief. The company initially ordered hose and nozzles, 
transporting its equipment in firefighters' cars until purchasing a 
hose cart in 1912. Firefighters pulled the cart by hand, running 
alongside it and holding on tightly to avoid being run over. The cart 
was last used in February 1940 and can now be seen at local parades and 
events.
  Milford was formally incorporated in March 1925. At the time, Borough 
Clerk Joseph Case, who also served as fire company chief, learned that 
the borough's election returns had not been filed with the county 
clerk. After contacting Hunterdon County's state senator, Case secured 
Milford's official incorporation through the state legislature. This 
occurred approximately one year after the company was incorporated and 
renamed ``The Milford N.J. Fire Company, Inc.'' in April 1924.
  Today, the Milford Fire Company is led by Chief Nathan Fleck. Through 
their leadership and the tireless dedication of every volunteer who has 
served over the past 115 years, the company continues its proud legacy 
and works to ensure the safety of Milford Borough.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in commemorating the 
115th anniversary of the Milford Fire Company and in wishing its 
members continued success for many years to come.
```
