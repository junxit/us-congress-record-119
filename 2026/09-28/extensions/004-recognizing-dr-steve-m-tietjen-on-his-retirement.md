# RECOGNIZING DR. STEVE M. TIETJEN ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Gray, Adam (G000605) [D-CA]

```
           RECOGNIZING DR. STEVE M. TIETJEN ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ADAM GRAY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. GRAY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my good friend, Dr. 
Steve M. Tietjen, who is retiring as Merced County Superintendent of 
Schools after 45 years of service to students and their families.
  Dr. Tietjen earned his Master of Arts in Educational Leadership and 
Administration from the California State University, Fresno, and his 
Doctor of Education, Curriculum and Instruction from the University of 
Southern California.
  In 1980, Dr. Tietjen started his career in education as a history 
teacher in Visalia. Just 12 years later, he became the Superintendent 
of Woodlake Public Schools in Tulare County, where he served for 15 
years before moving to Los Banos Unified School District to serve as 
Superintendent from 2009 to 2016.
  He came to the Merced County Office of Education as the Deputy 
Superintendent in 2016, and in less than a year, he was elected to 
serve as the Merced County Superintendent of Schools, where he leads 
the 1,400 employees of the County Office of Education.
  During the COVID pandemic, Dr. Tietjen oversaw countywide efforts to 
keep students learning, a time he calls the ``most challenging period 
of his tenure.'' He also expanded the Virginia Smith Trust scholarship 
program to award more than $500,000 annually to Merced County students 
and acquired two downtown Merced buildings to make services for 
families, mental-health support, and employment programs more 
accessible to all.
  He has always made the needs of students his top priority in all 
matters and empowered those he worked with to do the same.
  It is my privilege to thank Dr. Steve M. Tietjen for his support and 
friendship as well as for his 45 years in education where he has 
tirelessly worked to educate, elevate, and empower the students and 
families he served.
```
