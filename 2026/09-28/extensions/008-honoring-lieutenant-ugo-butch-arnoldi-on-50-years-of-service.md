# HONORING LIEUTENANT UGO "BUTCH" ARNOLDI ON 50 YEARS OF SERVICE TO THE SANTA BARBARA COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE

**Speaking:** Carbajal, Salud O. (C001112) [D-CA]

```
HONORING LIEUTENANT UGO ``BUTCH'' ARNOLDI ON 50 YEARS OF SERVICE TO THE 
                 SANTA BARBARA COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SALUD O. CARBAJAL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. CARBAJAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Lieutenant Ugo 
``Butch'' Arnoldi of the Santa Barbara County Sheriff's Office as he 
concludes an extraordinary 50-year career dedicated to protecting and 
serving the people of Santa Barbara County.
  On August 19, 1974, Lieutenant Arnoldi was sworn in as a Deputy 
Sheriff. On October 1, 2026, he will officially retire, bringing to a 
close one of the longest and most distinguished careers in the 
department's history.
  Throughout his career, Lieutenant Arnoldi has served in numerous 
roles, including Countywide Watch Commander, Chief of Police for the 
City of Carpinteria, Sheriff's Assistant, Commander of the Isla Vista 
Foot Patrol, Chaplain, Explorer Advisor, and member of the Canine and 
Mountain Patrols. A proud UCSB graduate, he earned a master's degree in 
public administration while working full-time and has continued to 
share his knowledge through guest lectures at local community colleges.
  Lieutenant Arnoldi has answered the call during some of the Central 
Coast's most significant moments, including the Painted Cave Fire, the 
2014 Isla Vista shooting, the 2016 Santa Barbara Post Office shooting, 
and the Thomas Fire and subsequent debris flow. His commitment to 
community extends beyond emergency response. Since 1982, he has 
organized Santa Barbara's Special Olympics Law Enforcement Torch Run, 
and since 1978, he has coordinated law enforcement support for Old 
Spanish Days Fiesta.
  His distinguished career has earned him numerous honors, including 
the T. Guerry Award for Overall Outstanding Performance, the H. Thomas 
Gueny Award for Superior Performance, the Special Olympics Community 
Service Award, and international recognition as a 2021 John Carion 
Unsung Hero Award recipient. In 2022, he was inducted into the Law 
Enforcement Torch Run Hall of Fame by Special Olympics Southern 
California.
  Lieutenant Arnoldi has worked under five different Santa Barbara 
County Sheriffs and has taken on extraordinary assignments, including 
overseeing security during the Michael Jackson trial in Santa Maria and 
leading a motorcade escorting Queen Elizabeth to President Ronald 
Reagan's ranch. His career is a testament to the trust and respect he 
has earned throughout the Central Coast.
  His commitment to service is also a family tradition. He and his 
wife, Marla, have two children, Giuseppi and Francesca, both of whom 
followed in their father's footsteps and served with the Santa Barbara 
County Sheriff's Office. Giuseppi is now the third ``Deputy Arnoldi'' 
in the department.
  After five decades of service, Lieutenant Arnoldi leaves an enduring 
legacy of leadership, professionalism, and dedication to his community. 
As he retires on October 1, I extend my deepest gratitude and 
congratulations to Lieutenant Ugo ``Butch'' Arnoldi for 50 years of 
exceptional service. I wish him and his family all the best in this 
well-earned next chapter.
```
