# RECOGNIZING THE SUCCESS OF THE AMERICA250 NATIONAL BIRTHDAY CAKE

**Speaking:** Watson Coleman, Bonnie (W000822) [D-NJ]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE SUCCESS OF THE AMERICA250 NATIONAL BIRTHDAY CAKE

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BONNIE WATSON COLEMAN

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mrs. WATSON COLEMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
congratulate Cake Artist Grace ``Grey'' Pak and her team for their 
success in creating the America250 National Birthday Cake this past 
July.
  Inspired by the longstanding tradition of marking milestone birthdays 
with cake, in January 2026, Grace ``Grey'' Pak, founder of the fine-art 
cake studio Duchess of Cameron, asked the congressional commission 
planning the Nation's 250th celebrations whether anyone was planning to 
make a birthday cake for the milestone. When it was clear a cake was 
not being prepared, she took on the responsibility to lead a very 
complex yet rewarding project: to bake a cake for this country's 250th 
birthday.
  She envisioned the tribute as a unifying symbol of the Nation's 
history and future potential. Created over several months with the 
support of volunteer culinary experts, artisans and partners and 
contributions from bipartisan, bicameral members of Congress from every 
state and United States territory, and the District of Columbia, the 
installation combined American craftsmanship, local customs, and 
stories from communities across the country, and comprehensive 
historical research with and inspiration from the Smithsonian 
Institution, Colonial Williamsburg, and George Washington's Gristmill 
into a single work of edible art. I was fortunate to be one of those 
members of Congress and help prepare New Jersey's element of the cake. 
The beautiful cake was 10.5 feet long, 4 feet wide, and nearly 7 feet 
tall. All 50 states, 5 territories, and the District of Columbia are 
represented in what can be described as a ``sweet gift'' to this Nation 
and a symbol of how all of us can defy partisanship to come together to 
achieve a remarkable goal. It was unveiled at a ceremony at the Library 
of Congress on July 4, 2026, and later displayed at the White House 
Visitor Center through August 3, 2026.
  Mr. Speaker, I would like to congratulate project lead Grace ``Grey'' 
Pak and her team of cake artists for their hard work and high 
achievement constructing our Nation's birthday cake. Her team included

Williams, and Edwin Vargas. I would also like to recognize the 
project's operations support team: Lauren Bell, David Leffler, and
```
