# HONORING THE FOOD BANK OF DELAWARE

**Speaking:** McBride, Sarah (M001238) [D-DE]

```
                   HONORING THE FOOD BANK OF DELAWARE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. SARAH McBRIDE

                              of delaware

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Ms. McBRIDE. Mr. Speaker, today, in recognition of Hunger Action 
Month, I rise to honor the Food Bank of Delaware for the extraordinary 
work they do every day on behalf of Delawareans.
  We often call Delaware a state of neighbors. To me, that means more 
than simply living beside one another. It means recognizing that we 
have a responsibility to one another--that when a neighbor is 
struggling, we do not look away. We step up. We lend a hand. We make 
sure no one has to face a difficult moment alone. Few organizations 
embody that spirit more fully than the Food Bank of Delaware.
  Roughly one in seven Delawareans experiences food insecurity. That is 
an unacceptable statistic, especially in the wealthiest, most developed 
Nation on Earth. No student should have to learn on an empty stomach. 
No parent should have to skip a meal so their children can eat. No 
senior should have to choose between groceries and medicine.
  Ensuring that our neighbors can meet their most basic needs is not 
simply a question of

[[Page E994]]

charity. It reflects our values. That is why I am so inspired by the 
work that is done at the Food Bank of Delaware. I have visited the Food 
Bank several times, and I am always impressed by the dedicated staff, 
volunteers, and community partners who go to great lengths to ensure 
that someone they may never meet has nutritious food on the table.
  That is what being a state of neighbors looks like in practice: It is 
people choosing to take responsibility for one another, not because 
they have to, but because they understand that the well-being of our 
neighbors is connected with our own.
  I have long been proud to support the Food Bank in their work, and I 
remain committed to working alongside them to confront hunger and 
strengthen food security across our state.
  Hunger Action Month is a perfect opportunity to recognize how 
critical this work is, but our responsibility to one another extends 
far beyond any single month. Ending hunger will require sustained 
commitment from all of us--government, community organizations, 
businesses, volunteers, and neighbors; and a shared refusal to accept 
hunger as inevitable.
  So today, I want to thank everyone at the Food Bank of Delaware, 
including President and CEO Cathy Kanefsky for her leadership, and 
every Delawarean who supports their mission. They remind us that a 
state of neighbors is not simply something we call ourselves. It is 
something we choose to be every time we show up for one another.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the Food 
Bank of Delaware and all those working to end hunger across the First 
State during Hunger Action Month and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
