# RECOGNIZING COMMUNITY LEADER ANDY NOGAL ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
       RECOGNIZING COMMUNITY LEADER ANDY NOGAL ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Andy Nogal, Deputy 
Director for the Housing & Community Development Department of the City 
of Anaheim, on his retirement.
  For the past 24 years, Andy has served as a key member of the City of 
Anaheim's Housing & Community Development Department, holding 
responsibility for all aspects of for-sale and rental housing 
development projects. Andy has extensive experience with housing 
development financing, including State and Federal Tax Credits, bond 
financing, and other funding sources. He has a profound passion for 
affordable housing development and was part of a team that facilitated 
the development of over 2,879 affordable rental new construction, 
acquisition/rehabilitation, and for-sale housing units since 2005.
  Andy also has a strong interest in the built environment, ensuring 
that Anaheim's affordable housing development projects are designed in 
a manner that are respectful to neighborhood character, provide quality 
architectural and landscape design, stand the test of time, and serve 
as an asset to the community.
  In times of reduced financial and staff resources, Andy continues to 
seek and identify internal and external resources to assist in the 
development of affordable housing. Andy worked with Anaheim City 
Planners to implement substantial developer incentives/waivers as part 
of the City's Multifamily Affordable Housing Ordinance/Density Bonus 
Ordinance to incentivize the development of affordable housing. Andy 
also assisted in the creation of the City of Anaheim's Affordable 
Housing Strategic Plan--establishing a five-year goal for the creation 
of more than 2,812 rental new construction, rental rehabilitation and 
for-sale affordable housing units. To help achieve these goals, Andy 
assisted with the implementation of an expedited entitlement process 
for affordable housing development projects and the prioritization and 
allocation of City Department resources for affordable housing projects 
such as energy star rebates, fee deferrals, and fee waivers. Andy is a 
City Planner by trade and holds a Bachelor of Science in Urban and 
Regional Planning from Cal Poly Pomona University.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Andy Nogal on his 
retirement from the City of Anaheim. I thank Andy for his diligent work 
serving our communities here in Orange County.
```
