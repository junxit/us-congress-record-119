# RECOGNIZING GOLD STAR MOTHER'S AND FAMILY'S DAY

**Speaking:** Crow, Jason (C001121) [D-CO]

```
            RECOGNIZING GOLD STAR MOTHER'S AND FAMILY'S DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JASON CROW

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. CROW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize September 27 as Gold 
Star Mother's and Family's Day. This day serves to honor the Gold Star 
Mothers and Families across Colorado and our Nation.
  Since World War I, families have marked the loss of a loved one in 
the Armed Forces by displaying a service flag with a gold star in the 
window of their homes. In 1936, President Roosevelt signed legislation 
establishing Gold Star Mother's and Family's Day, observed on the last 
Sunday of September to honor mothers and families of our Nation's 
fallen service members.
  Colorado has a long history of military service, and the mothers and 
families of our state's fallen service members have carried the loss 
with strength and grace. Many continue to support other military and 
veteran families in their communities, and keep the memory of their 
loved ones alive through this work.
  I honor the Gold Star Mothers and Families of Colorado. as well as 
Gold Star Mothers and Families across our country, for their sacrifice 
and for the lasting impact of the loved ones they lost in service to 
our country.
```
