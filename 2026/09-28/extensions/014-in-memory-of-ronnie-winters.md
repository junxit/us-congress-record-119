# IN MEMORY OF RONNIE WINTERS

**Speaking:** Fuller, Clay (F000485) [R-GA]

```
                      IN MEMORY OF RONNIE WINTERS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLAY FULLER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. FULLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in memory of Ronnie Winters. 
Ronnie Winters lived a life of service to his country, his family, and 
the people of Chattooga County.
  Ronnie proudly served our Nation as a Sergeant in the United States 
Army, serving in active combat in Vietnam with the 19th Brigade Combat 
Engineers from 1966 to 1968. His commitment to his fellow veterans 
continued long after he returned home through his involvement with VFW 
Post 6688 and American Legion Post 129.
  At home, Ronnie was a beloved husband, father, and grandfather. He 
was a hardworking small business owner who understood what it meant to 
build something of your own. He used that experience to mentor young 
entrepreneurs and help others get their start in business.
  Ronnie also cared deeply about the future of Chattooga County. He 
worked to strengthen the local economy through his service with the 
Northwest Georgia Joint Development Authority and gave back to local 
young people as a longtime member of the Summerville-Trion Optimist 
Club.
  Ronnie Winters represented the very best of Northwest Georgia: faith, 
family, hard work, service, and love of country. His impact can be seen 
in the businesses he helped, the young people he encouraged, the 
veterans he stood beside, and the family he loved.
  Mr. Speaker, I am grateful for Ronnie's friendship and for his 
lifetime of service. His legacy will not be forgotten, and I ask that 
you join me in keeping the Winters family in your prayers.

                          ____________________
```
