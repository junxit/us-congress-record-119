# RECOGNIZING CHRISTINE OLSON, FOUNDER OF TO INFORM FAMILIES FIRST, ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Buchanan, Vern (B001260) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 6051

```
 RECOGNIZING CHRISTINE OLSON, FOUNDER OF TO INFORM FAMILIES FIRST, ON 
                             HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. VERN BUCHANAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. BUCHANAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Christine Olson 
of Bradenton, Florida, as she retires from TIFF, To Inform Families 
First, and to remember her beloved daughter, Tiffiany Marie Olson.
  On December 7, 2005, Tiffiany, just 22, was killed by a drunk driver 
on United States 19 in Manatee County, only 15 minutes from her 
mother's home. Yet it took more than six hours for Christine to be 
told, because law enforcement had no way of knowing whom to call.
  Most people would have been broken by that. Christine turned it into 
a mission. In October 2006, she founded TIFF so no other family would 
endure those same agonizing hours. Working with local and state 
leaders, the Manatee County Sheriffs Office, and the Florida Department 
of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles, she helped create the Nation's 
first voluntary Emergency Contact Information program, letting drivers 
register a loved one's contact information for law enforcement to use 
in a crisis. Christine was the first to register.
  As of August 2025, more than 21.6 million Floridians were enrolled. 
Eighteen states have adopted similar programs, and Harvard's Ash 
Institute recognized the effort as one of the top 50 government 
innovations.
  For nearly two decades, Christine carried this cause from Manatee 
County to statehouses and city councils nationwide, sharing Tiffiany's 
story again and again so other families would be spared. Her work 
inspired me to introduce the To Inform Families First Act, H.R. 6051, 
in November 2025, to help state motor vehicle agencies everywhere 
establish voluntary emergency contact programs.
  As Christine steps away from TIFF, her legacy is secure. A flag has 
been flown in her honor and in Tiffiany's memory, a symbol of a 
mother's love and the courage to turn grief into help for strangers.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Christine 
Olson for her strength, compassion, and lifelong dedication to families 
across America, and in remembering Tiffiany Marie Olson, whose memory 
lives on in every family reached in time. I wish Christine a well-
earned and joyful retirement.
```
