# HONORING GRUPO GUAYACAN, INC. ON ITS 30TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Hernandez, Pablo Jose (H001103) [D-PR]

```
         HONORING GRUPO GUAYACAN, INC. ON ITS 30TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 28, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Grupo Guayacan, 
Inc. on the occasion of its 30th anniversary. The organization's roots 
trace back to a 1994 study commissioned by founder Enrique ``Ricky'' 
Adsuar Gonzalez, which proved that Puerto Rico lacked a local private 
capital market to drive economic development. Founded in June 1996 as a 
private-sector-driven nonprofit, Grupo Guayacan officially marked this 
milestone on June 13, 2026, and will honor it with a public celebration 
on October 3, 2026--the event that occasions this submission into the 
Record. Adsuar Gonzalez established the organization under three 
guiding principles: it must be a non-governmental effort, have a 
governance structure led by the private sector, and be structured as a 
non-profit entity. For three decades, Grupo Guayacan has been a 
cornerstone of Puerto Rico's economic development, spurring the local 
private equity market by providing local institutional investors with 
access to top tier private equity investing through a partnership with 
Abbott Capital, leading the creation of the entrepreneurial ecosystem 
by offering world class education and tools to entrepreneurs at 
different stages of growth and facilitating access to capital to early-
stage entrepreneurs in order to achieve lasting prosperity for the 
Island. This partnership with Abbott Capital was strategically designed 
to give Puerto Rican investors access to private equity investments and 
protect public funds, often referred to by Adsuar Gonzalez as ``the 
people's savings.''
  Puerto Rico's economic future cannot rest solely on federal transfers 
or public spending. It must be built on new businesses, sustained 
growth of both SME's and innovation-based companies, the successful 
transition of legacy family businesses, retained talent, and well-
paying jobs that keep Puerto Ricans at home. Grupo Guayacan has 
understood this from the beginning. Its founding premise--that a strong 
entrepreneurial ecosystem equals a strong economy, and that a strong 
ecosystem needs risk capital to grow--has been validated over three 
decades by over 3,500 entrepreneurs who have built extraordinary 
companies across the Island, hundreds of instructors and mentors, and 
dozens of investors who have seen their capital grow and remain in 
Puerto Rico with Guayacan's support.
  Grupo Guayacan's portfolio of programs addresses economic development 
at every stage of a venture's growth. I-Corps Puerto Rico, aligned with 
the National Science Foundation's Innovation Corps initiative and 
delivered in partnership with the Georgia Institute of Technology, 
converts research and innovation into viable businesses--a direct 
investment in Puerto Rico's knowledge economy, now in its 22nd cohort. 
The EnterPRize Competition, which has been running uninterruptedly 
since 2005, was the first program in Puerto Rico to provide equity-free 
seed capital to early stage entrepreneurs. This year, it welcomed 30 
startups from nearly 150 applicants across sectors central to Puerto 
Rico's economic diversification: health, technology, education,

[[Page E991]]

agriculture, and retail. The Scale Up Bootcamp program designed in 
collaboration with Banco Popular, the Island's principal financial 
institution, just awarded a $50,000 seed capital prize and a $20,000 
special prize to two SME's--a direct private-sector investment in the 
Island's high-growth companies. The Guayacan Venture Accelerator 
program, now in its 16th year, has served 202 growth companies, each 
with well over a million dollars in revenue, and 675 participants. 
Notably, the participating companies in this program only represent 
13,000 jobs in Puerto Rico's economy.
  On October 3, 2026, Grupo Guayacan will host its public anniversary 
celebration at Liberty Square in San Patricio Plaza, showcasing over 50 
alumni entrepreneurs and a full day of performances by program alumni. 
It will notably launch its first children's book, Enrique y el 
guayacan, to instill the possibility of becoming an entrepreneur in the 
next generation and illustrate the lasting impact of three decades of 
investment in Puerto Rican talent.
  Mr. Speaker, expanding economic opportunity in Puerto Rico is among 
my highest priorities in Congress. Grupo Guayacan is one of the most 
important private-sector partners in this work. I commend Executive 
Director Laura Cantero and the entire Guayacan team for their 
extraordinary service to Puerto Rico's economic future, and I 
congratulate this institution on 30 years of building the 
entrepreneurial foundation from which our Island's talent can build 
upon and thrive.
```
