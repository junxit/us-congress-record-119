# RECOGNIZING LONE ARRANGER

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING LONE ARRANGER

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Lone 
Arranger of Adams County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the Week.
  For over 28 years, Lone Arranger has served the community as a 
florist and gift shop, offering products for every occasion and 
providing customized decorating services. The business began as 
``Flower and Garden Center'' in the home of Dale and Caroline Jackson. 
It was later sold to Larry and Sherry Archer and then Ros and Darlene 
Peterson, who renamed it Peterson Floral Gifts & Antiques. In 1999, 
Jessica Wilson purchased the business after working for the Peterson 
couple in high school and completing two internships at the shop while 
earning her degree in floriculture from Kirkwood Community College. 
Jessica passed all required testing and became an Iowa Master Florist, 
IMF, in November 2009. As part of the celebration marking Jessica 
Wilson's 10-year anniversary as the shop's owner, she rebranded to Lone 
Arranger in 2009.
  Today, Lone Arranger has built a stellar reputation and contributes 
to many high-profile floral projects in Iowa. These include inaugural 
flowers for Governors Culver and Branstad and holiday decorations for 
the Iowa Governor's Mansion. Furthermore, Jessica Wilson contributed to 
the award-winning Rain Bird float for the 2010 Tournament of Roses 
Parade. Lone Arranger has also been a recurring vendor at the Adams 
County Fair.
  As the sole employee of Lone Arranger, Jessica has demonstrated her 
unceasing commitment to the Adams County community. In 2003, Jessica 
received the Young Entrepreneur of the Year award for Dedication & 
Commitment to the community at the Adams Community Chamber of Commerce 
Awards Banquet. Jessica was the coordinator for the Adams County 4-H/
FFA & Youth Fair Queen from 2004-2009. Jessica has further demonstrated 
her community involvement through her service as president of the Adams 
Community Chamber of Commerce, to which she was elected in 2008, 
serving two 3-year terms, and her role as an Adams County Chamber of 
Commerce ambassador beginning in 2009. To this day, Jessica makes sure 
the chamber windows are pristinely decorated. Jessica was also elected 
to the Iowa Florist Association, IFA, Board in 2007. Jessica currently 
serves as the president for Feed the Pack, a local backpack program 
ensuring children do not go hungry, coordinating food orders and menus 
for students. Jessica contributes to the annual 4th of July flight 
breakfast, banquet decorations, roadside cleanup, and children's 
treasure hunt at Lake Icaria. These accolades and involvement are a 
testament to how Jessica has touched the lives of many Iowans.
  It is with profound gratitude that I recognize Jessica Wilson of Lone 
Arranger for her longstanding dedication to Adams County. I look 
forward to all she will achieve and wish her success and happiness in 
her future ventures.●

                          ____________________
```
