# RECOGNIZING DISCIPLES COFFEE

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                      RECOGNIZING DISCIPLES COFFEE

● Mr. RISCH. Mr. President, as a member and former chairman of 
the Senate Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each month 
I recognize and celebrate the American entrepreneurial spirit by 
highlighting the success of a small business in my home State of Idaho. 
Today, I am proud to honor Disciples Coffee in Boise as the Idaho Small 
Business of the Month for September 2026.
  Chris and Joey Melrose started Disciples Coffee in October 2023 after 
inviting Daryl Gurden, then known for his work smoking meats, to try 
his hand at roasting coffee beans. The skillset turned out to be 
surprisingly transferable, and Daryl quickly became the company's new 
head roaster, bringing exceptional and fresh coffee to Boise. Earlier 
this year, Timothy Taylor assumed ownership of the day-to-day 
operations of Disciples Coffee, allowing Joey more time to focus on 
building and consulting with other businesses in the Treasure Valley. 
Despite the transition, Disciples Coffee's goal remains the same: to 
create specialty coffee and foster genuine connections over a cup of 
joe.
  The company's name is derived from the Great Commission, the call for 
followers of Jesus to go forth and make disciples of men. Each owner 
found meaning in Matthew 28:19 and saw coffee as a natural way to bring 
people together for meaningful conversation and to grow their faith. 
With each batch of coffee they roast, Disciples Coffee strives to roast 
to perfection and brew for connection.
  Disciples Coffee aims to serve more than just coffee to the Treasure 
Valley. Through its Mission of the Month Program, the business works 
with local organizations like Mighty Oaks, PAL, Idaho Diaper Bank, 
Foster + Heart, and the Whosoevers, to bring attention to those who 
serve the community, as well as donate tips collected throughout the 
month.
  Congratulations to the Disciples Coffee team on being recognized as 
the Idaho Small Business of the Month for September 2026. Your 
dedication to excellence makes Idaho proud, and I look forward to your 
continued growth and success.●
```
