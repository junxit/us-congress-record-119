# RECOGNIZING FIVE SONS LANDSCAPE & DESIGN

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                RECOGNIZING FIVE SONS LANDSCAPE & DESIGN

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize Five 
Sons Landscape & Design of Madison County, IA, as the Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  The idea for Five Sons began with a shared dream between Eric Pugh 
and his oldest son Brady to start their own landscaping company. Eric 
earned a forestry degree from Iowa State University and had long 
considered starting a business, while Brady gained several years of 
landscaping experience as a teenager. Their plans were put on hold when 
their military commitments sent both Eric and Brady to Afghanistan in 
2010. After returning home, Brady went back to college and began taking 
landscaping jobs on the side, purchasing a wheelbarrow and gathering 
tools from family garages to get started. What began as a summer hobby 
quickly grew into a passion and, ultimately, a lifelong career. After 
completing that first season on his own, Brady officially formed the 
business in April 2013. The company's name pays tribute to founder 
Brady and his four brothers--the five sons of Eric and Monica--who have 
all supported the family business throughout its growth.
  Today, Five Sons is proudly based in Winterset and remains a true 
family operation. Brady, Logan, and Eric own the company, while Monica 
manages the office and other family members continue to lend a hand 
when needed. Each brings unique skills to the business. Brady 
specializes in hardscape design and construction; Eric contributes his 
extensive knowledge of trees, shrubs, perennial plants, and land 
management; and Logan helps bring customers' visions to life through 
construction and maintenance. Together, their talents have helped 
transform a small landscaping operation into a full-service family 
business.
  Five Sons offers a variety of landscaping services, including paver 
patios and sidewalks, retaining walls, boulder walls, plantings, and 
property maintenance. The family also incorporates its commitment to 
conservation into its work by using environmentally friendly products 
whenever possible and prioritizing native plants and shrubs that 
attract beneficial insects. Eric works with urban and rural landowners 
on prairie restoration and conservation, while the entire team 
continues to research landscaping best practices. Through this work, 
Five Sons strives to provide customers with the two standards it values 
most: high-quality service and long-lasting outdoor spaces.
  Five Sons is also a veteran-operated business. Eric served in the 
military for 28 years, while Brady has served 25 years and continues 
his service in the Army National Guard. The family's commitment to 
service extends beyond military duties into the greater community, 
where Five Sons is actively involved with local schools and veterans' 
organizations, including the American Legion. Through this involvement, 
the business continues to demonstrate its commitment to serving fellow 
Iowans and setting an example for other small businesses.

  More than a decade after Brady started with a wheelbarrow, borrowed 
tools, and a determination to build something of his own, Five Sons 
continues to demonstrate the hard work, perseverance, and family values 
that define Iowa small businesses. I want to congratulate the Pugh 
family and their entire team for their dedication to their customers 
and commitment to serving Iowa communities. I look forward to seeing 
their continued growth and success.●
```
