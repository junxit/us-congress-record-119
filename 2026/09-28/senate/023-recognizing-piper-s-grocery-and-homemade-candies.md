# RECOGNIZING PIPER'S GROCERY AND HOMEMADE CANDIES

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
            RECOGNIZING PIPER'S GROCERY AND HOMEMADE CANDIES

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize 
Piper's Grocery and Homemade Candies of Lucas County, IA, as the Senate 
Small Business of the Week.
  Piper's has been a fixture of downtown Chariton for more than a 
century. Joe L. Piper opened Piper's Grocery in 1905 on the northeast 
corner of the square, where the business remains today. Piper's quickly 
became a trusted local grocery store, serving the community with fresh 
meats, bread, and other everyday essentials. When Joe retired, his son 
Bob took over the business with his wife Ruth. The couple continued 
providing quality goods but added a new specialty: homemade candy. Bob 
learned candy-making techniques from his brother John and began selling 
the treats at the store. Piper's continues to use those trusted recipes 
today.
  In the 1980s, Anne and Jim Kerns, longtime employees at Piper's, 
purchased the business and continued Bob's legacy. The couple operated 
the business with the same high standards and commitment to quality 
that Bob had established. Their daughter Jill Kerns returned to 
Chariton in 1997 and purchased the store from her parents the following 
year. Today, Jill and her team continue making Piper's candies in small 
batches using the same recipes and techniques passed down through 
generations. The store has also maintained its old-fashioned grocery 
roots, offering deli meats and cheeses, fresh sandwiches, groceries, 
and a variety of Iowa-made foods and locally crafted products.
  While Piper's has preserved its historic character, Jill has also 
found new ways to share a taste of Chariton far

[[Page S5070]]

beyond Lucas County. Customers can now order Piper's handmade candies 
online and have them shipped across the country and around the world. 
Piper's has also earned statewide recognition for preserving a piece of 
Iowa history, including features from Iowa PBS and Travel Iowa 
highlighting the store as a destination for visitors looking to 
experience an old-fashioned Iowa main street business.
  Even as its reach has grown, Piper's remains deeply connected to the 
Chariton community. Jill credits generations of loyal customers with 
helping the business thrive and takes pride in carrying locally made 
products and providing jobs for area residents. In 2023, Chariton 
Chamber/Main Street recognized Piper's in its Small Business Spotlight, 
highlighting the store's contributions to the local community. Piper's 
also supports local nonprofits, participates in community auctions, and 
remains involved in events throughout the area.
  More than 120 years after Joe Piper first opened his grocery store, 
Piper's continues to bring together history, entrepreneurship, and 
community. Jill and her team have preserved the traditions that made 
Piper's a Chariton institution while adapting the business to serve a 
new generation of customers. The entrepreneurial spirit and commitment 
to community demonstrated by Piper's Grocery and Homemade Candies 
embody the very best of Iowa small businesses. I want to congratulate 
Jill Kerns and the entire Piper's team for their hard work, dedication, 
and exceptional service to Lucas County. I look forward to seeing their 
continued growth and success.●
```
