# RECOGNIZING HENNINGSEN'S MEAT PROCESSING

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                RECOGNIZING HENNINGSEN'S MEAT PROCESSING

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor 
Henningsen's Meat Processing of Cass County, IA, as the Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  After years of working in construction, Randy Henningsen decided to 
return home to Atlantic to be closer to his father and brother. Randy's 
father had previously owned a meat locker that tragically burned down 
in 1972. In 1978, Randy, his father, and his brother decided to carry 
on the family tradition and opened Henningsen's Meat Processing. 
Together, the Henningsens set out to provide Atlantic and the 
surrounding communities with high-quality, locally sourced meat.
  Henningsen's Meat Processing specializes in custom pork and beef 
processing for farmers and operates a full-service meat counter for 
customers throughout southwest Iowa. In addition to its meat selection, 
customers can find products ranging from fish and frog legs to locally 
made cheeses. Randy later purchased his father's and brother's shares 
of the business. Henningsen's remains committed to the same quality and 
service it was founded on.
  Today, Randy leads a team of 14 full- and part-time employees serving 
Iowans across the State. Henningsen's is also an active member of the 
Atlantic community and the Atlantic Area Chamber of Commerce. Randy and 
his team regularly support local fairs, churches, museums, fundraisers, 
and other community events through donations. In 2022, Henningsen's 
donated 200 pounds of meat, about 800 servings, to the New 
Opportunities food pantry in Audubon County and contributed an 
additional $100 toward the processing fee. It is one of many ways Randy 
and his team have given back to the communities that have supported the 
business over the years.
  Nearly five decades after Henningsen's Meat Processing first opened, 
Randy continues his family's legacy of providing quality meat and 
dependable service to southwest Iowa. For Randy, the people he serves 
are what make the business special and continue to bring energy and 
purpose to his work after all these years.
  The entrepreneurial spirit, commitment to quality, and dedication to 
community demonstrated by Henningsen's Meat Processing embodies the 
very best of Iowa small businesses. I want to congratulate Randy 
Henningsen and the entire Henningsen's Meat Processing team for their 
hard work, dedication, and exceptional service to Cass County and 
southwest Iowa. I look forward to seeing their continued 
success.●
```
