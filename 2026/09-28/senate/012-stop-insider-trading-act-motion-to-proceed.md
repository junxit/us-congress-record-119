# STOP INSIDER TRADING ACT--Motion to Proceed

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 7008

```
              STOP INSIDER TRADING ACT--Motion to Proceed

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 548, H.R. 
7008.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 548, H.R. 7008, a bill to 
     amend chapter 131 of title 5 to require certain restrictions 
     on stocks for Members of Congress and their spouses and 
     dependents, and for other purposes.


                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 548, H.R. 7008, a bill to amend 
     chapter 131 of title 5 to require certain restrictions on 
     stocks for Members of Congress and their spouses and 
     dependents, and for other purposes.
         John Thune, Tim Sheehy, Rick Scott of Florida, Ted Cruz, 
           Ashley B. Moody, Steve Daines, Cynthia M. Lummis, John 
           R. Curtis, Pete Ricketts, Bernie Moreno, John Barrasso, 
           Eric Schmitt, Jon A. Husted, Mike Crapo, David 
           McCormick, Katie Boyd Britt, John Cornyn.


                    Withdrawal of Motion to Proceed

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I withdraw the motion to proceed to 
Calendar No. 548, H.R. 7008.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The leader has that right.

                          ____________________
```
