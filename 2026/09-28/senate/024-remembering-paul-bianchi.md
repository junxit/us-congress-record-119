# REMEMBERING PAUL BIANCHI

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                        REMEMBERING PAUL BIANCHI

● Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, I join Paideia and the city of 
Atlanta as they officially rename South Ponce in honor of the late 
Paideia founder and headmaster Paul Bianchi.
  After earning an undergraduate degree and a M.A. in teaching at 
Harvard University, Mr. Bianchi was offered an opportunity to teach at 
the Galloway School in Atlanta.
  After one year at Galloway, Mr. Bianchi was approached by a group of 
Atlanta parents who asked him to establish a new school that would 
emphasize individualized instruction, academics, and the arts.
  Mr. Bianchi agreed to lead that effort and, in 1971, helped open the 
Paideia School. Under Mr. Bianchi's decades of leadership, Paideia has 
grown to serve a student body of over 1,000 students. Mr. Bianchi added 
a high school in 1973 and managed the growth of a thriving campus until 
his retirement in 2023.
  Mr. Bianchi's leadership lifted thousands of young people to 
understand their world and their potential. His work instilled in 
generations of students the knowledge, curiosity, and compassion to 
pursue their dreams and to make a positive difference.
  I know firsthand the brilliance, wit, wisdom, and compassion that 
made Mr. Bianchi so special, and I am grateful for all he did to 
support me and so many others.
  I join the Paideia community and Paul's wife Barbara Dunbar; their 
daughters Melissa, Sarah, and Emily; their grandchildren; and the 
entire Bianchi family in celebrating today's renaming of ``Paul Bianchi 
Way'' and commend his decades of visionary leadership to our State, our 
Nation, and the world.●

                          ____________________
```
