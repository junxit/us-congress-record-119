# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty and gracious God, we ask You to bless our lawmakers with 
wisdom, understanding, and integrity as they do their work today. Guide 
their deliberations and decisions, and help them remember Your promise 
to give wisdom generously to those who ask. May Your wisdom lead them 
to truth, justice, civility, and peace. Lord, replace doubt with faith, 
greed with compassion, and division with understanding. May their words 
and actions serve the common good and promote the well-being of all 
those they represent.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
