                      RECOGNIZING THE BREAD BOARD

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize the 
Bread Board of Marion County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Heidi Rozenboom grew up baking fresh bread with her grandmother, 
always using the same bread board that would eventually inspire her 
business' name. Years later, Heidi turned her passion for baking into a 
business, selling fresh bread to local coffee shops and cafes. Demand 
quickly grew beyond what Heidi could handle on her own, leading her to 
hire additional help. In 2021, she took the next step and opened her 
own storefront in downtown Pella. Heidi named the business the Bread 
Board in honor of the cherished memories she made baking with her 
grandmother and set out to create a welcoming space where customers 
could experience that same sense of warmth and comfort. Today, the 
bakery serves fresh sourdough bread, pastries, homemade meals, and 
other baked goods.
  Heidi now leads a team of about 15 employees serving customers 
throughout Marion County. While the Bread

[[Page S5068]]

Board operates from its Pella location, Heidi continues to find new 
ways to extend the bakery's reach throughout the community. The 
business is a member of the Pella Area Community and Economic Alliance 
and regularly brings residents together through events such as its 
recent sourdough baking competition.
  Even after opening her own storefront, Heidi has continued 
collaborating with other local businesses and organizations. Bread 
Board products can be found at local cafes and the nearby produce 
stand, and Heidi also supplies products to the local school and band. 
For 4 consecutive years, the Bread Board has been named Best Lunch in 
Marion County, in the Marion County Development Commission's Best of 
Red Rock competition, reflecting the business' continued commitment to 
quality and service.
  For Heidi, the Bread Board remains deeply rooted in family. Growing 
up next door to her grandparents, some of her fondest childhood 
memories came from baking alongside her grandmother. The original bread 
board Heidi used with her grandmother remains in the shop today. 
Heidi's own family has also become an important part of Bread Board. 
Her mother is always willing to lend a hand, her husband has been by 
her side since the bakery's earliest days, and now her son and daughter 
are pitching in too, making the Bread Board a true family affair.
  The entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to community demonstrated 
by the Bread Board embody the very best of Iowa small businesses. I 
want to congratulate Heidi Rozenboom, her family, and her entire team 
for their hard work, dedication, and exceptional service to Marion 
County. I look forward to seeing their continued growth and 
success.●
