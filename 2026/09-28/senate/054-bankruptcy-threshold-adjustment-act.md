# BANKRUPTCY THRESHOLD ADJUSTMENT ACT

**Speaking:** Lee, Mike (L000577) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.R. 7730

```
                  BANKRUPTCY THRESHOLD ADJUSTMENT ACT

  Mr. LEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 7730, which was received 
from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 7730) to amend title 11, United States Code, 
     to modify certain bankruptcy eligibility requirements, and 
     for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. LEE. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read a 
third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 7730) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.

```
