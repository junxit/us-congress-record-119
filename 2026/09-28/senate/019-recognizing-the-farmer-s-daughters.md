# RECOGNIZING THE FARMER'S DAUGHTERS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                   RECOGNIZING THE FARMER'S DAUGHTERS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to recognize The 
Farmer's Daughters of Fremont County, IA, as the Senate Small Business 
of the Week.
  Stacy Burgoin-Burge's vision for The Farmer's Daughters began in 2014 
with small antique pop-up shops at events including Vintage & Made Fair 
and Junkstock. Offering vintage finds and home decor, the business 
quickly became too popular to operate out of Stacy's home, prompting 
her to purchase a storefront in Sidney. Soon after, Stacy recognized 
the community's need for a local florist and expanded the business to 
offer floral arrangements in 2017. As the business continued to grow, 
Stacy's husband Ron Burge joined her full-time, and the couple 
eventually expanded with additional locations in Glenwood and 
Shenandoah. Along with vintage items, home decor, and floral 
arrangements, the new locations began offering locally made candles, 
Iowa wines, and other unique products.
  Today, Stacy and Ron lead a team of 10 employees serving communities 
throughout southwest Iowa. Under their leadership, The Farmer's 
Daughters has become known for its carefully curated home decor and 
gifts, as well as its floral artistry and personalized arrangements. 
Since adding floral services in 2017, the business has expanded its 
delivery capabilities and now offers 6- and 12-month ``Bloom of the 
Month'' subscriptions featuring fresh seasonal flowers and custom 
floral designs. For more than a decade, Stacy and Ron have continued to 
find new ways to provide unique products and personalized service to 
their customers.
  The Farmer's Daughters is also deeply committed to giving back to 
communities it serves. Across its locations, the business is involved 
with organizations including Shop Sidney, United Faith Church, the 
Shenandoah Chamber and Industry Association, and the Red Oak Chamber 
and Industry Association. The Glenwood location regularly cohosts 
``Coffee and Connections'' with the Mills County Chamber of Commerce, 
helping foster relationships among local businesses and residents. The 
Farmer's Daughters also supports Fremont County 4-H participants and 
local fairs and events, while its locations partner with area churches, 
schools, and community organizations on fundraisers, workshops, and 
celebrations.
  The Iowa Small Business Development Center has also played an 
important role in The Farmer's Daughters growth, aiding with the 
company's website and helping Stacy and Ron use social media to reach 
more customers. In turn, Stacy and Ron remain committed to 
strengthening communities that have supported their business, 
generously contributing their time, resources, and floral arrangements 
to local fairs, youth programs, and educational initiatives.
  The entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to community demonstrated 
by The Farmer's Daughters embody the very best of Iowa small 
businesses. I want to congratulate Stacy Burgoin-Burge, Ron Burge, and 
their entire team for their hard work, dedication, and exceptional 
service to southwest Iowa. I look forward to seeing their continued 
growth and success.●
```
