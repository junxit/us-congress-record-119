# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend Chris Kellerman S.J., Jesuit Conference of Canada and 
the United States, Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, we thank You for creating us and calling us 
into relationship with You and one another. Forgive us for the times 
that we have not properly honored You nor loved our neighbor as we 
ought.
  Give us the grace to do better. We ask You to give us wisdom, so that 
we might be good stewards of the Earth; generosity, so that we might 
properly care for those who go without; charity, so that we might treat 
everyone as a child of the same Father; and holiness, so that we might 
glorify You in everything we do.
  We ask all this with hearts full of wonder and praise.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
