                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BALDERSON (for himself, Mr. Bilirakis, Mr. Joyce 
             of Pennsylvania, and Mr. Pfluger):
       H.R. 10615. A bill to amend title XIX of the Social 
     Security Act to require State Medicaid fraud control units to 
     conduct annual audits; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
           By Mr. BENTZ:
       H.R. 10616. A bill to require the Secretary of Health and 
     Human Services to create a repository of certain publicly 
     available data sets; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce, 
     and in addition to the Committees on Ways and Means, 
     Education and Workforce, and Natural Resources, for a period 
     to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case 
     for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Ms. BROWN:
       H.R. 10617. A bill to amend the National Agricultural 
     Research, Extension, and Teaching Policy Act of 1977 to 
     establish a demonstration grant program for veterinary 
     medicine work-based learning, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Agriculture.
           By Mr. EVANS of Colorado:
       H.R. 10618. A bill to amend title XIX of the Social 
     Security Act to promote electronic verification of non-
     emergency transportation services and applied behavior 
     analysis services under the Medicaid program; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.

     limitation on any direct spending program without a specific 
     level of authorized spending, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on the Budget.

             Mr. Williams of Texas, and Mr. Self):
       H.R. 10620. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code,
