# ENOUGH IS ENOUGH

**Speaking:** Stansbury, Melanie A. (S001218) [D-NM]

```
                            ENOUGH IS ENOUGH

  (Ms. STANSBURY asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. STANSBURY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on the House floor with a 
heavy heart for the family and the community of Renee Nicole Good.
  My heart is heavy and not just because of the unabashed and violent 
killing that happened last night by an ICE agent in Minneapolis, but 
because I am also sad for our country, to see the Vice President of the 
United States and officials impugn a young woman who leaves behind her 
community and an orphaned child.
  This is not our country. Shame on you. To those of you who are 
continuing to provide cover for this lawless, unconstitutional, 
abusive, and violent administration, shame on you.
  I will be joining to file Articles of Impeachment against Secretary 
Noem. Enough is enough.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to direct their remarks 
to the Chair.

                          ____________________
```
