# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, You are the source and strength of our lives. Great 
is Your faithfulness. We thank You today for our lawmakers who embraced 
the calling to serve You by serving our Nation and world. Bless them 
with divine insights as they grapple with complexities that require 
difficult choices. May they make it their ultimate goal to serve You by 
striving to do what is best for the building of Your Kingdom in a 
chaotic world. Lord, guide us all, for we are pilgrims in this land. We 
are weak, but You are mighty. Guide us with Your powerful hands.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
