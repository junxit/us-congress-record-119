# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 29, 2026

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 29, 2026

  Ms. LUMMIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
on Wednesday, April 29; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
that at 2:15 p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session and vote on 
confirmation of the Cekada nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Ms. LUMMIS. Mr. President, for the information of my colleagues, 
further votes are expected at 4:45 p.m. in relation to the Bennet and 
Rosen CRA joint resolutions.

                          ____________________
```
