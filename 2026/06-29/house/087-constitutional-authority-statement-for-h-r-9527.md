# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9527

**Measures:** H.R. 9527

```
           By Mr. OWENS:
       H.R. 9527.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the 
     Constitution of the United States. Clause 1 authorizes 
     Congress to provide for the general welfare of the United 
     States, and Clause 18 authorizes Congress to make all laws 
     necessary and proper for carrying into execution the powers 
     vested in the Government of the United States and its 
     officers. This legislation governs the administration of a 
     federal statistical classification system maintained by the 
     Office of Management and Budget and requires reporting to 
     Congress.
```
