# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3300

**Speaking:** Hurd, Jeff (H001100) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3300

```
                              {time}  1910
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3300

  Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 3300, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative LaMalfa of California, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. DesJarlais). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Colorado?
  There was no objection.
```
