# HONORING MOUNT ZION ON THE HILL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
             HONORING MOUNT ZION ON THE HILL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge Mount Zion 
Baptist Church on the Hill, a congregation whose foundation dates back 
to August 1898, when the late Reverend Herman Morrison purchased the 
church property and became its first pastor. The church branched from 
Mount Zion Baptist Church in the Field and began with worship services 
held in a tent on the Noble Tract. Early officers included Brothers 
Hoarse Richardson, Norse Evening, King Hills, and Ben Rowe, along with 
Sisters Elizabeth Beach, Nancy Rowe, and Emma Hughes. The first church 
building served not only as a house of worship but also as one of the 
earliest schoolhouses in the area. Over time, the church was rebuilt on 
Highway 37 at Redwood Creek, continuing its mission to serve God's 
people in the community.
  Following Reverend Morrison's tenure, Mount Zion was blessed with 
pastors including Reverend Addison Barber, Reverend Henry Smith, 
Reverend Ball, and Reverend Earnest Harris, all of whom served 
faithfully until their passing. After many years, the original church 
was remodeled with the addition of a secretary's office, choir stand, 
and a bell installed in the new belfry. During these early decades, 
members traveled on horseback, wagons, and on foot to attend worship 
services--demonstrating unwavering faith and devotion. In March 1965, 
young Brother Emmitt Spurlock, Sr. was installed as pastor. Under his 
leadership, the congregation agreed through prayer to construct a new 
church building. In 1983, the old structure was torn down and replaced 
with a modern building, including a baptismal pool, dining hall, 
fellowship space, and new restrooms.
  Mr. Speaker, the newly constructed Mount Zion Baptist Church on the 
Hill was completed in February 1984 under Reverend Emmitt Spurlock's 
leadership. Though some advocated relocating, the congregation rebuilt 
on the historic site. The pastor, officers, Miss Mount Zion, and 
members marched from Vince's Grocery to the church for the ribbon-
cutting ceremony and first communion service. Reverend Spurlock served 
faithfully for thirty-four years until departing this life in January 
1998. One year later, in February 1999, his son Reverend Gus Charles 
Spurlock, Sr. was elected, ordained, and installed as pastor. Reverend 
Dr. Gus C. Spurlock, Sr. has continued the legacy of faithful 
leadership for twenty-six years, honoring the historical significance 
of the church's roots, including the nearby Port Hudson battlefield, a 
pivotal Civil War site.
  Mr. Speaker, Mount Zion Baptist Church on the Hill remains a 
steadfast symbol of faith, resilience, and community. From its founding 
in 1898 to its reconstruction in 1984 and continued leadership under 
Reverend Dr. Gus C. Spurlock, Sr., the church has endured trials while 
standing firmly upon the solid rock of Jesus Christ. Today, Mount Zion 
continues serving the spiritual needs of its congregation and the 
greater Baton Rouge region, honoring its historic legacy and embracing 
its future with unwavering commitment. I ask my colleagues to join me 
in commemorating Mount Zion Baptist Church on the Hill for more than a 
century of devoted service to God, community, and the advancement of 
His Kingdom.
```
