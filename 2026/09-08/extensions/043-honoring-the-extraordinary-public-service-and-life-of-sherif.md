# HONORING THE EXTRAORDINARY PUBLIC SERVICE AND LIFE OF SHERIFF STEVE DRAPER

**Speaking:** Griffith, H. Morgan (G000568) [R-VA]

```
  HONORING THE EXTRAORDINARY PUBLIC SERVICE AND LIFE OF SHERIFF STEVE 
                                 DRAPER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. H. MORGAN GRIFFITH

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. GRIFFITH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the extraordinary 
public service and life

[[Page E887]]

of Martinsville Sheriff Steve Draper, who passed away on August 29th, 
at the age of 73.
  Sheriff Draper was born on March 26, 1953, in Martinsville, Virginia. 
From an early age, Mr. Draper felt a natural calling to serve his 
native home. His interests inspired him to volunteer for the 
Martinsville Volunteer Fire Department and the Martinsville Henry 
County Rescue. Mr. Draper held roles of firefighter, EMS provider and 
President of the Fire Company. These roles helped him build relations 
with his community and address the safety needs of the local area.
  More notably, Mr. Draper pursued a career in law enforcement. Mr. 
Draper's first stint in law enforcement is where he exercised duties as 
deputy sheriff in the Martinsville City Jail.
  As a symbol of trust and appreciation, Sheriff Draper won the support 
of his community in elected office. After Martinsville elected Mr. 
Draper to serve the Martinsville City Council, he then won election to 
the Martinsville Sheriff's position in 1993.
  Starting in 1994, Sheriff Draper would lead his Office with 
dedication, honor and strength. For over 30 years, Sheriff Draper made 
strides to foster working relationships, support law enforcement 
officers and keep his community safe.
  At the time of his passing, Sheriff Draper was the longest-serving 
sheriff in the Commonwealth of Virginia. The Virginia Sheriff's 
Association, of which Sheriff Draper at one time served as President, 
noted Sheriff Draper was a mentor to many and a friend to law 
enforcement professionals throughout Virginia.
  A career that lasts for so long requires hard sacrifices. We are 
grateful to Sheriff Draper's family for supporting him throughout his 
career, even when job demands pulled away his attention. Sheriff Draper 
is survived by his wife, Donna Draper; children, Courtney Bennett (Jose 
Padilla) and Christopher Bennett; grandchild, Antonio Lee Bennett 
Padilla; and special niece, Skylar Sink. His brother, Bobby L. Draper, 
Jr. (Sammy); niece, Kelly Draper; cousins, Kenneth Draper (Pam Wright), 
Cody Draper (Natalie), Sarah Dru Draper, Laura Beth Gruettner (Todd), 
Katherine Eanes Amato (James), Stacy Eanes Clark (Daniel), Bill Draper 
(Debra), Robin Humlan (Mark), Nancy Draper Oaks (Joey), Bettie Draper 
Bowles (Gene), Susan Draper Thompson (Matt), Jerry Draper (Jeannie), 
Karen Berryman, Ann Powers, Drew Conrad, Jim Call, Susan Call, Charles 
Harris, Barry Harris, Rick Elkins, Ted Elkins, Chuck Alexandar and 
Jeffrey Alexandar.
  Virginia's 9th Congressional District is proud to be the home of one 
of Virginia's most storied law enforcement careers. Sheriff Draper's 
lifelong commitment to the Martinsville Henry County area will help 
define his legacy. Sheriff Draper exhibited some of the finest 
qualities, not just of a public servant or law enforcement official, 
but as a friend, neighbor and family man.
```
