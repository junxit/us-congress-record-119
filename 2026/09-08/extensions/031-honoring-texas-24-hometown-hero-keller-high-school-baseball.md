# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO KELLER HIGH SCHOOL BASEBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO KELLER HIGH SCHOOL BASEBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, the Keller Indians baseball team as the Texas High School State 
Champions for the first time in program history.
  On June 6, they secured the Class 6A Division 1 title with a 2-1 
victory over Austin Lake Travis. Led by Championship MVP Cole 
Koeninger, a Tennessee signee and MLB draft prospect, Keller completed 
an incredible season with a 40-4-2 overall record.
  The title concludes Head Coach Rob Stramp's remarkable 28-year career 
and serves as a testament to the dedication and leadership he brought 
to Keller Baseball. Congratulations to the Keller High School Indians 
on this historic achievement, which reflects the talent, hard work, and 
commitment of the entire program.

                          ____________________
```
