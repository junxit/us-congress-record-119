# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DR. CHANDRAMALLIKA BASAK

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
        HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DR. CHANDRAMALLIKA BASAK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I use to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Dr. Chandramallika Basak, a neuroscience professor and researcher 
at University of Texas at Dallas who's on a mission to protect seniors' 
memories.
  Dr. Basak studies how cognitive and physical training, from targeted 
exercises to meditation, can slow cognitive decline in older adults. 
She's best known for pioneering the use of video games as brain 
training for seniors experiencing mild cognitive aging.
  Her research sparked a startup, Neuroflight Inc., founded with 
support from UT Dallas' Big Idea Competition and the Darrell K. Royal 
Alzheimer's Research Fund. The company's patented brain training game 
helps boost memory, focus, and cognitive vitality--and is already 
partnering with local assisted living facilities like Belmont Village.
  In 2026, Dr. Basak was named University Innovator of the Year at the 
Texas Innovation Conference. She is now working on a consumer app to 
bring Neuroflight's brain training to everyone.
```
