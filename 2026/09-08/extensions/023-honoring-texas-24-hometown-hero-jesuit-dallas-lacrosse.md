# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO JESUIT DALLAS LACROSSE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
         HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO JESUIT DALLAS LACROSSE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, the Jesuit Dallas Rangers lacrosse team, as earlier this year 
they won the 2026 Texas High School Lacrosse League Class AA State 
Championships.
  Fueled by championship grit, and a never-quit mentality, the Rangers 
battled through adversity and upset higher-seeded opponents on their 
way to bringing home the state title. Senior goalie, Gabriel James, was 
named the Defensive Most Valuable Player after making 11 saves against 
the Highlanders, and Senior David Atwell earned the Offensive MVP and 
Senior Jack Steinbach was the Championship MVP.
  Congratulations to Coach Chris Surran, the players, coaches, and 
families on securing the program's fifth state championship and 
continuing a tradition of excellence both on and off the field.

                          ____________________
```
