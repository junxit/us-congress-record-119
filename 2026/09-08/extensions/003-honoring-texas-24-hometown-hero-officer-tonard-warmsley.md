# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO OFFICER TONARD WARMSLEY

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
        HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO OFFICER TONARD WARMSLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Officer Tonard ``Tee'' Warmsley, a dedicated school resource 
officer at Coppell ISD. After serving in the U.S. Army, Officer 
Warmsley joined the City of Coppell Police Department in 2007, and has 
served in roles ranging from patrol officer to detective, and now as a 
School Resource Officer. In 2017, he was named Texas School Resource 
Officer of the Year by the Texas Association of School Resource 
Officers and continues to serve as a regional co-director, supporting 
officers across North Texas.
  His impact is best seen through his mentorship of students, like 
Coppell High School graduate Modjet Brown Jr., whom he has guided since 
middle school. From stepping in on Senior Night to standing beside him 
as he took his oath to join the U.S. Army. Officer Warmsley has 
consistently shown what it means to show up for North Texans. His 
commitment reflects a deep belief that mentorship can change lives and 
that every student deserves someone who believes in them.
  I thank Officer Tonard ``Tee'' Warmsley for his dedication and for 
making a meaningful difference in the lives of students and families 
across North Texas.

                          ____________________
```
