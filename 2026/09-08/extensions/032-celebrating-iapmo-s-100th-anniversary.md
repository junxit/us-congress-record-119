# CELEBRATING IAPMO'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Torres, Norma J. (T000474) [D-CA]

```
                 CELEBRATING IAPMO'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NORMA J. TORRES

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mrs. TORRES of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
International Association of Plumbing and Mechanical Officials (IAPMO) 
as it celebrates its 100th anniversary.
  In 1926, 39 plumbing inspectors founded IAPMO in Los Angeles with a 
mission of mitigating public health concerns caused by the city's 
plumbing practices and improving health safety by setting uniform 
plumbing regulations. In 1945, these efforts led to the development of 
the Uniform Plumbing Code, which has served Californians by promoting 
safe plumbing systems. Today, this framework serves as the foundation 
for plumbing regulations implemented across four continents and is 
viewed as one of the world's leading model plumbing codes.
  Today, IAPMO proudly calls the Inland Empire home, with its 
headquarters in the heart of my district: Ontario, California. Having 
grown from a local association of plumbing inspectors to a global 
nonprofit organization, it maintains a deep commitment to serving 
California's communities, industries, and labor force. They 
continuously advance environmental and public safety through plumbing, 
mechanical codes, product certification, and workforce development--
helping to ensure people have access to safe drinking water and 
reliable sanitation.
  The organization partners with local governments, industry, and 
building professionals to strengthen the safety and resilience of 
communities throughout our great state. Its presence in the Inland 
Empire has supported high-quality technical and professional jobs while 
reinforcing Southern California's leadership in building safety, 
product innovation, and public health. Through its codes, standards, 
and other innovations, IAPMO has advanced California's goals for 
drought resiliency, water conservation, and more affordable housing 
through efficient plumbing systems. One example is IAPMO's Water Demand 
Calculator, a modern, statistically based method for estimating water 
demand and properly sizing plumbing systems. The tool helps conserve 
water and energy, protect water quality, and reduce residential 
construction costs.
  IAPMO's work has been instrumental to California's leadership in 
public health, water

[[Page E884]]

conservation, housing, and infrastructure. Their Water Efficiency and 
Sanitation Standard (WE-Stand) provides a voluntary framework for the 
safe and efficient use of water in residential and commercial 
buildings. The standard demonstrates IAPMO's commitment to water 
stewardship by helping communities conserve potable water, expand safe 
onsite water reuse, and make greater use of alternative water sources.
  Its programs strengthen career opportunities throughout California 
while helping ensure a highly skilled workforce for the state's growing 
construction and manufacturing sectors through education, personnel 
certification, apprenticeship support, and professional training for 
plumbers, engineers, contractors, and other building professionals who 
contribute to the health and safety of Californians.
  Not only does IAPMO play a vital role throughout California, but its 
world-renowned Uniform Codes advance public health and safety around 
the world. The organization works with governments and industry to 
remove technical barriers to trade and promote greater alignment of 
standards and market-access requirements. These efforts strengthen 
global supply chains, expand opportunities for American manufacturers, 
and increase access to safe, high-quality plumbing products. In 
recognition of these contributions, IAPMO received the President's 
``E'' Award for Export Service in 2019 and the President's ``E Star'' 
Award for continued excellence in export expansion in 2024. Its global 
commitment extends even further through its charitable foundation, the 
International Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Foundation (IWSH), which 
provides humanitarian assistance to those in need of safe water and 
sanitation.
  For their remarkable contributions throughout the Inland Empire and 
beyond, it is my honor to recognize IAPMO as it celebrates its 100th 
anniversary. The organization's commitment to advancing public health 
and safety is worthy of the highest commendation.
```
