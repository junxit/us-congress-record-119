# HONORING ROSE HILL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                   HONORING ROSE HILL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a historic 
church in my district, Rose Hill Church in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, as 
it celebrates more than 140 years of faith, fellowship, and service to 
the people of East Baton Rouge Parish.
  Built on the foundation of love, Rose Hill Baptist Church continues 
to spread the Gospel. For over a century, Rose Hill has remained 
faithful to advancing the kingdom of God and bettering its surrounding 
communities.
  In 1882, Reverend Byrd founded Rose Hill Baptist Church by purchasing 
land from Mr. Johnson Davis in the amount of fifty dollars. Mr. Davis 
originally desired to donate the land; however, Rose Hill members 
insisted on purchasing it. Seventy-one years later, on Friday, October 
16, 1953, the church was officially incorporated as Rose Hill Baptist 
Church of Baton Rouge.
  After seventy-six years of spirit-filled worship, the original 
edifice of Rose Hill Church deteriorated to the extent that a new 
building was needed. In 1958, Rose Hill moved from its original 
location and built its new home on Greenwell Springs Road. The new 
location included both a church sanctuary and a cemetery. The Rose Hill 
Church cemetery is still in existence today.
  On Sunday, October 6, 1985, Rose Hill expanded its ministry efforts 
by moving from Greenwell Springs Road to its present location.
  Upon the unexpected passing of Pastor Campbell in 2004, the 
congregation's present leader, Pastor Danny M. Donaldson, Sr., 
inherited the ministry through the election process. Innate to his 
character, Pastor Donaldson exudes quintessential leadership while 
preaching a life-changing Word.
  As a yielded vessel, the Lord has allowed Pastor Donaldson to 
substantially grow the ministry of Rose Hill Church. Pastor Donaldson 
adamantly believes in the transformative power of Jesus Christ and 
seeks to tell nations of Christ's unconditional love. Mr. Speaker, I 
ask my colleagues to join me in acknowledging Rose Hill Church for more 
than a century of faithful service, and in wishing its pastor and 
congregation continued blessings in the years to come.
```
