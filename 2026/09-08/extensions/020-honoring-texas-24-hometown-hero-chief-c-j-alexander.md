# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF C.J. ALEXANDER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
          HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF C.J. ALEXANDER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, C.J. Alexander as the new Fire Chief of the Addison Fire 
Department, stepping into the role after seven years with the 
department after serving as the Assistant Chief. With over 24 years of 
experience in fire and emergency medical services, Chief Alexander 
brings a wealth of knowledge and leadership to the department. Before 
joining Addison, he held leadership roles in Farmers Branch and Allen 
and spent more than a decade teaching fire and EMS courses at Collin 
College. Throughout his career, he has remained committed to protecting 
our North Texas communities.
  Surrounded by colleagues, friends, and community members at his 
sweating-in ceremony, the strong turnout reflected the respect and 
trust he has earned throughout his time serving Addison. He is known 
for his commitment to public service and for preparing teams to respond 
when it matters most.

                          ____________________
```
