# HONORING NEW SUNLIGHT MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
            HONORING NEW SUNLIGHT MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize New Sunlight 
Missionary Baptist Church of Baton Rouge, Louisiana--an institution 
whose history is woven into the fabric of the seventeen hundred block 
of America Street. Organized in 1909 under the leadership of the late 
Reverend Samuel Williams, pastor of Mt. Moriah Baptist Church, the 
congregation was formed when approximately fifty members of Mt. Moriah 
relocated to the city and faced transportation challenges returning to 
their original church. They established Mt. Moriah Number Two, holding 
services in a hall on Knox Street. In 1912, the congregation purchased 
a lot for $450 and constructed a framed building, adopting the name New 
Sunlight Missionary Baptist Church.
  After Reverend Williams passed in 1922, he was succeeded by Reverend 
Isaac J. Anderson, under whose leadership the membership erected a 
larger stone structure Upon Reverend Anderson's passing in 1939, 
Reverend

[[Page E876]]

Daniel Smith became pastor and expanded the rear of the church to 
include a kitchen, dining area, and two choir stands before his death 
in 1942. Reverend Edward Brown began his service in 1942 and led the 
purchase of pews, an organ, and songbooks. He initiated interior 
renovations and established a pastor's salary and a building fund. 
Reverend Brown resigned in 1958, and Reverend Joseph R. Williams 
succeeded him, bringing strong leadership that resulted in 
modernization of the building and significant organizational growth.
  Under Reverend J.R. Williams's leadership, numerous auxiliaries and 
programs were created, including the Lena S. Johnson Scholarship Fund 
(1969), Male Chorus (1990), Mass Choir (1992), Summer Enrichment 
Program (1993), After School Tutorial Program (1995), and the Gospel 
Drill Team (1999). The fellowship hall was enlarged in 1993, and land 
was purchased to expand parking. After Reverend Williams's passing, 
Reverend Dale W. Flowers was elected pastor in September 2000. His 
administration brought sanctuary and office renovations, new furniture 
and equipment, a church van, and expansion of church property. Pastor 
Flowers also established a library, a bi-annual health fair, and new 
ministries such as Married Couples, Singles, Drama, and Youth Ushers 
Ministries, along with the institution of Hallelujah Night and New 
Members classes.
  Mr. Speaker, New Sunlight Missionary Baptist Church continues to 
thrive through its outreach programs, including free Saturday 
breakfasts for the homeless, dependency counseling, literacy programs, 
youth and teen rap sessions, and expanded family-centered activities. 
Renovations to the inner sanctuary and vestibule, installation of a 
drum stand and shield, and acquisition of additional property 
demonstrate the church's commitment to growth and service. Operational 
improvements include deacon member assignments, creation of a church 
website, computerization of financial and membership records, and the 
addition of an 8:00 a.m. worship service. Through witnessing, praying, 
and relying on the Holy Spirit, New Sunlight Missionary Baptist Church 
continues its mission of winning souls for God's Kingdom.
```
