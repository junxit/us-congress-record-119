# HONORING THE NORTHLAND NEIGHBORHOODS, INC. (NNI) ON ITS 30TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Cleaver, Emanuel (C001061) [D-MO]

```
     HONORING THE NORTHLAND NEIGHBORHOODS, INC. (NNI) ON ITS 30TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EMANUEL CLEAVER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. CLEAVER. Mr. Speaker, it is with great pleasure that I extend my 
congratulations to the Northland Neighborhoods, Inc. (NNI) on its 30th 
anniversary. For three decades, NNI has demonstrated what can be 
accomplished when neighbors come together under a shared commitment: to 
make a community a better place for all who live there.
  Since its founding in 1996, NNI continues to play a crucial role for 
those living in the Northland, covering a diverse range of efforts to 
build upon the foundations of their community. One key way this has 
been done is by supporting vulnerable homeowners through the Home 
Repair Program. The program, which allows homeowners to cover 
potentially costly repairs at critical moments, allows neighbors to 
maintain their community pride and lessen the possibility of 
deterioration or vacancies of communities.
  As a result, homeowners can directly invest in their future as 
members of their streets and subdivisions. These efforts have 
culminated in $1.4 million dollars spent on 103 home repairs in the 
last year. Whether it be a plumbing issue, roof leaks, or the thousands 
of other home repairs that can occur and start to snowball, the 
Northland knows it can turn to NNI for help.
  In celebrating this milestone, it is worth focusing on how NNI has 
kept residents connected and engaged over the past three decades. NNI's 
engagement through public meetings and service has been directed at the 
chief cornerstone of what makes communities strong and unique, 
providing a shared space for people to unite and share their concerns 
and beliefs. Your continuous advocacy and core mission has demonstrated 
the significant impact of housing and its role in constructing a 
healthy community. Local progress begins in the homes where the lives 
of those young and old unfold. NNI has shown that its commitment to the 
people they serve is enduring and strong no matter their stage in life, 
from being brought home from the hospital, buying one's first house, to 
new families introducing children into the world.
  Commemorating these 30 years of service, we must recognize the people 
who have made NNI's accomplishments possible. To the leadership, 
contractors, and community partners, thank you for your contributions 
that have placed the voices of the Northland at the center of all you 
have done. Their dedication represents the time, energy, and talents of 
an organization built in service to others.
  As the Northland continues to grow, your work will have played no 
small part in what makes the area special. I am confident that the new 
opportunities and new challenges that this growth brings will be met by 
the same

[[Page E880]]

spirit and dedication that has defined NNI since 1996. Mr. Speaker, I 
ask my colleagues today to join me in celebrating this anniversary. We 
thank them for making the Northland a stronger, safer, and more 
connected place for future generations of residents and families and we 
wish them all the best in their future endeavors.
```
