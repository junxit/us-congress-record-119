# HONORING REDWOOD BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                    HONORING REDWOOD BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge Redwood Baptist 
Church of Zachary, Louisiana, a congregation whose origins date back to 
1857 when the late Reverend Kerchgo Hills founded the church on a Noble 
Tract, with the first services held in a tent. Early officers included 
Brothers Hoarse Richardson, Norse Evening, King Hills, and Ben Rowe, 
along with Sisters Elizabeth Beach, Nancy Rowe, and Emma Hughes. The 
first building served not only as a place of worship but also as one of 
the area's earliest schoolhouses. Many years later, the church was 
rebuilt on Highway 37 at Redwood Creek. Reverend Hills served 
faithfully until his passing, and Redwood was blessed with numerous 
remarkable pastors in the years that followed.
  In 1959, God showed His favor on Redwood Baptist Church when the 
property on Highway 37 was sold to the Louisiana Highway Department, 
prompting the need for a new church site. Under the guidance of the 
Holy Spirit, the pastor and deacons contacted Brother and Sister Jett 
of Zachary, faithful Christians and supporters of God's work. In 1962, 
the Jetts agreed to sell a parcel of land on which Redwood constructed 
its present church home. Redwood remained resilient through trials and 
blessings, receiving a significant renovation in 2001 as the 
congregation continued to grow spiritually, financially, and 
physically.
  In 2014, Reverend Dashawn Harris served as interim pastor until 
Reverend Dale W. Flowers was elected on December 16, 2015, and 
installed on January 3, 2016. Pastor Flowers has since faithfully 
fulfilled the mission of Jesus Christ by preaching, teaching, and 
equipping the saints. In 2016, he published the New Member's 
Orientation and Study Class to teach foundational Christian principles 
and help new members discern God's purpose for their service. Under 
Pastor Flowers' leadership, Redwood continued to prosper, undertaking a 
full sanctuary renovation in 2022 and constructing a beautiful 
fellowship hall in 2024 without requiring a bank loan--a testament to 
both faith and unity within the congregation.
  Mr. Speaker, Redwood Baptist Church continues its legacy of service 
and faith under Pastor Flowers and the deacons, who together 
established a Community Prayer Ministry. Once a month, members go into 
the community to pray and share the message of salvation through Jesus 
Christ. Additional ministries have expanded the church's outreach, 
including Women's Ministry, Hospitality, Health and Wellness, Pastor 
Aide, and Faith Inspired Youth in Action (F.I.Y.A.). Built upon the 
solid rock of Jesus Christ, Redwood Baptist Church has weathered many 
storms and stands today as a testament to steadfast faith, spiritual 
growth, and God's unending blessings.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Redwood Baptist Church 
for its enduring impact and continued devotion to God's Kingdom.
```
