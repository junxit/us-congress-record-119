# HONORING NEW CREATION CHRISTIAN CENTER

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING NEW CREATION CHRISTIAN CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge New Creation 
Christian Center of Baton Rouge, Louisiana, a ministry birthed from a 
divine vision God gave Pastor Cross J. Richard, Sr. in 1999. The 
instruction was clear: `Teach My people to fully trust God through His 
Word.' This mandate became the foundation and guiding direction of the 
ministry. Pastor Richard, a Vietnam War veteran

[[Page E877]]

and Bronze Star Medal recipient, opened New Creation on April 22, 2000, 
with five members, holding services in his home with his wife, Sis. 
Lois Richard. God soon multiplied the membership, blessing the church 
with faithful believers devoted to the vision.
  As the ministry grew, Pastor Richard sought a permanent home. In 
2001, God directed him to 1934 Scenic Highway. After discovering the 
property was indeed for sale, Pastor Richard purchased it, and New 
Creation began renovating the building--an effort requiring over a year 
of prayer, work, and faith. On November 17, 2001, the congregation held 
its first service in the new sanctuary. In 2000, the church met Sis. 
Agnes Mbonyinkebe and her family, who became an integral part of New 
Creation. In 2005, Pastor Richard, Sis. Lois, and Sis. Agnes traveled 
to Rwanda, where Sis. Agnes reunited with family displaced by genocide. 
This journey sparked a global mission that has resulted in the planting 
of seven churches in Rwanda and support for construction of a new 
sanctuary completed in 2019.
  God continued expanding New Creation's impact. In 2005, the church 
purchased 7.74 acres on Oak Villa Boulevard for $506,000--property that 
appraised for nearly double the purchase price. In 2010, the George L. 
Harris School of Ministry opened, teaching believers how to hear the 
voice of God. The land was paid off in the same year. On September 16, 
2012, the Holy Spirit led New Creation to plant a new location at 
Perkins Rowe Cinemark Theater, known affectionately as the `Church with 
the Smell of Popcorn.' With campuses at Scenic Highway, Perkins Rowe, 
and Rwanda, New Creation became one church in three locations. 
Preparations for a new sanctuary began in 2020, marking a new phase of 
growth.
  Mr. Speaker, in 2025 New Creation Christian Center entered a new 
season of leadership as Pastor Cross J. Richard Sr., and Sis. Lois 
Richard transitioned the pastorate to their son, Senior Pastor Joseph 
Hollins, and Leading Lady Casey Hollins. Today, as New Creation 
prepares to complete a nearly four-million-dollar campus expansion in 
2026, the ministry continues advancing its mission locally and 
globally. From a living room gathering to three campuses and 
international outreach, New Creation Christian Center stands as a 
testament to faith, obedience, and God's provision. I ask my colleagues 
to join me in recognizing this church for its steadfast commitment to 
teaching God's people through His Word and serving communities around 
the world.
```
