# HONORING MAJOR KELSIE D. HOVENDEN

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
                   HONORING MAJOR KELSIE D. HOVENDEN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and pay tribute to 
Major Kelsie D. Hovenden, United States Marine Corps. For the past 
three years, Major Hovenden has served with honor and distinction as a 
Congressional Fellow and Appropriations Liaison Officer for the 
Department of the Navy Appropriations Matters Office, Office of the 
Assistant Secretary of the Navy (Financial Management and Comptroller), 
representing the needs of our Marine Corps before the United States 
Congress.
  During her time as a congressional fellow, she gained an immersive, 
behind the scenes understanding of the legislative process and the 
critical work of funding our Nation's defense. Her sharp intellect, 
dedication, and deep understanding of national security and budgetary 
policy were immediately apparent and proved to be an invaluable asset.
  Upon completion of her fellowship, Major Hovenden seamlessly 
transitioned to one of the most strategically consequential billets for 
the Marine Corps, serving as an appropriations liaison officer. In this 
role, she conducted daily engagement with members and staff, ensuring 
that the Marine Corps' concepts, programs, and requirements were widely 
under stood. Her expert navigation of the congressional appropriations 
process and her steadfast advocacy ensured vital funding for our 
Marines across multiple fiscal years. Her work was instrumental in 
supporting the largest budget in Marine Corps history, securing over 
$57 billion in annual resources. This funding was essential to 
advancing the Marine Corps Force Design initiatives, enhancing 
operational readiness, and ensuring our Marines have what they need to 
meet any challenge.
  Furthermore, Major Hovenden played a central role in preparing Marine 
Corps senior leaders for congressional testimony, official visits, 
responded to hundreds of congressional inquiries, and coordinated and 
led congressional and staff delegations. These visits allowed my 
colleagues and me to see Marine Corps capabilities firsthand and gain a 
deeper appreciation for the resource requirements of the force.
  Mr. Speaker, Major Hovenden's performance has been characterized by 
the upmost professionalism, strategic insight, and steadfast commitment 
to her duty. She has personally strengthened the Marine Corps' 
credibility on Capitol Hill and ensured the corps' priorities were 
clearly communicated. I ask that my colleagues join me in recognizing 
and commending Major Hovenden for her service to our country and wish 
her and her family the best in their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
