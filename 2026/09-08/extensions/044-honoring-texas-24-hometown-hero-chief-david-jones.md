# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF DAVID JONES

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CHIEF DAVID JONES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Chief David Jones, whose career reflected a profound dedication 
to public safety, with 31 years in the fire service, including 28 
serving the Town of Addison.
  Chief Jones started at the Addison Fire Department in 1998 as a 
firefighter and licensed paramedic before being promoted to various 
roles. As Chief, he oversaw 59 employees in the department and managed 
operations, fire prevention, training, and compliance. Jones was 
recognized with a Chief Fire Officer designation and holds a four-time 
Chief Training Officer redesignation, reflecting his excellence in 
leadership. His guidance and impact will shape the Addison Fire 
Department for years to come.
  I thank Chief Jones for his service in the Addison Fire Department 
and for helping to keep North Texas safe.
```
