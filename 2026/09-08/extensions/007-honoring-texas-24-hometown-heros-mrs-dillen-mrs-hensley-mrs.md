# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS MRS. DILLEN, MRS. HENSLEY, MRS. EIMERS, AND MRS. LANDOLFA

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HEROS MRS. DILLEN, MRS. HENSLEY, MRS. 
                       EIMERS, AND MRS. LANDOLFA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Heros, Mrs. Dillen, Mrs. Hensley, Mrs. Eimers, and Mrs. Landolfa at 
Forest Vista Elementary School in Flower Mound. In their classrooms, 
they foster a welcoming environment where every child feels valued and 
encouraged to learn. Through their dedication, they support both 
academic growth and the confidence of their students.
  At Forest Vista Elementary, they go above and beyond to ensure that 
every child feels seen and supported. Their impact was especially 
meaningful during Purple Up Day on April 15th, also known as Military 
Child Day, when they wore purple to honor military children and 
presented a student with a special recognition certificate. This 
heartfelt gesture demonstrated their deep understanding of the 
sacrifices military families make and their commitment to celebrating 
the strength and resilience of their students. Their actions reflect 
the best of what our North Texas schools offer, including creating a 
classroom community, and encouragement at the foundational stages of a 
child's education.
  I thank Mrs. Dillen, Mrs. Hensley, Mrs. Eimers, and Mrs. Landolfa, 
for their unwavering dedication to their students and for the 
meaningful difference they make every single day at Forest Vista 
Elementary School.

                          ____________________
```
