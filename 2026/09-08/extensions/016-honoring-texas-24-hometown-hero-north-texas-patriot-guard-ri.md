# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO NORTH TEXAS PATRIOT GUARD RIDERS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO NORTH TEXAS PATRIOT GUARD RIDERS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, the North Texas Patriot Guard Riders. They are a dedicated 
volunteer organization that honors the service and sacrifice of 
America's military members, veterans, and first responders. At the 
invitation of families, the group attends funeral services for fallen 
heroes across North Texas, participating in an average of 35 missions 
each month. Their members come from all backgrounds, some are veterans 
themselves, and while many ride motorcycles, others drive cars or 
trucks. What unites them is a shared commitment to showing respect for 
those who served our country and communities.
  The Flag Line is their most revered symbol, embodying the Patriot 
Guard Riders' values of honor, appreciation, and respect. When asked, 
they display American flags outside funeral homes and at burial sites 
to pay tribute to these heroes. The North Texas Patriot Guard Riders 
provide these services at no cost, exemplifying the spirit of service, 
patriotism, and community that represents the very best of North Texas.
```
