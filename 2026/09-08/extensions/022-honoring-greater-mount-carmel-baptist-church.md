# HONORING GREATER MOUNT CARMEL BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
              HONORING GREATER MOUNT CARMEL BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge a venerable 
congregation in my district, Greater Mount Carmel Baptist Church in the 
Scotlandville community of Baton Rouge, Louisiana, as it celebrates 
more than a century of faith, fellowship, and service.
  Greater Mount Carmel Baptist Church's founding dates back to 1919, 
led by major movers for a new church in Scotlandville. They were 
Jenkins Johnson, J.S. Jones, I.S. Powell, Jake Spears, and Wesley 
Gaines. Initially, the services were held in Odd Fellows Hall with the 
Reverend Netters officiating. When the membership reached about thirty 
in 1920, the first sanctuary was erected. The first home of the church 
was constructed of cypress planks and measured forty by sixty feet at a 
cost of three hundred ninety dollars. Ernest O'Connor was the builder. 
The building was situated on the present site, consisting of two lots; 
one was purchased by the congregation for ninety dollars, and a local 
citizen, Mr. A.R. Bacon, donated the second.
  In 1925 and 1926, new pews were installed and weatherboard siding was 
added. Spearheading the financial drives to accomplish these 
improvements were two of the founders, Jake Spears and Jenkins Johnson. 
The names of Eddie White, Dudley Smith, and Annie Watkins were added to 
the list.
  The second period, which extends from 1939 to 1951, has been labeled 
the Building Period, a building of physical structure and a building of 
congregational membership. The second edifice to house the membership 
and its activities was constructed in 1939. In addition to Pastor 
Brown, the movers were Henry Franklin, Sr., Jessie Early, Foster Reese, 
Sr., JE. LaBoye, George Lewis, W.H. Johnson, Obie Vernon, Fred Davis, 
and Granville Haggood. The cost of the new facility was five thousand 
dollars, and Mr. Henry Christenterry was the contractor.
  In 1949, J.E. LaBoye provided leadership for an expansion of the 
physical plant. Renovation costs were three thousand dollars, and W.S. 
Devall was the contractor. It was in 1951 that the name of the church 
was changed to the Greater Mount Carmel Baptist Church.
  Under the pastoral leadership of Reverend K. Edward Popleon, the 
congregation was led into a new annex, identified as the Educational 
Wing of Greater Mount Carmel Baptist Church. The new facility provided 
four offices, three restrooms, five classrooms, one fellowship hall 
with a kitchen that accommodates groups of fifty, one hundred, or two 
hundred, three spacious foyers, ample storage areas with furnishings, 
and a choir room with robe storage.
  Other additions to the main building include a new baptistry, a 
hallway enlargement, a Lord's Supper preparation area, and an area for 
ushers. Furniture and furnishings for the new facility cost sixteen 
thousand dollars. Raymond Honore of Down South Construction was the 
general contractor, and Lonnie Wilkinson was the architect. Additional 
property was purchased on February 18, 1986. Today, Greater Mount 
Carmel is pastored by Pastor Clee Lowe.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in acknowledging Greater 
Mount Carmel Baptist Church for more than a century of faithful 
service, and in wishing its pastor and congregation continued blessings 
in the years to come.
```
