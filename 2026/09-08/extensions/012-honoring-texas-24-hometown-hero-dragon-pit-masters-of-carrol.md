# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DRAGON PIT MASTERS OF CARROLL ISD

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO DRAGON PIT MASTERS OF CARROLL ISD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, the Dragon Pit Masters of Carroll ISD. The Dragon Pit Masters 
have earned the title of Grand Champion at the Texas High School BBQ 
State Championship, showcasing exceptional skill, teamwork, and 
dedication. Competing

[[Page E878]]

against top student teams from across the state, these talented 
students demonstrated mastery in grilling, presentation, and time 
management to take home the highest honor.
  Through countless hours of preparation, the Dragon Pit Masters have 
built a program that emphasizes leadership, collaboration, and 
excellence both inside and outside the competition. Representing the 
Southlake community with pride, these students continue to set a high 
standard and inspire others through their achievements.
  Congratulations to Carroll's Grand Champion Team, Preston Inouye--
12th Grade, Darien Kawai--11th Grade, Caden Roberson--12th Grade, Cruz 
Paredes--10th Grade, and Ryan Vu--12th Grade.

                          ____________________
```
