# HONORING THE SERVICE OF OLGA MONTES

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
                  HONORING THE SERVICE OF OLGA MONTES

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Olga H. Montes for 27 years of dedicated service to the City of Combes.
  Since first being elected to the Combes City Commission in 1999, she 
has devoted herself to strengthening her community and improving the 
quality of life for its residents. During her tenure, Combes has grown 
from a small town into a thriving city with new businesses, programs, 
and services for families and seniors.
  Ms. Montes always understood that public service extends beyond 
holding office. She has worked to ensure families have the support they 
need, providing Thanksgiving meals, clothing for children, and 
celebrations for local youth.
  Beyond the City Commission, Ms. Montes has also made a lasting impact 
by mentoring young women interested in public service. Her guidance and 
encouragement have inspired the next generation of leaders in South 
Texas.
  After nearly three decades, Ms. Montes plans to retire from the 
Combes City Commission at the end of her current term. Her retirement 
marks the conclusion of an extraordinary chapter, but her legacy will 
endure in the hearts of the families she helped uplift and the city she 
helped build.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the 34th Congressional District of Texas, I 
extend my deepest gratitude to Olga H. Montes for her decades of 
leadership and commitment to the people of Combes. South Texas is 
stronger because of her contributions, and I wish her continued health, 
happiness, and success in the years ahead.
```
