# RECOGNIZING JORGE MARIO ART DECKER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                   RECOGNIZING JORGE MARIO ART DECKER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jorge Mario Art 
Decker for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Jorge Mario has overcome many challenges along his journey to 
success, demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive 
to make the most of their education, like Jorge Mario, develop crucial 
skills and a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their 
lives. This award is a testament to Jorge Mario's hard work, 
determination, and perseverance at Drake Middle School and is clearly 
just the beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Jorge Mario Art Decker on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
