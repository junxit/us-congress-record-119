# HONORING THE SERVICE OF SCOTT C. CUNNINGHAM ON THE OCCASION OF HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Guest, Michael (G000591) [R-MS]

```
  HONORING THE SERVICE OF SCOTT C. CUNNINGHAM ON THE OCCASION OF HIS 
                               RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL GUEST

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 8, 2026

  Mr. GUEST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Scott C. 
Cunningham, who is retiring from the House of Representatives after 25 
years of exemplary service.
  Upon graduation from Emory University, Scott relocated to Washington, 
D.C., to begin his career as a Legislative Assistant for the Honorable 
Pete Sessions, a Member from his home state of Texas. Scott's hard 
work, problem-solving skills, and can-do attitude quickly made him a 
valued member of Congressman Sessions' staff.
  In 2007, Scott took his talents to the office of another Texan, the 
Honorable Kenny Marchant, serving in multiple capacities from Policy 
Advisor to Deputy Chief of Staff over the next fourteen years. During 
his last two years with Mr. Marchant, Scott was a Senior Advisor and 
the designated liaison to the House Ethics Committee. After Mr. 
Merchant's departure from Congress, Scott continued to serve the House 
by joining the office of Congressman Scott DesJarlais as a Special 
Assistant.
  In 2021, Scott joined the Office of General Counsel (OGC) as its 
Director of Administration and Member Services. Scott took on new tasks 
and responsibilities with aplomb and immediately became an 
indispensable part of the OGC team. It would be impossible to list all 
his contributions, but among them, he has taken the lead role relating 
to all aspects of office management and administration, including the 
office budget and computer systems, and was very often the first point 
of contact for a Member office when interfacing with OGC. During his 
time with OGC, Scott displayed an admirable level of dedication to 
serving Members, Officers, and employees of the House, proving to be 
remarkably trustworthy and utterly fearless when faced with novel, 
sensitive, and difficult matters. Scott's unwavering resolve and 
commitment to the House as an institution and OGC's particular role 
within that institution was always evident, as was his ability to find 
a solution to seemingly any problem.
  It is hard to imagine a congressional staffer much finer than Scott, 
who embodies the true spirit of public service. Scott has been an 
irreplaceable member of every team on which he has served. He has been 
a kind and thoughtful coworker to all, treating everyone from the 
Members to the maintenance staff with the utmost respect and kindness. 
As scores of current and former colleagues can attest, Scott is the 
kind of coworker who makes the workday better for everyone with whom he 
comes into contact. It is no exaggeration to say that he has become a 
fixture of the House community over his years on the Hill. Indeed, it 
is practically impossible for Scott to go anywhere on the Hill and not 
run into at least one person who wanted to stop and catch up with him. 
Scott is always eager to share his passion for all things aviation and 
travel, being a frequent wedding guest, and of course rooting for the 
U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Teams, especially when it comes to the 
sport of curling. Scott's convivial and welcoming presence on the Hill 
will be sorely missed by all those who had the pleasure of working with 
him.
  It will be hard to imagine the House without Scott. Every day for the 
past 25 years, Scott has served the House with dedication and 
enthusiasm. Regardless of the task assigned, Scott will always find a 
way to accomplish it--a quality that will continue to serve him well in 
all future endeavors. On behalf of the Office of General Counsel and 
the entire House community, I thank Scott for his decades of service 
and wish him a joyous and relaxing retirement among his many friends, 
family, and former colleagues whether here in Washington, in Texas, or 
around the world.

                          ____________________
```
