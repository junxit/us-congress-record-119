# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10311

**Measures:** H.R. 10311

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 10311.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution of the 
     United States, relating to Congress's authority to provide 
     for the general welfare, and Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, 
     relating to Congress's authority to make all laws necessary 
     and proper for carrying into execution the foregoing powers.
```
