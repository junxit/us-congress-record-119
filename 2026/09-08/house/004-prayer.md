# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Lord, when we have found ourselves slipping, when we have chastened 
ourselves for mistakes or bad decisions, Your steadfast love has helped 
us up. Help us to learn that in our humanity, we will often find 
ourselves falling short of the mark, inadequate to the task, or daunted 
by the responsibility laid on us. As You have done before, so You will 
again help us up, dust us off, and send us forth, forgiven and restored 
to do the work You have called us to do.
  Likewise, when we have been overwrought with concerns, distressed by 
circumstances, Your consolations have cheered our souls, and Your 
ministrations are not mere platitudes or feeble words of encouragement.
  No, You who have watched over us day and night calm us with Your very 
presence, so that even when our fears are many, we need only lean on 
Your guidance and listen for Your Word.
  To You, O Lord, we offer this day and all that it brings with it, 
knowing that Your steadfast love and Your abiding presence will see us 
through.
  So then may Your name, in which we pray, be glorified.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
