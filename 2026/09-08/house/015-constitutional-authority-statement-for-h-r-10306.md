# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10306

**Measures:** H.R. 10306

```
           By Mr. AMODEI of Nevada:
       H.R. 10306.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to authorize Appropriations under 
     Article I, Section 9 of the United States Constitution.
```
