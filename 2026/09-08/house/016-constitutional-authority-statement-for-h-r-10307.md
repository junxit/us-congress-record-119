# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10307

**Measures:** H.R. 10307

```
           By Mr. BEGICH:
       H.R. 10307.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2 of the United States 
     Constitution, which grants Congress the power ``to dispose of 
     and make all needful Rules and Regulations respecting the 
     Territory or other Property belonging to the United States.''
       This bill amends section 201(4)(a) of the Alaska National 
     Interest Lands Conservation Act (16 U.S.C. 410hh(4)(a)) to 
     clarify permissible modes of surface transportation for 
     subsistence users at Gates of the Arctic National Park and 
     Preserve, land held and managed by the United States. The 
     regulation of access to and use of federal land is a needful 
     rule respecting property belonging to the United States 
     within the meaning of the Property Clause.
```
