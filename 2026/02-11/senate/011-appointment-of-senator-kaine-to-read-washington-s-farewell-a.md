# APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR KAINE TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

```
   APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR KAINE TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, as modified by 
the order of February 11, 2026, appoints the Senator from Virginia, Mr. 
Kaine, to read Washington's Farewell Address on Monday, February 23, 
2026.
```
