# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

**Speaking:** Britt, Katie Boyd (B001319) [R-AL]

```
 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Mrs. BRITT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the order of January 24, 1901, the traditional reading 
of Washington's Farewell Address take place on Monday, February 23, 
2026, following the prayer and Pledge of Allegiance to the flag.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
