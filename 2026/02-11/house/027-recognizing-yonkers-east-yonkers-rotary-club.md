# RECOGNIZING YONKERS-EAST YONKERS ROTARY CLUB

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
              RECOGNIZING YONKERS-EAST YONKERS ROTARY CLUB

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I recognize the community service provided 
by the men and women of the Yonkers-East Yonkers Rotary Club. Every 
day, they live out their voluntary efforts of the Rotary Four-Plus-One 
Test, ensuring truth, fairness, good will, benefit, and fun in their 
activities under the banner of service above self.
  Recently, the club raised needed funds for their community service 
projects, completing a 25-hour-plus marathon filibuster, assisting 
families in need.
  The club is led by President Alan Gray, President-elect Anne Murphy, 
Vice President Jeffrey Landsman, Immediate Past President George John, 
and other key officers: Greg Arcaro, Christian Fassetta, Philippe 
Charles, Matt Koppe, Alix Schnee, and many more.
  From the floor of the House of Representatives, we salute the Rotary 
Club of Yonkers-East Yonkers, and I wish them continued success in all 
of their good works.

                          ____________________
```
