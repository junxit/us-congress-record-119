# RECOGNIZING BAILEY LITTLE

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BAILEY LITTLE

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 
Bailey Little, a 2023 Bellefonte High School graduate.

[[Page H2173]]

  Bailey recently received an American degree from the Future Farmers 
of America. Bailey is the first Bellefonte FFA member in nearly two 
decades to receive an American degree. She has been an active FFA 
member since her freshman year.
  The American degree is the highest FFA honor and is given to members 
for their dedication to their chapter, leadership, and community 
involvement.
  To receive an American degree, members must receive their State FFA 
degree, be an active member of their chapter for at least 3 years, and 
take part in various agricultural education programs.
  Bailey's FFA projects included raising beef cattle, dairy cows, 
goats, poultry, and rabbits. Along with raising livestock, Bailey is a 
small business owner, selling Western jewelry and clothing. 
Additionally, Bailey and her fiance raise beef cattle in Centre County.
  Mr. Speaker, Bailey should be proud of her accomplishments, and it is 
a great example for FFA members in our community. I congratulate Bailey 
on receiving the American degree.

                          ____________________
```
