         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

       Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were

     Institutes of Health; with an amendment (Rept. 119-734). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. GARBARINO: Committee on Homeland Security. H.R. 8897. A 
     bill to improve travel for American families, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-735). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GARBARINO: Committee on Homeland Security. H.R. 7574. A 
     bill to provide for the reorganization and realignment of the 
     Engagement, Liaison, and Outreach Office of the Department of 
     Homeland Security, and for other purposes (Rept. 119-736). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. GARBARINO: Committee on Homeland Security. H.R. 8770. A 
     bill to amend title 49, United States Code, to establish 
     funds for investments in aviation security, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-737). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GARBARINO: Committee on Homeland Security. H.R. 8168. A 
     bill to require an assessment of terrorism threats to the 
     United States posed by foreign terrorist organizations and 
     Specially Designated Global Terrorists present in countries 
     that are major non-NATO allies, and for other purposes (Rept. 
     119-738). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
