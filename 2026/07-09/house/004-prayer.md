# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, we give thanks for this Nation and for the 
Representatives who have answered the call to serve its people.
  As we mark the recent anniversary of our country's founding, renew in 
us a grateful appreciation for the freedoms we enjoy and the duties 
they entail.
  Grant our Representatives wisdom, humility, and steadfast hearts as 
they seek the common good. Bless them, their families, their staffs, 
and all who serve in this House, and continue to watch over this Nation 
with Your gracious hand.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
