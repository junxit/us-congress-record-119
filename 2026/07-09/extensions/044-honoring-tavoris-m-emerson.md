# HONORING TAVORIS M. EMERSON

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                      HONORING TAVORIS M. EMERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. 
Tavoris M. Emerson for his outstanding service, leadership, and 
unwavering dedication to the citizens of Mississippi.
  Tavoris M. Emerson was born and raised in the Mississippi Delta, 
where he developed a strong sense of duty, integrity, and commitment to 
sewing others. Throughout his life, he has demonstrated a passion for 
public service and a steadfast dedication to protecting and improving 
the communities he serves.
  A proud veteran of the United States Army, Mr. Emerson served his 
country as a combat engineer, embodying the values of courage, 
discipline, and selfless service. Following his military service, he 
continued his commitment to public safety through a distinguished 
career in law enforcement and corrections.
  Over the course of more than 16 years in public service, Mr. Emerson 
has held numerous leadership positions throughout Mississippi. His 
career has included service as an internal affairs investigator, chief 
of investigations, probation and parole agent, and community 
corrections officer with the Mississippi Department of Corrections. In 
each role, he earned a reputation for professionalism, accountability, 
and a relentless pursuit of justice.
  Today, Mr. Emerson serves as the assistant chief of police for the 
Marks Police Department, where he plays a vital role in overseeing 
daily operations, mentoring officers, strengthening community 
relationships, and ensuring the safety and well-being of the citizens 
of Marks and the surrounding communities. His leadership has been 
instrumental in advancing public trust, enhancing departrnentai 
effectiveness, and promoting professional standards within law 
enforcement.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Mr. Emerson is a devoted 
father who takes great pride in the achievements and development of his 
sons. He is deeply committed to family values, mentorship, and serving 
as a positive example for future generations. His dedication to both 
his family and community reflects the character and integrity that have 
defined his life and career.
  Throughout his journey, Mr. Emerson has faced challenges with 
resilience and determination, continually demonstrating faith, 
perseverance, and an unwavering commitment to serving others. Guided by 
the Scripture, ``Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do 
not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be 
with you wherever you go,'' Joshua 1:9, he has remained steadfast 
through adversity while continuing to serve his family, community, and 
state with honor and distinction.
  Mr. Emerson's life exemplifies the principles of courage, service, 
leadership, and faith. His dedication to public safety, commitment to 
his community, and perseverance through life's challenges serve as an 
inspiration to those around him and to future generations of public 
servants.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. 
Tavoris M. Emerson for his distinguished service to his community, his 
state, and his Nation. His commitment to public selvice, leadership, 
faith, and excellence makes him a worthy recipient of this recognition 
and an example for all Mississippians to follow.

                          ____________________
```
