# HONORING FALLAH HEARD, KNOWN AS "FALLAH THE FOODIE"

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
         HONORING FALLAH HEARD, KNOWN AS ``FALLAH THE FOODIE''

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Fallah Heard, widely known as ``Fallah the Foodie,'' a 
passionate entrepreneur, social media influencer, and community 
advocate whose work is helping shine a spotlight on Mississippi's 
vibrant culinary scene.
  A native of Byram, Mississippi, Ms. Heard developed a love for food 
and community at an early age. Inspired by family traditions and a 
desire to connect with others, she transformed her passion into a 
platform that celebrates local restaurants and the hardworking people 
behind them. What began as sharing food experiences online has grown 
into a respected voice that highlights businesses throughout Jackson 
and surrounding communities.
  Through her social media presence, Ms. Heard has become a champion 
for locally owned restaurants, helping introduce residents and visitors 
alike to the unique flavors and dining experiences available across 
Mississippi. Her authentic approach and genuine enthusiasm have helped 
increase visibility for many small businesses, contributing to their 
growth and success while encouraging greater appreciation for the 
state's rich food culture.
  Beyond reviewing restaurant, Ms. Heard is committed to fostering 
community connections and creating opportunities for local 
entrepreneurs. She has organized events that bring together food 
vendors, business owners, and residents, helping strengthen 
relationships and support economic development within the region. Her 
efforts reflect a deep belief in the power of food to bring people 
together and build stronger communities.
  Ms. Heard's work has also helped elevate Jackson's reputation as a 
destination for exceptional dining experiences. Through her advocacy 
and promotion of local establishments, she continues to demonstrate the 
important role that small businesses play in the economic and cultural 
vitality of our communities.
  Her dedication, creativity, and commitment to uplifting others serve 
as an inspiration to many. By using her platform to support local 
businesses and celebrate Mississippi's culinary talent, Ms. Heard is 
making a lasting impact on communities throughout our state.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Fallah 
Heard for her outstanding contributions to Mississippi's restaurant 
industry, her support of small businesses, and her continued efforts to 
showcase the best of our state.
```
