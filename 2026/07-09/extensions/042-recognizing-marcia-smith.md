# RECOGNIZING MARCIA SMITH

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
                        RECOGNIZING MARCIA SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
leader from Florida's 3rd Congressional District, Marcia Smith, on her 
well-deserved induction into the Florida FFA Hall of Fame.
  This prestigious honor reflects Marcia's remarkable career devoted to 
advancing agricultural education, student leadership, and the values 
that make our rural communities so strong. Through her unwavering 
commitment to the Florida FFA, she has helped shape generations of 
young leaders, equipping them with the skills, confidence, and 
character to succeed both inside and outside the classroom.
  Marcia's passion for agriculture and belief in the potential of every 
student have left a lasting impact across Florida FFA. The countless 
young people she has mentored and inspired will carry forward the 
lessons she instilled and continue strengthening Florida's agricultural 
community for generations to come.
  Marcia's influence extends far beyond any single chapter or 
classroom. Through her selfless service and steadfast dedication to 
mentoring the next generation of agricultural leaders, she has 
exemplified what it means to lead with purpose and passion.
  I congratulate Marcia on this well-deserved recognition. I thank her 
for her years of dedicated service to Florida's students and 
agricultural community. Her leadership, commitment, and lasting impact 
have made Florida's Third District proud.

                          ____________________
```
