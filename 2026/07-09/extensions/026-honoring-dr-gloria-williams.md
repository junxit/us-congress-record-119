# HONORING DR. GLORIA WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                      HONORING DR. GLORIA WILLIAMS

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor one of South Florida's most respected and heralded religious 
leaders, Dr. Gloria Williams. She is the Pastor and co-founder of Jesus 
People Ministries Church International Incorporated, located in Miami 
Gardens, Florida.
  Dr. Williams is a woman who walks in faith and higher education. She 
has a Bachelor of Science Degree in Criminal Justice from Florida 
International University. Dr. Williams has received an honorary Doctor 
of Divinity Degree from All Nations for Christ Institute International; 
previously affiliated with Oral Roberts University. Her administrative 
gifts and skills have proven vital to the Church and her ministry.
  Dr. Williams is the Chief Overseer of the Right Connection 
Association and the executive producer and host of the weekly broadcast 
``Right Connection,'' which is seen nationally and worldwide. The 
broadcast affords Dr. Williams the opportunity to challenge and 
encourage her followers to develop and mature in their faith. She has 
been affectionately deemed a Christian role model and her dynamic 
character has proven to be an asset and an inspiration.
  Dr. Williams is the proud mother of daughter Richelle and wife of the 
late Bishop Isaiah S. Williams, Jr.
  Please join me in honoring Dr. Gloria Williams Pastor and co-founder 
of Jesus Ministries Church International for unwavering faith and 
commitment to her congregation and to her community.

                          ____________________
```
