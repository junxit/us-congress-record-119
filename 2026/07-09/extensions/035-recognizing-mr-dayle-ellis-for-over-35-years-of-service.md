# RECOGNIZING MR. DAYLE ELLIS FOR OVER 35 YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Vindman, Eugene Simon (V000138) [D-VA]

```
        RECOGNIZING MR. DAYLE ELLIS FOR OVER 35 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Mr. 
Dayle Ellis for 36 years of Federal Service and his commitment to the 
Department of Defense Education Activity (DoDEA).
  Throughout his career, including as an administrative officer, Mr. 
Ellis has provided critical guidance to military families, helping them 
to navigate difficult K-12 education options and determine student 
eligibility as they transitioned between duty stations around the 
world. His expertise, professionalism, and compassion have ensured the 
families and students receive consistent support during some of the 
most challenging periods of military life.
  Mr. Ellis's dedication has supported military retention, helped 
families navigate education options for their children, and contributed 
to the success of military communities across different branches. 
Countless families have benefited not only from the positive outcomes 
he has helped achieve but also from the care and unwavering commitment 
he has brought to every interaction. His service demonstrates DoDEA's 
mission of supporting military-connected students and their families 
with excellence and integrity.
  I want to personally thank Mr. Ellis for the outstanding impact he 
has had on his colleagues, families, students, and the many individuals 
he has served over the past 36 years. His leadership and expertise 
reflect the very best of public service.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Dayle 
Ellis for his service and the difference he has made in our 
communities. I wish him all the best in his well-earned retirement and 
in the years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
