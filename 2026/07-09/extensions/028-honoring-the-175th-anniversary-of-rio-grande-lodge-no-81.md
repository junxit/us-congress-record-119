# HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF RIO GRANDE LODGE NO. 81

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
       HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF RIO GRANDE LODGE NO. 81

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the 175th Anniversary of Rio Grande Lodge No. 81, Ancient Free and 
Accepted Masons, and honor their unwavering commitment to serving the 
Brownsville community.
  Since 1851, Rio Grande Lodge No. 81 has strengthened South Texas 
through dedicated civic engagement and public service. By supporting 
youth initiatives, assisting seniors, and investing in local 
facilities, the Lodge has strengthened our community and improved the 
lives of countless families.
  Education has long been at the heart of the Lodge's mission. From 
sponsoring early literacy programs to funding scholarships, their work 
empowers and provides vital resources for students to finish high 
school and earn their bachelor's degrees. These initiatives have 
fostered a love of learning, greater civic responsibility, and 
patriotism within generations of young people.
  Beyond the classroom, the Lodge has worked to improve the lives of 
families throughout our community. Its members have helped provide 
dental care, medical treatment, and financial assistance, while also 
helping parents and grandparents by building ramps in homes to enhance 
safety and accessibility.
  Rio Grande Lodge No. 81 has also worked diligently to preserve the 
rich history of Brownsville and South Texas. Through its longstanding 
support of local museums, the Lodge has helped ensure that future 
generations understand the sacrifices, traditions, and stories that 
have shaped our region and our Nation.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Texas' 34th Congressional 
District, I extend my deepest gratitude for the 175 years of service 
provided by the Rio Grande Lodge No. 81. Its outstanding contributions 
have inspired generations of South Texans, and I'm confident they will 
inspire many more.
```
