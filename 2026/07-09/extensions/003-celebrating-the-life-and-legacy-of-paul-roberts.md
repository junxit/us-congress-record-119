# CELEBRATING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF PAUL ROBERTS

**Speaking:** Larsen, Rick (L000560) [D-WA]

```
            CELEBRATING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF PAUL ROBERTS

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. RICK LARSEN

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, July 9, 2026

  Mr. LARSEN of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of Paul Roberts, a dedicated public servant, environmental 
advocate, policy expert, musician and proud resident of Everett, 
Washington. Sadly, Paul passed away on June 24, 2026 after a courageous 
battle with cancer.
  Paul Roberts was instrumental in making Everett the city it is today. 
His legacy can be seen in the fabric of everyday life in Everett--in 
its environmental plans, transportation investments, schools, arts and 
music scene and in its future light rail system. Whether as a city 
planner, policy consultant, representative for regional boards or as a 
long-time member of the Everett City Council, Paul used his 
extraordinary talents to serve the people of Everett and the greater 
Puget Sound region.
  From a deep passion for protecting our cherished environment, to his 
foresight on bringing the naval base to Everett, Paul was the 
embodiment of what a lifelong public servant is. Even after leaving the 
City Council, he continued his work in public service for my office, 
ensuring that local communities benefited from the Bipartisan 
Infrastructure Law. His knowledge of local government and unwavering 
focus on delivering results helped Northwest Washington communities 
access critical resources to build a cleaner, greener, safer and more 
accessible transportation network.
  Paul was one of the kindest, most dedicated and hardworking people I 
have ever known. He was an amazing mentor and inspiration for the 
members of my staff that were lucky enough to work with him. He had a 
real gift for bringing people together to seek common ground and solve 
tough problems.
  Mr. Speaker, Paul Roberts' impact on Everett and Northwest Washington 
is immeasurable. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his 
remarkable service, celebrating his legacy, and extending our deepest 
condolences to his wife, Marianne, their family and all who were 
fortunate to know and work alongside him.
```
