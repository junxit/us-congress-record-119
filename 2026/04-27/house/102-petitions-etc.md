# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-10. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Miami-Dade 
     Board of County Commissioners, relative to Resolution No. 
     R194-26, urging President Donald J. Trump, Secretary of State 
     Marco A. Rubio, and the United States Congress to (1) engage 
     and consult with the Cuban exile community in deliberations 
     concerning the future of Cuba and (2) provide resources to 
     local governments and Cuban exile groups in South Florida, if 
     necessary, in the event there is regime change in Cuba; which 
     was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
