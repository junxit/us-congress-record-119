# White House Correspondents' Association Dinner Shooting (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
        White House Correspondents' Association Dinner Shooting

  Mr. SCHUMER. Well, Mr. President, I and we all are relieved that no 
one was seriously injured in the shooting at the White House 
Correspondents' Dinner over the weekend. No matter the perpetrator, no 
matter the motive, political violence endangers our democracy.
  I denounce political violence in all its forms, as I always have and 
always will.
  I commend law enforcement's swift response to apprehend the gunman, 
which undoubtedly saved many lives, and that includes the Secret 
Service.
```
