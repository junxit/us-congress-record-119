# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been 
presented under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the 
motion.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 827, Daniel Mack Traynor, of North 
     Dakota, to be United States Circuit Judge for the Eighth 
     Circuit.
         John Thune, Tim Sheehy, John Barrasso, Pete Ricketts, 
           Thom Tillis, Chuck Grassley, Ted Budd, Shelley Moore 
           Capito, Bill Hagerty, Kevin Cramer, Cynthia M. Lummis, 
           Marsha Blackburn, Tim Scott of South Carolina, Tom 
           Cotton, John Hoeven, Darline Graham, Ted Cruz.
```
