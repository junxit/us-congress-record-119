# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 177, S. 179, S. 726, S. 1414, S. 1537, S. 1690, S. 1716, S. 2006, S. 2042, S. 2195, S. 3378, S. 3524, S. 3589, S. 3691, S. 3778, S. 4395, S. 4429, S. 4632, S. 4784, S. 4824, S. 4952, S. 4974, S. 5023

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 177

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the name of the Senator from North 
Carolina (Mr. Budd) was added as a cosponsor of S. 177, a bill to 
prohibit Federal funding of Planned Parenthood Federation of America.


                                 S. 179

  At the request of Mr. Tuberville, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 179, a bill 
to amend the Defense Production Act of 1950 to prevent harm and 
disruption to the United States agriculture industry by protecting 
against foreign influence over agriculture production and supply 
chains, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 726

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from Rhode 
Island (Mr. Whitehouse) was added as a cosponsor of S. 726, a bill to 
amend chapter 44 of title 18, United States Code, to require the safe 
storage of firearms, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1414

  At the request of Mr. Paul, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1414, a bill to 
amend the Public Health Service Act to provide that clinical studies 
required for licensure of biological products as biosimilar shall not 
be required to include the assessment of immunogenicity, 
pharmacodynamics, or comparative clinical efficacy.


                                S. 1537

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from Ohio 
(Mr. Moreno) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1537, a bill to amend title 
38, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs 
to approve interstate commerce carrier apprenticeship programs for 
purposes of veterans educational assistance, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1690

  At the request of Mr. Whitehouse, the name of the Senator from 
Connecticut (Mr. Blumenthal) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1690, a 
bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase funding for 
Social Security and Medicare.


                                S. 1716

  At the request of Mr. Cramer, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Lujan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1716, a bill to amend title 
XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to improve health care coverage 
under vision plans, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2006

  At the request of Ms. Warren, the name of the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Hickenlooper) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2006, a bill to amend 
title 10, United States Code, to prohibit discrimination in the Armed 
Forces on the basis of gender identity, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2042

  At the request of Ms. Cantwell, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Mr. Van Hollen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2042, a bill to provide 
lasting protection for inventoried roadless areas within the National 
Forest System.


                                S. 2195

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the names of the Senator from Texas 
(Mr. Cornyn) and the Senator from Alaska (Ms. Murkowski) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 2195, a bill to award a Congressional Gold Medal, 
collectively, to the brave women who served in World War II as members 
of the U.S. Army Nurse Corps and U.S. Navy Nurse Corps.


                                S. 3378

  At the request of Ms. Cantwell, the name of the Senator from Virginia 
(Mr. Warner) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3378, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to establish name, image, and likeness 
investment accounts for student-athletes, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3524

  At the request of Mr. McCormick, the name of the Senator from Texas 
(Mr. Cornyn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3524, a bill to amend title 
54, United States Code, to modify certain cost-sharing requirements for 
grant programs under the American Battlefield Protection Program, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 3589

  At the request of Ms. Hassan, the name of the Senator from Maine (Mr. 
King) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3589, a bill to amend the Higher 
Education Act of 1965 to provide students with disabilities and their 
families with access to critical information needed to select the right 
college and succeed once enrolled.


                                S. 3691

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from 
Colorado (Mr. Hickenlooper) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3691, a bill 
to establish the Commission on Equity and Reconciliation in the 
Uniformed Services.


                                S. 3778

  At the request of Mr. Sheehy, the name of the Senator from Kansas 
(Mr. Marshall) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3778, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to expand the carbon oxide sequestration 
credit to include solid or liquid carbon capture facilities.


                                S. 4395

  At the request of Mr. McCormick, the names of the Senator from Idaho 
(Mr. Risch) and the Senator from Connecticut (Mr. Blumenthal) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 4395, a bill to reauthorize the Terrorism 
Risk Insurance Act of 2002, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4429

  At the request of Mr. Moreno, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4429, a bill to 
prohibit the importation, manufacture, sale, resale, or introduction 
into interstate commerce in the United States of connected vehicles and 
related software and hardware associated with foreign adversaries.


                                S. 4632

  At the request of Mr. Lankford, the names of the Senator from Alaska 
(Mr. Sullivan), the Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Tillis), the 
Senator from Texas (Mr. Cornyn) and the Senator from Oklahoma (Mr. 
Armstrong) were added as cosponsors of S. 4632, a bill to provide for a 
period of continuing appropriations in the event of a lapse in 
appropriations under the normal appropriations process, to establish 
procedures and consequences in the event of a failure to enact 
appropriations, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4824

  At the request of Mr. Sheehy, the name of the Senator from Florida 
(Mrs. Moody) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4824, a bill to amend 
section 5545 of title 5, United States Code, to provide hazard pay for 
carrying out prescribed burns, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4952

  At the request of Ms. Ernst, the names of the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mr. Ricketts), the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Marshall), the Senator 
from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer), the Senator from Florida (Mrs. Moody),

                                S. 4974

  At the request of Mr. Tuberville, the name of the Senator from 
Virginia (Mr. Kaine) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4974, a bill to 
amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act with respect to food 
safety.


                                S. 5023

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the name of the Senator from

amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide for
```
