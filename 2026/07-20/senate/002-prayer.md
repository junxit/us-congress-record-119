# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, source of righteousness, joy, and peace, challenge us 
when we assume we know what is right instead of seeking Your wisdom.
  Lord, guide our leaders and lawmakers to seek Your truth, follow Your 
direction, and make decisions with humility and discernment. Help them 
pursue what is best for our Nation and world, trusting in Your 
sovereignty, power, and love.
  Remind them that nothing is impossible with You. Strengthen them with 
Your wisdom, fill them with Your peace, bless them with Your abundance, 
and prepare them for each day ahead.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
