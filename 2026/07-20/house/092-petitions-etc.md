# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-17. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Embassy of 
     the Republic of the Union of Myanmar, relative to Information 
     Sheets Volume VIII, Issue 29 and 30, to better understand the 
     real situation and true facts about Myanmar; to the Committee 
     on Foreign Affairs.
       PT-18. Also, a petition of the Embassy of the Republic of 
     the Union of Myanmar, relative to Information Sheets, Volume 
     VIII, Issues 50 to 55, to better understand the real 
     situation and true facts about Myanmar; to the Committee on 
     Foreign Affairs.
```
