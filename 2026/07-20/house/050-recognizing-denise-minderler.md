# RECOGNIZING DENISE MINDERLER

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING DENISE MINDERLER

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Denise Minderler for her commitment to the Special Olympics.
  Denise, a longtime resident of Potter County, Pennsylvania, has 
participated in the Special Olympics for 47 years. This incredible 
organization provides children and adults with intellectual 
disabilities the opportunity to train and compete in sports.
  Denise's first competition was in 1978. She competed in bowling, 
which remains one of her favorite sports.
  During my time in Congress, I have had the distinct honor of meeting 
Denise and hearing stories about the annual event.
  Since her first competition in 1978, Denise has collected 142 medals, 
a remarkable achievement and reflection of her hard work and talent.
  She also serves as a global messenger for Potter County. This 
position allows athletes to share their personal stories and highlight 
the positive impact the Special Olympics has on participants.
  Mr. Speaker, Denise is a phenomenal representative for the Special 
Olympics and a mentor to athletes across Pennsylvania. I congratulate 
Denise on this incredible milestone.

                          ____________________
```
