# RECOGNIZING PETER DeMARCO

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING PETER DeMARCO

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my good friend 
Peter DeMarco for his 25 years of service to the constituents of Orange 
County.
  Most recently, Peter served in the Orange County Executive Office, 
where he was the county's top, principal advocate at the California 
Legislature and U.S. Congress.
  Peter worked for Orange County residents to support funding for 
public safety, healthcare, housing, and veteran services.
  Now, Peter begins a new chapter of public service with Mesa Water 
District.
  On behalf of the people of California's 46th Congressional District 
and the people of Orange County, I congratulate Peter on his new 
position and wish him continued success in the future. I thank Peter.

                          ____________________
```
