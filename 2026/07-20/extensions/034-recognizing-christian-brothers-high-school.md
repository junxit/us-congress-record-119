# RECOGNIZING CHRISTIAN BROTHERS HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Matsui, Doris O. (M001163) [D-CA]

```
               RECOGNIZING CHRISTIAN BROTHERS HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DORIS O. MATSUI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Ms. MATSUI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Christian Brothers 
High School as it celebrates 150 years of distinguished service and 
education in the Sacramento region.
  Since its founding in 1876, Christian Brothers High School has served 
as one of Sacramento's most respected institutions, dedicated to the 
Lasallian tradition of faith, community, and service. For a century and 
a half, Christian Brothers has remained committed to providing a 
rigorous academic foundation while fostering integrity, leadership, and 
compassion in its students. From its original home downtown to its 
current campus in Oak Park, the school has continually adapted to the 
needs of its neighbors while preserving its core mission: ``Enter to 
Learn, Leave to Serve.''
  Over the course of its 150-year history, Christian Brothers has 
expanded its academic programs, strengthened its commitment to 
inclusive education, and earned numerous national honors. The school's 
faculty has consistently upheld the values of St. John Baptist De La 
Salle, encouraging students to pursue curiosity, service, and personal 
growth. Its celebrated arts programs, competitive athletics, and wide 
variety of student-led clubs and initiatives reflect a campus culture 
rich in leadership and fellowship.
  This milestone is a testament to the thousands of lives affected by 
Lasallian education, the wisdom and insight of its educators, and the 
core principles instilled across generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask all my colleagues to join me in honoring Christian 
Brothers High School's 150 years of education and service to the 
Sacramento community.
```
