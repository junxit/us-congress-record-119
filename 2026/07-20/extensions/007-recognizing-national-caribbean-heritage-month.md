# RECOGNIZING NATIONAL CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
             RECOGNIZING NATIONAL CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Caribbean-Americans to South Florida and the Nation 
through the arts and humanities, business, science and engineering, 
activism, and military leadership as we celebrate the 8th anniversary 
of National Caribbean-American Heritage Month. I include in the Record 
the following proclamation in honor of National Caribbean Heritage 
Month:
       Whereas, the President of the United States of America, 
     Barack Obama, has issued the annual proclamation in 2013 
     recognizing June as National Caribbean-American Heritage 
     Month;
       Whereas, National Caribbean-American Heritage Month 
     recognizes the significance

[[Page E705]]

     of Caribbean people in U.S. history, and the influence of 
     Caribbean people and their descendants on American culture;
       Whereas, South Florida is home to many Caribbean-Americans 
     who make up a large percent of the population;
       Whereas, Caribbean-Americans have played a major and 
     notable role in the growth and development of South Florida, 
     from holding public office, prominent positions in education 
     and religion, to owning successful businesses and being vocal 
     community activists; and now, therefore, be it resolved that 
     I, Frederica S. Wilson, a member of the United States House 
     of Representatives, representing the 24th Congressional 
     District of Florida, and who also has Caribbean roots, am 
     proud to recognize the contributions of Caribbean-Americans 
     to South Florida and to our country. I encourage all 
     residents to celebrate the rich culture of the Caribbean 
     during National Caribbean-American Heritage Month.

                          ____________________
```
