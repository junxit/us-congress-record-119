      to honor Lieutenant General Chris Mohan as he concludes an 
 extraordinary 40-year career of service to our Nation and retires as 
  the 22nd Commanding General of the United States Army Materiel Command.
Lieutenant General Mohan has dedicated four decades to strengthening 
our Nation's military readiness and supporting the men and women who 
serve. Throughout his distinguished career, he has led soldiers and 
civilians at every level, including multiple command assignments across 
the United States and overseas. His leadership has been instrumental in 
sustaining our Armed Forces during Operations Desert Shield, Desert 
Storm, Enduring Freedom, and Iraqi Freedom.
  As Commanding General of Army Materiel Command, Lieutenant General 
Mohan has overseen one of the Army's largest organizations, ensuring 
our servicemembers have the equipment, resources, and support they need 
to accomplish their missions around the world. His previous leadership 
at the Army Sustainment Command at the Rock Island Arsenal further 
strengthened our region's proud tradition of supporting America's 
military.
  Mr. Speaker, Lieutenant General Mohan's career reflects the highest 
ideals of service, leadership, and commitment to our country. I join 
his family, colleagues, and grateful Nation in congratulating him on 
his well-earned retirement and thanking him for his decades of selfless 
service to the United States.
