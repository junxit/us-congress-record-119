# HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO THALES CARLTON ROBBINS

```
         HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO THALES CARLTON ROBBINS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to a 
remarkable hero from Houston County, Tennessee, who answered the call 
of duty during a critical chapter in our Nation's history. As the years 
pass, the profound debt we owe to our Korean War veterans only grows.
  Of the 6.8 million Americans who served during the Korean War era, 
fewer than 300,000 remain with us today. This generation, now largely 
in their nineties, represents a rapidly dwindling connection to a 
pivotal struggle for global freedom. It is our sacred obligation to 
ensure their stories are told and their sacrifices are never forgotten.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
extraordinary service, sacrifice, and lifelong dedication of Thales 
Carlton Robbins. He represents the very best of Tennessee's Volunteer 
Spirit and the enduring resilience of the American servicemember.
  Born and raised in Houston County, Mr. Thales Carlton Robbins, known 
warmly throughout his community as ``Carlton,'' graduated from Erin 
High School in 1948 before answering the call to serve. He enlisted in 
the United States Navy on December 27, 1950, and honorably served until 
October 20, 1954, as a U.S. Navy Hospital Corpsman (HM-8404-77).
  During the Korean War, Mr. Robbins served with the 7th Marine 
Regiment in 1952 and the 1st Marine Division in 1953. Navy Corpsmen 
assigned to Marine combat units earned a legendary reputation for 
courage, repeatedly risking their own lives to render lifesaving 
medical care to wounded Marines under direct enemy fire. Mr. Robbins 
upheld that proud tradition through his service in some of the most 
demanding combat conditions of the Korean War.
  For his faithful and courageous service, Mr. Robbins received the 
Good Conduct Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Korean Service 
Medal with two bronze service stars, United Nations Service Medal, 
Korean Presidential Unit Citation, and the Fleet Marine Force Insignia. 
His distinguished service brought great credit upon himself, the United 
States Navy, and the people of Houston County. Mr. Robbins currently 
resides in the Clarksville-Montgomery County area.
  Please join me in offering our deepest gratitude for his faithful 
service and proudly preserve their legacies in the United States 
Congress.
```
