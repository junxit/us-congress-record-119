# PAYING TRIBUTE TO TEQUILA JANAE TEKEYSHA FORSHEE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
            PAYING TRIBUTE TO TEQUILA JANAE TEKEYSHA FORSHEE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor the late 
Tequila Janae Tekeysha Forshee. I include in the Record the following 
proclamation in her honor:

       Whereas, Tequila was a vibrant, fun loving and beautiful 
     12-year-old girl who loved to help others and dreamed of one 
     day becoming a chef and a detective;
       Whereas, Tequila enjoyed mathematics and excelled in her 
     studies at Brentwood Elementary School and looked forward to 
     attending Miami Carol City Middle School;
       Whereas, Tequila's hard work as a student earned her a 
     place on the Assistant Principal's Honor Roll and numerous 
     awards such as the Language Arts Achievement Award, and the 
     Mathematics Achievement Award;
       Whereas, Tequila was a member of the dance praise team at 
     Gospel of Tabernacle and a water girl at Brentwood Park; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     recognize and pay tribute to Tequila Janae Tekeysha Forshee, 
     a precious angel with a big heart and who was deeply loved by 
     her family, friends and classmates.

                          ____________________
```
