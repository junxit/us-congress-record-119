# HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO JOE L. THOMAS, SR.

```
           HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO JOE L. THOMAS, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to a 
remarkable hero from Houston County, Tennessee, who answered the call 
of duty during a critical period in our Nation's history. As the years 
pass, the profound debt we owe to our Korean War veterans only grows.
  Of the 6.8 million Americans who served during the Korean War era, 
fewer than 300,000 remain with us today. This generation, now largely 
in their nineties, represents a rapidly dwindling connection to a 
pivotal struggle for global freedom. It is our sacred obligation to 
ensure their stories are told and their sacrifices are never forgotten.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
extraordinary service, sacrifice, and lifelong dedication of Joe L. 
Thomas, Sr. He represents the very best of Tennessee's Volunteer Spirit 
and the enduring resilience of the American servicemember.
  Mr. Joe L. Thomas, Sr., served honorably in the United States Air 
Force from October 29, 1954, through September 10, 1957. During his 
military service, he was assigned to the 3914th Air Base Squadron. 
Local historical accounts and newspaper records from 1954 and 1955 
proudly document his service during the pivotal final years of the 
Korean War era.
  Like so many members of his generation, Mr. Thomas quietly went 
wherever his country needed him, performing his duties with 
professionalism, skill, and quiet resolve. His willingness to serve 
during a critical period in our Nation's history helped preserve the 
peace secured through the sacrifices of those who fought in Korea. We 
honor his service today, remembering that the freedoms we enjoy were 
secured by men like Mr. Thomas who answered their country's call 
without hesitation.
  Please join me in offering our deepest gratitude for his faithful 
service and proudly preserve their legacies in the United States 
Congress.

                          ____________________
```
