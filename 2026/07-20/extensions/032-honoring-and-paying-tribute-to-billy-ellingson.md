# HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO BILLY ELLINGSON

```
             HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO BILLY ELLINGSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to a 
remarkable hero from Houston County, Tennessee, who answered the call 
of duty during a critical chapter in our Nation's history. As the years 
pass, the profound debt we owe to our Korean War veterans only grows.
  Of the 6.8 million Americans who served during the Korean War era, 
fewer than 300,000 remain with us today. This generation, now largely 
in their nineties, represents a rapidly dwindling connection to a 
pivotal struggle for global freedom. It is our sacred obligation to 
ensure their stories are told and their sacrifices are never forgotten.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
extraordinary service, sacrifice, and lifelong dedication of Billy 
Ellingson. He represents the very best of Tennessee's Volunteer Spirit 
and the enduring resilience of the American servicemember.
  Mr. Billy Ellingson served with pride in the United States Navy from 
1954 to 1962, including four years of dedicated active duty during the 
Korean War era. Beyond his years in uniform, Mr. Ellingson has spent a 
lifetime giving back to his fellow veterans and his local community. 
For more than twenty years, he faithfully served as a member of the 
American Legion Honor Guard, ensuring that fallen comrades received the 
final, dignified honors they so richly deserved. As he approaches his 
90th birthday this July, Mr. Ellingson continues to support and uplift 
the Houston County community, embodying a lifetime of selfless service.
  Please join me in offering our deepest gratitude for his faithful 
service and proudly preserve their legacies in the United States 
Congress.

                          ____________________
```
