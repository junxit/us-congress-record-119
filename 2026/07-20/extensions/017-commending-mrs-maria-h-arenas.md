# COMMENDING MRS. MARIA H. ARENAS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                    COMMENDING MRS. MARIA H. ARENAS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor the late 
Mrs. Maria H. Arenas, beloved educator, wife and mother who passed away 
on June 21, 2013. I include in the Record the following proclamation in 
her honor:

       Whereas, Mrs. Arenas was an enthusiastic educator and 
     administrator in the Miami-Dade County Public School system 
     for over thirty years until her retirement in summer of 2006;
       Whereas, Mrs. Arenas was a lifelong devotee to the pursuit 
     of knowledge, earning her high school degree from Miami 
     Northwestern High School in 1965, a Bachelor of Science 
     degree in Speech and Theater Arts from Florida Agricultural 
     and Mechanical University, a Master of Science degree in 
     Educational Curriculum, and had, at the time of her passing, 
     completed all of the required coursework toward earning a 
     Doctoral degree in Education;
       Whereas, Mrs. Arenas travelled extensively around the world 
     and allowed others to share her experiences through social 
     media websites;
       Whereas, Mrs. Arenas was a vibrant member of the Red 
     Hatters and numerous other civic and social organizations; 
     and now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. 
     Wilson, a member of the United States House of 
     Representatives, representing the 24th Congressional District 
     of Florida, commend Mrs. Maria H. Arenas for her years of 
     service and dedication to Miami-Dade County Public Schools 
     her community, and to her family.

                          ____________________
```
