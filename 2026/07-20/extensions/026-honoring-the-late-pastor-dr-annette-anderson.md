# HONORING THE LATE PASTOR, DR. ANNETTE ANDERSON

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
             HONORING THE LATE PASTOR, DR. ANNETTE ANDERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor the late 
Pastor Dr. Annette Anderson, a true servant of God, a beloved and 
respected spiritual leader, and a dedicated social activist in Miami 
Gardens, Florida.
  Pastor Annette Anderson devoted her life to serving God and spreading 
His Word. She lived her life based on the teachings and lessons in the 
Bible, which also served as a guide to raising her family and working 
to improve our community and quality of life.
  Pastor Anderson never met a stranger, nor tired of helping those in 
need. Her unwavering faith in God, love for all people and words of 
wisdom are credited with bringing comfort to the suffering at their 
darkest hour and restoring their faith.
  As pastor and teacher of Light of Life Ministries, a weekly prayer 
and Bible study ministry, and assistant pastor of Breakthrough 
Deliverance and Healing Prayer Ministries, Incorporated, Pastor 
Anderson touched the lives of countless people. She did not believe in 
dwelling on the past and past circumstances but using life's struggles 
as steppingstones to strengthen one's faith in Jesus.
  Pastor Anderson earned a bachelor's degree from Jacksonville 
Theological Seminary; a Master of Theology Degree and Posthumous 
Doctoral Degree in Theology from Practical Christianity Institute of 
Evangelism (PCIE), School of Bible Theology.
  Please join me in honoring the late Pastor Dr. Annette Anderson, for 
her commitment, dedication and service to God, her congregation, her 
family and friends and our community.
```
