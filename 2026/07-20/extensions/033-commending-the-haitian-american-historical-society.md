# COMMENDING THE HAITIAN AMERICAN HISTORICAL SOCIETY

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
           COMMENDING THE HAITIAN AMERICAN HISTORICAL SOCIETY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize the grand reopening 
of the Haitian Historical Museum and Archives in North Miami, Florida 
and include in the Record a proclamation on commending the efforts of 
Haitian American Historical Society (HAHS):

       Whereas, the Haitian Historical Museum and Archives--
     through artifact, paintings, photography and performances--
     tells the story of Haiti's history and rich culture;
       Whereas, the Haitian Historical Museum and Archives 
     documents and showcases the contributions of Haitians and 
     Haitian descendants to South Florida, the United States and 
     other parts of the world;
       Whereas, the Haitian Historical Museum and Archives serve 
     to educate future generations of Haitian Americans about 
     their heritage and build a connection to Haiti;
       Whereas, the Haitian Historical Museum and Archives serve 
     to foster understanding, tolerance and cooperation between 
     South Florida's diverse communities; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 25th Congressional District of Florida, 
     commend the Haitian American Historical Society for 
     preserving Haitian history and fostering a resurgence of 
     interest in Haitian culture among South Floridians.
```
