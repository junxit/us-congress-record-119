# HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO CHARLES BROWN POTTER

```
          HONORING AND PAYING TRIBUTE TO CHARLES BROWN POTTER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 20, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to a 
remarkable hero from Houston County, Tennessee, who answered the call 
of duty during a critical chapter in our Nation's history. As the years 
pass, the profound debt we owe to our Korean War veterans only grows.
  Of the 6.8 million Americans who served during the Korean War era, 
fewer than 300,000 remain with us today. This generation, now largely 
in their nineties, represents a rapidly dwindling connection to a 
pivotal struggle for global freedom. It is our sacred obligation to 
ensure their stories are told and their sacrifices are never forgotten.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
extraordinary service, sacrifice, and lifelong dedication of Charles 
Brown Potter. He represents the very best of Tennessee's Volunteer 
Spirit and the enduring resilience of the American servicemember.
  Mr. Charles Brown Potter is a distinguished United States Air Force 
veteran who dedicated his military service to our Nation during the 
Korean War era. Following his honorable service, Mr. Potter established 
roots in Tennessee, where his presence as a resident of Houston County 
has strengthened the local network of military families and contributed 
to the proud tradition of veteran leadership and historic preservation 
within the community.
  His exemplary behavior, efficiency, and fidelity during active duty 
earned him the Good Conduct Medal. His specialized training and unique 
operational capabilities are further reflected in his receipt of the 
Special Forces Badge. Mr. Potter's life of service and continued civic 
dedication stand as a testament to the enduring legacy of America's 
veterans, and Houston County remains deeply enriched by his leadership.
  Please join me in offering our deepest gratitude for his faithful 
service and proudly preserve their legacies in the United States 
Congress.

                          ____________________
```
