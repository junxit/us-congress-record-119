# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Sacred Father, open the hearts of our lawmakers to the power of Your 
providence. Lord, provide them with a greater appreciation of Your 
wisdom, power, and love. Remind them of Your plans to keep them from 
stumbling or slipping, as You prepare them for their accountability to 
You. Give them strength for their challenging pilgrimage, and provide 
them with a faith that will not shrink, though pressed by many a foe. 
Empower them with the moral and spiritual stamina to walk in the paths 
of integrity, courage, and joy.
  And, Lord, bless all who labor to bring Your peace on Earth.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
