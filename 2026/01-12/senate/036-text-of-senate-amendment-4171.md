# Text of Senate Amendment 4171

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
  SA 4171. Mrs. BLACKBURN submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the bill H.R. 6938, making consolidated 
appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. _____. MOTOR VEHICLES.

       Section 2119 of title 18, United States Code, is amended--
       (1) in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking ``, 
     with the intent to cause death or serious bodily harm'' and 
     inserting ``knowingly''; and
       (2) in paragraph (3), by striking ``if death results,'' and 
     inserting ``if the motor vehicle is taken with the intent to 
     cause death or serious bodily harm, and death results,''.
                                 ______
```
