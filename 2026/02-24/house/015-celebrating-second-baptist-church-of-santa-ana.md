# CELEBRATING SECOND BAPTIST CHURCH OF SANTA ANA

**Speaking:** Min, Dave (M001241) [D-CA]

```
             CELEBRATING SECOND BAPTIST CHURCH OF SANTA ANA

  (Mr. MIN of California was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. MIN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 103rd 
anniversary of Second Baptist Church of Santa Ana, the largest 
predominantly Black church in Orange County.
  Under the leadership of Senior Pastor Ivan S. Pitts, Second Baptist 
Church has become a cornerstone of our faith community. With 
congregations in both Irvine and Santa Ana, Second Baptist serves as a 
place of worship where people can find care, dignity, and belonging.
  Through a variety of activities, including health fairs, vaccination 
clinics, youth programs, cultural events, and other civic engagement, 
the church has met people where they are and served as a vital cultural 
hub for our region.
  In times of crisis, Second Baptist Church has led with action and led 
with works. From delivering groceries to seniors and single-mother 
households during the recent government shutdown to dispatching 
emergency response teams to support flood and fire victims, their 
commitment to service never wavers.
  Madam Speaker, Second Baptist Church represents the very best of 
Orange County, and I am so proud to honor them today.
```
