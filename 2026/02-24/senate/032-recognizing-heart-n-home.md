# RECOGNIZING HEART `N HOME

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING HEART `N HOME

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Heart `n 
Home of Pocahontas County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Founded in 1992, Heart `n Home began with Julie Wurr's simple desire 
to create something beautiful that brought joy, comfort, and 
inspiration to others. After participating in 4 years of craft shows, 
this hobby soon grew into something much larger. Thanks to the 
enthusiastic encouragement of customers and family, Julie and her 
husband Bill transformed their garage into a small storefront, marking 
the official start of Heart `n Home's journey as a local floral and 
gift destination.
  In the early 2000s, Heart `n Home made a pivotal move to downtown 
Laurens, IA, where the shop continues to thrive today. The small 
business has evolved over the years and grown to include entertaining 
and home decor, silk and dried arrangements, gourmet foods, and an 
ever-changing selection of unique gifts. Heart `n Home's reputation 
bloomed as both a floral studio and a welcoming boutique filled with 
creativity and warmth.
  Most recently, Heart `n Home brought fresh floral arrangements into 
its lineup, further elevating the services offered. Julie specializes 
in couture arrangements, which are carefully crafted pieces that 
reflect the individual personality and story of the person receiving 
them.
  Over 33 years, Heart `n Home has expanded from a garage-based floral 
shop into a distinctive retail experience defined by artistic 
expression, innovation, and personal service. With a dedicated team of 
five employees, the store continues to adapt to changing trends and 
customer needs while remaining deeply rooted in the values on which it 
was built.
  Heart `n Home is also an active part of the local community, 
participating in organizations such as Pocahontas County Economic 
Development and Iowa State University Extension. In 2018, Heart `n Home 
was named the Pocahontas County Business of the Year. Julie is also the 
president of the Laurens Public Library Foundation and is a part of the 
Laurens Chamber of Commerce.
  Julie Wurr's vision has shaped Heart `n Home into a thriving small 
business that not only delivers flowers and gifts, but has created 
meaningful, memorable experiences. Today, Heart `n Home stands as a 
shining example of how creativity, dedication, and heartfelt service 
can transform a small, homegrown idea into a longstanding community 
treasure.
  It is my honor to recognize Julie Wurr and the entire Heart `n Home 
team for their outstanding work and dedication to their community. I 
look forward to their continued success and wish them the very best in 
the years ahead.●

                          ____________________
```
