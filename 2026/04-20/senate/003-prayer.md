# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Sovereign God, thank You that Your mercies endure forever. Show us 
Your ways and teach us Your paths as You lead us with Your truth. 
Today, set the hearts of our lawmakers on Heaven's way. In all of their 
actions, may they seek Your celestial approval. Remind them that You 
are the only constituent they absolutely must please. May our Senators 
stand on Your promises, lean on Your grace, and trust in Your guidance. 
Lord, thank You for Your mercy. You lift the lowly, satisfy the 
thirsty, and fill the hungry with good things. And Lord, continue to 
comfort Senator Warner and his wife Lisa as they grieve the loss of 
their daughter Madison.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
