# CELEBRATING PASTORS TODD AND KELLY HUDNALL 20 YEARS OF MINISTRY

**Speaking:** Crank, Jeff (C001137) [R-CO]

```
    CELEBRATING PASTORS TODD AND KELLY HUDNALL 20 YEARS OF MINISTRY

  (Mr. CRANK asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CRANK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 20 years of 
ministry of Pastors Todd and Kelly Hudnall of Colorado Springs Radiant 
Church. Pastor Todd's compelling expository preaching of the Bible has 
strengthened Radiant Church as well as the broader Pikes Peak region.
  The Hudnalls have raised their children in our community, and we 
admire their testimony of a Christ-honoring home. I thank the Hudnalls 
for courageously applying Bible teaching to

[[Page H908]]

every sphere of life, including public issues of concern.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you join me in congratulating Pastors Todd 
and Kelly Hudnall for their 20 years of ministry, and here is to the 
next 20.

                          ____________________
```
