# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3674

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S. 3674

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3674

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3674) to expand and clarify the grounds for 
     civil denaturalization proceedings for individuals who have 
     defrauded a governmental program, joined a terrorist 
     organization, or committed certain criminal offenses.


[[Page S263]]


  

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule 
XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.
```
