```
            PREGNANT STUDENTS' RIGHTS ACT--Motion to Proceed

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 300, 
S. 3627.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to Calendar No. 300, S. 3627, a bill to

  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the

     proceed to Calendar No. 300, S. 3627, a bill to require 
     institutions of higher education to disseminate information 
     on the rights of, and accommodations and resources for, 
     pregnant students, and for other purposes.
```
