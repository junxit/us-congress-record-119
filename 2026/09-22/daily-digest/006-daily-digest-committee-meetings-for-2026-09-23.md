```
                   COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR WEDNESDAY,

                           SEPTEMBER 23, 2026

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Armed Services: to receive a closed briefing on an 
update on the United States Central Command area of operations, 9:30 
a.m., SVC-217.
  Subcommittee on Cybersecurity, to receive a closed briefing on 
cyber operations and readiness for the second and third quarters of 
Fiscal Year 2026 as well as an update on election security, 2:30 
p.m., SVC-217.
  Committee on Environment and Public Works: to hold hearings to 
examine updating America's nuclear waste management policy, 10 a.m., 
SD-406.
  Committee on Foreign Relations: to hold closed hearings to examine 
combating and deterring wrongful detentions, focusing on an update 
on U.S. efforts, 10 a.m., SVC-217.
  Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: business 
meeting to consider the request to direct Senate Legal Counsel to 
file an application seeking immunity, 10 a.m., SD-342.
  Committee on Indian Affairs: business meeting to consider S. 5354, 
to reauthorize the Native American Housing Assistance and Self-
Determination Act of 1996; to be immediately followed by hearings to 
examine S. 1750, to reaffirm the applicability of the Indian 
Reorganization Act to the Poarch Band of Creek Indians, S. 3859, to 
amend the Catawba Indian Tribe of South Carolina Land Claims 
Settlement Act of 1993 with respect to future membership in the 
Catawba Indian Nation, S. 4053, to take certain land in the State of 
California into trust for the benefit of the Pechanga Band of 
Indians, and H.R. 226, to take certain Federal lands in Tennessee 
into trust for the benefit of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians, 
2:30 p.m., SD-628.
  Committee on the Judiciary: to hold hearings to examine standing 
up for women in sports, focusing on ensuring opportunity, fairness 
and safety for female athletes, 10:15 a.m., SD-G50.
  Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism, to hold hearings to 
examine flock's nationwide AI surveillance network, 2:30 p.m., SD-
562.
  Committee on Veterans' Affairs: to hold hearings to examine the 
nominations of Maria Lopez, of Florida, to be Chairman of the Board 
of Veterans' Appeals, Department of Veterans Affairs, and Robert R. 
Fleck, of Pennsylvania, David M. Jones, of Virginia, Daniel F. 
Rendleman, of Illinois, and James Lewis Quinn, of Virginia, each to 
be a Judge of the United States Court of Appeals for Veterans 
Claims, 4 p.m., SR-418.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: to receive a closed briefing on 
certain intelligence matters, 3 p.m., SH-219.
  Special Committee on Aging: to hold hearings to examine leveling 
the playing field, focusing on restoring America's edge in generic 
drug manufacturing, 3:30 p.m., SH-216.


                                 House

  No hearings are scheduled.
```
