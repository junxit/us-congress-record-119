```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

            Week of September 23 through September 25, 2026

                             Senate Chamber

  On Wednesday, Senate will continue consideration of Thune (for Cruz) 
Amendment No. 6776, to S. 4668, Protect College Sports Act, post-
cloture.

Angela Veronica Colmenero, of Texas, to be United States District Judge 
for the Southern District of Texas.

legislative and executive business.
```
