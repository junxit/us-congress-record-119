# Iran (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                  Iran

  Mr. President, on Iran and the war, only Trump could try to present 
himself as a peacemaker and then threaten to ``annihilate'' an entire 
country in the same breath, but that is just what he did at the U.N. 
this morning.
  Trump said he had to make a ``big decision'' either to cut a deal 
with Iran or to ``drive them into hell with no chance of survival.'' 
What a sickening statement from a wannabe Nobel laureate for peace. He 
thinks he is being smart by threatening them. His threats over the last 
several months haven't worked.
  Donald, nothing you have done has worked; end the damn war.
  And hey, Donald, you made another big decision 7 months ago; you 
decided to take our country to war without a second thought. Trump had 
no idea what he was doing when he got our country into this mess. Seven 
months later, he has no clue how to get us out, even though everyone 
told him the first thing to do is block the Strait of Hormuz, secure 
the Strait of Hormuz.
  He didn't do it. He doesn't listen. Whatever pops into his own head 
seems to be what America does, and it almost always works out very 
badly for America and the American people. Trump needs to reckon with 
the cost of his catastrophic blunder in Iran.
  Over the weekend, we learned that the Pentagon has spent at least $45 
billion on the war. Trump's war led diesel prices to hit another record 
high today at $6.52 a gallon. That means higher prices on everything 
that is transported by truck. Worst of all, Trump continues to try to 
cover up the human costs of this failed war.
  Anonymous Pentagon officials just revealed that the Trump 
administration has refused to disclose the deaths of at least 4 
servicemembers bringing the sad total up to 22 at least.
  The administration's refusal to recognize these fallen servicemen 
ought to make everybody's skin crawl. I hope their families know how 
much we appreciate their loved ones' service and sacrifice. This week, 
Senate Democrats will force another vote on our War Powers Resolution 
to end this war and get our troops out of harm's way.
  Trump, stop bragging about the fake number of wars you have 
supposedly ended and end the real one right in front of you. Every 
Republican who votes against ending the war this week is choosing to 
pander to the President instead of protecting our troops and our 
country.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Illinois.
```
