# NISSAN AND TOYOTA

**Speaking:** Wicker, Roger F. (W000437) [R-MS]

```
                           NISSAN AND TOYOTA

● Mr. WICKER. Mr. President, I commend Nissan's and Toyota's 
investments in Mississippi. These efforts are part of a broader pattern 
of Japanese-brand automakers' expansion into the American Southeast to 
build vehicles closer to their customers and to create

[[Page S4855]]

good-paying American jobs. As of the end of 2025, Nissan's Canton 
assembly plant and Toyota's Blue Springs facility together employ over 
5,600 workers and represent more than $5.6 billion in capital 
investment. This has catalyzed the growth of suppliers and logistics 
operations that provide additional employment across our State.
  The Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association (JAMA)'s historical 
assessment shows that Japanese-brand automakers have expanded from 
early plants in the Midwest to a network of more than 2 dozen 
manufacturing facilities and dozens of R&D and design centers across 
the country, with the Southeast playing an increasingly important role. 
Mississippi's plants are integral to that network, supplying vehicles 
and components that feed national and international markets.
  These companies have also invested in the future of Mississippi's 
workforce. Nissan and Toyota participate in STEM initiatives, support 
technical training, and offer earn-and-learn programs and scholarships 
that help Mississippians gain skills in automotive manufacturing, 
robotics, and maintenance. Research published by JAMA underscores how 
this kind of effort helps transform education and build a talent 
pipeline for the future. Community engagement, including charitable 
contributions, volunteerism, and support for local organizations, 
further demonstrates their commitment to Mississippi. As we mark JAMA 
USA's 50th anniversary, the story in Mississippi is instructive. It 
reminds us that steadily increasing, long-term investment from trusted 
allies like Japan is about more than factories and numbers. Investment 
supports workers, strengthens communities, and reinforces a U.S.-Japan 
relationship that is essential to both of our countries' economic and 
strategic success.●
```
