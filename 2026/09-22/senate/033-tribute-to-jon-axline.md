# TRIBUTE TO JON AXLINE

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JON AXLINE

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, today I have the honor to recognize 
Jon Axline as Montanan of the Month.
  Born and raised in Billings, MT, Jon is a fourth-generation Montanan. 
As a youth, he watched John F. Kennedy's Presidential motorcade from 
his grandparent's front yard in Billings, which was a profound moment 
that inspired his interest in history. Jon attended Montana State 
University where he earned his bachelor's and master's degrees in 
American history.
  His passion for history extended to several experiences such as 
working for the Museum of the Rockies, the Montana State Prison Museum, 
the Bear Paw Development Corporation, and the Powell County Museum and 
Arts Foundation. Jon finally settled into a historian position for the 
Montana Department of Transportation in 1990.
  Jon is one of the superstars of the Montana history profession. Jon's 
work covers hundreds of years of Montana's legacy, from the history of 
unions in Butte, to the Smith Mine disaster. Now retired after a 35-
year long career, Jon is also remembered for his excellent writing on 
transportation history in the State.
  I am thrilled to recognize Jon for his outstanding work in portraying 
Montana history for the past three decades. Jon's storytelling will 
continue to help educate people about our great State and bring 
communities together. It is my distinct honor to recognize Jon Axline 
as Montanan of the Month.●

                          ____________________
```
