# REVERSIONARY INTEREST CONVEYANCE ACT

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** H.R. 952

```
                  REVERSIONARY INTEREST CONVEYANCE ACT

  Ms. LUMMIS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Energy and Natural Resources be discharged from further 
consideration of H.R. 952 and the Senate proceed to its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

[[Page S4883]]

  The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 952) to convey the reversionary interest of 
     the United States in certain land in Sacramento, California.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Ms. LUMMIS. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read 
a third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 952) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
