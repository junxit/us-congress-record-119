# Text of Senate Amendment 6803

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 6803. Mr. BOOKER (for himself, Mr. Murphy, Ms. Blunt Rochester, 
Ms. Alsobrooks, and Mr. Warnock) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Cruz (for 
himself, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, and Mr. Coons)) to the bill S. 
4668, to protect the name, image, and likeness rights of, and provide 
protections for, student athletes and to promote fair competition among 
intercollegiate athletics, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

        Strike section 307 and insert the following:

     SEC. 307. APPROPRIATION.

       There is appropriated to the Assistant Secretary to carry 
     out this title for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2032, 
     out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, 
     $180,000,000, to remain available until expended.
                                 ______
```
