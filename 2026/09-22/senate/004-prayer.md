# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, in whom we live and move and have our being, we come 
before You with grateful hearts. We ask You for Your blessing and 
guidance upon our Senators as they seek to serve this Nation and world.
  Lord, we lift our hearts to You today, for You alone reign over all 
creation. You are our source of strength, wisdom, and hope, and You 
sustain us through both good times and difficult seasons.
  Grant our Senators wisdom to make thoughtful decisions, courage to 
face difficulties, and humility to listen to the voices and concerns of 
those they represent. Give us all a deep sense of fairness, compassion, 
and responsibility in all we do.
  And, Lord, we thank You for the life and legacy of former Senator Jon 
Kyl.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
