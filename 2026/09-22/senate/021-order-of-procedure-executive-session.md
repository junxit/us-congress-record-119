# Order of Procedure (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

```
                           Order of Procedure

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, if cloture is invoked on Executive Calendar 
No. 828, all postcloture time be expired and the Senate vote on 
confirmation of the nomination at a time to be determined by the 
majority leader, in consultation with the Democrat leader, no later 
than Friday, September 25; further, that the mandatory quorum call with 
respect to the cloture motion on the substitute amendment No. 6776 be 
waived; and if cloture is invoked, that all time during recess, 
adjournment, morning business, and leader remarks count postcloture on 
the amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there an objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
