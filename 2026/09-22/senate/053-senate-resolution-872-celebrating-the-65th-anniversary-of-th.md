# SENATE RESOLUTION 872--CELEBRATING THE 65TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE PEACE CORPS, AND COMMENDING ITS VOLUNTEERS AND CONTRIBUTIONS TO GLOBAL PEACE, FRIENDSHIP, AND DEVELOPMENT

**Measures:** S.Res. 872

```
    SENATE RESOLUTION 872--CELEBRATING THE 65TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE 
  ESTABLISHMENT OF THE PEACE CORPS, AND COMMENDING ITS VOLUNTEERS AND 
       CONTRIBUTIONS TO GLOBAL PEACE, FRIENDSHIP, AND DEVELOPMENT

  Mr. BOOKER (for himself and Mr. Tillis) submitted the following 
resolution; which was referred to the Committee on Foreign Relations:

                              S. Res. 872

       Whereas on March 1, 1961, President John F. Kennedy issued 
     his historic call to service and established the Peace Corps, 
     launching a new era of American volunteerism abroad;
       Whereas over the past 65 years, nearly 250,000 Americans 
     have served as Peace Corps volunteers in 144 countries, 
     fostering people-to-people diplomacy in areas such as 
     education, health, agriculture, environment, youth in 
     development, and community economic development;
       Whereas the Peace Corps continues to exemplify ``the 
     toughest job you'll ever love'', building sustainable 
     partnerships and strengthening communities around the world;
       Whereas the Peace Corps has enhanced global perceptions of 
     the United States by demonstrating American values through 
     service, humility, cultural exchange, and partnership rather 
     than through political or military means;
       Whereas returned Peace Corps volunteers bring home 
     invaluable linguistic, cultural, and technical skills that 
     strengthen the United States workforce, expand America's 
     global competency, and enhance the Nation's diplomatic and 
     economic competitiveness;
       Whereas the Peace Corps has contributed to United States 
     national security by strengthening international stability, 
     promoting economic growth, and addressing root causes of 
     conflict through education, empowerment, and community 
     development;
       Whereas Peace Corps volunteers have exemplified American 
     leadership through volunteerism, generosity, and service to 
     others; and
       Whereas Peace Corps service has inspired generations of 
     Americans to pursue careers in public service, education, 
     economic development, diplomacy, and health, creating a 
     lasting pipeline of globally minded civic leaders; Now, 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) celebrates the 65th anniversary of the founding of the 
     Peace Corps;
       (2) honors the nearly 250,000 individuals who have served 
     as Peace Corps volunteers for their dedication to peace, 
     friendship, and community-driven development; and
       (3) commends the Peace Corps' enduring mission to put 
     purpose, passion, and skills to work for the United States 
     and the world.
```
