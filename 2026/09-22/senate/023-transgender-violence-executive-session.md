  Mr. TUBERVILLE. Mr. President, I come to the floor today to talk 
about the rise in violence coming from people whom the left refers to 
as ``transgender.''
  I have come to the floor many times in the last 6 years to talk about

conversation about the serious gender confusion-mental illness that is 
taking over this country.

``transgender'' because it implies that it is possible for a person to

Earth, there have been two genders--male and female. So I will call 
these people what they are--gender-confused. Unfortunately, this 
gender-confused mental illness is on the rise in our society today, and 
it is very, very concerning.
  You know, for decades, the radical left--Democrat socialists--have 
ignored science and brainwashed our young people into believing that 
you can change your gender by changing your clothes; by getting on some 
hormone-altering medications; or, God forbid, by undergoing some crazy 
surgery.
