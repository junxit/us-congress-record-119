# China (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                 China

  Mr. President, on the Xi Jinping visit, I urge Trump not to sell out 
America for a headline when he meets with Xi Jinping tomorrow. Trump 
keeps talking a big game about winning the AI race against China, but 
he is forfeiting our lead every day by refusing to block the sale of 
our most advanced chips and manufacturing equipment to China. Just 
today, Trump was bragging to the U.N. about how America's technology is 
the best in the world.
  Well, then don't give that tech to the Chinese Communist Party. 
Prohibiting China from accessing these chips and equipment they 
desperately want is our best tool to keep China's AI ambitions at bay, 
and Trump wants to just hand it over to them. It doesn't make any 
sense.
  Trump needs to make clear to Xi that our cutting-edge AI technology 
is not up for negotiation; it is off the table, period--no ands, no 
ifs, no buts. Trump can block these sales before Xi's plane even hits 
the tarmac tomorrow.
  We have put forward bipartisan bills to ban the sale of these 
advanced chips to China, cut off access to chipmaking equipment, and 
prevent smuggling of this technology. Just get on board, Donald. Don't 
stand in the way.
  Sadly, we all know what Trump's goal is with summits like this. He is 
chasing a headline, chasing a photo op, not a victory for the American 
people.
  In almost every one of these summits when he chases a headline, 
Americans lose.
```
