# RECOGNIZING BETHESDA-CHEVY CHASE HIGH SCHOOL'S CENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
       RECOGNIZING BETHESDA-CHEVY CHASE HIGH SCHOOL'S CENTENNIAL

● Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize 
Bethesda-Chevy Chase High School as it celebrates its 100th anniversary 
and a century of academic excellence, innovation, and service to its 
students and community.
  For 100 years, Bethesda-Chevy Chase High School has stood as a beacon 
of academic excellence, innovation, and service to its students and the 
community. The school has played an important role in educating 
generations of students and creating a lasting sense of connection 
among students, families, educators, administrators, and alumni. As the 
proud parent of three B-CC alumni, I am especially grateful for this 
milestone, which provides an opportunity to reflect on the school's 
rich history and the countless individuals who have contributed to its 
legacy.
  To commemorate the centennial, the B-CC community will come together 
for a weekend of celebration, including a homecoming game, alumni 
gatherings, an open house, and the unveiling of its new museum, which 
highlights the school's history. The museum will feature exhibits 
celebrating B-CC's milestones over the past century, including a scene 
from ``The Betty Play,'' which tells the story of one of the first 
black students to integrate B-CC.
  I commend Bethesda-Chevy Chase High School on reaching this 
remarkable milestone and ask my colleagues to join me in expressing our 
best wishes for continued success to the students, faculty, staff, 
alumni, and the entire B-CC community.●
```
