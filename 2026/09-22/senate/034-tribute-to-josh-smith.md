# TRIBUTE TO JOSH SMITH

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JOSH SMITH

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, today I have the honor to recognize 
Josh Smith.
  Born and raised in Lincoln, MT, Josh is the president, founder, and 
master bladesmith for the Montana Knife Company. Starting at the age of 
11 years old, Josh learned the art of his craft from Rick Dunkerly, his 
baseball coach, where he learned how to make knives and remove stock 
blades in Rick's shop.
  Over the next few years, Josh began forging and honing his craft. At 
the age of 19, he earned his master smith rating from the American 
Bladesmith Society, becoming one of the youngest bladesmiths to ever 
pass the test.
  Years later, Josh moved to Frenchtown, MT, where he lives today with 
his wife and kids. Over the past 5 years, Josh grew his business from 
his garage to a new 50,000-square-foot facility where he now employs 
130 people. He has dedicated his life to delivering the highest quality 
of knives, with the kind of value to his customers know and trust that 
stands the test of time to be passed down for generations. Josh has 
done immense work creating opportunities for Montanans, giving back to 
veterans and outdoorsman alike.
  It is my honor to recognize Josh Smith for his outstanding 
contributions to the knife industry and the manufacturing craft in 
Montana. The legacy of your craftmanship and contributions to American 
manufacturing will not be forgotten.●

                          ____________________
```
