                   Unanimous Consent Request--S. 3101

  Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Mr. President, I rise today to again ask for 
unanimous consent to pass the SAFE KIDS Act.
  When I first came to the floor in July to advocate for this bill, my 
Democrat colleague called the issue of surrogate birth tourism a 
conspiracy theory. It was suggested that I was trying to prevent 
Americans from having families through surrogacy or other means. That 
was untrue then, and it is clearly untrue now.
  In July, I came to the floor to warn about Chinese birth tourism 
based on a story in my home State of Florida. It was a real story 
hurting one of my constituents, and I wanted to raise the alarm.
  I warned this body months ago of this national security threat. Here 
is what has happened since. This story from my State and others like it 
have made national headlines.
  CBS: `` `Who do we hand the baby to?' Loopholes and shady practices 
taint U.S. surrogacy industry.''
  The Wall Street Journal: ``The Chinese Billionaires Having Dozens of 
U.S.-Born Babies Via Surrogate.''
  Fortune: ``Chinese billionaire who has fathered more than 100 
children''--you can't make this stuff up--``hopes to have dozens of 
U.S.-born boys to one day take over his business.''
  The Wall Street Journal: ``A Florida Surrogate, a Father in China, 
and the Babies Caught in Between.''
  New York Magazine: ``Whose babies are these? A Chinese man hired a 
Florida surrogate, then didn't pick up his kids, sparking a custody 
battle that could upend the industry.''
  So the Wall Street Journal, New York Magazine, and CBS News have all 
run major stories about birth tourism since I last brought this to the 
floor. We cannot ignore it anymore, and we know it is not a conspiracy 
theory.
  Anyone overseas, including citizens of enemy countries--these are the 
types of people that could have surrogates--can get American birthright 
citizenship for a child by contracting a surrogate in America. They 
don't even have to set foot in America. You can't make this stuff up. 
No sane country would ever allow this.
  The threat is obvious: foreign adversaries having a backdoor to every 
privilege of being an American while spending no time--no time; they 
don't even have to show up in this country. And their kids are not 
being taught to love our country.
  This threat isn't empty. Our laws are being leveraged by foreigners 
right now. Chinese billionaire Xu Bo, a member of the CCP, has 
allegedly fathered between 100 and 300 American children via surrogacy, 
with birthright citizenship. As of today, no Federal legislation exists 
to prevent this. Right now, Xu Bo's actions are completely legal. Do we 
think he is having American children and taking them back to China to 
make America better? Of course not.
  This is a critical problem that could undermine American sovereignty, 
but even worse, we are talking about child trafficking. These children 
are being treated like commodities instead of human beings.
  We should all be asking ourselves: What happens to these children? 
Who raises them? What are they taught? What country are they taught to 
love? And what happens when they are old enough to return to the United 
States? Can they attend our universities? Can they serve in our 
military? Can they receive security clearances? Can they run for 
President? These are legitimate questions and a real vulnerability if 
we don't act on this today.
  One of my constituents Kyla Simpson is dealing with those questions 
right now. A Chinese citizen contracted her to be a surrogate, and then 
he never showed up. Her sons are now almost 2. The ``father'' has never 
set foot on U.S. soil, but she is fighting tooth and nail to keep them 
in the only home they have ever known.
  My bill fixes the problem by doing two things. First, it will 
invalidate commercial surrogacy contracts involving designated foreign 
adversaries--communist China, Iran, Russia, North Korea.
  Second, it will create a misdemeanor penalty for any broker who 
knowingly facilitates one of these prohibited arrangements.
  This is a very narrow bill. The bill is targeted at solving this 
exact problem

[[Page S4852]]

we are seeing today. It would mean people like Xu Bo cannot father 
American citizens while living in communist China and take them back to 
be raised by the CCP. It is going to protect women and children like 
Kyla Simpson and her two sons.
  American citizenship should not be for sale, and our enemies cannot 
be allowed to buy it. This country, its sovereignty, and its future are 
worth protecting. Everyone in this Chamber should believe that and 
support this bill.
  I hope that my colleagues have seen the reports and the problem with 
birth tourism since we last discussed it, and I hope all of my 
colleagues see the solution here: passing a very narrow bill, the SAFE 
KIDS Act.
  Notwithstanding rule XXII, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on the Judiciary be discharged from further consideration of S. 3101 
and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration. Further, I ask 
that the bill be considered read a third time and passed and that the 
motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  The Senator from California.

  The Senator from Florida.
  Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Mr. President, this is not controversial. It is 
a national security bill that protects women and children.
  So if you object to this bill, you are not protecting women and 
children and you are not protecting the United States.
  American citizenship cannot be bought. It is precious and sacred, and 
it comes with duties. Think about it. Countless men and women have 
given their lives to make it mean something. We owe it to them and to 
future generations to keep this country safe and protect our 
citizenship from those who want to destroy us. That is why it can't 
just be raffled off to our enemies. We can't let our enemies turn our 
laws into weapons against the American people. We can't let them 
traffic children right under our noses on our own soil in the process.
  Every Senator should be enraged that this is happening. Every Senator 
should support closing this dangerous loophole. That is why I am going 
to continue working with my colleagues to support the SAFE KIDS Act. If 
I have to keep coming down here and keep doing unanimous consents after 
we continue to see story after story, I am going to keep doing it. 
Mothers, children, and the American people deserve better.
  I yield the floor.
