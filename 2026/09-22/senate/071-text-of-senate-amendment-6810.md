# Text of Senate Amendment 6810

**Speaking:** Tuberville, Tommy (T000278) [R-AL]

```
  SA 6810. Mr. TUBERVILLE (for himself and Mrs. Britt) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. 
Thune (for Mr. Cruz (for himself, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, and Mr. 
Coons)) to the bill S. 4668, to protect the name, image, and likeness 
rights of, and provide protections for, student athletes and to promote 
fair competition among intercollegiate athletics, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        On page 130, line 11, insert after ``conferences.'' the 
     following: ``Nothing in the preceding sentence shall be 
     construed to create any inference that a media rights 
     arrangement outside the covered entity that is entered into 
     by an institution or conference before, on, or after the date 
     of enactment of this subsection violates the antitrust 
     laws.''.
                                 ______
```
