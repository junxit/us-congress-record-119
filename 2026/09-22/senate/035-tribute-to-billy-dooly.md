# TRIBUTE TO BILLY DOOLY

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO BILLY DOOLY

● Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize the 90th 
birthday of Billy Dooly.
  Born and raised in Fort Smith, Billy graduated from the University of 
Arkansas and began a distinguished 20-

[[Page S4854]]

year career in the U.S. Army after receiving a direct commission as an 
air defense artillery officer. While serving in uniform, he earned an 
MBA in industrial management from the University of Arkansas and went 
on to complete two tours with the Army general staff at the Pentagon. 
He also deployed to the Far East, including twice as a helicopter pilot 
in Vietnam.
  Dooly earned numerous military decorations, including the 
Distinguished Flying Cross, the Air Medal with 21 oakleaf clusters and 
three ``V'' devices for exceptional valor, and the Bronze Star Medal 
with one oakleaf cluster.
  Following his retirement from the Army in 1981, he returned to his 
hometown to be near his family but continued his commitment to serving 
a community we both cherished. He initially worked as vice president 
and general manager of North American Foundry before joining the Fort 
Smith Chamber of Commerce as president in 1987, providing invaluable 
leadership for 16 years.
  During his tenure at the chamber, Dooly was an outspoken advocate for 
the business community and a champion for the military and veteran 
community throughout the region. Among his many projects, he was a 
tireless advocate to complete the I-49 corridor, working with leaders 
from multiple States to pursue this critical project. Although he 
retired from the chamber in 2003, his impact on the area's 
infrastructure and strong community support for the military continues 
today.
  His contributions over the years have been recognized through many 
honors including the Mayor's Spirit of the Frontier Award and 
inductions into the Arkansas Tourism Hall of Fame, the Arkansas 
Hospitality Association Hall of Fame and the Arkansas Military 
Veterans' Hall of Fame.
  Above all, Dooly has always been a tremendous leader, whether in 
uniform, in business, or making a difference in his community. As he 
celebrates his 90th birthday, I thank him for his continued service to 
Arkansas. I wish him a happy birthday and many more years of health and 
happiness and very much appreciate his friendship of many 
years.●

                          ____________________
```
