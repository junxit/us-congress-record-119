# Tribute to Colonel Robert Sucher (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Budd, Ted (B001305) [R-NC], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                    Tribute to Colonel Robert Sucher

  Mr. BUDD. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize Col. Robert Sucher 
of the U.S. Marine Corps for his exemplary 30 years of faithful service 
to our Nation.
  Many of my colleagues in the Chamber, including myself, have had the 
distinct privilege of working closely with Colonel Sucher over the past 
2 years, during his tenure as the Director of the Marine Corps Senate 
Liaison Office.
  I came to appreciate his steadfast leadership firsthand while he 
served as the commanding officer of Marine Barracks Washington, where I 
was deeply honored to be a guest of honor at one of their historic 
Tuesday sunset parades.
  Throughout his time on Capitol Hill, Colonel Sucher and his dedicated 
team have consistently prioritized the relationship between the Marine 
Corps and the U.S. Senate. In this liaison role, Colonel Sucher enabled 
the successful execution of more than 50 international congressional 
delegations.
  My team and I have had the privilege of traveling with him on several 
of these official trips, including most recently to Central and Eastern 
Europe. On these demanding missions, his foresight and leadership were 
constantly on display.
  In addition to leading congressional delegations, Colonel Sucher 
distinguished himself through exceptional support to every Member of 
this body. He expertly managed inquiries and requests for information 
from nearly all 100 Senate offices, always providing timely and precise 
answers grounded in truth.
  He has proven an indispensable asset to this body, consistently 
demonstrating exceptional problem-solving under pressure, outstanding 
communication skills, and a wealth of operational knowledge, 
representing both the Marine Corps and the Department of War.
  He exemplifies the absolute professionalism we have come to expect 
from the U.S. Marines. Through his three decades of service, Colonel 
Sucher has proven to be an officer of the absolute highest caliber, and 
he is leaving the Marine Corps well positioned to continue its vital 
contributions to our national security.
  My colleagues and I bid a fond farewell to Colonel Sucher. We extend 
our deepest gratitude to his wife Caroline and their sons Will--who, by 
the way, is a proud North Carolinian--and Drew for the immense 
sacrifices they have made in supporting their husband and father as he 
guarded our freedom.
  To my friend Robb, we wish you fair winds and following seas in all 
of your future endeavors.
```
