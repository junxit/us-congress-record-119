# TRIBUTE TO ROGER HARRIS

**Speaking:** Wicker, Roger F. (W000437) [R-MS]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ROGER HARRIS

● Mr. WICKER. Mr. President, today I commend Roger Harris, who 
has been an excellent partner to the State of Mississippi and to 
passenger rail across the country. This year, Mr. Harris is concluding 
7 years of service with Amtrak, a tenure which has been marked by 
dedication and success.
  Roger joined Amtrak in January 2019, following a decades-long career 
in commercial aviation. He excelled and, within 3 years, was promoted 
to president. He devoted himself and the company to running a great 
railroad, building for the future, and increasing revenue and ridership 
across the entire Amtrak network.
  Roger's 7 years at Amtrak spanned some of the most consequential in 
the railroad's history. In the depths of the pandemic, he helped 
provide steady leadership, enabling the company to continue delivering 
essential service, even as daily ridership declined by up to 97 
percent. He led the company through an impressive post-pandemic 
recovery. Amtrak has set back-to-back revenue and ridership records 
over the last 2 years, and the company is currently on track for 
another banner year. It is worth noting that Amtrak has met this 
growing demand for train service while delivering major capital 
projects, including new trains and facilities, more accessible 
stations, and the biggest Northeast Corridor infrastructure program in 
Amtrak history.
  Under his leadership, Amtrak has been ambitious: launching the 
NextGen Acela fleet, beginning the delivery of the Airo fleet, laying 
the groundwork to replace much of the long distance fleet, advancing 
major infrastructure projects like the Portal North Bridge and the B&P 
Tunnel Replacement, and expanding service in places like Virginia and 
the Midwest Borealis train. Of particular interest to my constituents, 
he helped accomplish what Mississippians had anticipated for two 
decades: the return of passenger rail to the gulf coast.
  The Mardi Gras Service is personal to me and to the people I 
represent. Hurricane Katrina silenced that line in 2005. For 20 years, 
the gulf coast lost not just a train but one of its connections to the 
rest of the country. I spent the better part of a decade in the U.S. 
Senate working to restore this service. I testified before the Surface 
Transportation Board, secured Federal investment, and spoke to every 
Amtrak and Federal Railroad Administration official that I could reach. 
On August 18, 2025, the Mardi Gras Service was restored. Through it 
all, Mr. Harris and his team were also integral to restoring the 
service.
  A year into its operations, the Mardi Gras Service has exceeded every 
expectation. Amtrak projected 71,000 riders during the service's first 
year. The line has carried more than 100,000 passengers just in 8 
months. Riders have embraced the service, giving it the highest 
customer satisfaction rating in the Amtrak system.
  That success did not happen by accident. It reflects a leadership 
philosophy that values customer service, operational excellence, and a 
willingness to invest in communities that had waited far too long for 
passenger rail. Roger believed the gulf coast corridor was worth 
restoring, and the people of Mississippi are better served because of 
his belief.
  In my interactions with Roger, he has always been straightforward, 
accessible, and willing to work together. Because of that, Mr. Harris 
does not only leave Amtrak in a stronger position than he found it. He 
also leaves it with many a friend in Mississippi. On behalf of the 
people of my State and the taxpayer--and of the riders along the gulf 
coast who have a Mardi Gras Service to be proud of--I thank Roger 
Harris for his service. I wish him and his family well in the next 
adventure.●

                          ____________________
```
