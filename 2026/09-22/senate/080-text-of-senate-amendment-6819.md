# Text of Senate Amendment 6819

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

```
  SA 6819. Mr. BLUMENTHAL submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Cruz (for 
himself, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, and Mr. Coons)) to the bill S. 
4668, to protect the name, image, and likeness rights of, and provide 
protections for, student athletes and to promote fair competition among 
intercollegiate athletics, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

        At the end of title II, add the following:

     SEC. 208. CONFERENCE MEMBERSHIP LIMIT AND COVERED INSTITUTION 
                   LOOKBACK.

       Section 7 of the Sports Broadcasting Act of 1961, as added 
     by section 205, is amended--
       (1) in subsection (a)(1)(B), by striking ``19 
     institutions'' and inserting ``20 institutions''; and
       (2) in subsection (f)(2)(A)(i), by striking ``preceding 3 
     years'' and inserting ``preceding year''.
                                 ______
```
