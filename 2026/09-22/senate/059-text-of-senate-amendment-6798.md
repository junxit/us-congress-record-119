# Text of Senate Amendment 6798

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

```
  SA 6798. Ms. HIRONO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Cruz (for himself, Ms. 
Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, and Mr. Coons)) to the bill S. 4668, to protect 
the name, image, and likeness rights of, and provide protections for, 
student athletes and to promote fair competition among intercollegiate 
athletics,

[[Page S4869]]

and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 51, strike lines 20 through 24 and insert the 
     following:
       (c) Appointment and Term.--
       (1) Appointment.--The Ombudsman shall be appointed by the 
     Secretary of Education after meaningful consultation with 
     relevant stakeholders, which shall include college 
     presidents, athletic directors, college coaches, and student 
     athletes, among others, representing geographically diverse 
     schools and schools of different size (including large 
     schools (more than $80,000,000 in athletics revenue), medium 
     schools (more than $50,000,000 but less than $80,000,000 in 
     athletics revenue), and small schools (less than $50,000,000 
     in athletics revenue)), from a diverse variety of sports and 
     different divisions (Division 1, Division 2, and Division 3).
       (2) Term.--The Ombudsman shall be appointed for a term of 7 
     years and may serve not more than 2 full terms.
       (d) Administration.--
       (1) In general.--An intercollegiate athletic association 
     shall hire and provide salary, benefits, and administrative 
     expenses for an Ombudsman and support staff for the Office.
       (2) Salary.--The Ombudsman shall receive an annual salary 
     of $197,200 per year, plus benefits.
       (3) Budget.--The budget of the Ombudsman shall be not less 
     than $25,000,000 per year.
       (e) Confidentiality.--
                                 ______
```
