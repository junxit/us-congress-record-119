# JAPANESE AUTO INVESTMENT IN OHIO

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

```
                    JAPANESE AUTO INVESTMENT IN OHIO

● Mr. HUSTED. Mr. President, Ohio's relationship with Japanese-
brand automakers is perhaps the oldest and most significant in the 
entire country. When Honda chose our State for automotive manufacturing 
operations in 1982, no one could have imagined what that would lead to. 
It has grown into an over $15 billion commitment, and Honda directly 
employs over 15,000 Ohioans. This investment has had great impact on 
the communities of central and southwest Ohio. Honda's plants in 
Marysville, East Liberty, Anna, and Russells Point support the 
production of hundreds of thousands of vehicles, engines, and 
transmissions. The company also has a decades-long research and 
development presence in the State, helping cement Ohio as a leading 
destination for automotive research and testing. Honda continues to 
expand with the start of mass production of lithium-ion battery cells 
for energy storage systems at its L-H Battery facility in 
Jeffersonville, OH. In addition, a joint venture with Korean battery 
maker LG Energy Solutions represents another $4.4 billion investment in 
Ohio.
  Honda's Ohio operations also have a profound impact on Ohio's 
workforce and education initiatives. The Japan Automobile Manufacturers 
Association's (JAMA) workforce development white paper highlights 
Honda's 20-plus-year partnership with The Ohio State University, which 
connects engineering research with manufacturing, as well as training 
programs in robotics, automation, and quality. Honda also supports 
earn-and-learn programs, co-ops, scholarships, and technical curricula 
through community colleges and training centers. Honda's long-term 
commitment to Ohio is evident through its community engagement, 
including charitable giving, driving safety campaigns, and 
sustainability initiatives. As JAMA marks 50 years representing 
Japanese-brand automakers in Washington DC, Ohio's experience shows how 
foreign direct investment, paired with a long-term vision and 
commitment, can strengthen manufacturing communities while driving 
automotive innovation.●
```
