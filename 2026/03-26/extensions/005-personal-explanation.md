                        Thursday, March 26, 2026

  Mr. STANTON. Mr. Speaker, today, due to unforeseen circumstances, I 
missed Roll Call Vote No. 104, Pay Our Homeland Defenders Act. Had I 
been present, consistent with my three previous votes to oppose 
additional funding for the Department of Homeland Security, without the 
needed accountability measures for ICE, I would have voted NO.

Act, which included $75 billion in funding for ICE, and my leadership 
of the PUBLIC SAFETY Act to redirect this funding away from ICE and 
toward local law enforcement agencies that invest in well-trained 
officers with strong ties to the communities they serve.
