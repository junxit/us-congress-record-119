# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 5103

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:00 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 5103. An act to establish a program to Beautify the 
     District of Columbia and establish the District of Columbia 
     Safe and Beautiful Commission.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 2(b)(2) of the 
Commission to Study the Potential Creation of a National Museum of 
Asian Pacific American History and Culture Act (Public Law 117-140), 
and the order of the House of January 3, 2025, the Speaker appoints the 
following individual on the part of the House of Representatives to the 
Commission to Study the Potential Creation of a National Museum of 
Asian Pacific American History and Culture to fill the existing vacancy 
thereon: Mr. Anh ``Joseph'' Cao of Metairie, Louisiana.

                          ____________________
```
