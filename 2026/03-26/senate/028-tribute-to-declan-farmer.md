# TRIBUTE TO DECLAN FARMER

**Speaking:** Moody, Ashley (M001244) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO DECLAN FARMER

● Mrs. MOODY. Mr. President, today I ask the Senate to recognize 
an extraordinary athlete and a proud son of Florida's Suncoast, Declan 
Farmer for once again bringing honor to our Nation with his gold medal 
victory at the 2026 Winter Olympic Games.
  This remarkable achievement marks a historic fifth consecutive gold 
for Team USA in para ice hockey, a testament to the talent and tenacity 
Declan brings to the ice.
  Declan's journey began in Clearwater at the age of 9. A bilateral 
amputee, he discovered a passion for sled hockey that would propel him 
from the local rinks to the world stage. From his early days at 
Berkeley Preparatory School to his time at Princeton University, Declan 
has exemplified excellence both on and off the ice.
  His local roots in Florida remain deep. He has competed with the 
Tampa Bay Lightning Sled Hockey Team, the Space Coast Hurricanes, and 
Florida Bandits, inspiring athletes across our State and Nation. Since 
joining the U.S. National Team at just 14 years old, Declan has become 
a dominant force in the sport. His accomplishments include:

       Paralympic gold medals in 2014, 2018, 2022, and now 2026; 
     leading scorer honors at the 2013 World Championship, as well 
     as the 2018 and 2026 Paralympics; and recognition as the Best 
     Male Athlete with a Disability at the 2014 ESPY Awards.

  Through tireless dedication and unmatched skill, Declan Farmer 
represents the best of the American spirit. It is a privilege to 
recognize his achievements and his contributions to Team USA and honor 
the legacy he continues to build for future athletes in Florida and 
across our Nation.●
```
