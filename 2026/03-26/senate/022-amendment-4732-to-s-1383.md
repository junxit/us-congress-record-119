# AMENDMENT 4732 to S. 1383

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

**Measures:** S. 1383

```
                       AMENDMENT 4732 to S. 1383

  Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, I support voter ID. Indeed, the State of 
Georgia has voter ID. It is only reasonable that eligible voters should 
prove their identity. I will not support a half-baked partisan 
``gotcha'' amendment cooked up with no serious deliberation, no 
committee hearings, no bipartisan consultation, no input from State and 
local election officials, where the stakes are potential infringement 
of the most sacred right at the core of our constitutional Republic.

                          ____________________
```
