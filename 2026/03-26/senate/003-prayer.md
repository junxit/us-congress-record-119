# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord, You are our light and salvation. So we have no reason to fear. 
We ask that, through Your gracious intervention, You convey Your 
marvelous wisdom, knowledge, and strength to the lawmakers of this 
great Nation.
  We place our trust in Your powerful providence, entreating You to 
send Your divine inspiration in order to empower our legislators to do 
Your will. May, through this empowerment, You provide them with the 
ability to calm this restless Nation and world.
  Lord, let there be peace on Earth, and let it begin with us.
  We pray in Your adored Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
