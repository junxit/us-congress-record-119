# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 32, S.Res. 662, S.Res. 663, S.Res. 664, S.Res. 665, S.Res. 666

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. KELLY:
       S. Res. 662. A resolution honoring the life and service of 
     United States Marine Corps veteran Nicholas Douglas Quets, 
     expressing condolences to his family, and condemning cartel 
     violence; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. MORENO (for himself and Mr. Husted):
       S. Res. 663. A resolution honoring the service and 
     sacrifice of the Ohio Air National Guard airmen who were 
     killed in the Boeing KC-135 Stratotanker crash in Iraq during 
     Operation Epic Fury; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. SCHATZ (for himself, Ms. Hirono, Mr. Padilla, 
             Mr. Coons, Mr. Booker, Mr. Wyden, Mr. Schiff, Mr. 
             Welch, Mr. Merkley, Ms. Duckworth, and Mr. 
             Fetterman):
       S. Con. Res. 32. A concurrent resolution supporting the 
     goals and ideals of International Transgender Day of 
     Visibility; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. THUNE:
       S. Res. 664. A resolution to constitute the majority 
     party's membership on certain committees for the One Hundred 
     Nineteenth Congress, or until their successors are chosen; 
     considered and agreed to.
           By Mrs. HYDE-SMITH (for herself, Ms. Hirono, Mr. 
             Husted, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Wicker, Mr. Booker, Mr. 
             Bennet, Mrs. Shaheen, and Mr. Heinrich):
       S. Res. 665. A resolution designating April 2026 as 
     ``National Native Plant Month''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. MERKLEY (for himself, Mr. Daines, Mr. Durbin, 
             Mr. Markey, Mr. Booker, and Mr. Padilla):
       S. Res. 666. A resolution designating the first week of 
     April as ``National Asbestos Awareness Week''; considered and 
     agreed to.
```
