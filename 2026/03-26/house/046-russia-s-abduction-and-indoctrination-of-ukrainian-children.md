# RUSSIA'S ABDUCTION AND INDOCTRINATION OF UKRAINIAN CHILDREN

**Speaking:** Landsman, Greg (L000601) [D-OH]

```
      RUSSIA'S ABDUCTION AND INDOCTRINATION OF UKRAINIAN CHILDREN

  (Mr. LANDSMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of the 35,000 
children in Ukraine that Putin and others have stolen during this 
unprovoked and brutal war.
  Shockingly, our government just made things worse as it relates to 
these children. Last week, Trump waived sanctions on Russian oil 
companies, including two Russian oil companies that, based on recent 
reporting, have directly funded and facilitated the abduction and 
indoctrination of Ukrainian children.
  Lifting the sanctions will do little to nothing to ease gas prices 
but will significantly benefit these two Russian oil companies. These 
companies are helping to steal children, and Trump just helped them 
out. They will make billions of dollars.
  In the coming days, we will work to rally Congress to compel Trump to 
reverse these sanctions. Every single person responsible for stealing 
Ukrainian children must be held accountable.

                          ____________________
```
