# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9102

**Measures:** H.R. 9102

```
            By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
        H.R. 9102.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1, 3, and 18--the powers to 
     lay and collect taxes, regulate commerce with foreign 
     nations, and make all laws necessary and proper for executing 
     the foregoing powers, as well as the powers vested in the 
     Government of the United States.
```
