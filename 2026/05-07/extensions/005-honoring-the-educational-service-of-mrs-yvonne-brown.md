# HONORING THE EDUCATIONAL SERVICE OF MRS. YVONNE BROWN

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Vicente (G000581) [D-TX]

```
         HONORING THE EDUCATIONAL SERVICE OF MRS. YVONNE BROWN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 7, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
commend Mrs. Yvonne Brown for her commitment to educational excellence 
and for receiving the Teacher of the Year award at St. Patrick's School 
in Corpus Christi, Texas. Mrs. Brown was honored by the St. Patrick's 
community for over 19 years of service and dedication to its students.

  Her first role in education was at Marine Corps Base Quantico, 
serving as an elementary school English-as-a-Second-Language (ESL) 
instructor, where she approached her students with patience, 
intentionality, and compassion. She has always been motivated by a 
desire to help others through learning just as her teachers once helped 
her.
  Mrs. Brown currently serves as the computer and technology teacher at 
St. Patrick's School. In the computer lab, she has developed an 
innovative curriculum to teach elementary schoolers digital literacy, 
typing, coding, and robotics. These foundational skills will serve 
students for life in a world with rapidly developing technology.
  Not only do these classes help students meet the growing 
technological demands of the 21st century, but they also inspire 
students to pursue advanced studies in areas such as computer science, 
artificial intelligence, and engineering.
  In addition, Mrs. Brown serves as a leader of the St. Patrick's 
theater program, casting, directing, and producing multiple plays per 
year. This provides her students with the opportunity to express 
themselves creatively and faithfully in artistic outlets.
  Outside of the classroom, Mrs. Brown has helped organize the school's 
Halloween Carnival, led her local Girl Scout Troop, and kept her 
community safe by coordinating a Watch Dog Program.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the 34th Congressional District of Texas, I 
congratulate Mrs. Yvonne Brown on her many accomplishments and thank 
her for her continued service. Her leadership serves as a model for 
teachers to uplift communities across South Texas and the Nation.
```
