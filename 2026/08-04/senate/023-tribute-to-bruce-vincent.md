# TRIBUTE TO BRUCE VINCENT

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BRUCE VINCENT

● Mr. DAINES. Madam President, today I have the honor to 
recognize Bruce Vincent.
  Bruce Vincent is from Libby, MT, and a third-generation logger. After 
completing college, Bruce moved back home and joined his father's 
family business Vincent Logging as a business manager.
  In 1988, Bruce helped form the Communities For A Great Northwest, a 
nonprofit organization dedicated to educating groups about the use of 
natural resources. He also founded the community outreach firm 
Environomics, focusing on issues for resource-dependent rural 
communities and responsible environmental stewardship.
  His dedication to the great outdoors speaks volumes as he has 
received various recognitions for his hard work over the years. Bruce 
has been awarded the National Timberman Industry Activist of the Year, 
Montana Timberman of the Year, and several other accolades that speak 
to his passion for protecting the Montana that we love.
  Bruce Vincent has dedicated his life to finding ways to protect the 
Last Best Place. It is my honor to recognize Bruce for his 
contributions to environmentalism, the logging industry, and his 
passion for the great outdoors.●
```
