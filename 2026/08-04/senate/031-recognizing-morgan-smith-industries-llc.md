# RECOGNIZING MORGAN SMITH INDUSTRIES, LLC

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
                RECOGNIZING MORGAN SMITH INDUSTRIES, LLC

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, as a member of the Senate Committee 
on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, I would like to recognize an 
outstanding Kentucky small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. It is my privilege to recognize Morgan Smith 
Industries, LLC of Columbia, KY, as my Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  Morgan Smith Industries, LLC is a family-owned architectural 
millwork, custom cabinetry, casework, furniture, and interior 
construction manufacturer whose roots in Kentucky craftsmanship span 
nearly eight decades. The company's history began in 1948 when Willis 
D. McCammish and his son Doug McCammish established a small cabinet and 
countertop business in Winchester, KY. Through hard work, quality 
craftsmanship, and a commitment to serving their customers, the 
business expanded into commercial architectural millwork, institutional 
cabinetry, and contract furniture, earning a reputation for excellence 
throughout the Commonwealth. In 2012, the company entered a new chapter 
when J. Andrew ``Andy'' Keith and his nephew Jonathan Keith acquired 
the business and continued its tradition of family ownership while 
building upon its legacy of quality and service.
  Today, Morgan Smith Industries employs approximately 30 skilled 
Kentuckians and continues to invest in the workforce and economy of 
Adair County. By combining experienced craftsmanship with modern 
manufacturing techniques, Morgan Smith Industries has become one of 
Kentucky's premier producers of architectural millwork and custom 
interior furnishings. The quality of Morgan Smith Industries' work can 
be found in some of Kentucky's most significant public and private 
facilities. The company has completed projects for universities, public 
schools, courthouses, healthcare facilities, government buildings, and 
commercial developments across the Commonwealth. Among its notable 
projects are the new Adair County Middle School, the Lexington Federal 
Courthouse, and the Kentucky State Capitol.
  Congratulations to Andy Keith, Jonathan Keith, and the entire team at 
Morgan Smith Industries, LLC. I look forward to their continued growth 
and success in the Commonwealth of Kentucky.●
```
