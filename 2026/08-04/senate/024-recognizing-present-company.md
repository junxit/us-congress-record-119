# RECOGNIZING PRESENT COMPANY

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING PRESENT COMPANY

● Ms. ERNST. Madam President, as chair of the Senate Committee 
on Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Present 
Company of Audubon County, IA, as the Senate Small Business of the 
Week.
  In 1990, Present Company opened its doors as a home decor boutique. 
The business quickly became a destination for quality home furnishings 
and unique gifts for the Audubon community. After more than three 
decades serving southwest Iowa under three different owners, Present 
Company transitioned into the hands of Telia Carter in 2022. Telia 
spent 16 years working at a local bank and embraced the opportunity to 
head up the beloved boutique. Under her leadership, Present Company has 
remained true to its original mission while evolving alongside changing 
customer preferences to meet the needs and interests of Audubon and the 
surrounding communities.
  Today, Present Company continues to offer carefully selected home 
decor along with a variety of gifts, apparel, jewelry, and specialty 
items. They also provide a tuxedo rental service for local school 
dances making the store a staple of Audubon's downtown business 
district. With a small but dedicated team of three employees, the 
boutique has earned a reputation as a place where customers can find 
unique items and personalized service that reflects the character of 
smalltown Iowa.
  In hopes of expanding the offerings on Main Street, the business 
began to house The Nail Room by Lisset, a local nail salon founded by 
former National Guard Lisset Huerta. Beyond its retail offerings, the 
Present Company regularly donates to organizations that assist children 
in buying animals to show

[[Page S4429]]

at the county fair. They also donate to local golf tournaments and the 
Audubon T-Bone days to promote the beef industry. During the holidays, 
the business will organize community events including a Thanksgiving 
Shopping Extravaganza and a Christmas tour of homes. Present Company's 
ongoing investment in their community demonstrates the important role 
locally owned small businesses play in strengthening Iowa's rural 
communities.
  I am pleased to recognize Telia Carter and the team at Present 
Company for their dedication to Audubon County. I look forward to their 
continued success and wish them the very best in the years 
ahead.●
```
