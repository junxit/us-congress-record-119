# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  O Lord, in whose hand is the life of every living thing and the 
breath of all humanity, we come before You in humility, and trust that 
all wisdom, justice, and mercy are Yours alone. Apart from You, we can 
do nothing.
  Lord, we pray for our Senators and all entrusted with governance. 
Manifest Your presence among them. Order their steps in Your truth, and 
lead them in righteousness, courage, and unity. Give them discerning 
hearts to choose what is right over what is merely expedient and the 
grace to pursue justice with humility, wisdom, and integrity.
  We pray in Your precious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
