# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF MIAMI SPRINGS, FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF MIAMI SPRINGS, FLORIDA

● Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Madam President, I rise on behalf of all 
Floridians to recognize the 100th anniversary of the city of Miami 
Springs, FL, and to celebrate a century of its community. Since its 
incorporation on August 23, 1926, Miami Springs has grown from one of 
Florida's first communities, founded by aviation pioneer Glenn Hammond 
Curtiss, into a city with deep roots. For 100 years, Miami Springs has 
helped shape south Florida's aviation industry while preserving the 
history, landmarks, and strong community that make the city special. As 
Miami Springs celebrates

[[Page S4431]]

its centennial, I join my fellow Floridians in congratulating the city 
on this remarkable milestone and commend its residents for their 
contributions to the prosperity and history of the State of Florida and 
the United States.●

                          ____________________
```
