# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Cassidy, Bill (C001075) [R-LA]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. CASSIDY. Mr. President, I have five requests for committees to 
meet during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of 
the Majority and Minority Leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


                      COMMITTEE ON INDIAN AFFAIRS

  The Committee on Indian Affairs is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 4 p.m., to conduct 
a hearing.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session

[[Page S4444]]

of the Senate on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 9 a.m., to conduct an 
executive business meeting.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 2:30 p.m., to 
conduct a hearing.


                    SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  The Select Committee on Intelligence is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 3 p.m., to conduct 
a closed hearing.


                      SUBCOMMITTEE ON HEALTH CARE

  The Subcommittee on Health Care of the Committee on Finance is 
authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, August 
4, 2026, at 10 a.m., to conduct a hearing.
```
