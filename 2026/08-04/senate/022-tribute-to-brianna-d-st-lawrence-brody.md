# TRIBUTE TO BRIANNA D. ST. LAWRENCE-BRODY

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                TRIBUTE TO BRIANNA D. ST. LAWRENCE-BRODY

● Mr. DAINES. Madam President, today I have the honor to 
recognize SSgt Brianna D. St. Lawrence-Brody as Montanan of the Month.
  Brianna serves with the Montana Air National Guard in the 120th 
Airlift Wing in Great Falls and last week was awarded the Air Force's 
2026 Total Force Outstanding Airmen of the Year, earning one of the Air 
Force's highest honors. As command post controller, she assists in 
helping share information and coordinate communication during 
emergencies and major events.
  The award recognizes enlisted airmen who exemplify exceptional 
leadership, stellar job performance, self-improvement, and a sustained 
commitment to their communities.
  Brianna not only serves as a part-time member of the Montana Air 
National Guard but is also a full-time school nurse in Great Falls, 
where she continues to serve others in her civilian career. She is a 
mom of four.
  Brianna is a selfless, hard-working, and selfless-serving member, 
representing the best of Montana and the values of our great Nation.
  I am thrilled to recognize Brianna for her selfless actions as part 
of the Montana Air National Guard, her extraordinary performance in her 
civilian career, and her dedication to caring for our communities. It 
is my distinct honor to recognize Brianna D. St. Lawrence-Brody as 
Montanan of the Month.●

                          ____________________
```
