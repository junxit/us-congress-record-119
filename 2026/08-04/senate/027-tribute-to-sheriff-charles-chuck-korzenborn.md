# TRIBUTE TO SHERIFF CHARLES "CHUCK" KORZENBORN

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
            TRIBUTE TO SHERIFF CHARLES ``CHUCK'' KORZENBORN

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, I rise today to recognize Sheriff 
Charles ``Chuck'' Korzenborn of Kenton County, KY, upon the occasion of 
his well-earned retirement from a distinguished career in law 
enforcement and public service.
  For 26 years, Sheriff Korzenborn has served the people of Kenton 
County with integrity, professionalism, and an unwavering commitment to 
public safety. As sheriff, he has been entrusted with one of the most 
vital responsibilities in our communities: protecting the lives, 
rights, and property of the citizens he served. Through his leadership, 
the Kenton County Sheriff's Office has upheld the highest standards of 
law enforcement while fostering trust and cooperation between officers 
and the community.
  Sheriff Korzenborn's career reflects a deep dedication to service 
above self. He has led with fairness, accountability, and respect for 
the rule of law, earning the confidence of his colleagues and the 
gratitude of the residents of Kenton County. His steady leadership and 
experience have helped guide his department through both everyday 
challenges and extraordinary moments, always with the safety and well-
being of the community as his top priority.
  Sheriff Korzenborn's legacy is one of mentorship and example. He has 
influenced countless deputies and staff members, instilling in them the 
values of duty, honor, and service that will continue to shape the 
Kenton County Sheriff's Office for years to come.
  As Sheriff Korzenborn enters retirement, he leaves behind a record of 
service that reflects great credit upon himself, his family, the Kenton 
County Sheriff's Office, and the Commonwealth of Kentucky. I extend my 
sincere appreciation for his years of dedicated service and wish him 
the best in the next chapter of his life.●

                          ____________________
```
