# TRIBUTE TO TERRY McINTOSH

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO TERRY McINTOSH

● Mr. PAUL. Madam President, I rise today to honor the service 
of a true American patriot, Terry McIntosh of Paducah, KY.
  At the age of 17, motivated by a sense of duty to his country during 
the Vietnam war, Terry enlisted in the United States Army. Despite his 
youth, he would soon find himself on the frontlines, eventually 
assigned to Operational Detachment A-414, a Green Beret ``A-team'' 
operating along the Cambodian border. For his service in combat, which 
included multiple missions and a February 1969 firefight that

[[Page S4430]]

earned him the Combat Infantryman Badge, Mr. McIntosh stood shoulder-
to-shoulder with the elite of our Armed Forces.
  For decades, the formal recognition of this service remained elusive. 
It was not until March 2026, following a review of his personnel 
records, that the Army retroactively awarded Mr. McIntosh the Special 
Forces tab--a long-overdue acknowledgment of his status as a Green 
Beret.
  Mr. McIntosh's story is a testament to the quiet professionalism of 
the soldiers who did the job without the need for fanfare or special 
recognition. As he noted, he sought this recognition not for himself, 
but so his children would know the nature of his service to this 
Nation.
  It is my privilege to recognize Mr. McIntosh today. His dedication at 
such a young age is an inspiration to all who value the courage of 
those who wear the uniform. We honor his service, we celebrate his 
official recognition as a Green Beret, and we thank him for his 
enduring commitment to this country.●

                          ____________________
```
