```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, list or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                        Offered By Mr. Crawford

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary and the Permanent Select Committee on 
     Intelligence in

     Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 through October 20,

     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

     Act of 2008 to extend the authorities of title VII of the 
     Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 through October 
     20, 2027, and for other purposes, do not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
