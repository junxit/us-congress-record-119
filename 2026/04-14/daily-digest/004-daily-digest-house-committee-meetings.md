# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Res. 1156, H.R. 6387, H.R. 6398, H.R. 6409, H.R. 8035

```
Committee Meetings
FIRE ACT; RED TAPE ACT; FENCES ACT; EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR TAX POLICIES 
THAT SUPPORT WORKING FAMILIES; TO AMEND THE FISA AMENDMENTS ACT OF 2008 
TO EXTEND THE AUTHORITIES OF THE FOREIGN INTELLIGENCE SURVEILLANCE ACT 
OF 1978
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 6387, the 
``FIRE Act''; H.R. 6398, the ``RED Tape Act''; H.R. 6409, the ``FENCES 
Act''; H. Res. 1156, expressing support for tax policies that support 
working families; and H.R. 8035, to amend the FISA Amendments Act of 
2008 to extend the authorities of title VII of the Foreign Intelligence 
Surveillance Act of 1978 through October 20, 2027, and for other 
purposes. The Committee granted, by a record vote of 6-4, a rule 
providing for consideration of H.R. 6387, the ``FIRE Act'', H.R. 6398, 
the ``RED Tape Act'', H.R. 6409, the ``FENCES Act'', and H. Res. 1156, 
Expressing support for tax policies that support working families. The 
rule provides for consideration of H.R. 6387, H.R. 6398, and H.R. 6409 
under closed rules. The rule waives all points of order against 
consideration of each such bill. The rule provides that each such bill 
shall be considered as read. The rule waives all points of order 
against provisions in each such bill. The rule provides one hour of 
general debate on each such bill equally divided and controlled by the 
chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and 
Commerce or their respective designees. The rule provides each such 
bill one motion to recommit. The rule further provides for 
consideration of H. Res. 1156, Expressing support for tax policies that 
support working families, under a closed rule. The rule provides that 
upon adoption of the resolution it shall be in order without 
intervention of any point of order to consider H. Res. 1156. The rule 
provides that H. Res. 1156 shall be considered as read. Finally, the 
rule provides one hour of general debate equally divided and controlled 
by the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on Ways and 
Means or their respective designees. The Committee granted, by a record 
vote of 6-4, a rule providing for consideration of H.R. 8035, To amend 
the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 to extend the authorities of title VII 
of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 through October 
20, 2027, and for other purposes, under a closed rule. The rule waives 
all points of order against consideration of the bill. The rule 
provides that the bill shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the bill. The rule provides one 
hour of general debate equally divided among and controlled by the 
chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on the Judiciary or 
their respective designees and the chair and ranking minority member of 
the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence or their respective 
designees. Finally, the rule provides one motion to recommit. Testimony 
was heard from Chairman Jordan, and Representatives Palmer, Tonko, 
Kelly of Pennsylvania, Sanchez, Raskin, and Himes.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
