# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 16, S.J.Res. 32, S.J.Res. 112, S. 124, S. 219, S. 275, S.Res. 604, S.Res. 627, S.Res. 657, S. 775, S. 952, S. 1367, S. 1454, S. 2061, S. 2265, S. 3159, S. 3400, S. 3739, S. 3857, S. 3869, S. 3894, S. 3949, S. 4095, S. 4097, S. 4140, S. 4167

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 124

  At the request of Mr. Moran, the name of the Senator from Alaska (Mr. 
Sullivan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 124, a bill to amend title 38, 
United States Code, to provide for disciplinary procedures for 
supervisors and managers at the Department of Veterans Affairs and to 
modify the procedures of personnel actions against employees of the 
Department, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 219

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the name of the Senator from Alaska 
(Mr. Sullivan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 219, a bill to direct the 
Secretary of Veterans Affairs to carry out a pilot program to improve 
the ability of veterans to access medical care in medical facilities of 
the Department of Veterans Affairs and in the community by providing 
veterans the ability to choose health care providers.


                                 S. 275

  At the request of Mr. Moran, the name of the Senator from Alaska (Mr. 
Sullivan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 275, a bill to improve the 
provision of care and services under the Veterans Community Care 
Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 775

  At the request of Mr. Graham, the names of the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Scott), the Senator from Rhode Island (Mr. Whitehouse) and the 
Senator from Oregon (Mr. Merkley) were added as cosponsors of S. 775, a 
bill to amend the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 to prohibit the 
slaughter of equines for human consumption.


                                 S. 952

  At the request of Mr. Cassidy, the name of the Senator from Kentucky 
(Mr. Paul) was added as a cosponsor of S. 952, a bill to amend the 
Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to provide a uniform 8-
digit subheading number for all whiskies.


                                S. 1367

  At the request of Mr. Coons, the names of the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Welch) and the Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 1367, a bill to protect intellectual property rights 
in the voice and visual likeness of individuals, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 1454

  At the request of Mr. Kennedy, the names of the Senator from Missouri 
(Mr. Schmitt), the Senator from Florida (Mr. Scott), the Senator from 
California (Mr. Schiff) and the Senator from Illinois (Ms. Duckworth) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 1454, a bill to amend the Animal Welfare 
Act to provide for greater protection of roosters, and for other 
purposes.


                                S. 2061

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from 
Colorado (Mr. Bennet) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2061, a bill to 
require the Interagency Working Group on Toxic Exposure to conduct 
research on the diagnosis and treatment of health conditions of 
descendants of individuals exposed to toxic substances while serving as 
members of the Armed Forces, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2265

  At the request of Mr. Padilla, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2265, a bill to 
require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in commemoration of 
the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games in Los Angeles, California, and 
the 2034 Olympics and Paralympic Winter Games in Salt Lake City, Utah.


                                S. 3159

  At the request of Mr. Lankford, the name of the Senator from 
Mississippi (Mrs. Hyde-Smith) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3159, a 
bill to amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to temporarily 
provide for long-term care pharmacy supply fees in connection with the 
dispensing of certain drugs.


                                S. 3400

  At the request of Mr. Curtis, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Mr. Coons) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3400, a bill to amend title 
XXVII of the Public Health Service Act, the Employee Retirement Income 
Security Act of 1974, the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, and the 
Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to require coverage of 
hearing devices and systems in certain private health insurance plans, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 3739

  At the request of Mrs. Blackburn, the name of the Senator from 
Montana (Mr. Sheehy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3739, a bill to 
direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to carry out a demonstration 
project on coverage by the Department of Veterans Affairs of over-the-
counter hearing aids, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3857

  At the request of Mr. Scott of Florida, the name of the Senator from 
Utah

[[Page S1741]]

(Mr. Lee) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3857, a bill to prohibit the 
Secretary of Homeland Security from admitting to the United States any 
national of the People's Republic of China without a valid visa, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 3869

  At the request of Mr. Sanders, the name of the Senator from Oregon 
(Mr. Merkley) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3869, a bill to allow 
Americans to earn paid sick time so that they can address their own 
health needs and the health needs of their families.


                                S. 3894

  At the request of Mrs. Shaheen, the name of the Senator from Virginia 
(Mr. Kaine) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3894, a bill to prohibit the 
Department of Homeland Security from constructing, acquiring, 
renovating, or operating any new processing site or detention center 
without providing a mechanism for public comments regarding such 
activity, entering into a signed, written agreement with appropriate 
State and local officials, and providing Congress with advance notice 
of such activity.


                                S. 3949

  At the request of Mr. Wyden, the names of the Senator from West 
Virginia (Mr. Justice) and the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Booker) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 3949, a bill to amend the Food and 
Nutrition Act of 2008 to require the promulgation of cybersecurity and 
digital service regulations relating to the use of EBT cards under the 
supplemental nutrition assistance program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4095

  At the request of Mr. Welch, the name of the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Sanders) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4095, a bill to call for 
accountability for the killing of innocent civilians, including 5-year-
old Hind Rajab and two paramedics, in an attack in Gaza City on January 
29, 2024, by Israel Defense Forces, to require the Secretary of State 
to report to Congress on the attack, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4097

  At the request of Ms. Murkowski, the name of the Senator from 
Oklahoma (Mr. Lankford) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4097, a bill to 
establish that a State-based education loan program is excluded from 
certain requirements relating to a preferred lender arrangement.


                                S. 4140

  At the request of Mr. Blumenthal, the name of the Senator from Nevada 
(Ms. Rosen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4140, a bill to amend title 
38, United States Code, to improve the Department of Veterans Affairs 
schedule for rating disabilities, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4167

  At the request of Mr. Warnock, the names of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Mr. Scott) and the Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Tillis) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 4167, a bill to establish the Federal 
Clearinghouse on Grant Opportunities for Historically Black Colleges 
and Universities, and for other purposes.


                              S.J. RES. 16

  At the request of Mr. Cruz, the name of the Senator from Florida 
(Mrs. Moody) was added as a cosponsor of S.J. Res. 16, a joint 
resolution proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United 
States to require that the Supreme Court of the United States be 
composed of nine justices.


                              S.J. RES. 32

  At the request of Mr. Sanders, the name of the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Welch) was added as a cosponsor of S.J. Res. 32, a joint 
resolution providing for congressional disapproval of the proposed 
foreign military sale to the Government of Israel of certain defense 
articles and services.


                             S.J. RES. 112

  At the request of Ms. Warren, the name of the Senator from New York 
(Mr. Schumer) was added as a cosponsor of S.J. Res. 112, a joint 
resolution providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of 
Industry and Security of the Department of Commerce relating to ``One 
Year Suspension of Expansion of End-User Controls for Affiliates of 
Certain Listed Entities''.


                              S. RES. 604

  At the request of Mr. Markey, the name of the Senator from California 
(Mr. Schiff) was added as a cosponsor of S. Res. 604, a resolution 
recognizing that it is the duty of the Federal Government to develop 
and implement a Transgender Bill of Rights to protect and codify the 
rights of transgender and nonbinary people under the law and ensure 
their access to medical care, shelter, safety, and economic security.


                              S. RES. 627

  At the request of Mr. Scott of Florida, the name of the Senator from 
Maryland (Ms. Alsobrooks) was added as a cosponsor of S. Res. 627, a 
resolution designating March 5, 2026, as ``National Slam the Scam Day'' 
to raise awareness about pervasive scams and to prevent government 
imposter scams and other types of scams by promoting education about 
such scams.


                              S. RES. 657

  At the request of Ms. Duckworth, the name of the Senator from West 
Virginia (Mr. Justice) was added as a cosponsor of S. Res. 657, a 
resolution commending Taiwan on the 30th anniversary of its first 
direct presidential election in 1996, and expressing support for Taiwan 
in the preservation of its democratic institutions.
```
