# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 15, 2026

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 123

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 15, 2026

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, April 15, 2026; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate be 
in a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each; further, if Senator Duckworth or her designee 
makes a motion to discharge S.J. Res. 123, that the Senate vote on that 
motion at 2 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
