# TRIBUTE TO ABBEY ALLEN

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ABBEY ALLEN

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Abbey Allen, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has done 
on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. Allen is a 
graduate of Madison High School in Madison, SD. She is currently 
attending Augustana University, where she studies government/
international affairs and international studies. Ms. Allen is a 
dedicated and diligent individual who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of her internship experience. She has been a true asset to my 
office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ms. Allen for 
all of the work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
