# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 4282, S. 4283, S. 4284, S. 4285, S. 4286, S. 4287, S. 4288, S. 4289, S. 4290, S. 4291, S. 4292, S. 4293, S. 4294

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. MOODY:
       S. 4282. A bill to designate the POW/MIA Memorial and 
     Museum in Jacksonville, Florida, as the National POW/MIA 
     Memorial and Museum, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Ms. BALDWIN (for herself and Ms. Collins):
       S. 4283. A bill to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     reauthorize a public health

[[Page S1740]]

     and bio-preparedness workforce loan repayment program; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. LEE (for himself and Mr. McCormick):
       S. 4284. A bill to encourage the development and deployment 
     of nuclear energy, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mrs. BLACKBURN:
       S. 4285. A bill to ensure National Guard members performing 
     full-time National Guard duty in support of Federal law 
     enforcement operations receive benefits equivalent to those 
     provided for service during a national emergency; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND (for herself, Mr. Fetterman, Mr. 
             Murphy, Mr. Welch, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Merkley, Mr. 
             Wyden, Mr. Booker, Ms. Warren, Mr. Whitehouse, Mr. 
             Sanders, Mr. Markey, Mr. Heinrich, Mr. Schiff, Ms. 
             Alsobrooks, and Mr. Kim):
       S. 4286. A bill to amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 
     to streamline nutrition access for older adults and adults 
     with disabilities, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.
           By Mr. WYDEN (for himself and Mr. King):
       S. 4287. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to modify rules for grantor trusts; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Ms. WARREN (for herself, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Markey, 
             and Mr. Murphy):
       S. 4288. A bill to establish an Educational Equity 
     Challenge Grant program administered by the Department of 
     Education; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
           By Mr. SCHIFF:
       S. 4289. A bill to amend the Comprehensive Environmental 
     Response, Compensation, and Liability Act of 1980 to provide 
     for the consideration of climate change, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. LANKFORD (for himself and Mr. Fetterman):
       S. 4290. A bill to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     require that a court decree, court order, or other similar 
     process expressly provides for an annuity supplement payment; 
     to the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental 
     Affairs.
           By Ms. COLLINS (for herself and Mr. Warner):
       S. 4291. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to allow additional catch-up contributions for certain family 
     caregivers; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Ms. COLLINS (for herself and Mr. Warner):
       S. 4292. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to allow certain family caregivers to contribute to a Roth 
     IRA; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. CORNYN:
       S. 4293. A bill to close and dispose of Federal ownership 
     of the Cesar E. Chavez National Monument in the State of 
     California, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. CURTIS (for himself and Ms. Cortez Masto):
       S. 4294. A bill to require the Secretary of War to submit 
     an annual report to Congress that assesses the capacity of 
     the United States to fully implement the Taiwan Relations 
     Act, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.
```
