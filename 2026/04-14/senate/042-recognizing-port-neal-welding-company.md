# RECOGNIZING PORT NEAL WELDING COMPANY

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                 RECOGNIZING PORT NEAL WELDING COMPANY

●  Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, as chair of the Senate Committee on 
Small Business and Entrepreneurship, each week I recognize an 
outstanding Iowa small business that exemplifies the American 
entrepreneurial spirit. This week, it is my privilege to honor Port 
Neal Welding Company of Woodbury County, IA, as the Senate Small 
Business of the Week.
  In 1994, Dan Lee created a welding business out of his home, 
specializing in industrial fabrication of stairs, catwalks, and 
agricultural products. As Port Neal Welding undertook more regional 
projects, Lee moved the business into a new facility in 2011. The team 
also began to create custom artwork and sculptures using their steel 
products. With a team of 17 employees, including four of Dan's sons, 
the company takes pride in their high-quality craftsmanship and ability 
to fix any issue, whether something is bent, broken, or cracked.
  Dan Lee and Port Neal Welding are active in various community 
initiatives. Lee currently serves on the Westwood Community School 
District Board, as well as his church board. The business is a member 
of the Siouxland Chamber of Commerce and played a key part in 
constructing a replica of the Korean War Memorial at Siouxland Freedom 
Park. In their charitable endeavors, they support the Woodbury County 
Sheriff's Office and local fire departments and schools. The business 
is also involved in charity auctions where they offer fabricated items. 
Their role as a central figure in the community was reflected in 2023, 
when multiple candidates for the Republican Presidential nomination 
visited the business. Their work on various fronts highlights the role 
of small businesses in American towns, both economically and socially.
  It is my honor to recognize Dan Lee and the whole team at Port Neal 
Welding Company for their outstanding contributions and presence in 
their community. I look forward to their continued success and wish 
them the very best in the years to come.●
```
