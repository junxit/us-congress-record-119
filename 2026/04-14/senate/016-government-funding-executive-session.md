# Government Funding (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                           Government Funding

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I come to the floor today to tell you, 
to tell the body, and to tell the country that Republicans are going to 
fully fund Immigration and Customs Enforcement as well as Border 
Patrol, and we are going to do it through the end of President Trump's 
term in the White House, all the way through--not just to the end of 
this year, this fiscal year, the calendar year--all the way to the end 
of the time that President Trump

[[Page S1720]]

is in the White House. And we can do it without a single Democrat vote, 
and we are going to move forward without them.
  The Democrats want to defund law enforcement in America. It is 
disgraceful. It is shameful. But that is who that party has become. 
They want to end immigration enforcement in the United States. 
Republicans will never let that happen.
  Senator Schumer and the Democrats engineered this dangerous, reckless 
shutdown that we are currently living in. It is a shutdown of American 
security and American law enforcement. Now, they have done it for one 
reason: They would rather protect illegal immigrant criminals than 
protect law-abiding American citizens.
  For the last 2 months, Democrats have fought to turn America into 
what some would call a sanctuary country and what I would call a 
country that harbors and protects illegal immigrants. That is what they 
are trying to do. Those are their beliefs. They rally around the 
country to proclaim it.
  They voted to defund ICE, and they voted to defund Border Patrol--the 
very men and women who are hired to keep our Nation safe and secure. 
They tried to ban masks on ICE agents. That would allow the gangs of 
radical thugs who travel the streets at some of these rallies to 
threaten agents in their own homes, to terrorize their families.
  Those were the demands the Democrats made in the negotiations. They 
all have one thing in common: They make our Nation less safe. Clear and 
simple, they make America less safe. That is who the Democrats are, and 
that is why Republicans have rejected all of the things they have been 
demanding.
  Thanks to Republicans, immigration enforcement and border security 
will be fully funded, and they will be funded without conceding a 
single radical Democrat demand.
  Democrats have spent the last 2 months trying to handcuff ICE and 
Border Patrol agents. Republicans are going to make sure that ICE and 
Border Patrol are fully funded as long as President Trump is in the 
White House.
  So here is what comes next. I met with President Trump at the White 
House on Friday afternoon. Senator Lindsey Graham, who is chairman of 
the Budget Committee, and I met, sat, and visited with the President. 
We shared with him that Senate Republicans are not slowing down. We are 
united around a targeted, focused reconciliation bill, and we are 
moving immediately and quickly to fully fund and strengthen ICE and 
Border Patrol. We are going to do it with a simple majority right here 
in the U.S. Senate, and we are going to do it for the long term--no 
more hostage-taking by the radical Democrats. The safety and security 
of the American people are much too important for that. Senate 
Republicans intend to meet President Trump's June 1 deadline.
  The men and women of ICE and Border Patrol are American heroes. They 
protect our Nation from murderers, from drug traffickers, from rapists. 
They protect our Nation from repeat illegal immigrant criminals. These 
are the individuals who should not have been let into the country in 
the first place, but Joe Biden and the Democrats let 10 million illegal 
immigrants flood into our Nation during those 4 years.
  When Democrats obstruct this critical work, innocent Americans pay 
the price.
  Remember Sheridan Gorman. She was 18 years old, just 18 years old and 
a student in Chicago. Last month in Chicago, she was shot and killed in 
cold blood by an illegal immigrant criminal.
  Sheridan's killer entered our Nation in 2023. The Biden 
administration let him in. He was one of 10 million illegal immigrants 
allowed to enter the country. And, of course, Chicago's sanctuary city 
laws that they are proud of there shielded him from deportation. The 
city of Chicago and their mayor protected him. He turns out to be a 
murderer.
  And don't forget Richard Williams. He was that 83-year-old U.S. Air 
Force veteran. Last month, in New York City--the minority leader's 
hometown--he was shoved onto the subway tracks by an illegal immigrant 
criminal. He died of his injuries.
  His killer had a lengthy criminal record, but the sanctuary city laws 
of New York protected him. So he kept doing criminal deeds and acts 
against American people and their property.
  Sheridan Gorman deserved better. Richard Williams deserved better. 
Every American citizen and every American community deserve better than 
what the Democrats are trying to hoist upon all of us.
  This is what Republicans are fighting for. That is why we are here. 
From day one, President Trump and Republicans have fought alone to 
secure the border and to enforce the laws. Democrats responded with 
opposition, with obstruction, with extremism. Republicans will do what 
is necessary to fully fund our national security, and we will protect 
law-abiding citizens, not illegal immigrant criminals.
  The American people have long demanded secure borders and safe 
communities. President Trump and Republican leadership are going to 
continue to deliver. Senator Schumer and the Democrats may scream. They 
are not going to stop us.
  I yield the floor.
  Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
