# SAVE america Act (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                            SAVE america Act

  Mr. President, on the SAVE Act and election interference, perhaps it 
is unsurprising that an administration and a party that are so out of 
touch with the American electorate and so fearful of a November washout 
would go to such great lengths to rig the rules in their favor, but 
that is exactly what Donald Trump and the GOP are doing.
  Yesterday, the Brennan Center reported that more than half of local 
elected officials are concerned that political leaders will interfere 
in their work.
  We all go to the polls. We see these election workers. They are 
dedicated. It is an act of patriotism when they sit by the polls, count 
the votes, and administer the election fairly. But Donald Trump and the 
GOP threaten them. I would be worried, too, based on what we are seeing 
from Donald Trump's party, if I were an election official.
  Apparently, the GOP has given up on trying to win over the electorate 
by delivering results, so they are instead trying to change the 
electorate with their SAVE Act. It would kill online, mail, and motor 
registration, forcing Americans to drive sometime hours to register in 
person. That is hard and costly in the best of times but especially 
when gas is $4 a gallon. It would purge millions of Americans from the 
voter rolls. It would make it harder for married women to vote. It 
would reject the most common form of IDs Americans use to register.
  The only thing Republicans are trying to save with this legislation 
is their own skin in the next election. The only thing they are trying 
to save is their own skin because they know they are going down in 
defeat electorally.
  If the GOP spent the same time and energy trying to deliver for 
working families as it does trying to rig the rules, maybe Republicans 
would have a chance. But that is not what they are doing, and we are 
going to do everything we can to stop this evil act from ever becoming 
law.
  I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority whip.
```
