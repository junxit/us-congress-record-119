# RECOGNIZING NORTHEASTERN PENNSYLVANIA BASKETBALL ACCOMPLISHMENTS

**Speaking:** Bresnahan, Robert P. (B001327) [R-PA]

```
    RECOGNIZING NORTHEASTERN PENNSYLVANIA BASKETBALL ACCOMPLISHMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ROBERT P. BRESNAHAN, JR.

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 14, 2026

  Mr. BRESNAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with pride to recognize the 
University of Scranton Women's Basketball team, the Holy Redeemer 
Girls' Basketball team, and the Old Forge Boys' Basketball team for 
their outstanding achievements.
  These young men and women represent the very best of Northeastern 
Pennsylvania, a region built on resilience, hard work, and 
determination. The University of Scranton women captured the Landmark 
Conference Championship and made an incredible run to the NCAA National 
Championship game. While their season ended in a hard-fought battle 
against Denison, their accomplishments will not be forgotten. I also 
recognize the Holy Redeemer Royals of Wilkes-Barre, who advanced to the 
PIAA 3A Championship game, becoming the first Wyoming Valley Conference 
girls' team to reach a state final since 2000, a testament to their 
determination and belief in one another.
  I am proud to honor the Old Forge boys' basketball team, who won the 
District Two title and advanced to the state final for the first time 
in nearly 100 years. Their grit and teamwork inspired their entire 
community. To each of these athletes, I congratulate them. They have 
achieved excellence on the court and uplifted their communities along 
the way.
  Northeastern Pennsylvania stands united in pride and gratitude for 
all they have accomplished. I have no doubt their best days are still 
ahead. I thank them for representing NEPA and making us all proud.

                          ____________________
```
