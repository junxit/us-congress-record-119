# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You are our strength and song. Who is like You--majestic 
in holiness, gracious in mercy, and wondrous in mighty deeds?
  Today, give our Senators understanding to legislate responsibly, 
discerning the difference between good and evil. As they seek to govern 
in a way worthy of Your goodness, guide them by the light of Your 
truth. Lord, infuse them with Your perfect peace as they keep their 
minds on You. May they overcome cynicism with civility, hostility with 
love, and doubt with faith. Create in them a desire to work 
harmoniously together.
  And, Lord, be with our military servicemembers in harm's way.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
