                       TRIBUTE TO ROBERT STIVERS

● Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I rise today to pay tribute to a 
good friend of mine and a titan of Kentucky politics. He is the steady 
hand that has led the Kentucky State Senate as president for a record 
of more than 12 years--and counting--during which time his leadership 
has delivered exceptional wins for the Commonwealth. His name is Robert 
Stivers.
  Senator Stivers broke the record for longest serving Kentucky Senate 
leader last November, and as someone familiar with breaking a similar 
record, I am honored to recognize my friend, fellow leader, and a true 
Kentucky statesman of our time. I ask my colleagues to please join with 
me in applauding President Stivers on achieving this milestone and 
celebrating his impressive service over the years on behalf of his 
constituents and all Kentuckians.
  Robert's mother Joan graduated from Harvard and moved from 
Massachusetts to Clay County in eastern Kentucky where she met and 
married Bertram Stivers, a prominent lawyer who later served as a 
circuit judge. They were married 57 years and raised four children in 
eastern Kentucky, instilling in each of them a deep value of education 
and strong roots anchored in the mountains.
  When Robert ran for the 25th Senate District for the first time in 
1996, the Beattyville Enterprise praised him stating, ``He's made of 
the same stuff as we are . . .''. There is no doubt that Robert leads 
every single day with the interests of his constituents at the 
forefront of his decisions. He is a proud graduate of the University of 
Kentucky and the University of Louisville--like me--and his legislative 
work shows his understanding of how critically important these 
universities are to our Commonwealth's success.
  After being elected to the Kentucky State Senate, he quickly made a 
name for himself working on important legislation and building 
relationships within his conference and across the aisle. This 
diligence earned him the trust of his colleagues who elected him to the 
position of majority floor leader in 2008. Robert held this position 
until being elected senate president in 2013. His accomplishments are 
numerous, and Kentuckians across the Commonwealth have undoubtedly felt 
his impact.
  Senator Stivers has led the charge on combatting drug abuse and 
maintains an unwavering commitment to helping find solutions to this 
issue that plagues his constituents and so many other Kentucky 
residents. He has paved the way for increased cancer research funding 
and operations at the University of Kentucky Markey Cancer Center. In 
the most recent State budget, President Stivers helped UK secure $20 
million, and previously, he helped them acquire designation as a 
Comprehensive Cancer Center, which allowed increased access to Federal 
funding and attracted top talent in the medical research field. 
President Stivers' work in these spaces has improved thousands of lives 
and continues to set Kentucky up for future success in healthcare.

[[Page S1045]]

  Robert is a champion for Kentucky businesses, tirelessly pushing for 
significant incentives that attract investment to the Commonwealth. 
These include lowering the State income tax, providing tax credits to 
the entertainment industry, and a host of other policies, all of which 
have helped foster a business-friendly atmosphere in Kentucky. And it 
is worth noting that all of President Stivers' legislative wins have 
been accompanied by an impressive stewardship of taxpayer dollars, 
ensuring Kentuckians are being served by good government and not losing 
their hard-earned dollars to wasteful spending.
  Kentucky's bourbon industry has President Stivers as a close ally in 
the legislature. He helped change the State tax code to ease costs for 
the bourbon industry and change the flow of revenue so that our 
signature industry's success drives other sectors of Kentucky's 
economy, including education. Economic development in Kentucky is 
rapidly increasing, and when we search for those to thank, we should 
look no further than Robert Stivers.
  In his role, he has also worked to elevate Kentucky's profile in 
national legislative organizations. He has served as chair of the 
Southern Legislative Conference, chair of the Council of State 
Governments, and as chair of the Senate Presidents Forum. In 2015, he 
was recognized by Governing Magazine as Public Official of the Year.
  President Stivers leads with integrity every day, and while his 
portfolio proves an incredibly successful career, Robert's character is 
what distinguishes him the most. As a good listener and collaborator, 
he helps resolve disputes among his colleagues to make sure that minor 
disagreements refrain from blocking monumental progress. This is what 
it takes to make things happen, and Robert exemplifies this type of 
leadership all the time.
  As a prudent advocate for Kentucky, Robert has been an invaluable 
partner in my work in the U.S. Senate. I have always seen it as my duty 
to ensure Kentucky punches above its weight in securing Federal 
funding, and I have had the pleasure of working with Senator Stivers on 
these efforts for many years. I am grateful for Robert's friendship and 
his steadfast leadership in the Kentucky State Senate. He is a trusted 
adviser, and I am honored to work with him on behalf of our 
constituents.
  There is no doubt that Robert's work has made Kentucky a better place 
and has set our Commonwealth up for tremendous success. I know his 
family, and especially his four children, are incredibly proud of what 
he has been able to accomplish. So once again, I ask my colleagues to 
join me in honoring Robert Stivers for becoming the longest serving 
State senate leader in Kentucky history.●
