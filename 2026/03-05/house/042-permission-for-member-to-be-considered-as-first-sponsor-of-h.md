# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 537

**Speaking:** Magaziner, Seth (M001223) [D-RI]

**Measures:** H.R. 537

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 537

  Mr. MAGAZINER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 537, INCREASE 
Housing Affordability Act, a bill originally introduced by 
Representative Sherrill of New Jersey, for the purpose of adding 
cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Rhode Island?
  There was no objection.
```
