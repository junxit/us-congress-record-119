# Text of Senate Amendment 4353

**Speaking:** Britt, Katie Boyd (B001319) [R-AL]

```
  SA 4353. Mrs. BRITT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
her to the bill H.R. 4553, making appropriations for energy and water 
development and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2026, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       Strike all after the enacting clause and insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 101. The Continuing Appropriations Act, 2026 (division 
     A of Public Law 119-37) is further amended by striking the 
     date specified in section 106 (3) and inserting ``March 27, 
     2026''.
       Sec. 102. For the purposes of the Continuing Appropriations 
     Act, 2026 (division A of Public Law 119-37), the time covered 
     by such Act shall be considered to include the period which 
     began on or about February 14, 2026, during which there 
     occurred a lapse in appropriations.
```
