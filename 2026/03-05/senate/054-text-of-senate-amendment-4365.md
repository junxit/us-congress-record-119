# Text of Senate Amendment 4365

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
  SA 4365. Mrs. BLACKBURN submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the supply of 
housing in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. BANKRUPTCY TREATMENT OF PRIMARY RESIDENCES THAT ARE 
                   NOT REAL PROPERTY.

       (a) In General.--Section 1322 of title 11, United States 
     Code, is amended--
       (1) in subsection (b)(2), by striking ``real property'' and 
     inserting ``property''; and
       (2) in subsection (c)(2), by striking ``real property'' and 
     inserting ``property''.
       (b) Reorganization.--Section 1123(b)(5) of title 11, United 
     States Code, is amended by striking ``real property'' and 
     inserting ``property''.
       (c) Liquidation.--Section 722 of title 11, United States 
     Code, is amended by adding at the end the following: ``This 
     section shall not apply to tangible personal property that is 
     the debtor's principal residence.''.
                                 ______
```
