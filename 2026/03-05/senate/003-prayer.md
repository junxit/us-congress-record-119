# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, Your hand is open wide to satisfy the needs of every 
living creature. Make us always thankful for Your prevailing 
providence, matchless wisdom, and precious love. Enable us to remember 
the account we must one day give to You.
  Lord, empower the Members of this body to be faithful stewards of 
Your good gifts. May they use their influence and power to bring glory 
to Your Name in all the Earth. Inspire them to live lives that provide 
exemplary models of excellence, integrity, and faith for others to 
follow.
  And Lord, protect our military people in harm's way.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
