# Text of Senate Amendment 4345

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

```
  SA 4345. Mrs. SHAHEEN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 4308 proposed by Mr. Scott of South Carolina (for 
himself and Ms. Warren) to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the 
supply of housing in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       In section 301(d)(2)(C), in the matter preceding clause 
     (i), insert ``, consistent with paragraphs (1) and (2) of 
     section 413(b) of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 
     2007 (42 U.S.C. 17071(b))'' after ``shall''.
                                 ______
```
