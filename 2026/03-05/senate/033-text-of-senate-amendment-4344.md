# Text of Senate Amendment 4344

**Speaking:** Cruz, Ted (C001098) [R-TX]

```
  SA 4344. Mr. CRUZ submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 4308 proposed by Mr. Scott of South Carolina (for himself 
and Ms. Warren) to the bill H.R. 6644, a bill to increase the supply of 
housing in America, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the end of section 210, add the following:
       (e) Limitation on Funds Based on Property Tax Revenue 
     Growth.--No funds made available under this section may be 
     obligated or expended for an eligible entity unless the 
     eligible entity submits to the Secretary an annual 
     certification that the aggregate increase in total property 
     tax revenues collected by the eligible entity during the most 
     recently completed fiscal year did not exceed 2 percent over 
     the preceding fiscal year.
                                 ______
```
