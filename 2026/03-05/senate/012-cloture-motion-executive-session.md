# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 655, the following named officer 
     for appointment in the United States Army to the grade 
     indicated while assigned to a position of importance and 
     responsibility under title 10, U.S.C., section 601: to be 
     General, Lt. Gen. Joshua M. Rudd.
         John Thune, Bernie Moreno, John Barrasso, Tim Sheehy, 
           John R. Curtis, John Boozman, Ted Cruz, Tommy 
           Tuberville, Jon A. Husted, Marsha Blackburn, Ted Budd, 
           David McCormick, Pete Ricketts, Todd Young, Cindy Hyde-
           Smith, Ashley B. Moody, Steve Daines.

                          ____________________
```
