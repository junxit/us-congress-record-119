Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Agriculture: Full Committee concluded a markup on H.R. 
7567, the ``Farm, Food, and National Security Act of 2026''. H.R. 7567 
was ordered reported, as amended.
INSPECTORS GENERAL FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND THE 
DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Transportation, Housing 
And Urban Development, and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled 
``Inspectors General for the Department of Transportation and the 
Department of Housing and Urban Development''. Testimony was heard from 
Mitch Behm, Deputy Inspector General, Department of Transportation; and 
Brian Harrison, Acting Inspector General, Department of Housing and 
Urban Development.
MEMBER DAY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled 
``Member Day''. Testimony was heard from Representatives Luttrell, 
Gillen, Wasserman Schultz, and Edwards.
MEMBER DAY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies held a hearing entitled ``Member Day''. Testimony was 
heard from Representatives Titus, Wasserman Schultz, and Radewagen.
U.S. DEFENSE STRATEGY AND POSTURE
Committee on Armed Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``U.S. Defense Strategy and Posture''. Testimony was heard from 
Elbridge Colby, Under Secretary of Defense for Policy, Office of the 
Secretary of Defense, Department of Defense.

H.R. 7720, the ``Child Care Payment Integrity and Fraud Accountability

7722, H.R. 7723, H.R. 7724, H.R. 7725, H.R. 7677, and H.R. 7726 were 
ordered reported, as amended.

7757, the ``Kids Internet Digital Safety Act''; H.R. 2657, the

FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS

and Opportunities for Financial Institutions''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
THE ROLE OF SELF-REGULATORY ORGANIZATIONS IN THE U.S. MARKETS: 
EXAMINING FINANCIAL INDUSTRY REGULATORY AUTHORITY AND MUNICIPAL 
SECURITIES RULEMAKING BOARD
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Capital Markets held a 
hearing entitled ``The Role of Self-Regulatory Organizations in the 
U.S. Markets: Examining FINRA and the MSRB''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.

Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Advancing National Security through Public Diplomacy''. Testimony was

D'Esposito, Inspector General, Department of Labor; and public 
witnesses.
