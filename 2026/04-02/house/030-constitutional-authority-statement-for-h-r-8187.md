# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 8187

**Measures:** H.R. 8187

```
           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 8187.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to provide for the common defense 
     and to promote the progress of science and useful arts, and 
     to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution these powers, pursuant to Article I, Section 8, 
     Clauses 1, 8, and 18 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
```
