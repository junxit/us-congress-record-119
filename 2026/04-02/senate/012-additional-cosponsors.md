# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.Res. 655, S. 4134

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 4134

  At the request of Mr. Ricketts, the names of the Senator from Iowa 
(Mr. Grassley), the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer), the Senator 
from Texas (Mr. Cornyn), the Senator from Wyoming (Ms. Lummis), the 
Senator from Montana (Mr. Daines), the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch) 
and the Senator from Alabama (Mrs. Britt) were added as cosponsors of 
S. 4134, a bill to amend chapter 131 of title 5, United States Code, to 
require certain restrictions on stocks for Members of Congress and 
their spouses and dependents, and for other purposes.


                              S. RES. 655

  At the request of Mr. Ricketts, the names of the Senator from Iowa 
(Mr. Grassley), the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer), the Senator 
from Texas (Mr. Cornyn), the Senator from Montana (Mr. Daines), the 
Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch) and the Senator from Alabama (Mrs. 
Britt) were withdrawn as cosponsors of S. Res. 655, a resolution 
expressing support for the annual designation of the first Saturday 
after the Spring Equinox as ``National Day of Play'' .
```
