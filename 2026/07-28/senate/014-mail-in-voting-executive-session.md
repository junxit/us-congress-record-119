# Mail-in Voting (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Mail-in Voting

  Mr. President, now on mail-in voting, Trump's desperation--this is my 
opening statement since we just opened at 5 o'clock.
  Trump's desperation to avoid accountability at the polls this 
November grows every passing day. Yesterday, Trump begged the Supreme 
Court to help him block mail-in ballots in every corner of the country.
  Hey, Donald, did you also tell the Court that you yourself voted by 
mail just a few months ago? Everyone knows that mail-in voting is safe 
and secure, and apparently so do you.
  It is absurd. Trump is pleading for the highest Court in the land to 
make it harder for millions of Americans to vote using the same method 
he used to submit his ballot earlier this year.
  We all know why. Trump sees the writing on the wall for November and 
thinks he can escape accountability from voters. Trump knows just how 
bad a bind he is in. Trump can't defeat the crippling costs. Trump 
can't defend the crippling costs working families see every time they 
go to the grocery store or the gas pump. Trump can't defend the rampant 
corruption that has allowed his family to make billions of dollars off 
the American people. And Trump can't defend the unrelenting chaos he 
has unleashed in American streets.
  But Trump can't defend himself from Americans' wrath at the ballot 
box in November either. Senate Democrats are working around the clock 
to make sure that every eligible American voter can cast their ballot 
without interference, intimidation, or obstruction.
  You can hear in every lawsuit, Executive order, and desperate plea to 
the Court: Trump is afraid of the voters, not of voter fraud.
  I yield the floor.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from New Hampshire.
```
