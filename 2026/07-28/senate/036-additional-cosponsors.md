# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 65, S. 119, S.J.Res. 181, S. 618, S. 691, S. 1169, S. 1829, S. 2006, S. 2195, S. 2562, S. 2813, S. 2903, S. 2955, S. 3085, S. 3267, S. 3332, S. 3524, S. 3940, S. 4117, S. 4172, S. 4318, S. 4429, S. 4456, S. 4641, S. 4775, S. 4784, S. 4801, S. 4814, S. 4905, S. 4942, S. 4944, S. 4950, S. 5126

                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 65

  At the request of Mr. Cornyn, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 65, a bill to 
allow reciprocity for the carrying of certain concealed firearms.


                                 S. 119

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 119, a bill to 
amend title 18, United States Code, to discontinue the collection by 
the Federal Government of firearm transaction records of discontinued 
firearms businesses, to require the destruction of such already 
collected records, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 618

  At the request of Mr. Tuberville, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 618, a bill 
to prohibit the purchase or lease of agricultural land in the United 
States by persons associated with certain foreign governments, and for 
other purposes.


                                 S. 691

  At the request of Mr. Young, the name of the Senator from Michigan

[[Page S4298]]

(Mr. Peters) was added as a cosponsor of S. 691, a bill to amend the 
Tariff Act of 1930 to improve the administration of antidumping and 
countervailing duty laws, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1169

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1169, a bill to 
prohibit State excise taxes on firearms and ammunition manufacturers 
and dealers.


                                S. 1829

  At the request of Mr. Hawley, the names of the Senator from West 
Virginia (Mrs. Capito) and the Senator from Rhode Island (Mr. Reed) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 1829, a bill to combat the sexual 
exploitation of children by supporting victims and promoting 
accountability and transparency by the tech industry.


                                S. 2006

  At the request of Ms. Warren, the name of the Senator from Rhode 
Island (Mr. Reed) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2006, a bill to amend 
title 10, United States Code, to prohibit discrimination in the Armed 
Forces on the basis of gender identity, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2195

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the name of the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Scott) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2195, a bill to award a 
Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to the brave women who served 
in World War II as members of the U.S. Army Nurse Corps and U.S. Navy 
Nurse Corps.


                                S. 2562

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2562, a bill to 
promote fair and equal treatment by the Administrator of the Small 
Business Administration with respect to certain firearms industry 
applicants for assistance, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2813

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the name of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2813, a bill to 
amend chapter 44 of title 18, United States Code, to prohibit capacity-
based restrictions on firearm magazines, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2903

  At the request of Ms. Murkowski, the names of the Senator from 
Mississippi (Mr. Wicker), the Senator from Massachusetts (Ms. Warren) 
and the Senator from Colorado (Mr. Bennet) were added as cosponsors of 
S. 2903, a bill to amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
1974 to require a group health plan or health insurance coverage 
offered in connection with such a plan to provide an exceptions process 
for any medication step therapy protocol, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2955

  At the request of Mr. Banks, the name of the Senator from Florida 
(Mrs. Moody) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2955, a bill to amend title 
18, United States Code, to establish Federal penalties for the knowing 
and intentional administration of any abortion-inducing drug to a woman 
without her informed consent, if the abortion-inducing drug has been 
shipped or transported in interstate commerce, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3085

  At the request of Mr. Risch, the names of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Ms. Graham) and the Senator from Tennessee (Mrs. Blackburn) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 3085, a bill to ensure that certain 
operations, functions, and services of the Federal Government relating 
to enforcement of firearms laws and firearm export licensing continue 
during a lapse in appropriations.


                                S. 3267

Social Security Act to provide for Medicare coverage of blood-based

                                S. 3524

title 54, United States Code, to modify certain cost-sharing

                                S. 3940

                                S. 4429
