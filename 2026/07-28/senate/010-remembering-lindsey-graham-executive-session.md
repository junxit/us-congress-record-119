# Remembering Lindsey Graham (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Britt, Katie Boyd (B001319) [R-AL]

```
                       Remembering Lindsey Graham

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, today at the U.S. Capitol and the 
National Cathedral, at those places, we honored the life of Senator 
Lindsey Graham.
  Over the past few weeks, we have mourned Senator Graham's untimely 
passing. Here on the Senate floor and in committees, I and my 
colleagues have spent time honoring his life and recalling fond 
memories.
  Senator Graham loved this country. His forceful and quick-witted 
presence continues to be missed.
  Barbara and I continue praying for Senator Graham's family and newly 
appointed Senator Darline Graham.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. BRITT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
