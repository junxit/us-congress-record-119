# BUDGET RECONCILIATION

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                         BUDGET RECONCILIATION

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, if you want to understand Republicans' 
priorities right now, just look at the $72 billion reconciliation bill 
they revealed last week. The Republican bill would pump tens of 
billions of dollars into ICE and Border Patrol without any guardrails 
to prevent these rogue Agencies from continuing to unleash violence on 
American streets.
  But that is not all. To add insult to injury, the Republican bill 
charges American taxpayers $1 billion to build Trump's gilded ballroom, 
while including zero dollars to help Americans with rising costs.
  Their billion-dollar bill has zero dollars to help Americans deal 
with groceries, and their prices are going up. Gas prices are going up. 
Zero dollars to bring down Americans' healthcare, housing, and 
electricity bills.
  At a time when Americans are struggling to put food on the table, 
Republicans say, ``Let them eat cake,'' and demand American taxpayers 
build Trump a palace, while they are at it.
  Keep in mind, Donald Trump said, just a few months ago, that ``not 
one penny is being used from the Federal Government'' for the 
ballroom--``not one penny,'' said Trump.
  Well, give me a break. He has put a billion dollars in the budget for 
it. This staggering waste of taxpayer dollars has nothing--nothing--to 
do with security and everything to do with Trump's ego.
  How long before Trump asks for his name to be emblazoned in gold on 
the side of the ballroom?
  The American people are calling out for help, but these ballroom 
Republicans have a constituency of one: Donald Trump's ego. That is a 
disgrace.
  Democrats will use every tool in our arsenal to fight this bill. We 
will bring challenges. We will bring challenges under the Byrd rule. We 
will offer floor amendments. We will force Republicans to take vote 
after vote, showing that instead of helping working families, 
Republicans are forcing Americans to pay for Trump's billionaire 
ballroom.
  Americans don't want this ballroom blitz. They want Congress to bring 
down costs.

                          ____________________
```
