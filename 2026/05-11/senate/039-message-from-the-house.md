                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 2 
U.S.C. 2062, and the order of the House of January 3, 2025, the Speaker 
appoints the following individuals to the Advisory Board for the House 
of Representatives Child Care Center: Mr. John Byers of Alexandria, 
Virginia, Ms. Rachel Collins of Washington, D.C., Ms. Giulia Leganski 
of Potomac, Maryland, Mr. Derek Harley of Avon Lake, Ohio, Ms. Lauren 
Hodge of Alexandria, Virginia, Mr. Thomas McAllister of Washington, 
D.C., Ms. Anna McCormack of Alexandria, Virginia, and Mr. Kevin O'Keefe 
of Falls Church, Virginia.

the National Defense Authorization Act for fiscal year 2026 (Public Law 
119-60), and the order of the House of January 3, 2025, the Speaker 
appoints the following individual on the part of the House of 
Representatives to the Commission on the National Defense Strategy: Mr. 
Robert O'Brien of Heber City, Utah.
