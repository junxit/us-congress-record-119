# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 98, S. 1020, S. 4465

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on April 30, 2026, she had 
presented to the President of the United States the following enrolled 
bills:

       S. 98. An act to require the Federal Communications 
     Commission to establish a vetting process for prospective 
     applicants for high-cost universal service program funding.
       S. 1020. An act to require the Federal Energy Regulatory 
     Commission to extend the time period during which licensees 
     are required to commence construction of certain hydropower 
     projects.
       S. 4465. An act to amend the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 to 
     extend the authorities of title VII of the Foreign 
     Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
