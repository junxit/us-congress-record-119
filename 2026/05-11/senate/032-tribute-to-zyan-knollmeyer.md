# TRIBUTE TO ZYAN KNOLLMEYER

**Speaking:** Schmitt, Eric (S001227) [R-MO]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ZYAN KNOLLMEYER

  Mr. SCHMITT. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize Zyan Knollmeyer 
of Jefferson City, MO, a 2026 graduate whose wrestling career showcases 
American resilience. Born in the U.S. Virgin Islands, Zyan faced 
hardship at an early age with the tragic loss of his mother and spent 
time in Hawaii's foster care system. However, God had greater plans for 
him. Through several dedicated case managers, Zyan found his closest 
relatives in Missouri, who showed incredible compassion and quickly 
adopted him.
  Not long after arriving in the Show-Me State, Zyan had his first 
encounter with wrestling--and wasn't impressed. But with the guidance 
of a phenomenal coach who helped him discover his own exceptional 
strength and determination, Zyan quickly fell in love with the sport 
and committed himself to mastering his skills. That commitment led him 
to become the varsity heavyweight for Helias Catholic High School in 
his sophomore year.
  Over the years, Zyan has proven to be one of Missouri's most 
promising young athletes, finishing a remarkable season undefeated at 
40-0 and claiming both the 2026 Missouri State Championship and NFHSC 
National Championship in his class. His dedication to honing his craft 
is truly remarkable. Zyan has not become complacent, instead taking 
every opportunity to practice new techniques to ensure he is always at 
the top of his game. He is the kind of young man who earns respect, 
leads by example, represents his community with pride, and takes time 
to encourage younger athletes who look up to him.
  Zyan has a bright future ahead, and as he looks forward to 
graduating, any university would be lucky to have him represent their 
wrestling program. His journey is a powerful ``only in America'' story, 
and I am proud to call him a fellow Missourian.●

                          ____________________
```
