# HONORING "CANTO A LA LIBERTAD" AND THE MISSION OF THE SANTUARIO DEL COBRE

**Speaking:** Gimenez, Carlos A. (G000593) [R-FL]

```
 HONORING ``CANTO A LA LIBERTAD'' AND THE MISSION OF THE SANTUARIO DEL 
                                 COBRE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 11, 2026

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the ``Canto a la 
Libertad'' concert and the inspiring mission it supports--the Santuario 
de la Virgen de la Caridad del Cobre in Santiago de Cuba.
  This effort, led by faithful members of our South Florida community, 
reflects the deep spiritual bond between the Cuban people and Our Lady 
of Charity, the patroness of Cuba and a symbol of hope, resilience, and 
freedom.
  At the Santuario del Cobre, Father Rogelio Dean Puerta and his team 
serve some of the most vulnerable, providing nourishment, care, and 
spiritual guidance to children, the elderly, and families in need. 
Despite the challenges facing the Cuban people, this sanctuary 
continues to stand as a beacon of faith and compassion.
  The ``Canto a la Libertad'' concert is a powerful act of solidarity. 
It brings together our exile community and supporters of freedom to 
provide direct assistance to those on the island who need it most, 
while keeping alive the call for a free and democratic Cuba.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing the organizers, 
supporters, and all those contributing to this noble cause. Their 
generosity and faith are making a real difference in the lives of many. 
May we continue to stand with the Cuban people, support those working 
to alleviate suffering, and never waver in our commitment to freedom.

                          ____________________
```
