# HONORING CHARLES VICTOR LAMPE, MD, FACC

**Speaking:** Gooden, Lance (G000589) [R-TX]

```
                HONORING CHARLES VICTOR LAMPE, MD, FACC

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LANCE GOODEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 11, 2026

  Mr. GOODEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 
distinguished career and accomplishments of Dr. Charles Victor Lampe, a 
devoted cardiologist and respected leader in the Dallas, Texas 
community. A Fellow of the American College of Cardiology, he now 
serves with Texas Health Heart and Vascular Specialists at multiple 
locations across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, specializing in 
electrophysiology and cardiovascular disease.
  Dr. Lampe began his medical journey in 1994 at the University of 
Texas Southwestern Medical School, one of the Nation's premier 
institutions. He went on to complete his internship at Rush University 
in Illinois before returning to UT Southwestern to complete his 
residency in internal medicine, his fellowship in cardiovascular 
disease, and his fellowship in clinical cardiac electrophysiology. Dr. 
Lampe is further distinguished through academic contribution, authoring 
a paper on the mechanism of supraventricular tachycardia, which 
underscores his commitment to advancing our understanding of complex 
cardiac conditions.
  Physicians like Dr. Lampe often serve without public recognition, yet 
their work profoundly shapes the vitality of our communities. It is my 
privilege to formally acknowledge his many contributions to the people 
of East Texas and to the field of cardiology.

                          ____________________
```
