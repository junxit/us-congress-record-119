# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, You have made yourself known to us, and yet You remain 
beyond our comprehension. Despite all our attempts to seek answers, 
master information, and solve even our most mundane problems, we come, 
more often than not, face to face with our finitude, our fallibility, 
and our failures.
  Were we honest with ourselves, we would acknowledge that what we are 
searching for are answers that can only be found in You; that within 
our hearts is an awareness, an echo of You, which we long to see, hear, 
and understand more fully.
  While You may remain a mystery, You are never far from us. As the 
psalmist professed, ``You hem us in, behind and before, and lay your 
hand upon us.''
  Though such knowledge is too wonderful for us to fully attain, 
nonetheless, You are not unknown to us. In Your hands are our lives; in 
Your care is the living of this day.
  In Your sovereign name, we are bold to lay before You all that we do 
not yet know, entrusting it into Your keeping.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
