# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 93, S.Res. 189, S.Res. 285

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-71. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Louisiana, relative to Senate Resolution No. 
     93, to ensure timely and full funding aligned with the 
     National Defense Authorization Act in order to support the 
     readiness, stability, and well-being of United States 
     military personnel and their families.; which was referred to 
     the Committee on Armed Services.
       ML-72. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Wyoming, relative to House Joint Resolution HJ0002, 
     requesting Congress to introduce a bill and enact law to 
     increase the state of Wyoming's share of federal mineral 
     royalties from fifty percent (50%) to eighty-seven and one-
     half percent (87.5%).; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.
       ML-73. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Wyoming, relative to Senate Joint Resolution SJ0009, 
     requesting Congress recognize the protection of Wyoming's 
     freedom of access to public lands, to allow responsible 
     development of resources, recreation, agriculture, hunting, 
     fishing, trapping, and conservation under multiple-use 
     frameworks, to protect local participation in land management 
     decisions, and to keep public lands available to the people 
     of Wyoming.; which was referred to the Committee on Natural 
     Resources.
       ML-74. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Wyoming, relative to Senate Joint Resolution SJ0001, 
     requesting Congress to introduce a bill and enact law to 
     amend the federal Mineral Leasing Act to authorize the state 
     of Wyoming to administer and manage mineral leasing on 
     federal lands located in Wyoming.; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
       ML-75. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the Commonwealth 
     of Kentucky, relative to Senate Resolution No. 189, 
     expressing support for the Ohio River Restoration Program Act 
     to advance clean water, economic development, community 
     stability, and expanded recreation opportunities in the 
     Commonwealth of Kentucky.; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
       ML-76. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of Utah, relative to Joint Resolution 18, urging 
     Congress to eliminate marriage penalties in federal law.; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Ways and Means.
       ML-77. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the Commonwealth 
     of Kentucky, relative to Senate Resolution No. 285, 
     recognizing the filing of the Major Richard Star Act, a bill 
     exploring an opportunity for veterans with disabilities to 
     receive their full United States Department of Defense 
     retirement pay and United States Department of Veterans 
     Affairs disability compensation without offset.; which was 
     referred jointly to the Committees on Armed Services and 
     Veterans' Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
