# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, we draw near to You. Draw near to us.
  We dare to come into Your presence when so much has caused us to 
wander farther and farther from You. We confess that we have allowed 
ourselves to be distracted by our sense of what is important and 
delayed by things on which we have chosen to spend our time.
  Should You, in Your mercy, receive us back into fellowship with You, 
we would repent of our sin and look to You for forgiveness from our 
wrongdoing. Should You, in Your steadfast love, accept us again into 
communion with You, we would confess the fickleness of our hearts and 
yield to You to purify our unrighteousness.
  Lord, from this day forth, go before us and remain with us. Never 
leave or forsake us. And to this promise, then, may we remain faithful, 
without fear or despair.
  In humility we offer ourselves to You, and in the strength of Your 
name we dare to pray.
  Amen.
```
