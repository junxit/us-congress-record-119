# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

```
  APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS 
                                FREEDOM

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to section 201(b) of the International Religious 
Freedom Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6431) and the order of the House of

to the Commission on International Religious Freedom for a term

  Mr. Theogene Anthony Mills III, Sunshine, Louisiana, to succeed Mrs.
```
