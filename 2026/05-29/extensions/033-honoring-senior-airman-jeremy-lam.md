# HONORING SENIOR AIRMAN JEREMY LAM

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
                   HONORING SENIOR AIRMAN JEREMY LAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 29, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Senior Airman Jeremy Lam as a 
distinguished leader in Central Florida for Military Appreciation 
Month. Senior Airman Jeremy Lam served in the United States Air Force 
for eight years, two months, and nine days as a Far East Chinese 
Mandarin Crypto linguist for the National Security Agency at Fort 
George Meade, Maryland, from October 12, 1993, to December 21, 2001.
  Prior to his military career, he graduated in the top 10 percent of 
his class from Dr. Phillips High School on May 4, 1993. He got involved 
in the Air Force Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps and the German 
Club and played violin in the orchestra. He is also a Troop 6 Eagle 
Scout from St. Luke's United Methodist Church, where he currently 
serves as a committee member, Merit Badge coordinator, Eagle Scout 
mentor, and Eagle Scout

[[Page E503]]

Project Committee member for the Eagle Empire District Central Florida 
Council.
  He is a department chair for Mission and Evangelism for the Orlando 
Chinese Baptist Church under Pastor Sean Ma and is an avid volunteer 
with the Disabled American Veterans Chapter 16 as a chapter service 
officer, helping over 400 veterans process their VA claims for free 
under the mentorship of Commander Laymond Rose. He also volunteers at 
the FDVA Alywn C. Cashe Nursing Home, where he conducts monthly bingo. 
He is also a member of the Wounded Warrior Project and serves as the 
team lead for snacks for Soldiers' Angels.
```
