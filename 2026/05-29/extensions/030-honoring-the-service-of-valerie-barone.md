# HONORING THE SERVICE OF VALERIE BARONE

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING THE SERVICE OF VALERIE BARONE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 29, 2026

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Valerie Barone 
as she retires from the City of Concord.
  Valerie has worked diligently to support Concord residents and city 
staff in her role as City Manager. Throughout her career, she has 
demonstrated her steadfast commitment to service, starting her career 
as a Community Development Specialist with the U.S. Peace Corps, 
serving in the Philippines. In 2008, she joined the City of Concord as 
Assistant City Manager. She was appointed as interim City Manager in 
2012 and began serving as City Manager in 2013.
  During her tenure, Valerie worked tirelessly to build a vibrant 
community in the City of Concord and guided the city as it continued to 
grow and develop. She was critical in promoting the city's economic 
growth, helping to establish Visit Concord, turning the Toyota Pavilion 
into a revenue generator for the region, helping the city to invest $27 
million provided through the American Rescue Plan to strengthen the 
city's infrastructure and nonprofits, overseeing the development of the 
Veranda shopping center, and fostering the city's cultural landscape by 
preserving Concord's Music and Market concerts and public art 
installations through Creative Concord. Additionally, Valerie 
championed numerous regional initiatives, allowing Concord to serve as 
a leader in programs such as the Central County Family Justice Center, 
the County's CORE Program, and the County's A3 program. Her leadership 
was critical as she guided the city through the COVID-19 pandemic and 
budget challenges, helping the city to stabilize its budget and driving 
infrastructure improvements throughout the city.
  Valerie's leadership has been key in improving quality of life for 
Concord residents and helping the city to thrive. Her work has left an 
indelible impact on her neighbors and peers. Please join me in 
congratulating Valerie Barone on her retirement after 38 years of 
devoted public service.
```
