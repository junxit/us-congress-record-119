# HONORING ENRIQUE ACOSTA GONZALEZ

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
                    HONORING ENRIQUE ACOSTA GONZALEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 29, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Enrique Acosta Gonzalez as a 
distinguished leader in Central Florida for Military Appreciation 
Month. Enrique Acosta Gonzalez is the CEO of Triad Leadership 
Solutions, LLC, and has served as an executive, veteran and advocate, 
board member, non-profit administrator, speaker, mentor, coach, and 
avid volunteer. Over the past 25 years, Enrique has held numerous 
executive, advisory, and senior positions while serving in both the 
United States Navy and the civilian sector. Through education and 
training, he helped strengthen organizations by providing sound 
decision support and fostering open communication at all levels. 
Enrique also helped formulate and implement policies on morale, 
welfare, job satisfaction, discipline, and personnel training.
  He is the recipient of the Meritorious Service Medal, Navy and Marine 
Corps Commendation Medal, and Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal. 
He was named the 2013, 2014, and 2017 Federal Mentor of the Year, and 
is the recipient of the inaugural 2014 Chief John Dungan, Jr., 
Oceanographer of the Navy Award for exceptional leadership. He was also 
awarded the Arnold Fletcher Award and inducted into the SALUTE national 
honors society for his academic achievements.
  He is a multi-award-winning podcaster with shows focusing on 
leadership development and career success. He was named the Active 
Duty, Veteran & Military Spouse Entrepreneur Awards' Media Professional 
of the Year by The Rosie Network and No. 3 of the Top 50 Leadership 
Podcasts. His down-to-earth approach to leadership has gained him 
notoriety as one of the most sought-after leadership trainers.
  Enrique has been an active mentor to transitioning service members 
for nine years as part of the Camaraderie Foundation's Military 
Leadership Program (MLP). His impact on our Veteran community has been 
far-reaching, as he serves as a keynote speaker, trainer, and host for 
military-centered activities in Florida's 9th Congressional District. 
He also provides complimentary dress shirts to Veterans in preparation 
for job interviews through his podcast giveback program and is serving 
as the inaugural Mr. Military Florida for 2026.
```
