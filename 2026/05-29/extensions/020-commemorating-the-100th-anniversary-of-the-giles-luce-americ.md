# COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE GILES-LUCE AMERICAN LEGION POST 364 IN WINNECONNE, WISCONSIN

**Speaking:** Grothman, Glenn (G000576) [R-WI]

```
 COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE GILES-LUCE AMERICAN LEGION 
                   POST 364 IN WINNECONNE, WISCONSIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GLENN GROTHMAN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 29, 2026

  Mr. GROTHMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to commend and congratulate Giles-
Luce American Legion Post 364 in Winneconne on its upcoming 100th 
Anniversary, which will be celebrated on June 14th. This milestone 
reflects a century of unwavering commitment to the community, and is a 
testament to the enduring spirit of service demonstrated by its 
members.
  As we reflect on the past 100 years, it is clear that the Giles-Luce 
American Legion Post 364 has been a cornerstone of the Winneconne 
community. Through countless local charitable events, outreach 
programs, and ongoing dedication, the members have enriched the lives 
of veterans and their families, fostering a sense of unity and 
belonging. Their efforts have preserved important traditions and 
instilled a spirit of service that continues to inspire new 
generations.
  The legacy of Giles-Luce American Legion is not measured solely by 
its accomplishments, but also by the deep respect and gratitude it has 
earned from those it has helped. Their dedication sets a high standard 
for what it means to serve with honor, compassion, and integrity. I am 
proud to recognize their outstanding contributions and wish them 
continued success in the years to come. May their commitment to service 
and community continue to inspire future generations, and may their 
impact be felt for many years to come.
  Once again, I congratulate the Giles-Luce American Legion Post 364 on 
its 100th Anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
