# ADJOURNMENT FROM WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 25, 2026, TO THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2026; ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2026, TO MONDAY, MARCH 2, 2026; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM MONDAY, MARCH 2, 2026, TO...

**Speaking:** Kiley, Kevin (K000401) [R-CA]

```
 ADJOURNMENT FROM WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 25, 2026, TO THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 
  26, 2026; ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2026, TO MONDAY, 
MARCH 2, 2026; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM MONDAY, MARCH 2, 2026, TO TUESDAY, 
                             MARCH 3, 2026

when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow; that when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet 
at 9 a.m. on Monday, March 2, 2026; and when the House adjourns on that 
day, it adjourn to meet at noon on Tuesday, March 3, 2026, for morning-
hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
