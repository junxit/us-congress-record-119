# Remembering Eden Holguin (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Remembering Eden Holguin

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, before I begin, I want to mention Special 
Agent Eden Holguin, a member of the Capitol Police's Dignitary 
Protection Division, who passed away last week following a brief 
illness.
  It is men and women like Special Agent Holguin who allow us to 
complete our work here each day in safety. I am profoundly grateful for 
his service and for that of our other Capitol Police officers.
  My prayers today are with Special Agent Holguin's loved ones and his 
family.
```
