                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 441

  At the request of Mr. Sheehy, the name of the Senator from Kansas 
(Mr. Marshall) was added as a cosponsor of S. 441, a bill to direct the 
Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of the Interior to develop a 
plan to reorganize Federal wildland fire response, and for other 
purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Sullivan, the name of the Senator from New 
Jersey (Mr. Booker) was added as a cosponsor of S. 556, a bill to 
impose sanctions with respect to persons engaged in logistical 
transactions and sanctions evasion relating to oil, gas, liquefied 
natural gas, and related petrochemical products from the Islamic 
Republic of Iran, and for other purposes.

Virginia (Mrs. Capito) was added as a cosponsor of S. 679, a bill to 
amend title 18, United States Code, to improve the Law Enforcement

concealed weapons by law enforcement officers, and for other purposes.
