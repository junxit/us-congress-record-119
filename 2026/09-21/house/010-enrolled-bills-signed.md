# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 3657, H.R. 5334, H.R. 7250

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled bills of the House of the following titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker on Thursday, September 17, 2026

       H.R. 3657. An act to amend the Federal Power Act to require 
     the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to annually submit 
     to Congress a report on the status of ongoing hydropower 
     relicensing applications.
       H.R. 5334. An act to impose sanctions and other measures 
     with respect to the Russian Federation, as championed by the 
     late Senator Lindsey O. Graham, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 7250. An act to reauthorize the Fort Peck Reservation 
     Rural Water System Act of 2000.
```
