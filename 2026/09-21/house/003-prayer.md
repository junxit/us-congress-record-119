# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Eternal God, we ask for Your divine presence among us this afternoon. 
As the God of the ages, Your Almighty hand has led us in the past, 
directs us in the present, and guides us toward the future. We give You 
thanks for Your abiding presence throughout the 150 years the Capitol 
Guide Service has presented the history of this Capitol to tens of 
thousands of visitors who tour this building every day.
  In gratitude for their enthusiasm and expertise, their generosity of 
spirit, and genuine passion for the history entrusted to them, we 
commend them for holding fast to their mission to bring the history of 
these Halls to life.
  While tourists and staff alike may only know these men and women by 
their red coats, Lord, You know them each by name. You have graced them 
each with both the capability and the responsibility of not just 
memorizing dates and facts, but of sharing the stories of our forebears 
with this generation and the next.
  By their steadfast efforts, may the citizens of this country discover 
how to build on the Nation's successes, learn from its mistakes, and 
grow in the wisdom and truth from the lessons of those who have gone 
before us, that we may faithfully pass them on to those who will follow 
us in the years to come.
  In Your eternal name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
