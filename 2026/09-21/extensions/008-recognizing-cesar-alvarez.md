# RECOGNIZING CESAR ALVAREZ

**Speaking:** Diaz-Balart, Mario (D000600) [R-FL]

```
                       RECOGNIZING CESAR ALVAREZ

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MARIO DIAZ-BALART

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my dear 
friend Cesar Alvarez, an outstanding leader in law, civic advocacy, and 
an inspiring member of my community. His longstanding commitment to 
justice and leadership in the Cuba-American community has culminated 
today in his recognition by the Greater Miami Chamber of Commerce in 
awarding him the ``Sand in My Shoes Award.''
  Originally from Havana, Cuba, Cesar exemplifies what it means to 
pursue and live out the American dream. His father, Licinio Alvarez, 
played a pivotal role in his upbringing, both in making the decision to 
pursue a life in Miami and instilling the importance of hard work and 
community involvement. With these values driven into him at a young 
age, it is no surprise Cesar chose one of the most rigorous and 
demanding career paths available in our Nation: law. In 1973 Cesar 
began his life's work at Greenberg Traurig as an associate. Throughout 
his time at the firm, he has been involved in nationally recognized 
cases, taking Greenberg Traurig time and again to new heights.
  Notably, Cesar has worked on cases that have significantly impacted 
the Cuban American community. During the Cuban Rafter Crisis in 1994, 
Cesar led a legal challenge against the government stance that 
Guantanamo was not U.S. territory. This involved seeking representation 
for those being detained and those that were denied asylum protection. 
This case served as essential legal precedent that influenced future 
policies regarding detainment at Guantanamo Bay, a reality which 
continues to this day.
  Perhaps the most recognizable of these victories includes his work in 
bringing justice to the families of the ``Brothers to the Rescue''. 
This horrific attack in 1996 resulting in the death of four unarmed 
American pilots tore through Miami's Cuban-American community; an 
attack perpetrated by the Castro regime in cold blood. Cuban MiGs shot 
down two aircraft operated by ``Brothers to the Rescue,'' seeking to 
save the lives of countless Cuban men and women crossing the Straits of 
Florida and forced into exile by the Cuban regime. Alongside Frank 
Angones, Cesar fought for justice for the pilots through U.S. anti-
terrorism laws. While successful in their heroic efforts, they 
encountered challenges upon attempting to collect the damages from the 
Cuban regime from the wrongful death suit, Alejandre v. Republic of 
Cuba 1999. This galvanized congressional action aiming to increase 
pressure for democratic change in Cuba, leading to the introduction and 
passage of the Cuban Liberty and Democratic Solidarity Act in 1996, an 
act for which my dear brother Representative Lincoln Diaz-Balart and 
close friend Representative Ileana Ros-Lehtinen each provided critical 
support. This marked a singular moment in history for the Cuban 
American community, and Cesar's contributions were monumental.
  Similarly, Cesar played a pivotal role in the legal dispute between 
George W. Bush and Al Gore in 2000. He was contacted by Florida 
Governor Jeb Bush's legal team personally and ultimately approved of 
the firm's participation in this case. He worked to ensure Greenberg 
Traurig remained neutral and provided bipartisan representation. This 
case was essential in Greenberg Traurig's growth, opening the door for 
high profile cases, and placing them among other major players in the 
legal world.
  In clear acknowledgment of his historic contributions, Cesar ascended 
to CEO and President of Greenberg Traurig in 1997, leading the firm 
from these to become an internationally recognized organization with 
thousands of attorneys. Today, the heart of the firm remains in Miami, 
with Cesar serving as Senior Chairman, where it not only provides its 
legal services but contributes to the community by providing leadership 
and financial support to numerous local and national charitable 
organizations. Whether in law or in service to our community, Cesar 
Alvarez remains a singular figure in our Cuban-American community.
  Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to recognize my close friend Cesar 
Alvarez. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his remarkable 
career and achievements. His work has truly shaped the Miami community, 
and his leadership has left a significant impact.
```
