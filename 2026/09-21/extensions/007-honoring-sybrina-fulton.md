# HONORING SYBRINA FULTON

```
                        HONORING SYBRINA FULTON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Flordia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, as Chair of the 
U.S. Commission on the Social Status of Black Men and Boys, on the 
occasion of the Congressional Black Caucus Foundation's Annual 
Legislative Conference, where we gathered to officially cut the ribbon 
for the Commission and to honor the memory of young Black boys who were 
murdered, and the parents who continue to carry their sons' legacies 
forward. It is in that spirit that I pay tribute to Sybrina Fulton, the 
mother of Trayvon Martin, whose advocacy in the years since her son's 
death has given comfort and community to countless mothers across this 
country.
  Trayvon Martin was 17 years old when he was fatally shot in Sanford, 
Florida, in February 2012. In the aftermath, Sybrina did not have the 
benefit of an established community of mothers who understood exactly 
what she was carrying. So she built one. Alongside Tracy Martin, she 
co-founded the Trayvon Martin Foundation to raise awareness of gun 
violence and support families facing similar loss. From that same grief 
came the Circle of Mothers, an organization Sybrina created to bring 
together mothers who have lost children or family members to gun 
violence, offering them a space for healing, empowerment, and 
fellowship.
  For more than a decade, the Circle of Mothers has hosted an annual 
retreat where grieving mothers gather not as strangers but as a family 
bound by shared loss. Sybrina has described the organization as 
something she dreamed into being because nothing like it existed when 
she needed it most. What began as one mother's search for connection 
has grown into a national network that has reached mothers far beyond 
South Florida, offering them what Sybrina herself was once without.

[[Page E957]]

  Sybrina has carried this mission into every space available to her, 
speaking to communities, students, and policymakers across the country 
about the toll of gun violence and the resilience required to survive 
it. She co-authored ``Rest in Power: The Enduring Life of Trayvon 
Martin,'' bringing her family's story and her ongoing advocacy to an 
even wider audience, and she has continued to show up, year after year, 
for mothers who never asked to join this circle but found strength in 
it nonetheless.
  Sybrina has often said that losing her son forced her to stand up 
rather than stay on her knees, and that spirit runs through everything 
the Circle of Mothers represents: turning unimaginable grief into a 
source of strength for others. Her willingness to keep opening that 
circle to new mothers, again and again, has made her one of the most 
enduring voices in this country's fight against gun violence.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Sybrina 
Fulton for her advocacy through the Circle of Mothers, and for the 
healing and hope she has extended to mothers across this Nation who 
have suffered as she has.
```
