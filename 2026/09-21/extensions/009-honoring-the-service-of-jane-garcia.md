# HONORING THE SERVICE OF JANE GARCIA

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                  HONORING THE SERVICE OF JANE GARCIA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jane Garcia as 
she retires from La Clinica de La Raza.
  Jane has worked tirelessly to serve the East Bay community through 
her role as the Chief Executive Officer of La Clinica. Since beginning 
her role in 1982, Jane has led La Clinica in providing culturally 
sensitive care to over 86,000 patients throughout the East Bay.
  Jane has been a steadfast proponent for accessible, culturally 
competent, community-focused health care. She has helped thousands of 
patients in navigating the health care system and has worked diligently 
to build La Clinica into what it is today. She fostered strong 
partnerships with providers like Kaiser Permanente, allowing La Clinica 
to reach underserved communities. Under her leadership, La Clinica has 
not only become a major asset to communities in need of accessible 
health care, but it also helps to enroll patients in Medicaid and other 
insurance programs. During the COVID-19 pandemic, Jane facilitated 
programs at La Clinica that helped patients re-enroll in health care 
after their coverage had expired, delivered meals to families in 
quarantine, and helped people find resources so that they were able to 
keep their homes. Furthermore, in 1997 she took then-California 
Governor Pete Wilson's administration to court for defunding Medi-Cal 
prenatal care for undocumented immigrant women--and she won. She also 
serves on various boards such as The California Endowment, the Alameda 
Alliance for Health, and Oakland Thrives, and she served a remarkable 
three-year term as President of the California Primary Care 
Association. Through working on the ground with patients and navigating 
policy, she has been a fierce advocate for historically marginalized 
communities and has been a true leader in protecting and promoting 
public health throughout the Bay Area.
  Jane's commitment to community health has earned her numerous awards, 
including the Equal Rights Advocates award, the YMCA Martin Luther 
King, Jr. Leadership Prize for Human Dignity and Brotherhood award, and 
the National Association of Community Health Centers 2010 Betsey Cooke 
Advocacy MVP Award. She has also been inducted into the Alameda County 
Women's Hall of Fame, as well as the Women's Hall of Fame by the Contra 
Costa Commission for Women, and was recognized as one of UC Berkeley's 
School of Public Health's 75 Influential Alumni.
  Jane's leadership has been integral to providing quality health care 
to marginalized communities around the Bay Area, and her stellar 
commitment to service has left a lasting impact on countless 
individuals. Please join me in congratulating Jane Garcia on her 
retirement after her four decades of service to our region.
```
