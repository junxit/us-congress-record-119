# HONORING STEVE BASSETT FOR 60 YEARS OF CONTRIBUTIONS TO VIRGINIA MUSIC

**Speaking:** McClellan, Jennifer L. (M001227) [D-VA]

```
 HONORING STEVE BASSETT FOR 60 YEARS OF CONTRIBUTIONS TO VIRGINIA MUSIC

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. JENNIFER L. McCLELLAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Ms. McCLELLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Steve Bassett, 
an accomplished musician, songwriter and proud Virginian, who for 60 
years has made extraordinary contributions to the Commonwealth's 
musical heritage.
  For six decades, Steve has helped shape Virginia's musical heritage 
through his songwriting, performances and commitment to the arts. His 
career has taken him to stages across Virginia and beyond, sharing 
music that reflects the people, places and spirit of the Commonwealth.
  Among Steve's most enduring contributions is ``Sweet Virginia 
Breeze,'' which he co-wrote with Robbin Thompson. Beloved by 
generations of Virginians, the song celebrates the

[[Page E958]]

natural beauty and history that define our Commonwealth. In 2015, the 
Virginia General Assembly designated ``Sweet Virginia Breeze'' as 
Virginia's official popular state song, ensuring that Steve's 
contribution to Virginia's musical legacy will endure for generations 
to come.
  On October 10, 2026, friends, fellow musicians, and members of the 
community will gather at the Perkinson Center for the Arts and 
Education in Chester, Virginia, for ``Steve Bassett: 60 Years in 
Music.'' The celebration will look back on Steve's remarkable career 
through music and storytelling while also looking ahead by highlighting 
the next generation of Virginia artists.
  Steve's legacy extends beyond any single song or performance. 
Throughout his career, he has demonstrated the important role artists 
play in telling the stories of our communities, preserving our shared 
culture and bringing people together. His six decades of artistry 
represent an extraordinary contribution to Virginia's rich musical 
tradition.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Steve 
Bassett on 60 years in music and thanking him for his lasting 
contributions to the arts and cultural heritage of the Commonwealth of 
Virginia. I wish him continued success as he celebrates this remarkable 
milestone.

                          ____________________
```
