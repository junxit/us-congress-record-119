# RECOGNIZING KNOT YOUR AVERAGE BOYS FROM COPPELL HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
      RECOGNIZING KNOT YOUR AVERAGE BOYS FROM COPPELL HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I use today to recognize Knot Your 
Average Boys, a group of students at Coppell High School led by senior 
Prajit Sreenivas. Together, they crochet baby caps and blankets for 
patients at Scottish Rite for Children in Dallas.
  Veer Talsania, a former patient at Scottish Rite, participates in 
Knot Your Average Boys as an opportunity to give back to the hospital 
that cared for him. All five members are in the percussion section of 
the Coppell High School band and crocheting reminds them of performing 
together, a chance to build something bigger than themselves.
  I thank Prajit, Veer, Jayden, Satvik, and Arnav of Knot your Average 
Boys, for giving back to our community in such a creative way.

                          ____________________
```
