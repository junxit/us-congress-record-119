# HONORING CHIEF JUSTICE JAMES T. "JIM" WORTHEN

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
            HONORING CHIEF JUSTICE JAMES T. ``JIM'' WORTHEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Chief Justice James T. 
``Jim'' Worthen of the Twelfth Court of Appeals in Tyler, Texas, as he 
concludes nearly three decades of distinguished judicial service to 
East Texas and our great state.
  Chief Justice Worthen is a seventh-generation Texan who grew up in 
Big Sandy, and he has spent his entire professional life dedicated to 
serving the region he calls home. He earned both his bachelor's and 
master's degrees from The University of Texas at Tyler, his law degree 
from South Texas College of Law, and a Master of Laws degree from the 
University of Virginia. Before taking the bench, he practiced law in 
Tyler for nearly two decades, building a strong and respected 
reputation within the East Texas legal community.
  Chief Justice Worthen was first elected to the Twelfth Court of 
Appeals in 1998 and has served as Chief Justice since January 2003. 
Over his 27 years on the bench, he has provided steady, principled 
leadership and has earned the deep respect of his colleagues and the 
broader legal community for his integrity, professionalism, and 
unwavering commitment to the title of law. His service has extended 
well beyond the courtroom, including his tenure as Chairman of the 
Council of Chief Justices and his longstanding involvement in the Texas 
legal community.
  Chief Justice Worthen's career represents the very best of public 
service; a profound respect for the law, a deep sense of responsibility 
to the office he holds, and an unwavering commitment to the people of 
East Texas.
  I am proud to join Chief Justice Worthen's family, friends, 
colleagues, and fellow East Texans in thanking him for his exceptional 
service and congratulating him on a well-earned retirement. I thank him 
for his dedicated service to East Texas and the State of Texas.

                          ____________________
```
