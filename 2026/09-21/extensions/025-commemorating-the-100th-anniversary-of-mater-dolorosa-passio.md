# COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF MATER DOLOROSA PASSIONIST RETREAT CENTER

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
   COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF MATER DOLOROSA PASSIONIST 
                             RETREAT CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate the distinguished Mater 
Dolorosa Passionist Retreat Center on its 100th anniversary.
  Mater Dolorosa Passionist Retreat Center was first acquired by the 
Passionists in 1924 and welcomed its first retreatants in July 1926. 
Today, several Passionist Retreat Centers can be found across the 
United States, welcoming all individuals who seek a deeper relationship 
with their faith.
  Over the past 100 years, Mater Dolorosa Passionist Retreat Center has 
faced many challenges, from economic hardships to natural disasters 
including earthquakes and

[[Page E962]]

wildfires. Most recently, the Eaton Fire burned many of their 
structures and left others filled and covered with smoke and dust. Yet, 
through perseverance, the Retreat Center has been able to rebuild and 
recover, while continuing to welcome new retreatants.
  Every year, over 10,000 people receive services from Mater Dolorosa 
Passionist Retreat Center. It hosts weekend retreats offering 
opportunities for people from all backgrounds and every stage of life, 
including men, women, couples, and youth, to reconnect with themselves 
and their faith.
  The 100th anniversary of Mater Dolorosa Passionist Retreat Center is 
an incredible milestone and I am proud to recognize its legacy of the 
organization and their impact in Sierra Madre and across the San 
Gabriel Valley. Because of their services, members of our community 
have been given the tools they need to heal, grow, and thrive.
```
