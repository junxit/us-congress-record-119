# HIGHLIGHTING THE UCS TOWN HALL

**Speaking:** Clark, Katherine M. (C001101) [D-MA]

```
                     HIGHLIGHTING THE UCS TOWN HALL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. KATHERINE M. CLARK

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Ms. CLARK of Massachusetts. Mr. Speaker, I recently had the privilege 
of joining the Union of Concerned Scientists and more than 300 of my 
constituents for a town hall to discuss the Trump administration's 
continued, devastating attacks on science.
  The event--``What's at Stake, What's Possible: A Massachusetts 
Science Town Hall''--featured distinguished professors from Boston 
University, a Ph.D. candidate at MIT, and a local small business owner.
  Each spoke movingly about the damage inflicted by this 
administration's irrational, indefensible decision to cut research 
funding, politicize federal grantmaking, and destroy the institutions 
responsible for driving American innovation.
  The takeaway was clear: The cost of this war on science is nothing 
less than our global competitiveness, our environment, our health, and 
ultimately our lives.
  We must act now to restore scientific integrity and protect our 
scientists from the personal, destructive whims of politicians.

                          ____________________
```
