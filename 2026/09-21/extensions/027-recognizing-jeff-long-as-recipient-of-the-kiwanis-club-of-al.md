# RECOGNIZING JEFF LONG AS RECIPIENT OF THE KIWANIS CLUB OF ALTOONA'S DISTINGUISHED CITIZEN AWARD

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
  RECOGNIZING JEFF LONG AS RECIPIENT OF THE KIWANIS CLUB OF ALTOONA'S 
                      DISTINGUISHED CITIZEN AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of PENNSYLVANIA

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Jeff Long 
for his selection as the recipient of the Kiwanis Club of Altoona's 
Distinguished Citizen Award.
  The award is given in recognition of major contributions to the 
betterment of society and advancement of the Altoona area.
  With nearly 50 years of achievement in the construction industry, 
including development of the popular Graystone properties, Jeff has 
generously supported numerous charitable and nonprofit organizations 
throughout the communities where he lives and works.
  Impressively, Jeff measures success by the people he has helped, the 
communities that he has built and strengthened, and the opportunities 
that he has created for so many others.
  As a businessman and community volunteer, Jeff has truly devoted his 
life to investing in Central Pennsylvania.
  On behalf of constituents throughout Blair County and the entire 13th 
Congressional District, I congratulate Jeff Long on this prestigious 
award and thank him for his contributions to the community.

                          ____________________
```
