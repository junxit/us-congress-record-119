# HONORING MEL ORPILLA

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING MEL ORPILLA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mel Orpilla, in 
recognition of nearly 40 years of distinguished public service, 
community leadership, advocacy and dedication to preserving the history 
heritage of Filipino Americans. Mel Orpilla is a lifelong resident of 
Vallejo, his commitment to public service began through community-based 
organizations, including Filipinos for Affirmative Action and Fighting 
back Partnership. He then spent the next nine years as the Community 
and Government relations manager for Kaiser Permanente. Mel 
subsequently brought his experience in community advocacy to 
Congressman Mike Thompson's office, serving as his Senior Field 
representative for nearly ten years in the Vallejo office.
  Mel has continued his work, serving as our Senior District 
representative for the last four years, where he has been pivotal in 
bringing federal funds and resources to Vallejo and Solano County.
  Throughout his work, he became the trusted resource for constituents, 
elected officials, city staff, and everyone in between to assist. 
Beyond his professional work, Mel has devoted decades to preserving 
Filipino American history, promoting cultural pride, and ensuring that 
the contributions of Filipino Americans are recognized and remembered 
by future generations.
  As a leader of the Filipino American National Historical Society 
(FANHS), Mel played a significant role in the effort to establish 
Filipino American History Month in California. He and his team helped 
advance legislation through the California Legislature recognizing 
October as Filipino American History Month. Under the leadership of key 
figures like Mel, FANHS helped advance the recognition of October on 
the federal level. In 2015, the White House recognized FANHS's 
longstanding efforts to establish Filipino American History Month and 
hosted the first-ever White House celebration of Filipino American 
History Month, with Mel, then FANHS National President, delivering 
keynote remarks.
  Mel Orpilla's career reflects the very best of community and public 
service, a commitment to people and determination to ensure that his 
community's history and voice are valued and remembered
  We proudly recognize Mel Orpilla for his decades of service and his 
lasting contributions to Vallejo, Solano County, the Filipino American 
community and the people he has had the privilege of serving.
```
