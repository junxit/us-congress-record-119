# RECOGNIZING THE TITZMAN FAMILY AND WARRIOR KID MEDIC

**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

```
          RECOGNIZING THE TITZMAN FAMILY AND WARRIOR KID MEDIC

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a remarkable 
initiative born in my district in Jourdanton, Texas, that is helping 
change how we approach youth safety and emergency preparedness.
  Recognizing that children are often the first individuals present at 
a medical emergency, my constituents Dustin and Michelle Titzman, along 
with their children Penelope, Everly, Adeline, and Stokes, founded 
Warrior Kid Medic.
  This program is the culmination of Dustin's two decades of experience 
as a San Antonio firefighter, paramedic, and Army Reserve Blackhawk 
pilot, combined with over 14 years of youth leadership development at 
their summer program, Camp SkyWay.
  Together, they have translated real-world emergency experience into 
lessons children can understand and use. Through camp programs, 
homeschool groups, and community trainings, they have taught more than 
1,000 children.
  Their work does far more than teach practical first aid; it builds 
lifelong character. In an emergency, it is easy for a child to feel 
scared, helpless, or like a victim. This training helps them step 
forward and determine, `What can I do? How can I help?' It replaces 
fear with critical thinking, leadership, and a steadfast commitment to 
serving others.
  Furthermore, this work addresses a real training gap. While it is 
often difficult to find the time to train adults, our youth are an 
attentive audience already in the mindset of learning. By teaching 
these skills early, we give students knowledge they can carry for life.
  The child we prepare today becomes the parent, banker, coach, 
coworker, neighbor, and community leader who not only knows exactly 
what to do in a crisis, but cares enough to act.
  Mr. Speaker, empowering our youth to lead builds stronger, more 
resilient communities. On behalf of the 28th District of Texas, I 
commend the Titzman family and Warrior Kid Medic for their dedication 
to cultivating the next generation of citizens and first responders, 
and I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing this important work.

                          ____________________
```
