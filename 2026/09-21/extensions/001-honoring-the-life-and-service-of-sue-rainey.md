# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF SUE RAINEY

```
              HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF SUE RAINEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. DeSaulnier. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life and 
service of Sue Rainey.
  Sue served as the Mayor of Walnut Creek for three years between 2001 
and 2010. With experience from her time as a nurse in the Army, her 
mayorship in Moraga, her time as a Walnut Creek city councilmember, her 
service on both the Board of the Central Contra Costa Sanitary District 
and the Contra Costa County Local Agency Formation Commission, she 
brought seasoned and steady leadership to Walnut Creek.
  During her tenure as mayor, Sue worked diligently through many 
challenges to develop Walnut Creek into the business, retail, and arts 
hub that it is today. She led the city through the opening of the 
public library, which was finished under budget and on time, bringing 
accessible resources and green space to the community. She also helped 
to lay the groundwork for the overwhelmingly successful expansion of 
Broadway Plaza, which has become an integral economic engine for the 
city, bringing in millions of dollars in revenue. Additionally, Sue was 
committed to improving the lives of Walnut Creek residents. She led an 
effort that resulted in Walnut Creek having its first Sentinel of 
Freedom scholarship beneficiary, which offers education subsidies, 
financial assistance, mentoring, and other resources to injured post-9/
11 veterans.
  Sue's leadership and service touched many lives in the community. For 
over 20 years, Sue volunteered with the Cancer Support Community (CSC) 
of the San Francisco Bay Area, raising funds for the organization, 
serving on committees, joining the Board of Directors, and eventually 
serving as Board Chair. As the organization grew over the years and 
needed to expand into a new space, Sue also led the initiative to move 
into a larger center. When CSC grew to serve over 2,000 individuals 
annually, she again chaired the campaign to develop a modernized, 
state-of-the-art center for cancer support. Sue strived to uplift all 
those affected by cancer, which is why she led yet another initiative 
that provided counseling and education programs for youth grappling 
with cancer. These programs now benefit more than 100 families per 
year. Her efforts on behalf of the Cancer Support Community resulted in 
Sue winning the Lifetime Achievement Award from the East Bay Leadership 
Council.
  Sadly, Sue passed away on June 17, 2026. Please join us in honoring 
Sue for her incredible dedication to the community, her outstanding 
leadership, and her invaluable contributions to our region.
```
