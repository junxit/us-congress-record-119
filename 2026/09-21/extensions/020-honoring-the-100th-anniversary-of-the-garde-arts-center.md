# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE GARDE ARTS CENTER

**Speaking:** Courtney, Joe (C001069) [D-CT]

```
        HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE GARDE ARTS CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOE COURTNEY

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. COURTNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 100th 
anniversary of the Garde Arts Center in New London, Connecticut, and to 
honor the citizens, leaders, volunteers, donors, and community partners 
whose vision and dedication preserved one of southeastern Connecticut's 
most treasured cultural landmarks.
  Originally opened as the Garde Theatre on September 22, 1926, the 
theater emerged during the golden age of American movie palaces, 
becoming a gathering place where generations experienced film, 
performance, and shared community life. For decades, the Garde stood as 
a prominent symbol of downtown New London's civic and cultural 
identity.
  Like many historic theaters across America, the Garde faced 
significant challenges as patterns of entertainment, commerce, and 
urban development changed during the twentieth century. By the 1980s, 
its future was uncertain and demolition appeared a real possibility.
  Rather than allow a cherished landmark to be lost, the people of New 
London came together to save it. Beginning in 1985, community leaders 
and citizens launched an extraordinary preservation effort that 
transformed the historic theater into a nonprofit regional arts center 
dedicated to serving future generations.
  The story of the Garde is a powerful example of what can be achieved 
when a community commits itself to preservation, renewal, and public 
purpose. Through sustained investment, stewardship, and vision, the 
Garde became a catalyst for downtown revitalization and a center for 
arts, education, entertainment, and civic engagement throughout 
southeastern Connecticut.
  Today, the Garde Arts Center welcomes audiences from across 
Connecticut and beyond. From world-class music, theater, dance, film, 
and comedy to educational programs, community celebrations, and 
cultural partnerships, the Garde continues to enrich the lives of 
residents and visitors while strengthening the economic and cultural 
vitality of the region.
  The Garde's accomplishments have earned national recognition. In 
2022, the League of Historic American Theatres presented the Garde with 
its Outstanding Historic Theatre Award, recognizing the institution as 
an exemplary model of historic preservation and community stewardship.
  As the Garde Arts Center celebrates its Centennial Season, we 
recognize not only a remarkable building, but also the countless 
individuals whose commitment ensured that this historic theater 
remained a living institution rather than a relic of the past. We honor 
the artists who took its stage, the audiences who filled its seats, and 
the volunteers, trustees, staff members, donors, businesses, and public 
officials whose efforts sustained it through challenges and 
opportunities alike.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the Garde Arts 
Center and the City of New London on the occasion of this historic 
milestone and in recognizing the Garde's enduring contributions to the 
cultural, educational, and economic life of Connecticut.
  As the Garde begins its second century, may it continue to inspire 
creativity, strengthen community, preserve our shared heritage, and 
serve as a beacon for the arts for generations to come.
```
