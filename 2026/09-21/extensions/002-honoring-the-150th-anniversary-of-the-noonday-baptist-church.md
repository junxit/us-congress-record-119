# HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NOONDAY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
      HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NOONDAY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Noonday Baptist Church 
and its pastor, Scott Gorbett, as the congregation celebrates its 150th 
anniversary of faithful service to the Noonday community.
  The congregation's roots trace back to 1866, when settlers in the 
Neches River area organized the Seven League Baptist Church in Christ. 
In 1876, several members withdrew to form a new congregation at the 
crossroads of the Seven League area, naming both the community and the 
church Noonday. On October 10, 1876, the Cherokee Baptist Association 
formally received Noonday Baptist Church into its membership--the 
beginning of a legacy that continues to this day.
  In 1888, the congregation purchased the land on which the church 
still stands, and the original building was constructed soon after. 
Since 1912, the church has been remodeled and expanded many times over, 
reflecting the growth and resilience of a congregation deeply rooted in 
its community.
  For a century and a half, Noonday Baptist Church has remained a 
steadfast presence in the lives of its members and neighbors offering 
Bible study, worship, and fellowship for children, youth, and adults 
alike. Under the leadership of Pastor Scott Gorbett, the church 
continues to embody its founding philosophy: that faith is more than 
what happens on Sunday mornings, but a part of who its members are, 
inside and out.
  Noonday Baptist Church has faithfully shared the gospel, served its 
community, and welcomed all who walk through its doors for 150 years.
```
