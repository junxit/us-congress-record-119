# RECOGNIZING STE. GENEVIEVE INDUSTRIES, INC. ON ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Smith, Jason (S001195) [R-MO]

```
  RECOGNIZING STE. GENEVIEVE INDUSTRIES, INC. ON ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JASON SMITH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. SMITH of Missouri. Mr Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Ste. 
Genevieve Industries, Inc. on its 50th anniversary.
  SGI was established to provide a place for individuals with 
disabilities to experience the value of dignified work. Over the years, 
this non-profit has offered opportunities for people to share their 
skills and talents, including two who have worked there since the very 
day SGI opened its doors. Katherine Blechle and Linda Wolk went to work 
at SGI on September 20, 1976, and they are still on their jobs today.
  SGI began by having its employees make custom pallets. In the decades 
since, they've added contract work--delivering bagged ice, shredding 
documents and operating the Ste. Genevieve County Recycling Center. 
They host electronic waste disposal events where residents can bring 
old computers, printers and other devices for safe disposal.
  SGI employs 28 adults with disabilities and 20 staff members at its 
location at 10929 Industrial Drive. Josh Adams is the director, and the 
Board of Directors is led by President Bob Hoog. Board members Clement 
Donze and Mary Ann Roth are also celebrating a half century of service 
to SGI.
  Ste. Genevieve Industries, Incorporated is a treasure in the Ste. 
Genevieve community, and it is my great pleasure to recognize SGI today 
on its 50th anniversary before the United States House of 
Representatives.
```
