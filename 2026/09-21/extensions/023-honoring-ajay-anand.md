# HONORING AJAY ANAND

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                          HONORING AJAY ANAND

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ajay Anand of Kern 
County, California for his numerous contributions to Kern County, 
healthcare in the Central Valley, and the local communities of which he 
is a part.
  Decades spent exploring every corner of both the Central Valley and 
our healthcare systems have given Mr. Anand a wealth of experience 
which he uses to his advantage. His contributions include building 
healthcare infrastructure across the region, such as bringing `DaVinci' 
robots into the operating room for precision surgery, as well as 
leading philanthropic programs for underprivileged, at-risk youth.
  One of his most recognizable contributions to the Central Valley was 
the work he did for the Bakersfield Heart Hospital; which, in 2016, was 
incredibly close to closing after their `corporate parent company' went 
bankrupt. Mr. Anand put almost the entirety of his savings and 
resources into salvaging the hospital and even acquired corporate 
ownership to keep the hospital open and functional, helping save over 
400 jobs in the process.
  Mr. Anand led both the development and creation of Centric Health, 
founded in 2013, a clinically integrated delivery platform in Kern 
County specializing in patient-centered healthcare and delivering care 
to over 120,000 patients a year; as well as QualCare, a network of 
independent physicians with over 600 providers, extending care to 
Medicare beneficiaries in Bakersfield and Kern counties. More recently, 
he and his family contributed to the Thomas M. Hardt Tower expansion at 
Mercy Southwest Hospital, in recognition of which the hospital's 
maternity and obstetrics center was named the Anand Family Birth 
Center.
  Mr. Anand's impact on Kern County and the Central Valley extends much 
further than the healthcare field, however. He is a co-founder and 
managing partner of the H2H Asset Group, a real estate and hospitality 
company contributing to the local economy, and has helped create jobs, 
services, and housing for his community.
  He is also committed to philanthropy and public works, creating the 
Anand Legacy Foundation to support at-risk youth, and supporting Garden 
Pathways, an organization committed to providing mentorship and 
education in the Central Valley. He also helped found the Centric 
Community Care Foundation, a nonprofit initiative promoting community 
health and well-being through education, outreach, and disease 
prevention. The Anand Legacy Foundation also provides support for the 
`emPOWERment Dess Perkins Foundation', a local organization dedicated 
to assisting survivors of human trafficking. His work and support in 
this field reflects a deep commitment to protecting vulnerable youths 
in our communities and strengthening our district. His commitment to 
helping the next generation doesn't extend only to the less fortunate 
and at-risk, however. Mr. Anand and the Anand Legacy Foundation also 
consistently support educational initiatives in the district, making 
annual contributions towards the Kern High School District Educational 
Foundation's Superintendent's First In Class Award program. Most 
recently, Mr. Anand has pledged $500,000 in support of the ``Trump 
Accounts'' initiative, to establish investment accounts for newborn 
children, with the contribution specifically directed toward supporting 
vulnerable and at-risk newborns in Kern County, including children born 
to mothers in rehabilitation and recovery programs. As with his 
charitable works, the focus he places on the protection and well-being 
of future generations show his great contributions to our community.
  Over the last 20 years, Mr. Anand has played a pivotal role in 
improving the quality of healthcare in the Central Valley in addition 
to his numerous charitable works. His dedication and work earned him a 
2026 Hometown Hero Honoree selection by the Bakersfield Police 
Activities League, as well as the 2019 Bakersfield Mayor's Medal and 
Community Ambassador Award, and a Certificate of Recognition from the 
Kern County Board of Supervisors.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in recognizing the 
incredible work that Mr. Anand has done for the Central Valley. Few 
individuals have made such a broad and lasting impact on our district, 
from strengthened healthcare access and support for vulnerable 
populations to investments in a stranger future for our district and 
Nation. We wish him nothing but continued success in his endeavors and 
thank him for all that he has done.
```
