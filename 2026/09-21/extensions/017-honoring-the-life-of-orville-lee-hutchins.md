# HONORING THE LIFE OF ORVILLE LEE HUTCHINS

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
               HONORING THE LIFE OF ORVILLE LEE HUTCHINS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an outstanding 
member of Pennsylvania's 1st Congressional District, Orville Lee 
Hutchins, and to recognize a life defined by service, sacrifice, 
family, and devotion to his community and country.
  Orville's life of service to our Nation began in 1944, when he 
entered the United States military. He served for more than two 
decades, retiring in 1965 with the rank of E-7, Sergeant.
  During his distinguished military career, Orville served in the 
Pacific Theater during World War II, including combat service on the 
Philippine island of Luzon. In 1945, he was part of the occupying 
forces in Japan. In 1949, he was transferred to France, where he met 
Genevieve, who would become his wife. Orville later deployed to Okinawa 
on several occasions with his guided missile battalion.
  When the war ended, the 27th Regiment was part of the occupying 
forces in Japan where he was stationed from 1945 to 1946. While there, 
Mr. Hutchins came upon an orphanage which was being continuously looted 
by locals. He immediately provided armed security, food, and blankets 
for the children. This was the beginning of the 27th Regiment's now-
continuous support of the Holy Family Orphanage to this day.
  Orville was married to his beloved wife, Genevieve Hutchins, for 64 
years. Together, they raised their two sons, Cliff and Stephen 
Hutchins. Stephen proudly served for 21 years in the United States Air 
Force, retiring as a colonel. For the past 37 years, Orville has called 
Bucks County home and currently resides in Langhorne, Pennsylvania.
  On behalf of the United States House of Representatives and the 
people of Pennsylvania's 1st District, in recognition of his 100th 
birthday, I extend our congratulations to Orville, his family, friends, 
and all who love him. And may God bless Orville Lee Hutchins with 
happiness and good health for many years to come.
```
