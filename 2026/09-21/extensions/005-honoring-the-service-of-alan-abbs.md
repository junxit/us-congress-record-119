# HONORING THE SERVICE OF ALAN ABBS

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                   HONORING THE SERVICE OF ALAN ABBS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with my colleagues 
Congresswoman Nancy Pelosi, Congressman Jared Huffman, and 
Congresswoman Zoe Lofgren to recognize the service of Alan Abbs upon 
his retirement from the Bay Area Air Quality Management District.
  Alan has worked throughout his career to improve air quality and 
public health in California. As the Legislative Officer for the Bay 
Area Air Quality Management District, Alan has been a strong advocate 
for strengthened air quality policies and investments to improve the 
health of our community.
  Throughout his career, Alan has shown his steadfast commitment to 
service and has worked tirelessly to strengthen California's existing 
environmental laws and advocate for critical new programs. Prior to 
joining the Bay Area Air Quality Management District, Alan served as an 
officer in the U.S. Navy, Executive Director of the California Air 
Pollution Control Officers Association, and as an Air Pollution Control 
Officer for multiple counties. He helped lead the charge to establish 
the Wildfire Smoke Clean Air Centers for the Vulnerable Populations 
Incentive Pilot Program, providing funding to retrofit public 
ventilation systems and other infrastructure to prepare for smoke 
events. Alan also worked on behalf of the Bay Area Air District to 
strengthen statewide statutory penalties for air quality violations, 
culminating in legislation that provides additional incentives for 
groups that prioritize safety and public health. Beyond his work to 
advance air quality protections, Alan has been a strong supporter of 
other environmental policies. He supported the expansion of the 
Protecting Blue Whales and Blue Skies Program, which protected marine 
life and promoted sustainable shipping along the California coast.
  Alan championed pollution reduction efforts at the federal level, as 
well. He testified before the House Science Committee on the importance 
of reducing auto emissions, utilizing his expertise to provide a 
framework for federal legislation. He was also a fierce advocate for 
the Clean Corridors Act, a bill I introduced that led to important 
funding for cleaner fueling and charging infrastructure across the 
Nation.
  Beyond his professional achievements, Alan is also an accomplished 
runner, regularly running 50- and 100-mile races alongside his wife, 
Beverly. He is also a long-suffering Detroit Tigers baseball fan.
  Alan's leadership has been integral to preserving clean air in 
California and beyond. Please join us in honoring Alan for his 
outstanding leadership and congratulating him on his well-deserved 
retirement.
```
