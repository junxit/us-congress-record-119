# HONORING AUTUMN MORLEY

**Speaking:** Diaz-Balart, Mario (D000600) [R-FL]

```
                         HONORING AUTUMN MORLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MARIO DIAZ-BALART

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 21, 2026

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate 
the honorable and decades-long career in public service of my Counsel 
and Senior Policy Advisor, Autumn Morley.
  While Autumn started her education at Davidson College in North 
Carolina, an internship in Washington, D.C. so impacted her, that she 
chose to transfer to George Washington University, where she graduated 
summa cum laude with majors in political science and religion. In 2004, 
she received her J.D. from Emory University School of Law and Masters 
of Theological Studies from Emory's Candler School of Theology. That 
same year, she joined the Florida Bar.
  Autumn began her career on Capitol Hill as a Staff Assistant in the 
office of Representative Lincoln Diaz-Balart in 2005. However, it was 
in 2008 in her role as Legislative Assistant that Autumn would begin to 
hone her knowledge and skills in defense of democracy and on behalf of 
those suffering under repression. Taking charge of the Congressman's 
Cuba and foreign policy portfolios, Autumn began to lay the groundwork 
for her near-unparalleled expertise on Capitol Hill in the fight for 
freedom; a fight not only in our Nation or our hemisphere, but across 
the globe. Following her years of dedicated work as a Legislative 
Assistant, Autumn was later promoted to Legislative Director.
  In 2011, following Representative Lincoln Diaz-Balart's retirement, I 
had the immense privilege of having Autumn join my team as Counsel and 
Senior Policy Advisor. Once again, our community in South Florida was 
afforded the opportunity to have the best and brightest at the helm of 
our freedom agenda. Overseeing Cuba and Western Hemisphere affairs more 
broadly, democracy promotion, and the work in opposition to Communism, 
Autumn became an asset not only to my office or community in Miami, but 
she also became the preeminent expert in the fight for freedom for 
offices across Capitol Hill. Never seeking after credit, accolades, or 
praise, Autumn was for years an unsung hero on Capitol Hill, fighting 
for democracy and for the human rights of men and women she will likely 
never meet.
  Among her many contributions, Autumn was critical in drafting pro-
freedom language for numerous appropriations bills, in preparing floor 
statements and other speeches, and always worked hand in glove with my 
entire team and committee staff in facilitating the passage of key 
legislation. Throughout the years, Autumn was clear in her convictions, 
unwavering in her commitment and hard work, and perpetually delivered 
for our community with humility and grace.
  For this and so much more, I wish to express my deepest gratitude on 
behalf of myself, our team, and our district for the 21 years of 
steadfast service Autumn has given to our community and her resolute 
dedication to the cause of freedom.
  Mr. Speaker, as a lifelong public servant, a dear friend, and a true 
patriot, Autumn has been instrumental in our team. Autumn now begins a 
new chapter, as she retires from public service. I am grateful for the 
time that she has graciously given to serve our community, state and 
country. She is a source of great pride to us all, including her 
husband, Michael, and her two sons, Zachary and Nicholas. I ask my 
colleagues to join me in recognizing the service and accomplishments of 
this outstanding individual.

                          ____________________
```
