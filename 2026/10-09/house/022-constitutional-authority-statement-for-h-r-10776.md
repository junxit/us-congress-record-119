# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10776

**Measures:** H.R. 10776

```
           By Mr. EZELL:
       H.R. 10776.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: the Commerce Clause, grants 
     Congress the power to regulate commerce with foreign nations, 
     among the states, and with Indian tribes, forming a 
     foundation for federal economic authority.
```
