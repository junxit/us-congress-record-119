# APPOINTMENT OF HON. ERIN HOUCHIN TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH REMAINDER OF THE 119TH CONGRESS

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. ERIN HOUCHIN TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN 
  ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH REMAINDER OF THE 119TH 
                                CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:
                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, October 9, 2026.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Erin Houchin to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through the remainder of the One Hundred 
     Nineteenth Congress.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the U.S. House of Representatives, 119th Congress.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
