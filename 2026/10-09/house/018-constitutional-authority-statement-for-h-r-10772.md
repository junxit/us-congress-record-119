# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10772

**Measures:** H.R. 10772

```
           By Mr. COSTA:
       H.R. 10772.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution (to 
     provide for the common defense and to pay the debts and 
     provide for the general welfare); clause 12 of section 8 of 
     article I of the Constitution (to raise and support armies); 
     clause 13 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution (to 
     provide and maintain a navy); clause 14 of section 8 of 
     article I of the Constitution (to make rules for the 
     government and regulation of the land and naval forces); and 
     clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution (to 
     make all laws which shall be necessary and proper for 
     carrying into execution the foregoing powers).
```
