# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, we are grateful for this land You have entrusted to our 
care, and we humbly thank You for calling us to be faithful stewards of 
it.
  Bless all who serve our Nation in Congress, and bless the staffs and 
families who support them in that service. Guide them all with wisdom 
and care, granting clarity of mind, patience in deliberation, and 
strength to remain faithful to the work entrusted to them.
  Amen.
```
