# HONORING ZION BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                      HONORING ZION BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Zion Baptist Church, 
located in Shreveport, Louisiana. Zion Baptist Church was established 
in 1937 and has served the Shreveport community for nearly nine decades 
as a place of Christian worship, fellowship, spiritual growth, and 
community service. Throughout its history, the church has remained 
committed to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and providing spiritual 
guidance to generations of individuals and families.
  Since its establishment, Zion Baptist Church has developed a rich 
history rooted in the Baptist faith and the traditions of the African 
American church. Throughout changing times in Shreveport and the 
surrounding community, Zion Baptist Church has continued to provide a 
place where members can worship, strengthen their faith, develop 
meaningful relationships, and serve others. The church's longevity 
reflects the dedication of generations of members and leaders who have 
worked to preserve its ministry and ensure that it remains an important 
part of the community.
  An important part of Zion Baptist Church's history has been its 
strong tradition of worship and gospel music. Music has played a 
meaningful role in the life of the congregation, complementing the 
church's ministries of preaching, Christian education, fellowship, and 
service. Through these ministries, Zion Baptist

[[Page E1062]]

Church has sought to strengthen both the spiritual lives of its members 
and its connection to the broader Shreveport community.
  For more than six decades, Zion Baptist Church has been led by Dr. 
Brady L. Blade Sr., who began serving as pastor in 1961. His 
longstanding tenure represents a significant period in the history of 
the congregation and has provided continuity in the church's ministry 
across multiple generations. Under his pastoral leadership, Zion 
Baptist Church has continued its commitment to Christian teaching, 
worship, music, fellowship, and service.
  Today, Zion Baptist Church continues the mission that began with its 
establishment in 1937. After nearly 90 years of ministry, the church 
remains a longstanding institution within Shreveport's faith community, 
carrying forward a legacy built by generations of faithful members. Its 
history reflects an enduring commitment to faith, perseverance, 
service, and the spiritual development of the community it has called 
home for generations. I urge my colleagues to join me in acknowledging 
Zion Baptist Church for its dedication to service in spreading the word 
of God, and goodwill in the Shreveport community.
```
