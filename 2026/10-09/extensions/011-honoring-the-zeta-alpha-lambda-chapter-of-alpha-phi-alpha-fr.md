# HONORING THE ZETA ALPHA LAMBDA CHAPTER OF ALPHA PHI ALPHA FRATERNITY, INC.

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING THE ZETA ALPHA LAMBDA CHAPTER OF ALPHA PHI ALPHA FRATERNITY, 
                                  INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Zeta Alpha Lambda Chapter of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc. for its 
extraordinary record of leadership, service, mentorship, and civic 
engagement throughout Broward County.
  For decades, the Brothers of Zeta Alpha Lambda Chapter of Alpha Phi 
Alpha Fraternity, Inc. have demonstrated a steadfast commitment to 
strengthening our community and creating opportunities for future 
generations. The Chapter has promoted civic participation through voter 
education and registration, Souls to the Polls initiatives, community 
education regarding election laws, transportation to the polls, and 
engagement within Broward County, Florida.
  That commitment to service is exemplified by the Chapter's Men of 
Tomorrow mentoring program, which has prepared generations of middle- 
and high-school young men for college, careers, leadership, and 
adulthood. During the past fraternal year, 51 young men participated in 
programming focused on college preparation, public speaking, financial 
aid, entrepreneurship, personal development, community service, and 
leadership. The Chapter's collaboration with Big Brothers Big Sisters 
has further expanded these valuable mentoring and enrichment 
opportunities. Zeta Alpha Lambda has also remained steadfast in 
supporting important health and charitable causes, including its near 
20-year commitment to the March of Dimes' March for Babies and the 
American Cancer Society's Relay for Life.
  This year, Zeta Alpha Lambda celebrated another remarkable 
milestone--its 50th Annual Martin Luther King Jr. Celebration, marking 
five decades of preserving and advancing Dr. King's legacy of service, 
equality, leadership, and community.
  It is with great pleasure that I recognize and thank the Brothers of 
Zeta Alpha Lambda for the profound and lasting difference they continue 
to make in the lives of others. Their legacy reflects the very best of 
public service, brotherhood, and community leadership. May the Chapter 
continue to build upon this distinguished record and uphold the 
timeless principles of Manly Deeds, Scholarship, and Love for All 
Mankind--strengthening our community, inspiring future generations, and 
leaving a legacy of service for many years to come.

                          ____________________
```
