# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: LORI PETERSON GRUNDMAN

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: LORI PETERSON GRUNDMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lori Peterson 
Grundman as she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional 
District's Women of the Year. Since retiring from a career in the 
semiconductor industry, Lori has dedicated herself to serving the 
Benicia community. Through her extensive volunteer work, she has helped 
protect the environment, support residents in need, strengthen public 
safety, and encourage civic engagement.
  Lori leads the Benicia Litter Pickers, a volunteer organization she 
has grown to more than 400 members. Through neighborhood cleanups and 
community events, the group has removed thousands of pounds of litter, 
helping keep Benicia's streets, parks, and waterways clean. She also 
serves with the Benicia Lions Club, helping organize community 
traditions such as the annual Lions Crab Feed, Classic Car Show, and 
Oktoberfest. After serving two years as Club Secretary on the Lions 
Club Board of Directors, Lori continues to give her time to local 
causes, including St. Paul's Community Meal Program, which provides 
hundreds of hot meals to residents in need each week.
  Lori's commitment to her neighbors also includes emergency 
preparedness and civic responsibility. She serves on the Benicia Fire 
Department's Community Emergency Response Team and is expanding her 
public safety knowledge through the Benicia Police Department's 
Citizen's Academy. For many years, she has also served as a poll worker 
with the Solano County Registrar of Voters and as an Inspector 
coordinating a local Adopt-A-Poll precinct, helping ensure elections 
run smoothly for Benicia residents.
  Through her consistent dedication across so many areas of community 
life, Lori has made a lasting difference in Benicia. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor and recognize Lori Peterson Grundman for her outstanding service 
and contributions to our community.
```
