# HONORING LAKE BETHLEHEM BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                 HONORING LAKE BETHLEHEM BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lake Bethlehem 
Baptist Church of Shreveport, Louisiana, a historic institution of 
faith, spiritual growth, and community service. Established in 1868, 
Lake Bethlehem Baptist Church has served as a place of worship, 
fellowship, Christian education, and spiritual encouragement for more 
than 150 years.
  Founded during the Reconstruction Era, Lake Bethlehem Baptist Church 
emerged during a significant period when African Americans were 
establishing independent religious institutions and strengthening their 
communities. Since its founding, the church has remained committed to 
providing individuals and families throughout Northwest Louisiana with 
opportunities for worship, fellowship, spiritual development, and 
service. Guided by its mission, ``A Helping Church for a Hurting 
World,'' the congregation has consistently emphasized faith, 
compassion, unity, and care for others.
  Throughout its history; Lake Bethlehem Baptist Church has played an 
important role in the religious and social development of the 
Shreveport community. For more than a century and a half, the church 
has provided spiritual guidance, promoted Christian values, and 
encouraged community involvement through worship, religious education, 
and outreach initiatives. Its longstanding presence reflects the 
perseverance, dedication, and faith of generations of members who have 
worked to preserve its mission while responding to the changing needs 
of the community.
  As a historic African American congregation, Lake Bethlehem Baptist 
Church represents an enduring legacy of religious independence, 
community leadership, and spiritual empowerment. Across changing 
social, economic, and cultural conditions, the congregation has 
remained dedicated to its foundational principles of faith, service, 
and Christian fellowship. Its continued emphasis on strengthening 
families, encouraging youth participation, and providing spiritual 
resources reflects a longstanding commitment to the overall well-being 
of the community.
  Under the leadership of Reverend Dennis R. Everett, Sr., Lake 
Bethlehem Baptist Church has continued to strengthen its ministry and 
expand opportunities for spiritual growth and community engagement. The 
establishment of a satellite church has provided nontraditional worship 
opportunities and helped address the spiritual and practical needs of 
individuals and families. This expansion reflects the congregation's 
commitment to reaching people beyond traditional church settings and 
ensuring that its ministry remains accessible to those seeking 
encouragement, guidance, and support.
  Through worship services, Christian education, prayer gatherings, and 
outreach efforts, Lake Bethlehem Baptist Church continues to promote 
spiritual development and meaningful connections within the community. 
The church's ability to preserve its historical identity while 
embracing new opportunities for ministry demonstrates the strength of 
its mission and the dedication of those who have carried its legacy 
forward.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring Reverend 
Everett and the faithful congregation of Lake Bethlehem Baptist Church 
for more than 150 years of Christian service, perseverance, and 
community involvement. Since its establishment in 1868, Lake Bethlehem 
Baptist Church has remained a meaningful part of Shreveport's religious 
heritage and a place of hope, encouragement, and spiritual enrichment. 
May Lake Bethlehem Baptist Church continue to serve as a helping church 
for a hurting world and inspire future generations through its enduring 
legacy of faith, unity, and service.
```
