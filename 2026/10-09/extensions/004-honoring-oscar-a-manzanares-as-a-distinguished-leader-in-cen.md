# HONORING OSCAR A. MANZANARES AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING OSCAR A. MANZANARES AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Oscar A. Manzanares was raised in Hudson 
County, New Jersey. He started out in the field as a laborer and worked 
his way through the ranks, learning the trade the way most laborers do, 
from the men working next to him.
  He served as a steward, representing the members on the job when 
something isn't right. He worked as a foreman, responsible for the 
crew's pace and its safety. He became an organizer, sitting down with 
workers who had never been in a union and explaining what opportunities 
they had with the union.
  Today, he serves as the proud business manager of Laborers' Local 517 
in Orlando, where he negotiates agreements, enforces them, and 
advocates for Florida's construction workforce on wages, safety, and 
training. He represents hundreds of members who are counting on this 
work to give their families something better. He advocates for them, 
fights for them, and stands by them no matter what obstacles are in 
front of him. He pushes members toward the training and certifications 
that move them up, because a career in the laborers should not stop at 
the first paycheck.
  Manzanares volunteers at food pantries, feeds the homeless, and 
mentors young people who are looking for a direction. He goes out into 
the community and talks with people who want a better future for 
themselves. He talks to Latinos who want to get into the trades, and he 
helps them find ESOL classes so nothing stands between them and the 
work. He knows what it means for a family when one person finds steady 
union work, and he has made it his business to open that door for as 
many people as he can.
```
