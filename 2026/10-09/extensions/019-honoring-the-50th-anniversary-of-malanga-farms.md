# HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF MALANGA FARMS

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
             HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF MALANGA FARMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 50th anniversary 
of Malanga Farms in Warren Township, New Jersey.
  Since its establishment five decades ago, Malanga Farms has 
consistently worked to provide residents of Warren Township and nearby 
municipalities with agricultural products of the utmost quality. They 
understand the importance of supplying fresh, local, non-GMO and 
pesticide free produce and plants to their customers and the role of 
the family farm as stewards of the land.
  Malanga Farms' ownership is rooted in strong family ties that span 
generations. Chris Malanga, the current owner, assumed leadership from 
his father, Jerry, who inherited the farm from his parents. The 
enduring family legacy of ownership at Malanga Farms serves as a 
testament to generations of hard work, a shared vision of sustainable 
agriculture and a commitment to uniting the communities around them 
through family-oriented, seasonal activities.
  As Malanga Farm Market and Greenhouses enters its next decade of 
operation, it remains an exemplary representation of the ``Garden 
State.'' I extend my congratulations to Malanga Farms on an 
extraordinary 50 years and my best wishes for continued success.
```
