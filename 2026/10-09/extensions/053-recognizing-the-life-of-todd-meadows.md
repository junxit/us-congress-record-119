# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF TODD MEADOWS

**Speaking:** Randall, Emily (R000621) [D-WA]

```
                  RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF TODD MEADOWS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. EMILY RANDALL

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. RANDALL. Mr. Speaker, I use this opportunity to recognize the 
life of one of my constituents, Todd Meadows of Elma, Washington, who 
passed away tragically on February 25, 2026, while crabbing on the 
Bering Sea in Alaska.
  Todd was the newest member of the ``Deadliest Catch'' crew and had 
been filming his first season on the show at the time of his death. He 
was just 25 years old and leaves behind his fiancee Kennady and four 
children, River, Lawson, Kolson and Aspen, his parents, Angela and 
Lucas Meadows, six siblings, and many other family members who loved 
him.
  His father said they started fishing together when Todd was just 
three years old and it became his greatest passion. He loved to fish 
and loved to watch other people fish. He was proud of the hard work and 
adventure that came with life on the water. He wanted nothing more than 
to show his children how to chase their dreams. He always wanted, 
``Just one more cast!''
  He loved spending time with family and friends and enjoyed fishing, 
hunting, camping, and country drives. Those who knew Todd will remember 
his adventurous spirit and the deep value he placed on time with the 
people he loved most. He worked hard, loved deeply, and brought joy to 
everyone around him.
  On May 24, 2026 at the Fisherman's Memorial in Westport, Washington a 
moment to honor his life and lives of others lost at sea took place as 
the community gathered for a day of remembrance and blessing of the 
fleet.

[[Page E1059]]

  Communities all over my district have deep ties to the maritime 
industry with their identities closely linked to the sea. I extend my 
condolences to Todd's family, friends, and colleagues as they mourn 
this loss and with the many others in my district who have lost a loved 
one to the sea.

                          ____________________
```
