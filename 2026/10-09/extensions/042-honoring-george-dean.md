# HONORING GEORGE DEAN

**Speaking:** Stanton, Greg (S001211) [D-AZ]

```
                          HONORING GEORGE DEAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GREG STANTON

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. STANTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of 
George Dean, a pillar of our Arizona community, who passed away in 
September at the age of 88.
  George led the Greater Phoenix Urban League for more than three 
decades, serving as its President and CEO. He worked tirelessly to make 
a real difference in people's lives, creating pathways to jobs, to 
education, and to homeownership. And he did it all while remaining a 
steadfast voice for civil rights and economic opportunity.
  I had the privilege of seeing George's commitment firsthand. When I 
was Mayor of Phoenix, I worked with George to help low-income families 
get assistance with their energy bills. I saw how deeply he cared about 
lifting up every member of our community.
  My thoughts are with his family, his friends, and the entire Greater 
Phoenix Urban League community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring George Dean, 
whose impact will be felt across Arizona for generations. We owe him a 
tremendous debt of gratitude for his decades of service.

                          ____________________
```
