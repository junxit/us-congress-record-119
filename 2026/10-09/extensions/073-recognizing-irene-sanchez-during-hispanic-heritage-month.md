# RECOGNIZING IRENE SANCHEZ DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
        RECOGNIZING IRENE SANCHEZ DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Hispanic American 
Heritage Month to recognize an extraordinary community leader from 
Rochester, New York: Irene Sanchez.
  As co-founder and Executive Director of Enlace, Irene has dedicated 
herself to advancing justice, dignity, and opportunities for 
farmworkers, migrant families, and newly arrived members of our 
community.
  For more than 15 years, Irene has worked to connect people with the 
tools they need to build stable, successful lives, from educational and 
legal support to workforce opportunities and mentorship. Her leadership 
is grounded in a simple but powerful belief: every person deserves the 
opportunity to thrive.
  That commitment extends far beyond any single organization or 
program. Irene has built her career around empowering others, helping 
families navigate difficult circumstances, and ensuring people too 
often overlooked have an advocate standing beside them.
  At a time when immigrant and migrant families face profound 
uncertainty, leaders like Irene remind us what community truly means. 
Her compassion, persistence, and commitment to human dignity have 
strengthened Rochester and touched countless lives.
  It is my great privilege to recognize Irene Sanchez during Hispanic 
American Heritage Month. I thank her for her years of service and for 
her unwavering commitment to building a more welcoming and equitable 
Rochester.

                          ____________________
```
