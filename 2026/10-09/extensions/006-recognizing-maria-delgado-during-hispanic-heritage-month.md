# RECOGNIZING MARIA DELGADO DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
        RECOGNIZING MARIA DELGADO DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Hispanic American 
Heritage Month to recognize an extraordinary community leader and 
advocate from Rochester, New York: Maria Delgado.
  Maria is a co-founder of Latinos Unidos Contra Cancer at Cancer 
Support Community at Gilda's Club Rochester, where she has helped build 
a vital network of support for Spanish-speaking individuals and 
families affected by cancer.
  Through outreach across Rochester and surrounding rural communities, 
Maria has worked to ensure that language or geography never stand in 
the way of people receiving the information, resources, and support 
they need. Her efforts have helped raise awareness, expand access to 
cancer screenings, and connect thousands of people with a community 
ready to stand beside them.
  Maria's work is rooted in a profound commitment to others. She 
understands that confronting cancer can be frightening and isolating, 
and she has dedicated herself to making sure families facing those 
difficult moments know they are not alone.
  Through compassion and action, Maria has helped create a stronger, 
more supportive community for people navigating some of life's greatest 
challenges.
  It is my great privilege to recognize Maria Delgado during Hispanic 
American Heritage Month. Her dedication, compassion, and service 
represent the very best of Rochester, and I thank her for the countless 
lives she continues to touch.

                          ____________________
```
