# HONORING PEACEFUL REST MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
            HONORING PEACEFUL REST MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Peaceful 
Rest Missionary Baptist Church of Shreveport, Louisiana--an enduring 
and dynamic institution whose spiritual, social, and civic influence 
has enriched the Cedar Grove community and the wider Shreveport region 
for more than half a century. Since its emergence as a central force in 
the city's African American religious life in the 1960s, Peaceful Rest 
has stood as both a place of worship and a powerful agent for community 
uplift, compassion, and transformation. Rooted in the Gospel of Jesus 
Christ, the church has long demonstrated an unwavering commitment to 
Christian fellowship, education, service, and outreach.
  The modern era of Peaceful Rest Missionary Baptist Church began in 
1994 with the arrival of Rev. Dr. R. Timothy Jones, whose visionary 
leadership ushered in unprecedented growth and revitalization. Under 
Dr. Jones' compassionate guidance and dynamic preaching, the 
congregation grew from approximately 200 members to over 2,000, 
establishing the church as one of the most vibrant and active faith 
communities in Shreveport. His ministry strengthened Peaceful Rest's 
presence in Cedar Grove, deepened its engagement with families, 
seniors, and youth, and expanded its capacity to meet the spiritual and 
practical needs of the community.
  Peaceful Rest's impact is felt not only within its sanctuary but also 
throughout Shreveport's neighborhoods, where the church has become a 
trusted anchor of support and service. Through scholarship programs, 
educational initiatives, senior ministries, recreational activities, 
and family support services, the church has consistently invested in 
the growth and well-being of its members and neighbors. The beloved 
`Church in the Park' outreach initiative exemplifies Peaceful Rest's 
mission to meet people where they are--offering fellowship, hope, and 
spiritual nourishment beyond the church walls. Additionally, the Cedar 
Grove Community Development Corporation, the learning center, and the 
adult academy reflect the church's dedication to addressing long-term 
community needs, including education, workforce development, and 
neighborhood revitalization.
  Peaceful Rest's influence extends far beyond Shreveport. With the 
establishment of a church in Malawi, Africa, in 2016, the congregation 
demonstrated its commitment to global missions and international 
Christian outreach. This act of service underscores the church's belief 
that its calling reaches beyond local boundaries to uplift communities 
around the world. Through this expansion, Peaceful Rest has become not 
only a regional spiritual leader but also a participant in the global 
Christian mission.
  Today, Rev. Dr. R. Timothy Jones continues his more than three 
decades of devoted leadership, guiding a congregation that remains 
deeply rooted in faith, service, and community engagement. Peaceful 
Rest Missionary Baptist Church stands as a testament to what a faith 
community can achieve when it embraces both spiritual growth and social 
responsibility. Its remarkable legacy reflects the enduring power of 
faith to transform lives, strengthen communities, and inspire hope 
across generations. I urge my colleagues to join me in acknowledging 
Peaceful Rest's enduring legacy, and service to the Shreveport 
community.
```
