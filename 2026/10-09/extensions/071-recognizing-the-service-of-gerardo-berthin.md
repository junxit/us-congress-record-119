# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF GERARDO BERTHIN

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
               RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF GERARDO BERTHIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
commend Gerardo Berthin, President of Freedom House, for more than a 
decade of tireless dedication and exemplary service in advancing the 
cause of global democracy, particularly during the most recent 
disruptions in the freedom and democracy landscape globally and within 
the United States.
  In an era marked by profound challenges to democratic institutions, 
Mr. Berthin has demonstrated unparalleled leadership and resolve. 
Relentlessly, alongside the entire leadership team and staff of Freedom 
House, he has protected dissidents from harm around the world, 
leveraging his extensive network and strong, trusted relationships to 
safeguard those who stand against oppression and tyranny. His efforts 
have provided critical support to vulnerable individuals and 
communities, ensuring their voices are heard and their rights defended 
amid rising threats.
  Furthermore, with great determination, Mr. Berthin has transitioned 
Freedom House with dignity and care for its staff and partners, while 
incredibly navigating the volatile worldwide of democratic backsliding, 
resulting from years of unchecked authoritarian rule. Under his 
guidance, the organization has adapted to these turbulent times while 
maintaining its core mission, emerging stronger and more resilient in 
its commitment to human rights, freedom of expression, and the rule of 
law.
  Freedom House, founded in 1941 by Eleanor Roosevelt and Wendell 
Willkie, remains a cornerstone of American leadership in the global 
fight for freedom and democracy. Mr. Berthin's stewardship has upheld 
and enhanced this legacy, positioning the institution as an 
indispensable force in countering authoritarianism and fostering 
democratic renewal worldwide.
  His leadership and commitment will be missed greatly at Freedom 
House, as his mark of excellence will be recorded in the historical 
annals and remembered by all who have witnessed his profound impact. On 
behalf of the U.S. House of Representatives, we wish him continued 
success and look forward to his enduring leadership in the continuous 
struggle for a world where all are free.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Gerardo Berthin's 
extraordinary service and the enduring legacy of Freedom House--a 
legacy that continues to inspire hope and action in the pursuit of 
freedom for all.
```
