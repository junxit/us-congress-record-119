# RECOGNIZING KEVIN HAYS, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING KEVIN HAYS, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Kevin Hays, OPC Line Superintendent with Withlacoochee 
River Electric Cooperative (WREC), for his leadership and outstanding 
service as part of WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo 
Project. The Light Up Navajo initiative brings together utility 
professionals who volunteer their specialized skills to extend 
electricity to Navajo families who have lived without power for 
generations. As a Line Superintendent and supervisor, Mr. Hays brought 
valuable technical expertise and leadership to this demanding mission, 
helping his team carry out its work safely, efficiently, and 
professionally. For the families served through this initiative, 
electricity represents far more than convenience. It can mean light for 
a child to study after dark, refrigeration for food and medicine, and 
greater safety and comfort for an entire household. Mr. Hays and his 
colleagues understood the importance of this mission and willingly put 
their expertise to work to improve the lives of others. Their efforts 
are a powerful example of the cooperative principle of neighbors 
helping neighbors and demonstrate how skilled professionals can use 
their talents to make a lasting difference.
  I commend Mr. Kevin Hays for his leadership, technical expertise, and 
dedication to this worthwhile mission. His service reflects great 
credit upon WREC and the community we are proud to call home.

                          ____________________
```
