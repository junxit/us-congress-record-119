# HONORING CAROLINE GAUTHIER'S SERVICE AS PRESS SECRETARY

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
        HONORING CAROLINE GAUTHIER'S SERVICE AS PRESS SECRETARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate 
Caroline Gauthier for her service in my office as Press Secretary.
  Caroline, a native Minnesotan, was truly passionate in communicating 
our stories to our constituents. She dramatically improved our social 
media footprint and regularly had some of the most read newsletters my 
office has ever seen.

[[Page E1047]]

  I know Caroline is passionate about public service as she previously 
interned for both Congresswoman McCollum and Leader Hakeem Jeffries. I 
can't wait to see what she does next.

                          ____________________
```
