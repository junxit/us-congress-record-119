# RECOGNIZING EDDIE SANTIAGO DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
       RECOGNIZING EDDIE SANTIAGO DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Hispanic American 
Heritage Month to recognize a dedicated public servant and labor leader 
from Rochester, New York: Eddie Santiago.
  For 30 years, Eddie served the people of Rochester as a member of the 
Rochester Fire Department, rising to the rank of Lieutenant. Throughout 
his career, he demonstrated the courage, discipline, and commitment to 
others that define the firefighting profession.
  But Eddie's service has never stopped at the firehouse door.
  Beginning in 2015, he served as President of IAFF Local 1071, 
representing nearly 500 Rochester firefighters and becoming a trusted 
advocate for the men and women who put their lives on the line to keep 
our community safe.
  In 2023, Eddie took on an even broader responsibility as First 
District Vice President of the New York State Professional Fire 
Fighters Association, representing 28 IAFF unions across eight counties 
in Western New York.
  His colleagues once again placed their trust in him when he was 
selected to serve as Secretary/Treasurer of the New York State 
Professional Fire Fighters Association, the first member of the 
Rochester Fire Department to serve in a statewide leadership role with 
the organization.
  It is my great privilege to recognize my great friend Eddie Santiago 
during Hispanic American Heritage Month. His decades of service, 
leadership, and advocacy have made Rochester stronger, and I thank him 
for his enduring commitment to the people and firefighters he has so 
proudly represented.

                          ____________________
```
