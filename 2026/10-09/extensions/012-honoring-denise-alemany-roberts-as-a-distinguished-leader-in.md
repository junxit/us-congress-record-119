# HONORING DENISE ALEMANY ROBERTS AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING DENISE ALEMANY ROBERTS AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Denise Alemany Roberts serves as the Deputy 
Chief of Police for the St. Cloud Police Department and is the first 
woman in the department's history to hold the rank.
  A law enforcement professional since 1997, Roberts began her public 
safety career with the Kissimmee Police Department before joining the 
St. Cloud Police Department as a Sworn Police Officer in 2001. Over two 
decades of service to St. Cloud and Central Florida, she has advanced 
from Police Officer to Sergeant, Lieutenant, Captain, and now Deputy 
Chief of Police overseeing the Operations Bureau.
  Throughout her tenure, Roberts has led Patrol Operations, Special 
Operations, and Criminal Investigations Divisions. Her leadership has 
advanced public safety through intelligence-led policing, major 
criminal investigations, critical incident command, and modernization 
of department operations. She oversees the Real-Time Intelligence 
Center and led the successful 2025 launch of St. Cloud's Drone as First 
Responder program, enhancing rapid response, situational awareness, 
officer safety, and public safety. She has helped implement advanced 
Axon technologies, FUSUS integration, body-worn cameras, and virtual 
reality-based officer training. A member of the Crisis Negotiation Team 
since 2003, Roberts currently serves as the High-Risk Incident 
Commander, directing tactical communication and de-escalation during 
high-risk incidents.
  Born in San Juan, Puerto Rico, Roberts moved to Central Florida in 
1988 and is bilingual in English and Spanish. In 2023, she became the 
first woman and first St. Cloud Police Department representative to 
attend the FBI National Academy, graduating from Session 286. She 
earned her Bachelor of Arts in Criminal Justice Administration and 
Master of Science in Criminal Justice--Law Enforcement Administration 
from Columbia College. Her service has been recognized through numerous 
departmental awards, including Distinguished Service and Administrative 
Excellence Awards, Chiefs Commendations, and Leadership Awards.
  Roberts remains committed to professional leadership, innovation, 
community partnership, and public service throughout St. Cloud, Osceola 
County, and Central Florida.
```
