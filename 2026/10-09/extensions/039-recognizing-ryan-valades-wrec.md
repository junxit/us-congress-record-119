# RECOGNIZING RYAN VALADES, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                     RECOGNIZING RYAN VALADES, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Ryan Valade, a WH Lineman with Withlacoochee River 
Electric Cooperative (WREC), for his exceptional service as part of 
WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. The Light 
Up Navajo initiative relies upon skilled utility professionals willing 
to volunteer their time and expertise to help bring electricity to 
Navajo families who have lived without power for generations. Mr. 
Valade answered that call, contributing his technical knowledge and 
hands-on experience to this challenging and worthwhile mission. 
Reliable electricity can fundamentally improve daily life. It gives 
children light to study after dark, allows families to refrigerate food 
and medicine, and provides greater safety and comfort for everyone in 
the home. By helping extend electrical service, Mr. Valade and his 
colleagues helped create opportunities that will continue long after 
their work is completed. His willingness to travel far from home and 
put his specialized skills to work for families in need embodies the 
cooperative principle of neighbors helping neighbors.
  I commend Mr. Ryan Valade for his technical expertise, 
professionalism, and commitment to service. His contribution to Light 
Up Navajo reflects great credit upon WREC and our entire community.

                          ____________________
```
