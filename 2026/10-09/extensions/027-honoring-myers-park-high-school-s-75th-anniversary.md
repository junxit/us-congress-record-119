# HONORING MYERS PARK HIGH SCHOOL'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
           HONORING MYERS PARK HIGH SCHOOL'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina-

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 75th 
anniversary of the opening of my alma mater, Myers Park High School, in 
Charlotte, North Carolina.
  From September 22nd through the 26th, Myers Park students, faculty, 
and alumni celebrated the anniversary. The celebrations included 
recognition of the milestones and traditions which have shaped Myers 
Park over the decades. Myers Park also had a special anniversary home 
football game in Gus Purcell stadium, where the Mustangs went on to 
defeat Charlotte Country Day School by a score of 32-29.
  For three quarters of a century, Myers Park has played a lasting role 
in shaping the Charlotte community, providing generations of students 
with the knowledge, opportunities, and foundation to lead and serve 
with distinction. As a graduate of the Class of 1990, Myers Park High 
School holds a special place in my heart and helped shape the person I 
am today.
  I also want to recognize the Myers Park Foundation. The Foundation 
strengthens the educational experience at Myers Park by expanding 
opportunities for students, meeting critical needs, and supporting 
innovative initiatives to enrich their education. The Foundation 
provides graduation coaches, college visits and trade fairs to help 
students plan for their future. I am incredibly proud of the Myers Park 
Foundation and all the work they do providing opportunities for 
America's next generation.
  I want to thank Myers Park High School for the opportunities provided 
to students and the community for the past 75 years. As Myers Park 
looks toward the future, I am confident its tradition of excellence 
will continue for generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today celebrating the 75th anniversary of 
Myers Park High School.

                          ____________________
```
