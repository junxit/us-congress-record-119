# HONORING SIRI HOFF'S SERVICE AS LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
         HONORING SIRI HOFF'S SERVICE AS LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Siri 
Hoff for her service to my congressional office (and her home district) 
of Minnesota's Second District.
  Siri started in my office as a Staff Assistant, handling several 
types of constituent requests and providing exceptional service. She 
quickly became my Legislative Correspondent, writing dozens of 
constituent response letters to all kinds of policy issues. Siri has 
been vital to my work as a Co-Chair of the Congressional Equality 
Caucus and helped lead the introduction of the Every Child Deserves a 
Family Act and became a Legislative Assistant.
  Siri has a passion for ensuring the folks in Minnesota's Second 
District get the best representation possible and fighting for them. I 
can't wait to see what she does next.

                          ____________________
```
