# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ADA BROWN

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ADA BROWN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Ada Brown as she is honored as one of California's 8th 
Congressional District's Women of the Year. Ada is a dedicated leader, 
community advocate, and nonprofit executive committed to creating 
opportunities for young people and families in underserved communities 
throughout the Bay Area.
  In 2018, following four years of community-focused work, Ada formally 
established the Lady Echelon Project, Inc., a nonprofit organization 
dedicated to youth development. Through Lady Echelon, she has worked to 
provide young people with educational resources, job-readiness 
training, and essential life skills. Recognizing the importance of 
preparing students for emerging industries, her organization partnered 
with the Solano County Workforce Development Board in 2025 to host a 
biotechnology career fair at Solano Community College's Vallejo campus. 
The event connected more than 200 local high school students with 
industry professionals and potential career pathways.
  Drawing from her own experiences, Ada has dedicated herself to 
creating opportunities for others. As a young mother who grew up in an 
under-resourced community, she understands the barriers that can limit 
access to opportunity. These experiences have shaped her commitment to 
empowering young people and families, a mission she continues to 
advance through her work.
  Her leadership reflects resilience and a firm belief that every young 
person deserves access to opportunities, regardless of their background 
or economic circumstances. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Ada 
Brown for her positive contributions to our community.
```
