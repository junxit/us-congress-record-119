# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TERESA TATE

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TERESA TATE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Teresa Tate as 
she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. For more than 25 years, Teresa has served the 
Chinese American Association of Solano County (CAASC), helping provide 
scholarships to over 400 students totaling more than $300,000. As the 
organization's treasurer for over a decade, she has helped maintain its 
financial stability and ensure its continued ability to support 
students and serve the community. Beyond her work managing the 
organization's finances, Teresa volunteers on numerous CAASC 
committees, including those dedicated to scholarships, silent auctions, 
and raffles. Her commitment to education has helped sustain annual 
scholarship opportunities for Solano County students, while her service 
has also supported community outreach efforts, including card-making 
activities for seniors during the pandemic.
  Teresa has also played an important role in celebrating and promoting 
Chinese and Asian American cultures throughout Solano County. Through 
her involvement in organizing Chinese New Year and Lunar New Year 
celebrations, as well as ensuring CAASC's participation in Vacaville's 
Fiesta Days Parade and Fairfield's Fourth of July Parade, she has 
helped strengthen cultural visibility and bring the community together.
  Through her decades of dedicated service, Teresa has demonstrated a 
steadfast commitment to education, cultural appreciation, and community 
engagement. Her contributions have strengthened CAASC and helped create 
meaningful opportunities for residents throughout Solano County. On 
behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is 
my pleasure to honor and recognize Teresa Tate for her longstanding 
service and contributions to our community.
```
