# RECOGNIZING SAM HORNER, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING SAM HORNER, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Sam Horner, a CR Lineman with Withlacoochee River 
Electric Cooperative (WREC), for his outstanding service as part of 
WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. Through 
Light Up Navajo, utility professionals volunteer their skills to help 
bring electricity to Navajo families who have lived without this basic 
necessity for generations. Mr. Horner joined his colleagues in 
undertaking the difficult, hands-on work necessary to extend reliable 
electrical service to families in need. For many Americans, electricity 
is so much a part of everyday life that we rarely stop to consider its 
importance. Yet access to power can mean light for children to study 
after dark, refrigeration for food and medicine, and greater comfort 
and security for families and elders.
  Mr. Horner's willingness to use his professional skills in service to 
others reflects the very best of WREC and the cooperative tradition. 
His hard work helped further a mission that brings not only 
electricity, but also greater opportunity and hope. I commend Mr. Sam 
Horner for his craftsmanship, dedication, and commitment to serving 
others. His contribution to this worthwhile mission reflects great 
credit upon WREC and our community.

                          ____________________
```
