# RECOGNIZING ED KENEALY

**Speaking:** Neal, Richard E. (N000015) [D-MA]

```
                         RECOGNIZING ED KENEALY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD E. NEAL

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. NEAL. Mr. Speaker, I am pleased to recognize and congratulate Ed 
Kenealy on his retirement at the end of this year from Liberty Mutual, 
where he serves as Executive Vice President and Deputy General Counsel, 
after more than two decades with the company.
  I have had the pleasure of knowing Ed for many years. With his great 
sense of humor and dedication to his profession, he has earned a strong 
reputation as a knowledgeable and invaluable resource for Liberty 
Mutual and his community. A graduate of Dartmouth College and 
Vanderbilt Law School, Ed joined Liberty Mutual in 2005 and has held 
many senior positions, including Senior Vice President for Liberty 
International and Chief Public Affairs Officer. In these prestigious 
roles, he has been an effective advocate for Liberty Mutual and his 
industry. In addition, his work with many other organizations, 
especially the New England Council and the Greater Boston Chamber of 
Commerce, has greatly benefited his community and the Commonwealth of 
Massachusetts as a whole. In particular, Ed has been a dedicated 
advocate for and participant in the Pan-Mass Challenge, an annual bike-
a-thon that brings cyclists across Massachusetts to raise money for 
cancer treatment and research.
  Mr. Speaker, I want to join Ed's wife, Deborah, his children, Diana 
and Andrew, and all his friends, family, and colleagues in celebrating 
his tremendous career. On behalf of the United States of America, I 
wish him all the best in his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
