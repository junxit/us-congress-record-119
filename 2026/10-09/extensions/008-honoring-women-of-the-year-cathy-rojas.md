# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CATHY ROJAS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CATHY ROJAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Cathy Rojas as 
one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. A 
lifelong Vallejo resident, Cathy was born and raised in the community 
she continues to serve today. A graduate of St. Vincent's High School, 
she built both her family and professional life in Vallejo and has 
become a trusted and familiar presence throughout the city.
  Cathy has worked as a full-time realtor since 2004, earning a 
reputation for professionalism, honesty, strong communication, and 
dedication to her clients. Her leadership in the real estate profession 
has included serving as President of the Solano Association of 
REALTORS, and she has received numerous professional honors throughout 
her career.
  Cathy's impact, however, extends far beyond real estate. She is an 
active member of Soroptimist International of Vallejo and has served as 
the club's president, helping advance its mission of improving the 
lives of women and girls through education, empowerment, and 
opportunity. She has also served on the board of St. Patrick-St. 
Vincent Catholic High School and is an alumna of Leadership Vallejo.
  Those who know Cathy also know her for her generosity and willingness 
to step forward whenever her community needs support. She gives freely 
of her time, resources, and energy and remains a dedicated champion for 
local organizations, causes, and neighbors throughout Vallejo. On 
behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is 
my pleasure to honor her as a 2026 Woman of the Year and recognize her 
many positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
