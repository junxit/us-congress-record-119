# HONORING PASTOR ERIC B. AMPADU

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING PASTOR ERIC B. AMPADU

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record a 
proclamation to honor Pastor Eric B. Ampadu, a spiritual leader in 
South Florida, on this occasion of becoming chaplain in the United 
States Army:

       Whereas, Pastor Ampadu is the outgoing senior pastor of 
     Northside Seventh Day Adventist Church located in Miami-Dade 
     County. He was called to the gospel ministry in 1999 and 
     ordained as a minister in 2010. He received a Bachelor of 
     Arts degree in Theology in 2000 from Oakwood University. In 
     2007 he received a Master of Divinity degree from Andrews 
     Theological Seminary and in 2012 a Master of Science degree 
     in Criminal Justice from Capella University; and
       Whereas, Pastor Ampadu was born in Ghana, West Africa. He 
     migrated to the United States in 1996. Pastor Ampadu is a 
     family-oriented individual and has been married for 22 years 
     to Elta who is a counselor. He has three children, Faith at 
     Oakwood University in Huntsville, Alabama, Mayia and Jeremiah 
     at Pine Forge Academy in Pennsylvania; and
       Whereas, Pastor Ampadu has pastored churches in Alabama, 
     Mississippi, Tennessee, Kentucky, Michigan, Florida and 
     Canada; and
       Whereas Pastor Ampadu is a member of the 5,000 Role Models 
     of Excellence Project; and
       Whereas, in the past 5 years, under Pastor Ampadu's 
     leadership the Northside church has sponsored several 
     successful and highly beneficial programs for the community; 
     and
       Whereas Pastor Ampadu is a faithful man of God. Unselfish 
     with his time as he routinely fellowships with the sick and 
     shut ins. Including those who are not members of his church; 
     and
       Whereas Pastor Ampadu accepted another call to serve God 
     and his country. He will be a chaplain in the United States 
     Army. Captain Ampadu will be stationed at Fort Drum in 
     Northern New York State. Pastor Ampadu is a very caring and 
     community-oriented individual. Pastor Ampadu's spiritual 
     leadership will truly be missed in our church and in the 
     community,
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     honored to recognize Pastor Eric B. Ampadu for his 
     commitment, dedication and service to our community and our 
     country.

                          ____________________
```
