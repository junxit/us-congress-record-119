# HONORING YINETTE LIZARDO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING YINETTE LIZARDO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                      FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Yinette Lizardo is a U.S. Army combat Veteran and the 
County Veteran Service Officer for Osceola County, Florida, where she 
has dedicated her post-military career to advocating for Veterans, 
their families, and survivors.
  Originally from the Dominican Republic, Lizardo served more than 10 
years on active duty in the United States Army. Her military service 
included deployments in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom and 
Operation Enduring Freedom, as well as a humanitarian mission in Africa 
supporting the response to the Ebola outbreak. Her experiences in 
uniform shaped a lifelong commitment to service and a deep 
understanding of the challenges Veterans and their families face both 
during and after military service.
  Today, Lizardo continues that mission by serving the Veteran 
community throughout Osceola County and Florida's Ninth Congressional 
District. As County Veteran Service Officer, she assists Veterans, 
dependents, and survivors in navigating the complex U.S. Department of 
Veterans Affairs system and accessing the benefits they have earned 
through military service. Under her leadership, Osceola County Veterans 
Services has served more than 2,000 Veterans and dependents this year 
and generated millions of dollars in return on investment through 
Veterans' benefits and services brought back into the community.
  Beyond claims assistance, Lizardo has worked to strengthen Veteran 
outreach, expand access to services, build community partnerships, and 
connect Veterans with employment, housing, healthcare, and other 
critical resources.
  For Lizardo, serving Veterans is more than a profession; it is a 
continuation of her military service. Her passion after leaving the 
Army has remained firmly rooted in her Veteran community and in 
ensuring that those who served our nation, along with their families 
and survivors, are seen, supported, and never forgotten.
```
