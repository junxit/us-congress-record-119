# HONORING CALVARY MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
               HONORING CALVARY MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Calvary Missionary 
Baptist Church of Shreveport, Louisiana, which was established on 
February 22, 1948. For nearly 80 years, Calvary has been a ministry 
dedicated to faith, worship, and service, and a steady source of 
spiritual guidance and fellowship for the people of Shreveport. Its 
story is woven into the story of the city's faith community, and it 
stands as a reminder of what a congregation can mean to the place it 
calls home.
  Over the decades, Calvary has served generations of residents and 
maintained a strong presence in the surrounding community. For many 
families, the church has been the place where faith was taught, where 
milestones were celebrated, and where neighbors gathered for worship 
and fellowship. That continuity has made Calvary more than a building 
on a street. It has been a spiritual home that has helped hold the 
community together across changing times.
  Calvary has also played an important role in the life of Shreveport 
through worship, prayer, outreach, and efforts to support residents in 
times of need. Its ministry reflects a commitment to strengthening 
families, encouraging faith, and addressing the needs of the broader 
Shreveport community. A church that serves in this way meets people 
where they are and shows, through service, that faith is meant to be 
lived out in the community as well as in the sanctuary.
  Public records document the church's long history of community 
engagement, including its annual citywide Prayer Vigil. The vigil 
brought together church members and community leaders to pray for 
educators, elected officials, law enforcement, and the nation. By 
lifting up the people who teach our children, serve in public office, 
and keep our communities safe, Calvary used its voice to unite the

[[Page E1054]]

faith community of Shreveport and showed that a church's prayers can 
reach well beyond its own walls.
  The church is associated with Dr. Joe Rascoe Gant, Jr., who served as 
its pastor for more than four decades and was recognized by the City of 
Shreveport for his service to the church and community. Pastoral 
leadership sustained over so many years gives a congregation continuity 
and direction. It is a mark of the faithfulness and commitment to 
service that have defined Calvary's ministry and its place among the 
churches of Shreveport.
```
