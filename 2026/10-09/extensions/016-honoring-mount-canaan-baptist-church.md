# HONORING MOUNT CANAAN BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
                  HONORING MOUNT CANAAN BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mount Canaan 
Baptist Church of Shreveport, Louisiana, a historic institution of 
faith, fellowship, spiritual leadership, and community service. 
Established in 1894 by six members of Galilee Baptist Church, Mount 
Canaan has stood for more than 130 years as a cornerstone of the 
Allendale community and a spiritual home for generations of individuals 
and families.
  From its humble beginnings, Mount Canaan Baptist Church was founded 
on the vision that families should have a place to gather for worship, 
grow in their relationship with God, and serve the surrounding 
community. As the church grew, it became one of Shreveport's 
longstanding religious institutions, providing stability, 
encouragement, spiritual guidance, and fellowship during periods of 
significant social, economic, and cultural change.
  Throughout its history, Mount Canaan has remained deeply connected to 
the people of Allendale and the broader Shreveport community. Its 
ministry has extended beyond the walls of the sanctuary through 
Christian education, youth development, outreach, fellowship, and 
community service. In doing so, the church has demonstrated a lasting 
commitment to strengthening families, supporting those in need, 
developing young people, and promoting Christian values.
  A defining chapter in the history of Mount Canaan began in 1966 with 
the pastoral leadership of Reverend Harry Blake. During his remarkable 
52 years of service, Reverend Blake provided spiritual leadership 
during a period of growth and transformation for the congregation and 
the surrounding community. Under his guidance, Mount Canaan expanded 
its ministry, strengthened its presence in Shreveport, and continued 
building upon the foundation established by its early members.
  One of the most significant milestones during Reverend Blake's tenure 
was the construction of Mount Canaan's current sanctuary at 1666 Alston 
Street. Completed in 1976, the sanctuary represented not only the 
physical growth of the congregation but also the faith, sacrifice, and 
dedication of its members. The continued development of the church's 
facilities and ministries enabled Mount Canaan to better serve the 
spiritual and community needs of its congregation.
  For generations, Mount Canaan Baptist Church has served as more than 
a place of Sunday worship. It has been a spiritual home where children 
have been raised in the faith, families have celebrated important 
milestones, community members have gathered during times of joy and 
hardship, and individuals have found encouragement and guidance. 
Through its ministries and service, Mount Canaan has consistently 
demonstrated its commitment to ministering to the whole person and 
strengthening the community it calls home.
  The legacy of Mount Canaan is reflected in the generations of 
pastors, ministers, church leaders, deacons, ministry workers, 
volunteers, families, and faithful members who have devoted their time, 
talents, and resources to advancing the mission of the church. Their 
collective service has allowed Mount Canaan to remain rooted in its 
history while continuing to respond to the changing needs of the 
community.
  Today, under the leadership of Reverend Greg L. Oliver, Sr., Mount 
Canaan Baptist Church continues to build upon the strong foundation 
established more than a century ago. The congregation remains committed 
to worship, Christian discipleship, community engagement, youth 
development, fellowship, and service. While honoring the sacrifices and 
accomplishments of those who came before, Mount Canaan continues to 
look toward the future and seek new opportunities to positively impact 
individuals, families, and the broader Shreveport community.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring Reverend 
Oliver and the faithful congregation of Mount Canaan Baptist Church for 
more than 130 years of faith, perseverance, fellowship, and dedicated 
service. From six founding members in 1894 to the generations of 
worshippers who have called Mount Canaan their spiritual home, the 
church's history represents a remarkable legacy of commitment and 
endurance. May Mount Canaan Baptist Church continue to serve as a 
beacon of faith, hope, fellowship, and service for generations to come.
```
