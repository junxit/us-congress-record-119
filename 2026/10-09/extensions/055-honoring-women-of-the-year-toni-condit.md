# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TONI CONDIT

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TONI CONDIT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Toni Condit as 
she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. As co-founder and Board Chair of Future Leadership 
Solano (FLS), Toni has dedicated herself to expanding opportunities for 
young people to develop leadership skills and become active 
participants in their communities.
  Since its founding, the program has provided leadership training and 
community engagement opportunities to nearly 80 local youth, connecting 
them with civic, business, educational, and nonprofit leaders. Through 
these experiences and hands-on service projects, participants learn how 
their communities operate and how they can make a meaningful 
difference.
  Beyond her work with FLS, Toni remains actively involved in civic and 
community organizations, including the Vallejo Chamber of Commerce and 
Rotary Club. She also supports the annual Vallejo Fourth of July Parade 
and serves on the board of Vallejo Waterfront Weekend, helping bring 
residents together to celebrate their community and strengthen local 
connections.
  Across her many roles, Toni demonstrates a genuine commitment to 
service and inspiring others to give back. Her leadership has helped 
grow Future Leadership Vallejo into Future Leadership Solano, extending 
opportunities for young people throughout the county. By encouraging 
the next generation to take an active role in shaping their 
communities, Toni is helping cultivate future leaders whose 
contributions will continue for years to come. On behalf of the people 
of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor 
and recognize Toni Condit for her dedicated service, commitment to 
youth leadership, and lasting contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
