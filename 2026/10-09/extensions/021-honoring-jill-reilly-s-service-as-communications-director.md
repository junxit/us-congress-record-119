# HONORING JILL REILLY'S SERVICE AS COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTOR

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
       HONORING JILL REILLY'S SERVICE AS COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTOR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Jill 
Reilly for an amazing two years as my Communications Director.
  Jill is one of the most skilled communications professionals I've 
ever worked with. She's been a true professional having brought 
strengths with her from Senator Jon Tester's office.
  While it may have been only two years, the success of Jill in our 
office feels like several more. Jill has some of the best pitching 
skills for an elected official that I have ever seen and is skilled 
enough to craft a successful narrative with all types of reporters.
  I'll always be grateful for the work Jill did in traveling with me to 
El Paso to better share a constituent's story and work for their 
release from ICE detention.
  I know Jill will continue to be a strong public servant and 
communications professional and I wish her the best in her future.

                          ____________________
```
