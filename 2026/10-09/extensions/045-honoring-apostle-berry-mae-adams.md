# HONORING APOSTLE BERRY MAE ADAMS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                    HONORING APOSTLE BERRY MAE ADAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record a 
proclamation in recognition of Apostle Berry Mae Adams, as she is 
honored by Heart of God Ministries, family, and friends for her many 
contributions to the community through 45 years of service to the 
Kingdom of God:

       Whereas, Apostle Berry Mae Adams was born, in Sidon, 
     Mississippi, to the late Vernie and Thomas Lewis of Osceola, 
     Arkansas. In 1946, her family moved back to Arkansas to live 
     the farm life for ten years. In December of 1956, the family 
     of eleven relocated when Vernie was able to secure seasonal 
     work in Miami, Florida; and
       Whereas, in 1961 on New Year's Eve Day, Apostle Adams 
     received Jesus as her personal Lord and Savior. She developed 
     an authentic personal relationship with Christ through 
     prayer, study of the Bible and serving in many capacities in 
     her local church. This journey led her to obtain a Doctor of 
     Divinity degree; and
       Whereas, with 5 adults and 6 children, Apostle Adams 
     founded The Bible Way Prayer Mission church in 1976 where she 
     currently serves as Apostle of the ministry. Bible Way has 
     been a part of the Liberty City community for more than 45 
     years with its first location opening on Northwest 62nd 
     Street and 22nd Avenue; and
       Whereas, Apostle Adams' ministry specializes in helping 
     hurt people. She supports the homeless by providing shelter 
     to them in her own home as well as in the church building. 
     Clothing drives, feeding the homeless, after school tutoring, 
     and toys for tots drives are amongst the projects initiated 
     through her ministry. Apostle Adams serves as an advisor to 
     parents whose children have become wards of the state. She 
     helps families to be reunited. The Bible Way Prayer Mission 
     holds special services that are organized and facilitated by 
     children to prepare the next generation for future ministry 
     involvement and leadership; and
       Whereas, Apostle Adams works with the police department to 
     help prevent crimes by counseling Liberty City and Brown Sub 
     residents. She also works with Promise Keepers Ministry to 
     encourage young men and women to abstain from fornication as 
     a method to prevent HIV/Aids and teenage pregnancy. Apostle 
     Adams has poured her lifetime of learning and experience into 
     the people she serves; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     honor Apostle Berry Adams for all her contributions to Miami-
     Dade County.

                          ____________________
```
