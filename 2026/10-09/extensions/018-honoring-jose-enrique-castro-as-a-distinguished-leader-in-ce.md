# HONORING JOSE ENRIQUE CASTRO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING JOSE ENRIQUE CASTRO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Jose Enrique Castro is from Jayuya, Puerto 
Rico. He has dedicated much of his career to helping workers understand 
their rights, address workplace concerns, and make their voices heard. 
His work serves employees of all backgrounds, with a particular focus 
on those whose first language is Spanish. He also provides Spanish 
interpretation at meetings and union activities.
  Jose Enrique began working at Disney in 2009 as a Housekeeper at 
Coronado Springs. In 2012, he became involved with UNITE HERE Local 
737. He later saved as a Lead Organizer and Housekeeping Director, 
working with employees in housekeeping and food and beverage.
  In September 2026, Jose Enrique became a Director of Local 737's new 
Grievance Department, where he helps investigate workplace issues and 
represent union members. He is also studying business administration 
and hopes to use what he learns to continue serving workers and his 
community.
```
