# HONORING THE SERVICE OF HARVEY LAKE

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

```
                  HONORING THE SERVICE OF HARVEY LAKE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM WALBERG

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to recognize Harvey Lake 
of Jackson as the Veteran of the Month for October and honor him for 
his service on behalf of a grateful Nation.
  Harvey was drafted into the United States Army in 1972, answering the 
call to serve his country during a pivotal time in our nation's 
history. He completed his basic training at Fort Knox, Kentucky, 
followed by Advanced Individual Training at Fort Jackson, South 
Carolina, where he trained as a 63B20 Wheel Vehicle Mechanic. Following 
his training, Harvey was stationed in Ludwigsburg, Germany, where he 
served for over 18 months with Company B, 34th Signal Battalion, VII 
Corps. His unit played an important role in relaying communications to 
the VII Corps at Kelly Barracks in Stuttgart, Germany, and contributing 
to the United States' efforts during the Cold War.
  Harvey completed his service in June 1974 and was later honorably 
discharged from the Army in June 1978 at the rank of Specialist 4. For 
his service, Harvey earned the National Defense Medal, Marksman 
recognition, and a Wheel Vehicle Mechanic Certificate. His commitment 
to our nation is also part of a proud family tradition of military 
service. His father served during World War II as a B-24 gunner in the 
Pacific, while his brother Roger also answered the call to serve in the 
United States Army and was honorably discharged.
  Since his time in the military, Harvey has continued to exemplify 
service, hard work, and devotion to his community. Though retired, he 
remains active in his community, reflecting the same character and 
determination that defined his military career.
  Harvey Lake's faithful service and continued commitment to both our 
nation's armed services and Michigan's local communities should serve 
as an inspiration to us all. May others know of my high regard for 
Harvey, as well as my deep gratitude for his lasting contributions to 
the country we love.
```
