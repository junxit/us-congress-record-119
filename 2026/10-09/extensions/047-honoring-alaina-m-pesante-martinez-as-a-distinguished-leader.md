# HONORING ALAINA M. PESANTE MARTINEZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING ALAINA M. PESANTE MARTINEZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN 
              CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Alaina M. Pesante Martinez is a dedicated 
educator and community leader whose commitment to students, families 
and the Hispanic community has made a meaningful impact in Florida. 
Born in Ponce, Puerto Rico, Mrs. Pesante began her teaching career in 
2004 and brought more than 11 years of experience teaching general 
education, art, and physical education before moving to Florida in 
2015.
  After arriving in Florida, Mrs. Pesante faced the challenge of 
continuing her career while overcoming a language barrier as a native 
Spanish speaker. She began volunteering at Mill Creek Elementary School 
while studying English at Valencia College. She later worked as an ESE 
paraprofessional and became an emotional behavioral disabilities 
teacher before continuing her career as an elementary educator.
  Mrs. Pesante graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of Puerto 
Rico, where she was also a collegiate volleyball athlete and achieved 
outstanding academic performance. She holds an elementary education 
certification with endorsements in reading and ESOL. Known for her 
enthusiastic, dynamic, creative, and authentic teaching, Mrs. Pesante 
has dedicated herself to helping students succeed academically. In 
2023, she was recognized for achieving the highest proficiency in 
reading and math at Mill Creek Elementary School. She was also 
recognized for outstanding achievement at her grade level.
  In 2023, Mrs. Pesante became the first Hispanic teacher at Mill Creek 
Elementary School to receive the Teacher of the Year award. She 
continues to serve her school as a first-grade team lead, Hispanic 
teacher mentor, PBIS member, and SAC member.
  Mrs. Pesante remains committed to supporting students, families, and 
fellow educators, particularly those facing language barriers. She 
proudly celebrates her Puerto Rican heritage and continues to model 
perseverance, leadership, education, and service within Florida's 
Hispanic community.
```
