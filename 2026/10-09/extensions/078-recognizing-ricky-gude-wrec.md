# RECOGNIZING RICKY GUDE, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING RICKY GUDE, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Ricky Gude, Team Lead with Withlacoochee River Electric 
Cooperative (WREC), for his outstanding leadership and service as part 
of WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. Mr. 
Gude joined his fellow WREC employees in answering a call that extends 
far beyond the traditional boundaries of their work. Through the Light 
Up Navajo initiative, utility professionals volunteer their expertise 
to help bring electricity to Navajo families who have lived without 
this basic necessity for generations. As Team Lead, Mr. Gude helped 
guide WREC's efforts and ensure that this important work was completed 
safely, professionally, and with the spirit of service that defines our 
electric cooperative community. For most Americans, flipping a switch 
and having the lights come on is something we rarely think about. Yet 
for families without electricity, access to power can be life-changing. 
It means children can study after dark, families can safely store food 
and medicine, and elders can enjoy greater safety and comfort in their 
homes.
  Mr. Gude and his colleagues chose to put their skills to work on 
behalf of families far from their own community. Their willingness to 
serve demonstrates that the cooperative principle of neighbors helping 
neighbors knows no geographic boundary. I commend Mr. Ricky Gude for 
his leadership, compassion, and commitment to this worthwhile mission. 
His service reflects great credit upon WREC and the community we are 
proud to call home.
```
