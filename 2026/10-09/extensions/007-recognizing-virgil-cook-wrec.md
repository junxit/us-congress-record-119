# RECOGNIZING VIRGIL COOK, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                     RECOGNIZING VIRGIL COOK, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Virgil Cook, a WH Lineman with Withlacoochee River 
Electric Cooperative (WREC), for his outstanding service as part of 
WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. Through 
Light Up Navajo, utility professionals volunteer their specialized 
skills to help extend electricity to Navajo families who have lived 
without power for generations. Mr. Cook joined his WREC colleagues in 
taking on this demanding work, demonstrating professionalism, 
perseverance, and a strong commitment to helping others. The 
significance of this mission goes far beyond installing electrical 
infrastructure. Reliable power means children can study after dark, 
families can safely store food and medicine, and elders can enjoy 
greater safety and comfort. It helps provide families with 
opportunities and conveniences that most Americans experience every 
day.
  Mr. Cook's participation in this mission demonstrates the power of 
service and the difference individuals can make when they choose to put 
their talents to work for others. His efforts embody the cooperative 
spirit of neighbors helping neighbors. I commend Mr. Virgil Cook for 
his dedication, skill, and commitment to improving the lives of 
families in need. His service reflects great credit upon WREC and our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
