# HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF TEMPLE HAR SHALOM

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
           HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF TEMPLE HAR SHALOM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 50th anniversary of 
Temple Har Shalom in Warren Township, New Jersey. For more than five 
decades, this Reform Synagogue has steadfastly supported Jewish 
communities across Somerset, Union, and Morris counties. Since its 
founding in 1970, Temple Har Shalom has served as a cornerstone of 
spiritual fulfillment, offering extensive educational programs for both 
children and adults. Under the leadership of Rabbi Joshua Greenbaum, 
who began serving as Senior Rabbi in July 2025, the Temple continues to 
embrace

[[Page E1063]]

Jewish tradition with an innovative, 21st-century approach.
  This year's milestone celebrates Temple Har Shalom's enduring 
dedication to service. Their commitment to a wide range of programs 
enables members of the Jewish diaspora to thrive within a welcoming and 
supportive community. Through weekly Shabbat services, lifecycle 
events, social activities, auxiliary groups, volunteer opportunities, 
as well as a premier preschool and comprehensive K-12 Jewish 
Educational Program, the Temple ensures that all members receive 
spiritual support and education.
  Mr. Speaker, I offer my sincere gratitude to Temple Har Shalom for 
their unwavering dedication to enriching and uplifting New Jersey's 
Jewish community. Their 50th anniversary stands as a significant 
milestone in our state's faith and cultural history. As we celebrate 
their achievements, I am confident that Temple Har Shalom will continue 
to make a lasting impact for many years to come.
```
