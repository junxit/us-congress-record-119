# HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BROWARD COUNTY MOREHOUSE COLLEGE ALUMNI CLUB

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BROWARD COUNTY MOREHOUSE COLLEGE 
                              ALUMNI CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor the Broward County Morehouse College Alumni Club on its 50th 
anniversary and to celebrate the generations of Morehouse men who have 
strengthened our community through leadership, service, fellowship, and 
a commitment to excellence.
  The story of this organization began in the fall of 1966, when a 
small group of Morehouse brothers gathered at the Fort Lauderdale Elks 
Lodge. The meeting, called by Brother Leonard Gainey II, was an effort 
to establish a Morehouse College Alumni Club in Broward County and to 
build an organization that would serve its members and the broader 
community. Like many organizations that have endured for generations, 
the Broward County Morehouse College Alumni Club experienced its share 
of growing pains. Yet its founders remained committed to the vision of 
creating a lasting brotherhood dedicated to the ideals of Morehouse 
College.
  Over the course of its 50-year journey, the club has been led by a 
remarkable succession of Morehouse alumni, including Norbert C. 
Williams, Benjamin F. Miller, Homer C. Arnold, Jr., Gordon Merritt, 
Arnold L. Williams, Herman Allen, William M. Nix, Travis Allen, Walton 
McDuffie, Robert Holmes, and Danny Holmes. Their leadership reflects 
the enduring Morehouse tradition of scholarship, service, and 
responsibility to community. As we commemorate this milestone, we 
recognize not only the organization's founders and past presidents, but 
also every brother who has contributed his time, talent, and service to 
keeping this legacy alive.
  I extend heartfelt congratulations to the Broward County Morehouse 
College Alumni Club on 50 years of brotherhood, leadership, service, 
and excellence, and recognize the countless Morehouse men whose 
contributions have helped strengthen Broward County and South Florida.
```
