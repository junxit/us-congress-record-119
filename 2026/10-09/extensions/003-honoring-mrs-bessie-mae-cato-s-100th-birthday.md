# HONORING MRS. BESSIE MAE CATO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
             HONORING MRS. BESSIE MAE CATO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the 
following proclamation to commemorate Mrs. Bessie Mae Cato on her 100th 
Birthday:

       Whereas, Mrs. Bessie Mae Cato was born on August 15, 1924, 
     in Quitman, Georgia, to Paris Holland and Mary E. (Williams) 
     Wright, and received her grade school education in Tampa, 
     Florida, where she was inspired by the great educator, Mary 
     McLeod Bethune; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Cato moved to South Florida in the 1930s, 
     where she graduated from Booker T. Washington High School in 
     Miami in the 1940s, and went on to have an illustrious career 
     working at the legendary Sir John Hotel and the famed Harlem 
     Square Nightclub, where she served as head waitress, meeting 
     and befriending iconic figures such as Ella Fitzgerald, Sarah 
     Vaughn, Flip Wilson, Jackie Robinson, Sammy Davis, Jr., and 
     Sam Cooke; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Cato was present during the historic 
     recording of Sam Cooke's Live at the Harlem Square Nightclub 
     in 1963, a night that remains etched in music history as one 
     of the greatest live performances ever captured; and
       Whereas, alongside her husband, Mr. Paul Cato, who served 
     as a personal assistant to Jackie Gleason and later became an 
     entrepreneur, Mrs. Cato herself became a successful Black 
     businesswoman and entrepreneur, opening and operating her own 
     hair salon, the ``Gay Peree,'' in the Overtown neighborhood 
     of Miami, where she served her community with grace and 
     dedication for more than 40 years until her retirement in 
     2002; and
       Whereas, Mrs. Cato, now a centenarian, continues to be 
     celebrated as a matriarch, a trailblazer, and a beloved 
     figure in her community, who has witnessed and contributed to 
     a century of history, progress, and cultural evolution; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     proud to honor and celebrate the extraordinary life of Mrs. 
     Bessie Mae Cato, and we extend our heartfelt wishes for her 
     continued health, happiness, and blessings.

                          ____________________
```
