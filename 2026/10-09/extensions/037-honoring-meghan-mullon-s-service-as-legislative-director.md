# HONORING MEGHAN MULLON'S SERVICE AS LEGISLATIVE DIRECTOR

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
        HONORING MEGHAN MULLON'S SERVICE AS LEGISLATIVE DIRECTOR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Meghan 
Mullon for an amazing four years and two full sessions of Congress with 
my office, most recently as my Legislative Director.
  A native Minnesotan, Meghan joined my team in 2023 from the Energy 
and Commerce Committee. She immediately began strengthening my office's 
work on health care and mental health legislation. Meghan is a true 
public servant at heart, always striving to provide constituents with 
the best possible legislative outcome. She has fostered several strong 
stakeholder relationships with our office. Meghan saw several bills she 
led signed into law and several others pass the House.
  She has also steered our legislative team during a trying time for 
Minnesota and navigated several complex political challenges. I'm 
confident Meghan will continue a long career in public service to 
Minnesotans.
  I've always appreciated Meghan's advice and counsel over the years 
and wish her the best in her future.

                          ____________________
```
