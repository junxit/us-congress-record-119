# HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF ANTIOCH BAPTIST CHURCH OF TYLER, SAND FLAT, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF ANTIOCH BAPTIST CHURCH OF TYLER, SAND 
                              FLAT, TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Antioch Baptist 
Church of Tyler, Texas, located in the Sand Flat community of Smith 
County, as it celebrates its 175th anniversary on Sunday, October 11, 
2026. Founded in 1851 by local residents, Antioch has been a 
cornerstone of faith, fellowship, and service in East Texas for 
generations.
  Since 1851, Antioch Baptist Church of Tyler has stood through the 
early Texas settlements, the Civil War, and the Great Depression, and 
carries on today in the modern digital age. Through every season, this 
congregation has been a spiritual and social anchor for the Sand Flat 
community and the greater East Texas family around it. Generation after 
generation, families have called Antioch their church home. 
Grandparents, parents, and children have worshiped together, served 
together, and leaned on one another in good times and hard times. That 
is the work of a church. It is the faith of its founders, the 
fellowship of its families, and the faithful service of its members, 
passed down year after year. East Texans are grateful people, and we 
are grateful to God for Antioch. A church does not last 175 years by 
accident. It lasts because faithful people kept showing up, kept 
serving their neighbors, and kept trusting God with the rest.
  The strength of a church is measured not by its walls but by the 
lives it touches. For 175 years, Antioch Baptist Church has fed the 
hungry, encouraged the discouraged, raised up young people, and stood 
by families in their times of need. Its legacy lives on in the many 
members and neighbors whose lives have been shaped by its ministry.
```
