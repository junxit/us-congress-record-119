# HONORING STEPHEN RICHARDSON'S SERVICE AS DIRECTOR OF OPERATIONS

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
    HONORING STEPHEN RICHARDSON'S SERVICE AS DIRECTOR OF OPERATIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate 
Stephen Richardson for a successful two and a half years in service to 
my congressional office, most recently as my Director of Operations.
  Stephen joined my team as my staff assistant and adopted a clear 
constituent services mindset with answering phones and providing 
countless Minnesotans with tours of the Capitol.
  At the beginning of this Congress, I asked Stephen to be my 
scheduler, not knowing the path ahead and recently becoming Ranking 
Member of the House Agriculture Committee. He truly succeeded in 
keeping our office and my time on track in the best ways possible.
  I am confident Stephen will continue to thrive in his career and wish 
him the best.

                          ____________________
```
