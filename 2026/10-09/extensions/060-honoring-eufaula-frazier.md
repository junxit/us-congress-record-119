# HONORING EUFAULA FRAZIER

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                        HONORING EUFAULA FRAZIER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record a 
proclamation to honor Eufaula Frazier on this special occasion of her 
100th birthday:

       Whereas, Eufaula Frazier has been a steadfast advocate and 
     activist for human rights, dedicating more than 60 years of 
     her life to the fight for equality and justice in Miami-Dade 
     County, standing strong against disparities that unfairly 
     affected the Black community; and
       Whereas, born in Dodge County, Georgia, into a family of 11 
     children, Frazier's early years were spent on one of the few 
     Black-owned farms in the area, teaching her the importance of 
     resilience and determination. She later moved to Miami in 
     1950, where she raised four children and opened a successful 
     beauty shop called the Little Nook; and
       Whereas, witnessing injustices in her Liberty City 
     neighborhood, Ms. Frazier acted by founding the Tenants 
     Education Association of Miami, advocating for tenants' 
     rights, and playing a pivotal role in passing a state law to 
     protect those rights, helping countless families in the 
     process; and
       Whereas, Ms. Frazier's activism extended far beyond housing 
     rights. She taught school, registered voters, and advocated 
     for HIV testing. She stood firm in the face of civil unrest, 
     such as the Liberty City riots of 1980, when she walked the 
     streets trying to calm the community, and continued her work 
     to uplift others even in challenging circumstances; and
       Whereas, she earned a degree in social work from Florida 
     International University at the age of 53, showing her 
     lifelong commitment to education and social justice, and she 
     continued to give back to her community by opening Michelle's 
     grocery store, the only store left standing on her block 
     after the Liberty City riots; and
       Whereas, Ms. Frazier has been an active participant in 
     national political movements, attending every Democratic 
     National Convention since 1972. In 2008, at the age of 83, 
     she witnessed Barack Obama's historic nomination as the first 
     Black presidential candidate, a moment that stood on the 
     shoulders of her own decades-long fight for civil rights; and
       Whereas, her contributions to the civil rights movement, 
     her dedication to tenants' rights, and her unyielding spirit 
     in the face of injustice have made a profound impact on her 
     community and beyond. Ms. Frazier's legacy includes not only 
     her activism but also the countless individuals she has 
     mentored, whose lives stretch from Miami-Dade to across the 
     nation; and
       Whereas, Ms. Frazier's family, including her daughter 
     Michelle, and her three sons, Maurice Coes, Norland Frazier, 
     and Edwin Frazier, continue to honor her legacy of service 
     and commitment to justice, embodying the values she has 
     instilled in them throughout their lives; and
       Whereas, at the age of 100, Eufaula Frazier continues to 
     inspire those around her, and her unwavering dedication to 
     justice, equality, and community service stands as a beacon 
     of hope for generations to come; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, do 
     hereby recognize and celebrate we honor and celebrate Eufaula 
     Frazier, on this special occasion of her 100th birthday, for 
     her lifelong dedication to fighting for justice, equality, 
     and human rights. Her enduring legacy is a testament to the 
     power of activism and the impact one person can have on the 
     world.

                          ____________________
```
