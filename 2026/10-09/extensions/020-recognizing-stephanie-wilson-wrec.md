# RECOGNIZING STEPHANIE WILSON, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                   RECOGNIZING STEPHANIE WILSON, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Ms. Stephanie Wilson of Withlacoochee River Electric 
Cooperative (WREC) for her important service as a Multimedia Contact 
during WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. 
The Light Up Navajo initiative brings skilled utility professionals 
together to help provide electricity to Navajo families who have lived 
without power for generations. While much of the mission necessarily 
focuses on the physical work of extending electrical service, 
documenting and sharing that work is also important. Ms. Wilson helped 
capture the people, stories, teamwork, and impact behind this 
extraordinary effort. Through those efforts, others are able to better 
understand what access to electricity means for families who have gone 
without it. It means children having light to study after dark, 
families being able to safely store food and medicine, and elders 
enjoying greater safety and comfort. Sharing these stories also helps 
recognize the skilled professionals who volunteer their time and 
talents to make that progress possible.
  The Light Up Navajo Project is ultimately a story about service, 
human connection, and neighbors helping neighbors. Ms. Wilson helped 
ensure that story could be seen, shared, and remembered. I commend Ms. 
Stephanie Wilson for her professionalism, dedication, and contribution 
to this important mission. Her service reflects great credit upon WREC 
and our community.
```
