# HONORING LAURA COTTRELL'S SERVICE AS CHIEF OF STAFF

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
          HONORING LAURA COTTRELL'S SERVICE AS CHIEF OF STAFF

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Laura 
Cottrell for over four years of service with my team, most recently as 
my Chief of Staff.
  Laura has been a dedicated public servant, having previously worked 
in the offices of former Congressman Anthony Brindisi and Senator Jon 
Ossoff.
  Laura first joined my team during the 2022 election cycle. She came 
on board as my Communications Director and immediately elevated our 
office's approach to sharing our accomplishments. Our franking program 
dramatically improved under her leadership and she secured several 
strong headlines showcasing our wins for constituents.
  Laura rose to the role of Chief of Staff at the end of 2024 and led 
my office through countless new challenges, all while maintaining a 
strong office culture. It has been amazing watching Laura excel in her 
career throughout her time with my team. She navigated several changing 
dynamics including becoming Ranking Member of the House Agriculture 
Committee.
  I've always appreciated Laura's advice and counsel over the years and 
wish her the best in her future.

                          ____________________
```
