# RECOGNIZING MIKE MLYNARSKI, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                    RECOGNIZING MIKE MLYNARSKI, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Mike Mlynarski, a BP Lineman with Withlacoochee River 
Electric Cooperative (WREC), for his outstanding contribution to WREC's 
second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. Through Light Up 
Navajo, utility professionals volunteer their time and specialized 
expertise to help bring electricity to Navajo families who have lived 
without power for generations. Mr. Mlynarski joined his WREC colleagues 
in this important effort, using his experience as a lineman to help 
perform the demanding work necessary to extend electrical service. 
Their work has an impact far beyond the infrastructure they install. 
Reliable electricity provides families with light, refrigeration, 
greater safety, and opportunities that many Americans take for granted. 
For children, parents, and elders who have lived without these basic 
necessities, gaining access to power can be truly lifechanging. Mr. 
Mlynarski's participation reflects a willingness to use his skills not 
only as a profession, but also as a means of serving others. That 
spirit embodies the cooperative values that have long strengthened 
communities across our country.
  I commend Mr. Mike Mlynarski for his technical expertise, dedication, 
and commitment to helping families in need. His service reflects great 
credit upon WREC and our community.

                          ____________________
```
