# HONORING MICHAEL NORMAN

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                        HONORING MICHAEL NORMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record a 
proclamation to honor Dr. Michael Norman, who is being recognized and 
honored for his contributions to Christian Music and leadership at the 
2024 State of the Church Summit, in Miami, Florida:

  Whereas, Dr. Michael Norman, the second of five siblings, born in 
Orlando, Florida, to Carey and Mary Norman, has displayed exceptional 
talents and contributions throughout his life; and
  Whereas, from the tender age of five, Michael exhibited prodigious 
skills in playing the piano, evolving into the primary church musician 
by the age of eight, laying the foundation for his future as a 
performer, orator, and proficient leader; and
  Whereas, Michael's journey began as a gifted musician, attributing 
his musical prowess to his mother's prayers and divine intervention, 
turning away from potential pitfalls during the challenging times of 
the 80s; and
  Whereas, his remarkable musical journey has culminated in numerous 
accolades, including multiple awards for Best New Artist, highlighting 
his ability to resonate with diverse audiences through the universal 
language of music; and
  Whereas, Dr. Michael Norman is a native of Orlando, Florida, and, for 
the past 25 years, has been in a sacred union with Prophetess Francina 
Norman, demonstrating a commitment to marital bliss and spiritual 
partnership. Dr. Norman and Prophetess Francina Norman have traversed 
the globe, establishing churches, overseeing ministries, and providing 
valuable consultation to Pro Athletes, Celebrities, and Political 
figures, contributing to the betterment of societies both foreign and 
domestic; and
  Whereas, Dr. Michael Norman, alongside his cherished wife Francina, 
serves as pastors of The Movement Church in Orlando, FL, demonstrating 
unwavering commitment to the spiritual growth and well-being of their 
community; and
  Whereas Dr. Michael Norman holds a bachelor's degree in biblical 
studies and Pastoral Counseling, as well as an Honorary Doctorate from 
All Nations Christian University, attesting to his scholarly pursuits 
and commitment to expanding his knowledge for the betterment of others; 
and
  Whereas, Dr. Michael Norman is the esteemed founder of MCN 
Productions International, a testament to his entrepreneurial spirit 
and commitment to excellence in the realms of recording artistry and 
production; and
  Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, a member 
of the United States House of Representatives, representing the 24th 
Congressional District of Florida, recognize Dr. Michael Norman's 
outstanding accomplishments, unwavering commitment to community, and 
his dedication to the betterment of individuals and relationships.

                          ____________________
```
