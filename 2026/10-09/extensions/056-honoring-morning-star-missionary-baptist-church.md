# HONORING MORNING STAR MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Fields, Cleo (F000110) [D-LA]

```
            HONORING MORNING STAR MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. CLEO FIELDS

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. FIELDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge Morning Star 
Missionary Baptist Church of Shreveport, Louisiana, an institution 
whose history reflects nearly a century of faith, fellowship, and 
dedicated service to the community. Founded in 1929 as a Sunday school 
that met from house to house, Morning Star soon grew into a formal 
congregation following a revival led by its first pastor, Reverend O.C. 
Johnny. From its earliest days, the church established itself as a 
steadfast source of worship, guidance, and community for families 
across Shreveport.
  In 1953, Morning Star Missionary Baptist Church was officially 
incorporated, marking an important step in its development and long-
term presence in the community. The congregation continued to grow and, 
in 1968, relocated to its current area after purchasing the former 
Emmanuel Baptist Church property. This move enabled Morning Star to 
expand its ministries, broaden its outreach, and strengthen its role as 
a spiritual foundation for its members and surrounding neighborhoods.
  Throughout its history, the church has remained deeply committed to 
serving the broader Shreveport area through ministries focused on 
education, youth development, senior care, family support, social 
services, and community outreach. These initiatives reflect Morning 
Star's enduring mission: to nurture individuals and families while 
meeting the evolving needs of the community. Its work stands as a 
testament to the church's dedication to faith in action.
  Today, under the leadership of Dr. Theron J. Jackson, Morning Star 
continues to flourish. Dr. Jackson has guided the congregation in 
expanding its ministries and deepening its commitment to community 
service, building upon the foundation laid by generations before him. 
Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Morning Star 
Missionary Baptist Church for its longstanding legacy of faithful 
service and in wishing its pastor and congregation continued blessings 
in the years to come.
```
