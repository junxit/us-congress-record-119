# HONORING RACHEL HUNTER'S SERVICE AS SENIOR LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
    HONORING RACHEL HUNTER'S SERVICE AS SENIOR LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Rachel 
Hunter for five years of service in my congressional office, most 
recently as a Senior Legislative Assistant.
  Rachel began her time in my office as a Staff Assistant in 2021. 
She's risen from that position to Legislative Correspondent, 
Legislative Assistant and now Senior Legislative Assistant. It has been 
a privilege to watch Rachel grow in her career. She has regularly been 
a creative legislative staffer focused on government reform legislation 
and strengthened my office's work within the Congressional Equality 
Caucus.
  Rachel has truly found her calling as she's started part-time law 
school and has focused her efforts on tech policy. I look forward to 
following Rachel's career and wish her the best.

                          ____________________
```
