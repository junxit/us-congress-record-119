# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CAPT. SAMAR BANNISTER

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
           HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR: CAPT. SAMAR BANNISTER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Captain Samar 
Bannister as she is honored as one of California's 8th Congressional 
District's Women of the Year. A U.S. Coast Guard-credentialed Master 
Mariner, Captain Bannister serves as Master of California State 
University Maritime Academy's training ship Golden Bear III and 
Director of Marine Programs. A member of the Class of 2000, she 
returned to her alma mater in 2022 as its first female training ship 
captain.
  Under her command, Golden Bear III has safely traveled more than 
50,000 nautical miles, representing Cal Maritime and the United States 
at 19 domestic and international ports while providing hands-on 
training to more than 1,100 future maritime officers. Through her 
leadership, Captain Bannister is helping prepare the next generation of 
mariners for successful careers in a vital industry.
  Captain Bannister has also played an instrumental role in advancing 
the Boat Basin and Pier Expansion Project, the future home of the 
training ship Golden State II. As construction progresses and Golden 
Bear III operates from Mare Island, she has coordinated alternative 
training arrangements to ensure cadets can continue meeting their 
academic requirements and U.S. Coast Guard credentialing standards. Her 
efforts to establish shoreside watch stations, facilitate safe access 
to training vessels, and relocate instructional equipment demonstrates 
her commitment to student safety and educational excellence.
  Beyond her professional accomplishments, Captain Bannister is a 
dedicated mentor and role model who supports cadets in their academic, 
leadership, and extracurricular pursuits. Her leadership and service 
are helping strengthen maritime education and advance the future of the 
maritime industry not only in Vallejo but throughout the greater San 
Francisco Bay and Delta region. On behalf of the people of California's 
8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize 
Captain Samar Bannister for her outstanding leadership and 
contributions to our community.
```
