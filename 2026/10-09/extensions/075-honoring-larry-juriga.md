# HONORING LARRY JURIGA

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING LARRY JURIGA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record a 
proclamation to honor Larry Juriga, chief of police in North Miami, 
Florida, as he enters retirement with over nearly four decades of 
service to the citizens of the city and county:


[[Page E1064]]


  

       Whereas, Larry Juriga is deeply rooted in the North Miami 
     Community, born in the city and raised in South Florida; and
       Whereas, Chief Larry Juriga during his stellar law 
     enforcement career, has made major philanthropic 
     contributions to young men and to the 5000 Role Models of 
     Excellence Project. Role Model Chief Juriga is passionate 
     about helping and mentoring youth. He is an advocate for 
     second chances within our juvenile community; and
       Whereas, Chief Juriga began his career with the City of 
     North Miami in 1987, in the Parks and Recreation Department. 
     However, being inspired by his father, a 30-year veteran of 
     the North Miami Police Department, Larry attended the police 
     academy in 1993 and joined the police department in 1994; and
       Whereas, Chief Juriga is committed to serving the 
     community. He worked as a patrol officer before being 
     transferred to the General Investigations Unit in 1997. He 
     oversaw major criminal investigations before joining North 
     Miami S.W.A.T. team a year later. His promotion to rank of 
     sergeant, appointment to major, then rank of Assistant Chief 
     of Police followed in rapid succession; and
       Whereas, Larry Juriga's appointment to Interim Chief of 
     Police in May 2017 was followed, less than a year later, by 
     the pinnacle of Juriga's law enforcement career, the 
     appointment to Chief of Police. During his tenure as Chief, 
     the North Miami Police Department earned its accredited 
     status from the Commission for Florida Law Enforcement 
     Accreditation after an extensive review of the Department's 
     enhanced policies and procedures that reflect law 
     enforcement's highest standards; and
       Whereas, Chief Juriga earned a Bachelor of Science Degree 
     from Barry University, competed as a scholar-athlete in 
     basketball. He also earned a Master of Public Administration 
     Degree from Florida International University, and graduated 
     from the prestigious FBI National Academy, 236th Session; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     congratulate Chief Larry Juriga as he starts his retirement 
     and celebrates nearly four decades of service and peacekeeper 
     to the citizens of North Miami, Florida and Miami-Dade 
     County.

                          ____________________
```
