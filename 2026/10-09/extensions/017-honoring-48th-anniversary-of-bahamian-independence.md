# HONORING 48TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAHAMIAN INDEPENDENCE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
           HONORING 48TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAHAMIAN INDEPENDENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, as a descendant of 
Bahamians, to commemorate the 48th Anniversary of Bahamian Independence 
and to pay tribute to its citizens

[[Page E1050]]

and Bahamian Americans living in South Florida and across the United 
States. Therefore, I include in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, The Islands of the Bahamas became an Independent 
     Commonwealth Country on July 10, 1973, after centuries of 
     British rule; and
       Whereas there are some 700 islands and 2,400 cays in total 
     that make up the Islands of the Bahamas; and
       Whereas the proud people who, through uncertainty and tough 
     economic times, remained steadfast and determined to build a 
     brighter future after its hard-fought independence, showed 
     great strength and unwavering will; and
       Whereas, today, The Bahamas has a thriving economy built 
     around tourism and financial services; the tenacious and 
     enduring spirit of the Bahamian people, and its rich 
     traditions and culture; and
       Whereas Bahamian culture is joy personified. It is a 
     Junkanoo celebration, parading down the street. It's bold and 
     colorful art. The culture is a fresh conch salad, a lively 
     rake `n' scrape tune, and a welcoming smile from a stranger; 
     and
       Whereas Bahamians and Bahamian Americans have made 
     significant contributions to the United States through the 
     arts & humanities, business, activism, local and national 
     government, and remain an integral part of the South Florida 
     Community; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, and 
     a person of Bahamian descent, am proud to recognize the 48th 
     Anniversary of this resilient, free and sovereign nation, and 
     pay tribute to the wonderful people and descendants who make 
     it great.

                          ____________________
```
