# HONORING SKYLAR BORCHARDT'S SERVICE AS DEPUTY CHIEF OF STAFF

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
      HONORING SKYLAR BORCHARDT'S SERVICE AS DEPUTY CHIEF OF STAFF

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate Skylar 
Borchardt for more than five years of service throughout three sessions 
of Congress to my office, most recently as my Deputy Chief of Staff.
  Skylar began his time in my office as a Legislative Assistant, was 
promoted to Legislative Director and ultimately Deputy Chief of Staff. 
As a native Minnesota, he thrived with stakeholder relationships, 
particularly among labor unions and law enforcement. He was integral in 
helping pass the Public Safety Officer Support Act and ultimately led 
my office's work delivering millions of dollars back to Minnesota in 
community project funding.
  He was always willing to take on new challenges, including serving as 
the Acting Staff Director for my first couple of months as Ranking 
Member of the House Agriculture Committee and moving to my Senate race 
as Political Director.
  I've always appreciated Skylar's advice and counsel over the years 
and wish him the best in his future.

                          ____________________
```
