# RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN SMIT MACHCHHAR FOR HIS BRAVERY

**Speaking:** Krishnamoorthi, Raja (K000391) [D-IL]

```
           RECOGNIZING CAPTAIN SMIT MACHCHHAR FOR HIS BRAVERY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. RAJA KRISHNAMOORTHI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Captain 
Smit Machchhar of India, a husband, father, and pilot with 17 years of 
flying experience, including 11 as a captain, whose extraordinary 
bravery on September 30, 2026, helped save the lives of 172 passengers 
and his fellow crew members aboard Flydubai Flight 1073 from Dubai to 
Tel Aviv, including 4 United States citizens and 27 children.
  Midway through the flight, the first officer attacked Captain 
Machchhar with the aircraft's emergency crash axe and attempted to 
seize the flight controls. The aircraft plunged approximately 16,000 
feet in about 30 seconds. Despite grave injuries, Captain Machchhar 
fought his way to the cockpit door and opened it, allowing passengers 
and crew members to enter and overpower his attacker. Two reserve 
Flydubai pilots traveling aboard the aircraft then took control and 
landed it safely in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia.
  Even as Captain Machchhar fought for his own life, he understood that 
the lives of everyone aboard depended on what he did next. Despite his 
injuries, his years of experience told him that the aircraft was going 
down, and he found the strength to reach that door. His presence of 
mind, determination, and courage gave the passengers and crew the 
chance they needed to stop the attack and avert a catastrophe.
  Behind every person aboard Flight 1073 is a family that was spared an 
unimaginable loss. Because Captain Machchhar refused to give up, the 
passengers and crew aboard that aircraft had the chance to return to 
the people who love them.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Captain Smit 
Machchhar, whose extraordinary courage helped save hundreds of lives 
aboard Flight 1073. We extend our warmest wishes to him, his wife 
Sonal, their two children, and their entire family, and we hope for the 
captain's full-speed recovery from his injuries at the earliest.
```
