# HONORING NELLY KORDA, BRADENTON'S HOMETOWN CHAMPION

**Speaking:** Buchanan, Vern (B001260) [R-FL]

```
          HONORING NELLY KORDA, BRADENTON'S HOMETOWN CHAMPION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. VERN BUCHANAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BUCHANAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Nelly Korda of 
Bradenton, Florida, one of the greatest golfers in the world, whose 
achievements have filled our hometown with pride.
  Nelly was born in Bradenton on July 28, 1998, into a family that 
loves sports. Her father, Petr, was a champion tennis player who won 
the Australian Open, and her mother, Regina, played tennis at the 
Olympic Games. Her older sister, Jessica, is also a winner on the LPGA 
Tour, and her younger brother, Sebastian, is a professional tennis 
player. The Kordas could have lived anywhere in the world, but they 
chose Bradenton as their home, and our community has cheered for Nelly 
every step of the way.
  Nelly turned professional in 2016 as a teenager and quickly showed 
the world what Bradenton already knew. In 2021, she won her first major 
championship and, that same summer, stood atop the podium at the 
Olympic Games in Tokyo with a gold medal for the United States. In 
2024, she put together one of the most impressive seasons the sport has 
ever seen, winning seven tournaments and being named the LPGA Tour's 
Player of the Year.
  Her bond with Bradenton was on full display in January 2024, when a 
professional tournament came to Bradenton Country Club, just down the 
road from the hospital where she was born. Friends, family, and 
neighbors lined the course to cheer her on. Late on the final day, it 
looked as though the title might slip away, but Nelly fought back with 
two brilliant shots on the closing holes and went on to win in a 
dramatic finish. Afterward, she thanked the hometown crowd for staying 
positive and carrying her to victory. For one unforgettable week, the 
golf world turned its attention to our city, and Bradenton's own 
daughter delivered.
  Like every great athlete, Nelly has faced setbacks. In 2025, she went 
the entire season without a win. Rather than be discouraged, she went 
back to work, and in 2026 she came back stronger than ever. She won the 
very first tournament of the year and finished first or second in each 
of her first six events, something only one other woman in history had 
ever done. She went on to win the Chevron Championship and the U.S. 
Women's Open, two of the most important events in women's golf, and 
returned to her place as the number one player in the world. In 
September, she once again wore the colors of the United States at the 
Solheim Cup in the Netherlands, where she and her teammate made history 
as the first pair ever to win five matches in a row together.
  Yet Nelly's greatest gift to Bradenton may be the example she sets. 
Across Manatee County, young girls now pick up a golf club believing 
they, too, can become champions, because someone from their own 
hometown has shown them the way. Parents point to her as a model of 
hard work and perseverance, and whenever Nelly's name appears in 
headlines around the world, the name of Bradenton travels right 
alongside it.
  This tribute is personal for me as well. My own granddaughter is a 
golfer, and a very good one. Like so many young players in our 
community, she considers Nelly a true inspiration. She watches how 
Nelly competes, how she handles both victory and disappointment, and 
how she carries herself on and off the course. I have seen firsthand 
how much it means to a young athlete to watch someone from her own 
backyard rise to the very top of the game.
  Through it all, Nelly has remained humble and gracious. She is quick 
to credit her family, her team, and the people who have supported her 
since she was a little girl. She represents the very best of our 
community: talent matched with determination, success matched with 
kindness, and a deep love for the place she calls home.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Florida's 16th Congressional 
District, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Nelly Korda 
on her extraordinary achievements, in thanking her for the honor she 
has brotlght to the City of Bradenton and to our Nation, and in wishing 
her many more years of success.
```
