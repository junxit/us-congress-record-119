# RECOGNIZING IVETTE TORRES DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
        RECOGNIZING IVETTE TORRES DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Hispanic American 
Heritage Month to recognize an extraordinary community leader and 
advocate from Rochester, New York: Ivette Torres.
  Ivette is a co-founder of Latinos Unidos Contra Cancer at Cancer 
Support Community at Gilda's Club Rochester, where she has helped 
create a place for Spanish-speaking individuals and families affected 
by cancer to find support, understanding, and community.
  For Ivette, this mission is deeply personal. As someone living with 
cancer herself, she understands firsthand the fear and uncertainty that 
can accompany a diagnosis, and the difference it makes to know you do 
not have to face it alone.
  Ivette has transformed her own experience into a source of strength 
for others. Through outreach across Rochester and surrounding rural 
communities, she has helped connect people with critical information 
and resources, raise awareness, and expand access to cancer screenings.
  Her courage is matched by her compassion. Even while facing her own 
battle with cancer, Ivette continues to stand beside others confronting 
the disease, helping ensure they are seen, supported, and surrounded by 
community.
  It is my great privilege to recognize my dear friend Ivette Torres 
during Hispanic American Heritage Month. Her courage, resilience, and 
commitment to others represent the very best of Rochester, and I thank 
her for the countless lives she continues to touch.

                          ____________________
```
