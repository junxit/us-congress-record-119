# RECOGNIZING QUINT SHEAR

**Speaking:** Hurd, Jeff (H001100) [R-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING QUINT SHEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JEFF HURD

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Quint 
Shear of Grand Junction, Colorado. Quint is the 2026 recipient of the 
Colorado Oil and Gas Association's Fred Julander Lifetime Achievement 
Award.
  Quint is a Colorado native and graduate of Colorado Mesa University. 
After more than 25 years in banking, he became President of Shear Inc., 
an exploration and land services company based in Grand Junction. For 
more than 40 years, the company has worked throughout Western Colorado 
and across the Rocky Mountain West.
  Quint's contributions go well beyond his business. He serves on the 
executive board of the West Slope Colorado Oil and Gas Association and 
on the boards of the Grand Junction Regional Airport, the Grand 
Junction Economic Partnership, and Mesa County Search and Rescue.
  That record of professional achievement and service to his community 
makes this recognition especially fitting.
  I was honored to join Quint in Denver as he received the award, and 
I'm proud to recognize him and his wife, Shari, for all they do for 
Western Colorado.

                          ____________________
```
