# HONORING LINSY DI PIETRO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING LINSY DI PIETRO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                      FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Linsy Di Pietro is the Founder and CEO of 
Warehouse Equipment and Supply, Inc. (WES). She is a Central Florida 
entrepreneur whose story embodies resilience, enterprise, and the 
belief that business success carries a responsibility to create 
opportunities for others.
  In 2016, Di Pietro founded WES from the ground up, entering the 
historically male-dominated warehouse storage and material-handling 
industry. From its beginnings in an open-air yard, she built WES into 
an established company operating from an approximately 18,000-square-
foot facility, with a growing team serving businesses throughout 
Florida and beyond.
  Her work contributes to the infrastructure behind American commerce. 
The U.S. material-handling industry contributes approximately $266 
billion to the national GDP and supports more than 1.9 million jobs, 
enabling businesses to store, move, and distribute the products that 
keep the nation's supply chain moving.
  Di Pietro's commitment to growth includes completing the 2025 
Business Scaling Program through LBAN and Stanford Graduate School of 
Business, complemented by executive development in supplier diversity, 
capital readiness, exports, and procurement.
  Her impact extends beyond business through service supporting hunger 
relief, education, scholarships, women's empowerment, and survivor 
advocacy, including involvement with Second Harvest Food Bank, Heart of 
Florida United Way, Nuevo Sendero, HAPBWA, and the Hispanic Chamber of 
Commerce of Metro Orlando.
  Her leadership has earned honors, including Ignitor of the Year, 
Mujer Valerosa, Ambassador of the Year, Outstanding Community Leader, 
the El Josco Award, and recognition as a Don Quijote Awards finalist 
for Hispanic Enterprise of the Year.
  Through enterprise and service, Linsy Di Pietro has transformed 
opportunity into economic contribution and community impact, 
strengthening Central Florida while helping build the infrastructure 
that keeps American commerce moving.
```
