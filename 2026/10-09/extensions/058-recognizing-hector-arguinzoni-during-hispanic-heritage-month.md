# RECOGNIZING HECTOR ARGUINZONI DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
      RECOGNIZING HECTOR ARGUINZONI DURING HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Hispanic American 
Heritage Month to recognize a beloved musician, broadcaster, and 
community leader from Rochester, New York: Hector Arguinzoni.
  Born in Cayey, Puerto Rico, Hector moved with his family to Rochester 
when he was just two years old. Raised in a musical family, he learned 
to play instruments including the guitar and cuatro, beginning a 
lifelong relationship with music that would ultimately take him around 
the world.

[[Page E1060]]

  Hector became a well-known musician and singer in Rochester, 
including as a member of the renowned Antonetti Orchestra. But for 
Hector, music has always been about more than performance. It has been 
a way to bring people together, celebrate culture, and strengthen 
community.
  That same spirit has guided his decades of public service and youth 
outreach. As Director of the Puerto Rican Arts and Cultural Center 
within Rochester's Department of Recreation, Hector helped create 
opportunities for young people while preserving and celebrating the 
rich cultural traditions of Rochester's Puerto Rican community.
  And when Poder 97.1, a local Latino radio station, began broadcasting 
in 2015, Hector became the station's first DJ on the air, bringing his 
unmistakable love of music to a new generation of listeners. Today, he 
continues that tradition on the airwaves, connecting Rochester's 
Spanish-speaking community through music, culture, and conversation.
  It is my great privilege to recognize Hector Arguinzoni during 
Hispanic American Heritage Month and thank him for a lifetime of music, 
cultural leadership, and service to Rochester.
```
