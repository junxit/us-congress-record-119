# HONORING THE 35TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE VFW VETERANS

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
           HONORING THE 35TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE VFW VETERANS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the VFW Veterans 
Village in Fort McCoy, Florida, as it celebrates its 35th anniversary.
  The VFW Department of Florida opened the Village on October 13, 1991, 
on 42 acres shaded by mature oaks and palms. Like those oaks, the 
Village has put down deep roots in Fort McCoy.
  The Village was built on a simple principle: provide affordable, 
independent living for the Veterans who defended our country and the 
families who stood behind them. Honorably discharged Veterans, their 
spouses, widows of Veterans, and those who qualify for the VFW 
Auxiliary are all welcome. The Village opened with 34 rooms, and demand 
filled them quickly. In March 2006, the Department completed a 19-room 
addition to serve even more Veterans.
  The VFW Department of Florida owns and operates the Village without a 
single government subsidy at any level. Rent covers about 75 percent of 
day-to-day operating costs, and donations and fundraisers make up the 
rest. By the Department's account, the Village is the only facility of 
its kind anywhere in the world run this way. Our Veterans saw a need, 
and they met it themselves.
  In 2016, the Department added a covered pavilion that has become a 
gathering place for the Fort McCoy community. The pavilion and grounds 
have hosted Easter Sunrise Service, the Veterans National Honor Society 
Pow Wow, the Armed Forces Weekend Patriotic Concert in the Forest, 
Bluegrass Fest, Veterans Day programs, Christmas in Fort McCoy, and 
many other events. Residents live on their own terms, surrounded by 
neighbors who understand what service means.
  I am grateful to work alongside the VFW Department of Florida, both 
in Washington and back home in North Central Florida. Its leaders are 
steady advocates who make sure our Veterans' priorities are heard in 
Congress. The Veterans Village puts that same commitment into practice 
every day.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating the VFW Department of 
Florida and the residents of the VFW Veterans Village on this 
milestone. I thank the Veterans and families who call the Village home 
for everything they have given our Republic, and happy 35th 
anniversary.
```
