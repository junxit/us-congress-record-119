# HONORING APOSTLE DR. KWAME NKRUMAH'S

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                  HONORING APOSTLE DR. KWAME NKRUMAH'S

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the 
following proclamation in honor of Apostle Dr. Prince Alex Kwame 
Nkrumah:

       Whereas, Apostle Dr. Kwame Nkrumah's passion for the word 
     and the work of God was ignited from an early age. At eleven 
     years old, through his engagement with the Scripture Union 
     Fellowship at his primary school, the Holy Spirit enlightened 
     his understanding of the word in 2 Corinthians 5:17, 
     ``Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; 
     old things have passed away; behold, all things have become 
     new.'' His life was transformed by this word, made new, and 
     God's call on his life became crystal clear to him. His 
     passion for the work of God was sustained from that time 
     forward; and
       Whereas, Apostle Dr. Kwame Nkrumah's tertiary studies 
     centered on Bible Theology, Law in Ministry, Psychology and 
     Christian Counseling, Church Mission and Leadership. He 
     obtained a Diploma of Arts in Bible Theology in 1995, an 
     Associate of Arts Degree in Bible Theology in 1996, and a 
     Bachelor of Arts Degree in Bible Theology and Administration 
     in 1999, all from All Nations for Christ Bible Institute 
     International in Benin City, Nigeria; and
       Whereas, Apostle Dr. Kwame Nkrumah's passion is for 
     apostolic ministry and missionary outreach to win souls for 
     the Kingdom of God, and to encourage and strengthen believers 
     in their walk with God. He has a special anointing for 
     winning souls to Christ, breaking new ground and church 
     planting, using such mechanisms as training of leaders, 
     teaching, crusades, and indoor revival

[[Page E1051]]

     events. His missionary outreach activities have taken him to 
     ten African countries as well as Norway, Turkey, Dubai, 
     Israel, and the United States; and
       Whereas, Apostle Dr. Kwame Nkrumah uses his leadership 
     positions in various international ministries strategically 
     to further his outreach and missionary work for the kingdom. 
     He is a Member of the Global Christian Ministry Forum 
     International, (Ghana Chapter), The Africa/Israel Initiative, 
     (Ghana Chapter), the All Nations for Christ Bible Institute 
     Alumni, Ghana, and the Global Alumni of the All Nations for 
     Christ Bible Institute International. He is the Convener of 
     the digital platform, Vision Life Clubhouse; and
       Whereas, Apostle Dr. Prince Alex Kwame Nkrumah founded 
     International Vision Church in Accra, Ghana in 2011, and has 
     served as its Senior Pastor from that time to the present. 
     His pastoral work supports the vision of the church which is 
     to build believers as people of wellbeing, stability and 
     satisfaction in spirit, soul and body, through foresight, 
     founded in the word of God; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, am 
     proud to recognize Apostle Dr. Kwame Nkrumah for his 
     dedication and outstanding commitment to spread the teachings 
     of the gospel.

                          ____________________
```
