# RECOGNIZING JOSEPH A. CAMPBELL FOR HIS SERVICE TO CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

```
       RECOGNIZING JOSEPH A. CAMPBELL FOR HIS SERVICE TO CONGRESS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BRYAN STEIL

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I kindly ask my colleagues to join me in 
recognizing Joseph A. Campbell for his service to the United States 
Congress. Today, Joe is retiring after almost a decade of service with 
the Architect of the Capitol, concluding a professional career defined 
by not for self, but for country.
  Raised in a Navy family, Joe is a graduate of the United States Naval 
Academy and served for twenty-five years in the United States Navy. 
Rising to the rank of Captain, Joe brought his professional training as 
a Civil Engineer to bear in combat operations and non-combat 
assignments around the world. His service was distinguished and 
meritorious, earning a Bronze Star, four Legions of Merit, and even 
jump wings as a Naval Parachutist.
  Since joining the Architect of the Capitol in 2016, Joe has served in 
numerous positions across the agency, from the Supreme Court Building 
to the House Office Buildings to his final role as Deputy Architect 
since September 2024. Joe has worked tirelessly to directly support 
Congress' legislative and the Court's judicial missions. As Acting 
Chief Financial Officer, Joe ended fiscal years with clean audits and 
guided the agency through multiple continuing resolutions.
  Joe's steady hand and integrity have garnered him the respect of 
colleagues and stakeholders across Congress. His leadership across 
Capitol Hill has strengthened the effectiveness and credibility of the 
Architect of the Capitol with a tenure defined by composure, 
efficiency, accountability, and quality.
  As Joe completes his service today as Deputy Architect of the 
Capitol, we extend our warmest wishes for a fulfilling next chapter 
still rooted in public service, stewardship of taxpayer dollars, and 
protection of the homeland. Joe's three and a half decades of 
outstanding performance of duty stands as an example honor, courage, 
and commitment, and his service merits this special recognition and the 
deep gratitude of the American People.

                          ____________________
```
