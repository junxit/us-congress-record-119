# RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF LINDA HEARN

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
               RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF LINDA HEARN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I ask for the House's attention 
today to recognize and honor Linda Hearn. After 36 years of service, 
Linda will retire as Executive Director from the Calhoun County Area 
Chamber & Visitors Center. Linda joined the Chamber in November of 1990 
and has been responsible for management of the professional staff as 
well as oversight of all Chamber programs and operations.
  She is a graduate of the Chamber Institute for Organization 
Management, earning her IOM degree in 2000 and a graduate of the 1993 
Leadership Calhoun County class. Linda earned state-level honors from 
the Chamber of Commerce Association of Alabama (CCAA) for her multi-
decade service.
  Linda earned a Bachelor of Science in Marketing at the University of 
Arkansas and recently obtained her MBA from Jacksonville State 
University alongside her daughter, Becca.
  Linda is married to Dr. Mark Hearn, Associate Dean of the College of 
Business and Industry and professor in the Department of Management and 
Marketing at Jacksonville State University. They are blessed with two 
children, Braden and Rebecca.
  During her time at the Chamber, Linda led the team to receive the 5-
Star Accreditation from the U.S. Chamber of Commerce, placing the 
organization in the top tier of chambers nationwide for sound policies, 
effective organizational management and impactful program execution. 
She championed formal corporate partnership agreements and Memoranda of 
Understanding (MOUs) with Jacksonville State University and Gadsden 
State Community College to offer tuition scholarships and educational 
pathways, directly supporting local workforce development and regional 
economic growth. Linda also directed initiatives through the Chamber's 
Military Security Affairs Committee to support regional defense assets 
like the Anniston Army Depot, fostering strong ties between military 
leadership and local business communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing and 
celebrating the extraordinary career of my friend, Linda Hearn. I thank 
Linda for her service to Calhoun County and the region.
```
