# RECOGNIZING JUSTICE KENNETH C. HOLDER FOR A LIFETIME OF LEADERSHIP

**Speaking:** Meeks, Gregory W. (M001137) [D-NY]

```
   RECOGNIZING JUSTICE KENNETH C. HOLDER FOR A LIFETIME OF LEADERSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GREGORY W. MEEKS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. MEEKS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the distinguished 
service and impactful leadership of Justice Kenneth C. Holder. His 41-
year legal career is defined by his commitment to the public good and 
his overwhelming compassion for the people he served. He approached the 
law with the belief that the justice system should uplift communities 
and create lasting, positive change throughout Queens.
  Born in London to a Jamaican mother and Guyanese father, Justice 
Holder lived in Canada and Jamaica before eventually settling in New 
York. His path across countries and cultures ultimately led him to the 
borough he would serve so faithfully. Justice Holder earned an 
undergraduate degree from Lincoln University, the nation's first 
degree-granting Historically Black College and University (HBCU) and 
later attended the University of Toledo School of Law.
  Justice Holder began his legal career as a Queens Assistant District 
Attorney and rose to become Chief of Narcotics Trials. He did far more 
than carry out prosecutions, he worked to change the system itself. 
Recognizing the need for compassion-centered alternatives within the 
justice system, he helped establish Queens County's first Felony Drug 
Treatment Court. He secured $6 million in federal funding to support 
the initiative and chaired the team responsible for its rules, 
regulations, and protocols. His leadership built a program that offered 
thousands of individuals battling drug addiction a real opportunity for 
rehabilitation, dignity, and a path forward.
  In 2006, Justice Holder was elected to the Queens County Civil Court. 
He was later elected to the New York State Supreme Court in Queens in 
2008 and reelected in 2021. Throughout his tenure on the bench, he 
presided over hundreds of serious felony cases, ensuring the safety and 
well-being of more than 750,000 Queens residents. In 2024, he was 
selected to serve as Interim Administrative Judge of Queens Supreme 
Court. His service extended far beyond the courtroom. He also served as 
an undergraduate instructor at St. John's University, preparing the 
future generation of lawyers and leaders.
  Justice Holder's dedication to the people of Queens is evident in 
every facet of his career. His legacy, built on fairness, innovation, 
and steadfast service, will continue to strengthen our community for 
generations to come.
```
