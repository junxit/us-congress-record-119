# RECOGNIZING MATT PLATT, WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING MATT PLATT, WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege to rise today to 
recognize Mr. Matt Platt, Safety Specialist with Withlacoochee River 
Electric Cooperative (WREC), for his outstanding service as part of 
WREC's second mission supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. The work 
performed through Light Up Navajo is demanding and requires skilled 
professionals to operate in challenging conditions while extending 
electrical infrastructure to families who have lived without power for 
generations. In an undertaking of this nature, safety is not simply a 
supporting responsibility--it is fundamental to the success of the 
entire mission.
  As Safety Specialist, Mr. Platt played an important role in helping 
WREC's team carry out its work responsibly and safely. His expertise 
helped protect the men and women performing this demanding work so they 
could remain focused on accomplishing their mission and serving Navajo 
families. The result of their collective efforts reaches far beyond 
electrical infrastructure. Reliable power means light for children to 
study, refrigeration for food and medicine, and greater safety and 
comfort for families and elders. Mr. Platt's contribution exemplifies 
the teamwork behind Light Up Navajo. Every successful mission depends 
upon individuals who understand their responsibilities and look out for 
one another while working toward a common goal.
  I commend Mr. Matt Platt for his professionalism, vigilance, and 
dedication to the safety of his colleagues and the success of this 
important mission. His service reflects great credit upon WREC and our 
entire community.

                          ____________________
```
