# HONORING BEATRIZ ANDREKOVICH AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING BEATRIZ ANDREKOVICH AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Beatriz Andrekovich was born in Mexico City, 
where she developed an early appreciation for languages, culture, 
sports, and the arts. She practiced tennis, swimming, canoeing, piano, 
and dance from youth throughout college, where she organized 
international students to showcase their countries through food, music, 
dances, and exhibits.
  Beatriz graduated with honors with degrees in finance, business 
administration, and arts and humanities and earned a master's degree in 
finance. She built a career in corporate banking with Citibank and Bank 
of Nova Scotia, working with international corporations and traveling, 
during which she developed a deeper appreciation for the museums, 
nature, and the diverse cultures she encountered.
  After moving to Florida with her husband and having four children, 
Beatriz shifted her focus toward art, culture, and community 
engagement. She volunteered with the school PTA and SAC in science, 
math, Junior Achievement, nature, reading, and art programs while 
participating in sports.
  Through foundations Casa de Mexico and CABETCAL, which she founded, 
she collaborates with organizations, museums, galleries, public 
libraries, and festivals to showcase international dances, music, 
exhibits, folkloric clothing, books, and cultural celebrations to 
highlight and connect the diverse communities that make up Central 
Florida.
  Beatriz volunteers in food pantries, with Seminole Beautiful to help 
preserve the Wekiva River, serves on the board of HAPBWA, supporting 
professional women and connecting them with resources to build 
successful businesses, writes for magazines, and highlights local 
places and interviews community members on radio and social media 
programs.
  Her contributions have been recognized through congratulatory letters 
from Seminole, Orange, and Osceola Counties and the Consulate of 
Mexico; awards from HAPBWA, El Josco with the Puerto Rican Chamber of 
Commerce, and iLatina with the Central Florida Hispanic Chamber of 
Commerce; and recognitions from Honors Lifetime, Peace Organization, 
and an honorary doctorate degree from the World Leaders Organization 
and University.
```
