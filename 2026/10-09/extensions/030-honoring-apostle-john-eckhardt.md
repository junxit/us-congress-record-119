# HONORING APOSTLE JOHN ECKHARDT

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING APOSTLE JOHN ECKHARDT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the 
following proclamation in honor of Apostle John Eckhardt, who is being 
recognized and honored for his significant impact to the Body of Christ 
at the 2024 State of the Church Summit, in Miami, Florida:

       Whereas John Eckhardt's journey began on the South Side of 
     Chicago on November 19, 1957, where he was born into a legacy 
     of achievement, with his father being a professional baseball 
     player in the Negro League, and his mother a first-generation 
     descendant of Italian immigrants; and
       Whereas Apostle Eckhardt's pursuit of knowledge led him 
     through the Catholic school system, earning a scholarship to 
     Loyola Academy for high school and Northwestern University 
     for college, where he studied pre-med, showcasing his 
     dedication to academic excellence; and
       Whereas, Apostle Eckhardt, through divine intervention, 
     experienced salvation in 1978 during his junior year in 
     college, leading him on the path to ministry, where he met 
     his wife, Wanda Eckhardt, at a local church, later marrying 
     her in 1979, and together they have raised a family of five 
     children and two grandchildren; and
       Whereas, Apostle Eckhardt, with unwavering dedication, 
     assumed the role of Pastor in 1988 and, over the years, has 
     become a beacon of inspiration for countless individuals, 
     imparting his apostolic mandate and prophetic insight; and
       Whereas Apostle Eckhardt is the visionary CEO and founder 
     of Impact University and John Eckhardt Global, institutions 
     that stand as pillars of knowledge and empowerment in the 
     heart of Chicago. He has dedicated his life to serving as an 
     international speaker, missionary, and overseer of Crusaders 
     Church in Chicago, Illinois; and
       Whereas Apostle Eckhardt has demonstrated a commitment to 
     fostering spiritual growth and understanding, reaching

[[Page E1053]]

     across continents with a weekly broadcast on Clubhouse and 
     Facebook, titled ``The Decrees for Breakthrough and The 
     Porch''. Apostle Eckhardt's influence extends far beyond 
     national borders, having ministered in more than 90 nations 
     and six continents, leaving an indelible mark on the global 
     body of Christ; and
       Whereas Apostle Eckhardt is a prolific author, having 
     penned over fifty books, including renowned titles such as 
     ``Prayers That Rout Demons,'' ``Prayers That Break Curses,'' 
     ``Prophet Arise,'' ``God Still Speaks,'' and his latest 
     release, ``You Shall Recover All''; and
       Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, 
     a member of the United States House of Representatives, 
     representing the 24th Congressional District of Florida, 
     recognize Apostle John Eckhardt for his outstanding 
     contributions to the global community, his unwavering 
     commitment to spiritual enlightenment, and his transformative 
     impact on the lives of countless individuals.

                          ____________________
```
