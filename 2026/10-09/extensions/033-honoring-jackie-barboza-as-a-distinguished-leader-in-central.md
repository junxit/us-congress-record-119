# HONORING JACKIE BARBOZA AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING JACKIE BARBOZA AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                      FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Jackie Barboza is an award-winning 
entrepreneur, humanitarian, philanthropist, educator, and community 
advocate whose life's mission is to restore confidence, inspire hope, 
and empower others to thrive. With more than 27 years of experience, 
she has become a respected leader recognized for her dedication to 
wellness, business, women's empowerment, education, and community 
service.
  As the president of HCI Hair Solutions, JSB Professional Suite, HCI 
Enterprise, and the Hispanic American Business Professional Women's 
Association (HABPWA), Jackie has transformed thousands of lives. 
Through HCI Hair Solutions, a family-owned company serving the 
community for 35 years, she provides custom cranial prostheses and 
advanced non-surgical hair restoration for men, women, and children 
experiencing hair loss due to cancer, alopecia, and other medical 
conditions. Her work restores confidence, dignity, and hope during some 
of life's most difficult moments.
  Jackie is also the founder of HCI Academy, where she mentors 
cosmetology professionals and entrepreneurs, equipping them with 
advanced education in medical hair restoration and cranial prosthetics. 
By elevating their technical expertise and business knowledge, she 
helps them expand their careers, increase their income, and make a 
meaningful difference in the lives of their clients.
  A dedicated philanthropist, Jackie leads HCI Hair Solutions in 
supporting numerous charitable foundations and nonprofit organizations. 
For more than 15 years, HCI has proudly partnered with Wigs for Kids, 
organizing hair donations and creating custom prosthetic wigs for 
children experiencing medical hair loss. She also provides ongoing 
support to cancer patients and their families through compassionate 
care, education, and specialized prosthetic solutions.
  Jackie is the founder of the HCI Global Foundation, built on the 
principles of heart, care, and impact. The foundation unites people, 
organizations, and communities to create lasting change by supporting 
women, men, children, families, cancer patients, individuals 
experiencing hair loss, survivors of hardship, and animal welfare 
initiatives through compassion, education, wellness, and service.
  Beyond her commitment to wellness, Jackie serves as an informal 
ambassador, bringing diverse communities together through cultural 
festivals celebrating art, dance, music, and food. By helping connect 
the Peruvian community with the broader multicultural community, she 
promotes cultural understanding while creating economic opportunities, 
supporting local businesses, generating jobs, and strengthening 
community partnerships.
  Her service also extends to the Salvation Army, the Humane Society, 
ASPCA, and Quinceanera de Suenos. Through HABPWA, she mentors women 
leaders and entrepreneurs by providing leadership development, business 
education, financial literacy, and access to valuable resources that 
empower them to grow personally, professionally, and financially.
  Jackie's leadership has earned numerous honors, including recognition 
from U.S. Senator Rick Scott for outstanding community service, as well 
as Top Businesswoman, Top Business Leader, Entrepreneur of the Year, 
Business of the Year, Mujer Emprende, and Portada Latina. Her legacy is 
defined by compassion, innovation, mentorship, philanthropy, and an 
unwavering commitment to empowering others and strengthening 
communities.
```
