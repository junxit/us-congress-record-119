# HONORING YVETTE TIRADO GONZALEZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING YVETTE TIRADO GONZALEZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2026

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Yvette Tirado Gonzalez is a dedicated 
kindergarten teacher in Osceola County with more than 38 years of 
experience. Throughout her career, she has remained committed in 
inspiring young learners and strengthening families and the community.
  Proud of her Puerto Rican heritage, Yvette earned her bachelor's 
degree from the University of Puerto Rico. Her cultural background has 
played an important role in shaping her philosophy as an educator. She 
firmly believes that diversity enriches both education and society, and 
she encourages students and colleagues to celebrate their heritage, 
language, and traditions proudly.
  Inspired by the values her parents instilled, Yvette has built her 
life and career around two guiding principles: Unity and Respect. These 
values motivated her to pursue a career in education, where she has 
dedicated herself to helping others overcome challenges, recognize 
their potential, and believe in what they can achieve.
  Her dedication to education has earned recognition, including the 
2009 Distinguished Minority Educator of the Year, the 2010 
Superintendent's High Five Award, and the 2025 A+ Teacher honor from 
Spectrum News 13. She was nominated as a Top Ten Finalist for the 2026 
Osceola County Teacher of the Year.
  As the community around her became increasingly diverse, Yvette felt 
called to extend her impact beyond the walls of her classroom. She 
became actively involved with nonprofit organizations including Relay 
for Life, March of Dimes, and Give Kids the World Village. Through her 
involvement, she has worked to bring people together, strengthen 
connections between cultures, and demonstrate the importance of 
service, compassion, and community.
  Yvette believes that embracing diversity and building meaningful 
connections across cultures can strengthen not only in Florida, but 
throughout the world.
  Her guiding motto reflects the legacy she continues to build:
  ``Diversity is the thread that holds us together.''
```
