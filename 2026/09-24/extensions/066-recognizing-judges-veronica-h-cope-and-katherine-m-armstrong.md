# RECOGNIZING JUDGES VERONICA H. COPE AND KATHERINE M. ARMSTRONG

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
     RECOGNIZING JUDGES VERONICA H. COPE AND KATHERINE M. ARMSTRONG

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Judge 
Veronica H. Cope and Judge Kathrine M. Armstrong and would like to 
congratulate them on their appointment to serve on the Gwinnett 
Judicial Circuit Superior Court.
  Governor Kemp recently selected these two women for these important 
and admirable positions. Both judges have esteemed legal careers and 
have shown a dedication to justice through their service to their 
communities.
  Judge Cope has promoted social justice for Georgians throughout her 
career and has fought for fairness and equality for all Georgians.
  Judge Armstrong is a lifelong citizen of Gwinnett County and has led 
a life serving her friends, neighbors, and community from the very 
beginning.
  I have no doubt that both of these judges will serve with dedication 
and shall hold the utmost respect for their office and their 
communities.

                          ____________________
```
