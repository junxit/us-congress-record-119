# HONORING SOMERSET HILLS EDUCATION FOUNDATION'S 30-YEAR ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
   HONORING SOMERSET HILLS EDUCATION FOUNDATION'S 30-YEAR ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 30th anniversary of 
Somerset Hills Education Foundation. For 30 years, the foundation has 
dutifully pursued its vision to empower every student in the Somerset 
Hills School District to excel academically, develop strong character, 
and embrace lifelong learning.
  Established in 1986, this independent nonprofit has consistently 
dedicated itself to supporting the next generation. Through a wide 
range of academics professional development, and community partnership 
programs, they inspire students to ``stand on their toes and reach for 
the stars.''
  As we commemorate this remarkable milestone, we express our sincere 
gratitude for Somerset Hills Education Foundation's three decades of 
service to Somerset Hills School District and the State of New Jersey. 
I wish them continued success in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
