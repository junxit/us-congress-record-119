# RECOGNIZING DOWNTOWN BRUNSWICK

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING DOWNTOWN BRUNSWICK

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
downtown Brunswick, Georgia, on being named the downtown of the year by 
the Georgia Downtown Association.
  This grand achievement was presented at the GDA annual conference in 
Rome. The award celebrates communities that demonstrate outstanding 
achievement in planning, historic preservation, economic development, 
placemaking, organization, and community engagement.
  Over the past two decades, downtown Brunswick has transformed into 
one of Georgia's premier downtown destinations by restoring historic 
buildings, opening thriving businesses, and welcoming more than 1.3 
million visitors annually. Brunswick exemplifies the very best of 
downtown revitalization, having created a downtown atmosphere that is 
economically resilient, culturally vibrant, and positioned for future 
success.
  Its achievements serve as an inspiration for communities across 
Georgia. It is a great honor to represent the city of Brunswick, 
Georgia, and I could not be more proud of the economic growth we have 
had in this part of the district.
```
