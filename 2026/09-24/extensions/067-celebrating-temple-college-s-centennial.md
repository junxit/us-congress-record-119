# CELEBRATING TEMPLE COLLEGE'S CENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
                CELEBRATING TEMPLE COLLEGE'S CENTENNIAL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Centennial of Temple College, an institution whose century of service 
has provided educational opportunities, supported generations of 
students, and made a lasting impact through Central Texas.
  Founded in 1926 as Temple Junior College, the institution first held 
classes in the basement of Temple High School under its first 
president, Major Joseph S. Cook. Established through the efforts of 
civic, business, and education leaders, the college provided students 
with greater access to higher education closer to home.
  From its earliest years, Temple College remained dedicated to 
providing students with a quality education. Despite the challenges of 
the Great Depression and World War II, Temple College continued to grow 
expanding its academic programs and establishing its own campus in 
south Temple. As Temple College enters its second century, it continues 
to grow and expand opportunities for students across Central Texas. 
With new facilities supporting a variety of academic and workforce 
programs, students have greater opportunities to learn and prepare for 
their futures. Temple College has also expanded its academic offerings, 
providing students with additional pathways to continue their education 
and pursue their career goals.
  I am honored to congratulate Temple College on its 100th Anniversary 
and recognize its lasting contributions to Central Texas, now serving 
more than 8,600 students and offering more than 80 associate degree and 
certificate programs while supporting veterans and military-connected 
students. I commend the faculty, staff, students, and alumni who have 
shaped the College over the past century and wish Temple College 
continued success for the next hundred years.
```
