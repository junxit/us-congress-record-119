# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF GEORGE KOIKOS

**Speaking:** Patronis, Jimmy (P000622) [R-FL]

```
             HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF GEORGE KOIKOS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JIMMY PATRONIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. PATRONIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of George Koikos, a true legend of the Tallahassee restaurant industry 
and someone I am proud to call my family.
  George is my cousin through marriage and came to America from Greece 
to build a life rooted in the American Dream. He opened The Spartan 
Restaurant in 1969, followed by Brothers Three, Torreya Grill, and 
other successful restaurants before founding Georgio's Fine Food & 
Spirits, which became a Tallahassee institution.
  Long before hospitality management schools were commonplace, George 
helped shape the restaurant industry through his unmatched commitment 
to his customers and employees.
  Throughout his career, he had the privilege of serving Governors, 
United States Senators, Members of Congress, and legendary football 
coaches, while becoming a trusted and beloved fixture in the 
Tallahassee community.
  George leaves behind his beloved wife, Karen Koikos, and his three 
daughters, Katerina Maroney, Leni Spears, and Nikoleta Koikos. He also 
leaves behind countless friends, employees, and members of the 
community who were fortunate enough to know him.
  I am grateful for the memories I have of George and for the legacy he 
leaves behind. My thoughts and prayers are with his family, friends, 
and all those who had the privilege of knowing him.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the life of 
George Koikos. May he rest in peace.
```
