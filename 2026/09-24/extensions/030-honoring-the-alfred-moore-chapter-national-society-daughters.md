# HONORING THE ALFRED MOORE CHAPTER, NATIONAL SOCIETY DAUGHTERS OF THE AMERICAN REVOLUTION

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
 HONORING THE ALFRED MOORE CHAPTER, NATIONAL SOCIETY DAUGHTERS OF THE 
                          AMERICAN REVOLUTION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Alfred Moore 
Chapter of the National Society Daughters of the American Revolution 
(NSDAR) for its generous gift of an America 250 Revolutionary War 
Patriots' Marker to Moore County.
  On October 1, 2026, members of the Alfred Moore Chapter, descendants 
of Revolutionary War patriots, community leaders, and citizens will 
gather at the Moore County Courthouse in Carthage to dedicate this 
permanent tribute to the men and women whose courage, sacrifice, and 
service helped secure the independence of our great Nation.
  It is particularly fitting this tribute to our revolutionary 
generation should stand in a county bearing the name of one of North 
Carolina's own revolutionary patriots. Both Moore County and the Alfred 
Moore Chapter, NSDAR, were named in honor of Alfred Moore, a 
Revolutionary War patriot and distinguished statesman.
  Moore served as an officer in the Continental Army during the 
American Revolutionary War and later devoted himself to public service, 
ultimately serving as an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the 
United States from 1800 to 1804. His legacy of patriotism and service 
provides a meaningful connection between this community and the 
revolutionary generation honored by this marker.
  For more than a century, the Alfred Moore Chapter, NSDAR, has worked 
to preserve our history, educate future generations, promote 
patriotism, and serve the people of its community. This marker 
continues the tradition and establishes a permanent legacy for the 
250th anniversary of our country in Moore County.
  I am especially proud to recognize the Alfred Moore Chapter for 
ensuring the legacy of our revolutionary patriots is preserved here in 
Moore County for generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today celebrating the Alfred Moore 
Chapter, National Society Daughters of the American Revolution.
```
