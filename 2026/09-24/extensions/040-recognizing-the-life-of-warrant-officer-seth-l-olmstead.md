# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF WARRANT OFFICER SETH L. OLMSTEAD

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF WARRANT OFFICER SETH L. OLMSTEAD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
the life and service of Warrant Officer Seth L. Olmstead, a devoted 
soldier, husband, son, brother, and friend whose dedication to his 
family, community, and country will not be forgotten.
  W.O. Olmstead pursued his lifelong dream of becoming a pilot when he 
entered the United States Army as a warrant officer candidate. He went 
on to become an AH-64 Apache pilot, serving at Fort Hood with Bravo 
Company, 1st Battalion, 227th Aviation Regiment, 1st Air Cavalry 
Brigade, 1st Cavalry Division. During his time at Fort Hood, W.O. 
Olmstead served alongside fellow soldiers as part of the Army's 
aviation community, turning his lifelong passion for flying into a 
career dedicated to serving his country. Raised in Bell County, Texas, 
Mr. Olmstead graduated

[[Page E976]]

from Harker Heights High School, where he formed lasting friendships 
and continued to develop an interest in aviation. His early enthusiasm 
for flying, along with the dedication and determination he demonstrated 
throughout his life, helped shape the path that would eventually lead 
him to military service.
  Beyond his military service, Mr. Olmstead will be remembered for the 
love and joy he brought to those around him. W.O. Olmstead was deeply 
devoted to his wife, Lauren, and cherished the time he spent with his 
family and friends.
  Warrant Officer 1 Seth Logan Olmstead demonstrated dedication to his 
country and those he loved. On behalf of a grateful community, I extend 
my deepest condolences to his family, friends, and fellow soldiers, and 
I honor his life, service, and enduring legacy.

                          ____________________
```
