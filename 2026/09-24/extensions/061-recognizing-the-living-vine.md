# RECOGNIZING THE LIVING VINE

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING THE LIVING VINE

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize The 
Living Vine, a Savannah maternity home celebrating its 30th anniversary 
this September.
  Since opening its doors in 1996, the nonprofit maternity home has 
provided pregnant women and their babies with a safe residential 
environment, life skills preparation, and spiritual support aimed at 
helping mothers build stability and work toward independence.
  This organization is one of Savannah's crown jewels and stands as a 
testament to the heart and care of the Savannah community.

[[Page E981]]

  Countless mothers and their babies have passed through the doors of 
this organization, and are much better for having done so.
  The work that The Living Vine does is crucial and often overlooked by 
many.
  I would like to join the countless babies and mothers helped by The 
Living Vine in saying thank you for their work, and please do not stop 
any time soon.

                          ____________________
```
