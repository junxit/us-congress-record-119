# RECOGNIZING JESSE DIAZ OF WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                     RECOGNIZING JESSE DIAZ OF WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Jesse Diaz, 
a WH Lineman with Withlacoochee River Electric Cooperative (WREC), for 
his dedicated service as part of WREC's second mission supporting the 
Light Up Navajo Project. The Light Up Navajo initiative brings skilled 
utility professionals together in service to Navajo families who have 
lived without electricity for generations. Mr. Diaz volunteered his 
expertise as a lineman and helped undertake the challenging work 
required to extend electrical service to families in need. The results 
of this work can transform everyday life. Electricity means children 
can study after dark, families can safely preserve food and medicine, 
and elders can live with greater comfort and security. It brings 
connection, opportunity, and greater independence to the families who 
receive it. Mr. Diaz's willingness to use his professional skills in 
service to people far from his own community reflects tremendous 
character. His contribution exemplifies WREC's commitment to service 
and the cooperative principle that neighbors help neighbors wherever 
the need may be.
  I commend Mr. Jesse Diaz for his skill, dedication, and compassion. 
His service to the Light Up Navajo mission reflects great credit upon 
WREC and our entire community.
```
