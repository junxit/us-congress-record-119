# HONORING DR. L.G. PINKSTON SR. HIGH SCHOOL ON ACHIEVING AN A RATING BY THE TEXAS EDUCATION AGENCY

**Speaking:** Crockett, Jasmine (C001130) [D-TX]

```
HONORING DR. L.G. PINKSTON SR. HIGH SCHOOL ON ACHIEVING AN A RATING BY 
                       THE TEXAS EDUCATION AGENCY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JASMINE CROCKETT

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Ms. CROCKETT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
accomplishments of the students, faculty, staff, families, and entire 
community of Dr. L.G. Pinkston Sr. High School in Dallas, Texas, on 
achieving an A rating from the Texas Education Agency.
  This historic achievement is the result of years of steady growth, 
hard work, and a sustained commitment to excellence. In just 3 years, 
Pinkston has risen from a D rating in 2023 to a C, then a B, and now an 
A rating--demonstrating what can happen when students, educators, 
families, and a community remain committed to progress.
  The Texas Education Agency also recognized Pinkston for student 
progress, social studies performance, and its work helping all student 
groups succeed. These accomplishments reflect not simply a single 
year's performance, but the dedication and consistency of a school 
community determined to ensure that every student has every opportunity 
to succeed.
  Pinkston is preparing its students for success well beyond high 
school. The school offers college and career pathways that include NAF 
academies, career and technical education programs, technology and 
healthcare pathways, and early-college opportunities. Through its H-
TECH healthcare pathway, students can work toward an associate degree 
alongside their high school diplomas.
  This achievement is also a testament to the leadership and vision of 
Principal Tameca Ward, the teachers, and staff who support students 
every day, and the families and community members who continue to 
believe in the promise of Pinkston's students.
  For generations, Dr. L.G. Pinkston Sr. High School has been an 
important part of Dallas' West Side. Today, the Pinkston Vikings are 
building on that legacy and showing what is possible through 
perseverance, high expectations, opportunity, and community.
  I congratulate the students, faculty, staff, families, and community 
of Dr. L.G. Pinkston Sr. High School on this historic A rating. Their 
achievement is a source of pride for Dallas, and I look forward to 
seeing all that the Pinkston community will accomplish in the years 
ahead.
```
