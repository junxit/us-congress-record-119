# HONORING MARIE BEERY

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING MARIE BEERY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Marie 
Beery, as she is recognized for her 25 years of extraordinary 
dedication and service to the Lake County Wine Alliance.
  Marie was born in Indiana. She began her professional career as an 
Accounting Specialist with Kendall-Jackson Winery, where she met 
winemaker Jed Steele. Together, they founded Steele Winery, 
contributing significantly to the growth and recognition of our Lake 
County wine community. She later founded the Saw Shop Gallery & Bistro, 
cultivating it into one of Lake County's most beloved and successful 
dining establishments. Her entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to 
excellence helped elevate the region's culinary landscape.
  Marie is a founding member of the Lake County Wine Alliance. Under 
Marie's leadership as Vice President, as well as her creativity and 
unwavering persistence, the Wine Alliance has raised over $3.2 million 
for vital programs throughout Lake County, more than any other local 
nonprofit organization. All five Lake County high schools have 
collectively received over $1 million from the Alliance, supporting 
Music, Theatre, and Art Departments, as well as Future Farmers of 
America (FFA) and Culinary Programs.

[[Page E980]]

  In addition to the Wine Alliance, she serves as the Treasurer for 
Kelseyville Sunrise Rotary Club, was a past treasurer of Kelseyville 
Business Association, and is also a Meals on Wheels delivery volunteer 
at the Kelseyville Senior Center. Marie is also a Rotary International 
Paul Harris Diamond Circle Member.
  Marie is known for her generosity, reliability, and steadfast 
commitment to her community. She is always willing to lend a hand, step 
into leadership when needed, and bring people together to accomplish 
meaningful work. Her perseverance and community-minded spirit have made 
her an indispensable part of Lake County's philanthropic landscape.
  Marie is one of five siblings and remains closely connected to her 
large extended family who still reside in Indiana. She is the mother of 
one daughter and another son and daughter from her past marriage to the 
late Jed Steele.
  Mr. Speaker, we thank Marie Beery for her years of tireless 
dedication to our community and through the Lake County Wine Alliance. 
Therefore, it is fitting and proper that we honor her here today.
```
