# HONORING THE SONOMA VALLEY WOMAN'S CLUB

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE SONOMA VALLEY WOMAN'S CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
Sonoma Valley Woman's Club for their 125 years of tireless service to 
our community.
  The Sonoma Valley Woman's Club is a historic and vibrant organization 
dedicated to empowering and uniting the women of our beautiful Sonoma 
Valley. Established in 1901, the club has been a cornerstone of our 
community, bringing together women of all backgrounds and ages who 
share a passion for service, learning, and friendship.
  Over the years, the club has hosted countless events, workshops, and 
initiatives that have enriched the lives of our Sonoma Valley 
community. The Sonoma Valley Woman's Club boasts notable contributions 
including bringing the first free library to the city of Sonoma and 
writing the grant that opened a Carnegie library. Today, the club 
engages in educational programs and workshops, philanthropic endeavors 
that benefit local organizations, social gatherings and networking 
opportunities, and advocacy for issues that impact women and families 
in our community.
  The Sonoma Valley Woman's Club also focuses on the preservation and 
maintenance of their historic clubhouse. The Club House was constructed 
by renowned architect Maynard Jones and opened on September 15, 1916. 
Part of the Register of Historic Places, the Club House remains a focal 
point of Sonoma Valley history to this day.
  The Sonoma Valley Woman's Club is led today by President Victoria 
Mann, Vice President Jody Leeds, Treasurer Erika Petroni, Director 
Marilyn Booth, and Director Diane Termini. Without their hard work, the 
legacy of this impactful organization would not be what it is today.
  Mr. Speaker, we thank the Sonoma Valley Woman's Club for their 
dedicated service to our community. Therefore, it is fitting and proper 
that we honor them here today for their 125th anniversary.
```
