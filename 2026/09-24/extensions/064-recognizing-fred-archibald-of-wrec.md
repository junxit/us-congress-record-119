# RECOGNIZING FRED ARCHIBALD OF WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                   RECOGNIZING FRED ARCHIBALD OF WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Fred 
Archibald, an SO Lineman with Withlacoochee River Electric Cooperative 
(WREC), for his outstanding service as part of WREC's second mission 
supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. Through this initiative, 
skilled utility professionals volunteer their time and expertise to 
help bring electricity to Navajo families who have lived without power 
for generations. Mr. Archibald brought his technical knowledge, 
experience, and strong work ethic to this important effort, helping 
provide the infrastructure necessary to connect families to reliable 
power. For the families served, electricity means far more than 
convenience. It provides light after dark, refrigeration for food and 
medicine, and greater safety and comfort at home. These basic 
necessities can open the door to greater opportunity and a better 
quality of life.
  Mr. Archibald's willingness to use his specialized skills to serve 
people far beyond his own community reflects the very best of the 
cooperative tradition. His participation demonstrates professionalism, 
compassion, and a genuine commitment to helping others. I commend Mr. 
Fred Archibald for his technical expertise and selfless service to this 
important mission. His efforts reflect great credit upon WREC and our 
community.
```
