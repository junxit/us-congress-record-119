# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER 2 DEONTRE TREVON HUEY

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER 2 DEONTRE TREVON HUEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
the life and service of Chief Warrant Officer 2 Deontre Trevon Huey, a 
dedicated soldier, aviator, father, brother and son whose life was 
guided by service, leadership, and an enduring commitment to his family 
and country.
  In 2014, Chief Huey began his military career as an 88M 
Transportation Operator, marking the start of 12 years of dedicated 
service to our Nation. He served at Joint Base Lewis-McChord, Fort 
Bragg, and Fort Hood. Seeking a new direction in his military career, 
he attended Warrant Officer Candidate School and pursued his goal of 
becoming an Army aviator. In February 2022, he began serving as an AH-
64E Apache pilot at Fort Hood. Through flying, Chief Huey found a true 
passion that brought him great pride and fulfillment.
  A proud native of Killeen, Texas, Chief Huey graduated from Killeen 
High School, where he displayed leadership on and off the football 
field. He later attended Temple College and Oklahoma Panhandle State 
University. A gifted artist, his love for drawing was another 
reflection of the passion and creativity he brought to his life.
  Above all, Chief Huey will be remembered for the love and joy he 
brought to those around him. He leaves behind his children, Naomi 
Victoria and Ezekeial Titus; his parents, Velvet Dancer-Huey and 
Douglas Huey; his brothers; and many family members and friends.
  Chief Warrant Officer 2 Deontre Trevon Huey represented the very best 
of service, leadership, and dedication to our Nation. His legacy will 
live on through his family, fellow soldiers, and the place he proudly 
called home. On behalf of a grateful community, I extend my deepest 
condolences to his family and friends, and I honor his life, his 
service, and his legacy.

                          ____________________
```
