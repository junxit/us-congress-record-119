# RECOGNIZING GARY STEELE OF WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                    RECOGNIZING GARY STEELE OF WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Mr. Gary Steele of 
Withlacoochee River Electric Cooperative (WREC) for his continued 
dedication to the Light Up Navajo Project and his service as a 
Multimedia Contact during WREC's second mission supporting this 
worthwhile initiative. Mr. Steele previously helped share the story of 
WREC employees who answered the call to bring electricity to Navajo 
families who had lived without power for generations. His continued 
participation in this second mission demonstrates an enduring 
commitment to ensuring that both the work and the people behind this 
remarkable effort are recognized. Documenting the Light Up Navajo 
mission helps tell a story that goes far beyond electrical 
infrastructure. It shows what electricity means in the daily lives of 
families--children having light to study after dark, parents being able 
to safely preserve food and medicine, and elders enjoying greater 
safety and comfort. It also captures the compassion, teamwork, and 
commitment of the men and women who volunteer their skills to make 
those moments possible.
  By helping share these stories, Mr. Steele helps others understand 
both the need that remains and the tremendous difference this 
initiative is making. I commend Mr. Gary Steele for his continued 
commitment to Light Up Navajo and for helping tell this powerful story 
of service, compassion, and community. His efforts reflect great credit 
upon WREC and our community.

                          ____________________
```
