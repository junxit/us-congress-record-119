# HONORING FREEMAN T. WYCHE, SR.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING FREEMAN T. WYCHE, SR.

                                 Florida
                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of Florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th District of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor Dr. Freeman 
T. Wyche, Sr., an inspirational man of God and a revered spiritual 
leader in the Miami's Liberty City community who has served as senior 
minister of the Liberty City Church of Christ for 35 years. I include 
in the Record the following proclamation:

       Whereas, Dr. Wyche is dedicated to his church and to his 
     community, having chaired and served on the boards of many 
     civic and educational organizations in Miami-Dade County and 
     the State of Florida; and
       Whereas, Dr. Wyche was mentored by the late renowned Dr. 
     Marshall Keeble, and has earned several degrees from the 
     University of Houston, New York Theological Seminary, and 
     Faith College; and
       Whereas, Dr. Wyche provided an invaluable service to his 
     country, having served as an air traffic controller and a 
     hospital administrator in the United States Air Force and 
     achieving the rank of master sergeant before retiring at 
     Homestead Air Force Base in 1990; and
       Whereas, Dr. Wyche's unwavering devotion to God has been 
     evidenced by his decades of ministration, teaching, 
     evangelizing, and counseling across the Nation and around the 
     world; and
  Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Frederica S. Wilson, a Member 
of the United States House of Representatives, representing the 24th 
Congressional District of Florida, commend Dr. Freeman T. Wyche, Sr., 
for his years of faithful service to his family, his church, and our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
