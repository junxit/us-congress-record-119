# RECOGNIZING BRIAN ALLEN OF WREC

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                    RECOGNIZING BRIAN ALLEN OF WREC

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of FLORIDA

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 24, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Brian 
Allen, an SO Lineman with Withlacoochee River Electric Cooperative 
(WREC), for his exceptional service as part of WREC's second mission 
supporting the Light Up Navajo Project. The Light Up Navajo initiative 
brings skilled utility professionals together to help extend electrical 
service to Navajo families who have lived without power for 
generations. Mr. Allen volunteered his expertise as a lineman and 
helped undertake the challenging, hands-on work required to bring this 
essential service to families in need. The importance of this mission 
cannot be measured simply in poles, wires, or electrical connections. 
Electricity gives children the ability to study after dark, helps 
families safely preserve food and medicine, and provides greater safety 
and comfort for elders. It brings new possibilities to homes and 
communities. Mr. Allen's willingness to put his professional skills to 
work on behalf of others demonstrates tremendous character and embodies 
the cooperative tradition of neighbors helping neighbors.
  I commend Mr. Brian Allen for his hard work, professionalism, and 
dedication to this worthwhile mission. His contribution to Light Up 
Navajo reflects great credit upon WREC and our entire community.

                          ____________________
```
