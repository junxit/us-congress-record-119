# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** S. 3257, S. 4128, H.R. 4624, S. 4668

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 6830. Mr. SCOTT of South Carolina submitted an amendment 
     intended to be proposed to amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. 
     Thune (for Mr. Cruz (for himself, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, 
     and Mr. Coons)) to the bill S. 4668, to protect the name, 
     image, and likeness rights of, and provide protections for, 
     student athletes and to promote fair competition among 
     intercollegiate athletics, and for other purposes; which was 
     ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 6831. Mrs. MOODY submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed to amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. 
     Cruz (for himself, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, and Mr. Coons)) 
     to the bill S. 4668, supra; which was ordered to lie on the 
     table.
       SA 6832. Ms. ROSEN submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by her to the bill S. 4128, to prohibit Cabinet 
     Members and senior executive political appointees from using 
     taxpayer funds to hire political consulting, advertising, and 
     marketing firms, from expediting competitive open bidding 
     processes to contract for official advertisements, and from 
     using official advertisements for self-promotion; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs.
       SA 6833. Mr. CRUZ (for himself and Ms. Rosen) submitted an 
     amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 
     4624, to amend the Professional Boxing Safety Act of 1996 to 
     establish requirements for unified boxing organizations, to 
     further enhance the well-being of professional boxers, and 
     for other purposes; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
       SA 6834. Mr. HUSTED (for Mr. Hoeven (for himself and Ms. 
     Duckworth)) proposed an amendment to the bill S. 3257, to 
     require the Administrator of the Federal Aviation 
     Administration to revise regulations for certain individuals 
     carrying out aviation activities who disclose a mental health 
     diagnosis or condition, and for other purposes.
       SA 6835. Mr. BOOKER (for himself and Mr. Murphy) submitted 
     an amendment intended

[[Page S5012]]

     to be proposed to amendment SA 6776 proposed by Mr. Thune 
     (for Mr. Cruz (for himself, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Schmitt, and 
     Mr. Coons)) to the bill S. 4668, to protect the name, image, 
     and likeness rights of, and provide protections for, student 
     athletes and to promote fair competition among 
     intercollegiate athletics, and for other purposes; which was 
     ordered to lie on the table.
```
