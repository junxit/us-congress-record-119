# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 898, S.Res. 899, S.Res. 900, S.Res. 901, S.Res. 902, S.Res. 903, S.Res. 904, S.Res. 905, S.Res. 906, S.Res. 907, S.Res. 908, S.Res. 909, S.Res. 910

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Ms. ROSEN:
       S. Res. 898. A resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Senate that the additional tariffs imposed on Canadian goods 
     under section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 and the rhetoric 
     surrounding the bilateral relationship between Canada and the 
     United States harm United States consumers and businesses and 
     the tariffs should be rescinded; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. MURPHY (for himself, Mr. Tillis, Mr. Reed, Mr. 
             Peters, Mrs. Capito, and Mr. Justice):
       S. Res. 899. A resolution recognizing suicide as a serious 
     public health problem and expressing support for the 
     designation of September as ``National Suicide Prevention 
     Month''; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
           By Mr. HEINRICH (for himself, Mr. Lujan, Mr. King, Mr. 
             Sanders, Ms. Warren, Mr. Schatz, Ms. Smith, Mr. 
             Gallego, Mr. Hickenlooper, Mr. Kaine, Ms. Hirono, Mr. 
             Merkley, Mr. Schiff, Mr. Padilla, and Mr. Booker):
       S. Res. 900. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of the second Monday in October 2026 as 
     ``Indigenous Peoples' Day'' to celebrate and honor Indigenous 
     Peoples and their shared history and culture; to the 
     Committee on Indian Affairs.
           By Ms. DUCKWORTH (for herself and Mr. Wyden):
       S. Res. 901. A resolution recognizing that the United 
     States needs to support and strengthen mothers in the 
     workforce by investing in the Mom Economy; to the Committee 
     on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. SCHATZ (for himself, Mr. Wicker, Mr. Warner, 
             Mrs. Hyde-Smith, Mr. Welch, and Mr. Barrasso):
       S. Res. 902. A resolution supporting the designation of the 
     week of September 20 through September 26, 2026, as 
     ``Telehealth Awareness Week''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mrs. BLACKBURN (for herself, Mrs. Murray, Mr. Lujan, 
             Mr. Hickenlooper, Ms. Cantwell, Ms. Rosen, Mr. 
             Hagerty, and Mr. McConnell):
       S. Res. 903. A resolution designating October 30, 2026, as 
     a national day of remembrance for the workers of the nuclear 
     weapons program of the United States; considered and agreed 
     to.
           By Mrs. HYDE-SMITH (for herself and Mr. Warnock):
       S. Res. 904. A resolution designating the week of September 
     20 through September 27, 2026, as ``Gold Star Families 
     Remembrance Week''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. KING (for himself, Ms. Collins, Mr. Blumenthal, 
             Mr. Murphy, Mr. Reed, Mr. Whitehouse, Mr. Markey, Ms. 
             Warren, Ms. Hassan, and Mr. Scott of Florida):
       S. Res. 905. A resolution designating September 25, 2026, 
     as ``National Lobster Day''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. WYDEN (for himself, Mrs. Gillibrand, Ms. Hirono, 
             Mr. Padilla, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Booker, Mr. Lujan, 
             Mr. Markey, Ms. Blunt Rochester, and Ms. Klobuchar):
       S. Res. 906. A resolution supporting the designation of the 
     week of August 24 through August 28, 2026, as the fourth 
     annual ``National Community Health Worker Awareness Week''; 
     to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Ms. SLOTKIN (for herself and Mrs. Britt):
       S. Res. 907. A resolution supporting the designation of 
     September 2026 as ``National Ovarian Cancer Awareness 
     Month''; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
           By Ms. KLOBUCHAR (for herself and Mr. Cassidy):
       S. Res. 908. A resolution supporting the designation of the 
     week of September 21 through September 25, 2026, as 
     ``National Hazing Awareness Week''; to the Committee on 
     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. SCHATZ (for himself and Ms. Hirono):
       S. Res. 909. A resolution designating September 2026 as 
     ``Hawaiian History Month'' to recognize the history, culture, 
     and contributions of Native Hawaiians and reaffirm the United 
     States Federal trust responsibility to the Native Hawaiian 
     Community to support their well-being; to the Committee on 
     Indian Affairs.
           By Mr. BOOKER:
       S. Res. 910. A resolution expressing support for the 
     designation of October 2026 as ``Lewy Body Dementia Awareness 
     Month''; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
```
