# TRIBUTE TO MAJOR GENERAL GREGORY C. PORTER

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
               TRIBUTE TO MAJOR GENERAL GREGORY C. PORTER

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I rise today to honor MG Gregory 
Porter and his 40 years of service to our country, State, and the 
Wyoming National Guard.
  After graduating from the University of Wyoming, General Porter began 
his service in the Wyoming Army National Guard as an aerial observer in 
the 115th Field Artillery Brigade. At the start of Gov. Mark Gordon's 
administration, Greg was selected to lead the Wyoming National Guard as 
the Adjutant General.
  Throughout his time as the Adjutant General, Greg has led the Wyoming 
Army and Air National Guard through many deployments overseas to the 
Middle East and Africa. Most notably, under his command, the Wyoming 
Army Guard 2-300th Field Artillery Regiment completed their first full 
battalion deployment for a field artillery mission in over 70 years. 
The 2-300th were also under the command and control of the 115th Field 
Artillery Brigade Headquarters, another first. I have had the honor to 
visit many of these Wyoming soldiers and airmen overseas on 
Thanksgiving.
  Over the years, we worked shoulder-to-shoulder to secure eight C-130J 
Super Hercules aircraft for the 153rd Airlift Wing. This effort stands 
as a testament to the determination of the hard-working Wyoming 
servicemen and women who serve our State and Nation every day.
  Through the State Partnership Program, General Porter deepened and 
broadened cooperation between Wyoming and Tunisia. These of military-
to-military and civilian-to-civilian exchanges include education, 
agriculture, and tourism. The Wyoming National Guard has been in 
partnership with Tunisia for over 20 years and General Porter is 
leaving the relationship stronger than ever.
  General Porter coordinated the successful Exercise Agile Chariot with 
the U.S. Air Force Special Operations Command and the Wyoming 
Department of Transportation, WYDOT. Landing a MC-130J with a payload 
of MH-60 Little Birds, A-10 Thunderbolts, and a MQ-9 Reaper drone on a 
Wyoming highway is a first. Agile Chariot proved that Wyoming can 
support multi-domain operations and exercises. This exercise was a 
preview of the daring rescue mission to recover the two F-15 aviators 
downed in the mountains of Iran.
  From second lieutenant to the Adjutant General, Greg Porter's 
leadership and dedication have equipped our National Guard with 
operational prowess.
  I want to thank General Porter for his unwavering commitment to the 
Wyoming National Guard and to our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
