# ILLEGAL RED SNAPPER AND TUNA ENFORCEMENT ACT

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S. 283

```
              ILLEGAL RED SNAPPER AND TUNA ENFORCEMENT ACT

  Mr. HUSTED. Madam President, I ask that the Chair lay before the 
Senate a message from the House on S. 283.
  The Presiding Officer laid before the Senate the following message 
from the House of Representatives:

       Resolved, That the bill from the Senate (S. 283) entitled 
     ``An Act to require the Under Secretary of Commerce for 
     Standards and Technology and the Administrator of National 
     Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration to develop a standard 
     methodology for identifying the country of origin of seafood 
     to support enforcement against illegal, unreported, and 
     unregulated fishing, and for other purposes.'', do pass with 
     an amendment.


                            Motion to Concur

  Mr. HUSTED. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
concur in the House amendment and that the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
