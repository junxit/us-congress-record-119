# Text of Senate Amendment 6832

**Speaking:** Rosen, Jacky (R000608) [D-NV]

```
  SA 6832. Ms. ROSEN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
her to the bill S. 4128, to prohibit Cabinet Members and senior 
executive political appointees from using taxpayer funds to hire 
political consulting, advertising, and marketing firms, from expediting 
competitive open bidding processes to contract for official 
advertisements, and from using official advertisements for self-
promotion; which was referred to the Committee on Homeland Security and 
Governmental Affairs; as follows:

       On page 5, strike lines 12 through 15, and insert the 
     following:

     SEC. 5. PROHIBITION ON SELF-PROMOTION THROUGH OFFICIAL 
                   ADVERTISEMENTS.

       Neither the President of the United States, the Vice 
     President of the United States, nor a Cabinet Member may use 
     any official advertisement for the primary purpose of self-
     promotion.
                                 ______
```
