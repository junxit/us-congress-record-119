# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2026

**Speaking:** Husted, Jon (H001104) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S. 4668

```
                 ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2026

  Mr. HUSTED. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, September 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, morning business be closed, and the Senate resume consideration of 
S. 4668, as amended, post cloture; further, that postcloture time from 
4 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. be equally divided between the proponents and 
opponents of the bill and with the opponent time under the control of 
Senator Booker; finally, that the postcloture time expire at 5:30 p.m., 
the bill, as amended, be read a third time, and the Senate vote on 
passage of the bill, as amended.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
