# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10549

**Measures:** H.R. 10549

```
           By Mr. DUNN of Florida:
        H.R. 10549.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to spend money for the general welfare, 
     enumerated in the Tax and Spending Clause of Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause I of the Constitution.
```
