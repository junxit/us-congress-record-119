# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 897, H.R. 952, S. 1363, H.R. 1721, S. 1962, H.R. 2481, S. 2933, S. 3404, H.R. 4467, H.R. 5345

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agreed to the following resolution:

                              S. Res. 897

       Whereas Jon Llewellyn Kyl (referred to in this preamble as 
     ``Senator Kyl'') was born on April 25, 1942, in Oakland, 
     Nebraska;
       Whereas Senator Kyl earned a bachelor's degree in 1964 and 
     a law degree in 1966 from the University of Arizona, where he 
     met his wife, Caryll;
       Whereas Senator Kyl was admitted to the Arizona State Bar 
     in 1966, practiced law in Phoenix for 20 years, and served as 
     chairman of the Phoenix Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce from 
     1984 to 1985;
       Whereas Senator Kyl was elected to the United States House 
     of Representatives in 1986 and represented the people of 
     Arizona's Fourth Congressional District for 4 terms, from 
     January 3, 1987, to January 3, 1995;
       Whereas Senator Kyl was elected to the United States Senate 
     in 1994, reelected in 2000 and 2006, and represented the 
     people of Arizona in the Senate from January 3, 1995, to 
     January 3, 2013;
       Whereas Senator Kyl was selected by his colleagues to serve 
     as chairman of the Senate Republican Policy Committee, 
     chairman of the Senate Republican Conference, and Republican 
     Whip;
       Whereas Senator Kyl made lasting contributions to Arizona 
     and the United States through his work on water policy, crime 
     victims' rights, national security, defense, and 
     intelligence;
       Whereas, following the death of Senator John McCain, 
     Senator Kyl once again answered the call to public service 
     when he was appointed to the Senate on September 4, 2018, 
     took the oath of office on September 5, 2018, and served 
     through December 31, 2018;
       Whereas, after leaving elected office, Senator Kyl 
     continued to serve the United States, including as vice chair 
     of the Congressional Commission on the Strategic Posture of 
     the United States;
       Whereas the Kyl Center for Water Policy at Arizona State 
     University's Morrison Institute for Public Policy and the Kyl 
     Institute for National Security at the University of Arizona 
     were named in his honor, reflecting his enduring 
     contributions to Arizona and the United States; and
       Whereas Senator Kyl passed away at the age of 84, leaving 
     behind a legacy of distinguished service to the people of 
     Arizona and the United States: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate--
       (1) has heard with profound sorrow and deep regret the 
     announcement of the death of Jon Llewellyn Kyl, former member 
     of the Senate;
       (2) respectfully requests that the Secretary of the Senate 
     communicate this resolution to the House of Representatives 
     and transmit an enrolled copy thereof to the family of Jon 
     Llewellyn Kyl; and
       (3) stands adjourned as a further mark of respect to the 
     memory of the late Jon Llewellyn Kyl when the Senate adjourns 
     today.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed without 
amendment bills of the House of the following titles:

       H.R. 952. An act to convey the reversionary interest of the 
     United States in certain land in Sacramento, California.
       H.R. 1721. An act to direct the Secretary of Commerce to 
     conduct a study on the feasibility of manufacturing in the 
     United States products for critical infrastructure sectors, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2481. An act to require online dating service 
     providers to provide fraud ban notifications to online dating 
     service members, and for other purposes.

       H.R. 4467. An act to modify the boundary of the Vicksburg 
     National Military Pack in the State of Missippi, and for

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed bills of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1363. An act to provide for greater cooperation and 
     coordination between the Federal Government and the governing 
     bodies and community users of land grant-mercedes in New 
     Mexico relating to historical or traditional uses of certain 
     land grant-mercedes on Federal public land, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 1962. An act to amend the Secure and Trusted 
     Communications Networks Act of 2019 to prohibit the Federal 
     Communications Commission from granting a license or United 
     States market access for a geostationary orbit satellite 
     system or a nongeostationary orbit satellite system, or an 
     authorization to use an individually licensed earth station 
     or a blanket-licensed earth station, if the license, grant of 
     market access, or authorization would be held or controlled 
     by an entity identified on the Covered List or an affiliate 
     of such an entity, and for other purposes.
       S. 2933. An act to redesignate a playground in the New 
     River Gorge National Park and Preserve in the State of West 
     Virginia as the ``Heart of Gold Playground: In Honor of West 
     Virginia Children and Families Impacted by Childhood 
     Cancer''.
       S. 3404. An act to require a report on Federal support to 
     the cybersecurity of commercial satellite systems, and for 
     other purposes.
```
