# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God, our creator, we pray by the power of Your Holy Spirit that in 
these days of division, we would build ourselves and each other up in 
faith. We ask, given what we know of You--Your steadfast love, Your 
supreme justice, Your unconditional mercy--that You would continue to 
surround us and inspire us to regard one another with the kindness and 
forbearance You have shown us.
  May we be merciful to those who hesitate to believe in You. May we be 
willing to draw others away from the danger of their own willfulness. 
May we show mercy to those caught up in sin, even as we remain mindful 
of Your judgment should we veer from Your perfect will.
  Loving God, may we keep ourselves in the confines of Your love this 
day and until the day we are united with You in eternity.
  In Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
