# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 10575

**Measures:** H.R. 10575

```
           By Ms. LOFGREN:
       H.R. 10575.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 8 of the Constitution of the 
     United States, which grants Congress the power to promote the 
     progress of science and useful arts by securing for limited 
     times to authors the exclusive right to their writings, and 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, which grants Congress the 
     power to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution its enumerated powers.
```
