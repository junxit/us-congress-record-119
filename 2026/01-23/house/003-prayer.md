# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Exalted God and King, let us praise Your name today and always. We 
need only look at the handiwork of Your creation, and there we discover 
how great You are and due our praise. Who You are and what You have 
done are beyond our wildest imaginings.
  Let our generation carry on the legacy of those who have gone before 
and told us of Your majesty. For in thick and thin You continued to 
reveal to them the wonder of Your care and provision. Though they had 
to endure challenges to their lives and well-being, nonetheless, they 
gave testimony to the awe of Your goodness and mercy.
  As they meditated on Your presence in their lives, so may we. As they 
proclaimed Your great deeds, so may we. As they acknowledged Your 
compassion and sang of Your righteousness, so may we.
  For You are a God of eternity. In our lives, as in theirs, You remind 
us that You have been gracious and compassionate. Despite our mistakes, 
as with theirs, You have always been slow to anger and rich in mercy.
  This day, O Lord, may we bear witness to Your goodness that those who 
follow us will know You.
  In Your timeless and gracious name, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
