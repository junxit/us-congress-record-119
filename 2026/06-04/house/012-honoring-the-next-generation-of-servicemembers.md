# HONORING THE NEXT GENERATION OF SERVICEMEMBERS

**Speaking:** Haridopolos, Mike (H001099) [R-FL]

```
             HONORING THE NEXT GENERATION OF SERVICEMEMBERS

  (Mr. Haridopolos of Florida was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)

[[Page H3837]]

  

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
extraordinary event on Florida's Space Coast.
  At the 2026 Space Coast Enlistment Ceremony, more than 230 young men 
and women took the next step in their commitment to serve our Nation by 
enlisting in the United States Armed Forces. This is a remarkable event 
and was the vision of Brevard Public Schools Board Chair Matt Susin, 
whose leadership helped create a meaningful opportunity to honor these 
young Americans as they begin a life of service.
  As the father of a servicemember, I recognize that these individuals 
are choosing a path defined by duty, sacrifice, and love of their 
country. They will serve in the Air Force, Marine Corps, Army National 
Guard, Navy, and Coast Guard, carrying forward the legacy of those who 
have defended our freedoms in the past.
  The ceremony also brought together 241 Junior ROTC cadets from across 
Brevard County, a reminder that the next generation is already 
preparing to lead.
  On the Space Coast, we understand freedom is protected by those who 
are willing to step forward, and today we honor these enlistees, their 
families, and the leaders like Matt Susin and the members of the 
Brevard County Schools Board, who are helping to inspire the next 
generation of American service.


    Strengthening Port Canaveral and Florida's Space Coast Ceremony

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight a major 
investment on Florida's Space Coast.
  I was proud to join local leaders at Port Canaveral to announce a $20 
million Federal grant that will support the construction of new cargo 
berths that will strengthen one of the Nation's most important ports.
  Port Canaveral is far more than just a cruise destination. It is a 
critical economic engine for our region, supporting jobs, commerce, and 
supply chain necessary to keep Florida moving.
  This investment will expand not only the cargo capacity but the 
overall infrastructure that is needed in challenging times. For the 
Space Coast, this means more opportunities, more jobs, and continued 
economic momentum for the families and businesses that call the Space 
Coast home. They will have the fuel necessary in all times of trouble.
  This is exactly the kind of practical investment that we need to 
continue to make sure Florida continues to lead the Nation in growth.


               Honoring the Life of Darcia Jones Francey

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of 
Darcia Jones Francey, a true champion of Brevard County whose impact 
will be felt for generations.
  A third-generation Brevardian, Darcia devoted her life to making her 
community and our community stronger. She was known as a connector of 
people, someone who brought together people in support of causes that 
mattered and endured. This effort is not unnoticed.
  Darcia served on countless boards and different community groups, 
supporting organizations across the Space Coast, from the arts to 
healthcare and programs helping those truly in need. Her dedication as 
a volunteer and community work leader helped raise awareness, build 
partnerships, and strengthen the fabric of Brevard County.
  Darcia also had a unique ability to make everyone around her feel 
valued and important, leaving a lasting impression on all of us who 
knew her.
  Today, we remember Darcia not only for what she did but how she 
lived--with purpose, passion, and a deep love of our community.


                  Honoring Alec Hall of Alliance Cyber

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding veteran, entrepreneur, and leader from Florida's Space 
Coast, Alec Hall.
  Alec is the president of Alliance Cyber, a service-disabled, veteran-
owned small business based in Cocoa, Florida.
  Through Alec's leadership, Alliance Cyber provides cybersecurity and 
IT solutions that protect government agencies, nonprofits, and 
businesses across our State and beyond.
  Alec's impact goes far beyond business. Through the Alliance Cyber 
Gives Foundation, his team supports STEM education, workforce 
development, and career pathways for transitioning servicemembers, 
investing directly in the next generations.
  It is because of these efforts that we recognize Alec Hall and the 
amazing work at Alliance Cyber.
```
