# RECOGNIZING COACH JACK CLARK

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING COACH JACK CLARK

  (Mr. DeSAULNIER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Coach Jack 
Clark, the architect of one of the greatest college sports programs in 
the history of the United States and his induction into the Bay Area 
Sports Hall of Fame.
  A remarkable athlete from a young age, Jack played varsity football 
and rugby at the University of California before shifting his focus 
solely to rugby and playing for the U.S. national rugby team, where he 
was selected the first American to be amongst the best rugby players in 
the world.
  Since 1984, Jack has served as the varsity rugby coach at Cal, 
leading the team to an astounding 31 national championships. He also 
coached the U.S. national rugby team to the 2003 World Cup. Jack's 
mentorship has touched the lives of countless young athletes in 
California and, indeed, from across the country.
  Please join me in recognizing this remarkable leader and coach, Jack 
Clark, for his induction into the Bay Area Sports Hall of Fame and for 
his legendary career as Cal's head rugby coach. Go Bears.

                          ____________________
```
