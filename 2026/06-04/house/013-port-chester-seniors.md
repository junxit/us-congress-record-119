# PORT CHESTER SENIORS

**Speaking:** Latimer, George (L000606) [D-NY]

```
                          PORT CHESTER SENIORS

  (Mr. Latimer of New York was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
programs provided by the Village of Port Chester that benefit the 
senior citizens of that community.
  Under the able leadership of Maryann Nielsen in the Port Chester 
Senior Community Center in Village Hall, the Port Chester Seniors 
serves hundreds of seniors annually with a daily nutrition program at 
lunchtime 6 days a week, along with activities and programs to engage 
seniors of all ages, including on Saturday, which is unique among many 
other programs.
  There are fitness classes from tai chi to chair yoga, mental fitness 
programs, a variety of board games--mah-jongg; bridge; along with the 
classic senior staple, bingo. Maryann and her talented crew host annual 
events, as well, for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, St. Patrick's Day, 
and a host of celebrations. There are day trips planned to shopping 
locales and tourist sites, as well.
  The programs are supported by the village board under the leadership 
of Mayor Luis Marino and Village Administrator Stu Rabin.
  From the floor of the House of Representatives, we salute the work of 
the Port Chester Seniors, the active and energetic members that show 
that age is more a state of mind.


                              Pride Month

  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Pride Month.
  As a supporter of the LGBTQIA+ community and a member of the 
Congressional Equality Caucus, I rise to honor the achievements in the 
fight for equality but also refocus on the work that lies ahead.
  Many of the champions in the fight for equality are organizations 
like the LOFT Center in White Plains, New York. This organization 
provides acceptance and access for the LGBTQIA+ community in the wake 
of the current administration's attacks on equality.
  The LOFT Center offers services ranging from support services, 
educational seminars, health screenings, training series for allies and 
businessowners, silver connections for members over 55, and much more.
  I commend them for being a champion in this fight, and I am proud to 
represent such a welcoming district, where people are judged by the 
content of their character. We will work to ensure that everyone has a 
place where they are accepted, even in the face of prejudice.


                  Dismantling of Affordable Healthcare

  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to condemn the intentional 
dismantling of affordable healthcare in our country.
  Following the big, ugly bill last July, which failed to extend the 
premium tax credits critical to lowering the cost of healthcare for 
working families, Affordable Care Act enrollment for 2026 has fallen to 
about 23 million people, a decline of just over 1 million people.
  Studies predict that this may climb to as many as 5 million people 
dropping out of the ACA market by the end of this year alone. This 
isn't surprising given that the last year represented the highest 
increase in deductibles ever.
  Many Americans have shared their stories about how they cannot afford 
to take on the rising premiums, and my colleagues choose instead to 
prioritize nonsense legislation and policies that do nothing to address 
real problems.
  Mr. Speaker, more people will die younger or put off preventative 
health visits if they do not have access to affordable healthcare. If 
we will not address America's healthcare situation head-on, then let 
those of us who are willing to address the situation take action.
```
