# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Call us together, O God, into the embrace of Your everlasting arms. 
Whoever we are, whatever we bring with us, hold us near to You that You 
would, in Your mercy, forgive our shortcomings and, in Your all-
sufficient grace, mend our imperfections.
  And even as You work within us individually, move among us as a body. 
As we come together, we bring with us a multitude of concerns, the best 
and the worst of intentions, and the full spectrum of desired outcomes. 
Take the breadth of our disparate perspectives and competing agendas 
and make us see that our mutual goal should always be for the building 
up of this body and the betterment of the Nation we serve.
  Move Your spirit over the waters of our discord. You, who are the 
author

[[Page H3839]]

not of confusion, but of peace, bring us to order under the authority 
of Your divine will.
  Lord, hear our prayers, forgive our sin, establish the work of our 
hands, and heal our land.
  In Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
