# Text of Senate Amendment 5687

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

```
  SA 5687. Mr. BLUMENTHAL submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUNDS FOR CERTAIN FEATURES 
                   RELATING TO THE EAST WING MODERNIZATION 
                   PROJECT.

       None of the funds made available by this Act or Public Law 
     119-21 (139 Stat. 72) (commonly known as the ``One Big 
     Beautiful Bill Act'') may be used for any above-ground 
     features relating to the East Wing Modernization Project.
                                 ______
```
