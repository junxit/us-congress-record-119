# Text of Senate Amendment 5558

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

```
  SA 5558. Mr. PETERS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham to the bill S. 2, to provide 
for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place in title I, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUNDS TO SEARCH HOMES.

       None of the funds made available under this title may be 
     used to conduct a search of the home of a United States 
     citizen unless such search is lawful under the Fourth 
     Amendment to the Constitution of the United States.
                                 ______
```
