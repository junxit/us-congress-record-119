# Text of Senate Amendment 5673

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

```
  SA 5673. Mr. BLUMENTHAL submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUNDS FOR DETAINING OR 
                   DEPORTING UNITED STATES CITIZENS.

       None of the funds made available under this title may be 
     used to detain or deport United States citizens for 
     immigration enforcement purposes
                                 ______
```
