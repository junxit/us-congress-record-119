# SECURE AMERICA ACT

**Speaking:** Warnock, Raphael G. (W000790) [D-GA]

**Measures:** S. 2, H.Con.Res. 14

```
                           SECURE AMERICA ACT

  Mr. WARNOCK. Mr. President, when the Senate considers the 
reconciliation bill, S. 2, it is my intention to make the following 
motions to the bill:

       A motion to recommit S. 2 to the Committee on Banking, 
     Housing, and Urban Affairs of the Senate with instructions to 
     report the same back to the Senate in 3 days, not counting 
     any day on which the Senate is not in session, with 
     amendments that--
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) would increase homeownership and lower housing costs by 
     providing $20,000 in downpayment assistance for more than 
     3,500,000 first-time homebuyers.
       A motion to commit the bill S. 2 to the Committee on 
     Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry of the Senate with 
     instructions to report the same back to the Senate in 3 days, 
     not counting any day on which the Senate is not in session, 
     with changes that--
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) would fund assistance for farmers that have been hurt 
     by tariffs imposed by the current Administration.
       A motion to commit the bill S. to the Committee on Finance 
     of the Senate with instructions to report the same back to 
     the Senate in 3 days, not counting any day on which the 
     Senate is not in session, with changes that--
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) provide assistance to lower Medicare Part B premiums.
       A motion to commit S. 2 to the Committee on Banking, 
     Housing, and Urban Affairs of the Senate with instructions to 
     report the same back to the Senate in 3 days, not counting 
     any day on which the Senate is not in session, with 
     amendments that--
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) would lower costs for Americans by paying all rental 
     costs for a year for 4,250,000 households.
       A motion to commit the bill S. 2 to the Committee on 
     Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs of the Senate with 
     instructions to report the same back to the Senate in 3 days, 
     not counting any day on which the Senate is not in session, 
     with changes that--
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) would increase housing supply by constructing or 
     preserving more than 840,000 homes.
       A motion to commit the bill S. 2 to the Committee on 
     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions of the Senate with 
     instructions to report the same back to the Senate in 3 days, 
     not counting any day on which the Senate is not in session, 
     with changes that  
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) would provide funding for universal pre-kindergarten in 
     the United States for all children ages 3 and 4, including 
     through Head Start programs.
       A motion to commit the bill S. 2 to the Committee on 
     Finance of the Senate with instructions to report the same 
     back to the Senate in 3 days, not counting any day on which 
     the Senate is not in session, with changes that--
       (1) are within the jurisdiction of such committee; and
       (2) would repeal the funding and eligibility cuts to the 
     Medicaid program under title XIX of the Social Security Act 
     (42 U.S.C. 1396 et seq.) made by title VII of the Act titled 
     ``An Act to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II 
     of H. Con. Res. 14'' (Public Law 119-21).
```
