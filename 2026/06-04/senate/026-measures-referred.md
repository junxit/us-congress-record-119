# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 86, H.R. 7618, H.R. 7726

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 7618. An act to amend title 54, United States Code, to 
     modify certain cost-sharing requirements for grant programs 
     under the American Battlefield Protection Program, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
       H.R. 7726. An act to amend the Child Care and Development 
     Block Grant Act of 1990 to withhold funds from noncompliant 
     States under such Act; to the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions.

  The following concurrent resolution was read, and referred as 
indicated:

       H. Con. Res. 86. Concurrent resolution directing the 
     President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers 
     Resolution, to remove United States Armed Forces from 
     hostilities with Iran; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.

                          ____________________
```
