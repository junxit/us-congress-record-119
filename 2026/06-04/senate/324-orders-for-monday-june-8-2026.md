# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 8, 2026

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 8, 2026

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, June 8; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, that the 
cloture motion with respect to Executive Calendar No. 744 ripen at 5:30 
p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
