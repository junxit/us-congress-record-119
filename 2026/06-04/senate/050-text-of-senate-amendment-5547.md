# Text of Senate Amendment 5547

**Speaking:** Welch, Peter (W000800) [D-VT]

```
  SA 5547. Mr. WELCH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title 
II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:
       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. ANTITRUST ENFORCEMENT IN AGRICULTURAL INDUSTRIES.

       The Attorney General shall use funds appropriated by this 
     Act to support antitrust enforcement in industries that 
     supply agricultural inputs.
                                 ______
```
