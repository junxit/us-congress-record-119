# Text of Senate Amendment 5737

**Speaking:** Alsobrooks, Angela D. (A000382) [D-MD]

```
  SA 5737. Ms. ALSOBROOKS submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham to the bill S. 2, 
to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. _____. BUREAU OF CONSUMER FINANCIAL PROTECTION.

       Of the amounts appropriated under section 202(9), 
     $350,000,000 shall be appropriated to the Bureau of Consumer 
     Financial Protection.
                                 ______
```
