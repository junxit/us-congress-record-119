# Text of Senate Amendment 5792

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 5792. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Thune (for Mr. Graham) to the bill 
S. 2, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. 
Res. 33.; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:


        At the end of the amendment, add the following:

     SEC. 204. PROHIBITING IMMIGRATION ENFORCEMENT OPERATIONS THAT 
                   INVOLVE THE USE OF CHILDREN AS PAWNS.

       None of the funds provided by this Act or by any other Act 
     may be used by any Federal, State, or local agency for 
     immigration enforcement operations that involve the use of 
     children as pawns in immigration enforcement, including to 
     lure out family members.
                                 ______
```
