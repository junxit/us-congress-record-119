# Text of Senate Amendment 5654

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
   SA 5654. Mr. MURPHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham  to the bill S. 2, to 
provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       Beginning on page 6, strike line 17 and all that follows 
     through page 11, line 3, and insert the following:

     SEC. 202. PROTECTING THE LOW INCOME HOME ENERGY ASSISTANCE 
                   PROGRAM.

       It is the sense of the Senate that the amount appropriated 
     for the Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program should be 
     increased.
```
