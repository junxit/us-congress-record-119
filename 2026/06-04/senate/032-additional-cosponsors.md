# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 2, S.Con.Res. 33, S. 567, S.Res. 753, S. 1404, S. 1547, S. 1892, S. 2048, S. 2355, S. 3181, S. 3265, S. 3314, S. 3897, S. 3958, S. 4039, S. 4141, S. 4189, S. 4349, S. 4367, S. 4395, S. 4411, S. 4528, S. 4552, S. 4576

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 567

  At the request of Mr. Whitehouse, the name of the Senator from 
Virginia (Mr. Warner) was added as a cosponsor of S. 567, a bill to 
award a Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to the First Rhode 
Island Regiment, in recognition of their dedicated service during the 
Revolutionary War.


                                S. 1404

  At the request of Mr. Grassley, the name of the Senator from Indiana 
(Mr. Banks) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1404, a bill to combat 
organized crime involving the illegal acquisition of retail goods and 
cargo for the purpose of selling those illegally obtained goods through 
physical and online retail marketplaces.


                                S. 1547

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the names of the Senator from Alabama 
(Mrs. Britt) and the Senator from Hawaii (Mr. Schatz) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 1547, a bill to amend title 54, United States Code, to 
reauthorize the National Parks and Public Land Legacy Restoration Fund, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 1892

  At the request of Ms. Murkowski, the names of the Senator from 
California (Mr. Padilla) and the Senator from Missouri (Mr. Schmitt) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 1892, a bill to clarify that amounts 
from declinations should be deposited in the Crime Victims Fund and to 
temporarily provide additional deposits into the Crime Victims Fund.


                                S. 2048

  At the request of Mr. Ricketts, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2048, a 
bill to prohibit the purchase of certain securities from covered 
entities, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2355

  At the request of Mr. Marshall, the name of the Senator from Arizona 
(Mr. Kelly) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2355, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to provide for hospital and insurer price 
transparency.


                                S. 3181

  At the request of Mr. Cornyn, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3181, a 
bill to revoke security clearances for former personnel of the 
Department of Defense who engage in lobbying activities on behalf of 
China, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3265

  At the request of Mr. Cassidy, the name of the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mr. Ricketts) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3265, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to improve and enhance the work 
opportunity tax credit, to encourage longer-service employment, and to 
modernize the credit to make it more effective as a hiring incentive 
for targeted workers, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3314

  At the request of Mr. Sheehy, the name of the Senator from Utah (Mr. 
Lee) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3314, a bill to direct the 
Secretary of Veterans Affairs to expand a directive of the Veterans 
Health Administration regarding informed consent to apply to certain 
types of medications.


                                S. 3897

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the name of the Senator from Maine 
(Ms. Collins) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3897, a bill to revise 
administrative procedures relating to public safety officers' death 
benefits, and for other purposes.


                                S. 3958

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the names of the Senator from 
Arizona (Mr. Gallego) and the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Wyden) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 3958, a bill to amend the Food and Nutrition 
Act of 2008 to transition Puerto Rico to the supplemental nutrition 
assistance program, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4039

  At the request of Ms. Alsobrooks, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4039, a bill to 
amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to reverse the reductions in 
Federal loan availability for graduate and professional students 
enacted under Public Law 119-21.


                                S. 4141

  At the request of Mr. Moran, the name of the Senator from Idaho (Mr. 
Risch) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4141, a bill to amend the 
Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to direct the Secretary of 
Agriculture to make temporary zero-percent interest loans under the 
community facilities direct loan program to construct or renovate 
certain rural hospitals, and for other purposes.


                                S. 4189

  At the request of Mrs. Shaheen, the names of the Senator from North 
Dakota (Mr. Cramer) and the Senator from Maryland (Ms. Alsobrooks) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 4189, a bill to reduce the price of insulin 
and provide for patient protections with respect to the cost of 
insulin.

                                S. 4552

  At the request of Mrs. Gillibrand, the names of the Senator from

                                S. 4576

Pennsylvania (Mr. McCormick) was added as a cosponsor of S. 4576, a

                              S. RES. 753

resolution expressing the sense of the Senate to reduce traffic
```
