# Text of Senate Amendment 5585

**Speaking:** Gallego, Ruben (G000574) [D-AZ]

```
  SA 5585. Mr. GALLEGO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to 
title II of S. Con. Res. 33; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM FUNDING.

        The $1,776,000,000 made available to fund the ``Anti-
     Weaponization Fund'' established by the Attorney General on 
     May 18, 2026, shall be reallocated to carry out the 
     supplemental nutrition assistance program established under 
     the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.).
                                 ______
```
