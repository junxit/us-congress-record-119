# Text of Senate Amendment 5743

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  SA 5743. Mr. SCHIFF submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham to the bill S. 2, to provide 
for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of the amendment, add the following:
       Sec. 204.  No funds made available by this Act may be used 
     by Department of Homeland immigration officials to provide 
     security, crowd management, or other assistance for local law 
     enforcement agencies at or near polling sites.
                                 ______
```
