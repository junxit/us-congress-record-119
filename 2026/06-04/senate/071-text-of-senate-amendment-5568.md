# Text of Senate Amendment 5568

**Speaking:** Shaheen, Jeanne (S001181) [D-NH]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 14

```
  SA 5568. Mrs. SHAHEEN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation pursuant to 
title II of S. Con. Res. 33; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place in title II, insert the following:

     SEC. __. RESCISSION OF FUNDS FOR ICE; APPROPRIATION TO BOP.

       (a) In General.--Of the unobligated balances of amounts 
     made available under section 100052 of the Act entitled ``An 
     Act to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. 
     Con. Res. 14'', approved July 4, 2025 (Public Law 119-21; 139 
     Stat. 361) (commonly known as the ``One Big Beautiful Bill 
     Act''), $1,000,000,000 is hereby rescinded.
       (b) Appropriation.--In addition to amounts otherwise 
     available, there is appropriated to the Director of the 
     Bureau of Prisons $990,000,000, to remain available until 
     September 30, 2029, to be used for increasing salaries for 
     all Bureau of Prisons employees in custodial roles effective 
     January 1, 2027.
                                 ______
```
