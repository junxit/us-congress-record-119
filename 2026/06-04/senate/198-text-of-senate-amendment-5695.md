# Text of Senate Amendment 5695

**Speaking:** Blumenthal, Richard (B001277) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 14

```
  SA 5695. Mr. BLUMENTHAL submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 2, to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. DOJ OIG INVESTIGATION.

       The Inspector General of the Department of Justice shall 
     conduct an investigation into the allegations of manipulation 
     of Federal Bureau of Investigation arrest statistics and the 
     Most Wanted list of the Federal Bureau of Investigation using 
     such amounts as are necessary from amounts made available 
     under section 90007 of the Act entitled ``An

[[Page S2609]]

     Act to provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. 
     Con. Res. 14'', approved July 4, 2025 (Public Law 119-21; 139 
     Stat. 361) (commonly known as the ``One Big Beautiful Bill 
     Act''), which the Secretary of Homeland Security shall 
     transfer to the Inspector General of the Department of 
     Justice upon request.
                                 ______
```
