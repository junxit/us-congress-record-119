# Text of Senate Amendment 5761

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 5761. Mr. PADILLA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham to the bill S. 2, to 
provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUNDS FOR DEPARTMENT OF 
                   HOMELAND SECURITY OFFICERS OR EMPLOYEES TO 
                   ENTER HOMES WITHOUT CONSENT OR WARRANTS.

       None of the funds made available under this Act or Public 
     Law 119-21 may be obligated or expended to allow any officer 
     or employee of the Department of Homeland Security to enter a 
     home absent consent or absent a warrant issued by a neutral 
     and detached judge or judicial officer of a United States 
     District Court judicial official, which shall not include 
     U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement Form I-200 or Form 
     I-205.
                                 ______
```
