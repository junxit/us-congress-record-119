# Text of Senate Amendment 5660

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 33

```
  SA 5660. Mr. BOOKER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham to the bill S. 2, to provide 
for

[[Page S2605]]

reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUND FOR PRIVATE DETENTION 
                   FACILITIES.

       No funds appropriated or authorized under title I or title 
     II of this Act shall be used to enter into a contract with a 
     privately owned or privately operated facility for the 
     purpose of detaining non-citizens.
                                 ______
```
