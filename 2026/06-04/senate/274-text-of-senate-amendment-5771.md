# Text of Senate Amendment 5771

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mark (K000377) [D-AZ]

amendment SA 5453 proposed by Mr. Graham to the bill S. 2, to provide 
for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33.; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

     through page 11, line 3, and insert the following:

     SEC. 202. PROTECTING THE SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE 
                   PROGRAM.

       It is the sense of the Senate that the amount appropriated 
     for the supplemental nutrition assistance program established 
     under the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2011 et
