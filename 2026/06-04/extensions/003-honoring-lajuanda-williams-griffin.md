# HONORING LAJUANDA WILLIAMS-GRIFFIN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                   HONORING LAJUANDA WILLIAMS-GRIFFIN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 4, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor LaJuanda Williams-Griffin, a native of Vicksburg, 
Mississippi, for her outstanding professional achievements and her 
recent selection as a member of the National Black Lawyers Top 100 in 
Mississippi, one of the organization's most prestigious honors 
recognizing excellence among African American attorneys.
  The National Black Lawyers is an invitation-only professional 
development and networking organization composed of the Nation's top 
African American attorneys. Membership is earned through a rigorous, 
multi-phase selection process that includes peer nominations and 
independent research, making this recognition a significant distinction 
within the legal profession.
  LaJuanda is the found and managing attorny of Williams-Griffin Law 
Group, PLLC, a hybrid legal and strategic advisory firm serving clients 
across Mississippi in the areas of family law, child welfare, and 
alternative dispute resolution. Through her practice, she has 
demonstrated a steadfast commitment to advocating for families and 
strengthening legal outcomes for vulnerable populations.
  In addition to her private practice, LaJuanda represents the 
Mississippi Department of Child Protection Services in Warren County 
through a state pilot program designed to imporve permanency outcomes, 
ensure complicance with federal standards, and enhance the 
effectiveness of CPS professionals in the courtroom.
  She is also the founder of Stability Intelligence Group (SIG), PLLC, 
where she provides strategic guidance to organizations navigating 
employee life disruptions, including divorce, custody matters, and 
family instability. Her innovative approach reflects a broader 
commitment to integrating legal insight with organizational strategy. 
Beyond her legal and advisory work, LaJuanda is an entrepreneur and 
small business owner. She owns D'Cracked Egg, a family-friendly diner 
located in Tupelo, Mississippi, which was featured on America's Best 
Restaurants in 2023.
  LaJuanda is a proud United States Army veteran and a graduate of the 
Mississippi College School of Law. She is a Mississippi Supreme Court-
qualified mediator, a FINRA arbitrator, and a published author. Her 
professional accomplishments include recognition among Mississippi's 
Top 50 Businesswomen, admission to the United States Supreme Court Bar, 
and certification as an AI consultant by the U.S. Institute of 
Diplomacy and Human Rights.
  Her leadership extends to service on the boards of the Academy of 
Court-Appointed Neutrals and the Mississippi Women Lawyers Association, 
and she is a graduate of the 2022 to 2023 Mississippi Bar Leadership 
Class.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing LaJuanda 
Williams-Griffin for excellence, innovation, and a deep commitment to 
service. Her accomplishments bring pride to the State of Mississippi 
and serve as an inspiration to aspiring professionals across the 
Nation.
```
