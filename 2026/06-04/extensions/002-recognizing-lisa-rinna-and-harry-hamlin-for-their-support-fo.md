# RECOGNIZING LISA RINNA AND HARRY HAMLIN FOR THEIR SUPPORT FOR THE LGBTQ+ COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Takano, Mark (T000472) [D-CA]

```
   RECOGNIZING LISA RINNA AND HARRY HAMLIN FOR THEIR SUPPORT FOR THE 
                            LGBTQ+ COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK TAKANO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 4, 2026

  Mr. TAKANO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor two award-winning 
actors who have proudly and relentlessly advocated for the LGBTQ+ 
community--Lisa Rinna and Harry Hamlin.
  Lisa Rinna built a career in television, notably on the soap opera 
Days of Our Lives and the reality series The Real Housewives of Beverly 
Hills. Harry Hamlin gained fame through L.A. Law and the fantasy film 
Clash of the Titans. Together, these Hollywood legends exemplify 
allyship at its finest.
  Spanning decades of groundbreaking work on screen, they have not only 
helped shape culture, but consistently used their voices to affirm the 
dignity, humanity, and rights of LGBTQ+ people. Their support has been 
proud, unwavering, and deeply meaningful to a community that continues 
to fight for full equality.
  They have used their platform to champion diversity and inclusion, by 
participating in events and conversations that elevate and promote 
LGTBQ+ voices. Through both their public advocacy and their steadfast 
support, they have helped foster greater visibility, understanding, and 
acceptance of LGBTQ+ people.
  Lisa Rinna and Harry Hamlin's unwavering commitment to full equality 
reflects the highest ideals of humanity.
  As we celebrate this Pride Month, I rise to honor them in their 
accomplishments both as actors and activists.

                          ____________________
```
