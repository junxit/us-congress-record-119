  lifelong native of Hampton, New Jersey.Mr. Gibson was drafted into 
the United States Army in 1943. There he trained as an anti-aircraft 
artilleryman. Mr. Gibson bravely landed in the second wave at Utah 
Beach during the historic ``D-Day'' invasion. Following this Mr. Gibson 
served through Normandy, the Battle of the Bulge, and continued his 
service into Germany until the end of the war. When Mr. Gibson returned 
home, he devoted his life to serving the people of Hampton, New Jersey. 
Mr. Gibson proudly served as a fire fighter, fire chief, councilman, 
and mayor, demonstrating his strong commitment to his community. As a 
humble and devoted man, Mr. Gibson, opened up and shared his wartime 
experiences with students and fellow citizens. He looked to educate 
others on his heroic experiences, using his personal journey to inspire 
future generations. Gibby passed away peacefully on January 12, 2026.
  Mr. Speaker, as I recognize the passing of Robert ``Gibby'' Gibson, I 
am pleased to commend him for being an integral part of the Hampton 
area. I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring this heroic 
gentleman, truly dedicated to serving his country and community with 
courage, bravery, and commitment.
